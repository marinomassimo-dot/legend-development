## The refinement

> **A quantity derived against an undefined set is worse than an undefined one, because it arrives
> with the authority of having been computed.**

Rigor was applied and produced a wronger answer than the assertion it replaced. This is the same
shape as `prohibition`-near-`Mirror` and as my own literal-presence check, with one difference that
matters: the derivation makes the wrong answer *more* credible, not less.

So `LEARN-MIRROR-004`'s remedy is incomplete as stated. **Define the set, then derive against it.**
Otherwise the second quantity measures a different population than the first, and agreement and
disagreement are equally uninformative.
