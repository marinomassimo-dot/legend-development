# A correction is scrutinised less than the claim it corrects

## The observation

`LEARN-MIRROR-004` established that a measurement defect escapes its author when the instrument
returns a plausible answer with no second quantity to reconcile against. This record names **where
that condition is most reliably met**: in a correction.

Four corrections were offered in the closing exchange. **Three were themselves instances of the
defect they were operating on**, and one of the three was mine:

| *"G.2 shut it by inference; §29.2 names it"* (orch) | G.2 names `learning clustering` in the same enumerated list, and adds *incl. budget E.5* |

## Why the act is the carrier

A correction arrives holding the authority of having caught something. That authority is earned
against the *original* claim and is then spent, unexamined, on the *corrective* one. Both actors in
this exchange verified originals to the point of re-running commands in disposable clones — and
shipped corrections after a single grep.

The mechanism is `LEARN-MIRROR-004`'s exactly: a correction is typically a single observation with
nothing to reconcile it against, made at the moment the author is most confident. The conditions
that let a defect escape are therefore **maximised** in the act whose purpose is to catch one.

> **A correction receives less scrutiny than the claim it corrects, precisely because it arrives
> carrying the authority of having caught something.**

## Why this is not an argument against correcting

Every one of the four corrections was worth sending, and two of them were productive **while being
wrong**: the prohibition correction was mistaken in its claim and made me re-run a search that had
covered two refs of five, which is how the true conclusion was established. A refinement wrong in
substance and right in effect is what review is for.

The consequence is not *correct less*. It is: **apply to a correction the standard the original
received.** Concretely, and cheaply — re-read the text being compared to, not only the text being
cited; state the scope searched; produce a second quantity for the corrective claim as well.
