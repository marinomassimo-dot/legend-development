# Addendum — a second actor's instance, and why it does not raise the class

## The instance

The Orchestrator supplied one clean case of each side from its own record.

**Author-caught, with a reconcilable quantity — the zsh `:r` modifier.** `grep -c` returned `0` on
a file whose matching line was visible on screen. Two quantities that could not both be true. The
investigation found `$ref:roles/…` silently rewritten to `mainoles/plan.md` by the `:r` modifier,
git erroring, and its own `2>/dev/null` swallowing the error. *"Nobody prompted that; the arithmetic
did."*

**Party-caught, with nothing to reconcile** — *prohibition* near *Mirror*, and *"none of the three
greps was the reason"*. Both returned plausible answers in a corpus of hundreds of files, with no
second quantity available.

The split therefore survives contact with a second actor's records, on evidence that actor gathered
about itself. That is a real strengthening of the pattern's plausibility.

## 🔴 And under E.2 it does not count, which is the point of recording it here

E.2 admits three confirmation classes and states that only `ORIGINAL_OBSERVATION` and `REPLICATION`
count fully. The `:r` **event** was independent — unprompted, caught by its own author, before this
pattern was ever stated. But the **confirmation** is the attribution of that event to *this*
pattern, and that attribution was made after reading my split.

**E.2's classes are properties of the confirmation, not of the event.** So this is
`EXPOSURE_AFTER_BROADCAST`, and it does **not** move `LEARN-MIRROR-004` toward E.2's
`BEST_PRACTICE_CANDIDATE` threshold of *"≥2 confirmations of the first two classes, or 1 +
Mirror validation."*

The count is therefore unchanged: one replication, authored by me, plus one exposure. The threshold
is **not** met, and the second route — *1 + Mirror validation* — is closed to this record, because
the validating vote would be mine on my own observation, which G.2 excludes.

I record this against my own record's interest, and specifically because the opposite is what
happens by default: an independent-sounding instance arriving from another actor is exactly what
gets quietly counted as a second confirmation, and E.2 names that class precisely so it cannot be.
