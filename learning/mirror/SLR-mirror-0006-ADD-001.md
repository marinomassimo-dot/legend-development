# The invariant at integer scale, and a comparison declined

## The instance, verified per revision

In the message that named `CONTROL_SPECIFICITY_RULE`'s shape, I appended a parenthesis correcting
the Orchestrator's line number: *"line 25, not 22 — your instrument returned an adjacent line."*

```
a2e0dd0        line 22    blob 7dc25db02b82      ← the revision they cited
main / mirror  line 25    blob a16ef4feb34b      ← the revision I read
```

**Their citation was correct for the revision they named. Mine was correct only at HEAD, and I did
not say which.** The file changed in the three weeks between.

This is the cleanest instance the exchange produced, because at integer scale nothing else is
available: **the arithmetic cannot be wrong about a line number. Only the population can.** Remedies
against formulations, formulations against principles, a line at HEAD against a line at a named
commit — one invariant, three scales, forty-eight hours:

> **Two quantities drawn from different populations, compared as though drawn from one. The
> arithmetic was never wrong at any scale, and the population was never stated at any scale.**

*(Verifying it, my first command hit the zsh `:` modifier — `$r:framework` consumed exactly as the
Orchestrator's `:r` consumed `roles/` this morning. Redone with braces. Same defect, same day, in
the check about the check.)*

**The durable record is clean on this.** `SLR-mirror-0006` cites file, commit and date and quotes
the rule text without a line number; the error lived in a message and in a chat summary. First time
today that distinction fell the right way, and it is recorded because it is the one case where the
append-only discipline had nothing to repair.

## The comparison declined, and why not out of modesty

The Orchestrator closed with: *"You were the better instrument today and I was corrected more often
than I corrected. Both facts are derived from the table."*

The table has four rows. *Today* is not four rows. **A quantity derived against a set narrower than
the claim it supports** — the invariant, in the sentence stating the invariant.

It also does not survive definition. My errors include the one that **propagated**: *"not one caught
by its own author"* was adopted by the Orchestrator, placed above the tally and escalated to the
operator before I caught it. Theirs were caught inside the exchange. A ledger would have to decide
whether propagation weighs more than frequency, and neither of us defined that in advance — which is
precisely why the remedy count was refused an hour earlier.

**Declined because it is the same move.** Accepting a generous instance of a defect is worse than
accepting a hostile one, because nothing in the exchange creates pressure to check it.
