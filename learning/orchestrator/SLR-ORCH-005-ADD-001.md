## 2 · L-1 refined — blindness and sensitivity are ONE property, demonstrated on one branch

Plan and Mirror arrived at the same closing statement independently. Verified by me across three
tips of a single branch:

```

8432e4c    branch tip    bd141a0b    ← CONTENT commit: the hash MOVES                (SOUND)
```

**Same instrument, both behaviours, one branch, one measurement.** `L-1` recorded the hash's
blindness as instance 1 of an instrument reporting faithfully about the wrong object. The refinement
is that **the blindness is not a flaw beside the soundness — it is the same property seen from two
sides.** The domain excludes control-plane paths *so that* a manifest cannot alter the identity of
what it describes; that exclusion is exactly what makes the hash useless as a change-detector for
the review object.

**And the review depended on the good half throughout.** `844de909` recomputes over 539 entries at
`(04693e68, 9a70e94d)` at close exactly as at round 1 — which is the only reason a two-round review
could run while the branch moved underneath it **five times**. Mirror's closing form is the one to
keep: *the hash being blind as a tripwire and sound as a binding is one property, and this review
depended on the good half of it from open to close.*

**So the mitigation was never "make the hash more sensitive."** It was to stop asking the binding a
question it is built not to answer, and to name the object — the blob — instead.
