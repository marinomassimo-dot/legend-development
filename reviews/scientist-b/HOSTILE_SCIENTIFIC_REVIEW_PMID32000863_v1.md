## 2 · ATTACKS THAT LAND

### 2.1 🔴 O-12 discriminates nothing, and the repository's own canonical claim says so

**Attacked:** O-12 and, through it, the reconciliation's loop-closing sentence —

> *"**O-12 closes the loop:** WWOX protein is dosage-graded, pSer9 is not. That is what a systemically
> driven readout looks like and what a WWOX-proximal one does not."*

**The attack.** The inference requires that a WWOX-proximal readout *would* be dosage-graded. It
would not, and this repository asserts so canonically. `CLAIM 032`, `in observation`:

> *"WWOX haploinsufficiency is not deleterious … Aldaz 2014: 'loss of one Wwox allele (i.e.
> haploinsufficiency) appears not to be deleterious'; 'the lifespan of the Wwox heterozygotes was
> indistinguishable from WT mice'. Tochigi 2019: i ratti `+/lde` hanno 'a single band of normal
> molecular weight, but its intensity was almost half', con immunoistochimica corticale intensa come
> i `+/+` … Ogni famiglia umana pubblicata … ha genitori portatori eterozigoti sani."*

**If one WWOX allele suffices to hold the brake — which is what "haploinsufficiency is not
deleterious" means — then a WWOX-proximal pSer9 readout is flat in `+/−` and falls only in `−/−`.**
That is exactly the observed pattern. O-12 is equally predicted by both hypotheses and therefore
separates neither.

Worse for the sentence as written: `CLAIM 032` supplies the *positive* reason the WWOX-proximal
reading survives O-12 — a threshold effect — while the reconciliation presents O-12 as the thing
that closes the loop against it.

**Verdict: the observation O-12 survives; the inference drawn from it is REJECTED.** WWOX protein
being dosage-graded while pSer9 is not is a *threshold* signature, not a *systemic* one.

**What this costs I-6.** Not the composition itself — the alternative account still stands on O-15 +
O-16. It costs the composition its closing argument, and the reconciliation italicised that argument
as the thing to carry forward.

### 2.2 🔴 The composed account's A-leg rests on a prediction neither paper ever tested

**Attacked:** the pivot of I-6 —

> *"losing the WWOX brake predicts raised GSK-3β activity **with Ser9 unchanged** (A). Cheng observed
> Ser9 *changed*. So the observed event is not the signature of the lost brake."*

**The attack.** Wang's pS9-unchanged observation is made in a **gain-of-function** context. Verified
verbatim, with its surrounding paragraph:

> *"Increased expression of WWOX was observed in the RA-treated SH-SY5Y cells, accompanying the
> decreased phosphorylation of Tau at both S396 and S404 … We found that the phosphorylation levels
> of phospho-GSK3β S9 and phospho-β-catenin remained normal."*

RA treatment; WWOX **rising**. The very next sentence introduces the loss-of-function experiment —
*"Furthermore, SH-SY5Y cells in which WWOX expression was reduced by RNAi showed increased pTau
S396 levels"* — **and pS9 is not reported for it.**

⇒ **In neither paper, in either direction, is pSer9 ever measured under WWOX loss.** Wang measures
pS9 when WWOX goes up. Cheng measures pS9 when WWOX is absent and has no Wang-side assay. The
proposition *"WWOX loss leaves Ser9 unchanged"* — on which the whole "therefore not the signature of
the lost brake" step depends — is an **extrapolation from a gain-of-function observation to a
loss-of-function prediction**, and it is precisely the measurement that would settle L-1.

**Verdict: WEAKENED, substantially.** The A-leg is an untested prediction presented as an
entailment. I-6's own summary sentence uses the word *"predicts"*, which is honest; the surrounding
text then treats the prediction as established.

**This is not fatal and I say so.** The prediction is *reasonable* — a docking-motif inhibitor
plausibly acts without touching Ser9 — and it is exactly what F-3 would test. But the reconciliation
ranks F-3 **third**, and by this attack F-3 is the experiment that decides the document's central
claim. See 2.5.

### 2.3 O-14 establishes a narrower proposition than the one it is used for

**Attacked:** I-6's A row — *"the S9A mutant behaves like wild type, with internal controls ⇒ the
Ser9 axis is dispensable **by design**"*.

**The attack.** Verified verbatim: *"Transfection with GFP–GSK3β WT and S9A notably decreased
SH-SY5Y cell differentiation, whereas KD and R96A did not."* That is an **overexpression** experiment
about what **GSK-3β** needs in order to suppress differentiation. It shows the Ser9 switch is
dispensable **for the kinase's own output**.

It does **not** show that **WWOX's inhibition of GSK-3β** is S9-independent. Those are different
propositions with different evidence: the second rests on the *separate* pS9-unchanged observation
attacked in 2.2. The reconciliation's phrasing — *"dispensable by design"* — silently merges them.

⚠️ Second-order: ectopic overexpression can saturate a regulatory input and make it look dispensable
at levels where it is not. Neither Wang nor the reconciliation addresses endogenous stoichiometry.

**Verdict: WEAKENED.** O-14 as an *observation* stands and was verified at source by C. The
inference from O-14 to *"WWOX's mechanism does not use Ser9"* is a step, not a restatement, and the
document does not mark it as one.

### 2.4 🔴 I-5's direction claim is a category error; the conclusion survives on a simpler ground

**Attacked:** *"The confound has a direction: higher cumulative exposure makes lithium more likely to
clear significance in exactly the low-ceiling control arms where ETS did not."*

**The attack.** The "higher cumulative exposure" is `3 × 60 = 180 mg/kg` LiCl against `150 mg/kg`
ethosuximide. **Those milligrams are not commensurable.** They are unrelated compounds with unrelated
potencies, mechanisms and pharmacokinetics — a T-type Ca²⁺ channel blocker and a monovalent cation.
`180 > 150` licenses no statement whatever about which arm received the greater pharmacological
exposure. On a molar basis the ratio is different again (LiCl ≈ 4.2 mmol/kg vs ethosuximide ≈ 1.1
mmol/kg), and molarity is not effect either.

**What survives, and it is enough.** The two arms differ in **schedule** (three administrations
within 1 h vs one at −45 min) and in **design** (7b has a saline control arm, 7d has none). That
alone makes them unmatched and makes the cross-panel contrast uncarryable. **The withdrawal of the
ETS-versus-lithium argument is correct. Its stated reason is not.**

**Verdict: the conclusion SURVIVES; the argument for it is REJECTED and should be restated.** I flag
this as the review's clearest case of a right answer reached by an unsound route — the failure mode
this pilot has documented four times in its own actors and did not catch in its own consensus.

### 2.5 🔴 The falsifier ranking is wrong: F-2 cannot attribute, and F-3 decides the central claim

**Attacked:** F-2 promoted as *"decisive"* and as superseding the pair-fed design, with F-3 third.

**The attack on F-2.** *"Ser9 falls there too → the Cheng observation is a readout of terminal
illness"* does not follow. A non-*Wwox* sick animal showing a Ser9 fall establishes that systemic
illness is **sufficient** to lower Ser9. It does not establish that systemic illness is what lowered
it **in Cheng's animals**. Both routes may operate; the reconciliation itself insists at I-6 that
they are *"not mutually exclusive"*, and then adopts a falsifier whose positive arm assumes they are.
**A positive control demonstrates sufficiency, never attribution.**

**Why F-3 outranks it.** F-3 measures GSK-3β output through a substrate that does not use Ser9 —
Wang's own pTau S396/S404 — in `Wwox−/−` brain. It discriminates directly:

| F-3 outcome | Reading |
|---|---|
| Substrate output raised **with Ser9 held constant** | the Wang mechanism operates in vivo; the metabolic route does not explain the biology |
| Substrate output tracking Ser9 alone | the metabolic route explains the panel |

And by 2.2, F-3 also supplies the missing measurement itself: **pSer9 and a Ser9-independent
substrate under WWOX loss**, which no paper has ever reported.

**Verdict: the ranking is REJECTED.** F-3 should be first — it is cheapest (*"uses only tissue the
original study already collected"*, per the document's own note), it is the only proposal that tests
the untested prediction at 2.2, and F-2's positive arm is not decisive. **F-1 (C's L404A knock-in)
remains the best falsifier for the *edge*, which is a different question, and I do not touch its
ranking.**

### 2.6 The metabolic route imports a claim whose own premise is declared open

**The attack.** `CLAIM 036` is the sole source of the systemic phenotype, and its own
`Evidence boundary` carries an unclosed premise tag:

> *"🔴 `PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK` — **l'ablazione di Wwox nel cervello non è mai mostrata**: il
> western copre rene, polmone e milza … e l'IHC solo il rene. Una Cre zigotica rende attesa la
> delezione globale, ma **l'attesa non è misura**. `REVIVAL_TRIGGER`: un western o un'IHC su lisato

## 5b · 🔴 COVERAGE AUDIT OF THIS REVIEW — and a gap in it that I found and closed

Before claiming the phase delivered, I enumerated the object: **40 numbered propositions** —
20 `O`, 8 `I`, 7 `L`, 5 `F`. Then I checked which my review actually names. Two results.

**Correct by measurement, not by luck.** I attacked O-20 for being single-actor while load-bearing,
and left O-18 and O-19 alone. That is right: `O-20` is cited downstream at I-4's *"CONTRADICTED?"*
row, whereas **O-18 and O-19 are cited nowhere in the document except the caveat that introduces
them.** They are terminal observations carrying no inference, so there is no inference to attack.
Aiming the single-actor argument at the one of the three that bears weight was correct; I record
that I verified it rather than assumed it.

**The gap: F-4 and F-5 were named zero times in my review, and both inherit the error I rejected
at 2.4.**

- **F-5**, verbatim: *"Ethosuximide and LiCl at **matched cumulative dose** and schedule, in one
  experiment with a shared control arm."* This is the same incommensurable comparison. You cannot
  match cumulative dose across a T-type Ca²⁺ channel blocker and a monovalent cation in any sense
  that makes the arms comparable. **As written, F-5 is not performable.**
- **F-4**, verbatim: *"a structurally unrelated GSK-3β inhibitor at **matched cumulative exposure and
  matched schedule**."* Same defect, one step milder: two GSK-3β inhibitors at least share a target,
  but matched milligrams across different chemotypes still is not matched exposure.

**The repair, which the same reasoning supplies:** match on an independently established
**equi-effective** basis — each drug at its own ED₅₀ against PTZ in wild-type animals — or on
measured free-brain concentration or target engagement. Then match schedule and administration
count. **Milligram parity is not a control; it is the appearance of one.**

🔴 **This is a failure of my own review and I state it as one.** I rejected the milligram argument in
I-5 and did not follow it into the two falsifiers built on it — so my attack was correct and
**under-propagated**, which is the same shape as the propagation defects this pilot has been
cataloguing in the registry all day. I found it by enumerating the population of propositions and
checking my own coverage against it, which is a step I would not have taken if I had trusted the
review to be complete because it felt thorough.

**Net: F-4 and F-5 move from unreviewed to WEAKENED — reparably.** Their scientific intent is right
and only their matching criterion is unsound.
