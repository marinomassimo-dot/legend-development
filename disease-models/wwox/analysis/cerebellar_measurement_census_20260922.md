## § 2 · PREREGISTERED EXPECTATION — written to disk BEFORE any external query in this act

🔴 **This section was written and flushed to disk before a single PubMed, PMC or web query was issued in this
act, and it has NOT been edited since.** It is preserved verbatim including the parts that turned out wrong.

**The hypothesis under test (a claim about the literature, not about biology):**
> *The cerebellum is systematically UNDER-MEASURED across the whole WWOX experimental literature, and that
> absence has repeatedly been read as evidence of cerebellar SPARING.*

**P-A · Expected ratio of studies that COLLECTED the cerebellum (explicitly stated in Methods).**
I expect **≈25–35 %** of WWOX animal-model / human-neuropathology studies to state cerebellar collection
explicitly, and I expect **`NOT STATED` to be the single largest cell in that column, at ≥50 %**. Reason: most
rodent Methods say "brains were removed" and name only the region they went on to assay; collection is
inferable but not stated.

**P-B · Expected ratio SECTIONED / ASSAYED / REPORTED.**
I expect a **monotone decreasing staircase**: COLLECTED > SECTIONED > ASSAYED > REPORTED, and I expect at
least **one** study where ASSAYED is `YES` and REPORTED is `NO` (that is the Aldaz lead, already flagged by a
sibling and therefore not an independent prediction).

**P-C · Expected count of studies that COUNTED Purkinje cells with a Purkinje-specific MARKER and a stated
DENOMINATOR.**
I expect **exactly 1** with a marker (`PMID 36828035`, calbindin) and **0** with a properly stated denominator
(per-mm of Purkinje-cell-layer length, or per unit volume with a stereological probe). I.e. I expect the
marker-and-denominator cell to be **empty across the whole literature**.

**P-D · Expected fate of the hypothesis.**
I expect the hypothesis to hold in a **narrowed** form and to FAIL in its broad form. Specifically I predict
that the literature will turn out to contain **at least two** studies that did section, assay and report the
cerebellum in a named allele (`PMID 32000863`, `PMID 36828035`), so "the cerebellum is never measured" will be
false; what I expect to survive is the weaker and still consequential *"the cerebellum's sampling status is
overwhelmingly `NOT STATED`, and `NOT STATED` has been read as `NORMAL`."*

**P-E · Expected fate of Scientist 2's `lde/lde` sampling claim.**
I predict it is **TRUE of `PMID 31340538` and FALSE as a general statement about `lde/lde`**, because the 2007
primary (`PMID 17803050`) contains the sentence *"we did not detect any marked pathologic changes in the
cerebella of lde/lde rats"* — a sentence that is only utterable if cerebella were sectioned and looked at.

**P-F · Expected fate of Scientist 3's Aldaz lead.**
I predict **SURVIVES but is NOT CLEAN**: Methods §4.14 names CB, the Results running text does not report it,
and I expect I will be **unable to exclude** a cerebellar panel in a figure or supplement, so the lead ends

## § 3 · THE PER-STUDY CENSUS

**Cell vocabulary, and it is enforced in every cell.**
`YES (quoted)` — a Methods or Results sentence states it and I can quote it ·
`YES (INFERENZA)` — not stated, but entailed by a reported result; tagged, never promoted ·
`NO (explicit)` — a sentence states the compartment was **not** taken, or was discarded ·
`NOT STATED` — the source is silent. 🔴 **This is its own value and is NEVER recorded as `NO`** ·
`UNKNOWN` — I did not read the source at sufficient depth to answer.

**The four cells are kept separate in every row and are never collapsed.**
COLLECTED = the tissue was taken into the experiment · SECTIONED = cerebellar tissue entered a
section series · ASSAYED = a measurement was made on cerebellar material by any modality ·
REPORTED = a cerebellar result appears in the published record.
