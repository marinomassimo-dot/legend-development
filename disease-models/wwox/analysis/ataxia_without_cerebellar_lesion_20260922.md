## § 4 · CONNECT — one adjacent literature, and it supplies the algorithm, not the biology

**Field: the clinical and experimental neurology of ATAXIA LOCALISATION** — the bedside/bench discipline that
decides, for a patient or an animal with an unsteady gait, whether the lesion is cerebellar, sensory,
vestibular, corticospinal, extrapyramidal, neuromuscular or functional. It is a mature field with a settled
discriminating battery, and it is **allele-agnostic, gene-agnostic and disease-agnostic by construction**.

### ✅ WHAT TRANSFERS

1. 🎯 **The diagnostic ALGORITHM — that "ataxia" is a localisation question with a fixed decision tree**, not a
   diagnosis. The tree's first split is the one this repository has never made: *is the deficit compensable by
   vision?* The romberg-class manoeuvre, in its rodent form (gait in darkness versus light), is the single
   cheapest discriminator in § 3 and it comes entirely from this field.
2. 🎯 **The ASSAYS, item by item, and they are all standard rodent neurophysiology and neuropathology:**
   nerve conduction velocity (motor and sensory, separately); F-waves and distal latency; somatosensory evoked
   potentials; central motor conduction latency versus amplitude; air-righting and contact-righting for the
   vestibular limb; repetitive nerve stimulation for the neuromuscular junction; instrumented gait kinematics
   with **body-size normalisation** as a required covariate; and stereological cell counts with a denominator.
3. 🎯 **The negative lesson that a light-microscopic "unremarkable" is not a localisation.** In this field, the
   histologically normal cerebellum is the *beginning* of the work-up, not its end — because the commonest
   causes of an ataxic gait with a normal cerebellum are, by construction, outside the cerebellum.
4. **The amplitude-versus-latency grammar** — that demyelinating lesions slow conduction while axonal and
   neuronal lesions reduce amplitude — which is what makes `H1`, `H2` and `H5` separable at all.

### ❌ WHAT DOES NOT TRANSFER

1. 🔴 **No aetiology transfers.** Not one disease of that field is imported. **No mechanism from any other gene
   is imported** — not `KCNJ10`, not `MLC1`/`GlialCAM`, not `PMP22`, not `MPZ`, not any spinocerebellar-ataxia
   gene, not any hereditary-neuropathy gene. `WWOX` has no established pathway relationship to any of them,
   and this file asserts none.
2. 🔴 **No biology transfers.** That a demyelinating neuropathy causes sensory ataxia in *some* disorder is not
   evidence that `lde/lde` has one. It is evidence that **the assay exists and is interpretable**.
3. 🔴 **No therapy transfers, and none is named.** The field supplies no intervention to this one.
4. 🔴 **The human clinical battery does not transfer to a dying dwarf rat as-is.** Romberg's test requires
   standing still on command; heel-shin requires instruction; a rating scale requires cooperation. Only the
   **principle** (visual compensation) transfers, and it must be re-instrumented as a rodent light/dark gait
   comparison. This is exactly the failure mode `cerebellar_functional_readout_census_20260922.md` records for
   human WOREE cohorts, *"[who] cannot perform the prerequisites of any cerebellar scale"* — the same trap,
   one species down.
5. 🔴 **Nothing transfers to a human WWOX-DEE genotype class.** `CLAIM 039` is `T3`. No statement in this file
   is about a person.
