🔴 **An intervention whose sign is unknown is not a node; it is a coin toss with a safety cost.** Worse, the
organoid pattern is **internally discordant** — mTOR down should *release* autophagy, and autophagy is down
anyway. And a lysosome-directed lever is **genotype-splitting in the dangerous direction**: for a null allele it
could only act downstream, while for the missense class `D-03` already warns that pushing chaperone/lysosomal
routing *"could **accelerate** Q230P destruction."*

**The experiment that would make it a node, and not before:** clamped autophagic flux (LC3-II ± a lysosomal
block, with p62) in isogenic human WWOX-KO neurons versus parental. **Until that returns a sign, this stays
parked.** `TFEB` returns **zero mentions** in the entire `disease-models/wwox/` tree, and I am deliberately not
proposing it as a lever — naming a master regulator whose required direction is unknown would be exactly the
`D-03` error.

## 4 · THE HONEST VERDICT ON SUPERIORITY

**The portfolio is weak, and I did not find anything stronger. Both halves of that sentence are findings.**

The reason is structural and it is measurable. Every candidate node in this disease must pass three filters at
once, and the filters are close to mutually exclusive in a field this size:

1. **It must act on the *developmental* endpoint, not the seizure endpoint** — `DL-MECH-039`: it is a **DEE, not
   an EE**; `N-15` calls counting seizure control as developmental protection *"the most consequential negative
   in the whole portfolio."* This filter removes every symptomatic axis, including the one genuinely
   genotype-specific in-vivo result the field has (ethosuximide).
2. **It must have a window that is still open after diagnosis** — `DL-MECH-011` puts the gene-therapy window at
   **P1–P5 in the mouse**; `DL-MECH-031` calls it neonatal; the radial-glia abnormality is **not corrected even
   by WWOX restitution**. This filter removes everything that acts on neurogenesis or corticogenesis.
3. **There must be an intervention arm somewhere in a WWOX-deficient system** — and the downstream census
   measured that there are exactly **four positive arms in the entire literature**, with `Wwox AND (animal
   model) AND (nine named drugs)` returning **3 PubMed records total**, all already held.

**`NODE 1` is the only thing I found that passes filters 1 and 2** — dendritic arborisation and myelination
continue for years postnatally, so its target process is still running when a child is diagnosed, which is
precisely what gene replacement, splice rescue, protein rescue and the MYC/Wnt arm all fail at. **It fails
filter 3 completely:** there is no arm, in any WWOX system, ever. So it is a **flag with a cheap first
experiment**, and it is ranked first because of the *shape* of its window, not because of the weight of its
evidence.

**The single most useful output of this node is therefore a negative, and it is stated plainly:**

> 🔴 **Tau-lowering must not be imported into WWOX-DEE.** The mechanism — GSK3β de-repressed, pTau S396/S404 up —
> looks like a tauopathy and will attract the tauopathy field's dominant modality. **The primary data say Tau is
> the effector of the benefit, not the toxin:** *"Neither WWOX overexpression nor GSK3 knockdown promoted neurite
> outgrowth in the Tau knockdown condition, indicating that Tau is the effector of both WWOX and GSK3"*
> (PMID 22193544, full text, this act). Removing Tau **abolished** the rescue. This belongs in
> `mechanism_intervention_map.md` §5 as a new negative alongside `N-01`.
