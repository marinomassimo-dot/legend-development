## § 2 · WHAT THE FIRST-HAND READ RETURNED

### 2.1 🔴 THE FINDING — the authors state the confound themselves, and this repository does not hold the sentence

Discussion § 3.3, verbatim as served:

> *"Another study showed a loss of GABAergic interneurons in the hippocampus of a ubiquitous S-KO model
> (). This is consistent with our observed decreased amplitude and frequency of spontaneous inhibitory
> currents. As the action potential properties of the interneurons themselves are unchanged in the WWOX
> S-KO, these findings may indicate a change in pre- or post-synaptic properties unrelated to action
> potential firing, **or reflect a WWOX-dependent decrease in the interneuron population () for which we
> did not account.**"*

⇒ 🎯 **`H6` is not LEGEND's inference. It is the source authors' own declared, unadjusted limitation,
in print, in the paper that carries the explanandum.**

🔴 **Absent from this repository.** Verified repo-wide, all Markdown:
`"decrease in the interneuron population"` → **0 hits**; `"did not account"` → **0 hits**;
`"ubiquitous S-KO"` → **0 hits**. The repository read this paper, extracted `H0` and `H6` as its own
reasoning, and did not capture the sentence in which the authors concede the same point.

🔴 **This is the FOURTH instance of the same propagation defect**, after `CLAIM 016`, `CLAIM 039`'s P1

### 2.2 🎯 THE CONSEQUENCE FOR THE SCOPE CORRECTION — and it cuts against the brief that commissioned this act

The standing classification is `GENERIC METABOLIC-DEMAND VULNERABILITY MODEL = REFUTED`, and the
counterexample it rests on is stated as: *a metabolically demanding population (fast-spiking
interneurons) remains relatively intact on the reported electrophysiological measure while pyramidal
neurons are altered.*

🔴 **The word "fast-spiking" never describes a recorded cell anywhere in this paper.** It occurs
**exactly once**, in Discussion § 3.6, and in a forward-looking sentence about a different literature:

> *"Given that fast-spiking interneurons and oligodendrocyte progenitors (NG2 cells) have coordinated
> development in the neocortex (), future studies may look at the relationship between
> oligodendrocyte-specific loss of WWOX and **the activity of selective neocortical interneuron
> subtypes**."*

⇒ The authors are naming subtype resolution as **future work**, which is a statement that their own
recorded population was not subtype-resolved. Methods § 5.3 and § 6.6 describe at length how *putative
pyramidal neurons* were identified and confirmed (*"spike-half width, input resistance, and membrane
time constant"*). 🔴 **They describe no identification procedure for interneurons at all.** The
operational definition of "interneuron" in this paper is therefore **the not-pyramidal residue**.

🎯 **Stated plainly, and against the framing that commissioned this act:**

> The refutation of the generic metabolic-demand model does **not** rest on a measurement showing that a
> metabolically demanding population is intact. It rests on a measurement showing that **an
> unsubtyped, non-pyramidal population of unknown composition and unstated `n`** showed no significant
> difference in evoked action-potential number.

🔴 **This does NOT rescue the generic metabolic-demand model.** Nothing here is evidence *for* it. The
refutation is weakened as *evidence*, not reversed as a *verdict* — and the correct move is to weaken
the confidence language, not to reopen the node. The node stays closed against the bioenergetic ceiling
(`metabolic_differential_vulnerability_reposed_20260922.md:605`), and stays explicitly **not** closed
against `H8` terminal mitochondrial logistics (`:547`). **`GENERIC METABOLIC-DEMAND MODEL REFUTED` is
still not `ALL METABOLIC MODIFIER BIOLOGY REFUTED`, and now it is also not `MEASURED IN A FAST-SPIKING
POPULATION`.**

### 2.3 🟡 A Results ↔ Discussion disagreement, recorded where figure/text identity matters

| Surface | Verbatim | |
|---|---|---|
| **Results § 2.6** | *"there were more action potentials generated in pyramidal neurons of S-KOs as compared to S-WT controls"*; and *"For the interneurons, **only the action potential amplitude** was diminished in the S-KOs compared to the controls"* | pyramidal AP **number up**; interneuron AP **amplitude down** |
| **Discussion § 3.6** | *"This could also be a reason why **action potentials are reduced in both pyramidal neurons and interneurons** in our model."* | a reduction asserted for **both** classes |

⚠️ **The disagreement may be a `number`-versus-`amplitude` elision rather than a contradiction** — *"action
potentials are reduced"* is ambiguous between the two, and the Results support an amplitude reduction for
the interneuron arm. 🔴 **But the pyramidal half cannot be checked from the served text at all**, because
every per-property value lives in a table the extractor stripped. Recorded as
`DISCUSSION OVERSTATES OR ELIDES; PYRAMIDAL HALF UNVERIFIABLE FROM THIS SURFACE` — **not** as a
demonstrated error in the source, and **not** as a new universal gate (§14: apply only where figure or
panel interpretation matters; here it does, because the sentence is the source's own bridge from
hypomyelination to excitability).

### 2.4 🔴 An allele-pooling error committed in the source, inherited by anyone who quotes the sentence

Discussion § 3.3 refers to *"a loss of GABAergic interneurons in the hippocampus of **a ubiquitous S-KO
model**"*. 🔴 **`S-KO` is defined by this same paper (Methods § 5.1) as the Synapsin-Cre conditional.
"A ubiquitous S-KO" is a contradiction in terms** — the cited comparison is to the **systemic null**.

⇒ Any repository statement that carries this sentence forward inherits a **null-versus-conditional
pooling** of exactly the class LEGEND forbids. Recorded so that it is not inherited. The underlying
datum — hippocampal GABAergic interneuron loss — belongs to the **systemic null** and does **not**
transfer to `S-KO` without a separate measurement, which this paper did not make.
