### 2.2 🎯 THE `6 of 9` DENOMINATOR, CHECKED AGAINST THE DOSSIER — and it does not carry what it appears to

**What the dossier literally says** (🔴 SIBLING-ATTESTED, `research/fulltext_dossiers/PMID19500159.md:111-112`,
read directly by me this act):

> *"Extracellular vacuoles in the hippocampus of **9/9** affected and the amygdala of 6, with **0/10**
> normals."*

**Four things follow, and three of them weaken the observation:**

1. 🔴 **The denominator "9" is INFERRED, not stated.** The dossier writes `9/9` for the hippocampus and a bare
   `6` for the amygdala. `6/9` is the natural reading and is the one the siblings use
   (`wwox_subfield_celltype_expression_20260922.md:229`), and I adopt it — **but it is a reconstruction of a
   fraction from a bare count, and the primary is not retrievable in this deployment to check it.**
   `PREMISE: INFERENZA` → the fraction `6/9` is an inference from a dossier's sentence structure, not a
   transcribed fraction.
2. 🔴 **The control denominator is 10, not 9.** Affected `n=9`, normal `n=10`. The cohorts differ, and
   **whether the amygdala was in-section in all 9 affected and all 10 normals is unrecorded anywhere.**
   A missing amygdala in three blocks produces `6` just as well as a spared amygdala in three animals.
   `NOT_SEEN ≠ NOT_AFFECTED`, and here the two are indistinguishable from the record.
3. 🎯 **The two penetrances are DIFFERENT, and nobody has said so: hippocampus 100%, amygdala ~67%.**
4. 🎯 **And the difference runs AGAINST the expression reading.** The structure the expression map singles out
   (amygdala) is the **less** consistently lesioned; the structure the expression map does **not** name
   (CA1/hippocampus) is the **fully penetrant** one. If baseline `Wwox` level set vulnerability, the ordering
   should be the other way round. 🔴 **This is an internal inconsistency inside the coincidence itself, and
   it uses only material the repository already holds.**

## 3 · `H0` — RANKED FIRST, AGAINST MY OWN INTEREST

> ### **`H0`: there is no coincidence to explain.**

### 3.1 The lesion side is not a measurement — and for the amygdala it is weaker than for the hippocampus

Everything in § 2.1–2.2 applies, and the amygdala carries **three** defects the hippocampus does not: an
**inferred** denominator, an **unstratified** structure, and a **lower** penetrance whose cause (true sparing
vs absent section) is unrecoverable from the record. 🔴 **The repository's own standing verdict —
*"an impression from an image, not a measurement"* — applies with more force to the amygdala than to CA1.**

### 3.2 🎯 THE BASE-RATE ARGUMENT — how surprising is this really?

**The comparison set is unstated, and everything turns on it.** *"Among the two top regions"* is a ranked
statement with **no scale, no threshold and no denominator of regions**. Three separate deflations:

**(a) The review's own sentence may not say "top two in the brain".** Read the quotation again
(§ 2.1): *"ISH probes significantly light up a region of **hippocampal formation**, specifically … `ENTm2` …
**and also** … `BLAa` and `BLAp`"*. The superlative is scoped to the **hippocampal formation**; `BLA` is
appended with *"and also"*. 🔴 **And the repository's own registry restatement of the same review lists
FOUR-to-FIVE regions, not two** — *"entorhinal cortex, basolateral amygdala, frontal-cortex layer 5 neurons
and cerebellar granule and basket cells"* (`paper_registry_current.md:7192`;
`CC-20260920-EIGHT-RECORD-CLASSIFICATION-01.md:125`, both 🟢 read directly this act). **Two independent
restatements of one sentence give two different region counts.** `Q2` in § 6 puts this at risk.

**(b) The arithmetic, with the comparison set treated as the free parameter it is.** Let the "top" set have
`k` named regions and the brain be partitioned into `N` regions at the granularity the review used; the lesion
names `m = 2` structures. Under a uniform draw, `P(at least one overlap) = 1 − C(N−k, m)/C(N, m)`:

| Granularity of the comparison set | `N` | `k` | `P(≥1 overlap by chance)` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Major forebrain grey structures a reviewer would enumerate | 12 | 4 | **0.58** |
| Allen "summary structure" level, coarse | 40 | 4 | **0.19** |
| Allen summary structures, full | ~300 | 4 | **0.027** |

🔴 **At the granularity a narrative review actually works at — a dozen or so named structures — a one-of-two
hit is MORE LIKELY THAN NOT.** And the review's granularity is precisely what is unestablished.

**(c) The draw is not uniform, and every departure from uniformity runs the same way.** CA1 and the
basolateral amygdala are (i) large, (ii) cell-dense and therefore conspicuous in any ISH image, (iii) present
in the standard coronal forebrain block, and (iv) the two structures a 2007 epilepsy paper would look at
first. **The lesion structures are drawn from exactly the pool a reviewer names, so even 0.027 is an
over-estimate of the surprise.**

> ### 🔴 **BASE-RATE VERDICT, STATED BEFORE ANY SEARCH: NOT VERY SURPRISING.**
> On the most defensible reading of the comparison set, a one-of-two overlap between a four-to-five-region
> "top" list and a two-structure lesion list is **roughly a coin flip**. The observation as stated carries
> **almost no evidential weight on its own.**

### 3.3 🎯 The cross-species check the repository already had, and nobody ran

If the basolateral amygdala were a genuine `WWOX` hot-spot, the human bulk amygdala should sit high. It does
not (🔴 SIBLING-ATTESTED, `purkinje_…:330`, `:896`; CSV cross-checked exactly by two siblings):

| Region | GTEx median TPM | Ratio to hippocampus |
|---|---|---|
| Cerebellar hemisphere | **12.13** | 2.17× |
| Frontal cortex | 6.9 | 1.24× |
| **Amygdala** | **6.17** | **1.10×** |
| Hippocampus | 5.59 | 1.00× |

🔴 **In the only numeric, cross-species check available, the amygdala is 1.10× the hippocampus and BELOW both
frontal cortex and cerebellum. It is not a hot-spot; it is unremarkable.** ⚠️ **Stated against myself:** this
is human, adult, bulk and **not nucleus-resolved** — a `BLA`-specific signal would be diluted across the whole
complex, and species and molecule both differ. **It cannot refute the mouse ISH statement. It does show that
the "amygdala is a top `WWOX` region" reading does not generalise**, and it is the only number in the whole
argument.

## 5 · CONNECT — the adjacent field that already had to tell a map from a circuit

**Field of origin: the network-degeneration / connectome-based lesion-pattern literature in human
neurodegeneration**, with the **prion-neuropathology "lesion profile"** as its quantification ancestor.
🔴 **Protein and aetiology of origin carried throughout and imported nowhere:** that field's substrates are
misfolded tau, TDP-43, α-synuclein and PrP. **None has any relationship to `WWOX`, and no mechanism, no
molecule and no therapy from it is imported here.**

### ✅ WHAT TRANSFERS — the method, and one hard-won negative lesson

1. 🎯 **The standardized region-stratified lesion score.** Prion neuropathology solved exactly the problem the
   `lde/lde` lesion side has: *how do you compare vacuolation across regions and across animals?* The answer
   was a **fixed region list, scored on every animal, with a denominator and a defined scale** — which is why
   that field can distinguish strains by pattern at all. 🔴 **This repository's lesion side has a per-animal
   presence/absence tally and nothing else.** The method transfers intact, is allele-agnostic and is what
   `E1` should be built on.
2. 🎯 **The formal competition between lesion-pattern models.** That literature explicitly posed the rival
   families this node needs — **shared regional vulnerability** vs **connectivity-based spread/stress** vs
   **trophic failure** — and built them as *competing predictors of the same lesion map* rather than as
   stories. **That study design transfers**: score the lesion, then test it against a connectivity predictor
   and an expression predictor **in the same model**, and report which survives when both are present.
3. 🔴 **THE NEGATIVE LESSON, AND IT IS THE MOST VALUABLE THING IN THIS SECTION.** That field learned the hard
   way that **regional gene expression and regional connectivity are themselves correlated**, and that brain
   maps carry strong spatial autocorrelation — so a naive overlap between a lesion map and an expression map
   is **confounded by construction**, and needs a null that preserves spatial structure rather than a
   uniform draw. 🔴 **The amygdala coincidence of this node is precisely the naive overlap that field
   abandoned**, computed on **one structure out of two** against an **unstated comparison set**. § 3.2's
   coin-flip arithmetic is, if anything, generous to it.

### ❌ WHAT DOES NOT TRANSFER

1. 🔴 **No aetiology.** There is no prion, no protein spread, no seeded aggregate and no proteinopathy in any
   `WWOX` model, and none is proposed. The `lde/lde` vacuole is not a spongiform prion lesion and the
   resemblance is at the level of *the word "vacuole"*, which the repository already records as a word that
   gets reassigned at electron microscopy (`new_discovery_node_20260922.md` § 4).
2. 🔴 **No biology.** Nothing about `PrP`, tau or TDP-43 touches `WWOX`. **No mechanism is imported.**
3. 🔴 **No therapy.** That field supplies no intervention here, and none is named.
4. 🔴 **No transfer of the human-imaging statistics to a rat histology series.** The connectome machinery is
   built on human network data at a scale a nine-animal rat histology series does not have. **What transfers
   is the design logic, not the statistics.**
