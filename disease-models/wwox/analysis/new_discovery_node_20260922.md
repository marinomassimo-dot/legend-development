## 4 · CONNECT — the adjacent field, with both halves

**Field of origin: the vacuolating/spongiform leukoencephalopathies and the ion-channel ataxia-epilepsies** —
specifically **MLC1/GlialCAM** (megalencephalic leukoencephalopathy with subcortical cysts) and **KCNJ10/Kir4.1**
(EAST/SeSAME syndrome), plus the classical toxic intramyelinic-oedema models. **Protein/system of origin carried
throughout: MLC1 is an astrocytic junctional/endfoot membrane protein; Kir4.1 is an astrocytic and renal-tubular
inward-rectifier K⁺ channel. Neither has any relationship to WWOX.**

### ✅ WHAT TRANSFERS

1. **The diagnostic algorithm, not the biology.** The MLC1/GlialCAM and toxic-oedema literatures settled, by
   electron microscopy, how to tell **intramyelinic splitting** from **astrocytic endfoot swelling** from **true
   extracellular-space expansion**. That algorithm is method, is allele-agnostic and is exactly what `H1`, `H4`
   and `H5` need. **It transfers.**
2. **The negative lesson that light-microscopic "vacuole" is not a compartment.** In that field, lesions called
   "vacuolar" at LM resolution were repeatedly reassigned at EM. This is the single strongest reason `H5` is
   live and has never been checked in any WWOX model.
3. **The phenotype grammar of a glial K⁺/water disorder** — ataxia + seizures + a *renal* abnormality — which is
   why `H1` is attractive at all, and which supplies its falsifier in the next section.

### ❌ WHAT DOES NOT TRANSFER

1. 🔴 **The renal signature is the wrong kind, and this is a sharp negative against `H1`.** A Kir4.1
   loss-of-function disorder is a **salt-wasting tubulopathy**: hypokalaemia, hypomagnesaemia, metabolic
   alkalosis. The `lde/lde` rat has **normal K⁺, Na⁺, Cl⁻ and Ca²⁺** with **raised BUN/creatinine and
   histologically normal kidneys** (O6, O7). That is not a tubulopathy; it is the opposite kind of renal
   abnormality. **If the mechanism were Kir4.1-class, the electrolyte panel should be deranged and it is
   explicitly not.** *(Preregistered as `P3` below — I state the expectation before checking it.)*
2. 🔴 **MLC1 biology itself does not transfer.** MLC is a **macrocephalic** leukoencephalopathy with subcortical
   cysts; `lde/lde` is a severe dwarf whose brain is spared in absolute terms and **relatively about twice
   normal** (🔴 SIBLING-ATTESTED, `research/fulltext_dossiers/PMID17803050.md:116-118`). Different organ-level
   phenotype, different protein class, no shared pathway. **No MLC1/GlialCAM mechanism may be imported.**
3. 🔴 **No therapy transfers.** Neither field supplies an intervention to this one, and none is named here.
4. 🔴 **The toxic intramyelinic models transfer no aetiology** — a toxin-induced myelin split is not evidence
   that a genetic myelin lesion exists. They transfer the **assay only**.
