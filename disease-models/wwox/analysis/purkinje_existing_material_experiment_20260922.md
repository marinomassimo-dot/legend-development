## TRACE J-2 — fully prospective (registered **before** any search on this axis)

**OBSERVATION.** The only cell-locating channel in the existing archive that is **independent of
WWOX** is the Hoechst 33258 nuclear counterstain. Whether Purkinje somata can be found without a
Purkinje marker therefore reduces to: what can a DNA stain do, in a 14 µm section, on a widefield
whole-slide scanner?

**DIVERGE (7).**
**D1 size** — Purkinje nuclei are several-fold larger in cross-section than granule-cell nuclei;
a plain area threshold might suffice.
**D2 position** — the Purkinje cell layer is a *monolayer* at the granular/molecular interface: a
one-dimensional geometric constraint no other cerebellar cortical population satisfies.
**D3 chromatin texture** — Purkinje nuclei are euchromatic and pale with a prominent nucleolus;
granule nuclei are small and intensely heterochromatic. This feature runs **opposite** to D1, so the
two are not redundant.
**D4 the confound** — Bergmann glial somata sit in the same row; candelabrum cells too, and Lugaro
cells just beneath. Layer occupancy is not cell identity.
**D5 section thickness** — 14 µm is **less** than a Purkinje soma diameter, so somata are truncated by
the cut; a nucleus is mostly contained but a soma is not.
**D6 optics** — a panoramic scanner is **widefield**, not confocal (`confocal` = 0). Out-of-focus light
from the very dense granular layer will contaminate the granular-facing side of the PCL; the
molecular-facing side is sparse and therefore clean. The contamination is **anisotropic**.
**D7 the circular alternative** — one could segment somata in the WWOX channel itself, which is
cheaper and wrong.

**CONNECT.** D1 and D3 are anti-correlated features of the same object, so their conjunction is
strictly stronger than either; D2 converts a soft two-feature gate into a hard one by supplying a
spatial prior; D4 is what stops the conjunction from being an identification; D5 and D6 decide which
*statistic* may be computed once a mask exists; D7 is the failure mode that would silently delete the
very state the experiment exists to detect.

**HYPOTHESIS HJ-2.** Purkinje somata can be **located** on the existing scans with no new marker,
using the Hoechst channel alone, by {large nuclear area} ∧ {low chromatin density} ∧ {position in the
single row at the granular–molecular interface} — and in a `Wwox`-null host driven by a
neuron-restricted promoter, a **WWOX-positive** large soma in that row is a Purkinje cell **by
elimination**, so the identification problem disappears in one direction and only in one direction.

**PREDICTIONS (ex-ante, written before the first search).**
- **PJ1** Published morphometry gives Purkinje nuclear diameter ≥ ~2× granule-cell nuclear diameter (≥4× in area).
- **PJ2** Marker-free automated Purkinje-layer or Purkinje-soma segmentation already exists in the image-analysis literature.
- **PJ3** Bergmann glial nuclei are reported as smaller and more heterochromatic than Purkinje nuclei and interposed in the same row — a real but size-separable confound.
- **PJ4** No published marker-free identification claims calbindin-equivalent specificity.
- **PJ5** Widefield (non-confocal) per-cell fluorescence quantification in brain sections is documented as feasible, with stated caveats.

**DISCRIMINATOR.** Scholar Gateway passage retrieval on cerebellar nuclear morphometry, marker-free
Purkinje segmentation, and calbindin double-labelling chemistry.

## 2.3 🆕 The secondary-antibody audit — and why it generalises the Purkinje blindness to the whole archive

**FIRST-HAND, verbatim.** Primaries: *"rabbit polyclonal anti-WWOX 1:5,000, mouse anti-beta III
tubulin (B364854) … rabbit anti-GFAP-clone EPR1034Y (AB68428) … rabbit anti-Iba1-clone HL22 (ab28937)

⇒ The entire image archive has **exactly three channels**: one DNA channel, one *rabbit* channel at

1. 🔴 **All four rabbit primaries compete for a single channel.** A section carrying **WWOX** can carry

2. 🎯 **The only identity partners WWOX has ever had are NeuN and βIII-tubulin.** NeuN is
   granule-restricted in murine cerebellum; βIII-tubulin is pan-neuronal and discriminates nothing.
   ⇒ **The archive is marker-blind to Purkinje cells as a whole**, not merely in the ≈61% figure. The
   sibling's `MAB377` finding is not a property of one panel; it is a property of the study's entire
   immuno-chemistry.
3. 🟢 **Adding a Purkinje marker costs zero new secondaries** — provided it is raised in **mouse**, so
   it inherits the existing goat anti-mouse 488. § 4.2 attests two such clones.
4. 🔴 **A future EGFP reporter would collide with Alexa 488.** Any reporter design must move the mouse
   secondary to 568/594 — one new reagent, and it must be stated up front rather than discovered at
   the bench.

# 3 · Can morphology alone identify Purkinje somata on existing WWOX-stained scans?

## 🎯 Answer: **it can LOCATE them without any new marker, and it can IDENTIFY them in one direction only — and the direction it can identify is the one that refutes state A.**

## 3.1 What morphology gives, and from which channel

| Step | Channel | Feasible? |
|---|---|---|
| Segment the three cortical layers | **Hoechst** | 🟢 **Yes.** Granular layer = extreme nuclear density; molecular layer = sparse; PCL = the single row between them. Published label-free precedent for automated PCL detection |
| Propose Purkinje somata | **Hoechst** | 🟢 **Yes**, by the published triple criterion — *"specific location next to the granular layer … large size, low nuclear DNA density"* ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1474-9726.2012.00870.x)) |
| Declare a proposed soma to **be** a Purkinje cell | — | 🔴 **No, not from morphology alone.** Every retrieved study that names Purkinje cells in fluorescence adds a marker; Jurk *et al.* list calbindin as a **fourth, concurrent** criterion. The PCL also holds **Bergmann glia** and candelabrum cells, with Lugaro cells just beneath |
| Measure per-cell WWOX inside the mask | **647** | 🟢 Yes — with the two constraints in § 3.3 |

## 3.2 🎯 The asymmetry that makes this worth doing anyway — genotype does the antibody's job, in one direction

The treated animal is a **`Wwox`-null** host carrying a **neuron-restricted (`hSynI`)** transgene.
Therefore, inside the Purkinje cell layer:

- **Any WWOX signal at all is transgene-derived** — there is no endogenous WWOX to confuse it with.
- **A glial cell cannot carry it** — `hSynI` cannot drive in Bergmann glia by construction.
- Candelabrum and Lugaro cells are small, and fail the size and position gates.

> 🎯 **⇒ A WWOX-positive LARGE soma in the Purkinje cell layer of a treated `Wwox`-null mouse is a
> Purkinje cell by elimination.** Genotype plus promoter supply the specificity that the antibody
> would have supplied. **A positive reading refutes state A with no new reagent and no bench work.**
>
> 🔴 **A negative reading is ambiguous and is not evidence of absence.** A dark large soma in the PCL
> may be an untransduced Purkinje cell, a Bergmann glial cell, or a Purkinje soma whose body the 14 µm
> cut mostly missed. **That** is what calbindin is for, and it is why § 4 is still required.

## 3.3 🔴 Three traps that would corrupt the analysis, stated before anyone runs it

1. 🔴 **The circularity bar — the single most important constraint here.** Somata must be segmented in
   the **Hoechst** channel and **never** in the WWOX channel. Segmenting on WWOX defines cells by the
   signal under test and makes **state A invisible by construction**: a Purkinje cell that never
   expressed the transgene would simply not appear in the denominator, and the analysis would return
   "the Purkinje cells we can see all express WWOX" as a tautology. This applies to the authors'
   own re-analysis exactly as it applies to ours.
2. 🔴 **Use MEAN intensity inside the mask, never INTEGRATED intensity.** 14 µm is **thinner than a
   Purkinje soma**, so large cells are truncated by the section plane while small granule nuclei are
   fully contained. Integrated signal therefore penalises exactly the cell type of interest, and would
   manufacture a spurious Purkinje deficit. A guinea-pig Purkinje **nuclear** volume of 470.9 µm³
   (≈ 9.7 µm equivalent diameter, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/joa.13434)) is largely contained in a
   14 µm slab; the **soma** is not.
3. 🔴 **Widefield contamination is anisotropic.** `confocal` = 0: the panoramic scanner is widefield.
   Out-of-focus light from the extremely dense granular layer contaminates the **granular-facing** side
   of the PCL; the molecular-facing side is sparse and clean. ⇒ Report the molecular-facing half-mask,
   or report both halves separately — never a symmetric perisomatic annulus.

# 4 · THE ONE COMPRESSED EXPERIMENT

> ## 🥇 **On one archived sagittal section per animal — `WT`, untreated `KO`, and `KO + AAV9-hSynI-hWWOX` at LD and HD — run three channels: Hoechst 33258 · mouse anti-calbindin-D28k → goat anti-mouse Alexa 488 · the laboratory's existing rabbit anti-WWOX 1:5,000 → goat anti-rabbit Alexa 647. Segment on Hoechst. Report, per cerebellar layer, the per-cell WWOX MEAN-intensity DISTRIBUTION and the positive FRACTION as two separate quantities, plus the calbindin⁺ Purkinje COUNT per genotype and arm.**
>
> **Cost class:** 🟡 `NEW STAINING ON EXISTING SECTION` — **one new primary antibody**, no new secondary,
> no new animal, no new instrument, no new fluorophore. **`NO NEW ANIMALS: YES`.**

## 4.1 Why exactly these three channels, and no fourth

**The rule applied:** a channel earns its slot only if some possible result in it changes what we
would do next. Everything that failed that test is listed in § 4.3 and left out.

| Channel | What it answers — and nothing else | Why it cannot be dropped |
|---|---|---|
| **Hoechst 33258** (already in the protocol) | **Where**, and **how many**. Segments the three cortical layers; supplies the *WWOX-independent* soma mask and therefore the honest **denominator**; supplies the section-plane control that makes mean-intensity comparable across cells | Drop it and § 3.3 trap 1 fires: state A becomes unobservable |
| **Calbindin-D28k**, mouse, on Alexa 488 | **Which cell.** Converts *layer occupancy* into *cell identity*, separating Purkinje somata from Bergmann glia and candelabrum cells in the same row. Second, at zero marginal cost, it yields the **Purkinje count** in a treated animal — a quantity `NOT ASSAYED` by anyone | Drop it and every negative reading stays ambiguous (§ 3.2) |
| **WWOX**, rabbit, on Alexa 647 (already validated in this exact tissue) | **How much transgene product is in that cell.** In a null host this channel is simultaneously the transgene-expression reporter — it collapses *arrival × transcription × translation × stability* into one per-cell number, and that collapse is a limitation to state, not to hide | It is the measurement |

🎯 **The design's strongest property is that its control is inside the frame.** Granule cells and
Purkinje cells are compared **in the same field, in the same section, in the same animal, under the
same antibody lot, exposure and threshold**. That makes the Purkinje-versus-granule contrast immune to
every source of staining and imaging variability that normally dominates IF quantification — and it is
the reason a *relative* readout is sufficient even though the anti-WWOX antibody is unidentified and
the absolute scale is unavailable.

## 4.2 The reagent, attested rather than assumed

Two **mouse monoclonal** anti-calbindin-D28k clones are attested as Purkinje-specific in mouse
cerebellum, and both re-use the existing goat anti-mouse Alexa 488:

> *"Two different **mouse monoclonal** anti-calbindins were used: one from Sigma (anti-calbindin-D28K,
> **clone CB-955**, ascites fluid, IgG1 isotype …) and the other from Swant (**McAb 300**) … **Both
> antibodies yielded Purkinje cell-specific staining** identical to that reported often before"*
> ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/cne.20912)) — and that same Methods section pairs the mouse calbindin
> with a **rabbit** primary in double labelling, which is exactly the host pairing required here.

`McAb 300` specificity is further attested by **knockout-mouse absence of labelling**
([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/cne.22424)); Sigma `C9848` (RRID `AB_476894`) is attested on
**deparaffinised FFPE** cerebellum alongside **Hoechst 33258** ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/cne.24735)),
i.e. on the same material class as the 2026 FFPE archive; and Purkinje counting on **6 µm sagittal**
sections with a blinded operator is established practice ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.15252/emmm.201606965)),
so 14 µm is not thin by the standards of the task.

🔴 **Host discipline.** A *rabbit* anti-calbindin also exists and is widely used
([DOI](https://doi.org/10.15252/emmm.201606965) lists `AB1778`) — it would **collide with anti-WWOX** and
must not be ordered. The mouse host is not a preference; it is the constraint.

## 4.4 🆕 The one addition that *would* earn a fourth slot, and why it is a second pass

The transgene is **human** `WWOX` in a **mouse** `Wwox`-null host. That species difference is a
built-in, zero-background orthogonal label: an ISH probe against the human transcript (or against the
vector's own `WPRE`/`bGH` elements — both first-hand present in the construct, `WPRE` 29 tokens, `bGH`
used for titration) would report **vector arrival and transcription** on the *same archived section*,
with no endogenous signal to subtract and no new animal.

> 🎯 **This is the only thing that fully separates A from a sub-threshold B**, because it sees the
> vector where the protein channel sees nothing. 🔴 It is a different chemistry (ISH + IF on one
> section), so it is a **second pass**, not part of the compression — and it is named here so the
> first pass is designed not to consume the section that would carry it.

# 5 · What the experiment distinguishes among A / B / C

**A** vector never reaches Purkinje cells · **B** vector reaches them, WWOX expression weak ·
**C** WWOX expression present but functionally insufficient.

| Reading in **treated KO**, Purkinje vs granule **in the same field** | Purkinje calbindin⁺ **count** vs WT | State | What it means, and where the programme goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| WWOX in calbindin⁺ somata **indistinguishable from untreated-KO background**, while granule cells in the **same field** are bright | reduced | 🎯 **A** | Delivery/expression never reaches the ataxia-relevant cell. **Change capsid, promoter or route.** More dose of the same vector is the wrong investment |
| WWOX **detectable** in calbindin⁺ somata but the distribution sits **far below** granule cells in the same field **and below WT Purkinje** | reduced | 🎯 **B** | The cell is reached; output is the limiting step. **Promoter strength, WPRE, transcript stability** — and, per the held result, the ceiling already sits at transcript→protein |
| WWOX distribution in calbindin⁺ somata **at or above WT Purkinje** | **still reduced** | 🎯 **C** | Abundance is sufficient and the cell is lost anyway ⇒ **function-per-molecule, or timing**. This is the cerebellar test of the held position that severity tracks residual **function**, not abundance |
| WWOX **at or above WT Purkinje** | **preserved** | ⚪ **none of A/B/C** | Purkinje rescue is working and the cerebellar residual lives elsewhere — composition, or a non-Purkinje contributor |
| WWOX **near-zero** | **preserved** | ⚪ **none of A/B/C** | Purkinje cells do not need cell-autonomous WWOX at this timepoint ⇒ non-cell-autonomous (`C5`), and `hSynI` cannot reach the glia that would then matter |

## 5.1 🔴 What supplies each discrimination — stated so nothing is claimed that the design does not deliver

- **A vs (B ∪ C)** is delivered by the **untreated-KO arm**, which is the true zero. 🎯 And that arm
  does a second job for free: a `Wwox`-null animal is a **genetic negative control for the
  unidentified anti-WWOX polyclonal**, per cell type. This does **not** repair the antibody's missing
  absolute scale, but it repairs its **specificity**, which is the part that matters for a
  presence/absence verdict.
- **B vs C** is delivered **only by the WT arm**, because "weak" and "sufficient" are meaningless
  without an endogenous Purkinje reference. 🎯 **And measuring that reference is the experiment's most
  valuable by-product:** it is the **WT regional protein baseline that the held record says was never
  measured** — obtained here not merely regionally but at **cell-type resolution**, which no
  homogenate can ever give. ⚠️ It repairs the *protein* failure mode only. The **vDNA** *"fold of WT"*
  stays **undefined**, because wild-type animals carry no vector; nothing in this design changes that.
- 🔴 **What it does NOT separate.** **TRANSLATION from PROTEIN STABILITY** — a steady-state per-cell
  amount is their product, and one channel cannot factor a product. And **B from C is a boundary, not
  a gap**: "below WT" and "at WT" are the two sides of one continuum, so a distribution that straddles
  WT returns `AMBIGUOUS`, which must be reported as such rather than rounded to whichever side is
  convenient.
- 🔴 **`NOT ASSAYED` is neither `NORMAL` nor `ABSENT`.** A dark Purkinje soma is a *detection-floor*
  statement, and the floor's height is set by the segmentation threshold that the paper never states.

## 5.2 Why this outranks the alternatives on information gain

1. 🎯 **Its outcome is genuinely unpredicted.** A delivery-limited account predicts near-zero; the
   carried cell-size/post-mitotic account predicts near-cortical or higher. Both are live, and they
   make **opposite** predictions from the **same** section. Contrast the host-reference qPCR, whose
   verdict is already `HOST REFERENCE WOULD REFINE` with every term bounded below the effect needed to
   overturn — **a foreseeable result is a validation, not a discriminator.**
2. 🎯 **It repairs the inference the ≈61% cannot support.** The single observation arguing hardest
   against delivery-limitation is measured on a population that by antigen cannot contain a Purkinje
   cell — and § 2.3 shows that blindness is a property of the *whole archive*, not of one panel.
3. 🎯 **Its co-product is a first.** No WWOX measurement in a Purkinje cell exists in **any** species or
   system. The WT arm alone closes that census gap.
4. 🟢 **Every arm it needs is already listed as perfused and sectioned** — *"WT, KO, and KO injected
   mice"* — and the FFPE blocks are re-cuttable without limit.
