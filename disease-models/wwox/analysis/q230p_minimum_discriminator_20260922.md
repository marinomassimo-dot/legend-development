# §1 · COMPONENT-BY-COMPONENT NECESSITY

**The rule, applied mechanically and identically to all seven:** a component stays only if there is
at least one cell of the §2 matrix that **no cheaper component can fill**. A component that owns
zero cells is a cost, not a control. Cells are of two kinds and both count: a **branch cell** (it
separates two of `H1`–`H4`) and a **validity cell** (it decides whether a branch cell may be read at
all). A component that owns only validity cells is kept as a **control**, and is labelled as a
control rather than promoted to a discriminator.

### 1.1 Soluble fraction `S` — 🟢 **KEEP** (branch + denominator)

**Owns:** with `P`, the pair `H3` vs `{H1, H2}` — row `R1`. It is also the **denominator** of the
partition `P/(S+P)`, and the **only lane comparable to all twelve published rows**, which is what
makes this a re-run of a published negative rather than a first measurement.

**Could it be cut and computed as `T − P`?** No. That computes a small difference between two large
numbers, propagates both errors into the quantity of interest, and severs comparability with the
prior. **Cost of keeping: one lane.**

### 1.2 Insoluble pellet `P` — 🟢 **KEEP** (the highest-value single component)

**Owns outright:** `H3`, in rows `R1`, `R4` and `R8`. Nothing else in the set can see an aggregated
species at all.

🔴 **Without `P`, this experiment is Johannsen 2018 with better documentation.** The census's
verified zero — `pellet` 0, `fraction` 0, `insoluble` 0 across every served body, 0/12 rows, 0/4
patient-derived rows — means that cutting `P` reproduces the exact structural gap the file exists to
close. **Specification:** wash the pellet once in the step-1 buffer, then resolubilise in
**2% SDS / 8 M urea / 50 mM Tris + DTT, 95 °C, 10 min, with sonication to shear DNA**.
🔴 **RIPA does not substitute** (0.1% SDS + 0.5% deoxycholate does not dissolve detergent-resistant
species), and in any case the clearing spin — *"collected by centrifugation at 13 300 × rpm"* in the
one paper with the deepest turnover work — is identical in both buffer classes. **Load at equal
cell-equivalents, never equal protein:** the pellet's protein composition is not the lysate's, and
equal-protein loading of a pellet is the single most common way this experiment is run wrongly.

### 1.3 Total strong-denaturing lysate `T` — 🟢 **KEEP**, as a **control**, and the brief's question answered explicitly

> **The brief asked whether `T` is arithmetically redundant with `S + P`. It is not — but my reason
> for saying so is weaker after `P2` than it was before, and §3.3 withdraws the originality claim
> that went with it.**

**Owns one validity cell outright — `R6`, and `R6` protects `R2`.** `R2` (nothing anywhere) is the
row that assigns `H1`, and it is a **double negative**. A double negative from a fractionation is
the observation most vulnerable to a handling artefact: the pellet wash discards a second time
(*"washed once … and centrifuged again"*, [DOI 10.1111/jnc.15591](https://doi.org/10.1111/jnc.15591)),
resolubilisation may be incomplete, and a protein trapped in unsheared DNA never enters the gel.

🔴 **`T` is the only lane in the entire design that involves no spin.** The census's core finding is
that this field's error is a discard step. A design that answers a discard problem using only two
more spin-derived lanes has doubled the discard. `T` removes it, at the cost of one paired well and
one lane.

**What the search changed, honestly.** I predicted (`P2`) that the literature quantifies `S + P ≠ T`.
**It does not, as far as I could reach.** What it does is run this architecture as standard: total
taken **before any spin** and **immediately** mixed with SDS loading dye, beside soluble and
insoluble ([DOI 10.1002/cpch.58](https://doi.org/10.1002/cpch.58)). ⇒ `T` stays because it is
**established practice plus a sound logical role**, not because I proved a loss. 🔵 **And the
arithmetic becomes a readout in its own right:** if `S + P ≈ T` in the **wild-type** lane, the
fractionation is quantitative in these hands and a `Q230P` double blank is a real absence; if
`S + P < T` in the wild-type lane, **no double blank in this experiment may be read at all.**

**Cheaper substitutes considered and rejected:** a recombinant-WWOX recovery spike (no purified
folded WWOX SDR exists); a housekeeping recovery control (housekeepers partition differently between
`S` and `P` — that is the premise being tested); total-protein stain summing across lanes (checks
bulk recovery, not WWOX recovery). **None is cheaper than one lane.**

# §4 · FUNCTION FOLLOWS ABUNDANCE

> ## 🔴 **`abundance rescue ≠ functional rescue`.** Solubility and stability are **NOT** surrogates for function. This phrase is preserved with its full force, and §4.1 is the reason it is not a slogan.

### 4.1 Two published precedents, same intervention class, opposite outcomes

| precedent | abundance | function | reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NPC1**, patient fibroblasts, 18 missense lines | proteasome inhibitors *"**partially recovered**"* mutant NPC1 in most lines | 🟢 *"these mutant proteins **partially recovered their activity** and substantially reduced free cholesterol levels"* — [DOI 10.1111/febs.12954](https://doi.org/10.1111/febs.12954) | Abundance rescue **did** give function — because the lesion was stability/trafficking on a protein with an intact functional core **and a validated functional readout** |
| **Ubiquitin-activating enzyme E1**, `TS20` | *"Inhibition of proteasome activity **increased the protein levels** of E1 mutants while inhibition of lysosomal protease activity showed no effect"* | 🔴 Figure legend, verbatim: *"**Stabilizing E1 mutant is not sufficient to rescue the temperature-sensitive phenotype.**"* — Lao, Chen & Sang 2011, *J Cell Physiol* 227(4):1561–1568, [DOI 10.1002/jcp.22870](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcp.22870) | 🔴 **Abundance restored. Function not restored.** A destabilising missense, proteasomal clearance, MG-132 rescue of level — and the cells still failed |

🔴 **Same mechanism class, same intervention, opposite functional outcomes.** That pair — not an
aphorism — is why function must be **measured** after abundance and can never be **assumed** from it.
It sits beside the two priors the repository already holds: **Lou 2018**, where all seven engineered
SDR mutants lost activity (the best retaining 28.7%) — a **methodological constraint**, *not*
evidence that `Q230P` is unrescuable — and **Atanasov 2007**, where 11β-HSD2 `Y338H` *"was
**partially retained**"* in activity at 26 °C and under glycerol, the only human-SDR
functional-rescue precedent in existence, whose lesion sits in a **peripheral C-terminal stability
element** with catalysis intact, where `Q230` is a **buried core proline**. **Different lesion
classes; only the glycerol and temperature arms transfer, and the abundance-under-rescue half of
that precedent is `abstract-depth` and unverified.**

### 4.2 Trigger, and the row where the question is empty rather than deferred

**Function is asked only where a species exists or can be made to exist: rows `R1`, `R3`, `R4`, `R5`,
`R8`.**

### 4.3 🔴 The `POLE4` + `GSK3β` engagement reasoning — re-evaluated, not inherited

1. *`P282A` loses `POLE4` binding.* The companion result (`P252A`) sits in **Figure S9 of the

2. *`P282A` is in the SDR.* 🔴 **The paper never names the domain** — `SDR`, `short-chain`,
   `dehydrogenase` all return **0** in the body. *"SDR-span"* is **the reader's arithmetic**, and the
   authors write that *"the precise molecular mechanism underlying the loss-of-function of the WWOX
   `P282A` variant is still unknown."*
3. *Therefore `POLE4` reports SDR fold integrity.* 🔴 **This does not follow even if (2) held.** A
   residue lying inside a domain does not make its binding partner a reporter of that domain's fold.
   `POLE4`'s binding site on WWOX is **unmapped** — no fragment mapping, no deletion series, no WW
   control. `PREMISE: NOBODY_LOOKED`.
4. *Therefore it will report `Q230P`.* 🔴 **And the anchor of the whole chain is `P282A`**, which is

   to let it anchor a reporter's credential**, which is the same error one inference removed. What
   the chain actually demonstrates is the loss of a binding event by **a benign polymorphism on a CMV

🔴 **Plus the disqualifier for this experiment specifically: every WWOX–`POLE4` measurement in
existence was made after UV** (`without UV` / `no UV` / `untreated` / `mock` = **0** in the body).

to avoid stress-induced artefacts — and UV perturbs the same clearance axes the inhibitor arms
manipulate.
⇒ **`POLE4` survives only as a hypothesis-generating channel inside the transgene plate, where its
