# `TX-007` — the window and the ceiling: what gene addition can reach, and how it depends on age

**Scientist A · 2026-09-21 · READ-ONLY toward every canonical file and every ledger. No `BATCH_COMMIT`, no commit candidate, no receipt, no canonical edit. `TX-001`–`TX-007` are NOT re-ranked here. No trial is proposed.**

🔴 **Nothing in this file is medical advice or a treatment recommendation.** It is an evidence assessment of a **preclinical ceiling**, reasoning about a WWOX-DEE genotype class assembled from published literature — never about a person. Therapeutic output supports discussion with a treating clinical team; it never substitutes for one.

**Why now.** Today's allele-level census ([`woree_therapeutic_class_census_20260921.md`](woree_therapeutic_class_census_20260921.md)) established that **56.8 % of classifiable individuals are null/null** and **71.6 % of alleles are predicted null**. For that majority no allele-specific lever is conceivable on either allele, so the only lever is allele-agnostic gene addition — `TX-007`. This file asks what that lever's ceiling actually is, and what the `TX-007` window caveat rests on.

---

## 1 · VERDICT

**For the null/null majority the realistic ceiling of gene addition is measured only in one place — a mouse dosed within five days of birth — and there it is high: near-wild-type survival to 300 days, spike-wave discharge rate returned to a wild-type-indistinguishable level, growth, glycaemia and fertility restored, and myelination improved but never quantified against wild type in any treated animal.** The **age-dependence of that ceiling is NOT measured**: every WWOX gene-therapy experiment LEGEND holds treats a mouse at **P0–P5** or an organoid, the single age-varying experiment spans **four days of neonatal life at one dose**, and the authors state explicitly that later dosing was not attempted because the model dies — *"rather than a definitive boundary for therapeutic responsiveness"* (`L03`). **One half of the `TX-007` caveat is a finding and the other half is an inference**: that a neuron-restricted vector leaves the radial-glia compartment transcriptionally and cell-cycle-wise untouched **is measured once**, in human organoids (`L11`), whereas *"the older the patient, the smaller the reversible fraction"* (`L33`) has **no measurement behind it in any species** — no migration or cortical-layering endpoint has ever been read out in a gene-therapy-treated animal. The honest statement of the ceiling is therefore **a per-endpoint profile of when each defect ARISES, not of when treatment stops working**; the second curve does not exist, and "the defect is prenatal" has not been joined to the irreversibility argument that would be needed to turn it into "gene addition cannot help it".

### 2.1 🔴 What the table establishes, stated plainly

1. **Every published WWOX gene-therapy experiment in a living animal treats a neonate.** The entire age axis of the evidence is **P0 → P5 in the mouse** — five days.
2. **So the age-dependence of the ceiling has never been measured in a living animal, and the `TX-007` caveat's second clause is an INFERENCE, not a finding.** The repository's own phrasing — *"The older the patient, the smaller the reversible fraction"* (`L33`) — is a **slope on an axis with at most four sampled points, all inside the first week of life, none past P5, and none with a long-term readout except P1 and P5.**
3. **The reason for the ceiling is declared, and it is the model, not the therapy.** *"Therapeutic rescue beyond this early postnatal window was not explored, as Wwox-null mice rapidly deteriorate with progressive neurological dysfunction and early lethality"* (`L02`), and the authors refuse to read their own limit as biology: *"the inability to assess later intervention likely reflects a combination of model-specific biological constraints and technical limitations, rather than a definitive boundary for therapeutic responsiveness"* (`L03`), adding that *"human patients … may exhibit different developmental trajectories, disease kinetics, and therapeutic responsiveness, potentially allowing for later intervention"* (`L04`). **This is the epistemically correct form of a negative and it must not be compressed into "the window is P0–P5".**
4. **Cross-species age mapping is mine, and labelled.** A mouse P0–P5 is not a human neonate. `DEFAULT` — the widely used developmental-neuroscience convention that **rodent ≈ P7 corresponds to human term** places the whole treated range **at or before human birth**. 🔴 **LEGEND holds no receipt for any cross-species staging source**, so this is cited as a **field convention, not as a fact**, and it is used only to say "perinatal", never to name a human age.

### 2.2 ⚠️ One inconsistency inside LEGEND's own therapeutics layer, recorded read-only

[`mechanism_intervention_map.md`](mechanism_intervention_map.md) describes the `TX-007` intervention as *"AAV9, human Synapsin-I promoter, WWOX cDNA, **WPRE element**; ICV"*. **The 2026 lead paper's clinical-track vector removed WPRE deliberately** (`L20`) and recovered efficacy by raising dose ~6-fold. ## 3 · DOSE–RESPONSE vs AGE–RESPONSE — two different curves

### 3.1 The dose–response EXISTS, is four points wide, and is a **threshold**, not a gradient

| Dose (vg, ICV, hSynI, WPRE-free unless noted) | Survival outcome |
|---|---|
| 4 × 10¹⁰ · 8 × 10¹⁰ | no benefit — death at ~17–25 days (panel reading; the running text disputes the 8 × 10¹⁰ arm and the dossier logs that unresolved) |
| **LD 1.23 × 10¹¹** | death by **~90 days — the curve reaches zero**. `p < 0.0001` vs untreated |
| **HD 2.63 × 10¹¹** | **~80 % plateau to 300 days**; **`p = 0.78` vs wild type**; `p < 0.0001` vs LD |

🔴 **The low dose does not rescue survival; it converts a rapidly lethal phenotype into a slowly lethal one.** `CLAIM 011` is `flagged for review` for exactly this: *"dose-dependent"* describes a continuum where the panel shows a threshold. The confirming prose is unambiguous — *"LD-treated mice did not survive to P90"* (`L17`). **Any inference of the form "a lower, safer dose would still help" is refused for survival in this model.** The corresponding `REVIVAL_TRIGGER` already standing in the repository — an intermediate arm between 1.23 and 2.63 × 10¹¹ vg — is the experiment that would locate the threshold.

**A second, unresolved feature of the dose axis:** across four brain regions, **seven of eight** vector-genome and mRNA comparisons between LD and HD are `ns`. Two doses that differ 2.1-fold in what is injected are statistically indistinguishable in what arrives and what is transcribed, yet produce opposite survival. The paper's own candidate explanation — non-survivors in both groups had lower WWOX — rests on a **survivor-versus-non-survivor** post-hoc comparison in unreachable supplementary panels. **So any dose statement from this paper rests on the survival curve, not on a measured expression difference.**

### 3.2 The age–response BARELY exists, and where it exists it is flat

**One experiment varies age at (nominally) constant dose: Supplementary Figure S8 — P1, P2, P3, P5.** Its own audited reading is the correct statement: *"efficacy is demonstrated at several early postnatal dosing times, including P5, but S8 does not map a complete P0–P5 window or establish uniform/full rescue across that interval"* (`L40`).

| What S8 does | What S8 does not |
|---|---|
| Survival to **P40** at P1, P2, P3, P5 | 🔴 **No P0 arm** (`L35`); **no P4 arm at all** |
| Survival to **P300** at **P1 and P5 only** | No long-term survival for P2, P3, P4 |
| P14 weight and glucose across P1–P5, `ns` vs WT | 🔴 **No treated-vs-KO comparison drawn anywhere**; `ns` vs WT is absence of detected difference, **not equivalence** |
| Neuronal WWOX expression and GFAP quantification at **P5** | Panels E–I examine **P5 only**; MBP is representative imagery and **not quantified** |

**So the age–response curve, such as it is, is: four points, four days, one dose, one model, one laboratory, and it is flat across that span.** There is **no data point at any age later than P5, in any species, at any dose.** 🔴 **"Nobody has varied age at treatment beyond the first week of life" is the finding of this file.**

### 3.3 🔴 The two curves are not interchangeable and the repository has been conflating them

`TX-007`'s caveat asserts a **monotone decline along the age axis**. The only monotone relationship actually measured in this literature is along the **dose** axis, and even there it is a step, not a slope. **A threshold on dose says nothing about a slope on age.** The one genomic-rescue data point that bears on the age axis (GT-7, correction present from before differentiation, the earliest possible intervention) does **not** deliver clean normalisation of cortical lamination — two markers normalised, one overshot (`L34`) — which is evidence that **"earlier is uniformly better" is itself not established** for patterning endpoints.

### 4.1 The window's biological basis cannot be built from WWOX's own expression

🔴 **A treatment-window argument built on a WWOX expression peak has no anchor in anything LEGEND can reach.** The only *measured* CNS developmental time course is rat PND 5→21, and it shows Wwox protein **rising**, not falling: *"the expression of Wwox protein increased with age in whole brains and cerebral cortices"* (`L16`). The single developmental primary that staged mouse CNS expression has **no PMCID and was never read**; four secondary restatements of it **disagree with one another** about whether early-embryonic WWOX is low or high. **No reachable source reports an embryonic day for WWOX expression at all.**

### 4.2 🔴 So do the different phenotypes have different windows? — Yes for ONSET, unknown for CLOSURE

**The onset windows are genuinely different and the difference is therapeutically load-bearing:** a migration defect that is complete before birth and a myelin defect that is still unfolding at PND 5–21 in rodents and over years in humans **cannot have the same deadline**. That much the evidence supports, and it means **the ceiling is not one number but a profile.**

**But the closure windows are unmeasured for every single endpoint**, and the repository must not let the onset profile silently substitute for a closure profile. Two guards, both of which this corpus has previously needed:

- 🔴 **"Not measured" is not "not rescued."** No migration or lamination endpoint has ever been read out in a treated animal. The statement "gene therapy does not fix the migration defect" is currently a **prediction from the timing of the defect**, not a measurement.
- 🔴 **"The defect is prenatal" is not "gene therapy cannot help it."** That step needs the prenatality **plus an irreversibility argument**, and irreversibility is itself a claim requiring evidence. **Nobody has made it.** The one relevant observation points the other way: the rat's cortical wall is **not thinner** and its total neuron number is normal — *"**Wwox is not required for proliferation and migration of immature neurons**"* was the same group's earlier, instrument-limited conclusion (`L24`), later refined to *placement*, not *number*. **A mislaminated cortex containing the right number of neurons is a different therapeutic object from a cortex missing them.**

## 5 · WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE 56.8 %

**Stated as a profile where the evidence supports one, and as `UNMEASURED` where it does not.**

| For the null/null majority | The ceiling as the evidence allows it to be stated |
|---|---|
| **Survival** | In the mouse, at ≥2.63 × 10¹¹ vg, within 5 days of birth: **wild-type-indistinguishable (`p = 0.78`) to 300 days.** Below threshold: **no survival rescue at all.** At human ages: **UNMEASURED** |
| **Seizures / SWD** | Mouse, same conditions: **returned to wild-type rate (`ns`)**, treated at developmental onset. At human ages: **UNMEASURED** — and this is the endpoint most likely to remain addressable late, on a structural argument nobody has tested |
| **Growth, glycaemia, fertility** | Mouse: restored by neuron-only restoration, despite zero peripheral expression. Human relevance: **UNMEASURED** |
| **Myelination** | Mouse: improved; 🔴 **never quantified in a treated animal against wild type.** Where the only WT-vs-treated myelin comparison was drawn (2021 EM), it was **significant AGAINST the rescue** — ~26 unmyelinated axons/field in WT vs ~52 in treated, `**`. Human, longer window: **UNMEASURED** |
| **Cortical layering / migration** | 🔴 **UNMEASURED in every treated animal.** The defect is prenatal in rat and human fetus; whether it is reversible, partially reversible or fixed is **unknown**. The nearest data point — an AAVS1 genomic correction present from before differentiation — normalises two of three layer markers and **overshoots the third** |
| **Progenitor / radial glia** | 🔴 **Measured NOT corrected by a neuron-restricted vector, once, in organoids.** This bounds the **coverage** of the current construct; it does not establish that the compartment is beyond reach of a different one |
| **Overall, for a patient older than the treated range** | The repository's existing formulation — *aim at functional protection and recovery, not reversal of developmental damage; "reduce damage / buy time", not a reset* — is **a reasonable prior and is not supported by an age-varying measurement.** It should be carried as `INFERENZA`, not as `DATO` |

⚠️ **And the only human data on symptomatic levers in this class is a single negative.** In one published biallelic-null WOREE case with first-day-of-life onset, four levers were tried and all four failed, including *"The ketogenic diet did not significantly improve seizures and was suspended."* (`L21`). **`n = 1`, an atypically early onset, and not a refutation of anything** — but it is the only test, and it is why a causal lever matters for this class rather than a symptomatic one.

## 6 · THE HUMAN ANCHOR — and why an enrolment window is not a biological finding

| Item | What it is | What it licenses |
|---|---|---|
| The first-in-human WWOX gene therapy | **News-level only.** An eight-month-old, compassionate-use `n`-of-1, **one month** of reported follow-up, no peer-reviewed case report; today's monitor found none at ≥3 months either | ✅ that the route is operationally possible. ❌ **nothing about efficacy, and nothing about age-dependence.** One month is not a developmental follow-up interval |
| The sponsor's sibling programme, ICV AAV9, **enrolling ages 2–25** | A registered Phase 1/2 in **a different gene and a different disease** | 🔴 **"A sponsor's enrolment window is a commercial, regulatory and recruitment decision, not a biological finding."** (`L39`) Designing a study for ages 2–25 is evidence that the sponsor is **willing to test** benefit at those ages, not that benefit is obtainable there |

### 6.1 What would turn an enrolment window into evidence

Two things, neither of which exists, and both of which the repository already names elsewhere:

1. **Clinically** — an **age-at-dosing-stratified outcome readout** with a prespecified developmental endpoint and enough power to detect an age × outcome interaction. An enrolment range produces that only if the analysis is designed to ask the question; a range alone never answers it.
2. **Preclinically** — the experiment the evidence itself names: **delayed, post-onset dosing in a model that lives long enough to be dosed late.** 🔴 This is the structural point: the `Wwox`-null mouse **cannot** answer the age question, because it dies at 3–4 weeks — the authors say so (`L02`). Asking the age question requires a **non-null / hypomorphic** model, and the authors point the same way from the delivery side: *"future studies should explore alternative strategies to extend the therapeutic window, including earlier prenatal delivery, less invasive or systemic administration routes"* (`L05`). **This is the description of an experiment the sources themselves call for, not a trial proposal, and nothing here is a recommendation for any person.**
