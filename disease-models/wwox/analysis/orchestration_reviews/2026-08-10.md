## 3. Proposals

**First: freeze the manifest contract for the duration of a reading round.**
Contract changes to `deepdive_manifest.py` land only at a round boundary — after the round's branches
are merged, never while they are open. A manifest records the validator commit it was written against;
a new required field applies to manifests authored after it, and older ones are `unknown_legacy` until
their next touch.
*Why first:* the intervention hierarchy puts *eliminate unnecessary work* above *add validation*, and
this is the only proposal that removes work rather than adding it. It cost ~1000 re-authored lines today
and is currently blocking 21 locator entries on three branches.
*Falsifiable success criterion:* in the next round, **zero** commits whose message or diff is a
manifest realignment to a field introduced mid-round; strict-validation blocks attributable to
post-authoring fields = 0.
*Trade-off:* a defect found at 16:00 in the contract waits until the round closes. Correctness
improvements are delayed by up to one round, and the round must therefore be short.

**Second: `coverage.supplementary: read` must be backed by an artifact.** At least one fingerprinted
`supplement_text`/supplement artifact **and** either one `surface: supplement` locator or an explicit
named waiver; otherwise the slot may only be `not_read` / `not_present` / `unavailable`. Enforced where
`require_work_manifest` already enforces the body.
*Falsifiable criterion:* re-running today's 7 readings under the rule, 7 of 7 either produce a
supplement locator/waiver or downgrade the slot honestly; corpus `surface: supplement` count rises
above 1.
*Trade-off:* readings get slower, and some "supplementary: read" claims will be revealed as
optimistic and must be re-opened — which is the point, and which is retroactive debt.

**Third: one paper, one actor, per round** — a written assignment before dispatch, checked against
open branches. *Criterion:* zero files modified on two branches in one round. *Trade-off:* the
Orchestrator serialises what it might have parallelised, and loses the accidental second-reader effect
that produced `385dd28` ("A second harness reads PMID 30755385, and finds a hole three reviews had
missed"). If second readings are wanted, they should be *assigned* as second readings, not collided into.

### Controlled comparison for the one thing I cannot explain

B finds contradictions at 29.3% per figure locator against Codex's 18.2% and A's 17.2%. I cannot tell
whether that is B's method or B's papers.
*Hypothesis:* contradiction yield per panel is a property of the paper, not the reader.
*Controlled variable:* the reader. Same paper (one of B's, e.g. PMID 38499540, plus one of Codex's,
e.g. PMID 36572673), read independently by Codex and by B, both at a **declared** figure budget of
one locator per main figure.
*Metric:* `text_contradicted_by_panel` per figure locator, and agreement on *which* panels contradict.
*Threshold:* if the two readers land within 5 percentage points on both papers, the yield is the
paper's and O1's dial can be set globally. If B stays >10 points above on both, it is B's method, and
the method — not the throughput — is what should be transferred.
