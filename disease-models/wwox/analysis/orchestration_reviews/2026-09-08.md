## 4 · One declared deviation, and why

§21e gives each actor its own worktree and has the author land its own branch. The three scientists in this
deployment are agent sessions **inside one checkout**, which changes two things and nothing else:

- **Commits are serialised** through an `flock`-guarded, path-scoped wrapper. Concurrent `git commit` in one
  checkout races on `.git/index.lock`; path-scoping means no actor can sweep up a peer's in-flight files.
- **The receipt ledger is not forked.** Three actors appending to one ledger under the writer's own exclusive
  lock is the case the writer was built for. It is the *branch* fork that produces the `rechain` problem of
  2026-08-10, and this deployment does not create one.

Nothing scientific is relaxed: receipts, verbatim locators, strict manifest validation, the locator audit on a
baseline reversal and BLOCK-1 all bind exactly as written.
