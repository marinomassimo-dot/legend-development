### 6.2 · Two receipt-contract questions, referred and unresolved

Both concern what `fulltext_read_receipt.md` permits. They arrived independently from two actors on
the same morning, which is itself the signal.

- **Coverage of cited-but-undistributed material** (`scientist-a`). Under the contract's own wording
  — a `complete_fulltext_read` may not contain `not_read`, and unavailable material is merely

- **No lawful route to correct a wrong `study_id`** (`scientist-c`). `receipt_correction` must

  incorrect one was persisted first.** The proposed and deliberately unimplemented remedy is a
  `record_kind: identity_correction` that changes `study_id` only, freezes every reading field, and
  links through `prior_receipt`. The schema is reserved, so it was referred, not written. The
  standing default meanwhile: omit the field, carry the true value in `evidence_basis` and the
  dossier, and never write a known-false value to satisfy a checker.
