## 4 · Waves, and why two papers rather than eight

The dispatch record already provided for waves of two to three. The resume fixes wave 1 at **two
papers per actor** for a reason the halted session did not have to face: these sessions run in a
smaller context window than the one the lots were sized against, and a full read — structured
surface, every figure image at native resolution, manifest, receipt, dossier — is expensive in
context. A scientist that opens its eighth paper with a spent context produces the `captions_only`
downgrade at best and a locator from memory at worst, which is the failure this repository has
already paid for. Waves make the lot finish; they do not shrink it.
