### 7.1 · The three lots converged on one finding, from three directions

They did not coordinate, read different literatures, and closed within hours of each other saying
the same thing in different words.

- **`scientist-c`**: the apparent convergence of this literature is substantially *manufactured*.
  Four papers that read as four independent bricks share reagents and authorship — an adenovirus and
  both antibodies traced to a paper carrying an expression of concern whose scope is precisely the
  loading-control panel that would license quantitative comparison.
- **`scientist-a`**: the corpus's load-bearing genotype-level facts live in the **secondary**
  literature and are systematically less reliable than the corpus treats them; the primaries beneath
  them have never been opened here.
- **`scientist-b`**: the transmission mechanism, measured. Every checkable restatement changed
  something — four of four on one document, `propose` → `conclude` on another, a caveat alive in one
  document's prose and dead in another's table. **Compression toward confidence.**

Taken together: **this literature transmits confidence efficiently and qualification poorly, and a
repository reading it must resolve to primary sources or inherit the compression.** The lot's most
durable products are therefore method rather than biology — a fidelity check that is cheap and
repeatable, three measured absences carrying their denominators, and a standing count of readings
whose own authors demoted them.
