# Evidence matrix — the WWOX SDR missense proteostasis line: what is known, what is missing, and the one experiment per question

**Scope.** Synthesis over evidence already held in this repository. No new retrieval, no literature census, no therapy proposed. Two full-text artefacts, two audits, three discovery-ledger entries, two therapeutic hypotheses and one therapeutic strategy were read; nothing was fetched.

**The distinction held throughout.** 🔴 **"More protein" is not "more functional WWOX."** A rescue that restores abundance and never measures function has not shown recovery. Every row below says where that applies.

---

## VERDICT

**The proteostasis line can be taken exactly this far and no further: one WWOX SDR-span missense protein (P252A) is demonstrably lost post-transcriptionally by a route that is lysosome-dependent, proteasome-independent and macroautophagy-independent, and that route is blockable in culture at the level of western-blot band intensity — but the route has been *named* chaperone-mediated autophagy rather than *shown* to be, no functional readout has ever been run on a WWOX missense protein whose abundance was restored, and the same paper contains a second SDR-span substitution (P282A) that loses function with no stability defect at all, which is a standing demonstration that abundance and function are separable in this domain.**

## The strongest current evidence

The strongest thing held today is **the route exclusion in PMID 41124647, not the route assignment**. Three arms were run and they agree: MG‑132 produced no accumulation, two chemically distinct lysosomotropic agents (chloroquine and ammonium chloride) each restored the mutant protein level, and the autophagosome inhibitor 3‑MA did nothing. Upstream of them sits an mRNA control that makes the loss post-transcriptional, and downstream a cycloheximide chase that makes it a turnover phenomenon. That combination — a transcriptional control, a chase, one negative proteolytic arm and two positive orthogonal ones — is a respectable standard, and it establishes a real and previously undocumented failure mode for a WWOX SDR-span missense protein: **present in transcript, absent in protein, lost through a lysosomal route that is neither the proteasome nor macroautophagy.**

It is also genuinely independent evidence. The audit records that the group is CAS Hefei / USTC with no NCKU and no Hebrew University affiliation, which matters in a field where most WWOX mechanism derives from a small number of laboratories.

Second-strongest, and the reason PMID 27869163 is in this matrix at all, is its **domain-resolved point-mutant dissociation**: WW1 (W44F/P47A) abolishes WWOX-dependent radiosensitisation while the SDR catalytic-site substitution Y293F retains it. That is a real constraint on readout choice — it proves that at least one WWOX function is indifferent to SDR catalysis, and therefore that a WW1-dependent assay cannot be used to score an SDR allele. It proves nothing about how a disease SDR missense protein behaves, because Y293F was designed to leave the fold intact and no protein level was measured.

## The single biggest missing link

🔴 **No functional measurement has ever been made on a WWOX missense protein whose abundance was restored.**

Everything in the proteostasis line is an abundance measurement: a band that goes down, a chase that shows it going down faster, two drugs that bring it back up. Under the rescue, PMID 41124647 measured **band intensity and nothing else** — no clonogenicity, no invasion, no comet, no γH2AX, no NHEJ reporter. The word `rescu*` does not appear in the paper.

The gap is not hypothetical, because the same paper contains its own counter-example. P282A is an SDR-span substitution that is **indistinguishable from empty vector in every functional assay while having no stability defect at all**. So within one paper, on one protein, in one domain: abundance can fall without function being the reason, and function can fail without abundance moving. `HYP-20260709-08` had already flagged "stabile-ma-inerte" as its central untested assumption; P282A converts that from a worry into a demonstrated phenotype for the SDR span.

Which means the load-bearing inference of the whole therapeutic line — that if the missense protein can be kept from being degraded, there will be more functional WWOX — is **the one step for which there is no data at any point in the chain**. It is not contradicted. It is unmeasured. And a matrix that reported the chloroquine result as a proteostatic rescue, without this sentence beside it, would be reporting an abundance experiment as a functional one.

## The best next experiment

**One experiment: an abundance-clamped, domain-resolved measurement of WWOX function per molecule.**

*Design.* In a single cell system expressing WWOX from a titratable promoter, produce a dilution series of **wild-type** protein and a dilution series of the **missense** protein, and quantify both by western against a common standard. From those series, select the wild-type and mutant conditions whose steady-state WWOX levels **match**. At those matched levels — and only there — run the domain-resolved readout panel from row 5: co-immunoprecipitation against an SDR-domain partner (tau or GSK3β, with POLE4 as a second), co-immunoprecipitation against a WW1-dependent partner (BRCA1 or an ERBB4/p73 PPxY partner) as the control that should remain normal for an SDR lesion, and subcellular localisation by fractionation and immunofluorescence. Report every output **normalised to the WWOX input in the same lysate**. Run the lysosomal-block condition (chloroquine 40 µM / 24 h, the published in-culture condition) as an orthogonal, second way of arriving at a matched level, with MG‑132 as the expected negative — but do not let it be the only route to level-matching, because a lysosomotropic agent perturbs lysosome-wide biology and would confound a localisation readout.

*What it would settle.* Whether a WWOX SDR missense protein, present at wild-type abundance, does wild-type work — i.e. whether "more protein" is "more functional WWOX" for that allele. It would settle it in the discriminating direction, because the WW1 control tells you whether a null result is a real SDR defect or a globally dead protein. It would also, as a by-product, put a number on the residual function that `DL-MECH-029` and `HYP-20260709-08` both name as unmeasured, and it would tell `HYP-20260709-02` the sign of its effect: if function per molecule is near normal, boosting expression has a direction; if it is near zero, boosting expression adds protein and no function.

*What it would not settle.* The identity of the degradation route — CMA versus another lysosomal pathway — which needs the LAMP2A and motif-ablation arms in row 3. Whether a druggable, non-lysosomotropic stabiliser exists, which is a separate screening question and which `TX-003` correctly holds as `conditional / not design-ready`. Anything about neurons, myelination, developmental window, seizures or clinical course: this is a cell-biology measurement, and no cancer-cell or fibroblast assay can predict the neurological severity of an allele — the 35-year-old proband of PMID 41124647, whose mutants were dead in every oncology assay and who *"did not suffer from WWOX‐related nervous system disease"*, is the standing proof of that. And it would settle nothing whatsoever about chloroquine as a treatment: **chloroquine appears in this document only as a mechanistic reagent in culture.**

## Relevance to a Q230P-class allele — stated with its distance

What transfers is **one mechanism class and one diagnostic test**, and they must be stated as that and not more.

*The class.* A missense substitution in the WWOX SDR span can be lost post-transcriptionally, with unaffected mRNA, through a lysosome-dependent, proteasome-independent, macroautophagy-independent route. Before PMID 41124647 this repository held that as a structural prediction in `DL-BIO-001`; it is now an observed instance on a WWOX protein. That is a genuine gain: *"present in transcript, absent in protein"* and *"present but impaired"* are different molecular diseases with different intervention points, and the first is now known to be real for a WWOX SDR missense substitution.

*The test.* The diagnostic question for a Q230P-class allele sharpens from *"how much protein and how much activity?"* to **"is the protein present at normal abundance, or is it being degraded despite normal mRNA — and if so, by which route?"** — answerable by a western plus a pulse-chase plus a lysosomal-inhibitor arm, with MG‑132 as an expected negative rather than a primary probe.

*The distance, which is not covered.* Six gaps stack between the evidence and the allele. Species and system: human cancer cell lines plus nude-mouse xenografts, and in the second paper mouse embryonic fibroblasts, against a human neuron. Cell type: CAL‑62 anaplastic thyroid carcinoma and BCPAP, against a donor-derived fibroblast or an iPSC-derived neuron. Endogenous versus transgene: every relevant result is a stably over-expressed Flag-tagged construct under a CMV promoter, where the synthesis rate is set by the vector and not by the cell. Cancer versus neuron: `seizure` 0, `neuron` 0, `epilep*` present only as background in the Introduction and Discussion with no data attached. Designed-conservative versus disease-derived: Y293F was built to abolish catalysis and preserve the fold, and the paper using it measured no protein level, so it cannot be made to say that an SDR missense protein is present and folded. And allele identity: Q230P appears once in PMID 41124647, in a background list, with no experiment; P252A and P282A are different residues, and `DL-MECH-019` together with `DL-MECH-029` already record that same-domain is not same-allele and that the Q230P protein's fate is undiscriminated between impaired translation, insolubility and premature degradation.

*The brake, carried deliberately.* The proband homozygous for two SDR-span missense alleles that are indistinguishable from empty vector in clonogenicity, viability, invasion, migration, xenograft growth, comet, γH2AX and an NHEJ reporter nonetheless *"did not suffer from WWOX‐related nervous system disease"* at 35. **Cancer-assay-dead is not neurologically null.** No functional result from a tumour assay may be used to predict the neurological severity expected of a missense allele, in either direction.

*Therefore.* The correct statement of relevance is not that the proteostasis route is a therapeutic opening for a Q230P-class allele. It is that the route is now a **named, testable hypothesis with a defined negative control and a cheap first measurement** — and that the measurement which would make it therapeutically meaningful, function at restored abundance, has not been performed on any WWOX missense allele by anyone.
