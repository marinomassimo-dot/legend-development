## 3 · Negative-translation audit — can a negative be verified from where it is read?

**The question.** Not *"is this negative true?"* but *"can a reader who lands on this block check it
without reconstructing which upstream section it came from?"* — because the block is the unit that
gets quoted downstream.

**Instrument:** count resolvable references — `PMID \d{7,8}`, `CLAIM \d+`, `DL-[A-Z]+-\d+` — inside
each candidate's own `###` block.

| Population | Blocks | Resolvable references inside them |
|---|---|---|
| Negatives `N-01`…`N-15` | 15 | **0** |
| Promoted `R-01`…`R-09` | 9 | **5** (`R-02` ×2 CLAIM, `R-03` ×1 CLAIM, `R-08` ×2 DL) |
| **Whole document** | — | 3 PMID · 50 CLAIM · 51 DL = **104** |
| …of which **before** the candidate section | — | 2 PMID · 47 CLAIM · 47 DL = **96** |
| …**inside or after** the candidate section | — | 1 PMID · 3 CLAIM · 4 DL = **8** |

🔴 **The finding is not about the negatives. It is about the whole candidate layer.**
A first pass reported *"15/15 negatives unanchored"* and stopped there. Running the identical
instrument on the **promoted** candidates as a control produced 5 references across 9 blocks — and
**`R-01`, the top-ranked candidate in the portfolio, carries zero**, exactly like `N-01`, the
second-ranked negative. The evidence is real and it lives upstream in the same document; what the
candidate blocks lack is any pointer to it. **Ninety-six of the map's 104 references sit before the
section that gets read, ranked and quoted.**

**And the consequence is measured, not hypothesized.** The two candidate blocks that have since been
audited in depth are precisely the two with zero references, and both turned out to carry a defect of
exactly the class this structure hides:

| Block | References in block | Defect found on audit |
|---|---|---|
| `N-01` | 0 | its only directional datum has **no verbatim locator anywhere in the repository** — 12-entry manifest, dossier and commit candidate all silent |
| `R-01` | 0 | `KNOWN_MAJOR_SAFETY_CONSTRAINTS` asserts **DRG toxicity** as a property of this vector; it is **review-sourced and class-level**, and no DRG assessment exists in either WWOX paper |

⚠️ **What this audit does not establish.** Thirteen of the fifteen negatives have **not** been audited
against their sources. The sweep measures **verifiability from the point of use**; it cannot
distinguish an unanchored *presentation* from an unanchored *premise*. Reporting "15 unanchored
negatives" as though it meant "15 unsupported negatives" would be the same error, one level up.

**Minimum delta:** each `R-` and `N-` block carries the reference for its own load-bearing assertion.
`CHANGE_CLASS: MINOR`, sub-canonical, and it is a documentation repair with an epistemic payoff —
the two defects found so far were both invisible from inside the block that stated them.

### 4.1 The single word

> 🔴 **In both AAV9 claims the conflation is carried by one token: `comportamento`.**

Measured against the sources: across the **49 verbatim locators** of the two AAV9 manifests, the
behavioural battery is **locomotor velocity, total distance, open-field and motor performance**.
**There is no cognitive assay** — no maze, no novel-object, no fear conditioning, no operant task, no
learning measure. So in `CLAIM 004` and `CLAIM 011`, `comportamento` denotes **locomotor and motor
behaviour**, and nothing else.

Placed in a list beside `crisi` and `mielinizzazione`, with no qualifier, it is read as *behaviour*
in the ordinary sense — which is the sense `CLAIM 031` says has never improved in a WWOX child whose
seizures improved. **Two `consolidated baseline`-class records in `P7 — gene therapy readiness` are
one unqualified noun away from asserting the thing `CLAIM 031` denies.**

**Minimum delta:** in both claims, `comportamento` → **`comportamento locomotorio/motorio (nessun
endpoint cognitivo misurato)`**, and add a one-line boundary naming `CLAIM 031`.
`CHANGE_CLASS:` **MAJOR?** → Mirror, fail-closed: it edits the Summary of a `consolidated baseline`
claim in the gene-therapy pathway, and although it *narrows a wording* rather than reversing a
finding, the narrowing is exactly what downstream scoring reads.

### 4.2 The asymmetry worth naming

`CLAIM 031` is `in observation`. `CLAIM 004` is `consolidated baseline`.

The claim that says *seizure control does not rescue development* — `T1`, human, direct — sits at a
**lower** status than the claim whose undifferentiated domain list invites the opposite reading. That
ordering is defensible on its own terms (`N = 2`, observational, uncontrolled, and the registry is
right to say so). It is worth stating anyway, because a reader ranking by status will meet the
global-sounding claim first and the corrective second, and this repository's own evidence says the
corrective is the one that survives contact with a child.
