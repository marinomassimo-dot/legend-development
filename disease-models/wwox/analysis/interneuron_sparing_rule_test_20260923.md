## § 1 · BASELINE ENUMERATION — unfiltered first, filtered second, both shown

### 1.0 🔴 The enumeration rule, and why a keyword filter would have failed

**A filter built from the expected terms returns only the expected files.** `grep -i interneuron` over
`disease-models/wwox/` returns the three rows the brief already names, because *"interneuron"* is a word that
appears **only in papers that measured one**. The rule under test is a statement about a **contrast** — an
interneuron class *beside* a principal neuron — and the contrast's most informative instances are the papers
where **one of the two terms is missing**. Those papers contain the word *"Purkinje"* or *"pyramidal"* or
*"NeuN"* and **not** *"interneuron"*, so a keyword filter deletes exactly the evidence that decides the node.

⇒ The population is therefore enumerated **by measurement instrument** — *which antibody, stain or recording
was pointed at a neuron of named class in nervous tissue* — a property of each paper's Methods section, not of
my hypothesis. Only then is the two-term criterion applied. Both stages are printed.

### 2.1 🔴 What the table says, in one line each

1. **No two rows share an outcome variable.** A stereological total, a spike count, an areal density and a
   TUNEL score are four different quantities. **A structural count paired with an electrophysiological null is
   not a replication** (§16), and nothing in this table is a replication of anything else in it.
2. **No two rows share an allele.** Four alleles, two of them not even nulls: `P47T` is a partial LoF with
   normal `Wwox` mRNA, `S-KO` is a post-mitotic neuronal conditional.
3. **No two rows share an age.** P13–17, P19–20, 2 weeks, and 80/250 d. The cerebellar rows are **60 to 230
   days apart**, which is most of a mouse's measured lifespan in the `P47T` line and **beyond the entire
   lifespan of the null**, which dies *"less than a month"* postnatally.
4. **The interneuron class is a different object in each row that has one:** a marker-defined PV⁺/NPY⁺
   population; an unidentified not-pyramidal residue; an Hcn1⁺ basket-cell population.
5. **Only one row has a valid denominator, and it is the row with no principal-neuron comparator.**

### 2.2 🎯 THE FINDING — the cerebellar reference space is a dependent variable of the genotype

🟢 **Verified first-hand this act.** `PMID 36828035` Methods § 4.13, verbatim as served:

> *"The entire CB was scanned for regions of atrophy and PC degeneration and the exact comparative regions
> between [WT] and [mutant] CB were imaged at 20x and 40x magnification. … Calbindin+ PC and Hcn1 + basket
> cell counting was done using dual labelled images in comparative 1000 μm[²] sections from [WT] and [mutant]
> pairs (n = 3)."*

And Results § 2.7, verbatim:

> *"Determination of PC and basket cell (Hcn1 +) numbers indicate that while PC numbers were significantly
> reduced in the cerebellar cortex from [mutant] mice, the number of basket cells in the same regions remained
> unchanged."*

🟢 **Both passages confirmed. The brief's transcription is accurate.** And in the *same* Results section, from
the *same* sections:

> *"The average molecular and granular layer thickness was significantly reduced in the cerebellar cortex from
> [mutant] mice in both age groups, indicative of significant CB atrophy in these animals (p-value < 0.001…)"*

⇒ 🔴 **The basket-cell result is a DENSITY — counts per fixed 2-D area — measured in tissue whose thickness
the same experiment reports as significantly reduced.** The denominator is not a constant of the assay; it is
one of the experiment's own significant outcomes. **"Unchanged density in atrophic tissue" is not "unchanged
number."** It is compatible with unchanged, reduced, or increased total basket-cell number, and the paper's
own atrophy result makes *unchanged total* the least likely of the three.

### 2.3 🔴 AND THIS IS WHERE I DISAGREE WITH THE BRIEF THAT COMMISSIONED ME

The brief argues that counting basket cells in fields **selected for maximal Purkinje pathology** is, for the
cell-type-specificity question, *"a conservative, internally controlled design"* and that finding them
unchanged there is *"a STRONGER sparing result than a random sample would be."*

**That argument is sound only if the denominator is fixed across the compared fields. It is not.**

- Field selection picks the fields with **maximal atrophy**. Atrophy compacts the surviving tissue.
- An **areal** density of a spared class is therefore **inflated most in exactly the fields the selection
  points at.**
- ⇒ 🔴 **The two biases run in OPPOSITE directions and neither is quantified.** Field selection is
  conservative; the moving denominator is anti-conservative; and the net direction is **UNKNOWN**, not
  conservative. **The brief's conclusion does not follow from its premise.**

🎯 **The published numbers point the same way.** 🔴 **SIBLING-ATTESTED figure values**
(`CC-20260826-CLAIM006-HARDENING-01.md:46`, box-plot **medians**): Hcn1⁺ basket, 80 d WT ≈80 · mut ≈78;
250 d WT ≈95 · **mut ≈112**. **At 250 days the mutant density is ~18 % HIGHER than wild type** in a cerebellum
the same paper calls significantly atrophic. That is the signature of compaction, not of abundance.

#### 2.3a ⚠️ A bounded sensitivity illustration — NOT a result, and not to be quoted as one

Taking the sibling-attested ML-thickness values (`CC-20260826-PMID36828035-02.md:123`: WT ≈190 → ≈222 µm,
mut ≈165 → ≈162 µm) and multiplying density by thickness to approximate **basket cells per unit tangential
length of Purkinje layer**:

| Age | WT (density × ML) | Mutant | Mutant / WT |
|---|---|---|---|
| 80 d | 80 × 190 | 78 × 165 | **≈ 0.85** |
| 250 d | 95 × 222 | 112 × 162 | **≈ 0.86** |

🔴 **Every assumption this rests on, named, and each of them may be false:** (i) that the counting frame spans
the full ML thickness — **the Methods do not say**; (ii) that box-plot medians and the thickness panel's
statistic are the same kind of average — **they are not necessarily**; (iii) that pixel-estimated values from
a stripped figure are accurate — they are **sibling-attested estimates, not reported numbers**; (iv) that
basket cells distribute uniformly through the ML — they do not, they sit in its inner third.
⇒ **This does NOT show a ~15 % basket-cell deficit.** It shows only that **a deficit of that order is fully
compatible with the published null**, and that nobody can tell from the published surface which it is.
🔴 It is an argument that the `ns` is **uninformative**, not an argument that the cells are lost.

### 2.4 🔴 A marker-validity caveat the paper does not address

`Hcn1` is used as the sole basket-cell identifier. In the mammalian cerebellar cortex `Hcn1`
immunoreactivity is **not confined to basket cells** — `PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK`, and I did **not**
verify it against a primary source in this act. The dual-labelling with calbindin gives the authors a way to
exclude Purkinje cells, but the paper **states no exclusion criterion, no morphological criterion and no
specificity control for the Hcn1 channel.** `REVIVAL_TRIGGER`: any cerebellar `Hcn1` expression map at
cell-type resolution, or a second basket-cell marker applied to this material.
🔴 **`NOT STATED` is not `NOT DONE`.** Recorded as a bound on the claim, not as an error in the source.
