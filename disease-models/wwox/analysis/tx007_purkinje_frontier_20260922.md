### 🥇 **P1 — Does the vector ARRIVE in the periphery, where the protein channel reads nothing?**
**Class: RAW DATA REANALYSIS / existing assay on already-harvested tissue · 🆕 NEW IN THIS FILE**

- **EXISTING ASSET.** A running vector-genome qPCR, already applied to four brain regions at P30
  ([`tx007_regional_wwox_forebrain_cerebellum_20260922.md:52`](tx007_regional_wwox_forebrain_cerebellum_20260922.md));
  **peripheral tissues the programme demonstrably harvested and blotted** — liver (S6H/S6I, both arms),
  spinal cord (S6A–S6D), sciatic nerve (S6E–S6G) — plus the 2021 harvest of liver, pancreas, kidney, testis
  and ovary at P17 and 9 months ([`:103`](tx007_regional_wwox_forebrain_cerebellum_20260922.md)); and
  matrix-matched vector-free blanks already in hand (the `KO+RI` / `WT+RI` vehicle arms).
- **OPEN QUESTION.** `B-f1`. The peripheral result on record is a **protein** negative; **vector genomes were
  never quantified outside the brain in either paper**. Whether the periphery is *vector-free* or
  *vector-present-and-silent* is unmeasured, and the CNS-reversibility argument rests on which it is.
- **MINIMAL ACTION.** Run the **existing** primer pair on DNA from those already-harvested peripheral tissues,
  in the same plate design, with the `RI` arms as the blank. No new assay, no new reagent, no new endpoint.
- **NEW ANIMALS?** 🟢 **NO** — conditional on tissue or extracted DNA retention (`A-f4`, `R3`), which is
  `NOT STATED` and is stated here rather than assumed.
- **INFORMATION GAIN.** 🎯 **Highest in this file, because the outcome is genuinely unpredicted and it is the
  premise every other peripheral conclusion sits on.** It also separates two quantities the record currently
  collapses — arrival and expression — in the one direction the existing data cannot.
- **RESULT A — genomes present where protein is absent.** The peripheral negative becomes an **expression**
  result (promoter restriction doing exactly what it was designed to do), not a biodistribution result. The
  *"anything that changes there changes through the brain"* premise must then be re-derived rather than
  assumed, and a direct peripheral contribution to any peripheral endpoint becomes a live alternative.
  ⚠️ It would **not** show the periphery is transduced-and-functional: genomes are not transcripts.
- **RESULT B — genomes at or below the blank.** 🟢 The strongest possible outcome for the programme's own
  interpretation: it converts an inference into a **measured delivery result**, and the glucose rescue
  (`B-s1`) becomes a demonstrated central-control effect on a firm footing rather than a protein-blot
  inference. It would also retire `B-f1` permanently.
- 🔴 **What it cannot settle.** Nothing about **Purkinje cells** (wrong tissue), nothing about causality
  (`B-f4`), and nothing about the sciatic-nerve and spinal-cord protein positives, which are a separate
  observation with **no quantification against WT** ([`tx007_regional_wwox_forebrain_cerebellum_20260922.md:571`](tx007_regional_wwox_forebrain_cerebellum_20260922.md)).
