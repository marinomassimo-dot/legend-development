## 1 · 🔴 THE SCOPE LIMIT THAT GOVERNS EVERYTHING BELOW

| | |
|---|---|
| **Genotype challenged** | **`Wwox^WT/P47T` HETEROZYGOTES** vs `Wwox^WT/WT` littermates |
| **Allele class** | `P47T` is a **SCAR12**-class allele impairing **WW-domain** protein–protein interaction. 🔴 **It is not a WOREE null allele**, and this is not a null model |
| **Design** | `n = 6 male + 3 female` per group · mean age **2.6 months** · **single** 10 mg/kg i.p. LPS · euthanised at **12 h** · OFT + Murine Sepsis Severity score + IHC + qPCR |

The paper confines its own claim to challenge, and the sentence is the most important one in it:

> *"our results contribute to prior studies demonstrating `Wwox^WT/P47T` mice **do not differ
> significantly** from `Wwox^WT/WT` littermates **at baseline**. However, when **challenged** with
> experimental sepsis, the `Wwox^WT/P47T` mice demonstrate a more severe phenotype, suggesting that
> loss on one Wwox allele is sufficient to increase response to injurious stimuli **in the
> appropriate setting**."*

🎯 **This is a gene–environment interaction result, not a disease model.** The heterozygote is
normal until stressed. That is precisely what makes it a **modifier / stress-sensitivity** lead and
precisely what disqualifies it from bearing on WOREE null biology.

## 2 · ENDPOINTS VERIFIED FIRST-HAND

| Domain | Result, as measured | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Sickness behaviour — distance** | Reduced post-LPS in **both** genotypes, `p < 0.0001` | 🔴 **Not genotype-specific.** Cannot support a genotype claim |
| **Sickness behaviour — time in centre** | Reduced in `WT/P47T` (`p = 0.0312`); not in `WT/WT` | 🟡 **A within-genotype comparison**, not a genotype × treatment interaction test |
| **Sepsis severity (MSS)** | `WT/P47T` LPS > `WT/WT` LPS, `p = 0.001617` | 🟢 Direct between-genotype contrast |
| **Microglia (Iba1)** | Cortex `p = 0.0282`; **hippocampus `p = 0.0490`** | 🟡 The hippocampal value is *just* under 0.05, **uncorrected** |
| **Astrocytes (GFAP)** | Cortex `p = 0.0005` (and `p = 0.0040` vs own PBS); 🔴 **hippocampus: no difference** | 🟢 **Regional dissociation confirmed exactly** as the brief described |
| **Apoptosis (CC3/NeuN)** | Cortex: **no difference at all**. Hippocampus: **CC3⁺ area ↑** `p = 0.0226`, but **no difference in CC3-positive neurons** | 🟢 Confirmed |
| **Cortical cytokine mRNA** | IL-1β up in both; **IL-6 and TNF-α up only in `WT/P47T`** (TNF-α between-genotype `p = 0.0016`) | 🟢 Confirmed |

**The authors bound the apoptosis result themselves, and correctly:**

> *"In the hippocampus, there was an increase in area of CC3+ immunostaining in the `Wwox^WT/P47T`
> LPS group compared to the `Wwox^WT/WT` LPS group (p=0.0226) **but no difference in CC3-positive
> neurons**. This suggests a regional difference in susceptibility to LPS between genotypes **that
> is not affecting neurons**."*

🔴 **The apoptotic cell type is unresolved, and the authors say so** — *"the affected cell type
remains to be determined."* Do not read this as neuronal death.

## 3 · 🔴 A CORRECTION TO THE PRELIMINARY READ — the peripheral response is DISCORDANT, not amplified

The Operator brief §24 listed *"greater: sepsis severity; sickness behaviour; gliosis;
**inflammatory response**; apoptosis."* The first, third and fifth hold. **The fourth does not hold
as stated**, and the direction is the opposite of what is implied:

> *"Plasma **IL-6** levels were significantly elevated in `Wwox^WT/WT` LPS mice compared to PBS
> control (p=0.0168) **with no difference in the `Wwox^WT/P47T` mice**. Plasma **TNF-α** levels were
> elevated in the `Wwox^WT/WT` LPS mice compared to PBS control (p=0.0025) **with no difference in
> the `Wwox^WT/P47T` groups**."*

🎯 **So: brain cytokine mRNA UP in the mutant; the two headline plasma cytokines fail to rise in the
mutant.** Only MCP-1 rose in both, and IFN-γ rose in the mutant alone (`p = 0.0430`).

The authors notice the discordance and attribute it to *"timing vs difference in technique."*
🔴 **The abstract's blanket *"increase in ... inflammatory response"* oversells the plasma data.**
The defensible statement is **compartment-specific**: *increased **cortical** pro-inflammatory
transcript, with no corresponding increase in circulating IL-6 or TNF-α protein at 12 h.*

## 4 · LIMITATIONS

**The authors' own:** no time course; blood–brain-barrier permeability not examined; signalling
pathways out of scope; and — notably — *"the possibility of **baseline inflammation** observed in
the `Wwox^WT/P47T` mice."*

**Added by this reading:** `n = 9` per group · a **single** 12-hour timepoint · **no
multiple-comparison correction** across the reported endpoints, which bears directly on the
hippocampal microglia value at `p = 0.0490` · several key comparisons are **within**-genotype
(LPS vs PBS) rather than between-genotype, and the two are presented side by side.

## 5 · WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CHANGE

| | |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Genuinely new** | The **heterozygote** is the unit of observation. A single `Wwox` allele's loss is shown to be **conditionally** consequential — silent at baseline, penetrant under acute inflammatory challenge. The repository's `P47T` material concerned **homozygotes** |
| 🟢 **New regional detail** | A cortex-vs-hippocampus **dissociation** that runs in *opposite directions for the two glial lineages*: microglia respond in both regions, astrocytes only in cortex, apoptosis only in hippocampus |
| 🔴 **Does NOT change** | Any mechanistic node in the WOREE / `Q230P` programme. Different allele class, different zygosity, different insult, different species-context |
| 🔴 **Does NOT license** | Any therapeutic inference. **No node is promoted.** *"this may be a target for future therapeutic interventions"* is the authors' speculation in a non-peer-reviewed venue, with no dose, route, schedule or clinical framing anywhere |
| 🟡 **Becomes** | A **modifier / stress-sensitivity lead**, parked at preprint tier, with a concrete revival trigger: **peer-reviewed publication**, or any independent replication of the heterozygote-under-challenge effect |

🔴 **BLOCK-1 note.** Nothing here is medical advice. The paper's closing line — *"this study is
applicable to the population at large"* — is an unsupported generalisation from `n = 9` mice at one
timepoint and is **not** carried into this repository at any strength.
