## 3 · TEST B — THE ANATOMICAL TEST (the strongest available discriminator)

**The prediction under test.** Intramyelinic oedema puts vacuoles **where myelin is**. The sibling states
this himself: `H5` predicts vacuoles *"in fibre-rich strata (alveus, stratum lacunosum-moleculare, external
capsule/stria terminalis) … tracks **myelin density**, not neuronal identity"* and explicitly *"**not** in
stratum pyramidale"* (`new_discovery_node_20260922.md:158`).

### 3.1 🔴 LAMINAR POSITION IS UNRECORDED. The test as designed cannot be run from held material.

A `grep` for `stratum | lacunosum | pyramidale | radiatum | alveus | oriens | fimbria | stria terminalis`
over **every** markdown file in `disease-models/wwox/` returns **no hit that describes the `lde/lde`
vacuoles**. The only hippocampal-laminar text in the corpus belongs to an unrelated TRAPPC6AΔ aggregate
study (`analysis/trappc6a_delta_node_audit_20260920.md:531-536`) and to the sibling's own *proposal* that
laminar position be measured (`new_discovery_node_20260922.md:158`, `:305`).

Everything the repository holds about vacuole position is at **subfield** resolution and no finer:

> 🔴 SIBLING-ATTESTED — *"**Vacuoles** in CA1 and amygdala, absent in normals; dentate gyrus and CA3 appear
> spared in the image."* — `research/fulltext_dossiers/PMID17803050.md:109-110`

> 🔴 SIBLING-ATTESTED — *"**Extracellular vacuoles** in the hippocampus of **9/9** affected and the amygdala
> of 6, with **0/10** normals."* — `research/fulltext_dossiers/PMID19500159.md:111-112`

⇒ **`CA1` is a subfield, not a stratum.** It contains stratum oriens, pyramidale, radiatum and
lacunosum-moleculare, plus the alveus at its border — compartments that differ from one another in myelin
content more than CA1 differs from CA3. **The single measurement that would discriminate `H5` from `H1`,
`H3` and `H4` is the one the sources do not report.** The sibling is right to make it `E1`
(`new_discovery_node_20260922.md:305`); I confirm independently that it is absent, and that **no amount of
re-reading held material can supply it** short of re-opening the 2007 figures themselves.

### 3.2 The surrogate test that *can* be run — and it runs against the bridge

Laminar resolution is unavailable, but **regional** myelin density is not. The corpus holds one structure
whose myelin was actually measured in this rat: the **corpus callosum**, alongside cortex —
*"APC/CC1-positive mature oligodendrocytes are reduced at PND15 and PND21 in a selected cortex/white-matter
region **and corpus callosum**"* (🔴 SIBLING-ATTESTED, `PMID31340538.md:78-79`), with MBP and CNP *"strongly
reduced during the PND5–21 interval"* (`:75-77`).

| Structure | Myelin content at ~P21–P28 | Myelin measured? | Vacuoles reported? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Corpus callosum** | 🔴 the **highest** in the sampled sections | 🟢 **yes — severely reduced** | 🔴 **not reported anywhere in the corpus** |
| **Cerebral cortex** | intermediate | 🟢 **yes — severely reduced** | 🔴 **not reported** |
| **CA1 (grey matter, pyramidal subfield)** | 🔴 **low** — a cell-dense/neuropil subfield | 🔴 **never measured** | 🟢 **yes, 9/9** |
| **Amygdala (grey matter nuclei)** | 🔴 **low** | 🔴 **never measured** | 🟢 **yes, 6** |
| **Cerebellum** | high (white matter present) | 🔴 never measured | 🔴 *"we did not detect any marked pathologic changes in the cerebella"* (`PMID17803050.md:104-106`; `CLAIM 039`) |

🔴 **The reported distribution is the inverse of the `H5` prediction.** Vacuoles are reported in the two
**least** myelinated structures named and are **not** reported in the two most myelinated, one of which
(corpus callosum) is precisely where the hypomyelination was measured. If the vacuoles were a myelin lesion,
the corpus callosum of a severely hypomyelinated rat is the last place they should be absent.

### 3.3 🔴 Three reasons this surrogate must NOT be over-read, stated against my own finding

1. **`NOT_REPORTED ≠ ABSENT`, and here it is nearly `NOBODY_LOOKED`.** The 2019 study used IHC panels, not
   H&E vacuole scoring, and its declared outcome list contains no vacuole endpoint (`PMID31340538.md:35-38`).
   A callosal vacuole would not necessarily have been described. The 2007/2009 papers describe a hippocampal
   lesion; whether they examined or scored white matter at all is **not recorded in either dossier**.
2. **The age windows do not overlap** (§ 4). Callosal absence at PND21 is not evidence about ~P28.
3. **"Extracellular" is an author's light-microscopic word, not a compartment assignment.** The 2009 authors
   call them *"extracellular vacuoles"* (`PMID19500159.md:111`) — which is weakly *against* an intramyelinic
   reading, but the sibling's own § 4 transfer lesson is exactly that LM "vacuole" labels are routinely
   reassigned at EM (`new_discovery_node_20260922.md:190-192`). I decline to score the authors' adjective as
   evidence in either direction, and note it only because it is the only compartment word on record.

⇒ **Test B verdict: the designed test is UNRUNNABLE from held material (laminar position unrecorded). The
available regional surrogate leans AGAINST the bridge, and the lean is weak because it rests on a negative
nobody searched for.**

| Vacuole histology | **~28 days** (same cohort as the 28-day serum panel) | `CLAIM 039` title `:716`; `PMID17803050.md:26` |

### 4.1 Precedence is real but nearly vacuous

The hypomyelination is measured from **PND5**, before the earliest possible seizure at day 16 and before the
~28-day histology. The sibling reads this as *"the timing fits the age arm"* (`new_discovery_node_20260922.md:281-283`).

🔴 **The ordering is between two measurements in two different regions.** Cortical/callosal myelin at PND5
precedes hippocampal vacuoles at ~P28 in the same way that any early measurement precedes any later one in a
different structure. **Hippocampal myelin was never measured at any age; hippocampal vacuoles were never
looked for before ~P28.** There is no time series of either variable in the tissue where the other was seen,
so **there is no ordering between the two phenomena — only between two papers' calendars.**

### 4.2 It is equally consistent with a third cause, and the third cause is itself measured

`DL-MECH-027` records, in this exact rat, that **Wwox is expressed in neurons, astrocytes and
oligodendrocytes** (`research/discovery_ledger_current.md:615`) and that **MAP2 is significantly reduced at
every age** — impaired neurite growth with neurons present (`:613`), while neuron number, cortical thickness
and laminar distribution are **unchanged** (`:612`). A single upstream failure of postnatal maturation in a
cell type-general way predicts hypomyelination **and** a neuropil abnormality independently, with no causal
arrow between them. The repository already holds the glial-sign warning that this axis is not uniform:
*"il segno del fenotipo gliale/neuroinfiammatorio è regione- e modello-dipendente"* (`:622`).

⇒ **Test C verdict: temporal precedence is NOT established between these two phenomena, and even the
precedence that exists between the two papers is fully consistent with both being parallel consequences of a
third, measured, cell type-general maturation failure. Precedence is not causation, and here it is not even
precedence.**

## 5 · TEST D — ADJUDICATING THE SIBLING'S OWN COUNTER-ARGUMENT

**The counter-argument, taken seriously** (`new_discovery_node_20260922.md:285-287`): classical intramyelinic
oedema requires **myelin to split**; severely hypomyelinated white matter has **less to split**; therefore
severe hypomyelination arguably predicts **fewer** intramyelinic vacuoles, not more.

🟢 **I judge this counter-argument SOUND, and stronger than the sibling allows.** It is not a caveat on `H5`;
it is a near-refutation of `H5a`, for two reasons the sibling does not use:

1. **It has a measured dose-response and the dose-response runs the wrong way.** The myelin deficit in this
   rat is not mild — MBP and CNP are *"strongly reduced"* across PND5–21 with mature oligodendrocytes reduced
   (`PMID31340538.md:75-79`). The more severe the deficit, the fewer lamellae exist to split. `H5a` therefore
   predicts the vacuole burden should be **lowest** where the myelin deficit is **worst** — and the worst
   measured deficit is in the corpus callosum, where no vacuoles are reported (§ 3.2).
2. **The two structures that do vacuolate are the two whose myelin content is lowest to begin with** (§ 3.2).
   `H5a` needs a substrate that CA1 stratum pyramidale and amygdalar nuclei barely possess at ~P28.

### 5.1 The fork, adjudicated

| Fork | Status after this act | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **`H5a` — splitting of existing myelin** | 🔴 **WEAKENED to the point of being the less likely fork** | Requires vacuole burden to scale with myelin present; the only structures where myelin was measured are severely depleted and carry no reported vacuoles, while the vacuolating structures are the least myelinated named (§ 3.2, § 5) |
| **`H5b` — abortive/unstable myelination producing fluid-filled spaces** | 🟡 **SURVIVES, and is the only surviving myelin fork — but it is untested and carries an unexamined size problem** | It does not require pre-existing lamellae, and it is compatible with reduced mature oligodendrocytes. See § 5.2 for its own defect |

### 5.2 🔴 `H5b`'s own defect, stated because nobody else has

`H5b` must explain vacuoles **large enough to be seen and called "vacuoles" in H&E at light microscopy**, in
9/9 animals, distinctly enough that a reader can classify a hippocampus from a low-magnification panel
(`PMID19500159.md:117-123`). An abortive ensheathment space or a dead-oligodendrocyte footprint is a
sub-micron to low-micron structure. **The repository holds no vacuole size, count, density or diameter for
any `lde/lde` section** — the dossiers record none, and neither does any claim.

🎯 **And there is a cheap, specific, unexploited retrieval target here.** The 2009 Figure 1 caption is
recorded as misspelling *size* as *"seize"* (`PMID19500159.md:120`) — i.e. **the caption contains a size
statement that LEGEND's dossier did not transcribe**. Recovering that one sentence would put a scale on the
lesion and is, on its own, capable of weakening or killing `H5b`. The paper is not retrievable in this
deployment (`is_open_access: false`, no PMCID), so this is recorded as a **retrieval target**, not a result.

⇒ **Test D verdict: the sibling's counter-argument survives and prevails. `H5a` is the weaker fork; `H5b` is
the only surviving myelin fork, and it is untested with an unmeasured size premise.**
