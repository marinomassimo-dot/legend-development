## B · The abundance-versus-severity framing, judged against the source

**What the registry says.** `PAPER 042`, *clinical relevance*: **HIGH — è il controllo che dimostra
che l'abbondanza di proteina non predice la severità.** `CLAIM 030` (status: in observation) states
the rule itself: *"In WWOX the severity tracks residual protein FUNCTION, not protein abundance"*,
and `DL-MECH-033` builds the allelic table that carries it.

**What the paper measured** (dossier §5, manifest entries 6–12, 29):

| Question | The source's answer |
|---|---|
| Individuals with a protein measurement | **One.** Patient II-3 of Family 1, `p.Pro47Thr` homozygous |
| Lanes | 3 passages (P10/P13/P14) of that one culture + 4 unrelated control lines + 1 mouse cerebellum lane declared as a band-position marker |
| Method | one polyclonal antibody (WWOX residues 1–19, 1:500), immunoblot, actin loading control — comparison declared *"Based on visual inspection"* |
| Quantification | **none.** No densitometry, no replicate quantification, no statistic, no error term anywhere in the paper; nothing on the panel is numeric |
| Comparator | control fibroblasts — **not** another affected individual, another genotype, or a severity stratum |
| Measurement for `G372R` | **none at all**, and that variant *"prevented reliable modelling"* too |

**Verdict on the framing — it is a cross-paper construction, and only one cell of it is this paper's.**

1. **At claim level the registry is sound and already says so.** `CLAIM 030` rests on four sources
   (`PAPER 042` Mallaret P47T · `PAPER 041` Johannsen Q230P · `PAPER 039` Steinberg G372R ·
   `PAPER 043` Abdel-Salam `p.Arg54*`), which is exactly how a dissociation claim must be built:
   this paper supplies **one row** — *P47T: protein present in the one individual measured, abundance
   unquantified; PPPY-peptide binding severely impaired; phenotype mild* — and cannot supply a
   dissociation on its own, because a dissociation needs
   at least two genotypes measured commensurably and this paper measured one.
2. **`PAPER 042`'s own `clinical relevance` sentence overstates that role.** *"Il controllo che
   dimostra"* attributes the demonstration to this paper. This paper demonstrates nothing about
   severity-versus-abundance: it measures abundance once, in one allele, in fibroblasts, by eye.
   The demonstration is `CLAIM 030`'s, assembled across four papers. 🔴 This is the finding, and it
   is about a **field of a paper record**, not about the claim: the claim's conclusion is not
   challenged here and its status does not move.
3. **The paper's own use of abundance runs the other way, and no record in this corpus carries
   that.** The Discussion explains the *milder* human phenotype **by** residual protein — the P47T
   protein *"is still present, at least in human skin fibroblasts"*, unlike the mouse and rat
   models — while the *lde* rat, the severe end, has **no detectable WWOX**. So Mallaret et al. use
   abundance *as* a severity explanation across species, in the same paper the registry cites for
   abundance failing to predict severity. Both readings are defensible about different comparisons
   (theirs: cross-species null vs partial; the registry's: within-human across domains), and the
   registry nowhere records that the cited paper argues the cross-species direction.
4. **The caveats the records already carry, and they are the right ones.** `CLAIM 030`:
   *"⚠️ methodological caveat obbligatorio: le misure di abbondanza confrontate non sono
   commensurabili (WB su fibroblasti per P47T e Q230P; IF su organoidi per G372R). La serie orienta,
   non quantifica."* `DL-MECH-033`, same limit: *"medio sull'inferenza unificante (misure eterogenee:
   WB su fibroblasti vs IF su organoidi — non confrontabili quantitativamente; è un limite reale,
   non retorico)"*. **My reading strengthens that caveat with a number the records do not have:**
   this paper's contribution is `n = 1` individual at one point, so the incommensurability is not
   only *between* assays but *within* the P47T row, which has no replication anywhere — measured
   this session, `WWOX AND fibroblast AND "western blot"` returns **1** record in all of PubMed, and
   it is this paper.

**Typed:** the row is `DATO` within its system (one individual's fibroblasts). The rule is
`INFERENZA` and the registry types it that way. `PREMISE: DATO` for the panel; the textbook default
it refutes for this allele — *"a destabilising missense must reduce steady-state protein"* — is
`DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK`, and the authors cite PQBP1 Y65C as their own precedent against it.

## C · Where a record and the panel disagree — reported, not repaired here

**`PAPER 042`'s note, as it read BEFORE `BATCH_20260913_004 (VPS batch, never on main)`** — that record now says *"compromette
gravemente il binding all'oligopeptide PPPY"*; the pre-batch wording is quoted here because it is the
text this section critiqued: *"Peptide pull-down: … → **P47T abolisce completamente il binding ai motivi
PPxY**"*. **The panel at 600 dpi qualifies "completamente" and "ai motivi PPxY" in two different
ways** (manifest entries 13–14, 30):

- The tandem `WWOX1-2` lane is **empty within measurement**; the WW1-only lane is **not strictly
  blank**, carrying **~2.5% of the wild-type lane's integrated density**, but that density is
  **contiguous with a tapering smear off the photographically saturated wild-type band** and the
  panel **cannot distinguish residual binding from lateral flare**. The authors state the result
  **absolutely and jointly for both constructs** — *"the p.Pro47Thr mutant versions failed to
  interact"* — with **no per-construct result anywhere in the text**, so a construct-specific claim
  **in either direction is the reader's addition**. **Nothing in the paper quantifies the mutant
  signal or measures it as zero. Direction unchanged: P47T severely impairs binding to the PPPY
  peptide assayed.** 🔴 *Superseded here by the blind R4 of `CC-20260913-24369382-02`, which ran
  after this note was written and refused the inference while confirming both measurements.*
- *"Ai motivi PPxY"* generalises from **one** peptide — WBP1's PPPY — to the motif family. The
  figure caption makes that generalisation itself (*"the affinity of WWOX for PPXY motif containing
  interacting partners"*), so the registry is faithfully repeating the authors; the evidence
  underneath it is a single synthetic oligopeptide.

qualification of that claim's absoluteness would narrow a consolidated baseline claim, which is an
