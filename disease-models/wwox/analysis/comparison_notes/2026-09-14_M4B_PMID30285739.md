# M4b — what PMID 30285739 (Bonin 2018) does and does not establish

> `scientist-b` · task `ALDAZ-FT-B-003` · 2026-09-14. A separate phase, opened only after the reading was complete and the manifest reached strict PASS.
>
> Public, disease-level, de-identified. Nothing here is medical advice.

## A · The question this lot was dispatched to settle

The corpus records this paper as **the only independent support for a VOPP1–WWOX interaction via the WW1 domain and a PPPY motif**, and it has carried that credit **without a receipt**. `BATCH_20260914_005 (VPS batch, never on main)` had just narrowed `CLAIM 026` to what its sole primary (Hussain 2018, 30619736) actually supports, recording that in *that* paper WW1-dependence was shown by pull-down for SEC23IP and SCAMP3 only and that **VOPP1 had no pull-down at all**. So the question was whether the credit is real.

**Verdict: the credit is real, and it is understated.** This paper carries more than the corpus credited it with, not less.

## B · What it establishes

| Proposition | Method | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| WWOX and VOPP1 co-associate | Three independent routes: Y2H (ten clones, VOPP1 C-terminus, placenta library), reciprocal co-IP of over-expressed proteins in HEK-293T, **endogenous co-IP in MDA-MB-468 with an IgG control** | **DATO**, and the endogenous arm is stronger than anything in Hussain: neither partner over-expressed |
| The **WW1 domain** is required | WWOX **Y33R** point mutant abolishes the co-IP | **DATO — measured by mutation**, not inferred. Premise that Y33R disables WW1 is imported from ref 23 |
| The **PPPY165** motif is required | All three VOPP1 PPxY tyrosines independently mutated; Y119A retains binding, Y165A abolishes it, WCL row confirms expression | **DATO — measured by mutation**, with two internal control motifs. Not a sequence-motif inference |
| The interaction is what drives the downstream effects | The binding-deficient Y165A fails to relocate WWOX, fails to displace p73α, fails to block WWOX-induced death | **DATO** — the single best design feature of the paper |
| The apoptotic effect is WWOX-**dependent** | Epistasis: si-VOPP1 has no effect in WWOX-depleted cells | **DATO** |
| VOPP1 over-expression transforms cells and grows tumours | NIH3T3 soft agar (n = 4); SCID xenograft (n = 10/group) | **DATO** for that model; mouse fibroblast over-expression, no WWOX genotype manipulated |
| VOPP1 is high in breast tumours and prognostic in WWOX-retaining ones | 448-tumour retrospective cohort; Cox interaction term HR 3.41 (p = 0.016, CI 1.26–9.24); ratio effect only in WWOX-high tumours | **DATO as association**; external replication for luminal B (Table S2) |

## C · What it does not establish

1. **Not direct binding.** `recombinant`, `GST`, `purified` protein: **zero occurrences**. No pull-down from purified components, no SPR/ITC/structure. A bridging protein is excluded by nothing. The paper is careful here — it says "interacts", "partner", "physically associated", and **never claims directness**; the Methods' "direct immunoprecipitation" is an IP format, not a binding claim. Any corpus statement that this paper shows *direct* WWOX–VOPP1 binding would be an overshoot introduced by the corpus, not by the authors.
2. **Not "sequestration" as a measured process.** What exists is steady-state colocalisation plus redistribution after knockdown. No flux, transport, retention or degradation assay. "Sequester" is the authors' inference from static images.
3. **Nothing in neural material, and no WWOX variant.** HEK-293T, MDA-MB-468, HeLa, NIH3T3, A549, SCID mice, human breast tumours. The only WWOX mutant is the engineered Y33R. WWOX-related recessive neural disease appears exactly once, as a Background citation. **Transferability to the reference genotype: T3.**
4. **Replication is undeclared for the blots.** No blot states a replicate count; Figure 4d's quantification has no error bars and no n.
5. **Only-Y165 is too strong.** Figure 1e lane 7 shows Y157A with a reduced but present signal — a partial contribution the text does not report.

## D · Relation to `CLAIM 026` — and the one thing that changes

`CLAIM 026` is `in observation`, not a consolidated baseline, so no R4 blind audit precondition applies, and this reading contradicts no persisted locator (none existed for this PMID).

The claim's current Summary says, correctly of Hussain: *"WW1-dependence shown by GST pull-down from lysate for **SEC23IP and SCAMP3 only** (VOPP1 has no pull-down here)"*. That parenthetical is accurate about Hussain and should stay. What changes is its **implication**: read alone it leaves VOPP1's WW1/PPPY dependence looking unevidenced. It is not. It is evidenced here, by mutagenesis on both sides, in a paper that until today had no receipt in this corpus.

**Proposed, in the commit candidate:** carry a sentence recording that VOPP1's WW1- and PPPY165-dependence **is** independently measured — in `30285739`, by point mutation, with an endogenous co-IP — while keeping the directness negative intact for both papers. This *strengthens* one leg of CLAIM 026's trafficking half and leaves the metabolic half and the un-measured coupling exactly as `BATCH_20260914_005 (VPS batch, never on main)` left them.

## E · A registry finding worth more than the reading

The corpus's own records do **not** hold this paper the way its prose assumed. `registry_records.py` returns exactly two records, both placeholders: `CORPUS-STUB-165` (`Status: not_processed`, `Claim links: none`) and `LIT-0181` (`Status: discovered`, authors/year/journal "not yet extracted"). Earlier notes in this batch state that "the registry holds PMID 30285739" — true only of an unprocessed stub with no claim link. A paper described in three separate working documents as *the only independent support* for a live claim existed in canon as an unscreened placeholder. That gap between what the prose leaned on and what the registry recorded is the transferable lesson here, and it is exactly the `declaration-is-not-attestation` failure pointed the other way: not an unbacked declaration, but **unrecorded support**.

## F · Premises and revival

- The directness negative rests on `PREMISE: DATO` — the absence was searched across every surface (text, seven figures, seven supplements) with named search terms.
- The "no neural material / no variant" negative rests on `PREMISE: DATO` — searched across all sections, figures and supplements.
- The Y33R→WW1 premise is `PREMISE: INFERENZA`, imported from ref 23.
- **REVIVAL_TRIGGER:** any experiment measuring WWOX–VOPP1 association with purified or recombinant components, or a structural/biophysical determination, would convert the co-association datum into a directness datum. Separately, any WWOX–VOPP1 experiment in neural material or with a WWOX disease allele would move transferability off T3.
