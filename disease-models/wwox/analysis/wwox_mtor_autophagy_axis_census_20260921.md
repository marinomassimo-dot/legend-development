### 1.1 What the census measures

- **Census size: 15.** The axis really is small. This is not a sampling artefact of three queries — `WWOX AND (autophagy OR autophagic)` returned a `total_count` of 11 with `has_more: false`, the mTOR query 8, the marker query 3. The corpus is exhaustible.
- **Papers stating a direction for WWOX acting on autophagy: 4** — 36621327, 33300063, 24008736, 28045433. Two say up, two say down.
- **Papers retrievable here with a non-empty body: 3** — 24008736 (held by another reader), 28045433 (a Review), 41124647 (arrow reversed). Plus 28045433 is the only one of the fifteen that `get_copyright_status` flagged `is_open_access: true` among the non-excluded candidates.
- **Chang laboratory (Nan-Shan Chang / Li-Jin Hsu, National Cheng Kung University, Tainan): 2 of 15** — 24008736 and 25595191. Both point the same way (WWOX suppresses autophagy), and they are lab-mates, so they are **one node, not two**.
- **Homonym trap, checked and cleared:** PMID 37615513 carries an author "Chang" in the middle of its list. That is a different Chang — the senior author is Qiu Lixin, Fudan University, Shanghai. It is **outside** the NCKU laboratory. Similarly, PMID 35984507 uses **Zfra1-31**, a peptide reagent originating from the Chang laboratory, but the authors are Coimbra, Portugal — an **independent group using a Chang-lab tool**. A shared reagent is not a shared laboratory, and it is also not independent corroboration of the reagent's own premise.

### 1.2 The finding the reasoning files did not have

The standing negative rested on four stubs. The census adds a **fifth** direction-stating paper, **PMID 28045433** (Maroni / Desiderio, Milano, IJMS 2017), which is **the only direction-stating paper in the whole corpus that is open access and retrievable on this surface**. It reports that transfecting a Wwox expression vector into 1833 bone-metastatic cells raised Beclin-1 — i.e. it sits on the **WWOX↑autophagy** side, from a laboratory unconnected to any of the other four.

It is typed by PubMed as `["Comparative Study", "Journal Article", "Review"]`, so it does not qualify as the Part 2 primary and no artefact was written for it. It was fetched only to **measure retrievability**, and that fetch returned a body. Two sentences from that body are worth recording verbatim, flagged as **review-embedded data, not an adjudicated read**:

> "As shown inB, the transfection ofexpression vector increased strongly Beclin-1 and less p62 protein levels in 1833 cells (quantification of the data is included in the graphic)."

> "It can be hypothesized that Wwox per se influences p62 induction notwithstanding autophagy flux"

Note the gaps in the first quotation — `inB` and `ofexpression` — these are the MCP extractor deleting an italicised figure cross-reference and an italicised gene symbol. See § 5.

The second quotation is the load-bearing one, and it cuts **against** the paper's own headline: the authors themselves say the Beclin-1/p62 pattern may reflect something other than autophagic flux. They did not clamp flux. So even this paper — the one readable paper on the "up" side — does not fix the sign.

### 1.3 A pattern the census exposes, tagged as hypothesis

The two-against-two split is not random with respect to context:

- **WWOX↑autophagy** — 36621327 (LPS acute lung injury, an inflammatory insult) and 28045433 (hypoxic bone metastasis, a microenvironmental stress).
- **WWOX↓autophagy** — 33300063 (paclitaxel) and 24008736 (methotrexate). **Both are cytotoxic-chemotherapy stress in a carcinoma line, and in both the stated purpose of suppressing autophagy is to permit apoptosis.**

`HYPOTHESIS` — the sign may be **stress-context dependent rather than contradictory**: under drug-induced apoptotic stress WWOX is reported to close autophagy down to let apoptosis proceed, while under inflammatory or hypoxic stress it is reported to open it. This is a reading of four abstracts and is **not** evidence. It is recorded because it is a testable alternative to "the literature contradicts itself", and because if it were true the axis would have **no transferable sign at all** for an unstressed neural context — which is the context that matters here.

A further observation, again abstract-level: three of the four direction-stating papers establish the sign by **overexpression or transfection** (36621327 "Overexpression of WWOX led to the activation of autophagy"; 33300063 "after WWOX overexpression was induced"; 28045433 "the transfection of...expression vector"). Only 24008736 reports a knockdown arm in its abstract ("small interfering RNA targeting WWOX", "When WWOX was knocked down in SCC-15"). **A sign established by sufficiency does not transfer to a loss-of-function genotype.** Even if all four were readable tomorrow, three of them would be answering a question the WWOX-DEE genotype does not ask.

### 2.1 Retrievability was measured, not assumed

`get_full_text_article` was called on four PMCIDs. Measured body lengths:

| PMCID | PMID | `full_text` length |
|---|---|---|
| PMC11071800 | 35984507 | **0 characters** |
| PMC6965410 | 31966718 | **0 characters** |
| PMC5297710 | 28045433 | 13,000+ characters (full body) |
| PMC12767083 | 41124647 | **68,491 characters** (full body) |

Two consequences:

1. **The standing "verified unobtainable" for PMID 31966718 is independently confirmed.** It has a PMCID (PMC6965410) that resolves to a record with an **empty body**. A PMCID is therefore not evidence of retrievability on this surface, and any future catalogue entry should record the measured body length, not the presence of an identifier.
2. **PMID 35984507 is the most scientifically relevant paper in the corpus and it cannot be opened here.** It is primary, it is **neuronal** (differentiated SH-SY5Y), it is **non-cancer**, and it works by **inhibiting** WWOX — the only loss-of-function-shaped design in the entire census. It is also the only paper that touches a neurodegenerative context. Its PMC record is present but empty. This is the single highest-value acquisition target the census produced.

### 2.2 An instrument caveat on the open-access flag

`get_copyright_status` reported `is_open_access: false` for both 35984507 and 41124647. For both, the returned `checked_sources` was `["pubmed"]` only — **PMC was not consulted**. For 41124647 that flag is demonstrably wrong as a statement about retrievability: the body came back in full, 68,491 characters. **`is_open_access: false` from a pubmed-only check is a not-checked reading, not a negative**, and must not be catalogued as "paywalled". Retrievability on this surface is established only by attempting the fetch and measuring the body length.

### 2.3 Why 41124647 does not answer the question — and what it does answer

PMID 41124647 is primary, retrievable and outside the four. It fails only on the arrow. Its 20 uses of "autophag-" all concern **autophagy degrading WWOX**, not WWOX regulating autophagy:

> "Mechanistically, WWOXmutant undergoes accelerated degradation via chaperone-mediated autophagy in the lysosome, directly linking protein instability to loss-of-function."

> "Since the KFERQ-like motif (LRSVQ at 187-191 amino acids) exists on WWOX, we speculated that lysosomal degradation of WWOXmutant protein occurred through chaperone-mediated autophagy."

(The missing token after "WWOX" in both quotations is an italicised superscript variant label deleted by the extractor.)

A string count over its body gives **mTOR = 0, mTORC = 0, rapamycin = 0, p70S6 = 0, S6K = 0, 4E-BP = 0**. These are **roman-type** tokens on this surface, so these zeros **are informative**: the paper does not measure mTOR. It therefore cannot contribute to the axis in either direction, and it was correctly excluded.

`SIGNAL — worth a separate look, out of scope here.` This paper reports that two **germline missense** WWOX variants (p.P252A, p.P282A) cause **loss of function through accelerated protein degradation** via a KFERQ-like motif at residues 187–191 recognised by HSC70. That is a mechanism by which a WWOX missense allele can be functionally null without being transcriptionally absent. It is not an autophagy-axis result and is not catalogued as one, but it is directly relevant to how missense WWOX alleles should be modelled, and it is **readable here**. It should be queued on its own merits.

## 3 · Does the standing negative conclusion survive?

**Yes, and it is now stronger — but its grounds have changed, and one of its supporting facts was wrong.**

**What survives.** No paper reachable on this surface fixes the direction of WWOX's effect on autophagy or mTOR. The axis contains exactly four direction-stating papers; they split two up, two down; three of the four establish their sign by overexpression only; and none of the four is both primary and openable here. The reasoning files were right that the sign is unfixed.

**What has changed, and must be corrected.** The old wording — that the negative rests on "four registry stubs, none of them read" — is no longer accurate in three ways:

1. **There is a fifth direction-stating paper**, PMID 28045433, which the reasoning files do not cite. It is on the "up" side, from an independent Italian laboratory, and it is the **only** direction-stating paper in the corpus that is open access and retrievable here. Its absence from the standing argument was a gap.
2. **The negative is no longer "nobody looked".** The corpus has been enumerated exhaustively (15 papers, `has_more: false` on all three queries) and every PMC-bearing candidate has had its body length measured. The negative now rests on **a measured census of a small, exhaustible literature**, not on an inference from unread titles.
3. **The two-against-two split is not a single-laboratory artefact.** Only 2 of 15 papers come from the NCKU/Chang laboratory, and those two are lab-mates — one node. The three non-Chang direction-stating papers (36621327, 33300063, 28045433) come from three unconnected groups in China and Italy and **still disagree with each other**. The disagreement is therefore real and distributed, not a house style. This removes the most obvious deflationary explanation for the contradiction and makes the negative harder, not softer.

**What the negative now rests on.** Four things, all measured this session: (a) the corpus is 15 papers and enumerable; (b) four state a direction and they split evenly; (c) the split survives removal of the Chang node; (d) of the direction-stating papers, the only one readable here is a Review whose authors explicitly decline to claim flux. Grounds (c) and (d) are new.

**What would move it.** Acquiring **PMID 35984507** by a route other than this MCP surface. It is the only primary, neuronal, non-cancer, loss-of-function-shaped paper in the census. It would not by itself fix the sign for a WWOX-DEE genotype, but it is the only item in this literature whose design and tissue are the right shape for the question. `PMID 36621327` and `PMID 33300063` are the two that would directly adjudicate the split, and both are confirmed unobtainable here.

**What must not be done.** The even 2–2 split must not be reported as "the literature is evenly divided, therefore the effect is null". Four abstracts in four different stress contexts, three of them overexpression-only, do not constitute a balanced body of evidence about anything. The honest statement is that **the sign is unmeasured in any context resembling a constitutive WWOX loss-of-function in neural tissue**, and that no paper in this corpus was designed to measure it.

## 5 · Declared limits

- **Every direction in § 1 is from a title or abstract. An abstract is not a read.** Nothing in § 1 is evidence and nothing in it may be promoted.
- **No full-text artefact was written.** `files/fulltext/` was not touched. No `FULLTEXT_READ_RECEIPT` is claimed for any PMID.
- **PMID 24008736 was deliberately not fetched**, as another reader holds it. Its row in § 1 is metadata only.
- **The MCP extractor silently deletes every italicised token** — gene symbols, *in vitro*/*in vivo*, the italic *P* in p-values, and all inline figure and reference cross-references — and drops tables, figure legends and reference lists. The gaps visible in the § 1.2 and § 2.3 quotations are that deletion, not the authors' text. **A zero string count for an italicised token is an instrument reading, never a negative.** The zeros reported in § 2.3 are for **roman-type** tokens (mTOR, rapamycin, p70S6K, 4E-BP), and those zeros are informative; they are labelled as such at the point of use.
- **No figure image is inspectable.** Per rule **D-14**, no negative asserted only by a figure has been adjudicated anywhere in this document. In particular, the Beclin-1/p62 quantification in PMID 28045433 lives in a figure and its panel reference was deleted by the extractor; it is reported as the authors' prose describes it and is not adjudicated.
- **Autophagic flux was not assessed for any paper**, because no primary paper was read. Whether any of the four direction-stating papers clamped flux (bafilomycin, chloroquine, mCherry-GFP-LC3) or measured only steady-state LC3-II is **unknown and is not claimed in either direction**. For PMID 28045433 the authors' own prose says flux was not established.
- **The census is bounded by three queries.** It does not cover WWOX and cancer generally, does not follow citation networks, and does not cover preprints or non-PubMed-indexed sources. A paper using neither "autophagy", "mTOR", nor the four marker names in its indexed fields would be missed.
- **Affiliations were taken from PubMed author records**, which give the affiliation at publication only. Lab membership is inferred from senior author and institution; it is not verified against funding records.
