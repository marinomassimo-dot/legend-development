## §3 · Q3 — THE ASYMMETRY, TESTED

**The proposition put to me:** *equal specific activity per µg is informative (the fold must be adequate);
reduced specific activity is ambiguous between fold and species composition.*

### 3.1 The algebra, written out

Let a preparation of a variant have total mass **M**, of which fraction **f** is competent in the assay, each
competent molecule contributing intrinsic activity **a**, at molecular mass **m**. Measured specific activity
**S = A/M = f·a/m**. Comparing variant to wild type:

**S_var / S_WT = (f_var · a_var) / (f_WT · a_WT)**

- **Reduced S is ambiguous.** `S_var < S_WT` is satisfied by *f* down, by *a* down, or by any product of the
  two. Nothing in the measurement separates them. 🟢 **The second half of the proposition is CORRECT and
  needs no qualification.**
- **Equal S is informative — but only conditionally.** `S_var = S_WT` gives `f_var·a_var = f_WT·a_WT`, hence
  `f_var = f_WT·(a_WT/a_var)`. To read that as *"the fold is adequate"* you must add **a_var ≤ a_WT**.

**🟢 VERDICT: the asymmetry HOLDS — and it holds for a reason stronger than the one offered.** What `S_var =
S_WT` establishes directly, with no added premise at all, is **function per unit mass is preserved** — and
that quantity *is* the thing condition (d) was asking for. The inference to *folding* is the weaker, derived
one. **A one-directional readout is still a readout, and this one points in the direction the therapeutic
question needs**: it is the direction that says *the protein you managed to make is working*.

### 3.2 🔴 Three conditions the proposition omits, and one of them nearly inverts it

**A1 · The assay must run SUB-SATURATING — and the primary's decisive panel did not.**
At enzyme excess, both wild type and a partially competent variant drive the output to zero, and equal output
means nothing. The paper's own wording is *"completely abolished in the presence of **excess** WWOX"* — read
verbatim by me this session. **Reconstructing the molar ratio from the paper's own arithmetic (§5.2): WWOX ≈
208 nM, GSK3β ≈ 31 nM, i.e. ≈ 6.7 : 1 molar excess.** So the single-point panel — the only one with stated
masses — sits **in the regime where the asymmetry does not work**, and the dose-response panel — the only one
in the right regime — **states no masses at all.** `INFERENZA`, resting on §5.2's volume derivation.
🎯 **This is the sharpest operational consequence in the file: the informative window is BELOW the published
operating point, at roughly 1:1 to 3:1, i.e. ≈ 0.05–0.15 µg GST-WWOX per reaction.**

**A2 · The non-competent fraction must be INERT — and for an INHIBITION readout that is not free.**
The algebra assumes misfolded molecules contribute mass to the denominator and nothing to the numerator.
For a readout whose positive direction is the **production** of a signal, that assumption is nearly automatic.
🔴 **This readout's positive direction is the DISAPPEARANCE of someone else's signal.** Any nonspecific sink —
an aggregate adsorbing GSK3β, Tau or ATP — **counterfeits inhibition**. A badly folded preparation could
therefore return *equal or greater* apparent specific activity, which attacks the **informative** direction,
not the ambiguous one. `PREMISE: INFERENZA` (general biochemistry; no WWOX measurement bears on it).
🟢 **Remedy, and it is cheap:** (i) clarify before assay — 0.22 µm filtration plus a hard spin, which
operationally redefines the denominator as **clarified soluble protein**; (ii) a **matched-mass GST-only**
arm, which the paper already runs; (iii) a **matched-mass `L404A`** arm, which the paper already runs and
which is the folded-but-docking-dead control the design needs.
🔴 **What the remedy does NOT do:** `L404A` is asserted folded on a **single-partner** c-Jun co-IP, which the
repository already flags as weak; and a folded control does not model a *misfolded* test article's sink
behaviour. Clarification is the load-bearing step, not the control.
🎯 **Restated as the correction condition (d) actually needs: criterion 4 does not require the folded fraction
to be COUNTED. It requires the non-competent fraction to be INERT — and inertness can be engineered by
removing aggregate rather than measured by quantifying it.** That is the sentence this repository did not have.

**A3 · The premise `a_var ≤ a_WT`.** A gain of intrinsic docking affinity is implausible for a core proline
substitution but is not excluded. `PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK` — **a research target, not a foundation.**
Consequence: equal S licenses *"function per unit mass preserved"* unconditionally, and *"the fold is
adequate"* only under A3.

## §4 · Q4 — RECOMBINANT vs PATIENT-CELL, AND THE CIRCLE

**The circle is real, and only on one side.**

**Patient fibroblasts.** You need protein to test protein, and the protein may not be there. `Q230P`'s single
protein datum is *"protein not detected"* on a Western blot — `PREMISE: UNREAD_PRIMARY`, abstract-depth, from
a body nobody here has opened. Below the blot floor, **every** WWOX readout returns the same number as a
complete null, and that number is uninformative. Worse, the denominator itself is unobtainable: no WWOX
targeted-MS absolute quantification exists, no thermal-stability measurement exists, and **nobody has ever
looked in the pellet for any WWOX allele**, so you cannot even state which pool a denominator would be
counting. 🔴 **Folded-monomer normalisation in a patient cell is not expensive. It is undefined.** B's
*"at any price"* is exactly right **about this half** and should be preserved as a statement about cells.

**Recombinant GST-WWOX-Q230P in BL21(DE3).** There is **no circle**, because **you make the protein.**
Expression is set by a T7 promoter, not by a human cell's quality control. The route is not hypothetical:
Wang 2012's Methods state that `ww1 (1–60)`, `ww2 (40–110)`, `ww (1–110)` and **`ADH (110–414)` — a construct
that CONTAINS residue 230 — were expressed as GST fusions and affinity-purified on glutathione beads**, and
the ADH matrix pulled down GSK3β while three other fragments and GST alone did not. **A third heterologous
WWOX expression exists** (`GST-SDR` fragment, `PMID 27869163`). "Never been expressed" is too strong;
**"never been purified to a stated yield, never been characterised"** is exact.

### What the recombinant route CAN answer about the patient allele

1. **Does the `Q230P` polypeptide partition soluble in a host with no mammalian quality control?** Soluble
   supernatant versus inclusion-body pellet, wild type and mutant side by side in one host. 🟢 **No mammalian
   experiment can do this**, because in a mammalian cell intrinsic folding failure and cellular clearance are
   entangled by construction.
2. **Does the soluble `Q230P` species inhibit GSK3β per µg as wild type does?** — the (d4) measurement.
3. **A real denominator, in three ascending tiers, all available on the bench:** total protein (today) →
   **clarified soluble protein** (one filter, one spin) → **monomeric species with a measured cooperative
   unfolding transition** (one analytical SEC run plus nanoDSF; standard instruments, days not years).
   🎯 **And the third tier closes the ambiguous direction**: collect the SEC monomer peak and assay *that*,
   and the denominator **is** monomer — so a *reduced* specific activity on a monomeric preparation with a
   wild-type-like cooperative Tm becomes a positive statement, **"folded and inert"**, instead of an
   ambiguity. **This is the cheapest route to criterion 4 as literally written, and this repository has never
   proposed it.** ⚠️ Residual: a molten globule can run as a monomer, and Tm equality is not structural
   identity — so the claim ceiling is *"monomeric, cooperatively folding, and inert"*, not *"natively folded"*.

### What the recombinant route CANNOT answer — and this is the hard part

1. 🔴 **Whether any `Q230P` protein exists in a patient's neurons.** That is set by human synthesis,
   co-translational folding, chaperone availability and clearance — **none of which exists in *E. coli***.
   A recombinant "active per µg" result is **fully compatible with a patient in whom there is no protein at
   all.** It does not answer "inert or absent"; it **relocates** the question from *inert?* to *present?*,
   and leaves the second entirely open. Anyone who reads it as a rescue signal has made the abundance error
   in a new costume.
2. 🔴 **Whether the SDR core is folded.** §3.3. A terminal linear motif presented on a GST fusion is not a
   core-fold report — and GST is itself a solubilising partner, which is precisely the confound.
3. 🔴 **Whether bacterial folding predicts neuronal folding.** No human chaperones, no Golgi, no PTMs.
   A `Q230P` that partitions soluble in *E. coli* has **not** been shown to fold in a neuron. It is a
   **prior**, at best.

## §6 · ONE COMPRESSED EXPERIMENT

> ## **A mass-matched, sub-saturating GSK3β-inhibition titration of recombinant GST-WWOX `WT` / `Q230P` / `L404A` / `GST`, with the denominator defined as CLARIFIED SOLUBLE protein and, on the same preparations, the SEC monomer peak assayed separately.**

**Why this one.** It is the only design that runs the readout that already passes criteria 1–3 (`A1`/`A2`),
on the allele nobody has tested, in the regime where the asymmetry is informative, with a denominator that
ascends from total → clarified-soluble → monomeric-with-a-Tm — **and it is the only route in this node where
the circularity of Q4 does not apply, because the protein is made rather than found.**

**The design, compressed.**
1. **Express in parallel** in BL21(DE3), one induction, one buffer, four constructs × two spans: full-length
   WWOX (the construct with the published readout) and `ADH (110–414)` (which contains residue 230 **and** the
   388–407 motif, and which is the construct the paper showed pulls down GSK3β). Alleles: `WT`, **`Q230P`**,
   `L404A` (folded, docking-dead), `GST` alone (the matched-mass sink control).
   ⚠️ `P282A` (`rs3764340`) may appear **only** as a direction-only assay-sensitivity check, never as a
   complete-loss anchor and never to set a scale. `Q230G` is excluded entirely.
2. **Report the three numbers nobody reports for any WWOX construct:** soluble yield per litre, SEC monomer

   because a preparation carrying aggregate misreports A₂₈₀ and the denominator is the whole point.

   Substrate: **GS-1 peptide as primary** (continuous, quantitative, rate-per-µg is its native output) with a
   **Tau S396/S404 arm** retained as the disease-relevant, historically-anchored comparator.

6. **Then assay the collected SEC monomer peak.** This is the step that converts the ambiguous direction into
