> **Step 0 — free, and ours to take.** Read the **already-published** cerebellar panel of Repudi 2021

### What Step 0 **can** answer
🟢 Whether the **Purkinje cell layer is in frame** in the published cerebellar field at all.
🟢 Whether the field shows cerebellar **cortex** (three layers) rather than a deep-nuclei or
white-matter field. 🟢 Whether **any** WWOX-channel signal appears in **large somata at the boundary
between the NeuN-bright granular layer and the NeuN-dark molecular layer** — the row where Purkinje
somata sit. In a `Wwox`-**null** host under a **neuron-restricted** promoter, WWOX signal is
transgene-derived and cannot be glial, so the sibling's asymmetric-identification argument applies
unchanged: **a positive reading in that row is informative; a negative reading is not.**

### What Step 0 **cannot** answer
🔴 Any **quantity**. A published panel is a contrast-adjusted, compressed reproduction of one field
selected by the authors for a different purpose; the paper states images *"included the global changes
of brightness and contrast"*. No per-cell intensity, no positive fraction, no denominator, no `n`.
🔴 **Identity.** No Purkinje marker is present — layer occupancy is not cell identity, and Bergmann
glia and candelabrum cells share the row. 🔴 **Generality.** One field, one arm, one age.
🔴 Anything about **TX-007's** doses (`1.23E11` / `2.63E11`): this is a 2E10-per-hemisphere experiment.

### What Step 1b **can** answer
🎯 **Promoter activity in a Purkinje cell, in vivo, with no anti-WWOX antibody and no wild-type
baseline** — because GFP has no endogenous counterpart, the reporter readout is baseline-free by
construction, which is precisely the property every WWOX-channel readout lacks. If large somata in the
Purkinje row are EGFP-positive, then `hSynI` **is** active in Purkinje cells in vivo and branch `E1`
(*"they are transduced and nobody looked"*) is directly supported.

### What Step 1b **cannot** answer
🔴 **WWOX protein**. EGFP reports promoter activity and vector arrival collapsed together, not
transgene product. 🔴 **The therapeutic dose.** This is 2E10/hemisphere; TX-007 is 5–13× higher, and no
transfer is licensed in either direction. 🔴 A **negative** reading. An EGFP-dark soma may be
untransduced, may be a Bergmann glial cell (which `hSynI` cannot drive **by construction**), or may be
a Purkinje soma the 12–14 µm cut mostly missed. 🔴 **Sensitivity is unbounded below**: `NOT DETECTED`
is a detection-floor statement, and the floor's height is unstated.

### 2.1b 🆕 FIRST-HAND — the 2021 study also produced whole-slide scans, **and it had confocal**

Verbatim, 2021 Methods, *Image acquisition and analysis*:

2. 🎯 **The 2021 material was imaged confocally as well.** This matters specifically for this question:

3. 🟢 **Image analysis was blinded to genotype and used ImageJ** — a named, reproducible analysis

⚠️ **Bound.** Which sections went to which instrument is `NOT STATED`, and whether cerebellar fields were

### 2.5b 🆕 Why no homogenate — DNA, RNA or protein — can answer the Purkinje question, stated as arithmetic

This is the *"say plainly when they cannot"* the brief asks for, and it deserves a number rather than
the word *"low-resolution"*.

**DATO, retrieved first-hand.** Rat, stereological: *"The rat cerebellum therefore contains **3.38 × 10⁵
Purkinje cells** and **9.2 × 10⁷ granule cells**, from which there are **274 granule cells for each
Purkinje cell**"* (Harvey & Napper 1988, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/cne.902740202)). Human,
stereological: *"total Purkinje cell number being **28 × 10⁶** … and total granule cell number
**109 × 10⁹**"* (Andersen *et al.* 2003, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/cne.10884)) — a ratio of ≈1:3,900.

**INFERENZA, arithmetic and explicit.** Purkinje cells are therefore **0.36 % (rat) to 0.026 % (human)**
of cerebellar cortical neurons, and the true share is smaller still because glia and molecular-layer
interneurons also occupy the denominator.

> 🔴 ⇒ **In a vector-genome assay whose input is a fixed DNA *mass* — so whose denominator is *nuclei* —
> the complete absence of vector from every Purkinje cell would depress a whole-cerebellum number by
> **≲0.4 %**. That is far below any qPCR's replicate variation.** The cerebellar-versus-cortical vDNA
> deficit on the record is **8.75–12.05×**, i.e. ~88–92 % — three orders of magnitude larger than the
> entire Purkinje compartment could produce. **The bulk vDNA result is a statement about granule-cell
> nuclei; it can neither be caused by Purkinje cells nor reveal anything about them.** This is not a
> limitation of anyone's execution — it is arithmetic, and it holds for every laboratory equally.

⚠️ 🔴 **The honest carve-out, which I decline to paper over.** This argument is **clean only for the
nucleus-denominated assay**. A **protein** homogenate is denominated in total protein, and a Purkinje
cell's share of cerebellar protein is dominated by an enormous dendritic arbour filling the molecular
layer, not by a soma — **that share is not 1/275 and I retrieved no measurement of it**, so I state no
number for it. An **RNA** homogenate sits between the two and is likewise unquantified. ⇒ For protein
and RNA the correct record is *"cannot resolve a cell type and the magnitude of the dilution is
unmeasured"*, **not** the vDNA arithmetic transferred across. `REVIVAL_TRIGGER` below.
⚠️ Species bound: **rat and human, no mouse value retrieved.** Both bracket the mouse far below 1 %,
and I decline to complete the mouse figure from background.
