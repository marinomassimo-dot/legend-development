## CLAIM 003
**Title:** Neuronal WWOX deletion induces non-cell-autonomous hypomyelination
**Status:** consolidated baseline
**Type:** DATO
**Pathway:** P4 — myelination / white matter
**Genotype/model relevance:** murino / delezione neuronale condizionale; non null/null sistemico; genotipo caution moderata; meccanismo mielinizzazione plausibilmente trasferibile
**Transferability:** T2
**clinical relevance:** MODERATE
**Summary:** Neuronal WWOX loss impairs myelination despite presence of OPCs.
**Clinical meaning:** Justifies MRI + DTI before discussing myelination adjuncts.
**Source:** Repudi et al., 2021, *Brain*
**Evidence boundary — quanto lontano arriva «non-cell-autonoma» (2026-08-10, `BATCH_20260810_005`):** il lavoro gemello del 2021 dallo stesso laboratorio ([[paper_registry_current#PAPER 005]], PMID 34747138, letto integralmente) rafforza il meccanismo e ne segna il limite nello stesso gesto. **Rafforza:** gli oligodendrociti **non sono mai trasdotti** dal vettore neurone-specifico, verificato con co-staining CC1/anti-WWOX, e la mielinizzazione migliora comunque — è la prova diretta che l'effetto passa per i neuroni. **Limita:** il recupero è **incompleto**, e gli autori attribuiscono il residuo a *«an oligodendrocyte-specific WWOX function»*. Quindi *non-cell-autonoma* descrive **una componente**, non l'intero fenomeno; una funzione WWOX autonoma dell'oligodendrocita resta aperta e non è esclusa. `PREMISE_TAG`: l'attribuzione del residuo agli oligodendrociti è **`IPOTESI` degli autori**; il divario residuo è `DATO`. Non promuovibile senza una delezione o un rescue Olig2/CNP-specifici. 🔴 **Quella delezione ora esiste, e non discharge nulla (2026-10-03, `CC-20261003W4-B-MYELIN-CELLAUT-01`).** Un preprint bioRxiv **non sottoposto a peer review** (PPR1124524, doi 10.1101/2025.11.22.689900, v1 2025-11-24, letto 2026-10-03, ricevuta `FTR-20261003-PPR1124524-01`) riporta una delezione condizionale Olig2-Cre di *Wwox*. Un preprint non alza mai lo stato di una claim, e il laboratorio è **lo stesso** di `PAPER 004`: non è un osservatore indipendente. **Title:** The three direct WWOX read-outs a published protocol demonstrates are Tier 1 modalities and none of them is a validated biomarker
**Status:** in observation
**Type:** DATO (le proprietà di misura) + INFERENZA (l'assegnazione di tier §13, che è una classificazione, non un reperto)
**Pathway:** P9 — measurement / assay specification
**Genotype/model relevance:** none — canine cutaneous mast cell tumour tissue and canine/murine mast cell lines; **no WWOX variant, no zygosity, no CNS tissue and no body fluid**
**Transferability:** T4 as biology; **T1 as an assay specification and as a ceiling on it**
**clinical relevance:** MODERATE — **`actionability: none`**: it states what the published protocol can and cannot do, so that a future reading cannot treat "WWOX immunohistochemistry" as an available endpoint
**Summary:** The three direct read-outs of WWOX that a published protocol actually demonstrates end to end — **transcript** by relative comparative-Ct, **protein** by immunoblot densitometry, and **protein in fixed tissue** by immunohistochemistry — are **Tier 1 modalities** under [`LEGEND_CORE.md` §13](../../../framework/instruction/LEGEND_CORE.md#13-biomarker-scope-hard-rule), and **none of them is a validated biomarker for any disease population**. In the one source that specifies all three ([[paper_registry_current#PAPER 223]], PMID 33129329) **no limit of detection, linear range, calibrator, sensitivity, specificity or cut-off is stated for any of them**; the transcript assay is normalised to 18S and reports a fold change within a run, never a concentration; the protein assay divides the WWOX band by β-actin on the same stripped and reprobed membrane, 47 kDa against 43 kDa; and **WWOX enzymatic activity was not measured at all**.
**The measured ceiling, which is the operative number:** the tissue read-out — the most clinically deployable of the three — is an ordinal score *«subjectively scored from 0 to 3»* by two boarded pathologists, and the authors measured its reliability themselves: *«There was fair interrater agreement for both staining intensity and percent of cells stained (K = 0.264 and 0.273, respectively)»*. **The transfer that IS warranted is the negative one:** a protocol that could not exceed *fair* agreement with unlimited tissue and expert readers does not become more precise in a harder matrix.
**Bound — the reagent, stated exactly.** The immunohistochemistry antiserum *«detects endogenous and recombinant human and murine WWOX amino acid residues 12–94»* and was applied across a species barrier on a stated homology argument (*«94.4% similarity at the protein level»*, *«96% sequence homology»* across the epitope). ⚠️ **Integrator amendment (blind audit, CONTRADICTED verdict repaired at source):** the candidate called this *«a homology argument rather than a validation»*, and that is **false** — the paper states a canine positive tissue control (*«Normal canine testes tissue»*) and an isotype-matched negative control. What is true, and what this claim asserts, is narrower: **no peptide-block, knockdown or orthogonal-detection validation of the antiserum in the canine species is reported**, and the only negative control for the *tissue* read-out is a reagent control, never a genetic one — the paper owns shWWOX knockdown lines but uses them for qRT-PCR, immunoblot and viability work only, never as an immunohistochemistry control.
**Bound — the blot.** ⚠️ A second amendment: on the uncropped membrane more than one immunoreactive species is present in tumour lysate and the reported band is selected by an arrow; **two of the five tumour lanes show no band at the arrow at all**. A densitometric ratio computed on an arrow-selected band in a multi-band lane is a measurement of the arrow as much as of the protein.
**Bound — the power.** ⚠️ A third amendment: the source says only *«This was a pilot study … so no power calculation was made»*. **The absence of a power calculation is not a declaration of being underpowered**, and this claim does not make it one.
**Clinical meaning:** None, and that is the point. There is **no validated WWOX biomarker** in this corpus; `claim_registry_current` was searched for `biomarker` and `biomarcatore` across all 45 pre-existing records and matched **zero**, so this claim narrows and reverses nothing — it is a floor, stated so that a future positive claim has to clear a measured bar rather than a vague one. **Non è parere medico.**

**REVIVAL_TRIGGER / condizione per superare questa claim:** a WWOX assay reporting a **concentration against a recombinant calibrator**, with a stated limit of detection and linear range, in any matrix obtainable from a living patient; or any published **inter-rater agreement above κ 0.6** for WWOX immunohistochemistry. Either would supersede this record. **What would falsify it as stated:** a source reporting sensitivity and specificity for a WWOX read-out in a disease population — which would move the modality from Tier 1 *class* to Tier 1 *validated*.
