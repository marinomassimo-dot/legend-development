## RC-013 — Experimental validation of Q230P instability
**Status:** high interest — actionable
**Linked research lines:** [[research_lines_current#RL-BIOM-001 — WWOX functional-state biomarkers|RL-BIOM-001]] / [[claim_registry_current#CLAIM 019]]
**Rationale:** the convergent in-silico prediction (AlphaFold + ThermoMPNN +1.51 kcal/mol + ESM-2 rank 1/19) indicates Q230P as a strong LoF via protein instability, but it remains a prediction. It must be confirmed by direct measurement. Two routes: (1) computational ΔΔG (FoldX/Rosetta) — the ThermoMPNN/ESM-2 cross-check already covers this at a robust HYPOTHESIS level; (2) **priority** — a cellular WWOX protein stability/quantity test in **LCL or variant-carrying primary fibroblasts** (MODERATE proxy per GTEx); whole blood excluded (WEAK proxy). A reduced WWOX-protein readout at normal mRNA localizes the lesion downstream of transcription, but **does not by itself discriminate impaired translation from insolubility or from premature degradation — the cause remains unresolved** and needs metabolic labelling, solubility fractionation and a chase. It would nonetheless be the first direct functional WWOX biomarker (the only route yielding an experimental DATO).
**Priority:** MEDIUM-HIGH
**Clinical status:** research only
**Next action:** design a minimal protocol on variant-carrying primary fibroblasts/LCL combining, in a single sample, (a) Western/quantification of WWOX protein (Q230P stability) and (b) allele-specific RT-PCR ± NMD block for the splice allele c.1057-2A>G — one sample, two readouts.
