## 3 · What the three new readings say, and for which P5 row

* **41378214, GAT-1 R44Q (and R44P, R44W).** The three variants reach an Endo H-resistant band comparable to wild type and show plasma-membrane signal, and **have no appreciable uptake** (Figure 1A, 2A): surface presence does not carry function. In HEK293 cells 4-phenylbutyrate, glycerol and HSP inhibitors give R44Q uptake of about zero (Figure 2D); proteasome inhibitors and trichostatin A raise it to **about 3 to 6 percent of untreated wild-type uptake** while the same treatments raise **wild-type uptake about two- to three-fold** (Figures 4A-D, 5A-B). The text says the trichostatin result is statistically insignificant and the panel marks it significant (relation declared); the supplementary figure shows the "1.4-fold" surface rise as a mean of three points one of which is below 1, without significance; the abstract's "partially rescued function" compares with these few-percent values. In the fly (astrocyte expression), R44Q expression is low; knockdown of two proteasome subunits and the compounds raise the signal in **single unquantified confocal planes**; 4-phenylbutyrate lowers the seizure-like fraction (about 36 to 16 percent, one star) although it did not rescue uptake in cells, a discrepancy the authors call unclear; no treated wild-type-fly control is shown. For rows **P4-A-01** and **P4-A-02**: one more case where the class and the proteasome route act non-selectively (wild type is raised alongside) and where protein/surface presence and function separate; the same laboratory family as the earlier G443 reading (40485335), so the two count as one origin for any statement about this class and GAT-1.
* **41373471, SIL1 p.E342K (homozygous missense, patient fibroblasts).** The nearest match to the Q230P premise among the R1 papers read so far: **mRNA about two-fold above control (log2 +1.05), protein about 0.18 of control in one blot and about 0.45 across three experiments (the two panels disagree on the size of the loss), the proteasome inhibitor brings the patient line to about the control level (about 1.07) and leaves the control line unchanged** (Figure 5A, one star, three experiments, one patient, one control line); in COS7 the variant is about 10 times lower in the native-soluble fraction and the medium while the urea-solubilised pellet is **not lower (about 2.5 against 1, wide spread)** — a pool that is made and insoluble, not absent. Limits the authors state themselves: one patient, no rescue experiment in fibroblasts; **no function readout** (the co-chaperone activity was not measured); the text's "10 times less" is not what either figure shows; the lysosomal co-localisation after the inhibitor is partial, unquantified, and present in control cells too. For row **P4-A-02**: a second patient-derived precedent for "normal or higher transcript, low protein, partial-to-full return with a proteasome inhibitor, selectively in the variant line" from a **different laboratory** than the HIKESHI study, still without a function readout. It does not change the card's state (`hypothesis`, a probe, not an agent); it adds an independent origin for the premise pattern.
* **42569303, neuroserpin Portland variant (ER lumen, soluble, glycosylated).** A route-labelling design applied to one misfolded client: lysosomal-endolysosome trapping with a V-ATPase inhibitor (about 12 to about 50 percent of endolysosomes positive), knockout panels (ATG5, ATG7, FAM134B, STX17 reduce; SEC62 and FIP200 do not), a glycan-site mutant (only the glycan at 321 matters). Its authors' own general statement is that large polymerising polypeptides **cannot** be dislocated for proteasomal clearance, and the paper has **no proteasome-inhibitor arm**; it measures delivery to a compartment, not the share of the pool cleared by it; panel H (FIP200) has two replicates and the workbook lists one value per cell with no replicate column, so the unit of the tests is not recoverable from it; the insoluble-fraction rise is as large for N157Q as for N321Q (Figure 6D, no test marked), so it does not track the delivery defect specifically. For rows **P4-A-04/05**: a demonstrated instance of "misfolded → ERAD" being false for one client (the default recorded in `epistemic_discipline.md`), measurable by the route design in the paper; a transfer to a **cytosolic** enzyme is by general knowledge only (a signal-peptide-less protein has no ER-lumen N-glycan route; `DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK`, a research target, not a premise).

## 4 · The Q230P dependence: the one measurement that would split it

The P5 comparison found **seven of twelve deep rows resting on one Q230P measurement** (CLAIM 019: one patient fibroblast line, "transcript comparable, protein not detected", one single-laboratory study). What would split that dependence, citing only what these readings show:

**A fractionated, allele-resolved measurement of the Q230P protein in a source independent of that fibroblast line, with and without a proteasome inhibitor, with wild type treated alongside.** Soluble (native lysis) and urea-solubilised pellet fractions beside the total, a wild-type comparator in the same experiment, protein and mRNA read together, and the number of independent replicates stated per condition.

Why that one, from the papers read: (a) the SIL1 study shows the paired readout (mRNA, soluble, pellet, inhibitor in patient and control line) separating "not made" from "made and insoluble" from "made and proteasome-sensitive", which is the branch table of cards A01, A02, A04, A05 and C03; (b) the GAT-1 R44Q study shows why wild type must be treated alongside (the inhibitors raised wild type two- to three-fold; a rise in the variant alone is not selectivity) and why a quantity is not a function; (c) the same studies show the weakness to avoid: one patient line, one blot, and the loss changing from 0.18 to 0.45 of control between two panels of the same paper. A second independent source turns the premise from one origin into two; the fractions decide which of the five rows keeps a stated target. **It does not supply the function readout none of these papers has.**
