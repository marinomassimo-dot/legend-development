**Standing statement for every row below.** There is no WWOX-deficient evidence on any of these interventions (transfusion, iron, plasma, FFP, plasma exchange, albumin, IVIG). Every paper in this note is a substitute: adjacent biology, a different disease class, or a clinical-practice paper in another population. None is WWOX evidence, and a substitute is read for what transfers and what does not. Caution (BLOCK-1): nothing here is a recommendation for or against any intervention in a child with WWOX-related disease.

### 42712566 Sinha 2026 (H1, tier 1) - a PROTOCOL, not a review

### 42313692 Bailey 2026 (H1, tier 3) - liberal versus restrictive haemoglobin thresholds and cerebral oxygenation, meta-analysis
Pools seven NIRS studies: crSO2 rises 6.40 points (restrictive) versus 2.75 (liberal), cFTOE falls 0.08 versus 0.03, certainty very low (I-squared 0.93). Does not support a threshold effect: the one randomised within-trial contrast (TOP arms) is 4.48 versus 4.84; the pooled gap is between-study (small-variance observational studies on one side, two liberal studies one with baseline crSO2 77-78 on the other); Aktas is classed restrictive with week-1 and week-2 thresholds equal to the TOP high-threshold values (cross-document reading with the TOP NIRS supplement); certainty stated as low in Results and very low in the Limitations and GRADE table; no outcome benefit shown; rise may be transient. H1 question 3: readout answered, no WWOX bearing. Figures 1-3, supplementary risk-of-bias images and GRADE table read. Depth: complete. No commit candidate.
