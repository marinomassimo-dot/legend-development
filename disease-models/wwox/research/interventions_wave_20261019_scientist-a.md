### 42712566 Sinha 2026 (H1, tier 1) - a PROTOCOL, not a review
Measures nothing: it is a plan for a systematic review of iron supplementation, myelination and neurodevelopment in children 0-3 years. The selection file calls it "a systematic review"; the title and body say protocol. It changes nothing in the model. Two negatives matter: (1) it cannot answer H1 question 2 (is WWOX-null hypomyelination modifiable by iron?), and (2) its eligibility text excludes participants with significant congenital neurological abnormalities, so a DEE cohort would probably not be in the eventual review. ### 39239479 Porras 2024 (H1, tier 2) - Irp2-null mouse behaviour (iron misregulation comparator)
Measures a constitutive Irp2-null mouse battery (rotarod about 40 percent shorter latency, lighter animals with weight-covariate-dependent hang-time result, reduced thermal nociception, reversal-specific learning deficit, n = 6 per group in the Barnes maze). Does not measure haemoglobin, iron content, seizures or any intervention; the human anaemia and epilepsy of IREB2 disease are cited context. By the authors' own statement the mouse is much milder than the human disease. For the model: nothing changes; it is a severity-discordance comparator, not WWOX evidence (the Wwox-null mouse is the severe side of that discordance). Figures 1, 8, 11 opened; supplement legends only (declared debt: eight TIFFs and the peer-review PDF). Depth: partial. No commit candidate.

### 34909297 Padda 2021 (H1, tier 2) - anaemia, epilepsy and antiseizure drugs, narrative review
Says (cited, nothing new): anaemia meets epilepsy through sickle cell disease, iron deficiency (contradictory for febrile seizures) and antiseizure-drug marrow, immune, oxidative and folate/B12 mechanisms; management is stop the drug, count monitoring, folic acid, transfusion where haemolysis is severe. Does not support: any disease-versus-drug separation in a DEE cohort, any WWOX or genetic-epilepsy anaemia datum, any effect of transfusion on seizures. Its two seizure-threshold statements contradict each other. Changes nothing in the model; H1 question 5 answered only as "both occur, in other populations". Figure 1 (a lifestyle graphic) opened. Depth: complete. No commit candidate.
