## 0 - The premises being qualified (read first, from the three recovered dossiers)

The dossiers sit under `vps_recovery_20260925/residue_q230p_20260922/`; their headers declare `UNREAD_PREMISE` and historic status. Nothing in them was relied on as evidence here. The premises each one carries are, in my own words:

| Id | Premise (what a design depends on) | Dossier |
|---|---|---|
| **PA** | A published "normal transcript, absent protein" for a missense allele may be an extraction artefact: the protein may be present but outside the mild-lysis soluble fraction (insoluble, or invisible to the antibody), so a three-fraction split (soluble S, strong-denaturant pellet P, spin-free total T) plus an orthogonal non-antibody read is the minimum architecture that separates "absent" from "absent from S". Five branches are enumerated: not synthesised (H1), synthesised then cleared (H2), synthesised and insoluble (H3), soluble but epitope-invisible (H4), truncated or cleaved (H5). | fractionation and orthogonal detection |
| **PB** | The deficit can be placed before, during or after the measurable mature pool; a cycloheximide chase started from an undetectable baseline has almost no discriminating power, so the order is inhibitor arm first, chase second; a two-epitope puromycin-PLA ratio is the one instrument that separates "during" from "before" and "after"; the soluble/insoluble split and an inhibitor arm are complements, not alternatives. | nascent-synthesis discriminator |
| **PC** | A systemic rescue premise (neuron-directed replacement versus peripheral phenotypes) that a proteostasis modulator is a rescue route whose site of action matters. R1 papers can speak to it only through exposure and brain-penetrance facts of the classes they test; it is not otherwise addressable by this group and is reported as such. | systemic rescue |

## 1 - Per-paper readings (tier order)

### 30266908 - Guiberson 2018, Munc18-1 missense destabilisation and chemical chaperones - NOT WWOX EVIDENCE

Receipt `FTR-20261021-30266908-01` (partial_fulltext_read: supplementary figures are captions_only except two pages opened as images; all eight main figures opened as images), manifest PASS, 16 locators. Primary, the nearest published precedent for the fractionation premise.

- **What it establishes (DATO, in this system).** Five missense variants of Munc18-1 (STXBP1) give total protein below 10 percent of wild type for four of five in primary Munc18-1-null neurons (Figure 3a, read from the image: P335L about 65, the other four about 4 to 8), with the transgene mRNA unchanged (Supplementary Figure 4a-c, opened). Turnover is faster by cycloheximide chase and by Dendra2 photoconversion; the 0.1 percent Triton-soluble fraction falls from about 80 to 25-45 percent (Figure 4a); aggregates are visible, not cleared at the soluble rate and not toxic.
- **The fractionation lane exists and works.** The soluble/insoluble split runs a 13,000 g spin and loads the pellet beside the supernatant at equal volume; total is a sonicated, boiled Laemmli lysate. This is an S / P / T arrangement in a missense protein, and the totals are low in the direct lysate: the loss is **not** merely an S-fraction artefact here.
- **Bearing on PA.** The branch that dominates in Munc18-1 is H2-like (fast turnover, low total) with a minority H3 pool. It is not a precedent for "protein present, only invisible in S". Munc18-1 mutant protein is also hard to see in neurons: the authors state that detectable protein for the mutants "were very low in neurons" and moved the chase to HEK293T and Neuro2a cells, which is the regime problem PB names (a chase needs a detectable baseline) and the cost of solving it (overexpressed cDNA in non-neuronal cells).
- **Bearing on PB.** No proteasome or lysosome inhibitor arm is reported (0 occurrences of MG132 or bafilomycin in the article XML; the only chloroquine is the transfection step). So the paper measures turnover but not its route, and cannot place the loss on the proteasome or the lysosome. Its own reference list carries a Munc18-1 C180Y proteasomal-degradation paper that does (carried to the adjacent-search reserve).
- **Quantity versus function: measured together, and not in proportion.** Figure 6a normalises each variant's treated bars to that variant's **own** untreated control (control fixed at 100): chaperones raise total mutant protein about 1.1 to 1.7 fold from a baseline below 10 percent of wild type, and the soluble fraction rises (Figure 6b, for example G544D from about 30 to about 68 percent with one compound), while the vesicle-recycling read-out is reported restored to wild-type level (Figure 6d: G544D about 33 untreated to about 75 treated against untreated WT about 55). Function recovered to wild-type level while total protein, on the arithmetic of these two figures, remains a small fraction of wild type (INFERENZA: the figure does not plot treated mutant as a fraction of wild type). Either the assay saturates well below wild-type protein, or the soluble pool rather than the total is what counts; the paper cannot tell which. It also means the quantity read-out alone would have under-called the functional rescue.
- **Chaperones are not mutant-specific.** The same screen raised wild-type Munc18-1 (Supplementary Figure 12a, opened: wild-type to about 180 percent with the phenylbutyrate class and about 195 with sodium butyrate). Both are HDAC-inhibitor-class compounds acting on a transfected cDNA, and no mRNA was measured under treatment, so a transcriptional contribution is not excluded here (the SLC6A1 paper in this lot, 42157447, tests exactly this).
- **What it does not support.** Nothing about an enzyme, a recessive homozygous allele, a protein expressed from its endogenous promoter, or an exposure window in a mammal: the rescue is cell culture and worms fed the compounds for two to three generations. The dominant-negative half (wild type co-aggregated, solubility down about 25 percent) is specific to a hetero-oligomeric client and does not transfer to a monomeric SDR enzyme.
- **Transfer verdict (class level).** For PA: partial support for the S/P/T architecture as a workable assay, and a measured case in which the pellet is a minority fate; for PB: supports "chase needs a detectable baseline" and "route needs an inhibitor arm" by showing the paper that skipped it. NOT WWOX EVIDENCE.
