## 1 · What changes

`CLAIM 002` (**Status: consolidated baseline**) currently reads, in its Summary:

> «… dyslamination corticale; firma OXPHOS↓/glicolisi↑; firing aumentato **e normalizzato dal rescue**.»

and in Clinical meaning:

> «Strongly supports the network-level model, la terapia genica e l'uso degli organoidi come piattaforma sperimentale.»

**Nothing in the claim is reversed and no evidence is demoted.** What is added is an endpoint-specific
boundary that the panel carries and the running text does not:

> **Evidence boundary (cortical layering endpoint).** The AAVS1 rescue (`W-AAV`, constitutive UBP
> promoter) is **marker-selective**, not uniformly normalising. In Figure 4H: for **CTIP2/nuclei** the
> WT-versus-W-AAV comparison is drawn and **significant (`**`) with the rescue ABOVE wild type** — an
> overshoot of the wild-type set point, not a return to it; for **TBR1** and the third (**SATB2**)
> sub-panel no WT-versus-W-AAV bracket is drawn, which under the legend's all-pairwise
> *"one‐way ANOVA with Tukey's multiple comparisons test"* means **tested and not significant**.
> The paper's own sentence scopes recovery to the knockout comparator
> («…recovered and layering improved in W‐AAV COs **compared with WWOX‐KO COs**, RNA levels did only
> partially»), and the section closes with «prevents these changes **to some extent**».
> **`normalizzato` is therefore accurate for firing rate (P = 0.7681, parents vs rescued patient
> organoids) and over-general for cortical lamination.**

🔴 **The OVERSHOOT was taken, not argued away.** The first wording said the WT-versus-rescue comparison
was *untested* for TBR1 and SATB2. The legend declares an all-pairwise Tukey test, and the CTIP2
sub-panel shows the authors do draw that pair when it is significant — so an undrawn bracket is
**tested and not significant**, not omitted. `entries[14]` was rewritten, and the premise was anchored
as `entries[17]` rather than left as background reasoning. The corrected finding is stronger and
narrower than the draft: **the rescue normalises two of three layer markers and overshoots the third.**

The auditor also flagged that the third sub-panel bears **no printed title**; SATB2 is an
identification by elimination from legends G and H. That inference is now stated in the locator itself.

## 4 · Adjacent negatives this reading also anchors

Neither is currently in CLAIM 002; both would constrain any future extension of it.

- **Organoid size is a clean null.** Appendix Table S1, WT vs KO: wk6 `0,3675`, wk10 `0,5619`, wk15 `0,9949`, wk18 `0,8952` (ordinary one-way ANOVA, Tukey); all W-AAV rows likewise null. The model does **not** reproduce the microcephaly seen in some patients — and unlike §1, this null is a **performed and reported** test. Relevant because the human phenotype is explicitly variable on this point («although microcephaly is seen in some patients, many other do not exhibit this condition»).
- **Dorsal/ventral identity is unchanged but not uniformly so.** Appendix Fig S3C rows: dorsal `1`, combined `1`, **ventral `0,064`** (Kruskal–Wallis). The running text reports this simply as no significant difference. `0,064` is not significant and is not nothing; recorded so a future regional-identity claim starts from the number rather than the summary.
