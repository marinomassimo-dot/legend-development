translocation into the nucleus where it activates ATM in a positive feed-forward loop manner"*,

was read under an earlier receipt and the audit covered only its six SI pages. **An editorial's
reference list is not evidence for or against a claim.** `CLAIM 029` is therefore left exactly as
open as it was, and what this reading contributes is a **reading order**: PMID 25245215 and PMID
24550385 before anyone attempts to reconcile the ITCH step.
