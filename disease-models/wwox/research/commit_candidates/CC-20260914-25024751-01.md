## 3 · What the reading establishes, and at what strength

1. **A trend, not an effect:** WWOX IHC median 30 → 100, **P = 0.0547**, n = 17 (L6–L9). The vector marker count independently reproduces both medians and the six pre-treatment zeros.
2. **3 of 6** WWOX-negative tumours converted to positive (L10) — with no control arm, conversion cannot be separated from sampling.
3. **WWOX is the weakest of the three responders.** FHIT rose in 8 of 8 (P = 0.014); the pooled three-gene P = 0.0002 is non-independent (L11).
4. **Two clean nulls** against global methylation (L12, L13) and one against tumour response (L19).
5. **The demethylation mechanism is not established here at all** (L15, L16) — by the authors' own statement.

## 4 · Relevance to a WWOX-DEE model — ESPANSIONE, and bounded twice

Recorded because it is the only human paired *in vivo* WWOX-protein measurement under a demethylating agent in this corpus, and **not** because it supports anything.

- A transcriptional de-repressor can only matter where a **functional allele is present but under-expressed**. This paper measures somatic tumour tissue with wild-type WWOX, not a germline loss-of-function allele.
- Nothing here addresses **brain tissue, development, or a child**. The agent is myelosuppressive and the cohort is adult refractory cancer at a median of five prior regimens.
- **No therapeutic inference is drawn, and none may be drawn from this candidate.** Nothing here is medical advice.
