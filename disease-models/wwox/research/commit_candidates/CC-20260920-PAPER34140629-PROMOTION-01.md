## 3 · Why `LOW` and `T3`, stated rather than assumed

Every endpoint is cancer growth, metastasis or cell repulsion. There is **no neuronal endpoint, no
seizure endpoint, no developmental endpoint and no loss-of-function rescue** anywhere in the paper.
The `WWOX286-299` epitope is real and the peptide work is competent; it simply does not address a
genotype in which WWOX is absent. Promoting it with `HIGH` relevance because it is mechanistically
interesting would be the error this registry's `DOMAIN_PARITY` rule was written to permit *and* the
one its transferability field exists to prevent.
