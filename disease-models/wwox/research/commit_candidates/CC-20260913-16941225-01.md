## 4 · What must NOT be done with this reading, recorded as part of the proposal

1. **Do not close `CLAIM 003`'s open oligodendrocyte question.** The claim says *"una funzione WWOX
   autonoma dell'oligodendrocita resta aperta e non è esclusa"*. This paper's *"Oligodendrocytes showed no
   WWOX immunoreactivity"* is adult human, two cores, no panel, no marker, no negative control — a **weak
   negative** that answers a developing-mouse question not at all. `REVIVAL_TRIGGER`: WWOX protein or
   transcript in human oligodendrocyte-lineage cells by a co-labelled or single-cell method.
2. **Do not carry "skeletal muscle negative".** The authors call it inconclusive.
3. **Do not quote a hormonal-tissue hierarchy as measured.** The Discussion's "highest levels" list is a
   qualitative ranking of categorical grades on duplicate cores, and it contains liver, stomach, salivary
   gland, adrenal, thyroid, parathyroid and distal nephron beside the hormonal organs.
4. **Do not treat `DL-MECH-010`'s "protein absent in oligodendrocytes and liver" as the same fact as this
   paper's negatives.** That record describes **AAV transgene** expression in mouse; this paper describes
   **endogenous** protein in human tissue, and finds hepatocytes strongly positive.
