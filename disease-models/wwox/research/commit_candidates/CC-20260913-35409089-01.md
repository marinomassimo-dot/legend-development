## 3 · A species boundary that the disease model should carry

**FILE:** `discovery_ledger_current.md` § DL-MECH-024 *(proposed append, jointly with
`CC-20260913-27869163-01` §2)*

The human WWOX–BRCA1 interaction is mapped to Wwox's WW1 domain binding BRCA1 in the exon-11 region
near `981PPLF984`; this paper shows the `981PPLF984` motif is *«conserved in primates but not in
rodent species»*, that **exon 11 is dispensable in mouse**, and maps the mouse interaction to the
**BRCT** domain. **The two species use different binding surfaces for the same partner.**

Operational consequence, and it runs against the intuitive direction: mouse WWOX-repair results are
**less** transferable to human genotype reasoning after this pair than before it. Any inference from a
mouse repair phenotype toward a human WWOX-DEE genotype must cross a demonstrated species difference
in the interaction itself. `PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK` on any assumption that a WWOX-DEE missense
or splice allele behaves like the nulls and knockdowns used throughout both papers.
