## Proposed negatives (dismissal-ledger form)

**N1. No WWOX-direct evidence exists on fasting, calorie restriction, dextrose or glucose infusion, fluid or sodium load, refeeding or thiamine.**
Premise chain: PubMed ESearch counts of 2026-10-19 (WWOX with fasting or restriction 4, all type 2 diabetes association studies; with mTOR or AMPK 8; with hypoglycaemia or dextrose 21, none an infusion; with oedema or hyponatraemia 2; with refeeding, thiamine or parenteral nutrition 0); every H5 lot paper (16) read and none contains a WWOX-deficient system with the intervention. Scope: over those queries and the lot, not the literature. **Revival trigger:** any report of a WWOX-null, knockdown, hypomorph or biallelic-loss system (animal, cell or patient series) with a fasting, restriction, glucose-load, fluid or refeeding readout, or a full text of the blocked WWOX/mTOR paper (PMID 36621327) that bears on it.

**N2. "Fasting or calorie restriction protects against seizures" is not a property of fasting in general and has no WWOX premise.**
Premises: protection was lost in a nutrient-sensing knockout despite lower glucose and higher ketones (PMID 36044864); fasting worsened absence seizures in another model (PMID 35134125); restriction raised a threshold but not the kindling rate (PMID 25814935). **Revival trigger:** a WWOX-deficient model tested with a fasting seizure-threshold assay (analysis note L1).

**N3. Glut1 deficiency is not established as an analogue of WWOX loss of function.**
Premises: Glut1-DS is transport failure (PMID 42638834); WWOX-deficient fibroblasts in the repository's readings show higher glucose uptake and a direction that changes with condition (`DL-MECH-020` update). The shared element is a diet response only. **Revival trigger:** a measurement of brain glucose or CSF glucose in a WWOX-deficient model or patient series.

**N4. A WWOX loss-of-function cell is not shown to be more vulnerable to nutrient stress; in culture it was less vulnerable to serum withdrawal.**
Premise: Su 2026 (PMID 41677633, serum-free medium, E14.5 fibroblasts): null cells kept viability about 42% against about 21% at 72 h; reactive oxygen species rose less. Scope: serum withdrawal, fibroblasts, not glucose, not neurons. **Revival trigger:** a glucose-withdrawal experiment in Wwox-null neurons (analysis note L2).
