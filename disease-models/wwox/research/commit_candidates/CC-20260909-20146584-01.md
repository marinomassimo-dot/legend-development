**This paper closes it**, citing the same primary sources:

> *"At birth, homozygous `Wwox`-deficient (`Wwox−/−`) pups were **indistinguishable from wild-type**

> disagreement is a **compression artefact, not a dispute about data**.

**Proposed:** close the reading debt against PMID 17360458 opened by the sibling candidate, and record
the time-course as the accurate form. **This is the lot's clearest demonstration that the unit of work
is the lot, not the paper.**

## 3 · Findings recorded, not proposed as claims

**3.1 · An explicit failure to replicate, and a caveat that dies in a table.** These authors *"were
unable to recapitulate p53 binding to Wwox reported by another group [43]"*. **Chapter 8's Table 1
lists `TP53` as a partner with no caveat**, while its running text carries the qualification. A partner
table is exactly the surface a downstream interactome consumer mines, **and the table is where the
negative disappears.** Ref [43] = PMID 16219768 is queued: reading it is what would settle whether that
table row is supportable.

**3.2 · A directional conflict between the two documents read this wave.** 2010: WWOX expression
*"reportedly **reduced** following UV radiation"*. 2014 (PMID 25245215): *"**increased** immediately
after IR and UV **(unpublished data)**"*, introduced by that chapter's own concession of conflicting
reports. **Aqeilan authors both**, and the 2014 direction is the one its DDR model requires. Recorded
as a conflict **between two secondary sources**, not as biology: neither measures it, and the primary
sources on both sides are unread here.

**3.3 · 🔴 The authors argue against their own thesis.** They state a concrete mechanism by which the
passenger reading could be **right** — replication-stress deletions falling **entirely within the large
introns** of fragile genes, as observed at FHIT, never touching coding sequence — and name the
experiment that would settle it: **delineating WWOX deletion end points**. Of the three
passenger-versus-driver documents in this lot, **this is the only one that supplies the counter-case**,
and the joint Aqeilan/**Huebner** authorship is visible in that balance. **Note the scope:** the
question concerns **somatic deletion architecture in cancer** and says nothing about a germline coding
variant.

**3.4 · The SDR domain is declared functionally uncharacterised — by the authors.** *"Although Wwox
contains an SDR domain that is predicted to be involved in oxidation/reduction processes, Wwox
signaling functions examined thus far are mainly determined by interaction of its WW domains…"*
🔴 **The reference genotype class includes SDR-domain missense variants**, so the field's own
interactome review of this period supplies **no mechanistic basis** for reasoning about them. The
absence is about the **domain**, not merely the phenotype.

**3.5 · The hypomorph is defined by an argument, not a measurement.** *"it was difficult to demonstrate
expression of Wwox protein, though some protein must be produced… or these mice would not be normal."*
Reasoning from phenotype to molecular state, which cannot then explain the phenotype. **A weak anchor
for any dose–response argument**, and this repository reasons about residual-function alleles.
