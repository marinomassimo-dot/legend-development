Public, disease-level, de-identified. **Nothing here is medical advice.**

## 1 · The defect

`literature_tracking_log_current.md` declares ten `Status` values in its own `## Status vocabulary`
table. After `BATCH_20260914_008 (VPS batch, never on main)`, 396 of 397 records are inside it. The one outside is `LIT-0401`,
Bendinelli 2017 (*Cell Death & Disease*), **retracted in 2022** and excluded after an integrity audit,
which carries `archived` — a value the table does not define. No existing value is true of it:

| Existing value | Why it is false for LIT-0401 |
|---|---|
| `background_only` | "archived as context, no operative function" — a retracted paper is not usable even as context |
| `filtered_out` | the table's own words are *"Excluded — reason logged"*, and on those words this value is arguably true of `LIT-0401`. It fails for a different reason: **no existing value carries the prohibition** — a status decides what a record may be used for, and none of the ten forbids use |
| `superseded` | replaced by a stronger or more recent paper; nothing replaces a retraction |

## 2 · The proposed change

Add one row to the `## Status vocabulary` table:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `excluded_integrity` | **Retracted**; kept only as an audit trail of what was excluded and why; may not support any claim, premise or context. An expression of concern does **not** move a record here: it is carried by `PUBLICATION_INTEGRITY_HOLD` on the record itself |

and move `LIT-0401` from `archived` to `excluded_integrity`, adding a `Status note` that keeps the
retraction DOI already present in its `Identifier value`.

## 3 · Why this is safe for the harness

- `lit_status.py` reads the vocabulary from the log's own table and keeps **no copy** of it, so a new row
  is recognised with no code change.
- `test_lit_status.py` asserts the ten values against **its own fixture log**, not against the canonical
  file, so adding a row to canon does not make a test false. Verified by reading the test, not assumed.
- Expected effect on LINT: the single `LIT_STATUS_LEGACY` warning (`NOT_IN_VOCABULARY: 1`) disappears and
  `lit_status.py validate` returns 397 valid, 0 outside the vocabulary. To be measured after propagation,
  not asserted from here.
- `lit_status_legacy.json` must be regenerated in the same window (`snapshot --write-json`), since its
  own documentation says the file can only shrink.

## 4 · What this candidate does not do

- It does not touch `LIT-0085`. That record is not itself retracted, and its prohibition is already
  carried explicitly by its `Flags` (*"integrity exclusion"*) and `Next action` (*"non usare come
  corroborazione"*). Whether a `Status` should carry what those two fields carry is a real
  question — and it is a question for a per-record integrity audit, not for this act, which would
  otherwise move a record no audit examined for this purpose.
  🔴 **Correction to my first draft:** I had rejected `background_only` for `LIT-0401` on the ground
  that *"a retracted paper is not usable even as context"*. `LIT-0085` refutes that argument by
  example — it is a not-usable-as-context record sitting under a value defined as *"archived as
  context"* — so the argument is withdrawn and the decision rests on the reason above.
- It does not re-open the eleven `LEGACY_FIELD` and thirty-five `LEGACY_FORM` records normalised in
  `BATCH_20260914_008 (VPS batch, never on main)`; they are inside the vocabulary already.
- It asserts nothing about the retracted paper's science. The record exists so that the exclusion is
  auditable, and the new value says exactly that.

## 5 · Repairs required by `SCI-CONSULT-20260914`, applied — and the case that forced the narrowing

**The wide wording was too wide by a measurable case.** The clause *"or under an expression of
concern"* would have captured PMID 16223882 on the day it landed: that paper carries an Editorial
Expression of Concern, `PAPER 082` holds it under `PUBLICATION_INTEGRITY_HOLD`, and the literature
log carries it at `LIT-0138` and `LIT-0411`, both `processed`. Verified by me on 2026-09-14 before
accepting the objection. Either those rows would have had to move too, or canon would have shipped a
definition it violates. The value is therefore narrowed to **retractions only**.

**To land in the same act:** regenerate `lit_status_legacy.json` (`snapshot --write-json`), or LINT
is expected to report `LIT_STATUS_SNAPSHOT_STALE`.
