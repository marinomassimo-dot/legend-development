## 3 · `CLAIM 029` — one sentence added, with its own two qualifications

No reversal, no status change, and **the ATM leg is untouched** — this paper measures no ATM, no γH2AX, no repair kinetics.

**Proposed addition:**

> In a *MYC*-driven murine plasma-cell model, B-cell *Wwox* deletion is accompanied by a genomic-instability phenotype that is **confined to the tumours, not the marrow**: no cancer-driver mutation in any of 5 wild-type marrow samples against multiple drivers in 4 of 4 knockout tumours (the plasmablastic lymphoma carrying 21), copy-number burden low and comparable across all marrow genotypes but elevated in tumours, and a base-excision-repair signature (SBS30) present only in 2 of 3 plasmacytomas (PMID 41090157, `FTR-20260914-41090157-01`). **Two qualifications travel with it and must not be dropped:** the mismatch-repair signature SBS26 is present in a **wild-type** marrow sample as well as in heterozygotes and a knockout, so it is not *Wwox*-specific in this data; and SBS85, the signature the authors attribute to indirect AID activity, appears in **none of the four tumours** whose hypermutation the paper attributes to AID/APOBEC, while the *Aicda*/*Apobec2* overexpression offered as that mechanism **fails its own test** (P = .2646 / .2680).

**Why this wording.** It is directionally consistent with the counter-directional note already under `CLAIM 029` (PMID 41562193, wild-type/knockout interleaving on burden) rather than in tension with it: here too the genotype-specific signal is in tumours and the marrow is quiet. Stating the SBS26 and SBS85 facts inside the addition is what keeps it from being read as a clean "WWOX loss causes repair deficiency" datum.

## 4 · New limits and qualifications to record

- **"Knockout" is a ~3–5-fold reduction, not an ablation.** Supplementary Figure S1b: KO ≈ 0.3 vs WT ≈ 1.5 (p = 0.0016); in the RNA-seq the *Wwox* transcript is down only **1.61 log₂** (padj 0.021) in sorted CD138⁺ cells.
- **The incidence result is marginal and test-dependent:** χ² P = .036 as printed; **Fisher exact two-tailed p = 0.052** on the same 17/27 vs 4/14 table.
- **Monoclonal gammopathy is genotype-independent here:** 24/24 KO and 14/14 WT, with no difference in onset age (log-rank 0.7064).
- **Wild-type Vk∗MYC mice get the same tumour kinds** (Table S1: 4 WT tumours, 3 PBP + 1 PBL), so the genotype effect is frequency, not class — and **none of those 4 WT tumours was sequenced**, leaving every "tumour vs marrow" contrast within-knockout.
- **The inflammation signature rests on 87 genes of which 31 are immunoglobulin V genes**, 19 of the top 20 by effect size, running both directions — a clonality pattern. The four genes the text names rank 28/30/34/46. No cytokine or NF-κB protein was measured, and `Tnf`, `Il6`, `Rel`, `Nfkb1` are not themselves differentially expressed.
- **Figure 7B's caption overstates its own table:** "widespread and pronounced … across all samples" against segment counts of 17, 59, 44 and **7**.
- **A direction the text omits:** the chr11 interval carrying *Rel*, *Xpo1*, *Bcl11a* is a **gain** in two tumours (cn 4, cn 3) and a **loss** in a third (cn 1).
- 🔴 **The paper contradicts itself about which tumour is which histology.** Figure 6A's column headers and Figure 6B's titles label KO1-PBT as the **PBL** and KO4-PBT as a **PBP**; Figure 6C's caption says the reverse — and the *Aicda*/*Apobec2* claim is attached to those two highlighted points. Nothing in the artefact adjudicates. Read at 400 dpi from the PDF's vector artwork.
- **The aggressiveness reading of CD138 "uropods" is imported** from PMID 11001907; no migration or adhesion assay exists here.
- **The only human evidence is a re-analysis** of four public microarray series by median split on probe `219077_s_at` (HR 0.46, n = 1416; HR 0.34, n = 801).
- **The heterozygous arm is the one dose-sensitivity handle in this lot** — genotyped, sequenced, signature-profiled, carrying drivers and SBS26 where wild types carry none — and it is **never analysed for survival or incidence**.
