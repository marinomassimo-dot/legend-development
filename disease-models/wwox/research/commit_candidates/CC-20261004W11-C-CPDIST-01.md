## 1 · The finding in one sentence

In adult mouse, one unilateral 10 microlitre injection of an engineered AAV2/ShH10 Y445F vector into the lateral ventricle transduces the choroid-plexus epithelium unevenly: the injected-side lateral ventricle most, the contralateral side less, third-ventricle choroid plexus very poorly and fourth-ventricle choroid plexus weakly, and the authors state that whether dose escalation can overcome the ipsilateral bias without toxicity "has not yet been examined in detail".

## 3 · What this adds, bounds or leaves untouched

- **Adds** (to the CLAIM 047 route bound): the choroid-plexus compartments are a measurable gradient after a CSF-route injection, and "choroid plexus" appears in none of the 1,539 registry records (`registry_records.py get --theme "choroid plexus"`, 0 of 1539), so no held record states it.
- **Bounds** any reading that the higher dose "overcame" the gradient: dose, transgene (EGFP versus SaCas9 plus guides), read-out (native fluorescence versus indel and AQP1 protein) and time (3 versus 6 weeks) all differ between the two experiments, so the dose effect is not isolated. The authors themselves compare the two figures only descriptively.
- **Bounds** the selection's premise that ICV is the route a WWOX attempt would use: no source here or held fixes a WWOX route; this is a route-level distribution datum only.
- **Leaves untouched** every immune, window and DRG statement: no immune endpoint, no age series, no ganglion read-out.

## 4 · Transfer limits

Adult female wild-type mouse, one capsid, reporter and CRISPR payloads, target is the choroid-plexus epithelium itself rather than parenchymal neurons. The explant data in rat and pig are tropism only. Nothing transfers to a WWOX cassette, to neuronal transduction, to a human CSF volume or to any WWOX-DEE allele.
