## 6 · Method note for the harness (not a canonical proposal)

Two reusable patterns, both `IPOTESI`, both earned here:

1. **When a review disposes of a negative result, check whether the disposal was tested.** Here two
   negatives are attributed to method insensitivity with no test — and because both negatives are papers
   this repository had read, the disposal was checkable and rested on nothing.
2. **A review's exemplar figure can supply visual support for exactly the relationship its text
   disclaims**, with every caption honest and no misstatement anywhere. Text-only and caption-only
   reading are both blind to it; only reading the panel *against* the text makes it visible.
