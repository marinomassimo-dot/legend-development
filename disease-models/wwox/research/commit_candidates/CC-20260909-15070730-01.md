## 1 · What is proposed

**`CLAIM 023` — NARROW.** Its status stays `consolidated baseline`; its **binding leg is untouched
and if anything better supported than the registry records**. One clause in its **title** is
proposed for removal, because the paper it descends from does not test it.

### The claim as it stands

> **Title:** *"WWOX controls partner-protein function not only by binding, but by
> **phosphorylation-dependent subcellular rerouting** that changes downstream biological output"*
>
> **Summary:** *"WWOX binds p73 via WW1; Tyr33 phosphorylation enhances binding; WWOX relocalizes
> p73 from nucleus to cytoplasm, reducing nuclear transcriptional activity while cytoplasmic
> cooperation contributes to proapoptotic output."*

🔴 **The summary is faithful and the title is not.** The summary lists **three separate facts**.
The title welds two of them into a **causal relation** — that the rerouting is *phosphorylation-
dependent* — and **no experiment in this paper manipulates phosphorylation and measures
localisation.**

**Made machine-checkable rather than asserted:** **20 sentences** in the body mention Src, and
**ZERO** of them also mention localisation, cytoplasm, nucleus, redistribution or sequestration.
Confirmed by both blind auditors, one with a ±400-character proximity screen whose only near-misses
are adjacent sentences. Fig 4 is entirely binding and phospho-blot biochemistry; Fig 2 and Fig 5 are
localisation **with no Src arm**.

### Proposed title

> *"WWOX controls partner-protein function not only by binding, but by subcellular rerouting that
> changes downstream biological output; Tyr33 phosphorylation regulates the **binding**, and its
> effect on rerouting is untested."*

### Proposed additions to the claim body

1. **The routing leg is overexpression-dependent, by the authors' own words:**
   *"Overexpression of Wwox caused the redistribution of p73 from the nuclear compartment to the
   cytosol."*
2. **The paper's only graded-dose titration does not support its own premise.** Fig 5 carries **no
   dose labels, no merged channel, no cell count, no n and no statistic**, while the proposition it
   supports is about a *proportion* (*"some cells"*); and the Wwox-positive **area** in the
   reduced-dose field is **not lower** than in the higher-dose field, so the panel does not display
   the decrease in Wwox expression its citing sentence asserts.
3. **The modal dies inside the paper.** Results: *"suggesting that phosphorylation enhances this
   interaction."* Discussion, four paragraphs later: *"We have also demonstrated specific
   phosphorylation of Y33."*

**Not proposed:** any weakening of the binding leg. It is the best-supported thing in the paper —
bidirectional co-IP, **endogenous** interaction in two cell types, PPxY dependence confirmed three
ways, direct GST pull-down with the isolated first 50 aa, and p53 excluded as a specificity control.

## 2 · The debt this closes, and what opening it cost the claim

The 2026-08-14 receipt declared itself partial for **exactly one** reason — Figures 5, 6 and 7 sat
behind PMC's proof-of-work — and recorded Fig 5 as *"the closest thing in the paper to a causal
titration and **the strongest form of the routing evidence**."*

**Opened, it is the weakest form of it** (§1.2). The debt is paid and it **cost the claim**: the
panel that was expected to strengthen the routing leg is the one that most clearly cannot carry it.
That is the whole argument for paying reading debts rather than declaring them permanently.

Fig 6, by contrast, **supports** the control it is cited for, on corrected numbers: WT/mutant
Wwox-to-tubulin is 1.07–1.13 at 0 ng p73β and 1.32–1.43 at 10 ng across three integration windows,
so the mutant is expressed **at or below** wild type and Fig 3B's restored p21 **cannot** be an
overexpression artefact. Fig 7 shows the nine histograms behind Fig 3C are **not on a common scale**
(y-maxima 148, 72, 88, 60, 132, 64, 124, 60, 120) and carry **no gate marker and no printed
percentage**, so they do not display the gating that produced Fig 3C's numbers.

## 3 · 🔴 What the blind audit changed — the part that matters most

**7 of 13 triples required correction, and my most attractive finding was withdrawn in full.**

I had claimed Fig 6 supplies the dose-to-expression step Fig 5 asserts and does not display — a
cross-panel rescue inside one supplement. **It fails on cell line:** Fig 5 is **NIH 3T3**, Fig 6 is
the **SAOS-2** p21 series. I verified that against the body myself rather than on an auditor's word.
**Had the audit not run, this candidate would have asserted a bridge between two different cell
lines as if it closed a gap in the paper's own argument.**

Also withdrawn: *"on any measure"* and its 99th-percentile pair (three measurements returned three
answers, so the statistic is windowing-dependent); *"both panels contain both morphologies"*;
*"straddling unity"* on Fig 6; *"lowest by a wide margin"* on lane 10. **One correction ran in my
favour:** my Src-sentence count was wrong at 8 and is **20** — the zero-co-occurrence finding on
which this whole narrowing rests is *stronger* than I claimed.

**The two auditors disagreed twice and I adjudicated from the source, not by preferring an auditor.**
On the Fig 7 y-maxima one read 68/68/128 and the other 60/60/120; re-rendered upright at 26×, the
disputed glyph has an open centre and no waist unlike the plainly waisted `8` of `148` — **60/60/120
stands and that auditor was wrong.** On lane 10's tubulin the window sensitivity is real but the
proposed mechanism does not reproduce (centroid 155.75, inside the window, ten-lane span
154.30–157.40). Both halves are recorded.

⚠️ **An assumption the audit forced into the open:** Fig 5 has **no caption** in HTML or PDF — only
the citing sentence's *"b vs. c"*. **Nothing states which panel is the reduced dose.** `c = reduced`
is the natural reading and it is an assumption, not a datum.

## 4 · Why ORDINARY and not MAJOR

The narrowing removes an **untested coupling from a claim's title**; it does not reverse the claim,
does not remove it, and does not touch the working model's architecture. WWOX remains a
routing/scaffold regulator — that is what the summary already said and what the reading still
supports, at `INFERENZA` strength and overexpression-dependent. **No `target_wm_version` bump is
proposed.** If the orchestrator judges a title change to a `consolidated baseline` claim to be
MAJOR by nature, that reclassification is its call and this candidate does not resist it.
