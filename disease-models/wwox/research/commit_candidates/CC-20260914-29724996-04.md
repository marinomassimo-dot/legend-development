## 1 · The defect

`PAPER 091` (PMID 29724996) carries, among the constraints that travel with the record:

> the only **quantified** mouse panel in this paper for WWOX loss in liver tumours is `Supplement Fig
> S1A`: **DEN-induced** tumours of control mice against **adjacent** parenchyma of the same mice, ~1.02
> vs ~0.49 on a fold-change axis, n=3 vs n=3 and **no significance mark**

The word **adjacent** is not supported for that panel.

## 2 · The evidence

- The supplement legend reads *"mRNA expression levels of Wwox in the paranchyma and the tumors of 10
  months DEN-treated control mice (Paranchyma n=3; Tumor n=3)"*. No form of *adjacent* occurs in the
  supplement text layer.
- The article uses the word once, for **human** *"cancer adjacent liver tissue"* on a commercial tissue
  microarray (Fig. 1c, d) — another experiment, another species.
- A blind R4 audit (`SCI-R4-29724996-E34`) returned **OVERSHOOT** on this word and **CONFIRMED** on the
  other ten assertions of the same locator, including both values (vector geometry: 1.016 and 0.490),
  n = 3 vs 3 and the absence of any significance mark. Record:
  `research/verifications/2026-09-14_sci_r4_29724996_entry34_audit.md`.
- The auditor also noted that panel A's reference bar is 1.016 rather than exactly 1.000, so the panel is
  not a per-animal paired tumour/adjacent ratio — which is what *adjacent* would imply.

## 3 · The proposed change

In `PAPER 091`, replace **"against adjacent parenchyma of the same mice"** with **"against parenchyma of
the same mice"**. Nothing else in the record changes: the arm, the values, the n, the missing mark and
the conclusion that the panel is a direction without a declared statistic all stand, confirmed.
