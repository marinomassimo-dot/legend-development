## 3b · Why the amended `CLAIM 007` wording is narrower than the correction that prompted it

The independent verification found *"normal transcript and normal protein"* too strong on its
protein half and proposed **"normal transcript, and protein not detectably reduced on an
unquantified n=2 input lane"**. R4 applies to an amended consolidated-baseline wording exactly as
it applied to the original, so the amendment went to a blind locator audit — triples only, no
dossier, no candidate, no reader identity — and **that audit refused the proposed wording too**,
for the opposite reason.

The auditor located the four antibody rows of Fig. 1a from a y-darkness profile and ran per-lane
densitometry at native 1801×1412: Anti-Wwox 10% input integrates to **1050 and 1082** in the mutant
lanes against **1468 and 1556** in wild type, with half-depth widths of 8–9 px against 10–12 px,
while Anti-Hsp90 integrates to **2620 and 2716** in the mutant lanes against **2447 and 2510** in
wild type. Loading is therefore even to slightly *favouring* the mutant, and **the mutant input
bands are detectably reduced** — so *"not detectably reduced"* asserts a negative the cited panel
contradicts. The verification's finding was right; its proposed sentence was not.

The same audit refused *"abolishes"*: the mutant pull-down lanes dip to gray 149/179 and ~163
against lane backgrounds of ~185–188, i.e. **faint residual recovery at roughly 3–13% of wild
type**. The original persisted snippet's word — *"near-absent"* — was the accurate one and is
restored. And the evidence is **two biotinylated PPPY oligopeptides** (Dvl2 HPYSPQPPPYHELSSY,
Wbp1 SGSGGTPPPPYTVG) in an *in vitro* peptide pull-down at n=2, so a claim about "PPxY motifs" as a
class, or about full-length partners, generalises beyond what was measured.

**Measurement caveat carried with the numbers:** gray value on a JPEG scan of film is not linear in
protein, so those per-lane figures are **direction and rank order, never a fold-change** — which is
also why the Summary claims no ratio.
