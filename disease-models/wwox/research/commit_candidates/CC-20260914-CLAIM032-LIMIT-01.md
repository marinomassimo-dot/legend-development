## 1 · The defect

`CLAIM 032` (*WWOX haploinsufficiency is not deleterious*) carries, among the limits in its
`Clinical meaning`:

> nei topi eterozigoti è documentato un aumento di tumorigenicità **sotto carcinogeni**.

That sentence locates the heterozygote tumour excess **only** under challenge. The source it inherits
from — the review read as `PAPER 053` — reports ref 50's heterozygote tumours only under carcinogen,
and **omits** that the same ref 50 reports an excess in **untreated** heterozygotes. `PAPER 053`'s own
`Note` already records the omission.

## 3 · The proposed change

In `CLAIM 032` `Clinical meaning`, replace:

> nei topi eterozigoti è documentato un aumento di tumorigenicità **sotto carcinogeni**.

with:

> nei topi eterozigoti un eccesso di tumori è documentato **sia dopo carcinogeno** (ENU: 37/46 contro
> 20/42, p = 0,002) **sia spontaneamente in animali non trattati** di 9–18 mesi (10/58 contro 2/60,
> p = 0,03; PMID 17360458, Table 1, aggiudicata sulla pagina resa a 300 dpi perché il text layer perde i simboli di genotipo). La coorte non trattata non è un'osservazione da stress; se linea,
> fondo genetico o età la riconcilino con i modelli condizionali **resta irrisolto**.

## 4 · What does not change, and why

- **The conclusion is not reversed and not weakened.** The claim's pillars that carry it —
  `PAPER 021` (Tochigi 2019, `+/lde` rats at half protein with normal cortical IHC) and the healthy
  heterozygous carriers of every published family (`PAPER 043`, `045`, `049`, `042`) — are independent
  of this datum.
- **The therapy corollary stands.** An intervention that *raises* WWOX is put at risk by neither datum.
- **The limit already in the field stays and is reinforced:** the threshold is known for survival and
  morphology, not for cognition and epilepsy. No cited primary measures any heterozygote CNS, seizure or
  neurodevelopmental phenotype.

## 5 · The tension this makes visible, stated rather than resolved

The claim's own `Impact on Working Model` carries the `BATCH_20260815_001` qualification that locates
heterozygote susceptibility in a `genotype×stress` interaction. The untreated cohort is not a stress
observation. This candidate does not rewrite that qualification; it asks the consultation whether the
qualification's framing must name the untreated excess too, or whether the limit sentence suffices.
