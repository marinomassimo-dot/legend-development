## 3 · The WW1 datum — context offered to `CLAIM 024` and `CLAIM 026`, not an amendment

**⚠️ And the mutant does not abolish it.** `W44F/P47A` — the WW1 point mutant — leaves a **weak but visible residual band** (L22), and **bait loading is not shown**. The paper's own wording is that binding is *lost*; the panel says *reduced*. ## 4 · What the reading establishes, with its strength stated honestly

1. **Moderate:** WWOX knockdown raises four SMAD3 target transcripts in MCF10 and re-expression lowers them, concordant at mRNA and secreted-protein level (L9, L10, L14, L15). Weaker and not uniform in 184B5 and MCF7, each with a single shRNA.

4. **Weak — IPOTESI:** cytoplasmic sequestration as the mechanism. **No fractionation, no import assay, one cell displayed** (L23, L24, L27).
