## 1 · The gap, precisely located

`CLAIM 025` already carries a context-dependence boundary — but it is about the **reporter gene
set**, not the **sign**:

> *"the responding gene set is context-dependent"* (PMID 29724996 boundary, 2026-09-09)

An independent test on this branch's own frontier extends it one step further, and that step changes
what the marker is:

> 🔴 **The ratio's association with outcome REVERSES SIGN between contexts.** `CLAIM 025` derives
> from GDM leukocytes where **low ratio = maladaptive**. In PMID 42589397, low ratio is associated
> with **worse** DFS in basal-like and HER2-enriched BRCA but **better** DFS in Luminal A, Luminal B
> and OV.

**A "marker of maladaptive state" whose direction flips by context is a *state descriptor*, not a
*severity index*.** That distinction is not currently made anywhere in `CLAIM 025`, and the claim's
own wording embeds the directional reading in three places: its **Title** (*"marker of maladaptive
biological state"*), its **Summary** (*"Lower ratio associates with…"*), and its
**Genotype/model relevance** (*"ratio marcatamente ridotto; cross-context support"*).

🔴 **The phrase `cross-context support` is the sharpest problem.** It currently reads as *the ratio
behaves consistently across contexts*. The independent test says the opposite of that, for the sign.

## 2 · What genuinely SUPPORTS the claim, and must not be lost in qualifying it

The same paper **strengthens** the claim's architectural half, and this is the larger half:

> In the combined BRCA model *"the individual genes lost significance"* while **the ratio did not**.

That is a direct argument for using a **ratio** rather than either transcript alone — which is
`CLAIM 025`'s core architectural proposition. The paper's own framing, a *"context-dependent
molecular index"* integrating hypoxia, immune and hormone-related states, is close in spirit to the
claim's *"systems-level marker of maladaptive biological state"*.

## 3 · What must NOT be inferred — the source's own bounds, preserved

🔴 **This paper cannot raise `CLAIM 025`'s corroboration weight, and licenses no transfer.** It is
**same-group, same-dataset, tumour-only and perturbation-free**. Verbatim from the source analysis:
it *"cannot raise its corroboration weight and provides nothing that would license transfer to a
non-tumoral CNS claim."*

🔴 **And its statistics are fragile in the paper's own words:** *"non-significant and exploratory"* ·
*"does not act as a uniform or strongly predictive prognostic marker … statistically fragile"* ·
optimism-corrected C-index *"centred close to 0"*.

> **So the qualification is asymmetric, and that asymmetry is the point.** The finding is strong
> enough to **constrain how LEGEND may use the ratio** — because a sign reversal invalidates a
> directional reading regardless of effect size — and far too weak to **strengthen, extend or
> transfer** the claim. A fragile result can still refuse a direction it fails to reproduce.

qualsiasi esperimento con **perturbazione** di WWOX o HIF1A che misuri l'esito in due contesti.
