## 3 · What the reading adds that canon does not carry at all

**(a) 🔴 It resolves a unit ambiguity canon has recorded as unresolvable.** `fulltext_dossiers/PMID18452537.md` (my own prior reading, same mandate) quotes from the Ramos 2008 **author manuscript** *"an antibody concentration of `140 mg / ml` whose unit cannot be resolved"*, and the string `140 mg / ml` is persisted in that paper's manifest. The present paper is the **same laboratory's** anti-WWOX polyclonal, on a clean JATS surface of a version of record, and it prints **`140 μg/ml`** (L2, Materials and methods, IHC).

- **Type: INFERENZA** (cross-reading, not DATO). It supports reading the Ramos string as a **lost micro sign** in manuscript conversion, for what is plausibly the same stock. It does **not** prove the two reagents are one lot, and the two papers are four years apart.
- **What to do with it:** offered to the orchestrator as a note on the Ramos record, not as an edit to a persisted quote. 🔴 **The quoted string in the Ramos manifest and dossier must not be altered** — it is quoted as printed, which is correct; only an interpretive note may be added.

**(b) A splice-level datum.** Two HCC lines carry in-frame exon 5→9 junctions (Δ6–8), one of them with a **96-bp intron-8-derived insert** encoding 32 additional residues, confirmed by sequencing (L14, L15, L18). DATO in two cancer lines. Any bearing on splice-site alleles in WWOX-DEE is **ESPANSIONE** and is labelled as such in the dossier. The predicted ~30 kDa product is the authors' INFERENZA and **was never shown** (L17), although their N-terminal antibody (epitope aa 12–94, L3) would have seen it.

**(c) A coding variant the paper dismisses without evidence.** **P252A** is reported in SNU387, alongside D183N (7703, Focus), A179T (HepG2, Hep40) and R314H (PLC/PRF/5) (L28–L30). All four are called *"very likely"* polymorphisms with **no functional assay, no population frequency, no matched normal DNA and no domain map** (L31; PREMISE `DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK`). Germline or somatic origin is unknowable from this paper. Recorded because canon reasons about WWOX missense residual function, not because this paper establishes anything about it.
