## §2 What the reading found, in one paragraph

Zfra1-31 at 20 µM protects differentiated SH-SY5Y cells against 48 h of 25 mM glucose across a
broad, internally coherent panel, and lowers the pTyr33-WWOX signal while doing so; WWOX activation
**precedes** the damage on the paper's own time-course. But there is **no genetic WWOX arm**
(`siRNA`, `shRNA`, `knockdown`, `knockout`, `CRISPR`, `transfect*`, `lentivir*` all zero in article
*and* supplementary), **no inactive-peptide control** (`scrambled` 0, `S8G` 0; the only specificity
statement is *"data not shown"*), and 🔴 **no total-WWOX measurement** although
`WWOX Merck Millipore (ABN413)` is listed in the methods and the blots are normalised to β-actin.
So the paper's central number — the **~64 %** reduction, which belongs to the **late-intervention**
arm — cannot separate *"Zfra inhibited WWOX"* from *"Zfra consumed WWOX"*.

## §3 🔴 Why this generates NO claim, stated so it cannot be read as modesty

The model organism is **wild-type WWOX** in a **hyperglycaemia** model. The reference genotype's
problem is *too little functional WWOX*; this paper's premise is *too much activated WWOX*. Nothing
in it measures a WWOX-DEE allele, a developing CNS, a seizure endpoint or a neurodevelopmental
outcome. **Transferability `T3`.** Its value to this model is entirely **negative and
therapeutic-directional**: it constrains a candidate class rather than describing the disease. A
`PAPER` record is the right home for that; a `CLAIM` is not.
