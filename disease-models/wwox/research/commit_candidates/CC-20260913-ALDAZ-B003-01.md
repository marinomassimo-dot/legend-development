## 0 · What this candidate is for, in one paragraph

A completed reading of `PMID 16941225` established that the antibody carrying that paper's whole
tissue map is not validated in it, and that specificity is handed off by citation to two 2005 papers
that had never been read here. Both are now read to `complete_fulltext_read`. **The answer is that the
delegation reaches immunoblot-grade validation on epithelial lysates against a cell-line null, and
contains no immunohistochemical control in either paper** — one asserts an unshown pre-absorption, the
other has none at all — **while both hand the antibody's characterisation onward to a third paper that
is still unread.** Nothing in the corpus is refuted by this. Several things are **qualified**, and the
qualification is now precise instead of being a declared unknown.

## 4 · The two findings this lot adds that no priority list could have predicted

1. 🔴 **The specificity citation chain does not terminate where the corpus assumed.** Both 2005 papers
   hand the antibody's characterisation to **their own ref 12 = `PMID 14526170`** (Ludes-Meyers 2003),
   which has no receipt here. `16941225 → {15982416, 15692750} → 14526170`. The corpus was treating a
   hand-off as a destination. Queued as `FT-179`, priority **HIGH**.
2. 🔴 **One reagent validation reported across two tissues, not two independent validations.** Shared:
   the antibody at 280 ng/ml, the PEO1 null (cited to the same `11572989`), the ACIS platform and its
   63/255 floor, the immunostaining protocol (the ovarian paper cites the breast paper for it), the
   laboratory, 7 of 9 authors, Fox Chase pathology. Not shared: tissue, cases, TMAs, blots, the upper
   scoring boundary. **The data are largely independent; the reagent validation is one pipeline.**
   Neither is an external replication — the only outside group named in the lot is Guler 2004
   (`15073846`), whose antibody neither paper states, which is why it is queued rather than counted.

Secondary, and worth a line because it is a hypothesis failing in public: the laboratory's
sex-steroid frame is **confirmed in breast (ER p = 0.0054) and flatly null in ovary (ER p = 0.984)**,
same reagent, same scoring, same year, with nothing hormonal measured in either. A hypothesis
restated across two papers is not evidence accumulating.
