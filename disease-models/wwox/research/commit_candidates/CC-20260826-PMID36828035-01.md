## 1. Why this paper matters to the current contradiction

It supplies the **methodologically strongest electro-behavioural seizure dataset in the WWOX
corpus**, and it does so in a mouse.

> *"We examined Wwox^WT/WT (n = 3) and Wwox^P47T/P47T (n = 3) mice over prolonged periods
> (21–53 h) … Wildtype mice displayed infrequent spike activity (0–68/hour) but no evidence of
> seizures. We detected very frequent interictal cortical spike discharges (0–3098/hour) and
> repeated spontaneous generalized convulsive activity (11–22 seizures during 43–53 h monitoring
> periods) in both male and female homozygous mutant mice. All three Wwox^P47T/P47T mutant mice
> had multiple (>4) seizures during a 24 h recording period."*

**Method (Methods §4.4):** silver-wire electrodes implanted **bilaterally**, subdural, over
**frontal and parietal** cortex — four channels; **14 days recovery before recording**;
simultaneous video-EEG and behavioural monitoring, 24-hour sessions, adult (>6 weeks), both sexes;
EEGs reviewed by **two trained observers**.

**Electro-behavioural concordance is scored on the same events:** *"These seizures were
accompanied by bilaterally symmetrical EEG discharges … which began with a single spike discharge,
rapidly followed by complex hypersynchronous activity … Motor contractions began at a high
frequency and progressively decreased in parallel with the spike discharge frequency."*
Seizures up to 1 min, postictal lethargy up to 3 min, **not lethal**.

**Comparison with the only null electrographic dataset (Obeid 2026):**

| | Obeid 2026 (`Wwox`-null) | Hussain 2023 (`P47T/P47T`) |
|---|---|---|
| Channels | 1, unilateral | **4, bilateral frontal + parietal** |
| Recovery before recording | **none** | **14 days** |
| Simultaneous video | subset, to confirm electrographic events | **yes, throughout** |
| Reader | 1 blinded | 2 trained (**blinding not stated**) |
| Age | P14–P21 | adult >6 wk |
| n | 5/group | 3/group |
| Convulsive seizures | not reported | **11–22 per 43–53 h** |

⚠️ **This does not make Hussain the better paper on the null** — it is a **different genotype**.
It makes the *montage and the recovery interval* the design to copy, and it shows what the
single-channel, zero-recovery null dataset cannot resolve: bilateral synchrony.

🔴 **`P47T/P47T` is not a null.** Nothing here licenses transferring a SCAR12 missense phenotype
to a WOREE null, or vice versa. What it *does* falsify is the framing that seizures in WWOX
rodents are a **rat** phenomenon.

## 2. Proposed canonical content

### 2.1 Longevity and survival — **`DATO`, and the headline needs its qualifier**

Taken from the **body**, not the abstract — see §5 for why that distinction was forced:

| Genotype | Mean long-term survival | n (Fig. 1b) |
|---|---|---|
| `Wwox^WT/WT` | **542 ± 8 days** | 14 |
| `Wwox^P47T/WT` | **495 ± 23 days** | 30 |
| `Wwox^P47T/P47T` | **393 ± 32 days** | 25 |

*"The difference in survival was statistically significant between the Wwox^P47T/P47T and the
Wwox^WT/WT and Wwox^P47T/WT groups (Log-rank p-value = 0.001)."*

- ⇒ The correct statement is **"long-lived but shortened"**, not "long-lived". A summary that
  keeps only the first half is the same over-transfer this repository has paid for before.
- ⚠️ The abstract's *"live beyond 1 year of age"* is the weaker form and was **refused as a
  locator** by the manifest validator for being abstract-only (§5).

### 2.2 🔴 Abundance/function dissociation — **`DATO`, and it is the cleanest in the corpus**

- *"The average mRNA expression levels between Wwox^WT/WT and Wwox^P47T/P47T CB were comparable,
  with no significant difference in n = 6 mice tested in each group (Supplementary Fig. S2a).
  Western blot analyses also showed similar levels of Wwox protein expression in homozygous
  mutant and wildtype mice."*
- Function is nonetheless abolished: Fig. 1a — pull-down from CB lysate with biotinylated **Dvl2**
  and **Wbp1** PPxY peptides shows *"p.Pro47Thr mutation is sufficient to abrogate Wwox affinity
  for PPxY motifs"* (n = 2/group).
- ⇒ **Normal mRNA, normal protein, abolished binding.**

🔴 **This directly reinforces `CLAIM 016`'s existing `PREMISE_TAG`** — that *"abundance of GSK3β
protein reports GSK3β activity"* is `DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK`. Here the same dissociation is
demonstrated for WWOX itself, in vivo, in the tissue of interest. The premise tag stops being a
cautionary note and becomes an **instance-backed** one. Propose: cross-link `CLAIM 016` and
`CLAIM 035` to this paper as a demonstrated abundance≠function case.

⚠️ n = 2 for the pull-down; n = 6 for mRNA; the protein panel is an **input lane of a pull-down
blot**, not a dedicated quantified western. Do not report it as quantified abundance.

### 2.3 Progressive neuroinflammation — **`DATO`, with the progression claim resting on two ages**

- Iba1⁺ microgliosis and GFAP⁺ astrogliosis in hippocampal CA3, compared at **80 and 250 days**
  (Figs. 3a, 4a), described as increasing with age.
- Reduced oligodendrocyte numbers (Olig2 pan-marker).
- ⚠️ "Progressive" rests on **two time points**, not a trajectory. Legitimate as stated; not a rate.

### 2.4 Cerebellar degeneration — **`DATO`**

Significantly reduced molecular and granular layer thickness; *"strikingly reduced number of
Purkinje cells with degenerated dendrites"* (abstract; quantification in figures not inspected).

### 2.5 Transcriptome — **`DATO` for expression, `INFERENZA` for function**

*"Suppression of GABAergic and glutamatergic synapses in CTX and HPC was also detected in KEGG
pathways suggesting abnormal neurotransmission likely related to the epileptic and seizure
activity."*

🔴 **This is an expression programme, not a measure of inhibition.** It is *not* evidence about
the sign or strength of GABAergic transmission, and must not be recruited to the E_GABA question —
see [`CC-20260826-EGABA-EXPERIMENT-01`](CC-20260826-EGABA-EXPERIMENT-01.md). The paper's own
hedge (*"suggesting"*, *"likely related"*) is the correct one and should survive into the claim.

### 2.6 Heterozygote — **relevant to `CLAIM 032`, and it cuts both ways**

- **Supporting `CLAIM 032`:** `P47T/WT` normal on hind-limb clasping, JSET social investigation,
  TCST sociability and social novelty; *"the introduction of the P47T mutation had no detrimental
  effect on normal mouse growth or reproduction."*
- 🔴 **But the survival negative is a trend, not a null.** *"Wwox^P47T/WT mice also displayed a
  reduced overall survival in comparison to the Wwox^WT/WT, but the difference was not
  statistically significance"* [sic]. **495 ± 23 versus 542 ± 8 days is a reduction that failed to
  reach significance, and no power is reported.** "Not significant" is not "not different" when
  the design cannot resolve the difference — the identical reading applied to panel 7C of
  PMID 42422765. A claim that haploinsufficiency is *not deleterious* may not cite this as a
  demonstrated negative.
- 🔴 **Against a blanket reading:** *"various Wwox^P47T/P47T **and Wwox^P47T/WT** mice displayed
  signs of cholestasis with enlarged gallbladders, and a few animals had evidence of focal liver
  degeneration (Supplementary Table 4)."* **Heterozygotes are named in a positive finding.**
- ⚠️ *"evaluations of both Iba1 and Gfap immunostainings were also performed in Wwox^P47T/WT mice;
  however no differences were observed when compared with the wildtype mice **(data not shown)**."*
  🔴 **An unverifiable negative.** It may not be cited as evidence of heterozygote normality.
- 🔴 **Transfer limit:** `P47T/WT` is a **missense** heterozygote, not a null heterozygote.
  `CLAIM 032` is about **haploinsufficiency**. A partial-LoF allele in trans with wild type is a
  different genetic object, and this paper is at best weak corroboration — with a liver finding
  attached.

## 4. What this paper does *not* establish

- **Not epileptogenesis.** Adults, established phenotype, no longitudinal transition measured.
- **Not a null phenotype.** `P47T/P47T` is partial LoF with normal protein abundance.
- **Not blinded EEG.** Two trained observers; blinding is not stated. Contrast Obeid 2026, which
  does declare a blinded reader.
- **Not powered.** n = 3 vs 3 for EEG. The seizure counts are large and the WT/mutant separation
  is complete, but three animals is three animals.
- **No GABA reversal potential, no chloride measurement, no drug.**
