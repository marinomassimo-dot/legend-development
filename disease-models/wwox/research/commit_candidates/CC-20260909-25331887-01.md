## 1 · `CLAIM 029` — **NARROW**, keep `Status: in observation`

Four narrowings, each from a locator the audit passed or corrected:

1. **"K63 ubiquitination of WWOX on Lys274" → Lys274 is required for the *damage-induced
   increment*, not for the bulk of it.** Fig. S6C: K274R retains substantial HMW ladders, and with
   K63-only ubiquitin the **mutant equals or exceeds wild type untreated (161 vs 156)**. What it
   loses is the NCS-induced rise (WT 111→137, 156→169; K274R 116→104, 161→156).
2. **The K63 assignment rests on the K63-only / K48-only HA-Ub construct comparison (S6C), not on a
   linkage-specific antibody.** The S6B K-63-Ub panel does not discriminate GST-WWOX from GST-WFPA
   at all (53, 70, 68, 60, 67, 65). 🔴 The reciprocal correction matters as much: because S6B is a
   different antibody, blot and exposure, **its faintness is not admissible evidence against K63
   linkage**, and **S6C is strong** (K63-only 156–169 vs K48-only 86–105).
3. **"compromises γ-H2AX induction/maintenance" → induction blunted at 15 min (342→30 px) and
   resolution delayed at 24 h (1→76 px); by 48 h neither genotype retains detectable foci (0 px in
   both).** The 48 h persistence asserted in the draft reading does not exist.
4. **"ITCH-dependent" is not evidenced anywhere in this supplement** — no ITCH blot, lane, label or
   legend mention on any of the six pages. Carry it only if the main text supports it, and **mark
   its provenance**; this source does not supply it.

Two **additions**, currently absent from the claim:

- **On the WWOX side the p-ATM interaction is assigned to WW1 and not SDR** — as an **isolated-domain
  sufficiency** test (Fig. S4A). The SDR negative is genuine (GST-SDR is itself recovered) and the
  WW1-WFPA and WW2 negatives are not explained by absent substrate or bait. **WFPA reduces recovery
  below detection on a single blot; it does not abolish the interaction**, and sufficiency of an
  isolated domain is **not** a requirement within full-length WWOX.
- **On the ATM side, residues ~1,530–2,100**, with a **faint additional signal in the kinase-domain
  fragment** (Fig. S4B). The Coomassie loading is **not** comparable (GST-WWOX lanes 1.4–1.6×
  darker), so this panel **orders** fragments rather than quantifying them.

> 🔴 **Why the claim stays `in observation`, in the auditor's own terms:** across all six SI pages
> there is **not one error bar, replicate count or statistical test.**

## 2 · `CLAIM 024` — **CORROBORATE (weak), with one narrowing rider**

Fig. S4A right shows isolated **GST-WW2 (WT or YFPA) recovers no p-ATM in any lane** while isolated
**GST-WW1_WT alone is sufficient** — consistent with WW2 not being a canonical standalone
PPxY-binding domain.

> **Rider to add:** for at least one partner (ATM), the **isolated WW1 domain is sufficient** in a
> HEK293 lysate pull-down, so **tandem cooperativity is not required for every WWOX partner
> interaction**.
>
> **Caveat to record with it:** this is a **lysate** pull-down and does **not** establish a direct or
> PPxY-mediated contact.

## 4 · `CLAIM 007` and `CLAIM 023` — **LEAVE UNCHANGED**
