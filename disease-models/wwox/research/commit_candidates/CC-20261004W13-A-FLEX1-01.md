## Finding — the wave's second false negative
`PAPER 128` states: *«🔴 The paper never states which WWOX intron or exon Flex1 lies in — the fact the
hypothesis would turn on is absent from the body.»* The two halves differ in truth value.

- **"absent from the body" — true.** No sentence of Summary, Introduction, Results, Discussion or
  Experimental Procedures gives an exon, intron or genomic coordinate for any Flex region, and the
  deposited supplement gives fragment sizes and restriction sites only (`WWOX` occurs in it once).
- **"the paper never states" — false.** It is in the figure. The Figure 2 legend says *«Below it, the
  exons of the WWOX/FOR gene are represented by grey boxes»*, and panel B draws that track: its two
  labelled boxes are **WWOX exon 8** and **WWOX exon 9**, and the black boxes for Flex4, Control, Flex1
  and Flex5-p all lie between them. The paper therefore publishes Flex1 as lying **inside WWOX intron 8**.

Neither the figure images nor the supplement were in the corpus: the held artefact was the PMC reader
HTML alone. The six author-manuscript figure images were fetched from the PMC CDN blob URLs printed in
that HTML (a browser-like user agent is required), and `NIHMS28262-supplement-01.pdf` through
`framework/scripts/pmc_pow_fetch.py`, which solved the proof-of-work interstitial. All are declared with
full sha256.

**What this changes and what it does not.** It supplies the gene-relative position that `LEAD-C1` (Flex1
AT-repeat length as a candidate rearrangement-risk covariate, `IPOTESI`) was missing, so the lead is no
longer blocked on a position that might not exist — only on Finnis 2005 for the allele-length
distribution. It does **not** make the paper WWOX evidence: every measurement is a yeast
artificial-chromosome breakage or fork-stalling rate, no WWOX transcript or protein is measured, and the
record's `Transferability: T3 — OFF-AXIS` stands unchanged. Nor is it convergence with the intron-8
regulatory architecture of `DL-MECH-107`: intron 8 is most of the gene, so landing there is close to the
base rate (`PREMISE: INFERENZA`), and a replication-barrier AT repeat and a reporter-tested cis-regulatory
element are different objects.
