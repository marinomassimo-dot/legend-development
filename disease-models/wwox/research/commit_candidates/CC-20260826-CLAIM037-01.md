## The seven propositions

The existing claim fuses these. They have different evidence, different strength, and three
different fates. **`NOT_REPORTED ≠ ABSENT` is applied throughout and is the axis on which the
old claim failed.**

### A — Whole-animal `Wwox`-null seizure evidence

Split further, because behaviour and electrography have opposite answers.

**A1 — Electrographic (SWD).** 🔴 **PRESENT.**
- `DIRECT_OBSERVATION`: *"Quantification of SWD events per animal across groups (WT, KO, and KO +
  AAV9-hSynI-hWWOX [HD]). WWOX KO pups exhibited a significantly higher number of SWDs relative
  to WT, whereas WWOX-rescued pups (HD) showed a marked reduction in SWD incidence."* Read off
  panel 7E at native resolution: **WT ≈ 0.02 SWD/hour** (5 points clustered at zero),
  **KO ≈ 0.63 ± 0.09** (5 points, range 0.33–0.80), **HD ≈ 0.13 ± 0.04** (5 points).
- `MODEL`: `Wwox`-null (Aqeilan allele), littermate WT controls; transmitter implanted P14,
  continuous 24/7 wireless ECoG for **7 consecutive days**, single-channel epidural over right
  dorsal cortex, reference contralateral; traces screened for interictal spikes and SWDs by a
  **blinded** investigator.
- `N`: **5 pups per group.**
- `STATISTICAL_RESULT`: **`****` WT-vs-KO** and **`****` KO-vs-HD**; **`ns` WT-vs-HD**
  (figure legend: `**** p < 0.0001`). ≈ 30× elevation, fully reversed by neuronal restoration.
- `LIMITATION`: n = 5; single channel; the paper itself says *"Although based on a limited
  cohort"*; ECoG acquisition began **immediately after isoflurane surgery with no recovery
  interval**, so the earliest recording hours carry post-anaesthetic and post-surgical state;
  recording starts at **P14**, so onset earlier than P14 is not covered; Methods declare
  mean ± SD while the Figure 7 legend declares mean ± SEM.
- `EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: **`DATO`.** And it is not merely associative: genotype → phenotype →
  **rescue reverses it to `ns` against WT** is the strongest causal design for a seizure-adjacent
  phenotype anywhere in the WWOX corpus.

**A2 — Electrographic (interictal spike rate).** **UNINFORMATIVE — and it is uninformative in
both directions.**
- `DIRECT_OBSERVATION`: panel 7B (`Spikes/hour/day`, days 1–7): every KO day-bar ≈ **7.8–12.0**,
  every WT day-bar ≈ **2.2–2.7**; complete separation across all seven days, **no significance
  marker drawn anywhere on the panel**. Panel 7C (`Average spikes/day`) prints **`0.2000`** over
  the WT-vs-KO bracket, with no asterisk and no `ns`.
- `N`: legend declares **n = 5 per group**. 🔴 **Panel 7C plots three KO points (≈ 11.8, 8.5,
  8.2) and two WT points (≈ 2.05, 2.10).** Read at ~9× from `gr7.jpg`.
- `STATISTICAL_RESULT`: 🔴 **`0.2000` is the floor of a two-tailed Mann–Whitney on n = 3 vs
  n = 2.** With complete separation, U = 0 and p = 2/C(5,2) = **exactly 0.2000**; that test
  **cannot return a smaller number** at those group sizes. The **declared** test — *"two-tailed
  unpaired Student's t tests"* — applied to the plotted values gives Welch t = 6.44, df = 2,
  **p ≈ 0.023**. At the declared n = 5 vs 5 with complete separation, a Mann–Whitney returns
  **p = 0.0079**.
- `LIMITATION`: the printed p is inconsistent with the declared test on the plotted data, and
  exactly consistent with a rank test at its power floor. The running text calls this comparison
  *"a significant elevation"*, which the panel does not print.
- `EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: **`UNRESOLVED`.** 🔴 **The correct reading is neither the text's nor the
  naive one.** The text over-claims significance; but reading `p = 0.2000` as evidence of *no
  elevation* is the same error with the sign flipped — a design that cannot produce a smaller
  p than 0.2 has not tested the hypothesis. **`0.2000` here means "not tested", not "not
  different".** `PREMISE_TAG: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK` on *"p > 0.05 means no effect"*.
- *(This refines `deepdive_manifests/PMID42422765.json` entry 4, which caught the text-versus-panel
  contradiction but read the panel as a plain negative and did not count the plotted points.)*

**A3 — Behavioural.** **NOT MEASURED in any Wwox-null mouse paper.**
- The one positive assertion is PMID 33914858 p. 3065: *"mice begin to show signs of growth
  retardation **and seizures** until they succumb by 3–4 weeks of age (Fig. 1A–C)"*. Adjudicated
  on the rendered page: **Fig. 1A–C is a photograph at P18, a weight curve and a Kaplan-Meier
  curve.** No seizure measurement. `EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: **`IPOTESI`** — an assertion whose cited
  figure does not contain it.

### B — Seizure reporting in the early null papers

| Paper | What it says about null-mouse seizures | EEG performed? |
|---|---|---|
| PMID 17360458 (Aqeilan 2007, *PNAS*) | nothing — `seizure`, `ataxi`, `behavi`, `motor` occur **zero** times | no |
| PMID 18487609 (Aqeilan 2008, *JBC*) | *"Although homozygous mice were runted, **they did not exhibit any abnormal behavior or impaired motor skills**"* | no |
| PMID 19936220 (Ludes-Meyers 2009) | epileptogenesis not measured in any form; the only brain measurement is organ weight | no |
| PMID 19500159 (Suzuki 2009) | states three times, plus a Table 2 whose `Epilepsy` row is empty for both mouse models, that null mice show no epilepsy — **a literature survey over papers that never did EEG** | no |

`EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: **`NOT_REPORTED`, and for PMID 18487609 an explicit *behavioural* negative.**
🔴 **None of the four is an electrographic negative, because none of the four recorded.** The
2026 ECoG result is not in conflict with them: it measures a quantity they never measured. This
is `NOT_REPORTED ≠ ABSENT` in its purest form, and the seventeen-year gap is the cost of having
treated the two as equivalent.

### C — Continuous EEG evidence

**PERFORMED, once, in 2026.** Design and limits as in A1. `EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: **`DATO` for SWD,
`UNRESOLVED` for spike rate.**

🔴 **Not to be inferred from this:** that continuous EEG was *normal* in any other model, allele
or age. Continuous ECoG exists for **one allele, one age window (P14→P21), one laboratory, n = 5**.
No continuous EEG exists for: the Aldaz/EIIA null, `Wwox^+/−`, `Nes-Cre`, `Syn-Cre`, the `P47T`
knock-in, or any animal outside P14–P21.

### D — `Syn-Cre` neuronal conditional phenotype

- `DIRECT_OBSERVATION`: *"S-KO mice also exhibited uncontrolled spontaneous tonic-clonic seizures
  beginning at P9 and ranging from several seconds to a few minutes (68.5 ± 13.4 s; n = 6 of
  P14–18)"* (PMID 33914858 p. 3065, rendered page). Nestin-Cre: *"N-KO mice showed
  tremors/seizures"*. Slice correlate: **86 % of S-KO slices burst (36/42) vs 0 % of WT (0/11)**,
  Fisher two-sided **p = 1.6 × 10⁻⁷** (computed here).
- `MODEL`: `Wwox^flox/flox; Synapsin-Cre⁺` and `Wwox^flox/flox; Nestin-Cre⁺`.
- `N`: 6 animals for seizure duration; 42 slices / 24 animals for bursting.
- `LIMITATION`: 🔴 seizures documented by *"a mobile camera, when monitoring the mice at the
  animal facility"* (Methods, rendered p. 3064) — **opportunistic behavioural video, not
  continuous video-EEG**. No EEG in this model.
- `EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: **`DATO`** for behavioural seizures and slice bursting;
  **`NOT_MEASURED`** for electrography.

### F — Survival

| Model | Survival | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `Wwox^−/−` Aqeilan allele | 100 % to ~day 19; 0 % by day 25 (WT n = 10, KO n = 11, log-rank **P = 0.0023**) | PMID 33914858 Fig. 1C, rendered page |
| `Wwox^ΔCre/ΔCre` Aldaz/EIIA allele | **43 % dead by 72 h**, 77 % by day 17, none past weaning | PMID 19936220 |
| `Syn-Cre` S-KO | premature death by 3–4 weeks (S-Control n = 13, S-KO n = 15, **P = 0.0001**) | PMID 33914858 Fig. 1I, rendered page |
| `Olig2-Cre` / `GFAP-Cre` | 100 % to ~120 days, **P = 1.0** | PMID 33914858 Fig. 1L/1O, rendered page |

🔴 **The two nulls are not the same animal.** `EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: `DATO`.

### G — The explanation *"they die before seizures could occur"*

🔴 **FALSIFIED, on three independent grounds.**

1. A mouse with the **same 3–4-week lifespan** (`Syn-Cre` S-KO) seizes **from P9**.
2. The null **itself** shows SWDs at P14–P21 — inside its own lifespan, `p < 0.0001`.
3. The explanation was never a measurement: it was offered by PMID 19500159's authors as a
   possibility and imported by `CLAIM 037` as a live hypothesis.

`EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: **retract.** What replaces it is not a competing biological explanation but
a methodological one: **the phenotype was not reported because nobody recorded it until 2026.**

## Missing decisive experiment

**Continuous video-EEG, P7–P21, five arms, one design:** `Wwox^−/−` (Aqeilan allele),
`Wwox^ΔCre/ΔCre` (Aldaz/EIIA allele), `Nes-Cre`, `Syn-Cre`, `Wwox^+/−`; simultaneous video so
electrographic and behavioural events are scored on the same animals; recording from **P7**, five
days earlier than the only existing dataset, because the conditional's behavioural onset is P9;
n ≥ 8/arm — the existing n = 5 is why 7C sits at its statistical floor.

Primary outcomes: SWD rate/hour; interictal spike rate; electro-behavioural concordance.
This single design closes propositions A2, C, E and the `Wwox^+/−` arm that
[`CC-20260826-CLAIM032-01`](CC-20260826-CLAIM032-01.md) needs.
