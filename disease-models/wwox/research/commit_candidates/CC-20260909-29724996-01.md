## 2 · The three boundaries this reading writes onto the record

### 2.1 The ChIP experiment is closed at five promoters, and PDK1 and LDHA1 are not among them

`Supplement Table 2` lists the complete ChIP primer battery: **mPKM, mGAPDH, mHK2, mGlut1, mAldoa**,
plus two negative-region controls. The paper's central positive result — HIF1α occupancy on
glycolytic promoters, `entries[0]`/`entries[1]` — therefore **does not establish promoter occupancy at
Pdk1 or Ldha**. Those two are measured by RT-PCR only.

This matters beyond the paper: **`PMID 30755385`** (muscle-specific knockout, `PAPER 061`) carries the
PDK1 bridge in this repository, and a reader combining the two records could easily conclude that
HIF1α occupancy at *Pdk1* has been demonstrated somewhere. It has not. `entries[30]`.

### 2.2 The glycolytic target set is context-dependent, and the flagship gene is the one that drops out

Under high-fat diet at 11 months (`Supplement Fig S4C`) **PKM2 is not elevated** — it sits slightly
below control, unmarked, direction reversed — while PDK1, LDHA1 and ENO1 carry marks. PKM2 is the
target with the highest ChIP enrichment (Fig 4f) and the only one with protein-level IHC confirmation
(Fig 4e). So *"HIF1α glycolytic targets rise when WWOX is lost"* is **not a fixed gene set**: which
members move depends on metabolic context, within the same tissue and the same species.
`entries[33]`.

### 2.3 Tumour load is not normalised to body weight, and the Methods contradict the Results

- Results: «tumor load, as assessed by liver weight **relative to body weight** …» (`entries[2]`)
- Fig 2E axis: `Liver Weight (g)` (`entries[3]`)
- Methods: «**Tumor load was assessed by liver weight in grams.**» (`entries[25]`, **new**)

The Methods side with the panel against the Results sentence. And `Fig 7C`'s axis reads
`Liver Weight % of BW` (`entries[26]`, **new**) — the authors possess the normalised measure and apply
it where tumour burden is not the endpoint. `Supplement Fig S2C` shows liver weight is **not
monotonic** across 1/3/6 months, which is what an unnormalised burden proxy would not predict.

## 5 · What must NOT be propagated

- **No neural transfer.** No endpoint in this paper is neurological — no EEG, no behaviour, no brain
  histology. Transfer verdict is **`ESPANSIONE`**, mechanistic and of principle only.
- **No therapeutic promotion of digoxin.** This reading *narrows* what the digoxin experiment shows:
  the rescue panel omits PKM2 and HK2, PDK1 rises under digoxin in both knockout and wild type without
  a significance mark, and the χ² is computed **on the control arm only** (`p = 0.3`, n = 4 treated).
  There is no tumour-incidence test in the knockout. BLOCK-1 is not engaged because nothing is
  promoted.
- **No mouse datum for WWOX loss in spontaneous liver tumours.** `Supplement Fig S1A` shows ~51 %
  reduction with **no significance mark**, n=3 vs n=3.
- **The human premise carries three cohort sizes** — 438 (Results and caption), 434 (Methods), 417
  (the survival panel's own legend, n=209 + n=208). Any record citing the TCGA analysis must name
  which number it used.
