### PART 1 — the Evidence boundary block (appended as a new field; nothing above it is edited)
**«Evidence boundary — what the sources show on binding, on Tau and on S9 (2026-11-20, `CC-20261120-ORCHESTRATOR-CLAIM035-S9-WORDING-01` part 1, class MINOR, additive; the Title, the Summary, the Clinical meaning and the S9-independence assertion are NOT rewritten by this block).**
* **B1 · Directness.** The authors state «We demonstrated biochemically that WWOX can bind directly to GSK3β through its short-chain alcohol dehydrogenase/reductase domain» (Abstract); the binding assays are GST pull-down of fragments produced in E. coli and incubated with cell lysates (cell type not stated), co-immunoprecipitation in SH-SY5Y cells and one immunoprecipitation from a mouse brain extract (Fig 2e: «Mouse brain extract was subjected to immunoprecipitation using anti-WWOX or anti-GSK3β»; strain, age, region and n are not reported in the text or legends, and the blot image was not examined for an IgG lane); the authors also cite colocalisation by immunofluorescence (Fig 2b) and molecular modelling (Fig 3f to h) as support; no purified-to-purified binding assay is reported.
* **B2 · L404 and the motif.** In the GST pull-down «the mutation of L404A, but not L311A, completely abolishes the binding of WWOX to GSK3β» (Fig 3d); in the co-immunoprecipitation «this interaction was diminished when L404 mutated to alanine» (Fig 3e); there is no internal deletion of 388–407 and no 1–407 construct, L311A (a residue inside 296–320 by its number; the alignment names 296–320 and 388–412 as carrying the motif) does not abolish the binding; INFERENZA: the 296–320 stretch is therefore not shown dispensable (the authors write that WWOX «physically interacts with GSK3β only through the WWOX388−407 region»).
* **B3 · The double manipulation.** The authors read it as «indicating that Tau is the effector of both WWOX and GSK3β» (Fig 6); the text also reports «the neurite outgrowth stimulated by RA was abolished when Tau was knocked down» (lanes 1–3); INFERENZA: the double manipulation is therefore partly confounded by loss of the baseline response.
* **B4 · What is measured.** no cell-derived GSK3β kinase assay is described: in cells GSK3β activity is inferred from Tau phosphorylation at S396 and S404, neurite outgrowth and the S9 blots of Fig 1b (the authors' headings state a regulation of kinase activity); kinase activity is measured only in vitro with purified components; the kinase assay and the microtubule assay are cell-free; the neuronal readout is SH-SY5Y cells treated with retinoic acid, not neurons of a Wwox-deficient animal.
* **B5 · S9.** The only measurement of S9 phosphorylation in this paper concerns the retinoic-acid time course of cells for which no transfection is mentioned, while WWOX expression rises: «the phosphorylation levels of phospho-GSK3β S9 and phospho-β-catenin remained normal» (Fig 1b); it is a correlation; no experiment manipulates WWOX and reads S9 phosphorylation in the text and legends of the paper (the supplementary figures and the figure images were not examined), and the phrase «S9-independent» occurs nowhere in the text. The registry's warning that a phospho-S9 western would give a false negative rests on that correlation. The only observation of phospho-S9 in Wwox-null brain ([[claim_registry_current#CLAIM 016]]: Cheng 2020, Fig 7c, postnatal day 20: «Increased activation of GSK3β was determined in the cerebellum, hippocampus and cerebral cortex of Wwox−/− mice at postnatal day 20, as evidenced by dephosphorylation of GSK3β at Ser9»; «The representative results of four independent experiments are shown»; the legend says the numbers depict the ratios; no statistical test is attached to that panel (the Methods give only a general one-way ANOVA sentence not tied to any panel); `STATISTICAL_STATUS: NOT_TESTED` in the registry) shows phospho-S9 LOWER, the opposite direction: the two sources are in tension and neither is shown to be right.
* **B6 · The S9 question as a measurement hypothesis (INFERENZA).** If the loss of WWOX de-repressed GSK3β without changing phospho-S9, a phospho-S9 western would not see it; phospho-S9 alone is therefore neither shown to be a false negative nor shown to be a valid readout; a readout of GSK3β activity in a WWOX-DEE study would read the substrate (Tau S396 and S404), Tyr216 and Ser9 together with total GSK-3.
* **Use constraint (D6) unchanged:** CLAIM 035 and CLAIM 016 may not cite each other as corroboration on the Ser9 axis until one horn closes.
