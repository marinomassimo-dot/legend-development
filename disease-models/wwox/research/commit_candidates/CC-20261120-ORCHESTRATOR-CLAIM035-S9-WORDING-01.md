## 2 · Proposed wording (old = verbatim registry text of CLAIM 035; every new interpretive sentence carries its anchor sentence copied from the held bytes, or says INFERENZA; section 4.2.1 of `prompt_batch_commit.md`)

### PART 1 — the Evidence boundary block (appended as a new field; nothing above it is edited)
**«Evidence boundary — what the sources show on binding, on Tau and on S9 (2026-11-20, `CC-20261120-ORCHESTRATOR-CLAIM035-S9-WORDING-01` part 1, class MINOR, additive; the Title, the Summary, the Clinical meaning and the S9-independence assertion are NOT rewritten by this block).**
* **B1 · Directness.** The authors state «We demonstrated biochemically that WWOX can bind directly to GSK3β through its short-chain alcohol dehydrogenase/reductase domain» (Discussion); the assays are GST pull-down of fragments produced in E. coli and incubated with cell lysates (cell type not stated), co-immunoprecipitation in SH-SY5Y cells and one immunoprecipitation from a mouse brain extract (Fig 2e: «Mouse brain extract was subjected to immunoprecipitation using anti-WWOX or anti-GSK3β»; strain, age, region, IgG control and n are not reported); no purified-to-purified binding assay is reported.
* **B2 · L404 and the motif.** In the GST pull-down «the mutation of L404A, but not L311A, completely abolishes the binding of WWOX to GSK3β» (Fig 3d); in the co-immunoprecipitation «this interaction was diminished when L404 mutated to alanine» (Fig 3e); there is no internal deletion of 388–407 and no 1–407 construct, L311A in the other motif-carrying stretch (296–320) does not abolish the binding, and that stretch is not shown dispensable.
* **B3 · Epistasis.** The authors read it as «indicating that Tau is the effector of both WWOX and GSK3β» (Fig 6); the text also reports that knockdown of Tau abolishes the retinoic-acid-stimulated increase on its own (lane 4), so the epistasis is partly confounded by loss of the baseline response.
* **B4 · What is measured.** GSK3β activity itself is not measured in cells (it is inferred from Tau phosphorylation at S396 and S404); the kinase assay and the microtubule assay use purified components (cell-free); the neuronal readout is SH-SY5Y cells treated with retinoic acid, not neurons of a Wwox-deficient animal.
* **B5 · S9.** The only statement on S9 phosphorylation concerns the retinoic-acid time course, while endogenous WWOX rises: «phospho-GSK3β S9 and phospho-β-catenin remained normal» (Fig 1b); it is a correlation; no experiment manipulates WWOX and reads S9 phosphorylation, and the phrase «S9-independent» occurs nowhere in the paper. The registry's warning that a phospho-S9 western would give a false negative rests on that correlation. The only observation of phospho-S9 in Wwox-null brain ([[claim_registry_current#CLAIM 016]]: Cheng 2020, one lane per genotype and region, `STATISTICAL_STATUS: NOT_TESTED`) shows phospho-S9 LOWER, the opposite direction: the two sources are in tension and neither is shown to be right.
* **B6 · The S9 question as a measurement hypothesis (INFERENZA).** If the loss of WWOX de-repressed GSK3β without changing phospho-S9, a phospho-S9 western would not see it; phospho-S9 alone is therefore neither shown to be a false negative nor shown to be a valid readout; a readout of GSK3β activity in a WWOX-DEE study would read the substrate (Tau S396 and S404), Tyr216 and Ser9 together with total GSK-3.
* **Use constraint (D6) unchanged:** CLAIM 035 and CLAIM 016 may not cite each other as corroboration on the Ser9 axis until one horn closes.
