## The defect

CLAIM 016 asserts an **abundance** datum in two fields:

- `**Type:**` — *"DATO (**abbondanza**, murino) …"*
- `**Summary:**` — *"In Wwox-null mice, **GSK3β is elevated** in cortex, hippocampus and cerebellum…"*

The primary asserts **activation**, never abundance, and names its evidence:

> *"a significantly increased **activation** of glycogen synthase kinase 3β (GSK3β) occurs in
> Wwox−/− mouse cerebral cortex, hippocampus and cerebellum"*

> Fig. 7 legend, panel c: *"**Increased activation** of GSK3β was determined … as evidenced by
> **dephosphorylation of GSK3β at Ser9**"*

Figure 7c densitometry (manifest entry 2, read at 3× on the native 1946×1627 image), across
`+/+` / `+/−` / `−/−`:

| Measure | Cerebellum | Hippocampus | Cortex |
|---|---|---|---|
| pGSK3β(Ser9) | 2.7 / 3.1 / **1.3** | 3.6 / 3.5 / **2.0** | 3.9 / 3.8 / **2.5** |
| **total GSK3β** | 2.2 / 2.4 / **2.4** | 2.3 / 2.4 / **2.6** | 2.2 / 2.4 / **2.6** |

**Total GSK3β is flat in every genotype and region.** Inhibitory Ser9 phosphorylation is reduced
in `−/−`. The mechanism is **dis-inhibition**, not increased abundance.

## Why the existing corrective wording does not already cover this

CLAIM 016 carries a later `Meccanismo aggiunto (BATCH_20260726_001)` block that shifts the causal
statement *"da «GSK3β è elevata» … a «GSK3β è de-repressa»"*. That block argues from the **Wang
2012** system (PAPER 056) and leaves the Cheng 2020 fields untouched. Its `PREMISE_TAG` reads:

> *"la premessa portante di CLAIM 016 è che l'abbondanza di proteina GSK3β riporti l'attività …
> `PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK`"*

That frames the problem as an untested **inference from abundance to activity**. In Cheng 2020
abundance was **measured and was flat**, and the paper's claim was always about phospho-Ser9. The
premise tag therefore describes a defect the claim does not have while the one it does have
persists — and the `Evidence boundary` paragraph reaffirms *"Cosa NON cambia: **il dato di
abbondanza**"*, preserving a datum Figure 7c does not support.
