**Proposed addition:**

> Independent of the ATM leg, *Wwox* loss changes DNA-repair **pathway choice** in primary, non-transformed, non-engineered cells: in murine splenic B cells undergoing immunoglobulin class-switch recombination, Sμ–Sγ1 junctions shift away from classical end joining toward microhomology-mediated joining — blunt joins fall from 26/77 to 5/61 (p < 0.001) and junctions with long microhomology rise from 1/77 to 8/61 (p < 0.01), mean overlap 1.2 → 2.6 bp — while **junction-adjacent mutation frequency is unchanged** (5.2 vs 5.0 × 10⁻³/bp, p = 0.8) and **AID protein is not elevated**. Spontaneous *Myc/Igh* translocations increase (p < 0.05), **magnitude unresolved — the text says 2.5-fold and Figure 6C's bars read ≈ 6.5-fold** (PMID 31275852, `FTR-20260914-31275852-01`). **Why this wording.** It adds a *choice* endpoint beside the existing *burden* endpoint, which is why it does not collide with the PMID 41562193 qualification: unchanged mutation frequency with changed junction structure is precisely the pattern a burden measurement cannot see. ### 🔴 The finding is half confirmed and half refuted, measured in the artefact

The orchestrator read the paper's own Methods in `files/fulltext/PMID31275852_McBride2019.xml`
before accepting the finding, and the two halves do not stand or fall together.

**Refuted — "germline" is correct for the repair experiments.** Methods, verbatim: *"Naïve B cells
were purified from spleens of wild-type and **Wwox KO** 16–17 day-old mice (38, 39)"*, and the
Methods describe that line separately from the conditionals: *"BK5-Cre TG and Cd19 Cre mice (32, 33)
**and the protocol to generate Wwox flox/flox and Wwox knock-out (KO) mice** have been previously
described (9, 34)"*. So the CSR, junction and translocation experiments use the whole-body KO line,
not the `Cd19`-restricted model and not the `BK5-Cre TG/TG × Wwox flox/wt` cross, which is the
mammary arm. The age, 16–17 days, matches a null that dies at 3–4 weeks. The consultation's
diagnosis — that the knockout is Cre-driven — is therefore **not true of these experiments**, and the
reader's manifest word stands. No persisted locator is corrected.

**Confirmed — "non-engineered" must go.** The cells are primary and non-transformed, but the animal
is engineered. In canon, beside "germline", the adjective reads as a spontaneous null. Replace with
what is measured: *primary, non-transformed cells from a whole-body `Wwox`-null line*.

**Therefore, for propagation:** (1) name the model **per experiment**, since this paper switches
between a `Cd19`-conditional tumour cohort and a whole-body null for mechanism; (2) keep "germline"
or write "whole-body null", and **drop "non-engineered"**; (3) carry the Cre-effect caveat only where
a Cre-driven arm is cited, which the proposed sentence does not do.
