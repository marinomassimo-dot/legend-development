## 3 · Proposal 2 — correct the model description wherever the corpus says "whole-body"

🔴 **This is the substantive correction and it is a narrowing, not a reversal.** The phenomenon stands; the
scope attached to it does not.

- The paper says *"global lung silencing"* and *"acute, global knockdown of lung WWOX expression"*.
  **"Global" is modified by "lung" in both places** — global across lung cell types, by airway delivery.
- The sequel says *"prior global knockdown experiments"*, meaning global relative to its own cell-type-
  restricted allele.
- **Neither means whole-body**, and the corpus rendered it as whole-body.

Proposed: wherever a non-canonical reasoning surface or a registry record describes `PMID 28283473` as a
*whole-body* or *global* knockdown model, it should read **"acute intratracheal (airway-delivered) lung
knockdown in wild-type mice, epithelium-weighted by imported citation"**. Known sites are listed in §7 for
the orchestrator; **I have edited none of them**, because none is a surface this dispatch gives me.

**And one attribution to fix.** *"Widespread pulmonary neutrophilic inflammation"* is carried in the corpus
as though quoted from this paper. The string `widespread` **does not occur anywhere in `PMID 28283473`**;
the phrase is the sequel's own wording. This paper says *"neutrophilic alveolitis"* and *"neutrophil
influx"*. Nothing substantive turns on it; a quoted phrase attributed to the wrong source is still worth
correcting at the source.
