## 1 · Proposed: a new `PAPER` record that carries the expression of concern as a FIELD

`paper_registry_current.md` holds **no record** for PMID 16223882 today, while
`full_text_queue_current.md` `FT-076` already names it as the source of the Ad-WWOX construct and
as a multi-hop debt from PMID 20530675. A record is proposed with these values:

- **Status:** `background_only` — oncology, no CNS material, no reference-genotype allele.
- **Evidence depth:** `full text reviewed`, backed by `FTR-20260909-16223882-01`.
- **Transferability:** T3 · **clinical relevance:** LOW · **Role:** reagent provenance.
- 🔴 **`Editorial status:` `EXPRESSION_OF_CONCERN — PMID 28373548, PNAS 2017;114(16):E3365, standing as of 2026-09-09; NOT a retraction`.**

**The last field is the substance of this candidate.** A standing expression of concern is a
property of the source, and a registry that records `Evidence depth: full text reviewed` without it
would let a future reader cite the paper as unqualified. It belongs in the record, in a named
field, not in prose at the end of a Note. If the registry schema has no such field, then the
finding is that it needs one — reported here rather than improvised.

## 3 · Three qualifications that are the reading's own, and are NOT what the notice is about

Found by reading the paper, not by reading the notice:

1. **The *in vivo* arm never shows Wwox inside a tumour.** *"Wwox expression was not found in any
   of the tumors"*, induced arm included. The authors' account is selection; the alternative — an
   infection/induction-efficiency ceiling — is not excluded. So the paper shows that cells
   **pre-treated** to express Wwox form fewer tumours; it does not show that sustained Wwox
   expression suppresses an established tumour.
2. **The "intrinsic pathway" is asserted on data shown in no figure.** Figure 3B, read as pixels,
   has no caspase 9 and no caspase 8 row — the Methods list both antisera. The intrinsic pathway is
   defined by caspase 9. `IPOTESI`, not `DATO`.
3. **"Completely suppressed" (H460) is contradicted by the paper's own figure and table** — Figure
   5A's box sits above the axis and Table 1 gives 0.03 ± 0.04 g.
