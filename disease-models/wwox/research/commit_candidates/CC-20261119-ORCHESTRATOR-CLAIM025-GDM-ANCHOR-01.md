## 4b · REVISION 1 (2026-11-19, after SCI's blind locator audit; this replaces section 4)
Source of the revision: SCI's note `advisor_pilot_20261113/brainstorm_20261116/claim025_gdm_anchor_sci_20261119/CLAIM025_GDM_ANCHOR_CHECK_SCI_20261119.md` (sections 2 to 5) and the audit report as delivered in the same folder (14 triples, one delegate, sonnet/high, read back x10/high). The audit found all quotes of section 3 VERBATIM and three proposed sentences wrong:
1. **'WWOX shows only a tendency'** followed the Results paragraph, but the paper is internally inconsistent: the Abstract says 'a significant downregulation of WWOX', the Discussion and Conclusions 'downregulated', Fig 2A marks it * (p = 0.0406, Mann-Whitney, uncorrected). Both statements must be given. **'The ratio is lower because of the HIF1A change'** is not a statement of the source (the paper presents the ratio as a better combined indicator and never apportions it; the ratio also tracks WWOX, R 0.61 in GDM and 0.65 in NGT per the auditor): by the stated folds the HIF1A change is larger (ln 3.8 = 1.34 against ln 1.4 = 0.34): an INFERENCE, to be labelled so.
2. **'WWOX is not correlated with the glycolytic genes in GDM'** is wrong for PFK (R = -0.23, p < 0.05); right for HK2, PKM2, SLC2A1 and HIF1A (about -0.05, Fig 4 only); WWOX against NFKB1 is -0.22 and against CTNNB1 positive. **'A ratio with HIF1A in its denominator'** is the dossier's inference: the word 'denominator' occurs nowhere in the paper.
3. **The sentence the first proposal removed was PARTLY right:** the paper reports weak ratio correlations with NFKB1 (-0.21, p < 0.05), DVL2 (-0.21, p = 0.05) and CTNNB1 (+0.38, p < 0.001); no association with CRP; they stay as weak, uncorrected, mRNA-level, with the CTNNB1 sign.
Also: the Abstract says HK2 is overexpressed in GDM while the Results and Fig 2E say unchanged (the claim must not inherit the Abstract's statement); no multiple-testing correction is named (absent by two term sets; 11 genes; Bonferroni over 11 genes would be about 0.0045, which WWOX 0.0406 and SLC2A4 0.0134 would not meet: the auditor's arithmetic); text-against-figure disagreements (Fig 6C and 6E; two SLC2A4 asterisks) are listed in SCI's note.
**Revised wording, SCI's restatement (section 5 of its note), adopted without change:**
* **First sentence:** «In blood leukocytes of 98 women with GDM and 37 with normal glucose tolerance (Baryla 2021, PMID 33520443; one clinic, GDM women older), HIF1A transcript is 3.8-fold higher (p < 0.0001) and WWOX 1.4-fold lower (p = 0.0406, uncorrected; the Results call it a tendency, the Abstract a significant downregulation, the Discussion and Conclusions a downregulation); the WWOX/HIF1A index is about 6.5-fold lower (p < 0.0001); the paper does not apportion the ratio change, and by the stated fold changes the HIF1A change is the larger (inference).»
