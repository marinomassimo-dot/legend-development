## 1 · CHANGE CLASS AND THE SPLIT (declared, with the reasoning both ways)
**Declared class: PART 1 MINOR (additive); PART 2 MAJOR (proposed, reserved for the operator).**

*Why not simply MINOR.* `CLAIM 011` is `flagged for review`, not `consolidated baseline`, so the baseline-reversal clause of `LEGEND_CORE` section 9 is not met on its letter, and the adjudication of 2026-10-21 (`CC-20261021-SCIENTIST-C-CLAIM011-DOSE-ADJUDICATION-01`) was MINOR. Figure 3B refuses it for survival»). Rewording them changes what an inference is allowed to say in the direction of the very inference the sentence was written to block, and the new measurement disagrees with two legs of that same adjudication: the adjudication narrowed «dose-dependent» away from **survival** (leg 2, «Survival is a step, not a gradient») and **body weight** (leg 3), whereas SCI's card reads the low dose as intermediate on weight (P10, P15) and on survival to P60. A rewording that carries those two readings therefore **reverses the polarity of an adjudicated verdict on a red-flagged guardrail**, and the `CLAIM 011` Title («... NOT on survival, body weight, ECoG/SWD, myelination or gliosis») would then disagree with the working model unless it is changed in a coordinated act. I treat that as a baseline-statement reversal in effect, so **MAJOR**, with the same split the claim-035 candidate used.
*The argument for MINOR* (for the operator to weigh): the Status is not a baseline status, the direction of the correction is toward the source, and the card `C01` already carries the corrected statement («Survival: a step and a steep continuum are indistinguishable; threshold not located.»), so the working model would only catch up with the repository's own card.
