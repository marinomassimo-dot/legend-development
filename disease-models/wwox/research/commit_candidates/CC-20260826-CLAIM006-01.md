### Two readings the panels do not decide between

1. **The Purkinje "improvement" (≈25 → ≈42).** Most plausibly **survivor selection** — the
   250-day cohort is drawn from animals that lived. It could also be a genuine plateau with
   cohort variance. ⚠️ **Neither is supported over the other by these panels**, and it must not
   be reported as recovery.
2. **The Iba1 area-fraction gap.** Widens 6.5 → 9.5 percentage points, but **entirely because
   wild-type microglial area falls** 14.5 → 11. Whether that WT decline is normal ageing or a
   staining/cohort effect is not addressed by the paper.

## 2. Minimum repair

### `CLAIM 006` — Title

**BEFORE:** `P47T model shows progressive neuroinflammation`

**AFTER:** `P47T model shows progressive astrogliosis; microgliosis is early and progresses only in morphology`

**WHY_MINIMUM:** the word `progressive` is the single false element. It is not deleted — it is
**bound to the lineage it is true of**. Nothing else in the title changes.

### `CLAIM 006` — Summary

**BEFORE:** `P47T murine model shows progressive microgliosis and astrogliosis.`

**AFTER:**
> In the `Wwox^P47T/P47T` hippocampus, **astrogliosis progresses** between 80 and 250 days —
> Gfap⁺ cell number ≈39 → ≈56 and Gfap⁺ area fraction ≈24 % → ≈40 %, each carrying a
> within-genotype significance bracket. **Microgliosis is established by 80 days and does not
> progress in abundance**: Iba1⁺ number is ≈34 at both ages and area fraction ≈21 % → ≈20.5 %,
> neither carrying a within-genotype bracket, and the widening area gap is driven by a **decline
> in the wild type** (≈14.5 % → ≈11 %). What does progress is **microglial morphology** —
> branches, junctions and total branch length all fall with within-genotype significance.
> Olig2⁺ oligodendrocyte loss is established by 80 days and flat.

**WHY_MINIMUM:** it preserves every measured direction the old Summary asserted (both lineages
*are* elevated vs WT at both ages, `*`), and changes only the **time course**, which is the part
the figures contradict. **No measured elevation is withdrawn.**
