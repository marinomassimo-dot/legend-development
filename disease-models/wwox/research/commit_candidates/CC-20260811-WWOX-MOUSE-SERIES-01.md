# Commit candidate — Wwox mouse series

**Candidate:** `CC-20260811-WWOX-MOUSE-SERIES-01`  
**Scope:** PMID 23254685, 18974271, 17575124, 17360458  
**Authority:** evidence package only; no canonical scientific file changed. Promotion remains an operator `BATCH_COMMIT`.

## Proposed canonical effects

1. **Qualify `CLAIM 036` rather than broadening it.** Global Wwox-null mice reproducibly show early systemic collapse plus bone and endocrine abnormalities. The endocrine paper cannot separate gonadal cell autonomy from pituitary suppression, developmental delay or terminal metabolic illness.
2. **Keep the osteosarcoma statement explicitly conflicting.** PMID 17360458 reports morphology-compatible periosteal lesions in 4/13 nulls; PMID 23254685 repeats rare malignant-appearing osteoblasts without an incidence denominator; PMID 19936220 reports 0/9 by necropsy, X-ray, histology and microCT. Do not promote “Wwox-null causes osteosarcoma” as settled.
3. **Qualify `CLAIM 032` by context.** Adult heterozygotes can be physiologically unremarkable in unchallenged assays yet show strong tumor susceptibility after NMBA or ENU. This is a genotype–stress interaction, not evidence that untreated heterozygotes phenocopy biallelic disease.
4. **Do not use protein positivity as proof of strict haploinsufficiency.** Neither tumor study genotyped or otherwise established integrity of the residual allele in tumor cells.

## Exact proposed language

> `ESPANSIONE — mouse oncology/genetics:` Global Wwox loss produces early multisystem illness, so bone, gonadal and brain-adjacent phenotypes from juvenile systemic nulls cannot by themselves establish cell autonomy. Heterozygous mice show strong carcinogen-dependent tumor susceptibility, but this does not imply an unchallenged carrier phenotype. Juvenile osteosarcoma remains conflicting evidence (4/13 morphology-positive in PMID 17360458; rare atypical cells without incidence in PMID 23254685; 0/9 by multimodal examination in PMID 19936220).
