  with a monotonically tapering streak off lane 1's saturated right tip**: deficit decays

  against lane 1's 92-px band core. Its centroid sits y ≈ 1244 against lane 1's y ≈ 1230 — at the
  **lower edge** of the wild-type band's height, not at it. **No band edges, no lane registration.**

- **The control the auditor ran against its own conclusion, and it is why this is `UNRESOLVED`
