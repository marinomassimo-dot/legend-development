## 4. Refined decisive experiment

**Myelin phenotyping of the existing `Olig2-Cre; Wwox^flox/flox` line — no new model.**

| Endpoint | Method | Timepoints | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **g-ratio** | TEM, corpus callosum + optic nerve, ≥ 100 axons/animal | P17, P60 | the endpoint that **normalises** under neuronal rescue in `PAPER 005`; if it is abnormal here, the two mechanisms are additive |
| **Unmyelinated axon count / field** | TEM, same sections | P17, P60 | the endpoint that stayed **significant against** the neuronal rescue (~26 WT vs ~52 treated) — the single best candidate for the residual gap |
| **CC1⁺ and PDGFRα⁺ counts** | IHC, cortex + CC | P10, P17, P60 | separates a maturation block (PDGFRα⁺ up, CC1⁺ down) from a lineage loss |
| **MBP, PLP, CNPase** | WB + IF intensity | P17, P60 | the markers `PAPER 011` uses, so results are commensurable with the rescue data |
| **Developmental timing** | the above at P10/P17/P60 | — | `PAPER 021` puts the rat's myelin deficit at **PND15–21**; a single adult timepoint could miss a delay that resolves |
| **Axonal conduction** | compound action potential, optic nerve *ex vivo* | P60 | the functional endpoint; `PAPER 004` reports impaired conductivity in the neuronal deletion |
| **Cuprizone challenge arm** | 0.2 % cuprizone 5 weeks, then 2 weeks recovery | adult | tests the conditional-requirement hypothesis of §1.2 at refereed standard, since its only current source is a preprint |

**n and unit:** ≥ 5 animals per genotype per timepoint, **animal as the statistical unit**;
blinded acquisition and blinded quantification, as `PAPER 004` did for its neuronal arm.

**Pre-declared interpretation.** Abnormal g-ratio or unmyelinated-axon count in the unchallenged
O-KO → the residual gap of `CLAIM 003` is oligodendrocyte-autonomous, and *"non-cell-autonomous"*
becomes one of two mechanisms rather than the mechanism. Normal at baseline and abnormal only
under cuprizone → the requirement is conditional, the review's reading is confirmed at refereed
standard, and every unchallenged oligodendroglial assay in this field is a designed false
negative. Normal in both → the residual gap in `PAPER 005` is not oligodendroglial and must be
sought elsewhere (transduction efficiency at 60–70 %, or the P0 window).

**Cost note, because it is the point of this repair:** this is a histology and EM run on animals
from a line that already exists and breeds. The experiment `CLAIM 003` currently asks for is a
new mouse.
