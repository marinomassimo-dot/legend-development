## 3 · A cross-document observation, recorded and deliberately not promoted

In this 2014 introduction the editor reports chapter 6 as having **concluded** that CFS fragility is
*"unlikely only a structural 'passive' incident"*. Thirteen months later the same senior author
publishes **PMID 27551470**, framing passive-versus-active as a **live two-sided controversy** and
citing that very chapter for the point.

🔴 **No claim is proposed from this, and the overclaim is named so it is not made later.** One
special issue leaning one way does not settle a field controversy, and an author may legitimately
present a question as open. The recorded fact is narrower: **the framing moved from *concluded* to
*debatable* within one author's own output across thirteen months**, and four of PMID 27551470's
seventeen references are chapters of this issue.
