**So the arm is DEN-induced, in control mice, against adjacent parenchyma of those same mice.** The
word *spontaneous* names a condition this paper never measures.

## 2 · Why the wording matters, and why it is not a synonym

The constraint as written says: *we have no datum for spontaneous tumours* — which is true, and which
implies the complementary statement *we do have one for DEN-induced tumours*. The panel does not
support that either: there is **no significance mark on n=3 vs n=3**. The limit the reading actually
established is stronger and narrower:

- a **direction** (WWOX mRNA roughly halved in tumour versus adjacent parenchyma) with a plausible
  effect size and **no declared statistic**, in **DEN-induced** tumours of control mice;
- and **no measurement at all** in carcinogen-free (spontaneous) liver tumours.

A reader who trusts the current wording concludes that the DEN arm is established and only the
spontaneous arm is missing. Both halves of that are wrong.
