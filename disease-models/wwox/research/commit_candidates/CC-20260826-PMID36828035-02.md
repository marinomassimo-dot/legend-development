## 2. 🔴 The headline correction: "progressive" is true of one glial lineage and false of the other

The abstract states: *"signs of progressive neuroinflammation with elevated astro-microgliosis
that increased with age."* Read at the panels, **that is accurate for astrocytes and not accurate
for microglia on any abundance measure.**

The paper's own design settles it: panels carry **within-genotype brackets between 80 and
250 days** where an age effect was tested. Their presence and absence is the evidence.

| Measure | 80 d | 250 d | Within-genotype age bracket? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gfap⁺ cell number** (Fig 4b) | WT ≈27, mut ≈39 `*` | WT ≈27, mut ≈**56** `*` | ✅ **`*` 80 d vs 250 d in mutant** | **PROGRESSIVE** |
| **Gfap⁺ area fraction** (Fig 4c) | WT ≈11, mut ≈24 `*` | WT ≈13, mut ≈**40** `*` | ✅ **`*`** | **PROGRESSIVE** |
| **Iba1⁺ cell number** (Fig 3b) | WT ≈22, mut ≈34 `*` | WT ≈22, mut ≈**34** `*` | ❌ none drawn | 🔴 **FLAT — identical at both ages** |
| **Iba1⁺ area fraction** (Fig 3c) | WT ≈14.5, mut ≈21 `*` | WT ≈**11**, mut ≈20.5 `*` | ❌ none drawn | 🔴 **FLAT in the mutant; the gap widens because WT DECLINES** |
| **Microglial branches / junctions / branch length** (Fig 3e–g) | mut ≈60 / ≈30 / ≈315 | mut ≈40 / ≈20 / ≈220 | ✅ **`*` in all three** | **PROGRESSIVE (morphology only)** |
| **Olig2⁺ cells** (Fig 4e) | WT ≈205, mut ≈122 `*` | WT ≈235, mut ≈117 `*` | ❌ none | **established early, FLAT** |

⇒ **Microgliosis is progressive in *ramification loss* and static in *number and area*.**
Astrogliosis is progressive in both. A canonical statement must not fuse them.

⚠️ **A widening gap is not the same as a worsening phenotype.** For Iba1 area fraction the mutant
is essentially unchanged (21 → 20.5) and the *control* falls (14.5 → 11). Reporting that
difference as "increased with age" attributes to the disease a change that occurred in the
wild type.

## 3. Cerebellar degeneration: the neuronal loss is early, not progressive, and it is cell-type selective

**Figure 5**, read at native resolution:

| Panel | 80 d | 250 d | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **d — Purkinje cell number** | WT ≈95, mut ≈**25** `*` | WT ≈100, mut ≈**42** `*` | 🔴 **NOT progressive.** The deficit is maximal at 80 d and the mutant value is **higher**, not lower, at 250 d |
| **e — basket cell number** | `ns` | `ns` | 🔴 **SPARED — a genuine cell-type specificity control** |
| **g — molecular layer thickness** | WT ≈190, mut ≈165 `*` | WT ≈**222**, mut ≈**162** `*` | gap widens, but **WT grows 190→222 while mutant is flat 165→162** |
| **h — granular layer thickness** | WT ≈103, mut ≈90 `*` | WT ≈**130**, mut ≈**92** `*` | same pattern |

⇒ The cerebellar phenotype is **an early, established, Purkinje-selective deficit plus a failure
to undergo normal postnatal layer growth** — not an ongoing degeneration on these measures.
🔴 **Basket cells being `ns` at both ages is load-bearing**: it excludes a generalised cerebellar
cell loss and localises the lesion to the Purkinje lineage.

⚠️ The Purkinje "improvement" from ≈25 to ≈42 is almost certainly **survivor selection** — the
250-day cohort is drawn from animals that lived — and must not be read as recovery. Neither
reading is supported over the other by these panels; both are recorded.

- `P47T/P47T (n=25)` declines from ~60 d; ≈0.62 at 350 d, ≈0.53 at ~470 d, ≈**0.26 still alive at

🔴 **The text's *"mean long-term survival of 393 ± 32 days"* is not reconcilable at face value
with this panel**, where the 50 % crossing is near ~470–500 d and a quarter of homozygotes are
alive at the last plotted day. The likely explanation is a mean taken over **decedents only**,
which the paper does not state. `EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: **`UNRESOLVED`** — use the curve, not the mean.
⇒ Candidate 01 §2.1's survival table is **superseded**: it reproduced the text's means.

**Panel 1d — video-EEG, four channels labelled `L-f`, `R-f`, `L-p`, `R-p`; scale `500 µV / 1 s`.**
Trace i (`WT/WT`) is low-amplitude background. Trace ii (`P47T/P47T`) shows frequent

generalized discharge — abrupt spike, then fast rhythmic evolving to arrhythmic — again across all
four. 🔴 **Bilateral synchrony is now attested from the image, not inferred from the montage
description**, and it is precisely what the single-channel design of PMID 42422765 cannot resolve.
