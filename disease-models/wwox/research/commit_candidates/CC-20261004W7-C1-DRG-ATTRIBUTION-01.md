## 1 · The finding in one sentence

Of the three sources, none adds a primate measurement; what they add is (a) a **relayed duration datum** (a time-limited antimetabolite withdrawn at day 60 was followed by a rebound and a delayed DRG phenotype), (b) the **discriminating experiment named in the field's own words** (an immune response followed by DRG toxicity with a promoterless payload), (c) a **three-stimulus decomposition of "dose"** (vector DNA, capsid sensing, transgene overexpression) that the held records carry as one word, and (d) the **first measured mouse datum on the exposure side of the DRG question**: neonatal mice injected by every route had 60 to 90 percent GFP-positive DRG area, and the euthanised animals had **no DRG abnormality** while the spinal cord of the same animals was injured, with mortality ordered by local spinal exposure and by dose.

## 3 · The record this candidate proposes, and its transfer limits

**One new record** (`INFERENZA`) that holds only the four increments of §1, each with its limit:

- **(a) Rebound.** Relayed from two earlier macaque papers (not read here); the commentators are employed by gene-therapy companies whose programmes use expression-limiting elements, and favour that lever. Species cynomolgus or rhesus, ICM route, one antimetabolite plus an mTOR inhibitor; no infant regimen; no WWOX cassette.
- **(b) The promoterless-payload experiment.** A design, not a result. No source held measures it for DRG (the held promoterless negative is hepatic).
- **(c) Three stimuli.** Generic to AAV at high systemic dose; the third (overexpression) is the cassette-specific one, and for WWOX nothing is known: whether WWOX overexpression stresses the ER is unmeasured.
- **(d) Mouse exposure datum.** Wild-type C57BL/6J, neonatal, self-complementary AAV9 with a strong constitutive promoter and GFP, total vg fixed per animal; euthanised-animal histology only; the death attribution (local GFP overexpression) is the authors' inference, with no null-cassette arm and no immune read-out; significant mortality differences at day 1 and day 5 only, none at day 10 or day 28. It models no human allele and bounds no WWOX dose, route or age.

## 5 · What would change this, and what would falsify it

- **Would strengthen (a):** a primate dataset in which a time-limited immunosuppressant is withdrawn and DRG histology rises after withdrawal against a matched never-withdrawn arm.
- **Would falsify (a):** the same design with no rise after withdrawal.
- **Would falsify the (d) mouse reading of "exposure without injury":** DRG histology of the survivors (not reported) showing the same lesion as the primate literature, or a dose at which a mouse DRG lesion appears.
- **Would change (c):** a measurement of unfolded-protein-response markers in a WWOX-cassette-transduced neuron.
