## 1. The two propositions the model currently fuses

**P1 — `GENERAL_GABA_OR_INTERNEURON_DYSFUNCTION`.** WWOX loss perturbs the inhibitory
compartment: marker abundance, marker protein, and inhibitory synaptic current amplitude.

**P2 — `IMMATURE_OR_DEPOLARIZING_GABA_SIGNATURE`.** The GABA_A response in WWOX-deficient neurons
is depolarizing/excitatory because intracellular chloride is elevated, as in immature neurons.

The working model and `CLAIM 002` carry **P2** as *"squilibrio E/I con firma GABAergica
immatura"*, i.e. as a **signature** — a word that means "a measured pattern". It is not one.
And P1 and P2 are **not two aspects of one mechanism: they are competing mechanisms**, which the
model does not say anywhere.

### 2.1 The one near-miss, and why it does not count

Breton 2021 **does** measure reversal potentials — of the **bursting events**, not of GABA:
total burst **−31 ± 9.5 mV**, burst onset **+6 ± 11.6 mV**, high-frequency component
**−36 ± 9.8 mV**, by whole-cell voltage clamp from −70 to +20 mV.

🔴 **This measurement is uninformative about GABA polarity by construction.** Whole-cell patch
dialyses the cytoplasm with the pipette solution, so intracellular Cl⁻ — and therefore E_GABA —
is **set by the experimenter**. Any native depolarizing gradient is destroyed by the recording
configuration. That is precisely why gramicidin-perforated patch exists, and it returns 0 in the
census above. **The corpus's only reversal-potential dataset cannot be read in either direction
on this question**, and must not be cited for it.

## 3. Where the depolarizing-GABA language actually lives

Adjudicated against `PMID34268881_Steinberg2021_PMC.xml` (sha256 `de340289…`):

- All **four** occurrences of `depolariz` in the article sit in **one Discussion paragraph**.
- That paragraph's entire support is four citations to general developmental neuroscience:
  Obata 1978; Ben-Ari 2007; Murata & Colonnese 2020; Khalilov 2005. **No WWOX data.**
- Its own chain, verbatim: *"a marked increase in GABAergic markers … even more surprising when
  considering the decrease in GABA receptor components seen by RNA-seq"* → *"several lines of
  evidence implicate that during development, GABAergic synapses have a depolarizing effect"* →
  the spectral-power result *"further strengthens **the idea** that depolarizing GABA plays a
  key role in seizure susceptibility"*.
- The same paragraph then draws a **clinical** inference from it: *"These findings shed a new
  light on the lack of efficacy of common anticonvulsant therapies on immature neurons."*

### 3.1 The marker pattern has two readings and the paper concedes it

GAD/GABAergic markers **up**, GABA_A receptor subunits **down**. The authors call the combination
*"even more surprising"*. Increased synthesis machinery with decreased receptor is at least as
consistent with **reduced GABAergic signalling** — plain hypofunction — as with depolarizing
GABA. Marker direction cannot discriminate.

### 3.2 And the corpus's one functional measurement points at hypofunction

Breton 2021: sIPSC amplitude **57.3 ± 31.0 pA (control) → 27.5 ± 19.4 pA (KO)**, more than
halved, while sEPSC amplitude moves 23.3 ± 12.0 → 24.7 ± 13.6 pA — a 1.4 pA separation inside a
12 pA spread `[manifest PMID34634460 entries 7–9]`. The paper's own Results:
*"favors excitation over inhibition, **primarily through an impairment in the amplitude of the
inhibitory currents**."*

🔴 **Reduced IPSC amplitude with normal polarity and depolarizing GABA are competing
explanations, not complementary ones.** The first says inhibition is weak; the second says
"inhibition" is excitatory. The model currently entertains both without recording that they
compete — and only one of them has a measurement.

## 6. Smallest decisive experiment

**Gramicidin-perforated-patch E_GABA in layer II/III pyramidal neurons, S-KO vs S-CTL, P13–P17.**

- **Why it is the smallest:** Breton 2021 already runs exactly this preparation, at exactly this
  age, in exactly these cells. The change is the pipette solution and the seal protocol. No new
  animal line, no new rig, no new cohort structure.
- **Design:** n ≥ 10 cells per genotype from ≥ 5 animals per genotype, **animal as the
  statistical unit**; interleaved genotypes; blinded analysis.
- **Primary outcomes:** (i) E_GABA; (ii) the **driving force** E_GABA − V_rest. The second is the
  quantity that matters and the one this design uniquely resolves: resting potential is already
  known to be depolarised by ~5.6 mV in the KO (−79.25 → −73.62 mV), so the driving force can
  differ **even if E_GABA does not**, and the two possibilities have opposite therapeutic
  readings.
- **Interpretation, fixed in advance:** E_GABA − V_rest positive in KO → depolarizing GABA
  supported, `IPOTESI` → `DATO`. E_GABA − V_rest unchanged, with sIPSC amplitude halved →
  hypofunction, and the immature-GABA hypothesis is **falsified for this model and age**, which
  removes it from the model rather than weakening it.
- **Extension, only if the first is positive:** bumetanide on the slice bursting assay of
  `CLAIM 021` — a pharmacological polarity test with a directly interpretable network readout.
- **What it does not do:** it does not speak to human neurons or to any age outside P13–P17. The
  organoid arm would need the same measurement in the WOREE/SCAR12 lines of PMID 42397075, where
  characterised `c.517-2A>G` material already exists `[manifest PMID42397075 entry 28]`.
