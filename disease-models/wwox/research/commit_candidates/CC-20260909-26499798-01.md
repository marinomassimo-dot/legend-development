## 3 · 🔴 A cross-source disagreement about the allele in trans, recorded and NOT resolved

Three records of the same allele, all secondary, and they do not agree:

| Source | Annotation of the trans allele |
|---|---|
| Banne 2021 Table 1 (wave-2 reading, audited) | `c.46_49del` **`p.Asp16fs`**, typed *"Deletion (Exon 1)/Nonsense"* |
| This review, running text | *"a WWOX **exon 1 frameshift** allele"* |
| This review, **Figure 2B** | **`A16*`** — an asterisk the caption's own legend defines as a **stop codon** |

Two of the three say **frameshift**; the figure says **nonsense**; and the amino-acid identity at
codon 16 differs between them — **Asp** in the cDNA-level record, **Ala** in the schematic.

**This is registered, not adjudicated.** A frameshift is routinely rendered at protein level as a
truncation, so the class difference need not mean two different alleles; the residue difference is
not explained by that. **Only the deposited variant record or the unread primary (PMID 25411445) can
settle it**, and no claim in the registry should carry either annotation as established until one of
them is read.

`REVIVAL_TRIGGER`: any reading of PMID 25411445, or of the deposited ClinVar/DECIPHER record for
`c.140C>G`, that names the second allele's annotation.
