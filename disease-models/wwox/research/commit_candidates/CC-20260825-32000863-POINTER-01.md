## The defect

`claim_registry_current.md:300` (CLAIM 016, `Evidence boundary` block) attributes the lithium
three-genotype reading to **Fig. 7b**. Figure 7b is the **ethosuximide** experiment. The paper's
own Results sentence places lithium at **Fig. 7d**:

> *"Injection of a potent GSK3β inhibitor lithium chloride significantly suppressed PTZ-induced
> epileptic seizure in Wwox−/− mice (Fig. 7d)."*

The manifest agrees independently — entries 0 and 22 anchor the lithium reading to *"Figure 7d,
image inspected"*; entry 1 anchors ethosuximide to *"Figure 7b"*.

Panel identities, verified from the primary:

| Panel | Content |
|---|---|
| 7b | PTZ ± ethosuximide, three genotypes |
| 7c | Western blot — pGSK3β(Ser9), total GSK3β, Wwox, β-actin |
| 7d | PTZ ± LiCl, three genotypes |

The pointer is **bundled**: one parenthetical carries a lithium assertion *and* an ethosuximide
clause under a single `Fig. 7b`. It is correct for the ethosuximide half and wrong for the lithium
half — which is the half the sentence is about. A reader following `Fig. 7b` lands on the
genotype-restricted ethosuximide panel and finds apparent contradiction of the sentence citing it.

## Proposed canonical effect at the next lawful BATCH_COMMIT

  `(Fig. 7b; per l'etosuccimide …)` so that **lithium is cited to Fig. 7d** and the ethosuximide
