## The instruction this candidate obeys

> Do not replace *"no phenotype"* with another global statement such as *"haploinsufficiency
> causes phenotype."*

Neither global statement is supportable. What the evidence supports is that **the shape of the
`Wwox` gene-dose response is different for different endpoints, inside a single cell type**, and
that **no endpoint has been measured with adequate power.** The two findings are separate and
both must survive the rewrite.

## 1. Components, measured one at a time

### 1.1 Organism-level: survival, growth, behaviour, fertility, lifespan

- `DIRECT_OBSERVATION`: *"Wwox heterozygous (HET) pups were indistinguishable from wild-type (WT)
  animals at all stages of development and postnatal life"* (PMID 18487609, Results ¶1, verified
  against `PMID18487609_Aqeilan2008_PMC.html`, sha256 `4e8d70ea…`).
  *"heterozygous mice did not show any abnormalities in postnatal growth and survival as well as
  blood or bone parameters"* (PMID 19936220, Discussion) `[manifest PMID19936220 entry 9]`.
  *"The heterozygote (Wwox^+/flox, Synapsin Cre⁺) … did not show any abnormal phenotypes and was
  behaviourally indistinguishable from control mice"* (PMID 33914858 p. 3065, rendered page).
  Every published human family has clinically well heterozygous carrier parents.
- `MODEL`: mouse constitutive `Wwox^+/−` (two independent alleles); mouse conditional
  `Wwox^+/flox; Syn-Cre`; human carriers.
- `N`: colony-level, unstated per endpoint.
- `STATISTICAL_RESULT`: no test reported; these are descriptive statements of normality.
- `LIMITATION`: 🔴 **a descriptive normality statement has the resolution of the instrument that
  produced it.** None of these sentences rests on a powered comparison.
- `EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: **`DATO`** — the organism-level normality is real, reproduced across two
  labs, two alleles, one conditional and every human family.

### 1.2 Bone / trabecular morphology

- `DIRECT_OBSERVATION`: *"The three-dimensional μCT images of femur metaphysis in mice at day 15
  (Fig. 3B) show **reduced trabecular member connectivity and bone surface area in both the HET
  and KO**. However, mineral content (tissue density parameter) of the trabecular bone in HET is
  not significantly changed from WT."* (PMID 18487609, verified here on the structured surface.)
  Magnitudes from the panel `[manifest PMID18487609 entry 14]`: trabecular number 3.62 → **2.92**
  (−19 %), connectivity density 128 → **64** (−50 %), bone surface 6.30 → **2.88** (−54 %),
  tissue density 722 → 703 (−2.6 %, and the one the text singles out as unchanged). Figure 4B
  repeats the intermediate HET position independently for bone volume at day 12.
- `MODEL`: mouse constitutive `Wwox^+/−`, day 15, femur metaphysis.
- `N`: 🔴 **not stated.** The Figure 3 legend gives no n, no error-bar definition and no test;
  the Methods describe the μCT instrument and facility but no group size.
- `STATISTICAL_RESULT`: 🔴 **none reported.** The error bars on the first three parameters do not
  overlap, which is an observation about the panel, not a test.
- `LIMITATION`: undeclared n and undeclared error semantics; bone is not a WWOX-DEE-relevant
  tissue; day 15 in an animal whose homozygous littermates are dying.
- `EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: **`DATO`** for the direction (the authors state it in running text);
  **`INFERENZA`** for the magnitude; **`ESPANSIONE`** for any transfer to human carriers — the
  tissue, species, age and allele all differ. *(This is `DL-META-097`, whose refusal to transfer
  to humans is correct and is preserved unchanged. What it does not license is the claim's
  general sentence about the **mouse**.)*

### 1.3 Neuronal intrinsic properties

Verified here directly against Table 1 of `PMID34634460_Breton2021_PMC.xml`. Layer II/III
pyramidal neurons; `*` = p < 0.05 vs S-WT, unpaired t-test; N = cells/animals.

| Property | S-WT (13/5) | S-HT (15/6) | S-KO (40/15) | HET shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AP amplitude | 76.30 ± 2.2 | **68.77 ± 2.07\*** | **68.67 ± 1.68\*** | 🔴 **saturating** — HET = KO |
| Input resistance (MΩ) | 102.88 ± 11.74 | **143.26 ± 15.49\*** | **143.03 ± 8.88\*** | 🔴 **saturating** — HET = KO |
| Resting membrane potential (mV) | −79.25 ± 1.99 | **−75.51 ± 1.81\*** | **−73.62 ± 1.36\*** | graded, monotonic |
| Sag (at −200 pA) | 1.18 ± 0.24 | **2.04 ± 0.44\*** | **2.57 ± 0.42\*** | graded, monotonic |
| Post-inhibitory rebound (mV) | 0.96 ± 0.19 | 1.24 ± 0.58 | **1.86 ± 0.44\*** | recessive — HET = WT |
| AP half-width, AP threshold, AHP, tau | ns | ns | ns | flat |

- `MODEL`: `Wwox^+/flox; Synapsin-Cre⁺`, acute neocortical slices, P13–P17.
- `N`: **15 cells from 6 animals** (HET). 🔴 The statistical unit is the **cell**, not the animal
  — pseudoreplication.
- `STATISTICAL_RESULT`: four of nine properties significant at p < 0.05 in the HET.
  🔴 **Nine unpaired t-tests, no multiplicity correction**; at α = 0.05 the expected number of
  false positives is 0.45, so four is well above chance as a *set*, while **no individual
  property is protected**.
- `LIMITATION`: pseudoreplication; no correction; late-stage window by the authors' own
  statement; no WWOX protein quantification in the HET, so the *gene* dose is 50 % and the
  *protein* dose is assumed, not measured.
- `EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: **`DATO`** that some intrinsic properties are altered in the HET;
  **`INFERENZA`** for which ones individually.

🔴 **The finding inside the finding.** On two of the four affected properties the heterozygote is
**not intermediate — it is at knockout value** (68.77 vs 68.67; 143.26 vs 143.03). On two others
it grades monotonically. On a fifth it is at wild-type value while the knockout is not. **Three
different dose–response shapes, in one cell type, in one table.** No global statement about
"haploinsufficiency" can be true of all three.

### 1.4 Network bursting

- `DIRECT_OBSERVATION`: *"0 of n = 11 slices from 7 S-WT mice; 4 of n = 23 slices from 14 S-HT
  mice; 36 of n = 42 slices from 24 S-KO mice"* `[manifest PMID34634460 entry 1]`.
- `MODEL`: as 1.3.
- `N`: 23 HET slices / 14 animals vs 11 WT slices / 7 animals.
- `STATISTICAL_RESULT` (computed here, Fisher exact two-sided): **HET vs WT p = 0.28**;
  KO vs WT p = 1.6 × 10⁻⁷; KO vs HET p = 7.4 × 10⁻⁸.
- `LIMITATION`: 🔴 **the design has 3.5 % power.** Exact enumeration over Fisher's test at
  α = 0.05, for a true incidence of 17.4 % against 0 %, at the observed group sizes 23 vs 11:
  **power = 0.035**. Reaching 80 % power requires ≈ **45 slices per group**; 89 % requires 50.
  The experiment had roughly a one-in-twenty-nine chance of returning significance **even if the
  effect is exactly what was observed.**
- `EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: 🔴 **`UNRESOLVED` — and it is the only honest label.** `p = 0.28` here is
  not evidence of absence; 4/23 vs 0/11 is not evidence of presence. The paper's Discussion calls
  the HET bursts *"manifested similarly"* to the KO's, which 17 % against 86 % does not support
  `[manifest entry 2]`. **All three readings — positive, negative, and "similar to KO" — exceed
  the data.**

### 1.5 Allele dosage — what 50 % of the gene delivers as protein

- `DIRECT_OBSERVATION`: rat `+/lde` shows *"a single band of normal molecular weight, but its
  intensity was **almost half**"*, with cortical IHC as intense as `+/+` (PMID 31340538).
  The hypomorph `Wwox^gt/gt` — protein low but detectable — is viable, while the null dies at
  3–4 weeks.
- `N` / `STATISTICAL_RESULT`: densitometric, no test reported.
- `LIMITATION`: 🔴 **this is the only measurement of protein dose in any heterozygote in the
  corpus, and it is in the rat.** No mouse HET and no human carrier has quantified WWOX protein.
  So "50 % gene dose → 50 % protein" is an assumption everywhere except one rat western blot.
- `EPISTEMIC_STATUS`: **`DATO`** for the rat; **`PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK`** for the
  mouse and the human — *"one allele gives half the protein"* is exactly the class of premise
  that is never written down because it seems too obvious to write down.

### 1.6 Endpoint specificity — the summary that replaces the global statement

| Endpoint | HET vs WT | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Survival, growth, behaviour, fertility | indistinguishable | recessive |
| Post-inhibitory rebound | indistinguishable | recessive |
| Resting membrane potential, sag | intermediate, significant | graded |
| AP amplitude, input resistance | **at knockout value** | **saturating at one allele** |
| Trabecular connectivity, bone surface | substantially reduced, no test | affected, unquantified |
| Neocortical bursting incidence | 17 % vs 0 % | **unresolved, 3.5 % power** |

## 2. Classification

**Primary: `ENDPOINT_SPECIFIC_EFFECT`.**
**Secondary: `NONLINEAR_DOSAGE`** — at least three distinct dose–response shapes coexist in a
single cell type (§1.3), so "the" dose–response for WWOX does not exist as a single object.
**Residual: `UNRESOLVED`** on whether any heterozygote phenotype is functionally consequential —
no endpoint has been measured with adequate power, and the one that matters most for the disease
(§1.4) had 3.5 %.

**Explicitly rejected labels:**
- `PARTIAL_HAPLOINSUFFICIENCY` — implies a uniform fractional effect. §1.3 refutes uniformity:
  two properties are at full knockout value and one is at full wild-type value.
- `LOW_THRESHOLD_EFFECT` — this is the label the current claim implicitly carries and it is the
  one the evidence least supports, because a threshold *below* 50 % would predict a
  wild-type-like heterozygote on every endpoint, and two endpoints refute that.

## 3. Reconciliation with CLAIM 011 — the two claims measure different axes

`CLAIM 011` (flagged 2026-08-10) established from Figure 3B that vector dose is a **threshold**:
below 2.63 × 10¹¹ vg survival is not rescued at all. `CLAIM 032` argues the therapeutic threshold
is **low**. Read together they look contradictory. They are not — they are **non-commensurable**,
and the model has been using one to answer the other's question.

| | `CLAIM 032` axis | `CLAIM 011` axis | `CLAIM 002` axis |
|---|---|---|---|
| What varies | **gene copies per cell** | **vector dose per animal** | **expression level per line** |
| Delivered state | 50 % of gene in **100 %** of cells | ~100 % of transgene in **60–70 %** of neurons, 0 % of oligodendrocytes | **0.4× to 7× WT protein**, same vector `[manifest PMID42397075 entry 29]` |
| Timing | from conception | single ICV at P0 | lentiviral, organoid |
| Shape observed | endpoint-specific (§1.6) | threshold | uncontrolled spread |

🔴 **A mosaic of 60–70 % of neurons expressing a transgene at an uncontrolled level is not "half
a WWOX".** The heterozygote is a *uniform hypomorph*; the AAV animal is a *high-variance mosaic*.
Nothing licenses reading a uniform-dose result as a bound on a mosaic-dose requirement, in either
direction. And §1.3 supplies the mechanistic reason to expect trouble: if some endpoints saturate
at one allele, a mosaic that leaves 30–40 % of neurons untouched cannot be assumed to sit on the
same curve as an animal in which every neuron has one working copy.

**Proposed canonical sentence, replacing the inference:**
> Heterozygote data bound the **uniform** gene-dose axis. AAV data bound the **mosaic** vector-dose
> axis. Neither transfers to the other, and no experiment has yet measured a network endpoint on
> both. Until one does, *"partial restoration suffices"* is `IPOTESI`, not a design principle.

## 5. Missing decisive experiment

**Dose–response of a network readout against *both* wild type and heterozygote.**
AAV9-hSynI-hWWOX at ≥ 3 doses spanning 1.23–2.63 × 10¹¹ vg in `Wwox`-null mice; readouts:
neocortical slice bursting incidence, sIPSC amplitude, resting membrane potential, plus SWD
rate/hour by continuous ECoG. **Both comparators drawn**: treated-vs-WT and treated-vs-HET.

- **Powering is the whole point and is now a number:** bursting incidence needs **≈ 45 slices per
  arm** for 80 % power at the observed effect size (§1.4). The existing 23-vs-11 design cannot
  answer this question and re-running it at the same n would produce another `p = 0.28`.
- **The decisive outcome:** if rescue plateaus at the heterozygote rather than reaching wild type
  on the saturating endpoints of §1.3, then the therapeutic target is **above** 50 % gene-
  equivalent and the portfolio's dose logic inverts.
- Add a `Wwox^+/−` arm to the video-EEG panel of
  [`CC-20260826-CLAIM037-01`](CC-20260826-CLAIM037-01.md) §"Missing decisive experiment" — the
  two designs share it.
