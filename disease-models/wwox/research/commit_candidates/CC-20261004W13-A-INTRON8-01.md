## Finding
`DL-MECH-107`'s 2026-10-02 paragraph says of its three intron-8 association signals: *«none reports
expression or function»*. For PMID 28763065 that is **false as worded**. Its Supplementary Appendix 2
(23 pages, one per gene) and Appendix 3 (510 pages, exon level) are **WWOX expression plot books**:
developmental BrainSpan trajectories by brain region. On Appendix 2 page 4, postnatal expression sits
above prenatal in 15 of 16 WWOX region panels (uncorrected titles P 0 to 0.0086), the hippocampus being
the exception (P 0.1055). What the paper does **not** report is expression **by genotype** — which is
what the record's `REVIVAL_TRIGGER` (2) actually asks for, and which still does not fire.

Those appendices were not in the corpus. They are separate PDFs listed in the JATS with md5 digests; I
acquired all three from the Europe PMC `supplementaryFiles` archive for PMC5611727 on 2026-10-04 (every
md5 equal to the JATS `suppdata-md5`) and declared them with full sha256. Their fonts carry no ToUnicode
CMap, so they are read as **rendered pages**, never through a text layer.

Second correction: the record writes *«rs10514437 (chr16 78.459 Mb)»* with no build. That is the
**GRCh38** coordinate; on GRCh37 — the build the same record family uses for its exon map — the SNP is at
16:78,492,860, which is 26,211 bp into intron 8. Unlabelled, 78.459 reads as intron 7 against a GRCh37
exon map (exon 7 ends at 78,458,952), which is the kind of silent build mix this ledger has been bitten by.

What the re-read also establishes, and what answers the wave question: **the appendix plot books contain
no intron-8 regulatory signal.** Supplementary Figure 4's WM panel shows rs10514437 standing alone at
−log₁₀ p ≈ 7.8 with no other SNP in the window above ≈ 3, so the signal has no LD-supported regional
architecture; Supplementary Figure 7 shows three genotype clusters with **no minor homozygote**, so the
association is carried by heterozygotes only; Supplementary Table 5 has no eQTL, chromatin or regulatory
column for any gene; and the one annotated intron-8 exonic segment in Appendix 3 is essentially
unexpressed in BrainSpan tissue. The permutation test gives an empirical p of **8 × 10⁻⁸**, above the
study's own 1.25 × 10⁻⁸ threshold and about five times the analytic value.

`PAPER 132`'s *«no eQTL and no functional link»* **holds** on every surface.
