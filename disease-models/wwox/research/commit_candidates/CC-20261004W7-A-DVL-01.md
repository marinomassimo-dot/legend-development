## What changed

Wave 6 wrote, into `DL-MOL-003`, that none of the three primaries fixing the Wnt direction had been read, so "tre studi indipendenti" was an abstract-level statement. One of the three, Celebi 2020 (PMID 32368285), is now read at full text. What it contains is weaker than its title and weaker than the role the ledger gives it:

1. **It measures no Wnt output.** β-catenin, TCF/LEF reporter activity and every Wnt target gene are absent from the entire paper. The step "nuclear Dvl → canonical Wnt hyperactivation" — the step on which the lever (Wnt inhibition, lithium contraindicated) depends — is cited by this paper, never measured in it.
2. **The silencing was done in a non-cancer oesophageal epithelial line** (HET-1A), not in a head-and-neck cancer line. The cancer line SCC-15 was never silenced.
3. **The readout is a cytoplasm/nucleus band-intensity ratio** from a fractionation kit, with no replicate count, no dispersion and no p value (Table 4), and the blot it comes from is not shown: Figure 4, the paper's only immunoblot, has no siRNA lane and no nuclear-fraction purity marker.
4. **In the tissue arm the WWOX localisation effect is absent**: the Wwox cytoplasm/nucleus ratio does not differ between tumour and normal (2.8 vs 2.7, p = 0.822), and all three DVL mRNAs are significantly *lower* in tumours.

This does not reverse the direction; it narrows what "fixed by three studies" can mean, and it leaves the belief where wave 6 left it.
