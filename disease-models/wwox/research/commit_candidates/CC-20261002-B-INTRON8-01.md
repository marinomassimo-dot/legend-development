## 1 · Why

`DL-MECH-107` records that WWOX intron 8 carries regulatory architecture and two independent lipid-association
signals, and sets three `REVIVAL_TRIGGER`s, of which (2) asks for expression stratified by intron-8 genotype in
human brain and (3) asks for a non-coding intron-8 variant in a WWOX-DEE or SCAR12 cohort.

This wave read **three more association signals that fall in the same intron** — one in a non-human primate, two
in human infants — and none of them fires either trigger. Recording them stops a later reader from counting them
as three new pieces of intron-8 evidence, and records why they are not.

1. **Marmoset (PMID 37501399):** one haplotype of 17 SNPs in the last intron (8 of 8) of the marmoset WWOX model
   transcript, suggestive LAMP pedigree association with handling-evoked seizures (P 2.4e-6 to 9.2e-6; λ 0.825;
   Supplementary Table S6, Table 1).
2. **Human infants (PMID 28763065):** rs10514437 (chr16 78.459 Mb GRCh38, dbSNP), white-matter volume at about
   5 weeks, P 1.56e-8 against a study threshold of 1.25e-8; unreplicated; minor allele with *more* white matter.
3. **Human infants (PMID 41378749):** rs28688166 and two neighbours (chr16 79.010 Mb), spina bifida, OR about 6.2,
   p 2.2e-6 at a suggestive threshold, imputed (R² 0.78), not in the technical replication.

**Why they do not converge.** (a) Intron 8 is the giant FRA16D intron, the majority of the gene's length
(`PREMISE: INFERENZA` from public RefSeq coordinates), so an intragenic hit landing there is the base-rate
expectation, not a coincidence. (b) The two human SNPs are about 551 kb apart and associate with unrelated traits;
nothing in either paper links them by LD to each other or to `rs2548861`/`rs2667590`. (c) No paper reports
expression, eQTL or regulatory function for its variant. (d) None reaches conventional replication.
