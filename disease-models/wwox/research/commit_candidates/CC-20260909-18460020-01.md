## 3 · 🔴 A deliberate NON-LINK, recorded so it is not re-derived as a link

PMID 18460020 is the **pancreatic sibling** of PMID 16223882's **lung** Ad-WWOX restoration
experiment. Both show Wwox restoration suppressing growth and xenograft tumorigenicity in a
Wwox-negative line. It would be natural — and wrong — to let them corroborate each other into a
general "adenoviral WWOX restoration suppresses tumours" node, and thence toward `CLAIM 002` /
`CLAIM 004`, which concern **AAV-WWOX rescue of neuronal phenotypes**.

**They are not linked, for three independent reasons, any one of which is sufficient:**

1. **They are not independent.** This paper's virus and both antibodies *come from* that paper.
   Agreement between a source and its own reagent-consumer is not corroboration; it is one result
   counted twice.
2. **That paper is under a standing expression of concern**, with the repository's own instruction
   of no canonical promotion until it resolves.
3. **The designs are different in kind.** `CLAIM 002`/`004` concern *rescue of an established
   neuronal phenotype*. This paper infects cells **ex vivo** and injects them 24 h later, so it
   tests **engraftment prevention** — and its own Fig 2A shows those cells collapsing below their
   starting number by 72–96 h, leaving *"the inoculum was already dying"* as an unexcluded
   alternative explanation.

**Nothing about adenoviral or AAV WWOX delivery in a neuronal context may cite this paper.**
