## Proposed paper-level scientific record

- **DATO:** Wwox loss lowers and overexpression raises thresholded CM-H2DCFDA fluorescence in
  dispersed fly larval cells; this is an indirect ROS assay with an arbitrary threshold.
- **DATO:** Wwox modifies Idh knock-down viability bidirectionally; double-mutant genetics
  independently support interaction, but the Idh insertion's precise functional effect is
  unknown and replicate-level significance is non-uniform.
- **DATO:** Wwox/Sod double mutants show synthetic loss of viability and lifespan.
- **DATO:** human SOD1 overexpression increases endogenous WWOX mRNA in HEK293; SOD1 G37R does
  not. Relative WT/G37R expression/activity is not quantified in the displayed panel.
- **INFERENZA:** WWOX participates in a redox-metabolic resilience network; no direction can
  be transferred wholesale to WWOX-DEE neurons.

  use that analogy as claim evidence.

## Handoff to Plan — tool change separate from the reading

now accepts a single PDF explicitly and refuses a non-PDF file, an empty directory or a missing
target instead of emitting a false zero-PDF result. File and directory routes share one
case-insensitive `is_pdf_file` predicate, so deposited `.PDF` files are not silently omitted.

the excluded non-PDF complement; the screened population is therefore visible rather than a
naked total. This is a disease-agnostic validator/tooling change required by the preflight, not
evidence from PMID 21075834 and not part of its proposed canonical promotion. Plan should review
