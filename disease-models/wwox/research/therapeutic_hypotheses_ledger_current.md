Il mutante non è degradato perché **rotto**: è degradato perché **floscio**. La flessibilità espone il KFERQ. Quindi il bersaglio corretto è un **stabilizzatore conformazionale del fold SDR**, che dovrebbe (a) ridurre l'esposizione del motivo → meno CMA → più proteina, e (b) forse restituire funzione.

### HYP-20261029-01 — Extracellular magnesium as the physiological NMDA-receptor gate in neuron-specific Wwox loss: a hypothesis for partial benefit, not a repletion, with the bath magnesium of the source preparation unstated
- **Track:** A (variant-independent); Track B unmeasured. **Route:** BENEFICIO_PRECOCE (a network-state lever; WWOX function stays lost). **Lever:** 3 (repurposing, nutrient class). **Tag:** `IPOTESI` (the arithmetic is an inference inside the hypothesis).
- **Falsifiable statement:** if the NMDAR-dependent bursting of the neuron-specific Wwox knockout slice is held open in part by relief of the voltage-dependent magnesium block (a resting potential about 5.6 mV more depolarised and about half the spontaneous inhibitory current), then a graded rise of bath Mg2+ in the source's own medium lowers burst frequency at a smaller Mg2+ rise in knockout slices than in wild-type slices made to burst by a route that does not change Mg2+ (for example raised K+ or 4-aminopyridine), with d-APV as a reference (if surmountability does not intervene); if bursts need a rise beyond anything reachable, or the response in those wild-type slices is of the same size, the strand closes.
- **Expected benefit (endpoint + direction):** lower burst frequency, duration and amplitude in slice; a maturation endpoint must be added because burst control is not recovery (N-15). Hypothetical: no magnesium arm has ever been run on a Wwox system.
- **Evidence PRO (Wwox system):** from one receipted paper: d-APV abolishes bursts and they return on washout; resting potential -73.62 mV in knockout, -75.51 mV in heterozygote, -79.25 mV in wild type; spontaneous inhibitory current amplitude 27.5 against 57.3 pA; the incubation solution holds 1.3 mM Mg2+. Crude arithmetic with named inputs (a single-site, log-linear model fitted to dopaminergic neurones of the P7 rat, not cortex): the depolarisation alone leaves about 1.3 times (range 1.2 to 1.6) the wild-type NMDAR conductance unblocked at rest, and a rise of external Mg2+ of about 30 percent (range 25 to 57 percent) offsets it; a cortical-type voltage slope gives about +42 percent, above the +31 percent cerebrospinal-fluid rise seen with the most brain-penetrant salt in healthy mice, so the reachable rise may fall short; the match is not a prediction.
