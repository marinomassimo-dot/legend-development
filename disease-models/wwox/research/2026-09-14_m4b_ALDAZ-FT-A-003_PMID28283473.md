## A · The question, restated exactly as it was put

`FT-178` records that the proposition *"WWOX loss de-represses inflammatory signalling in the lung"* is
**not measured** in `PMID 33058734`: there, two in vivo experiments measure it and do not find it — no
asterisk on **twelve bars per figure, twenty-four across the pair**. `PMID 28283473` is cited by that paper
as the antecedent reporting that intratracheal anti-WWOX siRNA produces *"widespread pulmonary neutrophilic
inflammation"*.

So: **what does 28283473 actually measure, in what model, with what n and what controls; is the inflammatory
readout a whole-body/global knockdown phenomenon; and does it support the claim the corpus has been resting
on its shoulders?**

---

## B · The answer, in one paragraph

**Yes on the phenomenon, and the corpus's description of the model is wrong in a way that matters.** The
paper measures the inflammatory readout directly and finds it: BALF neutrophil counts, BALF cytokines and
lung histology, with **the unstimulated knockdown arm asterisked on all eight quantitative panels of Figure
1**. But the model is **not whole-body and not a knockout**. It is a **single intratracheal instillation of
10 mg/kg anti-WWOX siRNA into wild-type mice**, and the compartment it hits is, by the authors' own imported
citation, **predominantly the alveolar epithelial cell**. The denominator is **n = 3 mice per group** in both
in vivo experiments, from **one siRNA sequence** the authors themselves flag as unreplicated. So the corpus
has the direction right, the readout right, and the **scope, compartment and weight wrong**.

---

## C · What it establishes

1. **A positive, stimulus-free inflammatory phenotype from lung WWOX knockdown, measured three ways.**
   *"Surprisingly, WWOX silencing alone caused neutrophil influx … and a corresponding vascular leak … to a
   similar degree that LPS did."* Confirmed on the panels: WWOX-siRNA + PBS is starred for BALF leukocytes,
   neutrophils, total protein, FITC-dextran, IL-6, IL-1β, KC and MIP-2.
   **This answers `FT-178`'s third sub-question — counts, cytokine, or histology — with "all three."**
2. **An IL-8/c-Jun-dependent epithelial mechanism, in a human cell line.** WWOX co-IPs c-Jun; silencing
   WWOX drives c-Jun nuclear (≈5% → ≈45% of cells) and raises secreted IL-8, IL-1β and IL-6; neutrophil
   transmigration roughly quintuples and is **abrogated by neutralising anti-IL-8**; **dual c-Jun silencing
   abolishes all of it**, including the NF-κB rise.
3. **That JNK inhibition attenuates the in vivo phenotype** across six endpoints — a pharmacological bridge
   from the cell-line mechanism back to the mouse.
4. **That basal, unstimulated JNK/c-Jun tone is the thing WWOX restrains** in this system. This is the
   paper's real conceptual contribution and it is a *de-repression* claim in the strict sense: no stimulus
   is required for the phenotype to appear.

---

## D · What it does **not** establish

1. **Not a whole-body phenomenon.** No germline or conditional allele; wild-type mice throughout.
2. **Not a measured compartment.** *"the predominant cell type affected by intratracheal siRNA delivery is
   the alveolar epithelial cell (52)"* — a citation to another paper's GFP-siRNA experiment, not a
   measurement here. **No endothelial cell is measured anywhere in this paper.**
3. **Not a chronic or genetic loss.** The authors' own closing limitation: *"in our disease model, we studied
   acute, global knockdown of lung WWOX expression"*, offered as a reason clinical translation *"may be
   reduced"*.
4. **Not a mechanism demonstrated in mouse lung.** c-Jun localisation, AP-1 activity and WWOX–c-Jun
   association are shown only in A549; the in vivo link is one systemic kinase inhibitor.
5. **Not a normalisation by JNK inhibition.** Figure 5A: the inhibitor moves the knockdown arm from ≈60 to
   ≈47 (×10⁴/ml) while both control arms sit at ≈5–8. Most of the excess survives.
6. **Not domain-resolved.** The SDR/oxidoreductase function is introduced and never tested.
7. **Not replicated.** One in vivo siRNA, n = 3 per group, one laboratory, no WWOX re-expression rescue.

---

## E · The word "global", and how the corpus acquired a wrong model

The corpus's framing — *"whole-body/global knockdown"* — is traceable to a real word in the sources, used in
a narrower sense than the corpus gave it.

- **This paper** calls its own model *"global lung silencing of WWOX"* (Introduction) and *"acute, global
  knockdown of lung WWOX expression"* (Discussion, as a limitation). **"Global" is modified by "lung" in
  both places.** It means *across lung cell types, by airway delivery* — global **within the organ**.
- **The sequel** then contrasts its endothelial-restricted deletion against effects *"difficult to detect in
  prior global knockdown experiments that were associated with massive lung neutrophil influx"* — again
  "global" relative to a cell-type-restricted allele, not relative to the organism.
- **The corpus** read "global knockdown" and rendered it *whole-body*.

🔴 **And one phrase should be attributed correctly.** *"Widespread pulmonary neutrophilic inflammation"* is
**the sequel's wording, not a quotation from this paper.** The string `widespread` does not occur anywhere
in `PMID 28283473`; this paper says *"neutrophilic alveolitis"* and *"neutrophil influx"*. The corpus has
been carrying a characterisation of this paper in quotation marks that this paper does not contain. Nothing
substantive turns on it — the phenomenon is real and starred — but a quoted phrase attributed to the wrong
source is exactly the kind of drift that survives inspection.

---

## F · What this does to the `FT-178` tension — and what it does not

**The two papers do not contradict each other, and after this reading that is a positive finding rather than
an assumption.** They differ on three axes at once:

| | `PMID 28283473` (this paper) | `PMID 33058734` (the sequel) |
|---|---|---|
| Perturbation | acute intratracheal siRNA, wild-type mice | genetic, tamoxifen-induced `Cdh5-CreERT2` deletion |
| Compartment | **epithelium-weighted** (imported, not measured) | **endothelium-restricted** (by construction) |
| Duration | hours to days | genetic/chronic |
| Inflammatory result | **positive, starred, without stimulus** | **negative — no asterisk on 24 bars across two figures** |
| WWOX-primary co-author | **none** | Aldaz (floxed allele) |

So the honest statement the corpus may now make: **the positive inflammatory phenotype is established for
acute, airway-delivered, epithelium-weighted lung WWOX knockdown, and the negative is established for
chronic endothelium-restricted WWOX deletion.** Neither result impeaches the other, because **no experiment
in either paper measures the other's compartment.** The stimulus/compartment bound that
`CC-20260913-ALDAZ-C003-01` recorded on two papers now rests on three — and the third is the one that
supplies the *positive* leg, which is why it was the hinge.

**What this reading does not do:**

- It does **not** license "WWOX loss de-represses inflammatory signalling in the lung" as a general claim.
  What is licensed is narrower and compartment-bound, and it rests on n = 3 per group and one reagent.
- It does **not** resolve whether the endothelial negative reflects compartment, duration, or the difference
  between knockdown and deletion. **All three differ simultaneously**, and no experiment in either paper
  varies one while holding the others fixed. That is a genuinely open question and this reading narrows it
  without closing it.
- It does **not** transfer to the reference genotype. See §H.

## G · Does it say less, or more, than the corpus believed?

**Both, and the two answers point in opposite directions — which is why they must be stated separately.**

**Less, in four respects:**
1. The model is not whole-body — it is acute intra-airway siRNA in wild-type mice.
2. The compartment is epithelium-weighted **by citation, not measurement**.
3. The denominator is n = 3 mice per group, and the caption's *"n = 3 independent experiments"* asserts a
   replication structure the described design does not contain.
4. The reagent is a single unreplicated siRNA whose off-target contribution the authors explicitly decline
   to exclude.

**More, in four respects the corpus had not recorded:**
1. **The phenotype needs no stimulus at all** — significance on all eight panels in the PBS arm. As a
   *de-repression* claim this is stronger than "inflammation is worse after WWOX loss".
2. **The cytokine arm is positive and specific**: KC and MIP-2, the mouse IL-8 analogues, plus IL-1β and
   IL-6 — the same class of BALF cytokine readout the sequel measured and found flat.
3. **The vascular leak is disproportionate to the inflammation**, which the authors flag as pointing at
   endothelial barrier function — an unexplored finding that **anticipates the sequel's own subject** and is
   the one thread genuinely connecting the two papers' mechanisms.
4. **JNK inhibition works in vivo here and failed in the endothelium there.** The corpus recorded the
   sequel's negative JNK result; it did not record that the antecedent's positive one exists, so the pair is
   a compartment-split on the *same* pharmacological target. 🔴 With the caveat that this paper spells that
   target three ways — `SP100625`, `SP500125`, `SP600125` — and only the axis label is a real JNK inhibitor.

---

## H · Transfer toward the reference genotype — stated separately, and it is weak

The reference genotype is a WWOX-DEE genotype class; the disease is neurodevelopmental. This paper contains
**no brain, no neuron, no seizure and no developmental endpoint** — the string *brain* does not occur. The
tissue is lung, the cells are a human alveolar carcinoma line and primary human neutrophils, and the mice
are wild-type adults.

What may transfer, at `IPOTESI` strength at most and **not** proposed as a claim here:
- **That WWOX restrains a basal, unstimulated c-Jun/JNK tone**, rather than only modulating responses to
  insult. If that generalises beyond lung epithelium it is a statement about WWOX's resting function, which
  is the kind of statement a neurodevelopmental model can use. The paper establishes it in one cell line.
- **That acute knockdown and chronic genetic loss can give opposite readouts in the same organ.** This is a
  methodological transfer and it is the more reliable of the two: it warns against reading any WWOX
  knockdown phenotype as a proxy for a germline genotype.

Nothing therapeutic is proposed. **Nothing here is medical advice.**

## J · What I refused to assert

- **That the inflammatory phenotype is a WWOX-loss-of-function phenotype of the gene.** It is an acute
  knockdown phenotype of unstated efficiency from one reagent. The distinction is the whole of §D.
- **That the epithelium is the effector compartment.** The paper asserts it by citation; I record it as
  imported and unmeasured, because writing it as measured is how a citation becomes a fact.
- **That the two papers disagree.** They measure different compartments with different perturbations over
  different durations; "disagreement" would require an overlap neither provides.

  approximate readings off the panels, marked `≈` throughout. The paper prints no numeric table.
