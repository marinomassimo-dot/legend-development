on the producer's word.**

The patient facts were re-read first-hand off `files/fulltext/PMID36779245_Oliver2023_PMC_2026-09-27.xml`
(sha256 `780f42de9b3982fa5bf9bf1e6bb76f71384c943aa197fb2d4de80be7ac7e7a34`, re-hashed **equal**), from
the Table 1 markup and not from its linearised text.

value across:** Table 1's `WWOX (NM_016373.3) variant(s) (hg19)`, `Genetic combination` and
`Country` rows carry **12 data cells for 13 patients**, because one cell is `colspan="2"`. Read
naively, a row like that shifts every column after it and the whole finding would rest on a
misalignment. Measured: the `colspan="2"` cell is the **ninth**, covering the sibling pair at
columns **9–10** — *after* both patients of interest — so columns 2 and 5 are unaffected and the
severity rows (14 cells, no colspan) align trivially.

|---|---|---|

opposite tails, and a pair homozygous for one allele cannot discriminate a rule stated over alleles.
