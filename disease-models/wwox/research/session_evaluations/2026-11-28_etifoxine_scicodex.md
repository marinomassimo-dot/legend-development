# WWOX | Etifoxine, endogenous flux and direct modulation

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Result.** The [etifoxine dossier](../drug_repurposing/continuous_compounds/etifoxine/README.md) replays native untreated expression, adult unbound-parent estimates and the published focal-epilepsy event ratio. Tspo was lower in three bulk comparisons and higher in separately sampled pooled hippocampal nuclei. The event ratio replays and its reported interval does not establish efficacy.

**DEFAULTS_TAKEN.** Kept TPM and normalized CPM on their source scales. Used omission of one library as sensitivity. Used a one-site free-exposure scenario and a count-only interval as illustrations. The published adult seizure results lack longitudinal participant data. Non-indexed journal evidence has a standalone partial receipt with exact source and page.

**STOP_LOG.** A repeat analysis of the same aggregate counts or nuclear pool would leave native flux, unbound juvenile exposure and recurrent inhibition unmeasured. Reopen at those measurements. No review requested on the present evidence.

**DECISIONS_TAKEN.** Retain a conditional in vitro comparison within INV-10 and defer pediatric development. Require a precursor/product bypass and physiological inhibitory recruitment for the next mechanism test.

**DREAM.** A time-resolved comparison with the same measured exposure can connect new steroid synthesis and native function. Labeled downstream product and chloride state are separate prerequisites.

**CAPABILITY_SCOUT.** Small reproducible replays reuse the local expression reader and standard Python. No new package or shared capability.
