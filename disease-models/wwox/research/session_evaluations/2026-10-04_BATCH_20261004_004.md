## Self-evaluation

**What the process got right.** Step 0 ran **before** the audits, which is the upgrade the last
batch earned, and it paid twice: it turned *«the supplement is absent»* into a measurement, and it
is why the seven-batch-old `CLAIM003-01` deferral could finally be re-authored instead of restated.
Five audits ran in parallel on sub-agents that had seen no candidate, and they caught **five**
sentences that every byte-level gate in this repository would have passed — including a mis-stated
construct, a mis-attributed citation and a causal claim nobody could verify. Every `old` string was
dry-run before `--apply`, and the two refusals (`OLD_ABSENT`, a wrong record span) were read as
measurements.

🔴 **What went wrong, and the upgrade.** I anchored an append on a string I had found by reading
*near* a record rather than *inside* it: lines 6870–6878 look like `FT-130`'s tail in a page of
context, and they belong to a different entry. `record_scoped_edit.py` refused, correctly, and the
cost was two cycles. **The upgrade, applied here and stated for the next batch: before writing any
append op, print the record's own span with `record_scoped_edit.py blocks --file <f>` and take the
anchor from inside that span — never from a `sed` window.** A record boundary is a measurement, and
a page of context is not one.

🔵 **A second finding, about the shape of a candidate rather than its content.** Two of the five
