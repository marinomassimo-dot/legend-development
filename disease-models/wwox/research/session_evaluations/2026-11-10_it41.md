## What changed behaviour (shown, not claimed)

* **A search that starts from the relation, not the name.** `relations_it41.tsv` + `action_search.py`: a new relation is a table row. * **Unchanged on purpose:** the gene-graph generator (994 proposals) and the selection. Nothing predictive is claimed.
