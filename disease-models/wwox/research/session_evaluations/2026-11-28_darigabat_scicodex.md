# Darigabat research increment, SCI_CODEX, 28 November 2026 UTC

**Decision.** Conditional ex vivo comparator for functional GABA-A subtype preference. The [frozen prediction](../drug_repurposing/continuous_compounds/darigabat/pretest.md) required absolute inhibition to increase while initial recruitment was preserved. Neither adult PET nor WWOX RNA measures that response.

**Corrections.** Human recombinant α1 binding was stronger than α2 binding despite lower functional efficacy. The adult focal trial adjusted endpoint is a response-ratio difference. The registry gives no efficacy confirmation despite acute photosensitivity crossover effects. The intermediate animal exposure is excluded from any concentration bridge because the MTLE Results conflict with the Methods. The GAERS abstract dose label conflicts with Results and Fig. 1.

**What would change the decision.** Reopen for native reversal, exposure and enhanced absolute inhibitory charge with early recruitment preserved. A contrary native subtype result or discrepant trial estimand would merit independent review. Current evidence justifies retaining the hypothesis in laboratory work.
