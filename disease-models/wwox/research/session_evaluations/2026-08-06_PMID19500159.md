## Process diagnosis

**The retrieval that the system could not do, and the operator could.** Eight tiers refused
this paper. Bronze OA with a single publisher location and no repository deposit anywhere is
the worst combination in the cascade: the right to read exists and the means does not. The
handoff card written two hours earlier is what made the human step cheap. Declaring the debt
instead of narrating a read was what left the door open.

**A hand-built artifact, and why that needed proving.** The paper exists in no machine-readable
structured form. A flat `.txt` transcription would have passed the validator while silently
destroying the schema-v2 abstract/body separation — every abstract sentence would have counted
as body. So the text layer was transcribed into HTML with the abstract in a recognised
container, and **the separation was proven before use**, not assumed: an abstract-only sentence
resolves to the abstract surface and not the body. Building your own evidence artifact is a
step that must be adversarially checked, because the person building it is the person it would
excuse.

**Mutation testing found two real holes in the validator, not in my work.** Nine mutations, seven
caught immediately. The eighth revealed that **`abstract_snippet` was never matched against the
abstract** — the field that exists to keep a locator verifiable when a source is unindexed
certified only that its author had typed something, while the identical fabrication in
`snippet` was caught. Fixed in `deepdive_manifest.py`, with two regression tests, and the new
test was itself mutation-tested: neutralising the guard makes it fail. The ninth survives and is
recorded as debt below rather than quietly accepted.

**Declared technical debt.** `source_fulltext_indexed` is a self-declared boolean that nothing
verifies; flipping it to `true` silently discharges the abstract-anchoring duty. It is not
checkable offline — it needs a Europe PMC lookup or a ratchet over existing manifests — and
retrofitting it at session end would have risked breaking manifests that already declare it.
Left open, named, and not silenced.

**A gate of mine was superseded within the hour, and that is the system working.** The
`SEALED_CONSUMER_CIRCULARITY_GATE` written earlier today was absorbed by the operator's
`FREEZE_SCOPE_GATE`, which is stronger and executable: the real defect was not only that the
verifier was sealed, but that living canonical files were pinned by whole-file hash at all. My
row is marked `SUPERSEDED` and points at its successor rather than being deleted, and the
status vocabulary was extended to declare that state instead of inventing it.
