# Padsevonil research increment, SCI_CODEX, 28 November 2026 UTC

**Decision.** The next useful comparison is an ex vivo test at matched SV2A engagement, with a control for benzodiazepine-sensitive modulation. Systemic pediatric repurposing stays deferred. The [pretest](../drug_repurposing/continuous_compounds/padsevonil/pretest.md) separated postsynaptic potentiation, altered release and loss of recruitment.

**Two corrections that matter.** The phase 3 outcome table uses 59 evaluable participants in the 100 arm. Using 60 from the baseline table would silently make the printed response percentage unreconstructable. The phase 2b geometric mean concentrations in Supplement S4A are in whole blood. The blood/plasma bridge supports SV2A saturation as an adult exposure scenario, without establishing target engagement in any trial participant or the magnitude of GABA-A modulation.

**Counterproof.** The largest phase 3 arm showed a 1.3-point crude improvement on the 75% responder endpoint, with uncertainty spanning benefit and harm. The positive phase 2b crude tail is not the trial's adjusted inference. In Koole, early versus late tracer findings persist under an aggregate replay of Table 1, while the published time-group ranges disagree. The exact fitted parameters are shared across reports with unverified cohort independence. Treat that as one calibration, not replication.

**WWOX and next action.** RNA directions for SV2 isoforms and the chloride transporter diverge by system. Unlike a larger receptor current in recombinant cells, useful WWOX function requires measured inhibitory recruitment and preserved first excitation. A direct drug-by-genotype readout at measured local exposure would justify adversarial review. Current uncertainty cannot be closed by rechecking the dose-response papers again.
