# Session evaluation — PMID 41984841 and PMID 32300104

## Outcome

Two complete reads were closed in the operator-specified order. PMID 41984841 moved from a
provisional PDF-only class to structured PMC XML before analysis; PMID 32300104 used its supplied
complete PMC XML. Every main figure and supplementary PDF page was inspected as pixels after
effective-PPI measurement. Supplementary spreadsheets were inventoried rather than silently
ignored. Both schema-v2 manifests passed strict artifact and locator validation with the command
executed from the shared checkout and evidence resolved from its persistent `files/` tree.

## What changed understanding

PMID 41984841 makes context dependence the main cSCC result: Wwox loss is not spontaneously
tumorigenic in the conditional skin model, but cooperates strongly with partial/complete p53 loss
and with chemical carcinogenesis. Its WWOX–p63 evidence is coherent, yet the paper itself does not
directly re-run the prior ITCH stabilization mechanism and its ChIP-seq is `n = 1`.

PMID 32300104 provides a direct miR-146a→SMAD3 3′UTR result and useful WWOX–DVL2/AXIN2 support.
The strongest audit discovery is that the supplementary expression table does not reconcile with
the stated gene counts/thresholds, and the nuclear β-catenin ratio divides by experimentally
manipulated WWOX. Those boundaries materially lower the weight of two headline extensions.

## Capability upgrade

`deepdive_manifest.py` now accepts an optional `--artifact-workspace`. Manifest discovery remains
on the branch named by `--workspace`, while artifact containment, SHA and locator verification use
the explicit persistent evidence root. The default remains the original single-root fail-closed
behaviour. This converts the operator's locality rule into a machine-checkable interface and avoids
both ephemeral `/private/tmp` proof and symlinks that correctly fail the escape check.

## Discipline and residual debt

No canonical scientific current file or append-only discovery ledger was edited manually. The
remaining work is BATCH_COMMIT promotion, independent replication weighting, and reconciliation of
the PMID 32300104 transcript-count discrepancy with GEO/source preprocessing. No clinical action
is supported by either paper.
