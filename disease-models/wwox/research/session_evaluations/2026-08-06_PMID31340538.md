## Scientific diagnosis

| Dimension | Evidence-backed judgement |
|---|---|
| Source and coverage | Primary rat-model study. PMC XML read sequentially; all six figure images inspected at original resolution; 56 references enumerated. The publisher package contains no tables or supplementary scientific files. |
| Model | Male inbred `lde/lde` rats with a germline exon-9 13-bp deletion, `c.1190_1202del / p.Leu371Thrfs*53`; systemic near-null phenotype with dwarfism and early mortality. This is useful for the exon-9/null side of WWOX disease, not for splice correctability or Q230P. |
| Main result | At PND5–21, measured NeuN abundance and cortical thickness were not detectably different, while MAP2-positive neurites, MBP/CNP and mature APC/CC1 oligodendrocytes were reduced. The defensible synthesis is dissociation between measured neuronal abundance and neurite/myelin maturation—not “intact neurons”. |
| Temporal boundary | Every new experiment was postnatal (PND5, 10, 15 or 21). A congenital lesion does not make these measured phenotypes prenatal evidence. This paper alone does not demonstrate prenatal migration or cortical-layering failure. |
| Cellular boundary | No OPC markers, lineage tracing, conditional deletion, electrophysiology, EM/g-ratio or rescue were performed. The study cannot distinguish OPC absence, differentiation arrest, systemic developmental delay, axon–glia signalling or a glial-autonomous defect. |
| Glial tension | Cortical GFAP and Iba1 measures were reduced, explicitly contrasting with astrocyte/microglial activation reported in the mouse hippocampal model. Direction must remain region-, species-, age- and model-bounded. |
| Quality | At least three affected and three control males per experiment/time point; multiple pairwise t-tests without reported multiplicity correction; no reported power, blinding or randomisation; litters reduced to improve mutant survival. Convergent IHC/WB supports the large phenotype, but precision and causal attribution remain limited. |
| Existing graph | The registry currently misattributes the paper to Kumada et al., gives a lissencephaly title, and uses it as a prenatal anchor. `DL-MECH-027` also overstates “neurons intact” and salvageability. Durable correction is recorded in `DL-MECH-071`; canonical propagation awaits `BATCH_COMMIT`. |
| Multi-hop | Of 56 references, 30 were WWOX-/lde-/disease-direct. PMID 17803050, the original phenotype paper, was absent from all five structured surfaces and is now `FT-041`; PMID 19500159, already load-bearing for mapping/seizures without a receipt, is now `FT-042`. |
