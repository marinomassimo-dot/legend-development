## The third Phase-5 round: a duplicate identity no gate reported

🔴 **The integrator's own post-propagation census found a duplicate the whole gate stack passed.**
`CC-20261004W7-A-REGISTRY-01` stated that PMID 32368285 *«needs creation»* of a `LIT` record and
created `LIT-0494`. It already had one: **`LIT-0151`**, the phase-2 corpus-alignment **placeholder
twin** of `CORPUS-STUB-132`, carrying the same `PMID 32368285 / DOI 10.7150/jca.40840`,
`Status: discovered`, `Date processed: not yet processed`, `Flags: corpus placeholder / not yet
screened`. Two `LIT` rows therefore named one PMID, **and one of them said the paper was unread**
while the batch had just landed its full-text reading.

**No LINT rule fires on this**, because neither row is malformed and neither id is duplicated —
the duplication is of *identity*, not of identifier. Post-propagation LINT was 0 BLOCK with this
defect in place.

The repair mirrors what the batch already did on the paper side: `LIT-0151` is **retired and
pointed at its replacement**, exactly as `CORPUS-STUB-132` now points at `PAPER 201`. Its
`Status` becomes **`superseded`** — a value in that log's own `## Status vocabulary` table, not
an invention — its `Date processed` records where the reading actually landed, its `Next action`
and `Flags` say *do not count as a separate paper and do not cite as unread*, and `LIT-0494`
gained the reciprocal *supersedes* pointer. **Nothing was deleted.**

**The first census was my own false positive, and the correction is the method note worth
keeping.** `registry_records.py get --pmid <P>` defaults to `--hops 1` and therefore also returns
**cross-linked** records: it reported nineteen `PAPER` records for PMID 42807679 and six for
PMID 42248868, which looked like mass duplication and was nothing of the kind. The measurement
that answers the question is a different one — **partition each surface at its own identity level
and read only each record's own `Identifier` / `Identifier value` line.** Re-measured that way,
and made status-aware so a retired-and-pointed placeholder counts as retired:

```
exactly one LIVE identity per PMID per surface: True
PMID 32368285 paper_registry_current: live PAPER 201 · retired-and-pointed CORPUS-STUB-132 (promoted)
PMID 32368285 literature_tracking_log_current: live LIT-0494 · retired-and-pointed LIT-0151 (superseded)
```

**A promotion has two sides and only one of them was in the candidate.** `CORPUS-STUB-*` records
have `LIT-*` twins from the same phase-2 alignment, so promoting a stub implies retiring its twin.
That is a general rule, it is not written down anywhere, and it is the fourth defect class this
batch met that only a measurement would have found. It belongs in the candidate template beside
the two authoring rules above: **before declaring that a PMID needs a `LIT` record, check for a
corpus placeholder twin, not only for a processed record.**
