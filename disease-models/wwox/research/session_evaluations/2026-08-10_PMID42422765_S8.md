## Content diagnosis

The recovery materially changed the answer. S8 does not show P0, does not include P4 in its
survival panels, and follows only P1/P5 to P300. P1–P5 are all present only in the P14
weight/glucose bars. Those bars compare WT-vs-KO and WT-vs-P5; they do not draw a treated-vs-KO
test, and their `ns` label is not equivalence. Histology is P5-only, MBP is unquantified, and
GFAP again lacks KO-vs-P5 statistics. The earlier shorthand “P0–P5 fully rescues” therefore
exceeded the panel.

The strongest defensible result is still important: treatment at P5 produces neuronal WWOX,
near-WT P14 systemic measures by visual inspection, improved CNS images and survival into the
long follow-up for some animals. What fails is uniformity and boundary mapping, not evidence
of a P5 effect. Beyond P5 remains untested.
