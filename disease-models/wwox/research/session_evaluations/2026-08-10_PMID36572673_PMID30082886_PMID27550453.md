## What changed understanding

PMID 36572673 shows that pancreatic-acinar Wwox loss is not sufficient for a detected phenotype,
but cooperates with KrasG12D to accelerate precursor lesions and PDAC. The isolated-acinar result
supports a cell-autonomous component; it does not erase immune/stromal contributions in vivo.
The TGFβ/BMP mechanism is associated and physically linked, but inhibitor necessity is untested.

PMID 30082886 supports a mammary WWOX–p53 relationship but cannot isolate genotype from strain:
Wwox tumour mice are C3H/HeJ while Trp53 tumour mice are B6/129. The paper itself requires a
C3H-facilitated second hit. METABRIC co-deletion is statistically enriched but based on only two
double-deleted cases, and the survival plot lacks the required pairwise contrast.

PMID 27550453 supplies the strongest reusable result. Wwox/p53 double loss accelerates
osteosarcoma in early Osx1 osteoprogenitors but not mature Oc osteoblasts/osteocytes. The same
comparison is explicitly drawn in both stages. This directly supports a distinction between the
tissue where disease is measured and the developmental cell population where WWOX is necessary.

## Pattern audit

No “needed comparison was not drawn” pattern is promoted from this run. In Del Mare the necessary
early-versus-mature comparisons were present and visually explicit. In Husanie the design limitation
that mattered was pooling heterozygous and homozygous KWC mice; in Abdeen it was strain confounding
and a missing pairwise *statistical test*, not an absent experimental group. Calling either a generic
missing-comparison occurrence would manufacture the pattern.
