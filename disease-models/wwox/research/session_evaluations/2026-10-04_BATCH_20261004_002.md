### The two CONTRADICTED verdicts, and what they stopped

🔴 **Shard 2 T11 — a false universal quantifier about where the lesion appears.**
`CC-20261004W8-C-EXPRESSION-NULL-01` attested from Figure 2 that cervical and thoracic
dorsal-root-ganglion degeneration occurred **only** at the 1.1 × 10¹⁴ GC dose. The auditor
measured the panel and found that in the **cervical** DRG the 3.1 × 10¹³ unpurified-control group
has **one animal at severity 1**, group bar about 0.25. Thoracic and lumbar are indeed high-dose
only. **The universal quantifier is withdrawn**, and `RL-C-20261004w8c2` states segment by
segment which is confined to the high dose and which is not. This matters beyond bookkeeping: the
whole value of that paper is as a **dose-and-expression discriminator**, and a lesion appearing
at a lower dose in one segment is exactly the kind of fact a reader would want not to have been
smoothed away.

🔴 **Shard 5 T08 — a negative asserted about a paper that does not deserve it.**
`CC-20261004W8-B-BIOMARKER-01` stated that the immunohistochemistry antiserum was applied across
a species barrier *«on a stated homology argument rather than a validation»*. The auditor read
the two sentences **immediately following** the quoted one: the paper states a canine positive
tissue control (*«Normal canine testes tissue served as a positive control»*) and an
isotype-matched negative control. The sweeping negative is **false**. What `CLAIM 046` asserts is
the narrower truth — **no peptide-block, knockdown or orthogonal-detection validation of the
antiserum in that species is reported** — together with the separate and correct finding (shard 3
T11, independently verified) that the only negative control for the *tissue* read-out is a
reagent control and never a genetic one, although the paper owns knockdown lines and uses them
elsewhere. 🔵 **A blind auditor defended a paper against its reader.** That direction is worth
recording: the audit is usually described as catching overclaims, and here it caught an
**under**-claim about a source's rigour.
