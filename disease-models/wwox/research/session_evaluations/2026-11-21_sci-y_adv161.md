## Dream pass
A marker's specificity is tested by a second perturbation that can produce it, but only where the marker is detectable in the cell type of interest; a snRNA sterol readout in oligodendrocyte-lineage nuclei is detection-limited (1 to 5 percent per gene), a property of readout and platform. It ties to the ADV-157 atlas lead (Wwox detection in oligodendrocyte-lineage cells): a transcript detected in few nuclei cannot carry a per-cell contrast. The astrocyte observation (T3 lowers Hmgcr and Hmgcs1 in one astrocyte state) is descriptive only; ADV chose not to pursue it. Discarded candidate: a per-animal sterol-module analysis from the raw counts (multi-gigabyte, no owner, no decision that depends on it).
