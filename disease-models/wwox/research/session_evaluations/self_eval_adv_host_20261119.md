## What this teaches (compact)

Two things were behind most entries: a **shortcut taken in the same step as an irreversible record** (a send and its ledger line; a statement and its place in a brief) and a **verification of the wrong object** (the quotation instead of the inference; the route instead of the ledger; a per-commit result read as a repository state). Both are cheap to prevent at the moment of writing and expensive afterwards. The micro-upgrades above put the check where the mistake happens: the receipt id in the ledger line, the activity block in the tick, the sentence read before the number written.
