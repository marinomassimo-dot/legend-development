## Dream pass
Group-level chronology fails when the exposure record and the outcome record come from different animals and the outcome is first observed late: check ascertainment before comparing markers. A heteroscedastic burst at one age hides a small shift at the neighbouring age under a pooled error term, so per-age intervals on per-animal values are the right unit when the sheet carries them. Connection kept: the early CA1 set at PWK 7 (NeuN -19 percent, Olig2 +20 percent, IBA1 +8 percent) is a neuron-lineage-microglia triplet that precedes GFAP and the cytokine transcripts; it is a design note for how early readouts are read in any adjacent series, not a WWOX claim.
