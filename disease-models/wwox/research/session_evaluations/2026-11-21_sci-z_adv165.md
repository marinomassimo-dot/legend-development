## Dream pass
Group-level chronology fails when the exposure record and the outcome record come from different animals and the outcome is first observed late: check ascertainment before comparing markers. A heteroscedastic burst at one age hides a small shift at the neighbouring age under a pooled error term, so per-age intervals on per-animal values are the right unit when the sheet carries them.
