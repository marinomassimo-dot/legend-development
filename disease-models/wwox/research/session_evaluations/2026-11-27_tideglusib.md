| Decision question | Result and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Does tideglusib isolate GSK3β? | The paired human enzyme assay gives a 1.81-fold difference. The distinct preincubation shifts and the single-point cross-kinase screen require concurrent α and β measurements at multiple times and an orthogonal control. |
| Can developmental or seizure effects be inferred? | The adult provoked mouse study found no acute EEG reduction and more delayed injury. The earlier rat paradigms and independent acute-versus-constitutive GSK3 experiments give different outcomes. Tissue exposure and attribution remain conditional. |
| Does pediatric DM1 close the exposure or efficacy gap? | The systemic PK and denominators are reproducible. The route, disorder, age and free neural exposure limit transfer. Randomized pediatric results did not establish superiority on the DM1 primary endpoint. |
| Which comparison moves first? | Prioritize a direct WWOX baseline GSK3 activity measurement. A rescued baseline deficit would determine whether a paired pharmacological test is informative. Piard's 19/20 is a retrospective drug-resistance fraction. The E1 EVPI replay depends on the prior and the stated payoff structure. |
