   choice among them, and do not exclude age.

   recovered **fraction** of the age deficit is nowhere quantified.

   dosing age and the assay and says nothing about tolerance.
5. **`RL-GT-002`** — the four-fold vector difference between age arms is the authors' **deliberate
   normalisation** to brain volume; the unmatched-antigen-load caveat is this repository's.
6. **`RL-GT-002`** — the three-quarters CSF-protein figure is *irrespective of attribution* above
   50 mg/dL; the drug-attributed rates are about 27 % and 14 %.
7. **`RL-GT-002`** — *«in some cases tacrolimus»*: the third agent is not universal in that cohort;
   and the 48 weeks is the **regimen total**, not each agent's duration.
8. **`RL-GT-002`** — the 16-of-17 adverse-event attribution is *«possibly related to
   immunosuppression»* in one patient, with several events also possibly related to study treatment.
9. **`RL-GT-004`** — the low-dose/high-dose equality holds for the **kindling paradigm only**; all
   other metrics improved more at the high dose.
10. **`RL-GT-004`** — a treatment-associated leucocytosis *«had diminished»* by one year rather than
    resolved; and the nerve/DRG findings are arm-level but **single-animal**.
11. **`RL-GT-003`** — the ten-fold self-complementary transduction advantage is a **prediction**
    carried from citations, not a measurement in that paper.
12. **`DL-METH-117`** — the per-gram dose is printed **with a parenthetical absolute total**; and the

14. **`PAPER 172`** — the 6.8 Mb deletion: the article names the two genes but no chromosome, so the
