# SCI_CODEX self-evaluation for alpha-tocotrienol

28 November 2026 UTC. Scope is compound #198 and the INV-06 first-sheath comparison.

The same nominal dose gives different outcomes in cells and purified Src. The rescue with active Src and the reported response after lipoxygenase phosphorylation make an exclusively upstream reading weak. The independent liposome result provides a membrane mechanism, with a reporter and dose boundary. The matched Q7 product calculations give an exposure requirement rather than a measured conversion. A single negative product trace is inadequate without recovery and chemical trapping controls.

Separating neuronal Src and oligodendroglial Fyn is a decision-changing control because Src bulk RNA directions disagree and Fyn has a documented developmental role. Human tissue data make total delivery plausible under a mixture and different tissue sources, and the pediatric mixture trial supplies limited feasibility context. Preserving first inhibitory recruitment and first-sheath conduction matters more than rescue after an isolated challenge. A matched speciated comparison could change the disposition.
