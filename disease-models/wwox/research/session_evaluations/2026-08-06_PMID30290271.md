## Scientific diagnosis

| Dimension | Evidence-backed judgement |
|---|---|
| Source and coverage | Primary systemic mouse-KO study. All 27 PDF pages, six figures, Table 1, both supplementary tables and 90 references were read. The 283-row DEG supplement was checked completely. |
| Model | Constitutive systemic `Wwox` knockout on mixed 129SV/C57BL/6 background, two-week mice, both sexes pooled. Controls are `Cre+; Wwox wt/wt`; no heterozygote or floxed Cre-negative comparator. This is T2 null-model evidence, not an allele-specific model. |
| Interneuron result | PV-positive counts are lower in DG, CA1 and whole hippocampus; CA3 has no significance marker. NPY-positive counts are significantly lower only in DG; CA1/CA3 are textually negative, while whole hippocampus is an unmarked Figure 2D observation rather than a reported equivalence test. Marker-positive stereology does not establish cell death. |
| Glial result | IBA1/GFAP area fractions and morphology support regional reactivity. Area fraction is not a cell count and cannot establish hyperplasia. DG effects are marginal. Only `Il6`, not `Tnf-a`, is significant. |
| GABA/function boundary | Lower combined GAD65/67 protein is directly measured. GABA abundance/flux, inhibitory currents, chloride gradient, E/I balance, EEG and seizures are not. The current graph crosses this boundary and requires correction. |
| Transcriptomics | Newborn cultured neurospheres, only two KO versus three WT samples. The 283-DEG list contains changes in both directions but is too small to establish either the authors' coherent epilepsy program or a coherent alternative “mixed” state. IPA disease categories are enrichment, the 16-gene epilepsy shortlist is preselected, and broad neurosphere qPCR validation is not shown. |
| Causality | No conditional deletion, rescue, temporal ordering or pre-seizure time course. The authors explicitly leave cause versus consequence unresolved. Epileptogenesis and death at roughly three weeks are imported from PMID 19936220/Mallaret 2014, so seizure/systemic confounding remains plausible but is not promoted until FT-043 is read. |
| Existing graph | `PAPER 006`/`LIT-006` lack identifiers and duplicate `CORPUS-STUB-085`; `CLAIM 005` turns one model into plural models and attaches a medication caution not tested here. The GABA meta overstates NPY, cytokines and cell loss. |
| Multi-hop | All 90 references were read; 20 are WWOX-/model-/disease-direct. PMID 19936220 is the load-bearing model-generation source without a receipt and is now `FT-043`; PMID 19500159 remains `FT-042`. |

## Process diagnosis

The full text and supplements were initially served behind a publisher proof-of-work page.
The published challenge protocol was completed and every resulting download was validated as
the expected binary type and fingerprinted; the earlier HTML challenge responses were never
treated as scientific sources.

The DOCX renderer could not run because its local image/PDF dependency was unavailable, and
no LibreOffice fallback was installed. The supplement contains one plain 19-by-2 table and no
scientific embedded images, so it was read structurally to completion. The spreadsheet
runtime and `openpyxl` were likewise unavailable; the unchanged XLSX was audited read-only
through its OOXML, including all 337 sheet rows, formulas, merges, hidden sheets, drawings,
charts, comments and embedded thumbnail. No format-dependent conclusion was drawn.

The capability scout produced `MARKER_TO_FUNCTION_GATE`: separately represent marker-positive
abundance, lineage/survival, transmitter quantity/flux and electrophysiological function.
The gate also forbids deriving a medication rule from the first two layers.
