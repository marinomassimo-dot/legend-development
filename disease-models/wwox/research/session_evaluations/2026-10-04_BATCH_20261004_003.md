### § 13: a Tier-1 read-out that is not an endpoint, and why it stayed out of the biomarker files

`LEGEND_CORE` § 13 was read before classifying. WWOX **protein** measured in L1CAM-immunocaptured
plasma vesicles is **Tier 1 by kind** — the readout is WWOX itself — and it is **not «validated»**:
§ 13 forbids the word without direct sensitivity/specificity evidence in the disease population, and
**no sensitivity and no specificity are printed for WWOX in any population**. It is **not an
endpoint**: the unit is relative (log2 NPX, the authors having declined the assay's quantitative
values because some were below detection), the assay was run **in singlet**, the design is
cross-sectional, the population carries **no WWOX genotype**, and the authors state the study
*«was not designed to provide confirmatory evidence for any single biomarker»*. ⚠️ The paper's own
WWOX sex sentence is **not the comparison its figure marks**: the sexes-combined WWOX panel carries
no significance bracket at all and the single bracket is between two male groups.

`biomarker_candidates_current.md` and **not** in `clinical_monitoring_endpoints_current.md`. A
Tier-1 modality with no validation and no disease-population measurement is a lead; the five-item
specification it would have to satisfy — absolute quantification, a measurement in people with
biallelic WWOX alleles (**the floor problem, and the gating item**), evidence that the vesicle signal
tracks brain rather than peripheral WWOX, within-person repeatability, and a demonstrated change
under a dose intervention — is recorded beside it so the gap stays legible.

than after it, six auditors in parallel, and it paid: three defects were repaired at source instead
of landing and being found by a Mirror review two batches later. The absent-artefact lesson was

were read as measurements rather than as obstacles.

🔴 **What went wrong, and the upgrade.** Three of the seventeen candidates carried propositions that
reached landed text with no blind audit behind them — `B-HORDEAUX-ATTRIBUTION-01`,
`B-VPA-DIRECTION-01` and `C-TRANS-REGULATOR-01`. Two of those three were unauditable **at dispatch
time for a reason this batch itself removed**: their artefacts were missing, and the artefacts were
found about twenty minutes later. **The upgrade, applied here and stated for the next batch: run
`evidence_presence.py --search` over the whole scope BEFORE dispatching the audits, not when an
identity measurement fails.** An audit skipped because an artefact was missing is a different thing
from an audit judged unnecessary, and this batch's own disposition blocks now say which of the two
each skip was.

highest id I had assigned, and the measured figure is **233** — because the earned-null stub counts
to `corpus`, not to `papers`. `growth_anchors` caught it as a BLOCK. A number derived from an id is
not a count of records, and the one ratchet that measures both is the place to learn it.
