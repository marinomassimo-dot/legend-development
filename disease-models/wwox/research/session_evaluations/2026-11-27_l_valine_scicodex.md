**Scope.** SCI_CODEX evaluated L-valine after calcitriol from shared base cab6bd453. The [dossier](../drug_repurposing/continuous_compounds/l_valine/README.md) compares substrate supply with impaired disposal. Six article receipts cover selected original material.

| Dimension | Evidence and residual |
| --- | --- |
| Original coverage | Viewed original Eller Figs. 4–7 and parsed the publisher's supplement. The other five receipts record selected full-text passages, tables and captions. |
| Original contribution | Restriction reduced mean brain valine about 42% in WT and 1% in primary ECHS1-deficient mice. The little change in mean mutant brain parent limits the interpretation of the seizure challenge. The exploratory interaction uses reported SEMs without litter covariance. |
| Rival and control | Lower BCAT2 expression could reduce precursor formation independently of ECHS1 capacity. The negative Echs1 contrast varies across hippocampal cell assignments. The older Ensembl mouse matrix uses Mut and Vars aliases, which are specified in the replay. |
| Proxy audit | Lysine accounts for much of the WT alanine/lysine change. Direct PDC activity and pyruvate oxidation are separate observations. SCPC measures conjugate formation, and SCEC has other possible sources. |
| Reopening | Verify measured local valine, precursor-normalized label partitioning and pyruvate oxidation in the same WWOX preparation. Independent review could change interpretation when native exposure data, covariance or a disputed assay measurement is available. |
