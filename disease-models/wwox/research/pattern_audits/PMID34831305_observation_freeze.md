## Observations arising during the neutral read

1. This is a narrative review and model atlas, not a new experiment. It synthesizes rat, global/hypomorphic/conditional mouse, cell-targeted mouse, human tissue/cell-line, and organoid evidence.
2. The most informative causal separation in the review is explicit in the prose: four CNS cell-targeted mouse lines are named (Nestin, Synapsin-I, GFAP, Olig2), and only the Nestin and mature-neuron deletions are said to recapitulate the null phenotype in the reported interval, including systemic phenotypes.
3. The authors describe neuronal Wwox ablation as causing oligodendrocyte maturation failure and hypomyelination non-cell-autonomously; mature oligodendrocytes fall while OPCs rise.
4. The converse intervention is striking: neonatal neuron-specific AAV9-hSynI-WWOX is reported to rescue CNS phenotypes and also bone, testis, hypoglycaemia, growth, and survival. The tissue where expression is restored therefore need not be the tissue where every rescued phenotype is measured.
5. A hypomorphic mouse with approximately 5% residual transcript had no reported neurological phenotype, but brain expression was not measured; the proposed sufficiency of minute brain expression is explicitly conjectural.
6. Human and rodent observations are not interchangeable: the review highlights normal metabolic screening in one severe human case despite systemic metabolic abnormalities in null rodents, and different regional/developmental WWOX expression patterns.
7. Human organoids add genotype and developmental-stage resolution absent from rodents: WOREE and SCAR12 organoids diverge, and early WWOX expression is concentrated in ventricular radial glia before broadening later.
8. Diffuse WWOX re-expression in organoids rescues many but not all phenotypes; mature lentiviral re-expression reverses electrophysiology. The authors themselves flag uncontrolled pan-cellular expression as a limitation and motivate cell-type-specific correction.
9. Figure 1 is a qualitative anatomical-expression schematic. Figure 2 is a qualitative model/phenotype infographic. Neither contains quantitative groups, points, uncertainty, statistical tests, or treatment-window comparisons.
10. Table 1 is a qualitative comparison matrix. Its cell-targeted column names only Nestin/Synapsin models and compresses their phenotypes as similar to null mice; the GFAP- and Olig2-targeted negative models named in the prose are not represented as separate columns or rows.
11. The therapeutic section moves from secondary summaries of lithium/ethosuximide and gene restoration to the phrase “efficient and safe treatment”; the review itself supplies no clinical safety experiment and no human treatment data.
12. No standalone Methods section, formal systematic-review search strategy, limitations section, or supplementary material is present in the XML. Limitations are distributed through the narrative.
