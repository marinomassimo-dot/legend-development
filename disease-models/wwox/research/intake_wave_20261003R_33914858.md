## 4 · The assigned questions

The full answers, with locators, are in `fulltext_dossiers/PMID33914858.md` part 2 §§ 2.1–2.6. In
brief:

**(a) Myelin quantities.** Corpus callosum P17: 55 ± 35 (S-KO) vs 180 ± 40 myelinated axons per
field; optic nerve: 270 ± 60 vs 55 ± 20 unmyelinated; g-ratio up in both tracts; CC1⁺ oligodendrocytes
halved with significantly more PDGFRα⁺ OPCs (0.5 mm², three sections); no significant oligodendrocyte
death — **an image with no count, no n and no test**. N-KO carries its own counts (45 ± 26 vs
148 ± 45; 440 ± 210 vs 195 ± 35). All of it from **n = 3 animals per genotype**, tested over **fields
of view** (13–15 points per panel), two-tailed Student's *t*, no multiple-comparison correction
anywhere. The text's fold changes are inflated in one direction: "~3.5–4-fold" measures 3.3×,
"~6-fold" measures 4.9× on the authors' own averages and ~4.2× on the panel.

**(b) The heterozygote arm.** Slice bursting 0/11 (control, 7 animals), 4/23 (S-HT, 14 animals),
36/42 (S-KO, 24 animals); the Figure 2B legend restates it per animal (3/14 and 20/24), which
resolves the discrepancy wave 4 flagged. **No S-HT-versus-control statistical test exists on any
measure**; no myelin, imaging, electron-microscopy or quantified behavioural endpoint exists for a
heterozygote. A behavioural null **is asserted** for both heterozygotes without data, and for the
Nestin one the authors transfer it to human carriers. Panel 2D adds a bound no sentence carries: the
heterozygote is above the homozygote below ~5 Hz in vivo and below the control in vitro — not a clean
intermediate.

**(c) Cell autonomy.** Nestin (N-KO) and Synapsin (S-KO) reproduce the null phenotype; Olig2 (O-KO)
and GFAP (G-KO) are flat negatives. 🔴 **The O-KO negative — which is what licenses the word
non-cell-autonomous — is survival and weight to ~120 days (n = 11 vs 10, log-rank P = 1.0), a
clasping image, and one unquantified MBP comparison at P17.** No EM, no g-ratio, no axon count, no
CC1/OPC count, no electrophysiology, no later age. The authors write that they cannot exclude a
cell-autonomous oligodendrocyte function. Two further bounds: the in-vitro arm uses **embryonic DRG
neurons from the constitutive null**, and the Synapsin deletion is **incomplete in cortex at
transcript level** (residual *Wwox* 172.7 / 187.0 / 331.4 vs 18.8 / 18.8 / 28.0 in hippocampus).

**(d) Organoids.** CRISPR *WWOX* knockout in a human ES line → oligocortical spheroids: RMP
−52.1 ± 0.90 (n = 3) vs −21.28 ± 6.91 mV (n = 4); low-frequency power up; CC1⁺/CNP⁺ down at weeks 14,
20, 30 with OPC markers unchanged or up; week-37 EM percentage from **n = 2 vs 3 organoids**, no
error bar, no test. Both genotypes are explicitly immature at week 15 and the authors read the
difference as developmental delay. Same laboratory: convergence, not independent replication.

**(e) Text-versus-panel.** Five, of which one reverses printed numbers: the **Supplementary Fig. 6B
legend transposes its own values** (says S-KO 12 ± 4 vs control 7 ± 3; the panel plots control ≈13 and
S-KO ≈6.8). Then the two inflated fold changes; the "significant" myelination GO term sitting on an
FDR scale of **0.09–0.11** while the twelve up-regulated terms are all spliceosome/splicing/proteasome
terms the text never mentions; and the evoked-response sentence that claims a contrast panel 2F does
not mark.

**(f) What it does not say.** No heterozygote test; no multiple-comparison correction; no sex-split;
**no WWOX immunoblot anywhere** — every WWOX measurement is immunofluorescence or transcript; no
seizure frequency and no unanaesthetised EEG; no deposited data; no anti-rabbit secondary listed
although four primaries are rabbit; no *Wwox* or housekeeping primer pair among nineteen; nothing
about remyelination, repair or any age past ~4 weeks; and no test, in any model, of whether rescuing
myelin rescues seizures.

  secondary at all** against four rabbit primaries, all imaged.

## 8 · What would change the model, and what would falsify this reading

- **Would change it:** an oligodendrocyte-lineage deletion with electron microscopy, a g-ratio and an
  aged or challenged cohort — the measurement the O-KO arm never made. A published, peer-reviewed
  version of such a result would move `CLAIM 003` from "non-cell-autonomous" to "partly
  non-cell-autonomous, with a measured intrinsic component".
- **Would also change it:** any statistical comparison of a *Wwox* heterozygote against its control
  on an electrophysiological measure, or any myelin endpoint in a heterozygote.
- **Would falsify this reading:** a copy of the same article in which Figure 5B's optic-nerve means
  sit at the ratio the text claims, or a Supplementary Fig. 6B whose panel matches its legend's
  parenthetical values. Both are checkable by anyone holding the declared digests and the render
  calls in the manifest.
