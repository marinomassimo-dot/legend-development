# Blind audit — group R4 manifests (10 papers) and the first nine candidate cards

Auditor: blind, read-only toward every canonical file; this file is the only thing written.
Date of the audit: 2026-10-22. The reader's design note, its commit candidate, its self-evaluation and the
author's card note were not opened. The card checker was run (it opens the design note by itself; the auditor did not read it).

Method. Triples printed with `locator_audit.py --pmid <PMID> --triples 0`. Every source artefact digest in each
manifest was recomputed from the file on disk before reading (all matched). Every text quote was matched against its
artefact (loose alphanumeric match for table and symbol formatting; all 184 text quotes are present; no stitched span
found). Figure triples were read from the best surface that `paper_packet.py rank` returned: where the manifest used a
300 or 400 dpi page render or a native 1700+ px embedded raster, that surface was opened as it is; where only a
publisher JPG existed (the Epilepsia and Orphanet figures) it was opened as is and its legibility is stated. Numbers in
the triples were recomputed from the printed table or figure (ratios, percentages, counts), not accepted.

## PART 2 — the first candidate cards

Scope: the nine packets (the bridge was not audited), the nine cards, the README, the checker, and the source passages the packets quote. The author's card note and self-evaluation were not opened.

### What `check_cards.py` does and does NOT check

Ran: `python3 disease-models/wwox/research/drug_repurposing/cards_early/check_cards.py` gave "packets checked: 9" and "PASS" (exit 0).

It checks: (1) the stored card equals what the bridge builds from the packet; (2) all 19 roadmap rows are present and non-empty; (3) no key name that matches score, rank, probability, dose, schedule, suitability, index, and no number-plus-unit string (mg, mL, g, IU, ng/mL and so on); (4) no e-mail-like string and no hexadecimal run of 12 or more that is not exactly 64; (5) every text inside « » occurs verbatim, after markdown stripping, somewhere in a fixed list of ten repository files, and has no ellipsis; (6) an assertion whose disease_id contains none of WOREE, WWOX or Wwox carries the label NOT WWOX EVIDENCE; (7) candidate cards name an endpoint and keep two time fields apart, abstentions start with ASTENSIONE and name a Missing datum.

It does NOT check: whether a quote supports its sentence, or is found in the file the locator names (it matches against a concatenation of all ten files; two quotes are found in the author's own design note as well, and the quotes taken from secondary files — the registry, the known-roads note, the discovery ledger and the R3 note — are not matched to the primary paper); words in free text for route or schedule (the ETX packet says "acute intraperitoneal pretreatment" and passes), or "protection", or categorical absences ("never", "no ... has been applied"); any claim without « » marks; that a section number cited still points at the claimed content; whether a negative is scoped; the label rule is vacuous for any assertion that writes the label inside its own disease_id (the pattern searches for WWOX and finds it in "NOT WWOX EVIDENCE", so the evidence_label key is then never required — the three self-labelled assertions in the ETX, VGB and PDANCHOR packets pass for that reason); the sentence-level label (a non-WWOX statement inside a WWOX field is not detected); and that cited notes have landed or been audited. The README states "It does not verify that they are true"; that is correct and is the whole of its coverage.

### Priority questions

**(a) KD card — the "previously published individuals" sentence.** Verified against the held surface of the source (digest of the delivered-chunks text file recomputed: b3a6d7a077c020ba402ae29556d9a8eba825c0d8d729216613d0d3cc69322ad9, matches the manifest). The text states "five patients ... including three new and two previously published patients whose data have been updated". Table 1's last row is headed "CNVs or SNVs reported in other studies or ClinVar database" and, read as five cells, attributes patient 1 to ClinVar identifiers, patients 2 and 4 to the same earlier cohort report, patient 3 to a different report and patient 5 to a third report plus a ClinVar identifier. So the row attributes variants, for four of the five patients, not individuals; it does not say which two are the previously published ones. A methods sentence cites a further earlier report for a homozygous exon-6 deletion, which Table 1 places in patient 2's genotype (the narrative the sentence belongs to is on a chunk that was not delivered). The card's sentence "two of the three responders (patients 2 and 4) are previously published individuals named by the source, so any pooled count ... double-counts them" turns a candidate identification into "named by the source" and drops the scope qualifier that the registry entry itself carries ("it reports whether a variant was reported elsewhere, so it names the candidates while the three-new/two-published split is the text's"). The cell alignment is itself an inference from the extracted stream. Verdict for the sentence: OVERSHOOT, repeated in the card's `shared_origin`, `evidence_pro_contra`, the README headline and `value_added`. The conditional consequence (a pooled count must say which individuals it counts) stands whichever two are the published ones.

**(b) VGB card — shared origin.** Two entries (A-VGB-1, A-VGB-2) come from the same paper and are flagged `shared_origin: "Shaukat 2018 (children 1 and 2)"`. They are two children, so one reporting origin and two independent observations; the packet's A-VGB-2 note says "not an independent confirmation" and the README says "one source counted twice", which conflates source independence with patient independence (the registry itself counts "n=2"). Three further defects on the same card: (i) A-VGB-2 is encoded DECREASE on the endpoint "clinical cessation of epileptic spasms" while its own locator says partial response with residual cluster spasms at 11 months, so the bridge counts as opposing a disease endpoint the child did not reach (the packet admits "partial lives in this note"); (ii) A-VGB-9 comes from the same paper and carries no `shared_origin`; (iii) CLAIM 001's summary carries two Chong 2023 vigabatrin observations (one resistant, one improved only in combination with the diet) that the card omits, so "ineffective in two others" is a count of two where the claim carries three; whether the Chong vigabatrin-resistant case overlaps a Shaukat child cannot be established (Chong's Table 1 cites the Shaukat report for patient 3's variants).

**(c) ETX card — the unlanded, unaudited narrowing.** At the time of writing the statement was honest in four places (`sources`, `verification_scope`, `status`, README: "in-flight, not landed", "owed a blind audit", "proposed and unaudited"). Three things make it incomplete or wrong now. (1) Staleness: the R2 note landed on main minutes after the cards (the integration commit follows the cards by about five minutes) and its closure records three blind audits (the Cheng 2020 locators 6 of 6 SUPPORTED with soft corrections), so "unaudited" and "not landed" no longer describe main. (2) The card cites an older commit of the note than the one on the branch at writing time: the note had since gained section 28 (a supplementary RNA-seq table of the Wwox P47T hippocampus listing Cacna1g up and Cacna1h down) and section 28b. Against that, the card states as fact "no T-type current or Cav3 expression measured in any Wwox system (R2 sections 3 and 13)", `coverage: none for WWOX`, and not_known "any T-type measurement in a Wwox system": the same note's section 28 says that "no WWOX-deficient system has any T-type measurement" does not stand, and section 3 is a non-WWOX paper that cannot support that sentence. The current-measurement part (no current) still stands. (3) The card carries the pre-repair wording that the audit softened: "differs ... by about as much as the late effect" and "cohort variability as large as the effect" (now "a comparable order"; "separate cohort" dropped because the Methods do not say), and "mouse age is not carried by the notes read" while the R2 note twice says the Wwox assay is a preweaning mouse. Verdict: dependency declared honestly as of the write time, but the card is built on a snapshot older than the note's own tip and contains a negative that the cited note contradicts.
