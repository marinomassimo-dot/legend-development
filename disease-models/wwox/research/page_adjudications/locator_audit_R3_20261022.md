| Day 1 Cmax | slope on raw means | 1.30 | 1.48 |

A slope of 1.0 is proportional. The 21 to 42 mg step: Day 5 AUC ratio 1.27 for a 2.0 dose ratio, Day 5 Cmax ratio 1.19, Day 1 Cmax ratio 0.83. Half-life column means 17.7, 24.8, 18.3, 45, 30.8, 38, 21 h average 27.9 h, so '28 h' reproduces. The CVs are 22 to 79 percent and N is 1 at two dose levels, so a proportionality test is not supported by the table either way; the printed statement 'dose-proportional between 1 and 42 mg' is therefore not reproducible from the supplement it cites.
