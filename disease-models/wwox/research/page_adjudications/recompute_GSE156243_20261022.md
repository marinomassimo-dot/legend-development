### 2.1 Gene universe 15,370: not reproducible by any defensible rule

Reproduced 19694 (row total >= 10). Tried, all on the six libraries, without tuning toward the target:
- any sample >= 10: 17743; at least 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 samples >= 10: 17034 / 16543 / 16146 / 15711; all six >= 10: 15134.
- minimum per-sample count >= k, k = 1..12: 18183, 17248, 16722, 16334, 16051, 15818, 15602, 15413, 15269, 15134, 15008, 14884.
- mean count >= 1 / 5 / 10: 20656 / 17714 / 16587; minimum 5 in 3 samples 17761; minimum 1 in 2 samples 22545.
- DESeq2 independent filtering cannot be reproduced without the DESeq2 p-values of the unlisted genes. It removes low-mean genes only,
  and every listed gene has mean >= 4.04 (row total >= 24.2); a mean cut that keeps all 2252 listed genes retains about 18 thousand rows (mean >= 1 gives 20656, mean >= 5 gives 17714),
  not 15370.
The only rule that gives exactly 15370 is mean count > 20.5, found by reading off the target rank. It is arbitrary, it would drop 16 genes
that are in the authors' own lists, and I do not offer it. The closest natural rule, "10 in every one of six libraries", gives 15134 (236 short) and
also drops 138 listed genes. Verdict: 15,370 NOT REPRODUCIBLE HERE; the Scientist's choice to label 19,694 a PROXY and the universe CONTEXT_UNRESOLVED is correct.
