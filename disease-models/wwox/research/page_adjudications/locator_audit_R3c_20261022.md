# Blind locator audit - group R3c (11 readings)

Auditor: blind adversarial auditor, read-only toward canonical files. Date 2026-10-22. Inputs: the (proposition, quote, anchor) triples printed by `locator_audit.py --triples 0` for the eleven manifests, and the source artefacts they name. The reader's note, self-evaluation and the R3 answers were not opened.

## Method and surfaces

- Source artefact for every triple: the PMC efetch XML in `files/fulltext/` named by the manifest. The SHA-256 of each of the eleven files was recomputed and equals the manifest's recorded digest (11 of 11 match) before any reading.
- Surface: XML text layer (body, legends, table cells read cell by cell from the raw XML). Resolution: native text, no rasterisation. No triple rests on a figure image. `paper_packet.py rank` was run for the two papers with Table 6 (33405376 and 33547201): no figure-bearing surface on disk, so no figure was read and no vector/page-render question arises.
- Surfaces keep their en-dashes and minus signs in the XML, so ranged and signed numbers were checkable; none is UNVERIFIABLE for that reason.
- Verdict rule used: SUPPORTED = source carries the proposition (quote may be short, flagged); OVERSHOOT = source says less than the proposition or a weaker verb; UNDERSHOOT = source says more than the proposition withholds (none found); UNVERIFIABLE = a negative or a part of the proposition whose absence/presence I could not establish from the surfaces on disk.
- Flags added to the note column: QUOTE-INSUFFICIENT = a numeral of the proposition is not in the quote (the reader's numeral-in-quote self-check, tested mechanically over all 121 triples); QUOTE-CLIPPED = a qualitative element is outside the quote but in the source.
- 'Numeral exception' column: the manifests carry no per-entry marker for the exception. I classified as exception the entries whose quote is a table row identified only by position, or a bare group-size digit in a legend (16 entries, listed below). If the reader's list differs, this column will differ.
