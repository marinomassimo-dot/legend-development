NOTES (limits, with what the anchor says more or less)
- P1. Limit: Wang's WWOX-knockdown blots could show a Ser9 band that the legend does not name; I judged from text only. "The one kinase assay" is slightly narrow: Wang also runs a GS-1 peptide substrate and a WWOXtide competition in the same cell-free assay format (supplementary figure and Fig 4d). "LOWER" rests on the Cheng text ("dephosphorylation… active GSK3β"); I did not see the ratio values.
- P4. Two limits. (1) The cortex Wnt result is delivery-specific. The SCI addendum's blind recompute put the cortex Wnt shift (adjusted -0.873) just outside its own null band (percentile 0.05), with the organoid adjusted sign positive under its covariate. The statement carries only the primary run's "inside the null band". (2) The interactome file says criterion (iii) is "partial": lithium Ki is abstract-level, and no GSK3beta-specific non-lithium constant was read in a primary source. The narrowings listed in the statement omit this. Both the pS9 tension and the "attributes its effect to binding" reading are carried from Wang and Cheng, which I did confirm. The SCI numbers themselves are carried from held data that I did not recompute.
- P5. "Public-database" is slightly broader than the sheet. The exposure section names only PubMed, "two routes each; titles read, nothing beyond". Only tideglusib's query strings are spelled out. Absence is title-level and route-bound ("not found by these routes is not absence"). Rodent-only is correct.
- P6. The sheet's own 2011 AR-A014418 pair (70 µM alpha against 330 µM beta) is 4.7-fold in the opposite direction. The statement keeps it outside "at most 2.5-fold" only through "set apart". SB-216763 and tideglusib have no alpha/beta pair, so "1.4 to 2.5" covers only CHIR-99021 and LY2090314. Sheet values were checked against the sheet only, not against ChEMBL.
- P7. Limit 1: Kim gives "died at P0" but no explicit causal attribution of death. The both-isoform attribution is explicit only for the brain phenotype, via the single-allele and triple-allele comparison. The "no major malformations" statement for single mutants is cited as "unpublished observations". "Germline" is implied by "Gsk3a null"/"Gsk3a −/−" but not spelled out. Limit 2: Ma's proliferation measure is a 2 h BrdU pulse at E18.5, four days after electroporation at E14.5. "Genetic" for siRNA knockdown is loose (knockdown, not deletion). Per-panel significance could not be mapped because the asterisks are stripped in the tool text.
