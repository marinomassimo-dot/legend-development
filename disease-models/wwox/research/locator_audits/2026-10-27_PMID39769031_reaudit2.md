## Card row DR-E02-006 ("not attributable to LDH alone")

Conclusion SUPPORTED, with two flagged weaknesses, neither of which reverses it.
- Supported by source: Yadav 2026 says LDHA knockdown and over-expression "suggested at least a partial role of LDH" and lysate inhibition was "moderate and significant ... only at 250 and 500 uM"; Bouchez 2026 shows stiripentol inhibits complex I and not MDH2 (Fig. 9); Guyon 2022 reports survival P = 0.002 at 150 mg/kg in the Results and legend, with 100 mg/kg printed in Methods inside the bevacizumab-schedule sentence, as the row says. The row's own hedge ("on the available data", "at least a partial role") matches.
- Weakness 1 (UNDERSHOOT of a caveat): the lysate-versus-viability contrast is offered as evidence of a non-LDH mechanism, but Yadav treated cells at the stated concentrations and then lysed them before the assay; Bouchez reports stiripentol is a reversible inhibitor (no inhibition after washing). A reversible inhibitor diluted at lysis would understate in-cell LDH inhibition, so the lysate leg is weaker than the row implies. The complex I leg carries the conclusion.
- Weakness 2: the row omits the counter-datum that Guyon 2022 shows lactate production falling about 50 percent (500 uM) and that the HepG2 LDHA-siRNA arm exists (both are in note 2.2). The row is therefore silent on the best argument for an LDH component, beyond "at least a partial role". Not a distortion; the negative stays "not LDH alone", not "not LDH".
