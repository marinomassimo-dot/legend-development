## 3 · Added findings from the audit (not owed by the manifest, but they change what the paper can carry)

A. Racemate versus its enantiomers across the whole IC50 table (Table 2, S65-S74), a pattern the producer saw only for stiripentol:

| Series | Racemate | Enantiomers | Racemate relative to both |
|---|---|---|---|
| stiripentol (percent at 500 uM) | 9.9 | 34.0 and 38.4 | weaker than both |
| 3 (IC50 uM) | 125.3 | 213.7 and 166.3 | more potent than both |
| 4, (2S*,5R*) | 199.8 | 101.7 and 128.4 | weaker than both |
| 4, (2R*,5R*) | 129.6 | 179.7 and 149.3 | more potent than both |

Racemate and enantiomers differ by up to a factor of two in either direction in every series; for a 1:1 mixture of two non-interacting inhibitors the mixture should fall between its components. The scatter therefore tells that between-preparation or between-run variation of IC50 is about two-fold, as large as the 100-200 uM band that separates "best" from "worst" analogue. This supports reading the paper as a screen, not a ranking.

B. The analogue-series limit already in the manifest (no detergent, 5 percent DMSO, steep curves) is intact; the absence of any selectivity datum (LDHB, other dehydrogenases) was not asserted by the manifest and is true on the text.
