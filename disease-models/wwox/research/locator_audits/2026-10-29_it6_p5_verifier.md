## 2. Design logic

Question asked: would the discrimination table (Section 8) really separate function, stability and over-expression artefact? Short answer: it separates them on paper, but three weaknesses mean that the published material underneath cannot yet bear the ruler, and the design should be narrowed before the A01 card is rewritten.

D1. The ruler is not anchored to a physiological range by any published dataset, and the in-vivo data suggest the bulk-protein axis is the wrong x-axis. In 42422765, the low dose (modest survival benefit) already shows 3 to 19 times wild-type protein in cortex and hippocampus at P30, while the cerebellum is below wild type at both doses and the high dose reaches 8 to 11 times wild type at P300. Survival tracks neither bulk protein in the two regions with the most protein nor DNA or mRNA. Whatever carries the phenotype (a region, a cell type, a time window, a peripheral tissue not measured) is not what a per-lysate bulk protein value reports. The design's "phenotype against measured per-cell protein" is therefore only as good as the choice of cell and compartment; the table should state that a ruler built in one cell type does not transfer to a tissue dose-response, and that pattern A holds only within the system that produced the ruler.

D2. Pattern A ("Y at or close to Pwt(P)", "the only pattern that permits the word rescue for L4") is too permissive for an SDR-domain missense when Tier 1 is WW1-dependent. The glucose and HIF1a readout of 25012504 depends on WW1 binding; a protein whose SDR domain is badly folded but whose WW1 module still binds would land on the wild-type curve and be called "present and functional". The producer mentions this in the transferability text (W06) but the table's reading cell says "rescue". Narrow the wording of pattern A to "recovery of the WW1-dependent axis" and keep the word rescue out of the table unless a second, SDR-region readout also agrees. Pattern A with a normal binding ratio B is also what an intact WW end would give for a destabilised SDR, so A cannot exclude SDR misfolding.

D3. The "binding-dead SDR-region control (L404A)" is a negative control for GSK3b binding and for the Tau and neurite readouts only. No published experiment shows L404A is dead for glucose uptake, HIF1a, or organoid firing, and L404A has never been shown to alter WW1-dependent HIF1a binding. For Tier 1 the supported dead arm is WFPA, a WW1 mutant. In controls item 3 and in the card text, L404A should be described as a probe of the SDR-region surface for WFR-2, not as a phenotype-null arm. In addition, the GSK3b site lies within residues 388 to 407 of a 414-residue protein, a C-terminal stretch outside the nucleotide-binding core; preserved binding there is weak evidence of SDR-core integrity (the same limit the producer states for c-Jun, applied to the other end, not fixed by it). Both ends of the folded-mutant test fail to cover the SDR core where a missense sits.

D4. Tier 2 (Ca2+ firing) is not a titratable ruler readout. The published add-back is AAV with WPRE in patient-derived lines (not null), organoid n of 3 to 8 per group, mean plus or minus SEM, a firing-rate difference between lines that changes with age, and no stated protein level relative to wild type. Use it as a binary confirmation, not as the curve. The title of Tier 2 in Section 6 should say so, and the design should say that no published readout in a human neural system supports a graded dose-response (only the mouse HD/LD pair does).

D5. Pattern table gaps. (a) No row for a compound that raises per-molecule function R (a pharmacological chaperone that raises both P and R): patterns E and F both require "same curve", so a compound that lifts the variant above its own untreated curve at equal protein has no label. Add a row. (b) Pattern D relies on "above the physiological range of the ruler", which needs the isogenic wild-type cell's endogenous WWOX level to be measured in the same plate; the producer's ruler goes "from below to above the endogenous level" but never names an endogenous WT arm among the required controls. Add an isogenic wild-type, non-induced arm. (c) Pattern B says "P wild-type-like but Y near N"; with the Q230P-class allele the expected case is low P, which falls under I, C, or G, and B needs the variant protein at wild-type-like P to be reached by induction, i.e. the ruler's variant arm must also be titrated, not tested at one promoter dose.

D6. Stability versus function versus over-expression is better separated than the producer states by one thing already in the table: mRNA flat plus S/P/T plus MS. That part is sound. The weaker discrimination is function versus over-expression in a null line, because wild-type add-back without a reference WT cell cannot tell whether a plateau is reached at or above endogenous level (D5b).

## 3. What the producer should narrow (defect list)

1. W06 / Section 6: remove "supra-physiological" and the "titration must start where that study did not" argument unless the Fig 3b panel has a WT comparator; the 25-fold is over a null empty-vector baseline and the figure values were not re-opened. Move to UNVERIFIED item 1 explicitly (it is there, but the table text and the choice paragraph state it as fact).
2. W06 "only published" should be "found in this review".
3. W10 arithmetic "78 to 89 percent consumed" is not reproducible from the text; cite panel or drop.
4. W10: add the observation to check (identical with-substrate curve across seven substrates, substrate-free curve above it) as a flagged item for the original figure, not as a result.
5. W11 and UNVERIFIED item 4: the Methods three times describe an ADH-domain-only construct; state that and drop "tells not which domain", or label it as conditional on which RNA was used.
6. W12 and Section 5: "no measured expression difference" should become "no statistically resolved difference in vector DNA or mRNA; protein shows an untested trend (cortex about 2-fold)". Also record that the low dose already gives several-fold wild-type protein in cortex and hippocampus.
7. W12 "opposite survival" should read "modest versus near-wild-type survival".
8. W01/W04: Y33F is in-vitro only; Y33R is not a dead control for the apoptosis readout.
9. L404A: reclassify as an SDR-region binding probe, not a phenotype-dead arm (controls item 3, card text, WFR-1 item 1).
10. Pattern A wording ("rescue for L4") and the missing "R raised" row (see D2 and D5a).
11. W09 anchor for pattern B rests on one report and unlabelled superscripts: re-read with original typography before relying on "stable but inert".
12. State the inference status of "c-Jun binds the WW domains" (the paper does not locate it).
