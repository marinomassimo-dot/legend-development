## Sentences in the target claims that the source directly contradicts

### CLAIM 037

1. **Claim (Title):** "…the **audiogenic/kindling** phenotype remains rat-specific"
   **Source:** "16-day-old knock-out mice were exposed to sustained sound (11–14 kHz tone) for 5–10 min. A few knock-out mice (three of eight) presented with audiogenic tonic-clonic seizures in the first minutes after sound exposure." / "At 20 days, the four surviving mice were exposed to a 14 kHz tone. All knock-out mice presented at different times with seizures…" / "No wild-type mice of matched age and background ( n = 8) presented with seizures upon 11 or 14 kHz sound exposure."
   The audiogenic part is contradicted. The source says nothing about kindling in the mouse.

2. **Claim (Title):** "…in the **Wwox-null mouse** (behaviourally since 2020…)"
   **Source (2013/2014 article):** "we observed that the Wwox knock-out mice start having a few spontaneous seizures at ∼2 weeks of age", plus the audiogenic results above. The behavioural seizure record for the Wwox knock-out mouse is therefore older than 2020.

3. **Claim:** "🔴 **`audiogenic` is DOCUMENTED rat-specific and ASSERTED for the mouse at abstract depth only, and the distinction is load-bearing.**"
   **Source:** the Results section ("Conditional knock-out mouse model") and Materials and methods ("Animal experiments") document the mouse audiogenic experiment with numbers, a protocol and controls (quotes in item 1, plus "Exposure to digital 11 and 14 kHz tones (5–10-min exposure) was conducted using speakers adjacent to three sides of the cages. Mice behaviour was monitored and recorded using a video camera."). It is not abstract-only.

4. **Claim:** "…the body is not served by any route available here, so **there is no `n`, no strain, no stimulus protocol and no control to read.**"
   **Source body provides:**
   - n: "three of eight"; "the four surviving mice"; wild-type "( n = 8)".
   - Strain and genotype: "male Wwox flox/flox mice (129SV/C57Bl/6 background) … with female BK5-Cre mice (129SV/C57Bl/6 background) … producing full knock-out progeny".
   - Protocol: "digital 11 and 14 kHz tones (5–10-min exposure) … speakers adjacent to three sides of the cages".
   - Control: "No wild-type mice of matched age and background ( n = 8)…".

5. **Claim:** "And **no Wwox mouse of any allele has ever been audiogenically provoked in any inspectable method** (`PREMISE: NOBODY_LOOKED`; the only provocation ever applied to a Wwox mouse is chemoconvulsant)."
   **Source:** "To investigate susceptibility to epilepsy, 16-day-old knock-out mice were exposed to sustained sound (11–14 kHz tone) for 5–10 min." / "At 20 days, the four surviving mice were exposed to a 14 kHz tone." / "Other stimuli such as animal handling also induced seizures on some occasions." Sound (and handling) were applied as provocations, not only chemoconvulsants.

6. **Claim:** "…a scientific commentary in the same journal **independently** transmits *«knockout mice displayed audiogenic seizures»*."
   **Source:** the sentence "Wwox knockout mice displayed audiogenic seizures." sits inside this article's own front-matter summary. It is the headless first block of the article's abstract section, just before "Keywords:", and it begins "Mallaret et al. report mutations…". In this artefact it is part of the same article record, not independent transmission. This contradicts "independently" as the artefact presents it; the artefact does not show whether a separate commentary also exists.

7. **Claim:** "⚠️ **Species and alleles stay separate:** … the mouse spontaneous and provoked data are the **NCKU WD1/WD234** nulls…" (and the tally that follows: "Four phenomena, four methods, three genotypes, two species.")
   **Source:** spontaneous and provoked (audiogenic, handling) seizure data are reported for the BK5-Cre × Wwox flox/flox constitutive full knock-out (Ludes-Meyers 2009 conditional allele; M.D. Anderson facility), which is not an NCKU allele. That the mouse data come only from NCKU is contradicted. The genotype tally is contradicted as a consequence.

8. **Claim (Evidence boundary, rat caveat):** "2–3 settimane nel topo contro **3–12 settimane** nel ratto…" (2–3 weeks in the mouse versus 3–12 weeks in the rat)
   **Source:** "These knock-out mice are characterized by a short lifespan of only 3 to 4 weeks maximum" / "They eventually died before 4 weeks of age from failure to thrive". Four mice were still alive to be exposed at day 20. The source's mouse range (3–4 weeks maximum) differs from the claim's 2–3 weeks. The contradiction is partial, since both ranges include 3 weeks.

9. **Claim (Impact on Working Model):** "Sposta l'epilettogenesi da premessa importata su un modello murino a `DATO` misurato su un modello **di ratto**…" (moves epileptogenesis from a premise imported onto a mouse model to a `DATO` measured in a rat model)
   **Source:** seizures (spontaneous and audiogenic) are observed first-hand in the knock-out mouse by the authors ("we observed…"; the Results quotes above). This contradicts the premise that the mouse seizure phenotype is only imported. The word "epileptogenesis" itself does not occur in the source.

### CLAIM 005

1. **Claim (Evidence boundary):** "Seizures in the Wwox literature are a **rat `lde/lde`** phenotype — see [[claim_registry_current#CLAIM 037]]."
   **Source:** "the short-lived Wwox knock-out mouse display spontaneous and audiogenic seizures" (Abstract). "Wwox knock-out mice start having a few spontaneous seizures at ∼2 weeks of age" and "three of eight … audiogenic tonic-clonic seizures" (Results). "The Wwox knock-out mice show a progressive susceptibility to spontaneous and audiogenic tonic-clonic epilepsy" (Discussion).

2. **Claim (Evidence boundary):** "Early death and epileptogenesis were attributed here to PMID 19936220 and Mallaret 2014. All three links of that chain have since been read in full." Read together with "🔴 **And that terminus asserts the opposite for the mouse**" and the conclusion drawn from it:
   This is only a partial contradiction. The claim treats PMID 19500159 as the terminus. The Mallaret source is itself a first-hand mouse seizure observation by the model's laboratory, so the chain's end-point for the mouse is not the rat-only negative. The two sentences themselves report what other papers say, and the source does not contradict those reports as reports.

Not contradicted: CLAIM 005's rule that no statement may assert EPILEPTOGENESIS as a measured process. The source's "progressive susceptibility … suggesting a neurodegenerative process" is interpretive, the paper measures no epileptogenic transition, and the word does not appear in it. CLAIM 005's Summary (PV, NPY, IBA1/GFAP) is outside what this source addresses.
