## 2. The arithmetic, recomputed

Producer model: Hill coefficient 1, `fraction_unblocked = 1/(1 + [Mg]/IC50(V))`, IC50(V) log-linear between the -90 and
-70 mV values (slope 0.0468 per mV). I reproduced the producer's table exactly (2.59 / 3.07 / 3.35 % at 1.3 mM).

Key property: in any single-site model of this form the Mg2+ rise needed to bring the knockout back to the wild-type
fraction is independent of [Mg] and of the absolute affinity; it equals exp(k * dV), where k is the voltage slope of
ln(Kd) and dV the depolarisation. So the "~30 %" figure is a statement about the chosen slope, not about the
physiology of cortex.

| Model / parameter set | WT / HT / KO unblocked at 1.3 mM | KO/WT | Mg rise that offsets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Producer: 2-point log-linear (k 0.0468/mV) | 2.59 / 3.07 / 3.35 % | 1.29 | +30 % |
| Source 3-point log-quadratic through -90/-70/-50 | 2.52 / 3.00 / 3.29 % | 1.31 | +32 % |
| Jahr-Stevens-type (Kd(0) 3.57 mM, 0.062/mV; from memory, unverified against the paper) | 1.98 / 2.48 / 2.78 % | 1.41 | +42 % |
| Slope grid k 0.04 .. 0.08/mV (any Kd(0) 1.5 .. 10 mM) | - | 1.18 .. 1.57 | +25 % .. +57 % |
| Uncertainty of dV (5.63 mV; 3.2 and 8.0 as an illustrative +-1 SE-scale range) | - | 1.21 .. 1.62 | +22 % .. +64 % |
| Liquid-junction shift of all potentials by -5/-10/-15 mV (uncorrected in Breton) | KO 2.05 / 1.52 / 1.12 % | 1.41 (unchanged) | +42 % (unchanged) |

Findings:
- The ordering conclusion survives every plausible range: the knockout leaves more NMDAR conductance unblocked at rest
  (1.2 to 1.6 times), and a Mg2+ rise of the order of 25 to 60 % would offset the resting-potential component only.
  "Order of magnitude, partial, nowhere near d-APV" is supported.
- The specific figures "1.3 times" and "about 30 %" are not robust: the producer's own source-derived slope is the
  shallowest of the reasonable choices (the source itself says delta 0.77 is shallower than for GluN2A/GluN2B, the
  cortical subunits). With a cortical-type slope the offset is about 40 %, which is larger than the +31 % CSF rise the
  README places beside it. The README does call that match a coincidence, but the coincidence weakens from "same
  size" to "reachable rise falls short". See D5.
- Unsourced parameters flagged by the producer (Hill 1, log-linear, dopaminergic for cortical, bath = incubation ACSF)
  are all disclosed. Not flagged: the uncorrected junction potential (absolute voltages shift the fractions by a factor
  of two but not the ratio or the offset), and that the amplitude of the burst-relevant conductance (voltage range
  during synchronised input, not rest) is outside this arithmetic; the README says the latter in words.
- The heterozygote (-75.51) lies between, consistent with the table; the card's untested prediction for carriers follows.
