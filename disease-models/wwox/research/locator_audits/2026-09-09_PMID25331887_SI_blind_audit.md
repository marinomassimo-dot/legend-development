## (b) Exact replacement wording

**Triple 0 →**
> The WWOX-induction figure (Fig. S1) shows one blot per cell line — HEK293 (A) and osteosarcoma LM7 (B) — each carrying a bare fold-change row: HEK293 1.0 / 1.4 / 1.9 / 2.7 / 2.7 / 3.5 and LM7 1.0 / 1.2 / 1.6 / 2.1 / 2.4 / 2.2 at 0, 5, 10, 30, 60 and 120 min after 10 Gy. Neither panel prints an error bar, a replicate count, an n or a significance mark, and the legend supplies none, so the induction is supported as a concordant trend in two cell lines and not as a quantified effect size. In panel A the HSP90 loading control is not flat: its density falls monotonically across the time course (net render density 32.4 → 18.6, ≈ −43%) while WWOX rises, so the printed fold changes are not supported as loading-normalised values.

**Triple 1 →**
> In Fig. S3B, WWOX depletion both blunts γ-H2AX focus induction at 15 min (red-positive pixels 30 in MCF7-sh-WWOX-CL3 vs 342 in MCF7-sh-EV) and leaves foci still present at 24 h, when the control has cleared (76 px vs 1 px). At 48 h neither genotype shows detectable foci (0 px in both; mean red-excess 0.086 vs 0.057). The panel therefore supports impaired induction *plus* delayed resolution at 24 h — a persistence phenotype the caption's word "phosphorylation" does not cover — but it does **not** support unresolved foci at 48 h. Representative fields only; no counts, no n, no statistics in the panel (the legend refers focus quantification to main-text Fig. 3C, which was not audited).

**Triple 3 →**
> Fig. S4A assigns the p-ATM co-precipitation to WW1. Left pull-down: p-ATM is recovered with GST-WW1+2 in the NCS lane (lane 3) and is not detectable in either GST-SDR lane (2 and 4), although GST-SDR is itself recovered in both — so the SDR negative is a genuine negative and not a failed pull-down. Right pull-down, with all eight lanes read column by column off the +/− grid (1 WW1_WT/−NCS, 2 WW1_MT/−NCS, 3 WW1_WT/+NCS, 4 WW1_MT/+NCS, 5 WW2_WT/−NCS, 6 WW2_MT/−NCS, 7 WW2_WT/+NCS, 8 WW2_MT/+NCS): p-ATM is detectable in lane 3 only, while lysate p-ATM is positive in all four NCS lanes (3, 4, 7, 8) and pull-down GST is positive in all eight — so the WW1-WFPA and WW2 negatives are not explained by absent substrate or absent bait. The WFPA mutation therefore reduces p-ATM recovery **below detection on a single blot**, which is not the same as abolishing the interaction; and the experiment tests the *sufficiency* of isolated GST-tagged domains over-expressed in HEK293, not the requirement of WW1 within full-length WWOX. Whether this assignment appears only in the supplement was not verifiable from the six SI pages.

**Triple 5 →**
> In Fig. S5, WWOX depletion reduces both readouts, but unequally. After NCS, MDC1 green-positive pixels fall from 245 (MCF7-Control) to 34 (MCF7-shWWOX) — an ~86% reduction, yet still about twice the shWWOX untreated field (17 px), so discrete MDC1 foci still form. p-CHK2 red-positive pixels fall from 705 to 7, i.e. to the shWWOX untreated level (5 px), so p-CHK2 is undetectable. The panel supports a graded, ordered defect — residual but strongly reduced MDC1 focus formation with undetectable p-CHK2 — not "MDC1 retained / p-CHK2 abolished". Single 30-min time point, representative fields, no cell counts, no n, no statistics.

**Triple 6 →**
> In the Fig. S6B pull-down the K-63-Ub panel is uninformative: it shows only faint vertical streaking with no high-molecular-weight ladder, and, unlike the HA, FK1 and FK2 panels run over the same pull-down, it does not discriminate the GST-WWOX lanes (1–3) from the GST-WFPA lanes (4–6) at all (per-lane render density 53, 70, 68, 60, 67, 65). Because it is a separate blot with a different antibody, exposure and background, its faintness relative to the HA/FK1/FK2 panels is not admissible as evidence against K63 linkage — it establishes only that this particular linkage-specific antibody panel carries no interpretable signal. This is not the supplement's only K63-linkage evidence: Fig. S6C uses K63-only and K48-only HA-Ub constructs and shows strong HA ladders in the K63-only lanes (density 156–169) against much weaker K48-only lanes (86–105).

**Triple 9 →**
> In Fig. S7A the WWOX blot shows the K274R construct expressed in MCF7-Sh+K274R above the endogenous WWOX level of parental MCF7 (saturated, render minimum 32, vs ~107), with MCF7-sh at background. In the p-KAP1 row (lane assignment verified against the GAPDH and KAP1 group bands), the 1 h band is strong in parental MCF7 (minimum 32, saturated) and only faint in both MCF7-sh (170) and MCF7-Sh+K274R (156) against a ~220 background. The panel therefore supports: K274R is abundantly expressed yet does not restore NCS-induced KAP1 Ser824 phosphorylation to parental levels. It does not on its own establish a functional defect of the K274R residue, because the panel contains no wild-type-WWOX re-expression arm in the same shRNA background, no p-ATM readout, and no replicate, n or statistic — and the residual faint band is a reduction, not an absence.

## (d) Disposition of the five claims (judged only from these triples and pages)

**CLAIM 007 — LEAVE UNCHANGED.** Nothing on SI pages 1–6 concerns P47T or the Hussain source. (Process note only: the word "abolishes" in this claim carries the same absolute-negative risk the audit found in triple 3; that is a wording flag, not evidence from this reading.)

**CLAIM 023 — LEAVE UNCHANGED.** No p73, no Tyr33, no relocalisation data on these pages.

**CLAIM 024 — CORROBORATE (weak, with one narrowing addition).** Fig. S4A right shows isolated **GST-WW2 (WT or YFPA) recovers no p-ATM in any lane**, while isolated **GST-WW1_WT alone is sufficient** — consistent with "WW2 is not a canonical standalone PPxY-binding domain." Add the narrowing rider: for at least one partner (ATM), the isolated WW1 domain is sufficient in a HEK293 lysate pull-down, so tandem cooperativity is not required for every WWOX partner interaction. Caveat to record: this pull-down is from cell lysate and does not establish a direct or PPxY-mediated contact.

**CLAIM 029 — NARROW (four specific narrowings; keep status `in observation`).**
1. "K63 ubiquitination of WWOX **on Lys274**" → Lys274 is required for the *damage-induced increment* in WWOX polyubiquitination, **not** for the bulk of it: Fig. S6C shows K274R retaining substantial HMW ladders, and with K63-only ubiquitin the mutant equals or exceeds wild type in the untreated condition (161 vs 156).
2. The K63 assignment rests on the **K63-only/K48-only HA-Ub construct comparison (S6C)**, not on a linkage-specific antibody: the K-63-Ub antibody panel (S6B) is faint and non-discriminating.
3. "compromises γ-H2AX **induction/maintenance**" → induction blunted at 15 min and resolution delayed **at 24 h**; by 48 h neither genotype retains detectable foci.
4. "**ITCH-dependent**" is not evidenced anywhere in this supplement — carry it only if the main text supports it, and mark its provenance.

Two supportable **additions**, currently absent from the claim: on the WWOX side the p-ATM interaction is assigned to **WW1 and not SDR** (isolated-domain sufficiency, Fig. S4A); on the ATM side to **residues 1,530–2,100**, with a faint additional signal in the kinase-domain fragment (Fig. S4B). Across all six SI pages there is **not one error bar, replicate count or statistical test** — that alone justifies keeping the claim in observation.

**CLAIM 030 — CORROBORATE (weak, principle-level; no edit to the claim body).** Fig. S7A is an abundance-versus-function dissociation in the same direction as the claim: K274R is expressed *above* endogenous WWOX yet does not restore ATM-dependent KAP1 phosphorylation. But it is an engineered ubiquitin-acceptor mutant in a cancer line, not a patient allele, on a single blot with no wild-type rescue arm. Log it as a mechanistic analogue only; do **not** add it to a T1 / VERY HIGH claim built on the human allelic series, and do not let it raise the transferability tier.
