## TSV check (Q6)

Done after my own findings. Full recheck rather than a 10-row sample: all 95 ChEMBL-sourced rows (abat 23, proteostasis 28, ttype 44) were matched programmatically to the live activity by assay id + molecule id + standard_type. Result: 94/94 rows with a matching activity agree on value, unit, relation and measure (0 unit or inequality errors; ">" values preserved). Assay organism and target organism labels agree with ChEMBL on every row (e.g. guinea pig assay with Homo sapiens target kept separate). The one non-matching row, abat line 28 (51.8-fold), is a computed ratio, and 5.7/0.11 = 51.8 is correct. Independent hand-checked random sample of 10 per table (seed 7, 30 rows): 0 numeric errors. Numeric error rate: 0/95 (0%) ChEMBL rows; 0/30 in the sample.

Label-level defects (interpretive, not numeric): 
1. abat line 7: rat vig Ki 10 mM (CHEMBL2148328, act 12045281) is labelled assay_type "inactivation" with a "time-dependent KI" note, but the ChEMBL description only says "Inhibition ... modified Scott and Jacoby method". Low severity (sibling record act 12173262 supports it).
2. abat line 16: patent record CHEMBL5732295 (vig Ki 3.2 mM) is labelled inactivation KI. Its sibling patent assay (CHEMBL5735863, line 23) states Ki computed from IC50 by Cheng-Prusoff, so these two "Ki" are IC50-derived, and line 23 says only "binding_or_biochemical" (correct) while line 16 is inconsistent with it. Low-moderate.
3. proteostasis line 36 ("none exists", assay type) omits the qualitative ChEMBL chaperone records for PB acid (acts 13477764, 15143468; "Active" at 3 mM, aggregation turbidity assay) and the ER-stress records. The "absence of a number" is true, "none exists" as a record statement is not. Moderate: this is the precise record a reader would look for.
Other: ttype line 5 (ethosuximide AC50 > 10 uM, rat L-type) is correctly labelled but is a different assay class (type F, AC50) than flux IC50; no error.

## Severity summary

- Numeric/unit/inequality: none found.
- Moderate: Q5a wording (qualitative chaperone records exist); Q2b provenance attribution of 850 uM (two independent routes, GtoPdb's own citation is the review/1977 paper); the printed 187 has two incompatible derivations in print and only the review's set reproduces it.
- Low: Q4c/d (ethosuximide 0.6 mM is persistent current; 0.3-1.2 mM is MPS; gap is 4.5-5.8 orders depending on endpoint); two assay-type labels in the ABAT table.
- Note for readers: ChEMBL's Homo sapiens tag on the 2002 vig record, the 2020 kinact/KI ratio and the 2023 OV329 IC50 is target mapping only (assay organism guinea pig or "unknown origin").
