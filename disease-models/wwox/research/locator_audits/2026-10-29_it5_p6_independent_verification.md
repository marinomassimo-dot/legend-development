# COMPARISON WITH PRODUCER (read after the above)
Agreement: sha256 of both files; ISR hNPC +0.636 vs my +0.64; neurons +1.316 vs my +1.36 (mine 11 members, theirs 10 because ATF5 <10 tags); progenitor markers identical (-1.7,-2.9,-3.1,-1.3); WWOX 3.35 vs 2.90 oriented correctly (the V2 wording of this brief is the one that is swapped); 9/26/61% composition; absent-gene list; ATF4 -0.15; five-member post hoc list is a subset of the eight I find.
Disagreements / overstatement / understatement:
1. LIBRARY SIZE ERROR (producer, minor-to-moderate): RESULTS/PRE_ANALYSIS take hNSC N = 31,702,049 (BED line count), but the hNSC TPM values are exact integer multiples of 1e6/51,007,825 (100% of 10,686 non-zero TSS), so the normalising total is 51.0M, not 31.7M. For the other three arms the BED count equals the implied total exactly (14,332,108; 32,686,868; 40,730,002). Consequences: WWOX hNSC is 148 tags not "92"; ATF4 hNSC 1408 not 875; the claim that the shWWOX hNPC library is "14.3 vs 31.7M tags" should read 14.3 vs 51.0M (3.6x, not 2.2x); their >=10-tag universe is slightly wrong for the hNPC contrast. Limitation 6 ("exact CAGEr total not stated") is in fact recoverable from the file. Direction of no conclusion changes.
2. Gate 2 headline overstates (see above).
3. "5 members rise at both stages": understated; 8 of 11 are positive in both.
4. ATF4 "unmeasurable in neurons": a producer threshold, not a deposit property; ATF4 is up ~4-5x there in centred terms (6 vs 41 tags); correctly harmless since mRNA is not activity, but "unmeasurable" should read "below the 10-tag rule".
5. Neuron WWOX: producer "3-4 fold" understates raw TPM 4.9x but then correctly warns about composition; the Poisson interval ("about +-0.5 log2") for 83 vs 21 tags is closer to +-0.7 log2 (SE of ln ratio 0.24 -> 1.96*0.24/0.693=0.68).
6. Producer states as fact that "the paper's control is shScrambled" and that the 9,427 genes equal the paper's GSEA universe; neither is in the GEO deposit (UNVERIFIABLE here; they were declared as paper-sourced; the control-identity ambiguity was handled correctly in limitation 3).
7. Producer correctly under-claims (no needs entry, no ISR/respiration inference). Understated risks that I would add: the HES5 and PAX6 silenced hNPC counts are only 11 tags each in a 14.3M-tag library, so those "markedly lower" values are noisy; the hNSC_WWOX_del arm may be a different cell state (SOX2/PAX6/HES5/FABP7/TUBB3 all fall), which is itself a strong sign that arms differ in more than WWOX.
