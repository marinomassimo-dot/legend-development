## Interpretation verdicts

1. "Discordant ISR sign" is fair as a descriptive statement, with limits. The organoid sign is the better supported (4 v 2, CHAC1 t=-11.6, TRIB3 t=-4.1, SESN2 t=-4.3). The hNPC rise rests on one library per arm and a handful of genes (CHAC1, TRIB3, PSAT1, SESN2, ATF5); gene-level count noise (Poisson) is the only error available. The producer says so repeatedly and calls it descriptive. Minor: the word "discordant" in the matrix and the "two human neural data of opposite sign" in sec 3.1 weigh an n=1 observation as an equal datum; n=1 supports only "no basis to assume the organoid sign generalises". Also the neuron ISR rise is a composition-sensitive statistic (the producer's own sensitivity A flips sign).
2. Kept apart: yes. KO vs knockdown, undemonstrated knockdown in hNPC (WWOX not lower in CAGE), 3D mixture vs 2D clonal, RNA-seq vs CAGE, possible native control, labs/lines are all listed in sec 0 and in the matrix notes column. Defect: the CAGE contrast is called "shWWOX" while the deposit column is "WWOX_del"; I did not check which; control identity is acknowledged as unproven.
3. Universes: correct and separate. Mine (14.4k / 9.4k / 8.4k) differ in thresholds from the producer's (15.8k / 9.3k / 8.5k) and results agree within 0.05 for the sets they share. Unmeasurable members correctly dropped (SLC7A11 in CAGE; ATF5 in neurons). One caution: the CAGE universe is expression-filtered by the control/silenced pair so very different libraries (neuron composition shift, 9 % of tags in gene table) change which genes qualify; centring handles this only if most genes are unchanged.
4. Pooling across studies: none found. The two organoid analyses are explicitly treated as one system; no combined statistic.
5. Concordance called validation/flux/enzyme activity/rescue: no. The text states transcript level only and "not independent validation". Weak point: the matrix verdict "CONCORDANT" for glycolysis combines a robust organoid shift with a CAGE neuron result at "null edge" (rank 0.045 with two of ten set members unmeasurable) and an hNPC result the producer admits is unstable to sensitivity A and stage-residual; and "PDK1 concordant down" is a single gene, inside its null in both systems.
6. Does it change or delimit the modules as claimed? (a) ISR direction/context: yes, defensible as a delimitation (the organoid sign should not be taken as general; next experiment protein/phospho) - but it should read "unresolved" rather than "two data of opposite sign". (b) Mitochondrial target: the delimitation holds (ribosome control moves as far; I reproduce this), and mito translation opposite sign org vs hNPC reproduces. (c) HIF1A-PDK-PDH: "no transcript support for PDK1 up" is correct, but PDK1 down is not itself supported either (null p 0.30/0.49); HIF1A mRNA is -0.27 organoid, -0.11 hNPC (neither differs), which the synthesis does not state and which strengthens the "chain unsupported" reading. (d) Differentiation context: the discordance of progenitor markers is robust in sign for hNPC (strongly down) but weak for the organoid on the four canonical markers; the generalisation warning stands, the organoid "higher progenitor" statement is set-dependent.

## Defects with severity
- MEDIUM: organoid progenitor "+0.38 outside null" and neuronal "-0.48 outside null" are set-dependent; on the four canonical progenitor markers and a 10-gene neuronal panel the organoid is inside the null (p=0.52, 0.11). Matrix/narrative should say "NES/VIM-driven; canonical four flat".
- MEDIUM: glycolysis "concordant" rests on a hand-picked 10-gene HK2/SLC2A1/LDHA-type set; with a 19-gene panel including core enzymes, hNPC is inside the null (p=0.20) and organoid mean is dominated by 5 genes (median +0.11). The robust statement is LDHA and HK2/GLUT up in organoid, LDHA up in hNPC.
- MEDIUM-LOW: PDK1 "concordant down" is one gene, inside its single-gene null in both systems (p=0.30, 0.49; hNPC interval +-0.45 spans zero relative to noise); the text calls it "concordant down" in the matrix though the module consequence (no support for PDK1 up) is the only safe reading.
