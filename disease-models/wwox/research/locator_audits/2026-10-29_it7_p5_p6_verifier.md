## 3. Defects, ranked

None is HIGH. None is a scientific error.

MEDIUM
* P5-1 / P6-4 (stale status). The P5 README and the P5 table (CMP-04, CMP-05, sources cells and section 6) and the P6 observations/counter-evidence (O5, O6, O7 limits "independent verification pending"; README sections 1 and 7) state that the P3 verifier was still checking and that P2 is not integrated. Both are no longer true: the P3 verifier report exists and reproduces every number quoted (it records the matched-null 95th percentile as 0.396 and exact p 0.0595 on its own draw against the producer's 0.389 and 0.060, same conclusion), and P2 is on main and in this branch. "Provisional" should be updated at integration; the quoted values 0.389 and 0.060 are the producer's draw and could carry the verifier's 0.396 / 0.0595 as the replication.
* P6-1 (omitted source caveat). The P3 verifier asked that the signature-route verdict keep "effect not detected, not excluded" (rho 0.143 is 0.007 under the threshold, 90 percent interval [0.039, 0.243] spans it). P6 observations O6/O7 and the P6 table say flatly "NO ADDED VALUE" for the signature route, and `inferred` lines say "the comparison showed no added value". The source also lists a weak positive counter-evidence to "no added value" for this route (lower bound 0.039, top-decile hit rate 15 percent); neither is carried. Not an efficacy claim and not in the wrong direction (it makes the result look slightly more negative than the source), but the sentence "not detected, not excluded" should travel with the number in the needs (O6 on the PDH need, O7 on the lactate need) and in the P5 MODEL cells.

LOW
* P5-2 (potency left UNKNOWN though the source now answers). CMP-05 reports dichloroacetate potency as UNKNOWN pending a retry. The retry today succeeds: ChEMBL holds enzymatic PDK assays for the sodium salt (censored IC50 above 1,000,000 nM against rat, human and porcine enzyme preparations) and a cell assay (EC50 130,000 nM for increased lactate oxidation in human skin fibroblasts). These should be added with their assay context (and the cell-versus-enzyme gap noted), or the cell should say "retry succeeded, not yet transcribed". Phenylbutyrate needs no change (CMP-01 already carries Ki 6.3 uM from the same source).
* P6-2 (work order does not derive). `view/work_order.json` is a hand-written file: the P6 sentences in its entries are copies of card text. Mutating a card (section 2, point 3) does not change the work order and nothing flags the drift; the producer's regression checks the presence of the sentences, not their consistency with the card. Acceptable for this iteration; a one-line consistency check (the work-order text contains the card's first sentence) would close it.
* P6-3 (README versus card wording). P6 README step 7 describes the new readout as "pre-declared secondary transcripts", while the E02 card and work-order entry say "LDHA and LDHB activity separately". Both are proposals; harmonise to avoid reading as two different measurements.
* P5-3 (small coverage and internal tension). The nine records show A-deep, B-deep and A-partial but no B-partial combination, so the independence of Track and depth is shown by three of four cells; the README says so implicitly. CMP-05 lists "exposure rises with repeated administration" (self-inhibition) in the opposed-effects cell and "plateaued rather than accumulating" in the exposure cell; each is from the cards, but the contrast should be stated (children, one cohort, versus the general mechanism). "22 of 27" GluN2B variants (CMP-07) is derived from "more than 80 percent of the 27" in the abstract-level source; give the source form.
