## Transfer and neuron-arm statements to check against source

* Card transfer_limits: "overexpression (CMV-promoter plasmid or lentivirus)". HEK is pCMV5 (supported). For neurons the paper states lentiviral expression was "moderate ... close to WT levels and not overexpression, even upon addition of exorbitant lentivirus amounts" (Supp 3a,b, text only; I did not view that supplement panel). The card's grouping of the neuron arm with overexpression is therefore not what the authors say; the README's "or the wild type is over-expressed relative to need" is a hypothesis against the paper's statement, partly offset by the authors' remark that massive overexpression of Munc18-1 is not deleterious (supports a saturating read-out).
* "Dominant-negative mechanism WWOX does not have": paper supports the dominant-negative framing (co-aggregation of WT with mutant; Fig 5); WWOX-DEE recessive is the repository's own premise, not from this paper.
* Receipt: figures coverage is "captions_only" although Fig 3, Fig 6 and Supp page 19 were opened; this under-declares, which is the safe direction. The source fingerprint could not be checked (files/ is absent in the worktree).

## Inference quality (questions asked)

1. Generic abundance increase presented as rescue: AVOIDED. Card and README repeatedly separate boost from rescue ("no abundance gain read as rescue until a wild-type function ruler exists"), carry L0/L1 limits, state wild type is raised alike. One favourable-direction statement ("a total-protein blot alone would have under-called this rescue", README 6.1) uses the word "rescue" for a Munc18 read-out with a saturating pixel-count metric and an unclear WT baseline.
2. Transfer between diseases/clients: AVOIDED as a WWOX result. Every external row is tagged NOT WWOX EVIDENCE; the compartment objection is correctly narrowed. Minor leak: "the compartment objection that withdrew this class (DIS-004) is not established for a soluble client" rests on one dominant-negative client in HEK/neurons.
3. Abundance / localisation / interaction / function kept apart: YES in README sec. 3 and 6.4. The WWOX "both directions" sentence is the one place that over-reads (see 6b).
4. Predictions registered in both directions: YES (P-A..P-E, README sec. 8). P-E is the unfavourable branch; P-A closes A01/A02/A05 for the allele. Gap: P-C reads "reproduced by osmolyte with mRNA flat" as physical-chaperone-like, but the osmolytes act at 200 mM; the design asks for an osmotic-matched control (stated), good. No prediction is registered for "butyrate-like arm reproduces the gain in a WWOX system" other than P-B.

## Defects (severity)

* D1 MEDIUM: "four of five" function claim (DR-A01-009) pairs with the "4-8% in four of five" sentence but the function set includes P335L (65% protein, little baseline deficit) and excludes R406H (PBA ~44 vs WT ~55). In the four low-protein variants it is 3 of 4. Producer's own TSV shows the 44 vs 55. Suggest rewording.
* D2 MEDIUM: README sec. 10 and card pharmacology present butyrate as 4-29x more potent than PBA on class I, from one ChEMBL document; the second document (which is the source of the 162 uM sodium PBA row) lists butyrate at 300 uM on HDAC1, weaker than PBA. The argument "class-I HDAC inhibition (butyrate) did not reproduce the mutant gain" depends on butyrate being a stronger class-I inhibitor than PBA at the applied level; that premise is document-dependent and the contrary row was seen (162 is quoted) but not carried.
* D3 LOW-MEDIUM: README sec. 7 "P252A and P282A show the two readouts come apart in both directions" is not supported; only stable-but-dead (P282A) is shown.
* D4 LOW: neuron arm described as overexpression (lentivirus); authors state near-WT expression in the neuron system (Supp 3). Card should say "the authors report near-endogenous WT level; saturation or WT excess not excluded".
* D5 LOW: counter-evidence omits that glycerol rescued G443D (the partly trafficked variant) in vitro, while the card says the partly trafficked branch is "outside the class's reach" (true of 4-PBA, not of osmolyte-type chaperones). Also G443D evidence is cited to Shah; if the audit brief attributes it to 42157447 it is not there.
* D6 LOW: GAT-1 "butyrate failed to raise ratio" - false for S295L (significant) and weak for V511M; SAHA's "did nothing" is for uptake only.
* D7 LOW: cross-figure arithmetic (Fig 3a vs 6a vs 6b, different loading normalisers, n=3 and n=5) produces the 2-7% figure; it is labelled reader arithmetic/INFERENZA, but "about 2 to 7 percent" is quoted in the card without the +-10% axis caveat; fold values near 1.05 are within that error and are not separable from 1.0.
* D8 INFORMATIONAL: README opens with "No e-mail address ..." statement; no violation observed in the cycle files I read.
