# IT6 P10 (glial reactivity, NEED-NEUROINFLAMMATION-GLIA): independent verification

Verifier, 2026-10-29. Judged from the sources, not from the producer's summary. Non-canonical; nothing here is medical advice.
Scope: the cycle directory `disease-models/wwox/research/drug_repurposing/partial_benefit_it6_p10_20261029/`.
Marks: SUPPORTED / OVERSTATED / NOT SUPPORTED / UNVERIFIABLE.

## Verdict

The cycle's core is sound and mostly reproducible: the organoid panel reruns identically from the GEO counts, the prediction file
precedes the data, the rat, null-mouse, P47T and astrocyte-deletion statements match the sources, and the abstention and the
P5-P17 design are defensible. Defects are in the external-precedent section (one-sided and one trial claim not verifiable),
two catalogue counts on an inconsistent base, one omitted neuron-restricted datum, and a seizure-onset statement that is
incomplete. No defect reverses the abstention. No registration, contact, spend or access bypass was used.

## The abstention

SUPPORTED. No compound has a WWOX-system glial assertion, the sign conflicts across species, and the time order was never
measured. Nothing in the defects below changes it. Its stated reason (section 9 item 5, "null or negative for the outcome that
matters") should be reworded to "uncontrolled or uninformative for long-term outcome, with acute seizure-control reports", which
is accurate and keeps the same conclusion.

## The next experiment (P5 to P17, before and after first spike)

Discriminates as claimed. Prediction table: H-seizure (equal then above), H-delay (below then crossing), H-signal (above before
spike) give three different pre-spike signs; the organoid result and the neuron-only astrocyte-activation transcripts both fit
H-seizure or H-signal better than H-glia. H-glia is correctly said not to be separable without an astrocyte-specific arm.
Weaknesses to add (D7):
1. Histology is terminal, so "the same animals" can only mean same-section across readouts within a time point; each age is a
   different cohort, and "before or after first spike" is classified per animal only if recording covers the pup from before the
   first spike. Seizures are reported from P9 in neuron-restricted knockouts, so P5 and P8 are the only plausible pre-onset
   ages; recording from P5 in a pup of about 3 g at P14 is a feasibility risk the README does not state.
2. A cortical recording absent before P14 cannot show that no spike occurred earlier; "before first spike" needs a recording
   from the sampled age itself.
3. Add a genotype-by-region note: the rat deficit is cortical, the mouse excess hippocampal or unstated; the design does measure
   both, which is its strength.
