## Non-SUPPORTED verdicts — offending words and minimal repair

### M3 — OVERSHOOT
Offending words: "attribuisce il fenotipo umano più lieve alla proteina P47T \"still present…\", **contro il ratto lde severo che non ne ha**".
Two defects: (a) presence is presented as *the* attribution, where the source conjoins it with a second reason ("and the dehydrogenase/reductase domain of the mutant protein is presumably still functional"); (b) the mouse/rat contrast that sentence actually draws is about the dehydrogenase/reductase domain ("unlike the dehydrogenase/reductase domain of the WWOX mouse and rat models"), not about protein presence — the rat's protein absence is a separate, cited sentence.
Minimal repair: "la Discussione usa l'abbondanza nella direzione OPPOSTA: attribuisce la presentazione umana più lieve rispetto ai modelli murino e di ratto a una *partial loss of function*, per due ragioni congiunte — la proteina P47T è \"still present, at least in human skin fibroblasts\" e il dominio deidrogenasi/reduttasi è \"presumably still functional in Family 1 and partially functional in Family 2, unlike the dehydrogenase/reductase domain of the WWOX mouse and rat models\"; l'assenza di proteina nel ratto lde è affermata separatamente e su dato citato (\"Mutated WWOX is not detected in western blots of lde rat tissues\", Suzuki et al., 2007, 2009)."

### M4 — OVERSHOOT
Offending words: the bare quotation **"the mutation does not alter global protein levels"**, which drops the governing hedge.
Minimal repair: quote as the source states it — `"suggesting that the mutation does not alter global protein levels"` — or render it as: Western blot on P47T patient fibroblasts (passages 10/13/14 vs 4 controls) → "similar amounts of the mutant and wild-type WWOX protein" (Fig. 2B, "Based on visual inspection"), from which the authors infer, hedged, "suggesting that the mutation does not alter global protein levels".

### M6 — UNDERSHOOT

Minimal repair: "P47T abolisce il binding all'oligopeptide PPPY nel saggio in vitro — i costrutti mutanti \"failed to interact with the PPPY containing oligopeptide\" e il cambio P47→T è \"sufficient to abrogate the affinity of WWOX for PPXY motif containing interacting partners\" — un solo peptide saggiato (WBP1, SGSGGTPPPPYTVG); la generalizzazione ai motivi PPXY è della didascalia degli autori, non del dato."

## Notes on scope (no verdict change; both remain SUPPORTED)

1. **"milder" applies to "the human disease", not to SCAR12 alone.** The source sentence compares the human disease (Family 1 *and* Family 2, i.e. p.Pro47Thr and p.Gly372Arg together) with the mouse and rat models. Both propositions attach "milder" to the SCAR12 / P47T phenotype. This is narrower than the source, not broader, so nothing is overshot; a reader could however take the comparison as P47T-specific. Optional tightening: "che gli autori descrivono come parte di una presentazione umana *milder* rispetto ai modelli murino e di ratto".
2. **M11's "binding in vitro al peptide PPPY perduto" drops the construct context** that M9''' carries (GST-WW fusion peptides, not full-length WWOX, not a cellular assay). "in vitro" keeps it defensible; if M11 is ever quoted standalone, prefer M9'''s wording "in costrutti di fusione GST-WW".
3. **"età attuale 17-26 anni"** mirrors the source's "current age", i.e. current as of the 2014 publication. Correct as worded; downstream use should not read it as present-day age.
4. The dehydrogenase/reductase clause in M9''' correctly restricts "presumibilmente ancora funzionale" to Family 1; the source's "partially functional in Family 2" is outside the proposition's scope and is not misstated.
