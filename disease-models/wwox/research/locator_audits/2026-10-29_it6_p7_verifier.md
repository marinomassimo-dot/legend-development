## C. Defects

- **D1 (MEDIUM, S8 reading).** The rendered S8A shows the P3 arm (n=3) falling to about 67 percent at about day 24; the producer reports only the P5 loss (S8B, 75 percent from about day 35). The main text says "any time point between P0 and P5 was sufficient to fully rescue"; the survival panels show losses in the P3 and P5 arms. The two S8 panels are also not obviously consistent for P5 (S8A to P40 shows no drop; S8B shows the drop at about day 35): reading-level, unresolved. Add the P3 loss to section 2.2 and to the C04 wording; keep "P1-P5 (S8), no P0 arm, no P4 survival".
- **D2 (MEDIUM).** "Untreated nulls are dead by about P15" is one cohort's curve; the same paper reads untreated KO at P18 and P20 and states about three weeks. Rewrite to "by about day 15 in the S8A RI cohort; about three weeks in the text". The S8 histology panels give no read age in the legend.
- **D3 (MEDIUM, card wording).** Proposed C01 `endpoint_pursued`: "Early-window restoration (before the first recorded phenotype at about P14)". P14 is the start of ECoG recording, not a first phenotype; glucose is already low at P10 (Obeid Fig 3E, panel reading) and hypoglycaemia is stated "from the second week" (Repudi). Change to "before the start of ECoG recording at about P14; onset never located". Likewise 4.3 "seizure onset (after P12)" should read "seizures observed after P12 (lower bound)".
- **D4 (LOW).** Need `NEED-WWOX-GENE-ADDITION` proposal says "treatment P0-P5" for every endpoint; state "P0-P1 main cohorts, P1-P5 timing arms in S8" so the P0-P5 span is not read as a tested continuum.
- **D5 (LOW).** Steinberg 2021 onset is "UNMEASURED" for excitability only; week-6 KO phenotypes (DNA damage, astrocyte markers) exist, so say so. W-AAV n (3 versus 4) not reconciled.
- **D6 (LOW).** Producer lists Repudi Brain 2021 cell-type necessity as not reopened; the operator-supplied full text is on disk (`PMID33914858_Repudi2021_Brain_operator_supplied.*`), so the UNVERIFIED label could be closed.
- **D7 (LOW).** "Chronic NMDAR block ... risk for activity-dependent myelination" in 4.3 is a hypothesis, not a source statement; label it so. "LD median about day 48" and the Repudi survival-curve readings were not re-verified by me.
