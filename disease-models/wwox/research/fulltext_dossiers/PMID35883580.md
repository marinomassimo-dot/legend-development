## 1 · What the paper measures

Nothing new: a perspective by the laboratory that proposed the model, restating its tumour-line, 4T1 sphere, FRET and triple-transgenic AD mouse work; 86 references, dominated by that laboratory.

## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. Model: pY33-WWOX proapoptotic and anticancer; pS14-WWOX accumulates in cancer and AD lesions; Zfra4-10 (a seven-residue peptide) suppresses S14 phosphorylation, cancer growth and AD progression; hence "WWOX is not an authentic tumour suppressor".
2. The review says it is unknown whether the Y33 to S14 shift occurs inside the HYAL-2/WWOX/SMAD4 complex during disease, and that the proteomic partner profiles of the two phosphoforms remain to be established.
3. No pY33 or pS14 measurement in any WWOX loss-of-function tissue is reported; tumour lines, 4T1 spheres and 3xTg mice carry the model.
4. The newborn statement (migration disorder or heterotopia causing seizures) is cited to two mouse papers; the 13-patient cohort of this repository does not use the word heterotopia.
5. Negative: Zfra4-10 plus WWOX7-21 together nullify each other and enhance tumour growth in mice.
6. STRING gives five first-level partners (TP53, ERBB4, DVL2, FAM189B, WBP2) and no affinity or domain data. No metabolic content beyond four incidental words.
7. One reference is a retracted WWOX-direct paper (HGF/MET, image duplication), citation only.
