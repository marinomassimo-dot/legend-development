## 3 · What is measured — and at what level

1. **Aberrant or absent WWOX transcripts in 7/20 tumours (35%)** (L9). Sequenced: exon 6-8 loss in #CA2, #CA5, #CA21, #CA24; exon 7 loss in #CA2; partial exon 8-9 loss in #CA12 (L10). Two tumours (#CA3, #CA18) gave no WWOX product while CK19 amplified (L11).
2. **A novel somatic missense mutation, S329F**, exon 8, SDR domain, called homozygous, wild type in blood, absent from 30 germline controls (L24, L25, L26, L27). DATO for the sequence; no functional assay.
3. **Reduced Wwox by IHC in 8/20 tumours (40%)** — five +2, three +1; all normal mucosas +3 (L18).
4. **Concordance, uncounted and untested:** 5 of the 7 transcript-altered tumours have reduced protein (L21). Three tumours with reduced protein have no transcript abnormality (L30); two with a Δ6-8 transcript have full staining (L23, L32).
5. **No product of the aberrant transcripts on the western** (L15) — the observation behind the paper's translation claim.

| The gene *"contributes to the carcinogenesis processes in oral cancer"* (L35) | **IPOTESI stated as a result.** No functional experiment exists in this paper. |
| mRNA alteration *"correlated with"* reduced protein (L33) | **No test.** The word carries no statistic anywhere in the paper. |
| S329F is consequential | Not measured. A residue substitution in the SDR domain, with no stability, activity, localisation or binding assay. |

**One epistemic point belongs to the reader, not the authors.** The antiserum is raised against **residues 12-94** (L6) — the WW-domain region, which an exon 6-8-deleted transcript *retains*. A translated Δ6-8 product would therefore be expected to carry the epitope, so failure to see it is informative only if the blot's molecular-weight window covered the expected size. It does not visibly do so (§ 5.3): `INFERENZA`, and it is why *"not translated"* cannot be taken from this figure.

3. 🔴 **The western shows 10 of the 20 tumours, and the caption does not say "representative"** while the text says all 20 were blotted (L17). Within those ten: a **faint 46 kDa band in #CA3**, whose RT-PCR found no transcript at all, and in #CA5, whose only transcript is Δ6-8; β-actin is lighter in the #CA3 and #CA11 lanes, so part of that faintness is loading.

## 6 · Strength, and transferability to the reference genotype

- **What is solid:** aberrant WWOX transcripts, including clean Δ6-8, occur in a minority of oral squamous carcinomas, and a novel somatic SDR-domain missense variant exists in one of twenty. The sequencing is direct evidence and the somatic status of S329F is properly controlled against blood and 30 germline DNAs.
- **What is weak:** every frequency rests on n=20 with no test; the IHC is extent-only, two-reader without an agreement statistic; the 50% headline is a composite across three assay layers; and the translation claim exceeds its own figure.
- **A method caution this reading carries forward:** the transcripts come from **two nested rounds of 35 cycles** (L3). Deep nesting on small inputs is a condition in which template-switching artefacts arise, and the paper excludes none. That does not make the sequenced junctions false — they were sequenced — but it bears on how the *frequency* is read (`INFERENZA`).
- **Transferability: T3, nil toward WWOX-DEE.** Adult somatic cancer in smokers, no germline allele, no neural material, no developmental or seizure readout, no child. The Δ6-8 transcript recurs here as it does in ovary, which is a statement about somatic tissue, not about a germline exon-deletion allele.
