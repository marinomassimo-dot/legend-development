## 1 · What the paper did

Four glioblastoma cell lines (U87MG, T98G, U251MG, DBTRG-05MG) were stably transduced with a GIPZ
lentiviral WWOX construct or a Puro-Blank control, giving a `"WWOX"` and a `"Vec"` variant of each.
CAGE-Seq in **biological triplicate** per variant. The transcriptomes were then analysed by WGCNA,
limma-voom differential expression, protein–protein network construction and functional annotation,
and the resulting three-gene signature (WWOX + POLE4 + HSF2BP) was used to stratify TCGA-GBM
patients for survival, temozolomide IC50 and immune infiltration.

🔴 **Provenance limit that governs everything below.** The *phenotype* that makes this paper
relevant — that raising WWOX increased proliferation in DBTRG-05MG — was **measured in the group's
preceding paper** (Kałuzińska-Kołat et al. 2023, a different record, **not read here**). This
article measures transcriptomes and cites that phenotype in the past tense.

## 2 · What the paper states

**A — one of four glioblastoma lines responded to added WWOX in the *opposite* direction.**
> *"proliferative potential after WWOX overexpression was increased only in DBTRG-05MG"*

In the other three lines WWOX overexpression reduced viability and increased apoptosis, the
expected tumour-suppressor behaviour. DBTRG-05MG also carries roughly double the COSMIC mutation
burden of U251MG and U87MG.

**B — the patient-level claim, hedged to a co-occurrence.**
> *"high WWOX expression with simultaneously elevated expression of other genes (herein: POLE4 and
> HSF2BP ) did not bring prognostic benefits and may be related to the more cancer-promoting
> profile"*

About 6–7% of the TCGA-GBM cohort carry the WWOX↑ POLE4↑ HSF2BP↑ profile. Only ~30% of the cohort
had an adequate expression profile to be classified at all.

**C — the mechanism offered is accessibility, and it is explicitly speculative.** The authors
propose that WWOX protein may be unavailable to its partners in this group, by analogy to TMEM207
and VOPP1, and state that POLE4 and HSF2BP *"were not confirmed to be direct WWOX interactors"*.

## 3 · What the paper does **not** state

- **It does not claim that raising WWOX caused the worse outcome.** The authors refuse the reading:
  > *"the elaborated resemblance of findings from GBM cell lines using data from GBM patients is
  > imperfect, and it cannot be assumed that POLE4 and HSF2BP levels were elevated in patients with
  > unfavorable outcomes due to the higher levels of WWOX"*
- **It measures no proliferation, viability or apoptosis itself.** Those are the prior paper's.
- **It quantifies no WWOX protein level** — transduction efficiency was confirmed by western blot
  in the prior study, and no fold-overexpression is given here.
- Nothing about neurons, brain development, WWOX-DEE, any WWOX allele, or gene therapy.
- No in vivo arm.

## 4 · Caveats the authors give themselves

They devote a *"Study limitations"* section to it, opening with
> *"the conclusions drawn from in vitro experiments may not accurately reflect the conditions within
> an organism"*

and going on to name: conventional serum-grown lines rather than glioma stem cells; **U87MG is the
ATCC line of unknown patient origin, not the original Uppsala line**; focusing only on DBTRG-vs-rest
may have hidden other comparisons; TCGA-GBM lacks transcriptomics for all individuals; and the
immune-infiltration explanations *"may require further verification using dedicated and expensive
methods that are currently out of our reach"*.

## 5 · Caveats this reading adds

⚠️ **Not independent of PMID 34204789** in the same wave: same department, same senior authors,
overlapping first authors. The two must not be counted as corroborating sources.

⚠️ The log2FC threshold was **lowered mid-study** from the authors' own "typically reasonable"
|0.57| to |0.37| specifically to surface the DBTRG-distinguishing genes. They disclose this and
justify it; it is still a threshold chosen after seeing the data.

⚠️ Glioblastoma lines are heavily mutated cancer cells. Nothing about how a cancer line responds to
ectopic WWOX transfers to a post-mitotic neuron without an argument this paper does not make.

## 7 · Where this paper stops saying anything about WWOX

It does not stop — WWOX is the organising variable throughout (112 mentions). What it stops short
of is **causation**: every patient-level statement is an association between expression strata, and
the authors say so.

## 8 · Use

This is the batch's second independent demonstration — after the HD preprint and alongside the
HT29 result cited by PMID 33195192 — that the consequence of raising WWOX is **context-dependent**.
Taken alone it would be a cancer-biology curiosity. Taken as one of three, it is the reason
`CC-20261002-WWOX-DOSE-CEILING-01` is written as a recorded absence rather than as a dismissal.
