## What the paper did

Docking, vesicle sedimentation and SPR of the DVL2 PDZ domain with different sterols; sterol-synthesis inhibitors (AY9944, statins, U18666A) and cholesterol depletion in HEK293T, including an endogenously tagged DVL2-mEGFP line; FoxK2 co-IP and a PDZ inhibitor; TurboID-DVL2 proximity proteomics; human iPSC-derived neural stem cells (control and Smith-Lemli-Opitz) and cortex from a Dhcr7 mutant mouse.

## WWOX content (all of it)

- One discussion sentence: silencing of Wwox increases nuclear DVL2 in head and neck cancer, citing ref 79 (Celebi 2020, *J Cancer*, PMID 32368285). WWOX is not measured or manipulated anywhere.
- The TurboID dataset (Table S3, about 3,100 protein groups including background) contains no WWOX protein group. This is an absence of detection in HEK293T, not evidence that DVL2 and WWOX do not interact.

## The finding that bears on LEGEND's Wnt reasoning

- Sterol inhibition moved DVL2 from the membrane to the **nucleus** (HEK293T, NSCs, mouse cortex), and **in the same conditions a TCF/LEF reporter showed canonical Wnt signalling inhibited**, not increased (Figure S6E: Wnt-conditioned-medium induction about 125-fold in standard medium against about 12–20-fold under AY9944, simvastatin or U18666A; three biological replicates; no statistics printed on the panel). The authors read this as nuclear DVL acting independently of β-catenin.
- So in this system nuclear DVL2 accumulation and canonical Wnt hyperactivation come apart. That does not test WWOX, but it weakens any inference that runs "DVL2 in the nucleus, therefore Wnt/β-catenin is hyperactive".

## What the paper does NOT state

- Anything about WWOX beyond the one cited sentence.
- That the DVL2–p53 relation is co-localisation: it is a STRING-integrated network (Figure 7C) and a single representative co-IP (Figure 7D) whose input lane is itself stronger in the treated condition.

## What the panels add to the captions

**Figure 5E (nuclear/membrane/cytosolic DVL2, HEK293T).** Nuclear DVL2 rises above LPDS with
AY9944 (`**`) and U18666A (`****`) while membrane DVL2 falls (`**`, `*`, `**` across the inhibitor
lanes) and cytosolic DVL2 falls (`*`). Figure 5G/H: DVL2-GFP nuclear-to-total intensity rises
under AY9944 (`****`). So nuclear DVL2 accumulation in this system is produced by **depleting
cholesterol**, not by removing a tumour suppressor.

**Figure 6 (the route into the nucleus).** 6A/6B: the nuclear DVL2–FoxK2 co-IP signal rises under
AY9944 (`**`) while the cytosolic signal falls (`**`). 6E/6F: the PDZ-domain inhibitor NSC668036
abolishes the AY9944-driven nuclear accumulation (`****` against AY9944 alone, on both the GFP
intensity and the nuclear/total ratio). 6C maps the cholesterol-, FoxK2- and NSC668036-binding
residues onto overlapping faces of the same PDZ domain. The import route measured here is
**sterol-sensitive, PDZ-dependent and FoxK2-dependent**.

**Figure S6E (the canonical read-out, panel read, not caption).** TCF/LEF luciferase fold
induction *with* Wnt-conditioned medium, read off the plot: FBS ≈ 125, LPDS ≈ 67, LPDS+vehicle
≈ 55, **+AY9944 ≈ 12**, +Simvastatin ≈ 17, +U18666A ≈ 21; every no-CM bar sits at ≈ 5–7. The
condition that maximises nuclear DVL2 therefore carries the **lowest** canonical response — about
55/12 ≈ 4.6-fold below its own vehicle. 🔴 **The panel draws no significance marker anywhere**,
and the legend reports no test: N = 3 biological replicates with 3 technical replicates, mean ±
SEM. The direction is visible and consistent across three independent inhibitors; the *statistic*
is not reported.

**Figure S7 (the control that limits Figure 6).** FoxK2 abundance in nuclear and cytosolic
fractions does not change under the sterol treatments — *«N = 2 independent experiments»*. The
control that makes the FoxK2 result a trafficking result rather than an expression result rests on
two experiments.

## What this does and does not support

- It **supports**, with panels rather than with a citation, that nuclear DVL and canonical
  Wnt/β-catenin activation can **dissociate**: in this system DVL2 enters the nucleus while the
  TCF/LEF read-out goes down.
- It **does not** measure WWOX, and it does not contradict the head-and-neck cancer result it
  cites: a different trigger (sterol depletion versus WWOX silencing) may move DVL2 to the same
  place by a different route and with a different transcriptional consequence.
- It adds **no independent support** behind the three primaries that fix the direction in
  DL-MOL-003; it remains one citing sentence.
- Nothing here transfers to any WWOX allele, to neurons in development, or to a patient. Not
