## What the paper did
Narrative review of movement disorders in genetic DEE; a Web of Science search (July 2024) fed a VOSviewer keyword co-occurrence map, which ranked ten genes (GNAO1, SCN8A, CACNA1A, SCN1A, **WWOX**, CDKL5, SCN2A, KCNQ2, STXBP1, KCNA2). For these ten, movement-disorder proportions were pooled from articles "that provided specific descriptions of movement disorder types and their corresponding proportions" (Table 2). Table 1 lists genes per movement disorder from **OMIM**.
