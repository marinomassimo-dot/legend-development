## What it is

A laboratory study in two parts. In vitro: primary rat hippocampal astrocytes (P8, serum-free) exposed to 0.2 mM albumin for seven days. In vivo: seven days of intracerebroventricular infusion of 0.4 mM bovine albumin in adult male mice, with the ALK5 inhibitor IPW-5371 or astrocyte-selective TGF-beta receptor type II knockdown. WWOX: zero occurrences. Epilepsy is mentioned only as background for the albumin-TGF-beta pathway.

## WWOX content

None. **No WWOX-deficient evidence.** The paper is about ageing and Alzheimer-type neuroinflammation, not a developmental encephalopathy.

## What it reports (DATO)

- In cultured astrocytes albumin raised SA-beta-gal (about 3 to about 35 percent), p16-positive cells (about 10 to about 30 percent) and mRNA of p16, p21, BCL family members and six cytokine or chemokine genes; the ALK5 inhibitor SJN2511 brought each back towards control.
- In mice, ICV albumin lowered Lamin B1 in GFAP-positive astrocytes (about 4.9 to about 2.0) but not in other cells; IPW-5371 prevented it (five mice per group).
- With astrocytic receptor knockdown, albumin raised pSMAD2, p-p38 and p16 in hippocampus two months after infusion in heterozygous mice; fl/fl mice did not show the rise.

## What it does NOT support (the negative is the product)

1. **Nothing about WWOX, a genetic DEE, or a Wwox-null brain.** Whether such a brain leaks albumin or carries senescent astrocytes is unmeasured here and in this repository.
2. **Brain exposure to albumin is not intravenous albumin or any plasma product.** The model delivers a commercial bovine albumin preparation into the ventricle at a serum-like concentration for a week; leakage, uptake and clearance are not modelled, and nothing in the paper concerns infused albumin, fresh frozen plasma or exchange. INFERENZA about relevance; DATO about content.
3. **"Prevented" rests on non-significance against the control only.** The Methods describe Dunnett comparisons to control, so albumin versus albumin plus inhibitor is never tested; Figure 1f IL-6 plotted for the inhibitor arm sits at about 1.8 against 1.0 while the text says no difference for all SASP genes. Three independent experiments per cell-culture panel.
4. **No inhibitor-alone arm and no fl/fl aCSF arm.** The effect of the inhibitor, or of knockdown, on baseline senescence markers is unknown; in Figure 3b fl/fl pSMAD2 sits below the aCSF control, so "protection" includes a floor shift.
5. **Senescence is inferred from a marker panel, in mice from one marker at one week (Lamin B1) and from bulk western blots at two months (p16).** No functional senescence assay, no cell-type attribution for the western signal, no proliferation read-out, and about 40 percent recombination efficiency (cited).
6. **Pooled correlations (r 0.54 and 0.60) mix the three groups,** so group differences generate the association; no within-group correlation is shown.
7. **Single sex, single age, one species per arm, culture from P8 rat pups; bovine albumin in mice;** the feedback loop in the Discussion is proposed, not tested.
8. **Data on request only.**

## Design and limits

Experimental laboratory study; small groups (3 experiments; 5 mice; 8-10 mice); no blinding statement found; commercial bovine albumin; supraphysiological or serum-like exposures in a defined medium.
