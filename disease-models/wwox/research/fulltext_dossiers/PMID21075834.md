## What the paper measures

Whole-animal Drosophila with Wwox loss (two alleles) or ubiquitous over-expression: 2D-DIGE (16 protein spots common to both mutants, 16 under over-expression), embryo microarray, seven qPCR-confirmed transcripts; then a viability screen of 10 of the 27 candidates by RNAi with Wwox up or down; a Wwox by Sod double-mutant lifespan test; FACS of a ROS dye; WWOX and IDH1 mRNA across 15 human cancer lines. Wwox loss alone has no phenotype in the fly.

## What the pass added

1. The Idh genetic interaction, the one result the text calls clear, is replicate-inconsistent in Table S2: enhancement by Wwox RNAi significant in 2 of 5 replicates, suppression by Wwox over-expression in 3 of 5; the Idh-RNAi-alone viability itself ranges from about 7 to 27 percent across replicates (Figure 3B); the RNAi line has a reported off-target. The text's "trend across all five" is a trend, not five hits. Other candidates have single-replicate P below 0.05 (CG11089, CG30152, CG14526) that the text does not weigh, with no correction across lines, replicates and two directions.
2. The "significant decrease of Idh protein" in Wwox mutants is a 12 to 19 percent spot-intensity change (Table 1).
3. The WWOX and IDH1 correlation (P 2.5E-12) uses four confluence time points for each of 15 lines (60 points), so the observations are not independent; it is across cancer lines, not tissues of a loss-of-function model.
4. The ROS curves (Figure 5B) have no error bars or replicate count; the loss and gain curves separate at different times; the dye is a non-specific oxidation indicator.
5. One reference is a retracted paper (FHIT and ferredoxin reductase, 2008), cited in the Discussion for the FHIT parallel only.

## Does not support

A mammalian or human WWOX-DEE metabolic mechanism; the direction of a WWOX effect on ROS in neurons or in loss-of-function mammals; a TCA-cycle defect in any WWOX-null tissue (nothing in this paper measures TCA flux, lactate, ATP, oxygen consumption or glucose). The paper's own aerobic-metabolism claim rests on candidate transcript and protein shifts of small size in whole flies. Transfer to the reference genotype: none; a fly null has no phenotype, so the system gap is the finding.

Revival triggers: a mitochondrial respiration or flux measurement in a Wwox-null mammalian tissue; independent replication of the Idh interaction with a second RNAi line without off-target. No commit candidate.
