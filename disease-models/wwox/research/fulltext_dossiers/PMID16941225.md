## 2 · Design

- **Western blot (Fig 1):** one commercial membrane of human tissue lysates (INSTA-Blot), 20 µg/lane,
  five lanes, anti-WWOX 1:2000, actin reprobe after stripping.
- **IHC:** anti-WWOX polyclonal raised against a GST fusion of the **WW domains**; ACIS automated
  intensity "as previously reported" plus a four-level manual grade; OSE as **positive** control.
- **Tissues:** commercial adult human tissues. TMAs from Ambion, Innogenex and Zymed give duplicate
  representation of 30 organs; a Chemicon Neuro TMA gives **two cores per brain region**; whole sections
  of breast, ovary, testis and prostate (Methods) and of skin (Results).
- **Nothing is quantified at cell-type level in the article:** no per-core values, no n, no statistics,
  no inter-observer agreement.

## 4 · What the paper does NOT support — the negatives are the product

Each negative carries its premise and its revival trigger.

1. **Antibody specificity is not established here.** The paper delegates it to Nunez 2005a/b (Peo1
   negative extract). No IHC specificity control is described or shown: no no-primary, preimmune serum,
   peptide block, or WWOX-deficient tissue. The immunogen is the WW-domain region.
   `PREMISE: INFERENZA` — the delegated control is adequate — resting on **unread** 15692750 and
   15982416. `REVIVAL_TRIGGER`: a complete read of those two papers showing an IHC-level control, or any
   later validation of this reagent on WWOX-null human or animal tissue. Queued under FT-079.
2. **No cell-type call is quantified.** The ACIS values are not reported; grades are categorical. The
   printed scale leaves 59, 68–69, 79–80 and exactly 80 unassigned (Table 1 footnote, locator 40).
3. **No negative is illustrated.** None of the 18 IHC panels shows CNS, is devoted to or cited for a Table 2 organ-level negative, or shows a negative
   control (locator 43). Every organ-level negative and every CNS cell-type call is text/table-only.
4. **The CNS regional negatives are not reconciled with Table 1.** Results calls parietal, limbic and temporal
   cortex and substantia nigra "negative" (9). Table 1 grades parietal/temporal/SN **+/−** (13) and
   places **limbic cortex in the +++ neuronal row** (12). The paper does not resolve this, and on two
   cores per region with no panel this reading cannot resolve it either. **Left open.**
   `REVIVAL_TRIGGER`: a regional human-brain protein map (IHC with a validated antibody, or proteomics)
   that includes limbic cortex.
5. **Oligodendrocyte and myelin negativity is a weak negative.** It rests on morphology-only
   identification, two cores, no panel, no oligodendrocyte marker, and an antibody whose sensitivity in
   FFPE white matter is untested. `PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK` — IHC absence equals protein absence.
   `REVIVAL_TRIGGER`: WWOX protein or transcript detected in human oligodendrocyte-lineage cells by a
   co-labelled or single-cell method.
6. **Skeletal muscle is NOT established either way.** The authors call it inconclusive (18); muscle is in
   neither table. Neither "muscle negative" nor "muscle positive" may be carried out of this paper.
7. **Heart is not examined by IHC.** It appears only as a faint band in Fig 1 with an overloaded actin
   lane; it is in neither table and the text never mentions it.
8. **Lymphoid negativity contradicts prior Northern RNA data, and the paper does not reconcile it** (19).
   Epitope masking, fixation and RNA–protein discordance are all untested.
9. **The steroid-metabolism frame is the authors' IPOTESI, untested here** (42). The paper measures no
   enzyme activity, steroid or receptor; the SDR assignment is by sequence motif. The Discussion's
   'highest levels' list includes liver, kidney, salivary gland and neural cells — with stomach and thyroid in it though graded ++ — which the frame does not
   explain. **Test of the ACK-disclosed prior:** hormonal organs are indeed among the Discussion's
   "highest" list (41), but that list is a qualitative ranking of categorical grades on duplicate
   cores. The claim *"highest in hormonally regulated tissues"* is supported as the authors' ranking,
   not as a measured ordering, and not as exclusive: liver, kidney, stomach and adrenal share it.
10. **No developmental, age, sex or donor axis.** Commercial adult tissue with no donor metadata (5); the
    paper says nothing about fetal or postnatal brain.
11. **Isoforms.** "Only the 46 kDa product" is an assertion with a citation (2), and the authors write that they have *no evidence* for other stable forms — an absence of evidence, not an asserted absence. The blot's two markers
    (48, 29 kDa) cannot resolve closely spaced products.

## 6 · The compartment answer, at the level the panels support

- **Carries the protein, with a panel behind it:** secretory, ductal and basal epithelial cells of
  reproductive, endocrine and exocrine organs; hepatocytes; gastric glands; proximal/distal tubular
  epithelium; Leydig cells; adrenal cortical cells; autonomic ganglion **neurons**.
- **Carries the protein, text/table only:** CNS pyramidal and other large neurons, astrocytes (soma and
  processes), ependymal cells, cerebellar granule neurons, molecular layer and Purkinje cells, pituitary
  stalk glia, enteroendocrine and Paneth cells, megakaryocytes (one sample), plasma cells (scattered).
- **Does not carry detectable protein, with a panel behind it:** Schwann cells and nerve processes (one
  field); glomerular tuft; goblet-cell and mucous-acinar vacuoles; fallopian-tube and endometrial stroma.
- **Does not carry detectable protein, text/table only:** oligodendrocytes, myelinated structures,
  lymphoid cells, alveolar epithelium, endothelium and all vessel walls, smooth muscle (except faint
  gallbladder), adipocytes, connective tissue, syncytiotrophoblast, exocervical squamous epithelium.
- **Not established:** skeletal muscle (inconclusive); heart by IHC (not examined); limbic, parietal and
  temporal cortex and substantia nigra (internally contradictory; '+/−' is undefined by the paper's own scale).
- **Whole-brain protein:** present by one immunoblot lane (Fig 1).

## 7 · Transfer verdict toward the reference genotype (WWOX-DEE genotype class)

**T2–T3, baseline only.** The paper describes where the protein sits in **adult** human tissue under one
antibody. It says nothing about any allele, dosage, loss of function, development or disease.

What transfers:
- **INFERENZA:** WWOX protein is present in adult human brain lysate (DATO, one blot). Neurons, and on
  text/table evidence astrocytes and ependymal cells, are candidate compartments for loss of function
  in the brain.

What does not transfer:
- the absence of protein from oligodendrocytes or myelin as a basis for excluding a cell-autonomous
  glial mechanism (§4.5, weak negative);
- any regional brain selectivity (§4.4);
- any developmental timing;
- any hormonal organising principle (§4.9).
