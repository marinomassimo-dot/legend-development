## 1 · What the study did

Retrospective single-centre cohort of neonates with seizure onset before one month, no provoked
cause, normal brain MRI, drug-resistant epilepsy (failure of two appropriate ASMs) and > 12 months'
follow-up. From 616 neonates with seizures, 531 provoked cases and 29 self-limited epilepsies were
excluded, leaving **56**. All patients had a gene panel (Appendix S1) plus array CGH; three had
exome and/or genome sequencing. Outcomes: intellectual disability, autonomy in four domains,
seizure freedom (Kaplan–Meier), death; logistic regression for predictors of poor outcome.

## 2 · What it measured and states (cohort level)

- Genetic diagnosis in **35/56 (62.5 %)**; KCNQ2 10, STXBP1 5, KCNT1 3; "other anomalies" 15 named
  single genes plus 2 chromosomal anomalies.
- ID in 51/56; moderate outcome 11, poor outcome (severe NDD / PIMD) 42; 3 lost to follow-up.
- **Six deaths (11 %)**, all before five years, median four months; causes listed without genes.
- Seizure-free at follow-up 8/56; abnormal neonatal neurological examination was the only
  independent predictor of poor outcome (adjusted OR 7.6, 95 % CI 1.17–49.8).
- Consanguinity 11/56 (19 %), cohort-level only.

## 6 · What the paper does NOT say

Nothing on WWOX genotype, mechanism, allele function, prognosis, mortality or treatment. The paper
stops saying anything about WWOX after the legend count and one Discussion mention. It is a
denominator datum only: **2/56 neonatal-onset pharmacoresistant MRI-negative epilepsies, 2/35 of
the genetically solved**, on a panel that includes WWOX. Transfer limit: the denominator applies only to
this selected population (neonatal onset, normal MRI, drug-resistant, one centre); whether the
MRI-negative criterion under- or over-samples WWOX-DEE is not addressed by this paper.
