## What the paper did

Male Lewis rats immunised with optic nerve homogenate antigen (ONA) plus adjuvant and pertussis toxin, against adjuvant-only controls; read-outs at 28 days. ERG (n = 8 eyes/group), whole-retina RT-qPCR (n = 3–4/group), immunohistology (n = 7/group), and Agilent whole-genome microarrays on laser-microdissected inner retina (ganglion cell, inner plexiform and inner nuclear layers; n = 3 eyes/group).

## WWOX content (all of it)

- **Microarray (Table 4, Figure 7H, Table S1):** probe A_64_P028011 (Wwox, NM_001106188) listed as down-regulated, p = 0.030, tabulated fold change 0.864. Table S1 shows the per-animal values are normalised **log-scale** intensities (immunised 6.80 / 7.36 / 6.77; control 8.35 / 7.56 / 8.30). The 0.864 is the ratio of the two group means of these log values; the linear difference is about 0.47-fold (reader's arithmetic from the deposited cells — INFERENZA on the scale, the numbers are the authors'). The FDR-adjusted p is 0.187 and the Bonferroni p is 1; **no probe of the 24 survives FDR** (the minimum p_fdr in the sheet is 0.187). In Figure 7H one of the three pairs nearly coincides.
- **qPCR (Results 3.5, Figure 9C):** whole-retina Wwox 0.24-fold, p = 0.002 (REST randomisation test), n = 3–4 per group. The panel shows the immunised box wholly below the control line (median about 0.22; lower edge near 0).
- **Discussion:** cites Wwox-deficient mice (optic nerve atrophy; PMID 32000863) and a human/rat cortical-development paper (PMID 32581702) and concludes "suggesting a regulatory role of Wwox in the retina". This is the authors' speculation; nothing in the study tests it.

The paper says nothing else about WWOX: no protein, no cell-type localisation, no function, no time course, no manipulation of Wwox.

## What the paper does NOT state

- That Wwox loss causes or contributes to ganglion cell loss (direction of causality is not addressed).
- Which retinal cell type carries the change.
- Anything about WWOX genotype; the model is immune-mediated, not genetic.

## Caveats the authors give

Retinal function was measured only by full-field ERG; RGC-specific function (pattern ERG) is proposed for future work.

## Transfer limit

Rat, acquired immune-mediated retinal injury, mRNA only, three to four animals per group. It shows that retinal Wwox mRNA can fall in a non-genetic CNS injury; it says nothing about a biallelic loss-of-function genotype class, about protein, or about a therapeutic threshold.
