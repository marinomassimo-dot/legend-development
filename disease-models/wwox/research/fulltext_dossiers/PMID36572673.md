## Figure and supplementary audit

Effective PPI was measured before rendering. Main article figures were available at about 300 ppi.
Supplement ceilings were: MOESM1 page 1, 1594.4 ppi, pages 2–3 vector; MOESM2 pages 1–6,
120.0/508.0/563.7/167.7/321.6/314.5 ppi; MOESM3 vector/text; and MOESM7 pages 1–3,

supplementary pages were inspected as images.

Figure 2B exposes a genuine text–panel contradiction. Its caption says `n = 3` for KWC, KC and
WT, whereas both the main image and the raw-blot supplement visibly contain two lanes per group.
The manifest therefore carries the required paired text and panel locators. Figure 3 exposes only
three WT, three KC and five KWC biological columns, with error bars between technical replicates.
Figure 4's two WT, three KC and five KWC lanes do match the caption.

Figure 5 prints 0/14 tumours in KC versus 16/64 in KWC, but the generic statistics statement names
an unpaired t-test and does not identify a binary-outcome or time-to-event test. Its BMP2 comparison
uses tumour versus spleen from the same mouse, not tumour versus normal pancreas. Figure 6 shows a
borderline survival association (HR 0.65, CI 0.43–0.99, P=0.043) without cohort/cutoff details in the
panel. The CRISPR “rescue” expresses WWOX at 5.9–6.7 times WT and drives colony formation below WT,
so it is an on-target supraphysiological rescue, not restoration to a physiological state.

The XLSX supplements were inventoried structurally: the count matrix has 14,748 rows and 11 samples;
the DEG table has 612 data rows; and the pathway table has 24 data rows. They support auditability but
were not treated as prose.

## Scientific verdict

Pancreatic-acinar Wwox loss alone produces no detected pancreatic phenotype in this experiment.
When combined with KrasG12D, however, it accelerates ADM/PanIN formation and yields PDAC in 16/64
KWC mice versus 0/14 KC mice. Isolated KWC acinar cells transdifferentiate faster ex vivo, providing
evidence for a cell-autonomous component, while macrophage infiltration and the intact in-vivo
microenvironment also support non-autonomous contributions. The correct conclusion is conditional
cooperation with oncogenic KRAS, not autonomous sufficiency of Wwox loss.

The molecular data associate Wwox loss with increased TGFβ/BMP/SMAD, ductal/EMT and stemness
programs. Co-IP supports association of WWOX with SMAD3 and BMP2. No TGFβ or BMP inhibitor is tested,
so pathway necessity and the proposed therapeutic route remain unestablished. PDX restoration and
knockout experiments support tumour-suppressive activity, bounded by line-specific effects and the
supraphysiological rescue noted above.

## Tensions, transfer and hidden gold

- Heterozygous and homozygous KWC mice are collapsed because lesions and tumour rates were described
  as similar; this prevents a clean dosage estimate. Loss of WWOX in heterozygous tumours suggests,
  but does not prospectively prove, selection for a second hit.
- RNA-seq biological n is 3 WT, 3 KC and 5 KWC; reported qPCR error bars are technical rather than
  biological replication.
- The incidence test is not adequately specified, and no sample-size calculation or randomisation
  is reported. Genotype-based allocation is not blinded outcome assessment.
- **Disease transfer:** low to WOREE; this is pancreas cancer. **Genotype transfer:** low; conditional
  somatic deletion plus KRAS differs from germline reference genotypes. **Intervention transfer:**
  low; inhibitors are proposed but not administered.

## Process report

- **Ambiguity:** “cell autonomous” could have been inferred from the conditional allele alone; it is
  supported more directly by the isolated-acinar experiment, while the in-vivo phenotype still
  includes stromal and immune effects.
- **Tool refusals:** PMC `/bin/` and the HTTPS rewrite of the OA-package route returned 404. The
  Europe PMC supplementary-files endpoint succeeded and supplied the persistent archive.
- **Deduced rather than read:** inadequacy of a generic t-test for the printed binary incidence,
  absence of a pancreas-matched BMP2 control, and the supraphysiological nature of rescue are audit
  inferences from methods and pixels.
- **Could not do:** reproduce RNA-seq processing, establish pathway necessity, recover an uncollapsed
  Wwox dosage effect, or infer therapeutic efficacy from the proposed inhibitors.
