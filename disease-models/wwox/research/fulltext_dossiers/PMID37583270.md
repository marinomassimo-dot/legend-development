## What the paper did

Cross-sectional multicentre study (six centres) of children with genetically confirmed IESS **tested between January
2018 and June 2022**, assessed in person (or by telephone and chart for those who could not attend or had died).
Structural-genetic and structural-metabolic causes were excluded. Only P/LP variants were admitted. Response to
treatment = clinical cessation of spasms for at least four weeks; no electrographic criterion (the authors' own
stated limitation).

## What the paper states and what it does NOT

- States WWOX (n = 4) among the dominant single-gene causes, with "characteristically" early-infantile onset.
- Does **not** give a denominator of all IESS assessed or of all tested, so no frequency or yield can be derived.
- Does **not** report electrographic resolution, developmental outcome scales, RNA, protein or functional data for any WWOX allele.
- Does **not** measure the consequence of `c.517-3C>A` or of the exon 6-8 deletion (the deletion is in-frame by arithmetic: c.517–c.1056 = 540 nt).

## Caveats the authors give

Clinical-only response definition; retrospective component; non-funded; denominator of IESS not collected.
