## What the paper did
Illumina PsychArray genotyping of 128 ASD probands, 238 parents and 365 controls without psychiatric history. Rare CNVs were called with three algorithms and a subset validated by qPCR; there is a CNV burden analysis, a rare-SNV transmission test, and exome sequencing of one multiplex family.

## Transfer limit
- The case CNV is **not a null-class allele of canonical WWOX**: an intronic loss for the canonical transcript, functional effect unmeasured.
- The control CNV is the one **exon-level, null-class heterozygote** in this wave, but it is unvalidated and the person was not neurologically assessed. It is not a demonstrated negative for haploinsufficiency.
- Neither CNV is a missense allele, and a CNV deletion is not a missense.
