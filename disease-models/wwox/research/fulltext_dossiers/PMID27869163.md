## 2 · What this paper establishes

**DATO — Wwox loss increases clonogenic survival of DSB-inducing agents, in four independent systems
with rescue arms.** Knockout MEFs above 7.7 Gy (Fig 1a); MCF10A shWWOX clones above 7.7 Gy and, far
more strongly, under bleomycin — 25-fold at 1–1.5 µg/ml and 100-fold at 3 µg/ml (Fig 1b, 1d); the
isogenic doxycycline-inducible MDA-MB-231 pair (Fig 1c); and re-expression in two knockout-derived
clones, KO3A and KO5F, which *lowers* survival (Fig 1e–f). The direction is measured, not assumed, and
it is measured in both directions — loss raises survival, restoration lowers it.

**DATO — Wwox expression shifts usage of all four DSB repair pathways, each in an integrated reporter
with a rescue arm.** HDR up on silencing (U87 DR-GFP, ~20% → ~50% GFP-positive, rescued to ~19.7% by
silencing-resistant myc-Wwox) and down on expression (HeLa DR-GFP, ~2.8% → ~2.0%); C-NHEJ down ~50% on
silencing; Alt-NHEJ down from ~12.1% to ~5.0% on silencing with partial rescue to ~9.6%; SSA down from
~3.1% to ~0.88% on expression. Summarised by the authors in Fig 3f, and the panel agrees with the text.
**This is the paper's strongest result, and it is a direct measurement of pathway usage** — not an
inference from a correlate.

**DATO — the radioresistance is HDR-dependent.** Rad51 depletion collapses the knockout MEFs' survival
advantage and does so *only* in the knockouts (Fig 3g). This is the causal link from the reporter
result to the phenotype, and it is the best-controlled experiment in the paper.

**DATO — Wwox and Brca1 co-precipitate, and the interaction requires the WW1 domain.** GST-Wwox
full-length and the WW1/WW2 fragment pull down HA-Brca1; a WW1 point mutant (W44F/P47A) does not; a WW2
mutant does; the SDR fragment does not (Fig 5a). Reciprocal co-IP confirms the complex (Fig 5b). The
interaction is reported as **not** damage-dependent, explicitly contrasted with the Wwox–ATM
interaction of ref 16.

**DATO — the functional consequence tracks the WW1 domain, not the SDR domain.** A WW1-mutant Wwox
fails to sensitise 231 cells to IR (curves superimposed, Fig 5f) while an SDR catalytic mutant (Y293F)
sensitises them normally (Fig 5g), with induction demonstrated for both lines (Suppl Fig 6b). This is
the cleanest domain attribution in the paper.

**DATO — mild genome instability accompanies Wwox loss in MEFs.** Near-tetraploid karyotypes with
shortened chromosome 7 and 4 homologues in both knockout lines (Suppl Fig 1), and three deletions
shared between MEFs from **two independent knockout mouse models** — 1 H6 (*Ints7*), 4 B3 (*Klf4*) and
8 C2 — which is the one genuinely model-independent observation in the paper (Table 1a/b).

**DATO, and it is a negative the paper needs — Wwox loss does not raise spontaneous damage.** No
difference untreated, so the repair phenotype is induction-specific (Suppl Fig 2). See §3 for what
qualifies it.

## 3 · What this paper does NOT support — the negative is the product

**The end-resection mechanism is inferred from a correlate, and the paper says so.** There is **no
resection assay anywhere**: no ssDNA quantification, no BrdU/native-ssDNA measurement, no SMART assay,
no resection-tract sequencing. The step from "more RPA32 and Rad51 foci at 1 h" to "Wwox suppresses end
resection" is carried by the authors' own verb — *«we hypothesize»* — and the model figure marks it as
`putative` in its legend. **Figure 7c draws a question mark beside the very arrow by which Wwox blocks
Brca1–Rad50/MRN.** No Rad50-competition experiment exists in the paper: no Rad50 co-IP, no competition
titration, no MRN nuclease assay. So *pathway choice* is demonstrated; *the mechanism of pathway
choice* is a hypothesis with a correlate attached.

**The foci correlate is weaker than the sentence it supports, in three ways visible only in the panels.**
- **RPA32** differs at 1 h (~1.35 vs ~3.53) and **inverts at 3 h** (~3.68 with Wwox vs ~2.73 without),
  unmarked and with wide bars, before both collapse by 6 h. A one-timepoint difference, not sustained
  suppression.
- **Rad51** already differs **at 0 h, before irradiation** (~4.07 vs ~7.35, unmarked). A baseline
  difference is not attributable to IR-induced resection. The Rad51 graph also stops at 3 h while the
  other five proteins run to 6 h.
- **Brca1 foci at 1 h** are ~2.67 with Wwox against ~4.27 without, and **their standard-error bars do
  not overlap**, yet the pair carries no significance mark while three other pairs in the same figure
  do. The sentence this supports — that Wwox does not interfere with Brca1 recruitment — is the
  load-bearing negative of the whole model, and from the panel one cannot tell whether it was tested
  and found non-significant or reported without a mark. *Unmarked is not "not significant", and it is
  not "not tested" either; it is unresolved.*

**The domain mapping does not support the motif the authors name.** They conclude Wwox binds *«near the
981PPLF984 motif»* because mutants lacking 305–1292 lost binding — but that range is covered by **two**
mutants, ΔM1 (305–770) and ΔM2 (775–1292), and **residue 981 lies only in ΔM2**. ΔM1's loss of binding
is therefore unexplained by the motif hypothesis, and the paper does not address it. Two further
confounds sit in the same panel: the detection antibody is directed at Brca1 400–1100, which the legend
itself admits gives *«weaker detection of ΔM1 and ΔM2»* — precisely the two negatives — and **the IgG
control lane is positive for Wwox in the ΔC lane** (Fig 5e), so one of the three mutants scored as
retaining binding is the one whose negative control fails. No bait-recovery quantification is shown.
What the data support is: the interaction needs Wwox's WW1 domain (well shown) and maps somewhere in
Brca1's central region (shown with the above caveats); *near residue 981* is not established.

**The cell-cycle alternative is excluded in one system and not in the other.** In the isogenic 231 pair
the distributions are nearly identical with and without Wwox — a good control. In the MEFs the four
lines **do** differ, and not only by genotype: WT4 ~90% G1 vs WT7 ~80%, KO3 carrying ~10% sub-G1, KO5
at ~48% G1 with a much larger S/G2-M fraction (Fig 4c). Since HDR is S/G2-restricted, a larger S/G2
fraction is a live alternative explanation for more HDR in KO5. The authors dismiss it via Suppl Fig 4,
whose checkpoint evidence is **raw single-film immunoblots with no densitometry anywhere**, graded in
Fig 4 as absent/medium/high shading, and taken **after 6 Gy** while every endpoint they are used to
exclude is at 10 Gy (foci, cycle) or 7.7–10.2 Gy (survival). A qualitative grading of one film per
protein at a different dose is what stands behind *«neither ... showed correlation»*.

**The untreated negative is non-significance with high variance, not equivalence — and one assay is
missing.** In Suppl Fig 2b the mean comet tail moment is ~60% **higher** on Wwox silencing (~21.8 vs
~13.6) with wide overlapping bars and no mark; only the MEF panel shows truly superimposed means. And
the two wild-type lines and two knockout lines were **«averaged together for analysis»** — pooling
exactly the line-to-line heterogeneity Fig 4c reveals. Further, the main text says the untreated
comparison included *«immunofluorescence assays for DSB markers, 53BP1 and γH2AX»*, but the cited
Supplementary Figure 2 contains **three panels and no 53BP1 panel at all** (γH2AX foci, comet ×2). The
untreated 53BP1 data are asserted and absent.

**The survival claims are narrower than stated, panel by panel.**
- Fig 1a: the *«10-fold better than wild-type cell lines WT4 and WT7»* holds against WT7 at 10.2 Gy,
  but **at 10.2 Gy WT4 sits on the knockout cluster** (~0.6%) while WT7 has collapsed (~0.0035%). At
  the top dose one of the two wild-type lines is indistinguishable from both knockouts.
- Fig 1c: the entire 231 effect rests on **one dose**, 5.1 Gy, whose error bar spans ~0.001–0.12%
  survival — over two decades.
- Fig 1f: *«at 7.7 Gy and above»* cannot be evaluated at 10.2 Gy in the KO5F clone, which has **no
  Dox+ point there**.
- Fig 3i: *«at 25 µm and higher concentrations vs WT4 MEF»* — **WT4 has no data above 25 µM**, so above
  that dose the comparison is not drawn at all.
- Fig 3c: the only NHEJ measurement normalises siCtrl to exactly 1.0 with no error bar, so the P<0.05
  compares a value with variance against a constant, and its Wwox knockdown blot is the weakest in the
  figure — a faint smear rather than a resolved band.

**The in vivo arm has an irreconcilable n.** Methods: *«A total of 10 athymic nude female mice»*, with
*«Positive control mice (n=2)»* and *«experimental mice (n=4 for each group)»*. Results: *«7/8 mice
injected with 231/Wwox-neg cells formed tumors, whereas 6/8 ... 231/Wwox-pos»* — 16 irradiated animals.
The two cannot both be true, and which n underlies the P<0.05 on tumour latency (17 vs 28 days) cannot
be determined from the paper. In Fig 2b the unirradiated control groups carry **no test bracket**, so
*«not different»* for the controls is an unmarked comparison, with n=2 per group on the Methods' count.

**The human arm is the weakest evidence in the paper and is not the authors' data.** It is a re-analysis
of the public REMBRANDT database through a third-party Kaplan–Meier application, with a two-fold change
used to call *«downregulated»*. The unselected cohort is a **null** (P=0.105, 42 down vs 497 normal);
the significant result is confined to the radiation-treated subset (P=0.007) with **11 down-regulated
patients**. Crucially, **the grouping is not reproducible from the deposit**: both spreadsheets have
`GENE_EXP = YES` for every row and carry **no WWOX expression value, no fold change and no group
label**, so the 42/497 and 11/144 splits cannot be re-derived. The row counts do not match the
Kaplan–Meier totals either (541 vs 539; 157 vs 155), unexplained, and the larger file includes 28
`NON_TUMOR` rows and 51 rows with no `EVENT_OS`. "Treated with radiation" is recorded under *prior*
therapy and pools photon, 3D-conformal, IMRT, radiosurgery and brachytherapy.
*One reconciliation did pass:* the radiation modality counts (95 photon + 39 3D-conformal + 12 IMRT +
7 radiosurgery + 4 brachytherapy = 157) agree exactly between the two files, so the subset relation
between them is internally consistent.

**Nothing here measures mutagenesis.** The paper's central interpretive move — that Wwox-deficient
cells carry a *higher mutational burden* because HDR is enhanced and possibly mis-timed into G1 — is
argued, not measured: no mutation assay, no sequencing, no G1-specific HDR measurement. The authors
state the inference openly (*«we believe»*, *«we propose»*, *«it is difficult at this point to assign»*).
The karyotype and CNV data are consistent with it and were obtained at **p18**, while the functional
assays use early-passage / ~p10 cells and the p21/p53/p16 blots p10–12 — so the instability
characterisation and the phenotype measurements are not from the same passage.

## 7 · Transfer verdict toward the reference genotype

**Weak to absent, and saying so precisely is the deliverable.**

Nothing in this paper concerns the nervous system, a developmental stage, or a WWOX-DEE allele. The
systems are two breast-derived human lines (MDA-MB-231, MCF10A), three reporter host lines (U87, HeLa,
HEK293/H1299), and mouse embryonic fibroblasts from a **whole-body null**; the interventions are
irradiation, bleomycin and cisplatin; the endpoints are clonogenic survival, reporter repair
efficiency, foci counts and tumour latency. There is no neuron, no glia, no brain, no seizure, no
behaviour, no post-natal timepoint. The one human dataset is adult brain **cancer**, which shares an
organ with the disease and nothing else — a coincidence of tissue, not of biology.

What could transfer, typed honestly:
- **`INFERENZA` (mechanistic, gene-level, not genotype-level):** WWOX has a constitutive,
  WW1-dependent protein–protein interaction with BRCA1 that biases DSB repair-pathway usage. The
  interaction and the pathway shift are measured; the genotype relevance is not, because a *missense*
  or *splice* WWOX-DEE allele may retain or abolish WW1 binding and **no WWOX-DEE allele is tested
  here**. `PREMISE: DATO` for the interaction; `PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK` for any assumption
  that a DEE allele behaves like the null used here.
- **`ESPANSIONE` — genome instability as a candidate contributor to a neurodevelopmental phenotype.**
  Post-mitotic neurons do not use HDR appreciably, and the entire effect here is measured in dividing
  cells; a transfer would have to survive that objection, and this paper offers nothing to make it
  survive. To be tested, never assumed.
- **Explicitly NOT transferable:** the therapeutic proposition. The authors propose HDR inhibitors
  with radiation for WWOX-deficient **tumours**. That is an oncology lever in dividing malignant
  cells, and carrying it toward a developmental encephalopathy would be a textbook context collapse.
  `BLOCCO 1` is not engaged because no clinical proposition is made toward the reference genotype here.

**Revival triggers** for the near-absent transfer: a WWOX-DEE patient allele tested for BRCA1-WW1
binding; any DSB-repair or genome-instability measurement in WWOX-deficient neurons, NPCs or brain
organoids; or evidence that replication/repair stress contributes to a WWOX neurodevelopmental
phenotype in vivo.
