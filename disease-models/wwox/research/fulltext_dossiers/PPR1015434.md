## 1 · What the preprint did

*Drosophila*, with *Wwox* moved **in both directions in the same model** — the only source in this
group that does so:

| Direction | Tool | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| Down | two independent UAS-RNAi constructs (TRiP, KK) | *elav* (pan-neuronal), *repo* (pan-glial), *timeless* (clock) |
| Up | **CRISPRa**: UAS-dCas9-VPR + tandem gRNAs at the *Wwox* transcription start site — **≈2-fold mRNA**, measured by qPCR (Fig. S1) | *elav* |
| Up | UAS-cDNA of *Drosophila* or **human** *WWOX* | *elav*, *repo* |

Background: neuronal expression of Arctic-mutant human Aβ42. Readouts: lifespan, startle-induced
climbing (RING), sleep and circadian behaviour (17 metrics, FDR-corrected), Aβ42 by
immunocytochemistry and by electrochemiluminescence on soluble and insoluble fractions, bulk
head RNA-seq (N = 4 × 10 heads), HILIC metabolomics (N = 5 × 10 heads), an ATP assay, an
Xbp1-splicing reporter, a *sima*:GFP (HIF1α homologue) reporter, and Atf4 immunostaining.

## 2 · What it measured

**Knockdown, no amyloid.** Neuronal: median lifespan 57 → 42 and 40 days (p_adj < 0.0001);
climbing unaffected (p = 0.4458). Glial: 74 → 70 days (p = 0.0003). Both increase sleep and sleep
pressure without disturbing circadian rhythmicity.

**Knockdown with Aβ42.** Median lifespan 41 → 26 and 24 days, with a **significant interaction in
Cox proportional-hazards modelling (p < 0.0001)** — synergy, not additivity. Climbing reduced.
Total Aβ42 up (p = 0.033), led by the **soluble** fraction (p = 0.0024); insoluble only trending
(p = 0.0602); aggregate number and size unchanged. Glial knockdown with neuronal Aβ42: lifespan
22 vs 24 days (p = 0.0095), locomotion unaffected — hence **neuronal, not glial, Wwox is the
relevant pool** for amyloid toxicity.

**Mechanism.** Ldh transcript +1.33 log2 over Aβ42 alone, L-lactate +1.30 log2 (p_adj = 0.0174) as
the single metabolite of the interaction; **Ldh knockdown rescues the climbing deficit** at 14, 21
and 28 days. Atf4 protein up (p = 0.0022); *sima*:GFP unchanged (p = 0.5185); Xbp1 splicing
unchanged — so the route is PERK–Atf4, **not** HIF1α. ATP unchanged.

**Upregulation.** In a control background, CRISPRa changes nothing (lifespan p = 0.1420,
locomotion p = 0.8770). With Aβ42: median lifespan **29 → 47.5 days** (p < 0.0001), climbing
rescued at days 14 and 21, **plaque size (p = 0.0003) and number (p = 0.0071) down**, soluble
(p = 0.0357) and insoluble (p = 0.0409) Aβ42 down. Human *WWOX* cDNA reproduces the rescue
(41 → 51 days, p = 0.0021). Glial overexpression does nothing.

**And the rescue does not run back down the same road.** Transcriptionally it reverses the Aβ42
profile (40 shared genes, r = −0.8341); metabolically its only interaction metabolite is
**L-methionine, down** — lactate is *not* restored.

## 3 · What it states

Reduced WWOX raises Aβ42 load and shortens life through PERK–Atf4-driven Ldh and lactate;
increasing neuronal *Wwox* ≈2-fold reduces amyloid load and rescues lifespan and locomotion
through a different, methionine-linked route.

## 4 · What it does NOT state

- No myelin, oligodendrocyte or white-matter endpoint of any kind. *Drosophila* has no CNS myelin.
- "AD risk gene" is a GWAS/eQTL attribution about an intergenic SNP between *WWOX* and *MAF*, not
  a statement about WWOX-DEE.
- RNAi knockdown is not one null allele, and no heterozygous or hemizygous *Wwox* fly is studied.
- Nothing is measured in a mammal, and no WWOX protein level is reported anywhere — the CRISPRa
  arm is quantified as **mRNA only**.
- The rescue is prophylactic/concurrent; no arm starts the upregulation after pathology is
  established.

## 5 · Caveats

- The up-arm is the interesting one for a therapeutic programme precisely because it is
  **endogenous upregulation at a modest, measured 2-fold**, which is the most transferable feature
  of the paper. But the only readout of that dose is transcript.
- The two mechanisms (lactate down, methionine down) are asymmetric; the authors say so.
- Ldh is bidirectionally constrained in this literature — the preprint itself notes that Ldh
  *knockdown* is also detrimental in fly Aβ42 models — so "lower lactate" is not a safe target.
- The paper's own measured contrast with the WWOX/HIF1A literature is a **negative**: *sima* did
  not move. That is directly relevant to the HIF1A axis this group's other paper rests on.
- Single laboratory (Cardiff), no conflicts declared, data deposited (ArrayExpress E-MTAB-14948 /
  E-MTAB-14949, MetaboLights MTBLS12344) — deposition is a strength worth recording against the
  myelin preprint's "available upon publication".
