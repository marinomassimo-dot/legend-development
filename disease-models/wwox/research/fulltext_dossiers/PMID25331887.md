## 3 · What the supplement establishes

**3.1 · The WWOX–ATM interaction is a WW1 function, and the SDR domain is not involved.**
*This is the finding with the longest reach for the disease model, and it was only ever in the supplement.*

- **Fig. S4A (left):** p-ATM is recovered with **GST-WW1+2** and is essentially absent from the
  **GST-SDR** lanes.
- **Fig. S4A (right):** across `GST-WW1_WT`, `GST-WW1_MT` (WFPA), `GST-WW2_WT`, `GST-WW2_MT`, a
  p-ATM band appears in the **`GST-WW1_WT` + NCS lane only**.
- **Fig. S4B:** on the ATM side the interaction maps to the **middle region, ~1530–2100 aa**.

> **Transfer, typed `INFERENZA`.** If severity in WWOX-DEE tracks the **SDR** domain
> (`CLAIM 030`), then the **DNA-damage arm of WWOX biology is dissociable from the alleles that
> dominate WWOX-DEE severity** — it is a WW1 function, and Q230P is an SDR variant.
>
> 🔴 **And the clean negative that follows.** **P47T sits in WW1 and abolishes PPxY binding
> (`CLAIM 007`), yet gives the MILD phenotype** — SCAR12, patients alive at 17–26 y. So **loss of
> the WWOX–ATM interaction is NOT sufficient for severe neurological disease.** The negative is the
> product here, not the residue.

**3.2 · K274 is at most a major ubiquitin acceptor, not the sole one.** In the **Fig. S6C**
pull-down the anti-HA ladder **persists on GST-K274R** — reduced against wild type, **not absent**.
This is a **fourth instance**, now in the supplement, of the construction the prior reading already
recorded three times in this paper's main figures: *a quantitative reduction reported as an absolute
negative.*

**3.3 · The K63-linkage-specific evidence is the weakest of the four antibodies shown.** In
**Fig. S6B**, the HA, FK1 (poly-ub) and FK2 (poly+mono-ub) panels carry strong ladders in the
GST-WWOX lanes while the **K-63-Ub panel is faint across all lanes**. `PREMISE: DATO` — this does
not overturn the K63 finding, which rests on the main figures and the K63-only ubiquitin constructs;
it bounds how much the *linkage-specific antibody* evidence carries on its own.

**3.4 · The half-life claim rests on one chase whose own control series is non-monotonic.**
**Fig. S7B** prints densitometry relative to HSP90:

| | 0 h | 2 h | 4 h | 6 h |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GST-WWOX, −NCS | 1.0 | 1.0 | **1.4** | 0.8 |
| GST-WWOX, +NCS | — | 1.0 | 0.9 | 0.7 |
| GST-K274R, −NCS | 1.0 | 0.7 | 0.8 | 0.4 |
| GST-K274R, +NCS | — | 1.1 | 0.5 | 0.4 |

No error bars, no replicate count, no statistics. 🔴 **Under cycloheximide, synthesis is blocked, so
wild type rising to 1.4 at 4 h cannot be real protein accumulation.** The internal noise is
therefore at least ±40% — comparable to the wild-type-versus-mutant difference being claimed. **The
direction (K274R less stable) is consistent across both series; the magnitude is not supported by
this panel alone.**

**3.5 · Two captions flatten a difference their own panels show.**
- **Fig. S3B** is captioned as impaired H2AX *phosphorylation*, but the panel also shows foci
  **persisting at 24 h and 48 h** in WWOX-depleted cells while controls have cleared — a
  **resolution** defect, not only a signalling one.
- **Fig. S5** is captioned as impaired activation and recruitment of MDC1 **and** p-CHK2, but
  **MDC1 foci are retained** (reduced) while **p-CHK2 is essentially abolished** — a **selective
  downstream** defect, which is sharper and more informative than the caption states.

**3.6 · A confound removed.** **Fig. S3C** shows the U2OS-DR-GFP clones used for the HR assay
expressing WWOX, WFPA and K274R at **comparable levels**, so the main Fig 3E result is **not**
confounded by absent K274R protein — even though main Fig 6A shows a far fainter K274R input in a
*different* experiment. **The stability deficit is not uniform across this paper's experiments and
the two panels must not be pooled.**

**3.7 · The induction premise is unreplicated.** **Fig. S1** prints fold-change rows (HEK293
1.0→3.5; LM7 1.0→2.2) with **no error bars, no replicates and no statistics** anywhere.
