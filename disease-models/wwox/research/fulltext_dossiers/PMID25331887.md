## 3 · What the supplement establishes — as audited

> All ten supplementary locators were put through a **blind locator audit**. It returned
> **SUPPORTED on four** and **OVERSHOOT on six**, struck **five clauses as not in this source**, and
> found **two of my attestations factually wrong**. What follows is the audited form; §7 records
> what changed and the auditor's own limits, which bind every number below.

**3.1 · p-ATM co-precipitation is assigned to WW1 and not to SDR — as an isolated-domain
sufficiency test.** *This is the finding I was most attracted to, and the audit narrowed it twice.*

- **The negatives are genuine, and that is the strong part.** In Fig. S4A the SDR lanes carry no
  p-ATM **while GST-SDR is itself recovered**, so it is a real negative and not a failed pull-down.
  In the eight-lane panel, p-ATM is detectable in **lane 3 (WW1_WT + NCS) only**, while lysate
  p-ATM is positive in **all four NCS lanes** and pull-down GST in **all eight** — so the WW1-WFPA
  and WW2 negatives are not explained by absent substrate or absent bait.
- 🔴 **Correction 1 — "abolished" is wrong.** WFPA reduces p-ATM recovery **below detection on a
  single blot**. That is not abolition.
- 🔴 **Correction 2 — this is a *sufficiency* test, not a mapping.** Isolated GST-tagged domains
  over-expressed in HEK293 show that WW1 **suffices**; they do **not** show that WW1 is **required**
  within full-length WWOX. The draft treated the two as the same thing.
- 🔴 **Struck as not in this source:** "the domain assignment the disease model needs, and it is
  only in the supplement." Unverifiable from the SI pages.

> 🔴 **And the inference that collapses.** The draft argued that because **P47T** sits in WW1 and
> abolishes PPxY binding yet gives the *mild* phenotype, loss of the WWOX–ATM interaction is not
> sufficient for severe disease. **That argument does not survive.** The auditor records that this
> is a **lysate pull-down that does not establish a direct or PPxY-mediated contact**, so nothing
> here licenses equating WW1's PPxY function with its ATM binding. The P47T step is withdrawn. What
> remains is narrower and still useful: *on the ATM axis specifically, an isolated WW1 domain is
> sufficient and the isolated SDR domain is not.*

**3.2 · On the ATM side the Middle fragment (1,530–2,100 aa) is the strongest recovery**, with a
**faint but real** additional signal in the **kinase-domain** fragment that the draft omitted.
🔴 **The draft's claim of comparable Coomassie loading was wrong**: GST-WWOX lanes run **1.4–1.6×
darker** than GST lanes throughout. This panel therefore **orders** the fragments; it does not
quantify them.

**3.3 · K274 is required for the damage-induced *increment*, not for the bulk of ubiquitination.**
The audit sharpened this into a better result than the draft had. K274R retains substantial
high-molecular-weight ladders, and with K63-only ubiquitin the **mutant equals or exceeds wild type
untreated (161 vs 156)**. What the mutant loses is the **NCS-induced rise**: wild type 111→137 and
156→169, mutant 116→104 and 161→156.

**3.4 · 🔴 The draft's K63 scope claim FAILS, and it failed in my favour, which is why it matters.**
The S6B K-63-Ub antibody panel is indeed uninformative — worse than I attested, since it does not
discriminate GST-WWOX from GST-WFPA at all (53, 70, 68, 60, 67, 65). But **two things follow that I
got wrong**: its faintness *relative to* the HA/FK1/FK2 panels is **not admissible** evidence (a
different antibody, blot, exposure and background), and **it is not the supplement's only K63
evidence** — **Fig. S6C is strong**, with K63-only lanes at 156–169 against K48-only at 86–105.
**The supplement's K63 evidence is strong; only this one antibody panel is uninformative.**

**3.5 · The stability panel: direction supported, magnitude not — and the word "half-life" is not in
the source.** Fig. S7B is a **single** cycloheximide chase whose **wild-type control rises to 1.4 at
4 h** under blocked synthesis, an excursion (0.4) **equal to** the 6 h wild-type-versus-mutant gap
(0.8 vs 0.4). 🔴 **Correction:** the **mutant series is also non-monotonic** (1.0→1.1); the draft
said only the control was. 🔴 **Struck:** "half-life" — the words half-life and decay appear nowhere
in Fig. S7 or its legend, which says only *"levels relative to HSP90"*.

**3.6 · Two captions flatten differences — but so did my draft, in the opposite direction.**
- **Fig. S3B:** impaired induction at 15 min (342 → 30 px) **plus delayed resolution at 24 h**
  (1 → 76 px). 🔴 **My attestation was factually wrong at 48 h: neither genotype shows detectable
  foci there (0 px in both).** Only the 24 h persistence is real.
- **Fig. S5:** the defect is **graded, not binary**. MDC1 falls 245 → 34 px (~86% loss) but remains
  about **twice its own untreated field**, so discrete foci **still form**; p-CHK2 falls 705 → 7 px,
  to its untreated level, and is **undetectable**. 🔴 **"Retained / abolished" flattens the result in
  the opposite direction from the caption.**

**3.7 · The induction premise is weaker than "unreplicated".** Fig. S1 is one blot per cell line,
with no error bar, n or significance mark — supported as a **concordant trend in two cell lines**,
not as an effect size. 🔴 **And the defect the draft missed:** in panel A the **HSP90 loading control
declines ~43%** (32.4 → 18.6) while WWOX rises, so **the printed fold changes are not supported as
loading-normalised values.**

**3.8 · One confound genuinely removed.** Fig. S3C shows the DR-GFP clones expressing WWOX, WFPA and
K274R comparably (80.7 / 66.2 / 74.6; EV at background), so the HR assay is not confounded by absent
mutant protein. Caveats: GAPDH is **lower** in the EV lane, and no densitometry is printed.
