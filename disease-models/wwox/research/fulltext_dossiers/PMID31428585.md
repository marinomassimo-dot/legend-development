## 2 · 🔴 The finding — a clinical-treatment claim, in an editorial, on the intervention this repository has rejected

> *"Preliminary findings from clinical treatment shows that despite mutations, **forced transcription
> of WWOX gene** appears to be a feasible approach to **lessen the symptoms of seizure** in patients
> with neurodegeneration **(personal communications with Dr. D. S. Lin at the Taipei Medical
> University)**"*

Everything about its evidentiary status is in that sentence: **unpublished**, **no genotype**, **no
n**, **no design**, **no seizure outcome measure**, **no transcript or protein readout**, and a
population — *"patients with neurodegeneration"* — that is **not stated to be WWOX-DEE newborns**.
Two lines earlier the same paragraph names the unmet need it claims to have moved: *"an effective
drug to lessen seizure in the newborn patients would greatly benefit them"*.

**It is recorded as a lead with named provenance and never as evidence.**

### 🔴 And it is not the only one — the second claim is weaker, and the audit found it

Both blind auditors independently reported that **no locator of mine covered** the sentence that
follows it:

> *"Furthermore, blocking the downregulation of WWOX in the middle aged individuals **would likely**
> to prevent the development of AD"*

**No attribution of any kind** — no reference, not even a personal communication. So the document
carries **two** forward-looking clinical claims without evidence, adjacent, and **the weaker of the
two is the unsourced one**. The finding of this reading is not "an editorial contains an unsupported
clinical claim"; it is that it contains two, and that I had only seen the one with a source attached
— the more visible one. **The audit's most valuable output here was an omission, not an error.**

### Why `DIS-003` does not move

`DIS-003` — *«Boosting WWOX expression» → rejection that holds* — rests on Johannsen 2018: **normal
transcript, protein absent** in Q230P-homozygous fibroblasts, so the bottleneck is downstream of
transcription. Its `REVIVAL_TRIGGER` is specific: *"if it emerged that a compound-heterozygous
transcript behaves differently from the Johannsen homozygote."*

**This statement does not touch that trigger.** It reports no transcript measurement, no genotype and
no comparison. And its own phrase *"despite mutations"* is precisely what the Johannsen datum makes
doubtful: more transcript does not help an allele whose transcript is already normal.

**Disposition: `DIS-003` UNCHANGED.** What would convert this lead into evidence is named instead: a
published report from that group giving genotype, n, a seizure outcome measure, and the transcript /
protein readout.

**Corpus crossquery:** `forced transcription` and `Taipei Medical` return **zero** occurrences
anywhere in the disease model outside this reading. The statement is **new to the corpus** — and new
