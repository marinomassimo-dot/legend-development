## 1 · What the study did

One child with a syndromic phenotype (growth restriction, dysmorphism, cardiac and skeletal
anomalies, speech and motor delay) and copy-neutral loss of heterozygosity over the whole of
chromosome 16 on microarray. Microsatellites (seven markers) established uniparental **isodisomy**;
FISH on blood and fibroblasts excluded trisomy-16 mosaicism; exome and trio genome sequencing
looked for unmasked recessive variants; MS-MLPA excluded the known imprinting disorders on other
chromosomes; trio nanopore sequencing measured methylation at the 16p13.3 imprinted ZNF597 locus.

## 2 · What it measured and states

- Copy-neutral LOH `16p13.3q24.3`, end to end (Figure 2 panel inspected: two LOH segments covering
  both arms, broken only at the centromere) — text confirmed by panel.
- **No unmasked pathogenic recessive variant on chromosome 16** (exome; trio genome added none).
  Three heterozygous pathogenic variants elsewhere (COG2, P3H2, CNGB3), none explanatory.
- Opposite-direction methylation changes at the two ZNF597 DMRs, read by the authors as an
  imprinting effect of the isodisomy and proposed as the cause of the phenotype (an INFERENZA of
  the authors, n = 1).

## 3 · The WWOX content — a mechanism class, by citation only

WWOX appears three times: once in the Introduction and once in the Discussion, each time in a list
of recessive genes (GPT2, FA2H, ABCA3, FANCA, SPG35, PMM2, ALG1, GAN, WWOX) whose disease has been
**unmasked by chromosome-16 uniparental disomy**, and once as reference 16 — PMID 38407561, the
primary report of a homozygous WWOX canonical-donor allele made homozygous this way.

**The index patient has no causal WWOX variant.** Her WWOX locus is necessarily homozygous (the
whole chromosome is isodisomic) and exome sequencing reported no pathogenic recessive variant on
chromosome 16; she is not a WWOX case and adds nothing to any WWOX count. The authors also exclude
recessive-allele cases from their Table 1 comparison, so no WWOX case appears in the table.

Mechanism, at class level, as the paper states it: isodisomy usually arises by monosomy rescue
(heterodisomy by trisomy rescue); in isodisomy every chromosome-16 locus is identical on both
homologues, so **a single carrier's recessive allele can become homozygous without the other
parent carrying it**. The paper adds no WWOX data to this; it only names WWOX as an instance.
