## What it is

A descriptive study: C3 transcript in resected human hippocampus (mTLE with sclerosis, n = 64) against post-mortem controls (n = 13), a public kainic acid mouse microarray, and a floxed C3-IRES-tdTomato reporter line in pilocarpine status epilepticus tracked by immunostaining from 72 h to 6 weeks, with NeuN, GFAP and Iba1 and cell-source counts. WWOX: zero occurrences.

## WWOX content

None. **No WWOX-deficient evidence.** Acquired mesial temporal epilepsy in adults and wild-type reporter mice.

## What it measures (DATO)

Human C3 modestly higher (adjusted p = 0.029); reporter signal absent at 24 h, weak in the stratum lacunosum-moleculare at 72 h, spreading to CA1 by 1-2 weeks and prominent at 3-6 weeks; all reporter-positive cells are GFAP astrocytes; CA1 neuron density lower and gliosis higher after status epilepticus.

## What it does NOT support (the negative is the product)

1. **Expression is not activation.** The reporter marks C3 gene expression (an IRES-tdTomato after the stop codon); no cleavage products (C3a, C3b/iC3b), no C1q, no synaptic tagging or microglial engulfment is measured, although the title says "complement C3 activation".
2. **Pseudoreplication against its own methods.** Methods say animal means (N = 3) were used for statistics, but Figure 4 plots nine image points per group with p at or below 0.0001, and the time course has N = 3, no error bars and no statistics (stated in the legend). The seizure comparison between knock-in and wild type rests on N = 3 and N = 4 mice and cannot support "no effect on epileptogenesis".
3. **The cell-source figure compares against a group with nothing to count.** Controls have no hippocampal tdTomato cells, so 100 versus 0 percent co-expression (three points each, p below 0.0001) is a presence/absence statement; microglial C3 is not excluded outside the stratum lacunosum-moleculare because analysis was confined to that region.
4. **Human and mouse legs do not meet.** Human controls are post-mortem tissue against surgical resections (a handling and agonal-state confound the paper does not discuss), the effect is modest and bulk (box plots overlap), the mouse sequencing is kainic acid and the histology pilocarpine, and no cell-type or spatial validation exists in human tissue.
5. **No functional or seizure readout.** Longitudinal EEG was not done, so C3 dynamics are not tied to seizures; the paper's mechanistic claims on inhibitory-synapse pruning are cited, not shown.
6. **Not WWOX, not genetic DEE, not a plasma product.** A locally produced astrocytic factor in an acquired model; nothing about circulating complement or plasma-based interventions.

## Design and limits

N = 3 per time point, one marker, one model, no stats on the main time course, bulk sequencing, reporter line characterised with underpowered seizure data.
