## 1 · What the paper did

Raw exome/genome data from 57 earlier tests re-analysed and 13 new trio exomes/genomes generated; variants
prioritised by rarity, consequence and in-silico tools and classified by ACMG/AMP; STRING enrichment over the
diagnostic gene set. Cases are grouped by reverse phenotyping into five disorder groups.

## 4 · Caveats the authors give themselves

Heterogeneous sequencing platforms; retrospective phenotype collection from hospital records; possible selection
bias toward genetically suspected cases; small cohort; functional validation of clinically relevant variants
still needed.

## 5 · Allele class and transfer limit

A homozygous missense at residue 239. It is not Q230P and the two are not interchangeable because both are
missense; the paper measures nothing about the protein. **No transfer.** What it adds is a referral route: a
WWOX homozygote reached through a cerebral-palsy-labelled stream, kept under the CP label by the authors' stated
policy (they follow the consensus that a P/LP finding does not by itself reclassify CP as a mimic).
