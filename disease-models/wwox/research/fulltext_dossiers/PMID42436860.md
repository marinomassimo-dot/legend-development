## Premise test
The selection note called this "a CNS AAV cassette". It is not: the target is peripheral sensory neurons (sciatic nerve injection, DRG, painful neuropathy). The AAV genome analysis is explicitly framed as peripheral-nerve-restricted. Transfer to a CNS restoration spec is route-different.

## What the paper did
- Vector: AAV6.2FF capsid carrying a concatemeric NaV-inhibitory peptide (CoNaViPA1) or a control peptide (CoNP); GFP removed from the cassette.
- Rat tibial-nerve-injury model, treatment 2 weeks after injury, 8 animals per group, males and females, 3 months of behaviour, then conditioned-place-preference test.
- Dose: 4 x 10^11 GC in 20 microlitres per rat, subepineural sciatic injection. Chosen from a pilot of two rats per dose (1, 2, 4 x 10^11 GC) read only as sciatic-nerve genome copy at 3 weeks; data "not shown". The text dose is therefore a per-animal figure, 2 x 10^13 GC per ml (recomputed: 4e11 / 0.020 ml). No per-kilogram figure is stated.
- qPCR of AAV genome (n = 6 stated), limit of quantification 500 copies in 2 ng DNA.
- Pathology/IHC at 12-14 weeks: H&E, GFAP, Iba1, MBP, ATF3, CD6/CD8, caspase-3.
- Human sensory neurons in culture: lentiviral (not AAV) delivery of the peptide; sodium-current and firing readouts.

## What it measured
- Sciatic nerve genome copy about 3.4 x 10^5 (plotted mean); DRG L3-L6, lumbar cord and paw skin about 2-5 x 10^3 (plotted means); liver, kidney, heart, blood, CSF below the 500 cutoff (Fig. 5 panel read; several liver/blood/CSF points sit just under 500). Recomputed: DRG/nerve about 0.6-1.5 percent.
- Pathology: no microscopic pathology versus naive; mild GFAP and Iba1 increase; ATF3 comparable across groups; no CD6/CD8 cells; caspase-3 negative (ATF3/caspase panels are in Fig. S2, not read).

## What the authors state as limits
Capsid-specific humoral immunity not examined; the reduced-immunogenicity reading "needs to be tested experimentally"; retrograde transport to spinal motor neurons and Schwann-cell transduction possible; marginal off-target genome signal in several animals; dose could be reduced with higher-titre vector.

## What it does NOT state / internal inconsistencies
- WWOX named zero times; nothing about any WWOX allele.
- No toxicology beyond IHC in one species, no clinical chemistry, no neutralising-antibody titre, no dose-response for safety (the pilot was efficacy-by-copy-number only, n = 2 per dose, not shown).
- Fig. 5 legend says injection "into RL4 and RL5 DRG"; Fig. 2 legend says "DRG delivery"; Methods and Results say sciatic nerve. Methods list only adult male rats while Results report female rats. Fig. 5 plots about eight points for sciatic nerve against n = 6 in text and legend. Figure 6 images only naive versus treated TNI; the DRG glial change cannot be separated from nerve-injury effect on those panels (the control-vector comparison is in the unread supplement).

## Transfer limit (exact)
Rat, peripheral nerve route, AAV6.2FF, peptide payload, 3-month horizon. It supplies a dose-and-readout grammar for peripheral AAV, not a CNS dose ceiling and not any WWOX datum. Not medical advice.
