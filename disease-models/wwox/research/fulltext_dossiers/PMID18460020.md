## 1 · What the paper is, and whose paper it is

PANC-1 (Wwox-negative) pancreatic carcinoma cells receive wild-type *WWOX* by adenovirus
(Ad-WWOX) or plasmid; the readouts are subG₁ DNA content, caspase-3 activity, growth, colony
formation and subcutaneous xenograft growth. In parallel, 32 resected pancreatic carcinomas plus
adjacent PanIN are stained for Wwox and Smad4.

🔴 **The lot is the "Aqeilan free-full-text sweep" and this is not an Aqeilan-lab paper.** Aqeilan
is **fourth of six**; the corresponding author is Semba and the senior author Yokozaki, Division of
Pathology, Kobe University. Re-derived by PubMed ESearch **2026-09-09** — closing the stale-count
debt this actor's own wave-1 self-evaluation raised against itself:

## 4 · What the paper does **not** support — the negative is the product

### 4.1 · "Caspase-dependent apoptosis" is decoupled at the lowest effective dose
🔴 At **Ad-WWOX MOI 10**, caspase-3 substrate signal is ~0.36 ΔOD₄₀₅ — **indistinguishable from all
four control conditions** and unasterisked — while Fig 1a shows MOI 10 already producing **~15%
subG₁ against ~4%** at MOI 0. **subG₁ accumulation and caspase-3 activation dissociate at MOI 10.**
Caspase dependence is demonstrated only at MOI 25 and 50. The figure title asserts it for the whole
series. *(Also: the Ac-DEVD-CHO inhibitor bar at MOI 50 sits **above** every control substrate bar,
so the inhibitor does not restore baseline.)*

### 4.2 · The panel said to *confirm* MOI-dependence has no MOI axis
The text: *"Activation of caspase-3 was also MOI-dependent, which was confirmed by detection of
reduced levels of procaspase-3 … and procaspase-8 expression (Fig. 1c)."* That blot has **three
lanes — No infection, Ad-GFP, Ad-WWOX — and no MOI axis at all.** It cannot confirm MOI-dependence
of anything.

### 4.3 · The in vivo result is engraftment prevention, not therapy — and has an unexcluded alternative
Cells were infected **ex vivo** and injected **24 h later** (Methods). Fig 2A shows those same
infected cells collapsing below their **starting** number by 72–96 h. So the inoculum was of cells
already committed to death, and **the xenograft experiment cannot distinguish "WWOX suppresses
tumorigenicity" from "the injected cells were already dying."** `INFERENZA`.
**`PREMISE: DATO`** — Fig 2A's own curve. **`REVIVAL_TRIGGER`:** an experiment injecting
established tumours and then delivering Ad-WWOX, or a viability/apoptosis assay on the inoculum at
the moment of injection.
*Also:* the volume formula omits the conventional 0.5 coefficient, so absolute mm³ are ~2× the usual
convention (between-arm comparison unaffected).

### 4.4 · The domain-function conclusion is confounded by unequal expression
🔴 **Fig 3b carries no loading control at all** — a single anti-Myc panel, no β-actin, though the
Methods list β-actin and Fig 1 uses it. The four constructs are **visibly unequally expressed**:
full-length pWWOX and pWWOX-Y33R broad and dark; both SDR-deletion constructs lower *and* fainter;
pΔWWOX-Y33R faintest. Since Fig 3d compares colony suppression across exactly these four,
**an expression difference is an unexcluded alternative explanation.** `INFERENZA`.
**`REVIVAL_TRIGGER`:** any β-actin- or total-protein-normalised re-blot of the four constructs.

### 4.5 · Four load-bearing results are "data not shown"
| What | Why it is load-bearing |
|---|---|
| PK-1 (Wwox-neg) and **PK-9 (Wwox-pos)** | the paper's **only** Wwox-status-dependent control; the negative is disposed of in a parenthesis |
| **22% LOH · 20% promoter · 83% exon-1 methylation** | the entire epigenetic argument of the Discussion |
| Ad-GFP subG₁ *"not more than 5%"* | the **control** for Fig 1a, declared in the caption and **absent from the panel** |
| **Smad4 mRNA unchanged** | the sole basis for *"post-transcriptional regulation"* — the paper's principal mechanistic conclusion |

🔴 The last is the one that matters most: **the mechanism rests entirely on an unshown negative.**
`IPOTESI`, not `DATO`. **`PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK`** — that unchanged mRNA with raised
protein implies post-transcriptional control; it does not exclude altered translation *initiation*,
which is transcript-level regulation of a different kind. **`REVIVAL_TRIGGER`:** publication of the
qPCR, or any independent Smad4 mRNA measurement under WWOX restoration.

### 4.6 · Table 1 — the one significant association, and what it survives
The counts are **internally sound** (every row sums to 32; columns to 25 and 7). Two things are not:

🔴 **The declared test is invalid for every row.** The Methods declare **χ²**; all seven 2×2
contingencies have a **minimum expected cell count below 5** (range **0.66–3.06**). Reconstructed:
the two near/at-significant P-values reproduce **Fisher's exact** almost exactly (lymph node
reported 0.026 vs Fisher 0.0258; venous 0.057 vs 0.0566) — so the *right* test was used and the
*wrong* one declared. **The other five reproduce nothing** — not χ², not Yates, not Fisher, and not
a 2×3 table under any admissible split.

🔴 **No multiplicity correction.** Seven tests; Bonferroni threshold 0.0071. **P = 0.026 does not
survive it.**

Percentage rounding is also internally inconsistent: **18.75% is printed `18%` in one row and `19%`
in another; 71.875% as `71%` and `72%`.** The header row prints `79%`/`21%` where 25/32 and 7/32 are
78.1% and 21.9%.

### 4.7 · The survival claim is unadjusted for the paper's own confounder
🔴 From **Table 1 itself**: the Wwox(++) group is **4 of 7 node-negative (57%)** against **3 of 25
(12%)** in the (–&+) group. Nodal status is the dominant prognostic variable in resected pancreatic
cancer. **The survival difference attributed to Wwox is confounded by nodal status, in 32 patients
with 7 in the index arm, and no multivariate analysis is performed anywhere in the paper.**
`INFERENZA`. **`REVIVAL_TRIGGER`:** any node-stratified or multivariate re-analysis.

### 4.8 · The Smad4 asterisk cannot be checked by the reader
Fig 5b marks a significance bracket over (–) vs (++). **The asterisk is never defined** — unlike
Figs 1, 2 and 3, the Fig 5 caption carries no `*P < 0.05`. No group sizes, no error bars. Table 1
fixes (++) at 7, so that bar is **3 of 7**; the (–)/(+) split is **not recoverable**, and across the
three splits consistent with the printed bar heights the marked comparison runs from
**Fisher p = 1.00 to p = 0.003.** Meanwhile the running text hedges it as *"tended to associate"* —
so **text and panel disagree about whether the paper's own result is significant.**

## 6 · One hypothesis tested and **refused**, and one measurement that corrected me

🔴 **Both are recorded because a reading that only reports its successes is not a reading.**

**(a) Fig 3c does not contradict Fig 3d.** My visual impression was that the pΔWWOX plate looked as
dense as Mock, against panel (d)'s reported 63% reduction. I measured dark-pixel fraction inside
each plate disc. The measurement disagreed with my impression *and* with the reported counts **in an
inconsistent direction** — the signature of a confounded measure, not a discovered defect: the Mock
plate is rendered at a **different scale and background luminance** (mean 206 vs 212–221) from the
other four. **The line was discarded.** What survives is the modest, checkable statement that panel
(c) is not a like-for-like comparison and cannot adjudicate panel (d).

**(b) The β-actin in Fig 5c is *not* the confound I expected.** My eye said the loading control
dipped in the Mock lane, which would have inflated the Smad4 result. Densitometry on the **authentic
embedded 83×146 stream** (not a re-render) says the opposite:

| Strip | No transfection | Mock | pWWOX |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| β-actin | 1.00 | 1.35 | 1.37 |
| Smad4 | 1.00 | 0.96 | **2.63** |
| Smad2/3 | 1.00 | 1.91 | 1.68 |

Mock and pWWOX are **well loading-matched**; Smad4 pWWOX-over-Mock is **2.73× raw and 2.69× after
β-actin correction**. 🔴 **The paper's central in vitro claim survives the attack I brought to it,
and that is reported as prominently as anything that failed.** What the measurement *does* show is
that **Smad2/3 — the implicit specificity control — is not invariant** (1.42× Mock, 1.23× pWWOX,
normalised). *Limit declared: the source raster is 83×146 and JPEG-compressed; ratios are
indicative, not publication-grade.*

## 8 · Transfer verdict toward the reference genotype

**`ESPANSIONE` · T3 · clinical relevance LOW.**

The system is a pancreatic carcinoma cell line and a resected-tumour cohort; the intervention is an
adenoviral vector delivered *ex vivo*; the endpoints are colony counts, subG₁ and xenograft volume.
**Nothing here transfers to a WWOX-DEE genotype class**, and the paper measures no neural endpoint
of any kind.

What it legitimately contributes, and only as expansion:
- **Y33/WW1 as the dominant functional determinant**, with the SDR domain contributing but not
  required — a domain-architecture datum that is genotype-relevant *in principle* for missense
  alleles, and is here **confounded by unequal construct expression** (§4.4), so it is a lead and not
  a finding.
- **WWOX → Smad4 protein stabilisation** as a candidate post-translational node (`IPOTESI`,
  mechanism unshown), read strictly at the protein level.

🔴 It is read **because** it is oncology and pancreatic, not despite it — `gold_is_in_the_details`
rule 3. What it yielded was almost entirely **method**: the missing arm, the decoupled dose, the
invalid test, the undefined asterisk. Those transfer; the biology does not.
