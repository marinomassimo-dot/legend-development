## Independent re-read: agreement

My first pass reached, without the earlier locators, the same findings as the 2026-08 reading: the variant is classified of unclear significance in the authors' own Table 1 and conclusion; the nucleotide is written c.991C>A in the Results and c.911C>A in Table 1 and Discussion (codon arithmetic: c.911 lies in codon 304 position 2, a C>A there gives Ser to Tyr; c.991 lies in codon 331 position 1); the child also carries a heterozygous paternal CACNA1A variant; the gene therapy is misnamed "adenoviral"; the child died at 6 months. The Sanger panel resolves the trio (proband homozygous on forward and reverse reads; both parents heterozygous; CACNA1A paternal, mother wild type).

## New

1. The assay is named three ways: an Illumina NextSeq 500 run with a 78-gene reporting list, "whole-genome sequencing" (Results) and "whole-exome testing" (Discussion). Which assay found the variant, and whether CNV or a second WWOX allele was sought, is not determinable.
2. Parents are cousins; a homozygous call there is expected, but parental genotypes are shown, which excludes hemizygosity better than the adult case of this wave (companion dossier).
3. Phenobarbital 26.2 ug/ml (inside the usual range) with poor seizure control; levetiracetam gave slight improvement; no ketogenic diet, steroid or vigabatrin (stated for this child: none mentioned). Treatment efficacy is anecdote.
4. Bilateral hearing loss, method unnamed; not a feature in the 13-patient cohort of the 2023 cohort paper; unreplicated here.
5. Death at 6 months with no cause, circumstance or terminal event; for the autonomic and respiratory question nothing is learnable.
6. A residue-level cross-reference, established by reading Table S1 of the 2023 cohort supplement today: that table lists a different substitution at the same residue (p.Ser304Phe, c.911C>T) as likely pathogenic in a published patient. This supports the plausibility of a pathogenic role of Ser304 changes (ACMG PM5-type) but is not evidence about this child; recorded for the registry record of this case, whose classification stays "unclear".

## Does not support

Pathogenicity of p.Ser304Tyr (no functional work; authors say validation is needed); attributing the phenotype to WWOX rather than WWOX plus CACNA1A; any metabolic or autonomic conclusion. Transfer to the reference genotype: none (different variant, VUS).
