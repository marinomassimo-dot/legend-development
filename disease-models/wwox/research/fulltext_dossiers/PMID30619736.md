## 2 · The study, as designed

## 3 · What the paper establishes (with its strength)

1. **A HEK293T co-purification list for tagged full-length WWOX.** 216 prey pass two computational filters; 14 score > 0.3 and 32 score > 0.2 (L7, Figure 2 attestation L9). This is **DATO** for co-purification under stable over-expression in one cell line. It is **not** evidence of direct binding (L8, L43).
2. **SEC23IP, SCAMP3 and VOPP1 co-associate with WWOX** when both proteins are over-expressed in HEK293T. The reciprocal co-IP bands are present and every IgG lane is blank (L13, L14). **DATO**, at the level of co-association.
3. **The capture of SEC23IP and SCAMP3 from lysate depends on WW1.**
   - It is lost with the WW1 W44F/P47A mutant and with WW2 alone.
   - It is retained with the WW2 mutant (L16, L18).
   - **DATO**. VOPP1 is not tested by pull-down; its WW1 and PPPY dependence is **cited** from Bonin et al. 2018 (L19).
4. **Motif-bearing prey score higher on average** (0.1875 vs 0.1287), which is reproduced exactly from File 2 (L20). The strength is qualified in § 4.
5. **The prey list is enriched for KEGG/Reactome pathway annotations.**
   - Four metabolic pathways: BCAA degradation, glycolysis/gluconeogenesis, pyruvate metabolism, fatty-acid degradation.
   - Four non-metabolic ones: protein processing in the ER, which has the most members; degradation of DVL; tRNA aminoacylation; ER-phagosome (L27).
   - **DATO for the annotation statistic, as InnateDB computed it.**
6. **Two WWOX interactomes largely disagree:** 16 prey are shared out of 216 and 218 (L24, L25). This is a result in its own right. It is also the paper's only cross-method replication test, and it mostly fails.

## 4 · Reader computations over Supplementary File 2 (reproducible; not quotes)

The File 2 xlsx (`sha256 138c9255…1361`) was parsed with the stdlib renderer declared in the manifest. The results below come from arithmetic over its columns: col 10 is the MUSE interaction score, col 12 the CRAPome frequency, cols 5–6 the motif counts.

- **216 non-bait rows**, sorted by score. The minimum retained score is **0.0295** and every CRAPome frequency is < 0.199. The 253 → 216 cutoff is therefore somewhere below 0.03, and the paper does not say where.
- **Motif test.**
  - 67 prey with a motif (mean 0.18750, median 0.139) vs 149 without (mean 0.12873, median 0.114).
  - Student t = 3.36, df 214: reproduces the reported p < 0.001 level.
  - **Welch t = 2.64** (p ≈ 0.009) and **Mann-Whitney z = 2.26** (p ≈ 0.02).
  - The difference survives. Its reported strength depends on equal variances in visibly right-skewed distributions (Figure 4C, L22).
  - The legend describes a comparison of medians, but the test named is a t-test of means (L23).

## 7 · Design limits

- **One cell line and two replicates.** There is no negative-control purification; specificity is borrowed from an external contaminant repository.
- **The 253 → 216 cutoff is unstated**, so the list cannot be regenerated from the paper.
- **No background or correction is stated for the enrichment**; the p-values are InnateDB's.
- **Over-expression throughout**, and no endogenous interaction for any prey.
- **No functional experiment of any kind** on trafficking or metabolism.
- **Group.** The corresponding laboratory cloned WWOX and has argued a metabolic role before (Discussion refs 5, 10). The biochemistry weighs as native expertise; the pathway interpretation is weighed below its observation (manifest `group_assessment`).

## 8 · Transfer verdict toward the reference genotype (WWOX-DEE class)

**T3, indirect at most.**

- **Nothing here was measured in neural tissue**, with a loss-of-function allele, or at a developmental stage.
- **What could transfer is a list of candidate partners of wild-type WWOX.** Mechanistically the most useful items are the WW1–PPXY-dependent co-associations with SEC23IP and SCAMP3, which are measured. The metabolic-enzyme prey are weaker candidates, and they are not measured interactions at the paper's own confidence level.
- **Whether a DEE-class WWOX loses any of these associations is untested.**
- The paper supports no statement about metabolism in the reference genotype.
