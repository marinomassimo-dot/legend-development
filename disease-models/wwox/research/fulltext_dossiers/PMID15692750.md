## 2 · The reagent question — answered for this paper

### 2.1 · 🔴 The finding that reshapes the lot: neither 2005 paper is the origin

This paper cites its antibody **out** of itself:

> *«WWOX protein was detected using affinity purified anti-WWOX polyclonal primary antibodies (280 ng/ml) **(12)**»* — and ref 12, from the deposit's own `[PubMed: 14526170]` marker, is **Ludes-Meyers 2003**, *Cytogenet Genome Res* 100:101–110.

```

                                    └──▶  PMID 15692750  ──cites for the antibody──▶  PMID 14526170
```

**The chain does not terminate in these two papers. It terminates in a 2003 paper that has no receipt

### 2.3 · Controls ABSENT — and the asymmetry is the point

**Immunohistochemistry has no specificity control of any kind.** The whole of the IHC reagent section
is peroxidase quench, EDTA epitope retrieval, 10% goat-serum block, primary at 1:50, Envision polymer,
DAB, haematoxylin. Its only named control is:

> *«Staining development was performed with DAB with timed monitoring using a **positive control sample**.»*

That controls chromogen development. It says nothing about what the antibody binds. **Absent: no
no-primary, no pre-immune, no isotype, no peptide or fusion-protein pre-absorption, no second
antibody, no WWOX-deficient tissue, no blot-to-IHC correspondence on the same specimens.**

🔴 **And this is the paper the ovarian series cites for its immunostaining method** (*«Anti-WWOX
immunostaining was performed as previously reported [12]»*). The ovarian paper at least *asserts* a
pre-absorption, `data not shown`. The method paper it points to contains **no IHC control at all**.
Following the citation does not find the control; it finds the absence.

### 2.4 · The standard the validation reaches, and what it does not license

**Immunoblot, on breast tissue extracts: good, and better than the ovarian paper's.** A loaded blank
null lane on two gels, a size axis, a declared composite, and a positive reference on each gel. The
panel supports *«detects only full-length WWOX protein»* as the **predominant** case — inspected at 4×,
a **fainter second species** sits below the main band in **T98**, and fainter still under T82, so
*only* is the author's word and not the panel's.

🔴 **What it does NOT license:**

- **Not CNS immunohistochemistry — not even ordinary immunohistochemistry.** This paper's IHC has no
  reagent control whatsoever. Its blot validation is on breast lysates with an ovarian-line null. An
  antibody validated on a lysate is not thereby validated on a fixed, retrieved, paraffin section,
  and a reagent validated on breast epithelium is not validated for brain, where the signal is judged
  by cell shape with no co-marker.
- **Not the 46 kDa identity.** 39 kD and 31 kD are marked; 46 is not. Consistent, undemonstrated.
- **Not "only full-length".** See T98.
- **Not a second independent validation of the reagent.** See §5.

## 3 · What the paper establishes in its own right

1. **IHC on 234 pooled cases** (16 normal, 15 DCIS, 203 IDCA) from Fox Chase and the Cooperative Breast
   Cancer Tissue Resource, ACIS-scored: **all 16/16 normals positive**, 13 of them strongly;
   **69/203 (34%) IDCA "completely negative"**, **52/203 (26%) weak** → **121/203 (60%) reduced or
   lost**, *p = 0.003* versus normal; **5/15 (33%) DCIS** negative-or-weak.
2. **ER association, measured on a contingency table over all 203** (ER status available for every
   case): completely negative **27% of ER+ vs 46% of ER−**, *p = 0.0054*; negative+weak **51% vs 73%**,
   *p = 0.003*. Table 2 gives ER− n = 79, ER+ n = 124.
3. **ER−/PR− is the extreme group**: 51% negative, 80% negative-or-weak, *p = 0.013* against the other
   three groups.
4. **Immunoblot, 23 independent IDCA** against normal organoids: **20/23 (87%)** below 50% of normal.
5. **Compartment**: *«not a single case out of the 235 samples analyzed showed nuclear WWOX staining»* —
   an explicit, case-level negative.
6. **Explicit nulls**: no association with lymph-node status, metastasis, clinical stage, nuclear grade
   or patient age.

## 4 · What it does NOT establish

- **No multivariable analysis.** Univariate χ² with Yates', plus Kendall's tau-b and Pearson's. So the
  ER association is **unadjusted** — and since ER status co-varies with grade, proliferation and
  lineage, the association is not shown to be independent of them.
- **No causality, and the paper says so**: its own preferred reading is progression
  (*«makes the second alternative more likely»*), from what it calls a *tendency*.
- **No mechanism, no hormone, no enzymology.** See §6.
- **"Negative" is ≤ 63/255 arbitrary intensity units**, not absence of protein.
- **No case-level link between the 23 blotted and the 203 stained tumours.**
- **Nothing neural, developmental, or germline.**

## 5 · 🔴 Independence — one pipeline, two tissues

The contract asks whether the two series are two independent validations of the reagent. **From the
artefacts: no.** Established by what each paper says, not by the author list:

| Shared | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Same antibody** | both name the affinity-purified rabbit polyclonal at **280 ng/ml** for blot; both cite **ref 12** for it (ovarian → this paper; this paper → 14526170) |
| **Same negative control** | PEO1, the same homozygous-deletion line, cited to the same source (11572989) |
| **Same scoring platform and same lower cutoff** | Chromavision ACIS, generic DAB application, whole-core area, **cutoff 63 of 255** in both |
| **Same immunostaining protocol** | the ovarian paper cites *this* paper for it |
| **Same laboratory, overlapping personnel** | 7 of 9 authors shared; Nancy W. Abbey is an author here and acknowledged in the ovarian paper |
| **Overlapping tissue-bank source** | Fox Chase Cancer Center pathology in both |

| Not shared | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Tissue and cases** | breast, 234 cases, Fox Chase + CBCTR TMAs vs ovary, 444 cases, MD Anderson + Fox Chase TMAs. **No case overlap is claimed or implied, and the series are separately described.** |
| **Blots** | 23 breast IDCA vs 38 ovarian carcinomas, different lane sets, different figures |
| **Upper scoring boundary** | breast: weak ≤ 70, strong > 70. Ovarian: weak ≤ 65, moderate 65–81, strong > 81 |
| **Handling of the opposing compartment report** | ovarian blames antibody differences; this paper attacks the other study's sample size and figure count |

**Verdict.** The **data** are largely independent — different tissues, different cases, different blots.
The **reagent validation is not**: one antibody, one null control, one scoring pipeline, one laboratory.
So the corpus must count **one** reagent validation reported across two tissues, not two. And neither
is an **external replication**: the only external corroboration named anywhere in the lot is Guler 2004
(ref 21 = PMID 15073846), a different group, whose antibody this paper does not state — which is why
it is queued rather than counted.

## 6 · The hormonal frame — restated, and here it is *strengthened* by an association

The Introduction states the prediction (*«The SDR domain is **predicted** to be involved in sex-steroid
metabolism»*). The Discussion then converts an immunostain correlation into support for it:

> *«These findings further strengthen the hypothesis that WWOX plays a role in sex-steroid metabolism»*

**Does the paper test it? No.** No hormone administered, no receptor manipulated, no substrate, no
enzymatic activity, no steroid measured. Two immunostains on the same sections. The inference is
`AUTHOR_INTERPRETATION`; an ER-status correlation is equally consistent with co-regulation,
differentiation state, or luminal-versus-basal lineage.

**Read across the lot, the frame does not accumulate — it wobbles.** Breast: ER *p* = 0.0054 and the
hypothesis *«further strengthened»*. Ovary, same laboratory, same reagent, same year: **ER flatly null,
*p* = 0.984**, only PR associated, with the authors raising histotype composition as the alternative
and leaving it open. A hypothesis restated across two papers, confirmed in one tissue and
disconfirmed in the other, is not evidence accumulating.
