## What the paper did
H9 hESC were differentiated to neuroepithelial precursor cells (dual SMAD inhibition, about 6 days) with valproate (VPA, 600 uM) or trichostatin A (TSA, 10 nM), two HDAC inhibitors. Histone acetylation, H3K4/H3K27 methylation at PAX6 and OTX2 promoters, marker transcripts and Affymetrix transcriptomes were followed for exposure windows (6 h, 4 d, 6 d) and pulse-chase designs (early, medium, late pulse with washout).
- Main finding stated by the authors: acetylation rises quickly and returns to baseline in 48 h; methylation changes persist; short exposures give reversible transcriptome changes, long ones a disturbed differentiation, and prolonged-exposure transcriptomes mainly reflect an altered developmental state rather than the drug mode of action.
- Treated-versus-control lists: same-day untreated controls, BH-adjusted p below 0.05, ratio above 1.5 or below 2/3.

## Caveats given by the authors
Early responses reverse; the 6-day transcriptome does not reflect the initial drug response (overlap of TSA lists across exposure periods is only 1-13 percent); outcome depends on the analysis time point.
