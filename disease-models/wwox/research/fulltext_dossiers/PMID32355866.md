## What the paper did
Eight male cynomolgus macaques (2-4 years, about 3 kg) with pre-screened low neutralising titres received a self-complementary AAV carrying GFP (CBA promoter with four miR-142 target repeats): AAV9 or AAVrh10 by lumbar intrathecal catheter (LIT-KT; six animals, 2.5E+13 vg total, 3 mL as three 1 mL deposits at cervical, thoracic and first-lumbar levels) or AAV9 by intracerebroventricular injection (ICV; two animals, 4.5E+12 vg, 0.5 mL). All animals were immunosuppressed (sirolimus 2 mg/kg/day, prednisolone 1-2 mg/kg/day, mycophenolate mofetil 40 mg/kg/day; start day not given in the Methods sentence). Euthanasia and tissue read-out three weeks after dosing. CSF and serum neutralising-factor titres and vector genomes in CSF and serum were followed (days 0 to 19).
- Reported: AAV9 LIT-KT gave consistent spinal motor-neuron and some brain transduction; AAV9 ICV gave brain transduction; AAVrh10 LIT-KT gave rare motor-neuron targeting; AAV9 also reached skeletal and respiratory muscle and heart; rapid escape of vector from CSF to blood; neutralising factors rose in serum within a week.

## DRG content (the question for this wave)
- Transduction: GFP in the soma of large-diameter DRG neurons and in fibres in all transduced DRG; mostly cervical and lumbar after LIT-KT, thoracic and lumbar after ICV. No co-localisation with substance P.
- Toxicity read-out: histopathology of DRG by haematoxylin-eosin-saffron. Mild multifocal mononuclear infiltration in thoracic or lumbar DRG of the two AAVrh10 animals; mild lumbar infiltration in two AAV9 LIT-KT animals; moderate infiltration in cervical and lumbar DRG of one AAV9 animal; infiltration and myelin digestion chambers in DRG fibres of three AAV9 animals. Brain: no lesions. Figure S5 (rendered and inspected) shows six representative panels only; there is **no grading scale, no neuron-loss count and no per-animal table** in the paper.
- Not measured or not reported: vector genome or protein quantification in DRG in the text slices read; electrophysiology or behaviour tests of sensory function; any lesion beyond three weeks.

## What the paper does NOT say
Nothing about WWOX; no therapeutic transgene; no efficacy; no long-term DRG outcome; no immunosuppression arm (all immunosuppressed), so the effect of immunosuppression on DRG findings cannot be read from it.

## Caveats given or visible
Eight animals, two doses and routes confounded with capsid (AAV9 LIT-KT 6 vs AAVrh10 two; ICV two), male only, short follow-up, a reporter transgene; the authors themselves cite earlier work on DRG degeneration after AAV9 and note their DRG findings as a safety aspect.

## Transfer limit
A GFP reporter at 2.5E+13 or 4.5E+12 vg per animal in juvenile macaques with triple immunosuppression for three weeks. It gives a descriptive DRG baseline for intra-CSF AAV9 and AAVrh10 under these conditions; it says nothing about a WWOX cassette, a dose per kilogram in patients, or a longer horizon.
