## 1 · What the study did

Retrospective review of 90 children (0–18 y) seen 2019–2021 at one paediatric neurology clinic with
unexplained epilepsy and/or developmental delay after metabolic work-up and MRI: exome alone (58),
exome plus microarray (20) or genome (12). Variants classified by ACMG/ClinGen from inheritance,
ClinVar, HPO match and in-silico predictors; no functional assay of any variant.

## 2 · What it measured and states (cohort level)

- P/LP: exome 20/58 (34.4 %), exome + CMA 11/20 (55 %), genome 5/12 (41.6 %); clinically relevant
  VUS in a further 11, 2 and 5. Figure 2 panel agrees with the text.
- Phenotype groups: epilepsy only 9, epilepsy + DD 53, DD only 28 (Figure 1 agrees).
- Consanguinity / same-village origin in 15/90; biallelic disorders 22.2 % of diagnoses.
- 12/90 received a diagnosis with a potential specific treatment (Table 4; WWOX is not among them).
