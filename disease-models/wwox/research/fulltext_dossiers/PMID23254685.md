## Verdict

The paper validates a second conditional `Wwox` allele, with loxP sites around exon 1. The unrecombined `Wwox^fl/fl` state is not detectably hypomorphic at organism level over about two years, although some founders had reduced expression. Germline EIIA-Cre recombination abolishes WWOX in the tissues tested and recapitulates the conventional-null syndrome: growth retardation, severe hypoglycaemia, death by three weeks and markedly reduced cortical/trabecular bone.

The neoplasia wording is materially weaker than the title-level narrative often built from it. Figure 5B shows a tiny pocket of atypical osteoblast-like cells in one 14-day femur, and the text calls them “malignant appearing”; there are no tumor markers, incidence denominator, longitudinal outcome or independent pathological validation. `INFERENZA`, not a robust osteosarcoma endpoint.

## Design and limits

- Exon 1 is floxed; the targeting cassette retains `Frt-PGK-Neo-pA-Frt`.
- Eight homologous ES clones were found; three generated chimeras with germline transmission.
- EIIA-Cre performs germline/global deletion. No tissue-restricted knockout is tested here.
- Bone was examined at day 14 by radiography, microCT and histology. The figure reports p-values but the text gives no group sizes, sex balance or blinding.
- “Severe hypoglycemia” and death are reported as `data not shown`, so their direction is first-hand but their magnitude is unavailable here.

## Horizontal impact

Supports `CLAIM 036`'s systemic-null confounding boundary and strengthens the distinction between a validated conditional reagent and a global-null phenotype. It also sharpens the unresolved tumor conflict: this paper repeats the Aqeilan osteosarcoma interpretation, whereas PMID 19936220 found zero neoplasia in nine null mice by necropsy, X-ray, histology and microCT.

Transfer to the reference genotype is `ESPANSIONE`: this is a homozygous global deletion with early metabolic collapse, not a human hypomorphic/missense genotype and not a nervous-system-restricted model.
