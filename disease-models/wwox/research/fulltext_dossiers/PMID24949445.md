## What the paper did
A retrospective description of seven children with hereditary neurodevelopmental or neurometabolic disorders, using karyotype, FISH, PCR, gene sequencing and array CGH (Agilent 180K, coordinates on NCBI36/hg18).

## WWOX content, class level — one child
- **Variant as printed:** `arr 16q23.1 (77,445,915–78,190,209) dup`, size 0.744–0.827 Mb, inheritance *Unknown* (Table 4).
- **Phenotype band:** a non-progressive neurodevelopmental disorder: delay from age two, severe expressive language impairment, nonverbal IQ falling from 75 to 52 to 42 across testing, dysmorphic features, scoliosis, stereotypies, and epilepsy (generalized and focal discharges) whose EEG normalised on valproate. **Not a DEE band.**
- **Call:** array CGH only; no confirmation by a second method is reported.
- **Segregation:** none. *"The significance of this change in our patient is limited by the lack of paternal DNA."*
- **Authors' interpretation:** *"The duplication found in Patient 1 affects two dose-sensitive genes: WWOX … and MAF"*, with the link to the phenotype called speculative.
