## 3 · What the paper establishes — accepted

`DATO`, each with a locator and each read from the panel as well as the prose:

- **p100/p52 loss substantially delays Tax-driven tumorigenesis in vivo.** Kaplan-Meier, n=17 vs 20.
- **Tax represses WWOX largely through the noncanonical pathway** — Tax-inducible Jurkat, CD40 yes,
  Tax-2 (canonical-only) no, and the knockout background rescues most of the repression.
- **WWOX knockdown increases, and WWOX expression decreases, Tax-driven transformation**, in soft
  agar and in SCID xenografts; three independent shRNAs, consistent rank order across two panels.
- **WWOX inhibits Tax-induced but not TNFα-induced NF-κB** — a genuine, well-placed specificity
  control that the paper deserves credit for.
- **WWOX binds Tax; the Y33R mutant binds it too and still fails to suppress it.** The paper says
  so itself: *"binding to Tax is not sufficient for WWOX to suppress the oncogenic actions of Tax."*
- **WWOX blocks the Tax-mediated *increment* of IKKα recruitment to RelA**, returning it to the
  Tax-negative baseline — in a panel (6H) whose inputs are controlled and which I re-read at 800%.

## 4 · What it refuses to support — the product, not the residue

### 4.1 · Two internal contradictions, one of which this reading missed

| # | The text says | The panel prints |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *"all the mice had tumors by 18 weeks of age (Figure 1A)"* | 🔴 **~41% of that cohort is still tumour-free at 18 weeks**; 0% only at ~25 weeks |
| 2 | *"almost complete recovery of the WWOX gene from Tax-mediated repression"* | Fig 2B's own printed percentiles: **94 → 63** in the knockout — a residual third |

🔴 **Contradiction 1 was found by the blind audit, not by me**, and it is harder than the section
heading I had built the reading around. Recorded with the attribution rather than absorbed.

### 4.2 · One surface overstatement, and the abstract is not part of it

The Results heading *"Knockout of p100/p52 **prevents** tumorigenesis in Tax transgenic mice"*
overstates its own paragraph: 10% remained tumour-free at 52 weeks, so **~90% developed tumours**
and the effect is delay. The **Discussion** says *"significantly reduce"* and the **abstract** says
*"significantly delays"* — both accurate. The defect is confined to **one heading**.

This extends the `DL-METH-081` surface-inversion class **to a laboratory outside the one where it
was first observed**, which changes what that class is evidence of: not a production problem in one
group, but a property of the heading/caption surface itself.

### 4.3 · "Dramatically" is attached to the weaker effect

| Panel | WWOX WT | WWOX Y33R |
|---|---|---|
| 4B colony | ~41 (**−59%**) `**` | ~128 (**+28%**) `*` |
| 4D tumour weight | ~21 mg (**−60%**) `**` | ~67 mg (**+28%**) `*` |

The suppression is twice the magnitude at an order stronger significance. *"Dramatically enhanced"*
describes the smaller, weaker one.

### 4.4 · Unmarked panels — stated as absence of a marker, never as a null result

- **Figure 5 carries no significance mark in any of its four panels**, yet the text claims
  dose-dependence and an *"inversely correlated"* relationship. In 5C the top Y33R whisker reaches
  ~85, below the control level of 100.
- **Figure 2G carries no significance mark on any bar**, yet the sentence citing it says
  *"significantly decreased"*. Its comparator is a **single** healthy donor set to 100 while the
  other two sit at 117 and 149.
- **Figure 1C**: the two arms that fall carry `**`; the two rescue arms that rise carry nothing.

Statistics for the whole paper are a two-tailed Student t test between two groups, with **no
multiplicity correction declared** despite repeated multi-arm comparisons, and no survival statistic
declared for the Kaplan-Meier at all.

### 4.5 · Two mechanistic claims resting on unshown data

- *"WWOX proteins undergo ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation through both Tax-dependent and
  -independent mechanisms (data not shown and see Figure 6D)."* — **Figure 6D contains no
  Tax-negative lane** (Tax spans all nine) **and no ubiquitin readout at all**. It shows WWOX is
  proteasomally degraded; it cannot separate the two arms, and both the *Tax-independent* and the
  *ubiquitination* halves are unshown.
- *"WWOX does not affect Tax subcellular localization (data not shown and supplemental Figure 2)."*
  — see §6. **The cited supplement cannot carry it.**

## 5 · 🔴 The finding that exists only in the supplement, and only in the pixels

**Supplemental Figure S3 measures HTLV-I *HBZ* transcript in the same nine ATL patients and three
controls — and the main text never cites it. The string "HBZ" appears nowhere in the article body
or abstract.**

Why it matters: **Tax is never measured in the primary patient samples.** Figure 2G is the paper's
only clinical anchor, and the driver its model requires is absent from it. The only viral transcript
measured in those patients sits in a figure the paper does not mention.

⚠️ **Stated as an assumption, because the audit was right to require it.** Nothing in either
caption, either figure or the Methods asserts that "ATL #1–#9" denotes the same patients in the same
order in Fig 2G and Fig S3. *On that assumption*, WWOX does not track HBZ:

| Patient | WWOX (Fig 2G) | HBZ (Fig S3) |
|---|---:|---:|
| #4 | **~20.5 (lowest)** | ~3 |
| #1 | ~35 | **~101 (highest)** |
| #6 | ~27 | ~23 |
| #9 | ~100 (normal) | ~3.5 |

Without the assumption, what stands unconditionally is the **absence of any Tax measurement in
patient material**. That alone qualifies *"wwox repression is an HTLV-I pathophysiologic event."*

## 8 · Premises and negatives

- **`PREMISE: DATO`** — *"an unmarked panel is not a tested null."* Every "no significance mark"
  statement above is an absence of a drawn marker. **`REVIVAL_TRIGGER`:** a corrigendum or a source
  data file supplying tests for Figures 2G or 5 reopens every one of them.
- **`PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK`** — *"a co-IP band means a physiological interaction."* Fig 6B
  recovers more Tax-bound WWOX from lines that contain **less** WWOX; striking, and still a
  qualitative overexpression-era co-IP. **`REVIVAL_TRIGGER`:** an endogenous, stoichiometric or
  proximity-ligation measurement of the WWOX–Tax complex.
- **Negative refused rather than recorded:** *"WWOX does not relocalise Tax"* is **not** entered as
  a negative, because entering it would create exactly the silent false negative the discipline
  exists to prevent. It is recorded as an **author assertion on unshown data**.
  **`REVIVAL_TRIGGER`:** a fractionation or imaging experiment with a WWOX-negative comparator and a
  Tax input row.

## 9 · Transfer verdict

**`ESPANSIONE` / T3.** An HTLV-I/NF-κB result in T-cell leukaemia makes no direct statement about a
WWOX-DEE genotype class. Its value here is **architectural**: it is a locator-backed instance of
WWOX acting through *competitive occlusion of a third protein's recruitment* rather than through
binding affinity or cargo rerouting — a mode worth carrying into how WWOX partner biology is read,
and typed as an extension rather than smuggled in as mechanism for the reference genotype.

Nothing here is medical advice.
