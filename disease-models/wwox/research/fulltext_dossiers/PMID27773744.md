## 1 · What the paper measures

Fhit knockout mouse kidney cells (gamma-H2AX foci, neutral comet assay, thymidine supplementation, TK1 protein), five human breast cancer lines and Wwox-knockout MEFs (clonogenic survival after radiation, mitomycin C with or without a PARP inhibitor), a Kaplan-Meier Plotter ovarian cohort (platinum, n = 1185), and a TCGA squamous lung expression split by FHIT.

## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. Fhit loss lowers TK1 and doubles DSB markers; 10 uM thymidine for 40 days suppresses DSBs in Fhit-null cells only. Nucleotide supply rescues a Fhit lesion; no thymidine experiment exists for Wwox.
2. Wwox-deficient lines survive radiation about 10-fold better at 4 Gy (three deficient against two expressing lines); late-passage kidney cells with reduced Wwox survive better than early passage; knockout MEFs survive mitomycin C and mitomycin plus a PARP inhibitor better.
3. Ovarian patients with low Wwox on platinum have shorter progression-free survival (Kaplan-Meier Plotter, n = 1185; no adjustment shown).
4. In TCGA squamous lung, Wwox expression is lower (P < 0.05) in the Fhit-low group; read as co-loss with Fhit first.
5. The repair-pathway mechanism is cited to the authors 2016 paper.
