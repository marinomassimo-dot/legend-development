## 1 · What the paper is

A two-patient case report in adults with SCN1A Dravet syndrome and parkinsonism: DAT SPECT normal in both, CSF monoamine metabolites measured once, levodopa-carbidopa started in both (Case 1 improved). It is the nearest published example of a CSF neurotransmitter work-up in a developmental and epileptic encephalopathy with a movement disorder.

## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. CSF HVA (dopamine metabolite) 36.6 and 79.3 against a quoted range of 145 to 324; 5-HIAA (serotonin metabolite) 20.4 and 52.2 against 67 to 140 (Table 2). Both below range.
2. CSF MHPG (noradrenaline metabolite) 27.7 and 40.7 against 35 to 65: low in Case 1, in range in Case 2. 3-OMD below 16 and 19.1 (limit 100). The text does not discuss MHPG at all.
3. DAT SPECT normal in both (Figure 1 C and D).
4. Case 1 improved on levodopa-carbidopa (a drug was also stopped at admission); Case 2 only started.
5. The authors name the lack of CSF data in Dravet patients without parkinsonism as the key limitation and say levodopa response in Dravet parkinsonism has been inconclusive.

## 3 · Comparison with the laboratory's records

- The registry has a WWOX parkinsonism case (PAPER 145, homozygous missense) recorded as "neurotransmitters not measured". This paper supplies the panel that case lacks and what it returned in a different DEE: a pattern of low dopamine and serotonin metabolites with normal DAT SPECT. It does not say what a WWOX panel would show.
- The registry has no HVA record, so the metabolite ranges here are new to the laboratory. MHPG, the one noradrenaline-pathway value, is unmodelled.
- The report is internally careful on method (collection as in the cited review, HPLC with fluorescence) but the confounders in Table 1 are not discussed.

## 4 · What the paper does NOT support

- Any WWOX statement. Dravet syndrome is an SCN1A channelopathy; a dopamine-synthesis deficit there cannot be carried to WWOX loss of function.
- A causal reading that reduced dopamine synthesis causes parkinsonism in Dravet: no Dravet controls without parkinsonism, no Dravet CSF reference, n of 2, reference ranges taken from a review of unstated age band.
- "Significant" decreases (single values against a range).
- Any claim about adrenaline or noradrenaline: the one noradrenaline metabolite is low in one patient and normal in the other and is not interpreted by the authors.
- Levodopa efficacy (uncontrolled, concurrent drug withdrawal in Case 1).
