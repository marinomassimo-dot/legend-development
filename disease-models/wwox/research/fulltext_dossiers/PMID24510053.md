## 3 · What the paper establishes

All of the following is `DATO` **about what the review reports**, and never `DATO` about the
underlying biology, which lives in primaries this corpus mostly has not opened.

- **Mouse allele → tumour mapping, close to first-hand.** Targeted deletion of *Wwox* gives
  osteosarcomas in nulls and spontaneous lung papillary carcinomas in heterozygotes (its own
  refs 13, 14). `Wwox (C3H)+/−`: **50% of females formed tumours against 7% of wild-type females.**
- **A regulated degradation route exists for WWOX.** Ack1, an intracellular tyrosine kinase,
  induces WWOX polyubiquitination and subsequent degradation in prostate cancer cells; in human
  androgen-independent prostate cancer, raised phospho-Ack1 accompanies reduced WWOX against
  benign tissue. The review's own modal for the causal step is *"suggesting"*.
- **WWOX loss is not the only direction reported.** Some studies document **increased** WWOX in
  breast, gastric and prostate carcinomas, which the authors read as complex regulation.
- **CNS content, all somatic:** WWOX LOH and promoter methylation in glioblastoma, correlated with
  Bcl2 and Ki67 (ref 100); WOX1 overexpression inducing apoptosis in GBM lines **harbouring mutant
  p53** (ref 101). Both qualifiers on the latter are load-bearing.
