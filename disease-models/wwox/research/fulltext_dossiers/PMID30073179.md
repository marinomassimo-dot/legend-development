## What the paper did

Two good-laboratory-practice-style studies pooled (22 rhesus macaques): image-guided suboccipital (ICM) injection of AAV9 carrying CB7-driven codon-optimised human IDUA. High dose 1e13 genome copies (15 animals; necropsy day 14, 90 or 180), low dose 1e12 (3 animals, day 90), vehicle (4). In the second study (10 animals, day 90) three high-dose animals also received mycophenolate mofetil plus rapamycin, starting three weeks before dosing, MMF stopped on day 60. DRG and trigeminal ganglia were sampled only in the second study.

## What it measured

- No procedure-related or clinical adverse events; no transaminase, coagulation or blood-count change in non-immunosuppressed animals.
- CSF mononuclear pleocytosis (5-30 cells/uL), onset by day 14, peak days 21-45, usually resolved by day 90: one low-dose and five high-dose animals; none in the three immunosuppressed animals (Figure 1, inspected).
- Anti-IDUA antibodies in serum and CSF in all non-immunosuppressed vector animals at similar intensity at both doses; fully ablated by immunosuppression at the tested dilutions (Figure 2A, inspected). AAV9 neutralising antibody titres lower with immunosuppression (Figure 2B). IFN-gamma ELISPOT to IDUA peptides in all high- and low-dose animals and in one of three immunosuppressed animals (Figure 2C).
- Histology: minimal to mild DRG neuronal degeneration with mononuclear infiltrate (CD20 B and CD3 T cells clustered round IDUA-positive neurons) and minimal to moderate bilateral dorsal-column axonopathy, in the vector groups only; axonopathy absent at day 14 and in vehicle (DRG themselves were not sampled in the first study, which contains the day-14 and day-180 animals, so there is no DRG time course: the DRG lesion is documented at a single time point, day 90, in ten animals); no brain parenchyma or spinal grey-matter change; no peripheral-organ lesion (Figure 3).
- Figure 3G (inspected): DRG cumulative score about 2.7 high dose, 2.0 high dose plus immunosuppression, 2.3 low dose; axonopathy about 5.0 and 4.7 (high dose, day 90 and 180), 2.4 (immunosuppressed), 4.4 (low dose). Three animals per group, error bars overlap in the DRG panel.
- Biodistribution: about 1e5 genome copies per ug DNA in brain, cord and DRG at high dose (about tenfold lower at low dose), about 1e6 in liver; vector genomes in DRG similar or higher with immunosuppression.

## What the authors state

- The sensory-neuron toxicity was "unexpected", asymptomatic, and resembles the DRG ganglionitis seen after IV AAV9-SMN1 in piglets and juvenile primates; absent in MPS I cats and dogs at similar intrathecal doses; "rhesus macaques and piglets seem to be more sensitive".
- T-cell clustering round transduced neurons and anti-IDUA responses were still present in animals with suppressed T-cell responses; "perhaps immune responses are not causative but worsen an initial injury", with a strong-promoter overexpression precedent cited.
- "IS can help alleviate" the findings, but "it is hard to draw any definitive conclusions due to study limitations, such as cohort size". The systemic toxicity seen after high-dose IV AAV was not detected here, at a total dose 50-fold lower.

## What the paper does NOT say

- No dose-response for the DRG lesion: the tenfold lower dose gave a DRG score near that of the high dose at three animals per group. The authors do not draw this.
- No test of whether the lesion is capsid-, genome- or transgene-driven (single cargo, single capsid); the cargo-swap comparison is the companion paper.
- No sensory function measure; no quantitative sensory or nerve-conduction data.
- Immunosuppression arm confounded by its own toxicity (diarrhoea, weight loss, anaemia from before dosing) and by early MMF withdrawal in one animal.

## Reader's observations (INFERENZA where marked)

- The running text says incidence and severity "trended toward lower scores" with immunosuppression. The panel qualifies this: the DRG score overlaps the untreated-immunosuppression arm within error; only the axonopathy score is visibly lower and still overlaps. Incomplete, not contradicted.
- Immunosuppression abolished pleocytosis and antibodies yet DRG neuronal degeneration persisted in two of three animals (Table S5, cervical and thoracic blocks, read from the PDF text layer; counts only, no dose labels carried): CSF pleocytosis and DRG lesion are dissociable here.
- INFERENZA: the same-size lesion at tenfold lower dose is compatible with a saturating or threshold-near-low-dose process or with n = 3 noise; the paper cannot say which.

## Transfer limit

Rhesus, ICM, AAV9 capsid, CB7 promoter, secreted lysosomal enzyme (cross-correction possible), single time of necropsy per cohort (14, 90, 180 days), n = 3 per histology group in the second study. Says nothing about WWOX, an intracellular cargo, a different promoter, or any human response.
