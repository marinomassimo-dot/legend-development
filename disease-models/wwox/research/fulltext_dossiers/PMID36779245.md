## 1 · What the paper measures

13 patients from 12 families (five centres), biallelic WWOX variants; centrally reviewed EEG and MRI; variants re-classified to ACMG; the 62 literature cases coded null or missense; Kaplan-Meier over 75 individuals by genotype class (null/null 45, null/missense 15, missense/missense 15); RNA studies for two CNVs.

## 2 · Findings, first pass of the new material

1. Treatment (new locators): ketogenic diet started by 3 patients (Patients 7, 11, 12), continued by 1; cannabidiol continued by all 4 who took it; vigabatrin in 7 of 10 with spasms; ACTH in one, prednisone in one. No efficacy outcome is given for any drug; drug resistance 12 of 13 (Patient 5, homozygous Q230P, is the exception and died at 8 years 3 months).

## 3 · Comparison with the laboratory's records

- The earlier locators (no intermediate class; denominators 45/15/15; deaths not concentrated in null class; Patient 2 and Patient 5 both homozygous Q230P at opposite ends of survival) are re-pointed unchanged and stand; Figure 4A as drawn agrees with the earlier panel reading.
- New against the laboratory's record: the paper gives the ketogenic diet 3 starts and 1 continuation with no outcome, so it cannot support either a KD benefit or a KD failure in WWOX-DEE; it is a base rate of tolerability and persistence, not efficacy. Relevant to RL-003 only as an absence.
- Adult teplyshova case (companion in this wave): its "biallelic missense with DEE phenotype" departs from the rule quoted from this paper's introduction (SCAR12 missense-only); this paper itself finds no regional or class separation for missense DEE versus SCAR12 and says so (Figure 3).

## 4 · What the paper does NOT support

- Genotype-specific survival for missense/missense by itself: the pooled "at least one missense" class carries the log-rank; the missense/missense curve ends at about 0.62 with a wide band and one very long survivor (Patient 2) forming its tail; censoring is age at publication.
- Any metabolic, mitochondrial or autonomic conclusion; any antiseizure-treatment efficacy conclusion; any causal mechanism for the respiratory deaths.
- That the Patient 6 intron 4 deletion, rather than the intron 3 deletion, causes exon skipping.

## 5 · Design and limits

Retrospective multicentre case series plus literature pooling; variant class coding is the authors'; literature case survival ascertained from published ages. 709 px figures; supplement EMF graphics (EEG and PET pages) not rendered here.
