## What the paper did

European-ancestry participants who died at 65 or older with imputed genotypes and autopsy data: NACC (n = 3,749 in the text) and ROSMAP (n = 1,390). Region tested: chr16 78,133,309–79,634,622 (hg19, WWOX start to MAF end) ± 250 kb; 8,256 variants shared and meta-analysed (fixed effect). Logistic models per endophenotype (LATE-NC, hippocampal sclerosis, brain arteriolosclerosis, Braak V–VI, frequent neuritic plaques) under additive, dominant and recessive coding. Significance was a **locus-wide** Bonferroni threshold over effective tests, 3.67e-5 — not genome-wide.

## What it measured and states

- Five variants passed the locus-wide threshold in meta-analysis: two for LATE-NC (additive, OR about 1.4), two for HS (recessive, OR about 2.3–2.4), one for arteriolosclerosis (dominant, OR 0.74). No variant passed for tangles or plaques; the clinical-AD lead variant from Kunkle 2019 failed QC in both cohorts and had no proxy.
- **Gene attribution.** No prioritised variant is an eQTL or sQTL in GTEx. In BRAINEAC only the two HS variants are nominal eQTLs for WWOX; the LATE-NC and arteriolosclerosis variants are nominal eQTLs for **MAF**. In Figure 2 the HS and LATE-NC leads plot under the WWOX gene track and the arteriolosclerosis lead between WWOX and MAF. The title and abstract call all of these "WWOX variants"; the functional link for the LATE-NC signal points to MAF.
- The previously reported HS variant rs55751884 replicated nominally (recessive meta OR about 3, p about 0.01); its plaque associations ran in opposite directions in the two cohorts.
- Associations with clinical AD for the five variants are weak (ORs 0.94–1.05).

## What the paper does NOT state

- Anything about WWOX protein, expression in brain tissue (beyond nominal eQTL), or mechanism.
- Anything about rare or loss-of-function WWOX alleles: all variants are common (MAF ≥ 1%), non-coding, and "recessive" means homozygous for a common allele.

## Caveats the authors give

Suggestive findings; European ancestry only; fixed-effect meta-analysis of two cohorts; corroboration needed in other datasets.

## Transfer limit

Common non-coding variants in an adult autopsy population, association only. Nothing transfers to a biallelic loss-of-function genotype class or to WWOX-DEE. The locus label "WWOX" is positional; for LATE-NC the only functional link is to MAF.
