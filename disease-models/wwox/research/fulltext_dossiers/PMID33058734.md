## 3 · What it establishes

1. **`DATO` — Exposure is associated with lower lung WWOX transcript, in two systems.** Human
   current smokers: **47% lower** WWOX mRNA than former/never-smokers (`P < 0.05`). Mice after 3
   months of nicotine-containing e-cigarette vapour: **average 57% decrease** (`P < 0.05`).
2. **`DATO` — and it is measured as transcript *and* protein, but asymmetrically.** mRNA is
   quantified with statistics (Fig 1A, 1B). Protein is a **representative Western blot with no
   densitometry and no statistics** (Fig 1C, 1D) — the paper's own words are *"demonstrates that
   downregulation of mRNA translates to decreased protein expression."* Direction: supported.
   Magnitude at the protein level: **not quantified.**
3. **`DATO` — endothelial-specific WWOX knockout increases vascular leak under challenge.** LPS:
   asterisks on BAL leukocytes, BAL protein and BAL:serum FITC-dextran (Fig 3A, 3B, 3D). MRSA:
   asterisks on BAL leukocytes and BAL protein (Fig 5A, 5B). Corroborated by H&E.
4. **`DATO` — the phenotype requires a second hit.** *"There were no significant differences between
   untreated EC WWOX KO mice and controls."* Unchallenged KO lungs are normal.
5. **`DATO` — a real Cre-only control was run.** `Cdh5-CreERT2` vs `WWOX^flox/flox`: no significant
   difference in EC permeability, so the phenotype is not an artefact of endothelial Cre.
6. **`DATO` — knockdown, not null.** Sorted lung ECs: WWOX mRNA falls to ≈0.15 of flox (Fig 2C,
   asterisk) — about **85%** reduction, with a visible residual fraction.
7. **`DATO` — cultured WWOX-deficient ECs are intrinsically leakier.** siRNA hLMVECs lose more TER
   and pass more FITC-dextran under LPS (Fig 1E, 1F). ECs from KO mice lose more TER and pass more
   dextran under MRSA (Fig 7A, 7B). 🔴 **And read as an image, Fig 7A shows `WWOX KO/PBS` falling to
   ≈0.71 while `flox/PBS` recovers to ≈0.95 — a baseline barrier deficit with no pathogen at all**,
   which the *in vivo* unchallenged comparison did not show.
8. **`DATO` — JNK inhibition does not rescue.** SP600125 before LPS gave *"no significant rescue of
   barrier function."* A clean negative against the pathway the group's own earlier whole-lung paper
   had implicated.
9. **🔴 `DATO` — THE CENTRAL NEGATIVE. Endothelial WWOX loss does *not* raise inflammatory cytokines
   in the lung, in either injury model.** LPS: *"a trend toward increased concentrations of
   inflammatory cytokines … none were statistically significant (Figure 4)."* MRSA: *"a trend toward
   increased proinflammatory cytokine (TNFα, IL-6, KC) secretion … these differences were not
   statistically significant (Figure 6)."* Both figure legends say it in their **titles** — *"do not
   exhibit greater BAL cytokines"* — and **read as images at 600 dpi there is no asterisk on any of
   the twelve bars of Figure 4 or any of the twelve bars of Figure 6 — twenty-four across the
   pair.** The absence is the finding: this is significance genuinely not reached, not significance
   left unmarked. 🔴 *Count corrected 2026-09-14 (`ALDAZ-DOSSIER-FIX-C-20260914`): this sentence
   said **"six bars"** per figure. `ALDAZ-VERIFY-W4` §§2.1–2.2 re-cut both figures at 600 dpi panel
   by panel and measured **four bars per panel** — WWOX flox and WWOX KO at PBS, WWOX flox and WWOX
   KO at the challenge — so 4 × 3 panels = **twelve per figure**, and **twenty-four across Figures 4
   and 6**, with no asterisk on any of them. The correction makes this negative **BROADER than first
   written, not narrower**: no mark over twenty-four bars is a stronger absence than no mark over
   twelve, so the finding is strengthened. The likely cause of the undercount is visible in the
   panels — the PBS pairs sit at or near zero and are easy to discount.*
10. **`DATO` — the one place inflammation *does* rise is ex vivo, in isolated cells.** Culture
    supernatants of MRSA-stimulated ECs from KO mice: IL-6, KC and MCP-1 all significantly increased
    (Fig 8A–C, asterisk on all three).
11. **`IPOTESI` (the authors', typed as theirs) — an inflammation-independent mechanism.** *"This
    observation suggests that additional inflammation-independent mechanisms exist by which EC WWOX
    deficiency may lead to increased vascular leak."* This is the authors' interpretation of their own
    null, not a result.

## 4 · What it does **not** establish

- **It does not establish that WWOX loss causes worse ARDS.** The title's own verb is
  *"is Associated with"*. The exposure arms and the genetic arms are **different experiments in
  different animals**: no mouse in this paper was both smoke/vapour-exposed and WWOX-manipulated,
  and no exposure arm has an injury endpoint. The paper says so and calls the missing experiment
  future work — *"The development of a dual-hit e-cigarette exposure and MRSA-induced ARDS animal
  model is needed to confirm this association."*
- 🔴 **The question in my brief, answered directly: the association between WWOX downregulation and
  injury severity is NOT a measured effect of WWOX loss.** It is an **inference bridging two
  separate experiments** — exposure→WWOX-down in one set of animals, WWOX-KO→more leak in another.
  It is *not even* a correlation between two consequences of one exposure, because severity was
  never measured in any exposed animal. There is no dose-response, no mediation analysis and no
  rescue.
- **It does not establish a mechanism for the downregulation.** *"The current study also does not
  pursue the mechanisms of CS-induced WWOX downregulation."*
- **It does not quantify human exposure.** *"There are no additional data regarding the intensity or
  duration of current/prior smoking habits."* The 47% attaches to a binary label; no dose-response
  of any kind is supported.
- **It does not show WWOX protein loss quantitatively** (see §3.2).
- **It does not measure mortality, gas exchange or any physiological outcome** — "severity" here is
  BAL leak and histology at 18 h.
- 🔴 **It does not show that WWOX loss de-represses inflammatory signalling in the lung.** It
  measures exactly that, twice, and does not find it. The only positive inflammatory readout is in
  **isolated cultured cells**, and no signalling pathway is measured anywhere: there is no NF-κB
  readout, no phospho-blot, no reporter, no transcriptome. The single pathway actually interrogated —
  JNK — was **pharmacologically inhibited and did not rescue**.
- **Cell-autonomous vs tissue-level:** the knockout arm is **tissue-level with a cell-type-restricted
  lesion**. `Cdh5-Cre` restricts the *lesion* to endothelium, which is much stronger than a global
  knockdown, but every *in vivo* endpoint is a whole-lung measurement (BAL, histology) in an intact
  animal with circulating leukocytes. **Cell-autonomy is established separately and only in vitro**,
  by the isolated-EC arms (Fig 7, Fig 8) — and those are the arms where the inflammatory result flips
  from null to positive. So: cell-autonomous *for barrier function in culture*; tissue-level *in
  vivo*; and the inflammatory positive exists **only** in the cell-autonomous system.

## 7 · Design limits

- **n = 3 per arm**, with the same legends also saying *"n = 3 independent experiments"* — so whether
  the unit is the mouse or the experiment is ambiguous in the paper's own words. The authors
  themselves offer the underpowering as the explanation for the cytokine null: *"It is possible that
  with greater numbers of mice and therefore greater statistical power, the increase in cytokines …
  will be found to be modestly greater with statistical significance."* 🔴 **That is a hypothesis
  about an unrun experiment, and it must not be read as a positive result. `PREMISE: INFERENZA`.**
- **No multiplicity correction** across 12 cytokine comparisons.
- **A knockdown (~85%), not a null**, and tamoxifen-induced in mature animals — so nothing here
  speaks to development.
- **Single timepoint, 18 h.**
- **Protein never quantified**; no densitometry on any blot in the paper.
- **The two halves of the title are never joined in one animal.**
- **Heat-killed MRSA** for the cell work and live MRSA for mice — stated, and not the same stimulus.
- **Group:** Singla's laboratory is primary for ARDS and lung endothelial barrier biology (19
  publications under `Singla Sunit[fau]`, 4 on WWOX) — the bare `Singla S[au]` count of 794 is not
  one person and is not used. Aldaz is primary for the **gene** (66 publications, 28 on WWOX) and his

  made against the group's own prior interest and is strengthened by that — while

## 9 · Transfer toward the reference genotype — stated separately from the tissue-level finding

**The tissue-level finding (lung/endothelium):** endothelial WWOX loss, on a ~85% knockdown induced
in adult mice, worsens pulmonary vascular barrier failure under LPS or MRSA challenge, without a
detectable increase in lung inflammatory cytokines, and with an intrinsic barrier deficit visible in
isolated cells even unchallenged.

**What transfers toward a WWOX-DEE reference genotype: essentially nothing mechanistic, and one
thing methodological.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Does not transfer — tissue** | Pulmonary microvascular endothelium is not CNS endothelium; there is no blood-brain barrier, no astrocyte, no pericyte and no tight-junction CNS phenotype anywhere in this paper. A tissue-level inflammatory or barrier mechanism in lung **is not a CNS mechanism**, and no measurement here bridges them. |
| **Does not transfer — allele class** | A tamoxifen-induced conditional **knockdown (~85%)** in an adult mouse is not a germline biallelic loss-of-function present from conception. `PREMISE: DATO` that the model differs; any inference across that gap is `ESPANSIONE`. |
| **Does not transfer — timing** | Everything is adult-onset and acute (18 h). WWOX-DEE is a neurodevelopmental disorder. The paper measures nothing developmental. |
| **Does not transfer — the inflammatory direction** | 🔴 This is the important one. Even taken at face value, this paper's *in vivo* inflammatory result is a **null**. It therefore supplies **no** support for a neuroinflammatory arm of a WWOX model — and an argument that used it as one would be using it backwards. |
| **Transfers as `ESPANSIONE`, to be tested and not assumed** | That WWOX loss can produce an **endothelial/barrier** phenotype at all is a lead worth holding, because a CNS barrier is also endothelial. But it is a lead about a *cell type*, not a finding about brain, and it is weakened by the fact that the strongest barrier signal here appears only in isolated cultured cells. |
| **Transfers methodologically** | The paper demonstrates a clean way to separate *inflammation* from *barrier failure* — measure both in the same animals — and its result shows the two can dissociate. That dissociation is the transferable idea. |

**Stated plainly, because the brief asks for it as a deliverable and not as a hedge: this is a lung
paper. Its mechanism does not reach the CNS, and its central inflammatory result is negative.**
