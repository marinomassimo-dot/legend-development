## The single most important finding of this reading

**"WWOX binds Tau via its C-terminal SDR domain" occurs exactly once in this paper — in its abstract — and zero times in its body.** (Measured over the fingerprinted artefact.) What the body states, in section 6.1, is the weaker and differently-shaped claim: *"SDR domain binds and blocks GSK-3β-mediated tau hyperphosphorylation and thereby promotes neuronal differentiation"*, attributed to references 41 and 49.

Those two references are:
- **ref 41** — Sze CI *et al.*, *J Biol Chem* 2004, "Downregulation of WOX1 induces Tau phosphorylation in vitro" (PMID 15126504). Chang NS is an author. LEGEND's own earlier triage recorded this paper as **abstract-only** in this corpus.
- **ref 49** — Wang HY *et al.*, *Cell Death Differ* 2012, "WW domain-containing oxidoreductase promotes neuronal differentiation via negative regulation of GSK-3β" (PMID 22193544). Chang NS is a co-author.

There is **no third, independent primary** for a direct WWOX–tau binding through the SDR domain. For an SDR-destabilising allele class this is the load-bearing sentence of the whole mechanism, and in this source it is a review abstract's sentence resting on two same-laboratory primaries, one of which LEGEND has not read in full.

## Table 1

These are **proposals**, not results, and the WOREE sentence is the only place in the group where the therapeutic programme is pointed at a WWOX-DEE phenotype at all — on no data.
