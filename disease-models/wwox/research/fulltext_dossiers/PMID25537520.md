## What the review is

A narrative review from the Chang NS / Sze CI laboratory (Tainan). Sections: WWOX in neuronal death signalling;
in neurological disease pathology (tau, GSK-3β, TGF-β/TIAF1); in metabolic syndrome and neural development;
conclusion. 54 references, **18 of them carrying Chang NS as author**. No methods, no new data, no tables; four
schematic figures redrawn from cited work.

## Internal contradiction

- Body (neuronal death signalling): *"Whether WWOX regulates the function of transcription factors in neuronal
  survival or death in vivo remains to be established."*
- Conclusion: *"In conclusion, substantial evidence has shown that WWOX participates in the control of the function
  of transcription factors in vivo."*

Both cite the same ref 31. The Conclusion upgrades what the body declines to claim.

## What it says about brain development, white matter and epilepsy

Development and epilepsy: only through refs 26, 27, 48 — all independently held in LEGEND from their primaries.
**White matter / myelin: nothing.** The review never mentions myelin, oligodendrocytes or white matter.
Where it stops talking about WWOX-specific neurology: after the paragraph on the `lde` rat; the remainder is
metabolism, mitochondria and p53 partnership, unsupported by brain data.
