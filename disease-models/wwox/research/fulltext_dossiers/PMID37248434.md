## What the paper did

1. **Yeast two-hybrid screen** (performed with Hybrigenics) using as bait "a WWOX isoform (WWOX variant
   2, WWOXv2, NP_570607) containing only the WW1 and WW2 domains, and a truncated SDR domain",
   against a human placenta library. MERIT40 residues 43–206 were recovered as the interacting prey.
2. **Co-immunoprecipitation** in HEK293 of tagged constructs (Flag-WWOXv2 / HA-WWOX / HA-WWOX-Y33R /
   His-WW1-WW2 / His-WW2-SDR with tagged MERIT40), and **endogenous** co-IP in MCF7 breast cancer
   cells with an anti-WWOX antibody (800 µg total protein input).
3. **DR-GFP homologous-recombination reporter assay** in HeLa DR-GFP cells (I-SceI induced DSB, GFP+
   fraction by flow cytometry), with MERIT40, WWOX, WW1-WW2 truncation and siRNA-WWOX arms; cell-cycle
   and sub-G1 controls by flow cytometry; γ-H2AX immunoblot.
4. **Irradiation-induced MERIT40 nuclear foci** (10 Gy, immunofluorescence; 50 cells per condition,
   "the experiment was done once" — the authors' own words).
5. **Competition for MERIT40**: co-IP of MERIT40 with Tankyrase ± ectopic WWOX / WWOX-Y33R, and
   ± siRNA WWOX.
6. **Clinical correlation**: MERIT40 mRNA by RT-qPCR in 499 primary breast tumours (institutional
   cohort) and in the public TCGA-breast cohort (n = 1025); survival and aneuploidy-score analyses.

**Domain mapping result, in the authors' words:** "We found that the N-terminal region of WWOX
containing only the WW1 and WW2 domains did not interact with MERIT40, whereas the region composed of
the WW2 and SDR domains did (Fig. 1D)." And: "these results … suggest that the domain of WWOX
interacting with MERIT40 is located in the N terminal part of the SDR domain".

## What the paper does NOT state or measure

- **No enzymatic measurement of WWOX whatsoever.** No substrate, no cofactor (no NAD(P)H), no
  oxidoreductase assay, no catalytic-site mutant, no steroid or quinone chemistry. The SDR domain is
  used only as a *fragment boundary* for binding.
- **No affinity measurement.** No Kd, no stoichiometry, no ITC/SPR/NMR; nothing with purified protein.
  Every interaction readout is a co-IP or a two-hybrid, in cells or in yeast.
- **No dead-mutant control for the SDR.** The only point mutant used is Y33R, which is a **WW1**
  ligand-binding mutant, not a catalytic mutant; the paper uses it to exclude PPxY-mediated binding,
  and it does not affect the MERIT40 interaction.
- No WWOX disease allele is studied: **no P47T, no Q230P, no G372R, no A141T, no P252A**, and no
  neurological phenotype. The constructs are wild-type WWOX, WWOXv2, Y33R and two fragments.
- The directness of the interaction is **not demonstrated biochemically**; the authors attribute it to
  "E. Formstecher personal communications" ("because this interaction was detected in yeast, it is very
  likely that it is a direct interaction").
- The claim that WWOX promotes BRCA1-A formation is cited as "(unpublished data)".
- The foci experiment is explicitly a single experiment.
- The paper reports no WWOX expression data of its own in the 499-tumour cohort beyond its use in the
  TCGA "altered expression(s)" grouping; the clinical analysis is MERIT40-centred.

## Caveats the authors themselves give

- "The effect of WWOX on HR seems therefore to depend on the cell type" — they state outright that
  Aqeilan et al. found WWOX *activates* HR in U2OS while they and Schrock et al. find inhibition.
- "(50 cells were counted in each condition, the experiment was done once)" (Fig. 3B legend).
- "Further analysis have to be perform to validate these hypotheses." (Discussion, final sentence.)
- Tankyrase competition is inferred from the overlap of TBM2 with the two-hybrid-mapped binding region,
  not from a mapped WWOX–TBM2 contact.

## Independence of the laboratory

The WWOX chain this wave is auditing (motif annotation → SDR-substrate claim) runs through
Bednarek/Aqeilan/Chang–Aldaz. This paper is from an independent Paris group (Lallemand/Driouch/Bièche,
Institut Curie) with its own anti-WWOX antibody and its own two-hybrid pipeline. It is therefore an
**orthogonal measurement of a WWOX interaction** — but of a *protein–protein* interaction, not of
enzymology or ligand binding in the catalytic sense.
