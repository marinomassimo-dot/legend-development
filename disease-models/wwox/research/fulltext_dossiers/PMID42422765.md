## What the pass added (glucose only)

1. 2. Figure 1 as drawn (P14, n 5 to 6): untreated knockout about 58 mg/dL against WT about 137. A CMV-driven vector normalises glucose (about 131) while every treated animal still dies by about P35; an EF1-alpha vector gives a partial correction (about 102) with death by about P20; an MBP vector does nothing (about 50); the synapsin vector normalises glucose and survives. Neuronal glycaemic correction therefore does not predict lethality: glucose normalises in animals that still die, so hypoglycemia is not shown to be the lethal driver, and survival and glucose are both rescued only by the same neuron-restricted, durable expression.
