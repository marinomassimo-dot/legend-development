## 1 · What the paper measures

Retrospective post hoc analysis of two Taiwanese ADI-PEG 20 trial cohorts in advanced hepatocellular carcinoma (113 on monotherapy, 47 with chemotherapy) for three germline SNPs (WWOX rs13338697, rs6025211, GALNT14 rs9679162) against OS, time to response and time to progression; a 214-patient resected cohort for prognostic value; WWOX and ASS1 protein (western blot, IHC) in resected tumour and non-tumour tissue by genotype; baseline plasma arginine and citrulline; shRNA WWOX silencing under CoCl2 in three HCC lines with ADI-PEG 20 dose-response. WWOX is a marker, not a disease gene here.

## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. Cohort-1 (monotherapy): GG genotype OS 15.1 versus 8.1 months (Cox P 0.025; log-rank 0.020); Figure 1B shows GG is 9 patients of 113.
2. Pooled cohorts 1 and 2 (160 patients): the WWOX GG effect is not significant (P 0.127; GG n 14, Figure 3B); only tumour number is an independent OS predictor. In cohort-2 nothing predicts outcome.
3. Surgical cohort: genotype not prognostic.
4. GG carriers have lower tumour-over-non-tumour WWOX and ASS1 (12 GG patients, P 0.02 to 0.05) and higher baseline plasma arginine (8 GG against 104 non-GG, P 0.006).
5. In cells, WWOX silencing alone does not change ASS1; ASS1 falls after CoCl2 and more with silencing; ADI-PEG 20 alone only attenuates growth, more so with WWOX silenced.
6. Authors state a small sample, a rare GG genotype and an unknown mechanism linking genotype to expression. Three authors are employed by the drug developer.

## 3 · Comparison with the laboratory's records

- The paper builds on the hepatic Wwox-HIF1A result (mouse hepatocyte deletion, HIF1A up). Here the extension is correlational and in tumours; it adds one non-obvious axis, ASS1 and arginine, but the genotype-to-WWOX-protein step rests on 12 GG patients and the cellular step needs a hypoxia mimetic.
- Selection question 3 of group G2 and the lactate and arginine readouts: if a WWOX LoF state shifted arginine handling in tissue, plasma arginine would be a cheap serum readout; this paper gives a hint in cancer patients (higher arginine in a rare SNP class), no measurement in any WWOX-deficient neural or developmental setting.

## 4 · What the paper does NOT support

- A durable WWOX-genotype predictor of ADI-PEG 20 benefit (lost on pooling; nine patients in the one significant stratum; no correction for three SNPs by three endpoints by three cohorts).
- A causal chain WWOX loss, HIF1A rise, ASS1 fall (silencing alone does not lower ASS1).
- Anything about germline WWOX LoF disease: rs13338697 is a common-variant tag with no functional data.

## 5 · Transfer verdict toward the reference genotype (WWOX-DEE)

ESPANSIONE. System gap: tumour pharmacogenetics. Value: arginine and ASS1 as additional metabolic nodes a WWOX LoF panel could include, and a reminder that WWOX SNP effects in cancer cohorts rarely survive pooling.
