## What the paper is

A broad review of hyaluronan (HA): synthesis and catabolism, HA-binding proteins and receptors, cancer signalling, the nervous system, and a final section on complement C1q, WWOX and Alzheimer's disease. It is written by the laboratory that originated the HYAL-2/WWOX/SMAD4 and C1q–WWOX work and re-presents that laboratory's own figures.

## WWOX content (all of it)

- **Where WWOX appears:** abstract, section 1.6 (HA, HYAL-2, WWOX, SMAD4 and apoptosis), one figure node in 3.2, and section 6 (C1q, WWOX, AD), plus discussion and conclusions; 55 mentions in the text layer. **Section 5, "The Nervous System" (neurodevelopment, neurogenic niches, perineuronal nets, homeostasis, injury), contains no WWOX mention at all.**
- **Every WWOX experiment shown is in DU145 prostate cancer cells**, most with over-expressed tagged constructs: Figure 1 (FRET of transfected SMAD4/HYAL-2/WWOX, bubbling cell death) and Figure 4 A–F (complement-depleted sera; EGFP-WWOX microvilli; apoptosis). Figure 4G–H, which link WWOX to amyloid and neurodegeneration, are schematics, one with a question mark. No neuron, glia, brain tissue or animal datum for WWOX is in the review.
- **The authors' own limit:** "Whether the HYAL-2/WWOX/SMAD4 complex is overexpressed in vivo is unknown"; apoptosis is stated to need over-expression.
- **Citation that does not support its sentence:** "WWOX ... is a risk factor for Alzheimer's disease [5,29,204,205,206]". None of the five is an Alzheimer genetic or clinical study: ref 5 (HA/Hyal-2/WWOX/Smad4 bubbling death), ref 29 (hyaluronan metabolism in lung-cancer tumour-initiating cells), refs 204–205 (WWOX-deficient cancer cells; a WWOX surface epitope), ref 206 (C1q activates WWOX in prostate cancer cells).
- "pS14-WWOX promotes the progression of Alzheimer's disease" cites one review from the same laboratory (ref 207).

## Other integrity points

- Figure 1B's chart title names p53 where the legend and the panel's own schematic describe SMAD4/HYAL-2/WWOX; n values (4, 5, 2) are printed without a unit.
- A patent on modified hyaluronan for cancer treatment held by the senior author is listed in section 9, while the review declares no conflicts of interest. Data "available from" the senior author's laboratory.
- Section 4 restates that high-molecular-weight HA is anti-inflammatory and low-molecular-weight HA pro-inflammatory; the discussion says that view "is not correct".

## What the paper does NOT state

- Any WWOX measurement in neural cells or tissue.
- Anything about WWOX loss-of-function genotypes, WWOX-DEE or SCAR12. The paradigm is over-expression, the opposite of the reference genotype class.

## Transfer limit

Review of one laboratory's cancer-cell work. It adds no neural evidence and no independence to the HYAL-2/WWOX/SMAD4 axis. Its neural framing is an extrapolation stated as such ("probably").
