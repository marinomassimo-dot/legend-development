## What the paper did
- Vector: AAV2 genome in the engineered ShH10 Y445F capsid; CMV-EGFP for tropism, and a 1:1 mix of two SaCas9 guides against mouse Aqp1 (AAV-MIX-AQP1).
- In vitro: choroid-plexus explants from mouse, rat and pig, vector added at 3.189 x 10^10 genome copies per ml of medium; EGFP read to day 10 (Fig. 1).
- In vivo: single unilateral injection, left lateral ventricle, adult female C57BL/6J mice, 10 microlitres at 0.5 microlitre per minute. Low dose 3.5 x 10^10 genomes (EGFP, 3 weeks; Figs. 2-3). High dose 1.0 x 10^11 genomes (SaCas9, 6 weeks; Figs. 5-6).
- Readouts: native EGFP fluorescence across sections (qualitative), SURVEYOR indel assay (Fig. 5), AQP1 optical density by confocal immunofluorescence at six rostro-caudal levels (Fig. 6; four animals per group on the rendered panels).

## What it measured (class-level, route-level)
- Tropism: EGFP in the outer choroid-plexus epithelial layer with apical AQP1, in all three species in vitro and in mouse in vivo.
- Distribution after one unilateral injection: strongest ipsilateral choroid plexus 0.8-1.0 mm caudal to the needle; contralateral weaker; third-ventricle choroid plexus very poorly infected; fourth ventricle comparatively better than third (low dose, qualitative).
- Editing (high dose): indels in injected and contralateral lateral-ventricle choroid plexus; contralateral about two thirds of injected side (Fig. 5b: about 20 and 13 percent by the plotted means, ratio about 0.66; p = 0.017 between sides).
- AQP1 protein removal (Fig. 6, text values recomputed from the panel means, agree within reading error): lateral ventricle -0.15 mm 59 percent ipsilateral / 39 percent contralateral; -1.8 mm 78 / 54 percent; -2.1 mm 45-49 percent both sides; third ventricle -0.98 mm about 37 percent; third ventricle -1.8 mm 26 percent, not significant (p = 0.168); fourth ventricle 3 percent, not significant (p = 0.885).

## What the authors state
Distribution is uneven toward the injected lateral ventricle; overcoming it "represents a challenge for clinical translation"; dose escalation without toxicity "has not yet been examined in detail"; scaling to human brains may be non-linear; secreted-protein designs may succeed before knockdown designs because they need less complete epithelial coverage.

## What it does NOT state
- WWOX is named zero times. Nothing about a WWOX allele, WWOX expression in choroid plexus, or WWOX phenotype.
- No toxicity, immune, neutralising-antibody or histopathology readout; no dose-response series (two doses, two different transgenes and durations, so dose and transgene are confounded).
- No neuronal or parenchymal biodistribution measure; the work is choroid-plexus-epithelium only.
- No large-animal in vivo data (pig is explant only).

## Transfer limit (exact)
Mouse, one capsid, reporter and SaCas9 payloads, ChPE target. It bears on a WWOX-CNS attempt only as a route-level caution that single-site ICV delivery does not reach all choroid plexus evenly in the mouse. It does not bear on neuronal transduction, WWOX dose, or any human allele. Not medical advice.
