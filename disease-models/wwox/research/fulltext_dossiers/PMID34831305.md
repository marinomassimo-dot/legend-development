## Core scientific verdict

This paper does not create a new datum. Its value is that a primary WWOX experimental group
places multiple model systems on one causal map. The strongest synthesis is not merely that
neurons are affected: mature-neuron deletion is reported to reproduce neurological **and
systemic** null phenotypes, neuronal deletion perturbs oligodendrocyte maturation
non-cell-autonomously, and neonatal neuron-restricted restoration is reported to rescue CNS,
bone, testis, glycaemia, growth and survival. Thus, within the reviewed mouse evidence, the
tissue where a phenotype is measured is not a reliable locator of the tissue whose WWOX is
necessary or sufficient.

That conclusion is `INFERENZA` at the level of this review. The primary Repudi papers carry
the experimental burden; this read cannot inherit their DATO status merely by enumerating
them.

## Deep biological dive

### DATO about the review surface

- The review names four cell-targeted deletions: Nestin-Cre, Synapsin-I-Cre, GFAP-Cre and
  Olig2-Cre. It says only Nestin and Synapsin models recapitulated the null phenotype during
  the reported interval.
- It summarizes neuron-to-oligodendrocyte coupling as a maturation defect: mature OLs fall,
  OPCs rise, and myelination declines after neuronal Wwox loss.
- It distinguishes a human developmental compartment absent or underdeveloped in rodents:
  outer radial glia/oSVZ, while early organoid WWOX is concentrated in ventricular radial glia.
- It summarizes transcriptomic enrichment in ATP-linked electron transport, oxidative
  phosphorylation, glycolysis/gluconeogenesis, cell-cycle control and Wnt regionalization in
  WWOX-KO organoids. These are expression programs, not flux measurements.
- It reports diffuse AAVS1 rescue as broad but incomplete and mature lentiviral re-expression
  as sufficient to reverse electrophysiological findings.

### INFERENZA

1. **Tissue of measurement ≠ tissue of necessity.** Neuronal perturbation can generate glial
   and peripheral readouts; neuronal restoration can reverse them. Blood glucose, bone or
   myelin therefore cannot identify the causal tissue by themselves.
2. **Neuron is an upstream control node, not necessarily the only autonomous lesion.** The
   same review notes abundant Wwox in OPCs and other lineages. Neuronal sufficiency for rescue
   does not prove absence of parallel autonomous roles elsewhere.
3. **Developmental stage matters.** Early vRG restriction followed by broader expression means
   that a promoter optimal at one stage may miss the causal compartment at another.

### IPOTESI / expansion

- A factorial primary experiment crossing cell-type deletion with reciprocal cell-type rescue
  and tissue-resolved endpoints would separate necessity, sufficiency and downstream readout.
- Tissue-resolved metabolic flux, rather than peripheral chemistry alone, is needed to decide
  whether the organoid transcriptional signature is a neural metabolic phenotype.

## Tensions and hidden gold

- **Synthesis inflation:** the review describes lithium as suppressing PTZ seizures in the KO
  context. The already persisted image-level audit of primary PMID 32000863 found lithium
  suppression displayed in WT, heterozygous and KO panels. The review therefore cannot support
  Wwox-specific pharmacological rescue.
- **“Efficient and safe” exceeds the review's evidence:** there are no human treatment data or
  formal safety experiments in this source. It is a preclinical design hypothesis.
- **Model compression:** Table 1 and Figure 2 collapse distinct strains, cell-targeted models
  and negative arms. This improves readability but obscures which model supports which causal
  statement.
- **Transfer tension:** one severe human report had a normal metabolic screen, while systemic
  null rodents had metabolic abnormalities and organoids had metabolic transcript programs.
  Peripheral normality does not exclude a neural energetic defect, but no biomarker follows
  directly from the review.

## Genotype/model/stage transfer

- **Genotype:** LOW–MODERATE. Most mechanistic anchors are complete-null models; the reference
  N/M class may retain allele-specific function.
- **Model:** MODERATE for causal architecture, LOW for quantitative translation. Rodents,
  organoids and human cases disagree on several regional, cellular and systemic features.
- **Stage:** LOW for treatment timing. Neonatal mouse rescue and embryonic-like organoids do
  not define a human therapeutic window.
- **Overall relevance:** HIGH as a research map; BACKGROUND as independent claim evidence.
