## 1 · What the paper did

Conditional deletion of murine *Wwox* in neurons (Syn1-Cre; **S-KO**), beside a Nestin-Cre
conditional (**N-KO**), a constitutive null, and — the arm that matters here and that no LEGEND
record mentions — a **Syn1-Cre heterozygote (S-HT)**. Readouts: in vivo and slice
electrophysiology (P13–P17), myelin transcripts, immunostaining for mature oligodendrocytes
(CC1) and OPCs (PDGFRα), transmission electron microscopy of corpus callosum and optic nerve at
P17, axonal conductivity, and *WWOX*-deleted human brain organoids.

## 3 · What it states

Neuronal *Wwox* loss produces hyperexcitability, intractable epilepsy, ataxia, postnatal
lethality, a myelin-transcript deficit, impaired oligodendrocyte maturation, fewer and thinner
myelinated axons, and delayed axonal conductivity; hypomyelination is reproduced in *WWOX*-deleted
human brain organoids. Because the deletion is neuronal, the oligodendrocyte phenotype is
non-cell-autonomous **by construction of the experiment**.

## 4 · What it does NOT state

- It does not test whether WWOX is *also* required inside the oligodendrocyte lineage. No
  oligodendroglial deletion, no rescue in that lineage, no co-culture.
- It does not report a statistical test for the heterozygote against the control on any measure;
  the 17 % figure is a proportion, and the spectral contrast that reaches significance is always
  S-KO.
- It reports no myelin, imaging or behavioural endpoint for the heterozygote at all.
- It makes no claim about remyelination, injury or repair: every measurement is developmental
  (P13–P17).

## 2.2 · (b) The heterozygote arm — what exists, and what is not tested

The Synapsin-Cre heterozygote (**S-HT**, *Wwox*⁺/flox; Syn1-Cre⁺) appears in exactly four places:

- **Slice bursting.** 0 % of S-Control slices (0/11, 7 animals), **17 % of S-HT slices (4/23, 14
  animals)**, 86 % of S-KO slices (36/42, 24 animals). The Figure 2B legend restates the same
  experiment by animal — four slices from **three** S-HT animals (~20 %), 36 slices from **20** S-KO
  animals (~84 %) — so the two percentages in text and legend have different denominators and both
  are correct.
- **Spectral power.** In vivo n = 7 S-HT subjects, in vitro n = 11 slices from 8 animals. The S-HT is
  present only as the *comparator of the homozygote*: "In vivo 12–20 Hz … elevated power in S-KO as
  compared with S-HT"; "For 0–4.9 Hz and 5–9 Hz, S-KO power was significantly elevated as compared
  with S-Control only."
- **Evoked response.** Figure 2F: first peak carries one bracket (S-Ctrl vs S-KO, `*`); second peak
  carries S-Ctrl vs S-KO (`**`) and S-HT vs S-KO (`*`). **No S-HT vs S-Ctrl bracket on either peak.**
- **Supplementary Fig. 3B.** The paper's only published heterozygote in-vivo trace set: six depths
  from 150 to 2000 µm beside the S-KO, no bursts visible at any depth, one animal, no quantification.

**What is NOT tested, stated exactly.** No statistical comparison of S-HT against S-Control is
reported on any measure in this paper. No myelin endpoint, no imaging endpoint, no electron
microscopy, no oligodendrocyte count and no quantified behaviour exists for the heterozygote.

🔴 **And one wave-4 sentence is too strong.** The wave-4 reading recorded that "no … behavioural
endpoint is reported for the heterozygote at all". The paper does assert one — "The heterozygote
(*Wwox*⁺/flox, *Synapsin Cre*⁺) carrying one intact allele of *Wwox* did not show any abnormal
phenotypes and was behaviourally indistinguishable from control mice" — with no data, no n and no
test behind it. The honest statement is *asserted but not measured*, which is a different and weaker
object than *absent*. The Nestin heterozygote gets the same treatment, and there the authors take the
further step of reading it across to humans: "did not show any visible abnormal phenotype, which is
in accordance with the absence of neurological symptoms in heterozygous carriers in human
individuals." An unmeasured mouse null is being aligned with an unmeasured human null.

Panel 2D adds a bound that no sentence in the paper carries: in vivo the S-HT spectrum runs at or
above the S-KO below ~5 Hz, while in vitro it runs **below** the S-Control. The heterozygote is not a
clean intermediate on this measure, and the direction of its departure differs between preparations.

## 2.3 · (c) Cell autonomy — what is deleted where, and what each conditional shows

| Driver | Genotype | What is deleted | What is shown | What is NOT done |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nestin-Cre | N-KO | neural stem/progenitors from E10.5 (neurons **and** glia) | growth retardation, tremors/seizures, ataxia, 100 % lethality by 4 weeks, hypomyelination with EM counts and qPCR | no electrophysiology; no organoid; the heterozygote only "visibly" normal |
| Synapsin I-Cre | S-KO | differentiated neurons from E12.5 | the whole phenotype: seizures from P9, hyperexcitability in vivo and in slice, transcriptome, hypomyelination, g-ratio, conductivity | nothing in oligodendrocytes is deleted, so nothing here tests an oligodendrocyte requirement |
| Olig2-Cre | **O-KO** | oligodendrocyte lineage | **no phenotype**: weight and survival to ~120 days (O-Control n = 11, O-KO n = 10, log-rank P = 1.0), normal hind-limb clasping, MBP "comparable" at P17 | **no EM, no g-ratio, no axon count, no CC1/OPC count, no electrophysiology, no age beyond P17** |
| GFAP-Cre | G-KO | astrocytes | no phenotype (G-Control n = 8, G-KO n = 9, log-rank P = 1.0) | same list |
| constitutive | *Wwox*-null | everything | hypomyelination, CC1↓/PDGFRα↑, qPCR panel | — |

**The cell-autonomy argument, reconstructed from what it actually rests on.** (i) WWOX protein is
stated to be *intact* in CC1⁺ oligodendrocytes of the S-KO (Suppl. Fig. 1D, an image with no
quantification of residual signal); (ii) the S-KO nonetheless hypomyelinates; (iii) wild-type OPCs
plated on *Wwox*-null DRG neurons differentiate less (n = 4 per condition, two experiments); (iv)
O-KO and G-KO have "no phenotype". The conclusion drawn is a non-cell-autonomous neuronal function.

Three limits, all from the paper's own pages:

- **The O-KO negative is the weakest arm in the paper and carries the most weight.** It is a single
  unquantified MBP comparison at P17 plus survival and weight. A myelin phenotype that is subtle, or
  that needs ageing or a challenge, could not have been seen by anything that was done. "We cannot
  exclude the cell autonomous functions of WWOX in oligodendrocytes" is the authors' own sentence,
  and it is the correct reading of their design — not a courtesy.
- **The in-vitro arm uses a different genotype and a different neuron class.** The co-culture
  neurons are embryonic **dorsal root ganglion** neurons from the **constitutive null**, not
  Synapsin-Cre cortical neurons. The demonstration of neuron→OPC signalling is real and is not in
  the same system as the in-vivo phenotype it explains.
- **The deletion itself is incomplete in cortex at the transcript level.** Supplementary Table 1's
  per-sample columns give residual *Wwox* normalised counts of 172.7 / 187.0 / 331.4 in S-KO cortex
  against 18.8 / 18.8 / 28.0 in S-KO hippocampus (controls 252–993 and 113–124). Cortical samples
  contain non-neuronal cells, so this is expected — and it is never stated, while cortex is where
  the CNP/MBP intensity quantification is done.

## 2.4 · (d) The human brain organoid result

CRISPR/Cas9 *WWOX* knockout in the WiBR3 human embryonic stem cell line, differentiated to
oligocortical spheroids. What is measured, with its n:

- **Week 15 electrophysiology.** Resting membrane potential −52.1 ± 0.90 mV (OS-WT, n = 3 organoids)
  versus −21.28 ± 6.91 mV (OS-WWOX-KO, n = 4); LFP power area under the curve 0.0285 ± 0.0097 versus
  0.0541 ± 0.0093 over 0.5–7.9 Hz (n = 9 slices/3 organoids vs 6 slices/2 organoids), Student's *t*.
- **Week 14 / 20 / 30 immunofluorescence.** CC1⁺ down with OPCs unchanged; CNP⁺ down with NG2
  unchanged; MBP and CNP down at week 30 (n = 3–5 per genotype).
- **Week 30 qPCR.** MBP and CNP down (`**`), PDGFRA up (`**`), SOX10 unchanged with a very wide error
  bar, NG2 and MYRF unchanged.
- **Week 37 electron microscopy.** A percentage-of-axons bar from **n = 2 OS-WT and n = 3
  OS-WWOX-KO**, with no error bar, no test and no axon count printed.

**Bounds that belong to the datum.** The two lines are a knockout and its parental wild type, not an
isogenic pair of a patient allele; both genotypes are explicitly immature at week 15 ("less negative
than typical mature neurons … still in the process of development"), so the RMP difference is read by
the authors themselves as developmental delay; the dispersion of the KO RMP is 7.7× the wild type's
with n = 3 vs 4; and the myelin bar rests on five organoids. This is a convergent human-system
observation, not an independent replication of the mouse quantities — and it is the same laboratory.

## 2.6 · (f) What the paper does not say

- It does not test WWOX inside the oligodendrocyte lineage beyond one unquantified MBP image at P17.
- It reports **no statistical comparison of either heterozygote against its control**, and no
  non-electrophysiological endpoint for a heterozygote.
- It states **no correction for multiple comparisons** anywhere; the whole paper is two-tailed
  unpaired Student's *t* (with log-rank for survival and Wilcoxon rank-sum for the evoked responses),
  and the headline "730 upregulated and 579 downregulated" is explicitly an **unadjusted** p < 0.01
  count — it reproduces exactly from Supplementary Table 1 on the raw p-value and not on the adjusted
  one (padj < 0.01 gives 281 / 280).
- It does not report sex anywhere except as "either male or female" in the electrophysiology methods;
  no analysis is split by sex.
- It does not measure WWOX **protein** in any model — no immunoblot of any kind appears; every
  WWOX measurement is immunofluorescence or transcript.
- It does not report a seizure count, a seizure frequency or an EEG in a freely moving animal: the
  in-vivo recordings are under ketamine–xylazine anaesthesia, and the only unanaesthetised seizure
  data are six videotaped S-KO animals with a duration (68.5 ± 13.4 s).
- It does not deposit its sequencing data. There **is** a data-availability statement — "available
  from the corresponding author upon reasonable request" — and no accession for either RNA-seq
  dataset. (This corrects the September receipt's "no data-availability statement"; the statement
  exists, deposits nothing, and the two spreadsheets remain the only public form of the data.)
- It does not name an anti-rabbit secondary antibody, although four of its primaries are rabbit and
  all four are imaged; its anti-WWOX row carries no catalogue number and an empty source cell; and
  there is no *Wwox* or housekeeping primer pair among the nineteen listed.
- It makes no claim about remyelination, repair, injury or any age beyond ~4 weeks in the mouse: the
  conditionals die by then.
- It does not test, in any model, whether rescuing myelin rescues seizures, or the reverse — it says
  explicitly that why hypomyelination should cause local hyperexcitability "remains unclear".
