## What it measures

System: human HCC cell lines (Huh7, HCCLM3, MHCC97H, PLC/PRF/5), PMA-differentiated THP-1 cells, humanized NOG mice (huPBMC) bearing orthotopic HCC derived from those cells; tissue microarray of 176 HCC patients; serum from 57 PD-1-treated HCC patients; tumour RNA-seq and CyTOF in 8 patients. WWOX genotype: shRNA knockdown or lentiviral overexpression in the cell lines. Readouts: tumour weight and volume, CD8 and CD68/CD206 staining, M2 markers in co-cultured THP-1, untargeted metabolomics and an ELISA of oleic acid (OA) in conditioned medium, SCD5 and NME2 expression, co-IP and DNA pulldown.

## What it shows (DATO)

- WWOX knockdown in Huh7 raises OA in the conditioned medium by about 1.3-fold (ELISA about 7.5 to 9.8 ng/mL; metabolomics log2 intensity about 26.9 to 27.2, n = 5 versus 5); WWOX overexpression in HCCLM3 lowers it (about 10.8 to 6.8 ng/mL). SCD5 mRNA and protein rise with knockdown; SCD1 does not.
- WWOX binds NME2, through WWOX residues 106-414 (the SDR-containing region) and NME2 residues 67-152; WWOX knockdown increases NME2 acetylation at lysine 31 and reduces NME2 binding to the SCD5 promoter.
- In humanized mice, shWWOX tumours are larger in IgG-treated animals (about 0.65 versus 0.25 g) and do not shrink under anti-PD-1; a dual SCD1/SCD5 inhibitor combined with anti-PD-1 lowers tumour weight in Huh7 models.
- Clinical series: serum WWOX is somewhat higher in responders (about 1250 versus 1060 ng/mL, overlapping); tumour WWOX protein negatively associates with CD68 and CD206 in 176 patients.

## What it does NOT support (the negative is the product)

0. Replication note: the direction of the OA shift is repeated across four lines (two knockdowns and two overexpressions, Supplementary Figure 3B and Figure 4C), always about 1.3-fold up with loss and 0.6 to 0.8-fold with gain. That is a consistent but small and in-medium effect; it does not alter points 1-8.
1. **The "WWOX loss" is a knockdown or overexpression in tumour cell lines, not a loss-of-function organism.** No germline allele, no neural, glial or developmental setting, no lipidomics of any tissue or serum from a WWOX-reduced animal. OA is measured only in conditioned culture medium (10 percent FBS-containing medium, a source of fatty acids the paper does not discuss).
2. **The oleate effect size is small and the dose-response link is not given.** About 1.3-fold up and 0.63-fold down in ng/mL in medium (tens of nM); the oleate used to polarise macrophages (Figure 4D) has no concentration in the text or in the supplementary methods read, and the supplementary ELISA method describes only the WWOX kit, not an OA kit or its extraction. Whether the tumour-cell-released amount reaches a polarising dose is therefore not shown. "Most significantly upregulated" is a rank within the roughly twenty features shown in the Figure 4A heatmap, not a size.
3. **PD-1 "resistance" is confounded with growth.** shWWOX tumours are about 2.6 times heavier than control without PD-1 (Figure 1G); that PD-1 does not shrink them is read against a baseline of much faster growth.
4. **The macrophage rescue lacks a control arm.** The clophosome experiment (Figure 3F) shows four groups, all under anti-PD-1; with clophosome the shWWOX tumours fall to control size. No IgG plus clophosome arm is shown, so macrophage depletion acting on growth independently of PD-1 is not excluded.
5. **The SCD5 inhibitor experiment does not test WWOX dependence.** The Methods describe Huh7 cell-derived models for the inhibitor, with no shWWOX arm; Huh7 is the highest-WWOX line in Figure 3A. The inhibitor lowers tumour weight alone and is named SCD1/5-IN-1 (dual), while the paper's own data say WWOX does not change SCD1. The title and abstract speak of "WWOXlow" tumours.
6. **Direction of the clinical association.** WWOX is higher in responders; knockdown is modelled as the resistance state. A tumour-intrinsic WWOX level correlated with response is not shown to be causal; the clinical n is 4 versus 4 for expression and 28 versus 29 for serum, with a second table whose WWOX-expression row (6/23 and 22/6) sums to 29 and 28 against header totals of 28 and 29 (Supplementary Table 2).
7. **SCD5 has no rodent orthologue in the reference database queried.** From outside this paper (Ensembl REST homology query, human SCD5 gene ENSG00000145284, targets mouse and rat, run 2026-10-19): zero orthologues returned. If that holds, the SCD5 arm cannot be tested in a Wwox-null or Wwox-hypomorph mouse and needs human cells; INFERENZA, with the tool and date as provenance. The paper's humanised model carries human cells, which is why it works there.
8. **No limitations paragraph.**
