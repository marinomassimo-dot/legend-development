## What it is

A mouse study in the pilocarpine status epilepticus model: time course of hippocampal microglial C1q and astrocytic C3 (1 day to 5 weeks), then a single intra-cisterna magna injection of a C1q-neutralising antibody (or isotype IgG) at day 7, with chronic-phase readouts at 5 weeks: interictal spikes on EEG, CA1 neuron counts, PSD95 colocalisation, Golgi spine density, Iba-1/CD68 and GFAP/C3 areas. WWOX: zero occurrences.

## WWOX content

None. **No WWOX-deficient evidence.** Acquired epilepsy in wild-type male mice; the antibody is delivered into the cisternal CSF, not by transfusion or plasma product.

## What it measures (DATO)

C1q peaks in the subacute phase; with anti-C1q at day 7, 5-week interictal spike rate is lower (median about 2 versus about 5 per minute, n = 8 per group), CA1 neuron counts, spine density, and CD68, GFAP and C3 areas are closer to naive.

## What it does NOT support (the negative is the product)

1. **Seizures are not shown to be reduced.** The authors say seizure frequency could not be precisely quantified in 12 hour recordings; the supplement's seizure count is about 0.9 versus 0.1 per 12 hours with p = 0.0816 (not significant). The efficacy claim rests on interictal spikes.
2. **The spectral effect is broadband and nearly total.** Supplementary Figure S2 shows 70-95 percent lower power in all five bands, with gamma close to zero, on selected resting epochs. A change this global invites non-specific explanations (signal quality, state, sedation); none is excluded. INFERENZA.
3. **Timing is asserted, not tested.** Only day 7 was treated; no early or late arm. The Discussion nonetheless argues the subacute window is critical.
4. **Small, partly pseudoreplicated units.** Eight (anti-C1q), five (control IgG) and five (naive) mice for most outcomes; Golgi in 3, 4 and 6 mice with cells (18-50 per group) as the plotted unit; one coronal section per animal; the methods describe six mice for spike epochs where Figure 2 shows eight per group. Two of eight anti-C1q animals look as dense as controls in the raster.
5. **Mechanism by correlation.** C1q neutralisation lowers CD68, GFAP and C3, which fits the cascade; it does not show that synapse engulfment causes the spike change, and no cognitive or behavioural outcome after treatment was measured (open field only before injection).
6. **Direction of complement lowering is not uniformly protective in the paper's own introduction:** C1q knockout mice show heightened seizure susceptibility and aberrant connections; the effect is timing- and context-dependent. The claim that the antibody did not affect homeostatic complement is unmeasured.
7. **Not a plasma-product study and not WWOX.** A locally injected antibody in a post-status model says nothing about plasma exchange, FFP, albumin or IVIG, or about a genetic DEE with early onset.

## Design and limits

Male mice only; 150 injected mice, about 30 percent survival beyond a week, 45 analysed; single dose and time; chronic readouts at 5 weeks; antibody delivered intracisternally, an invasive route not applicable to a child.
