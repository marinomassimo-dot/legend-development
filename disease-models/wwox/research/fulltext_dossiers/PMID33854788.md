## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. rs72487966 associates with serum leucine/isoleucine at p 2.92e-6 (beta 0.411, SE 0.088, minor allele frequency 0.03). Figure 2E: no point crosses the genome-wide line; the WWOX point sits between the suggestive and genome-wide lines. Figure 2F: a single peak with sparse LD support inside the long WWOX intron region. Table S3 labels it by nearest gene.
2. The authors grade it "suggestive ... more validation studies are needed".
3. Leucine/isoleucine is raised before T2D (adjusted OR per SD 1.43) and MR "proves" a causal effect on T2D; the printed MR line reads OR 0.004 with a 95 percent interval of 1.001 to 1.006, a point estimate outside its own interval. The MR is on leucine/isoleucine, with instruments from other cohorts; WWOX is not an instrument.
4. The statements "WWOX is a risk gene for T2D" and "WWOX is related to HOMA-beta" are cited to the 2012 East Asian meta-analysis (a different SNP, rs17797882) and to the 2019 Harbin replication (PMID 31145772), which found no association with T2D and a control-only nominal HOMA-beta effect.
5. Copy-paste inconsistency: the same OR, interval and p are printed for carnitine C14:0 (stated to rise) and pipecolinic acid (stated to fall).

## 3 · Comparison with the laboratory's records

- No registry record carries this paper. Its WWOX content is a second, independent human-genetics hint toward amino-acid handling (branched-chain amino acids) rather than insulin secretion, but at a lower evidentiary grade than the Harbin paper's already weak HOMA-beta trait: MAF 0.03 variant, about 60 carriers, no genome-wide significance, no replication. The two papers do not share a variant (rs17797882 versus rs72487966); the later one leans on the earlier one for a claim the earlier one barely supports.
- Selection question 2 (insulin secretion versus amino-acid handling): neither is established; both are single nominal signals from different, unreplicated variants. The leucine/isoleucine datum would be a testable serum biomarker in WWOX carriers, but nothing in the paper says WWOX loss changes it.

## 4 · What the paper does NOT support

- That WWOX variation causes higher branched-chain amino acids or T2D; the MR does not involve WWOX.
- That rs72487966 acts through WWOX (nearest-gene label, MAF 0.03, intronic, no eQTL).
- Any statement about WWOX loss of function: no carrier is characterised.

## 6 · Epistemic tags and reopening

- DATO: the suggestive association as printed. INFERENZA: it is compatible with chance across about 81 million SNPs by 206 metabolites. PREMISE: DATO (p 2.92e-6 against the authors' own 5e-8 line).
- Rejected: using the paper to support WWOX involvement in branched-chain amino acid metabolism. PREMISE: INFERENZA. REVIVAL_TRIGGER: replication of rs72487966 at genome-wide significance, or a WWOX eQTL or carrier metabolomic dataset.
