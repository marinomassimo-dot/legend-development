## 3 · What is measured — and at what level

1. **BK5-Cre; Wwox fl/fl mice all die between day 68 and day 117, cause undetermined** (L3, L4). This is DATO, n = 22, both sexes. Figure S1 puts the median near day 100 (L7).
2. **BK5-Cre; Wwox +/fl heterozygotes survive at 100%** (n = 41), plotted only to about day 118 (L5, L7).
3. **MMTV-Cre knockouts show no survival difference to 1.4 years** (data not shown) and no tumours (L11, L12).
4. **Mammary ductal branching is reduced**, but Cre expression alone accounts for about 45% of the loss in the BK5 model (L20, L21). Lactation is preserved (L10).
5. **The transcriptome changes:** 913 probes (777 up, 136 down) (L22). Wwox itself falls only 2–3-fold on the array against about 85% by qPCR (L23).
6. **Wnt5a is up** at 8 weeks, and modestly at P18.5 (L26, L27). **pStat3-positive nuclei go from about 33% to about 65%** (IHC, n = 3 per group). The Western is unquantified (L29, L30).
7. **No tumours or premalignant lesions** in either model, in hets followed beyond a year, or in transplants (L9, L12, L32).

## 5 · Discrepancies found only in figures, supplements or arithmetic

1. **Mean 115 days is impossible** (L7 → L6). With one death at day 68 and none after day 117, the maximum possible mean for n = 22 is 114.8. The curve's median is about 100.
2. **The Cre-toxicity share of the branching phenotype is large** (L21): Cre(−) WT about 5.45, Cre(+) WT about 4.05, KO about 2.35 branches/mm. The MMTV controls pool Cre(+) with Cre(−) animals.
3. **The qPCR box plots give p < 0.001 on 3 mice per group**, which suggests the technical triplicates were pooled as observations (L19; INFERENZA).
4. **Table S1 exceeds its own cutoff:** the Stat3 probe has p = 0.0102, and 19 of 913 probes have p > 0.01 (L25).
5. **PCR cycles:** 24/26/28/32 in the legend, 24/28/32 in the Methods (L28).
6. **Transplant follow-up:** 9 months in the Results, 10 in the Methods; recipient age 4–5 weeks against 4 weeks (L32, L33).
7. **Animal labels:** the array knockouts are KO5–7 while the validation gels use KO1–6, so the independence of the validation is not stated (L27).
8. **Antibodies:** the Wwox Western antibody is cited to ref 7 and the IHC antibody to ref 14 (L34).
