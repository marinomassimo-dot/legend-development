## What the paper did
Paclitaxel (PTX) dose and time series; WWOX, p-WWOX (Tyr33), caspase-3, PARP, autophagy proteins and mTOR-pathway phospho-proteins by western blot; WWOX mRNA by RT-qPCR; Annexin V flow cytometry; mRFP-GFP-LC3 confocal puncta; TEM; WWOX plasmid overexpression and one siRNA construct.

## What it measured (direction, with the measurement behind it)
- Basal WWOX protein: **highest in A2780/T**, moderate in A2780, lowest in SKOV3 (Fig. 2A). Basal mRNA higher in A2780/T than A2780.
- PTX raised WWOX protein and p-WWOX in A2780 with cleaved caspase-3/PARP; in A2780/T PTX did not raise WWOX or p-WWOX, lowered WWOX mRNA, and no cleavage appeared.
- WWOX overexpression increased PTX-induced apoptosis in A2780 (one representative plot: 46.4% vs 63.6%; no statistic).
- PTX raised LC3, Beclin-1 and Atg12-5 and lowered p62 in both lines. **Chloroquine clamp once**: PTX ± CQ in A2780, LC3-II higher with both → flux on the **drug** arm.
- PTX lowered p-mTOR and **raised p-4E-BP1 (Thr37/46)**; p-p70S6K "barely detected" (blank lanes in Fig. 4A).
- WWOX overexpression raised p-mTOR (A2780 and A2780/T); siWWOX lowered it (Fig. 4C/D).
- WWOX overexpression lowered Beclin-1 and LC3 (both lines); siWWOX raised them (Fig. 4E/F). **No clamp, no p62, no puncta and no TEM on any WWOX arm.**

## What it states
WWOX is pro-apoptotic under PTX and suppresses autophagy via mTOR; the Discussion says WWOX "activated mTOR/p70S6K signaling".

## What it does NOT state, or states inconsistently
- The abstract says A2780/T is "characterized by reduced WWOX expression"; the Results say A2780/T has the highest basal WWOX. The reduction is PTX-induced within A2780/T.
- p70S6K was not detected, so "mTOR/p70S6K" activation by WWOX is not measured; totals of downstream proteins were not measured (authors' statement).
- PTX lowers p-mTOR yet raises p-4E-BP1, the opposite of the canonical mTORC1 readout; not discussed.
- The Results sentence "high levels of WWOX did not promote apoptosis and activate mTOR" (A2780/T) sits beside Fig. 4D, where WWOX overexpression in A2780/T raises p-mTOR.
- No epistasis (no mTOR inhibitor on a WWOX arm): the route "via mTOR" is a correlation of two blots.
- No densitometry and no statistics on any blot; "three independent experiments" stated for flow plots.

## Caveats the authors give
No PTX above 3,200 ng/ml (DMSO toxicity); WWOX knockdown not done in A2780/T; total protein levels of mTOR targets not detected; the cause of high WWOX in resistant cells is unexplained.
