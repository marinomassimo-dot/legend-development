## WWOX content

DRG and liver. Wild-type C57BL/6 mice, eGFP reporter under CMV; no disease model, no therapeutic cargo.

## What the paper did
- Four capsids (AAV9, rAAV2-retro, AAV-PHP.eB, AAV-MacpnS1), each CMV-eGFP, one dose, one route (temporal vein), one age (P2), one
  harvest (P30). Dose stated as 1e10 vg per pup in a stated 2 uL volume (Methods 2.1) but the Methods 2.2 mixing protocol
  describes a ~22 uL injected mixture (internal inconsistency in the injected volume; the authors' conversion is about 5e12 vg/kg for a ~2 g pup).
- Readouts: eGFP fluorescence intensity and eGFP+ cell counts by region (olfactory bulb, cortex, hippocampus, thalamus, cerebellum,
  medulla, cervical cord horns, lumbar DRG L1-L3, heart, liver); NeuN and ChAT co-labelling; serum ALT, AST, ALP-2c, total protein at 28 days.
- n: "at least four" per group in Methods; n = 6 per group in the Fig 3 and Fig 5 legends; n = 8 per group in the Fig 7 legend.
- Imaging settings differ by tissue (e.g. DRG and dorsal horn 4.5 % laser, gain 420; brain 5 %, gain 470; liver 4.8 %, gain 430), so eGFP intensity is comparable across capsids within a tissue, not across tissues. The DRG is described as lumbar L1-L3 sections (Methods 2.3) and as a whole-mounted L2 segment (Methods 2.4).
- Serum controls are "WT controls" (uninjected); no vehicle-injected group is described.

## What it states (text, tabular-free paper; numbers carried from Results text)
- Brain: AAV9 and MacpnS1 give the most transduced cells; PHP.eB widespread but weaker intensity; rAAV2-retro confined to the medulla
  (675.25 +/- 35.36 eGFP+ cells, 100 % NeuN+).
- Spinal ventral horn eGFP+ cells: PHP.eB 847.4 +/- 125.56, AAV9 662.83 +/- 20.57, rAAV2-retro 535.33 +/- 23.38, MacpnS1 291 +/- 8.92.
  ChAT+ fraction: rAAV2-retro 100 %, MacpnS1 87.3 +/- 3.3 %, AAV9 70.7 +/- 4.8 %, PHP.eB 53.8 +/- 7.5 %.
- DRG (Results 3.3): AAV9 and MacpnS1 highly efficient; rAAV2-retro and PHP.eB about 4- to 6-fold lower eGFP intensity. DRG readout is
  reporter intensity only: no DRG histology, no neuronal loss or injury endpoint, no DRG vector-genome count.
- Heart and liver: AAV9 and MacpnS1 highest; liver ranking AAV9 and MacpnS1 > rAAV2-retro > PHP.eB (intensity).
- Serum at 28 days: ALT and AST significantly raised only with MacpnS1; ALP-2c raised in all four groups, most with MacpnS1; total protein unchanged.
  Results text says enzyme rise "does not definitively establish hepatotoxicity"; the Discussion calls the MacpnS1 rise "acute liver injury".

## What it does NOT state / measure
No immune endpoint (the authors list cytokines, anti-AAV antibody and complement as unmeasured), no liver histology or liver vector genomes,
one biochemical time point, no dose-response (one dose), no CSF or intracranial arm, no therapeutic transgene, no functional or survival
outcome, no DRG pathology, no sex-stratified analysis (sex not determined at P2), no adult comparison (the age contrasts in the Discussion
are cited from other studies, not measured here).

## Authors' own caveats
BBB immaturity at P2 inflates CNS transduction; "findings should not be directly extrapolated to adult CNS applications"; PHP.eB depends on
Ly6a, strain-specific, and Ly6a/Ly6c1 are absent in primates; single biochemical time point; no immune readouts.

## Transfer limits for the reference genotype class
Neonatal wild-type mouse with an overexpression reporter is not a WWOX-deficient brain: capsid tropism by eGFP intensity does not
transfer as a therapeutic window, as an efficacy dose, or as a safety signal for WWOX cargo. Neonatal mouse to human gestational age is
an assumption the paper does not make. Intravenous route is not the CSF route. MacpnS1 is the only capsid with a liver-enzyme signal in this
design; the design cannot separate capsid, dose-per-weight and CMV-reporter effects.
