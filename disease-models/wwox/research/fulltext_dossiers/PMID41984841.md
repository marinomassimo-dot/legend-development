## Scientific verdict

The strongest DATO is genetic context dependence. Somatic epidermal Wwox loss alone did not
produce spontaneous tumours, but one or two deleted Wwox alleles enabled cSCC in a p53+/fl
background and accelerated near-complete tumour penetrance in p53fl/fl animals. In the chemical
carcinogenesis model, by contrast, Wwox loss increased lesion burden even without engineered p53
loss. WWOX therefore modifies tumour initiation/progression under at least two cooperating stress
contexts; this paper does not show that WWOX loss alone is universally sufficient.

The phenotype is coherent across scales: DKO tumours are less differentiated, young DKO
epidermis is thickened and proliferative, RNA-seq enriches tumour/EMT programs, and lineage-traced
tumours contain epithelial, hybrid and mesenchymal populations. Human HaCaT/A431 WWOX knockout
increases colony formation, migration, invasion and lung colonisation, while CMV rescue trends
toward reversal. These experiments support a cell-intrinsic component but the overexpression
rescue is not physiological replacement.

The WWOX–p63 link is supported by endogenous proximity ligation, co-immunoprecipitation, reduced
p63 protein after WWOX loss, rescue-associated restoration, decreased p63 target occupancy and
parallel loss in mouse and human specimens. The paper's strongest mechanistic wording nevertheless
outruns its direct assay set. ChIP-seq is `n = 1`, no protein half-life, proteasome or ITCH perturbation
is performed here, and the ITCH-competition mechanism comes from an earlier paper. The defensible
conclusion is that WWOX associates with and preserves a p63-dependent epithelial program in these
models; direct biochemical stabilization is supported chiefly by prior evidence.

## Tensions, transfer and hidden gold

- Wwox heterozygous and homozygous loss often behave similarly in the p53-null background. This
  suggests a threshold/context effect, not a demonstrated dose-response.
- The human TMA correlation can be driven by the normal-versus-tumour separation. No covariate,
  survival or within-stage outcome model establishes biomarker utility.
- A global “two-tailed unpaired t-test” statement coexists with log-rank and chi-square tests in
  captions; multiple-endpoint correction, power calculation, sex-stratified effects and global
  randomisation are not reported. Several pathology and measurement steps were blinded.
- Rescue bands often exceed WT, so rescue establishes reversibility under ectopic expression,
  not a native therapeutic dose window.
- **Genotype transfer:** low for WOREE neurodevelopmental alleles; the work concerns somatic skin
  loss with p53 context. **Mechanistic transfer:** moderate for WW-domain/p63 biology, conditional
  on cell type. **Clinical transfer:** low; “biomarker and therapeutic target” is prospective.
