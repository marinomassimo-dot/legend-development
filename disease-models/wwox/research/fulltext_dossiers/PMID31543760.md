## 1. What the paper did

Human neural stem cells derived from the H9 hESC line (commercial, Thermo Fisher). *WWOX* silenced
by **lentiviral shRNA** (Santa Cruz), control = scrambled shRNA, puromycin-selected stable
transductants. On growth-factor withdrawal the cells differentiated **only into neurons** — MAP2⁺,
TUJ1⁺, GFAP⁻, GalC⁻ (Supplementary Fig 2) — so the authors reclassify them from neural *stem* to
neural *progenitor* cells (hNPC).

Assays: PrestoBlue (resazurin) reduction; adhesion to Geltrex and to fibronectin by crystal-violet
OD₅₇₀; gelatin zymography for pro-MMP2/pro-MMP9; 2-D and 3-D growth on Geltrex; and **CAGE**
(cap analysis of gene expression) transcriptomics on undifferentiated and differentiated cells,
with GSEA, STRING networks and a PCA over an oxidative-stress gene set. Deposited as GSE126075.

## 3. What the paper states

* WWOX is expressed unevenly across human brain compartments (FANTOM5/ZENBU re-analysis, Fig 1):
  highest in corpus callosum and medulla oblongata, lowest in postcentral and paracentral gyrus;
  adult whole brain 17.7 TPM RLE versus 9.2 in 20–33-week fetal brain.
* WWOX silencing lowers mitochondrial redox activity, increases adhesion to ECM, lowers secreted
  pro-MMP2/9, and abolishes 3-D network formation while leaving 2-D monolayer growth intact.
* The transcriptome shifts across cytoskeleton organisation, adhesion, cell signalling and
  chromatin remodelling; the top enriched sets in control cells are neural-crest differentiation
  and migration and homophilic cell adhesion (Table 1).
* Conclusion: *"appropriate WWOX expression is required for hNPC maintenance and differentiation"*
  and WWOX *"may be one of the master expression regulators in neural tissue"*.

## 4. What the paper does NOT state

* It gives **no degree of knockdown**. Methods say silencing efficiency was assessed by Western
  blot with ImageJ densitometry normalised to GAPDH, but **no number appears anywhere** in the
  paper or the supplement. Supplementary Figure 1 is a single blot with three lanes (native hNPC,
  shScrambled, shWWOX): the WWOX band in shWWOX is clearly reduced but **plainly still present**,
  and the shScrambled band is visibly stronger than the untransduced parental band. This is a
  partial knockdown of unquantified depth, shown once, without replication.
* It performs **no rescue**: WWOX is never re-expressed, so no endpoint here is shown to be
  reversible.
* It states **no n** for the adhesion, redox or zymography assays — "the results are presented as
  means", with an unpaired t-test after a Shapiro–Wilk check.
* It measures **no mitochondrial membrane potential**. "Mitochondrial redox potential" is a
  resazurin→resorufin reduction assay, which scales with viable-cell number and proliferation as
  much as with per-cell metabolism; the paper does not normalise to cell count, so a lower signal
  is equally consistent with fewer or slower-dividing cells. The PCA on an oxidative gene set is
  offered as corroboration but is transcript-level and uses the same samples.
* It measures **no electrical activity, no calcium, no synapse** — the word "neuron" here means
  MAP2⁺/TUJ1⁺ morphology, not function.
* It says nothing about any *WWOX* allele. There is no genotype in this paper; there is a
  knockdown.

## 5. Caveats the authors themselves give

* The precise nature of the WWOX effect on ECM adhesion *"remain unclear"*.
* The supplier's claim of tri-lineage potential did not hold in their hands — all cells became
  neurons — and they reclassify the line accordingly.
* Enrichment is reported at FDR < 0.25, an exploratory threshold, and is stated as such.

## 6. Reading the panels

* **Figures 5 and 6**: the text says shWWOX cells *"remained as isolated, single cells"*. Panels
  5C/5D and 6C/6D show **small multicellular clusters**, not isolated single cells. The contrast
  with the control networks is unmistakable; the description overshoots it.
* **Figures 5–7 scale bars**: every panel is labelled `50 µm` although the legends declare two
  different magnifications (200× and 40×) within the same figure. At least one of the two bars in
  each figure cannot be correct.
* **Figure 3**: panels A and B are on absolute OD scales an order of magnitude apart (0.08 versus
  1.10) because they use different plates and seeding densities (17,500 versus 100,000 cells per
  well). The two adhesion results are not comparable to each other, only each to its own control.

## 7. Answer to the assigned question, for this source

The only endpoint here with a **large, near-binary, human-neural-lineage** signal is **3-D network
formation in a thick ECM scaffold**. It is the right shape for a rescue readout — it is
qualitative-but-unambiguous, it was reproduced in three independent experiments and was
seeding-density-independent, and it is a tissue-architecture phenotype that matches the human
imaging finding the authors invoke (global atrophy, corpus-callosum hypoplasia). What this paper
does not supply is any of what would make it usable: no quantification, no n, no statistic, no
rescue arm, and no knowledge of how much WWOX was actually removed.

The resazurin endpoint fails as a WWOX readout twice over: it is confounded with cell number, and
under [`LEGEND_CORE.md` §13](../../../../framework/instruction/LEGEND_CORE.md#13-biomarker-scope-hard-rule)
a generic mitochondrial-reductase assay is not a readout of the functional state of *WWOX*
(Tier 3). pro-MMP2/9 by zymography is secreted and in principle samplable, but it is a distal
pathway marker shared with every migration phenotype, and the paper offers no disease-population
sensitivity or specificity.

**Transfer limit.** shRNA knockdown of unquantified depth in H9-derived hNPC is not a biallelic
null and is not a missense allele; a knockdown phenotype cannot discriminate loss of protein from
loss of function, which is exactly the distinction the reference genotype class turns on.
