## What the paper did

Three cynomolgus-macaque studies on onasemnogene abeparvovec (scAAV9, CMV enhancer / chicken-β-actin
hybrid promoter, human *SMN*).

| Study | Design | n | Dose | Route | Co-treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12-month GLP | interim 6 wk + terminal 52 wk | 5/sex/group (3/sex interim, 2/sex terminal), 13–19 mo, Asian origin | 1.2 / 3.0 / 6.0 × 10¹³ vg per animal | IT lumbar, 0.80 mL, **preceded by 0.20 mL Omnipaque 180 contrast** | none |
| 13-week non-GLP mechanistic | interim 2 wk + terminal 13 wk | 5/sex/group (3/sex interim, 2/sex terminal), 25–34 mo, **Mauritius origin** | 3.0 × 10¹³ vg per animal | IT lumbar, same contrast | **prednisolone 1 mg/kg/day oral, day −1 to 29 then alternate days to 41**; or **rituximab 20 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks from day −14 + everolimus 0.5 mg/kg/day oral from week −2 to day 14** |
| 6-month GLP intravenous | interim 6 wk + terminal 6 mo | 6/sex/group | 1.1 × 10¹⁴ vg/kg | i.v. | prednisolone arm as above |

Readouts: graded histopathology of DRG, trigeminal ganglia, spinal cord, nerve roots and peripheral
nerves; in-situ hybridisation with antisense and sense vector probes; **nerve conduction velocity**
in sural, peroneal, saphenous (sensory) and tibial (motor) nerves; serum/plasma and CSF **NfL**;
lymphocyte immunophenotyping; plus a review of clinical-trial and pharmacovigilance data.

## What it measured

| Observation | Measured value |
|---|---|
| Lesion type | Minimal-to-marked mononuclear cell inflammation, minimal-to-moderate neuronal degeneration, increased satellite glial cells in DRG and trigeminal ganglion; secondary axon degeneration in dorsal nerve roots, dorsal funiculus and peripheral nerves |
| Onset | Present by 2 weeks; peak severity around 6 weeks; "This degeneration occurred within 2–6 weeks of intrathecal administration" |
| Time course to 52 weeks | Incidence and severity "generally decreased after 52 weeks compared with 6 weeks"; satellite-glial-cell aggregates interpreted as scar/remodelling after neuronal loss; peripheral-nerve findings absent at 52 weeks |
| Dose relationship | Findings at **≥1.2 × 10¹³ vg/animal**, i.e. from the anticipated clinical dose upward; the paper states the findings "did not demonstrate a dose response" |
| Clinical correlate | None. Well tolerated at all doses to 52 weeks, no neurobehavioural change, all animals survived to scheduled euthanasia |

## What it states

That the lesion is non-progressive and trends toward resolution; that it is clinically silent and
unaccompanied by nerve-conduction change; that **systemic immunosuppression, including complete B-cell
depletion, did not prevent, attenuate or mitigate it**; and that "the pathogenesis is possibly a
consequence of increased vector genome transduction and/or transgene expression."

## What it does NOT state

- Nothing about WWOX.
- **It does not state that the immune system is uninvolved.** The authors are explicit: the negative
  "suggest[s] that primary **adaptive** immune responses may not be a critical mediator", and
  "activation of the innate immune response in transduced cells **was not excluded**" — indeed they
  note that the sense-probe signal is consistent with double-stranded RNA that "may elicit an innate
  immune response through retinoic acid-inducible gene I-like receptor signaling pathways".
  The negative is about two specific regimens, one of which is a steroid alone and the other a
  B-cell/mTOR combination; **no calcineurin inhibitor and no T-cell-directed agent was tested**.
- "Resolution" is explicitly redefined, not claimed literally: neuronal degeneration/loss "is
  generally not considered a reversible finding, because neurons are not thought to have
  regenerative capacity in NHPs"; "resolution" was used "to connote a decrease in the incidence
  and/or severity" plus absence of secondary axonal findings. A scar is not a repair.
- It does not establish human relevance: "The translational relevance of DRG pathology in humans is
  currently unknown"; the one published human case (a 22-year-old with SOD1-ALS after
  intrathecal AAV-miR-SOD1) could not be attributed to the therapy or to the disease.
- No immunophenotyping of the DRG infiltrate, no transcriptomics, no cytokine profiling, no
  pre-pathology time point earlier than 2 weeks — so the paper cannot see the day-5 events that
  PMID 41404412 reports.

## Statistical power of the negative, measured

The immunosuppression comparison is the 13-week mechanistic study: **n = 3 per sex per group at the
interim necropsy and n = 2 per sex per group at the terminal necropsy**. In the published female
cervical-DRG table the prednisolone arm at day 92 shows neuronal degeneration in **2 of 2** animals
against **1 of 2** in the unsuppressed arm. An absence-of-effect conclusion drawn from two animals
per cell is an absence of detectable signal, not a demonstrated absence of effect; the paper offers
no power statement.

## Additional methodological facts that bear on comparison with other studies

- Both intrathecal studies administered **iohexol contrast (Omnipaque 180) immediately before the
  vector**, to mirror the clinical procedure. PMID 41404412 did not.
- Animals were **pre-selected for low anti-AAV9 titre**, and "the monkeys with highest anti-AAV9
  antibody response(s) at screening were assigned to the control group" — i.e. the control group is
  not antibody-matched to the treated groups.
- The two intrathecal studies differ in animal origin (Asian vs Mauritius) and age (13–19 vs
  25–34 months).
- Ventral-horn (motor neuron) degeneration was also seen, with high ISH signal, and is treated as a
  separate finding "subsequent to greater transduction… and/or greater expression of the transgene
  product".

## Caveats the authors themselves give

Beyond those above: NfL findings "should be considered in the context of this nonclinical,
nondiseased test system, and appropriate caution should be applied for any potential translation to
human patients"; the lesion's silence is attributed to the limited fraction of affected neurons "in
a system with substantial redundancy"; clinical chemistry and biodistribution/immunogenicity data
are deferred to other publications and are not in this paper.
