## What this pass added

1. Human-genetics support is nominal. The text says several WWOX variants are "strongly associated" with metabolic-syndrome traits after re-analysis of 27 portal datasets. Supplementary Table 1: best T2D variant intronic, p 3.78E-06; best missense p 8.02E-05; best fasting glucose and waist circumference of order 1E-05; no variant reaches 5E-08 and no multiplicity correction is stated. This matches the pattern found for the other WWOX glycaemic genetics in this metabolic batch (nominal, uncorrected).
2. "Hyperglycemia" is a modest shift. Figure 1E, 18 h fasting, n = 8: about 78 mg/dL in muscle-cKO against about 67 in controls (one asterisk) on a 40 to 100 mg/dL axis. Glucose intolerance (Figure 1H, peak about 400 vs 250 mg/dL), insulin resistance, higher insulin (about 2.4 vs 0.5 ng/mL, n = 4) and weight gain (about 44 vs 34 g at week 37) are larger effects than the fasting value.
3. The deletion is developmental. Doxycycline was given in the drinking water of breeding cages and continued until euthanasia, so deletion is on from gestation or lactation; the weights already differ at the first weighing. "Somatic ablation in adult muscle" is not what the design does.
4. Direction contrast. Whole-body Wwox-null mice are hypoglycemic and die by 3 weeks (the Introduction says so); the muscle knockout is higher-glucose and insulin-resistant; hepatocyte and adipocyte knockouts have no fasting-glucose change. So a tissue-specific WWOX loss reproduces neither the null glucose sign nor the null lethality; the null hypoglycemia is rescued by neuron-restricted restoration (companion reading, Repudi 2021). The metabolic phenotype of WWOX loss therefore has at least two tissues of origin with opposite signs.
5. Integrity: two of the 54 references are retracted papers (a metabolic-syndrome review cited once for prevalence; a 2003 AMPK and HIF-1-alpha paper cited once in the Discussion); citation only, declared in the manifest.
6. What stays as the earlier reading found: reduced FDG uptake in two muscles, AMPK phosphorylation loss, WWOX and phospho-AMPK co-immunoprecipitation in C2C12 (high-exposure band only in Figure 4F), no tested equivalence for brain and liver FDG.

## Does not support

A role for muscle WWOX in the WWOX-null hypoglycemia or in the human WWOX-DEE metabolic phenotype; human variant causality for metabolic traits; direct WWOX-AMPK regulation (co-IP in overexpression and one endogenous high-exposure lane; the proposed mechanism is the authors' model). Transfer to the reference genotype: none measured; mouse, muscle-specific, mixed background.
