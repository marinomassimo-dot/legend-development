## What the paper did

Systematic truncation of the mouse *Gad1* upstream region to find the shortest fragment that keeps
inhibitory-neuron specificity and promoter strength when packaged in AAV. Candidate fragments drove
GFP in AAV-PHP.eB delivered intravenously (retro-orbital sinus, 100 µL, 5.0 × 10¹¹ vg/mouse) into
VGAT-tdTomato mice; specificity was scored as the fraction of GFP⁺ cells that were tdTomato⁺ in
M1/M2 motor cortex at three weeks. The winning 410-bp fragment (core promoter + the Rii Dlx-motif
enhancer) was named **cmGAD67**. The same strategy applied to *Gad2* gave a 486-bp **cmGAD65**.
cmGAD67 was then used to drive ChR2, hM4Di and GAD65, and AAV-cmGAD67-GAD65 ("AAV-GAD65") was tested
in a pentylenetetrazol (PTZ) kindling model at 30 and 35 mg/kg and in a Gq-DREADD focal
hyperexcitability model. Mice: wild-type C57BL/6J and VGAT-tdTomato, both sexes, 6–10 weeks.

## What it states

That a 410-bp cassette can combine high inhibitory-neuron specificity with promoter activity ≈1.8×
a 2 542-bp predecessor, that it is biased toward PV⁺ interneurons, that the bias is largely
cargo-independent, and that specificity is **route-dependent** — the authors attribute the drop
after parenchymal injection to "high local vector concentrations".

## What it does NOT state

- Nothing about WWOX, and nothing about gene **replacement**. The therapeutic arm adds GAD65 to
  modulate a circuit; no deficient protein is restored. The engineering transfers; the therapeutic
  logic does not.
- **No dose-response.** Every systemic experiment uses the single dose 5.0 × 10¹¹ vg/mouse. There is
  no low/high arm, so neither an efficacy threshold nor a toxic ceiling is measured.
- **No toxicology.** No DRG, trigeminal-ganglion, spinal-cord, liver or heart histopathology; no
  serum chemistry; no neurofilament or any other injury biomarker. Safety rests on behavioural
  normality (open field, home-cage circadian, rotarod/beam) at one dose.
- No human promoter. cmGAD67 is derived from the **mouse** *Gad1* locus; no human orthologous
  fragment is tested, and the paper does not claim one.
- No chronic or pharmacoresistant epilepsy model, and no large-brain species.
- The off-target fraction is **not resolved by cell class**: 92 % specificity means ~8 % of GFP⁺ cells
  were tdTomato-negative, and the paper does not say what those cells are.

## Caveats the authors themselves give

- "The present study primarily utilized acute seizure models, and validation in chronic epilepsy
  models and in larger brains will be necessary"; "further evaluation of long-term safety,
  off-target expression, and peripheral distribution will be required" (Discussion, Translational
  potential and limitations).
- The 35-mg/kg paradigm "does not fully recapitulate pharmacoresistant epilepsy or the chronic and
  heterogeneous nature of the human condition".
- "Although low-level expression in excitatory neurons cannot be completely excluded…"
- Seizure responses in chemically induced models "are inherently variable… which may limit
  statistical power".
- Seizure-like events alone did not reach significance in the PTZ arm (Figure 5F, p = 0.08); the
  positive result is a distributional shift from a combined events-plus-delta-power energy-distance
  test.

## Qualification found on inspecting Figure 2

The SST statement is made once, for both cassettes together: "fewer than 50% of SST+ neurons were
GFP positive". **Figure 2D plots them separately and they differ.** The cmGAD65 SST bar sits near
27 %; the **cmGAD67** SST bar — the cassette carried forward into every therapeutic experiment —
sits at about **51 %**, with per-animal points spanning roughly 35–65 %. The blanket statement is
true of one construct and not of the other, and the construct it is not true of is the one the paper
recommends. Figure 2F is the same shape: the text sets a threshold ("values greater than 1.5 for
PV+ neurons indicate preferential expression") and the cmGAD67 PV enrichment mean sits essentially
*on* 1.5, with individual animals from about 1.2 to 1.8. The PV bias is real; it is also marginal
against the paper's own stated criterion.

## Internal inconsistency found on reading

The dose in the focal chemogenetic experiment is reported three incompatible ways. Body text:
Gq-DREADD "1 µL, 1.0 × 10¹¹ vg/mouse" and the second AAV "1 µL, 1.0 × 10¹¹ vg/mouse". Figure 8A
legend: Gq-DREADD "1 µL, 1.0 × 10⁹ vg/mouse" and the second AAV "1 µL, 1.0 × 10¹⁰ vg/mouse". A
hundred-fold and a ten-fold discrepancy between the text and its own figure legend. Nothing in the
paper resolves which is correct, and the focal-model conclusion cannot be tied to a dose.
