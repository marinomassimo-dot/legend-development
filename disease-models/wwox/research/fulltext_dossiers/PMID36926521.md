## 1 · What the paper did

Short-read genome sequencing first, then RNA-seq, long-read sequencing or optical mapping depending on the
genome result. 13 of 15 individuals were solved. For the WWOX individual the authors used trio exome, genome
sequencing, junction PCR and Sanger, RT-PCR across exons 4–6 on blood RNA with amplicon sequencing, and a
fibroblast western blot.

## 3 · What the paper does NOT state

- No assay of the **missense allele alone** (RNA level, protein stability, localisation, binding). Its
  pathogenicity is predicted, not measured.
- No statement of the **frame** of the exon-5-skipped transcript. From the printed sizes, 293 − 186 = 107 nt,
  which is not a multiple of three, so a frameshift is likely — **INFERENZA from the printed sizes, not a reported
  result**.
- No **NMD** test (no translation-inhibitor arm), no quantification of the skipped fraction, no allele-specific
  expression.
- No quantification of the western blot, no replicate, no second control, no parental fibroblasts — so the blot
  cannot apportion the reduction between the two alleles, and "loss" is a qualitative reduction with a visible
  residual band, not a measured residual dose.
- No neural matrix. The authors' own Discussion says blood and fibroblasts may be inadequate for genes expressed
  mainly in the central nervous system.
- No WWOX gene-direct reference (74 references, none on WWOX).

## 4 · Caveats the authors give themselves, and defects found on reading

- Authors: clinically accessible tissues "are sometimes inadequate to model genes prevalently expressed in the
  central nervous system" (Discussion).
- Reader-found, supplementary methods (`DataSheet3.doc`, read through a byte-string extraction because no `.doc`
  converter is installed): the cell-culture paragraph names fibroblasts from a healthy donor and **individual 13**,
  not individual 11; the western paragraph names **vinculin** as loading control while panel S5G shows actin.
  Neither reverses the qualitative result; both weaken the blot as a quantified dose.
- WWOX antibody: Atlas Antibodies HPA023822 (supplementary methods). Its epitope is not stated in the paper, so
  whether the antibody would detect a protein from the exon-5-skipped transcript is unknown.

## 5 · Allele class and transfer limit

Neither allele is in the class of the reference genotype's alleles. `p.(Thr12Arg)` is an exon-1 missense near the
N-terminus; the second allele is an exon-5 inversion with flanking intronic deletions, whose measured RNA
consequence is exon 5 skipping. The fibroblast western measures this compound genotype only. **Nothing here
transfers to Q230P, P47T, G372R, A141T, P252A, to any splice-site allele, or to any other missense.** What it adds
to the model is one more example of a patient-cell protein measurement in WWOX-DEE, and one more structural
allele class (an exon inversion) whose consequence was measured at RNA level.
