## What the paper did

A narrative literature review of WWOX biology, genetics, the WOREE clinical picture and brain imaging; one table
(Table 1) tabulates MRI features per cited study; one figure re-uses images from Iacomino 2020 ("Courtesy of Prof. Salpietro").
No search strategy, inclusion criteria or new patient.

## What it tabulates (Table 1)

Nine sources with "Number of patients": Abdel-Salam 1 · Tabarki 5 · Johannsen 2 · Piard 20 · He 1 · Banne 56 ·
Tarta-Arsene 1 · Iacomino 2 · Oliver 13; "Total number of patients 101".

- Banne 2021 is itself a review of 56 published cases that predates, and by date can include, Abdel-Salam 2014,
  Tabarki 2015, Tarta-Arsene 2017, Johannsen 2018, Piard 2019, He 2020 and Iacomino 2020. **101 is a sum of
  overlapping sources, not a count of patients.** The Clinical section of the same paper says "84 patients have been
  reported".
- Corpus callosum hypoplasia is entered for all nine sources (Piard 15:20; Oliver 12:13); brain atrophy for most.
- "Progression of the abnormalities with age": Tabarki 5:5, Tarta-Arsene 1:1, Oliver 7:13. The text turns this into
  "approximately 13%" of all 101 — a numerator from serially imaged patients over a denominator that includes
  patients never re-imaged. In Oliver, 7 of 13 had serial MRI and all 7 progressed.
- Cerebellar vermis hypoplasia 2:2 comes only from Iacomino (one child and one fetus); Tabarki is described as having an unaffected cerebellum.

## What the paper states and what it does NOT

- Abstract: "All affected patients showed brain anomalies on MRI" — contradicted by its own report of Piard
  (abnormal MRI in 80%). It does not cite the case reports with normal imaging.
- "the p.Gln230Pro pathogenic variant ... in eight cases overall (1)" — carried from reference 1 (Aldaz and Hussain
  2020); not counted by these authors.
- "Recurrent deletions and duplications of exons 6–8 ... led to truncated proteins or unstable products that were
  quickly degraded (1)" — carried from a review; no primary measurement is cited.
- "48 WWOX pathogenic variants have been detected in affected patients (42)" for multiple sclerosis — reference 42 is a
  study of C6orf10 variants; the support is not evident from the citation.
- Death timing is given three ways: "generally dying within the first year of life", "death in first years of life",
  "early death before 2 years of age".
- Genotype-phenotype: the authors find correlation "only about the mortality risk" (null/null higher), citing Oliver 2023.
- No quantitative imaging (volumetry, callosal area, DTI, spectroscopy beyond one normal MRS) is reported.
- The review does **not** mention polymicrogyria at all (it neither reports nor denies it).
