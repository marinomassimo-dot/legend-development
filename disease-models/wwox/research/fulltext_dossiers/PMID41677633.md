## What the paper did

Three systems, three kinds of manipulation:

1. **Constitutive null versus wild type** — primary mouse embryonic fibroblasts, `Wwox+/+` and `Wwox−/−`,
   stressed by serum deprivation (24–96 h) or 100 µM H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub>.
2. **Inducible gain of function** — HeLa Tet-On cells, doxycycline-induced ectopic WWOX, 0–9 h.
3. **Knockdown** — SCC-15 human squamous carcinoma cells, lentiviral shRNA against WWOX versus shLuc,
   under serum starvation.

## What it measured

Immunoblot (WWOX, Bcl-X<sub>L</sub>, Mcl-1, Bcl-2, Bax, Bak, Bad, β-actin) with densitometry; RT-PCR and
real-time PCR (*WWOX*, *Bcl-XL*, *Mcl-1*); WST-8 viability; propidium-iodide sub-G0/G1 by flow cytometry;
**rhodamine 123 mitochondrial membrane potential** by flow cytometry; **DCF-DA reactive oxygen species** by
flow cytometry. Pharmacology: MG132 (proteasome), NH<sub>4</sub>Cl and chloroquine (lysosomal
alkalinisation), E64d and pepstatin A (lysosomal proteases), N-acetyl-L-cysteine (antioxidant),
cycloheximide.

## What it states

- Stress — serum deprivation, H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub>, etoposide, cisplatin — raises WWOX protein and
  *WWOX* mRNA in SCC-15, HeLa and wild-type MEFs (Figures 1–3, S1).
- **Under serum starvation the WWOX-expressing cell is the dying cell.** `Wwox+/+` MEFs lose viability
  (Figure 4A: ~35 % → ~21 % at 72 h) while `Wwox−/−` MEFs do not (~40 % flat to 96 h); sub-G0/G1 is ~21 %
  versus ~11 % (Figure 4B); mitochondrial membrane potential falls to ~0.34 of control in `Wwox+/+` and
  stays at ~0.79 in `Wwox−/−` (Figure 4C).
- Bcl-X<sub>L</sub> and Mcl-1 protein fall in `Wwox+/+` but not in `Wwox−/−` (Figure 5A; densitometry in
  Figure S2A: Bcl-X<sub>L</sub> 1.00 → 0.52 → 0.45 versus 0.92 → 0.90 → 0.82; Mcl-1 1.00 → 0.93 → 0.35
  versus 1.03 → 1.12 → 0.92). Doxycycline-induced WWOX lowers both in HeLa; shWWOX prevents the fall in
  SCC-15. *Bcl-XL* and *Mcl-1* mRNA do not fall (Figure 6) — the effect is post-transcriptional.
- The route is assigned to the lysosome: MG132 does not rescue, chloroquine rescues Mcl-1, E64d and
  pepstatin A rescue both (Figure 7, Figure S3).
- ROS rises on starvation in both genotypes but **more in `Wwox+/+`** (Figure 8A, significant at 6, 12, 24
  and 48 h); NAC pretreatment blocks the WWOX induction and with it the Bcl-X<sub>L</sub> fall (Figure 8B).

## What it does NOT state

- **No autophagic-flux measurement anywhere.** LC3-II, p62 and autophagosome counts are not measured in this
  paper; "lysosome/autophagy-mediated" is an inference from which inhibitor rescues which band.
- **No physical interaction.** "Co-immunoprecipitation experiments revealed that neither Bcl-X<sub>L</sub>
  nor Mcl-1 physically interacted with WWOX." The ligase or adaptor is explicitly left open.
- No neuron, no neural progenitor, no brain tissue. The disease framing in the introduction (WOREE,
  Alzheimer's disease) is background; no experiment in this paper is neural.
- No human missense allele. The loss-of-function arm is a constitutive mouse null; a destabilising SDR
  missense allele and a splice allele are not modelled here, and a null models neither.
- No statistics on the Bcl-2-family densitometry: Figures S2 and S3 are single bars without error bars or
  tests, although the blots are stated to be representative of ≥4 repeats.
- Nothing about dose: no heterozygote arm (`Wwox+/−` is absent from this paper).

## Caveats the authors themselves give

- "Because pharmacological inhibitors may have pleiotropic effects, knockdown of essential genes involved in
  the autophagy or lysosomal degradation pathway using RNA interference tools needs to be tested for
  verification."
- They leave open whether WWOX instead suppresses IRES-driven translation of Bcl-X<sub>L</sub>.
- They note that long-passage `Wwox−/−` MEFs in complete medium generate *more* ROS than wild type — the
  opposite sign to the acute starvation result reported here.

## Text-versus-panel

One qualification, recorded in the manifest as `panel_qualifies_text`: the Results name chloroquine as the
lysosome inhibitor that blocked the Mcl-1 fall, and Figure S3B shows that the other alkalinising agent in
the same experiment, NH<sub>4</sub>Cl, did **not** rescue (0.31 and 0.30 against a starved value of 0.37,
versus 0.90 and 0.88 with chloroquine). The sentence is accurate and incomplete; a reader taking it alone
will over-read the lysosomal attribution.
