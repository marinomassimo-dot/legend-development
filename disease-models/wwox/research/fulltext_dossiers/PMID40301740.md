## What the paper did

The preclinical package for GC101, an AAV9 carrying a **codon-optimised** human *SMN1* under a CA
promoter, produced in the Sf9/baculovirus system, delivered intrathecally in the clinic
(NCT05824169, NCT05901987, NCT06421831):

- In vitro: codon optimisation raised CAI 0.73 → 0.94 and GC content 45.20% → 58.52%; SMN protein
  rose 2.4-fold (HEK293) and 1.3-fold (BHK21) by western, and ~6-fold (U87-MG) and ~3-fold (NB1)
  by immunofluorescence after AAVDJ transduction at MOI 5000.
- Efficacy: Taiwanese **SMA type 3** model mice (Smn1−/−, SMN2+/+), ICV on postnatal day 1, doses
  2.7 × 10^13, 5.3 × 10^13, 8.0 × 10^13 and 1.6 × 10^14 vg/kg, followed one year.
- GLP mouse toxicology: neonatal C57BL/6N, ICV, 2.6 × 10^13, 5.8 × 10^13, 1.9 × 10^14 vg/kg,
  70 animals per group, 13 weeks.
- **GLP NHP toxicology:** 2.5-year-old cynomolgus, screened for anti-AAV9 neutralising antibody
  ≤ 1:50, single **intrathecal** injection of **4.67 × 10^13 vg/animal (≈1.87 × 10^13 vg/kg)** in
  800 µL; necropsy day 29 and day 91; treated n = 8 (4 M, 4 F), control n = 4 (2 M, 2 F).

## What it measured

SMN protein by western and immunofluorescence; ChAT+ motor-neuron counts in lumbar anterior horn;
tail length; muscle fibre area, ATPase (pH 4.5) and succinate dehydrogenase staining; body weight;
serum chemistry including ALT and AST; vector genomes by qPCR in CNS and peripheral organs and in
plasma over time; anti-drug antibodies; and H&E plus SMN immunohistochemistry of **DRG and spinal
cord** at day 91.

## What it states

1. **There is a dose ceiling on efficacy, not just on safety.** Tail-length preservation improved
   from 2.7 × 10^13 to 5.3 × 10^13 and again at 8.0 × 10^13 vg/kg, but "increasing the dose to
   1.6E+14 vg/kg did not result in additional therapeutic benefit"; likewise CNS SMN expression
   did not rise proportionally from 5.8 × 10^13 to 1.9 × 10^14 vg/kg, "suggesting that a
   saturation point may have been reached at the lower dose".
2. **A negative DRG result in NHP.** At 4.67 × 10^13 vg/animal, day 91: "H&E staining revealed no
   signs of inflammatory cell infiltration or neuronal necrosis in the DRG and spinal cord", with
   robust SMN expression in DRG neurons by IHC. The discussion repeats it: "at a dose of
   4.67E + 13 vg/animal, no toxic inflammatory responses were observed in the DRG, despite robust
   SMN protein expression."
3. **No hepatic signal.** No significant AST or ALT change over the study; plasma vector peaked
   within 10 minutes and returned to baseline by day 4; anti-drug antibodies appeared at two weeks
   and stayed stable.
4. **Wide biodistribution from a CSF route.** CNS vector >1 × 10^4 copies/µg DNA in brain,
   cerebellum, spinal cord and DRG at both day 29 and day 91, plus liver, kidney, lung and heart.
5. **The authors chose the dose from the published DRG-toxicity range.** They state that DRG
   toxicity "could observed in a dose range of 3E+12 to 1E+13 GC via intrathecal or
   intra-cisterna magna, or 1E+13 to 2E+14 GC/kg via intravenous injection as reported", citing
   Hordeaux et al. 2024, and then dosed at 1.87 × 10^13 vg/kg.

## What it does NOT state

- Nothing about WWOX, nothing about any DEE.
- **No immunosuppression is reported anywhere** — not in Methods, not in the NHP section. The
  negative DRG result is therefore, as printed, an unmedicated negative.
- No nerve-conduction measurement, no neurological examination, no sensory testing.
- One dose level in NHP, one histological timepoint (day 91), H&E and SMN IHC only — no
  severity-graded pathology table for DRG is printed.
- The NHP arm has no efficacy endpoint; it is biodistribution and safety only.
- It does not report the survival endpoint that defines SMA type 1 work: the efficacy model is
  type 3, scored on tail length, motor-neuron counts and muscle histology.

## Internal inconsistency found

The Results and Discussion state "no signs of inflammatory cell infiltration or neuronal necrosis"
in DRG, while the **Figure 7 legend** reads "Representative pictures showing minor detectable sign
of toxicity." The text and its own figure legend do not agree, and the legend is the weaker claim.
Since the figure panels were not inspected, this contradiction is recorded, not resolved.

## Caveats the authors themselves give

Production system matters for safety ("AAV vectors produced using the HEK293 system may exacerbate
these immune reactions due to higher residual host genome content"); species differences may
blunt the codon-optimisation advantage in mice; the therapeutic effect of later administration in
milder patients "need further study".

## Transfer limit

Cynomolgus macaque, n = 8 dosed, one dose level, one timepoint, no immunosuppression reported, no
functional sensory endpoint; and a neonatal-mouse efficacy arm in a type 3 SMA model. SMA is a
motor-neuron disease dosed on a weight basis; the WWOX-DEE genotype class shares neither the
endpoint nor the cell type. What transfers is the **negative control on the DRG question** — one
IT AAV9 study at ~4.7 × 10^13 vg/animal that reports no DRG pathology — and its weakness is that
the reporting is thinner than in every other member of this group. Nothing here is medical advice.
