## The question the brief put first — which gene is each variant in?
**Answered by reading:** `p.E17K` is **WWOX `c.49G>A`**; `p.Cys121Trp` is **SCN1B `c.363C>G`** (Methods: *"SCN1B c.363C>G (p.Cys121Trp) … and WWOX c.49G>A (p.E17K)"*). The supplement places the variant at chr16:78,133,724 (hg19), inside canonical exon 1 (Ensembl GRCh37 78,133,310–78,133,782), which is consistent with `c.49`.

## What the paper did
It introduces FoundHaplo, an IBD hidden-Markov method that infers carriers of a known founder variant from SNP-array data. It was validated by simulation (33 loci) and applied to two variants in an epilepsy cohort (Epi25 subset, n = 1,573) and in the UK Biobank (n = 468,481), with WES as truth.
