## 1 · What the paper measures

7,379 Japanese (5,315 type 2 diabetes cases, 2,064 controls), nine East Asian GWAS index SNPs including WWOX rs17797882 (a borderline, not genome-wide, signal in the originating meta-analysis), logistic regression per SNP and for a genetic risk score, quantitative-trait regression (HOMA-IR, HOMA-beta, fasting glucose) in controls, Bonferroni threshold 0.0055. No WWOX expression or function.

## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. WWOX rs17797882 (risk allele C, frequency 0.799 in cases, 0.792 in controls): adjusted OR 1.01 (0.91 to 1.12), p 0.75; not associated with type 2 diabetes. Seven of nine SNPs are not; two are nominal and fail Bonferroni; the 9-SNP score is associated (adjusted p 0.0004, OR 1.05 per allele).
2. In controls, WWOX rs17797882 shows no association with HOMA-beta (effect 0.003, p 0.91), HOMA-IR (p 0.59) or fasting glucose (p 0.59).
3. Supplementary Table S3: power to replicate the original WWOX association is 42 percent, the lowest of the nine loci.
4. Supplementary Table S2: with older controls the WWOX OR for allele C falls to 0.94 to 0.95 (age 50 or above) and 0.88 to 0.89 (age 60 or above), opposite to the original 1.08; the Discussion nonetheless says most SNPs keep the original direction except KCNK16.
5. Allele convention: this paper treats C (frequency about 0.79) as the risk allele; the Harbin paper read earlier in this lot analysed T (frequency about 0.24) as the "risk allele reported in the previous report". The two analyses are of opposite alleles of the same SNP; neither says so.

## 3 · Comparison with the laboratory's records

- This paper does what the Harbin paper could not: Bonferroni correction, a sample 3.7 times larger, an explicit power estimate. It returns null for both T2D risk and the glycaemic traits. The only WWOX-HOMA-beta datum in the human-genetics lot (Harbin, nominal p 0.014, controls only) is therefore not reproduced in Japanese controls. The Ouyang 2021 citation chain ("WWOX is related to HOMA-beta") ends in that unreproduced nominal result.
- Selection question 2 (insulin secretion or amino-acid handling): the insulin-secretion arm loses its only nominal support; the amino-acid arm (rs72487966, Ouyang 2021) has its own weak support and is a different variant.
- A reading-level warning for the registry: any record that says "rs17797882 associates with lower HOMA-beta" should carry the Japanese null and the allele-convention conflict.

## 4 · What the paper does NOT support

- A WWOX effect on T2D risk or glycaemic traits in Japanese; equally, it cannot exclude a small effect (power 42 percent).
- Any statement on WWOX function or loss of function.

## 6 · Epistemic tags and reopening

- DATO: no association of rs17797882 with T2D or HOMA traits in this Japanese sample. INFERENZA: the Harbin HOMA-beta signal is a chance finding or an allele or population artefact. PREMISE: DATO (Tables 3 and 4 and S2).
- Rejected: citing WWOX variation as a HOMA-beta or T2D determinant. REVIVAL_TRIGGER: a genome-wide-significant replication, or WWOX eQTL and HOMA-beta data in a matched cohort.
