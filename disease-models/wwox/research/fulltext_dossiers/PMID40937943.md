## 1 · What the paper did

Twelve iPSC lines — four African-ancestry, four Amerindian-ancestry, four European-ancestry; six from AD patients and six from non-cognitively-impaired donors; four APOE ε4/ε4 and eight ε3/ε3 — were differentiated into neural spheroids containing oligodendrocyte-lineage cells and profiled with the 10x Multiome platform (single-nucleus RNA-seq + ATAC-seq) plus Hi-C. 47 898 nuclei, average 3991 per sample. Clusters were labelled by marker genes; the two of interest are iOPC (progenitors) and iOL (oligodendrocytes). Differential expression between ancestry groups within each cluster used MAST with the cell detection rate as covariate.

## 2 · Where WWOX appears — all four occurrences

1. **Results sentence:** in the iOL cluster the AD-GWAS genes differentially expressed between ancestries are listed as "ADAM17, CLU, EPDR1, FGF12, MAF, and WWOX".
2. **Table 1, AF versus AI iOL:** WWOX, fold change **−1.34**, "increased in" AF, adjusted p **1.95E-05**, not a male-versus-female DEG.
3. **Table 1, EU versus AI iOL:** WWOX, fold change **−1.33**, "increased in" EU, adjusted p **9.59E-03**, not a male-versus-female DEG.
4. **All-female re-analysis:** in the iOPC cluster, WWOX (with ANK3) becomes significant between AI and AF females.

That is the entire WWOX content. The paper never perturbs WWOX, never measures WWOX protein, never relates WWOX to any myelin, cholesterol or AD readout, and never mentions WWOX again — including in the Discussion.

## 3 · What the paper states about WWOX, stated exactly

WWOX transcript abundance differs between ancestry groups of iPSC-derived oligodendrocytes, being lower in the Amerindian-ancestry lines than in the African- and European-ancestry lines, at an effect size of about 1.3-fold.

## 4 · What the paper does NOT state

- It does not state anything about WWOX and AD risk, WWOX and myelination, or WWOX function. WWOX is not among the genes it discusses for cholesterol biosynthesis or myelination, which are its actual findings.
- It does not compare WWOX between AD and control lines, nor between APOE genotypes.
- It does not measure WWOX in any brain tissue; the correlation with human brain transcriptomes (R² > 0.85) is reported for the model as a whole, not for WWOX.

## 5 · Measurement limits that bear on how far the number travels

- **Pseudoreplication.** The MAST model carries the cell detection rate as covariate; the donor line is not a random effect. With four lines per ancestry, p = 1.95E-05 is a nucleus-level statistic, not a per-donor inference, so it is not the strength it looks like.
- **Ancestry is confounded with line.** Each ancestry group is four donors; any line-specific effect is an ancestry effect in this design.
- **The all-female arm is two AI donors** against four. The authors themselves note that seven of nine AD-GWAS genes lost significance when males were excluded — an instability that bears directly on the two WWOX rows.
- **Sign convention.** Table 1 pairs a negative fold change with an "increased in" column, so a bare "−1.34" cannot be read without its row.
- **Internal inconsistency worth noting:** the Results sentence says "only five AD GWAS hits" and then names six genes, of which WWOX is the sixth. The Methods report 11 clusters (identities 0–10) while the Results say "seven cell clusters shared by all the individual samples".
- iPSC-derived oligodendroglia are immature relative to adult brain, and there is no myelin readout tied to WWOX.

## 6 · Transfer limit for the WWOX-DEE model

This is a measured WWOX expression datum in named oligodendrocyte-lineage cells of human origin — which the model otherwise lacks — but what varies is **ancestry background in a dish**, not WWOX dose, and nothing downstream of WWOX is measured. It cannot support any statement about cell autonomy, about the consequence of WWOX loss in oligodendrocytes, or about myelin. Its one legitimate use is as a **denominator fact**: WWOX transcript is present and quantifiable in iPSC-derived OPCs and oligodendrocytes, and varies between donor backgrounds by about 1.3-fold at baseline — a baseline-variance figure any future WWOX-in-oligodendrocyte experiment must exceed.
