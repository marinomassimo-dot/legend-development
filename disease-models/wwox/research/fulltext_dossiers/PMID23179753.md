## What the paper did
Five hESC-derived systems (named UKK, UKN1, UKN4, JRC, UNIGE) were exposed to valproic acid (VPA), methylmercury (MeHg) and negative controls (mannitol, DMSO) and profiled on Affymetrix arrays (4-5 independent experiments per condition; 190 arrays). Questions: can compounds be discriminated, how many arrays are needed, can batch effects be controlled, is the highest non-cytotoxic concentration the right one.
- UKK: H9 hESC, embryoid bodies, 14 days, all germ layers. UKN1: H9, dual-SMAD neural induction, chemical present from day of differentiation 0 for 6 days. JRC: H9 embryoid-body-derived neuroepithelial rosettes, up to 10 days. All H9 (one cell line). Exposure bars: Figure 1 inspected.
- VPA test concentrations are the benchmark concentration (highest concentration with at most 10 percent viability loss), system-specific: JRC 1.21 and 1.05 mM, UKK 2 mM and 0.5 mM, UKN1 0.6 mM. The authors cite 500-1000 uM as human-relevant for VPA.
- Statistics: limma moderated t; Benjamini-Yekutieli FDR for the supplementary lists.

## WWOX content (class level; no allele, no patient)
- WWOX appears **zero times in the running text** (and zero in the supplementary PDF by scripted search). It appears only as a gene symbol in the supplementary xls (Table S1).
- Fold-change convention stated in the supplement: below 1 down, above 1 up (ratio, not log).
- WWOX rows, all **up** with VPA, FDR-adjusted p below 0.05 in each: JRC 1.21 mM two probe sets (ratio 1.96, adj p 0.00229; 1.85, adj p 0.027); JRC 1.05 mM one (2.01, adj p 0.0372); UKK 2 mM two (2.12, adj p 0.00339; 1.47, adj p 0.0227).
- Absent from the UKK 0.5 mM list (4 significant probe sets in total) and from the UKN1 0.6 mM list (3697 probe sets); no WWOX row in any MeHg list.
- No row anywhere shows WWOX down with VPA.
- Direction: **increase**, in human neural-lineage cells, at concentrations at or above 1 mM (the 0.6 mM neural-induction system did not list it).

## What the paper does NOT say
Nothing about WWOX in any sentence; no qPCR, protein, or time-course for WWOX; no neuron or glia maturation stage beyond the immature neuroepithelial state; no therapeutic concentration; no WWOX allele.

## Caveats given by the authors
Responses depend on the test system (less than 20 percent overlap between systems); FDR correction removed most apparent hits at low concentration (UKK 500 uM: 2524 up before, four after); batch effects were detected and partly corrected; concentrations beyond the benchmark were not necessarily non-specific.

## Transfer limit
Cultured H9 hESC-derived cells at millimolar VPA; probe-level ratios; one cell line; no allele of any kind. It says nothing about a patient with two loss-of-function alleles; there is no residual WWOX to raise.
