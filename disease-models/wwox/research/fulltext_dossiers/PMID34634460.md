## What the pass added

1. The supplement contradicts the main text on the burst rates of the one dataset (119 bursts, 18 slices, 9 animals): median frequency 2.29 per minute in the Results against 0.84 in Supplementary Table S1; carbenoxolone reduces it by 87 percent in the text and from 0.84 to 0.03 (96 percent) in the table; the pannexin blocker leaves the table median at 0.78 against 0.84 while Figure 3D plots about a 2.5-fold rise. Neither source defines the median (per burst or per slice). The earlier reading's finding that the pannexin-blocker bar rises is confirmed on the panel; the drug-effect sizes are therefore not reliable to better than "carbenoxolone strongly reduces, d-APV abolishes, pannexin blocker does not reduce".
2. Metabolic condition of the preparation: slices are cut in sucrose and held and recorded in ACSF with 10 mM glucose; donors were pentobarbital-anaesthetised. The knockout slice bursts in a glucose-replete bath, so the cortical hyperexcitability of this neuron-specific knockout is not an acute hypoglycaemic effect in the dish. It does not tell whether prior in-vivo hypoglycaemia or ketosis of the donor primed the network (donor glucose is not reported; the companion gene-therapy paper shows null-mouse hypoglycaemia rescued by neuronal re-expression).
3. Supplementary Figure S3 (evoked delayed NMDAR response, washout above baseline) and S1 (phase-amplitude coupling controls: low-frequency power versus coupling index uncorrelated, animal-level significance in 8 of 9 for delta) were opened; coverage now complete.
4. Panels of Figures 1 to 6 re-read independently on the bucket JPEGs agree with the earlier locators (Figure 5D prints p = 0.0312, Figure 6 asterisks, Figure 4 asymmetry).

## Does not support

A metabolic mechanism of the hyperexcitability; a therapeutic effect (slice pharmacology only; carbenoxolone also blocks NMDA receptors; effect persists after washout); heterozygote equivalence (4 of 23 slices against 36 of 42); human relevance beyond a phenotype-level analogue. Transfer to the reference genotype: none (neuron-specific mouse knockout, P13 to P17, near death).

Revival triggers: donor blood glucose and ketones with slice phenotype; recordings in low or ketone-containing bath; a definition of the median in the supplement. Commit candidate: none (no registry record changes).
