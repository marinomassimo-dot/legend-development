## 3 · What it establishes

1. **`DATO` — zyxin is upregulated on WWOX knockdown, at protein and mRNA level, multiplicity-corrected.**
   Supplemental Table S1, row 2051: `ZYX`, UniProt Q15942, BH-adj *P* 0.0001, **log2FC +0.55**
   (16.19 → 16.75) ≈ **1.46-fold**. Reproduced by Western blot and by qPCR (Fig 1D, 1E; ~1.45-fold,
   n = 6). 🔴 **The effect is real, corrected, and modest** — it only just clears the ±0.5
   fold-change line the volcano plot draws.
2. **`DATO` — SOD2 is downregulated**, row 1742, BH-adj *P* 0.0001, **log2FC −0.94** — a larger
   effect than zyxin's. Not pursued experimentally in this paper.
3. **`DATO` — WWOX silencing roughly doubles stretch-induced IL-6 and IL-8 in human ECs.**
   IL-6 239.5 ± 21.8 vs 113.77 ± 5.0 pg/mL; IL-8 128.42 ± 3.11 vs 76.10 ± 3.5 pg/mL. This is a
   **cytokine measurement by ELISA**, not a signalling inference and not a transcriptome enrichment.
4. **`DATO` — the signalling change is directionally SPLIT.** ERK↑ and JNK↑, but **p38 DOWN**, both
   basally and under stretch. A uniform "MAPK activation" reading of this paper would be wrong.
5. **`DATO` — EC `Wwox` KO mice sustain worse VILI**, at n = 10 per group: BAL protein 0.82 ± 0.16
   vs 0.53 ± 0.08 mg/mL, BAL cells 1.59 ± 0.34 vs 0.91 ± 0.17 ×10⁵/mL, plus H&E infiltration.
6. **🔴 `DATO` — and here inflammation IS the positive, measured endpoint.** BAL **IL-6, KC, IL-1β
   and MCP-1 are all significantly higher** in VILI-exposed KO mice (Fig 5; every panel carries a
   significance bracket on the VILI KO-vs-flox comparison). This is what the companion paper looked
   for twice and did not find.
7. **`DATO` — isolated KO ECs reproduce it ex vivo, and stretch-conditionally.** All four cytokines
   higher after stretch; the unstimulated comparisons are marked `ns` for IL-6, KC and IL-1β.
8. **🔴 `DATO`/`INFERENZA` — zyxin's role is demonstrated by INTERVENTION, and it is SELECTIVE.**
   Dual silencing produced a **35% reduction** in stretch-induced IL-8 versus WWOX silencing alone
   (Fig 7D) — while **IL-6 was not reduced** (Fig 7E, the WWOX-vs-dual comparison marked `ns`).
   **This selectivity is the strongest thing in the paper**: it is what distinguishes a specific
   mediator of one cytokine limb from a general inflammatory switch, and it is an intervention
   result, not a correlation.
9. **`DATO` — WWOX silencing increases nuclear zyxin**, ~12% → ~35%, reverted by dual silencing
   (~8%), n = 3, blinded scorer.
10. **`DATO` — the deletion is endothelium-restricted.** Supplemental Fig. S1: WWOX is **retained**
    in BAL immune cells of EC WWOX KO mice, which is the control's intended result.

## 4 · What it does **not** establish

- **It does not establish causation from zyxin to the phenotype in vivo.** The dual-silencing
  intervention is **in vitro only**. No zyxin manipulation was performed in any mouse; VILI was
  never run with zyxin knocked down.
- **It does not establish the WWOX→zyxin mechanism.** The authors offer two untested alternatives
  (*"either by increasing zyxin promoter activity or decreasing zyxin mRNA degradation"*) and a
  SMAD3 hypothesis assembled from two **other** papers' findings (refs 31, 32), tested nowhere here.
  `PREMISE: INFERENZA` on both links.
- **It does not establish nuclear translocation rigorously, and the authors say so:** *"the analysis
  of zyxin nuclear translocation is limited by the absence of nuclear fractionation studies or
  confocal imaging with z-stacking."* An author-declared limit, and the endpoint is a blinded
  **visual** colocalisation score.
- 🔴 **It does not measure barrier function as a distinct quantity.** This is the direct answer to my
  brief's third question on this paper: **there is no ECIS/TER and no transwell assay anywhere in
  this paper.** The companion paper's barrier assays are *dropped*. "Barrier" here is represented
  only by **BAL protein concentration in vivo** — a permeability proxy that is equally a read-out of
  injury — so barrier function and inflammatory output are **not dissociated in this study**; they
  move together and are reported as *"correlated"*.
- 🔴 **The paper's own verb for the leak/cytokine relation is "correlated"**, not caused: *"Increased
  BAL protein and alveolar cellular infiltration … correlated with increased BAL IL-6, KC, IL-1β,
  and MCP-1 production."* No mediation analysis; no cytokine blockade.
- **It does not establish a p38 mechanism** — p38 falls, and nothing follows from it.
- **It does not link exposure to the phenotype.** No smoke and no e-cigarette arm exists here; the
  smoke premise is imported from reference 3 (the companion paper) and reference 4.
- **It measures no physiological outcome** — no mortality, no gas exchange, no compliance.
- **It says nothing about brain, CNS, development or a WWOX allele series.** The word *brain* appears
  nowhere in the article.

## 6 · Design limits

- **No in vivo zyxin intervention** — the mechanistic claim's intervention exists only in culture.
- **No barrier assay at all** (§4), so this paper cannot separate barrier failure from inflammation.
- **Fig 3B**: a significance claim with no visible statistics, over ratios computed **across two
  gels**.
- **`n = 3` transfections** for the decisive dual-silencing cytokine panels (7D/7E) — the paper's
  most important result rests on its smallest *n*.
- **Mixed units of replication**: mice (n = 10), cultures (n = 5), transfections (n = 3, 4, 6).
- **Uneven point counts within panels** (Figs 2 and 4) against a single stated *n*.
- 🔴 **The human cell type differs from the companion paper's**: pulmonary **artery** ECs here,
  lung **microvascular** ECs there. Both are "ECs" in running text; they are different vascular beds,
  and the microvasculature is the compartment where ARDS leak occurs.
- **A load-bearing dependency carries an erratum:** reference 15 (PMID 20705921, Wójtowicz 2010,
  *Circ Res*) — erratum *Circ Res* 2010;107(9):e15-6, printed in this article's own reference list
  and confirmed via Europe PMC. That paper is the **sole** source for zyxin's stretch-induced IL-8
  enhancer function, i.e. the reason zyxin was picked out of the screen at all. **Its scope is
  unknown to me — I did not retrieve it — so this is a prompt to read, never a claim about either
  paper. An erratum is not a retraction.** `dependency_integrity.py` itself returned no flag.
- **Group:** same senior laboratory as the companion paper (Singla, 19 publications, 4 on WWOX);
  Aldaz again contributes the **reagent** at position 9 of 11. The author list is *not* the same —
  Abdelwahid, Ascoli and Pham join; Ware, Crotty Alexander, Moshensky, Shakir and Khan (the human
  cohort and e-cigarette contributors) are gone, consistent with a study that dropped the exposure
  arms. 🔴 **Weighting cuts the other way from the companion paper:** this is a positive result
  congruent with the group's prior interest, so the *interpretation* is weighted as such — what
  carries the zyxin conclusion is the intervention and its selectivity, not the group's authority.

## 8 · Transfer toward the reference genotype — stated separately from the tissue-level finding

**The tissue-level finding:** in pulmonary endothelium, WWOX loss (~85% knockdown, adult-induced)
amplifies the inflammatory response to **mechanical** stretch, measured as cytokine secretion in
vitro and in BAL in vivo, with ERK/JNK phosphorylation up and p38 down, and with zyxin upregulation
accounting for part of the IL-8 limb by intervention.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Does not transfer — stimulus** | The stimulus is **ventilator-induced mechanical stretch at 40 mL/kg**. There is no physiological analogue of high-tidal-volume ventilation in the CNS. This is the sharpest non-transfer in the pair: the finding is *about mechanotransduction*, and zyxin is a focal-adhesion mechanosensor. |
| **Does not transfer — tissue** | Pulmonary **artery** endothelium here; not CNS endothelium, no blood-brain barrier, no astrocyte, no pericyte, no neuron. |
| **Does not transfer — allele class / timing** | Adult tamoxifen-induced conditional knockdown, not germline biallelic loss from conception; everything acute (4 h). |
| **Transfers as `ESPANSIONE`, to be tested** | Two named molecular nodes are *generically* interesting: **SOD2 loss** (mitochondrial superoxide handling, log2FC −0.94 — the *larger* proteomic effect, and unpursued) and the **WWOX–SMAD3** link the authors invoke. Both are gene-level, not tissue-level, so they are the only pieces with any claim to travel — and neither is tested here. |
| **Transfers as a caution** | 🔴 The **p38 decrease** matters for any model that treats "WWOX loss → MAPK activation" as a single direction. In this tissue the three MAPKs do not move together. |
| **Does not transfer** | The zyxin→IL-8 axis itself. IL-8/CXCL8 has no direct murine orthologue (KC is used as the proxy), the mediator is a mechanosensor, and the whole axis rests on one paper intersecting WWOX. |

**Stated plainly: this is a ventilator-injury paper about a mechanosensor. Nothing in its mechanism
reaches the CNS, and the one element with generic reach — SOD2 — is the element the paper does not
pursue.**
