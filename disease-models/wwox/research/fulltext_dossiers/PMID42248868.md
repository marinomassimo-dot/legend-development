## What the paper did
Retrospective review of 3206 consecutive ES/GS cases (May 2024–May 2025) with provider opt-in for targeted reflex RNA-seq. 131 cases (139 unique variants) were eligible (phenotype-concordant gene; VUS or LP; SpliceAI Δ ≥ 0.228). RNA from **EDTA peripheral blood**, amplicon-based targeted RNA-seq covering all junctions of the gene, ≥ 50× junction reads, nine healthy controls; a splice event counted when the net abnormal-junction fraction exceeded 20 % versus controls. 64 cases completed; 27 had ≥ 1 variant reclassified (42.2 %).

## Method transfer to splice-allele questions
Measured consequence via blood RNA is feasible for WWOX in a clinical assay; it gives junction usage, not transcript abundance or NMD, and a 20 % net abnormal-junction threshold. A canonical acceptor allele (e.g. a −1 or −2 position) would usually fall outside "eligible" only if already called P/LP; criteria (2) admit LP variants.

## Authors' caveats
About half of eligible cases were incomplete (awaiting a new blood sample); 20 % threshold internally set; EDTA blood only; no NMD or expression read-out; no functional validation.
