## What the paper did
- INS1201: AAV9, MHCK7 (muscle-derived) promoter, micro-dystrophin, for Duchenne muscular dystrophy. Target tissue is muscle, reached from CSF.
- Efficacy in male mdx mice, single ICV dose at about postnatal day 28: 8.0E+09, 2.0E+11, 4.0E+11, 8.0E+11 vg per mouse, plus 1.6E+12 in long-term
  studies (to about 1 year). Groups of 6-12. Untreated or vehicle mdx controls pooled (vehicle vs untreated no difference, Fig S8, not read).
- GLP mouse toxicology: wild-type male p28 mice, n = 45 per dosed group (vehicle and three doses), single ICV dose of 2.0E+11, 4.0E+11, 8.0E+11 vg, sacrifice at 22, 43 and 85 days.
- NHP: non-GLP biodistribution (n = 2 per dose, 2.5E+13 to 2.0E+14 vg, lumbar IT, 3 weeks, 1-3 years old); GLP 3-month study (n = 3 per dose at 5.28E+13, 1.09E+14, 3.05E+14 vg, 2.4 mL, plus 3 vehicle; 7-12 months old, 1-2 kg).
- Dose translation mouse to NHP by CSF volume: a 250-fold scale-up (0.04 mL to 10 mL); 8.0E+11 vg in mouse is called equivalent to 2.0E+14 vg in NHP.
- AAV9-GFP proof of concept in NHP: n = 1 per route (IT: three doses in one animal; IV: two doses in one animal).

The Introduction states the planned human fixed starting doses (5.0E+14 vg and 1.0E+15 vg), which exceed the highest NHP per-animal total tested (3.05E+14 vg); the margin is therefore computed through the scaling the authors choose (not printed in the body; Table S3 unread), a per-animal-total comparison alone would give a margin below one. The Introduction also says micro-dystrophin protein is not expected to be abundant in the CNS because of the promoter, i.e. DRG vector-genome presence without expression was the design expectation.

## What it states (carried numbers)
- Efficacy: histology and function improved in mdx from 2.0E+11 vg/mouse; abstract states wild-type-level neuromuscular strength was not reached; benefit plateaued across doses,
  which the authors ascribe to treatment age (p27-35, after cycles of degeneration), not to dose ceiling.
- Toxicity (mouse): no mortality or clinical/pathology effects; minimal focal mononuclear infiltrates in brain along the injection path, no dose-response, judged non-adverse;
  DRG "healthy cells and normal histopathology" at the high dose (Fig 8E, one image from one animal at 8.0E+11 vg). NOAEL = 8.0E+11 vg per mouse, the maximum feasible dose (volume and titre limited).
- Toxicity (NHP): no INS1201-related effects on clinical signs, neurologic exam, nerve conduction velocity, CSF cell counts, T-cell responses, histopathology; transient individual changes in platelets, AST and ALT with no
  microscopic correlate. Anti-AAV9 antibodies in all treated animals at days 28 and 92/93. NOAEL = 3.05E+14 vg per animal, the highest dose.
- Biodistribution: vector genomes in DRG, spinal cord, brain and liver after CSF dosing (mouse DRG 6.408 vg/diploid genome at 12 weeks high dose; NHP liver 187 and 285 vg/dg at 1.09E+14 and 3.05E+14);
  no statistically meaningful mouse-NHP difference in muscle, brain, DRG, kidney at the scaled dose; liver, lung, spleen and spinal cord higher in NHP.
  Transgene mRNA only in muscle (plus two of three midbrain samples in high-dose NHP); none in DRG or spinal cord. Micro-dystrophin protein absent in brain, cord, DRG and liver in mdx.
- Shedding: CSF vg cleared by day 24 after IT dosing; serum peak at 6 h on day 1, baseline by day 28; urine clear by day 28.
- Dose arithmetic from stated weights (INFERENZA, my calculation): mouse top dose 8.0E+11 vg / 18.6 g about 4.3E+13 vg/kg; NHP top GLP dose 3.05E+14 vg in a 1-2 kg animal about 1.5E+14 to 3E+14 vg/kg.
  The paper itself converts only the IV GFP animals to vg/kg (about 3.1E+13 and 6.0E+13).

## What it does NOT state / measure
No immunosuppression regimen is described for any study (the Introduction names it as a strategy for dystrophin immunity). T-cell response methods are not given in the text I read (Tables S4-S5 and Figs S7 not fetched).
Antibody titres are reported only as present/absent in treated animals, not as a magnitude or neutralising activity. No long-term (beyond 3 months) NHP safety. Males only. Single dose. NOAEL is not a threshold.
Not a neuronal transgene: DRG exposure was to vector genomes without expression, so neuronal overexpression toxicity (the usual DRG concern) was not tested. NHP DRG-specific histopathology is covered only
by the general "histopathology" statement; the DRG-specific text refers to mice. No data on neonatal or infant dosing; the animals are juvenile (p28 mouse; 7-12 month NHP).

## Authors' own caveats
Mouse top dose limited by ICV volume and titre; IT in mouse not feasible so ICV used and equivalence rests on a CSF-volume scale-up; higher NHP spinal-cord vector genomes attributed to the lumbar IT route; relatively high diaphragm
vector genomes with lower expression; sample sizes small for the species comparison ("small sample sizes were available").

## Transfer limits for the reference genotype class
Muscle-restricted promoter: DRG, brain and cord safety here is vector-genome presence, capsid and procedure only, not transgene dose in neurons. Wild-type juvenile males, not a WWOX-deficient or neonatal brain;
mdx efficacy dose-response does not carry. CSF-volume scaling is the authors' choice and is not validated against a neuronal target. A NOAEL at the maximum feasible dose bounds nothing from above. Sponsor study, not independently replicated.
