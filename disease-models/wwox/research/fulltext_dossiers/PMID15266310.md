## 3 · What is measured — and at what level

1. **WWOX mRNA is absent or low in many HCC lines** — Northern, unquantified, saturated at the strong end (L9, L11, L12).
2. **WWOX protein is below half of normal liver in all 17 lines shown, and below 10% in 13** (Figure 4B pixels, L19, L21). The text's denominator of 18 does not match the 17 lanes displayed.
3. **Aberrant transcripts in two lines:** a Δ6–8 isoform and a new Δ6–8 transcript carrying a 96-bp intron-8 insert, in-frame (L14, L15, L18). The encoded protein is predicted, never shown (L17).
4. **Four coding variants** in five lines: D183N (7703, Focus), A179T (HepG2, Hep40, a known polymorphism), P252A (SNU387), R314H (PLC/PRF/5) (L28–L30).
5. **Normal hepatocytes stain granular-cytoplasmic, stroma negative**; 2 of 5 HCC weak or moderate, 1 negative, 2 not described (L25–L27).
6. **Co-loss with FHIT** in 3 of 4 WWOX-negative lines, on a 10-line table (L32, L33).

## 6 · Strength, and transferability to the reference genotype

- **Strength:** descriptive expression survey in long-passaged cell lines, with no statistics, several "not shown" results and internal count errors. The **sequence findings** (the Δ6–8 transcript and its 96-bp insert) are the most solid items in the paper, because they rest on sequencing rather than band intensity.
- **Transferability: T3 overall.** Adult somatic liver cancer; no germline allele, no neural tissue, no developmental readout.
- **What this model might use, stated at the strength it has:**
  - **P252A is reported in the SNU387 line** and dismissed as a polymorphism without evidence (L28, L31). DATO that it was observed; nothing about its function. Its origin (germline or somatic) is unknowable from this paper.
  - **A splice-level datum:** in-frame exon 5–9 junctions (Δ6–8), one carrying 32 intron-8-derived residues (L15, L18). DATO in two HCC lines; any bearing on splice-site alleles in the disease is ESPANSIONE.
  - **A reagent datum:** the IHC primary is printed as **140 μg/ml** in this structured surface (L2). *INFERENZA (cross-reading, not canon):* the author manuscript of Ramos 2008, the form example for this task, prints the same group's anti-WWOX as "140 mg / ml". The present surface supports reading that as a lost micro sign, for what is plausibly the same stock. Not proven identical.
