## What the paper did
CNV calling from Illumina bead-chip intensities (cnvHap) and association (MultiPhen) with epilepsy phenotypes in SANAD (n = 916, discovery) and an Australian cohort (n = 380; ~30 % excluded for batch effects), meta-analysis of both; CNV-eQTL in UKBEC (134 neuropathologically normal brains, ten regions) and NABEC; methylation and RNA-seq look-ups in five **ICGC cancer cohorts** (Ewing sarcoma, CLL, lymphoma, paediatric brain tumours).

## WWOX content
1. **The common intronic deletion is a population polymorphism, used as a calibration target.** Methods: the WWOX intronic deletion at chr16:78,371,638–78,385,000 (GRCh37) from gnomAD was chosen to tune the CNV pipeline; in SANAD it was "re-discovered … as common deletions with an allele frequency of 47%". Supplementary Figure 19 (gnomAD panel, read as image) shows this deletion at allele frequency 3.39e-1 with **1447 homozygotes**. A deletion carried homozygously by more than a thousand gnomAD individuals is not a WWOX-DEE allele.
2. **The headline "most distinct" WWOX signal is not one signal.** The Results sentence joins (a) the common deletion (MAF 47 %, "48 contiguous probes"), (b) a meta-analysis genotype p = 0.00011 at chr16:79,246,323 and (c) an LRR meta-p = 4.65×10⁻²² at chr16:79,043,240 for "Time to first seizure censoring variable". Supplementary Table 4 (read cell-wise): (a) in SANAD the rows with |MAF| > 0.4 lie at 78,373,644–78,384,121 and their best p for drug_response is 1.68×10⁻⁴ (LRR univariate; genotype univariate 7.72×10⁻³); for every other phenotype p ≥ 0.037; (b, c) lie ~660–860 kb downstream, near the 3′ end of the gene, in LRR rows whose "MAF" column is not an allele frequency (negative values occur in LRR rows). So the genome-wide-strength numbers do **not** belong to the 47 % deletion, and the deletion's own association is nominal. The authors themselves caution: "Since WWOX has a well-known fragile site (FRA16D27) further experimental validation is required".
3. **"Exclusively associated with the drug-response phenotype in SANAD"** — supported in the narrow sense that the deletion's best p is for drug response; not replicated in the Australian cohort ("no CNVs with MAF > 1%").
4. **Methylation:** mean methylated read counts in the deletion interval 6.8 / 20.57 / 31.6 in BOCA-FR / PBCA-DE / CLLE-ES — these are **cancer** cohorts (Ewing sarcoma, paediatric brain tumours, CLL), not epilepsy or normal brain; Supplementary Figure 1b (read as image) agrees with the text.
5. **CNV-eQTL:** putamen gene-by-gene analysis names WWOX exon 5 (probe id 3700865) as most significant (Supplementary Figure 15; panel labels illegible at the deposited resolution — the exon call rests on the text).
6. **An imprecise disease sentence:** "Missense mutations in exons of WWOX have been reported to be associated with highly pathogenic WOREE syndrome" — uncited at that point; WOREE is not restricted to missense alleles (INFERENZA from held literature, to be checked against the claim registry in the comparison step).

## Transfer limit
Nothing here is a WWOX-DEE patient or an allele-class measurement. The paper speaks to (i) a common, often homozygous intronic deletion as a genotyping/fragile-site hazard and (ii) nominal association with antiseizure-drug response in adults and children with new-onset epilepsy (SANAD); it says nothing about biallelic loss of function.

## What the owed sections show

1. **The drug-response association does not survive a correction, even inside the gene.** Table 4 holds,
   for WWOX, 16,980 data rows (14,283 unique; the sheet repeats rows), SANAD drug-response rows covering
   559 distinct probe locations and 1,609 distinct (mode, location) pairs. The best drug-response p is
   1.68e-4 (LRR univariate, chr16:78383363, inside the deletion), above a Bonferroni threshold of
   0.05 / 559 = 8.9e-5 (or 3.1e-5 over the pairs). The best genotype-based p is 4.92e-3. The paper's
   stated correction is a Sidak-type adjustment for the correlation structure of the phenotype matrix;
   neither M nor the number of variant tests is printed, so the table's p is nominal as the record says.
2. **"Drug response" is binary 12-month remission** (Table 13: 1 = achieved, 0 = did not).
3. **Supplementary Figure 19 verified on the same bytes:** gnomAD row DEL_16_156229, intronic deletion,
   78371638-78384898 (13.3 kb), allele count 7355 / allele number 21694 = 0.3390 recomputed, 1447
   homozygotes; the overlapping multi-allelic record spans 78371850-78385000 at 5911 / 10847 = 0.545. The
   paper's printed region 78,371,638-78,385,000 joins the two entries (the text says "a deletion and a
   multi-CNV with allele frequency of 34% to 54%"). Earlier finding (AF 0.339, 1447 homozygotes) confirmed.
4. **Supplementary Figure 6** (viewed): UKBEC CNV genotype against exon-level WWOX expression, ten regions
   as heatmaps of -log(p); colour keys reach about 3-6; most cells pale or grey (missing); a few red cells in
   specific location rows (strongest in hippocampus and white matter). No cell values, direction, effect
   size or correction are printed, and the main text only points to these heatmaps for "other seizure
   associated epilepsy genes (e.g., WWOX)"; it makes no claim from them. Unresolvable at row level.

## Limits that bind

sheet's duplicate rows inflate row counts, so the test counts above use distinct locations. Nothing here is a
dosage datum for one functional WWOX copy.

## A · The 1447 annotation survives — at 400 dpi

Supplementary Figure 19 (page 21 of `MOESM15_ESM.pdf`) was previously read at **70 dpi**. Re-rendered

## B · What the same table shows around it

The row is the first of **fourteen** gnomAD structural-variant rows listed inside WWOX, nearly all
intronic, and it is not alone in being common. The next three rows, inside the same highlighted
block: `DEL_16_156234` (89 bp, AF **3.18e-1**, **220** homozygotes), `INS_16_101038` (321 bp, AF
1.86e-1, 122 homozygotes), `INS_16_101054` (321 bp, AF 1.01e-1, **327** homozygotes). The overlapping
multi-allelic `MCNV_16_842` (78371850–78385000) reads 5911 / 10847 = 0.5449 (printed 5.45e-1), with
no homozygote count. **WWOX carries several common intronic structural variants at population
frequency**; the deletion used in this paper is the most frequent of them, not a singular event.

## C · Supplementary Table 1 — the authors' own top results do not include WWOX

`MOESM1_ESM.xlsx` lists the **rank-1** result for every cohort × method × phenotype (139 packed

the only chromosome-16 entry is `2.91e-09; 16:75296761; BCAR1` in a UKBEC expression analysis. For
