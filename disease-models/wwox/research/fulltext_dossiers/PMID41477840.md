## 1 · What the paper did

Human stem-cell-derived glial progenitor cells (hGPCs) were converted in 3D spheroids into GABAergic interneurons,
including parvalbumin (PV) chandelier-like cells, with a lentiviral transcription-factor cocktail (ADLSF);
conversion was followed by snRNA-seq at days 0–21, patch clamp, immunostaining, O-PTIR and high-content imaging.
A "PV trajectory" was reconstructed by RNA velocity/latent time and diffusion pseudotime; adding RORA did not raise
PV yield.

## 3 · What the paper does NOT state

- No WWOX knockdown, knockout, overexpression or rescue; nothing about whether WWOX is required for PV fate.
- No WWOX protein measurement in the reprogrammed cells.
- No statement about WWOX in oligodendrocyte-lineage cells. The starting population is a stem-cell-derived glial
  progenitor culture; the panels show WWOX transcript in it, which is not a statement about myelinating cells.
- No dose: "average expression" over time is not a dose–response relation.

- "its ablation leads to a significant hippocampal reduction of PV interneurons (72)": Hussain 2019 (PMID 30290271,

- Ref. 73 is cited as *Front. Neurosci.* "9, 206–206 (2019)"; the held paper with that author list and title is

## 5 · Caveats the authors give themselves

The 3D culture lacks key inputs (glutamatergic), automated quantification is limited, RNA velocity was applied to
single-nucleus data (corroborated by a second method), bifurcation mechanism not explored, and few functionally
mature interneurons are generated.

## 6 · Transfer limit

No allele, no genotype, no perturbation: **nothing transfers to any WWOX genotype**, and an expression time
course in reprogramming is neither development nor requirement. At most it is one human, cell-resolved
observation that WWOX transcript rises transiently along an in-vitro interneuron trajectory and is already
present in the glial progenitors it starts from.
