**What the Ldh rescue is and is not.** It rescues *climbing* only. It was not tested for lifespan,
Abeta42 load, Atf4, 2-hydroxyglutarate or lactate. Controls missing (reader findings, INFERENZA): no arm
with Ldh RNAi and Abeta42 without Wwox knockdown (so a Wwox-independent benefit of Ldh knockdown cannot be
excluded — the authors themselves cite that Ldh knockdown is detrimental in other fly Abeta42 models);
both hairpins are UAS-RNAi lines on chromosome 3, so GAL4 sharing could weaken the Wwox knockdown in the
double line and Wwox mRNA in that genotype is not reported; lactate was not measured in the double-RNAi
flies; the double-RNAi line reaches or exceeds the *control* line at day 28, which is more than a restoration
to the Wwox-intact level. The authors state "the rescue we witnessed was partial" and that 2-hydroxyglutarate is
"only partially controlled by Ldh" (Discussion). The Discussion then places "Wwox upstream of Atf4, Ldh and
Lactate", wording stronger than the Results.

**HIF1A / sima, exactly.** Introduction: WWOX loss "has been postulated to mediate a Warburg-like effect ... via
modulation of HIF1alpha". Result: "No changes in endogenously tagged sima:GFP (HIF1alpha homolog) levels were
observed in the adult (day 14) fly brains after neuronal Wwox knockdown in Abeta42 overexpressing flies"
(t 0.6623, P 0.5185, n 7-9; intensity about 85 vs about 84, y axis 50-100, Fig 5F-G). Discussion: "our data
suggest that Atf4, rather than the HIF1alpha orthologue sima, is primarily responsible for the observed Ldh
upregulation in this fly model". **Shown:** reporter fluorescence unchanged, one genotype pair, one age, in the
Abeta42 background. **Not shown:** HIF1alpha/sima activity or target genes (no Pdk, Pdha, Pdhb, Hph or Tret1 gene is
in any significant list of Supp Tables 3 and 5 by symbol search; absence at the 0.58 log2 threshold is not a
test), the LacZ background, upregulation, hypoxia, nuclear localisation. The reporter is described three
ways: Methods "genetically encoded reporter line UAS-sima:GFP", Results/legend "endogenously tagged", Supp Table
1A a PBac-insertion stock (BDSC 42672, chromosome 2) crossed with `elav-GAL4`; whether it reports protein stability
or driver expression is therefore unsettled. The paper never writes "HIF1A-independent" as a finding; it
says Atf4 "rather than" sima is "primarily" responsible. LEGEND's CLAIM 025 nuance word is the registry's, not the paper's.

The authors call lactate plus Ldh a "Warburg effect-like shift towards glycolytic energy production" and read
unchanged ATP as the shift "sufficient to maintain energy supply and may be a protective response" — an inference.
There is no flux, glucose uptake, oxygen-consumption, extracellular-acidification or NAD/NADH measurement. **Direction
for LEGEND:** in this fly model lactate rises only when Wwox is *reduced and* amyloid is present, and falls
(nominally) when Wwox is reduced without amyloid (-0.43 log2, Supp Fig 5A). It is a stress-conditional effect of an
overexpressed amyloid, not a statement about the baseline energy state of a WWOX-null neuron.

## 4 · The overexpression arm (question 4)

With Abeta42, neuronal Wwox upregulation: lifespan 29 -> 47.5 d (chi2 104.5, N 78-91, Fig 6A); climbing days 14 and
21 (t 5.669, 6.169; N 3-7 vials, Fig 6B); plaque size about 1.45 -> 0.55 um2 (t 5.485, P 0.0003) and number about 205 ->
70 (t 3.374, P 0.0071), n 5-7 brains (Fig 6D-E); MSD total (t 3.252 in the text, 2.352 in the supplementary legend,
both P 0.0313), soluble (P 0.0357) and insoluble (P 0.0409), n = 3 pools, soluble/insoluble ratio unchanged (Fig 6F-G).
Transcriptome (N 3-4): 305 down, 59 up (Fig 7C); 40 genes shared with Abeta42-vs-LacZ, r = -0.8341 (Fig 7E).
**Lactate:** not shown in any panel; absent from the significant lists of Supp Table 7 for every term, and in that
cohort the Abeta42 main effect itself did not raise lactate, so there was no lactate rise to reverse (the preprint wrote
"without restoring lactate levels"; the published text writes "without a reduction in Ldh or 2-hydroxyglutarate").
Ldh is not reversed (Ldh +2.85 log2 in Abeta42 vs LacZ, Supp Table 5; no Ldh row for the OE comparison).
**Cysteine/methionine:** transcripts of Phgdh, Got1, Got2, Mdh1, Cbs, CG11134, SpdS fall (Fig 8D). L-methionine
(two peaks): Wwox OE lowers it in LacZ (padj 0.0047, 0.0091; about 2.85e6 -> 0.9e6, Supp Fig 8) but **not in Abeta42**
(about 1.5e6 vs 1.46e6); interaction padj 0.0073 and 0.0133. So the "interaction" is the absence of the OE-induced
fall under Abeta42; Abeta42 itself lowers methionine by about half in the control arm (Supp Fig 8; main effect not significant).
Not discussed by the authors: Wwox OE with Abeta42 also lowers glycolytic enzyme transcripts Gapdh1, Eno, Pgk, Pgi,
Tpi (log2 -0.59 to -0.70, padj 1.5e-3 to 7.8e-3; Supp Table 5), Ldh unchanged. *INFERENZA:* the direction is
"more Wwox, less glycolytic-gene expression", consistent with a restraint-of-glycolysis role, but untested here.

## 6 · Limitations (question 6)

**Stated by the authors:** startle climbing may fail late (Discussion, para 1); the Ldh rescue is "partial"; 2-hydroxyglutarate
is "only partially controlled by Ldh" and other enzymes can make it; protection by neuronal OE may run through glia, "an area for
future research"; "we did not determine whether WWOX is activating this pathway directly"; Ldh and lactate "need to be tightly
controlled" (Ldh knockdown is also detrimental in other fly Abeta42 models); future work on "therapeutic implications".
**Not stated (reader findings, INFERENZA unless noted):** one Wwox RNAi line (the preprint's second line, KK, is gone, DATO);
knockdown about 45% by qPCR on newly eclosed heads, no protein; no Atf4 loss- or gain-of-function, no PERK/eIF2alpha readout;
the Ldh rescue lacks an Ldh-alone arm and a Wwox-mRNA check; lactate never measured under rescue; whole-head metabolomics while
glia carry the highest Wwox expression (Fig 1A), so "neuron-derived lactate" is not established; N = 5 pools, 10% FDR, top-200
features, lactate's KD main effect only at padj 0.0636; 2-hydroxyglutarate enantiomer unresolved; no pyruvate, PDH, PDK, flux or
OCR data; ATP n = 4 with very wide spread; ROS in wing; sleep in males vs everything else in females; one omics age (day 14);
Aβ42 is a secreted Arctic mutant expressed in all neurons; MSD n = 3; text/legend disagreements (N 90-99 vs 45-68 in Fig 3A;
t 3.252 vs 2.352; Ldh log2 1.19 vs table 1.1097; sima reporter described three ways); no Limitations section; the title verb
"controls" and the Discussion "place Wwox upstream" exceed the experiments.

## 8 · Transfer verdict toward the reference genotype

Tag: **ESPANSIONE / hypothesis-generating only, T3.** Disease context differs (Alzheimer's amyloid vs WWOX-DEE); partial RNAi (about 45%) in
an adult fly with a transgenic amyloid; no myelin, seizure or developmental endpoint; the human link is cited GWAS/eQTL
expression data, not tested. Relevance to HYP-20260709-01 and DL-MECH-020: (i) the HIF1A->PDK->PDH->blocked pyruvate-to-TCA chain
is **not tested** (no PDH/PDK/TCA-flux data; sima reporter null in one condition); (ii) the only energetic readout is ATP, unchanged;
(iii) lactate rises only with Wwox loss *plus* amyloid, and trends down with Wwox loss alone, so this paper cannot be read for or
against the Warburg direction in WWOX-null neurons; DL-BIO-008 (human brain MRS lactate "extremely low") is not contradicted or
supported by it; (iv) Wwox up-regulation lowering glycolytic-enzyme transcripts is a hypothesis for the discovery ledger.
**Revival triggers:** an Atf4 loss-of-function with Ldh and lactate readouts; lactate measured in the Ldh-rescue genotype; an
Ldh-alone arm; pyruvate, PDH and PDK in the MetaboLights deposit; a second Wwox RNAi line or a null allele.
