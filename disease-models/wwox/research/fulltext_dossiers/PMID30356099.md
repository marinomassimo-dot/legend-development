## What the paper did

Collected 20 patients from 18 families with biallelic WWOX variants and epileptic encephalopathy across six
countries; molecular diagnosis by MLPA, array-CGH, Sanger, panel, exome or genome; parental testing for phase in every
case. Aggregated with the literature to 37 patients / 27 families and proposed genotype-phenotype correlations.

## Measured versus predicted

- [S1] The protein row is headed "(not based on experimental evidence)" for all 20.

  p.Ser318Leu (no splice anomaly in leukocytes; body). Every other consequence, including all four Q230P carriers, is

## Internal inconsistencies the reading found

1. **Death count.** Body: "Premature death (i.e., before 3 years) occurred in 8 of 20 patients (40%) with mean age at
   death of 40 months (range: 6 months to 8 years 11 months)". [S1] lists **nine** ages at death (P4, P5, P8, P9, P10,
   P11, P13, P14, P20) whose mean is **40.0 months**; only **5** are strictly under 36 months. The mean and range belong
   to nine deaths; the count says eight; the definition excludes four of them. [S4] "Deceased 20/37" reconciles only
   with nine deaths here plus the literature deaths **including a terminated pregnancy**.
2. **The null-genotype severity statement rests on seven hand-selected patients**: P10 (homozygous deletion
   chr16:77,745,868-78,187,104 [S1], which by Ensembl GRCh37 overlap also removes **NUDT7, VAT1L and CLEC3A** — a
   contiguous-gene deletion, not a WWOX-only null), the Abdel-Salam pair (one never genotyped), the Elsaadany W44*
   homozygous pair (**both alive**, at 7 y and 20 m [S3]) and the Valduga pair (one a terminated pregnancy). "5/7 died
   before 2 years" needs the termination and a death at exactly 2 y. The series' own homozygous p.Arg264* sibling pair
   (deaths at 8 y 11 m and 5 y 2 m [S1]) and other biallelic truncating genotypes are outside the seven.
3. **"Premature death has never been described in patients with missense ... only genotype"** holds only under the
   under-3-years definition: [S1] P20, **homozygous p.Gln230Pro, died at 3 y 3 m**.
4. **P47 mislabel.** [S2] lists the Mallaret homozygous family as p.(Pro47Arg); [S3] and Figure 1b give **p.Pro47Thr**.
   The body's "p.(Pro47Arg) in two families" depends on that row. P47T ≠ P47R.
5. [S2] p.(Gly137Glu) "3 families"; [S1] shows two (P9, P17).
6. Figure 1a labels "Δ exon 6 (IF)"; exon 6 is 89 nt on NM_016373.4 (out-of-frame by arithmetic). Figure 1b's codon-264
   truncation is labelled "L264*" (codon 264 is Arg).
