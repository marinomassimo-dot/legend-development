This is the section the expression of concern exists to change, and it changes less than the label

- that an adenoviral WWOX construct (Ad-WWOX) exists, was built from liver-derived *WWOX* cDNA in a

  reading: with the loading control in question, "equal loading across the nine lanes" is not
  established by the published figure. Any citation that leans on comparability between lanes must
  cite the 2017 replacement, not the 2005 panel.

**And the honest converse, stated because the temptation runs the other way too:** the notice is

## 4 · What the paper establishes, and what it does not

### 4.1 What it establishes — `DATO`

Restoring Wwox in three Wwox-negative lung cancer lines reduces growth and xenograft
tumorigenicity, and a Wwox-positive line is unaffected. Sub-G1 fractions read off Figure 2's own
printed statistics: A549 2.33 → 57.76%, H460 2.75 → 94.3%, H1299 2.0 → 17.46%, U2020 5.7 → 7.39%;
these match the Results text (58%, 94%, 17%, 7%) exactly. Table 1 tumour weights fall from
0.81–0.86 g to 0.08 g (A549), 0.61–0.64 to 0.03 (H460), 1.66–1.87 to 0.10 (H1299), with U2020
unchanged at 0.55–0.59.

### 4.2 🔴 The *in vivo* arm never shows Wwox inside a tumour

The ex vivo immunoblot is the paper's own most important negative, and it is stated plainly:

> *"Wwox expression was not found in any of the tumors"* — including the induced `H1299/I+` arm.

The authors' account is selection: *"the small tumors were derived from inoculated cells that had
lost expression of Wwox due to absence of inducer in vivo."* That is an **interpretation of an
absence** and it is the load-bearing premise of the entire *in vivo* result. `PREMISE: INFERENZA`.
The alternative it does not exclude — that tumours arise from cells that never expressed the
transgene, i.e. an infection/induction-efficiency ceiling rather than a Wwox-driven selection — is
not tested. **Consequence:** this paper demonstrates that cells *pre-treated* to express Wwox form
fewer and smaller tumours; it does **not** demonstrate that sustained Wwox expression suppresses an
established tumour. Those are different claims, and gene-therapy framing needs the second.

### 4.3 🔴 The "intrinsic pathway" rests on data shown in no figure

The abstract attributes apoptosis to *"the intrinsic apoptotic caspase cascade"*, and the
Discussion states *"we observed caspase-dependent induction of apoptosis through the intrinsic
pathway"* and, for H1299, *"cleavage of full-length PARP-1, but procaspase 3, 9, and 8 were not
cleaved."*

**Read as pixels, Figure 3B contains no caspase 9 row and no caspase 8 row.** Its labelled rows are
`Full length PARP`, `Cleaved PARP`, `Pro-Caspase 3`, `Cleaved Caspase 3`, `β-Actin` — and nothing
else. The Methods *do* list anti-caspase 9 and anti-caspase 8 among the antisera, so the
experiments were run; the results appear nowhere. **The intrinsic pathway is defined by caspase 9,
and no caspase-9 blot exists in this paper.** `IPOTESI`, not `DATO`.

### 4.4 The panel shows a gradation where the caption asserts an absence

Figure 3B's caption says caspase 3 is cleaved in lanes 3 and 6 *"but not in H1299 cells"*.
Measured peak darkness in the cleaved-caspase-3 row (0 = black, 255 = white): lanes 3 and 6 at
**27**; **lane 9 at 55**; every other negative lane 71–92; U2020 lanes 109–113. Lane 9 is clearly
weaker than the positives and clearly darker than every other negative.

Stated as a gradation, **not** as a refutation: a faint band at this resolution may be spillover,
and what is defensible is the ordering, not a call that caspase 3 *is* cleaved in H1299.

### 4.5 The specificity claim is made across a splice, on one cell line

The therapeutic argument turns on U2020: *"suggesting that normal Wwox-expressing lung cells would
be unaffected by Wwox overexpression after WWOX gene therapy."* Two limits, one of them visible
only in the pixels:

1. **U2020 is a lung *cancer* line, not a normal lung cell.** The inference from "one Wwox-positive
   tumour line is unaffected" to "normal lung would be unaffected" is `ESPANSIONE`, and the paper
   presents it as a suggestion — correctly — while the abstract's framing is firmer.
2. 🔴 **In Figure 3B the U2020 lanes sit on a physically separate blot segment.** Segmenting the
   β-actin row by non-white columns returns **two disjoint runs**, x 38–463 and x 468–640, at
   ~47 px and ~58 px per lane respectively. The differing lane pitch is visible in all four rows.
   Nothing in the caption or text says the comparison is made across a splice.

### 4.6 "Completely suppressed" is not what the figure or the table says

Results: *"tumor growth was completely suppressed in mice inoculated with Ad-WWOX-infected H460
cells."* Figure 5A shows the H460/Wwox box **above the axis** (~13 mm³, whisker to ~25), and
Table 1 gives that arm as **0.03 ± 0.04 g** — not zero. Four lines later the A549 arm is stated at
exactly the right strength — *"two of five mice … showed no tumors"* — which is what makes the
H460 sentence a lapse of wording rather than a convention.

### 4.7 Smaller internal inconsistencies, recorded because they are cheap to record

- **The timepoint is stated three ways.** Methods: *"harvested 5 days after infection"*. Figure 2
  caption: *"undergo apoptosis 5 days after restoration"*. Results: *"At 96 hr after Ad-WWOX
  infection"*. 96 hr is 4 days.
- **Figure 2 prints the identical sub-G1 value, 2.33%, for A549 CTRL and A549 GFP100.** The two
  panels are **not** duplicated images — cross-correlation 0.114, indistinguishable from the 0.115
  between two unrelated panels — so what repeats is the printed statistic, not the picture. No
  inference about how it arose is available from the figure and none is made.
- **Figure 6A has six lanes; its caption enumerates five.** Wwox is detected in lane 6 alone, and
  lane 6 is the one lane the caption never assigns.
- **Figure 6B is a ponA dose series** (0 / 5 / 7.5 / 10 μM); its caption describes a single 10 μM
  treatment and no series.
- **Figure 4D's y-axis reads `cell number ×10⁸`** against values to 5000 — 5×10¹¹ cells for a 9-day
  culture. Either the exponent or the scale is mislabelled. (`DATO`: the printed label.
  `PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK` on the implausibility, from general culture practice.)
- **Both densitometry charts in Figure 4 label their y-axis "Relative gene expression"** while
  quantifying a protein immunoblot normalised to GAPDH protein.
- **Figure 5B's uninduced control (`− Pon A`) produces the largest tumours in the panel**, above
  parental H1299 and Ad-GFP. Not commented on.
- **Figure 5C is qualitative**: one example animal per condition, no scale bar, no outline.

## 7 · Transfer verdict toward the reference genotype

**`ESPANSIONE` · T3 · clinical relevance LOW.**

No human subject, no germline variant, no neurological phenotype, no CNS tissue, no reference-genotype
allele. Four immortalised lung cancer lines and nude-mouse xenografts. The mechanism the paper
attributes (intrinsic caspase apoptosis) is asserted on unshown data; the *in vivo* result is
demonstrated on pre-treated cells and never inside a Wwox-expressing tumour.

**What transfers is a reagent and a caution, not a mechanism.** The reagent: Ad-WWOX and its
cumate-repressible design, which is provenance for later restoration work. The caution, and it is
worth carrying: **overexpressing Wwox in cells that lack it kills them.** In an oncology frame that
is the therapeutic effect. In a WWOX-DEE frame — where restoration is being pursued in *neurons* —
the same observation is a **dose-and-cell-type question**, and this paper is evidence that Wwox
restoration is not biologically neutral in a Wwox-negative cell. `IPOTESI`, and explicitly not a
safety finding about any WWOX gene therapy: the cells here are tumour lines with a p53 status the
paper itself flags as relevant, the vector is adenoviral, the dose is moi 100, and none of that
transfers to a neuron.

**`REVIVAL_TRIGGER`.** (a) An independent replication of Fig. 1B's loading from primary data would
settle what the 2017 notice left open. (b) A caspase-9 measurement in this system would convert the
intrinsic-pathway attribution from `IPOTESI` to `DATO` or refute it. (c) A dose–response for WWOX
overexpression in a non-transformed or neuronal cell would tell us whether the cell-death effect is
a tumour-cell property or a general one — which is the question that actually matters for the
reference genotype.
