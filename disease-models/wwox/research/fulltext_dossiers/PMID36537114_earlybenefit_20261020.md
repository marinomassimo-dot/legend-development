## 0 · Why this paper, out of the whole corpus

The roadmap's `BENEFICIO_PRECOCE` axis asks which WOREE endpoints are measurable. This paper is the
corpus's **only human observation of a therapeutic benefit in WOREE of any kind** — three of five
patients described as responding to a ketogenic diet. It was held at `partial_fulltext_read` with

human datum is unread, so this is the one reading the axis actually needed.

## 3 · What it does **not** support — the product of this reading

1. **It does not support a disease-modifying reading of the dietary benefit, and its own table refuses
   one.** E3 and E4 together are the strongest internal statement of that distinction available
   anywhere in this corpus: the endpoint that moved was seizure control, the endpoint that did not
   move was development, and in two of the three patients in whom seizure control moved the patient
   died inside the following two years. **A symptomatic benefit is not a slowing of the disease**, and
   here that is not a methodological caution imported from the roadmap — it is what one table prints.
2. **It does not support a response rate.** *«Three of five»* has no denominator of exposure: the
   surface never states how many patients were placed on the diet, only that three are described as
   responding. A measured absence over the delivered chunks, and it is exactly what `CLAIM 042`
   already says; this reading binds it to the surface.
3. **It does not support an effect size, a latency or a duration.** No seizure count, no frequency,
   no severity scale, no EEG change, no time from institution to response, no persistence criterion.
   `TIME_TO_BENEFIT = UNKNOWN` for the only human WOREE intervention observation in the corpus.
4. **It does not support three independent observations.** E11: two of the five patients are
   previously published, and which two is not said. Until that is resolved, a pooled count across
   WOREE series may double-count, and none of the three responses can be asserted as new.
5. **It does not support any WWOX-directed reading.** The paper has no assay: no protein, no
   transcript, no activity, no imaging quantity. Whatever changed in those three children, **nothing
   measured WWOX**, so the observation cannot be read as evidence about the gene's functional state.
6. **It does not support a mechanism.** The paper cites a preclinical rescue as a proof of concept
   for gene therapy; that is a citation, not a measurement, and this reading imports nothing from it.
7. **And it supplies no dose, no schedule, no paediatric-suitability statement, and this dossier
   derives none.** A diet is not safe because it is food, and nothing here is advice.

## 4 · The two positives nobody had recorded

Both are endpoint facts, both are n = 1, and both matter because the repository's endpoint files are
empty and its working model lists endpoints it has no source for.

- **A VEP exists in WOREE** (E7). A dated PubMed probe for `WWOX AND ("visual evoked potential" OR
  electroretinogram OR …)` returns **0** — so the absence is an *indexing* absence, and the
  measurement is in a case-series body. This is the bounded form of the negative, and the unbounded
  form would have been false.
- **Polysomnography exists in WOREE** (E8). The same caution applies, and it is the first human
  anchor under the sleep/network-state lead the discovery ledger carries as `DL-MECH-013`, which until
  now stood on fly work plus a human questionnaire association.

Neither is an endpoint yet: one patient, one timepoint, no parameter, no comparator.
