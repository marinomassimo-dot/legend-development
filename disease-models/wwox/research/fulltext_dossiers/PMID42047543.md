## 1 · What the paper measures

One patient-derived Glut1DS iPSC line (SLC2A1 P485L), undifferentiated, in 13.5 mM glucose, treated for 4 days with decanoic acid, octanoic acid or an 80:20 blend (150 uM), RNA-seq in triplicate, DESeq2 with FDR 0.05, KEGG and GO enrichment. No healthy control line, no second Glut1DS line, no protein, flux or ATP measurement. WWOX does not occur.

## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. 11,271 genes change significantly, 84 percent under decanoic-containing treatments; octanoic acid alone changes few.
2. Upregulated: beta-oxidation, TCA cycle and oxidative phosphorylation genes, mostly by 15 to 50 percent (log2 0.2 to 0.6); proteasome and lysosome genes; HMGCS1 (+2 log2) is the one large effect.
3. Glycolysis: the text reports reduced GLUT1, GLUT3, HK1, HK4 and a shift "away from glucose uptake and glycolysis"; Figure 1H and 1I show most glycolytic genes up (HK2, ALDOA, GPI, TPI1, GAPDH, ENO1, PGK1, PKM, LDHA) and a few down; the net direction is mixed.
4. AMPK: Results 3.1 says AMPK signalling upregulated; Results 3.3 and Figure 3 show AMPK subunit transcripts down.
5. Adenosine: ADA (breakdown) falls, interpreted as raised adenosine; NT5E (production), ENTPD2 and ADORA2B fall too; direction of net adenosine is not given by the data. Glutamate receptor subunits move both ways (GRIN2A, 2B, 2D, GRIA4, GRIK2, GRIK4 down; GRIN1, GRIN2C, GRM2 up).
6. The authors state: no matched healthy controls, no independent lines, no protein validation. Two authors hold IP and one works for the product manufacturer; the first author had an industrial studentship.

## 3 · Comparison with the laboratory's records

- Selection question 3 asked whether a non-ketone route can reproduce KD effects. This is a transcript-level suggestion in one undifferentiated cell line, so it is a hypothesis about energy-gene expression only; the paper does not show increased oxidation, ATP, or lower excitability, and the Joshi 2025 model (energy sufficiency) and the Klepper 2026 clinical failure rate (39 percent) put limits on any energy-only reading.
- For the WWOX arc: if WWOX loss produced a glycolytic shift in neurons (not shown in any neural system), a decanoic-acid route would be one way to supply energy without carbohydrate restriction; nothing in this paper bears on that lesion, and the direction of glycolytic genes here is itself mixed.
- Not in any record.

## 4 · What the paper does NOT support

- Reduced glycolysis (panels show mixed direction), increased oxidation, or any functional change.
- Neuronal or in vivo effects (undifferentiated iPSCs; no neurons).
- Glut1DS specificity (no healthy control).
- A net rise in adenosine.

## 5 · Transfer verdict toward the reference genotype (WWOX-DEE)

ESPANSIONE, weak. System gap: one Glut1 patient iPSC line, undifferentiated, transcripts. Value: a pointer to a clinically available non-ketogenic diet that may be a KD-alternative in a dietary-adherence sense; no mechanism evidence for WWOX loss.
