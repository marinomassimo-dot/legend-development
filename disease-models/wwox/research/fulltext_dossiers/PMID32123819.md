## What it measures

A whole-body Elovl1 knockout rescued in the epidermis (involucrin-promoter transgene) so that it survives birth; brain Elovl1 mRNA is near the detection limit. Readouts: TLC and LC-MS/MS of brain GalCer, 2-OH GalCer, sulfatide, sphingomyelin and ceramide species (3 versus 3 animals); Ugt8a, Cers2, Fa2h mRNA; EM of corpus callosum at 13 months; survival; a behavioural battery at 8 to 16 weeks (n = 10 to 12); retinal and corneal histology at 16 weeks. WWOX: zero occurrences.

## What it shows (DATO)

- ELOVL1 loss in brain shortens sphingolipid acyl chains (C24 and C26 species almost undetectable; C22:1 and shorter rise) and lowers total GalCer by 39 percent and total 2-OH GalCer by 34 percent; totals of sulfatide, sphingomyelin and ceramide are not significantly different; Ugt8a mRNA is 38 percent lower; Cers2 and Fa2h are unchanged.
- Myelin is thinner in the corpus callosum at 13 months, only in axons below 0.4 um and, more severely, at 1.6 um or above; axon density and diameter unchanged.
- Survival: 22 knockouts against 66 expected at three weeks (cause unclear); two more died of startle epilepsy by eight weeks.
- Behaviour: 15 percent lower body weight, slower travel and more slips on a narrow beam, reduced startle amplitude at 120 dB, reduced activity in light-dark transition, slightly fewer social contacts; retina and cornea normal.

## What it does NOT support (the negative is the product)

1. **No WWOX measurement; chain-length and GalCer amount, not cholesterol, are the readouts.** The paper does not measure cholesterol; the TLC image shows cholesterol unchanged by eye only ("other lipids nearly unchanged"). It is a template for the readout, as the selection note intended.
2. **Small, late and under-specified.** Lipidomics 3 versus 3; EM at a single age (13 months) with axons counted (96 and 73 in 13 fields) but animals per group not stated; behaviour n = 10 to 12 in males only; the GalCer total reduction is nominal significance at n = 3.
3. **Myelin thinning is modest and confined to the two ends of the size distribution.** The authors' own words are "mild myelin thinning"; the paper has no early-age EM, so developmental timing is unknown (the CerS2 papers read in this lot show P20 and 6-week defects).
4. **A text-figure discrepancy.** The text reports no genotype difference on the wide balance beam; the printed P for wide-beam paw slips is .0199 (Figure 4L), both groups near zero. Minor, but the text and panel disagree.
5. **A rescued model is not a model of the human recessive disease.** The human ELOVL1 patients in Wong 2025 carry homozygous variants with ichthyosis, hypomyelination and movement disorder; here the epidermis is rescued, the brain is null, motor deficits are mild with no paraplegia, and 2 of 22 knockouts died of startle epilepsy. That is the one place in this lot where a VLCFA-elongation lesion gives a seizure phenotype (in the mouse) while the human series records none.
6. **The GalCer reduction is attributed, not shown, to Ugt8a mRNA.** The paper says how Elovl1 loss lowers Ugt8a is unclear; the 38 percent mRNA reduction is whole-brain and n = 3.
