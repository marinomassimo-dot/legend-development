## What the paper did

Two affected sisters of a consanguineous sibship of four (two unaffected brothers, pedigree Figure 1). Commercial
exome sequencing of both, Sanger confirmation, parental segregation. Clinical course, metabolic work-up, EEG and MRI
described per patient; a literature table (Table 1) compares six reports.

## Genotype class and what was measured

- **Allele class × zygosity:** nonsense (exon 2, `c.131G>A p.Trp44*`) × homozygous in both sisters; both parents heterozygous.
- **Measured:** DNA only. Loss of function "through protein truncation or nonsense-mediated mRNA decay" is **predicted**; no RNA, NMD or protein assay.

Metabolic, mitochondrial (muscle and skin biopsies) and CSF neurotransmitter studies of the older sister were normal.

## What the paper states and what it does NOT

- States: the family fits WOREE; "ten families" reported to date including this one.
- Does **not** report premature death: both sisters were alive at report (Table 1 "Premature death: -"). The older
  sister survived to 7 years with a predicted null/null genotype, ventilator-dependent.
- Does **not** support its own Table 1 cell "Response to antiepileptics: Partial" for the older sister, whose text lists four failed drugs.
- No vigabatrin, ACTH or ketogenic diet is mentioned.
