## 1 · What the paper measures

A method perspective from the group that proposed the WWOX phospho-state and HYAL-2/WWOX/SMAD4 models: a three-colour (ECFP to EGFP to DsRed) FRET relay in transfected COS7, DU145 and NCI-H1299 cells over many hours, applied to IkBalpha/ERK/WWOX after ionophore and ester stimulation, and to Smad4/WWOX/Hyal-2 or p53 after hyaluronan, with TIAF1 self-binding as a third example.

## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. Panels are adapted from the authors earlier papers; the new element is an exponential regression (spreadsheet) of earlier curves defining a driver phase, a brief gap and an execution phase.
2. n is 2 for the key traces (Fig 1b, Fig 2a), 3 to 4 for controls; no statistical test is described; the gap phase "is unknown"; one supporting observation is cited to unpublished data.
3. Dominant-negative controls: the legend says they abolish signalling; the dominant-negative WWOX trace (n = 3) in Figure 2 shows an early peak to about 14,000 FRETc near hour 4 before falling, as large as the wild-type early phase.
4. All complexes are of ectopic, transiently overexpressed, tagged proteins; imaging was done in COS7 (chosen for size and adherence) while the biological claim concerns MOLT-4 T cells (end-point FRET only).
5. Retraction note: one reference (a leukaemia MEK/MCL-1 paper) is retracted, citation only.

## 3 · Comparison and method lesson

The lesson for any live-cell WWOX interaction assay: ectopic expression levels, n per trace, a catalogue of negative controls that actually reach baseline, and endogenous confirmation are what the claims need; this paper supplies the first two as n = 2 to 4 with no statistics and shows a dominant-negative control that does not fully reach baseline. It does not change any registry claim. It is the same laboratory as the pY33 to pS14 perspective, so the two are not independent support for each other.
