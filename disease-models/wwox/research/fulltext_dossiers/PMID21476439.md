## 2 · What the paper did

A single-laboratory enzymology study. No human subjects, no animals, no cells of human origin.

**Protein source.** Human *WWOX* cDNA (from A. K. Bednarek's own collection, i.e. the group's
2000 clone) subcloned `BamHI`/`EcoRI` from pcDNA 3.1.A into **pET 44a(+)**, giving an
**N-terminal NusA fusion** (NUS-WWOX), expressed in *E. coli* Origami B (DE3) pLysS; and a second,
**GST fusion** (GST-WWOX) in a pGEX 2TK vector. Induction 0.2 mM IPTG (NUS system) / 0.7 mM IPTG
(GST system), overnight at 16–18 °C. Lysis with CelLytic B II + lysozyme + RNase/DNase + sarkosyl,
then a stabilising buffer with Tween 20 and Nonidet P-40.

**Critical limitation, stated by the authors themselves.** Purification on glutathione-sepharose
(GST) or TALON Co²⁺ resin (NUS) **destroyed the activity in every attempt**, so *all* enzymology
was performed on the **soluble fraction of an unfractionated bacterial crude extract**
(400 µg total protein per 1 ml reaction). No purified WWOX enzyme was ever assayed.

**Assay.** Spectrophotometric, A₃₄₀, 25 °C, ε = 6270 M⁻¹ cm⁻¹ for the nicotinamide cofactor,
read at 5-min intervals over 30 min. Reaction: 100 mM potassium phosphate pH 7.4;
0.75 µmol NAD⁺ or NADP⁺ (or 0.15 µmol NADH/NADPH for the reduction direction);
0.24 µmol steroid; 400 µg crude extract; 1 ml total.
Dehydrogenase assays were quenched with methanol/chloroform (1:1) and the aqueous phase separated.

**Substrates (7).** 5α-androstane-3,17-dione (5α-A), 4-androstene-3,17-dione (4-A),
17β-estradiol (17β-E), estrone (E), 5α-dihydroprogesterone-*allo* (5α-DHP-allo),
progesterone (P), testosterone (T). A preliminary screen used the generic dehydrogenase
substrate **S-indan-1-ol** with NAD⁺ (Fig. 2) — its concentration is never stated.

**Controls.** The matched empty-vector extract (NUS alone / GST alone) with the same substrate,
and both extracts without substrate. WWOX "activity" is defined as the **difference** in A₃₄₀
between the NUS-WWOX extract and the plain NUS extract.

**Kinetics.** "Steady-state kinetics" with Lineweaver–Burk double-reciprocal fitting, substrate
varied in 0.025 or 0.05 µmol/ml steps over the ranges in Table I.

**Statistics.** Mann–Whitney, significance at p < 0.05. Replication: "the kinetics values were the
means of at least two or three demonstrations"; Fig. 2's caption says the curves shown are
"from one of two experiments, each done with a separate isolation of the soluble fractions".
