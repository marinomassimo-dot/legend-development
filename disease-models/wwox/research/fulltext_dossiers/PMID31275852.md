## 2 · The study, as designed

| Item | What the paper did |
|---|---|
| B-cell-specific deletion | `Cd19-Cre` × `Wwox^flox/flox` (**Cd19 Wwox KO**); controls `Cd19Cre/+ Wwox+/+` littermates. Deletion confirmed by immunoblot of FACS-sorted CD19⁺ B cells (L20, L26 attestation). |
| Survival | Kaplan-Meier, log-rank. **p = 0.007**; mean survival **777 vs 922 days** (L0, L1). Figure 1's legend states the cohort as **17 WT vs 44 KO** (L15). |
| Tumour phenotype | Full necropsy, H&E, IHC for B220 and CD138, Bethesda + mouse-plasmacytoma classification, **two pathologists blinded to genotype** (L17). |
| Incidence | **8/34 (23.4%) B-cell lymphomas and 8/34 plasmacytomas** in KO; **2/14 (14.3%) lymphomas and no plasmacytoma** in WT; stated as `p < 0.05` (L2, L3). |
| Monoclonal gammopathy | SPEP in triplicate: **10/27 (37%) KO vs 1/9 (11%) WT** M-spikes (L4); myeloma cast nephropathy shown by H&E and anti-light-chain IHC. |
| *Wwox* expression across B-cell development | RT-qPCR on FACS-sorted Pro-B, Pre-B, follicular, germinal-centre, plasmablast and plasma cells vs CD8⁺ and CD11b⁺ (L21). |
| CSR function | **Germline `Wwox` KO** (BK5-Cre, whole-body) and WT naïve splenic B cells, **16–17 days old**, LPS + IL-4 72 h, IgG1 by flow (L10). CSR at **~75% of WT** (L5). |
| Repair-pathway readout | Sμ–Sγ1 junctions cloned and sequenced: blunt joins **26/77 → 5/61**, long MH **1/77 → 8/61**, mean MH **1.2 → 2.6 bp** (L6, L7, L18); junction-adjacent mutation frequency **5.2 vs 5.0 × 10⁻³**, unchanged (L8). |
| Translocations | PCR/Southern *Myc/Igh* assay on the same juvenile germline-KO B cells; reported as a **2.5-fold** increase (L9). |

**Systems used, complete list:** mouse only — `Cd19-Cre` conditional KO, germline `Wwox` KO and `Wwox+/−` juveniles, primary splenic B cells in culture, plus FACS-sorted normal B-cell subsets. No human material, no cell line, **no WWOX disease variant**: every allele is wild-type, floxed or fully deleted.

## 3 · What is measured, with what controls

1. **Deletion is verified at protein level and is tissue-restricted.** Supp Fig 1B: the Wwox band is **absent in CD19⁺ B cells of the KO and present in cerebellum, lung and kidney of the same animal** (L26). This is the paper's cleanest control and is also the one datum of direct interest to a neural model — it shows the cerebellum here is *Wwox-replete by design*.
2. **Tumour classification is blinded** (L17) and rests on two markers (B220, CD138) plus morphology.
3. **CSR has an internal null control:** Figure 4D carries an **AID⁻/⁻ arm at ~0.3%** against WT 20.3% and KO 15.6% — visible only in the panel; neither the running text nor the caption names it (L23).
4. **The AID-level confound is excluded:** Supp Fig 3 shows AID/tubulin ≈ 1.0 in KO and WT (L19, L27).
5. **Mutation frequency is the specificity control for the junction result** — unchanged (L8), which is what separates "repair choice" from "more damage".

**Controls that are absent or weak:** no *Wwox+/−* data are shown anywhere although heterozygotes are stated to have been isolated (L33, searched negative); **no replicate count for any blot**; Figure 1's survival cohort (17/44) cannot be reconciled with the 34 KO / 14 WT of the incidence analysis or the 27/9 of the SPEP analysis, and no attrition is stated; the *Myc/Igh* assay has **no AID⁻/⁻ or naïve-cell negative arm of its own** — the claim that naïve cells carry no translocations is imported from refs 43 and 54.

## 4 · What is asserted but not measured

| Assertion | Status |
|---|---|
| A **"direct role for Wwox in B cell genome stability"** (Introduction) | **INFERENZA.** Nothing here is a direct assay of Wwox at a break: no recruitment, no epistasis with a C-NHEJ factor, no reconstitution. The data are a phenotype in *Wwox*-null cells. |
| The CSR and translocation phenotypes are **B-cell-intrinsic** | **Not established by this design.** Those experiments use **germline** KO juveniles (L10) — animals that die by 3–4 weeks — not the `Cd19`-restricted model. A systemic contribution is excluded by nothing, and the paper never states this distinction. |
| **"2.5-fold increase"** in translocations (L9) | **Not reconcilable with its own panel.** Figure 6C prints WT ≈ 0.22 and KO ≈ 1.45 per 10⁶ cells — about **6.5-fold** (L22). The XML and the published PDF both read "2.5-fold", so this is not a transcription artefact of one surface. What the 2.5 refers to is not shown anywhere. |
| Long-MH usage means **Alt-NHEJ/MMEJ engagement** | **Authors' interpretation, from a signature** (L11 concedes the mechanism "is not clear"). No Alt-NHEJ factor was depleted or measured. |
| *Wwox* is expressed **"throughout B cell development including germinal center cells"** (L21) | **True but flattened.** In Figure 4A the germinal-centre and plasma-cell values (~0.69, ~0.67) are the **lowest of all B subsets** and no higher than the CD8⁺/CD11b⁺ non-B comparators (~0.79, ~0.65). The sentence is used to license a GC-stage role that the panel does not support. |
| Tumour incidence `p < 0.05` (L3) | **Recomputed: Fisher exact two-tailed p = 0.0493** for 16/34 vs 2/14 — true, and marginal. Its components are not significant alone (lymphoma 8/34 vs 2/14, p = 0.70; plasmacytoma 8/34 vs 0/14, p = 0.085). |
| SPEP M-spikes 37% vs 11% (L4) | **No test is stated, and none is significant:** Fisher exact p = 0.22 for 10/27 vs 1/9. The Abstract nevertheless lists monoclonal gammopathies as a result. |

## 5 · Discrepancies found only in the images, the table or the supplement

1. **Figure 6C contradicts the text's fold-change** (L22 vs L9), as above — the single most consequential panel finding here.
2. **Figure 5's own caption miscounts its denominator.** The caption says *"a total of 76 WT and 61 Wwox KO unique clones"* (L14) while the same caption, the Results text and the pie chart in Figure 5B all say **77**; Supplementary Figure 2's summary table sums to exactly **77** (26+24+9+0+1 overlaps + 8+9 insertions) and **61** (L27).
3. **Replicate counts disagree between Methods and captions.** Methods say the junction analysis and the translocation assay each summarise **three** independent experiments (L12, L13); Figure 5A's and Figure 6C's captions say **n = 4** (L14, L24 context). Both cannot be true of the same data.
4. **Figure 4D's AID⁻/⁻ bar is undeclared** (L23) — a third genotype in a two-genotype comparison.
5. **Figure 5A supplies a p-value the text withholds:** insertions >1 nt carry **p = 0.15** in the panel (L24); the text calls them "not significantly different" with "a slight increase".
6. **Age of the CSR/translocation cohort is stated three ways:** "15 days old" (Results), "16–17 day-old" (Methods, L10), "15 day old" (Figure 6 caption).
7. **Ref 34 — the one WWOX-neural citation in the paper — is cited for a mouse protocol.** *"the protocol to generate Wwox^flox/flox and Wwox knock-out (KO) mice have been previously described (9, 34)"* (L20), where 34 is Mallaret et al., *Brain* 2014, the human autosomal-recessive cerebellar-ataxia-with-epilepsy paper. Whether that paper carries such a protocol is **not verifiable from this artefact** and is recorded as an unresolved ambiguity, not as an error.

## 6 · Strength, and transferability to WWOX-DEE

- **Tumour-suppression claim (B cells):** moderate. Two genotypes, long latency, blinded histopathology, a marginal composite p, and denominators that do not line up between analyses.
- **Repair-choice claim:** moderate-to-strong as a *signature* — 138 sequenced junctions, two independent shifts (blunt loss, long-MH gain) with unchanged mutation frequency and unchanged AID levels. Weak as a *mechanism*: no Alt-NHEJ factor was touched.
- **Translocation claim:** weak-to-moderate. One assay, `p < 0.05`, and a magnitude the text and its own panel do not agree on.
- **Transferability to the reference genotype: T3 / indirect.** The material is murine B-lineage; the readouts (CSR, *Myc/Igh* translocation, monoclonal gammopathy) have **no counterpart in a neurodevelopmental phenotype**, and the germline-null animals are used here only as juvenile B-cell donors. What does transfer is a **mechanistic property of Wwox loss measured in primary, non-transformed, non-engineered cells**: repair-pathway choice shifts toward microhomology-mediated end joining without any increase in lesion load. WWOX-related neural disease appears in this paper only as reference 34 (L20).
