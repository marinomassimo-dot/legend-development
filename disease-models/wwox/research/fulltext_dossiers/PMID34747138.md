## What the pass added to the earlier reading

1. The hypoglycemia rescue, the one metabolic datum of this paper, rests on Appendix Figure S1C and D only. As drawn (150 dpi page render): WT about 120 to 125 mg/dL; the control is one pooled series "KO+GFP or KO" at about 85 at day 10 falling to about 45 by day 20 to 24; treated curves overlie WT; n = 5 per group; SEM. Method: a single tail-prick glucometer reading; fed or fasted state, time of day, insulin, glucagon, ketones and IGF-1 are not given.
2. What it supports: in this mouse model, restoring WWOX in 50 to 70 percent of neurons (no WWOX in liver, pancreas, kidney or other peripheral tissue by immunostaining, Appendix S2) normalises blood glucose, growth, fertility and bone at the time points shown. What it does not support: that brain WWOX directly regulates glucose. Treated mice also stop seizing, feed and grow normally; hypoglycemia could be secondary to seizures, feeding or growth failure, and the paper tests none of these. Together with the 2019 muscle-specific deletion (a cell-autonomous glucose phenotype, cited in the Discussion) the model is a tissue-of-origin ambiguity, not a mechanism.
3. Panel versus text: transduction is stated as 60 to 70 percent; Figure 1E shows about 56, 63 and 52 percent and Figure 2G about 61, 71 and 57 (cerebellum below the stated range in both). Treated survival curves in Figures 1C and 2C reach 0 percent at about 350 and 330 days, so the "extended life span" is bounded and the late deaths are unexplained. Male rotarod trial 1 differs significantly from WT (Figure 6K) while the text says similar. The earlier reading's finding stands: the adult EM unmyelinated-axon count is about twice WT (Figure 5E) against the text's "minor differences".
4. Everything else in the earlier 22 locators re-points unchanged (the 2026-08 text surface and figure renders were absent). Declared-absent PNG page renders were dropped from `source_artifacts` and replaced by the PMC bucket JPEGs; the old SHA-256 values remain in the manifest's git history.

## Transfer and impact

Mouse, neonatal P0 dosing, partial transduction, one lab. Relevance to RL-003 (metabolic line): a hypoglycemia phenotype that is reversible by neuron-restricted re-expression means a metabolic readout of WWOX loss need not be a peripheral-WWOX effect, which cuts against attributing the null-mouse glucose phenotype to muscle or liver WWOX alone and is compatible with a secondary (seizure or nutritional) route. No claim is reversed; commit candidate `CC-20261019-SCIENTIST-B-GOLD-METAB-01` carries the wording constraint (MINOR). Revival triggers: fed and fasted glucose with insulin and ketones in treated versus null mice at matched seizure state; peripheral-only restoration.
