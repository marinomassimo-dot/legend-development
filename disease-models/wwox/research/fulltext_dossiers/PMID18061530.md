## 3 · What is measured — and at what level

1. **Altered or absent WWOX transcripts in 6/23 lesions (26%)** (L8). Sequenced products: #OL2 partial exon 2 + loss of 3-6 + partial 7; #OL14 partial 1 + loss 3-6 + partial 7; #OL18 partial 2 + loss 3-7 + partial 8; #OL23 two variants, one partial 1 + loss 2-7 + partial 8 and one clean **Δ6-8** (L9, L10). #OL22 gave no product; #OL16's aberrant band was never identified (L11).
2. **Reduced Wwox protein in 6/23 lesions (26%)** — five +2, one +1 (L13).
3. **Combined: 8/23 (35%)** altered at mRNA and/or protein level, with 4 of the 6 transcript-altered lesions also protein-reduced (L16, L22).
4. **Dysplasia gradient, as counted:** altered in 5/11 moderate-or-severe, 3/8 mild, **0/4 without dysplasia** (L17). Every count in that sentence reproduces exactly from Table 1.
5. **Recomputed here, because the paper reports no test:** any-dysplasia versus none, 8/19 vs 0/4, two-sided Fisher **p=0.26**; moderate-severe versus mild-or-none, 5/11 vs 3/12, **p=0.40**. The gradient the paper's conclusion rests on is not statistically distinguishable from chance in its own data.
6. **Two discordant lesions:** #OL22 no transcript with normal protein, #OL18 an aberrant transcript with normal protein (L18, L19, L20).

## 5 · Discrepancies found only in the images, tables or arithmetic

1. 🔴 **The scoring scale changed between this paper and the group's 2006 OSCC paper, with the same antiserum and protocol.** Here +1 is 0-50%, +2 is 51-75%, +3 is >76% (L6, L7); there +1 was 0-10%, +2 11-50%, +3 >50%. **A "+2" in the two papers is not the same quantity**, which matters directly because this paper's discussion compares its 35% with that paper's 50%. The scale also leaves 75-76% undefined.
2. **The number of control mucosas is never stated** (L1) — in a study whose every score is defined against "normal mucosa all +3".
3. **#OL16 is counted as altered without a sequence:** an abnormal band was seen and never identified for want of material (L11).
4. **The caption calls panel 2a "(strong)" cytoplasmic staining** (L14); in native pixels the normal mucosa shows **pale, diffuse** brown staining, moderate at most (L15). Scoring is by extent, so the score is not affected — the caption's adjective is.
5. 🔴 **Four of the five sequenced aberrant products join exon *interiors***, not exon boundaries — "partial loss of exon 2", "partial loss of exon 7", "partial loss of exon 8" (L9, L10). Canonical splicing does not predict mid-exon junctions, and with **two nested rounds of 35 cycles** (L3) on microdissected material, template switching is a live alternative the paper does not exclude (`INFERENZA`, reader). The one clean, splice-plausible product is the Δ6-8 in #OL23 (L10).
6. **The headline sentence says "lesions with WWOX mRNA and/or protein *expression*"** where *alteration* is meant (L24); the same wording is in the Summary.
7. **No statistical test appears anywhere**, and the counts in Table 1 are the only evidence for the paper's central claim.
8. **IHC and RT-PCR were performed on different parts of each lesion** (L21), so every concordance figure is across non-identical material.

## 6 · Strength, and transferability to the reference genotype

- **What is solid:** aberrant WWOX transcripts and reduced Wwox protein are each detectable in about a quarter of oral leukoplakias, every count in the paper reproduces exactly from its own table, and the sequenced junctions are real sequences.
- **What is weak, and the paper does not say so:** the dysplasia gradient that carries the conclusion is **not statistically distinguishable from chance in these data** (p=0.26 any-dysplasia vs none; p=0.40 moderate-severe vs mild-or-none, recomputed here). The negative arm is four lesions. Protein and transcript were measured on different tissue. Four of five aberrant products have non-canonical junctions from a deeply nested PCR.
- **Transferability: T3, nil toward WWOX-DEE.** Adult somatic premalignant mucosa, no germline allele, no neural material, no developmental or seizure readout, no child. The only observation with conceptual reach is that a **clean Δ6-8 transcript coexists with a second aberrant WWOX transcript in a single non-malignant lesion** (L10) — the same transcript class Gourley 2005 finds in most normal ovaries. That is a statement about somatic tissue and it is `ESPANSIONE` toward any germline exon-deletion question; this reading does not carry it there.
