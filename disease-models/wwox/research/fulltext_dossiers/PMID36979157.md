# Dossier — PMID 36979157 · Kaluzinska-Kolat et al. 2023, Biology · WWOX over-expression in four glioblastoma cell lines

context_policy: SOURCE_FIRST. Receipt `FTR-20261019-36979157-01` staged (`first_read`, partial: Results 3.3 to 3.5 and Figures 3 to 6 not read in detail). Manifest strict PASS. Gain of function in tumour lines: class-level only. Not medical advice.

## 1 · What the paper measures

Lentiviral stable WWOX over-expression against vector control in T98G, DBTRG-05MG, U251MG and U87MG: TUNEL apoptosis, BrdU proliferation, PrestoBlue reducing capacity (read as "mitochondrial redox potential"), adhesion to matrix proteins, colony, suspension and 3D growth, invasion and gelatinase activity, with a bioinformatic search for a reason why DBTRG-05MG behaves differently.

## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. Over-expression is 2.6-fold (T98G) to 14.7-, 12.1- and 10.2-fold (DBTRG-05MG, U251MG, U87MG) over control, from one blot per line with three lanes (Figure 1E).
2. Apoptosis rises in T98G, DBTRG-05MG and U87MG (about 35 to 80 percent on the TUNEL signal), not in U251MG. Proliferation falls in T98G and U87MG and rises about 2.8-fold in DBTRG-05MG. The PrestoBlue signal is lower in all four lines at every time after the first 10 minutes (reduced by roughly 20 to 55 percent at 80 minutes), including DBTRG-05MG, whose proliferation rose.
3. The text cites Figure 1B for the proliferation result (it is Figure 2B).
4. The PrestoBlue assay is resazurin reduction by the whole cell; the paper names it mitochondrial redox potential and cell viability interchangeably.
5. The remaining assays (adhesion, colonies, invasion) were not read in detail here; the authors report decreases in three lines and the reverse growth behaviour in DBTRG-05MG.
6. The Simple Summary (abstract, not usable as evidence) links the work to the WWOX gene therapy under investigation; the experiments are in malignant lines. The authors state in the Discussion that in-vitro findings often fail to extrapolate and that in-vivo validation is needed.
7. One reference (a paper-mill-retracted HOTAIRM1, miR-498 and WWOX lung paper) is cited for WWOX regulation by non-coding RNA; citation only.

## 3 · Comparison with the laboratory's records

The question "does excess WWOX harm a neuron" is not answered: these are malignant glioma lines, over-expression 3- to 15-fold, with pro-apoptotic and reducing-capacity effects in most lines. It is nonetheless the only brain-derived-cell WWOX gain-of-function experiment with a stated expression ratio that this repository holds; it says that large over-expression is not inert in glial-tumour cells, and says nothing about post-mitotic neurons or about AAV doses. Treat as a non-neuronal caution, not as a safety signal.

## 4 · What the paper does NOT support

Neuronal toxicity of WWOX; any statement about loss of function; mitochondrial dysfunction as such (the assay is not mitochondria-specific); a WWOX metabolic mechanism.
