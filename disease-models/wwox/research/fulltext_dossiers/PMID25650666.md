## What the earlier partial reading left unread, and what it changes

The PMC figure rasters are **550 px wide** and unreadable at panel level. The panels were obtained instead from images embedded in the article PDF at **1500–1750 px**. Three things only visible there:

1. **Figure 3A adjudicates the paper's own null.** The filter-retardation bars carry p = 0.942 (TPC6A) and p = 0.850 (TIAF1) between controls and Alzheimer cases — while p-WWOX (p = 0.036), NFT (p = 0.014) and Aβ (p = 0.003) *do* separate on the same blot. The null is therefore not an assay failure; the abstract's "aggregate formation of TPC6AΔ occurs preceding Aβ generation" is a reading of a flat cross-section.
2. **Figure 5D is the pT181-tau claim, and it is not graded.** Three photomicrographs plus a bar chart: `Wwox+/+` ≈ 11, `Wwox−/+` ≈ 6, `Wwox−/−` ≈ 26 aggregates, n = 5, a single bracket p = 0.0176 from +/+ to −/−. **The heterozygote mean is below wild type.** This panel supports no gene-dosage reading, and the selector's framing of it as the young-brain tau anchor has to carry that.
3. **Figure 1B contains per-donor age, sex and diagnosis rows.** Noted and deliberately not transcribed: the public edition carries no individual-level record.

Also newly read: the supplementary PDF in full. **Supplementary Figure 1 contradicts the Methods on antibody numbering** — Methods say the pan-specific peptide is residues 84–100 and the second site is Tyr112; the supplement says residues 70–86 and Tyr116, with the identical peptide sequence. The numbering, not the reagent, is inconsistent.

## What the paper did

Isolation of an alternatively spliced TRAPPC6A isoform lacking 14 N-terminal residues (GenBank FJ418644); four homemade rabbit antisera; IHC on post-mortem human cortex and hippocampus; filter-retardation assay on post-mortem hippocampal extracts; caspase-3 and Aβ readouts after transient over-expression in SK-N-SH; generation of a constitutive `Wwox` knockout mouse (loxP targeting of exon 1, and separately exons 2/3/4) and IHC of 3-week-old brain; siRNA knockdown of WWOX in COS7.

## What it does **not** state

- **No isoform-resolved human measurement.** The filter-retardation blot used the pan-specific antibody, which the Methods say detects both forms.
- **No antibody validation against a genetic null or a knockdown** — specificity rests on peptide blocking alone.
- No neuronal cell system with a WWOX genotype: the genotype work is mouse brain IHC and MEF; the mechanism work is COS7 and SK-N-SH over-expression.
- No tau biochemistry: pT181-tau is an aggregate count on sections, not a phospho-western.
- The authors state the knockout "can only survive for about a month", which caps every latency statement made from it.

## Caveats the authors themselves give

"Whether TGF-β1 regulates the binding of WWOX with TPC6AΔ is unknown and is being determined in this laboratory"; "The functional role TPC6A in the nuclei is unknown"; "how the binding regulates the hyperphosphorylation of tau is unknown".
