## Figure and supplementary audit

All five downloaded main figures measured at a 96-effective-PPI ceiling before inspection. The
four-page supplementary PDF was preflighted before rendering: page 1 and page 4 were vector/text,
page 2 measured 96 ppi, and page 3 contained rasters at 150–176.3 ppi. It was then rendered at
220 dpi with PyMuPDF because Poppler was unavailable.

Visual inspection uncovered a consequential Figure 1 legend error. The plotted PCA identifies
WWOX as green, EV as red and WFPA as blue, matching the Results text; the caption swaps WWOX and
WFPA colours. The same surface calls the heatmap “four major clusters” while the Results says two.
The pixels, not the caption, support separation of WWOX from EV/WFPA.

Figure 4 supports direct miR-146a targeting of the SMAD3 3′UTR because mutation removes the
reporter repression. The anti-miR rescue is partial: displayed densitometry rises from about 0.40
to 0.66 rather than returning to EV at 1.0. This supports mediation, not exclusive sufficiency.
Figure 5 supports WWOX–DVL2 co-IP and reduced AXIN2 with wild-type WWOX in MDA-MB231 and SUM149.
Supplementary Figure S2, however, quantifies nuclear β-catenin as a β-catenin/WWOX ratio. Because
WWOX is the manipulated denominator, the metric is mathematically coupled to treatment; Lamin is
shown but not used for the reported ratio. That panel is weak support for nuclear β-catenin loss.

The supplementary spreadsheets are not decorative. Supplementary Table 1 contains 1,332 data
rows (728 negative and 604 positive fold changes), not the 1,557 genes implied by the text's
833-down/724-up claim. Only 358 stored rows have absolute fold change at least four, despite the
Methods declaring a fourfold/FDR threshold, and the workbook exposes a p-value column rather than
an explicit FDR field. This is a reproducibility/reporting mismatch that requires source-data or
pipeline reconciliation; it is not evidence of misconduct. Other workbooks expose the pathway,
miRNA-target, IPA and 294-row IP-MS inventories.

## Scientific verdict

WWOX overexpression in MDA-MB435S metastatic melanoma cells separates the transcriptome from EV
and WW1-domain mutant WFPA controls. Integration with prior miRNA data enriches adhesion,
motility, invasion, TGF-β and Wnt programs. These are overexpression results in a melanoma line,
not direct evidence from a breast tumour; the article correctly identifies MDA-MB435S as melanoma
in Results and Methods even though the title frames TNBC.

The miR-146a branch is the cleanest new mechanism. Wild-type WWOX lowers SMAD3 more than WFPA,
miR-146a represses wild-type but not mutant SMAD3 3′UTR, and anti-miR-146a partly restores SMAD3.
The data support WWOX→miR-146a→SMAD3 as one route into TGF-β signalling, alongside direct WWOX–
SMAD3 interaction reported elsewhere. Partial rescue prevents a claim that this route is complete.

The protein-interaction branch is supportive but partly confirmatory. Three-replicate IP-MS
identified MYCBP, DVL2, SCAMP3 and CDC5L as wild-type-specific hits. Exogenous HEK293T co-IP
validates DVL2 association and Wnt increases the signal. Wild-type WWOX, unlike WFPA, reduces the
Wnt target AXIN2 in MDA-MB231 and SUM149. This corroborates a WWOX–DVL mechanism already present
in the repository; it does not newly prove the entire metastasis phenotype is DVL2-dependent.

## Tensions, transfer and hidden gold

- The caption labels computational consensus/intersection targets “validated”; Methods call them
  predicted, and only selected SMAD3 targeting receives direct experimental validation.
- Representative blots conceal point-level variation. Methods say blots were repeated two or
  three times; most panels do not expose all replicates or uncertainty.
- MDA-MB231/SUM149 extend the Wnt readout to breast lines, but the broad transcriptomic program
  was derived in MDA-MB435S melanoma and no in-vivo metastasis experiment is performed here.
- “Therapeutic introduction of WWOX” is an untested extrapolation from ectopic-expression models,
  not a dose, delivery, safety or therapeutic-window result.
- **Disease transfer:** moderate for cancer-cell pathway biology, low for WOREE. **Genotype
  transfer:** low, because enforced wild-type expression and a WW1 mutant are not patient alleles.
  **Intervention transfer:** low; no therapeutic construct or in-vivo treatment is tested.

## Repository impact

The paper should upgrade `CORPUS-STUB-050` and the surface census at the next BATCH_COMMIT. It adds
direct support for miR-146a targeting of SMAD3 and contextual replication of WWOX–DVL2/Wnt control.
The existing Wnt/DVL2 discovery record should retain the supplementary β-catenin denominator flaw
and the fact that AXIN2, rather than metastasis itself, is the functional readout here. No canonical
scientific file was edited.

## Process report

- **Ambiguity:** the title's TNBC framing could obscure that the discovery transcriptome is from
  MDA-MB435S melanoma; model identity was resolved from the body and Methods.
- **Tool limitation:** Poppler was unavailable, so rendering used the PDF protocol's PyMuPDF
  fallback after PPI measurement. XLSX files were inspected structurally, not treated as prose.
- **Deduced rather than read:** the transcript-count/threshold mismatch, partial-rescue boundary,
  manipulated-denominator problem and cross-model transfer limits are audit inferences.
- **Could not do:** reproduce GEO preprocessing, determine whether omitted spreadsheet rows explain
  the 833/724 counts, or infer therapeutic efficacy from forced-expression cell models.
