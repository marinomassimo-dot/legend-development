## What the paper did
A 172-gene NGS panel in 74 unrelated children with intractable epilepsy beginning before age three. SNVs and exon-level CNVs were called in-house (exon CNVs by relative depth), confirmed by Sanger, MLPA or array, and classified by ACMG with a monthly consensus meeting and parental studies.

## What it does NOT state
- Whether the duplication is tandem, its orientation, or whether it is in frame. **Reader's arithmetic** (Ensembl GRCh37 exon lengths 89 + 186 + 265 = 540 nt, divisible by 3): a direct tandem copy of exons 6–8 would be **in frame**, so this allele need not be a frameshift null. Its effect is unmeasured; the absence of PVS1 in the authors' own call is consistent with that.
- That `p.Gln354Ter` lies in the last coding exon. **Reader's note:** exon 9 begins at c.1057 on LEGEND's boundary map, so a stop at codon 354 is expected to escape nonsense-mediated decay. The authors' PVS1 (null) call does not address this.
- No RNA, protein or functional test of either allele.

## Transfer limit
Neither allele is the reference genotype's; both are of uncertain functional class (a truncation that may escape NMD; a possibly in-frame duplication). Do not file the duplication as a dose-gain datum: it is intragenic.
