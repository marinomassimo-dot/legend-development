## 0 · What this paper IS, and the weight that follows

PubMed types it `Review`; the Europe PMC deposit types it `review-article`. **It is a secondary
source and is treated as one throughout.** Under `gold_is_in_the_details` parity rules it is not
primary evidence for any experiment it reports, and no statement below inherits an assertion from
it about another group's work.

What it *is* primary for is its own **curation**: a variant dataset assembled from ClinVar,
DECIPHER, VarSome, gnomAD, PubMed and the authors' own patient database, deposited as five
supplementary files. That dataset is this reading's object.

The authoring group is the primary **WWOX experimental laboratory** (`Aqeilan RI[Author]` = 137
PubMed records, 64 with WWOX in title/abstract, measured 2026-09-09) and is **not** the primary
clinical group for WWOX-DEE: the patients it tabulates were ascertained by more than 21 other
groups, and the paper says so. Consequently, where it summarises its own rodent and organoid work
it is a self-summary of primary work already held in this corpus and adds no independent weight;
where it tabulates other groups' patients its value is **checkability, not observation**.

**3.1 · Its variant dataset is checkable, and it checks out.** This is the main positive result of

carry. Every variant-level number in §7.3 was **recomputed from the deposited XLSX rather than

| "93 missense, 6 intronic, 2 nonsense, 2 indel" | type column partitions **93 / 6 / 2 / 2** | ✅ |

`DATO` — the review's curated variant dataset reproduces from its own deposit.

**3.2 · A phenotype taxonomy, and one hypothesis clearly marked as the authors'.** The review
proposes that complete-loss-of-function alleles give WOREE while missense alleles may retain
residual function and give SCAR12 (§9). Recorded as `AUTHOR_INTERPRETATION`, not as a result —
the review performs no functional assay. It also declares, itself, that the missense-only SCAR12
genotype may be a **sampling artefact** rather than a rule.

**3.3 · A prevalence gap the review raises and does not resolve.** From 1057 alleles in ~250,000
gnomAD alleles (≈1:250), assuming trans phase and random mating, it derives ≈1:62,000 and projects
**≈121,000 WOREE cases worldwide** against **217** it can enumerate. It offers under-diagnosis
first and "many of these variants are benign" second, quantifying neither, and declares the
phase-unknown and random-mating limitations itself.

🔴 **A discriminating argument the review does not make, added here as the reader's own and typed
as such.** The review notes that none of the 103 VUS appears homozygous in gnomAD. `INFERENZA`:
that observation **discriminates neither hypothesis**, because gnomAD is an adult population
reference and a severe recessive disorder with early lethality would be depleted of homozygotes
whether the variants are pathogenic or benign. `PREMISE: DATO` (gnomAD's stated ascertainment).

## 4 · What the reading found that the paper's own text does not carry

**4.1 · Survival on the reference allele — the highest-value extraction.** Table 1 and Table S1
both record two **homozygous Q230P** patients from Johannsen 2018 (ref [68], PMID 29808465) as
**alive at 12 and 10 years**, seizure onset at 2 months, **refractory to multiple antiepileptic
drugs** (the table's own footnote defines MAD). A second homozygous Q230P patient, a different
family, has a recorded endpoint of **3 y 3 m** — a death, since Table 1's column is headed `Death`
and survivor rows there read *"alive at"*.

Q230P is present in **8 patients across 6 families**: 4 homozygous (Afghan ×2, Moroccan, Iranian)
and 4 compound heterozygous (2 French, and 2 Yemenite in trans with the `c.517-2A>G` splice
allele). Censused against the whole endpoint column, the two Afghan patients are the
longest-recorded in the DEE28 block.

🔴 **Three restraints the census and the blind audit forced, all of them narrowing.**

1. **Long survival is not confined to missense genotypes.** Homozygous nonsense **R264\*** patients
   are recorded at **8 y 11 m and 5 y 2 m**, and homozygous **W44\*** at **7 y and 20 months**. So
   the supported statement is *within-genotype survival variability is large across WWOX-DEE, and
   homozygous Q230P is its clearest instance* — **not** "a missense genotype confers survival".
2. **The endpoint column is not uniformly deaths.** Table S1's header is
   `Death/ last date of examination`. For the W44\* pair in particular, **whether the 7 y vs 20 m
   divergence is survival or follow-up length is not resolved by the table.**
3. **Sibship is not stated for these pairs.** The tables write *"4 siblings"* and *"2 siblings"*
   explicitly for the two SCAR12 rows and write only *"2 … F/F"* for the Q230P, R264\* and W44\*
   pairs. Sibship is what would make a within-pair divergence a shared-background comparison, so it
   is **not asserted here** and must be sourced to the primary reports.

`DATO` on the tabulated ages, `INFERENZA` on the variability being biological rather than
ascertainment. `PREMISE: INFERENZA` on treating death ages and last-examination ages as comparable
endpoints — the table mixes them and this dossier does not resolve it.

**4.2 · Section 8's therapeutic premise is broader than its own supplement.** The body states
flatly that AEDs *"are not effective, with most individuals becoming refractory to treatment"* and
cites Table S1. Table S1 **supports "most"** — the large majority are refractory or drug-resistant.
It also records, in the DEE28 block, **five explicit responders and nine partial responses**, none
of which the running text mentions:

| Genotype | Regimen | Recorded response | Endpoint cell |
|---|---|---|---|
| exon-5 deletion, homozygous (Emirati) | VPA + **steroids** | yes | 5 m |
| c.918del E306fs, compound het (Romanian), West syndrome | PB + VGB + **ACTH** | Yes | <3 y |
| c.231_409del D77Efs\*27, compound het (Italian) | — | **seizure-free since 2 y 3 m** | NA |
| c.229_230+2delGAGT, compound het (Chinese) | PB + TPM | Yes | 5 m |
| 6.8 Mb deletion, heterozygous (Japanese) | initially drug-resistant → **LTG + VPA** | responded | 4 y 7 m |

Plus, in the SCAR12 block, 2 of the 4 P47T siblings on phenobarbital-containing regimens.

🔴 **Four qualifications, stated with the finding and not after it.** (i) **Hypsarrhythmia and West
syndrome are frequent here, and ACTH and steroids are standard care for infantile spasms
generally** — a responder subset concentrated on hormonal regimens may reflect West-syndrome
pharmacology, not anything WWOX-specific. (ii) **Steroids and ACTH are not antiepileptic drugs**,
so two of the five "responders" to the sentence about AEDs were not on AEDs alone. (iii) **Response
does not track outcome**: two of the five have endpoint cells of 5 months. (iv) *"Yes"* is **nowhere
defined** in the deposit. `IPOTESI`, and nothing stronger.

**4.3 · Figure 3B mis-colours the codon-47 alleles, and the table cannot support the comparison
anyway.** This is where the blind audit changed the finding most, in both directions.

- **The panel is worse than the draft said.** Both auditors sampled the red channel independently
  and agree: in the merged label `P47R/T`, **only `P47R` is drawn in red and `/T` is black.** The
  caption assigns **red to SCAR12** and black to WOREE. So the panel does not merely collapse two
  alleles onto one stem — **it applies the SCAR12 colour to the WOREE allele and the WOREE colour
  to the SCAR12 allele.**
- **But the table underneath supports less than the draft claimed.** The two auditors *disagreed*
  about which allele P47R is paired with, so it was resolved against the table's **rowspan markup**
  rather than by preferring an auditor. Row 9 carries `c.46_49del (p.Asp16fs)` with `rowspan=2` on
  its case cells — 2 patients, Portuguese, compound heterozygous, onset 5 m/5 m, **alive at 4 y and
  3 y** — and the following three-cell row carries `c.140C>G (p.Pro47Arg)` as the **second allele of
  that same group**. So **P47R is in trans with an exon-1 frameshift, while the P47T SCAR12 patients
  are homozygous.**

  🔴 **The genotypes are not matched, so Table 1 supports no allele-level comparison between P47R
  and P47T.** P47R's phenotype cannot be separated from the null-like allele in trans. This
  directly qualifies `CLAIM 030`, whose load-bearing line is *"P47R — stesso residuo — dà fenotipo
  severo"*.

**4.4 · Figure 2A carries two items its own text does not.** The `Wwox−/−` panel prints
**"Hypomyelination"**, which §5's rodent phenotype list omits — though the audit notes the item is
traceable inside the review's own bibliography to ref [35] (Tochigi 2019, hypomyelination in the
lde/lde rat), cited in §4 for expression only. It also prints **"Die within 4 weeks"** where the
body says *"premature death by 2 to 3 weeks after birth"* — recorded as `panel_qualifies_text` and
**not** a contradiction, since 2–3 weeks ⊂ within 4 weeks. (The body's 2–3 weeks is stated for the
knockout **mouse**; the lde/lde **rat** gets only unquantified "postnatal lethality".)

**4.5 · The count discrepancies — two dissolve under audit, two survive.** This section is the one
the audit most changed, and the corrections all run against the reading's initial interest.

🔴 **RETRACTED — 56 vs 57 is not an error.** Table S1's DEE28 `Cases` column sums to **58**, and
exactly **two** rows record a prenatal **medical termination of pregnancy** (`22m/Mtp`;
`alive/Mtp`). **58 − 2 = 56** (body and abstract); **58 − 1 = 57** (supplement legend). The spread
is an **inclusion rule for terminations**, not a typographic slip, and it is no longer reported as
an internal inconsistency.

🔴 **RETRACTED — 21 vs 22 is not an error either.** Of Table S3's 22 distinct variants, one —
`c.872T>C (p.Leu291Pro)` — is annotated solely *"Esophageal squamous cell carcinoma, somatic"* and
is not a WOREE/SCAR12 epilepsy variant. **22 − 1 = 21**, exactly Supplemental Figure 1's terminal
node, and the flow chart's terminal sum (76 + 103 + 21 = 200) closes.

**SURVIVES — Supplemental Figure 1's cascade does not close at the 205-variant node.** It branches
to an excluded set of **5**, a 50–500 kb set of **4** and a sub-51 bp set of **200**, and
**5 + 4 + 200 = 209, not 205**. Independently, §7.3 describes **six** exclusions at that step (one
1 kb–50 kb variant plus five 51 bp–1 kb variants) where the figure box says five.

**SURVIVES, weakly — the "65 eligible patients" node.** Supplemental Figure 1's second node reads
*"DECIPHER, Published and Our database: 65 eligible patients"*. That number is **not derivable**
from the components the text states (56 or 57 or 58 WOREE + 6 SCAR12 + 4 DECIPHER gives 66, 67 or
68). Recorded as *not reconcilable from the stated components*, **not** as an error — the "own
database" contribution is never quantified.

**So the corrected headline is narrower than the draft's.** The review's **variant-level** dataset
is checkable and reproduces exactly; its **patient-level** counts differ across surfaces for a
reason the deposit itself supplies; and one figure's internal arithmetic genuinely does not close.

## 5 · What this reading does NOT support

- **No mechanism.** The review performs no experiment. Nothing here establishes a pathway, a
  partner interaction, or a degradation route. Figures 1 and 2 are drawings.
- **No primary evidence for Q230P protein loss.** The claim that Q230P abolishes WWOX protein is
  **Johannsen's**, reached here only through this review's citation. This repository holds only
  Johannsen's abstract.
- **The survival ages are secondary-source facts.** They are transcribed by this review from
  primaries it cites. They await primary verification and are typed accordingly.
- **No therapeutic recommendation.** The responder subset in §4.2 is an uncontrolled retrospective
  observation across >21 heterogeneous reports in which "Yes" is nowhere defined, with no control,
  no dosing and obvious ascertainment bias. **It is not medical advice** and it does not reopen the
  vigabatrin adjudication already closed in `therapeutic_overclaim_closure.md` (A1: symptomatic,
  does not modify WWOX).
- **Nothing transfers to prognosis for any individual.** Same-genotype divergence is the finding;
  it forbids, rather than enables, genotype→prognosis inference.
