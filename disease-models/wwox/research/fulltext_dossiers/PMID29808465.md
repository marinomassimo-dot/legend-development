### What the panels show (manifest entries 1, 4-6, 8)

- **Fig. 2.** Two affected females, each in a different nuclear sibship under a different consanguineous couple, the two sibships joined through shared ancestors. No genotype is marked on any symbol. The text says the same: *"Patient 2 is a cousin of patient 1"*. **The two patients are cousins, not sisters.**
- **Fig. 3a.** Patient cDNA only (core amplicon just below 300 bp, 3' amplicon about 200 bp) plus empty no-RT lanes, after 45 cycles. No control-cell cDNA on the gel: it attests amplicon identity and absence of gDNA carry-over, not quantity.
- **Fig. 3b.** One bar per line, no error bars, no points, no n (Methods say measurements were run in duplicate). Comparators are PaTu-8988t, SW620 and HEK293 — two cancer lines and a kidney line, **no control fibroblast**. PaTu-8988t sits at 100 percent in both regions; patient fibroblast about 102 (core) and about 100 (3'). The legend does not name which line was set to 100.
- **Fig. 3c.** One membrane, one exposure. WWOX band in PaTu-8988t wild type, SW620, HEK293; none in three knockout clones and none in the patient fibroblast lane. The patient lane carries the **strongest actin band** of the membrane, so the absence is not explained by under-loading. No healthy-control fibroblast lane, no size marker drawn. The second harvest stated in the Results is not shown in any panel.
- **Fig. 1.** Structural phenotype in both patients, no quantitative measure. Panel b's contrast reads T1-like although the legend says T2-weighted (non-expert reading, INFERENZA; it changes nothing about the phenotype).
