## 1 · What the paper did

Clinical-grade WGS plus a research pipeline for structural, splice-site and non-coding variants; diagnostic
yield 43/122 (35%), 47/122 counted as solved. Seven structural-variant cases (Table 1), one of them WWOX.

## 3 · What the paper does NOT state

- No RNA, protein, localisation or mitochondrial assay for the in-frame deletion. "Including the mitochondrial
  targeting sequence" is an annotation from coordinates, not a measurement.
- No parental genotypes for the WWOX case (one sequenced sample).
- No clinical narrative for this case in the main text.
- That the deletion was missed by an array: the row-level reason is "Gene not incorporated into validated panel
  test at the time of referral", with the note "Deletion should be detectable by array".

## 4 · Caveats the authors give themselves

- Validating SVs took a range of methods, which the authors present as a scaling challenge.
- They note that the ACMG system did not capture every variant they considered pathogenic.

## 5 · Allele class and transfer limit

An in-frame deletion of exons 6–8 and a frameshift. Neither is in the reference genotype's class. The in-frame
deletion was not measured here at RNA or protein level, so it is no "structure-function experiment by nature"
until someone measures the protein; "in-frame" at DNA level does not show a stable protein. **No transfer to
Q230P or any other missense or splice allele.**
