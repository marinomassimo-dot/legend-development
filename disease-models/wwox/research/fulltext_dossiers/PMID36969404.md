## What it says (DATO about the review's text)

- ELOVL1 makes C24 saturated and monounsaturated sphingolipid acyl chains; CERS2 is required for C24 sphingolipid production.
- Human ELOVL1 disease (dominant and recessive) is described with hypomyelination, spasticity, ichthyosis, deafness and dyskinesia; cell data show lower C24 ceramide and sphingomyelin and a transcriptome with myelination genes down.
- The mouse knockout is described with startle epilepsy deaths, shorter acyl chains, lower galactosylceramide and mild hypomyelination in large callosal axons.
- Seizures in a human recessive neuro-ichthyotic disease are attributed to ELOVL4. Table 1 lists seizures for ELOVL4 and none for ELOVL1.
- Astrocyte-specific ELOVL1 deletion reduced the toxicity of reactive astrocyte conditioned medium to oligodendrocytes in vitro (one cited study).
