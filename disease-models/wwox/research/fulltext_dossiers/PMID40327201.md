## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. Metabolic content is cancer-cell content: WWOX binds HIF1A and suppresses the Warburg effect; loss increases glucose uptake and glycolysis (Table 2 breast row: GLUT1 up). 2. HDL and cholesterol regulation and metabolic-syndrome linkage are stated as established, cited to a 2015 review and to 2008 and 2019 papers.
3. The neurodevelopmental paragraph attributes cerebellar vermis hypoplasia and impaired neuronal migration to "specific homozygous mutations" citing a 2024 case report; the text of that report (read 2026-10-19) has neither term.
4. The restatement of the 2021 AAV9 paper names growth, seizures, ataxia, myelination and behaviour, and says only that it "highlights involvement in regulating glucose metabolism within the CNS".
5. The review declares it is cancer-weighted.
