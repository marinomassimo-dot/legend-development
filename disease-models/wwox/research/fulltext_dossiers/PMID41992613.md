## What the paper did

Six studies, summarised in the paper's own Table 1.
1. **Study #1** — screen of >20 AAV9 constructs in SMNΔ7 mice (Smn⁻/⁻; SMN2+/+; SMNΔ7+/+, FVB/N,
   JAX #005025), intravenous on postnatal day 1, at 2 × 10¹⁴ or 4 × 10¹⁴ vg/kg; **survival as the
   sole selection endpoint**. Benchmark: scAAV9-CB-hSMN, structurally identical to onasemnogene.
2. **Study #2** — route comparison (i.v. vs i.c.v.) for the two leads, survival/weight/motor.
3. **Study #3** — biodistribution and protein by route and dose, necropsy at 2 weeks.
4. **Study #4** — i.c.v. dose range-finding, 68 SMNΔ7 mice, four dose levels.
5. **Study #5** — non-GLP pilot safety, 17 **male-only** Wistar Han rats, single IT/lumbar, 16 weeks.
6. **Study #6** — GLP toxicology, 32 juvenile cynomolgus macaques (1.5–2 y), single IT/lumbar,
   vehicle / 1.0 × 10¹³ / 3.0 × 10¹³ vg per animal, necropsy at week 4 and week 26.

The selected candidate, **EXG001-307** = scAAV9.CMVenh.hSyn1.hSMN (neuron-preferential human
synapsin-1 promoter plus a CMV enhancer, self-complementary, wild-type AAV9 capsid).

## Why the upper limb of the window is inferred rather than measured

The paper says so itself, twice. The toxic arm is a **different construct** from the beneficial arm
(EXG001-340 is codon-optimised EXG001-307), so dose and expression change together; and for that arm:

- "Animals that died early following administration of EXG001-340 at 4 × 10¹⁴ vg/kg did not undergo
  comprehensive necropsy or histopathological evaluation"; only gross examination was done
  (enlarged heart, systemic swelling).
- "Further evaluation of SMN protein expression in major organs and tissues following administration
  of EXG001-340 at 4 × 10¹⁴ vg/kg would be valuable **to define this therapeutic window** and
  substantiate this conclusion."
- "Comprehensive safety and histopathological evaluations were not conducted in the EXG001-340
  high-dose cohort and warrant further investigation in future studies."

So the two-sided statement "both insufficient and excessive transgene expression are suboptimal"
rests, on its upper side, on **survival plus a gross-necropsy impression**, with no histopathology
and no measurement of the SMN level that is alleged to be excessive.

## What it does NOT state

- **DRG was never examined in mice**: "Due to the technical challenges of collecting DRGs from mice
  at this age, DRGs were not harvested or analyzed." The mouse DRG safety statement rests on
  hindlimb-clasping and gait video at 6 months.
- No head-to-head of EXG001-307 against the benchmark in the NHP study; the authors state they
  "anticipate" comparable transduction because the capsid is shared.
- The miRNA de-targeting claim is explicitly unconfirmed: "confirmatory experiments were not
  performed in order to prioritize program progression".
- No measurement of SMN protein in the arm that defines the ceiling, and no definition of the window
  in units of protein rather than vector genomes.
- Nothing about WWOX. *SMN* is a dose-sensitive splicing-complex protein; WWOX dose sensitivity is a
  separate empirical question and is nowhere addressed.

## Internal inconsistency found on reading — in the paper whose thesis is dose precision

The rat doses are normalised to body weight three mutually incompatible ways:

| Location | Low dose | High dose |
|---|---|---|
| Table 1, Study #5 | 8.5 × 10¹¹ vg/rat = **3.0 × 10¹² vg/kg** | 1.7 × 10¹² vg/rat = **6.0 × 10¹² vg/kg** |
| Results, rat section | 8.5 × 10¹¹ = **4.3 × 10¹² vg/kg** | 1.7 × 10¹² = **8.5 × 10¹³ vg/kg** |
| Methods, "Rats" | 8.5 × 10¹¹ = **4.3 × 10¹² vg/kg** | 1.7 × 10¹² = **8.5 × 10¹² vg/kg** |

The Results figure for the high dose (8.5 × 10¹³) is additionally impossible on its face: doubling
vg/animal cannot multiply vg/kg twenty-fold. Table 1 and Methods also disagree with each other for
both doses. The rat arm's exposure therefore cannot be stated in vg/kg from this paper.

## Caveats the authors themselves give

Beyond those quoted above: the NHP study enrolled no animals with extreme pre-existing anti-AAV9
titres (highest NAb 1:3 962); "subtle quantitative effects on transgene expression cannot be
excluded"; and the week-4 DRG pattern is read as having "a possible contribution from procedural
effects" given the vehicle findings.
