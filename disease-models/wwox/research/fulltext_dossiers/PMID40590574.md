## What it measures

A clinical series: seven patients from five consanguineous families with homozygous ELOVL1 variants (one nonsense, four missense) with clinical review, MRI, exome sequencing; plasma VLCFA in one patient (compared with four siblings); biochemistry of that one patient's plasma (a targeted kit) and skin fibroblasts (sphingolipid chain-length, ELOVL mRNAs, expression and activity of the truncated protein in transfected cells). ELOVL1 elongates saturated and monounsaturated C20-C24 acyl-CoAs to C22-C26. WWOX: zero occurrences.

## What it shows (DATO, class-level)

- Neurological picture in all: delayed motor development, progressive lower-limb spasticity, head tremor 7/7, myoclonus 6/7, dysarthria 6/6, nystagmus 5/6; ichthyosis 5/7 (variable, absent in the two siblings with the S83L variant); mild intellectual disability in 4 of 5 evaluated.
- MRI: hypomyelinating leukodystrophy 6/6 (cerebellar involvement noted), thin corpus callosum 5/6, no progression on repeat imaging in one.
- Biochemistry, one patient only: plasma C24:0 18 versus 26.5-32.7 and C26:0 0.21 versus 0.34-0.68 nmol/mL in four siblings (reductions of about a third to a half; ratios C24:0/C22:0 0.43 versus 0.64-0.82); plasma sphingomyelins uniformly lower; in fibroblasts C24:0 ceramide and hexosylceramide fall, shorter species rise (C24:0/C22:0 ratio 1.65 to 0.42 for ceramide, 2.35 to 0.45 for hexosylceramide); ELOVL1 mRNA about 0.15; ELOVL2 and ELOVL6 up, ELOVL3 and ELOVL4 down.

## What it does NOT support (the negative is the product)

1. **No epilepsy in this VLCFA-elongation hypomyelination disorder, as far as the paper records.** The comparison table lists seizures as absent for recessive ELOVL1 disease. The clinical table (Supplementary Table 1) has no seizure or EEG row at all; the absence rests on a "-" in a summary table, not a row of data. If that holds, hypomyelination with a thin corpus callosum from a VLCFA lesion does not by itself produce the epileptic encephalopathy of WOREE; the WWOX seizures would then need another driver. INFERENZA, conditional on the recorded absence.
2. **The biochemical evidence is one patient, one variant.** Four of five variants are VUS; fibroblasts exist for case 1 only; the three patient columns in the plasma heat map are not described as separate individuals; one patient fibroblast line against one control line (n of independent experiments, not of individuals).
3. **The antibody cannot show what is claimed.** The antibody for the absence of ELOVL1 protein in fibroblasts targets residues 250-277; the nonsense variant truncates at residue 154, so a truncated product would be invisible to it. The paper then shows a truncated protein in transfected cells that localises to the ER and retains about 57 percent of wild-type elongation (not normalised for expression, n = 3); whether the patient makes it is untested.
4. **A supplementary typo and an unexplained cell.** Supplementary Table 2 gives the C22:0 of sibling 4 as 0.41 where the other four are 30.9-47.4; the Sanger legend writes c.284C>T for the c.248C>T variant.
5. **No genotype-phenotype or dietary information.** The authors say the sample is too small and name genetic modifiers and dietary VLCFA intake as possible contributors; ELOVL1-to-ELOVL4 deficiency is a plausibility argument.
6. **Different organ logic from WWOX.** The tissue-restricted VLCFA lesion (skin and myelin) and non-progressive hypomyelination; WWOX loss is multi-system with early lethality.
