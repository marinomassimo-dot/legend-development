## 3 · What is measured — and at what level

1. **UV-C lowers WWOX and FHIT mRNA at 24 h in MCF-7** (L8). In native pixels (L9): no discernible WWOX or FHIT band at 10 or 15 J/m², p21 up with dose — and **GAPDH is visibly weaker in the 15 J/m² lane**, so only the 10 J/m² effect is loading-clean.
2. **IR 10 Gy does not** lower either transcript while inducing p21 (L14, L17). The cleanest contrast in the paper: same cells, same blot, opposite result.
3. **BPDE lowers both transcripts** (L16) — but in the pixels the WWOX band is **reduced and still clearly present**, not abolished, and that lane's GAPDH is lighter too (L17). *"Dramatic"* is stronger than the panel.
4. **The effect survives in p53-null Saos-2** (L18, L19), with equal GAPDH. One dose, one line.
5. **WWOX protein falls only after repeated UV** — unchanged at 24 h, reduced at 48 h (2×), faint at 72 h (3×), while the 72 h 3× lane carries *more* actin (L20, L21, L23). So the protein fall is real and is not a loading artefact.
6. **UV raises the S-phase fraction** (~37% → ~45%) and blocks DNA synthesis (CPM ~133,000 → ~25,000); **IR gives G2/M arrest** (~19% → ~52%) (L25, L26, L27, L29).
7. **Caffeine abolishes the S-phase delay and restores much of the WWOX and FHIT signal** (L28, L29, L30).
8. **WWOX mRNA is flat across a serum-release time course** while cyclin E rises (L31, L32).

## 5 · Discrepancies found only in the images, tables or arithmetic

1. 🔴 **The text cites Figure 1B and 1C the wrong way round** (L10): it attributes cell number to 1B and viability to 1C, while the panel axes and the caption (L11) say 1B is *Cell viability (% of untreated)* and 1C is *Total Cell Number (% of untreated)* (L12).
2. 🔴 **Loading is not equal where the strongest claim is made.** GAPDH is visibly weaker in the **15 J/m²** lane, and lighter in the **BPDE 0.5 µM** lane (L9, L17). The 10 J/m² UV result and the IR comparison are loading-clean; the 15 J/m² and BPDE lanes are not.
3. **"Dramatic decrease" overstates the BPDE panel**, where the WWOX band is reduced and still clearly present (L16 against L17).
4. 🔴 **Caffeine does more than the text says, and the arm that would show it is absent.** In Figure 4A the S-phase fraction with caffeine falls *below* the unirradiated control (~37% → ~29%) while G1 rises above it (L29). **No arm receives caffeine without UV** (L4), in either the flow cytometry or the Northern, so a direct effect of caffeine on WWOX transcription is not excluded — and the 10 mM recovery lane carries **heavier GAPDH** (L30).
5. **Figure 1B/1C have no legible x-axis treatment labels** in the manuscript raster, so the ~74% viability bar cannot be assigned to a named treatment (L12).
6. **Figure 5B's cell-cycle phases are inferred** from elapsed time and cyclin E; no flow profile of the release is shown (L32), and GAPDH is lighter at 24 h.
7. **Each probe row of Figure 1A is a separately framed strip** (L9) — the panel is assembled, as such panels of the period routinely were, and no lane-to-lane comparison across rows is licensed by it.
8. **Nothing in the paper is quantified.** No densitometry, no replicate blot, no statistical test; the word *"significantly"* appears in the caffeine sentence (L28) with no statistic anywhere.
9. **Figure 3 has no time-matched untreated lane at 48 h**, and the FHIT protein comparison is *"data not shown"* (L24).
10. **The cited replication (Ishii 2005) attributes the same phenomenon to the G1-S checkpoint** (L41), not the S-phase checkpoint this paper argues for.

## 6 · Strength, and transferability to the reference genotype

- **The paper's solid core is a difference, not a magnitude:** in the same cells, on the same blots, **UV-C and BPDE lower WWOX and FHIT mRNA while 10 Gy of ionising radiation does not**, with p21 confirming that all three agents triggered a damage response. That contrast is internally controlled and survives the loading problems, because the IR lanes are the clean ones. It holds in a p53-null line as well.
- **The protein result is weaker and more interesting than its sentence.** One unquantified blot shows WWOX protein essentially unchanged 24 h after a single dose and falling only after repeated doses, with *more* actin in the most reduced lane (L23). What that supports is a **persistence** observation: the protein pool outlasts a near-complete loss of its transcript for at least 24 h in MCF-7 (`INFERENZA`). It is not a half-life, not a degradation route, and not a measurement in any neural or primary cell.
- **Everything mechanistic is hypothesis**, and the Discussion sets two of its own hypotheses against each other (L34, L35).
- **Transferability: T3.** Two transformed adult tumour lines, carcinogen exposures at doses chosen for cell-culture toxicity, no WWOX allele, no neural cell, no animal, no developmental or seizure readout. Nothing here bears on a germline WWOX genotype. The two observations with any conceptual reach are the transcript-loss-without-protein-loss dissociation above and the fact that **WWOX transcription is responsive to a DNA-damage context at all** — both `INFERENZA` in this system, both explicitly not carried into a CNS model by this reading.
