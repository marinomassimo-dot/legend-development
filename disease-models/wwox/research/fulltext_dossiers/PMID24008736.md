## What the paper did

Three human tongue-derived squamous carcinoma lines (SCC-4, SCC-9, SCC-15) treated with methotrexate;
WWOX manipulated four ways — ectopic GFP-WWOX overexpression (SCC-9), siRNA and lentiviral shRNA knockdown
(SCC-15), a dominant-negative Y33R mutant (SCC-15) — plus tumour biopsies from patients treated with
intra-arterial methotrexate infusion, and one sentence of data from `Wwox` knockout mouse embryonic
fibroblasts.

## What it measured

Crystal-violet viability, annexin V, DAPI nuclear condensation, DNA laddering, TUNEL and cleaved caspase-3;
immunoblot for WWOX, caspase-9/-3, Beclin-1, Atg12–Atg5, LC3-I/II, mTOR and p70S6K and their phospho-forms;
RT-PCR and real-time PCR for *WWOX*; GFP-LC3 puncta by confocal microscopy; **transmission electron
microscopy of autophagosomes**; co-immunoprecipitation of WWOX with mTOR.

## What it states

- Methotrexate sensitivity tracks endogenous WWOX: SCC-15 (high WWOX) dies, SCC-9 (minimal WWOX protein,
  high *WWOX* mRNA) resists; methotrexate raises WWOX mRNA and protein in the sensitive lines only.
- WWOX is causal for the death: overexpression sensitises SCC-9 (Figure 2b, ~87 % → ~73 % viable at 96 h),
  knockdown and the Y33R dominant-negative protect SCC-15 (Figures 2d, 2e).
- Autophagy markers fall in the sensitive line and rise or hold in the resistant one: Beclin-1 1.0 → 0.2 and
  Atg12–Atg5 1.0 → 0.4 in SCC-15, against 1.0 → 1.7 (Atg12–Atg5) in SCC-9 (Figure 3a); LC3-II 1.0 → 0.3 in
  SCC-15, flat in SCC-9 (Figure 3b); GFP-LC3 puncta and EM autophagosomes disappear in treated SCC-15 and
  not in SCC-9 (Figure 4).
- **A lysosomal clamp was run** (Figure 3c): with E64d + pepstatin A, LC3-II goes 1.6 → 1.0 under
  methotrexate at 12 h (and 1.2 → 0.9 at 24 h), i.e. the fall survives the clamp, so it is not accelerated
  lysosomal turnover. MG132 blocks the LC3 fall, routing LC3 loss to the proteasome.
- WWOX co-immunoprecipitates with mTOR; methotrexate raises p-mTOR (1.0 → 8.3) and p-p70S6K (1.0 → 7.5) in
  SCC-15 and lowers p-mTOR in SCC-9; WWOX overexpression augments and WWOX knockdown suppresses that
  phosphorylation (Figure 5).
- WWOX overexpression lowers LC3-II, Atg12–Atg5 and Beclin-1 directly (Figure 6a, c, d); shWWOX prevents the
  methotrexate-induced LC3-II loss (Figure 6b: 1.0 → 0.3 in empty vector, 1.0 → 0.9 with shWWOX).
- One loss-of-function arm outside the drug context: "increased expression of LC3-II protein was detected in
  the cytosolic protein extracts from the `Wwox+/−` and `Wwox−/−` cells … as compared with the `Wwox+/+`
  control cells" (Supplementary Figure 7).

## What it does NOT state

- **The clamp is never applied to a WWOX manipulation.** Every clamped lane is methotrexate versus vehicle in
  parental SCC-15. The WWOX arms (overexpression, knockdown, null MEF) are unclamped steady-state westerns,
  so the sign of the WWOX→autophagy step is never measured as flux.
- The causal order through mTOR is not tested: no rapamycin, no raptor or ULK1 manipulation. The authors
  write "raising the possibility that WWOX may regulate autophagy through mTOR activation".
- No quantification is shown for the GFP-LC3 puncta despite the text reporting percentages and per-cell
  averages; Figure 4A carries images only.
- Nothing neural, nothing developmental, no human WWOX missense or splice allele.
- The scope is bound by the authors' own summary: "Thus, WWOX suppresses autophagy for inducing apoptosis in
  MTX-treated human SCC."
