## 3 · What is measured — and at what level

1. **WWOX IHC rises after decitabine in initially low tumours, but not significantly:** median 30 → 100, **P = 0.0547**, with 7 increases, 8 unchanged and 2 decreases of 17 (L6–L8). The vector marker count reproduces both medians and the six pre-treatment zeros (L9).
2. **Three of six WWOX-negative tumours became positive** (L10). No control arm, so conversion cannot be separated from sampling.
3. **WWOX is the weakest of the three responders:** FHIT rose in 8 of 8 (P = 0.014); the pooled FHIT+WWOX+FUS1 result (P = 0.0002) mixes overlapping patients and is driven by FHIT (L11).
4. **Nulls:** WWOX score does not track LINE-1 methylation (n = 44, r = 0.09, P = 0.57), nor does its change track the change in LINE-1 methylation (n = 19, r = −0.04, P = 0.87) (L12, L13); no association with time since last therapy (L14).
5. **Tumour response:** 5 of 15 evaluable had any shrinkage (0.2% to 33.4%, three of them under 5%); the association with a three-gene increase was null (P = 0.25) (L18, L19).

## 5 · Discrepancies found only in figures, tables or arithmetic

1. **Table 2 prints WWOX's P as 0.05** where the text and figure give 0.0547 (L7) — rounding that reads as significance.
2. **A visual count of Figure 1's WWOX panel gives 16 points, not 17**, because markers overlap; only the vector count recovers the 17th and reconciles the median of 30 with the six zeros (L9). *This was my own error first, caught by the count.*
3. **The dot plots show no pairing**, so which patients moved cannot be read from the figure.
4. **Per-gene n differs** (WWOX 17, FHIT 8, FUS1 12, PTEN 16) because tissue was limiting, and the subsets are not characterised by tumour type or dose (L0, L2).

## 6 · Strength, and transferability to the reference genotype

- **Strength:** a small, heterogeneous, unblinded correlative analysis inside a phase I trial, with one scorer, pooled doses, no control arm and no gene-specific methylation. The WWOX result is a **trend, not a significant effect**, and the nulls are as informative as the trend.
- **Transferability: T3 / ESPANSIONE.** Adult refractory cancers, tumour tissue, a commercial antibody, no neural material and no germline allele.
- **What a WWOX-DEE model can take from it, at its real strength:** this is the only paired **human in vivo** measurement of WWOX protein under a pharmacological DNA-methyltransferase inhibitor that this reading has seen (`WWOX AND decitabine` = 6 records in PubMed, 2026-09-14). It says that a demethylating agent **may** raise WWOX staining in tumours whose expression is low — and it does not say that the route is WWOX promoter demethylation. For a germline loss-of-function disease the relevance is bounded twice over: a transcriptional de-repressor can only matter where a functional allele is present but under-expressed, and nothing here addresses brain tissue, development, or a child's exposure to a myelosuppressive agent. Recorded as an ESPANSIONE, with no therapeutic inference drawn.
