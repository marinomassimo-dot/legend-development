## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. Early-infantile DEE: evidence is small case series and reports; responsive etiologies are ALG13, SCN2A, KCNQ2, mitochondrial respiratory-chain disorders, creatine transporter deficiency and glycine encephalopathy; spontaneous remission and late introduction confound interpretation.
2. Migrating focal seizures: 25 of 40 KCNT1 patients with outcomes (62.5 percent) had some benefit, qualitatively described.
3. Gene-level: meta-analysis overall 58 percent responders among 849 patients (SLC2A1 96, SCN2A 73, SCN1A 64, STXBP1 54, KCNQ2 42, KCNT1 24, CDKL5 23, SCN8A 15); seizure freedom overall 29 percent; KD generally after two or more failed ASMs; transporter disorders best, structural-integrity and homeostatic-regulation genes worst in one cohort.
4. Adverse effects: hypoglycemia especially during induction, dyslipidemia, acidosis, hyperketosis, growth and bone effects; monitoring includes glucose, carnitine and acylcarnitine, lipids.
5. WWOX and DEE28 do not occur anywhere in the text.
