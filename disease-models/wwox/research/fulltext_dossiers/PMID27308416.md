## Scientific verdict

The commentary reports that WWOX-null primary mouse embryonic fibroblasts have higher glucose uptake,
lactate and NADPH, with lower NADH, ATP and oxygen consumption; that HIF1alpha inhibition reverses the
glucose-uptake phenotype; and that WWOX binds HIF1alpha through WW1. Those observations belong to the
cited primary PMID 25012504. This article contributes interpretation and a compact conceptual model,
not independent replication.

Figure 1 depicts WWOX restraining HIF1alpha, HIF1alpha inducing PDK, and PDK inhibiting PDH and entry
into the TCA cycle. Its evidential role is explanatory only. The paper's phrase that HIF1alpha
inhibition “reversed” glucose uptake is directionally interpretable only as reversal of the elevated
uptake phenotype; it should not be converted into a standalone quantitative claim.
