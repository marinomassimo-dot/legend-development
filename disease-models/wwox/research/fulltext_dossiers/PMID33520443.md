## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. HIF1A mRNA is higher in GDM by 3.8-fold (Fig 2B, three asterisks); WWOX mRNA is lower by 1.4-fold with a single asterisk that the Results text itself calls "a tendency" (about p 0.04); the ratio is about 6.5-fold lower. The abstract's word "significant" for WWOX is therefore stronger than the Results text. No multiple-testing correction is named anywhere in the article (eleven genes compared).
2. Fig 2A shows the two WWOX distributions overlapping almost entirely; the NGT box is far wider. The ratio panel (2L) has its spread in a handful of NGT outliers.
3. HIF1A-induced genes SLC2A1, PKM2, PFK, LDHA are higher in GDM; HK2, the first rate-limiting step, is unchanged; SLC2A4 (GLUT4) is lower.
4. Fig 4 (GDM Spearman matrix): WWOX versus HIF1A rho -0.05 (no asterisk); WWOX versus HK2, PKM2, SLC2A1 -0.07, -0.06, 0.04; WWOX versus PFK -0.22 (one asterisk). The ratio correlates strongly with HIF1A (-0.79) and with HIF1A-induced genes, which is the arithmetic of a ratio with HIF1A in its denominator and does not show that WWOX modulates HIF1A.
5. Fig 5 (NGT matrix): WWOX correlates positively with PFK (0.30) and LDHA (0.32); WWOX versus HIF1A -0.12 (no asterisk). In normal pregnancy WWOX also rises with insulin and HOMA-IR (R 0.52, 0.53), the opposite of the direction a WWOX-loss-causes-insulin-resistance reading would predict.
6. HbA1c correlates in GDM with HIF1A, HK2, PFK, SLC2A1 (R 0.31 to 0.35) and inversely with LDHA. HbA1c spans about 4.3 to 6.1 percent and GDM women have lower HbA1c than NGT women (stated, unexplained). Figure 6 prints C: R 0.35 p 0.0003 (text: p 0.004) and E: R -0.22 p 0.036 (text and Table S1: R -0.27 p 0.010). Two printed statistics disagree with the running text; neither changes the sign.
7. Postpartum WWOX is about 2-fold higher than the same women's GDM value and equals the NGT level (Fig 3; paired n 8 and 12, one asterisk each).

## 3 · Comparison with the laboratory's records

- The paper is carried in the registries only as a singleton queue node (FT-144) and as reference 15 of the Baryla 2022 review (FT-111); no record holds its measurements or its scope. Its own group authored the fibroblast paper (PMID 35328751) and the review (PMID 36271927), so the three are one laboratory's arc, not independent confirmations.
- The 2026-09-21 audit concluded that the HIF1A/glycolysis arc is measured in fibroblasts, muscle, liver, flies and cancer lines and never in neural tissue, and is at HYPOTHESIS strength. This paper adds the one human, clinical-state measurement, and it is the weakest kind of support for the arc: transcript levels in a mixed blood cell pool, an association, a marginal WWOX difference, and an internal correlation structure (Fig 4) in which WWOX and HIF1A transcripts are unrelated.
- The paper's own Conclusions ask for protein-level data and larger cohorts. Neither exists in this paper.

## 4 · What the paper does NOT support

- That WWOX loss raises HIF1A or glycolysis in humans. Direction of cause is untested; the data are cross-sectional associations with an NGT comparator that differs in age and HbA1c.
- That WWOX is measurably lower in GDM leukocytes at a convincing level (p about 0.04, uncorrected, overlapping distributions).
- That the ratio is "a better indicator": a ratio whose variance is dominated by HIF1A will correlate with HIF1A targets whatever WWOX does.
- Anything about the nervous system, development, or biallelic or heterozygous WWOX loss: no genotype was ascertained.

## 5 · Transfer verdict toward the reference genotype (WWOX-DEE)

Transfer: ESPANSIONE at best. System gap: adult pregnant blood leukocytes, expression-level variation, a metabolic state (gestational glucose intolerance) that the reference genotype does not have; the paper contributes no neural, developmental or loss-of-function evidence. Value to the model is as a negative bound: the one human glycaemia-state measurement of the WWOX/HIF1A pair does not show the pair co-varying.

## 6 · Epistemic tags and reopening

- DATO: HIF1A mRNA higher and SLC2A4 mRNA lower in GDM leukocytes in this cohort; WWOX and HIF1A transcripts uncorrelated within GDM (Fig 4).
- INFERENZA: the WWOX/HIF1A ratio shift in this paper is carried by HIF1A. PREMISE: DATO (stated fold changes 1.4 versus 3.8 and Fig 4 values).
- Rejected: reading Fig 2L as evidence of axis dysregulation. PREMISE: INFERENZA (ratio arithmetic). REVIVAL_TRIGGER: a replication with protein-level HIF1A and WWOX, leukocyte subsets and correction for multiple testing, or a Mendelian-randomisation estimate of WWOX expression on HbA1c, would reopen it.
