## 3 · 🔴 The principal finding — an allele-level rule against a genotype-level figure

The review's genotype–phenotype paragraph states the rule at **allele level**:

> *"Relatively milder phenotypes seem to originate from missense point mutations (P47T and P47R,
> G372R), whereas severe manifestations were observed in nonsense mutations (R54\*, K297\*, and
> W335\*) or partial/complete deletions"*

**Its own Figure 2B, read at 8×, records that three of those six variants sit in compound-
heterozygous genotypes.** Measured from the pixels, and reproduced independently by both blind
auditors:

| Variant | Row | Genotype as drawn | Phenotype letter |
|---|---|---|---|
| P47T | i | **homozygous** (one tick crossing both bars) | `S` |
| G372R | ii | **homozygous** | `S` |
| R54\* | iv | **homozygous** | `W` |
| **P47R** | vi | **in trans with A16\*** (green allele / blue allele) | **`S` ↔ `W`** |
| W335\* | viii | in trans with the Exon5del/H173\* allele | `W` |
| K297\* | ix | in trans with Exon1-9del | `W` |

**The missense→milder / nonsense→severe correspondence is clean only on the three homozygous rows.**

And the P47R row is the one the panel refuses to classify: **row vi is the only row whose phenotype
annotation is not a single letter.** It reads `S` and `W` joined by a double-headed arrow, while the
caption defines only *"S stands for SCAR12, and W stands for WOREE"* and never explains the arrow.
So the single patient the running text groups with the *"relatively milder"* missense alleles is the
one patient the figure declines to place at either pole.

**What the arrow means is not stated in this source, and it is left unresolved here rather than
resolved by choosing the more interesting reading** (`scientist_reading_modes.md` §3.4).

### What this does, and does not do, to `CLAIM 030`

`CLAIM 030`'s summary states *"**P47R** — stesso residuo — dà fenotipo **severo**"* as an allele-level
fact. This reading **corroborates the narrowing this actor made in wave 2** from Banne 2021
(PMID 33916893): P47R's severity cannot be read at allele level, because P47R is only ever seen in
trans with a null-like allele.

### The allele in trans is annotated three ways, and they do not agree

| Source | Annotation |
|---|---|
| Banne 2021 Table 1 (this actor, wave 2, audited) | `c.46_49del` **`p.Asp16fs`**, typed *"Deletion (Exon 1)/Nonsense"* |
| This review, running text | *"a WWOX **exon 1 frameshift** allele"* |
| This review, **Figure 2B** | **`A16*`** — an asterisk its own caption defines as a **stop codon** |

Two say frameshift, the figure says nonsense, and the residue at codon 16 differs — **Asp** in the
cDNA-level record against **Ala** in the schematic. **Registered, not adjudicated:** a frameshift is
routinely rendered at protein level as a truncation, which covers the class difference but not the
residue. Only the deposited variant record or the unread primary can settle it.

The two sources also **assign the phenotype differently** — Banne's table places the P47R compound
heterozygote in the DEE28/WOREE block (2 patients, alive at 4 y and 3 y), while this figure marks
that row `S` ↔ `W`. Whether the underlying patients are the same individuals **cannot be settled from
either secondary source.**

🔴 **It is not an independent patient, and the dossier says so.** This review's patients vi–ix and
Banne's P47R row **both trace to Mignot 2015 (PMID 25411445)**, which this corpus holds at **no depth
at all**. Two secondary sources over one unread primary is one observation, not two.

## 4 · The ubiquitination thread — what a review is worth when the primary is already read

The review states the ITCH event with a **non-degradative** outcome:

> *"the ubiquitin E3 ligase ITCH physically associates and functionally mediates Lys-63-linked
> ubiquitination of WWOX, leading to its stabilization and nuclear localization (41)"*

Reference 41 resolves to **Abu-Odeh 2014, *J Biol Chem* 289:8865–8880 = PMID 24550385**, which this
corpus **already holds at `complete_fulltext_read`** (`FTR-20260810-24550385-02`), with the
cycloheximide-chase and *Itch*-null numbers the review does not print. **This review therefore adds
no independent evidentiary weight for that biology**, and the corpus's `CLAIM 029` already carries

**What it does add is dated position.** Measured over the article prose with the 78-item reference

## 6 · Three source-level defects, each checkable

1. **The Figure 2 caption cites a paper with no patients.** *"The two alleles of each of the reported
   patients (59, 63)"* — verified on the **rendered PDF page 3** as well as in the HTML, because a
   digit is exactly what an extraction can damage. The per-patient list in the same caption names
   refs 20, 61, 62, 63, 65 and 75 and **never 59**; ref 59 is Nunez 2006, *"WWOX protein expression in
   normal human tissues"*.
2. **The point-mutation equivocation.** The Figure 3 caption says *"G372R, the only point mutation
   associated with CNS-related diseases"*; the body adds *"in the SDR region"*. Figure 2B shows P47T,
   P47R and G372R as point mutations in CNS patients, **and four further single-residue changes inside
   the SDR region** (H173\*, V202\*, K297\*, W335\*). **Both forms survive only if "point mutation"
   silently means "missense"** — and that equivocation, not the missing region qualifier, is the defect.
3. **Figure 2A's markers are unexplained by their own caption.** Measured against the protein bar's
   ends (x=68→residue 1, x=2121→residue 414; 4.971 px/residue): **four** stalks at residues **12.3,
   14.3, 33.2 and 286.9**, carried by three trapezoid boxes of which the leftmost is doubled. The
   caption glosses *"P, proline"*. Only 286.9 corresponds to a residue the article identifies at all
   (**Tyr-287**, its single named phosphorylation site); **Pro-47, the only proline the article
   discusses, carries no marker.**
