## What the paper did

**Two separate NHP studies**, not one, both ICM, both male-only, both 2–3 kg / 2–3-year-old
cynomolgus, all pre-screened as neutralising-antibody negative:

- **Study 1 (1 month):** buffer n = 1, AAVDJ n = 2, AAV9 n = 2; 2.0 × 10^13 VG/brain in 1 mL;
  necropsy day 30.
- **Study 2 (3 months):** buffer n = 2, AAV1 n = 3, AAV5 n = 3, AAV9 n = 3; same dose and volume;
  necropsy day 90.

One cassette for all four capsids: EFS promoter, chimeric intron, wild-type human *GBA1* with a
C-terminal HA tag, WPREmut6delATG, hGH polyA. ICM targeted by anatomical landmarks only, no
imaging; animals not placed in Trendelenburg.

**Both studies ran under weekly systemic corticosteroid cover.** Methods, "ICM AAV administration
and CSF collection": "For immunosuppression, methylprednisolone acetate … was administered
intramuscularly … weekly; at 40 mg/body … at 5 days before dosing, and at 20 mg/kg … from day 3
to day 24 in study 1, and at 40 mg/body from 5 days before dosing to day 24, and at 20 mg/body
from day 31 to day 87 in study 2." **This fact appears nowhere in the abstract, the results or
the discussion.**

## What it measured

Vector genomes by qPCR (VG/µg DNA, LLOQ 400 VG/µg), human *GBA1* mRNA by RT-qPCR, GCase activity
(4MUG) in tissue, CSF and plasma, capillary western (WES) against the HA tag, anti-HA
immunohistochemistry graded qualitatively (−, ±, +, ++) by a JSTP board-certified pathologist with
peer review, H&E histopathology of brain, spinal cord, DRG at four levels, trigeminal ganglion,
liver and nasal cavity, plus clinical signs, body weight, haematology, coagulation, clinical
chemistry, gross pathology and serial neutralising-antibody titres.

## What it states

1. **Capsid was not a lever on where the vector went.** "Our data show no significant difference
   in biodistribution among AAV serotypes." AAV1 trended highest and AAV5 lowest; AAVDJ slightly
   above AAV9; the authors conclude "we could not conclude which AAV would be superior".
2. **ICM loads the sensory ganglia and the cord, not the deep brain.** Highest VG (~10^5–10^7
   VG/µg) in spinal cord, olfactory bulb, DRG, trigeminal ganglia, optic nerve, deep cervical
   lymph nodes, liver and spleen; deep brain (caudate head, putamen, thalamus) ~10^4 VG/µg, which
   the authors note is below one vector copy per cell. mRNA was detectable only above
   ~5 × 10^4 VG/µg. "No HA immunoreactivity was detectable in deep brain region such as substantia
   nigra or striatum for any of the AAVs."
3. **Sensory ganglia were massively transduced.** Re-extracted cell-wise from Table 3, sacral DRG
   neuron HA positivity in study 2 was **++ (31–80% of cells) in all three AAV1 animals, + in all
   three AAV5 animals, and ++/++/+ in the three AAV9 animals**, against − in both vehicle animals.
4. **Yet DRG pathology was nearly absent.** "Neuronal degeneration and necrosis with the presence
   of mononuclear cell infiltrate was detected only in a sacral DRG of one animal of the
   AAV5-treated group" — 1 of 11 dosed animals across both studies. The authors state they cannot
   decide whether it was AAV5-related, and conclude "there is no clear evidence linking the
   transgene overexpression/increased vector genome transduction to the DRG findings".
5. **AAV9 is the liver outlier.** ~10-fold higher hepatic VG than the other capsids after ICM, in
   both studies.
6. **All animals seroconverted.** Anti-AAV neutralising antibodies turned positive in every animal
   by day 57–90 despite the steroid cover.

## What it does NOT state

- Nothing about WWOX, nothing about any DEE.
- **It does not attribute its low DRG-finding rate to the immunosuppression**, because it does not
  mention the immunosuppression outside Methods, and there is no untreated-for-steroid arm. The
  study therefore cannot separate capsid, cassette, dose, promoter strength and steroid cover.
- It reports no functional sensory testing and no nerve conduction.
- It is a biodistribution study with terminal, single-timepoint readouts per study (day 30 or
  day 90) and n = 2–3 per group; "well tolerated with no significant toxicity" rests on that.
- It does not test dose: one dose level (2.0 × 10^13 VG/brain) for every capsid.

## Caveats the authors themselves give

ICM targeting "was guided solely by anatomical landmarks and CSF outflow, without the aid of
real-time imaging", so targeting accuracy varied between animals; group-level variability makes
serotype differences non-robust; an unexplained discrepancy in substantia nigra VG between the
one-month and three-month studies; IHC was scored qualitatively.

## Transfer limit

Cynomolgus macaque, male only, young adult, one dose level, nAb-negative at entry, under weekly
systemic methylprednisolone, secreted lysosomal-hydrolase cargo under a weak ubiquitous promoter
(EFS). None of those conditions is a WWOX-DEE infant, and the cargo's secretability is the one
property WWOX does not share. Nothing here is medical advice.
