## 3 · What the paper states

**A — endogenous elevation in disease (observational).** WWOX was higher in HD prefrontal cortex
than control (Mann-Whitney U=31, p=0.0005) and higher in 65Q cortical neurons than isogenic 27Q
(n=4, U=0, p=0.0266). It did **not** correlate with CAG length (r=0.053, p=0.8397) or age at death
(r=0.059, p=0.8164).

**B — applied recombinant protein is cytotoxic, dose- and time-dependently.**
> *"Treatment with rWWOX caused cell death in a dose- and time-dependent manner in SH- SY5Y cells,
> with significant decreases observed at 100 or 500ng/mL after 24h and at 10, 100 or 500ng/mL after
> 48h"*

**C — and it is cytotoxic to human cortical neurons regardless of genotype.** This is the single
most load-bearing sentence in the paper for the dose question:
> *"Our results revealed a significant decrease in cell viability in both rWWOX-treated HD cortical
> neurons (65Q) and their isogenic controls (27Q) compared to the respective vehicle- treated
> cells"*

with
> *"Two-way ANOVA revealed a significant effect of treatment [F(1, 28)=78.44; p<0.0001], and
> genotype [F(1,28)=6.181; p=0.0192], with no significant treatment x genotype interaction
> [F(1,28)=0.3129; p=0.5804]"*

**No interaction term** ⇒ the toxicity is not a property of the HD background. It is a property of
the exposure.

**D — γ-H2AX has a threshold, not a slope.**
> *"While treatment with 10ng/mL of rWWOX did not alter g-H2AX levels (Tukey's test, p=0.6018),
> treatment with either 100ng/mL (Tukey's test, p=0.0139) or 150ng/mL of rWWOX (Tukey's test,
> p=0.0038) significantly increased g-H2AX levels compared to vehicle-treated cells."*

**E — intracellular overexpression also raises γ-H2AX**, in SH-SY5Y and in RPE-1. The only
quantification of *how much* WWOX was added anywhere in the paper is
> *"WWOX levels were significantly increased in SH-SY5Y overexpressing WWOX (WWOXOE) cells compared
> to NTC (Mann-Whitney U test=1, p=0.0476)"*

**F — the earned negative, and it is the title's second half.**
> *"WWOX overexpression did not alter the rate of CAG repeat instability at any time point"*

**G — the authors' own two-sided conclusion.**
> *"our findings indicate that elevated WWOX can also promote DNA damage. Together, these
> observations suggest that maintaining appropriate WWOX levels may be important for cellular
> homeostasis."*

## 4 · What the paper does **not** state

- **No in vivo experiment of any kind.** No animal, no vector, no delivered dose, no AAV, no
  promoter, no biodistribution, no survival endpoint.
- **No neuron-targeted transgene.** The neuronal result (C) comes **only** from applied
  extracellular protein. The transgene arm lives in a neuroblastoma line and in RPE-1. The paper
  never raises WWOX *inside* a neuron.
- **No mechanism by which extracellular WWOX acts.** WWOX is a ~46 kDa intracellular protein. How
  an applied recombinant preparation reaches the nucleus to generate γ-H2AX is not addressed, not
  measured and not discussed.
- **No fold-change for the overexpression**, in text or legend; only a Mann-Whitney statistic.
- **No claim about therapy, in HD or anywhere.** The direction is named as an open question:
  > *"Defining the relationship between mutant HTT and WWOX will be important to determine whether
  > modulation of WWOX can mitigate disease-relevant phenotypes."*
- **No mention of WWOX-DEE, WOREE, SCAR12, any WWOX missense or splice allele, or gene therapy.**
- **No causal claim for the human elevation.** The authors explicitly leave compensatory versus
  maladaptive open.

## 5 · Caveats the authors give themselves

- The post-mortem cohort is advanced-stage, so *when* WWOX dysregulation emerges cannot be determined.
- γ-H2AX *"does not directly quantify DNA double-strand breaks"*.
- Whether WWOX acts downstream of mutant HTT, independently, or as a generic neurodegeneration
  response *"remains to be determined"*.

## 6 · Caveats this reading adds

🔴 **The controls that would separate "WWOX" from "a commercial protein preparation" are absent.**
No heat-denatured rWWOX arm, no irrelevant-recombinant-protein arm, no tag-only arm, no endotoxin
assessment appears anywhere in the methods. An MTT fall at 100–500 ng/mL of an applied recombinant
protein is precisely where such an artefact lives. Until one of those controls exists, **(B) and
(C) are properties of the exposure, not demonstrated properties of WWOX.**

⚠️ One reagent (Abcam ab137726) serves for blotting, for immunodepletion and as the identity check
on the purchased protein; there is no orthogonal validation of WWOX identity.

⚠️ The n discrepancy between Methods (21 HD) and the Figure 3A legend (18 HD) is unexplained.

## What the panels show (hand readings from bar charts; n and statistics as printed)

1. **Fig 3.** HD cortex WWOX about 1.6 times control (~65 vs ~40 percent GAPDH; n = 18 vs 13). The
   "no correlation with CAG repeat length" is over the **inherited** tract (about 40-51 repeats), r = 0.05,
   p = 0.84: it is not a somatic-instability measurement.
2. **Fig 4.** WWOX 65Q about 1.9 times isogenic 27Q (n = 4). Viability after 100 ng/mL applied protein:
   27Q 0.112 to 0.079 (ratio 0.71), 65Q 0.104 to 0.067 (ratio 0.64). The legend reports a significant genotype
   main effect (F(1,28) = 6.181, p = 0.0192; recomputed 0.0191) and no treatment x genotype interaction
   (F = 0.3129, p = 0.5804; recomputed 0.5804). So the toxicity is quantified, present in both genotypes,
   not disease-specific on these data, and "no between-genotype comparison is reported" is not accurate
   (a main effect is reported; the interaction is not significant).
3. **Fig 5.** The gamma-H2AX rise from applied protein is small: vehicle ~30,000, 100 ng/mL ~33,000
   (1.10 times), 150 ng/mL ~33,300 (1.11 times), camptothecin ~36,500 (1.22 times). Stable overexpression
   in SH-SY5Y is ~1.9 times NTC protein and gamma-H2AX ~2.0 times (83 vs 41 million units; camptothecin 2.5 times).
4. **Supp Fig 2** (same line, 24 h): viability at 100 ng/mL ~0.024 vs ~0.076 vehicle (ratio 0.32), 48 h at
   10 ng/mL ~0.019 vs ~0.061. The concentration at which gamma-H2AX rises ~10 percent coincides with
   most of the viable signal lost: DNA damage and cytotoxicity are not separable.
5. **Fig 7 (the "not instability" half).** The instability null is a single model: RPE-1 cells with a
   CAG115 reporter at a safe-harbour site, six clones per arm, 42 days, **overexpression only** (no
   loss-of-function arm for instability), not neurons, not HD cells. WWOX overexpression ~40 times NTC
   (IDV/GAPDH); gamma-H2AX ~1.23 times NTC (camptothecin 1.40). Six traces per arm overlap at every time
   point (final gain ~2.0-4.0 control, ~2.4-3.1 WWOX-OE); legend regression p = 0.877 for WWOX.
6. **Arithmetic screen.** The legend of Fig 7C gives F(2,11) = 18.07 with p = 0.003; recomputed from the
   two printed numbers (df 2 and 11) p = 0.00033, a tenfold discrepancy in the unfavourable direction
   (the printed p understates). Other F-tests recomputed agree (4B genotype 0.0191, interaction 0.5804).
   Legend says "mean +/- SEM" for 7D, which plots individual clone traces.
