## 3 · What is measured, with what controls

1. **VOPP1–WWOX co-association — measured three independent ways.** Y2H (yeast, ten clones, C-terminus), reciprocal co-IP of over-expressed proteins in HEK-293T, and **endogenous co-IP in a breast cancer line with an IgG control**. The endogenous arm is the strongest single interaction datum: neither partner over-expressed.
2. **WW1-dependence — measured by mutation.** WWOX Y33R abolishes the co-IP (L6; Figure 1e lane 5 blank, L25).
3. **PPPY165-dependence — measured by mutation, with two negative-control motifs.** Y119A retains binding, Y165A abolishes it, and the whole-cell-lysate row confirms every mutant is expressed, so the loss is not an expression artefact (L25).
4. **The binding-deficient mutant is used as a functional control throughout** — Y165A fails to relocate WWOX (L14), fails to displace p73α (L16), and fails to block WWOX-induced death (L37). This is the paper's best design feature: it ties each downstream effect to the PPPY165-dependent interaction rather than to VOPP1 abundance.
5. **WWOX-dependence of the apoptotic effect — actually tested by epistasis** (L17): si-VOPP1 has no effect in WWOX-depleted cells.
6. **Clinical interaction — formally tested**, Cox interaction term HR 3.41 (L23), and the VOPP1/WWOX ratio separates survival only in WWOX-**retaining** tumours (L33).

**Controls that are absent or weak:** no IgG or empty-vector IP in Figure 1a (its only control is single-transfection lanes, L26); **no replicate count is stated for any blot** — the strings "replicate" and "independent experiment" occur zero times; Figure 4d's quantification is a single-experiment ratio strip with no error bars and no n (L27); the in-house anti-VOPP1 antibody has no dedicated validation panel (no peptide block, pre-immune serum or second antibody), though Figure 4a does show the signal falling on knockdown (L19, searched negative).

## 5 · Discrepancies found only in the images or supplements

1. **Two different WWOX cut-offs in one paper.** Table S1 dichotomises WWOX at `<1` / `>1` (302/146); Figure 7a and the Statistical-analysis section use `0.6` (193/255). "WWOX-low" therefore names different patients in different figures, with no reconciliation offered (L35).
2. **Figure 4e's system is visible only in the pixels.** The panel is headed **NIH3T3**, but neither the text nor the caption names the line for 4e — a text-only reader would carry the p73α result forward as HEK-293T or MDA-MB-468 (L28).
3. **The headline survival result is not significant in the whole cohort:** Figure 6d leftmost panel prints `P=0.09` for all 448; significance appears only in the luminal (0.003) and luminal B (0.005) subsets (L32).
4. **Table S3 is more precise than the text:** p = **0.016** (text rounds to 0.02) and CI **1.26–9.24**, which the text omits — a wide interval on a single retrospective cohort (L34).
5. **Figure S3A shows four Oncomine datasets but reports p-values for three**; Richardson (normal n = 7) carries no p-value anywhere (L39).
6. **The WWOX side of the survival analysis is the weaker side:** S4D reaches only `P=0.05`, in ER/PR-negative tumours, and is `P=0.1` overall (L40).
7. Typographical: Figure 6a y-axis reads "realtive"; Figure 5a's caption says "stably transfected with control VOPP1-expressing vectors" for what are two arms.

## 6 · Strength, and transferability to WWOX-DEE

- **Interaction claim:** strong for co-association and for WW1/PPPY165 **dependence**, because it is mutation-tested on both sides, replicated across three methods, and shown once on endogenous protein with an IgG control. Weak for **directness**, which nothing in the paper addresses.
