## What the paper did

Engineered two human-derived promoter cassettes (STXPro5, from the STXBP1 locus; SNAP25, from the
SNAP25 locus) to drive a codon-optimised, CpG-reduced human STXBP1 transgene in AAV9; screened a
10,000-element 3' UTR library for a DRG-detargeting element and selected DT-A (four tandem
hsa-mir-10b binding sites). Mouse: bilateral ICV at PND1 in Stxbp1⁺/⁻ and WT, dose range 1E10–1E11
vg/animal, behavioural battery at 8–11 weeks (open field, nest building, cued/contextual fear
conditioning, hindlimb clasping, PTZ seizure challenge, chronic cortical EEG). NHP: single
**unilateral** ICV, 2 mL at 0.1 mL/min, 1E14 vg/animal, juvenile cynomolgus macaques
(16–20 months in Results, 16–21 months in Methods; ~1.58–2.60 kg), 50-day study, no immunosuppression,
n = 3 per group except STXPro5 n = 4; biodistribution, transgene RNA, protein IHC, histopathology,
serum NfL, nerve conduction.

## What it does **not** say

- It does not establish a therapeutic window: PND1 is the only age dosed.
- The NHP study is 50 days with n = 3–4 per arm; it cannot speak to durability, to immune responses
  under a second challenge, or to rare events.
- DRG findings persisted at low grade even with DT-A, and the authors attribute the residue to a
  transgene-independent capsid class effect.
- Nothing here is about a recessive null disease. STXBP1-RD is dominant haploinsufficiency; the
  delivered transgene tops up a gene that is already expressed in every target cell.

## Transfer limit to WWOX-DEE (stated exactly)

Transferable: the **vector-engineering method** — mining human regulatory genomics for a promoter that
reaches GABAergic as well as glutamatergic neurons, and a 3'-UTR miRNA-site cassette that detargets
DRG without lowering brain expression. The authors state the DT-A element worked across three
promoters and two transgenes, which is the only basis for expecting promoter/transgene independence,
and that basis is three promoters and two transgenes, not a general law.
**Not transferable:** the dose, because the required expression level is set by the gene's dosage
biology and WWOX-DEE is a recessive null, not a 50 %-of-normal haploinsufficiency; the tolerability of
overexpression, because it was measured for STXBP1 protein in STXBP1-competent tissue and WWOX is an
SDR-family enzyme with partners (HYAL-2, ITCH, p73, ATM) whose stoichiometry is untested; and the
window, which this paper does not measure for any gene.
