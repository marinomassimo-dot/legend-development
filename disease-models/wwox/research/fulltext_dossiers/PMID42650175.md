## 1 · What the paper measures

One de novo SLC6A1 missense (p.Ala305Val) in a child with myoclonic-atonic epilepsy: nine stability predictors; radiolabelled GABA uptake in HEK293T, human iPSC astrocytes and cortical neurons; ER colocalisation; rescue by 4-phenylbutyrate (PBA), TUDCA, salubrinal and wild-type cDNA co-expression.

## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. Variant transporter retained in the ER (about 76 versus 30 percent overlap); PBA lowers the overlap to about 36 percent and restores total signal; uptake is reduced to about 30 to 64 percent of wild type depending on cell type.
2. Heterozygous-condition uptake 0.59 to 0.66 of wild type, restored to 1.01 to 1.04 by PBA (TUDCA 0.82 to 0.92, salubrinal 0.82 to 0.89); added wild-type cDNA and PBA together exceed either alone in the dose ranges used (PBA 0.5 to 2 mM; 4 mM compromised viability and was dropped).
3. Plasmid co-expression models gene augmentation; no viral delivery, no animal, no knock-in model; the clinical-trial feedback is stated as promising without data; absence of dominant negative effect under PBA is a hypothesis.
4. WWOX does not occur.

## 3 · Transfer (system gap, stated)

The design is for a membrane transporter that is retained in the ER and can be rescued by chemical chaperoning. WWOX is a cytosolic and nuclear adaptor and oxidoreductase; the reference missense allele is reported to give a normal transcript and no detectable protein, a degradation phenotype, not shown to be ER-retention. The paper therefore transfers only the logic (measure surface or total protein and function under a chaperone; test chaperone plus added wild-type dose), not any expectation that PBA would act on WWOX. It also offers a dose-limiting observation: 4 mM PBA reduced cell viability. No metabolic or autonomic claim.
