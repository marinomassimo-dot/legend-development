# Full-text dossier — PMID 30290271

**Study:** Hussain T, Kil H, Hattiangady B, Lee J, Kodali M, Shuai B, Attaluri S,
Tome-Garcia J, Meghed M, Jang M-H, Shetty AK, Aldaz CM. *Wwox deletion leads to reduced
GABA-ergic inhibitory interneuron numbers and activation of microglia and astrocytes in
mouse hippocampus.* Neurobiol Dis. 2019;121:163–176. DOI
`10.1016/j.nbd.2018.09.026`; PMCID `PMC7104842`.

**Reading status:** complete full-text read on 2026-08-06. The 27-page PDF was read
sequentially, including all sections, Table 1, six figure captions, back matter and all 90
references. All six figures were inspected at original resolution. Supplementary Table 1
(DOCX, all 18 primer pairs) and Supplementary Table 2 (XLSX, all 283 non-empty DEG rows)
were read in full.

## Verdict

This is a useful primary phenotype study of a systemic constitutive mouse knockout. Its
strongest result is assay- and region-bounded: at two weeks, PV-positive hippocampal
interneuron counts are reduced in DG and CA1 and across the whole hippocampus; NPY-positive
counts are significantly reduced only in DG. IBA1 and GFAP area fractions and reactive
morphology support hippocampal gliosis, while hippocampal GAD65/67 protein is lower.

The study does **not** directly measure GABA abundance or synthesis flux, synaptic inhibition,
the excitation/inhibition ratio, seizures, interneuron death, astrocyte proliferation, or the
causal direction between gliosis and epilepsy. Those stronger formulations in the current
graph must be narrowed. It also supplies no medication-specific safety rule.

## Experimental design

- Systemic constitutive knockout generated by crossing male `Wwox flox/wt` with female
  `BK5-Cre+; Wwox flox/wt` mice; persistence of oocyte Cre produces recombination in
  `flox/flox` embryos. Mixed 129SV/C57BL/6 background.
- Controls were `Cre+; Wwox wt/wt` littermates. No heterozygote or floxed Cre-negative control
  is described.
- Two-week mice of both sexes, reported as approximately 50:50, without sex-stratified
  analysis.
- `PREMISE IMPORTATA, NON VERIFICATA IN QUESTO DEEP DIVE`: the article says the model develops
  epileptogenesis and that all mice die at roughly three weeks, but attributes this to
  Ludes-Meyers 2009 (PMID 19936220, now `FT-043`) and Mallaret 2014. Those outcomes are not
  re-measured here and cannot carry a canonical rejection until the primary is read.
- Stereological PV/NPY counts: PV `n=7/group`; NPY `n=6/group`. IBA1 area fraction
  `n=6/group`; GFAP WT `n=6`, KO `n=8`; cytokine qPCR `n=4/group`; GAD Western `n=5/group`.
- RNA-seq used cultured, passaged hippocampal neurospheres derived from newborn mice, not the
  same two-week tissue: KO `n=2`, WT `n=3`. In-vivo hippocampal qPCR used `n=4/group`.
- Separate two-tailed unpaired t-tests were used across numerous regions and outcomes. No
  multiplicity correction, power calculation, randomisation or blinding is reported.
- No EEG, behavioural seizure endpoint, rescue, conditional deletion, lineage tracing,
  apoptosis assay, neurotransmitter assay or electrophysiology.

## What the study directly shows

### Interneuron-marker counts

- `DATO`: PV-positive stereological counts are lower in DG (`p<.005`), CA1 (`p<.05`) and the
  entire hippocampus (44%, `p<.005`). `OSSERVAZIONE DI FIGURA`: CA3 looks lower but carries
  no significance mark in Figure 1; this is absence of a reported mark, not equivalence.
- `DATO`: NPY-positive counts are lower in DG by 30% (`p<.05`); the text reports no obvious
  difference in CA1 or CA3. `OSSERVAZIONE DI FIGURA`: Figure 2D carries no significance mark
  for the whole-hippocampus comparison. This is absence of a reported mark, not proof of
  equivalence and not a textual report of a statistical test.
- `CORREZIONE`: the Results summary says both PV+ and NPY+ populations are reduced across
  “various subfields”, and the Discussion implies an overall reduction. The quantitative
  panels license a region-specific statement, especially for NPY.
- `UNRESOLVED`: marker-positive cell counts do not distinguish cell death from reduced marker
  expression, altered fate or delayed maturation. There is no pan-GABA lineage count,
  birthdating, fate mapping or apoptosis measurement.

### Glial readouts and cytokines

- `DATO`: IBA1 area fraction is higher in CA1, CA3 and the whole hippocampus, but only
  marginally in DG. Less-ramified cells with thicker processes and larger somata support a
  reactive morphology. The Figure 3 caption mistakenly calls IBA1-positive structures
  “astrocytes”.
- `DATO`: GFAP area fraction is higher in CA1, CA3 and the whole hippocampus, but only
  marginally in DG.
- `CORREZIONE`: area fraction is not a cell count and cannot separate hypertrophy from
  hyperplasia. The paper's assertion of both is stronger than the assay.
- `DATO`: both `Il6` and `Tnf-a` exceed 1.5-fold by qPCR, but only `Il6` is statistically
  significant. “Increased pro-inflammatory cytokines” in the plural hides this distinction.
- `UNRESOLVED`: reactive glia can be upstream, downstream or parallel to seizures and systemic
  illness. The authors explicitly leave cause versus consequence unresolved.

### GAD and functional inhibition

- `DATO`: combined hippocampal GAD65/67 Western signal is significantly lower (`n=5/group`).
- `INFERENZA`: lower GABA synthesis or abundance is imported from prior GAD literature. This
  paper measures neither GABA concentration nor synthesis flux.
- `NON MISURATO`: release, chloride gradient, inhibitory currents, E/I ratio, network activity
  and seizures. Functional inhibitory deficit belongs to other experiments, not this source.

### Transcriptomics

- `DATO`: newborn hippocampal neurospheres yielded 283 DEGs (184 up, 99 down) at the declared
  `|log2FC|>1`, `p<.005`, `FDR<.05` thresholds. The biological sample size appears only in
  Figure 6: KO `n=2`, WT `n=3`.
- `DATO`: the complete supplement contains changes in both directions: examples include
  downregulated `Kcna2`, `Grm3`, `Grin2b`, `Arx`, `Atp1a2`, `Grin2c` and `Wwox`, alongside
  upregulated `Gabbr2`, `Gabra2`, `Calb2` and `Htr3a`.
- `LIMITE / INFERENZA NON PROMOSSA`: with only two KO and three WT neurosphere samples, the
  dataset establishes neither the authors' coherent “epilepsy” program nor the opposite claim
  of a coherent mixed biological state. Bidirectional rows refute only a simplistic statement
  that every GABA-related transcript moves downward; they do not define an alternative state.
- `CORREZIONE`: IPA “neurological disease”, “epilepsy” and related categories are enrichment
  annotations, not functional validation or evidence that cultured progenitors seize.
- `CORREZIONE`: Table 1 selects 16 genes because they already occur in an epilepsy panel or
  the IPA epilepsy set. This is a targeted, partly circular shortlist, not an unbiased second
  discovery.
- `UNVERIFIABLE`: qPCR validation of all RNA-seq observations in neurospheres is reported as
  “data not shown”. In separate fresh hippocampi, only `Grin2c`, `Grm3` and `Kcna2` are shown
  as concordantly downregulated (`n=4/group`).

## Figure and supplement audit

- **Figure 1:** PV effect is marked significant in DG, CA1 and whole hippocampus; CA3 carries
  no significance marker. The whole-hippocampus 44% effect is not merely regional.
- **Figure 2:** only DG NPY carries a significance marker; the whole-hippocampus bars are
  similar and unmarked. The latter is a visual absence-of-marker observation, not equivalence.
- **Figure 3:** IBA1 area fraction and morphology support reactivity in CA1/CA3/whole, not DG;
  the caption contains the IBA1/astrocyte typo.
- **Figure 4:** GFAP CA1/CA3/whole effects; only `Il6`, not `Tnf-a`, has a significance mark.
- **Figure 5:** five lanes per group; absent WWOX and reduced combined GAD65/67 signal in KO.
- **Figure 6:** RNA-seq is only two KO versus three WT neurosphere samples; in-vivo qPCR is
  four versus four. The negative “relative linear FC” display is not explained by the usual
  non-negative `2^-ddCt` scale.
- **Supplementary Table 1:** 18 primer pairs; the `Il6` reverse-primer label contains `Fed-`,
  evidently a typographical error for `Fwd-`.
- **Supplementary Table 2:** all 283 DEGs checked; 184 up and 99 down, matching the paper.

## Transfer to the reference genotype

- **Genotype:** a systemic biallelic null is useful T2 evidence for conserved WWOX-loss
  vulnerability, but it is neither the reference splice-acceptor allele nor Q230P and cannot
  estimate residual function or splice correctability.
- **Stage/system:** the current paper's systemic design cannot isolate hippocampal cell
  autonomy or temporal order. Early death and epileptogenesis are imported from PMID 19936220
  and Mallaret 2014, not measured here; therefore the stronger claim that preceding seizures
  or terminal illness confound every endpoint remains a plausible `INFERENZA` pending FT-043,
  not an established premise of this read.
- **Clinical boundary:** the source tests no drug. It does not support “GABA forbidden”, a
  medication-class preference, or caution with “strong GABAergic burden” as a source-level
  clinical conclusion. Any such reasoning requires independent clinical and functional data
  and discussion with the treating clinical team.
