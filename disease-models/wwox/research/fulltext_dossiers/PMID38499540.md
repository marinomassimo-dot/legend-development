## 2 · What the paper establishes

**Design.** Mouse genetics (`K14-Cre` conditional alleles) plus human TNBC cell lines
(MDA-MB-231, BRCA1-WT; MDA-MB-436, BRCA1 `5396+1 G>A` splice-donor mutant) with a SeeSaw Reporter 2.0
NHEJ-vs-HDR readout and foci immunofluorescence (53BP1 = NHEJ surrogate, RAD51 = HDR surrogate,
γH2AX = DSB).

- **`DATO`** — Combined conditional loss of `Wwox` and `Brca1` in K14⁺ basal mammary epithelium
  produces basal-like mammary tumours; `Brca1` loss alone produces none in the observed window, and
  adding one `Trp53` null allele shortens median latency from **495 to 268 days** (Fig 1B).
- **`DATO`** — In normal mammary epithelium, genotypes lacking `Wwox` carry **fewer 53BP1 foci**
  (≈0.115 → ≈0.027 per nucleus, `**`/`***` between genotypes) and **more RAD51 foci**
  (≈0.014 → ≈0.051, `***`) than `Brca1`-only controls (Fig 2A, 2B, read at native resolution).
- **`DATO`** — In MDA-MB-231 (BRCA1-WT), WWOX overexpression **reduces RAD51 foci** (Fig 4C: `****`
  at cisplatin and at cisplatin+ATMi) and **raises 53BP1 foci** (Fig 5E/5F: `****`). The same
  contrasts in BRCA1-mutant MDA-MB-436 are weaker or absent.
- **`INFERENZA` (authors', and reported here as theirs)** — WWOX presence directs DSB repair toward
  NHEJ; its loss compromises NHEJ and raises RAD51-marked HDR, partly BRCA1-dependently.

**Group weighting.** Aqeilan laboratory: PubMed `Aqeilan RI[au]` = **137**, `AND WWOX` = **65**
(re-measured 2026-09-09, unchanged since 2026-08-10). **Primary group for the gene, NOT for the
disease** — this is mammary oncology, not WWOX-DEE. Field density `WWOX AND BRCA1` = **14 records in
all of PubMed**; `WWOX AND (NHEJ OR "homologous recombination" OR "DNA repair")` = **28**. Sparse
field: high weight on the mouse genetics, low transfer to the CNS phenotype.

**Retraction check** re-verified 2026-09-09 by PubMed esummary: `pubtype: ['Journal Article']` only —
no retraction, no expression of concern, **no erratum**. This confirms the orchestrator's wave-2
correction; `erratum_scope_check.py` has nothing to resolve here.

## 3 · What it does NOT support — the negative is the product

### 3.1 🔴 The SeeSaw experiment measures HDR at the floor, and its one significant mark is not supported by its own plotted values

Fig 3C prints its gate percentages **on the panels**:

| condition | GFP (NHEJ) | RFP (HDR) |
|---|---|---|
| 231 + EV | 4.28 % | **0.57 %** |
| 231 + WWOX OE | 10.43 % | **0.83 %** |
| 436 + EV | 11.32 % | **0.03 %** |
| 436 + WWOX OE | 8.86 % | **0.08 %** |

- **HDR sits at 0.03–0.83 % of events in every condition.** The paper's *"no significant difference in
  the HDR levels was found between the WWOX overexpressing cells and their control"* is therefore a
  null measured **at the floor of the assay**, not a demonstration that HDR is unchanged. This
  confirms the prior reading's observation exactly, at native resolution.
- 🔴 **The `**` on Fig 3D's MDA-MB-231 NHEJ comparison is not supported by the three values plotted.**
  Measured from marker centroids at 320 % against an axis calibrated on the printed ticks, and
  **cross-checked against the authors' own printed numbers** (Fig 3C's 4.28 % and 10.43 % reappear as
  plotted points 4.33 and 10.38 — an external anchor, the same standard applied in wave 2):
  - EV ≈ {9.37, 4.33, 4.28}, mean 6.0, SD 2.9
  - OE ≈ {11.08, 10.62, 10.43}, mean 10.7, SD 0.36
  - pooled two-sample *t*: t = 2.77, df 4 → **P ≈ 0.05**; Welch → **P ≈ 0.11**; paired → **P ≈ 0.09**.
  - **None reaches the P < 0.01 the caption asserts and the `**` denotes.**
  - Separately, the drawn error bar equals the sample **SD** (2.9), not the **SEM** (1.7) the caption
    declares.
  - `PREMISE: DATO` (the plotted marks and the printed gate values). Uncertainty: centroid reading
    carries roughly ±0.15 units; the conclusion is unchanged across that range and across all three
    test choices.

  **This matters because Fig 3 is the paper's only direct measurement of repair-pathway *outcome*.**
  Everything else is a focus-count surrogate. `REVIVAL_TRIGGER`: source data, or a stated pairing and
  test that yields P < 0.01 from these three replicates.

### 3.2 🔴 The running text reports a normal-vs-tumour elevation that all three of its own legends call not significant

Results: *"Mammary tumors … exhibited elevated number of 53BP1, RAD51 and γH2AX foci per nuclei
compared to their normal mammary (Fig. 2A, B and Supplementary Fig. 1)."*

The legends of **Fig 2A**, **Fig 2B** and **Sup Fig 2** each end: *"P value between normal and mammary
tumor- not significant."* The direction is as the text says; the significance is denied by the authors'
own legends. A non-significant trend is carried in the running text as a finding. **New — the prior
reading did not record this.**

Two further precisions, both obtained only by opening the supplement:

- The γH2AX quantification is **Supplementary Figure 2**, not Supplementary Figure 1. The sentence
  above cites *Supplementary Fig. 1* for it. **Supplementary Figure 2 is never cited anywhere in the
  running text** (three `Supplementary Fig` citations in the body, all to Fig 1). The one supplementary
  figure carrying an explicit null result is the one the article never points at.
- Sup Fig 2's legend prints the threshold as **`Pvalue>0.05`** where the main figures print
  `P value < 0.05` — the inequality is reversed. `INFERENZA` (well supported): a transcription defect,
  not a result statement, since the same legend then states the non-significance separately and the
  chart carries `**` and `***` marks.

### 3.3 🔴 The in vivo comparison is made in tissue the authors themselves say still expresses WWOX

The text attributes the Fig 2A/2B differences to *"the normal mammary tissue lacking Wwox"*, while the
same article states *"normal mammary epithelium of these two mouse models expressed WWOX in most
cells … only 5–30 % of mammary-gland epithelial cells express Cre"*.

**The panels confirm the mosaicism rather than the attribution.** Fig 1C's WWOX column shows strong
ductal WWOX in the DKO and TKO *normal mammary* rows; Sup Fig 1A shows BRCA1 retained in most cells of
`Brca1^fl/fl` normal mammary with arrowheads marking the scattered negative ones; Sup Fig 1B shows WWOX
present in normal ducts and absent in both tumour genotypes. So the foci counted in "normal mammary"
come from a population that is **mostly not recombined**. `panel_qualifies_text` — the marker belongs
to the panel locators (Fig 1C, Sup Fig 1A/1B), per `fulltext_read_receipt.md` open item 4. This is a
`MODEL_DEPENDENCE` / `CONTEXT_COLLAPSE` limitation the article does not reconcile.

### 3.4 🔴 The unit of statistical analysis is fields, not mice — disclosed only in a slide notes pane

`MOESM2_ESM.pptx`, `ppt/notesSlides/notesSlide1.xml`, the speaker-notes pane of the slide carrying
Supplementary Figure 2, carries an author working note in Hebrew, verbatim:

> `כל הttest נעשו על כל הfields ולא על כל עכבר`

— *"All the t-tests were done on all the fields and not per mouse."* (Codepoint-verified; the trailing
runs `10`, `20`, `um`, `1` are scale-bar fragments matching the legend's *"160X scale- 10 µm, 750X
scale- 20 µm"*.)

The published legends say only *"three 40× fields were quantified from each mouse. Statistical analysis
by t-test"* and declare mouse-level n (**3, 4, 3, 8**). They never state the unit of analysis. The
deposited note states it, and it is **fields, not animals** — i.e. pseudoreplication: the effective n
of the test is ~3× the number of mice, and the animals, not the fields, are the independent units.

- `DATO` — the note exists, verbatim, in the deposited MOESM2 notes pane on the Sup Fig 2 slide.
- `INFERENZA` — it describes the same procedure used for the **main** Fig 2A/2B, whose legends carry
  identical phrasing.
- **`UNRESOLVED_AMBIGUITY`, recorded and not resolved:** the note's *"all the t-tests"* is not scoped
  in the source. Whether it covers only Sup Fig 2 or every foci quantification in the paper is
  **not determinable from the deposit**, and choosing the wider reading because it is the more
  interesting one is exactly what §3.4 of `scientist_reading_modes.md` forbids.
- `REVIVAL_TRIGGER`: a methods statement, source data, or author correspondence fixing the unit of
  analysis for Fig 2A/2B.

This, taken with §3.2, is why the in vivo DSB-pathway result should be treated as **directional and
not as a quantified effect**.

### 3.5 🔴 Fig 1B does not show what the text says it shows

Read at 260 % of the native PNG:

- **The text says** *"K14-Cre;Brca1^fl/f female mice did not give rise to mammary tumors until 800 days

## 7 · Registered, measured, and deliberately NOT built — `.pptx` is not a verifiable text surface

`deepdive_manifest.py::_artifact_text` handles `.docx` (reads `word/document.xml`), `.xml`, `.html`,
`.txt`/`.md`. **It has no `.pptx` branch**, and for a `supplement_text` artifact of an unsupported type
it raises *"text verification is unsupported"*. `fulltext_receipts.LOCAL_ARTIFACT_SUFFIXES` and
`locator_audit.TEXT_SUFFIXES` likewise omit `.pptx`.

**Consequence, measured on this paper:** the single most consequential sentence in the whole reading —
the unit-of-analysis disclosure of §3.4 — **cannot be carried as a machine-verified text locator
today.** Declaring it `supplement` fails the validator; declaring it `figure` would be false, because
it is text. **3 of this article's 4 supplementary files are `.pptx`**, and the notes pane is a surface
no LEGEND tool currently reads at all.

**The fix is small and specific:** a `.pptx` branch in `_artifact_text` that runs
`_refuse_suspect_surface` on the raw part bytes and then flattens `ppt/slides/slideN.xml` and
`ppt/notesSlides/notesSlideN.xml` in numeric order, joining `<a:t>` runs within a paragraph with no
separator and paragraphs with one — the same inline/block law `_xml_surfaces` already uses, which is
what makes the Hebrew sentence above join into exactly the quoted form.

**It was not built, and the reason is not that it is hard.** The session was terminated mid-implementation
and the resume instruction was to land, not to start. A validator change written under a rate limit is
the kind that gets its tests written to match whatever it does — the same judgement recorded in wave 2
over the queue-ID cross-check, and the same conclusion. **The measurement is preserved here so the next
actor inherits the evidence and not a recollection of it.**
