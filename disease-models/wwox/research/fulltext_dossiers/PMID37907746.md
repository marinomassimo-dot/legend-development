## 1 · What the paper measures

Male db/db (leptin receptor deficient) mice versus m/m littermates at 24 weeks: hippocampal proteomics (TMT), RNA-seq, widely targeted metabolomics, sorbitol, fructose and ATP kits, immunofluorescence for KHK and GLUT5, TEM of microglial mitochondria, NOX4 localisation, ROS, Golgi spine analysis, CA1 patch clamp, Morris water maze; hippocampal AAV2/6 shRNA against Khk; primary microglia from Khk knockout and BV2 knockdown with fructose, conditioned-medium transfer to neurons. WWOX does not occur.

## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (screening-depth first pass)

1. Hippocampal fructose and sorbitol are higher and ATP lower in db/db mice; AR, KHK and GLUT5 are higher.
2. KHK and GLUT5 are located mainly in microglia by a public single-cell dataset and immunostaining.
3. In fructose-exposed microglia, KHK promotes NOX4 mitochondrial translocation, ROS and mitochondrial damage; Khk loss reduces them; conditioned medium from fructose-treated microglia lowers neuronal synaptic proteins unless Khk is knocked out or NOX4, mitochondrial ROS or ROS are inhibited.
4. In vivo hippocampal Khk knockdown (AAV shRNA, all cell types) raises ATP, spares peripheral metabolism, restores spine and synaptic protein measures, mEPSCs, and Morris water maze performance.
5. Methods state human-genome mapping for mouse RNA-seq; the DE threshold is fold change 1.2 and unadjusted p 0.05 (18,201 of 55,450 transcripts called).

## 3 · Comparison with the laboratory's records

- Not in the registries. Against Wang 2025 (same lot): two microglial fructose routes with different enzymes and opposite microglial states. Here endogenous, polyol-derived fructose is metabolised by KHK (not hexokinase) in activated, ROS-producing microglia of a hyperglycaemic adult; there exogenous fructose is metabolised through hexokinase to F6P in neonatal microglia that become hypo-phagocytic. The two papers do not agree on a single "fructose makes microglia X" rule.
- For WWOX: Wwox-null and hypomorph mice are hypoglycaemic (registry), so the db/db polyol-pathway fructose excess is a metabolic situation the model does not have; the paper is relevant only as a pathway checklist (aldose reductase, KHK, NOX4) for microglia if microglial activation is demonstrated in a WWOX model. No such demonstration is in this paper.

## 4 · What the paper does NOT support

- Any WWOX or developmental claim; adult male db/db only.
- Microglia-specific causality in vivo (the knockdown is not cell-type specific).
- Reliability of the omics (loose threshold, human-genome mapping statement).
