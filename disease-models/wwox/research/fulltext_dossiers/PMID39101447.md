## What the paper did

One child with DEE; seizure-panel NGS found a homozygous canonical donor variant at the exon 2 / intron 2 boundary;
copy-neutral LOH over most of chromosome 16 was read as uniparental disomy. A three-exon minigene (exon 1 - shortened
intron 1 - exon 2 - intron 2 - exon 3; Figure 8a) was transfected into HEK293T and the RT-PCR product sequenced.

## Genotype class and what was measured

- **Allele class × zygosity:** canonical splice donor × homozygous (isodisomy inferred from SNP-array LOH; no
  marker-based UPD test). Segregation figure: one parent heterozygous, the other wild type.
- **Measured (heterologous):** exon 2 skipping in the minigene — WT product 426 bp, mutant 361 bp; the 65 bp
  difference equals exon 2 (65 nt on NM_016373.4), which is **out-of-frame**. One band per lane on the published gel;
  no residual full-length product is visible in the mutant lane at that resolution; **nothing is quantified**.
- **Not measured:** patient RNA, NMD, any protein. "Protein truncation" is a Snapgene translation of the sequenced
  product.

## Phenotype axes

Focal seizures from day 19; tonic seizures at 5 months; spasms with tonic and myoclonic attacks at 8 months. LEV + PB,
then TPM, then vigabatrin added. "No apparent seizures" at 11 months, **but the 11-month VEEG still recorded focal
electrical attacks and spasms "not noticed at home by her parents"**; "no visible seizures" at the 13-month last
follow-up. Regression after 8 months; no head control; no fixation. MRI at 4 months: thin corpus callosum, widened
frontotemporal extracerebral space, irregular ventricles, periventricular microcysts. Atrial septal defect;
delayed right BAEP; peripheral nerve involvement on EMG.

## What the paper states and what it does NOT

- States: "vigabatrin can effectively treat seizures" (abstract) / "may be an effective management option".
- Does **not** show electrographic seizure freedom; follow-up ends at 13 months; vigabatrin was one of four drugs.
- No safety imaging is reported.
- Internal slips: Figure 4 caption "8 months" for the EEG the text dates to 11 months; "Riva et al. (20)" is cited but
  Riva is absent from the reference list (reference 20 is Shaukat 2018).

## Dedup

The same allele was reported once before in a compound-heterozygous child (He 2020, cited); different individual.
Count this child once.

## Transfer limits

A canonical donor allele skipping a 65 nt exon in a HEK293T minigene says nothing about the fraction of correct
splicing in patient neurons, nor about acceptor-site alleles elsewhere in the gene. The drug-response datum is n = 1,
predicted-null genotype, short follow-up.
