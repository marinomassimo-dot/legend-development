## Three arms, and what each actually measures

1. **`Wwox` genotype × aggregation — in fibroblasts.** `Wwox+/+`, `+/−` and `−/−` MEF, ± TGF-β1 or TNF-α, single blots with a "% increase in expression" row, no error bars, no n. **Figure 1B, the NFT panel, has no `Wwox+/+` lane** — it compares `+/−` with `−/−` — so the statement that NFT is elevated in the knockout has no wild-type comparator in the panel that shows it.
2. **MPP+ × TPC6AΔ — in a neuroblastoma line.** SK-N-SH, 62.5–250 µM MPP+, 24 h, non-reducing SDS-PAGE. The figure legend declares "Two individual repeats were performed for each experiment" and reports no statistics. This is the paper's claim that TPC6AΔ is "a common initiator for AD and PD"; it rests on dose-ordered band ladders in one immortalised line.
3. **Zfra4–10 in 3×Tg mice, and `Wwox+/−` behaviour.** Same non-WWOX model as PMID 29067327.

## Internal inconsistencies, measured

- **Text versus panel on the headline WWOX result.** Results 2.7 and the Conclusions assert pT12-WWOX aggregates in the `Wwox+/−` cortex but not in wild type. **Figure 7C's immunointensity chart reports p > 0.05.** The accompanying "# Plaques" chart shows three data points on the heterozygote bar at roughly 45, 22 and 1 — one of three animals has essentially no plaques — with a mean ≈ 22, an error bar reaching ≈ 45, and a `** p < 0.001` label at n = 3.
- **Dose schedule.** Methods 4.4: "once per week for 3 consecutive weeks"; Results 2.4: "four consecutive weeks".
- **Group sizes.** Methods: "five 10-month-old 3xTg mice"; Figure 3 legend: n = 10 and n = 10, naming the two groups "sham" and "control" — no Zfra group is named at all.
- **Figure 5 legend** states n = 5 in one sentence and n = 20 in the next for the same panels; **Figure 5B's learning-curve y-axis is labelled "% Exploration time"** while legend and text call it escape latency.
- The abstract says Zfra restores memory in **9-month-old** 3×Tg mice citing reference 5; reference 5 (PMID 29067327) treated **10-month-old** mice.

## What it adds that is new

The heterozygote arm is the most model-relevant content: `Wwox+/−` mice at 10–11 months show worse novel-object and water-maze performance than wild type (Fig 5A–B) and more hippocampal TPC6AΔ plaques (Fig 5C, ** p < 0.01). That is a **carrier-level** signal, measured, with an explicit text caveat that the heterozygote's other aggregates (pS37-TIAF1, TIAF1, wild-type TPC6A, pT181-tau) are **not** significantly increased — which the authors themselves attribute to the remaining functional allele.
