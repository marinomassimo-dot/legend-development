## What the paper did
Cross-sectional referral cohort, 2010–2025, 107 patients (106 families) with radiologically confirmed CCA; karyotype in all, FISH in 24, array-CGH (Agilent 4×44K, coordinates hg18) in 54, MLPA in one, WES in six unresolved patients. P/LP CNVs in 10/54 arrays.

## What the paper does NOT state
Any relationship with other Tunisian or North-African WWOX reports; the deletion's breakpoints at base resolution; the second WWOX allele; follow-up.

## Authors' caveats
Hospital-based referral bias; testing not uniform (array in 54, WES in 6); 15-year technology drift; incomplete segregation; no functional validation.
