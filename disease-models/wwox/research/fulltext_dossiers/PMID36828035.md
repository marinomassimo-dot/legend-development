## 3 · What the paper establishes

**Design.** CRISPR/Cas9 knock-in of the SCAR12 allele *Wwox* p.Pro47Thr (c.139C>A, c.141G>C,
exon 2), FVB/6N, one founder line, genotypes confirmed by Sanger in all three states
(Suppl. Fig. S1c). Cohorts: survival *n* = 14/30/25 (WT/het/hom); weight and blood *n* = 9/16/16;
histology *n* = 7/19/21; behaviour *n* = 19/17/16; imaging *n* = 3/group at 80 and 250 days;
RNA-seq *n* = 5/group per region; qRT-PCR *n* = 6/group; video-EEG *n* = 3/group; pull-down
*n* = 2/group.

1. **`DATO` — The mutation near-abolishes PPxY-peptide binding; transcript is unchanged and the
   protein arm is undetermined in direction.** 🔴 **Narrowed twice on 2026-09-13** — first by
   independent verification, then further by a blind locator audit that measured the panel (§ 6b).
   Dvl2 and Wbp1 oligopeptide pull-downs recover Wwox from wild-type cerebellum and retain only
   **faint residual** signal in P47T/P47T, so the loss is
   **near-total rather than literally complete**, and the evidence is **two PPPY peptides in an
   *in vitro* pull-down at *n* = 2/group**, not a motif class and not full-length partners. The
   transcript arm is now *seen*, not cited, and it is the quantified half: Suppl. Fig. S2a is `ns`
   at *n* = 6 (cerebellum, qRT-PCR) and Suppl. Fig. S9 shows comparable *Wwox* FPKM in **CB, CTX,
   PFC and HPC** at *n* = 5. **The protein half carries less than "normal protein":** on the 10%
   input lane Wwox is **present in both genotypes**, the two mutant bands are **thinner and lighter**
   than the two wild-type bands, and Anti-Hsp90 is even to slightly *stronger* in the mutant lanes —
   so the deficit is not a loading artefact, and at *n* = 2 with no densitometry anywhere in the
   paper, abundance is **neither shown unchanged nor shown reduced**.
   **The dissociation survives in the form that matters** — protein present, function near-lost —
   but "normal protein" was an overstatement and is withdrawn.
2. **`DATO` — Chronic spontaneous generalised convulsive epilepsy in the homozygote.** Video-EEG,
   bilateral frontal + parietal subdural electrodes, **14 days of recovery before recording**,
   simultaneous video: interictal spikes 0–3098/h against 0–68/h in WT, and 11–22 generalised
   convulsive seizures per 43–53 h with zero in WT. Bilateral synchrony is attested from Fig. 1d.
   **The denominator of every rate above is stated three ways by the source:** Methods 4.4 says
   monitoring was performed during *"24-hour sessions"*, Results 2.3 says *"prolonged periods
   (21–53 h)"* and then *"43–53 h"*, and Suppl. Fig. S3a plots a seizure **count** with no time
   unit at all. The per-hour spike ranges therefore carry a window the article does not fix.
3. **`DATO` — Survival is shortened, and "long-lived" needs its comparator.** 393 ± 32 d (hom) vs
   495 ± 23 (het) and 542 ± 8 (WT), log-rank *p* = 0.001. Weight 29.2 g vs 37.4 and 41.2.
4. **`DATO` — Progressive astrogliosis; microglial abundance never tested across age.**
   Gfap⁺ density and area fraction rise between 80 and 250 days **with a within-mutant
   significance bracket** (Fig. 4b–c) — the only genuinely demonstrated progression in the paper.
   Iba1⁺ *number* is similar at both ages (≈34 vs ≈33.5) and **no age comparison is drawn for
   Fig. 3b or 3c at all**, so its flatness is an untested comparison and not a demonstrated null;
   the widening area-fraction gap in 3c comes from a **wild-type decline**, not a mutant rise.
   Branching, junctions and total branch length do carry within-mutant age brackets (Fig. 3e–g),
   and wild type declines slightly there too, untested. The authors nonetheless title Fig. 3
   *"progressive microgliosis"*. **The honest statement: astrocyte-number progression is shown;
   microglial morphology carries the only age brackets in Fig. 3; microglial abundance was not
   tested across age.**
   🔴 **And the significance of every bracket in Figs. 3 and 4 is pseudoreplicated — § 5.** That
   weakens the astrogliosis progression this item keeps; it does not strengthen the microglial
   null, because an absent bracket stays an untested comparison either way.
5. **`DATO` — Cerebellar degeneration is early, severe and cell-type selective.** Purkinje number
   falls ~4-fold at 80 days; basket cells in the same sections are unchanged at both ages
   (Fig. 5d–e). Molecular and granular layer thinning is significant at both ages — and part of
   the widening gap is **normal wild-type growth** (ML: WT 190→222 µm, mutant flat 165→162).
   **Unit of analysis:** the layer-thickness brackets (Fig. 5g–h) are pseudoreplicated — 24
   measurements, 12 per animal, from *n* = 3 per genotype — while for the Purkinje and basket
   counts (Fig. 5d–e) the paper does not settle it; see § 5.
6. **`DATO` — Oligodendrocyte number falls in corpus callosum** (Olig2⁺ ≈122 vs ≈205 at 80 days,
   ≈117 vs ≈237 at 250 days, starred at both) **while myelination does not change** (Mbp area
   fraction `ns` at both ages, Suppl. Fig. S6c–d, *n* = 3). A cell-count deficit without a myelin
   deficit. No within-genotype age comparison is drawn, and the *relative* deficit **widens**
   because wild type rises — from ≈60% to ≈49% of WT — so "established early and static" is not
   what the figure shows; 80 days is simply the earliest age sampled. **Fig. 4e's asterisks are
   pseudoreplicated too** — *"a single independent region from n = 3 mice/group"* (§ 5).
7. **`DATO` — A region-wide inflammatory transcriptional programme.** 425/363/1508 DEGs in
   PFC/CTX/HPC, 208 common; BRETIGEA astrocyte and microglia signatures up in HPC and PFC;
   CREB1, TGFB1, TNF, NFκB, IL1A/B, RELA, P38 MAPK activated as upstream regulators in all three
   regions; TNFA/NFκB, IL6-JAK-STAT3 and INFLAMMATORY_RESPONSE enriched in all three.
   **The only direct cytokine measurement** is cerebellar *Il1b* and *Tnfa* qRT-PCR, ≥2-fold,
   *n* = 6 — and the mutant distributions overlap the wild-type range (Fig. 9d–e).
8. **`DATO` — The phenotype is not purely neurological.** Kidney and liver weight coefficients up;
   Suppl. Table 4 gives cholestasis 43% (hom) / 22% (het) / **0% (WT)** and nephropathy 52/72/14%.
9. **`DATO` — The knock-in is Mendelian and not embryo-lethal**: 18/45/19 observed vs 20.5/41/20.5
   expected across 82 mice from 12 litters (Suppl. Table 1). Blood chemistry and haematology are
   **uniformly `ns`** across all three genotypes (Suppl. Tables 2–3).
   🔴 **That negative is weaker than "unremarkable".** The Methods state mice were *"euthanized by
   CO2 asphyxiation at moribund stage"* with blood drawn immediately afterwards, so these are
   **terminal samples from animals in poor health**, not age-matched baselines; and two analytes are
   **off-scale in all three genotypes** — potassium *">8.48"*, *"> 9.38"*, *">9.22"* and creatinine
   *"< 0.24"*, *"< 0.36"*, *"< 0.31"*. An `ns` computed over censored values carries no information.

## 4 · What the paper does NOT support — the negatives, which are the product

- **Not epileptogenesis.** Adults, established phenotype, no longitudinal transition measured.
- **Not a null phenotype.** P47T is partial LoF with **full-length protein still produced** — which
  is a statement about presence, not about abundance (§ 3.1) — and an intact SDR domain; nothing
  here transfers to a WWOX-null genotype without an explicit argument.
- **Not blinded electrophysiology.** EEG was read by "two trained observers"; blinding is *not*
  declared. It **is** declared for the juvenile-social and elevated-plus-maze scoring — so the
  omission on EEG is a choice the paper makes visible by contrast.
- **Not powered.** *n* = 3/group for every imaging and EEG result, *n* = 2/group for the
  pull-down. No power analysis, no multiplicity correction across dozens of endpoints.
- **`ns` is not "no difference".** The heterozygote's survival reduction is non-significant with no
  power reported; the Mbp negative is `ns` at *n* = 3.
- **Not a measurement of inhibition.** The GABAergic finding is an *expression programme* in a
  KEGG enrichment. It must not be recruited to the E_GABA sign question — and see §5.
- **No drug, no rescue, no chloride or reversal-potential measurement.**
- **The heterozygote is NOT phenotype-free**, and two panels plus one table say so (§5).
- **An unverifiable negative stands:** the heterozygote Iba1/Gfap negative is "data not shown".

## 7 · Transfer verdict toward the reference genotype (WWOX-DEE class)

**`INFERENZA`, T2 with a named boundary.** This is a **missense, partial-loss-of-function**
genotype in which **full-length protein is present** and the enzymatic domain is intact. 🔴 **Protein
abundance itself is neither shown unchanged nor shown reduced** (§ 3.1), so abundance forms no part
of this verdict's basis. 🔴 An earlier wording of this sentence asserted that abundance was intact,
and that assertion is **withdrawn** — the panel it rested on shows protein present in both
genotypes on an unquantified *n* = 2 input lane whose mutant bands are weaker, which establishes
presence and settles nothing about quantity. (The withdrawn phrase is deliberately **not**
reproduced here: restating it verbatim is what kept it alive through the first repair.)
The reference genotype class
is DEE-severity WWOX dysfunction. What transfers is the **mechanism-independent** part: that
WWOX-dysfunctional brain mounts a progressive astroglial and morphological-microglial
neuroinflammatory response, loses Purkinje cells selectively, and loses oligodendrocyte number
without a myelin deficit. **The progression brackets are pseudoreplicated (§ 5), so what transfers
is the direction, never a demonstrated rate.** What does **not** transfer is severity, age of onset, survival, or any
quantitative endpoint — the paper's own framing is that P47T is *milder than* null, and its own
discussion attributes SCAR12-versus-WOREE severity to the *degree* of dysfunction.

**`PREMISE: DATO`** — abundance does not report activity. A claim of the form "WWOX protein is
present, therefore WWOX function is present" is refuted for this allele: protein is **present on the
input lane in both genotypes** while peptide recovery is near-abolished, and **transcript** is
unchanged in four regions (§ 3.1). 🔴 **The premise rests on protein PRESENCE and transcript
normality, not on equal protein abundance** — that last is what the 2026-09-13 audit withdrew, and
nothing here needs it.
**`PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK`** — *"gliosis means progressive gliosis"*. Fig. 3b–c is the
counter-instance: Iba1⁺ number is flat while morphology deteriorates. A research target, not a
foundation.
