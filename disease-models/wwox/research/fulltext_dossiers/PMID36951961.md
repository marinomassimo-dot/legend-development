## What the paper did

The full IND-directed preclinical package for intrathecal scAAV9/AP4M1 in SPG50, a recessive,
loss-of-function, childhood-onset neurological disorder:

- In vitro: scAAV2/AP4M1 in fibroblasts from five SPG50 patients with four different biallelic
  genotypes, tested independently in two laboratories at MOI 10^2–10^5.
- Efficacy: *Ap4m1*-KO mice injected IT at **P7–P10** (pre-manifesting) or **P90** (early
  manifesting) with 1.25 × 10^11, 2.5 × 10^11 or 5 × 10^11 vg/mouse; behaviour followed to
  17 months, survival to 20.5 months.
- Toxicology: non-GLP 1-year study in WT C57BL/6J mice; **GLP** 3-month study in WT Sprague-Dawley
  rats at 0.36 × 10^12, 1.1 × 10^12 or 3.3 × 10^12 vg/rat; **GLP** 3-month study in WT cynomolgus
  monkeys, 2/cohort, at **8.4 × 10^13 or 1.68 × 10^14 vg/NHP**.
- **The NHP arm was fully immunosuppressed**: i.v. methylprednisolone from day 1 to termination
  (10 mg/kg on day 1, then 1 mg/kg/day) **and** rapamycin 0.01 mg/kg twice daily from day −12 to
  termination.

## What it measured

ATG9A trafficking and AP4E1 levels by high-content imaging and western blot; AP4M1 mRNA by
RNAscope; IFN-γ ELISpot against AAV9 capsid and AP4M1 peptide pools; serum toxicology panels;
body weight and survival; hind-limb clasping, elevated plus maze, open field, rotarod, grip
strength, wire hang; GLP clinical pathology; **nerve conduction velocity and response amplitude**
(sural, peroneal, cauda equina) in NHP; vector DNA biodistribution and transgene expression; and
blinded histopathology including DRG at multiple levels.

## What it states

1. **Age and dose both matter for efficacy.** "clear age- and dose-dependent effects with early
   intervention and high dose achieving the best therapeutic benefits"; 2.5 × 10^11 vg was the
   minimally effective mouse dose, 1.25 × 10^11 gave no clear behavioural benefit, and even the
   highest dose with earliest treatment "did not completely normalize all behavioral phenotypes".
2. **DRG injury appeared in both rodent and primate, and the primate finding was universal.**
   Read cell-wise from the rendered **Table 1**: in the NHP study at day 91, minimal mononuclear
   cell infiltration in lumbar DRG was present in **100% of animals at every dose** — 1 male at
   1.68 × 10^14 (100%), 2 females at 8.40 × 10^13 (100%), 1 female at 1.68 × 10^14 (100%) — against
   0% in both vehicle animals. **Minimal neuronal degeneration** was 100% at 1.68 × 10^14 in both
   sexes and **0% at 8.40 × 10^13**. In rats, lumbar DRG minimal mononuclear infiltration reached
   40% (male) / 20% (female) at day 8 and 100% (male, 3.30 × 10^12) by day 8, with minimal-to-mild
   axonal and neuronal degeneration more frequent at days 29 and 91 than at day 8 and "a trending
   pattern toward resolution … by day 91".
3. **All of that happened under methylprednisolone plus rapamycin**, and the same regimen
   abolished the detectable T-cell response: "the AAV9/AP4M1 vector did not generate a detectable
   T cell immune response … **under the immunosuppressant protocol**".
4. **The histology had a functional correlate at the top dose only.** Axonal degeneration in
   sciatic, sural and tibial nerves at 1.68 × 10^14 "correlated with a decrease in NCV and response
   amplitude of the sural nerve noted on days 45 and 77", with no change in peroneal NCV or cauda
   equina latency — "consistent with dose-dependent damage to sensory but not motor neurons".
5. **The NOAELs.** Rat NOAEL 0.36 × 10^12 vg, set by DRG neuronal degeneration. NHP NOAEL
   8.4 × 10^13 vg. The human dose is reached by **CSF-volume scaling** with declared normative
   volumes — mouse 0.035 mL, rat 0.25 mL, NHP 12 mL, human 140 mL — giving HED 1.1 × 10^15 for the
   NHP NOAEL and 2.2 × 10^15 for the adverse NHP dose, i.e. the proposed clinical dose of
   **1 × 10^15 vg** sits about 2-fold below the highest dose tested.
6. **Rats can model this toxicity.** "similar degrees of minimal-to-mild toxicity in lumbar DRG
   were observed in both rat and NHP toxicity studies", which the authors offer as a reason NHP
   studies "may not be a requisite".
7. **The attribution the authors accept.** They cite a >200-NHP single-centre series finding
   minimal-to-mild DRG histopathology "consistently across most animals, independent of the
   transgene", with minimal correlation to behaviour or nerve conduction, and a follow-up in which
   "incorporation of a miRNA binding site specific to DRG resolved this histopathology, suggesting
   that high transgene expression was driving the toxicity in DRG".

## What it does NOT state

- Nothing about WWOX, nothing about any DEE.
- It does **not** show that immunosuppression reduced the DRG finding: there is no
  non-immunosuppressed NHP arm. The regimen is a constant of the study, not a variable.
- It does not report DRG histopathology in the *Ap4m1*-KO efficacy mice.
- The 1 × 10^15 vg human dose was **never administered to any animal**; it is an allometric
  construct from CSF volume, and the paper says the preclinical studies "provide safety data up to
  an approximately 2-fold overdose in the HED".
- It explicitly excludes cross-correction: "AP4M1 is not a secreted protein and … SPG50 disease is
  not thought to benefit from cross-correction."
- NHP n is 2 per cohort; the paper draws a dose–response from four dosed animals.

## Internal inconsistency found

The high NHP dose is written **1.64 × 10^14** in the Results and in the conclusion of that section,
and **1.68 × 10^14** in the Results paragraph on peripheral nerves and in the Discussion. The
rendered Table 1 prints **1.68 × 10^14**, which resolves the discrepancy in favour of 1.68 × 10^14.

## Caveats the authors themselves give

Two laboratories disagreed on fibroblast toxicity at MOI 10^5; incomplete rescue even at the best
dose and age; hepatocellular adenomas in 20% of high-dose male WT mice at 12 months, argued to be
within historical control rates; all efficacy and safety data are rodent and NHP.

## Transfer limit

Cynomolgus macaque (n = 2/cohort) and rodents, all wild type; cargo is a codon-optimised human
cDNA under a deliberately weak promoter (UsP, ~15% of CMV activity) in a self-complementary AAV9
delivered by lumbar intrathecal injection. SPG50 is recessive and loss-of-function like the
WWOX-DEE genotype class, which is the closest architectural match in this group; but AP-4
deficiency is slower and later in onset, so the **timing** argument does not transfer, and the
NOAELs are cassette- and promoter-specific. Nothing here is medical advice.
