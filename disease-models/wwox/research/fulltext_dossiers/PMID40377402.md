## What the paper is

A narrative review arguing that **up-regulated** Wnt signalling, not only its loss, appears in neurodevelopmental disorders (rare Mendelian NDDs, ASD, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, ADHD), neurodegeneration (AD, PD, HD, SCA1) and brain tumours, ending with Wnt inhibitors as a therapeutic prospect (LGK974, XAV939, pyrvinium, NSC668036, WAY-316606, sulindac, curcumin; Table 2).
