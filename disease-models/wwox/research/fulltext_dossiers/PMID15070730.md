## 2 · What the paper establishes — and it is a great deal

The **binding leg is the best-supported thing in the paper**, and nothing in this reading weakens it.

- **WWOX binds p73 through its first WW domain.** Co-IP in both directions (Fig 1A); **endogenous**
  interaction in etoposide-treated 293 cells (Fig 1B) and in MCF-7 (Fig 1F); direct binding by GST
  pull-down with full-length Wwox *and* with the isolated first 50 aa (Fig 1H).
- **The interaction is PPxY-dependent and specific.** p73β Y487A abolishes it (Fig 1D); p73γ, which
  naturally lacks `482PPPPY488`, does not bind (Fig 1E, 1I); **p53, which also lacks the motif, does
  not co-IP** (Fig 1C).
- **Y33 in WW1 is required for binding; Y61 in WW2 is not** (Fig 1G, Fig 1H).
- **Src phosphorylates Y33 directly.** GST-WI-WT and WI-Y34F are phosphorylated *in vitro*;
  WI-Y33F and WI-Y33,34F are not (Fig 4E).
- **Wwox suppresses p73 transactivation and the pair cooperates in apoptosis** (Fig 3A, 3B, 3C).

## 3 · What the supplement shows — the wave objective, and it weakens the routing leg

The 2026-08-14 receipt called Fig 5 *"the closest thing in the paper to a causal titration and the
strongest form of the routing evidence."* **Opened, it is the weakest form of it.**

### Fig 5 — the only graded-dose titration

- **No dose labels of any kind.** The amounts of Myc-Wwox the text says were varied appear
  **nowhere on the figure**.
- **No merged channel** — separate single channels `b1/b2`, `c1/c2`, the same absence already
  recorded for Fig 2A.
- **No cell count, no n, no statistic** — while the proposition it supports is explicitly about a
  **proportion** (*"some cells"*).
- **The asserted decrease in Wwox is not displayed.** The Wwox-positive **area** of the reduced-dose
  field is *not lower* than the higher-dose field. *(Only the area measure survived audit; see §5.)*
- ⚠️ **And nothing says which panel is the reduced dose.** Fig 5 has **no caption** in HTML or PDF —
  only the citing sentence's parenthetical *"b vs. c"*. `c = reduced` is the natural reading and it
  is **an assumption, not a datum**.

**What the panels do show, after correction:** the high-Wwox field `b2` contains **both** a solid
nuclear-pattern ellipse **and** a ring with a dark centre, side by side; the reduced-Wwox field `c2`
is **ring-dominated** — the nuclear pattern is hardest to find precisely in the field cited to show
*"retention of p73 in the nucleus."* The row-61 profile (`113 → 54 → 201`) reproduced **exactly**
under both auditors.

## 4 · 🔴 What the reading refuses to support — the finding that narrows `CLAIM 023`

`CLAIM 023`'s **title** asserts *"phosphorylation-**dependent** subcellular **rerouting**."*

**No experiment in this paper manipulates phosphorylation and measures localisation.** Fig 4 is
entirely binding and phospho-blot biochemistry; Fig 2 and Fig 5 are localisation **with no Src arm**.
Made machine-checkable: **20 sentences in the body mention Src; ZERO of them also mention
localisation, cytoplasm, nucleus, redistribution or sequestration** — confirmed by both auditors,
one running a ±400-character proximity screen whose only near-misses are adjacent sentences.

> **The claim's own SUMMARY is faithful** — it lists binding, phosphorylation-enhanced binding and
> relocalisation as three *separate* facts. **It is the TITLE that couples two of them into a
> relation the paper never tests.** And the title is the part that gets quoted.

Further limits on the routing leg:

- The authors declare it themselves: *"**Overexpression** of Wwox caused the redistribution of p73."*
- The enhancement of binding by phosphorylation is *"suggesting"* in Results and *"demonstrated"* in
  the Discussion — the modal dies **inside the paper**, four paragraphs apart.
- **Zero P-values anywhere in the body** (all spellings screened). Fig 3C does report
  *average ± SD from three experiments*; Fig 3A reports none.
- The Discussion credits **Fig 1G** with a Y34 binding result **Fig 1G does not contain**, and cites
  **"Fig. 3 D" twice** for a figure whose caption declares only (A), (B), (C).

## 6 · Premises and negatives

- 🔴 `PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK` — *"a co-IP band means a physiological interaction."* Every
  binding result here except Fig 1B and Fig 1F is overexpression. `REVIVAL_TRIGGER`: a stoichiometric
  or proximity-ligation measurement of the endogenous WWOX–p73 complex.
- `PREMISE: DATO` — *"an absent Src arm is not a tested null."* The zero-co-occurrence screen
  establishes that the experiment was **not done**, never that phosphorylation does *not* affect
  routing. `REVIVAL_TRIGGER`: any localisation experiment with a Src or DN-Src arm settles it in
  either direction.
- **Negative refused rather than recorded:** *"phosphorylation does not drive rerouting"* is **not**
  entered. Entering it would manufacture exactly the silent false negative the discipline exists to
  prevent. What is entered is that **the relation is untested here**.
- ⚠️ The Y33 rationale is inherited from **PMID 12514174** (Chang et al., murine Wox1/JNK), which
  this corpus does not hold. `PREMISE: DATO` in the source — a `DATO` we have never verified.
