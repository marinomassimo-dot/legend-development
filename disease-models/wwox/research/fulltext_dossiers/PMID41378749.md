## What the paper did

Case–control GWAS of spina bifida (myelomeningocele 82%, meningocele 18%) in Bangladeshi infants under one year.
Array genotypes (Illumina GSA, saliva) on 228 infants; after QC and covariate availability the models use **89
cases and 97 controls** (the abstract says 91/97, which is the folate-available count before arsenic). Imputation
on the TOPMed server (R² ≥ 0.7, MAF ≥ 1%, 7.15 million variants). Logistic-Firth regression with maternal age,
infant sex, 10 PCs; models adding maternal folate intake and toenail arsenic as covariates and interaction terms.
λGC 1.08–1.22; LDSC intercept 1.03–1.05. **Multiple testing:** only a suggestive threshold, p = 1e-5. Technical
replication: re-imputation with the 1000 Genomes South Asian panel.

## What it measured about WWOX

- **Three imputed SNPs in WWOX intron 8** (Results: "within the 8th intron of WWOX"), chr16 79.010 Mb (GRCh38):
  rs28688166 (lead; EA C, AF 0.22, OR 6.20, p 2.22e-6, imputation R² 0.78), rs7185147 (OR 6.15, p 3.09e-6),
  rs7184417 (OR 6.22, p 3.42e-6). Stable when folate and arsenic are added (Tables S3–S5).
- **"Coding region" in the abstract means gene body.** The variants are intronic.
- **Abstract–results mismatch:** the abstract names **rs7184417** with OR 6.20 and p 2.22E-06, which are
  **rs28688166's** statistics (Table S2). A copying error, not a different finding; but any record citing the
  abstract's rsID with those numbers is citing a pair that does not exist in the paper.
- **Not replicated:** the technical replication reproduced CNTN5 and CTNNA2 only; WWOX is not among the replicated
  loci (Results; Supplemental Figure 9 inspected — no WWOX label above threshold).
- **Signal shape:** Supplemental Figure 7A (inspected): a narrow three-SNP spike with few supporting variants in LD.
  Figure 1 Q–Q plots (inspected): observed tail at or below the null line — no excess signal.
- **Confounding the authors list or show:** cases were seen much younger than controls (44 vs 175 days), differ in
  parity and maternal education (Table 1); folate and arsenic are single-timepoint proxies, not periconceptional.
- Nothing functional: no expression, eQTL or mechanism; the Discussion paragraph on WWOX (developing spinal cord
  expression, Chen 2004; WW/SDR domains) is literature.

## What the authors state

"Our results, while only nominal associations"; "Future studies can replicate this hypothesis-generating analysis
in a larger cohort"; limitations: small cohort for high-dimensional GWAS, single-timepoint exposures, possible
unmeasured confounding.

## What the paper does NOT say

No genome-wide significance, no replication of WWOX, no exonic variant, no link to WWOX dosage, no neurological
phenotype beyond the neural tube defect.

## Transfer limit to the WWOX-DEE genotype class

T4. A nominal, imputed, common intronic association with a structural birth defect in a different organ-system
window carries nothing to the genotype class. Neural tube defects are not part of the WWOX-DEE phenotype in any
record I read here.

## Verdict

**INGEST as an earned null.** WWOX appears as a nominal, unreplicated locus with a mis-assigned rsID in the abstract.
