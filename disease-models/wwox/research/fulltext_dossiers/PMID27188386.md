## What the paper did
Two H9-derived systems: UKK (embryoid bodies, 14 days, three germ layers; chemical present throughout) and UKN1 (dual-SMAD neural induction, chemical day of differentiation 0 to 6). Genome-wide data for 12 test substances (six HDAC inhibitors incl. valproate, six mercurials) at the maximum non-cytotoxic concentration, plus valproate and thalidomide over wide concentration ranges (valproate in UKN1: 350-1000 uM). "Developmental genes" (spontaneously regulated during differentiation, fold change above 5) define indices: developmental potency (fraction of developmental genes altered) and developmental index. Main statement: compounds usually antagonise spontaneous development; more than half of developmental genes are compromised at 800 and 1000 uM valproate.

## WWOX content (class level; no allele, no patient)
- WWOX appears **zero times in the running text**. Supplementary Tables 1, 3 and 7 carry it (Tables 3 and 5 by probe-set id).
- Sign convention: Table 3 carries signed fold change; the PAX6 sentinel is -10.56 in the UKN1 VPA block (down), so negative = down and positive = up.
- VPA at maximum non-cytotoxic concentration: UKK, WWOX probe 219077_s_at **+2.127** (p 5.4e-16); UKN1, probe 223868_s_at **+1.683** (p 8.5e-5). Both **up**. The UKN1 value equals the 1.68 in the sibling classifier paper (PMID 26272509), i.e. it is the same measurement reused, not a second one.
- Other HDAC inhibitors: entinostat up in both systems (UKK 219077_s_at +1.565 and 223868_s_at +1.736; UKN1 219077_s_at +1.513 and 223868_s_at +1.505); SAHA up in UKK (223868_s_at +1.526). No WWOX probe set is negative in any Table 3 block; no mercurial block contains one.
- Supplementary Table 7 (UKN1, VPA at 350, 450, 550, 800, 1000 uM; fold change at least 2 in absolute value, FDR below 0.05): probe 219077_s_at sits in the 1000 uM column with the symbol WWOX; **no fold change and no direction** are given. From the up-direction at 600 uM in the sibling paper, up at 1000 uM is INFERENZA, not shown here.
- Table 1 (untreated cells): WWOX probe 221147_x_at is a developmental probe set in UKN1, +2.408 on day 6 versus day 0 (PAX6 +39.4), i.e. WWOX rises during normal neural induction; it is not a developmental probe set in the UKK block. The paper's general rule (compounds antagonise development) is NOT applied to WWOX here: the probe sets differ and no WWOX statement exists.
