## 1 · What the paper measures

Aphidicolin-sensitive fragile sites in three independent human iPSC lines by cytogenetic breaks with Monte Carlo correction and MiDAS mapping: 28 sites, a Major cluster of 8 (59 percent of breaks) and a Minor cluster of 20; five universal sites; comparison with lymphocyte and fibroblast maps.

## 2 · Findings relevant to WWOX (first pass; rest not read)

1. FRA16D (the fragile site inside WWOX): 18 breaks, Minor cluster, line-specific to one of three lines; in lymphocytes and fibroblasts it is among the most active sites. FRA3B likewise modest.
2. WWOX is named with FHIT, MACROD2 and PARK2 among tumour-suppressor genes at fragile loci that are not themselves recurrently rearranged in iPSC cultures; the authors flag potential instability for neural differentiation and long culture.
3. The activity of the 28 sites varies considerably among the three lines.

## 3 · What it changes for WWOX iPSC and organoid models

It lowers one worry and leaves another: FRA16D is not a dominant or recurrently rearranged iPSC fragile site in these three lines, so a WWOX-locus rearrangement is not a default hazard of culture, but it is line-specific (one of three) and the lines are control lines, so a patient-derived or edited line must be karyotyped at the locus and not assumed stable. No neural derivative, patient line or expression measurement was studied.
