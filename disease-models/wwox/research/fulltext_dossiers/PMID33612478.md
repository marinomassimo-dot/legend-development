## 1 · What the paper did

A candidate-SNP association study in one ethnic minority population of southern China: **864
normolipidaemic vs 1129 dyslipidaemic** unrelated adults, four SNPs genotyped — two in *TUBB*
(rs3132584, rs3130685) and two assigned to *WWOX* (rs2222896, rs2548861) — plus haplotypes,
gene–gene and gene–environment interaction models by GMDR, and a hypertension analysis.

## 2 · The WWOX content, at class level

- rs2222896 G carriers in the normal group have higher LDL-C and lower HDL-C and ApoA1; rs2222896
  genotype is associated with dyslipidaemia (AA vs GG, adjusted OR 0.18, 95 % CI 0.14–0.23).
- Two-SNP haplotypes across the WWOX pair separate into risk (G-G, G-T) and protective (A-T, A-G)
  sets, with one haplotype–disease OR of **15.08 (95 % CI 9.24–24.60)** — an implausibly large
  effect beside the per-genotype ORs, in the same data.
- The SNPs are **common by construction**: "Only SNPs with MAF greater than 10 % were included."

## 3 · Three structural reasons this bears almost nothing on WWOX function

1. **The two "WWOX" SNPs are ~500 kb apart and in weak LD** — stated by the authors themselves
   ("about 500 kb from each other, resulting in a weak LD between them"). A haplotype across that
   distance is not one allele of one gene. The positions they print (78 058 601 and 78 624 496)
   also place rs2222896 **outside** the WWOX gene body as that body is given in this wave's other
   source (chr16:78 133 551–79 246 564, supplemental Table S2 of PMID 35460704, same build) —
   roughly 75 kb 5′ of the gene.
2. **The authors withdraw the one WWOX association that mattered.** The Young Finns rs2548861 ↔
   HDL-C result fails to replicate in the normal group (P = 0.597) and appears only in the
   dyslipidaemic group (P = 0.001), which they attribute to non-genetic factors: *"we speculated
   that mutations at rs2548861 were not correlated with HDL-C concentration in these subjects."*
3. **Nothing is perturbed and nothing of WWOX is measured** — no transcript, no protein, no
   function. The authors list functional work among the study's own limitations.

Multiplicity: fourteen "optimal" interaction models over four SNPs plus environment, selected by
GMDR on the same dataset, reported alongside the primary tests.

## 4 · Internal inconsistencies found

- The abstract states the rs3132584 × rs2222896 interaction "increased the risk of dyslipidemia
  2.548-fold and predicted hypertension"; the Results give **1.523-fold for hypertension**, with
  2.548 belonging to a genotype-combination OR for dyslipidaemia.
- Table 1 prints the normal group's weight as `54.48±110.29` kg — an SD twice the mean.

## 5 · What it does NOT state

Nothing about neurological phenotype, rare variants, carriers of damaging alleles, or WWOX dose in
any tissue. `TUBB` is an unrelated chromosome-6 gene here, not a WWOX neighbour.

## 7 · Verdict

**OFF-AXIS, with the stub upgraded rather than dropped.** The reading's value is a recorded
negative: this is a common-variant association in one population whose own authors retract the
WWOX–HDL-C link, and it must not be cited in support of WWOX dose effects on lipids.
