## Scientific verdict

The review integrates mouse-null systemic metabolism, steroidogenesis, bone/RUNX2, HDL, glucose/HIF1
and Drosophila ROS literature. It usefully limits a common overclaim: proposed SDR dehydrogenase
activity came from crude bacterial extracts, and no authenticated physiological substrate was known.
Its HIF1alpha/glucose account is a secondary synthesis of PMID 25012504, not independent support.

Two transmission defects must travel with the record. First, the review says that depleting HIF1alpha
was hypothesised to *increase* glucose uptake and then says inhibition reversed the elevated phenotype;
the stated directions are internally incoherent. Second, it describes “overexpression of mutated
WWOX” in the cited Drosophila ROS work, although that wording requires primary-source verification and
must not be promoted as a new experimental arm. The review is valuable as a map of claims and debts,
not as a substitute for those primary papers.
