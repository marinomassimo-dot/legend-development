## Why it was re-read

To find out whether this review maps the WWOX phospho-code onto the organelle endpoints that PMID 41677633
and PMID 21212468 measure — i.e. whether "WWOX present" can be told from "WWOX functional" at the level of a
measurable organelle readout.

## What the phospho-code maps to, as this review states it

| Residue | What the review maps it to | Organelle endpoint? |
|---|---|---|
| pY33 | activation under UV, MPP+, light damage, nerve dissection, TBI; relocation to mitochondria and nuclei; binding pS46-p53; apoptosis; bubbling cell death | **compartment and cell fate, not a measured organelle readout** |
| pS14 | cell differentiation (leukaemia lines), and accumulation in 3xTg-AD brain lesions where pY33 does not rise; "supports the progression of neurodegeneration" | no |
| pT12 | **absent from this review** (it appears in the group's 2024 review, PMID 38542478) | — |
| pY287 | named only as the Ack1 site driving proteasomal WWOX degradation | no |

**Answer to the question it was re-read for: the mapping does not exist at endpoint level in this source.**
No residue in this article is tied to a measured mitochondrial membrane potential, ROS value, lysosomal
assay or autophagic flux. The lysosome appears exactly once, in an uncited list of compartments —
*"WWOX localizes in many subcellular compartments, including cell membrane, mitochondrion, lysosome,
nucleus, and others."*

## What it does add

- One loss-of-function direction stated for the constitutive null, in the death modality the review is built
  around: *"If cells are devoid of WWOX (e.g., Wwox-/- MEF), cell death is retarded"* — the same sign as the
  viability result of PMID 41677633 under a different stress.
- The ROS link is attributed to the C-terminal SDR domain and carried from two laboratories that are not the
  authoring group (Drosophila and mammalian cells), including the observation that WWOX expression tracks
  oxidative phosphorylation and falls under glycolytic/Warburg metabolism.
- Original analysis of its own (not a reprint): Allen Brain Atlas expression across brain regions in six
  postmortem normal brains (hippocampus low relative to pons and white matter), with the per-region table
  deposited as Supplementary Table 1.
- A proteostasis framing: WWOX as a candidate chaperone, and the TRAPPC6AΔ → TIAF1 → tau/Aβ aggregation
  cascade controlled by WWOX, which is where this review meets PMID 21368882.

## What it does NOT state

- No measurement of its own except the Atlas analysis; every mechanistic statement is cited.
- No autophagy anywhere: the word appears once, in a generic list of proteostasis machineries in the ageing
  section, never applied to WWOX.
- No human WWOX genotype class is given an organelle phenotype; the human clinical material is the published
  recessive cohort description and the Alzheimer hippocampal downregulation (~32 ± 5 % reduction, cited to
  the group's 2004 paper).
