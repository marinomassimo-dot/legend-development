## What the paper did
Retrospective cohort, 2019–2023, 140 children with epilepsy (multigene panel n = 116, WES n = 30, WGS n = 3; all tests included CNV/exon-level deletion and duplication calling by the commercial laboratory). Diagnostic yield 40/140 (28.6 %). Clinical impact scored in four categories.

## WWOX content (class level) — the body understates it; the supplement carries the patient
- **Body:** WWOX is listed among 15 "primary epilepsy genes"; "WWOX coding an enzyme"; and among the eight CNV/indel findings: "one indel/copy number gain of exon 5 of the WWOX gene". Table 2 lists WWOX under **Drug impact**.
- **Supplement (Causative_variants sheet, one row):** WWOX, NM_016373.3, "copy number = 3 exon 5/Intron 6, c.605+1_605+2delinsAA", ACMG "likely pathogenic/likely pathogenic", consequence "copy number gain/splice site", state **"compund-heterozygous"**, AR, diagnosis "Developmental and epileptic encephalopathy WWOX", onset 0.83 years, epilepsy type **FE** (focal epilepsy), observed phenotype "Hypotonia", 1 ASM, seizure-frequency code 0 (less than one seizure per year), evolution code 2 (stationary), population databases "no", reported in literature "no", segregation **"trans variants"**.
- **Supplement (CNV&indels sheet):** same patient: test "Invitae Panel", copy number 3, array **"no"**, segregation "Inherited (trans variants)". **The exon-5 gain was not confirmed by SNP array or array-CGH**: the body's "For three of the previously mentioned eight children, a SNParray or array CGH was performed" refers to three other rows (the 22q13.3, 16p12.2p11.2 and NRXN1 rows carry array results; WWOX carries "no").
- **Supplement (Impact sheet):** WWOX row: drug impact 1, ketogenic diet 0, clinical trial 0, multidisciplinary 0, genetic counselling 1. What the drug impact was is not stated.
- **So the WWOX patient is one child, compound heterozygous: a panel-called single-exon gain (exon 5) in trans with an intron-6 donor delins (c.605+1_605+2delinsAA).** Both alleles' consequences are **predicted, not measured**: no RNA, no breakpoint, no orientation (tandem vs elsewhere) for the gain. INFERENZA from RefSeq NM_016373.4 (exon 5 = c.410–516, 107 nt; exon 6 = c.517–605, 89 nt): a tandem in-transcript duplication of exon 5 would be out of frame, and skipping exon 6 would be out of frame — both predictions only.

## What the paper does NOT state
Sex of this patient; MRI; EEG; which drug changed and how; outcome beyond "stationary"; whether the gain was orthogonally confirmed (the CNV sheet says no array was done).

## Authors' caveats
Small sample; selection bias over an evolving testing era; segregation not done in all; impact assessed only in diagnosed patients; missing data.
