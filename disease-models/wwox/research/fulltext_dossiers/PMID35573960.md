## What the paper did

One child; trio exome (SNV + CNV); because the clinical suspicion stayed high after a single heterozygous stop, the
group ran RT-PCR on **patient fibroblast RNA** across exons 4-7, then qPCR and array-CGH.

## Genotype class and what was measured

- Stop `c.790C>T` (p.Arg264*, exon 7) from one parent; an **84.8 kb deletion removing exon 6 only** (checked against
  GRCh37 exon coordinates: exons 5 and 7 lie outside it) from the other. Class: predicted null / out-of-frame exon
  skip (89 nt).
- **Measured:** the deletion allele's transcript joins exon 5 to exon 7 in fibroblasts (Figure 1C-D). On inspection of
  Figure 1C the child's lane also carries a full-length-sized product, which can only come from the stop allele
  (exon 6 is absent from the other) — the stop allele's mRNA is detectable; nothing is quantified and NMD was not
  tested. **No protein.**
- **Exome CNV analysis missed the single-exon deletion** ("CNVs analysis was also unremarkable").

## Phenotype axes

Onset day 1 (tonic seizures with hypoglycaemia and respiratory distress); drug-resistant to valproate, vigabatrin,
clonazepam, clobazam, levetiracetam, rufinamide and CBD oil; phenobarbital and nitrazepam **stopped for adverse events**
(not reported as ineffective); three ACTH cycles "very poor"; ketogenic diet without significant effect; vigabatrin is
nonetheless among the current medications. Up to 50 seizures/day; no milestones; gastrostomy at 3 years; alive at the
last hospitalisation (> 3 y). MRI: periventricular cysts and **small inferior vermis** in the first week; delayed
myelination and MRS lactate at 4 months; at 2 y 4 m a posterior periventricular leukomalacia-like pattern, pons and
dentate signal change, no lactate (Figures 2-3 inspected).

## What the paper states and what it does NOT

- The "peculiar clinic-radiological pattern" is offered from one patient; the authors themselves ask for more studies
  to see whether the leukomalacia-like pattern is typical. No discriminant test is attempted.
- "Earliest epilepsy onset, presenting on the first day of life" — Piard 2019 S1 already records day-1 onset in three
  patients.
- Supplementary Table 1 lists Tarta-Arsene 2017 (one patient) and Piard 2019 (20 patients) as separate columns; see the
  Piard dossier on a possible double publication.
