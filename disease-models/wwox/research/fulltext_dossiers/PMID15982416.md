## 2 · The reagent question — answered for this paper

### 2.1 · What the antibody is, per this paper

An **affinity-purified rabbit polyclonal**, made by the authors' own laboratory, used at **280 ng/ml**
for immunoblot. Its provenance is a citation: *«anti-WWOX rabbit polyclonal primary antibodies
developed in our laboratory (final concentration 280 ng/ml) [12]»* — and **[12] is PMID 15692750**,
the second paper of this same lot. The immunostaining protocol is also not described here:
*«Anti-WWOX immunostaining was performed as previously reported [12].»* The automated scoring is
likewise *«as previously described [12]»*.

### 2.2 · Controls PRESENT

| Control | Present? | What exactly |
|---|---|---|
| **Genetic-null negative control (immunoblot)** | ✅ **yes, and it is the strongest thing here** | PEO1, an ovarian line carrying a **homozygous deletion of exons 4–8**, run as negative control; *«no immunoreactive products»*. Figure 1A, inspected: the null lane is blank, the transfectant lane carries one discrete band, actin present in both. |
| **Overexpression / add-back positive control** | ✅ yes | the same PEO1 line stably transfected with a WWOX expression vector — loss and gain of signal on one blot. |
| **Immunogen pre-absorption (IHC)** | ⚠️ **asserted, not shown** | *«immunoreactivity was abolished by pre-absortion to the recombinant GST-WWOX fusion protein used to raise the antibody (data not shown)»*. No panel, in the paper or in any deposited file. |
| **Loading control** | ✅ yes | actin, on both blots. |
| **Within-field internal negative (IHC)** | ✅ yes, but it is not a reagent control | unstained stroma beside stained epithelium in Figures 2–3, and genuinely blank tumour fields with intact haematoxylin (3F, 3H, 3I, 3K, 3L). This is contrast within a field, not a control on the antibody. |

### 2.3 · Controls ABSENT

- **No no-primary control** anywhere.
- **No pre-immune serum and no isotype control.**
- **No peptide-block panel** — the pre-absorption is the fusion protein, and it is *«data not shown»*.
- **No second, independent anti-WWOX antibody** on the same material.
- **No knockdown or dose–response correspondence** in tissue (the null is a cell line, not a tissue).
- **No molecular-weight ladder or marker lane anywhere in Figure 1** — inspected at 292×710 native and
  at 8× on the 2250×1500 composite. So the panel cannot establish that the band is the 46 kDa
  full-length species; the size identity rests on the text alone.
- **No blot-to-IHC correspondence on the same specimens.** 38 tumours were blotted and 444 were
  stained; nothing states that the 38 are among the 444, and no case-level comparison is shown.
- **No explicit statement of specificity for the IHC reagent lot** beyond the cited method.

### 2.4 · An internal tension in the paper's own account of its immunogen

Two sentences describe the immunogen differently and neither is measured here:

- Results: *«The anti-WWOX antibody used was raised against **the WW domains** [2]»*
- Results, three paragraphs later: *«pre-absortion to the recombinant **GST-WWOX fusion protein** used to raise the antibody»*

These are compatible (a GST fusion of the WW-domain region), but the paper does not say so, and the
only way to settle which construct was the immunogen is to read **ref [2] = PMID 14526170**, which has
no receipt in this repository. `IPOTESI` that they are the same construct — not `DATO`, and not
resolved here. `PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK` if anyone treats "GST-WWOX" as necessarily
WW-domain-restricted: a GST fusion may carry any part of the protein.

### 2.5 · The standard the validation actually reaches

**For immunoblot, on ovarian carcinoma and normal ovary lysates: a genuine loss-of-signal /
gain-of-signal control on a genetic null.** That is the strongest class of specificity evidence short
of mass spectrometry, and this paper really has it. Two qualifications, both from the artefacts:
the null is a **cell line**, not the tissue the antibody is then used on; and with **no size ladder**
the panel shows *a* band that appears and disappears with WWOX, not *the* 46 kDa band.

**For immunohistochemistry: the standard is an unshown pre-absorption.** One sentence, no panel,
`data not shown`, in a 2005 paper. On the published evidence, the IHC reagent's specificity in this
paper is **asserted with a named control that cannot be inspected**, and everything else IHC-side is
consistency argument: pattern plausibility, unstained stroma, blank tumour fields.

### 2.6 · 🔴 What this validation does NOT license

- **It does not license CNS immunohistochemistry.** Every control here is on ovarian epithelium,
  ovarian carcinoma lysate, or an ovarian carcinoma cell line. Nothing in this paper touches brain,
  neurons, glia or any neural tissue. An antibody whose specificity is demonstrated on a
  WWOX-null **ovarian line** and asserted on **ovarian sections** is not thereby validated for
  staining in brain — where the signal is judged by cell shape with no co-marker, cross-reactivity
  has a different partner set, and the null control does not exist in the tissue.
- **It does not license a claim about isoforms.** *«should detect all potential WWOX isoforms»* is an
  expectation from the immunogen, tagged by the authors with *should*. With no ladder and no isoform
  standards, the panels cannot support it.
- **It does not license subcellular-compartment conclusions as reagent-independent.** The paper's own
  Discussion, facing the opposite result of Watanabe 2003 (nuclear staining, ref 27 = PMID 14695174),
  offers *«Possibly this discrepancy is due to the use of different antibodies.»* That is the paper
  conceding that its compartment result is reagent-dependent, and it is an `AUTHOR_INTERPRETATION`,
  not an adjudication.
- **It does not license transfer of the 46 kDa identity** to any other reading, for the ladder reason
  above.

## 3 · What the paper establishes in its own right

`DATO` unless marked otherwise.

1. **Immunoblot, 38 ovarian carcinomas vs 5 normal ovaries.** *«26% (10/38) of tumors over-expressed
   WWOX, 34% (13/38) expressed normal WWOX levels and 40% (15/38) of tumors had levels lower than 50%
   that observed in normal ovary.»* Full-length WWOX is the predominant species detected.
2. **IHC, 444 pooled invasive epithelial ovarian carcinomas** from two institutions' TMAs (MD Anderson
   n≈441-sourced series; Fox Chase mucinous-enriched set of 86 tumours + 21 normals), scored in
   duplicate: **30% (133/444) negative/low, 70% (311/444) moderate/strong.**
3. **Histotype variation**, with the significance statement attached to the smallest group: mucinous
   **7/10 (70%)** negative/low, clear cell **8/19 (42%)**, serous 109/375 (29%), endometrioid 9/40
   (23%); footnote *«(χ2 = 8.67; p = 0.0131)»*.
4. **Normal ovary compartment**: strong cytoplasmic signal in ovarian surface epithelium and in
   cortical inclusion-cyst epithelium; **no nuclear staining in any tissue, normal or tumour**.
5. **Stage, not grade**: negative/low WWOX rises 23% → 29% → 31% → 48% across FIGO I–IV, *«p trend =
   0.007»*; grade (GOG) *«p = 0.707»*.
6. **Overall survival**: LOW vs HIGH Kaplan–Meier, **p = 0.03**, in the MD Anderson subset.
7. **Receptors**: ER **null** (25% low among ER-negative vs 25% among ER-positive, p = 0.984);
   PR associated (p = 0.008 in text and abstract).
8. **Scoring rule**: ACIS automated intensity, positive/negative cutoff **63 s.i.u. of 255**, with the
   analysis dichotomy at 65; normal OSE itself means **70 ± 4** — five units above the cutoff.

## 4 · What the paper does NOT establish — the product, not the residue

- **Not a multivariable outcome result.** *«Univariate analysis … by χ2 test with Yates' correction»*,
  and Figure 4 inspected at native resolution contains **no numbers at risk, no censoring marks, no
  confidence bands, no hazard ratio and no covariate**. The survival association is **not adjusted for
  stage or grade**, and the authors themselves offer the alternative: *«The association with overall
  survival could be due to an increased frequency of loss in higher stage tumors … or alternatively
  due, in part, to an increased frequency of clear cell and mucinous tumors which have a lower
  likehood of responding to therapy [31].»* **This paper leaves that open, and this reading does not
  close it.**
- **Not a mucinous-specific effect that survives its denominator.** n = 10.
- **Not a WWOX–PR mechanism.** The authors raise and leave unresolved *«whether the observed positive
  association between PR loss and WWOX loss is a consequence of the predominance of WWOX loss in the
  two aforementioned histotypes, rather than a direct mechanistic association»*.
- **Not a test of the sex-steroid hypothesis.** See §6.
- **Not a protein-level null anywhere.** "Negative" is *below 63/255 arbitrary intensity units on an
  automated reader*, not absence of protein.
- **Not a case-level concordance between blot and stain.**
- **Nothing about the nervous system, development, or any WWOX-DEE-relevant genotype.**

## 6 · The hormonal frame — restated, and in fact contradicted in part

The Background announces the frame: *«since we recently observed a strong association between loss of
WWOX expression and estrogen and progesterone receptor (ER and PR) status in breast cancer [12], we
also investigated any potential association between expression of sex steroid hormone receptors and
WWOX in ovarian cancer»*, and the sequence motif *«conserved in short-chain steroid dehydrogenases»*
is invoked in the same section.

**Does this paper test it? No — it measures an association and reports that half of it failed.** ER is
flatly null here (p = 0.984) against a *«strong correlation»* claimed in breast, and the authors say
so: *«In contrast to our previous observations in breast cancer … in this ovarian study, only PR was
associated with WWOX expression»*. No hormone was administered, no receptor manipulated, no
dehydrogenase activity assayed, no steroid substrate measured. The frame is **restated and partially
disconfirmed within one laboratory's own two tissues**, which is worth more than a restatement — and
it is still not evidence that WWOX acts in sex-steroid metabolism.

## 8 · Transfer verdict toward the reference genotype — **weak, and precisely why**

**Direct transfer: none.** No neural tissue, no developmental stage, no WWOX variant, no germline
genotype, no seizure, no CNS endpoint. Human somatic tumour tissue with acquired loss at a fragile
site is a different biological object from a germline WWOX-DEE genotype class, and the paper's own
`ESPANSIONE` domain is oncology.

**Indirect and genuinely useful, on exactly one axis: the reagent.** This paper is where the anti-WWOX
polyclonal used across this corpus's tissue-distribution arguments acquires a real negative control —
a genetic null, with add-back — for **immunoblot on ovarian material**. That is worth having, and it
is worth having *bounded*: it does not extend to CNS immunohistochemistry, to isoform claims, or to
compartment claims.

**One transferable methodological caution**, `INFERENZA`: an automated-intensity dichotomy whose
"negative" boundary sits five units below the mean of the normal reference tissue will classify
moderate reductions as absence. Any reading of this corpus that imports a "loss of WWOX protein"
figure from an ACIS-scored series is importing that boundary with it.
