## What the paper did
Three affected children from two consanguineous families were evaluated clinically, exome-sequenced by a commercial laboratory and confirmed by Sanger sequencing. The paper adds a review table of previously reported WWOX epilepsy cases.

## What it measured, at class level
| Family | Allele class | Zygosity | Phenotype band | How called | Segregation / function |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (one affected child) | canonical splice donor, `c.409+1G>T` (intron 4) | homozygous | DEE: focal seizures at two months evolving to infantile spasms, refractory; microcephaly; skeletal anomalies not previously reported with WWOX | WES + Sanger; array CGH negative | both parents and an unaffected sib reported as heterozygous carriers; **no RNA or functional test** |
| 2 (two affected sibs) | nonsense, `c.160G>T p.(Arg54*)` | homozygous | severe DEE: spasms, burst suppression on EEG in one, hypsarrhythmia in the other; brain atrophy, thin corpus callosum | WES | **no parental testing reported**; a further sib died in the first week of unknown cause |

The variants are named per patient. The pathogenicity call is the paper's own for the splice allele and inherited from the first report for `p.Arg54*`.

## What it states
- The splice allele's consequence is a possibility: *"This mutation might cause intron sequence to be included in the mRNA or the elimination of exon."*
- From the zygosity of reported cases the authors infer that *"one functional copy of the WWOX gene is sufficient for conducting normal neuronal activity."*

## What it does NOT state
- No examination, EEG or cognitive measure of the carriers; *"healthy"* is the authors' word.
- No RNA, protein or functional assay of either allele.
- No parental genotypes for family 2.

## Source-internal defects found on reading
1. **Table 1 transcribes the 2018 Q230P report as `c.689A<C` / `p.Gly230Pro`.** The held primary (PAPER 041) is the authority; this secondary table must not be used as a source for that allele. Nucleotide arrows are reversed (`<` for `>`) throughout the table.
2. **"All of these mutations are homozygous"** (Discussion) while the same Table 1 lists a compound heterozygote *"in trans configuration"*.

## Caveats the authors give
The small number of reported cases prevents them from deciding whether skeletal findings are allele-specific.

## Same patients elsewhere?
The `p.Arg54*` allele was first reported in a different family (PAPER 043). No LEGEND record names these three children. Overlap with later multi-centre cohorts cannot be excluded from this paper alone, and it is not asserted.

## Transfer limit
A homozygous splice-donor allele and a homozygous nonsense allele are both null-class by annotation; neither is the reference genotype class, and the splice consequence is unmeasured.
