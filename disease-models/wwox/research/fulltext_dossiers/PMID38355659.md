## 4 · What this reading refuses to support

The negative is the product here, and it is unusually large relative to the document.

- ❌ **It supports no biological proposition whatever.** It is not evidence that WWOX promotes or
  suppresses osteosarcoma; not evidence about Myc in bone; not evidence about RUNX2.
- ❌ **It does not resolve, and must not be read as resolving, the Myc-vs-RUNX2 question.** This
  actor read [[PMID20530675]] (Kurek 2010) from pixels in wave 1 and is the reader best placed to
  compare — but the Myc account lives in **PMID 38182577**, which this reading did **not** re-read:
  the duplicate-work gate forbids repeating a complete read that already has adequate coverage.
  Any coherence statement is therefore `INFERENZA` **over another reading's persisted locators**,
  attributed to that reading, and never this one's `DATO`. It is written up in §5 under that tag.
- ❌ **It does not repair the defect `DL-METH-079` records.** The erratum had the opportunity to
  correct a title that asserts the opposite of its own paper, and corrected a hyphen in a surname
  instead. `DL-METH-079` is **corroborated, not closed**.
- ❌ **It creates no `PUBLICATION_INTEGRITY_HOLD`.** An ordinary author-name erratum is annotated,
  never a hold. `hold: false`, consistent with the corpus rule and with 38182577's own record.

**`PREMISE` tag on the one non-trivial negative.** *"An author-name erratum cannot bear on the
scientific content of its article."* — `PREMISE: DATO`, and it is a datum rather than a textbook

## 6 · 🔴 A defect found by landing this reading — reported, not fixed

`erratum_scope_check.py`'s declared-empty branch references `entries` **before it is assigned**,
so a manifest that correctly declares `corrected_items: []` raises
`UnboundLocalError: cannot access local variable 'entries'`. The branch had never executed,
because until today **no manifest in the corpus declared an empty scope**. Its 12/12 self-test
does not see it: the self-test exercises `parse_panel`/`intersects` and **never calls
`check_manifest`**.

Consequence, measured: a **corpus-wide** run now crashes; `--pmid`-targeted runs still work.
The remedy the tool documents for PMID 38182577 — declare the empty scope — is currently the
input that breaks the tool.

**Not fixed here.** The file is a peer's (`ce7f89a`, `scientist-b`) and cross-actor fixes are the
orchestrator's. The exact one-line repair, and the regression that would have caught it, are in
`CC-20260909-38355659-01` §4.
