## 3 · What is measured — and at what level

1. **Variant-1 (full-length) mRNA is lower in tumours than in contralateral ovaries** — median 9.57 vs 22.9 relative units, Mann-Whitney p<0.0001 (L15, L16). DATO, on 71 vs 13, β-actin-normalised qRT-PCR.
2. **It is not associated with grade, stage or histology** (L18), and variant-1 level does not separate survival: the text gives no p (L19); **the panel prints p=0.792** (L20).
3. **Two tumours lack a full-length product by three primer pairs**, with every exon present in genomic DNA (L13, L14). One gives an uncloned ~950 bp product.
4. **Variant 4 (Δ6-8) is detected in 45/71 tumours** at 32-39 cycles and cannot be quantified (below the standard curve) (L21, L23). Every variant-4 analysis is therefore presence/absence of a late product.
5. **Variant 4 associates with grade 3 and with stage III-IV** — Fisher p=0.006 and 0.012 (L27, L28, L29). Recomputed from Tables IIIA/IIIB here: two-sided p=0.0065 and 0.0125. DATO.
6. **Variant 4 and survival:** all patients p=0.057, not significant (L30); high-variant-1 subgroup p=0.048 (L31, L32); low-variant-1 subgroup null, data not shown (L33); **not an independent prognostic variable in the multivariate model** (L34).
7. **Variant 4 is present in 9/13 normal ovaries (69%)**, a frequency similar to tumours, at similarly low relative levels (L35, L36). **This is the finding the title's "remains unclear" rests on.**
8. **Immunoblot is less sensitive than qRT-PCR in transfectants:** WWOX band in 4 of 6 clones, the two negatives being the lowest mRNA expressers (L9). The parent PEO1hyg1.6 lane has no band (L12).

## 6 · Strength, and transferability to the reference genotype

- **The reduction of full-length WWOX mRNA in ovarian tumours is the paper's solid result:** large effect, tight p, consistent across subtypes, with an internally coherent histogram. Its comparator is contralateral ovary from women with benign disease, not healthy tissue, and its normalisation is single-reference (β-actin).
- **Everything about variant 4 is weaker than the abstract makes it sound.** The transcript is unquantifiable, its associations are bivariate on 66 of 71 patients with two mutually inconsistent tables, its survival signal is a post-hoc subgroup at p=0.048 that dies in multivariate analysis, and **the same transcript is present in 69% of non-malignant ovaries**.
- **Transferability: T3, and effectively nil toward WWOX-DEE.** Adult somatic cancer, mRNA expression, no germline allele, no neural tissue, no developmental or seizure readout, no child. The one datum with any reach beyond oncology is that **an exon 6-8-skipped WWOX transcript is detectable at low level in the majority of non-malignant ovarian samples tested (9/13)** — a statement about the physiological background of aberrant WWOX transcripts in one adult tissue, by a late-cycle non-quantitative assay. Carrying it into a germline exon-deletion context would be `ESPANSIONE`, and this reading does not.
