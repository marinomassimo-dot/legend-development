## What it is

A narrative review from a sterol-biology and paediatric group: cholesterol synthesis in the developing brain, inborn errors of post-lanosterol synthesis (SLOS, desmosterolosis, lathosterolosis, others), medications that inhibit the pathway as a side effect (statins, several psychotropic and cardiovascular drugs), human biomaterial and epidemiology (the authors' own database study of maternal prescriptions and autism diagnosis), and a hypothesis of cumulative inhibitor burden. WWOX: zero occurrences. It measures nothing new about WWOX; the epidemiology is the authors' separate study cited as in press.

## What it says that bears on the lipid arm (DATO about the review's text)

- After the blood-brain barrier forms, the brain depends almost entirely on intrinsic sterol synthesis; plasma cholesterol does not supply it.
- Myelin growth needs very high cholesterol; suppressing cholesterol synthesis impairs myelin growth; oligodendrocyte cholesterol deficiency causes ataxia and tremor in mice (cited work, not new data).
- Table 1 (image): of nine inborn errors, desmosterolosis (DHCR24) and lanosterol synthase deficiency list seizures; callosal hypoplasia or agenesis is listed for SLOS, desmosterolosis, hemizygous male EBP deficiency and squalene synthase deficiency (LSS lists structural brain malformations); the SLOS row lists no seizures.
- Sterol-synthesis-inhibiting medications named: statins and, as off-target effects, aripiprazole, trazodone, amiodarone, cariprazine, buspirone, haloperidol, nebivolol, propranolol and others; heterozygous DHCR7 carriers (1-3 percent of people) show a sharper 7-DHC rise on exposure.

## What it does NOT support (the negative is the product)

1. **Nothing about WWOX.** No sterol or cholesterol measurement in any WWOX model or patient exists in this repository either; the review supplies a framework, not a finding. It does not support or refute a cholesterol or sterol defect in WWOX loss of function.
2. **Peripheral lipid genetics cannot speak to brain cholesterol.** Because brain sterol is synthesised locally behind the barrier (the review's premise), the WWOX plasma HDL and triglyceride associations read in this lot (Saez 2010, Leduc 2011, Liu 2022) say nothing about CNS sterol supply. INFERENZA from the review's premise.
3. **The epidemiological headline is not evidence.** A hazard ratio of 1.47 for any exposure (2.33 for four or more) in a registry-linked cohort is the authors' own prior study, cited as published online in April 2026; confounding by indication (the maternal conditions that lead to prescription) is not discussed in this review; the review itself notes most exposed pregnancies do not lead to the outcome.
4. **The "cumulative inhibitor burden" threshold is a stated hypothesis.**
5. **The sterol-disorder comparators carry seizures and callosal hypoplasia without a lipid-dose story for WWOX.** They are a reference class (how sterol lesions present), not a mechanistic link.
6. **The review's drug list is a class, not a statement about any individual.** Nothing here is a medication recommendation; it is a safety-screen prompt for the repository's discovery seeds (below).
