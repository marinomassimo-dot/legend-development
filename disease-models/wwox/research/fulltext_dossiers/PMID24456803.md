## What the paper did

Clinical description of one consanguineous family and whole-exome sequencing of one affected child, with
homozygosity-by-descent filtering, Sanger confirmation and segregation in the parents and two unaffected siblings.
A comparison table (Table 1) sets the child's phenotype beside the *lde/lde* rat and *Wwox* knockout mice.

## Genotype class and what was measured

- **Allele class × zygosity:** nonsense (exon 2, codon 54) × homozygous, in the genotyped child; one elder sibling with
  a similar course died in infancy, was never seen by the authors and was **never genotyped** ("probably affected").
- **Measured:** DNA only (WES, Sanger, segregation). **No RNA, no protein, no functional assay of the human allele.**
  Loss of function is inferred from the stop position. The "no protein produced" phrase in Table 1 belongs to the
  **rat** allele and is carried from the rat literature.
- **Nomenclature conflict inside the paper.** Body (Results) and Table 2: `c.160G > T`; Figure 1D and the authors' own
  Additional file 1 (IGV slide): `c.160C>T (p.Arg54*)`, with the IGV reads showing C→T. On NM_016373.4 position c.160 is
  **C** (codon 54 = CGA), so G>T cannot produce p.Arg54*; the correct description is **c.160C>T**. Piard 2019 S1 uses
  c.160C>T for the same allele in another patient; Sapuppo 2026 repeats c.160G>T.

## What the paper states and what it does NOT

- States: human-rat phenotype similarity; no tumours in the patient or heterozygous carriers.
- Does **not** state that carriers are free of cancer risk: "No tumors have developed so far" and, in the same
  paragraph, "an increased cumulative lifetime risk ... seems conceivable" with preventive check-ups advised.
- Does not measure the rat-human convergence on matched endpoints: Table 1 is a categorical side-by-side.
- Says nothing about the cerebellum.

## Authors' own caveats

Causality rests on the presumption of homozygosity by descent: "we can not exclude disease-causing compound
heterozygosity in a gene outside the HBD regions" (Conclusions). No autopsy.

## Dedup

This family is aggregated in Piard 2019 Supplemental Table 3 (two patients, one of whom is the never-genotyped sib)
and enters Piard's seven-patient "null" group as both. It is quoted again in Dong 2023 and Sapuppo 2026. **Count it once,
as one genotyped homozygote plus one presumed-affected untested sib.**

## Transfer limits

A homozygous early nonsense allele models neither human missense allele nor a canonical splice-acceptor allele; it is
the predicted-null end of the series, and even that is predicted, not measured.
