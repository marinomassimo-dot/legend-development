## 1 · What the preprint did

OGM (Bionano Saphyr, Solve v3.7) on 57 patient–parent trios from four centres, all exome-negative;
de novo SVs filtered first, then rare inherited SVs overlapped with a disease-gene list (± 50 kb);
candidate SVs validated by orthogonal tests (Sanger, CNV microarray, short-read genome, targeted
long-read sequencing). Three conclusive and five candidate SVs. WWOX is one of the three conclusive.

## 4 · Internal inconsistencies (the preprint's own text)

1. Results call the paternal variant a substitution (`c.107+119C>G`); the Discussion calls it
   *"an InDel on the paternal allele"*. One of the two is wrong; the HGVS string is the specific one.
2. Results: *"reanalysis of the genome data"*; Discussion: *"analysis of a newly generated short-read
   genome for this individual"*. Table S1 lists a short-read genome among the **prior** tests. Whether
   the second allele was missed in existing data or found in new data is therefore unclear.
3. Results order: deletion → clinical re-evaluation → genome reanalysis → RT-PCR. The phenotype was
   re-evaluated **after** one allele was known and **before** the second — genotype-first reasoning.

## 5 · Patient-overlap check (class level, against held sources)

No held source carries `c.107+119C>G` (repository search: zero hits). Held exon-6 deletion records
(PMID 37974179; PMID 30362252, a 22 kb exon-6 deletion homozygous by UPD16; the Dong 2023 compound
deletions) differ in allele pair (none pairs an exon-6 deletion with a deep-intronic SNV) and, where

families at FRA16D, so a shared exon is not evidence of shared ancestry.

## 6 · What the preprint does NOT state

but four other indexes; any functional test of the deletion. It does not claim novelty for the
intronic variant and does not report ClinVar submission.

## 7 · Transfer limits

A deep-intronic **cryptic-donor gain in intron 1** is not the reference genotype's acceptor allele,
not a canonical ±1/±2 site, and not a missense; nothing here transfers to P47T, Q230P, G372R, A141T
or P252A. n = 1 for the allele. Preprint.
