Mechanism: SOX10 carries a PPxY motif; endogenous WWOX–SOX10 co-immunoprecipitation from
HA-tagged knock-in cortex; GST pull-down shows dose-dependent SOX10 increase with WWOX, abolished
by the **WW1-domain mutant (WFPA)**; cycloheximide chase shows faster SOX10 decay in the
*Wwox*-KO Oli-neu line, rescued by MG132 in both genotypes; ubiquitinated SOX10 **"modest"**
increase.

## 3 · What it states

WWOX is a **cell-autonomous** regulator of OPC differentiation and remyelination, acting by
binding and stabilising SOX10 through its WW1 domain; WWOX is among the most dysregulated
oligodendroglial genes in MS lesions and is highest in remyelinating lesions.

## 4 · What it does NOT state

- It does **not** show a developmental hypomyelination phenotype from oligodendroglial deletion:
  at baseline, OPC and oligodendrocyte proportions, cortical and callosal MBP, and myelinated-axon
  counts are all unchanged; only myelin *thickness* is subtly reduced, "with only minor differences
  detected in the cerebellum".
- It does **not** contradict the neuronal-deletion paper and says so explicitly: *"While previous
  studies suggest that neuronal deletion of WWOX leads to hypomyelination via a non-cell autonomous
  mechanism, our data demonstrate that oligodendroglia-specific deletion results in a distinct
  phenotype."* The two are complementary requirements, not rival explanations.
- It does **not** exclude a non-cell-autonomous contribution; the authors keep it open for mature
  oligodendrocytes and for axonal cues.
- It reports **no heterozygote arm**. `Wwox +/−` mice are named in the Methods as existing in the
  colony and are used for nothing here — an earned null for the carrier question.
- MS is not WWOX-DEE: the proposed human mechanism is regulatory variants that "reduce WWOX levels
  just enough", explicitly contrasted with germline loss-of-function.
- No sex-stratified analysis is reported, although balanced sexes are declared for snRNA-seq.

## 5 · Caveats, including ones the authors do not state

- **Olig2-Cre is a lineage driver, not an OPC-timed one.** The deletion is constitutive within the
  Olig2 lineage from development; the preprint's own supplementary figure title calls the model
  "Conditional deletion of WWOX in OPCs". *Cell-autonomous* is established for the lineage, not
  for a cell that lost WWOX as an adult OPC.
- **The in vitro arm uses the constitutive null**, not the conditional; its "cell-autonomous"
  force comes from culture isolation.
- **snRNA-seq replication is thin**: N = 2, 2 and 3 pooled per genotype/condition, with
  differential abundance tested against *simulated* replicates obtained by sampling 33 % of nuclei
  without replacement. That is a resampling of the same animals, not biological replication.
- Statistics section states "p value < 0.005 being considered significant" while the stars are
  defined at 0.05/0.01/0.001.
- The baseline MBP comparison rests on **N = 2 mice per genotype**.
- **Not an independent laboratory from PMID 33914858**: same senior author, and one shared
  co-author. The corresponding author declares consultancy for a therapeutics company.
- Data availability: "will be available upon publication" — no accession today.
