## 1. What the paper did

Wild-type zebrafish embryos (pet-shop stock), injected at the single-cell stage with ~3 nL of:
* a **translation-blocking morpholino** against *wwox* (1 mM), with a 5-mismatch morpholino and a
  standard control morpholino as controls; **or**
* an **siRNA** against the N-terminal WW-domain sequence (50 µM); **and, for rescue**,
* co-injected *wwox* **ADH/SDR-domain mRNA** that does not base-pair with either reagent.

Numbers of eggs: 2,460 (MO), 2,590 (siRNA), 770 (rescue). Readouts: morphology at 48 hpf
(long-pec), body length and eye diameter at 48 hpf (n = 30 embryos, three independent experiments,
Student's t-test, significance at P < 0.01), fluorescent whole-mount in-situ hybridisation for
*wwox*, and FRET Ca²⁺ imaging with the yellow-cameleon indicator **YC2.12** at the prim-15 stage
(30 hpf; imaging is impossible after 33 hpf because of pigmentation).

## 2. What it measured, and the numbers

| Endpoint | Wild type / control MO | *wwox* knockdown | Rescue (ADH-domain mRNA) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pericardial oedema at 48 hpf | absent | **56 % penetrance (MO), 62 % (siRNA)** | phenotype rescued (Fig 2C, 2D) |
| Survival | normal | death within one week | — |
| Body length (Fig 2E) | ≈3.4 mm (WT), ≈3.45 (control) | ≈3.0 mm (≈−12 %) | ≈3.2 mm (partial) |
| Eye diameter (Fig 2E) | ≈265 µm (WT), ≈255 (control) | ≈205 µm (≈−23 %) | ≈270 µm (full) |
| Backbone | straight | twisted | — |
| Intracellular Ca²⁺ (Fig 4) | low, patchy | high at the oedema/yolk boundary, supraintestinal artery, dorsal aorta, common cardinal vein, pronephric duct | **not tested** |

Significance markers `**` (P < 0.01) are drawn in Fig 2E for knockdown-versus-WT and
knockdown-versus-rescue on both measures.

Expression: *wwox* mRNA is **maternally deposited** (highest at the 4-cell stage, ≈0.85 normalised
to β-actin) and falls to ≈0.45 by long-pec (Fig 1B). Protein-level alignment: zebrafish wwox is
412 aa against human 414 aa, 72 % amino-acid identity, with WW domains, NLS, ADH domain and Tyr33
conserved (Fig 1A). In-situ: strong signal in the developing heart from prim-5 (24 hpf), stronger
in ventricle than atrium, weak in putative digestive organs and optic nerves (Fig 3).

## 3. What the paper states

* *wwox* is required for normal zebrafish embryonic development; its loss produces pericardial
  oedema, developmental retardation (small eyes, short body, abnormal bone formation) and early
  lethality.
* Ca²⁺ dynamics are altered after oedema forms, with maxima at the oedema/yolk boundary and along
  the circulation.
* *"Monitoring of Ca²⁺ levels may provide an additional indicator for phenotype analyses."*
* The authors explicitly flag a **non-cell-autonomous** possibility: *"inhibition of wwox could
  have pointed a non-cell-autonomous phenotype."*

## 4. What the paper does NOT state

* **The Ca²⁺ result is not quantified.** Figure 4 is one representative colour-coded FRET-ratio
  image per condition, with anatomical arrows. There is no ratio value, no n, no statistic, no
  time course, no region-of-interest analysis, and **no rescue arm for the Ca²⁺ endpoint**. The
  knockdown arm of Fig 4 is the siRNA, co-injected with YC2.12.
* It does not claim a neural phenotype. *wwox* expression is cardiac-dominant here; the only
  nervous-system signal reported is *"weak localized expression… in optic nerves"*. Brain, spinal
  cord and neural activity are not examined.
* It does not show that Ca²⁺ change is a cause rather than a consequence: imaging is done
  **after** oedema forms, and the authors say so (*"after edema formation"*), with the high-Ca²⁺
  territories lying along a circulation the paper attributes to *"dysfunctions of the heart pump"*.
* It does not rescue with full-length *wwox*. The rescue construct is the **ADH (SDR) domain
  only**, i.e. the WW domains are absent from the rescuing protein.
* It does not include a sense-probe or no-probe control for the in-situ hybridisation, and the
  figure legend's description of panel 3B as *"a cardiac tissue section"* does not match the panel,
  which is labelled Right eye / Left eye / Atrium / Ventricle — a section containing both eyes and
  the heart.
* It measures no protein. There is no anti-Wwox immunoblot, so neither the knockdown efficiency
  nor the rescue construct's expression is shown.

## 5. Caveats the authors themselves give

* *"Further studies are required to determine whether the present zebrafish edema was derived from
  carcinogenic processes."*
* The non-cell-autonomous caveat quoted above.
* Ca²⁺ imaging is confined to ≤33 hpf by pigmentation, so no later or neural time point is
  reachable with this method.

## 6. Answer to the assigned question, for this source

The selector's premise that this paper has *"no rescue control stated"* is **wrong**: it has two
independent knockdown reagents and a quantified rescue on two morphometric endpoints
(`DL-MECH-021` already records this). But the endpoint that makes the paper interesting — Ca²⁺ —
is the one that is **not** quantified and **not** rescued.

* **Eye diameter at 48 hpf** is the only endpoint in this paper that behaves like a rescue
  readout: ≈−23 %, P < 0.01, n = 30 across three experiments, and restored to control by the
  rescue mRNA. It is a developmental morphometric, cheap and blindable.
* **Ca²⁺ by genetically encoded indicator** is, in principle, the kind of readout the question is
  looking for — continuous, live, non-terminal. In this paper it is a picture.
* The rescue by the **SDR domain alone** is the single most transferable fact here, and it is a
  structure–function statement, not a readout: it says that for *these* endpoints the
  oxidoreductase moiety suffices and the WW scaffold is dispensable. That bears directly on which
  WWOX functions a replacement construct must reconstitute.

**Transfer limit.** Morpholino and siRNA knockdown in zebrafish, read at 30–48 hpf, in a tissue
(heart) that is not the affected organ in the WWOX-DEE genotype class; no protein-level validation;
morpholino-era zebrafish knockdowns are a known false-positive class, and this paper predates and
does not apply the mutant-versus-morphant controls that became standard. Under
[`LEGEND_CORE.md` §13](../../../../framework/instruction/LEGEND_CORE.md#13-biomarker-scope-hard-rule)
intracellular Ca²⁺ is a Tier-3 distal readout and cannot be sampled in a living patient's neurons;
it is a model readout only.
