## 1 · What the paper did

One immortalised human skin fibroblast line, **1BR.3.N (ECACC 90020508)**, in four crossed
conditions (19 % or 1 % O₂ × 5.5 or 25 mM glucose), with **two opposite WWOX manipulations**:

- **down** — CRISPR/Cas9 sgRNA, puromycin-selected, polyclonal: **≈3× lower mRNA, ≈8.5× lower
  protein**. The paper calls this "KO" throughout; it is a knockdown.
- **up** — lentiviral WWOX cDNA: **≈850× mRNA, >700× protein**. A supraphysiological load, not a
  physiological re-supply.

Readouts: HRE-luciferase reporter, 2-deoxyglucose uptake ± insulin, RT-qPCR of transporters,
glycolytic and TCA genes, enzyme activity assays (hexokinase, LDH, PDH, citrate synthase), medium
lactate, fractionated immunoblots and immunocytochemistry for WWOX and HIF1α.

## 2 · What it measured — and where the direction fails to be monotone

| Endpoint | Knockdown | Overexpression |
|---|---|---|
| HIF1α transactivation (HRE reporter) | **up** in 3 of 4 conditions | **down** in all four, up to ≈6.5× in hypoxia |
| Glucose uptake, no insulin | **up** in normoxia/normoglycaemia, **down** in hypoxia/hyperglycaemia | **up** in normoxia/hyperglycaemia, **down** in hypoxia |
| Insulin-dependent uptake | **≈2× reduced** in normoxia/hyperglycaemia | increased in normoxia/hyperglycaemia |
| Medium lactate | **up** in all four conditions | **also up** vs wild type in normoxia/normoglycaemia and hypoxia/hyperglycaemia |
| Hexokinase activity | up in normoxia/normoglycaemia and hypoxia/normoglycaemia | — |
| LDH activity | up in normoxia (both glucose levels) | down in hypoxia/hyperglycaemia |
| **Citrate synthase expression and activity** | **no effect, any condition** | — |
| PDH activity | unchanged | down in hypoxia |

Lactate is the only knockdown readout consistent across all four conditions — and it is also the
readout the overexpression arm **fails to invert**. The glucose-uptake sign reverses with
condition in both arms.

## 3 · What it states

WWOX downregulation shifts human fibroblasts toward glycolysis via HIF1α and "probably predisposes
to metabolic disorders".

## 4 · What it does NOT state, and an internal disagreement

- Nothing neural, nothing developmental: *brain*, *neuron*, *epilepsy*, *seizure* do not occur.
- No metabolic intervention is proposed or tested (no ketogenic diet, dichloroacetate, metformin).
  The paper's only therapeutic sentence targets WWOX itself, in diabetes.
- No respirometry, no OCR/ECAR: "away from oxidative phosphorylation" rests on PDK transcript and
  on a citrate-synthase null.
- 🔴 **Two assays of HIF1α protein disagree, and only one reaches the Discussion.** The Discussion
  asserts increased HIF1α protein "together with its translocation to the nucleus". The
  fractionated blot reports HIF1α protein **significantly decreased in the cytoplasm** of the
  knockdown in two conditions, and "We didn't observed WWOX influence on HIF1α protein in nuclear
  fraction" — the blot was positioned to see translocation and did not. Whole-cell
  immunocytochemistry does show an increase in the two normoxic conditions. So the protein claim
  is carried by ICC, the translocation claim by nothing, and the robust positive is the HRE
  reporter.

## 5 · Caveats

One cell line, one laboratory, no primary cells, no animal. Each direction is a single clone pool.
The published PubMed abstract is itself corrupted by the typesetter's italic deletion
("The axis Downregulation"), so wording must come from the body — which it does here.
