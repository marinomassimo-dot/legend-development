## What it measures

A comparative-genomics re-analysis. Human input: the published three-cohort GWA meta-analysis for HDL (8,816 individuals; 2.5 million SNPs); 171 bins with a SNP at P below 1e-4, reduced to 92 loci with a mouse homologue. Mouse input: the published HDL QTL map (updated), combined QTL analysis of four crosses, haplotype comparison of parental strains, liver real-time PCR and microarray expression, resequencing, one promoter-reporter assay (Abca1). Outcome for WWOX: 53 percent of loci lie within 5 Mb of a mouse QTL peak; WWOX (human 16q, lead SNP rs2667590, P 2.25e-5, Table S4/S7) maps to mouse chromosome 8 at about 117 Mb, under the B6xCAST HDL QTL.
Tissue: liver. Genotype: wild-type inbred strains (B6, CAST, CASA). No Wwox-null or hypomorph animal and no brain, myelin or cholesterol-in-brain measurement anywhere in the paper.

## What the paper shows about Wwox (DATO)

- Wwox is one of three genes presented as newly identified for HDL (with Galnt2 and Cdh13); their GWA P values are 2.86e-7, 2.25e-5 (Wwox) and 2.46e-5, all above the genome-wide 5e-8 line the paper itself names (the paper's own "seven significant loci" do not include Wwox).
- Liver expression, B6 versus CAST, real-time PCR: no difference (fold -1.16, P 0.342; "did not find any difference in expression").
- Resequencing of all exons in B6, CAST and CASA: eight coding changes, six synonymous; two amino-acid changes, V186M (B6 versus CAST) and P365T (B6 versus CASA, one transcript only, "functionality could not be assessed"). V186M is scored "functional" by SIFT only, "not conserved", PolyPhen not scored (Table 1).
- V186M does not segregate in the second cross (B6xCASA). The B6xCAST candidate interval still holds 184 genes after haplotype filtering.
- The paper's conclusion for Wwox is stated as "because V186M may affect the function or structure of WWOX, we think that Wwox is a QTL gene for the B6xCAST cross". For the positive control Abca1 the paper has an allelic series, an expression difference and a promoter-reporter assay; Wwox has none of these.

## What it does NOT support (the negative is the product)

1. **No WWOX loss-of-function evidence.** The mouse alleles are natural strain polymorphisms; the paper has no knockout, knockdown, hypomorph or dose series. "Wwox is a QTL gene" is a candidate ranking, not a test.
2. **No brain, myelin or sterol evidence.** The queue note (FT-019) ties the paper to brain cholesterol and the myelin axis. The paper measures plasma HDL QTL and liver transcripts. That bridge is not in the paper and this reading withdraws it as a reason to read it.
3. **Internal tension on expression, in the supplement.** The Results say no Wwox expression difference. Table S10 prints two of four Wwox probe sets as differing between B6 and CAST: 1416334_at +2.14 (P below 0.001) in males and +2.28 (P below 0.001) in females, and 1431960_at +1.38 in males (P 0.001). The Table S10 footnote explains only 1431960_at (probe-site polymorphism) and is silent on 1416334_at. The Discussion then says the mouse "confirms" cis-regulation of WWOX, a sentence the Results do not carry. Fold change is high-HDL strain (B6) over CAST, so the printed direction is higher in B6. Whether 1416334_at is a hybridisation artefact is not stated and cannot be settled from the paper. INFERENZA: the paper's own expression data point two ways and the text reports only the null one.
4. **The knockout-mouse sentence cannot be traced to its citation.** The Discussion says the Wwox knockout mouse "has previously been shown to exhibit hypercholesterolemia (39)" and infers raised HDL. Reference 39 is the 2007 Wwox deletion paper; its own Discussion says serum lipids were altered "(data not shown)" and prints no cholesterol value (checked on the rendered page of the local PDF). The companion HDL-genetics paper read in this lot (Saez 2010) describes the same mouse as hypotriglyceridemic and hypocholesterolemic and cites a different paper (the 2008 one). Two secondary descriptions of the null mouse's cholesterol have opposite sign. The 2008 paper's serum chemistry was not re-opened here (its artefacts are not in this checkout), so which description is right is open; what is established is that Leduc's citation does not support its sentence.
5. **Species gap named by the authors.** No CETP in the mouse; some human loci fall outside any mouse QTL (47 percent of loci are more than 5 Mb from a peak).
6. **Coverage is a count, not enrichment.** The paper reports that 49 of 92 loci overlap a QTL peak; it does not test that against the overlap expected from the density of the mouse QTL map (about 40 QTL per species by 2005, many with wide intervals; Fig 1 shows bars covering much of several chromosomes). INFERENZA: with intervals this wide, overlap of a given gene with some QTL is a weak discriminator, and WWOX's B6xCAST bar spans about 70-127 Mb (Fig 3A).
