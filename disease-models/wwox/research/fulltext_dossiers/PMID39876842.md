## 1 · What the paper is

A narrative review of how lactate, histone and non-histone lysine lactylation, HIF-1 alpha, HMGB1 and HDAC/HAT activity might connect to epilepsy, framed on post-ischaemic-stroke epilepsy and ending with HDAC inhibitors (valproate, vorinostat, butyrate) as a possible strategy. No data of its own. WWOX does not occur. About 110 hedging words in about 8,400 words of body text.

## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. The review states "insufficient evidence to confirm that protein lactylation directly induces epilepsy"; the mechanism is introduced as speculation.
2. Described mechanisms (H3K18 and H4K12 lactylation, a HIF-1 alpha, PKM2, LDHA loop, lactylation of HMGB1 and mitochondrial proteins) come mostly from microglia, Alzheimer-model, cancer and ischaemia studies; none is from a WWOX or developmental epilepsy model.
3. Glial-to-neuron lactate and mitochondria transfer is presented with both readings, protective and excitatory.
4. HDAC1 to 3 as delactylases rest on in vitro work; the HDAC-inhibitor to HIF-1 alpha to lactylation to seizure chain is assembled from cited non-epilepsy studies.
5. The dependency screen flags one of 253 references (Ma 2020, retracted 2025 for image concerns); it is cited as one support in a run of citations on HDAC inhibitors and an HMGB1-p53 complex; nothing used here depends on it.

## 3 · Comparison with the laboratory's records

- Selection question: lactate is the WWOX-loss readout that survived across fibroblast conditions (lactate up in all four oxygen and glucose conditions, PDH and citrate synthase unchanged). This review gives the readout meaning in epilepsy only by hypothesis: lactate as excitatory fuel, as lactyl-CoA donor, as HIF-1 alpha feed-forward. It is a vocabulary, not evidence: no study cited measures lactate or lactylation in a WWOX-deficient brain.
- It offers the model one testable bridge: if Wwox-null brain tissue has high lactate, then histone H3K18 or HMGB1 lactylation and HMGB1 translocation are measurable consequences; and the HDAC-inhibitor class includes valproate, a drug used in WOREE (INFERENZA: no WOREE evidence on lactylation).

## 4 · What the paper does NOT support

- Lactylation as a cause of seizures (authors: insufficient evidence).
- Any statement about WWOX, hypomyelination, DEE or loss of function.
- HDAC inhibitors as therapy in post-stroke epilepsy (no trial).
