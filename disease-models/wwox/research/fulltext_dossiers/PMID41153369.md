## What the paper did

Single-centre retrospective review (records October 2017 – July 2020) of 129 children with DEE tested with a custom
55-gene amplicon panel (WWOX is on it); 20 panel-negative children then had exome sequencing. Trio segregation where
available. Tables 1-3 give one row per diagnosed patient (gene, variant, ACMG, case id, sex/age, clinical/EEG
features, syndrome, novelty, inheritance, consanguinity).

## WWOX content (all of it)

- **Count:** WWOX n = 2 of 29 P/LP diagnoses (Figure 1 bar and Results).
- **Table 1, two rows:** homozygous missense `chr16:78458877 NM_016373.4 c.716T>G p.L239R`, ACMG P (PM2, PM3, PP3, PP5),
  ClinVar 871669, carrier parents, consanguinity Yes —
  - case #49: male, 3 months at examination, "Multifocal epileptiform anomaly in EEG", West syndrome;
  - case #50: female, 11 months, "Hypsarrhythmia and multifocal epileptiform anomaly on a disorganized background in EEG", EIDEE.
- **Results:** the two are siblings ("case #49–50") from a consanguineous family.
- **Discussion:** WWOX listed among West-syndrome genes and EIDEE genes; one sentence each.

The paper says nothing further about WWOX: no seizure onset age, no drug or response, no MRI, no development, no
survival, no tone or movement description, no RNA or protein.

Which sibling had hypsarrhythmia is therefore not determinable from the paper.
