## What the paper did

Whole-exome sequencing of tumour and adjacent normal tissue from one adult male with a rare thyroid
tumour of mixed papillary and anaplastic histology; Sanger confirmation in the kindred; then
engineered stable overexpression of FLAG-tagged wild-type, P252A, P282A and P252A/P282A WWOX in two
thyroid-cancer lines (CAL-62, BCPAP) and HEK293T, plus CAL-62 nude-mouse xenografts; degradation
pharmacology; ubiquitin-chain typing; co-immunoprecipitation; comet / γH2AX / NHEJ-reporter DNA-repair
assays; IP-MS interactor screen; TCGA and a small IHC cohort.

## What it measured, and what it states

- Two germline **homozygous** missense variants, NM_016373 c.754C>G p.P252A (exon 7) and c.844C>G
  p.P282A (exon 8); homozygous in the proband by Sanger confirmation in adjacent normal tissue,
  heterozygous in one relative without a cancer phenotype and wild type in another.
- Both mutants are indistinguishable from empty vector across colony formation, viability, cell
  counting, Matrigel invasion, wound healing, xenograft volume/weight, comet tail moment, γH2AX foci
  and an I-SceI/EJ-GFP NHEJ reporter. The double mutant behaves identically (Supplementary Figure 2).
- P252A protein is low while its mRNA is not: the loss is **post-transcriptional**. Cycloheximide
  chase shows accelerated turnover. MG-132 (20 µM, 6 h) is **negative**; 3-MA (10 mM, 12 h) is
  **negative**; chloroquine (40 µM, 24 h) and NH₄Cl (250 µM, 24 h) are **positive**. K63-linked
  polyubiquitin predominates (K63R control). HSC70 co-IPs with P252A but not with wild type or P282A.
  LAMP1 immunofluorescence shows co-localisation.
- P282A loses function **without** a stability defect, and loses the POLE4 interaction.
- TCGA: WWOX mRNA lower in 9 tumour types including thyroid; lower in advanced stage; correlated with
  E-cadherin and inversely with fibronectin. siRNA knockdown induces EMT markers.

## What it does **not** state

- It makes **no neurological or developmental measurement of any kind**. It is not evidence for or
  against any WOREE, SCAR12 or seizure endpoint.
- It never demonstrates chaperone-mediated autophagy. The KFERQ-like motif (LRSVQ, 187–191) is
  identified by inspection and never mutated; LAMP2A is never manipulated; bafilomycin is never used;
  no half-life value is reported. The Results sentence is explicitly a speculation; the abstract and
  the closing Discussion paragraph state it as demonstrated.
- No functional assay is run under chloroquine or NH₄Cl rescue. The route is blockable; whether
  blocking it restores anything is untested.
- No nucleotide-excision-repair assay exists; the authors say the functional relevance of the
  WWOX–POLE4 interaction "remains to be elucidated".
- Abundance and function are never separated for P252A. The paper itself explains the lost POLE4
  co-IP by low abundance, then asserts causality.

## 🔴 Two corrections to what this repository currently holds

1. **The paper does name the domain, in Figure 1D, and it places P282A OUTSIDE the SDR.** The prior
   receipt recorded that "SDR", "ADH" and "short-chain" occur zero times and that domain placement was
   therefore "the reader's arithmetic". That is true of the **body text only**. Figure 1D, now
   inspected, draws the protein as two WW domains (ticks 1 · 16 · 49 · 57 · 90) and an **SDR box
   spanning residues 125–262** on a 414-residue protein, with the P252A tick inside the SDR box and
   the **P282A tick to the right of its boundary**. Only P252A is an SDR-domain missense substitution.
   The discovery-ledger entry DL-MECH-047 states both residues are in the SDR; that is wrong.
2. **Both alleles are common in the general population, and the supplementary table says so.**
   Supplementary Table 4 reports gnomAD allele frequencies of **0.0063 for P252A** and **0.0733 for
   P282A**, and for P282A gives SIFT "Tolerated" and MutationTaster "Polymorphism automatic".
   P282A is the known SNP rs3764340. At those frequencies, homozygotes are expected at roughly
   4 × 10⁻⁵ and 5 × 10⁻³ of the population respectively under Hardy-Weinberg — orders of magnitude
   too common for an allele that abolishes WWOX function in a living human. This is the
   population-genetic reason the proband has no neurological disease, and it is a stronger constraint
   than the authors' own "may have some residual protein function". The abstract's "complete loss of
   tumor-suppressive activity" is a statement about an engineered overexpression assay, not about the
   allele in an organism.

## Caveats the authors themselves give

"WWOX and WWOX mutants may have some residual protein function to maintain the development of the
neurological system"; POLE4 "appeared to" lose the interaction; the P282A mechanism "is still
unknown"; variants are "probably predisposing" carriers to cancer.

## Allele-class separation (mandatory)

P252A ≠ Q230P ≠ P47T ≠ A141T ≠ G372R ≠ P282A. Using the paper's own Figure 1D boundaries
(SDR 125–262), Q230P, A141T and P252A fall inside the SDR; P47T falls in WW1 (16–49); G372R and P282A
fall outside the SDR. Nothing measured here for P252A transfers to Q230P except the **question**:
is the protein present at normal abundance, or degraded despite normal mRNA? Nothing measured here
transfers to a neurological endpoint at all.

## Family counted once

One kindred: one proband plus two genotyped relatives. The paper's own prior report (its reference 29) is a
**different** proband with a 3'-UTR insertion and ovarian/colon cancer, from the same group. No earlier
publication of this thyroid kindred is cited or identified. Counting: one family, one affected
individual.

## A · The question this wave asked, answered first

**Is the loss of oxidoreductase activity quantified in any panel? No — there is no enzymatic assay
in this paper at all.** Census over the article text: `oxidoreductase` **1** occurrence (expanding
the gene's name in the Introduction), `SDR` **0**, `enzyme`/`enzymatic` **0**, `catalytic` **0**,
`dehydrogenase` **0**, `short-chain` **0**; `NAD` occurs once, inside a reference title. The loss
of function this paper measures is **phenotypic** — colony formation, viability, invasion,
migration, xenograft growth, comet tail moment, γH2AX, an NHEJ reporter — plus protein stability.
Nothing here measures what the SDR fold does catalytically, for either allele.

## B · Figure 2A read as a panel: the lines are not expression-matched

In **CAL-62** the Flag-WWOX band is strong for WT, **faint for P252A** and strong for P282A
(≈ WT). In **BCPAP** the WT band is the strongest of the blot, P252A is very faint, and **P282A is
visibly weaker than WT** while the GAPDH row is even across all four lanes. ⚠️ This is a *visual*
panel observation: the paper publishes no densitometry for this blot, and a crude lane-wise pixel
integration attempted here was discarded as unreliable (lane registration), so no ratio is carried.

It matters because Figure 2B–F compares the functional output of these same four lines: in both
cell lines WT lowers colony formation (`*`, `**`), invasion (`**`, `*`) and migration (`*`, `*`),
and **both mutants are `ns` against the empty vector on every one of those endpoints**.

## C · Figure 4A: the three lines are not transcript-matched either

Relative WWOX mRNA, read off the plot: empty vector ≈ 0, **WT ≈ 72, P252A ≈ 78, P282A ≈ 108**, all
marked `****` against vector. P282A therefore carries roughly **1.5× the WT transgene transcript**
(108/72). Figure 4B's cycloheximide chase shows P252A falling to ≈ 0.3 of its own time-zero by
10 h (`*`, `**`, `***`, `**`, `*` at 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 h) while **P282A tracks WT and is `ns` at every
timepoint**.

Read together: *«P282A loses function without a stability defect»* is right about the **decay
rate** — that is what a CHX chase measures — and it is not a statement about **steady-state protein
per transcript**, which no panel in this paper reports. With ~1.5× the transcript and a band no
stronger than WT in CAL-62 and weaker than WT in BCPAP, P282A's steady-state abundance is the open
question, not a settled control.

- 🔴 **It is a presence-only list.** Every one of the 261 rows carries `Lost Detection` in all

  Flag-tagged WWOX, no replicate, no statistic, a cancer cell line, and no localisation or
  signalling read-out of any kind. It is a lead, not a measurement of the axis.
