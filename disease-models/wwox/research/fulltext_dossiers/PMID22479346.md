## 1 · What the paper is

A gene-based association study of young-onset hypertension in Taiwan Han Chinese (400 matched pairs), with an expression array on 12 extreme pairs and replication in a Hong Kong family study and a British case-control study. It names WWOX among four replicated genes. Blood pressure is the closest quantitative proxy in group H3 for autonomic and vascular tone.

## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. No single SNP reached the stated threshold; gene-level hits are built from marginal SNP p-values: 101 prioritised, 100 after permutation.
2. WWOX: gene-based p 7.5e-14 and 1.2e-8 in the two continuous-coding models, only 0.017 and 0.022 in the nominal models; expression lower in hypertensive cases (beta minus 0.248 in Table S3; the text gives no direction) in 12 genotype-extreme pairs; 24 cis-eQTL SNPs in the gene region.
3. Replicated in the Hong Kong Han Chinese family study (sib-TDT p 1.05e-6, mean-pressure model 8.8e-7) and not in the British data.
4. The paper explains WWOX through mouse phenotypes (hypoglycaemia, hyperphosphatemia, lipids, uric acid) and a Japanese GWAS reference.

## 3 · Comparison with the laboratory's records

- The registry placeholder says no deep-dive and low relevance; this reading adds the actual numbers and the defects (below) and proposes a promotion record without changing a claim.
- Mouse knockout phenotypes that the article lists include hypoglycaemia and abnormal adrenal and pituitary gland morphology (Table S2 terms from the database), which agree in kind with the repository's known null-mouse endocrine findings; the article contributes no new measurement to them.
- The human association is a blood-pressure signal in an ancestry-restricted, exploratory scan; the repository has no record of hypertension or blood-pressure phenotypes in WWOX patients, and this paper does not supply one.

## 4 · What the paper does NOT support

- A causal or functional WWOX role in blood pressure: no SNP-level significance, no replication outside Han Chinese, no functional test.
- Autonomic or sympathetic involvement in WWOX loss of function: blood pressure is a distant proxy and the paper tests no autonomic variable.
- WWOX variant effects in mice: the lookup lists knockout phenotypes of the database.
- Direction and tissue of WWOX expression change: the source tissue of the array is not named in the sentence and the direction appears only in a supplementary table; mouse tissues disagree in sign (aorta up, heart, kidney and liver down).

## 5 · Transfer verdict toward the reference genotype (WWOX-DEE)

ESPANSIONE, small. System gap: adult common-variant hypertension in one ancestry group, expression in cells of selected samples. What transfers is a non-null prior that the WWOX locus is a candidate for blood-pressure regulation in the general population and a reason to ask whether autonomic or blood-pressure data exist in WWOX-DEE cohorts; not evidence about them.

## 6 · Epistemic tags and reopening

- DATO: gene-based association and expression signals for WWOX in a young-onset hypertension scan, replicated in one Han Chinese family study and not in a British case-control study.
- INFERENZA (authors): WWOX acts on blood pressure through glucose metabolism and ion homeostasis. Not measured.
- IPOTESI (ours, not a claim): blood-pressure or autonomic abnormalities may be part of WWOX-DEE; no datum. PREMISE: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK.
- Rejected: citing this paper as evidence for a WWOX variant effect on blood pressure. REVIVAL_TRIGGER: SNP-level genome-wide significance or a replication outside Han Chinese, or blood-pressure data in WWOX carriers.
- BLOCK-1 caution: blood pressure data here carry no treatment implication.
