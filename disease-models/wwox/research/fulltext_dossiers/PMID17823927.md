## 3 · What is measured — and at what level

1. **Expression reduction — measured, well.** Southern, RT-PCR, western and TaqMan agree that homozygotes lose wild-type transcript and protein in embryos and in the adult tissues examined; testis retains a faint band (L2, L5, L6; panels L11, L12, L13).
2. **Survival to 104 weeks — measured, and it is not a lifespan.**
   - Kaplan–Meier, sexes pooled, 35 vs 39 animals, Breslow P = 0.0188 (L21, L22).
   - In the pixels (L23): wild type loses no animal before ~84 weeks and ends ~74%; gt/gt loses its first animal at ~15 weeks and ends ~54%. **Neither curve crosses 50%, so median survival is not reached in either genotype.** No maximum lifespan is measured either, because observation stops at 104 weeks.
   - The abstract's *"significantly shorter lifespan"* is this curve difference (abstract_snippet on L22).
   - Early deaths: 9 of 39 gt/gt (23%) vs 0 of 35 wild type in the first 18 months, similar across sexes (L19, L24).
   - 🔴 **Necropsy found no definable cause of those early deaths (L20).** Whether moribund euthanasias entered the analysis as deaths or as censored observations is not stated (L18).
3. **Female malignancy — measured, small numbers.** 9/14 vs 3/15, χ² 5.85, P = 0.015 (L25, L26), reproduced here as an **uncorrected** Pearson χ² (5.855, P 0.0155); with Yates correction P = 0.041, and expected cells are under 5. B-cell lymphoma χ² 6.15, P = 0.013 (L29), which reproduces exactly as 8/14 vs 2/15.
4. **Males — no tumour difference** (L28, L27): 5/14 vs 5/18.
5. **Testicular atrophy — measured.** Young males: a clear difference, wild type all near 0. Old males: n = 4 per group, gt/gt values ~100, ~73, ~12 and ~3% — **two of four animals carry the old-group mean** (L36).
6. **Reduced male fertility — reported** (L37, L38), with the supporting table unseen.

## 5 · Discrepancies found only in the images, tables or arithmetic

1. **Figure 1D loading.** The legend says equal protein was loaded (L8); the upper blot's actin row is strongly unequal — kidney lanes faint, thymus and spleen heavy (L9). Within-tissue wt/hm comparisons still hold.
2. **Figure 1B heterozygotes are not uniform.** One lane is very faint, another near wild type, so "protein consistent with mRNA" (L10) holds on average only (L11). There are only two homozygous embryos.
3. **The replication statements disagree.** Methods say duplicate assays on three animals (L4); the Fig. 1C legend says triplicate on two embryos (L3).
4. **Figure 2G ages and P.** The axis reads 3–8 and 15–20 months while the text says 3 and 15 months (L33, L36). With n = 4 vs 4 a two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum cannot go below P = 0.029 exact (0.021 by normal approximation), so **the legend's P < 0.005 for the old group is not attainable by the stated test at the plotted n** (L34, L35, L36; INFERENZA, computed).
5. **Table 1 male footnote.** `P = 0.23` equals the uncorrected χ² *statistic* for 5/14 vs 5/18 (0.231), whose P is 0.63 (L27; INFERENZA, computed). The conclusion — no male difference — is unchanged.
6. **Table 1 denominators.** The tumour table uses 14 gt/gt females, 14 gt/gt males and 18 wt males against survival cohorts of 20, 19 and 20 (L16, L26, L27). The missing animals are not accounted for.
7. **The genetic background is never named** (L41). The ES line is 129/Ola (L40); the cross partner and F2 background are unstated, and the authors themselves invoke background for the lung adenoma rate.
8. **CD3 was used and never reported** (L30).
9. **Figure 2A shows wild-type IHC only**, so loss of Wwox staining in mutant testis is never shown in tissue (L47). **Figure 4's panels carry no genotype label** (L53).

## 6 · Strength, and transferability to the reference genotype

- **Construct validity:** strong for loss of wild-type transcript and protein in the embryo and in five adult peripheral tissues. **Unmeasured in brain.** The allele is predicted to make a WW-domain-bearing fusion, so it is neither a null nor a WWOX-DEE allele.
- **Survival:** a real, moderate reduction in cumulative survival to 104 weeks, on 35 vs 39 mixed-sex animals of an unstated F2 background, with unexplained early deaths. It is **not** a lifespan measurement and **not** a neurological phenotype.
- **Tumour:** a female-only malignancy excess on 14 vs 15 animals, significant only by an uncorrected test.
- **Testis:** convincing in young males; the old group rests on four animals, two of them near wild type.
- **Transferability: T3.** A whole-body gene-trap hypomorph read out for ageing, tumours and testis, with no neural tissue, no seizure or developmental readout and no human WWOX variant. The one datum a WWOX-DEE model can take from it is that **a large global reduction of Wwox expression, with residual expression detectable in at least testis, is compatible with survival into adulthood in this line** — while that survival is itself significantly reduced. It does not say which tissue's residual expression permits survival, and it says nothing about brain.
