## 1 · What the paper did

A translational package for an AAV9-delivered RNAi therapy for CMT1A: `AAV9.U6.miR871`, a
U6-promoter-driven artificial microRNA that **knocks down** PMP22. Two species.

- **Mice** (CMT1A C61-het model): dose-finding across four doses, efficacy, behaviour,
  electrophysiology, plasma NfL, morphometry, toxicology.
- **NHPs**: 20 wild-type *Macaca fascicularis*, **10 male and 10 female** — the only sex-balanced
  study in group C. Control (formulation buffer, n = 4), low dose (n = 8), high dose (n = 8).
  Lumbar intrathecal infusion **over approximately 6 hours via a pre-implanted catheter**,
  explicitly "to avoid increasing intracranial pressure". Necropsy cohorts at **6 weeks (day 43)**
  and **12 weeks (day 85)**.

The cargo direction is the opposite of a WWOX programme: this is knockdown of an over-expressed
gene, not restoration of a lost one. Biodistribution and procedure transfer; cargo and efficacy do
not.

## 2 · The DRG result — the body text and the figure caption do not agree

**Figure 5A caption:**
> "H&E staining of representative dorsal root ganglia (DRG) from miR871-treated animals show no
> significant histological abnormalities compared to control."

**Results, "IT of AAV9.U6.miR871 shows a strong safety profile in NHPs":**
> "Forty percent of animals showed subclinical, minimal-to-mild lesions in the dorsal root ganglia,
> which were also found in two saline-treated animals, suggesting these were unrelated to test
> article."

A reader who takes the caption alone concludes DRG lesions did not occur. The body says **40% of
animals had them**. The word doing the work in the caption is "representative". Reading the panel
(rendered and inspected) resolves it in the body's favour: the panel shows control and treated DRG
sections that look alike — which is consistent with lesions being present in both arms, not absent
from both.

### The genuinely important number is in the control arm

Two of the four saline animals — **50% of concurrent controls** — had minimal-to-mild DRG lesions.
Taken with PMID 41210171, where 2 of 3 vehicle animals had meningeal mononuclear infiltrate, two
independent NHP studies in this group show that **the histological findings routinely attributed to
intrathecal AAV9 also occur in vehicle-dosed macaques**. Any DRG claim built on treated animals
without a concurrent control incidence is therefore uninterpretable — and PMID 41210171 and
PMID 41257285 do not report one for DRG.

The authors' own inference ("unrelated to test article") is reasonable in direction but rests on
n = 4 controls; it cannot exclude a modest vector contribution.

## 3 · The one clean dose threshold in the group — and it is in mice

In CMT1A mice:

- the three lowest doses (1E11, 2E11, 5E11 vg/animal) **reduced** pre-existing DRG and spinal-root
  inflammation (fewer CD45+ and CD68+ cells at 5E11);
- **1E12 vg/animal increased** inflammation — raised CD3+ and CD68+ cells in sciatic nerve and
  raised CD3+ and CD20+ counts in liver — and the authors state they "believe [it] is toxic";
- no dose induced inflammation in lumbar spinal roots, DRG, brain, lung, kidney, quadriceps or
  heart.

A two-fold step from 5E11 to 1E12 converts an anti-inflammatory effect into a pro-inflammatory one.
This is the only explicit toxicity threshold in group C, and it is murine.

Note the confound the authors themselves flag: in a **diseased** model the vector can *reduce*
inflammation by correcting the disease. Whether a dose is "safe" is therefore not separable from
whether it is efficacious — a point that transfers to any WWOX-DEE programme, where the untreated
brain is itself inflamed and degenerating.

## 4 · NfL is bidirectional, and that is a problem for using it as a toxicity readout

- PMID 41257285 reports NfL **rising** with AAV9-induced DRG injury (peak day 28).
- This paper reports NfL **falling** as a marker of therapeutic benefit — "dose-responsive
  improvements in several molecular (decreased neurofilament, increased Mpz) … outcomes" — and notes
  NfL "is now being explored as a biomarker of CMT1A disease progression".

NfL reports axonal injury from any cause. In a disease that itself destroys axons, a rise cannot be
assigned to vector toxicity and a fall cannot be assigned to rescue without an independent anchor.
**For WWOX-DEE, where neurodegeneration is part of the disease, NfL alone cannot serve as the
patient-measurable DRG-safety readout.** This is the sharpest limit the group places on §5's
otherwise attractive answer.

## 5 · What the NHP arm did measure — and this is the answer to "measurable in a patient"

Endpoints assessed in life or at necropsy by an independent third-party veterinary team: clinical
and neurological observations, body weight, food consumption, **nerve conduction velocity (NCV)**,
ophthalmic examination, **ECG**, clinical pathology (haematology, coagulation, serum chemistry,
urinalysis), **cardiac troponin I**, cytokine ELISpot, organ weights, gross and microscopic
pathology. **NCV and compound muscle action potential (CMAP) were measured at baseline and again at
6 and 12 weeks**, with no significant post-treatment change (Figure 5C).

NCV and CMAP are **functional, serial, non-terminal** measures of the exact tissue at issue. They are
performable in a patient. Of everything in group C, this is the most directly importable safety
monitoring design.

Other findings: no adverse clinical observations attributed to test article; no cTnI elevation; one
high-dose female with enlarged lymph nodes of increased cellularity at 12 weeks, "the relevance of
which was unclear"; in mice, subclinical dose-dependent focal liver inflammation without AST or ALT
elevation.

## 6 · Three quantity defects in this paper — none of its numbers should be carried unchecked

1. **The low NHP dose is printed as two different values.** Results and Methods both say
   **6E13 total vg**; **Figure 5A labels the low-dose rows `5E13 vg`** (read from the rendered
   panel). The high dose is 1.2E14 in both text and panel.
2. **The infusion volume is printed as two different values.** Results: "a single **4-mL**
   intrathecal infusion over a 6-h period". Methods: "a **3-mL** single-dose intrathecal slow
   infusion over approximately 6 h". Volume relative to CSF volume is exactly the quantity
   PMID 42205472 says governs delivered dose at target, so this is not a trivial slip.
3. **The Figure 5A scale bars are given as "300 mm and 600 mm" in the caption.** The rendered panel
   prints **300 μm and 600 μm**. The caption's unit is wrong by a factor of 1,000; the panel is
   right. A locator taken from the caption would have been wrong.

A fourth, lesser slip: Methods report sedation with "10 mg/mL acepromazine" — a concentration where
a dose belongs.

## 7 · Dose scaling — a third scalar, different again

NHP doses were set from the murine minimally-effective and fully-efficacious doses (2E11 and 5E11
total vg) and **"scaled 371-fold for the increased CSF volume in monkeys compared to mice"**.

So group C contains three mutually non-convertible dose scalars: **per CSF volume** (this paper),
**per gram of brain mass** (PMID 41966056), and **fixed total vg per animal or per patient**
(PMID 41210171, PMID 41257285, and every human trial in PMID 42134074). See PMID 42205472 for why
the 371-fold figure does not match the published species CSF volumes.

## 8 · What it does NOT state

- Nothing about infants or juveniles — NHP age at dosing is not reported in the body or the methods
  section I read.
- No seropositive arm; pre-existing immunity is not a variable.
- No immunosuppression in the NHP study.
- No timepoint beyond 12 weeks, so durability and late DRG findings are unaddressed.
- No per-group DRG incidence table: "40%" is given for treated animals as a whole, and "two" for
  saline animals, without a by-dose breakdown in the text I read.
- It does not claim DRG safety for a protein-coding transgene. Its cargo is a U6-driven small RNA —
  on PMID 41257285's dividing line, a vector whose expression cassette is unlike a
  promoter-driven protein transgene. **The authors' conclusion that "at doses representing the FED
  and below, AAV9.U6.miR871 causes no DRG toxicity" is a claim about this cargo, not about AAV9.**

## 9 · Transfer limits toward a WWOX-DEE programme

| Datum | Transfers? | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Slow (~6 h) catheter infusion to avoid raising ICP | yes, as procedure | NHP; requires an implanted catheter, which is a different procedure from a single lumbar puncture |
| Serial NCV + CMAP at baseline / 6 wk / 12 wk as the DRG-safety readout | **yes — best in group** | functional, non-terminal, and performable in a patient; in an infant, both are technically harder and age-norm-dependent |
| Troponin I, ECG, ophthalmic exam as routine safety panel | yes | standard, cheap, patient-measurable |
| Background DRG lesions in ~50% of vehicle NHPs | **yes, and it is important** | n = 4 controls |
| A ≤2-fold dose step flipping inflammation direction | as a warning | murine, diseased model, cargo-specific |
| NfL as a vector-toxicity marker | **no, not alone** | bidirectional; see §4 |
| "No DRG toxicity at FED and below" | **no** | cargo-specific; a WWOX protein transgene is the other side of PMID 41257285's promoterless/full-vector line |
| Efficacy, target engagement, PMP22 biology | **no** | knockdown of an over-expressed gene; WWOX-DEE requires restoration |
| Any number from this paper | with checking | three internal quantity contradictions, §6 |

## 11 · Verdict

**INGEST** — for its monitoring design, its control-arm DRG incidence and its dose threshold. Its
efficacy half is off-axis for WWOX. Treat its printed quantities as unreliable until checked against
a panel.
