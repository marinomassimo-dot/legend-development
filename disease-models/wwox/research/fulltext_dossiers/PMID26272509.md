## What the paper did
UKN1 (H9 hESC, dual-SMAD neural induction) cells were exposed for 6 days (day of differentiation 0 to 6) to six HDAC inhibitors (valproate, trichostatin A, vorinostat, belinostat, panobinostat, entinostat) and six mercurials, each at its highest non-cytotoxic concentration (benchmark concentration; valproate 600 uM, TSA 10 nM, from Figure 1A), on Affymetrix arrays (4-5 experiments). HDACi "consensus genes" were defined as regulated by at least four of six HDACi. An SVM classifier (100 probe sets, then an eight-gene reduction: F2RL2, TFAP2B, EDNRA, FOXD3, SIX3, MT1E, ETS1, LHX2) separated HDACi from mercurials and was validated on legacy valproate and TSA data sets (VPA classed as HDACi from 350 uM, not at 25 or 150 uM).
- Supplemental Table S1 is genome-wide: 54,675 probe sets per sheet for 12 compounds and 2 solvents (columns Affy_ID, Symbol, Name, FC, p.value, adj.p.value, BH). Cut-off stated: p 0.05, fold change 1.5 (applies to lists, not to these full tables).

## Caveats given by the authors
Classifier features are chosen for separation, not biology; the authors flag the dependence on test system and on concentration (low VPA is not recognised as an HDACi).
