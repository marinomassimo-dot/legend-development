## 2 · What the study is

Five figures, no tables, no supplement. **Three experimental systems**, and the distinction between them is
the whole of this paper's relevance to the corpus:

| Arm | System | What is done |
|---|---|---|
| **A — in vivo** | Wild-type male C57BL/6 mice, 6–8 weeks | **Intratracheal instillation of 10 mg/kg anti-WWOX siRNA**, then intratracheal LPS 1 mg/kg or saline |
| **B — in vitro mechanism** | A549 human alveolar epithelial line | Plasmid miR silencing of WWOX and/or c-Jun; co-IP; confocal; transcription-factor activity assays; ELISA; Transwell chemotaxis with primary human neutrophils |
| **C — in vivo pharmacology** | Wild-type C57BL/6 mice | Anti-WWOX siRNA plus subcutaneous JNK inhibitor 30 mg/kg or DMSO |

🔴 **There is no genetic knockout anywhere in this paper, and no whole-body manipulation of any kind.**
The mice are wild-type; the perturbation is an airway-delivered siRNA duplex acting for hours to days.

## 3 · What is measured, in what system, with what n, against what controls

### Arm A — the in vivo inflammatory phenotype (Figure 1)

- **n:** *"Twelve C57Bl/6 mice were treated with intratracheal instillation of either control nontargeting
  (n = 6) or WWOX-targeting siRNA (n = 6)."* Then 3 of each 6 received PBS and 3 received LPS.
  **So each of the four groups is n = 3 mice**, and three data points per group are visible in every panel.
- **The caption nevertheless says** *"Results are shown as means ± SD in n = 3 independent experiments."*
  Twelve mice split 6/6 then 3/3 within one described animal experiment is n = 3 **per group**;
  "independent experiments" asserts a replication structure the described design does not contain.
  **Both readings cannot be true and the paper does not disambiguate them.**
- **Controls:** scrambled non-targeting siRNA (sequence printed), PBS versus LPS, both crossed.
- **Endpoints, all on bronchoalveolar lavage fluid unless stated:** WWOX Western blot on whole-lung
  homogenate (A); H&E histology (B); leukocyte concentration by automated counter (C); **neutrophil
  percentage by manual differential over 10 high-power fields** (D); total protein by BCA (E);
  BALF:serum FITC-dextran ratio (F); IL-6, IL-1β, KC and MIP-2 by ELISA (G–J).
- **Statistics:** *"Either a Student's t-test or an ANOVA were performed where applicable"*, GraphPad Prism,
  *"Significance was set at \*P < 0.05."* Two-way ANOVA for PBS-vs-LPS × control-vs-WWOX-siRNA, then
  t-tests on all significant comparisons. **No multiplicity correction is named** for the eight BALF
  endpoints.

**The result, and it is positive and starred.** *"Surprisingly, WWOX silencing alone caused neutrophil
influx … and a corresponding vascular leak … to a similar degree that LPS did in wild-type mice pretreated
with scrambled control siRNA."* Inspected at native resolution, **the WWOX-siRNA + PBS group carries an
asterisk on all eight quantitative panels** (C–J) — leukocytes, neutrophils, protein, FITC-dextran, IL-6,
IL-1β, KC, MIP-2. Knockdown alone, with no stimulus, is significant on every measured axis.

### Arm B — the mechanism, entirely in a transformed human cell line (Figures 2–4)

- **n = 3 experiments** throughout; controls are a negative-control plasmid, a mock immunoprecipitation,
  control IgG versus neutralising anti-IL-8, and single versus dual silencing.
- WWOX co-immunoprecipitates c-Jun (2A); silencing WWOX moves c-Jun to the nucleus — ~5% → ~45% of cells
  with strong nuclear staining (2B), confirmed by nuclear/cytoplasmic fractionation (2C).
- WWOX silencing raises secreted IL-1β, IL-6 and IL-8 and roughly quintuples neutrophil transmigration
  (~28 → ~150 neutrophils/hpf), **abrogated by neutralising anti-IL-8** (~70) (Figure 3).
- **Dual silencing of c-Jun abolishes all of it** — chemotaxis, all three cytokines, and the rise in nuclear
  NF-κB — which is the paper's argument that c-Jun, not NF-κB, is the driver (Figure 4).

### Arm C — the in vivo JNK rescue (Figure 5)

- Twelve mice again, n = 6 per siRNA arm, 3 per arm to inhibitor or DMSO. **n = 3 per group.**
- Neutrophil count, total protein, KC, MIP-2, IL-6, IL-1β all significantly attenuated by the inhibitor.
- 🔴 **Attenuated is not normalised, and only the panel shows it.** Figure 5A: WWOX-siRNA + DMSO ≈ 60,
  WWOX-siRNA + inhibitor ≈ 47 on `BALF neutrophil count/ml × 10⁴`, while **both** control-siRNA arms sit
  at ≈ 5–8. The inhibitor removes a minority of the excess; the knockdown arm stays several-fold above
  control. The text says only "significantly attenuated".

## 4 · What is asserted but not measured

1. **That the model is a WWOX-loss model of the gene's function.** It is an acute knockdown of unstated
   efficiency and duration. Silencing is shown by one representative blot on whole-lung homogenate (1A);
   **no densitometry, no per-animal knockdown efficiency, and no time course of recovery are reported**,
   although ImageJ densitometry is described in the Methods.
2. **Which cell type loses WWOX.** The compartment is **imported, not measured**: *"it is known through
   studies using GFP-targeting siRNA in GFP-overexpressing mice that the predominant cell type affected by
   intratracheal siRNA delivery is the alveolar epithelial cell (52)."* That is a citation to another
   paper's reagent, in another paper's mice, for a different transcript.
3. **That the c-Jun mechanism operates in the mouse lung.** It is established in A549 cells and bridged
   in vivo by one pharmacological experiment with a kinase inhibitor — not by measuring c-Jun localisation,
   AP-1 activity or WWOX–c-Jun association in any mouse lung in this paper.
4. **The endothelial inference.** *"This suggests an influence of WWOX deficiency on mechanisms of
   endothelial barrier dysfunction during LPS-TLR4 signaling events."* Inferred from leak being
   disproportionate to inflammation. **No endothelial cell is measured anywhere in this paper.**
5. **That WWOX's SDR/oxidoreductase domain is involved.** Introduced and never tested; no catalytic mutant.
6. **Off-target effects.** The authors' own first limitation: *"only one WWOX-targeting siRNA was used in the
   mouse studies, raising the possibility that off-target effects might be responsible for the observed
   increase in lung neutrophil influx."* The in vitro arm uses a different sequence against a different
   species' transcript, which the authors offer as partial mitigation — it is not a second in vivo reagent.
7. **Blinding and randomisation.** Not stated for the manual differential count or for group allocation.

**A sixth, textual only:** the Figure 3 panel letters in the running text are shifted against the published
caption, and the caption is the one that is right. The text cites *"Fig. 3, B–D"* for the cytokines and
*"Fig. 3, E and F"* for transmigration; the caption and panels put chemotaxis at B–C and IL-1β/IL-6/IL-8 at
D–F. Within a single paragraph the same panels are cited two incompatible ways.

**A seventh, on units:** Figure 1E labels BALF total protein `ng/ml`; Figure 5B labels the same quantity

(~0.2–1.4) and Figure 5B values (~0.5–1.1) are of the same order, so **`mg/ml` is the plausible true unit
and Figure 1E's label is the error**; recorded, not resolved, because nothing in the paper adjudicates it.

**And one quantitative discrepancy between figures, recorded because it bears on reproducibility:** the
WWOX-siRNA-alone arm reads ≈ 24 × 10⁵ BALF neutrophils/ml in Figure 1D and ≈ 60 × 10⁴ (= 6 × 10⁵) in
Figure 5A — a roughly fourfold difference for nominally the same condition in the same model. Both are
approximate readings off the panels. The paper does not remark on it.

## 6 · Design limits, stated neutrally

- **n = 3 mice per group** in both in vivo experiments; a manual differential read over 10 high-power fields,
  with no stated blinding.
- **One siRNA sequence in vivo**, no second reagent, no rescue by WWOX re-expression.
- **Acute knockdown**, not chronic or genetic; efficiency shown by one representative blot.
- **The mechanism is in A549**, a transformed line whose type-I/type-II identity the authors note is
  contested (their own second limitation).
- **The in vivo bridge is pharmacological**, with a kinase inhibitor at 30 mg/kg systemically — not a
  genetic or cell-type-restricted test of c-Jun.
- **Single laboratory, single city, and no WWOX-primary co-author** (see §7).
- **No multiplicity correction** across eight BALF endpoints tested with t-tests after ANOVA.
