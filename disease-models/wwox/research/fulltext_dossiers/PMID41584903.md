## 1 · What the paper is

A report of a 2025 Special Interest Group meeting: human amygdala-evoked apnea; genetic arrhythmia substrates (long QT syndrome, a rabbit model, sodium-channel DEE mouse models with cardiac recordings); and the HPA stress axis as a proposed SUDEP mechanism. It is the paper in group H3 that comes closest to catecholamines and the stress axis.

## 2 · Findings as the source gives them (first pass)

1. Terminal events in nine monitored deaths: convulsive seizure, terminal apnea, then asystole.
2. Amygdala stimulation in patients causes apnea without awareness; a focal "AIR" site.
3. Channelopathy genes expressed in brain and heart (SCN1A, SCN8A, SCN1B, KCNH2) give cardiac arrhythmia substrates; in Scn8a DEE13 mice arrhythmias appear on challenge with epinephrine plus caffeine, ECG normal at rest.
4. Stress axis: elevated cortisol in epilepsy; chronically epileptic mice with HPA dysfunction show more SUDEP, prevented by suppressing HPA signalling; reflex hyperactivity at the nucleus of the solitary tract proposed as the trigger.
5. Caveats in the source: rabbit data unpublished; a reference mismatch (SCN1B and Brugada carries a potassium-channel paper); HPA claims rest on a review and a preprint.
