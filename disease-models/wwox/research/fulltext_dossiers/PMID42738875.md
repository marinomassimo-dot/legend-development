## 1 · What the study did

140 age-matched archived plasma samples from an existing combined cohort study (people with HIV on
stable treatment, in three cognition bands, plus seronegative controls with normal cognition;
co-morbidities excluded). Total vesicles by size-exclusion chromatography, then **L1CAM
immunocapture** to obtain "neuronal-enriched" vesicles (nEV). Cargo measured by three platforms:
a 5-plex and a 10-plex bead immunoassay (duplicate), and an **Olink Target 48 Neurodegeneration
41-plex run in singlet**, reported as NPX — a relative, log2 unit, not a concentration. Statistics:
one-way ANOVA with Tukey (normal data) or Kruskal–Wallis with Dunn; two-predictor logistic
regression for discrimination. Cross-sectional, single timepoint, no genotyping.

## 3 · What the paper does NOT state

No WWOX concentration (NPX is relative); no replicate for the Olink measurement; no demonstration
that the captured vesicles are neuronal in origin (L1CAM capture is the operational definition); no
longitudinal or within-person change; no intervention; no WWOX genotype, no WWOX-related disease,
no child, no developmental cohort; no sensitivity or specificity for WWOX alone; no reference
interval; no assay validation for WWOX (precision, stability, pre-analytical sensitivity). The
authors state the study is exploratory and hypothesis-generating and not confirmatory for any single
biomarker.

## 4 · § 13 classification

WWOX protein in an L1CAM-captured plasma vesicle fraction is a **direct gene readout (Tier 1 by
kind)** — it is the protein itself — but it is **not validated** in the §13 sense: no
sensitivity/specificity evidence in the disease population, and the disease population here is not
WWOX disease. For a WWOX-DEE pharmacodynamic endpoint it would still need: absolute quantification
rather than NPX; demonstration that the signal tracks brain WWOX dose; a measurement in people
carrying WWOX alleles, where the expected level is low or zero — the floor problem, since a
hypomorphic allele's residual protein may sit below the assay's detection; within-person
repeatability; and a known dynamic range under a dose change. None of these exists today.

## 5 · Transfer limits

Adults with HIV and mild cognitive impairment; no WWOX allele anywhere; a single marked comparison
within one sex; singlet assay; exploratory by the authors' own statement. Nothing transfers to any
WWOX genotype class, and the sex difference in the text is not the comparison the panel marks.
