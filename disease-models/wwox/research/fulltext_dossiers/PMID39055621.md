## What it is

A single-hospital observational study of 35 treatment-naive infants with infantile spasms (mixed aetiology) and 35 healthy infants: serum IgG, IgA, IgM, lymphocyte subsets and IL-1beta, IL-2R, IL-6, and the same indices after two weeks of ACTH. WWOX: zero occurrences.

## WWOX content

None. **No WWOX-deficient evidence.** Infantile spasms is a DEE-class syndrome and WWOX-related disease can present with spasms, but no WWOX status is given (genes of the six pathogenic variants are not named).

## What it measures (DATO)

Patients had higher IgG and IgM, lower IgA, more B cells, fewer CD3 and CD4 cells and higher IL-1beta, IL-2R and IL-6 than controls; after ACTH IgG, NK cells, B cells and the three cytokines fell and CD3 and CD4 rose. 54.29 percent responded by spasm frequency.

## What it does NOT support (the negative is the product)

1. **No link between immune change and seizure response.** Markers changed in the whole group, 16 of 35 did not respond, and no analysis compares responders with non-responders. The abstract's inference that ACTH controls seizures by improving immune dysfunction is not tested; the authors' conclusion that the markers can "evaluate the therapeutic effects" has no response-linked data under it.
2. **Likely wrong test for paired data.** Table 3 t values equal an independent-samples comparison on the reported means and SDs (IgG: difference 0.84 over a standard error of about 0.385 gives 2.2, as printed; recomputed here), although the same children are measured twice. A paired analysis could differ either way. INFERENZA.
3. **Control group is thin and inconsistent.** Healthy infants at physical examination, aged 3-16 months mean 6.58 (patients mean 8.13); Results give controls an "age of onset" and a sex split different from Methods; no aetiology, drug or comorbidity data for controls; the patients had prior antiseizure drugs in 18 cases.
4. **Selection for BBB leakage and heterogeneous causes.** Inclusion required CSF albumin raised and increased BBB permeability; 30 of 35 had asphyxia or hypoxic-ischaemic encephalopathy, 14 structural lesions, a few genetic. The immune profile may reflect aetiology and illness more than the syndrome; the paper says it did not control for these.
5. **Internal slips.** The before-ACTH B-cell figure in Results text (19.73 percent) is the control value, Table 4 says 35.21; the cytokine figure y axis is labelled "lever"; controls sit at assay floor values for IL-1beta and IL-2R, so group differences partly reflect detection limits; a Discussion sentence names C-Fos as a cytokine.
6. **Not about immunoglobulin or plasma products.** Immunoglobulins here are endogenous serum levels; IVIG, plasma exchange and FFP are not studied. The Discussion's statement that immunoglobulins entering the brain inhibit cytokines is cited, not tested.

## Design and limits

Small, uncontrolled before/after, two time points, no blinding statement, no multiplicity handling, aetiologically mixed cohort, single centre.
