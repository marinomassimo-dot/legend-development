## Why it was in this group

It is the first link of the TIAF1 cascade that wave 2 was given downstream of, and it is a named reading
debt. It was read to find out what it actually fixes.

## What the paper did and measured

Cell work in COS7, U937, L929, Mv1Lu, HCT116 (TβRII-deficient), NCI-H1299, NT2D1, SK-N-SH, SH-SY5Y, MCF7,
MDA-MB-231, DU145, B16F10 and HEK293: yeast two-hybrid, co-immunoprecipitation, retroviral TIAF1 knockdown
(~60–75 %), fluorescent-protein-tagged TIAF1 overexpression, time-lapse FRET, cell-cycle sub-G1, and
immunoblot for TIAF1 multimers, APP, phospho-APP Thr668, AICD, Aβ and amyloid fibrils. Tissue work:
postmortem human hippocampus (filter-retardation assay, immunostaining) and APP/PS1 and APP transgenic mouse
hippocampus.

## What it states

- TIAF1 binds Smad4; knockdown releases Smad3/4 to the nucleus and activates a SMAD-responsive promoter
  (~1.5-fold spontaneous, ~2-fold with ectopic Smad4).
- TGF-β1 drives TIAF1 self-aggregation independently of TβRII (shown in TβRII-deficient HCT116), and the
  aggregated state is what kills; Smad4 co-expression prevents aggregation and death.
- TIAF1 aggregation is inducible by TNF-α, cell density, extracellular matrix and complement C1q, and
  correlates with the appearance of Aβ and amyloid fibrils in vitro.
- Human hippocampus, filter retardation: TIAF1 aggregates in 59.0 % of nondemented samples
  (age 59.0 ± 17.0, n = 41) and 54 % of Alzheimer samples (age 80.0 ± 8.8, n = 97); Aβ aggregates in 15 %
  and 48 %; within TIAF1-positive samples, 17 % carry Aβ in the control group and 48 % in the Alzheimer
  group.
- In SK-N-SH, TIAF1/TIAF1 overexpression **increased** APP Thr668 phosphorylation and TGF-β1 suppressed it;
  dephosphorylated APP is then degraded to AICD, Aβ and fibrils.

## What it does NOT state — and the WWOX null

**WWOX is not measured anywhere in this paper.** It occurs three times in the whole article: as a
yeast-two-hybrid positive control (WOX1/p53 growth control in Figure 1a), as cited background for the
TGF-β1/Hyal-2/WOX1/Smad4 route (Results and Discussion, citing reference 14), and once in a Discussion
sentence about C1q-induced membrane turbulence in WOX1-expressing cells. There is no WWOX manipulation, no
WWOX readout, no WWOX figure, no *Wwox* animal. **This is an earned null for the gene**: reading it closes
the debt and adds no WWOX datum.

Also absent: any time course in human tissue; any age adjustment between two groups that differ by ~21 years
of mean age; any n or statistic for the mouse hippocampal comparison; any organelle endpoint (no
mitochondrial, lysosomal or autophagic measurement in the entire paper).

## Internal tension worth carrying

The abstract says TIAF1 aggregation "leads to dephosphorylation of amyloid precursor protein (APP) at
Thr668"; the Results say overexpressed TIAF1 **increased** Thr668 phosphorylation and that TGF-β1 is what
suppresses it. The transferable form is the two-step one, not the abstract's one-step one.

The "precedes" conclusion is argued from cross-sectional prevalences in two groups of different age, and
TIAF1 aggregation prevalence itself is **not** higher in the demented group (54 % versus 59 %). What differs
is Aβ, and the conditional 17 %/48 % statistic.

## Caveats the authors themselves give

"How TIAF1 affects Smad4 in controlling APP production remains to be established"; "How AICD is generated
because of TIAF1 aggregation remains to be established"; "Formation of nuclear bubbles in the dying neurons
in vivo is unknown" (in the companion review's framing).
