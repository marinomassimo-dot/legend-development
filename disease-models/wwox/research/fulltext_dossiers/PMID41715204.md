## What it measures

CerS2 conditional knockout in mouse (nestin-Cre for CNS, olig-Cre for oligodendrocyte lineage; exons 2 to 9 flanked). Readouts: brain lipidomics (7 control, 5 knockout, 3-month males), DESI and MALDI imaging, whole-brain proteomics (4 versus 4), lipid-raft fractions, electron microscopy of corpus callosum, behaviour, survival, and primary NPC to oligodendrocyte differentiation and DRG-OPC myelination assays with brain lipid extracts, beta-cyclodextrin, S1P and C24:0 sphingomyelin. Genotype: a lipid-synthesis null; no WWOX; the word WWOX does not occur in the article.

## What it shows (DATO)

- Brain C22-C24 ceramides, sulfatides, galactosyl- and glucosylceramides and sphingomyelins fall (often tenfold or more), C16-C18 species rise, S1P falls about two thirds, phosphatidic acids fall; no change in the mRNA of the relevant synthetic enzymes.
- Hypomyelination (sheath number, g-ratio) is present at P20 and worse at 3 months; the oligodendrocyte-lineage deletion reproduces the phenotype; convulsions start about 4 months and mortality about 5 months; behaviour is largely normal except Y-maze alternation.
- Imaging places the deficit in white-matter tracts. Whole-brain proteomics (derived from the deposited table): MBP 0.32, MOBP 0.38, MAG 0.43, CNP 0.56, PLP1 0.58 of control, MOG 1.00, GFAP 3.2-fold up, APOE 1.7-fold up (ratios computed here from LFQ columns).
- In culture, knockout OPCs differentiate less (MBP-positive cells about 2 versus 3 percent), and extracts of control but not knockout brain lipids raise differentiation (about 4 to 8.5 percent) and myelinated length.

## What it does NOT support (the negative is the product)

1. **No WWOX content of any kind, and no cholesterol or VLC measurement in a Wwox model.** The paper is a comparator for what a lipid-chain-length readout in myelin looks like; transfer to WWOX is a design template, not evidence.
2. **The cell-of-origin differs from the one recorded for WWOX.** Here the oligodendrocyte-lineage deletion is sufficient. The repository's CLAIM 003 records the WWOX myelin defect as non-cell-autonomous after neuronal deletion (Repudi 2021), and CLAIM 005's boundary names astrocyte- and oligodendrocyte-conditional Wwox deletion as negative for that phenotype. A CerS2-like oligodendrocyte-autonomous VLC lesion therefore cannot be the WWOX mechanism as recorded; if lipids are involved they would be non-cell-autonomous (neuronal supply or signalling). The paper's DRG neuron-OPC co-culture, with independently genotyped partners, is the design that could separate the two.
3. **The microdomain "mechanism" rests on one non-specific experiment.** Beta-cyclodextrin depletes cholesterol, not very-long-chain sphingolipids. The text calls the effect severe; the quantified effect is about 40 percent and equal at 50 and 250 uM, and the image for 250 uM shows no MBP-positive segments (Figure 6C-D). The raft-localisation of MBP is a gradient blot of one fraction; the raft lipid table shows ratios without replicates.
4. **Small numbers and a sample-size mismatch.** P20 electron microscopy has two animals in one arm (legend n = 4 and 2; plotted points are the reverse, 2 and 4); MBP western n = 3 versus 4; brain lipidomics 7 versus 5; in vitro differentiation rates are 2 to 3 percent of cells with n = 5. The rescue by lipid extract uses n = 29 versus 15 (wells or fields, not stated) and a unit of "10 uM lipids" for a mixture.
5. **The causal chain is acknowledged as incomplete.** Purified individual species were not tested; C24:0 sphingomyelin at 100 uM raised differentiation (p 0.016, n = 3); S1P did not (p 0.053, so the text's "did not rescue" rests on a P just above 0.05).
6. **Neurological phenotype is unlike the Wwox-null mouse in time.** Seizures at about 4 months and death from about 5 months versus death by the first weeks in the Wwox-null; hypomyelination at P20 in both.
7. **Unreported finding in the deposit:** GFAP is about 3.2-fold higher in the whole-brain proteome of the knockout, an astrocyte-reactivity signal the paper does not discuss; reactive glia are also recorded in the Wwox-null brain (CLAIM 005). A shared feature, not evidence of a shared cause.
8. **The deposit labels do not match the Methods:** the primer table is titled Table S3 and the brain lipid table Table S1, while the Methods refer to Supplementary Table 1 for primers.
