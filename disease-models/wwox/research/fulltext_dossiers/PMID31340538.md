## What the pass added

1. Panel against text: the Results say Wwox protein was detected in all organs examined; Figure 1A marks the heart N.D., which the legend defines as not determined because of low beta-actin (a faint heart band is visible), so the heart is simply not quantified Liver carries the highest relative Wwox of the organs tested (about 2.6 against about 1.2 for brain), then adrenal, kidney and testis; the Discussion reads this as neuroendocrine and metabolic involvement.
2. Nothing metabolic is measured in the lde rat in this paper (no glucose, weight-adjusted intake, lipid or hormone), although the strain is a growth-retarded lethal dwarf with male hypogonadism; the rat therefore adds no support to or against the null-mouse hypoglycemia.
3. The Wwox-null reading of the lde/lde rat rests on blots in which the beta-actin bands in the lde/lde lanes of Figures 2D and 2E are visibly weaker than in the +/+ lanes; the authors also report a faint heavier band (46.2 kDa) and conclude it is non-functional by analogy with human C-terminal frameshifts.
4. Earlier findings stand: preserved neuron number with reduced MAP2, reduced myelination markers and mature oligodendrocytes, reduced (not increased) GFAP and Iba1 in cortex, opposite in sign to the hippocampal gliosis of the 2019 knockout-mouse study; litter reduced to under five pups at PND 5 to improve survival.

## Does not support

A metabolic or autonomic phenotype; that the lde rat is a clean null; a human WWOX-DEE mechanism (rat, male, early-postnatal, cortex only, separate t-tests per age). No commit candidate. Revival triggers: glucose and body-composition data in the lde rat; actin-normalised repeat blots.
