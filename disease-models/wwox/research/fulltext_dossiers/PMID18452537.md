## 3 · What is measured — and at what level

1. **WWOX staining falls with grade and stage — measured, weak-to-moderate in size.** Kendall τ-b: grade −0.319 (P 0.001), stage −0.277 (0.002), size −0.175 (0.040), progression −0.191 (0.028) (L20; the effect sizes exist only in Figure 4B, L22). Null for multiplicity (−0.123), N+ (−0.150, P 0.067), M+ (−0.031) and recurrence (−0.021) (L29, L22).
2. **Squamous-metaplasia carcinomas are the lowest** — 4 of 5 low, with 4 cases of total absence (L15, L16, L17, L23), on n = 5.
3. **Survival — one endpoint of three nominally significant, all unadjusted.** PFS P = 0.0421, recurrence P = 0.54, OS P = 0.053 (L31, L32, L33, L35). The strata are **not** a gradient: the *moderate* group has the best PFS and OS curves, above the strong group (L35).
4. **Normal urothelium stains strongly** — granular, cytoplasmic, non-nuclear (L39, L40, L41).

## 6 · Strength, and transferability to the reference genotype

- **Transferability: T3 / nil.** Adult somatic bladder cancer, tissue IHC. No germline WWOX alteration, no neural tissue, no developmental or neurological readout.
