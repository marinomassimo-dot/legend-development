## What it is
A narrative review with a documented multi-database search (363 included reports in 196 "Study Families", by its own count). It generates no measurement. Everything below is the review's statement about other people's primaries, so nothing here can be promoted to a claim; where a number is carried it is labelled "per the review".

## Statements relevant to a restoration specification (per the review)
- CSF delivery broadens distribution but parenchymal penetration may remain uneven (3.2); intracisternal and intraventricular AAVrh.10-APOE2 in animals gave widespread but variable distribution (3.2).
- High systemic doses gave severe toxicity in large animals; DRG sensory-neuron pathology has emerged across several CNS-directed AAV programmes; transgene detargeting from sensory neurons reduced DRG toxicity in non-human primates, i.e. a product-design lever (5.2). Primary papers behind these two sentences were not read here.
- Intracranial AAV exposure recruited B-cell responses in a dose-dependent manner and impaired later transduction (5.2): re-dosing cannot be assumed.
- Payload biology is a separate safety layer: sustained IL-10 expression helped in one model and harmed in another; an anti-amyloid scFv payload reduced parenchymal amyloid yet increased focal vascular amyloid and haemorrhage (5.2).
- Rodent performance can overestimate translation (AAV-PHP.B not reproduced in non-human primates) (5.1); AAV2-NGF post-mortem spread was about 1 mm.
- Box 1 (four AD programmes, audited registry search of 25 July 2026): one programme dosed by CSF administration at the craniocervical junction at four cohorts (1.4e10, 4.4e10, 1.4e11 gc/mL CSF and 1.4e14 gc fixed dose); evidence is conference-level. Recomputed: 1.4e14 gc at 3.4e11 gc/mL implies about 410 mL of CSF, which is above the usual adult CSF volume; the review's conversion is therefore either using a different denominator or contains an arithmetic slip. I do not carry any dose from Box 1.
