## 2 · Design

CRISPR/Cas9 `Wwox` **P47T** knock-in mice, **heterozygous `Wwox WT/P47T` only** — no homozygote
appears anywhere in this paper — against `Wwox WT/WT` littermates. Mean age 2.6 months,
**n = 6 male + 3 female per group**, randomised to **LPS 10 mg/kg i.p.** or PBS, single endpoint
**12 h**. OFT before treatment and post-injection at **four hours by the Methods and six hours by
the Results** — the source contradicts itself about the x-axis of its only behavioural endpoint,
and the panels are "Post-Pre" differences; murine sepsis severity score (MSS) every 2 h; brain
IHC (Iba1, GFAP, NeuN, CC3), cortical qPCR (IL-1β, IL-6, TNF-α), plasma cytometric bead assay.
**The line was made elsewhere:** *"generously donated by CMarcelo Aldaz"*, bred and genotyped at
Indiana University with the same KASP assay, under **Indiana University**'s IACUC.

## 3 · What it establishes

1. **`DATO` — Sepsis severity rises faster in the heterozygote.** MSS slopes **0.6161/h** vs
   **0.4262/h**, difference **p = 0.001617** (Fig. 2). This is the paper's strongest and cleanest
   between-genotype result.
   🔴 **It is a severity SCORE, and the paper's mortality language has no measured outcome behind
   it.** Methods: *"The risk for mortality was measured using a simple linear regression model."*
   **No mortality outcome is reported anywhere** — no deaths, no survival curve, no humane-endpoint
   count; every animal was euthanised at a fixed 12 h. What is modelled is the Murine Sepsis
   Severity score, which the paper's own ref 19 offers as a *correlate* of septic shock and
   mortality. So "risk for mortality" here is a regression on a clinical score at 12 h, and the
   Conclusion's *"increases disease severity"* and the Discussion's framing of *"sepsis mortality"*
   rest on that correlate, never on an observed death.
2. **`DATO` — Cortical astrogliosis is both genotype-dependent and challenge-dependent.** GFAP⁺
   density: LPS-vs-LPS **p = 0.0005**, and the heterozygote's own LPS response **p = 0.0040**, with
   wild type flat (0.9786) and no baseline difference (0.9147) (Fig. 5B). **The one endpoint where a
   genotype difference under challenge is accompanied by a demonstrated response to the challenge.**
3. **`DATO` — Cortical TNF-α mRNA is higher in the challenged heterozygote** (LPS-vs-LPS
   **p = 0.0016**; het PBS→LPS **p < 0.0001**; WT PBS→LPS ns at 0.4659) (Fig. 7C).
4. **`DATO` — Microglial density is higher in the challenged heterozygote in both regions**, cortex
   **p = 0.0282** and hippocampus **p = 0.0490** (Figs. 3B, 4B) — with the qualification in §5 that
   neither genotype shows a significant LPS response of its own.
5. **`DATO` — A hippocampal, non-neuronal apoptosis difference:** CC3 area LPS-vs-LPS
   **p = 0.0226**, while the CC3⁺ *neuronal* fraction is `ns` everywhere (Fig. 9B–C). The authors
   say so themselves: *"not affecting neurons"*, cell type undetermined.
6. **`DATO` — Two clean regional negatives:** hippocampal astrocytes are `ns` on **all four**
   brackets (Fig. 6B) and cortical apoptosis is `ns` on both panels (Fig. 8B–C).

## 4 · What it does NOT support

- **Not "increased sickness behaviour versus wild type".** Both OFT measures are **non-significant
  between the LPS groups** — distance **p = 0.9970**, time in centre **p = 0.2207**. The genotype
  signal is that the LPS-vs-PBS contrast reaches significance in the heterozygote (0.0312) and not
  in wild type (0.9352): **a difference in significance, not a significant difference.** The
  Conclusion's *"increase in sickness behavior"* and the abstract's *"more significant response to
  sepsis"* (abstract-only, therefore never used as a locator here) are not carried by Fig. 1.
- **Not "increased pro-inflammatory cortical cytokines"** in the plural: IL-1β LPS-vs-LPS
  **p = 0.0837** and IL-6 **p = 0.5967**. **TNF-α alone** carries that claim.
- **Not a systemic hyper-inflammatory phenotype — the periphery runs the other way.** None of the
  six cytokines measured differs between genotypes: IL-6 **0.2778**, TNF-α **0.2557**, MCP-1
  **0.3940**, IFN-γ **0.9279**, IL-12p70 **0.7772**, IL-10 `ns`. The *significant* plasma IL-6 and
  TNF-α responses are **wild type's** (0.0168, 0.0025), while the heterozygote's own responses for
  those two are `ns` (0.5314, 0.1469) and its own significant responses are MCP-1 (0.0027) and
  IFN-γ (0.0430). The heterozygote scores as more severely septic while mounting the **weaker**
  measured cytokinaemia.
  🔴 **Six of these numbers were re-mapped on 2026-09-13 and the conclusion did not move.** As
  first written this sentence assigned 0.5314, 0.1469 and 0.8591 to the between-genotype
  comparisons; those are the **heterozygote's own LPS responses**. Supplemental Figure 1 uses a
  different bracket layout from Figures 3–9 — lower-left is wild type's own response, **top** is the
  heterozygote's, right is between genotypes — and the layout is pinned independently of bracket
  geometry by the printed legend for S1C and S1D. Under the correct mapping every between-genotype
  value is still non-significant, so the claim this sentence makes was true throughout; the numbers
  offered as its evidence were partly the wrong ones, in a sentence whose whole point is which
  genotype a number belongs to. Verified at source and confirmed by a blind locator audit over all
  eighteen printed bracket values.
- **Not mechanism.** No barrier permeability measured (*"The role of blood brain barrier
  dysfunction was not examined"*), no pathway intervention, no WWOX protein or transcript measured
  **anywhere in this paper** — the "partial loss of function" is genotype by construction, never
  verified as reduced Wwox in these animals.
- **Not mortality, in a paper that models it.** The Methods declare a mortality-risk regression;
  **no mortality is measured** — fixed 12 h euthanasia for every animal, no deaths, no survival
  curve, no humane endpoint reported. MSS is a validated correlate (ref 19), not the outcome, so
  nothing here supports a statement about WWOX and sepsis *mortality*.
- **Not a time course.** One 12-h endpoint; the authors name this first among their limitations.
- **Not blinded.** No blinding statement exists anywhere — **searched, none found** — including for
  the observer-scored MSS.
- **Not powered, and the exclusions are asymmetric.** **7 of 36 animals removed by ROUT**: two LPS
  het, one PBS het, **three LPS wild types**, one PBS WT. Three of nine removed from the wild-type
  LPS arm — the comparator against which every between-genotype claim is made.
  **And the removal's scope is unstated while the panel counts do not reconcile with a single
  global exclusion:** post-removal group sizes would be 8/8/6/7, yet Fig. 4B plots 5–7 points per
  group, Fig. 9B 4–6 and Fig. 9C about 5. Further animals or samples are therefore missing from
  individual endpoints with no accounting, which is material to how much weight **p = 0.0490**
  (Fig. 4B) and **p = 0.0226** (Fig. 9B) can carry.
  **No sample-size rationale is offered anywhere, and no analysis is disaggregated by sex** —
  6 male + 3 female per group in an LPS model with well-described sex differences in the response.
- **Not a homozygote result.** Nothing here speaks to the P47T/P47T phenotype.

🔴 **The recurring shape, stated once:** in **five** of the six quantitative brain and plasma
endpoints, a significant *between-genotype* difference under LPS coexists with **no significant LPS
response in either genotype**, or with the response present only in the heterozygote. Cortical
astrocytes (Fig. 5) are the exception. This does not make the findings wrong — a between-group
difference at a fixed timepoint is a real observation — but it does mean the paper's own framing,
*"more severe neuroinflammation **in response to LPS**"*, is stronger than most of its panels, and
that **baseline** differences the design cannot exclude (Fig. 3B's 0.1165, and the authors' own
*"possibility of baseline inflammation"*) remain a live alternative explanation.

## 6 · Two citation defects in its use of the other paper of this lot

Both concern the **cited source**, not the new data, and both are checkable:

1. 🔴 **The GSEA comparator does not exist.** The Introduction states the GSEA showed enrichment
   *"in the WwoxP47T/P47T mice compared to the **Wwox WT/P47T littermates**"*. In the cited paper
   (ref 17 = PMID 36828035, which I read to completion earlier today) the RNA-seq compared
   **WT/WT vs P47T/P47T**, *n* = 5 per group, in PFC/CTX/HPC and CB. **No heterozygote was ever
   sequenced.**
2. 🔴 **The GSEA is attributed to the wrong reference.** The Discussion cites it as ref **13**,
   which this preprint's own reference list gives as *Aldaz CM, Ferguson BW, Abba MC, "WWOX at the
   crossroads of cancer, metabolic syndrome related traits and CNS pathologies", BBA 2014* — a
   **review published nine years before the P47T mouse existed**. The result is in ref 17.

And the inherited premise: *"The heterozygous Wwox WT/P47T mice did not differ significantly from
the Wwox WT/WT littermates"* is asserted from ref 17 and is **the load-bearing baseline of this
entire design**. My reading of ref 17 qualifies it: its Suppl. Table 4 gives heterozygote
cholestasis **22%** and nephropathy **72%** against **0%** and 14% in wild type with no statistics
offered, and its Fig. 2d carries a **significant** heterozygote-above-wild-type sociability
bracket. **`PREMISE: INFERENZA`**, not `DATO`.

## 7 · Transfer verdict toward the reference genotype (WWOX-DEE class)

**`IPOTESI`, T3 with two boundaries.** The clinically interesting proposition — that a *single*
impaired WWOX allele is phenotypically silent until an inflammatory challenge arrives — is
**generated** here and **not established**: it rests on a preprint, one timepoint, *n* ≤ 9 with
asymmetric exclusions, and a between-group pattern mostly unaccompanied by a within-genotype
challenge response. Boundary one: the allele is a **WW1-domain missense that still produces
full-length protein** — presence, not abundance, no WWOX protein having been measured in these
animals at all — not
the reference genotype's lesion. Boundary two: **no WWOX measurement exists in these animals**, so
"partial loss of function" is an inference from genotype throughout.

**Negatives with revival triggers** (destination `dismissal_ledger_current`):
- *Rejected:* that this paper shows heightened **systemic** inflammation in the heterozygote.
  **`REVIVAL_TRIGGER`:** a time-course plasma panel, or any peripheral endpoint with a significant
  between-genotype difference.
- *Rejected:* that it shows increased **sickness behaviour** versus wild type.
  **`REVIVAL_TRIGGER`:** an OFT or equivalent with a significant between-genotype contrast, or a
  powered re-analysis of these data without the three excluded wild-type LPS animals.
- *Rejected:* that it establishes a **mechanism**. **`REVIVAL_TRIGGER`:** a barrier-permeability or
  pathway-intervention experiment in this line.
