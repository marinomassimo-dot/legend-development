## What the paper did

One affected child: exome sequencing with ExomeDepth CNV calling, exon-6 qPCR dosage in five relatives, 30× genome
sequencing (CNVnator, BreakDancer), then gap PCR and Sanger across three breakpoints with segregation.

## Genotype class and what was measured

- WES called a **homozygous exon 6 deletion**; qPCR gave exon-6 dosage about 0 in the child and about 0.37-0.39 in
  three carriers (Figure 1b inspected).
- WGS + gap PCR: one allele carries a **177 kb deletion of exons 6-8** (c.517_1056del, in-frame, predicted
  His173_Met352del); the other carries **two separate deletions** — 13.26 kb wholly within intron 5 and 53.9 kb removing
  **exon 6 only** (c.517_605del, out-of-frame, predicted frameshift). I checked all three intervals against Ensembl
  GRCh38 exon coordinates: the exon-6 deletion ends before exon 7; the intron-5 deletion touches no exon.
- **Genotype class:** predicted out-of-frame exon-6 null / in-frame exons 6-8 deletion. **No RNA, no protein**; the
  residual function of the in-frame allele is "theoretically" stated.
- So the exome call was **right about exon 6 dosage** and **blind to the heterozygous loss of exons 7-8** and to phase.

## Phenotype axes

Onset day 15; valproate and topiramate ineffective (only two drugs named); 10-15 seizures/day; no rolling, sitting or
speech; MRI white-matter hyperintensity and delayed myelination; death at 2.5 years from persistent seizures; height
−6 SD.
