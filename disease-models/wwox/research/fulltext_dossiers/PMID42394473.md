## What the paper did
Retrospective single-centre cohort, 2021–2024: 250 children (115 F / 135 M) with epilepsy of unknown etiology (structural, infectious, immune and metabolic causes excluded), WES (n = 104) or CES (n = 146) on blood DNA, proband-only (no trio), ACMG/AMP classification, no CNV calling. Diagnostic yield 89/250 (35.6 %) P/LP; 55 more carried VUS.

## Internal inconsistencies the paper itself carries
1. **"R" in Table 2 has two incompatible definitions.** The Table 2 footnote says R marks "the 12 patients in whom genetic diagnosis directly informed therapeutic decisions"; Section 3.5 says the 12 are SCN1A (9), SLC2A1 (2), MECP2 (1) and that "6 were drug‐resistant (indicated with 'R' in Table 2)". Table 2 marks far more than 12 rows with R (including WWOX row 29, GRIN, TBCD, KCNQ2 rows). WWOX is not among the 12 therapy-informed genes, so the R on row 29 cannot mean "therapy informed". INFERENZA: R most plausibly means drug-resistant, but the paper does not let this be read as fact.
2. Table 2 footnote: "Full table includes all 98 P/LP and 47 clinically relevant VUS cases" vs 89 P/LP and 55 VUS in the text and Table 1.
3. Autosomal recessive share: 20/89 (22 %) in abstract and results; "26%" in the self-test question.
