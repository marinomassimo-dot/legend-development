## Phenotype axes

Seizures within hours of birth (tonic, spasms, eyelid myoclonus, nystagmus); levetiracetam then carbamazepine; first EEG
normal, then multifocal discharges; MRI at 10 days normal for age (Figure 1 inspected); birth head circumference −2.07
SDS called microcephaly; death after about 3 months from cardiac arrest with pulmonary infection.
