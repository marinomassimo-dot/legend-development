## What it says that touches H1 question 3 (DATO about the perspective)

- Haemoglobin increments do not reliably improve tissue oxygenation; the response depends on which step of the oxygen cascade is limiting (lung, flow, microcirculation, extraction, mitochondria).
- At the cellular level, one cited multicentre observational study found mitochondrial oxygen tension and consumption did not change consistently after transfusion and were not strongly tied to haemoglobin; many patients started with normal mitochondrial oxygen tension.
- Table 1: cellular hypoxia may persist despite normal systemic oxygenation; NIRS has no validated absolute threshold; the proposed trigger values are not validated against hard outcomes.

## What it does NOT support (the negative is the product)
