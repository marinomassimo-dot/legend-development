## 2 · 🔴 The principal finding — the field's taxonomy structurally excludes the reference genotype

The document's opening paragraph divides fragile sites in two:

> *"**Rare** fragile sites are seen in a small proportion of individuals, **inherited in a Mendelian
> manner and are associated with human genetic disorders**. In contrast, **common** fragile sites
> (CFS) are present in all individuals, represent the largest class of fragile sites and are
> **largely involved in cancer**."*

**WWOX spans FRA16D, a *common* fragile site. The genotype class this repository models is a
Mendelian, recessively inherited human genetic disorder of WWOX.** Under the taxonomy this entire
literature organises itself by, the WWOX germline disorder falls on the **wrong side** of the
dichotomy.

**This is not a criticism of the sentence**, which is a correct statement of fragile-site
classification. It is the **structural explanation for a measurement**, and the measurement is what

## 4 · 🔴 Cross-document — and it exists only because both wave-5 papers were read

**Here, in 2014**, the editor reports chapter 6 as having **concluded**:

> *"It is finally concluded that fragility of CFS is **unlikely only a structural 'passive'
> incident** but an event which might have a profound biological effect."*

**Thirteen months later**, the same senior author publishes PMID 27551470, which frames
passive-versus-active as a **live two-sided controversy** — *"The role of CFSs in cancer is
debatable and two major views dominate the discussion"* — and cites **that very chapter** (ref 6)
for the "sensors" proposal it builds on.

🔴 **Stated carefully, because the overclaim is available and would be wrong.** One special issue
leaning one way does not settle a field controversy, and an author is entitled to present a question
as open. **What is recorded is the narrower fact:** the framing moved from *concluded* to
*debatable* inside one author's own output across thirteen months, and the 2015 piece's own citation
for the point is the chapter described here as having concluded it.

**Four of PMID 27551470's seventeen references are chapters of this issue** (refs 6, 10, 16, 17).
The 2015 editorial is, to a substantial degree, a restatement of the 2014 issue this document
introduces.

## 8 · What a later reader should not take from this dossier

establishes is that its value to this repository is entirely structural**: a nine-item queue, a
dated statement of what the field was not studying, and a taxonomic reason — measured twice, on two
documents — why WWOX's phenotype is absent from the literature where WWOX's mechanism lives.
