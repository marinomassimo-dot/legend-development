## What the paper did
Retrospective two-hospital IESS series (June 2017 – May 2024), ILAE 2022 criteria (hypsarrhythmia not required). Etiology by ILAE 2017 categories, with genetic-structural split out. WES as main test. Seizure control on a four-level clinical scale (complete remission / effective > 50 % / poor < 50 % / ineffective). Treatment analysed only as **ACTH (n = 242) vs non-ACTH (n = 119)**.
