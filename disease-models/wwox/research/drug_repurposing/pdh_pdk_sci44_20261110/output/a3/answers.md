# SCI44 package A, step A3: answers (producer og-q, 2026-11-10)

Non-canonical research material. Not medical advice; no dose, schedule or compound recommendation; reference genotype class only; nothing here is a claim on WWOX. Labels DATO / INFERENZA. Row ids refer to `activity_primary.tsv` (A, V, C, D rows), `decisive.tsv` (A3D01-14) and `symbol_check.tsv` (SC).

**Q3a, AZD7545.** The primary source of the repository numbers (PMID 14641019) is a three-page Biochem Soc Trans symposium paper, indexed to the rat; it was not reachable (Cloudflare 403), so its species, construct, assay and the PDK2 8.19 stay unverified (A3D04). The companions give the measured picture: human SUMO-PDK1 IC50 87 nM and PDK3 600 nM with E1p on the E2p/E3BP core (A01-A02, Fig 2A viewed); without the core AZD7545 stimulates PDK1 1.3-fold and PDK3 10-fold (A05-A06); core-free PDK4 is stimulated about 2.8-fold (A07). PDK3 and PDK4 are therefore not "not tested": they were tested later and the sign follows the E2 scaffold. Deciding rows: A3D02, A3D03, A3D05.

**Q3b, VER-246608.** Table 1 reproduces the repository values exactly as IC50 35, 84 (n=2), 40, 91 nM (pIC50 7.46, 7.08, 7.40, 7.04 are recomputations, V01-V05): DELFIA, E1 substrate without E2, 5 uM ATP, truncated His-tagged constructs, species not named for the enzymes. ATP-competitive (FP, crystal in the PDK-2 ATP pocket). In human PC-3 cells it lowers E1 alpha Ser293 phosphorylation (266 nM at 0% serum) and raises lysate PDH activity to about 138-148% of control (Fig 3A viewed), with effects on lactate and glucose only under glucose-depleted conditions and near-complete loss of phosphorylation. The AZD7545 and VER-246608 numbers are not comparable (A3D08). Deciding rows: A3D06, A3D07, A3D08.

**Q3c, dichloroacetate.** Millimolar inhibitor of all isoforms in vitro, isoform-dependent: apparent IC50 0.29 mM (basal) and 0.48 mM (core) for human PDK2 (C01-C02); 40 mM leaves 4% (PDK1) and 28% (PDK3) with the core (C03-C04); Ki 0.2 mM (PDK2) to 8 mM (PDK3) at abstract level of a 1998 rat-isoenzyme paper (C07). It binds the N-terminal helix bundle (pyruvate site), not the ATP pocket (C09). Neural and brain DCA results are the A2 rows (copied, not reread); the MELAS trial is copied at abstract level from E29-E31. Enzyme inhibition, cellular PDH activation and clinical effect are kept apart (A3D10). The ChEMBL curated DCA row names two mechanism references that are not isoform measurements (an in-vitro cancer-cell paper and a case report).

**Q3d, symbol check.** The label PDK1 means the other kinase (PDPK1) for celecoxib (the source says "PDK1 (encoded by gene PDPK1)"), GlcNAc-selenazoline and 3-hydroxyanthranilic acid (signalling papers), and for OSU-03012 by pharmacology while the DGIdb identifier of that row is the dehydrogenase kinase; only the dichloroacetic acid DTC row is the dehydrogenase kinase (SC01-SC05, A3D11). Devimistat: targets are the PDH and KGDH complexes; the kinase link is declared as stimulation of PDKs 1-4 (sponsor-affiliated paper), so the four DGIdb PDK rows carry no inhibitor evidence (A3D12). No typed row is PDPK1: ChEMBL's DCA target is CHEMBL2096665 with Q15118, Q15119, Q15120, Q16654; the GtoPdb records for AZD7545 and VER-246608 were not reachable (login), so their identity rests on the HGNC route (labelled fallback) and, for VER-246608, on the paper (A3D13).

**Q3e, activity-only pointers.** The pointer rows are one measurement per ChEMBL record fanned over the four components of the family target (A3D14): nandrolone has one IC50 of 120 uM (doc "Triterpene and diterpene inhibitors of PDK", 1999; target = the dehydrogenase kinase family, species and isoform unrecorded, paper not reached, not a PDPK1 mis-assignment); the sodium salt has three family rows (two censored IC50 > 1 mM on porcine enzyme recorded against the human family, one functional EC50 130 uM of doubtful species); the acid has one censored IC50 > 2 mM, plus separate single-isoform rows from a 2017 document (PDK1 1,025, PDK2 504, PDK3 5,560, PDK4 727 uM, ELISA). Greater-than values are not potencies.

**What the primary sources change in the repository:** species of the AZD7545 rows (unsupported), the missing PDK3/PDK4 AZD7545 rows (measured elsewhere, opposite sign without the scaffold), the identity of four DGIdb PDK1 rows (OSU-03012, GlcNAc-selenazoline, 3-hydroxyanthranilic acid, celecoxib), the direction of the devimistat rows, and the reading of the 4 and 12 "rows" as single measurements.
