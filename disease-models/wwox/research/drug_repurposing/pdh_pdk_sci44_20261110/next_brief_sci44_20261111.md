## The question and why
**How pH-sensitive is the resting conductance carried by TASK-3 (Kcnk9), TASK-1 (Kcnk3) and TREK-1 (Kcnk2) in juvenile (P10-P25) neocortical pyramidal neurons, over the pH range a lactate-rich slice could reach, and which tools act on each with measured selectivity?** Decision it changes: whether the tissue pH measurement already proposed for the GluN2B comparison (SCI42 M01) is also the premise of a K2P arm of the K-branch ladder (step 2b), and which K2P tool leads that arm. Information: neuronal TASK-3 and TREK-1 RNA are expressed in P7 mouse neurons (`kchan_expression_sci44_20261111/`); no juvenile layer II/III somatic test of a K2P-selective tool was found in SCI43 and SCI44 searches; the pH sensitivity is background knowledge here, not a sourced statement.

## What a producer should read
1. Primary sources of the pH sensitivity of TASK-1, TASK-3 and TREK-1 (pKa and fraction of current at pH 7.4 against 6.8-7.0; recombinant versus native; heteromers of TASK-1/3) with the conditions.
2. Native evidence in cortical or hippocampal neurons: any resting-conductance, Rin or rest change on acidification and any tool effect (ML365, A1899, PK-THPP, spadin, fluoxetine, zinc, ruthenium red) with age, layer, temperature, pipette ATP, blockers; a drug-name-led search as well as gene-led (the SCI43 lesson).
3. Tool identity: potency with unit and assay, selectivity between K2P subfamilies, and engagement in neurons, for each tool a source used.
4. Which tissue pH range slices reach under the recording conditions of the Wwox paper (3.5 mM K+, 35 C, bicarbonate-buffered ACSF) is a measurement, not a reading; record only what sources state for comparable slices.

## Deliverable and criterion of conclusion
A table of pH sensitivity and tool identity with preparation, age, layer, temperature, solutions and panel locators; ends when each channel has a native juvenile figure or a bounded "not found" with the strings used (gene-led and tool-led). No laboratory result needed.
