## 5. DREAM across metabolism, physiology, development and the earlier results
* **Metabolism to the K branch.** Lactate or pyruvate close KATP (juvenile neocortex, perforated patch) and the K-branch implied reversal of the Wwox loss is near E_K: a connection **known in the literature but unused in LEGEND** (relation already known; new combination in the repository). The 2 mM ATP of the Wwox internal says the published values cannot be read as lactate effects; the connection survives only for ATP-undisturbed recordings.
* **A new chain through pH (IPOTESI; background knowledge, not read in a source this session).** Raised lactate means lower tissue pH; two-pore-domain channels of the TASK family are closed by extracellular acidification and TASK-3 RNA is neuron-specific at P7 (17 FPKM); a TASK-3 closure would raise Rin and depolarise with an E_K reversal, the same sign as the Wwox pattern. **The tissue pH measurement already proposed for the GluN2B comparison (SCI42, M01) would then serve three questions at once**: whether a pH-dependent GluN2B tool (NP10679) is relevant, whether a TASK-type share exists, and whether lactate is raised in the KO slice. Not the same question; the same sample and the same condition record.
* **Development.** Human astrocyte PDK4 rises with maturation (fetal 1.42, adult 22.59 FPKM) and Kir4.1 is high in astrocytes and the oligodendrocyte lineage; the Wwox preparation is P13-17, a window of glial maturation. Cell-type attribution is therefore the common design requirement of INV-07 (PDK per class), INV-18 (Ba2+ difference current) and INV-05 (NAM removable share): marker-resolved or sorted material, synaptic blockade, or a cell-specific control.
* **Results kept apart.** The SCI43 degeneracy (conductance, size, shunt) is unchanged by SCI44; SCI44's ATP finding narrows which metabolic reading can be tested in the published preparation.
