## Points asked for

(a) HDAC5 knockdown study: the chain states what the abstract states - knockdown in human primary muscle cells, GLUT1 down, GLUT4 and glucose uptake up; the only drug arm is the pan-HDAC inhibitor Scriptaid, with H3 acetylation, GLUT4, uptake, flux, GLUT1 not named. Nothing equates knockdown with a drug in the chain, although the paper's own text writes "inhibition of HDAC5". The one inexact line is the module README quoted in C03.
(b) HDAC4 and GLUT1 opposite sign: supported for PEDV cells and mouse heart models; non-neural; HDAC4 not HDAC5; the row attributes MEF2A to all three, wrong for 33164260. Contrary result missed (omission 1).
(c) Histone acetylation and class IIa engagement: the copurified-class-I point is in the abstract but about a recombinant enzymatic assay and hedged ("suggesting"); the extension to cellular histone acetylation as an engagement readout is the package's inference and E18 shows a class IIa compound raising histone acetylation.
(d) SLC2A1 direction in WWOX-deficient systems: organoid higher, mouse P17 lower, P47T nominally higher, MEF/tumour cells higher (uptake and GLUT1): all supported by the records; second human neural system (hNPC) omitted.
(e) Absence statements: C09 (bounded search, reproduced, OK); C03 (abstract-level absence, stated as such); C15 (false beyond the ChEMBL mechanism table); C16 and C13 unknown by status.

## Omissions that matter

1. 37843550 (HCC cells, HDAC4/IL-6/STAT3): Rh4 lowered HDAC4 together with glycolysis, LDHA and GLUT1, and forced HDAC4 expression reversed Rh4's effects on glycolysis - HDAC4 goes with higher glycolysis/GLUT1 there. It is one of the five hits in the package's own search (C09), yet C04 presents the HDAC4 sign as opposite to C01 without it. The HDAC4-GLUT1 sign is mixed within the paralog, not only "context-dependent" in the abstract sense of C04.
2. needs.json O5: shWWOX human neural progenitors and neurons also show a HIF-type subset including SLC2A1 up; C10's "three systems disagree" omits a second human system on the organoid side.
3. C15's absence of an isoform-level HDAC5 action ignores 42 compounds with gene-level HDAC5 claims in the catalogue.
4. E18 (LMK235 raised histone acetylation) contradicts the control row's expectation that a class IIa compound leaves H3 acetylation unchanged.
5. C08's agree/disagree count does not match its cited rows; MC1568/LMK235 selectivity dispute not carried into the row.
