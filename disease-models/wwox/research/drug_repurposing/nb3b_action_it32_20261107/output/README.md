## Answer first

**Outcome: earlier pharmacological link weakened; molecule question abstained, with a reopening condition. The action and the desirability are both unresolved.** Not "action supported".

1. DATO, mechanistic: the premise source (PMID 22991226) tested one thing, HDAC5 knockdown (protein amount) in human primary muscle cells. GLUT1 expression fell; GLUT4 expression and glucose uptake rose. The drug arm was Scriptaid, a pan-HDAC inhibitor, whose named outputs were H3 acetylation, GLUT4, uptake and flux; GLUT1 is not named for it. No catalytic-dead, localisation or partner arm exists. Depth: abstract only, the full text is not reachable by an open route. INFERENZA: the edge is a knockdown association in muscle and says nothing about which function of HDAC5 matters.
2. DATO: the same class IIa family has the opposite sign on GLUT1 in other tissues (HDAC4 present lowers glucose uptake or GLUT1 in some non-neural systems, and goes with higher GLUT1 in hepatocellular carcinoma cells, PMID 37843550: sign context-dependent; abstracts only, HDAC4, not neural). No source was found for any HDAC5 or HDAC4 manipulation with SLC2A1 read in a neural cell (bounded: Q08).
3. DATO/INFERENZA, catalogue: the 12 "inhibitor" compounds recorded at HDAC5 reach it through one protein-family mechanism row (ChEMBL "Histone deacetylase", 11 genes), the same 12 for HDAC1, 4, 6 and 9. The catalogue holds no isoform-level HDAC5 action, no degrader and no localisation or partner-binding agent. Compound X (TMP269, CHEMBL3110004) is not in the catalogue by that id.
4. INFERENZA, controls: histone H3 acetylation is a weak reading of engagement of a class IIa enzyme (class IIa enzymes are very inefficient on acetyl-lysine, and apparent activity in a recombinant assay may come from copurified class I enzymes; PMID 17956988, abstract only; the re-audit notes that a class IIa inhibitor, LMK235, raised histone acetylation in SH-SY5Y cells, PMID 34119632, so bulk acetylation can change for reasons other than engagement of the enzyme of interest, and no change cannot be assumed either). The HDAC6 inhibitor is a chemotype control that needs its own qualification, not a negative control. The class-I-only inhibitor gives an ambiguous result when it reproduces the knockdown. IPOTESI: add cellular engagement on endogenous HDAC5 before the compound arm, a catalytic gain-of-function variant as direction control, and a HIF readout beside SLC2A1.
5. Function versus desirability, R3: unresolved, no direction chosen. Higher SLC2A1 in WWOX loss is seen in the human organoid (transcript; uptake not measured), in shWWOX human neural progenitors and neurons (HIF-type subset, needs.json O5) and in non-neural Wwox-deficient cells with higher glucose uptake, but is lower in mouse P17 brain and nominal in P47T; whether it is compensatory or harmful is unmeasured. Lowering has a documented harm in a different lesion (GLUT1 haploinsufficiency). A change in SLC2A1 is not a benefit.
6. R5 (generalisation): the rule "association is not action" survives the HDAC3-myelin case, with a narrower form (below).

## R1 in detail (PMID 22991226, abstract only)
Cells: human primary muscle cells, knockdown also after differentiation onset. Output: GLUT1 expression lower (mRNA or protein not stated), GLUT4 expression and promoter activity higher, glucose uptake higher, PGC-1 alpha unchanged. Function tested: amount. Action: knockdown. The authors write "inhibition of HDAC5" for it, which the abstract does not support as catalytic inhibition. The module sentence "the one drug was reported for GLUT4 only" is consistent with the abstract for GLUT4 but the abstract is silent on GLUT1 for the drug arm, so unmeasured and unreported cannot be separated. It is not promoted to a verified fact. Net uptake rose while GLUT1 fell, so in muscle a lower GLUT1 output did not lower uptake (inference).

## R3 in detail
Observed: SLC2A1 higher in the organoid (4 v 2 libraries, maturity confound), lower in P17 mouse brain, nominal in P47T, higher with higher uptake in non-neural Wwox-deficient cells (PMID 25012504, complete full text in the ledger, abstract used here). Unknown: GLUT1 protein, surface level and uptake in WWOX-deficient neural cells. What would resolve it: paired gain and loss arms of SLC2A1 in isogenic WWOX-deficient human neural cells with a function endpoint; HIF1A and EPAS1 beside it; HDAC5 amount, nuclear fraction and catalytic status there. INV-07 (PDH) and INV-06 (myelin) do not decide the direction; the INV-07 movement note already rules out one class-I proposal to lower SLC2A1.

## R4: molecules coherent with the action (conditional)
No direction, no established function, so no compound is named as coherent. Branches, each with its missing premise: (a) lowering wanted and catalytic function carries the output: a class IIa catalytic-site inhibitor would be coherent; the catalogue holds none by an isoform-level row; TMP269 (CHEMBL3110004; DGIdb-only entry without a ChEMBL id, kept separate) has recombinant-domain values only (HDAC4 130, HDAC5 80, HDAC7 40, HDAC9 20 nM, one ChEMBL document, not a cellular result), no SLC2A1 measure, and a precedent of being inert where knockdown acts; the cardiomyocyte HDAC5 and HDAC4 papers give the opposite sign (inhibition raises the output). (b) the output is carried by amount or localisation: an inhibitor is not the matching action; a degrader or nuclear-exclusion agent would be, and none is recorded. (c) the output is carried by HDAC3-linked scaffolding: class I compounds are the coherent action and are the contrast arm, with the myelin counter-premise of IT29. Pracinostat (CHEMBL1851943) stays a conditional class I-type reading. MC1568, LMK235 and TMP195 are absent from the catalogue.

## R5: second case, HDAC3 and oligodendrocyte maturation (`phenotype_first_it29_20261105/p3_B`, rows B10 and B11, PMID 26859354, partial full text, reused)
Test chosen because it could contradict the rule: HDAC3-directed inhibitors agreed in sign with genetic Hdac3 loss (Olig2 down), which looks like association predicting action. Result: it does not contradict. A catalytically inactive NCoR/HDAC3 complex did not reproduce the fate switch, apicidin is not HDAC3-selective, and the inhibitor readout (Olig2, a specification marker) differs from the genetic readout (fate). In the arm C comparison the target-level sign was right for 7 of 11 reached compounds, 5 independent of the signing study, retrospective. Narrowed rule: an association can predict the sign of a marker; it does not establish which function (catalytic, scaffold, amount) produces it. The case of the qualification package (it22 p4) was not opened.
