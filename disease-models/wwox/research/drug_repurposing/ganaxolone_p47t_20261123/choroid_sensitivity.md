# Post hoc anatomy check, declared before opening independent marker values

The hippocampus score was negative; Ttr and Col3a1 were the largest increases in the down arm. This motivated a check for choroid plexus contribution to dissection or biology, a cell source absent from the seven-class reference. Ttr carries a weight in the outcome and must be excluded from the covariate. Misleading choroid plexus carryover in rodent hippocampus was documented by the primary study at doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0142195.

Use the other four genes listed together with Ttr in that paper: Aqp1, Prlr, Kcne2, Folr1. Check for overlap with either signature arm and exclude any overlapping covariate genes. Compute an equal-weight mean log expression, and record its association with Ttr and with the outcome after subtracting within-sex/genotype cell means. Compare genotype coefficients with and without this covariate for the complete signature, a Ttr-omitted signature and a version omitting both Ttr and Col3a1. Sex is effect-coded around 0 and its interaction with genotype is included, so the genotype coefficient is the average across sexes. Show all one-animal omissions and residual degrees of freedom. If removal of the shared anatomical component substantially attenuates the effect or changes the sign, the data support an anatomical explanation as a competitor; adjustment cannot identify dissection artefact, rule out true genotype-related change in this compartment, or establish an intrinsic microglial state.
