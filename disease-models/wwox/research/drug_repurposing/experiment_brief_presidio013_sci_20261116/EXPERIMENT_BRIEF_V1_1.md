Written for: the operator and a laboratory that holds a Wwox-deficient slice preparation; IT does BLOCK-1; ADV keeps the record. Non-canonical working note. Not medical advice: mechanism matching only, no dose, no schedule, no recommendation to use any agent; concentrations are PUBLISHED TOOL CONCENTRATIONS with their source, never recommendations. This file merges the brief of 2026-11-16 (V1, `EXPERIMENT_BRIEF.md`, commit 5af8e20c0) and its Addenda 1 to 4 into one text; **the original and the addenda are unchanged and are the record**; where this file and one of them differ, they are listed in the changelog (section 11). ## 1. The decision page
**Decision it serves.** The informed matrix cannot rank the antiseizure mechanism classes: at seizure P13-P15 five classes tie (V1: 5 of 15; V2 under rule v2: 4 of 12), all contributing threads come from one network (Breton 2021; Aqeilan-Carlen), and no literature search found an independent origin for the three threads that matter (NMDAR dependence, reduced inhibition, intrinsic excitability). The tie can be broken only by measurement.
**The experiment.** In Wwox neuron-knockout slices at matched age (P13-P17), one mechanism representative per class is tested head to head on the SAME spontaneous-burst readout, against a time-matched vehicle and d-APV (the published positive control), in neocortex (36 of 42 knockout slices burst, 0 of 11 wild-type; Breton 2021) and hippocampus (no bursts), with a matched trigger in wild-type slices or an evoked-current control so that 'knockout-specific' can be told from 'generic', at TWO exposure levels per arm analysed on the ratio scale, reading burst INTERVAL and burst DURATION separately.
**What one pilot decides.** (a) Which class leads (a specific NMDAR pore blocker leaves 1 of 15 on top, a specific GABA-A modulator 2, generic suppression everywhere leaves 4). (b) Whether the V1 top class, GABA-A positive modulators, aggravates (HSCI-2: it RAISES the burst rate). (c) Whether a burst is a two-stage event (H-B1, the judge's only survivor: a T-type trigger sets the interval, an NMDAR sustainer sets the duration). (d) Whether the NMDAR-sensitive share of the current is at least 40% in the knockout (LEAP-2; WO-10).
**The Aldaz question (a measurement that would decide whether it counts as a match).** The only independent signal on the inhibition axis, PV+ interneuron loss (Aldaz 2019), is HIPPOCAMPAL, while the bursts are NEOCORTICAL. Count PV+ and NPY+ interneurons in neocortical layer 2/3 and hippocampus of the same animals, with spontaneous inhibitory currents against an unpooled wild-type control. If neocortex shows the loss, the region confound dissolves and the PV loss counts as a match for GABA-A classes (V1 top set 5 to 2 in a scratch run); if only the hippocampus does, it stays unknown.
**First step, no animals.** Fix the conditions that decide interpretation (bath Mg2+, temperature, trigger, burst definition, window, the uptake condition for any tonic-current readout). **Animals:** about 5 to 29 per genotype per arm for one primary comparison (central 10 to 17), 8 to 29 for the drug-by-genotype contrast, 4 to 57 for the curve-shape interaction depending on the within-animal correlation, 6 to 34 for WO-10; roughly a quarter more for two burst endpoints; the variance is NOT held in the repository, so a pilot of four animals per genotype fixes variance and correlation first. **Stop** if the knockout neocortical slices do not burst under the laboratory's conditions or d-APV does not abolish them.
**What is already in the repository:** the preparation and its numbers (burst interval and duration are read in the source), the experiment ladder X0-X4, tool reviews for AMPA, Cav3 and GABA-A, WO-11, the frozen matrices and the generator. **Open gaps:** a variance for burst suppression, selectivity sources for the non-benzodiazepine GABA-A and sodium-channel arms, a second cannabidiol representative, the mouse line's availability, a second laboratory (section 10).

## 2. Preparation and conditions
* **Model and ages.** Synapsin-I-Cre Wwox knockout and wild-type littermates, both sexes, P13-P17 (Breton 2021, PMID 34634460, PMC8609180; `nmda_inv05_20261107/output/experiment.tsv` X0-X4). Held facts: neocortical bursts 36/42 knockout against 0/11 wild-type slices, absent in hippocampus; pyramidal resting potential -73.62 (KO) against -79.25 mV (WT), no liquid-junction correction; glucose 10 mM; incubation solution 1.3 mM MgSO4; recording-bath Mg2+ not stated; d-APV 50 uM abolished bursts in 5 slices from 3 animals, reversible, no test.
* **Burst definition (resolved from the arithmetic, INFERENZA):** the two printed baseline rates are two statistics: 2.29 bursts/min = 60 s / the median inter-burst interval of 26.2 s; 0.84 [0.57-1.53] bursts/min (min 0.36, max 3.89) is the per-slice frequency of Supplementary Table S1. Name which one is used. Median burst duration 0.66 s [0.53-1.06]; gamma shapes 2.45 (duration, 119 bursts, 18 slices, 9 animals) and 1.43 (interval, 101 intervals).
* **Still to fix first (X0):** bath Mg2+ and temperature; the window; whether the mouse line is obtainable (it appears to come from the Aqeilan laboratory; no contact is made from here).
* **WO-10** (IT15 definition): the NAM-sensitive fraction of the isolated NMDAR current in burst-age slices. The only published number is WO-11 (Mierau 2004, wild-type barrel cortex, evoked EPSC, 3 uM ifenprodil, 14% with interval 0-34% at P13-P15 against 53% at P6-P8; room temperature, 2 mM Mg2+, Cs-based internal with QX-314, bicuculline; pre-incubation at least 40 min and ifenprodil in the bath; holding potential of the comparison not stated). A knockout fraction of at least 40% against the wild-type interval is LEAP-2's prediction.

## 4. Design, readouts and exposure levels
* **Each slice is its own control:** baseline then agent (paired), time-matched vehicle group, washout (d-APV is reversible; the others' reversibility is not held); arm order randomised across slices; blind to genotype and arm; analysis fixed before unblinding. **Units:** animals, slices nested, mixed model.
* **Regions.** Neocortex (layer 2/3 to 5 network): spontaneous bursts. Hippocampus (no spontaneous bursts per Breton 2021): a provoked burst threshold or rate in both genotypes, with a trigger chosen BEFORE the run, matched to the knockout burst rate. Low Mg2+ unblocks NMDAR and would bias the NMDAR arms; a GABA-A block as trigger confounds the GABA-A arms; raised extracellular K+ does neither. The trigger is a declared choice, not a result.
* **Two exposure levels per arm, ratio scale (Addendum 1, ADV H-ADV-5).** Suppression as a proportion of the slice's own baseline; the analysis is the genotype-by-level interaction (curve shape: slope and plateau), not the mean effect; the second level on the same slice (cumulative, with a time-matched vehicle sequence and a washout check) or on a sister slice; levels set by MEASURED fractional block or tool exposure, not by the published concentration alone. A WWOX-specific mechanism predicts a left-shifted or steeper curve; a generic one a parallel curve (my NX1 result: on the absolute scale a null-specific effect cannot be told from a proportional one when baselines differ about 7-fold). No held curve pair exists.
* **Burst INTERVAL and DURATION read separately for every arm (Addendum 4, H-B1).** Per slice, the median inter-burst interval and median burst duration before and after the agent, as paired log ratios, per animal; the gamma shape parameters of both as secondary readouts; arms compared at a matched effect on incidence. Two endpoints per arm: about a quarter more animals per group.
* **Tonic GABA-A current (Addendum 3).** In the layer 2/3 cells already patched for sag, rebound, sIPSC and E_GABA: the holding-current shift on GABA-A block, then with GABA uptake blocked, with the ambient-GABA and uptake condition FIXED and written before the run (SCI42 M07). The condition decides the answer: the held source reports 9 +/- 3 pA rising to 41 +/- 12 pA with uptake blocked (Sebe 2010, mouse S1 layer 5, PMID 20846324). Extra readout in the GABA-A arm: the change of the tonic current under a neurosteroid-type against a synaptic-selective modulator, KO against WT.
* **Genotype-independent controls and other readouts:** evoked NMDAR and AMPA components in both genotypes (burst suppression expressed per unit of NMDAR current removed); drug exposure and achieved block verified, not assumed (INV-05 correction); evoked non-burst responses (sparing of physiology, X3); resting potential and sag/rebound; E_GABA at burst onset and end by perforated patch (HSCI-1); viability.

## 7. What else could decide the GABA axis (region-explicit)
* **Aldaz measurement** (section 1). * **H-ADV-3 (PV-axon myelin):** the held bulk myelin-gene loss in the SAME S deletion is SMALLER in neocortex (Mbp -56%, Plp1 -51%, Mobp -54%) than in hippocampus (-77%, -83%, -83%): not larger where the bursts are; PV-axon myelin itself is not in any held table; cheapest next test: MBP and Caspr staining of PV+ axons in both regions, same sections as the PV counts. * **H-ADV-1 (ErbB4):** not supported at transcript level (Erbb4 -13 to -16%); no new arm. * **HSCI-1 and HSCI-2** are decided by arms in section 3. * **NPY compensation (HSCI-5):** Npy is up in both laboratories but the Y1 and Y2 receptor transcripts are down in both (neuron-like nuclei and P47T), so a regional receptor asymmetry is not seen.

## 8. Sequence and stop rules
1. **Desk, no animals (X0):** bath Mg2+, temperature, burst definition (section 2), window, trigger, region, blinding, randomisation, the uptake condition. 2. **Pilot and WO-10** (four animals per genotype): baseline burst incidence, interval and duration in both regions and both genotypes; the NAM-sensitive fraction; d-APV; the variance and the within-animal correlation. **Stop** if the KO neocortical slices do not burst or d-APV does not abolish them. 3. **Decisive pair:** pore blocker against GABA-A positive modulator (with benzodiazepine) against vehicle, KO and provoked WT, neocortex, two exposure levels, interval and duration separately, tonic current in the GABA-A arm. 4. **Then, if resources are short, in this order:** the sodium-channel arm (AH1), the T-type arm (the H-B1 trigger test), the GluN2B NAM arm, the AMPA arm. 5. **The Aldaz measurement** alongside step 2 (counts, sIPSC, PV-axon myelin sections). BLOCK-1 triage of each representative is IT's; nothing here clears any agent.
