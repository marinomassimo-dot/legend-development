# EXPERIMENT BRIEF (PRESIDIO-013, SCI, 2026-11-16): the head-to-head slice experiment that breaks the five-way tie

Written for: IT and ADV (BLOCK-1 triage is IT's, after this brief), and then a laboratory that holds a Wwox-deficient slice preparation. Non-canonical working note. Not medical advice: mechanism matching only, no dose, no schedule, no recommendation to use any agent; concentrations below are PUBLISHED TOOL CONCENTRATIONS cited with their source, never recommendations. Reference genotype class only. Nothing here edits the frozen matrix (freeze f5b71a3f4). DATO = stated in a held source (named); INFERENZA = my reading; background pharmacology = general knowledge, flagged. Predictive capacity of this brief: none until the experiment is run.

## On one page

**Decision.** The informed arm of the AED matrix ranks five mechanism classes level at seizure P13-P15 (NMDAR pore blockers, GABA-A positive modulators, benzodiazepines, sodium-channel blockers, valproate-type; 5 of 15, `tie_set_weak`), and literature cannot break the tie because the three scoring threads come from one network (origin hunt, PRESIDIO-012). Only a measurement can. **The experiment**: in Wwox neuron-knockout slices at matched age, one mechanism representative per class is tested head to head on the same spontaneous-burst readout, against a time-matched vehicle and d-APV (the published positive control), in neocortex (where Breton 2021 saw bursts: 36 of 42 knockout slices against 0 of 11 wild type) and hippocampus (where it saw none), with a wild-type trigger or evoked-current control so that "knockout-specific" can be told from "generic".

**Why this one.** The VOI pass shows that lowering the pore-blocker link or the GABA-A link by one step each hands the top to the other, so a pore blocker against a GABA-A positive modulator is the pair whose outcome changes the ranking in both directions; the sodium-channel arm tests the aggravation hypothesis AH1 in the same slices; the GluN2B NAM arm tests the age-composition split (LEAP-2).

**What it changes** (table 4): a specific pore blocker alone leaves 1 of 15 on top; a specific GABA-A positive modulator leaves 2 (with benzodiazepines); if everything suppresses and nothing is specific the tie stays at 4; if nothing suppresses, no tested class leads.

**How many animals** (table 5): about 8 to 17 animals per genotype per arm for one primary comparison under assumptions I cannot check from the repository (the between-animal variance of burst suppression is not held); 6 to 34 for WO-10; a four-animal-per-genotype pilot fixes the variance before any arm is sized.

**First step, no animals:** fix the conditions that decide interpretation (bath Mg2+, temperature, trigger, burst definition, window length), then WO-10 with a genotype contrast and the baseline burst incidence in both regions. **Stop** if the knockout neocortical slices do not burst under the laboratory's conditions or d-APV does not abolish them.

**The Aldaz question** (a measurement that would decide whether it counts as a match): the only independent signal on the inhibition axis, PV+ and NPY+ interneuron loss, is HIPPOCAMPAL (Aldaz 2019, full knockout, 2 weeks), while the bursts are NEOCORTICAL. Count PV+ and NPY+ interneurons in neocortical layer 2/3 and in hippocampus of the same animals at the slice age, and record spontaneous inhibitory current rate and amplitude with an unpooled wild-type control in both regions. If neocortex shows the loss and the inhibitory deficit, the region confound dissolves and the PV loss counts as a match for GABA-A classes (top set 5 to 2 in the scratch run of PRESIDIO-012); if only hippocampus does, it stays `unknown`.

## 1. Preparation and conditions (what is already in the repository)
* **Model and ages.** Synapsin-I-Cre Wwox knockout and wild-type littermates, both sexes, P13-P17 (Breton 2021, PMID 34634460, PMC8609180; INV-05 `experiment.tsv` X1 to X4). DATO: neocortical bursts 36/42 KO v 0/11 WT, absent in hippocampus; pyramidal resting potential -73.62 (KO) v -79.25 mV (WT), no liquid-junction correction; glucose 10 mM; incubation solution 1.3 mM MgSO4; recording-bath Mg2+ not stated; baseline burst rate printed as 2.29 per minute in the text and 0.84 per minute in Table S1; d-APV 50 µM abolished bursts in 5 slices from 3 animals, reversible on washout, no test (registry wave notes of CLAIM 021, receipts FTR-20261029-34634460-01/-04/-05).
* **What must be fixed first (INV-05 X0, unresolved):** bath Mg2+ and temperature (they set how much NMDAR current is available); the burst definition and window; the 2.29 against 0.84 per minute discrepancy (measure, do not assume); whether the mouse line is obtainable (it appears to come from the Aqeilan laboratory, as in Repudi 2021; no contact is made from here).
* **WO-10** (IT15 definition): the NAM-sensitive fraction of the isolated NMDAR current in burst-age slices; the only published number is WO-11 (Mierau 2004, wild-type barrel cortex, evoked EPSC, 3 µM ifenprodil, 14% with an interval of 0-34% at P13-P15 against 53% at P6-P8; room temperature, 2 mM Mg2+, Cs-based internal with QX-314, bicuculline; pre-incubation at least 40 min and ifenprodil in the bath; holding potential of the comparison not stated). A knockout fraction of at least 40% against the wild-type interval is LEAP-2's prediction; inside 0-34% rejects it.

## 3. Design, both regions, both genotypes
* **Each slice is its own control:** baseline then drug (paired), with a time-matched vehicle group and washout (d-APV is reversible per Breton 2021; the others' reversibility is not held); arm order randomised across slices; recordings blind to genotype and arm; analysis fixed before unblinding.
* **Neocortex (L2/3 to L5 network):** KO spontaneous bursts are the readout (rate, duration, amplitude). **Hippocampus:** KO does not burst spontaneously per Breton 2021, so the readout is a provoked burst threshold or rate, in both genotypes, chosen BEFORE the experiment and justified. **Trigger caveat:** low Mg2+ unblocks NMDAR and would bias the NMDAR arms; a GABA-A block as trigger confounds the GABA-A arms; a trigger that does neither (for example raised extracellular K+) is preferable, and whichever is used must be matched to the KO burst rate. The trigger is a declared choice, not a result.
* **Genotype-independent control:** the evoked NMDAR and AMPA components in both genotypes, so each class's burst suppression is expressed per unit of NMDAR current removed (INV-05's conditional hypothesis); drug exposure and achieved block verified (INV-05 correction), not assumed.
* **Readouts:** burst incidence, rate, duration, amplitude; evoked non-burst responses (X3, sparing of physiology); resting potential; viability. **Units:** animals, with slices nested (mixed model).

## 6. Sequence, and what stops it
1. **Desk, no animals (X0):** fix bath Mg2+, temperature, burst definition and window, the trigger, the slice region, blinding and randomisation; resolve the 2.29 v 0.84 per minute discrepancy by plan, not by assumption. 2. **Pilot and WO-10:** baseline burst incidence in both regions and both genotypes; the NAM-sensitive fraction; d-APV. **Stop** if the KO neocortical slices do not burst, or d-APV does not abolish. 3. **Decisive pair:** pore blocker against GABA-A positive modulator (with benzodiazepine) against vehicle, KO and provoked WT, neocortex. 4. **Then, in this order if resources are short:** the sodium-channel arm (AH1, because an aggravation result is the most information per animal), the GluN2B NAM arm, the AMPA arm, the T-type arm. 5. **The Aldaz measurement** alongside step 2 (counts and sIPSC in the same animals), because it decides whether the GABA-A rows may rely on the hippocampal PV loss.

## Addendum 1 (ADV H-ADV-5, 2026-11-16): two exposure levels per arm, analysed on the ratio scale
**Requirement.** Every arm is run at TWO exposure levels, not one, and each level's burst suppression is expressed as a proportion of the same slice's own baseline (ratio scale). The analysis is the genotype-by-level interaction (the SHAPE of the response curve: slope and plateau of suppression against measured exposure or measured fractional block), not the mean effect at one level. A WWOX-specific mechanism predicts a left-shifted or steeper curve in knockout slices; a generic one predicts a parallel curve once normalised to baseline rate. The reason is my own NX1 result (`nx1_sizing_sci_20261115/`): on the absolute scale a null-specific effect cannot be told from a proportional one when wild-type baselines are far lower. **No held curve pair exists** (`leap_sci_20261116/ADV_HYPOTHESES_PRETEST_SCI.md`, H-ADV-5): every held WWOX pharmacology is single-level, so this is a design requirement, not something a table lookup can supply.
**How, without extra animals beyond one added level.** The second level is applied to the same slice after the first (cumulative, with a time-matched vehicle sequence and a washout check; d-APV is reversible per Breton 2021, the other arms' washout is not held) or to sister slices of the same animal. The two levels are set by MEASURED fractional block of the relevant current or by measured tool exposure in the slice (one partial, one near-complete), not by the published tool concentration alone; no concentration is recommended here.
**Sizing line (table 5, new row).** The contrast is the difference between genotypes of (suppression at level 2 minus at level 1). Its SD is SD times the square root of 2(1 minus rho), rho being an animal's correlation of suppression between levels. At rho 0.5 the animals per group equal the single-level drug-by-genotype row (8 to 29); at rho 0 they rise to 15 to 57; at rho 0.8 they fall to 4 to 13. The pilot (four animals per genotype) therefore also measures rho.
**Effect on table 4.** The codes S (genotype-specific suppression) and G (generic) become curve-shape outcomes: S requires a non-parallel curve, G a parallel one after the ratio-scale normalisation. The scenario table's top sets are unchanged, because they were computed from S, G, N and A as declared; what changes is how S is established.

## Addendum 2 (2026-11-16): what else could decide the GABA axis (held-data-consistent hypotheses, region-explicit)
The GABA-axis rows rest on one origin (Breton 2021), one independent anatomical signal in the hippocampus (Aldaz 2019), and a disputed transcript pattern. Besides the Aldaz measurement above, three hypotheses already name a handle, listed with what a held table can and cannot say:
* **H-ADV-3 (ADV; myelin of PV+ axons contributes to the reduced inhibitory output).** Held-data-consistent and region-explicit. Held tables (`leap_sci_20261116/ADV_HYPOTHESES_PRETEST_SCI.md`): in the single-nucleus pool mature-oligodendrocyte nuclei fall from 32.8% to 12.1% of the pool, so most of the pool-level myelin-gene fall is composition, but within mature-oligodendrocyte nuclei Mbp is still 36% lower and Plp1 32% lower; all ten myelin genes are lower in all four neuron-deletion bulk contrasts. **The region comparison is a held-table observation that already bears on the brief:** in the SAME S deletion the loss is smaller in neocortex, where the bursts are, than in hippocampus, where they are not (Mbp 4000.3 to 1752.1 TPM, -56%, against 3294.1 to 745.5, -77%; Plp1 347.0 to 171.0, -51%, against 398.6 to 66.4, -83%; Mobp 424.8 to 196.5, -54%, against 393.5 to 65.5, -83%; Cldn11 -50% against -75%). INFERENZA: bulk myelin-gene loss is therefore NOT larger where the bursts are, a counter-signal to myelin as the driver of the neocortical bursts, and it says nothing about PV-axon myelin (no held table has an axonal compartment). **Cheapest test, no new data: done (this region comparison).** Next cheapest, new sections and no new animals: MBP and Caspr (Cntnap1) staining of PV+ axons in neocortical layer 2/3 and in hippocampus, in the same animals and the same sections as the PV+ and NPY+ counts of the Aldaz measurement. Outcomes: hippocampal PV-axon myelin loss without neocortical loss fits the held region pattern and explains hippocampal inhibition but not the neocortical bursts; neocortical loss makes H-ADV-3 a candidate for the neocortical inhibitory deficit; no PV-axon loss in either region rejects it for PV axons.
* **H-ADV-1 (ErbB4 weakness in PV+ interneurons).** Not supported at transcript level (Erbb4 -13 to -16%, unchanged detection, unchanged in P47T); signalling untested; no new arm.
* **HSCI-1 and HSCI-2 (my leap file: within-burst chloride loading; inhibition-triggered rebound).** Both are decided by arms already in the brief (the GABA-A arm's sign; E_GABA at burst onset against end, which adds a perforated-patch measurement to the pilot animals).

## Addendum 3 (2026-11-16, ADV request): the tonic GABA-A current as an extra readout, and what a second cannabidiol representative needs
**Why.** Rule v2 (R3) keeps a split only if (i) each child has two representative agent ids and (ii) some thread links one child and not the other. In the V2 cells (`matrix_v2_cells_sci_20261116/`) T2 (reduced spontaneous inhibition) links BOTH GABA-A children, so the synaptic-selective and the neurosteroid/extrasynaptic classes were refused together. A tonic GABA-A current thread is the missing distinguishing thread: synaptic-selective modulators act on synaptic gamma2-containing receptors (T2), neurosteroid-type modulators on the extrasynaptic, tonic component. No held measurement of a tonic current exists in any WWOX preparation (Breton 2021 recorded none; `chloride_gradient_sci42_20261110/`, item M07).
**Readout, cheapest form (no new animals).** In the layer 2/3 pyramidal cells that are already patched for sag, rebound, sIPSC (M03) and E_GABA (M01): after the burst or baseline recording, the holding-current shift on GABA-A block with a tool antagonist (no concentration here), then, in the same cell, the shift with GABA uptake blocked, with the ambient-GABA and uptake condition FIXED and written down before the run (M07). The condition decides the answer: the held tonic-current source reports 9 +/- 3 pA that rose to 41 +/- 12 pA with uptake blocked in mouse S1 layer 5 slices (Sebe 2010, PMID 20846324, partial full text, evidence row EV24 of the SCI42 package), so a genotype comparison under unequal uptake states is not interpretable. Add one arm-level readout to the GABA-A positive-modulator arm: the change of the tonic current under a neurosteroid-type modulator against a synaptic-selective one, KO against wild type. Cost: about one extra step per cell; the unit remains the animal; the pilot (four animals per genotype) gives the first variance, and until then the tonic readout is exploratory, not sized.
**What each outcome does to the two children (R1 and R3, mechanical).**
| outcome (KO against WT, fixed uptake condition) | thread it creates | effect on the split |
|---|---|---|
| tonic current REDUCED in KO (tier 3, genotype contrast) and a neurosteroid-type modulator restores or raises it | links `gabaa_neurosteroid_extrasynaptic` (sign match, directness 3) and not `gabaa_pam_synaptic_selective` | R3 is met (a distinguishing thread exists); both children become scorable if each also has two representative ids (they do in the V2 cells: zolpidem, eszopiclone; ganaxolone, brexanolone) |
| tonic current unchanged or INCREASED in KO (compensation) and the modulator adds nothing beyond it | no match or aggravation link for either child | R3 stays unmet by evidence, not by design: the split is not justified and the unsplit parent should be scored instead (a v3 point, see below) |
| tonic current differs between the genotypes only under one uptake condition | none | the readout is invalid; repeat with the condition fixed and recorded |
The synaptic-selective child keeps its existing T2 link either way. E_GABA at burst onset and end (HSCI-1) is a separate thread and is not needed for R3.
**v3 point.** R3 as written refuses BOTH children of a split when either fails and thereby removes the parent class from the scoring. A v3 should keep the unsplit parent scorable whenever a split is not justified (ADV).

### A second cannabidiol representative: what it needs in held sources

* **Even then the split is weak:** fenfluramine_type's two ids are the parent drug and its active metabolite (fenfluramine, norfenfluramine), not two independent representatives; and R3 refuses BOTH children when either fails. If the receipts are not taken, keeping the unsplit parent (v3) is the honest option.
