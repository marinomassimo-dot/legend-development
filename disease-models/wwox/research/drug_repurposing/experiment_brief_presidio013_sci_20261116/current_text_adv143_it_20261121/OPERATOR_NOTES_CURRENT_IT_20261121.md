## The summary page for the operator (A, section 0)

Gist: a design brief for a laboratory that already works with Wwox-knockout mice, with the two drugs Cheng 2020 reported to reduce seizures in knockout pups (ethosuximide and lithium), reference drugs and a wild-type age series; it cannot show whether any drug would help spontaneous seizures, whether a drug works after seizures start, how a drug works, or anything about people. This content is now in the laboratory text itself, under "What this experiment cannot show" ("What may not be ready" is there too: whether any laboratory has the line and capacity is unknown; knockout pups are scarce and fragile; a test of "different between genotypes" on the full-hour area summary is not attemptable at the published variability).

The older texts also said the brief was written for the operator and for the design team ("through ADV, IT for the arm table"). That sentence is in the History of the current text.
