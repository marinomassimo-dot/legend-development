# Round-3 pass criteria (IT, 2026-11-21), written before dispatch
Reader: a NEW cold reader (not the RP-041 readers, not the round-2 reader), sonnet, effort high, given only the current text after the integration of the design team's group-B answers (39 answers, 67 OPEN-GAP, 13 CONFLICT-OPEN markers in the body).
PASS if: (1) every question the reader cannot answer maps to a marker already in the text or to an answer in the text (an unmarked gap is a finding); (2) no new conflict is introduced by the integration (an answer that contradicts the sentence it sits next to, or another answer; a conflict marked CONFLICT-OPEN is not new); (3) the reader states unaided which text governs; (4) the count of questions it calls blocking is reported against round 2 (15 of 84) and the first read (27 of 85), as a measurement, not a threshold. Repair rule: dated repair only for an unmarked gap or a new conflict, old text kept.
