## Corrections and conflicts (statements kept side by side; corrections say which is later; edited 2026-11-19 after IT's blind check, V2_BLIND_CHECK_IT59.md)
1. **Pup sizing (a CORRECTION in the originals, not an open conflict):** the sizing flag says the interaction needs four equal cells at 44 per cell (about 176 animals, 75 litters) and itself points to the ADV-061 addendum as correcting that arithmetic; the addendum says twice, not four times, the simple-effect number per cell, about 180 to 320 per cell (700 to 1,300 animals, 200 to 360 litters) and 22 per cell, 88 animals and 25 litters at the optimistic SD of 0.3. **The later number governs.** Both kept in P2. (One line of the original pup brief, in its sizing arithmetic, still says "four times": an inconsistency of the original, kept as history.)
2. **Conduction n:** the earlier slice-brief line said only "inconsistent n"; pup 9.11 and the 2026-11-19 note give 8 slices from 5 and 8 from 6; whether the control n is a copy of the S-HT n is not decided by the originals.
3. **Time to death:** pup 9.1 has untreated knockouts dying by about day 17 in the AAV9 comparator's panel; other sources give three to four weeks for the germline null. Different lines; not reconciled.
4. **Rat paper year (not a conflict of statements; the repository is consistent, and the resolution is SECONDARY):** the lde/lde rat paper requested is Suzuki 2009 (PMID 19500159; abstract only held); Suzuki 2007 (PMID 17803050; abstract only) is a different, earlier report. That the 2009 paper cites the 2007 report for its serum data rests on the dossier-relayed R4 report, not on a held original; neither full text is held. Only older analysis files confuse the two years.
5. **Washout reads and sag wording:** brief 13.11 reads at 30, 60, 120 minutes, later addenda add 5, 15 (optional 45); the hashed slice priors cover only 30 to 60 minutes and say "sag ratio", the paired readout is in mV. Stated in S2; priors not edited.
6. **Cheng 2020 blot age:** Methods say day 14, the Fig. 7 legend day 20; the PTZ test age is not stated.
7. **S-HT panels (Repudi et al., Brain 2021, PMID 33914858; Breton is the fourth author; NOT "Breton 2021"):** text gives 4 of 23 slices from 14 animals (17 percent) and 36 of 42 slices from 24 animals (86 percent); the legend gives 4 slices from 3 animals (20 percent) and 36 slices from 20 animals (84 percent) (IT's row check and blind check). S2 uses the text values. "Breton 2021" (Neurobiol Dis 160:105529, PMID 34634460) is a different paper, the preparation of the slice package; **whether it carries the same panels is OPEN (not checked)**.
