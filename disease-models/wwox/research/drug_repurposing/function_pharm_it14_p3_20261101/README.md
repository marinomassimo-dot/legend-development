## 0. Decision (first) [revised 2026-11-01]

1. **No compound is supported as a candidate. One probe-level hypothesis is retained, conditional, and one earlier proposal is demoted.** Function: synaptic (GABA-A) inhibition onto layer II/III pyramidal cells in Wwox neuron-deleted neocortex. H1: clobazam and its active metabolite N-desmethylclobazam (NDMC), both benzodiazepine-site positive modulators of alpha1/2/3/5-beta-gamma2 receptors with EC50 of about 0.1 to 1 uM, compensate a measured inhibitory loss and lower d-APV-sensitive bursting during exposure. H1 as a class-level question (does benzodiazepine-site potentiation compensate the measured inhibitory loss during exposure?) does not wait for the locus or for the subtype dispute: the locus (receptor- versus release-limited) and the subtype selectivity decide how a result is attributed, not whether the arm can be run (section 12, IT15). 2. **Correction of P3a, restated.** Breton Table 1: resting potential -79.25 (WT), -75.51 (HT), -73.62 (KO) mV and sag 1.18, 2.04, 2.57 are graded with gene dose; input resistance (143.26 v 143.03 MOhm) and AP amplitude are fully shared by HT and KO; HT v KO is never tested in Table 1 and the real KO-HT resting-potential gap is 1.9 mV; the t-tests treat cells as independent (13/15/40 cells from 5/6/15 animals): pseudoreplication. ## 5. Control against suppression artefacts

Any firing- or burst-reduction readout is read together with: viability, AP height and wash-out (requirements); evoked fEPSP slope in WT; the F-I curve and input resistance (measured and reported: for a GABA-A potentiator or a K+ channel opener a change in input resistance or in the F-I curve is the expected mechanism, so it is a marker and not an exclusion, whereas for a claim of selective rather than generic silencing it must be stated); the same agent on WT matched-trigger slices (suppression index, a specificity qualifier, not a condition for partial benefit); TTX as the generic silencer reference. (Revised IT15 P1.3.) Silencing is trivially achieved by many agents (P3b C3/C6), so burst reduction alone is symptomatic.

## 6. Prediction and design [revised 2026-11-01] (`experiment_arms_it14_p3.tsv`)

Predictions (hypotheses fixed before any experiment; none is a source finding): (E0) KO sIPSC charge clearly below Cre+ wild-type; heterozygote value unknown (my earlier "at least 80 percent of WT" is withdrawn as a threshold: it was a guess); isolated NMDAR and AMPAR currents measured, not inferred. Controls are **Cre+ wild-type** (Breton's), not Cre-negative.

Logic, corrected:
* **E1(i)** gabazine-induced d-APV-sensitive bursts in WT/HT slices shows that disinhibition suffices in a normal circuit; it does not show disinhibition is the KO step (it is satisfied by R3 alone, R3 plus R2, or a high-gain NMDAR circuit). **E1(ii)** bath K+ is not Vm-matching (no Rin, sag or rebound reproduction), so a K+ arm that fails does not refute R2; a "twice the fraction" criterion is weak (0.2 v 0.1 passes) and is dropped.
* **E2** (restoring IPSC charge stops bursts) is satisfied by repair and equally by counterbalance of another excess. Only E0 together with E1 (does the KO's measured excitatory change, in a WT circuit, need the inhibitory loss?) can separate repair from counterbalance. The WT matched-trigger control reflects network state, not KO specificity.
* **Missing arms added:** an AMPAR arm (the only excitatory change measured is mEPSC/sEPSC amplitude; perampanel or NBQX as comparator, noting AMPAR block is expected to stop NMDAR-dependent bursts whichever reading holds, IT6 P9); an Ih/input-resistance arm (ZD7288 or equivalent, with the authors' caution that an Ih blocker worsened 4-AP events); a graded GABA-A potentiator arm with measured IPSC charge, because restoration is not guaranteed. R4 (secondary state) and R5 (gap junction) are **not** separable by E0 to E2; E3 only records covariates.
* **Sample size is not fixed.** The animal is the unit. Dependencies to state before any number is chosen: (a) the unit (one value per animal vs slices nested in animals); (b) the baseline rate: 0.17 is HT spontaneous bursting, while E1 compares gabazine and K+ arms whose rates are unknown; (c) intraclass correlation; (d) E2 has no power calculation. For reference only, exact one-sided Fisher power from `design_counts.py` at independent units: n = 6 per arm gives 0.61 for 0.80 v 0.17 and 0.28 for 0.60 v 0.17; 12 slices from 6 animals with intraclass correlation 0.2 to 0.5 behaves like an effective n of 8 to 10 (power about 0.67 to 0.85 for 0.80 v 0.17). The earlier headline (0.94 at 12 independent slices) assumed independent slices and is withdrawn.

## 12. Re-weighing of experimental conditions (IT15 P1.3, 2026-11-01)

Question: which conditions written in IT14 had become stronger requirements than the rationale justifies? Method: each condition was read against what it is for. Methods support is at abstract level only (PubMed records PMID 1707966, Edwards, Konnerth and Sakmann 1990, quantal analysis of miniature and evoked IPSCs recorded with and without TTX; PMID 18691332, Kobayashi 2008, miniature IPSCs together with failure rate, coefficient of variation and quantal analysis of release sites and release probability as separate measures; neither is a full-text read, no receipt).

| Condition | Was written as | What it is actually for | Correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Locus (receptor- versus release-limited) established first | precondition to reopen H1 | interpreting mechanism; it also says whether a potentiator or reuptake inhibition is the sensible contrast | not a precondition for a class-level compensation arm |
| Subtype selectivity dispute resolved | precondition of H1 | attributing an effect to alpha1 or alpha2/3 | needed only for subtype attribution (zolpidem versus clonazepam arm), not for the class-level question |
| F-I curve, input resistance, membrane potential "unchanged" | counts only if unchanged | separating a selective effect from generic silencing | a GABA-A potentiator or a Kv7 opener is expected to change input resistance or the F-I curve; measured and reported, not an exclusion. Viability, AP height, wash-out remain requirements |
| Effect also in wild type | suppression index at most half the knockout | WWOX specificity | qualifies specificity; does not prevent a partial-benefit reading (compensation relative to untreated knockout) |
| sIPSC amplitude, or the E0 triad, locates pre- versus postsynaptic | E0 as the locus test | the triad (NMDAR current, sIPSC, Vm/Rin) tests the competing readings of the phenotype, not the locus | the locus needs miniature IPSCs in TTX (amplitude and frequency) and, where evoked IPSCs are possible, paired-pulse ratio or coefficient of variation or failure rate; each narrows, none proves (multi-quantal events, filtering, TTX effects on release) |
| Restoration to control charge | non-restoration uninterpretable | a dose-matched comparison | partial restoration with a graded effect is a result; absence of effect at sub-control restoration is insufficiency at that degree |

Five dimensions kept apart: probe interpretability (the measurements above), mechanism attribution (locus, subtype), functional benefit during exposure (burst endpoints against untreated knockout), WWOX specificity (wild-type and heterozygote effects) and therapeutic suitability (not addressed by any ex vivo arm). Uncertainty on one dimension does not block the others. No new global requirement was added; E0 gains two readouts.
