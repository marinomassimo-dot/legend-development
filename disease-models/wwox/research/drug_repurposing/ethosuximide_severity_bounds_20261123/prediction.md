# Does a smaller mean ordinal seizure score constrain the severe-stage burden?

The 15 November laboratory audit of Cheng 2020 Fig. 7b already quantified a lower mean score in the late window after ethosuximide in PTZ-challenged Wwox-null mice; those group means cannot reveal individual trajectories. Use the existing extractions without digitizing the source again. Before the new enumeration, define the outcome as the fraction of scored mouse-minutes at stage ≥3, the first stage with forelimb clonus. The clinically different stage ≥5 composite (generalized convulsion or death) and stages 3–5 excluding death are secondary. Compare PTZ alone minus PTZ+ethosuximide over minutes 31–60; use 11–30 and 1–10 as predeclared time controls. A lower bound above zero over all 30 late minutes, including uncertainty at every missing point, would support fewer clonic-or-worse mouse-minutes under the model. This does not identify the number of mice with seizures or distinguish a developmental drug effect.

Enumerate every unordered vector of six integer stages 0–6 (group n=6 in both arms). Use the envelope of available clean mean and SEM readings from the two rasters (two renderings of one figure), with ±0.085 mean and ±0.10 SEM tolerance in stage units. Treat an unreadable SEM under the marker (reported zero) as [0,0.2] before the SEM tolerance. If no histogram fits both, use the mean with no SEM constraint; if the mean or both graphics are missing or inconsistent with the integer model, allow every histogram. Always carry unresolved times to the full-window bound. Record how often each fallback is needed. Repeat mean-only, wider ±0.17/0.20 measurement uncertainty, and variable n=3–6 among survivors. Under varying n report the equal-minute average of percentages conditional on animals still scored, without inferring the original-cohort proportion. Check applicability of the statistic independently against the previous laboratory extraction of the vector panel 7d (different compound, knockout n=6 and 7) with 0.02 tolerance and an explicit allowance for hidden bars. Do not select a tolerance after looking at the result. Histograms with the correct aggregate moments need not be the animals' true outcomes.
