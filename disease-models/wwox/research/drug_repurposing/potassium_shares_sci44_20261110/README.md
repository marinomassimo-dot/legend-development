## Answer first
1. **A KATP contribution to resting properties is measured in juvenile neocortex, and its size bounds PH4 (SCI44 package A).** In juvenile (13-24 day) barrel cortex (Karagiannis 2021, 34766906; text, Figs 2 and 4 and the source-data workbooks recomputed by the verifier): KCNJ11 is the pore subunit; in 9 glutamatergic regular-spiking cells tolbutamide raised Rm by 32 percent (17.7 percent in the 8 responsive) and depolarised by 2.8 mV; diazoxide lowered Rm by 13 percent; the diazoxide-opened conductance is about half of whole-cell conductance (0.52, n 7, reversal -101.7 mV near E_K); in ATP-undisturbed perforated patch 15 mM lactate roughly doubled firing (absent in Kcnj11 knockout). **The resting KATP share is INFERENZA, about 11-17 percent with a very wide spread (within the RS cells alone not distinguishable from zero), probably an upper bound (ATP-free whole-cell dialysis).** The Wwox KO pattern needs a lost conductance of about 28 percent of the resting conductance (SCI43): **on this estimate lactate-closed KATP could carry a part of it, at most about half, not all** (INFERENZA; cell type, species, temperature and ATP condition differ from the Wwox layer II/III recordings).
2. **The measurement is not clean enough to call it a pyramidal-cell share.** Only 26 of 63 RS cells are pyramidal by morphology; the species of the Fig 4 and Fig 2 cohorts and the per-cell temperatures (20.3-34.1 C across the dataset) are not stated; two other juvenile preparations report no tolbutamide or diazoxide effect on rest (9826065 abstract; Lemak 2014 entorhinal layer III, p 0.397) and one reports a glibenclamide inward current with 2 mM pipette ATP (Kola 2024, auditory association layer II, 33-34 C, TTX plus blockers).
3. **K2P: still no selective share in juvenile neocortical somata.** The only neocortical K2P-blocker panel is dendritic, adult, 36 C, a pooled group; arms from source data (n 3-4): A1899 about +8 percent, fluoxetine about +44 percent, a Ba2+ 1 mM cocktail about +41 percent; pooled 88.5 to 111.3 MOhm (n 11). The tool-name strings (A1899, spadin, ML365, PK-THPP, ML133, fluoxetine with TREK or TASK, Ba2+ with layer 2/3) found no juvenile layer 2/3 somatic test of a K2P- or Kir2-selective tool (verifier reran the 92-hit string and fourteen drug- and gene-led strings: nothing new).
4. **Glia:** a Ba2+ or K2P-blocker effect in a slice is not purely neuronal (positive literature statements at abstract level: Kir4.1 and Ba2+; Ba2+ blocks TWIK-1; TWIK-1 and TREK-1 contribute to astrocyte conductance).
5. **No two shares were measured under the same age, layer, temperature, pipette ATP, blockers and compartment**, so no sum and no ranking is offered.

## What this changes in the K-branch decision
* SCI43's ladder step 2b (Ba2+ and K2P difference current per pF in the same cells) gains a **KATP arm with ATP undisturbed** (perforated or cell-attached; diazoxide and a sulfonylurea in KO and wild type; lactate or pyruvate add-back) and a **stated pipette ATP**; the same cells and preparation serve (package A, PH4).
* The expected size is smaller than the Wwox loss: if KATP is the only closed channel, the KO should show a diazoxide-sensitive current deficit of the order of the 11-17 percent upper bound, not 28 percent; a larger deficit would need Ba2+-sensitive Kir/K2P channels in addition.
* Mouse versus rat, layer I-IV pooled versus layer II/III, and ATP-free versus ATP-containing pipettes are transfer limits that the first measurement (capacitance, selection rule, junction potential, pipette ATP) settles.
