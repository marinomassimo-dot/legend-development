## What changes in the K-branch decision

* DATO that stands: KATP channels are functional in juvenile (13-24 day) barrel-cortex neurons including regular-spiking glutamatergic cells (diazoxide-opened conductance about half of the whole-cell conductance, reversal near E_K), KCNJ11 is the pore subunit, lactate and pyruvate raise firing in a KATP-dependent way in perforated-patch recordings; a KATP link to the metabolic branch exists in neocortex (replaces "not found").
* INFERENZA, revised: the resting KATP share of regular-spiking glutamatergic cells is about 11-16 percent with a very wide spread (central estimates 13-17 percent from three independent readouts; the RS-only mean cannot be told from zero at n 9) and is probably an upper bound (ATP-free dialysis, tolbutamide specificity untested in the knockout); the cells are not shown to be pyramidal; species of the cohort and individual-cell temperatures are unstated; two other juvenile preparations (Pissarek abstract, Lemak mEC) report no rest effect of tolbutamide, one (Kola, adult-like 21-35 days, 2 mM ATP) a glibenclamide inward current.
* K2P: still no K2P-selective share in juvenile neocortical somata; the adult dendritic arms now give A1899 about +8 percent, fluoxetine about +44 percent and a Ba2+-containing cocktail about +41 percent of dendritic Rm (n 4, 4, 3), all non-selective or small samples.
* Glia: a slice Ba2+ or K2P-blocker effect carries a positive, abstract-level literature reason to include astrocytic and oligodendrocytic Kir4.1 and TWIK-1/TREK-1; it is not an absence.
