# SCI44 package C: answers (one page)

Non-canonical research material. Not medical advice. Reference genotype class only. Labels DATO / INFERENZA / IPOTESI. Nothing is promoted. "same-preparation" below means juvenile rodent neocortical neurons (the question's preparation), with every deviation named on the row; it never means a Wwox preparation. No Wwox current datum exists in any source.

**C1. KATP in juvenile neocortex (rows C01-C06, C13).** DATO: in juvenile (13-24 day) rat barrel cortex, KATP is functional in regular-spiking pyramidal-type cells as in all other types: diazoxide-opened KATP conductance is about half of the whole-cell conductance (RS 0.52 +/- 0.08, n 7; E_rev -101.7 mV, near E_K), tolbutamide depolarises and raises Rm, KCNJ11 is the pore subunit (knockout), and lactate or pyruvate (ATP-undisturbed gramicidin recordings) roughly doubles firing through KATP (Karagiannis 2021, 34766906). The pyramidal result is separable from the pooled one only through per-cell source data: 9 RS cells, tolbutamide +32 percent Rm and +2.8 mV (INFERENZA: derived by me), implied resting KATP share about 16 +/- 8 percent (11 +/- 7 in the 8 responsive cells), with an ATP-free pipette, 25.3 C and layers I-IV; the authors report no subtype difference. Counter-evidence: one rat layer II-III pyramidal abstract (9826065) reports no effect of diazoxide or tolbutamide on Vm or Rin. Sada 2015 is reported second-hand only (LDH metabolites close KATP; intracellular ATP did not revert the opening produced by LDH inhibition); the paper was not opened.

**C3. K2P and other sources (rows C07-C10, C08).** The only neocortical pyramidal K2P-blocker panel read (40595546) is dendritic, adult, 36 C (correction to SCI43 D09, which said 30 C), and pools a Ba2+ 1 mM cocktail with A1899 and fluoxetine: membrane resistance +26 percent (88.5 to 111.3 MOhm, n 11), not a K2P-selective share. 41233736 contributes a solution and junction-potential record only (no Rin or rest in Results). 37321992 Supp Fig 14: retigabine leaves rest unchanged (about -77 mV) and moves WT Rin about +6 percent with a 'Drug *' label: no large Kv7 reserve, no Kv7 share. Tool-name search (15 PubMed and 3 Europe PMC strings): no juvenile L2/3 pyramidal somatic Rin or rest test of a K2P-selective or Kir2-selective tool found (perimeter in row C08 and queries.tsv). A related counter-reading of SCI43 D09: 33908633 is a quercetin paper where spadin blocked quercetin's effect, not a spadin-alone test.

**C4. Compartment, glia, conditions (rows C12, C13).** Neuronal and same-condition shares do not exist in this set: the KATP data are somatic, 25.3 C, ATP-free pipette; the K2P data are dendritic at 36 C in adults; the Ba2+ and ZD7288 data (carried) are P28-P42 auditory cortex at 34 C with 4 mM ATP; retigabine is P19-23 with blockers, temperature unstated. No source states a Ba2+, ML133 or blocker action on astrocytic Kir4.1 or K+ clearance; 34766906 says bath KATP modulators could act via network or astrocytes and argues against it by kinetics and reversal; 38942186 cites Kir6.1 as mainly astrocytic. No sum or ranking is offered.

**What this changes (descriptive).** The earlier SCI44 statement that a KATP link was "not found" is replaced by a measured one in juvenile neocortex (with the ATP-free and temperature limits above); a KATP effect shows that a current contributes, not that it is altered in Wwox. Surprise for the reader: the KATP row of this package, not the Ba2+ row, is the only juvenile pyramidal share with per-cell numbers.

## Corrections after independent verification (V4, Orchestrator, 2026-11-11)
The KATP numbers reproduce from the source-data workbooks (RS n 9: tolbutamide Rm +32.1 percent, Vm +2.8 mV; diazoxide Rm -13.0 percent; Fig 2 G_KATP/G_total 0.52, n 7, reversal -101.7 mV; Kcnj11 knockout 19 of 26 and 20 of 22 cells; lactate 209 / pyruvate 160 / diazoxide 71 percent of baseline firing). **The resting KATP share is INFERENZA, about 11-17 percent with a very wide spread (within the RS cells alone not distinguishable from zero), probably an upper bound (ATP-free whole-cell), in glutamatergic regular-spiking cells of barrel cortex layers I-IV (only 26 of 63 RS cells pyramidal), species and per-cell temperature unstated.** Two other juvenile preparations (abstract 9826065, Lemak 2014) report no tolbutamide or diazoxide effect on rest; Kola 2024 reports a glibenclamide inward current with 2 mM ATP. K2P: still no selective share in juvenile neocortical somata (adult dendritic arms: A1899 about +8, fluoxetine about +44, cocktail about +41 percent).
