# Round 2 of the leap round: what I would add (SCI opinion for the closing synthesis, 2026-11-16)

**OPINION, not a finding.** Written after the pre-tests of all four other authors' files and before the judge's table. Non-canonical; mechanism level only; no dose, no recommendation to use any agent.

## New perspectives (each tied to a gap that round 1 exposed)
1. **Allelic series.** Every held mechanism claim comes from a null or neuron-specific knockout or from the P47T hypomorph; the human alleles (splice, missense, truncating) are never a perspective. Ask which class fits a hypomorph against a null, since the two lines already disagree on PV loss, activity-dependent genes and myelin genes.
2. **Red team.** Assume the Breton burst result is a preparation artefact (one laboratory, pooled controls, a 2.7-fold printed discrepancy in the burst rate) and write the hypotheses that explain every held table without it. Round 1 never attacked its own single origin.
3. **Tool-compound pharmacology.** What do the known off-target profiles of the probes in the brief imply (an ifenprodil-site NAM, a pore blocker, a sodium-channel label) for what a slice experiment can attribute? The brief names one representative per class and does not ask what else each one hits.
4. **Pharmacovigilance.** Adverse-event and imaging signals (the vigabatrin-associated MRI abnormalities held in the corpus) as a hypothesis source, since aggravation is the thing the matrix cannot see.

## Template fields I would add
* **Origins behind the premise** (number of independent laboratories; 'one' is written, not hidden).
* **Tables seen before writing** (a log, so that a pre-test is labelled independent or consistency by rule, not by the pre-tester's memory; round 1 had two circular cases of mine).
* **Cheapest rejecting held table, named BEFORE the pre-test** (the pre-tester then does not choose the test).
* **Kill criterion with a number** (my statements PS-01 to PS-15 do this; most round-1 rejecting observations are verbal).
* **Cost in tokens and in animals** per hypothesis, so survivors per cost is computed, not estimated.

## Arms and judge
* **A second baseline arm with a different knowledge source:** a memory-free delegate given only PubMed abstracts and no repository file. Round 1 compares authors with the repository against a delegate with the repository bundle; the difference between 'the repository helps' and 'a changed perspective helps' is confounded. A third small arm, the same author writing WITHOUT a named perspective, separates the perspective effect from the author effect.
* **Calibrate the blind judge with planted items:** two hypotheses already rejected by a held table and two already supported, unlabelled, so the novelty and survival criteria have an error rate before they are applied to the real ones.
* **Count survivors after the counterpart's pre-test, not before:** that is the stage where round 1 removed ErbB4, mTOR and the recombination explanation at the cost of seconds.

## What I would not repeat
Authors choosing their perspective after seeing the held scan; counting 'survives' from the author's own pre-test; the push-race (agree an isolated-worktree push and a single landing order before the round opens).
