   census stands. What changes is its perimeter and its reasons (section 4).

## 2. How level (d) is judged

A dataset is **ADEQUATE** for a question only if all hold: (i) genetically WWOX-deficient **neural** system; (ii) the analyte class of the question is measured (protein abundance; a phospho-site; a
metabolic flux or enzyme activity; a time-ordered same-readout comparison of untreated, add-back and control after onset); (iii) biological replicates are stated and are the unit; (iv) controls
match; (v) the data are downloadable per sample for re-analysis. **PARTIAL** if (ii) and (i) hold with a stated limit. **NOT ADEQUATE** otherwise. P = protein, Ph = phosphorylation, F = flux or
activity, R = recovery after onset. "Level (b)" means what a reader of the paper sees without any deposit.
