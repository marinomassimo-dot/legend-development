# P3-12 defosbarasertib (AZD1152-HQPA, active metabolite; Q6, arm "opposes", edge AURKB -> MYC +1)

Decision: **DROP.** Decisive datum: the compound is a cytotoxic antimitotic whose effect on a population of cycling radial glia is mitotic failure, not a graded MYC reduction; its MYC edge is a T-ALL protein-stability record, and the Aurora B target is not detected in adult brain nuclei (so the only brain cells it would hit are the proliferating progenitors that the seed paper wants to keep). The P3 note that it "would be excluded under a strict reading of F1" is correct. Biological promise: low; depth: database, trial registry and abstract level; feasibility: very low (intravenous, nanoparticle, dose-limiting neutropenia); next-experiment priority: none.

1. **Identity.** ChEMBL CHEMBL215152 (DEFOSBARASERTIB), InChIKey QYZOGCMHVIGURT-UHFFFAOYSA-N; PubChem by name and by key agree. **VERIFIED.** The phosphate prodrug is a different molecule: PubChem for the name "barasertib" returns GBJVVSCPOBPEIT-UHFFFAOYSA-N, so the P3 alias "AZD1152" names the prodrug (converted in plasma to this active metabolite) and "AZD2811" is a nanoparticle formulation of the active compound (formulation fact from the abstract of PMID 36871042; the prodrug-to-active relation is general knowledge, UNVERIFIED here). ChEMBL max phase 2. No salt record.
2. **Target and selectivity** (human, nM, type B unless stated; ranges not pooled). AURKB Ki below 1 (censored "<"), 0.359 to 0.37 (2 "=" rows); IC50 0.3 to 17 (7 "=" rows; the 17 nM value is a cell-based pHH3 readout); Kd 2 to 7.2. Same-document comparisons with AURKA: Ki AURKB under 1 versus AURKA 1,400 (CHEMBL1147984); IC50 0.326 versus 1,386 (CHEMBL4765402); Kd 4.6 versus 400 (CHEMBL1908390). So Aurora B over Aurora A by at least 87 fold (Kd) and above 1,000 fold (Ki, IC50, bound). Other kinases with Kd below 100 nM in kinome panels: AURKC 4.4, FLT3 3.9, KIT 7.9, MAP2K5 2.8, PDGFRA 38, PDGFRB 41, EPHB6 50 (selective in the Aurora family, not kinome-wide).
3. **Exposure.** No label. Phase 1 of nanoparticle AZD2811 in 51 adults with solid tumours (PMID 36871042): 2-h intravenous infusion 15 to 600 mg; recommended dose 500 mg every 21 days with G-CSF; dose-limiting grade 3 to 4 neutropenia, stomatitis; neutropenia as pharmacodynamic marker. Earlier prodrug trials listed in the registry (AZD1152; AML, solid tumours, DLBCL). Brain or CSF exposure: **UNKNOWN (not retrieved)**. Paediatric: **none** (all 14 registry studies adult, minimum age 18 to 20 years).
4. **Safety and counter-evidence.** Myelosuppression (neutropenia dose-limiting), mucositis, fatigue (adult abstracts). Seizure-provoking reports: none retrieved. Neurodevelopmental toxicity: no direct study retrieved; Aurora B is required for chromosome segregation and cytokinesis and mitotic-gene disorders cause primary microcephaly (search hits only; general knowledge, UNVERIFIED as applied here). An inhibitor of Aurora B in radial glia, which the seed paper reports as accumulating in S and G2/M, would be expected to cause endoreduplication or death (inference, no data).
5. **Edge provenance.** AURKB -> MYC (+1): one SIGNOR record (PMID 32049046): AURKB phosphorylates MYC Ser67 and blocks its GSK3B/FBXW7-directed degradation in T-ALL, with a TAL1-dependent feed-forward loop; AURKB inhibitors induced MYC degradation and leukaemia cell death in those cells. Lymphoid, protein stability, cell-death outcome. **Context-free for neural cells; the abstract's outcome is cytotoxic, not a normalisation.**
6. **Expression** (HPA): AURKB **not detected** in adult brain single-nuclei data, "detected in single" brain region, enhanced in proliferating cell types elsewhere; consistent with expression restricted to cycling cells. Fetal radial glia: UNVERIFIED (adult atlas).
7. **Trials (2026-10-30; aliases defosbarasertib, barasertib, AZD1152, AZD2811).** 14 studies; epilepsy/seizure 0, hypomyelination 0, neurodevelopment 0, WWOX 0, CNS tumour 0, paediatric terms 0.
8. **Verification.** Not proposed. A graded-dose experiment in KO organoids could only distinguish "MYC fall by cell death" (pH3, cleaved caspase-3, polyploid nuclei) from "MYC fall with preserved neurogenesis" (NEUN+, SATB2+), and the first is predicted. Falsifier of the DROP: a dose window in which MYC protein falls and NEUN+ fraction rises without polyploidy or apoptosis. Transfer limits: cytotoxicity, adult-only data, intravenous nanoparticle.
