# P3-11 pomalidomide (Q6, arm "mimics", edge CUL4A -> MYC -1)

Decision: **DROP.** Decisive data: (i) the MYC sign is **not derivable**: the compound is a molecular glue, not a CRL4 inhibitor, and the network itself holds contradictory routes from the glue to MYC; (ii) the label carries a boxed embryo-fetal toxicity warning and the seed is a prenatal radial-glia substrate; (iii) the glue's best-known neosubstrates IKZF1 and IKZF3 are not expressed in neural cells in the adult atlas. Biological promise: not assessable (no testable direction); depth of evidence: database, label and abstract level; feasibility: very low; next-experiment priority: none.

1. **Identity.** ChEMBL CHEMBL43452, InChIKey UVSMNLNDYGZFPF-UHFFFAOYSA-N (racemic mixture, no salt); PubChem by name and by key agree. **VERIFIED.** ATC L04AX06, max phase 4.
2. **Target, action, measures** (human, nM, type B; ranges across assays; no pooling across types). ChEMBL curated mechanism: "CRL4(CRBN) E3 ubiquitin ligase inhibitor", action type INHIBITOR (target CHEMBL3833061). **That coding is the artefact that produced the sign.** The compound binds cereblon and redirects the ligase to neosubstrates: CRBN Kd 83 to 157 (2 rows), IC50 83 to 25,800 (8 rows, 7 documents, competition assays), Ki 3,300 to 10,000; IKZF1 degradation DC50 17 to 50 (3 rows), IKZF3 DC50 13 to 170, ZFP91 DC50 420; IKZF1 and IKZF3 ternary-complex EC50 20 to 24. So the pharmacological action is **induced degradation (gain of ligase activity toward IKZF1/3, SALL4 and others)**, not inhibition of CUL4A. The IMiD mechanism and the myeloma MYC result: lenalidomide-bound cereblon degrades IKZF1 and IKZF3, necessary and sufficient for the antitumour effect (PMIDs 24292623 and 24292625); in myeloma lines degradation of Ikaros and Aiolos is followed by **down**regulation of c-Myc and then IRF4 (PMID 26430725).
   **Sign derivation inside the network** (offline, the P3 input edge files): CRBN_CUL4A_DDB1_RBX1 -> IKZF1 -1 (SIGNOR, degradation); IKZF1 -> MYC **-1** (CollecTRI, transcriptional); IKZF3 -> MYC **+1** (CollecTRI); CRBN_CUL4A_DDB1_RBX1 -> MYC -1 (SIGNOR, a different record, see item 5). A glue that increases IKZF1 degradation therefore gives MYC **up** by the IKZF1 route, while the IKZF3 route and the myeloma experiments give MYC **down**. The network sign for IKZF1 is opposite to the one observed in the cell type where the drug acts. **The sign of the MYC effect is not derivable from this network, and is not derivable in radial glia at all from present data.** Both arms ("mimics" as coded, "opposes" if recoded) are therefore conditional on an unmeasured cell-specific outcome.
3. **Exposure (label, openFDA, 2026-10-30; Natco generic, set id in `labels_sections.tsv`).** Oral; AUC 860 ng.h/mL and Cmax 75 ng/mL at 4 mg in multiple myeloma; half-life 9.5 h (healthy) and 7.5 h (patients); P-gp substrate; CYP1A2 and CYP3A4. **Paediatric:** safety and effectiveness not established; two open-label studies in children with recurrent CNS tumours (25 patients aged 5 to under 17, and 47 aged 4 to under 17; label cites NCT02415153 and NCT03257631) found no new safety signals and exposure at the same body-surface dose in 55 children within the adult myeloma range. CSF or brain concentration: **not stated in the retrieved label text; not retrieved from the literature** (the phase 1 PCNSL abstract, PMID 30262659, reports efficacy, not CSF levels). Being a P-gp substrate argues against high brain exposure (inference, UNVERIFIED).
4. **Safety and counter-evidence.** Boxed warning: embryo-fetal toxicity (contraindicated in pregnancy, "thalidomide analogue", known human teratogen) and venous and arterial thromboembolism; restricted programme (REMS). Mechanistic link to teratogenicity: SALL4 is a thalidomide-dependent cereblon neosubstrate (PMID 30190590); an industry working-group paper states SALL4 degradation is unlikely to be the only mechanism (PMID 40015443). **Neurodevelopment-specific:** cereblon knockdown and thalidomide both gave small brains in zebrafish through reduced neural stem cell proliferation (PMID 31055217); cereblon is a gene for mild intellectual disability (PMID 33777938, review). In a WWOX-deficient developing brain this is a direct neurodevelopmental toxicity argument. Seizures: the retrieved label sections contain no seizure term (absence from fetched sections, not evidence of absence); FAERS seizure reports 193 of 102,194 pomalidomide reports (0.19 percent; reports, not incidence; `faers_seizure_counts.tsv`).
5. **Edge provenance.** CUL4A -> MYC -1 (see `seed_assessment_Q6_MYC.md`): one SIGNOR record on a **TRPC4AP**-receptor DDB1-CUL4 ligase in cancer cell lines (PMID 20551172); CRBN is not named in its abstract. The compound does not act through that receptor. Judgement: **context-free artefact, and the wrong receptor**.
6. **Expression** (HPA adult brain): CRBN detected in all brain single-nuclei cell types, low specificity, all regions; IKZF1 only in CNS macrophages and leukocytes; IKZF3 only in leukocytes; SALL4 detected in some regions, enhanced in fibroblasts only. IKZF1/3 in fetal radial glia: UNVERIFIED (adult atlas only). The IKZF1/3 mechanism that lowers MYC in myeloma has **no substrate in neural lineage cells on the data held**.
7. **Trials (2026-10-30).** Pomalidomide alone 331 studies; epilepsy/seizure 0, hypomyelination 0, neurodevelopment 0, WWOX 0; CNS tumour terms 12; paediatric terms 15.
8. **Verification in a WWOX-deficient system.** Not recommended. If a glue mechanism were ever to be tested, the first measurement is IKZF1/3 and SALL4 protein in KO radial glia (absent substrate means no mechanism); the discriminating readout would be MYC change with a CRBN-binding-deficient analogue as control and a CRBN knockout line as falsifier. Transfer limits: teratogen, P-gp substrate, prenatal window, no substrate.

Not done: CSF concentration literature; SIGNOR full record; fetal IKZF1/3 expression.
