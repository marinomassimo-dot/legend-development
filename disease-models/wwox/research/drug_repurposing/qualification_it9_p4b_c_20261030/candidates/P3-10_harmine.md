# P3-10 harmine (Q6, arm "mimics", edge DYRK1A -> MYC -1)

Decision: **DROP** as a candidate for the WWOX-DEE class. Decisive datum: in the same ChEMBL document harmine inhibits MAO-A (IC50 5 nM) 16 fold more potently than DYRK1A (IC50 80 nM), so it is not a DYRK1A tool, and the "mimics" arm asks the drug to move MYC in the direction the seed paper associates with the defect. Biological promise (WWOX-seed link and target action only): low. Depth of evidence: database and abstract level. Feasibility: low for the target population. Next-experiment priority: none for the compound (the seed-level valence test in `seed_assessment_Q6_MYC.md` comes first).

1. **Identity.** ChEMBL CHEMBL269538, standard InChIKey BXNJHAXVSOCGBA-UHFFFAOYSA-N (achiral); PubChem by name "harmine" and by this InChIKey return the same key (7-methoxy-1-methyl-9H-pyrido[3,4-b]indole). **VERIFIED by structure** (the P3 flag UNVERIFIED is closed). Parent record; the family holds the hydrochloride and hydrochloride hydrate salts (CHEMBL1593867, CHEMBL486818); ChEMBL max_phase 1. Human data use the hydrochloride.
2. **Target and selectivity** (`measures_by_target_type.tsv`, human, nM, biochemical type B, ranges across assays, not pooled): DYRK1A IC50 8.81 to 700 (44 "=" rows, 35 documents), Ki 33 (1 row), Kd 5.5 to 13.4 (2 rows); DYRK1B IC50 28 to 293, Ki 166; DYRK2 IC50 120 to 3,166; CLK1 IC50 26 to 299, Kd 12; CLK4 IC50 24 to 102; HASPIN IC50 590; **MAO-A Ki 1 to 17 (4 rows), IC50 1 to 60 (7 "=" rows)**; GSK3B IC50 350 to 32,100. Same-document pairs (DYRK1A and MAO-A, IC50, both "="): 80 versus 5 nM (document CHEMBL5104139), 22 and 150 versus 1 nM (CHEMBL5233266), 100 and 9 versus 60 nM (CHEMBL6115010). So MAO-A is as potent or up to 16 fold more potent than DYRK1A in the same assay series; **harmine is not DYRK1A-selective**. The medicinal-chemistry literature states the same ("potent inhibition of MAO-A strongly limits its application", abstracts of PMID 33339338 and PMID 33781856; harmol and AnnH75 are selectivity-improved analogues). Many assays have different ATP concentrations, so absolute DYRK1A values are not comparable across rows.
3. **Exposure.** No label (not a medicine). Human data: one single-ascending-dose phase 1 of pharmaceutical-grade harmine hydrochloride in 25 healthy adults aged 18 to 55 (PMID 39301926, abstract level): 100, 200, 300, 500 mg orally; maximum tolerated dose between 100 and 200 mg, weight-based, **doses above 2.7 mg/kg gave vomiting, drowsiness and limited psychoactivity**; adverse events gastrointestinal and neurological. Half-life, CSF and brain concentration in humans: **not retrieved (UNKNOWN)**. No paediatric data. The structure is a monoamine oxidase A inhibitor of the beta-carboline class that is a constituent of ayahuasca (psychoactive context in the same abstract).
4. **Safety and counter-evidence.** Psychoactivity and GI effects above (human, adult, single dose). Zebrafish: harmine disrupted the blood-brain barrier and caused cerebral haemorrhage through Claudin-5 and mitophagy changes (PMID 39742937, abstract; one fish study, high exposure unknown). A MAO-A inhibitor carries interaction risks with serotonergic and sympathomimetic drugs (general knowledge, UNVERIFIED here; not fetched). Seizure-provoking reports: none retrieved in the searched abstracts (a search count, not a negative). **Neurodevelopmental relevance of DYRK1A inhibition**: DYRK1A inhibitors raise proliferation of human neural progenitors in culture (harmine, +71.5 percent proliferating pool at 4 days; INDY same; pargyline, a MAO inhibitor without DYRK1A activity, did not; PMID 27957390). In a WWOX-deficient radial glia with S/G2M accumulation and reduced neuronal output, more progenitor proliferation is the direction the seed paper interprets as harmful. A prenatal DYRK1A-inhibition outcome study exists only as a preprint title in my searches (not read).
5. **Edge provenance.** DYRK1A -> MYC (-1) rests on one SIGNOR record (AML cell lines, DYRK1A overexpression, c-Myc degradation; PMID 24901999) and text-mining of the same paper. Not neural; protein stability. A 2025 KMT2A-rearranged ALL paper shows the same sign with the inhibitor (MYC and ERK activation, PMID 40148558, abstract level). No evidence for the edge in radial glia or in a neural progenitor. Judgement: **plausible only as a cancer-line edge; neural validity UNKNOWN**; as a transcript-level predictor for S100 it is a context-free artefact (see `seed_assessment_Q6_MYC.md`).
6. **Expression** (Human Protein Atlas, adult brain, queried 2026-10-30, `expression_hpa_summary.tsv`): DYRK1A detected in all single-nuclei brain cell types and all brain regions, low specificity; MAOA detected in many brain cell types, cell-type enhanced in endothelial cells and pericytes. The atlas holds no developing radial glia; DYRK1A in RG: **UNVERIFIED** (depth: adult atlas only).
7. **Trials** (ClinicalTrials.gov API v2, 2026-10-30): harmine alone 7 studies, all healthy volunteers, depression, or psychedelic pharmacology (phase 1 Mount Sinai NCT05526430); epilepsy/seizure 0, hypomyelination/leukodystrophy 0, neurodevelopment 0, WWOX 0, CNS tumour 0; paediatric terms 1 (a PET depression study). "0" means no hits for the query string on that date.
8. **Verification in a WWOX-deficient system.** Feasible in the published KO or patient-derived organoids or 2D NSCs, but only worth running after the seed-level valence test. If run: harmine and an AnnH75-type DYRK1A-selective analogue and a MAO-A-only inhibitor (pargyline) as controls, graded 0.1 to 10 micromolar, readouts SOX2+MYC+/SOX2+, pH3+ fraction, NEUN+/SATB2+ fractions, MYC protein by blot. Adaptive reading predicts neuron output not worsened; harmful reading predicts more S/G2M and fewer NEUN+ cells. **Falsifier of the compound as a candidate:** DYRK1A-selective analogue lowers neuron output in KO organoids. Limits on transfer: adult human exposure only; psychoactivity; MAO-A potency; the seed is a prenatal substrate.

Not done: harmine pharmacokinetic literature beyond the phase 1 abstract; FAERS (no medicinal product); full text of any cited paper.
