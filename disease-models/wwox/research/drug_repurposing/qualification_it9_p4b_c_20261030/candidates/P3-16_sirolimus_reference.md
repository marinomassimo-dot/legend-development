## Edge and seed question

* **Is sirolimus a Q6 proposal?** No. The P2 Q6 candidate files contain no row for sirolimus or rapamycin; its only P2 appearance is the Q3 direct-search arm (target token DDIT3, guard ISR_SIGN_UNRESOLVED).
* **Why the network never reaches MYC from it.** ChEMBL curates sirolimus as "FK506-binding protein 1A inhibitor" (FKBP1A CHEMBL1902); **FKBP1A has no outgoing edge** in the SIGNOR plus CollecTRI network used by P2 (0 edges). MTOR has 30 outgoing edges but **no MTOR -> MYC edge in SIGNOR**; the only MTOR -> MYC record in OmniPath is text-mining (PhosphoSite, MIMP and related), unsigned (neither stimulation nor inhibition). Within three hops there are 13 MTOR-to-MYC paths with mixed signs (for example MTOR -> BTRC -> MYC +1; MTOR -> AKT1 -> FOXO6 -> MYC -1; MTOR -> AKT1 -> EP300 -> MYC +1). The network therefore cannot sign the effect of mTOR inhibition on MYC. Absence is a limit of the network, not a finding that sirolimus does not alter MYC.
* **Does the MYC seed motivate sirolimus?** Not through anything held here. The seed paper (read) does not measure mTOR; its metabolic discussion points to PGC-1alpha and ERRgamma and to Wnt/GSK3B. A MYC-lowering rationale for mTORC1 inhibition (translational control) is general knowledge and is **UNVERIFIED** in this folder. The seed itself is WEAK-DIRECTION-ONLY (`seed_assessment_Q6_MYC.md`).
