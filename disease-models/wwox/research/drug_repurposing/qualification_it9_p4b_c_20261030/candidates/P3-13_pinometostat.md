# P3-13 pinometostat (EPZ-5676; Q6, arm "opposes", edge DOT1L -> MYC +1)

Decision: **DROP.** Decisive data: in the mouse the deletion of Dot1l in the telencephalon reduces progenitors and the generation of SATB2 upper-layer neurons and disturbs layering (PMID 30329130, abstract level), i.e. DOT1L loss moves the same readouts the seed paper reports as already reduced in WWOX-KO and WOREE organoids, in the harmful direction; and the network edge is a breast-cancer co-binding record, not a record of DOT1L raising MYC. Biological promise: low; depth: database and abstract level; feasibility: low (continuous intravenous infusion, no CNS data); next-experiment priority: none.

1. **Identity.** ChEMBL CHEMBL3414626, InChIKey LXFOLMYKSYSZQS-LURJZOHASA-N; PubChem by name gives LXFOLMYKSYSZQS-XKHGBIBOSA-N (CID 57345410) and PubChem by the ChEMBL key returns 404. Same 14-character skeleton block; the isomeric SMILES differ only in that ChEMBL specifies the cis/trans configuration at the two cyclobutane carbons while PubChem leaves them undefined; the ribose-type centres agree. **VERIFIED as the same compound (stereo specification differs at the cyclobutane ring)**; the P3 flag is closed, with that note. Max phase 2; no salt.
2. **Target and selectivity** (human, nM, type B): DOT1L Ki 0.012 to 0.08 (3 "=" rows, plus one "<"), IC50 0.74 to 52 (7 "=" rows, 5 documents), cellular EC50 9. ChEMBL holds no kinase or other methyltransferase panel for it (HDAC6 percent inhibition only; ENL/AF9 reader domains without values). The review abstract (PMID 28229434) states sub-nanomolar affinity and more than 37,000 fold selectivity against non-MLL histone methyltransferases (abstract level, not a ChEMBL value).
3. **Exposure.** No label. Adult phase 1 (51 patients, 54 and 90 mg/m2/day by continuous intravenous infusion, 28-day cycles; steady state at 4 to 8 h, rapid decline after stopping; PMID 29724899). Paediatric phase 1 in MLL-rearranged leukaemia was run (registry NCT02141828, ages 3 months to 18 years, completed; results not retrieved). CSF or brain concentration: **UNKNOWN**. Oral route: none.
4. **Safety.** Adults: fatigue, nausea, constipation, febrile neutropenia (abstract). Seizure-provoking reports: none retrieved. Neurodevelopmental biology: the Dot1l-deletion cortex phenotype above; a 2022 screen found a DOT1L inhibitor to increase dopaminergic neuron formation from stem cells (PMID 36578445), a different cell type and context.
5. **Edge provenance.** DOT1L -> MYC (+1), mechanism "binding", one SIGNOR record (PMID 26199140): DOT1L cooperates with the c-Myc-p300 complex at EMT regulator promoters in breast cancer, without a change in MYC level. **Context-free artefact for the transcript seed** (activity cooperation coded as activation). A different paper (colorectal cancer lines, PMID 31888761) shows that DOT1L silencing or inhibition lowers c-Myc expression via H3K79me2; that supports the sign in cancer but is not the network record and is not neural.
6. **Expression** (HPA): DOT1L detected in all brain single-nuclei cell types, low specificity, all regions.
7. **Trials (2026-10-30).** 4 studies, all leukaemia (adult and paediatric); epilepsy 0, neurodevelopment 0, CNS tumour 0, WWOX 0; paediatric terms 2 (NCT02141828 completed, NCT03724084 terminated).
8. **Verification.** If tested, H3K79me2 and MYC in KO radial glia first (is DOT1L activity the lever on MYC in this cell type?); inhibitor titration with a catalytically dead control cannot be bought as a tool, so use a second DOT1L inhibitor (SGC0946-type) and DOT1L knockdown. Harmful-direction prediction: fewer SATB2+/NEUN+ cells. Falsifier of the DROP: MYC protein falls and neuron fraction rises on inhibitor. Transfer limits: intravenous only, no CNS data, epigenetic effects across lineages, cortical-development phenotype in mouse knockout.
