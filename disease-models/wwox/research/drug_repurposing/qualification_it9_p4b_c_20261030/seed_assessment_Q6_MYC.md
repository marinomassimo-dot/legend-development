## Verdict (pre-registered scale: STRONG / MODERATE / WEAK-DIRECTION-ONLY / INSUFFICIENT)

**WEAK-DIRECTION-ONLY for generating drug candidates.** The direction (MYC higher in WWOX-deficient radial glia) is reasonably supported: three human genotype classes, two measurement levels, one laboratory. What the seed does not give is a sign of benefit or a causal handle that a drug could pull: the single causal test is a multi-kinase inhibitor, in one system, read in the supplement I did not open. A candidate generator that takes one transcript from this seed, one context-free edge and one compound is therefore producing "compatible action" hypotheses with no demonstrated valence.

## What does not support generating candidates

1. **No valence.** The paper's model says MYC up impairs differentiation (cell cycle S/G2M accumulation, high CytoTRACE potency, fewer neurons). It does not exclude a compensatory reading: the Discussion itself says elevated p53 "could suggest a compensatory response", and that damaged RG differentiate more readily in patient organoids. The seed row S100/S101 in P1 already records valence as UNCERTAIN; the P3 selection nevertheless produced both arms (opposes: AURKB, DOT1L; mimics: DYRK1A, CUL4A).
2. **The causal test is not MYC-specific.** A51 is described as a multi-kinase inhibitor "established to suppress Wnt and MYC expression", given at 125 nM, week 8 to 15, only in KO hESC organoids. Total SOX2+ progenitors fell as well as SOX2+MYC+; NEUN+ rose, CTIP2+ did not change, SATB2+ rose mildly. A kinase inhibitor with many targets cannot attribute the rescue to MYC. The Discussion concedes that silencing MYC is difficult "given the pervasive role of MYC". No WT-plus-A51 or MYC-specific (degron, shRNA, OmoMYC-type) arm is described in the main text.
3. **Cell-cycle composition inside RG.** MYC is higher in S and G2/M RG in KO but not WT (Fig 4D) and KO has more S/G2M RG (Fig 4A-C). A part of the MYC difference can therefore be cell-cycle composition inside the pooled RG label; the authors abstain from sub-clusters (cRG, oRG). The "within-cell-type contrast" answers the cell-type confound, not the cell-cycle one.
4. **Single laboratory, small numbers, no replication of the single-cell result**; the E-MTAB-14792 deposit is access-controlled (HTTP 403 on 2026-10-25 and 2026-10-29), so the seed cannot be re-derived.
5. **Developmental window.** The MYC signal is a week 7 to 16 organoid phenotype (mid-gestation equivalent; the paper places the MYC peak near w16 in vivo). Patients are treated after birth. The Discussion itself frames progenitor-targeted restoration as "time-limited". A drug aimed at the RG state is a prenatal question (window protection), which makes any teratogen or antimitotic ethically and practically unusable.
6. **Engine side.** The Q6 candidates reach the seed through one node (MYC) that is a hub; 13 of 15 selected proposals rest on a single network edge (P3 README). The four MYC edges I traced (below) are each a single record from a cancer cell line.

For all four: single record, cancer-cell context, protein-level or activity edge, no neural or progenitor measurement. By the pre-registered test they are **context-free for neural cells** (criterion (a) met); criterion (b) (sign refers to protein stability or activity, not transcript level) is met for the transcript seed S100 and only partly for the protein seed S101 (a fraction of cells, not a degradation rate).

## Priority of the next experiment at the seed level (before any compound)

Resolve the valence with a MYC-specific lever in the same KO and patient-derived systems: inducible MYC knockdown or degron (and, as a second modality, a MYC-MAX dimerisation blocker) at graded depth, started at the week 8 point the paper used for A51, with vector and WT-plus-lever arms; readouts SOX2+MYC+/SOX2+, pH3+ fraction of SOX2+, NEUN+ and SATB2+ fractions, tricycle-style S/G2M fraction by scRNA-seq with a donor-or-organoid pseudobulk (not cell-level) test. **Falsifier of "MYC rise is harmful":** graded MYC reduction fails to raise NEUN+/SATB2+ fractions or lowers neuron output (which would favour a compensatory reading and invert the opposes/mimics arms). Feasibility: the laboratory that holds the lines can run it; availability of the lines to others is UNVERIFIED.
