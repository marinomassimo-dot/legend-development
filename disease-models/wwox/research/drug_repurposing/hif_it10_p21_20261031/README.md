## 2. Task 1: the premises (table: `premises_it10_p21.tsv`; direction table: `direction_evidence_it10_p21.tsv`)

Keeping separate: transcript v protein; HIF-1 v HIF-2; WWOX effect v cell composition; protective adaptation v harmful component; probe activity v benefit.

### (a) The metabolic change in the WWOX-deficient neural system
* **Organoid (GSE156243, 4 KO v 2 WT, week 15, one hESC line)**: HK2, SLC2A1, LDHA, EPAS1 up (CPM ratios 4.73, 2.67, 1.70, 3.80; deposited padj 8.8e-34, 1.0e-9, 4.9e-6, 1.9e-3); HIF1A flat. **New detail:** the elevation is selective, not a uniform glycolytic upshift: ADM, HK2, P4HA1, SLC2A1, BHLHE40, ANKRD37, EGLN3, DDIT4 are up while PGK1, ALDOA, ENO1, PKM, PFKP, NDRG1 are flat (`maturity_stress_check_it10_p21.tsv`). A partly canonical hypoxia-response pattern, compatible with HIF activity, with oxygen limitation of the organoid, and with MYC (+0.56 log2, a primary-author rival).
* **Composition/maturity separated from a WWOX effect**: HK2, SLC2A1, LDHA fall steeply with culture time in WWOX-unperturbed spheroids (about -2 per log10 day), so immaturity could mimic the rise; but the KO's offsets in the 12 progenitor/neuronal markers predict only a small shift (median +0.08 to +0.16 against observed +0.75 to +2.18; best single marker +0.79 for HK2 and +0.99 for SLC2A1), and EPAS1/EGLN3, which rise with time, are higher in the KO although the KO is the less mature by the same markers. So **maturity as measured by these markers explains a minor part; this does not exclude a composition effect by unmeasured subpopulations or culture state** (organoids activate a glycolysis/ER-stress subpopulation in every protocol: Bhaduri 2020; Gruffi). Bulk stress markers (PGK1 -0.09, ARCN1 +0.18, GORASP2 +0.20) are not higher in the KO, which argues against a gross stressed-cell expansion at bulk sensitivity but cannot settle it. The primary authors also report more astrocytic markers in KO organoids (2021). The calibration is from another laboratory (6 iPSC lines, time is not maturity).
* **hNPC (GSE126075)**: one library per arm; WWOX is not shown lower in the shWWOX progenitor library (gate 1 failed in IT5); the glycolysis set (+0.46) was "not carried" by the declared rule. **It cannot count as a second neural system for a WWOX effect.**
* **Mouse brain (GSE158681/2, P17, four contrasts, new computation)**: no elevation; Epas1 -0.21 to -0.58 (animal-separated in 3 of 4), Slc2a1, Ldha, Pgk1 lower, Ndrg1 and P4ha1 lower in all four, Hk2 flat, Hif1a within +-0.2, Gfap up (gliosis). Caveats: bulk, transcript, animals from lines with severe epilepsy and death at 3-4 weeks, background differences between control groups (IT6). It does not side with the organoid. **A species/in-vivo-versus-organoid disagreement is the largest unresolved fact of this part.**
* **Fly (PMID 42909904)**: neuronal Wwox knockdown raises L-lactate (1.60-fold, Padj 0.0636) and, with amyloid, Ldh; the authors attribute it to Atf4, not sima (below).
* **Activity as a rival**: chronic neuronal activity raises LDHA through AMPK/HIF-1alpha in cultured neurons and human epileptic tissue (PMID 36655171); patient-derived organoids are reported hyperexcitable (abstract). A HIF-linked glycolytic shift is therefore expected in any hyperactive KO network, whatever WWOX does to HIF.
* **Absent**: no lactate, flux, MRS, FDG-PET or seahorse datum for a WWOX-deficient neural cell, mouse brain or patient (negative search B1, B4, B5, B9; not a negative result).
* **What could change it**: single-cell data of the KO organoids by cell type (controlled access; an EBI request draft exists in the repository), a hypoxia marker in matched-size organoids, a second KO clone/line, any lactate/flux measurement.

### (b) HIF-1alpha versus HIF-2alpha: protein v transcript, compartment, oxygen
* **HIF-1alpha, non-neural, protein**: Wwox-null MEFs show nuclear HIF-1alpha in normoxia (qualitative blot), less hydroxylation, no genotype difference at 1% O2 (PMID 25012504); muscle, liver the same direction (same laboratory). The human fibroblast pool (independent laboratory) shows **HRE-luciferase up in 3 of 4 conditions, HIF1A mRNA 1.5-1.9-fold, total (ICC) HIF-1alpha up in 2, cytoplasmic blot down in 2, nuclear blot unchanged, lactate up in all 4** (one blot repetition shown). Two laboratories, no neural cell: not five confirmations.
* **HIF-2alpha**: nothing on WWOX and HIF-2alpha/EPAS1 in any system (search B6: 0). The organoid EPAS1 transcript (3.80x, 44-52 raw counts in the WT libraries) is the only datum; Epas1 is lower in the mouse brain.
* **Neural protein**: only the fly reporter. sima:GFP is unchanged (t = 0.6623, P = 0.5185, N = 7-9; adult day-14 brain, normoxia, whole-brain ROI, amyloid background, one genotype pair). The Results call it "endogenously tagged", the Methods name "UAS-sima:GFP" (driver-expressed: reports protein stability, not transcription). Sima is the single fly HIF-alpha: **no HIF-1 v HIF-2 split is possible**.
* **Compartment/oxygen**: the three human/mouse readouts disagree on compartment; the MEF difference vanishes at 1% O2; physioxia of neural tissue (3-5%) was not tested in any WWOX system. This is why step 0 keeps compartments and three oxygen conditions.
* **What could change it**: HIF-1alpha and HIF-2alpha protein with hydroxylation in matched neural cells at 5% O2; the authors' clarification of the sima reporter.

### (c) The functionally useful direction (`direction_evidence_it10_p21.tsv`, 19 rows)
* **Acute hypoxic-ischaemic injury**: loss of neuronal HIF-1alpha increases injury in the neonatal P7 model (19672073), removes the benefit of hypoxic preconditioning (24713818) and of cooling (40159309) (three reports, one research group, abstracts); loss of neuronal HIF-2alpha increases neonatal injury (34487578); adult neuron-specific loss increases infarct and mortality (Baranova 2007, read) while the adult brain-wide deletion of Helton 2005 (read) is **protective** and shows no baseline phenotype. Lean: protective in neonatal acute injury; adult sign conflicts.
* **Chronic hypoxia**: HIF-2alpha-driven ceramide catabolism and synuclein phosphorylation impair cognition (36450714, abstract); neuronal HIF-1/2 deficiency modestly worsens behaviour (33966229).
* **Seizures/excitability**: DMOG (agonist) delayed kindling and lowered spontaneous seizures in the chronic phase, KC7F2 did the opposite, neither mattered acutely (24889205, abstract); HIF-1alpha overexpression raises CaV3.2 and network firing (42276303); miR-485 lowering HDAC5/HIF1alpha/PFKFB3 accompanies fewer seizures (34021541). **Two-sided.**
* **Developing brain**: HIF-2alpha neural deletion reduces retrosplenial pyramidal neurons (33154420) or has no effect on cortical development (42873777): conflict. Neuronal HIF-1alpha deletion is compatible with viability and normal gross brain weight (Baranova, read) but HIF-1alpha is required for NSC maintenance in adults (25505323).
* **A persistent glycolytic state in neurons has its own documented cost** (forced Pfkfb3: mitochondrial deficiency, cognitive decline; 38789798), which supports plausibility for H-harm without involving HIF.
* **What could change it**: any manipulation of HIF in a WWOX-deficient neural model with a functional readout (the experiment of section 5).

### (d) Transferability of the non-neural precedents
Not warranted by default. MEFs, fibroblasts, muscle and liver are proliferating or metabolically flexible cells; neurons are hypoglycolytic by construction (PFKFB3 degradation); the WW1-HIF-1alpha interaction was shown in HEK293 only; oxygen and compartment dependence is unmapped in neural cells; activity itself regulates HIF/LDHA in neurons. The fly datum is the only neural protein-level HIF-alpha readout and it is negative (with the caveats above). **What could change it:** the step 0 measurement.

## 3. Task 2: alternatives (`alternatives_it10_p21.tsv`, `verdicts_it10_p21.tsv`; raw rows in `identity_it10_p21.tsv`, `chembl_*`, `catalogue_lookup_it10_p21.tsv`, `pubchem_crosscheck_it10_p21.tsv`, `labels_us_*`, `trials_it10_p21.tsv`)

Identity was cross-checked between ChEMBL (via InChIKey) and PubChem (via name) for the 17 named agents: 14 give the same CID; three differ for stated reasons (acriflavine is a mixture with no single InChIKey; KC7F2 exists in PubChem only, not in ChEMBL or the catalogue; enarodustat differs by a tautomer representation). Points that matter: PX-478's name hit is the dihydrochloride salt record; acriflavine is a **mixture** (C27H25ClN6 = 3,6-diamino-10-methylacridinium chloride + 3,6-diaminoacridine, by formula arithmetic);
DMOG is a **prodrug** (hydrolysed to MOG, taken up by MCT2, active N-oxalylglycine); topotecan's active species is the lactone; enarodustat's ChEMBL and PubChem InChIKeys differ by a tautomer representation (same formula and synonym); PT2385 and belzutifan are isomers, not salts; IOX2 and roxadustat are isomers by formula. Potency figures are pooled ChEMBL rows from heterogeneous assays (n stated), not comparable potencies.

## 4. What the evidence does not let anyone say
No claim of benefit, harm or selectivity follows for any compound. "Insufficient support" never means "inactive". A compatible mechanism is not a predicted benefit. Nothing here was run.

### 5.2 Alternative hypotheses and the distinguishing measurement (`hypotheses_measurements_it10_p21.tsv`)
H0-composition/culture, H0-activity, H0-MYC, H-harm, H-protect, H-neutral. The measurement is **K01 step 0 followed by arms A-C, unchanged in structure**. Additions demanded by sources read here (marked NEW in the table): (1) physioxia (5% O2) as the primary condition beside 21% and 1% (PMID 42873777; the MEF difference vanishes at 1%); (2) a hypoxia marker and size matching in organoids, and stressed-cell filtering of any single-cell data (31996853, 35919947); (3) an activity-silenced arm (36655171); (4) a MYC loss-of-function arm (42397075); (5) network activity (multielectrode) as a functional readout, because HIF-1alpha can raise firing (42276303); (6) arm C starts with genetic VHL/EGLN1 loss, and any PHD inhibitor runs only beside HIF1A/EPAS1-null and PHD1-3-null cells and is read compound by compound (42150425; DMOG additionally needs an MCT2/glutaminolysis control, 30297875). The IT9 deciding readouts and the guard rule stay: lactate or acidification and glucose uptake decide; PGK1, ALDOA, ENO1, PFKP, PKM are reported individually; the six IT7 genes cannot decide. Two independent KO clones/lines with isogenic add-back are needed because the public organoid contrast is one line and clone assignment of libraries is not stated in the records read.

### 5.3 Reopening condition
Reopen the documentary search when any of these appears: (i) HIF-alpha protein, hydroxylation or activity in a WWOX-deficient mammalian neural cell; (ii) single-cell or proteomic data of KO organoids or Wwox-deleted brain (including the controlled-access organoid single-cell deposit); (iii) lactate, MRS or flux in a WWOX-deficient neural system or patient; (iv) a replicated shWWOX/KO hNPC or neuron dataset with demonstrated knockdown; (v) an independent report on WWOX and HIF-2alpha; (vi) a correction or retraction touching a premise paper (a retraction was found for acriflavine during this round, so a re-check of publication type is cheap). Re-run the queries of `literature_search_log_it10_p21.tsv` for A, B and C at that time.

## 7. Disclosures (non-blind steps, deviations) and UNVERIFIED

* **Non-blind:** I read IT9's K01 README, premises and records first and designed the questions against them; the corrections in section 0 are comparisons with those texts. No independent verifier saw this package.
* **Post hoc, unregistered computations:** `maturity_stress_check_it10_p21.py` and `mouse_p17_hif_genes_it10_p21.py` were written and run in one pass; their question is in each docstring but was not committed before the data were opened, and the extra hypoxia-response and stress genes (ADM, BHLHE40, P4HA1, ANKRD37, DDIT4, NDRG1, CA9, ENO2, ERO1A, VEGFA, GFAP etc.) were chosen by me from background knowledge after seeing the IT9 table. * **Not read / UNVERIFIED:** primary characterisations of KC7F2 (Narita 2009), echinomycin and acriflavine beyond abstracts, belzutifan's PAS-B mechanism and HIF-1alpha sparing at a primary source, topotecan and digoxin CNS penetration (titles only), the digoxin dose and schedule in the Wwox-null mouse experiment, PX-478 purity/supply/phase 1 results, the roxadustat thyroid-receptor rows (Morita 2025, via ChEMBL/IT9), molidustat's lack of thyromimetic action, the claim that most PHIs penetrate the brain poorly (an abstract sentence), every row of `direction_evidence_it10_p21.tsv` marked abstract, supplementary files of all papers, whether 35328751's line is a complete knockout, clone assignment of the GSE156243 libraries, the incubator oxygen of the organoid deposit, the fly reporter's true design, HIF-2alpha protein in any WWOX-deficient cell, the EPAS1 cell-type source in the organoid, the label status of molidustat, enarodustat and desidustat (ChEMBL phase only), paediatric data beyond labels and registry counts.
