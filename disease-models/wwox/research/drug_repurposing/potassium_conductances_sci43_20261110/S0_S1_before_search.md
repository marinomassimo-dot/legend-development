## S1 — the decisive question

**Can a change in K+ conductances explain part of the combination resting potential / input resistance / sag / rebound seen in the neuron-specific Wwox knockout layer II/III neuron, and does that offer a lever different from or complementary to HCN?** Level of each claim kept apart: neuron (membrane properties at P13–P17 in slices), circuit (bursts in 36/42 knockout, 4/23 heterozygote, 0/11 wild-type slices), seizure (none recorded in these slices), development (P13–P17 is a window of rapid change of Rin, tau and conductance expression).

**DATO from the one source (34634460, Table 1; read in earlier packages)**: Rin 102.88 / 143.26 / 143.03 MΩ (WT / HT / KO), tau 18.55 / 18.79 / 21.25 ms, rest −79.25 / −75.51 / −73.62 mV, sag at a fixed −200 pA step 1.18 / 2.04 / 2.57 mV, rebound larger only in KO, K-gluconate internal, 35 C, bath K+ 3.5 mM, no junction correction, cells (not animals) as units, no synaptic blocker in the Methods drug list (INFERENZA).

### What the one published table already constrains (INFERENZA, computed by the Orchestrator at S0, **not preregistered**)

The arithmetic was done by me once at S0, so a true preregistration is not claimed for it; the preregistered version is the S7 analysis on external calibration tables. With resting conductance G = 1/Rin and a single lost conductance of reversal E_x: ΔV_rest = ΔG·(V0 − E_x)/(G − ΔG). WT → KO: G 9.72 → 6.99 nS (−28 percent), ΔV +5.63 mV → implied E_x about −93.7 mV on the recorded scale; WT → HT: ΔV +3.74 mV, implied E_x about −88.8 mV. A pure K+ loss with the solutions' E_K (about −97.5 mV, no junction correction needed) would give +7.1 mV on the recorded scale (observed +5.6 KO, +3.7 HT) but only +2.1 mV if the recorded potentials carry the borrowed −13 mV junction offset. So: **on the recorded scale a K+-type loss is arithmetically compatible with the Rin–rest pair; on the corrected scale it is not, which makes the unmeasured junction potential and the E_K arithmetic decisive for E2.** Tau flat (18.6, 18.8, 21.3 ms) with Rin +39 percent is, under one compartment, the signature of a smaller capacitance (IT40: not a pure leak loss at constant C; the dispersion makes it weaker than first written). The sag at −200 pA and rebound are not used here. Both points are inference from means of cells treated as independent units, with no variance carried; they select what to read, they decide nothing.

### What would change the pharmacological choice

* If a K+ family contributes an appreciable share of the resting conductance of layer II/III pyramidal neurons at about this age (the share is what the literature can give; the lost share needed is about 28 percent of the resting conductance), a family-specific difference current (Ba2+ at a low concentration, an XE991-sensitive current, a ZD7288-sensitive current in the same cell) replaces "an HCN blocker first" as the first INV-18 arm. If no family carries that much (for example Kv7 contributes a small share at rest in these neurons), the E2 explanation is capped and the K+ branch cannot account for the Rin change alone.
* If the sources give a tool with documented engagement in cortical pyramidal neurons of that age, it can enter as a **sonda** (probe) for the difference-current experiment, not a candidate.
* A K+ opener as a **useful compensation without restoring WWOX** is a separate hypothesis (generated in S3 with its premise and falsifier): it asks whether a hyperpolarising lever reduces the burst at matched input-output suppression, not whether the K+ conductance is deficient.
