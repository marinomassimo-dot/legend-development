# SCI43 producer answers (og-k, 2026-11-10)

Non-canonical research material. Not medical advice; no dose, schedule or compound recommendation; nothing about WWOX is claimed. Labels: M = measured in the source, D = declared by the source, C = copied by the source from another paper, P = producer arithmetic. All shares are P, from printed means of cells treated as units.

**K1 (share of the resting input conductance, WT neurons).**
- **Kir (Ba2+ 60 uM):** about 23 percent (Rin 172.7 to about 224 Mohm, rest +1.5 mV) in mouse L2/3 auditory cortex, P28-P42, 34 C, synapses intact, K-gluconate; the only measured family share of the order of 28 percent, but a transfer in age and area (D01, D02; shares S01, S03). Kir2 and a K2P-type leak are not separable with Ba2+ (S15).
- **Kv7:** depends on where the cell rests. XE-991 5 uM did nothing near -83 mV (adult rat L2/3, D03) and is expected to do nothing there (D05); loss of KCNQ2 raised Rin 25 percent (share about 20 percent) at P15-P20 with rest near -67 mV (D04); an opener changed neither Rin nor rest in the text (D06). At the Wwox rest on any junction correction (below -79 mV recorded) the Kv7 share is probably small, but no source measured that (inference).
- **K2P:** not found in neocortical pyramidal neurons (D09; perimeter: PubMed tool and Europe PMC, 2026-11-10, Q11-Q17, Q22).
- **Ih (ZD7288 10 uM):** about 6 percent in L2/3 (D07); in deep layers it can carry most of the conductance at -80 mV (D08, 'data not shown').
- Blocker effects show that a current contributes, not that it is altered in Wwox (D16).

**K2 (development).** Layer-3 Rin falls about 47 percent per interval P7 to P14 to P28 (405.9, 213.4, 110.3 Mohm), rest -61.6, -70.0, -73.0 mV (D10, prelimbic cortex, 31 C); Kv7 current density about doubles P11-14 to P20-24 (D11). No developmental source for Kir2 or HCN contributions was reached (Oswald and Reyes 2008: abstract only, PMC bot check).

**K3 (tools).** XE-991 and linopirdine are state-dependent and weak at rest (D05); IC50 values are copied, not primary. No Ba2+ or ZD7288 IC50 was found; Ba2+ at 200 uM also blocks a leak-like component (T04); ZD7288 off-target actions were not located (T05). Engagement in the neurons studied: yes for each, as a functional or current readout (tools.tsv).

**K4 (models).** No model with Rin up and rest depolarised and a measured difference current was found (D12, M05). Two Kv7-loss models (Kcnq2 cKO +25 percent; Ank2 cKO about +62 percent) raise Rin with no significant rest change and no neuronal current; calibration list: Fmr1 KO (Rin -28 percent, rest -3.7 mV, Ba2+ current larger), Wnk3 KO (abstract only).

**K5 (junction potential).** Measured: -10 mV (114 K-gluconate, 6 KCl; value to add to recorded voltages; P17-P20 mouse L2/3, RT) and 8 mV (140/14, sign not stated, 32-35 C); calculated: -14.4 and 16.2 mV in two other papers (D14, D15). The Wwox solutions differ (135 K-gluconate, 10 NaCl, 1 MgCl2; ACSF 123 NaCl, 3.5 KCl; 35 C) and were not measured; a bracket of 8-16 mV puts the borrowed -13 mV inside it. P: the Ba2+-current reversal in D02 implies about 16 mV for that solution pair.
