## 2. An alternative the first mechanism may have hidden

**Extracellular K+ and glial buffering (KH4 variant).** The Ba2+ share was measured with a bath application, which also blocks astroglial Kir4.1 (abstract-level support, 19515915); a bath-Ba2+ Rin rise with a small depolarisation can therefore include a glial or extracellular-K+ component, and the same applies to any bath K2P-blocker group. In the Wwox slices 36 of 42 KO slices burst, so activity-dependent K+ accumulation is a competing source of a depolarised rest. The neuron-specific (Synapsin-Cre) deletion makes a primary glial defect unlikely, not a secondary one. **Measurement that separates it (IPOTESI):** the ladder's step 1 (same cells with and without blockers, bursting and non-bursting slices) and a blocker delivered only to the recorded neuron (not sourced here). **Consumer:** already in `hypotheses.tsv` KH4 and the Ba2+ row of `tools_roles.tsv` (limit); no new row. RNA tables in the repository carry Kcnj10 (an astrocytic Kir4.1 gene) as "measurable" in other datasets; an RNA profile is not function, and no use is made of it.
