## Direct answer

**Question (S0_S1_before_search.md).** Can a change in K+ conductances explain part of the combination resting potential, input resistance, sag and rebound, and give a lever different from or complementary to HCN?

* **A K+ change can carry the resistance-and-rest part, not the sag part; this is an IPOTESI with a measured scale, not a finding.** The printed Table 1 (34634460) means imply a net loss of about 2.7 nS, 28 percent of the resting conductance (Rin 102.9 to 143.0 MΩ), for a depolarisation of 5.6 mV. By measured share in wild-type neurons of other preparations: a Ba2+-sensitive resting conductance (Kir2-type and/or K2P-type) about 23 percent (adult mouse L2/3 auditory cortex, 34 C; a preprint the verifier could not open); Ih about 6 percent (same preprint); Kv7 is expected small at the Wwox rest because its current starts near −70 mV and XE-991 acts weakly at hyperpolarised potentials (28483800; the rest of the Kv7 share is not measured). The extra sag (1.18 to 2.57 mV) is not carried by a K+ conductance in this reading; a more resistive cell is driven further by the same −200 pA step, and Ih is an open contributor (INV-18 main arm).
* **The arithmetic that was meant to favour a K+ change over a smaller cell does not do so.** Verifier finding, reproduced by the Orchestrator: a membrane scaled to 0.67 of its conductance with a fixed 1.5 nS shunt reproduces the Wwox pair exactly and has the same "K+-like" implied reversal (−93.7 mV on the recorded scale; it equals the native rest of the scaled membrane). With the printed SEMs the 95 percent interval of the implied reversal is −143 to −77 mV (KO) and −149 to −66 mV (HT), which touches the maturation-type values (−72 to −79 mV), and for a junction offset of 8–16 mV the point estimate lies 4–12 mV below E_K on the recorded scale. **What survives:** a lost depolarising conductance of the Ih or cation type (reversal about −30 to 0 mV) is excluded as the single cause at 95 percent for KO (verifier propagation, intervals above); the pair constrains any single-cause explanation (a conductance loss of about 28 percent whose reversal is at or below about −85 mV on the recorded scale, or a size-plus-shunt change); it does not decide between them. Cell capacitance and per-pF difference currents decide.
* **New experimental choice (S4).** The first INV-18 arm should be a **difference-current ladder in the same cells** (`output/experiment_ladder.tsv`): step 0 capacitance measured directly and the cell-selection rule stated, step 1 synaptic blockade, step 2 Ba2+-sensitive current per pF (60 µM) and step 2b a higher-Ba2+ and K2P-blocker arm, step 3 XE-991-sensitive current **at a depolarised holding potential** (not at rest), step 4 ZD7288-sensitive current, step 5 dynamic-clamp sufficiency (with a control leak at the WT rest), step 6 an opener only after step 3 and at a matched effect. This replaces "an Ih blocker first" with "measure which conductance differs, per pF, first". Outcome matrix: `output/outcome_matrix.tsv`.
* **Pharmacological hypotheses kept with premise and falsifier** (`output/hypotheses.tsv`): KH1 a Ba2+-sensitive resting K+ conductance deficit (compensation lever to be tested; no activator tool was read; no Kir2 activator is in the catalogue), KH2 a Kv7 spiking-range deficit (opener as spike-frequency/AHP comparator; the published F-I slope does not support it, weakly), KH3 Ih for sag/rebound (existing arm), KH4 network consequence (weak HT argument), KH5 size/maturation (no lever). **No candidate; no compound is promoted; no benefit is shown or assumed.**

## Counter-evidence and limits

* Every K+ share comes from another preparation (adult, other areas, other temperatures); no source measures a K+ current in a Wwox-deficient neuron, and a blocker effect shows that a current contributes, not that it is altered.
* HT carries the Rin change only by point estimate; the neuron-specific Cre line and the selection of pyramidal cells by Rin and tau are stated in the paper (selection on the compared variables).
* The XE-991, linopirdine and Ba2+ potencies are copied by the sources, not primary; ZD7288 potency and off-target actions were not located; K2P tools were read in adult dendrites only.
* Ba2+ in a bath with synapses intact (the preprint states none) can act on glial Kir4.1 (abstract-level support, 19515915).
* HCN1 gain-of-function mouse: retigabine increased ECoG spiking at abstract level (IT35): an opener is not assumed protective.
