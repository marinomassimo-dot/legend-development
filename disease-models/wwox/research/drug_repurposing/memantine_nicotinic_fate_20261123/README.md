# Memantine, α7 nicotinic receptors and radial-glia fate in WWOX loss

**Decision, 23 November 2026.** Human developmental tissue supports a role for α7 nicotinic signaling in the balance of progenitors and neurons. An exposure-relevant effect of memantine on that balance in WWOX deficiency remains unestablished. The rat receptor result makes this off-target mechanism look considerably more plausible than the measured potency in a human receptor preparation. The WWOX organoids show no stable shift in total α7-family RNA. Prioritize a current measurement in identified human radial glia before using a change in neuronal markers as evidence for this drug mechanism. [Prespecified questions](prediction.md) · [Earlier NMDA comparison](../memantine_20261123/README.md)

## A chemically distinct way for memantine to alter development

If loss of WWOX disturbs cortical neuron production, a drug that transiently changes the exit of progenitors from the cell cycle could alter the apparent proportion of neurons independently of suppressing seizures. A higher neuronal fraction could reflect accelerated progenitor exhaustion or reduced survival of other cells. Human progenitor and neuronal yield over time would distinguish these possibilities. The proposed entry point is inhibition of α7 nicotinic acetylcholine receptors by memantine. The original drug and human cortical developmental studies did not test this combination in the disease. [Human α7 pharmacology](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14645141/) · [Human cortical perturbation and source data](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-61167-5)

## The receptor preparation changes the exposure prediction

In human α7 receptors expressed in Xenopus oocytes, memantine had a reported IC50 of 5 µM (reported bounds 2–13 µM). In native rat hippocampal neurons at −60 mV, the α7-mediated response had an IC50 of 0.34 µM, compared with 5.1 µM for the NMDA response under that experiment's conditions. The rat α7 inhibition slope was below one. Species, receptor composition, agonist, recording conditions and cell preparation differ simultaneously. The studies do not identify which difference causes the potency gap. A published adult CSF mean of about 0.36 µM is an exposure anchor. Free concentration in developing cortex and WWOX-specific pharmacokinetics were not measured. [Maskell et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14645141/) · [Aracava et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15522999/) · [Adult CSF measurements](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2019.00943)

At 0.36 µM, a Hill curve with unit slope and complete maximal inhibition gives **6.7% block** at the human central IC50 (2.7–15.3% over the reported potency bounds). At the central potency, slopes of 0.5 and 1.5 give 21.2% and 1.9% respectively. Meeting the prespecified 20% interest threshold at this concentration requires a slope no greater than 0.527 at the central IC50. We did not verify a numerical human inhibition slope, so the 20% boundary remains conditional. For the native rat α7 IC50, any positive slope below one implies 50–51.4% at 0.36 µM in the same model. These are normalized current predictions, not receptor occupancy or fetal exposure estimates. The human receptor experiment did not incorporate the human-specific CHRFAM7A duplicate. ## Testing the developmental premise in the authors' workbook

Reanalysis of the selective agonist PNU-282987 (10 µM) in healthy human cortical organoids at weeks 7–8 reproduced the published group means. The SOX2-positive fraction increased from 29.44% to 34.80% (+5.36 percentage points). The NEUN-positive fraction fell from 52.09% to 39.97% (−12.12 points). The study used three lines. The worksheet supplies 16–20 numeric rows per marker/condition without an individual line identifier for each row. These rows cannot establish across-line uncertainty. The source statistics tab labels the PNU/NEUN comparison paired and one-sided, while the figure legend labels the tests two-sided; we report the descriptive effect. [Figure 4, primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12217762/) · [Agonist recalculation](results/agonist.tsv)

In human cortical slices, CHRNA7 knockdown relative to a scrambled construct lowered the SOX2-positive fraction among marked cells by 14.80 points at gestational week 15 and 4.90 at week 21. The NEUN-positive fraction increased by 3.67 and 22.07 points, respectively. Knockdown of CHRFAM7A gave the same directions in both age-labeled blocks. Only three numerically documented NEUN sections per condition and age contribute. The paper's Figure 5 legend reports three biological samples and larger pooled neuronal percentages (22.5% control, 52% CHRNA7 knockdown). The two explicitly labeled worksheet blocks give 9.63% and 22.50% under equal-week weighting. We cannot reconcile those levels from the provided blocks. The direction agrees. The published magnitude and donor-level variance cannot be reconstructed from them. The ages label source tissue; a week is not a biological replicate. [By-week source-data recalculation](results/knockdown.tsv) · [Alternative weighting](results/knockdown_summary.tsv)

An exploratory calculation using Figure 7 gives a candidate downstream discriminator. Acetylcholine increased the HOPX-positive outer radial-glia fraction by 9.94 points relative to untreated tissue. In the presence of the YAP1-pathway inhibitor TAT-PDHPS1, the agonist difference relative to inhibitor alone was −3.55 points. The difference between these contrasts was −13.49 points. For nicotine the corresponding difference was −5.11 points. The grouped worksheet has 13–26 observations per arm and lacks a donor key for individual rows. These contrasts concern broad agonists and a separate inhibitor; they do not establish YAP dependence of an α7-selective drug. A primary interaction study found competition between WWOX and YAP for ErbB4 in a different context. We therefore nominate nuclear YAP localization as a candidate readout in the *same* radial glia, without inferring the direction of YAP change in WWOX from that interaction. [Human fate study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-61167-5) · [WWOX and YAP competition](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16061658/) · [Exploratory interactions](results/YAP_interaction.tsv)

## Is a distinct α7 receptor substrate visible in the WWOX organoids?

The human organoids were sampled at week 15, written “P15-week” in the locked note. The deposited Salmon estimates give CHRNA7 expression of 8.63 and 10.22 CPM in the two controls and 9.34–10.52 in four knockout libraries. The disease-minus-control difference was +0.085 log2(CPM+1) units and crossed zero on omission of one library. Subtracting the fixed radial-glia marker panel gave −0.282, while subtracting the neuronal panel gave +0.401. Both panels retained all four markers. These are relative bulk measures; their opposite signs make a cell-intrinsic change difficult to interpret. CHRFAM7A failed the prespecified coverage floor in one control. Combining both genes, which removes sensitivity to redistribution *between these two estimated gene counts*, gave +0.015 units, with omission spanning −0.035 to +0.066. [Deposited human WWOX data](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243) · [Individual libraries](results/human_counts.tsv) · [Sensitivity results](results/human_contrasts.tsv)

We compared exact windows in each gene's annotated transcripts against all transcripts of the other gene, in both orientations, using the same Ensembl release 99 cDNA reference as the original study. For illustration, 83.9% of 75-base positions in CHRNA7 ENST00000306901.9 and 80.9% in CHRFAM7A ENST00000299847.6 had an exact match in the other gene. Shorter and alternative isoforms differ markedly. These percentages are reference properties, not the fraction of misassigned reads. They motivate checking gene-specific exons in the actual cells before attributing an effect to either receptor component. The public recount3 human SRA index, consulted for an independent reprocessing, contained neither SRP277498 nor the corresponding runs among 329,290 indexed rows. ## What would change the decision?

At matched developmental age, compare the memantine-sensitive α7 current and the separately isolated NMDA current in identified WWOX-deficient and corrected human radial glia, with the local free concentration measured. Test whether a selective α7 antagonist reproduces and occludes the memantine effect on lineage output, alongside NMDA-selective and firing-matched controls. Follow cell-cycle exit, absolute surviving neuron yield and the remaining progenitor pool over multiple time points; a one-time increase in NEUN percentage is insufficient. Convergence of the current, genotype interaction and lineage outcomes at a relevant exposure would raise the priority of this route. Preserved lineage output under selective α7 block would weaken it even if appreciable current block were demonstrated.
