# Memantine, neuronal yield and α7 nicotinic signaling

23 November 2026, SCI_CODEX. An alternative to an NMDA-stress explanation is that memantine reduces nicotinic α7 signaling on developing human radial glia, changes cell-cycle exit, and changes cortical neuron yield in WWOX loss. More early neurons could also result from premature depletion of progenitors. Opposite long-term outcomes are plausible.

Before opening the numerical genotype counts or donor spreadsheet, prespecify three independent questions. (1) Quantify the current block predicted at 0.01, 0.36, 0.7, 1, 5 and 10 µM by the measured pharmacology of human α7 and the primary native rat α7 result. Prefer the actual fitted slopes and indicate any imposed slope separately. Use the reported human IC50 bounds for a conditional sensitivity at the observed adult mean CSF reference. A predicted block near or above 20% at this concentration would raise exposure plausibility; this is a prioritization threshold, not an efficacy threshold. Rat-to-human extrapolation remains inappropriate where the preparations differ. (2) In the primary human radial-glia study, summarize the human-specific α7 or duplicate-gene loss-of-function perturbations and selective agonist results in progenitor and neuronal endpoints. Define positive mechanistic evidence as reciprocal fate readouts on selective loss and gain, reporting the true biological and technical units, ages, available denominators and missing information. Distinguish proportions from absolute surviving neuron yield. Do not equate receptor knockdown with a channel blocker or assume that a mutant duplicate is present in any disease donor. (3) Inspect CHRNA7 and its partial human duplicate CHRFAM7A separately in the P15-week WWOX knockout cerebral organoids (four mutant, two control libraries). Show per-library counts, CPM, group contrast and all library omissions. Require expression at ≥1 CPM in each control for tissue-level interpretability. Compare CHRNA7 relative to a fixed radial glia panel (SOX2, PAX6, VIM, HOPX) and a neuronal panel (RBFOX3, SNAP25, MAP2, TUBB3), with at least two detectable genes in each. High homology limits the meaning of short-read counts for the duplicated regions. A disease signal dependent on cell mixture or paralog ambiguity cannot prioritize a dosage mechanism. A disease-specific effect ultimately requires measuring memantine-sensitive α7 currents at relevant free concentrations and following progenitor divisions and surviving neurons with NMDA-mediated firing matched.
