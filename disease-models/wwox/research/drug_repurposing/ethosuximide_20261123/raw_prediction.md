# Secondary reconstruction before opening BED target counts

The deposited H9 gene table and its mapped consensus clusters fail the primary panel coverage gate. This is a new sensitivity analysis, planned after seeing the organoid effects. The four public, strand-specific BED files may contain additional reads. Keep the primary results and missing rows visible.

Compare hg19 and hg38 RefSeq Curated annotation using 120 off-panel expressed genes sampled evenly in alphabetical order from entries with at least 3 TPM in every deposited library. Count 5′ ends in unique, same-strand gene regions within 100 and 500 bp of any annotated start. Exclude positions shared by different genes. Select an assembly only if the median across libraries of its rank correlation with the deposited off-panel expression at 100 bp is >0.6 and exceeds the other assembly by >0.20. No selection using panel results. Require at least 10 reads in each arm for a gene. Use the actual number of BED records in each library as the primary denominator, and test the independent deposited TPM-inferred library totals as a normalization sensitivity. Reapply the same panel coverage, direction and comparator criteria. Require consistent direction across both widths and both denominators to call support. These are the same four H9 libraries, so the two reconstructions share their lack of replication.
