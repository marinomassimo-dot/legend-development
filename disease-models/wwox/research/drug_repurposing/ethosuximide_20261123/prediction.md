# Ethosuximide — prediction before opening target gene values

**Scope.** Test the developmental premise that untreated WWOX loss shows a deficit in the same four transcripts increased by ethosuximide in differentiated mouse N2A cells (Chen et al., PMID 26419537). This would give a direction for an experiment on cell-cycle exit, distinct from acute seizure suppression. An inverse signature alone cannot establish rescue, FOXO activation, channel involvement or target engagement.

Seen beforehand: general WWOX organoid Wnt and maturation findings in LEGEND, the drug paper's results, sample names, deposit metadata and matrix formats. The proposed gene contrasts have not been opened. Do not select extra targets using WWOX results.

**Primary panel.** CCNG2, CDKN1B, RBL2 and GADD45A. Comparator = the remaining five assayed genes CDKN1A, BCL2L11 (BIM), CAT, SOD2, EIF4EBP1. Separately report cycling (MKI67, PCNA, MCM2, TOP2A) and neuronal differentiation (DCX, MAP2, TUBB3, RBFOX3). Increased expression of this panel would not necessarily benefit development. A reduction in cycling might protect differentiating cells or deplete a precursor pool.

**Analysis.** Score each sample by mean log2(normalized expression + 1); gene counts to CPM for human GSE156243, reported TPM for CAGE GSE126075 and mouse bulk TPM for GSE158682 and GSE158681. Primary biological contrast = WWOX KO minus WT human 15-week organoid; independent human context = WWOX knockdown minus control at progenitor and differentiated H9 stages separately (one library per condition). Record the difference of those two contrasts. Mouse cortex and hippocampus of neuronal Wwox deletion are a context check. Never pool these studies. Show the sign of each gene, mean panel, panel minus comparator, sensitivity to dropping one library where there are at least two per arm, and to dropping one gene. No cell-level or clinical inference. Where a gene is absent declare it, require at least 3/4 primary and 4/5 comparator genes; explicitly flag sparse expression. Compare organoid results to published heterogeneity without inventing clone labels.

**Decision.** Prioritize the inverse-signature developmental premise only if the primary panel is lower in organoids and in differentiated H9 cells, at least three of four genes decrease in each, and the difference against the five-gene comparator is below zero in both. If the panel difference vanishes or switches sign across cell states, report context dependence or failure. No universal p-value or aggregate benefit rank. Compare exposures by actual units, species and preparation. A low-concentration differentiation phenotype cannot be treated as evidence of high-concentration FOXO regulation or vice versa.
