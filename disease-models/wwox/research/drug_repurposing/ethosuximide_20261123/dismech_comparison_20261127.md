# Ethosuximide in WWOX-LoF | Clinical transfer and burst mechanism

**Decision.** Keep the direct Wwox acute evidence and prioritize one functional comparison of spontaneous cortical initiation and late maintenance. The Angelman and D/EE-SWAS observations widen the list of useful outcomes. Their different populations and denominators leave WOREE efficacy and a predictive PAC threshold unestablished. The existing developmental work remains a separate hypothesis.

## Inference and counterexample

**Two electrophysiological signals need their own tests.** Bretón Fig. 1E/§2.1 measured delta–gamma PAC (0.5–3 and 30–90 Hz) within spontaneous Wwox S-KO cortical field bursts. The paper's shuffled phase and power-dependence controls address specific nulls. Within-burst coupling in juvenile cortex and pretreatment scalp N2 coupling in DEE-SWAS represent different recording domains. In Bretón Fig. 3, d-APV abolished the observed bursts reversibly. Carbenoxolone lowered frequency and duration, with duration still lower at the washout comparison. Neither perturbation applies ethosuximide. Fig. 6/Table 1 rebound means of 1.86 versus 0.96 mV at −200 pA leave about 30.44 mV from spike threshold in the KO reconstruction. A fixed current pulse does not match membrane-voltage history, and group means cannot measure native CaV3 or HCN contributions. [Bretón et al., §§2.1–2.2 and §6.2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8609180/) · [Rebound replay](../ethosuximide_rebound_20261123/README.md) · [Washout bracket audit](../continuous_compounds/mefloquine/README.md)

**Reproducible PAC counterexample.** The [frozen comparison](dismech_prediction_20261127.md) uses known synthetic phase (0.8 Hz), matched white noise, 20 bins, analytic 40–80 Hz amplitude and a normalized KL modulation index. With a fixed sharp transient, raw median MI was 0.002256 versus 0.000132 for the smooth reference. Subtracting the phase-aligned mean gamma waveform reduced the former to 0.000133. With 5 ms timing jitter and 30% amplitude variation, the spike-only residual was 0.001819, and with 15 ms jitter it was 0.002197. The independent phase-gated 60 Hz positive control stayed near 0.024. The ranges in the replay summarize 32 noise seeds and have no patient meaning. The residual can therefore preserve artifact, even where it preserves a phase-gated oscillator. An exactly phase-locked real oscillator could also be removed by template subtraction. This counterexample checks identifiability, not whether either study's PAC arose from waveform shape. [Replay](results/pac_waveform_transfer_20261127.tsv) · [Waveform counterexample in primary literature](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jneumeth.2008.01.020)

**The thalamic results bound the mechanism.** Broicher (PMID 17675191) observed strain-dependent T-current effects and modeled combined T-type, persistent sodium and Ca²⁺-activated potassium contributions. Leresche (PMID 9634550, PMC6792570) is a different primary: it found no detectable T-current suppression under tested native conditions, with opposite shifts of burst and tonic firing in some cells. Use separate results and verify the current in the intended preparation. As a separate genetic-network comparator, Katsanevaki Fig. 4H–J shows a reduction in spontaneous spike-wave event counts in Syngap mutant rats within two hours. The number-of-events finding provides an initiation-sensitive readout for an absence-like model. It does not identify a common molecular target or the Wwox phenotype. [Broicher et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17675191/) · [Leresche et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6792570/) · [Katsanevaki et al., Fig. 4](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2024.114733)

**Developmental rival.** One treated mouse library carried the selected WNT marker signal, while a broader signed WNT3A response ranked 126/218. The eight-hour mouse cortical culture check at 700 µM did not reproduce the cell-cycle panel induction. Neither supplies an absolute lineage yield or a developmental rescue in WWOX. A lasting effect on myelin requires independent assessment of sheath coverage, thickness and conduction at matched neuronal activity. Curated graph coverage reflects assumed edges, forced convergence and scoring weights. It cannot justify a drug combination or turn a missing myelin measurement into a zero effect. ## Hypothesis and one discriminating comparison

**Hypothesis.** A low-threshold current supports a subset of Wwox spontaneous burst initiations in the appropriate membrane state. A rival is that ethosuximide principally shifts sustained firing or maintenance after glutamatergic initiation.

**Experiment.** In the same P13–17 Wwox cortical preparation use randomized, time-matched vehicle, ethosuximide and a qualified CaV3 reference, retaining genotype and animal as the replication level. Prefer the existing apinocaltamide candidate if it demonstrably engages the native low-threshold current and preserves relevant comparator currents in this preparation. Match voltage before release, verify local parent exposure, and record the CaV3 current, rebound latency and probability, first evoked response, sustained firing, and spontaneous burst frequency, duration and spatial spread. Assess whether control groups or individual readouts begin near floor before assigning a genotype interaction. Existing AMPA/NMDA measurements provide the chemical-input comparator. A PAC difference is interpretable only within matched physiological state, waveform and event count. [Existing electrical analysis](../ethosuximide_rebound_20261123/README.md) · [AMPA/NMDA comparators](../ampa_nmda_discrim_20261108/output/README.md)

| Result | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Both engaged perturbations reduce rebound-linked initiation while first and sustained firing are preserved | Prioritize testing spontaneous electrographic initiation in vivo. |
| Only ethosuximide shortens or limits the spread of bursts after initiation, despite matched CaV3 engagement | Prioritize late maintenance and alternative inward/outward current or synaptic effects. |
| Adequate CaV3 engagement with unchanged initiation, maintenance and spread | Lower the proposed CaV3 contribution in this preparation. |
| No target engagement, general suppression or a PAC-only change | Keep attribution unresolved. |

**EEG Review Project.** Keep PAC as a separate public-evidence research question. First test whether a fixed band/region measure adds held-out, participant-level information over N2 spike-wave index, spike shape and etiology in the original syndrome, with a prespecified multiplicity plan. Preserve stage and reference montage, use waveform-matched nulls, and test with and without sharp transients. A verified result could justify a WWOX validation question, where the primary endpoint still needs a WWOX-specific relation to outcomes. No clinical eligibility inference follows from the current public results.

**Pediatric feasibility.** Ethosuximide is available as an oral solution for absence epilepsy. The US label reports unestablished safety and effectiveness below age three, possible interaction with valproate and phenytoin, and a generalized tonic–clonic seizure warning for monotherapy in mixed epilepsy. That context limits transfer to early infantile WOREE even with older-child trial experience. The mouse exposure estimates remain scenarios, and neither the human comparators nor the provoked mouse experiment measures pharmacologically selective free cortical exposure in WOREE.
