# Blind priors per case (written BEFORE the forward snapshot and any IT21/IT22 stage table was opened)

Non-canonical research material. Public edition, WWOX-DEE reference genotype class. Not medical advice. Written after `reasons_blind.tsv`. Verbs: PROPOSE (list as a hypothesis row), QUALIFY (carry as qualified), HOLD (carry as conditional with a stated reason), ABSTAIN (do not list).

1. **Vorinostat: HOLD, never QUALIFY.** The metabolic HDAC5 route fails on typed attribution (HDAC5 Ki 3,600 nM against HDAC1 1.3 nM) and rests on one muscle reference, so as written it should be stopped for attribution; the only surviving reason (HDAC6/HSP90 stability) is a different object (a stability arm with a selective HDAC6 comparator) and is deferred by card A01 until the substrate exists. A careful generative path may list it, but the stated reason must be the stability arm, conditional on a substrate that is not shown, and the limit text must say that pan-HDAC action also brings class I effects.

2. **Tapinarof: HOLD at most; ABSTAIN is equally defensible.** Favourable: AhR agonist reported to co-activate NRF2 in skin cells, and NFE2L2 transcript is lower in two human neural systems. Counter: topical-only, no CNS exposure, the AHR to NRF2 link shown only in keratinocytes (abstract level), the NRF2 signature mixed and composition-confounded, and a more direct NRF2 route (omaveloxolone, KEAP1, genetic arm) already on record. If the engine reaches tapinarof it should be as an indirect, topical, CNS-unknown arm that is dominated by the direct NRF2 route; promotion is unjustified.

3. **Omaveloxolone: PROPOSE as a known thread and HOLD as conditional (it was KEEP-AS-PROPOSAL in IT10 and is carried already).** The new Q8 seed (NFE2L2 transcript lower) is a reason to say "low NRF2 tone is a possible premise", but it does not add a compound-level decision, and the compound acts post-translationally on KEAP1. Exclusion from the SCORED ranks of a signed-network path is justified (a compound that acts on the seed gene's own activity is a direct hit, not a regulatory chain); silent absence of the link from the output would be an omission, because the card, the IT10 verdict and the new seed all point at the same lever. The right output is a conditional row or a labelled direct-hit note tying omaveloxolone to Q8 with the unchanged development limits (age, perinatal exposure), not a rank.

4. **EZH2 inhibitors: HOLD as a cell-type-resolved in vitro question; ABSTAIN from any in vivo or sign-bearing claim.** The sign is contested by the edge's own reference, the net myelin-axis benefit is probably negative (PRC2 needed for oligodendrocyte differentiation; dual EZH1/2 compounds worst), exposure is efflux-limited and no EZH2/H3K27me3 measurement exists in Wwox glia. GSK343 stays conditional; the dual EZH1/2 compounds deserve a hazard flag rather than a proposal; compounds in the class are not interchangeable and no ranking within the class is supportable from typed values.

5. **BTK inhibitors: the Q4 NPY arm should ABSTAIN (premise refuted, scoped to the BTK-in-NPY-cell compartment); the exclusion should NOT leak to BTK inhibitors for the microglia/Q5 question, or to non-BTK STAT3 or NPY routes, and should not be read as a statement about CGI-1746's off-targets for other molecules.** The NPY seed itself is sign-contested (mouse higher, organoid lower with neuron loss), which independently weakens every NPY-first arm, so the caveat on S116 is a second, separate reason, not a duplicate of the BTK expression finding.

6. **Pracinostat / quisinostat / CUDC-101: HOLD as conditional, not PROPOSE as a cluster win.** Release from the nanatinostat veto is justified on the molecule's own profile: HDAC5 is within 1.0x (pracinostat), 4.7x (CUDC-101, with a potent EGFR confound) and 33.5x (quisinostat, but HDAC5 itself 3.69 nM) of the best typed target, against 66.7x for nanatinostat and 2,769x for vorinostat. But release only removes an attribution veto; it does not add evidence for the route, which stays one muscle reference, one origin, role unknown and a pan-HDAC action that cannot isolate HDAC5. CUDC-101 deserves a separate multi-target flag.
