## The two hits for IT

**1. Four distances, hypomyelination:** `doi:10.5061/dryad.gk117` (Grier et al. 2017, `doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0188417`;
CC0).
- Mouse corpus callosum slices, oligodendrocyte-lineage Rictor knockout (Olig2-Cre), P32-42, 25 C, 3 control and 3
  knockout slices.
- `Conduction Velocity.xlsx`: N1 time, N1 amplitude and velocity at four distances (0.5 to 1.25) per slice, so intercept
  and slope can be fitted per slice.
- Not there: stimulus intensity (the paper cites Crawford 2009, `doi:10.1016/j.jneumeth.2009.05.013`, for the method),
  amplitude and distance units, raw traces, N2.
- The paper's own result: velocity not changed (F = 0.305, p = 0.5866), in a transient hypomyelination.
- Access: Dryad's file download answered HTTP 401 (recorded, not retried). Dryad's public Zenodo copy
  (`zenodo.org/records/4999038`) served the file; its MD5 equals the digest Dryad publishes.

**2. Maximal responses, dysmyelination:** `doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.4040070` (Li, Velumian, Samoilova and Fehlings 2016,
`doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0165637`; CC BY 4.0).
- Isolated whole corpus callosum, suction electrodes; shiverer and wild type; anterior (cc3) and posterior (cc6) slices.
- Per slice: maximal amplitude, area, peak latency and velocity (wild type 14 and 29 slices, shiverer 8 and 11).
- One distance only: every row gives latency times velocity = 4.00 mm. The article's figures also use 2 mm; those rows
  are not in the deposit. Age not read. Peaks 1 and 2 are fibre classes, not proximal and distal components.
- HE counted rows and checked the distance; HE did not compare the groups.

The downloaded files were deleted after reading (all under 1 MB).
