## The INV-05 plateau rule (consumed from the narrow comparison)

A modulator that reaches a plateau **below** the abolition threshold x does not refute the mechanism: its removable share is bounded by composition (at most 0.88 times the GluN2B diheteromer share plus 0.32 times the triheteromer share, 0.50 with Zn2+; INFERENZA). The comparison with d-APV is made only **up to the modulator's measured removable share**, with the percent removed read at the burst. A plateau under x is "unresolved", not support for total gain and not a refutation of GluN2B participation.

## Minimum interpretable experiment, and what depends on it

1. **Minimum:** measured bath magnesium and pH; the isolated NMDA-receptor current and the d-APV abolition threshold x; the removable fraction of **one** ifenprodil-site modulator; burst frequency read against percent removed. This alone decides whether a GluN2B-directed comparison is the better question.
2. **Only if (1) shows a removable fraction:** a second modulator of the same site as chemotype control; the Zn2+-chelator arm; the release-reducing control matched to the same percent.
3. **Only if (2) separates composition:** glycine/D-serine level before any GluN2A-directed arm; a different-site comparator.
Not prerequisites for the module: the AMPA arm (separate question, tool qualification pending), the gap-junction attribution, the GABA-A arm.

No ranking, no recommendation, no dose, no efficacy. A compound that appears here is a candidate for a *comparison*, not for treatment. Novelty against the literature is not asserted.
