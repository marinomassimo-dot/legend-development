# IT41 P10 — two short research questions prepared for possible collaboration

Non-canonical working note. Not medical advice. Nothing here implies an agreed collaboration; no message is sent, no correspondence is cited, no experiment is commissioned. Public sources and existing repository records only (the open-question map row WO-09 notes that fly stocks are "not known"; the ISR mechanism row of `analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md` rests on a single Drosophila amyloid model with an Atf4 signal, n 6). The questions are written so that anyone may take them up, or not.

## Q-FLY — is a fly assay centred on loss of Wwox feasible, with a measurable phenotype and a rescue control?

* **Decision it could change.** Whether a fast in vivo tool arm exists for the physiological hypotheses and the INV-05 / INV-18 comparisons (a drug-arm discrimination that does not need slices), and whether a loss-of-function phenotype is robust enough to read a modulator against.
* **What would have to be true.** A characterised Wwox loss allele; a phenotype scored blind (candidates to check in the literature, not asserted here: stress- or stimulus-induced seizure-like behaviour, climbing, lifespan, a neural stress readout); a rescue control (fly gene, and human WWOX as a conservation test); vehicle and genotype controls; the fly receptor and channel orthologs of the targets in play stated before any compound is read (NMDA-receptor subunits and Ih / Kv7 channels are not one-to-one with the mouse).
* **Possible in silico first.** Ortholog and domain mapping of the targets of Cases 1–4; a literature census of existing fly Wwox phenotypes and stocks (public repositories only).
* **New measurement needed.** The phenotype itself, its penetrance, and the rescue.
* **Reopen condition.** A published or deposited fly Wwox loss phenotype with a rescue; or a stock list from a public repository.
* **Priority (reasoned).** Below the slice minimum experiment of `comparisons_it41.md` (it does not replace it; a fly phenotype is not a mammalian cortical burst); above the in vivo extensions, because it can be low cost if stocks exist.

## Q-VAR — what do a few public cases show once variant classification, molecular consequence and pharmacological premise are kept apart?

* **Decision it could change.** Whether a pharmacological premise (for example NMDA-receptor gain, or the absence of it) is borne by the case or only by the gene; and how the reference genotype class is used in the comparisons.
* **What would have to be true.** Each case taken from a public report; classification of the variant (as the report gives it, with the classifier named), the molecular consequence (as measured, or "not measured"), and the premise that a drug action would rest on, written as three separate columns; no individual-level linkage to any person.
* **Possible in silico first.** Selecting three to five public cases from existing records and filling the three columns from the text; marking each cell as measured, reported or inferred.
* **New measurement needed.** For any case with "consequence not measured", the measurement that would decide it.
* **Reopen condition.** A new public case series, or a re-classification in a public variant database.
* **Priority (reasoned).** Useful as a guard against borrowing an NMDA-receptor-gain premise from GRIN-variant series (the earlier memantine split by variant class); cheap; can run in parallel with the slice design.
