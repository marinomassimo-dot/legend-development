# Does WWOX loss create excess new recurrent synapses that gabapentin could correct?

**Disposition.** Park gabapentin for a broadly enrolled pediatric WWOX developmental trial. Retain the early, low-concentration TSP4 mechanism as a research hypothesis. The remaining uncertainty is whether excess *new functional connections* occur preferentially between principal neurons in an affected circuit. The available WWOX electrophysiology cannot establish that substrate. An independent review of recipient specificity was routed by ADV. This disposition can be revisited on a substantive reply.

**Matched comparison.** In neuron-specific Wwox knockout mice aged P13–17, Fig. 4B–C assessed miniature excitatory currents in neocortical layer II/III putative pyramidal neurons. Each group contributed ten cells from four WT, five heterozygous and five KO animals. The KO amplitude distribution shifted. For miniature frequency, the reported Kolmogorov–Smirnov comparison separated WT and KO (P≈0.00024) and WT and heterozygotes (P=.0018), while heterozygotes and KO were similar by this test (P=.5963). There is no per-animal effect size or numerical mean frequency in that panel. In the separate spontaneous-current comparison, the pooled control and KO means were 9.9 and 10.1 Hz. The laboratory's prior audit records a denominator ambiguity in the pooled control legend. These findings do not establish an excess of newly formed connections or identify an interneuron recipient. **Across models.** Gabapentin reduced miniature frequency in layer V pyramidal neurons after rat cortical injury. In the sensory–spinal preparation, early gabapentin prevented the TSP4-induced increment in structural contacts. These observations involve different brain regions, developmental states, targets of recording and endpoints. An apparent reduction in discharge frequency after treatment could result from lower release probability, reduced recruitment of both recipient classes, or a genuine reduction in abnormal new contacts. No WWOX response to the drug was measured in these comparisons. [Measured concentrations and primary comparisons](exposure.md)

**Protein and reopening criterion.** A November 24 PRIDE project-metadata check using WWOX, WOREE, DEE28, SCAR12 and the full oxidoreductase name retrieved no matching WWOX neural perturbation. The SCAR12 hit PXD029631 concerns skin keloids. A hippocampus lookup returned a correctly labeled project. This bounded search leaves local extracellular TSP4 levels unknown. Reopen for stage-matched evidence of excessive TSP4-sensitive new recurrent contacts in WWOX, with functional input onto identified inhibitory neurons preserved when the abnormal contacts are reduced.
