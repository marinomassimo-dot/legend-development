# Dipyridamole opening comparison

| System | Opening observation | Boundary for WWOX |
|---|---|---|
| Cloned human ENT1 and ENT2 in transporter-deficient cells | The reported dipyridamole IC50 values were 5.0 and 356 nM. Their nominal ratio is 356 / 5.0 = 71.2. | Recombinant potency in a transport assay cannot assign a CNS concentration window or the direction of net flux. [Ward et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10722669/) |
| Rat caudate-putamen in vivo | The original abstract reports no detectable change in transient duration under dipyridamole. | An observed duration and a fitted decay parameter answer different questions. The preparation and systemic exposure do not establish a WWOX response. [Nguyen et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27022463/) |
| Stimulated mouse hippocampal slice | A combined ENT inhibitor comparison including dipyridamole reduced the stimulated adenosine signal. | The combined comparison cannot attribute the effect to dipyridamole alone. Lower release can coexist with slower clearance. [Held Wall and Dale comparison](../cannabidiol/README.md) · [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23713028/) |
| WWOX reference analysis and pediatric feasibility | Lower WWOX A1 receptor RNA was reproduced across the five bulk contrasts. US oral labeling states that pediatric safety and effectiveness below age 12 have not been established. | Measure receptor responsiveness and distinguish vascular antiplatelet exposure from local neural ENT engagement. [WWOX purine dataset replay](../probenecid/purine_feedback_and_disposition_20261127.md) · [US oral tablet label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=f7ffd78a-e93a-40f4-8193-2d6ab4dafd00) |
