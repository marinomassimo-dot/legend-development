# Dipyridamole and the direction of nucleoside transport in WWOX loss of function

**Status.** Opening checkpoint for dipyridamole. LEGEND previously listed the compound in an adenosine augmentation screen. WWOX `ADORA1` was lower in the held bulk contrasts, while PANX1 direction depended on tissue. The CBD comparison already shows why an ENT inhibitor cannot be assumed to raise activity-evoked adenosine. Dipyridamole provides a human recombinant ENT1/ENT2 potency contrast and an in vivo clearance readout. This increment asks whether the tissue regulates adenosine release and clearance through different transporter contributions, at free concentrations that might distinguish the two isoforms. [Held screening rationale](../../advisor_pilot_20261113/brainstorm_20261116/deepening_sci_20261116/DOSSIER_I3_COMPENSATION_ASYMMETRY_SCI.md) · [Held WWOX purine comparison](../probenecid/purine_feedback_and_disposition_20261127.md) · [Human transporter study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10722669/)

**Prediction.** At matched initial drive, compare clearance after a controlled extracellular adenosine pulse with the peak and decay after precisely timed physiological stimulation. With inward ENT flux dominating clearance, dipyridamole should slow a matched pulse's decay and preserve or prolong A1-dependent post-train inhibition without lowering physiological recruitment. With substantial outward ENT flux supporting the native transient, the evoked peak can fall while the independent clearance pulse decays more slowly. Direct ATP and adenosine measurements in both conditions separate ENT effects from lower upstream purine production.

**Counterchecks.** Compare measured local parent and isoform engagement with validated ENT1/ENT2 perturbations and an A1 responsiveness control. Track extracellular pH and metabolic stage. Differentiating an A1-limited response from reduced adenosine delivery requires a matched receptor challenge. Distinguish local transport engagement from vascular or phosphodiesterase effects in intact systems. Prioritize exposure and selectivity evidence before extending claims to a pediatric trial.

**Next step.** Compare recombinant human transport inhibition, measured animal brain and human exposure, and kinetic effects in slice and intact rodent preparations. Reuse the held WWOX matrices and the CBD work before adding new measurements. [Opening comparison](first_comparison_20261127.md)
