# Prior comparison

Inspect GCLC, GCLM, GSS, GSR, SLC7A11/SLC3A2, SLC1A2/SLC1A3 and SLC1A1 in the three existing bulk datasets. The strongest common transcriptional rationale for limited glutathione synthesis plus impaired clearance would be a stable decrease in both glutamate–cysteine ligase subunits and both glial glutamate transporters in mouse cortex and human organoids. Treat discordance as reason to separate models and readouts. Single-library omission measures fragility only. In the juvenile hippocampal nuclei compare marker-assigned classes with an astrocyte-enriched subset co-detecting Aqp4 and Aldh1l1; the latter does not require the tested Slc1a2 transporter to be detected. No nuclear measurement is an independent animal replicate and co-detection selects cells with higher read depth. Inspect absolute gene counts and positive-cell counts. Basal abundance cannot determine transport flux, oxidation state, extracellular glutamate or a drug effect.
