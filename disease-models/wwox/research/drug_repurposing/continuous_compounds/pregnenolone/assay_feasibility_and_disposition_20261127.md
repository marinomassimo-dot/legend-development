# Pregnenolone | Individual hippocampal sample feasibility and disposition
context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Decision.** Retain pregnenolone as a conditional C21 precursor probe. Defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. Public assays establish that specific isomers can be separated and measured in other matrices. A public validation that reaches the needed labeled-product range in individual P17 hippocampal tissue remains unverified. The result sets a quantitative condition for reopening the mechanism work.

## What one specimen could supply

**Datum.** Chuang et al. give a C57BL/6 ex vivo MRI hippocampal growth fit of a=22 mm³, b=3.7 days and x₀=24.7 gestational days. Applying their sigmoid at 18.5+17 days gives 20.87 mm³. A separate stereology study by Petrik et al. reports WT 13.3 ± 0.8 mm³ at P14 and 13.7 ± 1.6 mm³ at P21 for its predefined section boundaries. Tissue handling and segmentation differ between studies. The calculation uses the P14 histological reference and the P17 MRI fit as separate volume scenarios, with an assumed density of 1 mg/mm³. A 50:50 allocation gives 6.65 or 10.44 mg per specimen before dissection and slice losses. These are volume-based mass proxies with unknown applicability to Wwox LoF. Weighed tissue from the intended viable preparation is the relevant denominator. **Reproducible comparison.** Use the intact-rat baselines from Kudová et al. Table 1 (P12 0.254 ng/g, P25 0.119 ng/g) as references. At a hypothetical 25% labeled product fraction, the P12 reference is 0.0635 ng/g and a hypothetical 50% lower product is 0.03175 ng/g. In an ideal 6.65–10.44 mg split, the reference gives **21–33 fg** on column at 5% transfer, and the lower product gives **11–17 fg**. Counting the whole 20.87 mg MRI proxy for one chemical observation gives 66 fg and 33 fg, respectively. At the plasma-validated 50 fg benchmark, the lower product would require **31.5 mg** at 5% perfect transfer, or net delivery of **15–24%** of its original mass with a 6.65–10.44 mg specimen. At the P25 reference the same lower-product scenario gives **5–8 fg** at 5% transfer for those splits. The lower product matters because detection in the reference alone could leave the apparent genotype deficit entirely censored. The calculation assumes 100% upstream recovery and transfers only an instrument benchmark across matrices. The label fractions and ratios are planning scenarios, with no assumed WWOX effect size. ## Discriminating observation and stopping condition

**Inference.** Partitioning each animal among times, analytical fractions and electrophysiology can move the lower group below quantification before an experiment has any power to distinguish modes of action. An extracellular fraction can add information about labeled products lost from tissue if recovery in both matrices is established. Released product alone remains ambiguous between transfer and formation.

**Hypothesis and verification.** Qualify a chemistry-only comparison before allocating tissue to field or patch measurements. Keep animal as the replicate and retain time or condition within animal only if the measured eligible mass and independently validated recovery allow both arms above the matrix LLOQ. Anchor the threshold to the lowest labeled product the test is intended to resolve (0.03175 ng/g in the illustrative P12 scenario). Require separation from the three stereoisomers, traceable labeled-product standards and negligible contribution from natural isotopes, cross-channel spillover, carryover and background at that threshold. Verify labeled progesterone, 5α-DHP and intact sulfate independently. An equal parent and labeled-progesterone trajectory with lower combined tissue-plus-bath 5α products favors a downstream conversion constraint. Low endogenous allopregnanolone with retained labeled conversion and recovery of native inhibition favors insufficient precursor. A downstream product comparator checks responsiveness after metabolism. A delayed NMDA increase or broader suppression of physiological recruitment would lower functional priority. A below-limit result cannot decide between these mechanisms. Direct approval or scheduling for wet work is outside the scope of the in-silico disposition.

## Pediatric evidence and transfer

- **Ayatollahi et al.** In ASD, 64 participants were randomized to pregnenolone (32) or placebo (32), each with risperidone. The primary abstract reports 30 and 29 completed, improvement on the irritability score over the study interval (p=0.025), and no significant between-arm difference in reported adverse-event frequency or severity. Full age distribution, formulation and adverse-event counts could not be verified from the accessible primary abstract. This supplies human adjunct exposure experience for a behavioral endpoint. The accessible result leaves CNS concentrations, seizure outcomes and interactions with background antiseizure medicines unresolved. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32947424/)
- **Stanford randomized pilot.** ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02627508 lists 40 actual enrollees, eligibility ages 14–25, exclusion for unstable seizures (>2 in the preceding month), and completion on 17 March 2026. As checked via its public API on 27 November 2026, summary results had not been posted. Registered endpoints include caregiver-rated irritability and peripheral neurosteroids. McGrath et al. report a 30.2% within-person ABC-I decrease over a placebo lead-in in 25 participants, without an active-versus-placebo drug contrast. The paper prints NCT01881737, which the registry identifies as a separate adult open-label study. The described randomized design is consistent with NCT02627508, and the paper's identifier discrepancy leaves formal linkage unresolved. No pediatric seizure or neural exposure estimate is taken from either record. [Randomized registry record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02627508) · [Lead-in, Methods and Results](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10803-025-06951-4) · [Adult open-label registry record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01881737)

**Reopening.** A verified stereoselective tissue calibration with the actual per-animal P17 yield and the predeclared lower term, or a public age- and region-matched labelled flux dataset, would reopen the chemistry question. Independent review by an assay specialist could change the decision once usable tissue mass and matrix-level validation data are available. The next clinical reconsideration requires WWOX-specific neural engagement and a functional effect at measured exposure.
