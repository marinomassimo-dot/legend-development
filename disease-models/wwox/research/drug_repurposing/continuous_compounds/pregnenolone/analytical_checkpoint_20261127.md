# Pregnenolone | What the P17 hippocampal RNA and public assays can distinguish
context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Checkpoint, 27 November 2026.** The comparison uses TPM from P17 mouse bulk hippocampus (GSE158681) and cortex (GSE158682), normalized RNA from human cerebral organoids at week 15 (GSE156243), and human progenitor and differentiated-cell CAGE TPM (GSE126075). Independent chemical comparators are juvenile rat hippocampal wet-tissue steroid means in ng/g, adult mouse brain matrix calibration in pmol/g, and bath concentrations in recombinant NMDA current measurements. No WWOX neural steroid concentration or flux is established by these sources.

| Datum | Observation with denominator | Decision it informs |
|---|---|---|
| Mouse hippocampal 5α step | SRD5A1 mean KO minus control log₂(1+TPM) is −0.98 in neuronal deletion (2 controls/3 KO), −0.40 in neural-progenitor deletion (2/2, sign changes after omitting one library) and −0.17 in the constitutive null (3/2). CYP11A1 is lower in all three. Neuronal AKR1C14 is 0.54→0.07 TPM with low abundance. | Precursor supply and downstream conversion are both plausible bottlenecks. Compare labeled progesterone with labeled 5α-DHP and allopregnanolone at matched parent exposure. |
| Mouse hippocampal sulfate branch | Gene-level SULT2B1 contrasts in the three P17 arms are +0.43, +0.36 and −0.51. In neuronal deletion, the transcript carrying the increase (ENSMUST00000209435.1) is annotated as a processed transcript in Ensembl r92. The sum of protein-coding transcripts goes 9.22→6.32 TPM. The progenitor and null coding contrasts go 7.51→6.26 and 2.67→3.48 TPM, respectively. | Measure intact sulfate as a separate product. Gene-level direction does not justify predicting increased sulfation. Total coding TPM also leaves the specific catalytic isoform and flux unknown. |
| Region and human stage | P17 cortex (4/3) has an unstable SRD5A1 shift of +0.09. In week-15 human organoids (2/4), SRD5A1 goes +0.37 and SULT2B1 −0.29 on the same descriptive log scale applied to normalized RNA. CAGE compares one library per arm per stage and gives SRD5A1 +0.29 in progenitors and −1.32 after differentiation. | Use P17 hippocampus first. Keep organoids as a separate replication question. The data come from different tissues, developmental windows and preparations. |
| Chemical sensitivity | Rat P12/P25 male hippocampal allopregnanolone means of 0.254/0.119 ng/g (n=5 per intact group, Kudová et al. Table 1) correspond to 0.797/0.374 pmol/g using 318.50 g/mol. Sharp et al. Table 1 quantifies the separated 3α,5α product at a lower limit of 2.5 pmol/g with 100 mg adult mouse brain input. That threshold is 3.13 and 6.69 times the respective rat means. | Qualify recovery, epimer separation and lower quantification in juvenile hippocampus at the intended input mass before interpreting any low value. A result under the assay threshold is indeterminate. |
| Chemical identity | The P12/P25 study hydrolyzed the conjugated fraction before measuring it (§§4.4–4.6). An adult rat direct LC–ESI–MS/MS study measured intact sulfate at 4.45 ng/g cortex and 10.25 ng/g hippocampus (n=10 adult males, Rustichelli et al., §§2.2–2.5, 3.5, Tables 2–3). Tracer counts at 30 minutes in Taggi et al. cannot distinguish intact sulfate from label retained after desulfation. | Check both sample handling and chemical form. Every wet-tissue mean remains distinct from free local exposure. |
| Functional window | At 100 nM sulfate, recombinant NR1/2A oocytes show +1 ± 2% concurrent versus +45 ± 3% delayed potentiation after 10 minutes (n=8–10, Kostakis Fig. 4B). NR1/2B had no detectable rapid or delayed effect at that concentration (Fig. 3C). The neonatal rat CA1 mEPSC-frequency response in Mameli Fig. 1B was limited to P3–P5 at the tested exposure. | Record early and delayed NMDA-associated currents separately. Direct subunit pharmacology or immature CA1 results cannot fix the effect sign in juvenile WWOX tissue. |

The bulk comparisons are descriptive. They retain their own controls, preserve near-zero measurements and show single-library omission bounds. They do not resolve cellular mixture or transcript assignment uncertainty. Sulfotransferase isoforms have different substrate preferences in the primary mouse assays. **Discriminating comparison.** In P17 hippocampal Wwox deletion and matched control, compare vehicle versus labeled pregnenolone within animal, with measured free parent and separately quantified intact sulfate, progesterone, 5α-DHP and 3α,5α-allopregnanolone. Establish recovery and activity of the product branch before using the chemistry result to interpret native-chloride GABAergic recruitment or the separately measured early and delayed NMDA current. A low basal 5α product with WT-like fractional conversion of incoming substrate, product recovery and selective improvement in native inhibition supports precursor limitation. A normal labeled progesterone yield and low labeled 5α-DHP and allopregnanolone at matched parent exposure supports a downstream constraint. An added downstream product matched to measured availability would separate conversion from receptor response. A high sulfate route requires intact identity, adequate mass recovery and correction for desulfation and uptake. Similar conversion across genotypes weakens the rerouting hypothesis. A sulfate-linked increase in delayed NMDA drive or loss of physiological inhibition weakens the functional rescue claim. **Disposition and next step.** Pregnenolone remains the active compound. The first decision is whether small-matrix chemistry with matched adjacent preparations can quantify the 5α product below the juvenile comparison range without pooling across animals. Identify and verify a validated higher-sensitivity method before interpreting any WWOX difference. Independent review of that specific matrix and assay could change the feasible readout. There is no clinical exposure or efficacy estimate for pediatric WWOX in this comparison.
