# Tideglusib | GSK3 engagement, network activity and developmental transfer

`context_policy: SYNTHESIS` · SCI_CODEX · 27 November 2026 · `DISMECH-PORTFOLIO-GNX-TIDEGLUSIB-20261127`.

**Decision.** Keep tideglusib as a conditional, nonselective GSK3-pathway perturbation tool. Defer pediatric WWOX trial priority and any inference from its numerical portfolio rank. Public evidence supports time-dependent enzyme inhibition and an adult brain substrate effect under local delivery. It also gives no demonstrated acute seizure suppression in the two selected provoked models and opposing injury outcomes across paradigms. Free neural exposure, GSK3α/β attribution and the direction of GSK3 substrate activity in the relevant WWOX cell class need to be established. [Registered prediction](gsk3_engagement_pretest_20261127.md)

## WWOX starting point

**Data.** The held Wang et al. analysis reports WWOX–GSK3β interaction, Tau phosphosite-specific effects in cell systems and a mouse-brain co-immunoprecipitation. It supports substrate-specific regulation. Cheng et al. report differences in inhibitory Ser9 immunoblots across brain regions in Wwox-null mice. The held figure audit treats these bands as qualitative and records the P14/P20 sampling ambiguity. Figure 7d is a provoked behavioral seizure comparison. Lithium lowered scores in WT as well as mutant mice. It supplies neither a selective WWOX genotype-by-treatment effect nor matched kinase activity in the same animals. ADV-214 covered the public or held WWOX omics, finding no qualifying phosphoprotein or activity comparison. Its RNA and total-protein entries cannot resolve kinase activity. [Wang primary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22193544/) · [Cheng primary, Fig. 7c–d](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40478-020-0883-3) · [Held phosphosite audit](../../../commit_candidates/CC-20260826-GSK3B-S9-AXIS-01.md) · [Held lithium figure comparison](../../advisor_pilot_20261113/brainstorm_20261116/adv067_lithium_regimen_sci_20261118/LITHIUM_REGIMEN_DECISION_ADV067_SCI_20261119.md) · [ADV-214](../../advisor_pilot_20261113/brainstorm_20261116/adv214_gsk3b_tau_held_tables_sci_20261124/RESULT_ADV214_GSK3B_TAU_HELD_TABLES_SCI_20261124.md)

**Inference.** Tau phosphorylation, GSK3β inhibitory-site phosphorylation and Wnt transcription are distinct readouts. In hippocampal slices, the independent PI4KIIα study found that its knockdown decreased isolated NMDAR currents and left AMPAR responses similar in that comparison. A shRNA-resistant wild-type construct restored the NMDAR response, while the S9/51A rescue construct failed. The hypothesized WWOX transfer is therefore testable by holding first recruitment comparable, then measuring NMDA charge and proximal substrate activity. A secondary comparison matched for actual NMDA charge can test whether residual late recruitment requires an additional mechanism. The PI4KIIα branch and Wnt/β-catenin must be measured separately. A pSer9-only readout cannot establish that a compound corrects either pathway. [PI4KIIα primary](https://doi.org/10.1111/ejn.14841) · [INV-09 retest design](../../openscientist_retest_20261108/inv09_gsk3/README.md)

At **50% matched β inhibition** the unit-slope replay predicts **35.6% α inhibition** for tideglusib and **6.5%** for BRD3731 using their respective paired recombinant ratios. At 80% β inhibition, the values are **68.9% and 21.8%**. For a cellular selectivity ratio of 4–6, assumed as a sensitivity, the corresponding cross-inhibition estimates are **14–20%** and **40–50%**. These are fractional responses of assumed curves. The data justify measuring both isoforms at multiple times and checking overlap during washout. BRD3731 and the chemically matched α-preferring BRD0705 are informative tools if their actual engagement is calibrated in the same preparation. An isoform-selective genetic perturbation is an additional attribution control. The approximately 41 h GSK3β protein half-life estimated in primary cortical cultures is a different quantity from binding residence, especially in intact cells with synthesis and turnover. ## Counterexamples from brain and alternate actions

- **Adult mouse provoked status, Engel et al.** Figure 4a shows no detected decrease in acute EEG power over 40 minutes with NP12 or NP103, n=10 per group. At 24 h, NP12's two exposure groups had roughly **+58 and +36 Fluoro-Jade B-positive counts** above the vehicle mean, from graph replay. This is direct local delivery and no free brain exposure was measured. A separate n=4/group comparison in Supplementary Fig. 2 shows lower AT100 and higher Tau-1 signal. AT100's known Thr212/Ser214 epitope is transposed in the supplementary caption, and the blot labels in panel b warrant checking. The signal directions are usable as proximal markers with that limitation. They cannot establish that β engagement and injury tracked within individual animals. In Fig. 4c the WT and Fas-null point-estimate increments were approximately +74 and +45, on different baselines. The displayed within-genotype tests do not estimate a Fas interaction. The gene-expression workbook has two transcript lists and no raw EEG or Fig. 4 values. [Engel et al., Fig. 4 and Supplementary Fig. 2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41419-018-0963-5) · [AT100 epitope primary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9546672/) · [Pixel-calibrated replay](engel_fig4_replay.tsv)
- **Adult rat provoked comparisons, Luna-Medina et al.** In the separate local injury arm, normalization of CA3 labeling changed from 15% to 95% with NP031112 without PPARγ antagonism and from 20% to 25% with antagonism. The difference of drug increments is **−75 points** from aggregate means, n=5 animals per group with five sections each. This has no independent interaction uncertainty. The oral arm reported an unchanged **80%** incidence of behavioral status, a latency shift from 104 to 126 min, and less injury in selected surviving animals at 72 h. Species, delivery, timing and injury measurement differ from Engel. Pharmacological blocking supports PPARγ involvement in that preparation. It does not identify direct binding in brain or a mechanism in WWOX. [Luna-Medina et al., Figs. 5–6](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1004-07.2007) · [Antagonist replay](pparg_context_replay.tsv)
- **Acute versus sustained GSK3β modulation, Aourz et al.** Acute TCS2002 lowered the cumulative behavioral seizure score from 14.7 to 4.7 in rats (vehicle n=10, treated n=6). It also protected in acute electrical provocation, with an effect reported across three strains and a nonmonotonic protection pattern in NMRI. The genetic deletion arm instead showed higher repeated-provocation behavioral scores with neuronal loss of GSK3β (1.8 versus 1.1) and early stopping after deaths in the deletion group. These arms do not share perturbation duration, model or readout. The primary report does not match α/β activity or free central exposure. Prefer the paired BRD tools for attribution because they have reported head-to-head isoform assays. TCS2002 supplies a context contrast for the acute phenotype. [Aourz et al., Figs. 1, 2 and 4](https://doi.org/10.1021/acschemneuro.1c00782)
- **Spontaneous events after a provoked insult.** In a separate model, constitutively active GSK3β[S9A] was associated with fewer delayed spontaneous seizures than WT after status, seven animals per genotype. That is a genetic perturbation in the opposite direction. It supplies neither spontaneous WWOX efficacy nor a drug comparison. An age-dependent developmental/memory finding for tideglusib in juvenile Cdkl5-knockout animals remains a separate indication lead from the abstract. [GSK3β[S9A] primary, Fig. 2](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2018.11.040) · [Cdkl5 primary, abstract only](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29603837/)
- **Wnt and target alternatives.** Cultured human SPG11 neural progenitors showed increased β-catenin under tideglusib, with TOP reporter values 4.98 and 2.71 in the two treated groups. These are treated SPG11 versus treated controls, not a within-group fold change. A neuroblastoma assay reported no nuclear β-catenin accumulation with tideglusib under conditions where another nominal GSK3 inhibitor increased it. Neither experiment matched GSK3 isoform inhibition. The 2026 NatA report and deposit supply a biochemical alternative and yeast acetylome data. Species-specific potency and parent attribution need the actual table before using an abstract range. Neither human neural NatA engagement nor a WWOX effect follows. Use NatA and PPARγ readouts when a phenotype is tideglusib-specific at equal kinase engagement. [SPG11 source, Fig. 6](https://doi.org/10.1002/ana.24633) · [Neuroblastoma source, Fig. 5](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms22168975) · [NatA abstract and supplement index](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42717869/) · [Yeast proteomic deposit](https://proteomecentral.proteomexchange.org/cgi/GetDataset?ID=PXD070476)

## Systemic exposure and pediatric evidence

Di Deo et al., Table 3, report model-derived median peak **513.5 and 1,170.9 ng/mL** and trough **4.1 and 5.6 ng/mL** in two earlier DM1 study groups at visit 4. Ages actually sampled were **13.8–34.9 years**. The group totals were 51 samples from 16 people, including nine below quantification, all pre-dose. The model borrowed informative elderly volunteer estimates. The paper's predictions at younger weights do not supply measured pediatric free exposure. Oral investigational formulation and food context matter, and the study discusses prior reports of transaminase changes. In the public search I located mouse plasma LC–MS/MS but no measured free brain/plasma bridge in a WWOX or age-relevant system. [Di Deo et al., §§3.1–3.2 and Table 3](https://doi.org/10.3390/pharmaceutics17081065) · [Mouse plasma primary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28982059/)

At these two modeled peaks, the required instantaneous **unbound-brain-to-total-plasma product** to match the 105 nM β assay midpoint is **6.84% or 3.00%**. With the other paper's time-dependent 139 nM midpoint it is **9.05% or 3.97%**. To match the matched-pair 502 nM midpoint it is **32.69% or 14.34%**. A hypothetical 1% ratio would give **15 and 35 nM** at those peaks. At the trough this scaling gives **0.12 and 0.17 nM**. This arithmetic does not estimate a blood–brain partition coefficient, brain kinetics or occupancy and assumes time alignment that has not been measured. It shows why the choice of enzyme protocol and time can flip the interpretation of the same systemic PK. [Threshold replay](plasma_threshold_sensitivity.tsv)

REACH CDM tested ages **6–16** in a different genetic disorder. It reports **27/26 randomized-period starters**, **25/26 for the prespecified week-20 analysis**, and **28/25 in the safety analysis**. Both registries give the intent-to-treat model difference active minus placebo **+1.75 points**, 95% CI **−0.01 to +3.51**, p=.0514. Higher CDM1-RS means greater severity. It did not establish superiority. The USA safety module explains a reclassification after measured active exposure in one placebo-assigned participant. Recorded serious adverse events were **1/28 and 0/25**; any reported nonserious adverse event affected **20/28 and 19/25**. This supplies tolerability observations in a specified young population and an oral suspension precedent, with no WWOX efficacy, exposure or long-term neurological safety demonstrated. In the earlier 16-person study safety was the primary endpoint, with zero registry-reported serious adverse events in either eight-person group. Distinguish the populations and denominators. ## Decision-changing comparison

**Hypothesis.** A reproducible baseline change in GSK3β substrate activity accompanies excessive late WWOX network recruitment. It can be corrected across orthogonal β-biased interventions at matched time-resolved engagement while preserving early responses and avoiding excess injury or developmental Wnt signaling. Reduced AT100 by itself is a weak discriminator.

**One experiment.** In a genotype-defined WWOX and control neural preparation at matched maturation, measure basal isoform activity and substrate/total ratios. Compare tideglusib and β-preferring BRD3731 at matched measured β engagement across time, with measured free concentration profiles for each tool, using the matched α-preferring tool and WWOX rescue as attribution controls. First establish safe overlap in engagement, measure α at every time point and track recovery. Read Tau plus PI4KIIα/NMDA and Wnt/β-catenin separately. Record first recruitment, late spontaneous or standardized-drive events and injury in the same preparation, with elapsed-time controls and biological replication by preparation. Interpret a drug-only network change against measured GSK3α, a prespecified PPARγ/NatA follow-up and equivalent starting drive. Keep delayed differentiation a separate readout.

**Predicted decision.** Concordant proximal and circuit correction with preserved recruitment and no added injury would support the control value of a matched-engagement GSK3 tool. Opposing phenotypes at matched β with different α engagement would favor a paralog contribution. A tideglusib-only response at comparable β and α would favor a separate action or a kinetic/compartment mismatch. If the WWOX baseline activity difference is absent, injury rises, or only a downstream Tau marker moves, keep tideglusib as a limited research perturbation. Reopen trial priority only for replicated genotype-specific benefit with measured feasible free exposure and no adverse circuit or injury tradeoff. Review is decision-relevant when this same-preparation dataset exists or when an independently auditable central PK record is found.
