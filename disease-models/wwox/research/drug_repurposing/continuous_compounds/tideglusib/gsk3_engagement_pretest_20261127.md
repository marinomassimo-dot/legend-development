**Active question.** Does tideglusib provide an interpretable test of a WWOX-dependent GSK3 kinase mechanism separate from lithium's pharmacology? The [INV-09 retest](../../openscientist_retest_20261108/inv09_gsk3/README.md), [target-first IT15 comparison](../../target_first_it15_qb_gsk3_20261101/README.md), [ADV-214 held phospho-table search](../../advisor_pilot_20261113/brainstorm_20261116/adv214_gsk3b_tau_held_tables_sci_20261124/RESULT_ADV214_GSK3B_TAU_HELD_TABLES_SCI_20261124.md) and [non-lithium compound audit](../../advisor_pilot_20261113/adv098_agent_sheets_he_20261119/BATCH9_GSK3_nonlithium_tideglusib_SB216763_CHIR99021_LY2090314_ARA014418_HE_20261120.md) are the baseline. ADV-214 found RNA and total protein in the held matrices and no eligible phospho or kinase-activity comparison. The acute lithium result in Fig. 7d is a provoked behavioral endpoint and includes an effect in WT. [WWOX mouse source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32000863/) · [WWOX–GSK3β–Tau cell primary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22193544/)

**Registered prediction.** If a WWOX-LoF cell class has an increase in the relevant GSK3β substrate activity, independent interventions matched for time-resolved GSK3β engagement should give concordant proximal substrate and late-network changes. A measured GSK3α contrast is required to assign an isoform. Record Tau and the PI4KIIα–NMDA arm separately from Wnt/β-catenin. A response restricted to tideglusib despite comparable engagement, an opposite Wnt effect under a similar Tau response, or parallel suppression in WT without a corrected WWOX activity deficit would favor mixture of mechanisms or ordinary circuit suppression. Measure recruitment at matched starting drive and separate acute network effects from developmental outcomes. Neither a Ser9 band nor a plasma PK curve directly measures neural substrate engagement.

**First comparison.** Preserve ATP, substrate, preincubation and washout conditions when replaying native and recombinant data.
