# Metformin hydrochloride | Native target audit and disposition

**Decision.** Retain metformin as a conditional preclinical AMPK and lipid-synthesis comparator and defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. Public juvenile reference data support an assay for ACC1 Ser79 and a functional test of the lipid-supply hypothesis. The evidence located leaves WWOX cell-resolved PEN2 protein, ACC1 activity, metformin exposure and selective developmental recovery unmeasured in the same preparation. The findings in exposed pediatric donor cells and developing mouse white matter make a rise in pAMPK insufficient to advance the compound. [Exposure and prior comparisons](exposure_and_ampk_20261127.md)

## Decision-changing observation

**Inference.** Lower bulk PSENEN RNA warrants measuring upstream metformin coupling by cell class. The OPC direction in the pooled nuclei weakens a generalized glial-loss interpretation. ACC1 Ser79 can be measured separately from ACC2 phosphorylation, and phosphorylation is an inhibitory signal for conversion of acetyl-CoA to malonyl-CoA in the mechanistic reference. A measure of total protein and labeled flux still has to accompany the site assay. [Fullerton et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/nm.3372)

**Hypothesis and comparison.** In the age-matched WWOX preparation, measure intracellular metformin and separate ACC1 Ser79 from ACC2 while matching the direct AMPK comparator to the downstream substrate response within the relevant lineage. Combine a labeled de novo carbon source with a parallel lipid add-back trace to distinguish synthesis from uptake. Follow absolute malonyl-CoA and new myelin lipid, ATP recovery, fraction of eligible axons with first compact sheaths, and evoked conduction. If both perturbations lower de novo lipid synthesis but lipid add-back preserves sheaths and conduction without restoring ACC activity, support a lipid-supply limitation. If metformin alone depresses respiration at matched pACC, prioritize a drug-specific energy penalty. If the direct probe engages ACC while metformin gives weak substrate response at equal intracellular parent, prioritize upstream sensitivity measurements. Preserve recruitment and biological replication across all arms.

**Reopen** on age-stage verified intracellular parent and a replicated genotype-specific substrate or flux phenotype, followed by a selective improvement of energy recovery and first-sheath function. An independent review would change the assessment if it can identify developmentally matched WWOX phosphosite, exposure or paired functional data or reconstruct the early P14 TEM result by mouse. Existing public results support the conditional disposition without resolving those questions.
