# Metformin hydrochloride | Compartment exposure, AMPK and the first myelin sheaths

**Checkpoint.** Retain the existing deferral for pediatric WWOX trial priority. Metformin remains the active mechanistic comparison. The quantitative source check separates three observations: parent can reach mammalian brain, AMPK responds under some exposures, and myelin formation can move in the adverse direction despite this response. WWOX-specific target activity and neuronal or glial parent exposure require a separate test.

## What changes the WWOX experiment

**Data.** In the held untreated WWOX data, PSENEN RNA is lower in P17 mouse neocortex, P17 hippocampus and week-15 human cortical organoids. Group mean log2(1+x) differences are −0.320, −1.373 and −0.193 and retain their signs with any one library omitted. ATP6AP1 and AMPK catalytic subunit results are less uniform. ACACA decreases in mouse cortex (35.09 to 14.49 TPM) and hippocampus (23.14 to 12.29 TPM), but increases in human organoids (140.77 to 208.42 CPM). The bulk tissue contrasts can include composition and developmental differences. No genotype-specific, cell-resolved lysosomal PEN2 protein, native ionized parent or absolute carbon flux was identified in these data. The prior connection between WWOX and impaired AMPK phosphorylation comes from skeletal muscle. [WWOX replay](wwox_ampk_bulk.tsv) · [Direct WWOX muscle study, Fig. 4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6437662/) · [Juvenile WWOX phenotype](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awab174)

**Inference and hypothesis.** A lower PEN2-dependent response could shift the drug effect away from lysosomal AMPK signaling without requiring reduced parent uptake. In mouse hepatocytes, nominal 5 µM metformin for two hours raises pAMPK and pACC in the PEN2-sensitive assay and does not detectably suppress respiration in a separate assay. This reference supports separating pathways at equal intracellular drug. It sets no neural potency threshold. A competing WWOX mechanism is that phosphorylation of a reduced ACC pool further limits lipid synthesis, even if energy recovery improves. A third possibility is metformin-specific respiratory stress at the tested intracellular exposure. [Ma, Fig. 2B–C and Extended Data Fig. 3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04431-8)

**Discriminating comparison.** In a juvenile WWOX and matched littermate neuron–oligodendrocyte preparation, first record untreated total ACC, pACC/ACC, respiration and absolute ATP recovery at comparable first recruitment. At measured intracellular metformin, compare with a verified direct AMPK probe at the same downstream substrate response. Use a probe calibrated for the AMPK subunits present and avoid assigning dependence from dorsomorphin alone. Track labeled carbon into malonyl-CoA and myelin lipid separately from ATP, with compact first-sheath number and conduction as later outcomes. A diminished pACC response to metformin at equal intracellular drug with preserved direct-probe response supports impaired upstream coupling and makes cell-local PEN2 a rational next measurement. If both interventions raise pACC and lower lipid labeling or sheath formation, the ACC-linked developmental hypothesis gains support. If only metformin depresses respiration or recovery at matched pACC, a separate drug action is favored. A selective WWOX gain requires both energy recovery and stable functional sheaths while preserving early recruitment. [Direct AMPK probe and isoform boundary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6341387/)

**Pediatric feasibility.** The exact immediate-release metformin hydrochloride tablet has US pediatric type 2 diabetes experience from age ten. The label identifies relevant renal and hypoxic context and highlights carbonic anhydrase inhibitors such as topiramate, zonisamide and acetazolamide because of acidosis risk. The acquired post-radiation pilot has pediatric-to-young-adult eligibility and sequence effects. Its first-period cognitive analyses have endpoint-specific denominators and its imaging endpoints do not establish first-sheath formation in WWOX. These observations leave a juvenile neural exposure gap and weigh against advancing the clinical question ahead of the developmental comparison. [DailyMed, §§5.1, 7, 8.4 and 12.3](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=0230a3c3-34cf-478b-88c3-4d96e5300018) · [Ayoub et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8176964/)
