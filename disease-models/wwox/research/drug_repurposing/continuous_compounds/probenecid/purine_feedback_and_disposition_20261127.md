# Probenecid in WWOX loss of function

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Disposition.** Park for pediatric WWOX trial priority. Retain as a conditional preclinical comparison of purine feedback and organic-anion handling. The WWOX slice result supplies no clear benefit from PANX1 blockade. The highest measured group-mean parent concentration in pediatric CSF was about 22 µM in severe traumatic brain injury, compared with nominal half-maximal current inhibition at about 150 or 350 µM in recombinant PANX1 systems. The contrast justifies measuring local exposure and engagement together. Differences in matrix, physiology and protocol leave effects below those reference concentrations open.

## WWOX experiment and RNA countercheck

**Correction to the opening checkpoint.** Breton et al. used 10 µM Brilliant Blue FCF (BB-FCF), a dye, for the pannexin-directed comparison. Figure 3C–D gives 73 bursts across 6 slices from 3 animals for that arm. These are nested counts, with three animals as the biological unit. Table S1 gives a median of 0.84 before and 0.78 under the dye, while Fig. 3D plots a larger normalized frequency after exposure. The record does not reconcile those estimators or specify a common denominator for comparing them. No effect size is assigned. BB-FCF did not reproduce carbenoxolone's suppression in the same study. Carbenoxolone has multiple actions and d-APV abolished bursts in its own arm. No probenecid response, PANX1 engagement, ATP transient or adenosine transient was measured in this series. [Source audit and panel identities](../../../fulltext_dossiers/PMID34634460.md) · [Breton et al., Fig. 3C–D and Table S1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34634460/)

## Clinical feasibility

PROBCLUSTER is randomized and placebo-controlled in presurgical focal epilepsy. Its registry version posted 24 November 2026 listed recruiting, estimated enrollment of 600, ages 6–50, and no posted results. Its registered primary outcome is absence of another seizure from 60 minutes to 12 hours after treatment, verified by video-EEG. A separate sponsor phase 2a study uses an extended-release formulation (PTI5803) as open-label adjunctive therapy in focal cortical dysplasia from age 14, according to the CTIS record. The populations and endpoints do not establish PANX1 causality, applicability to WWOX, efficacy against chronic spontaneous events, or developmental benefit. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07318870](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07318870) · [CTIS PTI5803](https://euclinicaltrials.eu/ctis-public/view/2026-525162-21-00?lang=en) · [Registry snapshot replay](clinical_registry.tsv)

The US tablet label contraindicates use in children under two and documents transporter-mediated interactions with other medicines. The phase 1 trauma study found no difference in the small 3-month Glasgow Outcome Scale comparison, with two per arm missing follow-up. It combined two agents and did not measure seizure or PANX1 outcomes. Current data do not define target-engaging pediatric WWOX exposure, sustained tolerability or a way to separate drug interactions from phenotype. In any future study design, stable background treatment and measured co-medication exposure would be part of attribution. [US label, Contraindications and Drug Interactions](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=ae126418-5474-4fbe-b83c-bde8b2b4ae6e) · [Phase 1 trauma outcomes, Table 6](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0180280)

## Prediction and counterproof

- **Prediction.** At matched early recruited output, compare cortical WT and WWOX tissue across defined substrate conditions, including the glucose-replete reference. Measure free parent, verified PANX1 engagement, ATP and adenosine transients, early inhibitory recruitment and late recurrence in the same preparation. If PANX1 supplies an A1-dependent brake, less extracellular ATP and downstream adenosine should precede more recurrence. Restoring the A1 signal should reverse that worsening without restoring ATP release. An excitatory ATP driver instead predicts less recurrence when ATP and P2 signaling fall, with an independent PANX1 perturbation matching that direction.
- **Counterproof.** Verify ATP and adenosine independently and check that probenecid or its solvent does not interfere with the sensors. A change in recurrence with preserved PANX1-mediated ATP release and altered P2X7 response favors a receptor effect. An effect that remains after PANX1 occlusion and tracks changed transport of a verified organic anion substrate suggests a transporter contribution. Stable pH and matched initial recruitment and inhibition guard against interpreting general suppression as selective benefit. Require exposures in the same preparation and an independent perturbation before assigning the effect to PANX1.
- **Checkpoint.** First measure endogenous purine timing and an A1 functional control without relying on the drug. Reopen for a replicable, exposure-supported, beneficial perturbation with physiological inhibition preserved. A future independent review could change priority if new age-matched free CNS exposure, trial outcomes with exposure controls, or WWOX target-engagement data become available. Reanalysis of the aggregate proportions alone leaves the disposition unchanged.
