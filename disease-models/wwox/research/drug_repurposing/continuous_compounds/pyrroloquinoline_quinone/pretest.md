# Pyrroloquinoline quinone and mitochondrial reserve

**Checkpoint #203, 28 November 2026.** Start after carabersat #202 closed on `development/main`. Evaluate pyrroloquinoline quinone (PQQ), tracking disodium salt and parent quinone equivalents explicitly. The question differs from the terazosin glycolytic ATP comparison and the earlier redox probes. It tests durable mitochondrial adaptation against direct chemical or short-lived signaling effects of a quinone.

**Question.** At measured local exposure, does PQQ improve ATP-linked respiration and recovery from matched work in juvenile WWOX loss through a persistent CREB/PGC-1α or SIRT1/PGC-1α response? An opposing explanation is that PQQ changes a redox reporter or survival without a coupled functional gain. A second possibility is that early oxidation is an adaptive signal with a different concentration and timing requirement from direct scavenging.

**Predeclared contrast.** First align intact oxidized and reduced parent, measured central and plasma quantities, exposure times and units across studies. Track derivatives such as imidazolopyrroloquinoline independently when measured. Compare acute application and washout to a later transcription-dependent interval. Require a concordant increase in ATP-linked oxygen flux and evoked recovery after washout at matched firing, with absolute viable-cell counts and output per viable neuron or preparation. Add a cell-free reporter blank and orthogonal measurements of ATP and flux, plus controls for mitochondrial content and selective survival. Retain either a redox or a pathway dependence explanation only if it predicts the temporal result under a discriminating perturbation. Rapid reporter-only shifts or more mitochondrial markers with no preserved evoked function lower priority.

**Decision rule.** First establish whether quantified parent exposure overlaps a relevant native functional range at a credible age. Lack of parent measured in young brain and no specific functional phenotype leaves pediatric research priority pending. Food-supplement status alone does not establish age-specific safety or efficacy. Hold the next molecule until this one has an outcome and checkpoint.

**Primary starting points.** [Chowanadisai et al., CREB and PGC-1α](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2804159/) · [SIRT1/PGC-1α study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29185343/) · [PQQ nutritional-status experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3140972/).
