# PQQ opening comparison

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Checkpoint #203.** This first exposure comparison follows the [pretest](pretest.md). Prior context admitted is WWOX neuronal and glial energy recovery and the existing distinction between a reporter signal and function at matched demand.

**Human observation.** In Harris Study 1, ten adults aged 21–34 received one exposure. Fig. 2A shows approximately **9 nM** at about 2 h through the authors' glucose-dehydrogenase-based assay. Methods §2.2 gives a spike recovery of at least 85%. The Methods and results use serum and plasma terminology inconsistently, with serum in the figure and assay description. The study describes recovered nonderivatized PQQ. It does not provide an equilibrium unbound fraction, CSF, brain or cell-resolved concentration. Selected urinary markers in Study 2 are indirect readouts and do not measure neural ATP flux. [Harris et al., pp. 2077–2079, §2.1–2.2 and Fig. 2A](https://escholarship.org/content/qt9w23d73d/qt9w23d73d.pdf)

**Cellular observation.** Chowanadisai et al. used **30 µM nominal PQQ for 24 or 48 h** in mouse Hepa1–6 liver cells for the oxygen experiment. Fig. 1J reports n=8 assay observations and representative results from experiments done at least twice. The endpoint is the change in relative oxygen-sensitive fluorescence between 10 and 20 min after onset of the assay, after cells were handled and transferred. Rotenone and FCCP were controls; the assay does not isolate the oligomycin-sensitive, ATP-linked respiratory fraction. Lower nominal exposures used for other biogenesis markers should not be substituted for the oxygen endpoint. [Chowanadisai et al., Methods / Oxygen Consumption Assay and Fig. 1J](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2804159/)

**Reproducible scale.** `30 µM × 1,000 / 9 nM = 3,333` as a nominal bath versus reported peak circulating assay value comparison. Oral timing, tissue distribution and media speciation differ. The comparison neither estimates a juvenile neural exposure margin nor excludes a lower-concentration pathway effect. The papers share authors and the comparison does not offer an independent replication. The EU food entry is specific to disodium salt for the adult population and does not provide pediatric research exposure or a neurodevelopmental endpoint. [EU authorization](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A32018R1122)

**Next step.** Seek chemically resolved intact parent measurements in neural compartments and treat radioactivity as an upper bound on parent only when its analytical basis allows it. Check the direct redox and catalytic controls in the original cell study, including IPQ, catalase and the ATP-linked component in an independent preparation if available. The discriminating observation remains persistent function after exposure and washout, at matched demand and verified concentrations, accompanied by ATP-linked flux rather than only more organelle markers.
