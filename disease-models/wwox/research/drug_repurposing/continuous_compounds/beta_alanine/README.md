**Disposition.** Retain β-alanine as a conditional precursor and direct-receptor comparator. Defer pediatric WWOX priority. A rat study measured more hippocampal carnosine after sustained oral exposure. The human study measured a composite spectral band in a different region and leaves the carnosine change unquantified. ## What the human result could resolve

Let *f* be the baseline fraction of the measured human spectral band contributed by carnosine. With creatine and the rest of the band fixed, a fractional change *c* in carnosine predicts a fractional change *f × c* in the band/creatine ratio. A **hypothetical** carnosine rise equal to the rat point estimate of 89.5% would yield a band rise of **4.5%, 9.0%, 17.9% or 26.9%** if *f* were 5%, 10%, 20% or 30%, respectively. The first three lie inside the observed paired interval. The scaled upper endpoint intersects this scenario at *f* ≈ **29.3%**. This is sensitivity to spectral mixture under fixed-component assumptions. It is neither an observed human fraction nor an interspecies effect transfer. Overlapping contributors or changing creatine would further weaken attribution.

## One discriminating WWOX comparison

**Inference.** Tissue content is compatible with conversion but does not establish new carnosine production locally, aldehyde capture or function. A small human mixed-band change cannot lower the synthesis hypothesis by itself. Direct GlyR recruitment and changes in taurine availability can change activity and thereby apparent oxidative burden without trapping aldehyde. The held carnosine and anserine comparisons provide chemical controls for distinguishing intact dipeptide from free precursor. [Carnosine kinetics](../l_carnosine/kinetics.md) · [Anserine comparison](../anserine/README.md)

**Hypothesis and countercheck.** In juvenile WWOX-deficient neural material and matched controls, first establish a baseline excess in a specified newly formed lipid-aldehyde adduct. Then compare measured free labeled β-alanine with vehicle, with and without independently verified CARNS1 dependence. Use paired chemical and functional endpoints in the same preparation. Track newly labeled carnosine and its aldehyde adduct separately from the new protein adduct, preserve first excitatory and inhibitory recruitment, and match evoked work for the recovery comparison. Read the acute native chloride-dependent response and taurine pool or uptake before the delayed comparison. Verify free-parent clearance with an assay sensitive to the preparation's acute response range. Compensatory baseline or exposure changes in the CARNS1 control would make the interaction ambiguous.

- **Favors local carnosine mediation.** After parent clearance, label persists in carnosine, a new aldehyde-protein adduct falls and evoked recovery improves at matched work. Both benefits depend on verified CARNS1 activity, with label in the matched carnosine-aldehyde product supporting direct capture. Retained function alone would leave the chemistry open.
- **Favors alternatives.** Effects follow the acute parent and native anion conductance, disappear when first recruitment is matched, persist with synthesis suppressed, or follow taurine changes without the predicted chemical result. Label in carnosine without functional recovery supports precursor conversion alone. A well-covered null baseline adduct abnormality would lower the priority of this pathway before exposure testing.

## Pediatric feasibility and reopening

The adult brain study used sustained release and the adult PK shows meaningful formulation dependence. These studies supply no age-appropriate CNS exposure, repeat-exposure circuit tolerability or interaction data with background antiseizure treatment for WWOX. Paresthesia, native chloride-dependent recruitment, taurine changes, and separation of electrographic activity from functional recovery would matter if the preclinical gate were crossed. A second literature review would change disposition if it could retrieve a chemically separated juvenile oral parent/product series or a native WWOX aldehyde-adduct comparison. Reopen on those observations, with adult MRS and aged rat content kept as complementary controls.
