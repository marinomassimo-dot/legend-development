# Oxaloacetate and peripheral glutamate removal in WWOX loss of function

28 November 2026 UTC. context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. Continuous compound #197, INV-05 with a metabolic control. The [pretest](pretest.md) fixes peripheral sink, direct or downstream central action, and injury-dependent transfer as competing explanations.

**Disposition.** Retain oxaloacetate as a conditional probe in a preparation with distinct vascular and neural compartments. Defer pediatric WWOX priority. A large fall in serum glutamate is experimentally documented. The published comparisons used here do not identify how much intact juvenile WWOX interstitial glutamate would fall, whether a local metabolite contributes, or whether recurrence improves with recruitment preserved.

## Reproducible comparison

The [original numbers](study_inputs.tsv) use within-animal normalized serum values from each study comparison. The 30- and 60-min OAA summaries give **33.2%** and **54.4%** lower normalized serum Glu than their respective concurrent vehicle groups. These are ratios of group summaries. They are not a fitted concentration-time curve and the two time points are not treated as paired. The two-hour dialysis comparison is **36.2%** below its paired sample-time mean. No individual covariance or confidence interval is recovered from these means. [Study contrasts](study_contrasts.tsv) · [Adult PK inputs](clinical_pk.tsv) · [PK normalization contrast](pk_normalization.tsv)

The brain model takes the blood compartment as a measured input. For normalized interstitial glutamate `E` at baseline 1, relaxation time `tau` and a fixed post-perturbation blood fraction `p`, let `tau*dE/dt = 1 - c*(1-p) - l - E`. Here `c` is an effective linear sensitivity of the local balance to the vascular boundary, with no claim about literal influx. `l` is a reduction in local drive such as less release or more local clearance. This is a sensitivity calculation, without fitting the step, `c`, `tau` or local drive to WWOX data.

With the Zhang 60-min serum ratio **0.456** as an illustrative step and a stipulated **30 min** relaxation time, low, moderate and strong coupling (**0.05, 0.20 and 0.40**) give **2.35%, 9.42% and 18.83%** interstitial reductions after 60 min. A low-coupling case with a local drive term gives the **same 9.42%** reduction as the moderate pure-sink case at every sampled time. If blood Glu is restored to the control trajectory while OAA and other conditions are matched, the pure-sink effect disappears in this model. The low-coupling case retains a **7.06%** reduction at 60 min. This is an identifiability counterexample and a discriminating perturbation, without fitting MRS or treating a small simulated effect as a power estimate. ## Prediction and decision

**Inference.** A circulating transamination response can coexist with a protective functional result, and separate evidence supports possible intracellular action. Paired vascular and interstitial samples with matched stimulation and an exposure measurement are needed to connect these routes. The stroke MRS result and lower WWOX SLC1A2 do not identify the vascular contribution or establish a native slow glutamate excess.

**One discriminating comparison.** In a stage-matched Wwox and littermate or corrected neurovascular preparation with characterized barrier integrity, apply vascular OAA at verified parent and hold local pH, first release, oxygenation and stimulation fixed. Compare the same OAA exposure with and without restoration of the vascular glutamate trajectory to control. Confirm the vascular reaction with glutamate, aspartate and OAA and quantify brain-to-blood flux with source-resolved glutamate tracer. In the neural compartment measure the first evoked glutamate peak, slower extracellular clearance, physiological inhibitory recruitment and spontaneous recurrence. Measure intact labeled OAA separately from labeled aspartate, malate or pyruvate so that systemic conversion cannot be counted as parent entry. A vascular-clamp-sensitive change in decay with preserved first recruitment favors the sink. Persistence under the vascular glutamate clamp with measured local parent or product and a corresponding redox or enzyme response favors a local route. If the vessel and extracellular outcomes remain uncoupled at matched input, lower the priority. Effects confined to injury or evoked challenges do not advance intervention priority in WWOX.

**Feasibility.** The adult oral formulation has short tolerability observations and uncertain plasma engagement. Qualify formulation, concentration stability and blood assay preanalytics before connecting oral and intravenous results. The evidence used here does not quantify pediatric free central exposure, vascular engagement, long-term tolerability, interactions with background antiseizure drugs or a spontaneous WWOX effect. The decisive missing measurement is the matched neural time course and tracer transfer under a defined peripheral perturbation in an intact juvenile preparation. Independent review could change disposition if a source provides that paired transfer or distinguishes labeled parent from products after clinically relevant exposure.
