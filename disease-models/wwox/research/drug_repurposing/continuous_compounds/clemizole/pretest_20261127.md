# Clemizole hydrochloride, serotonergic recruitment versus nonspecific suppression

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. Clemizole hydrochloride (EPX-100) is the sole active compound after the [guanosine closure](../guanosine/exposure_clearance_and_disposition_20261127.md).

**Selection.** The existing phenotype-first work already reports electrographic activity in scn1lab and stxbp1b zebrafish comparisons and imperfect 5-HT2B binding-to-phenotype agreement among analogues. Do not repeat that census. Pediatric Dravet and Lennox–Gastaut trials make the formulation and tolerability transferable questions, with distinct clinical endpoints. The earlier WWOX fenfluramine dossier shows why sparse serotonergic transcripts do not establish endogenous serotonin release. Use the held organoid and juvenile mouse matrices to ask about receptor competence, and only use receptor-level pharmacology with concentration and assay identity preserved. [Held DEE model analysis](../../phenotype_first_it29_20261105/p3_D/README.md) · [Held fenfluramine model competence](../fenfluramine/README.md) · [ARGUS](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04462770) · [LIGHTHOUSE](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05066217)

**Registered prediction.** If clemizole directly recruits a native 5-HT2-dependent inhibitory pathway, an effect on late electrographic activity should track measured free parent and receptor-competent tissue, with preservation of first recruitment. A matched tissue challenge with a validated receptor-selective perturbation should remove the late effect while leaving vehicle and first-response baselines interpretable. Quantify both external serotonin and native recruitment before assigning independence from serotonin release. If electrophysiological suppression follows H1 antagonism or generalized depression, the effect should be mimicked by a matched H1 control or typically accompany broader changes to first-response transmission. A reduction in swimming, sedation or a drop in the first response alone is insufficient. Distinguish binding displacement, functional signaling, receptor subtype and a possible peripheral 5-HT2B safety liability. [Primary parent comparison](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6075536/) · [Held analogue comparison and study pointers](../../phenotype_first_it29_20261105/p3_D/README.md)

**First comparison.** Retrieve the two phase 3 protocols and check status and outcomes directly. Trace the ARGUS open-label exposure denominators, background ASM and missingness separately from randomized estimates. Locate parent and any metabolite plasma data, DDI cohorts and functional 5-HT2B evidence. From the held record, compare assay binding versus electrography and test receptor-detection fragility by age, region and preparation, keeping H1 controls visible. Pediatric trial relevance requires a measured exposure and a usable WWOX native comparator. Reopen the design if the data separate function from general suppression at that exposure.
