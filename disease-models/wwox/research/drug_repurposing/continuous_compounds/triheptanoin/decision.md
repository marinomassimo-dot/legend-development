# Triheptanoin decision for WWOX loss of function

**Disposition.** Park general pediatric WWOX trial prioritization. Reopen for evidence that odd-chain carbon replenishes a deficient absolute cycle pool under demand and restores neural function beyond both reference intake and an energy-matched even-chain control in independent WWOX preparations. The initial hypothesis survives as a substrate-specific research question. Primary human findings from other genetic disorders constrain the endpoint. [Tracer and randomized clinical comparison](tracer.md) · [Three-arm control and cell classes](comparators.md)

**A ketogenic diet changes the interpretation.** In a ten-person Glut1DS study, C7 replaced part of dietary fat while the investigators sought to preserve the fat-to-carbohydrate-plus-protein ratio. In the eight participants above 2 mM baseline beta-hydroxybutyrate (C4), the authors classified four as compatible and four as poorly compatible. Comparing day 1 and day 3 morning values, published to one decimal place, three classified as compatible fell by less than 50%, one by exactly 50%, and all four poorly compatible fell by more than 50%. The figure caption specifies less than 50%. Keep the original grouping and the rounding boundary visible. One participant did not receive the fourth administration. This was an acute study without a randomized placebo comparison of clinical outcomes. [Primary study, Methods, Fig. 2 and Table 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10238483/)

Two participants, one from each compatibility group, showed cessation of electroclinical events on C7 followed by a transient increase after discontinuation. Their respective same-clock C4 declines were about 31% and 73%. One further compatible participant had reduced EEG abnormalities without an observed seizure change. All appreciable changes had returned to baseline by follow-up. Selected profiles in Fig. 3 show five-carbon ketones; the authors report no correlation with compatibility. A full numeric ten-person paired C5 series with time-aligned EEG is unavailable from the published tables, so these rows cannot establish that C5 compensated for loss of C4. A research endpoint should preserve both ketone species and the timing of seizure burden rather than using a change in C4 to assign benefit. **Clinical transfer.** Three of 13 people in one WWOX series had tried a ketogenic diet. Their background therapy and nutrition matter if this hypothesis is revisited. A WWOX-wide clinical effect, a reliable responder subgroup and a net benefit over nutritional displacement remain open. [WWOX series](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10952634/)
