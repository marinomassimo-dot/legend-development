# Does an even-chain oil isolate anaplerosis in an epilepsy model?

**Cell-type prediction recorded before measuring.** Multiple cell classes with lower mutase could support a widespread constraint. If glial mutase is preserved, a WWOX block on utilization of the glia-associated tracer cannot be inferred from bulk decline. In the juvenile WWOX hippocampal nuclear dataset, astrocyte-enriched Mut (MMUT) is actually higher (25.32 to 30.80 CPM, 37 and 53 UMIs). Excitatory nuclei also increase (9.05 to 10.53 CPM) while inhibitory nuclei decline (11.50 to 9.06). All assigned classes pooled increase from 11.50 to 13.33 CPM, with +1.39 CPM attributed to within-class expression differences and +0.44 to changes in class weights. This conflicts with the bulk hippocampal decrease. RNA compartment, selection, developmental sampling, and sequencing depth differ; one pool per genotype does not establish a change in enzyme capacity. A bulk-based prediction of a uniform bottleneck is weakened. [Nuclear counts](cell_types.tsv) · [Decomposition](mixture.tsv) · [Calculation](cell_types.py) · [Bulk contrasts](screen.tsv)

**Two different even-chain comparisons.** In the adult refractory epilepsy trial the comparator contained 55% C8 and 45% C10 triglycerides. Seventeen participants were randomized per arm and one person was excluded from the C7 analysis following a different diagnosis. Only 11 and 9 participants completed, respectively. Five comparators and one C7 recipient had over 50% seizure reduction in Table 2 (p=.16). Baseline focal impaired-awareness seizures were present in 16/17 comparator participants and 10/16 in the analyzed C7 arm, and all six responders had that seizure type. Median seizure frequency on treatment was 54% and 102% of baseline, respectively (p=.13). These are percentages remaining, not reductions of 54% and 102%. This study neither demonstrates C7 superiority nor cleanly isolates an odd-chain mechanism. [Borges et al., Methods and Tables 1–2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6398112/)

C10 is a potentially active pharmacological comparator. In acute hippocampal CA1 recordings, 300 µM decanoate reduced evoked AMPA-mediated excitatory current amplitude by 38.9% (SEM 5.5%, six recordings); the inhibitory currents did not show a significant change. The epilepsy trial did not measure free synaptic decanoate or AMPA occupancy. This controlled experiment motivates considering a C10-mediated circuit contribution to the active comparator, without attributing individual clinical responses to it. Pure C8 as a fuel comparator and separate AMPA-mediated currents would improve discrimination. [Chang et al., Fig. 2A–D](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4805082/)

In a separate four-month trial in long-chain fatty acid oxidation disorders, 32 participants were assigned to C7 or pure C8. The post-exercise muscle phosphocreatine comparison included eight and seven adults; it did not show superior recovery with C7. Cardiac measures had a different pattern; all but one had normal cardiac function at baseline. This demonstrates that the choice of tissue and readout can change the conclusion even with a chemically appropriate comparator. These muscle data provide no cerebral flux or pediatric WWOX efficacy estimate. [Gillingham et al., Results and Fig. 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6545116/)

**Pediatric feasibility.** The medicinal oral oil has precedent in molecularly confirmed long-chain fat oxidation disorders. Formulation and tube material matter where tube feeding is common. The label specifies silicone or polyurethane enteral tubes, describes tube dysfunction and excludes PVC tubes. A calorie-matched comparison would have to preserve age-appropriate total energy, essential lipid provision and intake when gastrointestinal intolerance occurs. The Glut1DS trial's background medication and dietary restrictions limit transfer to WWOX. [US label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2020/213687s000lbl.pdf) · [Glut1DS clinical comparison](tracer.md)

**Diet is an effect modifier to test.** The primary WWOX series lists a ketogenic diet in three of 13 histories, and the authors say one of the three continued it. No C7 exposure or response is given. A Glut1DS combination study is a lead for possible reduction of four-carbon ketosis after adding C7 in some individuals; its primary tables still need review before using it as a decision premise. Track C4 and C5 ketones separately, since total ketosis could mask a shift between them. [WWOX series, Table 2 and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10952634/) · [Glut1DS dietary interaction study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-36001-x)

**Why retain a third, reference diet.** In Table 4 of a three-week CD1 mouse experiment, hippocampal succinate was 0.12, 0.06 and 0.13 µmol/g on standard, C8 and C7 diets, respectively (n=11, 10 and 9). C7/C8 is 2.17, yet C7/reference is 1.08. Malate gives the same warning: 0.23, 0.13 and 0.22 µmol/g, so C7/C8 is 1.69 while C7/reference is 0.96. A two-arm comparison would suggest large odd-chain benefits although both C7 means are close to reference. These ratios use rounded group means; they are descriptive and convey no uncertainty on individuals. Static levels do not resolve flux, and the mice had no WWOX lesion. For a genotype-selective rescue use controls under the reference diet as well as C7 and C8 and compare absolute change before interpreting a C7 advantage. [Hippocampal diet comparison, Results and Table 4](https://doi.org/10.1038/jcbfm.2013.184)
