# Triheptanoin and replenishment of the WWOX tricarboxylic acid cycle

**Why this molecule.** The laboratory previously used triheptanoin as a fuel comparator for altered pyruvate dehydrogenase signaling. A generic developmental-epilepsy drug ranking also returned it, with little disease specificity. The new question is whether odd-chain carbon can specifically restore a depleted cerebral cycle intermediate pool. A rise in ketone availability or provision of extra calories might account for improved function independently. [Earlier fuel classification](../../pdh_pdk_sci44_20261110/README.md) · [Earlier ranking audit](../../proton_it18_20261101/README.md)

**Prediction before inspecting the clinical comparator and WWOX gene contrasts.** Following digestion, the seven-carbon fatty acid can supply both acetyl-CoA and propionyl-CoA. Propionyl carbon can reach succinyl-CoA through propionyl-CoA carboxylase and methylmalonyl-CoA mutase. A WWOX-selective replenishment mechanism predicts stronger functional recovery with odd-chain than with energy-matched even-chain substrate, accompanied by net recovery of the depleted cycle pool and labeled odd-chain contribution downstream. Fractional labeling alone can increase merely because an unlabeled pool shrinks. Compare absolute pools and recovery during demand, matched substrate consumption and ketone supply. Treat direct neural heptanoate use and liver-derived five-carbon ketones as distinct routes. If even-chain substrate reproduces the functional result, or if both fail where downstream respiration limits recovery, lower the priority of a specific odd-chain effect.

**First checks.** Compare PCCA, PCCB, MCEE, MMUT, associated cofactor enzymes, ketone transport and ketolysis in untreated WWOX tissue and organoids. Find a direct energy-matched comparator and controlled pediatric experience, separating brain, systemic metabolic and seizure endpoints. Record exact formulation, gastrointestinal tolerability, nutritional displacement, age and delivery constraints before considering trial feasibility.

**Initial finding.** Methylmalonyl-CoA mutase (MMUT, annotated MUT/Mut in these archived datasets) fell in all three WWOX contrasts. Differences in mean log2(1 + normalized expression) were −0.30 in mouse cortex, −0.81 in mouse hippocampus and −0.12 in human organoids. Omitting any single library preserved all three signs. PCCA fell in hippocampus and rose in organoids. Thus WWOX-intrinsic replenishment may face a downstream constraint even when an odd-chain substrate bypasses PDH. Reduced RNA alone does not establish a flux bottleneck. If mutase capacity limits throughput, odd-chain substrate could increase upstream C3 or methylmalonate while yielding little net succinyl-CoA recovery. An energy benefit could still arise from acetyl units. Pair substrate tracing with absolute pools, competing even-chain fuel and functional recovery to distinguish these possibilities. The clinical Glut1DS trial missed its primary seizure endpoint. Next examine the even-chain comparator, actual substrate utilization and functional readouts.
