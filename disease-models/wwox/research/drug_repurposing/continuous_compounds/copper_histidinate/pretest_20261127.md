# Copper histidinate and mitochondrial complex IV copper delivery in WWOX loss of function

**Status.** Opening checkpoint. Evaluate a trace-element delivery mechanism in stage-matched models. Copper histidinate is a drug for a defined pediatric copper-transport disorder. Its label describes a different disease, a parenteral route and toxicity from copper accumulation. [US prescribing information, §§1, 5.1, 8.4 and 14](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=e3aeefc2-f44d-4337-9c2e-3dba44a89e48)

The WWOX programme has evidence motivating oxidative function and myelin phenotypes. Copper histidinate offers a specific comparison between substrate delivery and downstream respiratory capacity. A positive result in inherited copper transport impairment provides an assay control and illustrates the kind of starting deficit needed. Neither low ATP nor altered bulk RNA establishes deficient copper delivery in WWOX. [Laboratory roadmap](../../roadmap_current.md) · [Earlier oxidative-flux comparison](../levocarnitine/README.md) · [Human CTR1 study and Fig. 3 caption](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35913762/?dopt=Abstract)

**Prediction.** In stage-matched WWOX-deficient and isogenic corrected neural preparations under the same copper-replete baseline, first test for reduced complex IV activity per amount of assembled enzyme and reduced mitochondrial or enzyme-associated copper. At the same measured extracellular availability, ask whether a distinguishable copper tracer delivered as copper histidinate reaches the mitochondrial enzyme fraction before complex IV activity, respiratory reserve and evoked recovery improve preferentially in WWOX cells. Prespecify mitochondrial content, an independent respiratory-complex assay, free or labile copper and oxidative injury as controls. A histidine control addresses ligand effects. A known copper-deficient or uptake-limited preparation establishes the dynamic range.

**Interpretation.** More whole-cell copper with unchanged enzyme-associated tracer or activity favors an intracellular-delivery or assembly limitation. Engaged enzyme without better evoked recovery supports biochemical engagement only. Equal recovery in corrected and WWOX cells following deliberate copper deprivation supports a generic deficiency effect. Improved membrane markers or survival with unchanged complex IV again shifts the mechanism search. An increase in labile copper or oxidation without selective functional recovery stops advancement.

**Next comparison.** Audit the direct respiratory preparations and compare the compound's CTR1 development evidence with the Menkes exposure record and the prior WWOX mitochondrial data.
