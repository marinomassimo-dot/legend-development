# Copper histidinate and complex IV delivery in WWOX loss of function

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. The registered question is whether neural low complex IV function arises from inadequate copper delivery or from respiratory abundance, assembly or upstream substrate use. The comparisons use human fibroblasts, juvenile mouse bulk tissue and assigned hippocampal nuclei, human cerebral organoids, and regulatory aggregate pediatric survival. Units remain within assay.

**Decision.** Park copper histidinate for pediatric WWOX trial priority. Keep a compartment-resolved copper response as a narrowly defined preclinical test. A modest mouse bulk transporter signal changes after cell-class weighting, and the human organoid signal is close to zero. The existing WWOX respiratory evidence motivates the assay, while copper amount, metal-site loading and complex IV activity per assembled enzyme in WWOX remain unmeasured in the verified sources. [Registered prediction](pretest_20261127.md)

## Positive control and what it measures

Batzios et al. report low cellular copper and reduced cytochrome c oxidase activity in fibroblasts with a defined CTR1 defect. In the activity experiment they pooled two affected lines and two control lines across three experiments. Nominal 50 µM copper histidinate added for approximately 21 hours increased activity to the control range according to the authors. Normalization was to total cellular protein. The same preparation showed higher basal, ATP-linked, maximal and spare respiratory outputs after exposure from three experiments. Two related affected lines constrain generalization. [Batzios et al., advance-access PDF, pp. 5 and 8–9, Fig. 3A–D, Cell culture and enzyme Methods](https://pediatricsnationwide.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Batzios-S-Tal-G-DiStasio-AT-et-al.pdf)

This is an appropriate assay control for copper-responsive dysfunction. Activity per total cellular protein leaves assembled enzyme amount and metal-site occupancy unresolved. The mutant transporter localized to the membrane and whole-cell transporter protein did not fall in this study, illustrating why abundance alone cannot measure uptake. In the authors' clinical observations, serum values in the reference range coexisted with low CSF copper before treatment. Serum repletion therefore cannot substitute for a neural exposure measurement. No patient-level result is used to infer a WWOX treatment effect. [Batzios et al., pp. 2, 4–6, Fig. 2B–C](https://pediatricsnationwide.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Batzios-S-Tal-G-DiStasio-AT-et-al.pdf)

## WWOX respiratory evidence and RNA replay

The held Abu-Remaileh experiment reported reduced basal and maximal oxygen consumption in Wwox-null mouse embryonic fibroblasts, alongside changes in lactate and other metabolic measures. Whole-cell oxygen consumption can change through substrate, cell state, mitochondrial amount or several complexes. The Choo respiratory-complex report was accessible only at abstract level through the free routes used here. The held genetic-interaction evidence places Wwox as a modifier of outgrowth caused by imposed respiratory-complex perturbations in flies. The reverse causal direction, from Wwox loss to a copper-limited complex IV defect, remains unverified. [Held Abu-Remaileh dossier, Fig. 2](../../../fulltext_dossiers/PMID25012504.md) · [Choo et al., abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26390919/) · [Held interaction direction](../../rs_r6_it30_20261106/evidence_rs_r6.tsv)

## Pediatric reference and transfer

- FDA pooled two open-label single-arm studies for the treated cohorts. The early survival comparison used 31 treated and 17 eligible external controls with reported HR 0.22 (95% CI 0.10–0.49). The late comparison used 35 treated and 16 external controls with HR 0.27 (0.12–0.57). The endpoint clock starts at birth, treatment timing defines the treated groups, and the 16 late controls are a subset of the 17 early controls. They cannot be counted as independent control cohorts. These hazard ratios use external controls, eligibility including severe ATP7A genotypes and censoring. The fractions alive in the FDA tables are descriptive cutoff status. [Integrated review §6.2.1.3, printed pp. 32–34](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2026/211241Orig1s000IntegratedR.pdf) · [FDA results Tables 2–3](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-trials-snapshots/drug-trials-snapshots-zycubo) · [Denominator replay](menkes_comparators.tsv)
- The earlier published neonatal cohort had 12 early recipients and 15 historical late comparators who also received copper. The later regulatory comparison uses untreated controls. Without a patient-level mapping, the numerical overlap with the early publication is unresolved. The early report is kept as historical context and supplies no independent denominator to pool with the FDA estimates. [Kaler et al., Abstract and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3477514/)
- The labeled route is parenteral in pediatric Menkes disease and circumvents impaired intestinal absorption. The 50 µM nominal fibroblast concentration supplies no pediatric WWOX dose or compartment exposure. The label identifies copper accumulation risks including renal, hepatic and hematologic effects. A pediatric prior in transport deficiency therefore informs feasibility and safety monitoring, with neural exposure and WWOX benefit still to establish. [US label §§1, 5.1, 8.4, 12 and 14](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=e3aeefc2-f44d-4337-9c2e-3dba44a89e48)

## Discriminating result

**Test first.** In age- and region-appropriate WWOX and corrected neural models under a copper-replete baseline, measure labile and mitochondrial copper, assembled complex IV amount, and complex IV activity normalized to that amount. Pair this with mitochondrial mass, another respiratory complex and evoked recovery. In the copper-responsive control, verify assay dynamic range with the same activity and exposure readouts. The most informative WWOX result would be low enzyme-associated copper and low activity per assembled enzyme, followed by selective recovery of both after measured copper delivery and then evoked recovery. Background isotope and specimen integrity controls are necessary to distinguish incorporation from nonspecific adsorption.

**Counterchecks.** Greater whole-cell copper with low enzyme-associated signal favors compartment delivery or assembly. Low total activity with preserved activity per assembled enzyme localizes the baseline defect to enzyme abundance. Copper-dependent assembly remains possible in that case. Early productive isotope incorporation followed by recovery of assembled amount and circuit function would distinguish it from a fixed assembly limitation. More isotope without activity or assembled amount suggests a nonproductive fraction. A response limited to experimentally depleted cells establishes generic deficiency reversal. If labile copper and oxidative injury rise without functional recovery, stop advancement. Review becomes decision-changing with direct evidence of a copper-responsive WWOX neural deficit or a credible exposure bridge for such a measured deficit.

## Reproduction

From this directory run `python3 -B replay_copper_comparison.py /home/desktop/legend-development/files --out /tmp/legend-copper-replay`. The code checks public matrix digests, reuses the held gene and class-assignment logic, and reproduces the expression tables and FDA cutoff fractions. The decomposition uses the mean class UMI weights for within-class rate differences and the mean class rates for UMI-weight differences. It includes a descriptive sensitivity requiring at least 100 assigned nuclei in each pool for a class. Neither the number of nuclei nor the short-term cell exposures determines a clinical inference.
