# Acetoacetate and vesicular glutamate filling in WWOX loss of function

28 November 2026 UTC. context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. Continuous compound #196, INV-05 with an energy-metabolism control. The [pretest](pretest.md) defines the competing explanations.

**Disposition.** Retain acetoacetate as a conditional ex vivo probe. Defer pediatric WWOX priority. The direct transport experiment supports chloride-dependent inhibition in a reconstituted transporter. The native slice experiment offers a circuit observation with a different extracellular chloride recipe in each arm. Neither establishes a WWOX vesicle-filling defect or the free presynaptic exposure required to engage it.

## Reproducible chloride sensitivity

We digitized the fitted lines at the zero crossing of the Hill plot in Juge Fig. 3D (PDF page 5, printed p. 103). The [coordinate input](figure_coordinates.tsv) gives the 300 dpi crop positions. To reproduce the crop from the [university-hosted paper](https://graylab.ucdavis.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/202/2015/02/2010-Juge-et-al-Neuron.pdf), use `pdftoppm -f 5 -l 5 -r 300 -x 1725 -y 400 -W 590 -H 620 -png juge.pdf chloride-axis`. Use the fitted lines and their published Hill slopes. Interpolating in log₁₀ molar chloride gives apparent activation midpoints **3.73, 11.2 and 82.48 mM** at **0, 0.2 and 1 mM AcAc**, respectively. An illustrative ±2-pixel change gives **3.63–3.83**, **10.91–11.53** and **80.24–84.78 mM**. This brackets reading precision only.

At 0.2 mM AcAc, the normalized fitted uptake is **42%** of the normalized baseline at **10 mM chloride** and **88%** at **20 mM chloride**. The original Fig. 3A functional IC₅₀ is an independent reported fit at fixed chloride. The replay divides values normalized to each curve's own fitted maximum and diagnoses sensitivity to assay conditions. It cannot estimate absolute transport, binding affinity, vesicle gradients or a pediatric exposure target. ## Inference, prediction and decision

**Inference.** The acute assay makes a chloride-sensitive transport component credible. The native phenotype also admits a receptor-side explanation, and energy supply can change terminal or network behavior independently. Bulk RNA gives no reason to assume that excess vesicle filling is shared by WWOX preparations. Native VGLUT1-heavy boutons are a better verification context than extrapolating an effect size from reconstituted VGLUT2.

**Prediction.** At verified local AcAc, direct loading inhibition should reduce vesicular content and cleft glutamate per independently counted successful fusion. Under matched pH, ATP and vesicle driving force, a measured change in presynaptic cytosolic chloride should attenuate that component. A delayed, ketolysis-dependent recovery in ATP under matched activity with unchanged single-event glutamate would favor the fuel mechanism. A shifted fusion probability or decay with unchanged content and initial glutamate favors release or clearance instead.

**One discriminating comparison.** In a staged juvenile Wwox and littermate or isogenic-corrected preparation, compare AcAc with an anion-, pH-, osmolarity- and counterion-controlled vehicle. Separate direct chloride dependence in calibrated VGLUT1 vesicles from intact-terminal measurements. In intact terminals, verify local parent, BHB and exposure stability, and measure cytosolic and luminal chloride separately. Match first afferent input and measure presynaptic ATP, independently counted fusion events, single-event glutamate with decay and a vesicular-content corroboration. Compare across a validated chloride perturbation and a reduced-ketolysis control verified by tracer flux, with matched baseline ATP and vesicle driving force. Check exogenous-glutamate postsynaptic responsiveness and first excitation of inhibitory cells. Read physiological inhibition and spontaneous recurrence under the same preparation. A smaller cleft peak without content corroboration cannot uniquely distinguish filling from fusion-pore changes or clearance. Equal effects across genotypes could validate generic modulation. A WWOX circuit advance requires abnormal baseline filling or recurrence, correction at verified exposure, and preserved early excitatory and inhibitory recruitment.

**Pediatric feasibility.** The formulation and evidence differ between a lithium salt used in a slice, endogenous ketones during a mixed diet and a putative isolated AcAc intervention. The sources used here supply neither a pediatric isolated formulation with verified brain free exposure nor an interaction or tolerability estimate with WWOX background antiseizure drugs. CSF, plasma and metabolic PET are useful exposure boundaries. The next informative measurement is native juvenile terminal exposure with a selective per-fusion and circuit effect. Loss of inhibitory recruitment, failure to resolve single-event glutamate or an implausible exposure bridge keeps the compound at probe status. A new independent review could change the decision if it identifies a direct native AcAc filling result with matched chloride or verified juvenile presynaptic exposure.
