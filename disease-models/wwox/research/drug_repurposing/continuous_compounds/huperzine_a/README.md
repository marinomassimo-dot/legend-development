# (−)-Huperzine A in WWOX loss of function

28 November 2026 UTC · SCI_CODEX · INV-05 · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. [Opening checkpoint and rivals](pretest.md) · [Printable brief](../../view/research_reports/huperzine-a.pdf)

**Disposition.** Retain as a conditional probe for cholinergic control of recurrence. Defer systemic pediatric WWOX development. The strongest preclinical EEG evidence concerns a short, fixed-order exposure in acquired adult temporal-lobe epilepsy. Its cholinergic dependence is supported in a separate kindling comparison. No direct WWOX response, native septal cholinergic phenotype or pediatric brain exposure was found in this review.

## Native WWOX evidence

Held bulk libraries are untreated P17 mouse cortex (GSE158682, 4 WT/3 KO), P17 hippocampus (GSE158681, 2 WT/3 KO) and week-15 human cerebral organoids (GSE156243, 2 controls/4 WWOX deficient). The replay uses deposited values on each dataset's own scale (mouse TPM, normalized human CPM), contrasts mean log2(1 + expression), and checks each single-library omission. The predeclared abundance floor is a mean of at least 1 in both arms, not a reliable detection claim. Nuclear data in GSE158684 are pooled by genotype and compared by broad classes, not treated as independent animals.

- **CHRM1** was lower across all three above the floor and with every omission (cortex 115.2→87.0, hippocampus 65.3→44.1, organoids 23.5→15.0 on their native scales). Pooled hippocampal nuclei also show lower CHRM1 (45.9→40.2 CPM). Most of that aggregate decrement follows the descriptive within-class component (−4.9 CPM of −5.7). This prioritizes receptor reserve as a rival; receptor occupancy, evoked response and cell-type localization remain unknown.
- **ACHE** was higher in cortex (72.4→99.1 TPM), with no direction surviving all omissions in hippocampus or organoids. Pooled nuclei signal (11.6→14.2 CPM) does not settle the hippocampal bulk result or enzyme activity. **CHAT** and **SLC18A3** were below the prespecified floor in hippocampus. Septal somata lie outside the hippocampal sample, and RNA yields no measure of cholinergic terminal density or release. Cortical CHAT decreased (3.66→1.20 TPM), with uncertain anatomical source. **CHRNA7** hippocampal bulk was similar across arms (7.75→7.82 TPM, omission range crosses zero); pooled nuclei trend higher without animal resolution. **GRIN2B** fell in mouse hippocampus and rose in organoids with robust directions to omissions, further limiting extrapolation of a single receptor profile. BCHE and CHRM2 checks are in the full table.
- The organoid contrast cannot represent an intact septal projection. AChE abundance, ability to release endogenous ACh and the sign of recruited inhibitory or excitatory currents remain separate unknowns.

## Exposure and pediatric feasibility

Li et al. reported a peak of 2.59 ng/mL in total adult plasma after a single tablet in 12 healthy adults, equivalent to 10.7 nM at parent MW 242.32. The nominal 126 µM half-inhibition for NMDA-evoked current in dissociated rat CA1 neurons is about 11,800 times that scale and about 1,260 times the nominal 100 nM local solution in Yu's kindling comparison. These are cross-assay scales. They leave unbound synaptic concentration, SPN-817 exposure, voltage/use dependence and other receptor states unmeasured. Direct NMDA blockade should not be the default explanation for the low-concentration local comparison, and remains a testable rival. [Adult PK abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18348466/) · [Rat CA1 current abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11516831/) · [PubChem identity](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/854026)

The defined synthetic extended-release product SPN-817 has adult investigational precedent. The May 2024 sponsor interim report gives 41 enrolled, 19 maintenance completers at cutoff and 16 with focal seizures among those completers. The reported 63% with at least 50% reduction corresponds to approximately 10 of those 16. The 19/41 is a snapshot at cutoff rather than permanent retention, and the absence of placebo and the conditioned denominator prevent an intention-to-treat efficacy estimate. The report describes gastrointestinal, appetite and central adverse effects, with adverse-event discontinuation reported at 22% in titration and 2.4% in maintenance. These are phase-specific sponsor percentages. The open-label adult trial NCT05518578 (60 estimated) and blinded placebo-controlled focal trial NCT06798896 (216 estimated) listed recruiting, eligible ages 18–70 and no posted results on 28 November 2026. There is no pediatric exposure bridge in these records, and over-the-counter extract and investigational ER formulation are distinct preparations. A pediatric research path first needs age-appropriate free exposure, a pharmacodynamic target range, and tolerability sufficient to monitor feeding, vigilance, cardiorespiratory effects and background drug interactions. ## Inference, hypothesis and discriminating observation

**Inference.** A target can be present at the RNA level and its substrate absent at an affected synapse. The reproducible cross-model change is lower CHRM1 RNA, while the strongest specific drug experiment implicates local α7 signaling in kindling. The direction of the drug in WWOX therefore depends on the combination of residual release, receptor reserve and native circuit inhibition.

**Hypothesis.** WWOX may have enough evoked cholinergic input for AChE inhibition to increase local ACh dwell time, yet insufficient receptor reserve or an altered inhibitory response for the same ACh extension to suppress recurrence. The active compound could retain a useful effect in a preparation that preserves this input and intact GABA-mediated inhibition.

**One discriminating comparison.** Compare juvenile Wwox-deficient and matched WT in a preparation with demonstrable septal input to dorsal CA1 and spontaneous recurrent activity. Read native ACh amplitude and decay, AChE catalytic engagement, first excitatory recruitment, inhibitory charge at native chloride and recurrence in the same comparison. Under equal temperature, vigilance and recording sensitivity, compare huperzine A to vehicle and one orthogonal reversible AChE inhibitor matched for local engagement and free exposure measurement. Use reversible local receptor interruption with its own control to check whether the recurrence effect requires cholinergic signaling. Lower recurrence with both inhibitors, longer ACh dwell time, maintained first recruitment and loss of the effect under receptor interruption would support target-mediated action. Engagement without ACh prolongation suggests a source constraint. ACh prolongation with absent or reversed physiological inhibition disconfirms the network-rescue hypothesis. A reproducible residual that tracks NMDA current at fixed ACh and local exposure supports the direct-current rival and warrants independent functional testing.

**Next and review trigger.** Stop compound escalation at the mechanistic-probe stage. Reopen only for a demonstrated native cholinergic deficit or spontaneous-network response at matched engagement with preserved circuit function. A review could change this decision if it locates parallel-control spontaneous data with temperature and state measurements or an applicable human central exposure bridge. Reinterpretation of the same fixed-order series does not close either gap. Next queue selection checks for overlapping work and considers a distinct developmental lipid or glial mechanism.
