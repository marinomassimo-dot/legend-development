SLC35A4 and FKTN decline in all three bulk comparisons and every single-library omission. In the one human progenitor comparison, the delivery transcripts move in the opposite direction. DAG1 and LAMA1 also move in opposing directions between mouse cortex and organoid. Mixed cell types and the developmental stage are viable alternatives to an intrinsic transfer deficit. The data support separating cytosolic donor synthesis, Golgi access, transfer to α-dystroglycan and relative abundance of laminin-rich endfeet. They leave each of those rates unmeasured. ## Target and compartment anchors

Gerin et al. started C57BL/6 exposure at three weeks and measured tissues three weeks later. Fig. 7B reads approximately 0.29→0.49 normalized HPLC peak units in whole brain and 0.11→0.36 in skeletal muscle (four control and three exposed mice). A significance marker is shown for muscle only. The peak can include coeluting CDP-glucose, and the study did not measure unbound brain ribitol, cell-level donor, cerebral α-dystroglycan function or rescue. Its two responsive CRPPA-mutant fibroblast lines out of four had improved IIH6 staining, and the investigators verified restored laminin binding in those selected responsive lines. The POMT1-mutant comparator did not show a staining response. The cell exposure, six-week brain homogenate and human clinical compartments cannot be treated as interchangeable. [Primary Fig. 4, Fig. 7 and Mice methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms11534)

The US label reports a steady-state mean total plasma peak of 141 µg/mL, approximately **0.93 mM**, and a 24-hour average inferred from AUC of **0.22 mM**. The nominal 3 mM fibroblast medium was about 3.24 times the reported plasma peak and about 13.9 times the AUC-derived average. These are compartment and timing comparisons, with no clinical neural concentration inferred. The FDA approval covers LGMD2I/R9 in patients weighing at least 30 kg. In FORTIFY, ages actually enrolled were 12–60 years. The interim efficacy set included 73 of 112 randomized, with 61 participants contributing observed or multiply imputed walking velocity and 57 for seated FVC. Month-12 differences were +0.27 m/s (99% CI 0.15–0.38) and +4.89 FVC points (99% CI 0.21–9.57). Muscle glycosylated α-dystroglycan changed by an assay-relative +17.16 points versus placebo at month 3. The label specifies assay bridging across different protocols and flags gastrointestinal effects, arthralgia and distinct juvenile-animal findings. This is a muscle target and function benchmark, with no established WWOX CNS or younger-child exposure. [US prescribing information §§6, 8.4, 12.2–12.3 and Table 2](https://welgemda.bridgebio.com/resources/Welgemda-USPI-23-Nov-2026.pdf) · [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05775848) · [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-treatment-limb-girdle-muscular-dystrophy-type-2ir9)
