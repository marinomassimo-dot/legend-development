# Ribitol | Matriglycan substrate and radial glial attachment
context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Opening checkpoint, 27 November 2026.** Ribitol (BBP-418) follows the closed pregabalin analysis. It offers a product-specific comparison for the developmental glycosylation branch already explored in WWOX. Genetic dystroglycan loss at radial glial endfeet disrupts the pial basement membrane, while Wwox models show altered neuronal positioning or radial-glial state. The phenotypes motivate testing an adhesion defect separately from altered progenitor proliferation, migration competence and lineage composition. Similar anatomical features alone do not demonstrate shared pathology. The FDA approved oral ribitol on 23 November 2026 for FKRP-related limb-girdle muscular dystrophy type 2I/R9 in adults and pediatric patients weighing at least 30 kg. The trial's muscle biomarker and functional endpoints establish a useful external positive control. Drug exposure in developing neural compartments and an endogenous dystroglycan defect in WWOX remain unmeasured. ## Fixed first comparison

The substrate test requires a ribitol-to-CDP-ribitol route, phosphoribitol transfer and downstream matriglycan assembly. Compare CRPPA (legacy ISPD), FKTN and FKRP with upstream O-mannosylation (POMT1/2 and POMGNT2), the LARGE1 pathway, the core DAG1 protein and laminin/basement membrane transcripts. Use the same held WWOX bulk models and report gene-level single-library omissions. An independently deposited human WWOX-depleted progenitor CAGE resource gives a stage sensitivity check with one culture library per arm, and is left descriptive. Marker changes will flag lineage composition, and transcript stability will not rule out insufficient donor or altered glycosylation. [Primary reaction mapping](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms11534) · [Ribitol in FKRP mutant muscle](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-05990-z) · [Human precursor CAGE](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE126075)

**Discriminating prediction.** In WWOX and matched control radial-glial preparations with documented laminin-rich endfeet, verify intracellular parent and new labeled CDP-ribitol. If donor limitation contributes, ribitol should first correct an endogenous deficit in ligand-binding matriglycan per surface α-dystroglycan, then improve attachment or migration at fixed radial-glial abundance and cell cycle. A donor rise with persistently low α-dystroglycan function favors acceptor transfer, downstream elongation or membrane delivery as the limiting step. Intact binding with altered cell cycle or neuron positioning weakens the matriglycan hypothesis. Include a separate residual-FKRP positive control and a matched condition that lacks the relevant transfer capacity. Compare a measured glycan product and ligand binding in the same preparation. A rise in total DAG1 or bulk laminin has a different interpretation.

**Exposure and stage.** First compare tissue-resolved parent and CDP-ribitol in the positive-control studies with their muscle outcomes, and locate juvenile neural exposure if available. Keep embryonic migration and any later synaptic or myelin effects in distinct developmental windows. Record formulation and actual age/weight support from the approved label and posted trial data.
