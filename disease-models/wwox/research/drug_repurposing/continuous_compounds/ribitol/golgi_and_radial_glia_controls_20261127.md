# Ribitol | Controls for Golgi delivery and radial glial attachment
context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Decision.** Keep ribitol as a conditional developmental pathway probe and defer its WWOX clinical priority. The next WWOX comparison should establish whether ligand-binding matriglycan is already deficient in untreated radial glia at the relevant stage. A whole-cell donor rise alone is compatible with a Golgi delivery bottleneck. The strongest human organoid comparator links glycan loss to disordered endfeet in a different genetic disease and uses a different compound. [RNA and exposure replay](first_comparison_20261127.md)

## Single discriminating comparison

Compare independent WWOX-deficient and matched control radial-glial preparations at the same maturation stage, with intact endfeet verified before interpreting adhesion. Record cell abundance and cycle state alongside baseline ligand binding per unit of surface α-dystroglycan. In paired samples under ribitol and vehicle, follow labeled parent into cell-total and, where resolvable, Golgi-localized CDP-ribitol, then into phosphoribitol on α-dystroglycan and the laminin-binding product before scoring attachment and migration.

An untreated deficit with sequential correction of donor, product, binding and attachment favors a substrate-responsive branch. More total donor with low Golgi donor favors delivery limitation. A rise in Golgi donor with little new phosphoribitol implicates the acceptor or transfer step. Phosphoribitol accumulation without functional ligand binding directs attention to downstream matriglycan and surface delivery. Preserved binding or generalized changes in ATP, viability or progenitor composition without the predicted product sequence weaken the adhesion hypothesis. In the analysis, keep units at donor, litter or independently differentiated batch level. Use a residual-FKRP or FKTN model to establish assay dynamic range, an upstream acceptor-deficient model as a negative control for the product, and respiratory measures to check the separate STREMI hypothesis.

## Drug-specific controls and disposition

- **P448L mice.** Cataldi et al. measured parent, ribitol-5-P and CDP-ribitol by targeted LC–MS/MS after a month of oral exposure from age four weeks. The cohort sizes were four mice per arm. The sampled tissues were heart and quadriceps. The paper reports at least a fourfold CDP-ribitol increase, with similar starting pools in untreated mutants and healthy controls. Figs. 1, 3 and 5 assess muscle glycosylation or physiology. The labeled parent-to-donor demonstration was in C2C12 myotubes. The endpoints do not include paired brain measurements. [Cataldi et al., Figs. 1–3 and cells/LC–MS methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-05990-z)
- **L276I mice.** The separate experiment with a different FKRP allele reports muscle IIH6 and functional results after prolonged oral exposure. The sampled product panels cover heart, diaphragm and tibialis anterior and do not provide a neural donor-binding pair. This is a different allele from P448L and overlapping investigator provenance. [PLOS ONE, Figs. 2–4 and tissue methods](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0325239)
- **Human FP4 myotubes.** After five days at nominal 50 mM ribitol, CDP-ribitol and ribitol-5-P increased (three independent LC–MS/MS experiments per condition). IIH6 rose in five independent experiments and the laminin overlay improved in the responsive FP4 line. FKRP-null and C318Y myotubes did not rescue IIH6 under the tested conditions. In the FP4 assay, the reported extra IIH6 signal with an NAD⁺ comparator did not come with a detected further increase in total donor. This separates donor abundance from glycan output in another assay. Nominal 50 mM culture exposure is about 54 times the human mean total plasma peak and 232 times the AUC-derived mean in the official label. These ratios compare extracellular culture medium with plasma and cannot estimate brain exposure. [Ortiz-Cordero et al., Figs. 2–5 and Fig. 4 supplement 2](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.65443) · [Replayed exposure comparison](exposure_anchors.tsv)

**Disposition and reopening.** The located controlled ribitol positives with paired donor and functional product concern muscle. The healthy mouse brain estimate uses a coeluting HPLC peak, and the human organoid comparator uses Mn007. The held WWOX material establishes dysregulated cell state and positioning but lacks a verified untreated deficit in ligand binding per surface α-dystroglycan. Reopen when an independently replicated, stage-specific deficit in that readout can be paired with local parent, labeled donor conversion and functional correction. The approved FKRP muscle formulation and adolescent trial provide a safety and feasibility reference for their population, with no supported brain exposure or WWOX clinical estimate. A further RNA comparison or a separate review of the same proxies would leave the decision unchanged. The next informative work is the baseline comparison in a preparation with documented endfeet.
