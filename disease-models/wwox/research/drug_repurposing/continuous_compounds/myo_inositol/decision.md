# Myo-inositol disposition

Park myo-inositol for WWOX pediatric trial priority. Retain the activity-dependent phosphoinositide resynthesis hypothesis for neural models. The evidence supports studying the cellular defect. It does not show that systemic supplementation corrects it.

**A fourth WWOX comparison.** In the GSE126075 day-14 human neural cultures, WWOX RNA fell from 2.54 to 0.52 TPM in the perturbation column. ISYNA1 fell from 180.26 to 68.38, IMPA1 from 13.19 to 2.92, and KCNQ2 from 119.01 to 54.26. CDIPT increased from 0.40 to 1.20; the table lacks SLC5A3 and KCNQ3 rows. One library per condition prevents a variance estimate. WWOX itself increased in the progenitor perturbation column, so the latter cannot verify transfer to that stage. The neural result adds support for reduced endogenous synthesis or recycling RNA. Enzyme activity, myo-inositol flux and accessible PIP2 remain unmeasured. **Time and compartment change the inference.** Dai et al. found that 100 µM of *additional* myo-inositol could accelerate KCNQ2/3 recovery after receptor- or phosphatase-mediated PIP2 depletion in a heterologous system. The substrate-only effect was smaller than the combination with transporter overexpression. Fig. 1C reports recovery half-time, Fig. 1H a time constant, and Fig. 1I about three hours to maximal acceleration with increased transporter. Numerical substrate-only bar heights have not been independently recovered. Manville et al. found that the transporter itself changes channel gating without added extracellular substrate. In a separate in vivo study, focal hippocampal infusates of 55 and 1,000 mM were tested against electrically evoked afterdischarges. The 55 mM contrast was 5 versus 40 minutes after infusion. The higher concentration also had a within-animal preinjection comparison. The acute effects had resolved at 40 minutes. These input concentrations are at least 180 times the highest measured CSF concentration (0.3003 mM) in the PLCB1 case. This is a comparison of nominal infusate with sampled CSF; tissue dilution and free synaptic exposure were not measured. Neither a five-minute local effect nor a fall in electrographic activity establishes that oral substrate restores WWOX neuronal lipid recycling. [Dai, Fig. 1B,C,G–I](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4988571/) · [Manville, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28793216/) · [Gamkrelidze, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31568207/)

**Developmental control.** Reanalysis of Vos et al. Tables S3–S5 gives mean knockout embryonic CSF at day 18.5 of 137.55 µM without maternal supplementation (six embryos) and 266.40 µM with supplementation (six). Unsupplemented wild type averaged 557.46 µM (11 embryos). Thus treated knockout CSF reached only 48% of the untreated wild-type mean at this stage. At day 10.5, untreated knockout already exceeded wild type (165.16 versus 115.26 µM). An absolute concentration or a pooled genotype contrast across ages would be misleading. Maternal exposure occurs at the dam level; the supplement does not identify independent litters for each observation. These are descriptive embryo means, without individual longitudinal sampling or claims of genotype-specific efficacy. InsP5 and InsP6 in brain tissue were surrogate measures; they do not establish normal short-timescale PIP2 dynamics at neuronal membranes. **Pediatric transfer and reopening condition.** In the PLCB1 case, four CSF measurements came from one treated individual, and there was no pretreatment CSF denominator. An independent re-extraction confirmed the control-mean discrepancy as documented below. The separate randomized extremely preterm-infant trial found increased mortality; its population and regimen differ from WWOX. A WWOX trial would require evidence of a lesion and of selective functional rescue at relevant exposure, alongside age-specific safety evidence. An informative preclinical test pairs activity-triggered PIP2 depletion with M-current recovery at matched transporter abundance, osmolarity and firing demand. Recovery of both lipid and current, with preserved inhibitory recruitment, would support substrate limitation. If the same current change occurs without additional lipid synthesis, or global firing is suppressed with impaired inhibitory recruitment, the proposed mechanism loses support. **Independent control arithmetic received, 24 November.** Codex Strategy re-extracted the numeric observations from Vos et al., supplemental Tables S1–S2. Its sample means and standard deviations agree with the SCI_CODEX calculation. The 18 CSF controls give 118.25 ± 38.39 µM, compared with 188.2 ± 38.4 in the published Results. The 24 plasma controls give 27.93 ± 11.59 µM, compared with 31.9 ± 18.9. The treated series has four CSF measurements in one PLCB1 patient and no pretreatment CSF measurement. The descriptive ratio to controls is 2.38 using the tabulated controls and 1.50 using the printed Results control, and neither ratio estimates an individual change.
