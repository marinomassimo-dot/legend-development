# Sobetirome | Developmental differentiation, free exposure and disposition

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · `context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. Outcomes are APC-positive cells per lineage-labeled cell in P14 mouse callosal splenium, occipital cortex and optic nerve, CNP-positive cells in pediatric-derived gray-matter cultures, total parent in mouse whole brain (ng/g) and serum (ng/mL), and thyroid-receptor reporter EC₅₀ (nM). The [WWOX RNA screen](README.md) and [adult repair comparison](parent_repair_checkpoint_20261127.md) preceded this step.

**Decision.** Retain the parent compound as a conditional probe of glial thyroid-responsive maturation. Defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. Developmental lineage effects and adult same-field myelinated-axon recovery justify a functional comparison. The oral exposure data leave parent in the unbound local compartment unidentified. A thyroid feedback effect directly competes with the interpretation of selective glial engagement. [Printable brief](sobetirome-research-brief.pdf)

## Exposure, assay selectivity and thyroid feedback

**Long oral exposure.** Hartley et al. 2020 Figs. 2C–D quantify parent after 12 weeks of chow in male Abcd1-null mice. The parent arm reads about 13.2 ng/mL in serum and 0.265 ng/g in whole brain. The equimolar prodrug comparator reads 5.76 ng/mL and 0.253 ng/g of active parent, respectively. The authors describe similar brain totals and lower serum in the prodrug arm. Equal free exposure was not tested. The reported concentration standard curve is 0.1–100 ng/g in brain. The sample-harvest method describes prompt tissue collection after euthanasia, with no vascular wash or drug-specific vascular correction stated. The administered oral form and regimen resemble those of the adult repair model, but the measurements come from a separate Abcd1-null study. [Primary oral exposure, Fig. 2C–D and STAR Methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chembiol.2020.02.008)

**Vascular and concentration sensitivity.** The plotted parent whole-brain amount is equivalent to about 0.81 nM if one uniformly distributes the total parent across tissue at 1 g/mL. As a sensitivity calculation, subtract `serum_ng_per_mL × effective_vascular_µL_per_g / 1000` from measured ng/g. An illustrative 7.99–10.3 µL/g assumption accounts for roughly 40–51% of the parent-arm brain signal, leaving about 0.129–0.159 ng/g. Those two volumes come from rat plasma-protein and plasma-water spaces, with unmeasured applicability here. About 20.0 µL/g would account algebraically for the whole parent-arm signal. The result is sensitive to an unmeasured sample quantity and to free versus bound and erythrocyte partition. This is a scenario, with no correction or receptor occupancy inferred for the actual mice. Confirm exposure in a comparable preparation by measuring plasma, vascular contribution and free parent in the target region. [Residual-blood primary methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/jcbfm.2009.200) · [Molecular mass](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/9862248)

**Perfused parenteral positive control.** Hartley et al. 2018 treated Mct8/Dio2-null mice from P30 and obtained 20 ± 4 ng/g in perfused hemi-brain 4–6 h after the final parenteral parent exposure (n=6 assigned to the parent arm, plasma 522 ± 65 ng/mL). Whole-brain Hr rose roughly 3.5-fold to the WT baseline, while other markers including Nefh and Sema7a failed to respond. This establishes a direct brain-entry precedent in another genotype with a different exposure. The roughly 61 nM homogeneous total equivalent is neither free concentration nor transferable to the oral repair cohort. The 2017 Supplemental Table 6 download did not yield readable original rows. The quantitative oral inference here uses the separate 2020 source figure. [Perfused comparison, Figs. 1, 2 and 4](https://doi.org/10.1089/thy.2018.0008) · [Earlier oral report](https://doi.org/10.1210/en.2016-1842)

**Receptor and endocrine controls.** In Ferrara et al. 2016 Table 1, matched transactivation assays in transfected HEK293 cells give EC₅₀ 2.8 ± 1.8 nM for TRβ and 74.7 ± 28.9 nM for TRα, or roughly 27-fold preference on the reported means. This supplies a reporter-based selectivity reference with possible action through both isoforms, rather than assigning the WWOX response to β. Neither those nominal reporter concentrations nor the nominal 50 nM OPC transcription assay can set a free in vivo threshold from a whole-brain homogenate. In a separate male C57BL/6 model after 29 days of daily parent exposure, circulating T4 and T3 decreased despite TSH remaining within the euthyroid comparison range, with four mice per exposure group. The Abcd1 model supplies a more direct counterproof to treating serum T4 normalization as proof of CNS mechanism. Restoring T4 on the same oral sobetirome chow improved selected peripheral VLCFA outcomes. It did not improve any of the four compared CNS lipid outcomes. Brain C26/C22 and spinal C26-LPC comparisons shifted against an additional lowering, whereas brain C26-LPC and spinal C26/C22 were indeterminate relative to parent alone. These are lipid outcomes in Abcd1-null mice. Thyroid feedback remains a separate measurement on a WWOX sheath experiment. [Matched reporter assays](https://doi.org/10.1002/cmdc.201600408) · [Chronic feedback, Ferrara Figs. 3–4](https://doi.org/10.1210/en.2018-00065) · [T4 add-back, Hartley Figs. 5D and 6](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chembiol.2020.02.008)

## Discriminating test and feasibility

**Inference.** Sobetirome can modify lineage maturation and adult repair under some conditions. WWOX bulk and cell-class differences cannot establish whether limited thyroid-receptor induction, a neuron-side defect or both constrain first sheaths. First measure the response at a fixed stage and hold baseline T3/T4 constant in vitro. In an in vivo follow-up, record serum T4/T3 and TSH alongside brain free parent and local response markers.

**Predicted separation.** In the held reciprocal neuron/oligodendroglia genotype comparison, verify a baseline deficit of first stable sheaths on viable, initially unmyelinated axons of comparable caliber. Compare measured parent exposure with vehicle while recording early glial KLF9 and HR or a validated local receptor-response pair, with receptor-subtype attribution treated separately. If thyroid-receptor engagement bypasses the neuronal constraint, initiated sheaths should stabilize and absolute patterned conduction should improve even on Wwox-deficient axons. If the local response rises but first stable sheaths and conduction still segregate by neuron genotype, the preparation supports glial engagement with a persistent neuron-side block. Count eligible axons in the same fields and distinguish initial transients from stable ensheathment. Recovery limited to survival, marker expression or a change in the denominator lowers priority for a first-sheath mechanism.

**Pediatric boundary and review.** The two verified X-ALD registry records enrolled zero participants. Pediatric-derived OPCs provide mechanistic evidence without exposure or tolerability data. Human free brain exposure, developmental-stage endocrine effects and interactions with background antiseizure therapy remain undetermined. The earlier independent Tcf4 audit already checked the parent-versus-prodrug and lineage-versus-structure distinction. An additional review would change the decision if it identified actual paired oral free exposure and first-sheath function in a developmentally comparable model. The current evidence supports deferral of a pediatric WWOX trial and leaves the stated test as the reopening condition. [Registry replay and prior comparison](README.md)
