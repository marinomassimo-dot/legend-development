**What changes.** The adult repair evidence reaches a true same-field fraction, so dismissing it as a marker-only result would miss an important positive comparison. Its null thickness result highlights why sheath number, thickness and conduction should have distinct denominators. In WWOX, hold initial ligand and thyroid hormone conditions fixed and confirm stage-matched induction before asking whether first sheaths and patterned conduction recover on Wwox-deficient axons. Keep sobetirome active. Resolve total versus free exposure and the thyroid feedback countercheck before deciding whether the adult repair evidence transfers.
