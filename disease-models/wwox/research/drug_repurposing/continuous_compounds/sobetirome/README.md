# Sobetirome | Thyroid-receptor response versus first-sheath formation
context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Opening checkpoint, 27 November 2026.** Compound 132 follows the recorded zinc acetate closure. Sobetirome is a thyromimetic research compound with an independently checked developmental comparator and a different inference from the recent zinc and glycan analyses. The held Tcf4 heterozygous comparison measured OPC and oligodendrocyte endpoints after parent sobetirome in culture, and after its prodrug Sob-AM2 in vivo. The parent culture concentration differs between Results and Methods. The thyroid-agonist arm did not measure electron-microscopic sheath structure or conduction. Those endpoints from the clemastine arm cannot be transferred. The existing WWOX neuron–oligodendrocyte comparison raises the question of whether thyroid-receptor engagement can overcome a neuron-dependent deficit in first ensheathment. [Held Tcf4 evidence and independent review](../../myelin_phenotype_first_sci50_20261113/README.md) · [Tcf4 primary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37068912/) · [WWOX neuron–glia primary, Fig. 6](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awab174)

## Comparison fixed before new RNA output

Read P17 mouse cortical and hippocampal bulk TPM and week-15 WWOX-KO human organoid processed abundance for `THRA/THRB`, `SLC16A2/SLCO1C1`, `DIO2/DIO3`, `KLF9/HR` and the early versus late myelin readouts `CNP/MOG`. Repeat each bulk contrast with one library omitted. In P17 hippocampus report OPC, COP, NFOL, MFOL and MOL separately in each pooled genotype, with UMIs and nuclei counts. Treat <10 feature UMIs in either pool as low coverage when describing a direction. Stage abundance can dominate a bulk signal. Baseline RNA, even within a class, cannot establish receptor protein, ligand exposure, isoform-specific response, or thyroid-hormone deficiency. KLF9 and HR are candidate inducible responses, with KLF9 alone insufficient to establish late differentiation. [Primary OPC comparison, discovery relay](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22472204/)

**Discriminating prediction.** Verify a deficit in the fraction of caliber-matched viable axons acquiring a first sheath. Hold the baseline hormone content of the culture and axonal opportunity fixed. Compare free parent sobetirome and vehicle in the neuron genotype × oligodendrocyte genotype pairings already motivated by WWOX, and check an inducible thyroid-receptor response within a matched lineage stage. If glial receptor activation can bypass the neuronal constraint, first sheaths per eligible axon and patterned conduction should recover even when the axons are Wwox-deficient. If receptor-response markers and mature-cell counts rise while the first-sheath deficit tracks the neuron genotype, the axonal constraint remains. A marker-only response, a gain explained by altered axon number or a conduction change that precedes sheaths would lower priority for the proposed mechanism. A receptor-specific control is needed to attribute any recovery to THRB and to separate other stage-promoting effects.

**First decision boundary.** Adult remyelination after induced Myrf loss and the Tcf4 developmental comparator answer different questions. Quantify active parent in the relevant compartment and separate the parent from pharmacokinetic evidence on Sob-AM2. The planned human studies will be checked for actual enrollment and posted results before inferring feasibility.
