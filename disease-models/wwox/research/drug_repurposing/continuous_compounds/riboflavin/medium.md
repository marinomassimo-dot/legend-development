# What the untreated WWOX organoids received

GSE156243 specifies WiBR3-derived cerebral organoids sampled at week 15. The primary study (Fig. 4) reports two WT and four KO RNA-seq samples. Its Methods describe equal parts DMEM/F12 and Neurobasal in cerebral differentiation medium. From day 16, maturation medium used the same basal mixture with changed supplements, and was replaced every two to four days. The paper names two possible suppliers. Its Methods and GEO records identify neither the lot used for each library nor a measured extracellular or intracellular flavin concentration. [Study, cerebral organoid generation and Fig. 4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8350905/) · [Data deposition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243)

**Formulation-based calculation.** For the specified Gibco options, the manufacturer's DMEM/F12 formulation lists 0.219 mg/L riboflavin and Neurobasal lists 0.400 mg/L. Before supplements, an equal-volume blend would give (0.219+0.400)/2 = 0.3095 mg/L, or about 0.82 µM using 376 g/mol. This is a nominal fresh base-mixture concentration for one permitted supplier pairing; actual final-medium dilution, supplements, handling, replacement interval, cellular consumption and tissue penetration remain unquantified. The B27 change concerns vitamin A. There is no basis to interpret the KO expression pattern as a response to intentional riboflavin deprivation. The discriminating comparison should measure the starting and spent medium and intracellular FMN/FAD formation in the same batch.
