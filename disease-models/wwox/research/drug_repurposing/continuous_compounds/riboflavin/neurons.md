# What riboflavin alone recovered in a transporter-deficient neuron

**Patient-derived positive control.** Niceforo et al. compared untreated, riboflavin, antioxidant and combined arms in motor neurons derived from two SLC52A2 patient lines. Methods identify the antioxidant as N-acetylcysteine *amide* (A0737). The riboflavin-alone exposure was 10 µM added overnight; the control was untreated. The paper describes culture medium and nominal additions but the free, time-resolved vitamin available to each line was not measured. Fig. 6C reports average neurite length. Methods require the soma and processes to fit in the image and describe at least 100 neurites across three experiments. Cropping can exclude long neurites. The biological evidence comes from two patient lines. Images of treated control cells appear in Fig. 6B, but Fig. 6C supplies only an untreated control mean, so genotype-by-treatment effects on length cannot be calculated from this panel. [Niceforo et al., Methods and Fig. 6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7927654/)

**Original calculation.** Digitizing Fig. 6C on its 0–200 µm axis gives about 145 µm untreated control, 62→89 µm with riboflavin alone in P1 and 36→54 µm in P2. These close about 32% and 17% of their respective untreated patient-to-control gaps. The combined arm reached about 110 and 70 µm. On an absolute-length additive scale the interaction contrast (combined − vitamin − amide + untreated) is approximately −9 and −2 µm. A one-pixel difference is about 1.14 µm; the second contrast is particularly sensitive to graph resolution. Raw replicate covariance is unavailable. The highest combined mean does not establish pharmacological synergy. The analysis measures neurite length among imaged cells; it cannot tell whether the added branches conduct or whether mitochondrial FAD was restored. **Functional endpoints.** Fig. 7B measures a fluorescence response to ionomycin, an ionophore; it does not measure evoked spiking or axonal transmission. Fig. 1D uses MTT in the precursor cells. That viability-associated reduction is insufficient to identify SDHA cofactor occupancy or complex-II activity per enzyme molecule. A companion study reported improved mitochondrial ultrastructure in RTD motor neurons after 1 µM riboflavin *plus* 100 µM of the amide. Its Fig. 2 cannot attribute rescue to riboflavin alone. Concentrations, preparations and endpoints differ between papers; the antioxidant cannot be silently equated to the parent compound reviewed in the WWOX program. [Niceforo et al., Figs. 1, 6–7](https://doi.org/10.1242/dmm.046391) · [Companion study, Methods and Fig. 2](https://doi.org/10.3390/antiox9121252)

**WWOX bridge.** In Wwox-null embryonic fibroblasts, Abu-Remaileh et al., Fig. 2F, report four metabolites in a five-versus-five comparison. The displayed fumarate, malate, succinate and fructose bisphosphate are consistent with altered carbon metabolism but do not measure free or bound flavin. The figure alone cannot establish that the broader profiling service never assayed a flavin. In WWOX, pair a measured conversion rate with a specific flavoprotein activity and stimulus-evoked neural function under replete conditions. Treat vitamin-responsive length or inducible calcium mobilization alone as insufficient for pediatric trial prioritization. [Abu-Remaileh et al., Fig. 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4211377/)

**Specificity of the expression screen.** Across 14,202 shared, unambiguous upper-case gene symbols with positive mean control signal in all three preparations, we chose the 200 nearest neighbors for each of SLC52A2, RFK and FLAD1 by their three control log-abundances standardized within dataset. Among those reference genes 33/200, 26/200 and 27/200, respectively, also fell in all three models and under every one-library omission. The 100- and 500-neighbor sensitivities preserve the finding that this pattern recurs outside flavin biology. Gene-symbol matching approximates cross-species correspondence. Similar neighbor counts do not model correlation within pathways and must not be multiplied into a significance value. What remains distinctive enough to test is the consecutive biochemical role of the three proteins, with direct cofactor formation as the readout. **Disposition.** Park riboflavin for general pediatric WWOX trial prioritization. Retain the uptake-versus-conversion question. Reopen for a deficit in the production of labelled FMN/FAD per available labelled precursor in WWOX neural preparations, coupled to recovered cofactor-dependent enzyme activity and evoked function at measured attainable exposure with a vitamin-replete control. Where a blood nutritional deficiency is documented, it is a separate clinical context. The SLC52A2 and FLAD1 human studies provide positive controls for distinct inherited flavin disorders.
