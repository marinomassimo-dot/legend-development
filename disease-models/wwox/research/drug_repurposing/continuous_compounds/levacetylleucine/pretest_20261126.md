# Levacetylleucine · prespecified transport and metabolic countercheck

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · fixed before the WWOX RNA extraction

**Choice and duplicate check.** Levacetylleucine is the L-enantiomer of N-acetylleucine. In the WWOX portfolio it appears in a cross-database identifier collision and has no dedicated compound analysis. The earlier L-leucine lane found no shared direction for the LAT1 transcript and separated intracellular precursor shortage from reduced leucyl-tRNA capacity. The present question changes the delivery route and adds a potential cost to native monocarboxylate transfer. It retains the L-leucine findings as a comparator. [Prior L-leucine work](../l_leucine/README.md) · [Laboratory alias collision](../../alias_scan_it26_a2_20261104/collisions_it27.tsv)

Transport experiments report use of MCT1, OAT1 and OAT3, compared with LAT1 for free leucine. Cellular conversion can yield acetate and L-leucine. MCT1 has an independent role in oligodendroglial metabolic support. WWOX neural preparations show deficient myelination and altered excitability. This motivates asking whether delivery of leucine or acetate, or a cost to native lactate transport, dominates net recovery with levacetylleucine. A parental drug concentration or donor-cell uptake is insufficient to infer transfer into a receiving axon. **Fixed screen.** Compare SLC16A1, SLC16A3, SLC16A7, BSG, EMB, SLC22A6, SLC22A8, SLC7A5, SLC3A2, ACY1, ACSS1 and ACSS2 in the three standard WWOX bulk models, with one-library omissions, and in classified mouse hippocampal nuclei. Keep detection frequency and absolute nuclear UMI counts alongside CPM. Distinguish MCT1 from MCT2 and MCT4 and distinguish cytosolic and mitochondrial acetate use. An RNA result nominates where to measure transport, protein and flux. [Held input fingerprints](../d_mannose/inputs.tsv)

**Discriminating prediction.** Compare separate tracers labelled on the leucine and acetyl moieties with parent, free leucine and acetate comparators, alongside a short, matched native lactate measurement. Equalize extracellular pH, lactate and electrical drive. A delivery bottleneck predicts additional intracellular labelled leucine and ATP or charging recovery. At equal intracellular leucine enrichment and free pool, the benefit should converge with the free-leucine comparator. An acetate-supported explanation predicts labelled acetyl-derived product and functional recovery which follows the acetyl arm at matched leucine. A physiological transport cost predicts reduced native lactate transfer and poorer axonal recovery despite parent uptake or enhanced leucine signalling. Evaluate a pharmacological or genetic transport perturbation against a no-drug lactate control because altering MCT function changes baseline transport. Use evoked axonal output, recovery and the donor/receiver measurements together. Compare targeted effects before attributing an improvement to a parallel lysosomal mechanism.

**Pediatric feasibility.** The current US oral suspension label lists neurological Niemann–Pick C and ataxia-telangiectasia at body weight at least 15 kg. EU NPC product information covers a distinct age, weight and concomitant-treatment scope. The controlled NPC crossover is a useful precedent for function in a different disease. Food or unmodified amino-acid assumptions are inappropriate for this formulation. Reconsider WWOX ranking only with an attainable measured parent or product exposure and functional selectivity that preserves native transport.
