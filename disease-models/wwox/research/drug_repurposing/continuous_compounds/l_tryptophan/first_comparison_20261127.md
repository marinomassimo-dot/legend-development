# L-tryptophan first comparison

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Checkpoint.** One of eleven prespecified genes shows an increase across all three untreated bulk profiles and all single-library omissions. AADAT warrants a direct product measurement. The comparison gives no consistent transport deficit, serotonergic deficit or direction for net NMDA modulation. [Registered prediction](pretest_20261127.md) · [Bulk data](contrasts.tsv) · [Nuclear denominators](nuclei.tsv)

- **Untreated WWOX.** AADAT, which encodes kynurenine aminotransferase II and also metabolizes other substrates, rises by **+0.256**, **+0.591** and **+0.242** in mean log₂(1 + abundance) in P17 mouse cortex, P17 mouse hippocampus and week-15 human organoids, respectively. The omission ranges are **+0.213 to +0.341**, **+0.445 to +0.723** and **+0.125 to +0.360**. In the assigned hippocampal nuclear pools, raw Aadat UMIs are **7 vs 23**, or **0.38 vs 1.39 CPM** across assigned classes. Astrocytes contribute **0 vs 3** and microglia **0 vs 1**. At this depth neither class establishes the origin of the bulk difference. SLC7A5 changes direction by region while TPH2 has a fragile hippocampal decrease and increases in cortex and organoids. Bulk is descriptive with related mouse samples, shared organoid lines and separate TPM and CPM normalizations. One nuclear pool per genotype cannot supply independent replicates. [Public mouse cortex](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) · [Mouse hippocampus](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) · [Nuclei](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158684) · [Human organoids](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243)
- **Adult peripheral comparison.** In Badawy and Dougherty's 5.15-g L-tryptophan amino-acid formulation group (n = 25), at 3 hours free plasma tryptophan was **15.5 times** its starting group mean (74.5 ÷ 4.80), while kynurenine was **5.64 times** its starting group mean (15.22 ÷ 2.70). Control 2 (n = 12) used the same amounts of the other amino acids and a smaller tryptophan amount. It was an active formulation. The 10.30-g group doubled the mixture and cannot isolate the effect of the parent increase. The group means show different peripheral kinetics for precursor and metabolite. Concentration-derived enzyme proxies cannot verify metabolic flux. The comparison reports neither brain levels nor pediatric tolerability. [Primary Tables 1 and 7](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.4137/IJTR.S38189)
- **Countercheck.** Sekine et al. varied other amino acids in rat cortex slices with **2 µmol/L** kynurenine and measured tissue kynurenine and extracellular kynurenate separately. Leucine's fitted half-inhibition values were **30.4 µmol/L** for tissue kynurenine and **36.9 µmol/L** for kynurenate production. These are preparation-specific estimates. The authors omitted tryptophan from the screen because of kynurenine and kynurenate in the available reagent. The control this suggests is an LC–MS reagent and medium blank plus labeled-precursor mass balance, alongside matched competing amino acids. The paper provides no tryptophan effect size for that assay. [Primary Results, Fig. 1 caption and Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4318830/)
