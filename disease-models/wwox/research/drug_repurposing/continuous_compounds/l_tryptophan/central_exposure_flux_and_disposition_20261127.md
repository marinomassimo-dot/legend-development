# L-tryptophan central exposure, metabolic allocation and disposition

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Disposition.** Defer direct pediatric WWOX trial priority. Preserve a bounded mechanistic comparison of isotope allocation. Human experiments establish that parent tryptophan can reach brain tissue in selected settings. Which route supplies local kynurenine is unresolved in WWOX, as are the ensuing kynurenate and quinolinate exposures and the effect on evoked function. The held AADAT RNA pattern supports measuring products and activity before considering a supplementation claim. | Historical human oral and IV exposure | Young and Gauthier sampled lumbar and cisternal CSF during pneumoencephalography after oral loading and reported an approximately equal maximum rise in the serotonin synthesis proxy at the two loads (just under twofold). Their measured proxy was CSF 5-HIAA. Gillman et al. reported approximately sixfold greater total frontal cortex tryptophan in IV versus saline exposure during adult psychosurgery. Free plasma parent tracked cortical parent more closely than total plasma parent in that small historical comparison. | Different regions, routes, sampling schedules and clinical selections. Cortical tissue total is a composite and 5-HIAA depends on turnover and clearance. These public abstracts support directional delivery and product response. They do not establish the denominator-level time course, a WWOX exposure target or allocation to the kynurenine pathway. A neuron–astrocyte preparation missing microglia cannot exclude that diversion. Agonism in the published preparation gives no WWOX activation threshold. |

**Concentration-scale countercheck.** In Hilmas et al.'s cultured hippocampal neurons, the apparent KYNA IC₅₀ for NMDA-evoked current was **15.4 ± 2.6 µmol/L** without added glycine and **238.9 ± 5.8 µmol/L** with **10 µmol/L** added glycine. The fitted IC₅₀ changes **15.5-fold** within one study. Even the lower value is **about 10,900-fold** higher than the **1.41 nmol/L** adult mid-fraction lumbar mean. This cross-preparation comparison shows why a rise in lumbar kynurenate or AADAT RNA alone cannot establish meaningful direct NMDA block. It does not give the concentration or coagonist occupancy near a WWOX synapse. The appropriate follow-up compares measured local metabolite concentrations and evoked response with glycine, D-serine and magnesium controlled. [Hilmas Results, Figs. 6B and 7B](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/jneuro/21/19/7463.full.pdf) · [Concentration and mixing replay](compartment_replay.tsv)

## A test that can separate transport from local conversion

**Inference.** Central concentration at steady state constrains the sum of incoming and locally produced kynurenine flux divided by removal. It leaves the composition of the sum unresolved. With equal arbitrary removal and equal total input, 80% import and 20% import give the same pool size. If a single labeled precursor gives 50% enrichment to both routes, the central pool is 50% enriched in both cases. The same model with a tracer confined to incoming kynurenine predicts **40% versus 10%** labeled central kynurenine. The complementary local-precursor tracer predicts **10% versus 40%**. These values illustrate identifiability with known enrichment, pool mixing and matched labeling. They do not estimate real flux or dictate a protocol. [Replay](compartment_replay.tsv)

**Prediction and counterproof.** Start with untreated, stage-matched WWOX-deficient and corrected neural preparations that contain the cell types needed for the measured branch. Establish basal free parent, labeled pathway products, local exposure and evoked recovery. Trace incoming kynurenine and locally available tryptophan independently with distinguishable labels, with equivalent total substrates and matched competing amino acids at the barrier. Measure precursor enrichment on each side and labeled 5-HIAA, kynurenate, 3-hydroxykynurenine and quinolinate over time. Include medium and reagent blanks and verify analytical recovery. A substrate-rescue hypothesis predicts recovery of a prespecified deficient product and patterned function after intracellular parent increases, under matched coagonists. A transport limitation predicts weak neuronal labeling despite more free parent in the source compartment. A diversion hypothesis predicts greater labeled kynurenine products without the expected functional recovery. A rise in quinolinate or lipid oxidation, or exaggerated NMDA-dependent recruitment at measured exposure, weighs against broad supplementation. If neither baseline deficiency nor a selective reversible functional change is present, stop the compound lane. With mixed products, require a branch-specific control before attributing any current change to a single metabolite.

**Pediatric feasibility.** Cruz-Sanabria et al. enrolled **26** of **51** screened children under five with neurodevelopmental disorders. **13** completed the combination study, **9** on melatonin plus 5-hydroxytryptophan and **4** on melatonin plus L-tryptophan. The endpoint was actigraphic sleep after at least four weeks. There was no L-tryptophan-only or melatonin-only comparator, and the small L-tryptophan completion denominator cannot establish tolerability or efficacy on WWOX seizures or function. Historical case–control evidence associated eosinophilia–myalgia syndrome with specific manufacturing conditions. Any later feasibility assessment would need a characterized formulation, relevant co-medication review and validated exposure. The observed adult exposure, traceable flux proposal and pediatric combination data leave those prerequisites incomplete. [Pediatric primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41643230/) · [Historical case–control primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2370887/)

**Reopen** with measured WWOX tissue exposure and branch-specific product recovery linked to selective functional benefit at the same exposure. Independent review would change this decision if it brings a comparable source-resolved WWOX dataset or a measured response spanning both function and diversion. The current source and cell-type uncertainty determines the next experiment.
