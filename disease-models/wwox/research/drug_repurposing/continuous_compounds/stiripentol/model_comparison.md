# Can stiripentol act without clobazam, and would that predict a WWOX response?

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**An independent effect is plausible in young mice.** In six four-week-old Scn1a RX/+ mice given stiripentol alone, mean temperature at the onset of a provoked seizure rose from 39.67 to 41.32 °C. Six older mutants given the same nominal monotherapy went from 40.82 to 40.98 °C. Neither age group showed a significant change in seizure duration. The estimated difference between the two pre/post changes is 1.49 °C. Interpreting the reported t statistics as paired tests and the age cohorts as independent gives an approximate conservative 95% interval of 0.06–2.92 °C. This is an exploratory reconstruction from rounded numbers. Both ages had a baseline challenge before treatment. The repeat-challenge control was pooled across ages. There was no age-matched vehicle contrast for the difference of changes. Total plasma stiripentol was measured only in the older monotherapy group. Age, exposure, heat handling and receptor maturation remain alternative explanations. The association with α3 maturation was already proposed by Cao and colleagues. [Cao et al., pp. 1141–43, Tables 2–3](https://mhlw-grants.niph.go.jp/system/files/2012/123081/201224114A/201224114A0013.pdf#page=10) · [Calculation](age_contrast.py) · [Results](age_contrast.tsv)

**An independent laboratory narrows the interpretation.** In P14–16 Scn1a heterozygous mice, monotherapy elevated the heat-seizure threshold at high exposure. At the highest exposure, 3 of 13 animals remained protected during the challenge. The effective exposure exceeded the authors’ human total-plasma reference range. A lower exposure and subchronic monotherapy targeting that range did not demonstrate protection in the corresponding thermal and post-priming spontaneous seizure assays. An isolated response at high exposure supports an intrinsic antiseizure action in some settings. Its relevance to a developing WWOX network depends on free exposure, developmental stage and seizure endpoint. [Hawkins et al., Table 1, Figs. 2C and 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5420810/)

**Native synaptic currents.** In P8 rat CA3, mean miniature inhibitory current decay increased from 14.4 to 17.6 ms at 30 µM nominal bath stiripentol (9 cells), from 13.5 to 25.7 ms at 100 µM (10), and from 13.4 to 31.5 ms at 300 µM (7). Each cell provided its own control. The concentrations were tested in different cell groups. Spike blockade isolates a synaptic effect but precludes measuring postburst firing. The lowest tested concentration is not an effect threshold. [Quilichini et al., Table 1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16650136/) · [Group means and ratios](native_currents.tsv)

**The metabolite control depends on the experiment.** Comparing clobazam + valproate with the same drugs plus stiripentol, total brain exposure to norclobazam increased 3.18-fold in acute wild-type mouse pharmacokinetics (AUC), and mean total brain concentration increased 4.09-fold after 14 days in Scn1a A1783V/WT mice. Parent clobazam changed 12.07-fold and 0.44-fold respectively. The 14-day regimens used different clobazam inputs. In Cao’s two-drug experiment, postchallenge plasma norclobazam instead decreased in the combination groups, to 710 from 1504 ng/mL in young animals and to 526 from 1371 ng/mL in older animals. This single time point and different regimen cannot be pooled with brain AUC, 14-day means or pediatric troughs. Each experiment needs its own parent/metabolite control. [Acute study, Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11496002/) · [14-day study, Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12313240/) · [Cao et al., pp. 1142–43](https://mhlw-grants.niph.go.jp/system/files/2012/123081/201224114A/201224114A0013.pdf#page=9) · [Recalculated ratios](mouse_exposure.tsv)

**Decision and next discriminator.** Retain stiripentol for a preclinical comparison. The clinical combination data and these experiments do not establish a WWOX-selective response or justify prioritizing a pediatric WWOX trial. Test an age-dependent effect with an age-matched vehicle group at each stage and matched free stiripentol in WWOX and isogenic control networks. First ask whether stiripentol alone increases evoked charge and lowers postburst spike probability with native chloride. Then ask whether any residual effect survives matching both free clobazam and norclobazam. An effect at an early stage in both genotypes would support developmental sensitivity. A genotype-by-treatment difference persisting within stage and exposure would support WWOX selectivity. A change confined to the high-exposure heat assay would weaken transfer to spontaneous network activity. The archived data check below addresses feasibility.

**WWOX developmental check.** In the wild-type mouse developmental reference (four bulk brain libraries per age), Gabra3 rises from 28.4 RPKM at P3 to 40.5 at P14 and stays near 40 at P28. Gabra1 rises from 9.4 to 80.9 and then 102.2. The decline in their ratio cannot be interpreted as disappearance of α3. In the WWOX hippocampal bulk comparison the mean within-library log2 ratio, adding one unit to both genes, increases by 0.506 even as both transcripts fall. In cortex it increases by 0.381. In the 15-week organoids GABRA1 is near the detection floor. With a 0.1 CPM offset the leave-one range for the ratio difference crosses zero. Each WWOX expression cohort used for these comparisons lacks a stiripentol arm. [Developmental atlas](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/arrayexpress/studies/E-MTAB-6798) · [Per-library calculation](development_expression.py) · [Stage, genotype and offset sensitivity](developmental_ratios.tsv)

In independently assayed hippocampal nuclei assigned to broad cell classes, the aggregate Gabra3/Gabra1 UMI ratio instead falls from 0.319 to 0.281 in excitatory neurons and from 0.392 to 0.339 in inhibitory neurons. There is one pool per genotype. Nuclear RNA, whole-tissue RNA and the sampled cell populations differ. These comparisons cannot identify a causal composition effect, splice editing, receptor stoichiometry or drug sensitivity. **Disposition.** Park for pediatric WWOX trial priority. Reopen for a response in WWOX compared with corrected cells at the same developmental stage and measured free exposure, with spike suppression at native chloride surviving the companion-drug controls. A lower α3/α1 ratio at later ages or a higher ratio in pooled WWOX tissue is insufficient. Independent review of the same expression means cannot resolve the missing intervention.
