# What changes in the pediatric trial when stiripentol is added?

**Different denominators give different numbers.** The FDA 2018 clinical pharmacology review of STICLO France reports placebo-arm versus stiripentol-arm total clobazam concentrations of 0.20 and 0.31 mg/L, and total norclobazam of 0.95 and 4.32 mg/L. These comparisons give 1.55-fold and 4.55-fold differences. Consequently the metabolite-to-parent **mass ratio of the group concentrations** changes from 4.75 to 13.94 (2.93-fold). The table also gives dose-normalized between-arm ratios of 1.87 and 5.52; the adjacent text describes baseline versus treatment periods in the stiripentol group, giving 2.15 and 3.22 from the respective reported means. The headline “approximately two and five” cannot substitute for all three comparisons. No individual free concentration, individual within-person ratio or CNS concentration is identifiable from these group summaries. The trial tested add-on therapy to clobazam and valproate. [FDA review, printed p. 15, Table 1 and adjacent text](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2018/206709Orig1s000%2C207223Orig1s000ClinPharmR.pdf#page=16) · [Calculation](exposure.py) · [Results](exposure.tsv)

**Direct receptor exposure.** The review reports mean minimum total plasma stiripentol of 10.0 mg/L in STICLO France and 10.2 mg/L in STICLO Italy. At 234.295 g/mol and an assumed unbound fraction of 0.01, the French group mean corresponds to approximately 0.43 µM unbound plasma. The same review used 0.943 µM for a separate modeled drug-interaction calculation. Neither number is measured free brain stiripentol. The α3 receptor potentiation EC50 was 24.6 µM in an expression system; the M314I and benzodiazepine combination tests used 100 µM. A lower free-plasma estimate raises the value of a direct low-exposure functional comparison. It cannot establish a no-effect threshold or a brain-to-plasma partition coefficient. [FDA clinical pharmacology review, steady-state exposure and CYP model footnote](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2018/206709Orig1s000%2C207223Orig1s000ClinPharmR.pdf) · [Primary receptor pharmacology](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2665930/)

**Next comparison.** Start with a drug-free baseline, then hold concurrent free clobazam and norclobazam fixed when comparing stiripentol with vehicle. Separately reproduce the altered companion-drug mixture in the absence of stiripentol. Capture evoked spike probability as well as late current with native chloride. The existing [adult cannabidiol/clobazam analysis](../clobazam/exposure_and_subtypes.md) examines a different interaction. It does not supply the missing paired free concentrations for this pediatric cohort.
