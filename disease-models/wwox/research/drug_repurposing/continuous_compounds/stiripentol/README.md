# Stiripentol, receptor kinetics and the effect of concomitant clobazam

**Selection.** An existing LEGEND assessment found weak support for stiripentol as a probe of direct LDH inhibition, including cell or enzyme measurements with very different substrates, endpoints and concentrations. Repeating that search has little value. Here the question is whether a developmental change in GABA-A receptor composition or current decay can explain a WWOX-specific effect on evoked firing after holding parent clobazam and norclobazam exposure constant. Compare editing-dependent baseline kinetics, direct drug potentiation, and a shift in the benzodiazepine mixture as separate mechanisms. [Existing LDH assessment, §3](../../qualification_it9_p4a_20261030/README.md)

**One proposed interaction already has a counterexperiment.** Fisher tested the α3 M314I substitution at the developmentally edited site and reported retained stiripentol potentiation under the assay conditions (Fig. 9, 100 µM stiripentol, submaximal GABA). This weakens a claim that the edited residue is required for stiripentol action. A single concentration does not exclude a smaller potency shift. A separate kinetic study with a 5 ms GABA pulse found a larger fast component of deactivation in the edited form, 67.7% (7 patches) versus 45.3% in the unedited form (6), a 22.4 percentage-point difference in the group means. Fast time constants were 25.4 and 24.8 ms respectively. That comparison involved GABA alone. Neither study determined α3 editing or the sign of a network response in WWOX. [Stiripentol primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2665930/) · [Editing and kinetics, Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2775542/)

**Untreated WWOX tissue.** KO–control differences in mean log2(1 + expression) for GABRA3 were −0.66 in P17 mouse hippocampus, +0.15 in cortex and +0.64 in human cerebral organoids. Omitting individual libraries preserved the hippocampal and organoid signs. GABRD and the chloride transporter SLC12A5 declined in both mouse regions and increased in organoids; organoid GABRD was low at approximately 1.45 versus 2.16 CPM. The underlying quantities are bulk TPM for mouse and CPM for organoids. They cannot resolve editing at one base, receptor co-assembly, the native chloride gradient, or net spike suppression. **Separating a direct effect from altered co-medication exposure.** For recombinant α3β3γ2L receptors, simultaneous clobazam and stiripentol produced additive potentiation. With 100 µM stiripentol present, the clobazam and norclobazam EC50s from the displayed fits were 544 and 408 nM after normalization to the new baseline; the drug-free fits were 514 and 508 nM. These are nominal bath concentrations and fits to group data. In clinical use stiripentol can also increase exposure to clobazam and its active metabolite. Both activities can coexist. [Receptor data, Figs. 1–2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3656601/) · [Prescribing information, §§7 and 12](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=58304ba8-9779-4658-811e-94ffe08c3f16)

**Discriminating prediction.** At matched free clobazam and norclobazam, matched initial GABA response and preserved physiological chloride, a direct network effect predicts an incremental change in evoked inhibitory charge accompanied by fewer test spikes after a burst. If a parent/metabolite mixture adjusted to the observed on-treatment levels reproduces the effect without stiripentol, the mixture can account for the difference at that exposure. If the current grows but evoked spikes persist or increase, do not interpret potentiation as restored inhibition. Matched WT and WWOX models are needed for any claim of genotype selectivity.

**Pediatric feasibility and checkpoint.** Capsules and oral suspension have a US pediatric precedent for Dravet syndrome with clobazam, from 6 months and 7 kg. Somnolence, appetite and weight effects and blood count changes matter for interpreting pediatric outcomes. Trial priority in WWOX is pending an attainable unbound exposure and a matched functional comparison. Next check measured parent, norclobazam and unbound stiripentol exposure on the same sampling schedule, then compare that range with the direct receptor measurements and evoked network endpoints. [US label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=58304ba8-9779-4658-811e-94ffe08c3f16)

**Exposure checkpoint.** In STICLO France the measured total parent/metabolite mixture differed substantially between arms. The ratio depends on dose normalization and on whether baseline or placebo is used. [Trial table, exposure calculation and remaining test](exposure.md)

**Monotherapy checkpoint.** An age-dependent thermal-threshold signal in a Dravet mouse study makes a direct effect plausible. Independent monotherapy results place an exposure limit on transfer. The next comparison needs age-matched vehicle and exposure in addition to the two benzodiazepine analytes. [Reconstructed age contrast, native currents and animal exposure](model_comparison.md)
