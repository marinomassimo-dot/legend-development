# L-valine | Prespecified comparison

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

The held WWOX RNA comparison reports lower ECHS1 across three bulk preparations. ECHS1 substrate handling is a distinct question from the prior TUDCA comparison of ER and lineage response. L-valine is an essential dietary amino acid. The question is which direction a controlled substrate change would have in the WWOX context. Search the compound and active assignment register before proceeding.

**H1, precursor or carbon supply.** Increasing locally measured valine could relieve neutral amino acid supply or support mitochondrial carbon recovery. At matched leucine, isoleucine and early demand this predicts greater labeled downstream carbon incorporation and a functional improvement with physiological growth and axonal recruitment preserved.

**H2, overflow at the hydratase step.** If WWOX loss reduces functional valine disposal, increasing valine could favor methacrylyl- or acryloyl-derived adducts over productive flux and depress pyruvate oxidation, while severe valine restriction could still impair protein synthesis. The two arms predict different products and different dose directions.

**Prespecified comparisons.** Verify baseline WWOX ECHS1 abundance and RNA cell mixture separately from enzyme activity, and resolve mouse region and stage. Identify an orthogonal ECHS1-specific or HIBCH-specific positive control, with actual sample units, exposure and valine-derived products. Check whether human clinical signals use targeted valine restriction or broader diet changes, and whether the drug-like perturbation actually altered serum parent or local neural exposure. Recover synthesis and redox/energy readouts separately rather than infer benefit from fewer abnormal metabolites. Include a paired free-valine and branched-chain competition check, and state if exposure data only support systemic parent.

**Decision.** A change in clinical research priority requires a demonstrated native bottleneck and a selective functional response at a measured parent concentration without a deficit in essential amino acid incorporation. No treatment or dietary regimen is proposed.
