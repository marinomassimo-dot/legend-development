# Etifoxine in WWOX loss of function

28 November 2026 UTC · SCI_CODEX · INV-10 · Question-driven comparison. [Opening checkpoint](pretest.md) · [Printable brief](../../view/research_reports/etifoxine.pdf).

**Decision.** Retain etifoxine as a conditional laboratory probe for distinguishing endogenous neurosteroid production from direct GABA-A modulation. Defer systemic pediatric WWOX development. The human parent exposure estimate sits far below the concentrations in the assays that establish the two actions. The native expression replay prioritizes measurements of flux and inhibitory output and does not establish that either action is transferable.

## Inference and discriminating prediction

Ganaxolone provides a direct downstream comparator for GABA-A modulation. The earlier [pregnenolone comparison](../pregnenolone/README.md) addresses provision and conversion of an intermediate, while the [ganaxolone steroid-flux audit](../../ganaxolone_steroid_flux_20261123/README.md) treats the chemically separate WWOX-linked C19 reaction. The etifoxine hypothesis concerns C21 conversion to unbound, unconjugated allopregnanolone in a defined neural preparation. C19 conversion, circulating Allo-S and unconjugated neural C21 steroids carry distinct information.

**Hypothesis.** A subset of WWOX hippocampal cells may have a low basal flux to one or more GABA-active C21 products. Alternatively, the same circuit phenotype could result from the receptor population or chloride state, with etifoxine acting directly only at higher exposure. A reduction in bursts alongside lost inhibitory recruitment or increased later rebound would weaken both therapeutic interpretations.

**One comparison.** In developmentally matched WWOX-deficient and isogenic control hippocampal preparations, pair measurement of local free parent and any active metabolite with newly labeled unconjugated steroid products and rapid versus delayed GABA-A responses. Compare label entering from cholesterol versus pregnenolone, then test whether interrupting downstream conversion removes the delayed response and whether defined exogenous allopregnanolone restores it. Measure GABA reversal relative to spike threshold, first inhibitory recruitment and late spontaneous recurrence in the same preparation.

- **A limiting steroid step** predicts a basal WWOX-specific product deficit and concordant restoration of labeled product and delayed function at a plausible free exposure. Restoring downstream product should bypass the conversion block. A TSPO-specific claim additionally requires loss of the delayed response after an independent TSPO perturbation with matched free exposure, mitochondrial function and viability. Rescue from pregnenolone but not cholesterol would locate a bottleneck upstream of pregnenolone in that preparation.
- **Direct GABA-A modulation** predicts an immediate current response that survives downstream conversion interruption, with unchanged labeled steroid flux. Association with a defined receptor assembly strengthens the interpretation. Equivalent effects in both genotypes would establish a pharmacological comparison while leaving WWOX-specific rescue unsupported.
- **Ambiguous or adverse result** includes flux or current change only at high free exposure, current alone without preserved first recruitment, or reduced initial activity followed by rebound. Maintain the secondary-probe decision in each case.

## Pediatric feasibility and review trigger

The official [French product information](https://m.base-donnees-publique.medicaments.gouv.fr/rcp-63136857-8) describes etifoxine hydrochloride for anxiety-related indications. The [EMA referral](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/referrals/etifoxine-containing-medicinal-products) documents rare serious skin and hepatic reactions. The product information also flags additive central depression with sedating agents. The adult epilepsy report cannot establish pediatric exposure, interactions with WWOX polytherapy, or rare harms. This scan found no qualifying pediatric WWOX exposure, efficacy or age-matched free neural concentrations.

Further aggregate reanalysis or review of the same transcript tables would leave the decision unchanged. Reopen for measured juvenile free parent and relevant metabolite exposure linked to steroid flux or receptor response, with preserved inhibitory recruitment and spontaneous circuit function. At that point, request a pharmacology review of sample times, matrix effects, assay range, and the causal comparison.

## Reproduce

From the repository root, with the held GEO matrices listed in the laboratory data manifests. Set the input directory to the canonical lab data root when running in a separate checkout.

```bash
drug=disease-models/wwox/research/drug_repurposing
dossier="$drug/continuous_compounds/etifoxine"
output=$(mktemp -d)
python3 -B "$dossier/replay_expression.py" "$drug" /home/desktop/legend-development/files "$output"
python3 -B "$dossier/replay_comparisons.py" > "$output/comparisons.tsv"
for table in native_bulk mixture cell_types comparisons; do
  cmp "$output/$table.tsv" "$dossier/$table.tsv"
done
```

The saved tables contain derived values and source-level aggregates. The full-text ledger records the actual portions read for indexed papers. No publisher files, per-person clinical records, or third-party figures are redistributed.
