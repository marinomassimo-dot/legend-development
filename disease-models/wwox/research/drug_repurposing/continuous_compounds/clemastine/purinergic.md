# A conditional injury signal for clemastine during myelination

Kocot et al. provide a way to distinguish failed sheath initiation from injury under inflammatory conditions. In human oligodendroglial cultures the narrow endpoint is extracellular LDH, an indicator of membrane damage. The monocytic cell line adds gasdermin and caspase evidence at its own exposures. Transferring that molecular attribution to oligodendrocytes requires additional evidence. [Primary study, methods and Figure 4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12077908/) · [Supporting data values, Kocot et al., CC BY 4.0](https://s3.amazonaws.com/pmc-oa-opendata/PMC12077908.1/jci-135-183941-s134.xlsx)

**Reanalysis.** Supplemental sheets labelled Figure 3F and Figure 3G match final Figure 4A/B by culture identity, number of replicates and treatment columns. Calculate the difference of differences in percentage points of LDH release as (combined − ATP) − (clemastine − control). In the oligodendrocyte-enriched culture from **one donor**, the excess is +29 points. In the commercial cell preparation the two independent experimental runs give +24.75 and +23.75 points. Adding a P2X7 antagonist to the combination lowers LDH from 45.5 to 11.5, and from 60 to 26.25, respectively. Technical wells contribute within-run means. The data support an exposure-dependent interaction in these preparations. They provide no estimate of between-child variability or of the concentrations at which the interaction starts. **Exposure matters.** The cells received nominal 10 µg/mL clemastine fumarate alongside 2 mM added ATP, following inflammatory priming. With the US product's rounded salt-to-base equivalence of 0.67:0.50, the nominal clemastine base concentration is about 7,460 ng/mL. In a separate Dutch crossover study of 37 healthy adult men who completed both periods, mean total plasma peak after a single 1 mg tablet was 0.895 or 0.854 ng/mL, depending on formulation. The nominal assay to plasma ratios are about 8,340 and 8,740. Neither measured free CNS drug nor local extracellular ATP in WWOX is available from these comparisons. This ratio cannot establish a clinical safety margin or an absence of effects at lower exposures. [Dutch assessment, study design and Table 1](https://db.cbg-meb.nl/pars/h106795.pdf) · [US product information, composition, pediatric use and CNS depressants](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5c99e7af-86a5-4c17-82d5-2d4119e4fe72)

**Clinical context.** In TRAP-MS, 16 adults entered the clemastine arm and nine reached a scheduled follow-up. Three met the excessive-progression stopping criterion. The 63 comparators were patient-specific treatments in other arms, not 63 independent randomized controls. That safety signal matters for the adult population studied. It cannot provide a pediatric WWOX event rate. The US syrup label covers allergic symptoms at ages 6–12. Sedation and additive CNS depression with sedating background medicines can confound a functional endpoint, particularly one based on alertness.

**Decision for WWOX.** A developmental model should distinguish fewer first sheaths on surviving axons with viable glia from a loss of viable glia that tracks extracellular ATP at the same measured free drug exposure. Compare conduction at matched recruitment and initial axonal caliber. A shift in maturation markers with unchanged absolute conduction and first-sheath fraction would lower priority regardless of LDH. Trial prioritization remains unsupported until a WWOX-specific functional benefit is demonstrated at an attainable exposure. Current evidence gives a concrete alternative explanation for harm and a reason to measure it alongside function.

**Untreated WWOX comparison.** Juvenile mouse hippocampus, GSE158684, one pooled single-nucleus library per genotype. In 167 control and 171 knockout marker-assigned microglial nuclei, P2rx7 rose from 181 to 252 counts per million (52 to 73 molecules). Gsdmd remained at 28 in both pools (eight molecules each) and Casp1 changed from 21 to 14 (six to four). Oligodendrocyte precursors had similar P2rx7 abundance (32 versus 34), while newly formed oligodendrocytes fell from 35 to 3 (15 versus one molecule). Downstream counts in glial subclasses are generally too sparse to resolve a response. No class met the predeclared criterion of a coordinated increase of all three transcripts. This leaves an ATP-dependent injury mechanism plausible under stress, with neither direct evidence of execution nor evidence of its absence in untreated WWOX cells. Nuclei are subsamples.
