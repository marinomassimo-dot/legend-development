# Clemastine: when more mature oligodendrocytes coexist with slower conduction

**Question.** Could the WWOX lesion limit axon–oligodendrocyte engagement or the formation of a first sheath even when a drug increases oligodendrocyte maturation? The laboratory has already examined M1 versus EBP attribution, developmental adverse results, and rescue of selected endpoints in Tcf4-deficient mice. The incremental comparison here asks whether two apparently conflicting functional measures actually estimate the same quantity. Preserve the background neuronal lesion and separate cell maturation, the fraction of axons engaged, compaction of existing sheaths, recruitment amplitude and conduction velocity. [Prior cross-model evidence](../../myelin_phenotype_first_sci50_20261113/output/evidence.tsv) · [Prior receptor and sterol comparisons](../../m1_ebp_reagents_sci51_20261113/output/answer.md)

**First calculation.** In developing mice with intact Wwox, Palma et al. found slower myelinated callosal conduction despite increased oligodendrocyte differentiation. The earlier WWOX figure audit read the N1 velocities as 0.507 versus 0.428 in axis units; their ratio is 0.84, about 16% slower. At equal separation the reciprocal implies 18% longer conduction time for this component. Keeping the ratio dimensionless avoids inferring absolute latency from a schematic.

At 2 mA the normalized N1 amplitude in Fig. 2C measures about 47% in controls and 66% under clemastine. Suppose those values are fractions of their respective maximum responses, as suggested by the axis and their common 100% endpoint. Let k be the treated-to-control ratio of **absolute** maximum N1 amplitude. The absolute response ratio at 2 mA is then about 1.40 × k. The treated response exceeds control only if k exceeds about 0.71. For example, with k=0.60, absolute recruitment is about 0.84 times control despite the larger normalized fraction. This conditional calculation does not establish a change in maximum amplitude. Raw amplitudes and the exact sample-level normalization would resolve the ambiguity. If curves were normalized per animal, the product of means need not equal the mean of products. Panel B includes 10–12 treated and 12 control mice; panel C reports 7–8 per group. These readouts cannot establish that the same animal conducts more slowly while recruiting more axons. **Discriminating prediction.** If the primary WWOX constraint is failure to engage axons, clemastine could increase mature-cell markers or compact existing sheaths while the fraction of *all* eligible axons with a first sheath stays low. An actual rescue should increase that fraction within matched axon-caliber bins and improve latency versus distance or absolute early response at controlled recruitment. Compare both against firing-matched WWOX and controls. Next reconcile the original WWOX and Tcf4 readouts by axonal denominator and neuron-versus-glial genotype, with a specific comparison between the developmental and injury windows. A response confined to normalized amplitudes or already-ensheathed axons cannot by itself justify a pediatric trial.

**Competing mechanism to examine in parallel with the axonal denominator.** The primary [TRAP-MS study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40371642/) is a lead for an exposure-by-extracellular-ATP interaction through P2X7, with possible cell-type-specific injury. Treat its [preprint](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39802756/) as the same investigation. Before quantitative extraction, distinguish an ATP-dependent cytotoxic effect from deficient sheath initiation. Establish actual clemastine and ATP concentrations, compartment, response with either ligand alone, target-selective control, and which readout directly demonstrates pyroptosis. Then ask whether any untreated WWOX sample has relevant cell types and signaling evidence. P2RX7 RNA alone cannot establish extracellular ATP, pore opening or a clinical parallel with progressive MS.

**Prospective interpretation of the hippocampal nuclear data.** For the injury alternative, check P2rx7 in immune and oligodendroglial classes with Gsdmd and Casp1 as independent downstream components. A sustained increase of all three within a lineage would strengthen the rationale for probing susceptibility. Scarce reads or discordant directions leave that question open. A single cell pool per genotype is descriptive. These observations cannot establish the concentration of extracellular ATP. The separate [reanalysis of the drug–ATP interaction](purinergic.md) preserves exposure and replicate units.

**Follow-up on native Wwox propagation.** At the published Fig. 5D geometry the knockout N1 peak is about 0.74 ms later. A fixed latency offset and slower travel both fit that geometry. The next test compares distance dependence with absolute recruitment held comparable and structural denominators measured in the same tract.
