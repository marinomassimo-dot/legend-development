# Brivaracetam and loss of inhibitory restraint in WWOX-deficient cortex

**First result.** A preferential effect on late excitatory responses during a stimulus train could coexist with substantial depression of inhibitory input. What magnitude of excitatory selectivity would be needed to bring a simple spontaneous-input measure in Wwox-deletion cortex back to its control value? We estimated this from the published untreated waveforms before attributing any effect to brivaracetam. [Prospective drug question](prediction.md) · [Breton et al., Fig. 4E/G](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8609180/)

## From a detected event to an input index

We integrated the absolute direction of the mean inward current traces over the approximately 24 ms displayed in the original high-resolution figure and multiplied each area by its published group event frequency. The peak and decay both enter the index. The displayed mean and SEM curves touch or overlap. We estimated the center of the visible color band at each time and repeated the integral along its upper and lower edges. Where one color is entirely obscured, the visible band bounds its location under the overlap assumption. Black control pixels were completely covered in 69/234 excitatory and 24/230 inhibitory sampled columns. [Image analysis](wave_charge.py) · [Areas](wave_areas.tsv) · [Every digitized point](wave_points.tsv)

| Input to layer II/III pyramidal neurons | Control event area (pA·ms) | KO event area (pA·ms) | Control / KO event rates (Hz) | Control / KO estimated mean current magnitude (pA) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Excitatory | 82 | 87 | 9.9 / 10.1 | 0.81 / 0.88 |
| Inhibitory | 272 | 131 | 10.3 / 8.4 | 2.80 / 1.10 |

The resulting excitatory-to-inhibitory index was 2.74 times as large in the knockout as in pooled heterozygous and wild-type controls. Aligning the visually favorable and unfavorable envelope limits gives 1.46–5.16. Repeating the calculation with reported mean amplitude times frequency gives 2.76. These are descriptive readbacks of the same experiment. The bounds are **not** confidence intervals. The IN and EX recordings used different solutions, and inhibitory currents were measured with imposed high intracellular chloride. Distinct cells contributed to each group, event detection had an amplitude threshold, and the product of means can differ from the mean of per-cell products. The index cannot establish native electrical balance, synaptic conductance, seizure probability or a drug concentration. [Study methods and recorded responses](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nbd.2021.105529)

## A counterexample to selective benefit

Let the knockout index be R times the control index and let the drug reduce detected excitatory and inhibitory input by fractions e and i. The treated index would be R(1−e)/(1−i). Matching the control index would require e = 1−(1−i)/R. Equal fractional reductions preserve the increased ratio even if overall activity falls. For the central R estimate, the required excitatory reduction is 63.5% if inhibition is spared, 67.2% if inhibitory input falls by 10%, and 72.7% if it falls by 25%. In the last scenario the graphical envelope yields 48.6–85.5%. These are conditional scenarios. A seizure could improve without returning this index to control. [Scenario calculations](thresholds.tsv)

**Discriminating measurement.** Measure the first and cumulative absolute excitatory and inhibitory charges, at matched synaptic activity, on the same recorded cell where possible. Measure network bursting independently. A smaller late excitatory response after normalization to the first response cannot identify the sign of a change in absolute inhibitory recruitment. Short, repeated Wwox-like patterns and long trains should be reported separately. An endpoint recovering under a single strong artificial train has limited bearing on brief spontaneous events. A separate native-chloride or firing measurement is needed for inhibitory direction.

**Next decision.** Compare these conditional requirements with *direct* brivaracetam drug experiments, specifying whether they measured excitatory transmission, GABAergic transmission, a compound field response or vesicle recycling. Seek an inhibitory comparator under the same drug before projecting any fraction onto this index. The direct train/recycling paper and comparator paper are still source leads in the prediction record. This WWOX experiment did not administer brivaracetam. For eventual pediatric feasibility, the European product information specifies adjunctive therapy for partial-onset seizures from two years of age and includes an oral solution. The relevance to age at onset, seizure type, background treatment and WWOX etiology must be evaluated separately. [EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/briviact-epar-product-information_en.pdf)
