## WWOX context

The [replay table](wwox_s1p_context.tsv) gives SPHK2 WWOX-minus-control mean-expression contrasts of **−0.483** in mouse neocortex (4 controls, 3 KO), **−1.094** in mouse hippocampus (2 controls, 3 KO) and **−0.265** in human organoids (2 parental and 4 KO libraries). The sign stays negative under all single-library omissions. S1PR1 falls in both mouse regions and rises in the organoids. S1PR5 falls in mouse and changes little in organoids. The human cultures compare a single KO clone with its parent. Bulk composition, enzyme activity and local drug conversion can differ independently of gene-level RNA. The tissue and organoid panels use different abundance units with a one-unit offset before each library-level log₂ transform. Both mouse panels compare Synapsin-Cre conditional neuronal deletion with the corresponding Cre-positive controls. [Cortex GSE158682](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) · [Hippocampus GSE158681](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) · [Mouse model primary](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awab174) · [Human GSE156243](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243)

The [hippocampal nuclei replay](hippocampal_nuclei_s1p.tsv) contains one pooled Synapsin-Cre library per genotype. Among signal-assigned nuclei, pooled SPHK2 fell from 7.43 to 5.67 CPM. Applying the existing [symmetric class-weight calculation](../creatine/README.md) to these CPM gives a −1.52 CPM within-class term and a −0.24 CPM class-weight term, by averaging each term across the two reference arms. The excitatory class carries the deepest SPHK2 counts (85 versus 59 UMIs, 6.87 versus 5.70 CPM). Every glial class contributes 11 or fewer SPHK2 UMIs per pool. OPC and mature-oligodendrocyte contrasts are individually sparse, and myelin-forming oligodendrocytes show the opposite CPM direction for SPHK2 despite fewer assigned nuclei. For S1PR5, pooled CPM is 2.39 versus 1.33, with −0.72 within-class and −0.33 class-weight CPM terms. This makes an explanation based only on measured class weights less compelling for these two libraries. Unit weights reflect captured UMIs and no independent animal-level test or universal glial deficit is established. ## Inference, hypothesis and discriminating comparison

The positive slice study bypassed local phosphorylation. The in vivo parent study detects a CNS change in S1PR1 and fails to establish a corresponding gain in structural repair. Bulk WWOX SPHK2 gives a reason to test local activation capacity, with cell composition and downstream receptor state as alternatives. [Biochemical activation primary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/13129923/) · [SPHK2 disruption primary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16093248/)

Compare parent, active phosphate and vehicle within the same stage-matched WWOX and control preparations without circulating lymphocytes. Measure locally formed unbound phosphate under parent, match the measured free phosphate under metabolite add-back, and read early receptor function and later surface availability alongside new axon-linked sheaths and evoked conduction. Lower local phosphate with parent followed by rescue at matched phosphate would support a local activation deficit. A persistent WWOX response deficit at equal measured phosphate favors downstream cell or receptor state. Rescue restricted to an injury control would keep the result in the repair context. Report axonal excitability and viability in the same preparations. In vivo circulating phosphate and immune effects require separate attribution.

**Active checkpoint.** Preserve the developmental experiment as a conditional research comparison. The current evidence leaves pediatric WWOX priority deferred. Next check the timing and receptor attribution in pre-injury protection versus post-injury recovery, prioritizing the young Krabbe model and the matched cuprizone comparisons. The juvenile WWOX nuclei limit a simple class-weight explanation but leave biological replication and cell-specific phosphorylation open. PXD012676 is a traceable assay control for the injury-model S1PR1 result. Its pooled proteome cannot measure either WWOX-local drug conversion or first-sheath function. The source extractions are partial. Independent review becomes decision-relevant if biological replicates change the within-lineage direction or a first-sheath comparator resolves the context dependency.
