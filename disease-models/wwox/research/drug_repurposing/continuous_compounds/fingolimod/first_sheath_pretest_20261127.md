# Fingolimod | Does central S1P modulation improve first-sheath formation?

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN` · SCI_CODEX · 27 November 2026.

**Why this compound.** The WWOX programme repeatedly tests formation of functional myelin, and the held S1P receptor qualification left the branch inconclusive. Fingolimod is already catalogued without a compound-specific decision. Primary studies report a slice repair signal after a focal injury and mixed or null results in independent remyelination models. This provides a concrete direct-versus-context contrast, with controlled pediatric exposure in a separate disorder. Formation of the first sheath in WWOX is a different developmental endpoint. [Held qualification G10](../../qualification_it21_p3b_20261103/README.md) · [Existing compound catalogue audit](../../partial_benefit_it6_p10_20261029/README.md) · [Slice study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20413685/) · [In vivo counterexample](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31739156/) · [Pediatric comparator study](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1800149)

**Registered prediction.** At verified free active phosphate and matched neuronal maturity, a direct, developmentally useful S1P receptor effect should increase new axon-linked sheaths and improve conduction in a WWOX-deficient preparation without circulating lymphocytes. Initial activity and viability should be preserved. A gain confined to previously injured tissue, to marker intensity or survival, or to an astrocyte-dependent context would qualify or lower the first-sheath premise. Distinguish receptor activation at an early time from surface loss under longer exposure.

**First comparison.** Compare the untreated and injury-recovery arms in the organotypic source with a nonimmune counterexample, retaining timeline, parent versus phosphorylated species, receptor attribution, biological denominator, structural endpoint and functional endpoint. Check public juvenile or human WWOX S1PR coverage and regulatory pediatric exposure before translating. If parent-to-phosphate or endpoint comparability fails, keep those bounds explicit. Use cell-type attribution and the previously registered WWOX first-sheath/conduction comparator. No clinical advice or intervention protocol.

**Status.** Active after bexicaserin closure. The registered comparison fixes the outcome and the context controls before quantitative extraction.
