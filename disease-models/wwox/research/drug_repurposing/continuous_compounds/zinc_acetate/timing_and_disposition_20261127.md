# Zinc acetate dihydrate | Circuit timing and pediatric disposition

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · `context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. Mouse measurements are unitary NMDA charge in pC from somatosensory cortical L2/3, phasic GABA-A currents from auditory cortical L2/3, tonic zinc in dorsal cochlear nucleus slices, and GABA-A currents in adult hippocampal preparations. Human outcomes are caregiver-reported seizure categories in children with idiopathic generalized intractable epilepsy and monitored feasibility in a separate GNAO1 population. The [WWOX RNA panels](README.md) and [exposure checkpoint](receptor_exposure_checkpoint_20261127.md) preceded these comparisons.

**Disposition.** Retain zinc acetate as a conditional circuit probe. Defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. Sustained bath zinc can diminish the first excitatory input to SST interneurons at an exposure that suppresses later NMDA charge. The result changes the discriminator from “less late NMDA” to “less late recurrence while both recruitment and inhibitory output of SST neurons are preserved.” The sign of the net circuit effect in WWOX and oral delivery to the relevant labile zinc compartment remain unresolved. ## Clinical comparison

Saad et al. randomized 45 children aged 3–12 years with idiopathic generalized intractable epilepsy to zinc sulfate syrup or placebo for six months. The primary analysis used caregiver questionnaires. Methods and Table III define ≥50% response by combining partial response and freedom from seizures. The report's prose attaches 31% to five partial responders. Table III gives five partial plus two seizure-free with zinc and one partial with placebo. The replay checks the printed counts and uses two-sided Fisher exact tests on the reconstructed binary tables. It does not reproduce the center-stratified test printed in Table III. [Saad primary and Table III](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4610753/#tIII-181-185)

The study did not measure serum zinc before or after treatment. Its printed zinc sulfate heptahydrate mass and claimed elemental-zinc equivalent are inconsistent under stoichiometry, so this audit does not reconstruct the concentration or dose. The article reports mild transient adverse events without arm-specific counts in the consulted Results. Different formulation, clinical population and endpoints prevent pooling with zinc acetate in GNAO1. The same-formulation GNAO1 pilot supports feasibility under monitoring, with no clear change in seizures. The US maintenance label for Wilson disease describes observations from age ten and includes a warning about acquired copper deficiency. These observations do not establish the relation between serum and central labile zinc, a benefit–risk estimate in WWOX or interactions with background antiseizure treatment. ## Reopening comparison

**Data.** WWOX bulk tissue and pooled neuronal nuclei disagree on the direction of SLC30A3. Source studies separate input, timing and postsynaptic cell type. [WWOX inputs and RNA replay](README.md)

**Inference.** A sustained increment can erase input specificity and reduce SST recruitment despite slowing recurrent excitation. Direct SST-to-pyramidal transmission, tonic GABA-A conductance and native chloride can move differently. A test based on late NMDA charge alone could favor the wrong condition.

**Hypothesis and discriminant.** In age-matched Wwox-deficient and control superficial cortex, compare zinc acetate with acetate-matched vehicle in an input that shows WWOX recurrence. Prequalify the tonic and evoked labile-zinc measures using a calibrated fast probe and test the effects of zinc chelation during baseline and trains, with a target-specific GluN2A zinc-site control if available. At measured local free zinc, retain early pyramidal drive and measure late NMDA charge and actual recurrence. In the same preparation, distinguish pyramidal-to-SST recruitment from directly recruited SST inhibitory output at native chloride, with PV input as a cell-class comparison. Separate tonic GABA-A conductance from the phasic SST current. A selective WWOX reduction in recurrence with early recruitment and native inhibition preserved would raise mechanistic priority. A loss of SST recruitment or inhibition, or a fall in isolated NMDA charge with unchanged recurrence, would lower it. A response in both genotypes at matched exposure would support general circuit pharmacology. Clinical prioritization would also require independent evidence of an attainable central exposure and relevant pediatric tolerability.

**Review and next step.** A new re-reading would not supply the missing WWOX circuit response. Decision-changing independent review would require either per-pair data that reverse the SST finding, or a WT/WWOX preparation that establishes both free-zinc timing and inhibitory output. Preserve those reopening conditions and move the continuous queue to the next compound.
