# Zinc acetate dihydrate and activity-dependent synaptic inhibition
context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Opening checkpoint, 2026-11-27.** Compound 131 follows closure of L-fucose. The existing PROTON screen names zinc acetate as a discovery-only graph hit. The prior levetiracetam analysis separately found more Slc30a3 RNA in pooled juvenile Wwox-null hippocampal nuclei and considered endogenous zinc as a modifier of GABA signaling. Neither analysis tested added zinc acetate. Here the question is whether the salt raises *extracellular labile zinc* in a way that changes the balance of NMDA recruitment and native inhibition. [PROTON screen](../../proton_it18_20261101/README.md) · [Prior WWOX nuclei and circuit evidence](../levetiracetam/README.md) · [Prior endogenous-zinc comparison](../levetiracetam/zinc_and_exposure.md)

## Fixed comparison before examining the new RNA output

Compare zinc loading and export (SLC30A3, SLC30A1, SLC39A1), receptor availability (GRIN2A, GRIN2B, GABRG2, GABRD) and the inhibitory reserve branch (GPR39, SLC12A5) in juvenile Wwox-null mouse hippocampus and cortex and WWOX-KO human organoids. Compare excitatory and inhibitory nuclear classes in the juvenile hippocampal pools separately. Report omitting each bulk library once and showing nuclear counts for descriptive resolution. Concordant bulk direction after omissions and the same direction in the relevant nuclei strengthen an anatomical lead. Discordance limits pooling across preparations and does not invalidate direct physiology. Expression cannot determine synaptic zinc, receptor stoichiometry or chloride driving force.

**Discriminating prediction.** In a juvenile WWOX circuit with a measurable late NMDA component, a calibrated free-zinc increment could lower late NMDA charge and recurrence while preserving first recruitment and native inhibitory recruitment. If tonic zinc already occupies the relevant receptors, a zinc scavenger could reveal endogenous restraint while added zinc produces little additional NMDA effect. If the inhibitory compartment is more sensitive, the increment weakens native inhibitory recruitment or increases recurrence even if isolated NMDA charge falls. Compare zinc acetate and acetate-matched vehicle in age- and input-matched knockout and control slices. Pair extracellular zinc calibration with early release, late NMDA charge, inhibitory recruitment at native chloride, and recurrent activity in the same circuit. Extracellular chelation after confirming its baseline effect supplies a countercheck on zinc dependence. A receptor-selective comparison would still be needed to attribute the result to GluN2A.

**Translation question.** U.S. labeling for this dihydrate describes maintenance treatment of Wilson disease after initial chelation, limited observations from age ten, and copper deficiency after prolonged use. Intestinal copper handling, circulating total zinc and extracellular labile zinc describe separate measurements. Verify a compartment-specific bridge before drawing conclusions about pediatric WWOX feasibility.
