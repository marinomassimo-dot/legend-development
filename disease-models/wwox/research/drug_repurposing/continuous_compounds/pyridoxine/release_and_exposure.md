# Pyridoxine disposition after testing the circuit and exposure comparisons

**Decision.** Park pyridoxine for WWOX-specific pediatric trial priority. It remains a mechanistic probe for failure of cofactor-dependent transmitter replenishment during activity. Existing WWOX bulk and nuclear profiles point in different directions across preparations, and no WWOX-specific drug-by-genotype functional response is available in this assessment. [WWOX data](gaba.md)

**What is released under activity?** Guilarte reported reduced potassium-evoked GABA efflux in cortical and hippocampal slices from B6-restricted 14-day-old rats. Evoked cortical glutamate release also fell. Dietary restriction affected several transmitters and the abstract gives no quantitative size for the absolute GABA effect. There was no WWOX genotype, no pyridoxine rescue contrast in that report, and potassium depolarization is different from a physiological train. Measure excitatory output alongside inhibitory output in any WWOX comparison. [Guilarte, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1673547/)

**Quantitative positive control.** In Tian et al., mouse CA1 recordings, lowering the interval between stimuli from 10 s to 1 s yielded normalized inhibitory currents of 0.56 (WT) and 0.58 (Gad2 knockout). Fig. 4A2 instead averages the final 100 ms of a 1 s train at 100 Hz, using the previous ten low-frequency responses as the reference. Digitizing the plotted bars gives approximately 2.8 baseline units in WT and 0.83 in knockout, a KO/WT ratio near 0.30 (8 and 13 recordings, published p < 0.05). The estimated magnitude is approximate: shifting either bar top by one native pixel changes its normalized value by about 0.04. A common shift of the baseline by one pixel gives ratios from 19/66 to 21/68. The plotted means use each recording's baseline; they cannot establish absolute inhibitory charge or the number of animals. Genetic absence of GAD65 is a reference for demand dependence, not a model of exposure to pyridoxine. [Primary study, Fig. 4A1–A2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC23160/#F4) · [Digitization](evoked.tsv)

**Pediatric exposure.** Albersen et al. studied diagnostic samples from children aged 1–18 years. In 35 paired samples, the median CSF/plasma ratio was 1.3 for pyridoxal (PL) and 0.5 for PLP. In Table 3, 35 children receiving antiseizure medication had median CSF PL of 21.7 nmol/L, compared with 28.2 in 41 comparators, a 23% lower median. PLP was 15.7 versus 18.2 nmol/L, a 14% difference. When the original and additional treated samples were combined in the text (51 children), nine had PL below the observed comparator minimum and seven had low PLP. Only three had both, six had isolated low PL and four isolated low PLP. These thresholds derive from the observed range in a diagnostic cohort, which excluded three extreme observations from the original dataset. They provide no validated deficiency threshold or estimate of the causal effect of medication. Age, other diagnoses, sampling conditions and medication burden limit transfer to WWOX. CSF vitamers cannot establish neuronal enzyme occupancy. [Albersen et al., Tables 1–3, CC BY](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0120972) · [Median differences](csf.tsv) · [Threshold cross-tabulation](thresholds.tsv)

**Feasibility and reopen condition.** A randomized trial assigned 51 children/adolescents per arm to pyridoxine or placebo for behavioral adverse effects associated with levetiracetam. There were no serious adverse events over eight weeks; the endpoint does not address seizure rescue or progressive WWOX disease. An adult case of long, very high exposure documents sensory neuropathy and defines a different safety window. The WWOX case reporting continued seizures under B6 and zonisamide leaves the vitamer unspecified. Reopen for impaired formation of active cofactor from actual pyridoxine in WWOX neurons, followed by selective restoration of stimulated GABA replenishment and native inhibitory function at measured attainable exposure. Measure the consumption arm, absolute excitatory and inhibitory responses, paired-pulse behavior and cell survival. Recovery confined to a B6-depleted culture would favor correction of experimental deficiency. Without these observations, low plasma or CSF PLP alone gives little basis for selecting WWOX children. [Pediatric trial, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40913882/) · [Prolonged exposure case](https://doi.org/10.1136/bcr-2018-225059) · [WWOX report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38407561/)

**Reproduction.** Run comparisons.py with the directory containing the [publisher XML](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article/file?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0120972&type=manuscript) saved as albersen.xml and the [original Fig. 4](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/74d1/23160/1ef5b70eeb1c/pq2293746004.jpg) saved as tian_fig4.jpg. The program parses the published table, verifies the marginal counts against the article text and remeasures the bars with ImageMagick. Data are reported as aggregate summaries.
