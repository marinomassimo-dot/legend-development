# Pyridoxine and activity-dependent synthesis of GABA

**Opening prediction.** In WWOX-deficient inhibitory neurons, a deficient supply of pyridoxal 5′-phosphate (PLP) to glutamate decarboxylase during repeated activity could limit replenishment of GABA. Pyridoxine is the specific precursor under assessment. If supply limits transmission, adding it at measured attainable exposure should increase intracellular PLP, restore stimulated glutamate-to-GABA flux and sustain inhibitory charge on successive bursts, with preserved postsynaptic inhibition at the native chloride gradient. If intracellular PLP rises without flux recovery, or restored synthesis leaves release or native inhibition impaired, the case for this mechanism weakens. Compare isogenic neurons at equal maturation, inhibitory cell number and background B6 in medium. Normal GAD65 itself has a cofactor-responsive reserve, so require a genotype-by-treatment difference and distinguish resting from stimulated activity. [Primary isoform study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8194030/)

**Direct clinical context.** The WWOX case abstract records vitamin B6 and zonisamide with continued seizures. It does not specify the vitamer, its independent effect, exposure or change in GABA metabolism. Keep it as evidence against assuming a general B6 response. Existing pediatric experience in genetic B6 disorders establishes clinical exposure in another setting. Exact formulation, peripheral neuropathy risk with sustained exposure and central target engagement require separate evaluation. [Reported WWOX case](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38407561/)

**First dataset check, specified before calculation.** Inspect PDXK, PNPO, PLPBP, PDXP, ALPL, GAD1/2, ABAT, ALDH5A1, SLC32A1 and ALDH7A1. Compare juvenile cortex, hippocampus and week-15 organoids separately. Look for replicated changes in cofactor handling relative to inhibitory-neuron abundance. Transcript direction cannot measure PLP occupancy. Separate loss of GABA-producing cells from lower supply within surviving cells. Next inspect cell-specific contrasts and primary GAD cofactor measurements.

**First results.** PDXK and PNPO RNA diverged between juvenile hippocampus and human organoids. The measurements now need to cover GABA synthesis and consumption, which both depend on PLP. [Contrasts, cell classes and refined test](gaba.md)
