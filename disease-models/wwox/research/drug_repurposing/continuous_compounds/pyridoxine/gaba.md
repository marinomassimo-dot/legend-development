# A two-sided PLP-dependent GABA balance in WWOX

**Observed RNA contrasts.** Mean log2(1 + expression) KO minus control for PDXK was −0.58 in juvenile mouse cortex, −0.88 in hippocampus and +0.015 in week-15 human organoids. PNPO was −0.17, −0.86 and +0.65 respectively. ALDH7A1 was lower in both juvenile mouse tissues but rose in human organoids. There is no shared three-model transcriptional deficit in converting pyridoxine to PLP, and lower ALDH7A1 RNA alone cannot establish cofactor inactivation. Check free PLP, pyridoxine conversion and enzyme-bound cofactor directly. GAD1 and SLC32A1 rose in the organoids from low baseline. Samples are four control and three KO cortical libraries, two and three hippocampal, and two and four organoid libraries. Bulk comparisons use TPM in mice and source normalized expression in organoids. The within-model contrasts and leave-one-library-out bounds are descriptive. **Source of apparent differences.** In pooled juvenile hippocampal inhibitory-marker-enriched nuclei, Pdxk fell from 25 to 16 counts per million, Pnpo from 13 to 6, and Abat from 191 to 133. Gad1 and Gad2 mildly increased within the assigned inhibitory class. Both genes and Slc32a1 define this class, so their expression cannot independently validate classification. For total Gad2, the nuclear signal increased 9.26 CPM overall, with a +10.01 CPM class-weight component and −0.75 within-class component. One pool per condition limits inference about animals. The adult P47T hippocampal comparison gave a much smaller Pdxk decrease (−0.13 in females and −0.12 in males), and Pnpo did not decrease in either sex. Age and genotype both differ. **Revised discriminating observation.** The active cofactor supports GABA degradation by GABA transaminase as well as synthesis by glutamate decarboxylase. Nutritional B6 deficiency in rat brain reduced holoenzyme activity on both sides. A separate rat synaptosome experiment used isotope-labelled precursors to measure both formation and utilization, illustrating why a steady GABA pool has no unique interpretation. In WWOX, if pyridoxine replenishes PLP and both fluxes rise while inhibitory charge on repeated activity fails to recover, an increase in cofactor availability does not rescue the proposed circuit mechanism. If formation and train-evoked absolute inhibitory output preferentially recover in WWOX, measure the postsynaptic sign at native chloride and the effect on recruitment.
