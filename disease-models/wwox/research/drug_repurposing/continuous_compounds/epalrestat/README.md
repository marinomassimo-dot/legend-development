# Epalrestat · polyol flux and glycan occupancy in WWOX-LoF

**Unit and region.** P17 mouse cortex and hippocampus bulk libraries, week-15 human isogenic forebrain organoid libraries, P17 mouse hippocampal pooled nuclei by assigned class, and adult Drosophila head metabolomics in five pools per experimental arm. External comparators are donor-derived human skin fibroblasts and cortical organoid lines, whole zebrafish larval pools, SORD-deficient fly heads, and human plasma or urine. Checkpoint 27 November 2026, SCI_CODEX. [Prespecified comparison](pretest_20261127.md)

**Disposition.** Retain epalrestat as a conditional aldose reductase probe. Defer pediatric WWOX intervention priority until identified endogenous polyol flux, verified local exposure and site-level glycosylation or a specified alternative mechanism can be tested together.

## Prediction and counterproof

**Inference.** PMM2 cells show that reducing glucose entry into the polyol pathway can change donor pools and terminal sialylation. WWOX could instead have limited GDP-mannose production or use downstream of that diversion, or a redox or osmotic phenotype without a donor limitation.

**One discriminating comparison.** In stage- and lineage-matched WWOX-deficient and corrected neural preparations, compare vehicle with epalrestat at measured unbound parent exposure. Use independent culture batches and parallel pulses of labeled glucose and labeled mannose. Resolve sorbitol from mannitol and galactitol against authentic standards, then chromatographically resolve and validate fragment specificity for GDP-mannose against UDP-HexNAc. Measure precursor amounts and enrichment over time, lipid-linked glycan abundance, glycopeptide site occupancy and mature glycoforms per corresponding protein. Use responsive PMM2 fibroblasts for donor-assay calibration and SORD-deficient fibroblasts for sorbitol suppression calibration. Treat PMM2 neural organoids as a separate low-polyol comparator when a matched preparation is available. If assay qualification allows it, a non-drug AKR1B1 perturbation provides an orthogonal on-target check. Pair the readouts with NADPH/NADP, carbonyl stress, osmotic recovery and one staged neural recovery measure such as first response and recovery following a standard stimulus, with viability and ATP tracked.

- **Diversion limiting glycan synthesis.** Reduced labeled sorbitol precedes increased labeled GDP-mannose and restored occupancy at sites deficient in WWOX, followed by maturation and physiological recovery. Matched engagement in corrected preparations tests WWOX-specific rescue against general pathway modulation.
- **Downstream or stress limitation.** A validated fall in sorbitol with unchanged deficient occupancy rejects glycan rescue under the tested exposure and stage. Precursor recovery without mature glycoform recovery favors a downstream limit. Functional recovery without an occupancy change can support the redox or osmotic rival if the corresponding stress readout changes. Failure to see a quantifiable baseline deficit or pharmacological engagement leaves the rescue test uninterpretable.

## Reproduction

Run the following from the dossier directory. `DRUG_DIR` points to the repository's drug-repurposing directory, `HELD_FILES` to the shared evidence directory, and `OUT` to a writable output directory. Accession paths and hashes are documented in [inputs](inputs.tsv) and in linked manifests. The reproducible outputs are gene contrasts, exact-mass QC and ratios, and registry plus PK conversions. There is no inferred WWOX clinical response.

```bash
python3 -B replay_expression.py "$DRUG_DIR" "$HELD_FILES" "$OUT"
python3 -B replay_fly.py "$DRUG_DIR/continuous_compounds" "$HELD_FILES/deepening_downloads/l_leucine_loop_20261126/positive" "$OUT/fly"
python3 -B replay_clinical.py "$HELD_FILES/deepening_downloads/epalrestat_20261127/NCT04925960.json" "$OUT/clinical.tsv"
```
