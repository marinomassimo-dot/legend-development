# γ-L-glutamyl-L-cysteine pretest in WWOX loss of function

**Data scope.** Held P17 mouse Wwox neuronal-deletion cortex and hippocampus TPM, week-15 human WWOX-knockout cerebral organoid library CPM, P17 mouse hippocampal cell-class UMI CPM, adult human lymphocyte glutathione, and mouse cortical or brain glutathione and redox measurements where separately verified.

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. Opened after the guanfacine and perampanel checkpoints landed. The [N-acetylcysteine analysis](../acetylcysteine/README.md) supplies the cysteine-delivery comparator. γ-L-glutamyl-L-cysteine is a candidate to test a step downstream of glutamate–cysteine ligase. The adult pilot abstract reports a peripheral GSH change. Mouse and neuronal reports provide candidates for testing brain transfer and direct redox activity. Those are different compartments and mechanisms. [Human pilot](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28131081/) · [Primary mouse and neuronal study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21159318/) · [Alternative GPX1 mechanism](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22395609/)

**Fixed held comparison.** GCLC, GCLM, GSS and GSR for synthesis and recycling. GGT1 and GGT5 for cleavage context. GPX1 and GPX4 for peroxide use. SLC25A39 and SLC25A40 for mitochondrial GSH import. SLC7A11 and SLC3A2 for cystine–glutamate exchange and CTH for alternate cysteine supply. Compare the same model-specific library scales and all single-library omissions. Read assigned nuclear counts as descriptive, with one pool per genotype.

**Competing predictions.** If the intact peptide reaches neural cells and bypasses a limiting first synthesis step, early appearance of labeled intact parent and glutathione made from it should persist under a validated reduction in GCL activity, at comparable exposure and viability. That glutathione signal should depend on GSS. Hydrolysis followed by reassembly can also place both labels in glutathione, so measure free labeled amino acids and the authentic peptide early. If γ-glutamylcysteine engages GPX1 directly, peroxide protection may persist despite a validated GSS perturbation and without a gain in newly synthesized glutathione. Pair the redox measures with absolute energy and evoked-output endpoints at matched input and baseline work. A change in peripheral GSH, isolated oxidative reporters or brain homogenate alone leaves the genotype-selective functional hypothesis unresolved.

**First check.** Audit the exact form and exposure route in each primary study, trial registration versus the reported human endpoints, animal and participant denominators, tissues and sampling times. Compare direct peptide identification with GSH only, including the possibility of an extracellular cleavage product. Reopen clinical feasibility only with formulation-specific pediatric evidence or a justified research bridge and measured relevant neural exposure. Priority remains open at this pretest.
