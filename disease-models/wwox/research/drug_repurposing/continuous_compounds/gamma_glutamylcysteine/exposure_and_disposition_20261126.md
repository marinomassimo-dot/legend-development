**Endpoint and material audit.** The original registration ACTRN12612000952842 specified brain MRI/MRS as well as lymphocyte GSH. Its linked pilot reports lymphocytes. Even a future MRI/MRS GSH signal would measure product rather than establish entry of the parent peptide. The longer mouse study used the same commercial sodium salt with partly overlapping authors, and its declared interests identify two co-inventors on manufacturing patents. The two studies provide distinct endpoints and populations without independent confirmation of oral neural parent exposure. Conjugated or esterified analogues have different exposure properties. [Registration](https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=362984) · [Accepted manuscript and disclosure](https://d1xe7tfg0uwul9.cloudfront.net/sbgg.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/1-SUPLEMENTO-ALZ.pdf)

**Pediatric feasibility.** The checked formulation is the studied sodium salt. The Australian compositional guideline defines ingredient identity and controls and provides no trial evidence in WWOX. The adult pilot reports no adverse effects during dosing days and the next seven days in a small sample. The newer phase-1 registry NCT07583251 enrolls people aged 55–80 with mild cognitive impairment. Its official API showed recruiting status and no posted results on 26 November 2026. Its listed biochemical endpoints include blood GSH and iron. Public searches did not establish pediatric exposure, repeated-use tolerability, CNS parent pharmacokinetics or interactions with background antiseizure medications. In a research comparison, document salt identity, reduced and oxidized content, preparation stability, age, background ASMs and renal and hepatic feasibility before interpreting pharmacodynamic measurements. ## Inference, test and decision

**Inference.** The WWOX data weaken a shared, isolated GCL bottleneck. They raise two limits downstream of the intervention. GSS is directionally lower in the held mouse hippocampus and higher in cortex and organoids. Bulk SLC25A39 is directionally lower in all three systems, with a discordant pooled neuronal check. The independent organelle assay shows how a rise in whole-cell GSH can miss the mitochondrial compartment. SLC25A39 also has protein-level feedback in non-neural cells, so its RNA does not fix the sign of protein abundance or transport. One plausible WWOX response is more newly synthesized cytosolic GSH with little matrix delivery or evoked recovery. Another is a GPX1-dependent response that affects oxidative markers even when the GSH pool changes little. [WWOX model comparisons](README.md) · [Transporter protein feedback, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37917749/)

**Discriminating observation.** At matched measured free exposure in WWOX and corrected neural preparations, track intact isotope-labelled γ-GC, free labelled breakdown products and labelled GSH in cytosolic and validated mitochondrial fractions. Compare the intact precursor with matched cysteine and glutamate, with GCL capacity, GSS and GPX1 evaluated separately. If intact parent appears and GSH synthesis survives reduced GCL but depends on GSS, that favors a downstream synthesis bypass. If protection survives impaired GSS with minimal new GSH and depends on GPX1, favor direct redox use. Label recycling after cleavage means double-labelled GSH alone cannot establish intact uptake. A rise in labelled cytosolic GSH with little matrix GSH or unchanged absolute ATP and evoked function would favor a downstream bottleneck over rescue. Require outcomes at matched baseline activity and include control cells in the same comparison.

**Disposition.** Keep γ-GC as a preclinical probe to distinguish precursor availability, GSS capacity, mitochondrial transfer and a direct GPX1 effect. Park for unselected pediatric WWOX trial priority. Reopen for measured intact WWOX neural parent exposure and genotype-selective preservation of absolute energy recovery and evoked function with the mechanism resolved. An independent reinterpretation of the small adult pilot or the homogenate figures would leave these missing results unchanged.
