# Baricitinib in WWOX loss of function

**Decision.** Retain baricitinib as a conditional laboratory probe. Defer pediatric WWOX development. A decrease in inflammatory signaling has a favorable interpretation only if the implicated local signal preceded functional deterioration and oligodendrocyte trophic signaling, survival and first sheaths are preserved. A ligand-specific perturbation should face baricitinib at comparable suppression of the measured harmful signal.

## Direct functional counterchecks on the compound

- In adult 5xFAD mice, Kedia et al. reported fewer abnormal cortical myelin profiles at nine months (Fig. 3g, four mice per arm) and less OLIG2/EdU labeling in an earlier cohort (Fig. 3f, four to five). An IFNγ-stimulated slice arm also provides an acute microglial-state comparator. These observations favor potential benefit in an acquired inflammatory setting. Lower proliferation at that age does not show formation of first sheaths, and systemic treatment does not localize the causally relevant concentration. Checked the 2026 correction. It changes Fig. 1h and extended source figures, including a behavior dataset, and does not identify a change to Fig. 3g. [Kedia et al., Fig. 3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-024-01682-8) · [Author correction](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-026-02502-x)
- In human iPSC-derived **peripheral sensory neurons**, 1 µM nominal baricitinib blocked the pSTAT3 response to **both** IL-6 plus soluble IL-6R and LIF at the tested time. This is a 51-fold nominal bath-to-median-CSF concentration ratio, with no dose response or free drug determined for that comparison. The paper's separate longitudinal firing arm used tofacitinib. This paired signaling result supports measuring loss of LIF response under baricitinib in the recipient lineage. Its magnitude and concentration cannot be assigned to CNS oligodendrocyte progenitors. [Li et al., Fig. 5E](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci.insight.186646) · [Supplementary Fig. 6, p. 7](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci.insight.186646DS1)

## Native WWOX check and pediatric boundary

**Data.** Mouse P17 neuronal-loss cortex and hippocampus and week-15 human organoid bulk data were reprocessed. Among five prespecified interferon-responsive genes (CXCL10, IFIT1, ISG15, IRF7 and USP18), requiring both arm means ≥1 on their native scale and a direction that holds under all single-library omissions leaves stable decreases for ISG15 in cortex, a stable rise for IRF7 in hippocampus, and stable decreases for ISG15, IRF7 and USP18 in organoids. At the same descriptive floor IFIT1 in organoids and IRF7 in cortex cross zero with omissions. Hippocampal CXCL10 rises from 0.25 to 1.36 TPM and remains a sparse candidate rather than an additional above-floor result. The floor tests sensitivity to low abundance and is not a significance test. The [bulk data](native_bulk.tsv), [class assignments](cell_types.tsv) and [composition check](mixture.tsv) use P17 hippocampal nuclei only at one pool per genotype. They cannot establish intrinsic signaling or secretion. [GSE158682](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) · [GSE158681](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) · [GSE156243](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243) · [GSE158684](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158684)

**Inference.** There is no uniform bulk interferon-response increase across these WWOX models. An early regional response remains possible. Lower bulk LIFR/gp130 RNA across all three models repeats the prior receiver observation while leaving inducible protein activity open. ADV-231 and ADV-233 separate the conditional P47T animal-state signal from a genotype-wide activation claim. No mechanistic direction can be inferred from a glial count, SOCS3/STAT3 RNA, or whole-blood cytokine suppression alone. Establishing who responds before function declines is more informative than choosing broad JAK inhibition from a bulk signature.

**Pediatric boundary.** Baricitinib has EU pediatric indications in specific inflammatory diseases from age two. In juvenile idiopathic arthritis, participants had to respond during the lead-in to be randomized to withdrawal. Of 163 randomized (82 continued drug, 81 placebo), the primary abstract reports treatment-emergent infection in 31/82 versus 15/81 in the blinded period and severe thromboembolism in one treated participant. The exposure-adjusted infection incidence rates were 102.1 versus 59.0 per 100 patient-years at risk. These observations establish pediatric systemic experience under a different disease and selected population. The systemic risk, local free drug, WWOX CNS engagement and developmental-function tradeoff preclude advancing study priority from this comparison. [EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/olumiant) · [Ramanan et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37423231/)

## One discriminating comparison

**Hypothesis.** With matched initial neuronal activity, a WWOX-specific inflammatory signal worsens glial maturation, and a selective reduction could rescue function. The rival is a response that reflects injury or supplies trophic support after neuronal loss. Baricitinib could lower the first assay readout in either case while either preserving or sacrificing CNTF/LIF-dependent maintenance.

**Experiment.** Use matched WWOX-deficient and corrected developing neuron–glia preparations with eligible axons and viable precursor counts recorded before perturbation. Measure basal and ligand-evoked pSTAT1(Y701) and pSTAT3(Y705) over time in separated astrocyte, microglial and oligodendroglial classes. Identify whether a preferential IFN or IL-6 arm precedes the functional defect at the same initial activity. Record CNTF and LIF responses as trophic comparators. Use measured free parent to compare baricitinib, vehicle and **one** independently validated ligand-specific reference chosen from the discriminated arm. Match inflammatory-branch engagement, document trophic engagement separately, and test survival, new persistent sheaths per initially eligible axon and conduction in the same preparation. An early versus delayed control keeps seizure-linked or stimulation-linked signaling separate from a basal cause.

| Outcome | What it would change |
| --- | --- |
| The local excess precedes dysfunction. The two perturbations reproduce a functional rescue at matched inflammatory engagement, with CNTF/LIF response and first sheaths preserved. | Prioritize the local mechanism and compare whether the narrower intervention retains the benefit at feasible exposure. |
| Both suppress the inflammatory signal, but only the ligand-specific reference preserves function or rescues first sheaths. | Move the mechanistic comparison toward the narrower pathway. Baricitinib remains a broad laboratory comparator. |
| The signal follows the decline, or falls under both perturbations without specific functional recovery. | Treat it as a downstream state marker in that preparation. |
| CNTF/LIF signaling, lineage survival or first sheaths deteriorate when baricitinib lowers the glial marker. | A lower marker accompanies loss of useful function. Decrease compound priority even if the early inflammatory readout appears favorable. |

**Checkpoint.** Use INV-14 for the inflammatory fork and the existing INV-06 first-sheath readout for the trophic countercheck. Reopen for a paired local phosphoprotein and functional trace or a direct exposure/engagement bridge. A further re-scoring of the same published cohorts leaves the decision unchanged. A review of the opposing developmental results could tighten the boundaries if it obtains stage-matched recombination, residual Stat3 and mouse-linked functional data. No such matched dataset was retrieved.
