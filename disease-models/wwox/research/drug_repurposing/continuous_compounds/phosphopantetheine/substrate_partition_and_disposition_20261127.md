# CoA-Z | Precursor partition, neural CoA and disposition

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Disposition.** Retain 4′-phosphopantetheine as a conditional preclinical substrate for locating a WWOX metabolic limitation. Pediatric WWOX trial priority remains deferred. In a defined upstream human cell defect, precursor treatment can leave residual CoA depletion. The WWOX data support a decrease in COASY RNA, while the RNA screen found no shared PANK4 induction. No reviewed human CoA-Z result establishes CNS exposure, absolute neural CoA recovery or WWOX benefit.

## Mechanism controls using original data

**Upstream genetic control.** In PPCS-deficient human fibroblasts, source values for Fig. S5 give whole-cell CoA means of 74.44% and 70.78% of untreated control in two patient lines. With the highest phosphopantetheine addition tested, these rose to 88.92% and 88.33%. The latter condition has three plotted observations per line, and untreated has eight and four. These are two lines with repeated assay observations. At the smaller pantethine addition both means were near 105% of untreated control, an informative processing counterexample to assuming the phosphorylated form will always rescue better. The extracellular concentrations, chemical species and possible precursor equivalents differ. There was no matched intracellular exposure or isotope attribution. The experiment measures whole-cell abundance and does not localize mitochondrial free CoA or establish a neuronal response. [Zhang et al. primary study, Results for Fig. 2 and Fig. S5, Supplementary Data 13](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43856-025-01017-z)

**Competing loss of precursor.** The Dibble Fig. 4c source workbook gives relative phosphate release 1.00 for phosphorylated pantetheine and 0.10 for phosphorylated pantothenate, at the reported assay conditions and with the former mean used to normalize. Water blank was 0.05. This is a substrate comparison under excess and does not estimate neuronal kinetics or an AKT effect on maximal activity. In Fig. 4d, full-length WT PANK4 re-expression in PANK4-null AKT-activated MCF10A cells lowered the labeled CoA pool by 39.67% relative to empty vector. The two catalytically inactive controls did not show that decrease. These are three reported biological replicates per group, with labeling supplied as vitamin B5 and mass +4 CoA normalized to protein. This non-neural experiment supports a drain on pathway flux and leaves its strength in WWOX neural cells unknown. The held PKAN mouse results concern other readouts and compartments. [Dibble et al., Fig. 4c–d and source data](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04984-8) · [Held PKAN mouse comparators](../pantethine/species_compartment_and_disposition_20261127.md)

## Decision-changing observation

**Hypothesis.** At matched intracellular delivery, the downstream substrate can improve absolute free mitochondrial CoA preferentially in WWOX-deficient cells if the principal limitation lies before the entry point and there is sufficient downstream capacity.

**Countercheck and prediction.** Start from stage-matched WWOX-deficient and corrected neural cultures with a verified native defect. Compare traced upstream and downstream entry in the same preparations at comparable measured usable substrate, with extracellular stability and intracellular uptake resolved. Measure labeled pantetheine, phosphorylated substrate, dephospho-CoA, CoASH and acquired acyl species, plus absolute matrix CoASH and matched-workload neural oxidative recovery. A downstream-specific gain in traced CoA, absolute free matrix pool and function supports bypass of PPCS/PPCDC or the intervening substrate supply. Rapid intracellular formation of labeled pantetheine despite verified delivery favors substrate loss. Accumulation of dephospho-CoA with little labeled CoASH favors the final COASY reaction. A rise in whole-cell CoA without a matrix gain instead favors compartmental transport or trapping in acyl species. The control with verified PPCS deficiency must show the expected route response before attributing WWOX rescue to bypass.

**Reopen** for a measured native phosphopantetheine or free matrix-CoA deficit with enough pathway coverage to distinguish substrate loss, terminal conversion and import. A directly measured neural exposure or a new controlled clinical result would warrant independent review if it could change interpretation. The observed UK enrolment does not alter the next mechanistic comparison.
