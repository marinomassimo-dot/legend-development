# R-alpha-lipoic acid disposition

**Park pediatric WWOX trial priority.** A residual endogenous pathway could respond indirectly, as observed in selected PKAN cell lines. In WWOX, a lower DLAT transcript could lower the enzyme abundance without changing the proportion carrying lipoate. Neither low occupancy nor selective functional rescue was demonstrated in the WWOX data examined. The non-neural protein measurements and the hippocampal class-specific results motivate paired neural protein, occupancy and flux measurements. [WWOX data and lesion-specific comparators](README.md)

**Adult exposure is formulation-sensitive and brief.** Keith et al. studied nine older and ten younger adults after a single fasting pure-R or racemate exposure. The average measured pure-R-arm plasma peaks in young and older adults were about 7.3 and 15.7 µM. Concentrations approached baseline by three hours. Chromatography did not resolve R and S in the racemate arm. The published young-adult R AUC of 1070 ng h/mL conflicts with the subgroup means: two groups of five with means 1070 and 1601 imply 1335.5 under a common complete denominator. Fig. 3D also puts all but one of the young female R points below about 1050 and the maximum below about 1700. Even the approximate ceiling (1700 + 4 × 1050)/5 = 1180 falls below the printed female mean of 1601. Neither reconstructed value replaces the source. [Keith et al., Tables 1–2 and Fig. 3D](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22609537/)

**Independent check.** Hermann et al. measured R and S separately in 24 fasting adults in a crossover with equal total racemic exposure in four formulations. Sampling extended to eight hours. For one tablet versus the oral solution, the group geometric mean R peaks were 13.68 and 38.02 µM, and R AUC through the last quantifiable sample was 11.74 and 18.26 µM·h. The positive PKAN experiment added 10 µM racemate (nominally 5 µM R) over a 20-day treatment. If that R concentration were maintained for one day, the area would be 120 µM·h; the two single-exposure adult plasma areas equal about 10% and 15% of that hypothetical day. Culture decay was unmeasured. At 5 µM, the observed plasma areas correspond to 2.35 and 3.65 equivalent hours. These are area-equivalents calculated from group summaries, not measured durations above threshold or infant brain concentrations. A short pulse could trigger a sustained transcriptional response even with a smaller area. The discriminating comparison uses measured pulse-and-washout versus prolonged exposure and reads subsequent endogenous lipoylation and intact-cell function. [Hermann et al., sampling and Tables 1–4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4258503/) · [All four formulations](pulse.tsv) · [Calculation from source tables](pulse.py)

**Baseline exposure in the organoid model.** The organoid protocol specifies equal volumes of DMEM/F12 and Neurobasal and names two possible DMEM/F12 suppliers in its stem-cell section. If the named Gibco DMEM/F12 (21331-020) was used at maturation, its 0.105 mg/L lipoic acid would contribute approximately 0.255 µM to the starting mixture before further additions. The bottle, stereoisomer fraction, free concentration and medium stability were unmeasured. This matters when comparing added labeled R with the endogenous precursor route. [Steinberg et al., cerebral organoid generation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34268881/) · [Manufacturer formulation](https://www.thermofisher.com/us/en/home/technical-resources/media-formulation.329.html)

**Reopen** for an abnormal lipoylated/total enzyme fraction or impaired flux in WWOX neural cells and a selective sustained functional response to a short measured R pulse, compared with corrected cells. Distinguish endogenous synthesis from intact external lipoate incorporation and a redox-only response. Pediatric diabetes studies do not establish infant brain exposure, antiseizure co-medication compatibility or developmental benefit. The positive chronic racemate experiment and human peak overlap alone do not justify promotion. [Pediatric and mitochondrial comparisons](README.md)
