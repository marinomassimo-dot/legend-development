# Carabersat, site affinity and network recurrence

28 November 2026 UTC. Continuous compound #202, INV-05. context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. [Prespecified contrast](pretest.md) · [Opening comparison](first_comparison.md).

**Disposition.** Keep carabersat as a conditional preclinical comparator of recurrent circuit activity and defer pediatric WWOX priority. The radioligand site is measurable and stereoselective. The slice result shows less evoked recurrence in a high-potassium preparation. Its target identity, response in native juvenile WWOX and the free exposure needed for a matched comparison remain unestablished. The interim description of the voltage study as independent is corrected here. It uses a different assay from the same sponsor and shares an author with the original efficacy paper.

### Analogue check

Digitising the twelve points in Herdon Fig. 6 gives Pearson **r = 0.861** between rat membrane pKi and mouse electroshock threshold increase at the study's fixed oral dose. Leaving out one structure at a time gives 0.836–0.884. Within the six highest-affinity structures the estimate is 0.628, and within the four highest it is −0.230. The high-affinity subsets are descriptive range restrictions. In the plotted data, 5s has about **pKi 8.04 and +90%** threshold, while 5r has **pKi 7.65 and +139%**. Broad assay concordance survives pixel perturbation. The top-range rank reversals rule out reading binding rank as functional rank at that oral dose. Cross-species assays, same-dose rather than matched free exposure, matrix binding and possible differences in efficacy at a bound site remain plausible. No confidence interval around the biological relationship can be obtained from pixel perturbation. [Herdon et al., p. 1689, Fig. 6](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjp.0701331) · [Visible point centers](figure6_points.tsv) · [Replay](comparison.tsv)

**Compartment check.** In the original electroshock-threshold experiment, the parent was measured in whole-brain homogenate at **0.12 ± 0.03 µmol/kg** at the minimum effective study dose (six brain samples per group, quantification floor 0.05 µmol/kg, taken after the threshold test). Assuming 1 kg/L only to put the quantities on a comparable arithmetic scale gives about 120 nM total. Free fractions of 1%, 10% and 30% would imply 1.2, 12 and 36 nM, respectively. Reaching 48 nM on those assumptions needs about 40% free. This is sensitivity analysis. It does not assert that a threshold effect requires reaching `Ki`. The 0.2 µM slice value is a nominal bath concentration at a different endpoint. [Upton et al., pp. 1680 and 1682](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjp.0701330) · [Replay](comparison.tsv)

### Target transfer limits

A same-sponsor anaesthetised-cat study confirms that carabersat and the related cis compound tonabersat both alter a trigeminal neurovascular reflex. At the same infusion rate the group-level maximal inhibitions were 33 ± 4% (n=3) and 30 ± 7% (n=4), at different times. It is a different compartment and outcome. Kim et al. report direct inhibition of Cx43 hemichannel currents by **tonabersat** in a separate cell system under a strong voltage challenge, and distinguish hemichannel effects from coupling at higher exposure. Neither study records carabersat in a defined Cx43 current or tests whether genetic dependence of site binding and network effect coincide. Cx43 and extracellular potassium are candidate readouts, not assigned targets. A family relationship to cromakalim likewise does not establish an ATP-sensitive K⁺ channel mechanism. The opposite stereochemical series has reported hypotensive properties, so the comparator also needs a functional check. [Parsons et al., Figs. 2–3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1572685/) · [Kim et al., direct current comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28560708/) · [Blackburn et al., primary abstract](https://doi.org/10.1016/0960-894X(95)00180-2)

## Decision-changing experiment

The matched juvenile Wwox-deletion preparation has spontaneous, NMDA-dependent neocortical bursts at baseline. This gives an endpoint without imposing an 8.5 mM bath. In the source rat assay 8.5 versus 2.5 mM would shift the ideal K⁺ reversal potential by **+32.2 mV** at 32 °C with fixed intracellular K⁺, a large change in one driving force rather than a predicted membrane voltage. The historical high-K result could therefore reflect a driven state. It could also reflect a drug effect available during spontaneous recurrence even at native K⁺. [Held WWOX primary record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8609180/) · [K⁺ sensitivity and assay alignment](comparison.tsv)

Test carabersat, opposite enantiomer and vehicle in littermate juvenile Wwox-deletion and control slices. Match local free concentration and equilibration, keep magnesium and the first excitatory/inhibitory responses comparable, and record spontaneous event count, duration and total late burden. Pair the native recording with a high-K challenge and a local extracellular K⁺ trace. Compare site displacement in matched tissue across enantiomers, followed if necessary by a tool compound with a second scaffold that displaced the original tracer. Convergence across scaffolds matters only at verified exposure and equivalent initial recruitment. If the late burden falls more with carabersat, the first response remains stable, and the result follows independently measured displacement even without elevated bath K⁺, favour a transferable site-linked circuit effect. If separation disappears at native K⁺ or follows reduced first recruitment, amended K⁺ clearance, or contrasting diffusion, favour a state or exposure explanation. Preserved binding with equal network response after matching would lower the site's priority. Any Cx43 interpretation requires an effect and a site-displacement test under an independent Cx43 perturbation, with hemichannel and intercellular coupling readouts distinguished.

## Human and developmental limits

The sponsor section of the 2002 Eilat report describes eight volunteer studies with 136 completers, approximate terminal half-life 24 h, increased oral availability with food and predominant hepatic metabolism. It describes a separate add-on randomisation in refractory focal seizures with 305 participants (228 assigned active across three arms, 77 placebo). Only the highest arm gets a summarized mean-frequency signal in the excerpt. The report does not give that arm's denominator, interval, attrition, defined analysis population, age strata, relevant treatment interactions or parent free central exposure. It also reports a later formulation with altered plasma profile. The brief sponsor report is insufficient for a comparable effect estimate or a pediatric formulation and feasibility judgment. A searchable ClinicalTrials.gov name/code query returned zero, which does not exclude an older study or unpublished records. No WWOX clinical outcome, developmental repair, or pediatric exposure and tolerability series was identified. [Eilat report, §2.1.2–2.1.4, pp. 32–33](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0920-1211(02)00106-7) · [Accessible excerpt](https://www.academia.edu/79904540/Progress_report_on_new_antiepileptic_drugs_a_summary_of_the_Sixth_Eilat_Conference_EILAT_VI_)

The original animal paper separates a rise in PTZ tonic-extension threshold from an unchanged myoclonic threshold. The accessible Upton and Thompson chapter extract also describes fewer treated animals reaching three consecutive fully kindled events by day 21 (9/17 versus 10/10), with a smaller, non-significant separation for the first full event. Drug preceded each stimulus and the accessible extract did not establish a washout effect. This supports an on-exposure recurrence hypothesis and leaves developmental or sustained benefits open. [Upton et al., Fig. 3](https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjp.0701330) · [Publisher's chapter extract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/amygdala-kindled-seizure)
