# 5-Aminolevulinate in WWOX loss of function

[Printable research brief](../../view/research_reports/aminolevulinic-acid.pdf) · [Pretest](pretest.md)

**Disposition.** Retain 5-aminolevulinate (5-ALA) as a conditional metabolic comparator and defer systemic pediatric WWOX priority. Direct parent perturbation can improve biochemical readouts in FLVCR1 fibroblasts. The exposure was far above measured pediatric plasma peaks, and the experiments leave precursor incorporation into respiratory complexes unmeasured. The pediatric Leigh trials used the hydrochloride with sodium ferrous citrate (SFC), a distinct combination. Context policy was SYNTHESIS.

**Sensitivity and counterevidence.** Citrate synthase activity itself rose **1.84-fold** in affected FLVCR1 lines. The fold-change in electron transfer divided by the fold-change in citrate synthase activity was **1.23** in affected lines and **0.92** in healthy controls. This descriptive check shows how the apparent magnitude changes when two responsive enzyme activities are compared. It leaves mitochondrial mass unresolved. The same article reports unchanged measurable basal total heme in the limited line comparisons of Fig. 3F–G, reduced ALAS1 activity with preserved abundance in Fig. 3H–I, and a separate mitochondrial calcium rescue in Fig. 6. An upstream synthesis defect is plausible in FLVCR1, while generalized energetic recovery has another demonstrated route in those cells. The workbook lacks a fourth treated affected value for panel E despite the caption's nominal n = 4. Its replay reports 4 untreated and 3 treated values and keeps the comparison unpaired. [Bertino, Methods and Figs. 3, 6–7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-026-09691-y)

## Dose-to-exposure and pediatric clinical checks

**Measured exposure.** The exploratory Leigh combination study randomized 10 children aged 15–23 months (5/5). Its 12-week controlled change in Newcastle Paediatric Mitochondrial Disease Scale was inconclusive. In the active-product period, Table 3 gives pediatric plasma ALA Cmax **7.00 ± 3.42 µM**, range **2.48–12.3**, n = 8, and mean PpIX Cmax **22.8 nM**. Compared with mean and highest *total plasma* parent peaks, 5 mM nominal FLVCR1 medium was about **714× and 407×** higher. Shimura's 50–200 µM nominal medium was **7–29×** the pediatric mean. The single-exposure adult surgical-formulation review gives 20.90 mg/L parent (approximately 159 µM using 131.13 g/mol), about 31 times below 5 mM. These comparisons use measured plasma peaks and nominal in vitro concentrations from different formulations and windows. Free intracellular 5-ALA, sustained exposure and pediatric brain access remain unmeasured. [Abe, Methods, Fig. 2 and Table 3](https://doi.org/10.3390/life15081168) · [PMDA, p. 24](https://www.pmda.go.jp/files/000153482.pdf)

**Randomized withdrawal.** In Leigh syndrome, 54 began the 24-week open combination run-in, and 28 with improvement in at least one eligible item were randomized (14 per group). The double-blind efficacy set omitted one active-group participant with no efficacy observations. The withdrawal criterion required a worsening of at least one point on a previously improved cranial nervous-system or myopathy item at two consecutive assessments. By 48 weeks, discontinuation for inadequate efficacy occurred in **2/13** with continued combination versus **7/14** with placebo. The absolute difference is **−34.6 percentage points** (reported exact interval −65.7 to +2.4; p = .1032). The age-stratified active-minus-placebo Mantel–Haenszel estimate reconstructed from 0/8 versus 1/4 and 2/5 versus 6/10 is **−22.2 points**, matching the direction and midpoint of the published interval (−56.9 to +12.4). The article prints the adjusted point estimate as +22.2. Its adjusted p = .2059. The time-to-event comparison (p = .0486) was secondary. Serious adverse events occurred in **9/14 versus 1/14** in the safety set. Their causal attribution remains unresolved by the aggregate distribution. Results apply to this enriched withdrawal design and combined product. The participants ranged from childhood into adulthood, and the trial supplies no parent-only clinical effect, brain heme measurement or WWOX endpoint. [Ohtake, flow and Tables 2–5](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0332283)

**CNS and formulation limits.** Adult-rat radiotracer influx across intact BBB was low, about 0.2 µL/g/min. In a distinct eight-week mouse paradigm, oral parent increased frontal-cortex PpIX in non-fatigued animals. The fatigued comparison did not clearly retain a PpIX gain, and the assay measured the precursor. Tumor fluorescence and PpIX do not identify heme incorporation in intact developing cortex. The U.S. oral hydrochloride surgical product has a separate indication. Its label identifies photosensitivity, porphyria and lack of established pediatric safety and effectiveness for that product. It describes reduced cellular PpIX accumulation with phenytoin and other antiseizure agents in vitro. Human interaction magnitude for the proposed mechanism is unmeasured. Any future pediatric feasibility work would require defined formulation, neural exposure and separate safety and functional outcomes, with porphyrins and iron status interpretable alongside baseline therapy. [Ennis, primary rat transport study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12493610/) · [Ohmori, §§2.6, 3.5 and 4.4](https://doi.org/10.3390/life15091465) · [FDA label §§4, 5, 8.4 and 12.3](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/208630s007lbl.pdf)

## WWOX-specific countercheck and one comparison

**Observation.** Lower oxygen consumption after Wwox loss in embryonic mouse fibroblasts establishes an energetic phenotype in that preparation. A HIF1α-linked fuel shift is another explanation of respiratory impairment. In the [bulk RNA replay](native_bulk.tsv), ALAS1 changed **103→88 TPM** in mouse hippocampus (2 control and 3 mutant libraries), with one-library omission crossing zero. In the same comparison PPOX fell **7.97→3.30 TPM** and FECH **25.19→19.45 TPM**. In week-15 human organoids (2 and 4 libraries), ALAS1 was about **99→100 CPM**, FECH rose **18.16→22.75 CPM**, and FLVCR1 fell **17.01→13.09 CPM**. The [pooled hippocampal nuclei](cell_types.tsv) show FLVCR1 falling in assigned excitatory and inhibitory cells and near stable in assigned astrocytes. The [mixture decomposition](mixture.tsv) assigns most of the Cpox group difference to class weights and most of the Flvcr1 difference to within-class changes. Pooling precludes an animal-level significance claim. FLVCR1 transcript cannot assign transporter isoform, choline handling or ALAS1 activity. These different directions leave both precursor supply and downstream conversion open in WWOX. [Wwox MEF respiration](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4211377/) · [Cortex GSE158682](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) · [Hippocampus GSE158681](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) · [Human organoid GSE156243](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243) · [Nuclei GSE158684](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158684)

**Hypothesis and discriminator.** In stage-matched WWOX-deficient and corrected preparations, cross measured local labelled-parent exposure with matched iron conditions, including vehicle and iron-only reference arms. Resolve labelled PpIX, heme b and respiratory-complex-associated heme, alongside ATP-linked oxygen use, independent mitochondrial biomass and lipid peroxidation. Use independent preparations as the unit. A gain in complex-associated label with improved respiration at matched iron supports a precursor bottleneck. A rise in labelled PpIX with flat complex-associated heme favors downstream conversion or iron limitation. A respiration change reproduced by iron alone or accompanied only by altered coupling keeps the parent mechanism deferred. A rise in damaging oxidation lowers priority even if a single bioenergetic endpoint improves. Reopen for the linked exposure, heme and functional result at the same preparation and developmental stage. Independent review becomes decision-relevant with that dataset or a documented donor-level mismatch in a primary source.
