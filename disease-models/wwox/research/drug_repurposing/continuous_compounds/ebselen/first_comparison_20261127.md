# Ebselen first comparison

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Checkpoint.** Published adult spectroscopy makes the proposed trade-off measurable. The initial direction of the ACC signal is lower myo-inositol at both fields, with lower bulk GSH and amino-acid pools in the 7 T study. Neither study measured functional rescue or free brain parent. [Registered prediction](pretest_20261127.md)

Masaki et al. obtained **19** anterior cingulate cortex (ACC) pairs from **20** adults, then excluded **3** paired spectra using the reported linewidth criterion, retaining **16**. The occipital cortex (OCC) retained **20** pairs. Units below are **µmol/g**, means only. Use `100 * (ebselen / placebo - 1)` for the change column. Nominal paired p values come from the paper; paired covariance and participant-level values are not reconstructed. [Primary Methods, Results and Tables 1–2](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00213-015-4189-2)

The study first tested regions with separate multivariate analyses. The occipital main effect was undetected and the design as reported here provides no formal region-by-treatment comparison. Glx is composed of the other amino-acid measurements and adds no independent denominator. The 3 T crossover by Singh et al. also reported an ACC inositol decline and an undetected occipital change, while surveyed secondary metabolites did not pass multiplicity correction. Neither change of means is a circuit or target-occupancy measurement. The methods, dose and spectrum exclusions differ. [3 T Results and Fig. 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4770517/)

In the held WWOX profiles, ISYNA1, IMPA1 and CDIPT RNA fell in all three bulk comparisons, while KCNQ2/3 bulk and pooled nuclear directions differed. Thus the direction and magnitude of any effect on native M-current remain open. The 7 T GSH reduction gives an additional reason to measure oxidized and reduced species and correctly assign pool depletion versus peroxide turnover. The first step after opening is to bound exposure and review direct biochemical IMPase, glutaminase and thioredoxin effects at comparable concentrations.
