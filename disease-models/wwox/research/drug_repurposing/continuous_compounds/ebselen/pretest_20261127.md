# Ebselen in WWOX loss of function

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · prespecified comparison · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Question.** Can ebselen lower native phospholipid oxidation at measured parent or active-species exposure without reducing the inositol-dependent recovery of physiological KCNQ current? A fall in redox markers can coincide with a loss of transmitter or signaling-lipid reserve. Choose this investigational, non-antiepileptic compound because human brain spectroscopy and two independent WWOX pathway comparisons give observable outcomes for both directions. The organoselenium drug and elemental selenium repletion have different proposed roles. [WWOX inositol and M-current comparison](../myo_inositol/README.md) · [WWOX selenite comparison](../sodium_selenite/repletion.md)

**First comparison.** Compare adult regional myo-inositol and glutathione in published crossover spectroscopy. Retain glutamate and glutamine separately, with documented region, paired denominator and reference. Compare ratios of means descriptively. Establish whether the directions are concordant across doses and methods without assuming cohorts are identical or independent. Spectroscopy inositol is a bulk pool and does not quantify membrane PIP2. Reduced glutathione alone cannot distinguish increased consumption, reduced synthesis and altered recycling.

**Discriminating prediction.** In stage-matched WWOX and corrected neural cells, establish lipid oxidation per unit parent phospholipid and activity-dependent recovery of membrane PIP2 and KCNQ current before the compound. Measure matched free parent and relevant metabolites or thiol adducts. If peroxide reduction is the dominant useful action, native oxidized lipids should fall and evoked function should recover at exposure compatible with preserved PIP2 and current recovery. If inositol recycling is the limiting adverse action, labeled phosphoinositide recovery and M-current should slow together. A validated myo-inositol add-back at the same osmolarity should restore both without erasing the independent redox effect. A glutamate or glutamine pool decline with no corresponding lipid/current rescue calls for separate transmitter and glutaminase measurements. A lower MRS-style pool alone supports none of these causal assignments.

**Decision rule.** Bank an exposure range only if a selective functional effect survives checks on thiol reserve, phosphoinositide turnover and inhibitory recruitment. Defer pediatric trial priority in the absence of relevant PK, tolerability or a separable functional benefit. Keep the adult hearing outcome separate from WWOX circuit function.
