# Ebselen exposure, thiol turnover and disposition in WWOX loss of function

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Disposition.** Keep ebselen as a mechanistic perturbation and defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. The most direct human brain observation is reduced anterior cingulate myo-inositol across adult settings. A small adult 7 T crossover also measured lower glutathione, glutamine and glutamate pools. The known drug activity in those pathways makes a selective redox benefit conditional on preserved phosphoinositide and inhibitory transmitter recovery. Free neural exposure, target engagement and selective functional benefit in WWOX remain unresolved. [Registered decision rule](pretest_20261127.md) · [Adult spectroscopy replay](first_comparison_20261127.md)

In an additional placebo-controlled study of adults with antidepressant-resistant depression, **51** were analyzed clinically after seven days. **43 of 48** completed MRS pairs passed quality checks. The nominal MRS group-by-time interactions favor lower ACC inositol (p = **0.044**) and Glx (p = **0.013**) on treatment. The glutamate interaction was nominal p = **0.021**, with an ebselen-arm pre/post comparison of p = **0.060**. This study used total creatine referencing, reported no detectable short-term MADRS benefit, and gave no free-brain parent measurement. In the **19** treated participants with both eligible spectroscopy and selenium, the selenium–inositol change correlation was **r = −0.38, p = 0.11**. Glx does not reconcile separate synaptic glutamate, intracellular glutamate and glutamine. The spectroscopy adds a biological perturbation signal in another population with co-medication. [Ramli et al., Results and Fig. 3](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41398-024-02899-8.pdf)

## A counterexample that changes the WWOX test

**Data.** Untreated WWOX bulk profiles show lower GLS and neuronal glutamine transporter SLC38A1 in mouse cortex, mouse hippocampus and human organoids. The signs survive library-omission checks. Mouse astrocyte-enriched nuclear GLUL changes opposite to the bulk hippocampal estimate. Earlier IMPA1, ISYNA1 and CDIPT bulk signs likewise were lower, while KCNQ2/3 bulk and nuclear results disagreed. These are substrate and recovery questions. They do not establish a deficit in transporter activity, myo-inositol concentration, or membrane current within either genotype. [WWOX glutamine handling](../l_glutamine/uptake.md) · [Phosphoinositide and KCNQ expression](../myo_inositol/README.md)

**Inference from the replay.** In an illustrative steady-state model, glutamine equals supply divided by the sum of GLS and other glutamine removal. Glutamate equals GLS flux divided by its removal constant. With the other rates held fixed, selective GLS inhibition increases glutamine in this model. The small 7 T study instead measured lower glutamine together with lower glutamate. Using the printed ratios of means (**0.9361** and **0.9726** of placebo, respectively), the replay reproduces both ratios with either unchanged GLS or half GLS activity by changing supply and glutamate removal. The resulting GLS flux differs by a factor of **two** at identical pools. This is a counterexample to identification from pools. The model is normalized, omits neural cell compartments and additional fluxes, and is not fitted to human concentrations or WWOX physiology. [Reproducible comparison](exposure_flux.tsv)

**Prediction and counterproof.** At measured free drug and matched delayed exposure, compare untreated and corrected WWOX neural preparations of the same maturation state. Separate the exposure arms for the parent and any abundant measured species with independent activity evidence. At equal extracellular glutamine, measure intracellular precursor enrichment and labeled glutamate and GABA appearance after matched stimulation. GLS inhibition predicts reduced conversion even after matching the intracellular precursor concentration and enrichment across genotypes and exposure arms. A supply or uptake effect predicts a fall in intracellular precursor and a smaller apparent flux that is recovered after matching intracellular availability. Pair the tracer with evoked inhibitory charge and a patterned recovery endpoint, since decreased excitatory drive can coexist with loss of inhibitory replenishment. Compare an exposure with and without iso-osmotic myo-inositol add-back and verify the add-back changes phosphoinositide recovery. Slower PIP2 and M-current recovery with recovery after add-back supports a substrate-mediated adverse branch. A lower native lipid-peroxide fraction with normal PIP2, Glu/GABA replenishment and evoked function is the observation that would justify a selective redox follow-up. Record reduced thiol reserve, delayed conductance loss or poorer inhibitory recruitment as adverse-direction outcomes even if bursts or oxidation fall. Include a removal/recovery comparison with measured residual exposure before interpreting persistence as a durable functional change.

## Pediatric feasibility and the next decision

An adult oral capsule has randomized short-exposure precedent. The noise-challenge study randomized **83** healthy adults aged **18–31 years**. Its transient 4 kHz endpoint was reduced in one active arm (difference **−2.75 dB**, 95% CI **−4.54 to −0.97**; 20 randomized in that arm and 20 on placebo), with the neighboring active arms failing to show the same effect. The cystic-fibrosis registry used age **18 and older**. These observations supply no relevant pediatric PK, polytherapy interaction or WWOX circuit outcome. Keep the distinction between adult formulation precedent and an established pediatric development route. [Kil et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28716314/) · [Adult registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02819856)

**Reopen** if free neural exposure to a chemically resolved species coincides with selective reduction in native oxidized phospholipid and improved evoked recovery, with preserved inhibitory replenishment and membrane PIP2/KCNQ function. A follow-up review could change the decision if it supplies validated neural exposure or a comparable study that isolates one of those effects. Fitting the observed spectroscopy alone cannot resolve the causal alternatives.
