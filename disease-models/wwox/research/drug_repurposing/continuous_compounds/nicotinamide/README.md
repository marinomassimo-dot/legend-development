# Nicotinamide | Axonal salvage and feedback on NAD turnover

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Opening checkpoint.** Nicotinamide is the sole active compound following primidone. The prior NR comparison describes compartment-specific NAD changes and a reversal by SARM1 deletion under low NMNAT2. Its direct nicotinamide arm was an entire wild-type explant. Direct nicotinamide tests a different first enzyme, NAMPT, and may act through altered consumption of NAD or methylation of the parent. An independent study of induced neurite degeneration in human CNS neurons reports protection by nicotinamide under NAMPT inhibition. This anchors a competing mechanism, with concentration and applicability to immature WWOX axons pending. [Prior NR adjudication](../nicotinamide_riboside/endogenous.md) · [Human CNS neurite study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11742042/) · [Related nicotinic acid comparison](../nicotinic_acid/exposure_nad_and_disposition_20261127.md)

**Prespecified prediction.** With verified local nicotinamide and age-matched neuron–glia preparations, a precursor-limited WWOX axon should increase labeled NAD and improve conduction after activity before any residual structural change. Loss of the gain when NAMPT is independently impeded would support salvage. Protection with little isotopic incorporation, diminished cADPR and dependence on available SARM1 activity would favor suppression of NAD consumption. A rise in the NMN/NAD ratio and deteriorating axon recovery despite improved somatic NAD would lower priority. Include labeled nicotinamide export, 1-methylnicotinamide, cell counts and matched baseline recruitment to expose competition and whole-culture artefacts.

**Next comparison.** Audit the tested parent concentrations and matched biochemical readouts in the human neurite paper, and find form-specific human plasma and CNS exposure. A pediatric diabetes endpoint supplies feasibility context in its studied age band. It leaves CNS uptake and WWOX neurological effects open.
