# Dextromethorphan and dextrorphan in WWOX loss of function

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. The prespecified comparison holds native late NMDAR charge while varying the parent-to-metabolite balance. Evidence comes from recombinant channels in Xenopus oocytes, radioligand binding in membranes, adult human plasma pharmacokinetics, aggregate Rett study outcomes and held juvenile mouse physiology.

**Decision.** Retain parent and metabolite as an exposure-conditional preclinical comparison. Park pediatric WWOX trial priority. A match on one NMDA readout cannot identify sigma-1 action. The pediatric precedents constrain the clinical claim and the bioanalytical measures do not establish protein-unbound brain exposure. [Registered prediction](pretest_20261127.md)

## Receptor pharmacology and a matched-block counterexample

Dravid et al. measured recombinant GluN1/GluN2 current block at −40 mV with no added Mg²⁺, 50 µM glutamate and 30 µM glycine. Table 2 reports 6–42 oocytes across assays at each pH without a compound-by-subtype denominator in each table cell. These are functional equilibrium IC₅₀s. The authors' direct single-channel association analysis is for MK-801 stereoisomers, so the present contrast does not borrow those kinetics for either morphinan. [Primary Methods and Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2075223/)

| GluN2 | pH 7.6 parent / metabolite IC₅₀ (µM) | pH 6.9 parent / metabolite IC₅₀ (µM) |
|---|---|---|
| A | 11.3 / 1.30 | 5.12 / 0.670 |
| B | 3.74 / 0.329 | 1.277 / 0.246 |
| C | 1.07 / 0.146 | 0.466 / 0.114 |
| D | 5.40 / 0.736 | 2.40 / 0.464 |

The metabolite was about 4.1–11.4-fold more potent across measured subtype and pH combinations. Parent acidification shifts were larger than the corresponding metabolite shifts. This direction is assay-bound. For a reproducible counterexample, use the Table 2 IC₅₀s with an illustrative Hill slope of one for each arm, and match 30% equilibrium inhibition of GluN2B at each pH. Equal GluN2B block then leaves unequal predicted GluN2A, C and D block. On GluN2A, the parent-minus-metabolite difference is **+2.64 percentage points at pH 7.6 and −3.94 at pH 6.9**. On GluN2C the respective differences are +10.84 and +5.96 points. This is a mechanistic counterexample with recombinant inputs. Native tissue kinetics, triheteromers, physiological Mg²⁺ and measured Hill slopes could change the result. [Script inputs and outputs](matched_block.tsv)

In the same hypothetical single-site equilibrium comparison, the metabolite molar fraction at equal parent and metabolite contributions to the blocking drive is the metabolite IC₅₀ divided by the sum of both IC₅₀s. For GluN2B the threshold is 8.1% at pH 7.6 and 16.2% at pH 6.9. These are model sensitivities, with no inference about blood, brain exposure, occupancy or doses. [Replay](matched_block.tsv)

Chou et al. report sigma-1 membrane-binding Kᵢ of **205 nM for parent and 144 nM for dextrorphan** in the same series. The reported affinities do not support a parent-selective sigma-1 binding premise. Both can bind and the effects on native sigma-1 function remain to measure. Their PCP-site competition also favored dextrorphan (0.9 versus 7 µM), a distinct assay from recombinant current inhibition. A residual parent-enriched effect at matched NMDA block needs an orthogonal sigma-1 perturbation and within-preparation response. [Chou et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10064839/)

## Human exposure and endpoint audit

**Separate the analytes.** In Schadel et al., five extensive metabolizers given one studied exposure had mainly conjugated dextrorphan and conjugated 3-hydroxymorphinan in plasma. Unconjugated dextrorphan concentration was about 100-fold below its conjugate, and parent was undetected under that condition. With CYP2D6 inhibition, parent increased and its reported half-life was 16.4 h. In four poor metabolizers receiving the same studied exposure, parent was the major plasma component and its half-life was 29.5 h. The source distinguishes chemical conjugation. It does not give protein-unbound WWOX neural exposure. The opening 1/101 arithmetic is a two-pool illustration applicable only where the reported ratio applies. [Primary PK abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7593709/)

The Rett open-label paper classified rapid metabolizers using a dextrorphan-to-parent ratio at 3 h. Its sample preparation included β-glucuronidase hydrolysis. The selection ratio does not quantify contemporaneous free or separately unconjugated dextrorphan in the brain. Likewise, Nuedexta adds a CYP2D6 inhibitor and has a different label, interaction and risk profile. It is evidence that the mixture can change under enzyme inhibition. It provides neither a pediatric WWOX efficacy precedent nor a valid substitution. [Rett primary Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5644464/) · [Combination label, §§1, 5 and 12.3](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c8cce784-8c5f-415b-8068-76e5d481d159)

An older uncontrolled intensive-care series measured both analytes in blood and CSF in a four-child cohort under heterogeneous acute conditions. The published assay used enzymatic deconjugation before quantitation. The reported CSF data therefore do not supply separately unconjugated, protein-unbound dextrorphan concentrations for the native mixture comparison. EEG changes in that setting do not estimate chronic seizure or developmental outcomes in WWOX. [Schmitt et al., Methods and abstract](https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/57/3/333)

## WWOX decision and discriminating observation

The held juvenile WWOX cortical data support d-APV-sensitive delayed recruitment. They do not identify whole-cell subunit allocation, pre- versus postsynaptic effects or a pH-specific drug-response profile. Existing subtype and Mg²⁺ work also shows that an incomplete receptor mixture can explain multiple native probe outcomes. [Held IT41 cortical controls](../../generation_it41_20261109/comparisons_it41.md) · [Held subtype and Mg²⁺ comparison](../../memantine_magnesium_20261123/README.md)

**Test first.** Confirm both unconjugated and protein-unbound concentrations in the same preparation. Match measured late NMDAR charge at defined physiological Mg²⁺ and pH, stimulation history and initial recruitment. Then compare complete current waveforms, direct release, evoked inhibition and burst persistence. If chemical mixtures differ only until subtype allocation or time course is matched, prefer an NMDAR explanation. A residual effect after that match that is attenuated by an independently validated sigma-1 perturbation would support a parallel mechanism. Parent-selective occupancy is insufficient and is not assumed. Test excitatory and inhibitory responses separately. Loss of evoked inhibition or impaired patterned function defers advancement even if spontaneous bursts decline.

**Reopening criterion.** A reproducible genotype-by-mixture interaction at measured local exposure and matched physiological current, with preserved inhibition, would justify revisiting. Independent review would be useful if it resolves the choice of current-matching control, identifies a relevant human unbound exposure data set, or supplies primary evidence for a receptor-independent component. Current data justify the comparison without advancing trial priority.
