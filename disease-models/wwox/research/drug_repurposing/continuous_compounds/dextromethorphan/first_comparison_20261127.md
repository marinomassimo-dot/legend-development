# Dextromethorphan opening comparison

| Preparation | Opening observation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Healthy adult oral pharmacokinetics and CYP2D6 phenotyping | Schadel et al. report a large separation between unconjugated and conjugated dextrorphan, approximately 100-fold in the cited condition. CYP2D6 inhibition shifted exposure toward parent and away from metabolite. | The primary matrix and analytical method distinguish chemical forms. Total concentrations reported after hydrolysis and protein-unbound concentrations require separate handling. This is a metabolic counterexample, with no pediatric exposure inference. [Primary pharmacokinetics abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7593709/) |
| Girls with Rett syndrome, age 3–15, selected as rapid dextromethorphan metabolizers | Thirty-five received treatment across three randomized dose arms in a six-month open-label study. The authors report no detected effect on EEG spike counts and preliminary dose-associated changes in reported seizures, receptive language and hyperactivity. | Separate EEG and caregiver-reported outcomes. There was no untreated comparison and fast-metabolizer enrichment constrains generalization. Distinct disease and ascertainment. [Primary trial abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28931647/) |
| Rodent sigma-1 binding and NMDA-linked pharmacology | A primary comparison included dextromethorphan and dextrorphan on sigma and NMDA-linked PCP assays. Subunit and pH-dependent channel-block measurements provide a separate route to judging native recruitment. | Binding and current block measure different outcomes. Reopen the primary pharmacology before assigning target preference to either species. [Primary binding comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10064839/) · [Primary channel comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17303642/) |
| WWOX cortical preparation | Held d-APV-sensitive delayed activity supplies an NMDAR-sensitive outcome. The normal recruitment and inhibitory endpoints can be measured separately. | The held experiment establishes an NMDAR contribution and leaves the direction of the parent–metabolite mixture effect unresolved. [Held WWOX native control](../../generation_it41_20261109/comparisons_it41.md) |

**Reproduce the scale check.** The reported order-of-magnitude ratio 1:100 would put unconjugated dextrorphan at approximately 1/101 = 0.99% of the sum of the two reported chemical forms. This is an interpretation of one condition, with no protein-binding correction or estimate of active brain dextrorphan. Check whether the primary quantities are concentration ratios or AUCs before comparing any further human summaries. [Primary pharmacokinetics](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7593709/)

**Checkpoint.** Audit source denominators, dextromethorphan versus unconjugated dextrorphan exposure, channel state and the specific Rett EEG outcome. The new decision turns on a reproducible difference between exposure mixtures at matched NMDA block with preserved physiological inhibition. [Discriminating prediction](pretest_20261127.md)
