# Dextromethorphan and the parent-to-dextrorphan balance in WWOX loss of function

**Status.** Opening checkpoint. Earlier LEGEND records covered the catalogue entry and flagged a calcium-flux comparison without voltage or magnesium control. The new question is whether the measured parent-to-dextrorphan balance changes the WWOX circuit outcome at the same late NMDA charge. This comparison treats dextrorphan solely as an active metabolite of the compound under study. [Held NMDAR catalogue limits](../../target_first_it15_qa_nmdar_20261101/README.md) · [Existing WWOX cortical comparison](../../generation_it41_20261109/comparisons_it41.md)

**Prediction.** Hold free concentration, Mg²⁺, pH, membrane voltage and preceding activity explicit in a stage-matched WWOX cortical preparation. Compare parent-enriched and metabolite-enriched exposures adjusted prospectively to the same reduction of late NMDAR charge, with matched early synaptic recruitment. Read whole-network burst persistence, physiological evoked inhibition and a direct release measure separately. A residual divergence at matched NMDA charge could reflect different channel kinetics, subtype engagement or a non-NMDAR contribution. Repeat at matched current time course and add an independent sigma-1 perturbation before assigning that mechanism. A residual parent-enriched effect that depends on sigma-1 and preserves evoked inhibition would support a specific parallel action.

**Counterchecks.** If the difference disappears after matching the current time course, favor NMDA kinetics. If neither mixture engages the relevant physiology at measured free concentrations, defer the compound. Chemical unconjugation, protein-unbound drug and total concentration after hydrolysis are distinct analytical states. Compare the two active species independently. Pediatric exposure and developmental transfer also require separate evidence.
