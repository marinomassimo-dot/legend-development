# ES-481, TARP γ8-dependent AMPA timing and recruitment

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. ES-481 was registered as the single active compound after the [clemizole closure](../clemizole/receptors_exposure_and_disposition_20261127.md). Search aliases ES-481, LY3130481, LY-481 and CERC-611 as one chemical identity, with the enantiomer and metabolite tracked separately. [Primary origin and binding comparison](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-37259-5) · [Alias disclosure](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2021188431A1/en)

**Selection.** This is an experimental antiseizure compound with an adult crossover report and an auxiliary-subunit-specific perturbation. The held WWOX cortical work identified larger miniature excitatory currents and an NMDA-sensitive burst phenotype. It remains open whether rapid AMPA recruitment, TARP-dependent late AMPA charge, and subsequent NMDA recruitment can be distinguished. The published LY-481 recordings provide a separate resensitization and steady-state contrast that can refine the existing IT41/perampanel test. [Held WWOX and IT41 bridge](../../perampanel_20261122/clinical_mechanistic_update_20261126.md) · [Primary AMPA kinetics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7586208/) · [Adult crossover](https://doi.org/10.1002/epi4.70294)

**Registered prediction.** In a preparation with demonstrated γ8-dependent current, ES-481 should preferentially reduce late AMPA current or charge and subsequent burst maintenance at the same measured reduction of the first fast AMPA response as a broader AMPA comparator. Compare first charge as well as first peak and measure NMDA current with its driving conditions controlled. A receptor preparation with little γ8 contribution should show little selective effect or a different response. Shorter bursts at the same nominal concentration or with weaker fast recruitment do not separate these possibilities. A native γ8-selective loss-of-effect control is needed because GluA composition, auxiliary stoichiometry and receptor state can change the magnitude or sign. Preserved first inhibitory recruitment and matched exposure are required to interpret a putative advantage.

**First comparison.** Reconcile the published crossover estimand, missing periods, baseline, diary and EEG endpoints with the trial registry. Reproduce the published AMPA late-charge versus first-peak contrasts and inspect the assumptions around auxiliary stoichiometry. Query checksum-pinned CACNG8 and alternative TARP/CNIH markers by mouse region, age and human organoid rather than assuming conserved receptor complexes. Then compare the identifiable free human exposure range to the relevant receptor condition. Only evidence that shifts the planned matched-current experiment or the exposure feasibility changes priority.
