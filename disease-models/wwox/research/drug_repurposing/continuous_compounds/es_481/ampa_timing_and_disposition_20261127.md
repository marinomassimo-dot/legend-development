# ES-481 in WWOX loss of function

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. This assessment follows the [registered comparison](pretest_20261127.md). ES-481 is LY3130481 (LY-481/CERC-611). The experimental question is whether γ8-dependent modulation changes sustained AMPA transmission at comparable first recruitment in the WWOX circuit.

**Decision.** Retain ES-481 as a conditional mechanism comparator for the IT41/perampanel experiment and defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. The case for separating early and late current is strong in recombinant assays. The direction of the desensitization change reverses with GluA composition. A symptomatic diary signal in a small adult study leaves electrographic replication, developmental transfer and attainable native engagement unresolved.

| Preparation | Reproduced or checked contrast |
| --- | --- |
| Zhang, GluA1i–γ8 tandem, nominal 10 µM, 2-s glutamate step | First peak retains **73.9%** of control. Peak-normalized equilibrium current falls **8.54% → 2.48%**, and the desensitization time constant falls **9.60 → 5.57 ms**. Figure 4B and workbook S6A/B, **n=7** assay cells per reported contrast. |
| Zhang, GluA2i(Q)–γ8 tandem under the same nominal step | First peak retains **91.9%** of control (n=9, p=.0578). Peak-normalized equilibrium current falls **33.08% → 15.98%**. Resensitization falls **5.59% → approximately zero** (post mean −0.27 percentage points on the measurement scale). Desensitization **slows**, **37.59 → 46.43 ms** (n=9, p=.0089). Figure 6B and workbook S9B. |
| Dohrke, GluA2–γ8, nominal 10 µM, 5-s glutamate step | Peak retains **93.6%** of control (n=9). Peak-normalized steady-state current **16.96% → 8.35%** (n=20) and resensitization **8.31% → 0.38%** (n=20). Weighted desensitization time **26.50 → 30.88 ms** (n=20). These groups and summaries are kept separate from Zhang and from each other where the source provides different n. |

The two studies reproduce a GluA2-like pattern of a relatively preserved peak, lower sustained current and slower initial desensitization. GluA1 shows larger peak suppression and the opposite desensitization change. The measured late endpoints are normalized currents late in long glutamate steps, not integrated charge after a physiological pulse train. A pure uniform current-scaling explanation predicts unchanged late/peak ratio and cannot explain these recombinant observations. A native synapse may still reduce bursts solely through lower first recruitment. A nonsignificant peak change does not establish equivalence on the first response. [Zhang Figs. 4, 6, S6 and S9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-37259-5) · [Original workbook](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-023-37259-5/MediaObjects/41467_2023_37259_MOESM6_ESM.xlsx) · [Dohrke Fig. 7](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.RA120.014135) · [Replayed values](ampa_components.tsv)

**Target controls and stoichiometry.** 8AYN has two modeled OLR molecules in the resting GluA1/2 complex with two tethered γ8 subunits. It establishes a pose in that construct and state. Kato finds less maximal block at lower γ8 stoichiometry and selectivity with native γ8 controls. Adding extra γ8 can change the sign of a distinct comparator, JNJ-059, in the Zhang constructs. That reversal belongs to JNJ-059. LY-481 retains lower equilibrium current in GluA2 despite a slower initial decay. These results motivate measuring native γ8 contribution and both early and late endpoints, without transferring the absolute recombinant kinetics. ## Exposure that could support the experiment

Foster §3.3.3 gives two distinct high-exposure total plasma summaries, **1190 and 1317 ng/mL**. Using the deposited ligand mass 382.436 g/mol and the programme estimate of 97–99% plasma binding yields **31–103 nM** as a scenario for adult free plasma near peak. The free fraction was not measured for individual trial participants in the published results. No matched brain concentration was found. The calculated unbound brain-to-plasma ratio needed to equal each benchmark is **0.26–0.87** for mouse WT hippocampus (27 nM), **0.80–2.67** for mouse WT cortex (83 nM), and **1.10–3.66** for human epileptic hippocampal membrane in Xenopus oocytes (114 nM with cyclothiazide, three independent experiments). Kato's corresponding native rat hippocampal and cortical values are approximately 66 and 93 nM. These are different assays and maximal inhibition varies with preparation. Matching the nominal 10 µM used for the detailed kinetic contrast would require an approximate ratio of **97–321**. Native target engagement near adult exposure is thus plausible under some assumptions, while the published kinetic effect size is not established in that exposure range. No exposure range can be transferred to pediatric WWOX from these inputs. ## WWOX inference and discriminating observation

**Held evidence.** Cacng8 declines in P17 mouse bulk cortex (60.23 → 48.83 TPM) and hippocampus (94.00 → 46.76 TPM), while CACNG8 rises in week-15 human organoids (15.18 → 22.79 CPM). All directions survive single-library omissions within each dataset. The P17 hippocampal pooled nuclei fall in excitatory assignments (139.71 → 90.55 UMI CPM, 1729 → 937 UMIs) and inhibitory assignments (81.73 → 55.98, 135 → 105 UMIs). Each genotype has one pooled nuclear library. The data support neither a uniform across-model target deficit nor a claim about γ8 surface stoichiometry. Test both inhibitory and excitatory recruitment because the target's functional contribution is relevant to both. [Bulk matrix replay](wwox_ampa_context.tsv) · [Nuclei replay](wwox_ampa_nuclei.tsv) · [Held WWOX cortical evidence and IT41 design](../../perampanel_20261122/clinical_mechanistic_update_20261126.md)

**Hypothesis and test.** In a prespecified juvenile Wwox-deficient circuit with demonstrated γ8-dependent current, ES-481 should lower late AMPA charge and burst maintenance more than a broader AMPA antagonist at the same first-response peak and charge. Run a concentration–response before matching measured early effect and free exposure, and verify equivalent first inhibitory recruitment. If a single dose cannot match both first peak and charge, report that and compare within the region of overlap. In the same preparation, measure AMPA train charge, NMDA current with fixed driving conditions, spontaneous burst duration and initiation, and verify loss or occlusion of the incremental late effect with an independently validated γ8 perturbation that preserves an interpretable baseline. A positive γ8 control and comparison to a γ8-poor preparation help distinguish insufficient target reserve from drug failure. Perampanel is a practical circuit comparator only with verified isolated AMPA-current matching and kainate effects excluded or measured separately. If equal early recruitment eliminates the late/burst difference, or the effect appears only at the nominal micromolar assay condition, do not advance the proposed exposure-relevant selective mechanism. [Registered test](pretest_20261127.md)

**Reopen** when measured native free exposure gives a WWOX-selective late effect with preserved first recruitment, or when new clinical evidence changes transportability. An independent review of the week-4 trial signal would become decision-relevant with available protocol, covariance and period-level aggregates.
