# ManNAc, neural delivery, pathway routing and WWOX disposition

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Decision.** Retain N-acetyl-D-mannosamine as a precursor comparator for the WWOX sialylation branch. Defer pediatric trial priority. Direct exposure of rat cortical slices supports use of the added carbon in ganglioside sialic acid. Human oral studies measure low systemic parent exposure and peripheral pathway engagement. They leave chemically resolved delivery to developing brain and preferential WWOX product and functional recovery open. Older direct-brain tracer work and the cortical-slice study bypass systemic delivery. The held free Neu5Ac oral tracer record also leaves molecular identity in brain unresolved. ## Test against the treated control

Sabir et al. reported charged-to-neutral glycosphingolipid ratios normalized to the vehicle-treated wild-type mean at each time point. The ratio analysis used **5–6 culture replicates per genotype-treatment group**. LysoTracker was a different assay with **4 replicates per group**. The available released data item is a representative cytometry plot and does not supply glycosphingolipid replicate values. Use the reported rounded ratios for descriptive arithmetic. [Primary Methods, Results, Fig. 1D–E captions and data statement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12441824/)

| Time | Deficient vehicle → ManNAc | Wild type vehicle → ManNAc | Apparent closure of initial gap to vehicle WT | Deficient / WT, vehicle → ManNAc at matched treatment |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 20 h | 0.70 → 0.80 | 1.00 → 1.13 | 33% | 0.700 → 0.708 |
| 24 h | 0.79 → 0.91 | 1.00 → 1.14 | 57% | 0.790 → 0.798 |

The absolute treatment-by-genotype contrasts are **−0.03** and **−0.02** normalized ratio units. A common-response interpretation survives both time points. The small relative shifts of about **+0.008** are also compatible with zero when the reported non-reference values are varied by ±0.005 for rounding. Neither the arithmetic nor the published within-arm contrasts establish equivalence or provide a replicate-level interaction test. The ratio does not identify its numerator and denominator changes or prove rescue of a defined ganglioside. Intracellular ManNAc-6-phosphate, CMP-Neu5Ac and function were outside this comparison. [Replay output](comparisons.tsv)

**Biochemical versus functional countercheck.** Neu et al. observed restoration of polysialylation in GNE-deficient HEK-293 cells at **5 mmol/L** ManNAc and little effect at **0.5 mmol/L**. In Gne-null C2C12 myogenic cultures two clones received ManNAc or Neu5Ac at **5 mmol/L** through an eight-day differentiation comparison (three independent experiments). ManNAc increased total sialic acid in clone 24 yet failed to rescue myosin heavy chain or produce a consistent polysialylation rescue. Neu5Ac increased polysialylation in clone 26, with a separate adverse-direction MYH result in wild-type cultures. Product rescue depends on clone and substrate, and a biochemical response does not identify a beneficial developmental response. These are non-neural experiments. [Primary Results and Figs. 2 and 7 captions](https://academic.oup.com/glycob/article/34/3/cwae004/7542432)

## WWOX-specific inference and prediction

The held WWOX profiles add a candidate phosphorylation limitation to the registered uptake-versus-downstream comparison. Reusing the N-acetylglucosamine panel, bulk NAGK log₂(1 + abundance) contrasts were **−0.117** in P17 cortex, **−0.549** in P17 hippocampus and **+0.095** in week-15 human organoid. Only the hippocampal decrease survives every within-dataset library omission. Across the nine assigned mouse hippocampal nuclear classes NAGK was **685→434 UMIs** and **37.15→26.17 CPM**. Pooled differences remain negative (−11.81 to −7.86 CPM) after omitting each class, and excitatory and inhibitory assignments both decrease. These are one aggregated pool per genotype and a deliberately restricted set of assignments. Gene and denominator counts are separate from enzyme abundance or catalytic flux. GNE and NANS in the held sialylation comparison also fail to establish a conserved upstream loss. Localizing the effect by chemical measurement is more informative than further expression screening. [Held NAGK bulk](../n_acetylglucosamine/bulk.tsv) · [Held nuclear counts](../n_acetylglucosamine/nuclei.tsv) · [Held GNE/NANS/NANP panel](../n_acetylneuraminic_acid/contrasts.tsv)

**Discriminating observation.** In stage- and composition-matched WWOX and corrected neural preparations, first confirm a low absolute donor or product pool. Under matched measured intracellular ManNAc, compare labeled ManNAc-6-phosphate appearance and use of separately labeled free Neu5Ac. Estimate genotype-by-substrate contrasts across independent preparations. Measure absolute and labeled CMP-Neu5Ac, NCAM-normalized polysialylation, defined ganglioside species and evoked recruitment. Include a substrate-limited positive comparator and use the established NANS loss phenotype for the downstream direction. Read serial labeling and absolute pools together to avoid treating exchange or new labeling in a steady pool as repair.

- Slower initial ManNAc-6-phosphate labeling and lower downstream labeling at equal intracellular ManNAc, alongside preserved conversion of free Neu5Ac to donor and products, support a recipient phosphorylation bottleneck. Lower NAGK RNA supplies a candidate mechanism whose contribution requires functional assignment.
- ManNAc-6-phosphate accrual with little labeled Neu5Ac or CMP-Neu5Ac favors a downstream biosynthetic bottleneck. Rescue by free Neu5Ac at matched local exposure would shift the next comparison toward bypass.
- Equal labeled and absolute donor recovery with divergent NCAM and ganglioside outcomes points downstream of donor synthesis. For an oral exposure experiment, brain labeled product could also follow delivery of a labeled peripheral metabolite. Resolve intact labeled parent and labeled Neu5Ac in blood and brain and cross-check with direct neural exposure before assigning local conversion.
- Preferential ManNAc rescue requires a preexisting deficit, recovery of the deficient absolute product and selectively improved evoked function without injury or generalized recruitment changes. A similar response in matched control cells would favor a shared substrate effect.

## Pediatric boundary and checkpoint

The relevant intervention would be a defined ManNAc formulation with measured local exposure. Adult GNE phase 1 found self-resolving diarrhea and nausea in **3/6** participants at its highest exposure arm, with neither event observed in the lower arms or placebo in that study. Adult and peripheral evidence leave pediatric formulation, age-dependent absorption, chronic interactions with background antiseizure therapy and a proximal neural endpoint unresolved. Published pediatric free Neu5Ac studies concern a distinct molecule. [GNE phase 1 safety results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5949875/) · [Pediatric Neu5Ac exposure study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8251509/)

**Reopen** for stage-matched WWOX evidence of a selective absolute neural product deficit, measurable precursor exposure and an interpretable two-route rescue with preserved patterned function. Independent review could change priority if it identifies chemically resolved oral CNS data or a held WWOX glycoconjugate and physiological comparison. Continue from the compound register.
