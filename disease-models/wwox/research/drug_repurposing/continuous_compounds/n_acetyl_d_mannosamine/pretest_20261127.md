# N-Acetyl-D-mannosamine, opening prediction

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Selection.** ManNAc tests a different entry route from the held free Neu5Ac comparison. It enters the biosynthetic sequence after GNE epimerization and requires phosphorylation and downstream NANS function to supply Neu5Ac. A human peripheral evidence chain can check whether plasma, intracellular donor, tissue product and function agree. WWOX brain delivery and selective functional correction remain the target questions. [Held free Neu5Ac dossier](../n_acetylneuraminic_acid/README.md) · [Primary GNE path and measurements](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8553608/)

**Prediction registered for the next comparison.** At the same maturation stage in WWOX and corrected neural cells, compare labeled ManNAc and labeled free Neu5Ac with measured intracellular parent and time-resolved absolute CMP-Neu5Ac. If a relative bottleneck lies at de novo precursor formation and downstream conversion is preserved, ManNAc will enter donor and downstream products and improve a reproducibly deficient product/function pair preferentially in WWOX. If phosphorylated ManNAc accumulates without proportionate Neu5Ac formation, the limiting process lies later in biosynthesis, with the NANS-deficient system as a directional control. If both inputs label CMP-Neu5Ac and fail to recover the selected products, locate the deficit downstream of donor supply. Interpret neural product changes separately for protein-normalized polysialylated NCAM and specified gangliosides.

**Decision rule.** Require a baseline WWOX biochemical deficit in the matched model before interpreting recovery, distinguish isotope fractional enrichment from an absolute pool increase, and use evoked recruitment or maturation as the linked functional outcome. Park for pediatric priority if evidence is limited to plasma, blood cells, skeletal muscle or a stage-mismatched neural culture. Reuse the held WWOX RNA, tissue and fly exclusions.
