# N-Acetyl-D-mannosamine, peripheral pathway engagement and WWOX checkpoint

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Active checkpoint.** In adult GNE myopathy, oral ManNAc changed the circulating metabolite, intracellular blood-cell donor and a skeletal-muscle product readout. A separate placebo-controlled conference report did not demonstrate a difference in the primary strength outcome. The next increment will test whether the evidence supports exposure of the developing nervous system and separates substrate routing from generic sialylation. [Registered prediction](pretest_20261127.md)

| Comparison | Primary observation and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| WWOX datasets already held | GNE RNA knockout minus control means in log2(1 + abundance) were −0.082 in mouse cortex, −0.075 in hippocampus and −0.303 in week-15 human organoid. Omission sensitivity crossed zero in the latter two systems. This does not establish reduced epimerase or kinase activity. NANS changes differed in direction across systems. [Archived WWOX numeric table](../n_acetylneuraminic_acid/contrasts.tsv) |
| Carrillo et al., 12 adults aged 25–55 with GNE myopathy, open-label phase 2 | Day-90 mean plasma Neu5Ac was **2.596 µmol/L** compared with **0.437 µmol/L** at baseline (**5.94-fold ratio of means**). White-cell CMP-Neu5Ac rose at reported time points. Normalized SNA lectin intensity increased in the analysis of **18 matched muscle biopsy pairs** (nominal p=0.009) out of 24 potential pairs. Biopsies are nested in participants and the comparison has no placebo arm. SNA primarily reports α2,6-linked sialylation, apart from the neural products of interest. [Methods, Table 1 and biochemical Results, Figs. 2–3 captions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8553608/) |
| MAGiNE, double-blind placebo-controlled study in GNE myopathy | The primary conference abstract reports **37 ManNAc and 17 placebo recipients**, with several baseline muscle groups weaker in the ManNAc arm. It reports no difference in the primary muscle-strength decline endpoint. The per-protocol direction weakly favored ManNAc and was nonsignificant. The registry lists ages 18–70 as eligible and no posted results in the public API at review. The abstract lacks numerical effect estimates and intervals. This is a functional countercheck in GNE myopathy, with no direct transfer to WWOX neurons. [Primary conference abstract, Methods and Results](https://index.mirasmart.com/AAN2026/PDFfiles/AAN2026-006191.html) · [Trial record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04231266) · [Registry API](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT04231266) |

**Next step.** Seek chemically resolved CNS ManNAc exposure. Compare phase 1/2 and controlled GNE data with aligned analytes, units and analysis denominators. Use an independent cellular or animal comparator only if its isotope or pathway perturbation can separate intact precursor use from free Neu5Ac delivery and downstream branch selection. No pediatric WWOX priority decision follows from the peripheral signal alone.
