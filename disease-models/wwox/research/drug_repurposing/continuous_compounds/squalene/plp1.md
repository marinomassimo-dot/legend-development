Berghoff et al., Supplementary Figure 4 (PDF pp. 8–9). Female Plp1-overexpressing mice received 0.5% dietary squalene from weeks 2–12 or months 2–5. In the early arm, treated mutants had fewer slips (8 treated, 13 mutant controls, panel b). Callosal histology (4 per group, panel d) showed fewer MBP-positive swellings (roughly 330 to 30/mm²) and more CAII-positive cells (roughly 330 to 710/mm²), while OLIG2-positive cell density fell. Bars are visual approximations. The total MBP-stained area showed no marked separation. CAII and OLIG2 are separately counted stains, unsuitable for deriving a fraction of double-positive cells. The late arm showed no clear improvement on rotarod or MBP-stained area (3 per group, panels f/h). Different motor tests, small cohorts and different endpoints preclude a quantitative treatment-by-age interaction. [Primary study and supplement](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-026-02489-5)
