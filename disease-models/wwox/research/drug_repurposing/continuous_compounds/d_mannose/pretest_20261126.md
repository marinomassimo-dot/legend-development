# D-mannose · prospective donor-versus-trafficking screen

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX

WWOX silencing has been linked to abnormal Golgi lectin binding and coatomer localization in a non-neural cell system. The candidate may change the supply of cytosolic mannose-derived donor. Require a discriminator that separates earlier N-glycan occupancy from subsequent processing and O-GalNAc maturation.

Fix the expression panel before reading the contrasts. Use HK1, HK2, MPI, PMM2, GMPPA, GMPPB, DPAGT1 and DPM1 for entry, donor handling and early N-glycosylation. Use MAN1A1 and MAN2A1 for mannose trimming, COG4, COG6 and COPB2 for coat/tether context, and C1GALT1 and C1GALT1C1 for the next step in mucin-type O-glycan processing. Read each individually. Compare the three held developmental WWOX bulk models with log2(1 + abundance) and all single-library omissions. A common sign requires the same direction across models and under every omission. RNA abundance supplies no donor measurement, transport capacity, glycan occupancy or protein localization.

The positive-control concept is limiting entry to mannose-6-phosphate with later steps intact, compared with a trafficking lesion. If donor supply is limiting in WWOX, raising measurable intracellular D-mannose-derived donor should improve N-site occupancy at a replete glucose baseline and improve the affected maturation readout. If mature Golgi processing limits the phenotype, donor labeling or improved early occupancy can coexist with persistent immature surface glycans and coatomer mislocalization. O-GalNAc maturation supplies an orthogonal endpoint because its initiating donor is UDP-GalNAc. A drop in ATP or nonspecific changes in surface protein abundance would weaken either apparent response. Compare absolute pool and isotopic enrichment and hold developmental stage and cell viability comparable.
