Units and preparations here are ng/mL and µM for adult human SPMS plasma and CSF MMF, qualitative LC–MS/MS adducts in mouse brain homogenate and human MS peripheral blood mononuclear cells, nominal µM parent in N1E-115 neuroblastoma-derived cells, and events per randomized participant in pediatric-onset MS. **Native RNA comparison.** The held mouse cortical and hippocampal bulk data show GAPDH +0.367 and +0.406 log₂ contrast (difference in means of log₂(TPM + 1)). The sign survives omission of any one input library. The human organoid comparison gives +0.091 on its own scale, with leave-one-out range −0.001 to +0.183. Pyruvate carboxylase RNA is lower in both mouse regions and in the organoid comparison (mouse Pcx −0.411 and −0.601, human PC −0.373). The P17 hippocampal marker-assigned nuclear pools instead show Pcx rising from 73.9 to 89.5 CPM in the astrocyte assignment, based on 108 and 154 UMIs. Assigned excitatory GAPDH has only 18 and 14 UMIs. These pools have one library per genotype and do not localize a functional deficit. The bulk-to-nucleus discrepancy makes a separately measured astrocyte anaplerotic readout useful within the test. Functional reserve, GAPDH adduct fraction and flux cannot be inferred from this RNA. **Inference.** A survival or NRF2 response and a fall in glycolytic reserve could coexist. The fibroblast comparison also permits a later rise in apparent reserve under the metabolite. Time, preparation and form may all affect the direction. A decrease in burst burden that travels with weaker baseline responses or depleted ATP would have a different interpretation from preserved recruitment and recovery. GAPDH protein abundance, adduct fraction and glucose flux measure different things. Under activity, activated leukocytes and WWOX neural circuits may have different rate-limiting steps.

**Discriminating prediction.** In the same stage-matched WWOX and control preparations, compare parent and metabolite separately with verified local analyte, vehicle and prechallenge baseline. At equal initial evoked recruitment, the acute cost explanation predicts a larger early loss of glucose-derived lactate flux and ATP recovery that scales with GAPDH adduct fraction, even if late ROS is lower. The protection explanation predicts improved postchallenge recovery and survival with preserved initial recruitment and little early flux cost. Include oxygen consumption and spike count per challenge to expose metabolic and activity-suppression alternatives. A glial or NRF2 perturbation only earns a second step if either signal survives this comparison. Extracellular adult-CSF MMF gives a measurement anchor for the model, with no assumption about intracellular concentration or a pediatric exposure.

**Decision and next step.** Keep dimethyl fumarate as the sole active molecule. Obtain an authorized open paper or data source for Gola's glycolysis and hyperexcitability assay concentrations, exposure duration, experimental units and measured firing. Look for measured glucose-derived flux from stage-matched WWOX or control neurons and astrocytes. The held RECON effort and [2-DG comparison](../two_deoxy_d_glucose/glycolytic_reserve_and_disposition_20261127.md) bring in fibroblast and native neural activity evidence, with the native genotype-by-exposure flux comparison still open. A focused independent review would add value after a neural assay exposure has been reconstructed or if the full primary source contradicts the abstract. The current coverage is scoped.
