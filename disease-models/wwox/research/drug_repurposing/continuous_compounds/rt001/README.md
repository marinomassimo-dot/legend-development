**Opening checkpoint, 27 November 2026.** RT001 is 11,11-dideuterated ethyl linoleate. This substrate-level intervention follows the vatiquinone disposition. It offers a separate probe of the WWOX peroxide hypothesis through membrane incorporation and altered hydrogen abstraction. The audit gives priority to a verified membrane denominator and an exact isotopologue control. [Vatiquinone disposition](../vatiquinone/exposure_and_disposition_20261127.md) · [RT001 identity and pediatric INAD experience](https://doi.org/10.1002/jmd2.12116)

**Starting clinical data.** The randomized Friedreich ataxia study reports 65 recruited and 45 completing the 11-month protocol. Its abstract reports a steady plateau by four months for deuterated linoleate and arachidonate in plasma and erythrocyte membranes, with no significant difference in the primary exercise oxygen-uptake measure. The INAD record includes two published cases and an open-label pediatric trial with concurrent external natural-history controls. These sources anchor reconstruction of setting and sampling. The accessible abstract and registry do not establish an effective target isotope fraction in WWOX neural membranes. [Friedreich trial abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36462055/) · [Pediatric trial registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03570931)

**First exposure anchor.** Brenna et al. Table 3 reports one ACOX1 gain-of-function case sampled after approximately one month. D2-linoleate as a fraction of total linoleate was 22.5% in plasma, 19.6% in erythrocytes and 17.0% in CSF. The corresponding D2-arachidonate fractions were 6.8%, 2.4% and 8.0%. CSF:erythrocyte fraction ratios were therefore 0.87 for linoleate and 3.33 for arachidonate in the same case. The authors estimated absolute CSF concentrations from typical lipid levels. Neither matched neural PE nor a population calibration for erythrocytes is available from that table. The public INAD registry record on 27 November lists 19 actual participants and no posted structured results. [Brenna et al., Table 3 and footnote b](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xphs.2020.08.019) · [INAD registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03570931)

**Enzymatic countercheck.** The detected arachidonate metabolite is 13,13-D2-AA. With purified enzymes and free arachidonate, the authors of the isotope kinetics study reported substrate-dependent maximal rates of 1.316 versus 0.007 µM/min for regular versus 13,13-D2-AA at human 15-LOX-2, and 11.07 versus 0.44 µM/min at COX-2. The respective ratios are 188 and 25.2. A simple no-interaction replacement model with a hypothetical 20% labeled fraction and a 188-fold reduction on those chains gives only a 19.9% drop in total oxidation, using \(f(1 - 1/188)\). This illustrative null assumes equal access, unchanged oxidation per unlabelled precursor, and no chain or enzymatic competition. Free-substrate kinetics and CSF fractions supply no numerical prediction for WWOX membrane PE. The comparison must measure unlabelled product per unlabelled precursor and may need orthogonal tests for COX/LOX product switching. [Brenna et al., analyte identity](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xphs.2020.08.019) · [Primary isotope kinetics, Table 1](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules23123331)

**Discriminating prediction.** Compare the exact deuterated and nondeuterated ethyl forms in a matched WWOX and control neural system after equivalent total precursor incorporation. Measure the isotope fractions separately for unoxidized and oxidized linoleate- and arachidonate-containing membrane species. Use absolute amounts and oxidized product per matched substrate, separating diacyl and ether PE. Follow labelled and unlabelled products, activity recovery and first recruitment. A selective fall in labelled product per labelled precursor with unlabelled oxidation and recovery unchanged would support chain-specific isotope protection only. A fall in coexisting unlabelled products with improved recovery and preserved early recruitment would support an effect beyond the substituted chains, once substrate displacement and uptake are accounted for. Quantify or independently vary initiation to distinguish fewer enzymatic initiation events from interruption of radical propagation. Similar improvement from the undeuterated ester would favor a lipid supply or remodeling explanation. [Primary autoxidation study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3437768/)

**Interpretation for reopening.** Qualify D0/D1/D2 product assignments before attributing light signal to unlabeled chains. Compare achieved membrane enrichment with fractions tested in model membranes. Keep the INAD concurrent external natural-history comparison separate from randomized comparisons.
