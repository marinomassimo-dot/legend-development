# RT001 | Isotope accounting and the WWOX conversion question

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Units and systems.** Hydroperoxyoctadecadienoic acids (HpODE) in photoirradiated linoleoyl-phosphatidylcholine large unilamellar vesicles, ng/mL. Isoprostanes and neuroprostanes in adult mouse tissue, ng/g where shown. WWOX neural transcript contrasts use individual mouse and organoid RNA libraries and mean differences in log₂(1 + normalized expression). Human trial outcomes use participants and the stated clinical endpoint units.

**Checkpoint.** RT001 remains a preclinical substrate probe. The exact isotopologue has an animal signal, and isotope-resolved artificial membranes show lower oxidation per unit of the remaining light substrate. The mammalian studies leave the size of total neural oxidation and the pathway to functional recovery unresolved. A pediatric WWOX efficacy claim is unsupported. [Opening hypothesis](README.md)

**A reproducible contrast.** Digitize bar means from Fig. 11B/C with published axes. At 50% light precursor, estimated light 9-HpODE per unit light precursor was **0.69×** the all-light comparison and 13-HpODE was **0.65×**. Pixel-only sensitivity is about 0.67–0.70 and 0.64–0.67. Total signals detected by those transitions were ≈0.55× and 0.49×, respectively. Both sets of estimates are approximate figure readings of means, without experiment-level errors or a propagation-specific attribution. An isotope shift out of the instrument's heavy channel cannot by itself account for a fall in the light signal per unit of light substrate. Changes in photosensitizer distribution and reactive species or chain initiation remain alternatives. [Author PDF, p. 9](https://repositorio.usp.br/directbitstream/368b7fc7-cc2e-4cc3-8bc7-6a88c1959d16/1-s2.0-S1011134422000392-main.pdf) · [Pixel anchors](figure_anchors.tsv) · [Calculated comparisons](isotope_audit.tsv)

**Visibility bound.** If a fraction f of an otherwise identical precursor pool is replaced by heavy chains, all products retain equivalent oxidation yield, and treatment-derived heavy products escape the reporter, the measured light signal falls to 1−f of control. More generally, if the heavy contribution to the light detector is nonnegative and light response is comparable, the ratio of light product per light precursor is at most r/(1−f), where r is the measured signal ratio. In Raefsky Fig. 8A the approximate AD-D/AD-H cortical F₄ reporter is 0.22. A **hypothetical** matched cortical DHA fraction of 50–70% gives an upper bound of 0.44–0.74 per light precursor and a reporter ratio of 0.50–0.30 from displacement alone. The WT reporter ratio of roughly 0.36 falls inside this displacement range. Actual same-animal precursor fractions for the measured product pool, analytical cross-talk, and heavy-product recovery are unknown. For the Q140 reporter 0.25 and a **hypothetical** 20% striatal ARA fraction, the same upper bound is 0.31. The source's 15–20% total-PUFA expectation is insufficient to apply that result in vivo. These comparisons motivate isotope-resolved validation and cannot recover total oxidation retrospectively. [APP/PS1 Figures 7 and 8](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5924637/) · [Q140 Figure 3](https://doi.org/10.1111/febs.14590)

**Competition sensitivity.** With 20% heavy free arachidonate, the simple one-enzyme substrate-competition model predicts light product per light substrate at 20 µM total to be 1.015× for human 15-LOX-2 and 0.999× for COX-2. Independently varying each Kₘ by the spread reported in Table 1 gives roughly 0.97–1.05 and 0.89–1.09. The table also varies total substrate from 5 to 100 µM. These rectangular scenarios are neither confidence intervals nor membrane kinetics. The assay needs matched product series and controlled initiation for a mechanistic attribution. [Primary free-substrate kinetics, Table 1](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules23123331) · [Replay](replay_isotope_audit.py)

## WWOX and clinical transfer

**WWOX conversion screen.** In P17 cortex (4 controls, 3 knockout) and hippocampus (2 controls, 3 knockout), the mean FADS1 shifts are −0.64 and −0.63 and ELOVL5 −0.68 and −0.53. All four retain direction under omission of one library. Week-15 human organoids (2 controls, 4 knockout) have +0.02 and +0.10 for those genes and both cross zero on omission. FADS2 shifts −0.23, effectively zero and −0.30, respectively. PLA2G6 is −0.43, −0.82 and −0.08 across the three comparisons. Counts and the complete mapping are in the replay. Tissue stage, cell mix and genotype differ. The data motivate measuring both conversion and membrane allocation. They do not show a PLA2G6 disease mechanism or flux equivalence to INAD. [Cortex](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) · [Hippocampus](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) · [Human organoids](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243) · [Expression table](wwox_conversion_expression.tsv)

**Cell-class countercheck.** Marker-assigned juvenile hippocampal nuclei in GSE158684 give one neuronal-deletion pool and one control pool. Fads1 rises by 1.99 CPM across assigned nuclei (+2.09 from class RNA-depth weights, −0.10 within classes). It is roughly stable in the excitatory class, rises in the inhibitory class and falls in astrocyte-enriched nuclei. Elovl5 falls by 3.90 CPM (−4.97 within assigned classes and +1.07 from class weights). Acsl4 falls by 15.56 CPM, with the drop concentrated in the within-class component (−16.13 CPM). Excitatory counts were 602/1,596 and 306/1,262 UMIs/nuclei for Acsl4, equivalent to 48.64 and 29.57 CPM. Astrocyte-enriched Acsl4 is 45.17 versus 29.06 CPM and the inhibitory rate is about 34–35 CPM. This comparison weakens a common FADS1 deficit and makes substrate allocation a useful rival to impaired conversion. It cannot assign flux, show a cell-autonomous effect or estimate between-animal uncertainty. Pools, ages and transcript metrics cannot be combined with bulk. [Assigned-class counts](nuclei/cell_types.tsv) · [Decomposition](nuclei/mixture.tsv) · [GSE158684](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158684)

**Friedreich ataxia.** The randomized 11-month NCT04102501 comparison used safflower oil placebo. Of 33 randomized to RT001 and 32 to placebo, 25 and 20 complete and appear in the posted primary endpoint. Completion was 75.8% versus 62.5%. The registry gives a change in peak oxygen uptake of +0.05 ± 0.04 and +0.08 ± 0.04 in its stated mL O₂/min/kg units (mean ± SE). Simple difference −0.03. Posted mixed-model p=0.5858. The posted safety pools each have one person with a serious adverse event and zero deaths. Baseline age category ≤18 contains 17 and 13, with no reported subgroup effect estimate. This result supplies a controlled functional counterexample to inferring benefit from peripheral lipid integration. The completion imbalance, non-isotopologue comparator and distinct disease bound its transfer. [Results, Participant Flow, Baseline, Primary Outcome and Adverse Events](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04102501?tab=results) · [Primary paper abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36462055/)

**Infantile neuroaxonal dystrophy.** The investigator abstract uses **concurrent natural-history controls**, not a randomized arm, with 19 treated and 36 controls. The modified Ashworth CAFS result is +6.42 rank points with adjusted p=0.14. The protocol uses a joint rank of function with death or pneumonia and describes a 19-per-group matching sensitivity. These analysis populations should remain distinct. The abstract reports exploratory mortality HR 0.112 (p=0.014) and progression-free survival HR 0.175 for death or pneumonia (p=0.021). The summary supplies no endpoint event counts, risk tables, confidence intervals or matching sensitivity results. Aligning time zero, eligibility and severity across two studies and checking event and censoring data are necessary before causal interpretation. The US record currently gives 19 actual participants and no structured results. [Protocol §§15.1–15.2](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/31/NCT03570931/Prot_SAP_000.pdf) · [Investigator conference abstract](https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.98.18_supplement.2343) · [Detailed conference abstract, p. 1](https://www.inadcure.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/INADSciCon21_Abstracts.pdf) · [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03570931)

**Discriminating WWOX observation.** At matched total precursor and evoked input, use labelled and unlabelled ethyl linoleate to separate metabolic incorporation from chain protection. Verify isotope-resolved substrate and product in intact diacyl and ether PE. A lower D2-GLA from equivalent intracellular D2-LA favors a first-step conversion constraint. Preserved D2-GLA with a drop in downstream isotope pools favors later conversion or turnover. Preserved D2-AA with lower isotope in the target PE favors allocation or faster class-specific removal. When target enrichment is comparable, a drop in light product per light precursor along with better recovery and preserved first recruitment is the required protection signal beyond isotope replacement. Separate initiation by matching or independently varying initial oxidation, and include all validated products with zero, one or two retained deuteriums. Include isotope cross-talk and blanks. An isolated raw reporter reduction with no paired recovery remains insufficient for escalation. An independent assay review would change the decision if raw reporter channels, a matched tissue precursor fraction or an existing corrected comparison could resolve the measurement ambiguity.

**Next checkpoint.** Search the held and public developmental data for paired linoleate-to-arachidonate flux and intact PE allocation. Follow the nuclear discordance with available cell-stage and compartment marker evidence only if it changes which isotope pools to measure. Determine whether validated measurement can resolve oxidized intact PE across the expected isotopes and distinguish matched functional recovery from lipid turnover. The current evidence supports a preclinical comparison with an exact chemical control. Pediatric feasibility remains conditional on measured target-lipid exposure, preserved function, and interpretable safety.
