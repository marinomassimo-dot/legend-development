# RT001 | Intact brain PE and isotope recovery

**Decision.** Retain RT001 as a conditional preclinical probe of lipid incorporation and protection. Defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. Public data define plausible target lipid species and show two feasible analytical approaches. The available measurements leave RT001-derived label recovery, native WWOX substrate and peroxide burden, and selective functional recovery unresolved. [Prior exposure and isotope-product audit](isotope_accounting_and_transfer_20261127.md)

## Developmental denominator

**Data.** Schmidt et al. profiled WT CD-1 mouse brain by direct infusion MSALL. The hemispheres at each individual postnatal age are five biological specimens. E10.5 samples each pool five embryos and are outside the P2–P21 calculation. Methods and Table S1 describe Orbitrap MS1, sequential 1-Da precursor windows and MS2 identification, with the same class-level internal standard for diacyl and ether PE. The comparisons use the workbook's reported mol% and exact molecular labels. They can reflect changes in either the numerator or the total lipid denominator. **Reproducible sensitivity.** For an isolated singly charged candidate PE +O₂ (negative ion `C43H77NO10P⁻`) and an illustrative oxidized arachidonate anion (`C20H31O4⁻`), the replay convolves natural carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen and oxygen isotope abundances. It solves the first three nominal mass bins for the molar fractions retaining zero, one or two tracer deuteriums. For matched nominal parent-to-fragment pairs, the model puts the tracer only in the selected fragment, assumes an isolated species and an effectively monoisotopic neutral loss, and uses the fragment isotope envelope. This requires experimental checks of fragment retention and precursor isolation. At the nominal intact-parent level a pure light sample gives apparent M+1 and M+2 shares of 29.80% and 8.29% of those three bins. For the matched-fragment scenario these are 17.67% and 2.52%. Without correction, those channels cannot be interpreted as tracer signal. [NIST isotope values](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/Compositions/stand_alone.pl?ascii=html) · [Replay output](intact_pe_isotope_sensitivity.tsv)

In a noiseless calculation both readouts recover the input fractions by construction. With independent ±1% relative perturbations of each peak area, an input D1 of 0.20% yields an unconstrained estimate of −0.75% to 1.16% from the intact parent, versus −0.24% to 0.64% for the matched-fragment scenario. For input D2 of 0.80%, the respective ranges are 0.33–1.26% and 0.70–0.90%. Negative estimates expose uncertainty near zero, and the calculation deliberately retains them. Across the same error corners the fragment blank can return an apparent D1 up to 0.45% and D2 up to 0.10%. Thus this ideal fragment scenario improves the low-D2 comparison and still gives substantial D1 overlap with blank. The numerical ranges use the assumed perturbations. They do not establish real detection limits, fragment yields, sample heterogeneity or a validated threshold. [Replay and assumptions](replay_intact_pe.py)

An exact D-for-H shift differs from one natural carbon-13 substitution by 0.00292191 Da. At m/z 798.5291 that corresponds to about 273,000 in m/Δm for one-width spacing. At m/z 335 it corresponds to about 115,000. D2 versus two carbon-13 atoms is twice that spacing, while D2 versus D1 plus one carbon-13 is the closer spacing again. Neither arithmetic threshold establishes recovery from unequal coeluting signals. A D0 molecule produced after complete loss of the tracer label also cannot be assigned to its precursor from deuterium alone. Retain that limitation in interpreting light product per light precursor. An orthogonal backbone tag or an empirical bound on full label loss would change that interpretation. [NIST masses](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/Compositions/stand_alone.pl?ascii=html)

## One discriminating comparison and reopening condition

**Hypothesis.** In WWOX, incorporated RT001 could protect neighboring unlabeled chains or affect early product initiation, in addition to protecting the substituted chains directly. Age-dependent PE allocation and isotope visibility can independently change measured signal.

**Experiment.** Qualify one empirically separable diacyl/ether PE pair and its matched native substrate before comparing WWOX with control. In a blinded single-matrix comparison, verify light blanks, recovery of known low label fractions, tracer purity and class/fragment identity. Only then compare the deuterated and ordinary ethyl linoleate forms at matched total incorporation and first recruitment, separating initial product formation from later accumulation. At comparable initiation and target enrichment, improved functional recovery alongside lower oxidation beyond the labeled pool would favor membrane protection beyond isotope replacement. If the effect is confined to labeled channels, estimate isotope-specific protection. If label recovery or first recruitment fails, the contrast cannot attribute protection. This separates an analytical adequacy check from the WWOX biological question. A core-facility review could change the decision once spectra of known mixtures and actual juvenile-matrix blanks are available.

**Disposition.** Park the WWOX transfer question pending a qualified isotope and matrix comparison and an untreated WWOX PE oxidation or recovery phenotype resolved by species, developmental age and tissue. No matched developmental genotype dataset of that kind was found in the inspected WWOX resources. Clinical readiness is limited by the missing neural exposure-response bridge, unresolved interactions with background antiseizure medication in WWOX and the clinical comparators' different diseases and endpoints.
