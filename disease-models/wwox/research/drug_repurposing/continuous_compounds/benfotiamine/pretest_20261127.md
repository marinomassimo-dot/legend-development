# Benfotiamine, central thiamine delivery versus a distinct response

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. Benfotiamine is the sole active compound after the [sarcosine checkpoint](../sarcosine/receptors_and_disposition_20261127.md). This is the oral S-benzoylthiamine O-monophosphate derivative. The comparison uses ordinary thiamine as a delivery control.

**Selection.** In the held thiamine dossier, extra extracellular thiamine failed to establish mitochondrial ThDP limitation in WWOX, and juvenile Wwox-null hippocampal nuclei had a descriptive reduction in the ThDP carrier transcript. Benfotiamine sharpens the delivery question because oral studies disagree on free thiamine reaching the brain, and neurological model responses may differ from changes in bulk brain ThDP. Prioritize distinguishing blood exposure from compartment-specific repletion and a parallel redox response. [WWOX thiamine checkpoint](../thiamine/compartments.md) · [Volvert brain and peripheral vitamer study](https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2210-8-10) · [Pan thiamine-derivative comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20385653/) · [Tauopathy and transketolase study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6077804/)

**Prediction before quantitative extraction.** If delivery rescues a WWOX-specific cofactor bottleneck, CNS free thiamine should rise first, followed by neural mitochondrial ThDP or native cofactor occupancy, coupled pyruvate/α-ketoglutarate oxidation and recovery of evoked activity. Compare benfotiamine with thiamine at matched blood free-thiamine exposure and matched basal substrate. A shift only in blood or in total brain free thiamine with stable matrix ThDP predicts little effect through this route. Recovery at unchanged verified matrix occupancy, with independently identified redox target engagement, favors the parallel explanation. Test cytosolic transketolase separately from mitochondrial enzyme activity. Account for tissue timing, cell type, recovery of the mitochondrial fraction and acute versus repeated exposure.

**First decision.** Digitize or extract brain, blood and liver thiamine, ThMP and ThDP in the original studies and compare directions, sampling time, organ preparation, and animal-level n. Audit the adult randomized trial for whether blood changes covary with its prespecified endpoint. Continue to a compartment-specific comparison only if a cited study or existing dataset can support a plausible differential test beyond the held thiamine dossier. Human blood changes alone leave pediatric WWOX priority unsupported. Investigate pediatric formulation, interactions and measured CNS exposure only where primary or regulatory evidence supports comparison. [Adult randomized trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7880246/)
