# Pantethine in WWOX loss of function

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · `context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`

**Disposition.** Park oral pantethine for pediatric WWOX trial priority. Keep it as a conditional substrate in a paired uptake and mitochondrial CoA comparison. Existing results separate oral processing, intracellular synthesis and mitochondrial import. No studied system establishes that oral pantethine supplies the WWOX neural free CoA pool or improves WWOX function. ## WWOX transfer and stopping condition

**Inference.** The fly CoA result, mouse respiratory result and pediatric product measurements cover different links. In the WWOX comparison, improvement can arise through pantothenate and residual kinase, extracellular formation of 4′-phosphopantetheine, direct intracellular conversion, or a change in respiration without recovery of free CoA. Conversion of the phosphorylated intermediate still requires COASY's final two activities. More cytosolic labeled CoA can coexist with a poor mitochondrial free pool, as the held human-cell SLC25A16/A42 results show. WWOX loss has yet to be shown to limit either the substrate or the carrier step in the relevant neural stage. Cell composition, workload and mitochondrial redox state can jointly affect the readouts. [Compartment data and limits](../d_pantothenate/transport.md) · [Acetyl-L-carnitine comparison](../acetyl_l_carnitine/stage_exposure_and_disposition_20261126.md)

**Discriminating observation.** First verify a deficit in free matrix CoASH and pyruvate oxidation or recovery in WWOX-deficient versus corrected neural cells, at matched maturation and workload. Compare measured parent exposure with pantothenate and cysteamine supplied separately and together at matched moiety equivalents. Track extracellular and intracellular parent, monomer and the phosphorylated intermediate early. A labeled 4′-phosphopantetheine reference tests the reaction downstream of this formation step. Dual-moiety signal in intracellular 4′-phosphopantetheine before dual-labeled CoASH, with a selective rise in the absolute free matrix pool and evoked recovery beyond matched products, would favor salvage. Reciprocal single-label inputs and early intermediate tracking test recombination. If only the phosphorylated reference improves the matrix pool, verified intact substrate was insufficient upstream of COASY in that preparation. If labeled CoA rises in whole cells while free matrix CoA stays low, test mitochondrial import or stoichiometric acyl trapping before attributing failure to precursor supply. A respiration change alone, or improved survival with activity suppressed, leaves the mechanistic question open.

**Pediatric feasibility and review.** The pediatric precedent is the studied oral capsule in 15 children (ages 3.5–13.6) with classical PKAN, in an uncontrolled trial with no significant W0–W24 change on either primary motor scale. A separate three-person PKAN pilot used a four-component combination and cannot isolate pantethine. The pediatric exposure audit supports measurable pantothenate with unresolved parent-specific sensitivity in the PKAN study and no quantified WWOX neural exposure. A future assessment would need the exact formulation, documented nutritional baseline, co-medications and route, a tolerability endpoint and an independently interpretable WWOX functional readout. Interaction data for background antiseizure drugs remain insufficient. Independent interpretation would be most valuable once a mammalian exposure or WWOX tracer result is available. Reopen for an independently verified measured neural exposure with a coherent CoASH-and-function response.
