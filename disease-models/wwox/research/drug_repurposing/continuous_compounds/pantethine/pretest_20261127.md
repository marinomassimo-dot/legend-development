**Discriminating prediction.** At matched maturation, workload and measured free exposure, a genuine salvage route predicts retention of distinguishable labels from both the pantothenate and cysteamine portions in intracellular phosphopantetheine and absolute newly formed mitochondrial free CoA. Measure early products, include the separated products at matched molar amounts, and account for redox interconversion, label recycling and medium breakdown. Recovery reproduced fully by pantothenate alone or by precursor-matched separated products lowers priority for the intact route. An oxidative or motor-marker effect without restored native mitochondrial free CoA remains compatible with a cysteamine-linked effect. Whole-cell CoA or an acyl-CoA rise alone requires a separate matrix and free pool comparison. A preferential recovery of pyruvate flux and evoked function with an increase in the measured free matrix pool would justify the next contrast.
