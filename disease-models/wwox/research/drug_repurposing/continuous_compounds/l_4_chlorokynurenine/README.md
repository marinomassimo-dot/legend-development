# L-4-chlorokynurenine (AV-101), local conversion and NMDA coagonist competition

28 November 2026 UTC · Continuous compound #209 · INV-05 · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. Units and preparations include adult human plasma and lumbar CSF (ng/mL), adult human auditory EEG, embryonic mouse hippocampal whole-cell current (µM, nM), adult rat prefrontal dialysate (nM and nM·min), rat amygdala kindling (nmol per local administration and number of stimulations), and transfected human HEK293 uptake (µM and pmol per million cells per minute). [Prespecified comparison](pretest.md) · [Opening checkpoint](first_comparison.md)

**Decision.** Retain AV-101 as a conditional preclinical comparator for local production of 7-chlorokynurenate and NMDA coagonist competition. Defer pediatric WWOX priority. The adult lumbar data leave local free exposure unresolved. Adult auditory EEG is evidence of a neurophysiological response in a separate population. A direct-metabolite rat comparison supports coagonist sensitivity in amygdala kindling, while an adult prefrontal comparison shows that a nominal concentration in the same range as an in vitro affinity can leave a particular circuit outcome unchanged.

## Replay and inference

Run `python3 -B replay.py` in this directory to reproduce [comparison.tsv](comparison.tsv). All inputs are explicit primary summary values rather than reconstructed individuals. Concentrations censored at the reported 1.4 ng/mL scale correspond to **6.26 nM** using 223.61 g/mol. The culture late and peak IC50 values at added glycine are **359 and 1,431 times** this scale. These are arithmetic ratios across preparations, with unknown correspondence to free exposure. [Source threshold](https://doi.org/10.1093/ijnp/pyaa025) · [Molar mass](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/1884) · [Current data](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2172523/)

For sensitivity, fixed glutamate and equilibrium at a single coagonist site give fractional reduction `q/(1+a+q)`, with antagonist `q = I/K_B` and coagonist `a = A/K_A`. This equates current with site occupancy for illustration. At `a=1`, the reported CSF threshold and ten times that threshold give **1.11% and 10.06%** reductions. At `a=5`, they give **0.37% and 3.59%**. Illustrative 20% reductions need **140 or 420 nM**, respectively. These are neither fitted human predictions nor equivalence margins. The model does not resolve local glycine and D-serine mixtures, multiple sites, changes in voltage or receptor kinetics, or the contribution of inhibitory cells. The direct prefrontal null comparison is one reason to require a native current readout alongside the network measure.

In Patel's Table 1, arithmetic ratios of means are **95.73×** for peak dialysate and **94.48×** for integrated exposure. The authors' 885× summary and the Fig. 5D AUC comparison of 107× are distinct quantities from these Table 1 ratios. Fig. 4 reports a separate 77 to 2,630 nM comparison. For the LAT1 fits at nominal 250 µM parent, the mixed-amino-acid to control uptake ratio is **0.404** holding fitted maximal velocity equal and **0.302** using the separate estimates. This is sensitivity to competition in the fitted cell system. Parent concentration alone cannot distinguish substrate competition, limited local formation and rapid product loss. Separate sampled appearance and washout of the active product would make that distinction testable.

**Competing mechanism.** The chlorinated 4-chloro-3-hydroxyanthranilate branch can lower quinolinate production. The isolated compound reduced precursor-driven quinolinate in awake rat hippocampal dialysate under an exogenous 3-hydroxyanthranilate challenge. Park's endogenous CSF analytes are sparsely detected and Murphy's blood comparison cannot resolve this branch in brain. Bulk WWOX transport-related RNA can motivate measurement of native coagonists. It does not establish excess quinolinate or a missing local coagonist pool. ## One discriminating comparison

**WWOX anchor.** The held cortical study found spontaneous bursts reversibly blocked by d-APV in P13–17 neuronal Wwox-deletion slices (51 bursts from five slices and three mice). That establishes an NMDA-dependent contribution in this preparation. The antagonist comparison leaves coagonist availability and a usable separation of late recurrence from first recruitment unresolved. Carry over the IT41 requirement to measure both in the same preparation. [WWOX primary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8609180/) · [Held source audit](../../ampa_nmda_discrim_20261108/output/README.md) · [IT41 comparison](../../ampa_comparison_it41_20261109/README.md)

**Hypothesis.** In an age- and region-matched WWOX preparation with elevated late recruitment, locally formed 7-chlorokynurenate preferentially reduces late NMDA charge and recurrent burden while first excitatory and inhibitory recruitment remain usable. Compare vehicle, precursor and direct metabolite with a local conversion control and an active-product bypass. Measure free product, glycine, D-serine, 4-chloro-3-hydroxyanthranilate and quinolinate in the same compartment. Keep synaptic drive and recording conditions matched. Evaluate the independent unit at animal or donor level, with cells and time bins nested within it.

**Discriminating prediction.** The parent and the direct product should converge at matched free product. Increasing coagonist availability should attenuate the reduction in native NMDA charge and late recurrence relative to the corresponding coagonist-only baseline. Early excitation and inhibition should retain their absolute responses. If parent reaches the preparation without measurable product and the bypass works, conversion or retention becomes the next question. If recurrence follows delayed quinolinate loss after coagonist matching, use a verified quinolinate restoration control before crediting the alternate branch. A change in recurrent burden coupled to lost initial excitation or inhibition fails the selective recovery test.

## Limits and checkpoint

Adult oral phase 1 exposure lasted up to 14 days across two studies with 86 total volunteers. Park Table 2 lists drowsiness or sedation in 7 of 17 active periods and 2 of 19 placebo periods. Differing crossover denominators limit this descriptive comparison. Age-specific pharmacokinetics, interaction with background antiseizure medication, cognition, development, usable initial inhibitory recruitment and free neural product are unresolved. The adult combination registry provides no posted results at this checkpoint. No controlled pediatric WWOX outcome was located. [Adult phase 1 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29229209/) · [Combination registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05280054)

The Park supplement was located but its content could not be retrieved through the examined free sources. Do not impute a CSF concentration or an inhibition estimate to individual participants. The independent human EEG and rat kindling and cortical controls already constrain opposite interpretations. A further review of the same summaries would not adjudicate local exposure. Reopen for analyte-specific paired central exposure with adequate sampling or a same-preparation WWOX coagonist-reversal comparison with the first response preserved.
