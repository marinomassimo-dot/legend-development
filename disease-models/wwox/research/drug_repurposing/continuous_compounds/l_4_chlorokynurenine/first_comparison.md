## First primary-text checkpoint

In Park et al., five participants supplied both plasma and CSF concentration data. The Discussion says CSF 7-chlorokynurenate was too low to resolve peak time in **four of five** after the first oral exposure. It gives approximate mean parent CSF concentration of **22 ng/mL** and describes most active-metabolite measurements as below the reported **1.4 ng/mL** threshold. For 7-chlorokynurenic acid (223.61 g/mol), that threshold corresponds to **6.26 nM**, calculated as 1000 × 1.4 / 223.61. A censored measurement is bounded only at the sampled time. These statements do not make all samples zero, give a group mean, bound unsampled peaks, or quantify free synaptic concentration. The analytical definitions and figure time bins remain to be reconciled.
