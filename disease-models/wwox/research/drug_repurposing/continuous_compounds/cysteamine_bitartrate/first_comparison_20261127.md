# Opening comparison for cysteamine bitartrate

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX

**Mechanistic anchor.** The corrected Fig. 6 of Jézégou et al. separates currents evoked by the cysteamine–cysteine mixed disulfide in a heterologous oocyte system (five oocytes from two batches) from retention of the mixed disulfide after PQLC2 silencing in cystinotic human fibroblasts. The cystine and mixed-disulfide panel is a representative experiment reporting three measurements. The correction concerns the displayed chemical structure. The transport mechanism is an indication-specific demonstration in cystinosis, with no documented WWOX lysosomal cystine burden. Also, retained mixed disulfide alone can indicate blocked export. The WWOX test therefore requires absolute cystine and an independent functional endpoint alongside early uptake and later clearance. [Primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23169667/) · [Corrected Fig. 6, p. 3197](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1300178110)

**Formulation and denominator.** The open-label randomized crossover was run in previously treated cystinosis participants aged 6–26 years (43 enrolled, 40 pediatric). The publication's Table 2 per-protocol analysis uses 38, while the current US product label reports 39 for its per-protocol analysis. The rounded difference in least-squares mean leukocyte cystine was +0.08 for delayed versus immediate release in both, with **95.8%** intervals of 0–0.16 in the paper and 0.01–0.15 in the label. The absolute means also differ across the analyses. Use the label's unit, nmol half-cystine per mg protein, for that tabulation. A separate report of 41 completing the controlled trial or the paper's 41-person ITT comparison does not replace either per-protocol denominator. These data compare cystine control by release formulation, with no CNS target engagement. [Original Table 2, p. 1116](https://cystinosis.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Langman-Clin-J-Am-Soc-Nephrol-2012.pdf) · [Official label, §14.2 Table 5](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d3a3ec28-f746-463a-bb92-3bc8826db09e)

In a separate labeled pharmacokinetic subset of 11 children aged 1–5 years with cystinosis, the mean plasma peak was 1.26 ± 0.86 mg/L. Using the cysteamine base molecular mass of about 77.15 g/mol, that is **16.3 ± 11.1 µM total**. Applying the label's overall 52% protein-binding estimate gives about 7.8 µM as a provisional arithmetic free-plasma comparison. There was no direct pediatric free-plasma or CNS measurement in that subset. The next comparison audits neural exposure, WWOX cargo evidence and the alternative whereby lysosomal amino-acid sensing itself changes with PQLC2 perturbation.
