# Cysteamine bitartrate in WWOX loss of function

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · `context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`

**Active molecule after the potassium citrate disposition.** Cysteamine bitartrate, comparing the active aminothiol and its inactive or metabolically distinct species by formulation and measured exposure. Pantethine processing left cysteamine as an alternative explanation for systemic effects. Here the candidate-specific question is whether a WWOX lysosomal substrate problem is tractable through the chemistry and export of the cysteamine–cysteine mixed disulfide. Compare this mechanism with broader thiol redox effects and the held lysosomal cargo and autophagy comparisons. [Pantethine disposition](../pantethine/species_compartment_and_disposition_20261127.md) · [Ambroxol lysosomal comparison](../ambroxol/cellular_turnover_and_disposition_20261126.md)

**Fixed comparison.** Separate an indication-specific cystinosin defect from any WWOX abnormality. Audit primary mixed-disulfide transport data, pediatric formulation coverage and leukocyte versus CNS exposure. Compare total cellular GSH, lysosomal free cystine, direct mixed disulfide, released sulfur moieties and completion of endogenous cargo turnover. Record where mitochondrial redox, axonal output or cell survival was also measured. Treat cystamine separately from cysteamine and distinguish salt mass from active base.

**Discriminating prediction.** Require excess lysosomal cystine or a reproducible burden-specific lysosomal defect in WWOX-deficient neural cells at a matched maturation stage. If cystine unloading is causal, cysteamine should raise the labeled mixed disulfide early, lower absolute lysosomal cystine and restore a dynamic cargo or evoked neural endpoint. The rescue should track verified mixed-disulfide export capacity. If thiol or GSH rescue occurs without cystine unloading or the effect persists independently of the exporter under matched exposure, a redox route is plausible. Include a thiol-matched reference, lysosomal pH, a non-depleting transport control and compartment and viability checks. Stop for lack of neural exposure or a WWOX substrate burden, or for more favorable lysosomal markers with unrecovered neural function. [Primary PQLC2 study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3528584/) · [Pediatric crossover](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3386675/)

**Competing lysosomal signaling route.** PQLC2 also participates in cationic amino-acid sensing. The planned export-dependence test must account for baseline lysosomal pH, growth signaling and cargo changes caused by perturbing the transporter itself, alongside the tracer clearance measurement. Audit the direct transport readout first, then compare cell-context-dependent cargo and amino-acid sensing studies.
