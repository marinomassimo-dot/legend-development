## A localized WWOX RNA change and three competing mechanisms

**WWOX data.** CTNS mean log2(1 + expression) changed **−0.46** in P17 mouse hippocampal bulk, with a leave-one-library-out range of **−0.71 to −0.33**, against **+0.01** in mouse cortex and **−0.03** in week-15 human organoid. Removing one library can change the sign in the latter two. In the mouse hippocampal nuclear pools, the direction reversed for excitatory assignments (**13.49 → 21.07** CPM, **167 → 218** UMIs), inhibitory assignments (**10.90 → 19.19** CPM, **18 → 36** UMIs), and astrocyte assignments (**16.42 → 25.57** CPM, **24 → 44** UMIs). SLC66A1/PQLC2 is modestly lower in both mouse bulk regions, near flat in human bulk and rises in the three selected mouse assignments. WDR41 and SMCR8 decrease in mouse bulk and increase in human organoids. CHAC1 (GSH degradation) decreases in human organoids, while both mouse omission ranges cross zero. The samples support region- and cell-context questions. They do not demonstrate low CTNS protein, impaired export or a cystine burden. [Bulk contrasts](contrasts.tsv) · [Pooled nuclear denominators](nuclei.tsv)

There were 4/3, 2/3 and 2/4 control/knockout libraries in cortex, hippocampus and organoids. The mouse bulk regions do not represent independent cohorts, the organoids share a parent line, and the nuclei use one pool per genotype. The classifier uses a 500-UMI filter and cell assignments based on marker scores. The archived symbols `Pqlc2`/`PQLC2` and mouse `3110043O21Rik` map to SLC66A1 and C9orf72, respectively. In the held WWOX stress study, lysosome-inhibitor-sensitive turnover of Bcl-XL/Mcl-1 was studied in fibroblast and cancer models under stress, with no neural lysosomal cystine or dynamic autophagic clearance result. [Mouse cortex](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) · [Hippocampus](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) · [Organoids](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243) · [Mouse nuclei](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158684) · [SLC66A1 nomenclature](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/54896) · [C9orf72 alias](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/73205) · [Held WWOX stress dossier](../../../fulltext_dossiers/PMID41677633.md)

1. **Cystine unloading.** In cystinotic fibroblasts, Jézégou et al. found that impairing PQLC2 trapped the cysteamine–cysteine mixed disulfide. In primary proximal tubule cells from 24-week Ctns-KO mice, cysteamine lowered lysosomal cystine and attenuated mTORC1 signaling. The Torin1 comparison attenuated signaling with cystine storage intact. Human CTNS iPSCs showed no comparable basal nutrient-response mTORC1 difference in the reported signatures. The magnitude and sign of any pathway response therefore need direct measurement in WWOX. [Corrected Fig. 6](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1300178110) · [Mouse proximal tubule study, Figs. 4h–j, 5, 6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-39261-3) · [Human CTNS iPSCs, Fig. S3B and adjacent Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7217405/)
2. **Signal from a luminal amino-acid substrate.** Amick et al. detected less starvation-induced co-immunoprecipitation of the C9orf72 complex with PQLC2 after 1 mmol/L cysteamine for 60 min in cystinosin-depleted cells. WT cells retained the interaction in the same comparison. Fig. 6A–C provides four independent experiments and a distinct endpoint from direct export. A second study's engineered epithelial RTK reporter regained degradation and lysosome properties after re-expression of PQLC2 variants defective for cationic amino-acid transport or WDR41 binding. PQLC2 depletion can therefore change the dynamic cargo endpoint through pH or other functions even before any drug. A concentration or cargo response after PQLC2 perturbation alone cannot identify export. [Amick, Fig. 6](https://doi.org/10.1083/jcb.201906076) · [BiDAC study, Figs. 7–8 and Discussion](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-59627-z)
3. **Thiol handling.** The held NAC screen indicates a possible alteration in cysteine supply and extracellular glutamate handling in WWOX. A cysteamine-associated change in GSH or evoked activity could arise without organellar cystine unloading. Cysteamine, cystamine and the mixed disulfide require separate chemical measurements because sample reduction can collapse these species into a cysteamine signal. [Held NAC comparison](../acetylcysteine/mechanism.md) · [Rat CSF reduction method, p. 3](https://www.cystinosisresearch.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/03/Dr.DohilIntraduodenalCysteaminePS.pdf)

## Decision test and feasibility

**Discriminating observation.** Compare matched-maturity WWOX and corrected neural cultures and a separate CTNS-deficient positive-control preparation. Before drug exposure, measure cystine in a validated lysosome-enriched fraction and whole cell, along with compartment recovery and purity, lysosomal pH, cargo turnover and matched viability. A high whole-cell cystine value without enrichment in the lysosomal fraction is insufficient. If a burden is found, directly follow early formation and later location or clearance of the mixed disulfide, free reduced cysteamine and independent cysteine/GSH pools. Compare a non-unloading thiol reference at matched measured exposure. Include a controlled PQLC2 perturbation with untreated genotype controls and verify pH, cell function and export independently. Transport- or WDR41-selective rescue controls become interpretable only after verifying the relevant transport/signaling separation in that neural preparation.

The unloading prediction succeeds if a reproducible WWOX lysosomal excess falls, the mixed disulfide forms and is cleared rather than trapped, and a dynamic cargo endpoint **and** evoked neural recovery improve in the same model at attainable exposure. A signal confined to mTORC1 or GSH gives those branches separate support. Substrate depletion without functional recovery, functional improvement without unloading, or effects only above the attainable exposure range would each redirect the hypothesis. If matched WWOX cultures show no excess, close the unloading question for those conditions. Another review would change the current decision if it brings WWOX compartment chemistry, paired human CNS exposure or low-exposure WWOX rescue with measured chemical species.

**Pediatric feasibility.** Delayed-release capsules and granules have U.S. precedent from age one in nephropathic cystinosis. The label documents formulation and food or gastric-pH constraints that are pertinent to tube feeding, ketogenic diet and background treatment. In the open-label, cysteamine-naïve pediatric safety group, the label reports vomiting in 13/17 and diarrhea in 6/17. The label also records reports of seizures, lethargy and other CNS symptoms with immediate-release exposure and notes neurological complications in untreated cystinosis. Neither uncontrolled rates nor the CNS reports yield WWOX attributable risks. The adult Huntington randomized comparison has a published primary motor difference of −1.5 ± 1.71 points (P = 0.385) at 18 months, based on 96 randomized participants in the article abstract. The EU registry's separate outcome row lists 45 + 44 analyzed and labels that analysis post hoc. This is cross-indication context with no verified CNS exposure or direct transfer to pediatric WWOX. [Official label, §§2.6, 5.5, 6.1, 7.1 and 8.4](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d3a3ec28-f746-463a-bb92-3bc8826db09e) · [Huntington primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28436572/) · [EU trial results](https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/ctr-search/trial/2010-019444-39/results)

Viltz and Trauner reported an association between starting treatment before age two and better scores on some cognitive measures in cystinosis. Their nonrandomized comparison by age of initiation leaves systemic disease control and a direct CNS drug effect inseparable. Visual-motor performance remained impaired in both treatment groups relative to controls in the published abstract. [Cystinosis primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23462307/)
