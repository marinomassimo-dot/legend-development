## DATA and INFERENCE — the substrate and product do not encode a fixed functional difference

Mouse Wwox overexpression in kidney cells changed androsterone disappearance. The plotted readout and background reactions prevent a quantitative assignment to 5α-androstane-3α,17β-diol formation. The substrate and product are both GABA-A modulators. This C19 C17-ketone conversion is chemically distinct from C3 reduction of dihydroprogesterone to the C21 steroid allopregnanolone. Plasma Allo-S measures yet another analyte. [Prior substrate and species audit](../../ganaxolone_steroid_flux_20261123/README.md)

Carver and Reddy compared each ligand separately against the same 1 µM GABA response in adult female WT dentate granule cells. The numerical product-minus-precursor difference ranges from **−0.17** at 1 µM to **+0.80** at 3 µM. At 0.1 µM the two means are near the GABA-only reference (0.92 and 0.94 of that current), while ganaxolone is 1.61 and allopregnanolone 2.50. These comparisons use published point estimates at the same nominal concentrations, with four to eight cells per steroid and concentration in Table 2. They supply a counterexample to assuming the product is always the stronger signal. The separate ligand arms do not test an actual mixture or the significance of the difference between the two ligands, and the upper concentrations include conditions where direct gating may contribute for some steroids. The experiment used whole-cell clamp and adult WT dentate cells. No extrapolation to WWOX tissue concentrations, cortical receptor composition or inhibitory firing follows. [Carver and Reddy, Table 2, Fig. 6B–C and methods](https://doi.org/10.1124/jpet.115.229302) · [Current replay](tonic_current_contrasts.tsv)

A reduction in androstanediol with retained or raised androsterone does not fix the sign of the GABA-A response. Even a drug-induced increase in tonic current can occur when the native steroid mixture is functionally adequate. Conversely, the net effect of extra conductance on a neuron depends on `E_GABA`, resting potential, threshold and recruitment. The juvenile WWOX cortical sIPSC observation used high intracellular KCl and pooled events. It does not measure endogenous steroid tone, native `E_GABA`, receptor capacity or event detection independent of amplitude. Across independent cells and animals, record tonic conductance and inhibitory efficacy separately. ## Clinical boundary and feasibility

Marigold randomized 50 to ganaxolone and 51 to placebo, ages eligible 2–21 years, median age 6, with molecularly confirmed CDKL5 deficiency. At 17 weeks, median reductions in 28-day major motor seizure frequency were **30.7% and 6.9%**. The Hodges–Lehmann location shift on the reduction scale was **27.1 percentage points** (95% CI 9.6–47.9 in the public accepted manuscript, 9.5–47.9 in the FDA review after sign alignment; p=.0036). This shift is a different statistic from subtracting the two arm medians. For the distinct ≥50% responder endpoint the counts were **12/49 and 5/51** (Fisher p=.0643). One treated participant lacked baseline seizure data. The observed risk ratio is **2.50** for response and **0.837** for failure to reach response. The 2024 pooled RR of 0.90 has unresolved event coding and orientation in the public abstract alone and is excluded from the WWOX rationale. The single-study replay illustrates how changing the event changes the direction of the ratio. [Primary Marigold report, Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(22)00077-1) · [FDA statistics, Tables 3 and 7](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2022/215904Orig1s000StatR.pdf) · [Event coding replay](marigold_event_coding.tsv) · [Pooled abstract, intake lead only](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38606458/)

## HYPOTHESIS and discriminating experiment

**Hypothesis.** WWOX loss changes a specific local steroid mixture, lowering functional inhibition in a defined immature neural cell class. Ganaxolone may bypass the lost receptor input without restoring the chemistry. The competing explanation is ordinary agonist-dependent GABA-A potentiation superimposed on altered release, receptor state or chloride.

**One comparison.** In matched WT, WWOX-LoF and WWOX-rescued neural preparations, measure the named C19 substrate/product and C21 unconjugated steroids with controlled isomer identity and a quantified free fraction. Separate cortex and hippocampus at comparable developmental ages, with animal or independent culture batch as the biological unit. In a common receptor preparation, compare the measured WT and LoF steroid mixtures for tonic conductance at fixed GABA, including selective restoration of the depleted analyte to the observed control concentration. In matched native cells, compare vehicle, physiological add-back and ganaxolone using the same starting drive, quantified free exposure and measured `E_GABA` by perforated patch. Record effective inhibitory recruitment and late firing as separate outcomes. A labeled substrate/product observation is required for the narrower claim about C19 enzymatic flux.

**Predicted decision.** A reproducible LoF mixture deficit that follows the mixture, closes after physiological add-back in a common receptor context, and is bypassed by ganaxolone with effective inhibition preserved would promote a replacement mechanism. If there is a mixture difference without a functional difference, or ganaxolone potentiates both mixtures similarly and the selective add-back does not close a deficit, retain a symptomatic rationale. If comparable tonic currents lead to dissimilar spiking when chloride or recruitment differs, prioritize the circuit mechanism. A measured deficit and similar drug responses can coexist, so the diagnostic contrast is the physiological add-back rather than the drug response alone. Keep the first experiment mechanistic and classify a missing analyte or below-limit value explicitly.

**Reopen condition.** Bring forward replacement only for a matrix-matched chemical and functional result that survives the add-back or availability control. Independent review has the greatest marginal value once such data could change the sign of the transfer. The separate Tideglusib comparison is next in the coordination queue, where a selective engagement result could change interpretation of the existing lithium experiment.
