# Does a cobalamin-processing lesion leave recruitable propionate flux?

**Prespecified before reading assay values.** Use Forny et al. Extended Data Fig. 2 and its primary workbook. Select the two low-MMAA-expression samples listed in Supplementary Table 1 (MMA206, MMA209) independently of the size of their assay response. Compare paired intact-cell propionate incorporation with and without hydroxocobalamin, absolute change and fraction of a recorded concurrent control where available. Check enzyme activity with adenosylcobalamin to identify capacity that an intact-cell assay alone cannot assign. Report missing values. Use the severe MMUT group as a descriptive comparator, check whether subgroup labels were themselves defined by cobalamin responsiveness, and keep supplementation of extracts distinct from treatment of intact cells. The WWOX question is whether modestly lower neuronal MMAA RNA predicts a reversible local flux deficit. These fibroblasts serve as a comparator with a different disease.

**Secondary question, specified before opening the MMAA rows.** Compare these two lines with the available unaffected lines on the authors' processed transcript and protein scales. Rank each line against controls rather than treating source expression units from fibroblasts and WWOX nuclei as interchangeable. Report missing protein separately.

**Within-reference check, prespecified after identifying the two severe outliers.** Restrict to unaffected fibroblast lines with both paired incorporation measurements and a measured MMAA transcript. Correlate transcript level separately with paired gain and treated incorporation using average-rank Spearman correlations, reporting the sample count and the range after omitting one line at a time. This examines the direction within the available reference range. Neither a small reference cohort nor rank association can identify the response threshold of WWOX neurons.

**Results.** Extended Data Fig. 2C measures intact-cell incorporation in pmol/mg protein/16 h. The two outliers changed from 1,500 to 3,800 and 670 to 3,250 with hydroxocobalamin: 2.53- and 4.85-fold, or +2,300 and +2,580 in the original units. The median of nine unaffected treated cultures was 6,367 (range 3,308–14,679); the two treated values were 59.7% and 51.0% of it. One lies inside the measured control range; the other is just below its lower end. A median is a reference point, not a threshold for normal function. The two cases are nested in the 60 other-MMA lines. Their individual classification rests on expression and functional or transcript evidence: Supplementary Table 1 specifies cblA by complementation for MMA206 and unamplifiable MMAA cDNA for MMA209. The main text describes complementation confirmation collectively. [Original paper and Extended Data Fig. 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9886552/) · [Paired flux summary](flux.tsv)

**Capacity and circularity.** Source Data Table 1 records extract mutase activities of 141 and 470 pmol/min/mg protein without and with adenosylcobalamin for MMA206. Both raw values are unavailable for MMA209; secondary zero-valued fields cannot replace them. The 120 severe-category MMUT lines show median paired incorporation change −16; the 30 residual-category lines show +1,001. Cofactor responsiveness contributes to this biochemical classification. Group separation cannot independently validate a biomarker predictive of drug response. The Results mention 123 non-increasing samples in the MMUT group; the figure and source table classify 120/30 for the incorporation analysis. We use the explicit source labels and counts. We did not interpret the spreadsheet's Simult fields as matched per-line controls, since a line-specific denominator was not established. The nine measured unaffected references are drawn from 20 enrolled. [Primary phenotypic workbook](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC9886552.1/42255_2022_720_MOESM4_ESM.xlsx)

**Scope of the WWOX analogy.** On the authors' reported transcript scale MMA206 was 0.113 and MMA209 zero; all 19 unaffected samples with expression were between 0.664 and 2.067. The processed protein matrix had no MMAA_HUMAN row. In nine unaffected lines with RNA and paired incorporation measurements the rank correlation with drug-induced gain was −0.20 (leave-one-out −0.714 to −0.048); with treated incorporation it was −0.25 (−0.786 to −0.119). These small correlations supply no calibrated response threshold. Dataset units, cell types and severity differ from WWOX nuclei. The measurements in these two extreme outliers support a recruitable biochemical pathway in selected non-WWOX fibroblasts, while leaving its availability in WWOX unmeasured. **Decision.** Park hydroxocobalamin for an unselected WWOX pediatric trial. Reopen for a WWOX-specific reduction of intact-cell propionate incorporation corrected by hydroxocobalamin, with retained extract mutase capacity under added adenosylcobalamin and recovery of a matched neural phenotype. Measure remethylation alongside incorporation to test the alternative of a broader intracellular B12 shortage. A disproportionate ratio rise from a very low baseline or a high circulating B12 concentration alone does not adjudicate this prediction.
