**Reproduce the initial exposure comparison.** With molecular mass 381.372 g/mol, the conversion to nM is concentration in ng/mL × 1,000 / 381.372. The published mean maximum values convert to 3.67 nM free plasma and 5.24 nM CSF. The 91 nM fibroblast IC₅₀ is 17.35 times the adult CSF mean maximum on the reported scales. This quotient compares unlike compartments and assays. It cannot predict occupancy, local inhibition, safe feasible exposures or efficacy. [NIST molecular mass](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C169590425) · [Dembo et al., abstract](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/1303969) · [Fibroblast study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16080279/)

```sh
python3 -c 'mw = 381.372; print(*(round(x * 1000 / mw, 2) for x in (1.4, 2.0)), round(91 / (2.0 * 1000 / mw), 2))'
# 3.67 5.24 17.35
```
