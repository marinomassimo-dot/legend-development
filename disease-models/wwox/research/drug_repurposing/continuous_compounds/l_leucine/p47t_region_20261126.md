# L-leucine · regional sensitivity in adult Wwox P47T

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · comparison with a partial-loss allele

The developmental deletion and organoid comparisons showed lower LARS1 and BCAT2 RNA. The same 12-gene panel was checked in the independent adult P47T count deposit without extending the panel. LARS1 **did not meet the stability rule in any region across both normalizations**. Each region's total-count analysis crossed zero on at least one mouse omission. The result narrows the original lower-LARS1 observation to the developmental preparations evaluated. A low-capacity charging phenotype remains to be tested directly. [Pretest](p47t_pretest_20261126.md) · [GSE215425 count deposit](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE215425)

The oxidation branch showed a stronger regional contrast. Sex-adjusted log₂ BCKDHA differences were **−0.19** in hippocampus, **−0.12** in parietal cortex and **+0.32** in cerebellum. DBT changed in the same respective directions (**−0.12, −0.27, +0.23**). Signs held in those three regions under both whole-library and upper-quartile normalization, including each one-mouse omission. The prefrontal decreases in both genes were sensitive to normalization and omission. BCAT2 was lower in prefrontal and parietal cortex, equivocal in hippocampus and higher in cerebellum, by the same criteria. BCAT1 and SLC3A2 were higher with stable signs in all four regions. SLC7A5 was higher in the three forebrain regions, with an equivocal cerebellar result. These are opposite region-specific RNA changes in the same cohort, not four independent experiments or measured enzyme activity, BBB transport or oxidation. [Total-count contrasts](p47t_panel.tsv) · [Upper-quartile sensitivity](p47t_panel_upper_quartile.tsv)

The deposit contains 5 WT and 5 P47T mice per forebrain region and 4 WT and 3 P47T in cerebellum. Age was reported only as a broad adult range (150–280 days); sex adjustment does not control age, gliosis, seizure burden or cell composition. The adult P47T knock-in, developing mouse deletion and human whole organoid contrasts differ by allele, age and cell mixture. The prior audit found a discrepancy between hippocampal count-derived changes and the paper's summary DEG sheet, so the hippocampal claims here refer to the public count file alone. [Held sample identity and scope audit](../../p47t_it12_20261031/README.md)

**Prediction and decision.** A single whole-brain leucine deficiency model is a poor working assumption. Start with hippocampal and cortical neurons at a defined developmental stage, measuring both leucyl-tRNA recovery and carbon conversion. If P47T is used for transfer, treat forebrain and cerebellum as a regional countercheck: lower early oxidative product at matched labelled α-ketoisocaproate in forebrain with a preserved cerebellar response would favour a genuine region-specific oxidative constraint. Concordant changes across regions would favour a post-transcriptional or shared energetic explanation over the RNA-based prediction. Any redirection of leucine requires function and compatible exposure. The adult result adds no pediatric dosing or benefit evidence. [Charging and ATP control](charging_capacity_20261126.md)
