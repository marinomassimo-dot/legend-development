# L-leucine · separating substrate rescue from charging capacity

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

## Comparators that change the interpretation

**Human LARS1 deficiency.** In Kok et al. a fibroblast lysate from one case with two LARS1 variants had **27%** of control charging activity in a separate standardized assay with nominal **300 µM** labelled leucine and **1 mM** ATP with added total yeast tRNA. Fig. 1c measured relative impedance after 72 hours at **0, 0.5, 1, 5 and 100 µM** nominal extracellular leucine. The patient–control contrasts were marked significant at 0, 0.5 and 1 µM and marked nonsignificant at 5 µM. Each donor's 100 µM result is its own normalizer. The reference bar cannot establish equal absolute proliferation between genotypes, and there is no above-reference arm. Two control donors were measured once each and the patient twice; these comparisons supply no independent patients. The study provides a positive control for sensitivity to deprivation. It does not test incremental leucine in an already replete neural preparation. [Kok et al., pp. 2202–2204, Fig. 1a/c and corresponding Methods](https://pure.rug.nl/ws/files/203713710/1_s2.0_S1098360021051753_main.pdf)

The same case switched from isoleucine to leucine after nine months of protein fortification and erroneous isoleucine use. Oral intake reportedly increased within one month and tube feeding ended after six months. Continued protein fortification, intake and time confound the switch, and outcomes were observed in a different genetic disease. This is pediatric feasibility evidence for a monitored single-compound intervention. It supplies neither a WWOX dose nor a central concentration. [Kok et al., p. 2205, patient treatment](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41436-021-01249-z)

**Use the correct biochemical constant.** Chen et al. report apparent leucine Kₘ values of **2.97 and 7.50 µM** in direct tRNA aminoacylation with different recombinant human cytosolic LeuRS preparations. Their separate amino-acid activation assay reports **45.6 µM**. Importing that value into full charging would inflate the assay's concentration scale about **15.4- or 6.1-fold**. The apparent ATP Kₘ for charging also varied across preparations (**112 and 773 µM**). These constants describe the listed cell-free assays. They cannot calibrate cytosolic free leucine, apparent Kₘ or effective charging capacity in a WWOX neuron. Measure ATP in intact cells and match ATP when assaying intrinsic lysate charging. [Chen et al., Tables 2–3 and aminoacylation methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3017609/) · [Reproducible calculations](charging_bounds.py) · [Results](charging_bounds.tsv)

## WWOX-specific decision

The human peripheral observations do not support a universal substrate deficit. In one seizure-free child with homozygous p.Ile136Val and unconfirmed functional WWOX loss, the plasma panel gives leucine **130** (reference **47–155**), isoleucine **78** (**31–86**) and valine **254** (**64–294**), with units absent in those rows. Two sisters homozygous for p.Trp44* had reported normal plasma amino-acid and urine organic-acid panels. They are one family. Individual leucine and α-ketoisocaproate numbers, nutritional state at sampling and a central compartment comparison are unavailable. The normal panels constrain a simple systemic deficit, while leaving an activity-dependent or compartment-specific defect open. [Sukkar et al., Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9193507/) · [Elsaadany et al., both case narratives](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4975905/)

The held WWOX interactome offers a reason to keep the oxidative route in the assay. BCKDHA appears at score **0.21** in the tagged HEK293T purification. DLD has a lower score **0.11**, while DBT appears in the pathway drawing without a corresponding prey in the underlying list. This is neither evidence of a complete complex nor a direction of change. It gives a mechanistically discrete measurement to place alongside the tRNA branch, consistent with the regional RNA results. [Held interactome review, Table 1 and Fig. 5](../../../deepdive_manifests/PMID30619736.json) · [Regional and cell-class RNA](README.md)

**Protein-level boundary.** In the held TMT proteome of WWOX-silenced human pulmonary artery endothelial cells, five of the 12 prespecified proteins can be located by exact deposited symbol, including LARS for LARS1. LARS1 changed by **+0.09 log₂** (reported BH-adjusted P = 0.0086) and DLD by **+0.13** (0.049). BCAT1 (**+0.10**), BCKDHA (**+0.18**) and SLC3A2 (**+0.10**) did not meet a reported adjusted P < 0.05 cutoff. LAT1 and BCAT2 are among the seven symbols without an exact row. To reproduce, extract the supplement with `pdftotext -layout` and read exact protein symbols in rows 193, 195, 456, 933 and 1708. The fold-change field is treatment minus reference. This is relative protein abundance in pulmonary endothelial cells with acute knockdown. It neither establishes BBB transport nor predicts the direction of neural protein abundance or activity. [Supplemental Table S1, PDF pp. 4, 10, 19 and 35](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.25137089) · [Held source and cell type](../../../deepdive_manifests/PMID38563965.json)

**Quantitative discriminator.** In a deliberately simple saturation model with the same apparent charging Kₘ, fix ATP and tRNA availability and let KO maximal activity be 70% of control. If control free intracellular leucine sits at one Kₘ, doubling it in KO gives **93.3%** of the control baseline charging rate. It would take **2.50-fold** to match baseline. If control starts at four Kₘ, doubling gives **77.8%**, and the KO limit even with unlimited substrate is **87.5%**. The 70% ratio is a sensitivity scenario unrelated to RNA abundance. The model is an initial-rate bound, with no inference about whole-cell steady-state charged tRNA or the human dose.

**Next comparison.** Measure free intracellular leucine and leucyl-tRNA charging (including recovery after a controlled demand) in the same stage-matched WWOX and corrected preparations at replete baseline and above it. Confirm ATP and use a matched-ATP lysate titration to distinguish an intact-cell energetic constraint from lower maximal charging capacity. Measure uncharged tRNA and a second large-neutral-amino-acid pool and charging readout so that competition can be distinguished from leucine rescue. In parallel, measure the labelled C6 α-ketoisocaproate precursor and C5 oxidative product with enrichment time courses. An extracellular concentration–response shift that disappears on the measured intracellular concentration axis favours substrate access. A shift that persists with a preserved plateau motivates testing apparent affinity and cofactor availability. A depressed plateau at verified intracellular saturation and matched assay ATP favours reduced charging capacity. Normal charging with a disproportionate precursor/product divergence favours the oxidative branch. Record evoked output and ATP recovery alongside the molecular effects.

**Disposition.** Keep the L-leucine trial priority low. Its rationale currently rests on a reversible mechanistic comparison, with no resolved mammalian WWOX CNS substrate or flux measurement located in this pass. The next useful data check is a public WWOX developmental proteome or metabolite table with matching lineage and time point, or an independent fly sample crosswalk. Existing peripheral and deprivation experiments alone cannot settle the choice.
