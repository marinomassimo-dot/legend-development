# L-leucine · prospective comparison

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX

The general-population WWOX-locus association concerns a combined leucine/isoleucine feature and does not identify a WWOX loss-of-function substrate shortage. This screen asks if transport, leucyl-tRNA and mitochondrial catabolism give coherent hypotheses in held knockout data. Select the following genes before looking at their contrasts.

- Transport: SLC7A5 and SLC3A2 (LAT1 complex). Bulk tissue cannot assign endothelial versus neural transport.
- Nutrient sensing and protein synthesis: SESN2 and LARS1. The two transcripts do not identify mTORC1 activation or tRNA charging.
- Branched-chain amino acid entry and oxidative control: BCAT1, BCAT2, BCKDHA, BCKDHB, DBT, DLD, BCKDK and PPM1K. Flux also depends on exchange, mitochondrial access and phosphorylation.

Compare KO minus control in the three held brain models (P17 mouse cortex, P17 mouse hippocampus and human week-15 cerebral organoids), after log2(1+value) within each library using the established loading and identity code. Report every gene and the range with any one library omitted. A shared expression direction needs the same sign in all three models and each omission. Differences between gene transcripts do not estimate enzyme stoichiometry, phosphorylation or compartment concentrations.

**Predictions fixed before the expression check.** At a replete baseline, a substrate-limited WWOX preparation should show increased intracellular labelled leucine, improved leucyl-tRNA charging or translational recovery, and preserved recovery of ATP and evoked function. A transport ceiling predicts increased extracellular exposure without the corresponding intracellular increment. An oxidation limit predicts label in intracellular leucine and α-ketoisocaproate with little downstream carbon recovery and possible displacement of other large neutral amino acids. A rise in nutrient-sensing phosphorylation without restoration of activity-linked translation, basal amino acid balance and recovery is insufficient. Use isotopic flux and separate physiological and generic protein-deprivation conditions. A bulk RNA contrast can constrain hypotheses but cannot select a patient trial.

**Follow-up fixed after the bulk result and before the nuclear read.** In the held P17 hippocampal nuclei, report counts and CPM within the established excitatory, inhibitory and glial marker assignments. Test whether the bulk LARS1/BCAT2 decreases persist within the neuron classes or vary with assignment, and whether a candidate can be interpreted when there are very few positive nuclei. There is one pool per genotype. This is a descriptive compartment check against the bulk data, without between-animal inference or claims about the blood–brain barrier.
