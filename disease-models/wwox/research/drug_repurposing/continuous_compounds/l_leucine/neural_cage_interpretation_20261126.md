# L-leucine · differentiated human CAGE comparison

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX

The published GSE126075 gene table has one library per condition in human progenitors and one per condition after differentiation. The held deposit audit found unequal library composition and failed to confirm WWOX reduction in progenitor CAGE. In that arm the deposited WWOX gene values are **2.90 and 3.35 TPM**, control and shWWOX. Report that arm descriptively and avoid interpreting its large target shifts as WWOX effects. This is partial knockdown in an H9-derived culture with no gene add-back. [Primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31543760/) · [Held library audit](../../hnpc_gse126075_it5_20261029/RESULTS.md)

The differentiated shWWOX library has lower raw LARS1 signal (**8.75 → 3.09 TPM**) and a lower WWOX signal (**2.54 → 0.52 TPM**), but all 12 panel genes fall on the uncentered log-ratio scale. The median difference is **−1.34 log₂** in the 6,859 measurable background genes. After centring, the LARS1 difference is **−0.03** with a 0.5 TPM offset or **+0.01** with an offset of 1. BCKDHA changes from **−0.60** uncentred to **+0.73** centred. These results explain why the apparent panel-wide decrease supplies weak pathway-specific evidence. Median centring itself cannot determine whether a transcriptome-wide change is technical or biological. The denominator sensitivity and lack of replication leave LARS1 charging and oxidation unresolved. [All contrasts and aliases](neural_cage.tsv)

In the held experimental study, a trace of resazurin reduction shows a group difference of about **−32%** near 270 minutes. This readout can depend on viable cell number, per-cell reducing activity and assay conditions. The paper provides no oxygen flux, KIC concentration, ATP recovery or normalization to viable cell count. It cannot resolve whether energy stress limits leucyl-tRNA charging or whether leucine oxidation is impaired. Direct charging and label-tracing remain the discriminators. [Held source review, Fig. 2](../../../fulltext_dossiers/PMID31543760.md) · [Prior kinetic checkpoint](charging_capacity_20261126.md)

The script subtracts the median log ratio of genes with at least 1 TPM in both libraries. The reported library-dependent contrast is a descriptive sensitivity.
