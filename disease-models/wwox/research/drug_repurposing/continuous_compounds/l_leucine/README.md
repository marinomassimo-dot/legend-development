## Result in held WWOX data

Two of 12 predeclared genes met the direction criterion in all three models and after omitting any one library. The changes were downward. For **LARS1**, the KO–control mean of log2(1 + abundance) was **−0.17** in P17 cortex, **−0.29** in P17 hippocampus and **−0.21** in week-15 human organoids. For mitochondrial **BCAT2**, it was **−0.31, −0.56 and −0.02**, respectively. The small human BCAT2 effect gives almost no evidence for the magnitude seen in hippocampus. Cytosolic **BCAT1** rose **+0.62** in hippocampus, and **BCKDHA** fell there (**−0.82**) and in organoids (**−0.43**). BCKDHA crossed zero under a single-library omission in cortex. **SLC7A5** tracked opposite regional directions despite stable signs under library omission, with **−0.15** in cortex, **+0.23** in hippocampus and **−0.86** in organoids. A shared LAT1 transcript deficit is unsupported. Organoid and tissue abundance mixes cell classes and says little about BBB transport. The neuronal-deletion mouse series contribute 4/3 and 2/3 control/KO libraries, respectively. The human assay has 2/4 libraries, with two KO lines. Single-library omissions leave line and animal dependence unresolved. Mouse abundance is gene-summed transcript TPM and human abundance is CPM from the processed count matrix. Values are descriptive within each system, and neither an RNA change nor its cross-model sign measures tRNA charging or mitochondrial catabolic flux. The archive uses Lars/LARS for current LARS1. ## What would discriminate mechanism

The lower LARS1 leads to two opposite predictions. If the limiting factor is **intracellular leucine relative to demand**, added leucine at a verified replete baseline should increase leucyl-tRNA charging and translation after activity along with functional recovery. If reduced ligase capacity limits charging, intracellular leucine can rise with little change in charging. Phosphorylation of an mTORC1 readout can increase in either case. Measure pool size and charging rather than using phosphorylation alone. BBB or plasma transport effects should be separated from neural uptake using measured extracellular and intracellular isotope enrichment.

For the catabolic branch, distinguish formation of **M+6 α-ketoisocaproate** from its oxidative decarboxylation to **M+5 isovaleryl-CoA** after supplying [U-¹³C₆]leucine. A lower product signal at comparable precursor enrichment can prioritize a block downstream of transamination. Measure pool sizes and enrichment through time before calling it a flux defect. The hippocampal BCAT1/BCAT2 contrast also motivates asking whether leucine nitrogen transfers to glutamate or glutamine while oxidation lags. Primary rat experiments found different brain/plasma isotopic enrichment ratios for carbon- and nitrogen-labelled leucine (**0.61 and 0.23**, respectively). These are tracer enrichment ratios under feeding, not brain/plasma concentration ratios. Preserve competing neutral amino acids, evoked recruitment and ATP recovery as outcomes. A larger response in a deliberately leucine-deficient positive control cannot establish WWOX specificity at a replete baseline. [Sakai et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14720211/) · [BBB transport comparison in a distinct genetic disorder, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27912058/)

## Pediatric feasibility and next decision

A nine-month oral L-leucine pilot in transfusion-dependent Diamond–Blackfan anemia enrolled **55**, with **43 evaluable** and a reported age range **2.5–46.1 years**. It supplies a precedent for single-compound oral use in a younger population with a different indication. It gives no infant or WWOX neural exposure in the abstract. The WWOX-associated population feature combines leucine/isoleucine and cannot tell whether a WWOX-LoF neuron has too little or too much leucine. [Hematology trial, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33025707/) · [Metabolite association](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8025395/)

**Disposition.** Park L-leucine for an unselected pediatric WWOX trial. In fly heads the assigned peak fell under Aβ42, with no stable decrease in LacZ. Preserve the conditional-stress experiment with matched exposure and no claim of a universal substrate deficit. [Results and reopening criterion](fly_reintegration_20261126.md)

**Earlier assessment.** The deprivation comparator and the charging bounds favour measuring intact leucyl-tRNA recovery and ATP before asking whether leucine is limiting above a replete baseline. Pair those measurements with a carbon precursor/product readout. [Charging-capacity checkpoint](charging_capacity_20261126.md) A resolved mammalian leucine/KIC or matching protein dataset at the implicated stage would strengthen that comparison. Seek pools or flux that separate leucine from isoleucine and affect the assay choice. A standard-matched fly peak was verified for Wwox overexpression, and the knockdown spectral crosswalk has unresolved label conflicts. [Fly identity and data availability checkpoint](fly_feature_20261126.md) Stop for a common supply claim if the only positive evidence remains a combined population feature and discordant RNA. Request an independent review if gene mapping, an assay denominator or precursor enrichment changes that choice.
