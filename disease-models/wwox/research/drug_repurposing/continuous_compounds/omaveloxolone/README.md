**Discriminating prediction.** At measured unbound parent exposure, compare WWOX and control neural preparations under matched initial recruitment and sulfur availability. A post-activity recovery mechanism predicts a time-ordered increase in an NRF2-dependent functional readout, then improved glutathione or respiratory recovery, ATP reserve and later evoked recruitment, with preserved native inhibition. If sulfur availability is limiting in WWOX, NRF2 activation may produce a reporter response while de novo glutathione synthesis and evoked recovery stay flat at the same background cystine. Independent verification of NRF2 dependence and early recruitment should separate either pattern from a direct reduction of excitability or generalized suppression. Record neuronal and glial compartments separately. An externally measured brain concentration merely anchors feasibility, and the acquired post-insult model establishes a separate dissociation to check.
