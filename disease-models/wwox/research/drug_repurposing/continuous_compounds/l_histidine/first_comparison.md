# L-histidine opening comparison of tissue and extracellular readouts

28 November 2026 UTC. Continuous compound #205, INV-05. context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. [Prespecified comparison](pretest.md). Evidence below comes from primary abstracts. Biological dispersion, complete methods and curves remain to be verified.

| Study and sample | Reported result | Meaning for the next assay |
|---|---|---|
| Prell et al., rat whole-brain tissue following acute injected L-histidine | Tissue histamine +102% versus saline at 1 h in one study arm. Tele-methylhistamine did not increase, and tele-methylimidazoleacetic acid was unchanged. In a separate higher-exposure comparison after a histidine-decarboxylase inhibitor, total tissue histamine was +112% versus inhibitor plus saline while both methylated metabolites did not increase. Inhibitor alone had lowered tissue histamine by approximately 30%. [PMID 8863498](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8863498/) | Total tissue histamine can rise without a concordant methylated product increase. The inhibitor did not eliminate baseline tissue histamine. The residual effect could involve incomplete suppression, a separate pool or another route and does not establish an HDC-independent mechanism. |
| Lozeva et al., male Wistar rat, one week of oral L-histidine versus control | Nine animals per group. Tissue histamine increased 40–70% in cortex, hypothalamus and remaining brain, with no detected change in cerebellar or plasma histamine. Cortex and hypothalamus tele-methylhistamine did not change. [PMID 12865089](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12865089/) | Repeated oral exposure also separates tissue abundance from the metabolite proxy. The study did not directly measure extracellular histamine or match wake state. A storage-pool interpretation is plausible. |
| Itoh et al., anesthetized rat medial hypothalamus microdialysis | Unperturbed output was 7.1 pg histamine per 30 min, with 7.8 nM obtained using in vitro probe recovery. Pharmacological H3 antagonism approximately doubled output and a distinct HDC inhibitor experiment lowered it by about 70%. No histidine loading was tested in this abstract. [PMID 1704419](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1704419/) | An independent extracellular readout can separate compound effects from tissue storage. The numerical recovery estimate cannot be used as a juvenile WWOX target. |

**Interim consequence.** Rank direct extracellular or isotope-resolved product data above total tissue histamine. The decisive comparison must retain vigilance, input recruitment and tissue compartment. A lack of change in a single methylated metabolite can also follow altered turnover and is insufficient to rule out a change in extracellular release.

**Next step.** Recover arm-level concentrations and biological denominators from the complete primary protocols where accessible. Look for route- and age-matched parent, labeled product and dialysate after L-histidine itself. The first established gap is the unmeasured bridge between increased total tissue histamine and functional release following precursor exposure.
