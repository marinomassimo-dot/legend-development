# L-histidine, extracellular histamine and circuit recruitment

28 November 2026 UTC. Continuous compound #205, INV-05. context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. [Prespecified comparison](pretest.md) · [Opening comparison](first_comparison.md) · [Reanalysis](lick_comparison.tsv).

**Disposition.** Retain L-histidine as a conditional preclinical precursor comparator and defer pediatric WWOX priority. A rat study directly measured higher extracellular striatal histamine following acute peripheral L-histidine. Neuronal source, transfer to a juvenile oral exposure and relevance to spontaneous WWOX activity remain unresolved. A repeat-exposure kindling result changed in the unfavorable direction, while the adult mouse feeding phenotype is broad and was measured without concurrent histamine photometry.

## What moves after precursor

| Primary result | Compartment and denominator | Decision boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Acute striatal dialysate response | Cumming et al. Fig. 4, four freely moving rats, 20-minute collections. Striatal histamine outflow rose to approximately **180% of each rat's pre-exposure baseline within one hour** after injected L-histidine. Observations continued for four hours. The separate basal pooled estimate of 3.8 pg/min (n=14) is not the four-animal treatment baseline. [Primary paper, pp. 1798–1801](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1471-4159.1991.tb02083.x) · [Author-posted Methods and Fig. 4 caption](https://www.academia.edu/46977212/Characterization_of_Extracellular_Histamine_in_the_Striatum_and_Bed_Nucleus_of_the_Stria_Terminalis_of_the_Rat_An_In_Vivo_Microdialysis_Study) | This is a positive extracellular result. A separate local tetrodotoxin check did not reduce basal dialysate histamine, and calcium omission reduced baseline by only about 20%. Those checks do not identify the source of the precursor-induced increment. Histamine accumulation, changed clearance, a non-neuronal pool or incomplete local blockade remain possible. No EEG-defined vigilance comparison or juvenile WWOX result. |
| Tissue abundance and methylated metabolites | In the rat whole-brain acute study, tissue histamine rose 102% at 1 h versus saline without a detected increase in tele-methylhistamine. After partial HDC inhibition and higher precursor exposure, tissue histamine still increased relative to inhibitor plus saline. After one week of oral L-histidine, another study reported 40–70% higher tissue histamine in cortex, hypothalamus and remaining brain (nine male Wistar rats per arm), with no measured increase in cortical or hypothalamic tele-methylhistamine. [Prell et al. abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8863498/) · [Lozeva et al. abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12865089/) | These data are compatible with a storage or slowly turning-over pool. A single metabolite concentration can also remain stable while production and removal change. They do not directly contradict a transient striatal dialysate rise in a different preparation. |
| Precursor access | After IV radiolabeled histidine in adult mice, Wang et al. found similar plasma profiles (11 WT and 12 Pht1-null in Fig. 6) and no detected CSF difference, while early regional parenchymal signal was 28–48% lower in null animals at 5 min (Fig. 7, 3–6 animals per plotted comparison). The study used a vascular tracer correction and found roughly twofold higher PEPT2 expression in the knockout. [Wang et al., Methods, Results and Figs. 6–7](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5173442/) | Matched plasma or CSF did not guarantee matched parenchymal uptake. The radioactivity readout and compensatory transporters prevent assigning an unbound parent concentration or an exclusive physiological route. This is an independent adult transport experiment, without a product-resolved WWOX result. |
| Induced seizure direction | In Wada et al., the acute exposure delayed bilateral forelimb clonus in already amygdala-kindled rats without a detected change in behavioral stage or electrical afterdischarge duration. Daily exposure at the same study dose before induction facilitated both behavioral and electrographic kindling development. Yawata et al. reported delayed episodes in another genetic mouse model. [Wada et al. abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8938266/) · [Yawata et al. abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15548424/) | Induction, expression of an established evoked seizure and spontaneous burden are distinct. The repeat-exposure result is a direct reason to track electrographic worsening if the precursor is studied as a probe. Between-study direction cannot be attributed to central histamine without a matched compartment and functional measurement. |

## Independent raw-data check of the feeding control

Volcko et al. report hypothalamic histamine photometry during feeding and a separate injected L-histidine experiment in eight food-restricted adult mice. The latter uses a counterbalanced within-animal crossover and behavior only. The two public photometry manifests share **zero animal IDs** with the L-histidine/saline sessions. Thus neither the paper's fiber-photometry transients nor a reanalysis of the photometry archive can supply the missing simultaneous target-engagement measurement for these eight animals. [Volcko et al., §§2.2–2.4, §3.3 and Fig. 6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12232117/) · [CC BY 4.0 data](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13981427)

The 16 raw event files independently reproduce the published mean total, **572.4 to 244.9 licks/session**. There are exactly 100 trials per session and the accepted lick events match the authors' counts for every animal, condition and concentration. Trials receiving at least one lick fell from **60.8 to 32.9 per 100**, with a decrease in eight of eight animals. Licks conditional on a nonempty trial fell from **9.28 to 6.74**, with a decrease in six of eight. Leaving out any one animal preserves both mean differences. An initiation change contributes to the drop in total alongside the conditional count. These are behavioral decompositions at the animal level. The data cannot distinguish satiety, malaise, differences in motivation or motor engagement, or central histamine signaling. The unmeasured state is especially material when interpreting motor events or feeding in developmental encephalopathy. [Reproducible table](lick_comparison.tsv) · [Replay code](replay.py)

## One discriminating comparison in WWOX

**Held evidence.** The WWOX histamine-pathway screen found weak or discordant bulk receptor changes in juvenile mouse and human organoids. The samples do not resolve activity in histaminergic nuclei or demonstrate a precursor deficit. Arousal is an independent determinant of cortical recruitment, so all-state event averages can shift through state occupancy. Food intake and engagement should remain separate endpoints. [Held WWOX transcript and vigilance audit](../pitolisant/vigilance_exposure_and_disposition_20261127.md)

**Hypothesis and test.** With verified juvenile neural exposure, L-histidine changes a WWOX electrographic phenotype through neuronal histamine synthesis. Compare vehicle and precursor in the same juvenile genotype and matched littermates. Use a retained-label histidine tracer to measure precursor and newly formed histamine in both plasma and the relevant cerebral extracellular compartment. Measure a methylated product and label enrichment separately to assess conversion and removal. Stratify by prespecified EEG/EMG vigilance state and preserve the first excitatory and inhibitory responses at comparable intake, temperature and activity. Compare early and repeated exposure because kindling induction and established responses separated. Use an independent perturbation that verifies engagement in the neuronal HDC compartment, with baseline-state and precursor-exposure controls. Parent delivered without an engagement check leaves a null comparison ambiguous.

- **Supports a neuronal conversion mechanism.** At matched state and first recruitment, a WWOX-relevant electrographic effect follows newly labeled extracellular histamine, with local chemical recovery verified. Both increments lose their precursor dependence with the independent neuronal HDC perturbation. Keep genotype specificity separate from evidence of conversion.
- **Lowers the hypothesis.** The label reaches brain or total tissue histamine rises without a corresponding extracellular signal. The network result follows vigilance, nutrition or initial recruitment, persists despite verified neuronal HDC perturbation, or worsens with repeat exposure. A rise in extracellular histamine with no network effect supports target engagement alone.

## Pediatric exposure and reopening

The adult graded oral study enrolled healthy participants aged 21–50 in two overlapping cohorts (40 in each, 27 completed both). Fasting plasma histidine rose at the upper exposures in the shared analysis. The short-term study noted changes in urea nitrogen and, at selected exposures, ferritin, AST or zinc despite values remaining within reference intervals. It supplied no pediatric CNS exposure, concurrent antiseizure medication interaction or chronic WWOX safety result. Pediatric histidinemia cohorts describe an inherited metabolic state rather than interventions generating a matched neural exposure. Appetite and spontaneous EEG burden would both matter in pediatric feasibility if the preclinical gates are crossed. [Gheller et al. abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32766885/) · [Newborn-screening cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8669938/)

Reopen once an age- and route-relevant parent exposure has demonstrable local conversion, a matched-vigilance WWOX network effect and a repeat-exposure countercheck. A second reader could change the decision by identifying a paired juvenile extracellular measurement or overturning the compartment assignment. Current tissue abundance alone leaves that priority deferred.
