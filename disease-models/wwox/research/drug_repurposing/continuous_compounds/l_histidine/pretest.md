# L-histidine, brain histamine synthesis and vigilance

28 November 2026 UTC. Continuous compound #205, INV-05. context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. L-histidine is the sole active compound after tezampanel #204 landed. Prior studies in this programme covered H3 receptor antagonism with pitolisant and histidine as a possible product of carnosine hydrolysis. The present question concerns the parent amino acid as a histamine precursor and the function of the resulting central signal. [Held H3 comparator](../pitolisant/README.md) · [Held carnosine comparison](../l_carnosine/kinetics.md)

**Question.** At verified free neural histidine exposure, does conversion through histidine decarboxylase increase central histamine turnover and shift a native WWOX circuit phenotype at equivalent vigilance and first recruitment? Competing explanations are a vigilance-mediated shift, transport competition with other amino acids, peripheral effects, and direct histidine or carnosine-related chemistry. A brain homogenate histamine increase alone does not demonstrate neuronal synthesis or receptor engagement.

**Comparison.** First align primary results for plasma and central parent, local histamine and N-methylhistamine, route, age, region and sampling window. A stable isotope tracer with parent and product tracked in the same preparations would distinguish precursor entry and conversion from a change in turnover of existing histamine. Compare circuit readouts against a validated pathway control and at matched wake states, with early excitation and inhibition measured separately. A reproducible effect at verified local exposure should track the central conversion readout and lose its increment when histidine decarboxylase is independently disabled in the relevant compartment. If it follows vigilance, persists without conversion or is explained by shifts in competing amino acid exposure, lower the histamine-specific claim.

**First falsifier.** Look for primary paired measurements that separate brain histidine from histamine, turnover and extracellular release. Verify age dependence and look for a matched independent negative or a dissociation. Even a strong adult transport result cannot support pediatric priority without juvenile exposure, tolerability and recruitment data in a relevant system.
