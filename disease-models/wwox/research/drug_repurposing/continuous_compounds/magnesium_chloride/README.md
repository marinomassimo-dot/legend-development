# Magnesium chloride and voltage-dependent NMDA current in WWOX

**Opening question.** A prior sulforaphane culture comparison induced epileptiform activity by removing magnesium. Reversal of that manipulation can occur in control neurons. Select magnesium chloride as an oral mineral candidate and identify the effective species as extracellular ionized Mg²⁺. Establish whether the WWOX preparation has an abnormal response within a physiological range. Adult intravenous sulfate studies can constrain compartment interpretation only; oral chloride requires its own pediatric exposure evidence.

**Prediction before data extraction.** With extracellular calcium, osmolality, chloride balance and starting membrane voltage matched, a modest measured Mg²⁺ increment above the same physiological baseline should preferentially reduce abnormal recurrent activity in WWOX while preserving evoked transmission. A benefit restricted to restoring Mg²⁺ after artificial deprivation supports a generic challenge effect. Similar depression of normal evoked transmission in both genotypes would also reduce priority. Include untreated controls, specify ionized versus total Mg and distinguish voltage-dependent current from broad changes in transmitter release.

**First comparison.** Recover free Mg²⁺ if measured, otherwise nominal Mg/Ca concentrations, from the original WWOX network and electrophysiology preparations. Compare with deficient-medium models and clinical ventricular versus lumbar CSF measured after systemic magnesium, recording timing, ionized fraction and formulation. The starting clinical leads are PMIDs 9339404, 11801809 and 15753761. These are separate adult intravenous studies. Pediatric oral exposure, a baseline deficiency subgroup, WWOX target engagement and functional benefit are open.

**First baseline check.** In Repudi et al., human oligocortical spheroids were cut with nominal 7 mM magnesium chloride and 0.5 mM calcium chloride. Baseline recordings used the subsequent artificial CSF with 1.5 mM magnesium chloride and 2.5 mM calcium chloride. These stages must remain separate. Breton et al. used nominal 3 mM magnesium sulfate and 1 mM calcium chloride for mouse slice cutting, followed by 1.3 mM magnesium sulfate and 1.5 mM calcium chloride for incubation. The 1 mM magnesium chloride described in the whole-cell pipette is intracellular. These two WWOX preparations provide magnesium-replete nominal baselines for a comparison; neither passage measures extracellular ionized Mg²⁺ or a genotype-specific response to increasing it. The next comparison is how far systemic supplementation can shift measured ionized CSF magnesium, retaining route, magnesium species, time and comparator.
