**The proliferation denominator changes the proposed falsifier.** Visually calibrated Fig. 7E,H,M of Shen et al. (version PMC2171688.1) shows CC1-positive nuclei at approximately 29% versus 19% of DAPI nuclei, NG2-positive cells at 26% versus 65% of DAPI nuclei, and NG2/BrdU double-positive cells at roughly 12 versus 11 per unit surface area (PBS, valproate). Pixel coordinates, axis ticks and linear calibration are in figure7.py and figure7.tsv. These are bar-top estimates; decimal places in the replay table are computational precision. One pixel corresponds to about 0.30 or 0.63 percentage points in E or H and 0.18 units per area in M. Neither between-animal variability nor a confidence interval can be recovered from these means alone.

A stable number of BrdU-positive progenitors per area can coexist with a lower BrdU labeling fraction among all progenitors when the NG2-positive pool grows. Dividing the published group averages to infer a measured cycling fraction would require compatible fields, absolute denominators and sampling times. Consequently a maturation delay remains plausible, but panel M alone cannot establish equal cell-cycle exit rates. In WWOX, the decisive measurement includes BrdU-positive/NG2-positive nuclei **among NG2-positive cells in the same fields** plus a time-resolved fate readout of new sheaths per surviving progenitor. Record absolute nuclear and axonal density as parallel controls. The plotted tissue is rat corpus callosum, whereas the WWOX nuclear baseline was mouse hippocampus.
