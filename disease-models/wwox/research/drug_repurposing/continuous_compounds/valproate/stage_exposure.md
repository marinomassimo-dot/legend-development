Shen et al. compared four subcutaneous injections in neonatal rats beginning at P6, P9 or P19, with harvest at P8, P11 or P21. Figure 6 assigns six pups per treatment per window, three per arm for the RNA readout. Figure 7E,H uses total DAPI-positive nuclei as denominator for CC1 and NG2. Total density was 1,488 versus 1,563 nuclei/mm² for valproate and control (−4.8% descriptively); this neither establishes equivalence nor excludes selective lineage loss. CC1 fell and NG2 rose, while the BrdU comparison did not identify a difference. Figure 9 distinguishes recovery of CC1 labeling from recovery of ensheathment after withdrawal: many cells had acquired CC1 while few had begun myelinating in the illustrated treated tissue. No conduction measurement or free concentration was recovered in these comparisons. The juvenile WWOX stage fractions use an oligodendroglial-lineage denominator; they cannot be directly subtracted from the rat marker fractions. Quantified Fig. 7 bars and the denominator for proliferation still warrant image review. [Primary study, Figs. 6–9](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2171688/)

A pediatric serum binding model uses Ct = Cf + 130 Cf/(7.8 + Cf) in µg/mL. Inversion at illustrative Ct = 50 and 100 produces a greater-than-twofold change in estimated Cf. These are model outputs from population parameters, not measured concentrations in WWOX, rat brain or oligodendrocytes. Unbound serum exposure and its time course need independent measurement in the developmental experiment before assigning clinical relevance. The reported rat dose alone cannot establish an exposure margin. [Pediatric pharmacokinetic study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18211614/)

Decision: prioritize the lag from acquisition of a differentiation marker to formation of new sheaths and recovery of conduction after washout, at matched initial lineage stage and measured free exposure. Marker normalization alone would leave functional recovery unresolved. Compare with activity-matched seizure suppression in WWOX. No clinical recommendation follows from the rat exposure or binding calculation.
