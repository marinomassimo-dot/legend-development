# D-biotin and glial maturation in WWOX loss

**Prespecified before opening the WWOX expression results.** D-biotin is a cofactor shared by acetyl-CoA carboxylases and pyruvate carboxylase. A limiting fraction of biotinylated ACC1 in surviving WWOX-deficient oligodendrocytes could reduce fatty-acid synthesis during new sheath formation. Alternatively, extra biotin could act through astrocyte pyruvate carboxylation and substrate supply, or improve survival without restoring myelin function. A positive control with an established biotin-dependent defect can verify the response assay. Untreated transcript abundance cannot establish cofactor occupancy.

**Discriminating prediction.** At matched viable mature oligodendrocyte number, measure intracellular free biotin, biotinylated/total ACC1 and PC, labeled acetate incorporation into fatty acids, separately attributed pyruvate carboxylation in astrocytes, new sheaths per differentiated cell and evoked conduction. A selective ACC1 occupancy and lipid flux response preceding improved conduction would support the glial lipid mechanism. Improved survival with unchanged per-cell sheath production and conduction would lower priority. Predefine uptake/recycling/ligation (SLC5A6, BTD, HLCS), carboxylases (PC, ACACA, ACACB, PCCA, PCCB, MCCC1, MCCC2), lipid synthesis and the myelin context (FASN, MBP). Measure proteins and flux before inferring an enzyme deficiency. Compare three untreated WWOX datasets, then check within hippocampal cell classes.

**What the untreated WWOX data support.** Pyruvate carboxylase RNA falls in all three tissues and survives every single-library omission. Mouse cortex falls from 49.6 to 38.1 TPM and hippocampus from 21.1 to 14.2 TPM (mouse symbol Pcx). Human organoids fall from 71.7 to 55.3 CPM (PC). The pattern for ACACA, HLCS and SLC5A6 reverses between the two mouse tissues and the human organoids. For example, cortical Acaca falls from 35.1 to 14.5 TPM while organoid ACACA rises from 140.8 to 208.4 CPM. Assay units and developmental contexts differ. Cortex, hippocampus and organoid comparisons have 4/3, 2/3 and 2/4 control/KO libraries. The leave-one-library checks are descriptive. **Cell class changes the inference.** In hippocampal nuclei assigned to astrocytes, Pcx rose from 73.9 to 89.5 CPM (108 versus 154 molecule counts), even as aggregate Pcx fell from 31.95 to 28.46 CPM. Acaca rose in oligodendrocyte precursors (209 to 232 CPM), then fell in cells classified as myelin-forming (377 to 210) and mature (336 to 225). The myelin-forming class has 186 control and 49 KO nuclei. Each genotype supplies one pooled library. Stage distribution, expression per nucleus, RNA recovery and intrinsic function may differ. These data change the first comparison to stage-matched oligodendrocytes with independent biological replication. Total PC, holo-PC and pyruvate carboxylation still merit measurement in parallel in astrocytes. **Published culture results and denominator check.** Cui et al. studied postnatal rat oligodendrocyte precursors in biotin-containing medium. Fig. 2B reports 2.796 sheaths per cell on synthetic fibers in control medium, 4.110 at 2.5 µg/mL and 3.480 at 250 µg/mL. Relative changes are +47% and +24%. The observed means lack a monotone concentration response. Fig. 2A’s printed control is 18.21%, while the Results give 16.31%; both give 27.58% at 250 µg/mL. The absolute difference therefore ranges from 9.37 to 11.27 percentage points depending on which reported baseline is used. The plot is normalized within comparisons, so its fold scale should not be substituted for a ratio of overall means. Fig. 2A also includes a significant 1.25 µg/mL response (26.82%), below the 2.5 µg/mL range emphasized in the Results. Fewer than 5% of cells expressed MBP. The per-cell finding weakens a simple cell-number explanation for wrapping. The assays leave compact axonal myelin, conduction and ACC1 activity unmeasured. Estimated ATP-linked oxygen consumption increased under a separate high-concentration condition. Seven experiments for the wrapping assay; per-cell points are nested observations. [Primary study and figure](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0233859) · [Calculation](comparisons.py) · [Results](comparisons.tsv)

**Pediatric exposure benchmark and a positive control.** The lowest figure-positive added concentration, 1.25 µg/mL, is 5.12 µM at a molecular mass of 244.31 g/mol. In one 14-month-old with inherited HLCS deficiency on enteral biotin, simultaneous fasting trough CSF and plasma total avidin-binding concentrations were 1.0 and 1.8 µM. Plasma peak was 4.9 µM. The measured CSF trough is approximately fivefold below the lowest figure-positive nominal addition, but neither a peak CSF concentration nor intracellular free D-biotin was measured. In 11 independently speciated diagnostic CSF samples, biotin averaged 42 mol% (individual range 15–70%) of the measured species. Applying this fraction directly to the supplemented child would introduce an untested assumption. The child had preserved maximal HLCS activity with an increased substrate requirement. WWOX RNA cannot establish an analogous biochemical defect. Formulation and absorption mattered in this case, and the background treatments included other metabolic support. No age-matched WWOX exposure-response, interaction data with background antiseizure medication or controlled developmental outcome were identified in this comparison. [Child with HLCS deficiency, methods and results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2630166/) · [CSF speciation, Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3097470/)

**Controls for a real deficiency.** In primary rat astrocytes, experimentally sequestering biotin reduced the three measured mitochondrial carboxylase activities to about 15% of control. The medium accumulated roughly tenfold more 3-hydroxyisovalerate despite preserved cellular growth. In an independent multi-cell-type study the remaining mitochondrial activities after low-biotin culture were 2–11% of maxima in the most sensitive neural and other cell classes and 12–28% in astrocyte/glioma cultures. These author abstracts describe imposed deprivation. They motivate paired sensing of carboxylase occupancy and an orthogonal metabolite, with the same baseline nutrients across genotype arms. A normal saturating enzyme capacity with a low basal holoenzyme fraction would support a cofactor limitation; low saturating total enzyme capacity would point to a different constraint. [Primary astrocytes](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1520405/) · [Cell-type comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12214565/)

**Human transfer and interim decision.** Adult progressive MS is a limited comparator for developmental WWOX disease. In SPI2, 39/326 improved on pharmaceutical-grade biotin versus 29/316 on placebo. The authors report OR 1.35 (95% CI 0.81–2.26). Biotin interfered with some clinical laboratory tests despite mitigation. The rat fiber result therefore supports a cellular hypothesis, while the human data give little basis for extrapolating a functional effect. Keep D-biotin at the preclinical evidence stage. A single biochemical comparison could change the decision: does biotin still increase ACC1 occupancy and lipid flux specifically in WWOX-deficient, stage-matched oligodendrocytes in biotin-replete medium at a measured intracellular exposure? If the same response occurs in corrected cells or only after artificial depletion, lower the WWOX-specific priority. Next audit the primary neuronal Wwox–myelin evidence for a cell-autonomous versus axon-dependent origin before closing the candidate. A limiting deficit in neurons could otherwise be assigned to oligodendrocytes. [Randomized comparator, author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33222767/) · [Existing LEGEND myelin assessment](../../myelin_phenotype_first_sci50_20261113/README.md)

Astrocyte Pcx remains higher (+35.2 CPM). In myelin-forming cells the unadjusted Pcx difference reverses from −15.9 to +24.0 CPM after depth matching, so avoid assigning a PC deficit to this class. The held audit of Wwox mouse Fig. 6D reports that wild-type precursors on null dorsal-root-ganglion cultures less often reached a myelinating morphology. Reduced axon density or an altered neuronal cue could therefore constrain the response even when oligodendrocytes have intact Wwox. The Olig2-Cre comparison at P17 reported little change in qualitative MBP staining and did not measure conduction. These observations prioritize reciprocal neuronal/glial genotype comparisons, with matched axon length/density and firing. A glial biochemical improvement confined to artificial fibers, with persistent impairment on Wwox-null axons, would lower the case for biotin as a disease modifier. The genotype swap is established LEGEND work; the new part is the stage-resolved ACC1 occupancy and lipid flux under equal biotin availability. The next assessment is to look for direct independent exposure–occupancy evidence in biotin-replete developing neural cells near the human CSF comparator and for a quantitative ceiling once carboxylases are saturated. **Independent enzyme comparison.** Suormala et al., Table II (printed p. 280), compared one passage in 10 µM versus about 0.0001 µM biotin. In the Roc-1 oligodendroglial line, depletion left 4.4% of pyruvate carboxylase activity and 43.2% of ACC activity. In primary rat brain cells consisting mainly of astrocytes, it left 20.5% and 66.6%, respectively. These are ratios calculated within each enzyme and cell model from the means of duplicate measurements in one representative experiment. Protein-normalized catalytic activities are distinct from cofactor occupancy. The difference argues against using sensitivity of mitochondrial enzymes as a proxy for ACC-mediated lipid synthesis. The figure on acute repletion concerns PCC after deprivation. A paired concentration series above nutritional sufficiency in developing WWOX cells is still missing. A predicted distinguishing result is an increase in cofactor-bound ACC1 and lipid flux under replete conditions even when mitochondrial carboxylases show little additional response. [Published primary study](https://doi.org/10.1024/0300-9831.72.4.278) · [Numeric comparisons](comparisons.tsv)

**A concentration overlap that changes the working mechanism.** In Results on printed p. 283, Suormala et al. report no observed activity difference across 0.01–10 µM in their CNS-cell assays; the underlying values were not shown. That interval contains the 5.12 µM *addition* associated with more wrapping in Cui et al. The latter used a different cell preparation, medium and readout. The overlap weakens the assumption that extra wrapping near the low end must reflect increased carboxylase activity. It cannot establish saturation or a quantitative equivalence bound for WWOX. Measure the starting biotin concentration in the actual medium as well as intracellular species. Fresh evidence of low holo-ACC1 or low reserve in the WWOX stage of interest is required to maintain a cofactor-specific rationale. A central question remains whether nutritional biotin deprivation in vivo causes a brain-region-specific decrease in correctly identified biotinylated carboxylase proteins that resembles the proposed WWOX state. This is the next independent comparator before disposition. [Primary study, Table II and Results](https://doi.org/10.1024/0300-9831.72.4.278)

**What nutritional deficiency actually looks like in brain.** In Yuasa et al., three-week-old male rats received an eight-week diet with both unrestricted and pair-fed controls. Compared with pair-fed controls, free biotin in hippocampus fell from 2.7 to 0.5 nmol/g protein (−81%); bound cortical biotin rose from 0.4 to 0.9 nmol/g protein (rounded group means, four to five samples). Cortex and hippocampus showed no reported between-diet difference for PC, transporter, ligase or biotinidase RNA. Fig. 2 shows representative biotinylated PC/MCC/PCC bands (n=3) and describes attenuation in brain with much greater loss in liver. The abstract emphasizes relative maintenance. The primary panel has no ACC band, no total-enzyme denominator and no quantitative activity result. An unknown biotin-bound cortical protein cannot be assigned to ACC1. These controls reject total protein-bound brain biotin and bulk RNA as standalone selectors for a WWOX biotin trial. [Primary study, Table 1, Fig. 2 and Table 2](https://doi.org/10.3177/jnsv.62.81) · [Calculation](comparisons.tsv)

**Remaining pediatric question.** Deprioritize an unselected WWOX trial. Keep D-biotin active only to distinguish a secondary deficiency associated with background antiseizure medication or nutrition from a WWOX-specific response. The discriminating comparison would match background exposure and nutritional state across genotypes and document basal cofactor occupancy or a functional carboxylase abnormality. Correction shared by deficient participants or models across genotypes would support secondary deficiency. A selective improvement in identically replete WWOX models with restored conduction would reopen the mechanism-specific hypothesis. Next verify which antiseizure exposures have actual pediatric biochemical or functional evidence, and which readout distinguishes biotin loss from interference by metabolites.

Two indexed pediatric primary studies are queued for that comparison. Extract the drug-specific groups, whether assays distinguish biotin from metabolites, urinary organic acids, pretreatment samples and potential nutritional confounding before interpreting their findings.
