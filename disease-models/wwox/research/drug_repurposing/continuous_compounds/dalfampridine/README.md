# Dalfampridine in WWOX loss of function

**Disposition.** Retain 4-aminopyridine as a conditional ex vivo probe of available axonal propagation. Defer systemic pediatric repurposing. The evidence supports an acute electrical experiment and exposes a weakness in using the slope of a compound peak alone. The hypothesis and opposing observations were frozen in the [pretest](pretest.md). Context policy for the comparison is SYNTHESIS. **Exposure.** The current AMPYRA label reports 17.3–21.6 ng/mL total fasting adult extended-release peak and 97–99% unbound in plasma. At molecular weight 94.11 g/mol the interval implies **0.178–0.227 µM** free plasma, computed from the outer bounds. The historically measured 0.5–1 µM nominal white-matter bath threshold divided by those bounds spans **2.20–5.61**. The ratio is a cross-study sensitivity comparison. It supplies no measured unbound brain/plasma ratio and makes no claim about which tissues reached that bath threshold. The printed adult aqueous-solution comparator gives 0.440–0.449 µM calculated free plasma and a distinct profile. Historical MS measurements paired function with serum over a wider range, with an estimated smooth-pursuit gain of 0.14 (95% CI 0.06–0.23) and a concentration association in 69 oral-phase entrants. That is an ocular-motor output and is distinct from a directly conducted axon response. FDA rat cerebrum radiolabel equivalents support central entry. ## What the comparison changes

**The WWOX phenotype is localizable further.** In the held Review of Repudi et al. Fig. 5D, callosal stimulation with cortical recording gave longer evoked latencies at one 250 µm nominal spacing (8 slices from 5 controls and 8 from 6 neuronal KO mice, P13–17). Those counts refer to slices nested within animals. The paper's Fig. 2E–F samples cortical field recruitment in a different geometry, with larger evoked peaks in KO. Cheng et al. measured 1.39 versus 2.44 ms in a transcranial motor response that includes peripheral transmission. The conduction route and biochemical compartment are therefore unresolved. Preserve the measured path length and split first direct fibers and later synaptic recruitment before interpreting a shortened latency. [RP-049 extraction and exact locators](../../advisor_pilot_20261113/brainstorm_20261116/adv298_functional_vs_structural_myelin_it_20261128/table_functional_rows_adv298.tsv) · [Repudi et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33914858/) · [Cheng et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32000863/)

The [Radomski Fig. 6B readback](figure6_readback.tsv) gives a within-injury N1 mean shift of about +0.058 m/s and a sham shift of about +0.087 m/s. Their subtraction is −0.029 m/s, within an illustrative ±0.116 m/s bar-edge sensitivity. This interval concerns digitization only. The published injury-by-drug interaction is p = 0.8171. For N2 the readback interaction is +0.021 m/s and the published interaction is p = 0.6222. The structural partial rescue is compatible with unresolved conduction recovery. The absence of a statistically detected interaction leaves a range of functional effects possible.

**Slope can still mix fibers.** In the [synthetic counterexample](recruitment_control.tsv), changing the fraction of the fast component from 0.2 to 0.8 at constant total component weight raises the fitted peak-based speed from 0.493 to 0.819 m/s although both underlying velocities are fixed. A distinct condition with unchanged weights and faster axons yields 0.782 m/s. This is an identifiability check, not an estimate for WWOX. It extends RP-049 by requiring component identity and directly measured propagation failures alongside the slope. [Primary support for a presynaptic rival, paired cortical recordings](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21224227/)

## Discriminating prediction

**Hypothesis.** A subset of surviving WWOX axons could have limited conduction reserve through developmentally disorganized axon–glial domains. Brief local 4-AP could raise the probability of a first response reaching a distal site or preserve train propagation without changing the speed of an already-conducting axon. The competing routes are recruitment of a different axon mixture and enhancement of late synaptic transfer. A new or remodeled sheath is a separate, delayed explanation.

**One next comparison.** Compare vehicle and verified local free parent in matched juvenile Wwox-deficient and control callosal preparations. Record a proximal fiber volley and at least two distal distances in the same tract, identify the same direct component or tracked units, and measure distal failures conditional on proximal recruitment across a train. Fit timing only for that identified response. Keep spontaneous activity and a separate late synaptic response to expose changes in network gain. Include washout and a matched vehicle time course. Keep the animal as the unit and preserve the underlying traces.

- A selective decrease in distal failures at matched proximal recruitment, with stable component identity and preserved spontaneous-network headroom, supports acute conduction reserve. Faster timing in the same tracked component would refine it. Reversibility before structural change would favor an electrical effect.
- A shorter compound peak coupled to different recruited components, or a change only in late synaptic responses, favors the recruitment or release explanation. Total proximal amplitude alone cannot exclude a shift in component composition.
- Adequate local exposure and a responsive assay with unchanged distal failures lowers the axonal hypothesis. Increased spontaneous recurrence or loss of physiological recruitment would override a larger evoked signal. A durable effect after washout would require paired same-animal structural and conduction evidence before attributing it to myelin.

## Pediatric feasibility and review

The exact entity is 4-aminopyridine free parent, including the regulated extended-release product. The current US label contraindicates AMPYRA in people with a seizure history and reports no established safety or efficacy below age 18. Renal clearance and inhibitors of OCT2 affect exposure. Background antiseizure combinations and juvenile WWOX neural exposure are uncharacterized. [Official label, §§4, 5.2, 7.1, 8.4 and 12.3](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=550eb76a-e4a6-4fa1-ad65-c0fd8b0ce783)

An infant KCNA1 gain-of-function report includes measured total serum at trough (10–27 ng/mL) and after administration (33–65 ng/mL excluding one explicitly uncertain sampling time). It used a modified preparation and measured neither brain exposure nor a definite peak. A multicenter KCNA2 gain-of-function series reported composite benefit in 9/11, with variable seizure-subtype responses. These are mechanistically defined channelopathies, with no equivalent WWOX potassium-channel gain-of-function premise established. They do not alter the systemic decision. A reviewer could change the ex vivo priority on receipt of local exposure plus a blinded genotype-by-drug contrast in direct fibers, or on recovery of public per-animal traces permitting a comparison of recruitment and propagation. Current numeric evidence supports the same next comparison under either polarity of a small post-washout slope change. [KCNA1 primary, Table 1 and Results](https://doi.org/10.1002/acn3.51742) · [KCNA2 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34516822/)
