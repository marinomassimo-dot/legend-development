# Ademetionine | Oral exposure, methyl-donor delivery and GAMT capacity

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Decision.** Keep ademetionine as a conditional mechanistic probe and park pediatric WWOX trial priority. The evidence shows formulation-dependent blood exposure, one central biofluid association, and a separate adult mouse neural counterexample. Native WWOX intracellular active SAM, SAH and creatine synthesis rates are still unresolved.

## Exposure and its boundaries

| Comparison | Reproduced observation and limit |
| --- | --- |
| [Bottiglieri et al., pp. 1096–1097, Table 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC488323/) | Four adults with Alzheimer’s dementia received Bioresearch enteric-coated oral SAM in an open series for four to eight months. The plasma mean changed 33.6 → 135.4 and CSF 35.5 → 66.0 ng/mL, measured 1.5–5 hours after the last oral dose. The ratio of CSF means gives **+85.9%**, versus 62.5% in the text. The percentage estimator is unspecified and the paired values are unavailable. A separate paired-plasma arithmetic problem is documented below. The double-isotope enzymatic concentration assay does not trace drug origin or intracellular free S,S-SAM. The placebo-controlled IV study in depression used distinct 2 h and 24 h post-treatment subgroups. |
| [Cameron et al., pp. 2–3, 7–9, Table 6](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40360-020-00466-7) | In the 24 completing healthy adults, fasted mean plasma AUCt by product was 9434.4 and 6643.8 ng·h/mL, a **2.84-fold** contrast per reported milligram free-base equivalent. MSI-195 contains disulfate tosylate with distinct excipients and enteric coating. Its fed AUCt was 0.554 of its fasted value and the reported Tmax summary shifted from 4.5 to 13 h. The food period followed the crossover periods. These results characterize those products and sampling windows, with no estimate of pediatric neural exposure. |
| [Jaggumantri et al., pp. 361–363, Treatment Outcomes Table](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26205312/) | Two boys with SLC6A8 deficiency aged seven and eight at initiation were already taking creatine, arginine and glycine. One post-addition MRS comparison reported stable basal ganglia and white matter creatine, despite some language gains on ongoing care. Both had sleep or behavioral intolerance under the initial exposure, including reported dechallenge and rechallenge in one. The enteric tablet was discussed with no exact salt established in the scoped report. The subgroup of one with follow-up provides neither a neural exposure estimate nor efficacy in WWOX. |

**Independent arithmetic check.** With the plasma marginal SDs 4.5 and 68.3 ng/mL in Table 3, any paired difference SD is at least 63.8 ng/mL. The reported plasma paired t of 6.33, difference 101.8 and n=4 require SD 32.16. The maximum feasible t under those stated moments is 3.19. Ordinary rounding cannot account for that discrepancy. Retain the descriptive group means and leave the plasma significance unresolved. The CSF t of 7.24 is compatible with the marginal moments.

**Direct import versus salvage.** A cerebral endothelial competition assay reduces tritiated adenosine uptake in the presence of SAM. It does not establish parent transcellular flux. In a separate primary tracer experiment, exogenous D3-methylthioadenosine (MTA) labeled methionine and SAM in MTAP-intact mouse macrophages. Thus a methyl label on intracellular SAM or a methylated product can follow reassembly. These systems supply orthogonal controls and no exposure calibration for WWOX. [Chishty et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12031851/) · [Barekatain et al., Fig. 4a–d](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-24240-3)

## Counterevidence and WWOX scope

**In vivo counterexample.** Fustin et al. Fig. 6c compares adult mouse hypothalamus after three weeks of oral food exposure, with two females and two males per arm. The published bar heights give approximate SAM/SAH ratios **1.4, 0.6 and 0.7** in control and the two SAM-enriched diet arms, respectively. SAM relative peak area also falls in both arms. Liver shows a different contributor pattern with higher SAH at the higher food-arm level. The distinct wheel-running cohort used male mice. The external exposure is SAM chloride dihydrochloride in food. Use the graph as a counterexample to assuming a universal gain from supplemental SAM and keep the normalized measurements separate from absolute tissue concentrations or GAMT flux. [Fustin et al., Fig. 6 and animal methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-022-03280-5)

**Native constraints.** Earlier WWOX RNA data show lower GAMT in three bulk comparisons, with MAT2A falling in juvenile mouse cortex and hippocampus and increasing in human organoids. In the P17 hippocampal nuclei replay, mature oligodendrocyte GAMT moves 27.8 → 15.9 CPM (11 versus 5 UMIs) and MAT2A 346.2 → 177.7 CPM. In myelin-forming oligodendrocyte assignments GAMT moves 39.1 → 58.1 CPM (28 versus 13 UMIs), with a smaller MAT2A change 428.6 → 411.4 CPM. One pooled library per genotype, small counts and changing developmental composition limit within-lineage inference. This favors age- and lineage-matched measures of both donor and enzyme. [Bulk precursor contrasts](../l_arginine/precursor_expression.tsv) · [P17 input series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158684)

**Enzyme comparison.** Recombinant human GAMT gave apparent SAM Km 5.6 ± 0.8 µM at fixed 100 µM GAA with a coupled assay, whereas older human cell extracts reported apparent SAM Km 68–78 µM. Different matrices and cosubstrate conditions prevent a single intracellular exposure threshold. Even at a fixed ratio of SAM to SAH, a normalized competitive toy calculation gives 0.27, 0.67 and 0.78 of the same Vmax as both concentrations scale upward together. The ratio and GAMT amount each need independent measurement. [Kinetic study, Table 1 and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10983791/) · [Human cell extracts, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10660808/)

## Discriminating prediction

**Dato.** Oral formulation and sampling change peripheral SAM, CSF total SAM does not resolve parent origin, and supplemented SAM can lower hypothalamic SAM/SAH in another preparation.

**Inferenza.** Donor supply, SAH clearance, intracellular delivery and GAMT capacity can each change the labeled creatine rate. A shift in another methylated substrate, or in total cellular SAM alone, cannot identify the limiting step of creatine synthesis.

**Ipotesi e verifica.** In juvenile WWOX-LoF and isogenic or stage-matched corrected preparations, measure absolute S,S-SAM, SAH, GAA, creatine, PCr and first recruitment before choosing a perturbation. At matched GAA and first recruitment compare vehicle and verified SAM exposure with matched degradation-product or methionine controls. Use independently labeled adenosyl and methionine moieties in two parent isotopologues, and test for crossed label combinations produced by reassembly. Crossed labels establish some resynthesis, while their absence leaves reassembly possible in segregated pools. Monitor the corresponding precursor labels and chromatographically resolve the active stereoisomer. At matched precursor delivery, a donor-limited phenotype predicts a higher initial creatine methyl-label flux and improved absolute PCr recovery with preserved activity. If free intracellular SAM rises and flux stays low, compare GAMT abundance and maximal activity at shared substrate and SAH conditions. An increase in SAH or a decline in reserve with exposure lowers priority. This is a preclinical assay comparison.

**Pediatric feasibility.** The clinical precedent is a small adjunct study in a different disorder, with meaningful sleep and behavior signals and an administration constraint for the enteric formulation. Product identity, neurodevelopmental tolerability, background medication interactions and active central exposure remain essential for interpretation. Reopen trial priority only after a reproducible WWOX donor-limited flux phenotype and genotype-selective recovery with measured exposure. A separate review of the clinical arithmetic would not resolve the missing native measurements.
