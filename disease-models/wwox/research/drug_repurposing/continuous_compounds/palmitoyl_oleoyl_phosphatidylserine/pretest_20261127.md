# Defined phosphatidylserine species in WWOX loss of function

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · `context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`

**Active molecule after the pantethine disposition.** 1-palmitoyl-2-oleoyl-sn-glycero-3-phospho-L-serine (16:0/18:1 phosphatidylserine, POPS). Intake of a soy or marine phosphatidylserine mixture provides only formulation-class precedent. Compare public evidence for the defined molecular species or label the exact species gap. In contrast to the held [L-serine](../l_serine/README.md) work, the question is whether a preformed membrane species can supply a limiting mitochondrial phosphatidylethanolamine pool with a functional consequence. Pair with the held [citicoline](../citicoline/README.md) membrane-substrate comparison and [L-ascorbic acid](../ascorbic_acid/peripheral_translation_20261126.md) lineage-stage result.

**Frozen comparison.** Audit oral-to-neural disposition separately from in vitro delivery. Distinguish preformed phosphatidylserine from lysophosphatidylserine, free serine, acyl remodeling and preformed phosphatidylethanolamine. Compare stable isotope retention, mitochondrial PISD-dependent phosphatidylethanolamine synthesis and actual respiration or evoked recovery. Cell death and extracellular phosphatidylserine exposure can change independently of mitochondrial phosphatidylethanolamine. Compare a mitochondrial pool assay, its biological replication and its delivery route across models. Record which published formulations overlap the selected species, the exact route and the age range supported by any pediatric studies.

**Predictions and stopping condition.** Require a defined deficit in mitochondrial phosphatidylethanolamine and a functional readout at a matched WWOX neural maturation stage. A preformed-lipid pathway predicts a coordinated mitochondrial signal retaining an independently marked glycerol backbone and N-labeled headgroup through phosphatidylserine decarboxylation, with a rise in absolute phosphatidylethanolamine and preserved respiratory reserve or evoked function. Headgroup loss of carboxyl carbon is expected in mitochondrial decarboxylation. Quantify intact versus remodeled acyl forms separately. Compare labeled free constituents and lysophosphatidylserine reference at matched measured availability, with early paired isotopologues and crossover labels to estimate reassembly. Reduced benefit under a validated assay of PISD dependence would favor mitochondrial conversion over a direct surface-lipid effect. Similar functional effects with degraded products, or shifts in membrane asymmetry without restored mitochondrial membrane lipid or function, lower priority for the hypothesized route. If existing oral evidence measures only circulating breakdown products, require a route and exposure bridge before considering a WWOX clinical step.

**First source priorities.** For species-level transport start with Kainu et al. and audit the interventions and lipid molecular-species measurements before transferring the results to POPS. For the downstream check, compare Tasseva et al.'s mitochondrial phosphatidylethanolamine depletion and lysophosphatidylethanolamine reference with the patient-fibroblast PISD study. For formulation precedent, verify trial allocation, completers and composition of the soy product in Hirayama et al. These are leads for the next checkpoint. **Assignment checks.** A total 34:1 phosphatidylserine or phosphatidylethanolamine peak is insufficient for a 16:0/18:1 assignment. Require acyl-fragment evidence and use an explicitly resolved sn-position standard if a positional claim matters. Pair the mitochondrial lipid measurement with a surface-exposure readout in viable cells and a check for oxidized phosphatidylethanolamine, so a larger mitochondrial pool and an outer-leaflet or peroxidation signal can vary independently. Tissue lipid abundance can shift with cell-type composition and mitochondrial mass.

**Molecular identity and formulation leads.** Fix the oleoyl double bond at 9Z and the palmitoyl residue at sn-1 (LIPID MAPS LMGP03010024, PubChem 5283499). The epilepsy-and-ADHD trial of EPA/DHA-conjugated phosphatidylserine and the recent ADHD trial with an atomoxetine comparator provide additional formulation and denominator checks. Keep PS-bound fatty acid identity and the relevant outcome separate during full-text review.
