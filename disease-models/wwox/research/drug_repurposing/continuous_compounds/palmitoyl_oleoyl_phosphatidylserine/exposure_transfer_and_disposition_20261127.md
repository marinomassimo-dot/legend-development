# POPS and mitochondrial lipid delivery in WWOX loss of function

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · `context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`

**Disposition.** Park defined 16:0/18:1(9Z) phosphatidylserine (POPS) for pediatric WWOX trial priority. Retain a preclinical comparison of phosphatidylserine (PS) supply, intracellular transfer, and formation of mitochondrial phosphatidylethanolamine (PE). The WWOX RNA contrast points to both synthesis and transfer. It gives no evidence that either step limits lipid flux or that orally administered POPS reaches the relevant neural compartment. ## Chain and route evidence

- **Exact species in a membrane transfer assay.** Ikhlef et al. tested POPS with the isolated human ORP8 lipid-binding domain between liposomes. Relative to transfer without the counterligand, selected acceptor PI(4)P species sped POPS transfer **4.8–6.8-fold** (Fig. 2B). This is a defined lipid–protein comparison. The membrane system lacks a native mitochondrial interface and cannot calibrate mitochondrial delivery. In a separate HeLa study, Monteiro-Cardoso et al. found mitochondrial PE approximately **34% and 20% lower** after ORP5 or ORP8 knockdown, respectively, by mass spectrometry of mitochondrial fractions. The two studies link related steps under different conditions. Their results do not demonstrate that adding POPS overcomes reduced ORP8-dependent transfer. [Ikhlef et al., Results and Fig. 2B](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12915-021-01183-1) · [Monteiro-Cardoso et al., Results and Fig. 6B](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2022.111364)
- **Analog selection and transport confounding.** In BHK cells given labeled synthetic lipids by donor vesicles/cyclodextrin, Kainu et al. reported that **more than 70%** of labeled 14:1/14:1 PS was converted to PE within the one-hour introduction period. Conversion was slower for the two longer-chain species. The Fig. 8 caption reports three independent experiments. These species and this delivery method do not supply a numerical POPS estimate. The carrier also depleted cellular cholesterol under some conditions, so vehicle and membrane composition must be matched. A related HeLa study's public abstract found that acyl species changed import and export in different directions. Exact POPS kinetics and denominators could not be verified from that publication's freely accessible material. [Kainu et al. 2010, Figs. 5 and 8](https://www.jlr.org/article/S0022227520313651/pdf) · [Kainu et al. 2013, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23159415/)
- **Energy upstream of transport.** Jia et al. used an NBD-tagged short-chain PS analog in mouse tauopathy neurons, tracking PE-related fluorescence at 2.5 hours and adding glutamine to stimulate oxidative metabolism. The study linked energetic stimulation to the reporter and PISD-dependent autophagy. The reporter and model cannot establish native POPS kinetics. Paired measurements of respiration and species-resolved transfer are needed because an energy deficit can precede low conversion as well as follow it. Independently, lyso-PE restored mitochondrial PE and ATP synthesis in the PSB-2 cellular control. [Jia et al., Results and Fig. 3E–G](https://doi.org/10.1080/15548627.2024.2392408) · [Held downstream control](first_comparison_20261127.md)
- **Oral evidence still ends before intact brain delivery.** In the accessible primary abstract from Babenko and Semenova, feeding a PS preparation to aged rats increased total hippocampal PS without a corresponding detected change in total PE or PE synthesis. It does not follow the absorbed molecules. An independent parenteral radiolabel study distinguished broad headgroup/glycerol fates and found uptake predominantly in liver and spleen, with decarboxylation and hydrolysis after administration of PS liposomes. Rat enterocyte studies support a lyso-PS and reacylation branch. Oral label in a PS fraction can therefore arise by rebuilding a species, and native PS abundance cannot identify the route. No primary oral study located here quantified intact 16:0/18:1(9Z) in perfused brain or human neural tissue. [Aged-rat primary abstract](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11055-010-9385-2) · [Parenteral primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2015419/) · [Rat intestinal primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1636493/)

The studies offer formulation-specific tolerability and feasibility data. They leave pharmacokinetic interactions with WWOX antiseizure combinations and applicability to younger children unverified. A transfer study would require the defined sn-1 and sn-2 acyl species, nutritional background including lipid intake, concurrent treatment and a prospectively interpretable functional measure.

## Predicted comparison and decision

**Prediction.** In isogenic WWOX-deficient and corrected neural cultures staged for comparable maturation and mitochondrial mass, measure a mitochondrial PE and respiratory or evoked recovery deficit before intervention. At matched intracellular tracer input, a substrate-limited model predicts early intact lipid retention followed by a rise in PISD-dependent, dual-moiety-labeled mitochondrial PE and absolute PE, with recovery of evoked function. A transfer-limited model predicts labeling of cellular PS with diminished delivery to mitochondrial PE. A downstream lyso-PE comparator can verify that the mitochondrial and functional deficit remains reversible in the same preparation. Include independent acute energy-state and lipid uptake comparisons. If controlling the starting energy state restores conversion before PE or evoked recovery changes, prior energetic limitation is a competing explanation for a low PE signal. Separate failed access from limited PISD capacity by pairing time-resolved outer- versus inner-membrane delivery with an independent PISD capacity readout.

Match labeled free constituents and the relevant lyso-PS reference, sample early and account for reacylation and coincident isotope recombination. Confirm PS species with acyl fragments and positional standards where needed. An increase in outer-leaflet PS signal or oxidized PE with no sustained independent mitochondrial benefit further lowers priority. Reopen for measured neural exposure with a genotype-preferential PE-and-function rescue beyond the matched degradation products. Further independent interpretation becomes decision-relevant once exposure or tracer measurements exist. The clinical and RNA evidence leaves this preclinical test unresolved.
