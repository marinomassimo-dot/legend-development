# A downstream control for the defined phosphatidylserine species

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · Opening evidence checkpoint

Tasseva et al. compared an acute CHO Pisd knockdown and the chronic PSB-2 PS-synthesis defect with their respective controls. In the knockdown, labeled PE from serine was about 80% lower and labeled PS about 40% lower. The reported proportion of label in PE within the combined PS-plus-PE pool was approximately halved, so the 80% PE signal reduction cannot be read as the fraction of substrate converted. Separately, mitochondrial PE mass was 22–27% lower across the two deficient models, and maximal respiratory capacity declined. Basal oxygen consumption declined in PSB-2 but was not significantly lower after acute Pisd knockdown. These are comparisons of hamster cell models with different deficits and timing. [Primary Fig. 1C–D, Fig. 2 and Fig. 8; print pp. 4159–4164](https://www.jbc.org/article/S0021925820463732/pdf)

The same study used 100 µM nominal lysophosphatidylethanolamine (lyso-PE) for 96 hours in the chronic PSB-2 model. The mitochondrial PE pool increased by 45% to approximately the wild-type level. Cell number approximately doubled, and ATP synthesis increased with complex I and complex II substrates. Fig. 13 describes triplicates from **two independent experiments**, so technical measures do not create six biological replications. A lipid and functional response after a downstream comparator provides a way to verify the dynamic range of a WWOX model before testing POPS. It supplies no experimental result for POPS, oral delivery or WWOX. [Primary Fig. 13 and text, print pp. 4168–4169](https://www.jbc.org/article/S0021925820463732/pdf)

For translation, the Hirayama abstract describes 36 children ages 4–14 and two months of an oral soy-derived preparation. Its denominator needs checking against trial flow, and the abstract does not specify the phospholipid species relevant here. The next step is to compare species-level disposition data with this formulation and with mitochondrial phosphatidylserine handling. In the fixed functional comparison, rescue by lyso-PE with no evidence of POPS-derived mitochondrial PE would favor a loss before the downstream pool is replenished.
