# Istradefylline in WWOX-LoF

28 November 2026 UTC · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. [Opening checkpoint](pretest.md)

**Decision.** Retain istradefylline (KW-6002) as a conditional A2A-directed probe for the post-burst phase. Defer systemic pediatric WWOX prioritization. Acute behavioral effects vary by provocation, a direct cortical EEG benefit in the relevant WWOX preparation remains open, and increasing exposure can recruit A2B. A small adult Parkinson PET study verifies striatal target engagement with prolonged exposure.

**Adult central exposure.** Two PET studies with ¹¹C-preladenant report mean striatal A2A occupancy of 39.5% and 52.1% after one administration (five adults per exposure arm) and 72.1% and 86.5% after more than two months (two per arm, four adults aged 78–82). Group differences between studies are +32.6 and +34.4 percentage points. Different cohorts and PET sessions make these descriptive contrasts. The measured region, population, background levodopa-based therapy and duration matter. Human juvenile cortex or hippocampus free-parent concentrations, A2B engagement and efficacy in genetic epilepsy remain unknown. [Single administration](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30253174/) · [Long-term study, Fig. 1](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.28378)

**A2B challenges a clean attribution at high exposure.** FDA NDA 022075 pp. 4–5 summarizes recombinant human Kᵢ values of 12 nM for A2A and 150 nM for A2B, with A1 and A3 >1,000 nM. A one-site equilibrium calculation without endogenous ligand gives 48 nM free parent for 80% A2A occupancy. It would give 24% A2B occupancy and <4.6% A1 occupancy. Keeping modeled A2B occupancy at or below 10% instead caps A2A at about 58%. The thresholds are illustrative assay-design choices. The assumptions of equal free concentration across recombinant targets, competitors absent and Kᵢ as an apparent Kd delimit the calculation. These model outputs are neither regional in vivo engagement nor a dosing proposal. They motivate measuring A2B function or adding an orthogonal control before interpreting a high-exposure result. [FDA receptor binding and functional study](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2019/022075Orig1s000OtherR.pdf)

## WWOX bridge and inference

The seven predeclared transcripts were replayed across P17 mouse cortex (4 control/3 null libraries), P17 mouse hippocampus (2/3) and week-15 human cerebral organoids (2/4). The change in mean log₂(1 + abundance) for ADORA2A was −1.10 in cortex and +0.34 in organoids, with all single-library omissions preserving the opposing directions. Hippocampal means were 1.11 and 0.98 TPM, putting the null arm below the opening detectability threshold. ADORA1 was lower in all three bulk preparations with sign retained across single-library omissions, agreeing with the prior caffeine comparison. Mixed samples do not resolve native receptor abundance, occupancy, synaptic adenosine dynamics or disease specificity. Mouse data are TPM and human data are normalized CPM. [P17 cortex](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) · [P17 hippocampus](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) · [Human organoids](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243) · [Caffeine comparison](../caffeine_citrate/README.md)

In the one-control-pool versus one-null-pool hippocampal nuclei dataset, A2A is sparse. There are 2 and 3 Adora2a UMIs in the excitatory class. Across retained classes there are 5 and 26 UMIs, with most null reads outside the excitatory class. The symmetric decomposition gives +1.28 CPM within annotated classes and +0.02 CPM from class weights. This follows only two pooled libraries and uneven read depth. The tails do not localize functional presynaptic receptor density to a particular WWOX cell type, and the pooled result should not replace the independent hippocampal tissue libraries. [Pooled hippocampal nuclei](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158684)

**Hypothesis.** Episode-dependent adenosine could recruit residual presynaptic A2A function and facilitate post-burst excitatory propagation despite a low resting bulk transcript estimate. Lower A1 function would weaken a parallel inhibitory brake even if direct drug binding at A1 is small. An alternative is benefit in the animal through reduced initial insult, state, vascular/metabolic responses, or downstream tissue protection. Binding selectivity alone cannot distinguish these routes.

**Discriminating observation.** Start in one age-matched WWOX and corrected preparation with established recurrent activity, preserving the first evoked response and native inhibitory output. In a post-recruitment comparison, match the initial burst and prior activity history, measure extracellular adenosine and local free drug, and record late recurrence and the evoked A1 response. Pair the istradefylline comparison with acute orthogonal A2A loss or blockade at measured engagement plus a separate A2B functional control. Selective A2A action predicts reduced excess late propagation with preserved first recruitment, inhibition and A1 response, and occlusion after the orthogonal A2A perturbation. A drug effect surviving A2A occlusion, following loss of A2B function or tracking state and early drive instead would downgrade that claim. Measure injury separately at matched recurrence burden. Ex vivo work can exclude systemic vigilance as a necessary cause. An in vivo follow-up would stratify EEG by vigilance and movement.

## Pediatric boundary and checkpoint

The marketed US formulation is an oral film-coated tablet for adults with Parkinson motor OFF episodes receiving levodopa/carbidopa. The FDA label does not establish pediatric safety or efficacy. The EMA refused the adult Nouryant application on the efficacy evidence for that indication. The FDA label reports approximately 98% plasma binding and a mean steady-state terminal half-life of 83 hours. Rifampin reduced systemic AUC by 81% and the label identifies carbamazepine and phenytoin as strong inducers. That numerical effect belongs to rifampin. Insomnia and hallucinations are among recorded adult adverse reactions and vigilance is an essential independent endpoint. Developmental exposure, formulation feasibility, interaction magnitude with pediatric antiseizure therapy, tolerability and electrographic benefit remain open. [FDA label §§5–8, 12.3](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=a7d008cb-b273-4049-a5d2-9c6902910d58) · [EMA decision](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/nouryant)

**Checkpoint.** Reopen for matched-history, target-confirmed WWOX network selectivity with A1 preservation and a juvenile unbound exposure bridge. Independent review could change the acute evidence if it resolves the Sun regimen discrepancy with allocation metadata or obtains the missing drug-specific EEG and exposure traces. The directly audited panels are sufficient for the current local-probe disposition. Next wake checks ownership and selects one distinct bottleneck.
