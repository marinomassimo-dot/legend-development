# Uridine triacetate and pyrimidine availability in WWOX loss

**Candidate choice and scientific premise.** Uridine triacetate is an oral prodrug of uridine. The FDA reviewed it for hereditary orotic aciduria, a defined defect of de novo pyrimidine synthesis. The WWOX records show changes in nucleotide-handling RNA and a competing requirement for phosphatidylcholine during myelination. Neither establishes uridine shortage. Prioritize this molecule for a discriminating comparison because its pediatric formulation and a known responsive disease make the test interpretable. Keep studies of uridine, UMP and other salts labelled separately from uridine triacetate. [FDA primary review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2015/208169Orig1s000CrossR.pdf) · [Held WWOX salvage data](../vigabatrin/salvage.tsv) · [Held membrane comparison](../citicoline/tracer.md)

**Prespecified before opening the transcript results.** Compare de novo input (CAD, DHODH, UMPS), uptake and salvage (SLC29A1, SLC29A2, UCK1, UCK2, UPP1, CMPK1), conversion to CTP (CTPS1/2), ribonucleotide reduction (RRM1/RRM2B) and membrane synthesis (PCYT1A). Use the three previously archived untreated WWOX datasets, report every prespecified gene and single-library omission signs, and distinguish nuclear cell-class results from tissue RNA. The historical mouse symbol Ctps maps to human CTPS1.

**Prediction and falsifier.** Measure absolute uridine nucleotide pools together with isotope incorporation in WWOX-deficient and corrected cells, matching developmental stage and cell division. If de novo pyrimidine supply limits function and salvage capacity persists, supplied uridine should raise UTP/CTP and restore the disease-relevant flux. A mitochondrial mechanism additionally requires balanced mitochondrial deoxynucleotide pools, recovery of nascent mtDNA per organelle mass and sustained function. Increased UTP or RNA labeling with persistent dNTP imbalance would weaken that mechanism. A selective increase in CDP-choline and phosphatidylcholine synthesis that precedes improved new sheaths and conduction would support the membrane alternative. Uridine alone cannot replace a missing purine pool. No WWOX drug response or local pediatric exposure has yet been established.

**Initial WWOX results.** Two of the 14 prespecified transcripts fall in all three bulk datasets under every single-library omission: SLC29A2 and RRM2B. Cortex/hippocampus/organoid SLC29A2 control-to-KO means are 10.2 to 7.6 TPM, 5.2 to 3.8 TPM and 23.8 to 18.4 CPM. DHODH has no sign preserved across omissions in any dataset. UMPS increases in the organoids. Reduced ENT2 transcript cannot establish impaired uridine uptake: this equilibrative transporter can exchange nucleosides in either direction, and ENT1 and kinase responses vary. In particular, Slc29a2 rises in the excitatory and inhibitory nuclear pools and falls in astrocytes (16 versus 9 astrocytic counts). Rrm2b falls most clearly in excitatory neurons and rises in astrocytes. Nuclear sampling is one pool per genotype. Prefer direct cell-specific uridine uptake and conversion to deoxynucleotides over assigning the bulk decrease to neurons. **Formulation and exposure comparator.** A quantitative adult study compared an equimolar load of pure uridine with a supplement whose *nucleoside fraction* was more than 90% triacetyluridine. These were separate cohorts of eight, four men and four women each. Day-one mean plasma uridine peaks were 36.1 versus 150.9 µM, a 4.18-fold contrast. In the repeated supplement cohort, day-seven baseline rose to 31.1 µM from 4.7 µM. Relative to day one, unadjusted AUC to infinity rose 36%, whereas the reported baseline-adjusted AUC changed by only 1.1% (606.2 versus 599.5). These are ratios of group means, without individual concentration-time curves. A higher trough changes aggregate exposure, but need not increase exposure attributable to the next administration. The study measured parent nucleoside in plasma after a mixed supplement. It does not measure pediatric brain concentrations, neuronal salvage, mitochondrial nucleotide balance or the effect of pharmaceutical granules in WWOX. [Original clinical study, Tables 1–2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3040752/) · [Calculated contrasts](formulation.tsv) · [Code](formulation.py)

**Checkpoint.** Continue uridine triacetate. Next compare the exact prodrug's plasma kinetics in the pediatric orotic-aciduria record with primary human or animal observations of brain UTP/CTP after a defined uridine exposure. Separate baseline from the post-administration increment and measure competition with uridine catabolism. Prioritize a mammalian model that measures function alongside nucleotide pools; a higher plasma peak alone does not establish rescue.

**Exact prodrug check.** FDA's 2015 cross-discipline review of NDA 208169, §5, printed pp. 16–19, reports four participants aged 3–19 years with hereditary orotic aciduria. Three had prior oral uridine data and one was treatment-naïve. The review gives relative plasma uridine availability based on AUC0–8 of 3.6-fold on an equal-weight basis and 5.5-fold on an equimolar basis. Only the latter uses equal numbers of molecules. The assay quantified uridine; parent prodrug in plasma was not measured with a validated assay. Some day-28 samples were late, and rapid clearance in the untreated participant could have several explanations. The mixed adult supplement study and this pharmaceutical study are distinct cohorts and formulations. This exact-product evidence supports systemic delivery; the next unresolved comparison is brain entry and downstream nucleotide utilization at measured exposure.
