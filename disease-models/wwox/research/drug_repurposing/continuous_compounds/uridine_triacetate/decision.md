# Uridine triacetate disposition

**Decision.** Park an unselected pediatric WWOX disease-modification trial. The approved oral formulation provides a practical route to systemic uridine. In the held WWOX models neither a limiting neuronal pyrimidine supply nor recovery of mtDNA synthesis, conduction or neural function under treatment has been demonstrated. RNA changes alone cannot localize the limiting step. No pooled treatment-effect estimate is justified across the following comparisons. [WWOX data and pharmaceutical exposure](README.md) · [Animal brain and exact-prodrug observations](brain_pools.md)

**A positive control upstream.** In Ng et al., Fig. 3B, uridine restored UTP, CTP and four UDP-sugar pools in one CAD-deficient patient fibroblast line relative to the average of two controls. Fig. 4A–B independently documents reduced incorporation of aspartate into RNA. Panel A uses labeled uridine incorporation as its denominator, a potential confound when applying that assay in WWOX. Panel B uses stable-isotope aspartate and reports preserved adenine labeling. The experiments establish a biochemical bypass and a baseline de novo defect in CAD. They leave the effect on neural function open. A retrospective clinical series included 20 people with CAD deficiency, 10 exposed across three uridine formulations. The authors report improvement in most treated patients. The aggregate abstract does not identify a triacetate-specific effect. [Ng et al., Figs. 3B and 4A–B](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4424951/) · [CAD clinical series, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32820246/)

**A downstream counterexample.** Pontarin et al. studied quiescent human fibroblasts carrying a pathogenic RRM2B mutation. In Table 1, adding deoxycytidine and deoxyguanosine raised whole-cell dCTP 3.17-fold and dGTP 4.00-fold in the mutant. Both exceeded untreated control means, although both remained below identically supplemented controls. dATP stayed at 1.4 pmol per million cells. In the separate depletion/recovery time courses (Fig. 1A–B), the pair gave no clear mtDNA recovery. Adding deoxyadenosine as a third substrate permitted full recovery. Cycling cells recovered in both genotypes. Table 1 reports five to seven experiments and whole-cell pools. The relevant uncertainty is within-cell compartmentation, cell state and balance across substrates. This study did not test uridine. A lower WWOX RRM2B transcript does not establish the enzyme phenotype of this patient. [Pontarin et al., Table 1 and Fig. 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3421225/) · [Input means and recalculated ratios](pool_rescue.tsv) · [Reproduce the ratios](pool_rescue.py)

**Human membrane readout.** In 17 healthy adult men studied over one week, normalized brain phosphorus signals after plain uridine rose 6.32% for total phosphomonoesters and 7.17% for phosphoethanolamine within the active arm. For phosphocholine the treatment-by-visit interaction reached p = 0.03, while the adjusted within-arm changes were individually inconclusive (uridine p = 0.35, placebo p = 0.74). The spectra use total phosphorus as the reference. These signals do not quantify newly formed phosphatidylcholine, myelin or conduction. Cell class, developmental stage, formulation and CNS exposure in WWOX remain unresolved. [Agarwal et al., Results and Tables 1–2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3020593/)

**Reopening observation.** Compare stage-matched WWOX-deficient and isogenic corrected neural cultures across independent differentiations, with baseline de novo flux measured against precursor enrichment, uridine salvage, cell-cycle state and survival. For a mitochondrial mechanism require a genotype-by-exposure response in balanced mitochondrial dNTPs, nascent mtDNA per mitochondrial mass and functional recovery at measured exposure. For a membrane mechanism require new phospholipid synthesis followed by improved sheath formation and conduction at comparable activity. Normalized precursor peaks or a plasma increase with persistent functional impairment leave the candidate parked. Independent literature review alone is unlikely to resolve the missing WWOX intervention measurement.
