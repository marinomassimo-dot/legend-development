# Prespecified positive-mode GAA exact-mass screen

Use the 38 held MTBLS12344 centroided positive-mode mzXMLs and the registered assay crosswalk. Query m/z 118.061102942 ([M+H]+, PubChem CID 763 neutral mass plus proton) within 5 ppm. Freeze peak selection without genotype labels. Find pooled-median maxima over the common acquisition interval and remove overlapping maxima within 30 s of higher peaks. Keep any peak with three nonzero scans in its ±15 s window in at least six of twelve pooled spectra, and pooled median area at least three times the six matrix blanks. A zero blank is reported separately. Screen ±5 s integration offsets and compare raw and total-ion normalized areas in the two held five-pool knockdown contrasts only after the QC gate. No published GAA standard retention time or sample MS/MS confirms identity in this study. A pass may justify a targeted follow-up and cannot prove a genotype effect for GAA itself. A miss constrains only this acquisition, ion and signal gate.
