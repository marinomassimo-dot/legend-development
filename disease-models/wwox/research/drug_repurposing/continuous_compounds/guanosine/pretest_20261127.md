# Guanosine, astrocytic clearance versus reduced release

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. At registration guanosine was the sole active compound after [azetukalner](../azetukalner/engagement_and_disposition_20261127.md). This is the endogenous nucleoside studied as an experimental exposure, analytically distinct from GMP, guanine, adenine nucleosides and urate.

**Selection.** Held WWOX bulk SLC1A2 is lower across the untreated preparations, while estimated expression in pooled juvenile hippocampal astrocyte nuclei can move in the opposite direction. Cleared glutamate, extracellular concentration and EAAT2 protein reserve need to be measured. Guanosine provides a different probe from inosine salvage or N-acetylcysteine cystine exchange. Primary studies report higher labeled glutamate uptake with a high substrate challenge and record parent in rat CSF after oral exposure. These are enough to ask whether a measured concentration affects clearance after activity, with an explicit opposing release mechanism. [WWOX clearance and pool composition](../acetylcysteine/mechanism.md) · [Primary slice uptake](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12469876/) · [Primary astrocyte uptake](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11277601/) · [Oral rat comparison](https://doi.org/10.1002/jnr.20327)

**Prediction before extraction.** If guanosine increases usable astrocytic transport reserve in WWOX, extracellular glutamate decay after a matched release challenge should accelerate when measured free parent is present. The change should track glutamate uptake or transporter current, disappear when the implicated uptake pathway is selectively inhibited at comparable first recruitment, and leave physiological first recruitment and late inhibition measurable. If the apparent clearance benefit comes from reduced release or adenosine signaling, endogenous evoked traces can shorten without improved removal of an independently matched extracellular challenge. If improved energy availability restores coupled ion gradients, uptake and function can change without evidence of more surface transporters. Check energy and gradients, age/region, and changes in parent and downstream purines separately.

**First comparison.** Extract absolute rat CSF parent concentrations, sampling time, formulation and denominator where available. Compare with low versus high substrate transport data and identify whether the published tracer change reflects capacity, affinity, viability or prior suppression. Preserve negative or contradictory findings by preparation and the behavioral/locomotor tradeoffs. Park pediatric priority in the absence of a measured exposure and native WWOX selectivity. **Closed checkpoint.** Retain as a conditional clearance probe and defer pediatric trial priority.
