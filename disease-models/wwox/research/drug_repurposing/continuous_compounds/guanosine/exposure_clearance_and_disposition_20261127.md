# Guanosine in WWOX loss of function

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. This comparison follows the [pretest](pretest_20261127.md). It asks whether intact guanosine can speed removal of extracellular glutamate at a measured neural exposure while preserving useful transmission.

**Decision.** Keep guanosine as a conditional preclinical probe for clearance. Defer pediatric trial priority. A low micromolar rat CSF measurement overlaps the low end of one positive slice assay. Another study using the same nominal oral formulation measured about one tenth as much. The most direct seizure paper identified its uptake responders after observing the outcome. There is no matched exposure–clearance–function result in WWOX.

## Counterproof and prediction

**Observation.** In Vinadé Fig. 5 and Methods §2.9, the cortex was collected after the ten-minute seizure observation. Quinolinic acid reduced uptake by about 30% in the reported vehicle comparison. The paper reports prevention of that decrement only in treated animals defined as protected by their behavior after provocation. The timing permits either direction of causation and does not give a prospective uptake response or a treatment effect on uptake across all challenged animals. In the held WWOX record, reduced bulk SLC1A2 is compatible with altered tissue composition, while both astrocyte assignments in pooled hippocampal nuclei give higher Slc1a2 in the KO pool. There is one pool per genotype. Neither result measures clearance or membrane GLT-1. [Primary Methods §2.9 and Fig. 5](https://lume.ufrgs.br/bitstream/10183/275982/1/000423648.pdf) · [Held bulk and marker-sensitivity analysis](../acetylcysteine/mechanism.md)

**Inference from a replay.** Integrating a one-compartment saturable removal model after a pulse gives AUC = (Km × peak + peak²/2)/Vmax and half-time = (Km × ln 2 + peak/2)/Vmax. In the example with a 10 µM glutamate peak and illustrative Km = 20 µM, increasing capacity by 23% and reducing release by 16.0% yield the same 18.7% decrease in extracellular AUC. Reduced release alone even shortens the evoked half-time by 4.2%. After an independently matched input, the release-only model predicts unchanged half-time, while the increased-capacity model still predicts 18.7% shortening. The grid spans three arbitrary Km values and three starting loads. Diffusion, continued release, sensor kinetics, metabolism and competing transporters are omitted. These numbers demonstrate identifiability for this model, without estimating WWOX physiology. [Model and source arithmetic](replay_exposure_and_clearance.py) · [Counterexample grid](release_clearance_scenarios.tsv)

**Hypothesis.** A recoverable transport reserve in juvenile Wwox-deficient tissue could become apparent during repeated activity, when glutamate loads are larger, even with weak effects on the first response. A parallel explanation is reduced release through purinergic signaling or a change in energy and ionic gradients. A tissue with preserved baseline removal or transporter reserve already at its limit could give no selective benefit.

**Discriminating observation.** In one prespecified juvenile region and layer, first establish the vehicle genotype contrast in low and high input clearance, using animals or litters as biological replicates. Randomize within preparations and retain all recordings at prespecified quality. Compare an equal externally delivered glutamate transient with the endogenous evoked transient. Match the applied peak and sensor dynamic range, and measure extracellular decay/AUC, astrocyte transporter current, presynaptic recruitment or vesicle release, and first and repeated evoked responses. Measure intact parent and relevant purines in the sampled compartment. A selective acceleration after equal input with intact first recruitment and an increase in transporter current, sensitive to a validated transporter perturbation, supports a clearance component. An effect confined to endogenous responses with reduced first recruitment or release supports a release component. A matched improvement in ATP/ion gradients with unchanged surface GLT-1 leaves metabolic support as an explanation. If shortening appears only at a substantially higher exposure or with weaker first responses, park the WWOX-selective clearance hypothesis. If low and high loads behave differently, preserve that interaction and test equivalence separately for first recruitment. The same experiment can report a mixed result. ## Pediatric feasibility and close

Targeted PubMed searches for intact guanosine administration and pharmacokinetics, including trial/epilepsy and pediatric terms, returned no qualifying direct human neurological intervention or pediatric exposure series in the records reviewed. The ClinicalTrials.gov API returned 28 records for the intervention query `Guanosine` on 27 November 2026. Inspection of their intervention names found zero direct administrations of intact guanosine. These searches do not exclude unregistered or poorly indexed studies. The endogenous human CSF study and measurements of other purines, analogues and pathway drugs do not bridge pharmacology for administered guanosine. No validated pediatric formulation, free central exposure, background ASM interaction or repeated developmental tolerability comparison was located. The adverse memory and exploration signals from the two-week adult rat formulation remain material and would require monitoring of activity, vigilance and learning independently of seizure counts if the preclinical gap closes. [ClinicalTrials.gov intervention query](https://clinicaltrials.gov/search?intr=Guanosine) · [Human endogenous-CSF study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9427368/) · [Two-week primary rat study](https://doi.org/10.1002/jnr.20327)

**Reopen** for quantified free parent in the relevant compartment, selective improvement of matched-input clearance in WWOX with useful evoked function preserved, or a qualifying human pharmacokinetic study. A separate review could resolve the Vinadé sample-size discrepancy with primary arm counts but would not change the disposition without one of those bridges. Review was considered and deferred.
