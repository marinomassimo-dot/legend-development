# Does anhydrous betaine donate methyl groups within WWOX neural cells?

**Disposition.** Park anhydrous betaine for a WWOX-specific pediatric trial. Oral pediatric use in homocystinuria supports formulation feasibility. A biochemical or functional response specific to WWOX at a measured neural exposure would reopen the question. Background antiseizure medication effects on that exposure and on evoked recovery remain unquantified. [Initial comparisons and pediatric precedent](README.md)

## Two direct enzyme assays disagree

Gaull et al. reported betaine-homocysteine methyltransferase (BHMT) activities of 0.20 ± 0.05 and 0.37 ± 0.07 nmol/mg protein/h in human fetal and mature brain. The corresponding liver means were 1.82 and 7.78. The brain-to-liver ratios of the reported means are 11.0% and 4.8%, respectively. McKeever et al. later reported undetectable brain activity in human, pig and rat preparations, with detectable liver activity in all three species. The published summaries leave assay thresholds, background subtraction, neural cell composition and product specificity unresolved. A negative whole-tissue measurement could conceal a small active population. A low positive signal needs an assay blank and verified product. The lab has been asked to review the two source assays independently. [Gaull et al., publisher extract](https://doi.org/10.1203/00006450-197305000-00006) · [McKeever et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1657504/)

## What the neuronal and mouse experiments establish

Adult MS cortical gray-matter blocks contained a mean 47.8 nmol/g betaine versus 85.5 in controls. These are concentrations across tissue blocks from different regions, with uncertain donor independence. In a separate experiment, 1 mM extracellular betaine increased the spare respiratory capacity of isolated mitochondria from treated SH-SY5Y cells 4.2-fold over control, and 2.9-fold over nitroprusside alone in the combined condition. In the unstressed condition the authors saw no significant increase in bulk H3K4 trimethylation. Increased respiratory reserve is compatible with several mechanisms. [Singhal et al., tissue collection, Tables 2–3 and Figs. 8–9](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6605362/)

Primary rat neurons exposed to 1 mM betaine overnight increased H3K4 histone methyltransferase activity 1.8-fold. Reducing Bhmt expression attenuated the response. The endpoint depends on a downstream enzyme and BHMT protein also associates with a chromatin complex. These findings do not quantify methyl transfer from intact betaine into methionine. In cuprizone-fed mice, betaine improved basal and maximal respiration of isolated cortical mitochondria (four animals per group), and a separate behavioral comparison showed improved beam traversal. Respiratory methods specify six weeks of cuprizone and four weeks of betaine. Beam methods specify four weeks and two weeks, respectively, with 4–6 animals/group. This prevents a within-animal inference connecting respiration to motor recovery. The toxin model supports a functional signal worth testing for transfer to WWOX. [Singhal et al., Figs. 2, 5 and 6 and corresponding Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7518703/)

## Which neural exposure was measured?

Two treated cases with CBS deficiency, hypermethioninemia and cerebral edema had CSF betaine of 6.6 and 0.12 µM. Corresponding admission plasma betaine was 98 and 40 µM. Exact pairing relative to the preceding dose is uncertain. In case A9, the original admission table confirms the CSF methionine value of 199 µM. The betaine value comes from the later case series. The experimental 1 mM bath exceeds those two CSF observations by factors of about 152 and 8,333. A bath, CSF and intracellular concentration are different quantities. These exceptional cases cannot define a WWOX ceiling, steady-state partition coefficient, or expected adverse-event rate. [Case series, §4.1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7052692/) · [Li et al., Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5965393/) · [Extracted values with missing analytes retained](csf_observations.tsv)

## WWOX-specific comparison and decision rule

Across untreated knockout and control samples, mouse cortical Bhmt rose from mean 0.048 to 0.712 TPM. The hippocampal change was in the opposite direction and sensitive to omission of a library. Human organoid BHMT means were near the floor (0.027 and 0.038 on the normalized-count scale). Mouse Slc6a12 decreased in both bulk comparisons. The organoid estimate changed sign when individual libraries were omitted. Nuclear counts offer little precision for Bhmt in the excitatory-marker group (3 transcripts in 1,596 control nuclei and 1 in 1,262 knockout nuclei). One pool per genotype limits inference and nuclear RNA is a poor proxy for enzyme abundance. None of these datasets involved betaine treatment. [Three bulk comparisons](betaine_bulk.tsv) · [Nuclear counts](cell_types.tsv) · [Composition sensitivity](mixture.tsv)

At matched methionine supply, activity and osmolality, local donation predicts transfer of a betaine methyl label into methionine and SAM with accompanying dimethylglycine formation, followed by preferential recovery of evoked transmission in deficient versus corrected neural preparations. Measure parent uptake and early product labeling together. Test methionine alone at a matched concentration to distinguish benefit through the circulating product. A BHMT-dependent response that requires catalysis would be strengthened by separation from chromatin-associated effects and from recycling of label through the folate pathway. Functional recovery tracking volume and endogenous osmolytes in the absence of detectable methyl flux would redirect the work to osmotic support. An RNA change, a CSF downstream marker, or histone methylation alone cannot select patients or prioritize a trial.
