**Decision at opening.** Retain an open probe question. The scale mismatch and the direction of the provoked seizure counterexample lower the case for clinical priority. Neither measures free neural parent or identifies how a particular WWOX circuit uses CCK. In the prespecified native comparison, loss of a peptide-triggered response at stable peptide lifetime should track verified receptor antagonism. A change in the duration of extracellular active CCK, with the initial response and receptor competition measured independently, would favor altered peptide handling. A late burst change after loss of early inhibitory recruitment would be a circuit liability signal.
