# Proglumide, receptor antagonism versus peptide turnover

28 November 2026 UTC · Continuous compound #213 · INV-15 · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. Preparations include human cloned CCK receptors (competitive binding IC50), adult rat pooled brain fractions (peptide cleavage), dog ventricular fluid and serum (total parent), adult human serum (total parent), rodent circuit function and held WWOX bulk and pooled nuclei RNA. [Prespecified comparison](pretest.md) · [Opening screen](first_comparison.md)

**Decision.** Defer proglumide as a pediatric WWOX candidate and as a stand-alone CCK2 probe. Keep it as a conditional ex vivo comparator of receptor antagonism and peptide persistence. The useful experiment first establishes a native CCK-dependent response with selective receptor controls and measures the parent and active extracellular peptide in the same preparation.

## Native circuit controls

- In naïve rat nucleus accumbens, authors report that nominal 100 µM proglumide opposed a response to sulfated CCK-8 in the evoked EPSC study. LY225910 and downstream GABA`_B` perturbation supported a CCK2-sensitive circuit. This is a native functional counterexample to treating the recombinant millimolar midpoint as a universal concentration threshold. Assay and regional context can explain part of the discrepancy. The contemporary abstract for the CA1 Schaffer collateral preparation instead reports no effect of 1 mM proglumide on a CCK-8-dependent depression, with an effect of a different CCK antagonist. [Accumbens primary, indexed Results and figure caption, PMID 14673185](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1664820/) · [CA1 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3032354/)
- Rodent primary reports on dorsomedial hypothalamus and hippocampus describe CCK-dependent potentiation of GABA drive and facilitation of glutamate release, respectively. In the PTZ mouse study abstract, proglumide alone did not detectably change clonic seizure threshold and reduced the threshold increase induced by some stressors or morphine. This direction is a counterexample for interpreting CCK blockade as uniformly beneficial. These preparations differ from spontaneous WWOX recurrence. [Hypothalamus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26400945/) · [Hippocampus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20392936/) · [PTZ](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15219760/)

## WWOX prediction and decision boundary

**Data.** In held mouse P17 hippocampus, bulk Cck mean falls from 580.6 to 240.7 TPM, a descriptive reduction of 58.5% across two control and three KO libraries. The leave-one-library log2(1 + TPM) contrast stays negative. Cckbr leaves both signs on omission in that region. In P17 cortex Cck and Cckbr decreases are smaller and stable to this omission check. In week-15 human organoids, CCK has sparse coverage with an unstable contrast, while CCKBR goes from 10.12 to 6.16 CPM with the same direction across omissions in two control and four KO libraries. In the mouse cortical nuclei worksheet, inhibitory CCKBR evidence is only 8 and 10 assigned UMIs across genotype pools, and CCKAR is essentially absent. Counts from pooled genotype nuclei are descriptive and cannot recover animal-level inference or identify a changed CCK-positive interneuron subset. Presence and abundance of RNA do not quantify secreted peptide, receptor engagement, chloride or sign of the GABA response. [Hippocampus](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) · [Cortex](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) · [Organoids](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243) · [Pooled nuclei](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158684)

**Hypothesis.** If reduced hippocampal CCK reflects less endogenous active peptide, the response to a selective CCK2 antagonist could be smaller in KO at baseline yet recover to the control contrast during a matched peptide input. In that case proglumide could change the duration of signaling by slowing peptide loss. If controlled peptide fails to equalize the contrast and the response to an independently validated selective probe or receptor response is reduced, altered receptor function or synaptic recruitment becomes more plausible. Both smaller late excitation and weaker compensatory inhibition are possible.

**Discriminating comparison.** In an age- and region-matched native WWOX preparation, compare vehicle, proglumide and a native-verified subtype reference such as LY225910 with a counterprobe for CCK1. Compare an evoked peptide transient with a matched extracellular active CCK time course. Read out intact sulfated CCK-8 and cleavage products separately from early excitatory and inhibitory recruitment, native chloride, and late activity, with the measured free parent preserved as the exposure variable. At a matched peptide trajectory, a response shared with the selective probe supports receptor blockade. A proglumide-specific prolongation of intact peptide that loses its circuit effect when the trajectory is matched supports altered clearance. A residual difference after matching peptide and selective engagement raises other mechanisms such as enkephalin handling. First recruitment must remain interpretable, and animal or donor is the unit of replication. Check each subtype probe's functional selectivity in the tested species and preparation.

**Checkpoint.** The immediate information gain comes from a native CCK response and a peptide time course in WWOX hippocampus, with a selective probe before compound priority is reconsidered. A clean local null with verified free exposure and a working reference drops the proglumide branch. An independent review could audit the apparent Fig. 2/3 assembly error, but resolving it would leave the native exposure and function gap and would not change this decision.
