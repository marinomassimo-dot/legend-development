# Chlorzoxazone and calcium-activated potassium feedback in WWOX

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Decision.** Retain chlorzoxazone as a conditional preclinical comparator and defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. Strengthening a slower potassium current could improve late spike adaptation. Faster BK-dependent repolarization, inhibition of calcium entry and subthreshold potassium conductance are competing routes. Read low-threshold recruitment, short-interval spikes and late recurrence in the same juvenile cortical preparation before attributing a response to SK.

## Native WWOX measurements

**Data.** In P13–17 mouse cortical layer II/III pyramidal cells, Bretón Table 1 reports a first-spike AHP magnitude of 15.14 ± 0.96 mV in S-WT and 14.42 ± 0.54 mV in S-KO, a difference of −0.72 mV (13 cells from 5 mice versus 40 from 15). The measurement is the first rheobase spike. Its AHP is relative to AP threshold. The mean first spike is wider in S-KO (1.35 versus 1.20 ms), and the publication shows no significance annotation on either AHP or half-width. Postinhibitory rebound is reported larger (1.86 versus 0.96 mV). A current-step firing count and a first-spike AHP do not isolate train-evoked SK charge. No linked animal-level or blocker-separated train dataset was identified in the article and held supplement. Keep the reported cell and mouse denominators. [Bretón et al., Table 1 and electrophysiology methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nbd.2021.105529) · [Existing conductance audit](../../potassium_conductances_sci43_20261110/verify/audit.md)

**Expression check.** KCNN2 RNA increases in P17 cortex and 15-week human organoids, by +0.48 and +0.61 in mean log2(1 + expression); the P17 hippocampal contrast (+0.08) changes sign with omissions. In the one hippocampal nucleus pool per genotype, Kcnn2 rises from 359 to 460 CPM in excitatory cells and 321 to 437 CPM in inhibitory cells. The gene encoding neuronal BK β4 (KCNMB4) decreases in the two mouse bulk preparations, −0.32 and −0.51, but the organoid omission range crosses zero. Excitatory and inhibitory hippocampal nuclei have lower pooled Kcnmb4, while pooled Kcnma1 rises. These counts challenge a simple loss of SK2 transcript and make BK timing worth examining. They cannot determine channel localization, assembly, calcium coupling or functional direction in viable cortical cells. [Bulk contrasts](native_bulk.tsv) · [Nuclear counts](cell_types.tsv)

**Inference.** The Alviña point means imply 48% and 62% less variability with 8% and 15% lower mean rate, respectively. The separate sentence reporting percentages for interval SD and firing rate does not reproduce those rates and cannot supply true SD changes from these aggregates. The cortical BK example has narrower APs and higher firing. Untreated *Wwox* cortex shows a wider first AP in the published means and has larger rebound. That bounds simple transfer of a post-seizure BK phenotype. It leaves divergent SK and BK effects after repeated activity open. [Calculations](contrasts.tsv)

**Mixed mechanism.** A recombinant SK assay supports a direct calcium-dependent route. In rat pituitary and human neuroblastoma cells, Liu et al. identify a BK response. They also report suppression of L-type calcium current in pituitary cells. In ATXN1[82Q] mouse Purkinje cells, Bushart et al. found that chlorzoxazone shifted the dendritic calcium-spike threshold in the preparation, with sensitivity to barium and no comparable response to the tested SK2/IK comparator. Those controls support a subthreshold potassium-channel alternative without identifying a unique causal channel. Somatic repetitive spiking and the chronic rotarod outcome in Bushart are from chlorzoxazone plus baclofen. Isolated calcium-spike threshold, first cortical response and chronic motor scores require different interpretations. [Syme et al., primary abstract](https://doi.org/10.1152/ajpcell.2000.278.3.C570) · [Liu et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12480161/) · [Bushart et al., Figs. 2, 4 and 5](https://doi.org/10.1002/acn3.527)

## Pediatric exposure and feasibility

Gade et al. studied single-exposure disposition in 70 participants aged 11–18 years (36 nonobese and 34 obese). Mean parent plasma peaks were 0.009 and 0.005 mg/mL, equivalent to **53.1 and 29.5 µM total parent**. Parent AUC in the obese group was 0.54 times that in the nonobese group. The parent and 6-hydroxy metabolite were assayed separately. The study did not measure binding in children or free central parent. No chronic efficacy or younger-child bridge follows from those measurements. [Gade et al., Tables 1–2 and Limitations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6046481/)

Let the unbound brain-to-plasma ratio at the relevant time be Kp,uu. If nominal 30 µM in the EA2 bath approximates the unbound benchmark, the required product of unbound plasma fraction and Kp,uu is 0.565 and 1.017 using the two group means above, respectively. At 100 µM (the apamin-sensitive rat result), these are 1.88 and 3.39. The adult study gives a measured mean serum free fraction of 3.9% in 10 healthy participants. Borrowing it solely for sensitivity gives free systemic peaks of about 2.07 and 1.15 µM and Kp,uu of about 14.5 and 26.1 to match 30 µM. These are conditional requirements, not pediatric binding or brain estimates. Even an adult total mean of 88.5 µM corresponds to an arithmetic estimate of 3.45 µM free systemic drug using that cohort's mean binding. A product of group means does not preserve individual covariance. Bushart et al. measured 4.80 µM in mouse whole-brain homogenate and 4.41 µM in plasma after oral exposure. The ratio of those total means cannot substitute for Kp,uu or for a time-resolved free cortical concentration. [Wang et al., Table 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1884181/) · [Bushart et al., Fig. 4C](https://doi.org/10.1002/acn3.527) · [Exposure inputs](exposure_inputs.tsv)

The tablet's US indication is symptomatic relief in acute painful musculoskeletal conditions. Reported drowsiness and additive CNS depression matter when interpreting alertness or motor outcomes. The label reports rare serious hepatocellular injury. A pediatric metabolic-probe study and separated mouse behavioral outcomes provide no direct basis for prioritizing a trial in WWOX. [US prescribing information](https://www.dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=0ed64990-f2f2-40e9-be91-874f0a862f83&type=display)

## One decision-changing comparison

**Hypothesis.** At matched calcium and voltage history, deficient slow SK feedback would predict smaller apamin-sensitive train charge, with preserved early responses and excessive late spikes in Wwox-deficient cortex. Faster BK repolarization can instead shorten individual spikes and increase early firing. If equalizing the relevant calcium transient removes the difference in SK charge, the bottleneck moves upstream toward calcium delivery or coupling. A dendritic threshold shift without a corresponding SK current points to a subthreshold route.

**Experiment.** In matched juvenile Wwox and control layer II/III, first compare first AP width and train-evoked SK-sensitive and BK-sensitive current at the same starting voltage, counted spikes and calcium signal. Then compare drug and vehicle at measured free parent, using independent SK and BK controls with baseline blocker effects measured. Track early pyramidal and inhibitory recruitment and spontaneous late recurrence. A genotype-selective recovery of SK charge and lower late recurrence with preserved early recruitment would justify a targeted pharmacology follow-up. A rise in early firing with BK engagement, or a change attributable to calcium delivery alone, would redirect the work. The native current comparison has higher information value than a chlorzoxazone-only test. Monitor calcium near the relevant channels when feasible, since the same bulk transient can conceal different local coupling.
