# Bexarotene in WWOX loss of function

28 November 2026 UTC · SCI_CODEX · INV-06 · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. [Opening checkpoint](pretest.md) · [Printable brief](../../view/research_reports/bexarotene.pdf)

**Disposition.** Defer systemic pediatric WWOX development and retain bexarotene as a conditional research probe for lipid supply versus oligodendrocyte response. The most informative new distinction is between making or retaining sterol inside a cell and transferring sterol to its neighbor. A rise in APOE expression can coexist with unchanged or lower exporter RNA. The previous program already rejected clinical advancement in adults with multiple sclerosis (MS) and recorded the receptor and sterol-screen data. This increment adds an animal-level public-data reanalysis, an explicit donor-versus-recipient experiment and source checks. ## Animal-level response and untreated WWOX

**Treated comparator.** Saibro-Girardi et al. profiled brain cells from eight 5-month-old APP/PS1 mice, four vehicle and four bexarotene, with two males and two females in each arm. The authors identified cell-state-dependent transcriptional responses. The open GEO deposit supplies a separate filtered count matrix per mouse and treatment assignment. We reconstructed independent, marker-enriched classes with two versus three positive markers, discarded tied assignments and aggregated counts within each mouse. We retained all eight mice in every summarized class at both cutoffs (minimum 20 assigned cells per mouse). The mouse is the replication unit. Increases and decreases below retain their direction with either gate and every single-mouse omission unless qualified. [Paper, Figs. 2–4 and Methods](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms27052435) · [GEO GSE283905](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE283905)

**Native comparison.** The frozen, untreated public datasets are mouse P17 cortex (4 control, 3 null), P17 hippocampus (2, 3), and week-15 human cerebral organoids (2, 4). With a full-arm average-abundance floor of 1 and a sign check under every single-library omission, **NR1H3 and NR1H2** decrease in all three. For example, NR1H3 shifts 2.64→1.48 and 2.77→1.03 TPM in mouse cortex and hippocampus, and 15.23→11.76 CPM in organoids. The hippocampal null mean falls to approximately 0.88 TPM when the highest null library is removed. NR1H2 passes the stricter expression floor after omissions, and the NR1H3 direction persists at low abundance. Mouse cortical **ABCA1/ABCG1** decrease, while both rise in organoids. Hippocampal ABCG1 decreases and ABCA1 fails the omission-direction check. RXRA and RXRG fall in cortex and rise in organoids, with hippocampal RXRG direction sensitive to omissions. The pooled hippocampal nuclei provide a different direction for ABCA1 (27.7→51.7 CPM) and APOE (211→328). Both increase in the astrocyte and microglia gates, with descriptive within-class contributions accounting for most pooled change. These are two pools and have no per-animal replication. Higher donor-like RNA in one pool or organoid cannot identify flux, localization, target engagement or a shared native defect. ## Adult efficacy, exposure and pediatric feasibility

- **CCMR One randomized adults.** Six months, 26 assigned per arm, 26 versus 24 exposed, 25 versus 24 with the primary efficacy outcome. Safety and tolerability were primary objectives. The pre-specified, participant-level primary efficacy outcome, within-participant submedian lesion MTR improvement, was **+0.16 percentage units** (95% CI −0.39 to +0.71), versus the +1.18 design target. The large test-based improvement targeted by this trial lies outside the interval. The exploratory P100 contrast in eyes delayed at baseline was **−4.06 ms** (−7.68 to −0.44, 29 versus 22 eyes). All recordable eyes gave −2.85 ms (−5.75 to +0.05; 42 versus 44). Exploratory regional MTR effects are spatially heterogeneous. Conduction-related VEP latency and lesion MTR have different scales and interpretations. No disability or high-contrast acuity separation was detected. Central hypothyroidism affected **26/26 versus 0/24**, raised triglycerides **24/26 versus 0/24** and drug discontinuation for adverse events **5/26 versus 2/24**, an exposed-arm difference of **10.9 percentage points** with small counts. Levothyroxine was given to 24 active-arm participants. This is both a tolerability burden and a rival when attributing exploratory changes entirely to isolated central RXR action. [Primary report, Figs. 1–3 and Tables 2–3](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(21)00179-4) · [Official registry results](https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/ctr-search/trial/2014-003145-99/results)
- **Follow-up selection sensitivity.** At a mean of 27 months, 20 of 31 Cambridge participants (12 versus 8, from 52 total originally randomized) returned. All recordable eyes, 24 versus 14, gave an adjusted **−7.79 ms** estimate (−14.76 to −0.82). For eyes delayed at baseline, 20 versus 7, the estimate was −5.39 ms (−16.11 to +5.32). Figure 1d's post hoc threshold bars reconstruct to **10/24 vs 3/14** all recordable and **10/20 vs 3/7** delayed eyes. The criterion is around 6 ms; the panel uses >6 and text uses ≥6. Without individual values, handle equality as unspecified. Observed binary differences are +20.2 and +7.1 points respectively. If all 52 originally assigned eyes per arm had a latent binary outcome and every unmeasured eye could have either outcome, the all-eye difference ranges **−59.6 to +67.3 points**. With no unmeasured active-arm responders, seven unmeasured placebo responders would erase the observed count difference on this common denominator. This is a missingness sensitivity of the exploratory threshold result, not a CI or an adjustment of the reported continuous contrast. It does not treat two eyes as independent people. Cohort selection, equipment change and later therapy remain relevant. [McMurran et al., Fig. 1 and Methods](https://doi.org/10.1177/13524585241233177) · [Replayed sensitivities](comparisons.tsv)
- **Exposure and limits.** In Ghosal et al., six healthy adults treated versus six placebo, oral drug produced an average total peak of 1.46 µM in plasma (section 3.1 prose). Over 95% of all CSF samples were below the 21 nM quantification limit. Five of six treated adults had at least one quantifiable sample. The estimated quantifiable-subset plasma-to-CSF ratio was around 85:1 (about 1.18% when inverted). This is not a juvenile free-brain ratio. Total CSF apoE area was about 25% higher. ApoE fractional kinetics did not clearly change and the assay did not resolve lipidation. Adult systemic oncology approval concerns another disease, and US pediatric safety and efficacy are unestablished. Thyroid, lipid, hepatic, marrow and reproductive risks matter for a developmental endpoint, independently of baseline MS risk. A formulation and age-appropriate unbound central exposure and engagement bridge remain missing. [Healthy-adult trial, Figs. 1–2](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trci.2016.06.001) · [US oral prescribing information, §§5, 7 and 8.4](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=1d056725-0576-4338-8089-6336e768ccdc&type=display)

## Discriminating WWOX test

**Inference.** Across the sources, more APOE, clearance of debris and maintenance of existing myelin are each compatible with the observed drug responses. None fixes the direction of sterol transfer to a WWOX oligodendrocyte or the direction of first-sheath formation. The strongest cross-model native RNA direction is less LXR transcript, while the output signatures for exporters differ. This prioritizes measuring recipient and donor competence before assigning a target deficit.

**Hypothesis.** In a developmentally matched WWOX preparation with viable, initially unmyelinated axons, a lipid-delivery limitation could be separable from a recipient metabolic limitation. The APP/PS1 pattern makes cell-specific lipid retention and selective synthesis credible alternatives to a global export increase. A favorable effect confined to injury-associated debris processing would have a different target population.

**One comparison.** In a debris-low juvenile WWOX versus corrected/WT preparation, match unbound local bexarotene and include donor-specific export interruption and recipient-specific RXR interruption. Compare donor-to-recipient stable-isotope sterol label in lipidated carrier, recipient precursor pool and nascent sheath, and measure absolute amount in the same preparation. Use pre-lipidated carrier to restore basal recipient delivery if donor perturbation lowers it. Record persistent first compact sheaths per initially eligible viable axon and conduction over measured distances at matched recruitment in the same tract. If donor interruption removes transfer and functional gain, and bypass recovers them with the drug dependence occluded, the donor route rises. If gain persists at equal basal delivery, follows recipient RXR and increased recipient incorporation from its own precursor, the direct recipient route rises. If apoE or lineage markers rise without transfer and function, or the effect requires a separate debris challenge, lower the developmental hypothesis. Capture target engagement, baseline genotype deficit, partner dependence and matched survival so loss of a drug response is interpretable.

**Next.** No clinical advancement is supported. Reopen for a selective juvenile WWOX response on first sheaths and conduction at measured exposure or for a verified compartment-specific deficit that can be bypassed. Independent review would be decision-changing if it finds a full factorial myeloid drug-genotype comparison with the same direct conduction and ultrastructural readout, or publicly usable flux data in a debris-free juvenile model. The current material provides candidates for this contrast. Next queue selection returns to a distinct tractable pathway after checking work already assigned.
