**Prediction.** If persistent sodium current is limiting, the decrease in late recurrence should follow independently matched suppression of that current while fast first recruitment and inhibitory recruitment remain usable. A reproducible extra effect after matching the persistent sodium current would favor a second mechanism if it tracks direct release or native GABA measures. Reduced bulk firing alone cannot resolve either pathway.
