# Cenobamate · adolescent exposure and age-specific limits

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN · total plasma µg/mL, calculated free plasma µM, participants with PGTC responses

EMA's Article 46 report EMADOC-1700519818-2992581 (pp. 6–10) describes study YKP3089C025 in patients with primary generalized tonic-clonic (PGTC) seizures, 169 randomized overall and 34 adolescents aged 12 to under 18. Adolescents randomized 18 versus 16. During maintenance, ≥50% responders were 15/18 vs 10/16, a descriptive +20.8 percentage points. Newcombe's independent-score 95% CI is −8.7 to +47.0 points; this is a reanalysis of the published counts, not the regulator's test (reported p = 0.1693). For independent Wilson limits \(L_T,U_T,L_C,U_C\), the Newcombe limits equal observed difference minus `sqrt((p_T-L_T)**2+(U_C-p_C)**2)` and plus `sqrt((U_T-p_T)**2+(p_C-L_C)**2)`. Median PGTC-frequency changes during double-blind treatment were −79.62% and −63.71%, p = 0.1762; these are a different estimand from maintenance responders. Neither comparison resolves genotype-specific WWOX efficacy. The study was powered for the overall population. [EMA assessment, printed pp. 6–8](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/variation-report/ontozry-pam-0000336294-epar-assessment-report_en.pdf)

In the adolescent active-arm exposure subset, visits 11 and 13 gave median total pre-dose **13.1 µg/mL** at both visits (n=13 and 15), and post-dose medians of **16.1** and **15.0 µg/mL** (n=15 at each visit). The observed maxima at the two post-dose sampling windows are 19.2 and 20.8 µg/mL. Using the adult label molecular weight and in vitro unbound fraction, `ug_per_ml * 1000 / 267.67 * 0.40`, gives approximately **19.6 µM** estimated free predose, **24.1 and 22.4 µM** estimated free postdose medians, and **31.1 µM** at the largest observed postdose sample. Sparse post-dose samples give no Cmax. The values are cross-sectional summaries at each time and may include repeated participants. If pediatric free fraction matched the adult label and free brain:plasma were 0.5 or 1, the postdose median scenarios would be **11–12** and **22–24 µM** respectively. The assumptions do not establish unbound forebrain measurements. At the larger postdose median of 24.1 µM calculated free plasma, a free-brain:plasma ratio of approximately 1.24, 1.52 or 1.73 would be needed to reach the 30 µM evoked-GABA observation, the 36.63 µM tonic EC₅₀ or the 41.8 µM persistent-current IC₅₀ respectively. Each ratio is simply reference concentration divided by 24.1 µM. These comparisons prioritize testing submaximal effects at the lower plausible exposure and give no estimate of pharmacodynamic effect size.
