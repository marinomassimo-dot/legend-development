## First measurable contrast

The FDA nonclinical review's rat hippocampal CA3 concentration-response panel gives **41.8 µM** for half-inhibition of persistent sodium current, versus **79.2 µM** for carbamazepine, **118 µM** for lamotrigine and **2.0 µM** for riluzole in the same panel. The same FDA review reports a **42–194 µM** EC₅₀ range for GABA potentiation across six recombinant receptor subtypes. A separate primary report gives **36.63 µM** for tonic GABA current and **164 µM** for GABA-evoked current in its rat hippocampal experiments. A peak-to-potency comparison therefore favors testing persistent current and tonic conductance before assigning an equivalent effect on evoked inhibitory charge. These assays use different cells, agonist conditions and response scales. [FDA nonclinical review p. 8, panels A–B](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2019/212839Orig1s000PharmR.pdf) · [Sharma et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32325146/)

The FDA clinical review's healthy adult QT study gives a steady-state geometric mean peak of **23.1 µg/mL** in the 200 mg/day study arm. With molecular weight 267.67 g/mol and 60% in vitro plasma binding, `23.1 * 1000 / 267.67 * 0.40` gives **34.5 µM estimated free plasma**. If the free brain:plasma ratio were 0.25, 0.5 or 1 at that moment, free brain would be **8.6**, **17.3** or **34.5 µM**. These are sensitivity scenarios, with no assumed transfer coefficient or pediatric equivalent. Under the middle scenario the concentration is approximately **0.41×** the rat persistent-current IC₅₀, **0.47×** tonic-current EC₅₀ and **0.11×** evoked-current EC₅₀. Concentration ratios compare reference scales and give no fractional response. A rat tissue/free exposure or human CSF measurement could reorder the comparisons. [FDA clinical review, study YKP3089C020](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2019/212839Orig1s000MedR.pdf) · [US label §§11 and 12.3](https://www.dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=565c2126-57ae-4e29-b443-723bbe7e2072&type=display)

In the held juvenile WWOX cortical histogram **85–100 of 119** pooled field events fall below one second. A generic long-step model can therefore miss the native event. Use these brief waveforms first, with a prolonged condition as a positive control, and check the natural genotype voltage alongside a matched-voltage condition. Measure interneuron spikes, tonic conductance, postsynaptic firing, evoked charge and propagation in the same preparation. Preserve native chloride during the measurement and record the GABA reversal relative to the operating voltage and spike threshold. A depolarizing reversal can still shunt depolarization. ## Prediction to discriminate

Compare vehicle, cenobamate, a sodium comparator matched on the persistent current and on recovery between pulses, and a separate GABA perturbation matched on tonic conductance. Retain a combined comparator to test whether independently matching those two effects reproduces cenobamate. Compare inhibition within the brief WWOX burst and the ability to propagate the first physiologically evoked spike. The dual-mechanism hypothesis predicts extra late-burst suppression above the sodium-matched comparator when tonic conductance is net inhibitory, with preserved early recruitment. The gain should follow measured chloride and change when the GABA component is matched. If the benefit follows sodium-current inhibition alone, or reduced bursts accompany impaired recruitment or propagation, the proposed circuit advantage fails. Match separate preparations by biological replicate, with age, baseline voltage and perturbation order recorded. Use corrected or WT preparations to test selectivity. A clean acute electrophysiological result would support a symptomatic hypothesis. Developmental recovery needs a separate persistent functional endpoint.

## Developmental and human comparator checkpoint

In P7 rat PTZ experiments, the high arms had fewer stage-5 events, while the latency to first motor signs showed no detectable shift. Adult focal-epilepsy TMS showed a change in motor threshold with no detectable paired-pulse inhibition signal. Candidate benefit should therefore be tested on progression after onset while directly measuring GABA-dependent recruitment. [Source checks and limitations](early_and_late_20261126.md)

## Pediatric feasibility and next checkpoint

The current US label supports adult focal seizures. The registered open-label focal-seizure trial includes ages 2–17. The sponsor reports a supplemental application; an application does not establish approval. The previous LGS case series included 16 people, eight pediatric. Its 12 responders are a mixed-age count. The existing label makes background clobazam and phenytoin important for attribution and flags QT shortening and DRESS. No WWOX-specific effect follows from these records. Reopen for an attributable WWOX clinical observation or for a response at measured free exposure that preserves physiological recruitment after matching both sodium readouts.
