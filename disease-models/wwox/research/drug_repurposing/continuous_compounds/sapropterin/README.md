**Opening checkpoint, 27 November 2026.** Sapropterin supplies (6R)-tetrahydrobiopterin (BH4). It asks whether a cofactor or recycling defect could limit coupled nitric oxide synthase (NOS) at adequate local arginine, a different bottleneck from the L-citrulline substrate hypothesis. The regulatory pediatric PKU experience is a feasibility anchor. It does not establish local brain BH4 exposure, neurodevelopmental efficacy or WWOX tolerability. [L-citrulline comparison](../l_citrulline/README.md) · [Pediatric SPARK trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5343543/)

**Prior prediction.** At matched local arginine and preserved first recruitment, a WWOX-associated fall in rapidly stabilized BH4 relative to 7,8-BH2, accompanied by lower NOS-derived nitric oxide/citrulline flux and higher NOS-dependent superoxide, would favor a cofactor or recycling constraint. A measured rise in intracellular BH4:BH2 followed by coordinated correction of both products and circuit recovery would support that interpretation. A benefit without NOS dependence would favor a different mechanism, including direct redox protection. A shift limited to catecholamines or serotonin would require a monoamine turnover or clearance comparison. If exposure mainly increases BH2 or lacks measurable change in the chosen neural compartment, the delivery or recycling bottleneck remains unresolved. [Primary reductionist eNOS comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19011239/) · [Primary alternative redox mechanism](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6978838/)

**First discriminating comparison.** Contrast BH4/BH2 and arginine-to-citrulline/NO flux at a fixed arginine pool together with product-specific superoxide and recovery, in a developmentally matched model with a NOS dependency control. Keep neuronal NOS and endothelial NOS distinct. Endothelial binding, cell-model ferroptosis and hepatic phenylalanine handling are comparison mechanisms. None establishes the direction of WWOX neuronal effects. First use existing WWOX RNA to look for a replicated synthesis/recycling signal and identify which NOS and cell classes are represented. Then check whether published PKU animal data resolve brain BH4 from BH2 and whether stronger pterin-specific human evidence can separate CNS delivery from a systemic phenylalanine change. [PKU mouse primary comparator](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4706464/)

**First RNA comparison.** In P17 mouse cortex (4 control, 3 knockout), P17 mouse hippocampus (2 control, 3 knockout) and week-15 human organoids (2 control, 4 knockout), mean SPR differences in log₂(1 + normalized expression) are −0.40, −1.02 and −0.65, with the same sign after any single library omission. These are contrasts within accessions, with distinct developmental context and cell composition. In the different juvenile mouse hippocampal nuclei contrast, aggregate SPR is similar at 5.53 versus 5.55 CPM in one control and one neuronal-deletion pool. This makes a pervasive neuronal SPR deficit uncertain. GCH1 decreases in both bulk mouse regions but increases in organoids. QDPR drops consistently under omission in the two mouse bulk cohorts and crosses zero in organoids. DHFR shows no consistent drop.
