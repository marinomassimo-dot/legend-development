# Sapropterin | Juvenile hippocampal assay margin and disposition

**Decision.** Park sapropterin as a conditional preclinical probe. Defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. The available data can screen measurement architectures, while the paired BH4/BH2 quantification threshold and recovery remain unverified in a weighed P17 WWOX hippocampal sample. Reopen for measured low-level paired recovery in an age- and region-matched neural matrix and either a local rise in reduced BH4 or a demonstrated reversible WWOX cofactor deficit. [Prior exposure, comparators and NOS prediction](exposure_and_nos_coupling_20261127.md)

## How much reaches the column

**Electrochemical comparator.** The optimized Vujacic-Mirski method separates ascorbate from BH4. Its Table 1 gives on-column quantification limits of **4.49 pmol BH4 and 0.38 pmol BH2** and a 20 µL injection. The published brain preparation uses roughly 1 g of rat tissue. At the Kim hippocampal anchor, even 30 mg contain only **2.67 pmol BH4 in total** before handling, below that BH4 on-column limit. At 10 mg the total is 0.89 pmol. Concentrating the same available extract cannot overcome this total material limit with the published sensitivity. This comparison is against the named electrochemical implementation. [Vujacic-Mirski Table 1, §§2.4–2.5 and Fig. 5](https://doi.org/10.3390/antiox11061182)

**Paired mass spectrometry.** The corrected Arning and Bottiglieri CSF table gives a 3–200 nmol/L calibration range and 1 nmol/L detection limit for each pterin. Methods use 30 µL sample prepared to 240 µL and 10 µL injected. The lowest calibration point corresponds to **3.75 fmol** per analyte injected under nominal recovery (1.25 fmol at the stated detection limit). Precision controls were reported at higher levels. Use the lower calibration point as a reference scenario, with no implied juvenile tissue LLOQ. The Shen mouse-tissue method cited by Hung specifies adding four volumes to the supernatant and a 6.25–400 nmol/L working calibrator range. Its description does not resolve low-end hippocampal matrix recovery or the minimum tissue mass. [CSF methods](https://doi.org/10.1007/8904_2014_336) · [Corrected Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1007/8904_2014_372) · [Shen, Analysis of BH4 and BH2](https://doi.org/10.1093/hmg/ddx032)

**Inference.** BH2 is the limiting channel for the low, relatively reduced scenario. For the reported whole-brain comparison the margin is larger. It cannot substitute for the age- and region-matched baseline. At fixed 1 mg per 10 µL and injection volume, increasing wet mass alone does not increase the predicted on-column amount. Sensitivity changes only if it improves the tissue-to-solvent ratio, usable injection fraction or practical handling losses. Whole homogenate chemistry and vascular contribution remain distinct from NOS1-specific engagement. [Sensitivity grid](juvenile_assay_load.tsv)

## Separate true cofactor loss from handling artifact

**Discriminating prediction.** If fraction `a` of native BH4 converts to measured BH2 after collection, and the two analytes otherwise have unchanged recovery, the observed ratio is `(1−a)R/(1+aR)` for true ratio `R`. At `R=10`, 1% conversion returns 9.0 and 5% returns 6.33. A difference in conversion of about **2.22 percentage points** relative to immediate zero-conversion handling can mimic a 20% ratio reduction in this specific scenario. This is a simplified sensitivity model. Additional oxidation to biopterin, unequal recovery and ion suppression require separate measurements. [Oxidation sensitivity](juvenile_assay_oxidation.tsv)

**One comparison.** Qualify the assay by comparing immediately stabilized aliquots and matched delays in an age- and region-matched reference matrix. Measure both analytes and, where feasible, oxidized biopterin. Use separate early BH4 spikes and BH2 recovery spikes, or distinguishable tracer labels, to resolve BH4-derived labeled BH2 from added BH2 standard. Compare the low end and process blanks against known amounts. A ratio shift that follows early tracer conversion supports a handling artifact. A difference that survives correction, has both channels in the qualified range, and tracks reduced BH4 and NOS1-normalized NO plus superoxide supports the biological comparison. If only one channel is qualified, carry the concentration or bound for that analyte and keep the native ratio unresolved. A preclinical assay review can change the decision when existing juvenile matrix recovery traces or sample-mass records are available. Pediatric trial feasibility still depends on verified neural exposure, function and interpretable safety beyond the PKU experience.
