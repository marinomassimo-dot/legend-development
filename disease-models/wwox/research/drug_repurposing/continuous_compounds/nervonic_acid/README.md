# Nervonic acid and very-long-chain myelin lipids in WWOX

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Decision.** Retain cis-15-tetracosenoic acid (24:1n−9) as a conditional isotope tracer and precursor comparator. Defer pediatric WWOX intervention priority. Resolve native precursor, esterified product and sheath delivery separately before testing whether more substrate helps.

## Native disease systems

**Data.** Neuronal Wwox loss was sufficient for hypomyelination and impaired oligodendrocyte maturation. The published conditional glial losses did not reproduce that phenotype under the studied conditions. In P17 neuronal-KO cortex (4 control, 3 KO libraries) and hippocampus (2 control, 3 KO), the ELOVL1/CERS2 expression changes are −0.61/−0.66 and −1.21/−1.35 in mean log2(1+expression). UGT8A, GAL3ST1 and GLTP fall in both mouse regions. The myelin markers MBP, PLP1 and MOG fall at least comparably. The direction is different in 15-week human organoids (2 control, 4 KO libraries). ELOVL1 is −0.11, while CERS2 is +0.40, UGT8 +0.15 and GLTP +0.35. These signs survive single-library omission. Absolute transcript abundance and developmental composition differ among models. [Repudi et al.](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awab174) · [Prior WWOX myelin review](../clemastine/axonal_denominator.md) · [Bulk replay](native_bulk.tsv)

**Composition check.** In one WWOX hippocampal nucleus pool per genotype, the myelin-forming oligodendrocyte class has 186 control and 49 KO nuclei. ELOVL1 is about 60→63 CPM, CERS2 208→237 and GAL3ST1 274→76. In the mature class, MBP and MOG also drop sharply (3,702→619 and 948→130 CPM). For the CERS2 contrast across all assigned nuclei (−5.99 CPM), changes in marker-class weights account algebraically for −4.87 CPM, with −1.12 CPM from within-class changes. This decomposition is descriptive. The classifier uses mature or precursor marker expression, including MOG and PDGFRA. Single genotype pools, dropout and uneven state within classes prevent animal replication or an intrinsic enzyme inference. Direct chain-resolved juvenile WWOX acyl-CoA and myelin measurements were not identified in the reviewed records. [Pooled counts](cell_types.tsv) · [Mixture decomposition](mixture.tsv)

**Inference.** Mouse WWOX RNA supports examining developmental state and the entire pathway. It leaves a selective 24:1 supply lesion less plausible than an interpretation based on ELOVL1 alone. The native neuronal phenotype also permits an axon-to-glia effect on any of these steps. In the ALD fibroblast comparator, 5-day exposure to added free acid yielded more than threefold higher C24:1 in the total lipid extract than erucic acid according to the Results. C24:1 ceramide and sphingomyelin were generally reported as comparable between fatty acids. These are reported arm comparisons across two ALD lines and a healthy control line, without an equivalence test or isotope accounting. A substrate that competes with saturated elongation in ALD could shift both useful and potentially damaging pools in a system with a different starting state. The hypoperfusion mouse primary abstract reports less oligodendrocyte death after oral exposure and in oxygen-glucose-deprived cultures. Cell preservation in injury is a competing explanation for total myelin marker increases. [Terluk et al., Methods and Figs. 1–3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9294126/) · [Zheng et al., primary abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0014299924006216)

## Pediatric feasibility

Yu et al. measured **0.76 versus 0.20 mg/g milk fat** in colostrum and mature milk across 224 serial samples from eight mothers of term infants. This is total analytically recovered 24:1n−9 in milk lipid after methylation. It does not resolve free parent or transport to the infant brain. In Sala-Vila et al., the published abstract reports higher 24:1 in plasma lipid fractions in breastfed term infants at 3 months than in either formula group. Feeding groups differ in whole diets and lipid sources. That comparison cannot quantify isolated-parent exposure. Bettger et al.'s primary abstract describes pup heart and liver sphingomyelin after altered maternal dietary lipid mixtures. Carroll's primary abstract describes little radiolabel in 12-day and adult rat brain lipids after labeled fatty acids. Radiolabel alone would not identify intact acid in a particular sheath lipid. Li et al. observed no significant body-weight or food-intake differences over four weeks, with four mice per group. These data give no comparable pediatric formulation, CNS pharmacokinetics, chronic tolerability or interaction data with background antiseizure medicines. Record a defined chemical form, purity and vehicle before making any exposure comparison across oil mixtures or free acid. [Yu et al., §§2.2–3.1](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11081892) · [Sala-Vila et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15051839/) · [Bettger et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12742544/) · [Carroll, primary abstract](https://doi.org/10.1139/o62-137) · [Li et al., Results](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neurot.2026.e00890)

## One discriminating comparison

**Hypothesis.** In juvenile WWOX neuron–oligodendrocyte preparations, low usable endogenous 24:1 may constrain myelin assembly only when downstream acyl incorporation and delivery remain competent. A competing result is maturation-linked loss of active incorporation or trafficking in recipient oligodendrocytes. A second alternative is reduced supply or eligibility of axons with preserved lipid delivery.

**Experiment.** Compare litter-matched controls and neuronal-Wwox-loss preparations with matched axonal and initial oligodendrocyte competence. Keep the reciprocal neuron–glia pairing to localize the baseline defect. Compare vehicle and a measured local exposure of chain-labeled 24:1n−9, using a matched labeled upstream precursor such as 22:1n−9 to test bypass of elongation. Normalize to measured intracellular precursor, and use molecular fragments to distinguish intact acyl-chain incorporation from recycled label. During a pulse and chase, measure absolute 24:1 acyl-CoA, GalCer and sulfatide in producing cells and delivered myelin, with saturated C24 and shorter species retained as controls. Track persistent first sheaths per initially eligible axon, survival and conduction at matched path length and early recruitment.

- Low baseline acyl-CoA with selective recovery of absolute product, delivery and function after direct 24:1 favors precursor limitation. A common response in control and WWOX is compatible with general lipid support.
- Normalized labeled acyl-CoA with little labeled mature product favors incorporation or downstream synthesis. More fractional labeling without a larger absolute product pool can reflect replacement of existing lipid.
- Recovered total mature product with reduced sheath delivery favors transport. Restored delivery with unchanged persistent sheaths and conduction puts the defect later, including axon eligibility, assembly or loss.

**Next decision.** Prioritize the paired native precursor and absolute product measurements in the same juvenile preparation. A membrane effect in culture alone cannot reopen pediatric study priority. A new independent review would change this decision if it identifies a chain-resolved WWOX dataset at the relevant stage or a measured local exposure and selective rescue. Recounting published bulk signals from the same libraries cannot supply either.
