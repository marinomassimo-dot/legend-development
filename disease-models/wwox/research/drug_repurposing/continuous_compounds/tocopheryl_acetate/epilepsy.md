# Seizure outcomes and the proposed membrane mechanism
context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Question.** Does the epilepsy evidence identify a benefit specific to the RRR ester, and does it link seizure control to lower lipid oxidation? The published outcomes have different denominators.

**Mechanism check.** In the 2016 study, mean serum malondialdehyde (nmol/mL, Table 2) started lower on the intervention (12.2 versus 15.4) and ended lower (11.6 versus 15.5). The difference in reported mean changes is −0.7 nmol/mL. Table 2 gives P = 0.07 for the changes, while the Fig. 2D caption gives P = 0.003 for a trend. The estimands and model are insufficiently specified to reconcile them. The serum thiobarbituric acid assay lacks a parent-lipid denominator. Neither the aggregate seizure summaries nor the EEG counts link individual lipid and clinical responses. Keep the trial estimates separate. Age modification cannot be identified across different designs and formulations. [Mehvari, Methods and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4815530/)

**Counterexample from transport deficiency.** In male Ttpa-null mice fed from weaning to 40 weeks, either RRR or all-rac acetate normalized elevated spinal Tnf RNA. The spinal 4-HNE assay and white-matter area showed no group difference. The cerebral cortex accumulated less total tocopherol under the RRR diet than under the higher-mass synthetic diets. Stereoisomers were measured in one pooled composite per group. These data allow a transcriptional response without a detectable parallel change in this lipid marker. Small samples and single-section morphology limit sensitivity. WWOX bulk TTPA RNA is low across the three preparations, which does not establish transporter deficiency of this magnitude. [Ranard et al., Diets, Results for Figs. 1, 3 and 4 and supplemental Fig. 6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7947595/)

**Disposition.** Park for an unselected pediatric WWOX trial. Pediatric precedent supports feasibility of collecting seizure diaries, with separate assessment needed for infants, concomitant drugs, nutritional status and exposure. The existing studies leave WWOX efficacy and developmental tolerability open. Reopen for a genotype-by-treatment effect in an oxidation-high WWOX neural preparation: recovery of evoked transmission alongside decreased radical-derived products per unit parent lipid, at matched membrane free tocopherol, substrate, viability and firing burden. A fall in peripheral markers, isolated Tnf normalization, or suppression of all firing would leave priority low. A documentary review could clarify the older trials, but neither trial measures the decisive WWOX functional endpoint.
