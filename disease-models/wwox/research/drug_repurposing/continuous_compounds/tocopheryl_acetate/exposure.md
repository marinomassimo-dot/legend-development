# Stereochemistry and infant brain exposure
context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Human tissue.** In 36 infant decedents, the mean ratio of RRR-alpha-tocopherol to the sum of the three other 2R stereoisomers was 2.5 in frontal cortex, 2.3 in hippocampus and 2.4 in visual cortex. The 79 regional specimens included all three regions for 14 donors. The observational series describes tissue composition. Individual dietary exposure, WWOX status and a functional treatment response cannot be inferred. [Primary study, methods p. 127, results and Fig. 3 pp. 128–129](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114516001719)

**Controlled feeding comparator.** At six months, macaques fed RRR-acetate formula (8) had higher occipital RRR than those fed all-rac-acetate formula (7). The mixed-diet group (8) is a separate comparator. The calculations below divide published group means for the two formula arms. RRR content in the formulas was calculated by the authors from source composition. [Primary study, Tables 1, 3 and 6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7467853/)

| Measurement | RRR formula | All-rac formula | Ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Formula RRR, µmol/L | 20.9 | 3.70 | 5.65 |
| Formula total, µmol/L | 20.9 | 29.7 | 0.70 |
| Plasma RRR, µmol/L | 30.9 | 18.2 | 1.70 |
| Occipital RRR, nmol/g | 27.3 | 17.2 | 1.59 |
| Occipital total, nmol/g | 28.9 | 23.9 | 1.21 |

**Interpretation.** Occipital RRR accounted for roughly 94% versus 72% of total alpha-tocopherol from the ratios of means. No region showed a statistically significant diet effect on total alpha-tocopherol. Formulas were matched by international units, differed in carotenoids, and delivered different RRR amounts. The use of saponification before analysis prevents attribution to pre-existing free versus esterified tissue pools. These data support dietary control over brain stereoisomer composition. They cannot establish superior antioxidant action at equal membrane exposure, a brain-to-plasma transfer rate or a pediatric exposure target. [Diet and analytical methods, Tables 1, 3 and 6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7467853/)

**Consequence for WWOX.** Preserve both total free alpha-tocopherol and its RRR fraction as denominators. Compare oxidation per unit parent lipid and recovery of evoked neural activity after matched free membrane exposure. A difference that disappears after matching supports delivery or retention as the explanation. A residual genotype-dependent functional benefit would warrant testing whether the stereoisomer contributes beyond delivery. Current WWOX transcript data and infant tissue studies leave both outcomes open.
