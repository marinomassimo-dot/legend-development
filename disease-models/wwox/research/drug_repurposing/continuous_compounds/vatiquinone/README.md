# Vatiquinone | Lipoxygenase-dependent phospholipid oxidation
context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Opening checkpoint, 27 November 2026.** Vatiquinone (EPI-743/PTC743, α-tocotrienol quinone) follows the pregnenolone disposition. This is a separate quinone from vitamin E acetate and omaveloxolone. The question is whether WWOX-LoF recovery is limited by a measurable, target-dependent oxidized phospholipid burden that can be reduced without losing physiological recruitment. Vatiquinone has a clinical epilepsy comparator in a distinct genetic disease class. Its negative primary endpoint makes it possible to calibrate the claimed transfer with controlled clinical evidence. [Pregnenolone disposition](../pregnenolone/assay_feasibility_and_disposition_20261127.md) · [WWOX redox comparator](../omaveloxolone/exposure_and_disposition_20261127.md) · [MIT-E trial](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04378075)

**Data anchors.** After nominal 1 µM vatiquinone for 3 h, RARS2-associated PCH6 fibroblast experiments detected 45–62% of the dosed compound as hydroquinone (Fig. 3A, six replicates for each of three cell lines). For purified human 15-LO, hydroquinone IC50 was 4.4 µM (Fig. 3B, n=2); parent gave about 20% inhibition at 100 µM. In one PCH6 fibroblast line under RSL3, 200 nM parent prevented the 15-HETE rise but not 12-HETE (17 replicates from three independent experiments, Fig. 3C–D). The direct hydroperoxide was below consistent quantification. This is acute chemically provoked fibroblast biology. Enzyme IC50, reported nominal cellular concentrations and free neural concentrations each require separate interpretation. In another laboratory, vatiquinone reproduced some acute cell protection, failed against two other oxidative stress insults and did not significantly improve survival in the inducible Gpx4 or Ndufs4(-/-) mouse models. P30 rotarod-provoked seizures were absent in six treated Ndufs4(-/-) mice, with Fisher p=0.156 against combined controls (n=24). It is a small provoked signal. Spontaneous seizure effects remain open. [Kahn-Kirby et al., Figs. 3–4](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0214250) · [Kayser et al., Figs. 1, 3–4](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13023-025-03582-x)

**Clinical countercheck.** MIT-E enrolled 68, with 34 per arm in the posted intent-to-treat comparison. At week 24, median changes in the 28-day count of observable motor seizures were −12.74% and −0.33% for vatiquinone and placebo. The registry's separately reported ANCOVA comparison is −8.31 percentage points, 95% CI −31.3 to +16.4, p=0.173. The simple subtraction of medians is −12.41 points and answers a different descriptive question. The registry records completion of the primary assessment and gives sponsor decision as the reason for overall termination. In the double-blind period, 18/34 vatiquinone participants and 9/34 placebo participants had a serious adverse event. The descriptive difference is +26.5 percentage points. Deaths were 3/34 and 0/34. These are all-cause aggregate counts from a small, severely affected population. Event terms, timing, attribution, genotype mix and background drugs require review before interpretation. Age-stratified safety and antiseizure comedication balance are unavailable in the extracted aggregate rows. The FDA complete response for Friedreich ataxia describes failure on the prespecified MOVE-FA primary and key secondary endpoints. These are distinct outcomes and disease populations. **Prespecified first comparison.** Screen the held WWOX cortical, hippocampal and organoid RNA for ALOX15, ALOX15B, ALOX12, PEBP1, ACSL4, LPCAT3, GPX4, SLC7A11 and AIFM2, plus NQO1 as a candidate reduction-pathway marker. Retain developmental stage and model, normalized abundance, detection counts and single-library omissions. Neither RNA nor a broad oxidative stress readout can identify endogenous lipid peroxide production. Identify whether a public WWOX preparation already measures pre- or post-activity oxidized phospholipids by species and compartment. In matched WWOX and control neural preparations, at measured quinone and hydroquinone concentrations, a selective improvement in post-activation recovery should track a fall in prespecified endogenous oxidized phospholipids and converge with a target-selective reference. An equivalent change from a broad antioxidant, a primary decrease in first recruitment, or protection only after direct GPX4 inhibition would weaken the WWOX-specific mechanism. Separate human and mouse lipoxygenase regioselectivity before choosing the product panel. [Recombinant comparative lipid products](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13246792/)

**Initial WWOX comparison.** The edited bulk screen finds consistent SLC7A11 decreases in P17 cortex and hippocampus and week-15 human cerebral organoids (mean KO minus control log₂(1+normalized expression), −0.37/−0.73/−0.45). GPX4 is −0.14/−0.74/−0.42, with one-library omission crossing zero in cortex. The neuronal-deletion hippocampal ALOX15 row is detected in one control and one KO library (2 controls and 3 KO total), close to the measurement floor. In human organoids (2 controls and 4 KO), ALOX15 has a small descriptive increase (+0.23) at low normalized abundance. PEBP1 decreases in the two mouse regions and increases in organoids. Mouse Alox15b is mapped via Alox8 in the archived Ensembl reference and decreases in both mouse contrasts. NQO1 decreases in P17 hippocampus (−0.81) and human organoids (−0.43), raising the conditional possibility that parent-to-hydroquinone conversion itself may limit response. Neither the contribution of NQO1 nor actual reduction capacity is assigned by this screen. These signals support measuring peroxide disposal and active metabolite alongside target-derived production. They leave baseline peroxide burden and the cell source unresolved. [Replay](replay_wwox_lipid_peroxide_expression.py) · [All transcript rows](wwox_lipid_peroxide_expression.tsv)

**Species control.** Recombinant human ALOX15 on free arachidonate yielded about 10:1 15-HETE to 12-HETE in Chen et al. Fig. 1I, and mouse Alox15 about 2:10 in Fig. 1L–M. Human ALOX15B and mouse Alox15b also had different major products. The first animal experiment should therefore not use a human 15-HETE-only signature, and either marker can have contributions from other enzymes. For the proposed mechanism prioritize identified oxidized esterified phospholipids, with free eicosanoids as separate context. A next calculation should ask whether the same active-metabolite exposure can align the lipid and circuit effects in both species. [Chen et al., Fig. 1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-026-10233-9)
