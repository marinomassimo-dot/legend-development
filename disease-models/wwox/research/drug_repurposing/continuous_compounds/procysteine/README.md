# Procysteine, cysteine supply versus glutathione replacement

28 November 2026 UTC · Continuous compound #210 · INV-07 · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. Sources include female rat brain extracts (µmol/g tissue and isotopic enrichment), unlocalized rat head-coil spectroscopy (ppm), adult human CSF and peripheral lymphocytes (nmol/mg protein), juvenile-rat-derived astroglial culture (nominal µM or mM), and P17 neuronal-Wwox-deletion mouse cortex and hippocampus bulk RNA (TPM), week-15 human WWOX-knockout cerebral organoid bulk RNA (library CPM), and P17 pooled hippocampal nuclei (UMI and CPM). [Prespecified comparison](pretest.md) · [Opening checkpoint](first_comparison.md)

**Decision.** Retain procysteine as a conditional preclinical tracer of cysteine supply and partition into glutathione. Defer pediatric WWOX prioritization. Rat brain extracts show incorporation of precursor carbon into the glutathione pool. The size of that pool and the labeling fraction dissociate. Adult CSF confirms accessible parent in another disease and leaves juvenile tissue concentration, reaction rate and functional benefit unresolved. Lower OPLAH RNA in three WWOX bulk datasets motivates an upstream conversion control.

## Reproducible result

Run `python3 -B replay.py` to regenerate [comparison.tsv](comparison.tsv). At 4 h the rat tissue cysteine mean was **2.23×** the control mean and GSH was **0.85×**. At 20 h GSH was **1.24×** and hypotaurine **1.62×** by ratio of means. The reported GSH comparison was inconclusive with five treated and four control rats. The 34.8% enrichment establishes incorporation into the cysteinyl residue. An endpoint fraction does not identify whether the synthesis rate or total glutathione increased.

The article's approximate detection scale was **0.2 µmol/g** labeled metabolite. A representative 8.33% GSH enrichment and a total pool of 1.0–1.2 µmol/g would yield **0.083–0.100 µmol/g** of labeled GSH, consistent with the difference between LC-MS and head-coil spectroscopy. Multiplying the 20 h group means gives **0.525 µmol/g** as an illustrative scale. The individual joint measurements are unavailable, so this product of means does not estimate uncertainty or a paired group stock. Labeled hypotaurine and taurine confirm other uses of precursor carbon. They do not identify a dominant branch or exclude delivery of labeled cysteine from outside the brain.

## WWOX expression and discriminating comparison

The [bulk replay](bulk.tsv) gives OPLAH differences in mean log2(1 + abundance) of **−0.426** in mouse cortex (4 controls, 3 knockout), **−0.661** in mouse hippocampus (2/3), and **−0.416** in human organoids (2/4). The sign survives every single-library omission in each matrix. GCLC and GSS do not share a common direction. Both fall in hippocampus. SLC7A11 and SLC1A1 fall in all three. OPLAH reads in [hippocampal nuclei](nuclei.tsv) are sparse and come from a control pool and a knockout pool. Astrocytic OPLAH has 40 versus 29 assigned UMI (27.37 versus 16.85 CPM), with descriptive falls in both neuronal pools. These source-specific observations support testing local conversion across cell types. They do not establish OPLAH enzyme deficiency, substrate concentrations or flux. [Cortex](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) · [Hippocampus](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) · [Organoids](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243) · [Nuclei](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158684)

**Hypothesis.** OPLAH cleavage could limit delivery from procysteine despite low transport-related RNA, and the resulting cysteine may encounter a second constraint at GSH synthesis. The cleavage consumes ATP. Establish baseline redox recovery and native evoked response by genotype and developmental stage. Measure intracellular labeled precursor, initial appearance of retained-carbon cysteine, incorporation into GSH/GSSG, labeled hypotaurine and taurine, and ATP recovery in the same preparation. Native 5-oxoproline is a useful control for altered OPLAH substrate use. [OPLAH biochemistry primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6940159/)

**Discriminating prediction.** Compare vehicle, retained-carbon procysteine and labeled cysteine bypass in matched WWOX and isogenic or littermate control preparations. Compare initial cysteine appearance at matched intracellular procysteine to isolate conversion. Compare new GSH at matched intracellular cysteine exposure to isolate downstream synthesis. If both labels converge on GSH and better redox and evoked recovery, precursor delivery remains plausible. If procysteine yields less cysteine while the bypass converges on GSH, the dominant constraint is upstream. If cysteine rises while glutathione flux and recovery fail, resolve downstream synthesis and diversion. A functional effect that persists with new GSH synthesis independently suppressed would favor an additional mechanism. Require preserved first evoked recruitment and matched workload. Use donor or animal as the independent unit.

## Feasibility and checkpoint

Historical clinical preparations included oral and IV study products. Blood and CSF measurements from other adult indications do not establish a formulation, exposure, age-specific safety or interactions with background antiseizure medications in WWOX. The FDA's 2016 response to the Cystox notification concluded that the submitted product was excluded from the US dietary supplement definition and did not review the submitted safety evidence. This is a product-specific historical regulatory decision. The neurological and childhood evidence retrieved here does not support an assumption of availability or feasibility. [FDA letter, p. 2](https://downloads.regulations.gov/FDA-2016-S-0023-0035/attachment_1.pdf) · [Adult IV trial abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7907662/)

The next decision requires time-resolved isotope incorporation and functional recovery at the same age, compartment and measured exposure. A further review of the same summaries would not provide these data. Reopen if an accessible paired human CSF profile supplies the needed exposure bounds or a native genotype-matched preparation resolves the OPLAH-bypass prediction. Only summary facts, links and original arithmetic are distributed with this dossier.
