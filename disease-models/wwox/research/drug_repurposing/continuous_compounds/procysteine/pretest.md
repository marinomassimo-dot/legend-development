# Procysteine, local cysteine formation and productive glutathione synthesis

28 November 2026 UTC · Continuous compound #210 · INV-07 · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. L-2-oxothiazolidine-4-carboxylate (OTC/OTZ), the L-isomer. Human peripheral thiols, adult human ALS pharmacokinetics and rodent regional brain chemistry are separate preparations. [Previous compound](../l_4_chlorokynurenine/README.md)

**Question.** Does verified local procysteine conversion improve age-matched WWOX neural redox reserve and recovery by supplying cysteine to new glutathione, or does cysteine rise without productive synthesis? This is an independent precursor mechanism relative to N-acetylcysteine and a different entry point relative to gamma-glutamylcysteine. [Held N-acetylcysteine mechanism](../acetylcysteine/mechanism.md) · [Held gamma-glutamylcysteine comparison](../gamma_glutamylcysteine/README.md)

**Prespecified comparison.** At measured local parent and equal neural workload, separate OPLAH-dependent conversion, free cysteine appearance and its incorporation into new glutathione. A label that remains in the cysteine backbone can identify delivery to GSH. Label released as carbon dioxide can indicate systemic cleavage and cannot assign product incorporation or a brain compartment. Measure reduced and oxidized glutathione, cystine/glutamate, ATP and usable first evoked recruitment. Compare with cysteine exposure matched locally, a conversion bypass, and a control that tests whether any functional effect requires new glutathione.

**Prediction.** If precursor supply is limiting, the rise in newly labeled GSH should follow local cysteine formation and precede better redox recovery at matched demand and evoked recruitment. If locally labeled cysteine rises while new GSH and evoked recovery remain unchanged, downstream synthesis or nutrient availability becomes the next bottleneck. An effect on redox without new GSH calls for an independent thiol or substrate-handling explanation. Assess only after defining the WWOX baseline direction.

**First step.** Reconcile adult CSF, blood and lymphocyte PK with regional rat cysteine and glutathione in accessible primary texts. Check whether the published breath tracer reports cleavage or incorporation, and whether reported activity and units can be converted without mixing compartments or tissue-normalization methods.
