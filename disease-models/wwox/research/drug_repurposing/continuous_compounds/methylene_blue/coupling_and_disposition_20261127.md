# Methylene blue chloride | Coupling, substrate-level ATP and WWOX disposition

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Decision.** Retain methylthioninium chloride as a conditional preclinical electron-routing comparator. Defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. The public WWOX RNA profiles offer no consistent evidence for a selectively weaker complex I. In adult brain mitochondria the oxygen response changes with respiratory state, and ATP can increase through another route even when ATP synthase is inhibited. Advance the compound only if native WWOX tissue first shows a functional bottleneck and the dye improves ATP-synthase-dependent recovery at matched workload and verified local exposure.

## Reproducible WWOX comparison

The [prespecified screen](expression_pretest.md) required an absolute decrease of the complex I subunit RNA score and a decrease relative to the equal-weight mean of complexes II–V in three separate WWOX contrasts. It also required stable direction with leave-one-library-out and without mitochondrially encoded transcripts. The score is the equal-weight per-library mean of log₂(1 + TPM) for mouse or log₂(1 + CPM) for human eligible genes. A gene enters when present in the MitoCarta v3.0 complex set and expressed in at least half of the WT libraries. Values below are KO minus WT, rounded. The absolute and relative scores are descriptive within a preparation.

## Inference and discriminating observation

**Inference.** Oxygen use, potential formation and net ATP appearance observe distinct stages of bioenergetics. The result with α-ketoglutarate shows that oxygen and ATP can both rise while ATP synthase is inhibited. A WWOX result based on those totals alone would confound an alternative electron route coupled to oxidative phosphorylation with substrate-level ATP and dye redox cycling.

**Hypothesis.** In a preparation with a verified upstream respiratory limitation, an alternative electron route could increase ATP-synthase-dependent production enough to improve recovery after a defined episode of neuronal work.

**Test.** First establish which substrate-linked deficit occurs in stage-matched WWOX-deficient and corrected cortical or hippocampal preparations, with starting mitochondrial mass and initial recruitment measured. Only then compare paired dye and vehicle states. Record O₂, ATP synthesis or recovery, and peroxide during equivalent demand, plus local parent and relevant redox or metabolite forms when analytically resolved. Include an ATP synthase inhibition arm with verified action, a substrate contrast that distinguishes α-ketoglutarate-linked phosphorylation from succinate, and an orthogonal ATP assay with dye-only blanks. An increase confined to the ATP-synthase-dependent fraction, improved ATP produced per oxygen consumed and preserved first recruitment without disproportionate peroxide would favor coupled recovery. An ATP gain that persists after ATP synthase blockade and tracks succinyl-CoA-linked flux would favor substrate-level phosphorylation. An isolated O₂ rise or excess peroxide would favor unproductive oxidation. A fall in recruitment makes lower demand an alternative explanation for preserved ATP.

**Pediatric feasibility.** The exact injection formulation has pediatric experience for acute acquired methemoglobinemia. The label cites retrospective data from two pediatric recipients of ProvayBlue and 12 recipients of another methylene blue class product. Label restrictions include G6PD deficiency and serotonergic and opioid interactions. The injection experience and the adult MRI result leave repeated pediatric CNS exposure, local unbound active species and WWOX efficacy unresolved. [DailyMed, §§1, 4, 5.1, 8.4, 12.3](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=4f6848e5-35ed-4046-b13c-3032b5ba3232)

**Checkpoint.** Reopen for a WWOX neural preparation with substrate-resolved, mass-normalized respiratory and ATP deficits, or for measured neural active-species exposure that makes a preclinical comparison interpretable. An independent review would be useful if such data looked positive or if a primary source resolved ATP/O by complex at the same exposure. The available RNA result and the substrate-level control already define the immediate decision.

## Replay

Run `python3 replay_primary_contrasts.py` from this dossier directory. The [TSV](primary_contrasts.tsv) contains condition means and direct arithmetic. Gureev's Fig. 1 percentage corresponds to a 19.13-fold ratio. The ratios carry no new confidence intervals or cross-study tests. To replay the [WWOX table](wwox_complex_rna.tsv), pass the WWOX research directory, the LEGEND files directory, and the directory containing the two MitoPathways GMX files to `python3 replay_complex_rna.py RESEARCH FILES GMX`. The [Broad downloads](https://personal.broadinstitute.org/scalvo/MitoCarta_Download/) are the human and mouse MitoPathways 3.0 GMX. The script checks both source hashes and the previously registered GEO and Ensembl inputs. It requires Python and NumPy.
