# Carisbamate opening comparison

| System | Verified opening observation | Limit for WWOX |
|---|---|---|
| Oral suspension, open-label phase 1 in Lennox–Gastaut syndrome | 18 participants, including 7 aged 6–11 and 3 aged 12–17. The planned age 2–5 cohort did not enroll. Sixteen completed the study. | Pediatric plasma sampling can inform the exposure bridge. This study design and age coverage do not establish efficacy, infant exposure, or free CNS levels. [Vashi et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42431071/) |
| Neonatal multiple-hit rat spasms | The primary record reports acute reduction of electroclinical spasms in the monitored P6–7 assay and no detected suppression by phenytoin in its P4 behavioral arm. The full text reports one death among 17 higher-dose P4 pups observed for 24 hours. | Different recording windows and unverified matched target engagement limit the comparison. Suppression over hours is distinct from sustained remission. [Ono et al., methods and results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3169712/) |
| Rat dentate gyrus and recombinant T-type calcium assays | Primary abstracts describe action potential-dependent depression of evoked glutamatergic transmission and an independently studied CaV3.1 effect. | The anatomical location, readout and exposure determine whether each component is a plausible WWOX comparator. [Dentate primary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21824485/) · [CaV3 primary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28230232/) |
| Recruitment, subtype and age | FDA orphan designation is listed for infantile spasms. A separate study record concerns drop seizures in Lennox–Gastaut syndrome. | Drop seizures, electroclinical spasms and developmental outcomes require separate evidence. [FDA orphan record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/opdlisting/oopd/detailedIndex.cfm?cfgridkey=362511) · [Lennox–Gastaut trial record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05219617) |
