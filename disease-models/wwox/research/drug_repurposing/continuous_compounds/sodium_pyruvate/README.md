# Sodium pyruvate and recovery of neural activity
context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Selection.** The existing WWOX organoid analysis investigated the LDH/PDH transcript ratio. ADV-189 evaluated how to interpret a mitochondrial pyruvate sensor. This dossier asks a drug-specific question about adding extracellular sodium pyruvate. An apparent rescue could arise through oxidative fuel use, cytosolic electron acceptance during lactate production, or consumption of peroxide in the medium. All three are plausible in neural cultures. A registered oral sodium pyruvate trial in Leigh disease supplies a pediatric feasibility lead. [WWOX branch-point analysis](../../gse156243/prediction_pyruvate_fate_20261026.md) · [Existing sensor assessment](../../advisor_pilot_20261113/brainstorm_20261116/adv189_tiwari_pyruvate_sensor_sci_20261123/RESULT_ADV189_TIWARI_PYRUVATE_SENSOR_SCI_20261123.md) · [UMIN registry](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000029639)

**Prediction before extraction.** At matched pH, sodium, osmolarity and firing demand, selective recovery of evoked activity together with increased pyruvate-derived mitochondrial carbon oxidation would support a fuel limitation. Recovery accompanied by increased labelled lactate and improved cytosolic NAD+/NADH with little change in oxidation favors electron acceptance. Rapid disappearance of peroxide in cell-free matched medium and loss of protection when peroxide exposure is equalized would instead favor extracellular chemistry. Alpha-ketobutyrate can act both as an electron acceptor and as a chemical peroxide sink, so its protection alone cannot distinguish those two routes. Separate a direct chemical or cell survival effect from restoration of neural function.

**Opening comparison.** Screen plasma-membrane monocarboxylate transporters SLC16A1, SLC16A7 and SLC16A3 and mitochondrial carrier subunits MPC1 and MPC2 in the three untreated WWOX bulk datasets. Record mean KO-minus-control log2(1 + normalized abundance) and omission ranges. Opposing directions across systems weaken a uniform transport-deficiency rationale. Transcripts cannot supply flux direction, NAD redox, transport kinetics or a tissue exposure target. Next reconcile the cell-free comparator with actual culture composition and oral/central exposure.

**Result.** SLC16A7 (MCT2) and MPC2 RNA fell in cortex, hippocampus and organoid. For SLC16A7, contrasts were −0.96, −0.93 and −0.40, respectively. MPC2 contrasts were −0.25, −0.99 and −0.18. The signs survived omission of individual libraries in each system. MPC1 rose in cortex and fell in the other two. Reduced MCT2/MPC2 may constrain response to added substrate; the same pattern could reflect altered cellular composition. Transporter abundance cannot predict whether pyruvate enters neurons, is reduced to lactate, or is oxidized. Reproduce with `python3 screen_pyruvate.py /path/to/files bulk.tsv`. [Library counts, contrasts and omission ranges](bulk.tsv)

**Cell-free check.** Desagher et al. mixed 2 mM pyruvate with 0.2 mM hydrogen peroxide in buffer at 37°C. With no cells present, half of the peroxide remained after two minutes and little remained at 15 minutes (three independent experiments with triplicate measurements). The input molar ratio was 10:1. This establishes that an apparent protective effect in an added-peroxide challenge can arise before neural metabolism is involved. It leaves endogenous oxidant production in WWOX open. The next comparison needs time-resolved extracellular peroxide in the actual organoid medium and basal substrate exposure, then a central exposure bound after the oral formulation. [Primary study, Results for Fig. 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6573585/)

**Nuclear countercheck.** In GSE158684, Slc16a7 fell from 161 to 90 CPM in assigned excitatory nuclei and from 101 to 66 CPM in inhibitory nuclei. Across assigned classes the decline was 62.7 CPM; the within-class component was −60.6 CPM and the change in RNA-depth weights contributed −2.1 CPM. Aggregate Mpc2 instead rose from 54.9 to 59.8 CPM. Thus MCT2 is the stronger lead for a cell-associated difference, while bulk MPC2 does not generalize to this nuclear aggregate. One biological pool per genotype and RNA-based cell assignments preclude independent replication or a functional transporter claim. Reproduce with `python3 nuclei.py /path/to/files`. **Tracer interpretation.** Chemical decarboxylation of uniformly labelled three-carbon pyruvate can itself yield labelled carbon dioxide and two-carbon labelled acetate. Under the published cell-free starting conditions, stoichiometry allows up to 0.20 mM acetate from the supplied peroxide, or 10% of the starting pyruvate. This is a theoretical ceiling; acetate was not measured in that time course. Carbon dioxide alone would therefore be ambiguous. If neurons or glia activate that acetate, even labelled acetyl-CoA in whole cells could include a bypass contribution. Measure labelled acetate in the cell-free matrix, compare its contribution at the observed concentration, and relate corrected carbon oxidation to evoked recovery and cytosolic redox. This ambiguity should be tested under endogenous culture conditions, where peroxide exposure can be much lower. [Reaction and cell-free comparison](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6573585/)

**Pediatric feasibility.** UMIN000025872 specifies age ≥2 years, oral sodium pyruvate, and clinical outcomes in Leigh syndrome, with optional CSF lactate and brain lactate by spectroscopy. A planned lactate endpoint cannot establish post-dose brain pyruvate. The next checkpoint seeks the time-matched oral blood and central pyruvate evidence and the actual basal pyruvate in the WWOX organoid recipe before judging trial priority. [Trial registry](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000029639)
