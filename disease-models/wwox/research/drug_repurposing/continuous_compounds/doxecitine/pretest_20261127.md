# Doxecitine | Prespecified deoxycytidine salvage and mitochondrial copy recovery

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Selection.** Doxecitine (2′-deoxycytidine, dC) is the sole active compound. The pediatric TK2-deficiency precedent is a combination with doxribtimine (deoxythymidine). Treat that combination as an external comparator. In WWOX directly measured muscle mtDNA content is lower, with mitochondrial mass and tissue composition unresolved. The held uridine and ABAT dossiers distinguish ribonucleotide replenishment, GABA-shunt effects and DNA precursor balance. The new question is whether the deoxycytidine branch can be shown to contribute independently to mitochondrial DNA synthesis in an affected neural preparation.

**Frozen comparison.** Compare dC alone, dT alone and dC plus dT arms where the original study supports independent contrasts. Record formulation, tissue, age and administration conditions. Compare measured intact parent, deoxyuridine loss, balanced matrix dNTPs, new mtDNA per mitochondrial mass, and functional respiration. Keep dCTP enrichment and absolute pool sizes separate. Clinical combination exposure, mouse brain homogenate, CSF, intracellular parent and mitochondria remain distinct measurements.

**Prediction and countertest.** If WWOX has a dC-limited salvage lesion, increasing intact parent at matched developmental stage, mitochondrial mass, cell cycle and workload should increase incorporation of a distinguishing pyrimidine label into mitochondrial dC residues, restore matrix dCTP without worsening the other dNTP pools, and increase mtDNA synthesis and respiratory reserve ahead of any change in mitochondrial biomass. Label in deoxyuridine or thymidine-derived products, a rise in total dCTP without new mtDNA, or parallel expansion in genomes and mitochondrial mass would weaken this mechanism. The independent dT and combined conditions distinguish balance from a dC-only effect. Use cytosolic phosphorylation and deamination measurements to test the alternative of failed delivery or precursor loss.

**Fixed WWOX RNA panel.** DCK, TK2, TK1, CDA, DCTD, SLC29A1/SLC29A2 (transport context), SLC25A33/SLC25A36 (mitochondrial nucleotide transport), RRM2B and POLG. Existing expression measures can establish only direction and detectability, not endogenous substrate, catalytic activity or target engagement. Evaluate leave-one-library-out direction and distinguish pooled nuclei from independent libraries.
