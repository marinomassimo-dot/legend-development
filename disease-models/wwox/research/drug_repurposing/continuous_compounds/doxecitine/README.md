# Doxecitine | Deoxycytidine salvage and native mitochondrial DNA synthesis

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Disposition.** Retain doxecitine (2′-deoxycytidine, dC) as a conditional metabolic tracer and substrate comparator. Defer WWOX pediatric study priority. A dC-limited neuronal substrate defect and an age-relevant neural exposure are both unresolved. The pediatric TK2-deficiency precedent concerns a fixed dC plus deoxythymidine (dT) combination. Only dC is evaluated here. [Prespecified comparison](pretest_20261127.md)

**A counterexample to assigning a dCTP limit.** RRM2B-null mouse brain at 12 weeks had around 30% of WT mtDNA (CytB/Actin, 6–10 animals per reported tissue group). In the separate seven-week tissue comparison, dATP and dGTP were lower in brain while dCTP was unchanged. Heterozygotes had comparable mtDNA to WT at 12 weeks. A modest WWOX RNA difference cannot transfer the knockout phenotype. It does make combined pyrimidine and purine measurement the relevant countertest before perturbing dC alone. In a second source, purified human SLC25A33 and SLC25A36 showed distinct phosphoform exchange profiles. In reconstituted SLC25A33, external UTP exchanged with internal dCDP and dCTP, and very weakly with internal dCMP. A cytosolic dCMP increase cannot by itself establish matrix delivery. ## Exposure and pediatric feasibility

The FDA label describes a 1:1 powder for oral solution for genetically confirmed TK2 deficiency with symptom onset on or before age 12. The primary label assessment used 78 treated/78 matched external controls and a hazard ratio of 0.14 (95% CI 0.04–0.39). It combines treatment sources and compares survival. It supplies no component-specific effect, no randomized control and no WWOX estimate. In the 18-person adult/pediatric combination pharmacokinetic analysis, estimated baseline-unadjusted geometric mean total parent dC steady-state plasma Cmax was **12 ng/mL (0.0528 µM)** and AUC for 24 h **108 ng·h/mL (0.475 µM·h)**. The 1 µM nominal lower fibroblast test is about 19 times this plasma mean. This scale comparison does not estimate age-specific unbound brain exposure or a therapeutic threshold. [FDA label §§12.3 and 14](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=7d5ce670-9314-4fd7-8b0a-1447910dcf9e) · [Conversions](exposure.tsv)

Section 8.4 reports 68 treated pediatric participants ages 0.7 to under 17 across its safety/support set. In Trial 1 the pediatric counts were vomiting 9/32, ALT elevation 8/32 and AST elevation 7/32. Study 1 and expanded access recorded two pediatric hospitalizations for diarrhea. These counts characterize the combination and the TK2 setting. Food, renal function and dC catabolism affect systemic exposure. The label's in vitro interaction studies do not identify a clinically relevant CYP mechanism. Compatibility with a WWOX antiseizure background and free CNS exposure remains unverified. Future functional endpoints would need to separate mitochondrial recovery, myopathy or respiratory support from WWOX seizure and developmental outcomes. [FDA label §§5, 6, 8 and 12.3](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=7d5ce670-9314-4fd7-8b0a-1447910dcf9e)

## Inference and discriminating observation

A lower mitochondrial DNA/nuclear DNA ratio could reflect organelle number, stage or an imbalance in substrates rather than a deficit of dC. The clinical and adult mouse results also leave intact parent availability separate from phosphorylation and incorporation. The strongest experimental prediction is a selective increase in new mtDNA per unit of mitochondrial mass after dC when matrix dCTP is low and the other three pools stay adequate. Compare dC versus vehicle, dT versus vehicle and the combination in matched-stage WWOX and corrected preparations. If there is a dT effect, compare the dC increment at matched measured dT rather than matched administered amount.

Track a retained ring carbon tag and a label on the exocyclic amino nitrogen of dC. Early retention of both labels in mitochondrial dCTP and mtDNA cytosine residues supports retained cytosine input. Loss of the amino label with ring label appearing in dU, dUMP or thymidine-derived products favors diversion. These measurements do not separate extracellular CDA from post-phosphorylation DCTD without follow-up on labelled intermediates and time order. Monitor enrichment and absolute pools independently, mitochondrial isolation purity, new nuclear DNA and mtDNA, two mitochondrial biomass references and respiratory reserve. A related fibroblast study measured isotope incorporation in total DNA, a reason to isolate the mtDNA product explicitly. [Human fibroblast method and boundary, Dombi et al. Fig. 8](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cell-and-developmental-biology/articles/10.3389/fcell.2024.1260496/full)

**Reopen** only if a selective native dCTP deficit is demonstrated and matched free exposure yields greater matrix dCTP, nascent mtDNA per mitochondrial biomass and functional recovery without a worsening imbalance. An independent curve/data audit would matter if the monotherapy mouse result determined a ranking. Here the conclusion also depends on unmeasured native WWOX substrate status and exposure. There is no clinical reprioritization from this increment.
