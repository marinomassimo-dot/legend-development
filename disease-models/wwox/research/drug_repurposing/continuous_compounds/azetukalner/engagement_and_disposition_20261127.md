**Scope of translation.** These randomized results concern countable focal seizures in adults and include tolerability tradeoffs. They do not estimate spasms, developmental function, or WWOX-specific benefit. At the 2 November 2026 posted X-ACKT update, eligibility started at 12 years for primary generalized tonic-clonic seizures and no results were posted. The exact oral, fed capsule is the evidenced form. Neither younger age, enteral alternatives, long terminal persistence, concomitant inducer exposure nor effects on arousal and preserved function can be filled from those results. A sponsor SEC filing in November reports that a US focal-seizure application had been submitted in September 2026. ## WWOX-specific inference and test

**A numerical counterexample to a resting-potential explanation.** Breton's group means for P13–17 neuronal Wwox deletion give a resting input resistance of 102.88 versus 143.03 MΩ and voltage −79.25 versus −73.62 mV. The reciprocal-of-means difference is 2.73 nS. Prior work showed that cell size and seal shunt can reproduce the resting relation. The Kv7 scenario replay uses an equilibrium Boltzmann activation curve, slope factors of 8/12/16 mV and a unit concentration Hill slope. For each assay midpoint it uses a common illustrative maximal V½ shift of 44.78 mV, holding maximal conductance fixed. At 12 nM local drug (one tenth of the 6-hour caption plasma total) and a 12-mV slope, the modeled increments in activated fractions are **0.018 at −73.62 mV versus 0.099 at −50 mV** for the lower-midpoint scenario and **0.0010 versus 0.0059** for the higher-midpoint scenario. A static slope-conductance calculation at the KO rest, assuming E_K = −85 mV, would require Gmax of about 80 nS versus 1.46 µS to equal the descriptive 2.73 nS gap, respectively. This is a scale comparison and depends on the assumed V½, channel density, local exposure, equilibrium and junction-potential scale. The native resistance test, axonal voltage trajectory, and rapid spike train are not simulations of this one-compartment equilibrium. The more direct comparison is late recruitment at measured cell-type Kv7 current and first response. [Held parent electrophysiology and alternative](../../potassium_conductances_sci43_20261110/verify/audit.md) · [Scenario table](kv7_voltage_scenarios.tsv)

**A separate reserve hypothesis.** KCNQ2/KCNQ3 bulk RNA fall in GSE158682 cortex, GSE158681 hippocampus and GSE156243 organoids, with the sign retained on one-library omissions. CDIPT is lower in all three; PIP5K1C falls in cortex and organoids and has uncertain sign in hippocampus. PLCB1 does not have a consistent sign. The RNA comparisons do not identify local PIP₂, Kv7 stoichiometry, open probability or the channel density of a defined initial segment. A held within-Cre-line comparison also warns that part of the Kcnq2 signal is as large as background shifts. A separate juvenile Fmr1 rat study reports transient age-dependent loss of the functional effect of Kv7 activation on firing or transmission, consistent with altered axon initial segment development (abstract boundary). This motivates pairing direct current with native use of that current rather than reading activity from RNA, binding or peak current alone. [Bulk results](wwox_channel_and_pip2.tsv) · [Held PIP₂ framework](../myo_inositol/README.md) · [Held Cre controls](../../functional_it13_p3a_20261031/README.md) · [Primary Fmr1 abstract](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-026-10958-7)

**Discriminating prediction.** At matched free drug and first recruited response in juvenile Wwox-null and control cortex, a functional Kv7 brake should increase a verified M-current during or following a repeated challenge and lower late pathological recurrence while preserving evoked inhibitory recruitment. A non-Kv7 comparator with the same early spike effect should fail to reproduce a Kv7-specific change. A dynamic current matched to the measured increment can test whether that increment is sufficient. Use a pharmacological current attribution under a voltage history that actually recruits Kv7, since blocker effectiveness depends on channel state. If azetukalner increases isolated current but fails to shift late recurrence, assess localization or circuit coupling. If prechallenge current responds and the drug effect collapses selectively after challenge despite matched exposure and earlier current, test PIP₂ availability/recovery as a competing explanation before attributing the loss to reduced channel abundance. Separate neurite location, capacitance, leak and HCN sag. A decrease of late and early physiological recruitment together weakens the WWOX repurposing case.

**Disposition.** Retain azetukalner as a measured-exposure preclinical Kv7 comparator. Hold priority for a pediatric WWOX study pending an age-relevant unbound exposure estimate and selective late rescue with preserved inhibitory function. A new review is most informative once a native concentration response or pediatric exposure becomes available. Replaying the controlled adult findings and varying the potency assumption already identifies the same next experiment.
