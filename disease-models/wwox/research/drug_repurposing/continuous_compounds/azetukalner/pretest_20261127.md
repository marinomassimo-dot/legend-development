# Azetukalner, Kv7 reserve and repeated recruitment in WWOX loss of function

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. Sole active compound after the [benfotiamine checkpoint](../benfotiamine/cofactor_and_disposition_20261127.md). Azetukalner (XEN1101) is an investigational Kv7 opener. LEGEND had already listed the compound as a candidate and documented a missing catalogue target edge. This batch tests translational and functional questions using the named molecule.

**Selection.** The held juvenile Wwox deletion preparation has a depolarized resting potential and larger input resistance. The arithmetic alone does not distinguish altered resting conductance from cell size or maturation. The earlier Kv7 screen identified an unmeasured M-current in the relevant neurons, especially at voltages traversed during recruitment. An adult phase 3 focal-seizure RCT publication and the phase 2b comparison give a reason to quantify evidence for this exact drug, with no assumed transfer to infantile or WWOX electroclinical outcomes. **Prediction before extraction.** A recruitable Kv7 brake predicts a larger verified M-current increment during repeated firing and reduced late recurrence in WWOX at comparable first recruitment and evoked inhibition. Compare with dynamic restoration of the measured current and a non-Kv7 comparator matched for early spike suppression. Loss of drug benefit when Kv7 current is prevented, plus preservation of physiological output, would favor this branch. The benefit can occur regardless of whether a Kv7 deficit caused the baseline phenotype. If a drug shifts the voltage trajectory with no separable Kv7 contribution, suppresses physiological recruitment to the same extent, or requires unattainable free concentrations, the hypothesis loses priority. Measure resting leak, sag and cell capacitance separately.

**First comparison.** Reconcile channel activation at the reported voltage and subunit composition with the exact human exposure species and whether free CNS estimates exist. Then compare the randomized adult primary endpoints, background therapies, retention, relevant adverse endpoints and original observation windows. Verify current pediatric trial age bands and the formulational and exposure gaps. Use held WWOX electrophysiology and test what level of extra conductance would be required under explicit assumptions. Keep efficacy in other electroclinical syndromes and developmental effects separate.
