## Inference and counterproof

A one-pool sensitivity calculation assuming immediate complete arrest of synthesis and unchanged first-order clearance gives approximately 5.5%, 20.2% and 28.7% depletion after 1, 4 and 6 hours at the reconstructed rat half-life. At 6 hours the illustrative half-lives of 6 to 36 hours give about 50% to 11% depletion. This is an algebraic sensitivity case, with no fit to soticlestat, no confidence interval and no slice exposure forecast. Both a faster-replenishing local pool and slower clearance in an isolated slice are possible. [Generated turnover scenarios](turnover_sensitivity.tsv)

The native hippocampal genetic control found a smaller NMDA-sensitive train-evoked response after nearly complete Cyp46a1 loss. The WWOX cortical phenotype specifies the event to test. The regional RNA replay reverses sign among Wwox genotypes and ages, and the bounded metabolomics search did not recover a native mammalian WWOX concentration. Nishi's acutely provoked PTZ subset yielded 2/9 protected with about 90% lower bulk 24S-HC versus 1/9 vehicle. Distinguish first recruitment, recurrent recruitment, inhibition and whole-animal response. ## Hypothesis and experiment

**Hypothesis.** If a locally available excess of 24S-HC supports late NMDA-dependent recurrence in juvenile WWOX cortex, suppressing local production should shift the late response only after the available pool falls. Rapid changes at preserved 24S-HC leave parallel mechanisms such as altered excitatory drive or inhibition in play.

**One comparison.** In age-matched juvenile Wwox-deficient and control cortex, pair a vehicle condition with acute soticlestat and an exposure condition already shown by serial chemistry to lower 24S-HC, plus local 24S-HC add-back at fixed drug exposure. Use matched handling and developmental age. If a short-window preparation cannot sustain a detectable drop, verify a time-matched preparation with a lower pool before comparing physiology. Quantify product in tissue and in the extracellular or exchangeable pool with isomer-resolved LC-MS and a validated recovery control. Measure drug exposure and viability in parallel. Estimate the within-animal D-APV-sensitive late response relative to its floor, early recruitment at matched input and inhibitory output by cell class. Hippocampus can serve as an assay control where native oxysterol physiology is established.

**Decisive observations.** A fall in local 24S-HC followed by preferential attenuation of the delayed Wwox-deficient response and restoration by add-back, with early and inhibitory responses preserved, supports the proposed link. A verified fall with preserved early and inhibitory responses and no accompanying late change lowers its priority. An effect that survives calibrated add-back favors a parallel mechanism. A null with no verified depletion is indeterminate. A low baseline pool weakens the excess premise and raises priority for measuring baseline circuit direction before inhibition. ## Pediatric boundary and stop condition

SKYLINE enrolled ages 2–21 in Dravet syndrome and SKYWAY 2–55 in Lennox–Gastaut syndrome. The public SKYLINE protocol used investigational oral mini-tablets and tablets. Both phase 3 trials missed their primary seizure endpoint despite plasma 24S-HC falling about 80% on drug. Adverse events leading to stopping drug were 11/73 versus 4/71 in SKYLINE and 19/134 versus 5/136 in SKYWAY, consistently drug versus placebo. Somnolence can affect function measured separately from seizures. A small healthy-adult interaction study reported parent AUC 16.4% of the reference with rifampin. It does not quantify an interaction for any individual background antiseizure medicine in WOREE. Formulation access, local unbound exposure, infant data and pharmacological response in WWOX remain unresolved. [SKYLINE, Table 2 and Supplement](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41784472/) · [SKYWAY, Table 2 and Supplement](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41891667/) · [SKYLINE protocol, §6.1](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/24/NCT04940624/Prot_000.pdf) · [Adult interaction study, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38123352/)

Reopen first for a measurable native WWOX pool and an engagement-matched, add-back-sensitive functional contrast, or for direct human WWOX evidence that changes feasibility. The exact supplement and original figure agree on the load-bearing comparison. Independent review has higher decision value when native measurements or a contradictory estimate become available.
