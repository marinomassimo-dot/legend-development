- **Native positive control, Sun 2016.** P18–32 mouse hippocampus, systemic Cyp46a1 deletion. Results and Fig. 1A report 326.0 ± 16.2 and 2.0 ± 0.5 ng 24S-HC/mg protein in WT and knockout slices, respectively (n=3 animals each). The deficient pool retained 0.61% of control. Fig. 1C used 15 WT and 9 KO cells for the NMDA/AMPA ratio. Fig. 3D–E used 18/17 cells, similar undrugged train-evoked capacity and a smaller D-APV-sensitive spiking component in KO. This supports separate total and NMDA-sensitive recruitment. The full genetic depletion does not calibrate an mRNA-to-current curve. Similar response to the synthetic analog in both genotypes does not make the analog a calibrated metabolite add-back. [Sun primary](https://doi.org/10.1152/jn.00890.2015)
- **Counterproof on acute interpretation, Popiolek 2020.** Results and Figs. 4–6. Two CYP46A1 inhibitors did not measurably change bulk 24S-HC after 0.5–6 hours in mouse hippocampal or striatal slices, sampled from three animals. Rat slice LTD shifted with both inhibitors. A single add-back restored LTD with one inhibitor but did not give a significant result with the second. Isolated evoked NMDA EPSPs were unchanged as reported under the LTD-producing conditions. In vivo compound 1 lowered brain 24S-HC between 4 and 16 hours. Isolated EPSPs, nominal bath exposure, tissue total 24S-HC, and post-conditioning plasticity measure different parts of the causal chain. [Popiolek primary](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnmol.2020.568641)
- **Decision for the next increment.** Take juvenile neocortex as the first WWOX functional comparator because late evoked recruitment is already characterized there, and hippocampus as the positive-control and region comparator. A negative acute result with no verified decrease in available 24S-HC is indeterminate. An inhibitor that reduces late recruitment while both the oxysterol and 24S-HC add-back response can be verified would support the registered hypothesis. The same response with falling native inhibitory recruitment would change the interpretation. The next check should compare pharmacological time courses and principal-cell versus inhibitory recruitment using the published target-engagement and cell-class evidence, with plasma, bulk tissue and metabolite available to the local circuit reported separately.
