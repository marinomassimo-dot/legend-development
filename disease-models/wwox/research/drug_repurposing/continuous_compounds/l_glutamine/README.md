# L-glutamine and competing demands for neurotransmitter replenishment

**Why this compound.** A regulated oral L-glutamine powder has pediatric experience in sickle cell disease from age five. The laboratory already recorded a ketone-plus-glutamine comparison in AGC1-deficient progenitors. That combination and its mitochondrial endpoint answer a different question from the one here. Reduced inhibitory currents in the Wwox mouse cortex, alongside an abnormal delayed excitatory response, make selective replenishment of inhibitory transmitter attractive but uncertain. Glutamine supplies both neuronal glutamate and GABA. [Existing AGC1 comparison](../../advisor_pilot_20261113/brainstorm_20261116/deepening_he_20261116/third_set/PART_B_SHORTLIST.md) · [WWOX stimulus-response evidence](../potassium_bromide/evoked.md) · [FDA prescribing information](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/208587s004lbl.pdf)

**Prediction before WWOX analysis.** At the same measured extracellular glutamine concentration and matched recruitment, a limiting inhibitory precursor pool predicts greater recovery of late evoked inhibitory charge in WWOX-deficient than corrected cells. If excitatory transmission recovers at least as strongly and delayed cortical responses grow, increased GABA synthesis alone will have little value as evidence for functional rescue. Measure absolute early and late excitatory and inhibitory responses, release during recovery and preservation of physiological evoked output. Analyze precursors and products separately from their labeled fractions. A response confined to artificial precursor deprivation would lower trial priority.

**Direct comparison from hippocampal physiology.** In CA1, Liang et al. found that 5 mM exogenous glutamine reversed the activity-dependent inhibitory-current loss caused by blocking glutamine synthesis. It failed to reverse the loss produced by blocking neuronal glutamine uptake. Miniature inhibitory currents at low activity did not reveal the same dependence. In a separate preparation, Tani et al. found that 0.5 mM glutamine supported evoked *excitatory* responses at isolated Schaffer collateral terminals during sustained 20 Hz stimulation. The glutamine and control curves were similar for the first roughly 1,000 pulses, then separated. A comparison of 5 mM in one assay and 0.5 mM in another does not estimate relative pathway potency. It does show why a resting readout or inhibitory recording alone would miss the competing effect. [Liang et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16914680/) · [Tani et al., Fig. 1 and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4001919/)

**Starting medium changes the biological question.** Steinberg et al. specify 1% GlutaMAX in developing cerebral and forebrain organoid media, including forebrain maturation medium. For the listed 200 mM L-alanyl-L-glutamine stock, this supplies nominally 2 mM dipeptide. Release of free glutamine and its use by cells can change between feeds. Verify absolute free glutamine, dipeptide, alanine, ammonia and glutamate when the functional readout is obtained. Compare addition to the existing medium with restoration after depletion as separate hypotheses. [Original WWOX organoid methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8350905/) · [Manufacturer formulation](https://documents.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/GlutaMAX_PI.pdf)

**Pediatric exposure boundary.** In one child with genetically determined glutamine synthetase deficiency, CSF glutamine rose from 121 to 146, 171 and 201 µM at weeks 2, 3 and 4, below the authors' reference interval (333–658 µM). The study included stepwise enteral supplementation and separate short parenteral trials with enteral supplementation paused. This is a mixed-route observation in severe substrate deficiency. The label reports a plasma peak around 1,028 µM after a single oral administration; that is total plasma at peak, not incremental free extracellular brain glutamine in WWOX. The available data do not establish disease-specific tolerability or interaction with background antiseizure medication. [Clinical study, Table 1 and supplementation methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3495849/) · [Product pharmacokinetics, §12.3](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=d5a783f4-12ef-4326-8faa-40018e45ba3b)

**Next comparison.** Check what WWOX datasets can resolve about astrocytic synthesis, neuronal uptake, glutaminase and transmitter production at matched developmental stages. Then test whether published paired excitatory/inhibitory measurements and plausible incremental CNS exposure support an advantage for inhibitory recovery.
