# A structural rescue in SLC44A1 deficiency and a stage-dependent contrast in WWOX

**Rodent treatment confirmed.** Chen et al., Fig. 7A, shows intraperitoneal treatment during the early postnatal P5–P21 interval in control and conditional Slc44a1 knockout mice. Panel B measures myelin staining (four mice); panel C counts CC1-positive cells in the corpus callosum (three mice); panel D compares the percentage of myelinated axons by electron microscopy in optic nerve and corpus callosum (three mice). The plotted means increase under citicoline in the mutants and remain below control means. Unlike the zebrafish per-area counts in Fig. 6H, Fig. 7D directly addresses the proportion myelinated. A cross-sectional percentage cannot separate initiation of new sheaths from retention of existing sheaths or differential axonal survival. Neither panel tests conduction. The mouse received a parenteral exposure and harbored a glial choline-pathway defect. [Mouse Fig. 7](https://www.zfin.org/ZDB-FIG-260129-71) · [Fish Fig. 6](https://www.zfin.org/ZDB-FIG-260129-70)

**Juvenile WWOX cell classes.** The hippocampal bulk Slc44a1 contrast (−1.01 in mean log2(1+TPM)) did not persist uniformly in nuclei. In GSE158684, total assigned nuclear signal was 219.8→219.4 counts per million. The within-class contribution (+10.4 CPM) approximately offset the class-weight contribution (−10.9 CPM). Oligodendrocyte precursors rose 162→233 CPM, committed precursors fell 718→576, newly formed cells fell 747→698, myelin-forming cells rose 440→461, and mature cells fell 414→251. For the latter, raw counts were 164 and 79 across 119 and 94 nuclei respectively. These are marker-enriched classes in one pool per condition; they do not supply animal-level confidence limits. The bulk and nuclear workflows also differ. Adult P47T hippocampus showed female and male Slc44a1 contrasts of −0.47 and +0.15. None of these data establish that a given WWOX cell is choline deficient. **Alternative route.** FLVCR1 fell from 41.8 to 28.6 CPM across assigned nuclei. CHPT1 and CEPT1 also fell. A distinct primary study in HEK293T cells found loss of choline uptake under FLVCR1 deletion; in a 30-minute comparison at 21.5 µM radiolabelled choline, FLVCR1 and SLC5A7 restored the uptake defect while expression of SLC44A1 failed to do so. FLVCR2 partially restored uptake and restored the phosphocholine pool in separate complementation experiments. The host cell and substrate concentration matter. Lower glial Slc44a1 RNA can motivate measuring uptake, but cannot localize the bottleneck by itself. Compare intracellular labelled choline, phosphocholine, CDP-choline and phosphatidylcholine over time alongside an absolute phosphatidylcholine measurement per viable oligodendrocyte. Little labelled free choline points to an uptake limitation; ample precursor with slow lipid formation directs attention to trapping or downstream synthesis. [Primary transport study, Figs. 3–4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10367582/)

**Prediction for WWOX.** A preferential substrate response in mature cells could coexist with relative sparing of early precursors. Compare maturation-matched WWOX and corrected cells, then check whether a lipid response co-occurs with myelin initiation per longitudinally followed viable axon and improved evoked propagation. Normalize to cell number and record axon caliber and firing. A change in CC1-positive cell count or in whole-tissue phosphatidylcholine can occur without more sheaths per viable axon. Equalizing extracellular substrate alone cannot distinguish this from differences in uptake and intracellular availability.

**Dated line, 2026-11-28 (ADV-298; added by the Orchestrator, the dossier owner reconciles).** Every observation in this note that supports a myelin effect (the CC1 counts, the percentage of myelinated axons and the myelin staining are structural; the juvenile WWOX cell classes are marker-assigned nuclear transcript classes) is structural or transcriptomic, none is a conduction readout, and the sentence above about improved evoked propagation is a prediction, not a held observation: in the held full texts, searched 2026-11-28, no Wwox-deficient preparation has a conduction or latency readout together with a rescue or drug arm. The held Cheng 2020 baseline conduction readout is not a clean central-myelin readout (the Repudi 2021 callosal-slice latency is central but also baseline only): the transcranial motor-evoked potential is recorded from forelimb muscles and includes peripheral nerve, the same paper reports sciatic demyelination in the null mice, and the heterozygous Wwox+/- mice have a longer latency (2.13 against 1.39 ms, P < 0.05) with normal amplitude, with their sciatic-nerve myelin reported as similar (data not shown). A latency change alone would therefore not show a myelin effect of a lever.
