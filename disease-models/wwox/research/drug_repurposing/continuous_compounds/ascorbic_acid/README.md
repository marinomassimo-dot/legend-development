# L-ascorbic acid and first ensheathment

Does measured reduced ascorbate above an already replete baseline reach differentiating WWOX oligodendroglia and restore functional sheaths? Vitamin C is an attractive supplement only if substrate availability limits a reversible process. Lower transporter abundance could also constrain the response to added substrate.

In juvenile hippocampal nuclei assigned to five oligodendroglial stages, Slc23a2 (SVCT2) counts per million were lower in knockout at every stage; the magnitudes varied. In committed precursors (COP) the difference was 137.9 to 66.8 CPM (29 and 13 molecules); in newly formed oligodendrocytes (NFOL), 109.7 to 71.8 (47 and 25). In myelin-forming oligodendrocytes the difference was only 57.2 to 53.7. Within NFOL all three Tet transcripts were lower, whereas in COP Tet1 and Tet3 were higher. A single pooled specimen per genotype, sparse transcripts and heterogeneous assigned stages preclude biological replication or a claim about enzymatic activity. The number of nuclei is not the animal sample size. [Counts and stage denominators](within_stage.tsv). Guo et al., Figure 7a–g: parent L-ascorbic acid and its more stable phosphate derivative were compared at the same nominal 150 μM. The authors report greater MBP-positive differentiation with the derivative. The transporter inhibitor and intracellular ascorbate measurements in panels e–g used the derivative. Unequal persistence of reduced ascorbate in medium can explain a formulation difference. Pharmacological inhibition alone does not establish a unique transporter mechanism in WWOX. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6001564/)

Discriminating prediction: at measured extracellular reduced ascorbate over time, compare intracellular uptake, 5-hydroxymethylcytosine at differentiation-associated loci, and the fraction of previously bare viable axons acquiring a sheath, with matched lineage stage and firing. A response confined to initially deficient medium would support correction of culture depletion. Equal intracellular ascorbate with persistent failure of downstream maturation weakens a substrate-limited mechanism. First sheaths and conduction would need to improve over the replete WWOX baseline without nonspecific survival differences to advance. Next examine the attainable change in central ascorbate above repletion and the contribution of non-oligodendroglial extracellular matrix.

Cross-model countercheck: SLC23A2 KO-minus-control bulk differences (mean log2[1+expression]) were −0.679 in mouse cortex, −0.327 in mouse hippocampus and +0.727 in human organoids. Omitting any single library preserved the opposing cortex and organoid signs; the hippocampal range included zero. This defeats a conserved whole-tissue transporter decrease. The organoid increase could reflect composition, stage or regulation. Prioritize a human cell-stage measurement of transporter protein and ascorbate uptake before testing a developmental intervention. [Bulk inputs and omission bounds](bulk.tsv).
