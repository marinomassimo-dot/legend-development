# L-ascorbic acid · axon-genotype countercheck

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Result.** In Repudi Fig. 6D (printed p. 3072), the OPCs were WT in both arms. Substituting Wwox-null DRG neurons for WT shifted the approximate box medians from **30.3% to 48.1%** for premyelinating morphology and from **45.1% to 28.1%** for myelinating morphology. Degraded morphology shifted from **19.1% to 21.0%** with a non-significant contrast in the figure. Four cultures per arm across two experiments. These are figure-derived medians of percentages, without class counts per culture or paired total denominators. Near reciprocal shifts support an axon-associated maturation phenotype. They cannot establish that individual cells changed state or exclude loss of eligible axons. [Primary study](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awab174)

**Denominator sensitivity.** Treat the myelinating box medians as illustrative proportions. Their ratio is 0.624. Equal absolute numbers of myelinating cells at those proportions would require the KO culture to have ~1.60 times as many total classified cells as WT (1 ÷ 0.624). That is a threshold, not a measured cell-count ratio. The separate category medians are not additive. Even a count of myelinating cells would leave first-sheath probability unresolved without a count of surviving, initially bare axons in comparable caliber bins. None of the three supplemental workbooks contains the Figure 6 culture denominators.

**Comparison with the vitamin experiment.** Guo et al., Methods §2.5 and Results §3.3/Fig. 4, used **postnatal P5–P10 DRGs**, collagen-coated plates, and measured MBP/NF-200-positive length and Caspr segments with the phosphate derivative. Repudi used **embryonic E13.5 DRGs**, Matrigel coating and morphology proportions. Both used cortical mouse OPCs collected at P0–P2. The data cannot be combined as a rescue estimate. They motivate a matched axon-genotype check in a single culture protocol, with an identical matrix across conditions. [Guo et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6001564/)

**Discriminating observation and checkpoint.** With corrected/WT OPCs and paired control and WWOX-deficient axons, compare a measured replete reduced-ascorbate baseline with verified sustained exposure above it. Record the number of previously bare, viable axons gaining their first sheath per caliber bin, evoked conduction in the same preparation, and intracellular reduced ascorbate in OPCs. If markers or apparent myelinated length improve while the first-sheath fraction and conduction remain low, reject the proposed functional rescue under those conditions. If both improve preferentially on deficient axons, follow up on which compartment responds. Keep L-ascorbic acid as the only active compound until its disposition is recorded. No trial-priority upgrade from these observations.
