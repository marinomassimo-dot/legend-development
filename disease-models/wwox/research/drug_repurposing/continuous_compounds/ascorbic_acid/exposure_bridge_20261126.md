# L-ascorbic acid · age, compartment and stability

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Checkpoint.** One active compound, L-ascorbic acid. The translation question is whether a change above a measured, already replete baseline can restore first ensheathment. Total CSF ascorbate and nominal culture concentration are separate exposure observations. Neither supplies a measured WWOX oligodendroglial concentration.

## Age and exposure comparison

- In 67 children sampled for clinical indications, Heinz-Erian et al. report 30.1 mg/L in the 14 with normal CSF laboratory results, about **171 µM**. The reported premature and school-age means are approximately **671 and 161 µM** after conversion. The age composition and subgroup sizes are unverified. These are not population reference intervals. [Primary abstract, PMID 3975061](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3975061/)
- Arad et al. report **216 µM at term** and **295 µM preterm** on day one and approximately **165 µM** in later neonatal determinations. Their original unit is mg/dL, which is ten times mg/L for the same numeral. The abstract identifies 13 term and 17 preterm neonates across the whole comparison, with six additional neurological patients. It does not state denominators for each sampling time, so the large premature-subgroup contrast in the other study remains unresolved. The correlation of CSF/plasma with plasma also has plasma in its denominator; this association alone is insufficient to establish saturation. No open full text verified for either pediatric report in this increment. [Primary abstract, PMID 1794949](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1794949/)
- The **150 µM** nominal L-ascorbic acid arm in Guo et al., Fig. 7a–b, starts at the same order of magnitude as concentrations in the later neonatal and school-age CSF summaries. The authors do not establish reduced ascorbate over time in the actual mouse OPC culture medium. Fig. 7g measured intracellular ascorbate after exposure to the stable phosphate derivative, with or without phloretin. The study's axon assay uses mouse OPCs co-cultured with DRG neurons and the **derivative** (Fig. 4c–h), with MBP/NF-200 and Caspr readouts. A developmental conduction rescue by the parent compound is still open. [Guo et al., OPC differentiation and intracellular measurements, Figs. 4 and 7](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6001564/)

## A measured reason why nominal doses are insufficient

Rantataro et al., Fig. 1D and associated results, found reduced-ascorbate loss of **87.0% at 6 h**, **93.7% at 8 h** and **98.6% at 24 h** after a nominal 200 µM bolus in their N2B27 medium. Scaling the nominal starting concentration by the measured retention gives **26, 12.6 and 2.8 µM**, respectively. These are derived concentrations for that assay, not measurements in the Guo culture. The studies used different mixtures, and the sensor read oxidation current, with an electrode-fouling control. The pseudo-first-order fit deteriorated after 8 h; the calculation uses the observed percentages without extrapolation. [Rantataro et al., Fig. 1 and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9684410/)

**Discriminating prediction.** In the same human WWOX-deficient and corrected oligodendroglial preparations, first measure extracellular reduced ascorbate and its oxidized form over time. If the derivative outperforms the parent only because it maintains reduced exposure, matching the extracellular concentration–time profiles above a replete baseline should narrow the difference in intracellular ascorbate and new ensheathment. If a difference persists at matched intracellular exposure, examine derivative-specific handling or a matrix contribution before attributing the result to limited SVCT2 transport. Separately record the fraction of viable axons gaining a first sheath and evoked conduction. The exposure bridge remains open for children with WWOX. Next decision point is whether human cell-stage data can establish a deficit in uptake or transporter protein that the opposing mouse/human bulk results leave unresolved.
