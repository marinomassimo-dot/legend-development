# Sodium dichloroacetate · active checkpoint

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

## Why this increment exists

The WWOX PDH/PDK dossier already gives dichloroacetate a reference-comparator role, records differences in PDK isoform RNA by cell type, and identifies the unmeasured premise of low neural PDC activity. The held human organoid analysis found a change of −0.138 log₂ units in the LDH/PDC transcript ratio. Approximately 74% of sampled random gene-set contrasts were at least as large in absolute magnitude (4 KO and 2 WT libraries). These results supply a comparison framework and no estimate of flux. The additional question is whether a new pediatric PDC-deficiency trial supports treating lower lactate as a bridge from target engagement to function.

## Controlled outcome and exploratory observations

The phase III study randomised 34 children with primary PDC deficiency to two double-blind crossover periods of four months each with a one-month washout. The primary motor score ranged from 0 to 20, with lower scores indicating less impairment. The paper reports a DCA-minus-placebo least-squares mean difference of **+0.29 points** (95% CI **−0.60 to +1.19**, P = **0.512**), with the point estimate favouring placebo. Plasma lactate fell by **0.48 mmol/L** relative to placebo (P = **0.006**, secondary endpoint). Each result answers a different question. The opposite signs on these separately scaled outcomes supply no patient-level association or surrogate validation. The published descriptive count above 2 mmol/L fell from 18/34 placebo to 11/34 DCA, a difference of −20.6 percentage points. If the counts refer to the same 34 paired observations, the number above threshold in both periods is bounded from 0 to 11. Across those pairings, the two-sided exact McNemar P ranges from 0.016 to 0.265. The pairing remains unknown. The sensitivity concerns the threshold counts only. [Results, Efficacy and Fig. 2 caption](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13596729/) · [Calculation](compare.py) · [Output](comparison.tsv)

The double-blind baseline-severity analysis was exploratory (n = 15, P = 0.066). The extension compared the most recent score against baseline without a concurrent blinded comparator, with different reporting schedules. The FDA letter reports nearly identical primary results (+0.29, CI −0.62 to +1.21, P = 0.52), found no support for circulating lactate as a surrogate in PDCD, and raised comparability problems for the external survival analysis. The letter refers to the submitted application. It does not resolve intrinsic responsiveness of WWOX neural tissue. The trial used GSTZ1-stratified exposure and recorded one possibly related mild neuropathy in the open-label extension. No participant underwent nerve conduction or EMG testing. Safety transfer to WWOX remains open. ## Prespecified WWOX discriminator

Compare WWOX-deficient and isogenic-corrected neural preparations at the same developmental stage. Measure early appearance of M+2 acetyl-CoA from labelled pyruvate, precursor enrichment, PDC regulatory phosphorylation and ATP recovery after matched activity. Keep total pyruvate constant across tracer arms because pyruvate itself regulates PDK. Include absolute labelled product or production rate as well as fractional enrichment to detect dilution by other acetyl-CoA sources. Measure exported lactate and activity independently, and add an independent PDK comparator selected for the cell class. If DCA lowers lactate without improving labelled oxidative flux and ATP recovery, leave its role as a biochemical reference. If phosphorylation falls and labelled oxidation rises without functional benefit, retain target engagement and reject rescue at that condition. A selective improvement in flux and function at attainable exposure would justify a second comparison if it also survives control for firing suppression and changes in redox state. The controlled PDCD trial cannot establish WWOX efficacy.

## A second cause of low PDC activity to distinguish

Garcia-Cortizo et al. measured lower PDC activity in GLO1-deficient HEK293T cells with no increase in inhibitory PDHA phosphorylation. In Fig. 2b, DCA served as a positive control for lowering PDHA phosphorylation. Oxidative inhibition extended to purified PDC exposed to peroxide in Supplementary Fig. 5d. Direct exposure to methylglyoxal in the purified assay did not reduce activity (Supplementary Fig. 5e). The proposed route runs through oxidation, with DLAT as the measured redox-sensitive subunit. The oxidative-stress comparison in Fig. 4 suggests this brake can also help maintain NADPH and reduced glutathione in the source system. The mouse in vivo comparison in Fig. 5 used liver with combined glucose and lipid stress, and the human Fig. 6 comparison used liver. None of these preparations establishes the mechanism in WWOX neurons. [Primary results, Figs. 1–2, 4–6 and captions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13669365/)

**WWOX inference.** The earlier WWOX comparison found lower bulk hippocampal RNA for GLO1 (−0.47) and HAGH (−0.41). Pooled hippocampal nuclear Glo1 rose from 14.6 to 16.5 CPM. The measurements differ in compartment and denominator and do not establish carbonyl load. In the matched WWOX test, add DLAT oxidation and NADPH/GSH redox state to PDC phosphorylation, isotope-derived flux and recovery. A fall in PDHA phosphorylation with persistent DLAT oxidation and low flux would weaken PDK as the dominant bottleneck. A rise in oxidation or fall in antioxidant reserve with better flux would identify a trade-off requiring a functional readout. Prioritise this branch for follow-up if baseline oxidation differs or dephosphorylation fails to recover activity. [Held WWOX carbonyl comparison](../l_carnosine/README.md)

## First exposure bound

The indexed primary Table 1 from Shroads et al. reports mean peak total plasma exposure of 23 and 35 µg/mL after the first dose and after six months, respectively, in five children with separate congenital mitochondrial diagnoses. AUC rose from 83 to 340 µg·h/mL (4.10 times; ratio of means). The six-month mean is about 0.271 mM on an unlabelled acid-equivalent basis, or 0.232 mM if expressed as sodium salt-equivalent mass. The two conventions are a sensitivity to reporting basis. The study used labelled DCA, and assay mass conventions should be checked before precise conversion. The six-month contextual values reach **48–57%** of the held human PDK2 core-bound apparent IC₅₀ of **0.48 mM**. The corresponding core-free IC₅₀ was 0.29 mM. These quantities use total plasma peak and purified enzyme, so their ratio establishes neither occupancy nor expected percentage inhibition. PDK isoform, scaffold, plasma binding, and free intramitochondrial exposure can each shift interpretation. [Shroads et al., Table 1 and Fig. 2 caption](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2613565/) · [Held kinase source, Table 1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19833728/) · [PubChem acid mass](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/6597) · [PubChem salt mass](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/517326)

The accessible abstract of the 1997 severe adult head trauma report describes serial serum and ventricular CSF measurements after intravenous DCA. It does not give usable paired concentration summaries for the intended comparison. A chronic MELAS trial in participants aged 10–60 also reports drug withdrawal in 15/15 on DCA and 4/15 on placebo during the initial period, with peripheral neuropathy accounting for 17 of 19 discontinuations across arms. These are separate populations and designs from the pediatric phase III study. The adult trauma report keeps central entry plausible, and the MELAS signal raises the burden on safety monitoring before considering chronic exposure in another disorder. [Adult trauma abstract](https://doi.org/10.1097/00006123-199709000-00180) · [MELAS randomised trial abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16476929/)

**Follow-up and next check.** The adult glioblastoma comparison measured target phosphorylation in tissue after oral exposure. It did not establish intratissue DCA concentration. A WWOX bulk and nuclear transcript reanalysis prioritises measuring intracellular DCA before using SLC16A7 or PDK2 RNA to explain a differential response. Check for quantitative pediatric central DCA exposure and the reporting basis in the original plasma assay. If the direct concentration remains unresolved, anchor the comparison to measured intracellular DCA, PDC phosphorylation and flux before deciding whether the research role can extend beyond a reference comparator.
