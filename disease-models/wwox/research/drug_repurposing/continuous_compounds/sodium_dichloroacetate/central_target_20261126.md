# Sodium dichloroacetate · human tissue target engagement

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

Stacpoole et al. offer an oral human comparator with a tissue pharmacodynamic endpoint. In their open-label adult recurrent glioblastoma study, presurgical DCA or no DCA was assigned sequentially for one week. Thirty-seven enrolled and 34 contributed to the study analysis. The reported contrast-enhancing subset includes **13 treated and 13 untreated**. In Figure 2A–B, contrast-enhancing tumour had lower phosphorylated PDHA1 (**P = 0.002**) and PDK4 (**P = 0.011**) with presurgical DCA. Figure 2C–D reports no group difference in phospho-PDHA1 in non-enhancing tumour, alongside lower PDK4 (**P = 0.008**). Protein expression used separate tissue lysate immunoblots with total protein and across-gel normalisation. The report describes DCA measurement in plasma. No free parent concentration in sampled tumour, adjacent brain or CSF is quantified in the methods and results passages reviewed. [Study, Methods and Results, Fig. 2A–D](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13557212/)

The within-tissue comparisons do not test an interaction between contrast enhancement and presurgical treatment. Differences in perfusion, vascular access, metabolic state and tissue composition can all contribute. Sequential allocation and the smaller tissue subset limit causal attribution. Phosphorylation is closer to the target than circulating lactate. It supplies neither direct PDC flux nor efficacy evidence in normal developing brain. This dataset supports context-dependent pharmacodynamics in adult tumour and cannot calibrate pediatric WWOX target exposure.

**Decision delta.** Preserve oral target engagement as possible within a human cerebral lesion. Require measurement of free parent and phosphorylation in the same WWOX preparation before interpreting a negative flux result. If p-PDHA1 falls but flux stays low, test the separate DLAT oxidation explanation. If phosphorylation remains unchanged with adequate free exposure, compare basal phosphorylation, PDK isoform and tissue competence before assigning the discrepancy to delivery. This update raises confidence in use as a pharmacological reference without advancing trial priority.

## WWOX transport and target expression countercheck

In untreated juvenile mouse cortex, mouse hippocampus and human organoids, respectively, the KO-minus-control log₂(1+expression) contrasts for **SLC16A7** were **−0.96, −0.93 and −0.40**. All remained negative when omitting one library. **SLC5A8**, the transporter with direct DCA uptake evidence, was weakly detected in these pooled profiles. SLC16A7 is a monocarboxylate transporter, and this RNA result does not establish its contribution to DCA transport. It is a lead for testing the uptake and pyruvate-use steps separately. PDK2 increased in both mouse tissues (**+0.31, +0.39**) and fell in human organoids (**−0.13**), with direction preserved in each model's omission range. DLAT RNA declined in all three (**−0.27, −0.86 and −0.08**). This makes DLAT abundance a competing explanation for low PDC activity and a necessary denominator for oxidation. All changes are descriptive within their own platform. Transcript measurements do not identify membrane localisation, enzyme activity or local drug exposure. [Reproducible screen](uptake_screen.py) · [Results](uptake_screen.tsv) · [Direct SLC5A8 evidence, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21499304/)

In the separate GSE158684 pooled hippocampal nuclei, Slc16a7 fell in all nine assigned classes. It went from **161 → 90 CPM** in excitatory nuclei and **101 → 66 CPM** in inhibitory nuclei. At the whole-pool level, the difference was **−62.7 CPM**, with **−60.6** attributable to within-class rates and **−2.1** to changing class weights under the symmetric decomposition. Pdk2 fell from **14.1 to 9.5 CPM** in these nuclei, opposite to the bulk hippocampal association. Dlat shifted only from **57.3 to 55.1 CPM** in the nuclear pools and moved in both directions across classes. This weakens use of either bulk result as a pan-neuronal premise. These are pooled genotype libraries and marker assignments, with no animal-level replication or endothelial class. **Next discrimination.** Measure DCA accumulation and the phosphorylated fraction of PDHA1 together under the same extracellular exposures. Include an independently delivered PDK perturbation. Lower DCA accumulation with preserved response to the comparator supports an exposure limit. Equivalent intracellular DCA with lower flux after equal dephosphorylation supports a downstream limit and makes total DLAT, oxidation per unit DLAT and native ATP recovery the informative measures.
