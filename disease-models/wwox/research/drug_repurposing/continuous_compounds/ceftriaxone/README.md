# Ceftriaxone and astrocytic glutamate clearance in WWOX loss of function

**Units and preparations.** Glutamate uptake in primary human fetal astrocytes uses tracer accumulation. Rodent brain experiments use protein from individual animals, extracellular sensor peaks and decay in nested slices or sites, in vivo microdialysis and animal-level EEG. Human CSF values are in mg/L or µM parent and are partitioned by study design. ALS outcomes are ALSFRS-R points per month, survival and participants with adverse events. WWOX evidence uses mouse cortical and hippocampal bulk TPM, human week-15 organoid bulk CPM and nuclear UMI CPM within a single hippocampal pool per genotype.

**28 November 2026 UTC.** Retain as a conditional ex vivo expression-to-function comparator. Defer systemic pediatric WWOX priority. The [frozen prediction](pretest.md) separates a delayed astrocytic surface transport effect from altered release or dispersion. Here EAAT2 is the human transporter and GLT-1 denotes the rodent orthologue.

## Exposure, clinical transfer and safety

- **Adult noninflamed CSF.** In an external-ventriculostomy cohort, five adults had quantifiable peak ceftriaxone values of 0.18–1.04 mg/L (median 0.43). At parent mass 554.6 g/mol this is 0.32–1.88 µM (median 0.78), around 8% of the nominal 10 µM fetal-astrocyte condition at the median peak. These are compartment-specific sampled peaks after a single exposure. [Nau Methods and Results](https://doi.org/10.1128/aac.37.7.1518) · [Parent molecular mass](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Ceftriaxone)
- **Pediatric infection.** A historical bacterial meningitis cohort of 30 gave two reported means of 5.4 and 6.4 mg/L at 1–6 h (9.74 and 11.54 µM). The newer sparse unbound serum–CSF model involved 98 children, 16 serum and 87 CSF samples. One participant provided both matrices. The uptake parameter was 20.1% (95% CI 5.9–68.6%). At the study's two multi-day windows, modeled median CSF values were 5.6 and 6.6 mg/L. Source populations, total versus unbound sampling and measured versus simulated values are kept separate. The overlap with 10 µM does not establish repeated unbound exposure at cortical synapses in WWOX. [Historical FDA table](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2015/050796Orig1s019lbl.pdf) · [Pediatric model, Methods, Tables 1–2 and Results Fig. 1 paragraph](https://doi.org/10.1128/aac.00747-24) · [Unit replay](exposure.tsv)
- **Adult ALS, CSF and randomized outcome.** The early study modeled an overall CSF/plasma ratio of 0.043 and used a 1 µM CSF protocol reference. Human CNS EAAT2 engagement was unresolved. The larger trial stopped for futility. Final phase 3 function was +0.09 points/month versus placebo (95% CI −0.06 to +0.24). Survival HR was 0.904 (95% CI 0.710–1.152). This limits a claim for ceftriaxone in that adult disease. It does not adjudicate a directly measured EAAT2 effect in WWOX. [Zhao PK Fig. 4](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcph.317) · [Cudkowicz Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(14)70222-4)
- **Feasibility.** In the final ALS population cholelithiasis occurred in 181/340 active versus 5/173 placebo (+50.35 percentage points), and serious hepatobiliary events in 41/340 versus 0/173. Stage 3 added ursodiol to the active arm. The parenteral pediatric infection indication comes with biliary and urinary precipitation, neonatal calcium and bilirubin restrictions, and reported severe neurological reactions including seizures and encephalopathy. These settings do not supply pediatric chronic neurological safety or justify a WWOX efficacy study. ## WWOX inference and discriminating observation

**The premise is functional transport, including ionic reserve.** Held SLC1A2 bulk declines from 180→116 TPM in P17 cortex (4 control/3 KO), 357→209 TPM in P17 hippocampus (2/3) and 90→74 CPM in week-15 organoids (2/4). All three directions survive single-library omission. In the original mouse hippocampal astrocyte assignment, pooled Slc1a2 rises 4,910→5,392 UMI CPM. Excluding Slc1a2 from the classifier gives 5,233→7,129 UMI CPM and retains the positive direction in independent double-positive Aqp4/Aldh1l1 nuclei. Atp1a2 goes 1,303→910 UMI CPM in the assignment excluding Slc1a2 and Slc1a3 goes 1,965→3,362 UMI CPM. One pool per genotype, changing membership and distinct denominators limit inference. Abundance, membrane localization and sodium-dependent function are separate. The Atp1a2 direction makes impaired maintenance of the uptake driving force during trains a testable rival to too little transporter. [Prior bulk and gate analysis](../acetylcysteine/mechanism.md) · [New marker exclusion replay](astrocyte_marker_sensitivity.tsv) · [GSE158684](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158684)

**Counterexample.** An illustrative two-compartment model keeps the glutamate input fixed. Doubling near-to-far dispersion and increasing near uptake in a different scenario both shorten the near decay from 83.3 to 62.5 ms and both give near AUC 0.0625 normalized concentration·s. The far area changes in opposite directions, 0.1250 with dispersion and 0.0625 with uptake, from 0.0833 at baseline. Reducing glutamate input gives the same lower near area while retaining the baseline decay constant. This is a linear model with no back-diffusion or sensor kinetics. It isolates identifiability at fixed input and extends the earlier [release versus removal comparison](../riluzole/exposure_clearance_and_disposition_20261127.md) with a second observation site. [Geometry replay](geometry_counterexample.tsv)

**One comparison that could change the decision.** In matched WWOX-LoF and corrected neuron–astrocyte preparations, establish transporter-dependent baseline clearance at comparable age and activity. After verified local exposure, compare an early exposure window with the delayed window. Measure surface EAAT2 and clearance at defined near and distal distances after the same calibrated local glutamate input. Pair with sodium recovery and direct substrate uptake and record evoked release or recruitment in a separate fixed-input experiment. The useful result is a null-selective increase in surface-localized transporter function, less distal spillover and preserved first recruitment after trains. It should be lost with a validated EAAT2 dependency control. Shorter local decay with greater distal spillover favors dispersion. A lower synaptic peak with equal fixed-pulse clearance favors release or recruitment. More surface protein with loss of uptake after a train would instead support limited ionic reserve. Compare independent animals or isogenic lines and preserve the distinction between baseline and already abnormal network activity.

**Disposition and review.** Keep as a conditional ex vivo comparator for whether expression can become transport without suppressing recruitment. Reopen for a reproducible null-selective transport phenotype at measured local exposure. An independent review could change the decision if it verifies a WWOX surface-activity deficit or a paired juvenile noninflamed exposure result. A human blood or CSF concentration alone would leave CNS engagement unresolved.
