# Ceftriaxone comparison, frozen before primary extraction

28 November 2026 UTC. Context policy SYNTHESIS. Resume from mitapivat. No dedicated dossier or active assignment for ceftriaxone or Rocephin was found. The existing riluzole comparison motivates separation of uptake from release and of early clearance from later rebound. This molecule tests a distinct proposed delayed EAAT2/GLT-1 expression and trafficking arm.

**Prediction.** If low or poorly localized astrocytic EAAT2 limits clearance in WWOX, sustained verified exposure should increase astrocyte surface EAAT2 and net uptake, then shorten the late glutamate transient after a fixed glutamate input. A useful null-selective effect should survive matched first recruitment and preserve subsequent transmission. An immediate synaptic effect that accompanies reduced release, or a protein gain without faster net removal, favors a different mechanism. Shorter local glutamate transients can also reflect dispersion or changed sodium-dependent transport under high activity.

**Decision rule.** Look first for studies in which ceftriaxone increased transporter abundance yet changed or failed to change extracellular glutamate kinetics, with release and evoked recruitment measured independently. Separate infectious pediatric CSF exposures from exposure in a developing noninflamed brain. Reopen the pathway as a conditional mechanistic tool only if delayed transporter-dependent clearance can be measured independent of release. Systemic priority requires a credible juvenile neural exposure bridge and indication-specific tolerability.
