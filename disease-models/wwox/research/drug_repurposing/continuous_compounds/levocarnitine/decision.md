# Levocarnitine decision in WWOX loss of function

**Disposition.** Park a general pediatric WWOX trial. Retain two research questions with different endpoints: whether a measured nutrient deficit associated with medication or feeding responds biochemically, and whether a WWOX-intrinsic cerebral flux defect responds at an attainable exposure. Evidence currently supports the first question under valproate more directly. It does not show that every child on valproate is deficient. [WWOX cases, basal pediatric CSF and mouse cell classes](compartments.md)

**What reduced CRAT might mean.** In ACLY-deficient hepatocellular carcinoma cells, added free carnitine increased labeling of fatty acids from glucose. Removing CrAT abolished that effect in two clones. Yet exogenous acetylcarnitine could still promote histone acetylation and lipogenesis without CrAT. The two interventions provide different carbon inputs. Fig. 7C used 10 mM free carnitine. The ratio of that medium concentration to the median CSF free carnitine of 4.11 µM in the independent infant group is roughly 2,430 (10,000/4.11). This compares concentrations in different experiments. It estimates neither brain uptake nor a required dose. The study also distinguishes new lipid originating from labeled glucose from total new lipid. These endpoints can diverge if carbon source changes while net synthesis stays stable. WWOX cells need not lack either ACLY or CrAT. Lower CRAT RNA could limit the response to more free carnitine, or residual enzyme could still support it. [Genetic and tracer controls, Fig. 7C–H](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10156126/) · [CSF comparator, Table 3](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmolb.2025.1643667)

**What the pediatric randomized study actually measured.** Children receiving valproate for over six months were allocated to 30 days of oral carnitine capsules or placebo (19 per group). Six of 38 dropped out, three in each arm. The end-of-study mean blood ammonia was 44.6 versus 81.4 µg/dL among the 16 completers per arm. The reported difference favoring carnitine was 36.8 µg/dL (95% CI 11.1–62.7). The relative difference was 45.2% of the control mean. Baseline ammonia, free carnitine and valproate concentration were unavailable, so this is a final between-group difference. The article describes the capsule as carnitine without specifying salt or stereochemical verification. Age eligibility started at one year, and group mean ages were 83.9 and 72.2 months. Liver or kidney impairment and urea-cycle disorders were among the exclusions. Seizure reduction and developmental improvement were not measured as efficacy outcomes. [Trial methods and Tables 1–2, Sidiartha et al.](https://www.cellbiopharm.com/ojs/index.php/MCBS/article/download/177/69)

**Discriminating prediction and stopping rule.** In age-matched untreated WWOX and isogenic control neural cells, the substrate-limitation account predicts a larger recovery of long-chain oxidation and function in WWOX cells when free intracellular carnitine recovers. At fixed total substrate uptake, an isolated rise in acetyl-group export or glucose contribution to lipid without a rise in absolute oxidation supports rerouting carbon. Retain glucose-derived C2, absolute C0 and C2, absolute oxidation and functional recovery in the same comparison. A better ammonia value confined to valproate exposure supports a separate peripheral response. Reopen trial prioritization for replicated genotype-selective functional rescue at measured attainable exposure, or, for the secondary question only, a documented WWOX subgroup with serial nutrient, medication, nutritional and clinically meaningful outcome data.
