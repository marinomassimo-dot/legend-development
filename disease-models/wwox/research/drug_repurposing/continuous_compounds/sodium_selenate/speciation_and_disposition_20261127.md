# Sodium selenate in WWOX loss of function

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. Compare parent exposure and tau direction before assigning a WWOX mechanism.

**Disposition.** Park pediatric WWOX trial prioritization. Retain a preclinical PP2A-versus-selenium-supply comparison conditional on a baseline tau defect and measured local selenate. The adult study quantifies elemental selenium in CSF. The cellular result raises the WWOX-relevant pS396 signal while lowering excitability. The acquired rat seizure result supports a separate delayed network effect and leaves its mediation open.

## Human exposure and chemical identity

Cardoso et al. studied adults with mild-to-moderate Alzheimer disease in a 24-week randomized pilot. Forty were randomized and 36 completed. In the higher selenate arm, total CSF selenium was **1.4 ± 0.5 µg/L** at baseline (n=17) and **20.2 ± 9.1 µg/L** at week 24 (n=14), with 13 paired observations and a reported paired change interval of 13.4–24.8 µg/L. Dividing the week-24 mean by the elemental molar mass (78.971 g/mol) gives **0.256 µM selenium equivalents**. One selenate ion contains one selenium atom. Even if all measured selenium at that visit were selenate, the mean free parent in those CSF samples could not exceed 0.256 µM. The nominal **1 and 3 µM** cell concentrations sit **3.91- and 11.73-fold** above that deliberately generous ceiling. This is a comparison of a group mean at one clinical visit and nominal cell exposure. Different CSF sampling times, individual concentrations, brain interstitium, intracellular delivery and cell-culture free concentration remain unresolved. The paired CI is for change in total selenium. [Cardoso et al., Table 2 and Fig. 2, pp. 196–197](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13311-018-0662-z) · [PubChem, disodium selenate CID 25960](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/25960) · [Elemental conversion](human_elemental_exposure.tsv) · [Parent ceiling](human_csf_parent_ceiling.tsv)

Size-exclusion ICP-MS in a volume-selected subset (n=11) captured a broader selenium response. The CSF peak provisionally attributed to selenate rose from 1347 to 1748 area units, about **1.30-fold**. The suspected selenite peak rose from 351.7 to 1551, about **4.41-fold**. Their selenium signal-area increases were 401 and 1199 respectively. The probable albumin-associated peak rose and the probable selenoprotein P-associated peak appeared above detection. Peak sizes are chromatography signal areas. Identity was provisional, and these data provide neither calibrated species concentrations nor free fractions. Differences in assay subset and resolution also preclude using the area percentages to apportion the concentration measured in Table 2. [Cardoso et al., Fig. 4B and Table 3, pp. 198–199](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13311-018-0662-z) · [Peak comparison](human_csf_peaks.tsv)

## Adult rat PK consistency check

Li et al. used ion chromatography calibrated with sodium selenate and separate ICP-MS on acid-digested samples for elemental selenium. Three checks restrict quantitative transfer from this source.

1. Read literally, Table 1 reports a 35.26 µg/mL parent peak after 0.04 mg/kg. Even if the whole dose were at once in plasma, this would restrict plasma volume to **1.13 mL/kg**. At a deliberately low sensitivity value of 20 mL/kg it implies **17.6 times** the administered dose present in plasma. The inconsistency persists across 20–40 mL/kg for all three groups. Independent direct plasma-volume measurements in adult rats are in the tens of mL/kg (for example 32.1 ± 3.3 mL/kg in an increased-permeability setting before albumin, n=30). [Rat PK, Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.1256) · [Direct rat volume study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00134-007-0756-2) · [Mass balance](rat_parent_mass_balance.tsv)
2. In the seven-day infusion narrative, ~50,000 ng/mL sodium selenate would alone contribute ~20,897 ng/mL elemental selenium, versus reported total selenium of ~350 ng/mL. The roughly **60-fold** discrepancy is between the reported steady parts of the curves. It flags units, calibration, recovery or analyte comparability for clarification. [Rat PK, Methods §§2.4–2.5 and Results §3.2, Fig. 5](https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.1256) · [Steady-state check](rat_steady_state_consistency.tsv)
3. The subcutaneous brain/plasma AUC ratios printed in Tables 3 and 4 do not follow from the displayed brain AUCs and the displayed plasma AUCs in Tables 1 and 2. For example, left cortical selenate 482.8/153.5 gives **3.15** versus **1.262** reported. The corresponding intraperitoneal rows reproduce within rounding. A different integration window or denominator could explain this discrepancy. No alternative denominator was specified in the sections examined. [Rat PK, Methods §2.5 and Tables 1–4](https://doi.org/10.1002/prp2.1256) · [AUC replay](rat_auc_consistency.tsv)

Use the result as evidence that the investigators used a parent-sensitive assay alongside total selenium. Quantitative brain exposure and partition from this paper remain on hold pending calibrated raw measurements, exact mass units and the integration windows. The replay tests the published units as printed and makes no correction to the source.

The held Wang study used differentiated human neuroblastoma, WWOX perturbations and a biochemical kinase comparison. It identified the WWOX-dependent direction at **S396/S404**, with **S422** spared in that comparison. The GSK3β Ser9 axis has a known evidence boundary in the laboratory record. Carry the phosphosite pair and a negative-control site forward without using pSer9 alone as a kinase proxy. A developmental WWOX phosphosite excess in the relevant neuronal state, and a phosphatase intervention able to reverse it, are separate hypotheses. The selenite comparison supplies a control for selenium incorporation and lipid-peroxide reduction. [Wang et al., held primary study](https://doi.org/10.1038/cdd.2011.188) · [Held WWOX evidence boundary](../../../../registries/claim_registry_current.md#claim-035) · [Held selenite comparison](../sodium_selenite/README.md)

**Discriminating observation.** In a stage- and genotype-matched WWOX system, qualify baseline pS396/pS404 over total tau, functional PP2A activity and candidate exposure before judging effects. Compare vehicle, selenate and equimolar elemental selenium as selenite, tracking species in the actual preparation. An early selective fall in pS396/pS404, with a directly measured increase in the relevant phosphatase activity, should precede better axonal transport and lower late recurrence at matched first recruitment. A selective PP2A perturbation should remove that advantage. Measure S422 as a pathway-specificity comparator and include a control matched on prior network activity to separate a change in phosphorylation from the consequence of reducing firing. Effects shared by both salts that track GPX4 activity and lipid-peroxide reduction favor selenium supply. Early firing suppression accompanied by unchanged or higher pS396/pS404 and weak axonal recovery favors a separate excitability effect. The observed SH-SY5Y result makes this last outcome a substantive rival.

## Pediatric feasibility and checkpoint

SeLECT is an investigational adult oral drug study in refractory temporal lobe epilepsy. The 15 July 2026 registry entry listed recruiting (22 accrued in that snapshot, target 124, ages 18–75). The randomized design compares 26 weeks of treatment and a week-52 composite of seizure frequency, adverse effects, quality of life and medication burden, with EEG and clinical components as separate endpoints. No aggregate outcomes were posted in the record examined. Adult studies establish a formulation and list tolerability signals including hair and nail changes. There is no verified pediatric WWOX free exposure, safety or response, and background antiseizure interactions remain unquantified here. The adult study design and open-extension safety experience give specific monitoring endpoints. They supply no pediatric therapeutic window. [SeLECT registry](https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=385697) · [Adult safety extension, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34988458/)

Reopen for credible age-relevant species-resolved exposure and WWOX phosphosite plus functional rescue at that exposure, or for a directly informative controlled study. A corrected rat PK table or blinded independent review with raw calibration and integration windows could change the exposure conclusion. Agreement across the text, original table and independently reproduced arithmetic already establishes the discrepancy as reported. Independent review without those missing inputs cannot resolve which value to use.
