# Oxytocin · developmental chloride extrusion versus acute recruitment in WWOX-LoF

**Unit and region.** P17 mouse cortex and hippocampal bulk libraries, week-15 human isogenic forebrain organoid libraries, P17 pooled mouse hippocampal nuclei by class, mouse primary hippocampal cultures, juvenile rat CA1 slices, male and female Fmr1-KO mouse sound-challenge cohorts, rhesus brain-region homogenates, and pediatric participant-level denominators reported in aggregate. SCI_CODEX, 27 November 2026. [Frozen mechanism and counterproof](pretest_20261127.md)

**Disposition.** Retain native oxytocin as a stage-specific preclinical probe and defer pediatric WWOX intervention priority. A drug effect on network activity could come from chloride extrusion, rapid interneuron recruitment or a different receptor. The decisive comparison is at matched native chloride load and measured local parent exposure.

## WWOX evidence and new comparison

The held P13–17 Wwox cortical field study and [chloride-recovery analysis](../../ganaxolone_chloride_recovery_20261123/README.md) describe a typical brief event (median **0.66 s**). Only **7–9/119** events could exceed 2 s in the original histogram from 18 slices of nine mice. The event ratio concerns recorded bursts. Native GABA reversal and interburst extrusion in the same Wwox preparation remain unresolved. This matters for an intervention claimed to change chloride handling rather than the current entering the cell during a burst. [Original Wwox study, Fig. 1D](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8609180/)

## Comparators that separate mechanisms

- **Developmental OXTR–KCC2.** At DIV8, embryonic mouse hippocampal cultures showed a more depolarized GABA reversal with Oxtr deletion than with WT in cell-attached single-channel recordings (22 vs 14 neurons, corrected using measured resting potential). That is a genotype comparison. In the separate acute pharmacology series, 100 nM nominal oxytocin for 10 minutes raised the pSer940/total KCC2 ratio at DIV3 and DIV4 (**1.357 and 1.321×** age-matched vehicle, reported n=6 and 8), with **0.956×** at DIV5 (n=6) and **0.867×** at DIV6 (n=8). Culture days do not map directly to the juvenile WWOX slice age. The DIV4 biotinylation result was **1.594×** surface KCC2 (n=4). In Fig. S2C, nominal **1 nM** changed phosphorylation at DIV4 (6 samples, 10 vehicle) and the separate 100 nM check in Oxtr-null cultures did not reproduce the phosphorylation response. Oxtr RNA rose across culture ages even as the acute KCC2 response disappeared. Fig. 3G and the methods assign the smaller GABA-evoked calcium transient to oxytocin versus vehicle in WT cells. Table S3 labels those same rows by genotype, contrary to the plotted comparison. Acute surface and calcium results leave native reversal and faster extrusion after drug exposure to be tested directly. [Leonzino et al., Figs. 1, 3, 4, Fig. S2 and Table S3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4826440/)
- **Acute circuit rival.** In P21–28 rat slices, the comparator TGOT, an OXTR agonist distinct from native oxytocin, raised evoked CA1 pyramidal spike probability **0.50 → 0.82** (15 cells) and lowered spontaneous firing **1.4 → 0.6 Hz** (23 cells) at nominal 200 nM. Fast-spiking interneuron activation and feed-forward inhibitory dynamics provide a route to improve evoked signal relative to background on an acute timescale. This assay did not require demonstrating a rescued native chloride gradient. [Owen et al., Fig. 1 and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5283693/)
- **A challenge phenotype with stage and sex boundaries.** In a 2026 Fmr1-KO preprint, P7–13 nasal oxytocin versus saline reduced adult sound-provoked ordinal severity in males (**0.57 vs 2.40**, 14 vs 15 mice). In females the means were **0.74 vs 0.78**, 23 vs 23 mice, with a lower baseline severity. The male latency curve reports later onset under treatment. Separate P30–36 male cohorts did not reproduce protection (13 treated, 12 saline). The adjacent text repeats the early means in the adolescent paragraph, whereas original Fig. 3B plots higher scores for the adolescent treated group and annotates the contrast as nonsignificant. Use that cohort's reported lack of statistically detected protection and avoid carrying over the early means or inferring a formally established sex-by-treatment interaction. Behavior elicited by sound, spontaneous EEG seizures and directly measured KCC2 are separate outcomes. [Chavez et al., preprint Figs. 2–3 and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308197/)
- **Native ligand selectivity depends on concentration.** Recombinant mouse binding gives oxytocin Kᵢ **0.83, 20.38 and 36.32 nM** for OXTR, AVPR1A and AVPR1B. A deliberately simple one-site comparison using Kᵢ as Kd gives **55%, 5%, 3%** at 1 nM and **99%, 83%, 73%** at 100 nM if those are free concentrations. These are scenarios for experimental specificity. Local ligand, receptor expression, efficacy, desensitization and competition are not measured by the calculation. The lower nominal concentration established a phosphorylation response only. [Busnelli et al., Table 1](https://jpet.aspetjournals.org/content/jpet/346/2/318.full.pdf) · [Receptor and exposure replay](source_comparisons.tsv)

## One discriminating test

Compare a stage series of WWOX-deficient and corrected identified cortical principal cells, with an age-matched early OXTR-responsive calibration model. Use the same chloride load and pre-load activity across arms. With measured unbound local parent, assess sustained and acute native E_GABA relative to resting voltage and spike threshold, and the recovery time course after the matched load. Use a chloride-preserving recording and an independent chloride or KCC2-surface readout, with buffer bicarbonate documented. Separately test OXTR, KCC2 and AVPR1A/1B dependence using controls verified for the species and concentration. Measure first evoked and inhibitory recruitment, spontaneous recurrence and washout.

- **Chloride mechanism.** Faster recovery after the same load and a more negative native E_GABA relative to rest precede reduced recurrence. The effect depends on OXTR and KCC2, with first evoked recruitment and inhibitory output preserved.
- **Recruitment mechanism.** Firing, inhibitory recruitment or recurrence changes while native chloride trajectory remains stable at the same load. A smaller chloride excursion caused by fewer bursts does not show faster extrusion. A depolarizing GABA reversal can still shunt spikes, so interpret it jointly with threshold and firing.
- **Stage or specificity failure.** The responsive control verifies the assay but affected later-stage neurons show no native recovery at equivalent exposure, or the effect tracks a vasopressin receptor or loss of first recruitment. This lowers priority for the proposed mechanism at that stage.

**Next step.** At a stage where WWOX and corrected preparations have comparable cell identity, qualify native chloride recovery and an OXTR engagement positive control. The pediatric studies leave free target-site exposure and effects with background antiseizure therapy unresolved. Review would be valuable once same-cell chloride and firing can resolve the rival mechanisms.
