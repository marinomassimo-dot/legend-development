# Trimetazidine opening comparison of long-chain substrate use

28 November 2026 UTC. Continuous compound #207, INV-07. context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. [Prespecified comparison](pretest.md).

| Primary abstract and preparation | Reported observation | What could distinguish mechanisms |
|---|---|---|
| Kantor et al., isolated working rat hearts | At 5 mmol/L glucose and 0.4 mmol/L palmitate, palmitate oxidation changed from 488 to 408 and glucose oxidation from 1,889 to 2,378 nmol/(g dry weight × min). Oxygen use and cardiac work showed no detected aerobic effects. Separate octanoate and butyrate conditions yielded no detected substrate-oxidation effect. A separate LC 3-KAT assay reported 75 nM IC50. [PMID 10720420](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10720420/) | Rat heart flux and enzyme inhibition are different results. Recover actual substrate ranges, assay fractions and biological denominators before comparing potency. |
| MacInnes et al., rat heart enzyme preparations and recombinant human LC 3-KAT | Did not detect inhibition with assays of substrate turnover or with a functional β-oxidation assay. The isolated working rat heart still recovered better after no-flow ischemia with 10 µM trimetazidine under their high free fatty-acid condition. [PMID 12869391](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12869391/) | Functional recovery and thiolase engagement separate under this protocol. A substrate-dependent false negative remains possible in the enzyme comparison. |
| Lopaschuk et al., companion study | Reported approximately half-maximal inhibition under one 2.5 µM 3-ketohexadecanoyl-CoA assay condition and argued that the higher substrate used by MacInnes could overcome inhibition. [PMID 12869392](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12869392/) | Check the exact pairing of substrate and drug concentrations in the full text. A shift in an enzyme reaction does not identify transport, coupling or net ATP production in juvenile neurons or glia. |
| Cavar et al., human adult coronary disease left ventricular tissue | Chronic treatment comparison and acute in vitro challenge found no detectable difference in fatty-acid oxidation; the chronic comparison also found no detected pyruvate oxidation or PDH activity difference. [PMID 26844527](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26844527/) | Independent no-detected-effect result in another species and preparation. Recover biological n, exposure, substrate range, positive controls and whether a null at the reported precision excludes the rat-scale flux effect. |

Replay of the two rat heart means gives **−16.4% palmitate oxidation** and **+25.9% glucose oxidation** using `100*(followup/baseline-1)`. These are separate between-arm comparisons of different substrate molecules. They cannot be added as ATP equivalents or used to attribute the cardiac recovery to a target. No clinical or neural inference is assigned at this checkpoint.
