# Trimetazidine, substrate use and evoked energy recovery

28 November 2026 UTC. Continuous compound #207, INV-07. context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. Start after [β-alanine #206](../beta_alanine/README.md). Adult cardiac metabolism, evoked rodent seizure behavior and juvenile WWOX neural function are distinct.

**Question.** At measured free parent and matched evoked work, does trimetazidine improve ATP-linked recovery in juvenile WWOX-deficient neural material through a substrate-resolved shift? A reduction in initial recruitment, altered mitochondrial coupling or a stress-pathway effect could preserve an ATP signal without thiolase-dependent substrate substitution.

**Published conflict.** An isolated rat heart comparison reports decreased long-chain palmitate oxidation and increased glucose oxidation. A separate rat and recombinant-human study reports functional cardiac recovery without detectable LC 3-KAT inhibition in its assays. A companion paper attributes the different enzyme finding to the concentration of 3-ketohexadecanoyl-CoA. Adult human myocardium offers an independent substrate-oxidation comparison. Recover exact enzyme preparation, substrate and inhibitor concentration, time, biological unit and assay positive control before judging these explanations. Keep the palmitoylcarnitine used in fibers distinct from the immediate thiolase substrate. [Kantor](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10720420/) · [MacInnes](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12869391/) · [Lopaschuk](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12869392/)

**Prespecified contrast.** First establish a native WWOX substrate-use abnormality. Compare isotope-resolved carbon use and ATP-linked recovery in the same preparation, with a calibrated thiolase-dependent comparator, identical substrate access and matched first excitatory and inhibitory recruitment. Include a condition in which glucose is dominant and a condition with verified long-chain fatty-acid flux. Measure coupling and a late functional endpoint independently of the first response. A genotype-by-exposure effect that covaries with verified long-chain flux change at matched work would favor metabolic mediation. Improved recovery without the predicted flux response, or an apparent benefit that vanishes after recruitment matching, lowers the thiolase hypothesis. A nominal exposure without available parent or usable long-chain flux provides an inconclusive test.

**First step.** Replay the cardiac flux changes and reconcile the primary assay disagreement by comparing substrate ranges and the independent human null. Examine provoked seizure data only with behavior and EEG separated. Keep pediatric exposure and tolerability as separate gates.
