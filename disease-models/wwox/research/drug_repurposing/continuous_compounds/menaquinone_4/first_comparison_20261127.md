# Opening comparison for menaquinone-4

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · rat P1–2 forebrain-derived premyelinating oligodendroglial culture and E14 cortical neurons, and human HeLa, COQ2 fibroblasts and HEK293 COQ4-null cells · nominal bath nM or µmol/L, dye/imaging-based viability, combined CII+III activity and protein-normalized ATP.

**Developing neural positive control.** Li et al. Fig. 2C reports approximate nominal EC₅₀ values of **2 nM MK-4** and **30 nM phylloquinone** for survival after 24 h cystine deprivation of the *same* rat oligodendroglial preparation (six separate experiments). The values distinguish compounds, despite the possible alternate reading of the abstract. The cultures were mainly progenitors and pre-oligodendrocytes, with no mature MBP-positive oligodendrocytes. Fig. 3A–B uses **0.1 µmol/L** in the separate primary oligodendroglial and immature cortical neuronal challenges (three experiments). The survival assay used Alamar Blue with visual checks. Fig. 3C reports no protection in an exogenous H₂O₂ challenge over the tested range. Nominal dose, GSH-depletion challenge and survival define the positive signal. Neither myelin formation nor basal WWOX injury was measured. [Primary study, Methods and Figs. 2C, 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6741273/)

**Mammalian respiratory countercheck.** Cerqua et al. found MK-4 after 7 days of **5 µmol/L** nominal exposure in a mitochondrial-enriched fraction of HeLa cells (Fig. 1, n = 3 in lipid measurements). At the same nominal concentration and duration in two human COQ2 fibroblast lines and HEK293 COQ4-null cells, MK-4 failed to restore combined CII+III activity. The COQ4-null ATP result also failed to recover with MK-4. Ubiquinone and a matched short-chain CoQ₄ rescue in the relevant arms support interpretability (Fig. 2A–E, n = 3–4, assay-level). The uptake measurement and the genotype-specific rescue comparisons are in different preparations. They leave concentration and redox state in the target inner membrane unmeasured. The Drosophila Pink1/Heix context has a published positive carrier claim. It cannot fill the mammalian gap. [Mammalian Figs. 1–2 and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6484000/) · [Fly primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22582012/)

**Working decision.** Prioritize the lipid-oxidation comparison before an electron-transfer claim. Next verify redox dependence in primary or developmentally relevant cells, diet and chemical-species dependence of attainable MK-4, and a baseline functional lesion that can be reversed in WWOX. A high-concentration respiration arm remains interpretable only with control target exposure and a coenzyme Q positive control. [Registered question](pretest_20261127.md)

Prioritize two next comparisons that can change the interpretation. The stable-isotope mouse study distinguishes dietary MK-4 from tissue MK-4 rebuilt after loss of the side chain. The GAS6/TYRO3 study provides a carboxylation-dependent remyelination precedent in a distinct model. Check whether label retention, the redox time course and carboxylated GAS6 can be distinguished under WWOX-relevant exposure and maturation. [Isotope study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8971004/) · [Carboxylation study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38572807/)
