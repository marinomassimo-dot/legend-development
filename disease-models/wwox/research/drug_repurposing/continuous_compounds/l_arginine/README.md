# L-arginine | Guanidinoacetate production versus conversion to creatine

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Opening checkpoint.** L-arginine free base is the sole active compound after minocycline. The existing creatine analysis found directionally lower GAMT RNA in mouse cortex, hippocampus and human organoids, with weaker magnitude in the human comparison. GATM differs across models. Added arginine may increase guanidinoacetate without improving local creatine or phosphocreatine if methylation or transfer between cell types is the bottleneck. A single inherited brain creatine deficiency case reported that pattern. A distinct pediatric SLC6A8 cohort used arginine without creatine, and rat neural aggregates trace guanidinoacetate conversion into creatine. These controls make this a separable precursor question. Keep nitric oxide and arginine competition as parallel explanations from the held citrulline and sapropterin strands. **Discriminating prediction.** At verified local arginine and stable initial activation, a substrate-limited WWOX preparation should show a rise in newly synthesized guanidinoacetate, then creatine and phosphocreatine, with better recovery after matched depletion. A rise limited to guanidinoacetate favors downstream methylation or intercellular transfer as a constraint. A response without increased creatine synthesis calls for the parallel nitric oxide, arginase and other substrate pathways to be checked. Prefer a stage-matched comparison with the existing creatine control and simultaneous native products.
