# L-arginine | Precursor exposure, creatine synthesis and recovery

**Decision.** Retain L-arginine as a conditional substrate-flux comparator and defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. Human precursor exposure and a urinary product response are documented outside WWOX. The evidence leaves local neural arginine availability, guanidinoacetate (GAA) handling and recovery of the phosphocreatine pool unresolved in WWOX.

## Entry and transfer are distinct

**Systemic exposure.** The parent Onalo study randomized 68 children aged 5–17 during sickle-cell vaso-occlusive pain. The hemodynamics report is the same trial. Its baseline analyses used 66 and Table 2 has paired plasma measurements for 22 active and 25 placebo participants. Arginine medians move 81→168 µM versus 93→125 µM, and ornithine 198→283 versus 208→240 µM. These are ratios of group medians (2.07-fold and 1.34-fold for arginine), with unequal measurement availability. They do not give the individual exposure trajectory or a treatment effect on brain arginine. [Parent oral trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33075179/) · [Paired plasma Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9954326/)

**Cross-system sensitivity.** A female-mouse brain perfusion study found a saturable arginine component with nonlinear half-saturation 55 µM, maximal rate 5.9 nmol/min/g and a separate linear term. Single-competitor experiments found lysine and ornithine inhibition. Substituting the active-arm plasma medians into the measured two-component mouse equation gives a 1.48-fold total rate in an arginine-only scenario. Including the observed ornithine medians and hypothetical fixed lysine 150–250 µM gives 1.89–1.94-fold. The absolute higher-composition rates in those scenarios are lower (3.77–3.96 versus 7.17 nmol/min/g) because modeled competition lowers baseline as well. The formula adds competitive terms using reported in-assay inhibition constants. The scenarios hold mouse parameters fixed, substitute group medians for perfusate composition and assume competition at a shared carrier. They demonstrate sensitivity to substrate context. They cannot predict human CNS concentrations or effects. [Perfusion primary, Figs. 2–3 and 6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13270607/) · [Replay scenarios](spectroscopy_and_transport.tsv)

**Transfer countercheck.** Jomura et al. detected labeled creatine after labeled GAA in a rat choroid plexus cell line. MCT12 knockdown lowered protein 57.8%, raw efflux 30.6% and the cold-subtracted efflux 53.2%. Assuming a linear relation between protein loss and pathway function gives 53% versus 92% inferred contribution, depending on that correction (Fig. 4, n=3 per caption). After ventricular dosing with creatine tracer, reported apparent brain transfer clearances were 4.65 for creatine and 5.18 for mannitol (µL/min/g). This precedes any claim about neuronal uptake or phosphorylation. Rat mixed neural aggregates show conversion of labeled GAA into creatine, and mouse oligodendrocytes can coexpress GATM and GAMT. Transfer dependence can vary by cell type and developmental stage. [Jomura, Figs. 3–5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9164341/) · [Rat aggregate primary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19879361/) · [Mouse coexpression primary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34418357/)

## WWOX-specific outcome and discriminating comparison

**Dato.** The held WWOX comparison finds lower GAMT expression in P17 hippocampus (−1.470), P17 cortex (−1.037) and human organoids (−0.390) on the difference-of-mean log2(1 + normalized expression) scale. GATM directions differ. The new contrast finds MAT2A down in both mouse tissues (−0.792 and −0.619) and up in organoids (+0.363), with directions maintained across one-library omissions. SLC7A1 and SLC16A12 also diverge across models. SLC22A15 is weakly changed in mouse tissues and rises in organoids. These are bulk coverage results in genotype-, stage- and tissue-distinct data, with few libraries. Native local GAA, creatine and methylation flux remain unresolved. [Held creatine dossier](../creatine/README.md) · [Native-pathway replay](precursor_expression.tsv)

**Inferenza.** Reduced conversion, reduced transport and increased upstream production can all raise GAA. A rise in labeled GAA is a proximal result. The critical question is whether it is followed by labeled and absolute creatine and phosphocreatine in the cells that need the energy reserve. A change in steady SAM/SAH or MRS alone cannot establish this. In global Gamt-null mice, early oligodendrocyte maturation and myelination lag. That developmental comparison supplies a relevant outcome, with a different severity and causal defect. [Gamt myelination primary, Figs. 2–3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9962777/)

**Ipotesi e verifica.** In a juvenile WWOX model and matched corrected preparations, compare L-arginine with vehicle and use the existing creatine comparator to test downstream rescue. Verify local arginine, lysine and ornithine and match initial recruitment and phosphocreatine depletion. Follow isotope enrichment and absolute levels of GAA, creatine and PCr in distinct neural and extracellular compartments, with SAM/SAH and functional recovery. Increased early labeled GAA followed by labeled creatine, PCr and faster recovery supports a substrate constraint. If GAA rises alone, determine whether labeled GAA reaches the GAMT-expressing compartment. Low intracellular GAA alongside extracellular accumulation favors impaired transfer. Preserved local GAA with poor conversion makes GAMT or methyl-donor capacity more plausible. Function changing without creatine flux calls for the nitric oxide or arginase branch. At matched input, increased GAA without functional gain or with worse late recurrence lowers priority.

**Pediatric feasibility.** Oral exposure data exist in school-age children and adolescents in unrelated indications. The small monotherapy series differ in follow-up, formulation reporting and MRS methods. The 108-participant sickle-cell IV trial used pharmaceutical-grade L-arginine hydrochloride and did not meet its clinical primary endpoint. That acute IV pharmacology has a different salt, timing and population. WWOX CNS exposure, long-term formulation tolerability with background antiseizure medicines and functional effects are undetermined. [Oral pediatric evidence](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33075179/) · [IV formulation and endpoints](https://doi.org/10.1002/ajh.27692)

**Reopen.** Require a documented WWOX substrate or pathway-flux deficit, measurable local exposure and a preferential recovery that does not accumulate GAA alone. A further review of the same MRS case series cannot supply the missing WWOX observation.
