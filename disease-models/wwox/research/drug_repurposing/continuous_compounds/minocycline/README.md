# Minocycline hydrochloride | Extracellular matrix proteolysis and inhibitory recruitment

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Opening checkpoint.** Minocycline hydrochloride is the sole active compound after N-acetylcysteine amide. The prior LEGEND inflammation scout identified limited uncontrolled seizure evidence and treated minocycline as a broad perturbation. The new comparison concerns postnatal perineuronal matrix and inhibitory function. Juvenile Wwox-deficient hippocampus has lower PV and NPY immunoreactivity and reactive glia. A separate developmental Fmr1 study links MMP-9 reduction, perineuronal nets and auditory circuit activity. WWOX cell abundance, PV expression per remaining interneuron and MMP-9 activity remain distinct questions. Minocycline offers a differential assay and pediatric trial precedent in another disorder, with formulation- and age-dependent labeling constraints. **Discriminating prediction.** If elevated extracellular MMP-9 limits maturation of surviving WWOX inhibitory interneurons, reducing activity within a physiological range should stabilize matrix around eligible interneurons and improve evoked inhibitory charge after a maturation interval. An independent selective MMP-9 comparator should reproduce this pattern at matched engagement. A reduced inflammatory marker without improved matrix and inhibitory recruitment favors a nonspecific glial response. A change attributable to missing interneurons or reduced first recruitment lowers priority. Include native activity, per-cell net coverage and a physiological plasticity control to detect excessive matrix stabilization.

**Next comparison.** Audit stage, region and the counts behind WWOX PV immunostaining. Check MMP9/MMP2 and matrix-readout coverage in existing WWOX profiles, distinct from extracellular protease activity. Then audit primary Fmr1 genetics, the selective comparator and the pediatric effect denominators, including age strata and whether people from the EEG and MMP-9 biomarker subsets overlap the randomized trial. Verify actual CNS exposure and formulation separately.
