# Minocycline hydrochloride | Matrix remodeling, protease attribution and inhibitory function

**Decision.** Retain minocycline as a conditional broad-response comparator, with pediatric WWOX trial priority deferred. Existing WWOX records justify a counts-first matrix and protease comparison. The first result that could change the decision is excess native extracellular MMP-9 activity at a WWOX stage with measurable nets and a physiological deficit, followed by selective functional improvement when that activity is brought toward control levels.

## WWOX starting condition

The independently reviewed INV-15 analysis reports 44% fewer PV-marker-positive cells in global Wwox-null hippocampus at two weeks (7 per group). Its cell counts can reflect loss, delayed PV expression, or both. Conditional cortical inhibitory current recordings used a distinct model and cells lacking PV identification. Preserve animal-level counts and the same-age, same-region comparison. **Direct activity and exposure.** Romero-Perez et al. reported apparent IC50 180 µM for active MMP9 in a fluorogenic assay at 0.6 µM enzyme. Casha et al. reported mean steady-state CSF minocycline 2.3 µg/mL (95% CI 2.1–2.5) after high IV exposure for acute spinal injury, approximately 5.03 µM (4.59–5.46) as parent. The cross-study scale difference is 35.8-fold. It does not estimate inhibition. The enzyme conditions, free drug in developing tissue, time course and clinical route differ. The result weakens direct catalytic inhibition as the default explanation for ordinary systemic exposure and leaves slower expression or activation changes open. ## Discriminating comparison

**Dato.** WWOX hippocampal MMP9 RNA and PVALB markers move in opposite directions, with different MMP directions across preparations. Net labeling per surviving lineage cell, active-to-total enzyme ratios and native protease activity remain open.

**Inferenza.** Activity-dependent release, proenzyme activation or inhibitors can change extracellular proteolysis even at stable RNA. Weak PV staining can lower the apparent density of PV-associated nets independently of their coverage per surviving inhibitory cell. Global inflammation also provides a plausible rival pathway.

**Ipotesi e verifica.** At a stage with measurable control nets, compare WWOX and corrected preparations at matched first recruitment. Use the existing counts-first lineage comparison and measure active and total MMP9 and MMP2 separately. Combine PV-independent lineage counts, WFA and a matrix core marker with a protease-dependent response. Select the orthogonal MMP9 perturbation according to whether the excess is activation-dependent or already catalytic. If modest verified MMP9 reduction restores matrix per surviving cell and evoked inhibitory charge while preserving early recruitment and physiological late potentiation, pursue the pathway. If minocycline improves only glial markers, the broad-response explanation gains weight. If matrix and recruitment track MMP2 or lineage loss, or potentiation falls, lower priority for the MMP9/minocycline hypothesis.

**Stop and reopen.** Seek direct stage- and region-matched evidence of excess proteolysis and a reproducible inhibitory functional gain with intact plasticity. Further reinterpretation of the same peripheral biomarker cannot supply this missing observation.
