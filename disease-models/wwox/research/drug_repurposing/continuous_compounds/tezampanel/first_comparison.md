# Tezampanel opening subtype comparison

28 November 2026 UTC. Continuous compound #204, INV-05. context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. [Prespecified contrast](pretest.md). Observations below come from primary abstracts and publisher metadata. No full-text coverage is claimed.

| Primary source | Unit, subtype and preparation | Decision consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Bleakman et al. | Kainate-evoked inward currents in HEK293 cells expressing human GluR5 (modern GluK1) or GluR6 (modern GluK2). LY293558 inhibited the GluK1 signal. No GluK2 effect was detected through **100 µM nominal antagonist** under the reported conditions, while NBQX inhibited that current. The same study reports inhibition in isolated rat Purkinje cells and dorsal root ganglion neurons. The abstract does not give the curve or agonist concentrations. [PMID 8609884](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8609884/) | A GluK2 homomer is a useful negative subtype comparison under those conditions. Purkinje and DRG responses cannot supply a matched GluK1 versus AMPA potency ratio. |
| Alt et al. | Recombinant human receptors in HEK293, recorded by whole-cell electrophysiology and calcium fluorescence. The panel contains GluK1, GluK2, GluK1/GluK2, GluK1/GluK5 and GluK2/GluK5. The public abstract reports the GluK1-containing versus GluK2-homomer distinction and identifies changed agonist responses on GluK2/GluK5. [PMID 15033339](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15033339/) | A GluK2-homomer result cannot stand in for GluK2/GluK5. Obtain the antagonist-by-construct table and the exact agonist and readout before assigning a numerical GluK2/GluK5 effect. |
| Vignes et al. | Rat hippocampal CA3 slices. **10 µM nominal LY293558** reversibly inhibited current evoked by **200 nM kainate**. The authors also report suppression of the current evoked by high-frequency mossy-fibre stimulation with NMDA and AMPA antagonists present. [PMID 9517417](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9517417/) | Confirms a native kainate-sensitive current under isolation. A recorded current from CA3 pyramidal neurons does not identify a presynaptic release response or the tetramer's composition. The concentrations here describe separate exposed compounds. |
| Contractor et al. | WT, GluK1-null and GluK2-null mouse hippocampal slices, mossy-fibre and associational/commissural inputs to CA3. The primary summary assigns suppression of evoked excitatory transmission to GluK2 and an increase in action-potential-independent miniature EPSC frequency to GluK1 upon kainate application. [PMID 11069933](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11069933/) | The genotype comparison gives a concrete alternative to assigning an endogenous presynaptic brake to GluK1 by default. Region, pathway, recruitment and the distinction between evoked and miniature outcomes matter. It does not resolve the effect of antagonist application under basal tone in juvenile WWOX. |

**Interim judgement.** Retain tezampanel as an unresolved mixed comparator. The first useful separation is a kainate subtype contrast under explicit agonist conditions. A clean AMPA-versus-kainate window in native tissue is still to be established. Rat CA3 sensitivity and mouse CA3 genetic separation concern different endpoints and species.

**Next step.** Find an accessible original numerical table with AMPA flip/flop, the relevant kainate assemblies and the actual agonist concentrations. Normalize competitive antagonism only within compatible protocols, retaining individual values and assay identifiers. Cross-check the original dual-target binding table and compare whether the available human or animal parent exposures can actually reach an interpretable range. The interim comparison does not transfer preclinical selectivity to pediatric WWOX.
