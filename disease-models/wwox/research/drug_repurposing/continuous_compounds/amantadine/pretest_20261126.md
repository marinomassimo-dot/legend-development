**Discriminating prediction.** A relevant direct NMDA blockade should change late NMDA-dependent charge in isolated juvenile Wwox cortex as a function of voltage, physiological Mg²⁺ and calibrated activation history, at a measured exposure that overlaps the attainable free range. The burst-recovery change should then track the independently measured NMDA current decrement with a matched-current d-APV comparator and preserved fast excitatory and inhibitory recruitment. A functional response confined to changed vigilance, or a response in isolated tissue with no NMDA current change at the same measured exposure, weakens the pore-block explanation. Measure vigilance and electrographic abnormalities separately.
