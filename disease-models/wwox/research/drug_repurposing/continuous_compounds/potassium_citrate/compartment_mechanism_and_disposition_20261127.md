# Potassium citrate in WWOX loss of function

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · `context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`

**Disposition.** Park tripotassium citrate for direct WWOX seizure or developmental trial priority. Keep the ketogenic-diet acid-base branch as a contextual comparator. The human clinical evidence concerns peripheral outcomes under ketogenic nutrition, with no post-dose neural exposure or WWOX-specific circuit response. The preclinical evidence supports testing the sign of a local pH effect and examining citrate carbon and glycolysis separately. ## Which neural mechanism is testable?

**Acid-base can change the outcome without citrate uptake.** In Choudhary et al., the triple-hit neonatal rat spasm model showed lower intracerebral pH under the ketogenic diet in lesioned pups (reported means 7.2 versus 6.9, six versus seven by MRS). Sodium bicarbonate partially restored spasm frequency in a separate intervention group (n = 8). The authors did not obtain MRS pH in the bicarbonate arm. There was no counterpart to this brain acidification in sham ketogenic pups. Diet, lesion and developmental stage matter. This is a direct reason to test for a loss of late-burst restraint under controlled local alkalinization, with paired pH and neural recordings. It is not evidence for altering systemic acidosis management in WWOX. The held bicarbonate-sensitive Kv7 result also motivates a fast-synapse-blocked intrinsic readout independent of GABA. [Choudhary et al., Fig. 5 and Discussion](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8557655/) · [Held Kv7 experiment](../sultiame/bicarbonate_kv7_20261126.md)

**Reversal direction cannot be inferred from bicarbonate in blood.** The two-anion GHK calculation in the replay fixes extracellular chloride at 130 mM, varies intracellular chloride from 5–20 mM, and uses a relative GABA-A bicarbonate permeability of 0.2–0.4. These are modelling scenarios. For extracellular bicarbonate 18→24 mM at fixed intracellular levels, E_GABA changes **−0.46 to −0.24 mV**. If intracellular bicarbonate also increases from 10→16 mM with chloride still fixed, the model instead gives **+1.18 to +5.85 mV**. The corresponding sign crossover in intracellular bicarbonate gain is **0.31–1.05 mM**. This comparison is neither a WWOX concentration estimate nor a late-network simulation. Chloride accumulation, carbonic anhydrase, extracellular potassium transients and shunting can independently change output. At stipulated equilibrium pH 7.30, an illustrative 18→24 mM extracellular bicarbonate manipulation co-varies pCO₂ by about 37.9→50.5 mmHg under the chosen buffer constants. With bicarbonate and pH both fixed the pCO₂ is fixed also. Thus pH match alone permits a CO₂-dependent effect. [Two-anion model and carbonate control](replay_comparison.py) · [Primary bicarbonate-dependent potassium study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6793904/) · [Held juvenile WWOX comparison](../acetazolamide/burden.md)

**Imported citrate is a weak bridge to synaptic energetics.** Kumar et al. quantified fasting adult plasma citrate at 108 ± 23 µM in eight individuals, before any compound exposure. In neonatal rat cortical cultures exposed to 500 µM [2,4-¹³C₂]citrate for 48 hours, they measured intracellular citrate and detectable α-ketoglutarate and labeled fatty acids, with no detected change in neuronal enrichment between the tested oxygen arms. Net medium uptake was visible only in the liver-cell comparison. The SLC13A5 loss test demonstrated transporter dependence in hepatoma cells. Whole-cell labeling, especially fatty acid labeling, cannot establish rapid oxidative supply at a synapse. In a separate 2025 preprint, Koshenov et al. found only a small acute axonal citrate-sensor signal during 1 mM extracellular citrate, with no significant control–SLC13A5 knockdown difference in this acute readout (12 recorded units per group). The study instead links activity-dependent clearance of **intracellular** presynaptic citrate to glycolytic recruitment and proposes a vesicular role. These measurements differ in time and compartment, and qualify the conjecture that providing extracellular citrate could improve rapid glycolysis. Citrate uptake or conversion might alter lipid handling or even inhibit glycolytic recruitment, depending on compartment and time. Neither study measured oral delivery into a WWOX neural compartment. [Kumar, Fig. 1A/F and Fig. 3B/D](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8500708/) · [Koshenov, Results and Fig. 3A–B, preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12224543/)

**Feasibility.** Pediatric precedent covers defined clinical products such as Polycitra-K crystals under monitored ketogenic therapy. A separate formulation labeled Cytra-K contains both tripotassium citrate and citric acid monohydrates. Keep product composition, coadministered salt, renal handling, potassium and background antiseizure treatment explicit. Interaction with carbonic-anhydrase-inhibiting antiseizure drugs is a confounder for alkali demand and urinary risk, with no established WWOX-specific interaction or exposure-response. Neither urinary alkalinization nor a serum bicarbonate result gives a direct CNS pharmacokinetic measurement. Adult product regulation and clinical precautions do not substitute for a matched pediatric WWOX endpoint. [Clinical audit and formulation](first_comparison_20261127.md) · [Primary renal and antiseizure-treatment context](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5590010/)

**Discriminating test and reopen condition.** At matched developmental stage, substrate availability, ketone level and starting recruitment, compare the same measured local pH/bicarbonate/CO₂ conditions with and without citrate. Match external potassium, sodium, chloride, osmolality and ionized calcium, and verify intracellular pH and chloride. Sample early and late E_GABA, Kv7-sensitive intrinsic firing and cumulative electrographic burst time, with evoked recovery as a separate endpoint. The same response without citrate favors an acid-base branch. An effect after citrate that follows a fall in ionized calcium and disappears with matched free calcium favors an ionic explanation. A persistent WWOX-preferential citrate effect at measured exposure, with labeled intracellular species and a matched activity-dependent ATP or glycolysis result, would justify testing direct handling. Substrate incorporation and allosteric glycolysis require independent readouts. Keep a slower, lipogenic tracer response separate from a rapid ATP response. Reopen for matched central exposure and functional evidence or a prospective pediatric comparison with equivalent sampling.
