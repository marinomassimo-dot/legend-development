# Tripotassium citrate in ketogenic-context WWOX research

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · `context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`

**Active molecule after the POPS disposition.** Tripotassium citrate, recording hydrate, excipients and other alkalinizing salts per preparation. The clinical precedent is adjunctive acid-base management during ketogenic diet initiation in pediatric epilepsy. The WWOX question is whether the resulting acid-base change improves metabolic recovery while preserving early inhibition, and whether a citrate-specific signal remains with acid-base state and extracellular ions controlled. This connects the held [acetazolamide](../acetazolamide/README.md), [sultiame](../sultiame/README.md) and [ketone uptake/oxidation](../d_bhb/decision.md) comparisons in a different experimental direction.

**Fixed comparison.** Recover the pediatric formulation, enrollment era, diet trajectory, age, baseline seizure phenotype, concomitant drugs and sampling schedules, including how often serum pH and bicarbonate were measured in each group. Keep acid-base and urinary stone endpoints separate from seizure burden, alertness and recovery. Extract the conditions under which GABA-dependent bicarbonate currents can change early versus late responses. Treat serum, cerebrospinal fluid and intracellular neuronal pH/bicarbonate as separate observations. Do not map blood pH to slice pH. Check if extracellular citrate reaches measurable target exposure, alters central carbon flux or simply accompanies systemic metabolism and alkali delivery.

**Discriminating prediction.** At matched developmental stage, ketone exposure and baseline recruitment, a measured acid-base shift reproduced in the absence of citrate predicts an equivalent change in late E_GABA, firing or recovery if the proximal effect is pH/bicarbonate-dependent. Match extracellular potassium and free calcium, report CO₂ with pH and bicarbonate, and monitor intracellular pH and native chloride. A citrate-associated functional effect that persists at matched acid-base conditions and free ions, accompanied by labeled citrate carbon in relevant downstream pools, would favor citrate handling. Repeat with alternative causes, including potassium-driven depolarization and citrate calcium binding. If only blood or urinary markers improve in the available data, record that effect as systemic and keep WWOX neural efficacy unresolved. [Primary pediatric comparison](https://doi.org/10.1111/dmcn.14393)

**First follow-up sources.** Audit cohort overlap in the McNally and earlier same-center kidney-stone studies before comparison. Use sodium-dependent citrate transport and synaptic glycolysis studies to check whether citrate is a fuel tracer, an allosteric signal for glycolysis, or both in a particular preparation. Separate net import across a cell membrane from uptake into the brain after oral exposure.
