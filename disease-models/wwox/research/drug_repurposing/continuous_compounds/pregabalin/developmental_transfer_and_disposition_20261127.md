## Reopening condition

Use one matched **CA3→CA1 release comparison at the juvenile age with an early-stage positive control**. First establish whether baseline selective GluK1 perturbation lifts a tonic brake in Wwox tissue using a direct glutamate-output readout, stable fiber recruitment and agonist-bypass measurements. In the same preparation compare pregabalin with matched vehicle at measured local exposure. Record presynaptic calcium and glutamate, direct GluK1 current or surface signal, and the effect of independent NMDAR control on the release change. Preserve native inhibitory efficacy as a parallel endpoint with cellular chloride undialyzed and with the effect on spiking measured separately. Keep animal as the replication unit.

- A fall in calcium and glutamate that survives NMDAR control, with stable direct GluK1 and native inhibition, favors the CaV-linked branch. A fall in release that depends on NMDAR signaling with preserved initial drive favors the presynaptic receptor alternative.
- Lower direct GluK1 with increased glutamate at preserved calcium is the predicted signature of removing a verified tonic brake at a Wwox terminal. A loss of direct response with stable release could arise at a recipient cell and still affect recruitment or native GABA action.
- Comparable changes across genotypes, an effect appearing only at the high comparator, or loss of physiological inhibition defers WWOX-specific advancement. Reopen for a verified Wwox-specific difference under the exposure and preservation controls, with spontaneous burden assessed separately after mechanism is identified.
