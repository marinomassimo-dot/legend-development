## Exposure and pediatric precedent

The measured adult lumbar CSF comparator is small (nine perioperative adults with serial lumbar sampling). At 2 and 6 h the reported parent concentrations are 0.115 and 0.359 µg/mL, approximately 0.72 and 2.25 µM, respectively. Median time to peak is 8 h. The 100 and 500 µM nominal slice exposures are about 44-fold and 222-fold above the 6 h value. These are ratios between measured compartments at different ages and timings. They do not estimate unbound pregabalin in juvenile WWOX hippocampus or exclude a sustained low-exposure effect. Only abstract-indexed quantitative PK was used for this source. [Buvanendran et al., PMID 20975469](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20975469/)

The FDA's rat brain extracellular-fluid series gives a separate compartment check. The plotted mean near 104 min after oral exposure is about 373 ng/mL (~2.34 µM) for extracellular fluid, with contemporaneous plasma around 3,900 ng/mL (~25 µM). This is a digitized plotted point without a variance or age-matched WWOX transfer. ## Decision and next comparison

**Data.** The Wwox P17 nuclear signal rises with either pore partner and chiefly follows α₂δ-2 detection. Acute work at high concentrations demonstrates state dependence, sometimes with opposing effects on excitation. Delayed work shows a measurable surface effect in a distinct α₂δ-1 context.

**Inference.** The strongest immediate comparator uses simultaneous measurements of terminal surface α₂δ-1/α₂δ-2 and CaV2.1/CaV2.2, calcium transient and absolute release. Direction and duration matter as much as fractional inhibition in this setting. Nominal bath comparisons cannot certify CNS exposure.

**Hypothesis.** At measured juvenile WWOX-relevant exposure, any useful pregabalin effect will preferentially reduce recurrent excitation through an α₂δ-dependent change in release while preserving first recruitment and output from inhibitory cells. Changes before surface protein declines favor state-dependent acute modulation. Changes emerging with reduced surface abundance after preserved initial response favor delayed trafficking. Neither follows directly from nuclear abundance.

**Discriminating comparison.** In age- and region-matched control and Wwox preparations, balance terminal protein, first calcium influx and native inhibitory recruitment at baseline. Measure parent concentration, early and late evoked release from identified excitatory and inhibitory inputs, and terminal surface complexes at early and delayed observations. Include α₂δ-1 and α₂δ-2 comparisons with baseline-matched functional controls, and a separate test that gates the effects of altered synapse count after sustained exposure. Comparable depression across genotypes, greater loss of inhibition, or a response confined to high nominal exposure lowers priority. Next audit the complete direct binding assay if openly accessible, verify which isoform perturbations are interpretable at baseline, and search for juvenile extracellular PK or public WWOX recordings before disposition. An external reread cannot supply the missing exposure or same-synapse calculation at this checkpoint.
