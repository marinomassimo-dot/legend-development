# Creatine monohydrate and energy recovery after firing

**Current decision.** Park creatine pending a WWOX-specific metabolic test. The [pediatric spectroscopy follow-up](mrs.md) found a strong dependence on the measurement reference. The evidence does not justify advancing it toward a WWOX trial. GAMT expression fell in all three bulk comparisons. The human change missed the threshold fixed before extraction. Human GATM and SLC6A8 changed in the opposite direction to mouse cortex. Expression of transporter and kinase in tissue cannot establish that extracellular creatine reaches the relevant neurons or replenishes phosphocreatine. **Countercheck on cell composition.** In a separate juvenile hippocampal single-nucleus dataset, Gamt fell from 5.59 to 3.44 counts per million among marker-assigned nuclei. A symmetric decomposition assigns −1.05 of the −2.15 CPM difference to altered class weights and −1.10 to altered expression within the assigned classes. Weights are fractions of the RNA molecules observed in these nuclei. They are sensitive to capture, sequencing and cell size as well as composition. There was one pool per genotype. Ckb actually increased by 54.0 CPM in the classified nuclei while bulk hippocampal Ckb fell. Neither the cell-class allocation nor the tissue average establishes a cell-autonomous defect or explains a functional response. **Discriminating prediction.** In developmentally matched control and WWOX-deficient neural preparations, compare phosphocreatine recovery after the same stimulation and initial depletion, with and without creatine. Measure absolute creatine, phosphocreatine, ATP, guanidinoacetate and cell composition alongside recovery time. Under a substrate limitation, increasing intracellular creatine should improve phosphocreatine recovery preferentially in deficient cells. If intracellular creatine rises but recovery remains slow, examine ATP supply and kinase activity. If intracellular creatine does not rise, the limiting step may precede the kinase. Lower baseline firing or a smaller initial depletion can mimic improved recovery. A total-creatine resonance, or a metabolite ratio using creatine as the reference, cannot by itself resolve these alternatives.

**Source leads identified before the spectroscopy follow-up.** The current datasets are untreated and contain no product-specific brain-exposure, safety or developmental-response measurement. Two primary healthy-youth spectroscopy studies and one WWOX case have been located for targeted reading. Check whether each reports absolute concentrations, ratios or time-resolved reserve, region, exposure duration and usable uncertainty. Then check whether pediatric participants and formulation match the intended population. These leads have not been treated as evidence in the present result. **Independent review received.** An independent laboratory calculation on the nine shared GSE158684 classes at snapshot `ec5610349` reproduced the pooled Gamt difference of −2.149 CPM. Omitting myelin-forming oligodendrocytes retained a negative difference (−1.543 CPM) but changed the class-weight component from −1.050 to +0.096 CPM. Every single-class omission preserved the negative within-class component. The near-even split of the original difference is sensitive to the weighting scheme and to sparse counts. This constrains interpretation of the two pools; it supplies no new animal replicate or evidence of creatine deficiency. Trial priority remains parked.
