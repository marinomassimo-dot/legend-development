# Creatine monohydrate, first decision before extraction

A specific hypothesis is that reduced endogenous creatine production with preserved cellular uptake could leave room for exogenous substrate to increase the phosphocreatine reserve after repeated firing. Transport or creatine-kinase limitation, impaired ATP generation, tissue composition and normal energy reserve are alternatives. The existing laboratory ranking abstained on creatine for lack of direct evidence of a relevant energy deficit.

Screen juvenile neuronal Wwox-deletion cortex and human knockout cerebral organoids separately. Prioritize a shared transcriptional signal only if GATM or GAMT (same gene) decreases by at least 0.5 log2(expression + 1) in both, all one-library omissions preserve the negative sign, and mean SLC6A8 and CKB expression each exceeds 1 TPM or CPM in each knockout cohort. The threshold is an exploratory triage convention, not a clinical or physiological criterion. Inspect SLC6A8 and CKB contrasts and juvenile hippocampus as a regional control. A positive result could prioritize direct assays. A negative result leaves substrate supply and kinase flux unresolved. None of these data measured creatine treatment or blood–brain transfer.
