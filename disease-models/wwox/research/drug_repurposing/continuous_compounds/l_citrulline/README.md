# L-citrulline · arginine recycling under neural metabolic demand

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Why this compound.** L-citrulline tests a substrate/recycling hypothesis that the L-lysine transport and inosine salvage dossiers do not cover. It is a supplement with a pediatric metabolic comparator using stable-isotope measurements (El-Hattab et al., MELAS). That study is a lead for separating systemic arginine and nitric oxide flux, not evidence of neural target engagement or WWOX benefit. [Primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26851065/)

**Initial WWOX result.** In untreated bulk data from juvenile mouse cortex (GSE158682, 4 control/3 KO), hippocampus (GSE158681, 2/3), and human organoids (GSE156243, 2/4), ASL KO-minus-control differences in group means of log2(1+expression) were **+0.87, −0.32 and −0.20**, respectively. The cortex increase and organoid decrease preserve direction under each single-library exclusion. NOS1 is likewise discordant, **−0.23, −0.02 and +0.50**, with stable opposite signs in cortex and organoids. ASS1 ranges cross zero in all three. These results weaken a conserved whole-tissue recycling deficit. They cannot distinguish tissue composition, conversion capacity, nitric oxide synthase coupling, or substrate deficiency. **Discriminating prediction.** At measured free exposure, add labelled parent to stage-matched WWOX-deficient and corrected neural preparations and quantify label in newly made arginine separately from the total arginine pool. Test a matched-arginine comparator and a control for restoration of reparative nitric-oxide signaling versus nitrosative stress. A rise in blood arginine without neuronal label supports a peripheral effect. Improved neuronal bioenergetic recovery with labelled conversion beyond the already replete control and without increased protein nitration favors a central substrate limitation; increased labelling or nitrate alone is insufficient. A localized endothelial response belongs to a separate mechanism when judging CNS transfer.

**Disposition.** Park for an unselected pediatric WWOX trial. Pediatric systemic isotope turnover, the neuronal counterexample and the pooled WWOX nuclear data point to different parts of the pathway. Reopen for evidence that attainable exposure yields WWOX-selective functional recovery with labelled conversion, using a vascular and a matched-arginine comparator.
