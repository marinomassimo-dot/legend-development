# L-citrulline · systemic conversion and the neuronal substrate gate

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

## What the pediatric tracer result measures

El-Hattab et al. studied five children with MELAS, ages 4–16, and five healthy controls. The MELAS participants received oral arginine in the first admission and citrulline in the second, at least one week later. The comparison was fixed order. Controls completed baseline isotope measurements without supplementation. Estimated rates use plateau plasma isotope enrichments. Synthesis of arginine from citrulline and synthesis of nitric oxide (NO) were assessed with different stable tracers, so their group means cannot identify how much newly synthesized neural arginine entered nNOS. No CSF, brain production, or relevant neurological function was measured. [Methods §2 and Results §3, Fig. 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4818739/)

After citrulline, mean systemic de novo arginine synthesis rose from **5.3 to 36.3 µmol/kg/h** and mean NO production from **0.13 to 0.41 µmol/kg/h**. Total arginine flux rose from **67 to 186 µmol/kg/h**. The ratio of group means for de novo synthesis divided by arginine flux rose from **7.91% to 19.52%**. The corresponding NO-to-flux ratio moved less, from **0.194% to 0.220%**. These are ratios of rounded group means, with neither individual pairing nor interval estimates for the ratios. They show that the larger absolute NO flux can coexist with a small change in NO relative to total turnover. They cannot establish a substrate-channeling effect. The arginine comparison is tabulated to make the fixed-order alternative transparent. [Calculation](tracer_counters.py) · [Tabulated data](tracer_counters.tsv)

## An intracellular counterexample

In Simon et al., Fig. 3C, A673 neuroepithelioma cells under lysine depletion showed about threefold higher total intracellular arginine with extracellular citrulline. The arginine assay used 1 mM citrulline. Even at 4 mM extracellular citrulline, Fig. 3D showed no restoration of the NO reporter in A673. In TGW-nu-I neuroblastoma cells, Fig. 5A showed an increase in the same reporter under lysine depletion. These are transformed lines with a deliberately depleted exchangeable arginine pool. The intracellular concentration and NO reporter are different assays at different concentrations. The 4 mM challenge is about 26 times the **156 µM mean plasma citrulline** after supplementation in the pediatric MELAS study. This comparison is contextual and gives no neuronal exposure estimate. [Simon et al., Figs. 3 and 5 and accompanying results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3706577/)

## Discriminating prediction and next check

At the same measured extracellular parent exposure and a replete baseline, test whether cell-resolved conversion produces labelled intracellular arginine, then whether that is accompanied by nNOS-linked signaling and functional recovery. A rise in labelled arginine with unchanged neuronal NO/cGMP supports a conversion pathway without delivery to nNOS. A response confined to brain endothelial cells supports a separate neurovascular mechanism. A neuronal increase accompanied by protein nitration or impaired recovery would weaken the proposed benefit. Confirm the appropriate label for the NO endpoint separately from carbon-label tracking into arginine. Directly compare matched arginine where feasible.

## Endothelial uptake versus parent delivery

Lee and Kang's TR-BBB study measures radiolabel recovered after washing and lysing immortalized rat endothelial cells, without an abluminal receiver. The two reported apparent uptake components have Kₘ values of **30.9 µM** and **1.69 mM**. In a hypothetical unloaded rat-cell assay with nominal citrulline set to the MELAS plasma group means of 21 and 156 µM, the fitted high-affinity term would move from **0.40 to 0.83 of its own Vmax**. The low-affinity term would move from **0.012 to 0.085**. The calculation uses S/(Kₘ+S), with no competition, and makes no prediction for BBB permeability or CSF concentrations. [Assay and kinetics, Fig. 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5424428/) · [Sensitivity calculation](barrier_sensitivity.py) · [Results](barrier_sensitivity.tsv)

The LAT1 knockdown reduced its mRNA by **60%** and cell-associated uptake by **34%** at 44.4 µM tracer (Fig. 6). The partial reduction is evidence consistent with a contribution, without identifying the share of net transendothelial flux. Table 2 shows strong inhibition by phenylalanine and glutamine at 2 mM in this assay. Those in vitro conditions do not establish a clinical interaction. In a polarized model, quantify intact labelled parent, labelled arginine and paracellular tracer separately in the receiver compartment and in endothelium. Use a physiological extracellular amino-acid mixture on both sides and a matched arginine arm. If the label remains endothelial or crosses predominantly as arginine, reject delivery of intact citrulline as the explanation under those conditions. [Lee and Kang, uptake methods, Table 2 and Fig. 6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5424428/)

## Untreated WWOX hippocampus at nuclear resolution

In GSE158684, the same marker assignment used for the ganaxolone dossier estimates ASL at **7.19 → 4.83 CPM** in excitatory nuclei and **8.48 → 14.39 CPM** in inhibitory nuclei (control → knockout, cutoff 500 UMI). Both directions persist at 1,000 UMI. NOS1 declines in both assigned classes at both thresholds. At 500 UMI, ASS1, ASL and NOS1 were jointly detected in **0/1,596 versus 1/1,262 excitatory nuclei** and **0/246 versus 0/293 inhibitory nuclei**. The sparse joint detection is uninformative about absence of pathway activity. These are pooled genotype libraries, so the cutoff check measures sensitivity to depth and supplies no biological replication. The classifier has no endothelial class. ## In vivo counterchecks and decision

After oral L-citrulline, Chowdhury et al. measured terminal citrulline in broiler diencephalic homogenate and plasma (eight birds per arm, separate thermoneutral and heat-exposed groups). Within each temperature condition, the ratio of treated to vehicle group means in tissue was **1,816/183 = 9.92** and **2,388/184 = 12.98**, respectively. The corresponding plasma ratios were **7,083/25 = 283.3** and **9,601/25 = 384.0**, using the table as printed. These within-matrix ratios avoid deriving central exposure from the absolute plasma units. The methods describe blood collection and dissection without documenting vascular perfusion. The achiral concentration assay used unlabelled supplementation, leaving residual blood, local production and intact parent transfer unresolved. This supports a tissue-associated change in an avian model. [Chowdhury et al., PDF pp. 2–5, Tables 1–2](https://doi.org/10.3389/fanim.2022.875572)

Two mammalian leads were assessed at abstract level. An adult transgenic Alzheimer mouse study reports higher CSF arginine after oral citrulline, no increase in sampled brain tissue and only a tendency toward higher NO. A separate mouse cerebral ischemia study reports restoration of endothelial NOS expression and improved hippocampal NO-metabolite response to **local arginine** after oral citrulline. These readouts fit a peripheral/vascular route and leave direct neuronal recycling open. The adult MELAS phase I study is a clinical lead for tolerability and cerebral perfusion measures. It does not substitute for pediatric WWOX pharmacokinetics or a controlled efficacy endpoint. [Mouse CSF study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33940559/) · [Mouse ischemia study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23685189/) · [Adult trial registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03952234)

**Disposition:** park L-citrulline for an unselected pediatric WWOX trial. The oral supplement and pediatric tracer precedent support feasibility of measuring systemic exposure. There is no demonstrated sustained central exposure, genotype-selective response or WWOX-specific pediatric tolerability evidence. Reopen if a relevant WWOX preparation shows labelled precursor conversion and functional recovery at measured attainable exposure, with endothelial effects and matched arginine assessed separately. Require a functional endpoint and absence of increased nitration alongside the biochemical readout. Independent review would be decision-changing if it identifies a relevant parent tracer or WWOX selective-response dataset. The sources located here supply neither.
