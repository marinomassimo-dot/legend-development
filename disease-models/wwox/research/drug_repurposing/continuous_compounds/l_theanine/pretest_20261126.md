**Discriminating prediction.** At the same measured free glutamine and L-theanine concentrations, acute competition predicts reduced labeled glutamine uptake and slower restoration of evoked inhibitory charge during and after repeated activity. Check excitatory output simultaneously. A delayed compensatory mechanism instead predicts increased uptake or faster recovery after washout with preserved inhibition and an independently verified transporter change. A larger late excitatory response or poorer inhibitory recovery is an adverse-direction mechanistic signal even if the aggregate event count falls.
