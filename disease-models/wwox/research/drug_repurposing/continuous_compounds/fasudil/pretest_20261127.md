# Fasudil and ROCK-dependent first-sheath formation in WWOX loss of function

**Status.** Opening checkpoint for a single compound. The earlier LEGEND target graph considered fasudil among pan-ROCK candidates in an inflammatory TNF chain. This comparison asks whether an exposure-supported axon–oligodendrocyte mechanism can change the formation and function of a first sheath. Measured adult CSF hydroxyfasudil motivates checking exposure by active species. [Prior ROCK graph and its limits](../../decomposition_it27_p6_20261104/README.md) · [Adult study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12741558/)

**Prediction.** In early myelination with neuronal WWOX loss, lower ROCK substrate phosphorylation should precede an increased fraction of all eligible axons that acquire a first compact sheath. This should be visible after Fasudil and its expected metabolite are matched to measured local exposure, with axon caliber, axonal firing and oligodendroglial stage recorded. In a pair of axon–glial preparations with matched activity, separate mutant axons plus corrected glia from corrected axons plus mutant glia. Measure first-sheath initiation before sheath geometry, and then test latency versus axonal distance after washout. Orthogonal ROCK1/2 attribution or stage-matched perturbation would determine whether the same cytoskeletal mechanism is engaged. [Held first-sheath and propagation denominator](../clemastine/axonal_denominator.md) · [Neuronal deletion study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33914858/)

**Competing explanations.** A change in branching or myelin protein with unchanged new axonal coverage supports a differentiation or morphology effect. If target engagement is absent at a measured free exposure, the mechanism may be relevant while fasudil is a poor probe. If recovery survives only when axons remain exposed or activity changes, test axonal recruitment before assigning a direct oligodendroglial action. Preserve patterned conduction and inhibitory recruitment. Vascular and anti-inflammatory effects should be assessed separately from function in an isolated developmental preparation.

**Next comparison.** Verify which compound generated the published oligodendrocyte and myelination endpoints, then compare parent and active metabolite ranges, timing and target engagement.
