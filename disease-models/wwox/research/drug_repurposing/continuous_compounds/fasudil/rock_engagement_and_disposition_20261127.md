# Fasudil, ROCK engagement and first-sheath formation in WWOX loss of function

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. The registered comparison asks whether neuronal WWOX loss leaves a reversible ROCK-dependent first-sheath bottleneck. The preparations include rodent oligodendrocyte precursor cells (OPCs), mouse tissue and human organoid bulk expression matrices, a mixed-species coculture, adult ALS lumbar CSF, and biochemical kinase assays. Results below carry their own molecule, readout and exposure scale.

**Decision.** Park fasudil for pediatric WWOX trial priority. Retain a stage-specific preclinical ROCK comparison. The strongest drug-specific OPC evidence concerns differentiation and myelin proteins. First compact sheath and patterned conduction at exposure-supported parent or active-metabolite concentrations remain to be shown. [Registered prediction](pretest_20261127.md)

## Species-matched exposure and target readout

In the ROCK-ALS adult trial, 120 were randomized; 118 received study medication and formed the reported efficacy and safety population. Lumbar puncture at day 26 was available for **67 across arms**. The body reports means of **5.8 ± 2.0** and **9.4 ± 4.5 µg/L** hydroxyfasudil in the two treated groups (mean ± SD). Parent fasudil was nearly undetectable in CSF. The PK analyses are exploratory, and the exact CSF sample count by exposure arm, assay LLOQ and within-day sampling times were not verified in the accessible body. Use 67 as the all-arm count only. Day-26 values are observations at one visit rather than a peak or a time-weighted exposure. [Adult RCT, Results pp. 1138, 1141](https://pub.dzne.de/record/272815/files/DZNE-2024-01233.pdf)

The resulting means are 18.87 and 30.58 nM hydroxyfasudil. In an independent cell-free kinase comparison the hydroxyfasudil IC₅₀s were 0.73 µM for ROCK1 and 0.72 µM for ROCK2. The assay values are **23.5–38.7 times** the adult group means on the same chemical-species scale. The higher group SD is 14.64 nM; it is between-sample spread and cannot bound individual peak or free neural exposure. This comparison quantifies a scale gap. It cannot estimate fractional inhibition in oligodendrocytes. The parent experiments stay on a separate scale. Nominal parent 5 µM in the O4 assay is 4.2 to 6.1 times the parent biochemical IC₅₀s of 1.2 and 0.82 µM. At 100 µM, off-target activity is plausible given the independently reported 5.3 µM parent PKA reference. [Kinase Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2633591/) · [Molecular mass](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/3064778) · [Replay](exposure.tsv)

A later 24-week open-label oral ALS trial had two sequential cohorts (31 each). Its abstract reports higher pAKT/total AKT in extracellular vesicles as an exploratory proxy. Without cell-specific ROCK substrate phosphorylation, a concurrent placebo arm for that endpoint, or confirmed WWOX neural exposure, this leaves direct developmental engagement open. Adult tolerability evidence has a different population, duration and outcome than a pediatric study of developmental myelination. [Oral study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42372734/)

## WWOX specificity and counterprediction

The held WWOX validation separated the approximate count of myelinated callosal profiles (55 versus 180 per field at P17) from unmyelinated counts in optic nerve. Callosal eligible axon density is required to estimate probability or first-sheath fraction. Fields were nested within three animals per genotype. The human organoid myelinated fraction and prior functional readouts motivate direct measurements in matched tracts or models. [Held WWOX denominator audit](../clemastine/axonal_denominator.md)

In the held public expression matrix replay, mutant minus WT values are log₂(1 + TPM) in mouse and log₂(1 + CPM) in human organoids. One-library omissions are sensitivity intervals. Mouse ROCK2 ranges remain below zero in neuronal cortex, neuronal hippocampus and global hippocampus, with a range that crosses zero in neural-lineage hippocampus. Human cerebral organoids give ROCK1 −0.422 (−0.543 to −0.358), ROCK2 −0.620 (−0.770 to −0.450) and PPP1R12A −0.739 (−0.836 to −0.642), with two control and four knockout libraries. Bulk RNA does not establish phosphotransfer or rule out a local effect. The decrease in total substrate RNA motivates using pMYPT1/total MYPT1 and a second substrate such as pMLC/total MLC in the relevant cell stage. ADGRG1 changes sign across WWOX models. [RNA replay and numbers](wwox_bulk_rock.tsv)

Two developmental comparisons justify a timing countercheck. Conditional mouse oligodendroglial RhoA loss accelerated myelination in the published abstract. In zebrafish with a different Gpr56 perturbation, the number of axons associated with pro-oligodendrocytes was increased early (30 hpf, p < 0.04) and lower later (46 hpf, p = 0.004, Fig. 5C). The later phenotype was associated with lower precursor proliferation. This is a temporal counterexample under a GPR56 perturbation, with broader signaling and a different species. It motivates distinguishing rapid maturation from depletion of the precursor pool. Neither perturbation establishes the direction of chronic fasudil treatment. [Mouse RhoA study, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39495111/) · [Zebrafish Gpr56 study, Fig. 5 and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4302765/)

**Discriminating comparison.** In organotypic or axon–oligodendrocyte preparations, compare early precursor expansion and later wrapping windows. Hold the starting axon caliber range and evoked recruitment comparable across genotypes and measure free parent and metabolite independently. Specify which cell compartment shows altered pMYPT1/total and pMLC/total before target attribution. A rise in the fraction of all eligible axons with a compact first sheath, preserved precursor reserve and better patterned conduction after washout at a supported measured exposure would favor targeted developmental recovery. More O4/MBP with stable sheath fraction, reduced precursor reserve later, or a response only with microglial debris or altered axonal activity supports a different route. An orthogonal ROCK1/2 control is needed for kinase attribution.

**Reopening criterion.** First establish a local ROCK activity phenotype and an exposure–substrate response in the same WWOX preparation. Advance only with compact first sheaths and conduction at preserved activity and precursor supply. Independent review would change decisions if it identifies a verified exposure-supported functional dose response or a source resolving the compound-specific low-concentration OPC discrepancy. The present results support a preclinical mechanism, with pediatric feasibility and benefit still unresolved.
