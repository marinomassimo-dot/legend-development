# Acipimox, cellular NAD renewal versus altered fatty-acid delivery

28 November 2026 UTC · Continuous compound #214 · INV-07 · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. Matrices include adult skeletal muscle and blood, and held WWOX neural expression and function. [Previous compound](../proglumide/README.md)

**Question.** Does acipimox increase new NAD in a WWOX-relevant neural cell at measured free parent, or does its functional phenotype follow extracellular fatty-acid delivery and timing? Both mechanisms could shift respiration, and total NAD can conceal altered renewal.

**Prespecified contrast.** Reconcile adult muscle crossover and longer comparisons by sampling interval and by contemporaneous nonesterified fatty acids. Check which papers measured NAD, which used mitochondrial proxies, and whether chemically traced acipimox-derived NAD was observed. For candidate transfer, pair measured parent and labelled NAD-pathway products with a known NAD-pathway reference in an age- and region-matched WWOX cell–glia preparation. Keep extracellular fatty acids and initial work matched. Pair the redox or respiratory readout with evoked recovery and cell-specific recruitment.

**Prediction.** A direct precursor mechanism should produce new NAD carrying verified parent-derived label before functional rescue at fixed substrate delivery. A response confined to changed fatty-acid delivery or late rebound, with no verified product at adequate parent exposure, favors supply or receptor signaling. Any apparent respiratory benefit driven by lower recruitment weakens the rescue interpretation.
