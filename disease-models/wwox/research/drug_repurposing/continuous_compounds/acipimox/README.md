# Acipimox, NAD pool renewal versus altered lipid supply

28 November 2026 UTC · Continuous compound #214 · INV-07 · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. Preparations are adult skeletal muscle and blood, C2C12 myotubes, rat tissue homogenate and plasma, human receptor assays and held WWOX mouse and organoid RNA. [Prespecified comparison](pretest.md) · [Opening screen](first_comparison.md)

**Decision.** Retain acipimox as a conditional comparator for HCAR2 and fatty-acid supply. Defer pediatric WWOX priority. A new neural NAD pool sourced from acipimox remains a separate hypothesis. The direct cellular NAD observation requires high nominal exposure, and a separate human study measured the tissue pool without detecting an increase.

## Which exposure and which mechanism?

**Parent.** Efthymiopoulos et al. measured mean adult total plasma Cmax **5.74 µg/mL** in ten volunteers after single oral exposure, approximately **37.24 µM** at 154.12 g/mol, with observed range **16.61–54.37 µM**. The nominal 10 mM cell exposure is approximately **269 times** the mean total plasma peak. The human paper also detected the 5-methylpyrazine-2-carboxylic acid N-deoxy metabolite. Average metabolite Cmax after repeated dosing was 0.48 µg/mL with wide variability. These are different study populations and matrices. Neither the exposure ratio nor the drug label establishes free brain parent or metabolite. [Human PK abstract, PMID 8356541](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8356541/) · [Identity and molar mass](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5310993)

**Rat brain.** Shen et al. (PMID 36234950, DOI 10.3390/molecules27196413) measured low parent in the brain homogenate of 7–8-week male rats after oral exposure. At matched 1 and 2 h points, Fig. 5 bars are approximately **0.20–0.38 µg/mL homogenate**. Fig. 4 shows materially different plasma curves in normoxia and hypoxia. Ratios of the graph read-offs are about **1.6–4.6%**, expressed only as homogenate-to-plasma signal quotients at matched times. Methods do not give a conversion from homogenate volume to tissue mass, a vascular correction, or free extracellular drug. For example, at 1 h normoxia a homogenate with roughly 4.6% plasma-equivalent carryover would produce that homogenate signal if carryover alone supplied it. That is an identifiability example with no claim about actual residual blood. The caption says n=6 and the described total is 12 rats across two groups, leaving tissue allocation across six sampling times unclear. Graphical uncertainty is material for these small bars. [Primary, Figs. 4–5 and Methods §§3.2–3.7](https://www.mdpi.com/1420-3049/27/19/6413)

**Receptor.** Wise et al. (PMID 12522134, DOI 10.1074/jbc.M210695200) showed acipimox activation of cloned human HM74A/HCAR2 via G-protein signaling in Fig. 4. Table II reports displacement IC50 **5.10 µM** in cloned-receptor membranes and **4.34 µM** in human adipocyte membranes, from 6 and 4 experiments respectively. These IC50s describe tracer displacement, not universal functional potency. Human subcutaneous adipose tissue strips also reduced stimulated fatty-acid output at nominal **10 µM** in a separate primary report. Receptor and adipose effects therefore have evidence on the low-micromolar scale. This establishes a competing mechanism for the clinical comparisons and warrants a subtype-specific functional reference when testing local neural work. [Wise et al., original PDF](https://www.zen-bio.com/pubs/Molecular%20Identification%20of%20High%20and%20Low%20Affinity.pdf) · [Adipose study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3964732/)

**Precursor test.** Acipimox has a substituted pyrazine N-oxide ring. Nicotinic acid in the Preiss–Handler pathway has a pyridine ring. The known N-deoxy metabolite retains the pyrazine. An increase in HPLC NAD+ cannot identify which atoms entered the canonical nicotinamide moiety, and simple deoxygenation does not make nicotinic acid. No primary report inspected demonstrated acipimox-derived canonical NAD labeling. Distinguish drug-derived synthesis, stimulation of synthesis from other precursors, slower consumption and changing NAD redox partition. A receptor-active reference and a separate validated tracer precursor are needed. [Clinical metabolite study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8356541/) · [Human nicotinate phosphoribosyltransferase](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17604275/) · [Primary NAD flux tracer study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29685734/)

## WWOX hypothesis and decision boundary

**Data.** The held P17 cortical and hippocampal Wwox-KO bulk libraries have lower Naprt, from 4.43 to 2.50 and 3.46 to 2.02 TPM, respectively. Week-15 WWOX organoid NAPRT drops from 39.23 to 30.68 CPM. These directions survive single-library omission, with 4/3, 2/3 and 2/4 control/KO libraries. NMNAT2 also decreases in each dataset. NADSYN1 is higher only in the mouse cortical omission check and mitochondrial NAD transporter SLC25A51 decreases reproducibly in the mouse regions, with a less stable human-organoid direction. HCAR2 expression is below 0.14 TPM or CPM in all bulk group means, with no captured assigned Hcar2 UMIs in the main excitatory, inhibitory, astrocyte and microglial pools. The pool data are sparse and cannot exclude an HCAR2-positive cell population or receptor activity. NAD pools, enzymatic capacity and pathway flux require separate measures. [Cortex](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) · [Hippocampus](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) · [Organoid](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243) · [Pooled nuclei](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158684)

**Hypothesis.** A true precursor response might stall at substrate entry, NAD synthesis or mitochondrial availability in WWOX. The same parent could produce a faster HCAR2-dependent response without making new NAD. Changes in peripheral fatty-acid delivery or compensatory rebound could change substrate demand without local receptor signaling. These explanations predict different tracer, timing and functional patterns.

**Discriminating comparison.** At matched extracellular fatty-acid supply, glucose and initial recruitment, compare acipimox with a measured native HCAR2 reference and an authentic NAD tracer control in a region- and age-matched WWOX neural preparation. Measure free parent and N-deoxy product alongside fractional production of canonical NAD, the NAD+/NADH pool, respiration while performing matched work, and evoked recovery. Chemically locate any parent-derived label in the canonical nicotinamide moiety, separate it from drug-derived analogs and nonspecific label recycling, and check that the authentic nicotinate control can enter the pathway. A rapid shared response without parent-derived NAD that follows verified HCAR2 engagement supports a receptor effect. A delayed response with parent-derived canonical NAD that requires productive synthesis and preserves initial recruitment supports the precursor explanation. NAD changes without incorporation trigger a turnover comparison using the established precursor tracer. Effects that disappear at matched extracellular fatty-acid supply favor the substrate-delivery explanation. Use biological donor or animal as the replication unit.

**Pediatric feasibility and next decision.** A historical acute endocrine study included 14 prepubertal children (mean age 8.2 ± 1.9 years) and measured growth hormone responses after exposure. That documents brief exposure and an endocrine endpoint, with no evidence here of juvenile CNS target exposure, sustained neurological tolerability, efficacy in WWOX or interactions with background antiseizure treatment. Olbetam is an oral capsule for specific lipid indications and the label highlights renal clearance and tolerability concerns. Require measured neural exposure with both a tracer and matched substrate or HCAR2 control before considering a translational comparison. A further review of the published concentrations cannot establish this missing measurement.
