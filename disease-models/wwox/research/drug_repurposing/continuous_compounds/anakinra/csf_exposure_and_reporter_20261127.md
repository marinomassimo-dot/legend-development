# Anakinra | CSF exposure, IL-1 response and physiological plasticity

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Decision.** Keep anakinra active for a defined preclinical comparison and defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. The WWOX RNA comparison favors testing a change in response to equal IL-1 input alongside extracellular ligand activity. A complete IL-1R8 loss model shows both rescue and impairment of plasticity with the same antagonist, depending on genotype. This adds a functional preservation criterion beyond reduced bursting. **Calibration inference.** The two month-three human CSF summaries equal **46–66 pM recombinant mass equivalents** if the full measured mass is converted using 17.3 kDa. These are mass-equivalent numbers for an immunoreactivity signal. Native isoforms, standard reactivity and antagonist activity are unresolved. The values are close to the lower fibroblast assay IC50 and lie far below the whole-blood assay IC50 before any dilution. Clarkson's FIRES reporter differed again. It used a fixed IL-1β challenge of 32 pg/mL with dialyzed CSF at 50% by volume in the index-case experiment. The published precedent supports calibrating matrix dilution, comparing recombinant spike response and reading functional antagonism separately from immunoreactivity. It supplies no universal threshold or WWOX clinical eligibility rule. ## What changes in the experiment

**Primary comparator.** Tomasoni et al. show that antagonism restores chemical-LTP-associated spine and mEPSC responses in DIV15–16 hippocampal neurons from a complete *Il1r8* knockout. Under the same treatment the normal WT potentiation is lost. The Figure 4 spine experiment uses 20 ng/mL nominal antagonist overnight and the mEPSC comparison uses 100 ng/mL. Figure 4E–F includes eight WT versus twelve KO treated neurons after LTP induction, with separate no-LTP arms. These are cell counts. The nominal concentrations are roughly 18–25 and 88–125 times the human CSF immunoreactivity medians above. The observation takes place in hippocampal culture after complete loss of a regulatory receptor and cannot predict the magnitude or sign in WWOX cortex. It gives an experimentally demonstrated cost of excessive pathway blockade in a control context. [Tomasoni et al., pp. 4, 6–8 and Fig. 4](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.21735)

**Circuit countercheck.** In the P21 cortical-impact model, Semple et al. report an effect on some provoked-seizure outcomes after early treatment. At later challenge, generalized provoked events were reported in 58% of vehicle-TBI mice versus 21% of antagonist-TBI mice (reported Fisher p=0.07), with an interaction on the composite severity score. The same study found no detected effect on late spontaneous EEG seizure incidence or the frequency contrast under seven days of monitoring (15–19 animals per injured arm). Separate spontaneous activity and provoked susceptibility even if biochemical engagement is observed. [Semple et al., Figs. 3, 6–7](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5559762/)

**Discriminating prediction.** Factor donor and recipient genotype using the same juvenile cortical preparation. Under common initial drive, use a calibrated IL-1R1 reporter to compare activity-conditioned extracellular material, and separately compare recipient cortical responses at the same verified IL-1 input. Confirm species-compatible ligand and antagonist responsiveness in matrix. A donor effect at fixed recipient would support higher net extracellular IL-1 bioactivity. A stronger KO recipient response to equal ligand would support altered sensitivity and motivate checking SIGIRR protein, IL-1R1 and IL-1RAP. Anakinra should reduce late pathological recurrence at measured local exposure while preserving early recruitment and a separately monitored physiological potentiation response. If reporter changes occur without network benefit, or if control potentiation deteriorates, the candidate loses priority. Keep released IL‑1α and IL‑1β and antagonist-sensitive bioactivity in distinct measurements. For an apparently negative medium result, check recovery and local tissue responsiveness before drawing a conclusion about short-range signaling.

**Next decision.** Check the exact-drug spontaneous-seizure comparators and whether an independent peer can validate the juvenile target readout using public assay or raw data. Favor a targeted check over adding more bulk transcript contrasts.
