# Anakinra | Spontaneous-seizure controls and disposition

**Decision.** Retain anakinra as a conditional preclinical IL-1R1 probe and defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. The autoimmune EEG result supports a context-specific pharmacological signal. Early treatment after juvenile cortical injury showed no detected effect on late spontaneous video-EEG seizures. None of these models establishes active IL-1R1 signaling or selective circuit rescue in WWOX. [WWOX RNA and exposure replay](csf_exposure_and_reporter_20261127.md)

**Pediatric feasibility.** The US Kineret label describes the subcutaneous prefilled formulation and distinct pediatric evidence in NOMID (36 children, including 13 under two) and DIRA (nine children, one month to nine years at initiation). Important constraints include infection, neutrophil monitoring, renal clearance and formulation limits in small children. The label reports no formal human drug-interaction studies. Concurrent antiseizure medicines and immunomodulation are major attribution issues in the observational FIRES setting. The available pediatric CSF measurements are immunoreactivity from an autoinflammatory setting. A free, active concentration in juvenile WWOX cortex and a relevant target-response endpoint are missing. [Kineret label, §§5, 7, 8.4, 8.6 and 12.3](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d9d74915-6606-4570-9c52-c4001d3177de) · [Exposure audit](csf_exposure_and_reporter_20261127.md) · [FIRES cohort](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7732241/)

## Discriminating comparison and condition to reopen

**Data and inference.** WWOX SIGIRR RNA was lower in the three examined contrasts, with changes in other components that could offset or reverse a functional response. A targeted public search found juvenile WWOX expression and electrophysiology and the separate adult partial-loss cerebellar cytokine RNA result. It identified no joint native juvenile measurement of extracellular IL-1R1 activity, recipient sensitivity and spontaneous cortical outcome. An exposure, dose or transcript comparison alone leaves this gap open. [WWOX replay](csf_exposure_and_reporter_20261127.md) · [Adult partial-loss model, Fig. 9d](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10835625/)

**Hypothesis and test.** With juvenile P13–17 neuron-specific Wwox-KO and littermate cortex, separate the extracellular input from the recipient response under matched stimulation. Use a fixed IL-1R1 reporter to compare local extracellular activity at comparable early recruitment, and a common verified IL-1 input to compare slice sensitivity across genotypes. Test whether anakinra reduces spontaneous or delayed recurrent discharges at verified active local exposure while preserving first recruitment and physiological potentiation. More activity in the fixed reporter with the KO donor supports excess extracellular drive. Greater response by KO recipients at equal IL-1 supports sensitivity. Verified target suppression with unchanged circuit activity, or lost potentiation, lowers priority. Reopen for a native activity or sensitivity difference that survives the matched-input controls. A review would change the decision if it uncovers a suitable paired WWOX dataset or corrects the differential-response interpretation. The uncertain PTZ denominator cannot change the disposition.
