# Topiramate, GluK1 and inhibitory recruitment

**Opening question.** A WWOX case already audited by the laboratory had seizure control on levetiracetam plus topiramate at last follow-up. Combination exposure and single-visit developmental status leave the individual drug effect unresolved. The acetazolamide dossier already covers carbonic anhydrase and bicarbonate. The present comparison asks whether a GluK1-dependent effect on inhibitory recruitment changes direction with the stimulation pattern in WWOX. The reported effects in rat basolateral amygdala motivate this question; circuit location, age and endogenous chloride determine whether it transfers. [Existing WWOX source audit, rows A1-32 and A1-33](../../batch_sci_20261105/verify/audit_A1/audit_A1_README.md) · [Rat recordings, PMID 19417176](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19417176/)

**Prespecified before extracting WWOX counts.** Look up Grik1 in excitatory and inhibitory mouse hippocampal nuclei and compare Grik2 and Grik5 as receptor subunit controls. Report absolute UMI counts and CPM and decompose the assigned-pool change into within-class and mixture terms. The two genotypes have one pool each. A preferential inhibitory signal would motivate separate spontaneous and evoked tests. Equal or opposite directions would reduce confidence in cell-specific target inference. None of these RNA results establish synaptic localization, channel activity or drug response.

**Discriminating prediction.** Compare WWOX and matched corrected networks during spontaneous activity and an identical afferent train at matched free drug concentration. Measure interneuron recruitment, evoked inhibitory charge onto principal neurons, survival of a physiological evoked response and burst duration. Verify the driving force for GABA in the cells examined. A reduction in discharges with preserved evoked inhibition and transmission is consistent with selective circuit benefit. Loss of feedforward inhibition or loss of the evoked signal would favor network suppression or disinhibition. A GluK1-selective comparator and matched pH/bicarbonate are needed to distinguish this route from sodium-channel and carbonic-anhydrase effects. Developmental improvement requires a separate durable endpoint.

**Pediatric feasibility.** An existing infant randomized video-EEG trial offers an age-relevant design and an important counterexample for extrapolation. Examine central-reader baseline seizure counts, treatment time, endpoint and bicarbonate outcomes before transferring an efficacy estimate to WWOX. [Original randomized trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20089937/)

**WWOX class check.** Inhibitory-class Grik1 was 1,878 to 1,444 CPM (3,102 and 2,709 UMIs); excitatory-class Grik1 was 71.0 to 67.5 (879 and 698 UMIs). Thus the inhibitory-to-excitatory RNA ratio was about 26 in controls and 21 in knockout. Across all assigned nuclei, Grik1 fell by 36.3 CPM: the within-class contribution was −79.8 and the difference in class weights contributed +43.5. Grik2 changed by −15.9 from a higher baseline of 1,510.5 CPM and Grik5 by +13.7 from 155.6. The result supports measuring inhibitory recruitment. Lower target abundance could also reduce drug sensitivity. It cannot determine whether interneuron recruitment or release from surviving terminals dominates, especially when transferring from hippocampal nuclei to amygdala physiology. **Age-relevant clinical control.** The 20-day adjunctive trial enrolled 149 infants aged 1–24 months with refractory focal seizures; 130 had evaluable baseline and final video-EEG for the primary analysis. The top-dose group had a median 20.4% reduction in daily focal seizure rate versus 13.1% with placebo; the prespecified rank-based comparison gave p=0.97. The 7.3 percentage point subtraction is descriptive, not the adjusted treatment effect. Severe drop in serum total CO₂ was recorded in 7 treated infants versus none on placebo using the authors' composite laboratory definition. Mean serum CO₂ change was −5.15 mmol/L in the highest arm and +0.72 on placebo. The efficacy and CO₂ findings make acid–base status a concrete competing explanation for a physiological drug response. The trial was not WWOX-selected and did not measure GluK1 currents or developmental benefit. [Novotny et al., Efficacy, Fig. 2 legend and Safety](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2836871/)

**Endpoint that could miss the mechanism.** The central read required a clinical manifestation with qualifying focal or asymmetric EEG evolution lasting at least ten seconds. An isolated change in interictal activity or in a brief spasm is a different endpoint. Some infants had no qualifying baseline events on the central read, and a worst rank was assigned if events subsequently appeared. Preserve baseline event eligibility and model spontaneous versus evoked inhibitory effects separately.
