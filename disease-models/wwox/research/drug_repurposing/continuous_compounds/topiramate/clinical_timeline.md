# What can the published WWOX treatment histories attribute to topiramate?

**Four informative narratives.** In AlBaradie et al., patient 1.2 had already experienced resolution of spasms at age five. Topiramate was documented with phenobarbitone and lamotrigine at age ten, when other seizure types were recorded. Earlier topiramate exposure cannot be dated from that narrative. Patient 3.1 had refractory seizures despite several drugs including topiramate; the text attributes a subsequent response to the ketogenic diet. Patient 4 had myoclonic and focal events and a history of multiple agents, with control reported on levetiracetam plus topiramate at last review. The narrative provides no isolated add-on or withdrawal estimate. Patient 5 was taking three drugs including topiramate when vigabatrin was added for spasms. The subsequent developmental status cannot be attributed to an individual agent. The four reports are selected exposure histories and provide no responder-rate denominator. [AlBaradie et al., case narratives 1.2, 3.1, 4 and 5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35792847/) · [Earlier laboratory audit](../../batch_sci_20261105/verify/audit_A1/audit_A1_README.md)
