# Developmental comparators for spermidine in WWOX loss of function

**Data scope.** Primary mouse neuron and hippocampal field measurements, two uncontrolled pediatric GRIN2B observations, patient-derived lymphoblastoid assays, and larval zebrafish morphology and optic tectal recordings. These are distinct systems and endpoints.

## GRIN2B, receptor and organism-level evidence

- In the Fig. 1C cell comparison, nominal **200 µM spermine** was used with glycine-driven chemical LTP in transfected primary mouse hippocampal neurons. The mean peak total plasma spermine during the active adult extract crossover was 9.45 ng/mL, or 0.04670 µM at a spermine free-base molecular weight of 202.34 g/mol. The ratio of these quantities is about 4,282. This is a scale comparison across preparations and assays. Neither quantity is a free WWOX brain concentration. [Santos-Gómez et al., §§2.5–2.9, 3.2 and Fig. 1 legend](https://doi.org/10.1002/jimd.70015) · [Senekowitsch et al., Table 1](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu15081852) · [Spermine molecular weight](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/1103)
- In Fig. 2D, post-weaning mouse **spermidine** exposure was associated with a stable-phase fEPSP slope of 156.1 at 60–100 minutes in Grin2b heterozygotes, compared with 140.4 in untreated heterozygotes and 176.3 in untreated WT (normalized slope, N=4–5 mice and 6–7 recordings per group). The treated-minus-untreated point contrast is 15.7 points, or 43.7% of the 35.9-point WT gap. The reported comparisons are against WT and the LTP result lacks WT plus spermidine. These summaries cannot establish the treatment-by-genotype interaction or the effect on inhibition. The reported plasticity outcome cannot determine seizure direction. [Santos-Gómez et al., Results §3.3 and Fig. 2D legend](https://doi.org/10.1002/jimd.70015)
- The two pediatric observations used **wheat-germ extract** after prior L-serine ceased. Both entered without clinical seizures, the adaptive assessments were uncontrolled, and central parent and metabolite concentrations were unmeasured. This is formulation-specific pediatric precedent. The comparator began from GRIN2B hypofunction. Held WWOX transcript co-detection provides a rationale to stratify by cell class, with receptor assembly and the direction of functional change unresolved. [Santos-Gómez et al., Results §3.4, Table 1 and Fig. 3 legend](https://doi.org/10.1002/jimd.70015) · [Held WWOX subunit co-detection](../../memantine_magnesium_20261123/README.md)

## Reduced enzyme capacity despite substrate availability

- DHPS deficiency lymphoblastoid lines showed altered DHPS protein and reduced eIF5A hypusination, with no detected change in DHPS transcript or measured intracellular polyamine pool. This makes substrate abundance and modified fraction separate readouts. The study does not resolve whether WWOX tissue has a similar constraint. [Padgett et al., Fig. 3 and Supplementary Fig. S1](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xhgg.2023.100206)
- In a DHPS zebrafish morpholino model, the DHPS mRNA add-back shifted normal morphology from 34% of 371 to 49% of 179 at 1 day post-fertilization (+15 percentage points). The separate optic tectal recordings at 4 days found means of 0.25, 0.68 and 3.74 ictal-like events per 20 minutes for uninjected (n=20), control-oligo (n=22) and DHPS knockdown (n=23), respectively. The last mean is 5.50 times that of the control oligo. The morphology add-back did not test electrical rescue or rescue of inhibitory arborization, and was a genetic restoration. Use separately timed and normalized measures for morphology, absolute inhibitory output and network activity. [Shojaeinia et al., Methods, Results and Fig. 3–4 legends](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13041-024-01139-w)
