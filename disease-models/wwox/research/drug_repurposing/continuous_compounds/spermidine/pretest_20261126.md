# Spermidine and eIF5A hypusination, prespecified test

**Data scope.** TPM in juvenile mouse neuronal-deletion cortex and hippocampus, library-normalized CPM in week-15 human cerebral organoids, and nuclear UMI CPM in assigned P17 mouse hippocampal cell classes. All WWOX datasets are untreated.

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. The question links polyamine routing and eIF5A hypusination to WWOX mitochondrial and lysosomal recovery. Aged animals and adult oral pharmacokinetics are comparator systems. Human WWOX genotype, stage and formulation must be stated independently.

**Molecule.** Chemically defined spermidine free base, using free-base equivalents for verified salts. Quantify intact spermidine, spermine and putrescine. Food extracts with other polyamines cannot isolate the molecule. Spermine is a matched metabolic and circuit comparator.

**Fixed expression panel.** ODC1, AMD1, SRM and SMS for synthesis and onward conversion. SAT1, PAOX and SMOX for back-conversion and oxidative products. ATP13A2 and ATP13A3 for transport context. DHPS, DOHH and EIF5A for the hypusination pathway. ATG3 and SQSTM1 for context of LC3 lipidation and cargo turnover. Reuse the held bulk log2(1+abundance) contrast and single-library omissions. Use assigned nuclear counts with at least 20 UMI in each pool as the descriptive within-class coverage flag. Keep raw denominators. RNA cannot establish polyamine abundance, enzyme activity or pathway direction.

**Predicted ordering.** If the relevant deficit is spermidine availability with residual DHPS and DOHH activity, a measured intracellular spermidine increment should precede incorporation of its labeled aminobutyl moiety into hypusinated eIF5A, then increased completed native cargo turnover and mitochondrial recovery under matched stimulation. Compare viable WWOX and matched control cells. Isolate changes in hypusinated/total eIF5A from global cell multiplication and ATP and do not require an RNA deficiency as a premise. A matched partial change in DHPS or DOHH activity should alter the treatment contrast with its baseline and toxicity measured. Assay the acute circuit response separately from post-washout recovery.

**Rivals and decision rule.** Conversion to spermine, oxidative back-conversion toward spermidine, direct channel effects, or generic stress can account for downstream measurements. Track parent and metabolite labeling separately and compare with spermine at matched measured local exposure. A response recreated by spermine without the predicted eIF5A timing, or worsened evoked inhibition despite more autofluorescent puncta or lipidated LC3, lowers priority. Progress requires completed turnover and preferential neurophysiological recovery, with a verified pediatric exposure and formulation argument. First-pass acquisition stops at a quantitative oral parent/metabolite comparison and a route-specific genetic comparator.
