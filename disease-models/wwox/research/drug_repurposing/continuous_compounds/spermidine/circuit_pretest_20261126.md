# Spermidine follow-up, developmental and extracellular comparators

**Data scope.** Held untreated WWOX juvenile mouse cortex and hippocampus TPM, human week-15 cerebral organoid CPM, and assigned nuclei UMI CPM. External astrocyte-conditioned medium, adult mouse plasma/CSF and cortex polyamine measurements, excitatory puncta (published source counts per image), and juvenile or young-adult field responses use separate units and preparations.

**New question.** In addition to acute spermine receptor action, extracellular spermidine may change excitatory synaptogenesis while a different intracellular pool supports hypusination. Test whether ATP13A4 is lower in the held WWOX astrocyte pool with adequate coverage, whether altered extracellular spermidine tracks excitatory contacts in source data, and whether the effect occurs at a concentration scale that moves the original comparison. This is a targeted, newly prompted add-on to the locked 14-gene panel.

**Reanalysis before source-data extraction.** Compare direct spermidine with its vehicle and with measured conditioned-medium concentration. Preserve control/knockdown or WT/KO in each compartment and avoid a tissue/plasma ratio across unmatched animals. Summarize independent biological preparations if IDs are present. Otherwise report pooled cell means as descriptive, with no independent-cell p value. Compare marker puncta and functional currents separately. Independently check whether the GRIN2B study crosses genotype and treatment for the specific plasticity readout. A treated-versus-control genotype comparison and a treated-versus-untreated contrast answer different questions.

**Discriminating result.** At measured intact and local exposure, classify a preclinical result by (i) an immediate voltage-, pH- or subunit-sensitive effect, (ii) early increase in absolute and fractional neuronal eIF5A hypusination followed by improved inhibitory recruitment after washout, and (iii) rising extracellular parent with delayed excitatory contact formation. The third mechanism can coexist with the second. Cross uptake and exposure in astrocytes, preserve absolute neuron and interneuron numbers, separate inhibitory dendritic complexity from inhibitory charge, and pair the two functional axes before further prioritization.
