# D-β-hydroxybutyrate, substrate supply and M-current

**Question.** Does adding the endogenous D enantiomer increase the neuronal KCNQ2/3 M-current at the background ketone exposure already present in WWOX? The alternative is that any functional gain follows oxidation as a fuel, differences in cell type or broader effects of ketogenic nutrition. This refers to one defined molecule. The WWOX cohort documents exposure to a ketogenic diet, which changes multiple metabolites; its outcome cannot be attributed to exogenous D-BHB. [Held dietary ascertainment](../triheptanoin/comparators.md)

**Discriminating prediction.** An acute channel mechanism should require a measurable increment in native M-current above physiological baseline, remain evident before meaningful new carbon oxidation, and depend on functional KCNQ2/3 at matched pH, extracellular salts and voltage. Compare WWOX and corrected neurons, and track recruitment of inhibitory as well as excitatory cells. A metabolic explanation predicts a relation to labelled ketone oxidation and energetic recovery during demand. If the channel effect saturates at ordinary extracellular exposure, a large additional effect from raising systemic ketones becomes difficult to explain by direct KCNQ2/3 activation. Predefine sensitivity to baseline concentration, measured compartment, Hill slope, stereochemical identity and culture-medium ketone content. [Channel comparator](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31757819/) · [Human brain MRS comparator](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11043913/)

**First result.** A positive BHB response from a zero-ketone bath can greatly exceed the predicted increment above baseline. The cited human fasting measurements were in adults. [Quantitative comparison and tests](baseline.md)
