**Disposition.** Park racemic baclofen for pediatric WWOX trial priority. Retain the GABA-B/glial assay hypothesis and the acute circuit counterexample. ## Circuit and developmental counterchecks

- **Presynaptic inhibition at a lower nominal concentration.** In P5–7 rat CA3 pyramidal neurons, **0.3 µM** nominal baclofen left **83.0 ± 5.5%** of control evoked inhibitory current (n=3), about a 17-percentage-point mean depression. The authors' quoted p-value compares neonates with the older group. The indexed figure caption describes concentrations down to 0.1 µM. Its exact 0.1 µM effect and reagent chirality could not be verified. The eIPSC assay cannot determine the simultaneous effect on excitatory input or the sign of GABA in an unperturbed WWOX cell. [Caillard Fig. 2C and targeted results](https://doi.org/10.1152/jn.1998.79.3.1341)
- **Recipient and readout matter.** In a separate P16–18 rat CA3 experiment, at **20 µM** nominal baclofen, evoked EPSC and IPSC onto interneurons remained **51.0 ± 4.6%** (n=14) and **20.5 ± 3.9%** (n=8) of control, a descriptive difference of **30.5 percentage points**. Half-maximal response concentrations were **8.5** and **1.7 µM**, respectively. The authors note that ambient GABA could shift the inhibitory response. The postsynaptic holding-current response in this recipient class was undetectable at P16–18 in their separate comparison and was present later. Reduced input onto interneurons has ambiguous net circuit sign. The previously held reticular-thalamic circuit worsened spike-wave discharges after baclofen, in a model distinct from WOREE seizure forms. [Lei and McBain Methods, Results and Fig. 1C–E and 2 captions](https://doi.org/10.1113/jphysiol.2002.034017) · [Reticular-thalamic primary Fig. 4](https://doi.org/10.1093/braincomms/fcac010)
- **Human task dissociation.** In the healthy-adult crossover, random-block reaction times showed little evidence of a change (paired p=0.875). Across learnable blocks the drug term was **F(1,13)=6.070, p=0.029**, equivalent to partial eta squared **0.318** by its test statistic. The drug-by-block interaction was **p=0.373**. This supports a task-dependent performance concern with uncertainty about learning slope. Non-significant alertness change (p=0.061) does not rule out a contribution from alertness. There was no chiral CSF sampling or WWOX endpoint. [Grigoras §§3.1, 3.4 and Fig. 2](https://doi.org/10.1162/IMAG.a.979)

Fang et al. found about **60% less MOG-positive sheath volume relative to PV axon volume** in the mPFC after conditional OPC Gabbr1 loss (6 control and 4 conditional-KO mice). The effect on all-axon MOG and corpus callosum compound conduction was undetectable in the reported comparisons. PV-positive cell density increased, physiological inhibition was impaired and developmental survival was linked to TWEAK signaling. This supplies a target-cell-specific causal comparator, without establishing an agonist reversal. In held WWOX tissue, PVALB falls **90.88→60.37 TPM** in cortex and **22.18→6.48 TPM** in hippocampus, and both directions survive every single-library omission. Week-15 human organoid PVALB is low (**0.18→0.44 CPM**) and shifts the other way. In WWOX mouse hippocampal inhibitory pools there are **30→10 PVALB UMIs**, with **246→293** assigned inhibitory nuclei. OPC TNFSF12 is unobserved in the two pools and inhibitory TNFRSF12A is sparse (**2→6 UMIs**). These pooled measurements do not identify cell loss, incomplete maturation or a WWOX TWEAK mechanism. They make a viable-cell denominator essential. [Fang Results and Fig. 2E](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-29020-1) · [WWOX bulk data](pv_tweak_bulk.tsv) · [WWOX nuclear data](pv_tweak_nuclei.tsv)

## Reopening criterion and pediatric boundary

At measured free R and S exposure in stage-matched WWOX and corrected preparations, a useful response requires more first sheaths **per viable PV axon**, with stable absolute axon/cell numbers and improved train-evoked inhibitory function **after washout**. Loss of poorly myelinated axons could artificially raise that ratio. Match activity and use glial GABA-B dependence to distinguish direct glial signaling from an indirect consequence of reduced firing. Acute presynaptic effects on inhibitory and excitatory input and delayed synchrony should be evaluated separately. Evidence of more sheaths with a drop in absolute inhibitory output or a rise in rebound discharges would oppose advancement.

Pediatric oral PK data come from cerebral palsy cohorts. In WOREE, one existing report describes poor tone response to baclofen and has no attributable seizure or myelin comparison. Oral labeling describes seizure-control deterioration, additive sedation with other CNS depressants, and serious withdrawal effects. Intrathecal logistics and regional gradients make it a distinct exposure question. Given the chiral gap and functional counterexamples, an independent review would change priority if it finds a reproducible low-exposure first-sheath and conduction result or measured age-appropriate target exposure with favorable circuit selectivity. Paper interpretation alone cannot establish those conditions. [Pediatric oral PK](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24607242/) · [WOREE report](https://doi.org/10.3389/fped.2022.847549) · [Oral label §§5.1, 5.3, 5.7](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=231ef477-822c-46fe-9bf3-d55cdf9bd4c2)
