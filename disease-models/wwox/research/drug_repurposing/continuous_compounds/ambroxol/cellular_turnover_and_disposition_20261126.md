**Disposition.** Defer ambroxol for pediatric WWOX trial priority. Keep it as a comparator for lysosomal transport, pH and GCase activity. Human CSF measurements support exposure in another indication. The controlled cell experiments separate enzyme activity, enzyme processing and lysosomal/autophagic outcomes. They leave a selective WWOX response and developmentally credible intracellular exposure unresolved. An existing WWOX chemical data set or a matched low-exposure neural study would change the disposition. For the fibroblasts the independent repeat count and the number of donor lines are different denominators. ## Bafilomycin challenge separates two types of evidence

Mouse cortex Fig. 2A upper panel includes vehicle and drug conditions with and without bafilomycin. The approximate bar tops are **98 → 220**, **195 → 265** and **203 → 199%** of unchallenged vehicle for 0, 10 and 30 µmol/L ambroxol. The digitized bafilomycin increments are **+122**, **+69** and **−4 points** of that common baseline. The 30 µmol/L shift is within reading tolerance of zero. The authors report an attenuated within-condition bafilomycin increment with ambroxol and an increase in basal LC3B-II. The comparison favors impaired turnover in this preparation. It neither estimates a flux rate nor distinguishes failure of clearance from a change in assay dynamic range or substrate supply by itself.

Human Fig. 7I shows **1.32 → 3.53** in actin-normalized LC3B-II signal for N370S/WT after bafilomycin in cells already exposed to 60 µmol/L ambroxol. The other two ambroxol-exposed groups also retain positive increments. Panel 7I contains no untreated arm at the same time point and on the same normalization. It demonstrates a bafilomycin response during treatment. It cannot establish that ambroxol increased turnover relative to vehicle. Species, donor genotype, culture age, exposure and challenge duration differ. The two figures jointly require full contemporaneous treatment-by-bafilomycin contrasts, tracked viability and endogenous cargo, and do not establish a contradiction under matched conditions. [Mouse Fig. 2A](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-19479-8) · [Human Fig. 7G–J](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stemcr.2017.01.011)

## WWOX decision and reopening condition

Even the 10 µmol/L bath associated with greater GCase activity in primary mouse cortex is about **12–91 times** the sampled CSF peaks in the two pediatric Gaucher cases. Bath and CSF are different compartments with unknown free concentrations and organellar partition. This comparison alone leaves a low-exposure CNS action open. Sparse pediatric Gaucher exposure with background disease and antiseizure treatment does not establish chronic co-medication safety for WWOX.

**Discriminating prediction.** In stage-matched WWOX and corrected neural cells, test baseline GCase distribution and native glucosylceramide and glucosylsphingosine with matched cell composition. At measured attained exposure and pH, a selective GCase-delivery response should persist after chemical verification of drug removal and increase the mature lysosomal fraction, improve native substrate turnover and preserve evoked recruitment. If total synthetic-substrate activity rises but the mature fraction fails to increase, endogenous lipids fail to clear or the bafilomycin increment contracts at matched viability, stop prioritizing ambroxol as GCase rescue. An independent review would change the decision if it brings lower-exposure neural turnover data or a WWOX lysosomal substrate measurement.
