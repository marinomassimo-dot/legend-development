# Ambroxol · CSF exposure, assay pH and lysosomal target engagement

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · parent ambroxol in serum and CSF (µmol/L or ng/mL), GlcSph in CSF (pg/mL), GCase activity in acellular adult CSF and purified enzyme in fixed-pH buffer

## Clinical exposure and activity refer to different compartments

Narita et al. Table 1 and Table 3 give a **0.11 µmol/L** sampled CSF peak for the 3-year-old and **0.83 µmol/L** for the 15-year-old. These participants have different GBA1 genotypes and other treatments. The five-person open pilot used nonuniform outcome windows and prior or concurrent Gaucher treatment. The table's trough CSF concentrations are calculated by multiplying the serum trough by the reported peak ratio. They are not independent trough samples. The youngest case's peak and CSF GlcSph are marked month 12 in the table footnote. [Original Tables 1 and 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4774255/)

The mean of the five individual CSF GlcSph reductions is **25.7%**, using `mean((baseline - post)/baseline)`. The relative decline of the summed concentrations is about **18.6%**, and the 3-year-old contributes about **63%** of the summed absolute decrease because she had the largest baseline. All measured post-exposure concentrations remain above the study's control quantification limit. The ordering is not a monotonic relationship with sampled CSF peaks and cannot identify a response threshold. Sampled CSF concentration is not a neuronal lysosomal measurement. The study reports a peripheral lymphocyte GCase response and a CSF substrate association, with disease, concurrent therapy and follow-up as alternatives to a direct CNS causal effect. [Individual source values for replay](gaucher_exposure.tsv) · [Biochemical results](https://doi.org/10.1002/acn3.292)

Mullin et al. Table 2 shows adult end-of-study serum **1,432 ng/mL** and CSF **156 ng/mL**, approximately **0.41 µmol/L** in CSF as ambroxol free-base equivalent. The 17-participant CSF means show activity **0.309 → 0.250 nmol/mL/h** and protein **250 → 338 pmol/L**. The ratio of group mean activity to group mean protein falls to **0.60** of baseline. This is a descriptive activity-per-protein contrast from summary means and cannot recover within-person covariance. CSF glucosylceramide means were **246 → 260 pmol/L**, with no significant decrease reported. The same study added **0.50 µmol/L** ambroxol to acellular human CSF and observed mean GCase activity suppression of **42%**, with five independent sample donors. This is a direct assay counterexample to interpreting lower extracellular activity as less cellular delivery or more protein as improved lipid turnover. [Adult open-label trial, Table 2 and in vitro Results](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2019.4611)

In Supplement 2, eMethods 3, **0.50 µmol/L** is the nominal spike during preincubation in CSF. The standard CSF activity assay uses 20 µL sample with 40 µL substrate buffer formulated at pH 5.9. If the spiked assay uses that mix, the nominal inhibitor in the catalytic well is about **0.167 µmol/L** and synthetic substrate about **3.33 µmol/L** before considering binding. The final reaction pH, unbound inhibitor and the time dependence of any preincubation effect were not measured in the read passages. Do not transfer the 42% change into an intact lysosome calculation. [Supplement 2, eMethods 3](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC6990847.1/jamaneurol-77-427-s002.pdf)

## A common exposure scale and different enzymatic conditions

Maegawa et al. used recombinant GCase and a synthetic glucoside rather than native membrane substrate. For 0.8 mM substrate, ambroxol's IC50 shifts from about **810 µmol/L at pH 4.7** to **31 µmol/L at pH 5.6** and **3.7 µmol/L at pH 6.7**. In Table 1 the pH 7.0 mixed-model parameters are `Ki = 5.2 µmol/L` and `alpha = 10`. The pH 5.6 parameters are `23 µmol/L` and `7`. For 0.50 µmol/L freely available inhibitor in the corresponding buffer, the limiting low- and high-substrate inhibitions in that model are `[I]/([I]+Ki)` and `[I]/([I]+alpha*Ki)`. The central parameter estimates give **0.95–8.8% inhibition at pH 7.0** and **0.31–2.13% at pH 5.6**. These are algebraic substrate limits under a specific assay model with fixed parameters, without uncertainty propagation. The human cell-free CSF result is larger under its assay conditions. The difference means buffer-derived potencies and an intact-child cellular occupancy estimate need separate calibration. It does not establish that pH alone caused the observed difference. [Maegawa et al., Fig. 2 and Table 1](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M109.012393) · [Acellular CSF experiment](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2019.4611)

In the same biochemical paper, the effective nominal range for augmenting residual activity in the N370S/N370S fibroblast assay was **5–60 µmol/L** (Fig. 3A and Results). The lower end is about **6–45 times** the sampled CSF peaks in the two pediatric Gaucher cases. This comparison leaves free concentrations, organellar partitioning, preparation and functional target engagement open. It cannot establish either a clinical minimum concentration or absence of a cellular effect at the lower sample concentrations. The matched WWOX test should measure intracellular drug and pH, repeat the activity assay with and without washout and quantify endogenous substrate in the same preparation. [Fibroblast study](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M109.012393) · [Pediatric sample source](https://doi.org/10.1002/acn3.292)

**Next comparison.** Use CSF GlcSph as a primary disease-specific comparator and separately verify whether a matched WWOX preparation shows an endogenous lysosomal substrate defect. If compartmental GCase protein is low but total protein is preserved, compare delivery with local enzyme activity under fixed and native pH. A sustained increase in mature lysosomal GCase and reduction of substrate after washout would support an enzyme-handling effect, provided that evoked recruitment is preserved. An apparent activity fall disappearing with removal of drug, with unchanged total lipid turnover, supports acute interference. A parallel shift in other hydrolases after a measured pH shift supports a wider lysosomal effect. Clinical priority requires attributable rescue and a realistic central exposure in the relevant developmental model. [WWOX data and test](README.md)
