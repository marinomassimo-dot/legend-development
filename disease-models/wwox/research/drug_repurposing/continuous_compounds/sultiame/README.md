# Sultiame · neural acid handling, bicarbonate and exposure

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

## Why this compound

Sultiame extends the held acetazolamide comparison with a human red-cell distribution measurement and a randomized infantile-spasm precedent. In assigned excitatory nuclei from juvenile WWOX mouse hippocampus, Car7 was **20.85 → 25.71 CPM** and Car2 was **15.27 → 11.02 CPM**. These are pooled samples and cannot estimate between-animal variability. The opposing changes support measuring how repeated GABA responses behave in this preparation. Bulk RNA does not establish intracellular bicarbonate regeneration or GABA polarity. The held acetazolamide study of electrically abnormal tissue also separates minutes spent in long discharges from their initiation rate. [WWOX and prior circuit comparison](../acetazolamide/burden.md)

## Exposure and the observed endpoints

In Dao et al., the same four healthy male adults received ascending single oral exposures in fixed order, separated by four to six weeks. Geometric-mean plasma Cmax values were **0.02, 0.18 and 1.88 µg/mL**. Whole-blood Cmax values were **7.91, 15.87 and 17.19 µg/mL**. At the highest dose level in the study, the plasma peak is **6.47 µM** using 290.4 g/mol and the whole-blood peak is **59.19 µM**. The peaks occurred at different times. The paper also reports erythrocyte measurements and a saturable-binding model. At the lowest exposure, the plasma peak is close to the **0.01 µg/mL** quantification limit. This small fixed-order adult study cannot establish infant CNS exposure. [Dao et al., Methods, Results §3.2 and Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6986439/) · [Molecular mass](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5356) · [Calculation](exposure.py) · [Results](exposure.tsv)

Leniger et al.'s nominal **1.0–1.5 mM** sultiame bath decreased somatic intracellular pH by **0.18 ± 0.05 units** within ten minutes in the majority of the CA3 neurons studied (n = 8). Their **1.0–2.5 mM** bath reduced action-potential and burst frequency in the 4-aminopyridine preparation. The lowest bath concentration reported for these effects is about **154 times** the highest geometric-mean total adult plasma peak in Dao et al. The ratio neither estimates unbound brain exposure nor defines the minimum effective bath concentration. In a separate crossover comparison of 11 healthy adults, oral sultiame raised resting motor threshold without a reported change in other TMS measures. Biochemical inhibition of mammalian CA II, VII, IX and XII spans **6–56 nM** in a separate assay. Together these are reasons to test an exposure-bounded neuronal effect while treating the millimolar acidification experiment as a mechanistic reference. The TMS result does not assign the mechanism or provide a concentration bridge. [Slice study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12027906/) · [TMS study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16930943/) · [Enzyme study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17588751/)

## Pediatric comparator and discriminating result

The original West syndrome trial counted **6/20 versus 0/17** complete electroclinical responses by day 9 on shared pyridoxine background. The absolute difference was **30 percentage points**, and the exact two-sided Fisher calculation from those counts gives **P = 0.022**. It was a small, short study in newly diagnosed infants and provides no WWOX-specific, chronic, or independent background-treatment estimate. In a separate retrospective monitoring study, lower blood pH was associated with higher measured sultiame exposure. The study reported a **3.5-fold higher dose- and weight-normalized N-desmethylclobazam concentration** with sultiame than with neutral comedication. Blood pH and background metabolite exposure are therefore competing explanations to measure and hold apart from neural mechanism. [Debus and Kurlemann primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14738417/) · [Norwegian monitoring study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38158595/)

**Prediction.** In age-matched WWOX and corrected preparations, preserve native chloride and monitor somatic intracellular pH. Hold extracellular pH, CO₂, and initial activity comparable. Compare the drug with an independent perturbation matched to its intracellular acidification. A selective reduction of late GABA-associated depolarization and time in long bursts, beyond the pH-matched control with preserved first responses, would support a contribution from bicarbonate supply. An equal effect of the pH-matched control would support general acidification. Suppression of the first evoked spike or propagation without a late-specific effect would prioritize intrinsic excitability as the alternative. Record initiation rate, duration and physiological evoked activity together.

**Active appraisal.** The measured adult repeat-dose troughs also remain well below the reported millimolar slice bath. The published CSF CA comparison used untreated samples. Continue with measurements of sultiame itself in pediatric CSF or another local compartment, and with neural concentration–response data below millimolar bath levels. If human exposure and cellular effects remain far apart, retain this comparison as a mechanistic probe and require a local-exposure result before trial prioritization.
