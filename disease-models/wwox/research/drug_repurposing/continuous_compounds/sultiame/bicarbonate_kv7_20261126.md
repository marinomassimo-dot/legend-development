# Sultiame · bicarbonate and the Kv7 alternative

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

## Measured physiological comparator

Jones et al. varied the nominal bicarbonate content of whole-cell pipette solutions in mouse CA3 pyramidal neurons. They verified pipette pH at **7.30–7.35**, used a bicarbonate-free external bath for the intracellular titration, and blocked fast synaptic transmission. At nominal **26 → 13 mM** pipette bicarbonate, median spikes per ramp assigned by waveform to the axon initial segment fell from **13 to 4** (−69%, reported P = 0.036). Somatically assigned spikes went from **22 to 19** (−14%, reported P = 0.788). These are descriptive ratios of separately reported medians. At nominal **26 → 0 mM**, the magnitude of the medium afterhyperpolarization area increased from **0.68 to 1.12 mV·s** (+65%, reported P < 0.0001). Table 2 also reports a change in fast afterhyperpolarization, so the scope of the result is the specified ramp and medium afterhyperpolarization effects. [Jones et al., Methods, Figs. 1–4 and Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3960478/) · [Calculation](bicarbonate_kv7.py) · [Results](bicarbonate_kv7.tsv)

In the separate bath wash-in experiment, mean spikes per ramp changed **17 → 53** without the Kv7 antagonist (n = 6) and **49 → 40** with it (n = 5). The two arms started at different firing rates. The within-arm changes give a pharmacological occlusion signal and do not on their own identify an interaction at equal excitability. The authors also report identical median ramp counts of 14 with strongly buffered nominal intracellular pH **7.0 and 7.6** in a bicarbonate-free comparison. This helps distinguish the bicarbonate result in that assay while leaving local pH transients unmeasured in the principal comparison. Waveform-based origin assignment is an inference. The recipe called the main stock “26 mM” while listing 15 mM KHCO₃ and subsequent CO₂ equilibration. Treat the nominal conditions as group labels, since actual neuronal bicarbonate was not quantified. [Jones et al., Methods, Results and Fig. 7](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3960478/)

## What changes in the WWOX comparison

The old sodium-versus-GABA comparison omitted a route in which **intracellular bicarbonate changes intrinsic excitability through Kv7**. The held WWOX datasets show lower KCNQ2/3 RNA in three bulk profiles and higher Kcnq2/3 CPM in the assigned excitatory and inhibitory hippocampal nuclei. This disagreement precludes assuming that the Kv7 reserve is reduced or increased. The held conductance analysis specifies a depolarized recording potential for a Kv7-sensitive difference current. A negative test near rest supplies little information about the spiking-range contribution. [WWOX bulk and nuclear comparison](../myo_inositol/README.md) · [Held WWOX difference-current comparison](../../potassium_conductances_sci43_20261110/README.md)

**Discriminating prediction.** If sultiame slows bicarbonate restoration after repeated activity, then at comparable pH its effect in WWOX could include a larger Kv7-sensitive current and medium afterhyperpolarization, with less repetitive firing under fast synaptic blockade. Test this alongside sodium current in the same exposure condition. A residual sodium-current effect at comparable pH and bicarbonate, with no change in Kv7-sensitive current, supports an independent sodium effect. If only the late GABA response changes in a separate native-chloride preparation, a synaptic bicarbonate mechanism is more plausible. Match baseline firing before interpreting occlusion and keep the metabolic-recovery outcome. A smaller Kv7 component despite comparable bicarbonate perturbation would favour limited conductance reserve over insufficient drug effect. No experiment in the comparator used sultiame or WWOX.

**Acid–base constraint.** At equilibrium, halving bicarbonate with dissolved CO₂ held fixed would shift pH by **−0.301**. At fixed pH, the same equilibrium bicarbonate ratio requires the same CO₂ ratio. A pH match alone does not hold bicarbonate and CO₂ fixed, and CA inhibition changes reaction kinetics. To attribute a drug effect to reduced bicarbonate restoration, measure its time course after activity or use a separately verified manipulation. In a distinct mouse brainstem preparation, bicarbonate-evoked excitation survived Kv7 blockade, so the hippocampal coupling should be tested in the circuit of interest. [Brainstem primary abstract and Fig. 5 caption](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29873079/)

**Disposition.** The Kv7 comparison changes the preclinical assay. It provides no pediatric central exposure or WWOX response measurement. No matched pediatric CSF drug measurement or open Madeja patch-buffer specification was located in the sources checked. Keep sultiame active until the range and comparators have been checked against attainable exposure. Retain low priority for an unselected pediatric WWOX trial.
