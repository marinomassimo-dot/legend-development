# Prior question for this cross-model screen

Could WWOX loss reproduce a context relevant to the fetal GAD67-low comparison, and is the machinery for uptake and chloride transport detectable in the same specimens? Read GAD1 and GAD2 separately; a lower GAD1 transcript is at most a reason to measure available GABA. Slc6a6 (taurine transporter), Slc12a5 (KCC2), Slc12a2 (NKCC1), Wnk1, Stk39 and Oxsr1 describe the two candidate routes. Examine the within-library log2(1 + KCC2 expression) minus log2(1 + NKCC1 expression), then KO minus control. Require direction concordant across cortex and human organoids and across every single-library omission before calling a direction reproducible. Hippocampus is a regional sensitivity check. Prior WWOX pathway work exists, so this is a prospective comparison of existing data rather than blinded discovery. Neither abundance nor this ratio identifies chloride reversal, phosphorylation, GABA concentration or the effect of taurine. Cell composition and maturation can shift either contrast.
