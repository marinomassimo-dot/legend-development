# A measured central exposure and its limits

In Ohsawa et al.'s 52-week open-label MELAS trial, oral taurine raised mean plasma taurine from 57.57 to 945.67 μM (10 participants) and mean CSF taurine from 11.24 to 42.11 μM (7 participants). One nmol/mL equals one μM. The relative increases were 16.4-fold in plasma and 3.75-fold in CSF. These are ratios of group means from different denominators. They cannot establish an individual blood-to-brain transfer fraction. Two of the ten participants were under 18 (ages 14 and 15). The CSF table does not identify their individual measurements. Six participants experienced treatment-related adverse events, all graded mild to moderate by the investigators. The disease-specific rationale involved mitochondrial tRNA modification. [Primary trial, Tables 1–3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6581075/)

**What the dispersion adds.** Table 2 specifies mean ± standard deviation. For n observations with sample SD s, the largest observation is at most mean + (n−1)s/√n. To see it, fix one deviation d above the mean. The other deviations sum to −d and their squared sum is at least d²/(n−1). Using both printed values rounded upwards by 0.005 gives an upper limit of 73.36 μM for any of the seven on-treatment CSF observations. Even the alternative population-SD definition yields a limit below 75.86 μM. The upper limit is a mathematical constraint on this sample, not the measured maximum, a peak over time or a limit for other patients.

**Comparison with a cortical experiment.** In P2–P4 control mouse cortex Sava et al. reported no significant increase in spontaneous postsynaptic current frequency or tonic inward current with taurine at or below 100 μM in their assay. At 300 μM they observed network recruitment, and cell-attached recordings documented increased firing in subsets of pyramidal neurons and interneurons. Below or at 100 μM a cell entered their nonresponder category only if it responded to a higher concentration. Inoue et al.'s three-day culture exposure for KCC2 was 3 mM. The seven sampled CSF values in MELAS could not have reached either 300 μM or 3 mM. CSF, transient cortical extracellular microdomains, intracellular neuronal taurine and culture bath are different compartments. Endogenous release, transport and local concentration were not measured in WWOX. [Immature cortex study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3912439/) · [KCC2 study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3375508/)

**Disposition.** Park oral taurine as a WWOX trial candidate. The MELAS trial establishes that a studied oral preparation can raise CSF taurine in a mixed-age cohort. It leaves infant formulation acceptability, interactions with background antiseizure medication, WWOX central exposure and developmental benefit unresolved. A prenatal migration assay cannot establish an attainable postnatal rescue. Reopen for a matched WWOX isogenic response in a relevant compartment, stage and exposure, preserving native chloride and measuring absolute firing or a durable maturation endpoint. An independent biomarker cohort in SSADH deficiency is a source lead for the postnatal direction; full quantitative data for this pass were unavailable from the checked publisher record. An effect on sleep, arousal or seizure burden would need an endpoint separate from maturation.
