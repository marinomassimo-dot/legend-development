# Does an axon respond to NR in the same direction as its cell body?

The prespecified comparison sought an endogenous NMNAT2 deficit without increasing NR kinase. Antoniou et al. provide a direct test of the compartment and an incomplete test of function. They exposed primary mouse superior cervical ganglion preparations to NR (2 mM) or PBS on days 0, 1 and 4 and collected on day 7. These are concentrations in culture. The authors identify the source as Tru Niagen capsules in Methods. [Original paper, Methods and Fig. 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11790816/#Fig5)

**What the comparison changes.** Loss of axonal NAD can coincide with a somatic rise even in intact cultures at endogenous kinase expression. SARM1 deletion reverses the direction of the neurite response. NMN was unmeasured owing to sample limitations. The study therefore cannot establish a rise in NMN or the time at which it occurred. It used the whole explant for the intermediate genotype and did not report separate neurite measurements at that intermediate point in Fig. 5E. This missing comparison matters more for translation than a more precise estimate of the severe genotype's effect. WWOX mouse and organoid RNA ratios cannot be converted into axonal NMNAT2 protein activity. A negative local biochemical change also cannot establish functional harm in WWOX. [Original Fig. 5 and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11790816/#Fig5)

**Checkpoint.** Defer an unselected WWOX pediatric trial. Retain NR as a preclinical question. First check whether existing WWOX samples show an axonal NMNAT2 protein or NAD-flux deficit and a tract-matched functional deficit; otherwise record the gap. A favorable observation would be improved propagation or axonal transport together with sustained local NAD recovery in WWOX compared with matched corrected cells at relevant central exposure. Measure the precursor and product in the same axonal compartment over time because cell-body and blood measurements can move in the opposite direction. A decline restricted to axonal NAD with preserved function establishes a biochemical effect only.

**Route check completed in this primary source.** Supplementary Fig. 1A–C uses wild-type, entire DRG explants (n=3) and samples 24 hours after one application. NR and the combined arm differed from vehicle for NAD and NADP; the NAM-only arm did not. The authors used different molar concentrations for NR and NAM, and Dunnett comparisons against vehicle. There is no direct significance test between precursors here. The mutant neurite experiment in Fig. 5 uses NR and vehicle. These panels leave the response of mutant neurites to recycled NAM open.
