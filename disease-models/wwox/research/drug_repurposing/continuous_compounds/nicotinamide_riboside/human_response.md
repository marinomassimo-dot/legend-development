# Does an oral NR response in blood predict an axonal NAD response?

**Acute study.** Table 1 of the 7 T proton spectroscopy study contains four scans for nine healthy adults. One other participant's measurements were excluded for motion. Following oral NR, all nine had higher apparent cerebral oxidized NAD at four hours than on the previous day's scan (mean change +0.065 mM). At two later untreated scans about a day apart, the mean change was −0.020 mM. Within-person subtraction gives +0.085 mM, positive in eight of nine, and +0.068 to +0.097 mM when omitting one participant at a time. This reconstructed contrast controls partly for repeat-scan change. Every participant received NR before their untreated scan pair, separated by 8–159 days; the study has no randomized treatment order or concurrent placebo. The voxel included multiple tissue and cell types. [Original Table 1 and repeatability results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11436296/)

**Eight days.** In NAD-brain stage 1, all six healthy adults increased whole-blood total NAD on NR (mean within-person change +161.2%). In the same individuals the mean change in the occipital total NAD/ATP-alpha signal was +5.0% (four of six positive; individual range −39.9% to +48.9%). Omitting one person gave means from −3.8% to +14.0%. Using PCr gave +1.9% and using all phosphates +12.2%, with three of six positive for each alternative. These are endpoint comparisons. The paper's longitudinal model gives p = 0.90 for cerebral change in this phase. A positive blood response was a poor indicator of the direction or size of the early cerebral response in this sample. [Original study and linked Data S3–S4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12996706/)

**Four weeks.** In stage 2, the mean within-person cerebral NAD/ATP-alpha change was +39.7% in six healthy adults and +29.4% in six adults with Parkinson's disease. In the latter group all six increased on that denominator. Relative to PCr their mean change was +48.1% (six of six positive), while relative to all phosphates it was +4.8% (three of six positive). The source's reference is ATP-alpha. The alternative ratios probe dependence on that choice; none provides absolute axonal NAD. Five healthy IDs recur across the two stages, so the healthy samples cannot be counted as two independent replications. The 7 T study measures oxidized NAD and the 3 T study fits total NAD including NADH. Differences between their estimates cannot be attributed to time alone. [NAD-brain Methods, Fig. 3 and Data S4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12996706/)

**Implication.** Adult human brain spectroscopy supports a central biochemical response under some conditions. It cannot resolve the early NMN:NAD balance in WWOX axons, the form in which the precursor entered the brain, or recovery of conduction. Next prioritize graded endogenous NMNAT2 activity in a model that measures axonal function and substrate handling together.
