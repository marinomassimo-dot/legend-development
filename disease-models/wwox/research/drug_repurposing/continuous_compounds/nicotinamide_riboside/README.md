# Nicotinamide riboside chloride and axonal NAD in WWOX loss

**Candidate selection.** The existing G09 evaluation left nicotinamide riboside (NR) as diagnostic only. It identified no measured NAD supply deficit in WWOX. Reopen the research question because axonal precursor utilization and subcellular NAD loss give competing predictions for a single named supplement. Use β-nicotinamide riboside chloride as the index formulation. Annotate other NAD precursors and salts separately. The existing G06 flag concerns reduced DNA-damage-response competence in non-neural systems, which does not establish excessive NAD consumption by PARP in WWOX neural cells. [Held G06 and G09 records](../../qualification_it21_p3b_20261103/proposals_it21_p3b.tsv)

**Prespecified before analyzing the WWOX data.** Report all three untreated bulk contrasts for Nmrk1, Nmrk2, Nampt, Nmnat1, Nmnat2, Nmnat3, Sarm1, Cd38, Parp1, Slc25a51 and Slc29a1, including baseline abundance and single-library omission signs. Treat carrier and enzyme RNA as pointers. A bulk NAD concentration can conceal an axonal deficit. An NMNAT2 bottleneck might permit NMN accumulation without restoration of axonal NAD. A shift in the NMN:NAD ratio could favor SARM1 activation in an axon already under stress. This is a conditional hypothesis in WWOX.

**Discriminating prediction.** At matched exposure and developmental stage, require local precursor incorporation into axonal NAD before recovery of axonal function. Follow absolute NMN and NAD separately, axonal integrity and conduction. An increase in NAD in cell bodies or blood with persistent axonal NMN accumulation and conduction failure would lower priority. A parent-versus-nicotinamide tracer comparison should resolve how the oral formulation supplies the tissue. A pediatric precedent in another genetic disease would answer a feasibility question only.

**Untreated WWOX signal.** NMNAT2 expression falls in mouse cortex (46.5 to 39.4 TPM), hippocampus (35.4 to 29.6 TPM) and human organoids (53.0 to 31.9 CPM). All single-library omissions preserve the direction. In hippocampal nuclei the decline also occurs within excitatory (176.1 to 133.9 CPM) and inhibitory pools (225.8 to 208.4). One pool per genotype limits inference. SARM1 rises in human organoids and falls in mouse hippocampus. NMRK1 also changes in different directions by model. Reduced NMNAT2 RNA does not establish lower axonal protein or SARM1 activity. Directly measure absolute axonal NMN, NAD and function. **An experiment that reverses direction with timing.** Fig. 1 of Figley et al. compared pretreatment intervals in primary mouse embryonic dorsal-root ganglion neurons with increased NR kinase expression. Axons injured one or two hours after NR addition degenerated faster than controls, whereas the 24-hour pretreatment protected axons over the reported observation period. At early times NMN had increased while NAD had changed little. Later, NAD had increased too. The resource table specifies NR chloride. This is a timing comparison in a manipulated injury model, and does not show injury from oral NR in WWOX. It changes the discriminating test: record both metabolites, their ratio and axonal function over time in intact WWOX and corrected axons *before* introducing any matched challenge. A single late NAD result could miss an earlier unfavorable interval. [Figley et al., Fig. 1A–D](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8174188/)

**Pediatric feasibility and inference limit.** In an open-label A-T study, 17 of 24 participants were under 18; the two ataxia scale analyses included 22 participants. Table 1 gives model-estimated SARA change of −2.4 points over four months (95% CI −4.0 to −0.9) and +3.9 during two months off treatment (2.5 to 5.3). ICARS changed by −10.1 and +12.7. Higher scores indicate more impairment. These are fitted changes, so subtracting the tabulated group means gives different values. The authors report no adverse signal during treatment and note that neither scale was validated below age 12. This is a mixed-age exposure precedent; uncontrolled clinical change or reversal on withdrawal cannot establish axon preservation in WWOX. The main Methods identify NR but the precise preparation of this clinical cohort requires verification in its supplement. [Original clinical study, Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9291897/)

**Checkpoint.** NR chloride remains active. Prioritize a graded NMNAT2-deficiency comparison with endogenous kinase expression or direct human compartmental exposure after oral NR. Establish whether precursor reaches neural NAD intact or via recycled nicotinamide and whether a reversible axonal phenotype exists in WWOX. Short locators: Figley key resources, “Nicotinamide riboside (NR) chloride”; A-T Methods, “linear mixed model analyses”.

**Oral route changes the test.** Liu et al. tracked NR bearing one label on the nicotinamide and another on the ribose. Fig. 7 distinguishes doubly labeled NAD (intact precursor use) from singly labeled NAD consistent with salvage of released nicotinamide. In mouse whole brain only the singly labeled product was detected under the tested conditions, including in the high-dose intravenous comparison reported in the Results. Muscle incorporated intact NR after intravenous exposure. The detection limit leaves small contributions possible. Whole-brain labeling does not identify a particular axonal compartment. In WWOX, direct bathing of neurons in intact NR could therefore give a misleading answer about an oral intervention. Compare nicotinamide and intact NR at measured enrichments and follow both axonal ribose and nicotinamide labels. NAMPT abundance and flux matter for the recycled route. [Liu et al., Results and Fig. 7a,e](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5932087/)

**Clinical formulation check.** The clinical supplement describes NR capsules and cites a regulatory assessment of NR chloride in its dosing rationale. The dosing description gives no manufacturer or lot that would independently identify the administered salt. The same assessors repeated the ataxia measures. Repeat testing, natural symptom variation and open-label observation remain competing explanations. Next compare the measured human central response to oral NR with plasma and whole-blood NAD and evaluate which readout could adjudicate axonal salvage. [A-T supplement, Dosing and Outcome measurements](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC9291897.1/MDS-36-2951-s001.docx)

**Human central comparison.** Across matched adults at day eight, blood total NAD increased in six of six while cerebral NAD/ATP-alpha increased in four. Week-four direction was more consistent, although changing the reference altered the estimated Parkinson cohort effect. Next examine graded endogenous NMNAT2 deficiency and axonal output, with separately measured local NAD and NMN. [Matched blood and brain reanalysis](human_response.md)

**Residual enzyme activity and an internal negative control.** In Dingwall et al., two siblings had compound-heterozygous NMNAT2 variants. Purified V98M and R232Q enzymes retained 14.6% and 4.4% of wild-type activity, respectively, at 37°C (Fig. 1E). These values characterize distinct purified proteins. Averaging them would not recover activity in a WWOX axon. Two-month mutant mouse sciatic nerves showed an eightfold rise in cADPR, which disappeared on a Sarm1-null background (Fig. 5A). Femoral and sural nerves both exhibited evidence of SARM1 activity, yet increased activated macrophages were limited to the femoral comparison (Fig. 6G–H). This supplies an anatomically matched counterexample to using cADPR alone to predict downstream injury. NR was not tested. WWOX shows a modest RNA change, with no established enzyme-activity equivalent. Next test the WWOX premise against its existing tract-specific evidence for axon loss, axon caliber, myelin and conduction before assigning a trial priority. [Dingwall et al., Figs. 1E, 5A and 6G–H](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9711884/)

**Direct WWOX constraint.** In the held P17 neuronal Wwox knockout record (Repudi Fig. 5B), myelinated callosal profiles fall from about 180 to 55 per field. Unmyelinated profiles were counted in the optic nerve, leaving total eligible axons in the same callosal fields unknown. A reduced density of ensheathed profiles could reflect failed initial ensheathment, altered caliber or axonal loss. A treatment that changes only myelination cannot validate prevention of SARM1-mediated axon degeneration. The priority observation is preservation of *all* identifiable axons and propagation in the same tract, relative to their pre-injury state, with sheath coverage and caliber separately accounted for. [Existing audit and anatomical denominators](../clemastine/axonal_denominator.md)

**Next comparison, prespecified.** Before treating lower NMNAT2 as selective, compare its within-neuron change to STMN2, TUBB3, NEFL, GAP43 and SNAP25 in the same excitatory and inhibitory nuclear pools. Evaluate each reference separately. A common shift of axonal or neuronal transcripts would favor a broader maturation or composition explanation. Even a selective RNA decrease would still need protein, metabolites and function.

**Matched marker result and next step.** For each neuronal class we divided the knockout NMNAT2-to-marker ratio by its control counterpart. All four measurable references gave ratios below one in both classes (excitatory 0.585–0.808, inhibitory 0.547–0.938). Tubb3 is present in the feature annotation but had zero assigned counts in both arms of each class. We left both ratios undefined. The direction survives the choice among four denominators, although relative effect size varies. The two classes come from the same pooled mouse libraries, one per genotype. They provide no independent biological replication, and altered neuronal substates, developmental timing and RNA localization remain viable explanations. Reproduce with `python3 axon_markers.py /path/to/files` from this directory using the same GEO inputs and NumPy as the nuclei analysis. [Raw counts and ratios](axon_markers.tsv)

**Decision.** Keep NR chloride active for a focused preclinical check of endogenous axonal precursor handling. Search for paired early and late axonal NMN, NAD and propagation or survival under endogenous NR kinase expression, with partial NMNAT2 activity, recording whether comparable data exist in WWOX. Rescue predicts sustained axonal NAD recovery and improved function relative to vehicle in WWOX, accounting for baseline differences in matched corrected cells. Early NMN accumulation without subsequent functional recovery would weaken prioritization despite a blood NAD rise. The pediatric ataxia exposure precedent alone cannot support a WWOX trial.
