# Which molecule was measured after oral NR?

**Human plasma.** Trammell et al., Supplementary Table 2, reports ten time points in one adult. Before dosing plasma nicotinamide was 0.70 µM; its highest sampled value over the first 23.8 hours was 1.2 µM, and the 167.6-hour value was 1.5 µM. Me2PY rose from 2.2 to 14 µM by 23.8 hours. Intact NR has no column in this table. At the observed first-day maximum the control NAM bath in Antoniou Fig. S1 (1,000 µM) was about 833 times the measured plasma NAM. This ratio describes two experimental conditions with different matrices and timing. It supplies neither a central exposure limit nor a safety margin. [Table S2 of the Trammell supplement, p. 3](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC5062546.1/ncomms12948-s1.pdf)

**Human cerebrospinal fluid.** At baseline and four weeks in adults with Parkinson's disease, Brakedal et al. found that the downstream product Me-2-PY rose in all NR recipients assayed. Results accompanying Fig. 3 explicitly list NAD+, NADH, NMN, NAAD and methyl-nicotinamide below detection in CSF. The paragraph does not supply concentrations or numerical assay limits for intact NR or unmodified NAM. A rise in this degradation product cannot identify the precursor species that reached an axon or its concentration. The exact number of paired CSF samples is not established by the Fig. 3 legend. [Brakedal et al., Results and Fig. 3; trial methods](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2022.02.001)

**Disposition.** Park NR chloride for an unselected WWOX pediatric trial. The combination of a direction-changing axonal response in an endogenous mouse hypomorph, absent direct WWOX axonal enzyme evidence, and unresolved local exposure makes further inference from peripheral NAD unlikely to change that decision. Reopen for measured neural exposure to the relevant precursor with paired axonal metabolites and functional recovery in WWOX. Evidence of biochemical divergence alone establishes neither benefit nor harm. Original quantitative comparisons here use reported facts only. The authors' PDFs remain in the local archive under their respective licenses.
