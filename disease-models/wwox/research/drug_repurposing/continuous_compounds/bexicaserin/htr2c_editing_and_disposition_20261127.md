## Data and counterproof for receptor attribution

- **Isoform effects are ligand- and assay-dependent.** In the same recombinant Gq/TRUPATH assay, lorcaserin has an EC50 of 17.0 nM at INI, 18.2 nM at VSV and 426.6 nM at VGV, a **25.1-fold** INI–VGV shift. The comparison uses the authors' reported log midpoints. VSV and VGV both showed lower basal signaling than INI, while VSV's lorcaserin potency was similar. In a separate receptor signaling study, editing reduced serotonin potency through Gq by about 3–13-fold and through G11 by 8–27-fold for the tested isoforms. These values belong to the assayed ligands and cell systems. They bound the size of a possible change for experiment design. The corresponding bexicaserin concentration-response curves across INI, VSV and VGV were unavailable in the sources examined. [Gumpper et al., Fig. 1 and Table S2](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2022.111211) · [Bonniwell et al., Fig. 5B and Table S4](https://doi.org/10.1021/acschemneuro.5c00647)
- **Lower coupling can coexist with greater receptor density and response.** In mice constrained to VGV, an agonist high-affinity binding component and GTP sensitivity were reduced, while forebrain membrane mesulergine Bmax rose from 163.3 to 4,266 fmol/mg, **26.1-fold**. An agonist-associated locomotor response was more sensitive in those mice. This is a direct counterexample to inferring in vivo agonist response from the edited-protein fraction alone. Binding sites in a membrane preparation should be distinguished from surface receptor in an identified immature neuron. The RNA editing and truncating splice branch can influence the number of receptors available in addition to G-protein coupling. [Olaghere da Silva et al., Figs. 2–5 and 7](https://doi.org/10.3389/neuro.23.001.2010) · [Alternative splice-site primary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15087490/)
- **Sensitivity test for the WWOX inference.** With the same per-receptor response, a 6.33-fold bulk RNA gain is canceled if productive surface receptor per RNA falls to **15.8%** of control. In a separate unit-slope model that holds productive surface per RNA and maximal response equal, a right shift in the knockout EC50 of **11.6-fold** or **19.4-fold** would cancel the gain at the lower or higher adult total-CSF peak scale, respectively. The calculation tests model identifiability. The adult CSF scale is used only for the arithmetic. Neither the 25.1-fold lorcaserin effect nor the membrane Bmax ratio is a bexicaserin parameter, and the two experiments cannot be combined into an estimate.
- **The native sign depends on where it is measured.** The EEC 2026 conference abstract reports interneuron depolarization and pyramidal hyperpolarization in hippocampus. The 2025 AES thalamic abstract instead reports decreases in spontaneous IPSCs and extrasynaptic tonic current in juvenile rat thalamocortical neurons. The public abstract ends mid-results, and no magnitude or antagonist result is assigned to it. Neither result holds native chloride, first recruitment and the circuit outcome together in WWOX. [EEC 2026, abstract 1810](https://www.ilae.org/files/dmfile/eec_2026_abstract_book_01_09_with_cover-1.pdf) · [AES thalamic abstract](https://aesnet.org/abstractslisting/bexicaserin-selectively-activates-5-ht2c-receptors-to-modulate-activity-in-the-thalamocortical-circuit-relevant-for-absence-seizures)

## One discriminating comparison

The held juvenile neuronal-Wwox-deletion neocortical recordings give a disease-relevant comparator. Spontaneous bursts were sensitive to d-APV in the tested slices, while the inhibitory-current recordings with high intracellular KCl left native chloride direction unmeasured. Begin in a defined cortical preparation to test whether an upstream 5-HT₂C effect alters that late network recruitment. Carry hippocampal or thalamic results as region-specific comparators. [Held cortical excitability crosswalk](../../perampanel_20261122/clinical_mechanistic_update_20261126.md) · [Held chloride audit](../ganaxolone/neurosteroid_replacement_and_disposition_20261127.md)

**Hypothesis.** WWOX-deficient inhibitory cells retain productive surface 5-HT₂C signaling capable of adding postsynaptic inhibitory efficacy, with a genotype-dependent effect on late recruitment. A direct reduction in pyramidal-cell excitability could produce the same network summary without improved native inhibition.

**Experiment.** In one stage- and region-matched WWOX/isogenic preparation, compare free-exposure-verified parent against vehicle, with 5-HT₂C interruption alone and with parent. Measure proximal signaling and surface receptor in known inhibitory and excitatory cells. Pair inhibitory recruitment and native-chloride postsynaptic spike suppression with pyramidal excitability during isolation of inhibitory synaptic transmission, and monitor first and late activity in the intact network. Anchor initial drive before the comparison and report the intact change separately from the fixed-first-response comparison. Treat independent preparations as biological units and repeated cells as nested observations. Assess whether parent can recruit signaling across the edited receptor calibrators at matched surface abundance when the native pattern and the recombinant reference disagree. Keep developmental outcomes separate from acute activity.

**Decision rule.** Receptor-dependent reduction of late activity with improved native postsynaptic inhibition and preserved first responses favors the inhibitory-reserve hypothesis. A receptor-dependent decrease in pyramidal excitability that persists when inhibitory transmission is separated, with native inhibitory efficacy unchanged, favors direct modulation of excitatory cells. The arms may coexist. Increased interneuron firing without additional postsynaptic inhibition, a response that survives receptor interruption, or loss of early physiological recruitment lowers the WWOX-specific rationale. A comparable response in controls supports general pharmacology. Reopen compound priority for a replicated genotype-selective benefit at measured feasible exposure with physiological recruitment preserved. A review would become decision-relevant upon release of native isoform-response data or a pediatric controlled WWOX-relevant result.

## Pediatric transfer

PACIFIC used an oral solution, including enteral administration in the protocol. The active group included adolescents, while the placebo range started at 19. In the safety denominators, somnolence occurred in **12/43 versus 1/9**, and decreased appetite in **9/43 versus 0/9**. These observations make vigilance, growth and feeding tolerance material in a younger WWOX population. Stable concomitant antiseizure drugs and the trial's interaction analysis provide context, with age-specific PK and comedication effects still unresolved. The open-label extension enrolled 41 trial completers and retained 38 through 12 months. Its conference-reported 59.3% median motor seizure reduction is relative to the original baseline among a selected cohort without a continuing concurrent control. The phase 3 registrations include ages 2–65, with outcomes absent from the registry snapshots examined. No efficacy estimate is assigned to WOREE. [PACIFIC, §§2.2 and 3.2, Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1111/epi.18689) · [AAN extension abstract](https://index.mirasmart.com/AAN2026/PDFfiles/AAN2026-001736.html) · [DEEp OCEAN](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06719141) · [DEEp SEA](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06660394)
