# Bexicaserin | Exposure and controlled evidence checkpoint

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN` · SCI_CODEX · 27 November 2026 · analysis in progress.

**Controlled DEE evidence.** PACIFIC enrolled ages 12–55 in the active arm and 19–41 in the placebo arm. The safety set was 43 versus 9. The full analysis set was 35 versus 9 and required entry into maintenance with at least one maintenance observation. The prespecified rank comparison of countable motor seizure frequency gave median changes of −59.8% and −17.4%, nominal p=.0538. The p=.0206 result applies to a separate post hoc comparison of means. For ≥50% response the counts were 21/35 and 3/9, nominal Fisher p=.2607, which reproduces. Treating each of eight excluded active-arm participants first as a nonresponder and then as a responder changes the active-minus-placebo difference from the reported-set +26.67 points to a descriptive range of +15.50 to +34.11 points. This sensitivity neither reconstructs missing seizure frequency nor addresses the lack of an adolescent placebo comparator. Treatment-emergent adverse events led to discontinuation in 9/43 versus 0/9. Resolve the discrepancy between Results prose and Table 2 for drug-related serious events before assigning a count. [PACIFIC, §§2.1, 2.5, 3.1–3.3 and Tables 1–3](https://doi.org/10.1111/epi.18689)

**Target and exposure.** The reference functional midpoint is 42 nM in the recombinant 5-HT₂C inositol phosphate assay. Maximal response there is 112% of reference, with the paper reporting n=23. The 2A and 2B numbers in Table 5 are binding limits above 50,000 nM. Rat choroid plexus provides a native-expression assay and no information on individual human edited isoforms. In healthy adults, Kallem et al. measured parent in CSF and reported a 17.6 ng/mL peak, about 59.6 nM. The source disagrees between running text and figure caption on amount units, so the calculation uses concentration alone. An open-label EEC 2024 poster gives mean steady-state CSF peaks of 12.287 and 30.357 ng/mL (n=9 and 7 for the summaries) across two cohorts. Those are 41.6 and 102.8 nM, about 0.99 and 2.45 times the nominal recombinant midpoint. The corresponding mean average CSF concentrations are 31.3 and 82.5 nM. These comparisons use total CSF, healthy adults and nominal assay concentration. They supply an exposure scale, with local unbound juvenile brain concentration, occupation and durable receptor signaling still open. The source poster reports CSF and plasma AUCs on different days and qEEG without a placebo comparator. The samples in the article and poster are kept separate pending verification of study overlap. [Ren et al., Table 5 and Fig. 3](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jmedchem.4c02923) · [Kallem et al., §3.4 and Fig. 5](https://doi.org/10.1002/bmc.70371) · [EEC 2024 poster P262, Tables 2–5](https://www.longboardpharma.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/EEC-262_A-Ph-1-Study-of-5-HT2C-Superagonist-Bexi-Shows-Robust-Brain-Penetration-and-Strong-Plasma-CSF-PK-QEEG-Changes-Reflecting-Receptor-Engagement.pdf) · [WHO INN formula](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/international-nonproprietary-names-%28inn%29/rl91.pdf?download=true)

**Discriminator to refine.** The registered inhibitory-reserve hypothesis has a specific alternative. A conference report describes depolarization of inhibitory cells and hyperpolarization of pyramidal cells in hippocampus. A thalamic abstract reports the opposite sign for some local GABA current measures. Treat these as region-specific leads. Under verified free exposure, a selective 5-HT₂C interruption tests receptor dependence. Separating native inhibitory transmission from pyramidal-cell excitability then tests which arm accounts for late-event suppression at preserved first recruitment. A persistent pyramidal response after inhibitory transmission is accounted for favors a direct excitatory-cell pathway. Both arms could contribute. [Registered prediction](htr2c_recruitment_pretest_20261127.md) · [EEC 2026 reports](https://www.ilae.org/files/dmfile/eec_2026_abstract_book_01_09_with_cover-1.pdf) · [AES thalamic abstract](https://aesnet.org/abstractslisting/bexicaserin-selectively-activates-5-ht2c-receptors-to-modulate-activity-in-the-thalamocortical-circuit-relevant-for-absence-seizures)

**Registry check, 27 November.** The PACIFIC registry lists eligibility at 12–65 and no submitted results, so the published safety discrepancy remains unresolved. Both phase 3 DEEp SEA and DEEp OCEAN list eligibility at 2–65 and no submitted results as of this check. Eligibility alone does not define the actual pediatric sample or the effect in WOREE.
