# A change in channel availability can precede a change in neuronal firing

**Concentration and waveform.** Jo and Bean measured human NaV1.7 in HEK cells at 21–23 °C. In Fig. 1B, 100 µM lacosamide reduced evoked current by 3% at a steady −120 mV, 43% at −80 mV and 61% at −70 mV. A holding-potential difference alone was sufficient to create a large apparent contrast. In a separate clinical comparison, 27 paired samples from 21 people with epilepsy had 1.24–14.95 µg/mL total CSF lacosamide (mean 7.37). Molecular mass 250.30 g/mol gives 4.95–59.73 µM (mean 29.44). The 100 µM bath was 1.67 times the highest measured CSF value in that series. Sampling times, total CSF, interstitial free drug and WWOX infant exposure cannot be interchanged. No linear extrapolation of block to the mean CSF concentration is justified. [Jo and Bean, Figs. 1 and 6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5363714/) · [Paired human CSF/serum, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25988882/) · [FDA label, molecular mass](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2022/022253s055%2C022254s045%2C022255s037lbl.pdf)

**Calculation.** We reconstructed the group-mean parameter summary for six paired cells from the Fig. 6 results. A step from −120 to −80 mV produces two rising exponential phases in each condition. At 0.1 s the added unavailable fraction at 100 µM versus vehicle was 10.50 percentage points. At 0.66 s it was 11.87 points, with only 0.87 points attributable to the slower phase. At 2 s the figures were 13.56 and 2.56 points. The means of fitted parameters do not reproduce the average of individual curves exactly. No single-cell variance or native WWOX block can be inferred. The durations match a timescale only. A single depolarizing step between artificial voltages differs from a train of action potentials. In the same study a 100-ms prepulse to −80 mV permitted rapid recovery at −120 mV with a mean time constant of 7.2 ms in drug. A 10-s prepulse generated an additional slow phase. These data cannot determine recovery at native interburst voltage. [Reconstruction](timing.py) · [Results](timing.tsv)

**Countercheck.** The rat cortical neuron study found reduced sustained firing over a 10-s challenge with no detected change in the first second, and an effect on a 30-s ramp but not a 1-s ramp. In a juvenile WWOX cortical field recording, 85–100 of 119 events fell below one second: 85 events occupied bins wholly below that cutoff and 15 occupied the straddling bin. Counts concern events in a pooled field histogram, not individual animals or single-cell voltage. Human epilepsy surgical tissue provides an independent precedent for suppressing high-frequency firing in dentate granule cells. Its anatomy, maturity and disease differ from the WWOX comparison. [Rat cortical neuron study, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17940193/) · [WWOX histogram and bin bounds](../../ganaxolone_chloride_recovery_20261123/wwox_burst_histogram.tsv) · [Human and rat dentate cells, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27864845/)

**Next decision.** A generic preference for late firing is a weak explanation for suppressing the typical short WWOX field event. A depolarized resting potential or an unusually high spike rate within the event could still matter. Compare first-spike probability, late spikes and recruitment of inhibitory cells under brief native waveforms and a prolonged comparator in isogenic neurons at matched starting voltage. Include a separate arm preserving each genotype’s natural starting voltage: loss of the drug-by-genotype interaction only after voltage matching points to baseline voltage as its mediator. Preserve stimulus-evoked propagation and examine drug-free recovery before interpreting a reduced burst count as functional rescue.

**Competing inhibitory effect.** In another cortical culture study from the same first author, 100 µM lacosamide reduced the incidence of both spontaneous excitatory and inhibitory postsynaptic currents. That directly motivates paired absolute current and interneuron-recruitment outcomes. It does not show preferential suppression of either input in WWOX. [Errington et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16620882/)

**Human primary comparison, resolved.** Holtkamp et al. used eight-second current steps in surgical temporal-lobe epilepsy tissue. Fig. 5D included nine human dentate granule cells. At 300 pA the authors report a significant decrease in firing with 100 µM lacosamide (omnibus p=0.012, followed by Dunnett comparison). The 30 µM arm does not establish a comparable effect. The separate Fig. 6C–D waveform analysis retained only cells with firing sustained throughout the eight seconds under drug and selected traces with similar spike counts across conditions. Five human cells entered that comparison (paired p=0.03 for the time-dependent waveform change). This design can measure slowing among persistent spikes. It cannot estimate the probability that a short native burst fails to initiate, or the percentage of neurons silenced completely. These are counts of cells; for this comparison the number of independent donors is unverified. [Primary paper, pp. 29, 37–39, Figs. 5–6](https://doi.org/10.1111/epi.13602) · [First author's public thesis reproducing the paper](https://files01.core.ac.uk/download/pdf/322961442.pdf)

**The voltage contrast differs by cell type.** In untreated juvenile mouse WWOX cortex, mean pyramidal-cell resting voltage shifted +5.63 mV (−79.25 to −73.62, 13 cells/5 WT animals vs 40/15 KO). Interneurons had means of −67.11 in pooled WT/heterozygote controls and −71.52 mV in KO (5 cells/4 animals vs 6/5). The latter −4.41 mV contrast lacks a significance mark, and its control includes heterozygotes. It cannot establish a protective shift in inhibitory cells. The KO interneuron mean was still more depolarized than the KO pyramidal mean, and interneuron spike amplitude was reduced. This motivates measuring both absolute recruitment and the *change* with drug. Under a strictly illustrative linear interpolation between the NaV1.7 data at −80 and −70 mV, the pyramidal shift would imply about 10.1 percentage points extra tonic current inhibition at 100 µM (5.63 × (61−43)/10). Simple monotonicity alone permits anything from zero to 18 points between those voltages. Both are assumptions about a heterologous channel, not estimates of a WWOX response. The control interneuron mean lies outside the interval and we decline to extrapolate. [WWOX primary Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8609180/) · [Clamp comparison, Fig. 1B](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5363714/)

**Disposition.** Park lacosamide for a WWOX-specific pediatric trial. Retain the acute, voltage-dependent circuit comparison as a preclinical question. Reopen for a differential effect in juvenile WWOX neurons on brief measured waveforms at a measured attainable exposure, with preservation of interneuron recruitment and evoked axonal propagation, or for attributable clinical responses with documented baseline seizure type and concomitant agents. The scientific case for developmental repair would additionally require positive functional axonal outcomes independent of acute activity suppression.
