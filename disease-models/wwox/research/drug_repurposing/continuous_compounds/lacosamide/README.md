# Lacosamide, sustained discharges and axonal development

**Question before inspecting the WWOX target contrasts.** An antiseizure medicine that preferentially reduces sustained firing could dampen prolonged WWOX discharges while leaving the first spikes of a physiological response intact. But if it reduces recruitment of inhibitory neurons or propagation in already hypomyelinated axons, the same drug could narrow the usable range of the network. Test event initiation, total duration, inhibitory-cell recruitment and stimulus-evoked conduction separately. The primary comparison is genotype by firing history at comparable unbound exposure and baseline activity, with independent preparations as replicates.

**Potential developmental association.** The proposed link of lacosamide to CRMP2 (DPYSL2) is relevant to neurite extension only if direct target engagement and functional axonal effects occur at plausible free exposure. Treat that link as contested until direct binding, functional perturbation and a negative comparator agree. A change in DPYSL2 RNA, morphology following broad firing suppression or fewer seizures alone would not demonstrate repair. If activity-matched controls reproduce the morphology change, retain a circuit interpretation. Compare sodium channel expression across neuronal classes, preserve splice and developmental limitations, and assess infant seizure evidence separately from pediatric focal epilepsy.

**First checkpoint.** Three direct WWOX exposures have no attributable response. The R-enantiomer binding assays disagree in overlapping concentration ranges, and DPYSL2 changes in opposite directions in the organoid and mouse hippocampus. Deprioritize a developmental rescue inferred from CRMP2 abundance. The circuit-level question remains active. [Pediatric evidence, WWOX screens and discriminating comparison](evidence.md)

**Disposition.** Park for WWOX-specific trial prioritization. The human tissue endpoint conditions on prolonged firing, and the juvenile WWOX resting-voltage shifts differ across cell classes and control groups. Reopen for preserved inhibitory recruitment and conduction during a selective, exposure-matched response on native brief discharges. [Timing, human-cell selection and genotype comparator](timing.md)
