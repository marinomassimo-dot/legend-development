# Does chronic oral DHA increase brain DHA?

**Matched comparison.** Sugasini et al. randomized adult wild-type mice on rodent chow to corn oil or an equal DHA moiety (1 mg/day) as nonesterified DHA, sn-1 LPC-DHA or sn-2 LPC-DHA for 30 days, eight mice assigned per group. The authors report more than a doubling of brain DHA with LPC forms while free DHA had little effect. The hippocampal assays included DHA-containing phosphatidylethanolamine and phosphatidylcholine species. The dose is the mass of the DHA moiety. [Original investigation, experimental groups and brain lipid results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5596017/)

**Independent replication.** The 2025 study matched the vehicle, background chow, duration, mouse strain, daily DHA amount, overnight fast and tissue collection. Its overlapping comparisons were vehicle, free DHA and sn-1 LPC-DHA. The fourth arm was di-DHA-PC. Five to six mice per group contributed to regional comparisons. Fig. 2 showed no significant between-group differences in either absolute DHA or DHA as a percentage of fatty acids in any of six brain regions. Baseline brain DHA was 23 µmol/g in cortex and 19 µmol/g in hippocampus. Terminal plasma DHA percentage increased in the treated groups, providing an absorption control. A fasting relative percentage cannot quantify free plasma exposure over the preceding day. Small groups and a nonsignificant omnibus comparison leave smaller effects possible. No replication of a cognitive endpoint or of the sn-2 LPC arm is inferred. [Replication, Figs. 2–3](https://www.jlr.org/article/S0022-2275%2825%2900175-0/fulltext)

**Consequence for WWOX.** A stable total concentration can coexist with rapid turnover, changed lipid species or cell-specific redistribution. The rat tracer experiment and the chronic mouse comparisons therefore answer separate questions. The replicated evidence lowers priority for a large chronic *net* increase in regional DHA from a carrier switch in an unselected WWOX population. It leaves uptake kinetics, specific lipid incorporation and the more rapid channel response open. A disease-specific baseline deficit plus recovery of structure or function would change this decision. [WWOX expression and acute brain tracer results](README.md)
