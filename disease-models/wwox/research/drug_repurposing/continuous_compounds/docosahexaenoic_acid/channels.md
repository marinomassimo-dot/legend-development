# Acute conductance and developmental comparators for DHA

**Exposure directly measured.** Huber and Kleinfeld's Table 3 gives total aqueous unbound fatty acids of 1.6 nM in pooled adult reference plasma. DHA accounts for mole fractions of 0.021, 0.021 and 0.020 in three technical replicates: 33.6, 33.6 and 32.0 pM. These are replicates of a reference material, not three independent people. Local release from brain phospholipids and binding in brain extracellular fluid can differ from circulating values. The amount of nonesterified DHA in a tissue extract includes protein- and membrane-associated material. [Table 3, pooled NIST material](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5335587/) · [Calculated values](channel_scale.tsv) · [Calculation](channel_scale.py)

**Opposing channel responses.** In isolated neocortical neurons, DHA suppressed sustained outward current. The concentration constants came from separate recombinant recordings at +40 mV: 1.8 µM for Kv1.2 and 0.69 µM for Kv3.1a. Albumin reversed the recombinant block within approximately two seconds. A separate recombinant HEK study found that DHA increased TREK-1 leak conductance across voltages and that resting channel activity influenced response magnitude. This is TREK-1 (KCNK2), whereas the most concordant decrease in the WWOX mouse neuronal pools involved TREK-2 (KCNK10). Neither experiment resolves which current determines WWOX firing. The adult plasma measurements and nominal bath concentrations have incompatible compartments and binding conditions for calculating occupancy. [Native current and cloned Kv studies, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8938727/) · [TREK-1 study, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42099013/)

**Pediatric feasibility.** The N3RO trial randomized 1,273 infants born before 29 weeks' gestation. Its physiological bronchopulmonary dysplasia endpoint occurred in 49.1% versus 43.9% (difference 5.2 percentage points, adjusted RR 1.13, 95% CI 1.02–1.25). The five-year cognitive follow-up at five of 13 centers found an adjusted IQ difference of +3.45 points (95% CI 0.38–6.53, missing values imputed). Scores were available in 480 of 656 eligible surviving children. Both reports concern one trial and an enteral fractionated tuna-oil triglyceride emulsion containing some EPA, against soy oil, with background milk/formula intake. This supplies developmental and safety precedent for that formulation and population. It cannot provide a benefit-risk estimate for free DHA in children with WWOX loss. [Original randomized trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28355511/) · [Follow-up and formulation](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa2206868)

**Decision for the next comparison.** Keep the membrane hypothesis low priority. For a circuit effect, require a selective and reversible change in leak conductance near resting voltage together with preserved fast-spiking inhibitory recruitment at measured unbound exposure. Compare first-spike and repetitive-firing responses with genotype-corrected controls. A lower pooled spike count with attenuated inhibitory repolarization could falsely favor treatment. The open question is whether primary native or matched cell-class recordings distinguish TREK-2 from voltage-gated Kv effects at comparable exposure. No WWOX intervention response or pediatric free central exposure was identified in the checked material.

Short locators: Huber and Kleinfeld, Table 3, “NIST all average”; five-year follow-up, Methods, “DHA in triglyceride form”.

**A native inhibitory circuit changes the prediction.** In an Fmr1-deficient mouse cerebellar circuit with excessive basket-cell inhibition, DHA shifted recombinant Kv1.2 gating at 1 µM and had little effect on peak amplitude during that ramp. In knockout slices, 10 µM DHA reduced both amplitude and frequency of spontaneous inhibitory postsynaptic currents (Fig. 5c–d, n = 8 recordings) and increased Purkinje cell firing toward wild type (Fig. 5e–f, n = 7). The paper distinguishes voltage-dependent recruitment from maximum current. This also explains how a test at a single depolarized voltage can give an incomplete account. Transfer depends on the initial direction of the inhibitory defect. Lower GABA release could be counterproductive in a WWOX network with inadequate inhibition. The presynaptic protein lesion in Fmr1 and the nominal slice exposure have no established equivalent in WWOX. [Original Results and Fig. 5a–f](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7473840/) · [Direct WWOX intrinsic circuit comparison](../brivaracetam/intrinsic.md)

**Disposition.** Park nonesterified DHA for unselected pediatric WWOX trial priority. Reopen for a genotype-specific rescue at measured local free exposure, with identified inhibitory output and principal-cell firing measured together, or a confirmed membrane deficit followed by species-specific lipid incorporation and functional sheath recovery. The mouse replication already challenges the broad oral membrane-enrichment claim. Additional literature review cannot substitute for either WWOX measurement.
