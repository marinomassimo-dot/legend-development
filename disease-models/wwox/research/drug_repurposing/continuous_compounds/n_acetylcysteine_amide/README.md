# N-acetylcysteine amide | Parent delivery versus conversion to NAC

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Opening checkpoint.** N-acetylcysteine amide (NACA) is the sole active compound after nicotinamide. The earlier WWOX NAC dossier found a redox/substrate question with a potentially confounding extracellular glutamate response. A separate held neurite study tested NACA as a single arm with riboflavin in riboflavin transporter deficiency. Neither establishes a WWOX effect of the amide. NACA is eligible because the rat injury literature includes same-study NACA and NAC arms and the mouse PK study distinguishes systemic parent from converted NAC. Compare the evidence for an amide-specific tissue delivery or compartmental effect before reusing any claim of improved central bioavailability. [Held WWOX NAC](../acetylcysteine/README.md) · [Held riboflavin replay](../riboflavin/neurites.tsv) · [Rat comparison](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4086163/) · [Mouse PK](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31740394/)

**Discriminating prediction.** If intact NACA reaches the limiting neural compartment, NACA should improve labeled glutathione synthesis and post-activity mitochondrial recovery beyond NAC at equal measured NAC exposure and first recruitment. Confirm enrichment or dependence of the effect in WWOX versus corrected cells, while preserving extracellular glutamate handling and physiological inhibitory recruitment. Similar responses at matched converted NAC exposure favor shared precursor delivery. A total glutathione rise without improved ATP recovery or evoked function leaves the priority unchanged.

**Next comparison.** Extract analyte-specific exposure by route and compartment, identifying direct measurements separately from permeability claims. Replay the same-study NACA and NAC endpoints at matched time, determine whether injury model and exposure explain the apparent advantage, and check the primary registry for formulation, ages and outcomes. Include a separate intact-parent criterion if the available exposure data are limited to plasma. [Registered formulation study](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04355689)

**Completed checkpoint.** Retain as a conditional preclinical comparator. Matched-mass injury function favors NACA under the reported conditions. Local intact amide, converted NAC, redox flux and WWOX function have yet to be separated. The adult sponsor PK and age-12-and-over HCCAA registration refine feasibility without establishing pediatric WWOX exposure. [Disposition and replay](compartment_and_disposition_20261127.md)
