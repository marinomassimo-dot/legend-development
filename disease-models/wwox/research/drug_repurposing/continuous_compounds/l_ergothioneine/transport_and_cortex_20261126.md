**Decision at this checkpoint.** Keep L-ergothioneine active as a transport and redox hypothesis. ## What blood correction does and does not reconcile

Cheah et al. quantify ergothioneine by LC–MS/MS in male mice retained on standard chow. The cortical label is explicit in the Fig. 3 caption. Groups have seven animals per condition. Collections are terminal, 24 h after the last oral exposure. Fig. 4j estimates (saline versus higher exposure, ng/mg wet brain) are **3.8 versus 2.9** after one day, **2.7 versus 5.6** after seven days and **3.0 versus 10.6** after 28 days. The approximate day-28 blood means from Fig. 4b are **75 and 192 ng/µL**. These are cohort bar estimates, with neither primary time-to-peak nor independent cell exposure. Control brain and blood already contain parent from normal feeding. [Primary Methods and Figs. 3–4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-20021-z)

Supplemental Table 4 includes **one high-exposure cortex sample per time** and no saline cortex rows. Calculating `100*(total−corrected)/total` from the listed values gives reductions of **5.6%, 7.4% and 3.5%**. Subtracting measured vascular contribution leaves **3.53, 6.12 and 8.54 ng/mg** of cortex in those selected samples. Small differences from the printed percentages can arise from use of rounded displayed inputs. Specimen selection and within-group variation are unavailable from this table. The results support a non-blood cortical signal under the correction and do not localize parent to neurons, extracellular fluid, endothelium or mitochondria. [Supplemental Table 4, p. 5](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC5785509.1/41598_2018_20021_MOESM1_ESM.pdf) · [Copied numeric inputs and provenance](mouse_blood_correction.tsv)

A descriptive carryover sensitivity check gives **0.065 µL/mg** residual blood required to explain the full 28-day cortical group difference, if the residual volumes are equal across groups. This divides an approximate **7.60 ng/mg** brain difference by an approximate **117 ng/µL** blood difference. By comparison, the 28-day selected sample's **0.31 ng/mg** correction corresponds to about **0.0016 µL/mg** at the group high-exposure blood concentration. The calculation illustrates scale under stated assumptions. The selected specimen and cohort means are different denominators, and its blood concentration is not recovered by this calculation. ## The carrier alternative is concentration- and cell-dependent

Human OCTN1 yielded apparent **Km 21 µmol/L** (95% CI 16–28) in an expression line. In the distinct SLC22A15 expression assay, the five-minute apparent Km was **354 ± 95 µmol/L** (mean ± SD), a central ratio of about **17**. The SLC22A15 study's multiple-comparison analysis supported uptake by SLC22A4 and SLC22A15. SLC22A5 gave a weak signal in a pairwise comparison but not in the multiple-comparison test. The older selectivity paper tested rat OCTN2 and found little ergothioneine uptake under its assay conditions. Carnitine handling is a comparator and should not be assumed to calibrate ergothioneine entry. [OCTN1 primary results, Figs. 2–4](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0408624102) · [SLC22A15 primary results, Fig. 3](https://doi.org/10.1096/fj.202001497R)

If the independent adult plasma mean of **107.4 ng/mL** were all available outside a cell, division by 229.3 g/mol yields **0.47 µmol/L**. The central `s/(Km+s)` terms would be **2.2%** and **0.13%** of their own Vmax for OCTN1 and SLC22A15 respectively. This calculation is a standardized concentration comparison. It does not recover the turnover per carrier, abundance, sodium effects, intracellular binding or direction across the blood–brain barrier. Low fractional saturation can coexist with large accumulation. The OCTN1 study reports water-space estimates of **850 versus 2.6 µmol/L** after 60 minutes at 10 µmol/L extracellular parent in expressing and control cells. This expression-system result cannot be transferred quantitatively to WWOX. [Adult plasma method](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23494799/) · [OCTN1 uptake and efflux results](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0408624102)

The mouse nuclear WWOX panel gives an additional testable distinction. SLC22A15 rises in assigned oligodendrocyte precursor cells (**19.78 → 30.77 CPM, 18 → 39 UMIs**) and declines in mature oligodendrocyte nuclei (**27.79 → 12.69 CPM, 11 → 4 UMIs**). It also declines in the larger excitatory pool. Cell-stage contrasts use a single pooled genotype each and sparse counts, especially in mature oligodendrocytes. This supports comparing precursor, mature glia and postmitotic neurons for uptake and redox function before interpreting a culture-wide pool. [WWOX nuclear output](nuclei.tsv)

**Next check.** Quantify formulation-specific human plasma and erythrocyte time courses, and search for developmental CNS exposures with defined fraction and age. The mechanistic test should distinguish greater cellular retention from lower mitochondrial oxidative injury and restored function. Separate direct extracellular quenching, mitochondrial access and compartment-specific uptake. The clinical decision remains open pending a selective WWOX effect at measured exposure.
