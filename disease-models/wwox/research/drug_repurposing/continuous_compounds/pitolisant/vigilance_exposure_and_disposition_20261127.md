# Pitolisant, H3 engagement, vigilance and WWOX circuit disposition

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Decision.** Retain pitolisant as a conditional H3 and vigilance comparator. Defer pediatric WWOX antiseizure trial priority. Human PET establishes brain H3 engagement near the observed adult serum range. The adult EEG signal concerns a provoked endpoint, focal-seizure follow-up is open label, and a native rat hippocampal experiment raises a separate physiological cost. The receptor, state and exposure tests below would change the decision. [Registered prediction](pretest_20261127.md) · [Opening comparison](first_comparison_20261127.md)

## Exposure and circuit counterchecks

**Measured brain engagement.** Rusjan et al. studied **six healthy adults** in fixed order, placebo scan first, in a blinded within-person PET comparison. After a single administration of **40 mg pitolisant hydrochloride**, equivalent to **35.6 mg free base** using the FDA formulation relationship, mean apparent H3 occupancy was **84 ± 7%** (SD, range 77–94%) by Lassen analysis about three hours after administration. Excluding caudate and putamen gave **83 ± 10%**. Mean total serum concentrations were **16.6 and 25.1 ng/mL** at scan start and end. The mass-dose-corrected estimate in the paper is model-dependent. The apparent occupancy is evidence of engagement in healthy adult brain, with no localization to presynaptic receptor class, WWOX state or sustained seizure response. [Primary Methods, Results, Figs. 2–3 captions and targeted Discussion](https://bpspubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/bph.15067) · [FDA label §§3 and 12](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/211150s006lbl.pdf)

**Systemic scale.** Applying the FDA binding range of **91–96%** to the observed PET mean serum levels gives approximate free serum scales of **2.24–5.05 nM** at start and **3.39–7.64 nM** at end, using 295.85 g/mol for parent. The label adult steady-state total peak of **73 ng/mL** yields approximately **9.87–22.21 nM** by the same calculation. This carries a binding assumption across datasets and is a sensitivity range, without a measured brain-to-plasma unbound ratio. The single-dose pediatric narcolepsy PK study (**24 participants, 7–17 years**) reported geometric mean Cmax and AUC₀–₁₀h **2.2× and 2.0×** adult values at ages 12–17 and **3.4× and 3.6×** at ages 7–11. These factors apply to that comparison. Applying them to adult steady-state free concentrations would combine different designs. ## WWOX evidence and discriminating observation

The same held WWOX RNA pipeline was applied to Hrh3, Hrh1, Hrh2, Hdc, Hnmt and Slc18a2. Hrh3 knockout minus control in log₂(1 + abundance) was **−0.252** in P17 mouse cortex and **−0.253** in hippocampus. Single-library omissions retained the negative signs, with nearest-zero values around −0.012 and −0.002. Week-15 human organoid HRH3 was **+0.143**, with a sign-changing omission interval. Mouse Hrh1 decreases in cortex and hippocampus, while human organoid HRH1 increases. Thus a common receptor-transcript direction is unresolved. The hippocampal assignments give **94→58 HRH3 UMIs**, with lower CPM in excitatory and inhibitory assignments, and one pool per genotype. Transcript, target occupation and synaptic receptor location require separate measurements. The low organoid HDC signal likewise cannot establish physiological histamine tone or intact arousal circuitry. **Hypothesis.** H3 inverse agonism might alter WWOX recruitment through a combination of histamine release and afferent transmission. Arousal alone can change the amount of time spent in a high-discharge state. H3 autoreceptor-mediated histamine feedback and an H3 heteroreceptor effect on glutamate release are separately supported in other settings. Their directions and contributions in juvenile WWOX are unmeasured. [Histamine autoreceptor primary study](https://doi.org/10.1038/302832a0) · [Corticostriatal primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33068216/)

**State counterexample.** An illustrative two-state system has baseline event rates of 12 and 2 per hour, spending 60% of the time in the high-rate state. The crude rate is eight per hour. Moving to 20% time in the high-rate state yields four per hour without changing either within-state rate. Halving both rates while preserving the original state proportions also yields four per hour. Standardizing each post-exposure condition to the same baseline proportions gives **eight** and **four** per hour, respectively. These inputs are synthetic. The exercise identifies the estimate needed next. [Reproducible calculation](comparisons.tsv)

**Prediction to test.** At measured local exposure in juvenile WWOX versus corrected preparations, use standardized baseline state proportions and report within-state rates for spontaneous discharges. In the preclinical comparison, use the same evoked input, confirm H3 dependence with an independent receptor control and document histamine tone. Record late NMDA-sensitive recruitment, early evoked responses, inhibitory charge and a direct release measure. Pair in vivo EEG/video vigilance with local physiology in an appropriate preparation. Treat animals or independent preparations as the replication unit.

- A genotype-preferential reduction in the late response persists within comparable vigilance states and with initial and inhibitory recruitment preserved. A matching H3-dependent slice result supports a local circuit contribution.
- A reduction confined to the crude EEG count, with unchanged within-state rates under matching and altered time awake, supports a vigilance explanation. Report that outcome as a distinct domain.
- Loss of physiological output or worsened late recruitment as peak exposure rises would deprioritize a seizure mechanism. A direct response only under unmatched histamine tone or regional spread requires resolving the local comparator before translation.

**Pediatric feasibility and reopen condition.** The FDA narcolepsy indication reaches age six. The evidence does not supply a WWOX developmental exposure or outcome, particularly in younger children. QT effects, CYP2D6 inhibitor and CYP3A4 inducer exposure effects, baseline therapy and sleep-state-dependent measurement are material design variables. Reopen for a selective, state-matched WWOX effect at confirmed free exposure with preserved evoked output in the same experiment. An independent review could materially change the decision if it locates a controlled spontaneous-seizure result, usable P04-07 denominators with a different effect, or held exposure-matched WWOX physiology. [FDA label §§1, 5, 7, 8.4 and 12](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/211150s006lbl.pdf)
