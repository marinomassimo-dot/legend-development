# 2-Deoxy-D-glucose opening comparison

The completed comparison parses the posted adult exposure and the original slice denominators. [Completed comparison and disposition](glycolytic_reserve_and_disposition_20261127.md)

| Preparation | Opening observation | Boundary for WWOX |
|---|---|---|
| Acute seizure-threshold studies in mice, with a separate fully kindled rat arm | The authors report higher threshold in the mouse 6-Hz test and lower threshold in the electroshock, PTZ and kainate tests. The nominal dose ranges overlap. The rat kindling response was a separate endpoint. | Protection depends on the seizure model. A single direction cannot be imported into WWOX. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20491877/) |
| CA3 slices under near-baseline and high extracellular potassium assays | The primary abstract reports no detected spontaneous PSC effect under the baseline potassium condition. Under high potassium the reported reductions in inward excitatory and outward inhibitory charge were 71% and 40%. | Excitation and inhibition both change in the provoked preparation. Inhibitory preservation, direct release and physiological recovery in WWOX require their own measurements. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30673364/) |
| Separate neurosteroid and epigenetic precedents | Independent experiments connect 2-DG to neurosteroid-dependent tonic GABA signaling and to NRSF/CtBP-associated changes in experimental epilepsy progression. | These assays use different endpoints and time scales. They leave the mediator of a WWOX response open. [Tonic inhibition study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27735054/) · [Kindling and chromatin study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17041593/) |
| Registered adult epilepsy pharmacokinetic study | Completed with nine enrolled, minimum eligibility age 18. Posted primary results cover systemic exposure AUC and secondary results cover other blood PK parameters. | The registry addresses systemic pharmacokinetics. Pediatric brain exposure and efficacy remain unmeasured there. [NIH record and results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05605301) |

**Replay the opening contrast.** The fractional charges remaining relative to the separate initial charge readouts in the high-potassium assay are 0.29 excitatory and 0.60 inhibitory. Their ratio is about 0.48. This normalized response ratio does not give the absolute excitatory-to-inhibitory charge in slices. It also shows why a favorable relative change can coexist with a substantial inhibitory loss. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30673364/)
