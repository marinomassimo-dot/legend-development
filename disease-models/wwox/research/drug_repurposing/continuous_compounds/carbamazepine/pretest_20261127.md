# Carbamazepine | Prespecified test of completed cargo degradation

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Selection.** Investigate carbamazepine as the sole active compound. The adjacent WWOX dossiers already cover acute sodium-channel modulation, myo-inositol-dependent PIP2 recovery, and generic cargo turnover. The new question is whether the parent antiseizure drug improves completed cargo degradation in a developmentally matched WWOX neural preparation at an exposure that preserves membrane signaling and physiological recruitment. Adjacent public cell studies hold opposing autophagy interpretations. Log the active epoxide separately.

**Primary outcome and countertest.** First document a baseline WWOX deficit in completed degradation after controlling for viable cell count, developmental stage, starting cargo and lysosomal pH. At matched measured free parent and epoxide exposure, a true recovery predicts increased independent cargo turnover with a lysosomal block-sensitive increment, restored evoked recovery after washout and preserved initial recruitment. A rise in LC3 puncta without increased cargo disposal, or loss of sustained PIP2/Kv7 recovery, lowers priority. Compare myo-inositol add-back at the same initial activity and free drug. Loss of the flux gain with add-back supports dependence on inositol depletion. Preservation of acute burst suppression without persistent cargo or functional recovery supports separate sodium-channel pharmacology.

**Freeze.** Keep LC3 and p62 abundance, their turnover rates and independent native cargo loss separate. Treat static RNA as a plausibility screen only. Keep measured free plasma, CSF, tissue and in vitro nominal exposure on separate axes. The unit is a biological replicate (animal, donor/independent differentiation as applicable). Assay-positive controls from other tissues can qualify an experimental branch, and leave WWOX mechanism, efficacy and pediatric feasibility unresolved.

**Fixed RNA panel.** SLC5A3 (SMIT1), SLC5A11 (SMIT2) and SLC2A13 (HMIT) for alternative inputs. ISYNA1 and IMPA1 for endogenous synthesis and recycling. CDIPT, PIP5K1C and KCNQ2 for lipid synthesis and excitability linked to PIP2. Inspect omission sensitivity and the marker-assigned neuronal pools, with an explicit missing or sparse annotation. Reuse the held trehalose results for initiation and lysosome transcripts. More RNA or transport redundancy cannot establish local inositol, channel function or completed autophagy.
