# Native TRP expression check for cannabidivarin

Before comparing WWOX RNA, choose TRPV1 as the primary target, TRPV2 and TRPA1 as channel alternatives tested in the same pharmacology study, TRPV3 and GPR55 as developmental and off-target context. Use three held untreated bulk comparisons and the pooled GSE158684 cell assignments. A consistent directional RNA signal requires mapped TRPV1, a stable nonzero leave-one-library-out direction in neuronal deletion cortex and hippocampus, and interpretable detection in the human organoids. Sparse bulk changes with means below 1 TPM or CPM and pooled bins below ten gene counts per arm are descriptive presence checks only. A pooled library per genotype cannot establish per-animal reproducibility. A lack of bulk detection does not exclude TRPV1 in a rare cell population or at a different stage. These are transcript data and do not measure native current, desensitization or pharmacological engagement.
