# Cannabidivarin | Age-dependent TRPV1 pharmacology versus network depression

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Opening checkpoint.** Cannabidivarin (CBDV, GWP42006) is the sole compound under active analysis. It is an investigational antiseizure compound with an adult randomized focal-seizure trial and a small pediatric Rett epilepsy study. The held WWOX age-window note already compared the P10 and P20 provoked-seizure studies at abstract depth. The new question is whether age-related target sensitivity transfers to the WWOX circuit at achievable free exposure. The held cannabidiol dossier and the earlier Presidio design serve only as comparators. [Adult randomized trial](https://doi.org/10.1089/can.2020.0075) · [Pediatric Rett study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35364618/) · [Prior age-window record](../../advisor_pilot_20261113/brainstorm_20261116/adv054_age_window_sci_20261118/ADV054_AGE_WINDOW_PER_CANDIDATE_SCI_20261118.md)

**Prespecified prediction.** In stage-matched WWOX-deficient and corrected neural preparations, a TRPV1-desensitization component should show an early channel activation signal followed by a reduced response to a standardized TRPV1 challenge and relatively selective suppression of late burst maintenance at verified free exposure. Compare the same initial recruitment across conditions, or carry the reduction of first recruitment as a network-depression alternative. Genotype and TRPV1 perturbation are separate factors. Persistence of the late effect with verified TRPV1 loss or failure of physiological recruitment shifts interpretation toward a TRPV1-independent action. Separate juvenile cortex from hippocampus and evoked from spontaneous activity. [Primary TRP and Mg²⁺-free slice study](https://doi.org/10.1021/cn5000524)

**First result to track.** In Huizenga et al. Fig. 7, at P20 the incidence of provoked tonic hindlimb extension on CBDV was 3/8 for TRPV1-intact mice and 10/10 for knockouts. The figure caption gives controls 8/8 and 9/9, whereas the main result sentence gives an ambiguous 8/8 wording for the two vehicle groups. Retain the caption denominator for the knockout control with that discrepancy explicit. Data support a model-specific contribution of TRPV1. They leave neonatal transfer, chronic spontaneous epilepsy, route-level selectivity and WWOX engagement open. [Primary data, §3.5 and Fig. 7](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6424614/#F7)

**Descriptive replay.** Use incidence of tonic extension, with each mouse the experimental unit. Treated minus vehicle risk was `3/8 − 8/8 = −0.625` in WT and `10/10 − 9/9 = 0` in TRPV1-null mice. The observed risk-difference contrast was −62.5 percentage points. With a ceiling response in both controls, this comparison cannot rule out differences in baseline seizure threshold. The main text's uncertain vehicle KO denominator does not change the descriptive difference and would matter for an inferential model.
