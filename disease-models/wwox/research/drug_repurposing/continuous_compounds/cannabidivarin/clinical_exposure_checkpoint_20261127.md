# Cannabidivarin | Initial human exposure and TRPV1 comparison

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Data, pediatric exposure.** The Rett phase 1 cohort included five girls aged 6.0–13.7 years, four with an initial PK curve. The four measured first-exposure parent Cmax values span 3.6–14.1 ng/mL, equivalent to 0.0126–0.0492 µM total plasma at 286.41 g/mol. Their arithmetic mean is 7.375 ng/mL or 0.02575 µM. The same first-exposure blood series measured 7-OH-CBDV at 4.7–106 ng/mL and 7-COOH-CBDV at 850–2948 ng/mL. The higher carboxyl concentration does not establish an active contribution at TRPV1. One of the four reported first-curve AUC measurements ends early. End-of-treatment trough has five listed parent concentrations. Their mean is 6.44 ng/mL, while the table's 5.3 ng/mL mean matches the four columns with first-exposure PK (5.275). The article's trough range uses all five. This is a descriptive denominator discrepancy and is not an exclusion criterion for future work. [Rett trial, Methods, PK Results and Table 6](https://doi.org/10.1111/epi.17247) · [Molecular mass](https://precision.fda.gov/ginas/app/ui/substances/c07ef76d-cb84-44bb-9ff3-b1651db7cf0e)

**Countercheck, adult comparative efficacy and tolerability.** The adult trial randomized 81 per arm, all in the primary analysis. The eight-week focal-seizure ratio was 0.95 (95% CI 0.78–1.17, p=0.648). At least one treatment-emergent adverse event was reported by 59/81 versus 39/81 (difference 24.69 percentage points) and discontinuation for such an event by 11/81 versus 2/81 (11.11 points). The separate adult PK cohort registered concomitant antiseizure drug monitoring. The standalone US PK record lists Cmax and AUC in the protocol and has no posted results. The combined EU record posts safety and efficacy but supplies no concentration table for an adult Cmax bridge. The uncontrolled pediatric Rett study is an exposure precedent only. Four of its five participants reduced baseline antiseizure therapy after CBDV initiation, and both decreases and increases appeared in recorded epileptiform activity. [Adult trial, Methods, Results and Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1089/can.2020.0075) · [US adult PK registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02369471) · [EU public trial results, distinct PK and efficacy cohorts](https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/ctr-search/trial/2014-002594-11/results) · [Pediatric Rett study](https://doi.org/10.1111/epi.17247)

**Comparator, target and native network.** In the 2014 Iannotti study, rat recombinant TRPV1 and TRPV2 desensitization was examined at nominal 10 µM in HEK293 cells. Male Wistar Kyoto rat CA1 slices at P24–28 in Mg²⁺-free solution used the same nominal concentration. CBDV reduced duration to 87% and amplitude to 74% of paired baseline. The antagonist condition retained the amplitude effect (69% of baseline) and the authors described attenuation of the duration effect. Capsaicin alone did not reproduce the duration effect. The CBDV arm was 33 electrodes from five slices from three rats. The methods specify an approximately one-minute arrival time followed by ten minutes of recording in the presence of the compound. Electrode-based comparisons do not establish animal-level replication or full mediation. This comparison is distinct from the P20 mouse MES knockout result. [Iannotti et al., author proof, Figs. 2 and 7 and MEA Methods, pp. C, F–G, I](https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/37398/1/cn5000524_final.pdf)

**Inference.** Ten micromolar nominal divided by the mean first-exposure pediatric total plasma Cmax yields approximately 388. This is a scale comparison between different experimental matrices and times. It does not give the free neural exposure or the minimum concentration for target engagement. Metabolite activity, adult and pediatric steady-state neural exposure, assay binding in each compartment, native sensitization, and duration dependence all remain unresolved. The primary contrast should separate early recruitment and burst amplitude from duration under verified TRPV1 perturbation.

**Native WWOX check.** In 15-week human cerebral organoids, TRPV1 was 8.03 versus 11.79 CPM (two WT and four KO libraries). The KO-minus-WT mean log₂(1 + CPM) difference was +0.497, with leave-one-library-out range +0.390 to +0.604. This is a detectable human organoid lead within one parental cell-line background. Per-library omission does not test robustness across independent human donors or permit a direct whole-clone sensitivity without library-to-clone assignments. In P17 neuronal deletion tissues the corresponding mean TRPV1 expression was 0.028 versus 0.179 TPM in cortex and 0.011 versus 0.045 in hippocampus. These are below the [prespecified bulk floor](trp_expression_pretest.md), and the hippocampal log effect reverses with omission. The pooled GSE158684 excitatory classes have five versus two TRPV1 UMIs (1,596 versus 1,262 assigned nuclei), with a single library pool for each genotype and no reliable within-class direction. For comparison, TRPV2 was more abundant in both mouse bulk preparations, declined with KO in each, and showed a smaller change in the other direction in organoids below the bulk floor. **Decision and next action.** Keep CBDV active for one exposure-and-mechanism boundary check, with pediatric WWOX trial priority deferred. Target engagement has higher marginal value in the human organoid preparation than a claim inferred from sparse juvenile mouse TRPV1 bulk RNA. Seek primary parent and metabolite activity, binding and brain exposure data. Use that evidence to bound the free-exposure comparison before deciding whether to prioritize a direct TRPV1 current/desensitization measurement in stage-matched human isogenic cultures. Pair it with late burst duration at preserved initial recruitment. An isolated amplitude change under confirmed TRPV1 interruption does not support that mechanism. Independent review could change the decision if free exposure and activity overlap or a different model shows reproducible endogenous channel engagement.
