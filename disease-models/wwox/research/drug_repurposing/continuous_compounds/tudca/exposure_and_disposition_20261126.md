# TUDCA · intact central parent, mechanism and disposition

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN · targeted source reads. [Primary Echs1 replay and opening WWOX screen](README.md)

**Disposition.** Defer priority for a pediatric WWOX trial. Retain TUDCA as a comparator for ER cargo recovery, stress adaptation and neuronal lineage progression. The new perfused-brain evidence establishes measurable intact parent in another biological context. There is no demonstrated WWOX rescue at measured intact neural exposure. A future head-to-head should distinguish an early local ER effect, altered bile-acid signaling and a proliferative shift that delays maturation.

**Oral TUDCA outside the combination.** The 12-adult randomized crossover report (Walsh, Fig. 2A) finds increased circulating TUDCA and UDCA at one **8 h** sample. The open text gives direction and significance. The original figure was unavailable through the permitted author-manuscript asset route in this pass, so no concentration or peak is inferred. In the progressive MS trial **54 randomized** and **47** eligible for modified-ITT analyses (26 TUDCA and 21 placebo), targeted serum metabolomics showed separate within-active-arm 16-week log changes for TUDCA **+2.52**, GUDCA **+2.30** and UDCA **+1.89**. These are within-arm slopes on the authors' scale. Separate placebo contrasts are in Table S4. The reported measures leave local parent-versus-product exposure unresolved. Clinical and fluid biomarker outcomes did not separate significantly in this exploratory setting. A larger adult ALS add-on trial missed its primary endpoint according to the sponsor's topline report. The results do not adjudicate developmental WWOX. ## What would distinguish the mechanisms

- **An early ER response.** Lebeau Fig. 4A shows partial preservation of ER calcium fluorescence in HuH7 after **500 µM** TUDCA pretreatment under two stressors. The TUDCA-only condition does not visibly reproduce the SERCA activator's increase in resting ER signal. The Fura-2 transient panel tests **4-PBA**. For WWOX, prioritize native cargo transit and luminal calcium in matched cultures before a stable lineage or stress shift. Baseline and evoked mitochondria/ATP responses distinguish ER filling from excessive retention that starves mitochondrial transfer. An effect limited to a chemical stressor challenge has weaker bearing on the untreated WWOX defect. [Lebeau Fig. 4](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbc.2021.100779) · [Held compartment-calcium countercheck](../dantrolene/README.md)
- **Generic protection is insufficient.** In the same independent Monteiro-Cardoso wild-type cortical culture, glutamate-only LDH was **143.4 ± 14.9%** of vehicle (n=10). With **100 µM TUDCA**, LDH was **116.3 ± 8.8%** (n=11), a **−27.1 pp** point contrast with reported **p=.4**. With **30 µM CDCA**, it was **76.3 ± 4.4%** (n=10), **−67.1 pp**, **p<.0001** against the shared glutamate arm. These comparisons have different concentrations and are not a TUDCA-vs-CDCA interaction test. The TUDCA estimate is imprecise and does not establish equivalence. The *Cyp8b1* perturbation changes a multianalyte pool, so higher TUDCA in a less injured brain does not identify the protective analyte. [Monteiro-Cardoso Fig. 3D–E](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jlr.2024.100712)
- **Lipid state and receptor alternatives.** Mouse neural stem cell lines exposed at **100 µM nominal** showed lower mitochondrial LCAD and higher nuclear SREBP-1. A separate partial LCAD knockdown raised Ki67 and lowered MAP2 RNA. More early proliferative cells can accompany slower maturation. That knockdown does not establish mediation of the TUDCA effect. In held WWOX bulk data, SREBF1 was lower in all three preparations, and ACADL, HADHA and CPT1A fell in mouse bulk but rose in the human organoid under every one-library omission. Mouse cortex and hippocampus can share model and samples. Organoid stage, tissue and species differ. Pooled nuclei also show an ACADL direction opposite to mouse hippocampal bulk in four assigned classes. RNA cannot sign oxidation flux or SREBP1 processing. In adult EAE, the oral benefit appeared in wild type, with no demonstrated benefit in global Gpbar1 knockout. That comparison and its endpoint do not localize the receptor-dependent effect to a neural compartment. In WWOX, check whether progenitor and cytokine changes follow parent-driven native cargo/calcium rescue or arrive with changes in GPBAR1 signaling, metabolite composition, oxidation or survival. [Neural stem cell comparator](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2020.00335) · [Independent receptor comparator](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/129401) · [Secondary WWOX panel](lipid_bulk.tsv)

**Reopen** when intact parent and metabolites are separated in the same matrix and experiment, and the measured parent window gives an early ER readout followed by more **absolute viable mature neurons** and improved evoked function after washout in WWOX relative to isogenic correction and vehicle. Include receptor competence, matched viable lineage starting counts, substrate oxidation and absolute cell output. A gain confined to restored survival, generic signaling or early proliferation changes the mechanism and the choice of trial endpoint. The concentration and durable phenotype are missing together. Independent review would change this disposition if it identifies a verified parent CNS exposure with an overlapping functional assay, or resolves the central versus peripheral attribution with relevant data. The exact fold mismatch in the endogenous mouse study alone would leave the priority decision unchanged.

**Pediatric feasibility.** The pediatric census yielded one enteral TUDCA neonatal report (PMID 12183720) in postsurgical patients on parenteral nutrition. Its free full text was inaccessible in this pass, so it is a historical exposure lead without adjudicated safety or PK denominators. Adult MS Table 3 records AEs in **10/26** versus **7/21**, including diarrhea in **3 versus 0** and one resolved cholangitis considered unlikely to be drug related. The trial excluded multiple pre-existing hepatobiliary conditions. These data leave developmental PK, chronic tolerability and concomitant antiseizure medicine effects unresolved. Any future prioritization requires a chemically defined oral preparation with reproducible exposure, independent parent/metabolite assays, an interpretable preclinical response and clinical endpoints that separate developmental function from symptomatic seizure change. [Neonatal study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12183720/) · [Adult MS safety and eligibility](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11911100/)
