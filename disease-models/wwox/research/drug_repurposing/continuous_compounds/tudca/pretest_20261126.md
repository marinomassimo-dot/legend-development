# Tauroursodeoxycholic acid · prespecified ER stress comparison

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · order 77 · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN · mouse P17 cortical and hippocampal TPM, human week-15 cerebral organoid CPM and pooled mouse hippocampal nuclei UMIs

**Question.** Can intact tauroursodeoxycholic acid (TUDCA, taurursodiol) relieve an ER-linked stress bottleneck and preserve production of new functional neurons in WWOX loss of function, with a distinguishable effect from bile-acid signaling or a change in which cells survive? Investigate the single defined taurine conjugate. Track TUDCA, unconjugated UDCA, GUDCA and liberated taurine separately as exposure or comparator species.

**Why now.** The existing PBA/TUDCA class record concerns partially rescuable protein alleles and does not establish a WWOX null response. A new single-molecule comparison asks whether the ER state is actionable under null loss of function. The inherited WWOX ER-calcium comparison and the new ECHS1 neural-progenitor study motivate a temporally ordered assay. Dantrolene's direct release mechanism and regulatory pharmacology are separate. ECHS1 deficiency also directly alters substrate metabolism. The comparison can calibrate a stress and lineage assay without importing the ECHS1-H3K9cr-FASN mechanism into WWOX. [Held missense class comparison](../../missense_recovery_it5_p8_20261029/README.md) · [Held ER-calcium comparison](../dantrolene/README.md) · [ECHS1 primary](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-75063-z)

**Frozen screen.** Compare ECHS1/FASN; PERK and ISR context EIF2AK3, EIF2S1, ATF4, DDIT3 and PPP1R15A; ER branches ERN1, XBP1, HSPA5, HSP90B1, HERPUD1 and DNAJB9; luminal calcium ATP2A2, RYR2 and CALR; receptor and transport context GPBAR1, NR1H4, SLC10A1, SLC51A and SLC51B. RNA does not identify relative pathway activation, phosphorylated eIF2α, spliced XBP1 or accessible intact drug. Single-library omission is a sensitivity comparison. The held model is pooled and small, with developmental windows between species only loosely aligned.

**Discriminating observation.** In matched WWOX and corrected neural progenitor preparations, ask whether measurable intact intracellular parent first restores luminal ER calcium or native cargo handling, followed by correctly timed stress-marker change and durable lineage output with preserved evoked function. Bile-acid-receptor dependence with a phenotype despite absent early cargo/calcium repair favors signaling. A lower average stress marker caused by selective disappearance of stressed cells predicts loss of absolute viable progenitors or neuron yield. Distinguish protective early translation slowing from late persistent stress. A stress marker shift without viable output or a circuit benefit cannot establish rescue. Apply the comparisons at measured exposure with a vehicle and congener identity control. Prioritize source-data extraction and analyte-specific CNS exposure before recommending an exposure window for an experimental compound comparison.
