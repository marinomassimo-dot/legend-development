**Primary comparator and counterproof.** I replayed the published source workbook, sheet **Figure 8**. In Echs1 conditional-null progenitor cells, untreated Atf4 is 1.335-fold and wild-type 0.978-fold. The 48 h drug effect is **−0.567 fold in cKO and −0.602 in WT**, giving an exploratory additive interaction of **+0.036 ± 0.130 SE** (three mice per arm). In vivo the fraction of BrdU-positive DG cells also positive for DCX went **42.16% → 69.05%** in cKO versus **70.49% → 72.48%** in WT, an interaction of **+24.90 ± 2.24 percentage points**. NeuN also shows a broader WT shift, **+16.82 pp in WT** and **+32.58 pp in cKO**, with the independent-arm interaction **+15.76 ± 2.54 pp**. The four mice per arm, scale choice and proportions among labelled cells preclude a durable absolute functional interpretation. Proliferation density was a separate endpoint. For lipid droplet diameter the replay gives 0.821 → 0.753 µm in cKO, with no cKO paired-arm significance bracket displayed in the original Fig. 8f. The individual droplets are not independently sampled mice. The tissue, genotype and parenteral model cannot assign CNS exposure or mechanism in WWOX. [Original Fig. 8 and source data](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-75063-z) · [Calculated contrasts](echs1_fig8_summary.tsv)

**First exposure boundary.** The FDA food-effect study with an adult combination formulation reported mean plasma intact TUDCA Cmax of **0.871 µg/mL fasted** and **0.836 µg/mL fed** (Table 5; time-matched baseline correction described for the concentration profile). At free-acid analyte mass 499.7 g/mol these are approximately **1.74 and 1.67 µM total plasma**. Applying the FDA report's measured **98.5% human plasma protein binding** yields illustrative free values of about **26 and 25 nM**, conditional on binding transferring to this cohort. The **100 µM** in-vitro Echs1 progenitor proliferation assay was a nominal bath and separate from the qPCR timeline. The rat combination PK study reported CSF TUDCA below quantification. This is a boundary from the FDA report and does not define zero brain exposure. Compare documented monotherapy, plasma timing and intact parent versus interconverting metabolites before using a free CNS range. [FDA clinical pharmacology, Table 5](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2022/216660Orig1s000ClinPharmR.pdf) · [FDA nonclinical review, PK summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2022/216660Orig1s000PharmR.pdf) · [Parent analyte mass](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/9848818)
