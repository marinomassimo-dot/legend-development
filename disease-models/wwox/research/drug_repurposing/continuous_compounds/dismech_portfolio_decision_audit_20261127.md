**Reproduce the E1 sensitivity.** With prior probability `p` on the positive state, subtract `max(3.498, p*3.90+(1-p)*3.17)` from `p*3.90+(1-p)*3.498`. Assumptions are binary and exhaustive states, a fixed tideglusib score, a common score scale, a risk-neutral choice of one option and perfect free information. Actual experiments have imperfect discrimination and their information value depends on sensitivity, specificity and cost. The report's numerical ranking is a record of its scoring exercise.

## One discriminator for each branch

| Branch | Data and inference | Hypothesis and discriminating observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GSK3 / tideglusib | Qualitative WWOX pSer9, lithium effect also in WT, and underdetermined time-dependent kinase and injury transfer. A pathway test needs direct activity and isoform-specific attribution. | In a stage- and genotype-matched WWOX preparation, compare tideglusib against a β-preferring orthogonal tool with measured α and β activity over time, preserved early recruitment and simultaneous late activity and injury. A basal β-substrate deviation and correction by both interventions favors the shared mechanism. A drug-only effect at comparable α/β activity, excess injury or an absent basal deviation lowers it. [Tideglusib comparison](tideglusib/engagement_and_disposition_20261127.md#decision-changing-comparison) |
| Neurosteroids / ganaxolone | WWOX has a steroid-pathway rationale and there is no eligible native neural steroid and chloride comparison. A reduction in a named steroid can have distinct functional effects depending on the mixture and native chloride. | Reconstruct measured stage- and region-matched WWOX and control steroid mixtures in native-chloride recordings. Compare physiological add-back of the depleted constituent and ganaxolone with controlled initial recruitment. Genotype-specific recovery of inhibitory efficacy after add-back favors a missing endogenous input. A similar ganaxolone response across genotypes favors general GABA-A modulation. [Ganaxolone comparison](ganaxolone/neurosteroid_replacement_and_disposition_20261127.md) |

**Order by marginal information.** Start with the GSK3 basal activity gate in a genotype- and maturity-matched preparation. It can close the specific correction hypothesis before screening the compound and currently has opposing acute and sustained findings to explain. Ganaxolone remains useful as a symptomatic comparator. Advance its replacement hypothesis when an identified CNS mixture and native inhibition can be compared. This sequence is a choice about the next measurement and carries no portfolio rank or clinical implication.
