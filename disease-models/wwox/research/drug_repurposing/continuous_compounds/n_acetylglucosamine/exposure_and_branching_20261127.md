# N-acetylglucosamine exposure, branching and functional myelin in WWOX loss

**Data scope.** P17 Wwox neuronal-deletion mouse bulk cortex and hippocampus in TPM, P17 mouse hippocampal pooled nuclear counts in UMI CPM, week-15 human WWOX-null organoids in library CPM, nominal mouse neural-stem-cell bath in mM, perfused mouse brain isotope signal, mouse callosal profiles per 105 µm² and human serum HexNAc in nM.

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. The [fixed pretest](pretest_20261127.md) distinguishes precursor supply, Golgi branching on already occupied sites, receptor trafficking and new functional myelin. The [D-mannose analysis](../d_mannose/neural_site_checkpoint_20261126.md) gives a competing Golgi processing explanation, while the [clemastine analysis](../clemastine/axonal_denominator.md) establishes why the same-tract axon denominator matters.

## Tracer and receptor scope

- **Perfused brain incorporates administered label.** Sy et al. detected labeled UDP-HexNAc in perfused adult brain and in P8 brains following maternal drinking-water exposure, with 3 per arm in adult brain and 4 per arm among pups in Fig. 2A. The method does not separate UDP-GlcNAc from UDP-GalNAc. It measures a nucleotide-sugar product after administration of ring-labeled GlcNAc, with no time-resolved assay of intact free GlcNAc in plasma, milk and brain interstitium. The label supports delivery of administered carbon to a brain-associated donor pool. It cannot resolve intact parent passage, deacetylation/recycling, cell type, subcellular location, fractional enrichment or net expansion of the unlabeled physiological pool. The tracer panel’s vertical scale is not labeled as a molar concentration. In the maternal comparison the dam/litter is the treatment unit, and the plotted pup count cannot establish the number of independent litters. [Sy et al., Fig. 2A, pp. 17415–17416 and targeted LC–MS/MS methods](https://edoc.mdc-berlin.de/id/eprint/19414/1/19414oa.pdf)
- **Surface signal and lineage markers, at high nominal concentrations.** Fig. 1A–D compared 0, 40 and 80 mM GlcNAc for 48 h in E12.5 medial ganglionic eminence cultures. L-PHA and surface PDGFRα increased, and so did selected O4/GalC lineage measures. Fig. 1 gives three technical replicates from representative experiments. Kifunensine and Mgat1/Mgat5 loss provide pathway controls for decreased branching and surface PDGFRα. The dose response measures surface abundance and lineage markers, with receptor internalization kinetics and target glycoform still needed in this preparation. A concentration-matched osmotic control and absolute surviving lineage counts are relevant to interpreting 40–80 mM. In a distinct E12 cortical progenitor study, Mgat5 deletion changed neuronal and astrocyte differentiation but did not detect a genotype difference in the already small oligodendrocyte yield. Region and cell state alter the readout. [Sy et al., Fig. 1 and Results](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.RA120.015595) · [Yale et al., Fig. 2 and Fig. S2B-associated Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10277816/)
- **Peripheral engagement has a different scale.** In Sy et al.’s open-label adult MS study, sequential cohorts remained on glatiramer acetate. Of 18 and 16 enrolled, 17 and 16 contributed to the Fig. 2 per-protocol biomarker comparison. Across the four treatment weeks, average serum HexNAc rose 65% and 112%, and L-PHA on activated CD4⁺CD25⁺ T-cell blasts increased about 3% and 7%. Serum LC–MS/MS does not resolve HexNAc isomers. Fig. 2 uses a 2,500 nM upper tick for group means and 8,000 nM for visit-averaged participant values. The 40 mM mouse cell bath is 16,000 and 5,000 times these respective axis ceilings. These ratios bound the contrast with averaged displayed values and neither estimate peak parent nor define an effective neural concentration. Eight of 16 participants in the higher cohort reported mild gastrointestinal symptoms, without serious events in the 34 enrolled. The open-label design, short period and lack of a treatment-period imaging or CSF endpoint bound what the human study can establish. [Adult trial, Results and Fig. 2A–H, pp. 6–8](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12974-023-02893-9)

## Development, injury and the axon denominator

The P3–P8 maternal treatment arm showed higher callosal MBP/FluoroMyelin staining (5 pups per arm, each represented by the average of three slices) and separate PDGFRα⁺-gated L-PHA and lineage measurements. This is developmental evidence in otherwise wild-type pups. The stains do not count first sheaths on surviving tracked axons or measure conduction. Adult inducible Mgat1 loss in the oligodendrocyte lineage reduced lineage and myelin markers after weeks, establishing branch-pathway relevance in those mice. It does not establish that a donor can bypass a branching enzyme limitation. [Sy et al., Fig. 2B–E](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.RA120.015595)

After cuprizone was stopped, adult Mgat5 heterozygotes treated for four weeks had more myelinated axon profiles and lower fitted g-ratio in the callosal sampling area. The schedule, panel headings, Results and 3G caption identify this as recovery, although a phrase in the Fig. 3F caption says active phase. In a different two-week wild-type cohort, rotarod performance and FluoroMyelin improved. Neither readout is direct axonal conduction, and the cohorts cannot be merged. From the published Fig. 3G bar edges, estimated **myelinated axons per 105 µm² field rose from 69.6 to 86.5**, while **total axons rose from 93.0 to 113.2**. Dividing like-for-like field means yields **74.8% to 76.5%** (+1.6 percentage points), with a sensitivity range of −3.3 to +6.5 points under independent two-pixel shifts of each bar top. This interval describes digitization only, without biological uncertainty. The figure counts **six fields in each of three animals per arm** and reports 18 observations per group for the count panels. The g-ratio plot uses individual axons (214 and 222 in the caption), also nested within three mice per arm. Supplementary Fig. S3A presents the same comparison as three animal means per group, with one-sided p=0.00510 for myelinated counts, p=0.0598 for total counts and p=0.0314 for g-ratio. Digitizing these points gives an independent **77.1% to 76.5%** ratio of group mean counts, a −0.5-point shift. The plots have no cross-panel animal identifiers. Across all possible pairings of a group's plotted total and myelinated measurements, the treated-minus-control difference of mean animal proportions ranges from about **−0.4 to +0.5 points** at the digitized values. This is a pairing sensitivity, with no biological uncertainty inferred. The two panels give slightly different digitized group means despite the supplement citing the same experiment, so the sources are reported separately. Neither plot gives a verified individual-animal myelinated proportion or first-sheath history. Greater survival or sampling density of myelinated profiles and sheath thickening could contribute. A ratio of group means cannot determine how many previously unensheathed axons first acquired a myelin sheath. [Sy et al., Fig. 3E–G and EM methods](https://edoc.mdc-berlin.de/id/eprint/19414/1/19414oa.pdf) · [Animal-level Fig. S3A](https://edoc.mdc-berlin.de/id/eprint/19414/2/19414suppl.pdf) · [Main panel coordinates](figure_coordinates.tsv) · [Supplement coordinates](supplementary_coordinates.tsv) · [Recalculation](external_results.tsv)

**Genetic counterexample.** Jiménez-Sánchez et al. provided the same nominal drinking-water concentration (1 mg/mL) to Coq9-R239X mice from P30 for two months. At the symptomatic stage, beam-balance performance improved, the pole test showed a trend, and selected inflammatory markers decreased. The untreated mutant’s whole-brain UDP-GlcNAc was not deficient and declined following supplementation. The study did not detect recovery of MBP/MOG or mature-oligodendrocyte markers and observed lower bulk PDGFRα under treatment. A late bulk receptor decrease cannot establish the direction of an early OPC surface signal. The separate enzyme ratio is not a direct measurement of O-GlcNAc on target proteins. This model provides an alternative route from drug exposure to motor behavior without demonstrating myelin rescue. The correction concerns author affiliations. [Coq9 comparator, Figs. 3–4 and treatment methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12913237/) · [Correction](https://doi.org/10.1111/apha.70201)

## Pediatric precedent and disposition

The TAME multiarm trial randomized 125 children with complicated severe acute malnutrition, aged 6–59 months, including 24 to GlcNAc and 25 to standard care (24 and 23 in the Table 3 endpoint summary). Over 14 treatment days the primary fecal inflammation score contrast for GlcNAc was **−0.20 (90% CI −1.01 to +0.60, p=0.67)** versus standard care. The secondary diarrhea rate ratio was **0.11 (90% CI 0.04–0.33)**. Across the trial, no adverse event was judged related to study product, and children with impaired consciousness or feeding conditions were excluded. These findings provide a short oral administration precedent and a gastrointestinal outcome. They do not establish chronic tolerability, parent exposure or neural effects in WWOX. An older uncontrolled 12-child oral IBD series supplies an additional gastrointestinal administration precedent. [Randomized TAME trial, Methods and Tables 1, 3–4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11024201/) · [Salvatore et al., clinical Results, pp. 1572–1573](https://magistralbr.caldic.com/storage/product-files/861963135.pdf)

**Dato.** In untreated WWOX, MGAT1 bulk RNA falls in the two mouse regions and slightly in human organoids. MGAT5 moves in opposite directions between mouse tissue and human organoids. PDGFRA falls in bulk hippocampus and rises in the assigned OPC nuclear pool. These outcomes prioritize a lineage-resolved measurement and do not establish a shared UDP-GlcNAc deficit or a membrane-trafficking defect. [Reproducible WWOX comparison](README.md)

**Inferenza.** If Golgi access, early branch initiation or trafficking limits WWOX tissue, adding carbon can raise a cytosolic precursor or an alternative glycan pool with little surface-branch rescue. At least three competing readouts need to stay distinct: site occupancy, branch structure on an already occupied receptor, and receptor residence time. Parallel monitoring of O-GlcNAc and inflammatory state can identify an alternative response.

**Ipotesi e predizione discriminante.** At an exposure linked to measured free parent, a supply-limited WWOX OPC should take up more label into isotopologue- and epimer-resolved UDP-GlcNAc, followed by increased branching on an occupied surface glycopeptide, reduced PDGFRα internalization and more newly ensheathed viable axons. Compare initial axon and OPC counts separately in corrected and deficient partner-cell combinations with fixed developmental stage, pulse history and osmolarity. Record intact parent and isotope distribution in plasma, vascular/parenchymal fractions and lineage cells to distinguish delivery from metabolic recycling, using independent measurements of the ring and acetyl portions if needed. Track individual viable axons to first sheath, then confirm nodal organization and conduction in the same tract at matched axon caliber and recruitment. An early label increase without branch or trafficking correction favors a downstream constraint. A behavior or inflammatory improvement with unchanged new-sheath fraction and conduction favors a parallel response. Analyze animals or independent differentiations as replicates, with pups nested in litters where maternal exposure is assigned.

**Decision.** Park for an unselected pediatric WWOX trial. Retain as a preclinical substrate lever once a WWOX lineage deficit and achievable parent or precursor exposure have been measured. Reopen for a reproducible gain in newly ensheathed axons and conduction at matched lineage and survival, linked to measured donor and branch response. Rechecking the printed means would not resolve the missing animal-level denominator or neural exposure.
