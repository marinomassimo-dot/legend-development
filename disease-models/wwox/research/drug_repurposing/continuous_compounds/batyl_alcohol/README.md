# Batyl alcohol in WWOX loss of function

**Decision.** Retain defined racemic 1-O-octadecylglycerol as a conditional local supply comparator. Defer systemic pediatric WWOX development. Plasma and erythrocyte responses in ether-lipid disorders establish peripheral conversion. The decision for WWOX depends on absolute vinyl-ether product in viable recipients and delivery to functional first sheaths.

## Independent replay of the public injury deposit

The two [public source tables](https://doi.org/10.17632/ysymtcyj4s.1) cover different contrasts:

- The CSV has five samples per group and **no batyl-treated samples**. In untreated day-28 injury, the sum of 17 PE P-18:0 signals is **0.561×** sham, and the sum of 17 PE P-16:0 signals **0.586×** sham. Single-sample omissions preserve both decreases. The results support a broad injury-associated deficit in this deposited scale. They do not identify an exclusive C18:0 shortage.
- The treatment XLSX contains 53 rows with signed normalized values. Both early sham sets have only generic labels, so the sham-treatment comparison uses six explicit day-28 samples per arm. The injured groups have 6/5 samples at day 1 and 6/6 at days 3 and 28. On the published workbook's transformed scale, day-28 injury contrasts are **+0.0647 ePC** and **−0.00925 ePE**, with one-sample omission intervals **[+0.0287,+0.0906]** and **[−0.0131,−0.0049]**. Time-matched sham differences **+0.0070** and **−0.00110** each cross zero under omissions. The three source columns named PE P-38:4, P-38:4.1 and P-38:4.2 have different signs or robustness and cannot be assigned to an intact C18:0 sn-1 product by sum composition alone.
- **Normalization prevents replay of an absolute rescue.** In the entire treated matrix the largest absolute ePL entry is **9.75 × 10⁻¹¹**, while ePC and ePE have **r = −0.989**. This is consistent with an aggregate constraint and leaves the scale needed to verify absolute class changes unavailable. The sign of transformed ePE refers to this deposited scale. Fig. 4B reports fold changes against sham from tissue-normalized abundance, not differences of the centered treatment values. The untreated CSV cannot supply missing absolute totals for other animals. [Quantitative reconstruction](lipid_comparisons.tsv)

## Native WWOX comparison

**Data.** Neuronal Wwox deletion is sufficient for hypomyelination and lower conduction in a juvenile mouse model. That causal result locates an important initiating defect in neurons and leaves open lipid limitation in glial recipients. The fixed four-source RNA replay shows **FAR1** lower in P17 cortex (**8.16→6.12 TPM**), hippocampus (**14.25→9.24 TPM**) and week-15 human organoids (**14.52→11.13 CPM**), with direction retained under single-library omissions. **AGPS** is lower in hippocampus and organoids with the cortical sign unstable. **TMEM189** falls in cortex (**79.0→65.9 TPM**) and rises in mixed human organoids (**35.1→46.0 CPM**). The fixed ≥1-unit floor excludes organoid **AGMO**. Controls and KO comprise 4/3 cortex, 2/3 hippocampus and 2/4 organoid libraries. One P17 hippocampal nuclear pool per genotype places detectable AGPS and FAR1 in assigned oligodendroglial cells. Within MFOL, FAR1 falls from 242 to 130 CPM and AGPS rises from 94 to 130 CPM. This is a pooled descriptive contrast. Neither expression nor oligodendrocyte proportions demonstrate a specific lipid deficit or pathway direction. [Neuronal deletion and myelination](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awab174) · [Bulk](native_bulk.tsv) · [Pool assignments](cell_types.tsv) · [Composition](mixture.tsv)

**Inference.** Measure whether the proposed upstream supply deficit is large enough to affect functional sheaths. GNPAT/AGPS sit before a potential bypass by the preformed ether linkage. TMEM189 and downstream lipid handling are still needed for vinyl-ether production. A published precursor can enter peripheral lipids while transport into brain or conversion into the relevant species remains weak. The Gnpat and WWOX oligodendrocyte phenotypes share a sheath endpoint with different causal origins, as shown by the neuronal deletion comparison.

**Countercheck.** A vinyl ether lipid is not uniformly protective under oxidative stress. A primary study reported increased sensitivity to GPX4-inhibition-dependent injury after adding unsaturated ether species in its test systems. Chain, vinyl linkage and the oxidizable sn-2 lipid must stay explicit. This supports retaining a lipid peroxide readout without assuming ferroptosis in WWOX. [Zou et al., primary report](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2732-8)

## Pediatric feasibility

Das et al. report erythrocyte plasmalogen increases during oral 1-O-octadecyl-sn-glycerol exposure in congenital ether lipid deficiency. The abstract identifies a stereospecific precursor, and does not provide the age-specific central exposure, denominator for a safety rate or controlled neurologic outcome. The related RCDP observational registration listed a target of ten and proposed red-cell and neurologic follow-up. Its record lists no results. These are peripheral biological precedent and a historical research plan, respectively. The WWOX decision requires the exact formulation, central delivery, interaction and tolerability data, plus a measurable clinical functional endpoint. A blood signal alone cannot provide that bridge. [Das et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1630273/) · [RCDP trial registry](https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN44820021)

## One discriminating comparison

**Hypothesis.** An upstream lipid supply deficit contributes to short or unstable sheaths in at least one developing WWOX preparation. The rival is impaired conversion, glial recruitment or axonal eligibility with a secondary lipid change.

**Experiment.** Use reciprocal stage- and activity-matched WWOX/corrected neuron–oligodendrocyte preparations, with verified viable axons and recipient cells before intervention. First measure absolute chain-resolved P-18:0 plasmenyl-PE and its O-18:0 alkyl precursor. Compare traced parent access across a barrier with matched direct recipient exposure and the corresponding vehicle. In the same experiment, track the intact labeled sn-1 ether chain and glycerol backbone, validated alkyl and vinyl identities, and absolute product in recipients and newly formed sheaths. Use the labeling pattern to distinguish intact transfer from breakdown and reassembly. Include chain-normalized lipid peroxides, persistence of initial wraps after washout and conduction on the same axons.

- **Low baseline pool and recovery only when labeled parent reaches recipients**, plus restoration of persistent sheaths and conduction, support a supply contribution and identify delivery as a lever.
- **Intracellular labeled O-18:0 with little labeled P-18:0**, even on direct access, shifts the next experiment toward downstream conversion. **Restored absolute P-18:0 without functional recovery** shifts priority toward axonal or assembly limitation. Increased peroxides or poorer survival outweigh favorable marker movement.
- **Normal native pools or equal physiology with and without additional product** deprioritize this replacement mechanism for that preparation.

**Checkpoint.** The current public trauma deposit does not support an absolute-treatment replay. Independent review could change the status of that precedent by inspecting absolute, tissue-normalized per-animal values and matched isotope or lineage data. Even a verified injury result leaves the native WWOX prerequisite open. Prioritize the paired native lipid and function readout in INV-06 before a systemic comparison.
