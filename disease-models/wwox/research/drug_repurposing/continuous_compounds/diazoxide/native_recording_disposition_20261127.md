## Reanalysis of the same-cell cortical contrasts

**Data.** In the Figure 4 workbooks, the median within-cell increase in input conductance was **+0.588 nS** in nine regular-spiking glutamatergic cells (8/9 positive) and **−0.109 nS** in three fast-spiking Pvalb cells (1/3 positive). Omitting one cell at a time gave +0.540 to +0.652 nS and −4.065 to +0.527 nS, respectively. Median changes in membrane potential were −3.95 and −4.93 mV. In the authors' responsive-only subset, the absolute conductance direction across classes reversed (+0.652 nS in eight regular-spiking cells, +1.163 nS in the only retained Pvalb cell). Relative conductance changes retained the original ordering (+23.7% and +15.9%, medians). These medians describe the observed cells, with no claim of animal-level replication or cross-class equivalence. Source data 1, sheet Feuil1, columns R Ctrl, R Diazo, Vm Ctrl and Vm Diazo; classification and responsiveness from source data 2, joined by cell ID. [Replayed results](cortex_drug_minus_baseline.tsv)

**Inference.** The apparent direction of cortical cell selectivity depends on the metric and on outcome-based selection in a small sample. A single absolute conductance contrast and the hippocampal mIPSC frequency both leave the response of evoked cortical inhibition unresolved. Keep the two prior interpretations of the Matsumoto somatic EC50. The free archival route returned article metadata without the original graph, and either reading leaves the inhibitory-frequency half-effect at a lower concentration in that preparation.

**Hypothesis and discriminating observation.** A WWOX-selective window would appear at a common measured unbound exposure in a specified juvenile cortical region. Deficient excitatory KATP conductance would recover, late recurrent activity would fall, and PV recruitment, evoked inhibitory charge and early recruitment would remain within predefined equivalence bounds. Measure the conductance in an ATP-preserving configuration. A cell-type-specific KATP control can distinguish the putative mechanism from effects persisting without that current. Earlier loss of inhibitory charge would rule against a useful window, even when the late event count falls. Stratify baseline membrane and metabolic state, because the available pharmacology uses very different intracellular conditions.

## Pediatric feasibility

Herrera et al. studied 295 children with hyperinsulinism. Echocardiography identified pulmonary hypertension in 14 across all observation periods, including seven with documentation before treatment. Of 288 eligible children, six had first documentation during the exposure window or up to 28 days after discontinuation, and one more had it 78 days after discontinuation. None of these seven had an available pre-exposure echocardiogram for review. Study design, underlying cardiopulmonary risk and incomplete ascertainment prevent a drug-attributable WWOX rate. [Herrera et al., Factors associated with PH and edema](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6207144/)

Kizu et al. found no significant covariate effect on estimated apparent clearance or volume when they pooled five children receiving assorted antiseizure medicines in their 22-child serum PK study (Table 2, objective-function changes of 0.798 and 0.594, both reported nonsignificant). This is a limited pooled estimate. It leaves individual interactions, unbound pediatric CNS exposure and similar exposure at an acceptable systemic safety margin unresolved. Existing oral use for hyperinsulinemic hypoglycemia and the FDA pulmonary hypertension warning for neonates and infants remain formulation- and population-specific. [Kizu et al., Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1159/000478696) · [Official oral suspension label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/017453s025lbl.pdf)

It uses paired difference in input conductance (1000/Rm nS for Rm in MΩ), includes all classified cells, and reports sensitivity to one-cell omission and author-defined responsiveness. Reopen on a region- and stage-matched WWOX comparison with verified free drug and intact evoked inhibition or on a separate native-current dataset with a qualified exposure window. That result would merit independent review. This checkpoint closes the held-data question.
