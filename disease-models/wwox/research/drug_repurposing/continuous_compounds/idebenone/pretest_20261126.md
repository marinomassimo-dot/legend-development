# Idebenone · prespecified WWOX comparison

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · order 75 · recorded before the new expression calculation

**Question.** In WWOX loss of function, can reduced parent supply electrons to complex III and restore coupled ATP production, or does limited quinone reduction leave oxidized parent, lower respiratory reserve or only change a peroxide marker? This is distinct from replenishing endogenous long-chain CoQ10 and from generic NRF2 induction. The small mixed WWOX mitochondrial and redox contrasts motivate testing and leave the site of rate limitation open.

Screen prespecified gene sets in the held P17 mouse cortex and hippocampus, week-15 human cerebral organoids, and assigned mouse hippocampal nuclei. Use NQO1/NQO2 and NRF2/KEAP1 for catalytic and regulatory capacity, pentose-phosphate and cytosolic reducing-equivalent enzymes G6PD, PGD, IDH1 and ME1, respiratory references NDUFS2/NDUFV1 and UQCRC1/UQCRFS1, and AIFM2/FSP1 as a native quinone-reduction alternative. Keep TPM/CPM, mapped zeros and the single pooled nuclear denominator explicit. Compare excitatory and inhibitory nuclei, astrocytes, microglia and oligodendroglial states. The redox ratio and catalytic capacity require direct measurements.

**Falsifiable predictions.** Measure intracellular oxidized/reduced parent, ATP-linked OCR and ATP recovery in WWOX and corrected cultures at comparable evoked demand. True bypass predicts that formation of reduced parent is required for improved respiration despite a separable complex I restriction, with retained downstream complex III capacity and loss of the incremental response under a selective NQO1-dependence control. An OCR rise without ATP recovery favors uncoupling or another redox sink. Lower phospholipid oxidation without respiratory rescue favors a separate redox function. Predominantly oxidized parent with suppressed basal complex-I-linked ATP and a disproportionate deficit in neurons would argue against priority. A mixed coculture can appear rescued by astrocytes alone; resolve the responding compartment and test evoked neural recovery.

**Primary comparators.** Jaber et al. rat cortical neurons versus astrocytes (PMID 32350039), patient NQO1 variants and treatment-response context in LHON (PMID 38272025), and reduced-parent redox cycling (PMID 21483849). Use adolescent LHON formulation experience separately from infant WWOX exposure, brain penetration, co-medication and efficacy. Match intact parent, redox state, tissue, age, dynamic response and assay conditions before any pediatric implication. Continue only while the data can adjudicate a genotype-selective functional benefit.
