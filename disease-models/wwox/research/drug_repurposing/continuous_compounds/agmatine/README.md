# Agmatine sulfate, voltage-dependent NMDA block and spontaneous recurrence in WWOX loss of function

28 November 2026 UTC. context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. Continuous compound #195, INV-05.

**Disposition.** Retain as a conditional ex vivo comparator. Defer pediatric WWOX priority. The [pretest](pretest.md) compares direct NMDA current block with NMDA–PSD95–nNOS signaling, altered release and metabolic fate. The direct current and the spontaneous circuit outcome can change in different directions.

**Direct biochemical countercheck.** Recombinant human AGMAT variants R105 and G105 hydrolyzed guanidinobutyrate and other acidic guanidino substrates. Agmatine hydrolysis was undetected under the tested conditions (Fig. 1B and Fig. 2C, 10 mM substrate for the survey). The AGMAT label is therefore insufficient to use its expression as a human agmatine-to-putrescine flux proxy. Keep possible alternative enzymes, independent ornithine-pathway flux and net pool changes distinct. A tracer would be needed to claim conversion of administered parent in the relevant tissue. [Sinn et al., original enzymology](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-26655-4)

The held juvenile cortical WWOX d-APV experiment supports a contribution of NMDA current to a burst phenotype. That endpoint does not distinguish direct channel block from downstream signaling or suppressing physiological recruitment at the same time. The memantine comparison already identifies free concentration, voltage and magnesium as necessary controls. Agmatine adds a distinct scaffold and a direct spontaneous EEG caution. ## Exposure and reproducible voltage comparison

- **Mass and route.** The parent mass is 130.19 g/mol, distinct from 228.27 g/mol for the sulfate. In the rat IV terminal study, mean total brain Cmax is **3,138 ng/g = 24.10 µmol/kg** at the first measured peak time, 15 min. Spinal cord Cmax is **814 ng/g = 6.25 µmol/kg** at 120 min. Brain-to-plasma is **0.0529 mL/g** at the common 15-min peaks and **0.3966 mL/g** by the reported AUCs. The longer AUC ratio describes different time dependence. It does not estimate unbound brain-to-plasma partition. The labeled spinal AUCs give 0.294 by division versus 0.291 in the source table. Use the published 0.291 when quoting its model estimate. The separate oral rat arms provide total plasma Cmax of 36.33 and 111.33 µM and bioavailability estimates of 29% and 35%. No oral brain value follows from these arms. [Clements et al., Figs. 2–3 and Tables 5–6](https://doi.org/10.1124/jpet.123.001828) · [Substance identity](https://precision.fda.gov/ginas/app/ui/substances/b68a0f87-beaa-458f-bdb3-6f1485ba099f) · [Exposure replay](rat_exposure.tsv) · [Partition replay](rat_partition.tsv)
- **Separate oral brain evidence.** In WT mice after a week of repeated oral exposure, hippocampal total agmatine was reported about **2–7 times** control at 30 min after the last exposure (n=4–5/group). Plasma rose much more, approaching 500-fold at the highest tested condition by the authors' description. No perfusion preceded hippocampal collection, which leaves an intravascular contribution. Hippocampal putrescine fell while plasma putrescine rose. An independent 15-week WT/APP/PS1 experiment sampled 24 h after the last oral exposure (n=13–16/group), a distinct endpoint. Neither study measures interstitial free parent or a labeled reaction pathway. [Bergin et al., Figs. 3–4 and 7, Methods and Discussion](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-49078-0)

The model uses Kd(V) = 952 exp(z × 0.62 × V/25.69) µM, and a separate Mg term m = [Mg]/3.57 × exp(−0.062 V). It tests two simple limiting assumptions for the joint term. With independent block, available current is proportional to 1/[(1+m)(1+a)]. With mutually exclusive block it is proportional to 1/(1+m+a), where a = [agmatine]/Kd(V). It fixes agonist conductance, a reversal of 0 mV and illustrative levels of Mg. Neither joint law has been measured for this compound in WWOX. Inputs z=1 or 2 bracket alternative effective charge assumptions. [Native parameter abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9918557/) · [Mg primary comparison](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.10-09-03178.1990) · [Voltage replay](voltage_replay.tsv)

At an illustrative bath input of 24.10 µM, **not a conversion of measured total tissue into interstitial exposure**, z=2, 1 mM Mg and −65/−35 mV, the independent model predicts losses in arbitrary current units of **1.43/1.22**. The exclusive model gives **0.13/0.39**. The ordering reverses even with the same agonist conductance. Fractional block in the absence of Mg does not determine which window loses more current. Real waveform, Mg and channel-state dependence need to be measured. This is a sensitivity calculation and cannot estimate an antiseizure effect.

## Pediatric feasibility and decision-changing comparison

The adult radiculopathy study evaluated dietary agmatine sulfate as a short oral intervention. During its randomized 14-day phase, 51/48 were assigned and 31/30 analyzed. Gastrointestinal events were reported in the separate dose-escalation study. These are adult peripheral pain and tolerability data, with substantial reduction between randomization and analysis. The bounded search identified no usable pediatric epilepsy intervention or free juvenile brain exposure after this formulation. Product identity, free parent and active co-exposures, interaction with background antiseizure drugs, and effects on vigilance and EEG would require independent study. [Keynan et al., original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20447305/)

**Hypothesis and one test.** In developmentally matched WWOX-LoF and corrected preparations, first measure NMDA currents on early and late voltage trajectories with physiological Mg. Within each genotype, match agmatine and an NMDA reference on the absolute decrement in early NMDA charge at the same baseline drive, and report fractional block separately. Leave late charge and spontaneous recurrence free as outcomes. Record first fast excitatory and inhibitory recruitment and local NO or cGMP. For the residual signaling contrast, compare NO or Ca²⁺ at matched measured total NMDA charge in the current assay. Include validated PSD95–nNOS disruption with sufficient baseline dynamic range. Track parent and turnover if a residual delayed effect survives current and NO matching.

- A directly measured, reversible NMDA current loss persisting after scaffold disruption supports a pore component. It favors a useful circuit hypothesis only if first recruitment is preserved and spontaneous recurrence falls at the same free exposure.
- A residual network or Ca²⁺ effect at matched current that shrinks with scaffold disruption supports a coupled signaling component. If delayed effects instead track labeled metabolite production, revisit substrate handling. A larger early loss, higher spontaneous recurrence or an effect only at supra-local exposure lowers priority.
- If the native model lacks an excess late NMDA or NO component at baseline, stop this comparison. Reopen priority only with measured juvenile unbound exposure and a selective benefit on spontaneous EEG alongside preserved initial function. An independent review could change the decision if it identifies a credible free concentration with a sign-resolved native joint Mg/current measurement or detects a classification change for the spontaneous WAG/Rij result.
