# OV329 and persistence of functional inhibition in WWOX loss of function

**Selection.** OV329 is an investigational GABA aminotransferase inactivator. The H02 class card and ADV-276 have already examined the myelin evidence and the mouse eye/brain concentration comparison. This increment moves to the duration of pharmacodynamic activity and the direction of inhibitory function in WWOX. The registered phase 2 adult focal-epilepsy comparison and the adult phase 1 cortical inhibition report provide separate design and readout anchors. [H02 class card](../../cards/H02_gabaat_inactivator_myelin_exposure.card.json) · [Adult focal-epilepsy registry](https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?ACTRN=ACTRN12626000488303) · [Phase 1 conference results](https://aesnet.org/abstractslisting/ov329-a-potent-next-generation-gaba-at-inhibitor-is-well-tolerated-and-produces-gaba-ergic-cortical-inhibition-in-a-phase-1-sad-mad-study-in-healthy-volunteers)

**Hypothesis.** At matched initial ABAT inactivation, sustained inhibition can reduce late recruitment in WWOX when native chloride driving force and interneuron recruitment permit GABA to inhibit firing. If the principal defect is a depolarized GABA reversal potential or inadequate evoked release, a rise in bulk GABA can accompany weak inhibition, loss of physiological responses, or increased recurrence.

**Discriminating observation.** Track verified parent, residual ABAT activity, usable GABA and native GABA reversal potential during exposure and recovery. At matched early recruitment, sustained improvement in evoked inhibitory recruitment and suppression of late recurrence after parent falls, tracking residual enzyme activity, supports the proposed mechanism. GABA accumulation without preserved inhibition or improvement only during measurable free exposure weakens it. Match the first time point and initial ABAT engagement for the reference inactivator vigabatrin. Analyze retinal and myelin safety as separate outcomes from both engagement and circuit function. [Primary sustained-exposure mouse report, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38937107/)

**Next step.** Replay the available ABAT activity and recovery measurements and audit which public time points pair parent PK with adult cortical measurements. Bound scenarios for recovery only from observed ranges. Cross-check WWOX baseline chloride and inhibitory physiology before choosing a native readout.
