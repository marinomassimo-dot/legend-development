# OV329 opening comparison

| Evidence | Observation | Limit for WWOX |
|---|---|---|
| Adult phase 1 conference report | The study enrolled 68 healthy participants in single and multiple ascending dose cohorts. The abstract reports increased long-interval intracortical inhibition with repeated exposure in the 3 and 5 mg cohorts. | The cohort count does not establish the denominator for each biomarker. Healthy-adult paired-pulse TMS does not establish pediatric spasm efficacy, ABAT turnover or preserved early inhibition in WWOX. Follow up by linking exact exposure times, biomarker denominators and comparator arms. [Primary AES abstract](https://aesnet.org/abstractslisting/ov329-a-potent-next-generation-gaba-at-inhibitor-is-well-tolerated-and-produces-gaba-ergic-cortical-inhibition-in-a-phase-1-sad-mad-study-in-healthy-volunteers) |
| Adult focal-epilepsy study | The ANZCTR record specifies adults aged 18–65, stable background antiseizure therapy, randomized placebo control, 12 weeks of treatment, one week of taper and three weeks of follow-up. | Registry design provides the observation window. A diary-based focal seizure endpoint and adult follow-up cannot establish the duration of WWOX-related or pediatric electroclinical benefit. [ANZCTR ACTRN12626000488303](https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?ACTRN=ACTRN12626000488303) · [EU CTIS 2026-525896-23-00](https://euclinicaltrials.eu/ctis-public/view/2026-525896-23-00?lang=en) |
| Mouse sustained exposure | The primary paper reports reduced brain GABA-AT activity, increased GABA and stronger GABAergic inhibition during sustained treatment. | This abstract does not identify paired enzyme and free-parent recovery time points. Extract the time course and assay matrix before translating the observations. [PMID 38937107](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38937107/) |
| Held class safety review | The earlier OV329 mouse distribution comparison used different OV329 and vigabatrin doses and did not measure a WWOX developmental white matter outcome. | Use the prior review to set the safety limit without repeating its evidence extraction.
