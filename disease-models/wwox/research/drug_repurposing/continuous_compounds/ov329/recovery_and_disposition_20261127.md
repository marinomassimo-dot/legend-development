# OV329 in WWOX loss of function

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. Targeted comparison of parent exposure, residual ABAT activity and inhibitory function.

**Disposition.** Retain OV329 as a preclinical comparator for persistent ABAT inhibition. Park pediatric WWOX trial prioritization. The mouse data support a temporal separation between falling parent and enzyme inhibition. The effect on native inhibition in developing WWOX circuits and the duration of human pharmacodynamic recovery remain open.

## Human exposure and feasibility

The sponsor's phase 1 adult study enrolled 68 participants. It measured a serum half-life of approximately 0.7–1.2 h. Fig. 1 reports a lower day-7 paired-pulse conditioned/unconditioned motor-response ratio with OV329 and a higher one in the comparator group. From the log-transformed summaries at 5 mg, the ratios of day-7/baseline ratios are 0.376 for abductor pollicis brevis (10 active vs 4 placebo) and 0.349 for first dorsal interosseous (15 vs 5). These are descriptive and use different sets of participants. Lower LICI ratio corresponds to increased inhibition. Six APB traces were unavailable due to electrode capture. The figure p values concern within-group changes. The poster also states a between-group FDI result, calling its procedure paired without clearly describing pairing. Fig. 2 relates LICI to day-7 serum AUC from 0–8 h across 27 observations. These data indicate pharmacodynamic activity in healthy adults during repeated exposure. They leave time to recovery after discontinuation and any superiority over vigabatrin unresolved. The poster's vigabatrin comparison used historical experiments. [AES poster 2.451, Methods, Figs. 1–2 and safety table](https://s201.q4cdn.com/176459324/files/doc_downloads/2025/12/AES2025-r13.pdf) · [EILAT XVIII, §§5.4 and 5.6, Figs. 6–7](https://doi.org/10.1002/epi.70354) · [Replayed contrasts](replayed_contrasts.tsv)

The adult controlled focal epilepsy study is designed around seizure diaries, 12 weeks of treatment, one week of taper and three weeks of post-taper follow-up. The November sponsor report describes a pediatric formulation and signal-finding studies planned in TSC and infantile spasms. These developments make pediatric formulation and outcome collection plausible. They supply neither pediatric WWOX outcomes nor the duration of biological carryover. Adult phase 1 eye testing through 30 days and the prior unmatched animal tissue comparison leave cumulative ophthalmic and developmental white-matter liability unresolved. Keep vigilance, electroclinical counts, vision and developmental white-matter outcomes distinct when judging future evidence. ## WWOX inference and discriminating observation

**Held disease evidence.** The P13–17 Wwox cortical study recorded spontaneous inhibitory currents with a high-chloride pipette. Native reversal potential and evoked inhibitory reserve were unresolved. The held RNA comparison found lower ABAT in juvenile mouse cortex and hippocampus, with higher ABAT in human organoids. RNA carries no enzyme-activity or chloride sign. Early inactivation should therefore be matched relative to the untreated comparator while absolute residual activity is measured as a separate readout. [Held inhibitory physiology audit](../../inhibitory_defect_sci47_20261112/README.md) · [Held ABAT RNA analysis](../vigabatrin/README.md) · [Primary Wwox cortical study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34634460/)

**Hypothesis.** Sustained ABAT inhibition can preserve evoked inhibitory recruitment and reduce late network recurrence after free parent declines when GABA still suppresses spiking at the relevant developmental stage. If native GABA depolarizes toward spike threshold or the limiting step is interneuron recruitment, total GABA and tonic conductance can rise while useful inhibition stays weak. A depolarizing shift relative to rest can still shunt incoming excitation, so the sign is decided by effects on evoked firing and spike threshold as well as reversal potential.

**Discriminating observation.** Compare vehicle, OV329 and the vigabatrin reference in stage-matched Wwox loss and control tissue. Match early fractional ABAT inhibition and first evoked recruitment. At prespecified early and delayed collections, quantify local free parent with explicit assay limits, absolute ABAT activity and GABA. Measure native reversal and effects of GABA on spike probability, train-evoked inhibitory recruitment and late recurrence in parallel preparations. Selective reduction of late recurrence after parent falls, accompanying preserved inhibition and tracking residual ABAT, supports carryover at the enzyme. Higher GABA with impaired inhibitory recruitment, increased late firing or response present only while parent remains measurable weakens that explanation. A shared phenotype at matched engagement supplies a class effect. An OV329-specific difference requires a measured divergence in engagement recovery, local distribution or an alternative mechanism. In a longer comparison, the held vigabatrin work keeps mitochondrial recovery and myelination separate from network suppression.
