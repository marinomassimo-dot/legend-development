# Fenofibrate and the systemic–circuit distinction in WWOX

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Decision.** Retain fenofibrate as a conditional probe for systemic fuel handling and neural PPAR signaling. Defer pediatric WWOX intervention priority. The best opening comparison is the direction and timing of native glucose and ketone handling in the global and neuronal models. A circulating correction, a direct neural response and metabolic suppression of firing have distinct implications.

## WWOX and developmental energy

**Data.** P18 surviving global Wwox-KO pups in Ludes-Meyers Table 3 had 143.5 versus 250.6 mg/dL mean glucose, with lower total CO₂ and higher urea nitrogen. The comparison does not resolve a pre-seizure or fed-state sequence. The P28 lde/lde rat comparison found similar group glucose and clear azotemia. Neuronal WWOX restoration in global null mice reversed hypoglycemia. This is compatible with neuronal state changing systemic glucose. It does not identify the mediating organ or time order. In a different genotype, Cotter et al. found reduced hepatic BHB enrichment from labeled octanoate in neonatal Ppara-KO mice (about 33% at P0 and 50% at P1), alongside relative hypoglycemia and preserved labeled octanoate contribution to the hepatic TCA cycle. This separates routing to ketones from general hepatic oxidation. [Global Wwox primary Table 3](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0007775) · [P18 WWOX source audit](../../../fulltext_dossiers/PMID19936220.md) · [Rat audit](../../../fulltext_dossiers/PMID17803050.md) · [Neuron-only restoration audit](../../../fulltext_dossiers/PMID34747138_locators.md) · [Cotter et al.](https://doi.org/10.1152/ajpendo.00087.2014)

**Transcript comparison.** PPARA shifts by −0.29 and −0.29 in P17 neuronal-KO bulk cortex and hippocampus, and +0.02 in week-15 human organoids. The effects are mean log2(1+expression) differences. The human omission range crosses zero. Mouse ACOX1 and CPT1A fall while the organoid contrasts are positive. HMGCS2 is higher in cortex and organoids with lower hippocampal RNA. In pooled hippocampal nuclei, astrocyte Ppara falls from 56 to 29 CPM, compared with 1.78→1.55 in excitatory nuclei and three→zero UMI in inhibitory nuclei. CHRNB2 changes in opposite directions in the two assigned neuronal classes. One pool per genotype and sparse neuronal counts prevent independent replication or a receptor-current inference. The previous D-BHB screen found lower SLC16A7 in the same bulk inputs, with attenuation after neuronal marker normalization in the human model. The combined evidence leaves hepatic engagement, cortical fuel flux and direct neuronal signaling open. **Alternative mechanism.** In rat muscle homogenate, the parent reduced complex I activity at a nominal concentration of 10 µM. This matches the nominal parent concentration used acutely in the nicotinic slice experiment, with distinct assay matrices and endpoints. The shared number motivates measuring respiration and initial circuit recruitment in any direct slice comparison. It cannot transfer the muscle effect to WWOX neurons. ## Human ascertainment and chemical exposure

**Adult seizure observations.** Puligheddu et al. 2017 enrolled 12 adults with sleep-related hypermotor epilepsy. Eleven completed (ages 23–52 in Table 2). Two chose fenofibrate monotherapy, and one withdrew after liver enzymes rose. The unblinded series had no concurrent controls. The reported diary statement of 8/11 seizure-free does not reconcile with the printed Table 1 column, where **3/10** available change entries are exactly −100% and one entry is missing. Raw diaries were unavailable. The paired single-night video-polysomnography total columns sum to **340→160 events**. Every completer had a recorded event after treatment, and 4/11 had no recorded major event that night. Minor motor events may occur without an epileptiform correlate. Two follow-up total entries differ from the sum of their event subtypes. Keep printed total, major and minor columns separate. Group totals are descriptive counts, without an exposure-time denominator or a causal effect estimate. The night raters were described as blinded. The series supplies no WWOX or pediatric estimate. [Puligheddu et al., pp. 3 and 6–8, Tables 1–2](https://doi.org/10.1111/epi.13863) · [Replay](comparisons.tsv)

**Acid versus ester.** Blednov et al. Table 1 measured total fenofibric acid 2 h after oral fenofibrate in adult male mice (n=6 per group). Day 1 and day 8 brain totals were **1.22 and 0.881 µg/g**, plasma totals **37.2 and 58.2 µg/mL**, and liver totals **103 and 131 µg/g**. The day-specific ratios of group means are 0.0328 and 0.0151 mL/g brain to plasma. A hypothetical plasma-equivalent volume of **32.8 or 15.1 µL/g**, respectively, could account for the full apparent brain signal if all that signal were in residual plasma. This is an arithmetic threshold, with no assumption that residual blood necessarily accounts for either measurement. The reported methods give no brain vascular correction, unbound brain fraction or regional distribution. The totals are 3.83 and 2.76 nmol/g. As an illustrative conversion at 1 g/mL, they are 40% and 29% of the nominal 9.47 µM fenofibric-acid EC50 in a human PPARα reporter. That comparison neither excludes lower-level response nor proves target engagement in brain. The same reporter gives 61 µM for PPARγ, and the slice experiment used parent rather than acid. [Blednov et al., Table 1 and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4308472/) · [Honda et al., Fig. 1F](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms23094726) · [FDA chemical identity](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2009/022418s000_ChemR.pdf)

**Pediatric feasibility.** Dalili et al.'s open randomized study describes 30 total participants aged 8–18 with hypertriglyceridemia, two oral treatment arms and 12 weeks of lipid endpoints. The public abstract does not report pediatric CNS or free systemic exposure, formulation comparability or adequate adverse-event denominators. The US TRICOR oral-tablet label states that oral fenofibrate is hydrolyzed to acid, unmodified parent is undetected in plasma, serum binding is about 99% and pediatric pharmacokinetics have not been studied for that product. It records liver, renal and muscle risk. Drug–drug data for the WWOX antiseizure background are insufficient. Overlapping liver effects could impair attribution in a study, particularly where other antiseizure therapies carry hepatic risk. Pediatric dietary or drug feasibility cannot be inferred from adult totals or from lowering triglycerides. [Dalili et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41636674/) · [US TRICOR label §§5, 7, 8.4, 12.3](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/021656s032lbl.pdf)

## One decision-changing comparison

**Hypothesis.** Systemic PPARα engagement could improve fuel availability if WWOX produces a juvenile deficit in synthesis or delivery. A separate direct arm could modify nicotinic recruitment or neural lipid handling. If elevated circulating ketone principally reflects reduced use rather than supply, further increasing it may leave local oxidative recovery unchanged. A parent-specific fall in respiration can reduce firing without correcting either bottleneck.

**Experiment.** In litter-matched global and neuronal Wwox-loss mice and controls, first pair pre-event fed-state glucose, D-BHB and acetoacetate with EEG, local concentration and labeled neural oxidation after measured demand. Compare vehicle and systemic fenofibrate with a control that matches the systemic substrates, with pair feeding and temperature controlled. Monitor the separate acid and ester concentrations in plasma and brain with vascular correction, hepatic versus neural PPAR-response markers, ATP/respiration and spontaneous recurrent activity. In a paired tissue check at the measured local exposures, distinguish rapid nicotinic responses from delayed transcription and preserve first excitatory and inhibitory recruitment. A selective PPARα comparison or orthogonal pathway perturbation is informative only at matched measured engagement.

- Reproduction by substrate matching, with a liver response and no residual direct neural effect, favors systemic support. Test timing because neuronal rescue can normalize the circulating phenotype.
- A disease-selective residual at matched glucose, ketones and preserved recruitment, with locally measured acid and neural engagement, favors a direct component. Rapid effects restricted to the ester require independent respiration and receptor controls.
- Higher circulating BHB without greater local labeled oxidation, or a higher provoked threshold without reduced spontaneous recurrence, keeps the molecule as a comparator.

**Next decision.** Pair native substrate appearance and neural oxidation with event timing before investing in broad treatment comparisons. Reopen on a juvenile WWOX substrate deficit and selective functional rescue or verified local neural engagement. Independent review could alter priority if it obtains animal-linked spontaneous outcome data from the post-status source or identifies matched-exposure juvenile measurements. A further reread of the inconsistent fixed aggregates cannot recover raw diaries, concentration-time profiles or spontaneous WWOX response.
