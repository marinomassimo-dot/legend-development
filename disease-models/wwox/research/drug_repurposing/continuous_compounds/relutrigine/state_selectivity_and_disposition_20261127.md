# Relutrigine | persistent sodium current, early recruitment and WWOX transfer

`context_policy: SYNTHESIS` · SCI_CODEX · 27 November 2026 · [Registered prediction](pretest_20261127.md).

**Decision.** Retain relutrigine as a conditional preclinical comparator and defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. Human recombinant NaV1.6 data identify an advantage over carbamazepine under a matched voltage-dependent block scenario, but its magnitude changes across channel states. The pediatric efficacy data concern SCN2A- and SCN8A-related DEEs. There is no measured WWOX persistent-current target excess or attainable free pediatric brain exposure in the evidence located. The comparative curves and RNA replay below define which measurement would reopen the decision.

At **141 nM** the fitted curves give 50% persistent inhibition, **1.6%** tonic peak block, **22.5%** train peak block and **30.8%** voltage-dependent peak block. At **20% matched voltage-dependent peak block**, the respective persistent blocks are **33.4%** for relutrigine, **9.0%** for carbamazepine, **9.6%** for lamotrigine and **14.5%** for cenobamate. These are predictions of separately reported Hill curves. The actual matching variable for WWOX tissue is early current and recruitment during native firing. The highly extrapolated 10 Hz midpoint fits for conventional comparators describe this recording protocol and are not reliable estimates of their concentration for native use-dependent block. The tonic fits for phenytoin and lacosamide were not available. The study did not fully recruit slow inactivation for lacosamide. [Kahlig et al., Methods §§2.2–2.3, Table 1, Figs. 1–2 and Supplementary Figs. S1–S3](https://doi.org/10.1111/epi.17149) · [Fitted inputs](sodium_state_fits.tsv) · [Matched replay](matched_block_scenarios.tsv)

Wild-type CA1 pyramidal recordings compare relutrigine and carbamazepine near their respective recombinant voltage-dependent midpoints. Both reduced evoked firing and carbamazepine reduced AP amplitude more strongly. The study matches recombinant inhibition rather than measured native early current, and its CA1 readout does not establish preservation of inhibitory recruitment. Relutrigine also shifted steady-state inactivation to more negative voltages relative to vehicle. Its reported persistent-current midpoints in hNaV1.1, hNaV1.2 and hNaV1.5 were 109, 172 and 172 nM. A persistent-current preference in NaV1.6 alone therefore cannot guarantee sparing of inhibitory neurons. An adult wild-type maximal-electroshock seizure-latency effect was associated with calculated free plasma and brain concentrations of 17.9 and 4.3 nM. This is a different in vivo endpoint and does not establish a native or human exposure threshold. [Kahlig et al., §3.2, Fig. 3, §§3.3–3.4, Table 2 and Supplementary Table S2](https://doi.org/10.1111/epi.17149)

The change in direction between hippocampal bulk and pooled cell classes weakens a cross-model abundance argument. Each genotype has a single nuclear pool. Class composition, library depth and normalization can change these descriptive contrasts, and RNA does not establish sodium-channel conductance, occupancy, gating or axonal localization. SCN1A and SCN2A also move slightly upward in both assigned hippocampal classes while their bulk hippocampal estimates fall. The juvenile WWOX firing precedents already justify using native and matched starting voltages and measuring inhibitory neurons separately. The adult phase 1 poster's day-14 +24-hour mean for the historical 90-mg cohort (plotted n=6) reads **roughly 700–850 ng/mL total plasma** from its log figure. This broad graphical bracket represents the reading precision. EILAT reports about 95% protein binding, an estimated pediatric half-life of about 110 hours and approach to steady state at about 28 days. The calculated free brain range for that adult plasma bracket is **86–104 nM** at an assumed `Kp,uu = 1` and **21–26 nM** at `0.25`. At `Kp,uu = 1`, reaching the 75 nM mutant persistent, 141 nM wild-type persistent and 317 nM wild-type voltage-dependent midpoints would require about **611, 1,149 and 2,582 ng/mL** total plasma, respectively. At `0.25`, multiply these requirements by four. Mouse partition, adult total plasma and the genetic mutant's assay midpoint cannot establish pediatric free WWOX exposure. For interpretation, the clinical and recombinant assays require different state, matrix and species conditions. ## Predicted observation and falsification

1. **Target test.** Carry forward the registered prediction of excess late sodium charge per cell in a defined WWOX excitatory class at fixed initial voltage and fixed waveform or drive. Record absolute and peak-normalized late charge, the early peak, spike threshold and first-spike success, between-pulse recovery, and inhibitory spike recruitment. Model or compare native resting voltage separately. Analyze regions, ages and cell classes separately and replicate across preparations rather than cells of the same preparation.
2. **Drug comparison.** At measured free exposure, bracket relutrigine against a broader sodium blocker such as carbamazepine at matched early inhibition. Use a train with relevant timing and report recovery, early peak and inhibitory recruitment in the same conditions. A known SCN2A/SCN8A gain-of-function model gives a mechanistically defined reference. An untreated or vehicle group establishes elapsed-time effects. Inspect exposure, first spike and late charge individually before treating burst counts as informative about mechanism.
3. **Discriminating result.** Support a WWOX-selective late-current effect only if a baseline excess survives matched starting voltage, and relutrigine reduces it and late network occupancy more than the matched comparator while first responses and inhibition are maintained. If the excess disappears after matching state, spike recruitment or inhibitory output falls, or the effect requires exposure above the defensible bracket, prioritize the competing interpretation of state-dependent broad suppression. Baseline gating and drug-specific circuit modulation remain distinct even if the drug lowers bursts.

**Reopen** for replicated native WWOX current and recruitment at quantified free exposure, or public WWOX-identifiable clinical evidence with a relevant comparator. Independent review would have greatest value when period-level EMERALD/EMBOLD data permit a different clinical judgment or when a matched native result informs trial priority. Public material currently permits a bounded placebo-window audit and a preclinical comparator decision.
