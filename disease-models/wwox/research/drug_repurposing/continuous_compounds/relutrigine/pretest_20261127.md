# Relutrigine, persistent sodium current versus recruitment loss

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. Relutrigine (PRAX-562) is the sole active compound after [ES-481](../es_481/ampa_timing_and_disposition_20261127.md). The held cenobamate comparison already shows why matching a sustained sodium-current readout alone may mask different recovery between pulses. Relutrigine supplies an investigational disease-state sodium-current comparator and a pediatric genetic DEE evidence base with explicit eligibility boundaries. [Held sodium and recovery comparison](../cenobamate/kinetics_and_disposition_20261126.md) · [Primary PRAX-562 study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35037706/)

**Registered prediction.** If WWOX loss creates a usable persistent-current target, a measurable excess of persistent or late charge at matched starting voltage should appear in a defined excitatory cell class. At measured exposure, relutrigine should lower that excess and late burst occupancy while preserving first spikes and inhibitory recruitment. Compare at native and matched holding potential and matched early peak-current inhibition against a broader sodium-channel tool. Measure recovery between pulses and separate late current from failure to recruit axons or interneurons. A matched-current effect confined to SCN2A/SCN8A gain-of-function systems, or broad loss of recruitment in WWOX, would support a different interpretation. Cell-class RNA alone cannot adjudicate it.

**Clinical starting point.** On the 27 November 2026 official API snapshots both protocols were active, no longer recruiting, and without posted results. EMBOLD (NCT05818553, ages 1–18, 77 estimated) required variant-defined early-onset SCN2A or SCN8A DEE and excluded documented or presumed channel LoF as specified and other causative variants. Being WWOX-LoF is a different genetic property. EMERALD (NCT07010471, ages 2–65, 160 estimated) tests broader DEEs and its posted eligibility text does not name WWOX. Eligibility alone does not identify a WWOX participant or response. Keep randomized, early-stopped, and extension denominators separate. The sponsor's 9 November filing reported ongoing FDA review for SCN2A/SCN8A DEEs with a December 27 target action date. The FDA orphan listing carries no approval for its listed SCN2A indication at this checkpoint. [EMBOLD](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05818553) · [EMERALD](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07010471) · [FDA listing](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/opdlisting/oopd/detailedIndex.cfm?cfgridkey=789420) · [Sponsor update filed with SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1689548/000168954826000086/praxisq32026pr.htm)

**Next comparison.** Reconstruct native persistent-to-peak selectivity and waveform dependence from the primary experiments, including first and repetitive firing and recovery. Determine what early-stopped EMBOLD data and broader EMERALD reports actually measure, including age, variant-specific denominators, exposure and functional outcomes. Compare the altered WWOX excitability premise against measured channels and available free human exposure. Reopen for a decision-level advance if late activity is reduced with early inhibition preserved at that exposure.
