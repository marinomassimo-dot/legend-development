# D-cycloserine, co-agonist reserve and NMDA response

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Active checkpoint.** Recombinant and native recordings make the sign of D-cycloserine a testable variable. The US contraindication in epilepsy limits the current comparison to mechanistic research. [Registered prediction](pretest_20261127.md)

| Primary and regulatory records | Observation and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Dravid et al., recombinant and single-channel evidence | D-cycloserine had lower efficacy than glycine at GluN1/GluN2B and higher efficacy at GluN1/GluN2C. In single active GluN1/GluN2C channels, it increased mean open time and open probability relative to glycine. The abstract establishes direction under that assay. The next comparison needs independently verified magnitudes, occupancy and native receptor context. [Primary abstract via institutional record](https://researchworks.creighton.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Structural-determinants-of-D-cycloserine-efficacy-at/991005929610202656) |
| Rat entorhinal cortex study | D-cycloserine had no detected change in the readout of tonic presynaptic NMDAR facilitation of glutamate release. The separate partial agonist ACBC reduced miniature EPSC frequency under that protocol. The result leaves reserve, subunit composition and transfer to juvenile WWOX open. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26193112/) |
| Pediatric social-skills study in ASD | The double-blind adjunctive comparison found no significant difference for the primary Social Responsiveness Scale change (p=0.45). Behavioral outcomes do not measure WWOX epilepsy, direct co-agonist occupancy or evoked physiology. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26770664/) |
| US antibiotic label | Epilepsy is a listed contraindication, separately from other characterized central adverse effects. Prior pediatric research does not provide an argument for clinical use in WWOX epilepsy. [DailyMed, contraindications and warnings](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=f7ad9d0d-3f3e-4116-8bc4-8d76da4b75ae) |

**Next step.** Reconstruct the same-assay exposure and efficacy matrix, derive the conditions for a sign switch and check with an independent native study. Then determine what control would diagnose limiting co-agonist occupancy in the existing WWOX model without assuming receptor subtype from transcript data. Journal evidence at this checkpoint is abstract-level.
