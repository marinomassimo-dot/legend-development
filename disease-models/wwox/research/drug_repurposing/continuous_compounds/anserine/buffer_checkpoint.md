# Anserine first checkpoint, matched aldehyde chemistry

28 November 2026 UTC. context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN. See the [prespecified comparison](pretest.md) and [prior carnosine dossier](../l_carnosine/kinetics.md). This is an interim analysis. The questions on intact exposure, native WWOX phenotype and pediatric feasibility remain open.

**Data.** Barski Table 1 gives acrolein forward rate constants of 0.58 ± 0.05 for anserine and 0.51 ± 0.03 for carnosine, in M⁻¹ s⁻¹. The 4-HNE values are 0.087 ± 0.004 and 0.048 ± 0.0025, respectively. The 4-HNE anserine entry is reversible, with a reverse rate of 6.2 × 10⁻⁵ s⁻¹. Fig. 2's caption specifies the potassium phosphate buffer, pH 7.4, 37 °C and measurement of free aldehyde by GC-CI/MS. Histidine in that table is not the Nπ-methylhistidine hydrolysis product of anserine. [Table 1, its footnote and Fig. 2 caption](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3869200/)

**Inference.** At matched intact exposure the acrolein ratio of the point estimates is 1.14. This alone provides little reason to prefer one compound, and the ratio is not a test of a difference. The 4-HNE forward ratio is 1.81. The simple two-state model gives a dissociation constant of 0.713 mM, consistent with the printed equilibrium value. With fixed parent, trace HNE and no other sinks, equilibrium adduct fractions are approximately 0.14% at 1 µM and 1.38% at 10 µM parent. These are buffer scenarios, without assumptions about attained brain concentrations. An irreversible trapping model would overstate HNE retention in this assay. Clearance or downstream metabolism of the conjugate could change the result. [Replay](replay_kinetics.py) · [Outputs](buffer_kinetics.tsv)

**Hypothesis and next verification.** A useful advantage for acrolein would most likely require higher intact exposure in the relevant compartment or different processing of the conjugate. Compare independently measured adduct and protein damage at matched intact anserine and carnosine exposure, with anserine hydrolysis controls. Verify adult plasma pharmacokinetics and the identity and range of aggregate pediatric CSF detections before calculating any cross-compartment bounds. The initial decision remains to test the exposure premise, with no candidate priority assigned.
