The first comparison is an independent Gjd2 perturbation with verified electrical coupling between juvenile cortical interneurons. Establish whether coupling changes spontaneous burst initiation or spread while preserving the first chemically mediated response.

## Inference | What the molecule can distinguish

- **Cx36 recombinant and native.** In paired transfected N2A cells, the reported Cx36 and Cx50 IC₅₀ values are 0.31 and 1.1 µM. At 10 µM, recombinant Cx43 junction current fell by 43%. In juvenile rat cortex (P13–16), nominal 10, 25 and 50 µM lowered the interneuron coupling coefficient by 29%, 66% and 99% after about an hour (3, 5 and 1 pairs, with 4 time-matched vehicle-free control pairs). Block developed slowly. The approximate native 15 µM half-block estimate is about 48 times the recombinant IC₅₀, comparing different readouts and exposure conditions. At 25 µM, 15/21 cells showed more spontaneous synaptic activity. In five fast-spiking cells the rate rose from 44.2 to 91.8 Hz and remained elevated with tetrodotoxin. Resistance also changed in uncoupled regular-spiking cells. A native burst response at this exposure could therefore include chemical inputs or intrinsic changes. A 25 nM screen would test an exposure with unknown native engagement. A one-site curve with Hill coefficient 1 would estimate 7.5% of maximal recombinant block from that concentration. [Cruikshank et al., Figs. 1–6 and Methods](https://www.pnas.org/doi/full/10.1073/pnas.0402044101)
- **Physical contact is supported.** Cryo-EM at nominal 1 mM in one preparation models the S,R component of the applied racemate in a pocket between TM1 and TM2. Quinine and quinidine occupy the same pocket region, so agreement among these compounds does not independently establish Cx36 causality. In separate human Cx36 brain-lipid nanodiscs, mefloquine was associated with an N-terminal conformational shift and pore-lipid occlusion. These structures do not supply native potency. [Cell Discovery, Fig. 1 and Methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41421-024-00691-y) · [Cho et al., Figs. 1–3 and Methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-53587-6)
- **Pannexin direction and reagent identity are unresolved in native WWOX.** The 2010 transfected-cell study reported PANX1 inhibition for two erythro racemate sources (52.7 and 47.3 nM), with 483.8 µM for a third (n=9/11/7). In 2025, human PANX1 in tsA201 cells showed increased current at 150 µM and strong depolarization with the nominal erythro racemate from one of those suppliers and with a single stereoisomer from a different supplier. Distinct assays, concentration and reagents prevent using either study to assign a net PANX1 direction in the Wwox slice. Capture salt, stereochemistry, vendor and lot for any challenge. [Iglesias et al., Figs. 3–5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2854254/) · [Li et al., Fig. 3, Supplementary Fig. 7 citation and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12698695/)
- **A causal negative control changes the interpretation.** In a separate 4-AP hippocampal preparation, carbenoxolone depressed GABAergic events in both WT and Cx36-null slices and mefloquine failed to reproduce the effect. In adult neocortex under low magnesium, a different study found excitatory effects of mefloquine in WT and no detected response in constitutive Cx36-null slices. These are preparation-specific counterexamples to treating CBX suppression as a Cx36 readout or assuming that uncoupling is always beneficial. Constitutive nulls can have developmental effects. [Hippocampal comparison, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21300748/) · [Neocortical comparison, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19486358/) · [Developmental comparator, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20831861/)

## Hypothesis and discriminating observation

**Hypothesis.** Juvenile Wwox interneuron coupling amplifies propagation after glutamatergic initiation. A rival is that coupling synchronizes effective inhibitory recruitment and buffers recurrence. A second rival is that the published carbenoxolone effect reflects chemical excitation or a glial mechanism, including altered Cx43-mediated intercellular buffering, Cx43 hemichannels or PANX1.

**One matched comparison.** In spontaneous P13–17 Wwox and sibling slices, compare an independent Gjd2-specific perturbation with its time-matched control in the same cortical region. Use the animal as the biological unit and nest slice and cell measurements. Measure paired interneuron junctional conductance and input resistance directly, together with spatial burst initiation, duration, amplitude and spread. Track fast AMPA and late NMDA responses, spontaneous chemical events and inhibitory recruitment under matched recording conditions. The existing [AMPA/NMDA comparison](../../ampa_nmda_discrim_20261108/output/README.md) supplies the chemical-drive control. An appropriately timed partial NMDAR comparison can test whether a change is reproduced by lower excitatory drive without eliminating activity. Reserve a characterized mefloquine challenge for confirmation after there is a measurable native coupling window and examine it at matched engagement and elapsed time. A conditional Gjd2 allele provides a genetic starting point. Qualify loss of native conductance and the timing in this preparation before adopting it. If only a constitutive comparator is available, interpret it as genetic dependence with possible developmental adaptation. [Conditional Gjd2 allele, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18258229/) A glial comparison, if required by the first result, should distinguish tracer transfer and extracellular potassium recovery from hemichannel or PANX1 activity. Global CBX is insufficient for that separation.

**Controls for the competing mechanisms.** The [oxytocin checkpoint](../oxytocin/README.md) identifies a readout for the protective-coupling rival. Measure native GABA reversal relative to rest and spike threshold, and ask whether the effect of uncoupling changes sign with the initial GABA driving force. Gap19 distinguished hemichannel-associated activity from astrocyte tracer transfer in its published preparations. D4(R) is another possible hemichannel comparator after native qualification. Its Figure 7A image and caption list Cx26 where the accompanying Results name Cx36. The separate Cx36 result is a hemichannel dye-uptake assay, so the paper does not establish sparing of native interneuron Cx36 conductance. In a different, kainate-induced mouse model, microglial TNFα deletion prevented astrocyte uncoupling without a detected spontaneous generalized seizure difference over four weeks. Preserved glial coupling and chronic seizure burden are therefore separate endpoints in that model. [Gap19 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25374505/) · [Lagos et al., §§3.4–3.5 and Fig. 7](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2026.1870076) · [Henning et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36373840/)

| --- | --- |

## Pediatric feasibility and next gate

The labeled oral hydrochloride contains 228 mg parent equivalent per 250 mg salt. Historical data are for antimalarial use. Ramharter et al. sampled artesunate–mefloquine combinations in 12 younger children aged 2.3–6.0 years with granules and 12 aged 6.3–12.0 years with co-blister tablets. At 54 hours, group median total plasma parent was 2,550 and 1,815 ng/mL, approximately 6.74 and 4.80 µM. Applying the US label’s approximately 98% plasma binding yields illustrative 0.135 and 0.096 µM free plasma, respectively. The 25 µM nominal slice condition is about 185–261 times those illustrative plasma values. The comparison uses a sparse measured time point, different ages and formulations, a common assumed plasma free fraction and no unbound brain measurements. It does not estimate a brain exposure margin. The label carries a neuropsychiatric boxed warning, restricts malaria prophylaxis with a history of convulsions, warns of seizure worsening and possible loss of seizure control through interactions with several antiseizure drugs. It reports a two- to four-week mean elimination half-life across healthy-adult studies. There is no exposure-backed WWOX functional endpoint. [Ramharter et al., Table 1 and Meﬂoquine Results](https://doi.org/10.1093/jac/dkm355) · [DailyMed label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=9cbe26c7-7313-4cd6-9514-9190af1de763&type=display) · [Arithmetic](exposure.tsv)
