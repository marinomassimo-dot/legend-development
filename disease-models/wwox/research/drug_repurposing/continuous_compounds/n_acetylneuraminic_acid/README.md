# Free N-acetylneuraminic acid in WWOX loss of function

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · active comparison · [prespecified hypotheses and controls](pretest_20261126.md)

**Decision at this checkpoint.** Keep free Neu5Ac in the mechanistic work queue and defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. The RNA data weaken the case for a conserved shortfall in de novo synthesis. Prioritize measuring the donor and its use in the same human neural preparation before moving to an exposure or functional rescue study. A derived difference worth testing is whether the same CMP-Neu5Ac supply supports NCAM polysialylation and ganglioside synthesis to different degrees. This would change the interpretation of a total sialic acid measurement.

## Evidence outside WWOX

- **Biochemical positive control.** Bu et al. report undetectable total and free sialic acid by DMB-HPLC in NANS-null human cultures across DIV 0, 15, 25 and 35, ManNAc accumulation (12–266 fold), and loss of the broad polysialylated NCAM signal despite detectable NCAM140/180 (Fig. 1B, D, E). This calibrates an upstream synthesis defect. In the same paper, NANS-null iPSC-derived cortical neurons have lower spontaneous activity on MEA (Fig. S16C–D, targeted Results). The WWOX report describes hyperexcitability in a different cerebral organoid assay. Genotype, cell preparation, developmental timing and the electrical endpoint differ. A rise in firing therefore has no generic interpretation as rescue. [NANS organoid primary study](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adf2772) · [WWOX organoid abstract and data](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34268881/)
- **Chemical specificity of a delivery signal.** Galuska et al. gave 8-week-old wild-type male mice [1,2,3-¹³C]-Neu5Ac. The oral-arm samples at 1, 3, 5 and 9 hours represent different animals. Brain-region measurements used elemental isotope ratios after combustion. The assay detected labelled carbon after oral exposure but could not assign it to intact Neu5Ac, its catabolic products, CMP-Neu5Ac or neural glycoconjugates. Chemically specific DMB-LC-MS was applied to plasma and urine. Intact labelled material in the oral free Neu5Ac plasma arm was seen in one sample at 5 hours near detection and could not be quantified (Methods §§2.1–2.4, Results §3.1, Figs. 1 and 5–6). The healthy young adult model and short sampling window leave chronic developmental exposure open. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jff.2020.104268)
- **Developmental exposure countercheck.** In 3-day-old piglets 120 minutes after intravenous radiolabelled Neu5Ac, the cerebrum contained 0.175% of injected radioactivity. The endpoint was total radioactivity. This is a neonatal exposure observation with a different route and chemical specificity from the desired oral parent or donor measurement. [Piglet primary study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17215187/)
- **Counterexamples to simple substrate rescue.** In human HEK293T cells, 1 mM nominal Neu5Ac for 24 h increased the intracellular Neu5Ac LC–MS/MS signal, with only a small CMP-Neu5Ac increase reported in wild type and NPL-knockout conditions (Huang et al., Fig. 3B/F and Results referring to Fig. S5A/E). This is evidence that parent and donor signals can separate in an exposed cell. The assay used normalized peak areas and did not trace a particular mature glycoconjugate. In mouse C2C12 Gne-null clones, direct Neu5Ac at 24 h failed to restore PSA-NCAM. Longer exposure left at most a weak, non-significant increase in the reported clone 24 signal, while reducing MYH in wild-type cultures (Neu et al., Figs. 5B/D and 7B–E). These non-neural models motivate simultaneous measurement of parent, donor and product in the WWOX experiment. Their bath concentrations and differentiation response do not transfer directly to developing human brain. [Human cell LC–MS/MS primary results](https://doi.org/10.1093/glycob/cwag038) · [Mouse myoblast primary results](https://doi.org/10.1093/glycob/cwae004)
- **Pediatric feasibility.** A short oral study included two children among six individuals with NANS deficiency and observed increased free Neu5Ac in plasma with early urinary excretion, without a consistent ManNAc improvement. A separate uncontrolled pilot studied five participants aged 0–28 in NANS-CDG. Its abstract reports no significant improvement after postnatal treatment. A single prenatal observation provides neither a matched efficacy estimate nor evidence of intact human brain delivery. These studies support formulation and exposure questions. Exact-age WWOX CNS exposure, chronic tolerability with antiseizure treatments, interactions and an interpretable proximal endpoint remain unknown. [Short oral exposure study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34258226/) · [Open-label clinical pilot abstract](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/jimd.12643)

## Discriminating next step

1. Check vehicle WWOX and isogenic corrected neural preparations at the same maturation stage for free Neu5Ac, CMP-Neu5Ac, protein-normalized PSA-NCAM and a defined ganglioside ratio such as GM3 to lactosylceramide. Correct for cell number or cell composition and carrier abundance. Measure downstream physiology as an evoked recruitment and recovery outcome. Use a low-sialylation control to establish isolation and detection.
2. If a deficit is measurable, follow intact labelled free parent into CMP-Neu5Ac and both product branches. Include unlabelled pool sizes, a blank medium control and matched osmolarity. Track intact isotopologue and potential isotope recycling with diagnostic fragments. Require a higher absolute product pool per carrier and evidence of selective functional improvement before calling a rescue. A matched WWOX-dependent loss of donor that normalizes before both product pools and function would favor a substrate bottleneck. Accumulating CMP-Neu5Ac with unchanged products favors impaired utilization. A change limited to one branch favors a branch-specific limitation.
3. Stop the formulation comparison for an unselected WWOX study if the matched preparation shows no deficit in the target branch, or if the label reaches products without absolute or functional recovery at credible exposure. A downstream defect might still justify an assay or a different candidate. The next desk check will compare chemical rescue in SLC17A5-deficient neural models and chemically specific developmental exposure. An independent review can change the decision if it supplies either an intact CNS exposure dataset or a set of existing measurements linking WWOX to a deficiency in donor or mature product.
