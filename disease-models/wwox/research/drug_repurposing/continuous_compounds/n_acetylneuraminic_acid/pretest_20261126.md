# Free N-acetylneuraminic acid · first comparative test

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN · WWOX mouse cortical and hippocampal TPM, human cerebral organoid CPM, hippocampal nuclear counts

**Question.** Does free Neu5Ac bypass a deficit in making sialic acid in WWOX, and can the recovered label support the same neuronal membrane output as an endogenous donor? This is the unmodified Neu5Ac molecule. Sialyllactose, N-acetylmannosamine and sustained-release aceneuramic acid have distinct delivery and metabolic evidence. The earlier mannose and galactose analyses tested earlier glycan stages or a different terminal donor. [Mannose comparison](../d_mannose/README.md) · [Galactose comparison](../d_galactose/README.md)

**Before examining the panel.** Compare `GNE`, `NANS` and `NANP` for de novo production, `SLC17A5` and `NPL` for lysosomal exit and competing metabolism, `CMAS` and `SLC35A1` for activation and Golgi entry, and `ST3GAL5`, `B4GALNT1`, `ST8SIA2`, `ST8SIA4`, and `NCAM1` for separate ganglioside and protein outcomes. A consistent upstream signal would favor measuring donor limitation. A downstream or opposite-direction pattern would favor first measuring uptake, intracellular localization or branch utilization. Any assignment from RNA is provisional until pool and flux are measured. Use the same library and cell-class denominators as the earlier stage-specific WWOX comparisons.

**Predicted observations.** In stage-matched WWOX and corrected neural preparations, use isotopically labelled unmodified Neu5Ac. Quantify free intracellular parent, CMP-Neu5Ac, labelled sialic acid in one defined ganglioside class and in polysialylated NCAM, and an evoked network or axonal functional endpoint at measured exposure. If limited de novo substrate drives the WWOX defect, donor, both product branches and function should rise together at a credible exposure. If labelled free parent accumulates with little CMP-Neu5Ac, test entry into the relevant compartment and activation. If CMP-Neu5Ac rises while product labeling stays low, test Golgi access and transfer. If only one product improves, check acceptor availability and the association of that specific branch with function. Reject a rescue claim if enrichment comes from label turnover alone without more mature product per cell, or if apparent maturation comes with poorer network control. Keep progenitor expansion, neuronal firing and circuit safety as distinct endpoints.

**Positive and limiting comparators.** NANS-deficient human organoids offer a substrate synthesis phenotype with distinct deficits in maturation and excitability. Use it to calibrate readout direction and chemistry. Do not assume WWOX reproduces the null's direction or developmental timing. Pediatric NANS supplementation offers human formulation experience. Oral uptake, central intact delivery and restoration of the relevant glycoconjugate are separate evidence gates.
