**Disposition.** Park free Neu5Ac for pediatric WWOX trial priority. Retain the compound as a tracer and substrate comparator when matched WWOX neural chemistry is available. ## What the two pediatric trajectories show

Tran et al. studied oral free Neu5Ac for three days in adults and separately in two affected siblings aged **7 and 4 years**. There was no pediatric control group, and the pediatric data did not enter the adult statistical model. Plasma and urine used stable-isotope dilution LC-MS/MS. The isotope was used as an analytical standard. The oral substrate itself was unlabelled. Day 1 samples were collected before and 2, 4 and 6 hours after the first administration during a multi-administration day. Table S2 gives fasting plasma free Neu5Ac at baseline of **1.03 and 1.04 µmol/L** and on day 4 of **1.00 and 0.99 µmol/L**, respectively. [Methods, Fig. 2 and Table S2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8251509/)

The largest observed values are about twice baseline. The 2 h sampling cannot establish the plasma peak or the area under the concentration curve. The **1 mM** nominal exposure in the non-neural HEK293T donor-pool comparator is about **440–480 times** these two observed concentrations. This is a scale comparison between a bath and a sampled plasma analyte, with neither a tissue delivery ratio nor an estimate of required clinical exposure. [Pediatric source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymgmr.2021.100777) · [HEK293T comparison, Fig. 3](https://doi.org/10.1093/glycob/cwag038)

The pediatric Results sentence places a rise in urinary free and total Neu5Ac at 2 h. Fig. S1D instead shows free Neu5Ac rising in one sibling and falling in the other at 2 h, and Fig. S1E shows both total signals falling at 2 h. Both measures are above baseline in both siblings at 6 h. The graph is carried for these time and direction details. The caption contains a generic group count alongside two actual patients. The Methods, table and two trajectories establish the pediatric denominator. Table S2 labels plasma total Neu5Ac in mmol/L while the plotted values and main article use µmol/L. The cross-unit total-plasma comparison is left unresolved. None of these points changes interpretation of the free plasma signal. ## Developmental oral and chemical counterchecks

- **Subchronic rat oral exposure.** Carlson and House exposed rat pups from P14 for eight days. In the primary abstract, oral and intraperitoneal free Neu5Ac increase bound sialic acid in cerebral ganglioside and glycoprotein fractions, and in cerebellar gangliosides, against a glucose control that was injected. The oral cerebellar glycoprotein exception is explicit. The oral arm therefore has positive evidence of a bulk product-pool association. The compared control route leaves handling and procedure as alternatives. The reported oral–intraperitoneal difference did not reach significance and does not establish equivalence. The primary full text is closed in the identified publisher and repository routes, so numerical fraction effects and their uncertainty are unavailable here. The abstract gives no tracer identity in the mature molecules. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3701463/)
- **Label delivery and developmental context.** Oral radiocarbon retained in newborn rat tissues after a free sialic acid or sialyllactose input does not by itself identify intact parent. Neonatal pig intravenous total radioactivity and orally administered label in young adult mouse brains have separate route, age and speciation limits. Whole-brain isotope, unlabelled bulk product and sampled free plasma describe three different stages. No directly measured intact oral delivery into a defined developing-brain glycoconjugate was located in these primary routes. [Newborn rodent tracer abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/759994/) · [Neonatal pig tracer](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17215187/) · [Adult mouse chemical-speciation comparison](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jff.2020.104268)
- **WWOX chemistry search.** The [public fly deposit](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights/MTBLS12344) contains 14 rows in the authentic metabolite assignment file for the HILIC assay. It contains no named Neu5Ac pathway compound in the prespecified name and formula check. The companion workbook's differential-metabolite Tables 4 and 7 have zero matching annotation or metabolite-name fields (columns D/E, row 4 onward, 51 and 54 populated fields). These are filtered or assigned records from fly heads. They cannot establish absence, assess mammalian neural substrate supply or support untargeted mass-only reassignment. The matched human and mouse WWOX resources used in the programme are RNA measurements. [Original fly dataset annotation](https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/metabolights/studies/public/MTBLS12344/m_MTBLS12344_LC-MS_alternating_hilic_metabolite_profiling_v2_maf.tsv) · [Original fly supplement](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13684411.1/fcag385_supplementary_data.zip)

**Reopen** on a chemically resolved WWOX deficit in donor or mature product, a selective repletion that preserves evoked recruitment, and a credible exposure bridge for trial priority. A competitor can distinguish delivery from utilization. A rise in labelled intracellular CMP-Neu5Ac with neither mature ganglioside nor NCAM polysialylation recovery favors a downstream limitation. A rise in isotope fractions without absolute product recovery is turnover rather than a corrected pool. An independent review would change the decision if it identifies an existing matched WWOX measurement or a chemically specific developmental delivery dataset omitted here. The two siblings' plasma values alone answer peripheral exposure and still leave the prospective CNS comparison open.
