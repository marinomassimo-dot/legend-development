# Dantrolene · human genetic calcium-disorder comparator

26 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN · translational comparator

Lu et al., Fig. 6F used patient-line and unaffected-parent-derived iPSC neural progenitors. In a thapsigargin challenge, the authors report lower cleaved caspase-3 with 10 µM nominal dantrolene (48 h pretreatment, reported n=3). The patient comparison is one family context, which does not represent replication across patient genotypes and the endpoint is apoptotic signal after experimentally impaired ER refilling. Fig. 6B gives a separate 10 µM cytosolic Fura-2 experiment in rat insulinoma and mouse motor-neuron-like cells (six independent runs). Fig. 6G shows a different four-week WFS1 mouse experiment using brain-lysate spectrin cleavage as a calpain readout. The same article reports failure to block WFS2 knockdown–associated death in separate assays (Discussion citing Fig. S4D/E). This contrast makes a useful specificity control: two genetic routes to elevated calpain can diverge in response. No WWOX target engagement or human neuronal exposure follows. [Lu et al., late Results/Discussion, Figs. 5–6 captions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4267371/)

The open-label six-month oral Wolfram study retained 19 of 21 participants qualified for baseline/run-in (90.5%), including **eight children aged 6–17** and **eleven adults aged 18–32** (Tables 1–2). Tables 1–2 imply fatigue and weakness were each reported by **3/8** pediatric completers. Two adults and no pediatric completers had elevated liver enzymes. Even zero events among eight gives a two-sided exact binomial 95% upper limit of **36.9%** under independent equal-risk sampling (`1 - 0.025**(1/8)`). This bounds only an illustrative small-cohort event estimate. The article reports no significant overall grip-strength loss. Pediatric Table 4 reports total WURS **21.3 → 18.4** (p=0.74), physician-rated exam subscore **3.1 → 3.1** (p=1.00), and LogMAR **0.6 → 0.6** (p=0.85). WURS is an ordinal disease rating and these are reported means. Table 4 alone does not establish the number of paired observations for each measure. The uncontrolled design and short window relative to disease progression leave efficacy unresolved. Use it as a tolerability and endpoint precedent in a distinct disorder. The reported plasma analyses were for F₂-isoprostanes. The analyzed article supplies no paired parent-drug plasma or CNS exposure with which to establish neural target engagement. [Abreu et al., Tables 1, 2 and 4](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci.insight.145188) · [Trial registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02829268)
