In a primary isoform study the response depended on assay conditions and differed across channels. The WWOX experiment needs baseline-relevant ryanodine receptor function, not just a gene or drug class. This is a mechanism-led non-AED addition after the completed substrate-supply compounds. [Primary isoform experiment, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11278295/)

The held WWOX datasets report neural hyperexcitability and altered developmental and energy pathways. The working contrast is between excessive ER calcium release and a deficient physiological ER-to-mitochondria signal during recovery. A third explanation is downstream compensation in membrane excitability, with internal calcium dynamics changing secondarily. Test at matched electrical drive. A smaller cytosolic peak alone cannot distinguish the alternatives.
