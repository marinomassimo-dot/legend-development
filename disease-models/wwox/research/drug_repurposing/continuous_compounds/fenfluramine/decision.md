# Fenfluramine disposition in WWOX loss

**Park for a WWOX disease-modifying pediatric trial.** The controlled Dravet seizure result remains relevant to symptomatic research. One exposed WWOX registry participant has no individually interpretable response. The WWOX organoids show several components of the proposed pathway but have no measured serotonin release or demonstrated excess sigma-1-dependent NMDA current. Pooled tissue drug concentration is insufficient to identify the active species. [Initial WWOX analysis and pediatric feasibility](README.md) · [Held cell-class comparison](../../memantine_magnesium_20261123/README.md)

**A reversal across assays.** Rodríguez-Muñoz et al. Fig. 1B right gives apparent ED50 values of 170 and 50 pM for the positive parent and metabolite in a washed recombinant sigma-1–GluN1 assay. Martin et al. Table 1 gives nonselective sigma binding Ki values of 266 and 2920 nM for the two racemates in guinea-pig brain. The ordering reverses, with different stereochemistry, endpoints and preparations. There is no common potency to pool. Their PRE-084-conditioned BiP experiment supplies a third endpoint. These experiments do not tell us the sign of a native neuronal response. [Rodríguez-Muñoz, Fig. 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5955088/) · [Martin, Table 1 and §3.2](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.yebeh.2020.106989) · [Within-assay ratios](sigma_crosscheck.tsv)

**How much protein would account for the blot?** The first lab supplied 20 pmol of sigma-1 before washing. At the two reported ED50s the ligand additions were only 0.051 and 0.015 pmol. Under a strictly one-use 1:1 displacement model and a linear relationship between signal and half of the baseline complex, the retained protein could be at most 0.51% and 0.15% of the supplied protein respectively. The retained amount was unreported in the examined methods. Turnover, partial displacement at maximum effect or a nonlinear signal would invalidate this bound. Quantification after the wash or independent native functional data would be more informative than treating these ED50s as neuronal potencies. **Paired pediatric exposure.** The EMA Dravet assessment reports geometric mean steady-state plasma peaks of 68.6 ng/mL for parent and 37.8 ng/mL for metabolite (CV 41% and 50%). Using approximately 50% binding measured in vitro for both, their estimated unbound plasma peaks are 0.148 and 0.093 µM. Their respective peak times differ. The corresponding daily average estimates are 0.126 and 0.089 µM from each AUC divided by 24 h. These summarize each analyte across patients and enantiomers. The report supplies no unbound brain concentrations in these children. The rat brain-to-plasma ratio of 15–60 uses whole tissue. The applicant's extrapolated human maxima also concern tissue. [EMA assessment, Plasma protein binding, Tissue distribution and Pharmacokinetics in target population](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/assessment-report/fintepla-epar-public-assessment-report_en.pdf) · [Metabolite molecular mass](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/15897) · [Calculated exposure](paired_exposure.tsv)

**An independent human measurement narrows the uncertainty.** In 12 women aged 38–54 taking dexfenfluramine, brain fluorine spectroscopy gave an average steady-state signal of about 4 µM for the combined positive parent and metabolite. This establishes measurable human brain accumulation. The combined signal cannot yield an individual free parent or metabolite concentration in pediatric WWOX neurons. In adult rats at serotonin-depleting exposure, the authors summarize approximately 50 µM parent in brain tissue and 2.5 µM extracellularly, a roughly twentyfold compartment difference. Raw dialysate peaks were reported separately. Neither the rat ratio nor the combined human datum can convert pediatric tissue into unbound intracellular exposure. In a separate acute rodent study all tested compounds protected against maximal electroshock, while none protected in the tested mouse 6-Hz assay. Rat d-norfenfluramine toxicity precluded estimation of an electroshock ED50. Tissue accumulation did not predict protection across seizure paradigms. These three comparisons use author abstracts; detailed recovery calibration, individual spectra and uncertainty remain unverified. [Human spectroscopy](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10407087/) · [Rat microdialysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9454806/) · [Electroshock and 6-Hz comparisons](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36995363/)

**A discriminating comparison.** The FDA patient model predicts a 4.29-fold increase in the 24-hour parent/metabolite AUC ratio with stiripentol plus clobazam, with or without valproate, at the compared nominal weight-based regimen with different dose caps. This provides a direction to vary the parent/metabolite mixture experimentally. Co-medications independently influence seizures and do not identify a causal pathway in a patient comparison. First establish a reproducible NMDA-response abnormality in WWOX versus corrected neural preparations, separating excitatory cells and interneurons. Independently vary parent and metabolite at measured free concentrations, quantify released serotonin and match postsynaptic serotonergic signaling, including direct agonism by the metabolite. Matching extracellular serotonin alone would miss this last mechanism. Test whether a genotype-selective improvement in calcium recovery and evoked recruitment persists and depends on sigma-1, with preserved physiological recruitment. Suppression of both genotypes or loss of interneuron recruitment would lower priority. A positive functional contrast would reopen the mechanism. Pediatric WWOX efficacy would still require separate evidence.
