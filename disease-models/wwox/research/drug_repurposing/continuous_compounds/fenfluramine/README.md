# Fenfluramine, serotonergic innervation and sigma-1-associated calcium signaling

**Opening prediction.** Explore racemic fenfluramine as the one active molecule. Its N-deethylated metabolite, norfenfluramine, must be tracked separately. A WWOX-specific extension of the antiseizure hypothesis is that, at matched network activity and serotonin input, fenfluramine alters an abnormal sigma-1-regulated calcium response to NMDA and improves recovery of neuronal function. A rival is conventional serotonergic suppression of network firing, with no rescue of an intrinsic calcium defect. Reduced calcium entry could also worsen physiological synaptic recruitment. First establish a baseline WWOX-versus-corrected abnormality. Compare pathological and evoked physiological activity, NMDA-evoked calcium responses and recovery after stimulation, alongside serotonin release. If response disappears when serotonergic input is matched and added serotonin reproduces it, the serotonergic explanation gains support. If an effect persists at measured achievable free exposure under matched serotonergic input and is lost on selective disruption of sigma-1-dependent signaling, that would strengthen the intrinsic hypothesis.

**Why these endpoints.** A positive response in a BiP dissociation assay in the presence of the reference ligand PRE-084 and disruption of the sigma-1–GluN1 association are different primary observations. Neither assigns a universal agonist/antagonist label or shows a WWOX effect. Parent and metabolite need individual evaluation in both contexts. The held laboratory comparison classified the serotonergic compound grouping as unscorable with its evidence requirements. This lane tests whether the WWOX organoid actually represents the necessary presynaptic compartment and whether the nominal exposure of the mechanistic assay is compatible with pediatric parent-drug exposure. **Prespecified model check.** In six GSE156243 bulk organoid libraries, inspect individual counts per million for serotonin synthesis, transporter and lineage genes (TPH2, SLC6A4, FEV and DDC), and for SIGMAR1, HSPA5 and GRIN1. Sparse or discordant presynaptic markers limit inference about endogenous serotonin release. A nonzero gene alone cannot establish mature raphe innervation, receptor complex formation or functional response. Check whether a model lacking verified serotonergic input would falsely favor an intrinsic mechanism. Use a matched serotonin-competent preparation to interpret drug responses.

**Result in WWOX organoids.** SLC6A4 was detectable in all six libraries (0.83–1.56 counts per million); TPH2 ranged from 1.47 to 5.73. FEV was zero in both controls and 0.02–0.83 in the four knockout libraries. DDC was low in controls and heterogeneous in knockout libraries. SIGMAR1, HSPA5 and GRIN1 were detected in all six libraries. A few serotonergic transcripts do not establish an endogenous serotonin pool, vesicular release or axonal input. Conversely, bulk RNA does not exclude sparse serotonin-competent cells. Quantify serotonin content and actual release in each preparation before interpreting a differential drug response. A negative result in an organoid lacking release capacity cannot falsify the serotonergic mechanism in an innervated brain. **Exposure test.** FDA reports a pediatric Dravet geometric mean parent-drug peak of 68.0 ng/mL at steady state (41% variation), and 50% plasma protein binding in vitro. Using free-base mass 231.26 g/mol yields about 0.294 µM total and an estimated 0.147 µM free at this group peak. The FDA pharmacology review, PDF p. 13, reports a fenfluramine effect at 1 µM or above in the PRE-084-stimulated BiP dissociation assay; fenfluramine alone had no effect in that assay. If nominal concentration equaled free concentration, matching 1 µM would require a free brain/plasma ratio of about 6.8 at the group peak; if 10% was free in that assay, about 0.68 would suffice. These are assumptions, not measured partitions or thresholds for clinical efficacy. Intracellular receptor access, protein binding in assay medium, brain kinetics and the active metabolite remain unresolved. As a comparator, the reported serotonin-release EC50 values in synaptosomes were 52 and 147 nM for the two parent enantiomers, 59 and 287 nM for the metabolite enantiomers. The clinical parent peak pools enantiomers; their individual free brain concentrations cannot be inferred. EC50 for serotonin release and the lowest active tested nominal concentration for a ligand-conditioned BiP readout have different meanings. **Pediatric feasibility and decision at entry.** A placebo-controlled Dravet study randomized 119 participants (mean age nine). The higher exposure arm had a 62.3% greater reduction in mean monthly convulsive seizure frequency than placebo (95% CI 47.7–72.8%). This establishes a pediatric antiseizure precedent for the compound. It gives no WWOX response rate and does not ascribe the clinical effect to sigma-1. Decreased appetite and weight are relevant in this population. The US label calls for echocardiography before, during and after treatment for valvular disease and pulmonary hypertension. Keep the WWOX clinical decision open pending interpretable WWOX functional or clinical outcomes and measured exposure. [Dravet trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31862249/) · [FDA prescribing information](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/212102s017lbl.pdf)

**WWOX human exposure, already adjudicated.** Villanueva et al. included one individual with a WWOX variant in a retrospective add-on registry. The exact variant, baseline individual seizure count and individual change are unavailable in the laboratory's source audit. In the WWOX column of supplement S7 the counts for responder, seizure freedom, worsening and adverse events are zero at the listed follow-ups. The group's denominator included all participants, and outcome data were missing for some other-DEE endpoints. The laboratory's subsequent denominator audit therefore qualified the earlier manifest: neither a confirmed nonresponse nor missing individual follow-up can be inferred for this person. Retention and prior exposure add feasibility information; the row measures no intrinsic signaling response or developmental rescue. This existing human observation reduces the value of another unstratified registry search.
