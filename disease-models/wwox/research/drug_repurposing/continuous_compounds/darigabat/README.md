# Darigabat in WWOX loss of function

**Units and preparations.** Adults are the units in human photosensitive crossover and focal-seizure trials. Human PET measures whole-brain tracer displacement in healthy adults. Recombinant GABA-A binding is in nM and current potentiation is a percentage of a low-GABA response. Mouse MTLE recordings count hippocampal paroxysmal discharges and their duration in seconds per animal. Rat GAERS recordings count cortical spike-and-wave discharges per animal. WWOX bulk RNA uses TPM by mouse cortex or hippocampus library and CPM by week-15 human forebrain organoid library. Hippocampal nuclei give CPM per cell class within one pool per genotype.

**28 November 2026 UTC.** Retain darigabat (PF-06372865, CVL-865) as a conditional ex vivo comparator for benzodiazepine-site functional preference. Defer pediatric WWOX priority. The [prespecified comparison](pretest.md) separates enhancement of actual inhibition from generalized suppression and separates light-evoked EEG activity from sustained control of spontaneous seizures.

The registry defines the primary REALIZE response ratio as 100(T−B)/(T+B) using each participant's focal seizure rate per week during maintenance and baseline. Averaging these ratios and inverting the average would lose the individual rate-change distribution. The primary placebo comparisons therefore remain in response-ratio points. The small crossover, acute mouse EEG measures and maintenance diary outcomes cannot be pooled. Together they support context-dependent pharmacology and leave sustained reduction of spontaneous seizures unconfirmed in the adult focal population. ## Inference and competing explanations

**Binding and functional efficacy can point in different directions.** From Table 1, α2 has a 16.2-fold higher Kᵢ than α1 and 6.3-fold greater QPatch maximal potentiation. α3 has a 5.9-fold higher Kᵢ and 4.3-fold greater potentiation with the same β3γ2 background. Manual patch confirms the direction, with different magnitudes. Together with the different efficacy of rat and human α1 constructs, this limits any extrapolation from rat behavioral sparing to human sedation. The article's corrected abstract reports decreased saccadic peak velocity. The trial safety results establish that dizziness and somnolence can occur under these study conditions. [Table 1 and Clinical data](https://doi.org/10.1111/bph.14119) · [Published correction](https://doi.org/10.1111/bph.14526)

**Sensitivity of the aggregate PET interpretation.** With the paper's assumed subtype half-maxima (3.28, 53.2 and 19.3 ng/mL) and common 95.6% ceiling, the same 68.6% signal is compatible with 23–50% modeled α2 occupancy if α1 contributes 75–25% of the measured signal and the remaining weight is divided between α2 and α3. At 50% α1 weight the scenario gives 38% α2. These are illustrative modeled ranges from deliberately chosen weights. They carry neither a confidence level nor a claim of participant exposure. Independent plasma data at the scan would constrain possible solutions; tracer displacement alone does not identify the subtype. The model omits α5 and free neural exposure remains unmeasured. [PET paper Fig. 9](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5786456/) · [Sensitivity replay](pet_sensitivity.tsv)

**Native WWOX context.** In mouse hippocampus bulk (2 control, 3 KO libraries), GABRA2 is 21.22→16.78 TPM, GABRA3 8.74→4.88 TPM, GABRA5 84.15→57.72 TPM and GABRG2 40.77→32.04 TPM. All four directions survive single-library omission. P17 mouse cortex (4/3) shows much smaller shifts where omission can change these signs. At week 15 human organoids (2/4), GABRA3 is 21.25→33.86 CPM and GABRG2 is 14.99→12.62 CPM. The genotype contrast of SLC12A5 is negative in both mouse bulk regions and positive in organoids. In one hippocampal nuclei pool per genotype, GABRA2 falls in both assigned neuronal classes, GABRA3 rises in the excitatory assignment and falls in the inhibitory assignment, and GABRG2 rises in both. Nuclear libraries do not estimate animal-level variability. Bulk expression and short-read ratios do not establish receptor assembly, cell surface abundance, or native chloride polarity. ## Hypothesis, discrimination and outcome

**Hypothesis.** In WWOX tissue with viable benzodiazepine-sensitive receptors and an inhibitory GABA driving force, favoring α2/α3/α5 functional efficacy may add native inhibitory charge while preserving the first evoked excitatory response. Inhibitory potency may differ between recipient cell types. Potentiating GABA-A on inhibitory interneurons or in a preparation with a depolarizing GABA reversal could instead weaken net inhibition or alter shunting and rebound. The effect remains a circuit hypothesis.

**One discriminating comparison.** Use a paired genotype-by-compound comparison in Wwox-null and matched control hippocampal preparations, recording darigabat and the broader benzodiazepine-site reference diazepam with vehicle and benzodiazepine-site blockade. Verify free parent and comparable overall site engagement independently of the circuit outcome. Measure native GABA reversal with a chloride-preserving recording, first evoked excitatory recruitment, absolute inhibitory charge during a fixed-input train and later spontaneous burst burden. Retaining first recruitment and gaining charge with reduced later bursts, especially in null relative to control and with a blockade-sensitive difference from diazepam, supports the selective comparator. This shows a site-dependent phenotype. Assigning α2, α3 or α5 requires an independent subtype-resolved contrast. A reduction in bursts with reduced first recruitment, absent gain in charge or persistent effect under effective blockade lowers the priority. Analyze animal or independent line as the unit and report spontaneous events separately from the evoked measures. This comparison complements the [SV2A strategy](../padsevonil/README.md) and the [neurosteroid comparison](../../ganaxolone_tonic_conductance_20261123/README.md).

**Pediatric feasibility and stop rule.** A search of ClinicalTrials.gov by three sponsor names returned 17 distinct records, all with minimum eligibility 18 years. The studies above establish adult tolerability limits and leave pediatric formulation, free neural exposure, effects of background medicines, respiration and developmental outcomes unresolved. Hold research priority at a conditional ex vivo comparator. Reopen when a preparation gives paired local exposure, native GABA reversal and inhibitory charge with stable first recruitment, or when age-relevant exposure and functional data become available. Independent review could change this decision if a native subtype-specific contrast or conflicting trial estimand emerges. The current uncertainty is in those missing observations.
