# Darigabat comparison, frozen 28 November 2026 UTC

**Context policy:** SYNTHESIS. This question was recorded after discovery of the photosensitivity paper and REALIZE registry and before opening outcome tables. The intervention is darigabat (PF-06372865, CVL-865). Existing comparators are the benzodiazepine-site arm of the padsevonil study and the native GABA/chloride analysis for ganaxolone. Count only independently observed units in statistical comparisons.

**Predictions.** A therapeutically useful functional preference for α2/α3/α5-containing GABA-A receptors should provide extra inhibitory recruitment at similar measured engagement, while preserving the first evoked response. A global benzodiazepine-site ligand signal can coexist with negligible potentiation at α1. A reduction in light-evoked EEG activity may depend on stimulus threshold, recording state and an intact GABA driving force. It can coexist with unchanged spontaneous seizures. If local GABA is depolarizing, current potentiation can increase excitation or shunting depending on reversal potential, conductance and timing. A sedation or general network-suppression rival should reduce recruitment in both branches.

**Comparison.** Use the focal-seizure registry as the mature comparator for the acute crossover. Extract prespecified outcomes, endpoint-specific populations, varied windows, rescue/dropout handling, and controlled harms. Do not combine endpoints across study designs. Re-derive accessible contrasts. Compare α-subunit binding and functional response in a single assay context; compare total human PET with the model's subtype assumptions. In established WWOX models, recheck α-subunit, γ2 and native chloride proxies in region-, stage- and preparation-specific files. Propose one discrimination at matched local exposure, native polarity and initial recruitment, with a reference that gives broad GABA-A potentiation.

**Decision rule.** Retain a conditional subtype comparator if the perturbation can separate α1 functional efficacy from broad binding and if the trial transport remains unresolved. Raise WWOX research priority only for a selective electrographic and absolute inhibitory recruitment benefit at compatible local exposure and native polarity. Lower priority for input suppression, incompatible exposure, or a null native functional contrast. Pediatric feasibility requires a separate exposure, observation and tolerability record.
