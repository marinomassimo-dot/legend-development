# Padsevonil and independently verified target engagement

**Frozen at selection, 28 November 2026 UTC.** Checkpoint #184 calls for a non-myelin strand with measured exposure and a functional perturbation. No padsevonil or UCB0942 dossier appeared in the continuous register, current portfolio or assignment search at `c940d2079`. Existing brivaracetam and levetiracetam work supplies the WWOX comparator. Public adult PET and randomized epilepsy studies make a distinction between occupancy and function testable.

**Hypotheses.** At matched SV2A occupancy, the incremental response could track short-lived GABA-A modulation, long-lived presynaptic occupancy or a different balance of release from inhibitory and excitatory neurons. Bulk SV2A engagement with no functional response would lower candidate priority. An apparent efficacy signal confined to an exploratory endpoint or a smaller study would have less weight than replicated prespecified results. Measure the direction of GABA's action independently of recording chloride and arousal.

**Decision rule.** Recalculate the exposure–occupancy window and separate measured PET, inferred occupancy and trial exposures. Reproduce arm-level contrasts when denominators permit. Keep focal-seizure and developmental outcomes separate. A treatment signal without a verified second-target contribution does not earn a dual-mechanism inference. Conditional ex vivo comparator status requires a test that can separate the alternatives without relying on disappearance of bursts alone.

**Proposed discriminator.** Within the WWOX preparation, match SV2A occupancy and direct first recruitment to a selective SV2A reference. Separate the short second-target window from the period after it fades. Delay-matched vehicle and measured synaptic release, inhibitory recruitment and spontaneous recurrence test proximal function. If a difference tracks the second-site window and blockade of that site removes it at equivalent SV2A engagement, it favors the GABA-A contribution. If it persists after second-site withdrawal or tracks inhibitory presynaptic loss, the receptor-modulation interpretation weakens.
