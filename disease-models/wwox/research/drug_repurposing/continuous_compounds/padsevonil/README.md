# Padsevonil in WWOX loss of function

[Printable research brief](../../view/research_reports/sv2a-inhibitory-recruitment.pdf)

**Disposition.** Retain padsevonil as a conditional ex vivo comparator for the contribution of benzodiazepine-sensitive GABA-A modulation at matched SV2A engagement. Defer systemic pediatric WWOX repurposing. The clinically unsuccessful program supplies a direct lesson for LEGEND: adult target occupancy can be measured or plausibly inferred while the relevant inhibitory-circuit function remains unresolved. The [pretest](pretest.md) fixed the causal alternatives before the numeric replay. Context policy was SYNTHESIS.

## Exposure, occupancy and function

1. **Measured adult PET.** With [¹¹C]UCB-J in healthy adults, two observations following the initial tested exposure were reported above 98% SV2A tracer occupancy at two hours. Eleven volunteers supplied usable SV2A PET data in Muglia. A fixed 100% maximum and Hill coefficient of 1 gave plasma EC50 3.1 ng/mL and EC90 27.9 ng/mL. Sustained high occupancy at clinical regimens was simulated using trial PK. The separate flumazenil study enrolled seven healthy men. At the highest regimen, the mean of the regional maxima was 13.4% in three men. At two and six hours most volunteers were at or below 5% averaged over regions. Occupancy was temporally limited and a quantitative plasma–GABA-A relationship was not established. PET measured neither SV2B nor SV2C engagement. [Muglia, PET Results and Figs. 2–4](https://doi.org/10.1093/braincomms/fcaa183)
2. **Internal PET check.** Re-reading the displayed observations in Koole Table 1 gives first and second scan means of 82.23% (10 people) and 39.21% (seven). Methods print 82.1% and 41.2%, with a second-scan range of 16–63% versus 2.2–66.2% for the table. The seven paired entries show a mean change of −42.94 points with heterogeneous times and exposures. The paper does not reconcile those outputs. Both support an earlier-versus-later group contrast, and the discrepancy precludes fitting a time constant or matching individual PK from the table. Koole and Muglia report identical EC50 and EC90 estimates with the same intervals in closely related Leuven PET work. Cohort independence is unverified, so this dossier treats the calibration as one evidential line. The early regional blood-flow decrease involved the thalamus and other regions. Because scan order and exposures were adaptive, and GABA-A occupancy was not measured in the same scans, blood flow cannot separate SV2 effects, GABA-A modulation, vigilance and vascular explanations. [Koole, Methods §§2.1–2.4, Table 1 and Results](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nsa.2023.101133)
3. **Trial exposure bridge.** EP0091 Supplement Fig. S4A reports *whole-blood* geometric mean troughs. The [digitized replay](trough_bridge.tsv) uses the separately reported blood/plasma sampling comparison at late times (ratio 0.60–0.70) and applies the adult SV2A equation (C/(3.1+C)) to inferred *total plasma*. At week 12, the lowest and highest arms had displayed parent troughs of approximately **19 versus 55 ng/mL** in blood, implying central estimated SV2A occupancy of **90.5% versus 96.5%** at those geometric means. Across weeks 8, 12 and 16 the highest arm's central estimates are 96.5–98.0%. Across the combined pixel, partition and EC50 sensitivity ranges, its lowest estimate is about 94.7%. These are scenarios, not trial PET measurements, true means of nonlinear occupancy, confidence intervals, or a statement that every participant reached the same occupancy. The adult bridging data include the phase 2b trial. Free juvenile neural exposure, active desmethyl contribution and the second target's occupancy in trial participants remain separate measurements. [Rademacher, Supplement p. 9](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9712475/#epi412656-sup-0001) · [Kramer, Fig. 3 and Table I](https://doi.org/10.1208/s12248-023-00866-7)

**Mechanistic countercheck.** A primary binding study reports nanomolar binding to SV2A/B/C and slower dissociation from SV2A than brivaracetam or levetiracetam. Measured dissociation from the adult human protein at 37 °C is a biochemical characteristic and leaves release kinetics in WWOX unknown. In the functional GABA-A primary, oocyte α1β2γ2, α2β2γ2 and α3β2γ2 EC50s were 295, 1,737 and 2,089 nM at GABA EC20. α5β2γ2 was 281 nM and α4β2γ2 showed no potentiation under that assay. The distinct CHO α1β2γ2 comparison gave 138 nM and 41% relative efficacy versus zolpidem. The native rat cortical preparation gave 208 ± 99 nM (n = 8). These are assay- and subunit-specific responses. A low average flumazenil displacement neither specifies which receptor subtype is engaged nor predicts net inhibition in a developing circuit. [Wood, binding study original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31619465/) · [Niespodziany et al., Table 1 and Results](https://doi.org/10.1111/epi.16497)

## WWOX-specific evidence

The [native bulk replay](native_bulk.tsv) shows SV2A at about 288→278 TPM in P17 mouse cortex and 143→85 TPM in mouse hippocampus. Human week-15 organoids instead give 182→216 CPM. SV2B falls in both mouse bulk regions and rises in organoids (6.09→19.73 CPM). SV2C declines in cortex and increases in hippocampus. The human SV2C rise begins below one CPM. Sample numbers are 4/3, 2/3 and 2/4 respectively. One-library omission intervals describe directional sensitivity. The RNA scales are kept separate. [GSE158682](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) · [GSE158681](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) · [GSE156243](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243)

In [pooled hippocampal nuclei](cell_types.tsv), SV2A falls in both assigned neuronal classes, while SV2B is substantially more abundant in excitatory than inhibitory assignments and goes **309→378 CPM** in the excitatory class. In inhibitory assignments the SV2C signal rises from **54 to 153 UMIs** (33→82 CPM). That sparse pool is a lead to check in individual animals. In the same pools GABRG2 rises in both assigned neuronal classes, while it falls in hippocampal bulk and organoids. SLC12A5 falls in mouse bulk and rises in organoids. The aggregate transcript measurements cannot assign protein abundance to terminal or receptor subtypes, determine GABA driving force, or prove release selectivity. The prior WWOX brivaracetam analysis already demonstrates why normalized late excitation can look favorable while absolute inhibitory charge also falls. ## Predicted comparison

**Hypothesis.** If benzodiazepine-sensitive postsynaptic modulation adds a useful effect in WWOX-deficient tissue, the benefit should appear only while measurable potentiation is present and increase inhibitory recruitment with a preserved first excitatory response. A competing mechanism is prolonged presynaptic engagement or recruitment of SV2B/C. A separate rival is suppression of both branches or altered GABA polarity.

**One matched comparison.** In stage-matched WWOX-deficient and corrected preparations, compare padsevonil and the existing brivaracetam reference at equal *measured SV2A engagement*. For each arm, compare vehicle and a functionally validated benzodiazepine-site antagonist with its own vehicle and baseline effect. Observe while the second-site effect is present and again when it is below detection, rechecking SV2A engagement at each window. Measure excitatory and inhibitory first-pulse and cumulative charge, native GABA reversal relative to resting and spike potentials, evoked inhibitory recruitment and spontaneous recurrence. Keep the animal or independent preparation as the unit.

- An incremental benefit that tracks direct GABA-A potentiation and is removed by the antagonist, with SV2A engagement and first recruitment preserved, supports the second-site mechanism.
- Persistence after second-site blockade with changed absolute release favors a presynaptic explanation. This result leaves SV2A residence and SV2B/C effects unresolved. Test isoform dependence only if that branch survives.
- Equivalent suppression after occupancy matching, or a drop in bursts coupled to poorer initial or inhibitory recruitment, lowers priority even when total engagement is high. Reopen therapeutic priority only for a genotype-specific functional gain with preserved inhibitory recruitment and a verified exposure bridge. Independent review could change interpretation if paired local concentrations and target-specific functional recordings become available.

## Pediatric feasibility

Padsevonil is an investigational oral compound registered by substance identity as UNII 0R1HN52K0N. The three cited controlled trials had a minimum age of 18 years. The phase 3 register states that the sponsor stopped development for adjunctive focal-onset seizures. Pediatric preparation, free juvenile neural exposure, WWOX efficacy and sustained developmental endpoints remain unverified. The adult experiments used up to three planned background antiseizure medicines and screened out strong metabolic inducers in the later protocols. Dedicated human pharmacokinetic studies identify CYP3A4 and CYP2C19 involvement and interactions with several antiseizure and other drugs. The observed excess somnolence means that a future functional research endpoint must explicitly distinguish seizure reduction from levels of alertness or broadly reduced circuit recruitment. [FDA substance identity](https://precision.fda.gov/uniisearch/srs/unii/0R1HN52K0N) · [Phase 3 protocol and status](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03739840) · [Human interaction data, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38932723/)
