# Edaravone in WWOX loss of function

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · `context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`

**Disposition.** Retain as a conditional probe of lipid oxidation in WWOX-deficient neural tissue. Park for pediatric trial priority. The strongest low-concentration result is a pretreatment effect against induced apoptosis in rat PC12 cells and supports an exposure-matched test in the relevant preparation. Neither that effect nor the oligodendrocyte and human motor-neuron results identify an endogenous lipid-peroxidation lesion in WWOX.

## Exposure and tests that could change the decision

Estimated adult oral free plasma peaks are 0.76–0.99 µM, based on group totals and 92% serum binding in vitro. Parent is measured in CSF in animal infusion studies. The initial comparison found 0.37–0.42 µM at one continuous-infusion condition in cynomolgus monkeys, with neither an oral equivalent nor human free brain exposure resolved. The induced mouse iNIL motor-neuron result crossed half of the absolute resazurin response at roughly 13.6 µM nominal bath, about 14–18 times those estimated adult group plasma peaks. That graphical crossing comes from three technical wells per condition in a single differentiation batch per line. It gives the scale of the readout for a strong RSL3 insult, with no free-bath measurement. The published viability assay permits a direct effect on redox-dependent fluorescence as a competing explanation. Genetic GPX4 sensitivity in the source system and the edaravone result under RSL3 answer different questions. A distinct liposome assay compared parent edaravone with a lipid-preferring derivative. Approximate inhibition of TBARS formation was 82% for parent and 57% for the derivative with aqueous initiation, and 26% and 43% with lipid-phase initiation. Fig. 2B is the aqueous AAPH condition and Fig. 2C is lipid-phase AMVN, as identified in the caption and the directional Results narrative. The available low-concentration studies therefore establish induced cell-death protection under some conditions, with chemically resolved changes in native neural phospholipids at low free exposure still unverified. ## Native WWOX premise and falsifier

**Held measurements.** The existing selenium screen finds GPX4 and ACSL4 bulk RNA both lower in P17 mouse cortex and human week-15 organoids. In the P17 hippocampal nuclear dataset, Gpx4 in excitatory cells is 29.0→29.8 UMI CPM, while Acsl4 is 48.6→29.6. In the assigned OPC class Gpx4 is 39.6→53.7 and Acsl4 is 47.2→39.5. One nuclear pool per genotype, low counts in some classes and different tissue denominators constrain transfer. Readout direction also differs across classes. Native lipid substrate and oxidation, active GPX4 and the fraction due to cell composition remain separate questions. The held Wwox rat model records lower cortical MAP2 and a reduction in mature oligodendrocyte populations, with no detected loss of NeuN-positive neurons in the tested comparison. It makes a sublethal development or circuit endpoint more relevant than generic prevention of acute cell death. No native oxidized phospholipid measurement for the matched preparation was located in the records examined. [Held bulk and nuclear source](../sodium_selenite/README.md) · [Rat model Figs. 3–4, Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6678113/)

**Hypothesis and counterprediction.** If propagation of membrane oxidation contributes to impaired recruitment or first-sheath maturation, untreated juvenile Wwox-deficient cultures should show an increase in absolute oxidized PUFA phospholipids and their fraction of matched unoxidized parent species at comparable cell composition, or selectively accumulate them after the same patterned workload. At a measured free edaravone exposure anchored to plausible neural availability, an early fall in chemically resolved oxidized phospholipids with stable GSH, GPX4 activity, ATP and viable-cell counts would support an effect on propagation. Measure the effect of a short exposure together with a distinct pretreatment condition, fixed activity and an orthogonal radical-trapping comparator. In the subsequent matched comparison, require more absolute mature cells or new sheaths and durable evoked output after washout. Early lipid suppression without subsequent function lowers priority even if cells survive.

If the effect requires prolonged pretreatment, shows no early lipid response and depends on trophic signaling, the Li result predicts RET/GDNF pathway engagement preceding rescue. An intervention testing RET dependence needs a baseline effect check in the same preparation. An early glutathione/GPX4 change instead favors a capacity mechanism. Proportional loss of both oxidized and unoxidized lipid, or lower total signal as the cell population changes, fails the propagation test even when generic oxidation dyes improve. A phenotype only under a provoked insult keeps a positive-control role. These comparisons use the [selenium](../sodium_selenite/repletion.md), [CoQ](../ubiquinol/decision.md) and [GlcNAc maturation/denominator](../n_acetylglucosamine/exposure_and_branching_20261127.md) checkpoints already held.

**Pediatric feasibility and reopen rule.** The abstract of a 17-child cerebral ischemia series describes clinical exposure with no reported hepatic or renal complications. Even under complete ascertainment and independent observations, zero events in 17 gives a one-sided exact 95% upper limit of 16.2% for that short-series risk. Follow-up and chronic safety are unresolved, and the acute vascular population cannot provide WWOX efficacy or exposure. The current US label states that pediatric safety and effectiveness are unestablished and lists hypersensitivity and sulfite reactions. Reopen only when measured free neural exposure, early native lipid oxidation, and preserved developmental or patterned function are observed together, with absolute cell and lipid denominators. Independent review would matter if it recovered a replicated, exposure-matched native neural lipid result. The current decision hinges on that missing measurement.
