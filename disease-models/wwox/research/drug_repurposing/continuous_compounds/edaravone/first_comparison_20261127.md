# Edaravone first exposure comparison

**Status.** Provisional checkpoint, with no WWOX disposition yet. See the [opening discriminating test](pretest_20261127.md).

FDA Table 9 gives arithmetic mean total parent peaks of 1,656 ng/mL in fasted healthy adults (n=42), 1,903 in ALS after oral administration (n=9) and 2,163 in ALS after gastrostomy tube administration (n=6). Divide by the parent mass of 174.20 g/mol to obtain 9.51, 10.92 and 12.42 µM. Apply the label's in-vitro free fraction of 0.08 as a simple sensitivity estimate to obtain 0.76, 0.87 and 0.99 µM. These are group estimates for plasma, with no matched concurrent CSF. The feeding-route comparison uses separate populations. PMDA study 95 reported mean parent CSF at day 29 of 0.0645 and 0.0726 µg/mL in males and females at its lowest continuous-infusion exposure in cynomolgus monkeys (0.370 and 0.417 µM). A regulatory report gives a separate single-infusion CSF/plasma concentration ratio of 0.50–0.65 in dogs. Do not transport either animal estimate to oral human exposures.
