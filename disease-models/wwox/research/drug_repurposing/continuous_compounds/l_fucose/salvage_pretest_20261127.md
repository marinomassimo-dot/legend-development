# L-fucose | Neural salvage versus rapid excitatory release

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · `context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. Fixed contrast before reading the WWOX panel results. Earlier work separated N-site occupancy from Golgi branching and terminal processing. The next test asks whether an intact L-fucose salvage route can restore a specific core-fucose deficit in WWOX without increasing early glutamatergic recruitment.

**Why now.** Oral substrate replacement has a reported peripheral fucosylation precedent in SLC35C1-CDG. A Fut8-haploinsufficient mouse study reports brain core fucosylation and separate inflammatory signaling outcomes. By contrast, a hippocampal physiology study reports rapid presynaptic excitation with free L-fucose, an effect that could matter in a WWOX cortex with NMDA-dependent recurrence. These are three distinct model/endpoint comparisons. A fucosylated milk oligosaccharide is a different intervention. [Pediatric precedent, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10590041/) · [Fut8 mouse primary, discovery](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10772726/) · [Hippocampal primary, discovery](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12594168/)

**Fixed RNA comparison.** Use the held P17 Synapsin-Cre mouse cortex and hippocampus and week-15 human WWOX-null organoids. Compare FUK/FCSK and FPGT for salvage with GMDS and TSTA3/GFUS for de novo supply, SLC35C1 for Golgi delivery, FUT8 for core transfer, and IL6ST, GRIA2 and L1CAM for candidate specific outputs. Keep the older source symbols `Fuk/FUK` and `Tsta3/TSTA3` for replay. Report separate single-library omissions and descriptive class-assigned P17 hippocampal nuclear pools. RNA direction can choose an assay and cannot prove GDP-fucose pool size or glycopeptide occupancy. [Previously verified input manifest](../../memantine_magnesium_20261123/inputs.tsv) · [Prior neural glycopeptide comparison](../d_mannose/neural_site_checkpoint_20261126.md) · [Alias reference for FCSK](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/197258) · [Alias reference for GFUS](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/7264)

**Discriminating prediction.** Compare one measured neural L-fucose exposure with matched vehicle in age-matched WWOX and control systems. Read early evoked glutamate release and late recurrence before new glycan incorporation, then follow labeled cytosolic GDP-fucose and a site-resolved core-fucosylated glycopeptide normalized to its carrier protein. If the limiting step is substrate supply, WWOX cells should show a baseline deficit, donor recovery and subsequent site and functional correction. A rise in donor with no site correction points to compartment delivery or transfer. Rapid release or recurrence before measurable site correction supports a separable fast effect. A delayed site change without functional improvement lowers priority even if the glycan readout shifts. Distinguish catalytic salvage dependence of fast signaling from dependence on Golgi incorporation. Measure early and delayed physiology at equal local exposure and preserve native inhibition as a separate readout.
