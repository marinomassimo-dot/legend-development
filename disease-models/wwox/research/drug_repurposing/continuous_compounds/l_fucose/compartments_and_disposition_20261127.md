## Inference, hypothesis and experiment

The [initial WWOX replay](salvage_pretest_20261127.md) places a possible limit after the first salvage step. FPGT and FUT8 pool-level RNA are lower in both mouse regions and organoids. Pooled excitatory nuclear FUT8 does not follow the bulk sign after depth normalization. The data leave total FUT8 activity and the available GDP-fucose pool undetermined. An increased fraction of administered label in a glycan can reflect source substitution with stable absolute occupancy. Quantify unlabeled and labeled pools and site occupancy separately.

Fut8-null hippocampal work connects lower core fucosylation to altered AMPAR co-association, higher postsynaptic AMPAR abundance despite similar total expression, and altered glutamate-linked calcium activity. This justifies screening **a core-fucose-resolved GluA2 glycopeptide** from the juvenile WWOX AMPA branch. Establish detectability and linkage in the native preparation first. The source used Fut8 deletion, and its receptor pull-down leaves individual glycosite causality unverified. WWOX-specific GluA2 editing and splice findings leave native stoichiometry unresolved. [Fut8-null primary, Results Figs. 4–5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25979332/) · [Held cortical and editing comparisons](../../perampanel_20261122/clinical_mechanistic_update_20261126.md)

**Discriminating experiment.** In age-matched Wwox-deficient and control cortex, compare vehicle with L-fucose at identical measured extracellular exposure, with fast and delayed endpoints in the same preparation. Verify an initially deficient, linkage-resolved GluA2 glycopeptide relative to carrier protein or end the site-specific test. Quantify the native free parent and labeled fucose-1-phosphate, GDP-fucose and the site-resolved core glycan. Measure release directly and retain first and D-APV-sensitive late recruitment and native inhibitory output. If parent entry and labeled fucose-1-phosphate are adequate but GDP-fucose delivery is impaired, prioritize the second salvage step. If substrate label reaches GDP-fucose while site correction fails, prioritize transfer or the acceptor state. If the site corrects at matched receptor abundance and the later postsynaptic effect requires FUT8 while early release is preserved, the site-linked hypothesis gains support. An early increase in release or late recurrence at matched exposure lowers priority regardless of the glycan result. Prespecify measurement precision for preservation of early recruitment and inhibition.

## Pediatric feasibility and reopening

Published oral free L-fucose experiences cover distinct congenital disorders. Recovery of peripheral selectin ligands in SLC35C1-CDG and absence of response in a separate low-transport-capacity setting establish mechanism-dependent precedent. A case report documented autoimmune neutropenia and reappearance of erythrocyte H antigen in LAD II, with no observed intravascular hemolysis. GFUS-CDG fibroblasts from one line recovered AAL signal from 27.05% to 86.08% of control under in vitro substrate exposure. The two-person FUT8-CDG series reported modest protein-specific biochemical changes and minimal change in the global MALDI profile. These observations involve different defects, exposure and endpoints. [SLC35C1 positive report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10590041/) · [SLC35C1 nonresponse](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11264438/) · [LAD II adverse events](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12406889/) · [GFUS fibroblast and clinical report](https://doi.org/10.15252/emmm.202114332) · [FUT8-CDG biochemical report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7719959/)

The 27 November 2026 ClinicalTrials.gov record for NCT07432490 lists a recruiting adult GLUT1 deficiency crossover study with ataxia scales and safety outcomes. It uses oral free L-fucose and has no posted results. Pediatric formulation standardization, exposure and interaction with background antiseizure medications remain unresolved in WOREE. The human CSF observation warrants a two-level exposure and target biomarker strategy in research. It leaves routine pediatric use and efficacy unestablished. Reopen for a native site deficit and reproducible genotype-preferential correction with preserved early recruitment and inhibition and no rise in late recurrence, or clinical evidence specific enough to change the WWOX estimate. A review can change this decision when new analytical coverage establishes linkage, local exposure or a direction of circuit effect. [Trial registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07432490)
