# Guanfacine in WWOX loss of function: an exposure and circuit comparison

**Data scope.** Rat medial prefrontal layer V normalized interictal event frequency, persistent and transient sodium-current amplitudes in dispersed pyramidal neurons, rat hippocampal CA3 extracellular burst-frequency concentration-response, rat medial prefrontal layer V–VI miniature EPSC and IPSC frequencies in Hz, rhesus dlPFC task firing in spikes/s, pediatric plasma guanfacine in ng/mL and estimated free plasma in nM. WWOX RNA uses mouse cortex and hippocampus TPM, human week-15 cerebral organoid CPM and P17 mouse hippocampal cell-class UMI CPM.

## Counterchecks and pediatric feasibility

**Workbook clustering.** Pasierski reports two animals for the five 10 µM slice event recordings. The publisher's workbook Arkusz1 I32 says “3 animals”. It does not map the five rows to those animals. All five rows decrease. Under every partition across two nonempty animal groups the equal-animal mean paired decrease ranges from 26.3% to 38.2%. With three groups the range is 26.9% to 35.5%. These are bounds on possible groupings of the observations, with no population confidence claim. The experimental direction survives reweighting. A recording-level p-value does not resolve animal-level precision. The same workbook gives the two medians reported above for the sodium currents, independent of the malformed printed persistent-current IQR. [Source workbook](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1007%2Fs43440-023-00458-4/MediaObjects/43440_2023_458_MOESM1_ESM.xlsx) · [Derived output](external_summary.tsv)

**Competitive-antagonist counterexample.** Transferring the CA3 functional pKb of 7.92 yields an idazoxan Kb of 12.0 nM. In a reversible competitive model, 20 µM idazoxan gives a 1,665-fold rightward shift of an agonist's concentration-response. Starting from the CA3 guanfacine EC50 of 49 nM gives an apparent EC50 of about 81.6 µM. With a unit Hill slope, 100 µM could still elicit 55% of its own modeled maximum. This uses a CA3 epinephrine-calibrated antagonist constant and guanfacine potency. It demonstrates a plausible failure of the single antagonist challenge to exclude α2A contribution, with no quantitative estimate of that contribution in prefrontal cortex. Region, drug access, stimulus, receptor reserve and slope may differ. Test subtype engagement at the relevant low concentration and confirm antagonist efficacy in the same preparation. [Biggane et al., antagonist results and Schild method](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10710878/) · [Pasierski et al., Fig. 2B](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43440-023-00458-4)

**Exposure scale.** Boellner et al. enrolled 14 children aged 6–12 and 14 adolescents aged 13–17 with ADHD. Mean total plasma peaks in the repeated 4 mg extended-release study condition were 10.1 and 7.01 ng/mL, or 41.0 and 28.5 nM. With the labelled approximate 70% binding, the corresponding estimated mean free peaks are 12.3 and 8.55 nM. The lowest tested prefrontal sodium-current concentration, 10 µM, is 244–351 times the two total means or 812–1,170 times the two estimated free means. The 49 nM CA3 point estimate is about 4.0–5.7 times those estimated free means, with wide reported dispersion and different compartments. TGA reports 13.3 nM binding affinity at recombinant human α2A in a separate assay. The TGA panel found significantly broader binding at 10 µM than at up to 100 nM. Together these data make the receptor-linked route a testable candidate and the high-concentration sodium result a poor clinical bridge. Measured free pediatric neural exposure, concentration-time coverage and WWOX target engagement are unresolved. Neither the binding affinity nor the slice EC50 estimates clinical occupancy. [Boellner, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17723079/) · [EMA SmPC §5.2](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/intuniv-epar-product-information_en.pdf) · [TGA nonclinical evaluation, p. 15](https://www.tga.gov.au/sites/default/files/auspar-guanfacine-180503.pdf)

**Feasibility.** Prolonged-release pediatric ADHD experience covers ages 6–17 under the labelled criteria. Early-onset severe WWOX, dysphagia or enteral delivery, baseline low alertness and concurrent sedating drugs alter trial feasibility. The formulation's label describes whole-tablet administration, hypotension, bradycardia and somnolence. The overall guanfacine-treated pool reports somnolence in 40.6% and sedation in 10.2%. Carbamazepine and other CYP3A4/5 inducers can lower exposure. The label notes potential increase in valproate exposure and central additive effects during co-treatment. Use formulation, co-medications, arousal, cardiovascular measures, EEG and evoked or behavioral function as separate factors in any feasibility assessment. [EMA SmPC §§4.1–4.5, 4.8 and 5.2](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/intuniv-epar-product-information_en.pdf)

## Decision and discriminating observation

**Hypothesis.** At measured low exposure, guanfacine could suppress spontaneous recurrent CA3 activity through a receptor-dependent route while changing input summation separately in superficial cortex. The regional sign could therefore diverge in WWOX with increased input resistance and weaker inhibitory control. Cortical HCN1 and HCN2 RNA changed in opposite directions. That leaves the cAMP sensitivity of native channel assemblies, surface localization and cell-type balance open. An increase in dendritic gain paired with loss of inhibitory recruitment would be an unfavorable combination, even if aggregate spontaneous firing fell.

**Discriminating comparison.** In juvenile WWOX and matched controls, measure parent exposure and compare CA3 and superficial cortical responses over the same low exposure range at matched baseline voltage and stimulus history. Quantify recurrent events, evoked excitatory and inhibitory charge, dendritic input summation and direct HCN and sodium-current readouts in separate established preparations. A useful receptor-mediated response would reduce excessive recurrent events preferentially in WWOX while preserving patterned recruitment and inhibitory charge, with loss of the effect after a confirmed α2A perturbation and an identifiable dendritic HCN component only where implicated. Pan-neuronal spike loss, a fall in evoked inhibition or amplified unreliable cortical recruitment falsifies the useful-response hypothesis. A sodium-current change that persists after receptor and HCN control identifies a competing route. Fixed applied current can produce different voltage excursions in WWOX, so a reproducible sag difference alone cannot identify altered HCN conductance.

**Disposition.** Retain α2A as a region- and exposure-specific preclinical probe. Park guanfacine for unselected pediatric WWOX trial priority. Reopen on a measured low-exposure, genotype-selective benefit with spared inhibitory and patterned excitatory function. An independent review of the spreadsheet denominator or the competitive scenario cannot fill the remaining missing WWOX response and central exposure. Those data would have higher decision value.
