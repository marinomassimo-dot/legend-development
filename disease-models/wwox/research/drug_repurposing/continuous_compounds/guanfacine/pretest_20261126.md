# Guanfacine, pretest for dendritic gain and generalized suppression in WWOX loss of function

**Data scope.** Juvenile superficial cortical pyramidal electrophysiology in the Wwox neuronal-deletion mouse, P17 mouse cortex and hippocampus TPM, week-15 human WWOX-knockout cerebral organoid library CPM, P17 mouse hippocampal assigned UMI CPM and public assay-specific guanfacine concentrations. Cortex and hippocampus are separate contexts.

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. Scope opened from the checkpoint published at `1aa6eb8f1`. Prior WWOX evidence includes higher input resistance and voltage sag at a fixed current in juvenile cortical pyramidal cells. The actual voltage excursion differs across genotypes. The existing ivabradine and lamotrigine dossiers cover direct HCN inhibition and a possible increase in dendritic HCN current respectively. The opening pharmacological evidence is the reported α2A-linked effect on dendritic integration in primate prefrontal circuits and a separate rat slice report of reduced pyramidal sodium current. These preparations leave the sign of transfer to juvenile WWOX superficial cortex open. **Fixed held panel.** ADRA2A/B/C for the receptor family, ADCY1/8, GNAI2 and PDE4B for cAMP context, HCN1/2/4 and PEX5L for channel and neuronal localization context, and SCN1A, SCN2A and SCN8A for the competing sodium-current route. Report raw assigned nuclear counts and bulk contrasts under all one-library omissions. A hippocampal nuclear pool cannot locate a receptor in juvenile cortical dendrites, and low counts cannot rule out protein or functional engagement.

**Discriminating prediction.** At measured free exposure and matched membrane voltage and stimulus history, a useful α2A-dependent response would move absolute recruitment by coherent patterned inputs toward the isogenic or littermate reference while preserving inhibitory charge and limiting recurrent bursts. Local reduced cAMP and a shift in dendritic HCN activation would precede the effect, which should diminish when α2A signaling is prevented or the relevant HCN contribution is occluded. Measure single-input and clustered-input responses separately. If the compound instead increases already excessive dendritic summation or weakens evoked inhibition, lower priority even if arousal falls. In a second contrast, persistent sodium-current or spike-output reduction after confirmed α2A and HCN occlusion supports a separate route. Compare pyramidal and inhibitory cells, and keep behavioral state, sodium current, spontaneous discharges and evoked input transfer as distinct outcomes.

**First falsification.** Check whether the concentrations in the rat sodium-current and event assays are compatible with measured pediatric free exposure and whether each response had a validated receptor-pathway intervention. Extract unit, age, preparation, cell type, event or current denominator and source of concentration. Reuse absence of clinical seizure evidence where established instead of treating slice events as seizures. Track pediatric formulation, baseline vigilance, blood pressure, pulse and interacting concomitant treatment separately.
