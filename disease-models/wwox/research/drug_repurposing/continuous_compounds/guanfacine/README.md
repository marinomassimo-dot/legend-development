# Guanfacine opening checkpoint in WWOX loss of function

**Data scope.** P17 mouse Wwox neuronal-deletion cortex and hippocampus TPM, week-15 human WWOX-knockout cerebral organoid library CPM and P17 mouse hippocampal cell-class UMI CPM, with one pool per genotype. The [pretest](pretest_20261126.md) fixes the competing functional predictions before inspection of these data.

**Held result.** Mean cortical ADRA2A was 15.43→14.39 TPM (4 controls, 3 KO), compared with 13.08→11.12 library CPM in human organoids (2 controls, 4 KO) and 7.06→7.66 TPM in mouse hippocampus (2 controls, 3 KO). Each ADRA2A contrast changes sign under a single-library omission. Mouse cortical HCN1 rose from 3.36 to 4.23 TPM and HCN2 fell from 105.30 to 64.37 TPM, with directions retained under every omission. These are distinct transcripts on the same tissue scale. The observations constrain a model of uniformly increased HCN expression without measuring the gating of either isoform. Hippocampal ADRA2A was sparse in the excitatory pools (5→6 assigned UMIs) and inhibitory pools (0→1). Sampling region, transcript detection and cell classification limit their relevance to cortical receptor location. [Bulk contrasts](bulk.tsv) · [Nuclear counts](nuclei.tsv)

**Disposition.** Retain the regional α2A comparison as a preclinical probe. Park guanfacine for pediatric WWOX trial priority pending measured exposure and preserved inhibition in the same system that shows a beneficial effect on excess recurrent activity. The high-concentration sodium-current effect is poorly matched to pediatric exposure. The CA3 result provides a distinct receptor-scale test. [Source-data replay, antagonism check and decision](exposure_and_disposition_20261126.md)
