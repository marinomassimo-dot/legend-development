# L-alpha-glycerylphosphorylcholine in WWOX loss of function

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Decision.** Retain α-GPC (choline alfoscerate, sn-glycero-3-phosphocholine) as an isotope comparator for phosphatidylcholine turnover. Defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. The new re-extraction finds accumulation under additional proteotoxic stress and leaves a baseline shortage unestablished. Pediatric studies confirm systemic transfer of the choline moiety. Intact brain α-GPC, a WWOX-selective response and durability of any structural change remain unmeasured.

## A downstream metabolite associated with a specific context

**Published data.** In the adult female fly **overexpression** experiment, Supplementary Table 7 row 245 assigns an exact standard match to GPC (258.11 m/z, 711.67 s). The reported main effect of Wwox overexpression is −0.482 log2 (about 0.72-fold, adjusted p = 0.0667) and the Aβ42 main effect is −0.882 log2 (about 0.54-fold, adjusted p = 0.00058). The paper's discovery threshold was 10% FDR. The interaction in that experiment has adjusted p = 0.245. The workbook tab is named Table 7, although the heading inside it says Table 6. The peak is absent from the knockdown experiment's *differential feature list*. The public assignment file records **no area for any of the 40 experimental samples** in either experiment for this feature. Those empty cells cannot be used as zeros or as evidence of unchanged GPC. [Lucas-Clarke et al., pp. 4–5, 15 and Supplementary Tables 4, 7](https://doi.org/10.1093/braincomms/fcag385) · [Public assignment and spectra](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights/MTBLS12344)

**New analysis.** The [prospective protocol](pretest.md) uses the declared standard match and the ±5 ppm, 30-second replay convention used previously by LEGEND for leucine. In the 12 pooled replicates, the independently integrated positive-mode signal peaks at 711.67 s. Median pooled area is about 2,511 times the median of six blank areas, with pooled Spearman agreement 0.888 against the deposit. Within each of the two six-sample QC sets, agreement is 0.314 and 0.771. The combined estimate can be influenced by separation between the sets. No secondary peak passing the prespecified rule was resolved in the local window. The matching m/z and retention time support the standard-assigned feature, with no new tandem-MS, isomer resolution or absolute concentration estimate. **Inference.** Increased GPC in the stressed knockdown could reflect faster membrane phosphatidylcholine breakdown, a bottleneck in GPC utilization, altered cell populations or a combination. Whole-head abundance cannot diagnose a supply deficit in developing WWOX neural tissue. The result lowers the case for blind substrate replacement and supports measuring both directions of turnover. Direct conversion of GPC by WWOX is unsupported.

## Mammalian counterchecks

- In mouse P17 bulk cortex and hippocampus, Enpp6 RNA shifts −1.22 and −1.08 log2(1 + expression), respectively. In the 15-week human cerebral organoid contrast it shifts +0.10 and has mean expression below 0.2 CPM in both arms. Gpcpd1 decreases in the three bulk settings (−0.21, −0.81 and −0.10), but pooled hippocampal nuclei broadly disagree with a generalized cellular reduction. For example, excitatory cells have ~155 to ~159 CPM and newly formed oligodendrocyte nuclei ~584 to ~1,123. In the same newly formed class, Enpp6 decreases from ~658 to ~279 CPM (282 to 97 reads). There is one pool per genotype. The directions justify checking the extracellular route in the juvenile mouse oligodendrocyte lineage and do not establish enzyme activity, a cross-species deficit, or the metabolic phenotype. [Bulk and omission analysis](native_bulk.tsv) · [Class counts](cell_types.tsv)
- In Morita et al., free choline rescues proliferation of choline-deprived Neuro2a and NIH3T3 cells, whereas α-GPC works when the cells express ENPP6. Figure 3I traces the same distinction into phosphatidylcholine in a rat **hepatoma** line. In mice, the strongest in vivo brain result in Figure 4D uses intravenous **β-GPC**, which has different blood stability from α-GPC. At three hours the α-GPC comparison shown in Figures 4D–E is **liver**, with reduced labeled PC after intraperitoneal but not intravenous exposure in the Enpp6 knockout. Each comparison was reported at n = 5. Figure 2 shows a separate myelin phenotype, without demonstrating α-GPC rescue in brain. The paper also distinguishes positional isomers that share a mass. [Morita et al., Figs. 2–4 and relevant Results](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep20995)
- As an independent turnover counterexample, skeletal-muscle Gpcpd1 loss directly reduced GPC hydrolysis and raised tissue GPC, alongside altered phosphatidylcholine composition and function. This is a cell-type and tissue-specific genetic perturbation. It establishes a mechanism by which GPC can rise when use is impaired. It supplies no WWOX or neural direction. [Aisyah et al., Fig. 2 and Results](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-024-06298-z)

The studies use separate enrollment periods and the same registry identifier. Neither was designed to assess WWOX disease, longer-term neurological function, or interaction with antiseizure treatment. Enteral nutritional experience supports feasibility of measuring systemic analytes. ## One discriminating comparison

**Hypothesis.** In a WWOX-deficient developing axon–oligodendrocyte system, accumulation will favor a processing bottleneck if it coexists with reduced appearance of choline and newly made phosphatidylcholine from α-GPC. High GPC with enhanced release of prelabelled PC into GPC and preserved conversion capacity would instead favor membrane breakdown. A low starting pool with intact conversion that selectively responds to substrate would support precursor limitation.

**Test.** In WWOX-deficient and matched corrected preparations at the same maturation stage, compare labeled α-GPC with labeled free choline at matched local free choline, confirming parent α-GPC is resolved from positional isomers. Follow labeled GPC to choline, phosphocholine and defined membrane PC species, and follow loss of a prelabelled PC pool into GPC in the same comparison. Record absolute pools, glial and axonal survival and ensheathment within the same cell cohort. Include early axonal recruitment and latency over measured distances if the preparation supports them. A response to matched free choline alone would account for a generic donor effect. Accumulated labeled GPC with little flux into PC narrows the target to use, while normal conversion with greater PC-to-GPC release points to membrane turnover. Reopen α-GPC as a candidate only after a measured native deficit or bottleneck, a selective improvement in absolute lipid and function, and verified local exposure. ## Boundaries from the substrate scout

- **Steroid replacement.** The human WWOX fusion-protein study relied on bacterial lysates and an NAD(P)H readout with appreciable host background. A mouse androsterone-to-androstanediol result is a distinct chemical lead, with no confirmed direction in juvenile mammalian WWOX brain. The next observation is paired isotope-resolved product and precursor in matched neural material. This supplies no endogenous replacement rationale for an additional steroid now. [Original enzyme paper](https://doi.org/10.1515/znc-2011-1-210) · [Laboratory assay critique](../../wwox_function_reference_it6_20261029.md) · [Steroid comparison](../../ganaxolone_steroid_flux_20261123/README.md)
- **Oxysterols.** CYP46A1 occupancy and 24S-hydroxycholesterol depletion have different time courses. The existing priority stays with an actual regional WWOX pool measurement and add-back-sensitive circuit response before using soticlestat as a bypass. The existing dossier already covers that question. [Soticlestat exposure and disposition](../soticlestat/occupancy_and_disposition_20261127.md)
- **Acid–base result.** In Ludes-Meyers et al., Table 3 reports serum total CO₂ 14.50 in four P18 Wwox-null mice versus 21.67 mEq/L in three WT, a relative decrease of about 33%. This is total CO₂ in a systemic chemistry panel. Paired pH and blood gas pCO₂ would be needed to establish the primary acid–base disturbance and any respiratory response. This result supplies no biochemical basis to select an acid–base compound here. [Primary report, Table 3](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0007775)

## Reproduction

Use Python 3 with NumPy and the two replay scripts in this folder. Pass a directory containing the three public MetaboLights JSON metadata responses to `replay_fly_gpc.py`, followed by the original TSV assignment file, a directory with the 38 positive mzXML spectra named in the two digest tables, and an output directory. The deposit locations and hashes for common metadata are in the [existing metadata manifest](../l_leucine/fly_leucine_metadata_inputs.tsv). Spectra are served under `FILES/DERIVED_FILES/Positive/` of the study. Recreate the RNA contrasts with `replay_native_expression.py` and arguments for the repurposing tree, the laboratory files directory, and a fresh output directory. Both scripts use the same published accessions and validated metadata as earlier replays. Only derived values and original code are distributed here. The primary publications are accessible without payment and raw spectra remain with MetaboLights.
