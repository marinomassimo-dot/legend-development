# Lamotrigine, dendritic excitation and inhibitory recruitment

**Reason to select this AED.** WWOX neuronal-deletion mice have abnormal cortical recruitment. The shared HCN analysis already considered inhibition of the current with ivabradine. Lamotrigine provides a different question about a possible increase in dendritic HCN current alongside use-dependent sodium-channel inhibition. Pyramidal dendrites and the interneurons that target them may respond differently. [WWOX inhibitory recordings audit](../../inhibitory_defect_sci47_20261112/README.md) · [Prior HCN dossier](../ivabradine/README.md)

**Prediction before data extraction.** At matched free drug concentration, baseline voltage and spike history, preferential dampening of excitatory dendritic integration should reduce the response to clustered distal inputs in WWOX more than the response to a single input, while preserving evoked inhibitory charge and interneuron recruitment. If interneurons instead lose spikes before pyramidal dendritic summation changes, any reduction in network firing could mask weaker inhibition. Measure somatic and dendritic input separately, direct HCN current, and absolute excitatory and inhibitory responses. Condition on genotype and distinguish changes in resting potential from changes in conductance. A developmental benefit requires a separate measure.

**First comparison.** Recover age, cell and dendritic identity, recording solutions, free versus nominal exposure, stimulation history and signs from the original Poolos and paired-recording studies. Starting sources are [the rat dendritic study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12118259/), [the initial basal inhibition study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19776733/) and [the inhibitory transmission study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27455251/). Check direct pediatric unbound exposure and relevant concomitant treatment independently. The source pointers do not establish a selective WWOX effect.

**WWOX benchmark already held.** At the same −200 pA current step, mean voltage sag in juvenile superficial cortical pyramidal neurons was 1.18 mV (WT), 2.04 (heterozygotes) and 2.57 (KO). The KO–WT difference is 1.39 mV. Because input resistance also changed, the same injected current can drive a different voltage excursion and recruit a different amount of HCN current. This anchors a specific comparison for lamotrigine: match the voltage trajectory as well as running a same-current condition, and record inhibitory cells in parallel. The observation establishes the baseline phenotype only. [Original laboratory extraction, rows O04–O05](../../membrane_hypotheses_20261108/output/observations.tsv)

An independent clinical evidence check was requested on whether a published WWOX case records an attributable change with exposure or withdrawal, including baseline seizures and concomitant medication. The request itself supplies no evidence of response.
