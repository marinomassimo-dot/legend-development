# Montelukast in WWOX loss of function

28 November 2026 UTC · SCI_CODEX · INV-06 · Question-driven comparison. [Opening checkpoint](pretest.md) · [Printable brief](../../view/research_reports/montelukast.pdf).

**Decision.** Retain montelukast as a conditional comparator for an oligodendroglial timing mechanism and an injury-related leukotriene mechanism. Defer systemic pediatric WWOX development. GPR17 direction differs by precursor stage, the available human exposures leave juvenile oligodendroglial engagement unresolved, and treated healthy organoids show a separate neuronal liability.

## Original data replay

**Native WWOX.** Untreated P17 bulk [cortex GSE158682](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) (4 WT/3 KO) and [hippocampus GSE158681](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) (2 WT/3 KO) showed Gpr17 decreases of 58.08→32.23 and 50.98→28.56 TPM respectively. The sign survived single-library omissions. BCAS1 and MBP also fell in both regions. CYSLTR1 was below 1 TPM in both arms. The week-15 [human WWOX organoids GSE156243](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243) have very sparse GPR17 (0.06→0.30 CPM) and measurable CYSLTR1 (6.38→5.60 CPM). Omission tests describe sensitivity and do not supply inferential significance.

**Precursor stage.** In [GSE158684](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158684) pooled hippocampal nuclei, the standard classifier includes Gpr17 in the committed oligodendrocyte precursor (COP) score. In the sensitivity replay, removing Gpr17 from scoring leaves the targeted observation free of that circularity. Gpr17 rises in OPC-class nuclei (17.32→37.53 CPM, positive nuclei 14/225→38/325), falls in committed precursors (183.17→105.35 CPM, 33/106→31/135) and falls in newly formed oligodendrocytes (70.26→52.06 CPM). The precursor sign survives both class definitions. Class counts are descriptive, with no biological replication across these pools. Microglia have detectable Cysltr1 (45.24→41.44 CPM), Ltc4s rises (173.99→718.26), and Alox5ap falls (34.80→6.91). The divergent enzyme RNA signs leave cysteinyl leukotriene production unresolved.

**Human stage coverage.** In the published fetal oligodendroglial subset [GSE155488](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE155488), GPR17 is detected in 35/142 precursor-gated columns, 52/101 TCF7L2-positive MBP-negative columns and 63/97 MBP-positive columns. CYSLTR1 is detected in 1/359 overall. These independent marker gates use neither receptor and are descriptive, with no donor labels. Overlapping expression in the fetal reference makes an absolute stage label premature. This lineage-enriched dataset has limited leverage on microglial CYSLTR1.

**Treated healthy human organoids.** In [GSE255033](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE255033), the raw deposit contains fractional estimated counts. A transparent descriptive analysis uses library-sum CPM across five libraries per arm, each pooling three organoids from a single line. ADCY1 falls from 73.29 CPM in control to **47.54** and **51.17** at the two nominal concentrations. Both directions survive every single-library omission. CAMK2B, DLG4 and GRIN2B show the same direction. GPR17 and CYSLTR1 are below 1 CPM in all arms, and MBP declines in both treated groups. The RNA result gives a distinct neuronal readout and leaves localizing drug action to GPR17 or inferring myelin changes unresolved. Deposited RNA-seq gives no raw electrophysiology replay. ## Hypothesis and one discriminating comparison

**Inference.** An actionable WWOX bottleneck would be an abnormal duration of the early precursor transition. The upward early and downward later Gpr17 signs put a broad sustained GPR17 excess upstream of all WWOX myelin defects in doubt. A separate lesion-context effect could depend on microglial or vascular cysteinyl leukotriene signaling. The treated-organoid ADCY1 result adds a competing effect on physiological activity, which itself can influence myelination.

**Hypothesis and comparison.** At matched local free montelukast, compare early-stage WWOX-deficient and isogenic control axon–glia preparations with oligodendroglial GPR17 interruption versus interruption of CysLT1 in the leukotriene-responsive compartment. Verify each target function in its own cells. Stage-match the starting cells and track time to first persistent compact sheath per initially eligible axon, survival, absolute physiological recruitment, and conduction at matched recruitment. Measure neuronal ADCY1/cAMP and spontaneous activity alongside the axonal readouts.

- If the drug shortens an abnormal precursor lag, the structural and conduction response should be occluded by verified GPR17 interruption and preserved after CysLT1 interruption at matched exposure. Check for residual room to improve to distinguish occlusion from a ceiling.
- If the effect depends on the leukotriene compartment, it should follow a measured CysLT signal and be occluded by compartment-specific CysLT1 interruption. Persistence after GPR17 interruption would strengthen that interpretation.
- A transient change in MBP or cell stage without durable first sheaths, loss of physiological recruitment, or a response restricted to implausible free exposure would leave the compound a secondary comparator. A reduction in spontaneous activity and a developmental rescue remain separate outcomes.

## Pediatric feasibility and stopping condition

The US [montelukast sodium label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=482dcc92-b47f-4ea6-854a-f5ac2aea7842) includes age-specific oral granules, chewable and film-coated tablets for approved respiratory indications, with asthma from 12 months. It reports over 99% protein binding and roughly 40% lower montelukast AUC with phenobarbital. Applying a 1% scenario to the label's mean total plasma peak for ages 12–23 months gives an illustrative systemic ceiling of 9.59 nM, conditional on that binding fraction applying. The label carries a boxed warning for serious neuropsychiatric events. Existing pediatric systemic use does not supply a free local bridge, a WWOX functional endpoint or transfer of the adult film findings. [FDA warning](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-communications/fda-requires-boxed-warning-about-serious-mental-health-side-effects-asthma-and-allergy-drug).

Reopen for matched local exposure and evidence that a defined precursor stage can form durable first sheaths while preserving recruitment. An independent review of a new local PK or target-dependence assay could change the decision. Repeated reading of the published aggregate endpoints leaves those inputs unavailable.

## Reproduce

Run from the repository root. The held inputs are fingerprinted in the replays. Public files are available from GEO, including the [treated counts](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE255nnn/GSE255033/suppl/GSE255033_raw_count.csv.gz), [treated matrix metadata](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE255nnn/GSE255033/matrix/GSE255033_series_matrix.txt.gz) and [fetal lineage matrix](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE155nnn/GSE155488/suppl/GSE155488_All_fetal_OL_lineage_subset.csv.gz).

```bash
drug=disease-models/wwox/research/drug_repurposing
dossier="$drug/continuous_compounds/montelukast"
held=/home/desktop/legend-development/files
out=$(mktemp -d)
python3 -B "$dossier/replay_expression.py" "$drug" "$held" "$out"
python3 -B "$dossier/replay_treated.py" "$held/geo/GSE255033" > "$out/treated_counts.tsv"
python3 -B "$dossier/replay_fetal.py" "$held/deepening_downloads/ascorbate_loop_20261124/GSE155488_fetal.csv.gz" > "$out/fetal_detection.tsv"
python3 -B "$dossier/replay_comparisons.py" > "$out/comparisons.tsv"
for table in native_bulk cell_types mixture stage_blind treated_counts fetal_detection comparisons; do
  cmp "$out/$table.tsv" "$dossier/$table.tsv"
done
```
