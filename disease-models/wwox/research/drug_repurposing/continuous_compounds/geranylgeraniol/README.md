# All-trans-geranylgeraniol · Rab modification and Golgi cargo in WWOX-LoF

**Unit and region.** P17 mouse cortex and hippocampus bulk libraries, week-15 human isogenic forebrain organoid libraries, P17 mouse hippocampal nuclei in one pool per genotype, mouse CA1 slice recordings, cultured human epithelial and carcinoma cell lines, and peripheral measures in humans. Prespecified 27 November 2026, SCI_CODEX. [Frozen mechanism and counterproof](pretest_20261127.md)

**Disposition.** Retain defined geranylgeraniol (GGOH) as a conditional laboratory probe of geranylgeranyl substrate salvage. Defer pediatric WWOX priority. A baseline deficit in geranylgeranyl diphosphate (GGPP) and native Rab modification, a corresponding trafficking defect and a measured neural exposure would change this judgment.

## Mechanistic controls that change the test

- **A label can replace endogenous prenyl groups.** Onono et al. measured geranylgeranyl-cysteine derived from labeled GGOH in total protein digests of breast carcinoma cells and non-tumorigenic mammary epithelial cells. The MDA-MB-231 experiments in Fig. 6C–D were small (n = 2). Label incorporation increased under mevinolin, and total geranylgeranyl-cysteine decreased in the experiment described with Fig. 8. Whole-protein digestion loses protein identity. The labeled GGPP data in Table 2 used labeled **farnesol**, a different input. The transfer to WWOX requires its own absolute GGPP measurement and protein-specific modified fraction, in addition to isotope enrichment. [Onono et al., Results, Figs. 6–8 captions and Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3779739/)
- **The Golgi hypothesis has an orthogonal biochemical check.** In non-neural cell lines, the authors of the statin–glycosphingolipid study used 10 µM **GGPP** in the rescue arm. Rab6, Rap1A and HDJ2 were tracked as separate type II geranylgeranyltransferase, type I geranylgeranyltransferase and farnesyltransferase controls. Rab transferase inhibition recapitulated a subset of the lipid and glucosylceramide synthase changes. The study tests a donor or transfer perturbation in non-neural cells, and it did not establish GGOH access to the WWOX Golgi. Oxidation to geranylgeranoic acid is a competing route supported in hepatoma systems. [Statin study, Fig. 3 and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4691287/) · [Oxidation primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32094232/)
- **A rapid synaptic response leaves new Rab modification unresolved.** Kotti et al. measured CA1 field potentiation in Cyp46a1-deficient and control slices. A five-minute pulse around induction produced transient potentiation in KO slices (13 slice recordings, 8 WT comparator recordings). Two pulses, the second at 35–40 minutes on the experiment clock, maintained potentiation (10 treated versus 18 untreated KO recordings). The later pulse alone did not initiate potentiation (11 versus 17). Some bath and electrode experiments used nominal **0.2 mM** parent (about **58 µg/mL**). Gunmetal slices have partial Rab transferase activity and responded to GGOH in a statin challenge (21 versus 25 slice recordings). Acute 3-PEHPC did not reduce LTP in 18 treated versus 20 control gunmetal slices. The authors explicitly qualify the inference on immediate inhibitor access and residual transferase. Longer inhibitor exposures did impair LTP. Farnesol could restore the Cyp46a1 phenotype without statin, which makes a chemically selective interpretation conditional. GGOH did not rescue Apoe-null slices under the reported conditions. LTP leaves cellular exposure, new Rab modification and the identity of the active chemical species unresolved. [Kotti et al., Fig. 2 and Fig. 5 captions, Results and Discussion](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.0805556105) · [Molar mass](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C24034739&Units=SI)

## One discriminating comparison

Use developmentally matched WWOX-deficient and corrected neural preparations from independent differentiations. First quantify native GGPP and the modified fraction of one Golgi Rab such as Rab6A. Then compare vehicle with labeled all-trans-GGOH at verified unbound parent exposure. Measure absolute intracellular parent, labeled GGPP, geranylgeranoic acid and labeled versus total modified Rab6A over an early and a delayed window. Distinguish unmodified, singly modified and doubly modified Rab peptides where the assay qualifies them. Normalize to total Rab6A and use soluble/membrane partition only as an orthogonal localization readout. Track a defined ER–Golgi cargo time course with matched initial cargo load, coat recruitment and cell viability. Keep sterols and carrier comparable. A validated Rab transferase II perturbation with preserved type I control and an upstream donor-depletion positive control separate substrate salvage from transfer capacity. Keep first neural recruitment and late recurrence separate from any acute slice response.

- **Donor limitation.** Low GGPP and Rab occupancy at baseline are followed by labeled donor, increased Rab occupancy and modified amount and recovery of cargo kinetics. Blocking Rab modification while holding donor exposure comparable removes the trafficking rescue. A labeled fraction with falling total modification is insufficient.
- **Downstream trafficking limitation.** At measured exposure, donor and Rab occupancy recover while cargo residence, exit or delivery remains abnormal. Normal baseline occupancy, or an occupancy response limited by the transfer apparatus despite recovered donor, also lowers the substrate-first hypothesis.
- **Acute or parallel mechanism.** Early functional change before quantifiable new Rab modification, or recovery despite verified interruption of Rab transfer, shifts value toward parent/metabolite or membrane effects. An early null is interpretable only after qualifying the time-dependent assay sensitivity. A reduction in physiological first recruitment weakens the functional interpretation.
