# What does exon 5 inclusion imply for an assembled receptor?

Using the neuronal-deletion hippocampal coding-TPM means solely as hypothetical subunit fractions (control 0.2209, KO 0.3223), consider a receptor with two GluN1 positions. Equal RNA-to-synaptic-protein transfer, a common pool, and equal detectability are assumptions. The assay in Li et al. compared individual splice variants with GluN2A or GluN2B. It leaves the relevant phenotype of a receptor with one cassette-bearing and one cassette-free GluN1 unresolved in that comparison.

If subunits paired independently, receptors bearing *two* cassette-positive units would rise from 4.88% to 10.39%, while receptors bearing *at least one* would rise from 39.30% to 54.07% (rounded, see table). Thus even idealized RNA-to-protein transfer predicts different magnitudes depending on whether one or two included copies confer resistance to glycine priming. Neither number is an effect size for current or surface receptor loss. If pairing varies across genotypes, the feasible ranges overlap under either rule and even the sign is unconstrained. Cell-class mixtures further weaken extrapolation.

Discriminator: first establish the delayed response of a receptor population with verified mixed GluN1 occupancy under the same conditioning and activation history as each uniform population. A simple mixture of two expressed RNAs does not assure mixed occupancy at individual receptors. In the WWOX comparison, separately record initial activation, surface receptor change and later current after matched co-agonist exposure and glutamatergic challenge. A difference confined to nominally different starting media leaves the splice explanation unresolved. These calculations are a mechanistic sensitivity analysis. They add no pediatric exposure or efficacy evidence.

This narrows the proposed novelty to the delayed trafficking endpoint and its relevance in WWOX. Check matched basal surface abundance and normal internalization of uniform controls in the same expression system: modifications used to enrich mixed receptors may themselves affect trafficking. Published kinetic intermediacy cannot predict whether one included copy prevents delayed surface loss.
