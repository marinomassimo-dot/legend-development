Cappelli et al., supplemental PDF p. 19, Fig. S1C–D, WT CA1 currents. Panel C (n=5, 8–10-week mouse study) shows reduced current on a later glycine challenge after a 1 mM exposure and intervening wash. Approximate bar heights from the displayed axis are 97% of initial baseline after wash and 79% on the subsequent challenge (about −18 percentage points); these are visually estimated group means, not paired raw data or an uncertainty interval. The summary bars, time-course application labels and caption identify the second exposure as 250 µM, but the small example trace labels it 10 µM. Panel D (n=4, dynasore) likewise has discordant annotations. Do not treat either panel as evidence for depression by 10 µM glycine. Tables S1/S2 are antibody and primer lists. The authors direct readers to request raw data. Individual paired uncertainty for the main WT versus GlyT1 comparison was not recovered here.

Decision: use separate exposure-naïve preparations at each concentration for the main WWOX comparison. Test carryover deliberately in a separate matched sequence, comparing vehicle→wash→challenge with glycine→wash→same challenge. A concentration series always run low-to-high or high-to-low could confound the genotype-specific crossover with exposure history even after apparent baseline recovery. Tissue age, unmeasured local concentration and lack of an independent surface assay in the displayed sequence limit transfer.
