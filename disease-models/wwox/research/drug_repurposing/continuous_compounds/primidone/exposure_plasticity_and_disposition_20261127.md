# Primidone | Exposure, TRPM3 state and circuit plasticity

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

Units and preparations include µM and µM·h of total serum drug in adults, nominal µM in recombinant rat or human TRPM3 preparations as specified, normalized fluorescence in expressing cells, sIPSC frequency and decay in P14 rat CA1, and EEG counts per mouse after focal kainate injury. The [opening comparison](README.md) contains the held WWOX RNA and the prespecified parent/metabolite hypothesis.

**Disposition.** Retain primidone as a conditional preclinical comparator for TRPM3-dependent recruitment. Defer pediatric WWOX trial priority. The adult paired pharmacokinetic experiment shows that primidone exposure alone is an unreliable description of the mixture. Recombinant channel activity, inhibitory recruitment, late pathological recurrence and evoked plasticity all need separate measurements. A developmental WWOX-selective benefit with local exposure and preserved physiological responses remains the reopening criterion.

**Clinical and pediatric boundary.** In the retrospective TRPM3-variant series, 10 of 31 people with epilepsy were taking primidone at ascertainment. Nine were evaluable and had a composite improvement across EEG, seizures or other domains. In the seven with drug-resistant epilepsy, five became seizure-free, one stayed seizure-free and one reported a reduction greater than 50%. Six of the ten used monotherapy and four combination treatment. The cohort includes 22 literature-derived cases, with possible reuse of the earlier Becker report. This selected genotype-specific series supplies a distinct clinical precedent. Concurrent drugs, time at exposure, parent and metabolite levels and maturational endpoints limit mechanistic transfer. The existing oral tablet and older pediatric measurements establish clinical experience. Infant CNS exposure, a feasible WWOX formulation and a selective functional comparison under background antiseizure therapy remain unresolved. [Jolitz et al., Results and Fig. 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11831877/) · [U.S. label](https://www.dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=ae0fa704-818e-4423-8e3a-6cc3390cdd7d)

**Predicted observation and countercheck.** Match WWOX and corrected preparations by developmental state and initial evoked recruitment. Measure unbound primidone, phenobarbital and PEMA, and compare single analytes with a reconstructed mixture at measured proportions. Measure basal TRPM3-related current and the response to a matched ligand challenge. Independently check excitatory recurrence, release-linked inhibitory charge with native GABA reversal, and potentiation. A parent-associated reduction of excess WWOX recruitment with physiological charge and potentiation preserved, disappearing after independent TRPM3 perturbation, would support the channel branch. Reproduction by a metabolite mixture with effects persisting after TRPM3 perturbation would favor a broader mechanism. A drop in pathological spikes accompanied by loss of inhibitory recruitment or potentiation would lower priority even with target engagement. Use the recombinant differential-sensitivity comparison to validate readout specificity before extrapolating to native cells.

**Reopening criterion.** A measured, stage-matched WWOX current or circuit result that separates the parent, the active metabolite and physiological recruitment would justify focused review. External opinion on the published contrasts cannot substitute for those data. Suitable public perturbation data, or paired unbound analyte measurements tied to response, could reopen the decision.
