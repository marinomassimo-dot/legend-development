# Primidone | TRPM3 inhibition by parent and metabolites

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Opening question, 27 November 2026.** Does primidone reveal a recruitable steroid-sensitive calcium current in WWOX? Its direct parent activity in recombinant TRPM3 and reported use in TRPM3-associated developmental epilepsy motivate the comparison. Whether WWOX loss alters TRPM3 or its native recruitment is an open question. The earlier [pregnenolone analysis](../pregnenolone/README.md) concerns precursor conversion and neurosteroid effects and supplies complementary context. [Recombinant and native animal pharmacology](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28106668/) · [TRPM3-associated phenotype series](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39749750/) · [Official formulation and metabolites](https://www.dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=ae0fa704-818e-4423-8e3a-6cc3390cdd7d)

**Predictions locked before the exposure or expression replay.** At verified parent and quantified phenobarbital and phenylethylmalonamide (PEMA), WWOX-specific TRPM3 involvement predicts excess steroid-sensitive calcium or current at equal first recruitment and endogenous steroid context. A parent-associated reduction lost following independent TRPM3 perturbation, with physiological inhibition and recruitment preserved, would favor this mechanism. Comparable loss of evoked response in corrected tissue, or a metabolite response that persists after TRPM3 perturbation, would favor broader suppression. A genotype difference that disappears under matched endogenous ligand favors altered ligand supply over a channel defect. Lower priority if the relevant lineage lacks measurable native current.

**First control.** In the same rat TRPM3 and pregnenolone-sulfate Fluo-4 assay, the reported IC50 is 1.2 µM for primidone and 4.4 µM for phenobarbital, a 3.67-fold potency difference. The figure reports two independent experiments. PEMA stays below half-maximal inhibition at the tested concentrations in the Results description. The relative potency leaves a substantive TRPM3 contribution from phenobarbital possible, depending on local exposure. Phenobarbital is therefore a mixed-mechanism control. The separate human TRPM3 patch-clamp in Fig. 3 tests primidone in both WT and WT+S1133P conditions, with the metabolite comparison measured only in the rat calcium assay. [Becker et al., Methods, Results, Figs. 2 and 3](https://doi.org/10.1111/epi.17586)

**Target coverage.** The contrast in mean log₂(TPM + 1) for cortical mouse bulk TRPM3 is −0.363 in KO (4 control and 3 KO libraries). Human organoids give +0.920 on the supplied organoid abundance scale (2 control and 4 KO), with both directions preserved under single-library omission. The opposing preparations favor two separate physiological comparisons and preclude assuming a conserved WWOX gain of channel function.
