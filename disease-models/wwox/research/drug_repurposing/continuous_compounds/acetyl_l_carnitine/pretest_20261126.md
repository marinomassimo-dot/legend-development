# Acetyl-L-carnitine, prespecified comparison

**Data scope.** Mean TPM in juvenile mouse hippocampus and neuronal-deletion cortex, mean library-normalized CPM in week-15 human cerebral organoids, and counts per million nuclear molecules in assigned P17 mouse hippocampal cell classes. Untreated WWOX models throughout.

`context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN`. The held C0 and pantothenate comparisons motivate the question. The primary tracer and compartment comparisons address how an externally supplied acetyl group reaches different sinks. Source excerpts and measurements are distinguished from WWOX inference.

**Molecule.** O-acetyl-L-carnitine (C2). Report parent-equivalent values only for an identified formulation. Free L-carnitine (C0) and acetate are controls. A formulation and non-WWOX pediatric experience are separate from central intact C2 exposure.

**Premise and panel, fixed before the new comparison.** The held C0 dossier observed low CRAT RNA within pooled excitatory and inhibitory nuclei. Reuse that premise and run the same analysis for CRAT, SLC22A5 and SLC25A20 (uptake and exchange context), SLC25A42 (CoA import and exchange context), ACSS1 and ACSS2 (mitochondrial and nucleo-cytosolic acetate routes), CROT (alternative peroxisomal acetyl transfer), PDHA1 and CS (pyruvate-derived carbon and citrate), and ACOT2 (an acyl-CoA hydrolase). Keep COASY from the held locus-resolved pantothenate comparison. Mean log2(1+abundance) knockout minus control and every single-library omission describe directions across bulk datasets. Nuclear sums and molecule denominators describe classes within one pooled library per genotype. Fewer than 20 summed gene molecules in either arm flag an unstable within-class comparison. No heart, muscle or tumor cell flux is used as a WWOX observation.

**Discriminating prediction.** If intact C2 supplies useful mitochondrial acetyl-CoA, pulse [acetyl-13C2] C2 at a measured feasible parent exposure. An early rise in the absolute amounts of mitochondrial M+2 acetyl-CoA and M+2 citrate should precede improved recovery of ATP and evoked output under matched workload in WWOX neurons, with mitochondrial free CoA preserved. A residual CRAT dependence should alter those effects after a matched change in enzyme activity that is interpretable with viability and baseline function controls. Extracellular labeled acetate, the combined C0 plus equimolar labeled acetate controls, and perturbations of ACSS1/ACSS2 or CROT help resolve cleavage and cytosolic/peroxisomal routing. A labeled histone or bulk lipid endpoint cannot substitute for mitochondrial oxidation. Record concentrations and fractional enrichment together, normalize to viable cells and evaluate stimulation and inhibition after washout.

**Rivals and stop condition.** Label can accumulate in C2 or peripheral/cytosolic sinks while citrate and functional recovery stay flat. Reverse acetyl transfer could consume scarce free CoA and worsen residual glucose-derived oxidation. An acetate or C0 control might explain the entire phenotype. Prioritize only a selective functional response coupled to early compartment-specific flux at attainable exposure without measurable depletion of free CoA. Treat uncharacterized parent exposure and lower RNA as reasons to resolve the biochemical premise first.
