## WWOX expression comparison

CRAT is the only transcript in the ten-gene panel that fell in all three bulk contrasts and stayed lower on every single-library omission. Mouse cortex was 28.92 → 24.24 TPM (4 control and 3 neuronal-deletion libraries) and hippocampus 65.06 → 27.96 TPM (2 and 3). Human cerebral organoids were 169.29 → 139.66 CPM (2 and 4). In the mouse nuclear comparison, it also fell in excitatory (18.18 → 15.17 CPM) and inhibitory (24.82 → 21.32 CPM) pools. COASY is lower in all three bulk preparations in the separate locus-resolved analysis. Free mitochondrial CoA, CRAT protein, enzyme activity and route-specific acetyl flux were unmeasured. The potential alternatives differ by preparation. ACSS1 and CROT fall in both mouse bulk samples and rise in human organoids, with those signs surviving each omission. ## Tracer comparison and exposure boundary

- **Developing brain.** After 100 mg/kg of [2-¹³C]acetyl-L-carnitine by intraperitoneal injection to healthy male P21–22 rats, forebrain glutamate C4 label was 0.378, 0.605 and 1.045 nmol ¹³C/mg protein at 15, 60 and 120 min (5, 6 and 8 brains). The 120:15 ratio of group means was 2.76 for glutamate C4 and 4.57 for lactate C3. The 15-min glutamate C3:C4 ratio of means was already 1.44. That is consistent with recycling and multiple turns before the first forebrain sample. It cannot resolve initial transfer or be interpreted as a rate of oxidation. The study reports no quantifiable labeled plasma lactate or alanine and low glucose labeling. Labeled acetate delivery and early intact brain C2 were not quantified with the reported plasma comparator. The acetyl label can survive cleavage, and whole-forebrain NMR cannot allocate a cell type or measure ATP reserve. [Scafidi et al., Methods, Results and Tables 1–2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4949601/) · [Transcribed aggregate inputs](tracer_observations.tsv) · [Replay](replay_tracer.py) · [Ratios](tracer_ratios.tsv)
- **Competing cellular sinks.** In U87MG glioblastoma cells, whole-cell labeling was limited near nominal 10 µM and substantial under standard growth near 1 mM. Lower glucose increased labeling. CRAT loss retained labeling from supplied C2. The CROT/CPT2 and CRAT/CROT editing comparisons reduced labeling of whole-cell acetyl-CoA and palmitate, respectively. Re-expression of either intact CROT or a form lacking its peroxisomal targeting sequence restored whole-cell labeling. This supports CROT catalysis while leaving the compartment of transfer open. C2 and ketone use ranked differently in tested noncancerous cells. These results provide a plausible alternative to mitochondrial CRAT-mediated recovery, while tumor cell enrichment and palmitate are unsuitable as neuronal ATP readouts. Independent held evidence reports CRAT-independent incorporation into histone and fatty-acid sinks under a different cancer model. [Hsu et al., Figs. 2, 4–6, Results and captions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9898754/) · [Held C0 comparison](../levocarnitine/decision.md)
- **Human compartment.** The held ≤1-year diagnostic group (n=15, meningitis excluded) had median lumbar C2 0.19 µM (IQR 0.11–0.23). The 10 µM and 1 mM culture concentrations are about 53 and 5,263 times this median. This is an extracellular scale comparison with a baseline sample, with no estimate of oral uptake or intracellular exposure. Parnetti et al. describe a sequential IV then oral study in 11 older patients with Alzheimer-type dementia. The indexed abstract reports that CSF increased but supplies no absolute CSF concentrations or lumbar sampling time by route. The publisher presents a subscription preview with an older, inconsistent dose summary. Use the corrected PubMed record if referring to the regimen. Neither abstract anchors a pediatric central-exposure range. The ADHD pilot abstract records 112 children aged 5–12 over 16 weeks and no significant primary intent-to-treat endpoint. This is documentation of an oral study population. Exact formulation equivalence, younger ages, concomitant antiseizure medications in WWOX and neural exposure are unresolved. ## Neural functional comparators

- **Primary cortical neurons.** At 1–10 mM nominal C2 in 5 mM glucose, Laird et al. detected no increase in normalized FCCP-stimulated oxygen consumption in DIV7 or DIV14 rat cortical cultures with or without acute glutamate exposure. At 10 mM they measured a decrease after glutamate in DIV7 (Fig. 4D, p < 0.05 versus glutamate without C2). The figure reports 3–6 different neuronal preparations. This is a short, glucose-replete uncoupled respiration comparison. It does not resolve ATP-linked recovery at physiological exposure in WWOX. [Laird et al., Methods and Fig. 4A–D](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3864688/)
- **Mouse oligodendroglia.** Yang et al. report higher OCR-derived ATP-linked respiration alongside higher proton leak and nonmitochondrial OCR after 24 h with a nominal 2 mM C2 addition in differentiated Oli-neu cultures (Fig. 3D–F, 3 independent cultures per group). The 100 µM acute arm tested undifferentiated cells and Slc22a5 knockdown (Fig. 3G–I). Neither arm measures labeled ATP-generating flux or free CoA. At 100 µM in culture, C2 increased MBP-positive area on coverslips and nanofibers, with partial residual responsiveness on nanofibers under Slc22a5 or Crat knockdown (Fig. 2F,H). This is membrane expansion, with no axonal conduction endpoint. The authors report higher total mPFC C2 in socially isolated C57BL/6N mice after oral C2 hydrochloride, 1.251 mmol/kg daily, P21–35 (Fig. 4A, water n=5 and C2 n=4, nmol/mg protein). The regular-housing cohort showed a serum rise without a detected brain rise in the Results summary. Total tissue C2 can include endogenous synthesis or residual vascular signal and cannot be equated with extracellular concentration or drug-derived mitochondrial label. [Yang et al., accepted manuscript, Fig. 2F,H, 3A–L and 4A](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41398-026-04214-z_reference.pdf)

The opposite oxygen consumption outcomes combine a cell-type change, different exposure durations and an endpoint change. They motivate a stage-stratified WWOX comparison that measures ATP-linked respiration and free CoA under matched workload. OCR-driven ATP estimates and broader membrane stains cannot establish that C2 supplies oxidative carbon.

## Oral mouse exposure and the C0 control

I reanalyzed all groups in Supplementary Tables S4, S6 and S8. For total C2 after two weeks of oral C2 hydrochloride, the social-isolation arm increased by 28.91 µM in serum and 62.98 nmol/mg protein in mPFC. The regular-housing increments were 35.16 µM and 17.36 nmol/mg protein, respectively. The direct housing-by-treatment contrast was −6.26 µM in serum (descriptive SE 6.54) and +45.61 nmol/mg protein in mPFC (SE 68.31). Each pair had 5 vehicle and 4 C2 mice. Removing any one mouse left a positive mPFC point estimate (+7.12 to +99.85), but variation in the four treated regular-housing mice was large (SD 124.19). The distinct significance calls reported for the two housing groups supply weak evidence of differential brain uptake. The interaction SE is a descriptive independent-group calculation. It does not account for potential clustering by cage, sex, or unknown timing after the last administration. The mPFC change is protein-normalized total tissue amount. There is no measured extracellular or free mitochondrial molarity for comparison with the culture bath.

In a separate Oli-neu uptake comparison (n=6 cultures per group), the mean total C2 was **9.91 nmol/mg protein after C0**, **3.44 after C2** and **1.38 after vehicle**. The primary OPC wrapping comparison found an increase in sheath area with added C2, with no significant increase under C0. Different preparations and readouts prevent a mediation analysis. Together, they make total C2 a poor surrogate for C2-specific sheath recovery. The next assay should compare early labeled mitochondrial metabolites and sheath function in the same preparation with C0 and matched acetate controls. ## Active checkpoint

The developing-rat experiment supports transfer of the supplied acetyl carbon into forebrain metabolites. The WWOX comparison locates a repeated CRAT RNA decrease and a nonconserved CROT alternative. A selective mitochondrial rescue remains a hypothesis. At measured intact C2 exposure, the discriminating observation would be a rapid increase in the absolute amount of mitochondrial M+2 acetyl-CoA and M+2 citrate before improved ATP and evoked function after washout, with free CoA preserved. A labeled cytosolic product with unchanged neuronal function, recreation by C0 plus labeled acetate, or loss of free CoA and glucose-derived acetyl flux shifts the decision away from that mechanism. Separate mitochondrial fractions and cell types and check recovery of uptake and inhibition after washout. [Controls and failure conditions](pretest_20261126.md)

**Next check.** Compare stages in the held WWOX myelinating models to pick a recipient for the C2 versus C0 plus acetate comparison. Verify whether the OCTN2 and CRAT tests isolate a C2-specific interaction and whether a non-WWOX neural result overlaps a measured post-oral human central exposure. An independent review could change priority if that overlap is supported but isotope identity or compartment remains ambiguous. Without that bridge, finish as a mechanistic comparator and require a WWOX-selective flux–function response to reopen trial priority.
