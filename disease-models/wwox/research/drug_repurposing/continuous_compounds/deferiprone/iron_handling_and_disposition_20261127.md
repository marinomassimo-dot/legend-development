# Deferiprone in WWOX loss of function

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Disposition.** Park for pediatric WWOX trial prioritization. Retain deferiprone as a conditional preclinical probe of excess redox-active iron. The current WWOX comparison gives tissue-dependent expression changes and no direct chemical measurement of an expanded labile iron pool. Three randomized trials in other disorders distinguish lower iron-sensitive MRI signals from uncertain or worse functional outcomes. A WWOX-specific rationale needs the pre-exposure iron state, the lipid response per available substrate, and functional preservation in the same model.

**Developmental countercheck.** In a mouse lineage-specific `Slc11a2` loss model, the NG2-targeted group at P30 showed fewer myelinated callosal axons, with no clear average g-ratio shift. Figure 6 used four animals per group with fibers nested within animals. CC1-positive mature cells were lower at P30 and P60 in the examined regions, while total Olig2-positive counts in that model showed no clear difference (Fig. 7B, six brains per condition). An early Sox10-targeted model showed lower myelin and mature lineage markers (Fig. 8). DMT1 transports several metals and forms a genetic developmental perturbation. This sets a positive control for impaired iron-handling and maturation without establishing an effect of deferiprone in WWOX. [Cheli et al., Figs. 4–8 and relevant Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6199407/)

## Hypothesis, discriminating observation and next action

- **Hypothesis.** A low-peroxide-clearance WWOX state may have excess labile iron in one cell compartment despite preserved or limited iron availability for mitochondrial and myelination functions elsewhere. A competing explanation is substrate and cell-state remodeling with normal or low available iron. Hold developmental stage and substrate availability constant before assessing either.
- **Predicted result for excess iron.** In two stage-matched WWOX/WT preparations with verified local parent exposure, a baseline labile iron excess and greater oxidation per parent phospholipid would be followed by early normalization of labile iron, then lower phospholipid oxidation, then improved absolute patterned output or first-sheath formation. Directly assay mitochondrial aconitase or another iron-dependent respiratory function and early inhibitory recruitment. These guardrails should remain intact in both genotypes.
- **Discriminating observation.** A rapid fall in the iron or oxidation readout with weaker mitochondrial activity, impaired first-sheath formation or reduced physiological recruitment favors depletion or suppression. Establish an orthogonal iron readout with a sensor-competition control, assay total iron separately, normalize oxidized species to their parent lipids and assess viable cell counts. Predefine a comparison that restores iron availability and a non-chelating radical control. If the latter restores function at unchanged labile iron, the comparison favors a downstream oxidation mechanism over the need for chelation. Distinguish early engagement, washout and persistent benefit.
- **Next checkpoint.** First establish the baseline compartment-specific iron and oxidation relationship in matched WWOX preparations. Reopen only for a reproducible excess with measurably selective benefit and preserved iron-dependent function at bounded exposure. Independent review before advancing would be valuable if such a signal is found or an adequately matched dataset materially changes the baseline conclusion.

**Pediatric feasibility.** The US oral solution has a labeled use from age three for specified transfusional iron overload indications, which sets a formulation precedent. This differs from the neurological rationale and baseline iron in WWOX. Agranulocytosis and neutropenia require clinical monitoring in the labeled setting. The analyzed records do not provide a WWOX free neural exposure window, a validated enrichment marker or a therapeutic index for a pediatric study. Regional human MRI engagement cannot supply these quantities.
