# Deferiprone and labile iron versus iron-dependent recovery in WWOX loss of function

**Status.** Opening checkpoint. The WWOX redox programme identifies uncertainty about phospholipid peroxide clearance and the amount of susceptible substrate. The deferiprone question is whether a compartment-specific excess of redox-active iron drives the problem. The alternative is that removing iron lowers oxidation markers while compromising mitochondrial and oligodendroglial function. Pediatric use in transfusional overload and MRI iron changes in PKAN supply precedents for other populations. [Held WWOX GPX4 and substrate comparison](../sodium_selenite/README.md) · [Primary PKAN trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31202468/) · [Pediatric oral-solution label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=f057cf4d-b06f-4fbd-aa2e-68c5e4d8a846)

**Prediction.** Establish basal labile iron, total iron and oxidation per parent phospholipid in stage-matched WWOX and isogenic controls under the same iron-replete conditions. A chelator-responsive iron excess predicts that measured local deferiprone lowers labile iron before excess oxidation falls and before patterned neural or glial function improves preferentially in WWOX. Measure a mitochondrial iron-dependent activity and physiological inhibition or first-sheath formation in the same exposure window. If diminished oxidation tracks an iron-dependent respiratory loss or impaired myelination, the functional signal favors depletion cost. Analyze neuronal and oligodendroglial stages separately.

**Counterchecks.** Verify exposure and chemically distinct iron readouts to distinguish chelator competition with a sensor from altered intracellular availability. Normalize oxidized lipid products to parent species and viable cell numbers. Use a matched comparison that restores iron availability when feasible, a non-chelating lipid-radical control and independent circuit measures. Reduction of total iron or MRI signal is a distribution endpoint. Preservation of normal iron-dependent function is necessary for prioritizing a ferroptosis-related response.
