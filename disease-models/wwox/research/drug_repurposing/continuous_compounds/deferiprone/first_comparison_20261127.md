# Deferiprone opening comparison

**Subsequent checkpoint.** The full comparison resolves the registry denominators, adds a second adult randomized countercheck and replays WWOX expression by model. [Completed comparison and decision](iron_handling_and_disposition_20261127.md)

| Preparation | Opening observation | Boundary for WWOX |
|---|---|---|
| Genetically confirmed PKAN, placebo-controlled 18-month trial | The paper abstract reports 88 assigned (58 deferiprone, 30 placebo) and 76 completed. The results registry distinguishes 89 started from 88 exposed. Basal ganglia iron fell. Mean Barry–Albright Dystonia worsening was 2.48 versus 3.99 points, a reported difference of −1.51 (95% CI −3.19 to +0.16; p = 0.076). | A syndrome with demonstrated regional iron accumulation. An imaging change can coexist with uncertain functional benefit. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31202468/) |
| Early Parkinson disease, 36-week placebo-controlled trial | 372 randomized, 186 per arm. Nigrostriatal iron decreased more with deferiprone while MDS-UPDRS worsened by 15.6 versus 6.3 points (difference +9.3, 95% CI +6.3 to +12.2). | Different disease, stage and outcome. It is a counterexample to inferring functional benefit from iron removal. [Primary study abstract](https://pure.amsterdamumc.nl/en/publications/trial-of-deferiprone-in-parkinsons-disease/) |
| Pediatric transfusional iron overload | The US oral-solution label includes children age three and older in the specified transfusional indication. It carries a boxed warning for agranulocytosis and neutropenia. | Systemic iron overload and monitored transfusion populations provide a clinical precedent with distinct baseline iron and risk. [US label §§1, 5.1 and 8.4](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=f057cf4d-b06f-4fbd-aa2e-68c5e4d8a846) |
| WWOX neural tissue and organoid comparison | The held transcript assay found lower GPX4 in some settings and lower ACSL4 in cortex and organoids. | Lipid susceptibility and clearance can move together. Neither RNA nor total iron alone establishes an excess labile iron pool or functional rescue through chelation. [Held transcript and countercheck](../sodium_selenite/README.md) |

**Replay the endpoint separation.** Subtracting the reported within-trial group changes gives 2.48 − 3.99 = −1.51 in PKAN and 15.6 − 6.3 = +9.3 in Parkinson disease. These are different scales and populations. Keep them separate and contrast their directions with the reported change in regional iron. [PKAN primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31202468/) · [Parkinson primary abstract](https://pure.amsterdamumc.nl/en/publications/trial-of-deferiprone-in-parkinsons-disease/)

**Checkpoint.** First verify whether WWOX tissue has a compartment-specific and functionally consequential labile iron increase. Then test whether a lower iron or oxidation readout coincides with preserved iron-dependent respiration and improved neural function. [Registered prediction](pretest_20261127.md)
