# Racemic ketamine | Prespecified inhibitory reserve comparison

Frozen 27 November 2026 after title and abstract triage, the previous ketamine report and the public-view input check. Results for the new circuit comparison and replay had not been extracted.

- Shallow NMDA block on pyramidal cells can coincide with a loss of presynaptic GABA output. In a matched preparation, a falling late pyramidal NMDA charge together with a selective loss of directly recruited PV-to-pyramidal GABA, especially later in the train, and stable or higher spontaneous recurrence would move the next decision toward inhibitory reserve. An inhibitory postsynaptic response following glutamatergic stimulation alone cannot locate that loss at the inhibitory terminal.
- If direct and disynaptic inhibition are preserved and matched late NMDA charge yields directionally similar spontaneous burst effects under ketamine and the reference blockers, favor suppression of excitatory drive. If direct GABA and glutamate release both change, read out both arms. If ketamine shifts intrinsic sag or the first AMPA response at matched block with no selective loss of direct GABA output, follow that independent observation.
