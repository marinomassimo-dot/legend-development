# Sodium L-lactate first comparison

27 November 2026 · SCI_CODEX · context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Checkpoint.** Added lactate is used by the adult human brain. Separate rodent preparations report greater NMDA-linked evoked current and lower excitatory recruitment through a receptor-dependent pathway. The preparation, exposure and outcome determine what can be inferred. WWOX-selective energy rescue remains to be measured. [Opening prediction](pretest_20261127.md)

| System | Exposure and observation | Transfer limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boumezbeur et al., adult human isotope MRS | At plasma lactate **0.8–2.8 mmol/L** after labeled substrate exposure, measured downstream brain labeling supported entry and utilization. The fitted transport half-saturation was **5.1 ± 2.7 mmol/L**, reported as a 68% interval. | Blood concentration and isotope-derived flux are distinct from the extracellular lactate concentration near a neuronal receptor. The paper's model of maximal fuel contribution is separate from directly measured labeling in this range. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20962220/) |
| Yang et al., cultured cortical neurons, fixed agonist application | At nominal **10 mmol/L** L-lactate, peak inward current after the same glutamate/glycine input was **−2.30 ± 0.40 nA** (8 experiments), versus **−0.89 ± 0.17 nA** (10 experiments) without lactate. The ratio of magnitudes, `2.30 / 0.89`, is **2.58**. An NMDA antagonist substantially reduced the enhanced response. | The fixed-input assay measures post-agonist current. Release requires a separate observation. This bath concentration is on a different scale from the adult plasma comparison. Dependence on NMDA signaling does not fix the molecular site or imply that the redox effect transfers to native juvenile WWOX. [Primary Results and Fig. 3A](https://www.pnas.org/content/pnas/111/33/12228.full.pdf) |
| Skwarzynska et al., mouse hippocampal slices | Bath lactate at **3 mmol/L** hyperpolarized CA1 pyramidal cells (**9 cells**, paired). Separately the lactate-associated change in spontaneous EPSC frequency was detected in WT (**6 cells**) with no detected effect in Hcar1-null cells (**7 cells**). The paired-pulse result at **20 ms** appeared in WT but not knockout (**5 pairs** per group). | The spontaneous analysis includes repeated events within cells. Paired-pulse change supports a presynaptic contribution. The Results report no detected spontaneous IPSC change (8 cells), which leaves evoked inhibitory recruitment and matched net function open. Within-group significance alone does not estimate a genotype interaction. Estimating the net response requires both readouts in the same preparation. [Primary Results and Figs. 6–8](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10411940/) |

The held WWOX comparison reports less SLC16A7 RNA in all three bulk systems and less pooled hippocampal signal, with transport capacity and redox direction unmeasured. It is useful as a testable recipient-side limitation. The existing astrocyte comparison already separates medium and cellular labeled pools and cautions against treating them as flux. The added step is to measure ATP and firing recovery alongside real recipient oxidation and the two potentially opposing synaptic effects at matched measured exposure.
