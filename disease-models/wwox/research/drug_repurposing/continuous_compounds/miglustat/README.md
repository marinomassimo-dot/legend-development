## Direct exposure and counterproof

- **Human parent.** The open-label type 3 Gaucher study randomized 21 versus 9, almost all on existing enzyme replacement. Four predose CSF samples ranged **201–629 ng/mL**, or **0.917–2.87 µM**. The measured subgroup ages and sampling curves were not established from the Results passage. The authors found no separation on saccadic and other neurologic endpoints at the controlled horizon. The EMA label separately summarizes CSF relative to plasma in six GD3 patients. Neither record supplies free exposure at intracellular ER/Golgi GBA2 or UGCG in developing WWOX cells. [Primary GD3 Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2605167/) · [EMA §5.2](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/zavesca-epar-product-information_en.pdf)
- **Assay scale.** Lahav Table 1 compound 1 gives miglustat **0.326 µM GBA2 Ki** from a human overexpression homogenate/artificial 4MU substrate assay, and **50 µM GCS IC50** from an in-situ assay. The GD3 CSF trough range is **2.81–8.80×** the first number and **17.4–54.5× lower** than the second. This arithmetic ranks comparisons to perform. Different assay quantities, native substrate, intracellular exposure and local compartment state prevent an occupancy estimate. [Original kinetics, Table 1 and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5677758/) · [Replayed values](exposure_replay.tsv)
- **Central sign reversal.** In Ashe et al. Fig. 1B, at the high tested dietary exposure in Sandhoff-deficient mice, miglustat increased brain GlcCer **more than tenfold** versus untreated mutant even as GM2 fell modestly to **about 90%** of untreated at days 84 and 112. The study separated GlcCer from GalCer chromatographically. The >20-fold value belongs to a separate analogue. Survival and inflammatory phenotypes can improve in the same model despite accumulation of the proximal GlcCer. In a separate NPC mouse study, Gba2 loss and pharmacological inhibition with other iminosugars delayed deterioration with storage pathology persisting. These results establish mechanistic alternatives under distinct disease lesions. They leave WWOX-specific efficacy and human intracellular target engagement unresolved. [Ashe Fig. 1B, LC-MS Methods](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0021758) · [Npc1/Gba2 primary](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0135889)

**Discriminating next step.** Audit held biological material for native GlcCer, GalCer and sulfatide chain-length and compartment data from the same WWOX line and developmental stage. Require a reproducible excess species before selecting substrate reduction. If both lipid routes are assayed, compare de novo GlcCer entry with clearance of prelabelled GlcCer, native GalCer and absolute functional sheath/evoked output at measured cell-associated and unbound exposure. Preserved early synthesis with slower clearance and higher GlcCer at an otherwise comparable exposure favors GBA2 over UGCG. A lipid-resolved comparison across corrected and deficient cells also tests the competing loss of structural lipids. Do not equate summed HexCer with GlcCer. Seek an independent recheck if a held lipidomic signal or a near-overlapping native-flux potency could change the mechanistic priority.

**WWOX-specific inference.** The two drug actions could push the shared ceramide substrate in opposite directions. Selective UGCG inhibition could spare ceramide for GalCer if the pathway still has access and active UGT8. GBA2 inhibition can reduce ceramide regenerated from GlcCer. If that recycled pool feeds a vulnerable WWOX myelin branch, chase label should disappear from newly made GalCer or sulfatide even when total GlcCer rises. This prediction depends on cell type, acyl-chain identity and compartmental routing. The low pooled Ugt8a and Gal3st1 signal prioritizes testing it in mature mouse oligodendrocytes. The opposing human bulk direction prevents extrapolation. Test the chain-specific tracer result with viable stage counts and conduction. [Held chain-length comparator and WWOX assay rationale](../../../fulltext_dossiers/PMID41715204.md)

**Pediatric boundary.** EU authorization concerns the progressive neurological manifestations of NPC in adult and pediatric patients. The small published study's separate younger cohort and the Gaucher exposure data cannot establish a WWOX benefit. Miglustat-related GI effects, tremor, weight/growth and renal clearance matter to interpreting any future developmental endpoint. The NPC trial's saccadic conclusion was sensitive to excluding patients on benzodiazepines. That is an endpoint-interpretation issue in a relevant background-therapy class, and cannot establish a pharmacokinetic interaction. WWOX background-drug exposure and tolerability remain to be checked.
