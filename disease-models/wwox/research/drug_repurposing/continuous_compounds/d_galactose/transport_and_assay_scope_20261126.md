## Re-extraction of a transporter positive control

The eLife workbooks for Qian et al. give three organelle preparations per group in Fig. 4G and three uptake experiments per group in Fig. 5B. Both figures use HEK293T SLC35A2-knockout control material and SLC35A2 re-expression/overexpression. Relative effects below are group-mean ratios. The omitted range removes one observation independently from each of the two groups and reports all nine resulting ratios. It is a sensitivity range and has no confidence-interval interpretation. [Qian et al., Figs. 4G and 5B, Methods and public workbooks](https://elifesciences.org/articles/65417/figures) · [All recalculated values](transport_calibration.tsv)

The workbook TIC denominator differs across groups, so the reported normalized fold cannot be treated as an absolute fold in UDP-Gal amount. The raw signal also depends on sample recovery and MS response. Figure 5B is a separate tracer measurement independent of the Fig. 4G normalizer. It registers radioactivity retained with a membrane fraction after incubation and filtration. A mixed membrane preparation cannot identify a Golgi-only signal or quantify free luminal UDP-Gal, and filter-retained label can include binding or metabolites. The much greater tracer retention in this re-expression control supports using an uptake assay with independent recovery and lumen-access controls.

Fasimoye et al. used rapid tagged Golgi isolation in HEK cells. In Fig. 4B, GolgiTAG immunoprecipitates gave **9.2-fold** higher pooled UDP-hexose signal than ControlTAG immunoprecipitates. Whole-cell signals were similar. In Fig. 4E the pooled Golgi UDP-hexose signal fell after SLC35A2 knockout and recovered with re-expression, while whole-cell levels were unchanged. The authors report comparable Golgi capture in knockout and WT and lack of enrichment of cytosolic lactate in the tag comparison. The **9.2-fold** number is a tag-versus-background ratio, separate from the Fig. 4E genotype effect. This is an orthogonal compartment-level control. The paper measures pooled UDP-hexose, and an isolated compartment's steady-state pool jointly reflects import and consumption. [Fasimoye et al., Fig. 4B–E Results and captions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10193996/)

## A decision rule for WWOX

Jáñez Pedrayes et al. used selective enzymatic removal of UDP-Glc to estimate its two isomers from whole-cell extract. Their standards showed more than **99%** conversion. In the untreated SLC35A2-CDG fibroblasts pooled UDP-hexoses were higher while the estimated UDP-Gal component was similar. Supplementation raised estimated UDP-Gal about **2–4-fold** (reported p=0.046) in those relative whole-cell results. Carbon-label incorporation into downstream glycolipids demonstrates delivery to downstream synthesis. It does not separately measure transport and transferase activity. Ng et al.'s permeabilized-cell comparison also depends on making a labelled product on an artificial acceptor. It has a transporter rescue control, but acceptor presentation or enzyme changes could confound applying the assay alone to WWOX. [Jáñez Pedrayes et al., Figs. 1A and 2D Results and Supplementary Methods](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00018-025-05759-w) · [Ng et al., Methods and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6661012/)

Use three independent measurements in the same WWOX neural preparations:

1. Measure distinct Gal-1-P, UDP-Gal and UDP-Glc pools and tracer fractions. Establish isomer and extraction recovery in the WWOX sample matrix. Lower Gal-1-P labelling followed by lower UDP-Gal labelling at matched extracellular galactose would prioritize an activation defect.
2. Measure isotope-resolved Golgi UDP-Gal and its early accumulation relative to cytosolic donor using compartment recovery markers and a cytosolic carryover control. Detect enough recovered Golgi in both genotypes before interpreting a lack of labelling. A plateau alone reflects consumption as well as uptake. At matched cytosolic donor, reduced early Golgi labelling with a processing-independent uptake control favors impaired delivery.
3. Measure absolute signal for core-1 O-GalNAc on the same cargo as the Tn precursor, terminal galactosylation on occupied N-sites, and an independent lipid comparator. Adequate early Golgi donor delivery with persistently reduced mature output favors transferase or trafficking limitation. A larger label fraction with unchanged mature product amount indicates substitution of the carbon source.

In resected SLC35A2-associated focal epilepsy tissue, the Mealer preprint reports that the relation between VVA binding and SLC35A2 variant allele fraction changed from **r²=0.67** using a neighboring block to **r²=0.98** using the same lysate, in its first six-case and four-control cohort (Fig. S8 Results). The separate cohort had three cases and four controls and reported **r²=0.99** (Fig. S10 Results). The combined analysis after omitting the highest-variant specimen reported **r²=0.89** (Fig. 7C Results). Neuraminidase-treated PNA did not clearly decline in the higher-variant samples. This gives an empirical reason to use a matched specimen and separate relative Tn exposure from total core-1 output. These values are from Results prose. The supplementary workbook retrieval returned HTML and the plots were not independently reconstructed. The preprint concerns a different genetic lesion and gives no D-galactose response in WWOX. [Mealer et al., human biopsy section](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13001337/)

## Exposure, missing disease metabolite and disposition

In a diagnostic cohort of seven adults aged **18–40**, the Mandal CSF study lists D-galactose at **107 ± 45 µM** (mean ± SD by GC-MS, Table 2), alongside **166 ± 99 µM** from earlier literature. Its Methods give seven total specimens and the galactose row gives no compound-specific denominator. The lumbar CSF measurements are endogenous adult values. The fibroblast study used **2 mM** extracellular added galactose, about **18.7 times** the 107 µM adult CSF mean. This is a cross-context comparison of a bath and a basal clinical measurement. A separate acute human study measured **2.3 ± 0.3 mM** circulating galactose under its higher oral galactose-alone condition, with altered systemic appearance when glucose was coingested. These inputs leave post-supplement CNS exposure, intracellular UDP-Gal and useful duration above a neural response threshold unresolved. The postsurgical MOGHE pilot informs tolerability in another epilepsy and supplies no WWOX target engagement. [Mandal et al., Methods and Tables 1–2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3446266/) · [Jáñez Pedrayes et al., Methods](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00018-025-05759-w) · [Sunehag and Haymond, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12077725/) · [MOGHE pilot, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37278968/)

The held fly knockdown Supplementary Table 4 lists **54** significant features. Neither its candidate names nor assigned names name D-galactose, Gal-1-P, UDP-Gal or galactitol. The public metabolite assignment export lists **14** records, also without those labels. The counts address these two tables and leave unassigned or unreported metabolites open. WWOX RNA remains the disease-specific evidence behind the hypothesis. [Fly primary study and Supplementary Table 4](https://doi.org/10.1093/braincomms/fcag385) · [Public assignment source](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights/MTBLS12344) · [Reproduced label counts](fly_assignment_check.tsv)

**Park for WWOX trial priority.** The transporter controls make the hypothesized split experimentally testable. No held result shows an isomer-resolved WWOX donor deficit or a D-galactose response in affected neural cells. Reopen for an exposure-linked correction of a measured pathway defect that restores a same-cargo glycoform and a WWOX neural functional phenotype, with viable corrected and deficient controls. If Golgi delivery increases and maturation stays abnormal, use it to locate the lesion and keep the compound parked. Independent assay review is decision-relevant once matched WWOX neural material and a compartment assay are available.
