# Does acetate delivered by triacetin restore synthesis and function of WWOX myelin?

**Disposition.** Park triacetin for unselected pediatric WWOX trial priority. The route has separate evidence for delivery to mouse brain, a synthesis defect in untreated ASPA-deficient mice, and improved behavior during treatment of ASPA-deficient rats. The studies do not connect drug-derived acetate to restored synthesis and functional sheaths in one system. The human WWOX spectroscopy result adds a disease-specific clue for follow-up.

**Two checks on the apparent lipid benefit.** If the galactocerebroside numerator and protein normalization stayed fixed, an approximately 16.9% decrease in summed stain intensity would yield the same 1.203-fold relative increase. This is a sensitivity example; neither that decrease nor unchanged absolute galactocerebroside was observed. More than one lipid moved and only the galactocerebroside comparison was flagged as significant. The table gives means and SEM, with 4–5 animals per group, and reports a paired t test. Pups from different litters were randomly allocated; the analyzed pairs and their covariance were not identified in the methods checked. If the groups were independent, the difference would be 0.73 standard errors. Under hypothetical equal complete groups of four or five true pairs, a two-sided p below .05 would require a correlation above approximately .948 or .931, respectively. Neither calculation reconstructs the authors' test. Even a confirmed paired lipid difference would leave the flux and function question open, so a separate statistical review would not change the present decision. Rotarod sample sizes varied across ages. The Fig. 2 caption names Tukey’s HSD, whereas the methods and Table 1 describe a rank-based analysis followed by Dunn comparisons. Raw repeated measurements were unavailable for an independent longitudinal fit. The gap fractions above describe two distinct endpoints; they are not estimates of mediation.

**Direct human WWOX constraint.** The primary spectroscopy description and Fig. 3M report a mildly reduced NAA peak in the right basal ganglia. No absolute concentration or quantified voxel composition is given for this observation. A localized peak could reflect neuronal abundance or maturation as well as altered NAA synthesis; it cannot measure transfer of acetate to oligodendrocytes. This qualifies the mouse RNA findings without supplying a responder biomarker. The small safety studies in Canavan disease described in the opening dossier provide limited pediatric exposure experience in a different disease. [WWOX primary report, Fig. 3M](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35573960/) · [WWOX RNA comparison and pediatric evidence](README.md)

**What would reopen the question.** In developmentally matched WWOX-deficient and corrected neuron–oligodendrocyte cultures, test the same time window for acetate delivery, absolute synthesis of lipid, and ensheathment. Distinguish the labelled acetyl groups of triacetin from glycerol carbon. Compare label recovery with total and newly synthesized lipid mass per culture and per viable lineage cell, and measure functional conduction at fixed axon density. A KO-selective gain in absolute synthesis followed by improved ensheathment and conduction would support a bypassable substrate deficit. Higher enrichment with unchanged lipid mass, synthesis rate and conduction would favor substrate substitution. A motor endpoint alone cannot decide between these mechanisms.
