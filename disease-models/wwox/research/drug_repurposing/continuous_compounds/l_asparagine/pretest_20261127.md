# L-asparagine and neural amino-acid sufficiency in WWOX loss of function

**Status.** Opening checkpoint for L-asparagine. WWOX organoid stress-response RNA is lower as a set, while ASNS itself was inconclusive. The small knockdown neural precursor dataset gives a different set-level direction and has library-composition and knockdown-verification limits. Neither result measures synthesis or a shortage of usable asparagine. ASNS deficiency is a related pediatric metabolic comparator with mixed observations after supplementation. The new question is whether exogenous asparagine corrects a measurable WWOX supply bottleneck or acts mainly through amino-acid exchange and nutrient sensing. [Held organoid comparison](../../gse156243/prediction_isr_ldh_chain_20261026.md) · [Held precursor study analysis](../../hnpc_gse126075_it5_20261029/RESULTS.md) · [Primary ASNS disease report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24139043/)

**Prediction.** Fix developmental stage, physiological starting amino-acid availability and prior activity in WWOX and corrected preparations. A supply limitation predicts low intracellular asparagine, impaired charging of asparaginyl tRNA or lower translation during a defined demand. Measured intact labeled parent should contribute to an increase in the absolute usable pool and charging, followed by recovery of translation and absolute evoked output. Quantify intracellular aspartate and glutamine, their incorporation into newly synthesized asparagine, and ATP recovery. Separate correction of an actual deficit from replacement of endogenous input by exogenous label.

**Counterchecks.** Measure extracellular and intracellular parent and competing amino acids. Track uptake of other amino acids and mTORC1 before interpreting any growth or translation response. An exchange mechanism predicts altered entry of other substrates or mTORC1 signaling with little change in asparagine charging when baseline is already sufficient. Use stage-matched and nutrient-matched comparisons, including an assay calibration for low ASNS flux. Require preserved inhibitory recruitment as well as evoked function. A rise in plasma or medium asparagine, restored growth alone, or rescue from a nutrient-depleted culture cannot establish the WWOX mechanism. [Exchange mechanism in another cell type](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms11457)

**Next step.** Verify the extant WWOX RNA and ascertain which human reports paired cerebrospinal fluid with plasma and which documented seizure, longitudinal function, and co-interventions. Then compare supply, exchange and safety without pooling selected case reports. [Opening comparison](first_comparison_20261127.md)
