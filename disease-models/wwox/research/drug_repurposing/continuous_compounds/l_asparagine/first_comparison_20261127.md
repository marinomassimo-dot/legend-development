# L-asparagine opening comparison

| Preparation | Opening observation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Human WWOX cortical organoid and neural precursor publications | The held GSE156243 comparison found a lower stress-response target set with no clear ASNS RNA change on its own. The separate GSE126075 knockdown comparison had a modest post hoc ASNS shift (+0.4 centered in both reported stages) with one library per arm per stage and uncertain knockdown in the precursor library. | The comparisons have different backgrounds and evidence strength. Neither measures endogenous asparagine flux, absolute concentration, or tRNA charging. [Held organoid contrast](../../gse156243/prediction_isr_ldh_chain_20261026.md) · [Held precursor contrast](../../hnpc_gse126075_it5_20261029/RESULTS.md) |
| Genetically confirmed ASNS deficiency, supplemented pediatric reports | One report describes two treated siblings and no observed further disease progression during treatment. A different report describes seizure worsening after supplementation in a child. | These are different patients and uncontrolled observations in a primary enzyme deficiency. The reported directions do not yield a response rate or a WWOX prediction. [Two-sibling report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31123592/) · [Seizure worsening case](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27268761/) |
| Human cancer-cell nutrient exchange assay | External or synthesized asparagine can participate in amino-acid exchange and affect mTORC1, translation-related and metabolic readouts in that experimental system. | These effects motivate measuring uptake and signaling separately in WWOX. A change in mTOR or growth does not verify substrate replacement in developing neural tissue. [Krall et al., primary study](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms11457) |

**Checkpoint.** Advance the supply hypothesis only if a compartment-specific starting deficit can be measured. The next comparison separates absolute pool and tRNA rescue from amino-acid exchange and asks where CNS exposure has actually been measured. [Registered prediction](pretest_20261127.md)
