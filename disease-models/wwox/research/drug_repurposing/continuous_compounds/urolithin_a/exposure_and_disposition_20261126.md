## Exposure and clinical feasibility

- **Acute intraperitoneal adult mouse comparison.** The Methods for Fig. 4a specify a separate single 5 mg/kg experiment with perfused brains at 5–60 minutes (2–3 treated animals per time point, nine in total). Parent and sulfate were detected and glucuronide was below detection in brain. The eight-week 2.3 mg/kg/day functional comparison is separate. The brain axis reads µg/mg tissue. An approximate 15-minute ordinate of 0.31 µg/mg implies 108.5 µg in the 350 mg tissue aliquot specified in Methods. That mass is the entire 5 mg/kg administered amount for a 21.7 g mouse, before accounting for any other tissue. The animal weights for this arm and numeric LC-MS values behind Fig. 4a were unavailable in the source data inspected. The scale warrants clarification. The figure supports detection but was excluded from calculations of quantitative free exposure or brain-to-plasma partition. [Jiménez-Loygorri et al., Fig. 4a, Urolithin metabolite determination](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-45044-1)
- **Oral adult mouse comparison.** In a separate PK arm, Mishra reports a parent brain signal 60 minutes after gavage (n=2 per group), no detectable parent plasma peak and qualitative conjugate detection. Supplementary Fig. 3A shows a nonzero value in the vehicle example. A matched perfusion step for this PK arm could not be verified in the inspected methods. The single representative chromatogram, limited denominators and route details leave oral unbound parenchymal exposure unresolved. [Mishra et al., Fig. 3B and Supplementary Fig. 3](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12868-026-01036-z)
- **Human oral formulation.** In Liu's trial of 66 older adults (33 per arm), the defined oral preparation was compared with placebo for 120 days. In the exposed group, month-four plasma summaries were 1038 pg/mL for aglycone (4.55 nM total) and 1014 ng/mL for glucuronide (2.51 µM total). The molar values use 228.20 and 404.30 g/mol, respectively. The 50 µM negative acute rat bath comparison is about 11,000× the adult total parent visit value. This is a scale comparison across systems. It provides no Cmax, free concentration, compartment partition or efficacy threshold. The two primary results, 6-minute walk and maximal hand-muscle ATP production, did not show a benefit over placebo at four months. The endpoints were amended after study start and Methods specifies hierarchical testing. Local muscle endurance differed at two months, with no detected difference at four months. Table 2 lists zero serious events in either arm and 16 versus 15 treatment-emergent events, counted as events. [Liu et al., Fig. 2, Table 2, Methods and Results](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.44279) · [Glucuronide mass](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Urolithin-A-glucuronide)
- **Older-adolescent and young-adult precedent.** Monsalve Acevedo randomized 20 male academy players (mean age 17.5 ± 1 years). The intervention was flavored Mitopure powder in a post-training dairy/protein drink at scheduled training occasions over six weeks. The ANZCTR eligibility field allows age 16+, and its brief summary still starts at age 18. Its primary test lists IRT2 while the article reports IRT1, without an explanation in the inspected materials. The paper reports a 239 m difference (95% CI 20–454) and p=0.048, alongside F(1,15.27)=0.661, which implies p=0.429 under an ordinary F distribution. The replay establishes the inconsistency without identifying the erroneous field. The formulation and acceptance observations provide limited late-adolescent experience. They leave younger children, epilepsy, multisystem impairment, co-medications and central exposure untested. The US GRAS notice covers specified food uses. An age-appropriate defined formulation, absorption during feeding, oral parent and conjugate exposure at the intended neural site, interactions with background antiseizure medication and neurological tolerability remain open. A muscle or locomotor endpoint would not by itself separate seizure effects, sedation or developmental gain in WWOX. Record evoked neural function, spontaneous seizure or discharge burden and longitudinal developmental outcomes as distinct endpoints if later data justify a study.

## Inference and discriminating observation

**Inference.** Convergent bulk PINK1 and OPA1 reductions in WWOX justify asking whether a quality-control bottleneck limits ATP recovery. The pooled lineage measurements weaken any assumption that deficient PINK1 or PRKN is a shared feature of every affected neuron. The two epithelial perturbations show that the apparent importance of mitochondrial versus lysosomal routes depends on the injury and the outcome. VDAC1/tubulin and baseline mitochondrial reserve offer additional explanations for an excitability or ATP change without selective disposal.

**Hypothesis.** In an affected WWOX developmental stage, if removal of damaged mitochondria is limiting, attainable intact parent exposure may increase the lysosome-dependent disappearance of pre-labelled native mitochondrial matrix cargo before absolute cellular ATP recovery and inhibitory recruitment improve. If replacement capacity is limiting, a rise in reporter-positive organelles or a decline in total mitochondrial mass can coexist with worsened absolute ATP recovery. A preserved benefit after partial PINK1/PRKN perturbation that disappears with SQSTM1 perturbation and tracks damaged-lysosome repair would favor the p62-linked route in that context. Match effects on baseline state and confirm the intended flux changes before interpreting these perturbations.

**Discriminating observation.** In matched-stage WWOX-deficient and isogenic control preparations, compare vehicle and verified parent exposure in each genotype, with matched initial work and survival. Measure intact parent and conjugates locally, report free exposure where possible, and cross the informative exposure with partial PINK1/PRKN and SQSTM1 perturbations. Pair an acidification/delivery reporter with a pulse-chase of pre-existing mitochondrial matrix material and a calibrated lysosomal turnover control. Follow absolute mitochondrial mass, ATP recovery and evoked inhibitory and excitatory output per viable preparation after washout. Favor the clearance route if greater loss of old damaged cargo precedes preferential durable WWOX recovery and both responses attenuate with perturbation of that flux. A reporter-only change, ATP gain per mitochondrial mass with falling net energy reserve, or a firing change that precedes cargo turnover and disappears on washout lowers that hypothesis. A response that follows changes in lysosomal damage independent of mitochondrial cargo points to a different bottleneck.

**Disposition.** Park for unselected pediatric WWOX trial priority and retain as a preclinical comparator. The missing local exposure and stage-matched functional contrast drive this decision independently of ambiguities in Fig. 4a and the sports pilot. An independent review of those numerical points would not change the current action. Reopen if the exact oral formulation yields measurable relevant exposure and preferential neural benefit with an interpretable flux control. The next research increment can select the next uncovered pathway and compound from the shared roadmap once this checkpoint is recorded.
