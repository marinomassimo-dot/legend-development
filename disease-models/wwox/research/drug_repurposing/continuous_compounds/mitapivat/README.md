# Mitapivat in WWOX loss of function

**Units and preparations.** Purified human pyruvate kinase assays report activation concentrations in nM and catalytic rates in µmol/s/mg. The FDA adult PK deficiency population model reports total parent in plasma in ng/mL and ng·h/mL. Male rat total brain-to-plasma exposure uses AUC in ng·h/g and ng·h/mL, respectively. Hematology trials count randomized people and disease-specific responses. WWOX RNA uses TPM in mouse cortex or hippocampus libraries, CPM in week-15 human forebrain organoid libraries and pooled nuclear CPM within a mouse hippocampal cell class.

**28 November 2026 UTC.** Retain mitapivat as a conditional ex vivo PKM2 perturbation. Defer systemic pediatric WWOX priority. Adult blood target engagement and pediatric blood-disorder exposure support the PKLR branch. The plausible neural branch requires evidence for isoform-specific engagement, local free exposure and an activity-matched energetic benefit. [Frozen comparison](pretest.md)

## Reproducible exposure and efficacy contrasts

The measured parent AUCs in the original EMA assessment give total rat brain/plasma ratios **0.0568 after one exposure and 0.0912 after repeated exposures** using a density approximation of 1 g/mL. These are high-exposure male rat tissue results. The in vitro human free fraction in plasma is 2.33%. FDA Table 6 reports modeled steady-state geometric means of 935.2 ng/mL peak, 62.1 ng/mL trough and 3,591.4 ng·h/mL over 12 h in its highest displayed PK-deficiency arm. The associated illustrative free **plasma** values are 48.4 nM peak, 15.5 nM interval mean and 3.2 nM trough. [Original EMA assessment, Distribution](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/assessment-report/pyrukynd-epar-public-assessment-report_en.pdf) · [FDA Tables 6 and 65](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2022/216196Orig1s000IntegratedR.pdf)

To test which additional fact could change the exposure decision, apply the repeated-dose rat total AUC ratio to the adult plasma coordinates and vary the unbound brain fraction. Reaching the purified PKM2 38 nM benchmark requires an unbound brain fraction of **20% at the illustrative peak** or **63% at the interval mean**. The illustrative total trough is 12.6 nM. This exercise assumes the rat total AUC ratio carries to human blood-to-brain distribution and that the same ratio applies at different coordinates. Those assumptions have not been established. The peak and trough are algebraic sensitivities rather than predicted time-specific concentrations. AUC partition, tissue binding and local intracellular exposure require independent measurement. [Exposure replay](exposure_sensitivity.tsv)

A competing action is H3 inverse agonism (EC₅₀ 12 nM) in the FDA assay; a separate H3 antagonism endpoint gave IC₅₀ 102 nM. At an assumed 10% unbound brain fraction, the favorable illustrative peak is 18.9 nM. It crosses the H3 inverse-agonism assay benchmark while staying below the PKM2 assay benchmark. Cross-assay comparisons of these numbers do not determine functional selectivity or prove H3 engagement in brain. They justify preserving activity/arousal controls and checking whether an apparent phenotype tracks PK flux. [FDA review, Histamine Receptor 3 Antagonism, printed pp. 65–66](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2022/216196Orig1s000IntegratedR.pdf)

## WWOX inference and decision-changing comparison

**Native context.** Total PKM is 1,785→1,833 TPM in P17 mouse cortex (4 control, 3 KO), 712→925 TPM in mouse hippocampus (2/3) and 769→866 CPM in week-15 human organoids (2/4). Hippocampus and organoid positive directions survive single-library omission, whereas the cortical direction can change. PKLR is below one unit in every bulk arm. LDHA falls in both mouse bulk regions and rises in organoids (53.5→90.8 CPM). MPC2 falls in all three matched bulk comparisons (genotype, stage and aggregate units remain separate). Pooled astrocyte PKM is 113→123 CPM and excitatory assignment is 68→91 CPM. Each class has one animal pool per genotype. These gene-level matrix releases do not separate PKM1 and PKM2 or measure cell-resolved protein, tetramerization, net carbon flux or neuronal ATP. [Bulk replay](native_bulk.tsv) · [Nuclear replay](cell_types.tsv) · [GSE158682](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) · [GSE158681](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) · [GSE156243](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243) · [GSE158684](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158684)

**Hypothesis and counterexample.** An activation-sensitive PKM2 pool in WWOX astrocytes or immature neural cells could increase terminal glycolytic ATP or glucose-derived carbon supply to a recipient neuron. If oxidative capacity caps downstream use, the same rise in terminal ATP-generating flux can coexist with unchanged oxidation and greater lactate release. If upstream supply is already limiting, changing PK capacity leaves all three fluxes unchanged. The [unitless replay](flux_examples.tsv) holds demand outside the model and illustrates those conditional outcomes. It treats nonoxidized pyruvate as lactate and does not predict net ATP or cellular biology. A gain in measured ATP recovery at matched activity can support a glycolytic benefit even without increased TCA labeling.

**One discriminating comparison.** Compare matched WWOX-null and corrected neuron–astrocyte preparations at equal first recruitment and matched activity demand. Measure free parent and functional PKM1/PKM2 activity in the relevant cell class. Time-resolve [U-¹³C]glucose labelling of PEP, pyruvate, extracellular lactate and early TCA products together with their absolute pools, ATP recovery and later patterned activity. Include a PKM2-specific dependency contrast, plus an H3 reference if that receptor is functional in the preparation. The PK hypothesis predicts a larger null-specific increase in precursor-to-product flux and ATP recovery while preserving first recruitment. Lactate release alone, lower activity demand, absent local PKM2 engagement, or an effect reproduced through H3 without a PK-flux gain lowers priority. A labeled oxidative gain addresses a separate bottleneck and would strengthen a claim of downstream recovery. Keep culture line or animal as the independent unit, with cell-class and metabolic readouts aligned in time. The measurement that could justify independent review is a reproducible PKM1 response or a disputed exposure-supported rescue, as either would change which preparation to prioritize.

**Stop and reopen.** Preserve as a conditional ex vivo probe. Reopen with paired neural isoform engagement and productive ATP recovery at verified free parent. The priority stays below clinical planning until a measured juvenile central exposure bridge and a relevant functional window exist.
