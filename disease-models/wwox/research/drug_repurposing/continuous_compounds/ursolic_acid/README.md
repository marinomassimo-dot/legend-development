# Ursolic acid | Astrocyte CNTF supply versus receiver limitation

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Disposition.** Retain purified parent ursolic acid as a conditional comparator for a glial trophic mechanism. Defer pediatric WWOX intervention priority. The WWOX transcript data prioritize testing whether oligodendrocyte lineage cells can receive CNTF before asking whether astrocytes can produce more of it. Functional first sheaths on WWOX-deficient axons and attainable free exposure remain the deciding endpoints. ## Separate conditioned medium, direct action and developmental relevance

| Preparation and unit | Observation and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| **Zhang et al., Fig. 5.** P1–2 mouse cerebellar slices cultured before lysolecithin injury. Nominal 10 µg/mL ursolic acid during recovery. | After 14 days, MBP–NFH alignment increased 2.1 ± 0.2-fold relative to injured control. The assay measures optical alignment of two markers. CNTF increased about 50-fold in the tissue RNA array and 3.9 ± 0.8-fold in supernatant protein. These readouts have different matrices, baselines and units. Their fold changes cannot quantify secretion efficiency. Caption reports 5–6 per group, representative of three experiments. The readout follows induced injury and leaves ultrastructure and conduction unresolved. [Original Fig. 5 and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7183235/) |
| **Zhang et al., Figs. 6–8.** Primary neonatal mouse astrocytes or OPCs. | CNTF-neutralizing antibody attenuated the change in MBP-positive cells and branching caused by conditioned medium. Separately, exposing pure OPC cultures directly to ursolic acid across nominal 1–10 µg/mL affected maturation markers, with PPARγ antagonism tested at the upper condition. Astrocyte reporter and CREB knockdown support pathway involvement. The paper's docking prediction supplies no binding measurement. MBP-positive morphology in pure OPCs gives no axonal denominator. [Original Figs. 6–8 and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7183235/) |
| **Other injury evidence.** Honarvar et al. 2019, adult mouse cuprizone; Honarvar et al. 2022, post-cuprizone recovery. | The first study began ursolic acid in drinking water alongside cuprizone and measured callosal staining and lineage markers. Preservation and recovery can both affect staining. In the second study, the abstract reports improved callosal markers after 6 or 12 weeks of cuprizone followed by 2 weeks of recovery. These extend the injury comparison while leaving the CNTF route and WWOX developmental transfer open. [Honarvar 2019, Figs. 2–6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7059067/) · [Honarvar 2022, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35976486/) |
| **Independent developmental countercheck.** Steelman et al., rat OPCs and lineage-specific mouse Stat3 deletion. | Recombinant CNTF activates STAT3 over 30–60 min in rat oligodendroglial cells. Knockdown or loss of the pathway blocks relevant culture responses, and adult lesion repair was impaired after lineage deletion. The authors found no developmental myelination difference in the tested Plp1- and Olig1-driven deletions, including a P14 comparison. At 21 days after adult injury, Fig. 6E labels 30% versus 68% of examined axons unmyelinated. The micrographs represent two mice per genotype and the axon-level contrast should not be read as an animal-level effect estimate. An intact signal can matter for repair and still have a different role in initial development. Developing Cntf-null optic nerve shows delayed lineage expansion with later convergence toward WT according to a separate genetic study. [Steelman, Fig. 2, Results 3.5–3.6 and Figs. 5–6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4962532/) · [Barres, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8954629/) |

**Carryover bound.** The main Fig. 6E caption describes a half-volume replacement with conditioned medium. If astrocyte medium began at 10 µg/mL, no wash occurred and parent remained completely recovered, the recipient could start at **5 µg/mL (10.95 µM)**. That lies inside the direct OPC concentration series. It is a conditional upper value for that source condition. The actual source concentration used for that panel, pretransfer wash and residual ursolic acid were unresolved from the material accessible here. Neutralization supports a contribution of CNTF under the tested conditions. It leaves open a direct contribution from residual parent or a cooperation between CNTF and that parent. Test cell-free matched carryover, parent-depleted conditioned medium with verified CNTF recovery, antibody isotype and absolute viable lineage counts. Assay both extracellular CNTF and cell damage to distinguish increased trophic signal from release associated with loss of cell integrity. [Fig. 6E–F and Fig. 8A–C](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7183235/) · [Transfer calculations](exposure.tsv)

**Opposite-direction control.** In the primary study by Kim et al., ursolic acid at nominal 20 and 40 µM for 24 h reduced pJAK2 and pSTAT3 in human colorectal cancer cell lines, alongside apoptosis measures. The lower concentration is near the 21.9 µM nominal slice exposure. That cross-context overlap supplies a testable rival: parent could increase astrocyte CNTF yet suppress the recipient's STAT3 response. Different cells, exposures and concurrent toxicity prevent transferring the sign. Compare recombinant CNTF with and without measured parent carryover in viable glia. [Kim, Results 2.2 and Fig. 2C](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6337206/)

## WWOX recipient competence

The WWOX preparation establishes an axonal constraint. In the published sensory DRG comparison, changing only DRG genotype with WT OPCs altered myelinating morphology between groups. Held P17 callosal ultrastructure and compound action potentials and week-30/37 human oligocortical spheroid readouts address different levels of myelination. Effects on OPC branching alone cannot establish that axons acquire functional sheaths. [Repudi, Figs. 5–7](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awab174) · [Held morphology and conduction audit](../calcitriol/README.md)

LIFR and IL6ST preserve direction under each single-library omission in all three preparations. CNTF is sparse and changes sign across models. In the single P17 hippocampal nuclear pool per genotype, Cntfr is **62.6→21.3 CPM** in OPC assignments. Raw detection falls from **44/242 to 26/352**. Expected detection after exact count thinning to 1,000 UMIs is **5.93%→2.29%**, restricted to nuclei at least that deep (237 and 338). LIFR and gp130 show mixed lineage-specific patterns. The pooled OPC gp130 detection estimate is 1.77%→1.81% at matched depth. CNTFR, LIFR and IL6ST were co-detected in zero OPC nuclei in either pool at their deposited depths, while committed and newly formed classes together had 3/184 in WT and 0/173 in KO. This is a detection floor. The data cannot establish whether a functional complex is absent, and soluble CNTFRα can confer responsiveness in another system. Cntf had no counts in the classified nuclei of either pool. Protein abundance, release and cellular competence remain unmeasured. **Discriminating prediction.** In stage-matched WWOX-deficient and corrected recipients, compare astrocyte-conditioned medium with parent removed and recovery verified against untreated medium spiked to the measured carryover. Use recombinant CNTF and LIF as signaling controls matched for their WT pSTAT3 response. A CNTF challenge with and without measured parent tests whether parent directly attenuates the recipient response. CNTF uses the ligand-specific α subunit and the LIFR/gp130 pair. LIF engages the shared signaling pair without CNTFRα. Add soluble CNTFRα with CNTF as a conditional rescue control, and measure early lineage-resolved pSTAT3(Y705) before tracking persistent first sheaths per initially eligible axon and conduction.

- **Ligand supply limits:** recombinant CNTF and treated, parent-depleted medium restore early signaling and functional sheaths. CNTF neutralization or loss of the relevant astrocyte pathway removes the medium benefit. LIF can still engage the receiver.
- **CNTFRα presentation limits:** the CNTF response is selectively weak with LIF response preserved. Restoration with soluble CNTFRα favors the presentation step. Weak responses to both ligands at matched WT engagement favor a common receptor or downstream signaling limit and require direct protein/activation checks.
- **Axonal or direct-OPC limit:** early signaling and marker improvement without persistent sheaths and conduction on WWOX-deficient axons leave an axonal constraint. Benefit following parent-only matched exposure despite CNTF neutralization favors the direct branch. Repeat the relevant arm on corrected axons at matched firing, caliber and survival.

This design separates the drug from the reference ligands, which are assay controls. Reopen once receptor competence and free local exposure can be measured in a WWOX preparation with a functional outcome. An independent review of the original supplement could narrow uncertainty on the transfer conditions. The decision depends on the missing WWOX exposure and response, so additional review at this checkpoint would be unlikely to change priority. ## Parent exposure and pediatric feasibility

The nominal 10 µg/mL bath condition is **21.9 µM**. The LC-MS study after apple-peel ingestion observed just **3.3 ± 1.1 ng/mL (0.0072 µM)** mean total plasma peak at about 4 h in four participants. This dietary matrix includes oleanolic acid, which the assay separates. The ratio of bath to total plasma is about 3,000 and describes two conditions, not a required fold increase in human exposure. The healthy-adult purified-parent oral conference abstract reports 11 completers out of 14, sparse and often subquantifiable samples, and a 10.5 ng/mL quantification floor. That floor cannot be interpreted as a Cmax or a universal upper bound. Male rat oral total plasma peaked at 2,025 ng/mL (4.43 µM, Chen Table 1), and adult IV nanoliposome cohorts reached mean total peaks of 1,835–3,457 ng/mL (4.02–7.57 µM, Zhu Table 2, eight per arm). These different products and compartments give no pediatric unbound brain concentration. The primary mouse chronic distribution abstract confirms tissue detection after feeding with triterpenes at 0.5%; the paper's numeric tissue table was unavailable on the free routes checked, so conflicting secondary summaries were excluded from the calculation. [Stebounova, Fig. 3 and assay tables](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6049622/) · [Hirsh, conference abstract](https://doi.org/10.1096/fasebj.28.1_supplement.1044.6) · [Chen, Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5697757/) · [Zhu, Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3540956/) · [Yin, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22816768/)

No pediatric WWOX parent PK, target engagement, CNS dose-response or interaction dataset surfaced in the public search of ursolic acid paired with WWOX, WOREE or pediatric pharmacokinetics on 27 November 2026. Adult nanoliposome work reported liver enzyme elevations and diarrhea, with formulation-specific tolerability. In vitro parent ursolic acid inhibited CYP2C19 in human microsomes at higher nominal concentration than the adult total peaks. This leaves interactions with antiseizure polytherapy unquantified. The analytical work gives a starting point to distinguish the isomers. A feasibility decision would require a defined formulation, matrix-qualified local assay and an age-appropriate structural or physiological outcome.
