# Follow-up on receptor co-detection

Bulk and pooled class marginals were available when this test was frozen. LIFR and IL6ST are lower in the three bulk preparations, while CNTFR is lower in several pooled hippocampal classes. Test whether the three subunits are ever detected together and whether the CNTFR difference persists when covered nuclei are compared at equal sequencing depth.

Compare OPC, COP and NFOL assignments and include other classified cells for context. Report raw count per retained nucleus and expected retention at 1,000 UMIs for nuclei with at least that depth, using sampling without replacement. Treat triple co-detection as an assay floor because dropout can hide any subunit. The single pool per genotype supports a descriptive observation without an animal-level inference.
