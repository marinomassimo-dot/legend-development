## Prespecified decision

Keep parent and the exact desglycinyl moiety distinct, including formulation or salt, stereochemistry if tested, timing and background enzyme induction. First compare measured human exposure for each moiety with quantitative functional engagement during physiological NMDA activation and action-potential trains. Derive a free exposure window only from sourced binding and same-matrix units. A total plasma concentration, radioligand displacement or veratridine-driven sodium influx supplies no direct estimate of free WWOX neural target engagement.

If the free windows support a comparison, use WWOX and corrected preparations at matched first recruitment. Match early NMDA charge against a pore-block comparator, measure late NMDA charge and recovery, and independently match sodium availability against a sodium comparator. The selective metabolite hypothesis predicts reduced recurrent activity beyond sodium matching, with preserved initial excitatory and inhibitory recruitment. A change in sodium availability or resting intracellular sodium that explains the late response lowers the priority of the NMDA-specific route. Failure of both comparisons within a justified exposure range parks the candidate. Include age, co-medications, timing and long-term tolerability limits before judging pediatric feasibility.

## First source triage

- [Pediatric PK and tolerability, PMID 11516608](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11516608/). The abstract notes both molecular species and lower plasma concentrations with enzyme-inducing background antiseizure treatment. Resolve age bands, sampling, denominator, induction grouping and free measurements in an original before using quantities.
- [Santangeli et al., primary abstract, PMID 11906711](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11906711/). The reported nominal IC50 of veratridine-stimulated sodium influx is 160.6 µM for the parent and 85.1 µM for the metabolite in rat cortical synaptosomes. The same-surface point-estimate ratio is 160.6/85.1 = 1.887. The abstract also reports higher resting internal sodium with the metabolite at 1 and 10 µM. Neither result isolates voltage-dependent human channel occupancy, late firing or juvenile circuit effects. Check whether the reported IC50 is relative to the separate fitted minima and how resting sodium was determined.
- [Metabolite panel, PMID 1388119](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1388119/) and [enzyme-induction study, PMID 12076846](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12076846/).
