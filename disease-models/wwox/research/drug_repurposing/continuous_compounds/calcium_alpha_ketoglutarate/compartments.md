# Does uptake of alpha-ketoglutarate predict HIF hydroxylation?

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Research decision.** Retain calcium alpha-ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG) as a mechanistic probe. Defer pediatric trial prioritization. The [opening comparison](README.md) linked reduced HIF-1α hydroxylation in WWOX-null mouse fibroblasts to an enteral increase in circulating parent in pigs. The present analysis tests two intermediate steps, uptake from the extracellular space and use of that substrate by an oxygen-dependent enzyme. The most informative new finding is that intracellular substrate enrichment and downstream succinate enrichment can move in opposite directions.

## Reanalysis of primary isotope data

Parker and colleagues exposed human pancreatic cancer cells to labelled glutamine and unlabelled non-esterified AKG (1 mM) or vehicle for 24 hours. M+0 is the fraction of a metabolite with zero heavy carbons. Endogenous metabolism also supplies unlabelled carbon. We calculated the percentage-point (pp) difference in mean M+0 between the supplemented and vehicle groups for each pool, using all three biological replicates per group. This metric differs from the paper's mole-percent enrichment metric in Fig. 5e. The [six contrasts and group SDs](compartment.tsv) are descriptive. [Parker et al., Figs. 5e and 6d](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-25228-9)

| Cell line and condition | AKG shift (pp) | Succinate shift (pp) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 8988T, 18.6% oxygen | +63.97 | +20.72 |
| 8988T, below 0.001% oxygen | +86.45 | +4.02 |
| HuPT4, intracellular | — | +15.26 |
| HuPT4, culture medium | — | +49.79 |

Oxygen deprivation increased the AKG shift by 22.47 pp while decreasing the succinate shift by 16.70 pp. A large AKG shift can therefore coexist with little downstream shift. Extreme anoxia tests the mechanism and has limited relevance to ordinary neural oxygen levels. In HuPT4 medium, 91.20% of succinate was M+0 after supplementation, but the vehicle mean was already 41.41%. The difference of 49.79 pp quantifies the change in isotopic composition. Absolute secretion, the proportion of dose recovered, and activity of a particular HIF hydroxylase cannot be recovered from these fractions. The authors also observed only 1–3% dilution in cytosolic and mitochondrial 2-hydroxyglutarate reporters in their tested lines (Fig. 6c). This gives a reason to measure access to a particular enzyme even when whole-cell uptake is clear. [Original study and source data](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-25228-9)

## Neural transport, enzyme activity and pediatric interpretation

- **Neural uptake is plausible at low concentration.** In two rat brain synaptosome fractions, net AKG influx at about 1 µM was strongly sodium-dependent. Label reached glutamate rapidly and GABA more slowly. One fraction contained more glial material. These isolated preparations bypass the blood–brain barrier and lack the intact neuronal nucleus required for the proposed HIF transcriptional endpoint. They identify a competing use of neural AKG and do not establish neurotransmitter release. [Shank & Campbell, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6699641/)
- **Selectivity depends on the assay.** A purified human PHD2 catalytic-domain assay reported an apparent AKG Km of 0.9 µM using labelled succinate production. In that single-enzyme assay the product can report enzyme turnover. Many reactions produce succinate in a cell. Applying the purified Km to pig plasma to predict neuronal HIF hydroxylation would require an unmeasured intracellular concentration and additional assumptions about oxygen, iron and competing substrate products. [Biochemical assay, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19498081/)
- **Whole-brain radioactivity leaves parent exposure unresolved.** A rat gavage study found labelled carbon in brain and other tissues after three hours. Its reported value of up to 70% refers to tissue distribution collectively. The sodium/calcium comparison concerned intestinal recovery in a separate experiment. The public abstract provides no brain concentration of chemically identified intact parent after the calcium salt. [Filip & Pierzynowski, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18336415/)
- **Age matters to the comparator.** A historical pediatric study reported declining endogenous CSF and plasma AKG with age. Its epilepsy and febrile-seizure groups showed no group difference from its reference children. It provides age context for a baseline measure. The data reviewed here do not establish exposure after Ca-AKG, tolerability with background antiseizure medication or a WWOX-specific clinical endpoint. [Pediatric measurements, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/985154/)

**Discriminating prediction.** At matched free calcium, pH, glucose, oxygen and iron availability, the co-substrate hypothesis predicts a larger movement toward baseline control values in directly measured hydroxylated/total HIF-1α in WWOX-deficient neural cells than in their isogenic corrected controls. Require separate quantities for hydroxylated and total protein, timed sampling before changes in glycolysis, and a concordant later HIF-dependent transcriptional response. Control for rapid proteasomal turnover when comparing the hydroxylated pool. Compare the treatment-minus-vehicle effects between genotypes, with independent differentiations as replicates and a calcium-only control. A rise in intracellular parent and labelled glutamate or extracellular succinate with persistent HIF abnormality would favor substrate uptake and diversion. An equal shift in both genotypes would support generic pharmacology. These are prospective alternatives. Neither the pancreatic cell study nor the synaptosome study tested WWOX.

## Oral tracer in mouse CSF changes the delivery assessment

The 2026 study by Dong and colleagues distinguishes three experiments. In the repeated treatment cohorts, 3×Tg AD and 5×FAD mice received oral calcium AKG with PBS controls (Supplement p. 7). The tracer study examined CSF eight hours after oral labelled AKG in 3×Tg AD and wild-type mice. Figure S30A–C (p. 49) shows a product-ion spectrum and two chromatograms, with a peak around 0.71 min for the reported M+5 AKG in both groups. A separate experiment used a fluorescent conjugate (Fig. S31). The mass spectra provide chemically more specific evidence of labelled AKG in mouse CSF than whole-brain radiocarbon or conjugate imaging. The route into CSF, neural cytosolic exposure and HIF engagement remain unresolved. [Open supporting information, pp. 7, 14–15, 49–50](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.5c19753.s001)

There is an internal discrepancy. The gavage method on p. 14 identifies the administered tracer as M+2. The analytical method on pp. 14–15 and Fig. S30 report M+5. The tracer salt is unspecified in this method. The figure contains representative traces with relative signal axes and no calibrated paired plasma–CSF concentration or group variance. The adult human serum and CSF comparison in the paper concerns disease-associated levels, without a post-supplement challenge. We therefore retain **qualitative mouse CSF detection with a tracer-identity caveat**, and leave the magnitude, unmodified transport route and exact calcium-salt exposure open. The isogenic WWOX neural HIF-protein prediction above still decides target engagement. [Study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41536056/) · [Supporting information, Fig. S30](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.5c19753.s001)
