# Calcium alpha-ketoglutarate and oxygen sensing in WWOX loss

**Opening prediction.** WWOX loss can change HIF-1α regulation in fibroblasts. Calcium alpha-ketoglutarate (Ca-AKG) is a defined oral salt that supplies a co-substrate for HIF prolyl hydroxylases as well as carbon for central metabolism. These facts raise a conditional question: at physiological oxygen and equal calcium, does added non-esterified alpha-ketoglutarate reach the relevant neural compartment and increase HIF hydroxylation before glycolysis changes? Loss of a WWOX–HIF regulatory interaction, inadequate oxygen or iron, or an already sufficient co-substrate pool could each leave this route unresponsive. The compound was selected because it tests an existing WWOX metabolic pathway with a calcium-matched human comparator available. [Primary WWOX study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4211377/) · [Adult trial of the calcium salt](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17896582/)

**Discriminating comparison.** Compare WWOX-deficient and corrected neural cells at matched oxygen, glucose, pH, osmolality and free calcium. Distinguish alpha-ketoglutarate and succinate in the cellular compartment, prolyl-hydroxylated versus total HIF-1α, nuclear target activity and oxidative carbon flux. A co-substrate limitation predicts that restoring available alpha-ketoglutarate will increase the hydroxylated fraction before restoring the functional readout. A rise in TCA carbon incorporation alone, a calcium-only effect, or a response confined to an ester preparation would change the interpretation. An altered HIF1A transcript alone cannot adjudicate HIF protein turnover.

**First evidence question.** Compare enteral and systemic tracer recovery, exact salt and free versus esterified analyte. Check whether young-animal exposure data constrain intact parent delivery to plasma or CNS. Separate pediatric evidence from the adult bone study. [Young pig first-pass study](https://jn.nutrition.org/article/S0022-3166%2822%2908524-8/fulltext) · [Primary study of ester hydrolysis](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-25228-9)

## First exposure comparison

WWOX-knockout mouse embryonic fibroblasts had lower HIF-1α hydroxylation in the original Fig. 5f experiment. A specific missing comparison is a rescue of this protein readout by non-esterified alpha-ketoglutarate in WWOX neural cells at measured intracellular exposure. Reduced hydroxylation can arise without an alpha-ketoglutarate deficit. [WWOX primary experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4211377/)

In young pigs given duodenal **sodium** alpha-ketoglutarate, mean plasma parent concentration rose from 13.8 to 27.4 µM in arterial blood and from 22.0 to 64.6 µM in portal blood. Net portal balance was 95.2 µmol/kg/h during infusion versus 19.7 with saline. Dividing by the input of 930 µmol/kg/h gives 10.24% net appearance, or 8.12% after subtraction of the saline group mean. These are ratios calculated from published group means. The subtraction is a sensitivity comparison with endogenous net appearance. Neither ratio estimates absolute oral bioavailability or the fraction reaching brain. In a separate labelled-tracer experiment, the authors estimated about 80% first-pass splanchnic utilization. That estimate and study 1's net balance have different denominators. [Lambert et al., Experiments 1 and 3, Table 1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17056800/)

**Decision and next step.** The enteral route can raise systemic parent in this preparation. There is no measured CNS exposure to oral calcium alpha-ketoglutarate in these results. Do not equate the approximately 14 µM arterial increment with cytosolic concentration, or the adult calcium-matched bone trial with a pediatric pharmacokinetic study. Ester preparations can hydrolyze outside cells and influence pH or respiration independently. Next compare transport of non-esterified alpha-ketoglutarate at brain interfaces or in neural cells with the concentration dependence of HIF prolyl hydroxylation, endogenous substrate and competing succinate. Distinguish an increase in extracellular parent, substrate entry and productive enzyme engagement. [Human exact-salt comparator](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17896582/) · [Ester comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34385458/)

**Reproduction.** Run `python3 portal.py` and compare the four outputs with [portal.tsv](portal.tsv). The input means and units appear in the source table.

**Retrieval leads for the next comparison.** Check the substrate attached to every transport constant, whether it measures uptake, competitor inhibition or current, and whether the system is native neural tissue or an expression system. For hydroxylase kinetics, retain isoform, HIF peptide, iron, oxygen and succinate conditions. Do not equate an arterial increment with a difference in neuronal cytosolic substrate. [Mouse-brain transporter clone](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11287335/) · [Human dicarboxylate transport comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21865262/) · [Human HIF prolyl hydroxylase study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12788921/)

A separate mouse study shows labelled AKG in CSF at eight hours, with conflicting tracer identities across methods and figure. Central exposure after the exact calcium salt remains unquantified.
