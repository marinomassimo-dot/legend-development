# Clobazam, its active metabolite and inhibitory recruitment

context_policy: QUESTION_DRIVEN

**Reason for selecting this antiseizure drug.** The existing WWOX programme already audited the maximal responses of recombinant GABA-A receptors to clobazam and N-desmethylclobazam. Its independent comparison found that the metabolite's maximal potentiation favored α2/α3 over α1 in those preparations, with substantial assay dependence and no corresponding subtype preference in potency. Reuse that audit. The unresolved question is whether a clinically observed change in parent/metabolite composition changes inhibition in an immature WWOX network at attainable free concentrations. [Laboratory subtype audit, Tables 1–3 analysis](../../gabaa_subtype_adv004_20261114/verify/audit.md)

**First exposure contrast.** In a controlled epilepsy interaction study with 20 participants overall, after addition of cannabidiol, the day-33/day-1 geometric mean ratio for total plasma AUC over a dosing interval was 1.1 (90% CI 0.9–1.2) for clobazam and 2.6 (2.0–3.6) for its active metabolite. Peak concentration ratios were 1.0 and 2.2, respectively. Using the rounded AUC ratios, the relative change in the metabolite-to-parent exposure ratio is 2.6 / 1.1 ≈ 2.36. This descriptive ratio of published estimates has no independently determined confidence interval. Actual free concentrations in developing brain and the baseline metabolite-to-parent ratio are needed before constructing a mixture. The interaction study did not test a WWOX response. [VanLandingham et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32652616/)

**Discriminating prediction.** At matched overall inhibition in genotype-corrected controls, two parent/metabolite mixtures reflecting different *measured unbound* exposures may have different effects on stimulus-evoked principal-cell spikes and interneuron recruitment in WWOX-deficient neural networks. Preserve native intracellular chloride. Record baseline and evoked firing, inhibitory current kinetics and receptor subtype sensitivity. A mixture effect persisting with parent and metabolite alone would support differential circuit pharmacology. An effect seen only with cannabidiol added directly would favor a contribution from cannabidiol. A fall in spontaneous firing together with loss of evoked transmission would favor generalized suppression. The predicted genotype-by-mixture difference requires replication across independent differentiations.

**Next step.** Locate paired unbound parent/metabolite exposure and the primary receptor concentration–response curves. Test whether a predicted mixture difference survives uncertainty in concentration, endogenous GABA and receptor composition before assigning pediatric trial priority. Distinguish exposure, seizure response and sedation in any pediatric record. Treat seizure treatment histories under changing co-medication as confounded.
