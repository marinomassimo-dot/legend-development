| Setting | Opening observation | Transfer limit |
|---|---|---|
| WWOX mouse, rat and human organoid evidence already assessed in LEGEND | The card documents higher glial measures in some mouse settings and discordant measures in rat or organoid settings. The selected WWOX organoid series carries little canonical microglial RNA. | A matched early phenotype with and without microglia would provide a more informative comparison than pooling different genotypes, stages or species. [Held glial card](../../cards/J01_glial_reactivity_modulation.card.json) |
| Neuron–microglia coculture with an induced inflammatory challenge | The original abstract reports that ibudilast reduced neuronal death elicited by LPS and interferon-γ activation of microglia and changed several secreted mediators. | The challenge is strong and exogenous. Exposure, controls for direct neuronal action, and how much carries over to a WWOX developmental state remain to be checked. [Mizuno et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14975696/) |
| SPRINT-MS, adults with progressive multiple sclerosis | The trial randomized 255 adults (129 ibudilast, 126 placebo). The reported annual change in MRI brain parenchymal fraction was −0.0010 versus −0.0019 over a 96-week study, a difference of +0.0009 (95% CI +0.00004 to +0.0017). | Brain parenchymal fraction summarizes brain volume. Disability, axonal conduction, new sheath formation and WWOX developmental function require their own evidence. [Fox et al., primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30157388/) |

**Replay the initial clinical contrast.** Subtract the reported annual MRI changes within SPRINT-MS, (−0.0010) − (−0.0019) = +0.0009. Use absolute changes and the trial CI. Check the modified intention-to-treat denominator and disability estimate in the original tables before labeling either endpoint.
