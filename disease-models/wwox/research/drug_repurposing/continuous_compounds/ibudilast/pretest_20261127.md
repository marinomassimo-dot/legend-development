# Ibudilast and the timing of glial feedback in WWOX loss of function

**Status.** Opening checkpoint for ibudilast. The held LEGEND glial card already lists it alongside several anti-inflammatory agents and finds that available WWOX glial signals are mixed and time-confounded. This increment asks whether a glial response amplifies recurrent dysfunction, or mainly follows neuronal injury. Ibudilast is a multi-target probe with prior work in activated neuron–microglia coculture and an adult randomized imaging comparator. The question is mechanistic and currently preclinical. [Held LEGEND glial evidence and caveats](../../cards/J01_glial_reactivity_modulation.card.json) · [Activated coculture, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14975696/) · [SPRINT-MS, primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30157388/)

**Prediction.** Compare developmental-stage-matched WWOX/WT neural preparations with and without defined microglia, with matched initial evoked activity and measured local parent. If the drug acts on a delayed microglial amplification loop, early cyclic-nucleotide responses and first recruitment can be similar while a later glial signal, pathological recurrent recruitment and axonal or neuronal injury fall only in the microglia-containing preparations. With matched timing and residual compound below the assay limit, medium transfer from exposed microglia could recapitulate the delayed response. A prompt response in microglia-free preparations or a benefit explained by lower initial output favors a direct circuit or broad suppression mechanism.

**Counterchecks.** Measure uptake and washout, microglial number and viability, phagocytic activity, and inhibitory recruitment with an absolute physiological readout. Compare an acute drug-only effect against conditioned medium and cell-contact conditions. Distinguish correction of an endogenous response from reversal of a strong exogenous immune stimulus. Use target-selective controls only after mapping feasible exposure and functional readouts. Neither an inflammatory marker alone nor reduced adult MRI tissue loss identifies first-sheath formation or seizure benefit.

**Next step.** Quantify the endogenous WWOX glial signal and its timing using existing data and available models before selecting an exposure and target-attribution comparison.
