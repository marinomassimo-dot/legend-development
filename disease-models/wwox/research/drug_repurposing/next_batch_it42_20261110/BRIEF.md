Non-canonical, not medical advice. One thematic batch, one question.

## The question chosen, and why this one

**Is the reduced inhibition of the neuron-specific Wwox knockout slice a deficit of GABA *supply*, of the postsynaptic *response*, or of the *chloride gradient*?** It was chosen from the updated investigations (INV-05, INV-18, INV-19 and the S7 relation of `generation_it42_20261110/second_relation_it42.md`) for the best ratio of decision to evidence to cost: the answer decides whether a supply-side lever (GAT-1 or GABA-transaminase block, entries V11 and V12 of the public list) is a coherent question at all, the receptor-side lever (S5) or the chloride lever (S6); the evidence is partly recoverable from public literature (what the source paper measured, what is known of the chloride state at P13–P17 in mouse cortex, what the repository's own records say), and the cost is reading, not a laboratory.

## Decision it would change

Whether the inhibition-restoring hypotheses (NEED-INHIBITORY-HYPOFUNCTION, alternative AB06) are kept as a supply-side, a receptor-side or a chloride-side question, and which one the minimum experiment (evoked IPSC, E/I ratio, chloride reversal) is ordered first. A "supply" answer moves V11 up; a "response" or "chloride" answer moves V11 down and S5/S6 up; an "undetermined" answer leaves the order as it is.

## Competing explanations to keep

Reduced GABA release or synthesis (supply); fewer or altered GABA-A receptors or interneuron loss (response); depolarising or less hyperpolarising GABA (chloride gradient); network-state or recording artefact (the source recorded without GABA-A block in the intrinsic recordings); a compensatory tonic inhibition change.

## Deliverable and criterion of conclusion

An evidence table with locator quotes (25 words at most) and read depth; for each explanation the measurement that would favour it and the outcome that would weaken it; a statement of what is already decided by the source paper's methods; the list of new measurements (evoked IPSC amplitude, E/I ratio, chloride reversal potential, a matched non-GABAergic suppression control) in order of interpretability. The batch concludes when each explanation has its favouring and weakening outcome and the source paper's recording conditions are stated; it does not need a laboratory result.
