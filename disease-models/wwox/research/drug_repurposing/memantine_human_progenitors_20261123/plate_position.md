# Spatial and exposure sensitivity

Before computing gene responses, the public plate layout identified a further confound. Memantine was always assigned to G15/G16/H15/H16 and the single concentration across these plates was 0.01 µM for six hours. Every donor's same plate has vehicle wells at other positions. A signal reproducible across donors can therefore be position-dependent. Repeat the fixed signature score using only the two closest (Euclidean row/column coordinates) available DMSO wells for that donor and as a separate test the same-row DMSO well(s). Where ties for the second-closest occur, include them all. Report agreement or attenuation descriptively; even stability cannot distinguish a fixed-well effect from drug biology. The series includes seven public drug-bearing matrices, MSA201 to MSA207. The supplement lists an eighth (MSA208); it was absent in the superseries listing checked and a GEO DataSets query for MSA208 returned zero. Do not impute the absent donors' gene responses from their published schizophrenia-signature ranks. Dose is 70-fold below the separate mouse cortical 0.7 µM exposure and 1,000-fold below the 10 µM arm of the acute challenge study.
