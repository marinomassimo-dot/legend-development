# Does direct exposure to memantine elicit a reproducible state in human neural progenitors that opposes WWOX loss?

Frozen before opening any memantine gene response or calculating any WWOX contrast for this hypothesis. The previously examined mouse mature-culture baseline experiment yielded a small unresolved effect. The Readhead et al. supplementary workbook identifies memantine among 135 drugs in human hiPSC neural progenitors (six-hour exposure; 24 listed treated cell lines across control and schizophrenia groups). Sample design, previously reported schizophrenia-signature ranks and group labels were accessible. These donors have no diagnosed WWOX disorder in the study, and no WWOX rescue experiment is present.

Primary question: can a signature learned exclusively from control donors after drug exposure reproduce in held-out control donors, and does it run opposite to the WWOX organoid state? Use the study's designated control NPC donor lines, never cancer cell lines, as the primary set. Pair a treated well with the median of vehicle wells from that line on its plate; retain only donors with both. Aggregate repeated wells of a drug within donor/plate and repeated plate contrasts within donor. Order donors by alphanumeric line identifier and split alternating, odd-position donors for discovery and even for validation. For the primary analysis restrict to public L1000-landmark-mapped gene identities; report whether actual direct intensity or a platform projection is available. If ambiguous, explicitly restrict wording to landmark-associated outputs. Aggregate probe log values by gene median, using only unambiguous single-gene probe mappings. Do not select candidates using disease data. Select the ten most positive and ten most negative median donor effects among genes of the matching sign in at least two thirds of discovery donors, after excluding weak/nonfinite features and requiring expression in the held WWOX human matrix by identifier only. For each held-out donor require the average up-minus-down drug effect to have the expected positive sign; require at least 5/6 (or ceiling of 80% if not six), positive medians of both signed arms, and a median contrast above the 95th percentile of matched pseudo-vehicle contrasts. Match nulls to plate and donor, with one vehicle compared to its other vehicles and fixed panels. If fewer than four distinct control donors per split or fewer than two vehicle wells per held-out line, report restricted validation. Repeat by exchanging discovery and validation splits as sensitivity; the second split uses different genes. Show leave-one-gene results.

Only a signature passing primary validation warrants a reversal decision. In untreated isogenic WWOX organoids require an opposite up-minus-down score stable on omitting each library; a contrast outside a central 95% distribution of random disjoint panels matched on WWOX control abundance, plus negative mean shift of the drug-up arm and positive mean shift of the drug-down arm, would prioritize a progenitor-state rescue hypothesis. Without validation, publish disease application as exploratory at most. Never use an imputed profile as functional evidence of NMDA current or a pharmacokinetic claim. Use RNA-seq in the same human study solely as a cross-platform calibration if memantine was not among those selected for sequencing.
