# How much of the Wwox cortical conductance deficit could a low-concentration ganaxolone response replace?

**Decision.** Ganaxolone can recruit a tonic GABA-A conductance in the same cortical layer in another genetic epilepsy model. Transferring that response to the Wwox-null baseline at 10 nM accounts for about **6%** of the WT–KO passive conductance gap using whole-cell current, or **34%** when scaled by effective capacitance. Directly measuring tonic conductance and native GABA reversal in the three Wwox genotypes has a clearer interpretation than using input resistance alone to select responders. A small conductance could still change a network near a firing transition.

## New transfer calculation

In superficial pyramidal neurons of P13–17 neuronal Wwox deletion mice, published mean input resistances were 102.88 (WT), 143.26 (heterozygote), and 143.03 MΩ (KO). At these means the equivalent conductances are 9.72, 6.98 and 6.99 nS. Thus the KO would need an extra **2.73 nS** to reach the WT reference. Heterozygotes and KOs start at almost identical resistance, while spontaneous field bursts occurred in 4/23 heterozygous versus 36/42 KO slices (WT 0/11). The cellular samples were 13/5, 15/6 and 40/15 cells/animals, respectively. Field slices and cells are separate denominators. These reciprocal means are equivalent model parameters, not means of cell-wise conductance. The prior laboratory review established that the Wwox high-chloride sIPSC recordings cannot determine native GABA reversal. [WWOX primary Table 1 and Fig. 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8609180/) · [Prior measurement audit](../chloride_gradient_sci42_20261110/README.md)

In P16–26 layer II/III pyramidal neurons from γ2-R43Q mice, 10 nM ganaxolone elicited an approximately 10 pA tonic current at −60 mV with a 140 mM KCl internal solution. The publication's Fig. 5a also shows about 0.4 pA/pF. The same investigator group's earlier disclosure gives 9.8 ± 2.5 pA and 0.38 ± 0.08 pA/pF, four cells; these are overlapping evidence. In this model systemic ganaxolone decreased spike–wave discharges at the lower tested exposure. The larger exposure did not reach significance versus its control, which does not establish a difference between the two exposures. Several authors are named on the patent. [Primary methods and Fig. 5](https://doi.org/10.31083/j.jin2301024) · [Earlier numerical disclosure, Fig. 8](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9629853B2/en)

| How the measured current is transferred | Added conductance at 60 mV clamp driving force | WWOX KO resistance after adding it | Fraction of WT–KO conductance gap closed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Whole-cell amplitude transferred unchanged | 0.163 nS | 139.8 MΩ | 6.0% |
| Density transferred to the KO effective capacitance (149 pF) | 0.941 nS | 126.1 MΩ | 34.5% |

For the density scenario, effective capacitance is the published KO time constant (21.25 ms) divided by mean resistance. It is an approximation in a branched neuron with active currents; the donor's measured capacitance and the recipient's effective capacitance are not interchangeable. Varying the clamp driving force from 50 to 70 mV gives **5.1–7.2%** and **29.6–41.4%**, respectively. These scenarios do not bound other possible WWOX receptor responses. Full passive normalization at 60 mV requires an equivalent 164 pA or 1.10 pA/pF at that KO capacitance, compared with the reference 9.8 pA and 0.38 pA/pF. Distinct genotypes, developmental ages and local drug partitioning limit transfer. The prior regulatory PK analysis placed estimated unbound plasma around 7–9 nM at a CDD model peak under a 1% unbound assumption. Free WWOX brain concentration and free concentration inside the experimental slice are unknown. [Exposure derivation](../ganaxolone_paqr_survival_20261123/README.md)

## A prediction that changes with native chloride

At an illustrative native GABA reversal of −65 mV (above the −73.62 mV KO rest but below its −41.32 mV spike threshold), the model predicts that adding the conductance **depolarizes rest** by 0.20 or 1.02 mV, yet increases the sustained current required to reach the original threshold by **3.87 or 22.28 pA**. When reversal lies above the threshold, that current increment switches sign. These values follow the passive steady-state current balance. They are not measurements of rheobase, transient shunting, chloride loading or spike output.

The discriminating comparison requires separate measurements of conductance per cell and per pF, native GABA reversal relative to each cell's spike threshold, and the change in firing during a matched input in WT, heterozygous and KO neurons. If the KO has unusually strong tonic recruitment relative to the heterozygote, the nearly identical baseline resistances make that informative. Equivalent tonic responses with differing network benefit would shift attention to cell-type recruitment and circuit dynamics.
