## Answer first

**The figure does not size NX1. The two autocorrelation bounds give animal numbers 16 to 26 times apart for the question that matters (is there any effect in wild type?), which is the stop condition set in the assignment.** What the figure can say is a range and the reason it is wide.

| Question (test) | gap to detect (window-average stage, minutes 30 to 60) | animals per arm, minutes independent (lower bound) | animals per arm, minutes perfectly correlated within animal (upper bound) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Any effect in the nulls (A or B against C), observed gap | 1.06 | <3 | 22 (power 0.8), 29 (0.9) |
| Same, if only half the observed gap is real | 0.53 | 5 to 6 | 84 to 112 |
| Effect in wild type under a proportional model (B against A), +/+ PTZ level | 0.14 | 12 to 16 | 311 to 416 |
| Same, from the +/- PTZ level | 0.30 | 5 to 6 | 80 to 107 |

Two-sided alpha 0.05, equal n per arm, normal quantiles with the small-sample term, power 0.8 and 0.9 (`output/sizing.tsv`). The formula was checked on the textbook case (difference of one SD: 17 and 22 per arm).

1. **Which summary (first step, stated before any number).** The per-animal window-average Racine stage over minutes 30 to 60 (the late phase, where the null effect is). Why: it is the only per-animal summary the figure's means let me bound (a mean over minutes of the per-minute means is the window average of the group), the area under the curve over a fixed window is the same quantity times the window length, so it gives the same test; a rank-based test on the window average is the better analysis for an ordinal, floor-limited score (wild type sits at 0.25 with most animals at 0) but its power depends on the shape of the distribution, which no figure of means and SEMs holds, so it cannot be sized here. **INFERENZA:** a rank test would need at least about 0.86 of the t-test's information in the worst case and can need less for a skewed score; the numbers above are for the t-test and are not inflated for that.
2. **What bounds the between-animal SD of the window average (DATO plus one stated assumption).** Per-minute between-animal SD = upward SEM x sqrt(N), N from the legend. The window average of correlated minutes has an SD between sqrt(mean(SD_min^2)/T) (independent minutes) and mean(SD_min) (perfectly correlated; an SD of an average cannot exceed the average of the SDs). The two are a factor of about sqrt(T) apart, so animal numbers are about T = 30 times apart before any rounding. The independent bound is physically implausible for a persistent score; the figure cannot say where between them the truth lies. Nulls: PTZ 0.31 to 1.56, PTZ+ETS 0.13 to 0.71 (`output/arm_summaries.tsv`).
3. **The wild-type question is the expensive one, and the upper bound there is partly a detection limit.** For wild type the upward bars are mostly shorter than the marker half height (hidden), so the upper SD uses 0.11 as the bar (the largest bar that could hide). That is an upper limit by construction, not a measurement: the 311 to 416 per arm is an over-estimate of unknown size. Equally, the lower bound is unreachable. The figure bounds nothing here beyond "between 12 and about 400 animals per arm to see a proportional effect of 0.14 stage".
4. **An absolute-scale interaction test cannot tell A from B (INFERENZA, arithmetic).** The null late-phase PTZ level (1.87) is 7 times the wild-type one (0.25). Under B (the same proportional reduction, 57%) the null gap is 1.06 and the wild-type gap 0.14, so the absolute genotype-by-drug interaction is 0.92 and is "significant" in a modest sample although B is true. A and B differ only in whether the wild-type gap is zero, or in a ratio-scale (proportional) interaction; the test that separates them is the wild-type simple effect (last two rows), or a test on the log of the ratio of means. A written NX1 that says "drug-by-genotype interaction" without naming the scale would be satisfied by B and would be misread as A.
5. **What this changes in the ranking row (a proposal; the integrator decides, nothing was edited).** `next_experiment_or_control` for the ethosuximide unit can say: "a drug-by-genotype test with a same-day control, read on the proportional scale; the Fig 7 extraction does not bound its size: any effect in the nulls is within reach at about 22 to 112 animals per arm worst case, a proportional effect in wild type needs between about 12 and several hundred per arm depending on a within-animal persistence the figure does not show; first obtain a per-animal table or a pilot variance". The lithium unit's wording is not sized here (different panel, exact values; effect already visible in wild type, so the B-versus-A question does not arise for it).

## Limits

Means and SEMs of an ordinal score; no per-animal point; one experiment. The observed gap may be an over-read single experiment (winner's curse), hence the half-gap row. Raster extraction error is stated in the parent package (0.004 to 0.025 stage between files, controls at 0.00 to -0.02, bias low when markers overlap). The wild-type and heterozygous minutes in which the PTZ marker is hidden under the treated marker are missing from the window (19 of 31 minutes for +/+ PTZ, 17 for +/-), which overstates the visible |gap| and leaves the window mean of those arms to the minutes that remain. Normal-theory formula; below 3 per arm it is not valid and is shown as "<3". Not independent of the parent: same files, same author; no blind check (my call: one reader, the weakest point is the within-animal persistence, already marked as unknown).
