Soundness of the rules: `ev_class` is sound as a screen (prediction = `knowledge_level` prediction, text-mining agent or source, or an extraction score; OBSERVED only for a knowledge_assertion with PMID/DOI, affinity or method; otherwise ANNOTATION). `sign_of` is conservative (compound must be subject, a direction qualifier and an aspect `activity`/`activity_or_abundance`; `expression`, `unspecified`, CTD aspects give 0). Weaknesses, UNCERTAINTY:
1. OBSERVED means "a curated assertion with a reference", not "the sign was measured": dactolisib is a PI3K inhibitor yet BindingDB and Pharos give `activity increased` (+1, OBSERVED); a source-encoding error is likelier than a finding. The reading text "has a non-prediction origin" should not be read as support for the EC-KG sign in those 4 pairs.
2. `sign_of` also admits `infores:lincs` regulation edges (aspect `activity_or_abundance`, upregulated/downregulated, about 10,500 rows, knowledge_assertion by a data pipeline, no publication, so evidence class ANNOTATION). That is an expression signature, not target activity. None of the 10 discordant pairs rests on LINCS, but the rule would count it. Whether any LEGEND pair agrees/disagrees only through LINCS was not checked.
3. `activity_or_abundance` mixes activity and abundance (text-mined rows), so even the text-mining sign need not be an activity sign.
