# RP-005 / ADV-109: the REVISED prospective test on three unread full texts (IT, 2026-11-20; hypothesis level; no dose is predicted, no recommendation; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

**Frozen in two steps, both BEFORE any prediction exists or anyone opens a target text.** Step 1 (this file, with the slot tables (reworded once after the wording pilot of section 3a), the domain-prior baseline table and its instantiated predictions, the decision margin and `INPUTS_MANIFESTS.tsv`) is committed first; step 2 (the three prediction sets per text, anonymised, with a scorer sheet and a sealed key hash) is committed second; SCI is then asked to score. Revisions over ADV-106 (RESULT_RP005_ADV106_IT_20261120.md section 5): at least eight slots per text, each with an `OTHER / NOT REPORTED` option; a domain-prior baseline table written before the generator ran; the abstract-only arm kept; three texts, one scorer; the decision margin written below; a pre-check of each slot against the abstract; a wording pilot by a reader who has not seen any text.

## 1. What is tested (the honest label)
The repository's organised knowledge about a compound (the cards, sheets and notes, as a reader sees them) plus a delegate as predictor, against (A) a domain-prior baseline table and (C) the same delegate given the abstract only. It is NOT a test of the compound-proposal engine (`run_generation_it21.py`) and not of any scientific claim about WWOX. Three texts, about 28 slots: a pilot of moderate size, not a trial. The result is used to say whether the engine can be described in a decision dossier as **predictive**, **organising** or **neither** (section 7).

### 3a. Wording pilot (done before the freeze)

## 4. Scoring rules (written now)
SCI scores blind to which set came from which source (three anonymous sets per text, order randomised per text, the key sealed). For each slot of each set: **HIT** (the option matches the text), **MISS** (another option, including OTHER / NOT REPORTED when the text does give a value), **NOT DETERMINABLE** (the text does not give it, or only in a figure that cannot be read, or the text could not be obtained). Every score carries a quoted sentence and a locator. `OTHER / NOT REPORTED` scores HIT only when SCI has searched the body, legends, methods and table titles and states what it searched ('found by these routes', never 'the paper does not'). A NOT DETERMINABLE slot leaves the denominator of every set. Where an option is ambiguous SCI reports the ambiguity and gives a second count; the first count stays primary.

## 6. Decision margin (fixed now)
Pool the slots that SCI scores determinable (a slot counts in a comparison only if it is determinable; the denominator is the same for A, B and C). B 'beats' X (X = A or C) when B has at least four more pooled hits than X AND B is not behind X in more than one of the three texts. Counts are reported per text, per arm, per slot type (protocol, exposure, readout), and as discordant pairs (slots where B hits and X misses against slots where B misses and X hits); no significance test is claimed.

## 9. Amendments written AFTER the predictions existed and BEFORE any text was scored (disclosed; none can favour an arm, because no true answer is known to IT)
1. **Scoring protocol made stricter than section 4.** SCI is asked for the TRUE option of every slot (a truth table with a quoted sentence and locator per slot, `SCORER_SHEET_RP005_ADV109_20261120.md`) and sees NO prediction of any arm. IT then computes every hit mechanically against the sealed prediction file, whose SHA-256 is committed in the scorer sheet (the file itself is committed only after SCI's truth table is on main; ADV holds a second copy). This replaces the anonymous-sets-plus-sealed-key design of section 4 and section 8; blindness of the scorer is total, and no source can leak through the order of the sets.
2. **Baseline instantiation erratum.** Rule R3 ('an unmentioned readout is absent') gives the 'not measured / not tested' option, which for T1-S4 is C and for T2-S3 is C; the instantiated baseline above wrote A and B. The frozen instantiation stays the primary arm A; the rule-faithful variant A' (the two slots changed) is reported as a sensitivity. Found while assembling the sealed file; the generator and abstract-only arms were already delivered, and the true answers were unknown.
3. **Power finding about the margin of section 6.** With all predictions in hand, B and C differ on only four of the 28 slots, B and A on only four. A margin of four more pooled hits can then be met only if every differing slot favours B. The margin of section 6 is NOT changed. Added: when B and X differ on fewer than eight slots, a failure to meet the margin is reported as **INCONCLUSIVE (too few slots on which the arms differ)**, not as 'neither', and the decision of section 7 for that case is 'REVISE ONCE MORE with slots chosen so that arms differ' rather than 'REMOVE'. The discordant-pair counts remain the primary report whatever the margin says.
