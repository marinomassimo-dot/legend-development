## 3. Reading (what the numbers do and do not carry)
* **No demonstrated advantage of the repository materials.** B's three hits that others missed are T1-S2 (phase I metabolites), T1-S4 (protein binding not measured, shared with C and A') and T3-S1 (eye ratio lower at the highest dose); **in B's own delivered reasons every one of them is 'general knowledge', not a manifest file** (T1-S2: 'general knowledge: thiazolidinone, LC-MS only, tentative phase I assignments'; T3-S1: 'general knowledge: GABA analogue, probably saturable transport'). The slots on which B cites a manifest file (T1-S3 cell potency, T1-S8 tissue order, T3-S3 whole eye) were hit equally by C, which had no manifest. So the B-over-C difference of one slot is delegate variance until shown otherwise (one run per text; no replicate was made); it is not attributable to the materials.
* **The abstract-only delegate was as good as, or slightly better than, the domain-prior table:** C 17 against A 15 (A' 17). Both delegate arms add about two hits over the frozen rule table, and both converge on 24 of 28 slots.
* **Where the arms are wrong together (the informative part).** Nine slots were missed by all four arms: T1-S5 (age 5 to 6 weeks, a band edge), T1-S6 (Cremophor EL:ethanol:saline, which none of the options names), T1-S7 (a PC-3 weight loss attributed to the tumour), T2-S6 (a non-Racine four-point scale), T2-S8 (no vehicle non-withdrawal group exists), T2-S10 (age not given), T3-S2 (brain value at the middle dose not available), T3-S6 (no weight under treatment reported) and T3-S9 (liver S/R ratio enriched against plasma, which every arm put at 'about the same'). They are the facts that only the body holds: vehicle composition, a lost sample, an unreported weight, an enrichment that contradicts the lipophilic-linear prior. A predictor built from general knowledge and abstract-level material converges on the modal protocol and cannot reach them; that is the same finding as ADV-106 (arms A and C agreed on 4 of 5 slots).
* **A definition trap found by the scorer (T3 note N1).** The abstract's 'tissue/plasma ratios' (eye 5.5, brain 1.5 and so on) are numerically the paper's tissue S/R enantiomer ratios, not total-drug partition ratios (brain about 0.13 and 0.20, eye about 1.8, 1.1 and 0.8, derived by SCI from Table 1); T3-S1 and T3-S2 named the quantity loosely ('as the abstract uses them'), and any predictor reading the abstract's numbers predicts the wrong quantity. The single T3-S1 hit by B (a lower ratio at the highest dose, 44 percent below) is right under both readings, but B reached it from a saturation argument, not from the S/R point; I count it as a hit and flag it as not evidence of understanding. Slot design again leaked ambiguity: ten of 28 slots are definition-exposed (SCI), versus the five of ADV-106's slots flagged by A1 to A5.
* **Contamination I declare.** IT wrote the slots after reading the abstracts; the T1 and T3 abstract facts were in the sheet context of delegates only through the manifest (T3's sheet held the title only); each delegate ran once; three delegate runs reported partly recalling the paper from training (T1 arm B, T3 arm B and T1 arm C), which no design here can remove; the T3 abstract given to arm C had its S and R symbols restored by IT (the source text had lost them).

## 5. Decision on RP-005 and how the engine may be described
* **Decision: REVISE ONCE MORE, narrowly (or park).** The test is inconclusive on its own pre-registered terms, and together with ADV-106 (single text, tie) it gives two pilots in which the repository-materials generator did not separate from a rule table or from the abstract-only delegate.
* **Description allowed in a decision dossier today:** 'the generative step has been tested prospectively twice (one text, then three texts, 33 slots); the arms converged on most slots and no advantage of the repository materials over the abstract was shown; it is not shown to be predictive, and its value, if any, is organising (it retrieves what the repository holds), not forecasting'. Do not cite it as predictive.
* **If revised (not recommended unless the funding question needs it):** (1) choose slots on which the arms are expected to DIFFER, that is facts the repository holds and the abstract does not (an internal one-sentence pre-check: 'does a manifest file hold the answer?'), for example facts from our own sheets about a compound's in vivo use that the new paper's abstract omits; (2) replicate each generator run three times so that delegate variance is measured; (3) fix the definition of each quantitative slot against the abstract's own wording after a pilot read that includes the quantity's numerical form; (4) drop protocol slots (twelve of 28 were hit equally by all arms: they measure the delegate's priors, not the repository).
* **What the test did show that is useful elsewhere.** The facts that decide a pharmacological comparison and that only a body holds (vehicle, sample loss, unreported weights, enantiomer enrichment) were missed by all arms; a sheet that says 'abstract read' about an exposure paper is therefore a statement of what is not known, and the open-question lines already say so.
