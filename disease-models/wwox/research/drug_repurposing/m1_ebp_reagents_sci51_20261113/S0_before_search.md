## 2 · What is asked, and the form of the answer
(1) **Identity** of each reagent named in SCI50 (the M1-selective antagonist; the EBP inhibitors: TASIN-1 and the CW9956 family), with the pharmacology the primaries document and the source, depth stated per paper (abstract, text, figures, supplement); identities that rest on abstracts or ledger rows only are stated as such.
(2) **Cross-activities**, measured versus unknown, **kept apart** (an absent measurement is not an absent activity): EBP activity of the M1 antagonist; M1 or other muscarinic activity of the EBP inhibitors; sterol-pathway effects of each; other off-targets (sigma, histamine H1, Kir, hERG where documented).
(3) **Conditions that make the comparison informative:** concentrations against measured potencies in the assay used (not against binding potency from another system), M1 tone in the preparation, readouts and their levels, the genotype crossing, the controls that change the reading.
(4) **Discriminating outcomes** (not expected results) that would separate: the M1 route, the EBP route, both, an OPC-intrinsic block, a neuronal ceiling.
Form: a qualified comparison (reagents with documented identity, activity and cross-activity, conditions and outcomes), or an improved design, or the precise statement of what prevents it (for example an unreachable primary) with the sources tried. ## 3 · Priors (recollection or inference; unverified; scored afterwards, may fail)
* **R1.** The EBP activity of PIPE-307 is unmeasured in every primary; no public data exist, and an M1 antagonist cannot be assumed EBP-inert (EBP binds many cationic amphiphilic drugs).
* **R2.** TASIN-1 and the CW9956 family have measured low muscarinic affinity in primaries, but the numbers sit in figures or supplements and the assays differ (CHO reporter, binding, concentration).
* **R3.** The four antimuscarinics that were inert in Hubler's EpiSC OPCs are the cleanest measured evidence that M1 antagonism alone does not enhance differentiation in that preparation, but the preparation's cholinergic tone is unmeasured, so the result does not exclude an M1 route.
* **R4.** No primary measures M1 expression or acetylcholine tone in the EpiSC-derived, primary rat and immunopanned mouse OPC preparations in the same assay as the drug effect (PIPE-307's ACh values are the exception for primary rat OPC).
* **R5.** The separating experiment needs at least three reagent classes (M1-selective, EBP-selective, both) plus a measured readout of each target (sterol intermediates for EBP; ACh tone and M1 occupancy for M1); two chemotypes are not assumed to share a target.
* **R6.** A Chrm1-null contrast is not interpretable when the null baseline occludes; an EBP-null contrast (genetic) has no published OPC datum.
