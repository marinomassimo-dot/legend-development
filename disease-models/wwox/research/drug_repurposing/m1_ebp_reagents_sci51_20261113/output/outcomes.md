# SCI51 outcomes: discriminating outcomes for the M1 versus EBP comparison (producer P; not medical advice)

Labels DATO / INFERENZA / IPOTESI. Arms: **A** vehicle; **B** M1-selective reagent (PIPE-307 class or MT7); **C** EBP-selective reagent (TASIN-1 or CW class, dosed against OPC potency); **D** B + C; **E** sterol-pathway control (ketoconazole) and mevastatin; **F** M1 reagent + ACh/carbachol add-back; **G** sgEBP (CRISPR) plus B; **H** clemastine as the dual comparator. Each arm set is run in OPC alone, and in the WWOX-class genotype crossing (reference-genotype OPC on wild-type neurons and the reverse). Readouts by level: sterol intermediates (GC/MS), MBP+ share, segment-length index. Gates first (INFERENZA): measured ACh tone in the preparation; B alone acts; sterol profile of B and C at the dose used.

| Outcome (arm set) | Favours | Weakens |
|---|---|---|
| B acts, C does not, sterols flat on B and rising on C; D equals B (arms A,B,C,D, tone present) | M1 route | EBP route as the cause of the clemastine effect; EBP-only reading of Hubler |
| C acts, B does not despite measured tone and a sterol-clean profile; D equals C | EBP route | M1 route; Mei's Chrm1 reading as sufficient |
| B acts and C acts, D exceeds both at submaximal doses, no sterol change on B, no M1 antagonism on C at the doses used | both routes, independent | single-target accounts of clemastine |
| B acts but sterols accumulate on B | B has an EBP activity (R1): the arm is not M1-selective; the contrast is uninterpretable until sgEBP + B (arm G) separates them | M1-only attribution for PIPE-307 |
| B inert only where tone is not measured or ACh add-back (F) restores the block | absence of tone, not absence of an M1 route | "M1 antagonism does not act in this preparation" |
| Neither B, C nor D shifts a deficit that is present with reference-genotype OPCs on wild-type neurons, while E and T3 shift it | an OPC-intrinsic block the reagents cannot reach (lipid route or thyroid route open) | both routes as the limiting route |
| Reagents act on reference-genotype OPCs on wild-type neurons but the gap persists on wild-type OPCs over reference-genotype neurons, and a medium arm reproduces it | a neuronal ceiling | OPC-intrinsic block as the sole cause |

**Inconclusive when:** the assay window is not open (T3 or ketoconazole fail to produce the positive control); tone below a measurable level; a dose ladder lacks the in-assay EC50 (CW3388 had no floor in the source); saturation (Hubler: no additivity at a maximal ketoconazole dose) so non-additivity is read as same-pathway; the Chrm1-null contrast (the null baseline is about 4 to 5-fold above WT, as large as the drug effect), which is interpretable only when positive; wells counted as independent preparations; a drug effect that is partly microglial or in vivo only.

**Three claims, kept apart.** (1) *Acts through its target in OPC*: needs the cross-activity profile (reagents.tsv, activities.tsv) and the sterol or tone gate; DATO from sources only for TASIN-1/CW (EBP) and clemastine/benztropine (both). (2) *Useful with a neuronal defect*: needs the genotype crossing; no source tests it; INFERENZA. (3) *Transfer to a proposal*: not supported by anything here; no dose, no ranking, no candidate nominated.
