## 2 · Identity (item 2)

* **TASIN-1, resolved on reachable evidence.** DATO: Fig 4I draws a 4-methoxyphenylsulfonyl piperidine whose 4-carbon bears the nitrogen of a 4-methylpiperidine (bipiperidine, basic second nitrogen). PubChem CID 1329829 "Tasin-1" is the same (C18H28N2O3S, XCBHYDPDIJQQGM). ChEMBL CHEMBL4514756 (same InChIKey) is in the De Brabander-laboratory J Med Chem 2019 analogue document (CHEMBL4396877; DLD1 63 nM, HT-29 53 nM, HCT116 > 5 uM, plus ADME). ChEMBL CHEMBL5287158 (C-linked 4-methylcyclohexyl, no basic second nitrogen) carries Zhang's 70 nM and > 50 uM values but its source document is a 2021 Eur J Med Chem **review** deposit (CHEMBL5226288): a redrawing in a secondary record. INFERENZA: the cationic-amine requirement for EBP inhibition stated by Allimuthu and Sax fits the bipiperidine and not the cyclohexyl variant. **Not resolved:** the structure drawn in Zhang 2016 itself (figure not opened by anyone).
* **PIPE-307: identity withheld, not unread (DATO).** PNAS Editorial Expression of Concern (PMID 40512803, June 2025, read in full): the authors did not include characterisation data; "A global license and development agreement has prevented the inclusion of the chemical structure of PIPE-307 in the paper", not disclosed to the editors at submission. The Correction (PMID 39432800, Oct 2024) adds that all authors hold shares and Contineum Therapeutics owns patent rights to PIPE-307. No structure in the main text or the appendix; PubChem, ChEMBL and Europe PMC patent search by name return nothing. **The producer file treats the paper as a clean source and does not mention either notice (defective, RG10 and a NEW row).** Consequence: PIPE-307 cannot be procured, checked for purity or tested for EBP activity by anyone outside the developer from the public record.
* **CW9956, CW9009, CW3388, CW1143:** identity rests on text sentences; their structures are in Fig 2B and Fig 3 and the data supplements, unreachable to me (the page behind the bot check; the supplement URL is forbidden). Unverifiable (verdict). No public database record by name.
* **Hubler's four inert antimuscarinics:** unreachable by every allowed route (PMC page reCAPTCHA; tool text has legends without heatmap labels). Narrowed (INFERENZA): the Methods list five candidates for four slots (pirenzepine, telenzepine, scopolamine, homatropine, p-F-HHSiD); the "Sigma H127" abbreviation footnote is in the ED6 legend for muscarinic modulators, so p-F-HHSiD is very probably among the six.

## 4 · `outcomes.md` and `conditions.tsv`, adversarially (item 4)

* **Overstated:** CD09 says the Chrm1-null baseline is "as large as the drug effect". Fig 4c: WT about 4, null about 16 per field (4x); drug-treated WT is 5.3-7.3x vehicle, about 21-29 per field, above the null baseline. The null is below the drug-treated level, so on these numbers the loss of drug effect is not a ceiling artefact (INFERENZA) and reads as M1-dependence at an unreported drug concentration. The conflict that matters is then between Mei's preparation (M1 required) and Hubler's (EBP route, antimuscarinics inert): a preparation difference rather than a reagent difference, and Mei's concentration cannot be compared with Hubler's.
* **Not discriminating as written:** outcome 1 ("D equals B ... M1 route") is what a ceiling also gives; it needs submaximal doses of both reagents and a headroom arm (T3) in the same plate (Hubler: non-additivity only at maximal ketoconazole, T3 stayed additive). Outcome 2 ("C acts, B does not") lacks an arm **C plus agonist add-back**: it is the one observation that tests the CW3388 M1 residue, since a pure EBP effect should not shrink under ACh or carbachol.
* **Genuinely discriminating, keep:** B acts and sterols accumulate on B (EBP activity of the M1 arm; the single most informative gate; confirm with sgEBP plus B because 24 h sterol readouts at n = 2 wells can miss weak inhibition); B inert only where tone is absent, restored by add-back (now sharper because tone scales with plating density, 12/5/2 nM); additivity absent with ketoconazole (only if submaximal); EBP CRISPR pooled not germline (EpiSC OPC-5 only, mRNA n = 1 well); CW3388 floor not reached (110 nM lowest dose); TASIN-1 tested at 100 nM against 1 uM for the others; CHO versus OPC potency (measured only for the rat M1 arm).
* **Wrong or incomplete statements:** CD02 and CD05 ("no EBP reagent in rat primary or immunopanned mouse OPC", "three preparations"): a fourth preparation (mouse primary cortical OPCs, TASIN-1 300 nM single concentration) exists in Hubler ED5; "no potency" still holds. CD01 treats PIPE-307 as buildable; it cannot be built by structure (section 2); the M1 arm with disclosed identity and documented OPC activity is MT7 (a peptide; EBP activity unmeasured), or a classical M1-preferring antagonist without an OPC datum.
* **Decoration for this question (drop):** outcomes 6 and 7 (OPC-intrinsic block, neuronal ceiling) and CD08 (genotype crossing) rest on no source in this package and do not bear on whether the reagent pair is interpretable; CD13 (exposure) is not a condition of the comparison; CHO-versus-OPC potency and rat-versus-mouse species are real caveats but not the decisive gap.
* **Is the pair interpretable now? No, and "improved design, not build-ready" is if anything mild.** Decisive gaps: (1) an M1-selective reagent of disclosed identity whose EBP activity is measured in the OPC preparation used (PIPE-307 fails on identity, not only on the missing datum); (2) a measured, density-controlled M1 tone in that preparation. Next in line: the CW3388 M1 residue at its OPC dose (add-back arm), and a same-preparation EC50 for each class.
