## Material things the items omit
- 12122058 species: NR1/NR2 cRNA species is not stated in the paper; if the WO-12 table lists a species for the oocyte system it must come from the cited earlier work, not this paper. Isolated-domain work uses rat NR2A and mouse NR2B.
- The 0.11 and 39.5 uM ifenprodil values in the 29446949 review (ratio about 359) are flagged "calculated at human receptors" and are consistent with the 8768735 abstract's approximately 350-fold, so they probably trace to that paper; not confirmed (I could not read the review's reference for the footnote). Neither 8768735 nor the review abstract gives the absolute values.
- 29446949 Table 2 footnote a ("3 µM glycine was used for IC50 determination") applies to some row(s); the table's conditions are therefore not uniform. Which rows I could not establish.
- 9482779 is labelled "Review" in PubMed article types, though it reads as primary data.
- 12122058 text has a trivial inconsistency: legend 155 nM / nH 0.98 vs Results 156 nM / 0.99.
- All full-text quotes were returned by a summarising page reader, not read raw; wording was consistent across repeated queries.
