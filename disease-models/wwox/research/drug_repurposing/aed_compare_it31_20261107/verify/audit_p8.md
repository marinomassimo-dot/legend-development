## The points you asked about

Mechanism sentences. Ethosuximide: "targeting thalamic low-threshold T-type calcium currents" is verbatim from 42536722 and fine; that abstract adds the dissociation (Cav3.1 deletion stopped seizures but not the social deficit). Levetiracetam: SV2A is source-supported; the negative "not a calcium-channel or glutamate-receptor mechanism" is the producer's addition. Perampanel: AMPA antagonist supported; "not an NMDA modulator" is added. In the Wwox context, the T-type attribution comes only from the Methods of 32000863 ("a T-type Ca channel blocker that has anticonvulsant activity (Luszczki et al., 2005)"), which the module already marks as inferred; the comparison keeps that distinction in row 12 but muddies it with the "source paper ... GIRK" wording.

"Levetiracetam appears only as co-therapy in specific child rows". Not supported as stated. The A1 table (cases_a1.tsv) names levetiracetam in the quoted locator or co-therapy cell of 17 rows. Distinct children in PMID 36779245 alone: patients 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 (rows A1-37, -38, -41, -42, -43, -44, -47, -49, -50, -51, -54 and duplicates), while the comparison lists only patient 5 from that report. Counting the other four reports the comparison names (first child of 30361190, 39101447, 35573960, patients 2.2/3.1/4 of 35792847), the floor is about 16 children in 5 reports, not "7 rows in 5 reports". Also A1-54 (patient 5) and A1-32 (patient 4) are rows where levetiracetam is a main drug (patient 5: "valproate and levetiracetam only"; patient 4: "controlled on levetiracetam and topiramate"), so "co-therapy in a row coded for another class" mislabels them. The "NO PERCENTAGE IS GIVEN" caution stands, and the statement is called a floor, but the number and the "only co-therapy" wording are both wrong in the direction of understatement. Related: the "0 child rows name perampanel / ethosuximide" statements (rows 16, 19) are false (A1-38, A1-43, A1-50). Whether those rows have no outcome per drug remains true (Table 2 is a current-drug list, no response).

Wwox-null pup ethosuximide experiment and its limits. The source supports: suppression of PTZ-induced seizure in Wwox-/- (Fig. 7b), no effect in +/+ and +/- at a low PTZ dose, ethosuximide 150 mg/kg i.p. 45 min before PTZ, preweaning mice. Limits stated in the comparison (small groups, no drug-by-genotype interaction test, graphical reading, floor-limited wild type, one lab, one experiment) all come from the module (needs.json O1 limits), not from the paper; the paper itself states none of them, and I could not see Fig. 7b, so "late-phase graphical reading", "N 6 per arm" and "significant only in null" were not verified against the figure. The comparison does carry them faithfully from the record. The paper's own framing (lithium "better than" ethosuximide) is stronger than the data in the text allow; the comparison treats it as a contrast row only, which is right.

Two cross-model studies. 21635238 (WAG/Rij rats): statement faithful; one addition worth carrying, that carbamazepine increased SWDs and zonisamide arms exist, and that the paper concludes depressive-like behaviour is unrelated to seizure control in this model. 40198515 (lithium-pilocarpine SE rats): faithful; the "only combinations did" is correct per abstract, the trio was best.
