## Answer first

**A Kir2 *mechanism* can be tested now at family level; a Kir2 *treatment* cannot be proposed, and no direction of benefit is assumed.** Frame fixed before searching (`S0_before_search.md`); one producer, one blind verifier (own strings, a narrow recheck), corrections applied; nothing below claims the Wwox deficit is Kir2.

1. **Blocker probe: ML133, at family level only.** It blocks recombinant Kir2.1/2.2/2.3/2.6 (IC50 1.9/2.9/4.0/2.8 µM at pH 7.4; Kir2.4 untested; Kir2.1 0.29 µM at pH 8.5) and spares Kir1.1, Kir4.1 and Kir7.1 by 17- to more than 150-fold. It also blocks **Kir6.2/SUR1 (7.7 µM)** and **hERG (about 5 µM in a PubChem automated-patch deposit of unstated final pH, about 2.7-fold more potent than Kir2.1 on the same automated platform)**; there is no K2P test, no isoform discrimination within Kir2 and no cortical-neuron test; its authors limit it to in vitro use. The producer's first reading of a 17-fold hERG window was a cross-platform comparison and is withdrawn (verifier). Role: **a family-level comparator probe that can attribute a conductance to "Kir2 family or KATP/hERG-type" only after KATP (glibenclamide) and hERG (subthreshold range) are excluded.** (`output/roles.tsv`, `compounds.tsv`.)
2. **Activator: none qualifies as a probe; documented direct-mechanism Kir2.1 activators exist, and the SCI43 statement "no Kir2 activator" was a search-route artefact.** Flecainide (EC50 0.8 µM; Cys311; +22% at −120 mV and +54% at −50 mV in CHO cells), propafenone (EC50 12 nM, Cys311, Kir2.1 only; **abstract depth**) and timolol (Cys311; abstract depth) raise Kir2.1 current in recombinant cells and spare Kir2.2/2.3 homomers. **Only flecainide** was tested on Kir2.1+2.2 and Kir2.1+2.3 heteromers (not increased) and on a native tissue (guinea-pig ventricular IK1, +19.5%). None is tested in neurons; flecainide and propafenone are also Na⁺ channel blockers. Zacopride (one laboratory, hypoxic CHO cells at 100 µM), tetramisole (rat myocytes) and sildenafil (Kir2.2 at 10 µM only, mixed effect) are claims without a direct mechanism. Roles: conditional hypothesis (flecainide, propafenone, timolol), not interpretable (the others); the direction of benefit is an unresolved premise, so none is a favourable candidate. No Kir2.2/Kir2.3 opener was found (IT44-Q01).
3. **The juvenile Ba2+-sensitive share (IT48-Q01, second half): not found.** Two independent string sets (producer and verifier) found no measurement at about P13–P17 in cortical pyramidal neurons; the nearest are layer V medial prefrontal at P21 (200 µM Ba2+, room temperature) and layer 2/3 medial prefrontal at P26–P51 (200 µM Ba2+ by a local flow pipe; values not extracted), and fast-spiking interneurons in the second to third postnatal week (leak = Kir2 plus TASK; abstract). **This is not an absence claim**; the P28–P42 about 23% share (IT48) remains one Ba2+ dose, not an isoform and not P13–P17.
4. **Hypotheses: one conditional kept, two dropped to notes.** **H1** (kept, conditional, a family-level mechanism test): the ML133-sensitive part of the Ba2+ difference in L2/3 neurons, with KATP removed (glibenclamide), a hERG-sparing range, a defined positive control (Kir2.1-expressing cells or an identified Kir2-dominant native cell, same bath, same day), and bath-versus-pipette delivery if the site of action permits; alternatives K2P/TASK, KATP, glial Kir2.1/Kir4.1 acting through K⁺ handling. The pH 6.5 arm of the first draft is withdrawn (pH acts on permeation; a TASK-type leak is itself acid-sensitive). **H2** (an activator acting on the outward region near rest) is **dropped**: heteromers are insensitive in the one test, the drugs are Na⁺ channel blockers, the direction premise is unresolved, and the discriminating control would be Cys311 genetics. **H3** (an indirect Kir4.1/extracellular K⁺ contribution) is a **design note**: VU0134992 acts equally on Kir3 and Kir4.2, so VU6036720 is the natural pair (its full text is unread). (`output/hypotheses.md`: the generation text, the review and the final form.)
5. **What this changes for INV-18 and what it does not.** Ladder step 2b now has a defined Kir2-directed step (H1) beside the K2P/high-Ba2+ step, and a list of exclusions that must come first; it does not make the Ba2+ share an isoform, does not locate the deficit, and does not say that raising or lowering Kir2 would help.
