## 2 · Item 1 — compound rows: verdicts

* ML133 (C01), correct on every printed number: Kir2.1 1.9 uM at pH 7.4 (abstract prints 1.8), 290 nM at pH 8.5, 10.0 uM at pH 6.5 (Hill 1.7 / 4.0 / 1.8); Kir2.2 2.9, 2.3 4.0, 2.6 2.8; Kir1.1 >300, Kir4.1 76.0, Kir7.1 32.9, Kir6.2/SUR1 7.7 uM (Table 1, pH 7.4; Kir2.1 mouse, 2.2/2.3/2.6 human, Kir1.1/4.1/6.2 rat, Kir7.1 human). Inside-out 4.3 uM (pH 7.4). No recording temperature printed; no K2P tested. The Ricerca panel is a radioligand binding panel; the hERG/KCNQ step was a Tl+ flux triage. DATO.
* **The PubChem hERG deposit (C02) — what it is and what it means.** DATO: AIDs 504555 and 504693 (the second is a re-deposit of the same dataset; identical EC50 4.98994 uM, SD 1.6154, n=4, Hill 1.03) are CHO hERG population patch-clamp concentration-response assays for the ML133 HCl resynthesis (CID 781301 / SID 85281105). Points (percentage = 100 x (Ipost - Ipre)/Ipre, so negative = inhibition): -18.6 at 1.11 uM, -26.1 at 3.33, -52.2 at 10, -102.6 at 30 uM, +20.5 at 14 nM. So ML133 does block hERG in a functional assay in this deposit, with an IC50 near 5 uM. The pH is NOT established: compound plates are prepared in pH 8.5 buffer, the cells in pH 7.4 solution, the final pH is unstated (producer: "pH 8.5": defective). The deposit also tags these hERG assays with the mouse Kir2.1 target (NP_032451). INFERENZA: the producer's ratio 4.99/0.284 = 17.6 compares a manual Kir2.1 value with an automated hERG value; on the matching automated platform the same deposit gives Kir2.1 IC50 13.63 uM (AID 2581, IonWorks) and 10.617 uM (AID 504557, Tl+ flux): hERG 4.99 uM is about 2.7-fold MORE potent than Kir2.1 there (0.37). The probe-report abstract says "modest selectivity versus hERG", which fits. There is no like-for-like Kir2-over-hERG window in the records; at pH 7.4 hERG (about 5 uM, pH unknown) lies within about 1.3-2.6-fold of Kir2.1-2.3.
* **Is ML133 a defensible family-level blocker probe? Yes for the question it was built for and no more.** It separates the Kir2 family (1.9-4.0 uM) from Kir1.1 (>158-fold), Kir4.1 (40-fold) and Kir7.1 (17-fold) in recombinant cells at pH 7.4, with a mechanism (pore, D172/I176, knock-off by K+). It does not separate Kir2 from Kir6.2/SUR1 (4-fold), hERG (about 1-3-fold or worse, by deposit), K2P (untested), nor Kir2.1 from 2.2/2.3/2.6 (all within 2-fold; Kir2.4 never tested). It is 99 percent protein bound with high clearance and the authors limit it to in vitro use. The producer's label "probe at family level only" is right; its two supporting sentences are not: the hERG window (see above) and the pH control (item 2).
* VU573 (C06-C08) correct on all values. Chloroquine 8.7 uM (C09) correct but it is the potency for the peak OUTWARD current under an action-potential command in HEK293 (bath pH 7.4); the same paper gives 1.1 uM inside-out for WT and no counter-screen. Flecainide (C10): numbers correct (0.8 uM, +22 percent at -120 mV; 0.4 uM, +53.9 percent at -50 mV; 1 uM +16.5/+45.8 percent), but the isoform restriction is understated: **Kir2.1+2.2 and Kir2.1+2.3 heteromers are also not increased**, A312C Kir2.2 and A303C Kir2.3 do respond, native guinea-pig ventricular IK1 rises (+19.5 percent at -120, +38.0 percent at -40 mV), human atrial IK1 does not, open probability rises (a PIP2-like action) and 24 h incubation raises channel density. Zacopride (C11): numbers correct; off-targets "not reported" is wrong (the paper names 5-HT3 antagonism / 5-HT4 agonism; the earlier paper claims no effect on other channels); the CHO (100 uM) versus myocyte (1 uM) 100-fold gap is unremarked. Gambogic acid, PA-6, VU0134992: abstract values correct; PA-6's hERG-maturation and expression effects at 10 uM are omitted. Dronedarone (C15): the ChEMBL mechanism row cites two papers; 25182566 (abstract) shows Kir2.1-only block (not Kir2.2/2.3) via E224, a Kir2.1-selective blocker the producer scored "not interpretable".
* VU6080824 (C05), 0.35 uM and the claim of no counter-screen in the main text: **unverifiable** (blocked, not retried).
