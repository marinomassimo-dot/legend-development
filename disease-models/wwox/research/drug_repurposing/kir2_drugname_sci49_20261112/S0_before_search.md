## What counts as a useful result
For a compound: identity verified (structure, name, database id); family and isoform selectivity from a **primary functional assay with a counter-screen** (patch clamp or a flux assay against other Kir/hERG); direction (block or activation) with potency and conditions (pH, PIP2, Mg2+, polyamines, temperature); native-neuron evidence if any; off-targets; human-use status (approved drug versus experimental probe) kept apart. **Roles:** *probe* (selective enough, direction documented, usable to attribute a conductance); *comparator of mechanism*; *conditional hypothesis* (needs the premise on direction); *not interpretable*. A useful finding for the decision is a **documented gap** as much as a reagent: "no selective Kir2 blocker with a counter-screen" or "no activator with a documented mechanism" is a result, with the perimeter stated, and is not a universal statement of absence.

## Priors (recollection, unverified; scored afterwards, may fail)
* **K1.** ML133 is the best-documented small-molecule Kir2.1 inhibitor from a probe project (low micromolar at pH 7.4, strongly pH-dependent), with selectivity over Kir1.1 and weaker on Kir4.1 and Kir7.1, but **little discrimination among Kir2.1–2.4** (so it separates "Kir2 family" from other Kir, not isoforms).
* **K2.** No Kir2-selective **activator** with a documented direct mechanism exists; flecainide (Kir2.1 current increase in heterologous cells via a pore cysteine) and zacopride (IK1 increase in cardiomyocytes) are reported as enhancers in cardiac contexts, with the mechanism or selectivity contested.
* **K3.** The approved or clinical drugs reported to block Kir2.x (chloroquine, quinacrine, tamoxifen, carvedilol, dronedarone, pentamidine) do so at micromolar concentrations with broader actions; none is selective enough to attribute a conductance.
* **K4.** No source tests a Kir2 modulator in layer II/III pyramidal neurons at P13–P17 or in a Wwox slice.
* **K5.** The compound-name route returns more than the one catalogue row (dronedarone): the SCI43 negative was an artefact of the by-symbol route.
* **K6.** A juvenile (about P13–P17) cortical Ba2+-sensitive share exists in at least one source, but not for the L2/3 pyramidal cell with K-gluconate at 34 °C.
* **K7.** Kir4.1 (astrocytic) and Kir2 contributions are separable by compound only partly; a Ba2+ share in a slice is not neuron-only.
