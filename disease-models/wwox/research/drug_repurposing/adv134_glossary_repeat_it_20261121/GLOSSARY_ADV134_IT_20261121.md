# ADV-134 glossary of terms of art (IT, 2026-11-21), fixed before the repeat batch is translated

Limit stated first: this glossary was produced by a model delegate (sonnet, effort high, stated in the dispatch) of the same family as every reader in the trial; the fr/de/es renderings are proposals no native specialist has checked. The senses are read from defining or first-use passages in the programme, not from landed renderings. `[D]` = stated in a defining sentence or definition table; `[U]` = derived from use, no defining sentence (weaker rows). (S) = standard term in that language, (C) = coinage or a standard adjective in a non-standard compound. RDR = `disease-models/wwox/research/drug_repurposing/`; paths are relative to RDR unless they start with `framework/`. No English string is edited by this work; the last column is a PROPOSAL to HE, who owns the strings and the renderer.

Producer's decision note on 'lead' (rows 3a to 3c): in the programme 'lead' never means a drug-discovery lead, a rank or a clue; do not use 'pista', 'piste' or 'Leitsubstanz'. 'lead position' is the governing position (3a); the 'lead' of the lithium and d-APV passages is a reference intervention with a WWOX-system result (3b). Note for the string owners: 'pista' is already the programme word in landed Italian-origin passages (ADV-129); the choice is theirs.
