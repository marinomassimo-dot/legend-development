## 4. Hypotheses H1-H6 and experiments XRX0-XRX4

Hypotheses
- H1: supports are transcripts; ROS in fly and late MEF are not NRF2 data. "Supports" is generous for those two systems; "status open; transcript only" is the right label. The W02 progenitor-arm NFE2L2 fall should not be counted (WWOX gate failed). Not treated as exclusive.
- H2: supports and refutes consistent with E12, E14, E16; a mixed signature is not read as compensation. Add "transcript only" to the status, as in H1.
- H3: E07-E09 correctly carried as another system and an oxidative-death endpoint; not presented as a WWOX result. Weakness: "P47T target score tracks astrocyte marker score" supports composition, not astrocyte-mediated benefit. Omitted point: IT11 notes organoid GFAP transcript 0.88x while astrocytes are reported increased (transcript/composition discordance). Also the neuron-restricted deletion keeps astrocytes wild type with the phenotype present, so endogenous astrocytic NRF2 does not prevent the phenotype; whether pharmacological raising of it would is untested (consistent with "plausible, untested").
- H4: acceptable; stage-resolved NRF2 datum absent (confirmed).
- H5: refuting item (E08, tBHQ, another system) is correctly labelled. XRX2 text lists "HCAR2 antagonist" as a residual-effect explanation; MMF's HCAR2 rationale is as an agonist, so the wording is ambiguous.
- H6: "what_supports: no WWOX-system datum" lists an absence as support; absence keeps H6 open, it does not support it. The omaveloxolone window used as support is tumour-cell and fibroblast growth/NF-kB data (E20).

Experiments
- XRX0: sound (isogenic, both clones, independent differentiations, composition counted on the same wells, transcripts never stand for activity). The "fixed acute oxidative challenge" is not named; its identity determines whether NRF2 or another stress route is probed. No GSK-3beta readout (pSer9 / activity), although a Wwox-null brain datum exists (section 6). No positive-control NRF2 inducer to size reserve (XRX1 covers part of it).
- XRX1: states the two guards (NAC not proof of independence; two electrophiles are not two mechanisms; MMF separate). Gaps: composition counts after treatment are not listed among XRX1 readouts (astrocyte proliferation or loss would change network activity without a neuronal effect); KEAP1 knockdown is not NRF2-pure (KEAP1 has other substrates, e.g. IKK-beta and BCL2 are mentioned in the Probst discussion), so it also needs the NFE2L2-loss cross; make that explicit.
- XRX2: correct core (NFE2L2 loss plus re-expression rescue, residual effect listed with candidates). Knockdown is incomplete, so a residual effect is ambiguous without the rescue arm, which is present. The compartment of NFE2L2 loss (neuron, astrocyte, both) is not specified here; E09 says it matters.
- XRX3: separates astrocyte NFE2L2 intact from null with WWOX-null neurons, good. Does not include the reciprocal arm needed to call cell-autonomous action: NFE2L2-null neurons with intact astrocytes. Add.
- XRX4: in-silico, panel fixed beforehand from a cited source, ranges not significance: appropriate. The P47T deposit has chronic seizures and composition shifts; a target score tracking astrocyte markers there cannot be read as an NRF2 result.
- None of the experiments counts NAC as proof of independence; none counts two electrophiles as two mechanisms.

**H4 (WWOX and NRF2 absence).** My searches, 2026-11-07:
- PubMed tool, `WWOX AND (NRF2 OR NFE2L2 OR "Nrf2")`: 1 hit (39894307). `WWOX AND NRF2`: 1. `WWOX AND NFE2L2`: 1. `"WW domain-containing oxidoreductase" AND (Nrf2 OR "NF-E2-related factor 2" OR "nuclear factor erythroid 2-related factor 2")`: 1 (same record). `WWOX AND (antioxidant OR "oxidative stress") AND (neuron OR neural OR brain OR astrocyte)`: 4 (35984507, 34214506, 23277037, 22202011); none shows NRF2 in the title or abstract.
- What the PubMed tool does with OR and parentheses: it accepts them, preserves the grouping, and rewrites every term by automatic term mapping into `[All Fields]` / `[MeSH Terms]` / `[Supplementary Concept]` expansions (shown in `query_translation`); WWOX maps to the supplementary concept "wwox protein human" plus all-fields; "neural" expands to neuralization, neuralize and so on, so OR groups of common words widen noisily. It returns `has_more: false` and total_count; the count is on the translated query, so quote the translation, not the typed string.
- Europe PMC REST: `ABSTRACT:"WWOX" AND (ABSTRACT:"NRF2" OR "NFE2L2" OR "Nrf2")`: 1 (39894307); title-field query: 1; with neuronal/brain/epilepsy terms: 0. I opened three that surfaced: 42216494 (toosendanin, cerebral ischaemia: cites 39894307 only), 41228229 (WWOX cancer review: repeats the 39894307 result and lists "KEAP1/NRF2" among WWOX pathways on that basis), 42939870 (CDDO-EA seizures: no paragraph with WWOX and NRF2 together). Reviews that cite 39894307 are not independent evidence.
- Europe PMC abstract-field `WWOX` with redox terms: 15 records (cancer, lung injury, fly, bladder, MEF 37897534, neuronal differentiation 31543760, Zfra1-31 35984507); none with an NRF2 protein or activity measure in WWOX-deficient neural cells at abstract level.
- Conclusion: the producer's claim ("no NRF2 protein, nuclear or activity datum in a WWOX-deficient neural system in any source read") is consistent with what I found at title/abstract depth; the 148 full-text hits were not read, and 35984507 full text was not reachable (not open access).
- 39894307 abstract confirmed: HCC cells, TSN, "impeded the nuclear translocation of NRF2 by inducing its ubiquitination degradation", ML385 and MIRA-1 and Fer-1 co-treatment in vitro and in xenograft; ferroptosis endpoint; non-neural; WWOX activation by a drug, not loss.
