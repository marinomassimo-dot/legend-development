# Memantine, glial GDNF supply and cortical interneuron development under WWOX loss

**Decision, 23 November 2026.** Deprioritize generalized GDNF replacement as a WWOX-specific rationale for memantine. One lower-concentration drug exposure gave a small, measurable change inside vehicle variation. In four mouse comparisons and one human organoid comparison, control-tissue ligand RNA was below the predefined abundance floor. Human organoids showed a strong increase in the GDNF receptor GFRA1, partly reproducible by changing regional composition. That makes the spatial distribution of responsive cells a more informative question than the bulk receptor average. The datasets contain no direct test of memantine in WWOX-deficient tissue. [Initial prediction](prediction.md) · [Drug result](results/drug.tsv) · [Disease outcomes](results/disease_summary.tsv)

## The proposed connection and its prior evidence

Rat astroglial cultures exposed to 10 µM memantine had an early increase in GDNF RNA, peaking at around 1 h and returning to control by 24 h. Protein increased at 6–12 h. Dialyzed medium conditioned by treated astroglia supported dopaminergic neurons. GDNF neutralization in mixed cultures reduced the drug-associated dopamine uptake response. These experiments establish a context for a glial action. They do not measure cortical interneuron migration. Separate mouse experiments showed that GDNF–GFRα1 signaling promotes the differentiation and tangential migration of developing cortical GABAergic neurons, including effects independent of RET and NCAM. **Our proposed bridge** was that memantine might help a WWOX-deficient developing network by increasing trophic supply to migrating interneurons, even if a sustained NMDA stress signature is absent. [Wu et al., Figures 2–4](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19536110/) · [Pozas and Ibáñez](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15748846/) · [Earlier NMDA test](../memantine_20261123/README.md)

## Direct drug exposure and the human cell reference

Mouse cortical cultures received 0.7 µM memantine for 8 h in GSE110256. Gdnf gave 21 reads, against 8–25 in the nine vehicles. This is 0.690 CPM versus median 0.498 (range 0.243–0.796), an increase of +0.174 on the log2(1+CPM) scale. It ranked 13/218 drugs and 5/31 in its identifier block. It failed both the baseline 1 CPM criterion and the requirement to exceed all vehicles. Astrocyte-associated Glul and Slc1a3 were abundant in the preparation. Tissue-level dilution, a transient response before 8 h and the approximately fourteen-fold nominal concentration difference from the prior astroglial experiment remain plausible. The single treated library does not estimate inter-culture variability. NGF, a named secondary comparator, exceeded the nine normalized controls by a small margin (6.997 versus maximum 6.888 CPM) and ranked 11/218. It does not validate the GDNF hypothesis. [Hadwen et al.](https://doi.org/10.1093/hmg/ddy221) · [Drug-specific sample and exposure audit](../memantine_cortex_20261123/README.md) · [All measured genes](results/drug.tsv)

In GSE73721 the six purified human fetal cortical astrocyte samples had median GDNF 0.1 FPKM (range 0.1–0.6). All 12 postnatal samples labeled cortical astrocytes showed 0.1, the bottom reported value in this comparison. The astrocyte markers SLC1A3 and GLUL were measurable in every sample in both groups. None of the included other cortical reference categories showed a GDNF measurement at or above 1 FPKM. These are baseline RNA measurements in selected samples, including surgical material. Human GDNF secretion after stimulation remains unmeasured. ## Disease-side challenge and a spatial alternative

| Untreated tissue | GDNF result | GFRA1 result, KO minus control |
|---|---|---|
| Human organoids, four KO and two controls | 0.080 to 0.174 mean CPM, both below floor | +2.17 log2 units, +1.40 to +2.94 across library omissions |
| P17 mouse cortex, neuronal deletion, three KO and four controls | 0.399 to 0.224 mean TPM, direction stable, both below floor | −0.81 log2 units, −0.99 to −0.57 on omission |
| P17 mouse hippocampus, global, neuronal and progenitor deletions | Below baseline floor in each comparison | Global −0.39, neuronal −0.21, progenitor +0.09 |

GFRA1 remained measurable in all KO group means. Normalizing the human ligand change to three astrocyte markers gave +0.045 and crossed zero on omission. In mouse neuronal-deletion cortex the corresponding reduction persisted but Gdnf was too sparse to meet the primary criterion. The separately labeled hippocampal groups come from the same deposited study. Library omissions describe within-study sensitivity and do not estimate biological uncertainty across independent human genotypes. [Human organoids](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE156243) · [Mouse hippocampus](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158681) · [Mouse cortex](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE158682) · [Gene-level estimates](results/disease.tsv)

As an exploratory challenge to the large human GFRA1 increase, we reused previously fitted mixture weights from independent day-105 pallial and subpallial spheroids. These weights were learned on interneuron markers without using GFRA1. The 30% pooled subpallial contribution predicts GFRA1 +0.98 (observed +2.17) and RET +1.06 (observed +0.89). Equal-cell weighting predicts GFRA1 +0.62. Even a total regional substitution gives only +2.09 or +1.87 in these profiles. Regional composition can account for part of the direction; the residual cannot be assigned uniquely to induction. Among 1,096 subpallial cells with detectable GAD1 or GAD2 and at least 500 molecules, 54 had detectable GFRA1 and four had GDNF. Sparse detection and pooled cells from a different preparation prevent a prevalence estimate for WWOX. [Mixture training](../ganaxolone_developmental_history_20261123/README.md) · [Independent GSE93811 deposit](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE93811) · [Regional comparison](results/region.tsv) · [Cell-level detection](results/region_detection.tsv)

**What would change the decision.** A local supply defect predicts that matched WWOX-null recipient interneurons retain a migration response to GDNF while their untreated environment supplies too little available ligand. A recipient defect predicts persistent impairment at matched ligand and opposing effects when the genotype of the ligand source and recipient are exchanged. A memantine-specific claim also needs increased extracellular GDNF following exposure and loss of its migration effect upon GDNF neutralization, with neuronal survival and overall firing assessed separately. Bulk RNA, high receptor expression and a change in dopamine uptake cannot distinguish these possibilities.

The [decision rules](prediction.md) were committed before target-specific analysis. Source review used selected experimental results, figure descriptions, methods and public numerical records. Data tables and source artwork from third parties are not redistributed. The two programs produce original derived summaries.
