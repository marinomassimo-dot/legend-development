# Memantine, GDNF supply and interneuron development

SCI_CODEX, 23 Nov 2026. Locked before reading target-gene values. Prior observations: memantine 10 µM raised astroglial GDNF mRNA transiently at 0.5–1 h and protein at 6–12 h in rat cultures. Conditioned medium had a GDNF-dependent trophic effect on dopaminergic preparations. Independent loss-of-function work in mice implicates GDNF/GFRα1 in the migration and maturation of cortical interneurons. Our proposed intersection is a limiting supply of trophic ligand to responsive cells during development in WWOX loss. The source experiments did not test this intersection.

1. Can direct drug exposure increase Gdnf at 0.7 µM in mixed mouse cortex? In GSE110256 demand median vehicle CPM >=1, drug value above all nine controls and in the top tenth of the 218 drugs. Report matched block rank, raw counts and all raw vehicle ranges. Show astrocyte (ALDH1L1, GLUL, SLC1A3) and neuronal (MAP2, SNAP25) markers, alongside BDNF, NTF3 and NGF as selectivity comparators. One 8h library cannot exclude a transient 1h response or a response specific to activated astroglia.
2. Before assigning a cortical cellular source, examine GDNF and GFRA1 in the GSE73721 annotated human fetal astrocyte samples, postnatal cortical astrocytes and other human cell groups. Exclude tumor, cultured, epilepsy and hippocampus from the main reference as labeled; keep other groups separate and show the detection floor. RNA gives neither secretion nor ligand responsiveness.
3. In the GSE156243 human organoids and separately the global hippocampal and neuronal-deletion hippocampal and cortical arms of GSE158681/2, score KO-WT for GDNF and GFRA1. Human threshold mean WT CPM >=1, mouse >=1 summed transcript TPM. Count balanced evidence for limiting ligand only with a reliably measured reduction stable on every library omission, with GFRA1 still measurable in cases; require the human result and a mouse cortex or hippocampal replication. Repeat GDNF relative to the mean of at least two detectable astrocyte markers as a change in relative RNA abundance. This reference adjustment is not cell deconvolution. An opposite or measurement-limited result lowers priority for generalized ligand replacement. It cannot exclude a local change in extracellular GDNF, migration, or a separately acting glial mechanism.
