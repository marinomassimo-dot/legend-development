# Answers, Q1 package (DATO unless marked; no recommendation, no ranking, not medical advice)

**Q1 ONO-TR-772 (PMID 40365404, D01-D06).** Compound 14c, a Boc-anilide of a 5-(4-fluorophenylethynyl)-3-methylpyridin-2-amine; structure checked (SI S28-S29). It is a TREK-1/TREK-2 dual inhibitor (TREK-2 QPatch 0.17 uM against TREK-1 0.15 uM), not TREK-1 selective. TREK-1 potency is reported four ways: Tl+ flux 0.26 uM (human, CHO), QPatch 0.15 uM, human manual patch clamp 15 nM and mouse 67 nM in the text. The paper's ">67-fold" over TASK-3, TRESK, TWIK-2, TRAAK, TASK-1, TASK-2 equals 10 uM / 0.15 uM, so the 15 nM headline (and the catalogue's pIC50 7.82 labelled Rat) looks like a unit slip; the SI cannot settle it (INFERENZA). Selectivity measured: TASK-3, TRESK, TWIK-2 above 10 uM; at one 10 uM manual patch clamp point TASK-1 17, TASK-2 7 percent, TRAAK activated 41 percent; at 10 uM Cav1.2, HCN4, Kir2.1, Kv1.5, Kv4.3, Nav1.5 below 3 percent, Kir2.2 52 (transient), KCNQ1/E1 47, hERG 38 percent. Binding off-targets (A3, L-type Ca, Na site 2) have Ki 0.48-2.6 uM. Not tested: concentration-response for TASK-1/3, neuronal Kv/Nav/Cav subtypes, heteromers. pH: no series; pH, temperature and activation of the patch clamp are not in the paper or its SI; the follow-up SI gives the group's method (CHO-K1, bath pH 7.4, no stimulus), which is INFERENZA for this compound. Neurons: none; only a mouse novel-object-recognition effect (MED 10 mg/kg IP) with an estimated unbound brain level (5.3 nM) below the mouse patch clamp IC50 (67 nM).

**Q2.** Cpd8l (40040241): abstract only, IC50 0.81 uM, more than 30-fold over other K/Na/TRP channels, neuroprotection in an OGD/R cortical neuron model that fell with TREK-1 silencing; full text blocked. KU124 (40635742): TASK-1 IC50 1.38 +/- 0.65 uM, four concentrations, no plateau, Tl+ flux only, no selectivity, channel species and pH not stated. AVE1231 (31001961): human TASK-1 IC50 0.2 +/- 0.1 uM, Hill 1.1, oocytes, 20-22 C, pH 7.4 only, no other channel measured. ONO-2920632 (39981749): TREK-2 preferring activator (TREK-2 0.30, TREK-1 2.8 uM, human Tl+); it acts on unstimulated basal flux in heterologous cells; the pH of the assays is not stated and acid-pHo activation was not tested.

**Q3.** Not answered with a stated slope. No reachable full text reports a Hill slope for TREK-1 pHo dependence; the only numeric pH50 is 7.51 (oocytes, 22.5 C, slope not stated; carried). Sandoz 2009 abstract: pK near 7.4, "steep"; Cohen 2008 and the Sandoz full text were blocked; nothing at 30-37 C found. A slope remains a measurement to make (D11).
