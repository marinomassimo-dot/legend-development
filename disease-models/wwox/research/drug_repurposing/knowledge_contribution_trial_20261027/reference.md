Written by scientist-a from primary sources only. Local cards, registry records, ledgers and

## Sources
- LC  = Lucas-Clarke et al. 2026, Brain Commun., PMID 42909904, PMC13684411 (full text read). Drosophila; neuronal/glial Wwox RNAi and CRISPRa, with or without human Abeta42 in neurons.
- CH  = PMID 32000863, PMC6990504 (full text read). Wwox-/- mouse; GSK3beta Ser9 dephosphorylation; LiCl vs PTZ seizures.
- WO  = PMID 36537114 (abstract only). Five WOREE patients; epilepsy responded to ketogenic diet in three.
- SA  = PMID 25792327 Sada 2015 (abstract only). LDH inhibition reduces seizures; stiripentol is an LDH inhibitor.
- SI  = PMID 23741617 Sidrauski 2013 (abstract only). ISRIB reverses eIF2alpha-P; reduces viability of cells under chronic PERK activation.
- GC  = PMID 24146979 (abstract only). GCN2 deletion in 5XFAD aggravates PERK/ATF4/BACE1/Abeta.
- DC  = PMID 16476929 (abstract only). DCA 25 mg/kg/day in MELAS: peripheral neuropathy, trial terminated, no benefit detected.
- G1  = PMID 30237424 (abstract only). Reducing GSK-3 activity (dominant-negative or inhibitors) worsened seizure severity/hippocampal damage in mouse status epilepticus; authors caution against targeting GSK-3 where hyperexcitability underlies disease.
- G2  = PMID 35253420 (abstract only). Neuronal GSK-3beta-/- mice more susceptible to 6 Hz seizures; GSK-3beta OE less susceptible but kindle faster; selective GSK-3beta inhibitor TCS2002 anticonvulsant acutely.
- HG  = HGNC: PDK1 is also an alias of PDPK1 (3-phosphoinositide dependent protein kinase 1), a different gene from pyruvate dehydrogenase kinase 1.

## Facts from LC that frame C1-C4
Neuronal Wwox knockdown with Abeta42: Atf4 protein up (immunocytochemistry, p=0.0022); not seen in LacZ background;
Xbp1 splicing (Ire1) unchanged; sima (HIF1alpha) unchanged; L-lactate and Ldh transcript up only in the
Wwox-KD x Abeta42 interaction (lactate interaction Padj 0.0174, log2FC 1.30; Ldh +1.29 log2FC); neuronal Ldh
reduction rescued the locomotor deficit, partially; lifespan not shown rescued by it in the text read; ATP unchanged;
Wwox overexpression rescued lifespan/locomotion/amyloid WITHOUT lowering Ldh or 2-hydroxyglutarate (dissociation);
phospho-eIF2alpha was not measured; no ISR drug, no DCA, no PDK/PDH measurement, no stiripentol, no diet in LC.
The pyruvate-pathway transcripts reported down were Pepck2, CG11052 and Adh. All of it is fly, an Abeta42 (Alzheimer) model.

## Rubric
C1 (ISR)
- 1.1 Locates the WWOX-ISR evidence in the Drosophila neuronal Wwox-KD plus Abeta42 model (LC), not in WOREE/human data.
- 1.2 Direction: Wwox loss goes with MORE Atf4 (PERK-Atf4 arm; Ire1-Xbp1 not involved) so a dampening of ISR output is the direction implied; and says the ISR drug itself was untested in WWOX models.
- 1.3 Context dependence: Atf4 rise requires Abeta42; absent without it.
- 1.4 Counter-evidence retrieved: ISR is not simply harmful (GC: GCN2 loss aggravates; SI: ISRIB toxic under chronic PERK activation) and/or the LC dissociation (Wwox OE rescue without Ldh change).
- 1.5 States that there is no mammalian or WOREE evidence of ISR activation in the sources used (transfer limit), or marks it unknown.
- 1.6 Next measure is a measurement or a test in a model matching the evidence (eIF2alpha-P/ATF4 in a WWOX-deficient neuronal mammalian model, or ISRIB in the fly interaction model), not a treatment.
C2 (LDH)
- 2.1 Fly-only direct evidence; lactate/Ldh rise only in the Wwox-KD x Abeta42 interaction; genetic Ldh reduction partially rescued locomotion.
- 2.2 Direction = inhibition; identifies stiripentol as an LDH inhibitor with a source (SA) and as clinically used antiepileptic.
- 2.3 Limits: rescue partial, 2-HG only partly Ldh-dependent, Wwox OE rescues without Ldh drop, genetic knockdown is not a drug, and a drug can have other targets.
- 2.4 States no WWOX-deficient mammalian/WOREE evidence of LDH/lactate elevation.
- 2.5 Next measure in a matching model (lactate/Ldh in WWOX-deficient neurons/brain without Abeta; or stiripentol or LDH knockdown in the fly interaction model).
C3 (PDK / DCA)
- 3.1 Says LC did not measure PDH/PDK or test DCA; PDK link is inference from lactate/pyruvate-pathway findings.
- 3.2 Direction: PDK inhibition raises PDH flux and is expected to lower lactate (inference, not WWOX data).
- 3.3 Retrieves DC (neuropathy, trial ended) or equivalent primary DCA toxicity evidence.
- 3.4 Notes lactate rise may be adaptive (ATP unchanged) or that lowering lactate is not shown beneficial except via Ldh reduction.
- 3.5 Identifier hygiene: recognises the PDK1/PDPK1 alias collision, or states that tool outputs for "PDK1" were checked for it.
- 3.6 Next measure is p-PDH/PDK activity/lactate measurement or an explicit abstention on the drug; no recommendation.
C4 (ketogenic diet and lactate)
- 4.1 Cites the human observation WO (KD-responsive epilepsy in 3 of 5 WOREE patients) as uncontrolled, small, abstract-level.
- 4.2 Says no source links the KD response to lactate/LDH in WWOX deficiency; the lactate link is a fly Abeta42 finding; the KD-LDH link is a general epilepsy hypothesis (SA).
- 4.3 Does NOT claim that KD works through the lactate axis in WOREE.
- 4.4 Abstains on the mechanism (or proposes only a measurement) with a stated reason and a reopening condition.
C5 (lithium / GSK3beta)
- 5.1 Direct evidence: Wwox-/- mouse, GSK3beta Ser9 dephosphorylation (active GSK3beta) at P20 in brain regions; LiCl suppressed PTZ-induced seizures; endpoint = acute chemoconvulsant seizure, mouse, not human.
- 5.2 Notes lithium is non-selective and the paper tested a single compound (target specificity and isoform not tested) or equivalent.
- 5.3 Counter-evidence retrieved: reducing GSK-3 activity worsened seizures/damage in other models (G1) and/or neuronal GSK-3beta loss increased susceptibility (G2).
- 5.4 Distinguishes acute inhibition from chronic/genetic loss (G2 contrast) or developmental vs adult timing.
- 5.5 Next measure: isoform-selective/dose-ranged test in the Wwox-/- model with Ser9 readout, or explicit abstention on lithium; no recommendation.
