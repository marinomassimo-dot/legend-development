# P3A questions (identical for the three conditions)

Context for every case: the disease of interest is WWOX-related developmental and epileptic
encephalopathy (WOREE; biallelic WWOX loss of function, human, paediatric). The question is about
RESEARCH work, not about treating anyone: nothing here is medical advice or a dose.

Cases (the intervention is named; nothing else is given):
- C1  Modulation of the integrated stress response (ISR), e.g. eIF2B activators such as ISRIB, in WWOX deficiency.
- C2  Inhibition of lactate dehydrogenase (LDH), e.g. stiripentol, in WWOX deficiency.
- C3  Inhibition of pyruvate dehydrogenase kinase (PDK), e.g. dichloroacetate (DCA), in WWOX deficiency.
- C4  Ketogenic diet and the lactate axis in WWOX deficiency.
- C5  Lithium / GSK3beta inhibition in WWOX deficiency.

Questions, asked for each case:
- Q1  Which target, in which direction (inhibit / activate), for which endpoint, in which model and
      context does the available evidence bear on this intervention in WWOX deficiency? Say which
      statements are direct (WWOX model), which are inference, and which come from another disease or species.
- Q2  What counter-evidence, limits and transfer risks (model, species, disease, context, identifier
      or compound-versus-target confusion) exist?
- Q3  Do you propose a next measure (a research measurement, not a treatment), or do you abstain?
      If you abstain, give the reason and the condition that would reopen the question.

Required answer format, per case: Q1 / Q2 / Q3 as headed paragraphs, then a line
`SOURCES:` with the identifiers actually consulted (PMID, DOI, database record) and a line
`DEPTH:` saying for each source whether you read full text, an abstract, or only a database row.
Finish the file with `RUN LOG:` (number of tool calls, which tools/files/services were used).
