## Projection, remaining risk, recommendation
* Size of the full set (shareable pages, ADV-128 detector, static, on the pre-change pages): fr 735, de 397, es 541 distinct English-authored text nodes; union about 770 distinct passages and 148,600 characters, against 50 passages and 4,732 characters in this trial. By count that is 31 batches of 25; by characters about 31 times this trial, because the remaining passages are longer (mean about 190 characters against 95).
* Cost at the measured rates (an estimate, linear in rows): delegate checks about 31 batches x 40,000 tokens plus about 31 third looks x 30,000, about 2.2 million tokens in about 2 hours of delegate time; producer time about 10 to 15 minutes per batch for the longer passages, so 5 to 8 hours of one window, sequential (one delegate at a time, as the budget rule asks); the sentence-length effect could raise the MINOR and CHANGED rates, which this trial cannot say.
* Remaining risk: (a) the later passages are long evidence sentences with numbers, hedges and limits, the class where a CHANGED row matters most; this trial's 50 were mostly short labels and questions, so 0 CHANGED in 150 rows is an upper-quality estimate; (b) the verifiers are models of the same family as the producer and disagree on borderline wording (two of them took opposite views of 'unrelated'; a verifier's own repair introduced the only CHANGED row), and no human bilingual reader has seen any of it; (c) partial translation inside a section makes the section notice inexact ('not translated' on `ranking` with 31 translated passages) and leaves the same text translated in one section and English in another until a batch covers it (a content-hash catalogue would fix the second by dropping the section filter); (d) terminology must stay consistent across about 770 passages (this trial fixed: salves / Bursts / ráfagas, tranches / Schnitte / cortes, fiche / Karte / ficha).
* Which treatment is proportionate for the shareable edition: the section notice (ADV-130) already states the language of every English-authored section truthfully at nearly no cost, and the trial shows translation is feasible and faithful at a low CHANGED rate. Translating all 770 passages is a day of work with a quality risk that only a human bilingual check would close, for passages that are explicitly team working text; I recommend keeping the notice as the default treatment and translating by section only when a named reader (the operator or a clinician reading in fr, de or es) needs that section, starting with the 22 overview and the ranking passages already done, with the notice wording made partial-aware at the same time (HE's string). This is a recommendation for ADV to weigh; it is not a decision.
