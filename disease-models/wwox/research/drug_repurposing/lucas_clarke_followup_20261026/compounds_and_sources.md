### The PDK1 collision, measured
DGIdb, asked for the symbol PDK1, returned 26 interactions, almost all from PRISM screens or Drug Repurposing Hub with no action type, including BX-795, BX-912, OSU-03012 (AR-12) and celecoxib. These are
PDPK1 (3-phosphoinositide-dependent kinase 1, HGNC:8816, O15530) agents; HGNC lists PDK1 as a historical alias of PDPK1. ChEMBL's curated PDPK1 mechanism rows (AR-12, UCN-01) sit under the PDPK1 target CHEMBL2534. Open Targets lists AR-12 and UCN-01 for PDPK1 and only dichloroacetate for PDK1-4. **A DGIdb result for the symbol PDK1 is not a statement about pyruvate dehydrogenase kinase 1.** For the PDK arm only the PDK2 to PDK4 rows, the ChEMBL target ids and the UniProt keys are used.

### 2.4 Ketogenic and lactate-axis interventions
No new compound-level datum. The ketogenic diet remains `HYP-20260709-01` and map record R-05; ketone bodies enter downstream of PDH and the published Lucas-Clarke article contains no ketone, diet, PDH or pyruvate arm (consequence note in HYP-20260709-01, 2026-10-26). Lactate transport (MCT1, `SLC16A1`): DGIdb lists AZD3965 (inhibitor), valproic acid (ClinPGx, no action type) and sodium butyrate (NCI, no action type); an MCT1 block would trap lactate in the cell, which no source says is wanted.

## 4. What this resolves and what it leaves unresolved
- **Resolved:** the target identities; the compound lists each source actually returns; that DGIdb and Open Targets do not carry the ISR/eIF2B or the potent LDH tool compounds; the PDK1/PDPK1 collision with its numbers.
- **Not resolved:** potency of any compound at its target in brain tissue at a paediatric exposure; selectivity of stiripentol for LDH over its other targets; whether any of these compounds moves a WWOX-linked readout in any WWOX-deficient human system (none was tested, by anyone, in the sources searched).
