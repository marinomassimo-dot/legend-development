## Outcome
**Not publicly retrievable today by any route I tried.** The simplest reading of the 404 on a folder whose neighbours exist is that the study is not yet released (in curation or embargoed) — **an inference from one listing, not a statement from MetaboLights**. The 401 on the web service is consistent with that but does not prove it: I did not test the same endpoint on a study known to be public, so I cannot say whether it answers 401 to every anonymous caller. **It does not prove that no public route exists**: a release after today, a reviewer-token link in the journal's supplementary material, or a request to the authors would each be a different route, and none was tried (no e-mail was sent, by rule).
