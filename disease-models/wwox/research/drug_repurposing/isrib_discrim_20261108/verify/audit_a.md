## What the items miss that changes their meaning

M1. The authors state "no differences" (decision-letter text): PS1, EN02 and the "direction is not reduced only" limit are too weak on this one point, but this raises the stakes of the antibody question: a band that does not move with Tm, Tm+ISRIB or ISRIB alone gives no positive control for the antibody's responsiveness to any perturbation; it cannot show ISRIB would have been detected. The p-S51 eIF2alpha row moves modestly with Tm, but that controls the stress, not the antibody.

M2. The human protein in HEK293T has Asp at 539 (U1). Any phospho-S539 antibody reading human endogenous eIF2Bepsilon must read S540 or S544 (or cross-react); in rat numbering the antibody name matches the priming serine, and rat Ser539 is itself "highly phosphorylated in vivo" (Woods 2001), so a constitutive signal in a rat-numbered site is expected. The package states the gap; the added fact is that the Discussion/reply attribute "S539" to the GSK site (inhibitory, GSK-phosphorylated), which in rat numbering is Ser535: the paper's own gloss conflicts with the numbering it uses.

M3. PS6's sentence derives from Cagnetta 2019 (PMC6375727), which uses a customised Proteintech "anti-p-eIF2Bε (Ser539)" in Xenopus retinal growth cones (~35% decrease with Sema3A, not with Tg) and cites Welsh 1998 for the conserved serine: a first-hand neuronal-type measurement that uses the S539 label with an undefined epitope. It is the same label-vs-residue problem in a neuronal system, and the cited reference list contains Quevedo 2003 (rat cortical neurons, PP1 dephosphorylation).

M4. Numbering drift also sits in the GSK3-site axis rows: 26974342 uses "Ser535" and "S535A" in adult mouse retina (mouse residue 535 is Asp; GSK serine 536, priming 540) with a p-eIF2Bepsilon antibody from EMD Millipore without a stated epitope. E15/E16/E17 and G02's "phospho-resistant epsilon" control rest on that numbering.

M5. Human Ser535 (S535-E-E-P-D) and Ser532 (annotated phospho in UniProt) are serines of their own; a reader applying rat "Ser535" literally to Q13144 lands on neither the GSK3 nor the priming serine.

M6. EI24's source_id is wrong (see Part 3); EI15/E21 duplicate; E01, E13, E14, E18, E19, E20, E22 are used in Part 4 without a row, so Part 4 cannot be audited on those.

M7. E03: the lithium result is a significant reduction in all three genotypes and is "abolish" only in the abstract text; the figure shows reduction. The item's "equally" is not what the plot shows.

M8. In 32457286 "GSK" means GSK2606414 (PERK inhibitor), not GSK3: in any text-matching over the package, hits for "GSK" there are not GSK3 evidence.
