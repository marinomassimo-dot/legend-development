## Answer first

* NON RISOLTO (site): `site_identification: undetermined`. The 2013 ISRIB paper measured one phospho-eIF2Bepsilon band in HEK293T cells with an Abcam "phospho-S539" antibody and says ISRIB "did not reduce" it. Which serine that antibody reads is not settled. Missing: the Abcam catalogue number and its immunogen sequence (the paper gives neither), and any phosphatase or point-mutant validation.
* DATO: human eIF2Bepsilon residue 539 is Asp (UniProt Q13144, positions 531 to 548 `QSMDSEEPDSRGGSPQMD`), so "S539" is not a human serine. The GSK3 serine is human Ser540 (rabbit 540, rat 535, mouse 536); the priming serine is human Ser544 (rat 539, mouse 540); see `../../gsk3_eif2b_20261107/output/residues.tsv`. In rat numbering Ser539 is the DYRK priming serine and Ser535 the GSK3 serine (abstracts of 11311121 and 11500362). The 2012 TLR paper the 2013 text cites names the site "Ser GSK" and, in the text retrieved, gives no number. The 2013 reply letter glosses S539 as "constitutively phosphorylated by GSK". INFERENZA: the numeral matches a serine only at the rat or hamster priming position, so the antibody may read the priming serine; this is not proven, and I did not choose the convenient numbering.
* DATO (image, one panel viewed at 447 x 228 pixels): the phospho-S539 and total epsilon rows exist only in the tunicamycin panel; the thapsigargin panel carries PERK, XBP1s, ATF4 and total eIF2alpha only, although the caption names thapsigargin. In the tunicamycin panel the phospho band is present in every lane and I could not see a change at this resolution. No quantification, no replicate number, no antibody validation lane. The result is "not reduced", on one stressor, one blot.
* DATO/INFERENZA (levels): results fall in four levels (`evidence_levels.tsv`, 35 rows): binding (cryo-EM, beta-delta interface; epsilon catalytic domain unresolved), biochemical effect (exchange, assembly; sources disagree on the basal effect), translation in the preparation (pharmacodynamic), function in a pathological system (survival; one gain, one null; lithium in the Wwox null is not WWOX-specific). Binding to the complex does not show action at the GSK3 serine, and a translation gain does not identify the step.
* INFERENZA (grid corrected, `grid.tsv`): the earlier grid over-stated that "ISRIB gives little" is the one outcome ISRIB discriminates cleanly. With phospho-eIF2alpha not raised, little ISRIB response is expected whatever the cause (E21, E22, EI15, EI28), so it neither supports the GSK3 chain nor refutes it; a missing response without an engagement control or effective exposure is uninformative (G07). It also over-stated "translation not lowered: the premise fails" (G04: stage and readout). GSK3-inhibitor rescue can run through TSC2 and mTOR (EN12, commentary abstract) and through compound effects on protein synthesis (E07); two chemotypes help but do not remove these. A pattern compatible with GSK3 to epsilon is not proof of that chain (G02).
* IPOTESI (first fork, `design.tsv`): step 0 qualifies readouts and engagement in one plate; step 1 asks only whether phospho-eIF2alpha is raised and ISRIB-reversible in deficient against corrected cells. Raised and reversible: ISR route, GSK3 branch deprioritised. Not raised with translation lowered: step 2 (two GSK3 chemotypes, GSK3B against GSK3A) then step 3 (phospho-resistant and catalytically dead epsilon, the Ser-to-Asp form only as a separate question, not as a stand-in for phosphorylation). Function and viability only after a translation difference exists.
* Consequence for ISRIB as a probe: keep it a probe; every arm needs phospho-eIF2alpha, an engagement control in the same plate (sedimentation shift, not ATF4 in neurons) and translation labelling (`cards.tsv` CARD-A1, `new_qualification`). A GSK3-comparator card abstains (CARD-A2): lithium is the only qualified comparator; a second chemotype has no card or documented epsilon-site action in the module.
* What remains unknown in WWOX: whether GSK3 acts on eIF2Bepsilon, whether translation or eIF2B activity is altered, and any ISRIB test. GSK3 observations in WWOX do exist and must not be called absent: phospho-Ser9 of GSK3beta lower in null brain at postnatal day 20 (one lane per genotype, E01, E02), lithium lowering a seizure score equally in controls (E03), GSK3beta phosphorylation raised after WOX1 knock-down in a neuroblastoma line (direction unread, E04). The gap is the chain and its experiment.
