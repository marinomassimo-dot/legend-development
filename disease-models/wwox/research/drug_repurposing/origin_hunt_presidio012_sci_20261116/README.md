## Answer first
1. **For the three threads that break the tie, no independent origin exists in the literature I could reach.** T1 (NMDAR-dependent bursts), T2 (reduced spontaneous inhibition) and T13 (intrinsic excitability) are all slice or cell electrophysiology, and every WWOX-deficient neuronal electrophysiology paper found by two routes is from one network: Breton 2021 (Toronto, last author Carlen, with Aqeilan), Repudi 2021 (Aqeilan; authors include Breton and Carlen), the Steinberg organoid work and the gene-therapy papers (Aqeilan). Same mouse line, shared authors; a different readout or preparation in that network (human organoids, EEG/ECoG) is not an independent laboratory. DATO for the author lists (Europe PMC `authorString`, `access.tsv` R2).
2. **Independent laboratories exist, but for other things.** (a) A seizure phenotype with EEG, in a different laboratory and allele (C4: Aldaz with Noebels, P47T hypomorph, video-EEG n 3 v 3, spontaneous generalized tonic-clonic seizures, interictal spikes; Q1-Q4). (b) A seizure phenotype in a different species and laboratory (C6, C7: Suzuki's rat lde/lde, audiogenic seizures, EEG). (c) An independent anatomical signal on the inhibition axis (C1: Aldaz 2019, PV+ and NPY+ interneuron numbers reduced in the hippocampus of 2-week-old full-knockout mice, n 7 v 7; Q7, Q8). (d) Independent transcript-level changes on the NMDAR and Kv axes (C2, C3: hippocampal RNA, n 4 v 4; Q9-Q11). None of these measures NMDAR dependence of bursts, spontaneous inhibitory events, or intrinsic properties.
3. **Reading can break the tie only through C1, and weakly.** In a scratch run of the official generator on the frozen cells plus the candidate cells (`output/sim_*`): if C1's sign is accepted as `match` for GABA-A PAM, benzodiazepines (3) and GABA-AT/GAT (2) at tier 2, the seizure P13-P15 top set shrinks from 5 to 2 of 15 (GABA-A PAM and benzodiazepines; informativeness 0.867); if C1's sign stays `unknown`, nothing changes (top set still 5). So the tie is broken by one independent origin on the inhibition axis, whose sign is an inference (fewer PV interneurons read as less inhibition), in a region (hippocampus) where Breton 2021 saw no bursts, with counts and not firing, and against the organoid GAD1/GAD2 markers that go up (T14). INFERENZA. The L7 head-to-head remains the only discriminating test.
4. **What this says about the origin structure.** The two Breton-axis claims that the matrix leans on (NMDAR dependence, reduced inhibition) are single-network; what is replicated by independent laboratories is the existence of a seizure phenotype, and a lower PV+ interneuron number. A replication of the burst slice or of a spontaneous inhibitory-event measurement in a second laboratory would be new evidence.
