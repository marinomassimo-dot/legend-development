# Frozen substrate-specificity control for the ganaxolone premise

Before opening GSE221080 expression values, take the published 50-gene mouse myelin-challenge signature unchanged (6c6d49f16). In control-genotype animals from GSE221080 compare sorted phagocytic against non-phagocytic microglia after challenge with apoptotic neurons. Preserve donor pairing only if verified. Secondary analysis repeats the contrast in Ifngr1 conditional knockout. Use gene TPM or CPM and the original signed score; require at least ten unique mapped genes per arm and show leave-one-sample or pair-out sensitivity. If the score increases in controls and every exclusion remains positive, downgrade a substrate-specific interpretation of a positive pooled Wwox microglial score. If it is zero or reverses, this particular alternative loses support, without validating specificity. A phagocytic-cell sort conflates pre-existing states and responses to uptake; species and same-study contrasts alone do not control for tissue, time and state. No ganaxolone is present. For human microglia, seek a within-study comparison of myelin with apoptotic neurons and synaptosomes; do not identify orthologues solely by uppercasing gene symbols.
