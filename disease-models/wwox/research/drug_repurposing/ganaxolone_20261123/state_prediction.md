# Does normal developmental microglial state account for the pooled myelin-response signal?

SCI_CODEX, 23 November 2026. Targeted sensitivity to a positive pooled result. The APOE3-derived myelin response and the 167 control/171 KO microglia assignment are already known. Freeze the following comparison before measuring state frequencies or stratified changes. Li et al. identify a developmental, phagocytic microglial state with a 12-gene set (Spp1 Gpnmb Igf1 Clec7a Lpl Cd9 Cd63 Lgals3 Fabp5 Itgax Apoe Tyrobp; PMC6336504, Fig. 5C). Remove any genes overlapping the trained outcome or original identity markers from the state definition. With the original identity rule and QC fixed, classify each nucleus by detecting at least two distinct remaining state genes, versus fewer. These are marker-detection strata, not established cell identities. As a sensitivity, repeat at thresholds of one and three markers.

For each pool construct pseudobulk expression for each stratum. Calculate the original two-arm signature in the original pooled counts. Then standardize the two pools to the same RNA weights (mean of the two pools' stratum RNA fractions), mixing on the linear scale before log scoring. Reproduce the original pooled delta within numerical tolerance. Require >=20 nuclei and >=8,000 UMIs per pool/stratum for the primary attribution. A decrease of at least half the original delta after standardization favors composition as a material contributor. Retention of at least 75% favors a residual beyond this measured state split. Intermediate or low-support outcomes remain unresolved. Record arm-level expression and number of panel genes with >=5 molecules in both pools. No cell-level P value or animal-level inference.

Repeat after stratifying by pooled library-size tertile as well as state, reporting common support, and after thinning each assigned nucleus to exactly 500 UMIs to equalize marker detectability (same identities, 100 multivariate hypergeometric draws, report distribution). If state or coverage collapses on thinning, explicitly qualify the attribution. This test can change whether to prioritize a within-cell myelin-response assay; it cannot measure ganaxolone exposure, myelin uptake or drug effect.
