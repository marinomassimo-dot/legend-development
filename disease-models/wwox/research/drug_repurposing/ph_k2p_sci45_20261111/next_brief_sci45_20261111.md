## The question and why
**By how much do the proton sensitivities of NMDA receptors (GluN1/GluN2A, GluN1/GluN2B and triheteromers) and of ASIC1a change the current of a juvenile (P10-P25) neocortical pyramidal neuron for a fall of extracellular pH from 7.4 to 7.1-7.2, and in which direction does their sum act relative to the K2P closure quantified in SCI45?** Decision it changes: whether the pHo-step arm of the INV-18 ladder must read burst incidence and an NMDAR component (design), whether a pH-dependent GluN2B tool (NP10679) is a state discriminator, and whether an acid shift is protective or pro-excitatory in this preparation. Information: SCI45 verified the K2P side (pH50, Imin, native pools); the NMDAR and ASIC side is background knowledge here.

## What a producer should read
1. Primary sources of the proton IC50/pK and the fraction of NMDAR current at pH 7.4, 7.2 and 7.0 for GluN1/GluN2A, GluN1/GluN2B and triheteromers (recombinant), with temperature, Mg2+ and glycine/glutamate conditions; native sources in cortical or hippocampal neurons (age, temperature, Mg2+).
2. ASIC1a: pH threshold and half-activation, desensitisation, native evidence in cortical pyramidal neurons (age), and whether a 0.2 unit fall at pH 7.3-7.4 produces a current.
3. Any source that puts these on one cell (K2P closure plus NMDAR inhibition) in a pH step on cortical neurons.
