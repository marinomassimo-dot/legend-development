# SCI45 P1 answers (producer og-u, 2026-11-11)

Non-canonical research material. Not medical advice; no dose, no ranking, no claim on WWOX. Every number below is recombinant or from another region unless it says otherwise; no row is a juvenile neocortical layer 2/3 measurement.

## One line per question

* **R1.** All three channels are closed by extracellular acid, with different midpoints and no single TASK-1 value: in one oocyte assay (22.5 C, 2 mM K+, no HCO3-) pH50 is TASK-1 7.30, TASK-3 6.29, TREK-1 7.51 (current remaining of the pH 9.0 value at pH 7.4: about 0.57, 0.97, 0.61, by eye); TASK-1 elsewhere 6.6 (murine, 70 mM K+) to 7.4; TASK-3 6.3 to 6.5; the TASK-1/3 tandem about 7.3 (abstract); TREK-1 is opened by intracellular acid (EC50 5.9, Hill 2.3, cell-free patch, so about 0.3 percent open at pHi 7.0), while TASK pHi sensitivity was not reached. Rows PH01-PH09, PH14, PH16-PH18; D01-D07.
* **R2.** Native pH-sensitive resting current is shown in adult rat brainstem neurons (pHo 7.4 to 6.8 removes about 42 pA of holding current, 14/18 cells, 34-36 C, HCO3- bath, HEPES pipette) and, at abstract level, in hippocampal CA1 pyramidal cells (acid depolarises and raises Rin), thalamic relay and cerebellar granule cells; the one cortical datum is a TREK-1 knock-out in adult S1 layers 2-4 (persistent firing 86.8 vs 42.1 percent) with no pH step; no juvenile layer 2/3 pH response was found (bounded absence). NE01, NE04, NE06, NE11; D08-D10.
* **R3.** Among five tools only ML365 separates two of the three channels by about 60 to 100-fold (TASK-1 over TASK-3, CHO cells, pH 7.4), with nothing measured against TREK-1 or the heteromer; A1899 and PK-THPP have about a 10-fold window and PK-THPP also inhibits native heteromer-rich channels; spadin did not block basal TREK-1 in the one independent oocyte test; Zn2+ is a TASK-3 discriminator at abstract level; no tool was tested at more than one pH. T01-T05; D11-D14.
* **R4.** Perfused-tissue pHo moves little with activity (in vivo mouse S1: none acid, about +0.01-0.02 after stimulation by the scale bar; slices: alkaline transients up to 0.17, acid offset from the bath through tissue PCO2); lactate and pHe are linked in ischaemia in vivo, and bath lactate (10 mM) lowers neuron pHi by 0.05-0.07 in a perfused human neocortical slice, but no abstract reports pHo after a lactate step in a perfused slice, and a pH-independent lactate action on TREK1 (15-30 mM) is reported. TP01-TP12; D15-D18.

## What the reading does not settle

The two founding full texts (Duprat 1997, Rajan 2000) and the TASK-1/TASK-3 heteromer, hippocampal pyramidal and thalamic native papers were abstract-level only (bot-check or not in the open bucket). No physiological-temperature pH curve for any channel was reached. The size check is for the Orchestrator: use pH50 and the unreported Hill slopes (not stated in the sources) with the caveat that slopes are missing except Piechotta (2.3, TREK-1 pHi) and the imposed 1 of Yuill.
