# IT27 P1 verification of the IT24 P4 analysis (GSE215425, GSE158681/2, GSE158684)

Verifier: independent of the IT24 producer. 2026-11-04. Non-canonical research material, not medical advice, reference genotype class only.
Zero spend, no registration, no contact, no e-mail address sent anywhere. Inputs were fetched from the public GEO FTP into a scratchpad directory and treated as untrusted data (code run with `python3 -I`, under `systemd-run --scope -p MemoryMax=4G -p MemorySwapMax=0`).

## 0. What was blind and what was not

* I read the producer's README before starting (design AND the headline counts and conclusions in it), so the headline counts were **not** blind.
* I wrote and ran my own pipelines (`v_p1a_gse215425.py`, `v_p1b_gse1586.py`) before opening the producer's per-gene tables (`p47t_*`, `mouse_p17_*`). I did not open `analyse_it24_p4.py` before my P1a results existed; for P1b I read lines 150-180 of it (the definition of the background flag) **after** my first pass. So per-gene values, sample mapping, leave-one-animal-out, permutation calibration and the DESeq2 contrast are blind; the comparison step (`v_compare_with_producer.py`) is by construction not blind.
* P1c: not re-run (see section 4).

## 4. P1c (GSE158684), answered from existing results, not re-run
Existing independent verification (`../gse158684_verify_it14_p1b_20261101/`, key_numbers.tsv, R0 rules) is enough for the precise question asked. One pool per arm (ctrl 3182 nuclei, KO 2920): nuclei are not replicates. Three things are separable only in definition, not in these data:
* **Composition shift**: oligodendrocyte-class nuclei 328/3182 v 72/2920 (R0; ambient-robust panel 7.8 v 2.0 percent), OPC-class 239 v 331 (R0), neuron share 1353/3182 v 1233/2920. Observed as pool-level counts; cause (tissue, isolation, delayed maturation, broader deletion) not separable.
* **Within-cell-type change**: immature neuronal markers higher in KO neurons (Sox11 252/1353 v 332/1233, Dcx 435 v 507, Igfbpl1 90 v 198 detected nuclei; direction holds in every rule, Nnat does not survive ambient subtraction); Wwox also falls in glia, not only neurons. Pool-level technical differences are not excluded.
* **Maturation**: OPC up while oligodendrocyte-class down is compatible with a block, with fewer cells, or with an age/stage offset between the pools; the glia line (`../glia_maturation_it14_p4_20261101/`) kept it at RESTRICT for the same reason. No new number is claimed here. Class: DESCRIPTIVE_USABLE for "oligodendrocyte composition differs between the two pools"; NOT_USABLE for PV composition and Pvalb-per-cell (undecidable at one pool).

## 5. Proposed questions for the drug-repurposing generator (proposals, not decisions)
1. **PFC/parietal P47T signature as a reversal target (exploratory).** Gene set: genes called by both my OLS and DESeq2 in PFC at FDR<0.10 and also at the same sign in parietal cortex (about 1200 shared candidates; the strict tier is the 178 genes at padj<0.05 and |LFC|>=1 in PFC). Question: which compounds' reference signatures run opposite to this set, and are they BBB-compatible? Evidence level: transcript level, 5 v 5 animals, one lab, mixed tissue, partial-loss missense (protein present), regions share animals. Alternative: the list reflects astro-microglial (Aif1 up) and neuronal composition or one-off animal differences, not WWOX function; Glra1/Mybpc3/Aldh3a1-type outliers may be region-specific noise. Exploratory.
2. **Oligodendrocyte/myelin maturation program, deletion models plus P47T (exploratory, role-resolving only).** Gene set: the 56 genes called in all four deletion contrasts with the same sign (48 down, myelin-enriched), restricted to the myelin subset and excluding red-cell genes; contrast: do pro-differentiation compounds predict reversal? Evidence: replicated direction across four contrasts of one study (n 2-4), attenuated same direction in P47T PFC/parietal (33 of 47), plus the existing IT14 glia line (RESTRICT). Alternatives: composition/maturation delay v seizure-secondary change v cohort offset (WT carries a background shift); the generator must keep this as a question, not a candidate. Exploratory.
3. **Energy-metabolism module in P47T (exploratory, lowest evidence).** Gene set: Hk2, Ldha, Ldhb, Slc2a1, Bnip3, Pdk1 plus decile-matched controls, PFC and parietal. Observation: Hk2 up in PFC (padj 2e-05, +0.66); Ldha up (+0.42 PFC, +0.75 CTX); set-level shifts are small and the cytosolic-ribosome control shifts as much (producer). Question: is a lactate/glycolysis readout worth assaying in P47T tissue before any compound is proposed? Alternative: glial reactivity or activity-dependent change. No RNA result here can be read as flux or activity.
None of these is evidence of a drug effect; all three hold only for a partial-loss missense (1, 3) or neuron-targeted/complete deletion (2), which must stay distinct.
