# Can rare ventricular radial glia account for most of the PAQR6 signal in captured ventricular-zone cells?

The earlier test measured cell detection. This conditional analysis asks about transcript abundance. Use the same public Nowakowski atlas with Cortex, age 10–20 and explicit VZ dissection. The sample metadata give six labeled preparations before inspecting the expression magnitudes. Within each, estimate the sum of per-cell PAQR6 TPM from cells labeled vRG divided by the sum from all captured cells. This is a captured-cell index, conditional on equal total RNA per cell. Repeat with the two dividing-RG clusters added and with all named RG types (add oRG, tRG and RG-early). Report fractions with 0.25-, 1- and 4-fold relative RNA contribution of the included cells, leave-one-sample-out ranges, and the same index with each sample carrying equal weight. For a dominant vRG source in this atlas, require a majority share at equal RNA weight in at least half the VZ samples with a measured PAQR6 signal and at least one captured vRG. If all observations for a gene are zero, report an undefined share. The analysis does not measure a true tissue fraction, discriminate technical dropout or assign the signal in WWOX organoids.
