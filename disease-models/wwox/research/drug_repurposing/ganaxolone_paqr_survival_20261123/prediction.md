# Does an extra-GABA-A receptor survival signal fit the affected WWOX lineage?

23 Nov 2026. Targeted results from Thomas and Pang identify gain of a protective response to ganaxolone in PAQR6-transfected cells challenged by serum withdrawal. Steinberg et al. report reduced apoptosis in week-10 WWOX-deficient ventricular-zone progenitors alongside elevated DNA damage foci. Hypothesis: the same survival signal could retain damaged progenitors, even if it helps injured differentiated cells. The alternative is that the drug-responsive receptor is too scarce in the implicated lineage, or that its activity helps cells repair damage and then yield healthy descendants.

Before inspecting gene or figure values, test PAQR6 (>1 FPKM in at least 75% of sorted fetal human apical RG profiles, and ≥1 CPM in each human WWOX organoid library); show 0.1 and 5 floors and alternate PAQR7/8 separately. Use source anatomical and lineage controls including SOX2, PROM1, DCX and WWOX. Where author sample labels permit, summarize sorted contrasts by biological specimen. In the disease study inspect source EV2H and measure relative ventricular-zone apoptotic-cell endpoint with a graphical bound if numeric source data are unavailable. Consider denominator, donor, nested sections and batches; use only the independent unit actually retrievable. In stressed neuronal cell lines compare directions and specificity controls. Study of birth asphyxia and juvenile toxicology can challenge universal tissue protection but cannot identify this receptor. Require endogenous PAQR6 dependence, increased damaged-cell retention and an adverse change in intact neuronal yield after firing-matched drug treatment before calling this detrimental. A decrease in apoptotic staining alone decides neither protection nor harm.
