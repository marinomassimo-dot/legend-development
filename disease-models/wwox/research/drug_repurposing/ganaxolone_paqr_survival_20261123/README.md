# Ganaxolone, membrane progesterone receptors and the fate of damaged WWOX-deficient cortical progenitors

**Decision.** The most direct extra-GABA-A survival mechanism for ganaxolone has a cell-type problem. In fetal human tissue the ventricular zone expresses PAQR6, and every WWOX organoid expresses it in bulk. Yet isolated cycling apical radial glia from healthy fetal cortex miss the prespecified PAQR6 expression criterion in all four profiles. PAQR7 and PAQR8 remain plausible alternatives, with weaker direct evidence of receptor-specific ganaxolone action. The existing disease phenotype makes decreased caspase staining alone an unsuitable success endpoint. The question is whether surviving progenitors clear their damage and generate intact neurons. [Prediction fixed before RNA results](prediction.md)

## Why the direction of the disease phenotype matters

In untreated WWOX-null week-10 human cerebral organoids, Steinberg et al. found more γH2AX and 53BP1 foci in ventricular progenitors. SOX2-normalized proliferating cells positive for γH2AX increased from 11.9% to 18.6%. Our reading of the median lines in EV2H yields about **24.5% WT, 9.2% KO and 16.1% after WWOX add-back** for the cleaved-caspase-3/SOX2 endpoint. Thus the KO median is about 15 percentage points lower; the add-back median is intermediate. Values were read from a published boxplot and rounded. At the source resolution one vertical pixel is about 0.25 percentage point. WT and KO contribute six and four organoids from two batches each; add-back has four from one batch. The joint fate of individual damaged cells was not tracked and underlying per-organoid values with batch IDs were unavailable in the figure. Survival of damaged cells is a hypothesis, and improved repair could also reduce caspase labeling. [Steinberg et al., Figs. 3E–F and EV2E–H](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8350905/) · [Graphical readings](results/figure_EV2H.tsv)

In Thomas and Pang, 20 nM ganaxolone reduced serum-withdrawal apoptosis in human breast cancer cells engineered to express PAQR6 (mPRδ). Vector-only cells lacked the corresponding viability response. In the PAQR6 model, 10 nM increased cAMP. Progesterone displacement from transfected membranes was incomplete even at the higher tested concentrations. In a hypothalamic neuronal line, protection was seen at 100 nM. In rat hippocampal H19-7 cells, 20 nM ganaxolone decreased cAMP, the opposite signaling direction, and the 100 nM ERK response persisted under the GABA-A antagonist bicuculline. No selective loss of endogenous PAQR6, PAQR7 or PAQR8 was shown to abolish ganaxolone's survival effect in human cortical radial glia. A pathway marker and a cell-death endpoint were not both demonstrated under receptor blockade in the same neural system. These are concentrations in culture; WWOX brain exposure is unknown. [Thomas and Pang, primary experiments in Figs. 1–2](https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2020.00417)

Values are FPKM for the two fetal studies and CPM for organoids. Cross-study fold changes are inappropriate. The prespecified target criterion required at least three of four apical radial glia preparations to exceed 1 FPKM and every organoid library to reach 1 CPM. The two observed counts were **0/4 and 6/6**. At the relaxed 0.1 FPKM threshold PAQR6 was detected in all four sorted libraries. All six ventricular zones exceeded 5 FPKM; the week-13 zone alone measured 8.52 FPKM. Elevated anatomical signal at the same gestational week does not locate the protein in cycling ventricular radial glia. Different isolation protocols, cell-cycle selection, RNA processing and cell mixtures provide several explanations. The Florio fractions were derived from two specimens, with two radial-glia preparations per specimen. GEO identifies profiles by replicate number without an explicit donor key, so individual libraries are presented without an inferred paired-donor test. The less pure neuronal fraction showed median PROM1 3.66 and DCX 245.68 compared with 40.91 and 73.15 in apical radial glia. The Fietz cortical-plate table gives missing values for PAQR6 in all six specimens. These cannot be substituted with zeros to claim ventricular enrichment. ## Independent cell-level check

A second human cortical atlas supports the distinction between an anatomical signal and broad availability in ventricular radial glia. Using the source's cell classes and eight sample groups with at least five ventricular radial glia each (ages 10–20 in the atlas), **PAQR6 was detected in 2/62 cells**, compared with **13/62 for PAQR7**, **14/62 for PAQR8** and **56/62 for SOX2**. The predeclared 10% detection threshold was met in 1/8 groups for PAQR6, 7/8 for each of PAQR7 and PAQR8, and 8/8 for SOX2. PAQR6 results were identical at ≥1 TPM. Restriction to ages 13–16 gave 1/42 PAQR6-positive ventricular radial glia and 0/5 groups meeting the 10% criterion. In the two dividing-radial-glia clusters together, PAQR6 was found in 9/207 cells across 16 eligible sample groups, compared to 43 and 48 for the alternatives. These groups use the atlas sample labels; the numbers of cells are descriptive, without cell-level inferential tests. Dropout, age and cell state leave open a responsive rare subpopulation. This check weakens a widespread PAQR6 mechanism while retaining a test of PAQR7/8 and of receptor protein in the actual WWOX lineage. ### How much signal can rare cells carry?

In a separate restriction of the same atlas to explicitly dissected ventricular zone (ages 10–20), **5 of 415 captured cells** had detectable PAQR6. All five had radial-glia annotations: one ventricular radial glial cell, two dividing radial glia and two outer radial glia. Among the three sample groups with both measurable PAQR6 and a captured ventricular radial glial cell, that class supplied more than half the measured transcript signal in **1/3**, below the prespecified threshold. Its share pooled over these three informative groups was 9.5% under equal cellular RNA weights (2.6–29.6% over fourfold down- or upweighting); omission of a single group moved the equal-weight pooled estimate from 0% to 54.5%. The largest PAQR6-positive group contained no annotated ventricular radial glial cell and therefore was ineligible for this within-sample comparison.

Across **all six** dissected samples, explicitly including the two with zero detected PAQR6, the total across captured cells was 354.38 summed TPM. One outer radial glial cell supplied 275.86 of this total (**77.8%**). Removing it changes the class contributing most of the remaining signal to dividing radial glia. The strict ventricular radial glia supplied 7.40 summed TPM (2.1% of the total); all annotated radial glial classes together supplied 100% of the captured signal in each of the four positive sample groups. Each cell has its own normalization denominator, so these fractions describe captured cells under equal RNA weights and do not estimate the tissue source fraction. Five positive cells leave high sensitivity to dropout, capture probabilities and cluster labels. The results keep **rare radial-glial states** eligible for a target-localization test, while giving little support to a widespread ventricular radial-glial PAQR6 response. The healthy atlas has no WWOX genotype or ganaxolone exposure comparison. ## Exposure check for the parent drug and a separate metabolite

The 20 nM ganaxolone survival result is a nominal culture exposure. The EMA population-PK assessment for oral treatment in CDKL5 deficiency estimated peak total plasma parent-drug concentrations of 247, 269 and 293 ng/mL in three pediatric age groups. With a molecular mass of 332.53 g/mol and a 1% unbound fraction, these correspond to **7.4–8.8 nM unbound parent in plasma** at the model peaks; the corresponding troughs give about 2.5–2.6 nM. These are estimates for another disease, not brain measurements. The regulatory assessment describes 1.1% unbound in one human equilibrium-dialysis experiment, less than 0.7% at 500 ng/mL in a four-hour experiment, and at most 0.1% at 5 µM in a separate experiment. Differences in concentration, duration and quantification prevent treating these as confidence limits for an individual. [EMA assessment, sections 2.5.3.2 and 2.6.2, CDD-POPPK-001](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/assessment-report/ztalmy-epar-public-assessment-report_en.pdf)

For unbound brain extracellular concentration to equal the **nominal** 20 nM culture exposure at a pediatric model peak, the unbound brain-to-plasma ratio would have to be 2.27–2.69 at 1% unbound in plasma, or 22.7–26.9 at 0.1%. If just 10% of the nominal drug in culture were freely available, the required ratios at 1% unbound in plasma would instead be 0.23–0.27. Free culture concentration, partition into neural membranes, unbound brain exposure and time at that exposure were not measured in these comparisons. A rat total-brain/plasma ratio cannot supply the unbound human ratio. In an exposure-matched replication, measure the unbound parent in the assay and distinguish the rapid cAMP endpoint from survival after four days. [Sensitivity grid](results/parent_exposure.tsv) · [Thomas and Pang, Fig. 1](https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2020.00417)

**A competing DNA-damage hypothesis receives limited support.** The parent and the circulating metabolite M2 (M60b) have different assay results. In the 4-hour M2 chromosome test at a nominal 256 µg/mL, the FDA table implies 9/300 aberrant lymphocyte metaphases versus 1/300 vehicle without metabolic activation, and 13/300 versus 2/300 with activation. The differences are +2.7 and +3.7 percentage points (one-sided Fisher P = 0.010 and 0.0034), with relative mitotic indices 71% and 49%. At 333 µg/mL without activation the incidence fell to 6/300. The 21-hour series up to 128 µg/mL gave 5/300 at each of three concentrations versus 2/300. These are metaphase counts and do not estimate inter-individual susceptibility. M2 was negative in the subsequent in-vivo rat bone-marrow micronucleus and liver comet assays at a reported peak exposure about 7.6-fold the human reference. The EMA's integrated conclusion was negative for genotoxic potential. The high-concentration result therefore provides little basis to prioritize a clinically relevant WWOX-specific DNA-damage effect. The available follow-up examined liver and marrow; it does not quantify developing cortical exposure or a genotype interaction. The exposure calculations above apply only to the parent compound. A WWOX susceptibility claim would need compartment-specific M2 exposure and excess DNA damage at matched free concentrations versus isogenic controls. [FDA pharmacology review, study 9603173, pp. 55–56](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2022/215904Orig1s000PharmR.pdf) · [EMA assessment, sections 2.5.4.3 and 2.5.6](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/assessment-report/ztalmy-epar-public-assessment-report_en.pdf) · [Recount and comparison](results/metabolite_aberrations.tsv)

Reproduce both retrospective checks with `python3 exposure_checks.py`. The inputs are targeted factual measurements from publicly accessible regulatory assessments. The repo contains the calculations and no agency artwork.

## What would change the decision?

The test must distinguish preserving functional cells from prolonging the survival of damaged progenitors. In age-matched isogenic human organoids, localize endogenous PAQR6/7/8 in ventricular radial glia and adjacent postmitotic neurons. Test whether a ganaxolone response survives matching network firing and depends on a specific PAQR. A rise in cAMP would fit the engineered PAQR6 response, and a fall would fit the alternate neuronal signaling result, but neither sign identifies a receptor on its own. In each lineage, jointly track DNA damage, cell survival and the absolute yield of intact neuronal descendants. Fewer apoptotic cells with persistent damage and reduced healthy yield would support adverse retention; fewer damaged cells with more healthy descendants would support protection. Different responses in the two lineages remain possible.

The randomized birth-asphyxia lamb study provides an independent challenge to any blanket claim of tissue protection. At 48 hours, cortical and CA3 necrosis improved in treated survivors, while NeuN-positive neuron counts did not differ significantly from untreated asphyxia in any of four regions. Seizure burden also changed, and the study had no group given ganaxolone after sham injury. Separately, the FDA describes neuronal death after direct exposure in neonatal rats. Different injury states, species, exposures and endpoints prevent estimating a treatment-by-injury interaction by subtracting the two studies. Neither experiment measured the PAQR pathway or WWOX. [Lamb study, Fig. 4 and Table 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9828769/) · [FDA pharmacology review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2022/215904Orig1s000PharmR.pdf)
