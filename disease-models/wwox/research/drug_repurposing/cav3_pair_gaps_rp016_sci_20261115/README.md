Labels: DATO-abstract (read in a PubMed abstract), DATO-full (read in a full text), INFERENZA.

**Decision this can change:** whether a sharper verdict than "qualified proposal" exists for the INV-03 pair (efonidipine, flunarizine) and WO-25, or whether the demotion trigger fires.

## Answer first

**No sharper verdict, no demotion. The pair stays a qualified proposal. Two new efonidipine Cav3.1 rows exist and they disagree at face value; Cav3.3 and hERG/QT stay absent after two routes each.**

1. **Cav3.1, efonidipine: one same-lab three-isoform assay now exists, at abstract level (DATO-abstract).** Tanaka 2010 (PMID 20858482): at 1 uM, S(+)-efonidipine blocked expressed Cav1.2 / Cav1.3 / Cav3.1 currents by 75.7 / 75.3 / 94.0 %, R(-)-efonidipine by 30.0 / 19.6 / 92.8 %, nifedipine by 84.0 / 43.2 / 14.9 %. It adds a Cav3.1 number to what ADV-009 held (Furukawa 2009 said only that alpha1G was "significantly blocked"). It is one concentration, an isomer each (not the racemate), no Cav3.2 or Cav3.3, no flunarizine, and cell line, channel species, holding potential, ion and n are not in the abstract; the body was not attempted (Elsevier, no PMC copy; ADV-009 met an interstitial for that publisher family).
2. **Hara 2020 (PMID 33243296, open access, full text read) gives a second Cav3.1 row that does not look like the first (DATO-full).** Human CACNA1G short isoform in HEK293T, whole-cell, room temperature, 2 mM Ca, hyperpolarising step to -100 mV; efonidipine at **5 uM** "insignificantly attenuated the Ca current density mediated by the mutant Ca3.1" and did not move the half-activation potential (wild type p = 0.695, mutant p = 0.567). Limits: the channel is the SCA42 R1715H mutant (the wild-type current-density result is not stated in the text), 5 uM was chosen from a plasma concentration and not as an IC50 probe, stimulation frequency is not stated, and the n of the efonidipine groups is in a figure I did not view.
3. **Why the two are not a contradiction yet, and what the pair's comparison now needs (INFERENZA from a DATO).** Masumiya 2001 (PMID 11191650, guinea-pig ventricular T-type current, abstract): efonidipine IC50 was 1.3e-8, 2.0e-6 and 6.3e-6 M at 1, 0.2 and 0.05 Hz stimulation, a 500-fold span with frequency alone. A single-concentration percent block, an IC50 and a "no significant change" are points on a use-dependent curve unless the stimulation frequency and holding potential are stated. Inayoshi 2011 (PMID 21466810) says the same for hCav3.2 (block depends on frequency and holding potential). **A comparison with flunarizine (Kd values from Santi 2002 at -100 mV) is therefore not made by matching concentrations; it needs the same protocol for both agents.** This turns "unmatched assays" from a caution into a specific requirement.
4. **Cav3.3: absent beyond the Furukawa 2009 abstract (DATO-abstract, two routes).** PubMed query on the isoform names with efonidipine or NZ-105 returns that one paper only; the 19-paper electrophysiology list (oocyte, HEK293, patch clamp, IC50) contains no other Cav3.3 or alpha1I measurement. "Not significantly blocked" is not "inactive".
5. **hERG / QT for efonidipine: absent by two routes (DATO-negative).** A hERG/IKr/QT/action-potential-duration query returns one rabbit sinus-node paper (action potential duration not prolonged, no hERG measurement); a hERG/torsade/QT-prolongation query returns nothing; a potassium-current/QTc/proarrhythmic query returns one clinical heart-rate paper with no QT value. The only K+ statement found is Masumiya 1998 (PMID 9671117): "no or only a weak effect on K+ currents" in guinea-pig heart, without naming IKr and without a number. Absence of a hERG value is a statement about what is indexed, not about the drug.
6. **Triggers (ADV-009 README).** Revival: per-isoform values with concentration and holding potential, or one lab with both agents on Cav3.1-3.3: **not met** (Tanaka covers three isoforms for efonidipine only and lacks the protocol detail; no lab has both agents). Demotion: efonidipine block only at concentrations well above the pair's range on every isoform, or a hERG value in flunarizine's range: **not met** (94 % block at 1 uM on Cav3.1 is not "well above"; no hERG value exists). The Hara row at 5 uM with no significant change is the one observation that leans toward demotion, and it concerns a mutant channel and an unreported frequency; it is recorded, not weighted.
