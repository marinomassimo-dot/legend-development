## 3. Decisions changed (old state, new state, the datum)

**D1 - lactate/LDH, pertinence of the compound (Q03).**
*Old:* work order `NEED-LACTATE-LDH` (verify a hypothesis) asked two things: is LDH or lactate raised in WWOX-deficient neural cells, and does stiripentol reach an inhibitory level at all, with exposure read against a "42 to 500 micromolar LDH range".
*New:* the second question is closed negative for stiripentol and is no longer asked of it; the compound arm is a selective LDHA tool of nanomolar potency; stiripentol stays only as a complex I and GABA-A comparator. The first question is unchanged and is still the verification.
*Datum:* (a) ChEMBL, molecule-first on the racemate (28 rows, none a fitted LDH potency): document CHEMBL4311969 gives LDH5 (A4) and LDH1 (B4) inhibition of **7 percent at 2 millimolar** (cell-free, UV-vis, pyruvate substrate); (b) the independent recombinant human LDHA assay already in the cards gives 9.9 percent at 500 micromolar (PMID 39769031, receipt FTR-20261026-39769031-01, not re-read here); (c) the US label (Diacomit, effective 2026-04-27, prescription, set id in `comparison.tsv`) states that the human mechanism is unknown and names GABA-A and cytochrome P450 inhibition as possible mechanisms, protein binding 99 percent, and no peak concentration; (d) the 42-500 micromolar range is a secondary figure resting on three sources (card E02, `sada_ldh_precedent_p2a_20261026.md`). The same ChEMBL document gives a GABA-A (alpha1/beta2/gamma2, HEK293T, -50 mV) EC50 of 46,000 nM, so the GABA-A action is at least about 40 fold more potent than the LDH action as measured.
*Not shown:* that stiripentol never inhibits brain LDH (the 2015 report is abstract-only, secondary); lactate in WWOX-deficient cells. Target-first on human LDHA (CHEMBL4835, accession P00338): 1,428 rows, **1,000 retrieved, TRUNCATED**, 134 molecules with an "=" value at or below 100 nM among the retrieved rows; they are listed by ChEMBL id in `measures_detail.tsv`. Their names, availability and the research-use terms were not determined.

**D2 - PDH axis, probe and feasibility (Q04).**
*Old:* work order `NEED-PDH-PYRUVATE-AXIS` and card I01: genotype-by-inhibitor with dichloroacetate, "a free-DCA exposure and a second PDK inhibitor of different chemistry as orthogonal controls".
*New:* the probe is a PDK inhibitor of nanomolar potency chosen from ChEMBL target rows; dichloroacetate is the clinically known comparator; a null with dichloroacetate alone is not read as absence of a PDK effect.
*Datum:* ChEMBL rows for dichloroacetic acid (human recombinant enzymes of unknown origin, three documents): PDK4 IC50 108,000 nM (CHEMBL6121009, 2025) and 727,000 nM (CHEMBL4007440, 2017); PDK2 504,000 nM (2017), 9,000,000 nM (CHEMBL4177695) and above 5,000,000 nM; PDK1 1,025,000 nM, 3,660,000 nM and 5,100,000 nM; PDK3 5,560,000 nM. The spread within one isoform across laboratories (about 7 fold for PDK4, about 18 fold for PDK2) is as large as between isoforms; the fibroblast lactate-oxidation EC50 of 130,000 nM (IT7) is cell-level, not target engagement. Target-first rows: PDK1, PDK2 and PDK4 each hold molecules with an IC50 at or below 100 nM in `measures_detail.tsv` (PDK1 truncated at 600 of 1,115 rows, PDK2 at 600 of 1,393; PDK4 complete, 122 rows). Identity: the curated PDK mechanism sits on the **sodium salt record CHEMBL306823, which is its own parent**, while the acid CHEMBL13960 has no curated mechanism and the two are not linked by ChEMBL's hierarchy: a mechanism query for "dichloroacetate" can land on either, and both were read.
*Not shown:* that a nanomolar PDK inhibitor engages PDK in neurons, that it is available for research, or its brain exposure; nothing was ordered or asked. Human plasma concentrations of dichloroacetate were not found in the abstracts retrieved (PMID 25079374, PMID 16651305 give kinetics qualitatively), so the Cmax is UNKNOWN here.

**D3 - bumetanide, feasibility of the in-vivo arm (Q08).**
*Old:* card F03 listed "brain exposure of bumetanide at a relevant dose" as unknown and predicted a lowering of burst frequency "at a brain-relevant exposure".
*New:* a systemic arm is not a test of the premise (a negative result there is uninformative about NKCC1); the premise measurement (E_GABA) is unaffected and the compound is applied in the bath ex vivo, where the NKCC1 range of about 1.5 to 2.5 micromolar is the exposure target.
*Datum:* mouse primary pharmacokinetics at abstract level: brain to plasma ratio about 0.015 to 0.02 (PMID 25449033); free brain concentration in the NKCC1-inhibitory range only at 10 mg/kg intravenous in mice (PMID 33279441); diuretic-range doses do not reach the concentrations that inhibit NKCC1 in hippocampal and neocortical neurons, and a close derivative that does not inhibit NKCC1 reproduces some CNS effects (PMID 30248303). Potency: ChEMBL HT-29 rubidium-uptake IC50 1,540 nM (human NKCC1); GtoPdb (human, one document, PMID 11882915) pIC50 5.60 for NKCC1 and 6.48 for the kidney transporter, i.e. about 7.6 fold more potent on the renal target. ChEMBL curates the mechanism on the renal transporter only.
*Not shown:* human CSF concentration; E_GABA in any Wwox system. F03 stays an abstention (P1).

## 5. Service handling

The ChEMBL adapter was used in activity mode (molecule-first; accession resolution for the target-first pulls; assay context; declared truncation) through a retry layer in `pharm_qualify.py`: HTTP 5xx, 429 and transport failures are retried at 0, 2, 6, 15 and 30 s; every attempt is logged in `service_log.tsv`; HTTP 404 is an answer. Outcomes are four distinct states: `SERVICE_ERROR`, `NO_ROWS_UNDER_FILTERS`, `ROWS_RETRIEVED`, `ROWS_RETRIEVED_TRUNCATED`. **No service error occurred**: 217 attempts per full run in three full runs (the first without the label-section widening), all HTTP 200 or an expected 404 for the dichloroacetate label query; the HTTP 500 seen once in IT7 did not recur. The handling is therefore tested by injection (`pharm_qualify.py selftest`: two 500s then success is retried with backoff; a permanent 500 is `SERVICE_ERROR` and never `NO_ROWS_UNDER_FILTERS`; a transport failure is retried; a 404 is not an error; groups never merge units, relations or assay types). openFDA: a 404 for "dichloroacetate" means no match for that generic name on 2026-10-29, not absence of a label. Truncations: LDHA target-first 1,000 of 1,428; PDK1 600 of 1,115; PDK2 600 of 1,393 (all declared in the status column of `comparison.tsv`).

The raw responses came to 1.64 MB compressed, over the 1 MB rule, so they are **not stored**; `raw_hashes.tsv` lists the SHA-256 of each response (payload as returned) and of `gtopdb/interactions.csv` and `dgidb/interactions.tsv` (cache, GtoPdb 2026.3.1 published 2026-10-12; DGIdb 2024-06-10 export, STALE_EXPORT, not cleared for public release). A verifier re-querying will see current ChEMBL data; a changed hash is a changed release, not necessarily an error. `collected.json.gz` lets `pharm_qualify.py rebuild` regenerate both tables byte-identically offline (checked twice).
