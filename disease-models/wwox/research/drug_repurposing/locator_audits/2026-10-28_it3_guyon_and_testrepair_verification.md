### Q1 Doses and n. SUPPORTED
- Results (stiripentol section): "P3 tumor-bearing mice were intraperitoneally injected with 150 mg/kg of stiripentol ... median survival was 80 days for control mice and 97 days for stiripentol-treated mice (Fig 6F)". No schedule in Results.
- Fig 6F legend: "Mice were treated either with vehicle (blue) or stiripentol at 150 mg/kg (red) (n = 15-16 mice per group) ... Log-rank (Mantel-Cox) test: P = 0.002."
- Methods, Intracranial implantation paragraph: "5 spheroids of P3 or BL13 knock-out for LDHA, LDHB, or LDHA/B (only LDHA/B for BL13) and control cell lines were stereotactically implanted ... (n = 8 for survival or n = 5 for stopping at a given point, 7-9-week-old) ... After 4 days, started the bevacizumab injections (10 mg/kg ip), and/or stiripentol (100 mg/kg ip), or phenformin (50 mg/kg ip) injection 3 times a week."
- No Methods sentence states 150 mg/kg. 100 mg/kg appears only in that sentence. The Fig EV3 legend (complement of Fig 3H) has bevacizumab in LDHA/B-KO P3 mice at n = 8 per group, so that paragraph is the knockout/combination cohort paragraph.
- Whether 100 vs 150 is the same experiment: NOT determinable from the source. Nothing links the Methods sentence to Fig 6F; it names no figure. It is a combined schedule for three drugs; no Results or legend reports a stiripentol-plus-bevacizumab arm. Two readings stay open: a different cohort/contrast, or a Methods inconsistency. Also unreconciled and not noted by the producer: Ethical issues says "8- to 11-week-old male mice", the implantation paragraph says "7-9-week-old".
- Producer comparison: receipt obs. (1), p2a line 76 and G4 are accurate. p2a line 76 says "may describe another cohort", "probably does not describe the Fig 6F cohort", "a discrepancy is possible, not established": correctly hedged. Verdict: no overshoot.

### Q3 Randomisation and blinding. SUPPORTED, with a scope caveat
- Ethical issues paragraph (Methods): "All experiments were performed with 8- to 11-week-old male mice and were randomly allocated to the treatment groups."
- Statistical analysis paragraph (Methods): "No statistical methods were used to predetermine the sample size. The experiments were not randomized, and investigators were not blinded to allocation during experiments and outcome assessment."
- Both are generic, whole-paper statements; neither names an experiment or figure. Different paragraphs; the first is about mice, the second about "the experiments". They contradict each other on randomisation only if "the experiments" includes the mouse work, the natural reading but not stated. Blinding is stated once, generically.
- Producer: receipt obs. (3) accurate. p2a line 147 "says both that mice were randomly allocated and that the experiments were not randomized and investigators not blinded" is accurate. OVERSHOOT (minor): the same row's parenthesis "(the survival result is unblinded by the paper's own statement)". The paper's statement never names Fig 6F; applying it to the survival endpoint is an inference. Suggested wording: "the paper's generic statement says investigators were not blinded in any experiment, which would include the survival experiment".
