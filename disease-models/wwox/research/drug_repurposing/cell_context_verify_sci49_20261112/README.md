1. **A1, the low held human KCNQ2 value.** The table permits: in one anti-Thy1-selected adult sample (one patient, 25 years) KCNQ2 is at or below 0.1 FPKM, as it is in the four whole-cortex samples (0.2–1.5). It does **not** permit absence of expression, a species difference with mouse P7 neurons, or any statement about human neurons in general. New and verified: **0.1 is a reporting floor** (the same laboratory's mouse paper sets any value below 0.1 to 0.1; it is the minimum of all 45 columns and 32% of genes in the neuron column sit exactly at it); KCNQ2/SNAP25 is 0.001–0.009 in human whole cortex against 0.187 in mouse whole cortex and 0.122 in HPA forebrain projection neurons; RBFOX3 is also near the floor. Cause: **not determinable**; six candidates listed, none asserted (`verify/audit.md` §2).
2. **A2, what the RNA supports about OPC.** *Corrected:* 0.73 is the ratio to **forebrain projection neurons**, not to "the neuronal maximum" (the highest neuronal cluster is ca1-3 excitatory, 164.3 nCPM; OPC/that = 0.68). *Strengthened at RNA level only:* OPC KCNQ2 ranks **8th of 48** cluster types, above 27 of 34 neuronal types, and is 0.35 of neurons in the mouse (P17 OPC against P7 neurons, n=2 each): "plausible" understates the RNA and overstates nothing else, because RNA is not protein, current or drug response, and the ages are adult human and P7/P17 mouse, not P13–P17.
3. **A3, what would make OPC relevant to a P13–P17 layer II/III readout.** Functional Kv7 evidence in OPC rests on **one study** (Wang 2011, cortex-derived rat cultures: an XE991-sensitive M-type current, peak about 61 pA; slices only immunostained, no opener tested, nothing recorded in slice). Functional GABA-A on OPC around the second postnatal week rests on **abstracts** in this verification (their full texts were unreachable: a bot check and a 403, recorded, not retried); the abstracts place OPC GABAergic synaptic input at a peak near P10, falling after the second week, with extrasynaptic phasic input persisting. **No source read links an acute OPC manipulation to sIPSC or burst readouts**; the known links are chronic genetic. The OPC contribution to a P13–P17 readout is **not determinable** from the literature; four data would settle it (identified NG2-cell recordings at that age with the blocker and the opener; KCNQ2/3 protein in NG2 cells with a validated antibody; neuron-restricted against OPC-restricted Kcnq2 loss with the same readout; a fast OPC-to-neuron route).
4. **A4, provenance and independence.** The HPA single-nuclei brain table is, **by the HPA documentation**, the Siletti et al. 2023 dataset (PMID 37824663; about 2.6 million nuclei from 11 regions, reclustered into 48 main cell types; the stored table has exactly those 48 cluster types). **Not confirmed against the paper** (the publisher page returned a bot check and there is no PMC copy; the HPA names are HPA's own re-annotation, not matched to the paper's 461 clusters). Independent: HPA against Zhang 2016 human and against Zhang 2014 mouse. **Not independent:** clusters within HPA (one experiment), and Zhang 2016 with Zhang 2014 (same laboratory, pipeline and floor). Their KCNQ2 disagreement is a between-assay discordance; neither side overrules the other.
5. **A5, which control a statement needs (the corrected formulation).** *An OPC-aware control is required only to state that a bath-applied Kv7 opener acts through neurons: identified NG2-cell recordings in the same slices and age showing whether a Kv7-blocker-sensitive current exists and responds to the opener and, if it does, a cell-restricted Kcnq2 manipulation to separate the neuronal from the OPC share. It is not required to report that the opener changes the burst or sIPSC readout, nor that the change is Kv7-dependent (a Kv7 blocker or genetic arm), nor that a component is Wwox-specific (a genotype-by-drug comparison).* Astrocytes need no such arm on KCNQ2 RNA grounds. **Microglia:** none for a KCNQ2-dependent attribution, but human microglia KCNQ3 RNA is the highest in the whole HPA table (2,632 nCPM against a neuronal maximum of 1,671) and the independent human myeloid samples echo it while mouse microglia Kcnq3 is at the floor: for KCNQ3-containing channels microglia are not excluded by RNA, and this discordance is not resolved.
