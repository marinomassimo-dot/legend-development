## The decisive propositions (neutral wording; the verifier tests each from the originals and need not agree with any)
* **A1 — what the low held human KCNQ2 value permits.** In the held human table (GSE73721, Zhang 2016) KCNQ2 reads 0.1 in the single neuron sample and 0.2–1.5 in four whole-cortex samples; RBFOX3 reads 0.6 in the same neuron sample against SNAP25 692.9; 100 raw cells in the examined columns carry exactly 0.1. *Question:* which conclusions about human neuronal KCNQ2 are and are not permitted by that table (a species difference with mouse P7 neurons? absence of expression? a sample or processing feature? what do the GEO sample description and the Zhang 2016 Methods say about how that neuron sample was obtained and about the 0.1 value?). A low value does not show absence of expression.
* **A2 — what the RNA data support about OPC participation in a bath-applied Kv7 manipulation.** In the HPA single-nuclei cluster table (SHA-256 b24e46d4…, stored in `files/geo/HPA_single_nuclei/`) KCNQ2 is highest in forebrain neuronal classes and OPC read 0.73 of the neuronal maximum; in the mouse table (GSE52564, Zhang 2014, P7 neurons, P17 OPC) Kcnq2 in OPC is 0.35 of neurons. *Question:* does this support "an OPC contribution to a bath-applied Kv7 opener is plausible", more, or less? RNA presence is not protein, channel function, current or drug response.
* **A3 — which functional, age and preparation data would make the OPC possibility relevant to a P13–17 slice readout.** *Question:* what, in the primary literature, shows (or fails to show) Kv7/KCNQ or M-type current in cortical OPC/NG2 cells, at which age; functional GABA-A on OPC around the second postnatal week and its full-text basis (the SCI47 premise "functional GABA-A in mouse OPC" came from an **abstract**); whether OPC-to-neuron coupling is needed for a readout to change; what the data cannot say.
* **A4 — provenance of the HPA table and independence of comparisons.** *Question:* from which dataset do the HPA single-nuclei brain cluster types come (PMID 37824663 is a **candidate to confirm or reject**, not an identified source)? Which of the comparisons used (HPA vs Zhang 2016 human; HPA vs Zhang 2014 mouse; clusters within HPA) are independent, and which share a dataset or an experiment?
* **A5 — is the OPC-aware control necessary for a specific causal attribution, or only useful?** *Question:* which statement about a slice effect of a bath-applied Kv7 opener or GABA-A modulator requires which control (a cell-restricted application, identified NG2-cell recordings, a Kv7-blocker-sensitive current in identified cells, none), and for which statement is it only an additional refinement; whether astrocytes or microglia need the same control (their RNA was low by a factor of 6.9 and over 40 against the neuronal maximum in the HPA table, a RNA statement only). Not every experiment needs new arms on every cell class.
