## Answer first

**Verdict: CONFLICT. The pre-registered sign prediction (glial markers are NOT yet different from wild type before the first seizure) is not borne out as a rule and is not refuted as a rule.** Rows of both kinds exist in the two models that carry fully qualifying rows, each side rests on rows with a stated thin margin, and the split follows the kind of readout rather than the model. FUNCTION readouts (lactate, glucose uptake, FDG-PET, energy metabolites) before onset: **THIN, a named missing measure: 0 qualifying rows in any model.**

1. **Stain-level glial markers (GFAP, Iba1, astrocyte density, Sholl) before onset: mostly no difference, one rise.** No difference: Scn8a N1768D cortex, GFAP and Iba1 at the preseizing age (GFAP P21 to P24, n 5 against 5, p = .5476; onset 8 to 16 weeks), and Scn1a exon-25 conditional heterozygote at PN19 (GFAP, astrocyte density, Sholl; n 5 to 12 per arm; p .21 to .74; one day before the paper's presymptomatic limit of PN20). A rise: the Scn1a replacement-type allele at P19 to P21 (GFAP p = 0.00001 and Iba1 p = 0.005 to 0.008 as printed in the text, from field-level statistics on 3 to 4 mice; the window touches the cited onset of "after P21").
2. **Transcript-pathway and cytokine readouts before onset: rises, in rows that each lack one part of the qualifying definition.** Scn8a hippocampal pathways (males only, 6 weeks: S100 signalling z 3.84, phagosome formation z 4.12; females: no glial pathway); Gabrg2 Q390X forebrain cytokines (TNF, IL-1β, IL-6 higher at 2 weeks, 2 to 3 days before the stated onset of P16 to P17; n given per panel, not per arm; significance as symbols only); Depdc5 forebrain cKO microglia (more bushy and amoeboid forms at P8, seven days before the stated onset of P15; n 3 against 3; no significance statement). One more near row is flat: Arx at P7 (no difference in GFAP, Iba1/CD68, cytokines), but P7 is the paper's own spasm-onset age, so it is "at onset".
3. **FUNCTION: no row qualifies.** The nearest items are a brainstem metabolomics study (glycolytic intermediates higher, P20 to P30, the paper's own mortality window, no first-seizure age), an FDG-PET study (higher uptake, but at 1 to 2 months with spontaneous seizures already present), and two Glut1 or Scn1a metabolomics papers at abstract level with no ages. This is a statement about the 25 full texts, one preprint and about 15 abstracts read, not about the literature.
4. **The counts the pre-registration asked for (independent models, strict rows).** Models with at least one fully qualifying MARKER row: **2** (Scn8a, Scn1a; the two Scn1a alleles are counted as one gene-model because the texts do not show different originating laboratories). Direction among them: no difference in 2 (Scn8a; Scn1a conditional), higher in 2 (Scn8a males, pathway level; Scn1a replacement-type allele), lower in 0. Adding the two caveat models (Gabrg2 Q390X, Depdc5): 4 models with a rise, 2 with no difference, 0 lower. The rejecting observations: RISE needs at least 2 independent models with a rise and none flat-conflicting; FLAT or LOWER needs at least 2 with no difference and none rising; both kinds are present, so **CONFLICT, handed to R3 with the conflicting rows below.**
5. **What it does for the pup axis.** The pre-onset test status of the glial arm is neither confirmed nor withdrawn by other models. Stain-level GFAP and Iba1 stay a readout scored beside the others (as they already are), not an arm. A flat pre-onset stain-level sign is the more common result in the rows that qualify cleanly (3 of 4 clean stain rows are flat); a pre-onset rise is reported for cytokines and pathway scores, which are tissue-level and do not tell astrocytes or microglia apart from neurons. That reading is a pattern in 5 models, not a rule.

## Counters and limits (carried)

* A "no difference" in n = 3 to 6 is a small-sample null, not absence: three of the four no-difference rows (the Scn8a GFAP, Iba1 and RNA-seq rows) have n of 3 to 5 per arm, and the RNA-seq null has no printed test.
* "Pre-onset" is set by the paper's stated onset, and onset statements disagree inside single papers (Scn8a, Gabrg2, Scn1a: 2 to 6 months; P16 to P19; "after P21" against "later than P22"). A reading one to three days before a stated onset is not a pre-onset reading by any margin that matters; a reading weeks before (Scn8a, Depdc5) is.
* Seizures at onset may precede the first video-EEG: the pre-seizure pups in the Scn8a, Scn1a and Depdc5 papers are not described as monitored individually.
* Cytokine and pathway scores are tissue-level and cannot be assigned to astrocytes, microglia or neurons; the stain-level rows are the only ones that name a cell class.
* One allele appears in three papers (Scn8a N1768D; the texts do not show the same colony or background) and counts once; two Scn1a alleles of different construction count once because the texts do not show different originating laboratories.
* Absence: no qualifying MARKER or FUNCTION row was found for Scn2a, Kcnq2, Stxbp1, Cdkl5, Syngap1, Pcdh19, Slc6a1, Gnao1, Kcnt1, Grin2a, Grin2b, Dnm1, Cacna1a or Hcn1 by two routes (Europe PMC and PubMed); screening was mostly title and abstract level, supplements were never opened, and the "metabolism" energy queries on PubMed were not exhaustively screened. Tsc models carried only a neuronal KCC2 readout.
* Unread: the published version of the Scn1a A1783V proteome paper, two metabolomics abstracts, two Cstb and one Cdkl5 abstract, the Tcf4, Nacc1 and Dravet-thalamus full texts (server error), and all supplements.
* Access results: Wiley and ScienceDirect pages stopped at a challenge or refusal (route result, STOP for that route); no contact-address service, no e-mail sent anywhere.
