# IT46-Q07: is there an adequacy criterion for NFE2L2 or KEAP1 loss in a neuron? (IT, 2026-11-21; ADV-approved, bounded)

## Answer first
**No criterion in the held sources. The one partial-knockdown neuronal line is not documented as adequate. The independence reading stays open.** Neither rejecting observation occurred: R1 (a held source states residual protein, time and a functional target readout as an adequacy criterion for neurons) did not; R2 (the LUHMES partial-knockdown line documented as adequate with a functional readout) did not. ## What this leaves, in the pre-registered form
- INFERENCE: the only held example of a loss that removes a target-gene induction is the microglial one (mRNA about 0.05 to 0.10 of control); the neuronal line sits at 0.68 (protein) and 0.36 (mRNA). That comparison is calibration, not a criterion, and the two cell classes differ.
- The form of the criterion (a residual-protein or null statement by a named method, the time of the read, and a functional target readout including survivors) is the design team's; its numbers are the laboratory's to fix before the experiment, as the map row asks.
