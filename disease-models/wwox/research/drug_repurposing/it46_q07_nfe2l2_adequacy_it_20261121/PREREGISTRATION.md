## What counts as an adequate loss (fixed now, no number supplied by me)
A held source states, for NFE2L2 or KEAP1 in neurons or neuron-like cells, ALL of: (a) a residual-protein level or a statement of complete loss, measured by a named method (blot, immunostain, or a genetic null); (b) the time after loss at which the effect was read; (c) a target-gene or pathway readout showing the loss was functional (for example loss of induction of a canonical target), including whether target genes survived. A source that states only a partial knockdown without (c) does not count as adequate.
