## 1. Decision (first)

1. **The generator consumes none of the cell-population observations as such.** The GSE158684 snRNA result (Pvalb UMI-share ratio 0.59; oligodendrocyte-class nuclei about 3- to 5-fold fewer, OPC-class higher) is in no seed row. `grep -rl GSE158684` over `generation_it9_p1_20261030`, `generation_it10_p1_20261031`, `generation_it9_p2_20261030`, `generation_it21_p2_20261103`, `qualification_it21_p3b_20261103` and `generation_it22_p1_20261103` returns one file: the IT21 P2 README diagnosis row D1 ("available but unlinked information"). The organoid scRNA composition (more radial glia, fewer neurons in the engineered knockout) is cited only inside reason strings that demote other rows.
2. **Two things are consumed that should be read as cell-population-sensitive.** (a) `S116` NPY down (human organoid, log2 -6.24) is the first contributing seed of 72 of 87 Q4 proposals in `generation_it21_p2_20261103/results/proposals_it21.tsv`; it was admitted by the README 3a cross-system rule through `S066`, an NPY-positive cell count, while the mouse P17 transcript contrast has the opposite sign in 4 of 4 contrasts and the organoid also lost neurons. (b) `S100`/`S101` MYC (560 of 560 Q6 proposals) is the one within-cell-type observation consumed; it is within radial-glia TYPE, not within cell STATE, and `S101` is a positive-cell fraction labelled "protein".
3. **One latent hazard.** `S079` (SET:MYELIN_OL_10, mouse bulk, down, usable yes) is a cell-type marker set whose fall is what GSE158684 explains by oligodendrocyte-class depletion. It is kept out of the engine only by a data gap: `hgnc_check.tsv` has a mouse ortholog link for MBP only, so the adapter drops the set (`adapter_counts.json`, Q5 `no_hgnc_ortholog_link_dropped` = 1; `seeds_p1/Q5_expression_tiersAB.tsv` = IL1B, IL6, TNF). Completing the link table would turn tissue composition into a 10-gene intracellular-regulation seed. Re-class S079 first.
4. **Smallest adequate representation.** Cell-population results cannot be gene seeds (state/process rows have no gene members: 15 process/state rows at tiers AB, 17 at ABC, and 6 pathology gene rows are dropped; `seeds_p1/adapter_counts*.json`). Proposed: (i) gene-level cell-type-marker transcripts (PVALB, NPY, KCNC1) enter the table as `usable=no` rows so the reason sits in the closed vocabulary, as S067-S069 already do; (ii) composition results enter as `STATE:` rows (usable presence and direction, magnitudes held out) with a new guard `COMPOSITION_NOT_REGULATION`; (iii) a 6-column relation table (`proposed_cell_population_relations.tsv`) that a phenotype-first or compound-to-WWOX route can join against: "which cell population, which way, measured how, in which system, how far may it be read".

## 3. What the evidence allows and does not allow

Allowed: lower pooled Pvalb transcript in the KO pool (ratio 0.59, exact 0.38 to 0.89) and lower Pvalb in all four bulk contrasts (log2 -1.70, -0.86, -1.16, -0.59); oligodendrocyte-class nuclei about 3- to 5-fold fewer with OPC-class higher under every classification rule (7.8 to 14.0 versus 2.0 to 4.7 percent of all nuclei), as an observed composition difference between two pools; more radial glia and fewer neurons in the engineered knockout organoid; MYC higher in KO radial glia.

Not allowed: fewer PV cells (cell loss versus less Pvalb per cell versus maturation is undecidable: one pooled matrix per arm, 1 to 7 inhibitory nuclei with Pvalb per arm); a PV-class count (the producer's 3 v 5 and 0.80 were not confirmed by the verifier, who finds 4 v 2 and 7 v 9 with only 31 of 69 inhibitory nuclei in common: do not quote); "same animals" for bulk and snRNA (not stated); tissue composition read as intracellular regulation; any conversion across levels (transcript, protein, cell count, positive fraction stay separate); a neuron-restricted-deletion reading of the glial Wwox fall (unexplained).

Counter-evidence kept: bulk Pvalb is twice the log2 size of the nuclei result; Gad1 is mildly lower in bulk and higher in the pool (1.22); Npy is higher in mouse bulk and pool but lower in the organoid and in the dentate cell count; the cortex bulk value -0.59 is unrelated to the snRNA ratio 0.59; hNPC shWWOX progenitor markers are lower (-1.17), against the organoid RG increase (n = 1 library, line state confounded); SCAR12 shows the MYC+ fraction up without RG expansion or neuron loss; S079's control-versus-control contrasts are inside their nulls in `panel_summary.tsv` at 1.0 TPM (the seed text says outside; not reproduced here). One origin counts once: the paper's cluster sentence, the CC1+/PDGFRa+ counts and GSE158684 are one origin (PMID 33914858); the bulk deposit and the nuclei deposit share the genetic contrast and their animal overlap is unstated, so they are not counted as independent systems. Unknown is not negative: no PV change resolved is not a PV-unchanged result.

## 5. Expected change in proposals (engine not run; expectation only)

* **Q6.** No expected rank change from this package: MYC stays the single seed (S100/S101).
