## 1. Answer first

**The NRF2 organoid values are consumed by nothing.** `grep -w` for NFE2L2, NQO1, HMOX1, KEAP1, G6PD over `generation_it9_p1_20261030/seeds.tsv`, `generation_it9_p2_20261030/seeds_p1/*.tsv` and `generation_it10_p1_20261031/origin_overrides.tsv`: 0 hits each. The only NRF2-axis gene that reaches the engine is SLC7A11, as one member of `SET:ISR_TARGETS_12` (seed S046, Q3, direction uncertain, guard ISR_SIGN_UNRESOLVED), i.e. as an ATF4 target, not as an NRF2 target. Every other redox observation is either a `STATE:` row (the adapter counts and drops it: S033 resazurin, 0 hits in `seeds_p1`), a tier D fly row (dropped: S111), or in no row (MEF ROS, MEF redox transcripts, P47T cerebellum ROS hallmark set).

**But omaveloxolone, ML385 and the NFE2L2 chain are already in the generated list, for a different reason.** `proposals_it21.tsv` holds exactly 3 rows with NFE2L2 as main target: IT21P2-0175 (OMAVELOXOLONE, ChEMBL family, H_HARM, blocker `decided_in_earlier_qualification_record`), IT21P2-0176 (Omaveloxolone, DGIdb-only twin, `identity_not_resolved`), IT21P2-0303 (Ml385, H_PROTECT, `identity_not_resolved`), all Q5, chain `NFE2L2 -T(-)-> IL1B`. They arise because NFE2L2 regulates the mouse cytokine seeds IL1B/TNF/IL6 (S084 to S086), not because NFE2L2 was observed lower. So the generator reaches the NRF2 axis from the downstream cytokine side and is blind to the upstream observation. This matters for interpretation: those rows rest on mouse transcripts; the human organoid NRF2 values never entered.

## 2. What the evidence allows (all numbers re-read from the source file)

Organoid (GSE156243, `axis_it10_p22_20261031/organoid_nrf2_rows.tsv`; recomputed in IT11 with 0 mismatches over 36 genes, `nrf2_it11_p23_20261031/premises_verified.tsv` V3): KO/WT mean CPM NFE2L2 0.752, NQO1 0.730, HMOX1 1.667 (my re-arithmetic from the per-library values agrees: NFE2L2 20.82 v 27.67, NQO1 6.31 v 8.65, HMOX1 10.16 v 6.09). Allowed: a transcript-level direction in 4 v 2 libraries. Not allowed: NRF2 activity, WWOX specificity, an intracellular-regulation reading (the engineered knockout shifts composition; the 2 WT are the survivors of a post hoc exclusion; clone and batch unassigned). Only NFE2L2 is in the authors' DE list (padj 1.43E-02, log2FC -0.58, above their stated alpha 0.01); NQO1 and HMOX1 are in neither list; one KO library of HMOX1 sits inside the WT range.

**New in this package (a decisive detail that was missing): the module's second human system, GSE126075 (hNSC and neurons, shWWOX, CAGE, one library per arm), read for these genes.** It was held in the module but never tabulated for the NRF2 axis. So under the module's own confound standard (`generation_it9_p1_20261030/README.md` 3a: a single-system organoid transcript row is usable only if a second independent system agrees in sign) exactly one gene, NFE2L2, qualifies; NQO1, G6PD and HMOX1 are demoted. This also answers the open lead DL-MECH-153 for the two systems read: G6PD and NFE2L2 do not move together. Caveat the module itself recorded: gate 1 of `hnpc_gse126075_it5_20261029/RESULTS.md` failed in the progenitor arm (WWOX not lower there, centred log2 about -0.02; lower in neurons, hard to size), and n = 1. The module already counts this deposit as the agreeing system for Q2 rows, so I follow that precedent for N22-A001 and state the strict alternative in the row (usable = no).

**Counter-evidence kept.** Nfe2l2 and Nqo1 transcripts are reported higher in early-passage Wwox-null MEFs (NEED-REDOX-NRF2:O2, manifest entry 14, one sample per genotype; I did not open the supplement) and MEF ROS is higher only at late passage. The organoid's other targets run both ways (SRXN1 1.33, GSR 1.20, MAFG 1.33 up; SLC7A11 0.65, BACH1 0.73, CUL3 0.73 down). Fly ROS runs counter-directionally across three systems (S111). The P47T cerebellum shows the Hallmark ROS set among the top enriched sets, direction not stated (V10). The organoid cannot test cytokines at all (IL6 0 in six libraries). Net: a mixed, transcript-only signature in which NFE2L2 lower is the single replicated direction; there is no NRF2 protein, nuclear localisation, NQO1 activity or ROS datum in any WWOX-deficient mammalian neural system. Role of every row: unknown (unresolved). Lower NFE2L2/NQO1 is compatible with a damage hypothesis (insufficient tone, arm R); higher HMOX1/SRXN1/GSR with a compensatory hypothesis; both with composition.

## 3. Smallest adequate representation

Not a gene set: no sourced NRF2-target member list exists in the repository and members are never invented; the 36-gene list was chosen post hoc. Not Q5 (glia/myelin, mouse cytokines up): adding an NFE2L2 seed there would count the omaveloxolone chain twice from two origins and mix a composition-confounded organoid transcript into a cytokine set. Instead:

1. **One new question Q8** (`proposed_question_Q8.tsv`, roles in `proposed_question_roles_Q8.tsv`: role unknown, `h_harm` opposes = raise NRF2 tone, `h_protect` mimics = lower it with no sourced benefit hypothesis, so it is kept but not handed over, like Q4/Q6/Q7). D6 stays closed for ROS: its reopen condition (a redox readout in a WWOX-deficient neural system) is not met by transcripts.
2. **Eight rows** (`proposed_seed_rows.tsv`, exact 20-column seeds.tsv schema, prefix N22-A): one usable gene seed (N22-A001 NFE2L2 down, expression, tier A), three demoted visible counter-rows (NQO1, HMOX1, G6PD), one carried mouse counter-row (Nfe2l2 up in MEF), two states without gene members that the adapter will count but not use (P47T ROS hallmark set, MEF late-passage ROS), and one Q5 not-evaluable state recording the cytokine floor. SLC7A11 is deliberately not added: it stays in S046 so one origin counts once.
3. Supporting additions: a guard `NRF2_TONE_UNRESOLVED` (same use as ISR_SIGN_UNRESOLVED), three `process_ids` STATE rows, HGNC ids (NFE2L2 7782, KEAP1 23177, HMOX1 5013 verified in the IT21 action table; NQO1 2874 and G6PD 4057 recalled, not verified).
Levels are never converted (all expression; MEF ROS is activity); one origin counts once (the two human deposits fold to one, per the IT21 convention; the fly H2O2 datum is the same under two needs).

## 4. What I expect to change in generated proposals (no engine run; expectation, not result)

- **Existing rows do not change.** Q5 rows IT21P2-0175, -0176, -0303 are driven by S084 to S086 and stay as they are; omaveloxolone stays blocked by its earlier qualification record (U59) and the other two by identity.
- **Additive Q8 rows, few.** A direct NFE2L2 seed can only meet the compound side as it stands: NFE2L2 has 3 signed families in `generation_it21_p2_20261103/results/actions/family_actions.tsv.gz` (omaveloxolone ChEMBL +1, omaveloxolone GtoPdb +1, ML385 GtoPdb -1), KEAP1 5 (bardoxolone methyl, dimethyl fumarate, diroximel fumarate, monomethyl fumarate, a peptide; all -1, i.e. KEAP1 inhibition), HMOX1 1 (a GtoPdb compound, no ChEMBL id), NQO1 0 and G6PD 0 (26 and 101 families with direction-unresolved association only). Expect the H_HARM arm to list omaveloxolone again (same blockers) and possibly the KEAP1-binding electrophiles via a KEAP1 to NFE2L2 edge, which I did not verify exists in the engine network; DMF/MMF carry earlier decisions (IT9 DROP of the old chain, IT10 conditional) and would be blocked by the earlier-decision and cluster rules. The H_PROTECT arm would list ML385 (identity not resolved) and be blocked as an arm with no stated benefit hypothesis. Net expectation: new rows, essentially no new hand-over.
- **What improves is the explanation, not the count:** the NRF2 proposals would be anchored on an upstream, human-level observation next to the mouse cytokine chain, with NFE2L2 dependence stated as the discriminating control and a sign-disagreement (NQO1, G6PD) shown rather than hidden.
- Demoted rows change nothing in the engine by construction.
