## 4. H1 to H5

**H1.** Clause by clause against the held text.
- "No seizures seen at home at 11 months while video-EEG recorded events": confirmed (E01).
- "Authors credit levetiracetam, phenobarbital and topiramate for early control as well as vigabatrin": confirmed (E07).
- "Age-referenced, no before/after contrast": true of the Results sentence only. The Discussion states that development "completely stagnated and regressed" after 8 months and that the EEG background slowed after 11 months. The H1 clause is incomplete (see E04, P-b, B4).
- "Coincides with spasm onset at 8 months": confirmed. Vigabatrin was added after that point. The authors tie the post-11-month EEG slowing to WWOX deficiency or epileptic encephalopathy (VEEG still detected spasms at 11 months).

**H2.** Confirmed against A1 README and `denominators_a1.tsv`: 14 reports with rows, 36 to 38 distinct children exposed to vigabatrin, a hormonal regimen, the ketogenic diet or cannabidiol, 6 with both endpoints (4 discordant, 2 concordant non-response). The seven-child series is named in A1 as outside the count. The 12-child series and the 50-person survey post-date A1 and are outside by perimeter. The perimeter is of exposed classes; the 12-child series probably contains children exposed to these classes (nine had spasms) but the abstract names no drug, so the perimeter is not the whole population.

**H3.** Confirmed. Cannabidiol in three reports (a single case, one series with four of thirteen children, and the cohort with 3 WWOX patients, 2 of 3 at least 50% responders). Perampanel in patients 6 and 13 of one series plus the adult case; ethosuximide in patient 6 of that series plus the adult case. No outcome per compound. Wording point: the cannabidiol cohort is a retrospective real-world study.

**H4.** Wang abstract confirmed for 2 seizure-free, 9 with seizures, 1 death; drugs and developmental timing not stated. The Specchio abstract is stated incompletely in H4: about 31% is the "slightly improved or better" threshold; "much improved or better" gives about 50% (-49.6%). Correlation 0.47 confirmed.

**H5.** Reproduced. PubMed tool: `WWOX AND cannabidiol`, `WWOX AND perampanel`, `WWOX AND ethosuximide`, and `WWOX AND (cannabidiol OR perampanel OR ethosuximide)` each returned 0. The tool honours OR and parentheses: its `query_translation` shows the group intact and automatic term mapping (each drug expanded to Supplementary Concept, All Fields and MeSH; WWOX mapped to the human protein concept plus All Fields). Second route, Europe PMC REST: title/abstract search returns 0 for each drug with WWOX; the full-text index returns 33 (cannabidiol), 17 (perampanel) and 16 (ethosuximide) hits, including the module's full-text sources (36779245, 40126049, 39507621, 32000863). Hence zero is an index statement (title, abstract, MeSH), not an absence statement. I did not screen the other full-text hits.

## 6. What the evidence supports on the traps named in the brief

- Causality from succession: handled correctly for E05 and the P-a wording. The remaining risk is the opposite one (E04, B4), reading the case as showing nothing when the authors themselves read regression.
- Home-observed absence as electroclinical freedom: correctly rejected (E01); the record is wider than stated (B2).
- Age-referenced statement read as stall or loss: the producer over-corrected. The Results sentence is age-referenced; the Discussion states loss in the authors' words, uninstrumented.
- Responder-development association read as a drug effect: not committed. P-d omits the ICISS association; Q7 mislabels E18.
- Polytherapy as efficacy (E20, E26, E21, E22, E23): correctly kept apart.
- Perimeter as whole population (H2): stated as a perimeter.
- KCNQ2 and TSC beyond design: E13, E14 and E17 are used as baselines and flagged as prevention or association. P-c's "poor-outcome majority" for KCNQ2 is the one slip.
- Safety conclusions from selected cases: Q5 and B6 keep the denominator and flag the card only.
