**2. Correct the causal conclusion: keep these propositions apart.** (a) Recovery of development as a consequence of seizure control is not demonstrated. (b) Some partial or transient responses were not accompanied by documented progress. (c) We do not know what the course would have been without the response. (d) It is not demonstrated that reducing seizures is irrelevant to development, preservation of functions or quality of life. A delay before onset shows a component of development not explained by later seizures; it does not exclude an additional contribution. Do not classify WOREE as a "fixed substrate" or an "irreversible course" on the basis of narrative descriptions and absence of complete responses. Reconcile the counts and state the perimeter: the 36 to 38 children of the earlier package concern the classes studied (vigabatrin, a hormonal regimen, the ketogenic diet, cannabidiol), not the whole WOREE population nor all antiseizure drugs.

**3. Specific discordances of the report.**
A. Vigabatrin case, PMID 39101447 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11298992/; complete read exists FTR-20261003-39101447-01; re-open only the decisive passages): the text separates absence of visible seizures at home from events still detected on video-EEG; do not code it as sustained electroclinical freedom; rebuild the chronology with respect to treatment, clinical observation, EEG and development without deducing causality from succession.
B. DEE comparisons: consume the existing analyses of EPISTOP, PREVeNT, UKISS and ICISS; keep apart randomised comparison, association between responders and development, preventive treatment, and treatment after onset. TSC is not a simple demonstration that seizure control produces development; KCNQ2 is not proof that a genetic lesion makes seizure control useless.
C. Drugs and mechanisms: correct "only lithium" by considering the ethosuximide experiment in PMID 32000863 (limits already qualified: `../compare_it28_p9_20261105/README.md`, `../ttype_20261107/output/README.md`). The d-APV block was done in the Wwox model: it does not validate felbamate or perampanel (targets, modes of action and contexts stay distinct); consume the NMDA package (`../nmda_inv05_20261107/output/README.md`) without duplicating it.

Safety: for the vigabatrin white-matter question reuse the B1 package (`../batch_sci_20261105/b1_whitematter/README.md`): two cases selected for the abnormality do not estimate a specifically higher risk in WOREE; keep denominators, co-therapies, symptoms and reversibility apart; produce no autonomous indication to exclude or change a therapy.

**4. Keep the different benefits distinguishable** (for the relevant cards and needs): clinical and electrographic seizures; acquisition of skills; retention of skills and regression; vigilance, sleep, comfort and participation; drug burden and adverse effects; quality of life and caregiver burden when measured. Do not fill missing outcomes by inference. A worsening of an age-standardised score does not by itself show loss of skills already acquired. An uncontrolled association between a drug and a benefit "beyond seizures" does not identify a direct drug action: mediation through seizures, changes in co-therapies and other confounders remain possible.
