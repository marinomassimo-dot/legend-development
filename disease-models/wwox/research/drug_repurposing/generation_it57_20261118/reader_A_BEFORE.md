# Reader A (BEFORE card): final message, copied unchanged (legend-delegate, sonnet requested; model and effort read back afterwards in result_it57.md)

**(1) First three actions**
1. Re-read the original paper's methods: genotype, seizure provocation, endpoint, variance, comparator, blinding. Fix the primary endpoint and the interaction analysis in writing before any animal is used.
2. Size the study for the interaction term, not the simple effect. Interactions need more animals, and a single-laboratory effect size is probably inflated, so I would also size on a deliberately shrunken estimate.
3. Register the protocol: randomisation, blinding, exclusion rules, same strain background and provocation as the original.

**(2) Arms, controls, measurements**
- A 2x2 factorial: WWOX-loss versus wild-type littermates, crossed with ethosuximide versus vehicle. Treated wild type is the key arm.
- The better-acting comparator drug in both genotypes, if resources allow.
- Controls: vehicle in both genotypes, littermates, sex as a factor, blinded scoring, randomised testing order.
- Measurements: the original seizure endpoint, plus the untreated genotype difference as the baseline. Drug exposure in both genotypes, to exclude a pharmacokinetic explanation. The genotype-by-treatment interaction with its confidence interval, and the wild-type effect reported on its own.

**(3) What it cannot show**
- It cannot show that T-type channel block is the mechanism; the card does not test the target.
- It cannot show relevance to patients or to spontaneous seizures.
- It cannot show that other compounds in the group behave the same way.
- It is not independent replication if it comes from the same laboratory.
- A non-significant interaction does not prove equal action. It only counts as support if the pre-set margin excludes a meaningful difference.
- It does not rank the drugs clinically.
- Nothing here is medical advice.

**(4) The decision I could not take from the card**
How large a wild-type effect, relative to the knockout effect, counts as "the same size" and so moves the thread out of its group. That margin must be set, and by whom, before unblinding. The card does not state it.
