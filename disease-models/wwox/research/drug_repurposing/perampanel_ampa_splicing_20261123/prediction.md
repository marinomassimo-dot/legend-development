# Does Wwox loss alter AMPA receptor desensitization through Gria2 splice choice?

SCI_CODEX, 23 Nov 2026. Locked before reading genotype-specific splice proportions. Primary question: both P17 neuronal-Wwox-deletion regions, hippocampus and cortex, should show enrichment of the slower-desensitizing GluA2 flip relative to flop variant if this is a shared substrate for sustained AMPA current. This hypothesis concerns a channel population potentially susceptible to perampanel. It makes no claim that perampanel preferentially binds a splice form or corrects the splicing. The existing GRIA2 Q/R editing question is separate.

Classify Ensembl release 92 mouse Gria2 cDNAs by a translated cassette spanning the multiple distinguishing residues in the primary GluA2 electrophysiology study; keep ambiguous or missing transcripts separate. Sum deposited transcript TPM within splice class and compute each library's flip share among classifiable Gria2 TPM. Require total classified >=10 TPM and >=80% of all Gria2 TPM in every library. Cross-check an independent mouse reference annotation if accessible. Do not silently include partial transcripts in either class. Report transcript IDs and expression changes, both region-specific phenotypes, and two additional genotypes (global-null and neural-progenitor-null hippocampus) as separate secondary contrasts.

Primary decision: a mean KO minus control increase of at least five percentage points in each neuronal region, with no leave-one-library-out change of direction, supports a shared shift large enough for follow-up. The five-point floor is a chosen prioritization criterion. The opposite stable effect of at least that magnitude in both regions weighs against this directional mechanism. Smaller or discordant results are reported regionally. Compare observable full-length coding forms and bound unclassified expression by assigning it to either class separately in each group. All contrasts are derived from quantified transcripts rather than junction counts. Models of selection and downstream pharmacology must also consider developmental state, cell composition, auxiliary subunits and the difference between heteromeric native receptors and recombinant unedited GluA2.
