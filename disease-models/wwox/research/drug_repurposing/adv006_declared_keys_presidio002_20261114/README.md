## Answer first
1. **The presidio's cross-check is right, and it does not show an inconsistency.** For all 7 SCI42 explanations the `requests_merged` tokens equal the `measurement_id` of the explanation's crosswalk row exactly, and are a strict subset of the rows that `source_names` reaches through the SCI42 ids named in `needs_new_measurement` (`keys.tsv`, column `comparison`; script result 7 of 7).
2. **The two keys answer different questions.** `requests_merged` says which source requests were folded into a row: for an explanation it is the one-hop primary mapping of each SCI42 measurement the explanation names (SCI42 M03 to ADV M01, M04 to M02, M05 to M04, M06 to M03, M08 to M09, M01 to M05, M09 to M11, M07 to M06, M02 to M08). `source_names` lists every name a row is known by in a source, including a part of a measurement.
3. **Each of the extra rows is one of three things, none an error of the token set** (`extras_and_reason`, each tied to a phrase the script asserts is in the cell):
   * **M07** (co-recorded covariates) for E-supply, E-response and E-artefact: a component. SCI42 M03 asks access resistance and noise reported per genotype, and SCI42 M05 is read with input resistance and cell size; ADV-006 split those out as M07.
   * **M26** (KCC2/NKCC1 protein) for E-chloride, E-chloride-secondary, E-compensation and E-maturation: a component. SCI42 M02 is "a programme that contains M03, M01, M07 and protein across ages"; ADV-006 maps its age series to M08 and its protein part to M26, but the crosswalk row of SCI42 M02 names only M08. This is the one place where the tokens are narrower than the package's own content.
   * **M22** (pH): a false hit. Its cell says "not SCI42 chloride M01" (the GluN2B package's M01).
4. **Which key to use.** For a registration of the SCI42 explanations: read `requests_merged` as the declared primary mapping, and add the components of `keys.tsv` (M07 for the explanations that need SCI42 M03 or M05, M26 for those that need SCI42 M02) as a stated rule, not by pattern-matching `source_names` (the pattern also returns M22). A reader that follows `requests_merged` alone sees the measurement each explanation names and not the covariates or the protein arm that the same SCI42 row contains; that is a narrower view, not a wrong one.
5. **The levers differ in kind.** The lever token (`sci47_inhibitory#levers:Lx`) gives one row per lever (L1 M16, L2 M17, L3 M05 and M20, L4 M18, L5 M19, L6 none). The rows that mention a lever in prose are mostly gating or comparator rows, which is a different relation. The keys that SCI47 itself declares for L1 are wider than its lever token: the O-ids in L1's status cell (O01, O04, O06) are tokens of `requests_merged` in M01, M02, M03 and M04 (`keys.tsv`, lever rows, column `source_names_or_O_declared_rows`). L2 has no O-id in any cell. The attachment rules for L1 and L2 are in `levers_owner_confirmation_presidio001_20261114/confirmation.tsv` (part 2).
6. **Does `measurements.tsv` need a dated correction?** No edit of a cell. The M26 gap (SCI42 M02) is a one-line dated note on the ADV-006 README, with the rule of answer 4; the other corrections found while reading it (M02 `interpretable_after`, CF01 typing, CF23) are in the PRESIDIO-001 folder (part 4).
