# Blind verifier verdicts: Fucke 2007/2008 Heidelberg thesis, Ba2+ and input resistance in rat L2/3 pyramidal neurons (2026-11-21)

Source read: only the thesis PDF (Fucke2008_thesis_heiDOK8349.pdf, 140 PDF pages). Page locators are given as "printed N / PDF M"; the offset is constant, PDF = printed + 8 (checked on ten Ba-bearing pages). Verifier: sonnet, effort high. Reference genotype class only, no dose, no recommendation.

## Answer first

For the youngest group in which barium was tested (the thesis calls it "p14"; there is no Ba experiment at p8), the thesis supports one measured, paired, within-cell result: 50 uM BaCl2 in the bath raised somatic input resistance (Rin) from 161 +/- 7 MOhm to 206 +/- 10 MOhm (p < 0.001, n = 16 cells from 5 animals), in current-clamp whole-cell recordings at 35 C from layer 2/3 pyramidal neurons of Wistar rats, parasagittal 300 um slices of "the rat cortex" (somatosensory cortex declared in the Introduction, no finer area). DERIVED from those two means: the Ba-blocked fraction of input conductance at rest is 1 - 161/206 = 21.8% (+/- 5.1 percentage points, SEM-level, independence assumed, which is conservative for paired data). The older tested group "p28" gives 1 - 50/93 = 46.2% (+/- 5.5). What the thesis does not support: (a) an age window: no group states its animal age range (only the overall "p8-p45" and the group labels), so "p14" cannot be shown to equal P13-P17; (b) a leak-versus-inward-rectifier split from Rin alone: the thesis states Ba-sensitivity of both a model "leak" conductance and the KIR conductance, but never calls the leak part K2P-type, and its fitted conductances in "uS" cannot be absolute whole-cell values next to the Rin values (units and arithmetic below); (c) Ba in TTX: TTX was not stated for the Ba experiments and action potentials are visible in the Ba traces, so TTX was evidently absent (INFERENCE); (d) one concentration only (50 uM), one cohort per age, no washout or time course, no Vrest-after-Ba value; (e) a journal version of the Ba result: none found (two-plus routes, see Q7; absence is not proof). The p14 pre-drug Rin of the Ba cohort (161) differs from the p14 all-cell mean in Table 2.1 (150 +/- 4, n = 57(16)); the Ba cohort is a 16-cell subset from 5 animals.
