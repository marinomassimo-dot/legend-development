# Does WWOX loss create an AMPA receptor population sensitive to low-dose memantine?

23 November, SCI_CODEX. A 2025 recombinant channel study reports that memantine can block calcium-permeable AMPARs, with large differences by voltage, TARP and Q/R status. This gives a target-level connection to the perampanel project that is separate from the GRIA2 flip/flop cassette. Memantine can also block GluA1 homomers without any change at the GluA2 Q/R site. Before inspecting disease values or digitizing curves, test the direction of relative GluA2 supply in untreated Wwox models and bound the potential impact on total AMPA current under explicit assay conditions.

1. For the ordinary recombinant configurations in the main figures, recover reported IC50 values and the lowest nonzero concentrations actually plotted. Distinguish ordinary GluA1 homomers, unedited and edited GluA2, mixed coexpression, TARP γ2/γ8 and the −100 mV condition with cyclothiazide. Separate any engineered pathogenic GRIA2 missense comparison from WWOX disease. Model 0.36, 0.7, 1 and 10 µM free concentrations with varying Hill slope (0.5, 1 and 1.5 unless the study fixes or estimates one) and CP fractions of total predrug AMPA current (0, 0.1, 0.5 and 1). Allow complete maximal block to derive a generous conditional upper limit for the unmeasured Emax. Quantify the minimum fraction of sensitive AMPA current needed for a 10% total-current reduction at the adult CSF reference. Assay extrapolations remain explicit. Use empirical low-dose points if retrievable, do not impute observations.
2. In neuronal-deletion mouse cortex and hippocampus, global-null hippocampus and human week-15 organoids, measure log2(1+GRIA1) minus log2(1+GRIA2). Repeat with mean log abundance of GRIA1/3/4 relative to GRIA2 and give both TARPs (CACNG2 and CACNG8) with an expression floor of 1 in original linear units. Require both indices to rise by ≥0.25 units in both neuronal-deletion regions, remaining positive on every library omission, before prioritizing a broad relative loss of GluA2 supply. The global-null and human results are independent model checks. In the pooled hippocampal nuclei, describe the excitatory and inhibitory classes separately and do not test cells as animals. Changes in mRNA ratios cannot identify surface stoichiometry or unedited GRIA2. Report the earlier human Q/R coverage limit and the receptor configurations that remain possible even when editing is normal.
3. The hypothesis ultimately predicts a disease-selective memantine-sensitive current after isolating AMPA from NMDA responses at relevant free concentrations, with sensitivity to a calcium-permeable AMPAR comparator and comparable initial firing. A large response needing an unrelated GRIA2 mutation or an extreme voltage would lower the priority for WWOX. Distinguish these predictions from perampanel inhibition of AMPARs across permeability states.
