# Does WWOX loss selectively expose an AMPA target for memantine?

**Decision, 23 November 2026.** Deprioritize a general increase in memantine-sensitive AMPA current inferred from WWOX loss. The prespecified relative GluA2-supply signature went in the opposite direction in both neuronal-deletion mouse regions. Human organoids gave a narrower, positive GluA1 contrast. Direct channel pharmacology supports a conditional hypothesis in cells with a substantial contribution from calcium-permeable AMPARs, but measurements below 1 µM and measurements in WWOX cells are missing. This decision concerns a potential mechanism beyond seizure control. [Prespecified analysis](prediction.md) · [Carrillo et al., primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12217772/)

## Two routes to the same current

A neuron could assemble surface AMPA receptors lacking GluA2, or incorporate unedited GluA2(Q). Both can carry calcium. Either route could change the fraction of total AMPA current susceptible to memantine. Perampanel gives a pharmacological comparison because its action on AMPARs extends across permeability states. Gene-level RNA estimates probe only the relative supply of subunit transcripts. The earlier analysis recovered 14 splice-supported reads at the human GRIA2 editing site across all six libraries, all edited, with insufficient depth per library. The separate flip/flop cassette regulates gating and answers a different question. [Q/R evidence](../perampanel_gria2_editing_20261123/README.md) · [Flip/flop evidence](../perampanel_ampa_splicing_20261123/README.md)

Values are changes in differences of log2(1 + abundance), using TPM in mouse tissue and CPM in human organoids. Omitting one library at a time preserved both negative signs in both neuronal-deletion tissues. The human first index stayed positive (+0.168 to +0.434), whereas the second crossed zero (−0.126 to +0.484). Human GRIA2 itself changed by only +0.052. CACNG8 was detectable throughout and increased in human organoids (+0.532), while GRIA4 and CACNG2 fell below 1 CPM in at least one library. Differences in age, cellular composition and tissue can explain disagreement. In pooled mouse hippocampal nuclei the first index changed by +0.059 in excitatory and +0.041 in inhibitory neurons, the second by +0.030 and −0.115. One pool per genotype limits this check. Translation, surface trafficking, RNA editing and current contribution can all diverge from these indices. ## Dose, voltage and composition

Carrillo et al. report central IC50 estimates of 10 and 15 µM for GluA1 with γ8 and γ2, respectively, and 48–49 µM for GluA2(Q) with those TARPs. Coexpression of GluA1 and GluA2(Q) with γ2 gave 23.5 µM and may produce several assemblies. Edited GluA2(R)/γ2 alone gave 1,129 µM with incomplete inhibition. GluA2(Q) with cyclothiazide changed from 48 µM at −60 mV to 4.1 µM at −100 mV. An unrelated pathogenic GRIA2 substitution, Q607E, gave 25 and 5.7 µM with γ2 at the two voltages. All are recombinant measurements. The train experiment (brief pulses at 10 Hz) used 10 µM memantine. Fig. 1E/G/M and 2C/E have cell measurements starting at 1 µM. The observed points at the lowest dose permit appreciable block in some conditions. Their existence motivates measuring the lower concentration directly. [Primary study, Figs. 1, 2, 4](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-60543-5)

For the most sensitive ordinary TARP-associated configuration, GluA1/γ8, at 0.36 µM a full-efficacy Hill model gives 3.47% block at unit slope, 15.95% at slope 0.5 and 0.68% at slope 1.5. If the rest of the predrug current is edited GluA2(R)/γ2, the shallow-slope model needs at least 58.1% of the current in the sensitive configuration to reach 10% total block. A unit slope never reaches that threshold in the assumed mixture. For comparison, pure GluA2(Q)/γ2 gives 0.73% at unit slope. Even the −100 mV cyclothiazide assay gives 8.07% at unit slope. Mixtures with that extreme condition use the −60 mV edited comparator only as a numerical sensitivity. Models assume zero basal block and permit complete maximal block for each component; results vary with slope, voltage, gating, potency, efficacy and current fraction. These are scenarios conditional on point estimates. The adult reference is a measured *mean CSF concentration*, distinct from pediatric brain interstitial exposure or free bath concentration. **Independent graphic check.** The text, caption and published reviewer correspondence give 697 ± 149 µM for the GluA1 + edited GluA2(R) coexpression. The Fig. 2E fitted line reaches about 72% absolute inhibition at 697 µM. Log interpolation between two pairs of unobscured points on the fitted line places 50% absolute inhibition at 90–93 µM. At 12:03 UTC the independent laboratory reviewer returned an ACK, found a graphical crossing near 83 µM and about 72% inhibition at 697 µM, and estimated half of the fitted asymptote near 45–46 µM. Differences in reading the line do not reconcile the order-of-magnitude discrepancy. The reviewer identified cell measurements at 1, 10, 50, 100, 500 and 1,000 µM. An adult reference of 0.36 µM falls below all those doses. The public author response describes moving the edited-subunit panel from supplementary material to the main figure; it does not resolve the discrepancy. We exclude this configuration from the quantitative mixtures. No revised potency is asserted. The graphic observations concern only one mixed configuration. **Discriminating prediction.** In identified WWOX-deficient and isogenic control neurons matched on developmental state and initial firing, isolate AMPA currents and measure the calcium-permeable fraction and block during the same stimulation protocol at 0.36, 0.7 and 1 µM. Compare with a selective calcium-permeable AMPAR blocker and with perampanel. The hypothesis predicts that the excess block in deficient cells tracks the independently measured permeable-current fraction, and diminishes when that fraction is removed. Direct NMDA responses constitute a separate comparator. A general reduction in firing or a higher GRIA1/GRIA2 RNA contrast on its own cannot establish this mechanism.
