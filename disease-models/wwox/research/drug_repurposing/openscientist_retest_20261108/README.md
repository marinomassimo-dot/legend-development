## 3 · The five distinctions asked for

1. **Generative value of the reports.** High where they supply a design or a link the module lacked (items 1, 4, 8, 11, 14, 15, 16 above are design or link additions: an AMPA arm, a PDK1/PDH step, an inositol arm with its own GSK-3 read-out, a nuclear-export lever, a cortical sag-block precedent, an allele-class gradient). Low as evidence: none of the eleven measures anything of its own; every WWOX datum they use comes from three papers (the neuron-deleted slice paper, the Synapsin-Cre myelination paper, the lithium/ethosuximide seizure paper).
2. **Reliability of their statements.** Every report contained at least one statement contradicted or overreached by its own cited source (counted in each README section (d)); identifiers were sound (all PMIDs resolved to the described paper; one, 21690096, is retracted); the most frequent faults were absence claims with no stated perimeter, a background sentence quoted as a finding, a transfer from another system or cell type presented as support, and an internally inconsistent verdict (for example "cannot be attributed" in the summary and "firmly attributable" in the conclusion).
3. **Quality of the previous hand-off to delegates.** The briefs wrote that the originals were not needed; they described them correctly in outline and corrected the right inferences, but they dropped about a quarter of the content (204 of 778 items omitted, 130 more narrowed). The omitted material was mostly side arms and named compounds; the omission cost the AMPA gap, the retraction detail, the Pdk1 datum, the HDAC5 opposite-sign datum, the input-resistance fact, the cortical ivabradine precedent and the inositol and EAE counter-arguments, and left some earlier files carrying a wording the full reading corrects (section 5).
4. **Difference made by reading the originals in full.** No compound moved. Three priorities sharpened (AMPA arm; HDAC5 sign check before any inhibitor or degrader arm; PDK1/PDH step), one premise weakened (independence of the T-type panel), two precedents added (cortical ivabradine; GAERS authors' reading), four absence claims bounded.
5. **Observed cost.** `COST_NOTE.md`.
