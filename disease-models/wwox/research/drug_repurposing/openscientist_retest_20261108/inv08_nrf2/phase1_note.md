# INV-08 NRF2, Phase 1 note

* The input is an OpenScientist report (138 lines) answering whether NRF2 is impaired, compensatory or context-dependent in WWOX-deficient neural systems. It was read in full.
* Structure: question, summary, seven findings F001-F007, a pathway diagram, a three-hypothesis table, preconditions for KEAP1/NRF2 intervention, an evidence table of 19 PMIDs, limitations, four-tier follow-up experiments, conclusion.
* Measured versus reasoned: nothing is measured by the report. The central claim (NRF2 impaired) is stated as mechanistic inference; the report says repeatedly that no NRF2, KEAP1, glutathione, ROS or ferroptosis datum exists for any WWOX model.
* Reported facts attributed to papers: GSK-3beta elevation and lithium seizure rescue in Wwox-null mice (WWOX datum); everything linking GSK-3beta to NRF2 (Fyn/Src, Tyr568) is from non-WWOX, non-neural systems; cell-type redox vulnerability from Gclm-KO, TBI and a schizophrenia review; NRF2 dependence of OPC differentiation from cuprizone and ischemia models; reductive stress from cardiac models.
* Counter-weight rows kept: reductive stress (F002), acquired-versus-genetic caveat (F001), compensatory-activation alternative, cross-tissue and species caveats.
* Internal inconsistencies in the text itself: header says "35 papers reviewed", the table lists 19; the zinc-deficiency/diabetes model (F004) is later grouped with cardiac, hepatic and fibroblast systems (Limitations); the "knowledge base" keyword audit is cited without an identifier; the decision matrix is said to "transfer directly" to Wwox yet no Wwox;Nfe2l2 double mutant is stated to exist; F005 shows vulnerability to glutathione deficit, which the summary then treats as NRF2-related; the report presents NAC as strictly NRF2-independent although this is asserted, not cited.
* Hedging pattern: the Summary says "most likely impaired" and the Conclusion says "weight of mechanistic inference"; the model diagram labels the NRF2 impairment "PREDICTED".
* Two PMIDs (the schizophrenia redox review and the miR-155-5p paper) carry high numbers; identifiers recorded exactly as written in Phase 1; the Phase 2 identity check resolved both (a 2026 Cells review and a 2025 Brain Behav Immun paper), confirmed by the verifier.
