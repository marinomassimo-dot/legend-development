Europe PMC core record of 21690096 lists "Expression of concern in" PMID 26773125 (J Biol Chem 2016 Jan 15;291:1199) and "Retraction in" PMID 28159761. The type is plain `retraction`, not a partial-retraction type, and the notice carries the whole article title, so the record-level reading is a retraction of the whole article.

Verdict D02: SUPPORTED at metadata depth (retraction of the article as a whole is what the records show; the reason is unreached and stays unreached). INFERENZA "links 2 and 3 have no usable support in the report" is SUPPORTED as a statement about the report: the report cites only 21690096 for GSK-3beta to Src import and Tyr568 export, and 23251339 as "corroboration" (see D03).

So the report's three-region statement is the paper's own claim, and the item's "the report's three-region claim is not located" is wrong. What the paper measures is a pSer9 proxy, not activity; the claim "validated causal node" is still not established (lithium is not GSK-3-selective; no genetic arm).

The item's "one blot" and the module's "single-lane blot per region" are about the displayed blot; the source says four experiments. Item wording should say "one representative blot shown".
