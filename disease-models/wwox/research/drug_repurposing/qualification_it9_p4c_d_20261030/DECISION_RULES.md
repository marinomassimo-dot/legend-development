# IT9 P4c-D decision rules (written 2026-10-30, after the selection was run and before any qualification datum was fetched or read)

Scope: the families chosen by `SELECTION_NOTE.md` (Part 1 rules, committed 1278c5d8; Part 2 holds the chosen set). Not medical advice. A database or enzyme measure is not therapeutic benefit; a compatible action of a context-free signed network is not a predicted benefit. Reference genotype: a WWOX-DEE genotype class.

Allowed decisions: DROP (a decisive datum is named), KEEP-AS-PROPOSAL (specific next experiment, readout, control, falsifier), REFERENCE-ONLY, INCONCLUSIVE (the exact missing datum). "Insufficient support" and "no activity" are labelled differently. Scores are separate and never summed: biological promise (given only the WWOX-seed link and target action), depth of evidence, feasibility, priority of the next experiment; each LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH with the reason.

Rules from P4b-A (kept): R1 exposure drop only on a primary-source statement; R3 target-engagement drop only if the declared target has no measured activity of the dosed molecule; R5 scores separate; R6 seed-direction conditionals never resolved for convenience; R7 spontaneous-report counts are counts; R8 HPA is adult and cell-line/tissue data, no developmental value claimed from it.

New for this round (the v3 path removes mode errors; it does not verify the primary record):

N1 **Edge reading (decisive edge).** For each candidate the decisive edge(s) are the terminal T edge into the seed and, when a P edge precedes it, that P edge. For each, the OmniPath record is fetched live (source database, references, sign, curation effort) and the primary paper behind it is read at the deepest depth available (PMC full text if open, else abstract; stated per paper). Four questions are answered per edge and kept as separate fields: (a) does the paper describe transcriptional regulation of the SEED gene (not a protein-activity, binding, or secreted-protein effect)? (b) in which cell type/species/context (neural or glial or immune-like cell relevant to the seed, e.g. microglia, astrocyte, macrophage for IL6/IL1B/TNF; a neuron for NPY; a cancer line, an overexpression system, a reporter in a heterologous cell is NOT a neural/glial/immune context)? (c) does the sign in the paper match the sign coded in the network? (d) is the COMPOUND's action on its target reported (in the paper, or in a separate paper found by search) to change that readout, in a relevant cell? (d) is the strongest link and is NOT assumed from (a) to (c).
N2 **Decision consequences of N1.** DROP on the edge if (a) is NO for the terminal edge and no alternative T edge from the same target to a seed exists in the network; DROP if (c) is NO (sign contradicts); an edge with (a) YES but only in non-relevant contexts (cancer line, overexpression) is a stated limit and caps promise at LOW-MEDIUM; (b) relevant AND (c) YES AND (d) YES in a relevant cell is the only combination that lets promise be MEDIUM or above. If the paper cannot be opened beyond the abstract, the answer is stated as abstract-level and a DROP on N1 alone is not allowed (INCONCLUSIVE with the exact missing datum).
N3 **Target expression (item 6).** Expression of the main target in the relevant cell types and age: HPA single-nucleus adult brain (microglia, astrocytes, oligodendrocyte lineage, neurons), the two local WWOX-deficient datasets (organoid GSE156243, hNPC GSE126075; descriptive, 'ABSENT' is not zero), and published developmental data found by search (state depth). The seed gene (IL6, IL1B, TNF, NPY ...) is checked in the same datasets: a seed that is not expressed in the compartment where the target is expressed is a stated limit.
N4 **BET and other class-specific safety.** Class toxicity found in the label/trials of the compound or its class (for BET inhibitors: thrombocytopenia, gastrointestinal events, retinal/ocular findings; for others as found) is reported with counts and source and weighs on feasibility and on the paediatric/chronic question; it is not a DROP unless a primary source states the toxicity is dose-limiting at exposures needed for target engagement in the nervous system (R1 form).
N5 **KEEP-AS-PROPOSAL** requires: N1 gives (b) relevant context at least reported for the TARGET-to-seed regulation OR a stated, feasible experiment that tests exactly that edge in a WWOX-deficient neural/glial system; the target is expressed in a relevant cell type; no decisive datum under R1, R3, N2; the next experiment names a readout that separates adaptive from harmful valence (not only the seed transcript) and a falsifier.
N6 **Valence.** Seeds in Q5 (IL6, IL1B, TNF up in mouse) and Q4 (NPY, GRIN2C) have unresolved valence (inflammatory cytokines can be adaptive, e.g. in repair, or harmful); no decision may assume that lowering a cytokine transcript is beneficial.
N7 **Read receipts** are staged only for papers read at full-text depth (all sections of the main text), next free FTR sequence for that PMID, validated with `staged_receipt_check.py --artifact-root <root> --check-doi`. Reading selected passages or abstracts stages none.
