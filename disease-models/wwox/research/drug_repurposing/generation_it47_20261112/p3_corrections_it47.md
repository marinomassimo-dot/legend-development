# IT47 P3: two possible jumps in the experimental reasoning

Non-canonical working note. Not medical advice. Historical texts are left as written; each correction is a dated update in place and is propagated to the live consumers. The scientific verification is SCI47 package A (`inhibitory_defect_sci47_20261112/`, in preparation at the time of writing).

## 1. The S5 amplitude and frequency wording
Source: PMID 34634460 as read by the IT46 reader (text only; figure legends, n and the statistics not in the delivered text). Printed means: amplitude 57.3 ± 31.0 to 27.5 ± 19.4 pA, **a fall of 52 percent**; frequency 10.3 ± 11.5 to 8.4 ± 10.0 Hz, **a fall of 18 percent**. The IT46 phrase "in frequency by about a fifth" meant a fall of about a fifth and read as a fall to a fifth; and the sentence that the amplitude falls more than the frequency "points at the receptor response or the GABA content" was an inference from two means whose spreads are as large as the means, over pooled events, with no statistics in the delivered text and a detection-threshold effect that can move both numbers. Corrected now (certain): the numbers and their meaning are explicit in V21, the IT46 README and `s5_case/result_it46.md` (dated update). Not resolved here (SCI47): whether the amplitude falls more than the frequency, on the figure, the legend and the statistics.
## 2. The two-lever design
`p9_two_levers_it46.md` received a dated update on four inferences: non-additivity does not show a shared mechanism or shared cells (ceiling, measurement limits, exposure, power, convergence), nor does additivity show independence; equal cellular variables do not make two network manipulations equivalent, so the non-specific arm is a comparator with stated limits and not a null; the sign of GABA's action needs E_GABA read with rest, threshold, conductance, time and activity-dependent chloride loading; a "larger GABA-specific effect" keeps the comparator's limits. IT46-Q04 carries the same limit.
