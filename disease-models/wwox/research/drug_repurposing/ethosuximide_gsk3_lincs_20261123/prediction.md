# Does low-concentration ethosuximide mimic a validated GSK3 inhibitor response?

SCI_CODEX, 23 November 2026. GSE92742 perturbagen metadata, examined before loading expression, lists ethosuximide at 10 µM in MCF7 at 6 and 24 hours and PC3 at 24 hours. The three original cortical markers are absent from the gene metadata, and LEF1 is inferred. This mandates a different readout using only directly measured landmark transcripts. These non-neural cells can test the breadth of a pharmacologic response at a concentration already tested in a separate nuclear beta-catenin assay. They cannot rule on a 100–700 µM neural effect.

Within each cell and time, take all SB-216763 profiles at 10 µM, using the median across profiles if a compound has more than one profile at the matched time/dose. Test held-out CHIR-99021 and GSK-3-inhibitor-IX separately at 10 µM in those same cell/time contexts wherever deposited. Verify distinct chemical identifiers. No dose substitution. Use the full measured-landmark Pearson correlation and a secondary score based on the 20 most induced and 20 most repressed SB-216763 landmarks. Ask whether each held-out agent and ethosuximide exceeds the 95th percentile of same-dose/time/cell reference chemicals by both metrics, with positive scores on both. Compare against up to 150 random chemicals with the same perturbation-plate prefix as the query where available and 200 independent random compound names from the full matched condition, fixed seed and removal of the named compounds. Keep within-plate and across-plate results separate and with fewer than 20 distinct same-plate chemicals mark the plate comparison limited. Use the recorded level-5 replicate and quality metadata at face value and report the timepoints separately. Hold-out validation requires at least one structurally distinct inhibitor that clears both metrics across the full chemical background; where a sufficient plate control exists, require it to clear both there for a confident cross-batch interpretation.

An ethosuximide response consistent with the inhibitor reference in at least two validated contexts would justify testing broad GSK3-like pharmacology at 10 µM alongside a neural-specific route. A negative in validated contexts would limit that claim. If references fail or coverage is limited, record the corresponding result as uninformative. Similar RNA profiles would not establish direct enzyme inhibition or a therapeutic direction.
