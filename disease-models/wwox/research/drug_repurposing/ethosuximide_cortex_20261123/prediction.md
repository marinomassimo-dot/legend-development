# Does a direct cortical ethosuximide exposure reproduce the N2A response?

Fixed before extracting any panel expression from GSE110256. Local time-stamped Git history records this freeze. This is a compartment-transfer check for the four-gene mechanism in the earlier ethosuximide dossier. DIV21 mouse primary cortical cultures received drug or vehicle for 8 h. One library represents ethosuximide. GSE110256 contains nine vehicle libraries and one library for each of the other treatments. Genotype is ordinary mouse. The sample identifier ending in 200 is a library label. Unknown dose precludes interpreting a human exposure window.

Primary comparison: subtract the per-gene median of nine vehicles from ethosuximide on log2(CPM+1), compute the mean over Ccng2, Cdkn1b, Rbl2, Gadd45a and subtract the mean over Cdkn1a, Bcl2l11, Cat, Sod2, Eif4ebp1. Retain all nine if at least one CPM in median vehicle for each. Require at least three of four target increases, positive primary mean and positive target-minus-comparator contrast larger than all nine leave-one-vehicle-out pseudo-treatment contrasts. Repeat with median ratio normalized expression, scaling to median library size, and compare the two contrasts within the screen's other drug exposures and within the HI.3140 identifier block. Block membership describes an identifier, not verified biological pairing. More than one library for the same drug is necessary for an independent precision estimate. Do not select active comparator drugs after seeing effects. A positive result prioritizes stage-specific drug testing. Failure weakens transfer to the measured mixed culture at its sampled time and exposure. Neither outcome decides progenitor-specific rescue.
