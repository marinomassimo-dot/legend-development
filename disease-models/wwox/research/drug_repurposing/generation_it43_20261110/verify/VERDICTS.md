## Disagreements with the rows as given (most important first)

1. S9 devimistat (4 PDK rows), celecoxib (PDK1) and OSU-03012 (PDK1): none of the sources I reached reports pyruvate dehydrogenase kinase inhibition. OSU-03012 and celecoxib are described as inhibitors of the other kinase (3-phosphoinositide-dependent kinase 1, PDPK1, whose HGNC alias symbol is PDK1); devimistat is described as acting on PDH and KGDH, with PDK linked only as a hypothesis in which PDH phosphorylation is increased. These rows should not count as PDK inhibitors (whether DGIdb mapped them wrongly is NOT_REACHED).
2. S9 PDK symbol clash is real and registry-confirmed (HGNC: PDK1 = pyruvate dehydrogenase kinase 1; PDPK1 has alias_symbol PDK1; UniProt O15530 synonym PDK1): any row whose only link is "PDK1" with a blank action, including the `DTC`-sourced rows in 3d, is at risk.
3. P05, P07 (AZD7545, "Human"): species not supported by the reachable abstract record; its MeSH shows rat and no human. Assay words and the numbers themselves are not in the abstract. Treat species as NOT_REACHED, not as Human.
4. P05-P10 comparison: the two sets (AZD7545 from 14641019; VER-246608 from 25404640) are not shown to be the same assay type; the AZD7545 assay was not reached, and for lipoamide-site compounds the substrate (E1 vs E1/E2) changes the answer (Nov3r example in 25404640). Do not rank across the sets.
5. AZD7545 is not a pan-isoform inhibitor: the abstract title says "selective inhibitor of pyruvate dehydrogenase kinase 2"; the abstract names no PDK1, PDK3 or PDK4; 25404640 notes lipoamide-site compounds can stimulate PDK3/PDK4 in biochemical assays. The absence of PDK3/PDK4 AZD7545 rows is consistent with that and should be explained, not filled.
6. P01-P04 (sodium dichloroacetate): the four isoform rows are one curated mechanism statement with no affinity and no PMID; DCA is called "a weak inhibitor of PDK" with "lack of specificity" (25404640); the molecule appears as a salt (sodium) and, in the DGIdb S9 rows, also as "Dichloroacetic Acid" (DTC): salt/parent duplicate.
7. P06-P10: each affinity is one datum shown as pIC50 and as a Table 1 IC50 in the paper (35, 84, 40, 91 nM reproduce 7.46, 7.08, 7.40, 7.04): not two measurements. PDK2 rests on n=2.
8. Part 3: R02, R20, R27, R28 (PIP2, cAMP x2, cGMP) are activators; they run against a "blocker wanted" direction and should be excluded or labelled as the opposite sign.
9. Part 3: R01, R11, R30 are one molecule (ivabradine, parent and HCl) on one target; zatebradine (4 rows), ZD7288 (3), cilobradine (3), MEL57A (3), EC18 (2) are repeated across isoforms. Counting rows as molecules inflates the list (30 rows, 15 entities).
