**2. Premises that change the experiment.**
A. GSK3 beta S9A: what makes it resistant is loss of the inhibitory Ser9 phosphorylation, not pharmacological inhibition in general. PMID 23099259 does not show an allele resistant to the proposed drugs. S9A is not conclusive proof of target attribution; a drug-resistant allele would need validation for each compound; alternatively consider acute isoform perturbations, genetic rescue and convergent pharmacology, with their limits.
B. Selectivity: CHIR99021/CT99021 alone does not separate GSK3 beta from alpha. Qualify separately selectivity between kinases, between isoforms and between substrates; do not move biochemical selectivity to cellular selectivity without checking.
C. The WWOX premise: reuse the audit of PMID 32000863: Ser9 is a regulatory indicator from a limited blot, not a direct activity measure on the different substrates. Distinguish the score after a fixed PTZ dose from an effectively measured convulsive threshold, spontaneous seizures and development. An effect also present in controls limits WWOX specificity without cancelling the knockout result. Lithium in the Wwox-null mouse is not exclusive proof that GSK3 beta causes the seizures.
D. Model and myelin: verify the Cre driver of the original model (PMID 33914858: Synapsin I-Cre line for the neuronal arm; Nestin-Cre, Olig2-Cre, GFAP-Cre arms): do not attribute a Nex/Nestin model without support; consume the qualifications already available on Syn1-Cre (`../nb4_delta_20261107/output/README.md`) and on the glial knockouts; do not describe WWOX hypomyelination automatically as an intrinsic oligodendrocyte defect.

**3. Separability, without presupposing it.** Compare only the precedents needed: PMID 21319221 (GSK3 inhibition and Wnt3a with opposite effects on oligodendroglial differentiation) and PMID 33015737 (SB216763 cancels the benefit tied to Foxg1 reduction) as already read in the myelin package; PMID 15791206 (Ser9/Ser21 regulation and Wnt regulation are not interchangeable); PMID 33197065 (the role of GSK3 alpha in LTD does not license attributing every synaptic effect to GSK3 beta). Classify dose, duration, developmental phase, cell type and model. A rise in beta-catenin is a signalling change; its functional meaning must be verified. That partial inhibition could keep a benefit while avoiding other effects is a prediction to test. A substrate-competitive inhibitor is not automatically selective for one branch.

**4. The most concrete generative lead.** Qualify the GSK3-PI4KIIalpha-NMDAR branch of PMID 32463939 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32463939/): identity of the target, system, knockdown, rescue with a shRNA-resistant construct and a mutant of the phosphorylation site; keep the null result on AMPA currents. Ask: could the branch contribute to WWOX pathological activity? Can it be modulated without compromising a needed synaptic function? Is there a usable probe or only a genetic possibility? What does it add to the direct NMDAR block already studied (`../nmda_inv05_20261107/`)? Pharmacological occlusion after knockdown may fit a common pathway but needs control of floor effects and basal transmission.

**5. The smallest experiment that changes a decision.** Start from a WWOX-deficient neural system and the appropriate control, with lithium as reference and one genuinely informative pharmacological or genetic comparison; justify each arm; do not adopt the report's six-arm animal panel automatically. Pair: a priority functional endpoint; a pertinent measure of action on the target; viability and control of non-specific suppression; beta-catenin and maturation read-outs in the system where they are interpretable. Separate acute current experiments from the prolonged exposures needed for maturation or myelin; do not assume a "post-development" phase without risk. A non-significant result does not show absence of damage; keeping the knockout's pathological level is not recovery. Restoration of WWOX can be a biological control when practicable; it is not a solution without limits nor this package's candidate. Say which checks remain in silico.
