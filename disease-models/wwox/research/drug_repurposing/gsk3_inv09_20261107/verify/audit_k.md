## Separation judgements

- SP1, lithium vs GSK3 dependence. done_so_far matches R02, E04, E30 (IT15 not checkable). The status line is consistent. Problem: see the X2/X4 design flaw below. A lithium effect that persists after depletion of ONE isoform cannot show non-dependence, because lithium inhibits both. The smallest_test says "after GSK3A and GSK3B knock-down", but X4 depletes one isoform per culture. Judgement: consistent on paper, not executable as X4 is written.
- SP2, alpha vs beta. Consistent with E03, E09, E16, E20, E21, E23. One misattribution: "CT99021 is dual (E22, E28)": E28 cannot show duality (the panel has no GSK3alpha). Duality rests on E22 (it behaves like the dual BRD0320 on beta-catenin) and on Wagner's own description of CHIR99021 as a "pan-GSK3 inhibitor", plus the carried ChEMBL ratio, which I could not check. The statement "title attribution to beta not supported by an isoform test" is correct (E16).
- SP3a, substrates and compartments. Consistent. Add that the PI4KIIalpha-GSK3 axis also has a presynaptic branch (PMID 37314927, abstract only), which this row omits.
- SP3b, Ser9/Ser21 vs Wnt. Consistent, but "Established outside WWOX and outside neurons" is strong for two experiments in one ES cell line with one ligand source plus muscle: use "shown in".
- SP4, seizures vs development. Consistent. E07 correctly cannot carry a development claim.
- SP5, benefit vs suppression. Consistent. E12 lacks a vehicle arm, so even the "reduces NMDAR current" step needs the time-matched control that X2 adds. The relayed Draffin data (BRD0705 lowered AMPA/NMDA ratio from 2.46 to 1.05 at 10 uM, a basal-transmission cost) bears on the "basal transmission cost" open point.

## Probe judgements

- Lithium: statements match E30 (10 mM, GSK3beta alpha not measured). The 10 mM assay concentration is far above a therapeutic serum level of about 1 mM (general knowledge, not in the sources read); the card should say so. The Mg2+ competition and IMPase lines are carried from IT15 and not checked.
- CT99021: "called most selective by two sources read here" overcounts: the second source (PMID 32463939) cites Bain et al. for it. One source, cited twice. The panel has no GSK3alpha. The "treat as dual" rule is sound.
- BRD0705: numbers match E20, E21, E24, E25, E26. "Beta-catenin rise only at high micromolar levels (60 to 80)" is loose: the source shows reporter induction at 60 uM (TF-1), and total beta-catenin "largely below" dual-inhibitor levels up to 80 uM. Say "reporter at 60 uM; no clear protein rise to 80 uM".
- BRD3731: matches E20, E21, E23.
- BRD5648: the enantiomer of BRD0705 only. The card's "matched negative control for the pair" follows the paper's wording ("corresponding negative control"), but a BRD0705 enantiomer is no structural control for the neopentyl BRD3731. Say so.
- AR-A014418: the statement "apart from AR-A014418 the other four inhibited several kinases" matches the text. The relayed Table 3 at 10 uM shows CDK2-cyclin A at 23 percent and ERK8 at 37 percent remaining; the card says values were not read.
- SB216763: kinase list matches the relay (ERK8, DYRK1A, PIM3, SRPK1, HIPK2). Used at 10 uM in R05.
- GSK3A / GSK3B genetic: consistent with E24, E27; E09 gives knock-down 57 and 69 percent only (relayed), so "compensation and efficiency must be read" is right.
- PI4K2A shRNA/rescue tool: matches E10-E15. "The only branch-selective tool found; it is genetic" is too narrow. Blumrich 2023 (PMID 37314927, abstract) uses a Ser-47 phosphomimetic peptide as a dominant-negative and kinase-dead and phosphomimetic mutants, in presynaptic ADBE, not NMDAR. The abstract of 32088394 names a PI4KIIalpha inhibitor without identity.

## H items

- H1: SUPPORTED. The abstracts were not separately checked, but the full texts were. - H2: SUPPORTED. Bain panel lists GSK3beta only (no GSK3alpha string in the text relayed from either half, relayed). CHIR99021 (the same compound as CT99021) stabilised beta-catenin in HEK293T like BRD0320 (E22). 8-14-fold biochemical, ">4-6-fold" in live cells (E20, E21). Phrase it as lower bound (">4-6").
- H3: SUPPORTED with two precisions. Every clause matches: halving (50 +/- 4 percent), AMPA 105 +/- 8 percent, CT99021 57 percent, 94 percent after knock-down, WT rescue, S9/51A no rescue, LTD intact. Precision 1: "units are neuron pairs" is true for the knock-down current comparisons (n = 30, 31 pairs) but the CT99021 time courses (n = 17, 11, 8, 7) and LTD (n = 8, 7, 4) count neurons, not pairs, and the number of slices or animals is not reported in the text. Precision 2: the S9/51A group retained about 61 percent of current against about 50 percent after knock-down alone, so "no rescue" is the authors' statistical reading. Isoform not tested: confirmed.
- H4 (absence): the strings and results. Route 1: (PI4KIIalpha OR PI4K2A OR "phosphatidylinositol 4-kinase type II alpha") AND (GSK3 OR "glycogen synthase kinase") returned 4 hits: 37314927, 35477991, 32463939, 25083612. Route 2: "PI4KIIalpha GSK3 NMDA receptor" returned 1 hit (32463939). Route 3: (GSK3 OR "glycogen synthase kinase") AND (NMDA receptor) AND (internalization OR synaptic currents) AND hippocampal AND inhibitor returned 3 hits (32463939 plus two unrelated). Route 4: (PI4KIIalpha OR PI4K2A OR "PI4KIIα") AND (inhibitor OR "small molecule" OR probe) returned 30 hits, 15 listed; 32088394 is the one on neuronal PI4KIIalpha pharmacology. What the PubMed tool does with OR and parentheses: it honours both, and the response shows a query translation in which every bare term is auto-expanded (for example GSK3 becomes a union of Supplementary Concept, All Fields and MeSH terms; "PI4KIIalpha" is searched only as an All Fields string, with no MeSH expansion). Consequence: the string "PI4KIIα" with a Greek letter is searched as typed and misses papers that write "PI4KIIalpha" or "PI4K2A", so synonyms must be OR-ed explicitly.
  Judgement: the clause "no pharmacological probe for the branch" is SUPPORTED within these four routes (no drug targets the GSK3-PI4KIIalpha interaction; a PI4KIIalpha inhibitor exists, abstract-level, not a branch probe). The clause "no second paper on the branch was found" is NOT correct as written: three other papers address GSK3 phosphorylation of PI4KIIalpha: 25083612 (Robinson 2014, Ser5 and Ser47, AMPA surface expression; the source of the plasmids in E15), 37314927 (Blumrich 2023, Ser-47, presynaptic ADBE, with phosphomimetic peptides), 35477991 (Arumugam 2022, GSK3beta phosphorylates PI4K2A in the Golgi; NLRP3). None of them reports NMDAR current, so the narrower statement "no second paper on the NMDAR-current branch" holds. All four were read at abstract level only.

## Omitted decisive points

1. PI4KIIalpha-GSK3 has a presynaptic role in activity-dependent bulk endocytosis (abstract of 37314927), which is dependent on Ser-47 phosphorylation and matters in a high-activity (bursting) setting. A PI4KIIalpha-directed manipulation may cost presynaptic function that the postsynaptic AMPA and LTD checks do not see. Add a presynaptic readout to X3 and a cost line to SP5.
2. The earlier PI4KIIalpha paper (Robinson 2014 abstract) found that reducing PI4KIIalpha raised surface AMPA receptors; the 2020 paper found no change in synaptic AMPA current and discusses the discrepancy. The package keeps "AMPA unchanged" without carrying the conflict as a limit.
3. X4 cannot test lithium dependence with single-isoform depletion (see above).
4. The cross-reference error in E16 ("see E30") and the overcount "two sources" for CT99021.
5. Relayed table values for CT 99021 at 1 uM (PLK1 41, MELK 45 percent remaining; E28 flag) and for AR-A014418 at 10 uM (CDK2-cyclin A 23, ERK8 37 percent).
6. Residue numbering: the 2020 paper writes S9/51A; the earlier paper's abstract (25083612) and the 2023 abstract name Ser5/Ser47 and Ser-47. The offset of four in both sites would fit an N-terminal tag in the construct, but I did not check this. X0 should resolve numbering before any residue is mapped across species.
7. Relayed Draffin et al. points not carried by E09: probes used at 10 uM, partial knock-down, rat versus mouse preparation, AMPA/NMDA ratio effect of BRD-0705.
8. Outside-source remark, unverified here: lithium also raises Ser9 phosphorylation in cells; so a Ser9 knock-in could blunt part of a lithium effect. A knock-in test of lithium would be informative but not conclusive. This is my background knowledge, not a checked source.
