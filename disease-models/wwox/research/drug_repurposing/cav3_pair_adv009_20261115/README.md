## Answer first

**Verdict: the pair stays a proposal, qualified. It is not a matched pair today, it is not demoted, and the isoform contrast the draft treated as unknown is partly known and points in opposite directions for the two agents.**

1. **No source tests both compounds on all three isoforms in one assay (DATO).** Flunarizine has a full matched triple; efonidipine has one isoform with numbers and a qualitative three-isoform pattern at abstract level only.
2. **Flunarizine, Cav3.1 / 3.2 / 3.3, one assay (DATO, full text read):** Kd 530.2 ± 107.9 / 3551.7 ± 119 / 837.2 ± 59.03 nM (n = 3 each); whole-cell patch clamp, stable lines in human HEK tsa201 cells, holding -100 mV, 2 mM Ca (Ba for Cav3.2; Ca also gives 3.04 uM for Cav3.2), 20-24 C. Cav3.2 is 4 to 7 fold weaker (p < 0.01). Santi 2002 (PMID 11784784). ChEMBL carries the same three values (CH, same origin, not a confirmation).
3. **Efonidipine, Cav3.2 only has numbers (DATO, full text read):** IC50 racemate 2.9 ± 1.0 uM, R(-) 2.0 ± 0.3 uM, S(+) 2.3 ± 0.7 uM; Fluo-4 calcium-influx assay in HEK293 cells with human Cav3.2a, no voltage clamp. The isomers are equipotent here. Perez-Reyes 2008 (PMID 18974361), found by a PubMed search, not named in WO-25.
4. **Efonidipine's three-isoform pattern exists at abstract level (DATO-abstract):** in Xenopus oocytes, one lab, 14 dihydropyridines, efonidipine "significantly blocked alpha1H and alpha1G, but not alpha1I" (Furukawa 2009, PMID 19401195). No IC50, concentration or holding potential is known to this note; the body was not reached.
5. **The two patterns run in opposite directions (INFERENZA).** Flunarizine reaches Cav3.1 and Cav3.3 more than Cav3.2; efonidipine reaches Cav3.1 and Cav3.2 but not significantly Cav3.3. On paper that is the shape of a pair that could separate isoforms. It is **not** established: the two patterns come from different assays (patch-clamp Kd in HEK cells v significance of block in oocytes), the efonidipine Cav3.1 and Cav3.3 values are not obtained, and "not significant" on Cav3.3 is not "inactive".
6. **Potency scale (INFERENZA):** on Cav3.2 both agents sit in the low micromolar range (efonidipine 2-3 uM in a flux assay, flunarizine 3.6 uM as a Kd), and flunarizine on Cav3.1 and Cav3.3 is 0.5-0.8 uM. The IT28 comparison states these agents act on Cav3 "at 10^-8 to 10^-6 M"; for these two, on the values above, the range is 10^-6 to 10^-5 M (the nanomolar range belongs to the apinocaltamide class). The statement needs narrowing for this pair.
7. **Cardiac channels (DATO-abstract, CH):** flunarizine on hERG, IC50 5.7 nM in CHO cells by whole-cell voltage clamp (PMID 16495758, abstract; also KCNQ1/KCNE1 0.76 uM); ChEMBL binding and screening values 446.7 to 605.8 nM, about 80 to 100 fold weaker, so the two kinds of assay disagree and are not reconciled here. Against its own Cav3.1 Kd (530 nM) the patch-clamp hERG value is about 90 fold lower: in vitro, a concentration that blocks Cav3 would already block hERG. Efonidipine: **no hERG or QT value was obtained** (ChEMBL holds 16 activity rows in all, none on KCNH2 or any calcium channel; PubMed title and abstract queries returned no efonidipine hERG record). Animal torsades models for flunarizine exist (abstracts) and are not human QT numbers.
8. **Flunarizine is a non-selective tool even before the isoform question (DATO-abstract):** in rat CA1 neurons it inhibited T-type and high-voltage-activated calcium channels, Na+ and Ca2+-activated K+ channels, while R(-)-efonidipine inhibited only T-type (Shin 2008, PMID 18278483; the one native-neuron comparison of the two, no numbers in the abstract). The racemate of efonidipine blocks L-type as well; R(-) spares L-type (PMID 15545287, 15599089, abstracts).
