## 5. What the items miss that changes their meaning

1. EI06/PE9 (largest): the 2013 text does state a result for phospho-S539-eIF2B epsilon: ISRIB "did not reduce" it, which the authors read as ISRIB not using that regulatory phosphorylation. It is the only ISRIB-by-phospho-epsilon measurement in the set. It enters in the context of Woo et al. (TLR4 activation of eIF2B via removal of an inhibitory phosphate by a phosphatase); in Woo 2012 (PMC3271190) the authors tested and excluded a GSK3-inactivation route for LPS. Whether S539 equals the GSK3 serine (rat 535 / human 540) is not settled by either paper; the repository README places a DYRK-annotated priming serine at rat 539, so the label may denote the neighbouring serine. Do not call the measurement absent; do not call it a GSK3-site test either.
2. U2/EI04: "unchanged" is wrong in one condition (p-eIF2 alpha rose further with tunicamycin plus ISRIB at 120 min in the 2013 assay). The safe claim is "not reduced".
3. PE7: the "no action at basal" statement is contradicted inside the cited set (Zyryanova's own Discussion; Oliveira's reference to Sidrauski's WT effect at a higher dose).
4. PE12/PE16(2): neuron-specific failure of ATF4 is shown only for rat cortical cultures under thapsigargin. In the mouse hippocampus paper ATF4 did follow ISRIB.
5. PE14: dephosphorylation, not phosphorylation, is what failed to activate eIF2B; the inhibitors have separate effects on overall protein synthesis (abstract).
6. EI02/PE1: the first report says the screened library sample "likely contains a mixture" of cis and trans, so the identity between the screened sample and trans is not just unproven, it is probably not exact.
7. EI19/PE3: "same face" on a 1,4-ring reads as cis by convention, in conflict with trans; wording, not a result, but it should read as an unresolved conflict rather than a neutral flag. The figure was not viewed.
8. R4/R6: both papers' own theses limit their use as GSK3 readouts: R4 title says the LiCl effect on Abeta is independent of GSK3; R6 reports raised inhibitory Ser9 phosphorylation under OA with GSK3 still active. R5's Tau 1 reversal is partly ERK1/2-dependent.
9. EI22: neuron translation recovery by ISRIB was "less efficient" than in HEK293.
