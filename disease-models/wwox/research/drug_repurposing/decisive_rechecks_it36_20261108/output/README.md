## A. HDAC5 and HIF-1alpha

DATO. PMID 26061431 (full text, figures not viewed): in Hep3B, HeLa, MCF7 only HDAC5 knockdown among HDAC1, 3, 5, 6 impaired hypoxic HIF-1alpha accumulation (proteasome-dependent); LMK235 does the same, also in rat H9c2; a C698/H704A mutant cannot stabilise; cytosol-locked HDAC5 raises HIF-1alpha, nuclear-locked barely; the substrate is Hsp70, HIF-1alpha acetylation is unchanged; knockdown blunted hypoxic CA9 and SLC2A1 mRNA (A01-A07). PMID 34021541 (full text): mouse hippocampal neurons in epilepsy models, no hypoxia; HDAC5 silencing lowered HIF1A and PFKFB3, HDAC5 co-precipitated HIF1A, ChIP only at PFKFB3 (B01-B02). PMID 19071119 (abstract only): HDAC4/5 raised HIF-1alpha transactivation without stabilising it, bound its inhibitory domain, shifted binding from FIH-1 to p300 (C01-C02). Counter, abstract only (D01-D03): PMID 42413489 HDAC5 loss potentiates HIF1alpha-target output through hyperacetylated FOXA1 (pancreatic cancer); PMID 36124413 HDAC5 overexpression suppresses HIF-1alpha/VEGFA/VEGFR2 signalling (PCOS mouse ovary); PMID 35704695 HDAC5 loss under intermittent hypoxia goes with more HIF-1alpha in PC12 cells (causal arrow undetermined).

Not given by any source: WWOX cells, normoxia, human neural cells. The two positive mechanisms disagree on HIF-1alpha amount.

Not measured in neural cells.
