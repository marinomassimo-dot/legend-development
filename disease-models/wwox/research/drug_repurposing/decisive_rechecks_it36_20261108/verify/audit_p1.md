Two extra checks the item does not make. (a) If the authors used the asymptotic Kolmogorov p-value (a common default in Matlab-type software; I did not verify which option they used), the attainable minimum is higher, not lower, for these N's (10 v 10: 9.1e-5; 18 v 17: 5.1e-8; 14 v 17: 4.3e-7; 17 v 17: 8.3e-8; 14 v 9: 3.5e-5; 7 v 9: 7.6e-4), and the count of reported p below it is again 10 of 18, same members. ## Things the items miss that bear on their meaning

1. PMID 34021541 (B01/B02): the Methods contain a stray reagent in the flow-cytometry paragraph ("neuronal cells were treated with 0.01, 0.10 and 1.00 nM LDM for 48 h") that belongs to no experiment of the paper, in addition to the "X of 90 mice died" placeholder and the rat/mouse mismatch. This lowers confidence in the Methods as a record, and the B-rows should carry it with the other flags. HIF1A mRNA was measured alongside protein (Fig 3 legend), so the item's "protein amount" change does not separate transcript from stability; the paper cites earlier work for "HIF1α is a downstream mediator of HDAC5" and ran no stability assay. HDAC5 silencing also lowered apoptosis and inflammation in the same cells, so a lower HIF1A in a model where HIF1A is elevated by the disease state may be secondary.
2. PMID 34021541 (B02): oe-HDAC5 raised HIF1A levels in the same system, so "more HIF1α combined" in an HIF1α-antibody IP is not normalised to input in anything I could read.
3. PMID 26061431: the "general mechanism" statement rests on three cancer lines plus one rat cardiomyocyte line; the paper's own Discussion says HDAC4 may also be involved and that HDAC5 is "highly expressed in cardiac muscle and brain" (an expression remark, not an experiment in brain cells). Not changing any row, but the A-rows' neural-gap statement is right and should stay.
4. PMID 34634460 sIPSC isolation: the Methods say sEPSCs and sIPSCs were isolated "by voltage-clamping pyramidal neurons at -70 mV in K-Gluconate or KCl intracellular solution respectively", and the Pharmacology section lists no GABA-A or glutamate-receptor antagonist. With high-chloride internal at -70 mV, IPSCs and EPSCs are both inward, so the sIPSC set may contain glutamatergic events. This bears on E03, E07 (the six sIPSC p values) and E15, and none of the items states it. Event detection is "threshold ... 4× the standard deviation of the filtered signal" with amplitude and decay "adjusted to ensure the automatically detected events matched our visual identification", i.e. partly manual, also unmentioned.
5. E01 and E02 are not equivalent inconsistencies (see table): H is a hard contradiction, F has an undeclared-subset explanation.
6. E09 and E08: the Fig 6 legend phrase "for all animals tested" is the one piece of evidence that the sag and rebound p values may be animal-level; the items cite it but E08 reads past it.
