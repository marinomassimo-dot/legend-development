## 2. Mechanical parity check (support, not proof of scientific fidelity)

Rule: PARITY_RULE_ADV132_IT_20261121.md, written and committed before the first run. It compares numbers, decimals (either mark), percentages, p-values, ranges, numbers with units, gene/compound/variant symbols and source-capitalised names after declared normalisations (NFC, digit-group spaces, decimal comma to point, signs, a units list, percent words). Negation and hedge cues are counted by category per language and a difference is a prompt for a human reading, not a violation: a language may express the same hedge with a subjunctive or a different construction, so words need not coincide. The check supports the blind readers; it does not prove scientific fidelity.
