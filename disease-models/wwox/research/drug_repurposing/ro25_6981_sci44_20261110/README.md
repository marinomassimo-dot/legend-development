## Answer first
**The role of Ro 25-6981 changes from "unresolved, abstract values only" to a diheteromer tool with primary potency and site, and stays unresolved for the question that matters in the Wwox slice (the maximum inhibition and the triheteromer).**
* DATO, primary (Hedegaard 2012, 22197913, text and Table 4; verified digit by digit): IC50 0.049 +/- 0.008 uM (human, n = 8 oocytes) and 0.042 +/- 0.006 uM (rat, n = 4) on GluN1/GluN2B at -40 mV, 100 uM glutamate plus 30 uM glycine, EDTA, pH 7.4, 23 C; "no effect" (less than 20 percent at 100 uM) on GluN1/GluN2A, 2C and 2D. In a separate rat oocyte experiment 59 nM against ifenprodil 105 nM (n = 22 and 20; same laboratory lineage, so not independent). The site is the GluN1/GluN2B amino-terminal-domain interface by structure (3.7 A crystal; cryo-EM).
* **No maximum inhibition is reported for any composition** in any text opened (the data were normalised to the maximal current of the same recording and fitted to 100 percent). The ifenprodil ceilings (88 percent diheteromer, 32 percent triheteromer, Hansen 2014) are **not** carried over to Ro 25-6981.
* **Triheteromer: no functional value.** There is a structure (cryo-EM, 6.0 A for the Ro-bound model) and a binding statement without a number readable here ("submicromolar", about 3-fold weaker than the diheteromer, a number in an unreachable figure). The sentence that GluN2A decreases the inhibition of ifenprodil "or Ro" cites papers that measured ifenprodil and CP-101,606, not Ro 25-6981.
* One native datum (rat entorhinal layer V, P28-35, 31-33 C, +40 mV): 500 nM removed about 93 percent of the evoked NMDAR current by eye; composition not verified, ambient Zn2+, presynaptic action possible; **not transferred**.
