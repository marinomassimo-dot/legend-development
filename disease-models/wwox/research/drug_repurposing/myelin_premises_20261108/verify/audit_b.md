## What the items miss that changes their meaning

1. The link of GSE158684 to PMID33914858 is better than "identical nucleus numbers" and the supplement itself carries them. File009 (Supplementary Methods, "Single nuclei sequencing"): "Quantification with cellranger revealed an estimated 3182/2920 cell count and median of 2185/2029 genes per cell for S-Control /S-KO groups", "cellranger count utility (version 3.0.2", "Quantification was performed separately on S-Control samples and S-KO". The GEO matrix header also says software_version 3.0.2, and GEO contributors are the paper's first and last author with a summary that paraphrases the paper's abstract. The plural "samples" in the supplement and eight distinct BioSamples per series fit a merged multi-sample run per arm; every barcode in both matrices ends in "-1" (one GEM group each), which makes a Cell Ranger aggr merge of four libraries unlikely. "Cannot be told apart without reads" remains true for one library registered four times versus four animals in one load, but the aggr alternative is weakened.
2. N-2 route (S5, S6, R16). The vendor page names the formulation basis (Bottenstein N-1). The original paper (PMID284369, abstract) lists the supplements as "insulin, transferrin, progesterone, selenium, and putrescine"; no thyroid hormone. This is abstract depth and not the Gibco list, so it cannot move the class to absence_documented, but it makes the N2 route "not shown" rather than open; the B-27 route is the only one with a listed T3.
3. S16 and R12: current vendor formulation of B-27 Minus Vitamin A (12587001, 12587010) also lists T3. Together with R11, the original protocol's maintenance medium after Day 70 is a T3-listing B-27 variant as well, so "T3 supplement stops at Day 70" holds for the original protocol in the same way as for the WWOX paper.
4. Tiratricol status. The catalogue entry for CHEMBL41632 carries "experimental" and no approval statement. PubMed record PMID40549098 (Drugs, "Tiratricol: First Approval", doi 10.1007/s40265-025-02197-7): "received its first approval on 12 February 2025 in the European Union, for the treatment of peripheral thyrotoxicosis in patients with MCT8 deficiency". Not a WWOX or myelin indication, but a card that names tiratricol as the transport-independent comparator should not inherit a stale "experimental" access field.
5. The spheroid knockout is whole-cell (hESC CRISPR line), so the spheroid hypomyelination with T3 given cannot by itself separate a neuron-nonautonomous block from an oligodendroglial cell-autonomous one; the mouse O-KO (Olig2-Cre) is the paper's own cell-autonomy test (Supp Fig 8 legend: no impairment). The DRG co-culture is the only neuron-origin test in a dish and uses Wwox-null DRGs (FVB background) with wild-type OPCs; the conditional mouse lines are C57BL6/J;129sv mixed.
6. S7: the protocol paper prints "5μg/ml N-Acetyl-Cysteine", the WWOX paper prints "5 mg/ml": a thousandfold difference in printed units, not thyroid-related but relevant to any recipe reconstruction.
7. The glial and DRG media differ in serum: the 2019 protocol's isolated-oligodendrocyte Sato medium holds "1% horse serum" besides T3/T4 (S8 does not list the serum); the Repudi co-culture medium has no serum, which sharpens the S4 carry-over question (serum only in the glial flasks, DIV0-10).
8. In R22 versus E3, the Slc16a1 OPC-like figure is stated two ways; one of them has to give.
9. The "absence_documented" label on S12 and S14 describes the added supplement only; the item limits say so, but a reader of the class column alone would read the medium as T3-free.
