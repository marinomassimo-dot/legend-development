## Answer first
1. The co-culture and spheroid media are not hormone-free by documentation. DATO: B27 is in the DRG plating medium, in the co-culture medium (with N2), in the spheroid neural medium (2 percent, Gibco 17504044) and in the oligo maturation base (1 percent) used from Day 51 and after Day 71 (`media.tsv`, M01 to M03, M06). DATO: the current vendor formulation of that B-27 code lists T3, concentration confidential (B02, read through a page summariser, to be re-read raw). INFERENZA: T3 exposure after Day 70 is undetermined, not zero; the lot used in 2021 is not documented. The module statement that thyroid hormone is "withdrawn from Day 71" (INV-06 E12) is true only of the added T3 supplement (Day 60 to 70 per the Supp Fig 9A scheme I viewed).
2. Consequence for INV-12: its "against hormone-depleted medium" arm cannot be run with B27 and N2 until the medium T3 is measured; a defined medium without B27 would change the neurons as well. A null agonist result in complete medium may be a ceiling effect. IPOTESI: step 0 is a measurement of T3 and T4 in complete medium (not done). Liothyronine, not tiratricol, is the primary receptor test in a dish with intact transporters.
3. DATO: knockout spheroids stay hypomyelinated after a T3 supplement given to every spheroid; INFERENZA: this argues against a simple hormone-limited block (it may equally be receptor-unresponsive) and there is no T3-free arm.
4. EX1 and EX5 on the deposited nuclei: DATO, one pooled library per arm (paper n = 1), hippocampus, P17, raw reads still `hup` (8 of 8). Description only. I ran a small descriptive count (no test): per neuron-like nucleus Hr, Klf9 and Thrb are flat (ratios 1.07, 0.99, 1.00), Hr (the hormone-target gene the module's source names) is detected in only 13 and 6 mature-oligodendrocyte-like nuclei and 0 and 1 OPC-like nuclei, and Slc16a1 in 11 and 7, so lineage-level comparison of those genes is not supportable. These data cannot separate a thyroid-type response from lineage composition (oligodendrocyte-called nuclei are about 5-fold fewer in the knockout pool).
5. New to the module's use: the thresholded bulk tables from IT24 and IT27 (independent animals, 2 to 4 per arm) already hold Hr (S-KO cortex 14.60 against 35.42 TPM), Dio3 (S-KO hippocampus 0.45 against 3.16; null 0.63 against 2.04) and Dio2 lower, with Thra higher in N-KO. None was linked to a thyroid reading. INFERENZA: direction fits hormone deprivation but is equally explained by oligodendrocyte depletion, activity or delayed maturation; descriptive only (T01 to T03).
6. Energy: DATO transcripts of glycolytic genes move with mixed signs (T04); transporter transcripts are detectable in neuron-like nuclei and in a handful of oligodendroglial nuclei. A transcript is not flux. Medium glucose of the DMEM and Neurobasal codes was not determinable; it is a precondition for reading any H2 dish arm.
7. Cards (`cards.tsv`, all `already_in_legend`, none favourable): CARD-H1-01 (tiratricol, liothyronine comparator, isoform control, with the medium baseline step), CARD-H2-01 (lactate as process probe; EX6 first), CARD-H2-02 (PDK inhibition; recorded only as dependent on INV-07's premise, not carried). No new molecule fills a gap.
