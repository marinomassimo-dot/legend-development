# NOTICE: third-party data in this directory (IT7 P3)

* **Network edge tables** `inputs/signor_omnipath_edges.tsv.gz` and `inputs/collectri_omnipath_edges.tsv.gz` are derived from the OmniPath web service (https://omnipathdb.org, accessed 2026-10-29) by `fetch_network_inputs.py`.
  Every source resource of every retained row, with its licence as labelled by OmniPath, is listed in `inputs/resource_licences.tsv` (also in each row's `sources` column). Main ones:
  SIGNOR (CC BY 4.0, Lo Surdo et al.), ExTRI / ExTRI2, NTNU curation, TFactS, CytReg, GOA, ENCODE, JASPAR, HOCOMOCO, ReMap, IntAct, ProtMapper family (CC BY 4.0 or CC0), BioGRID (MIT);
  **TRRUST (CC BY-SA 4.0; 6,458 + further rows via TRRUST_CollecTRI/CollecTRI2/DoRothEA), KEGG-MEDICUS (CC BY-SA 4.0), SignaLink3, Guide2Pharma, TFe (CC BY-SA 3.0), PhosphoPoint (CC BY-SA 4.0), InnateDB (DSL), TRED / dbPTM (NAR Open Access)** are share-alike ("alike");
  **HTRIdb, RegNetwork, PAZAR, ORegAnno (labelled LGPLv3 by OmniPath), ICELLNET, CellChatDB, CellTalkDB, LRdb (GPLv3)** are labelled with software licences and kept because OmniPath marks them commercial-use; DIP (CC BY-ND 3.0, 14 rows) carries a no-derivatives label and appears only inside rows that also list SIGNOR.
  The OmniPath composite names CollecTRI, CollecTRI2 and DoRothEA are accepted only for the CollecTRI table, where all their listed constituents passed the filter. Non-commercial and academic-only resources (for example GEREDB, PhosphoSite, HPRD) were excluded row by row.
* **Statement.** The committed CollecTRI table contains TRRUST-derived rows; the table as a whole is therefore distributed here under **CC BY-SA 4.0** (share-alike, attribution to the resources above), and the same applies to any derivative of it. The SIGNOR table likewise carries share-alike rows (SignaLink3, KEGG-MEDICUS and others) and is treated the same way. Attribution: "SIGNOR (CC BY 4.0) and the resources in resource_licences.tsv, via OmniPath (Turei et al.)".
  The SIGNOR terms and the licence labels are taken from OmniPath's own metadata; they were **not** checked at SIGNOR's site (connection refused from this host on 2026-10-29) and not independently audited. No verdict depends on any particular source row; the table can be replaced by a stricter one (CC BY / CC0 only) at the cost of a re-run.

* **GEO GSE215425** (PMID 36828035): public; only derived summary statistics are committed.
