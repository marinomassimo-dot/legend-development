## Slot options the text makes ambiguous
* **A1 (S1):** the abstract says normal-weight animals lower core temperature; the only temperature figure with TTA-A2 (Figure 6C) is in high-fat-diet mice, in the inactive phase, with a **rise** during the active phase. The slot has no option for a phase-dependent effect. Even a reader able to measure the plot would measure it in animals outside the slot definition.
* **A2 (S2):** NOT TESTED rests on the absence of any such experiment in the body text, legends and Methods of the page (no supplement exists on the page); it is not a stated "was not tested". The paper's rationale paragraph treats the knockout and the drug as separate approaches, which supports the reading. Note also that the abstract's "without changes in food intake" for the null mice sits beside Results, "Food intake in the KO animals was lower overall for the study duration, with only 1 time point of significant difference"; not a slot, but the abstract is looser than the body.
* **A3 (S3):** "the obese-rodent weight experiment" fits three different experiments: HFD for 2 weeks then drug (Figure 6, food intake reduced), established diet-induced obese mice (Figure 7, no food intake reported), obese rats on TTA-A7 (Figure 8, not TTA-A2, no food intake reported); a fourth, growing diet-induced obesity (Figure 5), is a prevention study with food intake reduced on HFD and unchanged on chow. Under any reading no experiment reports UNCHANGED food intake in obese animals; the slot has no option for "reduced in some experiments and not reported in others".
* **A4 (S4):** "8 weeks or older, stated or implied" cannot be checked for any animal except the telemetry mice ("adult") and the 135-day-old rats; "Growing diet-induced obesity" suggests unspecified young adults.
* **A5 (S5):** "normal-weight animals" is ambiguous between the 13-week wild-type mice on normal chow (no weight difference, body-composition difference) and the same mice on high-fat diet (weight gain halved, maintained); the abstract's own sentence ("prevented high-fat diet-induced weight gain") supports the second. "Temperature or weight" is an "or": weight was maintained, temperature was not followed over repeated dosing in normal-weight animals.

## Incidental, for the bridge pilot (not part of the scoring)
The temperature effect of TTA-A2 in the paper is **time-of-day dependent** (lower in the inactive phase, higher in the active phase) and was measured by telemetry in adult mice; the paper has no animal under 4 weeks. Neither point changes v3.1 6.15 or 6.19, which already state that nothing is held for any pup, but the rule "core temperature beside the energy variables" would, in pups without a mature circadian rhythm, need sampling at fixed clock times against vehicle, which the laboratory would have to state.
