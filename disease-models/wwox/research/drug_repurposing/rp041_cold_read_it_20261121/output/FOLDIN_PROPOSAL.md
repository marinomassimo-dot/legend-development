- Rescue trigger and procedure; expected status and death rates; observation conditions; experiment-level stop rules.
- How an increase (aggravation) is read.
- Who funds, who owns data, authorship, contact route, deliverables, timeline.
- The heterozygote bursting incidence (or delete the sentence).
- Whether the priors file is supplied; which definitions it fixes.
- Which add-ons need the neuron-targeted line and whether the serial and terminal measures can share pups.
- A plain definition of each term in TERMS.tsv that is kept.

## 5. Rules for the assembly (so that the clean text is checkable)

- Each dated line is deleted from the body once folded, and its date and gist are kept in the history section.
- Every number appears once, in the table that owns it; other sections point to the table.
- Programme-internal names (ADV, IT, HE, SCI, RP, dossier, "the loop", file paths) are removed from the laboratory text; where a result depends on an unavailable file, the result is stated with its level, not the file name.
- First-person remarks ("my inference", "checked by me") are removed; the conclusion is stated at its evidence level.
- Operator-facing content (who decides whom to approach) is separated from the laboratory text.
