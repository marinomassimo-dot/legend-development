2. **S2 read in full: O'Connell 2005 (PMID 15611021, PMC1665531), the original, 7 of 7 quotes found (`verify_output.txt`). The numbers hold; the live wording leaves out what the number is (DATO-full, DERIVED).** Murine TASK1 in Xenopus oocytes, 100 mM external K+, pH 8, room temperature 22-24 C; Ba2+ added "in concentrations between 0.3 and 10 mM" (Q3, Q4, Q6); "the K D at 0 mV for Ba 2+ was 36 ± 10 mM (n = 6)" (Q1). That 36 mM is **not a measurement**: "the K D at 0 mV (K D0) could not be determined experimentally because there were no currents at the equilibrium potential", so it is estimated per oocyte from the voltage-dependence fit (Q2). Block is voltage dependent, the site about 60 % of the way across the field (Q5, Q7). **Derived here, with the standard exponential form and the paper's mean parameters (z = 2, delta 0.64; the form and sign convention are my assumption, as the equation is not in the page text): KD about 0.25 mM at -100 mV, 1.8 mM at -60 mV, 4.9 mM at -40 mV, 36 mM at 0 mV, all in 100 mM K+, pH 8.** (The producer's own README derived 0.24 and 4.8 mM, the same arithmetic.) So the live Update's "KD 36 +/- 10 mM at 0 mV" is correctly worded, but a reader will take 36 mM as TASK-1's Ba2+ potency; at resting-range voltages the same mean parameters put the KD two orders of magnitude lower, in 100 mM K+ at pH 8; those conditions are not physiological (external K+ competes with Ba2+, and pH changes the channel), so no physiological number follows. What does follow: **the sentence "no primary support for a Ba2+ step in 50-200 uM for TASK" stays true as a statement about tested concentrations (lowest 0.3 mM), and it should not be read as "TASK-1 is blocked only at millimolar Ba2+"; the paper's own fit says the KD is sub-millimolar at hyperpolarised voltages in 100 mM K+.** The V14 row's decisive step (a low-micromolar Ba2+ block pointing to Kir-type channels) is not changed by this: 50 to 200 uM remains below the lowest tested concentration and below 0.25 mM.
