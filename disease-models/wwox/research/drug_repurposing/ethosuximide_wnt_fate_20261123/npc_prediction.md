# Does the three-marker drug signal transfer to a broader WNT response?

23 Nov 2026, SCI_CODEX. Fixed after seeing the supplement's contrast names and columns, before reading its gene values or scoring either target dataset. This is a follow-up to a positive three-gene screen. Primary reference: Matoba et al. (2024), Supplementary Table 3. Use the 20 most increased and 20 most decreased unique protein-coding symbols by shrunken log2 fold change at adjusted P < 0.05 after WNT3A versus vehicle. Exclude AXIN2, LEF1, NKD1 and APCDD1, which suggested the question. Do not select on either target dataset. Challenge the panel with both CHIR versus vehicle (expect same sign), and WNT3A plus XAV versus WNT3A (opposite). These are different perturbations in an overlapping donor cohort.

Primary score is half the mean change in the up arm minus half the mean change in the down arm on log2(1+CPM). Restrict to unique symbol matches and median vehicle CPM >=1 (mean WT CPM >=1 for human WWOX). For mouse use case-insensitive identical symbols, explicitly an approximation, and repeat with verified orthology if available. Require at least eight reliably mapped genes per arm or return insufficient coverage. Record both arms separately. An activation-like drug result requires positive up, negative down, score above all nine leave-one-vehicle-out scores and in the highest 10% of all drug conditions. Reverse all inequalities for inhibition. Vehicle concentrations and batches differ; also report rank among the other conditions sharing the captured sample-ID block. These ranks describe one treated well.

For untreated human organoids record direction of both arms, KO-WT score, all 15 genotype assignments and omission of each library. A same-direction disease score supplies a baseline-state comparison only. A positive or negative drug result alone cannot establish therapeutic benefit. Report both reference perturbations regardless of transfer.
