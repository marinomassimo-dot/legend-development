# Two post-primary challenges

The three eligible WNT readouts passed the uncorrected direct-drug rule, while the expected joint telencephalic-down/posterior-CNS-up pattern failed in WWOX. Before implementing further calculations we fixed two challenges.

First, the publicly displayed author analysis removes four technical principal components and evaluates expression against the full screen. Retrieve all four prespecified WNT genes for the same drug; compare directions and author-adjusted values. Also compare the raw three-gene increase to 10,000 random triplets matched independently on vehicle mean abundance and variation (SD of log2(CPM+1)), sampling from the 50 nearest other expressed genes without reuse within each triplet. Report how many decoys rise at least as much, and the rank of the real panel among the other drugs. These nulls calibrate a single library, not donor variability.

Second, the reference paper reports neural crest and peripheral nervous system lineages as important outcomes of CHIR, besides the central lineages in our initial prediction. Without altering the first analysis, examine Neural Crest, PNS Progenitors and PNS Neurons as exploratory alternatives. Use the same prespecified top-marker ranking/coverage rule, excluding all initial selected marker genes and overlaps between new panels. Ask whether WWOX bulk shares at least one reproducible rise in a peripheral lineage that CHIR actually enriches under the high-dose conditions. A shared shift cannot identify pathway direction uniquely. Neither extension tests ethosuximide in WWOX.
