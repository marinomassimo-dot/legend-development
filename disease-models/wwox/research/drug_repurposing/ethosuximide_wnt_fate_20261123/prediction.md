# Does an ethosuximide–WNT explanation align with restoration of telencephalic fate in WWOX loss?

Frozen before opening the new target expression values, morphogen enrichment estimates or cluster-marker rankings. Known: untreated WWOX knockout cerebral organoids have raised AXIN2, LEF1, NKD1 and APCDD1, and altered cortical identity. Efficacy on neural proliferation and differentiation was reported in ordinary rat hippocampal progenitors at 100 µM ethosuximide, with evidence of a role of PI3K/Akt/Wnt. A different DIV21 cortical drug screen used 700 µM for eight hours. A day-21 human neural organoid perturbation study has five single-agent CHIR99021 conditions, other patterning treatments and lineage marker rankings. These experiments answer different questions. CHIR acts on GSK3 and cannot be equated to ethosuximide or to WWOX deletion.

1. Direct drug readout: transfer the four canonical genes above to GSE110256. Compare the single ethosuximide library against the individual median of nine vehicles (log2 CPM+1); retain genes with ≥1 median vehicle CPM. Require at least three eligible, three increases, positive average larger than every leave-one-vehicle-out pseudo-treatment value, and stability under median-ratio normalization before describing a coherent rise. Record treatment rank within HI.3140 as a descriptive control. Lack of a result in ordinary mature cortical cultures rules out neither a precursor response nor a different time course.
2. Regional fate under pathway manipulation: using Supplementary Table 3 from Jonas et al. (doi:10.1038/s41592-025-02927-5; identify authors from record), evaluate CHIR_A to E alone versus matched control, separately by experiment and cell line. Keep raw odds ratios, source significance and missing rows distinct. Primary expectation: late in the dose series (D/E) telencephalic progenitors decline and at least one of hindbrain, spinal-cord or diencephalic progenitors increases in the same comparison. Report every eligible matched contrast and complete dose series for HES3 if available. Compare XAV939 alone in the deposited conditions when present. Interpret state abundance as relative enrichment; the table supplies no absolute neuronal yield.
3. Transfer to WWOX: take up to 12 separate high-AUC positive markers from each relevant cluster in Supplementary Table 1, sorted by AUC then logFC; require positive logFC, adjusted P<0.05, remove markers shared between the retained panels and exclude the four canonical WNT genes. Test telencephalic, hindbrain, spinal-cord and diencephalic progenitors plus CNS neurons at week 15 in GSE156243. Use mean log2(CPM+1) and a per-gene control-mean CPM floor of one; also show all mapped genes. Require at least four eligible markers per panel and leave-one-library-out sign stability; the direction must survive dropping one gene. Prespecified regional match requires telencephalic score down and at least one posterior or diencephalic score up with these safeguards, matching a lineage actually enriched in the reference. If panels fail, report the coverage or discordance. Other caudalizing signals (retinoic acid), cell composition, culture age and lineage protocol remain competitors. Compare single-agent RA D/E for the same fate criteria; a match shared by RA weakens specificity for WNT. No drug–genotype or direct causal link is measured.
