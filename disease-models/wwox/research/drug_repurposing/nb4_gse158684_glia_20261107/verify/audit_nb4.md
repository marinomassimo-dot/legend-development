## Material decisive premises the items omit

1. **Identity of deposit and paper.** The paper's Data and code availability statement gives no accession ("available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request"). The link between GSE158684 and the paper's snRNA is by numbers: the supplement says Cell Ranger estimated "3182/2920 cell count and median of 2185/2029 genes per cell for S-Control/S-KO". I recomputed from the deposit: 3182 and 2920 barcodes, median genes 2185.5 and 2029.5, so the identity is well supported (and stronger than the items imply), but it is inferred, not declared. The samples.tsv and E01-E05 never state it.
2. **Animals vs pools vs libraries.** Nothing states how many animals went into either arm, whether nuclei were pooled, or how many libraries were run. The paper supplement describes one quantification "separately on S-Control samples and S-KO", targeting 10,000 cells; the deposited barcodes are all `-1`; GEO says cellranger-aggr (which writes -1..-N suffixes). Mixed signals; the deposit supports "one matrix per arm, replicate structure unknown", which is how E03/E10 put it, but the items do not mention the paper-versus-GEO contradiction (aggr vs separate count, Seurat 3.2.0 vs 3.1.5, 300 vs 200 genes) or that the matrices are unfiltered by the authors' own QC (1281/1067 nuclei above 2500 genes, 122/118 above 15,000 UMI, which the paper excluded).
3. **Meaning of "glia-annotated".** No label is in the deposit. The authors annotated clusters of an integrated object from about 150 marker genes ("data not shown"; clusters under 100 cells ignored). "Astrocyte-called" and "oligodendrocyte-called" in this work are the pilot's rule-based classes (IT13b), which differ from the four rules I tried by 1.6-2.3x in astrocyte nuclei and 1.9-3.0x in astrocyte Wwox UMI (H1). The KO oligodendrocyte class is 45-70 nuclei, below the authors' own 100-cell cluster floor. KO oligodendrocyte-lineage composition itself is shifted (3-5-fold fewer mature, more OPC-class nuclei), so control and KO "oligodendrocyte" nuclei are not the same population (maturation state can alter Wwox).
4. **Transcript is not protein, activity or cell number.** The authors' glial protein evidence is one CC1 image in corpus callosum (E07); astrocyte WWOX in S-KO is not shown; neuronal loss is a cortex image. snRNA detection is sparse (32 percent of control astrocyte nuclei have any Wwox UMI; 2.5 percent in KO), the exon-only reference and the authors' own caveat ("snRNA-seq ... limited to abundant nuclear transcripts") apply, and a zero in a KO nucleus is a weak statement. Wwox UMI is not a count of cells with deletion.
5. **Which Cre line and what it does.** Jax 003966 (E06 source). Per PMC9353198 it reports Cre in about 36 percent of neurons and cannot exclude glia; per Rempe 2006 male carriers transmit germline-recombined floxed alleles. My inference, not in any source: germline recombination of a floxed allele would give flox/delta animals with Wwox halved in all cells, which could account for a 2-fold, not a 14-40-fold, glial fall, unless a homozygous germline null; breeding scheme of the Wwox colony is not stated. The Cre-only control genotype for the sequenced animals is not stated (samples.tsv pointer, above).
