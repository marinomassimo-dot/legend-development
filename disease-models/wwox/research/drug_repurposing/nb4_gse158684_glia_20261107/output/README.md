# NB-4 pilot: how to read the Wwox fall in glia-annotated nuclei of GSE158684

Producer `nb4-p`, 2026-11-07. Exploratory, non-canonical, not medical advice. Reference genotype class only. No contact address was sent anywhere. Files: `samples.tsv`, `explanations.tsv`, `evidence.tsv` (E01-E21), `queries.tsv`, `receipts/`.

## Answer first

The data and documentation do not allow an apportioning of the glial Wwox fall among the six readings; they allow a narrower statement of what is and is not established.

* DATO. In the KO pool, Wwox UMI are lower in glia-annotated nuclei (oligodendrocyte-called 193 to 1, astrocyte-called 156 to 9; E14, E15). One Cell Ranger-called pool per arm, paper n = 1 per arm, no library or animal index (E05, E10). Raw reads of all eight snRNA SRX are still `hup` on 2026-11-07T15:26:45Z (E21), unchanged since 2026-10-31.
* INFERENZA. The "20- to 44-fold" is a small-count figure. The KO side is 9 UMI (astrocyte) and 1 UMI (oligodendrocyte). An exact Poisson interval on the KO count gives about 10- to 43-fold for astrocytes and at least 7.0-fold for oligodendrocytes, against about 7- to 12-fold for excitatory neurons on the same basis (E15, recomputed by the auditor; control counts held fixed). Treating nuclei as independent, which they are not, the astrocyte-versus-excitatory difference in fold has p about 0.03 and the oligodendrocyte one about 0.06 (auditor), and the astrocyte fold is 11 to 15 against 7 under the auditor's own marker rules. So "glia fall too" is shown; "glia fall deeper than neurons" is suggested in this pool, depends on how the classes are drawn and cannot be tested beyond it, because one pool per arm leaves no replicate.
* INFERENZA. Readings (a) to (e) are not mutually exclusive and none is excluded or isolated (`explanations.tsv`). Supported as a pool-level observation: (a). Compatible, none decisive: (b), (c), (d), (e). Not supported as the main cause: ambient neuronal RNA (E12, E16) and a uniform depth difference (E11, E13). Not decidable: the share of each (f).
* DATO, new. Two sources on recombination of the Synapsin I-Cre line, both abstract only: SynCre expresses in testis and male carriers pass on germline-recombined floxed alleles, never seen from female carriers (PMID 16419044; E17, E18); the original line report claims neuronal ablation, with non-cell-autonomous astrogliosis after a neuronal deletion of another gene (PMID 11297510; E19, E20). Neither shows or excludes glial recombination in the Wwox cross. The auditor found a later direct characterisation of this line (PMID 35966957, E22): reporter in about 36 percent of neurons, weak testis activity, a glial subset not ruled out, and no reporter in GFAP-positive astrocytes in the original report (secondary description; read by the auditor, not staged).
* INFERENZA. A germline-recombined floxed allele would remove one allele in every cell: a partial fall in glia, not a 10- to 45-fold one, unless the second allele also recombines. The sex of the Cre-carrying parent is NOT_STATED in the sources read.
* IPOTESI. The discriminating measurement is reporter and genotype in glia: tdTomato (present in all conditional animals, E08) co-stained with astrocyte and oligodendrocyte-lineage markers in S-KO hippocampus, or Wwox exon-1 genotyping of sorted glia. A lower Wwox transcript in glia is not fewer glia, less protein or less activity; the paper shows intact WWOX protein in CC1-stained cells in one region (E07) and fewer CC1 cells (E09), both compatible with (e). Per nucleus the Wwox counts of control glia and excitatory neurons are about equal (0.5 to 0.55); the higher glial value per 10^4 UMI (E16) reflects fewer UMI per glial nucleus.

## What is sustainable for INV-15

* Sustainable: S-KO is neuron-targeted by design; the pooled snRNA shows lower Wwox transcript in neurons (about 7- to 9-fold) and in glia-annotated nuclei; neuron-restriction of the deletion is not established at the transcript level, and is not refuted either. Write "neuron-targeted deletion with lower Wwox transcript also in glia-annotated nuclei of one pool".
* Sustainable: a per-animal PV measurement (INV-15) should be designed with cell-identity co-staining, so that it does not depend on how glia are read; a lower bulk or pooled Pvalb transcript cannot be assigned to cell-autonomous loss in PV cells from this deposit.
* Not sustainable: "neuron-restricted deletion"; "glial Wwox loss is an ambient artefact"; "glial loss" (no cell count was measured); "cell-autonomous recombination in glia"; any statement that depends on nuclei as replicates.
