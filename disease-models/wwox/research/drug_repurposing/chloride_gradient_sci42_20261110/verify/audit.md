## B. Transfers

The burst series is whole-cell with the K-gluconate pipette (about 12 mM chloride, E_Cl about -63 mV at 35 C, INFERENZA) or, if the KCl one, about -2 mV. Either way chloride is pipette-set; the series says nothing about native E_GABA. Same issue in `new_measurements.tsv` M03 and M05: "physiological chloride" with a whole-cell pipette is experimenter-set chloride, not native; only gramicidin (or cell-attached) leaves chloride to the cell. M01 is correct.
2. **P08 dendritic E_Cl described as "GABA hyperpolarising"** (evidence.tsv EV16 note): Vrest -85 and E_Cl -83 mV is a chloride equilibrium at rest potential: shunting at rest, hyperpolarising only relative to threshold (-52 mV). ## C. The logic of explanations.tsv

- **KCl 140 mM and amplitude.** Right in the main: with E_Cl pinned near -2 mV at the soma, the driving force at -70 mV is about 68 mV in both genotypes whatever the native chloride, so a native E_GABA difference cannot produce the amplitude change; native E_GABA would shift the amplitude by an order of magnitude only if chloride were not clamped. Sharper than the file says: this means the measured halving is **not explainable by E-chloride** (except through incompletely dialysed distal dendrites), so E-chloride is untested but is not the explanation of the one datum; its only motivation is the textbook premise (NEED record: DEFAULT_FROM_TEXTBOOK). What the amplitude still reports is quantal conductance times a driving force that is the same by design, and an unaffected-by-chloride frequency of events above a 4 x SD threshold. Caveats that the file does not give: (a) at -70 mV in KCl, EPSCs are inward as well and no glutamate-receptor block is named; the S-KO sIPSC mean (27.5 pA) sits close to the sEPSC mean (24.7 pA, S-CTL 23.3), and S-CTL sIPSC and sEPSC frequencies (10.3 and 9.9 Hz) are close (INFERENZA: suggests but does not show contamination); (b) detection at 4 x SD makes the counted frequency amplitude-dependent, so "amplitude and frequency both lower" is not two independent findings; (c) input resistance is higher in S-KO pyramidal cells (143 against 103 Mohm): better space clamp would favour larger, not smaller, recorded S-KO amplitudes, so it cannot be the artefact (INFERENZA). Neither (a) nor (c) is stated in the E-artefact row.
- **"Decided by source methods".** `partly` is used for E-supply, E-response and E-artefact. The methods decide none of them: the halving fits supply, response, artefact and compensation alike, and the E-artefact cell itself says "the effect of each is not measured". Change to `no (informs only: non-discriminating)`, or define `partly` in the header. E-chloride, E-compensation and E-maturation say `no`: correct.
- **Weakening outcome for E-chloride** is built on a premise that P08 contradicts (B2). The measurable contrast is E_GABA of the knockout against age-matched wild-type littermates in the same preparation, and the axis that matters is E_GABA against resting potential and threshold in the same cells (gramicidin); the file's own M01 does this. The parenthesis "(resting potential of the knockout is already 5.6 mV more depolarised)" is right as a number (-79.25 against -73.62 mV), but both values are whole-cell K-gluconate, uncorrected for liquid junction (EV03): the sign of the difference holds, the absolute values do not.
- **Missing pathway: chloride shift as a consequence of bursting.** Repeated GABA-A activation in a bursting slice can load chloride and depolarise E_GABA secondarily. E-compensation covers "tonic or activity-dependent change in synaptic inhibition" but not this. M09 pairs wild-type driven bursts with sIPSC and tonic current only; E_GABA (M01) must be in M09 or E-chloride cannot be separated into primary and secondary.
- **E-maturation vs "correctable".** The age series separates a shifted or compressed time course from a constant offset; it does not separate "correctable" from either: correctability is a property of an intervention, which an observational series cannot show. E-maturation is also not a rival to the other five (a delay in supply, response or chloride is still supply, response or chloride); the six are not a partition. The series does **not** presuppose an external wild-type curve: M02 takes wild-type littermates at every age, which is the right reference. It does presuppose (i) knockouts exist at younger and older ages in recordable condition (survival 3-4 weeks; slices need P13 or older in this lab's preparation, younger is untested here), (ii) that burst onset is known, and (iii) that surviving older knockouts are representative (survivor selection): (iii) is not in M02's null control.
- **Omitted weak support for E-supply.** The soma action-potential amplitude of knockout interneurons is 26 % lower (45.03 against 60.78 mV, 5-6 cells, soma). A smaller presynaptic spike lowers calcium entry; the E-supply row files this only as "compatible". Fair as non-discriminating, but it should be said that it is the one measured interneuron change and that it points the supply way.
- Other cells checked and found logically right: E-supply favouring (agonist response normal with reduced evoked IPSC, failures, paired-pulse ratio); E-response favouring; E-compensation weakening (sIPSC reduced without bursts); E-artefact (blind rerun, per-animal summaries); the 36/42 against 0/11 burst counts (Results 2.1 confirmed; 4/23 heterozygote slices also burst, not mentioned).

## D. conditional_prediction.tsv

- No row is stated as benefit, rescue or developmental recovery; "the sign of the drug effect is not the sign of benefit" and "reduction is not recovery of development" are present. No compound is proposed. Bumetanide and a KCC2 antagonist/aldosterone appear only as source facts (P08, P09).
- **E-chloride / GABA-A positive modulator**: "shunting GABA gives little change" does not follow from the premise. Shunting inhibition still lowers excitability through conductance; "little change or increase" follows only for E_GABA at or above threshold. Also the "compatible" cell attributes a reduction to "hyperpolarising GABA, i.e. the premise absent" while the sentence before says a reduction does not show E_GABA is normal (shunting reduces too). Rewrite: hyperpolarising, a large reduction; shunting, a smaller reduction; depolarising to threshold, none or an increase.
- **E-maturation / PAM**: "size seen in age-matched wild-type bursting tissue": wild-type slices do not burst (0 of 11). The comparator exists only under M09-driven bursts; say so.
- **E-maturation / chloride manipulation**: the prediction "effect larger at the younger end ... in knockouts and wild type alike" and the weakening outcome "a genotype difference in the age at which the manipulation stops acting" are inverted: a shifted time course in the knockout is what E-maturation predicts, so a genotype difference in timing favours it. Swap, or restate the prediction as "stops acting later (or earlier) in knockouts".
- **E-response / PAM**: weakening "normal agonist response" makes the burst test redundant with M05; the burst test adds nothing over M05 for this explanation.
- **PAM as one class**: E-compensation says "the modulator also acts on tonic receptors". True for some (extrasynaptic, delta-containing, neurosteroid-sensitive), not for gamma2-selective synaptic modulators; the row treats the class as interchangeable. Brown 2015 (26626485) adds that a neurosteroid tone shapes L2/3 mIPSC decay up to P15 in mouse, so even the sign of a modulator's added effect is age-dependent. Likewise "NKCC1-directed block or KCC2-directed enhancer" is one row; P08 shows that bumetanide hyperpolarises E_Cl (-7 mV, <P25, n = 7) but depolarised it in P54-P58 slices pre-incubated, so the sign depends on baseline chloride.
- Signs that do follow: E-supply and E-response chloride rows (no gradient effect expected); E-chloride chloride-manipulation row (reduction with E_GABA shift in the same cells); E-artefact rows.

## E. new_measurements.tsv

- **Order**: not by interpretability or cost. M03 (re-record sIPSC with block, per-animal summary) is the cheapest and decides whether the headline datum stands; it should come first. M01 is the only measurement that can test E-chloride and is interpretable alone at one age. M02 is a programme (it contains M01, M03, M07 and protein across ages), not one measurement: "required from the start" cannot mean the whole series.
- **Deferred but indispensable (conditional on M03)**: M05 (agonist response) with M06 (evoked IPSC, failures, paired-pulse): if the amplitude loss persists under M03, these are the only measurements in the file that separate supply from response; they are marked "no".
- **Redundant or low-yield**: M04 partly duplicates M05 for the response arm (it adds frequency and kinetics, worth keeping only if M05 is done); M08 counts add little over the identity of the recorded cells (a reporter in M01/M06 cells), because the hippocampal count deficit is already in the registry (P14).
- **Unit of replication**: animal, cells nested, per-animal means or mixed model: appropriate. Add litter explicitly where the knockout is bred from heterozygous pairs (it is: S-WT 4, S-HT 4, S-KO 5 animals in the source).
- **Design assumptions presented as data**: M01 "10-30 min stability as in the reference method (source fact P08)": in Rinetti-Vargas this is the time to form the perforation ("hyperpolarizing current steps ... for 10-30 min until appropriate recording conditions were obtained"), not a stability criterion; stability was shown separately in 4 cells over the drug time course. "At least two ages on each side of P13-P17" is a design assumption and the older side is limited by survival. M10 "titrated to the same percent of evoked synaptic charge removed" is a design choice (flagged as such). M09 should add E_GABA to its readout (C).
