## Decision this changes

* **The existing inhibitory datum cannot test the chloride gradient.** DATO (34634460 Methods, read at the locator and checked again by the verifier, also in the supplement): the sIPSC recordings used a 140 mM KCl pipette solution at −70 mV, chosen by the authors to enhance the chloride gradient; no glutamate-receptor blocker or GABA-A antagonist is named for them; no mIPSC, evoked IPSC, tonic current, agonist response, E_GABA or interneuron identity was recorded; statistics are on pooled events (controls 14 cells from 7 animals, knockouts 9 cells from 5 animals). INFERENZA: with E_Cl pinned near 0 mV by the pipette, the amplitude cannot report the native chloride gradient, and a raised native postsynaptic chloride cannot lower the amplitude through the postsynaptic driving force; a presynaptic or interneuron chloride effect on release is not excluded either. **The chloride-gradient explanation is untested by this datum, not excluded.**
* **The answer to the IT42 question is "undetermined".** Supply (reduced release or interneuron loss), response (fewer or altered receptors), chloride gradient, a change secondary to bursting, a maturational offset and a recording artefact all remain open. The amplitude loss itself is not yet beyond the recording conditions (unblocked excitatory events at −70 mV in KCl, a 4×SD detection threshold that makes the counted frequency amplitude-dependent, an undeclared frequency metric; the higher knockout input resistance argues against a pure artefact). An undetermined answer leaves the order of the investigations as it is; it neither moves a supply-side lever up nor the receptor-side and chloride-side levers up.
* **The measurements that separate the explanations, in order of interpretability** (`output/new_measurements.tsv`): M03 re-record the sIPSC with glutamate-receptor block, a fixed absolute threshold and per-animal summary (cheapest; decides whether the headline datum stands); M01 E_GABA by gramicidin perforated patch in knockout, heterozygote and wild-type littermates (the only measurement that can test the chloride gradient); M02 an age series of littermates across the ages the knockouts survive, with a survivor-selection control (separates a constant offset from a shifted time course; it does not show that a defect is correctable); then, if M03 confirms the amplitude loss, M05 agonist response under TTX and M06 evoked IPSC with failure rate, paired-pulse ratio and E/I ratio (separate supply from response); M07 tonic current and M09 wild-type slices driven into a burst state (compensation and a chloride shift secondary to bursting); M10 a matched non-GABAergic suppression control for any drug arm.
* **A burst reduction by a GABA-A positive modulator is compatible with every explanation and discriminates none** (`output/conditional_prediction.tsv`, 13 rows: synaptic-selective and extrasynaptic classes kept apart; chloride-gradient manipulations tied to E_GABA measured in the same cells). The sign of an action on the target is not the sign of benefit, and a burst reduction is neither benefit nor developmental recovery. No compound is nominated; different mechanisms are not described as interchangeable.
* **Maturation.** No source read gives a layer II/III sIPSC, E_GABA and KCC2 series at P13–P17 in mouse. Wild-type timetables differ by region and method (switch from about P9 to P21; KCC2 in prefrontal membranes still rising from P9 to P23; visual cortex inhibitory in vivo already at P3; layer 2/3 mIPSC frequency 1.2 to 12.9 Hz and decay 12.1 to 5.4 ms from P7–8 to P20–24 with a flat amplitude, sampled at P15 only). A P13–P17 genotype difference can be read as maturational only against an age-matched wild-type curve of the same preparation, which does not exist for this one.
* **Nothing changes** a rank, a card verdict or a work order; no experiment is commissioned.

## Counter-evidence and limits

* The authors caution that P13–P17 recordings, taken while the knockouts die at 3–4 weeks, may mimic a late-stage disorder; only one age window was studied.
* Bursts occur in 36 of 42 knockout slices against 0 of 11 wild-type slices: a burst-free knockout contrast is hard to obtain inside the paper, and a reduced sIPSC in a bursting slice fits both cause and consequence.
* Every wild-type time course read is another region, layer, species-region combination or method (in vivo, layer 5, prefrontal, mIPSC with clamped chloride). None is carried to the Wwox slice as a value.
* The 140 mM KCl internal also changes driving force for every genotype alike; its effect on apparent amplitude is not measured here.
* The repository statement that 10 of 18 reported p values lie below an event-level floor is cited by need id and was not re-derived.
* The weakest unreached item is the layer II/III mouse series itself: its absence is a bounded-search absence (some PubMed strings returned zero hits through query translation).
