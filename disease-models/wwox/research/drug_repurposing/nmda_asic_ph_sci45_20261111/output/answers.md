* **N1 (recombinant NMDAR).** DATO: rat GluN1-1a/GluN2A in oocytes (22-24 C, Mg-free, 200 uM NMDA + 10 uM Gly) has pIC50 7.21, Hill 1.45; with the exon-5 insert 6.96, Hill 2.70 (N1-D01, D02; proton_sensitivity PS01, PS02). INFERENZA: from those two fits 25 percent (exon-5 absent) and 13 percent (present) of the current is lost between 7.4 and 7.2, with a zero floor and no stated residual (D03). A compilation of other primaries puts GluN1/2B at pH 7.3-7.5 and GluN1/2A at 6.74-7.20, so 2B is not less sensitive than 2A there (D04, copied). Triheteromer proton midpoint, slope and residual: not found in three sources (D05); only indirect facts (D06).
* **N2 (native, juvenile).** DATO: acutely isolated P13-P19 rat prefrontal pyramidal neurons, 22-24 C, Mg-free: NMDA proton pH50 6.97; pH 7.6 to 7.1 removes 22 +/- 7 percent (peak) and 26 +/- 3 percent (steady state) (D07, D08; NE01-NE03). By subunit: not found (D09). Not layer II/III, not 35 C, reference pH 7.6.
* **N3 (ASIC1a).** DATO/INFERENZA: no current from 7.4 to 7.2; about 2-3 percent of maximal activation at 7.0; half-activation about 6.5 recombinant, 6.19-6.29 native; steady-state desensitisation midpoint about 7.1 after 60 s, so a fall 7.4 to 7.2 desensitises about 16 percent of channels and opens none; native cortical neurons show no significant current at 7.1 (D10-D12; PS16-PS19).
* **N4 (one cell, several targets).** Not found within the stated perimeter: no source has K2P, NMDAR and ASIC on one juvenile layer II/III cell or a 0.1-0.3 unit step with separated contributions. Closest: NMDAR plus ASIC on one cortical pyramidal neuron (large steps, room temperature, Mg-free); a fourth target (voltage-gated Na+) reported at abstract level (D13, D14, D15).
