# SCI45 follow-up N1 — proton sensitivity of NMDA receptors and ASIC1a: priors and the net-sign rule, written before the reading (2026-11-11)

Non-canonical. Labels DATO / INFERENZA / IPOTESI. A later change is a dated addendum.

## Priors (background knowledge, unverified; to be scored after the reading)
* **N1.** Recombinant GluN1/GluN2A is the most proton-sensitive of the diheteromers (half-inhibition near pH 7.2-7.4); GluN1/GluN2B is less so (about 6.9-7.2); the GluN1 exon-5 splice form reduces sensitivity.
* **N2.** A fall of pHo from 7.4 to 7.2 removes about 15-35 percent of GluN1/GluN2A current and less of GluN1/GluN2B current.
* **N3.** ASIC1a gives no current for 7.4 to 7.2: activation threshold near pH 7.0-6.9, half-activation 6.2-6.6; its steady-state desensitisation may start near 7.2-7.4.
* **N4.** No source puts K2P, NMDAR and ASIC on the same juvenile layer II/III cell in a pH step.
* **N5.** Native juvenile cortical or hippocampal evidence for proton inhibition of NMDAR exists, at room temperature and in cultured or slice neurons, not at 35 C layer II/III.

## Net-sign rule (software, after the reading and its verification)
For a pHo fall d from pH0 in {7.4, 7.2}, d in {0.1, 0.2, 0.3}: for each target the fractional change of its own current from the verified pK, slope and residual (K2P rows from SCI45 P1 with Imin; NMDAR and ASIC from this package). The table gives, per target, the **direction** (depolarising, hyperpolarising/inhibitory) and the **fraction of its own current**, with the pool size where the source gives one (K2P 0.26 / 0.53 from SCI45; NMDAR share of the burst from SCI42 A1 if measured, else unknown). A sign for the neuron as a whole is written **only** where all contributing targets have a source magnitude on a common axis (current at a stated potential); otherwise the table states directions only and "net sign not defined". The table does not say whether pH differs between genotypes.
