# Decision before inspecting WWOX kainate-expression results

The prior laboratory abstract-level audit already identified GluK5-containing kainate receptors as perampanel targets. The new question concerns the sign and relative magnitude of a postsynaptic kainate effect and an immature presynaptic release brake. Compare direct potencies in the primary pharmacology table; distinguish ion-channel current from possible G-protein signaling. Test for (1) at least +0.5 log2 units of Grik5 relative to Grik2 in both neuronal-deletion mouse regions with consistent sign under one-library omissions, and (2) a potential immature GluK1c substrate in the supplied Ensembl reference and hippocampal TPMs. The 0.5 threshold is a prioritization rule, not a significance threshold. Check Grik4, Neto1, Neto2 and bulk human organoids for context. RNA tests detect candidate availability, not receptor assembly, drug action, native presynaptic function or WWOX causation.
