## 1. Headline findings that can change the result

1. **CHEMBL4308825 is a review, not an experiment.** ChEMBL document record (raw/doc_CHEMBL4308825.json): Burnell ES et al., J Med Chem 2018, PMID 29446949, "Positive and Negative Allosteric Modulators of NMDA Receptors: Structure-Activity Relationships and Mechanisms of Action". Its Table 2 (PMC6368479, raw/burnell_pmc.xml) lists ifenprodil 39.5 / 0.11 / 29.1 / 75.9 uM (GluN2A/2B/2C/2D) from its ref 90 = Hess 1996 (PMID 8768735, human receptors in oocytes, "approximately 350-fold") and Ro 25-6981 52 / 0.009 uM from its ref 91 = Fischer 1997 (PMID 9400004, NR1C with NR2A or NR2B in oocytes, ">5000-fold", rat forebrain context, activity-dependent block). The review's footnote b ("calculated at human NMDA receptors") is attached to ifenprodil only. ChEMBL's "Homo sapiens / unknown origin" labels are curation of a compilation. The same 9 / 52,000 nM pair is also in CHEMBL1132364 (Tamiz 1999, rat) - one origin counted twice. Consequences: (a) "same document, same assay format, two NAMs concordant" is NOT CONFIRMED; it is two independent papers from different labs and years that agree on a 359x vs 5,800x window (both numbers recompute exactly: `q1_ratios.tsv`); (b) the ifenprodil arms state 3 uM glycine and -40 mV, the Ro 25-6981 arms state neither; (c) the Ro 25-6981 window is a rat/Roche datum, not human.
2. **The prediction `0.90 f + 0.025 (1-f)` is an upper bound, not a prediction.** The arithmetic is right (occupancy 0.9009 / 0.0247; Ro 25-6981 0.9174 / 0.0019: `q2_occupancy.tsv`) but it equates occupancy with inhibition. Ifenprodil inhibition is incomplete (Amico-Ruvio 2012, PMID 22936815: "The inhibition is incomplete", with reciprocal effects of glutamate, glycine, zinc, protons and polyamines); in GluN1/GluN2A/GluN2B triheteromers each NTD ligand "produces only partial inhibition" (Hatton and Paoletti 2005, PMID 15848804); triheteromers show distinct ifenprodil behaviour including altered binding kinetics (Hansen 2014, PMID 24607230); inhibition is pH-dependent, smaller at alkaline pH (Pahk and Williams 1997, PMID 9143010). Also the oocyte IC50 is a -40 mV, 3 uM glycine, unstated-pH number. Restate: `dI/I = sum_i f_i * Emax_i * occ_i` over GluN2B diheteromer, triheteromer and GluN2A diheteromer shares; the producer's formula is the Emax = 1, no-triheteromer ceiling. Illustrative (GENERATED, Emax not sourced): with Emax_2B = 0.6, f = 0.2/0.5/0.8 give 13/28/44% instead of 20/46/73%. Because a native "f" measured as ifenprodil-sensitive current already includes Emax and triheteromer effects, define f operationally as the measured fractional reduction by a saturating NAM in that slice (E0), and drop the occupancy model for the burst prediction. Only the rank ordering (NAM about 10^2 to 10^3 more selective than any pore blocker) survives as a mechanistic claim; the percentages should be removed.
3. **The technique-keyword explanation is wrong, and the shipped grade column is not reproducible.** `selectivity_p6.py` line 28: `TECH = ("patch","clamp","oocyte","flipr","efflux","thallium","flux","binding","displacement","enzyme","substrate")` already contains "clamp" and "oocyte". Re-running the unchanged grader on the shipped `relations_qa.tsv` and `assay_context_qa.tsv` (`q5_regrade.py`, `q5_regrade_all.py`) grades ifenprodil, Ro 25-6981 (CHEMBL4308825) and memantine (CHEMBL1146243) INTERPRETABLE, and 449 of the 603 shipped LIMITED rows regrade INTERPRETABLE (regraded totals 496 / 154 / 396 vs shipped 47 / 603 / 396). The reviewer notes that "explain" the LIMITED grade by a missing keyword describe a cause that does not exist. The cause of the shipped grades is not determinable here (assay-context state at grading time). Do not edit the grader's keywords; regenerate the table and, separately, add a provenance guard (see `q5_proposal.txt`): INTERPRETABLE must not be granted to a document that is a compilation. On that rule CHEMBL4308825 stays LIMITED for a better reason.

Delucemine (CHEMBL2106165, NPS-1506) and acamprosate (CHEMBL1201293; 89 activities, none on NMDAR targets, no mechanism record) have no NMDAR record in ChEMBL. Felbamate has about 70 GluN2B-single-protein (CHEMBL1904) rows of type Kapp (0.87 to 34.5 mM) and % inhibition (61 to 90%), invisible to the producer's IC50/Ki/Kd/EC50 filter. ## 3. Item 7: the discriminating design

* "Bursts fall faster than total NMDAR-current fraction if GluN2B specifically sustains them" is conditionally valid, with a weak form. Burst generation in a recurrent network is threshold-like, so any antagonist can produce a steeper-than-linear fall of incidence per unit current removed; a steeper curve for the NAM than for a linear reference is therefore not by itself evidence of subunit specificity. The valid test is comparative at matched, MEASURED fractional reduction of the isolated NMDAR EPSC for two mechanisms in the same preparation: NAM titration versus d-AP5 titration (competitive, no subunit preference: 0.14x to 0.6x GluN2A-preferring, i.e. mildly) and a low-trapping pore blocker (memantine, 0.9-3x). A leftward shift of the NAM burst-versus-current curve relative to both references supports GluN2B-specific sustaining; coincident curves support "total current limits bursting".
* Alternative explanations the design must exclude: (i) network effects: GluN2B on interneurons or on recruitment of recurrent circuits can shift the threshold without altering pyramidal current (record interneuron NMDAR sensitivity or add GABA-A tone controls); (ii) extrasynaptic and tonic GluN2B receptors (ifenprodil and Ro 25-6981 have preference for these in many preparations; "activity-dependent" block, Fischer 1997, means the effect grows during bursts and so is not comparable to a constant-fraction d-AP5 effect); (iii) presynaptic and glial NMDARs; (iv) off-target actions of ifenprodil (sigma, alpha-1 adrenergic: prior knowledge, UNVERIFIED here) - use two structurally unrelated GluN2B NAMs (Ro 25-6981 and CP-101,606 or radiprodil); (v) the P13-P17 range is inside the GluN2B-to-GluN2A switch, so f differs between slices and genotypes - measure f in the same animals, genotype-matched; (vi) a knockout-specific shift in NMDAR composition would itself be an effect of Wwox loss (E0 comparison KO vs WT, not only drug).
* Controls: equal-current d-AP5 titration (primary); a low-trapping blocker titration; TCN-201 as the GluN2A arm (its potency depends on glycine, prior knowledge UNVERIFIED here, so fix glycine and report it); fixed pH, glycine, Mg2+ and temperature, since ifenprodil potency depends on pH and glycine/polyamines; a PAM arm is a good bidirectional control but SAGE-718's EC50 79/86 nM shows it is not subunit-selective between GluN2A and GluN2B, so it cannot discriminate subunits, only gain.
