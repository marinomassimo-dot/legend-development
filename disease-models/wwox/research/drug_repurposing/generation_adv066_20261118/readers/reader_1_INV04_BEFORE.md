**(1) First three actions**
- Run a pilot. It must fix the variability of the 60-minute summary, the assay age, and a stimulus that gives wild type a measurable response.
- Set the genotype-specificity margin and the analysis plan before any data are taken. The margin is the interaction ratio, and the cut-offs are 0.7 and 1.43 (the material cites the in vivo brief, section 2.7, which I do not have).
- Re-power the main study from the pilot variability. Do not use the published effect size.

**(2) Arms, controls, measurements**
- Knockout and wild type, each treated and vehicle, analysed with a genotype-by-treatment interaction term.
- Wild-type pups at the assay age and at two or more older ages.
- Reference agents with different age profiles, as an assay-sensitivity panel.
- A selective T-type blocker beside the carded agent.
- Per animal: seizure type and a motor control.
- A dose check at each age.
- Primary measure: the 60-minute provoked-seizure summary. The interaction ratio is the knockout effect ratio over the wild-type effect ratio, on the log scale.

**(3) Promising, withdrawn, unresolved**
- The material covers one thread only: ethosuximide and the carded T-type suppression. It ranks no candidate or class against another.
- The conditional-proposal generator has no row for this investigation.
- The only evidence level given is a published suppression in knockouts. That paper has no genotype-by-treatment test.
- Withdrawn: the published effect size as a basis for power.
- Unresolved:
  - whether the suppression is specific to WWOX loss;
  - the rat age floor for this agent, which is an inference, and the mouse age floor;
  - the cell pharmacology of this agent, which is contested.
- No T-type current has been measured in any WWOX system. The held transcript sets do not sample the thalamus.

**(4) Cannot be concluded**
- That the suppression is genotype-specific, or that wild type responds equally.
- That T-type blockade is the mechanism.
- A firm sample size. The 180 to 320 animals per cell is a bracket. It comes from a coefficient of variation of about 0.6 to 1.2, read from means and error bars rather than reported values.
- The pup age used in the paper.
- Any ranking against other candidates.

**(5) Decision I could not take alone**
Whether to commit to a full study of roughly hundreds of animals per cell once the pilot reports. That needs the owner of the animal and resource budget to approve, and the ethics approval to cover it.
