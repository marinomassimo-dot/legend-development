**(1) First three actions**
1. State the null before measuring (H5: the GSK3 route is not engaged). Then measure baseline eIF2B epsilon Ser535 phosphorylation (human Ser540), total GSK3 and phospho-eIF2 alpha, with replicate lanes, in WWOX-deficient cells against corrected controls. The earlier Ser9 blot was a single lane.
2. Use genetic knock-down of GSK3 beta and alpha, with double depletion, to find the isoform.
3. Run phospho-resistant (Ser to Ala) and catalytically dead epsilon as epistasis. Check species numbering first.

**(2) Arms, controls, measurements**
- WWOX-deficient against isogenic corrected cells, stage matched.
- A selective GSK3 inhibitor and a second inhibitor of another class, beside lithium as a labelled non-specific comparator.
- An isogenic isoform-knockout check per compound, because selectivity is only 4-6-fold in live cells.
- An inositol-precursor add-back arm.
- ISRIB only as a discriminating probe, with an engagement control and exposure check.
- In the organotypic assay, d-APV at matched NMDAR-current reduction.
- A general anticonvulsant comparator, an age series in wild-type pups, and lithium regimen (single against repeated) as a design variable.
- Measure pS535, exchange activity, new-protein labelling, one functional readout, and the second need's readout in the same experiment.
- Controls the material names for the epistasis constructs: Ala cannot separate GSK3 from DYRK2, the dead form needs a control in tissue with intact Gsk3b, and the phosphomimetic is unvalidated.

**(3) Promising, withdrawn, unresolved**
The material ranks no candidate or class, gives no endpoint ranking and no evidence level. It names no repurposing candidate. Lithium is kept as a comparator only. The package states no reopen or abandon criteria, and nothing is withdrawn.

Documented Ser535 dephosphorylation covers only LiCl, SB-415286 and SB-216763 (CHO cells, abstract level), plus genetic Gsk3b loss in mouse neurons. It is undocumented for LY-2090314, laduviglusib and TDZD-8.

Unresolved:
- whether the effect is attributable to the kinase;
- whether the opposing push on the second need is tolerable;
- whether the inositol-depletion arm is GSK-3-free (it is not);
- the isoform;
- the timing of time-gated dosing, which has no source.

**(4) Cannot be concluded**
- That the GSK3 route is engaged in WWOX-deficient cells.
- Any benefit, or its direction.
- A genotype-specific lithium effect (one experiment shows an effect in all three genotypes).
- Transfer from CHO, retina or rat to human neurons.

**(5) Decision needing someone else**
Whether the lithium thread stays in the selection. This is a priority call between the kinase need and the second need, and the material does not say how much opposition on the second need is acceptable.
