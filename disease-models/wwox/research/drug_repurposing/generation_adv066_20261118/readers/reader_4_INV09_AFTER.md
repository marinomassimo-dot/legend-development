**(1) First three actions**
- Run the shared provoked-seizure pilot (same as INV-04). It sets variability, assay age and a non-floor stimulus. Lithium, the comparators and a vehicle group per genotype run in one session, with the regimen stated before data.
- In parallel, measure the baseline in WWOX-deficient cells against corrected controls: eIF2B epsilon pS535 (human S540), total GSK3, replicate lanes and p-eIF2alpha.
- Write the null before any measurement: the GSK3 route is not engaged.

**(2) Arms, controls, measurements**
- Lithium in Cheng's regimen as the replication condition. A second, chemically unrelated GSK-3 inhibitor with published selectivity and rodent brain exposure. A general non-GSK-3 anticonvulsant comparator. An inositol add-back arm (not GSK-3-free).
- Cells: GSK3A/B knock-down for isoform and double depletion. S535A and catalytically dead epsilon as epistasis (S535A cannot separate GSK3 from DYRK2). ISRIB only as a probe, with an engagement control. d-APV at matched NMDAR-current reduction in slice.
- Measure p-Ser9, total GSK-3beta and a kinase or beta-catenin readout in the same treated animals. Include an age series in wild-type pups and the second need's readout in the same experiment. Measure E_GABA by perforated patch.

**(3) Promising, withdrawn, unresolved**
- No overall ranking is given.
- Lithium's effect is called general, at abstract level and by inference.
- Lithium has the most paediatric exposure of the GSK-3 agents (label level; label from age 7).
- On development, the KCC2 branch beats an NKCC1 blocker on evidence level only.
- Ethosuximide goes first as a design order, not as efficacy.
- Withdrawn: the Kasahara 2019 P11 datum, and any claim that it bears on lithium or GSK-3.
- Unresolved: GSK-3 attribution, the beta-catenin sign, the myelination sign, E_GABA never measured, regimen-dependent sign, DYRK2, and cell-level selectivity.

**(4) Cannot be concluded**
- That the lithium effect is GSK-3-specific or knockout-enhanced. The 82 versus 54 percent difference cannot be shown to exceed noise.
- That GSK-3 is more active in the knockout. This is inferred from one p-Ser9 blot with one lane per genotype and conflicting ages.
- Any efficacy, safety or infant data.
- Any interaction size: the interaction needs about 180 to 320 animals per cell, so it stays exploratory.

**(5) Decision needing someone else**
Whether the lithium thread stays in the selection. That depends on whether the opposing push on the second need is tolerable, which is a value judgement. The material sets no threshold for it.
