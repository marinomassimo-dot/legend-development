# Independent review of the F/C/P positions against the sources (IT6 P2, producer part)

Actor: scientist-a. Branch `task/it6-p2`. Date: 2026-10-29. Non-canonical. No score, no probability, no ranking of treatments, no efficacy or dosing statement. This is a review of how the label of each card x need link follows from the sources; a different actor rebuilds the labels blind afterwards.

Inputs read: `priority_rules_it5_20261029.md`, `framework/scripts/repurposing_view.py` (`classify_rows`, `assign_need_position`), `coverage/assertion_pertinence.tsv`, `needs/needs.json`, all 31 cards and their `drug_assertions`, `README.md` "Roadmap iteration 5". Sources re-opened by me are listed per row ("opened"); everything else is stated "not re-opened" and rests on the card note and its locator.

Conventions for locators: `M<PMID>` = `disease-models/wwox/research/deepdive_manifests/PMID<PMID>.json`, `verbatim_locators.entries[N]`; `FT/` = the root checkout's `files/fulltext/` (untracked cache, read only); `PUBMED <PMID>` = abstract returned by the PubMed tool in this session; `CHEMBL-TSV` = `lucas_clarke_followup_20261026/chembl_named_activities.tsv`.

## 1. The five questions

**Is "favourable hypothesis" read correctly?** Mostly yes; three corrections.
1. F1 is a statement about one readout, not about the card. It was carried for C04 gene addition by the least central endpoint (GFAP-positive cell count at P5, supplement S8I) because the survival and motor rows had their comparator recorded as "not stated in the listed note" although the source states it (Figure 3B caption, `M42422765` entries [46] and [40]: survival "significantly extended relative to ... the KO + RI controls (p < 0.0001)"; Supplementary S4A, entry [27]: KO about 3.6 against high dose about 0.3). The comparator fields were corrected (section 4, D5). F1 stays; its carriers are now the endpoints a reader would name.
2. F3 ("from the target, intervention not tested in WWOX") was read as a favourable hypothesis about the intervention when for E02 and E03 it is carried by tool compounds, a nedosiran liver precedent and a fly genetic precedent, while the candidate itself showed poor engagement. E02 stiripentol: 9.9 percent inhibition of recombinant human LDHA at 500 micromolar, IC50 "greater than 500" (opened: `FT/PMID39769031_RicoMolina2024_PMC_r2.xml`, 2.4 Inhibitory Activity; `M39769031` entries [2], [10]); the review of the same drug states that effective LDH concentrations (42 to 500 micromolar) are "globally higher than therapeutic concentrations" and that free brain concentration is 0.2 to 1 micromolar (opened: `FT/PMID38443647_Bacq2024_PMC_r2.xml`). The label F3 is kept (its definition says "from the target"), but its counter-evidence is now counted and tagged (rule R1, data D1 and D2).
3. F2 groups reasons that differ in kind. Lithium (G01) is F2 because the effect is also present in wild type (a design limit: `M32000863` entries [22], [28]); for the symptomatic need that limit does not remove the benefit, it removes the claim of a GSK3beta-specific mechanism, which is a different need (G01 / GSK3B, P3). The page now says which limit failed (rule R4).

**Do counter-evidence items stay visible at first reading?** Not before this review. Examples found: E02 (poor engagement and exposure gap invisible, F3 without tag); EB-VGB-01 on myelination (P3 "nothing favourable", while the same compound has seven harm rows in card H02); E03 on PDH axis, lactate and seizure (DCA, harm and null rows live in card I01); H01 on seizure (P3 although the abstract reports a benefit in generalised convulsive models and a null in focal seizures); I01 lactate (C3 although the same card holds a favourable lactate-lowering engagement row); B01 (P3 hid a benefit/null pair). Corrected by R1, R2 and the tags R3.

**Is reading quality distinct from experimental soundness?** Not on the page: one criterion "no weakening flag" mixed `abstract_only`, `graphical_reading`, `partial_read` (how well the source was read) with `one_experiment`, `tool_compound`, `nonspecific_also_in_wild_type`, `case_level` (how sound the experiment is). R4 splits it into two criteria. In addition, reading depth was invisible on C and on F3 (D01 MYC C1 rests on two rows of depth UNKNOWN; EB-VGB-01 C2 on six rows at "queried, not full read"; E01 F3 on four abstracts; H02 myelination C3 on seven abstract-level rows). R3 adds a depth tag. Neither changes a label: depth qualifies a reason to study, it does not remove it.

**Is the existence of a next step distinct from its usefulness?** Yes in the rule, and it should stay so. `has_next` is true for every non-abstention card (P2 count: 0 of 49 links, before and after); each card has its own `next_measurement` text (checked for all 31). The label does not and must not say whether the step is informative or feasible. That judgement is the work order in section 6, written per need with its own feasibility statement. P2 is an unused guard; kept, because deleting a position after a data change is easier than re-adding one, and flagged here as unexercised.

**Is a result for one need transferred improperly to another?** Three cases.
- D01 A51, need MYC / progenitor state: the signed row DR-D01-002 is a null on layer-neuron markers (CTIP2), a downstream endpoint; the progenitor-fraction row DR-D01-001 compares with wild type only. The card itself says "progenitor identity is restored, layer-neuron production is not". The C1 label stays (a measured failure to recover is the reason to study), but the page now says the null is downstream of the need (R3, D3).
- EB-ETX-01: the same single experiment (A-ETX-1, Cheng 2020 Figure 7b) is the favourable row for both the T-type and the seizure need. Two F2 are one observation. Kept; stated here, not coded (a tag would add noise to a case of two links).
- Same compound in two cards (DCA in E03 and I01; vigabatrin in EB-VGB-01 and H02; CPP-115/vigabatrin in H02): a position is computed from the card's own rows. Pooling by compound was considered and not adopted (it would move positions by a rule the physician cannot see); the sibling tag makes the other card's null or harm rows visible instead (R3).

## 4. Rule changes made (all in `framework/scripts/repurposing_view.py` unless stated)

- **R1** `classify_rows`: `o_counter` now also counts target-engagement rows with sign `null` or `harm` (`te = [... "target_engagement_assay"]`). Before: an engagement assay that showed no engagement was invisible.
- **R2** `assign_need_position`: `support = o_fav + n_engagement_fav` is computed before the C3 test, and C3 requires `o_counter > 0 and support == 0` (was `o_fav == 0`). F3 already used `support`. A favourable engagement row is a favourable outside signal for both families. Effect: I01 lactate C3 to F3.
- **R3** tags, no new position: `reading depth: n signed row(s) carry this position, none read beyond abstract, query or unknown depth` (new `shallow`, `n_basis` in `classify_rows`); `the signed WWOX row(s) ... measure an endpoint downstream of the need` (pertinence flag `endpoint_downstream_of_need`); `same compound, other card X: n null or harm row(s) on this need` (new pure functions `card_compounds`, `sibling_counters`); the outside-counter tag now also on P3; a P3 tag naming that the only reason for P3 is the missing WWOX-system observation of the need.
- **R4** `classify_rows` / `assign_need_position` / `CRIT_IT`: the criterion "carries no weakening flag" is split into "no reading-limit flag" (`reading_depth`, `graphical`, `micrographs`, `partial_read`, `abstract_only`: how well the source was read) and "no design-limit flag" (`tool_compound`, `nonspecific`, `one_experiment`, `case_level`, `no_instrument`, `no_knockout_arm`: how sound the experiment is); "meets all three" becomes "meets all four". F1 is still the same set of rows (`WEAK_FLAGS = READING_FLAGS + DESIGN_FLAGS`).

Data corrections in `coverage/assertion_pertinence.tsv` (curated input), each traced to a source:
- **D1** DR-E02-004 sign `benefit` to `null` (stiripentol racemate 9.9 percent at 500 micromolar, IC50 "greater than 500": Rico-Molina 2024 Table 2, `M39769031` entries [2], [10]).
- **D2** new row DR-E02-007, target-engagement `null` (Bacq 2024: effective LDH concentrations above therapeutic concentrations; free brain 0.2 to 1 micromolar).
- **D3** DR-D01-002 flag `endpoint_downstream_of_need` added.
- **D4** new rows DR-H01-002 (benefit) and DR-H01-003 (null) on NEED-SEIZURE-CONTROL-SYMPTOMATIC (PUBMED 40165594).
- **D5** comparators of DR-AAV-SURV-HD, DR-AAV-SURV-LD (Figure 3B caption, `M42422765` entry [46]) and DR-AAV-MOTOR-HD, DR-AAV-MOTOR-LD (Supplementary S4A, entry [27]) changed from `not_stated_in_the_listed_note` to `untreated_same_genotype`.

Tests: `test_repurposing_view.py` gains `test_it6_p2_review_rules`; two existing assertions were adapted (criteria names; C04 carriers, now five rows). The guard `RulesUntouched.test_position_rules_are_byte_identical_to_the_verified_revision` pins a hash of `classify_rows` + `assign_need_position` + `overview_positions`; it had to be re-pinned (`244882c7cd5d1965` to `e94c0aae178aaf2d`) because these three functions are exactly what this review revises.
