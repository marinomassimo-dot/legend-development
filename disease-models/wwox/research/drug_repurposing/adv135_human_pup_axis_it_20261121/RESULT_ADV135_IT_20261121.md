# ADV-135 / RP-035 result (IT, 2026-11-21): the pup axis in published human WWOX-DEE and SCAR12 reports

Reference-genotype class only; aggregate published literature only; hypothesis level; no dose, no schedule, no recommendation; nothing here is medical advice. No individual-level record is created or linked: every count below is a report's own count.

Limit stated first: every reader here (the producer delegate and this window) is a model of the same family; no clinician or native specialist read a source. The pre-registration (`PREREGISTRATION_ADV135_IT_20261121.md`, committed before any report was read for these analytes) fixed the thresholds and definitions used below.

## Answer first

**Neither threshold is met, for calcium or for lipids. The strand does not close and does not hand back: calcium and lipids are a NAMED MISSING MEASURE in the human reports read.**

* **Calcium (total or ionised), PTH:** named in no human report. 0 reports state it normal at an infant age, 0 abnormal. The only member of the row ever named is vitamin D: normal in 1 of the 2 cases of one report (age not printed), high in the single case of another (21 months at writing; spectrum membership borderline). Neither is infant by the pre-registered definition, and vitamin D is not calcium.
* **Lipids:** 0 reports normal at an infant age, 0 abnormal at an infant age. The one human lipid statement is "low" in a single case at 21 months (not printed as a test age; not infant), a case the authors themselves place at the edge of the spectrum and found through a single homozygous missense with in silico support.
* **Glucose:** abnormal in 1 report (hypoglycaemia in the first hours of life, 1 of 1 case, no value); not reported elsewhere. One report cannot meet the two-report rule for any state, and glucose decides nothing alone by the pre-registration.
* **Phosphate, cortisol/ACTH/adrenal, growth hormone/IGF-1:** named in no human report (ACTH appears only as a treatment).
* **Thyroid:** normal in 3 reports (one case each, plus 1 of 2 in a sibling pair); a "transient neonatal hypothyroidism" in 1 of 9 cases of one series, stated as history with a mother who had treated hypothyroidism and no value.
* **A metabolic work-up in general:** stated normal or negative in 11 reports, with the analytes named in the sentence (amino acids, organic acids, lactate, ammonia, acylcarnitine, very long chain fatty acids, CSF neurotransmitters). None of these sentences names calcium, glucose or lipids, so none of them counts for those rows.

**What this changes for the pup axis.** Nothing is settled in either direction. The human reference-genotype class has no stated early calcium or lipid value in any report read, so the sign neither translates nor fails to translate; the cheapest settling measurement stays the animal add-on already written as dated line 9.12 of the in vivo brief (serum calcium with albumin or ionised calcium, glucose, body weight and a milk-spot score at P7 to P14, germline null against the neuron-targeted line). A human work-up that did include a serum calcium or a lipid profile and called it normal is the observation that would change this, and the case-report style does not print it.

## Overlap and independence

The Romanian case of Tarta-Arsene 2017 (PMID 28721938) is probably one case of the 20-case series of Piard 2019 (PMID 30356099); neither prints a calcium or lipid value, so no tally changes. The five-patient series of Chong 2023 (PMID 36537114) contains two previously published patients not identified in the text read. Tabarki 2015 (PMID 25416187) restates two earlier reports. The literature tables inside Al Baradie 2022 and Sapuppo 2026 (PMID 42193054) restate earlier cases. Reviews restate and are not counted as reports. Identity of cases across reports by shared recurrent variants cannot be resolved from the text.

## Counters, carried

* Case reports select severe cases; a work-up in a severely affected child that omits calcium says nothing about milder children.
* A normal work-up at one age says nothing at another: most general-work-up sentences print no test age.
* Unreported is not normal: of the 24 reports and reviews read, none names calcium in a human case and 23 name no lipid value.
* The mouse statements (hypocalcaemia, hypoglycaemia, hypolipidaemia, impaired steroidogenesis, bone phenotypes in the knockout mouse) appear in 5 of the reviews and papers read and are excluded from every count above.
* The producer delegate's partial coverage (one report read in chunks) and the 15 unread reports bound every "NR" in this table: it means "not in the text read", not "not in the literature".
