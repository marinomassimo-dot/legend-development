**Item 2, the L1 reagent-pair premise.** The conflict is real, and the producer's statement that no source tests the two readings on one OPC preparation is correct within my perimeter. DATO: Mei 2016 (immunopanned OPCs from knockout mice): clemastine and benztropine raised MBP+ cells about five-fold in wild-type, Chrm2-5 and Hrh1 knockout OPCs, and the effect was completely abolished in Chrm1-null OPCs, which themselves differentiate about five-fold more; Chrm1-null OPCs also gave more myelinating OLs on rat DRG; OPC-restricted Chrm1 deletion left 8-week uninjured myelin unchanged. Hubler 2018 (EpiSC-derived OPCs): benztropine, clemastine, tamoxifen and a fourth compound accumulate zymostenol and zymosterol (n = 2 wells, 24 h) and inhibit EBP directly in a biochemical assay; six antimuscarinics all inhibited M1, M3 and M5 in CHO reporter cells at 2 uM, only the two with EBP inhibition enhanced MBP+ formation; additivity with ketoconazole absent; no Chrm term and no knockout anywhere in the text. Allimuthu 2019: CW9956, CW9009, CW3388 (and 76 analogues) are EBP inhibitors (zymostenol accumulation, direct enzymatic assay for CW9956 and CW1143 and CW3388, Ro 48-8071 blocks CW9956), with little M1, ER and KOR affinity in cell assays (values in figures not viewed); TASIN-1 is cited as a previously characterised EBP inhibitor with little M1 affinity, and Hubler used it at 100 nM. All EBP work is EpiSC-derived OPC monoculture; none in a co-culture; none in a Chrm1-null cell. Three points the producer missed change L1: (1) the Chrm1-null control as written (clemastine or benztropine in Chrm1-null OPCs; loss means M1) is not interpretable because the null baseline already equals the drug effect (occlusion); (2) an M1-selective antagonist exists and works in neuron-free primary rat OPCs and in a primary rat cortical neuron-OPC myelination assay (MBP/Tuj1 segment length): PIPE-307, Ki about 4.6 nM, more than ten-fold over M2 to M5, clean 47-target panel at 10 uM, EC50 38.6 nM, efficacy comparable to T3, an M3-selective antagonist inactive, and in EAE improved VEP latency and g-ratio (PMID 39083422); its EBP activity is untested, so it is a clean muscarinic arm and not yet a clean non-sterol arm; (3) the same paper shows acetylcholine in OPC-conditioned medium about 12 nM and exogenous ACh inhibiting differentiation (IC50 about 58 nM): an antagonist needs a ligand, so the four inert antimuscarinics in Hubler may reflect missing cholinergic tone in EpiSC-derived OPCs rather than a sterol-only mechanism (INFERENZA, untested). The conflict is already recorded in LEGEND (`phenotype_first_it29_20261105/README.md`, muscarinic class left unresolved), so L1 is a design change, not a new observation. Informative contrasts: an EBP inhibitor without M1 affinity in Chrm1-null OPCs; an M1-selective antagonist alone and with an EBP inhibitor (additive or not, Hubler's own logic); EBP loss with an M1-selective antagonist; M1 expression and ACh tone in the preparation used.
