## H1: the neuron sets a ceiling on sheath structure that an intrinsic enhancer does not lift
Chain. Observation (DATO, held): Wwox-null DRG neurons carry fewer myelinating-class and more premyelinating-class OPC-derived cells than wild-type neurons (two experiments, n = 4 versus 4). Mechanism (IPOTESI): the OPCs on null neurons arrest because the axon or contact is permissive-poor, not because the OPC cannot mature. Lever (INFERENZA): an OPC-intrinsic maturation accelerator, as a **pair** that separates two readings (clemastine or benztropine; an EBP inhibitor without M1 affinity), with Chrm1-null OPCs. Compound or probe: clemastine, benztropine verified in primary sources for marker-level OPC culture and for the Tcf4 het EM/CAP result; EBP inhibitors verified only in held ledger rows (in vitro); **no compound verified in a neuron-driven model**. Prediction: the pair raises the MBP+ marker share on both neuron genotypes; internodes per axon length rise on wild-type neurons and rise less on null neurons, leaving the null-versus-wild-type gap at sheath level. Experiment: the factorial of `minimum_comparison.md`.
Competing explanations. (i) The gap is an OPC-intrinsic block that these reagents cannot reach (the OPC is the limit but not by sterol or M1); (ii) the gap is axon-density or calibre (fewer axons per field, not fewer sheaths per axon); (iii) medium T3 or lipid saturation makes any reagent a ceiling effect.
Counter-evidence (DATO, read now): in the Tcf4 het model, a correction at marker level did not restore total myelinated-axon density (V01): this shows a ceiling can exist even with an intrinsic defect, and weakens the specificity of "ceiling means neuron". Held: OPC-restricted Chrm1 deletion leaves uninjured myelin unchanged (P14).
Rejecting outcome: the reagent pair closes the null-versus-wild-type gap at sheath level on null neurons (interaction absent, gap gone): then the block is intrinsic or permissive.
Decision changed: whether NEED-MYELINATION orders a genotype-by-intervention comparison with an interpretable reagent pair (instead of staying at INV-06's premise measurement).
Unresolved premise: the pair is interpretable only if the M1 versus EBP question is settled on one OPC preparation (OQ1); the sterol readout in a co-culture is not shown.
Transfer limits: peripheral DRG neurons, mouse, embryonic, seizure-free; OPCs from neonatal cortex; adult-injury evidence for the reagents; no human cell data read.
Expected under H1: gap persists on null neurons after the reagent. Expected under the strongest alternative (OPC-intrinsic or permissive block): gap closes. Novelty: (a) yes; (b) not checked; (c) not validated.

## H2: a supply limit at the axon-glia interface, separable by a lipid arm
Chain. Observation (DATO, read now): when astrocyte lipid synthesis fails the oligodendrocyte count is normal and myelin is thin, and a lipid-enriched diet from E15 raises fast callosal responses from about 5 to 50 percent of slices (V05-V07). Mechanism (IPOTESI): a persistent neuronal or axonal defect can limit myelin by changing membrane-lipid or energy supply at the axon-glia unit rather than the OPC programme. Lever: lipid supply (cholesterol, fatty acids) and ketone bodies as an axonal substrate. Compound or probe: **none verified for the dish**. Prediction: a supply arm raises internode number on null neurons only if the limit is supply; ketone bodies, if the limit is axonal energy, may change a conduction-level readout more than a structural one (V09). Experiment: add the supply arm to the factorial with a tracer-sterol check.
Competing explanations: signal limit (neuregulin/NMDAR mode); OPC-intrinsic block; consequential change in axon calibre.
Counter-evidence: cholesterol did little where the barrier is intact (V10); CNP-SCAP delay normalised by itself with age (P20); the astrocyte-lipid model is not neuron-driven; exposure began at E15.
Rejecting outcome: the verified supply arm raises sheath number equally on wild-type and null neurons (a general effect), or does not move it at all.
Decision changed: whether a lipid arm belongs in NEED-MYELINATION's next test beside INV-06's activity arm.
Unresolved premise: an interpretable dish reagent (OQ4). Transfer limits: diet in vivo to a dish; astrocyte to neuron defect; mouse. Expected under H2: supply arm lifts null-neuron sheaths. Under the strongest alternative (signal limit): no lift unless the neuregulin/BDNF mode is also set. Novelty: (a) yes; (b) not checked; (c) not validated.

## H3 (weakest): more but shorter sheaths on a persistent axonal defect: structure is the next level, not conduction
Chain. Observation (DATO, held): neonatal wild-type clemastine raised oligodendrogenesis and slowed callosal conduction with fewer nodes. Counter-pattern (DATO, read now): in Tcf4 het, clemastine raised the N1/N2 ratio. Mechanism (IPOTESI): where the axon is the limit, extra OPC output raises number of sheaths but not their length, so conduction lags markers. Prediction: in the dish the internode length distribution on null neurons shifts shorter after the enhancer while internode number rises. Experiment: add internode length to H1's readouts.
Competing explanation: no shift; length set by the axon regardless of reagent. Counter-evidence: the Tcf4 het conduction gain. Rejecting outcome: internode length unchanged while number rises, or length rises. Decision changed: whether a dish structural result may be read as a prelude to conduction. Unresolved premise: whether internode length in the DRG co-culture is quantifiable at the available resolution. Transfer limits: in vitro to conduction untested; wild-type neonatal evidence. Novelty: (a) a variant of INV-06's four-level rule; (b) not checked; (c) not validated. Explanation-weight only; kept to avoid dropping the conduction level.
