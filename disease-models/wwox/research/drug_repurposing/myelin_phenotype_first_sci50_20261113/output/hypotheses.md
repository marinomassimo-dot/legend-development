# SCI50 producer P: hypotheses (generation phase; non-canonical research; not medical advice; no dose, ranking or recommendation)

Three, written without softening, then the verifier's turn. Labels DATO / INFERENZA / IPOTESI. Novelty class: (a) new combination for LEGEND; (b) novelty against the literature: **not checked** beyond strings Q01-Q16; (c) prediction not validated.

## H1: the neuron sets a ceiling on sheath structure that an intrinsic enhancer does not lift
Chain. Observation (DATO, held): Wwox-null DRG neurons carry fewer myelinating-class and more premyelinating-class OPC-derived cells than wild-type neurons (two experiments, n = 4 versus 4). Mechanism (IPOTESI): the OPCs on null neurons arrest because the axon or contact is permissive-poor, not because the OPC cannot mature. Lever (INFERENZA): an OPC-intrinsic maturation accelerator, as a **pair** that separates two readings (clemastine or benztropine; an EBP inhibitor without M1 affinity), with Chrm1-null OPCs. Compound or probe: clemastine, benztropine verified in primary sources for marker-level OPC culture and for the Tcf4 het EM/CAP result; EBP inhibitors verified only in held ledger rows (in vitro); **no compound verified in a neuron-driven model**. Prediction: the pair raises the MBP+ marker share on both neuron genotypes; internodes per axon length rise on wild-type neurons and rise less on null neurons, leaving the null-versus-wild-type gap at sheath level. Experiment: the factorial of `minimum_comparison.md`.
Competing explanations. (i) The gap is an OPC-intrinsic block that these reagents cannot reach (the OPC is the limit but not by sterol or M1); (ii) the gap is axon-density or calibre (fewer axons per field, not fewer sheaths per axon); (iii) medium T3 or lipid saturation makes any reagent a ceiling effect.
Counter-evidence (DATO, read now): in the Tcf4 het model, a correction at marker level did not restore total myelinated-axon density (V01): this shows a ceiling can exist even with an intrinsic defect, and weakens the specificity of "ceiling means neuron". Held: OPC-restricted Chrm1 deletion leaves uninjured myelin unchanged (P14).
Rejecting outcome: the reagent pair closes the null-versus-wild-type gap at sheath level on null neurons (interaction absent, gap gone): then the block is intrinsic or permissive.
Decision changed: whether NEED-MYELINATION orders a genotype-by-intervention comparison with an interpretable reagent pair (instead of staying at INV-06's premise measurement).
Unresolved premise: the pair is interpretable only if the M1 versus EBP question is settled on one OPC preparation (OQ1); the sterol readout in a co-culture is not shown.
Transfer limits: peripheral DRG neurons, mouse, embryonic, seizure-free; OPCs from neonatal cortex; adult-injury evidence for the reagents; no human cell data read.
Expected under H1: gap persists on null neurons after the reagent. Expected under the strongest alternative (OPC-intrinsic or permissive block): gap closes. Novelty: (a) yes; (b) not checked; (c) not validated.

## H2: a supply limit at the axon-glia interface, separable by a lipid arm
Chain. Observation (DATO, read now): when astrocyte lipid synthesis fails the oligodendrocyte count is normal and myelin is thin, and a lipid-enriched diet from E15 raises fast callosal responses from about 5 to 50 percent of slices (V05-V07). Mechanism (IPOTESI): a persistent neuronal or axonal defect can limit myelin by changing membrane-lipid or energy supply at the axon-glia unit rather than the OPC programme. Lever: lipid supply (cholesterol, fatty acids) and ketone bodies as an axonal substrate. Compound or probe: **none verified for the dish**. Prediction: a supply arm raises internode number on null neurons only if the limit is supply; ketone bodies, if the limit is axonal energy, may change a conduction-level readout more than a structural one (V09). Experiment: add the supply arm to the factorial with a tracer-sterol check.
Competing explanations: signal limit (neuregulin/NMDAR mode); OPC-intrinsic block; consequential change in axon calibre.
Counter-evidence: cholesterol did little where the barrier is intact (V10); CNP-SCAP delay normalised by itself with age (P20); the astrocyte-lipid model is not neuron-driven; exposure began at E15.
Rejecting outcome: the verified supply arm raises sheath number equally on wild-type and null neurons (a general effect), or does not move it at all.
Decision changed: whether a lipid arm belongs in NEED-MYELINATION's next test beside INV-06's activity arm.
Unresolved premise: an interpretable dish reagent (OQ4). Transfer limits: diet in vivo to a dish; astrocyte to neuron defect; mouse. Expected under H2: supply arm lifts null-neuron sheaths. Under the strongest alternative (signal limit): no lift unless the neuregulin/BDNF mode is also set. Novelty: (a) yes; (b) not checked; (c) not validated.

## H3 (weakest): more but shorter sheaths on a persistent axonal defect: structure is the next level, not conduction
Chain. Observation (DATO, held): neonatal wild-type clemastine raised oligodendrogenesis and slowed callosal conduction with fewer nodes. Counter-pattern (DATO, read now): in Tcf4 het, clemastine raised the N1/N2 ratio. Mechanism (IPOTESI): where the axon is the limit, extra OPC output raises number of sheaths but not their length, so conduction lags markers. Prediction: in the dish the internode length distribution on null neurons shifts shorter after the enhancer while internode number rises. Experiment: add internode length to H1's readouts.
Competing explanation: no shift; length set by the axon regardless of reagent. Counter-evidence: the Tcf4 het conduction gain. Rejecting outcome: internode length unchanged while number rises, or length rises. Decision changed: whether a dish structural result may be read as a prelude to conduction. Unresolved premise: whether internode length in the DRG co-culture is quantifiable at the available resolution. Transfer limits: in vitro to conduction untested; wild-type neonatal evidence. Novelty: (a) a variant of INV-06's four-level rule; (b) not checked; (c) not validated. Explanation-weight only; kept to avoid dropping the conduction level.

## Revised after V13 (2026-11-13; the text above is kept)

Labels DATO / INFERENZA / IPOTESI; novelty (a) new combination for LEGEND, (b) not checked beyond the strings logged, (c) not validated. Non-canonical research; not medical advice.

**H1, narrowed (kept).** Its rejecting outcome was not discriminating: persistence of the gap after the reagent is predicted by H1 and by my own alternative (i) (an OPC-intrinsic block the reagents cannot reach); only the reverse pairing (Wwox-null OPC on wild-type neurons) separates them, so it moves to the first block. H1 also could not tell a neuron ceiling from reagents that are sub-maximal (Hubler: liothyronine additive with sterol-route enhancers). Restated: *after maximal OPC-intrinsic stimulation on a shared OPC pool, a residual null-versus-wild-type gap that follows the neuron in the reverse pairing points to the neuron.* Design additions: dose-response with a monoculture ceiling per preparation, a T3 co-arm, a conditioned-medium arm (H4), and a primary cortical neuron-OPC assay (MBP/Tuj1 segment length, as in the PIPE-307 paper) in addition to the DRG co-culture; pre-specify the interaction scale (additive or ratio; a proportional lift leaves an absolute gap with no ceiling). Even in an intrinsic or global-allele defect (Tcf4 het) density was not restored, so an unclosed gap is not diagnostic of a neuronal cause. H1 novelty for LEGEND is low; class (a) applies only to the pair-by-genotype design.

**H2 dropped.** No neuron-side datum supports the step from an astrocyte lipid defect to a neuron-driven one; in the double mutant the high-fat diet failed where the oligodendrocyte cannot make lipid; no dish reagent is verified. Kept only as one candidate arm.

**H3 dropped.** Contradicted by the neonatal clemastine re-read (V13): node number fell (fewer, not more, segments), internode length tended to increase non-significantly, myelinated-axon count fell, and the authors attribute the effect to microglia (absent in the dish). The Tcf4 conduction gain is an amplitude ratio, a different readout. Internode length stays as a measurement inside H1.

**H4 (new): Wwox-null neurons release a soluble inhibitor of OPC maturation.**
Chain. Observation (DATO, held): OPCs on Wwox-null DRG yield fewer myelinating-class cells (about 49 versus 29 percent hand-read, n = 4 versus 4, two experiments). Observation (DATO, V13): in neuron-specific Tsc models CTGF secreted by neurons is described as necessary and sufficient to block OPC maturation in vitro, rapamycin reverses the marker deficit, and neuronal CTGF loss restores percent myelinated axons but not g-ratio. Mechanism (IPOTESI): the null-neuron deficit in a seizure-free dish is carried by a secreted product (not contact, not activity). Lever: neuron-side perturbation (mTOR inhibitor as reference) and conditioned medium on OPC monoculture with neutralisation of a candidate. Compound or probe: none verified for Wwox (rapamycin and RAD001 verified only in Tsc1 models; CTGF-neutralising antibody only in Ercan). Prediction: medium from Wwox-null neurons lowers the MBP+ share of OPC monoculture relative to wild-type neuron medium, matched for neuron number and viability; a candidate-neutralising reagent removes it.
Strongest alternative: contact or substrate integrity (no medium effect, deficit only in co-culture and with fixed null neurons); second: medium differs by neuron number or viability; third: a short-lived factor lost in conditioning.
Discriminating outcome. A null-versus-wild-type medium difference at or above the co-culture's hand-read gap (about 20 percentage points, held; the monoculture variance is unknown and must be measured first) with the positive-control medium (Tsc2-knockdown neuron medium, per Ercan) working in the same run supports H4; no difference with a working positive control rejects a soluble inhibitor released under these conditions and favours contact or substrate (not short-lived factors, not activity). No positive-control effect makes the assay uninformative. Detection limit: set from the monoculture's own between-preparation variance, units independent preparations.
Decision changed: whether INV-06's soluble-product item has an answer and whether neuronal-side compounds (not OPC enhancers) become the lever class. Unresolved premise: Wwox-null neurons are not shown to release any factor; the candidate is unidentified. Transfer limits: DRG, mouse, embryonic, seizure-free; Tsc models are not Wwox; Ercan read by V13 at queried-not-full-read depth. Novelty: (a) yes for LEGEND (CTGF and neuronal Tsc1 myelination as a neuron-signal lever are absent from the repository except R50); (b) not checked; (c) not validated.

## Final form after the V13 recheck (Orchestrator, 2026-11-13; the sections above are history)
**H4 (the soluble-inhibitor hypothesis) is not a new combination for LEGEND.** The conditioned-medium test is already INV-06 X1 arms (b) and (c) (recorded in it36 ALT2); what is new is a **candidate mechanism from neuron-specific Tsc models** (CTGF, an authors' in vitro claim, no Wwox datum) and the **controls the old arm lacked**. Final form, conditional: *Wwox-null neurons release a factor that lowers OPC maturation (IPOTESI; no row links Wwox to CTGF or any secreted product).* Controls required for it to discriminate: a fresh-medium baseline and a mixing arm (a missing soluble cue versus an inhibitor), neuron purity (non-neuronal DRG cells carry the null genotype too), a dilution series, the B27/T3 caveat (medium depletion including T3), a positive control (recombinant CTGF or Tsc2-knockdown neuron medium), and a detection limit from the monoculture's own variance (not the 20-point threshold borrowed from the co-culture). Novelty: (a) modest, a refinement of INV-06 X1; (b) not asserted; (c) unvalidated. Decision changed: whether INV-06's 'soluble product' item can be closed before any compound is ordered. **H1 (the neuron ceiling)** stays conditional and is handed on only after gate 0: T3/T4 measured in the medium, the deficit reproduced blind, the OPC window checked with PIPE-307 tone and sterol engagement, and a clean rewrite of its block (reverse pairing first, a monoculture ceiling by dose-response, submaximal doses). **H2 and H3 are dropped** (H3 contradicted by the neonatal clemastine re-read; H2 has no neuron-side datum and the Camargo double mutant failed).
