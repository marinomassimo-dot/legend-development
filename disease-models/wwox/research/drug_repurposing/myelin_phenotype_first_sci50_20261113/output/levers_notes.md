# SCI50 producer P: levers chosen (non-canonical research; not medical advice; no dose, no ranking, no recommendation, no gene therapy)

Labels DATO (a source states it, read at the depth named), INFERENZA (my reasoning), IPOTESI (untested). Table: `levers.tsv`; evidence rows V01-V22 in `evidence.tsv`; perturbation rows P01-P38.

## What the search found, in one paragraph
DATO. Across Europe PMC strings Q01-Q16 and the PubMed tool (see `queries.tsv`), no source treats a **neuron-driven or axonal** hypomyelination with a compound and reads sheath structure or conduction. The one clear neuron-driven model found (Fig4-null; neuron-specific expression rescues; abstract only, P16) has a genetic rescue and no drug. Every compound result at sheath or conduction level sits in an **oligodendrocyte-intrinsic** model (Tcf4 het, P03-P05; PLP overexpression, P21-P22; alpha-synuclein, abstract, P15), in a **non-neuronal, non-oligodendrocyte** model (astrocyte SCAP, P17-P19) or in injury and wild type. This is a statement about the search perimeter (16 strings, one revision, one day), not a proof of absence.

## L1: the reagent pair (the lever chosen first)
Identity. (a) clemastine or benztropine; (b) an EBP inhibitor chosen for lack of M1 affinity (TASIN-1; the CW9956/CW9009/CW3388 family: identities and the sterol result come from held ledger rows of Hubler 2018 and Allimuthu 2019; I could not re-read them, the PMC page returned a captcha); (c) Chrm1-null versus wild-type OPCs. No compound is nominated for use.
- DATO (read now, text only): in the Tcf4 het model clemastine normalised OPC and CC1+ shares, shifted the uncompacted-myelin proportion and the g-ratio, and raised the callosal N1/N2 ratio, but **did not restore total myelinated-axon density** (V01-V02, P01-P05). The authors call the defect cell-autonomous (V03): this is an intrinsic-defect case.
- DATO (held, not re-read): the same two compounds raise MBP+ formation in OPC monoculture by a sterol phenotype (zymostenol, zymosterol) and independent EBP inhibitors work without M1 affinity (P11, P13); muscarinic antagonists lacking EBP inhibition did not (P12); but the genetic M1 result says the drug effect is lost only in Chrm1-null OPCs (P14). INFERENZA: these two statements are not both exclusive; they have not been tested together on one OPC preparation in what LEGEND holds. **Whether two chemotypes act on one target is not assumed** (shown for EBP in monoculture; not shown to be the only route for clemastine).
- DATO (held): in wild-type neonatal mice markers rose while callosal conduction velocity fell (P09-P10): a marker does not prove function. In Tcf4 het all three levels moved the same way (P01-P05). So the marker-function link is context-dependent, not shown.
- Role: probe pair, with clemastine as the reusable-drug comparator. Causal, compensatory or consequential: the OPC maturation delay on null neurons is a **candidate consequence of the neuron** (premyelinating-class accumulation on null DRG, P31), not shown causal; correcting it is tested, not assumed (the Tcf4 het result shows density not restored even when maturation is corrected).
- Label: most compound evidence for this lever is adult injury or wild type (lysolecithin, cuprizone, hypoxia; P14, P34, P37); transfer to development is **not established** and neonatal wild-type clemastine slowed callosal conduction.

## L2: lipid and substrate supply (the lever chosen second; weaker)
- DATO (read now): astrocyte-lipid-synthesis loss left oligodendrocyte counts normal and made myelin thin; fast callosal responses were present in about 5 percent of slices on standard diet versus 50 percent on a lipid-enriched diet from E15 (P17-P19). This is a defect outside both the oligodendrocyte and the neuron. It is **prevention** (exposure from E15), partial, slices pooled.
- DATO (read now): in an intrinsic PLP-overexpression model a ketogenic diet improved axonal energy resilience more than it improved myelination (P21-P22); cholesterol did not reverse the axonal mitochondrial change the ketogenic diet reversed; dietary cholesterol did little where the barrier is intact (P23; n = 2 patients, no control).
- IPOTESI: if the Wwox-null neuron limit is substrate or membrane supply, a supply arm raises internode number; if the limit is a signal, it does not. Role: **proposal without an interpretable reagent** in the dish (no source read verifies a cholesterol carrier or fatty-acid mixture in a DRG-OPC co-culture; B27 composition is partly confidential and medium lipid content is unmeasured).
- Transfer to a neuron-driven defect: not tested.

## Not chosen, and why
Neuronal-signal manipulations (activity, neuregulin, BDNF): the held evidence is context-dependent (P28-P30: no effect of TTX without added neuregulin; minus 82 percent with NMDAR block when neuregulin present; less activity raised markers in neonates) and is a **mode of the culture**, so it enters as an arm of the minimum comparison, not as a lever. Thyroid agonists: held, abstract level, with INV-12's T3 baseline step (P35). GSK-3: sign divided (P32-P33). HDAC: only a genetic loss is held (P38). GPR17: not opened (no PMID reached). Glucocorticoid-receptor agonists: one study carries most rows (P34). ISR: Jimpy rescue is small in absolute terms (P24; held); the eIF2B abstract ties ISR to the sterol pathway (P25; abstract only).
