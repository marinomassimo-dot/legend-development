# SCI50 producer P: minimum comparison (one page; non-canonical research; not medical advice)

**Question.** DATO/IPOTESI. Which pharmacological perturbation can raise a functional component of myelination while the neuronal defect persists, and does a limit sit with the neuron or axon or with an intrinsic block of the oligodendrocyte?

**Preparation.** Mouse co-culture as in the held Wwox paper (DRG neurons from E13.5 null and wild-type embryos, nonneuronal cells reduced, OPCs from wild-type P0-P2 cortex, 9-11 days), seizure-free by origin and checked for spontaneous firing before OPCs; the reverse pairing (Wwox-null OPCs on wild-type neurons) as a second block, not first. Medium: B27 and N2 are present; **step 0 is a measurement of T3 and T4 in the complete medium** (INV-12), one B27 lot for all arms; no deficiency of any factor is inferred from the medium. Cortical neurons are a second, later preparation to remove the peripheral-neuron limit.

**Indispensable arms (each changes the interpretation).**
1. Neuron genotype factor: wild-type vs Wwox-null neurons, with the same OPC pool.
2. OPC-intrinsic reagent pair: clemastine (or benztropine) and an EBP inhibitor chosen for no M1 affinity; vehicle; time-matched vehicle. Two timings kept apart: from plating (prevention) and after the null-neuron deficit is documented (recovery).
3. Mechanism-separating control: Chrm1-null versus wild-type OPCs in monoculture (neuron-free) with both reagents, plus sterol profile (zymostenol, zymosterol) as target engagement. If this arm fails, the pair is not interpretable and the dish arms stop.
4. Neuronal-signal arm: neuregulin- or BDNF-type factor with and without NMDA-receptor block (the held Lundgaard switch), because the activity arm is mode-dependent.
5. Supply arm (H2): only after OQ4 gives a verified reagent; otherwise omitted.
Unit of analysis: independent neuron-OPC preparations (embryos or litters), not coverslips or fields; no pooling of cells as n.

**Discriminating endpoint.** Sheath structure: MBP+ internodes aligned with axons, number per axon length and internode length, with the marker level (MBP+ share, PDGFRa, BCAS1) read beside it and axon length per field as the denominator. Next level named, not claimed: callosal-slice CAP or optic-nerve conduction in S-KO animals.

**Expected results.**
- Intrinsic or permissive block: the reagents close the null-versus-wild-type gap at marker and sheath level (no genotype-by-reagent interaction, null reaches wild-type).
- Limit set by the neuron or axon (signal, contact, substrate): the reagents raise marker share on both genotypes; sheath number or length on null neurons stays below wild-type plus reagent (interaction); the neuregulin/BDNF mode or the supply arm lifts null neurons only if the limit is that.
- M1 versus sterol: the clean EBP inhibitor acts in Chrm1-null OPCs while clemastine loses effect there if M1 carries it; both act if EBP carries it.
- Consequence only: the gap follows axon density or calibre, not internodes per axon length.

**Inconclusive if.** Marker rises with no sheath read; no sterol engagement; T3 baseline not measured; no positive effect in wild-type neurons (no assay window); coverslips counted as n; the null-neuron deficit is not reproduced in the preparation used.
