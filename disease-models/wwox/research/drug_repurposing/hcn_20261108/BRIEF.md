**Goal.** Translate sag, rebound and resting potential into discriminable pharmacological hypotheses, keeping apart: identity of the mechanism; origin of the alteration; harmful, compensatory or mixed role; the possibility of a benefit by intervening.

**1. Baseline and reuse.** Retrieve conclusions, corrections and relevant passages from `../functional_it13_p3a_20261031/README.md`, `../function_pharm_it14_p3_20261101/README.md`, `../function_pharm_verify_it14_p3v_20261101/README.md`, `../phenotype_first_it29_20261105/p3_D/README.md`. Consume the relevant results of the T-type (`../ttype_20261107/`), NMDA (`../nmda_inv05_20261107/`), PV (`../pv_inv15_20261108/` if present) and GSK3 (`../gsk3_inv09_20261107/`) packages; do not reopen them whole. The Kv7 lead is already present: judge what the new report adds.

**2. Correct the electrophysiological premise.** Reuse the Methods and Table 1 of PMID 34634460 (held full text under `files/fulltext/`; 7 receipts). Sag is a voltage change during a fixed current step, not an isolated HCN current. Input resistance is measured: do not treat it as absent. Check how initial potential, the excursion reached, resistance and step duration influence sag and rebound. Keep WT, heterozygote and knockout separate: some properties are shared, others differ, and the comparisons are not all equivalent. Compare at least: greater HCN conductance; different activation or distribution of HCN; HCN unchanged but recruited differently by other membrane properties; combined contributions of HCN, potassium currents and T-type. HCN is a candidate to test, not an identification already concluded. Kv7/M-current and potassium leak conductances are not synonyms.

**3. Correct the refutation criteria.** If a verified HCN block removes sag but does not improve bursts, separate support for HCN's contribution to sag from lack of benefit or possible worsening of the circuit; it does not refute the biophysical mechanism; a worsening by a compound does not by itself show compensation (consider off-target and cell compartments). An ineffective block does not refute HCN without adequate engagement. A recovery obtained by opening Kv7 can be compensation for another defect and does not prove a Kv7 lesion. Total HCN amount and activation curve do not always separate expression from gating: consider surface expression, isoforms, subcellular distribution and modulation. Explanations can coexist.

**4. Qualify the new leads with bounded searches.** A. Membrane lipids: PMID 39548079: system, lipid perturbation, effect on channels, controls; PMID 37732182 is linked as the preprint of the same work: do not count them as independent confirmations. Look only for the decisive bridge: are there WWOX data on cholesterol, phosphoinositides or nanodomains in the relevant neural cells? Liver or circulating-lipoprotein data are not enough. If the bridge is missing keep the hypothesis with the measurement that would open it; do not translate the precedent into a supplementation proposal. B. Trafficking: PMID 24403084 documents HCN1-filamin A regulation; the scaffold function of WWOX alone does not show it takes part in this interaction; keep the methodological precedent and the WWOX link separate; seek a direct link only if it can change priority. C. Prior activity: qualify PMID 33547341: the adaptation follows activity deprivation; do not transfer automatically to the hyperexcitable WWOX condition; homeostatic adaptation is not necessarily protective in every circuit.

**5. The minimum discriminating comparison.** A proportionate first experiment characterising the HCN contribution in the relevant cells, controlling voltage, resistance and protocol; then separate the cell effect from the burst effect. Qualify the probe: ZD7288 has documented off-target actions, dependent on system and exposure (verify in a primary source: at most one new source); an intracellular application to one cell does not test block of the whole network; poor brain penetration of a tool does not invalidate ex vivo use nor show the target is useless. For Kv7 and T-type keep the arms separate at first; a combination alone does not attribute the result to single mechanisms; add it only to answer a new question. Pair network activity, cell function and non-specific suppression controls; normalising the resting potential is not automatically a benefit. Do not deduce opposite pharmacological effects only from opposite developmental trajectories in pyramidal and PV neurons: measure, or keep the response of the different compartments explicitly open.

**6. Up to three hypotheses, no quota to fill.** For each: WWOX observation; proposed mechanism and the missing bridge; qualified compound or probe if available; action, cell and context; competing alternative; favourable result and the result that would weaken the strategy; next decision. Do not present increased HCN, lipid origin and trafficking as three independent confirmations: some are nested explanations of one phenomenon. For GSK3 reuse the cautions of the dedicated package (Ser9 does not measure activity on channels; CHIR99021 does not distinguish isoforms by itself); add no new general lithium deepening.
