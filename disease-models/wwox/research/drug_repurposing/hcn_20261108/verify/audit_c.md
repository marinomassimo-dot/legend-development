## 3. Hypothesis items

## 6. H1-H5

- H1 (derived estimate): arithmetic confirmed (section 2). The estimate is sound as a group-mean illustration under these assumptions: (1) Rin from the I-V slope equals the steady-state deflection at -200 pA (the range of the slope is not stated; inward rectification would make Rin x I an over-estimate where Ih/Kir are active); (2) the table's means can be combined as ratios of means; (3) sag is measured as peak minus end of step, so peak = steady + sag; (4) no variance. Wording fix: "driven about 8 mV further than WT (about -105 versus -101 mV)" conflates two numbers: HT and KO are about 8 mV further from rest (28.6 v 20.6 mV) but end only about 4-5 mV more negative in absolute potential (-106.2 and -104.8 v -101.0 mV) because their rest is 4-6 mV more depolarised. The final clause "sag as a fraction still rises (5.4 to 8.2 percent)" is not a tested result: propagating the printed errors (SEM assumed, independence assumed, Monte Carlo) gives about 5.5 +/- 1.2 (WT), 6.7 +/- 1.5 (HT), 8.3 +/- 1.3 percent (KO); the WT-KO difference of 2.8 points is about 1.5 standard errors (not distinguishable from zero), so say "the means rise; not shown to differ".
