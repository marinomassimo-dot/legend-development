## 0. Decision (first)

**Undecidable at n = 1 pooled library per genotype; the nucleus-level data neither support nor exclude "fewer PV cells" and cannot separate it from "less Pvalb per cell".** Pvalb is almost undetected in this deposit (70 UMI in 3182 control nuclei, 37 in 2920 KO nuclei; 53 and 33 nuclei with any Pvalb UMI); only 3 to 7 inhibitory nuclei per arm carry any Pvalb, so the PV-class count is 3 vs 5 (rule PV-A) or 12 vs 15 / 19 vs 18 (Pvalb-independent proxy PV-B), with exact intervals that include any ratio from 0.3 to 3. Directional, descriptive observations that a verifier can recompute:
1. **Pooled Pvalb is lower in KO, in the direction of the bulk P3a result but about half the size in log2.** KO/ctrl UMI share 0.59 (log2 -0.77, exact 0.38 to 0.89); relative to neuronal content (post hoc) 0.52 per Snap25 and 0.49 per Gad1+Gad2 (log2 -0.93 / -1.04; exact 0.32 to 0.79). Bulk GSE158681 S contrast: log2 -1.70. The nucleus-level deposit does not reproduce the bulk magnitude and does not locate it.
2. **PV-class composition: no detectable change** (PV-B/inhibitory 0.15 vs 0.12, ratio 0.80, p 0.53 descriptive; PV-A 3/80 vs 5/125; S2 sensitivity 0.80). **Pvalb per PV-class nucleus: undecidable** (PV-B: 1 UMI in 12 nuclei vs 1 UMI in 15; the Pvalb-defined PV-A set is circular and 3 vs 5 nuclei). Kcnc1 (a PV-class channel gene) is lower in inhibitory nuclei (normalised mean 2.60 vs 1.31, bootstrap interval -2.25 to -0.53) and in all neurons, while Gad1, Lhx6 and Kcnc2 are not: a pattern compatible with less PV-programme expression per cell (reading b) but equally with fewer PV-programme-high cells; one pool, so not a replicate result.
3. **The largest composition change is oligodendrocyte lineage, not interneuron**: oligodendrocyte-called nuclei 12.0% vs 2.4% of all nuclei (17.2% vs 2.9% of non-doublet), OPC 2.9% vs 6.2% (2.2-fold), pooled Mbp UMI share ratio 0.30 (log2 -1.72), matching the bulk myelin-set drop (log2 -0.8 to -1.8) and reading (d): composition shift plus delayed oligodendrocyte maturation. Because oligodendrocyte nuclei are depleted in the KO pool, every other class, including inhibitory nuclei (2.5% to 4.3% of all nuclei; ratio to neurons 6.4% vs 7.8%, ns), is inflated in the total-nucleus denominator. This dilution is the reason fractions are also reported against neurons and inhibitory nuclei.
4. **Maturation (reading c) is supported descriptively for neurons**: immature genes are higher in KO neurons (Nnat +2.64 normalised units, interval 2.14 to 3.21; Sox11 +0.84; Dcx +0.49; all positive; excitatory Nnat 1.92 to 2.84), Atp1a3 and Snap25 unchanged. A pool-level technical difference (depth, library batch) cannot be excluded.
5. **Same contrast as the P3a bulk result**: the sample titles are S_Ctrl1-4 vs S_KO1-4 ("Wwox deletion in neurons", hippocampus, P17), the same S label as the GSE158681 samples behind the Pvalb log2 -1.70 (S_Ctrl_1-2, S_KO_1-3). Same genetic contrast; different samples or at least a different assay (nuclear RNA); animal overlap is not stated in GEO.

What this changes for P3a: the "PV-selective transcript deficit" cannot yet be read as cell loss; the nuclear data point to a modest Pvalb-programme reduction at most, and show that the larger bulk consequence of the deletion is oligodendrocyte-lineage composition. Reopening condition for NP3A1: per-library nucleus counts (library index lost in the deposit), the raw reads/BAM (SRA SRP285669) to re-aggregate by library, or PV immunostaining counts in the neuron-targeted deletion.

## 3. Confounds, stated

* **Doublet/ambient rule**: the pre-specified rule flags 955 control nuclei (30.0%) vs 510 KO (17.5%) as doublets, mostly neurons carrying Mbp/Plp1 ambient RNA (median UMI of flagged nuclei 8236 and 9301, about twice a typical neuron's; the myelin ambient load is much higher in the control pool, Mbp 12276 vs 3356 UMI). So the primary tables remove more control neurons than KO neurons. S2 (post hoc) restores them: inhibitory nuclei 120 vs 142 (4.6% vs 5.0% of non-doublet), PV-A 5 vs 5, PV-B 19 vs 18 (ratio 0.80 again), Pvalb-detected inhibitory 7 vs 6; the conclusion (no resolvable PV change) does not depend on the rule.
* **Ambient Pvalb**: Pvalb UMI rate in glia-called nuclei is 0.00 to 0.09 per 10^4 UMI in both arms, versus 0.5 to 1 Pvalb UMI per 10^3 UMI in PV nuclei; ambient Pvalb is low and similar, but ambient myelin RNA differs 3.7-fold between pools, so pool-level technical difference is real. More than half of control Pvalb UMI (36 of 70) sit in flagged-doublet nuclei (11 of 37 in KO): the apparent KO deficit is concentrated where identity is least certain, which is another reason not to localise it.
* **Unassigned neuron-like nuclei** 6.4% vs 14.6% (pan-neuronal markers without excitatory or inhibitory markers; could be dentate granule cells, immature neurons or low-quality nuclei): unresolved, and it lowers the excitatory/neurons fraction (0.77 to 0.66) without that being a biological claim.
* One pool per arm, three depth/QC differences (mean UMI similar, 5809 vs 5705), no library identity: all p values are descriptive, none is a test of genotype.
* RNA is not protein is not function: a lower Pvalb or Kcnc1 transcript count is not fewer PV cells, not fewer PV synapses, and not lower inhibition. Direction of change is not role (damage, compensation, composition stay separate); the oligodendrocyte shift could be consequence, not cause, of the E/I change.
