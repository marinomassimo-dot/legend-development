### Scientific selection before laboratory work

**Standing operator direction, 2026-11-19.** The objective is a compelling scientific choice of one candidate intervention and its priority experiment for each of the existing objectives: broad functional recovery and partial or bridge benefit with a more conservative benefit/risk aim. An unresolved question belongs to a specific proposition and context, not to an automatic rejection of a whole candidate. If no candidate is convincingly preferable, state that and pursue the most useful remaining analysis or alternative; do not manufacture two winners.

The prices previously mentioned explained why experiments should be selected carefully. They were not a request for quotations, a monetary ranking or a dependency on operator-supplied costs; a laboratory might work pro bono. External contacts, spend and clinical decisions retain their existing boundaries. Computational and token resources still matter to efficient execution.

Choose by comparison, not by dossier completeness. Generate credible alternatives, reuse current knowledge, execute all pertinent in-silico analyses that could materially change their relative standing, and follow a better-supported alternative when one emerges. Pick methods for the question: existing-data reanalysis, pharmacology and exposure, causal alternatives, perturbation data, transferable systems, constrained mechanistic models or targeted structural work. No universal sequence, mandatory method, exhaustive tool sweep, new gate or approval is created. When further analysis is unlikely to change the choice, explain the remaining uncertainty and why experimental testing is the useful next step.

Make the comparison functional and context-specific: the benefit sought, genotype or variant class, cell type, developmental window, exposure plausibility and competing adverse effects. Acute seizure or burst suppression does not itself establish broad developmental recovery; a drug class, supplement label or prior paediatric use does not establish a conservative risk profile. General antiseizure activity may still be useful for the bridge objective; disease specificity and practical benefit are separate questions.

Test what could reverse the choice: rival mechanisms and model structures, uncertain parameters, source dependence, context transfer and comparable exposure. Simulations are predictions conditional on their assumptions; agent agreement and repeated reuse of one dataset are not independent confirmation. Use held-out observations, simple baselines and known controls where informative and available. A small or contaminated hindcast neither validates a predictor nor prohibits other scoring methods. Scores, ordinal comparisons and explicit judgements are replaceable aids, never efficacy probabilities without supporting calibration; unknown is not zero benefit.

Retain the useful outcome-to-decision table, controls and source traceability of the brainstorm dossiers. Their weakest-link and assay-sharing views describe preparation and gaps, not candidate superiority. A low-evidence link is not automatically the next link to resolve; missing clinical proof is not a universal bar to preclinical selection. What an in-vitro, ex-vivo or in-vivo study can establish depends on its model, intervention and endpoint, not on a price category. Do not require every causal detail before a functional comparison, nor treat observational clinical data as the default prerequisite for all bridge candidates.

The scientific output for each objective names the preferred candidate, its strongest rival and why the preference holds, the analyses actually run and their before/after consequences, assumptions that could overturn it, and one priority experiment with interpretable outcomes. Separate new possibilities from predictive performance demonstrated. ADV coordinates and follows consumption by IT/SCI/HE; analysis and implementation continue without awaiting a quote, an operator prompt or a laboratory contact. Current evidence labels and ranking records change only through their existing scientific routes.

## Guiding principle

> **Rendere durevole la capacità di lavorare e migliorarsi, mantenendo semplice cambiare direzione.**

Keep improvements lean, flexible, modular and reversible. Reuse existing mechanisms;
keep tools, plugins, scripts and patterns easy to adopt, replace or remove. Preserve
reusable guidance at its canonical home, without duplicating prompts in role contracts
or adding rigid gates and approval layers.

## Research objectives

Keep both broad, durable functional recovery and useful partial benefit open, including
drugs, supplements and dietary interventions where a biological rationale warrants study.
Genotype/lesion dependence and depth of benefit are separate dimensions. Gene replacement
belongs in broad-recovery comparisons; increasing protein abundance or normalising an RNA
profile alone does not establish restored function or complete recovery.

Combine two complementary inputs: qualified WWOX experiments and mechanisms, and external
perturbation data/methods that can suggest previously unlisted interventions. Evidence from
another disease is a methodological or mechanistic precedent until transfer to WWOX is
tested. Predicted effect, observed effect and priority for the next research action stay
distinct. A low model rank alone establishes neither inefficacy nor harm.

## First predictive experiment: bounded and reversible

Choose the smallest question for which an interpretable comparison is possible. Inspect
available samples, biological replicates, perturbation contexts and applicable reuse terms
before selecting the method. A curated signed subnetwork can be a first trial; it is not
labelled a validated causal model or a patient molecular twin.

- Fix the biological question, reference state, candidate universe, comparison methods,
  evaluation measures and resource budget before examining the evaluation results. Use
  existing analysis conventions and files; do not create a benchmark framework.
- Keep the same usable inputs across conditions. Compare an external-only condition with
  a WWOX-informed condition when testing the value of local knowledge, without exposing
  held-out answers through the curated network or cards.
- Retain cell type, concentration, time and signature identity. Include appropriate
  matched/randomised controls, frequent-hitter checks and sensitivity to network structure;
  do not repeat a known LINCS confound as new evidence.
- Prefer an independent biological contrast or dataset for evaluation. Multiple samples
  from one subject or library are not independent patients. If independence or replication
  is insufficient, label the result exploratory and name the missing comparison.
- Preserve inputs, versions, configuration, outputs, costs and counter-evidence. Return
  added value, no added value, or inconclusive with a reopening condition. Model robustness
  and recovery of known examples do not establish prospective clinical accuracy.

Use public, appropriately licensed data and existing lightweight computation first. A
free academic access request is not a redistribution licence. NeMoCAD/BioNAV is an external
methodological reference, not an assumed installable dependency. ToolUniverse is tried on a
capability the adapters lack; LINCS, TxGNN and MATRIX retain their existing measured limits
until new evidence, availability or terms justify reopening them.

## Presentation and comparison

Build the selection funnel from measured units: **catalogued → computationally usable →
actually evaluated → mechanistically pertinent → ready for a named next test**. Show unknown
and excluded counts with reasons. Distinguish model percentile, research priority and
calibrated clinical probability; do not display the last without supporting validation.

The pathway view links a need to an intervention through typed evidence. State whether
"coverage" means a target association, a directional prediction or an observed effect,
show overlap and the denominator, and keep unknowns visible. A pathway-coverage percentage
is not a percentage of disease treated. Compare adverse or opposed signals separately from
beneficial hypotheses; broader pathway engagement is not automatically better.

Use the current static view, filters, comparisons and exports before adding infrastructure.
Prepare the public deployment from the distributable package, with a reproducible build and
rollback. Respect existing publication authority and any operator approval already granted;
prepare the reviewable deployment before requesting only a genuinely missing reserved
decision. Keep private communications and individual-level information out of public outputs.

## Standing method

For each completed compound deep dive, prepare an English, printable [research brief](view/research_reports/README.md) and link it from the corresponding public investigation.

**Turn compound decisions back into research.** Keep the [compound register](continuous_compounds/README.md#comparisons-by-research-question) readable for families and auditable by clinicians. Make the informative evidence, the main limit and the comparison that would change the decision visible beside each developed lead. A compound explicitly named in a public investigation should have a single shareable analysis or a clearly pending entry. A deferred intervention can still be an informative probe. As a scientific result changes a compound's interpretation or makes a comparison possible, update the compound register and give the unresolved comparison to ADV in the existing portfolio or next MEETING. ADV compares this with current opportunities, including myelin, inflammation and glia, and gives the chosen question to Orchestrator for dispatch. At consumption, record what changed in the compound evidence, question or disposition. If results suggest complementary mechanisms, first determine whether the readouts are independent and the individual effects interpretable. Keep a combination hypothesis conditional.

**Second-pass pilot.** Start from the existing circuit, myelin/glia, energy and developmental dossiers in the comparison table. Use Ethosuximide and Perampanel as references and select six contrasts that can change a decision, including at least one pathway outside seizure suppression. For each selected question, write competing explanations and expected observations before running the new analysis. Look for a transferable perturbation from another disorder and for a credible disconfirming result. Test against the current dossier or a simple baseline and record whether the comparison, priority or next experiment changed. Use independent data for a predictive evaluation when available. Separate blind prediction, sensitivity analysis and retrospective explanation in the existing prediction ledger. Use Sol for the scientific pass, a targeted independent Astra read where an alternative could change a decision, and cheaper parallel extraction only for distinct sources. Use DREAM to look for a shared discriminating comparison. Offer the founder a bounded structural or kinetic question when its result could change that comparison, and use DisMech for independent evidence retrieval and counterexamples. Select at most one experiment per Foundation objective after the in-silico comparisons are consumed. No new registry or approval step is needed.

- **A question is a step of the mandate record, and its result must be found where it was returned (IT28).** A repurposing question that deserves a batch rides as a `challenge` object on a step of the task record the monitor already reads; `framework/scripts/challenge_batch.py check` validates its fields, routes it by nature (identity or measure: targeted reading; shared premise: thematic batch; need without interpretation: exploratory batch), reports whether its sources were already read (a repeat without a stated reason is flagged, a motivated re-evaluation is not) and whether every named consumer file carries its id. ## Delivery sequence and stop condition

Start with R1–R3 after reconciling the latest delivered iteration. R8/R9/R11 use existing
scouting and licence work alongside it. Then apply R4–R7 to a real question in each research
objective and expose the result through R10. Maintain R12 rather than rebuilding it.

Each increment closes with a reproducible output, an observed limitation and the next
decision it enables. A failed source is not a negative biological result; an abstention
names the search performed, missing datum and reopening condition. Continuous improvement
does not require an endless session: stop an increment when its comparison is resolved and
no further low-cost action clearly improves scientific integrity, safety or throughput.
