## Answer first

**Statement 1 holds with one refinement that matters for wording. Statement 2 holds. Statement 3 is too strong as written.**

1. **Statement 1: the 139.4 uM riluzole EC50 (PMID 21273422, PMC6623616). Holds; refine "attributed mainly to TREK-2" (DATO-full).** The value is in the text: "a dose–response curve for riluzole in the blockers solution provided an EC 50 of 139.4 μ m (supplemental Fig. 1" (Q1; the curve, its n and any Hill slope are in the supplemental figure, not read). Preparation: native current in mouse superior cervical ganglion neurons, perforated-patch whole-cell, room temperature (Q6, Q7); not a cloned channel. **Attribution:** the whole-cell current is attributed to "TREK-1 and/or TREK-2", with TRAAK "not discarded" (Q2); the "mainly TREK-2" statement (Q5) comes from cell-attached single-channel patches, TREK-like activity in 16 of 32 patches and TREK-2-like properties in 12 of those 16 (75%) (Q4, Q3). So the EC50 belongs to a whole-cell current of a TREK-1/TREK-2 (possibly TRAAK) mixture, and the TREK-2 weight comes from a different recording mode on a subset of patches. The row's "attributed mainly to TREK-2" is the authors' own phrase and is not wrong; it can be read as assigning the EC50 to TREK-2 alone, which the paper does not do.
2. **Statement 2: no primary Ba2+ concentration-response in 50-200 uM for TASK-1, TASK-3 or THIK-1. Holds, with two further rows and one context point (two routes, one channel read in full).** For THIK-1 route A is the IT44 delegate (the original, Rajan 2001, not reached) and route B is mine (a field-tagged PubMed query): Kang 2013 (PMID 24081450, PMC3972372, page read) tested THIK-1 in HEK293 cells with Ba2+ at a single concentration, 3 mM, which together with bupivacaine and quinidine was among the only three of 25 agents giving about 50% or more inhibition (Q8): a single point at 3 mM, not a concentration-response and not in the window. For TASK-3 (abstract-level only): Holter 2005 (PMID 15781965) reports a CHO-expressed mutant TASK3 current "blocked by acidic pH and millimolar concentrations of barium": millimolar again. **Context the row does not carry (DATO-abstract):** Hopwood and Trapp 2005 (PMID 16020457), a native TASK-like current in rat dorsal vagal neurones, "inhibited by BaCl2 (IC50 465 microM)" and attributed to TASK-1-containing channels. It is native not cloned, and its IC50 lies above the 50-200 uM window; it is the nearest concentration-response to the window found. If a window point is wanted, a Hill curve with that IC50 and slope 1 would give about 10 to 30% inhibition at 50 to 200 uM (INFERENZA, slope and holding potential unknown, not to be used as a measured value). The originals for TASK-3 (Kim 2000) and THIK-1 (Rajan 2001) were not reached by the delegate or by me (no PMCID; publisher pages not attempted), so the row's wording "was found" is correct and "does not exist" would not be.
3. **Statement 3: "the K2P tools are single-pH measurements, none tested at a second pH". Too strong (DATO-abstract).** Deshar 2024 (PMID 38975879) used pancreatic cancer cells "adapted to various pH conditions" and reports that riluzole hyperpolarised membrane voltage, "most likely by opening of pH-sensitive K+ channels such as TREK-1"; its keywords include BL-1249. That is not a channel-resolved pH series (membrane voltage in cells, attribution hedged), so the narrower claim, that no pH series was found on the cloned channels the delegate read, may survive; the universal "none tested at a second pH" does not. Abstract only; the body was not reached (no PMCID).

## Proposed wording (the owner decides; four languages follow)

* Statement 1: "The one primary EC50 reached for that modulator (139.4 micromolar) is a whole-cell current in mouse sympathetic neurons carried by TREK-1 and/or TREK-2 (TRAAK not excluded); single-channel patches point mainly to TREK-2; it is not a cloned TREK-1 or TRAAK value; the link from the review row to that paper is an inference."
* Statement 2: unchanged; optionally add "nearest: a native TASK-like current, IC50 465 micromolar (rat dorsal vagal neurones)" and "THIK-1 and TASK-3 single points at 3 mM and 'millimolar'".
* Statement 3: "the K2P tools read are single-pH measurements on the cloned channels; one cell-level report tests riluzole in cells adapted to several extracellular pH values (abstract only)".
