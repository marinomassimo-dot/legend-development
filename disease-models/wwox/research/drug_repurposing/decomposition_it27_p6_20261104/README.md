## What the decomposition is (so it is not oversold)
The engine already sums signed contributions over every admissible walk from every target of a family (`multitarget_it24_p6_20261104`). Per-target contributions, opposing contributions and paralog pairs are therefore existing outputs, and the sum of them is not a new capability. A target only appears if a signed walk reaches a seed: a target with no path contributes nothing, which is silence, not absence of action. The decomposition uses the listed targets, not measured potency. Sensitivity (`reverse_it24_p10_20261104`): with a 1 percent mass floor the SAR-407899 conflict, the pracinostat multi-positive label and the idronoxil multi-positive label all become single-target. No favourable and contrary item below is added into one number.

## P7: hypotheses worth deepening (from P5/P6 and verified WWOX inputs)
Honest count: **one** worth deepening (H1); one kept as a re-emission with a reason (H2) that I do not count as new work; no new molecule, no gene replacement. Table: `hypotheses_it27p7.tsv`.

**H1 - Is catalytic HDAC inhibition a valid lever on the HDAC5 -> SLC2A1 thread at all? Class: new question in a known thread.**
Chain. WWOX observation: SLC2A1, HK2, LDHA up in the human neural deposits (one derivation counted once; the progenitor arm failed its gate); P47T mouse concordant, not significant (Slc2a1 log2FC +0.27 PFC adj 0.09, +0.21 CTX, +0.18 HPC, -0.16 CB; Ldha +0.42 PFC adj 0.015) -> scenario: glycolytic/glucose-transport upregulation is part of the loss phenotype (role, harm or compensation, UNKNOWN) -> lever: lower HDAC5-dependent transcription of SLC2A1 (edge HDAC5 -T(+)-> SLC2A1, one non-neural muscle reference) -> required action: reduce HDAC5's transcriptional output; whether a catalytic-site inhibitor does that is not established, since class IIa HDACs are largely scaffolds with little deacetylase activity (BK, stated in P5 as not verified) -> compound: pracinostat (pan-HDAC, contested HDAC5 potency), TMP269 (class IIa, identity-blocked), HDAC6-selective SS-208/KA1010 as negative controls; all in the table -> context: human neural cells (hNPC/neurons), not muscle; CNS exposure UNKNOWN for all -> hypothesised benefit: lower SLC2A1 expression; a molecular effect, not functional recovery; partial benefit stays open -> counter-evidence: edge rests on one muscle reference and nanatinostat was REJECTED_INFORMATIVE on it; HDAC5 mRNA is nominally lower in P47T (PFC -0.20, CTX -0.36 log2FC, adj p 0.19 and 0.11) while Slc2a1 is nominally higher, the wrong direction for HDAC5-driven induction if mRNA tracked activity (it need not); one origin -> discriminating experiment, genetic arm first: HDAC5 knockdown (or catalytic-dead rescue versus knockdown) in hNPC/neurons reading SLC2A1; only if SLC2A1 moves, compare TMP269 versus pracinostat versus an HDAC6-selective control; if the genetic arm does not move SLC2A1 the compound arm is closed. P5's design already contains the compounds and the genetic control; the new element is the ordering and the catalytic-versus-scaffold gate. Cost-bearing step is the genetic arm, not a molecule.
