## Particular attention items

1. PMID 39923817 (catalytic-domain mutants). Lost: intrinsic deacetylase activity in vitro (HEK293T immunoprecipitates; double CSCS near-null, single cysteines partial; D870N null); in rat NAc neurons the CSCS mutants lost the suppression of cue-reinstated seeking, the Scn4b mRNA reduction (NAc tissue), and (in the abstract) MSN excitability effects. Kept: HDAC3 binding, MEF2C binding, MEF2-reporter repression in cultured rat striatal neurons, phosphorylation-dependent nucleocytoplasmic distribution in rat NAc neurons. Outputs: behaviour and Scn4b transcript; not SLC2A1 and no glucose-related readout. The enzyme claim in neurons rests on the CSCS pair, supported by the HEK-cell activity assay, not on a catalytic-site mutant tested in vivo. Rows E01-E03 are faithful; the rows slightly understate (WT-background CSCS, D870N activity loss).
2. Catalytic-dead or catalytic-site mutant in a neuron: E15 (mouse, not rat; five-residue Ala; survival), E04/E05 (Drosophila, Y1142H, morphology), E01/E02 (CSCS). None of them measures a transcript in a mammalian neuron after a catalytic-site point mutation other than Scn4b in E02.
3. Inhibitor versus knockdown, direction: E07 inhibitor and knockdown agree (raise NCX3). E17 inhibitor and shRNA agree (raise NRF2 targets, cardiomyocytes). E18 and E09 inhibitor and siRNA agree (raise neurite growth). E08 DISAGREES (inhibitor inert, knockdown active, HDAC7, HT22). The row set reports these correctly.
4. Null/absence rows: E19, E21, E23, E24 (E22 is a delay not a null). E23 verified by full-text search; E19 verified by keyword scan only.

## Omissions that matter

- E15: the authors' own conclusion that neuronal HDAC4 function does not require deacetylation; and species is mouse.
- E02/E01: D870N catalytic-site mutant lost activity in HEK cells (supports reading the CSCS loss as intrinsic); the nuclear-enriched 3SA is the positive control, WT HDAC5 behaviour alone was not shown in my reading.
- E20: CREB/c-fos reduction by MC1568 in laser-captured cortical neurons.
- E17: abstract states HDAC5/9 deacetylase domains are not required for anti-hypertrophic action, so class IIa catalytic dependence is output-specific.
- E07: in vitro cell line unknown from abstract; MC1568 selectivity not examined in the source.

Not done: figures/supplements; 30962285 full text; the species and cell type of the E16 reporter assay; PubMed/full text for supplementary tables. No e-mail address sent to any service.
