# Review of the two-lever design (`inputs_it46/p9_two_levers_it46.md`) and the minimum design that answers the question

Non-canonical research material. Not medical advice; no dose, schedule or recommendation. Labels: DATO (read in the source), INFERENZA, IPOTESI. The source is PMID 34634460 (Synapsin-I-Cre line, layer II/III, P13-P17, slices at 35 C, 1.3 mM Mg, 3.5 mM K).

## What p9 proposes (as read)
Lever A (Kv7 opener) and lever B (GABA reuptake block) at doses chosen to give "the same measured cellular effect", beside a vehicle arm and a non-specific hyperpolarising arm. The endpoint is the incidence of slice bursts. Readings written in advance: A and B both beyond the non-specific arm means each has a specific action; "A against B says whether the two act through the same cells (occlusion by combination)". Genotype-by-drug is the only reading said to speak to the Wwox lesion. Shared cells give input resistance, rest, E_GABA and Kv7-sensitive current.

## (a) Does occlusion or non-additivity separate same target, same cells, saturation, maximal effect?
No. INFERENZA. (1) The endpoint is incidence of a threshold event (36/42 KO slices burst; the NMDAR block abolished it, DATO). With a binary or near-floor endpoint a second action adds nothing as soon as the first reaches the floor, which is saturation of the readout and says nothing about target or cells. (2) Two submaximal actions on a threshold phenomenon can be super- or sub-additive without sharing a target, because the threshold is nonlinear. (3) Occlusion tells that two actions converge on a common step; it does not say the step is a cell population or a receptor. Reading "same cells" needs a cell-level readout, and Kv7 and GABA-A act on different channels of the same pyramidal cell. Fix: submaximal effects shown by a concentration-response in each arm, the additive reference computed from the single-arm curves (Bliss or isobologram), and the conclusion limited to "converge or not at the endpoint".

## (b) Are the manipulations comparable in site, duration, endpoint?
Not as written. INFERENZA. Site: a bath-applied reuptake blocker acts on astrocytic GAT-1 and raises tonic current (transferred P7 RNA; reused reader findings), a Kv7 opener acts on neuronal membrane, a non-specific hyperpolarisation is applied to one cell. Duration: the irreversible transaminase inactivator needs pre-incubation (reused), reuptake block and a Kv7 opener are acute. Endpoint: "same measured cellular effect" is a membrane-potential and input-resistance shift for A, but a reuptake blocker acts on membrane potential only through GABA-A conductance (tonic current), not as a direct hyperpolarisation; its matched measure would be a tonic current or sIPSC decay, a different quantity. Equal effect on one quantity is not equal effect on the burst. Fix: calibrate each arm on its own cellular readout and then on a shared, arm-independent readout (the LFP burst rate and a fixed fraction of cells' rest/threshold), in the same slice.

## (c) What can a one-cell measurement say about a slice burst?
Little. DATO: the burst is a network event recorded as an LFP; whole-cell data come from separate cells (Results 2.2; Supp S2 shows burst currents of several hundred pA at -70 mV in the patched cell). Whole-cell cells were selected as healthy, non-spiking (Methods 5.3). INFERENZA: a shift in the membrane potential of one pyramidal cell is neither the fraction of cells shifted nor the recruitment of NMDAR-dependent regeneration (the burst needed NMDAR, DATO). A single-cell equal-effect calibration therefore cannot be read as network equivalence. Fix: simultaneous LFP and whole-cell in the same slice (the source did this for the reversal experiment), several cells per slice, the slice as the experimental unit nested in the animal (24 KO animals for 42 slices in the source, so about 1.75 slices per animal).

## (d) Can a generically useful action compensate without correcting the cause?
Yes. INFERENZA. A burst reduction by an action that acts on many cell types (hyperpolarising, NMDAR-blocking) is not specific evidence of correction (the d-APV result is NMDAR dependence, not NMDAR gain); a GABA-raising action may not reduce bursts at all and may aggravate some seizure types or depolarised-GABA events (L1 and L2 counter-evidence). Burst reduction is therefore not evidence that the lever corrects the Wwox defect. A correction needs the defect itself to move in the right direction (sIPSC or mIPSC amplitude, E_GABA, passive properties) and a genotype-by-drug interaction on that measure, not on the burst alone. Risk of acting on a compensation: if the sIPSC fall is secondary to the bursting network (E5) or to an adaptation, a lever on it treats an effect; the heterozygote shows bursts in 4/23 slices and several intrinsic changes while sIPSC is pooled (DATO).

## (e) Controls that assess specificity to the Wwox context
INFERENZA, IPOTESI for the designs: (1) Cre-positive wild-type and heterozygous littermates (the source's own control), analysed separately for sIPSC; (2) wild-type slices given the same drug at the same concentration, to see the baseline effect and the floor; (3) a non-Wwox hyperexcitable slice preparation brought to a matched baseline burst rate, with the caveat that its burst mechanism may not be NMDAR-dependent; (4) non-bursting KO slices (6 of 42 in the source) versus bursting ones, to separate cause from consequence; (5) the genotype-by-drug interaction as the only reading that speaks to Wwox, computed on animals. A Cre-negative flox group and a recombination read-out are not in the source and are not added here.

## Minimum design that answers the question
The question (which of input, postsynaptic response, chloride gradient or network state) is answered by a **measurement phase without drug**; the two-lever phase is conditional.
* **Phase 0 (measurement; the minimum).** Same cell class (layer II/III pyramidal cells, P13-17, Synapsin-I-Cre), animals as the unit: Cre-positive WT, HT, KO. Not as one protocol in every cell (high-chloride whole-cell, perforated-patch E_GABA, TTX mIPSC and evoked IPSC with paired-pulse cannot all be run in one cell without compromise [V9]); the minimum that survives is a perforated-patch cohort for native-chloride sIPSC, mIPSC and evoked IPSC plus puffed agonist, and a whole-cell cohort for the high-chloride measures, nested in the same animals. The list: (i) sIPSC with a physiological-chloride pipette and with the high-chloride pipette in different cells, event identity by a GABA-A antagonist; (ii) mIPSC in TTX (amplitude, frequency, CV); (iii) evoked IPSC input-output with paired-pulse ratio and minimal-stimulation failures; (iv) response to a puffed or bath GABA-A agonist in TTX; (v) gramicidin-perforated E_GABA against rest, threshold and input conductance; (vi) Rs, Cm, Rin of the same cells; (vii) KO slices that do and do not burst. Mapping: matrix O01-O06, O10, O14.
* **Phase 1 (only for the lever Phase 0 selects).** One lever, vehicle, a non-GABAergic matched arm, submaximal concentration-response, burst LFP with simultaneous whole-cell, slice nested in animal, genotype-by-drug on the defect measure. Two levers in combination only as an exploratory occlusion test with the limits in (a).
* **Limits.** It stays in the Synapsin-Cre slice at one age; it does not settle the global knockout, other regions, development, behaviour or benefit; it does not use cells as animals (cells are nested, analysed with a mixed model); it does not extend to a new animal study.
