Non-canonical research material. Not medical advice; no dose, schedule or recommendation. Reference genotype class only. ## The answer
1. **Which observations distinguish input, postsynaptic response, chloride gradient or network state?** None of the recordings does (matrix O01-O06). The inhibitory data are spontaneous events only, in a 140 mM KCl pipette (E_Cl about 0, own arithmetic), at -70 mV, with no antagonist, no TTX miniature, no evoked IPSC, no paired-pulse, no E_GABA. Mean sIPSC amplitude falls by 52% (57.3 to 27.5 pA) and frequency by 18% (10.3 to 8.4 Hz); the amplitude fall is compatible with fewer large inputs (E1), less GABA per vesicle (E2) or fewer receptors (E3). The chloride gradient (E4) is fixed by the pipette and untested. Network state (E5) is not separable: 36 of 42 KO slices burst and no source says whether burst epochs were excluded. Passive properties (E6) are measured separately (input resistance +39%, rest 5.6 mV depolarised) but not tied to the IPSC recordings. Measured, not interpreted: the statistics are KS tests on events (unit undeclared); no animal-level test.
2. **Inversion check (two lines).** Not an inversion: the source and every held statement agree that amplitude falls more (-52%) than frequency (-18%); "about a fifth" is an ambiguous wording to be changed to "a fall of about 18%". The inference built on the order (receptor or content per event) is not supported by the source.
3. **Which pharmacological comparison stays interpretable?** Only comparisons read on the burst endpoint with the defect measured in the same slices, against a matched non-GABAergic arm and a non-Wwox hyperexcitable preparation. A GABA-supply arm (L1) and a receptor arm (L2) are interpretable only after mIPSC, agonist response and E_GABA map the deficit; the NMDAR arm (L4, the only arm with a direct effect on bursts in Wwox slices) is the generic comparator; a K+/Ih arm (L5) has measured passive correlates; chloride transport (L3) stays a measurement; gap junction (L6) is dropped.
4. **Minimum two-lever design.** Phase 0 without drug: sIPSC with identity and physiological chloride, mIPSC in TTX, evoked IPSC with paired-pulse, agonist response, perforated-patch E_GABA, Rs/Cm/Rin, bursting versus non-bursting KO slices, WT/HT/KO analysed with the animal as the unit. Phase 1 only for the lever Phase 0 selects, with a matched non-GABAergic arm and genotype-by-drug on the defect measure; occlusion of two levers only exploratory (it cannot separate target, cells, saturation, floor). Limits in `design_review.md`.

## Surprises
Supplement Table S1 gives baseline burst frequency 0.84 bursts/min [0.57-1.53] against 2.29 bursts/min in the Results for the same 119 bursts. The KO "sIPSC" mean (27.5 pA, 8.4 Hz) is numerically close to the KO sEPSC mean (24.7 pA, 10.1 Hz) in a recording with no antagonist at -70 mV in KCl. The Fig 4G example KO trace shows far fewer events than a fall of 18% suggests (single example). The burst-reversal experiment does not state its internal solution. A burst current of several hundred pA (Supp S2) would dwarf a 27-57 pA sIPSC if burst epochs were not excluded.
