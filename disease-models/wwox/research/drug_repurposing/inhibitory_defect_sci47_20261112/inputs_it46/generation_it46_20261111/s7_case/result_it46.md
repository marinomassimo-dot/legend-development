## What the reader found (decisive items; depth stated in the file)
1. **R1 sign premise: PARTLY.** The best primary measurement the reader reached (full text: Yuan 2019, Sci Rep, gramicidin perforated patch, layer II/III and hippocampal pyramidal cells pooled, two mouse models) gives a wild-type E_GABA of -66.7 +/- 5.6 mV at P13-17 (n = 5) and -77.8 +/- 5.9 mV at P18-19 (n = 5). Against the resting potentials carried for the Wwox slices (-79.25, -75.51, -73.62 mV by gene dose, PMID 34634460 as held in INV-18) the E_GABA of that paper at P13-17 would sit 7 to 12 mV **above** rest and well below spike threshold: depolarising but probably inhibitory by shunting. So the sign is not "hyperpolarising or depolarising"; it depends on the driving force **and** on conductance and threshold. The window of the phenotype (P13-17) is the late end of the switch. n is small, regions pooled, other mouse models. (INFERENZA: comparing values across preparations.) Epileptic or malformed human cortex with a depolarising shift in chloride homeostasis is SUPPORTED (Bakouh 2025, full text; others abstract level). No measurement exists for a WWOX tissue.
2. **R2:** the vigabatrin "Ki" values in three ChEMBL documents are **inactivation constants (KI) of an irreversible inactivator**, not reversible Ki, at different pH, enzyme sources and provenance (0.85 mM own measurement, 3.2 and 10 mM literature values, one 3.2 mM from a patent): **not to be placed side by side**.
3. **R3: four of the nine activity-only pointers are not measurements on GABA transaminase.** They are rows of a 10 uM receptor-panel screen (EUbOPEN document CHEMBL5465560, 45 panel entries) whose entry labelled "ABAT" was resolved by ChEMBL to the enzyme; which protein the panel entry really names is NOT_REACHED. Four false links (a wrong-target class), found by reading and now flagged by code.
4. **R4/R5:** vanillin, paroxypropione and aminobenzoic acid values (17 uM, 176 uM, above 1 mM) are enzyme-inhibition rows from one document whose system and conditions were not reached; tiagabine values mix uptake IC50 (131-380 nM), binding Ki (17-48 nM) and an apparent Kd of labelled GABA in the presence of tiagabine: not one potency.
5. **R6:** GABA-raising drugs aggravating or failing to control absence seizures is SUPPORTED (several cohorts and a tiagabine case); evidence in genetic developmental encephalopathies is two infants with non-ketotic hyperglycinaemia; nothing for Dravet, STXBP1 or WWOX. So any effect of a GABA-supply lever is **seizure-type dependent** before it is lesion-specific.
6. **R7: an overstatement in the module's baseline.** The text of INV-10 and `a2_baseline/README.md` section 1.3 says a responder with poor development is "near-universal in genetic strata". The cohorts support "majority outcome in infantile spasms cohorts" (72% and 65%) but not "near-universal": the genetic bound is a count of children who were not responders (0 of 49 favourable, not a responder rate), the 7 of 7 stratum includes trisomy 21 in 5 of 9, and KCNQ2 rows are 4 of 6 and 12 of 15. Further caveats: 145 of 175 responders had prednisolone added (30 were vigabatrin-alone responders, 63% unfavourable); responders were not sustained (39 of 61 relapsed). This is a correction to a premise already in the repository; it is **not** caused by the new method.

## What LEGEND proposes now for S7 (IPOTESI, conditional, no compound nominated)
1. **Measure first (indispensable):** gramicidin-perforated E_GABA by genotype at P13-17 in layer II/III, against the resting potential and the spike threshold of the same cells, with input conductance. Without it the sign of a GABA-supply lever is unknown. (IT46-Q01)
2. **Then, in the same slices,** a GABA-supply arm against an unrelated antiseizure or hyperpolarising arm of equal measured effect; a larger GABA-specific effect is a GABA-specific result, an equal one is generic; a drug-by-genotype interaction is the only reading that speaks to the Wwox lesion. (D3)
3. **Seizure-type caveat:** GABA-raising drugs can aggravate absence-type seizures; the seizure phenotype of the model must be stated before any effect is read.
4. **In silico, executable at zero cost:** identify which protein the panel entry labelled ABAT names (EUbOPEN selectivity table; reachable only through the panel documentation, not reached) (IT46-Q02); read the INV-10 base-rate sources again for Dravet and STXBP1 (not re-read here) and replace "near-universal" in the baseline text (IT46-Q03).
