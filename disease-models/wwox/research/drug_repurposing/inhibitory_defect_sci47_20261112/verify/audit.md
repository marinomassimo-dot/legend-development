**(c) Design review.** (a) occlusion, (b) comparability (one sentence overstated: a reuptake blocker does raise tonic GABA-A conductance), (c) one cell versus a slice burst, (e) specificity controls: SUPPORTED or minor. **(d) The sentence "a reduction of bursts by a hyperpolarising, GABA-raising or NMDAR-blocking action would appear in any bursting preparation" is not supported**: GABA-raising actions can aggravate some seizure types and depolarising GABA can aggravate events (the package's own L1 and L2 counter-evidence). Replace by: an action on many cell types is not specific evidence of correction, and whether it reduces bursts in a non-Wwox preparation is empirical.

**Minimum design that survives.** Phase 0 without a drug, animal as the unit, Cre-positive WT, HT, KO analysed separately; native-chloride recordings (perforated patch) of sIPSC, mIPSC in TTX and evoked IPSC with paired-pulse ratio, plus puffed-agonist reversal for E_GABA, in one cohort; a whole-cell cohort with a GABA-A antagonist and a glutamate antagonist for event identity; Rs, Cm, Rin per cell; bursting against non-bursting KO slices. "In each recorded cell (i)-(vii)" cannot be one protocol. The two-lever step is exploratory: concentration-response in each arm, additive reference from the single-arm curves, genotype-by-drug on the defect measure; conclusion limited to convergence at the endpoint.
