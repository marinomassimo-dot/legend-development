# IT58 audit of earlier pre-registered reader tests: criteria the reader task never elicited, and a diagnosis of the no-difference comparisons (rule written and hashed BEFORE the earlier preregistrations and tasks of IT52 and IT57 are re-read; IT, 2026-11-18)
Scope (approved by the advisor; measurement validity, reuse only, no spend): the pre-registered reader tests of IT52, IT57 and ADV-066 (round 1 and the K2 round). I already know the ADV-066 files (I wrote them); I have NOT re-read the IT52 and IT57 preregistrations or their reader tasks since writing them, and I will read them only after this file is hashed.
Rule 1, ELICITED: a criterion is elicited if the reader task text contains an instruction whose literal, honest answer would satisfy it. It is UNELICITED if satisfying it would need the reader to volunteer something the task did not ask for. Judged from the task text alone, before looking at any reader answer for that test; the judgement and its quoted task sentence are recorded per criterion.
Rule 2, WHAT CHANGES IN A VERDICT: for every test with at least one unelicited criterion, recompute the verdict under both readings: (a) as published; (b) counting only elicited criteria. A verdict that differs is reported as CHANGED by the audit and the original verdict stays recorded (never edited); one that does not differ is reported UNCHANGED. A verdict that depended on an unelicited criterion being met by one arm and not the other is reported as UNSUPPORTED BY THE TASK.
Rule 3, RERUN: only unelicited criteria whose absence could have changed a verdict, only with the task text amended by the one added sentence that elicits them, the same model, effort, tools and word allowance in every compared arm (inevitable differences declared), one pass per arm, no repetition of a comparison in the hope of another result. No rerun if no verdict could change.
Rule 4, DIAGNOSIS of each no-difference comparison (ADV-066 round 1; K2 round), from the existing material only, with the six classes of the advisor: (1) new knowledge that changes a decision; (2) knowledge already known to the model but not represented by the engine; (3) information loaded but not used; (4) a comparison too easy to show an advantage; (5) information insufficient to support a new hypothesis; (6) cost added without observable benefit. Operational tests, applied per observed gap or absence of gain: class 3 = the item is in the AFTER material and absent from the AFTER answer; class 4 = the criterion is met in BEFORE as well as AFTER; class 1 = the item is in the AFTER answer, absent from the BEFORE answer, absent from the BEFORE material, and it changes at least one proposed action, arm or control; class 2 = the item appears in BOTH answers although only the AFTER material holds it: this CANNOT be separated from 'the BEFORE material or the task already carried it' without a no-material condition, so such items are reported as 'class 2 or already in the BEFORE text' and the audit names the one no-material condition that would settle it; class 5 = the reader states that the material gives no margin or no pattern for an explanation; class 6 = tokens added to the material (AFTER minus BEFORE) with no class-1 item attached. No class is assigned without the quoted sentences of the answers and the material.
Rule 5, WHAT WOULD BE INFORMATIVE NEXT: if class 2 (or 'already in the BEFORE text') dominates, the audit names a real bifurcation, that is, a case where a context condition or a counter-evidence in the material could change the pharmacological comparison or the experiment, and states for it, before any reader is run: what would count as a better comparison, a more useful conditional hypothesis, a more discriminating experiment, no difference, and harm (an unjustified transfer of sign, or more confusion). If the case or its outcome is already known it is called retrospective and no predictive validity is claimed. ADV has started one case (ADV-067, the lithium regimen); a second is proposed only if the audit shows a concrete one.
