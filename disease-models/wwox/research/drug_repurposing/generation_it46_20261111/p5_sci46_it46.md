## Against the IT45 proposal
* IT45 named the pair as a design: a covalent modifier and "a non-covalent binder" (unnamed), compared on a shared NRF2-dependent readout with and without NFE2L2 knockdown. **Kept:** the design logic. **Changed:** the binder is named and its identity is checked in three databases (and the per-article numbering trap closed); the source's own knockdown arm exists only as categorical labels (no numbers, no efficiency), so it cannot be used as a potency or as dependence evidence; verified NRF2 dependence of the binder exists in primary rat microglia, not neurons; in a neuronal line a partial loss gave an uninterpretable result; one human neuronal use on an already NRF2-high background moved ferroptosis markers in the harmful direction. **Added:** an adequacy criterion for the loss fixed beforehand, a KEAP1-loss occlusion arm, a readout beyond NQO1 and a risk panel. The IT46 P4 requirement (exposure matched on an on-target response, a second knockdown reagent, an NRF2-independent control set, a WWOX genotype factor) and SCI46's (matched on on-target response, adequacy of loss) were written independently and agree.
* Outcome class (from the mandate's list): a **conditional proposal with a better-identified reagent pair**, no pair interpretable in neurons yet; the genetic arm stays first. Nothing was ranked; the binder stays a probe (neuronal permeability of an acid and brain exposure are unknown).
