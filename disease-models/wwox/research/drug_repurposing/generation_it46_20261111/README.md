## New case, fixed before execution (P3): S7 raising GABA availability (INV-10)
`p3_case_prereg_it46.md` (selection rule, need, input digests, baseline, expected difference, failure outcomes, reader perimeter; one addendum before scoring) → `s7_case/result_it46.md`, blind reader `verify_s7/VERDICTS.md`.
* **Counts:** 2 molecules by simple target search → 21 families with activity and interaction rows → 21 qualified rows with next steps → 1 conditional comparison with five alternatives; the context join added **nothing** (no held table carried the genes; stated before, held).
* **Added value:** the next experiment changed (a measurement of the GABA reversal potential against rest, threshold and conductance comes before any compound arm; an acute slice arm uses a reversible reuptake blocker because the transaminase inactivator is slow and irreversible; the separation of a GABA-specific from a generic antiseizure effect). The reader refined the sign premise (the reversal potential at P13-17 sits near rest: depolarising but possibly shunting, not a binary).
* **What the reading found that the engine did not:** four of nine activity-only pointers are rows of a receptor-panel screen under an enzyme label (false links; now flagged by code); vigabatrin "Ki" values are inactivation constants that cannot be compared across documents; the module's "near-universal in genetic strata" base-rate wording is overstated (corrected in INV-10 and the baseline note).
* **Not shown:** a better prediction, a new favourable compound, or that the gain holds on a case chosen by someone else. One case, selected by a rule, scored by the session that built the method; independent reader for the premises only.

## Second new case, fixed before execution (P9): S5 positive modulation of the GABA-A receptor
`s5_case/prereg_it46.md` (rule: lowest-numbered relation of S1-S8 never a case of IT43-IT46 with at least one gene in a held cell-class table) → `s5_case/result_it46.md`, blind reader `s5_case/verify/VERDICTS.md`.
* **Counts:** 71 mechanism molecules → 115 rows with the interaction table → +39 activity-only pointers (58 with a catalogue status, 13 of the 58 panel rows only) → 170 ladder rows = 142 families → 1 conditional comparison with six alternatives; the context part returned data for 7 of 7 symbols.
* **Two errors in my own pre-registered frame, found by the reader and by running the tools, disclosed in the result:** the Wwox slice paper does measure a reduction of spontaneous inhibition (amplitude about half, frequency about a fifth, high-chloride pipette, no evoked IPSC); and three of four gene symbols I wrote as "not in any held table" are in the extended tables.
* **Added value:** the amplitude falling more than the frequency points at the receptor response or the GABA content per event (inference), which puts the receptor lever (S5) and the supply lever (S7) on the same recordings; the next experiment begins with confirming the deficit in physiological chloride with evoked currents and the reversal potential; benzodiazepines in the WWOX reports held are two single-child responses against three non-responding groups, with no instrument. **Not shown:** a better prediction or a new favourable compound.
* Consumers: compound view V21, questions IT46-Q10 and IT46-Q11, `benzodiazepine_mentions_a1.tsv`, the three-arm design `p9_two_levers_it46.md` (IT46-Q04).
