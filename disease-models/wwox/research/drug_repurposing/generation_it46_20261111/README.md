## Limits
Reader depth is abstract or database copy for most documents of S7; translations are the assistant's, not native-reviewed; RNA tables are P7 mouse neurons and P17 oligodendrocyte-lineage classes with two pools each and one human neuron sample; no biological prediction is validated by these comparisons; the mouse against human KCNQ2 neuron difference (30.68 against 0.10) is unexplained.

**Update 2026-11-12 (IT47 P3, SCI47 package A).** Wording and strength, with the paper rechecked: sIPSC frequency falls **by** about 18 percent (10.3 to 8.4 Hz) and amplitude **by** about 52 percent (57.3 to 27.5 pA); neither is a fall *to* a fifth. The order of the two falls does not by itself point to the receptor response or to the GABA content per event: the frequency change is shown only by a distribution test on pooled events, detected frequency depends on amplitude, and event identity is not shown. The K-gluconate pipette of the excitatory-current recordings holds about 12 mM chloride. See `../inhibitory_defect_sci47_20261112/`.

## Update 2026-11-12 (IT47, from SCI47 package B; the text above is kept)
The "mouse against human KCNQ2 neuron difference (30.68 against 0.10)" is **not** a species difference: the held human table reads KCNQ2 low across its columns (0.1 in the one neuron sample, 0.2-1.5 in four whole-cortex samples) and its technical cause is not established; do not cite it as human neurons lacking KCNQ2. A second human table (HPA single nuclei; source dataset not verified, so independence is not claimed) gives KCNQ2 high in neuronal classes with OPC at 0.73 of the neuronal maximum (neuron to glia ratio 1.36 against a pre-registered rule that was not met). An OPC contribution to a bath-applied Kv7 opener is a plausible alternative to control (IT47-Q04), not an established action. RNA is not protein or current. The inferences were not independently verified by SCI47. No rank, direction or need changed.

## Update 2026-11-12 (IT49, from SCI49 package A; the text above is kept)
The held human KCNQ2 value 0.1 is the table's reporting floor (the same laboratory sets values below 0.1 to 0.1); human whole cortex is also low (KCNQ2/SNAP25 0.001-0.009 against 0.122 in HPA forebrain projection neurons); one patient was selected with anti-Thy1; the cause is not determinable, so "human neurons lack KCNQ2" is not permitted. KCNQ3 RNA in human microglia is the highest in the HPA table and echoed by Zhang 2016 myeloid samples, while mouse microglia Kcnq3 sits at the floor (unresolved; IT49-Q04). The OPC control is needed only for the statement that an effect is neuronal.
