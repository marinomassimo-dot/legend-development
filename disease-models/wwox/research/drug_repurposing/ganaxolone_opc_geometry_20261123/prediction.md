# OPC receptor availability and the geometry of myelin on fast-spiking axons

SCI_CODEX, 23 November 2026. A third, separable hypothesis is that ganaxolone could alter how differentiating oligodendroglia position myelin on fast-spiking axons. A controlled OPC-specific Gabrg2 deletion study already reported longer internodes and abnormal branching with preserved global myelin. This study did not use ganaxolone or a Wwox mutation. The new questions are quantitative: which endpoint most consistently distinguishes that genetic manipulation, and does Wwox loss produce a detectable ligand-sensitive receptor context in the relevant lineage? A positive answer would nominate a geometric endpoint, not establish a drug effect.

Before opening source numerical values: prioritize per-cell mean internode length and fraction of branch points myelinated, then first myelin distance, first branch distance and node length as ancillary endpoints in Benamer et al. (2020). Extract the authors' source spreadsheet if present. Show independent-mouse counts separately from observations. If donor IDs are unavailable, report only cell-level descriptives and ranges after dropping each cell, and do not compute animal-level uncertainty. If only pooled segment data exist, label the unit and do not replace animal counts with segment counts.

In the existing P17 mouse Wwox hippocampal single-nucleus pools, retain the established nine class assignments. For OPC, COP, inhibitory and excitatory classes (latter two are contextual references), quantify Gabrg2, the α and β genes GABA-A receptors require, plus Gabrd as an alternative partner. Retain all six alpha and three beta subunits; do not select a favorable isoform. A minimally credible OPC transcriptional substrate requires at least ten unique Gabrg2 molecules per original pool and a positive gamma2+alpha+beta co-detection in each pool. Report extent, ambient neuronal-RNA competition, and repeat requiring two original OPC markers and no detected Slc17a7, Gad1, Gad2, Snap25 or Rbfox3. State explicitly if the stricter filter removes most observations. Pooled, sparsely detected transcripts cannot show that a protein complex exists. Record differences as descriptive, not replicated. Corroborate OPC expression independently in normal purified mouse cells and distinguish Glu/GABA source, responding OPCs and axon geometry.

Separately compare Pvalb, Kcnc1, Kcnc2 against inhibitory-lineage comparators Gad1, Gad2, Lhx6 in P17 neuronal-Wwox-deletion cortex and hippocampus, tissue-panel log2(1+TPM) differences with animal omission. Require a consistent decrease exceeding 0.25 log2 units relative to the comparators in both regions before prioritizing a generalized fast-spiking maturation deficit. A shared shift cannot identify internode geometry. A geometry-specific hypothesis would ultimately require paired measurements of internode length and branch occupation on identified PV axons in drug-exposed WWOX-deficient and isogenic control preparations at matched firing and myelin abundance.
