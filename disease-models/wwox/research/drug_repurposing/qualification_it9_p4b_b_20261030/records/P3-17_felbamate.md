# P3-17 felbamate - reference (direct-search arm on GRIN2C); boxed-warning review

Decision: **REFERENCE-ONLY.** Not a candidate; kept as an antiseizure NMDAR-class reference. Not medical advice.

1. Identity. ChEMBL CHEMBL1094, InChIKey WKGXYQFOCVYPAC-UHFFFAOYSA-N, PubChem CID 3331 (same key). Achiral dicarbamate, MW 238.
2. Target and measures. Mechanism row: "Glutamate [NMDA] receptor antagonist" (family target). 300 + 10 rows retrieved. NMDAR rows are all on human GluN2B (a single document, CHEMBL1144021: oocyte inhibition percentages 61 to 90 percent for wild type and mutants at an unstated fixed concentration, and apparent binding constants Kapp **0.87 mM** for wild-type GluN2B and 2 to 35 mM for mutants). **No GluN2C row: UNKNOWN**, not zero; the catalogue's GRIN2C sign comes from the family-level mechanism row, not from a measure. Potency is millimolar, i.e. the same order as therapeutic plasma levels (label does not state the concentration range; not computed). Also a GABA-A TBPS displacement IC50 95 nM (rat brain, assay type B, unchecked target) which is outside the NMDAR question.
3. Exposure (openFDA label effective 2026-07-14 and nine other versions). Well absorbed; about 90 percent of plasma radioactivity is parent; protein binding 22 to 25 percent; terminal half-life 20 to 23 hours; clearance 26 to 30 mL/hr/kg; 40 to 50 percent excreted unchanged. CSF/brain concentration: not stated in the sections retrieved. Paediatric: "safety and effectiveness ... in children other than those with Lennox-Gastaut syndrome has not been established".
4. Boxed-warning review (full text of the box read in the label JSON). (1) **Aplastic anaemia**: incidence possibly more than 100-fold the background of 2 to 5 per million per year; case fatality about 20 to 30 percent (up to 70 percent historically); use only where epilepsy is so severe that the risk is acceptable; haematological consultation. (2) **Hepatic failure**: about 6 cases of liver failure leading to death or transplant per 75,000 patient-years (an underestimate); about 67 percent of reported cases died or were transplanted, typically within 5 weeks of signs, earliest onset 3 weeks after start. Contraindicated with history of blood dyscrasia or hepatic dysfunction, or other carbamate sensitivity. ChEMBL carries the black-box flag (1). Consequence for WWOX-DEE: any use is confined to refractory epilepsy under specialist supervision; the proposal engine's output may not be read as encouragement.
5. Edge: n/a (direct-search hit, hop 0).
6. Expression (HPA, adult): GRIN2C brain nTPM 113.7, astrocytes 148.6 and cerebellar astrocyte/Bergmann glia nCPM; GRIN2A/GRIN2B neuronal and OPC (253.8) in HPA single-cell data. Developmental windows not checked.
7. Trials: felbamate 5 name hits; neuro intersection 3, none a felbamate efficacy trial in DEE (a paediatric population pharmacokinetics study NCT03196466 lists epilepsy; a Medicaid database analysis; a ketogenic-diet comparison). 
8. Verification: no new experiment; as a reference arm it needs only the exposure caveat and the aplastic anaemia/hepatic boxed text.
