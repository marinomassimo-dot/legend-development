# P3-05 fosfestrol (diethylstilbestrol diphosphate) - Q4 protein, edge ESR2 -> GAD2

Decision: **DROP as a compound; the edge survives only as a question for selective ligands.** Not medical advice. A compatible action is not a benefit.

1. Identity. ChEMBL CHEMBL1200598, InChIKey NLORYLAYLIXTID-ISLYRVAYSA-N, parent itself (no salt); PubChem CID 3032325 returns the same InChIKey and the title "Fosfestrol" (structure-level match, `identity_pubchem.tsv`). Phosphate prodrug of diethylstilbestrol (CHEMBL411, InChIKey RGLYKWWBQGJZGM-ISLYRVAYSA-N). ChEMBL max phase 4; MW 428, alogP 4.4, PSA 134.
2. Target and measures. The ChEMBL mechanism row is "Estrogen receptor agonist" on the target family CHEMBL2093866 (ER alpha + ER beta). **The prodrug record holds 26 activity rows and none is an ER measure** (hepatotoxicity annotations, one BLM helicase potency, SARS-CoV-2 and HDAC6 screens); ER measures are on the active species DES (1,273 rows retrieved in total; ER rows kept in `measures_kept.tsv`). Same-document pairs (assay type B, human, "="): Ki 0.49 nM ER alpha vs 0.63 nM ER beta (CHEMBL1136756); IC50 3 nM vs 4 nM (CHEMBL1134479); RBA 430 vs 280 (CHEMBL1138654); RBA 372 vs 278 percent (CHEMBL1154151); EC50 binding-displacement 12 vs 9 nM (CHEMBL1135900). **DES is not ER-beta-selective (ratios 0.7 to 1.5 on every same-document pair).** The "ERbeta route" of the P3 note therefore does not select this compound over any ER agonist; the proposing edge needs ER beta, the compound activates both.
3. Exposure. No US prescription label (openFDA: 404 for fosfestrol; the only DES label hits are two homeopathic OTC products, irrelevant). Route, dose, half-life, CSF/brain and paediatric data: **not found in the services queried (absent data, not a negative)**. Conversion of the prodrug to DES is general pharmacology, not verified here.
4. Safety. Hepatotoxicity annotation rows (CHEMBL3137667, a curated drug-induced-liver-injury source: moderate liver toxicity frequency 6.3 percent in trials) are the only safety data retrieved. DES in-utero carcinogenicity and thromboembolic risk are general knowledge and were **not** retrieved (UNVERIFIED). Seizure and neurodevelopmental data: none retrieved.
5. Edge provenance. ESR2 -> GAD2 (+1): OmniPath record from CollecTRI/ExTRI, **a single reference (PMID 19587286, J Neurosci 2009)**, abstract read: 2.7 kb rat gad2 promoter-luciferase reporter, estradiol activates it through either ER alpha or ER beta; three EREs; neural cell line SN56.B5.G4 and MCF-7; ChIP in mouse GABAergic N42 cells shows **ER alpha** binding. Grade: TEXT_MINED_TF with a neural, reporter-level primary source; ER beta binding to the endogenous locus is not shown in the abstract. Seed level mismatch: the seed is GAD2 protein down (mouse hippocampus), the edge is a transcriptional reporter in rat promoter. Plausible in neural GABAergic lines, not shown at the relevant age or in vivo.
6. Expression (HPA, adult, `expression_hpa.tsv`). GAD2: brain nTPM 65.4, inhibitory neurons 385 nCPM. ESR2 and ESR1: tissue "enhanced" elsewhere; no brain cell type listed as enriched (a type not listed is not a type with no expression: the HPA field names enriched types only). Developmental or hippocampal-interneuron age data: **not checked (UNVERIFIED)**.
7. Trials (ClinicalTrials.gov v2, 2026-10-30, `trials_ctgov.tsv`). fosfestrol: 3 name-matched hits, none about fosfestrol in the titles (a radium-223, an imidafenacin and an ibudilast study: query-synonym noise, not read further); neurological conditions intersection 0. DES: 7 hits, all prostate cancer except the noise ones; neuro intersection 0.
8. Verification in a WWOX-deficient system. Wwox-null hippocampal slices or primary neurons: apply an ER-beta-selective agonist (tool compound; none carried in this catalogue family) and estradiol vs vehicle with an ER-alpha-selective arm; readout GAD2/GAD1 protein by the same western as the seed (five lanes per genotype in the source), plus gad2 mRNA to separate transcript from protein. Conditional arms: if GAD protein rises with ER-beta agonist only in the null, the edge is operative; if ER alpha only, the ESR2 label of the edge is wrong. Transfer limits: sex hormone confound (sex of animals), age, hormonal effects in children, ER beta abundance in the cells.

Biological promise: low-moderate for the edge, none specific to this compound. Depth: abstract-level literature, database rows. Feasibility of this compound: very low. Priority of an experiment with this compound: none.
