# P3-06 implitapide - Q4 protein, chain P4HB -> ERN1 -> XBP1 -> GAD1 (net sign -1)

Decision: **DROP. Decisive datum: the proposing target P4HB is the protein disulfide isomerase subunit of the MTP complex; the drug inhibits MTP lipid transfer, not PDI, and the chain's terminal edge has no regulatory source.** Not medical advice.

1. Identity. ChEMBL CHEMBL2105804, InChIKey AMNXBQPRODZJQR-DITALETJSA-N; PubChem CID 5745206 gives the same key (structure-level match). Max phase 2, MW 532, alogP 7.0, PSA 67.
2. Target mapping (the work-order question). ChEMBL mechanism: "Microsomal triglyceride transfer protein inhibitor" on target CHEMBL2364681, a **PROTEIN COMPLEX** with two components: P55157 (MTTP, large subunit) and P07237 (protein disulfide-isomerase, P4HB). The catalogue splits the complex into member genes, so MTTP and P4HB both carry sign -1 for implitapide ("P4HB mean_sign=-1.0000 sources=ChEMBL" in the P3 profile). **No activity row exists for implitapide in ChEMBL (0 of 0; NO_ROWS_UNDER_FILTERS, service answered)**, so there is no measured P4HB (or MTTP) potency. The literature available (a 2005 mouse atherosclerosis paper and a 2001 rabbit paper on an MTP inhibitor, abstract-level titles only) concerns lipoprotein secretion. This is a complex-to-subunit mapping artefact: it was predicted in the P3 note.
3. Exposure. No label (openFDA 404). Development stopped after three phase 2 trials (all terminated; adults, two also list CHILD age groups). CSF/brain: not found.
4. Safety. ChEMBL phase 2, discontinued for hepatic steatosis rationale per the P3 note (not re-verified); no seizure or neurodevelopment data found.
5. Edge provenance (OmniPath). P4HB -> ERN1 (-1): SIGNOR, one reference (PMID 32149426, EMBO J 2020; title: phosphorylation switches PDI activity to maintain proteostasis and attenuate ER stress) - a statement about PDI activity regulating IRE1 signalling, not about MTP inhibition. ERN1 -> XBP1 (+1): SIGNOR/SPIKE/KEGG, well curated (effort 4; IRE1 splicing of XBP1 mRNA is an established mechanism). XBP1 -> GAD1 (+1): TRRUST/DoRothEA/CollecTRI, the single reference is PMID 18335162, a family-based **association study of GAD1 polymorphisms in schizophrenia** (title read), not a regulatory experiment: grade NO_REGULATORY_SOURCE_FOUND for the terminal edge. Chain judged an artefact: wrong target level for the drug, and the last edge unsupported.
6. Expression (HPA). P4HB, ERN1, XBP1: ubiquitous/low specificity, no brain cell listed; MTTP: listed only in glial/ependymal snRNA (astrocyte 14.8, Bergmann glia 9.2, ependymal 30.4 nCPM, adult). GAD1: inhibitory neurons 309 nCPM. Developmental age not checked.
7. Trials: implitapide 3 hits (hypercholesterolaemia, triglycerides; all terminated), neuro intersection 0; "BAY 13-9952" 0 hits.
8. Verification: none proposed for this compound. If the UPR-to-GAD1 relay is of interest, test it with XBP1 or IRE1 manipulation (genetic) in Wwox-null neurons, reading GAD1 mRNA/protein; that is a different experiment that does not use this drug.

Biological promise: none attributable to the compound. Depth: complete for the identity/mapping question (database), abstract-level for the edge references. Feasibility: none. Priority: none.
