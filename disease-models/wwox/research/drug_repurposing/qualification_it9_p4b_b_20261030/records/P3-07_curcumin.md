# P3-07 curcumin - Q5 glia/myelin transcripts, edge EP300 -> TNF

Decision: **DROP as a candidate and as a probe. Decisive datum: the primary medicinal-chemistry review classes curcumin as a PAINS/IMPS compound that is unstable, reactive and not bioavailable (PMID 28074653), and the compound's own EP300 measures are micromolar and inconsistent between documents.** Not medical advice.

1. Identity. ChEMBL CHEMBL140 (child record CHEMBL1916041 is in the same family), InChIKey VFLDPWHFBUODDF-FCXRPNKRSA-N; PubChem CID 969516 returns it (structure match). Natural product, max phase 3 in ChEMBL (as assigned there; no approved indication stated).
2. Target and measures (ChEMBL, 3,657 + 7 rows retrieved, not truncated). Assay types: F 2,099, B 1,042, A 322, P 121, T 62, U 11. Assay type B potency rows (IC50, Ki, Kd, EC50, "=" value present) span **124 distinct target names** (377 rows): the promiscuity diagnostic of P3 (2 signed targets) underestimates it, as P3 predicted. EP300 rows, human, assay type B, kept in `measures_kept.tsv`: IC50 "=" 6.5 uM (two documents, one a catalytic-domain construct), 25 uM (seven rows in several documents, mostly the same value), 30 uM; and censored values "greater than" 40 uM and 400 uM (other documents); GST-p300 HAT rows with no fitted value. **The range of "=" and ">" values across documents spans over 60-fold and is not pooled.** No selectivity against CBP is separable (25 uM on CBP in one document). The GtoPdb link in the catalogue (EP300, sign -1) rests on these micromolar rows.
3. Exposure. No drug label (openFDA: one OTC homeopathic/supplement product, indications unrelated). Human oral bioavailability: described as poor in the review (abstract level); no CSF or brain datum retrieved. Paediatric: not found.
4. Safety/counter-evidence. Trial burden: 397 registered curcumin interventional studies (all conditions); the review states no double-blind placebo-controlled trial succeeded. No seizure data retrieved.
5. Edge provenance. EP300 -> TNF (+1): OmniPath CollecTRI/ExTRI2/IntAct, 15 references (curation effort 15), e.g. PMID 19103749 (BRD4/acetylated RelA/NF-kB, Mol Cell Biol 2009) and 25525796 (adenovirus E1A, p300/CBP repression of host genes) - titles read only; the contexts are cell lines and virology, a generic coactivator relation. Grade: AGGREGATE_TF, context-free, no glial-specific reference seen. A second record (Wang) is post-translational with no reference.
6. Expression (HPA). EP300: low tissue specificity (ubiquitous), no brain cell listed as enriched. TNF: microglia 7.8 nCPM (sc), CNS macrophage 11.6 (snRNA), adult.
7. Trials: curcumin x neuro conditions 2 hits: NCT03254680 (turmeric in epilepsy, Dravet/LGS, WITHDRAWN) and NCT04007224 (autism, phase 1). No result is a finding.
8. Verification: a selective catalytic p300/CBP inhibitor (a chemotype class not retrieved here) in Wwox-null microglia or mixed glia, readout Tnf mRNA and TNF protein, would test the edge without PAINS behaviour; curcumin itself needs a counter-screen design (detergent, redox/thiol reactivity, fluorescence quenching) before any readout could be read. Transfer limits: Q5 valence is UNCERTAIN (transcripts up in two mouse models, down in the rat).

Biological promise (edge only): low-moderate; (compound): none. Depth: database + abstract-level. Feasibility: poor. Priority: none for curcumin.
