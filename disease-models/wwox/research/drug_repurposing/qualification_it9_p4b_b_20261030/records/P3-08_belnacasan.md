# P3-08 belnacasan (VX-765) - Q5 glia/myelin transcripts, edge CASP1 -> IL1B; reference for card J01

Decision: **KEEP-AS-PROPOSAL, gated and low priority (also the reference compound for the abstention card J01).** The gate is a level mismatch: the edge describes protein processing, the seed is a transcript. Not medical advice.

1. Identity. ChEMBL CHEMBL2107819, InChIKey SJDDOCKBXFJEJB-MOKWFATOSA-N, parent itself; PubChem CID 11398092 ("VX 765") returns the same key. It is an ethyl-acetal prodrug. The **active acid VRT-043198 is a separate ChEMBL record, CHEMBL4802237** (found by name search, aldehyde SMILES), InChIKey SOZONDBMOYWSRW-QANKJYHBSA-N; a third record CHEMBL4303359 (a stereoisomer of the prodrug with the same connectivity) holds only SARS-CoV-2 screen rows. The catalogue's action sits on the prodrug record.
2. Target and measures. Prodrug record: 15 rows, **no caspase-1 potency** (viral screens, HDAC6 single-point inhibition, a 2024 mouse J774.A1 cell paper with no numeric values, one human-PBMC IL-1beta mRNA IC50 "=" 0.8 nM in an assay of unspecified target, CHEMBL5543064; the 0.8 nM there is a cell mRNA readout, not an enzyme constant and is not combined with the next). Active acid record: caspase-1 Ki "=" 0.8 nM (assay type B, human/"unknown origin", document CHEMBL5154657) and IC50 "=" 470 nM for LPS-induced IL-1beta release in PBMC (assay type F, same document). Selectivity against other caspases for the acid: **not retrieved here** (mouse caspase-3/7 rows of the prodrug have no values).
3. Exposure. No label (openFDA 404). The acid requires esterase conversion of the prodrug. Human CSF/brain concentration of the acid: **not found**. Mouse: systemic VX-765 reduced chronic epileptic activity at 50 mg/kg and above and acute seizures (PMID 21431948, abstract read; ICE/caspase-1 inhibition with IL-1beta synthesis blocked in activated astrocytes), and a 2026 pilocarpine-SE mouse paper reports fewer seizures, lower hippocampal caspase-1 activity and glial hyperactivation (PMID 42216646, abstract read). These imply CNS action in mice but are not a measured brain concentration.
4. Safety and counter-evidence. Phase 2 programme: psoriasis (adult), COVID-19 (adult), and **two adult treatment-resistant partial epilepsy studies** (NCT01048255, 60 participants, completed; NCT01501383, 55 participants, terminated by sponsor; no results in the registry record, whyStopped says only "Terminated by Sponsor"). Reason for termination: not stated in the sources consulted (UNKNOWN; no cause is asserted here). Paediatric data: none. Neurodevelopmental toxicity: none retrieved; caspase-1 has non-inflammatory roles in development, not examined.
5. Edge provenance (OmniPath). CASP1 -> IL1B has two records: SIGNOR (+1, post-translational, reference PMID 1919001, J Immunol 1991: IL-1-converting enzyme processes the IL-1beta precursor) and a multi-database record (BioGRID/HPRD/InnateDB, effort 17, stimulation and inhibition both flagged). Grade: CURATED_PHYSICAL, but it is **cleavage of pro-IL-1beta protein to the mature form**. The seed is the IL1B **transcript** raised in two mouse models (tier B). Inhibiting caspase-1 would lower mature IL-1beta protein; it has no direct reason to lower IL1B mRNA (an IL-1 autoinduction loop could link them but is not shown). The proposal is therefore compatible with the seed only if the transcript rise is read as a proxy of IL-1beta pathway activation; that reading is an assumption.
6. Expression (HPA, adult). CASP1: CNS macrophage 38.9, leukocyte 43, endothelial 12 nCPM; IL1B: CNS macrophage 27.1; brain-neuron or oligodendrocyte-lineage expression not listed. The relevant cell is microglia/macrophage; the age relevant to WWOX-DEE is not covered by HPA.
7. Trials (2026-10-30): belnacasan 3 hits, VX-765 3 hits (same trials), neuro intersection 2 (both epilepsy, adult).
8. Verification (gated). Gate 1: in Wwox-null hippocampus or microglia, is caspase-1 activation present (cleaved p20, caspase-1 activity) and is mature IL-1beta protein (ELISA/western) raised relative to wild type? If transcript is up and mature protein/caspase-1 are not, the edge is irrelevant to the seed: DROP. If they are raised: apply the active acid VRT-043198 (not the prodrug in culture) at graded concentrations bracketing the 0.8 nM Ki and the 470 nM cell IC50, in microglia-neuron co-culture or slices; readouts: mature IL-1beta, neuronal network activity (MEA burst rate), GAD1/2 protein, cell viability. Discriminating valence: if inhibitor lowers network hyperexcitability, the IL-1beta arm is harmful; if it worsens survival or does nothing, adaptive or neutral. Falsifier: no change in any readout at concentrations that suppress mature IL-1beta. Controls: wild-type, a structurally unrelated caspase-1 inhibitor, vehicle. Transfer limits: rat transcripts go the other way; pyroptosis-independent roles; adult-only human data.

Biological promise: moderate-low (axis carded, valence unresolved). Depth: medium (database rows, two abstracts, registry). Feasibility: high in vitro. Priority of the next experiment: low, after gate 1 (cheap).
