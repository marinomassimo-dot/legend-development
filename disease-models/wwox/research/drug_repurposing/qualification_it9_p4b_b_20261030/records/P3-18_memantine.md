2. Seed/edge question. Memantine appears through the direct-search arm: its catalogue targets include GRIN2C, a gene in the Q4 seed set at hop 0. **There is no network edge and no seed-specific (|Z| above 3 in both nulls) proposal; the compound is a hop-0 target hit.** The seed is a Q4 protein-level observation (GAD1/GAD2 protein lower in Wwox-null hippocampus), which does not name an NMDAR direction; GRIN2C as a seed gene gives no direction relation to memantine's channel block. Therefore the "seed" support is a gene-symbol overlap, not a mechanistic edge. GluN2C-specific measure in ChEMBL: IC50 "=" 3,162 nM (human GluN1/GluN2C, "unknown origin", overexpression assay, assay type B, document CHEMBL6115009), same document: GluN2A 31,623 nM, GluN2B 10,000 nM, GluN2D 10,000 nM; a different document (CHEMBL6083260, HEK293 patch clamp) gives GluN2A 840 nM and GluN2B 780 nM at -60 mV. Cross-document values are not pooled. One row (GluN1/GluN2A IC50 0.95 nM, CHEMBL2396530) is three orders away from every other value and is flagged as an outlier of unresolved curation (not used).
