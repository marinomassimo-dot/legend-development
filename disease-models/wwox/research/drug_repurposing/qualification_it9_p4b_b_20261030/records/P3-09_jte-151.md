# P3-09 JTE-151 - Q5 glia/myelin transcripts, edge RORC -> IL6

Decision: **INCONCLUSIVE (insufficient support, not "no activity").** Exact missing data: (a) RORgamma potency values and a CNS exposure measure for JTE-151, (b) a structure-keyed ChEMBL record, (c) RORC expression/function in Wwox-null microglia.

1. Identity. ChEMBL CHEMBL3545357 "JTE-151": **no structure in ChEMBL (InChIKey absent, molecule type Unknown)**; the identity is by name. The PubChem name lookup returns CID 71127425 whose title is the IUPAC name (4S)-6-(2-chloro-4-methylanilino)-4-[4-cyclopropyl-5-[3-(2,2-dimethylpropyl)cyclobutyl]-1,2-oxazol-3-yl]-6-oxohexanoic acid, identical in substance to the name printed in the 2024 abstract of the pharmacology paper (PMID 39675971, cis-cyclobutyl stereochemistry written there). So the name-to-structure link is supported by publication and PubChem; the ChEMBL-record-to-structure link remains UNVERIFIED because the ChEMBL record has no structure. A carboxylic acid (bulk lipophilic): CNS penetration cannot be inferred and is not asserted.
2. Target and measures. ChEMBL mechanism: RORC antagonist (target CHEMBL1741186). **0 activity rows (NO_ROWS_UNDER_FILTERS).** Abstract of PMID 39675971: dissociates co-activator and recruits co-repressor peptide on human RORgamma-LBD, inhibits transcriptional activity of human, mouse and rat RORgamma, "high selectivity against other nuclear receptors" - no numerical values in the abstract; the full text was not opened.
3. Exposure. No label. A human phase 1 is described in the discovery paper's abstract (PMID 38170624): "no serious adverse events and good PK profiles"; no CSF/brain datum, no paediatric datum. Registry: no ClinicalTrials.gov record (0 hits; Japanese registries not searched: UNVERIFIED).
4. Safety. Abstract-level only (above). RORgamma inhibitor class concerns (immune/Th17 suppression; thymic effects in rodents) are general knowledge and were not retrieved.
5. Edge provenance. RORC -> IL6 (+1): CollecTRI/ExTRI/NTNU curated, references PMID 20427770 (T-cell polarisation, FOXP3 promoter; not about IL6 in neural cells) and PMID 37419402 (Neuroscience 2023, abstract read): RORgamma inverse agonists SR2211 and GSK2981278 **suppressed LPS-induced Il1b, Il6 and Tnf mRNA in cultured microglia** and intrathecal SR2211 reduced Il1b/Il6 mRNA in spinal cord in an LPS model. Grade: TF edge with a glial primary source (adult mouse, LPS-activated, spinal cord pain model). Plausible in activated microglia; the Wwox-null context is not LPS-activated.
6. Expression (HPA, adult). RORC: choroid plexus epithelial (14.8) and ependymal (21.7 nCPM) snRNA; no microglia listed - tension with the 2023 paper (RORC may be inducible in activated microglia; not resolved). IL6: CNS macrophage 3.4, choroid plexus 2.1, endothelial 3.1.
7. Trials: JTE-151 0 hits (all, and neuro).
8. Verification: only after (c). Measure Rorc in Wwox-null vs wild-type microglia (baseline and LPS); if absent, drop. If present, a class-matched RORgamma inverse agonist with published CNS data (the 2023 paper's SR2211 type) is a better first tool than JTE-151; JTE-151 enters only if the missing potency/exposure data are obtained. Transfer limits: rat transcript direction opposite; immune compartment effects (Th17) in vivo confound a glial reading.

Biological promise: low-moderate. Depth: low (no rows, abstracts only). Feasibility: unknown (availability for research not stated by any source consulted). Priority: low.
