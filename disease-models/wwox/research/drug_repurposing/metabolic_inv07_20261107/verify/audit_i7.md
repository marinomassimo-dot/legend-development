## 3. Explanation items

- PDK/PDP-regulated PDH inhibition. Support list includes "premise of the PDK-inhibitor and ketone-bypass hypotheses": a premise is not evidence and is circular here; drop it. "Glycolytic targets up (E40)" supports a HIF-type shift, not PDH inhibition (E40 itself says PDK1 not up). Fibroblast lactate up (E36) is lactate, not PDH. "Cannot explain" is right and could add that the one PDH datum (E36) is a lysate kit, so it does not speak for the in-cell phosphorylation state either way (a lysate may or may not keep the phosphorylation). "Would separate" (E33, E34) is right. Not treated as exclusive.
- Lactate-side change. Support correct (E03, E07, E08). E19 is listed under "cannot explain" for a loss-of-Wwox explanation, but it is an overexpression result; label it as a different perturbation. E36 "lactate up with PDH unchanged" is the best-fitting row and is correctly used. "Would separate" is right; add a lactate measurement in the Ldh-RNAi flies, which is the missing arm in E08.
- Stress or compensatory response. Support rows right (E03, E11, E12, E16, E17). E26 is a gain-of-state stress model with an inhibitor peptide and does not support the fly-type conditionality; fine as stated. Not exclusive. The statement "cannot say whether the response protects or harms (E10)" is right.
- Downstream respiratory or TCA limitation. Two errors in the support/cannot-explain text. (1) "Citrate and 2-hydroxyglutarate rise with Abeta42 in the fly (E20, E23)": E20 and E23 do not contain citrate; citrate rises with Abeta42 in the Wwox-overexpression experiment (F = 8.197, Padj = 0.0667, Fig 8A), a separate experiment and a borderline value. (2) "E26 ... ATP production and basal respiration unchanged": in that model high glucose significantly lowered both (about 35 and 42 percent); only the peptide effect on them is non-significant. "No respiratory flux in a WWOX-deficient neural system" stands (the SH-SY5Y arm has activation of phospho-WWOX, not deficiency). "Would separate" is right and uses E35 correctly.
- Pyruvate transport. Correctly labelled as a placeholder with no datum. The line "lysate PDH activity normal with low cellular pyruvate oxidation points here" overshoots: a phosphorylation-limited state lost on homogenisation would give the same pattern, so the lysate result does not point to transport alone.
- Energy demand and activity. E42 supports it; correct. Not exclusive.
- Composition and maturation. Supported by E40 and E01; "cannot explain the fly interaction" is right.
No explanation is treated as exclusive; the "Secondary" and "Composition" rows are properly flagged as not tested.

## 4. Experiment items

- XM1: right, it cannot change whether PDH is limited. Note PDK1 is lower in sign (-0.39) in the organoid and in shWWOX progenitors (-0.67), inside the single-gene null (needs.json).
- XM2: the right first comparison; amount, three phospho-sites and activity before/after dephosphorylation are kept apart; a single Ser293-type site is explicitly excluded. Add: lysis with phosphatase inhibitors stated for the phospho arm (the phosphorylation state is lost otherwise), a composition/maturity read-out in the same series (EX composition, E40 age confound), and the activity assay must report actual and total (the one existing datum, E36, does not).
- XM3: "which isoform" cannot be answered by a "PDK-inhibited arm" if the inhibitor is pan-PDK; it needs isoform-selective knockdown. A pan-PDK inhibitor such as DCA carries documented peripheral neurotoxicity (E29-E31), a reason to prefer genetic arms. Otherwise gated correctly on step 2.
- XM4: keeps OCR and CO2-corrected acidification separate (E35). As written it does not separate transport from PDH: a PDH-independent substrate separates "before acetyl-CoA" from "after"; separating the carrier needs a carrier-bypassing PDH substrate (a cell-permeant pyruvate ester) or a carrier inhibitor. State that.
- XM5: enrichment versus absolute flux is kept distinct; sound.
- XM6: ratio versus cause is kept distinct; the lactate/pyruvate ratio is itself a proxy of cytosolic NADH/NAD+, so an independent redox read-out is the separate arm; pyruvate measurement is technically fragile (and was unobtained in the fly deposit, E22).
- XM7: states that beta-hydroxybutyrate recovery alone shows no PDH defect and is not a ketogenic-diet assessment. Correct. Three arms are matched to three hypotheses.
- XM8: a functional outcome is required beside each metabolic read-out; consistent with E10 and E08. Honest about missing endpoints.

## 5. H items

- H1: PMID 35328751 (Baryla 2022, abstract and full text read). Cells: 1BR.3.N, human normal skin fibroblasts, WWOX silenced by sgRNA/Cas9 lentivirus, cultured under four oxygen by glucose conditions for 6 h or 48 h; activities by colorimetric kits in cell homogenate. PDH activity: no difference KO versus control in any of four conditions (Fig 5b; text slip calls it "PDK" once). Lactate higher in all four. PDK mRNA significantly higher in normoxia normoglycemia only; Discussion names it PDK1. WWOX mRNA reduced only about 3-fold and not significantly in hypoxia hyperglycemia; protein about 8.5-fold. n not stated in text. Non-neural. The premise in the row is faithful, with two corrections noted at E36. The abstract does not mention PDH or PDK.
- H2: each clause of the fly paper checked against the held full text: lactate rises only with Wwox RNAi plus Abeta42 (interaction F = 23.606, Padj = 0.0174; LacZ-background effect about -0.43, no test alone); Atf4 up only in Abeta42 background (U = 0, P = 0.0022; LacZ t = 0.0894); Atf4 never perturbed; Ldh already up with Abeta42 alone (1.11 in the table, 1.19 in the text) with lactate not visibly up in the Abeta control bar; Ldh rescue partial, one locomotor endpoint, three genotypes, no Ldh-alone arm, no lactate in double-RNAi flies; top 200 features by variance; supplement lists significant metabolites only. All clauses hold. Additional points: no UAS-dilution control; lactate annotation not enantiomer-resolved; abstract language ("dysregulated pyruvate metabolism as a key mediator") outruns the data because pyruvate was not measured.
- H3: abstract of 16476929 states: 30 patients randomised (aged 10-60, A3243G), 3-year double-blind cross-over, DCA 25 mg/kg/day; initial 24 months: 15 of 15 on DCA and 4 of 15 on placebo taken off study drug; 17 of 19 discontinuations for onset or worsening of peripheral neuropathy; terminated early; mean GATE not significantly different; "unable to detect any beneficial effect"; neuropathy "overshadows the assessment of any potential benefit". Not stated in the abstract: split of the 17 by arm, reason for the other 2, how crossover was handled, post-discontinuation follow-up. Derivable: 19 of 30 (63 percent) discontinued overall; 17 of 19 is the share of discontinuations, not of 30; at least 13 of the 15 DCA discontinuations were for neuropathy.
- H4: reproduced partly. PubMed tool: "WWOX AND (pyruvate dehydrogenase OR PDH OR PDK1 OR PDK)" returns 1 hit (the fly paper), not 2; the tool keeps parentheses and OR in its translation, but it splits "pyruvate dehydrogenase" into pyruvic AND dehydrogenase, so the one hit is the fly paper through pyruvate plus (lactate) dehydrogenase, not a PDH hit. Separate strings "WWOX AND PDK", "WWOX AND PDH", "WWOX AND PDK1", "WWOX AND pyruvate dehydrogenase kinase" return 0. "WWOX AND lactate" returns 3 (42909904, 35328751 non-neural fibroblasts, 26166818 lung cancer cell line); "WWOX AND lactate AND (neuron OR neural OR brain)" returns 1 (the fly paper); "WWOX pyruvate" returns 1. Europe PMC REST, title/abstract fields: WWOX with pyruvate dehydrogenase/PDH/PDK/PDK1 gives 0; WWOX with lactate and neuron/brain/neural gives 2 (the fly paper and its preprint). Europe PMC full-text search "WWOX AND pyruvate dehydrogenase" gives 69 full-text hits (reviews, cancer, miRNA papers); I did not read them. So the absence claim holds at abstract level on both routes; it is not shown at full-text level. PMID 33629572 did not appear in any of my PubMed or Europe PMC strings ("WWOX AND PDK1" in Europe PMC full text restricted to that ID gives 0). It is a paper on exome sequencing in mitochondrial disease; its abstract, keywords and MeSH contain neither WWOX nor PDK. Its full text is not in Europe PMC, so which gene PDK1 stands for in it is UNVERIFIABLE here. PDK1 is the HGNC symbol of pyruvate dehydrogenase kinase 1, while PDPK1 is the 3-phosphoinositide-dependent kinase that older papers also call PDK1, so any hit needs reading in context; nothing in that abstract indicates a WWOX link at all.
