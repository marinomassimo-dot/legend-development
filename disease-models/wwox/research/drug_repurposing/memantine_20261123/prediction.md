# Memantine — prediction frozen before opening the WWOX target scores

Question: does untreated neuronal Wwox deletion resemble active NMDA-associated shutdown of a stimulated transcriptional program beyond generic loss of firing? If so, that could motivate testing whether memantine preserves a trophic activity response in WWOX-deficient neurons. This analysis does not measure endogenous glutamate, receptor location, drug engagement or rescue.

Seen before freezing: source paper and figure descriptions, prior WWOX immediate-early gene results, GSE184681 study design, sample titles/preparations, count header and first row identifier, supplementary workbook sheet names. No drug figure values or newly computed signature scores examined. Primary models are the mouse hippocampal and cortical neuron-specific deletions. Do not select genes using their WWOX effects.

1. Source GSE184681 has five independent mouse hippocampal preparations. Train on preparations 1, 3 and 6. Reserve 7 and 8 for directional validation. Work in log2(1 + CPM) and contrast within each preparation. Use genes with mean basal CPM at least 1 across training preparations, induced by 1 h bicuculline in each preparation and mean induction at least 0.5. At the 1 h poststimulation interval, rank by mean (TTX + NMDA) minus TTX after equal preactivation. Take up to 20 with negative differences in all three preparations. Comparator pool is the same induced genes, excluding hits, with training mean NMDA-minus-TTX between −0.15 and +0.15. Pair hits to different controls by nearest standardized Euclidean distance on basal expression, 1 h induction and passive shutoff (1 h Bic + 1 h TTX minus 2 h Bic), with no WWOX lookups. Report coverage, names, paired covariate imbalances, and all controls.
2. Require at least 8 matched pairs and a negative hit-minus-comparator NMDA contrast in **both** held-out preparations at the primary 1 h poststimulation interval. Show the separate 0.5 h poststimulation contrast and the effect of TBOA after 1 h bicuculline vs continuous 2 h bicuculline as secondary tests. TBOA blocks glutamate transport and changes activity as well as extracellular glutamate. Report it separately.
3. Map by Ensembl mouse gene ID and by unambiguous ortholog symbol for the descriptive human organoid context, discarding ambiguous/missing pairs with explicit counts. In WWOX cohorts use each animal/library as the unit. Score = per-sample mean of matched hit minus comparator log expression. Require a negative KO-minus-control difference in both mouse regions with no sign reversal on dropping one animal. A negative in one region only supports a region-dependent hypothesis. Compare acquired hippocampal kainate day 14 separately as a specificity challenge. Do not pool or ratio studies. Inspect movement of the two panel arms and individual pair contributions. Secondary markers Inhba, Bdnf and Homer1 are named by the source paper and cannot independently validate it. Bulk measurements cannot identify synaptic compartments; cellular composition and maturation remain alternative explanations.
4. Separately extract the source study's Figure 5 concentrations, viability and activity-transcript measurements for memantine at both exposures, with preparation counts and scope. Check human exposure from primary PK sources with units. Treat relief of excitotoxicity, preservation of induced transcription and clinical tolerability as distinct outcomes. If generic firing loss explains the WWOX profile or if the challenged culture response fails at a plausible exposure, lower priority for this mechanism. Directly test rescue only if an actual paired WWOX/drug dataset is available.
