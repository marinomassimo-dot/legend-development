## Decision this changes

* **Onfasprodil (MIJ821): "informazione insufficiente" for any mechanistic role.** DATO (absence within the perimeter named in `decisive.tsv` D01, D03, D04): no source reached states its receptor site on any basis, reports its maximum effect on GluN1/GluN2B (the only potency is one patent calcium-flux IC50 of 8.3 nM on an NR1/NR2B cell line, in a buffer without Mg2+, with no stated Zn2+ chelator or pH; D02), tests triheteromers, or measures subunit selectivity; "selective" is declared by sponsor-authored clinical papers without a citation. INFERENZA from structure (the audit confirmed the patent compound and its ChEMBL record): it shares the hydroxyaryl-ethanolamine arrangement of ifenprodil-type ligands, which is not a site datum. What stays interpretable: a **second scaffold** read after the ifenprodil-site tool, to see whether a different scaffold removes the same measured percent of the isolated NMDAR current (a target-level reading). What is not interpretable: site independence, a different assembly ceiling, any bearing of its human effects on a Wwox defect.
* **NP10679: "pista subordinata a una premessa".** DATO (declared by developer-affiliated authors, abstract level for the numbers): potency depends on bath pH (about 6-fold; IC50 21 nM at pH 6.9 and 130 nM at pH 7.6 on human GluN2B in oocytes, D08); DATO (measured, but for the series prototype 93-31, not NP10679): the pH effect moved potency about 9.5-fold while the fitted maximum moved from 76 to 83 percent, the size of the shift is molecule-specific within the series, and the residues proposed as the proton sensor lie at the ifenprodil site by docking and mutagenesis (D09, D10, D12, D17). No source reached gives NP10679 a maximum effect at either pH, a measured site, a triheteromer, Mg2+, voltage or use-dependence result, or a slice datum (D12, D13, D15). The premise it depends on: that the pH of the recorded tissue is known and differs from the pH at which a conventional NAM is characterised. **That premise is unmeasured, and no pathological pH is assumed.**
* **The favourable reading that fell.** My own draft (S3 A4) read a burst effect that "follows the recombinant pH-potency shift" as a specificity signature of on-target action. The audit showed it presupposes four things the sources contradict or leave open (ifenprodil and CP-101,606 may be pH-sensitive themselves; d-APV-type phosphono antagonists may follow pH in the opposite direction; protons alone cut the oocyte GluN1/GluN2B current to about 0.21 of its pH 7.6 value, so an acidic value may leave no window; a recombinant shift measured at about 23 C does not transfer to a 35 C bicarbonate-buffered slice). It was replaced by a **drug-by-pH interaction** compared with ifenprodil and d-APV at the same two pH values, which cannot separate a GluN2B-weighted circuit from proton-receptor coupling or from state.
* **What each compound adds that the earlier comparators do not.** Onfasprodil: a second scaffold with human exposure in depression, nothing mechanistic yet. NP10679: the pH dimension. The real change for the module is not the compound: **bath pH, and the pH of the tissue, enter the list of measured conditions** (M01 of the earlier matrix named Mg2+, K+, temperature; pH was absent), because GluN2B-directed potency can depend on pH for some ligands and the NMDAR current itself depends on protons.
* **What does not change:** the first experiment (measured conditions, vehicle, isolated NMDAR current, d-APV threshold x, one ifenprodil-site NAM with its removable share); no rank, card verdict or work order. Neither compound is a candidate.

## The measurement that distinguishes the explanations (S3, `S3_design.md`)

The percent of the isolated NMDAR current removed by each drug, read against the burst at the same percent, with d-APV as the reference and a matched drive-reduction control on a common axis (percent of evoked synaptic charge removed) so that "more effective per percent removed" can be told from "any cut in drive". For the pH dimension: ifenprodil (or Ro 25-6981), d-APV and NP10679 at the same two controlled pH values, after the tissue pH is measured and the recombinant maximum of NP10679 at each pH is known. The design lists what must be present from the first slice (six items), what is conditional (NP10679 pH contrast, onfasprodil as second scaffold, wild-type comparators), what may wait, and which thresholds are design assumptions rather than findings. It is a proposal for discussion with the laboratory; nothing is commissioned.

## Counter-evidence and limits

* Every NP10679 number except the series prototype's is declared by developer-affiliated authors and is a same-origin repeat of one body of work; its primary paper could not be read, so the maximum effect, the conditions and the mouse data in it are unverified. The registry value (pIC50 7.64 at pH 6.9) is probably (INFERENZA) the same measurement as the 21 nM reported in the abstract. The registry flag "approved" conflicts with the abstract and ChEMBL (phase 1) and is not carried.
* The class statements conflict: two older abstracts say ifenprodil and CP-101,606 are proton-sensitive, while the 2015 discussion says CP-101,606 and other GluN2B antagonists lack substantial pH sensitivity (Table S6, unread). Whether the ifenprodil-site NAMs used elsewhere in the module depend on pH is therefore open; it is a reason to run ifenprodil at both pH values, not a datum.
* The clinical papers are sponsor-authored. Onfasprodil: 40767837 (72 randomised in the Results, 70 in the Abstract and Methods; adults with treatment-resistant depression; a >90 percent occupancy projection is "data on file"), 37817426 (healthy adults; the text and the Fig 2 caption give different QTcF estimates, the caption's upper bound above 10 ms). NP10679: 36642931 (healthy adults; somnolence also in placebo subjects in the repeated-dose study; cohort numbers inconsistent between Methods and Results). Exposure and activity in another disease or in volunteers is not utility or safety in WOREE.
* H1 antagonism and hERG inhibition are declared for NP10679 (H1 73 nM IC50 in the paper, an "interaction" of 40 nM of unnamed quantity in the 2017 abstract; hERG 620 and 621 nM): a confound for behavioural or EEG readouts, a low-prior one for an isolated slice current. The "30-fold" margin has no stated pH (about 5-fold against the pH 7.6 potency, INFERENZA).
* A drug response in the burst slice would not show that the target is altered in Wwox; a reduction of bursts is not a developmental recovery.

## Novelty (three levels kept apart)

* **New for LEGEND:** NP10679 as a comparator and the pH dimension as a measured condition; onfasprodil as a comparator had an earlier QA-table relation, never discussed. Not a literature claim.
* **New combination of known knowledge:** pairing a GluN2B-selective, pH-sensitive NAM with a measured-pH slice design; the proton sensitivity of ifenprodil-site ligands is itself an older literature (1997–1998 abstracts).
* **Scientific novelty verified in the perimeter:** none asserted. The perimeter searched: PubMed (NP10679 OR NP-10679: 6 hits; MIJ821 OR onfasprodil: 7), Europe PMC (NP10679: 13; WWOX or epilepsy strings with either compound: no WWOX hit), ClinicalTrials.gov (4 MIJ821 records, 2 NP10679 records), on 2026-11-10; the first-in-human study of onfasprodil is not retrieved by the registry strings, so the registry search is demonstrably not exhaustive; national registries and company pipelines were not searched.
