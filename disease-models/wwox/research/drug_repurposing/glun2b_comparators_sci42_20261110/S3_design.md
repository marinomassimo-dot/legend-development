## 1 · The preparation actually studied, and what is missing from it

DATO (PMID 34634460, as carried in `ampa_nmda_discrim_20261108/output/observations.tsv`, rows OB01–OB04, OB11, OB15): neuron-specific (Synapsin-Cre) Wwox deletion, acute neocortical slices, layer II/III pyramidal cells, P13–P17, 35 C; spontaneous bursts abolished by d-APV 50 µM at one concentration, without a vehicle or time-matched control group; incubation ACSF 1.3 mM Mg2+ and 3.5 mM K+ (the recording ACSF is not stated); sIPSC amplitude about halved. The evoked delayed response falls to about 0.4 of baseline under d-APV (figure-read), not to zero: "abolition" holds for the spontaneous bursts. DATO (absence, perimeter in the earlier packages): no isolated NMDAR current, no subunit, glycine, bath pH or measured Mg2+ datum exists for it. Nothing from other preparations (oocytes, culture, adult or other-region slices, humans) is merged into this system: the compound facts (`D02`, `D09`, `D13`) are recombinant or declared and enter only as constraints on what an arm can mean.

## 2 · The four explanations

| Id | Explanation (IPOTESI) | Reading in this design |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Total NMDAR gain: the burst needs NMDAR-mediated drive beyond a threshold x (percent of isolated NMDAR current that must be removed to stop it) | The d-APV series defines x, if x exists as a graded threshold (see section 5: the earlier data show only binary abolition at one concentration) |
| E2 | A GluN2B-weighted burst-generating circuit in this preparation: the burst depends on GluN2B-containing receptors more than their share of the current of the recorded layer II/III pyramidal cells would predict | "Share" is defined for that cell population in these slices, measured in the same animals; a somatic isolated current may not represent the compartment where the burst is generated. A result in knockout slices alone is not a Wwox-specific alteration of GluN2B (that needs the wild-type comparison, A7) |
| E3 | State of the preparation: bath pH, Mg2+, K+, Zn2+, temperature, run-down | Burst incidence and drug effect move with the measured state and wild-type tissue can be made to burst |
| E4 | Alternative action or general loss of network function: any large cut in excitatory drive, off-target action of a tool, sedation-like or release-reducing effects | A matched cut in drive on a common axis reproduces the effect. For NP10679 a declared H1 antagonism (73 nM in the paper, 40 nM in an earlier abstract of the same developer, `D14`) is a low-prior candidate in an isolated slice (no histaminergic tone was measured) and a real confound for any in vivo, EEG or behavioural readout; the declared "40 CNS targets" list was not seen |

## 4 · Order of the experiments, and what may not wait

**Present from the first slice (a result is uninterpretable without them):**
1. Measured values, not recipes: bath pH (and whether it is set with bicarbonate/CO2 or HEPES), Mg2+, K+, temperature at the recording site; the isolated NMDAR current (AMPA and GABA-A blocked, holding potential stated) in cells or slices **from the same animals** as the burst recordings.
2. A time-matched vehicle (A0) at the same position in the sequence, and a **washout or reversibility test for each drug** (the arms are sequenced within slices; d-APV reversed in the earlier paper, a NAM may not, onfasprodil's "reversible" is only declared).
3. The percent of isolated NMDAR current removed for every drug at the concentration used (the x-axis), with the d-APV series defining x where x exists (A1). For every drug in the sequence (d-APV, the NAM, the AMPA/kainate pair) the percent of **evoked synaptic charge** removed, and the change of peak or slope, is read as well, because A6 can only be matched on that common axis.
4. One conventional ifenprodil-site NAM with its removable share r, with a Zn2+ chelator calibration in the isolated-current recordings (A2, A3): a plateau without r cannot be read.
5. A matched drive-reduction control on a common axis (A6): its instrument is the AMPA/kainate pair carried from `ampa_comparison_it41_20261109/` (a noncompetitive 2,3-benzodiazepine with NBQX as the independent competitive partner; the tissue block of 80.5–89 percent at 10 µM was measured in two other mouse slice preparations, and no block verified with a burst readout exists), titrated to the same measured percent of **evoked synaptic charge** and read also on peak or slope. Its own limits stay (the achieved block is 80–89 percent at 10 µM in tissue; an AMPA block also removes the depolarisation that relieves the Mg2+ block, so abolition under it does not separate AMPA-led from NMDAR-regenerative drive). A receptor-agnostic release-reducing manipulation would be a second instrument with no mechanism paper read here and is not required at the start. If A6 is not run, a favourable NAM result is reported as compatible with E2 and E4 and does not separate them.
6. Animals as the unit of replication; scoring blind to genotype and drug; the order of arms randomised within animal.

**Conditional:**
* **A4 NP10679**, only after (a) the preparation's tissue pH is measured (no pathological pH is assumed; two controlled values inside what the slice tolerates, a design assumption), (b) the recombinant maximum of NP10679 at each pH is measured with stated conditions (the IC50 at pH 6.9 and 7.6 exists only as an abstract-level, same-origin figure, 21 and 130 nM; the primary paper was not readable; `D08`), and (c) ifenprodil (or Ro 25-6981) and d-APV are run at both pH values as comparators, because ifenprodil itself may be pH-sensitive (older abstracts, `D11`) and a competitive phosphono antagonist may follow pH in the opposite direction (Yuan 2015 class sentence, INFERENZA). The question it can ask: is the pH dependence of the burst effect larger for NP10679 than for the comparators? It is a condition-linked comparison, not a proof of target.
* **A5 onfasprodil** as a second scaffold after A2.
* **A7 genotype comparators.**

**May wait:** the GluN2A-preferring arm (TCN-201 needs a measured slice co-agonist level first), heterozygous tissue as an intermediate (the earlier package shows it is not a clean one), a second ifenprodil-site NAM before the first has shown r (it would control scaffold; its ceiling is not assumed equal).

**Not to be done:** NP10679 at several pH values before the preparation's pH is measured; any matching to clinical exposure.

## 5 · Limits that this proposal keeps

* **Design assumptions, not findings:** the number of pH values; the concentration steps; the definition of "abolition" (percent of baseline burst rate); what counts as a plateau; that x is a single graded threshold (the earlier data are one concentration and binary abolition, and sequential wash-in in one slice can show hysteresis); that the burst depends on both charge and peak depolarisation; that tonic Zn2+ is a state variable. None is given a number here.
* **Statistical unit:** the animal (litter where applicable); slices and cells are nested and enter a mixed model or are summarised per animal; cells or nuclei are not independent animals. Bursting is binary per slice and a count per minute per slice; analysis choices are for the laboratory. Several drugs and two pH values per animal mean many knockout animals in a P13–P17 window (a feasibility question, not a logical one).
* A reduction of bursts by any arm is a reduction of an electrophysiological phenotype in P13–P17 slices. It is not a developmental recovery and not a rescue of the defect, and a drug response does not show that the drug's target is altered in Wwox.
* The recombinant pH shifts (oocytes about 23 C, Mg2+-free, EDTA for the series prototype; conditions unknown for NP10679) do not transfer to a bicarbonate-buffered 35 C slice, and bath pH does not fix synaptic-cleft pH (the abstract of the NP10679 discovery paper itself invokes acidic vesicle content at active synapses, `E26`).
* Onfasprodil and NP10679 have no data in this preparation; their roles (`output/roles.tsv`, checked in the audit recheck) concern the comparison, not benefit.
* Human exposure (onfasprodil in depression and healthy adults; NP10679 in healthy adults) is exposure in other populations: no dose, no pediatric statement, nothing about WOREE (`D05`–`D07`, `D16`).
