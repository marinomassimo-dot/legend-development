## Format limits (precise)

* `compound_view.tsv` has one `kind` per row: "a second scaffold, conditional" and "a pH-linked comparison" are expressed in `label` and `rationale`, not in `kind`.
* `investigations.tsv` `next_test` is one free-text string; it cannot hold ordered arms, the start set and the conditional ones.
* `hypothesis_packages.tsv` and its reader expect a `comparison.tsv` with a row id (row 9).
