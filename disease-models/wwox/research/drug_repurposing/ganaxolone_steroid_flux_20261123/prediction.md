# Does WWOX loss selectively deprive developing neurons of a GABA-active neurosteroid?

23 November 2026, SCI_CODEX. Two chemically separate hypotheses should be tested. The endogenous allopregnanolone branch involves C3 reduction of a C21 pregnane. Keller's 2006 cell study instead reports C17 reduction of two C19 androgens by mouse Wwox-expressing cells, including androsterone to 3α,17β-androstanediol. Failure to form the latter could conceivably alter inhibitory tone and change the effect of ganaxolone, even with no shortage of allopregnanolone. Both androsterone and the diol can modulate GABA-A receptors. The predicted sign of their net effect depends on free concentrations and the responses to both steroids in the same neural preparation. The human WWOX expression control in the thesis was ambiguous. Check source table, figure, original human enzymology and experiments with other enzymes before attributing any flux.

Pre-data prediction: if the same mouse C17 reactions can persist through Hsd17b12, the claim that absence of Wwox alone abolishes this route has a direct biological competitor. Inspect Wwox and Hsd17b12 in the two P17 neuronal-deletion regions, hippocampal global-null tissue and pooled hippocampal nucleus classes. Report per-genotype per-sample abundance, per-class molecule and co-detection counts, and all omissions for the mouse bulk contrasts. A substantial alternative substrate context requires Hsd17b12 ≥1 TPM in every relevant mutant sample and expression in ≥10 assigned neuronal nuclei in each of the two single-nucleus pools; a lower or higher RNA estimate does not give a flux. A co-detection observation in controls nominates a cell context. Include human WWOX-null organoid expression of both genes for localization of the analytic blind spot only. The human HSD17B12 substrate specificity measured independently differs. Do not equate transcript abundance between species with functional redundancy. No inference of flux from these markers will establish a ganaxolone effect.

Compare apparent Vmax and Km reported within one preparation at equal molar substrate across concentrations. For separate assays and unconstrained sink/uptake rates, such ratios are only conditional. Require isotope-resolved androsterone-to-diol conversion, verified expression and a positive control before attributing a human catalytic effect. For downstream substitution, require a measured endogenous receptor deficit consistent with measured free steroid mixtures and a genotype-dependent drug response after matching firing and receptor composition. A response to ganaxolone alone would leave the metabolic mechanism unresolved.
