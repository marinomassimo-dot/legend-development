## NP3P3 and the decision
**Re-scope, do not close.** The trigger as worded ("a head-to-head in one system with diazepam as comparator") is **met by Ralvenius alone**; it does not resolve the dispute. A sharper trigger: **an independent same-laboratory series at matched β (all α-x β2γ2, or all β3) with diazepam, clonazepam, clobazam and NDMC at α1, α2, α3 and α5, at a stated EC10 and EC20 with total and increment bases, n per subtype reported, and the Emax reference defined; or an independent replication of Ralvenius's NDMC α2/α1.** The daily scout's NP3P3 row can stop re-announcing hits that do not address that trigger. The L2 arm may carry the "may" sentence above and must not carry the "may not" wording.
**The one discriminating measurement (described, not commissioned):** the matched-β same-laboratory series above, with the clonazepam (or diazepam) ratio measured in the same new series as the benchmark for "non-selective" (Hammer's 1.21 / 1.40 is one small-n dataset, α1 n = 4, α2 n = 5, and only indicates that the benchmark is not 1.0); it would change whether any L2 wording about subtype preference may be stated.
