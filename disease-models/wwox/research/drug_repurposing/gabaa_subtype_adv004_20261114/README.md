3. **A head-to-head in one system with diazepam exists in one paper, and NP3P3's trigger, as I defined it before reading, is met by Ralvenius alone.** Ralvenius 2016 reports diazepam, clobazam and N-desmethylclobazam (NDMC) at α1, α2, α3 and α5 in one system (rat subunits, HEK293 whole-cell, GABA EC10 per subtype, co-application, 20–24 °C, −60 mV; EC50 and Emax as mean ± SD, **n only as "5–7 cells per data point"**). Hammer fails the trigger (diazepam only as a single 3 µM point, n 2–4, no potency; NDMC α5 n = 2) and Jensen fails it (binding only, no diazepam competitor). **Qualifications that travel with it:** the β isoform differs between α subtypes in Ralvenius (α2 and α3 with β3, α1 with β2; α5 printed β3 in Table 1 and β2 in Methods and the Fig 1 legend), so the α2-against-α1 contrast is also β3 against β2; the Emax reference ("100%") is **undefined in the text** (most coherent reading, INFERENZA: percent increase over the EC10 current); n is only a range; there is **no independent replication** (the 2026 Phase IIa paper, PMID 42886059, rests its α2 preference on Ralvenius alone and cites neither Hammer nor Jensen; its primary-outcome difference is 0.46 points, p = 0.47). A trigger met by one lab's one series does not resolve the dispute.
4. **The disagreement is smaller in direction than in wording** (INFERENZA from the printed values): NDMC α2/α1 is above 1 in both functional papers (1.77 printed in Ralvenius; Hammer's own Imax values give 1.33 on its total-response basis and 1.65 on an increment basis, derived: Ralvenius quotes 1.33 in its Discussion, Hammer does not print it), NDMC is α2 = α3 > α1 in both, clobazam is at or below 1 in both (0.78 and 1.02); potency shows no α-selectivity in either paper; Ralvenius's potencies are 2.8–5.9-fold lower than Hammer's in all eight pairs (derived). Hammer's "non-selective" is relative to clonazepam, whose own α2/α1 (1.21 total, 1.40 increment) is not flat, and its Table 3 shows NDMC α1 significantly lower than α2 and α3 (P < 0.05), called "not pertinent". Jensen is **affinity** (human subunits, 4 °C, [3H]flumazenil, Ki): "modestly α2-preferring in affinity" is a different quantity, and Ralvenius's own binding (IC50 at α1 and α2 only) differs by 1.1–1.3-fold where Jensen reports 2.5–4.3-fold.
5. **Do conditions explain the disagreement? Not shown, and not shown separable.** Between Ralvenius and Hammer at least eight conditions change together (species, system, patch versus two-electrode clamp, GABA fraction and absolute concentration (EC10 at 1–8 µM versus EC20 at 15–60 µM), 30 s pre-application versus none, holding potential, β isoform and γ2 versus γ2S, normalisation, laboratory); no paper varies any of them within its design. **Fisher 2011** (PMID 21237147, rat α3β3γ2L, HEK-293T patch; read at full text by the verifier) reports diazepam at 508 ± 28% (N = 5) at the subunit set where Ralvenius reports 227 ± 15% (text 237%), while the diazepam EC50 agrees (59 versus 71 nM): a roughly twofold efficacy difference between two laboratories in one nominal system, larger than the NDMC α2/α1 effect: efficacy values in this field are laboratory-dependent for reasons the three papers do not isolate (INFERENZA).
6. **What may and may not be said for the L2 arm (a conditional design position, not an ungating of Phase 0).** *May:* "in one laboratory's rat-subunit series NDMC potentiates α2 and α3 more than α1 in efficacy (printed ratio 1.77, with the β isoform and the Emax basis as stated), the human-oocyte series points the same way on a total-response basis (1.33) and calls it non-selective; potency shows no α-selectivity; no independent replication exists." *May not:* "α2-preferring" as an established property; a "subtype-discriminating pair" (the clobazam–NDMC efficacy contrast inside Ralvenius is confounded with β3 against β2, an undefined Emax basis and n as a range, and is not reproduced as a contrast by Hammer: 1.02 against 1.33); any attribution of an effect in a Wwox slice to α2.
7. **Not verified, not carried:** the producer's readings of Hammer's diazepam bars (about 254, 241, 335 and 225 at α1, α2, α3, α5, `output/numbers.tsv` N072–N076) **conflict with Hammer's own text** (clobazam at α1, 256, and NDMC at α2, 270, are "slightly but significantly smaller" than diazepam, which requires diazepam above 256 and 270); the verifier could not open the figure; the derived diazepam α2/α1 of 0.95 must not be carried.

## NP3P3 and the decision
**Re-scope, do not close.** The trigger as worded ("a head-to-head in one system with diazepam as comparator") is **met by Ralvenius alone**; it does not resolve the dispute. A sharper trigger: **an independent same-laboratory series at matched β (all α-x β2γ2, or all β3) with diazepam, clonazepam, clobazam and NDMC at α1, α2, α3 and α5, at a stated EC10 and EC20 with total and increment bases, n per subtype reported, and the Emax reference defined; or an independent replication of Ralvenius's NDMC α2/α1.** The daily scout's NP3P3 row can stop re-announcing hits that do not address that trigger. The L2 arm may carry the "may" sentence above and must not carry the "may not" wording.
**The one discriminating measurement (described, not commissioned):** the matched-β same-laboratory series above, with Hammer's non-selective clonazepam ratio (1.21 total, 1.40 increment) as the benchmark for "non-selective", not 1.0; it would change whether any L2 wording about subtype preference may be stated.
