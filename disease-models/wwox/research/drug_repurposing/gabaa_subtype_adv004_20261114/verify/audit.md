## 4 · Item 4, the conditions analysis and the trigger

* **"No single condition can be shown to explain the disagreement from the three papers alone": correct, and stronger than stated.** Between the only two functional papers, species, system, patch vs TEVC, GABA fraction and absolute concentration (EC10 at 1-8 µM vs EC20 at 15-60 µM), 30 s pre-application vs none, holding potential, beta isoform, gamma2 vs gamma2S, normalisation and laboratory all change together: one contrast with at least eight labels. No paper varies any of them within its design (Hammer's only manipulation, the gamma2S cRNA ratio at alpha2, changed nothing). **Added evidence the producer could not use: Fisher 2011** (PMID 21237147; rat alpha3 beta3 gamma2L, HEK-293T, patch; read at full text) reports diazepam 508 ± 28 % (N = 5) at the same subunit set in which Ralvenius reports 227 ± 15 % (text 237 %), while the diazepam EC50 agrees (59 vs 71 nM). A twofold efficacy difference between two labs in one nominal system exceeds the NDMC alpha2/alpha1 effect (1.33-1.77), and the within-lab order of NDMC against diazepam at alpha3 is not reproduced (Ralvenius 1.08, Fisher 0.53). INFERENZA: efficacy values in this field are lab-dependent for reasons the three papers do not isolate.
* **What confounds what.** Species, system, patch/TEVC, pre-application and laboratory are fully collinear in the Ralvenius/Hammer contrast. Inside Ralvenius, beta isoform is confounded with alpha subtype (alpha2 and alpha3 beta3; alpha1 beta2; alpha5 beta2 per Methods, beta3 per Table 1) and GABA concentration differs per subtype by design. Normalisation differs between papers (section 3).
* **"Smaller in direction than in wording": yes.** NDMC alpha2/alpha1 > 1 in both (1.77; 1.33 total / 1.65 increment), Hammer's Table 3 significant (P < 0.05) for NDMC alpha1 lower than alpha2 and alpha3, which the authors call "not pertinent"; "non-selective" is relative to clonazepam, whose own ratios (alpha2/alpha1 1.21, alpha3/alpha1 1.51, significant) are not flat. Potency shows no alpha selectivity in either paper (concordant). Clobazam: 0.78 vs 1.02, both at or below 1. **NDMC is alpha2 = alpha3 > alpha1 in both papers (253/245 vs 143; 270/270 vs 203), so "alpha2-preferring" is narrower than the data.**
* **NP3P3 trigger met by Ralvenius alone: yes as the S0 word it, with the producer's two qualifications right (n only a range, 5-7 cells per data point; Emax reference undefined) and three more:** beta differs between alpha subtypes (the alpha2/alpha1 contrast is alpha2 beta3 vs alpha1 beta2), Table 1 vs Methods disagree on alpha5, and there is no independent replication (the 2026 Phase IIa cites Ralvenius alone for the preference). Hammer fails the trigger (diazepam only a 3 µM single point, n 2-4, no potency; NDMC alpha5 n = 2); Jensen fails (affinity only, no diazepam competitor).
* **What "same system" requires that Ralvenius does not meet:** constant beta and gamma across the subtypes compared (matched-beta pairs are only alpha1/alpha5 and alpha2/alpha3), per-estimate n, a defined Emax reference, and one GABA concentration or a stated reason for per-subtype EC10.

## 8 · The producer's answer and phase note

(Section added after the verdicts above were written; `answer.md` and `phase_note.md` were opened only now.)

**Where the producer's wording agrees with the verification:** trigger met by Ralvenius alone with the two qualifications; no condition can be shown to explain the disagreement; the disagreement is smaller in direction than in wording; G1 partly, G2 held, G3 held, G4 partly, G5 held, G6 failed; all figures and bot checks disclosed; no ranking, dose or clinical statement in the design position. The 38 IT14 rows and the 122 value rows hold.

**Where it overstates or differs:**
1. **Section 3 and section 1, Hammer diazepam 3 µM "about 254 / 241 / 335 / 225" (and N072-N075).** Presented in the main table as a figure reading with a range of about ±5. Two of the four conflict with Hammer's own text (diazepam alpha1 must exceed the clobazam alpha1 value 256; alpha2 must exceed NDMC 270), and "comparable or slightly higher" (Discussion) cannot hold for values below the clobazam and NDMC maxima. The conflict is not mentioned; the 0.95 alpha2/alpha1 for diazepam derived from the bars is not safe. Mark the row unverified until the figure is read by a reader who can reach it.
2. **Section 2, 1.33 against 1.77.** The comparison sets a total-basis ratio (Hammer, EC20 response = 100 %) against a ratio whose basis is undefined (Ralvenius). On an increment basis Hammer's NDMC ratio is 1.65, within about 7 % of 1.77; on a total basis Ralvenius's would be 1.45. Section 3 says the quantities "are not the same" but section 2 and section 5 ("same direction, smaller magnitude") do not carry that into the magnitude.
3. **Section 5, suggested wording "alpha2-preferring in efficacy in one rat recombinant series".** The data are alpha2 = alpha3 > alpha1 (253/245 vs 143 in Ralvenius; 270/270 vs 203 in Hammer) and the alpha2/alpha1 contrast is alpha2 beta3 against alpha1 beta2; the wording should say alpha2- and alpha3-preferring over alpha1, with beta confounded, or not use alpha2-preferring for NDMC without that.
4. **Section 6, decision rule.** "Ratio near 1 in the human arm" has no benchmark. In Hammer's own dataset clonazepam, called non-selective, gives 1.21 (total) or 1.40 (increment); a threshold needs a non-selective comparator in the same system and a stated basis.
5. **Not stated:** the Fisher 2011 alpha3 series (read here: diazepam 508 %, clobazam 487 %, norclobazam 270 % in rat HEK patch) is the one cross-lab check of the same nominal system and shows efficacy values differing about twofold for diazepam against Ralvenius; this strengthens the answer's "inconsistent and no condition explains it", but the answer rests it only on the absence of within-paper variation.
6. **Small:** Table 1 "header" should read row label; N076/N077 normalisation cell copy-paste; the Fisher EC50 for diazepam (59.4 nM) is source-stated but not in N083; phase note "Method slip" sentence is accurate as a disclosure (one PMC page request each, no retry).

Net: the producer's position is conditional and mostly within the evidence; points 1-4 are the ones to soften or qualify before it reaches a consumer.
