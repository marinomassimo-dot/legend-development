## Rules carried into every package
* One animal or pool per arm: cells are not replicates and several records of one series are not independent experiments.
* A lower transcript is not fewer cells or lower activity.
* Evidence from other diseases or organs keeps its origin and limits and is never read as a direction for WWOX; no class transfer to a molecule; unknown is not negative.
* Seizure control, development, network function, myelin and quality of life are kept apart.
* No compound name that the shareable audit withholds may appear in text meant for the public view.
