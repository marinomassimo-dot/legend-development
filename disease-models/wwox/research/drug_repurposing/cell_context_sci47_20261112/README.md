## The answer first
1. **The rule I wrote before the run was not met, and the reason is OPC.** KCNQ2 in the forebrain neuronal classes (maximum 151.9 nCPM, forebrain projection neurons; forebrain excitatory 116.9; mge/cge interneurons 89.1 and 82.4) is 1.36 times the glial maximum, because OPC read 111.5 nCPM. Against astrocytes (21.9) the ratio is 6.9; against microglia (3.7) and oligodendrocytes (3.6) it is above 40. Controls passed: SNAP25 and RBFOX3 neuronal, GFAP and AQP4 astrocytic, P2RY12 microglial, MBP oligodendrocytic, PDGFRA OPC (321.0), SLC17A7 and GAD1 in excitatory and inhibitory neuronal classes. (DATO, one pooled value per cluster type, adult multi-region human nuclei; own ratios.) The contrary-result clause was written for "neurons not above glia" and was triggered by one glial class; the descriptive reading below is mine, not the pre-registered one.
2. **The held human table gives a low KCNQ2 value that is not confined to its neuron sample; the cause is not established.** In the held GSE73721 human table KCNQ2 is 0.1 in the one neuron sample and 0.2-1.5 in the four whole-cortex samples (45, 63, 25 and 53 years); RBFOX3 is 0.6 in the same neuron sample against SNAP25 692.9. In the examined columns 100 raw cells carry exactly 0.1, the lowest printed value of the table (a floor, not an expression level). The HPA table (a different portal and a different method; scale nCPM, not comparable with FPKM) gives KCNQ2 high in neuronal classes. So the disagreement between the mouse P7 neurons (30.68 FPKM) and the held human neuron sample (0.10) **does not show a biological difference between species**, and the held human value should not be cited as human neurons lacking KCNQ2. **Why the held table reads low is not known**: annotation or mapping of the locus, a feature of the sample, the library or the floor convention are candidates; none was tested here, and no specific technical cause is asserted. **Attribution it changes:** at RNA level KCNQ2 is neuron-enriched over astrocytes, microglia and oligodendrocytes in both the mouse and the HPA tables; **OPC also carry KCNQ2 RNA in both** (mouse P17 OPC 10.68 FPKM against neurons 30.68, ratio 0.35; HPA OPC 111.5 against 151.9 nCPM, ratio 0.73). That makes an OPC contribution to a bath-applied Kv7 modulator **plausible**; it does **not** show that any drug acts on OPC in a P13-17 slice, which needs protein, channel function, drug access and the stage, none of which these tables give (adult human nuclei; mouse OPC at P17). The control proposed stays: an OPC-aware readout (identified cell classes, or the Kv7-blocker-sensitive current measured in identified cells) or a cell-restricted application before a slice effect is attributed to neurons.
3. **A caution that rides on the same table.** KCNQ3 reads 2,632 nCPM in microglia nuclei against 846 in forebrain excitatory neurons; the mouse table has Kcnq3 0.10 in microglia. The species or technical difference is not resolved here and no KCNQ3 attribution to glia is made or excluded.
4. **What the table cannot say.** Adult human nuclei, multi-region, no layer; not P7, not P13-17; one pooled value per cluster type with no donor or replicate level (nuclei are not animals; no statistics are given); FPKM and nCPM are not comparable scales; RNA is not Kv7 current. The measure that resolves the stage and the cell class is an age-matched, layer-resolved Kcnq2/Kv7 readout in the recorded cells (labelling or in situ at P13-17, or the blocker-sensitive current in the same cells as the sIPSC programme of package A). No public processed table of that stage and class was found within the light-download rule; no search beyond the HPA file and the held tables was made (not an absence claim).

## Decision it changes
The Kv7 lever (IT46 S3/R5, lever L5 of package A) keeps its neuronal rationale at RNA level in the mouse and HPA tables. Two statements change: "human neuron KCNQ2 at the floor" is withdrawn as a biological reading (cause of the low held value unknown), and an OPC contribution to a bath-applied opener is added as a **plausible alternative to be controlled**, not as an established action.

## Corrections of wording in `nrf2_expression_sci46_20261111` (dated update; the original text stays)
Entered as an update at the foot of that README (this branch) and listed here:
* "has no expressing cell class" (HCAR2, item 4 and consumer row 4) → HCAR2 is **low in the pooled purified classes examined** (neurons and astrocytes 0.10, OPC 0.21 FPKM, microglia 18.12; two pools per class, the 0.10 value is the table's floor). It does not support "no cell expresses it": a minority population, other ages and conditions are outside the table; the human table has no HCAR2 row (not zero, not tested).
* "at or near the floor" and "readable (at least 5 FPKM)" → 5 FPKM was a working threshold of that note, not a detection limit or a universal rule; rows at 0.10 are the floor value of the table.
* "neuron-readable target panel" → Gclm, Txnrd1, Gpx4, G6pdx, Srxn1 and Gclc are **measurable** in neuron RNA; that note neither sourced nor tested that they are NRF2-specific readouts, and a gene well measured is not by that fact a specific readout of NRF2.
* RNA presence of KEAP1, NFE2L2 and the panel does not show functional protein, nuclear NRF2 or a pharmacological response.
