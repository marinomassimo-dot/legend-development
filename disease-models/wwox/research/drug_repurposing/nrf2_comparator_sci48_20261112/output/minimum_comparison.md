# Minimum comparison that would make a KEAP1/NRF2 reagent interpretable in a neural class (SCI48, rewritten 2026-11-12 after the correction round)

Non-canonical research material. Not medical advice; no dose, schedule or clinical use; no claim about WWOX or any reagent as a treatment. Labels: DATO read in a source, INFERENZA mine, IPOTESI proposal.

**Status.** INFERENZA: for sulforaphane, germline-knock-out designs with the reagent in both genotypes under the same insult exist in neural tissue in vivo (Li 2022, Zhao 2015; TBI, ICH, MPTP and spinal cord papers at abstract or verifier depth) and agree in direction (comparator role). None shows the loss adequate at the endpoint time, none uses an acute loss, and one (Holloway 2016) records a component independent of Nrf2 at the higher exposure. For the non-covalent binder RA839 no neural-class study exists (primary cells); KI-696 has one glial, shRNA-verified row (reused, floor-effect caveat, so comparator). No reagent is a probe by the package rule.

**Question.** Does a reagent's effect on a stated endpoint need NRF2, separately from other actions, and can a non-covalent binder partner an electrophile?

**Preparation.** Primary mouse or human iPSC-derived neurons, astrocytes and microglia as separate arms (not only mixed cultures; Li's and Mao's neuron cultures did not state glial content); acute slices only if the loss can be induced and verified in them. A WWOX-deficient and a matched control version only after INV-08 X0 shows nuclear NRF2 and NQO1 activity in it (IPOTESI; this comparison does not test WWOX).

**Indispensable arms (IPOTESI).** (1) Non-covalent binder with its inactive scaffold analogue (Winkel RA889/RA912; never run under loss). (2) An electrophile in the same cells at a matched on-target response, not a matched concentration (Winkel 50 nM CDDO-Me versus 20 uM RA839; single-gene Nqo1 match only: about 55 and 44 over about 12). (3) Genetic Keap1 loss (two siRNAs) for the expected response without a reagent. (4) Each of these under NRF2 loss, plus the loss-alone arm and the reagent-alone arm under the same challenge (Mao 2019 had OGD+Sfn+sh-Nrf2 but no OGD+sh-Nrf2 and no Sfn-alone arm). (5) Two exposures of the electrophile, because Holloway's partial effect appeared only at the higher one.

**Control on the target.** Written before the experiment: NRF2 protein and mRNA reduced at the endpoint time (not only an allele PCR, as in the TBI paper) and loss of the matched target-gene induction by the Keap1-loss arm. Occlusion: Keap1 loss must not add to a reagent effect that is NRF2-dependent. Prefer acute or inducible loss; a germline arm only as an additional arm. Matched conditions: the reagent must act in the control genotype in the same condition as in the loss genotype (Yao 2021 gave SFN to knock-out mice without the LPS challenge in which it acted in wild type).

**Discriminating endpoint.** Same readout for all reagents: molecular (NQO1 and a second target gene, mRNA and protein), then one functional readout in the dish (network firing or sEPSC/sIPSC frequency, reagent applied in the medium) with a non-cytotoxic exposure; viability only as a toxicity control.

**Expected results.** H1/H3: reagent effect lost under adequate loss, binder, electrophile and Keap1-loss agree. H5: effect unchanged under adequate loss. H5 for the electrophile only: binder matches the Keap1-loss arm, electrophile keeps an effect under loss (Winkel's Discussion suggests this contrast; not a result). H6: viability or network worse under activation.

**Inconclusive if:** loss below the criterion at the endpoint time; exposure cytotoxic or far above the on-target range. For RA839 that range depends on the Kd used: the printed ITC Kd is 6 uM (10-20 uM is 1.7-3.3 times it; 71-143 times the FP IC50 of 0.14 uM), but the tabulated thermodynamics (Delta H -28, T Delta S -22 kcal/mol) imply a Kd near 40 uM, which puts 10 uM below it (verifier R05; within the stated error). Also inconclusive if the Keap1-loss arm is absent or only NQO1 is read.

**Decision that changes.** The role INV-08 gives each reagent (probe, comparator, conditional hypothesis, not interpretable); claims 2 (useful in WWOX loss) and 3 (therapeutic transfer) stay untouched.

**Orchestrator note (2026-11-12, after the V10 recheck).** The status paragraph above reads KI-696 as comparator through the floor-effect caveat; the matrix keeps the two endpoints apart: probe at the molecular endpoint in primary rat microglia (M20: induction of HO1, NQO1, GCLC with a loss verified at mRNA and KEAP1-knock-out occlusion) and comparator at the viability endpoint (M21: a viability floor under loss is not excluded). In the sulforaphane clause "in vivo function" means the neurologic score, behaviour and the injury-model abstracts; residual hematoma haemoglobin is a tissue readout; neuronal death and contusion volume in the TBI paper were rat measurements without a loss arm.
