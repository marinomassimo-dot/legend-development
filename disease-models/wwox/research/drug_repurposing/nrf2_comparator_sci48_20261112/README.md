## Answer first

1. **The comparison that became interpretable is the design, not a reagent.** A pair of one non-covalent binder and one electrophile, each with and without an **acute** NRF2 loss **shown adequate at the endpoint time**, with the electrophile **inside** the loss arm, a KEAP1-loss occlusion arm, loss-alone and reagent-alone arms under the same challenge, and the **survivors after loss** read genome-wide or on a panel (`output/minimum_comparison.md`, `engine/comparison.tsv` C-N1, C-N2, `engine/new_measurements.tsv` M-N1 to M-N4). **No reagent reaches the role "probe" in a neuron or astrocyte** by the package rule (matrix 44 rows: probe 1, comparator 14, not interpretable 26, conditional hypothesis 3; the one probe row is KI-696 in primary rat microglia, reused from SCI46, molecular endpoint). Evidence grade: DATO for what the sources print (blind verifier V10 reproduced the Winkel rows 31 of 33 with one minor defect and one unverifiable; roles checked 23 of 29 at first pass, corrections applied and rechecked); INFERENZA for the roles; the sulforaphane picture is at mixed depth (three texts, one verifier-targeted text, the rest abstracts).
2. **IT46-Q06, answered: RA839 (Winkel 2015) does not qualify as an NRF2-dependent probe, and the SCI46 statement that it "reaches the knock-out criterion at abstract depth" is corrected.** The text and all nine figures were read (supplement Tables S1–S4 not reachable; the PMC supplement URL is an interstitial). The loss is a germline Nrf2-/- macrophage from 12-week C57BL/6J mice; **no check of the knock-out is printed anywhere**; the array (10 µM, 4 h) gave 105 probe sets in wild type and **two genes in the knock-out (Zfp192 and Pank1; direction not printed; not regulated in wild type)**; **CDDO-Me is in no knock-out arm and not in the array**; no knock-out arm exists for the NO/Nos2 endpoint with any reagent; Keap1 siRNA reached about 75–80% (mRNA only). The cells are myeloid. Role: **comparator for a transcriptome survivor set** (K01); NO/Nos2 and liver: not interpretable. The printed Kd is 6 µM but the tabulated thermodynamics imply about 40 µM (verifier arithmetic; within the stated error), so every "multiple of Kd" depends on which is used. No use of RA839 in primary neurons, astrocytes or microglia was found by two independent searches; glioma lines and the SVG astrocyte line use it without a loss arm.
3. **IT46-Q09, answered in part: sulforaphane has a germline-null result in neural tissue, and it is a comparator, not a probe.** In rodent brain and spinal cord injury or toxin models, sulforaphane given to both wild-type and germline Nrf2-null animals under the same insult lost its benefit in the nulls in studies from at least four independent groups (three at text-level reading; MPTP and spinal cord at abstract). Endpoint classes: function in vivo (Li 2022 behaviour, TBI neurologic score, ICH and spinal cord abstracts), viability (Li TUNEL and neurons; MPTP abstract), molecular (Li HO-1 increment, TBI oxidative markers); residual hematoma haemoglobin is a tissue readout. **What stays missing:** an acute or conditional loss, a protein/mRNA check at the endpoint time (the TBI paper has an allele PCR only), a Keap1-loss or occlusion arm, any cell-type resolution (global knock-out), a matched slice or culture function arm (Yao 2021's is unmatched), and a KEAP1-binder partner. One source records a partial effect persisting in the null at 50 mg/kg (Holloway 2016) and one endpoint retains about three-quarters of the gain under shRNA (Mao 2019): an NRF2-independent component is possible and not excluded. Target engagement without benefit is also documented (photothrombosis, no loss arm).
4. **The pair is still not paired.** No source has a covalent reagent and a non-covalent binder in one NRF2-loss design in a neural class (Moretti 2021 pairs sulforaphane with a reversible binder in primary neurons and astrocytes without a loss arm in the abstract; its text is unread). A pair built across papers cannot be compared.
5. **Three claims kept apart.** (1) Acts through NRF2: shown for KI-696 in rat microglia at the molecular endpoint, for RA839's transcriptome in macrophages (loss unverified in print), and for sulforaphane's in vivo benefit in germline nulls (comparator level). (2) Useful in WWOX loss: nothing read has a WWOX-deficient neural preparation; INV-08 X0 is unmeasured. (3) Transfer to a therapeutic proposal: exposure, brain access and safety are not addressed by any source. Proof of (1) resolves neither (2) nor (3).
