## Quantities, fixed before any value (stock, net change, gross flux kept apart)
1. **Stock:** the matrix pyruvate level as the sensor reports it (needs calibration, pH and range).
2. **Net change:** the rate of change of the stock (accumulation after a pyruvate step; decline after the carrier is blocked).
3. **Gross flux:** import, and utilization (by PDH and by other routes), and efflux; none is observed directly by a fluorescence stock.
Logic fixed in advance: after a complete carrier block, import is zero and the decline of the stock is utilization plus efflux (net loss); with RESIDUAL import the decline underestimates utilization; with an unknown sensor affinity or pH drift the rate is not in concentration units. A depletion curve therefore constrains AGGREGATE net loss under blockade, only as well as (a) the completeness of the block is shown, (b) efflux is addressed, (c) the sensor is calibrated in situ, and (d) replicates are independent. Attribution of the loss to PDH needs a PDH-side perturbation (inhibitor, activation, knockdown) or an orthogonal flux measure (labelled pyruvate to CO2 or citrate), with the direction checked against active PDH. The signal under blockade alone cannot identify a PDH flux.

## Readings and what each predicts
* **G (gross import constrained):** the paper shows the import side independently constrained: an initial accumulation rate that goes to about zero under the blocker in the same cells (completeness of the block), an in-situ calibration or a pH-insensitive control, and a saturation range stated for the concentrations used.
* **U (aggregate utilization constrained):** a depletion rate after the block, with a respiratory perturbation that moves it in the expected direction, in independent replicates, with residual import and efflux bounded.
* **P (PDH-specific):** a PDH-side perturbation or an orthogonal PDH flux alongside. Expected absent.
* **R (repeats the limitation):** the depletion is shown only as a trace with respiratory perturbations (which change NADH, ATP and redox, not PDH alone), no residual-import bound, no efflux control.
