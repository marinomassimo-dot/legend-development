## 4. What it means for the candidate: the quotes are right; three proposed sentences say more or less than the paper
1. **First sentence.** "WWOX shows only a tendency to be lower (1.4-fold, p about 0.04, no correction named)" follows the Results text faithfully, but the paper is **internally inconsistent on the label**: the Abstract, the Discussion and the Conclusions say "significant downregulation", Fig 2A marks it with one asterisk, and only the Results paragraph says "tendency" (p = 0.0406, Mann-Whitney, uncorrected). The sentence should say both. "The ratio is lower **because of** the HIF1A change" is **not a statement of the source** (it presents the ratio as a better combined indicator and never apportions it); by the stated fold changes the HIF1A change is the larger (ln 3.8 = 1.34 against ln 1.4 = 0.34, the auditor's arithmetic), and the ratio also tracks WWOX (R = 0.61 in GDM, 0.65 in normal pregnancy, Figs 4 and 5, per the auditor): label it an inference.
2. **Second sentence, two errors.** (a) "WWOX itself is not correlated with ... the glycolytic genes in GDM" is wrong for PFK (R = -0.23, p < 0.05); HK2, PKM2 and SLC2A1 are right, and WWOX against NFKB1 is -0.22 and against CTNNB1 positive. (b) "those of a ratio with HIF1A in its denominator" is the dossier's inference, not the authors' (zero hits for "denominator"); it fits the ratio against HIF1A (R = -0.79, in normal pregnancy too, where the ratio does not correlate with the glycolytic genes) and is plausible, not shown, for the glycolytic genes (HIF1A correlates +0.40 to +0.51 with them).
3. **The sentence the candidate removes was partly right.** The paper does report weak correlations of the ratio with an inflammatory gene (NFKB1, -0.21) and Wnt genes (DVL2 -0.21, p = 0.05; CTNNB1 +0.38, p < 0.001), all uncorrected for multiplicity and at mRNA level; it reports **no** CRP association and **no** correlation of WWOX or the ratio with metabolic parameters in GDM beyond HbA1c. The replacement should keep them as weak correlations, with the CTNNB1 sign, rather than drop them.
4. **Not to be inherited:** the Abstract's statement that HK2 is overexpressed in GDM contradicts the Results (Fig 2E: unchanged).
5. **Multiple testing:** a Bonferroni threshold over eleven genes is about 0.0045 (the auditor's arithmetic): WWOX (0.0406) and SLC2A4 (0.0134) would not meet it; HIF1A and several others would. The paper does not apply it.
6. **Text against figure, in the paper itself:** HbA1c against LDHA in GDM, text and Table S1 -0.27 (p = 0.010), Fig 6E -0.22 (p = 0.036); HbA1c against HK2, text 0.35 (p = 0.004), Fig 6C p = 0.0003; two text-significant correlations with SLC2A4 carry no asterisk in Fig 4 (WWOX 0.17, HIF1A -0.19).

## 7. Correction (SCI, 2026-11-19, ADV-081; the text above is left as written)
Section 4 item 1 says "the Abstract, the Discussion and the Conclusions say 'significant downregulation'", and the restatement in section 5 says "the Abstract and Discussion a significant downregulation". **Only the Abstract says "significant downregulation of WWOX"**; the Discussion and the Conclusions say "downregulated" and "downregulation" with no significance label (the Discussion's "significant" belongs to the HIF1A upregulation). The second blind audit (22 propositions) caught it as an OVERSHOOT, and I confirmed it in the held text. Use: "the Results call it a tendency, the Abstract a significant downregulation, the Discussion and Conclusions a downregulation".
