## 4. Contradictions and narrowed statements (no edit made)

* **Contradiction (identity line), PAPER 532.** Statement: "Genotype/model: WWOX added (recombinant protein and overexpression), not lost; a gain-side measurement." Source: Supplementary Figure 3 title and caption, "WWOX knockdown reduced g-H2AX levels in SH-SY5Y cells ... siWWOX-treated cells compared to siControl-treated cells (Tukey's test, p=0.0044)", and Figure 6, an immunodepletion arm. The paper has WWOX-lowering arms; the dossier lists them, the record line does not.
* **Narrowing of a reason, DIS-028** (gamma-H2AX rejected as a WWOX readout because it rises both when WWOX is lost, in the fibroblast paper, and when added, in this preprint). Still true across papers; the preprint's own loss arm goes the other way in SH-SY5Y (about 10% lower), so the reason is "direction depends on the cell context", not "non-directional". The rejection is not reversed.
* **Narrowing, clobazam README** ("20 participants overall", baseline ratio needed): the ratios come from 10 treated and 3 placebo participants, and the baseline ratio is 6.8 against 3.4.
* **Narrowing, programme note on the NAD-brain comparison:** the +39.7% against +29.4% comparison between healthy and Parkinson groups carries a 17.8-year age difference (p 0.01).
* **Unchanged:** CORPUS-STUB-190 and CORPUS-STUB-193 role lines (both gain detail, neither is contradicted); PAPER 557 identity record; the ABAT and vigabatrin decision text.
