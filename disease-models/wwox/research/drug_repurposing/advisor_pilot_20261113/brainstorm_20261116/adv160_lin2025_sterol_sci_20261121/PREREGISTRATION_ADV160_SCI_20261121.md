## Question
In the authors' N208Y model, do the sterol transcription response and the measured tissue cholesterol or synthesis track THE SAME CONTRAST (same tissue, age window, timing, analytical unit)? Does the answer change what INV-06's lipid-synthesis comparator should measure?

## Two interpretations (fixed now)
* **T (tracking):** the sterol-pathway transcript shift (oligodendrocyte or whole-tissue) and a measured cholesterol, precursor or synthesis-rate readout move in the same direction in the same contrast (N208Y against WT and, within N208Y, withdrawn against maintained). Then a transcription readout is an adequate stand-in for the pool within this model, and INV-06's comparator can read sterol transcripts first.
* **D (dissociation):** the transcript shift is printed but the pool or synthesis is unmeasured, measured in another tissue or age, only in a different contrast (for example whole-brain bulk lipid against a cell-type snRNA response), or does not move. Then a transcript shift alone leaves the sterol-pool and myelin-function claims unresolved, and INV-06's comparator needs a pool or synthesis readout beside the transcript.

## Directional predictions (fixed now; each can fail)
* **P1 (85 percent):** cholesterol-biosynthesis transcripts are lower in N208Y than in WT in the oligodendrocyte-lineage cluster at the printed window.
* **P2 (50 percent):** the paper contains a measured pool or synthesis readout (cholesterol, a sterol intermediate, or a labelled synthesis rate) in tissue; (50 percent that it is transcript-only).
* **P3 (55 percent, conditional on P2):** the measured readout is lower in N208Y than WT in the same direction as the transcripts but not in the same unit or tissue (a bulk tissue value against a cell-type transcript), so the verdict is D (unit mismatch), not a clean T.
* **P4 (65 percent):** within N208Y, withdrawal of the activator shifts the sterol transcripts in the same direction as the genotype contrast, with a larger magnitude than the maintained arm.
* **P5 (70 percent):** the data support at most one clean within-model comparison, N208Y withdrawn against N208Y maintained (WT control present), not two.
**Simple alternative to my reading of the contrast:** a nonspecific consequence of illness (weight loss, delayed maturation) would lower the same transcripts, so a transcript shift cannot by itself identify a sterol-specific mechanism; any genotype-by-treatment result must beat that.
