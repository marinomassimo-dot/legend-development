## Answer first
1. **They track the same contrast for the chronic genotype contrast, and they do not for the acute withdrawal contrast in the CNS.** Within the 2BAct-maintained state (N208Y against WT, same drug, spinal cord and cerebellum): sterol transcripts are lower, free cholesterol is lower (about 0.81 spinal cord, 0.77 cerebellum) and the labelled de novo fraction is lower (about 0.76 and 0.77). Within N208Y, 2BAct withdrawal (2 to 3 days) lowers the CNS sterol transcripts a little further (z -0.13 cerebellum, -0.42 spinal cord) but free cholesterol (ratio 0.92 spinal cord, 1.02 cerebellum) and labelled synthesis (0.996 and 0.98) do not move. In the same animals' peripheral tissues (kidney, liver, spleen) withdrawal lowers the transcripts by about one z unit. In cultured MEFs, with a hours-scale window, transcripts and labelling both fall on washout (labelled fraction 0.171 to 0.127, n = 3).
2. **Reading (interpretation T for the chronic contrast, D for the acute one):** within this model a transcription readout is a valid directional stand-in for the chronic fall of the cholesterol pool; it is NOT a stand-in for its magnitude or for an acute change, because a transcript can move for days while a slow-turnover CNS pool (the authors' own explanation) and a 2-week label do not. A transcript shift alone, as the pre-registration said, leaves the pool and function claims unresolved; here the pool is measured and agrees for the chronic contrast only.
3. **What it changes for INV-06 (the lipid-synthesis comparator beside the muscarinic one):** it should read a measured pool (absolute free cholesterol by LC-MS with a labelled standard) and a labelled synthesis rate with a window matched to turnover, beside the sterol transcripts, in the same preparation; the transcript must be labelled a chronic-lesion directional readout, not a pool. Bridge to WWOX stays **CONDITIONAL** (see below) and the ONE differentiating observation is named.

## Cell-type layer (why the pool cannot be assigned to oligodendrocytes)
The pool and label are bulk tissue. The snRNA oligodendrocyte-lineage result (Fig. 6D) is a **cluster** contrast, MFOL-1 against MFOL-2, and the clusters are nearly genotype-defined: MFOL-1 is about 0 percent of WT oligodendrocyte-lineage nuclei, about 40 percent in 2BAct-maintained N208Y and about 78 percent after withdrawal, while MFOL-2 falls from about 66 percent to about 35 and about 2 percent and MOL from about 28 to about 20 and about 15 percent (Fig. 6B). A lower sterol gene set in MFOL-1 therefore mixes a within-cell change with a change in which state the cells are in; it is the composition confound already met in ADV-065c, not a within-cell-type contrast. Immunohistochemistry (Fig. 6H, spinal cord, 4-month males, n = 6): CC1-positive mature oligodendrocytes are not different from WT in the maintained state and fall only after withdrawal, while TPPP-positive cells fall already in the maintained state; so the maintained-state pool fall (about 20 percent) is not explained by loss of CC1-positive cells. This is a reading of printed asterisks and panels, not a recomputation.

## Predictions scored (pre-registered)
* P1 (oligodendrocyte-lineage sterol transcripts lower in N208Y): **held in direction**, with the caveat that the paper's contrast is MFOL-1 against MFOL-2 (a state contrast) and MOL, astrocytes and neurons in the withdrawn-against-WT-maintained comparison.
* P2 (measured pool or synthesis present): **held** (free cholesterol by LC-MS and 2H2O-labelled fraction, in CNS tissue and MEFs).
* P3 (measured readout lower in the same direction but not in the same unit or tissue, verdict D): **mixed**: direction and unit mismatch held; the verdict is T for the chronic contrast, D only for the acute one.
* P4 (withdrawal shifts the transcripts further than maintained, same direction): **held** for transcripts (small in the CNS: -0.13 and -0.42 z beyond -0.83 and -1.07).
* P5 (at most one clean within-model comparison): **held**: the withdrawn-against-maintained contrast within N208Y with WT controls; it is the contrast that separates transcript from pool.
Simple alternative (illness lowers the transcripts non-specifically): **not excluded**. N208Y mice are smaller (supplemental body-weight panel, 4-week males, n = 44 against 32), there is no weight-matched control for the cholesterol result, and withdrawal lowers kidney, liver and spleen transcripts as well; lung is unchanged (z +0.13 and +0.30), which argues against a uniform systemic effect, not against illness.

## Bridge to WWOX (CONDITIONAL)
Matching WWOX context: INV-06 (neuron-dependent hypomyelination, partly prevented by neuronal restoration; a lipid-synthesis lever signed only in injury or wild-type settings) and INV-02 (ISR sign at protein level in a WWOX-deficient human neural cell unmeasured; transcript-level evidence contradictory). Lin's chain starts from a locked eIF2B lesion with a measured ISR (ISR CLIC genes; ATF4 protein in MEFs); WWOX has no such measured ISR, so a sterol shift in a WWOX system could not be called ISR-dependent. **The ONE differentiating observation:** in the WWOX-deficient neuron and oligodendrocyte-lineage preparation, a measured absolute free-cholesterol pool and a labelled synthesis rate in oligodendrocyte-lineage cells, taken with ATF4 protein and eIF2-alpha phosphorylation in the same preparation, at a window at least as long as the cholesterol turnover and with a fixed-neuron or conditioned-medium arm (INV-06's existing arm); a sterol fall without an ISR rise points to a neuron-dependent or ISR-independent route, a fall with an ISR rise to the ISR-glial-maturation route.

## What INV-06 should now say (proposed wording for IT, not edited here)
"Adjacent evidence (Lin 2025, VWM mouse, full text read): chronic ISR lowers the CNS sterol transcripts, free cholesterol (about 20 percent) and labelled synthesis (about 24 percent) in the same genotype contrast; an acute withdrawal moves the transcripts but not the pool or the labelled fraction in the CNS; the oligodendrocyte-lineage transcript contrast is a state contrast. The comparator should therefore read a measured pool and a labelled synthesis rate beside the sterol transcripts, label the transcript a chronic directional readout, and carry the bridge as conditional until a WWOX preparation with a measured ISR exists."
