# R3 pre-registration: is the sign of the proposed WWOX-ERBB4 relation compatible with two documented WWOX-loss phenotypes? (SCI, 2026-11-19; frozen before any original is read for this question; hypothesis level; no dose; not medical advice)

Proposed relation (from step B; nothing established): WWOX WW1 holds the ERBB4 intracellular domain (ICD) in the cytoplasm (cancer-cell data, abstract level); loss of WWOX therefore predicts **more nuclear ICD**.
Two documented WWOX-loss phenotypes to test the sign against: (P1) **enhanced astrogenesis** in WWOX-knockout human cerebral organoids (one parental line); (P2) **fewer PV-positive interneurons** in the 2-week Wwox-null mouse hippocampus (one laboratory; maturation not controlled).
## What I expect before reading (stated so the reading can refute it)
My recollection, unverified, is that nuclear ERBB4 ICD signalling **restrains** the timing of astrogenesis. If the primary report shows that, the release hypothesis predicts **fewer** astrocytes and is **incompatible** with P1: I expect to drop the simple release hypothesis (moderate confidence). For P2 I have no prediction: ERBB4 loss of function in PV interneurons is known to lower inhibitory synapse number, and an ICD gain could act in either direction.
## Rules (fixed now)
* P1 verdict: COMPATIBLE if a primary report shows that nuclear ICD accumulation or gamma-secretase-dependent ICD signalling **promotes** astrocytic differentiation or astrocyte number; INCOMPATIBLE if it shows ICD signalling **represses** it; UNDETERMINED if the evidence is not ICD-specific (ligand, kinase or ErbB4 deletion only), too indirect, or from a preparation that cannot be transferred.
* P2 verdict: COMPATIBLE if a primary report shows that ERBB4 ICD signalling or ICD gain **reduces** PV-cell number, maturation or function; INCOMPATIBLE if it **increases** them; UNDETERMINED otherwise (including every report that studies kinase signalling or ERBB4 loss without isolating the ICD).
* Evidence rule: complete originals opened lawfully; species, preparation, cell, age, genotype and endpoint stated; "abstract only" marked; no transfer of a sign across species, age or cell type without saying so.
## Decision it changes
COMPATIBLE on both or UNDETERMINED: the distinct experiment of step B stays as the cheapest test and the open question stays. INCOMPATIBLE on P1 (and not contradicted by P2): the release hypothesis is dropped without a laboratory, the open-question row is re-worded to ask only whether WWOX binds ERBB4 in neurons, and no localisation test is motivated by these phenotypes. Stop: three primary originals per phenotype at most, then stop and report.
