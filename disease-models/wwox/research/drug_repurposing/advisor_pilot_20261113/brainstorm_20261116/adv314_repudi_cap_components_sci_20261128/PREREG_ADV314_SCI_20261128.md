## Readings
* **E-A (conduction change).** N1 comes later because the same recruited fibre population conducts more slowly.
* **E-B (recruitment or composition change).** The proportion or number of responding fibres differs (fewer fast fibres, more slow ones, or a different recruitment by the stimulus), which moves the apparent N1 peak or the N1/N2 balance without any one fibre having to conduct more slowly.
The two are not exclusive.

## Rule for each reading: ALLOWED / NOT ALLOWED / NOT PRINTED
* **E-A, tested on its premise "same recruited population".** ALLOWED only if the paper prints, for the same slices or groups, N1 area or amplitude and N2 area or amplitude (or an input-output curve) that do not differ, at a stated stimulus rule that is the same in both groups. NOT ALLOWED if N1 AREA (or an area ratio, or an input-output curve) differs. NOT PRINTED if neither can be decided from printed material. A difference of PEAK amplitude alone does not exclude E-A, because slowing with temporal dispersion lowers a peak without changing the number of fibres; it is recorded as "premise unsupported".
* **E-B.** ALLOWED if any printed measure of composition or recruitment differs between groups (N1/N2 ratio, absolute amplitude or area, input-output curve, counts of unmyelinated against myelinated axons in the same region) and nothing printed contradicts it. NOT ALLOWED only if N1 and N2 absolute area (or input-output curves) are printed and equal. NOT PRINTED if no such measure exists. ALLOWED is not SHOWN.
* **Latency to velocity.** A velocity or "conduction slowing" statement is supported only if latency is printed at two or more distances (or a stated distance with activation delay removed). One distance supports "longer peak latency" only.
