The investigation found that both Figs. 2 and 3 have key problems of different types of manipulations of the vinculin WB used as loading control. ## Side notes for the integrator (not verdicts)
1. One review sentence cites ref 58 for something the article does not show: "In human bone metastasis, Wwox localizes at cytosolic-perinuclear and at nuclear levels [41,57,58]". The article's Methods give Wwox immunostaining only on mouse xenograft bone (n = 5; Additional file 3 Fig S3: "We found Wwox in bone-marrow supportive cells") and E-cadherin (not Wwox) staining on the 3 human pairs. Searched: Methods ("The sections of specimens from carcinomas and from bone with metastases were immunostained with anti-E-cadherin (1:100) antibody") and the Fig 7 legend (E-cadherin IHC only). The packet repository sentences do not rely on this.
2. The retraction note says the results are "unreliable" for the whole study. Fig 6 (exogenous Wwox activates HIF-1 with Twist) is not one of the two figures named, but the note's conclusion covers the whole paper. The repository statements that keep this paper out of the WWOX/HIF1α support are consistent with the note.
3. Limits: the note's claim that Fig 2 vinculin bands were used in other publications could not be checked from held files; MOESM1/MOESM3 are image-only and were not read; article Figs 1, 4-7 not opened.
