## 2. What a reader of those sentences is missing (ADD, not FALSE)
- ADD-1 Notice facts absent from all four blocks: date 15 Aug 2020; Editor-in-Chief acting after a University of Milan investigation; "results ... unreliable"; "The authors do not agree to this retraction."
- ADD-2 Scope of the reason: Figs 2 and 3 only, vinculin loading-control bands only (Fig 2: reuse in other publications of the same groups; Fig 3: band rotation to clone bands). The notice does not examine the other figures (Figs 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, S1-S3), but it says the results of the study are unreliable as a whole. Figs 4A-C and 6A also use vinculin (legends), unnamed; I did not do image forensics and cannot say anything about them.
- ADD-3 The review's four citations of [58], each with what the article actually has (all cell-line, in vitro, retracted):
  (1) "In human bone metastasis, Wwox localizes at cytosolic-perinuclear and at nuclear levels [41,57,58]": article Fig 7 and Fig S3 are IHC only (xenograft nu/nu mice n=5 for Wwox, Methods; 3 patient pairs for E-cadherin, Fig 7 legend: "samples from three different patients"). The article stained patient specimens for E-cadherin, not Wwox; the Wwox IHC is mouse xenograft (Fig S3 legend: "5 mice").
  (2) "HGF controls a Twist program for transient mesenchymal-epithelial transition ... via E-cadherin expression and vimentin/MMP2 downregulation, and probably also for osteoclast activity [58]": article Fig 4A, 1833 cells, HGF 100 ng/ml: E-cadherin 3.3x (0.5 h), 3.8x (1 h), 2.5x, 2.3x, 1x (24 h); vimentin 0.1x from 6 h; active MMP2 0.3x (numbers printed under panel 4A). The osteoclast clause has no data in the article: "Twist1 expression also regulates the activity of the osteoclasts in bone metastasis [27]" is a literature citation (Discussion).
  (3) "HGF permits Twist access into the nuclei ... Wwox phosphorylated exchanges with Twist ... TGFbeta1 signaling is activated downstream HGF ... colonization associated with osteolysis [58]": Fig 1A/B (nuclear pTwist1/Twist 2x, 2.2x, 2.8x at 1/4/8 h; pWwox/Wwox 1.8x at 16 h HGF), Fig 3C-D (pAMPK/AMPK up to 3.5x; 3TPLux about 67 vs about 6 in panel D bars; blocked by Compound C). Fig 3 is a figure the notice names. The osteolysis link is a hypothesis in the Discussion ("might be important for colonization coupled to osteolysis [43]").
  (4) "Wwox influencing E-cadherin expression contributes to MET and colonization [58]": article Fig 4E: WWOX expression vector lowered, siRNAWWOX raised E-cadherin-promoter luciferase (reporter assay, not endogenous E-cadherin); Discussion: Wwox overexpression "preventing E-cadherin induction". The review sentence does not state the direction.
- ADD-4 What the article does not contain: no Met measurement and no TAZ measurement (TAZ appears only in a reference title, Met only in one sentence citing refs [2,25]); no decitabine; no Beclin-1/p62; no patient-derived WWOX variant; no neural cell.
- ADD-5 Window and dose: HGF 100 ng/ml, TGFbeta1 5 ng/ml after 24 h starvation (Methods); WWOX expression vector gave Wwox 49x (Fig 6A) and about 50-fold (Fig 2D, read from bar), so supraphysiological; endogenous Wwox in MDA-MB231 "extremely low" (Fig 4B/C text). Species: human cell lines; mouse only for xenograft IHC.
- ADD-6 Notice ref [3] is the Cell Death Dis 2017 article that the review cites as B64 (28151481 per review ref string): the notice itself links 26041563's vinculin bands to "other publications from the same groups [2-4]", one of which is the 28151481 article. It does not say which publication matches Fig 2. This supports the "cluster" idea only at that level.
- ADD-7 The review (published 1 Jan 2017) predates the 2020 notice; searched the review text for retract, erratum, correction: 0 hits. It says nothing of any integrity problem.
- ADD-8 Direction conflict inside the review (for the WWOX/HIF-1 sentence in the repository): article Fig 6B, HRELuc about 27 control to about 63 with WWOX e.v. (read from bars; HIF-1 activated); review abstract says "exogenous Wwox destabilizes HIF-1alpha" (attributed by the review to independent refs, e.g. B40; I did not read B40). The retracted article points the other way, and the repository should say so if it uses that axis.
