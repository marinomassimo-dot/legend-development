## Question (one)
Does the WWOX perturbation in PAPER 542 (Aragon-Ramirez et al., bioRxiv 2026, PMID 42523332), under its ACTUAL controls, support a narrower cellular-mechanism category than the catalog label "NOT WWOX EVIDENCE"?

## What counts as controlled (fixed now)
A perturbation is CONTROLLED only if the text shows all of: (1) WWOX depletion verified at protein or mRNA in the system used; (2) two or more independent siRNAs, or a knockout line; (3) a non-targeting or parental control read in the same experiment; (4) a stated biological unit for the lectin or Golgi readout (independent experiments or independent wells, not only cells or fields), with its n; (5) the readout quantified, not only a representative image. Rescue (re-expression of siRNA-resistant WWOX) or an independent replication in a second line or by a second method raises the category; absence of rescue is named, not penalised as a failure of (a).

## Dispositions and tests
* (a) DIRECT, CONTROLLED: conditions 1 to 5 all met for the global lectin or glycosylation contrast. Then the catalog scope changes from "not WWOX evidence" to "mechanistic cell biology of WWOX in a non-neural context", with the category stated as narrowly as the controls allow (for example "WWOX-dependent plasma-membrane lectin binding in RPE1, siRNA, n = X independent experiments").
* (b) PERTURBATION PRESENT, A CONTROL OR FIGURE MISSING: any of 1 to 5 not shown, or the figure or supplement panel cannot be retrieved by a plain route. Keep the ambiguity visible and name exactly what is missing; no re-grade.
* (c) INCIDENTAL: WWOX appears as a hit, a correlation or a background gene with no perturbation of WWOX in this paper. The label stands, with the locator.
