## Answer first
1. **The rejecting observation of the pre-registration is not met, and the "platform-low" lean needs a scope, not a reversal.** Repudi 2021 (same programme as the benchmark: not independent) prints the fraction of NeuN-positive cells that carry the transgene protein in three regions, in two separate cohorts, and the cerebellum is about 0.92 of the cortex in both (Fig 1E at P17, 51.7 against 55.9 percent; Fig 2G at P19, 56.5 against 61.5 percent; n = 3 mice, three sections each, error bars overlap). That is not at or above cortex (the rejecting observation) and not at or below 0.5 (the platform-low threshold); it is near parity, **on a different quantity** from the protein-level rows of the parent result.
2. **Lukashchuk 2016 does not qualify for a cerebellum-to-cortex ratio.** Every bar of Fig 5e to 5h is the GFP-positive cell count per mm2 **divided by the CMV-promoter average of the same region** (legend and Methods, verbatim in the section below). SYN1 reads 1.43 in cortex and 1.35 in cerebellum (each relative to CMV in its own region); the ratio of those two is 0.94, but it is a ratio of ratios and cannot be turned into a cerebellum-to-cortex ratio without the CMV cerebellum-to-cortex value, which the figure does not give. The text states a "uniform distribution of the GFP-positive neurons throughout the brain" for SYN1 (qualitative, images). The cerebellar cells counted are mainly Purkinje cells (text), a different population from the granule cell majority. Row recorded, not counted.
3. **What this does to the parent verdict.** The parent lean rested on protein-level and neuron-fraction rows read together (Voronin 2024 protein percent of wild type; McLean 2014 neuron fraction 29.6 against 43.5; the benchmark's own P30 and later lanes). The bars separate two quantities: **fraction of neurons expressing at P17 to P19 (Repudi: about 0.92 of cortex, at a dose below the benchmark's low dose)** against **regional protein as percent of wild type (benchmark P30: cerebellum 0.2 to 0.7 against cortex 3 to 13; Voronin: 0.24 to 0.77 by dose)**. A platform-reach statement is supported for the second and not for the first. The parent's design-brief line ("expect the cerebellum to be the lowest region") stays correct **for regional protein**; it must not be read as "fewer cerebellar neurons transduced", which the only per-neuron-fraction row of the programme contradicts at P17 to P19.
4. **Three rivals now stand for the gap between a near-parity neuron fraction and a low regional protein** (hypotheses, none measured here): (a) a lower per-cell level in cerebellar neurons (promoter strength or dose per cell); (b) loss between P19 and P30 in the cerebellum (postnatal granule cell proliferation dilutes episomal genomes; the benchmark's vector DNA reading of about a tenth at P30 and Hammond 2017's fall of cerebellar GFP-positive cells from 11.3 to 5.9 percent between 3 and 6 weeks, other stratum, agree in direction); (c) a denominator effect (percent of wild type is computed within each region; a high wild-type cerebellar WWOX abundance lowers the percent for the same transgene amount). Each has a different consequence for the pilot: (b) predicts an early cerebellar peak that a P10 to P20 reading would see, (c) predicts nothing at the transduction level.
