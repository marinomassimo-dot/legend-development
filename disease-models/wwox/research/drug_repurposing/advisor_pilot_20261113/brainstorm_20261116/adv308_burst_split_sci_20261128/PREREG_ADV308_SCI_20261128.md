## The split to test
In PMID 40561753 (rat embryonic cortical cultures, 4-aminopyridine, preincubation 1 h): etifoxine and stiripentol suppress the number of bursts (103.9 and 24.4 per cent of baseline; 4-AP alone about 380), XBD173 and PK11195 and Ro5-4864 do not (319, 201, 289; P = .85, .58, .51), although spikes and firing rate fall with etifoxine, PK11195 and (partly) XBD173.

## Two explanations
* **E-A:** GABA-A modulation carries the burst suppression; selective TSPO ligands lower spike counts through another route and leave burst structure.
* **E-B:** a TSPO-steroidogenic route is real but masked in the culture panel by timing (1 h preincubation) or concentration (3 to 5 microM nominal).

## Predicted observable in a second dataset (same preparation class: primary cortical or hippocampal culture or slice network under a convulsant, a burst endpoint, a GABA-A modulator arm and at least one selective TSPO ligand arm)
* E-A: the modulator suppresses bursts (at most 50 per cent of the convulsant level); selective TSPO ligands leave bursts above 50 per cent while spikes or rate fall.
* E-B: a steroidogenic selective ligand (XBD173) suppresses bursts (at most 50 per cent) at a longer preincubation or a higher concentration, PK11195 (non-steroidogenic) does not.

## Counterexample sought
A dataset in which a selective TSPO ligand suppresses bursts and a GABA-A modulator does not (breaks E-A), or in which etifoxine suppresses bursts only together with every TSPO ligand (breaks the split).

## What counts
One second dataset in the same preparation class with all three arms (modulator, selective TSPO ligand, vehicle or convulsant alone) and a burst endpoint counts as a replication test; a larval or whole-animal dataset does not (different preparation and endpoint).

## Prediction (mine, as sent to ADV)
The split does not replicate outside the culture panel. If no second dataset exists the label is **NOT TESTABLE FROM HELD SOURCES** with the specific missing observation, not a negative on the split.
