## Answer first
1. **Conduction was measured once, with internal inconsistencies, and the earlier figure "8 slices from 5 animals" was incomplete.** The legend of the callosal-evoked experiment (checked by me in the held text) reads "n = 8 slices, five subjects; S-Control; n = 8 slices, six subjects S-KO": **8 slices from 5 animals for controls and 8 slices from 6 animals for the knockout**, with no slices-per-animal figure. The control n is identical to the S-HT group of a different experiment (Fig 2F, where S-Control is 6 slices from 3 animals), which may be a copied n; nothing in the originals decides it.
2. **Effect (graph-read, approximate):** first-peak (N1) latency about 2.5 ms in controls (range 1.4 to 2.9) against about 3.2 ms in the knockout (Wilcoxon P = 0.0104; a shift of about 0.7 ms, 28 percent); second-peak (N2) latency not different (P = 0.5737); N1/N2 amplitude ratio about 2.75 (range 0.95 to 5.65) against about 0.85 (P = 0.0030). **Stimulus in corpus callosum, recording in layer V at a vertical distance of 250 micrometres; acute slices P13 to P17; Synapsin-Cre neuron-targeted deletion only; never in the Nestin-Cre line or the germline null; never in vivo; no velocity; no rescue.**
3. **Further inconsistencies the delegate found (not resolved by the originals):** the text says "significantly lower peak amplitudes" but Fig 5D has no callosal amplitude panel; the methods describe layer V or dentate stimulation only (no callosal protocol); the methods name a t test and the figure uses Wilcoxon; the text says 6-fold more unmyelinated optic-nerve axons and the graph shows about 4-fold; one supplementary legend has the two group values reversed; the EM counts are per field of view (about 12 fields per group), so fields are treated as independent.
4. **Structural myelin endpoints are better supported than the functional one.** Electron microscopy of corpus callosum: myelinated axons per field 180 +/- 40 against 55 +/- 35 (3.5 to 4-fold fewer); optic nerve unmyelinated axons 55 +/- 20 against 270 +/- 60; the Nestin-Cre line shows the same direction (148 +/- 45 against 45 +/- 26 myelinated); immunofluorescence (3 animals per group, 9 points) for CNP, MBP and CC1 lower in the knockout. **Rescue is shown for the structural measures** by neonatal neuronal AAV9 re-expression (myelinated-axon counts, g-ratio with some residual difference, MBP and CC1; SD, 3 animals per group) and qualitatively for MBP in a dose series (images only). **No conduction measure was ever repeated after rescue.**
5. **Endpoint suitability as the originals allow:** the EM myelinated-axon count and MBP/CC1 are large effects with demonstrated rescue but need animal-level units, fields per animal and a blinding statement; callosal N1 latency and the N1/N2 ratio are **candidate** functional readouts with a modest, overlapping shift, an undefined N1/N2 and electrode geometry, no stated spread, no rescue, one laboratory and one age window, so **conduction is not ready to be an endpoint without a re-measurement in the same preparation, with a rescue arm and animal-level statistics.**
6. **Per the stop rule given to ADV:** conduction closes as "**measured once, with inconsistent n and no rescue; no lever from these papers; an endpoint note**"; the structural endpoints are the usable ones for the myelin cards and the pup brief; a conduction re-measurement is a laboratory item.
