## The check on "8 slices from five subjects"
The exact legend text (Fig 5D, both held copies): "Wilcoxon rank sum n = 8 slices, five subjects; S-Control; n = 8 slices, six subjects S-KO." It gives total slices and total subjects per genotype and no slices per animal, so the number of slices per animal and whether the unit is slice or animal is unknown. The same pair "8 slices, five subjects" appears in the Fig 2F legend for the **S-HT** group (S-Control there is 6 slices, three subjects; S-KO 8 slices, six subjects). So the S-Control wording in Fig 5D equals the Fig 2F S-HT group; it may be a copy of the wrong group's n or a coincidence. Nothing in the originals decides which. The Fig 5D graph shows about 8 control dots, consistent with the legend but not discriminating. Also: five subjects contribute 8 slices (at least some animals contribute two or more slices), and the test treats slices as independent.

## What the originals do not show
* No rescue of conduction (N1, N2 latency, ratio) in any held paper; conduction was not measured in any treated or rescued animal.
* No callosal conduction velocity (stimulus-to-recording distance along the callosum not given; latency from stimulus onset at a 250 um vertical offset).
* Single laboratory authorship cluster (Aqeilan lab with collaborators); one preparation, acute slices P13-P17, no in vivo recording.
* n = 3 animals per genotype for EM and IF with section, FOV or axon units (pseudoreplication); conduction n = 8 slices from 5-6 animals; slices per animal unstated.
* Only a neuron-targeted conditional line (S-KO) has conduction; N-KO and germline-null conduction untested.
* Rescue papers: no figure images for 34747138 (values not readable), 42422765 gives dose-dependence only as images without n.
* Text states "significantly lower peak amplitudes" without a callosal amplitude panel.
* lde rat full text and Hussain 2019 myelin content: nothing usable.

## Endpoint suitability as the originals allow
* **Callosal latency N1.** Group difference about 0.7 ms on a control of about 2.5 ms (about 28 percent), P = 0.0104 with 8 slices per group, overlapping individual values; N2 latency not different (P = 0.5737); the N1/N2 ratio differs more clearly (about 2.75 vs 0.85, P = 0.0030) but its control spread is wide (0.95-5.65). Read at the level of the figure, a small experiment could in principle see the N1/N2 ratio or N1 latency, but what is missing: a stated SD, slices per animal, exact N1/N2 definition and electrode geometry, any velocity, any measurement in a rescued animal, any age other than P13-P17, and any replication elsewhere. Depth: full text with Fig 5 viewed.
* **EM myelinated-axon count per FOV.** Largest and best-resolved effect (about 3.5-4 fold; control 180 +/- 40 versus 55 +/- 35), rescued in 34747138 (corpus callosum, P17 and 6 months) by neuronal AAV9-hSynI-mWwox. Needs: FOV per animal, animal-level unit, SD, blinding statement for EM counts (blinding is stated only for IF), common FOV placement. Depth: full text with Fig 5B viewed (Brain); text only for 34747138.
* **g-ratio.** Group difference significant, but no mean g-ratio is stated and the scatter shows modest separation; 100 axons per animal with axons as units; rescue reported as improved but with residual differences from WT. Suitable only if means with animal-level spread are re-extracted from raw EM. Depth: full text with Fig 5C viewed.
* **Unmyelinated axon count (optic nerve), MBP, CC1/PDGFRa.** Large, tight effects (about 2-6 fold; control MBP/CNP spread about 5-10 percent); rescued for MBP and CC1 in two rescue papers; but these are histological, not functional.
* Net: the originals support myelin structure measures (EM counts, MBP, CC1) as readable endpoints with demonstrated rescue (neonatal neuronal AAV9), and callosal N1/N2 as a candidate functional readout with a significant difference at P13-P17 but no rescue, no stated spread, and a legend-n inconsistency that has to be resolved before use. Hypothesis level only.
