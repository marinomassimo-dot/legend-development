## Answer first
1. **No signature, in the primary set or in the three descriptive ones.** None of the four sets passes the pre-registered criteria in either knockout cluster. For the primary set P53_RESP (14 genes, none missing from the table) the raw cluster shifts are -0.12 (cko1 + cko2) and -0.02 (cko3 + cko4) log2; after the proliferation adjustment N1 they are -0.40 and +0.12, both inside the 2.5th to 97.5th percentile band of 2000 expression-matched random gene sets (-0.49 to -0.04 and -0.04 to +0.53), and both under or near the 0.3 floor and the wild-type noise floor (0.16). The genotype term after maturity (N2) is -0.23, below the 0.3 criterion, with its sign stable when any one library is left out (-0.28 to -0.17). The pre-registration named S1 (one cluster only) and S2 (lost after normalisation); here **neither cluster shows a signal to start with**, a case the pre-registration did not name ("absent"), handled by the same stop logic.
2. **What is there, descriptively.** MDM2 is lower in all four knockout libraries (-0.32 to -0.54 log2 against the wild-type mean), which reproduces the authors' own statement (retrospective element, declared). The rest of the p53-response set is mixed: CDKN1A (+0.54 on average, one library -0.39), TNFRSF10B, FAS and SESN1 up; BAX, AEN, RRM2B, ZMAT3 and GADD45A down. There is no coherent direction at set level. Among the cell-cycle brakes, CDKN1C and BTG1 are up and RB1 down, which fits maturation or composition as well as it fits a checkpoint story. The sub-threshold negative genotype term for P53_RESP (-0.23) points the way of an attenuated p53 response (the authors' "loss of checkpoint inhibition") but it does not meet the rule and cannot be told from noise at this n.
3. **Detection limit.** With 2 wild-type against 4 knockout libraries the matched-random-set null bands are 0.45 to 0.86 log2 wide (for example -0.49 to -0.04 for the primary set in the first cluster), so only a set-level shift clearly outside such a band, of the order of half a log2 unit, could have been called: "absent" means **no large transcript-level DNA-damage signature at week 15**, not "no change".
4. **Decision it changes.** No DNA-damage-directed class enters the comparison set on this evidence (it did not before either). The row stays a **one-origin imaging observation** (foci at week 6 and week 10 in one parental line, one laboratory, with a rescue arm and WOREE-derived organoids; none in the SCAR12 forebrain organoids) **without a transcript-level echo** at week 15 beyond MDM2; the question is closed for tonight at a reasoned stop.

## 3. Alternatives that remain, and what the step cannot show

A signal would not have made a DNA-damage-directed agent a candidate.
