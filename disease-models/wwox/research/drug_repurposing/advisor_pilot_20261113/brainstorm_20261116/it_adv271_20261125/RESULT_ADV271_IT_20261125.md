## 2. Against the fly and the organoid (comparator conditions named)
* **Fly (PMID 42909904; Atf4 signal, n 6, amyloid-conditional, loss of WWOX goes with higher ISR output)** is a steady-state signal in a tissue with an added proteotoxic burden. The PEO1 pair has **no steady-state difference at all** for the one ISR phosphorylation it carries (no p-eIF2A band visible at 0 h in either line, Fig 4a and 4b) and shows differences only after a drug stress. In the one stress window where null is higher (early tunicamycin, 4-8 h, ratio 0.7 add-back/null, one clone each) the effect is within the clone scatter. **Agreement with the fly: none that survives the scatter.**
* **Organoid transcripts (knockout lower, target transcripts four libraries against two)**: GRP78 (visual), the late and sustained phase of p-eIF2A under tunicamycin, and the thapsigargin p-IRE-1 all run with null LOWER; the paclitaxel p-eIF2A and p-IRE-1 readings do not. **Direction agrees with the organoid in the readouts that exceed the scatter, in a different species-state (ovarian tumour line, drug stress, protein, not transcript), and in single clones.**
* The two systems cannot be reconciled by this pair because **neither measured the same thing**: fly and organoid are baseline, the pair is stress-induced. The pair does carry something new for R-10: in a human line, WWOX presence changes the **kinetics** of the eIF2 alpha phosphorylation under ER stress (earlier and shorter without WWOX, later and more sustained with it, one time course each) rather than its level at rest, which is a reading none of the other systems could give.

## 4. Limits and flags
* All values are **pixel estimates from published JPEG blots** (and one PDF-embedded image), not densitometry of raw films: saturation and gamma are unknown, WWOX-8 and Vector-9 time courses are separate gels (exposure may differ; the Hsp60 strip of each gel partly corrects it), and lane windows are equal divisions of each frame. Ratios are for orientation; nothing here is a measured fold change. Scripts and outputs are in this folder (`adv271_*.py`, `densit_output.txt`).
* One clone per line in the time courses, no statistics printed for any blot, independent experiments not stated for Fig 4a/4b and Supp Fig 6; the survival panels have n 3 and 4.
* Methods text names no ISR readout beyond p-eIF2A and GRP78 (no ATF4 or CHOP blot); the two supplements held (PDF with Supp Figs 1-9, DOCX legends) were searched by text and by page for ATF4, CHOP and eIF2: none other than the items above.
* Rejecting observations were fixed before the figures were opened: the fly reading would have been supported by null above add-back in a stated, quantified comparison; it was not. The organoid reading would have been rejected by null above add-back; two readings (early tunicamycin p-eIF2A, paclitaxel p-IRE-1 in one blot) go the other way within the scatter, so it is **not** claimed as confirmed.
