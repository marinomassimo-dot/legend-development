# Amendment to the 1L/1M pre-registration (IT, 2026-11-25), written after viewing the strips and before computing any ratio

**What was seen first.** The rendered pages 6 and 7 of File012, panels K to M of File010 and the two WWOX strips enlarged. No window sum, profile or ratio had been computed.

1. **The registered anchor rule fails on these strips.** Both WWOX strips carry a strong band near the top of the image, far above the 46 kDa position (more than 1.5 D away), absent from the published composite. Section 2.4 would take it as y0 because it is the lane maximum.
2. **Replacement anchor, mechanical:** y0 = the row of the largest value of the wild-type lane's row-mean darkness profile within the rows from 0.30 to 0.80 of the strip height. This is the band beside the right-hand "WWOX (46 kDa)" label, the band the composite shows; on both membranes the handwritten "50" marks the dark marker band beside it.
3. V1, V2, D, h, background, HSP90, noise, saturation, comparator, decision rule and predictions are unchanged. D = 18 rows on both strips; h = 8 rows (6 x strip pixels per point / 1.946, rounded: 7.8 and 7.5). Background rows 20 to 50 scaled by the same factor.
4. Lane centres are taken from the HSP90 strip and mapped to the WWOX strip through the page coordinates of the two images (their widths in pixels differ).
5. **Lanes without a printed value** (1M original has seven lanes; the composite shows five): WPM D1 AAV9 and WPM D3 AAV9 are measured and reported as outside the decision rule. 1L: WSM S2 and WSM S5 have no printed value, same treatment.
