## 3. What this means for the comparison (for SCI to take or leave)
* The catalogue cannot arbitrate between mechanism classes: it holds identities and target annotations whose direction is unresolved because the needs' directions are. The discriminating work is the premise screen and exposure mapping ADV's note names, not a catalogue ranking.
* For **INV-02** the canonical eIF2B-activator route (ISRIB-class) is not in the catalogue (identity absent), while the PERK-inhibitor arm is; the INV-02 premise (does the ISR run high or low in WWOX-deficient human neural cells) decides which of the two directions is even relevant.
* For **INV-06 and INV-12** the catalogue holds the standard OPC-differentiation and thyroid-receptor compounds as identities; no row ties them to a WWOX observation, and the INV-06 question (is an axonal signal left) precedes any compound.
* **INV-13**: the unit set already carries the chaperone, HSP70 and proteasome routes; forigerimod is held as a clinical-candidate identity with a `not_evaluable` direction.
* **INV-18**: the Ih tool arm needs two chemotypes; the catalogue holds three (ivabradine, ZD7288, the zatebradine and cilobradine pair).
* Nothing here is a statement about brain exposure, paediatric use, labels or doses; those are per-agent cells for the exposure table, not catalogue fields.
