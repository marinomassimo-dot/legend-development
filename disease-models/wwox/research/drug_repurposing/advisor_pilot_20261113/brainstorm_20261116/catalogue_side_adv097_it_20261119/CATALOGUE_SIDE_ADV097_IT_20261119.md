Hypothesis-level research support for SCI's comparative work. Reference genotype class only; no dose, schedule or recommendation for any agent in any person; not medical advice. ## 1. Answer first
1. **The ranking is not empty for the premise-stage threads; it carries the class, not the compounds.** Units exist for INV-02 (U12 ISR modulators, class), INV-06 (U22 pro-myelinating agents, class), INV-07 (U14 dichloroacetate and PDK inhibition), INV-08 (U59 NRF2 axis with a genetic arm and omaveloxolone), INV-17 (U63 MYC state), INV-13 (U01 4-phenylbutyrate and chaperones, U05 HSP70 axis), and a neighbouring U21 for d-APV. No unit carries INV-12 (thyroid-hormone receptor agonists), INV-14 (EZH2, only a tool compound GSK343 in the probe list), INV-16 (class IIa HDAC) or INV-18 (Ih block). For the broad PHARMACOLOGICAL objective the field is therefore a set of **mechanism classes with unresolved premise**, not a set of compounds with an effect.
2. **The catalogue's compound links to these needs are almost all `direction_unresolved`.** Each need is `uncertain` in direction, so `repurposing_search.py need` returns a labelled EXPLORATION list (e.g. NEED-ISR-ATTENUATION: 78 rows over 6 gene symbols, of which DDIT3 alone has 46 rows made of generic DGIdb gene listings such as platinum, vitamin E, paclitaxel; EIF2B2 to EIF2B5 have no catalogue target row at all). Catalogue presence is identity and target annotation, **not** evidence of action in WWOX-deficient cells.
3. **Access and status flags are thin:** most records say `unknown/unverified` for access; a status class (approved from any source, clinical candidate, no status statement) is a catalogue label, not a label review, and not a statement about children, brain exposure or use in this condition.
4. **Credible alternatives the ranking text does not carry** (count of the name in `ranking.json` is zero; catalogue presence in section 2): PERK-pathway tool compounds and trazodone (ISR); the OPC-differentiation screen compounds clemastine, benztropine, miconazole, clobetasol and the RXR agonist bexarotene (myelination); the TR-beta-selective agonists sobetirome and eprotirome beside tiratricol (INV-12); a PDK tool compound AZD7545 and the PDH cofactors thiamine and lipoic acid (INV-07); sulforaphane and bardoxolone beside the two fumarates and omaveloxolone (INV-08); arimoclomol and ambroxol (proteostasis, INV-13); the EZH2 inhibitors tazemetostat, GSK126 and valemetostat beside GSK343 (INV-14); the class IIa tool compound TMP269 (INV-16); zatebradine, cilobradine and ZD7288 beside ivabradine (INV-18). **These names are mine, written from general pharmacology knowledge before the lookups, not from a source read in this window**; the lookup tests only whether the catalogue holds them.

## 3. What this means for the comparison (for SCI to take or leave)
* The catalogue cannot arbitrate between mechanism classes: it holds identities and target annotations whose direction is unresolved because the needs' directions are. The discriminating work is the premise screen and exposure mapping ADV's note names, not a catalogue ranking.
* For **INV-02** the canonical eIF2B-activator route (ISRIB-class) is not in the catalogue (identity absent), while the PERK-inhibitor arm is; the INV-02 premise (does the ISR run high or low in WWOX-deficient human neural cells) decides which of the two directions is even relevant.
* For **INV-06 and INV-12** the catalogue holds the standard OPC-differentiation and thyroid-receptor compounds as identities; no row ties them to a WWOX observation, and the INV-06 question (is an axonal signal left) precedes any compound.
* **INV-13**: the unit set already carries the chaperone, HSP70 and proteasome routes; forigerimod is held as a clinical-candidate identity with a `not_evaluable` direction.
* **INV-18**: the Ih tool arm needs two chemotypes; the catalogue holds three (ivabradine, ZD7288, the zatebradine and cilobradine pair).
* Nothing here is a statement about brain exposure, paediatric use, labels or doses; those are per-agent cells for the exposure table, not catalogue fields.
