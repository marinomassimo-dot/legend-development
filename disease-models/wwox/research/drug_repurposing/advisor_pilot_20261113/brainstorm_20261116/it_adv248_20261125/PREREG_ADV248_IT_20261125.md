## 1. Question
Is the week-7 plateau among altered lines (ADV-246) a biological floor (any loss below the WT band intensity gives the same hyperexcitability) or an **assay ceiling** (the readout cannot rise further)? A ceiling would make a graded protein question unanswerable by this assay; a floor would not.

## 2. Two kinds of ceiling (kept apart)
* **Hard cap**: values pile at one number set by the method (spike-detection or recording limits), visible as equal maxima or a flat top across lines.
* **Soft saturation**: single neurons can exceed the plateau, but the group median cannot move above it under any manipulation in the assay. Only a held graded or higher-activity condition in the same assay can address it.

## 3. Evidence taken (frozen)
E1. Fig 1D read-off per line (tops, whiskers, spread of the top points): hard-cap signature.
E2. Every held cell-attached dataset of week-7 organoid neurons outside Fig 1D (Steinberg 2021 Fig 5C and its appendix panels; any table in the 2026 supplement): the range of group values, and whether any condition exceeds the plateau band.
E3. The method descriptions: any documented bound on the rate (sampling, threshold, recording length), and the selection step (blind patch, spikes as the criterion to start), which is a selection issue and is recorded separately from a ceiling.
Absence claims (no cap, no higher dataset) need two routes: a figure/legend read plus a text or table search of the held files, and a second held paper or registry lookup.

## 4. Dispositions (frozen)
* **HARD_CAP_SUPPORTED**: maxima equal within the read-off resolution (about 5 spikes) in at least three lines, or a method sentence that bounds the rate at or near the plateau.
* **NO_HARD_CAP**: maxima differ and at least one line's top points reach 1.5 times the highest plateau median (about 600) or more.
* **SOFT_SATURATION_EXCLUDED**: a held week-7 cell-attached group value (any line or arm) of at least 1.5 times the plateau median (about 550 spikes per neuron per 4 min, or the same in Hz), not an individual neuron.
* **SOFT_SATURATION_NOT_EXCLUDED**: otherwise.
The result states the hard-cap and soft-saturation dispositions separately.
