# ADV-248 result: does the week-7 cell-attached assay saturate? (IT, 2026-11-25)

Actor `orchestrator` (IT). Hypothesis level; reference genotype class; nothing is medical advice. Held files only, no spend, no contact, no delegate. Pre-registration: `PREREG_ADV248_IT_20261125.md` (commit beea4a9db, before the 2021 Fig 5C reading and the repository search).

## 1. Answer

1. **Hard cap: NO_HARD_CAP.** The per-line maxima in Fig 1D differ (read off in ADV-246: WWOX-KO 895, WPM S 718, WSM S 507, WPM D 474, LM-iPS 402 spikes per neuron per 4 minutes) and two lines have neurons above 1.5 times the highest plateau median (397; threshold about 600). The methods name no bound on the rate (blind patch with spikes appearing as the criterion to record; high-pass filter; threshold "well above" noise; a 4-minute average per cell).
2. **Soft saturation: NOT_EXCLUDED.** No held week-7 cell-attached group value reaches 1.5 times the plateau median (about 550). The highest is the 2021 WSM S group, 1.67 Hz = about 400 spikes per 4 minutes, which is the plateau again.
3. **What the second assay does show.** In the same assay (Steinberg 2021, Fig 5C, read off the 712-pixel figure, axis 0 to 2 Hz): WSM P (parental) about 0.42 Hz (about 101 per 4 minutes), WSM S about 1.67 Hz (about 401), WSM S with AAV-WWOX about 0.45 Hz (about 108). So (a) the 2021 group means reproduce the 2026 Fig 1D scale on the same line: WSM S median 382 against 401 mean, parental 101 against WT about 95; (b) the assay resolves a roughly fourfold fall back to the parental level when WWOX is re-expressed: the **low end of the range is fully resolved, in both directions of the rescue contrast**; only the top end is unprobed.
4. **So a ceiling cannot be told from a floor, and the ADV-246 verdict stays "not testable".** What would decide it: a held or new week-7 condition in this assay that raises firing above the altered-line plateau (a graded manipulation or a positive control), or a graded WWOX re-expression series. None is held.
5. **Independence of the two datasets is not shown.** The 2021 WSM S group (41 neurons, 3 organoids) and the 2026 Fig 1D WSM S group (24 neurons, "one experiment") may overlap; I found no statement either way, so the agreement is reported as consistent, not as a replication.

## 4. Limits

* Fig 5C values are read from a 712-pixel image to about plus or minus 0.03 Hz; Fig 1D from pixels as in ADV-246; unit conversion assumes the rate was averaged over the full 4-minute recording, as the methods state.
* Mean (2021) against median (2026) are not the same statistic.
* Both papers come from one group and one pipeline; the second assay is not independent of the first.
* A different assay (week-16 calcium imaging, 6A(ii) of 2026) separates the altered groups (events rate about 0.075 against 0.26 Hz) and returns to about zero with AAV9-WWOX; it says nothing about the week-7 ceiling because the assay, age and unit differ.

## 5. DREAM (not consumed; a prediction, not a finding)

At week 16, the blot of the rescue arms prints 0.4, 0.4 and 0.9 (WOREE, blot 1L) and 7 (SCAR12, blot 1M) while the events rate of those arms is about that of wild type; the altered arms print at most 0.3 and fire at 0.075 to 0.26 Hz. Read across two blots, that is a step between 0.3 and 0.4 of the wild-type band on the authors' own scale, not a gradient. It is cross-membrane, cross-line, group-level and rescue timing differs (infection at week 10), so it is only a prediction: **below about a third of wild-type band intensity activity is not normalised; above about 0.4 it is**. A decisive observation would be one preparation with an intermediate WWOX level measured on the same blot as its activity. Not tested; the RP-045 forecast is untouched.
