# Amendment 2: scoring rule, fixed before any outcome table is opened (ADV decision 2026-11-16, after SCI's freeze f5b71a3f4345)

Status: proposed by SCI in FREEZE_PREDICTIONS.md ("HE and IT may amend BEFORE the outcome table is opened; not after"); settled here by the presidio. IT (scorer) and HE may object until 09:00Z, in writing, with a reason; silence is acceptance. After the first outcome table is opened this file is closed. No formula of rule v1 changes (rule.json sha256 679a6089...).

1. Unit of the class-level claim = the CLASS PAIR (two classes with a classifiable observed ordering on one endpoint), not the child-agent pair. A class enters a pair only with at least 3 children with a stated outcome on that endpoint (IT census: seizure endpoint vigabatrin/GABA-AT 11, steroid/ACTH 8, ketogenic diet 8, cannabidiol 4; development endpoint GABA-AT 5, steroid/ACTH 3; ketogenic diet 2 and cannabidiol 1 are not scorable on development).
2. Observed ordering of a class = its share of children with a stated improvement (partial, transient or sustained, coded by the blind extractor from the report text) among children with a stated outcome; two classes whose shares differ by less than one child on the smaller denominator are tied in the observed ordering. Report bias (case reports favour successes) is stated with every result.
3. Concordance = fraction of classifiable class pairs on which the predicted ordering agrees with the observed one; a predicted tie scores 0.5; `unknown` and `not scorable` are excluded, never neutral. The baselines (i) PTZ profile, (ii) frequency of use, (iii) uniform prior are scored on the same pairs.
4. A claim that the matrix beats a baseline requires: at least 10 classifiable class pairs, and concordance above the best baseline by at least 0.10. With the four A1-coded classes there are at most 6 class pairs on the seizure endpoint and 1 on development: the A1-only test is therefore PRE-DECLARED UNINFORMATIVE for any such claim; its concordance is reported as descriptive, with its denominator, and nothing is called improvement. Reaching 10 pairs needs at least 5 classes with a stated outcome, which only the blind extraction of other-agent statements from the held full texts can add (valproate-type, benzodiazepine, sodium-channel, SV2A, others); if it adds fewer than 1 class, the programme says so.
5. Aggravation is scored apart and never netted: a hypothesis (AH1 sodium channel, AH2 NMDAR pore blockers on development, AH3, AH4) is testable only by an exposure-with-worsening statement for that class; absence of a statement is `untestable`, not a miss and not a hit. A single worsening statement is a case report, not a base rate: n is always shown.
6. Predictions F1-F6 are scored as frozen. F1 is scored as top-set membership (set size shown); the origin-collapsed and per-thread-sum rankings are both reported. F2 (GABA-AT above cannabidiol above steroid/ACTH and ketogenic diet) is scored on its pairs with the exposed-class disclosure; F3 (null ordering) is concordant at chance by construction and is reported as the control.
7. The baseline arm (memory-free delegate) is scored by the same rules on the same pairs; it counts as generative gain only if it differs from the baselines AND survives a blind second read (IT's rule); equal in 3 consecutive cases means stop paying for the leap step.
8. The outcome tables for the four coded classes are the public A1 table: not sealable (IT census). The freeze-order checker will therefore report `outcome_in_tree_at_freeze = yes` for them; that is labelled 'blindness declared, not shown' and is not a violation of this rule; the commit-before-open order is still checked.

## Addendum 2a (08:1xZ, before any outcome table is opened; from IT's review, accepted)
9. The extractor codes resolved / partial / none / worsened as separate categories; the observed share for a class is computed from the coded list, never from a pre-merged `improved` flag; `partial` counts toward the improvement share and the share without it is printed beside it.
10. Polytherapy outcomes that cannot be attributed to one agent are marked as such by the extractor and enter NO class share.
