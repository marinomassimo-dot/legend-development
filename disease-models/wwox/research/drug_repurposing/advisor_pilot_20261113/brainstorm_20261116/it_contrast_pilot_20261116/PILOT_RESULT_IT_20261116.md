# Contrast-set pilot: result (IT; the engine is measured, not the biology; n = 3 questions, nothing is claimed; non-canonical; hypotheses; mechanism level; not medical advice)
Design: it_dream3_20261116/CONTRAST_SET_PILOT_DESIGN_IT_20261116.md (stated before the run). Blind builder (legend-delegate, sonnet, effort high read back x4: output 39,358 tokens, total 63,495, 5 minutes) built contrast sets for three questions from 12 quoted observations only (OBSERVATIONS_BUNDLE.md); blind second reader (sonnet, effort high x3: output 7,979, total 33,098, 1 minute) assigned the 156 cells (3 questions x alternatives x 12 observations) from the observations and the alternative statements only, without the builder's cells or reasons. Files sealed by digest (SEAL.sha256). Pilot sums: output 47,337, total 96,593 tokens.
**What the engine had to beat (fixed before the run).** (a) residual alternatives after the held results: builder Q1 residual {A1 phasic, A2 intrinsic, A0 none}, reader Q1 residual {A1, A2, A3 tonic, A0}; the day's SUPPORT/REFUTE path carried three graded candidates (phasic, tonic, chloride) as live until SCI's rivals scan, so the contrast set is not worse and not clearly better (3 versus 3 to 4). Q2 stays undetermined (one timing observation, no time constant: 3 alternatives live in both readings). Q3: the builder opposes the THIP-suppression frame because wild type has 0 of 11 bursting slices; the reader scored it silent: one of four frames disputed. (b) first experiment: builder Q1: in the same slices record bursts then sIPSC and intrinsic profile (rebound), comparing bursting with non-bursting heterozygote slices: this is the heterozygote sIPSC measurement that SCI's rivals scan pointed to; the day's actual first choice for Q1 was the tonic-current test (I13 killer test), then the S-HT sIPSC. Q2: sample evoked NMDAR efficacy, evoked IPSC and intrinsic properties at fixed lags after a burst (the day had no such design). Q3: one ladder per slice, balanced order, long window per step, four scores (the same elements as the I15 second read). (c) inter-reader agreement: threshold fixed before the second reader started: at least 80 percent of cells. Result: 148 of 156 cells (94.9 percent; Q1 97.9, Q2 97.9, Q3 90.0), so the pre-fixed threshold is met; but 140 of the builder's and 148 of the reader's cells are silent, so on the 16 cells that are non-silent in either reading the agreement is 8 of 16 (50 percent): the readers agree on 5 of the builder's 6 supports cells in Q1 and Q2 (A1 on O4 and O6, A2 on O5 and O11, B1 on O7; the builder's B3 on O11 was silent for the reader) and on 3 of its 8 supports cells in Q3 (C2 on O7, C3 on O6, C4 on O8); the reader scored no cell as opposes, so the builder's two opposes cells (the tonic alternative against the minute wild-type tonic current; the THIP frame against a wild type with nothing to suppress) are not shared. Verdict as pre-declared for n = 3: nothing claimed; cost 96.6k tokens; disagreement concentrated where a cell needs a fact outside the quotes (8 of the 10 alternatives with content need one).
