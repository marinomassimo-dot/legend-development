**4.2 Correction: the P47T "nephropathy is age-typical" reading is over-credited.** The authors' sentence attaches "typically observed in aging mice" to the heterozygote (33%) and wild-type (29%) groups; those two percentages are the neoplasia column. It does not address the homozygotes' nephropathy (52%), and Supplementary Table 4 shows nephropathy at 1 of 7 in wild type, 13 of 18 in heterozygotes and 11 of 21 in homozygotes, with no test, a wild-type group of 7, and tissue taken at study end or when moribund (per the producer, with homozygote survival 393 against 542 days for wild type, not re-checked by me). So the finding is **an unadjusted excess in both mutant groups, heterozygote above homozygote, that the authors read as ageing and that the held text does not test**. It is a lead, not a harm baseline either way.

**4.3 Correction: the lithium liver cell.** The label does contain "hepatic": neonatal lithium toxicity ("a floppy baby syndrome including neurological, cardiac, and hepatic abnormalities ... similar to those seen with lithium toxicity in adults") after late-pregnancy exposure, and the elderly dose line ("decreased hepatic, renal, or cardiac function"). No chronic-therapy hepatic adverse reaction and no transaminase or bilirubin term is in the label (second concept set: 0 hits). The cell stays NO for chronic use; it is not "no hepatic statement".
