# ADV-095 unit A2 pre-registration, frozen before any lineage-stage value is opened: block, delay or loss of the oligodendrocyte lineage behind the lower mature-oligodendrocyte transcripts (SCI, 2026-11-19; hypothesis level; held data only; no dose, no recommendation; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

## Question and why it decides the next unit
Unit A (result `PREMISE_SCREEN_RESULT_UNIT_A_ADV095_SCI_20261119.md`) found mature-oligodendrocyte transcripts lower in all four P17 mouse contrasts, about 45 percent of the cortex shift carried by the stage covariate, and the registered M3 rule returned NEITHER block nor loss. A pro-differentiation agent can only help if the lineage is **blocked or delayed**, not **lost**. This step asks, from the held per-animal P17 data with lineage-stage markers, which reading the data support, and writes the other explanations of a lower mature-oligodendrocyte transcript and what each predicts for an agent. It decides whether the oligodendrocyte-class agent unit is worth running.

## Data (held)
* D2 cortex (GSE158682, neuron-targeted deletion, 3 knockout against 4 control animals; one GEO sample = one animal, assumed): the only contrast that can carry a label (at least three animals per arm).
* D3 hippocampus (GSE158681: S 3 v 2, N 2 v 2, null 2 v 3): descriptive.
* Age reference: `geo/E-MTAB-6798_mouse_timecourse/Mouse.RPKM.txt`, brain, ages P14 and P28 (four samples each; Ensembl gene identifiers mapped to symbols with the pinned Ensembl r92 headers). It gives the expected change of each gene between P14 and P28 in wild-type brain: the delay model's yardstick.
* Nuclei (descriptive, one pool per arm, P17 hippocampus, GSE158684): one library per arm, nuclei are not replicates; no p-value is computed.

## Lineage-stage gene sets (mouse symbols; general knowledge of oligodendrocyte lineage markers, NOT taken from these data; fixed now; a judgement)
PAN (Olig1, Olig2, Sox10); OPC (Pdgfra, Cspg4, Cspg5, Pcdh15); COP (Gpr17, Neu4, Bmp4, Vcan); NFOL (Tcf7l2, Itpr2, Enpp6, Bcas1, Tmem2, Fyn, Casr); MFOL (Mal, Mog, Opalin, Serinc5, Mag); MOL (Klk6, Apod, Pmp22, Slc5a11, Ermn, Hapln2); MYELIN_STRUCT (Mbp, Plp1, Mobp, Cnp; reported, not ranked). Stage rank for the gradient: OPC 1, COP 2, NFOL 3, MFOL 4, MOL 5 (PAN not ranked). Covariate for stage adjustment: the ADV-068 stage score (late set Grin2a, Slc12a5, Nrgn, Camk2a, Scn8a minus reference set Snap25, Rbfox3, Syt1, Stmn2, Tubb3, Map2), per animal, baseline the control mean. Every registered gene must be found; any not found is listed (mapping defects cost a first run in unit A).

## Quantities
Set value per animal = mean log2(TPM+1) over the set; R = knockout mean minus control mean; A1 = genotype term c of y = a + b stage + c KO, with leave-one-animal-out range. Age slope per gene g = (mean log2(RPKM+1) at P28 minus at P14) / 14 days (per day, valid between P14 and P28).
**Delay model.** A knockout at P17 behaving as a control d days younger shifts each gene by -d x slope_g. d_neuro is fitted by least squares through the origin over the covariate genes only (late set and reference set; slopes from the reference timecourse; observed per-gene shifts R in the cortex): d_neuro = minus the sum of (shift x slope) over the sum of slope squared. The predicted shift of each lineage-stage set is minus d_neuro times its mean slope; the explained fraction f = 1 minus the sum of squared residuals over the sum of squared observed shifts, over the five ranked stage sets (negative f is reported as is). d_OL is the same fit over the lineage-stage genes themselves, and the ratio d_OL over d_neuro is reported. d is valid between 0 and 3 days (the reference starts at P14); outside it the delay model is an extrapolation and is labelled.

## Readings, tokens and rejecting observations (cortex only; fixed now; thresholds not calibrated)
* **LOSS** (fewer lineage cells): token T_LOSS = PAN A1 at or below -0.5 **and** OPC A1 at or below -0.5. Rejected if OPC A1 is above -0.3.
* **BLOCK** (progenitors present, progression to maturity impaired): token T_BLOCK = OPC A1 and COP A1 both at or above -0.3, the mean of the MFOL and MOL A1 at or below -0.5, and the Spearman correlation between stage rank and A1 across the five ranked sets at or below -0.8. Rejected if OPC or COP A1 is below -0.3 or the gradient is flat (Spearman above -0.4).
* **DELAY** (the whole tissue is younger; the oligodendrocyte profile follows the age yardstick): token T_DELAY = d_neuro positive and between 0 and 3 days with every leave-one-animal-out fit positive, f at or above 0.5, and the OPC set shift of the sign the delay model predicts. Rejected if f is below 0.3, or any leave-one-out d_neuro is not positive, or d_OL over d_neuro is above 2 (an oligodendrocyte-specific deficit larger than the tissue-wide delay explains).
* Reading rule: exactly one token true: that reading is supported; two true: compatible with both (block and delay both predict OPC at or above its control value); none: UNDECIDED. Hippocampal contrasts show the same quantities with no label.

## Other explanations of a lower mature-oligodendrocyte transcript (each with its prediction, reported descriptively)
(a) fewer lineage cells: T_LOSS and the nuclei lineage fraction lower; (b) fewer transcripts per mature cell: in the nuclei, expression of Mbp, Plp1 and Mog per mature-class nucleus lower while the class share is similar; (c) stage or delay: T_DELAY; (d) dissection or regional carry-over: the per-animal mature score against a choroid-plexus and regional panel (Ttr, Kl, Folr1; not oligodendrocyte genes) across all animals of a contrast; a score that follows the panel across animals regardless of genotype points to dissection; (e) indirect loss of neuronal support: the per-animal mature score against the immediate-early panel (Fos, Egr1, Npas4, Arc, Junb, Nr4a1) and the stage score; with 3 to 4 animals per arm and genotype confounding both, these correlations are descriptive and cannot separate causes.

## Nuclei (descriptive; no p-value; marker-score assignment not calibrated)
Per nucleus, per class score = mean of log2(CPM+1) over the detected class genes, divided by the 99th percentile of that class score over all nuclei of both arms; each nucleus is assigned to the class with the largest value, unassigned if that value is below 0.2. Classes: excitatory (Slc17a7, Camk2a, Satb2), inhibitory (Gad1, Gad2, Slc32a1), astrocyte (Aqp4, Aldh1l1, Slc1a2), microglia (Cx3cr1, P2ry12, Csf1r) and the five ranked lineage stages. Reported per arm: nuclei per class, the lineage share of all assigned nuclei, the OPC share of lineage nuclei, the mature (MFOL plus MOL) share of lineage nuclei, and per mature-class nucleus the mean log2(CPM+1) of Mbp, Plp1, Mog. Strength of conclusion: counts of nuclei in one pool per arm; a difference is a lead, never a replicated result.

## What this cannot show
Cell numbers in tissue (only transcripts and nuclei assigned by markers), differentiation dynamics, the cause of a block or a delay, protein or myelin ultrastructure, anything in the human; whether the knockout age yardstick (wild-type CD-1 whole brain) fits a cortical or hippocampal conditional knockout; and independence (one laboratory and cohort family).

## Decision it changes (stated before the run)
LOSS supported alone: the oligodendrocyte-differentiation agent unit is not run; the broad lead class moves to survival or proliferation of the lineage or to a different mechanism, and the broad case is carried as undecidable on the held data with the cell-level measurement as the priority experiment. BLOCK or DELAY supported (alone or both): the agent unit runs, with the priority experiment taken from the reading (block: a lineage-stage readout with a differentiation challenge; delay: age-matched stage readouts at two ages). UNDECIDED: the agent unit runs with the premise labelled undecided and the cell-level readout as the priority experiment.
