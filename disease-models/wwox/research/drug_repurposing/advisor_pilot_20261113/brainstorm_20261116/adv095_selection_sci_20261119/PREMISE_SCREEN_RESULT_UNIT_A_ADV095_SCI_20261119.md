## Answer first
1. **No broad mechanism reaches the registered label PREMISE_SUPPORTED_T** (the same-direction, adjusted, outside-the-matched-null reading in both the human organoid and the mouse cortex). Labels as run: GSK-3 and Wnt targets ADJUSTMENT_DEPENDENT; ISR (ATF4 targets) PARTIAL, and its one qualifying reading is the **opposite** sign in the organoid; NRF2 and MYC FLAT; glycolysis and PDK ADJUSTMENT_DEPENDENT; astrocyte reactivity ADJUSTMENT_DEPENDENT by the rule but its mouse reading is strong (point 3); class IIa HDAC with MEF2 and EZH2 UNDECIDED_NO_RNA_READOUT as registered. The broad case therefore does not acquire a transcript-level premise by this screen for any of the registered pharmacological mechanisms, and per the registered decision the question moves to the agents (unit B and a perturbation-reversal stage), it is not closed.
2. **Where the held data are firm: myelin and astroglia, mouse only.** Mature-oligodendrocyte transcripts (MBP, PLP1, MOG, MOBP, MAG, CNP, ENPP6) are lower in all four P17 mouse contrasts (raw -1.06 cortex, -2.11, -1.60 and -1.80 in the three hippocampal contrasts; stage-adjusted -0.58, -0.81, -1.24, -1.36). In the only contrast that can carry a label (cortex, 3 against 4) the adjusted value lies outside the matched-random band (-0.50 to +0.25) and keeps its sign in every leave-one-animal-out fit (-0.92 to -0.30). **About 45 percent of the raw cortex shift is carried by the stage covariate** (-1.06 to -0.58): the data cannot say whether that part is confounding or a maturation delay that is itself part of the disease (the two readings below). The OPC-lineage set is only mildly lower (-0.38 raw, -0.14 adjusted in cortex), so the **registered rule returns NEITHER differentiation block nor lineage loss** (a block needs the OPC set at or above -0.3 and mature at or below -1.0 after adjustment too). Reading post hoc, outside the rule: previously held nuclei (ADV-065c; hippocampus, one pool per arm, descriptive) show the oligodendrocyte-like fraction falling 19.6 to 4.9 percent and the OPC-like fraction rising 1.4 to 3.9 percent, a pattern closer to a block than to a lineage loss; a bulk contrast cannot separate fewer cells from a block in the same cells.
3. **Reactive-astrocyte set (the benchmark's GFAP endpoint) is higher in the cortex** (raw +0.59, adjusted +1.18; leave-one-out +1.04 to +1.72; outside the null band -0.83 to +0.06) and in hippocampal S (raw +1.02, adjusted +0.50); it is not higher in the null contrast (-0.08) and in the organoid it flips with adjustment. A marker of the phenotype the gene-addition benchmark reverses, not a mechanism.
4. **The Wnt sign divides across the held datasets, as it does across the literature.** Human organoid: higher raw in both knockout clusters (+0.71, +0.77), unstable after adjustment (-0.07, +0.36). Mouse cortex: lower (raw -0.34, adjusted -0.71, leave-one-out -0.99 to -0.58) but **inside** the matched-random band (-0.78 to +0.09). The GSK-3 premise (Wnt output lower, so that inhibition would raise it) is therefore not supported in the organoid and not distinguishable from random sets in the cortex. This leaves the lithium effect as ADV already read it: general, a regimen question, not a broad-recovery candidate.
5. **ISR attenuation has no transcript premise and one opposite reading:** ATF4 targets are LOWER in both organoid clusters (raw -0.39, -0.34; adjusted -0.82, -0.36; both outside the null) and flat in cortex (-0.21 raw, -0.05 adjusted). Opposite is not exclusion (an adaptive response may be absent, or the protein-level activity may differ), and the proteome echo is too sparse to decide (3 of 16 ATF4 targets detected, mean +0.09).
6. **Control that failed, stated plainly:** the WWOX transcript is **not lower** in the organoid knockout deposit (raw +0.43; adjusted -0.41 and +0.10). The deposit's own RNA cannot confirm the deletion at transcript level, which is a property of that knockout design and not a reason to doubt the authors' protein-level deletion, but it means every organoid contrast here is read with that limit. In the mouse cortex the control works (-1.45 raw). The negative control (stable neuronal reference) shifts 0.05 in the cortex, as expected.
7. **Unbiased replicated list (post hoc reading; no druggable class is read from it).** Genes with the same sign of adjusted term in the organoid and the cortex: lower in the knockout, in rank order, HOPX, EGR1, CRABP2, KLF2, IL6ST, DDN, METRN, ALPL, PDPN, CAMK2N1, FOS; so an **activity-regulated and immediate-early transcript deficit** is replicated across species after adjustment, consistent with ADV-070's cortex reading. Higher: TTR first (most plausibly choroid-plexus carry-over in a dissection, flagged as an artefact until shown otherwise), then RAB3B, GAS8, SORL1, PHF20, DYNC1H1, CCND2. Nothing from this list enters the comparison on this evidence alone.
8. **What it changes.** For the broad objective the held data place the firmest transcript-level premise on **myelination (oligodendrocyte maturation, mouse) and astroglial reactivity**, the same endpoints the gene-addition rescue moved, with the composition-versus-block question open. The pharmacological broad comparison is therefore built around an **oligodendrocyte-differentiation route as the lead class under test**, with the GSK-3 class carried as sign-divided, and ISR, NRF2 and MYC carried only with a named protein-level test and no premise. Class IIa HDAC and EZH2 are carried with description only (cortex transcripts: HDAC4, HDAC5, HDAC9 lower, MEF2C higher, EZH2 +0.21 raw). This is **not yet a candidate**: it needs agents, exposure and endpoint mapping (unit B group, next increment), and the screen on these deposits has reached its reasoned stop (2 to 4 animals per arm, covariate collinear with genotype).
