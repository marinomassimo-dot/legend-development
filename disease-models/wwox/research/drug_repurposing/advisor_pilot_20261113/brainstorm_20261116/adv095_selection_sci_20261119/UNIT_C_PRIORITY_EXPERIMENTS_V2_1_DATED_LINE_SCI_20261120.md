# ADV-095 unit C: dated line v2.1 on the two priority experiments (SCI successor, 2026-11-20; amends `UNIT_C_PRIORITY_EXPERIMENTS_V2_REVISION_SCI_20261120.md` at two points; v1 and v2 stay; hypothesis level; no dose, no schedule, no recommendation; not medical advice)

IT's second read of v2 left two remarks, marked as IT's reading. I accept both.

**1. Broad experiment, thyromimetic engagement control (v2 A.2 item iii).** The Hairless transcript is not independent of the outcome: my own thyroid check (`PREREGISTRATION_UNIT_C_THYROID_...`, post hoc note) records that Hairless is probably expressed in mature oligodendrocytes (from memory, not read) and the same cortex shows a lower Mbp; in treated knockouts it could rise because more mature cells appear, not because thyroid signalling was engaged. **v2.1 replaces it:** (a) a peripheral thyroid-axis readout in the same pups, which cannot be moved by oligodendrocyte maturation: serum total T3, T4 and TSH, and the transcript of a thyroid-hormone-responsive gene in the liver (for example Dio1 or Thrsp), measured at the same time points as the brain levels; (b) in the cortex, a thyroid-response transcript **not enriched in the oligodendrocyte lineage** chosen by the laboratory from a cell-type-resolved brain expression atlas before data (Klf9 is a candidate, from memory, to be checked against the atlas; it must show neuronal rather than oligodendroglial enrichment), read in the vehicle and treated knockouts and in wild-type littermates. Hairless is dropped from the control. The reading rule is unchanged: a thyromimetic arm without peripheral and neuronal thyroid-response change at confirmed brain exposure is a failed engagement, not a negative.

**2. Bridge experiment, what the experiment can and cannot settle (v2 B.4 and B.5).** With the power statement of v2 (log SD of S about 0.54 to 0.94; a simple effect of ratio 0.7 needs about 36 to 109 animals per arm; the interaction needs about 700 to 1,300 animals for one drug), the interval-spanning row ("unresolved, underpowered") is the most likely single outcome of a run at feasible n. **The pilot (stage 1) is therefore the first step and the decision it yields is "set n or stop":** if the pilot's observed log SD gives a required n above what the laboratory can field for the simple effect in the knockout, the study stops there and the result recorded is that ethosuximide's effect in the knockout cannot be resolved by this readout; if it gives a feasible n, the n of the main run is set from it. **Whether the ethosuximide effect is specific to the Wwox-deficient genotype stays the INV-04 design question** (D03 and D08) and is not claimed to be settled by this experiment; the main run is read as a provoked-seizure lead in the knockout (v2 B.6). The decision table of v2 B.5 is unchanged; its row "interval spanning 0.7 and 1" now names the pilot and "set n or stop" as its decision.
