### Group C, oligodendrocyte-class agents
C1. Mei 2014, PMID 24997607. Statement: clemastine and benztropine promoted OPC differentiation at concentrations as low as 10 nM, most effective at 500 nM; clemastine, benztropine or T3 "promoted differentiation and myelination without altering the number of oligodendroglial cells". Also report: species and medium of the dose-response, and whether the 10 nM to 500 nM range is for clemastine, for benztropine or for both.
C2. Odell 2024, PMID 38942888 (held file). Statement: oral clemastine 7.5 mg/kg/day to hypoxia-exposed mouse pups from P3; plasma "Cmax 44.0 ng/mL, t1/2 4.6 h, and AUC24 280.1 ng*hr/mL" at steady state on day 8, total concentration; 7.5 mg/kg/day is the minimum effective dose for rescue of hypoxia-induced hypomyelination assessed at P14 by IHC and EM. Report the exact lowest dose tested and whether a lower dose was tested, the age at which the plasma was drawn, and any brain, CSF or unbound value anywhere in the paper (including supplementary mentions). Also the two sentences on sedation and on overt behavioural abnormalities.
C3. Palma 2022, PMID 35372341 (held file). Statement: clemastine 50 mg/kg/day i.p. from P5 to P21 in healthy developing mice; "despite the increase in the number of oligodendrocytes, conduction velocity of myelinated fibers of corpus callosum decreased in clemastine treated mice"; fewer myelinated axons, reduced nodes of Ranvier and myelin thickness; CD11c+ microglia and IGF-1 reduced. Report the other doses tested if any (is 50 mg/kg/day the only dose?), the strain, and the n.
C4. Pediatr Res 2018, PMID 29584714. Statement: two rat models of Pelizaeus-Merzbacher disease, clemastine 10 to 30 mg/kg/day from P1 to P21; "No changes in neurological phenotype or survival were observed even at the highest dose of clemastine"; mature oligodendrocytes unaffected. Report which two models these are (names), the route, and whether myelin was measured.
C5. Neuron 2018, PMID 30078577. Statement: chronic hypoxia, clemastine or U50488 at 10 mg/kg/day; "MBP staining for myelin and CC1 positive OL numbers were greatly enhanced at P10 as compared to the vehicle controls".
C6. Tcf4 Pitt-Hopkins mouse, PMID 37068912. Statement: "clemastine treatment reduced excess oligodendrocyte precursor cells and normalized oligodendrocyte density"; clemastine 10 mg/kg i.p. P28 to P42; the OPC pool was in excess, not intact; sobetirome and Sob-AM2 at 1.0 mg/kg/day P28 to P42 normalised OPC and oligodendrocyte density and behaviour.
C7. Triac, PMID 36834863. Statement: Mct8/Oatp1c1 double knockout mice; Triac 400 ng/g/day; "Triac treatment (400 ng/g) induced normalized myelination, cortical GABAergic interneuron differentiation, electrophysiological parameters, and locomotor performance only when administered during the first three postnatal weeks"; a start in postnatal weeks 3 to 6 did not give the same result.
