GROUP C
C1 Mei 2014: VERIFIED_NARROWER. The quote is a substring: "Both compounds were most effective at 500 nM but promoted differentiation at as low as 10 nM", and "promoted differentiation and myelination without altering the number of oligodendroglial cells (Fig. 4f-j)". The source spells it "benzatropine". The 10 nM to 500 nM range is stated for both compounds jointly. The dose-response itself is in Supplementary Fig. 1g, which I did not read. The main text and Methods do not state its species or medium. Screening used 40,000 rat OPCs in DMEM/B27/N2/NAC/forskolin without PDGF-AA, compounds at 1 uM for 3 d. "Without altering cell number" refers to the DRG cocultures (Fig. 4f-j; rat E15 DRG neurons).
C2 Odell 2024: VERIFIED_NARROWER. The abstract quote is exact. The paper's own definition is "lowest oral dose ... full pro-myelination and OL differentiation effect". Doses tested were 0.5, 2, 7.5, 10 mg/kg/day, so the lowest tested is 0.5. At 2 mg/kg, MBP intensity was already significantly higher (p = 0.003) but CC1+ density and the OPC proportion were not. Discussion: "It is possible that the true MED lies between 2 and 7.5 mg/kg/day". EM g-ratio was done at the MED only, not across doses. Plasma was drawn around the 8th daily dose; by my arithmetic from "P3 to P10" that is P10 (the 24 h sample is P11). Values are plasma only: LC-MS/MS, geometric mean, Tmax 0.5 h, C24 0.974 ng/mL, n 4-5 per time point (Fig. 6 legend; Methods say "at least 3"). I found no brain, CSF or unbound value. It cites Turski 2018 for brain penetration, which is PMID 29584714 (ref 45). Sedation sentence: "Treated mice did not appear sedated and did not otherwise have any overt behavioral abnormalities." Limitations: "we observed no overt signs of toxicity in mice treated with clemastine at any dose, we did not perform formal toxicity studies or determine the maximal tolerated dose in neonatal mice. Clemastine has side effects attributed to muscarinic antagonism, such as sedation, that may limit high doses in neonates." Behaviour was not examined.
C3 Palma 2022: VERIFIED_NARROWER. The abstract quote is exact. Only 50 mg/kg/day i.p. is described, with no other dose. Strain is C57BL/6 wild type (CD11c-eYFP for the microglia work); sex is not stated. Methods say P5 to P21, Results say "P5-20". n = 6 for histology, 10-12 for CAP (N1 slower, p < 0.01), 3 mice for EM. "Number of oligodendrocytes" is in the abstract, but the Results report a higher proportion of APC+ cells normalised to Olig2. Thinner myelin was only in 0-0.5 um axons ("no differences ... 0.5 to 1 um"). Node number was reduced, node length unchanged. The CD11c+ reduction used CD11c-eYFP mice treated P5 to P10. The IGF reduction is a gene-expression result in microglia (Fig. 6A, n = 4).
C4 Pediatr Res 2018: VERIFIED_NARROWER. The quote is exact (abstract). The models are the myelin-deficient (md, males) rat and the homozygous PLP transgenic (plp-tg) rat. Route is s.c. daily, P1 to P21, in saline or water. Myelin was measured (LFB, MBP IHC, ultrathin sections, stereology). Qualifiers: "The higher daily doses of clemastine, 20 and 30 mg/kg, induced mild sedation lasting 1-2 h", and one small significant effect (20 mg/kg, md males, MBP fiber bundles in brainstem).
C5 Wang 2018: VERIFIED_NARROWER. The quote is exact. Oral gavage P3 to P10, hypoxia exposure P3 to P10, n = 3 mice per group, Student's t-test.
C6 Tcf4: split verdict. The quote is exact, but it is the abstract's "In vitro" sentence about cultures. In vivo, clemastine 10 mg/kg i.p. P28 to P42 also raised OLs and lowered OPCs (n = 4 per genotype). The OPC excess in mutants is supported. Sob-AM2 was given in vivo at 1.0 mg/kg/day i.p. for 14 d (P28 to P42), with behaviour (open field) normalised. Sobetirome itself was tested only in vitro (1 uM in Results; Methods text says "10 uM per day", an internal inconsistency). NOT_SUPPORTED: sobetirome in vivo at 1.0 mg/kg. The Discussion loosely says sobetirome acted "in vitro and in vivo".
C7 Triac: VERIFIED. The quote is exact (abstract). The Results text for the late start is: "in Dko mice that received Triac only between postnatal week 3 and 6, myelination was not improved while a partial recovery of PV+ immunoreactivity could still be detected." Treatment days were P1 to P20 or P22 to P42, analysed at P43, n = 3-4, 400 ng/g s.c. daily. Comparators were saline Dko and Wt (values normalised to Wt). Sex is not stated. Narrower: the late start gave partial PV recovery, not nothing.
C8 Hartley 2019: VERIFIED. The quote is exact (abstract). The model is iCKO-Myrf (tamoxifen-induced genetic demyelination); hyperthyroid T3/T4 chow made the course worse. Sobetirome (80 ug/kg/d in chow) and Sob-AM2 (84 ug/kg/d p.o.) improved rotarod and myelin in iCKO-Myrf. In the lysolecithin and cuprizone models, T3 and sobetirome (1 mg/kg/d i.p. in cuprizone) promoted repair (sobetirome less than T3). Sob-AM2 was not tested in those gliotoxin models as far as I read.
C9 Maternal Sob-AM2 and sobetirome: VERIFIED. The quote is exact. Doses are s.c. daily E12.5 to E18.5, sobetirome 1.0 mg/kg and Sob-AM2 0.3 mg/kg. The effect was seen in Mct8+/-/Dio2-/- dams, n = 5. The Discussion says "possibly due to hyperthyroid-like effects".
C10 Tiratricol: VERIFIED. EMA Emcitate page: "marketing authorisation valid throughout the EU on 12/02/2025". The SmPC says "indicated for the treatment of peripheral thyrotoxicosis in patients with monocarboxylate transporter 8 (MCT8) deficiency (Allan-Herndon-Dudley Syndrome), from birth."
