# ADV-095 oligodendrocyte-class agent unit: producer brief (SCI, 2026-11-19; hypothesis level; no dose, no schedule, no recommendation for or against any use of any agent in any person; research concentrations and animal doses are reported as findings of the source, kept apart from any patient dosing; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

Dispatch settings (stated per `framework/protocols/delegation_budget.md`): delegate type `legend-delegate`, model sonnet, effort medium, one active delegate, no sub-delegation, one round. Output is the delegate's FINAL MESSAGE (the harness does not keep subagent files; SCI persists it verbatim).

## Why this unit
Units A and A2 (folder `adv095_selection_sci_20261119`, on main) found, in the held WWOX-deficient P17 mouse data, a progenitor pool intact (OPC set about unchanged after stage adjustment) and every oligodendrocyte stage from the committed precursor onward lower by about -0.5 log2, with a nuclei composition that looks block-like (OPC share of lineage up, mature share down, lineage share of all nuclei unchanged); tissue-wide delay and lineage loss were rejected; the reading is "a differentiation defect at the progenitor-to-committed-precursor transition", undecided between block and fewer cells passing. The agent question: which agents act at that step, with what evidence of target engagement, exposure and endpoint effect in developmental dysmyelination or hypomyelination, and with what harm, so SCI can compare them against each other and against the gene-addition benchmark (survival, weight, motor score, structural myelin, GFAP, glucose; the myelin endpoint is the one this class could move).

## Agents
Named: (1) **clemastine** (muscarinic M1 antagonism; OPC differentiation); (2) **benztropine** (same class of action); (3) **the thyroid-hormone class**: liothyronine (T3), tiratricol (Triac), and the CNS-penetrant thyromimetics sobetirome and its brain-targeted prodrug; (4) up to **two more** that you judge the best supported at the OPC differentiation step from the primary literature (for example an EBP-inhibiting azole, a glucocorticoid-receptor modulator, metformin, an RXR agonist, a GPR17 antagonist): name them with the single reason each, and drop the weak ones rather than padding.

## What each row must hold
1. **Target and potency with the assay**: receptor affinity (Ki or IC50), and the concentration range at which OPC differentiation or myelin gene expression changed in vitro, with species, preparation, medium (serum-free or not), exposure time, and the endpoint (MBP-positive cells, MBP or PLP transcript, myelin-wrapping in co-culture).
2. **Exposure**: brain or CSF concentration in mice or rats at the regimens used in the myelination studies (total or unbound, matrix, time, age), with neonatal or juvenile values where they exist; state where only adult or plasma values exist; do not invent juvenile free concentrations.
3. **Endpoint evidence** in developmental dysmyelination or hypomyelination (neonatal hypoxia or hyperoxia, genetic hypomyelination, thyroid-deficient animals, MCT8 deficiency models) and in demyelination models (cuprizone, lysolecithin, EAE) kept in separate columns, because remyelination is not developmental myelination; include **negative, null and failed-replication results** (for example any independent study that did not reproduce a published effect) and the effect on the **number of progenitors against mature cells** if the paper separates them.
4. **Cell autonomy**: whether the effect is shown on isolated OPCs or oligodendrocyte lineage cells (acting without neurons) or only in a tissue where neurons are present; this matters because the held deletion is neuron-targeted.
5. **Harm and label, kept separate**: age-specific adverse findings in animals and humans (anticholinergic effects and age restrictions for clemastine and benztropine; cardiac, metabolic and growth effects for thyromimetics; regulatory status of tiratricol for MCT8 deficiency stated from the label or the regulator text), each with its source and level. Pediatric use or an over-the-counter status does not show a conservative profile for this question.
6. **What was not found**, with two routes tried.
