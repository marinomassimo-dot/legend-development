# ADV-095 unit A pre-registration, frozen before any gene value of this screen is opened (SCI, 2026-11-19; hypothesis level; held data only; no dose, no recommendation; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

## Question and what the screen is
For each broad-recovery mechanism the ranking and ADV's note carry (GSK-3 and Wnt, integrated stress response attenuation, pro-myelinating, NRF2, glycolysis and PDK, MYC) and for the astrocyte-reactivity endpoint of the gene-addition benchmark: in the held WWOX-deficient datasets, in the unit of biological replication, does the pathway output the candidate would act on move in the direction the candidate would reverse (the PREMISE direction), **before and after** the maturity and composition adjustments? The screen is **evidence, not a universal condition of access** (operator direction, 22:19Z): absence of an RNA change or an opposite sign does not exclude a lever, because protein activity, phosphorylation, localisation, flux and compensation are not transcripts. Its output is a per-mechanism reading with its adjustments shown and the non-RNA evidence pointer named; it removes nothing from the comparison by itself. It answers the single decision: which mechanisms enter the broad comparison (unit C) with a transcript-level premise, which enter with a premise undecided at transcript level, and which enter with a transcript-level opposite sign to be explained.

## Per-set shift and the three contrasts shown for every set and dataset
Set value per sample = mean over the detected genes of log2 expression; shift = knockout minus the mean of the controls. **R (raw)** = the shift as it stands: the phenotype including any maturity or composition difference. **A1 (stage-adjusted)** = the genotype term c of the ordinary least squares fit y = a + b x covariate + c KO, with leave-one-sample-out range (D1: covariate = NEU minus PROG maturity score, after PROLIF subtraction as N1; D2: the stage score, leave-self-out for sets sharing genes). **A2 (composition-aware, D2 only, fragile at df 4)**: stage and OL_GUARD together; reported, never decisive. For every set the difference R minus A1 is reported as "carried by the covariate" and **two readings are stated, not chosen by me**: (i) the covariate is a confounder (the effect is stage, not disease) or (ii) the covariate is a component of the phenotype (a maturation delay or a composition change is part of the disease, and adjusting removes a real effect). The held data cannot choose between them; the stage-score separation between arms (D1, D2) is reported as the evidence on how much covariate difference there is.

## Nulls, floors and controls (fixed now)
* **Expression-matched random sets:** 1000 sets of the same size as the set, drawn from genes whose mean log2 expression over all samples of that dataset is within 0.5 of the set's own mean (seed 20261119); the observed shift is placed in that distribution (two-sided empirical percentile).
* **Label permutation (D2: all 35 splits of 7 animals into 3 and 4; D1: all 15 splits of 6 libraries into 4 and 2):** the set's shift under each split gives an exact two-sided p; D1's minimum attainable p is 0.13, so it can never confirm by itself (stated, not hidden).
* **Noise floor:** D1 the wild-type pair difference; D2 the spread of leave-one-out control means.
* **Positive control:** the WWOX transcript falls in D1 and D2 (deletion check). **Negative control:** REF_STABLE set (stable neuronal reference) shifts by less than 0.3 in D2 and D1; if not, every shift of that dataset is annotated "reference moved".
* **Not an independent confirmation:** D1, D4 are human and one origin each; D2 and D3 are mouse and one laboratory and cohort family; "replicated" below means two datasets of two species, never two laboratories.

## Labels (a reading, thresholds not calibrated, no gate)
For a mechanism set in D1 (both clusters) and D2 separately: **Q** (qualifies in the premise direction) = |R| and |A1| both at least 0.3 log2 with the sign of the premise direction, the A1 sign kept in every leave-one-out fit, outside the 2.5 to 97.5 percentile of the matched null, and above the noise floor. **Q-opp** is the same in the opposite direction.
* **PREMISE_SUPPORTED_T**: Q in D1 and in D2. **PREMISE_OPPOSITE_T**: Q-opp in D1 and in D2. **ADJUSTMENT_DEPENDENT**: |R| qualifies and |A1| does not (shrinks below 0.3 or changes sign) in D1 or in D2: reported with both readings (i) and (ii). **PARTIAL**: Q in one dataset only. **FLAT**: neither dataset reaches 0.3 in R. **UNDECIDED_NO_RNA_READOUT**: M7, M8. M3 uses its own definition above. D3 and D4 annotate and never change a label.
* **Rejecting observations, stated before the run:** the transcript-level premise of a mechanism is rejected for the purpose of this screen if it is PREMISE_OPPOSITE_T; it is **not** thereby excluded as a lever (compensation, protein-level activity, a pathway that is high because it is attempting rescue); the broad comparison then carries it as "premise opposite at transcript level, lever kept alive only with a named protein-level or functional test". A mechanism is FLAT or ADJUSTMENT_DEPENDENT: carried as undecided, not removed.

## Unbiased replicated-gene list (so a better alternative can enter)
Genes with expression floor (mean control log2 expression at least 3 in D1 and D2) whose A1 term has the same sign in D1 (mean over the four knockout libraries against the two wild-type libraries, maturity and PROLIF adjusted) and in D2, ranked by the smaller of the two |A1|; the top 40 in each direction are written to a table. Reading of that list is post hoc and labelled as such; no gene set enrichment is run (no held gene-set collection is assumed).

## What this screen cannot show
Protein activity, phosphorylation, localisation, flux, compensation or any in vivo drug effect; causation; anything at ages other than 15 weeks (D1) and P17 (D2, D3); independence beyond one origin per species; the cell-type location of a change in bulk data; whether adjustment for maturity removes a component of the disease. A PREMISE_SUPPORTED_T would make a mechanism a candidate for the broad comparison, never a candidate intervention by itself; a perturbation-reversal analysis (a stage A2) and the endpoint mapping come after, each with its own pre-registration or dated rule before its first value.

## Decision it changes (stated before the run)
Which mechanisms the broad comparison (unit C) carries and with what premise status. If no mechanism is PREMISE_SUPPORTED_T, the broad case is "undecidable at transcript level for the listed mechanisms", the next investment is the protein-level or functional readout best matched to the leading pharmacology evidence (unit B), and the question moves to the perturbation-reversal stage A2 for generation; the screen does not end the broad objective.
