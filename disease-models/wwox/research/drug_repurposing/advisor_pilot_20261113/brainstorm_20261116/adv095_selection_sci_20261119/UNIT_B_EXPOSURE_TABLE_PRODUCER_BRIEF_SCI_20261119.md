# ADV-095 unit B: producer brief for the target and exposure table (SCI, 2026-11-19; hypothesis level; no dose, no schedule, no recommendation for or against any use of any agent in any person; research concentrations in model systems only; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

Dispatch settings (stated per `framework/protocols/delegation_budget.md`): delegate type `legend-delegate`, model sonnet, effort medium, one active delegate, no sub-delegation, one round. Output is the delegate's FINAL MESSAGE (the harness does not keep subagent files; SCI persists it verbatim).

## Question
For each agent of the bridge shortlist, which concentration of the agent, in which model system, changes which target, and what free exposure at that target is documented in the juvenile mouse at the regimens actually used in the Wwox-null or comparable seizure studies. Purpose: let SCI choose, per agent, the exposure-effect relation (occupancy C/(C+IC50) only where the assay supports it) and let HE read a normalisation-independent comparison. Not asked: any ranking, any dose for a person.

## Agents (and the one question that matters for each)
1. **Ethosuximide.** T-type (Cav3.1, 3.2, 3.3) potency in recombinant cells against native thalamic neurons (state assay: holding potential, temperature, charge carrier), and competing targets (persistent sodium, GIRK or Kir3, calcium-activated potassium, Na/K-ATPase) with whatever potency is stated; brain and plasma levels in mice after the regimens used in the held Wwox-null reading (find the regimen in `adv054_age_window_sci_20261118/` and `adv046_review_sci_20261118/`, which are on main; quote it); protein binding; any pup or juvenile pharmacokinetics.
2. **Kv7 openers** (retigabine or ezogabine, flupirtine, ICA-069673 or ICA-27243) and the blocker XE991: EC50 or voltage-shift potency at Kv7.2/7.3 and at Kv7.4, 7.5; subunit selectivity; the GABA-A action of retigabine (concentration); mouse brain exposure at regimens used in seizure models; juvenile or neonatal data; the label or withdrawal facts as harm information (retinal and skin pigmentation) with the age they were seen.
3. **Tonic-inhibition enhancers:** ganaxolone, allopregnanolone, gaboxadol (THIP). Potency at extrasynaptic delta-containing against synaptic gamma-containing GABA-A; mouse brain exposure; and the developmental sign: GABA-A action depends on the chloride gradient, so state what is documented about tonic-current enhancement against seizures in immature (P7 to P21) rodents. Label status in children is to be verified from the label text, not assumed.
4. **NMDAR class, exposure side only** (kinetics are held: `adv050_nmdar_agents_sci_20261118/`): memantine, ketamine, a GluN2B-selective modulator (radiprodil), and d-APV: documented free or total brain concentration in mice at the regimens used; for d-APV state what is known of blood-brain barrier passage (it is used in slices; say whether any in vivo systemic use is documented). Add the **pharmacology data that distinguish REAL agents inside the class**: IC50 with 1 mM Mg2+, subunit preference, trapping, in the same table format as ADV-050; do not redo what ADV-050 holds, cite it.
5. **Lithium, exposure side only** (regimen table is held: `adv067_lithium_regimen_sci_20261118/`): brain lithium concentration at the regimens used against its two best-documented targets (GSK-3 direct inhibition constant and inositol monophosphatase), and any in vivo evidence that GSK-3 inhibition (for example Ser9 or Ser21 phosphorylation) is reached at those concentrations in mouse brain.
6. **Vigabatrin and the ketogenic diet:** these do not act by occupancy. For vigabatrin state the mechanism (irreversible GABA-transaminase inactivation) and the pharmacodynamic marker documented in mouse brain (GABA level) against time, for the diet the ketone-body concentrations documented in mouse or rat pups on the diet; say plainly that no C/(C+IC50) mapping applies.

## Rules
- Plain HTTP clients and the PubMed tools only. Never send any e-mail address or personal detail to any service (no contact-address parameter, no User-Agent with an address); no browser-header spoofing; a reCAPTCHA or challenge page is a stop for that route, switch route (Europe PMC article page, efetch, publisher media route) and say which.

- Do not invent juvenile free concentrations: where only adult or total values exist, say so and leave the juvenile cell empty.
- No opinion on which agent is preferred. Report disagreement between sources as disagreement.
