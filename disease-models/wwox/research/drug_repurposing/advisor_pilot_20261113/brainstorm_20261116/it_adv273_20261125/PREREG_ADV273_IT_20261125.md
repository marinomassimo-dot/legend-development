## Definitions fixed now
* **Platform A (the one DL-REPO-003 carries):** H9-derived neural-lineage differentiation cells (UKN1 and the hESC-to-neural systems) of the Leist group (Balmer 2014, Rempel 2015, Waldmann 2017 and the shared neural-induction data; PMID 23179753, 24935251, 26272509, 27188386, 28001369).
* **A second platform** = a measurement of WWOX transcript or protein after an HDAC inhibitor in a **different laboratory AND a different cell system** (another cell line, primary cells, tissue, or an in vivo exposure), written by authors who do not share the platform-A data. Two second platforms count as two only if they differ from each other in lab and cell system too.
* **A measurement** = a WWOX-specific readout (blot band, probe set, qPCR) in the exposed against the unexposed arm; a mention in text without data, a database annotation, or a gene-list membership is **not** a measurement. An epigenetic co-treatment (5-aza-2'-deoxycytidine) confounds: a combination row does not isolate the HDAC inhibitor.
* **Compounds searched:** valproate (valproic acid, sodium valproate), entinostat, trichostatin A, panobinostat, vorinostat (SAHA), belinostat, sodium butyrate (butyrate), 4-phenylbutyrate (phenylbutyrate), plus the generic terms "histone deacetylase inhibitor", "HDAC inhibitor", "HDACi".
