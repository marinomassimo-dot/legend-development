## 3. Independence statement for DL-REPO-003 (proposed; candidate `CC-20261125-ORCHESTRATOR-ADV273-INDEPENDENCE-01`)
* **Platforms by the definition:** two (platform A, neural lineage; HO8910, ovarian carcinoma), direction UP in both, one blot per panel at the second.
* **Not independent evidence of each other's weakness:** HO8910 is a cancer line, with an expressed WWOX at baseline (control bands visible) and 1 to 4 mmol/L; the abstract-level cancer-line reports (rows 2 to 6) come from lines chosen for **epigenetic silencing** of WWOX and test **reactivation** with a demethylating agent, so they are not a test of how an HDAC inhibitor moves expressed WWOX: they must not be counted as independent replications of the platform-A direction.
* **One report disagrees** in a context of silencing (row 7, HCC lines, combination, "not shown"): a negative with no data.
* **Class heterogeneity (SCI's corrected Rempel table):** VPA, entinostat and trichostatin A move at least one of five WWOX probe sets (trichostatin A one up and one down), panobinostat, belinostat and vorinostat do not (largest 1.23 and 1.24). The entry should carry this sentence: "three of six HDAC inhibitors move WWOX in the one table that has all six".
* **Not established anywhere:** a neural second platform; any measurement in a WWOX-deficient cell; a dose or exposure link.
