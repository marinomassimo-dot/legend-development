## 2. Outcome codes (frozen), per unit
* **RESOLVED_ANIMAL**: a record or sentence says each library is one animal (or gives an animal identifier per library). Effective n = the number of libraries.
* **RESOLVED_POOLED**: a record or sentence says libraries pool animals or neurosphere batches; effective n and the consequence are stated.
* **RESOLVED_BY_COUNT**: no sentence, but the stated counts exclude a reading (for example fewer hippocampal than cortical libraries make a one-to-one mapping to the same mice impossible). Stated as an exclusion, not as a resolution of the unit.
* **NOT_RESOLVABLE**: none of the above; the missing object is named (which record or sentence would settle it).
Library names (S_Ctrl_1 and the like) are **consistent with** one library per animal but are not evidence for it; they alone never give a RESOLVED code.

## 3. Evidence and two-hit rule
Routes: (a) GEO series and sample records (one SOFT text per series, spaced requests, generic client name; a 429 or a challenge page is a result and stops that route); (b) the paper's Methods, legends and supplement as held (Repudi 2021, PMID 33914858; Hussain 2019, PMID 30290271); (c) the deposits' own naming and counts; (d) my IT14 identity table. A resolution or an absence claim needs two different routes with the matching lines read, not counted. PubMed and Europe PMC count as one route.
