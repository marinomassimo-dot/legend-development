## A6 E03 dichloroacetate
Held answers: dichloroacetate.json

> dichloroacetic acid acts at millimolar concentrations in most rows read (the fibroblast EC50 below is sub-millimolar). Within ONE 2017 document (an enzyme-linked assay on full-length PDHA1 as substrate, 30 minutes; four assays of the same design) the IC50 is 1.03 millimolar for PDK1, 0.50 for PDK2, 5.56 for PDK3 and 0.73 for PDK4: an eleven-fold range across the four isozymes within that document, PDK3 the least inhibited there. Other documents disagree: a second 2017 enzyme-linked document (tagged enzyme expressed in insect cells, 1 hour) gives PDK1 5.1 and PDK2 9.0 millimolar, the reverse PDK1 to PDK2 order, and carries no PDK3 or PDK4 value; a 2025 radiometric document gives PDK1 3.66 and PDK4 0.108 millimolar, with PDK2 and PDK3 only as bounds above 5. So PDK4 runs from 0.108 millimolar (2025 radiometric assay) to 0.73 (2017 enzyme-linked assay), the isozyme order differs by document and assay and is not a property of the compound, and no held row shows dichloroacetate inhibiting PDK3 better than another isozyme; two 2023 rows against PDK1 are bounds only (above 100 and above 50 micromolar); the database flags the 1999, 2017 and 2025 values as outside its typical range. A 1999 row gives only a bound, above 2 millimolar against PDK in vitro; an EC50 of 100 micromolar in normal human dermal fibroblasts is a different readout, with no order read between it and these. Five leukaemia cell-line rows give 8 to 14 millimolar (MTT cytotoxicity, 48 hours, 2016). Separately, and not from these potencies: Pdk3 is not higher in two held mouse Wwox-deficient tissues of one study (see the transcript note of this card), so there is no evidence here for weighting PDK3 as the relevant isoform in this genotype class, and none for excluding it as a target.
