# ADV-185 result: is there a per-gene ERBB4 comparison, shScrambled against shWWOX, in the held Kosla 2019 material (PMID 31543760; GEO GSE126075), and what does it say about "less ErbB4 in WWOX-deficient neural precursors"? (SCI successor Z, 2026-11-22; hypothesis level; transcript data cannot test the post-translational stabilisation arm; no compound, direction, ranking, candidate or NRG1 handle; no WWOX claim beyond what the source measures; no author contact; no e-mail address to any service; not medical advice)

Pre-registration: `PREREGISTRATION_ADV185_SCI_20261122.md` (commit feec6e38c, before any per-gene value was opened). Receipt: `RECEIPT_ADV185_SCI_20261122.json`.

## Answer first
**CLOSE, by the pre-registered control rule; the numbers are reported descriptively and are not read as an ERBB4 lead.** A per-gene deposit exists and was read (GSE126075, gene-level TPM, one sample per genotype per stage, no replicates). The pre-registered positive control fails where the question lives: **WWOX itself is not lower in the silenced progenitor sample (TPM 2.90 against 3.35, log2 +0.21)**, and at the neuron stage the silenced sample is shifted globally (median log2 of all genes -1.43, summed TPM 89,581 against 264,496), so per-gene folds there are scale-confounded. The paper's contrast was also named shScrambled against shWWOX while the deposit labels the control "wild type". With the control failing and no replicate structure, no ERBB4 reading is made.

**The three quantities, kept apart.**
1. **Set membership (not a size, not an abundance):** ERBB4 and NRG1 are in the leading edge of the GO_Stem_Cell_Differentiation set on the hNPC/shScrambled side in Fig 8B ("dualistic enrichment ... in both phenotypes"); the audit's quotation (`hnpc_differentiation_audit_20260920.md` lines 106 to 111) is accurate to the paper's Results sentence. The paper's GSEA tables are not held with per-gene values (Data_Sheet_1 carries Supplementary Fig 1, the Western blot, and no ERBB4 or NRG1 line).
2. **Transcript fold (measured here, one sample per cell):** progenitor pair ERBB4 16.19 against 4.12 TPM (log2 -1.98); neuron pair 58.40 against 17.26 (log2 -1.76); NRG1 0.39 against 0.14 and 3.12 against 1.89 (near or below the 1 TPM floor in the progenitor pair); ERBB2 9.96 against 9.49 (progenitor). Descriptive only, for the reasons above.
3. **Protein level (not measured anywhere in the paper):** the only protein measurement is the three-lane WWOX blot (native, scrambled, shWWOX). No ErbB4 protein, so the stabilisation arm of the L result is untouched and the premise stays unmeasured.

Where ERBB4 sits in the progenitor comparison: log2 -1.98 is rank 88 from the bottom of 9,067 genes (about the first percentile); 7% of genes are at or below -1 and 14.6% at or above +1, so a fold of this size is not rare in this deposit, which fits an uncontrolled state difference as readily as a gene-specific effect. After removing the stage median, the neuron-pair ERBB4 fold is about -0.3 (post hoc normalisation, not pre-registered).

**A side observation the audit left unreconciled (descriptive, not tested).** In this gene list, 5,366 of 6,859 genes (78%) are at least 2-fold lower and only 18 at least 2-fold higher in the silenced neuron sample, against 635 lower and 1,328 higher of 9,067 in the progenitor pair. A one-sided shift of that size fits a library-level or normalisation difference. The paper reports 2,282 differentially expressed genes in the progenitor pair and 7,392 in the neuron pair (|log2 FC| > 1, p < 0.05; raw CAGE tag counts normalised "with simple TPM method"), and the 2026-09-20 audit flags that asymmetry as never reconciled by the paper. A scale shift in the silenced neuron sample would be one candidate explanation. I called no differential expression and did not test it; it bears on how the neuron-stage counts are read, not on ERBB4.

## Why CLOSE and not NARROW
My pre-registered rule: WWOX must be lower in the silenced sample than in its stage-matched control, otherwise labels or the knockdown are in doubt and no ERBB4 reading is made. It fails at the progenitor stage, which is the stage the premise concerns. A failing control does not prove the knockdown failed (shRNA may act without lowering CAGE 5' tag counts, WWOX is near 3 TPM so a single sample is noisy, and the paper's Western shows the protein reduced); it means this deposit cannot carry the comparison. The exits also required a replicate structure for KEEP, and none exists.

## Reading limits
One sample per genotype per stage by the GEO record; gene-level TPM from a CAGE promoter-level method (the merged file has 9,427 genes, so it is a filtered gene list; the TSS-level file has coordinates and promoter names, was not used per gene); no variance, so no test can be computed; a partial knockdown in an H9-derived line verified by one blot; the deposited control is labelled "wild type" while the paper contrasts shScrambled against shWWOX, and the deposit does not say which control it is (native cells or scrambled vector); not the DEE genotype class.

## Scope added after the pre-registration (Codex return 26, relayed by ADV; recorded here, the frozen file is unchanged)
The deposit has one sample per genotype and stage, so everything above is TPM by sample and stage, DESCRIPTIVE only: no inferential P value, no genotype-by-stage result, and no claim of a second, independent confirmation of a protein change was made or is implied. A lower RNA value can nominate a transcriptional alternative or a paired readout; a flat value does not rule out post-translational turnover. Set membership, transcript value and protein level remain three different quantities. The useful contrast is a PAIRED full-length JMa ErbB4 protein and RNA observation in the relevant neural system at comparable cell state, which is the minimal measurement named below.

## What measurement would answer the question (minimal)
Full-length JMa ErbB4 protein together with ERBB4 RNA, in WWOX-deficient human neural precursors or organoids against a matched control, from at least three independent derivations, with WWOX knockdown or knockout shown in the same samples and stage identity markers matched. Nothing smaller separates "less ErbB4 at RNA level" from "less ErbB4 protein" from a progenitor-state difference.

## Consumers, DREAM, rejecting observation
* **Consumer:** none new. The IT59-Q01 clause (from the L result) already carries the stabilisation arm as untested; nothing is added by this read. No ranking, compound, direction or NRG1 handle changes.
* **DREAM:** set membership, transcript fold and protein level are three quantities, the same measured-object shape as ADV-181 (area for new sheath) and ADV-184 (colocalisation for surface abundance); here the deposit's own WWOX control (a number at 3 TPM in both samples) was the check that stopped a tempting reading. Weak association, kept as a reading rule: name the quantity the premise needs before reading the number.
* **Rejecting observation:** a replicated deposit with WWOX clearly lower in the silenced progenitor sample and ERBB4 lower beyond sample-to-sample variance would convert this to a descriptive RNA-level lead; the 2019 deposit has neither.
