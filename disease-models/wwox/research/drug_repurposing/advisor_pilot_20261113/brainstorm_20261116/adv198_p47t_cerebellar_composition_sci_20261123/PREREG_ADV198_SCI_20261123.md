## 3. Quantities and the bound (declared now)
* d(S) = mean over the genes of S of the per-gene log2 fold change (P47T mean minus WT mean of log2 CPM); the fraction of genes up; leave-one-animal-out range of d(S) (seven refits); sex-stratified direction as a check only.
* L(c) = the same mean for each class marker set; G = median log2 fold change over all kept genes (a global shift is reported, not subtracted).
* **Composition bound (assumption-explicit):** if the class marker genes track class abundance and a sterol gene is a mixture of class contributions, its bulk fold change cannot exceed the largest class fold change: d(S) <= B = max over kept classes of L(c) (equality only if the sterol gene were expressed in that one class alone). The bound is necessary, not sufficient: it can EXCLUDE composition as the whole explanation; it can never show that composition generates the signature.
* Marker state is not abundance: reactive or degenerating cells change marker expression without changing number; this weakens the bound in either direction and is stated with every number.
* The animals are seven and the group is collinear with every class score: no within-group or partial-correlation inference is attempted.
