# DREAM pass after ADV-068 and ADV-069 (SCI, 2026-11-19; hypothesis level; not a claim; no dose; not medical advice)

Something material changed (a stop met at its boundary, a bounded null, two corrections to my own census), so the pass was run; it ends in one chain and one consumer check.

**Chain (a new combination in the repository, not a novelty claim against the literature, which is not checked here).** Observations: at P17 the Pvalb transcript is PV-weighted lower with a late-maturing neuronal set and the myelin genes falling with it (ADV-068, one cohort family); the KCC2 transcript falls with the same set and is the one gene deciding that stop; the single-nucleus note of the same cohort shows higher immature-neuron genes in the knockout neurons; hypomyelination is documented in several preparations; behavioural seizures begin after about P12 and the first EEG is at about P14. Proposed relation: the PV, KCC2 and myelin readings may share one cause, a delayed or arrested postnatal maturation programme (or the late-stage state of an ill pup) rather than three independent lesions. Competing explanations: three independent lesions of one deletion; a late-stage consequence of seizures, hypoglycaemia and poor growth; composition and capture effects in bulk tissue. Question: do PV, KCC2 and myelin genes move together **per animal** with a general late-maturation score, and does the shift exist **before** seizure onset (P10 to P12)? Discriminating measurement: the optional tissue measurement already added to the outreach package and the brief (PV, a late-maturing neuronal marker and a myelin marker, age-matched controls at two ages), with KCC2 protein as a fourth reading if the laboratory can take it; leanings as written there (together at both ages: a programme-level effect; only at the later age: stage of illness; separate time courses: independent lesions). No public Wwox bulk deposit at a pre-seizure age is known to me; that absence is one route searched, not an exclusion.

**Consequence for searches.** Any lever argued from a P17 bulk transcript (a PV-directed class, a KCC2 enhancer, a myelin-directed class) inherits the stage confound; the age-matched two-age control is the cheapest shared discriminator, so the factorial chloride layout and the PV and myelin tissue read should share the same animals and ages.
