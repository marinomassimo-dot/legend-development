## Answer first
1. **IT56-Q05: the original statement concerns total protein (and mRNA), not folded protein.** Both abundance observations are denaturing-gel readouts of total polypeptide in a lysate. Neither measures folded state, solubility, half-life or turnover; the census already records all three as NEVER TESTED for P47T. The ddG +2.81 is therefore not contradicted by "protein present", and it is not supported either (the predictor is inverted against the six measured variants: census 6.1).
2. **"Wild-type level" is supported in neither matrix; "present" is supported in both.** Fibroblasts: not quantified in any held source (one individual, three passages of one culture, four control lines, one N-terminal antibody, "based on visual inspection", no densitometry, no statistic). Knock-in cerebellum: the 10% input lane of a pull-down blot, two lanes per genotype, no densitometry in the paper; the blind audit's per-lane integration finds the two mutant bands lighter than the two wild-type bands (1050 and 1082 against 1468 and 1556, a mutant/wild-type ratio of about 0.70 in both pairs) against a loading control that is even to slightly stronger in the mutant lanes. The audit's own caveat applies (grey value on a JPEG of film is not linear): direction and rank order only, never a fold-change. The transcript arm is quantified (n = 6 per group, no difference).
3. **The proteostasis-directed lever is not excluded for P47T, and no held observation supports it either.** The P47T arm speaks for "binding loss with protein present". My own earlier wording ("protein at wild-type amounts", "nothing to restore") overstated it; I correct my files below.
4. **Wording.** Class B: "binding loss; protein present, level not quantified, stability untested". C069: "needs protein present" (not "retained"); see section 4 for what that does and does not say.

## 2. The competing reading, and why the held data cannot choose
**Reading R-A (the authors'):** a binding-defective protein at wild-type amount; the lesion is local to the PPxY groove. **Reading R-B:** a binding-defective protein that is also partly destabilised (a lower steady-state level, a smaller folded fraction, or both), with the folded state unmeasured. Both are compatible with: present protein in two matrices; the unchanged transcript (so any lowering is post-transcriptional); the near-total loss of PPxY recovery.

What each datum does to the two readings:
* The input lane (about 0.70, rank order, n = 2) leans toward R-B and does not establish it: two lanes per genotype, one blot, film on a JPEG, a mixed-anatomy lysate. Judgement: if a quantified, replicated Western were run on the knock-in cerebellum, I would give 0.55 that it shows a mutant level below 0.8 of wild type (JUDGEMENT, labelled; the direction is the same in both lane pairs against an even control, the size is not estimable).
* The ddG +2.81 (MODEL) leans toward R-B in the abstract and is of no weight in this repository: the census shows the predictor inverted over the measured variants, and P47 is partially exposed (relSASA 0.286, beta-strand, DL-MECH-037), which the same file uses to argue a functional rather than structural lesion. That argument is itself an inference from burial and the "normal" blot, so it is not independent of the datum under question.
* General WW-domain biophysics (small, marginally stable domains; a conserved C-terminal proline packing against the first tryptophan) would make R-B plausible. That is background knowledge that I did not check against a source here and that is not in the repository (a grep of the analysis files found none): ASSUMED, and I use it only to say that "folded state unmeasured" is a live question, not to put weight on R-B.
* A destabilised WW1 module in a multi-domain protein need not lower the whole-protein level: the SDR domain may fold independently and quality control may not remove a partially unfolded module. So even a true folded-fraction loss can sit beside a near-wild-type Western (INFERRED, mechanism level).

**What would separate them (named, not run; nothing here is a request to spend):**
| Measurement | R-A predicts | R-B predicts |
|---|---|---|
| Quantified replicated Western of knock-in cerebellum and a second region, with a linear-range loading series | mutant/wild-type about 1 | below 1 at the sizes of the lane (0.7) or lower |
| Limited proteolysis or thermal-shift of the WW1 module (purified or cell-expressed construct 16 to 50; the census notes it existed in 2022) | the same melting behaviour as wild type with a lost binding groove | a lower melting temperature or faster proteolysis |
| Chase (cycloheximide) in knock-in fibroblasts or cultured cells, with a proteasome or lysosome inhibitor arm | wild-type half-life | shorter half-life, rescued by an inhibitor |
| Peptide binding of a stabilised construct (a stabilised variant that keeps the binding groove) | no recovery of binding | partial recovery of binding |
Each row stands alone; the first is the cheapest and is a Western in a laboratory that already holds the line.

## 3. Consequences for scope
* **Proteostasis-directed lever (R4 section 2).** R4 says it applies "only where the protein is destabilised, not absent from transcript" and lists no WWOX arm. For P47T the premise "destabilised" is neither shown nor excluded: stability was never tested. So P47T is **not out of scope** for the class; it is **not in scope on evidence** either. The class stays "conditional, no WWOX arm held", and P47T enters the table as an unmeasured candidate with the first row of the table in section 2 as its decisive measurement.
* **A limit on the lever even if R-B holds:** the binding recovery is near-total loss (about 3 to 13% or less). If the groove side chain, not the fold, carries the loss, a lever that raises the amount or folded fraction restores nothing. If the fold carries it, the last row of the table above is the test. So a proteostasis lever for P47T has a second unmeasured step after the first; it is a hypothesis with two named steps, not a lead.
* **What the P47T knock-in arm speaks for:** "binding loss with protein present", on the pull-down and the epilepsy, neuroinflammation and ataxia phenotype. It does not speak for "wild-type-level protein".
* **What does not move:** the burial-versus-severity argument and the retraction of the P47T to Q230P read-across stand (protein present against not detected is the decisive difference; the proteostasis_rationale file line 89 already says "present (not quantified)"). The G372R undecided row is untouched.
* **Circuit levers are unaffected:** this change is neutral for them (I1, I13, Kv7, sodium and the rest): the slice readouts do not know the variant.
