## 2 · Chain: the Gabrd fall and the reactive axis (NEW COMBINATION; replaces Chain 1 of DREAM-1)
* **Sources:** P47T per-animal composition test (bulk, aged: Gabrd tracks the reactivity axis); S-line hippocampal bulk (-1.67) and nuclei (excitatory -1.40, dentate -1.56, n = 1 per arm); seizure models: bulk hippocampus reproduces most of the panel (Gabrd -0.40 at 14 d, -1.01 rat 84 d), sorted adult neurons do not (-0.35).
* **Relation:** two different lines carry the same transcript fall with different structure: in the S-line it is present in neurons (not composition alone) in a region that does not burst; in P47T it is bulk and tracks reactivity. A transcript fall of this kind in neurons can come from loss of a WWOX function in neurons, or from a developmental or non-cell-autonomous change of the neuronal state; a seizure state in the adult produces a small neuronal fall and a large bulk one.
* **Competing explanations:** (a) neuron-intrinsic WWOX-dependent regulation (supported at P17 hippocampus by the nuclei); (b) non-cell-autonomous (oligodendrocyte-lineage share 22.5% to 7.8%, astrocyte share up, in the same nuclei: the tissue differs and neurons may respond to it); (c) activity in vivo (not excluded: slice quiescence is not animal quiescence); (d) transcript without receptor or current.
* **Discriminating measurement:** a second animal per arm; nuclei from the neocortex of the same line (where bursts occur); neurons from a seizure model at P17 or the S-line nuclei before the first seizure; the tonic current with the THIP reserve (laboratory).
* **Consumer:** I13 status line (done), I3 update (done), I20 R2 (the 'reactive cell-state' rival now has a dataset against it for the S-line and none against it for P47T).
