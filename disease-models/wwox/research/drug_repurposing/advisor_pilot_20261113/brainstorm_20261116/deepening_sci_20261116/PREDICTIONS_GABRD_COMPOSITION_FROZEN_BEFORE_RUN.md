# Does the Gabrd fall in P47T track composition or cell-class scores? Predictions written BEFORE the per-animal run (SCI, 2026-11-16; composition rival R2 of I20; ADV's addition of interneuron-class scores; hypothesis level; not medical advice)
Data: GSE215425_processed_data.csv.gz (public GEO supplementary, one GET with a generic client, sha256 d654f6bdd2a4ac5b3e24741f16962503a711575c72aa92a2c729a7a0cf04c18c, identical to the file IT12 used; stored under `files/deepening_downloads/gse215425/`, gitignored): normalised counts per animal, forebrain regions PFC, HPC, CTX (5 wild type v 5 P47T/P47T; WT 3 male 2 female, P47T 2 male 3 female) and cerebellum CB (4 v 3). Value analysed: log2(count + 1).
Scores per animal (mean of log2(count + 1) of the genes): neuron (Snap25, Rbfox3, Syt1, Stmn2, Slc17a7), astrocyte (Aqp4, Slc1a3, Aldh1l1, Sox9, Gja1), microglia (Aif1, Cx3cr1, P2ry12, Tmem119), oligodendrocyte (Mbp, Plp1, Mog, Mobp); single genes: Lamp5, Sst, Cck, Pvalb, Vip, Npy, Ndnf, Gfap.
Analysis (fixed now): (a) Gabrd log2 fold, P47T minus wild type, by OLS with sex as covariate (and without), per region. (b) Adjusted genotype coefficient: Gabrd ~ genotype + sex + score, for each score alone; shrinkage = 1 - (adjusted / unadjusted coefficient). (c) Animal-level tracking: residuals of Gabrd and of each score after regressing on genotype and sex (10 animals, 7 degrees of freedom left after three parameters), Pearson r and a permutation p (10,000 draws, seed 1). The test can detect only |r| above about 0.63 at n = 10: stated, not hidden.
Predictions:
P1. Unadjusted genotype effect on Gabrd: cortex (CTX) about -0.8 log2 and prefrontal cortex about -0.8 (the table's values -0.80, -0.82); with the sex covariate within 0.15 of those.
P2. No single class or composition score absorbs the effect: shrinkage below 50% for neuron, astrocyte, microglia, oligodendrocyte, Gfap, Pvalb, Vip, Sst, Npy, Ndnf and Cck; for Lamp5 (which falls in P47T) shrinkage between 20% and 60% (the one score that could matter).
P3. Residual tracking: all |r| below 0.63 and permutation p above 0.05 (no animal-level correlation detectable at this n), except possibly Lamp5.
P4. Cerebellum: genotype effect on Gabrd within +/- 0.3 log2 of zero.
What each outcome changes: shrinkage of 50% or more by the neuron or astrocyte score, or a tight tracking, supports composition (R2) and removes the transcript support for I13; shrinkage below 50% everywhere with Lamp5 as the largest supports a cell-state change within neurogliaform-type cells as a competing reading; neither can speak for the tonic CURRENT, which stays the decisive measurement.
