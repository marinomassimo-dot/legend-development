# Candidate [4b], per-neuron Gabrd and nuclear composition in the S-line hippocampus (GSE158684, single-nucleus RNA-seq): rule, classes, predictions, written and hashed BEFORE any matrix of that series is downloaded (SCI, 2026-11-16; non-canonical; hypothesis level; mechanism level; not medical advice)

Written for: ADV, HE, IT. Why: `RESULT_CAUSE_OR_CONSEQUENCE_SCI.md` named as the discriminating next test a neuron-resolved Gabrd measurement in the S-line. A public single-nucleus series of exactly that line exists: GSE158684, hippocampal nuclei from S-Control and S-KO at P17, 4 + 4 samples, 10x Genomics 3' v3, 3,182 control and 2,920 KO nuclei after quality control (series metadata only; I have read the series header, the sample table and the file names, and no matrix, barcode or feature file). The same laboratory and animals' family as GSE158681/2, which supply the S-line bulk values used so far (HELD). Labels: HELD, MODEL, INFERRED, ASSUMED.

## Question
In the S-line (Synapsin-Cre neuronal Wwox deletion, P17), does Gabrd fall inside neurons, and does the nuclear composition of the hippocampus differ between genotypes? The bulk S-line values (Gabrd -1.67 log2 in hippocampus) cannot separate a per-neuron change from a change in the mix of cells; the seizure-model neuronal reference is -0.35 (sorted NeuN+ nuclei, 3 months, GSE330624).

## Classification of nuclei (fixed now; marker-set argmax, no clustering, no integration)
Counts are normalised per nucleus to counts per 10,000 and log1p-transformed. Marker sets (mouse symbols): excitatory neuron: Slc17a7, Slc17a6, Camk2a; inhibitory neuron: Gad1, Gad2, Slc32a1; astrocyte: Aqp4, Gja1, Slc1a2, Aldh1l1; oligodendrocyte lineage: Mbp, Plp1, Mog, Mobp; microglia: Cx3cr1, P2ry12, Csf1r. Score of a set = mean of the transformed values of its genes present in the feature file; a nucleus takes the class of the highest score if that score is above 0, otherwise 'unassigned'. Dentate granule subset (secondary): excitatory nuclei with Prox1 above 0. 'Neuron' = excitatory plus inhibitory. A gene absent from a feature file is reported as absent, not imputed.
Genotype is taken from the file (ctrl or ko). **Sample identity:** the matrices were produced with an aggregation step, so the barcodes may carry a library-index suffix (-1 to -4). If the barcode suffixes give four groups per genotype, the unit of analysis is the sample (pseudobulk: sum of counts in a class per sample, counts per million, log2(CPM + 1), 4 v 4, bootstrap over samples, 10,000 draws, seed 1). If they do not, the unit is the nucleus and every interval is labelled **pseudo-replicated** (it ignores animal-to-animal variance) and no statement of significance is made.

## Readouts and registered predictions
R1. **Gabrd, excitatory neurons, C = log2 fold KO over Control (pseudobulk or pooled).** Bins and my probabilities: C at or below -1.0: 0.35 (a per-neuron effect of the genotype, reading b); between -1.0 and -0.4: 0.40 (partly per-neuron, partly composition); above -0.4: 0.25 (the bulk fall is composition, reading a). Gabrd is expressed in few nuclei: the fraction of excitatory nuclei with any Gabrd count is reported with C; if fewer than 30 excitatory nuclei per genotype express it, R1 is reported as 'not readable (detection)'.
R2. **Composition:** the share of oligodendrocyte-lineage nuclei among all assigned nuclei is lower in KO than in Control (probability 0.70; the series' own abstract reports reduced oligodendrocyte maturation, HELD); the share of astrocyte nuclei is higher in KO (probability 0.55).
R3. **Reactive and peptide genes:** Gfap in astrocyte nuclei C above +0.4 (probability 0.6); Npy in inhibitory nuclei C above +0.4 (probability 0.6).
R4. The same C for the other panel genes (the 25 of `PREREGISTRATION_WW1_PANEL.md`) in the neuron class that expresses them most, reported beside the bulk S-line value; no further prediction registered.

## What each outcome changes
R1 at or below -1.0 with readable detection: a per-neuron Gabrd fall exists in the S-line that a chronic seizure state does not produce at that size; I13 and I20 may state it as a genotype-associated neuronal change (still an mRNA change at P17, not a receptor or current measurement). R1 above -0.4: the S-line bulk Gabrd fall is largely a change in tissue composition, and the delta-subunit lever argument loses its WWOX-specific transcript footprint. The middle bin: both contribute, size undetermined. R2 shows whether composition differs at all in this line.

## Limits stated before the data
Marker-set argmax is cruder than clustering and will misassign nuclei with ambient RNA; nuclear RNA captures pre-spliced transcripts (the series processed with full genes as exons); Gabrd is lowly expressed and 10x sparse; two genotypes' matrices were integrated by the depositors for their own analysis but the raw matrices are separate; P17 only; the neuronal deletion by Synapsin-Cre is not complete across all neurons, so the 'KO' neuron class is a mixture of deleted and non-deleted nuclei (ASSUMED), which shrinks any per-neuron effect.
