# I16 astroglial K+ buffering: predictions written BEFORE the genotype-axis run (SCI, 2026-11-16; hypothesis generator, not evidence)
Model: the frozen I1 oscillator plus one variable, extracellular K+ above the bath, K: dK/dt = kappa_K u - K g_glia / tau_K (kappa_K 3 mM/s of activity, tau_K 4 s, ASSUMED), added to the drive as beta_K (K + K_bath) (beta_K 0.02 x-units per mM, ASSUMED; 1 mM is a few mV). g_glia is the astroglial clearance capacity (1 = the fitted knockout). File `rebound_oscillator_k.py` (a separate file; the frozen model is unchanged). A first look at the one-parameter dependence (seed 5, 8 networks of 600 s, written before this file): rate per minute 2.68, 2.19, 2.12, 1.71 for g_glia 0.5, 1, 2, 4 (a weak knob: 1.6-fold over an 8-fold range).
Genotype axis (as in the S-HT test): rebound J_T x (1 - 0.48 lam), excitatory J_A and J_N x (1 - 0.2 lam), g_glia = 1 + t lam with t in {1, 3, 9, 19} (wild type 2, 4, 10, 20 times the knockout's clearance), S-HT at lam 0.5; sigma 0.07; 100 slices; at least 1 burst in 600 s. Success: S-KO >= 70%, S-HT 5-35%, S-WT <= 10%.
R1. No t in the scan satisfies the criterion (a graded K+ clearance is a weak knob; S-WT stays above 10% or S-HT above 35%).
R2. Median burst duration of bursting slices rises when clearance falls: duration at g_glia 0.5 at least 10% longer than at g_glia 1 (K+ accumulated in the burst sustains it); at g_glia 4 within 10% of g_glia 1.
R3. Median interval changes by less than 25% between g_glia 0.5 and 4.
R4. A bath K+ challenge (K_bath increment) shifts the 50%-incidence threshold of each genotype by the same amount in resting-drive units (it enters as a drive term), so it does not separate the K+ buffering idea from any other graded factor on the K+ axis alone; what separates it is the dependence of the threshold on the activity level (the clearance acts on K+ released by activity), which the model has as a K accumulation term only.
What each outcome changes: R1 true -> the idea is not a model-level candidate for the incidence gradient (not killed: the test that could kill it is the Ba2+/gap-junction-sensitive current and the K+ transient, D5); R1 false -> I16 enters the list of graded components.
