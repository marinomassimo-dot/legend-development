## The candidate readouts of the design
(a) guide enrichment in starved Wwox-/- MEFs over wild type (selection); (b) lactate output or acidification in the null cells with and without the hit (confirmation); (c) the registered predictions: an Ldh or glycolysis-entry hit (0.25), all top-10 hits generic (0.35), no gene significant (0.30).

## Where the chain breaks, in order
1. **Step 1 (class label) passes only for the metabolic hits.** An Ldha, Ldhb or glycolysis-entry gene has a pharmacology class (a label, not an agent); a generic death-pathway hit has one too, but it is the class of a general survival lever, and the design already expects those (0.35). Any other interaction hit is a target without a ligand class: it stops here with 'a ligand class appears'.
2. **Step 2 (target in neurons of the bursting region) is UNREAD, not failed, for the metabolic genes.** My single-nucleus panels ([4b], [4c], [4d]) do not carry Ldha, Ldhb, Pkm, Hk1 or Slc2a1; I have not read them and I do not claim a result. These genes are broadly expressed, so I expect step 2 to pass trivially, which is exactly why it is uninformative for them: passing step 2 does not make the MEF phenotype neuronal. For a non-metabolic hit with MEF-only expression the first failure is the one IT and I named: parked as 'target-present trigger'.
3. **Step 3 (a held readout that the hit can move, with a sign) is where it breaks first for the hit the design itself expects.** (i) **No held neuronal metabolic readout exists in Wwox.** The only lactate measurement is a relative LC-MS intensity in a fly knockdown x Abeta42 background (tier 3; one origin chain with the MEF glycolytic shift). In slices the held readouts are burst incidence and rate, the intrinsic profile, the tonic current and the K+/Mg2+ ladders; none is metabolic. (ii) **The sign is not fixed even in the model.** The only metabolic-type lever my model carries is the resource class (mapping ASSUMED): slowing depletion lengthens the burst 2.5 times and shortens the interval 17%; so a lever that raises available resource would lengthen bursts, and one that lowers it would shorten them. Whether Ldh lowering raises or lowers the resource that sustains a burst is unmeasured; a hit could therefore be 'a lead for survival' and the model-conditional sign at step 3 stays two-sided. The qualification table (`TRANSFER_QUALIFICATION_SCI_20261117.tsv`, rows R16, R17c) records this: the substrate cells read 'duration first', and the MEF row is tier 3 with sign not entitled.
4. **Step 4** (the paired slice experiment at two strengths in S-KO, S-HT and wild type) is not reached for any hit today, and for a metabolic hit it also lacks an agent class that is cell-permeant in slices; I name no agent.

**Verdict of the attack: the chain breaks first at step 3 for the Ldh-type hit (no held neuronal readout and a two-sided model sign), and at step 2 for MEF-only hits. The screen can return a lead for a target, not for a lever, which IT already wrote as the first failure to expect.**

## Attacks on the design itself (not on the bridge)
* **Selection strength differs between genotypes.** Wild-type MEFs pass starvation, the null cells fail; a gene whose loss helps survival will enrich more where survival is lower. Falsifier (b) rejects a hit that enriches equally in wild type, but a **generic survival gene with an amplified effect size in the null cells passes as an interaction**. Rival to a Wwox-specific suppressor: dynamic range. Repair on paper: set the starvation duration per genotype to matched kill, or fit the enrichment of known generic survival genes across both genotypes and call a hit only a residual off that line. Without it, the design's 0.35 (all generic) is probably too low.
* **The three registered probabilities are not a partition.** 0.25 + 0.35 + 0.30 = 0.90, and an Ldh hit can co-occur with generic ones; score each as a separate binary statement and say so before any run.
* **Clonal and compensatory drift of knockout MEFs.** A phenotype of a knockout line can differ from that of acute deletion: a rival the positive control (re-expression) does not exclude. A transfer-fail measurement: acute deletion in matched cells gives the same survival fall.
* **One source for the selection phenotype** (one group, Su 2026); replication in a second null line is not in the design.

## What this changes
* For ADV: I21 as designed produces, at best, targets with a MEF phenotype. The route to a lever needs one named missing item, a neuronal metabolic readout in Wwox-null slices (trigger: such a readout exists), not more screen replicates.
* For IT: add the matched-selection repair and the binary scoring of the three predictions before a run is ever funded; no agent or dose is implied.
