## Answer first
1. **Fidelity of the quotes: clean.** Every locator fragment in all 170 exposure rows was checked mechanically against the source file it names (`i2_locator_check.py`, output `i2_locator_check.tsv`; verbatim substring after whitespace, hyphen and quote normalisation; markup stripped for XML and HTML sources only; fragments are the pieces between the extractor's ellipsis marks): **311 of 311 fragments are present in the held files**. The remaining **12 fragments belong to two abstract-only reports (PMID 38407561 and 41442931) whose abstract files are not in `files/fulltext/` under the names given; I checked them against the PubMed abstracts (according to PubMed; DOIs [10.1002/ajmg.a.63575](https://doi.org/10.1002/ajmg.a.63575) and [10.1016/j.pediatrneurol.2025.12.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pediatrneurol.2025.12.001)) and all 12 occur there. So 323 of 323 quoted fragments exist; the extraction has no invented text.
2. **Your headline stands: the held reports cannot answer the segregation question.** I reproduce the counts: 50 exposure rows from 17 children have a specific semiology at exposure and a stated outcome; by group and attribution: spasm group single-agent rows 20 (resolved 10, partial 5, none 5) and not-attributable rows 5 (none); rebound group (myoclonic, absence-like) single-agent rows **2** (both partial) and not-attributable rows 8 (resolved 2, none 6); other group 6 single-agent and 9 not-attributable. A contrast between groups inside one class cannot be made; I agree with 'not answerable at class level'.
3. **Three things in the extraction that bias any class comparison made from it (none changes your headline; each should be stated next to every share).** (a) **Attribution follows success.** Over all rows with a stated outcome: single-agent rows 33, of which resolved 13, partial 13, none 7 (benefit 26 of 33 = 79%); not-attributable rows 30, of which resolved 5, partial 0, none 25 (benefit 5 of 30 = 17%). A drug added just before a good result is coded as single-agent ("Vigabatrin was added and the spasms resolved"); the failures arrive as lists ("refractory to multiple ASMs, including ...") and are coded not-attributable and so leave every class share. Case-report bias (rule 6 of the rubric) is therefore *compounded* by the attribution rule; class shares from single-agent rows alone over-state benefit by construction. Report the not-attributable counts beside each share (you do) and add the benefit rate by attribution (this table). (b) **One sentence feeds many rows.** Riva 2022's single sentence "Several anti-seizure medications (ASM) were ineffective (i.e., valproate, vigabatrin, clonazepam, clobazam, levetiracetam, rufinamide, and CBD oil)" creates seven 'none' rows with one semiology ('tonic') taken from another sentence; the rows are not independent, as you state, and the unit for any later contrast is the child (17) not the row (50). (c) **Transient 'resolved'.** Rows coded resolved include 'completely controlled for a [short] month, however, epileptic spasms and hypsarrhythmia subsequently reappeared' (28721938, vigabatrin) and 'the spasms resolved, however, he soon developed other seizure types' (30361190); the rubric has no durable/transient field, so 'resolved' mixes the two.
4. **Class assignment decides two cells: zonisamide and topiramate.** The rule 'first listed in the closed list' puts both in `sodium_channel`. Zonisamide has a T-type action as well (my readiness sheet for the class: 'T-type blocker, not T-type selective'; topiramate is a multi-mechanism agent). The only resolved sodium-channel row in the spasm group is zonisamide in Nagarajan 2023 Table 4, WWOX child 39 (FIHT, response with ZON; single-agent); the sodium-channel 'rebound, 1 resolved' is topiramate in a polytherapy row. Re-assigning zonisamide to the T-type class (or reporting both) removes the only sodium-channel spasm success and adds a T-type spasm success: a sensitivity line the dossier needs, because your D7 contradiction ("sodium-channel agents with resolved rows in both groups suggest semiology is not the only sorter") rests on exactly these two rows.
