## What this does and does not say
* **Against composition alone for the S-line Gabrd fall.** The neuron-resolved fall (-1.40, dentate -1.56) is about the size of the bulk fall (-1.67) while the share of excitatory nuclei is unchanged and the shifts in composition are in glial classes. A composition-only account of the S-line bulk Gabrd is therefore not supported at this n; this does not say the fall is cell-autonomous (it can still be a non-cell-autonomous consequence in neurons of activity, hyperexcitability or myelination changes) and it is not a statement about P47T, whose bulk Gabrd fall tracked the reactivity axis in the per-animal composition test (HELD).
* **Seizure reference.** The sorted-neuron seizure value (-0.35, adult, 3 months after kainate, GSE330624) is much smaller than the S-line neuronal value. Not a decomposition: the S-line nuclei are P17, when spontaneous slice bursts already occur in this line (incidence 36 of 42 slices, HELD), so a consequence-of-activity reading in neurons at P17 stays open, and age differs between the two series.
* **Unit and replication.** n = 1 per arm; a difference between the two pools is also a difference between two animals (or two pooled samples). The intervals understate uncertainty. Every statement above is 'one dataset of one animal or pool per arm shows', and no dossier line should be moved beyond that.
* **Detection.** Gabrd is detected in few nuclei; the log-fold is driven largely by the fraction of nuclei with any count (9.7% against 3.3%). It is an mRNA measurement; it says nothing about receptor protein or tonic current.
* **Classification.** Marker-set argmax, not clustering: nuclei with ambient RNA are misassigned; unassigned 0.9% and 3.9%.
