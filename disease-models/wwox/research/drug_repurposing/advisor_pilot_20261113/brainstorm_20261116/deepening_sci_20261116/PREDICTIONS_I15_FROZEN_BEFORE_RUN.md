Primary endpoint of I15: the drive increment at which 50% of slices burst, per genotype. In the frozen oscillator a graded K+ challenge enters as an increase of the resting-drive term, b0 -> b0 + dx (dx >= 0, depolarising; the conversion to mM of K+ is ASSUMED and not made here), and a graded Mg2+ challenge as a multiplier m >= 1 on the NMDAR weight J_N (lower Mg2+ unblocks more current; the conversion to mM is ASSUMED and not made here). Per genotype (S-KO lam 0, S-HT lam 0.5, S-WT lam 1) and per graded component of the earlier tests: tonic weight (t 0.8), steady chloride (t 0.3), phasic inhibition (t 2.0), NMDAR sustainer (t 0.4, which failed the incidence test and is kept as the contrast); rebound weight x (1 - 0.48 lam) and excitatory weights x (1 - 0.2 lam) graded in all; sigma 0.07; 60 slices per point; incidence = at least 1 burst in 600 s.
Quantities: dx50 (K+) and m50 (Mg2+): the smallest scanned value at which incidence reaches 50% (linear interpolation).
Predictions:
P1. K+ axis, tonic, chloride and phasic variants: dx50(S-KO) < dx50(S-HT) < dx50(S-WT) strictly, with dx50(S-KO) = 0 (S-KO already above 50%); dx50(S-WT) between 0.03 and 0.15.
P2. NMDAR-sustainer variant: dx50(S-HT) - dx50(S-KO) smaller than 0.01 (S-HT behaves as S-KO, the step near wild type).
P3. Mg2+ axis, all variants: m50(S-KO) = 1 (already above 50%), m50(S-HT) <= m50(S-WT); the wild type needs a larger NMDAR gain than the heterozygote.
P4. The K+ axis alone does not separate the inhibition-side variants: the spread of dx50(S-WT) among tonic, chloride and phasic variants is within a factor of 2; the Mg2+ axis separates the NMDAR variant from the inhibition-side variants: m50(S-WT) of the NMDAR variant differs from the median of the other three by a factor of at least 1.5 (smaller, because the NMDAR variant has already lowered J_N).
Reading: P1 and P2 fail -> the threshold shift does not discriminate graded from step shapes; P4 holds -> K+ and Mg2+ challenges together discriminate, as argued in the meeting reply; P4 fails -> a single challenge is as good as two for the model.
