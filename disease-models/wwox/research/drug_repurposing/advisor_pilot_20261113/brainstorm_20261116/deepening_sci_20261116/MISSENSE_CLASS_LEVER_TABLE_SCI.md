# Missense mechanism classes and the lever classes each would make pertinent (SCI, 2026-11-16; non-canonical; hypothesis level; mechanism level; no agent recommended; not medical advice)

Written for: ADV (RP-001 with RP-015), IT (children per class), HE (pilot P47T arm). Labels: WWOX observation (HELD, a measurement on a WWOX protein, source named) | transfer (an observation on another protein or on another WWOX allele used by analogy) | assumption (ASSUMED, not checked against a source here) | model (MODEL, a computed prediction; a hypothesis generator, not evidence). Sources read before writing: `analysis/missense_proteostasis_matrix_20260921.md`, `missense_rescue_methodology_census_20260921.md`, `missense_splice_reclassification_risk_20260921.md`, `wwox_missense_stability_census_20260922.md`, and my `P4_MISSENSE_LITERATURE_TABLE_SCI.md`; children from IT's `P4_step1_missense_variants.tsv`.

## Answer first
1. **The lever should be keyed to the mechanism class, not to "missense", and the held evidence distinguishes four classes plus one that hides under the word missense.** (A) protein lost with transcript normal (Q230P is the only member with a measurement); (B) protein present, one ligand-binding function lost (P47T is the only member with measurements); (C) catalytic-site class (no WWOX measurement of any member; the one computed claim, C299R acting through catalysis, was retracted by the repository's own second audit); (D) computed-tolerant (no measurement); and (E) **a missense label that may be an RNA-level change**: G137E is the first nucleotide of exon 5 (c.410G>A), and c.406A>G, probably p.Ile136Val, carries a published, untested in-silico splice flag (splice-risk audit, abstract and exon map held). Eight further variants have no observation and are not assigned.
2. **The two measured classes call for opposite things.** For B the protein is already at wild-type amounts, so a proteostasis-directed class has nothing to restore; the relevant levers are downstream circuit components (the tonic and phasic GABA-A components, I13 and I1), on the P47T knock-in evidence. For A, a proteostasis-directed class is conditional on one unmeasured step: **function per molecule at restored abundance has never been measured on any WWOX missense protein** (matrix row 4), and the same paper's P282A is a demonstrated "stable but inert" substitution. If class A protein is also absent in neurons, the downstream circuit levers apply to it as they do to a null genotype, with the S-line neuronal knockout, not P47T, as the closest held model line.
3. **The pilot's P47T arm speaks for class B only, on direct evidence.** It says nothing about class A unless Q230P is shown protein-null in neurons, and nothing about C, D or E.
4. **Counts of children are counts of variant carriers on IT's list, not of unique children** (a child with two missense variants can appear twice, and most carry a second allele that is not a missense): class A 7 of the 42 and 4 of the 37 (Q230P); B 3 and 0 (P47T, homozygous); E 0 and 3 (G137E 2, c.406A>G 1); D 0 and 1 (T12M, computed tolerant); not assigned 4 and 10 (E17K, S318L, H150P, K200E;T358I, S304Y, S304F, L239R, A363P). Totals 14 and 18.

## Distinctions kept apart
Every lever label above is a **WWOX-rationale** statement (a mechanism makes a lever class pertinent). **No drug x genotype interaction is demonstrated for any class**, no general anticonvulsant activity is argued, and no development benefit is argued. "Pertinent" means the mechanism would make the class worth testing in that setting, not that it works.

## Corrections and cross-checks from reading the held files (dated)
* c.406A>G as probably p.Ile136Val (my P4 note) was already in the repository: the splice-risk audit carries it as p.(Ile136Val), with its own paper's untested in-silico splice flag; my inference by codon arithmetic agrees, and it adds the splice question, not a new variant.
* PMID 39039877 (Wang 2024, the Peking cohort) is held at abstract level in the registry (paper registry and tracking log); IT's closed-access status concerns the full text. It adds six novel missense alleles named in its abstract (A149T, R156S, L186V, H263R, S304Y, M326R), none yet in the table.
* G137E sits at a junction by the held exon map; the P4 table's "cofactor-binding region" remark for G137 stays an ASSUMED label, and the junction position is the more specific reason it is in class E.
