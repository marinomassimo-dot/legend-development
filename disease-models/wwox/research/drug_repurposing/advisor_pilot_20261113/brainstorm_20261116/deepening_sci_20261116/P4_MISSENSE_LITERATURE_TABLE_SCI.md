Labels: HELD (a file or verbatim quote already in the repository, with the file), PUBMED (title/abstract retrieved from PubMed in this session; abstract = discovery input, not a reading), INFERRED (arithmetic or reasoning from held material), ASSUMED (general knowledge, not checked here), MODEL (a computed prediction, a hypothesis generator, not evidence).

## Answer first
1. **Of IT's 13 distinct missense variants, 11 have no experimental observation of any kind in WWOX.** Their evidence is clinical (a case, a cohort row) plus, for some, an in-silico class stated by the report. Only two have a protein-level observation, and **they point in opposite directions**: p.Gln230Pro (Q230P) shows normal transcript and **no detectable protein** in patient fibroblasts (Johannsen 2018); p.Pro47Thr (P47T) shows **protein present at wild-type amounts** in patient fibroblasts and in the knock-in mouse cerebellum, with a stated loss of PPxY binding (Mallaret 2014; Hussain 2023). So "missense = hypomorph" has no support as a class statement: one of the two measured alleles looks protein-null at the abundance endpoint, the other keeps the protein and loses an interaction.
2. **The repository's computed ddG does not predict either observation.** Held ddG: P47T +2.81 (highest of the classified set) with normal protein; Q230P +1.51 with absent protein. The census `analysis/wwox_missense_stability_census_20260922.md` (section 6.1) already reports this inversion over its six measured variants; this table adds no new measurement to it.
3. **Three corrections to the list, none changing a count of children:**
   (a) **S304Y: the report's own nucleotide position is wrong, not the protein change.** c.911 is codon 304, position 2, and the held UniProt Q9NZC7 sequence (AlphaFold file `analysis/data/WWOX_Q9NZC7_AlphaFold.pdb`) has Ser at 304: TCx to TAx gives Ser to Tyr, consistent. c.991 is codon 331, position 1, where the held sequence has Ile: c.991C>A cannot be Ser304Tyr (INFERRED by codon arithmetic). Use c.911C>A.
   (b) **Q230 is glutamine.** The held sequence has Gln at 230 (context TFQVN) and c.689A>C is codon 230 position 2 (CAG to CCG, Gln to Pro). The PubMed abstract of Johannsen 2018 says Gln230; IT's keyword pass over the report text quotes "glycine 230", so the report's body text and its abstract disagree with each other; the residue identity does not depend on either (INFERRED). I have not read the body (no open full text is held).
   (c) **c.406A>G is probably p.Ile136Val.** Codon 136 position 1; the held residue 136 is Ile (context SGIGF, next to G137); A>G at position 1 of any Ile codon (ATT, ATC, ATA) gives Val (INFERRED; conditional on the report using the same transcript as UniProt Q9NZC7; the report names no amino acid).
4. **Two things IT should check on the list:** (i) **PMID 39039877** (Wang 2024, Chinese Journal of Pediatrics; 12 children, all compound heterozygous) lists p.Ser304Tyr among its novel variants; it is a different centre from PMID 39416860 (Feng 2024) and is not among IT's reports for S304Y. If it is not in the 25-report set, S304Y has one more report and the children count may change. (ii) Two PubMed variant strings **belong to other genes**: E17K (AKT1/AKT2, six case reports and a mouse paper in the hits I retrieved: PMIDs 42871118, 42788321, 41368924, 41144131, 40894918, 40785978, 38344362) and G137E (ESRRG, PMID 41265451). Neither is WWOX. Same trap as GTPBP3 Q230P in the census. A variant-string search in this gene needs the gene name AND the three-letter residue form (the census found `WWOX AND Q230P` returns nothing for the paper that is about it).
5. **What would change a conclusion.** Q230P protein status in a neuron-relevant matrix with degradation routes opened (the census section 8 already specifies the cheapest experiment; I do not restate it). If Q230P protein is absent in neurons, it belongs with the protein-null class and the knockout-based panel of I20 applies to it; if protein is present, the fibroblast result is a detection or matrix effect and the class assignment reopens. P47T is the only allele of the 13 that sits in the "protein retained, interaction lost" class on direct evidence.

## What this means for the arms (MODEL transfer, labelled)
The pilot arms and I20 are weight-level and genotype-class-level; no arm depends on a single missense allele. The class assignment does matter in one place: **I20's "copied in a line that keeps the protein" classification applies on direct evidence only to P47T**. Q230P would enter the protein-null class if the fibroblast result holds in neurons. A claim that a drug lever "fits the missense patients" is therefore unsupported for 11 of 13 variants and class-dependent for the other two; it should read "fits the protein-retained class (P47T)" or "fits the protein-null class (Q230P, if confirmed)". This is a distinction in rationale (WWOX), not a drug×genotype interaction (none demonstrated), not general anticonvulsant activity and not a development benefit.
