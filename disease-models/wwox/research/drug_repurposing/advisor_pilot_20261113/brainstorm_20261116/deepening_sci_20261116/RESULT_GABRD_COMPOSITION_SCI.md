## Answer first
**The composition rival is not excluded; at the animal level the Gabrd fall is entangled with a reactivity axis (Gfap, Npy, Lamp5), so the transcript support for the tonic-deficit idea (I13) is weaker than my earlier statement.** Three of four frozen predictions held in part, two failed:
* **P1 holds:** genotype effect on Gabrd -0.89 log2 in CTX (-0.89 with a sex covariate), -0.74 in PFC (-0.75), -1.02 in HPC (-1.04): the table's values within 0.15.
* **P4 holds:** cerebellum -0.05 (-0.06 with sex): flat, as the table said.
* **P2 FAILS in part:** no single score absorbs the effect for neuron (-4%, -21%, -1% shrinkage in CTX, PFC, HPC), astrocyte (-2%, +9%, -3%), microglia, Pvalb, Vip, Gfap in CTX and PFC (10%, 28%); but adjusting for **Npy** shrinks the genotype coefficient by 74% in CTX and 68% in HPC, for **oligodendrocyte** by 76% in PFC, and for **Lamp5** by 54% in CTX, 77% in HPC and reverses it in PFC (+162%). Caution: Npy (genotype effect +1.51 log2), Lamp5 (-0.69) and Gfap are close to proxies of genotype in a 5 against 5 design, so a large shrinkage is partly mechanical (collinearity) and is not evidence of mediation by itself.
* **P3 FAILS:** after removing genotype and sex from both variables (7 degrees of freedom left), Gabrd tracks the scores at animal level: CTX Npy r -0.80 (permutation p 0.004), Gfap -0.75 (0.010); PFC oligodendrocyte +0.86 (0.004), Lamp5 +0.83 (0.007), Pvalb +0.70 (0.025), Ndnf -0.72 (0.026), Gfap -0.71 (0.030); HPC Lamp5 +0.77 (0.007), Npy -0.79 (0.006), Gfap -0.76 (0.013), Pvalb +0.68 (0.037). That is 12 scores in each of 3 regions (36 tests, strongly correlated with one another), so about 0.4 hits at p < 0.01 are expected by chance and 7 were found; the repeated pattern across regions (animals with more Gfap and Npy have lower Gabrd; animals with more Lamp5 have higher Gabrd) is not noise-like, and it fits the progressive neuroinflammation the P47T paper reports (abstract: elevated astro-microgliosis increasing with age).
**What this does and does not say.** It does not show that a neuron has fewer delta receptors or more: bulk transcript tracks a reactive-state axis, and the animals are 150 to 280 days old with per-animal age not in the deposit (age is a plausible common driver of reactivity and of a fall in Gabrd). It does show that the transcript cannot be used as evidence of a per-cell tonic deficit without the tonic current. **Status change for I13:** transcript direction replicated in two lines, now with an animal-level association to reactivity; tonic current remains the only decisive measurement; the incidence argument is already withdrawn. The cerebellum flat finding (no fall with a thinner granule layer) still argues against a simple neuron-loss composition effect.
