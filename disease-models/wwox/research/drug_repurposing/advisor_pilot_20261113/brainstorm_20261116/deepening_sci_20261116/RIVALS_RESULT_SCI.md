## Answer first
**Tonic inhibition is not uniquely supported by the incidence. The model's discrimination is by direction and steepness of the graded component, not by identity: inhibition-side components (tonic, steady chloride, and phasic inhibition if it changes a lot) succeed; the NMDAR sustainer does not.** The I13 sentence must read: *a steep graded inhibitory component of roughly a third to a half of effective strength, tonic one candidate*. The S-HT arm of the tonic-current experiment stays useful (an intermediate tonic current in S-HT fits the incidence) but it is no longer the model's unique discriminator; the same S-HT arm applied to the sIPSC (phasic) and to resting chloride would separate the candidates.

**Held for S-HT (Breton 2021 Fig. 4 legends):** sIPSC amplitude and frequency distributions differ between S-HT and S-KO (KS p 2.5e-69 and 4.6e-5) as between S-WT and S-KO (1.8e-100 and 6.2e-8); there is no S-HT against S-WT test and no mean value in the text, so the S-HT size cannot be placed between the two; the size of the held S-KO reduction of sIPSC is not in the text read (it is in the figure). mEPSC amplitude is graded in all three pairs (S-WT v S-HT p 2.0e-4; S-WT v S-KO 5.0e-7; S-HT v S-KO 1.3e-6); mEPSC frequency is a step (S-HT v S-KO ns); mEPSC decay time constant is unchanged (5.18 against 4.84 ms), which the authors read as no change in the NMDAR proportion.

**In plain words (for the 20:30Z reading):** steady chloride is the most efficient graded component in this model: a 30% lower steady chloride in the wild type reproduces 0 / 17 / 86% by itself, against a 60 to 100% higher tonic weight or a 100 to 200% higher phasic inhibition. That makes E_GABA minus resting potential per cell in S-HT, S-KO and S-WT a first-rank measurement (it is cheap in the same cells as the tonic and sIPSC readouts), not a side one. Caveat: the chloride effect enters the model through the relative driving force of both tonic and phasic inhibition together, so 'chloride' here stands for 'a graded driving force of GABA-A', which a graded KCC2/NKCC1 balance or a graded resting potential could also supply (the held resting potential is a step, S-HT as S-KO, which argues against a purely resting-potential origin).

## What this changes
* I13: 'tonic inhibition' becomes one of three inhibition-side graded candidates; its chain is unchanged but its model-level claim is reduced to 'a graded inhibitory component of about 30 to 60% in effective strength'. The S-HT experiment should read, in the same cells: the tonic current, the sIPSC (phasic) and the resting chloride (E_GABA minus resting potential); an intermediate S-HT value in the *one* quantity that is graded is the result.
* I6: the chloride route is as supported by incidence as the tonic route (fractional change 0.3), and it has a direct measurement (perforated patch).
* I1: the NMDAR-sustainer graded route is disfavoured by the incidence shape; the mEPSC amplitude gradient (held) is carried in every variant by the excitatory weight at e = 0.2 (unknown size).
* Limits: the mapping from a graded feature to a model weight is assumed linear; the model's knockout overshoots (about 100% against 86%); slice variability is assumed; the incidence curve depends on the 600 s window.
