# COMPOSE rubric for the two-stage brief: pair an outside lever with a held readout and name the one bridge experiment (SCI, 2026-11-17; non-canonical; a draft, untested until a one-outcome target and a budget exist; hypothesis level; not medical advice)

Written for: ADV, HE, IT. Built from two real cases of 2026-11-16/17: (A) the anchored interneuron share of the mixed sodium arm (`RESULT_ANCHORED_INTERNEURON_SCI.md`, brief 13.7) and (B) the missense class table (`MISSENSE_CLASS_LEVER_TABLE_SCI.md`). One line per field; a record is COMPOSED only when every field is filled or marked 'none held'.

| # | Field | Rule (what fills it) | Case A | Case B |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Unit and region, first line** | dataset or preparation, region, age, unit of analysis, animals per arm | slice preparation, neocortex, P13 to P17, weights not concentrations; S-HT counts about 10 per 40 slices | held variants; children are variant carriers, not unique children |
| 2 | **Lever (label)** | a pharmacology class or mechanism, never an agent or dose; the class-to-parameter mapping stated as ASSUMED | sodium-channel class with an interneuron share: sigma x0.9 plus J_I factor | proteostasis-directed classes versus downstream circuit classes, per mechanism class |
| 3 | **Held readout it must move** | a measured number with its source and the sign the lever is expected to give | S-HT burst count and incidence; S-KO rel_bursts; the held phasic fall (sIPSC -52%) | protein level of the variant (Q230P: absent; P47T: wild-type amount) |
| 4 | **The pairing, with columns** | WWOX observation / transfer from another system / assumption / model, each in its own column | held: AP-amplitude falls 25.9% and 10.0%; assumed: coupling k and ratio r; model: the oscillator | held: two protein measurements; transfer: P252A and P282A; assumption: degradation route; model: ddG |
| 5 | **Rival readings that diverge on one observation** | at least one rival; the observation on which they differ; 'the data cannot separate them' stated when so | pre versus post origin of the phasic deficit: mIPSC fraction rho_m | protein-null versus protein-retained class: protein in neurons |
| 6 | **The one bridge experiment** | ONE added condition in cells or preparations that already exist, with its unit; named, not designed in full | mIPSC in TTX in the E_GABA cells (brief 13.7) | protein and half-life of Q230P in carrier cells with lysosome inhibition |

| 8 | **Four distinctions** | WWOX rationale / drug x genotype interaction demonstrated / general anticonvulsant activity / development benefit: which is claimed, which not | WWOX rationale only; no interaction demonstrated | WWOX rationale only |
| 9 | **Predictions before the run** | bins and probabilities hashed before the data are opened; the naive baseline written; losses reported | Q1 to Q7, E9; 5 of 7 and 3 of 7 | none run |
| 10 | **BLOCK-1 and limits** | 'not run' stated; weights not doses; transcript is not a current | not run | not run |

**Acceptance tests (one line each, a reader can apply them without a script):** (a) the first line names region and unit; (b) the lever has no dose and no agent; (c) every number in field 3 has a source line; (d) the four columns of field 4 are not merged; (e) field 6 is one condition in an existing preparation; (f) field 7 names a dossier line that moves under each outcome; (g) field 9 digests precede the data and the naive baseline is written.
**Failure modes seen on these two cases:** an unstated unit (4+4 samples against n = 1); a region carried across (neocortical bursting applied to hippocampus); a bin edge not stated; a transcript read as a current; an equal-block versus selective agent confused (case A: aggravation needs an interneuron effect about 2.5 times the pyramidal one in log terms).
**Not tested:** no one-outcome target exists yet; the rubric has produced no hypothesis; it should be judged by whether a reader other than its author can fill all ten fields for a new lever from held material alone.
