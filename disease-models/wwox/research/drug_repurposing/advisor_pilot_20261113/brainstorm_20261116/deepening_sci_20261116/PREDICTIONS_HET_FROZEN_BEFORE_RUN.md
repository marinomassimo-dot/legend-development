# S-HT held-data test: predictions written BEFORE the run (SCI, 2026-11-16; hypothesis generator, not evidence)
Held target (Breton 2021 Results, files/fulltext/PMID34634460_Breton2021_EPMC_2026-09-27.xml): neocortical slices with spontaneous bursts: S-WT 0 of 11 (0%), S-HT 4 of 23 (17%), S-KO 36 of 42 (86%). Held features: resting potential, input resistance, sag and AP amplitude are a STEP (S-HT as S-KO); rebound is raised in S-KO only (0.96, 1.24 ns, 1.86 mV); mEPSC amplitude is larger in S-KO than in S-WT and S-HT (Fig. 4C, text).
Design (`het_incidence_test.py`, the frozen I1 model unchanged, imported): a genotype axis from knockout (lam 0) to wild type (lam 1). Step features (the resting drive b0) stay at the knockout value for S-HT and are set at lam 1 for S-WT only through nothing (b0 is not moved: it is a step, so S-WT differs from S-HT only in the graded features). Graded features: rebound weight J_T x (1 - 0.48 lam) (held rebound ratio 0.52, ASSUMED proportional to J_T); excitatory weights J_A and J_N x (1 - e lam) with e the unknown fractional difference of mEPSC amplitude (scanned 0.1, 0.2, 0.3); tonic GABA-A weight J_tonic x (1 + t lam) with t unknown (scanned 0, 0.3, 0.6, 1.3 = 1/0.43 - 1, the Gabrd ratio read as a conductance ratio, ASSUMED). S-HT is lam 0.5, S-WT lam 1. Slice-to-slice variability: each of J_T, J_N, J_A, J_tonic multiplied per slice by a log-normal factor (sigma 0.05 or 0.10, ASSUMED). A slice 'bursts' if it has at least 1 burst (duration above 0.1 s) in 600 s after burn-in; 100 slices per genotype and setting.
Predictions (signs and ranges):
P1. With t = 0 (no tonic change) the model cannot reproduce 0% in S-WT together with 17% in S-HT: S-HT incidence above 70% and S-WT above 20% for every e and sigma scanned.
P2. With t = 1.3 and e = 0.2 S-WT is silent (0 to 10%) and S-HT incidence falls between 5% and 60%.
P3. For any setting, S-HT incidence lies strictly between S-WT and S-KO incidence (graded), S-KO above 80%.
P4. A setting that matches all three held incidences (S-KO 70-95%, S-HT 5-35%, S-WT 0-10%) exists only for t >= 0.3.
Outcome and what each would change: P1 and P4 hold -> the graded incidence needs a tonic (or other steep, graded) component beyond rebound and excitatory amplitude in this model, which supports I13 as the model's explanation of S-HT; P1 fails -> rebound plus excitatory amplitude suffice and I13 is not needed for the gradation.
