# Panel-wide classification of circuit genes across two lines: panel, rule and predictions, written and hashed BEFORE any P47T value is joined to any S-line value (SCI, 2026-11-16; non-canonical; hypothesis; mechanism level; not medical advice)

Question. For a fixed panel of 25 circuit genes, which transcript changes of the neuron-targeted S-line knockout (GSE158681 hippocampus, GSE158682 cortex; P17; bulk TPM; knockout 2 to 3 against control 2 to 4 animals; Synapsin-Cre conditional deletion) are also present in the P47T knock-in (GSE215425; homozygote against wild type; 5 v 5, forebrain; germline missense Pro47Thr, protein and mRNA present; reported ages 150 to 280 days, per-animal age not in the deposit: IT12)? 'Present in both' is at most 'reproduced in a line that keeps the protein'. It is NOT read as 'WW1-route', and P47T is NOT read as 'missense = hypomorphic' (the P47T paper calls it a partial loss of function on phenotype evidence: survival beyond one year, SCAR12 phenocopy; that evidence belongs to this variant, not to missense variants in general).
Differences reported beside every row: genotype (germline missense against conditional neuronal deletion), age (P17 against adult), laboratory and pipeline (TPM from two GEO tables against normalised counts), sex handling (FDR_no_sex and FDR_with_sex both given for P47T), n, regional match (S-line cortex CRTX against P47T CTX; S-line hippocampus S against P47T HPC; P47T PFC given as an extra cortical row).

## The panel (25 genes, named now, with the reason each is in)
Gabrd (tonic delta subunit; I13). Gabra5 (hippocampal tonic alpha-5). Gabra1 (synaptic alpha-1; zolpidem and benzodiazepine target class). Gabrg2 (synaptic gamma-2; benzodiazepine site). Slc6a1 (GAT-1; tonic GABA supply). Npy, Npy2r (peptide brake; I3). Sst (dendritic inhibition; I3). Pvalb (the interneuron marker in the PV-loss premise). Gad1 (GABA synthesis). Grin2b, Grin2a (NMDAR sustainer; I3, I7). Cacna1h, Cacna1i (T-type Cav3.2 and Cav3.3; I1 trigger). Hcn2 (the dominant HCN transcript of the tables; I1/I8). Slc12a5, Slc12a2 (KCC2, NKCC1; I6). Kcnk2 (TREK-1, a K2P leak candidate; I8 v3). Kcnq2 (Kv7; M-current). Gja1 and Kcnj10 (astroglial K+ buffering; I16). Gfap (composition and reactivity control). Adk, Adora1 (adenosine; I4). Wwox (control: the line difference in the target itself).

## Classification rule (applied in this order; fixed now)
For gene g and region pair: S = log2(mean knockout TPM / mean control TPM) of the S line; P = log2FC_centred of P47T with 95% interval P +/- t(0.975, 8) x |P| / |t| (the table's t statistic gives the standard error; 5 v 5 gives 8 degrees of freedom). A gene is **readable** if |S| >= 0.4 (otherwise 'not readable': no S-line change to reproduce). Readable genes are then:
1. **copied**: P has the sign of S and |P| >= 0.5 |S|.
2. **partly copied**: same sign and 0.2 |S| <= |P| < 0.5 |S|.
3. **opposite**: the sign differs and the P interval excludes 0.
4. **not copied**: |P| < 0.2 |S| and the P interval excludes S.
5. **not separable**: every other case (the P interval includes both 0 and S).
Each call is printed with FDR_no_sex and FDR_with_sex of P47T; a call is 'direction only' when FDR_no_sex >= 0.2. Counts are reported for cortex and hippocampus separately and never pooled.

## Predictions (fixed before the join; signs and categories)
Cortex: Gabrd copied; Npy copied; Sst copied; Gfap copied or partly copied; Slc12a5 copied or partly copied; Npy2r opposite or not separable (the S line rises, P47T falls); Pvalb not copied or not separable (the S line falls, P47T is flat); Kcnj10 not copied or not separable; Grin2b not readable in cortex (S line -0.17 log2), Wwox not copied (mRNA unchanged in P47T). Hippocampus: Grin2b not copied; Sst not copied; Gja1 copied or partly. Counts: of the readable cortical genes at most half 'copied'.
What each outcome changes: a majority 'copied' among the readable tonic and peptide genes (Gabrd, Npy, Sst) says these features appear in a line that keeps the protein (relevant to non-null missense children only if their variant impairs the same function: not assumed); a majority 'not copied' says they need the null state, neuronal deletion, or age; 'not separable' rows list what sample size would be needed.
