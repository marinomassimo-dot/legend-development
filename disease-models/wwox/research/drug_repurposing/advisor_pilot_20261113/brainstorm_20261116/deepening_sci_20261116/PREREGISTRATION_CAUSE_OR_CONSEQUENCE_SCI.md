## Question
In the WWOX lines (S-line neuronal knockout, GSE158681 hippocampus and GSE158682 cortex; P47T knock-in, GSE215425) a set of circuit transcripts moves together with glial markers (HELD, `RESULT_GABRD_COMPOSITION_SCI.md`, `ww1_panel.tsv`). Is that movement what a **chronic seizure state does to forebrain tissue in a mouse or rat with an intact Wwox gene** (the axis is then a consequence of seizures or of gliosis, and does not by itself locate a WWOX-specific effect), or is it absent in a seizure model (the axis then needs the genotype, or the age, or something the seizure model lacks)? A dataset cannot separate cause from consequence by itself: it tells which of two readings the transcripts allow, and names the follow-up (time course in the WWOX lines before the first seizure would be the discriminating experiment; it is not available here).

## Dataset selection rule (fixed now, applied to series metadata only)
A series qualifies if ALL hold, read from its title, summary, sample table and platform, not from any expression value:
1. Bulk RNA-seq or a sequencing-based method (array series are excluded from the primary test; they may be read afterwards as a secondary check and are then labelled secondary).
2. Rodent forebrain tissue, hippocampus or neocortex, from animals with intact Wwox (no genetic model of WWOX).
3. A **chronic** phase, defined as at least 21 days after the inducing event or a stated spontaneous recurrent seizure phase, with **controls in the same series** and processed alongside.
4. At least 3 animals per compared group.
5. Counts, TPM or FPKM obtainable as a downloadable supplementary file or series matrix by a route that does not meet an interactive challenge (no CAPTCHA step; NCBI E-utilities or the FTP path, rate-limited at roughly one request per second or slower). If the only route meets a challenge, the series is **parked with the trigger "a route that does not require one"**, not read and not dropped.
Ranking among qualifying series: first the larger chronic-phase group, then a stated control, then the earlier accession. Candidates so far (HE's metadata; I have not opened any): **GSE319769** (hippocampus, intra-amygdala kainate, 36 samples, acute/intermediate/chronic) is the first to be checked against the rule; GSE72402 (pilocarpine hippocampus, 8 samples, controls not stated) fails criterion 3 unless the sample table names controls; GSE122228 is an array (criterion 1). If GSE319769 fails the rule on metadata, the next qualifying series is used, and the failure and the reason are recorded.
Only the chronic-versus-control contrast is used for the primary test. Acute and intermediate time points are read afterwards, labelled secondary, and never replace the chronic one.

## Limits stated before the data
Bulk tissue; cell-type composition changes (neuron loss, gliosis) move bulk transcripts without any per-cell change, as the P47T composition test already showed (HELD); the seizure model has a different insult, species or strain; a chronic phase sampled long after the inducing event mixes seizure burden with injury; the mouse WWOX lines are P17 (S-line) and adult (P47T) so no time-matched comparison exists. The result is a transfer test of a hypothesis generator, not evidence of mechanism.
