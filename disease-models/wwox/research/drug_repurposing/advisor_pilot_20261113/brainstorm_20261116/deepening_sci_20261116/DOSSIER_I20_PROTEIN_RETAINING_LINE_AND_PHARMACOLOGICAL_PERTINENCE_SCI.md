# I20 Which features of WWOX loss are reproduced in a line that keeps the protein, and what that does and does not say about the pertinence of a pharmacological lever (dossier v1, SCI, 2026-11-16; hypothesis; mechanism level; no dose and no recommendation to use any agent; not medical advice; reference genotype class only)

Written for: IT and ADV (comparison, next test, what each outcome changes), HE (second reader of the panel), the operator (first block). Branch chosen after checking with IT and ADV (no overlap; ADV: partly covered, go as a systematic panel): P47T knock-in against the S-line null across a fixed 25-gene circuit panel, then the pharmacological reading, with the corrections of the day built in (incidence and rate are different endpoints; distance to threshold does not by itself change a sign; aggregated sIPSC does not reconstruct the heterozygote; missense is not assumed hypomorphic or milder).
Four kinds of statement, labelled everywhere: **OBS** (a WWOX observation in a held table or full text), **TRANSFER** (an observation in another system), **ASSUME** (an assumption), **MODEL** (a simulated result, hypothesis generator, not evidence).

## Answer first
1. **What the P47T line reproduces.** Preregistered panel (`PREREGISTRATION_WW1_PANEL.md`, sha256 0e03a8ce645339aa415cca2202499614c07129b3ed5198b68b8b90b27b292cdb, written before the join) applied to 25 genes: of the **14 readable cortical genes (|S-line log2| >= 0.4; Wwox, the control, excluded: it is 'not copied' by construction, mRNA unchanged in P47T)**, 6 are copied in P47T cortex (Gabrd, Npy, Sst, Cacna1i, Slc12a2, Gfap), 3 partly (Slc6a1, Hcn2, Slc12a5), 2 are opposite (Npy2r, Gad1), 2 not separable (Pvalb, Kcnq2), 1 not copied (Kcnj10); 10 genes are not readable. (OBS; tables: `ww1_panel.tsv`; counts per region never pooled; several 'copied' calls are direction only, FDR_no_sex of P47T 0.2 or more: Cacna1i CTX 0.35, Gfap 0.74.) Hippocampus: 6 copied (all direction only: Gabrd, Npy, Npy2r, Kcnk2, Gja1, Gfap), 6 partly, 9 not copied (Wwox included), 1 opposite, 3 not readable. The preregistered prediction (at most half copied among readable cortical genes) held (6 of 14); the signs I predicted for Gabrd, Npy, Sst, Npy2r, Pvalb, Kcnj10, Grin2b and Wwox held, but **many of those P47T values I had already seen in earlier replication work, so those predictions are not blind**; the blind part is what I had not predicted: **Gad1 is OPPOSITE** (the S line falls -0.41, P47T rises +0.27, FDR_no_sex 0.09), **Kcnq2 is opposite in P47T prefrontal cortex** (-0.72 against +0.45, FDR 0.014), **Cacna1i (Cav3.3) falls in both lines** (-0.93, -0.71 CTX direction only, -0.49 PFC FDR 0.10), **Slc12a2 falls in both** (-0.49, -0.36).
2. **What that is and is not.** 'Copied' means 'reproduced in a line that keeps the protein and the mRNA' (OBS); it does NOT say WW1 route (the lines differ in age, deletion type, laboratory, pipeline, sex handling and n: below) and does NOT say missense is hypomorphic. For P47T the paper itself calls it a **partial loss of function on phenotype evidence** (knock-in mice live beyond one year against about one month for the knockout; epilepsy, ataxia, neuroinflammation; the Pro47Thr change impairs interaction with PPxY motifs; according to PubMed, [10.1016/j.pneurobio.2023.102425](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pneurobio.2023.102425)): that is functional evidence for this variant, not for missense variants in general. In humans, the largest genotype survey (Gao 2025, 50 individuals; [10.1212/WNL.0000000000213883](https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000213883), abstract only) finds null/null more often with seizures, hypertonia and respiratory complications than null/missense or missense/missense, by genotype class and by parent report, with no functional assay of the missense alleles: a class-level association, not evidence of residual function.
3. **What does NOT carry over to a protein-retaining line:** the PV-transcript fall (S line cortex -0.59, hippocampus -1.76; P47T flat: 0.00 to +0.13), the hippocampal Grin2b fall (-1.81 against -0.16), the hippocampal NKCC1 fall, GAT-1 (-0.58, -0.94 against -0.12, -0.07). So **PV-marker loss is not necessary for epilepsy in a line that keeps the protein** (P47T has epilepsy with intact PV transcript; ASSUME: transcript = cell number is not established), and the 'inhibition deficit' premise must be carried by something else there.
4. **The tonic-subunit direction is the feature that survives both lines** (Gabrd -1.20 against -0.80 cortex, -1.67 against -0.93 hippocampus; cerebellum flat in P47T, +0.08, where the granule-cell layer is thinner, `Hussain 2023`): this makes a composition explanation (fewer neurons) a poorer reading, because the one region with a documented thinner neuronal layer and the highest delta level shows no fall (OBS + INFER; it is a rival test that can fail).
5. **Pharmacological reading (one-sentence form):** *if* the null and protein-retaining neocortex carry a tonic (delta-type) inhibition deficit, a delta-preferring neurosteroid-type agent (ganaxolone) and a gamma-2 benzodiazepine-type agent (clobazam, clonazepam) are distinguishable by pharmacology on that deficit, and the premise that would make the comparison pertinent is **a measured tonic-current deficit in layer II/III pyramidal cells whose pharmacology is delta-like (neurosteroid- and THIP-sensitive, diazepam-insensitive)**; that measurement does not exist. The held phasic deficit (sIPSC amplitude 57.3 against 27.5 pA, ratio 2.08) already suffices at model level for the held incidences if S-HT is near controls (MODEL), so **tonic is not needed for the incidence; it would be an additional deficit with a different drug-class implication.**
6. **Deferral (not refutation):** the benzodiazepine-versus-neurosteroid comparison in children is deferred as an experiment-ready claim: low marginal utility until the tonic current is measured; the cheap executable step is the composition rival on public counts (below), no laboratory needed.

## D5 Simulation and its limits (MODEL; hypothesis generator; the model was fitted to two held medians, so agreement with those is not validation)
Simple rival: phasic-only deficit (R4). It reproduces the held incidences within the binomial windows if S-HT is near controls (it fails if S-HT is midway), and a tonic deficit is then not needed. Both rivals predict the **same sign** for a tonic potentiator and for a phasic potentiator (suppression; the aggravation arm is inert); they differ only in magnitude through unidentified weights, so **drug response in slices cannot separate R1 from R4; only the tonic current can**. Not identifiable: the tonic weight (any t from 0 to about 1 fits), the S-HT sIPSC position, the sizes of the graded excitatory and rebound components. Not reproduced: the held duration shape, the serial independence (the model predicts rho about +0.13), any aggravation by GABA-A potentiation in the network (only 2 of 47 sets with tonic-selective potentiation), the held P47T phenotype (no P47T slice data). Incidence and rate are different endpoints: a plateau of incidence at the null is not resistance in rate (rate still falls 68% under a tonic increase of 20% at S-KO).
