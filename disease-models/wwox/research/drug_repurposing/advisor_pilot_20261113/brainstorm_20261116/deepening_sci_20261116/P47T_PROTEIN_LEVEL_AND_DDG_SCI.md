# The P47T protein-level statement against the held computed stability class (SCI, 2026-11-16; ADV's P4 sub-question; WWOX observation, computed prediction, and assumption kept apart; mechanism level; not medical advice)

Written for: ADV, IT (join to the per-child variant table), HE (variant_domain_map). 
## The question
The held computed class says p.Pro47Thr has ddG +2.81 (`analysis/data/WWOX_pathogenic_missense_classified.csv`, class 'stability (rescuable window)', distance to the catalytic site 48.2 A; p.Pro47Arg +2.53; p.His46Tyr +0.99, all in WW1), while the knock-in is described as keeping wild-type-level protein. Do the two statements conflict?
## The original statement (full text read, not a note)
Hussain 2023, Progress in Neurobiology, [10.1016/j.pneurobio.2023.102425](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pneurobio.2023.102425) (PMC10835625; Europe PMC full text `PMID36828035_Hussain2023_EPMC.xml`), Results 2.1: "The average mRNA expression levels between Wwox WT/WT and Wwox P47T/P47T CB [cerebellum] were comparable, with no significant difference in n = 6 mice tested in each group (Supplementary Fig. S2a). Western blot analyses also showed similar levels of Wwox protein expression in homozygous mutant and wildtype mice (see Fig. 1a, Wwox 10% input panel)." Fig. 1 legend: the same figure shows pull-downs with biotinylated Dvl2 and Wbp1 PPxY peptides from cerebellar lysates of **2 wild-type and 2 P47T/P47T mice** and the 10% input panel, with HSP90 as loading control; title of the figure: "p.Pro47Thr mutation is sufficient to abrogate Wwox affinity for PPxY motifs". The RNA-seq statement for the forebrain is about **mRNA** (Wwox expression comparable in PFC, CTX, HPC; Supplementary Fig. S9). The human statement the paper cites is Mallaret 2014: "expression of the protein remains intact and its enzymatic domain is unaffected".
## What the statement is, and is not (OBS)
(1) Total Wwox protein by SDS-PAGE western, **cerebellum only**, a qualitative 'similar levels' on a 10% input lane, two mice per genotype visible in the legend, no quantification stated, no forebrain protein, no folding or half-life measurement. (2) Forebrain: mRNA only. (3) The same blot series shows **loss of PPxY binding** (pull-down of Wwox by both peptides is abolished in the mutant while input is similar).
## Which reading holds
**The two statements do not conflict, because they are about different quantities.** The computed ddG +2.81 predicts local destabilisation of the WW1 fold (Pro47 is a conserved proline of the first WW domain: the paper's own words: 'critical for the function of the first WW domain'); the held observation is that the **binding function of WW1 is lost while the total protein level in the cerebellum is similar**. A small domain destabilised enough to lose its ligand binding can sit inside a full-length protein that is not degraded at steady state. So: reading A (the computed class does not predict total abundance for this variant, only local fold and binding) is consistent with all held data; reading B (the abundance statement concerns total protein, not folded protein) also holds in the narrow sense that SDS-PAGE western cannot report folding. What the data do NOT show: forebrain or neocortical protein level, protein half-life, folded fraction, or any quantity at the age of the transcriptome animals (150 to 280 days; the western age is not stated in the passage read). The 'rescuable window' label is a computed class for a variant that is *present and misfolded locally*, which is compatible with an intact total level; it is not a measured rescuability.
## For P4 (missense functional evidence)
* P47T (and probably P47R, H46Y by position and class, ASSUME): **WW1 ligand-binding loss with preserved total protein** (mouse OBS; abundance qualitative, one tissue) and a computed destabilisation of the WW1 fold (PREDICTION). Functional class: 'WW1 binding' (OBS for P47T; computed for the others).
* Q230P (ddG +1.51, 6.8 A from the catalytic site), R310P (+3.82), G355R (+1.84), G372R (+1.58): **computed destabilisation class, outside WW1**; no assay held in this note; the held human series put Q230P children in IT's set (Johannsen 2, Chong 1, Al-Baradie 1).
* C299R: computed 'catalytic/active-site' class (3.4 A to the catalytic site): a different mechanism (enzymatic, not binding).
* T12M, D58N, M352I, M1L/M1T: computed 'tolerant/other'; pathogenic assignment in the clinical literature is not tested by this file.
**What it does not license:** 'missense = hypomorphic' or 'missense = milder'. The only variant with both a functional statement (mouse binding loss with total protein similar, partial LoF by phenotype) and held clinical children (3 homozygotes, onset 6 months) is P47T; for every other missense variant the held information is a computed class, not an assay.
