# What a hit from a Wwox-null suppressor screen must cross to reach a lever table: hit, held readout in the slice, the experiment that decides (SCI, 2026-11-17; non-canonical; half a page for I21; hypothesis level; no agent, no dose; not medical advice)

Written for: ADV (I21), IT. A hit is a gene or a compound that suppresses a cellular phenotype in a Wwox-null background. It reaches the circuit levers only by crossing four steps; each step names what is held and what is not.
1. **From hit to a class label.** Map the hit to a target class and a pharmacology class (a label, not an agent); record which of the two it is, because a gene hit gives a target and not a drug. A hit with no known pharmacology class stops here with the trigger 'a ligand class appears'.
2. **Is the target present in the cells that burst?** Read the target's transcript per cell class in the held single-nucleus set (S-line hippocampus P17, [4b]/[4c]: unit nucleus, n = 1 per arm; the neocortex has none held, [4d] adds a different line) and in bulk neocortex: present means at least 30 nuclei of the class express it. A target absent in neurons of the bursting region is parked, not refuted (the cellular screen cell type may differ from the neuron).
3. **Which held circuit readout can it move, and with which sign?** Choose one of the held readouts, with its source: burst incidence and rate in S-KO and S-HT slices (incidence and burst frequency are different endpoints), the tonic current with the THIP reserve, the intrinsic profile, or the K+/Mg2+ threshold ladder (I15). Write the model-conditional expectation with the arm table (weights, not concentrations); a lever class with no arm in the table gets a stated parameter mapping as ASSUMED, or is marked 'not simulable'.
4. **The one experiment that decides.** The paired slice design of the brief (baseline then agent, a time-matched vehicle group, the animal as the unit, S-KO and S-HT and wild type, the heterozygote because near-threshold effects can flip sign), run at two strengths; the outcome is the burst count and incidence read separately. What the outcomes change: a fall at S-KO and S-HT with no wild-type effect is the pharmacological pertinence statement of I20; a rise at S-HT is the mixed-component aggravation of the anchored run; no effect parks the hit with the trigger 'a target-present, ligand-class-present neuron preparation'.
**What it cannot show:** that the cellular phenotype of the screen is the neuronal one; a drug x genotype interaction (needs wild type and heterozygote arms and a rival general-anticonvulsant reading); any development benefit; safety (BLOCK-1 by IT, not run). **First failure to expect:** the screen phenotype (a non-neuronal cell line) has no held neuronal readout in step 3; then the hit is a lead for a target, not for a lever.
