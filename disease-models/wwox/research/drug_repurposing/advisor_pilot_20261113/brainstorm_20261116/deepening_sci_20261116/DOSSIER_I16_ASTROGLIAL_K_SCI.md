## Answer first
* **The idea.** The graded factor behind the slice incidence (S-WT 0 of 11, S-HT 4 of 23, S-KO 36 of 42) may not be a neuronal channel at all but the clearing capacity of astroglia for extracellular K+ released by activity: less clearance, more K+ accumulation, a depolarising feedback that lowers the distance to burst threshold; the hippocampus (different astroglial complement) stays below threshold. It is a different cell type, it makes K+ challenge thresholds (I15) shift with dose, and it can be killed by a Ba2+-sensitive or gap-junction-sensitive current that is NOT smaller in the knockout.
* **Held markers, with the weakest named.** Bulk (S-line tables, control to knockout; HELD `regional_astro.tsv`): neocortex Atp1a2 (astroglial Na+/K+ pump alpha-2) 0.61, Kcnj10 (Kir4.1) 0.60, Slc1a2 (GLT-1) 0.64, Gfap 1.8-fold up; hippocampus Atp1a2 0.53, Gja1 (connexin 43) 0.57, Slc1a2 0.58, Gfap 4.3-fold up, Kcnj10 1.16 (not down). Independent P47T line (`p47t_all_regions_tested_genes.tsv.gz`, log2 fold; FDR_no_sex): Gja1 cortex -0.48 (0.04), Kcnj16 cortex -0.39 (0.06), Kcnj10 cortex -0.06, Atp1a2 cortex -0.21 (0.61): the Kir4.1 and pump falls of the S line are **not** reproduced in P47T. Held single-nucleus pool (GSE158684, hippocampus, one pool per arm): astrocyte-like nuclei Atp1a2 -32%, Aqp4 -49%, Gja1 -43%, Wwox-positive astrocyte-like nuclei 28.1% to 3.3% (from the reach and arm-B work; not recomputed here). **Weakest marker: all of these are transcripts, the single-nucleus numbers come from one pool per arm and from the hippocampus, the region that does not burst; there is no held neocortical astroglial measurement of clearance, and Gfap up (reactive astrocytes, composition) confounds every astroglial transcript.**
* **What the model says (MODEL, `i16_k_buffering.tsv`; predictions frozen before the run, `PREDICTIONS_I16_FROZEN_BEFORE_RUN.md`, sha256 28c8e79617a6e8a00b25d7f966da9b54d9cbba491170d0b1c705fe5031182ede): K+ clearance is a weak knob.** A K+ accumulation variable added to the oscillator (a separate file, the frozen model unchanged) changes the burst rate only 1.6-fold over an 8-fold change of clearance (rate 2.7, 2.2, 2.1, 1.7 per minute for clearance 0.5, 1, 2, 4 times the knockout's), and on the genotype axis (wild type with 2 to 20 times the knockout's clearance, rebound and excitatory amplitude graded) the wild type still bursts in 65 to 76% of slices and the heterozygote in 98 to 99%: **the incidence criterion (S-KO at least 70%, S-HT 5 to 35%, S-WT at most 10%) is not met for any clearance ratio scanned (R1 holds).** Two other frozen predictions failed: burst duration does not lengthen when clearance falls (0.80 s at 0.5 and 1.0, R2 fails) and the interval is not nearly unchanged (17 s at 0.5 against 25 s at 4: K+ acts on ignition, not on duration; R3 fails).
* **So:** as a *model-level* candidate for the incidence gradient, K+ buffering is weak (it behaves like the excitation-side components of the rival test, not like the inhibition-side ones). It is not killed (the model's K+ term is a one-compartment stand-in, and the only test that could kill it is a measurement that has not been done): ADVANCE with depth *seed to chain*, status named below. Its most useful consequence is the K+ challenge in I15: if the threshold shift by dose follows the model's chloride/tonic components and not K+ clearance, the K+ route is dead for the incidence.

## D1 Statement and chain
1. Wwox loss lowers astroglial markers of K+ clearance in neocortex (HELD S line bulk: Atp1a2, Kir4.1, GLT-1; not reproduced in P47T except Gja1).
2. Lower clearance raises activity-dependent extracellular K+ (ASSUMED; not measured).
3. Higher extracellular K+ depolarises neurons, lowering the distance to threshold (KNOWN physiology, ASSUMED to operate here).
4. The effect is graded with the astroglial deficit (ASSUMED); HELD: gliosis markers rise (Gfap).
5. In hippocampus the astroglial complement differs (Kir4.1 not down, S line), consistent with no bursting (INFERRED, one line).
6. Prediction: a K+ challenge or a K+ clearance blocker shifts the 50% incidence threshold more for S-HT and S-WT than for S-KO.

## D2 Why non-obvious
The leading picture treats the graded factor as inhibitory (I13, I6) or excitatory-synaptic. A non-neuronal graded factor would let a neuron-specific deletion (Synapsin-Cre) act through glia: the held astrocyte-like nuclei lose Wwox-positive signal too (28.1% to 3.3%, one pool), which the 'neuron-specific' wording does not predict; either the Cre is not neuron-restricted at this age or neuronal Wwox loss alters astrocytes secondarily. Either way an astroglial route is not excluded by the genotype name.

## D3 Consequences
Cell: a smaller Ba2+-sensitive (Kir4.1-type) astrocytic current and slower K+ transients after stimulation in null slices. Slice: lowering bath K+ rescues bursts (less drive); K+ clearance blockers lower the incidence threshold; the effect on the incidence threshold is graded S-WT > S-HT > S-KO. Animal: spreading-depolarisation threshold lower. Patient (observational): none. **Surprising predictions:** (1) the astroglial route changes the *interval* (ignition) and not the *duration* (MODEL: duration 0.80 s at clearance 0.5 and 4), the opposite of what one would expect from a K+ story; (2) wild-type slices burst at a high enough bath K+ with a threshold that depends on clearance support.

## D5 Discriminating experiments
(a) **No new data.** Held bulk and pool numbers above; one line (P47T) does not reproduce the S-line fall of Kir4.1 and the pump: the cheapest test is a protein-level Kir4.1/Atp1a2 comparison in the existing tissue. (b) **One slice experiment.** The same cells as the I13 arm: barium-sensitive astrocytic current and an extracellular K+ transient after a fixed stimulus (ion-selective electrode or a K+ indicator) in S-KO, S-HT, S-WT neocortex and S-KO hippocampus; plus I15's K+ challenge with and without a clearance blocker (a label-level mechanism: Kir4.1 or gap-junction blockade). Outcomes: (1) clearance graded (WT > HT > KO) and the threshold shifts with a blocker: the idea stands; (2) clearance equal to wild type in the knockout: **killed** for incidence; (3) clearance lower and the blocker does not shift the threshold: present and not sufficient. (c) **Clinical:** none feasible.

## D7 Combination
With I13 and I6: a K+ term and an inhibition term add in drive (the model has K+ as a drive term and chloride/tonic as inhibition strength); with I4: the pump is ATP-limited, so an energy deficit lowers clearance (the I4 axis); with I5/I9: none. Contradiction to settle: the model says K+ acts on interval (ignition), I13 acts on the incidence by a steep inhibitory component; the experiment (same cells, same slices) decides.

## D8 Stronger / dead

Killed by: equal clearance in the knockout and wild type (named test). Parked-with-trigger: none; status ADVANCE (seed to chain).
