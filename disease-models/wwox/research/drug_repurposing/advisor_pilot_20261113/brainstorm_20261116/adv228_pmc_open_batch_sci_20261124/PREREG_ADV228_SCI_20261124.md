## 2. Frozen selection rule (script `select_six_adv228.py`, digest in the companion file; deterministic)
* Pool: the 48 of IT's list minus IT's three reads and the gnomAD paper, minus PAPER 495, 157 and 447, minus ADV-222's eight PMIDs (37804316, 30029003, 32652616, 25738457, 23611579, 41858901, 42190665, 42395553): 44 records expected.
* Relevance score ("a registry claim or a portfolio strand rests on the paper"): +3 if a **claim-level file** names the PMID (`working_model_current.md`, `working_model_history.md`, `disease_model.md`, or any `CC-*.md` commit-candidate file); +2 if at least one **note-level file** names it (a census, dossier audit, discovery ledger, batch-selection module, lens selection or analysis note; seed lists, receipts, listings, other papers' manifests and generated files are excluded by name, the exact exclusion list is in the script); +1 if two or more distinct files of either kind name it. Maximum 6.
* Ranking: score descending; ties by lower registry number (the number in the record id).
* Group rule: at most one record per research group, the group being the last author of the Europe PMC record (a public metadata call made after freezing, no e-mail, no contact data). If two selected records share a last author, the lower-ranked is dropped and the next in rank is taken; the same check applies to the replacement and to the authors of ADV-222's eight and IT's three records (a last-author match with those is also dropped).
* The six are the first six after the group rule. No manual override; a record whose PMC text cannot be obtained by a lawful route is reported as a route result and replaced by the next in rank (the replacement is listed).
* Expected from the tally of file names before freezing (not a prediction of content): the first two places go to records named in the working-model files or in two commit-candidate files; the remaining places are decided by ties among record numbers. A weakness stated in advance: file mentions measure how often the repository names a paper, not whether a statement rests on it; the reading will show which.

## 3. Reading (as the brief asks)
* Artefact: the PMC full text saved under `files/fulltext/` of the root checkout as `PMID<pmid>_<FirstAuthor><year>_PMC.xml` (Europe PMC fullTextXML or the PMC article page as fallback; figures not downloaded unless a section reads "figure" data; supplementary files only if PMC lists them as open).
* Read completely, section by section, with `fulltext_receipts.py record --dry-run` then `record`; honest coverage vocabulary (read, captions_only, not_present, unavailable, not_read); verbatim locators for every quoted statement; PMIDs only in receipts and research/ files.
* Per record: (i) what the abstract-level class missed (counter-check: what the abstract-level class could NOT have seen); (ii) one of: NEW open question (question text plus the observation that would answer it), NARROWED row (name the row), CONTRADICTION (statement and quote, no edit), UNCHANGED; (iii) promotion candidate yes/no by the ordinary candidate route; (iv) for each new question: could a held source answer it (yes: name the source; no: name what would).
* Predictions (fixed now, for scoring afterwards): P1 at least four of the six records are about cancer cell lines or tissue and no OPEN row of the questions map is narrowed by any of them (the IT59 class D and ADV-218's three results point that way); P2 at least one record's abstract states a result the text weakens (a significance, a design or a time point the abstract does not carry), as ADV-218 found in all three of its cancer papers; P3 at most two new questions with a held source that could answer them. Rejection: P1 rejected if an OPEN row is narrowed; P2 rejected if no abstract-versus-text difference is found in any of the six; P3 rejected if three or more new questions have a held answering source.
* New questions go into `new_questions_adv228.tsv` with the open-questions-map columns (qid, source_ref, question_text_max300, decision_at_stake_max200, kind, status and the remaining columns of the map header at the time), for IT to merge into the ADV-225 list by file path (IT's instruction; I do not append to its list). Each is duplicate-checked against `open_questions_map.tsv` and `git grep` over the research folders before it is written.
