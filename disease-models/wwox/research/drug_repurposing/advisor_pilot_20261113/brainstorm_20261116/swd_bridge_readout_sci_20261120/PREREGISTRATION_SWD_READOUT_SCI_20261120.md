# Item 1 pre-registration (SCI, 2026-11-20): Obeid 2026 Figure 7, spike-wave burden in Wwox-null pups, as a candidate primary readout for the bridge experiment (hypothesis level; no dose; not medical advice; written before any value was read from the figure)

Approved by ADV 2026-11-20 with three conditions, all adopted: (1) survivorship per age, and whether the figure reads at one age or pools ages; (2) vector first, then raster, resolution stated; rate and duration per animal if the panels allow it, otherwise "plausible", not "adopted"; (3) a spontaneous burden is a count outcome, so the bridge's 36 to 109 per arm is NOT carried: its n needs its own SD from the held per-animal values, or stays open.

Already read before this note (legend and Results sentences, held text): recording began at about P14 after P0 intracerebroventricular injection; 7 consecutive days of continuous ECoG; n = 5 littermates per group (WT, KO, KO plus high-dose neuronal AAV); "limited cohort"; panel 7B daily spike frequency (spikes per hour), 7C averaged spike counts, 7E SWD events per animal; SWDs "largely absent in WT"; p < 0.0001 for 7E by Student's t test.

## Questions and closing rules
A. Ages and survivors: which postnatal days the figure covers, whether 7B is a day-by-day curve or pooled, how many of the 5 per group contribute at each day. Closes when stated or "not stated in text or figure".
B. Per-animal values: the five points per group in 7C and 7E (rate or count), read from the vector layer of the article PDF if the panel is vector, else from the raster; stated resolution; no value read by eye from a bar height without the axis calibration.
C. Duration: whether any panel gives SWD duration; if not, "not given".
D. Wild-type pups: SWD count in WT (zero, or not).
E. The SD for a count outcome: from the five KO values, log scale or dispersion, with 4 degrees of freedom stated and its one-sided 95 percent upper bound (about 2.1 times at 5 df, here 4 df: the factor is computed, not assumed).

## Decision rule, fixed before data (what each outcome does to the bridge)
* **ADOPT as a primary or co-primary readout** only if (i) per-animal values for all five KO and five WT pups are readable from vector or from a raster with a verified axis calibration, (ii) the KO values are separated from the WT values with no overlap, and (iii) the recording ages are stated and the survivors per age are stated. Then the bridge gains a spontaneous-burden readout with no provocation; its n is **computed from the per-animal SD, with the upper bound**, and the PTZ numbers (36 to 109 per arm, 177 to 318 per cell) are not carried across.
* **PLAUSIBLE, not adopted** if only means or a representative trace are readable, or ages are pooled: the readout stays a candidate in the pilot (the pilot records ECoG anyway from P14, v3 bridge line "Spontaneous burden"), n open.
* **DROP** if SWD are present in WT pups at comparable frequency, or the KO values overlap the WT values, or the KO values are carried by one animal.
* **What the result does to ethosuximide's position (the second half of the question):** none by itself. A spike-wave burden in a Wwox-null pup is not evidence that an agent acts on it; it gives the T-type class a readout that its best-known indication (generalised spike-wave in absence epilepsy) can move, and that is all. Rodent pup spike-wave-like activity can be developmental; a WT comparison at the same age is the control.
