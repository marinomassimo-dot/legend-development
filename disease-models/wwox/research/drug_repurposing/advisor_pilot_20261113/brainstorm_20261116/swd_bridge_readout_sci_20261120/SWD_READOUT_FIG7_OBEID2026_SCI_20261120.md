## Answer first
1. **Verdict by the pre-registered rule: PLAUSIBLE, not adopted.** Panel 7E has per-animal values with no overlap between knockout and wild type, but the readout is pooled across a seven-day recording, the survivors per age are not stated, and panels 7B and 7C carry fewer animals than the legend says and a p value that the Results sentence contradicts.
2. **Per-animal spike-wave rate (panel 7E, spike-wave discharges per hour), measured on the raster:** knockout 0.33, 0.61, 0.69, 0.73, 0.80 (mean 0.63, SD 0.18); wild type 0.00 to 0.07 (mean 0.03); knockout plus high-dose neuronal vector 0.00, 0.09, 0.11, 0.18, 0.25 (mean 0.13). Lowest knockout 0.33 against highest wild type 0.07 and highest rescued 0.25: no overlap in either direction; no single animal carries the knockout mean. The legend's mean bar for the knockout (about 0.64) agrees with my 0.63.
3. **What the panel does not give:** duration (not given); age resolution (7E is one number per pup over the whole recording, "beginning at approximately P14", seven consecutive days); survivors per day (not stated in text or figure); the definition of an "event".
4. **Two internal inconsistencies, stated and not resolved:** (a) panel 7C shows two wild-type and three knockout points with p = 0.2000 (the smallest exact two-sided p for 2 against 3), whereas the legend says n = 5 littermates per group with "*p < 0.05; ns" and the Results sentence calls the elevation "significant" (Figure 7C); panel 7E has 5 per group. Whether panels B and C and panel E are the same animals is not stated. (b) the representative trace in 7D shows four bursts inside about 50 seconds, whereas the knockout mean in 7E is 0.63 per hour: "event" may mean a cluster or a recording epoch, not a burst; the definition is not in the text, so the rate cannot be turned into a burden in seconds per hour.

## Survivorship (ADV condition 1)
The recorded pups are the global null (the line of the gene-therapy report) from about P14 for seven days, so about P14 to P21. My Kaplan-Meier measurement of the held Repudi curves (`v3_one_page_sci_20261120/KM_MEASUREMENT_REPUDI2021_FIG1_SCI_20261120.md`) gives for the global null first death day 19, median day 22 (n = 11, another laboratory and another cohort, neonatal surgery absent): **the last two or three recording days fall inside the death window**, so a burden pooled over seven days is conditioned on survivors from about day 19. The paper does not say how many of the five knockout pups were alive and recorded on each day; panel 7B has no visible drop at day 7 (bar about 8.5 against about 10.5 on day 1), which is compatible with all recorded pups surviving but does not show it.

## What it does to the bridge (ADV condition 3)
* **A spontaneous spike-wave rate is a count outcome; the PTZ numbers are not carried.** The 36 to 109 per arm (simple effect, R 0.7) and 177 to 318 per cell (interaction) belong to the provoked stage and stay with it.
* **Indicative n from the held per-animal values, as a floor and not as a plan:** knockout coefficient of variation 0.29 (log SD 0.284, 4 degrees of freedom; one-sided 95 percent upper bound 2.37 times). For a reduction to 0.7 of the knockout rate (normal approximation plus 2, 80 percent power, two-sided 0.05): **about 12 per arm at the observed SD and about 58 at its upper bound**; for a halving, about 5 and about 17. **Caveats that make these a floor:** the SD is from five untreated pups; the rescued arm's coefficient of variation is 0.75 (counts near the floor), so a treated arm is more dispersed than the untreated one and a count model is needed, not the log normal; wild type is near zero, so no genotype-by-treatment contrast is available on this readout (the question "is the effect specific to the knockout" cannot be asked of a readout whose wild-type value is about zero; the wild-type arm serves as the developmental control instead).
* **Pilot role.** The pilot already records ECoG from P14 (v3 bridge "Spontaneous burden"). With this result the pilot should additionally record SWD per hour by day, the definition of an event, duration, and the number of pups alive and recorded per day, so that the SD and the survivors are the laboratory's own.
* **Ethosuximide's position:** no change by this panel alone. It supplies a readout that the best-known indication of the class (generalised spike-wave in absence epilepsy) can move; whether an agent acts on this pup spike-wave is untested. Wild-type pups of the same age show about 0.03 per hour, which argues against a purely developmental origin in this cohort (one laboratory, n = 5).
* **Decision it changes:** the bridge page keeps the provoked-seizure primary and adds the spontaneous SWD rate as a **plausible co-primary under pilot conditions**: adopt only if the pilot shows knockout rate separated from wild type with per-day survivors stated, and then size n from the pilot's own SD with its upper bound.

## Limits
One figure, raster only, one laboratory; n = 5 per group in the panel I measured and fewer in two sibling panels; the text and the panels disagree on significance and n; unit of "event" undefined; duration absent; the measurement is mine only (not independently repeated).
