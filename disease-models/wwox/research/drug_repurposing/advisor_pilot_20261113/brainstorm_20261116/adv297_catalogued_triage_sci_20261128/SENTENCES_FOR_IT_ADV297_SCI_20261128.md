# ADV-297: proposed changes for IT (SCI, 2026-11-28; hypothesis level; not medical advice)

Source of every item: PMID 25662954 / PMC4374002 (Farooq, Exp Biol Med 2015), a single-author **review** that also prints the author's own material. Both a full-text reader and a blind reader said the sentence would change (reports in this folder); SCI checked the numbers and words on the PMC text on 2026-11-28. Figure images were not looked at. Until today the registry carried this paper as catalogued only. Wording is a proposal; IT decides placement. Nothing here reverses a conclusion.

## I1. Active-site map, discovery ledger line 29 (attribution and evidence level)

Held: "la mappa del sito attivo SDR di WWOX è S260 (substrato), S281 + motivo YxxxK Y293–K297 (catalisi), motivo Rossmann GxxxGxG N-terminale per NAD ([struttura, PMC4374002])".

The cited review gives S260, the YxxxK dyad Y293/K297 and GXXXGXG for **NADP+/NADPH**, from sequence alignment and a homology model (not an experimental structure). Its text has no S281 (0 hits for S281, Ser281 and 281). S281 and NAD appear in another held source, PMID 21476439, introduction (that the ledger line took them from there is SCI's inference): sequence analysis (citing Bednarek 2000) naming a coenzyme site "[NAD(H) or NADP(H)]" GANSGIG 131-137, YNRSK 293-297 and a serine 12 residues upstream (SCI targeted search of the held text, not blind-checked).

Proposed (dated correction, history not rewritten): "la mappa del sito attivo SDR di WWOX è un'assegnazione da sequenza e modello, non una struttura sperimentale: S260 (substrato), diade Y293/K297 del motivo YxxxK, GxxxGxG per il cofattore NADP(H) (review PMID 25662954); S281 e il sito «NAD(H) o NADP(H)» sono riportati, da analisi di sequenza, in PMID 21476439."

The inference built on it (Q230 is not a catalytic residue) is not touched.

The blind reader judged this a change only if a statement without data may change such a sentence. SCI's suggestion: if written at all, write "stated without data in a 2015 review", and do not use it as a substrate or binder.
