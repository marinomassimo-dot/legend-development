# ADV-181 result: what Peiro-Moreno et al. 2026 (Glia) lets a myelin readout distinguish among lineage expansion or survival, uptake of retained material, and new sheath formation, and what it changes in INV-06 (SCI successor Z, 2026-11-22; test-design comparison; adjacent non-WWOX primary rat culture, mouse and zebrafish; no drug promotion, no direction transfer, no WWOX efficacy inference, no assay commission; no author contact; no e-mail address to any service; not medical advice)

Pre-registration: `PREREGISTRATION_ADV181_SCI_20261122.md` (commit c9ff8d85b, before any body text, figure or supplement was read). Receipt: `RECEIPT_ADV181_SCI_20261122.json`.

## Answer first
**NARROW.** One reading rule for INV-06 (a boundary with one conditional control), not a new arm.

**The rule (INV-06 and X1 readouts):** an MBP-positive count, share or area is evidence of NEW myelin only when (1) it is expressed per cell AND per field or culture, with the unit named; (2) in any preparation that contains MBP-bearing material that cells can take up (added myelin, debris, dying cells), the readout is also scored with that material excluded or controlled (a debris-only or no-cell control and, where the material is labelled, the label channel masked); and (3) an independent per-cell new-sheath measure (sheath number or length along a fibre or axon, or conduction) sits beside it. Without (3) the claim stops at "MBP-positive cells or area increased"; without (2) it does not separate (b) from (c). Readout type (RP-045 rule): a STOCK of MBP-positive material or cells; claim type it is offered for in the paper: NEW formation ("myelination", "myelinated area per cell").

**Why NARROW and not KEEP:** the confound is visible in the paper's own images but its size is not measured, INV-06's preparations do not add myelin (so the control is conditional on debris being present), and the primary has no origin-resolved sheath control that would justify a sharper arm. **Why not CLOSE:** neither INV-06 nor X1 names a per-cell denominator, retained-material control or an independent new-sheath measure (my read of the INV-06 row and the X1 README: the row's readouts are an MBP-positive share and "sheath structure"; no text on debris, uptake or per-cell denominators), and the paper is a concrete case of "markers or area change while independent per-cell new sheaths and function are absent".

## Where (a), (b) and (c) stand in the paper
* **(a) expansion or survival:** supported at the culture level (Ki67 up, more NG2+ and MBP+ cells, PI death down), with n = 3 to 4 cultures and paired t tests; Olig2 total count is not significant by its own printed P. The unchanged NG2-to-MBP ratio is consistent with expansion of both pools and carries no information on per-cell change.
* **(b) uptake of retained material:** strongly present: more than 90% of OLs contain myelin (Fig 1D), lipid droplets colocalise with labelled myelin (Fig 3, S4), etomoxir blocks the viability gain, fatty acids partly mimic it. In Fig 1C and Fig 4K the labelled exogenous myelin lies inside the MBP-stained cell and overlaps the MBP signal (yellow). The donor myelin is a rat brain myelin preparation scored with an anti-MBP antibody, so MBP-positive area and MBP-positive "cells" can include internalised MBP-bearing debris. The paper does not quantify how much of the MBP+ area is exogenous.
* **(c) new sheath:** not measured in any arm. No per-cell sheath count or length, no thickness, no axon-contact assay, no electrophysiology. The only "myelination" readout is MBP+ area on nanofibers (cultures n = 3). The zebrafish arm is origin-resolved at the level of cells (the mbp reporter cannot be carried by donor myelin) but counts cells, not sheaths.

## Comparison with INV-06, X1 and the held record
* **INV-06 and SCI50 X1/H4:** the question and arms concern a Wwox-null neuronal signal, conditioned-medium and fixed-neuron arms, a positive control and a variance-based detection limit; the readouts are an MBP-positive share and sheath structure. Nothing separates a share or area from retained material, per-cell from per-field denominators, or markers from a measured sheath. The paper does not change the INV-06 question; it shows concretely why a share or area needs the three conditions above.
* **ADV-123 map:** the map's recurring pattern is a myelin marker moving beside a functional gain with no row separating the route to function; this paper is a primary-culture instance with a marker-and-area rise and no per-cell sheath or function measure, so it weakens a functional-rescue reading of "area" in the same way. No Wwox row is involved.
* **Held neuron/OPC record:** not reopened beyond the rows quoted by INV-06 and X1.

## Strongest rival and the refuting observation
* **Strongest rival to "exposure produces new sheaths":** the MBP+ area and count rise reflect retained MBP-bearing exogenous material and more cells (expansion, survival, size), with no per-cell change in sheath formation.
* **Refuting observation (for the retained-material reading of the area readout):** with the labelled-myelin channel masked or a debris-only control subtracted, MBP+ area per cell still rises AND a per-cell sheath count or length along the nanofibers or axons rises in the same cells. If the area returns to control with the mask, the rise was retained material; if cell number rises while per-cell sheath count does not, it was expansion.

## DREAM: do the INV-06 nulls and the count-versus-recovery distinction expose the same trap?
Weakly, in one form. In ADV-179 the numerator followed how many clones were recovered; here the numerator follows how much MBP-bearing material sits inside the scored footprint and how many cells are in the field. Both are a stock (a count, an area) offered for a rate (new formation); the RP-045 reading rule already names that shape. Association: moderate for the shape, none for a shared mechanism. The lipid-droplet link to RP-036 (lipid storage with retained myelin) stays a DREAM candidate; the paper adds only that droplets colocalise with labelled myelin in culture.

## Limits
Adjacent, non-WWOX, rat optic-nerve OL cultures at 1 DIV (more than 98% O4+, so the "OPC" pool in this model is a small NG2+ fraction), mouse and zebrafish arms with small n; figures viewed as images (all eight image files: seven figures and the graphical abstract) and values read approximately, nothing recomputed; the supplement is captions only (Figs S1 to S7); raw data are on request (no contact); Methods for the nanofiber area readout are absent from the text I read. A per-cell sheath count or conduction measure from this preparation would turn the area readout into a test of (c); none exists.
