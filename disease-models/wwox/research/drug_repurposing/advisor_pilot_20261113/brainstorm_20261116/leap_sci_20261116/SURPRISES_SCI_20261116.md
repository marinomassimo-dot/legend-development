# Five most surprising held-data results of 2026-11-16 (SCI; OPINION, for the closing synthesis)

Labelled opinion: "surprising" means it contradicted a premise I or a peer was carrying, not that it is large or established. Each line names the table, the n and what it does not show. Held transcript data only; transcript is not function. Mechanism matching only; reference genotype class only.

1. **The hippocampus is not spared because it escaped recombination.** Wwox falls more in the spared region (hippocampus S line -85%) than in the bursting neocortex (-68%), so a lower excision fraction cannot be the reason the slices there do not burst (J17/HSCI-5 pre-test; one pool per arm; a protein-level or per-slice excision fraction is not held).
2. **Npy rises in two laboratories while its receptors fall in both.** Npy up in the neuron-deletion and P47T tables, but Npy1r, Npy2r, Npy5r are down (nuclei -53 to -69%; P47T Npy2r -0.89, FDR 0.047), so "more NPY = more brake" is not supported at the receptor end (J17; transcripts only; peptide release and receptor function not measured).
3. **Wwox-positive nuclei fall in astrocyte-like nuclei too (28.1% to 3.3%).** The "neuron-specific" wording of several hypotheses does not hold for the knock-out as recorded, so any neuron-only reading must also explain an astrocytic loss (J18; one pool per arm, S line, P17).
4. **The mTORC1 signature is one line, not the disease.** Present in the independent hypomorph line (P47T, hallmark NES 1.5-2.1) and absent in the organoid (panel median -0.11, p 0.21) and in the neuron-deletion tables, which makes a shared mTORC1 premise a line-specific observation (J09; 5 v 5 in P47T, 4 KO v 2 WT in the organoid).
5. **The organoid is not simply immature on the receptor axes.** Projected from the maturation calibration, GRIN2A should be -1.66 and is +1.11; KCC2 should be -0.14 and is +0.73, so a "frozen immature GABA/NMDA state" reading fails on those two genes (J04; composite rests on SNAP25 and five lines; transcript only).

What would change my mind on each: (1) a per-slice or protein excision fraction that is lower in hippocampus; (2) an Npy-receptor protein or response assay showing preserved signalling; (3) a second pool with Wwox-positive astrocytes unchanged; (4) a second independent line with the signature; (5) the same calibration run in a larger line set where GRIN2A and KCC2 track maturity.
