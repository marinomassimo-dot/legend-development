## H-HE-1 (sodium-channel targets are absent at P6-P8 and present at P13-P15, so "no link" at P6-P8 is not a negative) - INDEPENDENT TEST of the age claim, assay-confounded
* **Reproduction of HE's numbers.** Held P7 purified neurons: Scn1a 2.5 FPKM (OPC 4.85), Scn3a 8.64 (OPC 11.28), Scn8a 6.22 (astrocytes 8.89, OPC 4.92); Scn2a and the carbonic-anhydrase genes are absent from the table, as HE said.
* **New held readout HE said does not exist.** HE wrote "there is no later-age mouse neuron class". The GSE158684 control pool is P17 hippocampus, 2,320 neuron-like nuclei (Snap25 UMI > 0). Sodium-channel subunit CPM and share of nuclei with the gene detected: Scn1a 244.27 (60.6%), Scn2a 348.98 (76.9%), Scn3a 143.41 (53.5%), Scn8a 399.28 (78.6%), against Snap25 935.88. Ratio to Snap25 in each assay: P7 purified neurons Scn1a 0.017, Scn3a 0.059, Scn8a 0.042; P17 nuclei Scn1a 0.261, Scn3a 0.153, Scn8a 0.427, i.e. 15-fold, 2.6-fold and 10-fold higher ratios at P17.
* **For or against the rejecting observation.** HE's change-my-mind condition ("a later-age mouse neuron RNA table showing Scn1a, Scn3a and Scn8a at P13-P15 neuron values at or below the P7 ones") is NOT met: the P17 ratios to a neuronal marker are higher. Direction consistent with age-gated target presence.
* **Limits (decisive):** whole-cell P7 against nuclear P17 RNA, cortex-wide against hippocampus, different depth and capture; the ratios can differ by assay alone. P7 values are not "absent": Scn1a at 2.5 FPKM in a purified neuron class is detectable, so "barely read" is the strongest wording the tables bear. **Result: not rejected, consistent in direction; a within-assay P7 against P13-P15 comparison does not exist.**

## H-HE-2 (the GABA-AT/GAT class acts on a glial compartment) - INDEPENDENT TEST (a table could have rejected it)
* **Reproduction.** P7 classes: Slc6a11 (GAT-3) neuron 35.11 against astrocyte 557.37 FPKM (15.9-fold), Slc6a1 (GAT-1) 128.67 against 419.72 (3.3-fold), Abat 53.59 against 53.02 (shared): HE's numbers hold.
* **Second assay (P17 hippocampus nuclei).** Astrocyte-like nuclei (Aqp4, Slc1a3 or Gfap UMI, no Snap25; 395 nuclei) against neuron-like nuclei (2,320): Slc6a1 316.12 CPM (46.6% detected) against 63.80 (22.2%); Slc6a11 154.43 (25.8%) against 21.98 (11.9%); Abat 111.94 (21.3%) against 55.50 (24.8%).
* **For or against the rejecting observation.** The rejecting observation (a neuron-enriched GAT-1 or GAT-3) is not met in either assay: the astrocytic enrichment of the transporters replicates at P17 (GAT-1 5-fold, GAT-3 7-fold by CPM), and Abat is not compartment-specific (about 2-fold astrocyte-like by CPM, similar detection). GAT-1 is also detected in a fifth of neuron-like nuclei, so "glial" is enrichment, not exclusivity.
* **Limits:** the compartment premise is read; the hypothesis' claim (effect lost in a neuron-enriched preparation) is a functional one no table reaches; "astrocyte-like" is my definition. **Result: premise supported in two independent assays; functional claim untested.**

## H-HE-3 (a valproate-type signature moves the WWOX-loss glycolysis/lactate module the loss direction: an aggravation hypothesis) - INDEPENDENT TEST of whether the held sign is distinctive
* **Reproduction.** Held family scores (neuron-like `O_E` / progenitor-like `O_B_SIG` / frequent-hit rate): valproic acid -1.2768 / -0.3240 / 0.033; carbamazepine +0.7367 / -0.2964 / 0.045; lamotrigine -1.3090 / +1.2131 / 0.151; zonisamide -0.9655 / -0.8393 / 0.079. The other antiseizure compounds I searched for (phenytoin, ethosuximide, topiramate, levetiracetam, vigabatrin, clobazam, diazepam, phenobarbital, oxcarbazepine, lacosamide, perampanel, cannabidiol, tiagabine) have no family row whose name equals the compound name in this table (a salt or code name could hide one; not searched further).
* **Base rates in the same table (what HE's pre-test did not ask).** Of 451 neuron-like signatures, 18.0% have an opposition score at or below -1.05 and 16.6% at or above +1.05 (median 0.02), so scores of that size occur in either direction about equally often. Of 256 families, 11.3% have `O_E` at or below valproic acid's -1.2768. Valproic acid's frequent-hit rate (0.033) is well below the table's median (0.098), so it is not a frequent hitter by that measure. Four of four negative neuron-like signatures is about 6% by a coin-flip count, and the replicates are not independent (same compound, same dose, two times).
* **For or against.** The rejecting observation is functional (no change of HK2/LDHA/SLC2A1 or lactate under a valproate-type label in wild-type neurons); no table reaches it. The held table shows the valproate sign is real in the table but not distinctive: an equally negative neuron-like score belongs to lamotrigine (-1.31), whose progenitor-like score is the opposite sign (+1.21), and carbamazepine, the other sodium-channel label, is positive, so the sign does not follow a mechanism class here. HE already notes the one dose and no within-class contrast; the base rates add that the magnitude is within the table's ordinary range.
* **Limits:** a LINCS-derived signature is a knockout-signature opposition, not a rescue or aggravation measurement (the held file says so); one dose, two cell contexts; no WWOX readout. **Result: not rejected, not distinctive; the aggravation reading cannot be separated from a LINCS context artefact with held data.**

## H-HE-4 (seizure and development endpoints decouple for glial-target classes, track together for neuron-target ones) - target-compartment half only; the endpoint half deliberately untested

The steroid class is therefore not cleanly "glial-target". The rejecting observation is about children with both endpoints stated and needs A1, which I did not open and which is sealed. **Result: half-tested; the NR3C1 half of the premise is moderately supported at best; the endpoint half goes to the prospective registry, as HE says.**
