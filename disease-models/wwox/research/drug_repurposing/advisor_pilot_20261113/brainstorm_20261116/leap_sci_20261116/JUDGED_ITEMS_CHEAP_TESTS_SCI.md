## 1. Held-data pre-test of H-B1 (J11, the only survivor): is burst interval versus duration held anywhere?
* **Yes, as baseline and for two agents, not for the agents H-B1 is about.** The source paper reads both quantities from the layer II/III field potential: median burst duration 0.66 s [0.53-1.06]; median inter-burst interval 26.2 s; gamma-fit shapes 2.45 [1.93-3.11] (119 bursts, 18 slices, 9 animals) and 1.43 [1.11-1.84] (101 intervals) (Breton 2021 Fig 1D, held full text). Supplementary Table S1 (held `mmc1_text.txt`) adds two agents: **carbenoxolone** frequency 0.84 [0.57-1.53] to 0.03 [0.03-0.05] bursts/min with duration 0.66 s to 0.54 s [0.52-0.73] (13 bursts, 6 slices, 3 animals): the interval lengthens about 28-fold, the duration changes by about -18%; **brilliant blue FCF** 0.78 [0.44-1.09] bursts/min, duration 0.53 s [0.51-1.46] (25 bursts, 6 slices, 3 animals).
* **For or against.** The dissociation H-B1 assigns to its TRIGGER arm (interval up, duration about unchanged) is the pattern of carbenoxolone in the held table: the readout can dissociate. Limits: carbenoxolone is not a T-type blocker (a gap-junction blocker with broad off-target actions; the registry withdrew the attribution of its burst effect to one mechanism), 13 remaining bursts under it, no variance by animal printed, durations under 0.7 s sit near the detection floor of the table (minimum 0.26 s). The SUSTAINER half (an NMDAR-active agent shortens bursts and leaves the interval) has no partial data: d-APV abolished the bursts (51 bursts in 5 slices from 3 animals, no after-drug durations). **Result: not rejected; the readout is real and already measured; one non-specific agent shows the dissociation; the two decisive arms are not in held data.**
* **A by-product that closes an open item (X0 of INV-05).** The two printed baseline burst rates are two statistics, not a contradiction: 2.29 bursts/min is 60 s divided by the median inter-burst interval of 26.2 s (60/26.2 = 2.29), whereas the 0.84 [0.57-1.53] bursts/min of Supplementary Table S1 is the per-slice frequency (min 0.36, max 3.89). INFERENZA from the arithmetic; the source does not say so. The brief's burst definition should read one of them, not both.
* **Experiment side:** Addendum 4 of the brief (`experiment_brief_presidio013_sci_20261116/EXPERIMENT_BRIEF.md`) makes the same slice recordings read interval and duration separately for every arm and lists what H-B1, HSCI-1, HSCI-2 and HSCI-3 each predict.

## 2. Results of the lookups run now for three items
* **J04 (H-B3, one latent delayed-maturation axis):** organoid KO against WT log2 fold change: progenitor markers up (NES +1.32, VIM +0.86, SOX2 +0.51), neuronal markers down (MAP2 -0.62, SYT1 -0.50, SNAP25 -0.42), GRIN2B +0.36 (immature direction), but **GRIN2A +1.11 (3.0 against 0.9 CPM; low counts) and SLC12A5 (KCC2) +0.73 move OPPOSITE to an immature signature, and SLC12A2 (NKCC1) is flat (+0.04)**: the NKCC1-up-relative-to-KCC2 part of the single axis is not seen in the held organoid table (the ratio goes the other way). The rejecting condition ('subunit and chloride-transporter genes move opposite to or independently of the composite') is met for two of three gene predictions in this table. Limit: 4 against 2 libraries, transcript, human organoid culture age.
* **J08 (H-B4, astrocytic K+ and glutamate clearance):** astrocyte-like nuclei (Aqp4, Slc1a3 or Gfap UMI, no Snap25; about 395 nuclei per pool), control to KO CPM: Kcnj10 (Kir4.1) 71.5 to 73.3 (unchanged); Slc1a2 (EAAT2) 4580 to 6210 and Slc1a3 (EAAT1) 2042 to 3539 (HIGHER); Atp1a2 1250 to 851 (-32%); Aqp4 224 to 113 (-49%); Gja1 305 to 175 (-43%); Gfap 145 to 328 (reactive). Bulk neuron-deletion tables agree for Atp1a2 and Gja1 (hippocampus N down) and P47T shows no change. Mixed: the glutamate-transporter transcripts are up (the glutamate-clearance part is not supported), Kir4.1 is unchanged, Na/K-ATPase alpha2, aquaporin-4 and connexin-43 are lower (a K+ buffering and spatial coupling signature). Not rejected for the K+ half, counter-signal for the glutamate half; one pool per arm.
* **J18 (H-B5, burst occurrence as a network threshold set by the excised fraction):** per-slice excision fractions are not held. At pool level (hippocampus S line): nuclei carrying a Wwox UMI fall from 32.0% to 4.8% (neuron-like nuclei 34.0% to 5.4%, so about 16% of the control detection remains); **astrocyte-like nuclei also fall from 28.1% to 3.3%**, which the 'neuron-specific' wording does not predict (Synapsin-Cre activity in a glial or progenitor lineage, or nuclei whose class is assigned by markers that overlap; not resolved here). So excision is high but incomplete at pool level; the per-slice test the hypothesis needs is not held.

* **J04 (H-B3, one latent delayed-maturation axis), projection from the held calibration set GSE150123** (WWOX-unperturbed spheroids, 62 samples, six iPSC lines, slope of log2 CPM against log2 differentiation day within line; KO shift of GSE156243 projected from the progenitor and neuronal markers by the same composite). * **J06 (HSCI-4, blunted activity-dependent transcription):** within neuron-like nuclei (Snap25 UMI; 2,320 and 2,219 nuclei) the immediate-early genes are LOWER in KO: Fos 3.05 to 1.40 CPM, Arc 9.40 to 4.68, Npas4 6.96 to 4.68, Egr1 10.32 to 6.15, with lower detection fractions too. * **J17 (HSCI-5, NPY compensation):** the ligand is up in both laboratories but the RECEPTORS are down: neuron-like nuclei Npy1r 17.5 to 7.6 CPM, Npy2r 9.6 to 3.0, Npy5r 9.7 to 4.6 (-57%, -69%, -53%); P47T (independent hypomorph) Npy1r -0.96 (CTX, FDR 0.22) and -0.94 (PFC, FDR 0.028), Npy2r -0.89 (CTX, FDR 0.047), -0.82 (HPC), -0.60 (PFC), Npy5r about 0.
