# PRESIDIO-015: held-data pre-test of the five ADV leap hypotheses (run by SCI, the author's counterpart; 2026-11-16)

Non-canonical working note. Not medical advice; no dose, no recommendation to use any agent; reference genotype class only. A separate file: ADV's `LEAP_ADV_HYPOTHESES.md` (frozen b64910d41d4c0bba7916db8bc9f94573207c9a48) is not edited. I am not the judge: nothing below scores novelty. Labels: **INDEPENDENT TEST** = the lookup was fixed by ADV's text and I had not looked at those values before running it; **CONSISTENCY CHECK** = I could not avoid prior knowledge of the value or the lookup cannot reach the hypothesis' own claim. Everything is read from held files by `adv_pretests.py` (outputs in `adv_pretest_output/`, reproducible: `python3 -I adv_pretests.py run <repo> <files> <xlrd dir> <out>`).

## Held material and its limits (apply to every line below)
* **Single-nucleus pair GSE158684:** hippocampus, P17, S (Synapsin-Cre) deletion, ONE pool per arm (control 3,182 nuclei; knockout 2,920). No replicates: every number is descriptive. Wwox itself is a check: 110.43 to 12.25 CPM pseudobulk; within interneuron-like nuclei 145.04 to 17.69 CPM; within mature-oligodendrocyte nuclei 123.71 to 11.28 CPM, so the deletion is present in those classes too.
* **Bulk tables (GSE158681/2):** committed gene-level rows only where |centred log2 ratio| >= 0.585, 2 to 4 samples per arm (`descriptive_only`). A gene "not among the rows" changed by less than that or was filtered; it is not shown unchanged.
* **P47T (independent hypomorph line, Aldaz laboratory):** all tested genes, 5 v 5 (cerebellum 4 v 3), FDR from the committed table.
* **Organoids (GSE156243):** 4 knockout against 2 wild-type libraries, library-size normalised in my script.
* Transcript levels are not signalling, myelin ultrastructure, currents or metabolic flux.

## H-ADV-1 (WWOX loss weakens ErbB4 signalling in PV+ interneurons) - INDEPENDENT TEST at transcript level
* **Lookup:** Erbb4, Nrg1, Nrg2, Nrg3, Pvalb, interneuron markers, in snRNA, bulk tables and P47T.
* **Values.** snRNA pseudobulk CPM, control to knockout: Erbb4 1406.12 to 1185.24 (-16%); detected in 33.3% and 34.7% of nuclei. Within interneuron-like nuclei (>=1 UMI of Gad1, Gad2 or Slc32a1 and Snap25; 555 and 556 nuclei, 17.4% and 19.0% of the pool): Erbb4 4176.69 to 3651.69 CPM (-13%), detected 52.3% and 54.1%. Nrg1 1108.66 to 1506.59 CPM (up); within interneuron-like nuclei 659.99 to 1179.45 (up). Pvalb 3.79 to 2.22 CPM (70 and 37 UMI in total: too few to say anything; within interneuron-like nuclei 10.24 to 3.74). Bulk: Erbb4 not among the neuron-deletion rows; Pvalb down in all three hippocampal contrasts (22.18 to 6.48 TPM S; 21.34 to 11.57 N; 19.49 to 8.47 null); Nrg1 down in H_S (6.86 to 2.44 TPM) but up in the single-nucleus pool, Nrg2 down in H_N, Nrg3 up in H_N. P47T: Erbb4 -0.24, +0.08, -0.13, +0.08 (CTX, HPC, PFC, CB; all FDR >= 0.4); Pvalb +0.13, -0.04, 0.00 (not reduced). Interneuron-subtype shifts are line-specific (Cck down in all three neuron-deletion hippocampal contrasts, up in P47T CTX +0.90 and PFC +0.70; Lamp5 down in P47T CTX and PFC).
* **For or against the rejecting observation.** The rejecting observation ("ErbB4 or its target genes are not reduced in PV-class pools of Wwox-deficient tissue") is MET at transcript level: Erbb4 is not reduced in interneuron-like nuclei (-13%, same detection rate), nor in P47T. This is evidence against a transcript-level ErbB4 deficit, not against a signalling deficit (the claimed mechanism is binding and signalling, which neither mRNA nor these tables measure). The class-localisation table (GSE52564) shows Erbb4 is not PV-specific (neurons 7.37, OPC 7.40, NFO 7.06, myelinating oligodendrocytes 3.22 FPKM), so a PV-class pool cannot be read from a bulk class.
* **Limits:** one pool per arm; Pvalb is detected in 1.7% and 1.1% of nuclei, below what a comparison needs; "interneuron-like" is my definition; no phospho-ErbB4 or neuregulin-stimulation data are held. **Result: not supported at transcript level; neither rejected nor tested at the signalling level.**

## H-ADV-2 (burst depends on energy substrate through KATP or the ketone/lactate route) - CONSISTENCY CHECK only
* **Lookup:** glycolysis, lactate and ketone-handling genes, KATP subunits, in bulk tables, P47T and class localisation.
* **Values.** No coherent direction across lines. Glycolytic genes: Gpi1 up in cortex S (1207.29 to 1587.54 TPM) and hippocampus N (727.34 to 1008.36) and in P47T CTX +0.35 and PFC +0.32 (FDR 0.032, 0.0093); Pkm up in hippocampus N (580.74 to 1053.98) and P47T CTX +0.34, PFC +0.22; Pfkl DOWN in three neuron-deletion contrasts (cortex S 100.74 to 56.88; hippocampus S 77.98 to 37.29; null 39.91 to 22.44) but UP in P47T (+0.48 CTX, +0.35 PFC); Ldha down in hippocampus N (269.56 to 112.06); Hk2 up in P47T PFC (+0.67, FDR 0.004). Ketone and lactate transport: Oxct1 down in hippocampus N (284.87 to 116.28 TPM), Slc16a1 down in hippocampus N (27.01 to 10.52), Slc16a7 down in hippocampus S (9.27 to 4.39), Hmgcs2 up in cortex S (7.29 to 17.42; low counts), Bdh1 not among the rows. KATP: Kcnj11 down in hippocampus S (18.95 to 8.80 TPM), Abcc8 down in cortex S (11.64 to 5.69) and lower in all four P47T regions (log2FC -0.37, -0.77, -0.46, -0.81; FDR 0.84, 0.37, 0.16, 0.12). Class localisation (GSE52564, FPKM): Kcnj11 and Abcc8 are neuronal (5.91 and 5.50 in neurons, <= 1.82 elsewhere); Oxct1 and Bdh1 are broadly expressed; Slc16a1 (MCT1) is endothelial (1003.01) against 5.85 in neurons.
* **For or against the rejecting observation.** The rejecting observation is a functional one (no genotype-by-substrate interaction on burst rate); no held table reaches it. The only directional hint is lower KATP subunit transcripts in two neuron-deletion contrasts and a downward trend in P47T, which would, if it reflected protein, leave fewer KATP targets for an opener class; it does not test the hypothesis.
* **Limits:** bulk tissue transcript levels; no flux, no lactate or ketone measurements; the lines disagree on glycolytic genes in direction. **Result: untestable from held data; no consistent metabolic transcript signature; KATP subunit transcripts mildly lower (mild counter-signal to a KATP-opener route).**

## H-ADV-3 (myelin thinning of PV axons contributes to reduced inhibitory output) - CONSISTENCY CHECK (the Plp1 fall was already known)
* **Lookup:** Plp1, Mbp, Mobp and other oligodendrocyte-lineage genes, with the composition control (nuclei fraction), in snRNA, bulk, P47T.
* **Values.** Pool-level fall: Plp1 392.42 to 135.68 CPM (-65%), Mbp 664.19 to 201.47 (-70%), Mobp 217.93 to 46.05 (-79%), Mog 88.24 to 29.54, Mag 71.42 to 31.94, Cnp 45.93 to 24.67. **Composition:** nuclei carrying >=1 UMI of Mobp, Mog or Mag fall from 1,045 of 3,182 (32.8%) to 352 of 2,920 (12.1%), a 63% fall of the share. **Within mature-oligodendrocyte nuclei** (the composition-adjusted view): Mbp 1647.68 to 1050.52 CPM (-36%), Plp1 1090.21 to 739.29 (-32%), Mobp 696.93 to 345.96 (-50%), Mog 282.20 to 221.92 (-21%). The progenitor/OPC side: Pdgfra 14.66 to 29.60 CPM (up), Sox10 29.97 to 39.92, Olig2 7.57 to 14.23 (up). Bulk: Plp1, Mbp, Mobp, Mog, Mag, Cnp, Olig1, Enpp6, Cldn11, Nfasc DOWN in all four neuron-deletion contrasts (e.g. hippocampus S Mbp 3294.14 to 745.47 TPM, -77%; cortex S Mbp 4000.26 to 1752.07; null 2767.34 to 684.64); Cntnap1 down in hippocampus S (50.58 to 23.70). P47T: Plp1 -0.51 (CTX), -0.33 (HPC), -0.60 (PFC), +0.35 (CB); Mbp -0.71, -0.36, -0.65, +0.79; none with FDR < 0.1; Cldn11 down in PFC (-0.72, FDR 0.033).
* **For or against the rejecting observation.** The rejecting observation ("Mbp unchanged after composition adjustment") is NOT met: after restricting to mature-oligodendrocyte nuclei Mbp is still 36% lower, so more than composition is involved in this one pool (selection by Mobp, Mog or Mag UMI is correlated with the genes compared, which biases a within-class Mobp comparison; Mbp and Plp1 are the cleaner readouts). The pool-level fall is mostly composition (fewer mature-oligodendrocyte nuclei, 63%), as the CTGF package said. **The specific claim, PV-axon myelin, cannot be reached**: snRNA has no axonal compartment and Mbp, Caspr and PV-axon identity are not held.
* **Limits:** one pool per arm; nuclei capture and ambient RNA differ by pool; the neuron-deletion bulk tables have the same composition problem; P47T shows the same direction, smaller and not significant. **Result: consistent with the myelin-gene fall being larger than composition alone in the single pool, but untestable for PV axons.**

## H-ADV-4 (an mTORC1 activation signature in the held knockout tables, not explained by progenitor state) - INDEPENDENT TEST of the signature; the held GSEA is a held result, the panel is mine
* **Lookup:** (i) the held IT12 pre-ranked hallmark GSEA of P47T (`p47t_it12_20261031/results/gsea_reimpl_it12.tsv`); (ii) my own DECLARED 25-gene panel of canonical mTORC1 anabolic targets (Eif4ebp1, Srebf1, Hmgcs1, Hmgcr, Insig1, Ldlr, Fasn, Acly, Slc7a5, Slc3a2, Cad, Ppat, Gart, Mthfd2, Shmt2, Pgk1, Ldha, Tpi1, Hk2, Gpi1, Eif4a1, Eef1a1, Rps6, Rpl13a, Rpl3) and the Rps/Rpl ribosomal genes, median centred log2FC against 5,000 random same-size gene sets, in P47T and organoids; (iii) the neuron-deletion rows; (iv) progenitor and maturation markers in organoids. **The MSigDB hallmark gene list itself is not held, so I did not recompute the hallmark score; the panel is not the hallmark.**
* **Values.** Held GSEA (IT12 reimplementation; gene-label permutation, anti-conservative by its own README): HALLMARK_MTORC1_SIGNALING positive in all four P47T regions, NES 2.06 (HPC), 1.78 (CTX), 1.54 (PFC), 2.02 (CB), BH q 0.0025, 0.0085, 0.0081, 0.0069. My panel in P47T: median log2FC +0.143 (CTX, empirical p 0.011), +0.214 (HPC, p < 0.0002), +0.115 (PFC, p 0.013), +0.160 (CB, p 0.024): small and consistently positive. Ribosomal-protein genes (TOP mRNAs) are NOT consistently up: CTX +0.004 (p 0.90), HPC -0.09 (p < 0.0002, down), PFC -0.12 (down), CB +0.41 (up). Organoids (KO 4 v WT 2): panel median -0.112 (p 0.21, no signal); ribosomal genes -0.221 (p < 0.0002, down). **Composition control in organoids:** progenitor markers up (Nes +1.32, Vim +0.86, Sox2 +0.51, Hes1 +0.39 log2) and neuronal markers down (Map2 -0.62, Syt1 -0.50, Snap25 -0.42, Stmn2 -0.41), i.e. a progenitor shift exists in the knockout organoids and no mTORC1 signal rides on it. Neuron-deletion rows: mTORC1 targets are mostly DOWN where present (Hmgcs1, Hmgcr, Ldlr, Cad, Pgk1, Ldha, Eif4a1, Rpl13a down in hippocampus N; Ldlr down in all four contrasts; Gpi1 up; Ppat up).
* **For or against the rejecting observation.** The rejecting observation ("no hallmark mTORC1 signature in the held KO tables, or a signature that tracks progenitor state") is partly met and partly not. NOT met in the independent P47T line: a positive, replicated hallmark enrichment and a small positive panel shift in all four regions, with no organoid-style progenitor shift to explain it (the P47T composition control in IT12 covers glia and HIF sets, not mTORC1). Met in the organoids (no signal) and in the neuron-deletion tables (targets mostly down, ribosomal genes not up). So the signature appears in ONE independent hypomorph line and in neither of the other two model families.
* **Limits:** the hallmark enrichment is the IT12 reimplementation with a known anti-conservative permutation; the panel is my own and small; effect sizes are small (median 0.1 to 0.2 log2); transcript signature is not mTORC1 kinase activity (no phospho-S6 held); P47T has no composition control for this set. **Result: a weak, line-specific signature in P47T; absent in organoids and in the neuron-deletion tables; not composition-explained in P47T by anything held, and the organoid contrast has a progenitor shift without the signature.**

## H-ADV-5 (the discriminating readout is the SHAPE of the dose-response curve in null against wild type) - design hypothesis: held curve pairs
* **Lookup:** any held WWOX-model result with two or more exposure levels of one agent in both genotypes.
* **Result: none found.** The held WWOX pharmacology is single-level: d-APV 50 uM in knockout slices only (Breton 2021, 5 slices, 3 animals); ethosuximide and lithium at one level each against PTZ-alone (Cheng 2020, Fig 7; my quantification `fig7_quant_ethosuximide_lithium_sci_20261115/`), with the wild type near the floor; Mierau 2004's ifenprodil at 3 uM only (wild type). No agent has two levels in either genotype, so no held curve pair can show parallel or non-parallel shifts. What the held data do contain is the reason the hypothesis matters: my NX1 sizing note (`nx1_sizing_sci_20261115/`) shows that with wild-type baselines about 7 times lower the absolute-scale interaction cannot separate null-specific from proportional effects, so the ratio-scale/curve-shape reading is the only one available. **Cannot be pre-tested from held data; it is a design requirement for the L7 brief (two exposure levels per arm, ratio scale), not a table lookup.**
