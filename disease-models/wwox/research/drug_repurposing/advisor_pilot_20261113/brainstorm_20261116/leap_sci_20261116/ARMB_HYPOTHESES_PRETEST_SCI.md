## H-C4 (PTZ as a probe of GABA-A reserve) — U, independent (premise corrected)
* The held numbers (`fig7_quant_ethosuximide_lithium_sci_20261115/output/window_statistics.tsv`, PTZ-alone mean stage per window, null over wild type): ethosuximide experiment 1-10 min 2.20, 10-30 1.68, 30-60 **7.38**, 1-60 3.26; lithium experiment 1-10 5.29, 10-30 4.36, 30-60 **4.17**, 1-60 4.53. The delegate's "about 7 against about 3" compares a late-window ratio in one experiment with a peak-stage ratio in the other. Matched on window, the two experiments give 3.3 v 4.5 over the whole hour and 7.4 v 4.2 in the late window: a between-experiment spread of about 1.4-fold over the hour and 1.8-fold in the late window, which is the size of the effect the hypothesis wants to read, so the same-day design the delegate asks for is needed, as it says.
* Heterozygote over wild type is intermediate for PTZ in both experiments from minute 10 on (1.3 to 2.1; whole hour 1.39 and 1.59; 0.97 in the first ten minutes of the ethosuximide experiment).
* Other convulsants: the held origin hunt contains no same-litter second convulsant for a Wwox mouse. It does hold a different stimulus in a different species and laboratory: audiogenic seizures in 95% of lde/lde rats (Suzuki 2009, PMID 19500159) and spontaneous seizures in the neuron-deletion mouse, so non-PTZ seizure susceptibility exists, but no ratio can be formed from it (different species, stimulus and scoring).
* Reading: nothing in the held data can reject or support the specificity claim; the quantitative premise needs the correction above.
