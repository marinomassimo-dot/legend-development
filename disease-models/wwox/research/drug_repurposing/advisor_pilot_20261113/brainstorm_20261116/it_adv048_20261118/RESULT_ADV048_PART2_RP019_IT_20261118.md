2. **What it measured:** the NUMBER OF PV-IMMUNOPOSITIVE CELLS by stereology: "The overall reduction of PV+ interneurons in Wwox KO mice amounted to 44% when the hippocampus was taken in its entirety (p < .005)"; KO n = 7 against WT n = 7 (unit: animals); dentate gyrus p < .005, CA1 p < .05, CA3 "significantly low" with no p value; "some hippocampal areas ... showed a complete absence of such cells". ## What the disinhibition explanation needs from the PV facts
The explanation: an NMDAR pore blocker, acting preferentially on fast-spiking PV interneurons that are driven through NMDARs, lowers inhibition and so raises net excitation in a slice whose spontaneous inhibition (sIPSC) is already smaller. It needs, and the repository does not hold:
1. **PV cells present and functional in the burst preparation** (neocortex L2/3, P13 to P17, neuron-targeted deletion): the only count is hippocampal and global; if PV cells are lost, there is less PV output to disinhibit; if only the marker is low, the cells may still be there (explanations a vs b differ here).
2. **An NMDAR-dependent excitatory drive onto those PV cells** (and a pore blocker's access at the use-dependent state), which no Wwox report measures.
3. **That the smaller sIPSC is PV-derived**: sIPSC is not cell-type specific, and PV vs somatostatin or other sources are not separated; with a chloride-loaded pipette the amplitude does not give a physiological drive.
4. **Net effect sign in the held slice:** the competitive blocker d-APV abolishes the bursts there (rebound above baseline after washout is the held hint of a counter-effect); so any disinhibition has to be weaker than the excitatory block in that preparation, or specific to pore-blocker kinetics. This is a tension the repository holds, not a result.
Each need is an IPOTESI; none is a held measurement.

## One discriminating observation
In the Synapsin-Cre knockout neocortex at P14, P21 and P30 (the same animals as the burst recordings), with a genetic PV reporter (marker-independent): (i) PV-reporter-positive cell density, PV protein and Pvalb mRNA in the same sections (with a pan-GABAergic or pan-neuronal denominator), and WFA and Kv3.1 per PV cell; (ii) from identified PV cells, the evoked PV-to-pyramid IPSC at physiological chloride, and PV firing under a pore blocker against a matched-current d-APV condition.
* Reporter cells reduced, all markers lower in proportion: **loss (a)**.
* Reporter cells present, PV protein and mRNA low, evoked IPSC normal: **marker down-regulation (b)**.
* Reporter cells present, marker present, WFA and Kv3.1 low at P21 and normal by P30: **delayed maturation (c)**.
* Reporter cells and marker normal, evoked IPSC or PV firing reduced: **circuit change (d)**; if in addition the pore blocker does not lower PV firing or the burst threshold more than d-APV, the disinhibition route has no support in this preparation.
It needs no therapeutic target and no drug at the first step (i).
