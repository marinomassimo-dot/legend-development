BLIND VERIFICATION PACKET A (hypothesis level; public, class level; nothing here is medical advice)

Each block holds one sentence set that a repository record is about to carry, the record's own context fields, and the held source files.
Check the sentence set against the sources only. Do not open the repository's cards, result files, reports or other notes: read only the files listed in the block
(and, for a figure, an image file of the same article under the same files/ tree). No network.
For each block give one verdict:
  MUST CHANGE: a word, number, attribution, comparator, unit, species, cell, group size, direction or generalisation is wrong, unsupported or says more than the source;
  ADD: supported, but an omission would mislead a reader who sees only this sentence set;
  STANDS: supported as written.
Grade every clause, not only the main fact. Absolute words (only, no, none, every, all) need the whole table or text read; if you cannot, say UNSETTLED for that word.
For a negative, say what you searched. For a number read from a figure, say figure, panel and bar, and view the image. A clause about another assertion id (for example DR-A02-004
or DR-A02-008) or about the repository's own plan is outside your check: say OUTSIDE. For MUST CHANGE and ADD give the minimal replacement wording; a card may not carry
a dose written with a mass-per-body-weight or concentration symbol, so describe doses in words (millimolar and micromolar written out are allowed).
Report: a summary table (id, verdict), then per block the findings with locators. Say which files and figures you opened and which you could not.

=== BLOCK N02
record: card on the NMDA-receptor intervention class, field 'functional evidence available' (WWOX systems)
SENTENCE SET: d-APV abolishes bursting in the knockout slice, reversibly, and suppresses a delayed evoked response at higher stimulus strengths; excitatory current amplitude differs by about six percent at -70 mV (AMPAR-weighted); inhibitory current amplitude about halved and frequency reduced; depolarised resting potential. No pharmacologically isolated NMDAR or AMPAR current and no receptor subunit protein or surface level has been found measured in any WWOX system (search of held sources, 2026-11-26); Wwox-knockout mouse hippocampus and hippocampal neural stem cells show lower Grin2c transcript (Hussain 2019, PMID 30290271), and neuron-specific (Synapsin-Cre) knockout neocortical layer II/III pyramidal neurons show larger miniature excitatory currents at -70 mV, not pharmacologically isolated, with unchanged decay (Breton 2021, PMID 34634460). No subunit-selective, pore-blocking, glycine-site or AMPAR drug was found applied to any Wwox system.

=== BLOCK DR-A02-007
source: Gupta 2023, PMID 37319242 (Results 'Low dose/long-duration studies'); NOT WWOX EVIDENCE
target_id: CBS p.I278T
endpoint: liver CBS activity under long, low-exposure bortezomib, alone or with a heat-shock co-inducer
direction: NO_CHANGE
reading_depth: partial_fulltext_read
context: {"disease_id": "CBS deficiency, homocystinuria (not WWOX)", "genotype": "human CBS I278T transgene on a Cbs-null mouse background", "species": "mouse", "cell_type": "liver", "developmental_stage": "adult, about a month"}
SENTENCE SET: With better-tolerated but not toxicity-free exposure the activity gain was not significant (the authors cite limited assay sensitivity at very low activity); a white-cell and lymphocyte reduction was still present and one animal was moribund. The authors conclude that proteasome inhibitors are probably not feasible as monotherapy at non-toxic exposure.
