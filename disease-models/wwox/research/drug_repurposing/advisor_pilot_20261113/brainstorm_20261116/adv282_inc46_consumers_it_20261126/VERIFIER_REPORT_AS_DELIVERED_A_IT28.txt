BLOCK N02: ADD
Checked against Hussain (PMC XML) and Breton (PMC XML, figures, supplement).

Supported clauses:
- d-APV (50 micromolar) eliminated spontaneous bursting in the slice, and frequency, duration and amplitude returned to normal on washout (Breton Results; fig 3C and 3D, d-APV bar at zero, washout bar present; n = 51 bursts, 5 slices, 3 animals).
- Delayed evoked response suppressed "at higher stimulus strengths":
  - The main text only says the knockout response was larger "at multiple stimulus strengths" and that d-APV "reversibly suppressed" it.
  - Supp Fig S3C (supplement legend and image viewed): d-APV p values left to right were 0.48, 0.65, 0.17, 0.023, 0.010, 0.0006. So it is significant only at the three highest strengths (40, 50 and 100 microamp, about 0.4 of baseline).
  - Washout rose above baseline. This clause STANDS.
- Spontaneous excitatory current: mean amplitude 23.3 picoamp (control) against 24.7 picoamp (knockout), a 6.0 percent difference. This is "S-CTL", wild type plus heterozygote, at -70 mV.
- Inhibitory current: amplitude 57.3 against 27.5 picoamp (about halved). Frequency 10.3 against 8.4 Hz (reduced).
- Resting potential of pyramidal neurons: -79.25 (wild type) against -73.62 (knockout) mV, marked significant against wild type (Table 1).
- Miniature excitatory currents: larger amplitude in knockout against wild type and heterozygote (KS test). Only TTX was added, so they are not pharmacologically isolated. Decay 5.18 against 4.84 ms, p = 0.2982.
- Grin2c: lower in hippocampal neurospheres (RNA-seq, log2 fold change -3.22; 2 knockout against 3 wild type) and in whole hippocampus (qRT-PCR, n = 4 against 4).
- In these two sources I found no isolated NMDAR or AMPAR current, no receptor protein, and no NMDAR-drug arm other than d-APV. Searched: NMDA, AMPA, APV, CNQX, NBQX, memantine, ketamine, MK-801, ifenprodil, perampanel, glycine, d-serine, Grin, Gria.
- The absolute clauses "in any WWOX system" and "to any Wwox system" are UNSETTLED. I can only verify the two held sources.

Omissions that would mislead (ADD):
1. The first clause says "the knockout slice" without the model. It is a neuron-specific Synapsin-Cre knockout, in neocortical slices. The hippocampal slices did not burst.
2. "Excitatory current amplitude differs by about six percent" gives no direction and does not say spontaneous. The next sentence uses "miniature", so a reader could confuse the two. It is larger, in sEPSC.
3. Hussain Table 1 (neurosphere RNA-seq) also lists Grin2b, an NMDAR subunit, as lower (log2 fold change -2.14, p = 2.75E-06, FDR 3.6E-04). The text promotes only Grin2c, Grm3 and Kcna2 as lower in vivo. Grin2b is not reported in the in vivo qPCR. Omitting it understates the subunit signal on an NMDAR class card.
4. "AMPAR-weighted" is the record's inference, not a statement in the source. The source only says unchanged decay implies "no overt change" in the NMDAR:AMPA proportion. Mark it as an inference.

Minimal replacement wording:
"In the neuron-specific (Synapsin-Cre) knockout neocortical slice, d-APV (50 micromolar) abolishes bursting, reversibly, and suppresses a delayed evoked response at the three highest stimulus strengths; spontaneous excitatory current mean amplitude is about six percent larger than in wild-type-plus-heterozygous controls at -70 mV (inferred AMPAR-weighted, not isolated); spontaneous inhibitory current amplitude is about halved and frequency reduced; pyramidal resting potential is depolarised against wild type. ... Wwox-knockout hippocampus and hippocampal neural stem cells show lower Grin2c transcript; the neural stem cell RNA-seq also lists lower Grin2b, not reported for hippocampus (Hussain 2019). ..."

Faults:

   - "Weaker on HDAC1 in a third that also lists the sodium salt" is wrong or dangling. There is no "second" document in the sentence. BLOCK DR-H02-008: MUST CHANGE
Source: PMC page PMC4102583 (PMID 24321005).

Supported:
- CPP-115 and vehicle arms only; vigabatrin appears only in Table 1 and the Discussion, citing the authors' earlier study.
- Reduction was transient: reduced behavioral spasms only on PN5 to PN7, and none after PN7. 5 mg/kg/day was "eventually lethal" (100 percent by PN8, 8/9 dead by PN7).
- The abstract says "did not accelerate spasms cessation" and "did not alter neurodevelopmental outcomes or visuospatial learning". It does not mention brain histology.
- The page reports no myelin or white-matter histology (only vigabatrin background in the Introduction and references).

Fault:
- "The held full-text page scores hippocampal sclerosis" is wrong. The paper scores hippocampal injury (HS1 to HS3, with 0.5 added for left anterior-dorsal involvement), defined as the extent of injury from the model's right-sided infusion (Table 2, thionin-stained PN20 sections). The word "sclerosis" occurs only in "tuberous sclerosis".
- "Equal across groups": "HS scores were equally distributed across treatment groups (Fisher's exact test, P = 0.69)". Basis: 23 brains from the Barnes-maze animals in the 0, 0.1 and 1 mg/kg groups only (8, 10 and 5). The 5 mg/kg group is absent, and 91 percent of scores were HS3 to HS3.5, i.e. near ceiling. Cortical injury was graded by autopsy inspection, not histology.

Minimal replacement:
"The held full-text page scores model-induced hippocampal injury (extent score on thionin-stained sections, 23 brains from the 0, 0.1 and 1 mg/kg groups; 91 percent scored 3 to 3.5), equally distributed across those groups, and reports no myelin or white-matter histology."
