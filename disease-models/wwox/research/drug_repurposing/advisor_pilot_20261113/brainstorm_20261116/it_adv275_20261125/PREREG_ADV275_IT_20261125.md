## 1. Question and comparator

Does any deposited neural-lineage expression dataset (GEO, ArrayExpress/BioStudies) measure WWOX (mouse Wwox) after valproate or another HDAC inhibitor, and in which direction?

Comparator: the concurrent vehicle or untreated control of the same series, same system, same time point. No comparison across series.

## 2. Eligibility, fixed now

- **Tier A (can fill the gap):** human or mouse non-transformed neural material: primary neurons or glia, neural stem or progenitor cells, ESC/iPSC-derived neural cells, brain organoids, brain or spinal-cord tissue after in vivo (including prenatal) exposure.
- **Tier B (reported separately, does not fill the gap):** neuroblastoma, glioma, glioblastoma, medulloblastoma lines or xenografts.
- Exposure: an HDAC inhibitor alone, against a concurrent control. Combinations only if a single-agent arm exists.
- Replicates: at least 2 biological samples per arm for a value; at least 3 for a direction call.
- Retrieval: processed table from the NCBI or EBI file service by a plain request, no login, no contact address, no browser-style identity. Anything else is a route result.
- Excluded: papers' own thresholded tables, rat and other species (counted, not read, because the brief says human or mouse), non-expression assays, single-cell deposits without a processed per-sample table.
- Platform A family (the Leist and Hescheler/Sachinidis ESNATS-lineage deposits behind Balmer, Rempel and Krug) is tagged and never counted as an independent second platform.
- Cap: if more than 25 Tier A series are eligible, read them in this order until the time budget ends: human before mouse, VPA before other compounds, larger n first; the unread ones are listed by accession.

## 3. Reading rules, fixed now

- WWOX located by gene symbol and by every probe or row that maps to it; the file, column or probe named for each value.
- Value: mean of treated over mean of control on the linear scale (from log2 values for arrays and log2 CPM for counts), with n per arm; Welch two-sided t-test on log2 values when n >= 3 per arm. One pre-named gene, so no multiplicity correction; the number of series and probes is stated instead.
- Call per probe: **up** = p < 0.05 and ratio >= 1.25; **down** = p < 0.05 and ratio <= 0.80; otherwise **not distinguished**. Ratios >= 1.5 are noted. Series call: all called probes agree, else **mixed**; no called probe is not distinguished. n = 2: descriptive only.
- Detectability: array probe intensity below the 25th percentile of its arrays, or RNA-seq mean raw count under 20 in both arms, is flagged **near background** and gives no call.
- **Sentinel read first** (exposure took effect): H1F0 (H1-0, mouse H1f0) and CDKN1A (Cdkn1a); the sentinel passes if either rises at least 1.5-fold. A series whose sentinel fails gives no WWOX reading for or against.
