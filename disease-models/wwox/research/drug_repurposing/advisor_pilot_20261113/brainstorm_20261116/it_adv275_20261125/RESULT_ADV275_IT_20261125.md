**Correction, 2026-11-25 (blind check).** The failed prediction was registered on series, not on the «7 of 22» contrasts (3 of 14 series; one lenient rule gives 8 of 15); 28 of the 57 contrasts fail the sentinel and give no reading; GSE185453 is CI-994, not valproate; the GSE129241 1.28 call is 1.21 under median-of-ratios. ## 1. Answer

**Yes, many do, at transcript level.** In these deposits WWOX mRNA is higher after an HDAC inhibitor in several neural systems, not distinguished from control in some, and **no series call is lower** in any of the 57 contrasts. Numbers below use the replicate units corrected after the blind check (section 4).

* **Valproate, human neural material, up in four deposits from four institutions** (n control/treated; WWOX ratio): NGN2 induced neurons, added day 1 (GSE129241, 4/4, 1.28), iPSC brain organoids at 400 uM (GSE232218, 4/4, 1.41), iPSC spinal-cord organoids (GSE241903, 3/3, 1.91), forebrain organoids of two lines (GSE187006, 3/3 each, 1.50 and 1.63). A fifth deposit agrees in direction but is **one experiment with technical replicates** (GSE166297, 1.63 on NCBI counts, 1.45 on the depositor's DESeq2 values): consistent, not a fifth replication.
* Other compounds, one deposit each: trichostatin A in iPSC cortical neurons 4.1 (GSE302545, 4/4), sodium butyrate in iPSC neurons 2.6 to 2.7 on two probes with a third probe at 0.73 not distinguished (GSE46043, 3/3), vorinostat 2.5 uM in iPSC neurons 1.41 (GSE115205 control line, 5/5).
* Mouse: up with prenatal valproate in E14 brain on one of four probes (GSE42904, 3/3, 2.2), with valproate in E16.5 cortical neurons (GSE249964, 4/4 mice, 1.80) and with trichostatin A in striatal neurospheres at 24 h on three of four probes (GSE31792, 6/4, 1.45 to 2.17).
* **Not distinguished with a passing sentinel** (pre-registered rule: H1F0 or CDKN1A at least 1.5-fold): posterior spinal-cord organoids with valproate (GSE305320, 3/3, 0.88, 95 % interval 0.64 to 1.22); adult mouse hippocampus and striatum with CI-994 (GSE185453, 4/4, 0.95 [0.75 to 1.18] and 0.88 [0.73 to 1.06]); E18 mouse hippocampus after prenatal valproate (GSE42904); and, with intervals that include the rise seen elsewhere, NPC1 organoids (1.42 [0.96 to 2.11]), organoids at 200 uM (1.12 [0.95 to 1.34]) and vorinostat 1 uM neurons (1.34 [0.99 to 1.78]).
* **Protein: not measured here and not searched.** The queries cover expression deposits; no proteomic repository was queried.

**Rejecting observation (pre-registered): met by the rule as written, but weak.** The three contrasts whose intervals leave little room for a rise each have a defect found afterwards: in GSE305320 the sentinels reach 2.1 to 2.7-fold but not p < 0.05, and dose and time are not stated; in GSE185453 the tissue was taken about two hours after one injection and the sentinels move only 1.1 to 1.6-fold (that a transcript of a gene as long as WWOX could change in that time is doubtful: my inference, not sourced). With a sentinel that must also reach p < 0.05 (a post hoc rule, stated as such) the only not-distinguished contrasts left are the vorinostat 1 uM one, which fits the rise at 2.5 uM, and the two CI-994 brain regions, which carry the two-hour limitation. So these data **neither establish that the rise is general nor show a solid neural system in which an exposed culture keeps WWOX unchanged**.

**Disposition for the DL-REPO-003 neural gap.** A neural positive control **exists at transcript level**: valproate in human neural cultures (four independent deposits up, none down). It is a control for those kinds of system, not a general one. Still missing: **WWOX protein** after valproate or any HDAC inhibitor in neural material, and a normalisation that survives the global expression shift an HDAC inhibitor causes. The valproate "Decreases expression" annotation that opened DL-REPO-003 finds no support in any neural deposit read.

## 6. Limits

Transcript only. Ratios of counts per million or of array intensities, no spike-in: an HDAC inhibitor shifts the transcriptome globally and this is not controlled. One pre-named gene, no multiplicity correction across 32 deposits; a p near 0.05 in one deposit carries little by itself (GSE129241 day 1). The sentinel rule (fold only) was too lax: a sentinel should also have had to differ from control. A passing sentinel shows the exposure acted, not that it matched across systems; doses and times differ and are not compared. Arms were assigned from sample metadata before values were read; four contrasts have a blind second reading (section 4), the other 53 do not. Methods sections of the depositing papers were not read.

"Deposited neural expression tables (ADV-275, 2026-11-25; hypothesis level): WWOX mRNA is higher with valproate in human neural cultures in four independent deposits (1.28 to 1.91-fold, 3 to 4 units per arm), and no neural contrast read calls it lower. Some neural contrasts do not distinguish it from control, but each of those has a weak exposure check, a very short exposure or an interval that includes the rise. ## Update 2026-11-25 (ADV direction after ADV-277)

The sentences above are kept as written and **no count changes**.

**Recall limit.** "No series call is lower in any of the 57 contrasts", "none down" and "no neural contrast read calls it lower" are bounded to the contrasts read, found by the strings and routes of `search_log.tsv` on 2026-11-25. ADV-277 showed on the same day that a keyword search of repository records can miss the most informative study when its title carries only an abbreviation; deposits not returned, and the unread ones of section 5, are not covered.

**Unscored, sentinel failed.** `adv275_unscored_sentinel_failed.tsv` lists the 28 contrasts (20 deposits; 15 deposits with no sentinel-passing contrast) whose transcript sentinel failed, with their WWOX direction: series call up 5, not distinguished 15, descriptive (n < 3) 5, near background 3. None is lower by the series call (the descriptive ratios include values below 1). They are not evidence for or against and are not in any count. One of the "up" calls is platform A (GSE64123), and in GSE17735 the series call "up" sits beside probe rows near background.

One further transcript table, outside the 57: the forebrain-organoid study behind E-MTAB-15191 (PMID 42032298; Supplementary Table 2, day 50, one line, four batches by its Supplementary Table 1) prints WWOX 2.06-fold with valproate (adjusted p 1.2e-13) while H1F0 (1.08-fold) and CDKN1A (0.71-fold) fail; its H1-0 **protein** rises about two-fold (ADV-277). So a transcript sentinel can fail where the exposure plainly acted, and the sentinel rule may have set aside working experiments. The rule is not changed after the fact.
