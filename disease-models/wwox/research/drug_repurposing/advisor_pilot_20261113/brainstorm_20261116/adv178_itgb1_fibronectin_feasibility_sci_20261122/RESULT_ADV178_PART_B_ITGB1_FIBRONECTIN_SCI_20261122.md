## What the adhesion and lectin results can and cannot carry
* **Fig. 3B (viewed):** adhesion to fibronectin: optical density at 570 nm about 1.1 (control) against 1.6 (WWOX shRNA), P < 0.05 by unpaired t test; the ECM-mixture panel (Fig. 3A) about 0.08 against 0.2 with large error bars, P = 0.0626. The legends and Methods give no n or replicate unit. Cells were seeded at a fixed number (100,000 per well, 90 minutes) and the read is a stain extraction, with no stated normalisation to cell number or viability; the same silenced cells have lower redox activity (Fig. 2). So adhesion strength per cell, cell size or spreading, and number adhered are not separated: this is also compatible with (c) or with a generic state change.
* **The Golgi preprint (PMID 42523332; existing partial receipt FTR-20260921-42523332-01; I did not reopen the text):** per the repository's own records the lectin readout is increased plasma-membrane HPA and GNL binding after WWOX siRNA in RPE1 cells, by microscopy on about 30 cells, as a side observation. It is non-neural, a global lectin response, and says nothing about ITGB1 glycoforms or neural glycosylation.
* **The repository's prior reads:** the 2026-09-20 audit (figures not inspected) is superseded for the figures: later receipts for the same paper (2026-08-11, 2026-10-03, 2026-10-19) record the figure images read, and the 2026-10-28 receipt read the gene-set names for other questions. From their workflow and question lines (I read those, not each receipt in full) none was about the surface or activation question or about the source of the ITGB1 sentence. This read adds that trace: I found no panel.
