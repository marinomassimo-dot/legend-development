## 3. Sources with at least one FUNCTIONAL row in a WWOX-deficient preparation
- AS (Abdel-Salam 2014), one girl with homozygous Arg54*:
  - VEP (CONDUCTION): "reduced wave amplitudes and great implicit time delays". The authors read this as impaired retinocortical transmission and attribute it to macular and optic nerve dysfunction.
  - Flash ERG (OTHER-EVOKED): moderately reduced.
  - Flicker ERG (OTHER-EVOKED): markedly reduced.
  - The patient also had optic atrophy and retinal dystrophy, so the VEP latency delay cannot be assigned to myelin.
- Borderline only, test type unnamed and not clearly an evoked potential, none counted as strict:
  - PI, 5 of 11 patients had abnormal "electrodiagnostic tests".
  - AB, hearing assessment normal in P1.1.
  - FE, hearing loss on "audiological testing".
  - AS, audiological evaluation normal.
- Secondhand only: SH Table 1 lists abnormal ERG for 8 patients described in other papers.
- Across the main text and tables of the 12 files I found no nerve conduction study, ABR, SSEP, compound action potential or conduction velocity in any WWOX patient. The Piard supplements were not available to me.

## 4. Notes for the integrator
- TE Teplyshova 2024 has no myelin readout. The own patient (40 y, homozygous p.Thr12Met) had no MRI, and CT at 35 y "did not show any significant pathological lesions". The Discussion cites, without measurement, "disrupted myelination" in imaging of WWOX-DEE patients (Battaglia 2023, Oliver 2023). It also says AAV-SynI-WWOX in Wwox-null mice rescued "myelination deficits" (Repudi 2021). These are leads to other papers, not rows.
- IA Iacomino 2020 cites Tochigi 2019 as describing "severe hypomyelination (with a reduced number of mature oligodendrocytes)" in lde/lde rats. IA's own rat work is P1 cortical-layer migration only. That too is a lead only.
- Text and legend disagreements: TA says "mainly affecting the grey matter" while the Fig 2 legend says "delayed myelination" (TA2). PI Fig 3 shows three white-matter-hypersignal panels but the text gives 2/20 (PI1). EH Table 1 puts "Posterior white matter hyperintensities" under Abdel-Salam, where AS has no such statement (EH4).
- n is unstated in two rows: TB1 (how many of the three siblings were imaged) and EH4 (per column).
- In AS, the rat lde/lde and mouse knockout columns of Table 1 list extracellular vacuoles and no myelin readout. Teplyshova and Sapuppo mention WWOX knockout mouse or lde rat only as background.
