## Question
For every held source with a myelin readout in a Wwox-deficient or WWOX/WOREE preparation: one table (source, model, age, genotype, readout, STRUCTURAL or FUNCTIONAL, n with unit, direction and size as stated, locator). Then from the table only: (a) how many sources carry a FUNCTIONAL myelin readout in a Wwox-deficient preparation; (b) which functional readout would separate a remyelination-type lever (citicoline cell-type observations, bexarotene/LXR, Slc44a1 stage dependence) from seizure suppression or a neuron-intrinsic effect, with the rival and the rejecting observation.

## Frozen matching rule (mine)
* FUNCTIONAL: a readout of signal conduction or propagation: compound action potential, conduction velocity or latency (in vivo or slice), evoked potential (motor, visual, auditory brainstem, somatosensory), nerve conduction study, propagation success.
* STRUCTURAL: histology, electron microscopy, immunostaining or immunoblot of myelin or oligodendrocyte markers (MBP, PLP, MOG, CC1, Olig2, MAG), g-ratio, myelin thickness, myelinated-axon counts, nodal or paranodal structure, MRI or diffusion imaging, myelin-gene transcript levels.
* OTHER (listed, not counted in the shares): behaviour, survival, seizure counts, body weight.
* A row is one readout in one preparation in one source. A source counts toward (a) once if it has at least one FUNCTIONAL row in a Wwox-deficient preparation (global or conditional knockout, knock-in, patient-derived or engineered knockout organoid, WOREE patient material) with a direction stated in the source. A rescue arm or a drug arm does not change the preparation.
* Abstract-only sources are labelled ABSTRACT-ONLY; no locator claim is made for them, and they count in (a) only if the abstract itself states the readout.
* The number of structural rows is reported per source and in total; shares use STRUCTURAL plus FUNCTIONAL as the denominator.
