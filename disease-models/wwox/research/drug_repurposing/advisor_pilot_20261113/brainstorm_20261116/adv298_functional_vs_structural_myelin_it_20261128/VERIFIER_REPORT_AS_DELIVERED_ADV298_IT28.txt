=== TASK 3: SYSTEMATIC PROBLEMS ===
- Rows with an unnamed test are classed FUNCTIONAL (b): AS4, PI7, AB9, FE2. The rule requires "a readout of signal conduction or propagation", and an unnamed "audiological evaluation" or "hearing assessment" does not meet it. PI7 pools unnamed tests; FE2 may be behavioural. They should be UNCLASSIFIED or OTHER. Retinal ERG (AS3, E4) is arguable as well. If hearing loss with an unnamed method is counted FUNCTIONAL, the Chong, Piard and AlBaradie 2.2 hearing entries must be added.
- The "evoked potentials of any modality" clause is applied unevenly. Breton hippocampal fEPSP/PS amplitudes (4c, synaptic excitability) and the late NMDAR response (4b) are included. The comparable Repudi Fig 2E–F evoked field amplitudes are missing from section A. This is an omission, not an error in the rows given.
- The row 4 family (Breton) carries the caveat "network propagation, not axonal conduction". The A-rows include 4, 4b and 4c at the same level as Cheng's Tc-MEP and Repudi's callosal latency. The functional set mixes conduction, synaptic excitability and unnamed clinical tests under one label.
- The STRUCTURAL sub-labels are inconsistent. MYELIN-STATED is applied to AB2 and PI3, with no myelin statement. ANATOMY-ONLY is applied to O2, whose entries do name myelin for P2, P6, P7 and P8 (and the Oliver table names it for P3 and P10 as well).
- The A1/A2 P-value assignment is the earlier reader's interpretation. The legend places N1/N2 P values in panel (i) without saying what they test, and the text says the significant latency is in 5D(i).
- Two preparation caveats. B5 is a rescued Wwox-null mouse. C11 is post-cuprizone remyelination. The human single-patient rows D2 and 8b involve uniparental disomy.
- Under "OTHER = list, do not count", normal single-patient results (R3, S3, 11f, E4, AS4, AB9) sit in the same FUNCTIONAL list as the positive findings.
