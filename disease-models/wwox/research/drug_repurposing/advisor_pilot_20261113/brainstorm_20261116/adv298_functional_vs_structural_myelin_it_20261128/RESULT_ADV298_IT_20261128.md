## 6. The held functional readouts compared (REJECTING branch)
* Direction. Every conduction or latency readout with a stated direction is abnormal in the Wwox-deficient preparation, in the same direction (longer latency or delayed): two mouse preparations (callosal slice latency; Tc-MEP onset 1.39 to 2.44 ms, amplitude 59.2 to 11.8 uV) and three single patients (VEP twice, BAEP once; no values).
* Sizes. Only Cheng gives numbers with n (mice: 10, 4, 5). Repudi Brain gives no ms values in text or legend; the 2.43 to 3.17 ms bars quoted in the repository note are that note's pixel readback, not the paper's number.
* Heterozygote. Cheng's Wwox+/- mice have a prolonged Tc-MEP latency (2.13 ms) with normal amplitude, and the text says the +/- myelin thickness and fibre density were similar (data not shown): a latency delay with no reported myelin change, unquantified.
* Pathway. Tc-MEP reads corticospinal, peripheral nerve and muscle; the same paper shows sciatic demyelination in the nulls. It cannot be assigned to central myelin. The callosal slice latency is the only central one.
* Confound. In the neuron-targeted slices bursting is the rule (86 percent of S-KO slices, 0 of 11 controls) and the evoked field peaks are larger (A23); Breton adds a late NMDAR-dependent evoked response in the same preparation. Amplitudes and peak timings of evoked responses in these slices therefore reflect excitability as well as conduction.
* Patients. Of the 11 single-patient evoked-potential or nerve-conduction rows, 5 are normal (Elsaadany ERG, Davids nerve conduction, one Weisz-Hubshman BERA, Riva ABR, Serce Pehlevan ABR) and 6 abnormal (Abdel-Salam VEP and ERG, Elsaadany VEP, You BAEP, one Weisz-Hubshman BERA, Chong VEP absent). No author attributes an abnormal one to myelin; Abdel-Salam's patient also had optic atrophy and retinal dystrophy.
* Gap that remains after the comparison: no functional readout with an intervention arm, none paired per animal with a structural readout, and no conduction velocity (latency against measured path length) in any held source.

## 7. Part (b): which functional readout would separate a remyelination-type lever from seizure suppression or a neuron-intrinsic effect (from the table only)
* Readout: callosal latency of the first component (fibre volley or N1) against measured path length, at several electrode distances, with the input-output curve kept, in slices from matched Wwox-deficient and control mice with and without the lever; velocity is the slope, offsets are the intercept. This is the design the repository note `continuous_compounds/clemastine/callosal_propagation.md` already states; no held source contains it.
* Rival: seizure suppression or a neuron-intrinsic effect. A lever that lowers burst incidence or excitability changes amplitudes, recruitment and the second and later components (the A2, A3, A23 and Breton rows are all of this kind) and the intercept, not the slope.
* Rejecting observations. (1) A change in N1 latency at one distance together with changes in amplitude and burst incidence, with the slope unchanged: rejects a remyelination-type effect. (2) A slope improvement with burst incidence unchanged: rejects seizure suppression as the explanation. (3) The same animals' myelinated-axon count and g-ratio (the Repudi Brain EM design) moving in the opposite direction or not at all with an improved slope: points to a neuron-intrinsic effect.
* Why not the held readouts alone: Tc-MEP latency is delayed in heterozygotes without reported myelin change and includes the peripheral nerve; the callosal latency has no distance series; the evoked amplitudes track excitability.
* Reachability: the raw waveforms are not held (the Brain supplement is not in the held files); contact with the authors is operator-reserved. The cheapest reachable step without spend or outside contact is a pre-registered analysis plan for the slope readout, which this section is; running it needs new data.
* Levers named in the assignment: no held source measures any functional readout after citicoline, bexarotene or an Slc44a1 manipulation in a Wwox-deficient preparation. The structural observations they lean on (CC1 counts, myelinated profiles, g-ratio) are the Repudi rows.
