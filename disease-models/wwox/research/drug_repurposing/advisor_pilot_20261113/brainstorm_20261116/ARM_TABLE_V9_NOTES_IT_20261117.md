Update 2026-11-18 (IT57, on SCI's dated early-read line in brief 13.11 (Addendum 14, after IT's independent rerun ADV-063) and the outreach package; cells C007, C011, C054; no cell, tier or origin row changed): the washout reads of the readout design keep their dates but not their meaning. The classification of the model's adaptation rebound is robust to the seed and to the time constant (second seed, tau_w 1800, 3600 and 7200 s: window 30 to 60 minutes within the band in every case), but the DATE of its end is not (10 to 30 minutes across the rerun cells), so: the reads at about 5 and 15 minutes lie inside or at the end of the rebound; **30 minutes is the end of the rebound zone, not clear of it**; **the 60-minute read is the clean one**; one optional read at about 45 minutes can be added. A burst rate near baseline at 30 minutes therefore does not by itself exclude an adaptation with a long time constant. The model is a hypothesis generator and its transfer to a slice is an INFERENZA.
