## Answer first
1. **"The tonic class" is two mechanisms, and the held rows do not make the second one tonic.** Gaboxadol is a direct agonist whose potency rises by about three orders of magnitude when a delta subunit is present (recombinant alpha4 beta3 delta 30 to 50 nM, one paper with native confirmation in delta-knockout slices, against the held 14 uM at alpha1 beta2 gamma2S). The steroids (ganaxolone, allopregnanolone and its analogues) are positive allosteric modulators of both synaptic and extrasynaptic receptors: ganaxolone 103 nM at alpha4 beta3 delta against 266 nM at alpha1 beta2 gamma2 is a 2.6-fold difference in two cell lines of one document, and zuranolone is described as acting at synaptic and extrasynaptic configurations with no selectivity claim. A modulator needs ambient GABA; an agonist does not.
2. **Age and chloride (Q1): no held or found datum places the S-KO cortex at P13 to P17.** In mouse neocortex tonic current is robust at P2 to P4 and falls steeply by P7 to P10; prefrontal E_GABA is depolarised at P10 and mature at P20 (both abstract level, other regions or ages); the P13 to P17 interval lies between the measured ages. The readings that separate hyperpolarising from depolarising action are gramicidin-perforated E_GABA against resting potential in S-KO cells, a bumetanide-free bath, and the burst and resting-potential readout already in the slice panel 6.17.
3. **Juvenile exposure (Q3): not found by these routes for any of the five agents**; what exists is trial ages (gaboxadol children 2 to 12 years and adolescents; ganaxolone 2 to 21 years with a label from 2 years; brexanolone and zuranolone adult women only), one preterm guinea-pig pup study with sedation and higher mortality on ganaxolone, and the held P8 to P9 mouse window. No agent has a measured pup brain or CSF level.
4. **Harm and failures (Q4):** gaboxadol was well tolerated in children but **missed its primary endpoint in a phase 3 Angelman trial** (difference zero, P = 0.83) after a phase 3 insomnia study in adults that showed efficacy over placebo (its abstract calls gaboxadol "previously in development"; the reason is not in the abstract); ganaxolone's controlled efficacy in a genetic developmental epilepsy exists (CDKL5, median seizure frequency -30.7 against -6.9 percent, P = 0.0036) with somnolence, pyrexia and upper respiratory infection in at least a tenth of treated patients.
5. **Seizure-model readout in a developmental or genetic epilepsy (Q5):** only ganaxolone has one in both a patient population (CDKL5) and a mouse model (adult Ube3a-deficient); gaboxadol's in vivo seizure readout is a neonatal kainate model at P6 to P9; nothing is held in any Wwox line.
6. **The one experiment (Q6):** the slice panel 6.17 session with a gramicidin-perforated E_GABA recording added in the same S-KO cells at P13 to P17, THIP as the tool and the burst readout beside d-APV; it decides at once whether the class is a candidate for a pup arm, and a pup head-to-head against ethosuximide is uninterpretable without it.
7. **The closing paragraph (end of this note):** the tonic class earns **the slice panel's gate slot (E_GABA plus THIP), not a pilot arm now**; ganaxolone, not gaboxadol, is the only conditional pilot-arm candidate, on the 6.14 condition.

## Q6. The one experiment that would order the best tonic agent against ethosuximide
**The 6.17 slice session with a gramicidin-perforated E_GABA recording added in the same S-KO layer II/III cells at P13 to P17**, THIP as the tool, burst frequency and duration and resting potential as the readout, d-APV as the positive control already specified. Why this and not the pup head-to-head: it settles the gate every tonic agent shares (is GABA depolarising here) in hours; both of its outcomes move the preference (a hyperpolarising or shunting result with burst suppression inside a plausible band moves the class forward; a depolarising result or increased bursts demotes it below ethosuximide); and a pup head-to-head (the IT59 design) cannot be read without it because no agent has a pup exposure and the sedation margin is narrow. The pup arm follows only if the slice result is positive **and** a tolerable pup exposure is measured (6.14).

## The closing paragraph: does a tonic-class agent deserve a conditional pilot arm or the slice panel's priority slot?
**The slice panel's gate slot, yes; a pilot arm now, no; if one arm later, ganaxolone rather than gaboxadol.** Gaboxadol is already in the panel and is the right **tool**: delta-preferring agonist with a measured delta sensitivity, a P6 to P9 in vivo seizure effect, and the cleanest test of the chloride gate; as an **arm** it carries two clinical programmes without a clinical win (adult insomnia, described as previously in development, reason not found; Angelman children, primary endpoint zero), no pup exposure and a narrow neonatal window. Ganaxolone is a modulator, not a delta agonist (2.6-fold in one document), so its "tonic" label is weaker; but it is the only agent with a paediatric label from 2 years, a controlled seizure benefit in a genetic developmental epilepsy and an adult mouse anticonvulsant result in a genetic model; its pup liabilities (sedation, weight, mortality in a preterm guinea-pig study) and the absence of any juvenile exposure number keep it **conditional on the 6.14 condition** (tolerable pup exposure measured, no pup tolerability signal) **and on a positive chloride-gated slice result**. Allopregnanolone, brexanolone and zuranolone add nothing here: adult-only data in these searches and no exposure or paediatric fact. **What would reorder the bridge preference** (ethosuximide weakly over the tonic class, ADV-097): (i) S-KO E_GABA hyperpolarising or shunting at P13 to P17 with THIP or ganaxolone suppressing bursts inside a plausible brain band; (ii) a non-sedating pup exposure measured for ganaxolone; (iii) ethosuximide's pup brain level well below 0.6 mM (held reversal condition). **What would demote the tonic class:** depolarising or heterogeneous E_GABA, THIP increasing bursts, or a sedation margin that leaves no efficacious non-sedating step in the pups.
