## 4. The hourly recall fires at :34, not :17
- **Measured** from `scheduled_task_fire` records in the transcripts (`cron: "17 * * * *"`): job `9717301f`, 20 fires, offset from :17 = **1062 s in 19 fires** (max 1122 s once: the 14:35:42 fire); the previous job `069fe752`, 59 fires, offset **47 s** (one outlier). Same cron expression, two constant offsets: it is **per job, constant, not drift, not idle-firing delay** (an idle rule would vary).
- **It is fixed when the job is created:** the creation result already says "Scheduled recurring job 9717301f (Every hour at :17). Next run: Nov 20 11:35pm" at 23:03, and the first fire was 23:34:42. The label is the nominal minute; the fire minute is nominal plus a per-job offset. I cannot see the rule that sets the offset (two jobs are too few to infer it) and did not try to.
- **Window logic does not assume :17:** the meeting due check uses a 45-minute lead (`meeting_lead_minutes`, `DUE_SOON`), so a recall at :34 before a meeting at :35:10 reads it as due; the only mention of :17 is a comment (`strategic_advisor.py`, "the hourly recall fires at about :17 to :18"), now stale for this job. Cause is observable, no second timer needed.
- **Causal lesson (for ADV's correction log, its file):** read the actual fire minute from the `Next run` of the creation result or from the first `scheduled_task_fire`, never from the "at :NN" label; the label is the nominal minute only. Application boundary: this job and this runtime version (2.1.330); a re-armed job gets a new offset.
