# RP-019 first pass from complete held originals: what is measured about PV cells, and what separates loss, delayed marker onset, state and circuit change (SCI, 2026-11-19, ADV-085; hypothesis level; non-canonical; reference genotype class only; no dose, no recommendation, no therapeutic target assumed; not medical advice; nothing applied)

## 0. Answer first

1. **The stop rule applies, with one qualification.** The held originals contain **one** PV cell count and **no** per-animal or per-cell PV value. The count is the CLAIM 005 premise itself: Hussain 2019, germline full Wwox knockout, hippocampus, 2 weeks, stereology of PV-immunoreactive cell bodies, **7 knockout against 7 control animals**: dentate gyrus p < .005, CA1 p < .05, CA3 "significantly low" with no P value in the text, whole hippocampus **−44 percent** (p < .005). Per-animal counts sit in bar charts of figures that are **not held**. No other held original (11 read; 82 held full texts scanned) counts PV cells, measures Pvalb in its own tissue, or identifies PV cells in a recording. ADV-068 used per-animal bulk transcripts only, so this count is "beyond" it only as a group-level premise already known.
2. **For RP-019's decision (does the premise move from "not measured at cell level" to a stated range?):** it moves only to "**one group-level marker-positive count, one laboratory, one preparation, no per-animal values accessible**". Not a range: a single point estimate for the whole hippocampus (−44 percent) with no dispersion in the text. The count is compatible with loss (a) and with PV-negative cells that are present (b) alike: nothing in the paper has a denominator (no pan-interneuron, pan-neuronal or fate-map count).
3. **The new facts relative to IT's RP-019 result of 2026-11-18 and my ADV-068/070/076 notes** (IT's result stands; section 5): (i) the human organoid papers are **not silent on the GABAergic population, they run the other way from the mouse count**: GAD67 immunostaining up, GAD1 and GAD2 transcripts up, GAD1 and SLC32A1 up in RNA-seq, GABRB2 and GABRB3 down, NPY down; PVALB, SST, VIP, GAD2 and the interneuron-lineage genes LHX6, DLX1/2/5/6 have **no row** in either differential-expression list (a nominal-P-cut list, 2 WT against 4 KO: absence is not "unchanged"); the 2026 single-cell organoid data have **no inhibitory cluster**. (ii) **NPY points three ways across systems**: cell count down (mouse hippocampus DG, 30 percent), bulk transcript **up** (mouse cortex P17, neuron-targeted deletion: my ADV-070, "+0.95", fits seizure-induced expression), bulk transcript **down** (organoid, log2FC −6.24); the CLAIM 005 sentence "concordanza umana indipendente" should not be read as agreement of the GABAergic picture. (iii) the one cell-level inhibitory-circuit datum (Breton 2021) is **not PV-identified**: the interneuron columns of its Table 1 are 5 and 6 cells and the identification of the interneurons is not described.
4. **The single measurement that separates (a) to (d) in the pilot's neocortical tissue** (section 4): per animal, the fraction of PV-immunoreactive cells **within a Wwox-independent, fate-mapped interneuron population** (a lineage reporter such as Nkx2-1, Lhx6 or Dlx, not a Pvalb-driven reporter, which would itself depend on PV onset), counted in the same slices as the recordings at P14, P17 and P21 against age-matched controls, with per-animal values published. Reporter-cell number falls: **loss**. Reporter number normal, PV fraction low and rising with age: **marker onset delayed**. Then the reporter cells' firing and the IPSCs they evoke separate **state** from **circuit change**.

## 3. Independence and what the two readouts share

- The PV count, the NPY count, the GAD blot, the gliosis and the neurosphere RNA-seq are all one laboratory and one colony at 2 weeks (different animal subsets: 7, 6, 5, 6 and 8 animals; overlap likely, not stated). Hussain 2023 is the same laboratory, a different mutation, with no PV measure.
- The circuit result (Breton) and the neuron-targeted cortex and hippocampus deposits I used for ADV-068 and ADV-070 come from the Synapsin-Cre preparation (one laboratory family; the other deposits of those notes are stem-cell-targeted and null; Repudi 2021 Brain shares the preparation; slice overlap not verifiable). The organoid work is the Aqeilan laboratory with a human cell source; within it the immunostaining, qPCR and RNA-seq are different organoid sets of the same lines.
- **No animal, region, age or cell has both a PV measurement and an inhibitory-current measurement.** The count is hippocampal, germline, 2 weeks; the currents are neocortical, neuron-targeted, P13 to P17. A statement that "inhibition is reduced because PV cells are lost" combines two systems.
- Within ADV-068/070 (my own notes): at P17 in the neuron-targeted cortex contrast the Pvalb transcript falls about twice as much as a late-maturing set (PV-weighted, not PV-specific); stage and deletion depth are not separable at n of 3 (ADV-070, ADV-076). The cell-count premise and the transcript premise are therefore different measurements of possibly different things at two different ages, and neither separates (a) to (d).

## 4. The separating measurement and what the pilot's measurement must include

Candidate (the delegate's, reasoned against IT's 2026-11-18 version, which used a genetic PV reporter): a **fate-map lineage reporter for the interneuron population** (Nkx2-1, Lhx6 or Dlx lineage, independent of PV expression), crossed to the neuron-targeted deletion used in the slice work, with age-matched littermate controls; in the same neocortical slices as the recordings, at P14, P17 and P21 (the knockout mice are reported to "die between 3 and 4 postnatal weeks" (Breton), so the window ends near week 3):
- per animal, reporter-positive cells per unit area, the PV-immunoreactive fraction of them, Pvalb transcript and protein per section, and one late marker per cell (a perineuronal-net or Kv3 marker), with the **per-animal values published**;
- reporter number falls: **loss (a)**; reporter number normal, PV fraction low and rising by P21: **delayed marker onset (b)**; reporter cells present, PV normal, reporter-cell firing or evoked IPSC (physiological chloride) reduced: **state or circuit change (c, d)**.
Why not a Pvalb-driven reporter: it depends on the onset it is meant to test. It needs no drug and no therapeutic target. It shares animals with the optional PV and late-marker measurement already on the ordering page (brief Addendum 19) and with the two-age design of ADV-070. Outside the held files and **not checked here**: PV protein is reported to appear around the second postnatal week in rodents, so a 2-week count sits near onset (general knowledge; it would make (b) more likely to matter at 2 weeks).
