FIRST READER REPORT, Record 6, PMID 32719346 (Liu 2020; the paper's own author line is "Cao et al."), consumer DL-MECH-170. Dates: all searches below were run 2026-11-28.

BOTTOM LINE. The pattern in the clause exists, but only for behavioural readouts, and "follows the SWD phenotype" is the authors' inference ("likely", and "cannot rule out ... independently of the SWDs"). Verdict for the clause as a whole: ADD. I have no strict WRONG candidate. One borderline item, S08, is listed in section (3).

Meaning of "restriction": an ethosuximide (ETX) behavioural benefit seen in the Nlgn2 knockout (the line with spike-wave discharges, SWD), but not in wild type or in two other ETX-tested mouse models whose social-memory deficit is not rescued. The restriction is in behaviour (open field, elevated plus maze, social recognition). It is not an SWD readout, because SWD was measured only in the knockout.

STUDY FACTS (all from held files)
- The only Nlgn2 line is the global Nlgn2 KO (JAX #008139), from the main text Methods "Animals". Background is mixed 129Sv x C57BL/6.
- Other mutants in the paper:
  - NLG1 KO and NLG3 KO: EEG-recorded, no SWD (Fig 1c,d; Supp Fig 1c,e). ETX was not given to them. The reason is not stated (search "ETX" and "NLG1" in the full text found no such experiment).
  - PAK3 transgenic mice (in-house, C57BL/6J): ETX given, social test only (Supp Fig 8).
  - FMR1 KO (JAX #004624, FVB; wild-type control #004828 from homozygous breeders, so not littermates): ETX given, social test only (Supp Fig 9).
- SWD proof (Fig 1e, source data sheet "Fig. 1"):
  - KO n=10: 8.47 to 20.61 % of time (mean 12.94 %), 493 to 818 events.
  - WT n=8: 0.0185 to 0.3194 % of time (mean 0.147 %), 4 to 59 events (mean 24.6).
  - The text says SWDs were "not observed" in WT, but the source data show a few sub-threshold-to-low events. WT is "near zero", not literally zero.
- ETX in the knockout (Fig 2a):
  - 200 mg/kg intraperitoneal, in 0.3 % tween 80, given after a 90 min baseline and recorded for 120 min. Vehicle control n=8, ETX n=8. p<0.0001.
  - The readout is "baseline discharge (%)", i.e. SWD proportion. Source-data group means after ETX at +20, +40, +60, +80, +100 and +120 min are 32.7, 12.6, 6.5, 5.2, 10.4 and 11.1 % of baseline. Vehicle means are 89 to 108 %.
  - So "abolished in all tested mice" is a text claim. The source data hold group means only, and they show about 5 to 11 % of baseline, not 0.
  - Local ETX: thalamus reduced SWD (n=5 vs 4); cortex (n=5 vs 8, p=0.5226) and hippocampus (n=7 vs 7, p=0.7673) did not.
- Replicate unit and design:
  - n is the animal for EEG and behaviour, and the neuron for slices (Methods, Statistical analyses).
  - Age is 6 to 18 weeks. Sex is "sex matched", with "similar number of males and females" (Reporting Summary p3). No sex split per group.
  - ETX behaviour tests are within-animal before vs after injection. Searching "vehicle" in the full text finds 1 hit, and it is the SWD experiment. I found no vehicle or saline arm for the behavioural before/after tests. This is a negative from that dated search on held text only.
  - The dose for the WT, PAK3 and FMR1 behaviour tests is not stated. Supp Fig 7 to 9 legends say only "systemic injections of ETX". Searching "200 mg" gives 3 hits, all NLG2-KO context. Dose is NOT ASSESSABLE for those groups, and I assume it is the same.
  - Timing: EPM and open field tests were "30 min prior to and after" the injection. Timing for the three-chamber test is not stated.

R6-S08 | "a restriction that follows the SWD phenotype" | INCOMPLETE (borderline ADD) | It is a pattern over four genotype groups (KO, WT, PAK3, FMR1), with different n (8, 7, 12, 7), backgrounds (mixed 129xB6, C57BL/6J, FVB) and one dose. (3) STRICT CANDIDATES (WRONG as written)
None found. The nearest items, which I grade ADD or INCOMPLETE and not WRONG:
- S05: an unqualified "no effect in wild type" is false for slice rebound burst firing. The clause sits among behavioural or "designs with a wild-type arm" claims, so I read it as in vivo behaviour.
- S08: "follows the SWD phenotype" is stronger than the data, given the authors' own caveat.
- S06: "non-SWD" for the two mutants is citation-based, not measured, and FMR1 KO has seizure susceptibility.
A blind sentence-level reader might call one of these MUST CHANGE. I would not count it as strict without the second check.
