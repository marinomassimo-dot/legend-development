LINE 3710: ### DL-METH-118 — A primate dorsal-root-ganglion finding without a concurrent-control incidence is not an attribution
LINE 3711: 
LINE 3712: - **Tag:** INFERENZA
LINE 3713: - **Status:** open
LINE 3714: - **Created:** 2026-10-03 · `BATCH_20261003_004`, from `CC-20261003W5-C-DRG-ATTRIBUTION-01` (intake wave 5, Scientist C). **It does not supersede `RL-C-20261003w4a` and does not close its question:** it is additive on one dimension that record does not address — whether the lesion's increment over concurrent control has been measured at all.
LINE 3715: - **Causal statement:** Minimal-to-mild dorsal-root-ganglion lesions occur in vehicle-dosed cynomolgus macaques at a rate high enough to account for a substantial part of what is reported as AAV9-related DRG toxicity. Where a concurrent-control incidence is reported, it has been of the same order as the treated incidence in the studies cited in this chain, but **not** in a further one (PMID 35229008, supplement Tables S2 and S5: vehicle 1/4 against 3/4 to 4/4) — ⚠️ and that comparison is not of like with like, because that study counts as test-article related only diffuse infiltrates of grade 2 or worse, a threshold its own vehicle case (grade 1) falls below; where it is not reported — the common case — the attribution to the vector is not established by the paper making it.
LINE 3716: - **Reasoning chain:**
LINE 3717:   1. In one NHP study that reports both arms, subclinical minimal-to-mild DRG lesions appeared in 40 % of animals overall and in **two of four saline-dosed controls**, which the authors themselves read as unrelated to test article — while that study's own Figure 5A caption, read alone, says treated animals showed no significant histological abnormality.
LINE 3718:   2. In a second, independent NHP study the only quantified histopathology shows meningeal mononuclear infiltrate in two of three vehicle animals, while the running text classes that lesion among test-article-related findings.
LINE 3719:   3. That same study reports DRG lesions in prose keyed to a representative photomicrograph, with no incidence and no control comparison **for that tissue** — ⚠️ integrator amendment (blind audit): the article does give a qualitative severity range (*«mostly minimal to slight … with high individual variabilities»*) and does tabulate incidence and severity, but **for brain only**; the absence is DRG-specific, not article-wide. Its methods confirm DRG at four spinal levels were sectioned and stained, so the unreported data were generated.
LINE 3720:   4. The lesion is explicitly stated by one of these studies to occur independently of pre-existing anti-capsid antibody titre, so it is not the pre-existing-immunity component.
LINE 3721:   5. The human record does not scale monotonically with dose: signals at 1.2 × 10^14 vg intrathecally, and none detected by MRI and nerve conduction at 5 × 10^14 – 1 × 10^15 vg. ⚠️ Integrator amendment (blind audit): that negative belongs to the **four-subject CLN7 trial** (plus one separate single-patient programme assessed clinically and electrophysiologically), not to the highest-dose programmes in general; and in the single-patient intracisternal report no formal nerve-conduction study was performed at all, though reflexes and symptoms were assessed — so "not measured" means no electrophysiology, not no assessment.
LINE 3722: - **Counter-evidence:** primate DRG findings carry real regulatory weight — they produced a hold on enrolment in a human paediatric intrathecal programme that was then terminated early — and human DRG toxicity has appeared with imaging and electrophysiological correlates in at least one adult. Background lesions in controls do not mean the vector adds nothing; they mean the increment has not been measured in most reports, and control arms of n = 3 and n = 4 cannot exclude a modest increment.
LINE 3723: - 🟢 **Append-only, 2026-10-04 (intake wave 8, `CC-20261004W8-C-EXPRESSION-NULL-01`, `BATCH_20261004_002`): the convergence below now has a DRG-side primate source, and this lead's own falsifying experiment is partly answered.** The convergence was carried on a **hepatic** promoterless negative. [[paper_registry_current#PAPER 232]] (PMID 35229008) supplies the same proposition **for the dorsal root ganglion, in cynomolgus monkeys, with vector-DNA parity**: an AAV9 *«Null»* genome *«which lacked a functional promoter and does not produce mRNA or protein»* reached DRG, cord and brain *«at similar or greater levels»* than the expressing vector and produced *«no evidence of DRG toxicity»*, while the lesion appeared at **all three purification routes** (74.1 %, 86.3 %, 95.5 % full capsids) — so this lead's cassette-varied arm (*full vector / weak or cell-restricted promoter / promoterless*) is answered for the promoterless limb, and **empty-capsid fraction is excluded as a determinant**. ⚠️ Three limits travel with it: the Null arm shows *«a higher incidence of increased cellularity (two of four animals) in the DRG (LS only)»* against one of four vehicle animals, so it is not wholly clean; **expression was never measured in DRG neurons**, so what the design separates is «transcription, protein or the specific cDNA», and the authors' own wording is *«supports a hypothesis that»*; and n = 4 per group at four weeks with one Null construct and one dose. 🔴 This **does not** resolve this lead's main gap, which is a **concurrent-control incidence by arm** — that study reports a vehicle group and, in its supplement (Tables S2 and S5, read as rendered pages in intake wave 12 and re-measured by a blind locator audit on 2026-10-04), the graded per-arm and per-animal incidence this lead asks for: DRG neuronal degeneration in 1 of 4 vehicle animals (one animal, lumbosacral, grade 1) against 3 to 4 of 4 in each expressing arm, with maxima to grade 3, and 0 of 4 in the Null arm. Nothing below is restated or edited.
LINE 3724: - **A convergence worth recording, and its limit:** the lesion requires a transcriptionally productive cassette (neither empty capsids nor a promoterless genome reproduced it), yet in the same study the magnitude of transgene expression did not predict which tissue was injured — which is in tension with the review position that attributes the toxicity to supraphysiological expression under strong ubiquitous promoters. Both are carried; neither is adjudicated here.
LINE 3725: - **Falsifying experiment:** an NHP study powered to report DRG incidence and severity **by arm**, with a concurrent vehicle group, at two or more dose levels, with the cassette varied (full vector / weak or cell-restricted promoter / promoterless) and functional electrophysiology at baseline and terminal. LINE 3726: - **Operational consequence (proposed, not a gate):** a DRG-safety statement entering this repository from a primate source must record the concurrent-control incidence, or record that the source does not report one. The second is a legitimate and common answer.
