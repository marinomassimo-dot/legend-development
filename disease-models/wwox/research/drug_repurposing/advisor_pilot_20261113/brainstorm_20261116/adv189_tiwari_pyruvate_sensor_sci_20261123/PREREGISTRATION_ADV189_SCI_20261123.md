## Quantities, fixed before any value (EMPATHY: what the intervention perturbs, what is measured directly, what is only inferred)
1. **Perturbed:** entry (a carrier block or genetic loss), or PDH state (a kinase inhibitor, activation, a substrate or cofactor change), or consumption by other routes.
2. **Measured directly:** the matrix pyruvate sensor signal (a STOCK: entry minus consumption, plus sensor and matrix conditions); an ATP readout; vesicle release; any pH or calibration control.
3. **Only inferred from function:** pyruvate oxidation (from respirometry or ATP), PDH activity (from flux or function), "entry" as such (from a stock).
The decision (WO-07) needs to know which of two states a WWOX-deficient cell is in: **entry change with preserved PDH** or **normal entry with reduced ACTIVE PDH**. Both give lower oxidation; they differ in the matrix stock and in the PDH state.

## Logic stated in advance (independent of the paper)
The matrix pyruvate pool is entry rate minus consumption. If entry is limited, the pool fills slowly and sits low, oxidation falls with it. If PDH is limited, entry can be intact, the pool fills at the normal initial rate and sits normal or HIGHER because consumption is down, while oxidation and ATP fall. So a stock read at one time point can be ambiguous (a low stock can come from low entry or from high consumption; a high one from low consumption); the INITIAL RATE after a pyruvate step, the steady state under consumption blocked, and the response to an entry block together are the readouts that carry the discrimination. The sensor also depends on matrix pH (pyruvate entry is proton-coupled), on sensor expression and on calibration.
