- Sciatic: "Myelin thickness, axonal organization and the density of myelinated fibers were similar in Wwox +/- and Wwox +/+ mice (data not shown)". The fragment's "thickness and fibre density ... similar (data not shown)" is correct. "Sciatic" is right, because the sentence sits in the sciatic-nerve paragraph.
- ADD (units and groups): the 2.13 against 1.39 ms values are group means of mice at 3 weeks of age, 5 heterozygous against 10 wild-type. The legend gives n = 5 and n = 10 for amplitude. I found no separate n for latency. Minimal wording: "(3-week-old mice, n = 5 against 10)".
- Counter-example to "no Wwox-deficient study held has both" (a wild-type drug-only arm and a conduction readout). Cheng 2020 has both elements, but in separate experiments. Ethosuximide was given to Wwox+/+ and +/- mice in the PTZ seizure assay (Fig 7b). Tc-MEP latency was a baseline in separate mice. Nothing pairs them.
