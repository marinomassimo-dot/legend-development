4. **The circuit pilot stays the follow-up, with a named trigger:** the first of E_GABA in a Wwox system, per-animal EEG with cell identity and age (AES poster 5 December if it prints them), or a ventralised/assembloid inhibitory readout.

## 3. Holdout and the ONE prediction
**Holdout (units and studies intact):** the unit is the **line** (not the organoid, not the ROI, not the cell); studies are never mixed (no organoid values used to fit a mouse model or the reverse); the withheld object is the **per-line, per-organoid week-16 event-rate table behind Fig. 6A(ii)** (not released; the reading note recommends requesting it). Nothing is fitted: the prediction is fixed from group-level held values only (0.257 and 0.075 Hz, printed pooled ratio 3.4).

**Prediction P-SCI-1 (timestamped 2026-11-21T17:21Z, before any per-line value of Fig. 6A(ii) is seen):** in the untreated AAV9-EGFP groups at week 16, **the mean event rate of each WOREE line is higher than that of each SCAR12 line** (all four pairings), and each pairwise line ratio lies between about 1.5 and 8 (a range I define, INFERENCE, bracketing the printed pooled ratio of 3.4 on a log scale; not a distribution).
* **Reverses it:** any WOREE line at or below any SCAR12 line (ratio at most 1) means the printed gap is a **line or batch effect** (row G), not a genotype effect; the "WOREE about 3 times SCAR12" sentence then stands only as a pooled statement, and any allele-specific mechanism built on it falls.
* **Unevaluable if** the table gives only group aggregates, lacks line or batch labels, or lacks the ROI denominator per organoid; then nothing is scored (no partial credit).
* **Improvement, tie, overconfident failure (against the current evidence-only reading, which makes no per-line statement):** improvement = the ordering holds in all four pairings and the ratios fall in range; tie = ordering holds but a ratio is outside the range or one pairing is within error with n of about 4; overconfident failure = any reversed pairing. The comparator is "no line-level claim", so the prediction can only add information by being right on a statement that could have failed.
* **What it changes:** whether the WOREE-over-SCAR12 contrast stays an input of the ranking or is carried as pooled-only; and whether any circuit simulation is parameterised with a WOREE-specific rate at all. Optional cheap held check, **not done here** (it would contaminate nothing, but I fixed the prediction first): read Fig. 1D by line at week 7 to see whether the line ordering exists at another age and assay.

## 5. Strongest counter-explanation to my own preferred pilot
A **batch or differentiation confound** produces the same ordering without any genotype effect: if the two WOREE lines were imaged in one session and the SCAR12 lines in another (one experiment, lines pooled, differentiations not stated), the prediction can come true for the wrong reason, and with about 4 organoids per line a genuine genotype effect can fail it by chance. Mitigations I can state now: score only if batch and date labels are in the table; a pairing that fails with n of about 4 is a tie, not a failure; and the prediction answers genotype-versus-line only, never a structure.

## 6. Uncertainty, kept apart (no number below is a probability of a structure)
* **Grid coverage:** ADV-096 covered 84 cells (7 baseline plus 77 continuation), 71 crossed, 13 had no crossing in the explored range; this says what range was explored, not what is true.
* **Uncertainty within a structure:** the bisection brackets are numerical resolutions (0.094 in strength, 3 seeds), not intervals; the Aqeilan per-line prediction carries sampling uncertainty with n of about 4 per line.
* **Uncertainty over structure:** R1 to R3 and C0 are not weighted; the held data do not identify weights. Any scenario fraction would be a property of the grid, not of a patient or a drug.
