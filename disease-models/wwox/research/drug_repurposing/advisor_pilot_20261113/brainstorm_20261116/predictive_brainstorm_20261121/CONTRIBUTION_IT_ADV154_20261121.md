## Answer first
1. **Today no prediction from the circuit model changes which compound goes first in either cluster.** What it can change is (a) which claims are demoted (the class order as evidence; a Kv7 or T-type arm), and (b) one readout set that is fixed before data. The order of first experiments is set by what is unmeasured (exposure, genotype by treatment, E_GABA), not by a simulated order.
2. **A prediction earns a place only when it names an observable that held or soon-released data can check, and an agent's reachability is mapped first.** For most agents of the adjunctive cluster the link from the simulated parameter to the compound is missing at two places (target action at a comparable potency; free brain exposure at the age): the mapping below says which.
3. **The one pilot-shaped comparison I would keep** is the existing exposure-measured ethosuximide series with a wild-type arm and a generic-effect control, because its outcome classes are written and an exposure-failed null cannot end as "negative". The simulated class order does not enter it.

## 3. The decision as research comparisons (wild-type or generic-effect control where interpretable)
**Adjunctive (the one I keep).** In Wwox-null and wild-type pups at ONE stated age, is the reduction of a provoked-seizure score by ethosuximide larger in null than in wild type (genotype by treatment), with plasma and brain measured in the same pups, and the effect read against a **generic-effect control**: the lithium arm of the same system is the precedent for a "general" response (effect in all three genotypes), and a vehicle arm per genotype shows the floor. Outcome classes written before data: (i) larger in null at measured exposure in the target range: ethosuximide advances, T-type link still missing; (ii) equal in wild type: a generic antiseizure effect, acceptable for the bridge aim and **said so**, no mechanism claim, the tonic class is not demoted; (iii) exposure below the target range or unmeasured: **uninterpretable null, not a negative**; (iv) a harm signal at repeated administration: excluded at that age for the bridge aim. Control caveat from the records: the wild-type arm of the one experiment was possibly floor-limited (`aed_compare_it31_20261107/output/README.md` item 2), so the provocation strength must leave room above the wild-type floor or the wild-type control is uninformative.
**Broad.** In neuron-restricted knockout pups, which exposure-matched candidate moves the benchmark endpoint set (survival, weight, motor, structural myelin, glucose), with wild-type drug controls and the untreated knockout as the reference gap; the benchmark is the neuronal rescue's recovered fraction, so the comparison needs an untreated knockout in the same cohort. A prediction table fixed before data (section 1) is the only model-derived element.

## 4. Why exposure or mechanism would rule out a comparison even if a simulation looks favourable
- **The simulated parameter is not a compound.** In ADV-096 the same parameter encoded d-APV and NMDAR pore block, so a simulated "block" says a parameter change helps; it does not say any agent reaches it. A class is eligible only after a comparable target-action row and an exposure row exist (section 2).
- **Potency depends on method or condition, so a concentration cannot be taken from a simulation.** The K2P pointer ONO-TR-772 reads 15 nM (human manual patch clamp, conditions not printed), 0.15 uM (QPatch) and 0.26 uM (Tl+ flux): about 17 times between methods; the paper's own ">67-fold" selectivity uses the QPatch value; single pH; no neuronal data (`trek_pointer_q07_it_20261121/README.md`). Ketamine and memantine potency moves 30 to 100 times with holding potential (HE batch 4). Vigabatrin has no single potency (irreversible, time-dependent).
- **Exposure ceiling or overlap.** Kv7: effective and harmful exposures overlap in the one juvenile set; ethosuximide's low-affinity target (0.6 mM to above 5 mM) makes occupancy at a brain level near 1 mM partial at best, so a target-action claim needs the measured regimen and age; bumetanide's brain exposure is the stated limit (transporters).
- **Age.** The model has no age; targets do: T-type transcript flat P14 to P28 in the one dataset; Kcnq2 rises P14 to P28 with background confounding; labels differ by agent and age (ethosuximide below 3 years not established; bumetanide below 18 not established; ganaxolone from 2 years for one disorder; lithium from 7 years). A comparison at an age where the target is not measured is ruled out as a *mechanistic* test even if it stays open as an empirical one.
- **Harm gates.** Vigabatrin's boxed visual-field warning and Kv7 apoptosis signal are cells of their own; a favourable simulated effect cannot offset them for a bridge aim.

## 5. Two goals kept separate
Broad developmental recovery needs endpoints that persist and move together (survival, growth, motor, myelin structure, glucose) and the neuronal rescue as the benchmark; the oscillator is irrelevant to it. The narrower adjunctive goal needs a provoked or spontaneous seizure endpoint at one age with exposure and a harm cell. A result in one is not spent in the other: lithium works in all genotypes (a general effect, not broad recovery); a myelin-only move is not a bridge claim; an adjunctive success does not change the broad ranking.

## 6. Strongest counter-explanation to my own mapping
That **"link missing" mostly measures what was searched**, not what exists: the sheets read ChEMBL ids, PubMed titles and abstracts and openFDA, so the agents with the longest literature (ethosuximide, retigabine) look both most eligible and most penalised, and the agents with no sheet (levetiracetam, perampanel, cannabidiol, stiripentol, magnesium) look empty because nobody looked. The selection note itself calls the ethosuximide preference "partly an absence artefact". A second counter-reading goes deeper: if the one in-system effect is a **generic** suppression of provoked seizures (lithium also works in wild type; the ethosuximide drop looks proportionally similar in all genotypes by ADV's reading), then every target-action row above is beside the point for the adjunctive aim, and the only decision-relevant variables are **exposure and harm per age**. In that case no prediction, simulated or mapped, changes anything, and the right record is "no current design would change the decision" for that cluster. I think this is the likelier outcome for the adjunctive cluster, and I state it as INFERENCE.
