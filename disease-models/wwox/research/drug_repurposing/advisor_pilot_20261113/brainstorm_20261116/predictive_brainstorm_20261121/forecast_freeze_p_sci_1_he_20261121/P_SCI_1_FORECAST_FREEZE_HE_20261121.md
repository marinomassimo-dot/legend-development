Ids: `PSCI1-W1S1`, `PSCI1-W1S2`, `PSCI1-W2S1`, `PSCI1-W2S2` (the two WOREE lines against the two SCAR12 lines, in the order the released table lists its lines; because the four are scored together, the order does not change any outcome). - **Bins on the ratio:** below 1 (the pairing is reversed or tied), 1 to 1.5, 1.5 to 8 (SCI's range), 8 and above.
- **Forecasters in the sidecar** (`who`): `genotype_effect` = P-SCI-1 as SCI wrote it (0.05, 0.15, 0.70, 0.10 on the four bins); `line_or_batch_effect` = no genotype effect, the order of a pair is a coin flip (0.50, 0.25, 0.20, 0.05); `pooled_rule` = if the evidence-only reading is forced to speak at line level it can only say that the printed pooled ratio of 3.4 holds per line (0.8 on 1.5 to 8, the rest equal, the convention of the earlier sidecar).
- **The evidence-only reading itself is UNEVALUABLE at line level:** it makes no per-line statement, so it has no forecast to score. `pooled_rule` is the closest scorable stand-in and is labelled as mine; it points to the same bin as `genotype_effect`, so P-SCI-1 differs from it only in how much risk of reversal each admits. That is the whole of what the forecast can add: being right on a statement that could have failed.

## 4. Reading rule (ADV's, binding; written before any value exists)
- **A reversed pairing** (any WOREE line at or below any SCAR12 line: a ratio at or below 1 in any of the four ids) **refutes the genotype reading**: the printed gap is then a line or batch effect, and the "WOREE about 3 times SCAR12" sentence stands only as a pooled statement.
- **All four pairings in order is a TIE, not an improvement**, whether or not every ratio falls in 1.5 to 8 (the ratio range is recorded beside it, not scored as a win). **Improvement is not reachable** with this forecast.
- **The floor (ADV's arithmetic):** with two lines per genotype, the chance under no genotype effect that both WOREE lines exceed both SCAR12 lines is 1 in 6 (one of the 6 equally likely arrangements of two WOREE and two SCAR12 lines in rank order). My added note: the printed pooled ratio of 3.4 is already known, and conditioning on it can only raise the chance of that ordering under no genotype effect, so **1 in 6 is a lower bound** on the null probability and does not weaken "tie".
- **UNSCORABLE** (status `unscorable`, nothing scored, no partial credit): the table gives only group aggregates; or lacks line labels or batch labels; or lacks the ROI denominator per organoid; or reports a unit other than the organoid with no way to the organoid. The scorer's refusals (no locator) apply on top.
- **Shared provenance (declared):** the four lines come from **one laboratory, one experiment, one paper** (the lines and batch are shared with Fig. 5 and 6C to E; the wild type is not vector-matched). They are **one cluster**: an effective independent n of 1 for the claim "genotype effect", so no calibration is attempted and no rate is built from this row.
