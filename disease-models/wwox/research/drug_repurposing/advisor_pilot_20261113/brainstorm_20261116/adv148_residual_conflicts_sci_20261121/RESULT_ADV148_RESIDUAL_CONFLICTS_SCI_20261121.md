## What I found, item by item (full text in the TSV)
* **C012 is a real correction to my own earlier wording, not only a labelling issue.** The Cheng 2020 group sizes are in the figure legends of Fig. 7 (they are not in the Methods text): knockout groups 6, 6 and 7; wild-type and heterozygous groups 8 to 20 (7b: +/+ 20 and 16, +/- 18 and 12; 7d: +/+ 12 and 8, +/- 12 and 12). The text's "12 to 20 wild-type or heterozygous" understates the range at the low end (8, the 7d lithium wild-type group) and "6 to 8 is the Cheng scale" should read "6 to 7 knockouts".
* **C004 and R3C04 are caused by my ADV-144 Q19 answer.** I wrote that the pilot "uses the ages the brief already names: P11 and P14"; the brief names those two ages only for the chloride-window read (V2 line 26) and leaves the assay age to the laboratory. The pilot ages are two ages the laboratory chooses inside the working window P12 to P17 (an inference from held ages: spontaneous seizures after P12, ECoG spikes from about P14, slice bursting P13 to P17, death at three to four weeks). The pilot arithmetic (2 ages) does not change.
* **C002, C003 and R3C02 are one issue.** My CLAR-SERIES "correction" made the straddle of P11 and P14 sound like a requirement; V2 treats it as the optional cheap chloride read. Making it optional removes the contradiction with the rule sentence and keeps the three-age totals; the four-age case is stated beside them (34 cells, 374 animals against 297 at 11 per cell).
* **R3C01 (the largest design conflict):** two different cells. The replication cells use the published stimulus; every cell that enters a contrast, the panel or the age series uses the pilot stimulus at which wild-type vehicle pups are above the floor. If the pilot shows they coincide there is no cost; if not, a second stimulus carries its own vehicle group per genotype (the existing rule that a shared vehicle holds only within a session). The pilot decides; I do not.
* **R3C05 (my first reading was overturned by the blind check):** I first redefined E2 as a floor reading; the frozen priors say otherwise: set E is conditioned on "panel valid, wild type above floor", the floor is wild-type vehicle S below the laboratory's detection limit, and an arm at the floor is E5 "not interpretable". "Wild-type floor" in E2's name means a small wild-type drug effect while the wild-type vehicle is above the floor, not a floor-bound arm. S11 now carries the corrected E2 row and withdraws my draft.
* **R3Q37:** I did not close the single application figure by arithmetic; I give a range with its basis (pilot 36 to 48 animals; stage 2 at the credible scale 62 to 187 litters for the three knockout cells; wild-type supply 33 to 132 litters; the interaction excluded) and say it is illustrative until the pilot gives the SD.
* **Q51:** window start = the PTZ injection (held: Cheng 2020 scored 60 minutes after PTZ); latency rule (INFERENCE) = start of the first 1-minute bin reaching the type's lower grade bound, from the PTZ injection, censored at 60 minutes.
