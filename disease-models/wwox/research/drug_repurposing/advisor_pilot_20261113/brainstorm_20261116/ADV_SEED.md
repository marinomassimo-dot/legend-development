## Leaps (divergent seeds, ADV; each may be wrong)
* L1 PTZ is the wrong ruler. PTZ blocks GABA-A; it rewards T-type and GABAergic agents and ignores sodium-channel agents in any genotype. The repository's only AED-by-genotype signal therefore cannot, by itself, separate WWOX-specific from PTZ-generic pharmacology. A matching score built on PTZ would only recover the PTZ profile. Rule: a thread counts toward 'WWOX-specific' only with a genotype contrast.
* L2 Mismatch first. If reduced inhibitory drive (T2) is real, a loss-of-inhibition logic predicts an aggravation hypothesis for sodium-channel blockers, as in other loss-of-inhibition DEEs. A ranking of what to avoid has more information per datum than a ranking of what to try. Test: exposure-with-worsening statements in the held case reports.
* L3 Nearest electrophenotype, not nearest gene. Instead of asking what the WWOX literature says, ask which known DEE electrophenotype WWOX most resembles (NMDAR-dependent bursting plus reduced inhibitory events plus an age-dependent GluN2B share), and import that class's published antiseizure-agent pattern as a prior. INV-10 already uses Dravet/STXBP1 as a base rate; this generalises it. Risk: analogy is not identity.
* L4 Age as the hidden variable. The GluN2B share falls from 53% (P6-P8) to 14% (P13-P15). If the NMDAR thread matters, its drugs should matter early and fade; a pooled 'responder' definition across ages would wash it out. A phenotype x age x mechanism table before any ranking.
* L5 Resistance as information. Non-response to sodium-channel blockers and SV2A ligands (reported drug resistance) says the driver sits in targets those agents do not touch. Which threads live outside those targets? (T-type, NMDAR, GABA-AT, K channels, GSK3beta).
* L6 Convergence count. Valproate-type pharmacology intersects T-type, GABA-AT, GSK3beta and histone-deacetylase threads at once (the WO-32 class IIa residue); breadth is what a multi-target agent always shows, so test whether the count predicts anything beyond breadth before reading it as a match.
* L7 Experiment before drug. The slice burst assay already has a measured WWOX-system readout (NMDAR block abolishes the burst). Four mechanism representatives, one per top class, against that readout in null versus wild-type slices is a discriminating experiment; the drug choice becomes the output, not the input.
