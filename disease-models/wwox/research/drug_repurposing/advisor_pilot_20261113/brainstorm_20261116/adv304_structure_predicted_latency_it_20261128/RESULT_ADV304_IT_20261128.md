3. **Why the calibration fails is itself the finding:** in the same callosal tract, a drug-treated wild-type P21 mouse (Palma) loses about 16 percent of N1 velocity with a near-flat g-ratio and 13 percent fewer myelinated axons (287 to 249 per 10^3 um^2; the paper also reports fewer nodes of Ranvier), so g-ratio at fixed geometry does not carry the velocity change; the structural features that do (nodes, internodes, which fibres are recruited) are not measured in the Wwox rows.
