## STEP 1: the independent source, the relation, the held inputs, the rule (frozen)
* **Independent source with measured conduction AND matched structure in a callosal tract:** Palma et al. 2022 (PMID 35372341, held `PMID35372341_Palma2022_PMC_r2.xml`, figure pages in `files/figure_renders/PMID35372341_r3c/`): wild-type mice, corpus callosum, **P21**, compound action potential recorded at 4 distances, **N1 conduction velocity as the slope of distance against latency** (Fig 2B; vehicle n = 12 and clemastine n = 10 to 12 mice), and in the same treatment contrast callosal EM: myelinated axons per field and **g-ratio by axon-calibre bin** (Fig 4A-B, n = 3 mice per group). Values are figure-only (the text prints none).
* **Relation R (theory, calibrated on the independent source before use):** at fixed axon diameter and fixed node and internode geometry, conduction velocity scales as f(g) = g * sqrt(-ln g) (Rushton 1951 form). Predicted velocity ratio for a change from g1 to g2: f(g2) / f(g1).
* **Calibration rule (before applying R to Wwox):** from Palma Fig 4B take the mean g-ratio, vehicle and clemastine, for the calibre bin that carries the N1 fibres (the bin the figure reports for small axons, 0 to 0.5 um, and, separately, the other bin); compute the R-predicted velocity ratio clemastine/vehicle; compare with the measured N1 velocity ratio from Fig 2B. **R counts as usable in this tract if the predicted and measured ratios have the same direction and the log of the measured ratio lies within a factor of 2 of the log of the predicted ratio (i.e. |ln measured| between half and twice |ln predicted|); otherwise R is not validated, the prediction is UNRESOLVED, and the row is parked with the resume condition below.**
* **Application to Wwox:** g-ratio mean in callosum, S-Control and S-KO, from Repudi Fig 5C (figure-only; mean of the plotted summary, with the plotted spread reported). Equal axon diameter in the two genotypes is assumed (not measured here: said in the result). Predicted N1 latency ratio = f(g_Control) / f(g_KO). **Predicted shift = (that ratio - 1) * N1 latency of S-Control read from Fig 5D(ii)** (upper bound: all of the control latency treated as propagation along the same path; any onset offset in the control latency lowers it).
* **Decision rule (frozen):** predicted shift **at most 0.37 ms** (half of 0.74): B supported (structure predicts at most half); **at least 0.6 ms**: A suffices (rejecting for B); between 0.37 and 0.6 ms: inconclusive. **Also rejecting:** the calibration fails, or an input cannot be read, or the g-ratio is not reported as a callosal mean for both genotypes: no prediction; register the missing observation and park.
* Every figure value is read by a delegate (no image in my context), twice independently (producer, then a blind reader who is not shown the first values), and every printed ratio is recomputed by me from the reader values.

## Prediction (mine, frozen; not independent of ADV's)
B: the structure-predicted shift is at most 0.37 ms. Reason stated before the figures: the Wwox text reports a g-ratio increase without a value and the myelin-protein transcripts fall less than the myelinated count, and Rushton's f(g) is flat near g about 0.7 to 0.8, so a modest g-ratio rise gives a small velocity change; a g-ratio as high as about 0.87 from about 0.70 would be needed for 0.6 ms at a 2.4 ms control latency.

## Limits stated now
Theory plus one calibration contrast (a drug model in wild-type mice at P21, not a Wwox model); N1 fibres are the larger myelinated ones and a mean g-ratio over all callosal axons can differ from the g-ratio of the fibres that carry N1; equal diameter assumed; slices, not animals, are the unit of the physiology; a fixed-distance latency is not a velocity; transcripts and EM counts are not conduction.
