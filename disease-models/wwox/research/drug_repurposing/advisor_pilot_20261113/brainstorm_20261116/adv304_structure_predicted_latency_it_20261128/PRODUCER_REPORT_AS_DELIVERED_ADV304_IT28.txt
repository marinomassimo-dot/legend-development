=== What makes numbers less certain ===
1. Palma 2B: error type not stated in the legend; the clemastine lower whisker is not drawn or is hidden; N2 whiskers are too small to read. The n is in the legend only (vehicle 12, clemastine 10-12), not on the panel.
2. Palma 4A, 4B: the legend states no whisker type; y axes are truncated (150 to 350; 0.70 to 0.84; 0.60 to 0.74), so bars start above zero. The 4B dots per bin are 3 per group, assumed to be per-mouse means.
3. Palma 4B: the legend's "distribution of g-ratios" is a scatter with linear fits, not a histogram, so there is no modal g-ratio. Also, the 0.5-1 um bar values (0.66 to 0.67) look lower than the scatter, where nearly all axons above 0.5 um sit at g of about 0.7 to 0.85 and the lowest g is about 0.58. I cannot tell from the figure whether the bin "diameter" is the same quantity as the scatter x axis (no unit is printed on the scatter x axis). I did not resolve this.
4. Palma 4A axis unit is per 10^3 um^2 as printed, not per field.
5. Repudi: low-resolution embedded raster (150 ppi, 1000 px wide). The red and blue 5C dots overlap heavily, and the S-KO line is hidden where it crosses the red cloud. The 5D(ii) N1 whiskers are occluded by dots and the mean line. The 5B lower KO cap is occluded. The legend does not name the error type for 5D(ii).
6. All dot values are read from ring centres at about 1 px accuracy; dots were not counted exhaustively.
