## 3. Correction candidates for IT (hypothesis level; nothing applied; each names the sentence it touches)
* **F1 (T22, M).** The closing sentence of the Summary and the Clinical meaning of CLAIM 021 state a primary network-state pathology and extend it to the reference genotype's EEG. The source supports "neuronal deletion of Wwox induces a hyperexcitable network" in a slice preparation and leaves open a generalized effect (hypomyelination) and the Wwox-null phenotype. A reading that fits the source: "supports a network-level hyperexcitability phenotype in neuron-specific knockout slices; whether it is primary and whether the human EEG reflects it is not tested here". The record's Type is DATO; the sentence is an INFERENZA.
* **F2 (T1, M).** The title's "directly" and "through combined synaptic and intrinsic mechanisms": the authors call the mechanism "elusive" and the membrane potential "a factor". Candidate: drop "directly"; say "is associated with synaptic and intrinsic changes".
* **F3 (T13, M).** "UNDERSTATES the effect": supported for the four starred Table 1 rows; untested for the pooled sEPSC and sIPSC measures. Candidate: scope the sentence to Table 1.
* **F4 (T21, m).** Propagation direction holds for the 0.5-6 Hz components only (100-400 Hz leads the other way by a median 9.5 ms); "~11 mm/s" is the authors' Discussion figure without printed derivation; the biomarker equivalence is the authors' and loose.
* **F5 (T6, m).** Add that the slope of the frequency-current curve did not differ and that the authors conclude the network hyperexcitability "was not due to action-potential dependent frequency changes".
* **F6 (T24, m; mine, not the delegate's).** The wave note says "No NMDAR-specific measurement exists in any Wwox system". The paper's Results report that the field response to layer V stimulation had "a delayed negative deflection in the Wwox S-KO slices that was not observed in the wildtype controls", larger in S-KO than S-WT at multiple stimulus strengths, and "NMDA receptor mediated, as addition of d-APV reversibly suppressed the response" (Supplementary Fig S3, not held). The repository carries it elsewhere (CC-20261027-SCIENTIST-C-W2-THERAPEUTIC-01 line 16, the PMID34634460 manifest) but not in the CLAIM 021 wave note. Candidate: scope to "no isolated NMDAR current under voltage control; an NMDAR-mediated delayed field response is reported (text only)".
* **F7 (T16, m).** The 18% duration fall is the text's; Fig 3D d2 reads about 0.57 by eye. Candidate: state both and that Table S1 is not held.
* **F8 (T8, n).** "Whole" is too wide; add the N mismatch (31 against 18 cells for sEPSC) and the sEPSC frequency p = 0.0315 (s-HT vs s-KO) beside the pooled p = 0.9805.
* **F9 (T15, n), F10 (T20, n), F11 (T25, n), F12 (T2, n).** Add that the Abstract states the NMDAR dependence unhedged; the hippocampal CA1-to-CA3 population-spike increase; the glucose and magnesium composition belong to the incubation solution; the S-HT burst count 4/23 (carried by CLAIM 032 work).
* **F13 (record text, not the paper).** The record says the figure image is absent from this checkout; Fig 3 is held at `files/figures/PMID34634460_r2/gr3.jpg`, so the "about 1.85" washout overshoot can now be read from the image (about 1.8 by eye, with an error bar to about 2.75).
