# ADV-099 unbiased discovery of a neuron-internal lesion candidate: pre-registration (frozen before any value is read; SCI successor, 2026-11-20; hypothesis level; evidence, not a gate; no dose, no recommendation; not medical advice)

**Question (ADV-099, ADV message of 2026-11-20).** Unit C found the oligodendrocyte class most likely treats a downstream consequence, because neuronal re-expression rescues survival, growth, motor, myelin structure and glucose together. A different broad candidate can then only come from the lesion inside the neuron. Which transcripts change in neurons in two held neuron-containing systems, in the same direction, after maturity adjustment? Keep only what replicates; map it to druggable targets and to classes with pharmacology data; check against the phenotype endpoints the neuronal rescue normalised; name the candidate class it generates and its rejecting observation. **Stop rule (ADV):** if nothing replicates beyond chance, say so, name the data that would show it, and stop.

**Correction of the data description (sent to ADV before this registration).** GSE156243 is bulk (human cerebral organoid RNA-seq at 15 weeks, one parental hESC line WiBR3, 2 control and 4 knockout libraries), not neuron-resolved. The "two knockout clusters" of my earlier units are two GROUPS OF KNOCKOUT LIBRARIES (cko1+cko2 and cko3+cko4) that I fixed from an earlier whole-transcriptome recomputation; the deposit metadata do not state clonal identity, and they are not cell clusters (a first draft of this note and my message to ADV said "clones": that was wrong and is corrected here). The single-cell organoid data are not public by the routes searched (supply note of 2026-11-20). The held AAV9 rescue paper (Repudi 2021, held full text) contains no transcriptome: the "programmes the rescue normalised" are phenotype endpoints (survival, weight, motor score, myelin structure, GFAP, glucose), so the mapping to them is by annotation, a proposal and not a test.

## 1. Systems and units (held data, no network except where stated in section 6)
- **System N (mouse hippocampal nuclei, `GSE158684`).** Neuronal deletion of Wwox, P17, one 10x pool per arm (control 3,182 nuclei, knockout 2,920 nuclei). **There is no biological replication: one pool per arm; genotype is confounded with pool and library; the result is descriptive and a nucleus is not an animal.** Nuclei with total counts below 500 are dropped. Class assignment is the unit A3 rule, unchanged: marker score per class (excitatory Slc17a7, Camk2a, Satb2; inhibitory Gad1, Gad2, Slc32a1; also astrocyte, microglia and oligodendrocyte-lineage classes for the exclusion below), each divided by its 99th percentile over both arms, the maximum class if at least 0.2, else unassigned. Classes analysed: excitatory and inhibitory neurons, each alone.
- **System O (human organoid bulk, `GSE156243`).** 2 control and 4 knockout libraries (library groups cko1+cko2 and cko3+cko4 as in the earlier units; clonal identity is not stated in the deposit), the Salmon gene counts table; unit of replication the library; "reference moved" caveat of unit A applies. Human symbols are matched to mouse symbols by upper-casing the mouse symbol (exact equality); orthologues with different names are lost, and that loss is listed by count.
- **Corroboration only (not a criterion):** the bulk neuronal-deletion cortex `GSE158682` (3 against 4) and hippocampus `GSE158681` S contrast (3 against 2): for each replicated gene the sign and size of the stage-adjusted effect are reported. Animals are the unit there.

## 2. Quantities
**N, per class and gene.** x = log2(count / nucleus total x 1e6 + 1). A gene is analysed if detected (count above zero) in at least 10 percent of the class nuclei in at least one arm. Maturity covariate per nucleus: m = mean(x over Grin2a, Slc12a5, Nrgn, Camk2a, Scn8a) minus mean(x over Snap25, Rbfox3, Syt1, Stmn2, Tubb3, Map2), the unit A3 late-against-reference definition, computed with the analysed gene left out of both panels, then z-scored within the class. Per gene: ordinary least squares x on intercept, m and arm (1 for knockout); effect c_N = the arm coefficient. Because the nuclei depth control rose in every knockout class (ambient RNA from the control pool, unit A2), c_N is **centred**: c_N' = c_N minus the median of c_N over the analysed genes of the same class. Raw difference of means is reported beside it.
**N stability.** 20 random stratified half-splits of the nuclei of each arm within the class; c_N' computed in each half; the gene is stable if the two halves have the same sign in at least 18 of 20 splits.
**O, per gene.** y = log2(count per million + 1); the gene is analysed if mean CPM is at least 1. Maturity covariate per library: mu = mean(y over STMN2, DCX, TUBB3, MAP2, SNAP25, SYT1) minus mean(y over SOX2, PAX6, HES1, VIM, NES, FABP7), the unit A definition, analysed gene left out; per gene OLS y on intercept, mu and knockout; effect c_O = the knockout coefficient, **centred** by the median of c_O over analysed genes (the reference-moved correction). Group agreement: the knockout library-group mean minus the control mean, for each of the two library groups, has the sign of c_O and absolute value at least 0.25.

## 3. Replication rule (fixed)
A gene is REPLICATED in a direction if, for a neuron class: |c_N'| at least 0.25 and the gene is N-stable; |c_O| at least 0.5 and group agreement holds; and the two signs are equal. UP means both positive, DOWN both negative. Counts are formed per class (excitatory, inhibitory) and per direction.
**Null.** 2,000 permutations: the O values are reassigned among the genes that are analysed in both systems, shuffled **within ten bins of the N class mean log2 expression** (deciles); the replicated count (same rule) is recomputed. A direction in a class is REPLICATES_BEYOND_CHANCE if the observed count exceeds the 97.5th percentile of its null; otherwise it is NOTHING_REPLICATES_BEYOND_CHANCE. If no direction in either class exceeds the null, the stop rule applies and the report names the missing data. Genes expressed only in glia by the class assignment (a neuron-class gene with larger expression in a glial class by more than 4 times in both arms) are flagged "glial-enriched in nuclei" and reported separately: ambient-RNA contamination of a neuron class by glial transcripts is a failure of the class, not a neuron-internal lesion.

## 4. Mapping (only if a direction replicates beyond chance; all local)
(i) Enrichment: hypergeometric test of the replicated set against the MSigDB Hallmark gene sets held in `files/reach_audit_downloads/MSigDB_Hallmark_2020.gmt` (human symbols; background = genes analysed in both systems); Benjamini-Hochberg at 0.1; sets with fewer than two hits are not reported. (ii) Druggability and agent classes: HGNC gene to UniProt accession from the held `hgnc_complete_set_20261022.json`, ChEMBL REST (plain GET, public read) target by accession and mechanism rows; for a replicated set above 200 genes only the 200 with the largest smaller-of-the-two absolute effects; the result is crossed with the agent classes that have a sheet in `adv098_agent_sheets_he_20261119/` (ethosuximide, lithium, memantine, ketamine, d-AP5, retigabine and the Kv7 openers, XE991, ganaxolone, gaboxadol, vigabatrin, bumetanide, clemastine, sobetirome, tiratricol, benztropine and the sheets of batches 7 and 8). A target is "druggable by an agent class with data" only if a ChEMBL mechanism row names that target for that agent. (iii) Endpoints: each replicated gene and each enriched set is placed against the phenotype endpoints the neuronal rescue normalised (survival, weight, motor, myelin structure, GFAP, glucose) by the Hallmark set labels only (for example glycolysis, mTORC1, oxidative phosphorylation, inflammatory response for GFAP and glia); any further link is written as a proposal with its source and carries no test.

## 5. What a result may say
- A replicated set is a **neuron-internal change shared by an in vivo neuronal deletion and a human knockout organoid**, after a global-shift correction. It is not a cause, not a dose-responsive target and not a rescue candidate by itself; the nuclei have no biological replication; the organoid is bulk with a differentiation lag; the two systems differ in species, age and cell mixture.
- A candidate class named from it carries its rejecting observation (the measurement in a neuron-resolved, replicated system that would remove it) and the cell-resolved data that would test it.
- If nothing replicates beyond chance, the report says so and names the data that would show it (a neuron-resolved organoid deposit, a cortical single-nucleus deposit with more than one animal per arm, or the same classes in a second replicated nuclei experiment).
