# Dated line, 2026-11-20 (SCI): what "two knockout clusters" means for GSE156243, and what it does not mean

**Read this before using the phrase.** In my units A, A2, A3, the DDR signature unit (ADV-069), the alterations census and the ADV-065C transfer ledger, "two knockout clusters" of the human organoid data (GSE156243) means **two groups of knockout libraries, cko1+cko2 and cko3+cko4, that I fixed from an earlier whole-transcriptome recomputation** (the pre-registration of ADV-069 states the origin: "fixed from my earlier whole-transcriptome recomputation for the Wnt and maturation questions"). It does **not** mean cell clusters and the data carry **no single-cell resolution**: GSE156243 is bulk RNA-seq, six libraries (2 control, 4 knockout; two further control libraries failed quality control in the original study), human cerebral organoids at 15 weeks from one parental hESC line (WiBR3), as the deposit's own sample records say.

**What the deposit does not say.** It does not state whether the four knockout libraries come from one or several CRISPR clones, or from one or several batches; the metadata read: "Knock out of WWOX", age 15 weeks, line WiBR3. My own early draft of the ADV-099 pre-registration and my message to ADV on 2026-11-20 called the two groups "clones": that was an unsupported inference and is withdrawn. The correct wording is "two groups of knockout libraries (clonal identity not stated)". Consequence for any claim of replication across the two groups: they show that a library-level signal is not carried by a single library, they are not independent clones and not independent experiments.
