# Held tables in the repository: names and one-line descriptions only (contents are NOT given; do not try to open them)
1. GSE156243: human cerebral organoids, WWOX exon-deletion knockout (4 libraries) versus wild type (2 libraries), week 15, bulk RNA counts and the authors' DESeq2 lists; gene-level.
2. GSE150123: human organoid differentiation time course (day bins), bulk RNA; used as a maturation calibration.
3. GSE158681 / GSE158682: mouse P17 hippocampus and cortex bulk RNA TPM tables of Wwox conditional lines (null, neuron-targeted N, S-line deletions) with controls, 2 to 4 animals per group; transcript-level TPM plus committed gene-level summaries.
4. GSE158684: single-nucleus RNA of P17 hippocampus, S-line knockout pool versus control pool, one pool per arm (about 3,000 nuclei each); matrices of UMI counts.
5. GSE215425: P47T WW1 missense knock-in versus wild type, four brain regions (hippocampus, cortex, cerebellum, prefrontal cortex), 5 versus 5 animals, bulk RNA with DESeq2 results.
6. GSE52564 and GSE73721: public cell-class RNA tables of mouse and human brain (neuron, astrocyte, oligodendrocyte lineage, microglia, endothelium), class means in FPKM.
7. GSE126075: human NSC line with WWOX knockdown, CAGE TPM, one library per arm.
8. LINCS-type signature census and the derived lists of the GSE156243 contrast (query sets, composition class per gene, lists at padj thresholds).
9. Single-cell and nuclei class tables of the repository packages (interneuron population counts, glia, PV class pools, cell-class expression for channel and transporter genes).
10. Clinical report tables: a table of case-report rows (children with a treatment, a seizure outcome and a development outcome as stated, 57 rows from 14 reports), and a sealed extraction of treatment statements (not available to you).
11. Compound catalogue (approved and investigational compound records with targets and mechanism rows), compound cards (hypothesis-level) and an open-questions map (a table of registered questions).
12. Published-paper-derived tables held as package outputs: intrinsic properties of knockout pyramidal cells (input resistance, rest, sag, rebound, one paper), burst statistics of the slice preparation (inter-burst interval, duration, per-slice frequency), a developmental NMDAR current measure (an ifenprodil-sensitive fraction at two ages in wild type), an ethosuximide and lithium PTZ seizure-score figure quantification, supplementary tables of channel tool pharmacology (K2P, Kv7, Kir, Cav3).
