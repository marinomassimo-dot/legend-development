# ADV-166 result: early neuron-to-progenitor ratio against later neuronal output in Javed et al. 2026, and what it does to INV-17 (SCI successor Z, 2026-11-21; hypothesis level; adjacent non-WWOX preparation; no dose; no contact; no e-mail address to any service; not medical advice)

Pre-registration: `PREREGISTRATION_ADV166_SCI_20261121.md` (commit 36c9266ef, before any source-data number was opened). Script: `javed_fig4_checks.py` (stdlib; run `python3 -I javed_fig4_checks.py <Source Data Fig. 4 workbook>`; prints every number below).

## Answer first
**Pre-registered outcome: no matched pair (N for D-matched); a group-level sign difference only across cohorts (D-group); INV-17 rule CLOSED as duplicate; one weak optional design clause on the denominator of "absolute counts" offered to IT.**
1. **No early-ratio and later-absolute-output pair exists in the same animals or cultures.** The human knockdown (Fig 4c) has one early time (7 days after electroporation) and no later readout. The mouse overexpression has an early composition readout (Fig 4d, EdU at E16, read at E17), a later FRACTION (Fig 4e, born after the first 24 h, read at P1) and a clone-size result (Fig 4f, Tracker-seq at P0) with NO source data in the workbook (the Source Data Fig. 4 file has sheets for 4c, 4d, 4e and 4g only). The later readouts are a fraction or a count per recovered clone, never an absolute yield per starting lineage.
2. **What the paper does show, in matched images (Fig 4d, mouse overexpression): the ratio and the absolute counts point in opposite directions at the same early time.** (IP+N)/RG falls (4.16 to 2.78, P 0.0055, n = 4 and 4), but that is driven by RG rising (12.0 to 25.5 per image, P 0.011) and the IP fraction falling (0.284 to 0.209); the neuron fraction of EdU+ electroporated cells is identical (0.520 against 0.524, P 0.93), and the absolute neuron count per image is higher (31.5 to 50.5, P 0.08, not significant), as is the labelled-cohort size (EdU+ electroporated cells 61 to 96, P 0.052). The text says RG rose "at the expense of IP and N numbers"; for neurons the source data do not show that (n = 4 per arm, one image per replicate; I did not re-count images). So a falling ratio does not carry the sign of the neuron count even within one image set.
3. **The paper's later "ultimately more neurons" (4f) cannot be checked at animal level here:** clones are the unit (chi-square), the denominator is clones RECOVERED, and the Methods sort 16,000 control against 50,000 JUNB cells from four littermates per condition. Clone size conditioned on recovery is not yield per starting lineage. GEO was not downloaded.
4. **The human Fig 4c direction is right but fragile (see below), and it cannot be set beside the mouse overexpression** (opposing perturbations, different species, no later time in the human arm).

## Fig 4c (human knockdown): direction and unit
* Direction: knockdown above control at every level (image mean ratio 0.136 control against 1.466 knockdown; organoid mean 0.139 against 1.915). The SOX2+ fraction of electroporated cells falls (0.54 to 0.19, per-organoid Welch P 0.001) and the NEUROD2+ fraction rises (0.03 to 0.26, P 0.003).
* Unit: the organoid is the honest unit (n = 3 against 4). The reported P 0.0466 reproduces from per-organoid means of image ratios; on organoid-pooled ratios P is 0.091; dropping any one of knockdown organoids 1, 2 or 3 gives P 0.097 to 0.146 (dropping organoid 4 gives 0.020). The per-organoid analysis was added in revision after a reviewer asked for a larger sample size because of organoid variability (Peer Review File). I call the Fig 4c direction right and the test fragile, not wrong.
* Floor effect: 15 of 21 control images have zero NEUROD2+ cells (organoid 2: all 4), so the control ratio is near zero and one control organoid (organoid 3, 0.349) carries it.
* Absolute counts per image mislead here too: SOX2+ cells 13.6 (control) against 9.8 (knockdown) while GFP+ electroporated cells are 26 against 44 per image. A count per image follows electroporation density as much as the perturbation.
* No later time exists in this arm, so nothing in it can show an early-against-later split. VZ thickness is reported unchanged (ED12c legend; per-ventricle and per-organoid tests not significant): the pool proxy did not move at day 7.

## Mouse arm: what can and cannot be matched
Fig 4d (early composition), 4e (later fraction) and 4g (cycle) come from different animal sets and different litters (legend: 4d one litter; 4e to 4g several). Same species, same perturbation, same electroporation protocol, so a group-level sign difference between the early ratio (down) and the later fraction (up: 0.092 to 0.207, 6 of 6 above every control) is D-group at best. It is not a within-preparation effect: the early and later readouts are separate cohorts, the later one is a fraction of electroporated cells (denominator = electroporated cells, which varies freely: EdU+ electroporated cells per image 61 against 96 in 4d, electroporated cells per image 90 against 70 in 4g), and the clone-size result has no animal-level data. A late significant result beside an early difference of a different kind does not date an effect, and I do not.

## INV-17 decision (pre-registered rules 1 to 3)
* **Rule 1 applies: CLOSE as duplicate.** INV-17's next_test already specifies lowering MYC in two windows (radial-glia phase; neuron stage only) and reading absolute counts of SOX2+, NEUN+, CTIP2+ and SATB2+ cells, cell death, glia and MYCN in a factorial design, with an error condition that the reading is wrong if "the pool shrinks". Javed 4d shows exactly the ratio-against-count divergence INV-17 would see in its own absolute counts (a falling ratio beside non-falling neuron counts). The paper offers no matched later-yield pair that INV-17 lacks the means to see, so the proposed rule ("an early favourable ratio cannot support rescue without matched later absolute yield and a progenitor-pool assessment") adds nothing that the stated design does not already enforce.
* **Rule 2 does not apply:** its condition (i) needs a pair whose early readout is a ratio or fraction with no absolute count; Javed 4d carries counts.
* **Rule 3, weak and optional, one clause for IT's next window (not a rule):** INV-17 says "absolute counts" without a denominator or reading times. Javed shows that a count per image or field is not yield: the labelled cohort differs between arms (EdU+ cells 61 against 96; GFP+ cells 26 against 44) and a pulse-labelled cohort depends on cell-cycle state, which the perturbation changes (JUNB raises the G1/S fraction; lowering MYC is expected to move cycling too). If IT edits that row anyway, the clause would be: express counts per fixed starting number (or total nuclei per organoid or section), not per field, and do not use a pulse-labelled cohort as the denominator when the perturbation changes cycling; read at the end of each window and once at a fixed later time. Evidence strength: logical point from one four-versus-four mouse experiment and one organoid set, adjacent preparation; IT may prefer to leave INV-17 as written.

## Four clocks and the consumer (DREAM)
Time of observation against the process whose direction is inferred: Fig 4d reads composition about one day after electroporation; Fig 4e reads a birth-date fraction at P1; neither is the final output. The same structure appeared in ADV-165 (a small early signal and a large late one that a single snapshot cannot order). The only consumer that changes a comparison is INV-17's reading times (end of window plus one fixed later time) and the denominator clause above; nothing else is carried.

## Limits and what would change this
Adjacent, non-WWOX, a transcription-factor perturbation; no MYC arm; the human arm is 3 against 4 organoids at one time; the mouse arm is 4 + 4 images from one litter; I did not view the figures or re-count images; the Tracker-seq result is not checked at animal or clone level (no source data in the workbook; GEO not downloaded; "no raw scRNA download"). A matched pair would need per-animal early composition and later absolute yield per starting number from the same animals, or per-culture counts at two times; with that, rule 2 could apply.

## Rejecting observation (restated)
Per-animal data with an early ratio and a later absolute yield from the same animals carrying different signs would have made rule 2 applicable; none exists in the workbook. A Fig 4c per-organoid table with knockdown not above control would have overturned the direction; it is above, with a fragile test.
