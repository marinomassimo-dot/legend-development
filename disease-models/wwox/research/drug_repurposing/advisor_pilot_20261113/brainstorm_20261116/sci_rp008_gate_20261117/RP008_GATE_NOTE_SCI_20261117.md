## Q1 in detail: level and replicates for Wnt-5α and JNK (P361)
* Level: protein. Locator, Results: "Western blotting results demonstrated that the recombinant plasmid group had significantly higher protein levels of Wnt-5α and JNK (1.539±0.055 and 1.565±0.042, respectively) compared with the empty plasmid group (0.816±0.021 and 1.087±0.035, respectively) and the control group (0.849±0.007 and 1.085±0.026, respectively)." Figure 5 legend: "Detection of Wnt-5α and p-JNK protein in three groups of cells by western blotting."
* The abstract's "upregulate the mRNA expression levels of Wnt-5α, JNK and caspase-3" and the Discussion's "significantly higher levels of Wnt-5α, JNK and caspase-3 mRNA" are not what the Results describe: only caspase-3 was measured as mRNA (end-point PCR, 30 cycles, gel band ratio to β-actin). A reader of the abstract alone would take Wnt-5α as an mRNA change, which is the mistake the stop rule's wording guards against.
* JNK versus phospho-JNK: the Methods antibody list and the Figure 5 legend say p-JNK; the Results and abstract say JNK; total JNK is not reported. The signal is therefore, at best, phospho-JNK without a total-JNK denominator.
* Replicates: none stated. The errors are SEM (Methods: "mean ± standard error of the mean"), which implies repeated measures but not how many, whether from separate passages, or whether the bands are from one blot. The figure images were not inspected (see Limits).
* Reagent identity cannot be checked from the paper: the catalogue numbers listed in the two papers collide. In P237: CD133 MAB4310, CD117 MA1-12192, BCRP 254515, ABCG2 AM1125a, Nanog AP1486c, secondary antibody 13764022678. In P361 the same numbers name different targets: CDK2 MAB4310, **Wnt-5α MA1-12192, p-JNK 254515**, cyclin D1 AM1125a, CDK4 AP1486c, cyclin E 13764022678; and each P361 primary is described as "goat-anti-rabbit monoclonal" at 1:10,000, which names a secondary reagent. At most one of the two lists is right for each shared number. This is a reporting defect, not a finding that the antibodies were wrong, but it means the identity of the Wnt-5α and p-JNK reagents is not established by the text.

## Q2 in detail: both controls
Yes for the two measured proteins, with the empty-plasmid and untransfected groups indistinguishable (Results: "No statistically significant difference was identified between the empty plasmid group and the control group (P>0.05; Fig. 5)"). The ratio is about 1.8 to 1.9 fold for Wnt-5α and about 1.4 fold for JNK. The same cells show G0/G1 arrest and 28.19% apoptosis (against 8.27% and 7.11%), so the Wnt-5α and JNK rise sits inside a stressed, dying population; nothing in P361 separates cause from consequence (rival R4 of IT's note stands untouched).

## Q3 in detail: one origin
* Same isolate and place: P237 "Human ovarian cancer stem cells were isolated and stored in the Laboratory of Obstetrics and Gynecology at The Affiliated Hospital of Xuzhou Medical College"; P361 "Ovarian cancer stem cells and the pcDNA3.1-WWOX eukaryotic expression vector were provided by and stored at the Affiliated Hospital of Xuzhou Medical College". Both cite the same isolation paper (Yan et al., Jiangsu Medical Journal 2012, in Chinese; not held). P237's Discussion names the parent line: "cancer stem cells derived from the human ovarian cancer cell line HO8910".
* Different transfection events by the text: P237 "Lipofector Liposomal Transfection Kit (Beyotime ...)", "transfection efficiency, 68%"; P361 "Lipofectamine 2000 transfection kit", selection in neomycin. Whether the stable cells are one frozen pool is not stated. Passage number: not stated in either.
* Consequence: treat P237 and P361 as one origin (one parental isolate, one construct, one laboratory) with an unverified relation between the two stable lines. Any statement that "two papers" support a WWOX to Wnt-5α effect over-counts.

## What the papers do not support (reading notes that change IT's table)
* IT's O1 shorthand "lowers self-renewal ... raises E-cadherin" is half of the authors' own reading. P237's Discussion says: "Collectively, the results suggest that WWOX-expressing cells lose their potential for differentiation." WWOX cells sit at the post-differentiation marker level already (CD133+ 21.3%, E-cadherin 0.69) and **do not move on serum** (CD133+ 21.3 to 19.9%; E-cadherin 0.69±0.09 to 0.71±0.02), whereas both controls move (CD133+ 76.4 to 25.3% and 79.7 to 23.1%; E-cadherin 0.41 to 0.72 and 0.46 to 0.71). The phenotype is a cell state fixed at the differentiated end with slower growth, not evidence of an active differentiation cue.
* Selection is open: the CD133+/CD117+ fall and the 28% apoptosis in the same cell family cannot be told apart from loss of the CD133+ fraction.
* Small internal inconsistencies in P237 lower the trust in its numbers (reporting level, not claims): Table I names a mitoxantrone dose and the text calls it "doxorubicin at 0.25 μg/ml"; the text gives a tumour-forming time of 76.9 days for WWOX cells where Table II shows one tumour at 68 days for that dose; one SD (0.34±0.79) exceeds its mean.
