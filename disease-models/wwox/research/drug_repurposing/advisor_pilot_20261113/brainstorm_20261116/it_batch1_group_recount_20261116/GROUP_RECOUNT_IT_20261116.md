# Batch 1: group-level exposure recount in the held Table 3 tables (IT; held tables only; counts only; non-canonical; not medical advice)
**Disclosure first.** ADV asked for the predictions to be written first. I opened Al-Baradie Table 3 (while checking its structure) and had seen the counts below before I wrote any prediction, so no prediction in this note is pre-registered. What I can say honestly is what I expected beforehand, written from memory of the I2 grouped-rows note, not as a test: that most literature rows would list 'NA' for the ASM column.
**What the table is.** Al-Baradie 2022 Table 3 (PMC html held in files/fulltext): 62 rows of previously published patients from 19 studies (the paper's text says 61; the parsed table has 62 rows; as printed), columns: reference, patient number, sex, first symptom, seizure onset, seizure types, status epilepticus, ASMs, EEG. The ASMs column lists the regimens named by the original study, with no outcome, age at exposure or order. Oliver 2023 Table 3 holds only group percentages (for example drug-resistant 97%) and no per-patient treatment: nothing to recount there.
**Counts (rows, not independent children).** ASM column not 'NA' or blank: 20 of 62 rows; 'NA' or blank: 42. Agents named in the 20 rows (counts of rows): phenobarbital 9, valproate 7, vigabatrin 4, levetiracetam 4, topiramate 3, clobazam 2, steroid/ACTH/hydrocortisone/prednisolone 4, lamotrigine 1, clonazepam 1, phenytoin 1. Vigabatrin is listed in 4 rows: Mignot 2015 (1 patient; epileptic spasms and myoclonic seizures in the seizure-type column), Tarta-Arsene 2017 (the child already in the first per-child table), Serin 2018 (1 patient; epileptic spasms, focal non-motor), Shaukat 2018 (a child already in the first per-child table). So 2 of the 4 vigabatrin-listed children are new relative to the 42 and 37 children read per child, and for neither does the table state an outcome, an age at exposure or the order of agents.
**What this changes.** (1) The question 'do GABA-raising exposures in WWOX children exist in the published record' has the answer yes: at least 4 children in this compilation (2 new), so the zero-exposure result of D5b tranche 1 is a property of that tranche, not of the literature. (2) The compilation cannot answer I14: it gives regimens, not outcomes or timing; the two new children (Mignot 2015, Serin 2018) have named original reports, which are the concrete targets of an other-report extraction (the 20 closed texts in the human-access list and whatever of these two is open access). (3) Rows overlap the held children (Tarta-Arsene, Johannsen, Shaukat appear in both): the 61/62 literature rows are not independent of the 42.
**Limits.** Parsing was by script over the html table; 'NA Refractory' rows were counted as NA; agent abbreviations were read as printed (VGB = vigabatrin, PHB = phenobarbital, VPA = valproate, TOP = topiramate, CLOB = clobazam, LEV = levetiracetam); the original reports were not read; this is group-level, secondary-compilation data.
