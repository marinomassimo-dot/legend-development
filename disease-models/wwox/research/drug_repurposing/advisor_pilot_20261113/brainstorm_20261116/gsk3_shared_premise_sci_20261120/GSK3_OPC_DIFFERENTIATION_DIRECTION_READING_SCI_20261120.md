# Direction of GSK-3 inhibition and beta-catenin stabilisation on oligodendrocyte-precursor differentiation: a time-boxed reading for ADV (SCI successor, 2026-11-20; hypothesis level; the doses below are the cited studies' own conditions, not a dose, schedule or recommendation for any person; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

Assignment: ADV, message of 2026-11-20 after `bb1f634a7`, accepted as step 49 of `ORCH-SCI-ADV065-DEEPEN-20261118` (accepted 2026-11-20 04:18Z). Question: does GSK-3 inhibition (which stabilises beta-catenin) help or hurt oligodendrocyte-precursor (OPC) differentiation and remyelination, with models and doses, and must the v3.2 broad table carry an "oligodendrocyte stage readout under the GSK-3 inhibitor arm" cell. Companion: `GSK3_VS_OLIGODENDROCYTE_CLASS_ONE_UNIT_NOTE_SCI_20261120.md` (its section 2 already calls the sign for oligodendroglia "divided"; this note reads the studies behind that word and says why it is divided).

## 0. Answer first
1. **The literature is divided, and the division has a visible structure: the sign follows the cell system and the stage, not the drug class.** Inhibition of GSK-3 **promotes** differentiation or myelination in two systems (postnatal mouse intracerebroventricular and optic-nerve organotypic; an injured preterm-rabbit white matter), and **suppresses** differentiation in two others (rat OPC cultures). Constitutive beta-catenin activation **delays** differentiation and myelination in vivo (Fancy 2009), but at the onset of differentiation the same transcription factor partner (Tcf7l2) changes role through a co-factor (Kaiso), so "Wnt up" has no single sign inside the lineage (Zhao 2016).
2. **No study found tests GSK-3 inhibition in a model whose defect looks like ours** (a hypomyelination-type stage deficit from the committed precursor onward with the precursor pool intact, P17 cortex, a gene knockout that is not an injury). The two in vivo positives are an injury model and a postnatal intracerebroventricular injection; the two negatives are cultures. Nothing predicts the sign in the Wwox-deficient preparation.
3. **v3.2 broad: yes, add it, as an interaction cell with no predicted sign** (ADV's expectation confirmed by the reading). Pre-register both directions (section 5). It costs no animals: the stage readout is already in the broad table for the differentiation-support arm, and the GSK-3 arm opened by an S-up panel runs in the same animals.

## 2. The studies, with models and the conditions as reported
**Inhibitor promotes (positive).**
* *Preterm rabbit IVH (item 4).* Glycerol induces IVH in preterm pups; AR-A014418 intramuscular **20 mg/kg twice a day for seven days from postnatal day 1** against vehicle (dimethyl sulfoxide); analysis at days 3, 7 and 14; n 5 pups per group for the cell counts, 10 per group for the day-14 behaviour. In IVH: the Olig2-positive Nkx2.2-positive cells (the differentiation-committed stage) were reduced against no-IVH (P = 0.005) and treatment raised them (P = 0.004); the total Olig2-positive pool was similar in the three groups; platelet-derived-growth-factor-receptor-alpha (PDGFRalpha) positive cell density fell with IVH and rose with treatment; MBP rose with treatment. **Recombinant Wnt3A (intracerebroventricular) raised activated beta-catenin but did not raise MBP and raised inflammatory cytokines and Notch signalling.** The authors' own reading is that the effect may run through **suppressed inflammation and Notch** (microglia density and IL1beta lower, Notch intracellular domain, Hes1 and Hes5 lower), not through beta-catenin as such. **In pups without IVH the same dose "did not show difference in myelination" (the text says "data not shown").** Ultrastructure: myelinated-axon density 150 percent of IVH vehicle, not significant; g-ratio 0.71, 0.73 and 0.71 in the three groups (n 3 to 4).
* *Azim and Butt 2011 (item 2, abstract).* Several GSK3beta inhibitors (AR-A014418, lithium, indirubin, L803-mt) increased OPCs and oligodendrocytes and promoted myelination; GSK3beta inhibition increased differentiation while Wnt3a inhibited it, in a postnatal mouse and an optic-nerve organotypic system. IT's row T4-01 carries the same sentences. Doses not read.

**Inhibitor suppresses (negative).**
* *Zhou 2014 (item 1, abstract).* In rat OPC cultures pharmacological inhibition and knockdown of GSK3beta "remarkably suppressed OPC differentiation", activated GSK3beta promoted it, nuclear beta-catenin accumulated under inhibition (the abstract; no concentrations read).
* *Dong 2021 (item 6).* Primary rat OPC cultures; **SB216763 10 micromolar** reversed the beta-catenin fall after Foxg1 knockdown; on the culture readouts it raised the proliferation and the NG2- and O4-positive shares and **lowered the CNPase-positive share**; the authors state that inhibiting GSK-3beta "inhibits the differentiation of OPCs while promoting their proliferation". The same lab's in vivo arm is Foxg1 deletion (cuprizone), not a drug.

**Wnt and beta-catenin themselves.**
* *Fancy 2009 (item 3, abstract).* Constitutive beta-catenin activation in the lineage and APC-heterozygous mice show a profound delay of developmental myelination and of remyelination; Wnt activity is raised in human multiple-sclerosis lesions. This is the basis of the "Wnt delays differentiation" reading ADV cites.
* *Zhao 2016 (item 5).* At the onset of differentiation Tcf7l2 binds the co-repressor **Kaiso** and **blocks beta-catenin signalling**; the Kaiso-Tcf7l2 association "reverses the role of the beta-catenin/Tcf7l2 transcriptional complex from a differentiation repressor to an activator"; at maturation Tcf7l2 recruits Sox10 and drives cholesterol biosynthesis genes. A mechanism by which stabilising beta-catenin can have opposite effects at two stages of the same lineage, in mice with an Olig1-Cre Tcf7l2 mutant (genetics, not a GSK-3 drug).
* *Monocyte Wnt 2025 (item 7).* Removing Wnt release from monocytes improved remyelination in lysolecithin lesions (MBP area, P = 0.013, n 3 to 5): a non-OPC compartment where Wnt impairs repair. It shows that a systemic GSK-3 inhibitor reaches cells besides the OPC.

## 3. Why the sign differs (what the reading supports, and what it does not)
Three differences line up with the sign, none of them tested head to head in any paper read:
(a) **stage:** inhibition read at the precursor (proliferation, NG2, O4) and at the late marker (CNPase, MBP) in the same culture can move in opposite directions (Dong 2021: proliferation and NG2 and O4 up, CNPase down), so "differentiation" depends on the marker;
(b) **injury or not:** the in vivo positive with a full-text methods section is an inflammatory white-matter injury in which the drug lowered microglia and Notch; in the uninjured pups of the same study the myelin readout did not change (data not shown);
(c) **cell system:** pure OPC cultures (negative) against whole tissue with other cells (positive). Plus the Kaiso mechanism that lets one transcription complex flip role at differentiation onset.
This is a reading of the held reports, not an experiment: an alternative (species, compound, concentration, timing) is not excluded by anything here.

## 4. What this means for our preparation
The Wwox-deficient P17 cortex (v2 A2; unit A2): mature-oligodendrocyte transcripts lower, every stage from the committed precursor onward about -0.5, the precursor pool intact; the lineage meaning of the deficit is UNDECIDED. A GSK-3 inhibitor arm opened by an S-up panel would meet this deficit in **uninjured** tissue, so item 4's "no change in uninjured pups" is the nearest in vivo datum and it is an unshown result; the in vitro negatives and the in vivo positives point in opposite directions; there is **no model of a knockout-type stage deficit with an intact precursor pool in the studies read**. The sign of a GSK-3 inhibitor on this stage is therefore **not predicted** by the literature: it could help (the injury-model reading), hurt (the culture reading, the Wnt-delay reading) or do nothing (the uninjured rabbit). That is a reason to measure it in the same animals, not a reason to exclude the arm.

The cell is a **readout**, not a claim that GSK-3 inhibition is expected to hurt or help.
