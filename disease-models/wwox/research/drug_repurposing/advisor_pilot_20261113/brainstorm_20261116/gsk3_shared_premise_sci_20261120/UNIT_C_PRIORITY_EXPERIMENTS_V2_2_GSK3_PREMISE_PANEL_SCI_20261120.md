## A.6 GSK-3 premise panel (shared tissue)

**A.6.1 What it tests, and what it does not.** The premise of the GSK-3 candidate (class: GSK-3 inhibition by a chemistry unrelated to lithium; Wwox loss as a loss of a docking-motif partner of GSK3-beta, SDR region, L404) is **protein-level**: that in a Wwox-deficient neuron the GSK3-beta substrate phosphorylation is higher than in controls. The panel measures that in the P17 cortex of the neuronal-deletion line. It does **not** test (i) directness of the WWOX-GSK3-beta binding (Wang 2012 is lysate pull-down and co-immunoprecipitation; no purified binding; the authors' own abstract says WWOX "can bind directly" to GSK3-beta, and I checked that sentence in the held text after IT's correction of 2026-11-20: "direct" and "inhibitor" are the authors' words, what the assays do not show is directness, and "S9-independent" occurs nowhere in the paper), (ii) whether the reference missense allele Q230P changes the binding (**effect on binding untested**; Q230P lies outside both docking stretches 296-320 and 388-412 and the deletion line is a loss model, so a result here transfers to the missense class only if Q230P behaves as loss for GSK-3 binding, which nothing held tests), (iii) any benefit of a GSK-3 inhibitor (no inhibitor arm is added), (iv) cell type: the panel is bulk cortex (tau is mainly neuronal in cortex and total GSK-3 and beta-catenin are pan-cellular: both from memory, not read here).

**A.6.2 Animals, tissue and the handling change that "same tissue" requires.** Readout animals: the vehicle knockout pups (12) and the vehicle wild-type littermates (8) of v2 A.3, plus the treated arms as an exploratory second use (A.6.5). Age P17, cortex (primary) and hippocampus (secondary; Cheng 2020 saw lower pS9 in cerebellum, hippocampus and cortex). **The panel needs unfixed tissue: one hemisphere (or the cortex of one side) per animal is snap-frozen at collection and the other side goes to the v2 section or nuclei readouts.** A perfusion-fixed brain cannot supply a phospho-panel; v2 A.4 reads sections or nuclei, so v2.2 states the split as a **handling change** (no new animal; the nuclei prep, if used, takes the frozen side and the panel takes a separate aliquot of the same hemisphere). Because tau phospho-sites are labile with anaesthesia and the hypothermia it causes (source at abstract level, found by the blind verifier: Planel 2007, PMID 17376970, mouse brain, anaesthesia gave rapid tau hyperphosphorylation with hypothermia and normothermia rescued it; supporting abstract Run 2009, PMID 19276556; the dependence on **post-mortem delay is from memory, no source reached**), the same procedure is applied to every animal: the same interval from decapitation to freezing (fixed before data, in seconds), no anaesthesia, or the same anaesthesia with core temperature monitored and held, genotype-balanced order within each litter, phosphatase and protease inhibitors in the lysis buffer, coded samples, one operator blind to genotype and arm.

**A.6.3 The panel (each per animal, each with its total).**
| Readout | Antibody pair | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Tau phospho-S396 over total tau | pS396, total tau (the sites Wang 2012 reads; GSK3-beta-dependent in that paper) | primary |
| Tau phospho-S404 over total tau | pS404, total tau | primary |
| GSK-3 regulatory sites | beta Ser9 (alpha Ser21 if the antibody is paired) over total GSK3-beta; Tyr216 (alpha Tyr279) over total GSK-3 | pattern (decides which reading holds, not whether the candidate stands) |

| Controls on every blot | a lambda-phosphatase-treated lysate aliquot (phospho-specificity), a dilution series of a pooled sample (linear range), beta-actin or total protein stain for loading, a pooled reference lysate on every membrane | assay validity |
Cheng 2020's Fig 7c reports phosphorylated and total GSK3-beta **each as a ratio to beta-actin**, not phosphorylated over total (read by me in the held XML legend and confirmed by the blind verifier, H6; the legend gives "the ratio of phosphorylated or total GSK3-beta to beta-actin", four independent experiments, P20, three regions): a lower pS9 over actin can come from lower total GSK3-beta. v2.2 reports **pS9 over total GSK3-beta in the same lane and total over loading separately**.

**A.6.4 Prediction, rejecting observation, decision per outcome, interpretable null.**
*Prediction (from the premise).* In the vehicle knockout cortex the primary substrate readouts (tau pS396 over total tau, pS404 over total tau) are higher than in vehicle wild-type littermates; the regulatory-site pattern is not predicted by the premise (Wang 2012's only pS9 statement is a correlation during retinoic-acid differentiation, and its mechanism is binding, not a regulatory-site change; Cheng 2020 reports lower pS9, the activating direction; the two sources are in tension, interactome result A.2).
*Rejecting observation.* Both primary readouts within the equivalence margin of the wild-type value (A.6.6) with an interval that excludes an elevation beyond the margin.
| Outcome (vehicle knockout against vehicle wild type, cortex) | Reading | Decision for the GSK-3 candidate |
|---|---|---|
| **S-up:** both primary readouts above the margin (interval excluding no change), same direction in the hippocampus not required | the premise holds at protein level in this line at P17 | **candidate stays**; a GSK-3-inhibitor arm is opened in the same design (A.6.7); the pattern rows below name the sub-reading |
| S-up with pS9/total lower or pY216/total higher | consistent with Cheng 2020's direction (activation by regulatory-site pattern) | stays; the pS9 tension with Wang 2012's S9-independence statement is resolved toward "pS9 moves" in this tissue (a finding for the registry owners; the registry is not edited here) |
| S-up with pS9/total and pY216/total unchanged | binding-restraint route (Wang 2012's model) without regulatory-site change | stays; pS9 would then be a false-negative readout for this route, which is an observation, not an assumption |
| S-up with beta-catenin lower | coherent with more active GSK-3 across substrates | stays |
| S-up with beta-catenin higher or unchanged | tau-specific loss of restraint, not a general GSK-3 activation (the two readings of IT's T4-06 predict opposite beta-catenin signs) | stays, narrowed: a global GSK-3 inhibitor would act on a substrate the data do not show moved |
| **S-flat:** both primary readouts inside the margin, regulatory sites flat | no protein-level premise in this line, tissue and age | **candidate closed for this model** (ADV's rule), stated with its limits: bulk cortex, P17, neuronal-deletion line (not Cheng's global null at P20), an effect smaller than the margin or confined to a minority cell population is not excluded |
| S-flat with pS9/total lower (Cheng replicates in the regulatory site only) | a regulatory-site change without a substrate consequence: pS9 is not a readout of active kinase in this tissue | closed on the substrate criterion; the S9 observation is recorded separately, not as support |
| **S-down:** tau phosphorylation lower in the knockout | opposite to the premise | not supported; read first for composition or maturity (total tau, a neuronal marker, v2's lineage readouts on the same animals) before any inference |
| **Unresolved:** one primary above the margin and one inside, or an interval wider than the margin | underpowered or mixed | no decision; the n that would resolve it is computed from the observed SD (A.6.6) and the repeat is named |
| **Assay failure:** phosphatase control still reads, linearity fails, or handling interval differs between genotypes beyond the fixed tolerance | uninterpretable | repeat on the retained frozen aliquots; never read as a null |

**A.6.5 What the elevated outcome gives the oligodendrocyte readout, and the exploratory second use.** ADV's reading: an elevated panel keeps the GSK-3 candidate and gives the oligodendrocyte readout **an independent cousin**, because both are read in the same animals. Stated precisely: (i) the per-animal mature-oligodendrocyte score (v2 A.4 tier 1) and the substrate score are two readouts of the same 12 knockout animals, so their association across knockout animals can be examined (exploratory, no causal statement, n = 12 gives a coarse estimate); (ii) **GSK-3 is also a regulator inside the oligodendrocyte lineage with the sign divided in the literature** (IT's abstract-level rows T4-01 and T4-02: inhibition raised oligodendrocyte differentiation in one system and suppressed it in another), so a bulk panel cannot say whether a substrate change is neuronal (the premise) or lineage-internal; a GSK-3 effect on the oligodendrocyte readout and a neuronal premise remain two separate hypotheses; (iii) in the treated arms of v2 (clemastine, thyromimetic), reading the same panel is an **exploratory** question: does a differentiation agent move the neuronal substrate panel at all. No decision rests on (iii); if the panel is flat in vehicle knockouts it is not read.

**A.6.6 Analysis plan fixed before data (design, not a registration).** Unit: the animal; litter as a blocking factor (genotype contrast within litters; a mixed model with litter as random effect, or a stratified permutation test). Primary: the two tau ratios in the cortex, log scale, knockout over wild type, interval from the litter-aware model; Holm across the two. Everything else (pattern rows, hippocampus, treated arms) is secondary and labelled. **Equivalence margin for "flat": the ratio interval inside 0.80 to 1.25** (a design choice to be revisited before data if the laboratory's own assay variance makes it unreachable; it is not derived from a held effect size, because no held protein-level effect size with n exists: Cheng 2020 gives representative blots and ratio numbers without a test and without n). **Power, order of magnitude:** with 12 knockout and 8 wild-type animals and 80 percent power at two-sided 0.05, the difference detected is about 1.3 standard deviations (normal approximation, ignoring litter); an elevation of 0.5 standard deviations would need about 64 animals per genotype. So the panel is a **large-effect detector**: S-flat is a statement about large effects. If the observed SD gives an equivalence interval wider than the margin, the outcome is "unresolved", never "closed". Per-animal values, both blots' raw images and the handling intervals are archived with the animal identifiers.

**A.6.7 Exposure and engagement control, conditional on S-up (no arm is opened by v2.2).** If S-up, a GSK-3-inhibitor arm of non-lithium chemistry is added in the same design with its own controls. What the sheets say about the agents (ADV-098 batch 9, HE, on main at 7a9f35635, read before citing): potency in vitro is low-nanomolar to sub-micromolar for five agents, **none has a brain or plasma concentration found by those routes**, alpha against beta is at most about 2.5-fold in same-document pairs so a beta-selective agent is not supported by the data, and **tideglusib is non-ATP-competitive and time-dependent (IC50 139 nM with 3 h pre-incubation against 1.78 microM without)**, so a pharmacodynamic readout must wait for that time. Therefore: (i) brain concentration of the chosen agent at the readout time in satellite pups, total with the tissue basis stated; (ii) **the same panel is the pharmacodynamic control**: tau pS396/pS404 over total tau in treated against vehicle knockouts and wild type; an arm that does not lower the substrate readout at confirmed brain exposure is a failed engagement, not a negative for the class; (iii) the embryonic-progenitor harm cell and the label limits of lithium are written in the one-unit note and carried into any such arm as named harm readouts (wild-type littermates, body weight, seizure threshold).

**A.6.8 What A.6 does not claim.** No dose. No claim that the panel changes any arm of v2. No claim that a positive panel makes GSK-3 inhibition a treatment, or that it ranks above or below the oligodendrocyte class (the note `GSK3_VS_OLIGODENDROCYTE_CLASS_ONE_UNIT_NOTE_SCI_20261120.md` compares the two on one unit and ranks neither). The panel is a design for a laboratory; none of it is started by this note.
