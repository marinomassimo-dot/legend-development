## Strong counterexample to my preferred explanation (E1)
1. CO2 asphyxiation before the draw and a 30-minute room-temperature clot can move total CO2 and glucose in every genotype alike (the two analytes that carry the null's "acidotic" and "hypoglycaemic" labels): glucose means of 254 to 296 mg/dL in all groups are, in my inference (not stated in the source), what non-fasted or stressed draws give; the effect of CO2 asphyxiation and of the clot delay on total CO2 and glucose is background knowledge, not something this source tests. An ns for glucose is not an absence of a null-type derangement. Total CO2 was not tabulated at all, so the "acidotic" arm of line 23 cannot be tested in this animal by this source.
2. The WT and het comparators are probably aged (inference from the histology wording and the survival means; the age of the blood-panel animals is not stated): histology shows neoplasia in WT and het (29 percent and 33 percent as printed) and nephropathy "as typically observed in aging mice" (Results 2.2); necropsy and histology samples were taken "either at the end of the experimental period or after euthanasia due to poor health" (Results 2.2), while Methods 4.3 says euthanasia at the moribund stage, and the cohort survival means are 542 (WT), 495 (het) and 393 (hom) days. The age at draw per genotype is not stated and no stage is assigned to any genotype, so I cannot say whether the comparison is moribund against moribund or moribund against end-of-study; if it is the latter it is a pooled-stage comparison.
