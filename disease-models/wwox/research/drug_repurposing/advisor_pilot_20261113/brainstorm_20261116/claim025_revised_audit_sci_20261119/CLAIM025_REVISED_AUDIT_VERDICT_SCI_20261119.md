## 4. Wording points that change no verdict (mine, each with its source; IT's to take)
1. **"Weakly" and "strongly" are our adjectives.** The paper uses "weak" for the WWOX correlations (0.17 to 0.32, GDM and NGT) and for insulin against HK2 and PFK in NGT (-0.34, -0.36), and "strongly" for HIF1A against NFKB1 and DVL2 in NGT (0.66, 0.60); it uses neither word for the GDM ratio correlations, where the Results say "many correlations were found" (NFKB1 -0.21, DVL2 -0.21, CTNNB1 +0.38 p < 0.001; ratio against HIF1A not in the text). Either drop the adjectives on the ratio and give R, or mark them "ours".
2. **Balance of the ratio.** The second sentence gives the ratio against HIF1A (-0.79, Fig 4 only) but not against WWOX (+0.61 in GDM, +0.65 in NGT, Figs 4 and 5), and the ratio against HIF1A is **-0.79 in normal pregnancy too** (Fig 5, which I read). With the first sentence's "the HIF1A change is the larger (inference)" the pair leans toward "HIF1A drives the index", which the paper does not say and the first audit rejected. Suggest adding the ratio-against-WWOX values and "also -0.79 in normal pregnancy (Fig 5); the index contains HIF1A (inference)".
3. WWOX against PFK is -0.23 in the text and **-0.22 in Fig 4** (I read the cell): give both or say "text".
4. Age: the GDM women are older by two years in the median (31.0 against 29.0, p = 0.02, Table 2, read by me); the paper says "slightly".
Not changes, only facts for the record: 3.8 x 1.4 = 5.3, not the paper's 6.5 (the paper does not reconcile them; the sentence reports the paper's numbers); the Discussion's "linkage of WWOX to HIF1α at their transcriptional levels" rests on opposing group means and the ratio, so it does not contradict the within-GDM -0.05, but a reader should not take "not correlated" as the authors' reading; **two label errors in the paper's figures, to add to the text-against-figure list:** Fig 2 panels I and K are swapped against the caption (the image has I = NFKB1, K = DVL2; the caption says DVL2 (I), NFKB1 (K)) and the Fig 5 caption says "GDM group" for a plot whose values (-0.54, -0.43) are the NGT values of the text.
