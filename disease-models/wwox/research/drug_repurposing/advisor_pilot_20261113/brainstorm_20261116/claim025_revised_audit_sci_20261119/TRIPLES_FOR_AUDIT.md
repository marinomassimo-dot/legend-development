# Triples to audit (proposition, quote, anchor)

## T1
- Proposition: The comparison is between 98 women with gestational diabetes (GDM) and 37 women with normal glucose tolerance (NGT), all studied at one clinic.
- Quote: "A total of 135 Caucasian pregnant women consisted of 37 women with normal glucose tolerance (NGT) and 98 women with GDM were enrolled and studied at the Outpatient Diabetological Clinic in Lodz, Poland."
- Anchor: Materials & Methods > Study participants (first paragraph)

## T2
- Proposition: The GDM women were older than the NGT women.
- Quote: "the patients diagnosed as having GDM were slightly but significantly older than the NGT subjects"
- Anchor: Results > Clinical and biochemical characteristics (first paragraph); Table 2

## T3
- Proposition: HIF1A transcript in blood leukocytes is 3.8-fold higher in GDM than in NGT, p < 0.0001.
- Quote 1: "leukocyte HIF1A, PFK, PKM2, DVL2, NFKB1 ( all p < 0.0001 )"
- Quote 2: "a 3.8-fold increase for HIF1A in the diabetic patients"
- Anchor: Results > Gene expression studies during and after pregnancy (first paragraph); Fig 2B

## T4
- Proposition: WWOX transcript in blood leukocytes is 1.4-fold lower in GDM than in NGT, p = 0.0406 (Mann-Whitney U test).
- Quote 1: "There was also a tendency for lower leukocyte WWOX expression in the women with GDM vs the controls with NGT (p = 0.0406)."
- Quote 2: "an 1.4- fold decrease for WWOX"
- Quote 3: "Differences between variables were calculated using the Mann–Whitney U test."
- Anchor: Results > Gene expression studies (first paragraph); Methods > Statistical analysis; Fig 2A

## T5
- Proposition: The p-values reported for the WWOX comparison, the gene-gene correlations and the ratio are not corrected for multiple testing (eleven genes were compared).
- Quote 1: "A p-value <0.05 was considered as significant."
- Quote 2: "the relative mRNA expression of the 11 individual genes involved in the WWOX/HIF1A axis was investigated"
- Anchor: Methods > Statistical analysis; Results > Gene expression studies (first paragraph). ABSENCE CLAIM: search the whole article for any multiple-testing or multiplicity correction

## T6
- Proposition: The Results section calls the WWOX decrease a tendency.
- Quote: "There was also a tendency for lower leukocyte WWOX expression in the women with GDM vs the controls with NGT (p = 0.0406)."
- Anchor: Results > Gene expression studies during and after pregnancy (first paragraph)

## T7
- Proposition: The Abstract describes the WWOX decrease as a significant downregulation.
- Quote: "GDM patients displayed a significant downregulation of WWOX with simultaneous upregulation of HIF1A"
- Anchor: Abstract, Results

## T8
- Proposition: The Discussion describes the WWOX decrease as a significant downregulation.
- Quote 1: "In contrast to the significant upregulation of HIF1A leukocyte WWOX expression was downregulated in diabetic pregnancy versus normal controls"
- Quote 2: "Hence, leukocyte WWOX downregulation seen in our GDM patients may be a unique feature of diabetic pregnancy"
- Anchor: Discussion (paragraph beginning 'The tumor suppressor WWOX'; paragraph beginning 'Strikingly')

## T9
- Proposition: The WWOX/HIF1A index (ratio) is about 6.5-fold lower in GDM than in NGT, p < 0.0001.
- Quote: "the WWOX/HIF1A index was decreased by approximately 6.5-fold (p <0.0001) in the GDM group compared to the NGT group"
- Anchor: Results > Gene expression studies (first paragraph); Fig 2L

## T10
- Proposition: The paper does not apportion the change in the WWOX/HIF1A ratio between the WWOX change and the HIF1A change.
- Quote 1: "thus it appears to be a better indicator of leukocyte transcriptional response of the two related genes to GDM than each of these two genes separately"
- Quote 2: "GDM patients displayed a significant downregulation of WWOX with simultaneous upregulation of HIF1A which resulted in approximately six times reduction in WWOX/HIF1A ratio"
- Anchor: Results > Gene expression studies (first paragraph); Abstract. ABSENCE CLAIM: search the whole article for any statement that attributes the ratio change to one of the two genes

## T11
- Proposition: By the stated fold changes (HIF1A 3.8-fold higher, WWOX 1.4-fold lower) the HIF1A change is the larger of the two.
- Quote: "as evidenced by an 1.4- fold decrease for WWOX and a 3.8-fold increase for HIF1A in the diabetic patients"
- Anchor: Results > Gene expression studies (first paragraph). Say whether this is a statement of the source or arithmetic on the source's numbers

## T12
- Proposition: Within GDM, WWOX is not correlated with HIF1A (Spearman R about -0.05); this value is given in Figure 4 only, not in the running text.
- Quote: "there were weak but significant positive associations of WWOX with CTNNB1 ( R = 0.25) and SLC2A4 (R = 0.17) and negative with PFK (R = − 0.23) and NFKB1 (R = − 0.22; all p < 0.05)"
- Anchor: Fig 4 (GDM correlation plot), cell WWOX x HIF1A; Results > Gene expression correlations. FIGURE VALUE: read the image and say what is printed

## T13
- Proposition: Within GDM, WWOX is not correlated with HK2, PKM2 or SLC2A1.
- Quote: "there were weak but significant positive associations of WWOX with CTNNB1 ( R = 0.25) and SLC2A4 (R = 0.17) and negative with PFK (R = − 0.23) and NFKB1 (R = − 0.22; all p < 0.05)"
- Anchor: Results > Gene expression correlations; Fig 4 cells WWOX x HK2, WWOX x PKM2, WWOX x SLC2A1. FIGURE VALUE

## T14
- Proposition: Within GDM, WWOX correlates weakly and inversely with PFK (R = -0.23) and NFKB1 (R = -0.22), and positively with CTNNB1.
- Quote: "weak but significant positive associations of WWOX with CTNNB1 ( R = 0.25) and SLC2A4 (R = 0.17) and negative with PFK (R = − 0.23) and NFKB1 (R = − 0.22; all p < 0.05)"
- Anchor: Results > Gene expression correlations; Fig 4

## T15
- Proposition: Within GDM, the WWOX/HIF1A ratio correlates inversely and strongly with HIF1A (R = -0.79); this value is given in Figure 4 only, not in the running text.
- Quote: "In the patients with GDM, many correlations were found between WWOX/HIF1A ratio and other genes"
- Anchor: Fig 4 (GDM correlation plot), cell ratio x HIF1A; Results > Gene expression correlations. FIGURE VALUE: read the image and say what is printed; say whether 'strongly' is the paper's word or an interpretation

## T16
- Proposition: Within GDM, the WWOX/HIF1A ratio correlates inversely with the glycolytic genes (SLC2A1, HK2, PFK, PKM2), with R from -0.31 to -0.45.
- Quote 1: "SLC2A1 (R = − 0.31; p = 0.01), HK2 (R = − 0.41), PFK ( R = − 0.45) and PKM2 (R = − 0.42; all p < 0.001)"
- Quote 2: "negative correlations observed in the GDM group between the WWOX/HIF1A ratio and several glycolytic genes driven by HIF-1α (SLC2A1, HK2, PFK, and PKM2)"
- Anchor: Results > Gene expression correlations (first paragraph); Discussion (paragraph beginning 'The tumor suppressor WWOX')

## T17
- Proposition: Within GDM, the WWOX/HIF1A ratio is not correlated with LDHA.
- Quote: "In the patients with GDM, many correlations were found between WWOX/HIF1A ratio and other genes"
- Anchor: Fig 4 (GDM correlation plot), cell ratio x LDHA; Results > Gene expression correlations. ABSENCE / FIGURE VALUE: LDHA is not named among the ratio correlations in the text; read the image

## T18
- Proposition: Within GDM, the WWOX/HIF1A ratio correlates weakly and inversely with NFKB1 (R = -0.21) and DVL2 (R = -0.21, p = 0.05).
- Quote: "negatively with NFKB1 ( R = − 0.21; p < 0.05) and DVL2 ( R = − 0.21; p = 0.05)"
- Anchor: Results > Gene expression correlations (first paragraph); Fig 4

## T19
- Proposition: Within GDM, the WWOX/HIF1A ratio correlates weakly and positively with CTNNB1 (R = +0.38).
- Quote: "positively with SLC2A4 (R = 0.27; p < 0.01) and CTNNB1 ( R = 0.38; p < 0.001)"
- Anchor: Results > Gene expression correlations (first paragraph); Fig 4

## T20
- Proposition: The expression values and correlations are measured at mRNA level (RT-qPCR), in leukocytes that were not separated by cell type; no protein was measured.
- Quote 1: "The pellets containing leukocytes were washed with the phosphate-buffered saline (PBS)."
- Quote 2: "the validity of these findings need to be confirmed in larger studies with more statistical power and with the analysis of protein expression of the components of the WWOX/HIF1A axis in leukocytes of GDM subjects"
- Anchor: Methods > Leukocytes separation and RNA extraction; Conclusions

## T21
- Proposition: In normal pregnancy (NGT group), WWOX correlates positively with insulin and with the HOMA-IR index (R about 0.5).
- Quote: "insulin and HOMA-IR index positively associated with WWOX expression (R = 0.52 and R = 0.53, respectively; p = 0.001 for both)"
- Anchor: Results > Correlations between maternal metabolic parameters and the expression of WWOX/HIF-related genes (NGT paragraph); Table S1

## T22
- Proposition: In normal pregnancy (NGT group), WWOX correlates positively with PFK, LDHA and CTNNB1 (R about 0.3).
- Quote: "weak positive associations of PFK, LDHA, and CTNNB1 were found for WWOX transcript (R = 0.30, R = 0.32, and R = 0.30, respectively; all p < 0.05)"
- Anchor: Results > Gene expression correlations (NGT paragraph); Fig 5
