RS-R6: is there a perturbation that suppresses a Wwox-null or hypomorph phenotype in fly, zebrafish, mouse or organoid? The first route was HE's key-less flat-file lookups (queued) and the repository's earlier searches (IT12: none found). This is a different route.
**Route A, a literature query (Europe PMC REST, title/abstract):** (Wwox OR WWOX OR Fra16D) AND (suppressor OR suppresses OR modifier OR genetic screen OR rescues OR rescued OR epistasis) AND (mutant OR knockout OR null OR knockdown OR Drosophila OR zebrafish): 75 hits, 60 screened by title. Items that bear on a rescue or modifier of a Wwox-DEFICIENT phenotype: 42909904 (Lucas-Clarke 2026: Ldh RNAi partial rescue of locomotion in Wwox-knockdown x Abeta42 flies; already consumed), 34747138 and PPR1220099/42422765 (neuronal WWOX gene addition rescues null phenotypes: a rescue by the gene itself, not a suppressor), 32000863 (Hsu 2020: Wwox deficiency with GSK3beta-mediated neuropathy; the held lithium arm; already the origin of units 2 and 3), 22193544 (WWOX promotes neuronal differentiation by negative regulation of GSK3beta: a cell-culture mechanism), 21075834 (Drosophila Wwox orthologue in aerobic metabolism and reactive oxygen species; the paper that superseded the radiation-sensitivity phenotype of 16007179). All others are cancer, signalling or inflammation papers that use WWOX as a suppressor of tumour growth (the word 'suppressor' in 'tumour suppressor') and are not genetic suppressors of a Wwox-null phenotype. **No new suppressor, rescue or modifier of a Wwox-null or hypomorph phenotype beyond the ones already held.**
**Route B, a screens/interactions aggregator (Monarch Initiative API v3, key-less; aggregates FlyBase, MGI, ZFIN, BioGRID, STRING, HPO):** WWOX (HGNC:12799): 522 pairwise interaction records, all physical or predicted (BioGRID, STRING; examples BRCA1, TP53, MAPK8/9, TFAP2C), 125 phenotype annotations (HPO); orthologues: mouse Wwox (MGI:1931237), zebrafish wwox (ZFIN:ZDB-GENE-040426-858), fly Wwox (FB:FBgn0031972). Fly Wwox: 1 interaction record (TfAP-2, STRING, predicted) and 3 phenotype records: viable, fertile (consistent with the need for a sensitised background); mouse Wwox: 7 interaction records (BioGRID/STRING), 116 phenotype records (premature death, skeletal, body size), no genotype-to-gene association; zebrafish wwox: 2 interaction records (STRING), 4 phenotype records (eye size, pericardial oedema, body length, calcium homeostasis). **No genetic suppressor or enhancer interaction (the association types that would carry one) is recorded for Wwox in fly, mouse or zebrafish in this aggregate.**
**Meaning and limits.** Two routes, both bounded: absence of a recorded Wwox suppressor is not shown beyond the sources (Europe PMC title/abstract; Monarch's aggregate, which depends on curation of FlyBase/MGI/ZFIN and is silent on unpublished or full-text-only screens); the aggregator's BioGRID interactions overlap the flat files HE will use, so route B is not independent of them for physical interactions, only for the genetic-interaction question. The RS-R6 claim therefore stands as: 'no suppressor of a Wwox-null phenotype was found beyond the partial invertebrate Ldh rescue, the held lithium/GSK3 arm and gene addition'; HE's flat-file lookups remain optional. Consequence for the I21 design (parts 2 and 3 due 05:00Z): a suppressor screen would have to be designed on a sensitised background (fly Wwox nulls are viable and fertile; the Abeta42 background gives a measurable phenotype), because no existing genetic-interaction dataset supplies candidates.
