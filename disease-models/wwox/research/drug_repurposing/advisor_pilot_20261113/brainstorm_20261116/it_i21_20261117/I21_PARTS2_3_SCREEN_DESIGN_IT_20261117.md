## Part 2: the design
**Question.** Which gene perturbations restore survival of Wwox-null cells under a stress that the null cells fail and wild-type cells pass, more than they help wild-type cells?
**Sensitised background (why).** The fly has no phenotype to select on without a second hit (HE; Wwox fly viable and fertile, part 1); the one in-vivo precedent is a Wwox-knockdown x Abeta42 background in which Ldh RNAi gave a partial locomotion rescue (42909904, already consumed). The cell background that carries a quantifiable Wwox-null phenotype in public work is serum-starvation survival of Wwox-/- MEFs (Su 2026, PMID 41677633; HE's minutes 02:35Z), with a glycolytic shift (DL-MECH-020) as a second readout; ISR markers are weaker and are not used for selection.
**Arms.** Wwox-/- MEFs and matched wild-type MEFs, each with a genome-wide knockout (or CRISPRi) library, 3 independent infections per genotype, nutrient-complete and serum-starved arms. Readout 1 (selection): guide enrichment in starved survivors versus nutrient-complete at matched time. Readout 2 (confirmation of each hit, not selection): lactate output or extracellular acidification, in the null cells with and without the hit.
**A hit is** a gene whose loss enriches in starved Wwox-/- cells with a genotype x condition interaction (enrichment in null above enrichment in wild type), rejected if it enriches equally in wild type (a generic survival gene: apoptosis, autophagy or mTOR-class), rescued by re-expression of Wwox in the null arm as the positive control, and checked against non-targeting guides as the negative.
**Pre-declared falsifiers (fixed before any run).** (a) Positive control fails: Wwox re-expression does not restore survival in the null arm -> the screen is not interpretable, stop. (b) Every top hit also enriches in wild type -> no Wwox-specific suppressor in this assay: a result, parked with the trigger 'a second null phenotype with a genotype contrast'. (c) Hits recur only in one of three replicates -> not a hit.
**Registered predictions (IPOTESI, probabilities mine, not evidence).** P(at least one of Ldha, Ldhb, or a glycolysis-entry gene is among the interaction hits) = 0.25: it follows from the glycolytic shift plus the fly Ldh partial rescue, which is one origin chain, not two; P(top-10 interaction hits are all generic death-pathway genes) = 0.35; P(no gene reaches interaction significance) = 0.30. Rival: the shift is a consequence of starvation stress in any genotype, so Ldh-type hits would be generic; the wild-type arm decides.
**Cost (paper units).** Wet-lab only: 2 genotypes x 2 conditions x 3 replicates x one library; analysis a table lookup per hit. No laboratory is held by the project, so this path is blocked as an experiment and stays a design; that blocks only this path (operator item: laboratory).

## Part 3: crossing from a screen hit to a lever table
SCI's four steps (`deepening_sci_20261116/BRIDGE_FOR_SCREEN_HITS_SCI.md`) are adopted unchanged: (1) hit to class label; (2) target present in the bursting cells (>= 30 nuclei expressing in the held single-nucleus class table); (3) which held circuit readout it can move and with which sign (arm table, weights not concentrations); (4) the paired slice experiment with S-KO, S-HT and wild type. What I add:
* **Step 2 reference from [4d].** In the PV-Scn1a neocortex table, Wwox itself does not move (+0.18 [-0.07, +0.50], excitatory), and the genes of the lever arms (Gabrd -0.66, Kcnk2 -0.37, Slc12a2 -0.02) move with an epilepsy state, so a hit whose target moves in that table is not by itself a Wwox-specific neuronal target.
* **A mapping stop for the screen cell type.** MEFs are not neurons: a hit with no expression in held neuronal classes is parked as 'target-present trigger', not refuted; this is the first failure SCI expects and I expect it too.
* **Origin rule.** A hit that agrees with the glycolytic shift and the fly Ldh rescue is counted once in the arm table (one origin chain), not three times.
