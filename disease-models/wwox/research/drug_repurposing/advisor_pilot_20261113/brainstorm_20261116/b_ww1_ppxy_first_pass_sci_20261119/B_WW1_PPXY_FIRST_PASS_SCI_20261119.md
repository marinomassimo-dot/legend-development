## 4. Hypothesis chain and the discriminating experiment (conditional; nothing is claimed)
* **Observation:** WW1 binds ERBB4 PPxY peptides directly but weakly; in HeLa and MCF-7 cells WWOX retains the ERBB4 intracellular domain and suppresses its YAP-dependent transcriptional function (abstract); P47T mouse cerebellum loses PPxY-peptide binding with the protein intact; Wwox-null hippocampus has fewer PV-positive cells at two weeks (one laboratory, no maturation control; ADV-068 shows the transcript fall is PV-weighted, not PV-specific).
* **Relation (proposed):** WWOX WW1 holds the ERBB4 intracellular domain, competing with YAP. **Hypothesised action:** loss of that hold releases more intracellular domain to the nucleus. **Context:** mouse cortical or hippocampal neurons and interneurons after PKC-driven cleavage; whether this pathway operates in them is unknown.
* **Pharmacological comparison:** none justified now. **Prediction (sign fixed first):** after PKC-driven cleavage, Wwox-null and P47T neurons show **more** nuclear ERBB4 intracellular domain than wild type; wild-type WWOX re-expression reverses it and a WW1-dead mutant does not; the peptide pull-down with ERBB4 PY motifs retrieves WWOX from wild-type but not P47T lysate, with Dvl2 as positive control and a mutated-motif peptide as negative control. **Genetic control that separates ERBB4 from the other WW1 partners:** reduce Erbb4 (or block intracellular-domain release) in P47T neurons and test whether the localisation readout normalises; without it a P47T effect is a WW1-in-general effect.
* **Rejecting results:** no ERBB4 co-precipitation difference; no localisation difference in P47T or null neurons; a localisation difference that does not respond to the Erbb4 manipulation (then WW1 acts through other partners).
* **Alternative explanations to keep:** other WW1 partners (the P47T allele removes them all); stage and composition of the tissue (ADV-068 and ADV-070 limits); low affinity meaning that binding in cells needs avidity (dual-motif) or scaffolds; line and age differences; in neurons the ERBB4 ICD pathway may be inactive.
* **Separations asked by the operator:** WWOX specificity: not shown (no partner is WWOX-specific; the WW1 platform is shared by many proteins); generic anticonvulsant activity: not applicable (no compound); target function: the ERBB4 intracellular domain acts with YAP and other nuclear partners (background; one adjacent reading to verify below); alternative actions of any candidate compound: unassessable (no candidate); symptomatic against developmental: unassessable; repurposing drug against probe: probe-class tools only.
