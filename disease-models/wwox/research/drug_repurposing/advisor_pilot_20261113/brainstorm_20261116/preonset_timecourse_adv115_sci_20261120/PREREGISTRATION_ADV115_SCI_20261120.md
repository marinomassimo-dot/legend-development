## Usability criteria (ADV's, made exact; all must hold for a dataset to be USABLE)
U1 a developmental or genetic epilepsy or hypomyelination mouse model, brain tissue; U2 **at least three ages that bracket the documented seizure onset** (one before, one at or just after, one later), onset taken from the dataset's own paper or a cited primary, not from my choice; U3 **wild-type or matched controls at every age**; U4 **at least three biological replicates (animals) per genotype-by-age cell**, pooled libraries and technical replicates counted as one unit and named; U5 a processed matrix or raw counts retrievable by plain client from GEO or ArrayExpress without login. Missing U1 to U5 makes the dataset NOT USABLE with the failing criterion named. A dataset with sex, region or batch confounded with age is USABLE WITH A CAVEAT only if the confound is stated in its metadata.

## Analysis (only if at least one dataset is USABLE; fixed now)
* Unit = animal. Per dataset, per set, per age: the **set score of a sample = mean over the set's genes present of the gene's z-score** computed across all samples of the dataset on log2 normalised expression; the **difference = mean score of mutants minus mean score of controls at that age**; sd units. A **departure** at an age requires |difference| at least 0.5 standard units, the same sign at the later ages, and **leave-one-gene-out keeps the sign and at least 0.35 in magnitude** at the departure age; boundary verdicts (0.35 to 0.65 as measured) are reported as boundary, never as a result. Age-matched controls only; no cross-age contrasts. The **first age with a departure** is compared with the documented onset in sampled ages, never interpolated between samples.
* **Composition caveat (bulk):** a departure in MICRO_ID or ASTRO identity genes may be a change in cell proportion rather than state; MICRO_ID and the Aqp4, Aldh1l1, Slc1a2 astrocyte identity genes are read beside ASTRO_REACTIVE and MICRO_ACT as the composition control, and a shared move of identity and reactivity genes is labelled "composition or state, not separable".
* **Seizure confound:** any departure at or after the first documented seizure is NOT "before"; the competing explanation (seizures drive the signal where onset is early) is stated beside every result; **the observation that weakens the transfer prior** is no departure before the first documented seizure, or sampled ages too coarse (more than 4 days between the last pre-onset age and onset) to say.
