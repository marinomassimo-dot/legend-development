# ADV-115: public time-course data for glial and energy transcripts before the first seizures: UNANSWERABLE under the pre-registered criteria (SCI, 2026-11-20; RP-012; hypothesis level; a transfer prior question, not Wwox evidence; no dose; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

Pre-registration: `PREREGISTRATION_ADV115_SCI_20261120.md` (committed f61687edb before any search; gene sets, criteria U1 to U5, 10 searches, at most 20 candidate series, no widening after values). **No expression value was opened for any series: every verdict below rests on GEO metadata (series title, summary, design, sample names) and, for two series, the brief SOFT record.** Plain client, generic client name, one request per URL.

## Answer first
1. **No public series found meets U1 to U5: the question "do astrocyte, microglial and energy transcripts change before the first seizures, in a developmental epilepsy mouse model with a time course spanning onset" is UNANSWERABLE from the 19 series inspected.** Ten fixed GEO DataSets searches (S1 to S10) returned 11, 1, 4, 6, 6, 230, 39, 5, 5 and 15 records; 19 series were inspected at metadata level (cap 20); none has three or more ages bracketing a documented onset with wild-type controls at each age and at least three animals per cell. I did not search ArrayExpress (the pre-registered condition for it, "GEO returned nothing for S1 to S5", was not met: S1, S3, S4 and S5 returned records).
2. **One near-miss, reported but not computed: GSE112627** (hippocampus, F1 and 129 strains, wild type and Scn1a+/-, **P14 and P24 only**, 2 to 3 replicates; the summary calls the F1 phenotype "juvenile-onset spontaneous seizures"; PMID 30347190 and two later papers). It has **one** pre-onset age with **3 knockout and 3 wild-type animals in the F1 strain** (sample names read: P14_F1_KO 15, 16, 17 and P14_F1_WT 18, 19, 20) and a post-onset age, so it fails U2 (two ages, not three) and cannot give a "first age of departure". It could answer a **narrower** question (is any set already different at the one pre-onset age) if the documented onset age is verified from its paper; **that is a separate pre-registration and needs ADV's decision, because widening after seeing the series is the thing the pre-registration forbids.**
3. **Consequence for the satellite draws P5/P7/P9/P12 (6.18, 6.19): none from public data.** No transfer prior exists, so the ages and markers stay as set by ADV-104 and ADV-105; the draw that carries the cause-or-consequence question is the **pre-onset pair P5 and P7** (the S-KO onset is P9, the null earlier), since a departure at P9 or P12 cannot be told from the effect of seizures; **P12 is a post-onset reading**. The panel stays the ADV-095 E1 reactive-astrocyte set, a microglial identity and activation pair, glycolysis (M5) with lactate and beta-hydroxybutyrate (6.19). This is the design that was already in place; the public data neither move nor support it.
4. **The finding itself is useful:** public mouse epilepsy transcriptomes are dominated by single-age genotype contrasts, acquired-epilepsy models and cell-culture designs; a pre-onset glial or energy time course in a genetic developmental epilepsy model is **not deposited in GEO as far as these ten searches reach**. That is a statement about these searches, not about the archive.

## Limits
Metadata only: sample names and series summaries can hide an age column; for GSE171191, GSE169481 and GSE169485 the ages are **not stated in the metadata I read**, and an age series there would have to appear in the paper, not in the series record. Ten GEO searches and 19 series are not the archive; ArrayExpress and BioStudies were not searched (the pre-registered condition did not arise); single-cell and single-nucleus datasets were not excluded by type but none had a time course. A series that exists only in a paper's supplement is out of reach by this method. The depositor contact in the SOFT records is not used. A transfer prior would in any case be a different strain, gene and region from Wwox, and bulk composition and seizure-driven signal would remain open.
