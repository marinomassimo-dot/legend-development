# ADV-286 C1: questions frozen before AES 2026 poster 1.019 (presentation 2026-12-05)

Hypothesis level; reference genotype class only; nothing here is medical advice.

## What the two public abstracts already say (not questions)

AES 2026 abstract 1.019 (public 2026-11-17) states, at class level: the model classes "cell type-specific deletion
and a disease-associated variant model"; loss of Wwox in inhibitory neurons sufficient to induce spontaneous seizures
and premature mortality; "impaired excitability and altered synaptic recruitment of cortical inhibitory neurons" in
vitro; in vivo EEG; and "Targeted pharmacological treatment improved neuronal firing ex vivo and reduced seizure
burden in vivo." SfN 2026 abstract NANO11.01 states "interneuron dysfunction contributes to the epileptic phenotype
associated with Wwox loss" and "Treatment partially restored firing properties in vitro and reduced seizure burden in
vivo." Neither names a Cre line, an inhibitory subtype, a drug, the treated model, an n or a per-animal value.

## Rules for all questions

Choose exactly one option, or ABSTAIN naming the missing datum. Options are in a fixed order that carries no
preference. "Shown" means presented on the poster itself. If the poster shows no in vivo treatment experiment, Q1,
Q2, Q5 and Q6 are UNRESOLVABLE.

**Q1. In which model is the treatment tested for seizure burden in vivo?** A "cell type-specific deletion model" is a
deletion restricted by a Cre driver to a named neuronal class; a global or pan-neuronal deletion is (d).
(a) both a cell type-specific deletion model and a disease-associated variant model
(b) cell type-specific deletion model(s) only
(c) disease-associated variant model(s) only
(d) another model, or one that is neither class

**Q2. What is the primary target of the treatment whose seizure effect is shown?** Classify by the target the poster
states. If several drugs, use the one whose seizure effect is shown; a stated dual action, or drugs from different
classes, is UNRESOLVABLE. Chloride-transporter drugs go to (b); HCN channels and ligand-gated channels go to (c).
(a) a voltage-gated ion channel (sodium, potassium or calcium)
(b) GABA signalling (receptor, transporter, metabolism) or a chloride transporter
(c) another target

**Q3. Which driver(s) delete Wwox in inhibitory neurons on the poster?** Drivers of the medial ganglionic eminence
lineage (for example Nkx2.1 or Lhx6) are subclass-restricted, not broad.
(a) only broad drivers (for example Gad2, Vgat/Slc32a1 or Dlx)
(b) at least one parvalbumin-restricted driver, with or without others
(c) subclass-restricted drivers, none parvalbumin-restricted
(d) other, or a deletion not made by Cre

**Q4. What does the poster show for a deletion restricted to excitatory neurons?** Classify Emx1- or Camk2a-based
drivers by the poster's own description.
(a) no deletion restricted to excitatory neurons is presented
(b) presented, with spontaneous seizures reported in vivo
(c) presented, with no spontaneous seizures reported in vivo
(d) presented, in vivo seizure status not reported or mixed

**Q5. Are individual-animal values shown for the effect of the treatment on seizure burden in vivo?**
(a) absent (group summaries only, or no quantification)
(b) present (a point or a row per animal, in every model tested)

**Q6. What links the recovered cellular function to the seizure effect?**
(a) nothing beyond the two parallel statements
(b) an association only (per animal or per group), with no intervention on the cell type
(c) an intervention on the cell type with the seizure effect tested to follow (a cell-type-specific rescue of the
treatment's action, silencing or occlusion)
