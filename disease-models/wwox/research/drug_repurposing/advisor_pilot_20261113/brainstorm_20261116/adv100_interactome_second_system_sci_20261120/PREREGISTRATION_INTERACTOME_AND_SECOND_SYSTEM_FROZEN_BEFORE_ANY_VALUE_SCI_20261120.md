## Part A. Interactome of held literature: which WWOX partner can carry a candidate
**Universe.** Every WWOX binding partner named in the held registries (`disease-models/wwox/registries/claim_registry_current.md` and `paper_registry_current.md`) by a search of their text for: interact, interacts, binds, binding, co-IP, co-immunoprecipitation, pull-down, partner, complex, associates, PPxY, WW1, WW2, SDR. Each partner is one row; a partner named only in a review or only as a hit of a mass-spectrometry list without a reciprocal assay is a row with criterion (i) not met. The registry is held literature, not all literature: the round says "exhausted for held literature" if it stops.
**Criteria, as ADV set them (all three needed for a partner to count).**
(i) **A direct interaction is documented with its assay and domain.** Counts as documented: purified or recombinant protein or peptide binding (ITC, NMR, SPR, pull-down or far-Western) with a motif or domain mutant that loses it, or a reciprocal co-immunoprecipitation of endogenous proteins with a domain or motif mutant that loses it. Does not count: a mass-spectrometry hit alone, FRET between over-expressed fusions alone, a co-localisation, a review statement. The row records the assay, the WWOX domain and residues, and the preparation.
(ii) **A functional readout exists in a neural WWOX-deficient or WWOX-manipulated system.** A measurement of a partner-related functional change after WWOX loss, knockdown, mutation or over-expression, in neurons, brain tissue, a neural cell line (for example SH-SY5Y, SK-N-SH, neuroblastoma) or a neural organoid. Co-immunoprecipitation alone is criterion (i), not (ii). The row records the tier: **N1** neurons or brain tissue of a Wwox-deficient animal or neurons from one; **N2** a neural cell line or organoid; **N3** non-neural cell system (does not count).
(iii) **The target has pharmacology** in one of HE's sheets (`adv098_agent_sheets_he_20261119/`) or in a verified public record (a primary paper with a measured constant read by this round or by the blind verifier, or a regulatory label).
**Stop rule.** If no partner meets (i) and (ii), the interactome is recorded as exhausted for held literature and the round stops (no candidate class is named). If one or more meet all three, each is a candidate row with its prediction and its rejecting observation, and the partner nearest to the genotype class (domain affected by the allele) is stated.
**Procedure.** Classification from the registry text and, where the registry names a source, the held full text in `files/fulltext/` (read first, per the read-but-not-carried pattern); a blind verifier (a fresh delegate, effort high) then re-reads only the sources of the rows that meet (i) and (ii), as (statement, source, locator) triples. Rows never read at full text are labelled by their depth. No row is promoted on a review's statement.

## Part B. Second independent human neural system among the 39 WWOX GEO series
**Question.** Is any of the 39 GEO series listed in the supply note (2026-11-20) a human neural or neuron-like WWOX-perturbed transcriptome other than the organoid libraries? **Reading rule fixed now:** a series counts if it is human, neural or neuron-like cells (neural stem cells, neurons, neuroblastoma or neural-lineage lines used as a model of neural cells), and WWOX is knocked down, knocked out or mutated. WWOX over-expression in tumour lines (the glioblastoma and bladder series) is a different manipulation and is reported as "not a loss-of-function system", not scored.
**If a series counts (the held series `GSE126075`: human neural stem cells, native against shRNA-silenced WWOX, before and after differentiation into neurons; CAGE; four samples; no replicate per condition; only the header and the first two gene rows of the held TPM table were seen before this registration),** it is system H and is analysed **descriptively** (n = 1 per condition: no inference at the level of a gene is possible):
- Effect h = log2(TPM + 1) of the silenced neurons minus that of the native neurons, per gene with TPM at least 1 in either; centred by the median h over analysed genes. The stem-cell pair is reported with the same quantity.
- A maturity flag: the NEU minus PROG panel of unit A (STMN2, DCX, TUBB3, MAP2, SNAP25, SYT1 minus SOX2, PAX6, HES1, VIM, NES, FABP7) in the native and in the silenced neurons; if the two differ by more than 0.5 the pair is flagged "maturity-confounded".
- Concordance with the two earlier systems (the ADV-099 centred effects, `adv099_neuron_discovery_sci_20261120/out/nuclei_effects_*.tsv`, excitatory and inhibitory classes, and the organoid centred effect): genome-wide Spearman of h with each, against a permutation null (2,000 shuffles of h within ten bins of expression); genes with |h| at least 0.5 forming UP and DOWN sets for the pair counts below.
- **Two-system replication counts** under the ADV-099 thresholds: genes passing N (excitatory or inhibitory, centred at least 0.25, stable) and H (|h| at least 0.5, same sign); genes passing O (centred at least 0.5, group agreement) and H; counted per class and direction against a within-decile permutation null (2,000); the verdict per cell is REPLICATES_BEYOND_CHANCE if the observed count exceeds the null 97.5th percentile, else NOTHING_REPLICATES_BEYOND_CHANCE. **Three-system counts** (N, O and H) are reported for reference with the same null.
- This system adds a second human system but **no biological replication**; a positive count is descriptive and is read as a lead to replicate, never as a finding on its own; a negative count is read as no second-system support, not as exclusion.
**Not done in this round:** no pharmacology mapping unless a cell is beyond chance; no new thresholds after the first value.
