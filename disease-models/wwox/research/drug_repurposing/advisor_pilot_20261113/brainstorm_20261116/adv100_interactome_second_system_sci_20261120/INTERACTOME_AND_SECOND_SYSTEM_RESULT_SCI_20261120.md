### A.2 What the blind check changes in the GSK3-beta row (all from `VERIFIER_REPORT_GSK3B_AS_DELIVERED_20261120.md`)
- **"Direct" is not shown.** The registry claim is titled "a direct, residue-mapped inhibitor"; the source has GST pull-down from lysates and co-immunoprecipitations, no purified-to-purified binding. Reading: **documented interaction with a residue-mapped motif; directness untested.**
- **"388 to 407 required" and "L404 strictly required"** rest on two truncations (no internal deletion, no 1 to 407 construct) and on L404A, which abolishes binding in pull-down and diminishes it in co-IP; the stretch 296 to 320 also carries the motif and was not shown dispensable (L311A did not abolish binding).
- **"The inhibition is S9-independent" is not supported as worded.** The only pS9 statement is that pS9 and phospho-beta-catenin stayed normal across retinoic-acid differentiation while endogenous WWOX rose (a correlation); no experiment manipulates WWOX and reads pS9. The registry's biomarker warning (a pS9 western would give a false negative) rests on that correlation. **Cheng 2020 reports lower pS9 in the Wwox-null brain** (a Ser9-dependent reading; ratio numbers without a statistical test, n not stated), so the two sources are in tension, not in agreement. This is a finding for the registry owners (the four scientific current files change only through `BATCH_COMMIT`); I do not edit the registry.
- **Neuronal readout is a cell line with retinoic acid, not neurons of a Wwox-deficient animal;** GSK3-beta activity itself was not measured (it is inferred from tau phosphorylation); the tau epistasis is partly confounded because tau knockdown also removes the baseline retinoic-acid response; microtubule assembly was cell-free with purified components.
- **(iii) is partial:** the lithium constant is an abstract-level Ki; no GSK-3-beta-specific constant for a non-lithium class was read in a primary source.

### A.3 Candidate row (registered stop rule not triggered)
**Candidate class: GSK-3 inhibition by a chemistry unrelated to lithium** (not a dose, not an agent recommendation). **Prediction:** in Wwox-deficient neurons the GSK3-beta substrate phosphorylation (tau at S396 and S404) is higher than in controls and neurite or maturation readouts are lower; a GSK-3 inhibitor at confirmed brain exposure moves both. **Rejecting observation:** no higher substrate phosphorylation (S396, S404, and active-site Tyr216 rather than only Ser9) in neurons or brain of a Wwox-deficient animal (neuronal deletion at P17 or the null), or no change in those readouts and in neurite or lineage maturation at confirmed exposure of a non-lithium inhibitor. **Nearest domain to the genotype class:** the docking motifs lie in the SDR domain at 296 to 320 and 388 to 412; the reference missense allele Q230P is in the SDR domain at residue 230, outside both stretches; whether Q230P alters GSK3-beta binding is untested (the registry already marks 388 to 407 as a region not to touch for design).
**Relation to earlier units.** The transcript-level GSK-3 and Wnt premise of unit A (sign divides, not supported) answers a different question from a protein-level partner premise; the lithium effect in Cheng 2020 was general (all genotypes), so lithium alone cannot separate a GSK-3 route from a general property (pup brief stage 3 adds a second GSK-3 class and a general comparator for that reason).

### A.4 The cheap next step that serves both candidates
The v2 broad experiment (`UNIT_C_PRIORITY_EXPERIMENTS_V2_REVISION_...`) already collects cortex and hippocampus of neuronal-deletion and control pups at P17. **Add a GSK-3 premise panel to the same tissue:** tau phosphorylation at S396 and S404 with total tau, GSK-3 Tyr216 and Ser9 phosphorylation with total GSK-3, beta-catenin, each per animal with a stated n (the n of v2, 12 per knockout arm, is enough for a large effect). Decision it names: **no elevation of the substrate panel in the knockout tissue closes the GSK-3 candidate for this model; elevation, with a pS9 pattern read as such, opens a GSK-3-inhibitor arm in the same design.** This changes no arm of v2 and adds no animal.
