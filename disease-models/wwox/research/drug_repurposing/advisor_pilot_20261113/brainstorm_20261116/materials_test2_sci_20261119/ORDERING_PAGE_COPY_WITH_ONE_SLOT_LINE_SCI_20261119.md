## The loop's judgement for a laboratory with one slot (added 2026-11-19 from the second materials comparison; ACCEPTED by ADV on 2026-11-19 as the loop's judgement at hypothesis level: from one comparison with unequal originals; not validated; the reasoning, not the comparison, carries it; no laboratory is named; no dose; not medical advice)
**If a laboratory can take only ONE of the optional measurements (rows 6 to 11), take row 7, glucose tied to seizure timing, first, and row 6, PV count and signal per cell with a late-maturing and a myelin marker, next.** The attempt that produced this order ranked the other four EEG or ECoG before P14, callosal conduction, structural myelin, heterozygote carrier latency; the blind verifier judged only the first choice and the comparison, not that tail, so the tail is not part of this line.

**Why row 7 first.** Checked in the held texts in this window: the neuron-targeted deletion paper (Repudi et al., Brain 2021) reports seizures beginning at P9 and its text (the supplement was not searched) contains no glucose, insulin, lactate or ketone measurement (its glucose mentions are recording-solution compositions); the held AAV9 gene-therapy paper (Repudi et al., EMBO Mol Med 2021) reports that neuronal re-expression restored blood glucose together with seizures, growth and survival, and that null mice were hypoglycaemic from the second week. That pattern shows a central origin of the hypoglycaemia; it cannot say whether low glucose drives the seizures or only accompanies them, because no held original manipulates glucose: **glucose is here a readout that discriminates timing, not a lever.** Per the ADV-074 note and not re-checked here: no glucose is reported before P10, there is no same-animal glucose and seizure pair in any held original, and the glucose-by-age values in the held figures are read from plots (approximate). A same-animal glucose and seizure-timing measurement is the one of the six that separates "metabolic driver" from "co-rescued accompaniment", and it changes how the other readouts are read, although it changes no arm, size or tier.
**Next: row 6.** Per the ADV-068 note and not re-checked here: the P17 bulk deposits (3 knockout against 4 control animals) lean to developmental delay or an illness state with a PV component, one gene decides that verdict, and they cannot give the cell-level answer; the tissue work needs no new animals when slice-animal tissue exists.
**What it needs.** Seizure-and-death lines (neuron-targeted or neural-progenitor-targeted), pups P7 to P14, blood glucose at seizure time, ketone, lactate and milk intake by age; the blood-sampling burden in small pups is for the laboratory to state. Row 11 (EEG or ECoG before P14) is the prerequisite for same-animal pairs and gains most when paired with row 7.
**What would change it.** No seizure-and-death line, or no way to sample pups at P7 to P14: row 6 first. A laboratory whose main experiments centre on a myelin-promoting arm: rows 9 and 8 move up.
**Provenance and limits (one case, no general claim).** Two delegates (sonnet, medium) answered the same question under a hashed pre-registration (`generation_it59_20261119/PREREG_SECOND_COMPARISON_IT59.md`): one lean with held originals only, one with the loop's notes added. They chose different first measurements (the lean one: callosal conduction with a rescue arm; the notes one: glucose). The blind verifier judged the notes arm better by informativeness, defensibility, separation of alternatives and a retrieved question, and found a borderline harm in the lean one (a rescue arm credited with separating a myelin deficit from a seizure effect, which a neuronal rescue cannot do, because it restores seizures, glucose and myelination together). **Limit:** the list of originals given to both delegates omitted held papers that carry the decisive glucose facts (the AAV9 gene-therapy paper among them), which the notes arm received through the ADV-074 note; so the result does not show that the notes beat a lean arm given the same complete originals. Full record: `MATERIALS_TEST2_RESULT_SCI_20261119.md`.
