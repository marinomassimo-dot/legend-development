## 4. The positive-control field: decision and why

No field. (1) In ADV-281 two readers gave different classes to 4 of 13 nulls, and ADV-282 repeated it (loose class 0 of 14 by the first reader, 5 of 14 by the blind reader): the disagreement is about the matching rule, so a flag would not be stable. (2) A field keyed to the row's `direction` would miss the nulls that sit in text only. (3) No script or ranking reads such a field today. (4) The instrument that works is the README rule (a null names the check matched to its own compound, dose, exposure and system, or says that none was found, with the search date), because the real errors in 27 audited sentences (MUST CHANGE 8 of 27) were in the compressed description of the source, not in a missing check. SCI's alternative is better than a boolean: **where the target-loss or engagement check sits** (same preparation and time / same paper at another time / another paper / none). If a comparison or ranking ever has to filter nulls by this property, adopt those four values as the field's definition; this is written in the cards README.
