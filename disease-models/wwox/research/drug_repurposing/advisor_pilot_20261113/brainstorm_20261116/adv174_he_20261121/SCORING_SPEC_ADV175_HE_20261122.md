# Scoring spec, ADV-175 diagnostic (HE as scorer, frozen 2026-11-22 before any score file exists). SEALED until IT's freeze; its sha256 is committed first.

Inputs: `predictor_input.tsv` (digest 7d63de87...f66f127), `outcome_key.tsv` (digest accaaceb...9e8214), IT's committed score file (engine score and declared-baseline score per id, higher = more likely to normalise), the local S7 workbook (for library-level viability percentiles only). Script: `score_adv175.py` (digest recorded with this spec).

1. TWO DISTINCT LABEL RULES (stated separately, ADV's request)
   (a) The paper's rule: Parameter Recovery (PR) at or above 0.6 ("more than 60 percent of features recovered").
   (b) My viability screen: signed viability in S7 above the library's 10th percentile (sign convention and unit are undefined in the sheet; this is NOT the authors' screen).
   PRIMARY positive = (a) AND (b); PRIMARY null = PR at or below 0.35 AND (b). The middle band and the toxic stratum are not in the file. Because the file was built after applying (b), the paper's rule alone (a without b) cannot be scored except on the recognition rows; this is a limit, stated.
2. ELIGIBLE ROWS for the primary: all rows of the file outside the recognition stratum, with their own row label positive or null: 15 positive rows (15 alias groups) and 154 null rows (150 alias groups). One row whose own label is null but whose alias group is positive-dominated is dropped from the primary (counted and reported). Recognition stratum (5 rows, 4 alias groups: the four leads named in the paper and their alias entries): reported apart, never in the primary or sensitivities.
3. PRIMARY ENDPOINT: Delta = AUC(engine) minus AUC(declared baseline), same eligible rows, ties counted as one half. INTERVAL: paired, stratified cluster bootstrap: resample alias groups with replacement WITHIN each class (positive groups, null groups), recompute both AUCs on each resample, 10,000 draws, seed 175, percentile 95 percent interval. Counts of rows, groups and positives reported. Honest-interval note chosen after inspecting the inputs: with 15 positive groups the percentile interval is approximate and probably somewhat narrow; the frozen leave-one-positive-group-out range of Delta is reported beside it, and the reading rule below is applied to the percentile interval as frozen. Also reported, descriptive only: AUC(engine) and AUC(baseline) each against 0.5, and the share of draws with Delta above 0.15.
4. READING RULE (ADV's, binding): interval excludes zero on the positive side = "incremental lift detected on this diagnostic"; interval spans zero and includes +0.15 = "incremental lift unresolved in this diagnostic"; upper end below +0.15 = "that gap was excluded on this diagnostic" (0.15 is a crude planning value, not a bound); lower end above +0.15 is not expected and would be reported as it is.
5. SENSITIVITIES (each recomputes Delta and its interval; none changes the primary):
   S-V: viability screen at the 20th instead of the 10th percentile (drops eligible rows between them).
   S-N: nulls restricted to PR at or below 0.30.
   S-L: without the named leads' classes: rows whose vendor target or vendor pathway text matches HCN, muscarinic or AChR, GSK-3 or GSK3, Notch or secretase (regular expression frozen in the script).
   S-R (rank check, descriptive): Spearman between engine score and the published PR over all non-recognition rows of the file (a truncated range by design), and the same for the baseline.
   The paper's 60 percent rule is fixed by the sampling and cannot be varied downward; positives at PR 0.65 or above are 3 rows only and are not analysed separately.
6. RECOGNITION STRATUM: for each of engine and baseline, the percentile rank of its rows among all rows of the file (median and range, aggregate; no row labels), and how many of its rows lie in the engine's and the baseline's top decile of the file. No AUC (too few rows).
7. VALIDITY: IT's file must score every id of the file; a missing id takes the lowest score and is counted; more than 5 percent missing makes the scoring INVALID. Score file hash and the time of IT's commit must precede the first reveal. Nothing is reported per compound.
8. SCOPE DISCLOSURE (after IT's freeze, the rule not per-compound outcomes): the file was sampled from the two extreme tails of the published recovery score (all qualifying high-tail alias groups, a random draw of low-tail groups, middle and toxic rows excluded, aliases whole). The estimand applies to that defined subset ONLY: no library-wide precision, calibration, prevalence or predictivity; one CDD line, one dose, technical replicates (one cluster); no WWOX inference.
