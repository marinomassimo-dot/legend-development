# ADV-174: can the Negraes 2021 CDKL5 spheroid screen carry a masked retrospective test of the generation engine? (HE as outcome custodian, 2026-11-22)

Written for: ADV. Hypothesis level, in-silico evaluation planning; reference genotype class only; **no WWOX evidence, no efficacy claim, no dose, no ranking change, no contact**. Source: Negraes et al., *Molecular Psychiatry* 2021, PMID 33888873 (open access, CC BY 4.0). Read here: the complete main text (text extraction, two columns re-read in flow order), Fig. S5 (one page, rendered and viewed), and the supplementary table S7 workbook inspected locally. The three originals stay in `/tmp` (SHA-256 re-checked: match ADV's) and out of git. **No per-compound outcome value appears in this file or in any message; only shapes, counts and categories.** The key, the sampling rule and the predictor file are sealed outside git (section 6).

## 0. Verdict: NARROW (a low-powered diagnostic on a noisy label, not a calibration)
The screen is a good *shape* for a masked test (the whole 1,112-compound library was run and reported, misses included, nothing preselected on outcome). The *label* is weak: one CDD line, one dose, aggregates of 3 technical replicates, no treated healthy control, coarse ranks, and a measured reproducibility across exact-structure duplicates of about 0.2 for the recovery score. Supported claim for a test: **can the engine rank compounds by "normalisation of calcium-oscillation features in one CDD1 spheroid line at 1 µM, viability-screened"**, never "rescue" in the sense of a genotype-specific effect and never anything about WWOX. If ADV wants more than a diagnostic, the study cannot supply it.

## 2. Reproducibility of the label (the part that limits any test)
Across the 13 exact-structure duplicate pairs (different library entries, so independent wells and plates): Spearman about **0.54 for SP, 0.21 for PR, 0.40 for viability** (n = 13; wide intervals, a rough guide only); **no pair has both members in the upper quartile of PR, and four pairs disagree about it**. A PR label therefore carries a large technical noise share. This is a **reproducibility warning, not a mathematical ceiling on predictive performance** (corrected 2026-11-22 after Codex's check; 13 pairs, different salt forms and plates), and a cheap internal replicate the paper never uses.

## 3. Does the preselection or the paper leak the target?
- **Library preselection: no outcome-derived filter** (all 1,112 were screened; "neurologic research and associated assays, two thirds approved drugs"; the sheet header gives a vendor plate layout). Genuinely unknown rows exist for a predictor that has not read the paper.
- **The paper leaks the top.** It names four leads, with their rank in the SP and PR lists, and the pathway classes of the top compounds (Fig. S5h and i; Notch, GSK3, protein-tyrosine-kinase, PI3K/Akt/mTOR). **All four are among the 25 compounds at or above 60 percent recovery and in the lowest-50 SP list**, so a model or a reader who has seen the paper recognises exactly the positives. Exact aliases exist (one lead has two entries). Treat the four plus their alias entries as a **recognition stratum, scored apart**, and report a sensitivity analysis without their drug classes (HCN channel, muscarinic receptor, GSK3, Notch): these classes are named in the open text.
- **A threshold trap:** one of the four named leads falls in the lowest decile of the sheet's signed viability although the paper says the four had "no effect on cell viability"; the sign convention and the unit are undefined, so **any viability cut is my choice, not the authors' screen** (the lowest-5-percent cut removes none of the 25; the lowest-10-percent cut removes 7).
- Vendor text in the sheet (the "Information" column) mentions epilepsy, seizure or anticonvulsant use for about 8 to 20 compounds; it is withheld from the predictor file.

## 4. What the evaluated label must be called
**"Normalisation of calcium-oscillation features toward the control line at 1 µM, in one CDD patient-derived line, as an aggregate of technical replicates, with a viability screen of undefined unit."** Not "rescue" (no treated control, so broad suppression of activity cannot be told from a genotype-specific effect: a compound that silences the network lowers peak counts toward control by any route), not "efficacy", not a patient-level or WWOX calibration; one CDD line in technical replicates is one cluster.

## 5. Power and what a result could mean (for the later frozen evaluation, not mine)
Positives at the authors' rule: 25 of 1,112 (2.2 percent); 18 after my viability screen, 15 once the named leads are set apart. An engine cannot score the whole library cheaply, so the test needs a subset; with 15 positives the standard error of an AUC is about 0.07 even with many nulls, so only an AUC near 0.7 or above against chance is detectable and **an engine-minus-class-baseline difference smaller than about 0.15 cannot be seen**. The label noise (section 2) is a further warning about how much any gap can mean. The honest expected result is a tie. A simple declared baseline with the same inputs (a pharmacology-class rule such as ion-channel or kinase-inhibitor membership, taken from the vendor target and pathway columns the predictor also gets) and a fixed null (random order) should be frozen before any outcome is opened; the recognised-pair stratum should not enter the headline.

## 7. Limits and what I did not do
Single sheet read by script (the heading rows, the column names and the shapes above were all inspected; per-row values were not tabulated or kept). Duplicate-pair concordance rests on 13 pairs. The text extraction of the figure panels is by eye at low resolution (Fig. S5 viewed once). I did not search for other data of this screen (a public repository, a later paper by the same group); the Data availability statement points to the authors only. I did not test any engine and did not open any WWOX material.
- **DREAM (kept only with a consumer):** a published screen that reports misses is a better test of a generator than exemplars, but only if the readout has a nameable null; here the null is the low tail of a noisy 48-level score. Transferable method lesson, for the existing prediction-ledger wording only: **count the distinct values and the share at the modal value of an aggregate before calling any class "inactive" or "rescued"**.
