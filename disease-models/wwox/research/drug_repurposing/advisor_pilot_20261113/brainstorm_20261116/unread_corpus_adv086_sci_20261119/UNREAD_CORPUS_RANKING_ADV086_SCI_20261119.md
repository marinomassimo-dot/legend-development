## 0. Answer first
1. **The stop rule applies; no text was read.** Of the unread WWOX corpus texts that a plain client can obtain now (PMC, or already held without a receipt) none could change a decision of the module. The texts that could (the clinical series and cohorts) are either closed or are open copies that sit on hosts which refuse a plain client. Delivered: the ranking, the access list, and one request for a person with a browser (section 4).
2. **Census** (`list_unread_corpus.py`, output `corpus_unread_census.tsv`): 706 corpus PMIDs; 254 have a receipt (complete, some or staged), **452 have none** (the source-base note said 453; the join here adds the staged receipts); 3 of the 452 are held without a receipt; 239 carry the PubMed free-full-text flag; 71 pass a deliberately broad neural or mechanism title screen without a cancer word; a further 75 non-cancer titles with no neural word were read by title as well. A title is not a reading, and the screen is a census, not an exhaustion check.
3. **Two routes disagree, and both are reported.** Europe PMC (one search call over 28 candidates) says only one candidate is in PMC (a 2015 review, PMC4935225). OpenAlex (one call, 27 returned) flags eight as open (gold, bronze, green or hybrid): the 2026 burst-suppression EEG cohort (repository copy), the 2025 prospective infant-epilepsy cohort (preprint copy), the 2020 compound-heterozygous case report (publisher PDF), the 2021 Argentine DEE cohort (national repository), the 2018 diagnostic-yield paper, the 2025 Parkinson cognition paper, the 2026 sulfate-pathway paper and a 2012 review. **Plain GETs of the open copies I tried were refused:** the 2020 case report (Wiley, "Just a moment..." challenge page, 403), the 2025 cohort (SSRN, 403), the 2026 EEG cohort (HAL, a challenge or JavaScript page, 403), the 2026 sulfate paper (Taylor and Francis, 403); the two Elsevier papers returned only a redirect stub to ScienceDirect (not followed); the Argentine repository returned an application shell (not pursued: low value). **A challenge is a stop for that route; none was circumvented.** The route is blocked, not the text closed.

## 2. Why the obtainable texts cannot change a decision
Every mechanism an obtainable text offers is one the module already uses by file count (row 8), or belongs to the closed PPxY branch (row 9); the "read but not carried" case (WW1 and PPxY biochemistry) is already audited. The decisions that a new text could move (onset age and assay age, callosal correlate, antiseizure response, genotype class) belong to the clinical rows, and those texts are not obtainable by a plain client now.
