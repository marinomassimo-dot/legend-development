## Rival explanations
R1 canonical Wnt de-repression is the delay (the A51 reading); R2 loss of a non-canonical Wnt-5a/JNK differentiation cue is the delay and canonical TCF4 rise is secondary; R3 both are consequences of an upstream MYC or proliferation state (the held MYC rescue fits R3 and R1, does not exclude R2); R4 the O2 changes are stress and apoptosis markers of an overexpression line, not a pathway; R5 the cancer-stem-cell phenotype of O1 to O3 has no counterpart in a diploid progenitor.

## One non-obvious prediction and its rejecting observation
Prediction (IPOTESI, to be fixed by the Scientist before any held table is opened): in WWOX-knockout cortical organoids WNT5A-pathway activity is lower than in rescued ones while TCF4 is higher, and a canonical-Wnt inhibitor (tankyrase class, the XAV939 arm already named) does **not** restore neuronal production to the level of the MYC arm, whereas raising Wnt-5a/ROR2 signalling does. The usual default predicts the opposite order. This also predicts that JNK inhibition, the RP-004 and Chang lever, would not help differentiation in this system: an aggravation entry to be counted apart, never netted with the tau or apoptosis arms.
Rejecting observation: WNT5A, ROR2 or non-canonical targets unchanged or higher in knockout organoids, or the tankyrase arm restoring neuronal production to the MYC arm with WNT5A unchanged; either result rejects R2 and leaves A51 intact.
