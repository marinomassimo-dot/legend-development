## 3. A rejecting observation (stated before any data, to fix what would count)
The premise "the CSNK2B pattern (lower cortical PV density, raised gamma relative power, lower theta/gamma, lower gamma coherence and Granger strength) has a Wwox counterpart" would be **rejected** by this observation: in adult Wwox-hypomorph mice against littermate controls, per animal, n of at least 6 per group in the same behavioural state, the difference in gamma relative power and in theta/gamma lies inside an equivalence margin fixed beforehand (suggested: the 95 percent interval of the difference excludes the CSNK2B effect sizes read off its own tables) **and** cortical PV density is not lower. Either half alone narrows rather than rejects. The 3 against 3 hypomorph recording cannot reject (an interval from three animals per group excludes almost nothing); it can only show feasibility.
