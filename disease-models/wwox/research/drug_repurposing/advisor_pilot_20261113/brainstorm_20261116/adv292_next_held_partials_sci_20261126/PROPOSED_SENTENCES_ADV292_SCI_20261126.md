## A. Strict

**A1. Card A03 (`A03_client_binding_chaperone.card.json` and `.packet.json`), 39216838 (Wang 2024).** Current: "GABRA1 receptor-binding modulators raised variant protein and current together (measured in HEK293T, one knock-in iPSC line)".
Proposed: "Two benzodiazepine-site GABA-A positive modulators, hispidulin and TP003 (of 10 receptor ligands screened at 10 micromolar for 24 h on total alpha1 protein of three variants in HEK293T: four raised it in at least one variant, one lowered it in one variant), raised variant surface protein and GABA-evoked current in HEK293T for most of eight alpha1 variants (hispidulin significant for current in 8 of 8, TP003 in 6 of 8; not D219N and R214C); in one heterozygous G251D knock-in iPSC line, surface subunit fluorescence and proximity-ligation puncta rose and no current was measured (research compounds, not approved drugs; not WWOX evidence)". Sources: Supp Fig S3 (screen), Fig 4A-C (surface protein and current, n = 4 to 11 cells), Fig 7B-E.

**A2. Card A01 DR-A01-002 (`A01_pba_class.card.json` and `.packet.json`), 40485335 (Shah 2025) part.** Current: "The class raises wild-type protein in every target of the lot: the effect is not variant-specific."
Proposed (for the part that rests on 40485335; the three other papers of the row were not read in this batch): "In HEK293 cells 4-phenylbutyrate and glycerol increased the wild-type hGAT-1 mature band (blots not quantified) and 4-phenylbutyrate increased wild-type uptake (Fig 3A); in Drosophila astrocytes neither changed wild-type hGAT-1 expression (Fig 6B) while the retained variant G443V rose about 14-fold (Fig 6I). The cell-line effect on wild-type protein is not shown to be variant-specific; in vivo it was variant-selective in this paper." Consider dropping "every target of the lot".

**A3. Discovery ledger DL-MECH-170, "Designs with a wild-type arm", 31781019 (Gureviciene 2019).** Current: "an APP/PS1 line (PMID 31781019) shows same-direction effects in both genotypes for the spike classes present in both".
Proposed: "an APP/PS1 line (PMID 31781019; 8 APP/PS1 and 6 wild-type male mice, 5 to 6 months, ethosuximide 200 mg/kg intraperitoneal, within-animal, three-hour recordings) changed cortico-hippocampal spikes (down), fast-spindle spikes (down) and spindle-associated spikes (up) in the same direction in both genotypes by group means; the response of simple cortical spikes had opposite signs by group means once the one wild-type outlier the authors remove from the cortical analysis is excluded (wild type up, APP/PS1 down; neither significant), muscle-twitch spikes and spike-wave discharges showed no significant effect in either genotype, significance differs by genotype (wild type: spindle and fast-spindle; APP/PS1: cortico-hippocampal and fast-spindle), and no genotype-by-drug test exists (the authors write that the wild-type response 'seemed to follow' the APP/PS1 pattern)". Source: Supplemental Table 1 recomputed per mouse (rates per hour of immobility), Fig 11, Results.
