# ADV-121 pre-registration: pilot power note from the benchmark's measured spreads (SCI successor window R, 2026-11-20; hypothesis level; no dose; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

**Frozen before any n is computed.** This file fixes alpha, power, the SD rule, the effect sizes, the reading-age rule and the rule for measuring the three panels ADV-120 left unmeasured. It is committed to git before `compute_power_table.py` exists, so the commit order is the evidence of the freeze.

Error bars represent mean ± SD"**, so a drawn bar half-length is read as one SD, not a standard error.

## 1. Scope
Input: ADV's assignment ADV-121 (message of 2026-11-20 to SCI [86edd5]; text in `assignments/ADV-121.md` when it lands): the n per arm at which a pilot can tell the partial band (the low-dose arm, LD) from the broad bar (the high-dose arm minus spread, section 4 of the ADV-120 result), for the short-window families at P17 to P18 (weight P10 to P15, clasping P18, glucose, spike-wave rate, glia), Holm over the four treated arms of v3.1 6.8, with the SD upper bound alongside. No literature search, no delegate, no spend, no candidate value looked at. The pilot can classify only on these families (v3.1 6.25); survival is out of the table.

## 2. The statistic (fixed)
* **Method of v3.1 4.2 and 6.8, unchanged:** normal approximation plus 2, two-sided, power 0.80. Two-arm comparison of equal n, pooled SD:
  `n per arm = 2 (z_(1 - alpha/2) + z_(power))^2 (SD / delta)^2 + 2`, with `z_(power) = 0.8416`.
* **alpha:** two-sided 0.05 uncorrected (reference) and **0.05 / 4 = 0.0125 (Holm worst case over the four treated arms, the primary column)**. The three-arm value 0.0167 is not computed.
* **delta (the separation to be resolved):** the **broad-bar level minus the LD level**, in the family's own unit, where the bar level is `KO + c (WT - KO)` with `c` the bar closure of the result section 4 (weight 0.5; clasping 0.80), or the stated ratio to WT (glucose at P20 0.92; glia 1.2), using the card's measured means. The LD level is the card's LD mean. Where the benchmark has no bar (glucose P10) delta is the observed HD minus LD, labelled so. This is a **two-arm comparison between a candidate at the bar and a candidate at the LD level**; a pilot has no LD comparator, so this n is a stand-in for "can the estimate be placed on one side", not a design for a contrast the pilot runs.
* **Spike-wave rate has no LD arm in the benchmark**, so no partial reference exists: the table gives, instead, the n for the **candidate at the bar level against the knockout vehicle arm** (delta = KO mean minus bar level) and says so; it is not a partial-versus-broad separation.
* **Reading age:** per family with several ages (weight P5, P10, P15; glucose P10, P20, P30) the standardised separation `d = (HD - LD) / SD_pool` of the **observed** means ranks the ages; the reading age is the age with the largest d, ties stated. Single-age families carry that age.
* **Rounding:** one decimal in the table, plus the ceiling (an enrolment plan rounds up, 6.8).
* **Classes (descriptive, not a decision):** "fits the design's n" if n at the observed SD and Holm is at most 14 (6.8); "exceeds the design" from 15 to 50; "beyond the 6.8 upper-bound reference" above 50.

## 3. The SD rule (fixed)
* **Per arm:** SD = the drawn error-bar half-length (legend: mean ± SD), or the SD of the counted dots. **Weight (Fig 3C):** measure the bar extent beyond each marker for the LD and HD series at P5, P10 and P15 from the same raster (`mmc2.pdf` page 6, 500 ppi, crop recipe of `REPRODUCE_CROPS.txt`), two calibrations as in ADV-120. **A bar that does not protrude beyond the marker is recorded as "at most the marker half-height" (the marker height in pixels over the y scale) and the n computed from it is a ceiling marked "<="**, not a value. **Clasping (S4A):** half-length = the larger of the upper and lower whisker (occlusion only shortens a whisker). **Glucose:** the dot SD of the card. **Glia:** cap minus top of S7F and S7H. **Spike-wave:** the SD of the five per-animal values of the earlier SWD note (`swd_bridge_readout_sci_20261120/SWD_READOUT_FIG7_OBEID2026_SCI_20261120.md`, section on panel 7E).
* **Pooled:** `SD_pool = sqrt((SD_LD^2 + SD_HD^2) / 2)`; the bar-level arm has no measured SD, so the HD arm stands for it (the bar is HD minus spread). For the spike-wave contrast against the vehicle arm the pool is the KO and HD arms.
* **Observed SD column:** SD_pool. **Upper-bound column:** SD_pool times the **one-sided 95 percent value of an SD from 5 degrees of freedom (4 control and 3 knockout animals), 2.09**, the factor of 4.2 (recomputed, not copied, in the script). For glucose the benchmark's own SD has more degrees of freedom (dots minus one per arm); the table also states the factor at that df, labelled as the benchmark's own, and the 5 df factor stays the column the design uses.
* **Pixel spread and sampling spread kept apart:** the pixel spread is the change of n when the second calibration (B) replaces the first (A), reported as a maximum relative difference per family; the sampling spread is the 2.09 factor and the dependence of delta on the benchmark's LD mean, stated in words for the family where the n of the benchmark arm is counted (glucose) and as unknown elsewhere.
* **Implied n of the benchmark arms against its legends:** reported as the card's table for survival (10, 10, 10, 13 implied at risk against 20, 10, 20, 30), the counted dots for glucose, and "not printed" for weight, clasping, glia. Other legends of the supplement print n = 5 to 6 per arm for a different series (promoter comparison); they are not used.

## 4. The three panels left unmeasured: decided now
* **S7B and S7D** (P20 knockout against wild type, Iba1 and GFAP, "percentage of area in KO compared to WT"): they would supply the untreated-knockout baseline the glia row lacks, so **they are measured iff the glia row, computed first, is not "beyond the 6.8 upper-bound reference" (n at the observed SD and Holm at most 50)**; above 50 a closure unit cannot rescue the row and the panel cannot change an n. If measured: two calibrations, the stop rule of ADV-120, and what changes (an n, an age, or nothing) is stated.
* **The 2021 survival graph and the 2021 optic-nerve electron microscopy are not measured:** they belong to survival and myelin structure, outside the P17 short-window families of 6.25, and they carry no SD, delta or age for any row of this table. If a reader holds that one of them changes a short-window n, the way to say it is a new assignment.

## 6. Stop and what would change the plan
Stop rule for measurement: the stop rule of ADV-120 (two calibrations that agree; marks that overlap are not read). If the Fig 3C bars cannot be separated from the marker for any series, the weight row carries the ceiling and the report says weight is unresolved by the benchmark's own spread. A result that makes a family "beyond the 6.8 upper-bound reference" is reported as such; no family is dropped for an inconvenient n.
