## 1. Question
Do the held measurements of WWOX-deficient cells (Wwox knockout mouse embryonic fibroblasts, PMID 25012504; WWOX-silenced and knockout human fibroblasts, PMID 35328751) support a prediction for a compound that lowers complex I output (stiripentol in the tumour lines of the ADV-225 paper, PMID 41570991): a stress on these cells, or no effect beyond what the deficiency already shows?

## 2. Two readings and what each predicts
| Reading | Prediction from the held data | Observation in the held papers that would reject it |
|---|---|---|
| S (stressor) | the knockout cells already run with low respiratory reserve and low ATP, so further loss of complex I output has little room | a normal or raised spare respiratory capacity, or ATP equal to control, in the same model and state |
| N (non-event) | glycolysis already carries the ATP supply at its ceiling, or no dependence on respiration is measured, so the added loss changes little | glycolytic rate that is below its own ceiling in the knockout cells (a measured further rise on challenge), or a measured dependence on respiration |

Both readings may fail: the third disposition is "not decidable from held sources", with the missing measurement named.
