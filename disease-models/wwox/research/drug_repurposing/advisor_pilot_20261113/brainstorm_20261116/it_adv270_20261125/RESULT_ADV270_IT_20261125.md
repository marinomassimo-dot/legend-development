# ADV-270 result: held WWOX-deficient cell bioenergetics against a respiratory-chain inhibitor in tumour lines

IT (Orchestrator, window 0d3f20fd), 2026-11-25. Pre-registration: `PREREG_ADV270_IT_20261125.md` (frozen before the values were opened). Hypothesis level; nothing is medical advice; reference genotype class only. Held sources plus one publisher figure image; no spend, no contact. No dose, no recommendation.

## 1. Answer first
**Not decidable from held sources, and neither wording is licensed.** The held WWOX-deficient cell data are all non-neural (embryonic fibroblasts, adult-derived fibroblasts) and none of them measures the thing each reading needs: the stressor reading needs a respiratory reserve that is lower in the deficient cells than in control (the one panel that can show it, Fig 2b of PMID 25012504, shows about no reserve in **either** genotype), and the non-event reading needs glycolysis at its ceiling (never measured: only lactate output and enzyme activities, which are capacity, not reserve). The held tumour-line paper (PMID 41570991) points against the shortcut "a glycolytic cell is unaffected by lower complex I output", but it is not a WWOX system. **Missing measurement, named:** in one WWOX-deficient neural preparation and its matched control, OCR and ECAR (or an ATP production rate split into respiratory and glycolytic parts) at baseline and after a respiratory-chain challenge, with an oligomycin-stimulated glycolytic-reserve readout, normalised to cell number or protein, unit of replication = independent preparations or lines (not wells); the complex I-independent respiration control and LDH arm already named in ADV-225 IT-B stay.

Two readings of this table. (a) **FCCP/basal is 0.99 in WT and 0.96 in KO**: the uncoupler raises OCR to about the basal value in both genotypes, so the assay shows no spare capacity in either, and cannot show a KO-specific low reserve; a reserve near zero in the control is itself a sign the uncoupler step may not be at its optimum (not resolvable here: the legend prints the drug units as mM). (b) **The KO/WT ratio is 0.53 to 0.64 in every phase, including the residual after respiratory-chain inhibitors (0.53)**: a uniform scaling that includes the non-mitochondrial residue is what a per-well difference in cell number or material would give; the methods print the seeded number (5 x 10^4 cells, 24 h) and no normalisation after the run. So "reduced oxygen consumption" is drawn as written but is not separable from a per-well scaling difference on the printed methods; the ATP value has the same open point (no normalisation stated).

## 5. A premise correction on ADV-225 (my own earlier reading)
`it_adv225_20261124/RESULT_ADV225_IT_20261124.md` section 2 item 2 read PMID 41570991 at Tables 1 to 3 only (ATP/ADP, RNA-seq, docking) and called the compound "a complex I inhibitor in these cells" without naming the direct evidence. The paper has it: high-resolution respirometry (items above), respiration inhibited **immediately after injecting 500 µM and similarly after permeabilisation of the plasma membrane** (Fig 4B–D), rotenone-, Aptenin A5- and antimycin A-sensitive partition of uncoupled respiration (Fig 2G–I, n = 5), and biochemical and spectroscopic assays of NADH oxidation (abstract: acts upstream of the ubiquinone site, N-module). The ADV-225 conclusion stands and is better supported than that section showed; the sentence in `NR-03`'s note (stiripentol "acts as a complex I inhibitor in the cell line of 41570991") is right. Not re-read for new claims otherwise; the 250 to 500 µM are medium concentrations, not tissue exposures, and nothing here transfers a concentration to a WWOX system.
