# ADV-251 result: the knockout floor in Supp Fig 1K (IT, 2026-11-25)

Actor `orchestrator` (IT). Hypothesis level; reference genotype class; nothing is medical advice. Held raster only; no spend, no contact, no delegate. Pre-registration: `PREREG_ADV251_IT_20261125.md` (commit 44634674f, before any pixel of the blot was extracted). Every value below is **my read-off from a JPEG, not author data**.

## 1. Answer first

1. **The 1K wild-type lane is not saturated** (darkest grey of the 46 kDa band 88 of 255; the same strip reaches 2 elsewhere; rounded peak over rows 92 to 95, no flat top). So the floor fractions are **not** upper bounds for saturation. (Display JPEG; clipping in the original film or exposure cannot be seen from it.)
2. **The knockout floor on the authors' own membrane is not small:** HSP90-normalised, as a fraction of the same membrane's wild-type lane, WKO-1C **0.07** and WKO-2C **0.15** (literal pre-registered windows); with per-lane HSP90 windows 0.05 and 0.12; WWOX band alone, no HSP90 correction, 0.05 and 0.09. The larger lane is at or above 0.08 in all three variants.
3. **Decision rule: FLOOR_AT_OR_ABOVE_0.1.** The G372R lines printed at 0.1, 0.1 and 0.3 (membrane 1M, author densitometry) cannot be separated from the knockout floor class by this read, so **the band-present reading stays unproven** for the two 0.1 lines; the 0.3 line is nominally 2 to 4.5 times the floor read (0.3 / 0.15 and 0.3 / 0.07). Cross-membrane, order of magnitude only.
4. **The calibration check fails** (section 3). My read-off does not reproduce the authors' printed rescue values (0.8 and 1.4) by a factor of about 6, so the floor values are **not on the authors' scale** and the uncertainty is a factor of about 6 in either direction of that scale. What the failure does and does not say: it gives no evidence that the authors' scale would put the floor lower; CLEARLY_BELOW (at or below 0.04) is not reached under any variant.

## 3. Calibration against the authors' printed values (pre-registered check; failed)

File010 (Supplementary Fig 1, page 2) prints 1 under JH WT and 0.8 and 1.4 under the two AAV9 lanes of panel K (no value under the knockout lanes). In the pre-registered 46 kDa window my AAV9 lanes read 0.121 and 0.239, i.e. 6.6 and 5.9 times below the printed values (limit: a factor of 1.5). The rescue band in these lanes sits about 16 rows above the wild-type 46 kDa band (peak rows 77 and 78 against 93; marker positions not calibrated here), so the registered window excludes most of it. Read at its own position (post hoc, exploratory, not part of the decision) the two lanes give 0.50 and 0.54, still 1.6 and 2.6 times below the printed values. Therefore the authors' densitometry used something my read does not reproduce (different band, window, exposure or a different image of the same membrane). Per the pre-registration this widens the stated uncertainty of the floor to the factor observed (about 6) and is reported before the floor.

## 4. Decision, by the frozen rule

f = the larger knockout lane = 0.145 (fixed windows) or 0.116 (per-lane): at or above 0.08 -> **FLOOR_AT_OR_ABOVE_0.1**. The lower lane alone (0.05 to 0.07) would fall in NOT_SEPARABLE; the rule uses the larger lane, so the code stands. The two knockout clones differ by a factor of about 2 (0.067 and 0.145), so even the floor is not one number.

## Dated note, IT39 (c817e528), 2026-11-28: which bands these values cover

The knockout-lane and rescue-lane values for uncropped Supp Fig 1K (PMID 42397075, week-16 human iPSC organoids) in this record were measured on the **rows inside the cropped strip** (the wild-type band the authors label 46 kDa and the position about 16 rows above it). They do **not** include the two strong bands at about 48 and 50 kDa of the uncropped membrane, which are present in both engineered-knockout lanes (HE, ADV-336, blind: `adv336_blind_antibody_he_20261128/TABLE_ADV336_HE.md`, where the strong wild-type-only band computes to about 41.6 kDa on the printed ladder; an IT39 delegate saw the same pattern). **Which pixels the legend's "upper band at ~48kDa" means is unsettled.** No claim wording changes with this note (ADV, 2026-11-28).
