## 1. Question
On the membrane of Supp Fig 1K (week-16 organoids: wild type, WWOX-KO clones, AAV9-hSyn-hWWOX rescues), what is the 46 kDa signal in each knockout lane as a fraction of the same membrane's wild-type lane, HSP90-normalised, by read-off densitometry on the held raster?

## 2. Method (frozen)
1. Extract the embedded images of PDF page 5 at native resolution (pymupdf `extract_image`); record pixel dimensions and format (JPEG or not). Convert to 8-bit grey with ImageMagick; parse pixels with plain Python (no PIL, no numpy).
2. Lane map: lane centres from the column darkness profile of the HSP90 strip (the strongest bands); a lane is the centre plus or minus 40 percent of the median lane pitch. The lane labels are read from the page rendering and tied to lanes by order; any lane whose label cannot be tied is excluded and named.
3. Band window: rows within plus or minus 6 pixels of the 46 kDa band centre, set from the wild-type lane (peak of the row profile); the same window for all lanes of the WWOX strip. HSP90 window set the same way on the HSP90 strip. If WWOX and HSP90 are one image, the two windows are two row ranges of that image.
4. Signal = sum over the window of (background minus pixel), grey inverted so dark is signal; background = the median of the same lane's pixels in two off-band row ranges (20 to 50 pixels above and below the window, away from other bands). Net negative signals are reported as 0 with the raw value.
5. Normalised ratio per lane = (WWOX net / HSP90 net) divided by the same quantity of the wild-type lane. Reported with a resolution statement (pixel height of the band window, jpeg quantisation) and as read-off, never as author data.
6. Calibration check: the printed rescue values (0.8 and 1.4) are compared with my ratios for the same lanes. The check passes if each of my rescue ratios is within a factor of 1.5 of the printed value. A failed check does not discard the floor value; it widens its stated uncertainty to the factor observed and is reported first.
7. Saturation check of the wild-type lane: the WT band window is saturated when the darkest 3 or more adjacent rows of the lane reach the image's darkest value present in the whole image (flat-topped profile) or when the peak grey is within 3 levels of the image minimum. If saturated, the wild-type signal is a lower bound and every floor fraction is an upper bound, and it is stated so in the first line of the result.

## 3. Decision rule (frozen; f = the larger of the two knockout lanes' ratios, upper-bound-corrected when saturated)
| f | Reading |
|---|---|
| f at or above 0.08 | FLOOR_AT_OR_ABOVE_0.1: the G372R lanes at 0.1 to 0.3 of wild type are not separable from the knockout floor by this read; the band-present reading stays unproven |
| f at or below 0.04 | CLEARLY_BELOW: the 0.1 to 0.3 lanes are above the floor as a class |
| 0.04 < f < 0.08 | NOT_SEPARABLE: neither; reported with both numbers |
Cross-membrane clause: SCAR12 lanes are on membrane 1M and the floor is on 1K; the comparison is of orders of magnitude only and is never written as a same-membrane result. If the WT saturation check fails the upper-bound rule above applies and CLEARLY_BELOW requires the upper bound, not the point value, at or below 0.04.
