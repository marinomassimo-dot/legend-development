## Wording for INV-02 (replaces the ADV-168 clause; IT's text, hypothesis level; no compound, no new mandatory test)
> If ATF4 is high in the WWOX-deficient line, read baseline phospho-eIF2 alpha (total and phosphorylated, ratio) together with ATF4 at a stated time, and add, as an optional controlled comparison, the response of ATF4 to a short exposure to an mTOR or eIF4E inhibitor in the WWOX-deficient line and its isogenic control, next to an ATF4-high canonical challenge in the isogenic line at a matched time (thapsigargin was the comparator in the published fibroblast experiment). In that experiment, in mouse fibroblasts, ATF4 induced by depletion of eIF2B epsilon was lost under Torin 1 at 1 to 2 h while Tg-induced ATF4 persisted on the same blot; the canonical arm was untransduced, the result is a representative blot of three independent experiments with no quantification shown, and a second eIF2B-reduced preparation (eIF2 beta depletion) responded only partly. Treat the result as an observed pattern in that preparation, not a classifier of route and not an eIF2B activity measurement in WWOX. PERK mobility shift is context for an ER-stress challenge only: an absent shift does not exclude a canonical response driven by another eIF2 alpha kinase. Read GADD34 and CHOP protein only at a late, matched time (acute blots do not show them), and note that GADD34 and CHOP mRNA rise in the eIF2B-reduced state and do not separate the routes.

## Part 2: the R132H oligodendrocyte-progenitor endpoint (Extended Data Fig. 3e)
**What was found.** Labels: the Results sentence and the Methods name mouse induced-pluripotent-stem-cell-derived OPCs carrying eIF2B epsilon **R132H** ("Mouse induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) carrying the eIF2Bε(R132H) alteration"; the ED 3 legend says progenitors "from WT or Eif2b5 R132H/R132H mice", a minor wording slip against "iPSC-derived"). The ES cells and all ISR experiments carry **R191H**: two alleles, two systems. Shared conditions: both genotypes were differentiated for 72 h in one medium containing 40 ng/ml triiodothyronine plus noggin, NT-3, cAMP and IGF-1 ("40 ng ml−1 triiodothyronine (thyroid hormone; Sigma T-6397)"); O1 stained live, MBP after fixation, imaged on a high-content system, analysed by script; outliers removed by a ROUT test (Q = 1 percent) and a Welch t-test run on wells.
**Units (Source Data ED Fig. 3, read in full).** Three independent repeats, 4 to 8 wells each (O1+: wild type 5, 8, 4 wells counting one well not determined; R132H 6, 8, 4; MBP+: wild type 6, 6, 4; R132H 6, 8, 4), values normalised to the wild-type mean of each repeat (wild-type repeat means 0.998, 1.000, 1.002; what is counted per well, and whether the denominator is nuclei or cells, is not stated). The printed P values (1.0E-18 and 3.5E-17) are well-level (my Welch recomputation on the pooled wells gives 1.8E-16 and 3.4E-17; same order of smallness, not identical for O1+, so the outlier handling or test details differ; neither is a repeat-level test), which treats 17 to 18 wells per arm as independent. **At repeat level the direction is robust: ratio of R132H to wild type 0.519, 0.357, 0.464 for O1+ (mean 0.447) and 0.441, 0.434, 0.362 for MBP+ (mean 0.412); in every one of the three repeats every R132H well lies below every wild-type well, for both markers.** A one-sample test of three repeat ratios against 1 is only descriptive (the wild-type arm has no variance by construction: P 0.007 for O1+, 0.002 for MBP+).
**Whether the protein or perturbation result is in the same cells: no.** The paper reports no phospho-eIF2 alpha, ATF4, exchange activity or ISR perturbation in the R132H progenitors; the GEO series contain no progenitor data; the Source Data hold only the O1 and MBP sheet. All ISR and metabolic experiments are in MEFs, R191H ES cells or edited ES and 3T3 cells.
**Reading against the pre-registered rule.** P-weak and, on the cross-check, P-red. Reasons: (1) only one T3 condition (T3 present in both genotypes), no T3-free or T3-dose arm, so "fails to mature despite T3 supply" is shown, but "T3 insufficiency against intrinsic block" is not tested; (2) no ISR readout in these cells (no ISR mediation inferred); (3) denominator unstated; (4) different allele and system from the R191H ES cells and from any WWOX system; (5) **an existing held comparison already carries the concept**: the IT28 `A_glia_myelin` rows B1 to B4 (an oligodendrocyte-specific Eif2b5 mutant: higher Ki67 share, no cell death, immature oligodendrocytes, with a normal bulk ATF4 set) are an in-vivo, cell-type-specific, eIF2B-lesion maturation deficit, and the same IT28 table lists the VWM R191H mouse with the ISR "set up with no p-eIF2alpha rise". The new result adds an in-vitro, T3-containing effect-size benchmark (about 0.4 of wild type in all three repeats), nothing in kind. (6) INV-06's X1 positive controls (`x1_discriminating_observations_presidio005_20261115`) are MEDIUM-type controls (recombinant CTGF; conditioned medium from Tsc2-knockdown neurons), not genotype comparators; an eIF2B-mutant progenitor culture cannot serve as the "medium positive control that works in the same run".
**Decision: REJECT as new work for the soluble-signal against intrinsic comparison.** No clause is carried to IT. One record-keeping sentence for ADV only: "Chen 2025 ED 3e (R132H iPSC-derived mouse OPC, 72 h with T3, O1 and MBP at about 0.4 of wild type in 3 of 3 repeats, wells as the printed unit) is an adjacent confirmation of the held IT28 B-row observation; it is not an intrinsic-block comparator for X1." Kept apart in every sentence above: O1/MBP maturation from sheath formation or conduction; two alleles and two systems; the transcriptomic brain overlap.

## The brain overlap (Part 1 completion)
The Supplementary Discussion adds one more brain statement: of 33 mRNAs raised in forebrains of Eif2b5 R191H mice (cited from earlier work, not measured here), "11 overlapped with the transcriptomic signature of s-ISR, including PCK2", and eIF2-alpha-p levels are reduced in the mutant mouse and in patient brains (cited). Still a published gene-list overlap and a citation, group level, MEF depletion against bulk forebrain; no new brain measurement exists in the paper.
