## Part 1: source completion — questions and readings (fixed)
Question: with the Methods and the remaining figure images, do the paired-condition data actually separate the two routes with matched challenge, matched time and sound units, so that a conditional INV-02 readout (sensitivity of ATF4 to an eIF4E or mTOR inhibitor, read beside baseline phospho-eIF2-alpha) is interpretable? Or do those details make it uninterpretable, so that it is withdrawn?
* **KEEP (conditional readout stands):** the inhibitor result is shown in the same cell model with the Tg arm and the depletion arm on the same blot or in the same experiment, the inhibitor dose and exposure time are the same for both arms, ATF4 is lost in the depleted arm and retained in the Tg arm, and the replicate unit is an independent experiment (n of 3 or more) or the limitation (for example n = 2) is stated.
* **REWORD (keep, narrowed):** the contrast holds only for specific preparations (for example mouse fibroblasts and mouse ES cells) or only for one inhibitor; the wording must say so, and must not make inhibitor sensitivity a stand-alone classifier or an inferred eIF2B activity in a WWOX line.
* **WITHDRAW:** the inhibitor arms are not paired on one blot or one experiment, the exposure times differ in a way that could by itself separate the arms, or the replicate count and the controls leave the contrast uninterpretable.
* **PERK wording check (fixed):** PERK status is context for an ER-stress challenge (a PERK shift marks PERK activation by ER stress), not an exclusive marker of all canonical responses, because other eIF2-alpha kinases drive the canonical route and PERK may be indifferent to an eIF2B lesion. If my ADV-168 sentence "baseline phospho-eIF2-alpha (with PERK status)" reads as PERK being an assignment marker, it is corrected here.
* **Time-matched caveat (kept):** GADD34 and CHOP protein only at a late matched time.
* No RNA-seq reanalysis. The GEO series are inventoried by accession and size, not downloaded.

## Part 2: the R132H oligodendrocyte-progenitor endpoint — readings and decision (fixed)
Question: does the Extended Data Fig. 3e result add an ADJACENT POSITIVE COMPARATOR for an INTRINSIC differentiation block despite thyroid hormone (T3) in the existing soluble-signal against intrinsic comparison (INV-06 / RP-017), or is it redundant or uninterpretable?
* **P-pos:** progenitor-derived cultures of the R132H genotype differentiate less than wild-type cultures under the same T3-containing conditions, with independent experiments (not wells) as the unit, an identified readout (O1 and MBP positivity), and the comparison shown within one system.
* **P-weak:** the same direction but the replicate unit is wells or the T3 condition is not shown to be shared, or the allele and system differ from the ones the INV-06 / RP-017 comparison uses; then "weak" and no clause.
* **P-red:** an existing qualified comparison already holds the same kind of comparator (named, with the clause that makes the new one redundant), or the contrast is uninterpretable (unclear labels, different media between arms, purity of the progenitor culture not shown).
* Keep apart, in any outcome: O1/MBP maturation against sheath formation or conduction; the R132H progenitors against the R191H mouse ES cells (two alleles, two systems); the transcriptomic brain overlap (Part 1); and no ISR mediation inferred in progenitors or in WWOX (the paper does not show phospho-eIF2-alpha, ATF4 or an ISR perturbation in the R132H progenitor cultures unless the Methods say so; this is checked).
* **Decision rule:** carry ONE clause to IT only if P-pos holds and the clause changes how the soluble-signal against intrinsic comparison is interpreted (a stated limit on what T3 supply can show); otherwise reject as new work. No separate experiment.
