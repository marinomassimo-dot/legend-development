## Question

## Two explanations
* H1, usable control: S540E is compared with wild-type and/or S540A in the same cells, at matched expression, on a readout of translation or ISR output (puromycin or other global synthesis, ATF4), with at least 3 independent replicates, and moves in the direction opposite to S540A (or as loss of function against wild type).
* H2, reporter-only observation (my lean, about 70 percent): S540E is read on the BACE1 5' UTR reporter (and possibly BACE1 protein) only; no global translation or ATF4 readout; no matched S540A arm in the same experiment, or expression not shown to be matched; replicate unit unstated or technical.

## Predicted observable
Under H2: panels with reporter activity or BACE1 level for silencing, wild type and S540E; no puromycin and no ATF4 panel for S540E; no dead-form (E572A numbering of the retinal paper, or F259L) arm anywhere. Under H1: a panel with S540A and S540E side by side on global synthesis or ATF4.

## Strong counterexample (would break the "mimetic" reading)
S540E at matched expression does not differ from wild type, or S540A moves the same way as S540E, or S540E protein is itself lower than wild type (then the effect can be loss of expression or instability and not a phosphorylation-state mimic).
