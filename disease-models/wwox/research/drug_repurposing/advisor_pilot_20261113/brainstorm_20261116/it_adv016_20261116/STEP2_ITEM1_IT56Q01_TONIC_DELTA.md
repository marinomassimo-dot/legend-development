# ADV-016 step 2, item 1: IT56-Q01 tonic current and delta-subunit pharmacology (IT; literature step; abstract level from PubMed, full texts NOT read; non-canonical; not medical advice)
According to PubMed (abstracts retrieved with the PubMed tool on 2026-11-16 ~21:10Z; DOIs as links). Reference genotype class only; mechanism labels; no dose; no recommendation.
**The gap SCI named is partly filled by three mouse layer 2/3 pyramidal-cell papers in the model's age window or near it:**
* Drasbek and Jensen 2006, Cerebral Cortex, [10.1093/cercor/bhj055](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhj055) (PMID 16221925): frontoparietal neocortex slices of P13-P19 mice; THIP (delta-preferring agonist) 1 uM induced a robust tonic GABA-A current in layer 2/3 neurons, 44% larger than in layer 5; only a minute tonic current was induced by mimicking in vivo endogenous GABA levels; miniature IPSCs not affected.
* Drasbek, Hoestgaard-Jensen and Jensen 2007, J Neurophysiol, [10.1152/jn.00651.2006](https://doi.org/10.1152/jn.00651.2006) (PMID 17215511): mouse layer 2/3 pyramidal cells: THIP tonic current 44 +/- 5 pA; zolpidem (alpha1/2/3-gamma2 selective) did not alter it; etomidate (beta2/3) potentiated it by 126%, propofol by 117%; the authors infer alpha4beta2/3delta receptors.
* Vardya et al. 2008, J Neurophysiol, [10.1152/jn.01224.2007](https://doi.org/10.1152/jn.01224.2007) (PMID 18463187): mouse layer 2/3: pyramidal cells showed 66 +/- 19 pA to THIP 1 uM; somatostatin interneurons did not (8 pA); pyramidal cells were sensitive to the delta-preferring neurosteroid THDOC (100 nM) and to elevated endogenous GABA.
**Counter-evidence and cautions (abstract level):** Yamada et al. 2007, Cerebral Cortex, [10.1093/cercor/bhl087](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhl087) (PMID 16997904): RAT somatosensory cortex: midazolam augmented a tonic current, larger in layer V than layer II/III; alpha1 involved in both layers, alpha5 in layer V only; zolpidem equal in both layers. This conflicts with the zolpidem result of Drasbek 2007 in the mouse (species, agonist-evoked versus endogenous current). Keros and Hablitz 2005, J Neurophysiol, [10.1152/jn.00520.2005](https://doi.org/10.1152/jn.00520.2005) (PMID 15987761): rat layer II/III: neither a GAT-1 blocker nor a GAT-2/3 blocker alone raised the tonic current; together they did (supports my attack on I13: transporter compensation). Lambert et al. 2025, J Neurosci, [10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0601-24.2025](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0601-24.2025) (PMID 40204438): delta in PV interneurons shapes sleep EEG (NREM oscillations, REM theta bursts): relevant to I10.
**What this changes (decision):** (1) The endogenous tonic current of L2/3 pyramidal cells at P13-P19 is small at ambient GABA (Drasbek and Jensen), so IT56-Q01 as a comparison of endogenous tonic currents between genotypes may be underpowered; the delta-agonist-evoked (THIP) current is the receptor-reserve readout, as my I13 attack proposed, and it has a published reference size in this layer and age (44 to 66 pA at 1 uM). (2) For the neurosteroid-versus-benzodiazepine arm of the matrix: in mouse L2/3 pyramidal cells the delta-preferring route is neurosteroid-sensitive and zolpidem-insensitive (two mouse papers); the rat paper says benzodiazepine-sensitive: the arm table cell 'tonic-preferring versus synaptic-preferring GABA-A modulator' (I13) stays a prediction with a species-split source, not a settled difference. (3) A GAT-blocker arm needs both transporters; a single GAT-1 blocker is not expected to raise the tonic current (Keros and Hablitz), which changes the I14/I13 'raised ambient GABA' reading: vigabatrin (raises GABA by transaminase inhibition, a different route) is not equivalent to a single transporter blocker.
**Limits:** abstract level only; no full text read; ages P13-P19 are the model's window but the cells are wild-type; no Wwox data; the species split (mouse versus rat) is unresolved; I did not search for P13-P17 L2/3 benzodiazepine pharmacology beyond these queries (the PubMed tool returns nothing when more than about five concepts are combined).
