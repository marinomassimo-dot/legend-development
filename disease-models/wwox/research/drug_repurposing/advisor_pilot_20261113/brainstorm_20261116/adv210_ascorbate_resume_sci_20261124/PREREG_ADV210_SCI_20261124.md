* **C2, composition, marker-weighted.** Frozen panels (mouse symbols; human upper-case): neuron Snap25, Syt1, Rbfox3, Slc17a7, Gad1; astrocyte Gfap, Aqp4, Slc1a3, Aldh1l1, Gja1; oligodendrocyte Mbp, Plp1, Mog, Mag; OPC Pdgfra, Cspg4; microglia Cx3cr1, P2ry12, Tmem119; endothelial Pecam1, Cldn5. Report (i) KO minus control shift of each class score per dataset; (ii) the across-library correlation of SLC23A2 with each class score; (iii) the SLC23A2 genotype delta adjusted by ONE class score at a time (a leave-one-class analogue). Rule: composition CAN explain a dataset's SLC23A2 sign only if a single-class adjustment moves its delta at least 50 percent toward zero or across zero.
