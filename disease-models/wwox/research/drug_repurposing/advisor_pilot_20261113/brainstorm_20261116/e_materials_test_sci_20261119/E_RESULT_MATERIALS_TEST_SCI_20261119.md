3. **What the materials contributed, read from the structured arm's own report and not credited by default:** two items marked secondary, the mouse Erbb4 motif positions (checked by no one) and the caution that the Pvalb fall is PV-weighted, not PV-specific. The NETO2 motif position, the other pack-derived item, was found independently by the lean arm (verifier: the PPHY at residue 395 is correct in mouse and human). **The discriminating design element that decided the primary outcome (null as the comparator, three-way rejection logic, mutated-motif negative control) is the arm's own reasoning, not a pack item.** The structured arm also spent about 1.9 times the cache-read and 1.4 times the tool calls of the lean arm inside the same cap and read more full text (the 2019 and 2018 papers), which the lean arm could have reached with the same tools; the lean arm reached the weak-affinity full text (Schuchardt 2013) that the structured arm's route was blocked from (a reCAPTCHA on PMC; it stopped as instructed). **So the difference is not credited to the structure** (criteria rule); one case does not show whether the structure helps.

The Dvl2/Wbp1 positive control is sound (the pull-down is n = 2 per group, as the legend states).

## 3. The verifier's judgement in short (full text: `outputs/VERIFIER_REPORT_AS_DELIVERED.md`)
* **For X (structured):** a rejection set that separates "protein needed" from "WW1 binding needed" by using null as well as P47T as comparators, a NETO2 pull-down with a mutated-motif negative control, full-text quantification of the neural phenotype (PV-positive cells down 44 percent, n = 7 against 7; NPY-positive down 30 percent in dentate gyrus; GAD65/67 down), the cerebellar basket-cell and Purkinje data of the P47T paper.
* **For Y (lean):** the better-calibrated reading of the ERBB4 binding evidence (human WW1 against three ERBB4 PPxY peptides: a little over 100 micromolar for the strongest motif, hundreds of micromolar for the others, WW2 without binding, the authors' own caveats), the separation of SDR-domain partners (GSK3-beta, Tau) from WW1-PPxY partners, parallel functional readouts, the Neto2-knockout PV-maturation abstract, an age caveat.
* Verified pivotal rows: no claim contradicted in either; unchecked pivotal claims about five per arm. Descriptive counts of questions with a verified full-text anchor: three in each (Q2, Q3, Q6 for X through the 2019 and 2023 papers; Q1, Q2, Q6 for Y through Schuchardt and the 2023 paper); Q4 and Q5 are negative or absence items in both and have no anchor in either.
* Minor items: both state absence claims about drugs without a compound-search perimeter; one cell-line mention in Y (COS) could not be confirmed from its abstract.

## 4. What the result may and may not say
One case, one model, one run per arm, one verifier, a decision whose two reports agreed. It can say that, on this decision and at an equal tool-call cap, a delegate that had the loop's pack and one that had only the question reached the same decision, differed in which strengths each brought, and that **the specific items the pack added were two secondary caveats, neither decisive**. It cannot say that the structure is better or worse in general; the structured arm cost more. The case did not test whether the pack would have mattered for a decision whose answer lives in the project's own held data (such as the conduction measurement), which is where the pack's value would be expected; a later test should be attached to a decision of that kind, not repeated here.
