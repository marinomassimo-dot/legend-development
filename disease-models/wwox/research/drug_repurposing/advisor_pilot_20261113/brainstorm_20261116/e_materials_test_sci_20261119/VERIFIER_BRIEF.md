# Verifier brief (blind comparison of two outputs, X and Y)

You compare two reports, X and Y, written for the same decision. You do not know how either was produced. Do not start agents. Send no personal or contact data to any service. Open **primary sources only for the pivotal claims** (the ones that decide the comparison) and say which ones you opened and which you did not; a claim you did not check is "unchecked", not "supported".

## Decision under comparison

## What to deliver (one file, at most 1800 words)

One category per judgement; you may give a primary and one secondary category, with the reason in two sentences.
2. `HARM` is defined as: a candidate or lever justified by an interaction that is only proposed; relation identity (direct binding, which WW domain, species, assay) asserted beyond its source; a sign or function transferred across species, age or cell type without saying so; an absence claim without its search perimeter. List each instance per output with the sentence and why.
3. `NO_DIFFERENCE` applies when both reach the same decision and neither has an extra item you judge able to change the comparison or the experiment; then list the extra items as traceability only.
4. Descriptive counts (not the winner rule): per output, how many of the six questions are answered with a full-text anchor in the pivotal table (check that the cited locator exists and says what is claimed for the pivotal rows you open); the number of claims you found contradicted or unfindable in their cited source; the number of pivotal claims you did not check.
5. Items found by one output and not the other, each marked: could change the comparison or the experiment / could not.
Do not guess the origin of either output. Do not rewrite the criteria. Write your report to the path given in your dispatch.
