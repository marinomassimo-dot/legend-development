## 1. Answer first
1. **One included hit rejects the strong form of R-08 and DL-MOL-013: PMID 35433113** (Feng et al., Mol Ther Nucleic Acids 2022, DOI 10.1016/j.omtn.2022.03.015; open full text PMC8987850, read by IT in full on 2026-11-25). The variant is SLC26A4 c.919-2A>G ("A-2G"): "located at the 3' splice site of intron 7, disrupts the highly conserved dinucleotide AG and causes predominant skipping of exon 8". A 2'-O-methoxyethyl, phosphorothioate antisense oligonucleotide raised exon 8 inclusion in cultured blood cells from two homozygous patients "from 31% (untreated) to 67% and from 21.5% (untreated) to 61.5%"; the untreated full-length and skipped transcripts were sequenced. The authors write: "none of those previously developed therapeutic splice-switching ASOs target mis-splicing caused by disruption of canonical splice sites. Our data prove that these mis-splicing events are still rescuable." Decision rule § 4 of the pre-registration: an included non-trans hit exists, so the disposition is **REJECTED_BY_HIT_X (X = 35433113)** for the statement as worded.
2. **What the hit does and does not show** (all from the full text): (a) the oligonucleotide does **not** target the acceptor; it occludes an hnRNP A1/A2 intronic splicing silencer ("ISS-F1", intron 8 positions 10 to 32), so the route is exon definition, not rebuilding the AG; (b) the destroyed AG (A to G, giving GG) is **not dead**: untreated patient cells already make 19 to 33 percent full-length transcript, and the authors call the site a non-canonical splice site "surrounded by high density of SREs"; the oligonucleotide **raises residual use**, it does not create it; (c) the readout in patient cells is RT-PCR product identity by band and sequencing of the untreated bands; I did not see the junction sequence after treatment (Figure 3B image not opened); (d) evidence level: two patients' cultured blood cells (n = 3 and 2 per bar), a minigene in HEK293, and a partially humanised mouse that does not mimic the endogenous allele (a third, intron 7 retained product appears); full-length rose from 13 to 28 percent in liver only, with no change in inner ear (the target organ), kidney changed by qRT-PCR only; pendrin protein 2.2-fold in liver. One gene, one allele, one group.
3. **So the statement "a destroyed invariant AG cannot be restored by an oligonucleotide" is not supported in the strong form; the narrower statement stands:** no report found in which an RNA-level reagent rebuilds a destroyed canonical AG or restores correct splicing at an acceptor that retains no residual use. Whether an oligonucleotide can help depends on whether the AG-destroyed allele keeps residual correctly spliced product in the tissue of interest. For WWOX c.1057-2A>G that is unmeasured (`analysis/splice_allele_rna_evidence_20260922.md`, § 3.3: no one has measured residual correctly spliced transcript from any WWOX acceptor allele). The comparison to c.1057-2A>G is therefore a **conditional hypothesis**, not a finding: if a residual correct product exists, a silencer-targeting route is a candidate; if none exists, this hit does not apply.
4. **Near misses (acceptor weakened, AG intact), listed so they are not later read as the answer:** PMID 22362925 (F9 polypyrimidine-tract lesions at the acceptor corrected by U1 loaded on the downstream donor; abstract only), PMID 38182646 (ABCA4 c.5461-8T>G, polypyrimidine region, AG intact, an out-of-protocol find; abstract only). **Unresolved:** PMID 25421123 (ATP8B1: modified U1 rescued several donor and acceptor mutations in a minigene; which acceptor lesions are at the AG is not in the abstract; paywalled; the publisher page returned 402): not scored as included, and not counted as absent. **Excluded on the mammalian rule:** the S. pombe reports (PMIDs 1617727, 9358179: compensatory U1 changes partly restored lariat formation with 3' AG mutations in one report and failed in the other).
5. **Searched and not found:** an exon-specific U1, a U2/U6 adaptor, an engineered U2AF35 or SF3B variant, or trans-splicing rescue of an AG-destroyed acceptor with correct product (no trans-splicing hit met the rule; PMID 13130143 is a 5' exon trans-splicing of CFTR with no splice-site lesion). SCN2A c.1177-2A>C (PMID 40225931, full text read by the delegate) was an AG-destroyed variant in a modified-U1 study and was **not** rescued or attempted: "Except for SCN2A c.1177-2A>C variant, all variants we analyzed ... are at the 5' splice site". PMID 25491247 (Sanfilippo C c.372-2A>G) was treated at the protein level only.
