## 1. Inclusion rule (frozen)
A hit is INCLUDED only if all four hold:
1. the lesion is at the canonical intron-end AG itself (AG to a non-AG, or deletion of it), in a human or mammalian gene, a minigene, or a model of one;
2. the intervention is RNA-level (antisense or splice-switching oligonucleotide, modified U1/U2/U6/U7 snRNA or adaptor, ExSpeU1, trans-splicing, engineered splicing factor such as U2AF35/SF3B variants, minigene or suppressor tRNA-like RNA); DNA or base editing is excluded;
3. the readout is correctly spliced product at the original acceptor (or, for trans-splicing, a correct replacement product), measured by RT-PCR, sequencing or protein;
4. the splice used by the rescued product is not a nearby cryptic or newly created site (a shift of 1 nt or more, or a branch-point or enhancer effect on a weakened but intact AG, is NEAR_MISS, not INCLUDED).
Each hit gets one of: INCLUDED, NEAR_MISS (cryptic or created site, or a weakened but intact AG), EXCLUDED (reason). Trans-splicing hits are INCLUDED only if the readout is correct protein or mRNA from an endogenous or minigene transcript whose acceptor is destroyed, and are tagged TRANS.
