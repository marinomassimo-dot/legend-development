## Correction note (2026-11-25, after IT's blind verifier B, relayed by ADV): which quantity each number is

The "SDD 17.7 to 31.7" in the table of section 2 is the **unadjusted detectable difference at 80 percent power**, SDD = (t(.975,df) + t(.80,df)) x sp x sqrt(1/n1 + 1/n2); it reproduces exactly from the blind report's inputs (pooled WT 17 v 15 plotted points, df 30, sp 17.2: (2.042 + 0.854) x 17.2 x 0.354 = 17.6; WT NT 4 v 7 organoids, df 9, sp 16.1: (2.262 + 0.883) x 16.1 x 0.627 = 31.7). It is **not** the critical difference, which is a different quantity: the unadjusted critical difference CD = t(.975,df) x sp x sqrt(1/n1 + 1/n2) is 12.4 and 22.8 for the same inputs, and the Tukey critical difference of the paper's own four-arm test, CDT = q(.05;4,df)/sqrt(2) x sp x sqrt(1/n1 + 1/n2), is 16.6 and 31.5. The verifier's point stands in this form: the two quantities are easy to confuse because the unadjusted SDD (17.6, 31.7) lies within one point of the Tukey critical difference (16.6, 31.5) for these inputs, and the SDD I carried is **not adjusted for the multiple comparisons the paper used (Tukey, four arms)**. The detectable difference at 80 percent power under the Tukey critical value, SDDT = (q/sqrt(2) + t(.80,df)) x sp x sqrt(1/n1 + 1/n2), is **21.8 (pooled WT, plotted points) to 40.4 (WT NT, legend organoids)**, in line with ADV's "about 25 to 40 points". Against the disease effect of 24 to 27 points that is 81 to 91 percent and 150 to 170 percent of the effect: the verdict "claims more than the data bear" gets stronger, not weaker, and the class (DERIVABLE, bounded) does not change. Inputs and the four values are printed by `sdd_label_check_adv284.py` (executable; quantiles tabulated, studentized range q for four arms). Unit-and-n line for every later use: say, each time, whether a number is a critical difference or a detectable difference, whether it is adjusted for multiple comparisons, and which unit and n it uses. The earlier figures in this file (12.5 to 31.4 first reading, 17.7 to 31.7 blind reading) are unadjusted SDD values.
