In one cuprizone demyelination (mouse corpus callosum slices, 6 weeks; percentage of myelinated axons about 72 and 89 percent lower relative to sham at two coronal levels, read from group means in Figure 3E,F of PMID 31140740) the N1 velocity, from a single 0.75 mm electrode distance, was not significantly different from sham (n 8 to 10 mice per group, per the Figure 4 legend) while N1 area was lower and the N1 half-width was also lower (stated P < 0.001 at both levels, 1 mA stimulus; a timing change, so conduction is not shown unchanged); a brief therefore names which compound-action-potential component it measures and reports its amplitude or area together with its velocity and half-width.
