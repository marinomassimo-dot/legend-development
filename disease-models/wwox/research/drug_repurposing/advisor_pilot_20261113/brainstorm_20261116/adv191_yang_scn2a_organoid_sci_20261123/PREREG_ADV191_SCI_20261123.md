## 2. The quantity the criterion needs (fixed before reading)

A genotype-dependent drug response is inferable only from this quantity: for each readout (mean firing rate, spike count, burst or network-burst rate, synchrony index), the within-unit change after drug relative to its own vehicle or pre-drug baseline, on the same replication unit, compared BETWEEN genotypes against the matched edited control on the same background, with a test of that between-group difference (genotype by treatment term or a direct contrast of the two responses). Replication unit = independent differentiation (batch) nested in clone; organoids, wells and electrodes are pseudoreplicates unless the source shows otherwise. "Significant in one genotype and not in the other" is not an interpretable contrast and counts as NONE.

Three things the criterion must separate: (i) normalisation toward matched control, (ii) suppression in all backgrounds including the control, (iii) an apparent change from a floor or from different starting levels. Spiking, synchrony and network bursts are scored separately.
