## 2. Per-agent summary (what is established, from how many laboratories, what was NOT found; routes tried)

Routes: EPMC-A = Europe PMC field-tagged agent + age words + seizure words; EPMC-B = Europe PMC different assay/age wording (search_*.json); PM = PubMed esearch (pm_*.xml). Query URLs are in raw/*.url.

**(a) valproate.** In rat PTZ/metrazol seizures valproate is anticonvulsant at every age from P5-P7 upward (Prague 1981, 1987, 1989; no age at which it is proconvulsant against PTZ). Qualifications from the same Prague family: duration of protection is shorter at P7 (1989); against NMDA-induced seizures it protects at P12 but not P25 and raises clonic-seizure incidence at P25 (Kubova 2010, paradoxical); modestly less effective on cortical afterdischarges at P12 (Polasek 1996); anticonvulsant in P21 heat-stress seizures (Palmer 1998, other group). Independent groups: Prague (several papers, one family), Palmer et al. (1). Mouse P7-P28 provoked-seizure data: NOT FOUND. In vitro, valproate worsened seizure-like activity in P3-P5 rat slices (Wahab 2011; out of the P7 window and in vitro). Routes: EPMC-A (a1, a2, a3), PM (vpa). The 2024 mouse 6-Hz paper (38214837) compares young-adult vs middle-aged mice, outside the window.

**(b) NMDA antagonists.** MK-801: anticonvulsant against PTZ generalized tonic-clonic seizures at all ages P7-P90 (Velisek 1991) but at P12 it raised minimal-seizure incidence; in flurothyl, clonic seizures protected only at P9 and P60 at the top dose and at P15 at the low dose, tonic-clonic at all ages (Velisek 1995; authors state age-specific effects); mouse PTZ latency prolonged at P7 and P21 (Tsuda 1997); hyperthermia seizures protected (Morimoto 1995, ages not in abstract); P21 heat stress reduced (Palmer 1998). Ketamine: P15 rat kindling strongly suppressed (Trommer 1990); hippocampal afterdischarges age-dependent, duration effect nearly absent at P18 (Mikolasova 1994); intranigral ketamine ineffective at P16 vs adult in flurothyl (Wurpel 1992). No systemic ketamine PTZ/MES ontogeny paper found. Memantine: tonic-clonic PTZ seizures suppressed at P7-P25, minimal seizures potentiated (Mares 2009); P9 rat NMDA-induced convulsions delayed (Dhir 2015). Competitive antagonists: CGP 40116 only quantitative change P7-P90 (Haugvicova 1998); CGP 39551 needs more drug in younger rats (Velisek 1997); AP7/CGP 40116/MK-801 have different developmental profiles on afterdischarges (Slamberova 2005); CPP, AP5, CGS19755, MK-801 block NMDA convulsions at P7-11 (Schoepp 1990). Laboratories: Prague (most), Moshe/Velisek-Wurpel (Albert Einstein), Tsuda (Japan), Trommer/Pasternak, Morimoto (Japan), Palmer, Dhir (India), Schoepp. Not found: any P7-P28 MK-801/ketamine paper in MOUSE other than Tsuda 1997; NMDA-antagonist proconvulsant effects are seizure-type-specific, not global. Routes: EPMC-A (b1, b2, b3), EPMC-B (j2), PM (ket).

**(c) GluN2B-selective.** Strongest stated ontogeny. Ifenprodil: anticonvulsant at P7 and P10, not P14 or P21 in mouse PTZ (Tsuda 1997); rat PTZ tonic phase abolished P7-P18 not P25 (Mares 2009); rat afterdischarge pretreatment anticonvulsant at P15 only and PROCONVULSANT at P18 and P25 (Mares 2014, paradoxical). Ro 25-6981 effective at P12, failed at P25 (Mares 2021). Radiprodil largest protection at PN12 in rat PTZ (Auvin 2020; days not itemised). Independent laboratories: Prague (3), Tsuda (Japan), Auvin (Paris). Eliprodil: NOT FOUND in an age-window paper. Radiprodil in juvenile MOUSE provoked-seizure assay: not found (38529699 is a Grin2a mutant audiogenic model with no age comparison in the abstract). Routes: EPMC-A (c1), EPMC-B (c2).

**(d) GABA-A modulators.** Phenobarbital: anticonvulsant against PTZ at P7-P90 in rats (Kubova 1991) with minimal seizures newly appearing at P7 and P12; mouse ischemia seizures: not efficacious at P7, efficacious at P10 and P12 (Kang 2015); rat NMDA seizures: P7 less responsive than P15 (Li 2025); P10 DMCM, P10 flurothyl protected (Kulick 2014; Raol 2009); P7 mouse hypoxia seizure burden reduced (Quinlan 2018). Midazolam and clonazepam: anticonvulsant at all ages P7-P90, quantitative change only (Kubova 1989, 1992); diazepam: only P10 rat data inside a flupirtine paper (Raol 2009), no dedicated diazepam ontogeny paper found. Neurosteroids: ganaxolone anticonvulsant at P12 and P25, more active at P12 (Mares 2010); ganaxolone P15 NMDA spasms (Yum 2014); allopregnanolone P9 (Dhir 2015). Laboratories: Prague, Kang/Kadam group (US), Forcelli/Kulick (US), N'Gouemo, Quinlan/Galanopoulou-type mouse hypoxia, Dhir, Velisek-Moshe, Yum. Not found: any paper reporting a CLEAR proconvulsant GABA-A modulator effect at P7-P28 in a provoked in vivo assay beyond the seizure-type effects listed; one explicit P7 failure (Kang 2015). Routes: EPMC-A (d1, d2), EPMC-B (j1, j3), PM (dz).

**(e) Kv7.** Retigabine anticonvulsant in rat PTZ at P7, P14 and P25 with age-dependent potency (Forcelli 2012); kindling at P14, P21, P35 with antiepileptogenic effect at P14 and P21 but not P35 (Mazarati 2008). Flupirtine anticonvulsant at P10 in kainate and flurothyl (Raol 2009), P10 hyperthermia (Yu 2011) and neonatal hypoxia (Sampath 2015; days not in abstract). Laboratories: Forcelli (Georgetown), Mazarati (UCLA), Raol (Johns Hopkins), Yu (China), Sampath. ICA-069673: NOT FOUND in an age-window provoked-seizure paper. XE991: NOT FOUND in vivo at P7-P28; only in vitro linopirdine (Pena 2006: epileptiform activity only in neonatal rat slices) and zebrafish. Routes: EPMC-A (e1), EPMC-B (e2), PM (xe).

**(f) lithium / GSK-3 inhibitors.** NO age-window provoked-seizure paper found. Hits were lithium-pilocarpine models (lithium as sensitizer, not the test agent) and adult-mouse Chir99021 in intrahippocampal kainate (38522659, no age comparison). TCS2002, SB216763, AR-A014418, kenpaullone: nothing. Routes: EPMC-A (f1), EPMC-B (f2, k1, k2), PM (li).

**(g) T-type etc.** Zonisamide (mixed mechanism): selective tonic-phase suppression at P7-P90 with no age difference (Mares 2010). Z944, TTA-A2, mibefradil: NOT FOUND in age-window seizure papers. Cav3.2 paper (Sack 2025) is on dentate granule cell excitability, not an anticonvulsant assay. One laboratory family only. Routes: EPMC-A (g1), PM (t).

**(h) sodium-channel blockers.** Phenytoin and lamotrigine: no change of PTZ action across development (Stankova 1992); phenytoin in flurothyl anticonvulsant against tonic-clonic only in the adult (Velisek 1995). Carbamazepine: efficacy rises with age in PTZ and bicuculline at P10, P18, P25 (de Feo 1991, Italian group) and at P7/P12 lower doses increase minimal seizures (Kubova 1993). Lacosamide effective in neonatal mouse kainate seizures (Langton 2022), no age contrast in vivo. Laboratories: Prague, Velisek-Moshe, de Feo (Rome), Langton. Routes: EPMC-A (h1), PM (na).

**(i) CBD, fenfluramine, stiripentol.** CBD: P7 vs P21 rats, PTZ onset delay only at P21, MES duration reduced at both, authors state efficacy increases with age (Witherspoon 2022); P12 rats PTZ tonic phase abolished at the high dose, NMDA ineffective (Uttl 2021, Prague-linked); cannabidivarin (analogue) P10 PTZ only, P20 PTZ + DMCM + MES (Huizenga 2019). Stiripentol more effective at P21 than P75 (Auvin 2013). Fenfluramine: NOT FOUND in an acute provoked-seizure age comparison (hit 38018342 is chronic dosing in Scn1a+/- mice, survival and pathology). Routes: EPMC-A (i1, i2), PM (fen).
