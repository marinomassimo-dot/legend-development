2. **Three results change the design, not just the table.** (a) **Seizure-type specificity**: several agents (MK-801 at P12, memantine, phenobarbital at P7 and P12, carbamazepine at P7 and P12) **increase minimal seizures while suppressing generalised tonic-clonic seizures** at young ages, and ketamine, like the other NMDAR antagonists, acts on generalised tonic-clonic seizures with a moderate or absent effect on clonic (minimal) seizures. Cheng's wild-type pups sit at a stage of about 1 on the 0 to 6 scale, i.e. at minimal-seizure level, and a mean stage mixes the two directions. (b) **The GluN2B class loses its effect across exactly the window** (mouse P14 and P21: none), which matches the held NMDAR-composition numbers (ifenprodil-sensitive fraction 14% at P13 to P15 against 53% at P6 to P8, WT barrel cortex, one study, from the brief). (c) **Lithium, the one agent measured in a Wwox pup, has no age-window literature** in rodents by two routes, so its effect at other ages cannot be borrowed from elsewhere.
3. **What it does to the arm order of ADV-051 and to the wild-type age series.**
   * **Ethosuximide first stays the right first arm only if the age series comes with it**: the mouse ontogeny is unresolved (HE), the rat floor cannot be carried over, and "young-age effect plus floor" stays UNRESOLVED, not a finding.
   * **The age series should carry more than ethosuximide: use reference agents with documented different age profiles as an assay-sensitivity panel in wild-type pups** (INFERENZA from the table): **valproate** as the flat-profile agent (PTZ protection at all ages in the rat; mouse unknown, so it is itself a check), one agent with a **rising** profile (carbamazepine in PTZ and bicuculline, or CBD) and, if an NMDAR arm is included, one with a **falling** profile (ifenprodil, Ro 25-6981). A null for ethosuximide at an age where valproate and the rising agent work is interpretable; a null at an age where the assay cannot show any drug effect is not.
   * **Report seizure type, not only a mean stage**: per-animal incidence of minimal (stage 1 to 2) and generalised (stage 4 to 5, tonic) seizures, because the agents above move them in opposite directions at young ages (a mean can cancel).
   * **Lithium** stays in the first session as the positive-control-like arm; it is the one agent with a measured effect in a Wwox pup, but with no age information it cannot calibrate the age series.
   * **NMDAR pore-blocker in vivo arm (last)**: ketamine is expected (rat) to suppress generalised tonic-clonic seizures at these ages with little effect on minimal ones, and memantine and MK-801 may **raise** minimal ones; in all cases the pup is ataxic at low exposure, so the arm's readout must separate seizure types and carry a motor control, or it can show "no change" or "worse" for opposite reasons. This strengthens "last, with harms and stopping rules written first".
   * **GluN2B probe in vivo**: informative only at the youngest ages (P7 to P12 in the rat data); a Wwox-null seizure experiment at P12 and later would sit where the class is fading.
   * **Valproate as the general comparator** (ADV-051) is supported at the rat level and is the only comparator with PTZ protection documented at every age, but its protection against NMDA-driven seizures reverses at P25: choose the provocation (PTZ) accordingly.
4. **Arm-table cells I5, I9, I10 (ADV).** I5 remyelination-type arm "needs the P10 to P20 window": not touched (no remyelination agent in this table). I9 "PAM `-` only after maturation": **not confirmed for in vivo provoked-seizure endpoints**: GABA-A modulators are anticonvulsant already at P7 to P25 in rat PTZ (midazolam, clonazepam, ganaxolone, phenobarbital from P10 in mouse, not at P7); the maturation remark concerns the slice and the PV network, which this table does not address. I10 "in vivo level only": T-type `-` for sleep-clustered events, PAM `+` or `0`: **no T-type age datum exists beyond ethosuximide and zonisamide**, so the T-type cell is age-unknown, and PAMs are expected present (not aggravating in these assays) at the window.
5. **The GluN2B NAM dossier (unit 2): not worth doing now, and I stop the chain after (1) with that reason.** At the ages of the experiments (slice P13 to P17; Wwox pup in vivo from about P12) the class is at or past the point where its effect fades (mouse ifenprodil gone by P14; the held ifenprodil-sensitive fraction 14% at P13 to P15), so the probe is unlikely to remove enough current to discriminate; the cheap decisive step is the measurement the brief already carries (**WO-10**, the NAM-sensitive fraction in burst-age slices), after which a dossier would have a basis. A dossier without that number would describe the class and not the experiment.

## 3. Limits that bear on the use of this table
Abstract level only; strains and ages are as the abstracts give them (many do not itemise days); roughly half the rows come from one laboratory family (Prague, treated as one origin) with its own assay conventions (minimal vs tonic phases, afterdischarges); a rat age profile does not transfer to mouse by default (the one agent with both, ifenprodil, fades earlier in the mouse report: P14 against P18 to P25 in rat afterdischarge pretreatment, different assays); provoked-seizure assays are not the spontaneous seizures of the null pup; "present" means an effect in a provoked assay at that age, not a benefit for the null phenotype; the first in vivo experiment will use its own stimulus and scale. ## Dated correction (2026-11-18, SCI, after the ADV-064 blind re-search; `adv064_not_found_recheck_sci_20261118/`; nothing above is edited)
The statement "diazepam: only a P10 rat datum, no ontogeny paper" (the GABA-A row, section 1 and the not-found list) is **overturned**: a blind re-search found three diazepam papers with an explicit age comparison (rat status epilepticus not stopped at P9 and stopped at P15 and P21, Hasson 2008; rat P15 against P28, Suchomelova 2006; mouse febrile seizures, low dose anticonvulsant at P11 and high dose **exacerbating** the induction phase at P11 but not at P14, with a bumetanide-sensitive component, Kasahara 2019) and three single-age papers. Diazepam is therefore age-dependent, not flat, and a benzodiazepine can be paradoxical at P11 in the mouse. Fenfluramine: one additional Scn1a+/- rat hyperthermia-seizure paper (Qian 2026) with the age not stated in the abstract. Z944, TTA-A2, mibefradil, ICA-069673, XE991 in vivo, eliprodil and other selective Cav3 blockers: confirmed not found with a stated age (two in vivo XE991 papers and one TTA-A2 electroshock paper exist with no age in the abstract).
