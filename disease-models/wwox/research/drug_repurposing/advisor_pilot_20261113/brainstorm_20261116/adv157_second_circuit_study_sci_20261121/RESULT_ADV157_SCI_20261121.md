# ADV-157 result: no second independent Wwox circuit study; fallback on mouse cortical interneuron classes (SCI window X, 2026-11-21; hypothesis level; no dose; no contact; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

Pre-registration: `PREREGISTRATION_ADV157_SECOND_CIRCUIT_STUDY_SCI_20261121.md` (commit `b990b15f4`) and `ADDENDUM1_ROUTE_RESULTS_AND_FALLBACK_PREREG_ADV157_SCI_20261121.md` (commit `d0bdb4b5f`, predictions P5 to P8 fixed before any gene value was opened). Reproduction: `wmg_mouse_wwox.py` (stdlib) and its output `WMG_MOUSE_WWOX_DETECTION_ADV157_SCI_20261121.tsv`. Reading rules (Codex return 01, adopted by ADV): the atlas is read at its ACTUAL unit, the fraction of cells above 0 per cell type; there is no animal-level replication and no depth adjustment; a pooled detection contrast can flag a discrepancy and is neither replication nor evidence that a subtype mediates seizures.

## Answer first
1. **Part 1 (the search): none.** No study meets all five pre-registered criteria (independent laboratory; allele and age stated; at least 3 biological units; a comparable burst interval, duration or a measurable inhibitory phenotype; obtainable). The nearest independent studies fail on measure or allele: the P47T video-EEG (Aldaz with Noebels; n = 3 per group; partial loss of function; adult; representative traces, no per-animal numbers) and the lde rat (Suzuki; null allele; audiogenic seizures in 95 percent; EEG described qualitatively; full text not held). No public deposit of Wwox electrophysiology exists on the routes tried (Zenodo, figshare, DANDI). **Breton 2021 is not independent of the Aqeilan laboratory** (shared neuronal conditional line, Repudi and Aqeilan authors), so the held interval 26.2 s and duration 0.66 s share a mouse line and a laboratory with the organoid papers. Details and routes: addendum 1.
2. **Part 2 (fallback), at the unit measured: Wwox transcript is detected in mouse cortical interneuron classes at fractions at or above those of glutamatergic neuron classes, in every dataset where the comparison is informative.** Pooled over all mouse brain datasets of the portal (47,108 to 63,284 cells per interneuron class in total, 12,579 to 25,018 of them expressing): Pvalb 0.531, Sst 0.333, Lamp5 0.272, Vip 0.226 against glutamatergic classes 0.093 to 0.355 (median 0.200). Within single datasets (which removes the mixing of technologies): Allen isocortex 10x, Pvalb 0.466, Sst 0.294, Lamp5 0.252, Vip 0.222 against a glutamatergic median 0.122; snRNA of mouse M2 cortex, Pvalb 0.815, Sst 0.670, Lamp5 0.587, Vip 0.446 against a median 0.306. **The pre-registered rejecting observation (any interneuron class below half of the glutamatergic median) is not met anywhere.**
3. **It is a detection fraction, not a level of function, and the unit limits every comparison:** cells above 0 in a count matrix, aggregated over animals and datasets; no animal-level replication; no depth adjustment (detection rises with depth, as ADV-147 showed in the human data); interneuron nuclei and cells from these datasets may carry more transcripts per cell, so the ratio 1.1 to 3.8 is an upper bound on a depth-free contrast, not an estimate of one. It does not show that any interneuron class mediates seizures.

## DREAM (taken because it changes what may be written, not a decision)
* **Concordance with the earlier readings, with species, stage and aggregation explicit.** The repository's earlier statement (`analysis/wwox_subfield_celltype_expression_20260922.md`, mouse adult, DropViz via a review, second hand: interneurons not below excitatory) is concordant with this primary mouse adult result in direction. The human fetal result (ADV-147: inhibitory neurons 9.4 percent against excitatory 10.8 percent crude, depth-stratified ratio 1.49, interval 1.07 to 2.06) is the same direction at a lower magnitude; the mouse ratios here are larger (1.1 to 3.8), and the two are not comparable in size (species, adult against fetal, nuclei against cells, a different aggregation, unadjusted depth here).
* **Traceable consequence, kept small:** for RP-042 (INV-15) and RP-019: no ranking changes; wording that may be added where the cell-class premise is carried: 'Wwox transcript is detected in mouse cortical interneuron classes (Pvalb highest, Vip lowest of the four) at fractions at or above glutamatergic classes in a public atlas; detection fraction only, no depth adjustment, no animal-level replication; it neither supports nor opposes an interneuron-mediated mechanism'. A weak connection otherwise: the claim that inhibitory deletion causes seizures (AES 1.019) is not contradicted by expression.
* **Lead, not a result:** oligodendrocyte and OPC detection fractions in the snRNA set (0.45 and 0.52) are as high as or higher than in glutamatergic neurons, consistent with the OPC-intrinsic question of RP-017; no decision depends on it now.

## Blind check
A delegate of a different model (requested `opus`, briefed at effort medium, not read back) re-queried the portal from a description of the API and the cell types by name, without my values. **Every pooled and per-dataset fraction reproduces to the third decimal**, and the glutamatergic medians agree (pooled 0.2000; snRNA 0.3056; Allen 10x 0.1224 from three types only). **It found one error of mine, now fixed:** in the API `aggregated.n` is the number of expressing cells and the cell count is `total_count` in the label block, so the counts in my first table were expressing cells, not cells (the fractions were right; the pre-registered n of at least 200 holds on the true totals, smallest interneuron class 2,127 cells). It also added, and I adopt: (a) the pooled figures still contain motor-cortex cells through the rollup, so per-dataset ratios are the cleaner unit; (b) the order pvalb > sst > lamp5 > vip holds in every usable unit; (c) without the SMART-Seq set the pooled Vip ratio is 0.84 (still above half, so P5 holds, but Vip against glutamatergic is the least stable comparison); (d) the four datasets amount to two sources at most (the thyroid-hormone M2 pair is one publication with manipulated cohorts; the Allen 10x and SMART-Seq pair one atlas), and the SMART-Seq set is degenerate, with its ratios resting on single- or double-digit counts of expressing cells; (e) glia sit at or above the glutamatergic median in the usable datasets.

## Limits
The percentages above are descriptive readings of an API summary; I did not open any per-animal data. The cell-type labels are the portal's; leaf and umbrella types were chosen by name before reading values for the pre-registered classes (the glutamatergic median uses 7 to 8 leaf types; excluding primary-motor-cortex-restricted duplicates). The pooled median differs from a quick first read (0.13) that included duplicates; the verdict does not depend on it.
