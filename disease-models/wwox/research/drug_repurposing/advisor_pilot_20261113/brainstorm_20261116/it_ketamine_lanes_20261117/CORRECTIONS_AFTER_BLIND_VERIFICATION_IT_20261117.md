## Update 2026-11-17 (IT; ADV's second DeVine line, after reading the full-text page; the P2-03 item above is not edited, this completes it)
In the paper's own words: patient 2, FIRST course (hospital day 52): "Seizures ceased within 1 hour of initiation of ketamine in conjunction with midazolam"; the midazolam rate was weaned over 60 hours; infusions continued "until 36 hours without evidence of seizures"; after both were stopped, "on hospital day 56 the patient had signs of clinical seizures and ketamine continuous infusion was reinitiated" (second course); resolution on a spot EEG 15 days after that course ended (patient 2, admission 2). Patient 1: five days of ketamine "without successful control of subclinical seizures", EEG resolution on day 14 after ketamine was weaned, on four maintenance drugs. Patient 3: no resolution, death after withdrawal of care. **What is supported:** a temporal association within one hour in ONE course (with co-infused midazolam, then recurrence off both), resolution after ketamine stopped in two others, one death; the authors' "in 2 of 3 cases seizures resolved with therapy including ketamine" is their reading. My earlier P2-03 sentence ("resolution after ketamine was weaned or stopped") was true for patient 1 and for patient 2's second admission but omitted the first-course sentence. **The pooled prior reads: 0 to 2 attributable DeVine responders, with one course showing a one-hour temporal association under midazolam co-infusion.**
