## 1. What is tested, and a framing correction

ADV's brief names the premise "severity tracks residual protein" (CLAIM 030, CC-20260920-DETECTION-FLOOR-01). CLAIM 030's own title is the opposite emphasis: severity tracks residual protein **function, not abundance**. A line-level table of abundance against an organoid readout therefore cannot confirm or refute CLAIM 030's thesis as written. What it can test is the weaker premise that cards and briefs use when they treat lower WWOX band intensity as a proxy for more severe loss: **"across patient-derived lines, less WWOX protein orders with more organoid hyperexcitability."** That is what is registered here. Reading of outcomes against CLAIM 030: discordance fits its thesis (abundance is not the determinant); concordance does not contradict it (function and abundance may co-vary); neither moves its status.

## 2. Tabulation rules (frozen)

* **Protein** = the number printed under a lane on Supp Fig 1L (WOREE lines) or 1M (SCAR12 lines): author densitometry, HSP90-normalised, relative to the JH wild type on the **same membrane**. A lane with no printed value and no visible band at 46 kDa is recorded "absent by eye (no printed value)". Rescued (AAV9) lanes are listed and never used in the ordering. Week 16 organoids.
* **No cross-membrane or cross-antibody comparison.** An ordering is tested only between two lines on the same blot (1L: WSM S and LM-iPS; 1M: WPM S and WPM D). L against M is not compared; each relative to its own wild-type lane, and the two membranes are different gels.
* **Function** = Fig 1D, week 7, spikes per neuron, per line, 4-minute cell-attached recordings, "collected from one experiment" (n neurons: WT 30, WWOX-KO 30, WPM S 21, WPM D 21, WSM S 24, LM-iPS 14). Values are **read off the raster page** (the accepted-manuscript PDF holds the figure as a 1430-pixel image, no vector drawing); I record each box's median, quartiles and whisker ends with the pixel-to-value resolution stated. Not author data. The week-16 calcium event rate (Fig 6A(ii), S9C) is available **by group only** (WT, WOREE, SCAR12 and arms) and is quoted, not tabulated per line.
* **Age mismatch** (protein week 16, per-line function week 7) is a stated limit of every pair.
* **Ties.** Protein: two lines on one blot are tied if both print a value and the values differ by less than a factor of 2; a line absent by eye against a line with a printed value is distinguishable. Function: two lines are tied if their interquartile ranges overlap, and ordered if disjoint (the unit is the neuron from one experiment, so overlap of quartiles is the conservative rule). Wording fixed before any measurement (editorial correction of the first commit, ec1aefb6a).

## 3. Ordering test and disposition (frozen)

* A **pair** is counted when two altered lines on the same blot are distinguishable on **both** axes. n = the number of such pairs. Wild type against an altered line is reported but is not a counted pair (a two-level contrast fits any monotone or non-monotone relation).
* **supported (n)**: n at least 3, all counted pairs concordant (more protein, lower function).
* **not supported (n)**: at least one counted pair reversed, whatever n.
* **not testable (n)**: otherwise, including every case with n below 3 and all concordant.
