## 1. Answer

1. **Disposition: not testable (n = 0).** Across the patient-derived lines, the premise "less WWOX protein orders with more organoid hyperexcitability" cannot be ordered, because no pair of altered lines on one blot is distinguishable on both axes. The one pair that is distinguishable on protein (WSM S against LM-iPS, same blot) has overlapping interquartile ranges on firing; the other same-blot pair (WPM S against WPM D) is tied on both.
2. **Function sits on a plateau.** At week 7 the five altered lines (WWOX-KO, WPM D, WPM S, WSM S, LM-iPS) have medians of about 335 to 397 spikes per neuron (read off) and overlapping quartiles, against about 95 for wild type. Their protein, on the authors' own scale, runs from a trace or no band (WSM S2, S5) to 0.3. A plateau across that protein range means the week-7 readout does not discriminate among altered lines.
3. **Week 16 exists only by group.** Fig 6A(ii) gives events rate by group: about 0.002 Hz (WT), 0.075 (SCAR12 AAV9-EGFP), 0.26 (WOREE AAV9-EGFP); the text says "about 3-fold". The direction at group level (WT more protein and quietest, WOREE least protein and most active) fits the premise, but it is a three-group, cross-blot comparison, the WOREE group contains LM-iPS whose 0.2 sits inside the SCAR12 range, and no per-line week-16 rate is published, so the 3-fold gap cannot be assigned to a line (P4 met).
4. **Against CLAIM 030.** The claim's own title is *severity tracks residual protein FUNCTION, not abundance*. A not-testable abundance table neither supports nor refutes it, and no status moves. Discordance would have fitted it; none was found, because nothing was orderable. The repository's cards already carry the careful form (for example the needs diff of the P6 recovery cards: "severity tracks residual function, not abundance (CLAIM 030)"); I found no card that treats lower band intensity as more severe loss, so no card needs changing.
5. **Forecast.** RP-045's frozen prediction (P-SCI-1, week-16 per-line organoid event-rate ordering) is untouched and is not informed: the supplement gives per-line protein but no per-line week-16 rate.

## 6. Limits

* Protein and function are 9 weeks apart (week 16 against week 7); every pair carries that.
* One blot per line, no replicates, author densitometry on faint bands; the WT lane of blot 1L is saturated, so values on 1L are not comparable with 1M (never compared) and are upper bounds on their own blot.
* Fig 1D uses neurons from one experiment as the unit; patient lines differ in donor background and have no isogenic control; WWOX-KO is an engineered line on another membrane (its blot, 1K, prints no value for the knockouts).
* The Fig 1D reading is mine, from pixels; WT quartiles carry about plus or minus 15.
* Not done: week-16 per-line event rates (not in the held files; the Fig 6A(ii) data table is not in the supplement) and any per-line protein at week 7 (the week-7 immunostaining in Fig 1B shows no visible WWOX signal in any altered line, and almost none in WT either, so it does not discriminate).
