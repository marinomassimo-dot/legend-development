## Value against more work on the leaders, and the decisions
* **A earns a conditional comparison, and its research priority rises above B, C and D.** Reason: one measurement set answers three open questions at once (the lithium-in-WT confound, the GSK3 arm of ethosuximide's mechanism, the premise tideglusib needs), where another leader package refines a mechanism for one compound. It does not displace the leaders: the ethosuximide arm and the lithium arm of the published figure use the same PTZ challenge, so one protocol serves both. Condition for the slot: the lab has Wwox-null tissue at P14 and P20 with a same-animal PTZ protocol. If that lab dependency holds for weeks, register it as the missing observation and move the slot to B at no cost, because B shares the perampanel slice preparation.
* **B stays a conditional comparison, no change.** Etifoxine stays a probe only: at estimated free exposure the TSPO route has occupancy 0.004 percent; it is an in-vitro question. Ganaxolone is placed above etifoxine for the E_GABA measurement because it needs no precursor premise.
* **C: research priority lowered for the next slot; timed reopening.** The DCA dossier (README, `central_target_20261126.md`, `pediatric_csf_20261126.md`: 0 hits for "35328751", "Baryla", "fibroblast" and "25012504", grep 2026-11-28) does not cite the one held WWOX-depleted human measurement of PDH activity: Baryla 2022 (PMID 35328751), skin fibroblast line 1BR.3.N, partial depletion (mRNA about 3-fold lower), 48 h: lactate higher in all four conditions, PDH activity and citrate synthase not different (Fig 5b, 5e). Limits: non-neural; the kit assay (Methods 4.10) reports mU/mL with no statement that phosphorylation was preserved, so a higher PDK-driven phosphorylation is not excluded; the lab's PDH/PDK record also holds PDK1 transcript lower in sign in the two neural transcript systems (organoid -0.39, 4 against 2 libraries; GSE126075 -0.67, one library per arm), no HIF1A change. Together these remove support for the premise but do not refute it. Reopen on the first of: a PDHA1-phosphorylation or labelled-flux dataset in WWOX-deficient neural material; the portfolio reconciliation of 2026-12-28 with a dated fresh search. DCA stays the reference comparator.
* **D: no change; rides on INV-06.** Bexarotene and montelukast need the conduction and first-sheath readout that the INV-06/RP-049 package is already built to give; no separate slot.
* **Baricitinib:** not reopened (RP-039 negative stands; no per-animal pre-deficit observation exists); the DisMech prompt 1 asks for exactly that observation.
