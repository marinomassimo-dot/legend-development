## Method (fixed)
Processed counts of GSE215425, one analysis per region (CB 7 libraries: WT M3, M2, F5, F4, P47T M6, F8, F10; PFC, HPC, CTX 10 each), TMM factors and log2 CPM exactly as `composition_bound_adv198.py` (functions copied unchanged from `p47t_reanalysis_it12.py`), gene kept if CPM at least 1 in at least 3 libraries of that region, duplicate symbols summed. Per gene: log2FC = mean log2 CPM (P47T) minus mean (WT). Per set and region: **median log2FC over the set's genes that are kept**; number of genes present reported; a set with fewer than 3 kept genes in a region is UNDETERMINED there. Exact two-sided permutation p over genotype labels of the set-median statistic (CB 35 labelings, forebrain 252); leave-one-library-out range of the median. All p values nominal, not corrected.
