Conventions I fixed:
- log2FC = mean(P47T) minus mean(WT) of log2 CPM.
- The kept set and the TMM factors are label-independent, so I fixed them in the permutations.
- A set's median is over its kept genes. With an even number of kept genes it is the mean of the two middle values.
- Permutation p = share of relabelings, the observed one included, with |median| >= |observed| (tolerance 1e-12; a strict comparison gives identical p).

(4) Three things that look wrong, ambiguous or could change a number
1. Tmem2 is not in the counts file, so COMMITTED has 6 of 7 genes in all regions. With six genes the median is the mean of the 3rd and 4th values (CB: Neu4 +0.0843 and Enpp6 +0.2822, mean +0.1832). Any analyst who substitutes an alias, or who treats the gene as present with zero counts, gets a different COMMITTED median. Also, the sterol_core list uses Cyp51 and Msmo1, and the file uses Cyp51 as well. The Hallmark list in the same JSON uses Cyp51a1, which does not exist in this file. That does not affect sterol_core.
2. The CB design is unbalanced and thin. It has 4 WT and 3 P47T libraries, with no M1 or M7 and no F9, so the animal set differs from the other regions. Only 35 relabelings exist, so the smallest attainable two-sided p is 1/35 = 0.0286. All CB p = 0.0286 values are at that floor, not at a graded level. "Same number of P47T libraries" does not allow the swapped 4-versus-3 relabeling, which would change counts if allowed. The CB COMMITTED and MATURE medians depend heavily on WT_M3: dropping it gives +0.028 and +0.304.
3. "LOO" is ambiguous (fixed normalisation versus full recompute), and the TMM reference library is chosen from the 75th percentile of the nonzero fractions, so it changes with the library set. I report both LOO variants above (max difference about 0.012). The kept set and TMM factors are fixed under permutation, which is legitimate because they do not depend on labels. The 107 duplicate symbols (for example Ndor1 with 3 rows) are summed as specified. None of them is in the four sets, but they affect library sums and TMM slightly. Median-ties and floating-point equality in the permutation p were guarded with a 1e-12 tolerance, and the strict comparison gives the same p.
