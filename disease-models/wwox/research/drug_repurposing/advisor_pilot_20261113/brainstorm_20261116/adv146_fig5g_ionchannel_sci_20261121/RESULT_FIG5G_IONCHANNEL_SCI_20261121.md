## Answer first
1. **Fig. 5G does not show subtypes.** Both Fig. 5F and Fig. 5G carry only two categories, early and late. The legend of 5G says "neuronal subtype proportions"; the panel plots the log2 fold change of the early and the late share against WT. **Per-subtype proportions per genotype: NOT PRINTED in the main figure** (the seven subgroups are in Fig. 5B and Supplementary Fig. 8C, supplement not held). Row B cannot be tested at subtype level from held bytes.
2. **Fig. 5G adds no information beyond Fig. 5F.** From 5F (VISUAL: early share WT 0.33, WOREE 0.66, SCAR12 0.68) the 5G values follow arithmetically: expected early log2 +0.99 (WOREE) and +1.03 (SCAR12), late -0.98 and -1.06; measured on the 5G bars +0.99, +1.02, -0.96, -1.03 (all within 0.03). So there is one measurement per genotype, drawn twice. WOREE and SCAR12 differ by about 2 percentage points in early share (0.66 against 0.68), no test printed.
3. **No ion-channel or synaptic gene is named as different between WOREE and SCAR12 neurons.** The only between-patient-genotype gene statement is the AM's "two genes" (EV9C: the two ribosomal genes RPS29 and RPL38; read earlier in ADV-142). The ion-channel genes in the text (SCN2A, SCN1A, KCNA3, KCNA4, SYT1; Fig. 5D) are **late-stage markers** (heat map of correlation with the late category), and the gene sets of Fig. 5E (neurotransmitter secretion, synapse, membrane depolarisation, carbohydrate catabolism) are enriched in late against early cells. Both patient-derived genotypes are early-heavy; that is a statement about composition against WT, not about WOREE against SCAR12.
4. **The one place an ion-channel gene set is compared is within WOREE, treated against untreated (Fig. 6D-E, the AAV9-WWOX rescue):** SynGO sets for voltage-gated and ligand-gated ion-channel activity, neuronal dense-core vesicle and synaptic vesicle membrane are LOWER in the treated neurons (NES about -1.2 to -1.5, VISUAL), translation at the synapse and synaptic ribosome HIGHER (about +1.5); pathway sets OXPHOS, hippo signalling, oligodendrocyte specification and myelin, oxidative stress higher; PPAR-alpha, synaptic vesicle, cholesterol sets lower. Which genes drive the channel sets (leading edge) is **NOT PRINTED**; the text says only "reduced voltage-gated and ligand-gated ion channel expression" after treatment, and that treated organoids "did not affect the RGs". The share of early against late neurons after treatment is **NOT PRINTED** either.
5. **What this does to rows B and G** (competing explanations of ADV-140/142): **B (immature state drives activity) is not strengthened by any held value**: the early share is equal in WOREE and SCAR12 while their week-16 rate differs 3.4-fold (Fig. 6A(ii) EGFP arms), and the channel-gene sets that move with rescue point opposite to a composition account (the late, channel-rich neurons are the minority in untreated WOREE yet its channel sets are higher than after rescue). That is two different comparisons, so it is a tension, not a test: within WOREE the rescue changes ion-channel gene sets while the early share after rescue is not printed. **G (line or allele effect) cannot be tested here**: one scRNA sample per genotype (the text says neurons "appeared in all three organoids analyzed"), the lines pooled, no per-line breakdown for Fig. 5, 6D or 6E.
6. **A text and figure labelling defect, recorded as a reading note:** the AM Results cite "Fig. 5C and Fig. 5D" for the channel genes and pathways, while the printed panels carry the genes in D and the pathways in E; the legend lists F as "log fold change" and G as "subtype proportions" while the printed F is the composition bars and G the log fold change. The Fig. 6 legend labels the gene-set panels "(C-D)" for what the figure prints as D-E. Readers should cite panels from the figure, not the legend.
