## Composition against within-cell: what the pooled data can and cannot separate (ADV's three cautions)
1. **A composition shift is not a joint fall.** In a mixture of fixed cell-type profiles, if a gene set is expressed MORE in late cells, lowering the late fraction lowers the pooled signal and raising it increases it (ADV's check: early = 1, late = 2 gives 1.34 at an early share of 0.66 and 1.67 at 0.33; I reproduced it). So in untreated WOREE (early share 0.66) the pooled channel signal is already the LOW composition value; a rescue that moved neurons toward the late state would RAISE it, yet 6D shows the channel sets LOWER after rescue. Under the stated premise (channel genes higher in late cells, Fig. 5D) that sign is **not what a shift toward late would give**; it would fit a shift toward early or a within-cell fall. But I cannot say which, because (a) the early share after rescue is NOT PRINTED, (b) the 5D premise is a correlation of gene expression with the early or late category across cells, not a per-cell expression level of the channel sets, and (c) 6D is a rank-based gene-set statistic on treated against untreated neurons, not a pooled mean. An unchanged early share would not rule out a within-cell effect either. My earlier wording ("composition account runs opposite") is withdrawn as overstated.
2. **Different measures, no same-gene statement.** Fig. 5D = Pearson-type correlation (colour scale -0.5 to +0.5) of the named genes (SCN2A, BCL11B, KCNA3, KCNA4, SYT1, FEZF2, SCN1A; DCX, EMX2, FOXG1, HES6, PAX6) with the late or early category. Fig. 5E = normalised enrichment score of GO terms, late against early cells. Fig. 6D = SynGO normalised enrichment score of ion-channel and vesicle sets, treated against untreated WOREE. EV9C = per-gene log fold change (two labelled genes). **Gene identity by identifier across the panels: only the 5D genes are named; the 6D sets and EV9C do not list the genes of the channel sets, so whether the 5D channel genes are the genes behind the 6D channel sets is NOT PRINTED. EV9C names RPS29 and RPL38, which are not channel genes; no overlap with the 5D names.**
3. **Across subtypes against within a type.** The data are pooled: one sample per genotype, seven neuron subgroups collapsed into two stages in 5F-G and 6D-E, lines pooled. What cannot be separated: a shift between subtypes (composition) from a change inside a subtype (within-cell), the line from the genotype, and a per-organoid difference from a per-cell one (cells are the unit of the gene-set statistic; organoids are one per genotype). **The observation that would separate them:** the same gene-set statistic computed **within each of the seven subgroups** (or within the late stage alone), in treated against untreated WOREE and in WOREE against SCAR12; if the channel sets still fall within a single subgroup, the effect is within-cell; if they fall only in the pooled data and not within subgroups, composition carries it. That needs the cell-level object (E-MTAB-14792) or Supplementary Fig. 8 and 10, neither held.

## Limits
By-eye or pixel readings of printed bars only; the supplement (Supplementary Fig. 8, Table EV1) is not held; the EV9C reading stands as recorded in ADV-142 (crop cut the x axis, two labelled genes). The scRNA is one organoid per genotype at week 16; the rescue scRNA is "one experiment" with the organoid count not given. Gene-set enrichment is a rank statistic, not an expression level, and does not show whether any single channel differs. Nothing here concerns a person.

## CHOICE
* **Next verifiable question:** is the fall of the ion-channel gene sets after AAV9-WWOX present WITHIN each neuron subgroup (or within the late stage alone), not only in the pooled neurons? This separates a within-cell effect from a composition shift (the pooled early share alone cannot: see the section above, a shift toward the late state would raise, not lower, the pooled channel signal).
* **Rejecting observation:** the gene-set statistic recomputed per subgroup; if the channel sets are not lower in any single subgroup while the pooled statistic is, composition carries the change and the within-cell reading is rejected; if they are lower within subgroups, the composition reading is rejected for that change. The early share after treatment, if printed, adds a second readout but does not decide the question.
* **What unblocks it:** the cell-level object (E-MTAB-14792, access-limited, re-check not before 2026-12-06) or Supplementary Figs. 8 and 10; nothing else on held bytes.
