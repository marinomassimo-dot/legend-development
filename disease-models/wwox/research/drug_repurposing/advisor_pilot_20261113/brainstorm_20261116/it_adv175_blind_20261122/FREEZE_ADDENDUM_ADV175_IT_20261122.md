### Headline estimate and interval (addendum)
Headline: **AUC(engine) minus AUC(B1) on the same eligible rows**, point estimate, number of eligible rows and groups, number of positive rows and groups, and a **paired** percentile interval from a cluster bootstrap that resamples alias groups with their rows (2000 resamples, both AUCs recomputed on every resample, ties by midranks); a 95 percent interval and a 90 percent interval both stated. If HE finds a better honest interval after inspecting the inputs (few positives per cluster, many ties in B1), HE freezes it before the reveal. Recognition stratum reported separately; sensitivity without its classes by HE. Useful gap for reading: **0.10 AUC** (the smallest engine-over-baseline gap that would change a choice between a class prior and a reasoned selection on an assay like this); ADV accepted 0.10 as the PRESPECIFIED value; it is not restated after this freeze. The 0.15 quoted earlier is a crude design value, not a bound on the paired contrast.
Reading (ADV's rule): if the paired interval spans zero and the useful gap: "incremental lift unresolved in this diagnostic"; if its upper end is below the useful gap: "that gap was excluded on this diagnostic" with interval and scope; a clearly positive interval: an adjacent-assay signal to replicate, not an engine-wide or WWOX statement. Not a rescue, efficacy or WWOX claim.
