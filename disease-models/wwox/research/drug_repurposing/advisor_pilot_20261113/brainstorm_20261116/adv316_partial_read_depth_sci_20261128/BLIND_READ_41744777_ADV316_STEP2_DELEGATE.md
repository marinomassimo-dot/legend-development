Notes:
- Two internal contradictions in the paper: the Results (3.4) say "AAV9-16 and AAV9-DM being reduced to a lesser degree" in brain, and the Discussion says AAV9-DM is "similar to AAV9-16, higher than AAV9-HR". The Highlights say "biodistribution similar to AAV9" and the abstract says "similar". Fig 4D shows AAV9-DM significantly lower than AAV9 (*), and by eye AAV9-HR is ~180-fold lower. A reader should not take "similar biodistribution" as the paper's brain result.
- Heart (Fig 4B): AAV9-DM is lower, by eye ~1.3e8 to ~8e5 (ratio ~160).
- Spinal cord (Fig 4E): AAV9-DM is not significant, but by eye ~30-fold lower.
- Gastrocnemius (Fig 4C): n=3, AAV9-16 and AAV9-HR undetectable.
- Fig 4A and 4D: values read by eye from log axes, accuracy about ±30%. The authors' 127 is the only stated ratio.
- Packaging differences were not carried into dosing: all groups got the same 1e11 vg, so the signal drop is not a dosing artefact.
- The paper's own "lower dose" claim is a Highlights implication only. No immune or dose-response data exist.
