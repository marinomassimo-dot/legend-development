## A. PMID 41744777 (Nabakowski 2026, mouse AAV9 capsid variants; WWOX count 0)
Study frame the sentences omit: female 8-week C57BL/6 mice, tail vein, 1e11 vg per animal, one dose; capsids AAV9, AAV9-16, AAV9-HR, AAV9-DM; CBA-Fluc cassette; imaging n = 5, tissue qPCR n = 3-5; mean ± SEM.
1. **PAPER 228 Role, "double-mutant AAV9-DM":** the XML has no "double mutant" or "two mutations" (SCI text search, window ba) and says "five point mutations". Replace with "AAV9-DM (five point mutations)" or the paper's own label.
2. **"hepatic vector genome copies ~127-fold":** unit is vg per ug liver DNA at week 9 (a concentration, not total liver genomes); mouse; one dose. Fig 4A by eye: AAV9 about 1.2e8, AAV9-DM about 9.5e5 (about 125), consistent with the authors' 127.
3. **"~8-fold worse packaging":** Fig 1B prints AAV9-DM 4.53e12 (ratio 7.90); the text prints 4.5e12 (ratio 7.96); one production run from 1.6e9 HEK293 cells, no error bar.
4. **"peak 7.3e7 to 5.8e5 (a factor of 126)":** printed only in Results 3.3 text, in no panel; the statistic is not stated; the ROI is "the ventral side of the animal" and the text cites "Figure 2". Fig 3 liver means (by eye) are about 1.7e7 and 1e5 at week 3 (ratio about 170): Fig 3 does not reproduce the text peaks. Say "text-stated".
5. **"fewer brain vector genomes" / CLAIM 047 clause "a liver-de-targeted capsid costs ~8-fold packaging and brain exposure":** the ~8-fold is packaging only. Brain vg per ug DNA is about 60-fold lower (Fig 4D, by eye: AAV9 about 4.5e7, AAV9-DM about 7e5; one star, p <= 0.05; SCI checked on the image). Rewrite so the two numbers cannot be read as one: "packaging ~8-fold lower; brain vg about 60-fold lower". The other clause of that sentence (primate brain 1-4 vg per DG, sublinear scaling) is NOT THIS PAPER: it comes from another source and was only cut from a multi-paper claim.
6. **Brain RNA and "similar biodistribution":** Fig 5D shows no brain Fluc RNA difference from AAV9 while Fig 4D shows brain DNA lower; the abstract and Highlights say "similar biodistribution". Heart (Fig 4B) is de-targeted as much as liver (about 160-fold by eye); liver RNA (Fig 5A) falls about 28-fold, not 127 (first reader, by eye). A "liver-specific" reading is not what the paper measured.
7. **Supported (no change):** no toxicity endpoint of any kind (Methods 2.1-2.6; one dose); WWOX appears zero times.

## B. PMID 42395553 (Petrozziello preprint, HD cortical neurons, rWWOX)
1. **"not disease-specific on these data":** rests on a non-significant interaction (treatment x genotype F(1,28) = 0.3129, p = 0.5804). The genotype main effect (F(1,28) = 6.181, p = 0.0192) is real; the 65Q vehicle bar is already about 7 percent below the 27Q vehicle bar (by eye).
2. **Dose, window and unit:** "100 ng/mL" with the 24 h window (stated only in Results text, not in the Fig 4 legend); one dose, one time; readout is MTT raw OD (the neuron MTT assay is named only by the axis label; Methods describe MTT for SH-SY5Y). The fractions 0.71 and 0.64 are ratios of raw OD read by eye (0.70 and 0.64 on hand reading), not normalised values in the source.
3. **n unit unresolved:** Fig 4B states F(1,28) only (eight per cell if balanced); Fig 4A states n = 4 biological replicates; the replicate unit is not defined.
4. **"raising it":** WWOX level or uptake in treated neurons is not measured (exogenous protein, Pepmic, applied to the medium); "Increases in WWOX induce neuronal toxicity" is the authors' inference. "Three sources now say" and "outside the vector setting" are NOT THIS PAPER.
5. **Silence, not a stated negative:** no heat-denatured or endotoxin control, vehicle and purity not stated (source silent).
6. **Comparison line:** at 100 ng/mL, 24 h, SH-SY5Y falls to about 0.32 of vehicle (by eye) against about 0.64-0.70 in neurons: the same dose and time is much less toxic in neurons.
7. **Supported:** the SH-SY5Y dose-time series (Supplementary Figure 2: 24 h significant at 100 and 500 ng/mL; 48 h at 10, 100, 500) and the statement that 100 ng/mL is the lowest dose with a significant 24 h loss. Figures 1, 2 and Supplementary Figure 1 do not bear on any sentence (first reader).
