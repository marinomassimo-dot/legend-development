# ADV-161 result: does the thyroid-dependent maturation programme move the sterol transcript set? (SCI successor Y, 2026-11-21; hypothesis level; ADJACENT evidence (adult mouse frontal cortex, systemic T3); no dose; no contact; no e-mail address to any service; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

Pre-registration: `PREREGISTRATION_ADV161_SCI_20261121.md` (commit `ec00191bb`; gene sets A, B, C, T, named states and four decision rules fixed before any gene row of Hochbaum Table S1 or S2 was read). Reproduction: `xlsx_min.py`, `hochbaum_sets.py` (output `HOCHBAUM_SETS_OUTPUT_ADV161_SCI_20261121.txt`), `wmg_hochbaum_coverage.py` (output `WMG_HOCHBAUM_SET_COVERAGE_ADV161_SCI_20261121.tsv`), `POSTHOC_VIEW_ADV161_SCI_20261121.txt`.

## Answer first
1. **By the pre-registered rule 3 the sterol set was NOT moved by T3 in any of the three named oligodendrocyte-lineage states** (OPC, Oligo.Enpp6_2, Oligo.Opalin_2): 0 of 19 core cholesterol-synthesis genes and 0 of 7 regulator genes appear in Table S1 for those states, while the T3 responsiveness controls do (Klf9 and Ier5 in OPC; Klf9 and Hr in Oligo.Opalin_2) and the maturation baseline does (Bcas1 and Ugt8a in OPC; Mbp and Enpp6 in Oligo.Enpp6_2; Bcas1 in Oligo.Opalin_2). P1 and P2 held; the rule-3 pattern (S) is met.
2. **That absence is weak evidence, and I do not read it as "T3 leaves the sterol set alone".** Table S1 is a thresholded list (adjusted P below 0.05), and in these nuclei the sterol genes are barely detected: median fraction of nuclei with any counts is 1.6 percent (OPC) and 4.5 percent (oligodendrocytes) for the 19 core genes, none at or above 20 percent, against 70 percent for the myelin baseline in oligodendrocytes (CELLxGENE WMG, T3 and vehicle nuclei pooled; coverage only). The control Hr is itself detected in 0.9 to 3.5 percent and was listed in only one of the three states. A single-nucleus sterol readout in oligodendrocyte-lineage cells therefore has little power to show a T3 effect, with this design (n = 4 animals per condition, a pseudocell test).
3. **One unplanned observation where the genes are detectable (descriptive, post-hoc to the named states):** in the astrocyte state Astro.Aqp4_Gfap (970 nuclei) T3 lowers two core rate-limiting genes, Hmgcr (log FC -0.59, adjusted P 0.0018, robustness -0.875) and Hmgcs1 (-0.35, 0.040, -0.625), in the same state that carries Klf9 and Thrb as T3 targets; no set-A gene is listed in the other astrocyte state (10,771 nuclei) or in neurons. About 0.06 core genes would be expected by chance among 40 listed genes (two or more: about 2 in 1000 for one state, about 4 percent across 21 states). A sterol shift in a tissue or a mixed single-nucleus table can therefore be moved by a thyroid-type signal in an astrocyte state.
4. **Decision for ADV (the question's two parts).** (a) The sterol score cannot be promoted to a discriminator between the ISR-dependent glial-maturation route and a neuron-dependent or ISR-independent route on the strength of this dataset; the dataset does not vary the ISR at all, it only shows that a thyroid-type perturbation did not produce a listed sterol change in oligodendrocyte-lineage nuclei, at low detection. Keep it as an **exploratory marker**, read within a named glial state (the ADV-160 caveat), not as the separator. (b) A neighbouring-programme comparator is needed beside it **only where INV-12 reads a sterol transcript in a preparation that contains astrocytes**; in the neuron plus precursor-cell co-culture the INV-12 row describes, the vehicle arm of the agonist experiment already is that comparator, and the better partner for the sterol readout is the maturation baseline read within state (item 5). No new assignment follows.
5. **The maturation baseline is not unidirectional under T3**: in OPC and Oligo.Opalin_2 Bcas1 rises (+0.33, +0.24) and in OPC Ugt8a rises (+0.46), but in the immature oligodendrocyte state Oligo.Enpp6_2 (428 nuclei) Mbp falls (log FC -1.69, robustness -1.0) and Enpp6 falls (-0.74). A "simple maturation-marker" comparator must be read per state; a pooled marker score would cancel.

## Design facts that bound every reading
* Cohorts: Table S1 is the endogenous cohort only (adult males, T3 for 2.5 days, n = 4 animals per condition, M2 frontal cortex). The receptor-perturbation cohort (DN-THR and WT-THR, 2 to 3 mice per genotype, Cre-positive neurons only, robustness scores not calculated by the authors) has no compact table in the supplement: **NOT scored**, and the two cohorts were never pooled. Glia do not carry the construct (neuron-specific promoter), so that cohort cannot say whether a glial change is neuron-dependent; this is the question INV-06 needs, and it stays unanswered here.
* Unit: the authors' test unit is the pseudocell (about 30 nuclei per sample); the animal enters through `robScore_logFC` (fraction of sample pairs, T3 against vehicle, agreeing in direction; a TRG needs adjusted P below 0.05 and absolute robustness of at least 0.5). I report their numbers and recomputed no animal-level p. Every listed row passes the TRG definition.
* Missing means "not listed": a gene absent from a thresholded table is not a zero. Detection (`pc`, WMG) and expression (`log FC`, authors) are kept in separate columns and never mixed.
* The WMG detection figures pool T3 and vehicle nuclei and are a coverage check, not a contrast (dataset total 107,600 nuclei, close to the 107,742 reported (142 fewer)).

## What INV-06 and INV-12 should say (proposed wording for IT, not edited here)
"Specificity of the sterol readout (adjacent evidence, Hochbaum 2024, adult mouse frontal cortex, systemic T3, n = 4 animals per condition): a thyroid-hormone perturbation listed none of 26 sterol-synthesis and SREBP2-related genes in OPC or oligodendrocyte states while it moved the maturation baseline in a state-dependent direction (Bcas1, Ugt8a up in OPC; Mbp, Enpp6 down in the immature state); the sterol genes are detected in only about 2 to 5 percent of these nuclei, so the absence is coverage-limited, and T3 did lower Hmgcr and Hmgcs1 in one astrocyte state. The sterol transcript therefore stays an exploratory marker: read it within a named glial state, beside the maturation markers read per state and, where the preparation contains astrocytes, with the astrocyte contribution separated. It does not by itself separate an ISR-dependent from a neuron-dependent or ISR-independent route, and an adult cortex result does not transfer to neonatal WWOX."

## CHOICE
* **Result against the decision it was meant to change:** the sterol score is demoted from "possible separator" to "exploratory marker read within state"; no neighbouring comparator is added except the astrocyte separation above. This is a narrowing, not a reversal of ADV-160 (the chronic directional readout in a locked-ISR model stands as adjacent evidence).
* **Better comparator or next reachable question:** a per-animal, per-state sterol-module score from the single-nucleus data itself (raw counts or the annotated per-cell collection, a multi-gigabyte route; not done, out of the stated limits) would give the module-level power that a per-gene thresholded table cannot; it would also let one read set A inside Oligo.Enpp6_2 and in the astrocyte states at animal level. It has no owner and no consumer beyond this wording, so I do not propose it as a task.
* **Rejecting observation for this reading:** a module-level, per-animal analysis showing set-A movement under T3 in OPC or oligodendrocyte nuclei would reverse item 1 and rule 3; the same analysis showing no astrocyte-state movement would remove item 3.
* **DREAM kept (concrete):** a marker's specificity can be tested by a second perturbation that can produce it, but the test needs the marker to be detectable in the cell type of interest; a snRNA sterol readout in oligodendrocyte-lineage nuclei is detection-limited (1 to 5 percent of nuclei per gene), which is a property of the readout in this tissue and platform, not a finding about T3. This ties the ADV-157 atlas lead (detection of Wwox in oligodendrocyte-lineage cells) to the same limit: a transcript that is detected in few nuclei cannot carry a per-cell contrast.
* **Consumer:** IT (INV-06 and INV-12 wording), through ADV. If IT does not carry it, the result lapses.

## Limits
Adult male frontal cortex, systemic T3 2.5 days, n = 4 per condition; adjacent evidence only; thresholded table (no statistics for unlisted genes); pseudocell test; detection figures pool both conditions; the receptor-perturbation cohort not scored; no blind delegate was used for this short pass (two independent routes confirmed the key numbers: a second raw-XML read of Table S1 for the Hmgcr, Hmgcs1 rows and the per-state row counts, and a single-gene WMG query for Hmgcr and Hr); the astrocyte observation is post-hoc to the named states; no ranking delta from abundance, no assay recommendation, no treatment claim.
