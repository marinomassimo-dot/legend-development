# Cluster 2 note: the clinical-data project as the default first step (SCI, ADV-091 item 3, 2026-11-19; hypothesis level)

Hypothesis level. Reference genotype class only. No dose, schedule or recommendation for any agent in any person. Not medical advice. No personal or individual-level information is used or recorded.

**The note.** For the conservative cluster (ethosuximide with the wild-type age series, a Kv7-class agent, a ketogenic or glucose-tied dietary comparison, and the antiseizure responses in the published clinical series), the default first step is a **clinical-data project**: reading the eight published clinical series of the supply sheet (rows 4 to 11 of `supply_sheet_adv087_sci_20261119/SUPPLY_SHEET_FOR_THE_HUMAN_VISIT_SCI_20261119.md`) and the aggregate clinical questions they could answer (onset age, seizure types, drug response, severity by genotype class, how early the disease shows). It answers what no in vitro or in vivo study of the shared assay can: what is already recorded in people with this genotype class about benefit, which is the unknown of the cluster. **Its first step costs no money.** It waits on **access, not money**: none of the eight texts is held (no open copy was found; all are on the library route of the supply sheet, and the machine routes tested on 2026-11-19 were refused or returned a restricted stub), so it starts the day the supply arrives (decision page item 14, owner: the operator through ADV; the unblock marker of the mandate record is the step L file `checkpoint_sci_20261119/L_TEXT_ARRIVED.txt`). Until then nothing in the project may be written as read: a series read only by its abstract is an abstract, not a reading. Two limits stay in the note for any later use: the eight series are case-level or small-cohort reports, so drug response in them is anecdote without a comparator, and publication favours reported responses; and a clinical series can describe what was given and what followed, never what a drug would do in a person now.
