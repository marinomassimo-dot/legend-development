## Answer first
1. **Premise: narrowed, not confirmed as worded.** "A recorded effect beyond two single-child reports" is answered **no**: the record holds **two single-child reports of response** (one nitrazepam, one clobazam as an add-on) and **no class-level effect**, but the primary reports state three things the nine A1 rows do not: **one adverse-event statement** (nitrazepam), **one acute abortive effect** (intravenous diazepam on a non-convulsive status), and **one sentence by the authors of a pooled series that no single medication is particularly effective**. None of these is a rate, none has a denominator.
2. **By class, after reading (7 patient groups behind the 9 rows):** response reported, single child: **2** groups (1 nitrazepam, 1 clobazam); regimen-level non-control, agent not separable: **4** groups (of which **1** states "ineffective" for clonazepam and clobazam by name, in one sentence listing seven agents); co-therapy with no outcome stated: **1** group. IT's labels on the rows were 2 / 4 / 3 rows (response / not effective / co-therapy only); the difference is one row and the row-versus-group counting (A1-18 and A1-19 are one group; A1-22 and A1-23 are one group).
3. **One reclassification from the primary:** the row for PMID 30361190 (A1-03) is labelled "co-therapy; outcome not stated". The paper states a **regimen outcome**: at the later evaluation, spasms were still occurring in clusters despite a four-drug regimen that included clobazam, after an earlier partial response to another agent. The per-agent outcome is not stated, so the group is "regimen-level non-control, agent not separable", the same class as A1-08. Nothing in the nine rows was found to be wrong in a way that reverses a label.
4. **Nine rows are mentions, not exposures.** No source states how many WWOX children received a benzodiazepine-type agent. The class has therefore **no denominator** and every count above is a count of what was written, not of what happened.

## What the record cannot state (class level)
* **A rate or a direction for the class.** Two responders and four non-controlled groups come from different reports, endpoints, ages and co-treatments; a response in one child and a regimen failure in another neither cancel nor sum.
* **Whether the agent caused the change.** Both response statements are in children on several agents with an evolving disease (spasms can remit with age or with a co-agent); neither report had a washout, a counter-trial or a seizure diary; one has a follow-up of about a month.
* **A dose, a duration or a timing relative to other agents** (not stated; deliberately not asked).
* **A difference between agents of the class** (clobazam, clonazepam, nitrazepam, diazepam appear once or twice each, and in different contexts).
* **Selection:** positive responses and refractory courses are both reported, but cases are published for other reasons (a phenotype, a variant), so neither direction is a sample.
* **That the two response reports are independent of the others:** the pooled series re-tabulates children from the case reports.
* **A generic symptomatic effect "already documented for the class in this lesion"** (the map row's question): not documented. What is documented is a single-child attribution each for two agents and the absence of any class-level statement.
