# P4 step 1 (ADV-016 / RP-015): missense-carrying variants in the held clinical reports (IT; non-canonical; not medical advice)
Written for: HE (joins to UniProt: variant_domain_map.tsv), SCI (literature step per distinct variant, I20), ADV. Nothing was searched outside the 25 reports (the 14 held and the 11 of D5b tranche 1); the function column is a keyword pass over those 25 texts (a sentence containing the variant and one of: functional, in silico, predict, damag, pathogen, ACMG, western, protein level, expression, stability, conserv, catalytic, domain) and quotes or paraphrases only what the reports say, including what they cite from earlier literature.
Rule: a child is counted for a variant if one allele has a residue-to-residue protein change (p.Xxx123Yyy or p.(Xxx123Yyy), not Ter/Stop/*/fs). In-frame deletions and duplications are NOT missense and are kept apart (below). A child with two missense alleles appears in two rows, so row totals (14 in the 42, 18 in the 37) count child-variant pairs. Fields `domain` and `protein stated` are the REPORT's own words (or IT's addendum reading of them); nothing is inferred from position here: HE assigns the domain by the UniProt join.
Flag, per ADV and SCI: c.139C>A p.Pro47Thr (3 homozygous children, 2 families, Al-Baradie Patients 1.1, 1.2 and 4, Table 1 'Missense') is the variant of the P47T knock-in mouse; position 47 lies in WW1 (UniProt 16-49); the report does not name the domain and does not state the protein, so 'protein present' is assumed from the mouse only. Earlier statements of mine that no held child has a WW1 missense homozygote, and that the WW1-with-protein contrast is empty as to the variant, were wrong; the protein state of these three remains not stated.
