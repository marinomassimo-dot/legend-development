# The 42 sealed children by genotype severity class and response: a record of what cannot be said (IT; not a test; post hoc; non-canonical; not medical advice)

**ERRATUM (IT, 2026-11-16 ~21:10Z, before this file was published).** The first version of this record (and the message sent to ADV and SCI at 19:37Z and 20:55Z that quoted 22 'missense' children and classes 17 / 22 / 3) used a keyword rule whose protein-change pattern also matched nonsense and frameshift notation (for example p.Trp288Ter, p.Arg264Ter, p.Phe348SerfsTer57, p.Arg54*), so about nine null-like children were counted as 'residual or missense'. Corrected rule (v3 after a second fix: protein changes written p.(Xxx123Yyy) with a parenthesis were missed in v2, which moved the 42092735 neonate from unknown to residual/missense): a child is `residual_or_missense` if at least one allele has a protein change from one residue to another residue (not Ter, Stop, * or fs) or the text describes a glycine-230-to-proline change; otherwise `biallelic_loss_or_null_like` if the variant text names null, deletion, truncating, frameshift, nonsense, splice or duplication changes; otherwise `unknown`. The rule is post hoc (written after seeing the table) and is NOT the pre-defined classification of addendum 3. One mislabel recorded: Nagarajan Table 4 serial no. 37 is labelled 'Deletion and missense' by the authors but its protein notation is a nonsense change (p.Trp398Ter), so it falls in the null-like class here.

Not a test. What cannot be said: (1) whether proximity to threshold, severity or the report matters, because 'residual or missense' is a label for 'not clearly null' (it includes compound missense/null children, 6 of the 14 from Oliver) and does not show residual function; no child states the protein present; (2) any variance statement, since the outcome per child is the best of several exposures in different regimens; (3) any worsening statement, because the table holds none; (4) any comparison across classes, because exposure rows are not independent and the class mix of agents differs. The pre-declared minimum of 8 children with a stated outcome per class is met for the null-like class (16) and NOT met for the residual/missense class (7); the earlier statement that both classes reached 8 was a result of the counting error.
