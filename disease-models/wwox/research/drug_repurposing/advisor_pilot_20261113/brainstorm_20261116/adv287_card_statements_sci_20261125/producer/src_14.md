No printed arithmetic. S466L: matches (activity as high as in some Tg-hCBS animals, high-exposure arm). I278T, R266K, R336C "improved": matches (bortezomib restored activity; the paper does not say they reached the wild-type range). G307S "not at all": the only support in the held text is a one-clause Introduction statement about earlier work (ref 14 not held); the allele was not tested in this paper, so the sentence puts a non-tested allele next to the tested ones without saying it. The low-exposure arms (S466L, I278T: no rescue at tolerated exposure) are in separate assertions of the same card (DR-A02-007, -010).
