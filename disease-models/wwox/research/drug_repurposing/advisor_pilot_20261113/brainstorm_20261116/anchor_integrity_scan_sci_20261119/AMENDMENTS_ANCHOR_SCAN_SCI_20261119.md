# Dated amendments to PREREG_ANCHOR_SCAN_SCI_20261119.md (the pre-registration file is not edited; every line here is POST HOC)

Hypothesis level; no dose; not medical advice. The first run of `anchor_integrity_scan_sci_20261119.py` (frozen method, 2026-11-19 about 13:55 UTC) returned 189 anchors (171 A1, 18 A2), 47 claims, 26 claims with consumption above zero, no event-id/PMID disagreement. Its result also showed two things the frozen method could not handle. Both are recorded here before the second run, with the numbers the first run gave.

## Amendment 1 (2026-11-19): wikilink-only papers are shown as class A3 with their depth; flag F7
**Seen:** CLAIM 017 and CLAIM 020 (both `consolidated baseline`) had ZERO anchors under the frozen definition (A1 = the paper's own `Claim links` or a PMID in the claim's `Source`; A2 = CORPUS placeholder). Their `Source` fields name the papers by author and year ("Abdel-Salam 2014 / Piard 2018 / Oliver 2023 / Teplyshova 2024 / Banne 2021", "Teplyshova & Sharkov 2024") and their `Wikilinks` field carries the PAPER records; no paper record lists the claim in its own `Claim links`. The tool's rule (`wikilink_only` is not evidential) is kept; the scan did not promote A3 to anchor.
**Change:** A3 papers (in the claim's `Wikilinks` only) are written to the anchor TSV with their attested depth, and the claim table gets `n_A3`, `A3_deepest`, `A3_weakest`. **F7 WIKILINK_ONLY_ANCHOR** = no A1 and no A2 anchor but at least one A3. A3 never enters the counts of anchors, the F1 to F4 flags, or the rank key.
**Reading of F7:** a claim whose source is declared by name and wikilink only is a DECLARATION gap (the paper side does not name the claim back), distinct from a reading-depth gap.

## Amendment 2 (2026-11-19): F6 HAS_WEAK_ANCHOR, because F1 is an any-of test
**Seen:** F1 (no A1 anchor at `complete_strict`) uses the deepest anchor, so a claim with one strong and one weak anchor passes. CLAIM 025 has the shape: two `complete_strict` anchors that do not carry the GDM statement, one `partial` anchor that does, and one placeholder. F1 does not flag it.
**Change:** **F6 HAS_WEAK_ANCHOR** = at least one A2 anchor, or at least one A1 anchor below `complete_loose` (partial, abstract-only, not-read, none, placeholder). F1 stays as frozen and is reported next to it.
**Limit stated:** F6 says an anchor is weak; it does not say that the weak anchor is the one that carries the claim's load. That mapping needs a reading (section 7 of the pre-registration, amended below).

## Amendment 3 (2026-11-19): the second-layer set
**Seen:** under the frozen rule of section 7 (F1 together with a strong or medium F3, or F2a, among the slice) the set is EMPTY: in the slice F1 occurs only for CLAIM 042 (`in observation`, anchors `complete_loose` and `partial`), no slice claim carries F3 and none is F2a. That is a result of the frozen screen and is reported as such.
**Change (post hoc):** the second layer by reading is applied to the five highest-ranked slice claims that are `consolidated baseline` (the strongest wording the registry allows) and carry F6, consumption ranking within. Reason: a consolidated baseline claim is the one whose wording must hold against every anchor; the second layer asks, for each weak anchor, whether the claim's sentence depends on it and whether the reading depth covered what the claim says.

## Amendment 4 (2026-11-19): ties at the slice boundary
Ranks 19 to 21 (CLAIM 006, CLAIM 009, CLAIM 014) all have key 2; the frozen rule cut at 20 by claim number (006 and 009 in, 014 out). Stated so the cut is not read as a gap in consumption.

## Amendment 5 (2026-11-19, after the second run): a CORPUS record that carries an identifier is levelled by its study's receipt; duplicates are not separate anchors
**Seen:** my script forced every A2 anchor to `placeholder` (I coded that reading while writing it; it is not in the frozen text, which says "placeholder" for Identifier PENDING or no PMID/DOI). Eleven of the 18 A2 records carry a PMID or DOI: CORPUS P210 (CLAIM 021) names the same study (PMID 34634460) as PAPER 031, which has a `complete_strict` receipt; CORPUS-STUB-058 and -127 (CLAIM 028) name the studies of PAPER 026 and PAPER 028; and so on. Calling those placeholders overstated the weakness of CLAIM 021 (rank 1, 138 consumers).
**Change:** (a) a record is `placeholder` (CORPUS) or `no_identifier` (PAPER) only when it has no PMID and no DOI, or says PENDING; a CORPUS record with an identifier is levelled by the receipt of that study, like a PAPER record. (b) An A2 record whose PMID or DOI matches an A1 anchor of the same claim is marked `dup_of_A1` and is not counted as a separate anchor (column `n_A2_dup`). F2 and F6 are computed over the effective anchors. The anchor TSV keeps every row, duplicates included.
**Numbers moved by this amendment** (second run against third run): placeholders among the 189 A1 and A2 anchor rows 21 to 9, plus 3 `no_identifier`; claims with F6 30 to 28.
(c) **F6 is a depth flag only** (any effective A1 or A2 anchor below `complete_loose`): a CORPUS record that carries an identifier and whose study has a complete receipt is not weak by kind. The record-kind issue stays visible in F2 (a CORPUS record anchors the claim). Claims with F6 28 to 27.

## Amendment 6 (2026-11-19, after the third run): a `Claim links` field that begins "none" links nothing, whatever claim numbers its explanation names
**Seen (reading CLAIM 002 and CLAIM 003 against their anchors):** CLAIM 002 showed three `complete_strict` anchors, two of which are PAPER 082 and PAPER 083, whose own `Claim links` field says "none — deliberately none. `CLAIM 002` and `CLAIM 004` must NOT be linked to this record". The anchor builder I used (the `claim_links` edge of `trace_claim_foundation.py`, taken from `Foundation.supporting_papers`) finds claim numbers with the pattern `\b(\d{3})\b` anywhere in the field, so a field that NAMES a claim in order to refuse the link counts as a link.
**Measured (script `strict_links`, compare the naive and the strict reading of every `Claim links` field over the 558 PAPER records that have one):** 167 naive edges against 146 strict edges; 14 PAPER records differ, every one a field that starts with "none" (PAPER 082, 083, 112, 122, 125, 126, 127, 129, 130, 132, 171, 176, 189, 289), 21 false edges; in every other field the strict and the naive readings agree. The tool's output is therefore wrong for those 14 records: it credits claims with evidence the registry explicitly withholds (an `evidential: true` support edge in the JSON output).
**Change:** an A1 edge from `Claim links` requires an affirmed number (`strict_links`: field not starting with "none"/"no"/"not"; backticked text and parenthetical annotations ignored; text after a spaced dash ignored). The 21 refused edges are written to the anchor TSV as class **A4 NEGATED_LINK** with their depth, never counted as anchors (column `n_A4_negated`). CORPUS records' `Claim links` use the same parse.
**Numbers moved by this amendment** (third run against fourth run): A1 anchors 171 to 150, A2 18 to 14, A4 0 to 21; anchors among A1 and A2 189 to 164; claims with F6 27 to 24. CLAIM 003 goes from three anchors (one partial) to ONE anchor, PAPER 004, `partial`; CLAIM 011 from two to one; CLAIM 036 from six to four.
**To HE (a tool defect, not applied):** `trace_claim_foundation.py` should treat a `Claim links` field beginning "none" as no link (14 records, 21 edges today; the 2026-09-20 `support_linkage.py` has the same input and the same exposure).

## Amendment 7 (2026-11-19, after the fourth run): a "partial" receipt is not a thin reading; union coverage and flag F8
**Seen:** reading the receipts of the weak anchors of CLAIM 002, 003, 005 and 019, the `partial_fulltext_read` label covers very different readings. PMID 34268881 (Steinberg 2021) has 14 surviving receipts, one of which (`FTR-20261004-34268881-07`) read every section including the supplement and is still `partial` because the Appendix Figures S1 to S6 are absent; PMID 33914858 (Repudi 2021) has 12 receipts whose union reads abstract, introduction, methods, results, discussion, tables and supplement. The index receipt the tool returns is the LAST of equal depth, which for 33914858 is a methods-and-supplement receipt.
**Change:** the anchor TSV gets `n_receipts_study` and `union_read` (sections read in at least one surviving receipt of the same study, by the receipt's own study_id). **F8 PARTIAL_NO_RESULTS** = an anchor at `partial`, `abstract_only` or `not_read` whose union does not include Results. F1, F3 and F6 are unchanged (they stay label-based, as frozen); F8 is the screen for a reading that is thin in substance.
**Number:** of 46 `partial` rows among the A1 and A2 anchors, 42 have Results read in at least one receipt and 4 do not (CLAIM 028 CORPUS-STUB-127 and PAPER 028; CLAIM 043 and 044 PAPER 141).

## Amendment 8 (2026-11-19, after the fourth run): a superseded record is levelled at the better of itself and its successor
**Seen:** CLAIM 002 lists PAPER 001 (Steinberg 2024 bioRxiv preprint, Identifier "preprint", Status `superseded`) as an anchor; the record's own note says it was superseded on 2026-09-27 by PAPER 094, the refereed version (PMID 42397075, `complete_fulltext_read`, `FTR-20260810-42397075-04`). CLAIM 041 lists PAPER 112, superseded by PAPER 007.
**Change:** column `effective` = the better of the record's own level and its named successor's level (`superseded_by` names it); F1 and F6 use `effective`; the TSV keeps the own level in `attested`. Two anchors are affected (PAPER 001, PAPER 112), both rise to `complete_strict`.
**Caveat that stays:** PAPER 094's complete reading declares bytes that are absent from every checkout (its own record says so, with a replacement surface acquired 2026-10-04); the level is the receipt's attestation, which this scan cannot re-check without the bytes.

## Amendment 9 (2026-11-19, after the fifth run): "promoted" records follow their successor; PENDING means a literal PENDING identifier
**Seen:** CORPUS P206 (named in the Wikilinks of CLAIM 023 and CLAIM 028) has Status "promoted — see PAPER 081" and an Identifier that names PMID 15070730 and quotes the old literal `PENDING` in its history. My script read the word `PENDING` anywhere in the identifier and called it a placeholder, although its study has two receipts, one `complete_fulltext_read` (`FTR-20260909-15070730-02`), and PAPER 081 carries it.
**Change:** (a) a record is a placeholder only when its whole Identifier is `PENDING` or it carries no PMID and no DOI; (b) the successor rule of Amendment 8 also applies to a Status of the form "promoted — see [[PAPER nnn]]". Four rows now carry a successor (PAPER 001 and CORPUS P206 twice and PAPER 112).
**Numbers moved** (fifth run against fourth run): placeholders among the A1 and A2 rows 9 to 7; `complete_strict` 99 to 101; declared above attested 14 to 12 rows, below attested 25 to 27. No slice row, flag or rank moved (the change touches CLAIM 023 and CLAIM 028, outside the slice).
