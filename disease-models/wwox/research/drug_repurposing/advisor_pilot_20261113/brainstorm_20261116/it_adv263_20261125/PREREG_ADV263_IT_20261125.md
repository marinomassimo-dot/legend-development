## 5. Sampling
If the sentences left are under 150, all are read. Otherwise 100 are read: allocation proportional to stratum size with a floor of 8 per stratum (a stratum smaller than its floor is read whole), selection by ascending SHA-256 of `ADV263|<surface>|<record>|<sentence>`.

## 6. Classification of each sampled sentence
Kind:
- **W (corpus or world)**: asserts that something is the only, the first, the single, unique, never or none among reports, studies, datasets, drugs, models or the repository.
- **S (source-internal)**: the same form, bounded to what one named paper, table or dataset contains.
- **O (out of scope)**: a restriction of a conclusion ("supports only X"), an instruction or ordering ("measure X first"), a design fact stated as a quantity, a logical or conditional use.

Support, for W and S:
- **SUPPORTED**: the sentence or its record states a search with queries or date, or a held complete table or census, or (for S) names the read source and the claim is of a kind that source prints.
- **BOUNDED**: states its perimeter ("in the papers held", "in this module") but no search, query or denominator.
- **BARE**: neither.
- For the checked set only: **FALSIFIED** (a held source contradicts), **NOT-CONTRADICTED** (the quick check of section 7 finds no counterexample; this does not make it true), **UNCHECKED**.

## 7. The top 20

Ties: W before S, then CARD, INV, MAP, HYP, PORT, CLAIM. Statements already carrying a dated correction of the same assertion are skipped and listed. Each of the 20 gets one query route (PubMed or Europe PMC, spaced, queries recorded, or a held complete table) and one repository grep. Disposition: keep, reword with denominator ("in the N reports held, none ..."), or correct.
