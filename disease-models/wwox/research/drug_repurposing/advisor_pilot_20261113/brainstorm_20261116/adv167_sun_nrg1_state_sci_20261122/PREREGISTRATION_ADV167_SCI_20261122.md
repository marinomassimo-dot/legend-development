## Question
Is the response to NRG1/ErbB4 enhancement (a) concentrated in the LOW-INPUT (deprived) baseline state and dependent on PV-ErbB4, or (b) a generic increase in PV drive in both deprived and control contexts? Then: should the RP-038 / INV-15 readout set (phospho-ErbB4 and inhibitory output; design brief line 9.13) ADD "baseline PV-identified excitatory input" as a third paired readout, or is the design sufficient? And does the paper meet the row's adjacent-context trigger ("an NRG1-modulator seizure or PV readout under neonatal or juvenile dosing") without the WWOX premise?

## Rival predictions (fixed)
1. **Generic (rival 1):** NRG1 raises PV excitatory drive (or evoked inhibition) in BOTH deprived and control contexts. Supports only a generic circuit perturbation; baseline input would not be a useful moderator.
2. **State-conditional and PV-ErbB4-dependent (rival 2):** the response is concentrated in the low-input state and is absent when PV-ErbB4 is removed or ErbB blocked. Then low baseline excitatory input plus engagement is a useful condition for reading the conditional WWOX assay. NOT asserted: that the WWOX system enters that state.

## Readings (fixed)
* **S-direct:** a reported contrast between states for the SAME readout, tested directly (a ratio compared between normal and deprived cells, or a state-by-treatment term), not two separate within-state tests.
* **S-subgroup:** significant in the deprived state, non-significant in the control state, no direct contrast test; a significant subgroup beside a non-significant one.
* **E-same:** PV-ErbB4 dependence (conditional knockout or ErbB blocker) shown for the same readout and the same state as the response.
* **E-other:** dependence shown for a different readout only.
* **C-sep:** the design contains an arm that separates low excitatory input from low endogenous NRG1/ErbB4 phosphorylation (lowers one without the other).
* **C-conf:** the manipulations that lower input (monocular deprivation, chemogenetic PV silencing) also lower NRG1 and phospho-ErbB4, so a state-specific response cannot be assigned to low input rather than to low ligand. (Prediction at registration: C-conf, from the Results text.)

## Decision rules for the deliverable (fixed)
* **D1, ADD baseline PV-identified excitatory input as a paired readout** only if S-direct AND E-same AND the state effect is also seen on a route that does not need the ex vivo recording of that same cell (intact-animal immunostaining or in vivo function), AND C-sep. Predicted at registration: NOT all met.
* **D2, ADD as an optional, labelled state descriptor (not a moderator claim):** if S-direct or S-subgroup with E-same holds but C-conf prevents assigning the effect to low input. The reason would be to record the state in which phospho-ErbB4 and sIPSC are read, not to claim a moderating role.
* **D3, KEEP the design as sufficient:** if the state effect rests on S-subgroup alone, or the response is generic, or controls are not matched.
* **CLOSE:** no matched control arm, or no distinguishable effect after scoping.
* **Trigger statement:** state separately whether the source meets the LETTER of the row's "or" branch (juvenile dosing, PV readout, NRG1 modulator) and whether it meets its INTENT (a seizure or PV readout that bears on the Wwox-deficient sign premise or on the neonatal exposure window). Meeting the letter does not move the row's status.

## Scoping checks I will make (fixed)
* **Age:** age at treatment against age at readout, per readout (virus P14; CNO P28; deprivation and recording at P27 to P31; ocular-dominance imaging window); juvenile critical period against neonatal.
* **Model:** wild-type C57BL/6, PV-Cre;Ai9, PV-Cre;ErbB4 flox/flox (PV cells knockout), PV-Cre;Ai32; sex.
* **Intact-animal phosphorylation against acute-slice physiology:** which panels measure phosphorylation in fixed tissue after in vivo dosing (Fig 2H, 2I) and which measure synaptic input in slices after bath or in vivo dosing (Fig 3 to 7); the intact-animal functional readout (Fig 8).
* **Unit:** animals against cells for each test. The paper states n is cells except immunostaining and ocular dominance. Cells from few animals are pseudoreplicated; the number of animals per cell group is looked for and, if absent, stated as absent.
* **Direct contrast against subgroup:** for each state-by-treatment claim, which test is printed.
* **Sign and route cautions:** in vivo dosing is subcutaneous recombinant NRG1 EGF-domain peptide in micrograms per mouse; no brain exposure measurement is claimed here.

## Rejecting observation (named in advance)
Rival 2 would be rejected by a significant NRG1 effect on PV excitatory input or on evoked inhibition in the control (non-deprived) context in a matched cell group; rival 1 would be rejected by the absence of an effect in the control context beside a direct contrast between states. A PV-ErbB4 knockout readout that still responds to NRG1 would break the dependence claim.
