## 1. Answers
**(1) A carrier readout beyond the Tc-MEP latency: not at animal level in the germline heterozygote; one more candidate at cell level in a neuron-targeted heterozygote.** Ranked as candidates (nothing here is validated):
1. **Tc-MEP onset latency, germline Wwox +/-** (Cheng 2020, PMID 32000863): wild type 1.39 +/- 0.13 ms (n = 10), heterozygote 2.13 +/- 0.22 (n = 5; p < 0.05 against wild type), null 2.44 +/- 0.37 (n = 4; p < 0.01); amplitude comparable to wild type in heterozygotes (59.6 +/- 17.2 against 59.2 +/- 9.0 microvolts); heterozygote against null not different; 3 weeks of age; one laboratory; the test is named only by significance stars in the passages the producer read. The numbers were also checked by me against the held text earlier today (the dated correction in the G audit summary). **Still one source.**
2. **Cortical pyramidal-neuron membrane properties in S-HT** (Wwox +/flox with Synapsin-Cre; Breton 2021, PMID 34634460, Table 1; **I checked the values in all three held copies**): input resistance 143.26 +/- 15.49 (S-HT, starred) against 102.88 +/- 11.74 (S-WT); action-potential amplitude 68.77 +/- 2.07 against 76.30 +/- 2.2; resting potential -75.51 +/- 1.81 against -79.25 +/- 1.99; sag 2.04 +/- 0.44 against 1.18 +/- 0.24; the S-KO column is close to the S-HT column for these four (143.03, 68.67, -73.62, 2.57). The unit is the cell (per the producer: 15 cells from 6 animals against 13 from 5, not re-checked by me), the genotype is neuron-targeted, and it is the same laboratory as the 2021 Brain paper. **Why it matters for the slice package:** the heterozygote slice group already differs from wild type on the passive properties and on sag, so a knockout-specific reading of a sag or passive endpoint must be made against the heterozygote as well; the package's heterozygote group (its 3.4) has this precedent.
3. **S-HT slice bursting**, 4 of 23 slices against 0 of 11 (Brain 2021 text; its Fig 2 legend gives 4 slices from 3 animals, 20 percent): no heterozygote-against-control test; Breton re-quotes the same dataset, so **one source**.
4. **mEPSC amplitude and frequency in S-HT** (Breton, Fig 4 legend; p values **checked by me in three copies**: amplitude S-WT against S-HT 1.9831e-04, frequency 0.0018): Kolmogorov-Smirnov on pooled events, 10 cells from 5 animals against 10 from 4; the unit is the event, not the animal.
5. **P47T/WT behaviour** (Hussain 2023): open field and elevated plus maze intermediate with no heterozygote-against-wild-type test seen by the producer; sociability above wild type; a different allele (missense), a different kind of heterozygote.
**Nothing else measures Tc-MEP, conduction or MEP latency in any other heterozygote**, and no source repeats a germline-null heterozygote electrophysiology measure. Every S-HT readout is a cell, slice or event unit with 4 to 8 animals.

**(2) Heterozygous pups and harm baselines: the sources give reasons, and no pup-age data.** The only germline heterozygote harm measures are P20 brain weight (N = 20 / 21 / 18, not different) and brain water (N = 9 / 7 / 4, not different) from Cheng 2020 Suppl Fig S1 (per the producer, panels opened; I did not open them). No pup-age organ, weight, survival or glucose datum exists for any heterozygote genotype in these sources; Cheng 2020 reports no +/- body weight or glucose. **For the pup brief:** it already runs +/+, +/- and -/- in the same sessions (2.1), so the harm readouts named in the ADV-079 note (section 5: liver and kidney histology, weight, glucose with the fed state) can be carried per genotype at no extra arm; the vehicle heterozygote group would be the first pup-age baseline anyone has. ## 3. Cautions on the producer's report
* **"Normal" is often "not shown".** Heterozygote statements that rest on "data not shown" or on observation without n carry no data: Cheng sciatic-nerve myelin (data not shown), Hussain Iba1 and Gfap (data not shown), Iatan 2014 lipid profile (data not shown), Repudi S-HT and N-HT "no abnormal phenotype" (observation, no n). A "like wild type" cell in the register is a statement, not a measurement, unless a test and an n are in its row.
* **No animal-level heterozygote test exists for any S-HT readout** (cells, slices or events; 5 to 8 animals), and the two papers (Brain and Breton) are one animal source for the bursting counts; the S-KO animal count differs (24 in the text, 20 in the Fig 2 legend).
* **Opposite readings of the same heterozygote in one paper (Breton):** its text lumps S-HT with S-WT as controls "where no significant difference exists", while its own Table 1 and Fig 4 legend show S-HT differing from S-WT (per the producer for the text, checked by me for the table and legend p values).
* **The register is not a prevalence or a validation of any carrier readout**; the n of animals for the best germline candidate is 5.
