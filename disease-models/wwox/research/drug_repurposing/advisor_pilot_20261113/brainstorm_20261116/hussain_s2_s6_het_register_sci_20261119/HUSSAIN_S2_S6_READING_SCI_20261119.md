## S2: organ weight coefficients (n = 9 wild type, 16 P47T/WT, 16 P47T/P47T)
* Caption: relative organ weight ("weight coefficient") of kidney, liver, spleen, pancreas and adrenal; kidney `*p < 0.05` by one-way ANOVA with Tukey, liver `*p < 0.05` by Kruskal-Wallis; **the only marked comparison is wild type against P47T/P47T**; spleen, pancreas and adrenal carry no mark; panel a: cerebellar Wwox mRNA, n = 6 per group, not significantly different.
* Bar heights read by eye from the rendered figure (plus or minus about 0.1): kidney about 1.55 (wild type), 1.8 (P47T/WT), 1.97 (P47T/P47T); liver about 4.5, 4.7 and 5.1. Heterozygotes sit between.
* **The main text** (Results, same paragraph as the weights): "At necropsy, no significant macroscopic organ abnormalities were observed. Relative organ weights (weight coefficients) were determined, and a slight increase in the kidney and liver weight coefficients was observed in the Wwox P47T/P47T group compared to the Wwox WT/WT (p-value < 0.05)". The same paper gives body weight 29.22 +/- 4.28 g (P47T/P47T) against 41.17 +/- 9.52 g (wild type) and 37.39 +/- 6.08 g (P47T/WT), p < 0.005.
* **Reading.** The caption does not define the denominator. If the coefficient is organ weight over body weight (kidney of about 1.5 and liver of about 5, in percent, fit that), a kidney coefficient 27 percent higher in a mouse 29 percent lighter means an absolute kidney weight about the same or lower, and for the liver a lower absolute weight (by this arithmetic: about 0.64 against 0.58 g for kidney and 1.85 against 1.49 g for liver; means of ratios times group mean body weights, possibly from different animal subsets, an order-of-magnitude check only). **So "kidney and liver weight up" is not evidence of organ enlargement**; it is at least as well explained by lower body weight, and S2 shows no absolute weights. Audit G's phrase ("kidney and liver weight up") and the producer's W1 weight line in the ADV-079 report should be read this way; **the kidney and liver cells of the ADV-079 table rest on Supplementary Table 4 histology (and the label sentences), not on the weights.**

## S6: Mbp in the parietal cortex above the corpus callosum
* Caption: Mbp area fraction percent in a top region (0 to 325 micrometres from the top of the image at 80 days, 0 to 450 at 250 days) and a middle region (326 to 650 and 451 to 900), wild type (n = 3) and P47T/P47T (n = 3) mice, 80 and 250 days, "p-value is not significant, unpaired Student's t-test"; heading "Wwox P47T mutation does not affect myelination in parietal cortex".
* By eye: 80 days, top about 11 against 12, middle about 36 against 39; 250 days, top about 12 against 9, middle about 35 against 30 (percent).
* **Unit of analysis (counted from the page geometry, not by eye):** the plot carries **6 points per group per region at 80 days and 9 per group per region at 250 days**, against n = 3 mice per genotype per age: the plotted and tested unit is a field (two and three per mouse), not an animal. The Fig 4 legend says the same for the main figure ("each data point shows measurement from a single independent region from n = 3 mice/group").
* **What it supports:** no significant difference was detected at region level in cortex Mbp area fraction at two ages; **not** equality (no equivalence test, three mice per group, a difference of the order of 5 points in the 250-day middle region that the test did not resolve).

## The Olig2-against-Mbp composition caveat: it STAYS, and is sharpened
Main text: the corpus callosum of P47T/P47T mice has fewer Olig2-positive cells than wild type (p < 0.01, unpaired t-test; Fig 4d and e), then "we also analyzed myelination ... in parietal cortex above corpus callosum ... no significant differences in Mbp staining" (S6). Methods: Olig2 counted in three independent 500 square-micrometre regions per image, "all quantitative data was averaged from three mice per group". Four facts for the caveat:
1. **Different regions:** Olig2 in the corpus callosum, Mbp in the cortex above it. The Mbp panel says nothing about callosal myelin.
2. **Both are region-level tests on n = 3 mice per group** (Olig2: each point a single independent region, as the Fig 4e legend says; I did not count the Fig 4e points, the main figure was not opened). The p < 0.01 for fewer Olig2 cells may therefore also rest on regions as units; the animal-level result is not given.
3. **Age of the Olig2 panels** is not stated in the legend text I read (panels a to c state 80 and 250 days).
4. **"Oligodendrocytes lost without hypomyelination" is not supported**: one side is a callosal count, the other a cortical area fraction with a null at n = 3 mice. The v2 wording ("cross-region; a composition caveat only") is right and now has its two reasons; it stays.
Consequence for the brief: the pup brief v2 5(g) and the slice package v2 sentence already say "composition caveat only"; they need no further change except that the supplement is held and S6 is the panel (done in the 2026-11-19 edit). The decision it could change (use of Olig2 or Mbp as a myelin endpoint in a design) is unchanged: the structural endpoints with rescue evidence (EM, g-ratio, MBP and CC1) stay the ones to use; a count of Olig2 cells is not one.
