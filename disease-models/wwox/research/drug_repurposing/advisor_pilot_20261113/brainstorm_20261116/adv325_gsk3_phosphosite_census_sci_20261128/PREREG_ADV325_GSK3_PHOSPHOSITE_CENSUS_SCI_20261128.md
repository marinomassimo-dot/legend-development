# ADV-325 pre-registration (SCI bd): GSK3-substrate phosphosite census over held WWOX full texts

Frozen 2026-11-28T12:48:22Z, before any held text was opened for phosphosite content. Already seen before freezing: ADV-322 note, ADV-214 result (tables only, negative), and a filename list of a loose regex screen over 400 held WWOX texts in `files/fulltext` (25 or more hits, duplicates such as Cheng 2020 and Hussain 2023 counted twice); no hit was read. Hypothesis level; not medical advice.

## Inclusion rule (a PASS)
A held full text (main text, legends, Methods, supplementary figures or tables) that reports, for WWOX-deficient material (null, knockout, knockdown, mutant, patient-derived) against a control, an immunoblot or immunoassay readout of: GSK3A/B inhibitory phospho-Ser21/Ser9 or activating Tyr279/Tyr216; or a phosphosite of a GSK3 substrate (beta-catenin Ser33/Ser37/Thr41, CRMP2 Thr514, glycogen synthase Ser641, Tau at Ser199, Thr231, Ser396/Ser404, AT100, AT8), with ALL of: genotype, tissue or cell type, age (or passage and time), n per arm, and a QUANTIFIED readout (densitometry value, or bar or box with points). Classes: **Q** quantified, n per arm printed or countable and at least 3; **R** representative blot only or quantification without n; **N** none. Total-protein, RNA and overexpression-only arms never pass. Tumour or cell-line material passes as class Q-cell and counts for H1 never. Read: legends and Results sentences naming the phosphosite, the figure panel, and Methods for n; abstracts are not a reading.

## Hypotheses
* **H1:** the held texts already hold at least one same-tissue Wwox-null against +/+ brain readout at a developmental age (P7 to P28) of class Q, usable as the GSK3 baseline comparator.
* **H2:** only adult, cell-line or unquantified (class R) readouts exist; the same-animal phospho-substrate panel stays a new observation.
* My lean: H2, 70 percent. Cheng 2020 Fig 7c (pSer9, regional immunoblots) is class R by the lab's own earlier figure audit (qualitative, P14 or P20 ambiguous, densitometry withdrawn).

## Predicted observables
* Of the loose-screen hits (about 20 distinct PMIDs after duplicates), passes of the inclusion rule (Q or Q-cell): **3**, plausible range 1 to 6; class Q in Wwox-null brain at P7 to P28: **0** (30 percent that it is 1 or more).
* Where the same GSK3 site is read in two papers, direction in the WWOX-deficient material is expected as reduced inhibitory pSer9 or raised substrate phosphorylation, but sizes will not be comparable across tissues.

## Strong counterexample to H2
A quantified pSer9 (or beta-catenin Ser33/Ser37) readout in Wwox-null brain at P14 to P21 with n of 3 or more per arm. Its presence moves the GSK3 entry from "new observation" to "held comparator exists, sampling to be checked".
