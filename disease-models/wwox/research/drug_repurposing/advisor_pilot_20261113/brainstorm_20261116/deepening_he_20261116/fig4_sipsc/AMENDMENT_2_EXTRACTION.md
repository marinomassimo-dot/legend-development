# Amendment 2 to PREREGISTRATION_FIG4_MEPSC_SECOND_READ.md: the first extraction run is discarded and the extraction is widened (HE, 2026-11-16; committed before the second run)

**What the first run gave and why it is discarded.** The first run (the band of ±1 pixel around the cumulative-probability-0.5 row, as preregistered) returned, for panel B, S-WT −47.8 pA (3 pixels), S-HT −13.8 pA (3 pixels), S-KO −15.3 pA (2 pixels), and for panel C S-WT 45.4 Hz (6 pixels), S-HT hidden, S-KO 3.8 Hz (1 pixel). Two of these are artefacts, not curve values: the S-WT value in B at pixel 325 is the black text of the inset's y-axis label, and the S-WT value in C at 45 Hz is the black legend sample line. The curves are almost vertical at probability 0.5, so a ±1-row band holds 1 to 3 pixels per curve. The file is kept as `results/fig4_mepsc_first_run_DISCARDED.json` and is not used.
**This change is therefore made after seeing a result that exposed a method defect (text and legend inside the search band; too few pixels), not after seeing the genotype ordering made to look a particular way.** Both runs are reported.

Changes (everything else, including the decision rule, the colour classes, the calibration and its cross-check, is unchanged):
1. **Excluded regions:** panel B: the whole inset including its axis labels (x 316 to 430, y 18 to 112); panel C: the legend box and its sample lines (x ≥ 470, y 205 to 270).
2. **Wider band with a line fit:** for each colour class, for every row whose cumulative probability lies between 0.40 and 0.60, the mean x of that class's pixels in the row (rows without a pixel of the class are skipped); a straight line x against probability through those rows (least squares) is evaluated at 0.5. At least 5 rows are required, otherwise the curve is reported as hidden. The uncertainty is the fit's standard error at 0.5 in pixels, combined in quadrature with the ±1.5 px curve-position and the ±2 px anchor uncertainties of the preregistration.
3. The rest of the preregistration applies: f and its extremes, SCI's classes, 'not separable' when |S-WT − S-KO| is within the combined uncertainty, 'hidden' reported as hidden.
