## Answer first
1. **Counted by independent feature group, I8 reaches only a small part of the held profile.** The five pyramidal features are about three groups (SCI): rest_conductance (resting potential, input resistance), deflection (sag, rebound), spike_amplitude (AP amplitude, threshold; partly a consequence of a depolarised rest); the interneuron AP amplitude is the one independent feature. Signs were written before the count; Kcnh7 and Kcnt2 are marked uninformative for resting features. Result (`sign_fit_by_group.tsv`):

| group | candidate and sign that fits | candidates that are mixed or oppose |
|---|---|---|
| rest_conductance | **none** | HCN1 mixed (RMP fits M+, input resistance opposes); Trpm3 mixed under both signs; Kcnh7, Kcnt2 uninformative |
| deflection (sag, rebound) | HCN1 under M+ (weak: sag in mV is not an Ih measure; the sag ratio is the Ih-specific quantity) | HCN1 under M− opposes |
| spike_amplitude | Nav1.1 and Nav1.2 under M− (not independent of the depolarised rest; threshold, predicted lower under M+ and higher under M−, is not different: not seen) | Nav under M+ opposes |
| interneuron_amplitude (independent) | **Nav1.1 under M−** | Nav1.1 under M+ opposes |

   So the best-supported statement stays the one SCI named: the interneuron AP-amplitude fall fits a lower Nav1.1 surface level under the shield reading; everything else in the profile is either not reached by a PY candidate (resting conductance) or weak.
2. **The K2P, Kir and Kv7 extension is negative.** All 16 genes SCI listed (Kcnk1, 2, 3, 4, 9, 10, 13, 18; Kcnj2, 4, 6, 10, 12; Kcnq2, 3, 5) are in the scanned universe with annotated topology and carry **no cytoplasmic PPxY and no LPxY** (`out/k_channel_families_scan.tsv`; family totals: Kcnk 11 genes, Kcnj 15, Kcnq 5 with one LPxY in Kcnq1, none in Kv1 to Kv4, BK, SK, CNG). The recollection that Kcnq2 and Kcnq3 carry PY motifs is not supported by this scan (ASSUMED recollection against a computed absence; the scan reads UniProt cytoplasmic topology and the strings PPxY and LPxY only). **The missing candidate for the resting-conductance part of the profile does not exist in the PPxY/LPxY set.** For the route "WW1-PPxY (or LPxY) competition at a resting K+ channel" this is a test that could have failed (a motif in any of 16 genes would have kept it alive) and did: **KILLED for canonical PPxY/LPxY motifs at those 16 genes; PARKED, with the trigger "a WW-domain ligand screen or structure that shows a non-canonical WWOX ligand in a K2P, Kir or Kv7 channel or in an adaptor of one"** (not for lack of literature: for lack of a motif).
   P47T transcript pointers (SCI's and ADV's, descriptive, n 5 v 5; mRNA of a missense line, not protein): Kcnq2 PFC +0.45 (FDR 0.015 without sex term, 0.033 with), CB −0.47 (0.12/0.18); Kcnk2 CTX −0.45 and HPC −0.57 (p 0.018 and 0.0026; FDR 0.17/0.32); Kcnj2 CTX −0.38 (FDR 0.08/0.12); Scn1a PFC −0.47 (p 0.0083; FDR 0.067/0.095), CTX −0.37 (FDR 0.45). Only Kcnq2 PFC passes FDR 0.05; none of these is a PY test.
3. **Heterozygotes: I8 can explain at most the step.** Held (ADV's premise check; Breton 2021 Results): burst incidence is 0 of 11 slices (WT), 4 of 23 (HT) and 36 of 42 (KO), while resting potential, input resistance, sag and AP amplitude are S-HT = S-KO, a step from wild type, and rebound is significant only in S-KO. Whatever a surface-level PY mechanism explains is therefore the step (haploinsufficiency-like: present at half dose); bursting incidence is graded (about 0, 17%, 86%), so it needs a second, graded factor, and rebound and mEPSC amplitude are the two held candidates. I8 does not say what the graded factor is. In the P47T arm a heterozygous group tests the same thing at the protein level (step against graded) if animals exist; SCI has none held.
4. **What I8 is now.** A route to a sodium-channel-density component and to the interneuron spike amplitude, weakly to a deflection component through HCN1, and **not** to the resting conductance that dominates the profile. The P47T decomposition arm remains the experiment for that narrower claim; its value is also negative: a P47T that copies nothing in the rest_conductance group says what the scan already says.

## Limits
* The directions are qualitative physiology (ASSUMED) and the group status is a mechanical rule (fit if every informative, observed feature matches; oppose if all oppose; mixed otherwise). The rule treats threshold, which was not different, as a prediction not seen and does not count it.
* Absence of PPxY/LPxY in the annotated cytoplasmic parts does not exclude WW binding through other motifs (PPLP, proline-arginine), through adaptors or through the WW2 domain; the scan covers the canonical PY motifs only.
* One paper's Table 1 (read through a repository note and SCI's re-reading of the full text), small n, and the S-KO and S-HT groups are Syn-Cre deletions, not the P47T line.
