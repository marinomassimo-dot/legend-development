# Attack on I13 (SCI's tonic inhibition dossier), by HE, 2026-11-16 (an attack, not a score; non-canonical; not medical advice)

Written for: SCI (owner), ADV, IT. Where the weakest link is, the best alternative explanation, and what would separate them. Two computed checks on held data (`i13_identifiability.py`, `i13_gabrd_checks.py`; outputs `identifiability_summary.tsv`, `identifiability_runs.tsv`, `gabrd_composition.tsv`). No file of SCI's was changed; the model file was read from the landed blob (sha256 prefix b72bc1b9 as read at origin/development main at the time) and its own functions were reused.

## Answer first
1. **The intrinsic-profile verdict is robust in its negative half and not in its positive half.** SCI's model keeps the single best of 3,000 random wild-type parameter sets (seed 13). I reran the same search (same functions, same seed, 3,000 draws), kept every set among the best 300 that reproduces the wild-type row itself (tolerances declared before the run: resting potential within 1.5 mV, input resistance and tau within 15%, sag and rebound within 0.3 mV): **8 sets qualify, 9 with the single best.** Applying the knockout manipulations to all 9 (reproduction of the S-KO row = resting potential within 1.5 mV, input resistance within 15%, sag and rebound within 0.5 mV, declared before the run):

| manipulation | sets reproducing all four | resting potential ok | input resistance ok | sag ok | rebound ok |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K+ leak ×0.7 | **0 of 9** | 4 | 4 | 0 | 4 |
| K+ leak ×0.5 | 0 | 1 | 7 | 4 | 3 |
| tonic GABA-A ×0.5 | 0 | **0** | 2 | 0 | 0 |
| tonic GABA-A ×0 | 0 | **0** | 5 | 1 | 2 |
| tonic ×0.5 and K+ leak ×0.85 | 0 | 0 | 5 | 0 | 4 |
| Ih ×2 | 0 | 0 | **0** | 8 | 9 |

   (1) **Robust:** a tonic GABA-A loss never gets the resting potential right (0 of 9, including the complete loss) and Ih up never gets the input resistance right (0 of 9). So "tonic loss does not explain the resting potential; Ih does not explain the input resistance" holds across wild-type sets, as SCI says. (2) **Not robust:** "a loss of about 30% of the resting K+ leak reproduces the knockout profile". On SCI's own best set it gives RMP −74.77, Rin 150, sag 2.05, rebound 1.78 (against −73.62, 143, 2.57, 1.86): within my tolerances on three features and 0.52 mV short on sag; across all 9 sets (the best one included) the resting-potential and input-resistance fits are 4 of 9 each, rebound 4 of 9 and sag 0 of 9, so in the 8 other sets they are 3, 3, 3 and 0. A K+ leak loss moves the profile in the right direction in every set; the size ("about 30%") and the claim of a complete reproduction depend on the wild-type set drawn, and the profile has about three independent groups (SCI's own count) to constrain a model with five conductances and a capacitance. Say "direction supported, size not identified". (3) With 9 sets the check is small; a larger search is cheap and I can run it.
2. **The Gabrd fall comes with interneuron-subtype shifts in the same samples.** In the P47T cortex and prefrontal cortex (the regions where Gabrd falls, −0.80 FDR 0.062 and −0.82 FDR 0.085) **Lamp5 (a superficial interneuron marker) falls (−0.65 FDR 0.048; −0.86 FDR 0.013), while Sst rises (+0.84 FDR 0.089; +0.94 FDR 0.021) and Npy rises (+1.59 FDR 0.056; +1.12 FDR 0.015)**; Gad2 rises in PFC (+0.68, FDR 0.039); Slc17a7 rises (+0.66 FDR 0.046; +0.52 FDR 0.016); Pvalb is flat; the delta-lacking cerebellum moves the other way on Lamp5 (+0.59, ns). The held P7 class table cannot assign Gabrd to a subtype (neurons 1.2, microglia 3.0 FPKM, others ≤1.3; delta is low at that age). So **"fewer delta receptors per neuron" is not separable from "a different mix of the cells that carry delta or supply the ambient GABA"** (neurogliaform-type cells are the volume-transmission source of tonic GABA: ASSUMED general physiology, not checked against a source here). The tonic current is the right measurement; the transcript is not evidence for a per-cell receptor loss.
3. **The most useful role of I13 may be as the graded factor.** Held (ADV's premise check; Breton 2021): burst incidence 0 of 11, 4 of 23, 36 of 42 slices (WT, HT, KO) while resting potential, input resistance, sag and AP amplitude are a step (HT = KO). A candidate that is graded would explain incidence; tonic current is one. **A pre-stated prediction for the experiment's heterozygote arm:** tonic current is intermediate in HT (between WT and KO, not equal to KO), as incidence is. If it is equal to KO or to WT, it cannot be the graded factor, whatever it does in the KO. This sharpens D5b, which already contains the HT group.
4. **GAT-3 is astrocytic and falls beyond the astrocyte share.** In the held class table Slc6a11 (GAT-3) is 35 FPKM in neurons and 557 in astrocytes; the P47T cortex shift is −0.48 (FDR 0.38) against a median −0.08 for astrocyte-enriched genes of that region (`i4_metabolic/composition_check.json`), so the GAT-3 transcript fall is larger than an astrocyte-share change would give, but not significant. For the ambient-GABA argument (lower uptake could keep the tonic current normal), D5b should hold GAT-1 and GAT-3 apart (a GAT-1 blocker label and a GAT-3 condition), not as one 'uptake' step.

## What would separate the alternatives
* Tonic current in WT, HT and KO neocortex and in KO hippocampus, with the GAT-1 and GAT-3 conditions separate (D5b, as amended). Outcomes: lower in KO and graded in HT: I13 stands and is the graded factor; lower in KO and equal in HT: it explains the step, not the incidence; equal to wild type: killed (SCI's named test).
* A cell-type readout from the same slices (Lamp5, Sst, Npy, Pvalb by in situ or immunostaining counts per area): separates a receptor loss per cell from a change of the mix.
* The model: a larger search and the wild-type-set ensemble in `identifiability_runs.tsv`; the verdict to carry is the robust half.

## Limits
The identifiability check reuses SCI's functions and its assumptions (every conductance and the 500 ms pulse are ASSUMED); only the choice of wild-type set varies. Acceptance tolerances are mine and were declared before the run; SCI's best set misses the sag tolerance by 0.02 mV, so the 0-of-9 for the K+ leak is sensitive to that choice (its partial counts are in the table). The P47T rows are mRNA of a missense line (n 5 v 5; FDR as the table gives it). The class table is P7 and P17 wild-type pools.
