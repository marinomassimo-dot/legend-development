## What this says (and does not)
The public record contains no perturbation that suppresses a Wwox-loss phenotype, because the models in which it could exist are few and the curated ones score Wwox as a modifier of other genes' phenotypes: in the fly loss of Wwox alone has no phenotype; the mouse genotypes are single-gene or conditional-tumour constructs without annotated phenotype; the cell-line screens never used a WWOX-deficient background. The Lucas-Clarke Ldh lowering is therefore still the only suppressor known to us (a primary paper, abstract read only), and it is not in any curated source. Absence here is absence from curated and public screen data, not evidence that no suppressor exists: a screen in a Wwox-null background (cells or fly, for example by the ORCS format) has not been run in public data.
