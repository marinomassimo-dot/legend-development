## A HIT (defined before reading)
A hit is a perturbation of ANOTHER gene or pathway that SUPPRESSES (rescues, reduces) a phenotype caused by loss or reduction of Wwox / WWOX (null, knockdown, hypomorph) in an animal, organoid or cell system, with the suppressed phenotype named, in a source I can locate. NOT hits: an unrelated physical interactor; a genetic enhancer or a synthetic-lethal interaction (reported separately as 'enhancer'); a perturbation that suppresses a phenotype of WWOX OVERexpression or of a different gene; a rescue by re-expression of Wwox itself; a drug. Hits are counted per system; a hit whose phenotype is unit-less or whose organism, region or cell fraction is not stated is listed with 'not stated' and counted separately (the unit AND region rule of 2026-11-17).
