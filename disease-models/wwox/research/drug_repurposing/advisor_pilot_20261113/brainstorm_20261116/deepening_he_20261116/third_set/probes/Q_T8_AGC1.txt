Loss of a mitochondrial carrier in neuronal progenitors derived from patient stem cells causes failure of pyruvate-driven respiration, a shift to glycolysis, reduced proliferation and increased cell death. Propose 10 distinct levers that could restore energy production in these cells. For each, name the single manipulation (genetic, or a class at mechanism level) whose outcome would discriminate it from its nearest rival, and say what it predicts for oxygen consumption AND for cell survival.
