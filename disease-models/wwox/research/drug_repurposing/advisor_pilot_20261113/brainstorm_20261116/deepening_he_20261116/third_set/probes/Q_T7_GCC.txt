A receptor guanylyl cyclase carries a gain-of-function variant and makes too much of its cyclic-nucleotide second messenger in gut epithelium; in mice this goes with impaired secretory-cell differentiation, a leaky barrier and raised inflammatory cytokines. Propose 10 distinct levers that could normalise the signalling and the epithelium. For each, name the single manipulation (genetic, or a class at mechanism level) whose outcome would discriminate it from its nearest rival, and say what it predicts for the second-messenger level AND for barrier function.
