# Axis coverage of the set-2 generator outputs against the seven axes of the v4 template (HE; 2026-11-17; non-canonical; not medical advice)

Written for: ADV and the 08:46Z-opened MEETING (ask b). A free check, no delegate, no network: `axis_coverage.py` (keyword test over the 10 final hypotheses of each of the 18 runs of set 2; the keyword lists were fixed in the file before it was run) and its output `axis_coverage_output.txt`. It measures what the engine names, not whether it is right; crude and noisy (a word match, one pass, hypotheses can name no axis).
## What it shows
- Share of final hypotheses naming each axis (percent), baseline / v1 / v3: catalytic cycle (cofactors, ions, nucleotides, pH) 5 / 7 / 7; folding and stability 23 / 27 / 20; assembly, trafficking, localisation 37 / 20 / 20; substrate and metabolite supply 3 / 0 / 0; degradation and turnover 18 / 25 / 20; downstream bypass 15 / 20 / 15; cell type, timing, dose 22 / 27 / 33. **The two axes that carried the missed atoms of this programme, the catalytic/ion axis (T4 zinc) and the supply axis, are named by 0 to 7 percent of hypotheses in every condition, against 15 to 37 percent for the other five.** That is the descriptive fact the v4 enumeration step is built on: the unsampled axes are those two.
- Mean axes named per run (of 7; 6 runs per condition): baseline 4.33, v1 3.83, **v3 5.00**. v3's stage 1 widens the axis spread relative to v1 (and the final tens with no axis fall from 18/60 and 19/60 to 13/60), which is a correction to the reading 'v3 finds no more': it covers more axes, but not the atom axes; the T6 row is low for all conditions because a phage-repair question is not a protein-defect question (the axes apply poorly).
- Origin of the v3 final tens (from the stage-2 reports): 7 to 9 of every ten come from stage 1 (memory-free) and 1 to 3 are built from the held circuits (ENGINE): T4 2 and 1, T5 2 and 1, T6 3 and 2 (runs A and B), mean 1.8 of 10. So the corpus stage contributes about 18 percent of what v3 finally proposes; the rest is the memory-free list filtered by the corpus step.
## What it does not show
It does not show that naming an axis means the right lever; a keyword hit on 'metal' is not a metal lever; the share of the ion axis in the baseline (5 percent) is 3 of 60 hypotheses. It uses three targets of one set; T6 is off-model. It is a descriptive anchor for the v4 expectation (v4 should raise axes a and d from about 5 percent toward a seventh of the hypotheses), which QX on the set-3 v4 runs will measure with the same keyword lists, fixed now: the lists in `axis_coverage.py`.
