# Sixty hypotheses (statement and discriminating manipulation only)

## Z01
Statement: A limiting epithelial cGMP-hydrolysing capacity co-causes the overload, so raising hydrolysis, rather than lowering synthesis, normalises cGMP and the epithelium.
Discriminator: Epithelial overexpression of a cGMP-hydrolysing PDE on the variant background, compared with epithelial Abcc4 deletion. PDE gain gives intracellular cGMP down and extracellular cGMP down, barrier improved. Abcc4 loss gives intracellular cGMP up, extracellular down, barrier worse. A PDE gain that equally lowers cAMP confounds H3.

## Z02
Statement: The GOF raises the steady-state level of active receptor (reduced clearance, or over-stable protein), so lowering receptor protein through chaperone-client dependence lowers cGMP and restores the epithelium; the direction of any chaperone manipulation must be measured, not assumed.
Discriminating manipulation vs nearest rival H8 (dose/dimer composition): pulse-chase with proteasome and lysosome arms in GOF versus wild-type epithelium (the folder's own E-5 design, mechanism_intervention_map.md:853), then Hsp90-axis reduction against halving the gene dose. If abundance-driven, Hsp90-axis reduction lowers receptor protein and cGMP proportionally (cGMP per receptor unchanged) and the barrier improves. A proteasome block restores receptor level and cGMP under Hsp90 loss but not under gene-dose reduction. If specific-activity-driven, protein falls but cGMP per receptor stays high and the barrier is not rescued. Caveat: Hsp90-axis reduction is pleiotropic, including on inflammatory kinases.

## Z03
Statement: The excess cGMP drives CFTR-dependent anion/fluid secretion and NHE3 inhibition, and the resulting luminal flow and osmotic stress, not nuclear signalling, injure the barrier and raise cytokines.
Discriminator: Epithelial Cftr deletion (or a class that blocks anion channel gating) on the variant background. Predicts cGMP unchanged (the lever is downstream), barrier restored if flow-driven. An intact secretory-cell defect would show the route is not secretion alone.

## Z04
Statement: The excess comes from the number of variant receptors reaching the apical surface, so lowering ER export or raising ER-associated clearance of the variant lowers cGMP at fixed specific activity.
Discriminating manipulation (vs H2): Reduce ER-to-surface export of the variant (COPII cargo-adaptor reduction, or an ER-retention signal) and measure cGMP against surface-biotinylated receptor. H3 predicts cGMP falls in proportion to surface number, with ligand removal still acting additively and specific activity unchanged. H2 predicts cGMP per surface receptor is unchanged by export reduction. Barrier improves in step with total cGMP.

## Z05
Statement: The variant raises the receptor's gain toward normal luminal guanylin/uroguanylin tone, so cutting ligand supply lowers cGMP and restores the epithelium.
Discriminating manipulation (vs H2, the autoinhibition-loss rival): Ligand-null (Guca2a/Guca2b double knockout) on the variant allele, compared with variant plus ligand-null plus wild-type controls. H1 predicts cGMP falls toward wild type and barrier and secretory-cell counts recover with it. H2 predicts cGMP stays high and barrier stays abnormal. Include the wild-type ligand-null arm. The held lithium case failed its specificity test because the drug worked in wild type too (mechanism_intervention_map.md:477), so a ligand-null effect must be shown to exceed what it does in wild type.

## Z06
Statement: Epithelial cGMP-hydrolysing PDE capacity is too low to buffer the faster synthesis, so raising PDE activity, or removing PKG-dependent negative feedback only when it is itself the limiting step, lowers steady-state cGMP and relieves the epithelium (IPOTESI).
Discriminating manipulation vs nearest rival (cGMP export through ABCC4/5, a rival): epithelium-specific PDE gain-or-loss against Abcc4/Abcc5 deletion, in the GOF mouse. PDE gain predicts intracellular and luminal cGMP both fall, with a better barrier. PDE loss predicts both rise, with a worse barrier. Export loss predicts intracellular cGMP rises while extracellular cGMP falls (the two pools move in opposite directions), with a worse barrier. The PDE isoform that dominates gut epithelium is not known to me, so use the class-level manipulation.

## Z07
Statement: The variant lowers the ligand threshold, so normal guanylin/uroguanylin tone becomes supramaximal. Reducing ligand drive therefore lowers cGMP and restores secretory-lineage and barrier programmes.
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H2, abundance/half-life): Guca2a/Guca2b deletion on the GOF background. - If H1 holds: cGMP falls toward the wild-type window with total receptor protein unchanged. Barrier and goblet differentiation improve, but only down to a floor, because ligand loss is itself damaging to the barrier in wild type (S1 caveat, not a corpus datum). Over-reduction gives a loss-of-function-like leak. - If H2 holds: cGMP stays high.

## Z08
Statement: Barrier and differentiation damage is carried by PKG2 (PRKG2) acting downstream of cGMP, so removing PKG2 normalises the epithelium while cGMP stays high or rises (loss of PKG feedback on PDE) (IPOTESI).
Discriminating manipulation vs nearest rival H10 (secondary, flux-driven injury): epithelium-specific Prkg2 deletion against epithelium-specific Cftr or Slc12a2 deletion. Prkg2 deletion predicts cGMP unchanged or higher, fluid flux down, and both barrier and secretory differentiation recovered. Flux block predicts cGMP unchanged, flux down, the barrier recovered, and secretory differentiation not recovered. Recovery of differentiation is the discriminant.

## Z09
Statement: The raised cytokines are downstream of the epithelial lesion and then sustain barrier leak independently. Neutralising a TNF/IFN-γ-class signal restores the barrier without correcting cGMP. IPOTESI.
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H8): TNF/IFN-γ-class neutralisation on the GOF background, without touching the allele. - If H9 holds: cGMP is unchanged (or changed only via feedback on ligand or receptor expression), and the barrier improves partly. - If H8 holds: epithelial reversion normalises cGMP and the barrier first, with cytokines falling later.

## Z10
Statement: The phenotype scales with the fraction of GOF-active catalytic dimers in the epithelium, so a catalytically dead or null partner and adult-onset or compartment-restricted removal lower cGMP in proportion and reverse the barrier defect in the cell types where the damage is still reversible.
Discriminating manipulation vs nearest rival H6 (abundance): GOF with a catalytically dead partner against GOF with a null partner. Equal protein per cell cannot be assumed, so read receptor protein alongside. If it is dimer composition, the dead partner lowers cGMP more than the null partner at equal protein and the barrier follows. If it is abundance alone, the two give the same cGMP per receptor protein. Add tamoxifen-inducible adult removal in Lgr5 stem cells against villin-driven differentiated cells: stem-cell removal reverses secretory differentiation, differentiated-cell removal reverses only acute cGMP effects.

## Z11
Statement: cGMP damages differentiation and the barrier through PKG2 (Prkg2) as effector, so the second-messenger level can stay high while the damage stops when the effector is removed.
Discriminator: Epithelial-specific Prkg2 deletion on the variant background. Predicts cGMP unchanged or slightly up (loss of PKG feedback), cAMP unchanged, barrier normalised if PKG2 is the effector. If barrier is unchanged, H3 is favoured.

## Z12
Statement: The leak is due to missing secretory output downstream of the signal. Raising Atoh1 or lowering Notch restores secretory cells and barrier independently of cGMP.
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H10, Wnt tone): epithelial Atoh1 stabilisation or γ-secretase-class Notch reduction with Prkg2 intact. - If H6 holds: cGMP is unchanged, goblet and Paneth counts and barrier improve. - If H10 holds: improvement depends on the sign of the Wnt arm. - If counts rise but the barrier does not: the leak is not due to missing secretory cells, which favours H5 and H7.

## Z13
Statement: The GOF allele lowers the barrier set by ATP occupancy of the kinase-like regulatory domain, so the cyclase domain closes with too little ligand drive; reducing KHD-ATP coupling lowers cGMP and restores the epithelium (IPOTESI; the ATP-potentiation premise is recall, not source-checked).
Discriminating manipulation vs nearest rival H2 (ligand-driven): put the cis pocket-dead mutation on the GOF allele in a ligand-null (Guca2a/Guca2b-deficient) background and read basal cGMP. If the ATP route is real, basal cGMP falls below the ligand-null GOF value and the barrier improves. If the GOF is ligand-driven, the pocket-dead allele changes nothing in the ligand-null background. Caveat: if the pocket-dead allele abolishes all cyclase activity (ATP is classically needed for ligand activation, recall), it cannot discriminate. A titration of the pocket mutation, or a partial-coupling allele, is then needed.

## Z14
Statement: Excess cGMP/PKG damps epithelial Wnt/β-catenin signalling, so there are too few Wnt-dependent stem and Paneth cells and secretory differentiation fails.
Discriminating manipulation (vs H7): Epithelial stabilised β-catenin (Ctnnb1 exon 3) or R-spondin supply on the variant allele. H8 predicts cGMP is unchanged, Paneth and stem cells recover, and barrier improves while the Notch/Atoh1 status is unchanged. H7's Atoh1/Rbpj manipulation does not restore Wnt-dependent cells. The held "less Wnt is not a safe default" rule means a hyperactive arm should be run too. Excess Wnt should itself worsen the epithelium.

## Z15
Statement: Excess cGMP/PKG shifts progenitors away from the secretory lineage (loss of Atoh1 or gain of Notch/Hes1 signalling), so goblet, Paneth and enteroendocrine cells are too few. The mucus and antimicrobial layer thins, and the barrier leaks as a result.
Discriminating manipulation (vs H8, the Wnt-niche rival): Epithelial Rbpj deletion, or induced Atoh1, on the variant allele. H7 predicts cGMP is unchanged, goblet and Paneth numbers and mucus recover, and barrier improves. H8 predicts the Wnt-dependent compartment stays abnormal and barrier stays abnormal.

## Z16
Statement: Sustained cGMP signalling overloads the ER of secretory cells, so the terminal UPR arm kills or silences goblet and Paneth cells.
Discriminating manipulation (vs H7): Delete Ddit3, or overexpress spliced Xbp1, on the variant allele. H9 predicts cGMP is unchanged, secretory-cell survival, mucus and barrier improve, and Atoh1-positive lineage commitment stays unchanged. H7 predicts Ddit3 loss does not restore secretory numbers while Rbpj loss does.

## Z17
Statement: The leaky barrier and the cytokine rise are partly secondary to hypersecretory fluid and ion flux and luminal changes in a globally expressing GOF animal, not to a cell-autonomous epithelial lesion, so reducing the flux relieves the barrier while cGMP stays high (IPOTESI).
Discriminating manipulation vs nearest rival H4 (PKG2): epithelium-restricted Cftr or Slc12a2 deletion against Prkg2 deletion in the GOF mouse. Flux block predicts cGMP unchanged, luminal fluid down, the barrier improved, and secretory differentiation not recovered. Prkg2 deletion predicts differentiation recovered as well. Epithelium-restricted versus global GOF expression adds the cell-autonomy readout.

## Z18
Statement: The variant output depends on ER folding, glycosylation and exit of the transmembrane receptor to the apical surface, so lowering surface density, not catalytic activity, normalises cGMP.
Discriminator: A folding- or exit-defective receptor allele (e.g. a glycosylation-site allele) on the variant background, compared with a catalytic-site-dead allele. Predicts cGMP down and barrier improved if surface density is the lever. A catalytic-dead allele would instead remove output without changing density.

## Z19
Statement: Cytokines are secondary but form a feed-forward amplifier (cytokine → junction/secretory-fate damage → more cytokine), so cutting the receptor limb partly restores barrier without changing cGMP.
Discriminator: Epithelial Tnfrsf1a deletion on the variant background, run in parallel with an IL-6-family receptor (Il6st) deletion given the held IL-6-over-TNF pattern. Predicts cGMP unchanged (or slightly lower if cytokines raise receptor expression) and barrier partly restored if the loop is causal. The first of those two is the only one that is a single manipulation.

## Z20
Statement: The secretory-lineage defect is a fate-switch imbalance (Notch tone too high, or Atoh1 too low) driven by the signalling, so forcing secretory fate restores goblet and Paneth cells and the barrier even with cGMP high (IPOTESI).
Discriminating manipulation vs nearest rival H9 (Wnt tone): inducible epithelial Atoh1 gain (or gamma-secretase-class Notch reduction) in the GOF mouse, with nuclear β-catenin and a Wnt reporter read alongside. Predicts cGMP unchanged, goblet and Paneth cells restored, Wnt-reporter tone unchanged, and the barrier restored only if mucus or secretory function is causal. If Wnt tone moves and the rescue follows it, H9 is favoured instead.

## Z21
Statement: Only the epithelial compartment, in a dose window, drives the phenotype. Reverting the GOF allele in epithelium restores differentiation and barrier, and the dose-response is U-shaped, because over-reduction reproduces a loss-of-function-like leak.
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H7): inducible epithelial reversion of the GOF allele in adults with microbiota held constant. - If H8 holds: cGMP falls to near wild type, and the barrier recovers faster than the cytokine profile. Over-reducing the allele produces a loss-of-function-like leak. - If cGMP normalises but the barrier does not recover: a non-epithelial or microbial driver (H7, H9) is implicated.

## Z22
Statement: Export of cGMP across the basolateral or apical membrane (ABCC4/MRP4 class) is a rate-limiting sink, and the excess pool persists because efflux is saturated.
Discriminating manipulation (vs H5, the effector rival, and the PDE sink a rival): Epithelial Abcc4 deletion on the variant allele. H4 predicts intracellular cGMP rises further, extracellular cGMP falls, and barrier worsens. A PDE-sink mechanism predicts deletion changes little. A matched epithelial PDE-overexpression arm predicts intracellular cGMP and extracellular cGMP both fall.

## Z23
Statement: The GOF receptor is ligand-hypersensitive, so normal levels of endogenous guanylin or uroguanylin, tuned by luminal pH, drive the excess cGMP, and removing the ligand returns cGMP and the epithelium toward normal (IPOTESI).
Discriminating manipulation vs nearest rival H1: ligand deletion (Guca2a/Guca2b) in the GOF background, with the same deletion in wild type as the control. If ligand-dependent, GOF cGMP falls close to the wild-type ligand-null level and the barrier improves. If constitutive, cGMP stays high and the barrier is unchanged. The wild-type control separates a ligand effect from the known barrier phenotype of ligand loss alone (stage-1 recall, unverified).

## Z24
Statement: The secretory-differentiation defect is mediated by an epithelial Wnt/β-catenin tone that the cGMP overload shifts, and the direction of that shift has to be measured before any lever is chosen.
Discriminator: Epithelial Ctnnb1 heterozygous deletion on the variant background, after a baseline measure of nuclear β-catenin. Predicts cGMP unchanged. Barrier and secretory cells improve if Wnt is high and worsen if Wnt is low, so the sign decides the outcome. Dropped from stage 1, with the reason for each (none dropped only because the folder is silent): - a rival is folded in as H4's rival. - a rival is folded in as H1's rival. - a rival (desensitisation): its test is a phospho-site knock-in on wild type, which asks whether feedback exists rather than offering a lever; the folder is silent (0 hits), which is "not stated". - a rival (microbiota): the lever acts on luminal cause, not on the signal, and is covered by the generic germ-free test. The folder is silent on gut microbiota; LPS hits are sepsis/brain only. - a rival (STAT3 repair): not on the second messenger; held STAT3 text is the WWOX brake axis (discovery_ledger_current.md:223-226), in the opposite direction. - a rival (ligand gradient): the folder is silent; its discriminator duplicates H1.

## Z25
Statement: Insufficient cGMP-hydrolysing capacity lets the GOF flux accumulate. Raising epithelial PDE5/PDE9-class hydrolysis clamps cGMP regardless of the receptor lesion and relieves the epithelium. The ABCC4 efflux branch (a rival) is a sub-branch with an opposite extracellular prediction.
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H2): epithelial PDE5/PDE9-class overexpression with the GOF receptor and its abundance left unchanged. - If H3 holds: intracellular cGMP falls with total GC-C unchanged. The barrier improves only if intracellular cGMP is the damaging pool. - If H2 holds: PDE overexpression does not alter receptor mass, so only the degron arm changes that. - Abcc4 deletion (sub-branch): intracellular cGMP rises and extracellular falls. The barrier worsens if the intracellular pool is the harmful one.

## Z26
Statement: The GOF receptor escapes normal lysosomal turnover, so its half-life and steady-state abundance are raised. Restoring the turnover route, not folding capacity, lowers cGMP and the epithelial damage. IPOTESI.
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H1, ligand tone): an induced-degradation degron knock-in on the GOF allele, with a lysosomal-block arm as the control. - If H2 holds: cGMP and total receptor fall together irrespective of ligand. Lysosomal block raises both. Barrier recovers with a lag. - If H1 holds: ligand deletion lowers cGMP with receptor mass unchanged. - Predicted risk: over-depletion gives a loss-of-function-like leak.

## Z27
Statement: The cytokine rise is a causal amplifier of the barrier defect (an epithelial lesion driving a cytokine loop that opens junctions further), so neutralising the loop at the cytokine or NF-κB/inflammasome class partly restores the barrier without changing the second-messenger level.
Discriminating manipulation vs nearest rival H8 (epithelium-intrinsic dose/timing): cytokine-receptor deletion class in the GOF mouse against inducible removal of the GOF allele in the adult epithelium. Cytokine-axis deletion predicts cGMP unchanged and a partly rescued barrier. Adult allele removal predicts cGMP down and the barrier rescued only if the damage is reversible.

## Z28
Statement: The variant removes an intramolecular brake in the receptor's regulatory (kinase-homology) domain, giving ligand-independent cyclase activity that is rescued only by restoring the brake in cis.
Discriminating manipulation (vs H3, the surface-abundance rival): Add the cis nucleotide-site-dead allele to the variant receptor and compare specific activity (cGMP per surface receptor, from surface biotinylation) at unchanged surface level. H2 predicts per-receptor activity falls and total cGMP falls with surface number unchanged. H3 predicts per-receptor activity is unchanged. Barrier tracks cGMP in both cases.

## Z29
Statement: Excess cGMP/PKG signalling shifts crypt Wnt/β-catenin tone, and the secretory-differentiation defect follows that shift. The sign is open: tone could be too low for Wnt-dependent Paneth maturation, or too high. Resolving the sign selects which Wnt-directed lever is correct (IPOTESI; the GC-C/Wnt link is recall, not folder).
Discriminating manipulation vs nearest rival H5 (Notch/Atoh1): measure crypt nuclear β-catenin in GOF versus wild type first. Then run epithelial Ctnnb1 stabilisation against Ctnnb1 deletion in the GOF mouse (the folder's two-armed design, mechanism_intervention_map.md:858). Predicts cGMP unchanged in both arms. The arm that matches the true Wnt sign improves Paneth-cell maturation and the barrier, and the opposite arm worsens them. Atoh1 gain (H5) predicts no change in Wnt tone.

## Z30
Statement: Much of the damage is physical and secondary to CFTR-driven, NHE3-inhibited fluid secretion (mucus washout, shear). Blocking that output protects the barrier without touching cGMP. INFERENZA.
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H4): epithelial Cftr deletion (or NHE3 restoration). - If H5 holds: cGMP is unchanged, fluid output is normal, and mucus and barrier improve. - If H4 holds: diarrhoea resolves but differentiation and barrier do not.

## Z31
Statement: PKG2 (PRKG2) carries the damaging signal that suppresses secretory-lineage and barrier programmes. Removing the kinase normalises the epithelium while cGMP stays high.
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H5): epithelial Prkg2 deletion with CFTR intact. - If H4 holds: cGMP is unchanged or higher (loss of kinase feedback), differentiation and barrier improve, and diarrhoea may persist. - If H5 holds: Prkg2 deletion changes fluid secretion only. Only Cftr deletion would help, and it would leave cGMP unchanged.

## Z32
Statement: Cytokine elevation and part of the leak are driven by the microbiota and by deficient supply of barrier-supporting metabolites (butyrate, tryptophan/AHR ligands), not by cGMP directly. IPOTESI.
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H8, epithelium-intrinsic): rederive GOF mice germ-free, or colonise with a defined butyrate/AHR-producing community. - If H7 holds: cGMP is unchanged, cytokines fall, barrier partly recovers. - If H8 holds: with microbiota constant, inducible epithelial reversion normalises cGMP and barrier.

## Z33
Statement: Excess cGMP inhibits a cGMP-inhibited cAMP phosphodiesterase (the PDE3 class), so cAMP rises, and the cAMP excess is the damaging signal.
Discriminating manipulation (vs H5): Epithelial expression of a PDE3-resistant cAMP-degrading allele, or a cAMP-specific PDE, on the variant allele. H6 predicts cGMP is unchanged, cAMP falls, and barrier improves. H5 predicts barrier does not improve and Prkg2 loss is what rescues.

## Z34
Statement: The epithelial damage is carried by the apical cGMP-dependent kinase PKG2 (PRKG2) and not by cGMP itself, so removing the kinase decouples high cGMP from the phenotype.
Discriminating manipulation (vs H6, the cAMP relay): Epithelial Prkg2 knockout on the variant allele. H5 predicts cGMP is unchanged or higher (feedback lost) while barrier and differentiation normalise. If the cAMP relay carries the damage, barrier stays abnormal. An H6 arm should add a cAMP sink and show cGMP unchanged but barrier improved.

## Z35
Statement: The variant raises receptor output by amplifying the response to endogenous guanylin/uroguanylin, so cutting ligand drive pulls cGMP and the epithelial phenotype back toward wild type.
Discriminator: Guca2a/Guca2b double deletion on the variant background, compared with a heterozygous receptor-null allele on the same background. Ligand loss gives cGMP down toward wild type and barrier restored if H1 is right. If the variant is constitutive, ligand loss changes nothing and only the receptor-dose reduction works. Beware an overshoot: held rule "neither too little nor too much", mechanism_intervention_map.md:412 and :669. Full ligand loss may undershoot cGMP and damage the barrier, because receptor-null mice leak too (a rival premise, not held).

## Z36
Statement: Excess cGMP inhibits cGMP-inhibited PDE3 and raises cAMP, and cAMP-PKA, not PKG, carries the harm.
Discriminator: Epithelium-restricted gain of a PDE3-class cAMP hydrolase (or a PDE3-resistant allele) on the variant background. Predicts cGMP unchanged, cAMP down, barrier restored if H3 is right. If Prkg2 loss (H2) rescues instead, this manipulation leaves barrier unchanged.

## Z37
Statement: cGMP signalling suppresses the Atoh1-dependent secretory programme directly, so the differentiation defect comes first and the leak and cytokines follow from the thinned mucus and Paneth-cell layer.
Discriminator: Inducible epithelial Atoh1 re-expression on the variant background. Predicts cGMP unchanged (it acts downstream), secretory cells restored, barrier improved only if differentiation is causal. If barrier stays leaky, H6 is favoured.

## Z38
Statement: Excess cGMP signalling displaces crypt Wnt/β-catenin tone out of the window needed for secretory maturation. The direction is unknown and must be set empirically. Correcting the tone in the right direction restores differentiation without changing cGMP. IPOTESI.
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H6, direct lineage bypass): the two-armed Wnt-tone test in GOF crypt organoids, a tankyrase-class lowering arm versus a GSK3β-inhibitor-class raising arm, after measuring nuclear β-catenin. - If H10 holds: cGMP is unchanged, and only the arm of the correct sign improves differentiation and barrier. The wrong arm worsens them, and the crypt-turnover risk applies. - If H6 holds: Atoh1 stabilisation or Notch reduction improves differentiation irrespective of arm.

## Z39
Statement: Excess cGMP remodels tight-junction composition directly (a claudin-2-type pore pathway), independent of secretory cell fate, which makes the leak a junctional effect.
Discriminator: Epithelial claudin-2 deletion on the variant background. Predicts cGMP unchanged, pore-pathway leak reduced, secretory cells unchanged. If leak persists, H5 is favoured.

## Z40
Statement: The raised cytokines are a feed-forward cause of the leak through an IL-6/gp130/STAT3-dominated loop, not through TNF/MLCK, and cut the loop and the barrier recovers with cGMP left high.
Discriminating manipulation (vs the a rival TNF/MLCK rival): Il6 deletion versus Tnfrsf1a deletion, each on the variant allele. H10 predicts cGMP is unchanged, Il6 deletion improves barrier, and Tnfrsf1a deletion changes little. The TNF/MLCK rival predicts the reverse. Avoid epithelial Stat3 deletion as the arm, because STAT3 is also a regenerative signal and its loss could worsen barrier.
