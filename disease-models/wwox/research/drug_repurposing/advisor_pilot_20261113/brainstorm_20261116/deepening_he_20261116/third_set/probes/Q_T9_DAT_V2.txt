Missense variants in a plasma-membrane neurotransmitter reuptake carrier cause misfolding and loss of the carrier at the cell surface. Knock-in mice carrying such a variant show impaired uptake and locomotor hyperactivity. Propose 10 distinct levers that could normalise the hyperactivity. For each, name the single manipulation (genetic, or a class at mechanism level) whose outcome would discriminate it from its nearest rival, and say what it predicts for locomotor activity AND for the carrier's level at the cell surface.
