## 2 · Addition: a second proteostasis lever, pifithrin-mu
The Discussion and Results report that pifithrin-mu (an inhibitor of HSP70, given i.p. three times 48 h apart) INCREASES the midbrain level of the A313V DAT protein, with no detectable change in locomotor activity (two-way ANOVA: no main effect of treatment). So the chaperone/proteostasis class gives, in this paper: noribogaine, no rescue of DAT expression at the tested regimen (the authors note that longer treatment might be needed to rule it out); pifithrin-mu, a rise of midbrain carrier without a behavioural rescue. Scoring consequence for NEGATIVE ATOM 1 (unchanged in wording): a run that ranks a chaperone, folding corrector or proteostasis lever as a RESCUE of the hyperactivity is a false positive; a run that ranks it as raising the surface carrier while predicting NO behavioural rescue, or only a partial one, is not a false positive. The reader prompt for T9 (QN1) asks for a predicted behavioural rescue, not only a surface rise.
