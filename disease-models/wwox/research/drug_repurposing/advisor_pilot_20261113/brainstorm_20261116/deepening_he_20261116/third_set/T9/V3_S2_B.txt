H1. Origin: S1-6 (OUTSIDE)
- Statement: ER export capacity, not folding, limits delivery of the fold-competent fraction of the variant carrier, so raising neuronal export capacity increases surface carrier and lowers hyperactivity.
- Corpus test: NOT STATED. COPII/Sec24/SAR1/ER exit/ER export gave 0 hits in disease-models. Rival terms (ERAD, Hrd1, Sel1L, p97, VCP) gave 0 real hits. The nearest deciding line is /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/research/dismissal_ledger_current.md:68 (D-02). It says the ER-quality-control and chemical-chaperone logic is valid only for ER/secretory proteins, which a plasma-membrane carrier is. That meets the compartment condition but says nothing about export rate. The 52 generic "trafficking/surface/plasma membrane" hits were not read individually.
- Usual reading vs this one: ER exit is passive once folded. Here export is rate-limiting and separable from folding.
- Rejection: export-adaptor gain raises wild-type surface carrier but leaves the variant's surface carrier flat. Or the ER pool rises with no surface gain.
- Discriminator (nearest rival H6, degradation triage): neuron-restricted gain of the export adaptor, with the carrier's export motif mutated in cis as the control. H1 predicts surface carrier up, and the pulse-chase ER-to-surface transit faster with no rise in total carrier or half-life. Locomotor activity falls. The export-motif-dead allele loses the gain. H6 predicts total carrier and half-life rise.

H2. Origin: S1-12 (OUTSIDE)
- Statement: Hyperactivity is set by transmitter supply as well as by clearance, so partial reduction of transmitter synthesis or release lowers extracellular tone and activity without touching the carrier.
- Corpus test: NOT STATED. Tyrosine hydroxylase/reuptake/autoreceptor/vesicular gave 0 hits. "Dopamin" gave 1 unrelated hit (/tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/research/fulltext_dossiers/PMID42128308_partial_locators.md:214, a WWOX-JNK Parkinson's line). Rival (downstream receptor, H10) is held only as NMDAR (see H10). The corpus rule that marker abundance, transmitter concentration and circuit function are separate questions (/tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md:53-56) is a caution, not a contradiction.
- Usual reading vs this one: only reuptake sets tone. Here supply is an independent lever.
- Rejection: heterozygous loss of the rate-limiting enzyme in the knock-in leaves activity and extracellular transmitter unchanged.
- Discriminator (nearest rival H10): cross in a heterozygous allele of the rate-limiting synthesis enzyme, versus conditional deletion of the postsynaptic receptor. H2 predicts activity falls, extracellular transmitter falls, and surface carrier is unchanged. H10 predicts activity falls with transmitter still high and surface carrier unchanged.

H3. Origin: S1-13 (OUTSIDE)
- Statement: A partly redundant clearance route (a paralogous or low-affinity carrier, neuronal or glial) can be recruited to substitute for the lost surface carrier, lowering hyperactivity with the variant carrier's surface level unchanged.
- Corpus test: NOT STATED. paralog/GAT-3/OCT3/PMAT/alternative or glial uptake gave 0 hits across disease-models. The closest held carrier circuit is the NKCC1/KCC2 pair, which is unmeasured in every WWOX system (/tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/meta/meta_gaba_paradox_current.md:116,156). It concerns the chloride gradient, not compensation. /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/research/commit_candidates/CC-20260826-EGABA-EXPERIMENT-01.md:188 says total carrier can be normal while surface carrier is not, so surface level must be measured separately.
- Usual reading vs this one: the carrier is non-redundant. Here redundancy is partial and recruitable.
- Rejection: paralog gain changes neither extracellular transmitter nor activity in the knock-in.
- Discriminator (nearest rival H2): paralog-null cross on the knock-in background. H3 predicts activity rises further, extracellular clearance falls, and surface variant carrier is unchanged. The benefit of paralog gain is lost in the paralog-null. The H2 effect (reduced supply) persists in the paralog-null.

H4. Origin: ENGINE (built on CLAIM 030, the held rule that residual function, not abundance, sets severity)
- Statement: Variant carrier that reaches the surface keeps reduced per-molecule transport, so potentiating the transport cycle of the surface-resident fraction normalises uptake and activity without raising surface carrier number.
- Corpus test: SUPPORT by analogy only (WWOX protein, not a transporter). /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/registries/claim_registry_current.md:559 (CLAIM 030): protein abundance does not predict severity, residual function does (P47T normal protein, binding abolished, mild; Q230P absent, severe). Stable-but-inert is a demonstrated phenotype: /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/analysis/proteostasis_rationale.md:96 and /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/research/discovery_ledger_current.md:1027 (P282A). Weakness: no WWOX variant has been shown to be rescuable by any class (proteostasis_rationale.md:35), so the lever is untested.
- Usual reading vs this one: rescue means restoring carrier number at the surface. Here the surface fraction is the target and function per carrier is the lever.
- Rejection: the surface-resident variant fraction measures transport-dead, or the potentiating class changes activity equally in an ER-retained variant control.
- Discriminator (nearest rival H5, abundance rescue): a positive allosteric potentiator class of the transport cycle, applied in the knock-in and in a line with an ER-retention signal in cis. H4 predicts surface carrier unchanged by surface biotinylation, activity falls in the knock-in, and no effect in the ER-retained control. H5 predicts surface carrier rises.

H5. Origin: S1-1, with S1-2 as the rival (INSIDE)
- Statement: A site-specific ligand that binds and stabilises the folding intermediate raises surface carrier and lowers hyperactivity, but it can bind the carrier's own transport site and so rescue abundance while abolishing function.
- Corpus test: SUPPORT for the class and CONTRADICT for the unqualified claim. Three chaperone families: /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/analysis/proteostasis_rationale.md:53-57. Stability-versus-function tension, "stability is bought with occlusion": /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md:348-353 (and discovery_ledger_current.md:1107). Compartment: 4-PBA/TUDCA were withdrawn for WWOX as "wrong compartment" (cytosolic) at mechanism_intervention_map.md:714-718 and dismissal_ledger_current.md:68. For a plasma-membrane carrier that verdict flips to "right compartment", which supports the generic route S1-2 for this question.
- Usual reading vs this one: rescue is one switch (more protein). Here a ligand can raise abundance without function, and the generic-versus-specific split is tested.
- Rejection: ligand-rescued surface carrier is transport-dead, or surface carrier rises with no activity change.
- Discriminator (nearest rival: generic chemical chaperone/proteostasis class): a substrate-site-dead but fold-competent mutation in cis on the variant. Site-specific ligand: surface rescue lost, no locomotor change. Generic class: surface carrier still rises and activity still falls, if the carrier retains function.

H6. Origin: ENGINE, reworked from S1-3 (INSIDE)
- Statement: A chaperone-driven triage step routes the misfolded carrier to degradation, so removing the delivering step (not boosting folding) raises total and surface carrier and lowers hyperactivity.
- Corpus test: SUPPORT by analogy for a route-dependent sign, CONTRADICT for a uniform "more chaperone = better" reading. /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/research/dismissal_ledger_current.md:69 (D-03): the sign depends on which chaperone and which route, and HSC70 delivers to the lysosome. /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md:720-723 (N-12): raising HSP70 may accelerate destruction. /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/analysis/data/redteam/discriminating_experiment.md:38,41: the proteasome probe is mute, so the lysosomal arm and the LAMP2A/ESCRT route-split are the discriminators. Evidence is P252A only and CMA is not shown for Q230P (discovery_ledger_current.md:1000-1027). S1-3's "reduce Hsp70 holding to free ER exit" has the opposite sign from the held WWOX result, and the carrier's route is unknown.
- Usual reading vs this one: degradation is the default fate and unavoidable. Here a specific delivering step is removable and the proteasome-versus-lysosome route must be measured.
- Rejection: knock-down of the delivering chaperone leaves total and surface carrier flat, or shifts the carrier to an ER aggregate without a surface gain.
- Discriminator (nearest rival H1): neuron-restricted haploinsufficiency of the delivering chaperone (HSPA8-class). H6 predicts total carrier and half-life rise, surface carrier rises, and activity falls. The rise is lost if the export motif is also mutated, and a proteasome-arm genetic block does nothing. H1 predicts the surface gain with no change in half-life.

H7. Origin: S1-9, with S1-15 (timing) as the modifier (INSIDE)
- Statement: Neuron-restricted delivery of wild-type carrier adds functional surface carrier and lowers hyperactivity, with an efficacy ceiling set by whether the variant traps it and by the age of delivery.
- Corpus test: SUPPORT. Gene addition, chain A: /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md:164-199. Window P0-P5 and a dose threshold, not a gradient: mechanism_intervention_map.md:410,414. Haploinsufficiency tolerated, so partial restoration may suffice: /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/registries/claim_registry_current.md:593 (CLAIM 032). Caution: after high-dose WWOX AAV the open-field velocity and distance exceed wild type by about 1.21x and 1.26x (/tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/analysis/therapeutic_routing_and_endpoint_hardening.md:180-181). Hyperactivity versus overexpression overshoot was not discriminated (lines 240-243). Dominant-negative gave 0 hits, so the trapping question is not stated.
- Usual reading vs this one: the mutant has no role once wild type is supplied. Here a trapping mutant and a closing window both cap the effect.
- Rejection: the vector gives the same surface gain and the same activity fall in the knock-in as in the null.
- Discriminator (nearest rival H8): the same neuron-restricted vector in the knock-in versus the null of the carrier, at the same age. H7 (no trapping) predicts equal surface gain and equal fall in activity in both. A shortfall in the knock-in indicates H8. Overexpression above wild type may push activity below normal.

H8. Origin: S1-10 (OUTSIDE)
- Statement: The variant co-assembles with wild-type carrier into a trapped oligomer, so allele-specific lowering of the variant raises wild-type surface carrier and lowers hyperactivity.
- Corpus test: NOT STATED. dominant-negative gave 0 hits and oligomer/co-assembly gave no carrier-relevant hit. Haploinsufficiency is tolerated for WWOX (claim_registry_current.md:593, CLAIM 032), but WWOX is not a known oligomeric carrier, so that does not decide it. The allele-asymmetry argument that boosting expression preferentially amplifies the missense allele (mechanism_intervention_map.md:660-671, N-05) gives indirect support for allele-specific logic. The corpus does not cover it for carriers.
- Usual reading vs this one: the phenotype is simple haploinsufficiency. Here the variant poisons its wild-type partner.
- Rejection: the knock-in/null compound and the knock-in/wild-type heterozygote have equal activity and equal surface carrier.
- Discriminator (nearest rival H7): knock-in/null compound versus knock-in/wild-type. H8 predicts the knock-in/wild-type heterozygote is more affected, and allele-specific silencing of the variant raises surface carrier (wild-type-derived) and lowers activity. A null/wild-type heterozygote is near normal.

H9. Origin: S1-5 (INSIDE, partial)
- Statement: Chronic unfolded-protein-response/integrated-stress activation by the retained variant causes network dysfunction independently of the missing transport, so dampening it lowers hyperactivity without restoring the carrier.
- Corpus test: NOT STATED for a carrier knock-in. It is held only as an assay panel. /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/research/discovery_ledger_current.md:390-395 (DL-MECH-023): belief "low-medium in the neurone", with p-PERK/p-eIF2a/ATF4/CHOP as the proposed readouts. The CNS aim is survival, not apoptosis. A Drosophila ATF4/UPR branch is noted at /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/meta/meta_metabolism_current.md:163. ISRIB/eIF2B gave 0 hits.
- Usual reading vs this one: hyperactivity is pure loss of function. Here part of it is gain-of-toxicity from ER stress.
- Rejection: ISR markers (eIF2a phosphorylation, ATF4 targets, CHOP) are normal in the knock-in brain.
- Discriminator (nearest rival H5): a signalling-dead PERK/eIF2a-phosphorylation allele crossed to the knock-in. H9 predicts activity falls and surface carrier unchanged. The cross is silent if ISR markers are normal. H5 predicts surface carrier rises.

H10. Origin: S1-11 (principle INSIDE, dopamine instance OUTSIDE)
- Statement: Hyperactivity is carried by postsynaptic hyper-stimulation, so blocking the downstream receptor lowers activity whatever the carrier does.
- Corpus test: SUPPORT by analogy. /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/research/commit_candidates/CC-20260826-NMDAR-CONJUNCTION-01.md:30,49-51: NMDAR blockade abolishes the burst in the WWOX-deficient slice, with a rebound of about 1.85x baseline on washout. /tmp/legend_cut_1867a26a8/disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md:421-441 (R-02) holds it at PROMISING_BUT_MECHANISTIC_GAP. Dopamine receptors: 0 hits. Caveat: symptom control did not change development (mechanism_intervention_map.md:748-757, N-15). The rival global inhibitory/arousal push is held as unmeasured premise plus a safety signal (mechanism_intervention_map.md:651-658, N-04).
- Usual reading vs this one: the only route is restoring the carrier. Here the downstream receptor is sufficient.
- Rejection: a receptor-blocking class does not lower activity in the knock-in.
- Discriminator (nearest rival H2): conditional deletion of the downstream receptor in the target neurons of the knock-in, compared with the global-tone rival, whose wild-type littermates fall by the same fraction (S1-14). H10 predicts activity falls, surface carrier is unchanged, and extracellular transmitter stays high. Wild-type activity is spared.
