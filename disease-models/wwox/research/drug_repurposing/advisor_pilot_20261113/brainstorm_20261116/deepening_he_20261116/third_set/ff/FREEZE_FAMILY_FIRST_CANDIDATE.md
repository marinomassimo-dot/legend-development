## The idea
The C2M trial (87,817 tokens) showed the gap is the CUE: asked for members and directions of the metal-ion class named by an enumeration without zinc, the model listed Mg2+, Mn2+ and Ca2+/chelators and never zinc, although it knows zinc inhibits cyclases (T7 probe, and C2M run B1 where zinc was in view). The T7 probe also showed that asked about a NEIGHBOUR the model volunteers the fact ('Zn2+ inhibits adenylyl cyclase as a general principle'). The family-first step asks first for the defective protein's family and its two or three closest related families, then for every substance, ion, cofactor or condition known to modulate ANY member of those families with the direction, and only then maps them to the defect (the 'connect domains' move: the adjacent literature holds the assay).
