Scope note, stated once. The folder holds no measured social phenotype in any WWOX model. Behaviour in the files I read is limited to open-field, anxiety, rotarod and locomotor readouts (PMID42422765_partial_locators.md; "neurobehavioral and motor outcomes indistinguishable from WT"). therapeutic_overclaim_closure.md line 75 lists `three-chamber` and `social interaction` as assay-name probes that surfaced no new file. All "social" content below is therefore ESPANSIONE or IPOTESI built on WWOX circuit, myelin, GABA, GSK3β and metabolic data.

The folder also says three things that constrain every seizure prediction below:
- Seizures are a rat `lde/lde` phenotype, and mouse seizure status is contested (CLAIM 037; CC-20260826-SEIZURE-RECONCILIATION-01). Mouse seizure readouts here mean ECoG SWD (spike-wave discharges) or PTZ threshold.
- Sodium-channel blockers are "target not causally downstream of WWOX" (N-03). Class-2 agents (LEV, LTG) are described as bypassing GABA (meta_gaba_paradox).
- CLAIM 031 (in observation, N=2) is the clinical precedent: "cognitive and psychomotor impairment preceded the onset … did not improve" despite better epileptic control.

All manipulations are assumed to run in a Wwox-deficient background on a background ASM that already controls the ictal phenotype. "Seizures" then means residual ECoG/PTZ susceptibility off or on drug.

H1. Statement: A prenatal or perinatal wiring defect (progenitor/radial-glia migration and layering) fixes the social-circuit architecture before any seizure exists, so seizure-suppressing drugs act on a downstream excitability layer and cannot reach it. 
Held evidence: CLAIM 014/015/022 in registries/claim_registry_current.md say loss perturbs prenatal cortical development; mechanism_intervention_map.md (Chain A boundary 3) says neuronal rescue "does not repair the progenitor/radial-glia defect, which is largely prenatal"; CLAIM 031. Against it: PAPER 021 (Tochigi) is corrected to PND5–21 maturation, not prenatal assembly. 
Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading treats the social deficit as a consequence of seizure burden. This one makes it a pre-seizure lesion that is timing-locked, so it is fixed once the window has passed whatever the seizure state. 
Rejecting observation: embryonic-progenitor-restricted Wwox loss leaves social behaviour and layering normal, while postnatal post-mitotic loss alone reproduces the deficit (three-chamber test plus layer-marker histology). 
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H2, ongoing network state): Wwox-flox with an embryonic progenitor driver (Emx1-Cre) versus a tamoxifen-timed postnatal excitatory-neuron driver (CaMKIIa-CreERT2), with matched restoration arms. Predicts: progenitor-only deletion gives a social deficit with little or no seizure phenotype, and postnatal deletion gives seizures with intact social behaviour. The opposite pattern supports H2.

H2. Statement: A primary, NMDAR- and gap-junction-dependent bursting state in medial prefrontal cortex (mPFC) pyramidal neurons (a state, not a seizure) corrupts social-relevant processing, and the ASM suppresses ictal spread without normalising that state. 
Held evidence: CLAIM 021 and meta_network_myelin_glia_current.md (bursting, phase–amplitude coupling, rebound, "bursting depends on NMDAR and gap junctions"); mechanism_intervention_map.md (Chain B; d-APV abolishes the burst; carbenoxolone downgraded; N-03). WM BLOCK 1 prioritises EEG state over seizure count. 
Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading counts seizure freedom as network control. This one separates the ictal and interictal network state, and says the state persists in social-relevant cortex. 
Rejecting observation: mPFC slice or in vivo burst and phase–amplitude-coupling measures are normal on the ASM while social behaviour is still impaired. 
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H3, PV output deficit): mPFC-restricted deletion of the NMDAR obligatory subunit (Grin1-flox, AAV-Cre, restricted to layer 2/3 pyramidal cells) in the Wwox-deficient animal. Predicts: bursting and the social deficit are rescued, and seizure susceptibility (generated elsewhere) is unchanged. If social behaviour is not rescued, H2 is rejected.

H3. Statement: Functional output of parvalbumin (PV) interneurons in mPFC is reduced, so excitation–inhibition balance and gamma synchrony fail for social coding, while the ASM acts on pyramidal firing or release. 
Held evidence: CLAIM 005 (PV marker −44% hippocampus in one systemic KO; narrowed, marker only); CLAIM 021 (reduced spontaneous inhibition in L2/3). Against it: meta_gaba_paradox_current.md and mechanism_intervention_map.md §2.3 say marker, survival and function are separate layers, and the systemic-null confounder (CLAIM 036) prevents separating cell-autonomous from secondary injury. The mPFC and social localisation is an IPOTESI. 
Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading counts PV markers as lost cells. This one reads them as mis-timed or weak output, which a pyramidal-targeted ASM would not fix. 
Rejecting observation: PV-to-pyramidal IPSC amplitude and PV-cell spike timing in mPFC are normal in the model. 
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H4, depolarising GABA): Pvalb-Cre hM3Dq (Gq-DREADD) activation in mPFC. Predicts: it rescues the social deficit with little change in seizures, because the generator is hippocampal or elsewhere. Failure of PV drive, or worsening, favours H4.

H4. Statement: GABA stays depolarising or shunting (an NKCC1/KCC2 chloride gradient defect) in a subset of circuits, so inhibition is excitatory where social coding needs inhibition. The ASM suppresses seizures through a non-GABA route and leaves this circuit untouched. 
Held evidence: DL-MECH-035 (research/discovery_ledger_current.md; the authors' hypothesis in organoids only); meta_gaba_paradox_current.md Hypothesis 1 and Open Questions 1–2 (no E_GABA, chloride, NKCC1 or KCC2 measurement in any WWOX model). Contradicting statement: mechanism_intervention_map.md §2.3 and N-04 record that vigabatrin resolved spasms in a WWOX-null child, "as a clinical predictor it was falsified" (FM-018). I keep H4 only as a circuit-restricted social-deficit mechanism, which the vigabatrin case does not address. 
Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading treats depolarising GABA as the cause of seizures and drug failure. This one makes it a separate cause of the social deficit that is independent of seizure. 
Rejecting observation: perforated-patch E_GABA in mPFC pyramidal neurons from adult Wwox-deficient animals equals wild type. 
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H3): KCC2 (Slc12a5) overexpression in mPFC pyramidal neurons using an AAV with a CaMKIIa promoter. Predicts: the social deficit is rescued while seizures are unchanged. In the same animals, Pvalb-Cre Gq activation (H3's rescue) fails or worsens the social deficit, because it increases excitatory GABA.

H5. Statement: Neuron-driven, non-cell-autonomous hypomyelination slows long-range conduction (mPFC–amygdala, callosal), producing a connectivity-based social deficit that is independent of seizure generation. 
Held evidence: CLAIM 003, 004 and the meta_network_myelin_glia_current.md integrated model (Syn-Cre deletion causes hypomyelination, Olig2-Cre and GFAP-Cre give no anomaly; oligodendrocytes never transduced yet g-ratio improves; unmyelinated axons per field stay abnormal after rescue); Abudiab 2025, a cell-autonomous oligodendroglial role, is review-sourced only. The meta file treats hypomyelination as a possible amplifier of seizures, which argues against pure independence. I keep H5 because the amplifier role is a possible, not a demonstrated, route. 
Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading treats myelin as secondary pathology that follows seizures. This one makes it the causal substrate of the social deficit, and a second-order contributor to seizures. 
Rejecting observation: a myelin-matched control cohort (comparable g-ratio and conduction velocity) still shows the social deficit. 
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H1): in neuron-restricted Wwox-null mice, an oligodendrocyte-lineage-restricted pro-differentiation manipulation (Pdgfra-CreER driving Myrf or Sox10 expression) that restores myelination without neuronal WWOX. Predicts: g-ratio, conduction velocity and the social deficit improve, and seizures change little or only slightly. If social behaviour does not improve, H5 is rejected, which supports H1.

H6. Statement: Cerebellar (Purkinje) WWOX-dependent circuitry contributes the social and cognitive deficit through cerebello-cortical or cerebello-VTA pathways, and cannot be reached by cortical seizure control. 
Held evidence: discovery_ledger_current.md line 355 (WWOX-KO: loss of hippocampal GABAergic interneurons and Purkinje cells); CLAIM 039 (ataxia 95% in rat `lde`, cerebellum histologically intact, so ataxia is non-cerebellar there); mechanism_intervention_map.md note that the cerebellum was under-transduced by AAV; meta_gaba_paradox_current.md cites P47T cerebellar degeneration (Hussain 2023). The social link is ESPANSIONE. Against it: CLAIM 039 and the rat data show no structural cerebellar lesion, so I treat this as the least anchored hypothesis. 
Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading looks for the social deficit in cortex and hippocampus. This one places it in a structure that no ASM targets and that is outside the seizure generator. 
Rejecting observation: Purkinje-restricted Wwox deletion yields normal Crus I/II physiology and normal three-chamber behaviour. 
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H2): Pcp2-Cre Wwox deletion or restoration, with Crus I Purkinje-cell hM4Di inhibition as the circuit arm. Predicts: social deficit without any seizure or ECoG change (the seizure phenotype is absent). If Purkinje deletion leaves social behaviour unchanged, H6 is rejected.

H7. Statement: Loss of the direct physical brake of WWOX on GSK3β (Axin-like docking motif, residues 388–407, L404 required) produces Tau and cytoskeleton hyperphosphorylation, impaired neurite and spine maturation and social-circuit connectivity, while seizure threshold is controlled by a separate pathway. 
Held evidence: CLAIM 035 (Wang 2012: GSK3β binding, Tau S396/S404, neurite outgrowth, S9-independent); CLAIM 016; mechanism_intervention_map.md Chain C and its note that "CLAIM 016 and CLAIM 035 exclude each other on the S9 axis" and that lithium suppressed PTZ seizures in all three genotypes, wild type included (N-02). The 016 and 035 conflict and the lithium non-specificity argue against a seizure-neutral reading. I keep H7 only for a structural, seizure-independent role of the docking function. 
Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading treats GSK3β as a seizure-susceptibility amplifier. This one gives it a structural, seizure-independent role in social-circuit connectivity, and the folder's measurement warning (phospho-S9 is blind) means abundance cannot test it. 
Rejecting observation: Tau S396/S404 phosphorylation and neurite or spine morphology in the Wwox-deficient cortex are normal. 
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H5): in Wwox-null forebrain excitatory neurons (CaMKIIa), AAV re-expression of the L404-mutant WWOX (cannot dock GSK3β) versus wild-type WWOX. Predicts: the L404 mutant rescues network bursting and seizure susceptibility but not the social deficit, and wild-type WWOX rescues both. A mutant that does rescue social behaviour rejects H7.

H8. Statement: The drug suppresses clinical seizures but not subclinical interictal epileptiform activity or phase–amplitude-coupled bursts, and this interictal load disrupts social cognition during the critical period. 
Held evidence: WM_v4.4 BLOCK 3 and CLAIM 021 (EEG state over seizure count); meta_network_myelin_glia_current.md. Contradicting statement: CLAIM 031 — social and cognitive impairment "did not improve with achievement of better control of epileptic activity". The folder reports N=2 and does not say whether interictal activity was quantified, so I keep H8 only as a state that persists on drug. 
Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading counts seizure freedom as control of the epileptic process. This one makes interictal activity the harmful part, and seizures a poor proxy for it. 
Rejecting observation: interictal spike rate and bursts are matched between animals with and without the social deficit, or are abolished on the ASM while the social deficit persists. 
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H2): hM4Di (Gi-DREADD) in the interictal-spike generator (for example CA1 or CA3 pyramidal cells), leaving mPFC untouched. Predicts: spikes fall, seizure susceptibility falls further, and the social deficit improves only if spike load drives it. H2 predicts no social effect without mPFC change.

H9. Statement: Bioenergetic vulnerability (a WWOX/HIF1α → PDK1 glycolytic shift) in high-demand cells such as PV interneurons produces a social deficit that an anticonvulsant does not correct. 
Held evidence: M6 in mechanism_intervention_map.md (HIF1α de-repression, Warburg signature in human organoids); CLAIM 009 and 025; WM BLOCK 2 CLAIM 017 (ketogenic diet, 3/5 patients improved seizures). Against it: N-06 says the Warburg phenotype is "not in patients" (lactate readouts normal), CLAIM 034 says the sign of WWOX↔ROS is context-dependent, and CLAIM 036 shows the systemic confounder. I keep H9 as a cell-restricted energetic hypothesis. 
Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading treats metabolic findings as systemic or secondary. This one treats them as a cell-type energetic limit on circuit function. 
Rejecting observation: PV-cell mitochondrial and glycolytic flux (Seahorse or NADH imaging) is normal in the model. 
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H3): Pvalb-Cre Hif1a deletion in the Wwox-deficient animal. Predicts: bioenergetics normalise and the social deficit improves, with seizures reduced only modestly. Under H3 (output deficit) the same deletion does nothing, and only direct Gq drive helps.

H10. Statement: A microglia- or cytokine-driven synaptic remodelling (the Il6/IBA1 response) persists after seizures are controlled and prunes social-circuit synapses. 
Held evidence: CLAIM 005 and 006 (IBA1/GFAP area fractions; only Il6, not Tnf-a, significant, n=4); DL-MECH-012; mechanism_intervention_map.md M10 ("downstream"), and the meta file says gliosis falls after neuron-restricted rescue (Obeid 2026). That makes microglia secondary to the neuron, which argues against an independent glial cause. I keep H10 because "downstream of the neuron" does not mean "settled once seizures stop". 
Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading treats glial activation as a seizure consequence that resolves with control. This one treats it as a self-sustaining effector. 
Rejecting observation: microglial pruning markers (for example CD68, synaptic puncta) and social behaviour are normal after the ASM, or the social deficit is not rescued by microglial inhibition. 
Discriminating manipulation (nearest rival H7): Cx3cr1-CreER hM4Di (or Il6 deletion) in microglia of neuron-restricted Wwox-null mice. Predicts: the social deficit improves and seizures are unchanged. If the neuronal L404 mutant (H7) rescues social behaviour while microglial silencing does not, H10 is rejected.
