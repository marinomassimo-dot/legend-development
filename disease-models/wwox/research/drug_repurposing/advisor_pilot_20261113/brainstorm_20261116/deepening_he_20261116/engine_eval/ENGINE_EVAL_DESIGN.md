## Standing target-selection rule (added 2026-11-17 on ADV's decision; applies to every future target set)
1. Same contamination procedure for every target: whole-tree grep at the cut for PMID, first author and distinctive terms, counts written beside the claim; publication date AND corpus presence both stated; a memory probe when the paper is within two months of the model cutoff; atoms from the whole abstract and the open full text, the whole held file read (see the README section 13 row 'you freeze a retrodiction target').
2. Both credit rules (strict and loose) registered before any run, results reported side by side.
3. **A set of targets must include at least one target whose headline is a ONE-outcome effect.** Reason (DREAM-2b, 2026-11-17): in targets 1 to 3 the discriminating atom was an intervention moving both outcomes of the question, and every generator produced one-sided effects; the atoms were chosen by me for being the paper's headline, which is an unexpected bridge by construction. A target with a one-outcome headline separates 'generators miss two-outcome levers' from 'generators miss headlines'.
4. 'The engine improved' is claimed only on a second set of targets, never on the set that motivated the change; one target per meeting until the operator sets a token budget (ten targets per condition pair is about 3.3 to 3.5 million tokens).
