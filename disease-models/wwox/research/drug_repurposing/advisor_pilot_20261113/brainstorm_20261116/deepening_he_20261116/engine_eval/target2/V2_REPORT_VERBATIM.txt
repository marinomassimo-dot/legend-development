# HYPOTHESES

**H1. [HELD, inverted] Persistent excitatory cortical network state**
1. Statement: A seizure-independent cortical network state in excitatory neurons persists after the drug. It is NMDAR/gap-junction-dependent bursting with abnormal phase-amplitude coupling (PAC) and reduced spontaneous inhibition. It corrupts the oscillatory coordination social circuits need, so seizures fall and the social deficit stays.
2. Held evidence: for it, CLAIM 021 (claim_registry_current.md l.382, "primary network-state dysfunction, not only epileptiform activity"; meta_network_myelin_glia_current.md; mechanism_intervention_map.md Chain B). Limits: NMDAR/gap-junction dependence is not shown to be pharmacological (map l.214-221), the data are neuron-specific slice/anaesthetised work graded T2, and no social assay exists. The folder contains nothing that contradicts it.
3. Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading treats the abnormal EEG as the epilepsy. This one makes the EEG state a separate, drug-insensitive lesion that carries the social deficit.
4. Rejection: record ECoG PAC and burst state during the social task in drug-controlled animals. It is rejected if PAC and burst state are in the wild-type range while the deficit is intact.
5. Manipulation vs nearest rival H2 (prenatal wiring): tamoxifen-inducible Wwox re-expression in Emx1-CreER excitatory cortical neurons, started at P21 under the drug.
   - Seizures: stay at the drug floor; the interictal ECoG state normalises.
   - Social deficit: rescued if H1; not rescued if H2.
6. Audit: CONTAMINATION: "phase-amplitude|gap junction|connexin" gives 55 hits in 16 files, so the folder already names this mechanism. FRAMING: worded from CLAIM 021 and Chain B; this is a restatement made to predict the dissociation, not a new result. CITATION: claim_registry_current.md (opened, l.560-610; the CLAIM 021 text from Grep context), meta_network_myelin_glia_current.md (opened), mechanism_intervention_map.md (opened).

**H2. [HELD] Fixed prenatal wiring error**
1. Statement: Late-born upper-layer neuron migration/layering and cytoskeletal programmes are mis-set in utero, creating a structural wiring error. Seizures are a later, drug-suppressible expression, while the social-circuit mis-wiring cannot be reached postnatally.
2. Held evidence: for it, CLAIM 014/015 and meta_prenatal_structure_current.md. Against or limiting: the CLAIM 015 INFERENZA rests on one fetus against one control (ADVERSARIAL l.548-558); adult WWOX expression exceeds fetal (l.562); Syn-Cre post-mitotic loss alone gives P9 seizures and bursting (CLAIM037-01 §D). I keep it because the Nes-Cre layer-marker experiment (ADVERSARIAL l.580) is untested, not refuted.
3. Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading says "early rescue protects development". This one says the social deficit is set before any postnatal rescue or drug can act, and that the network state (H1) is downstream.
4. Rejection: birth-dating and layer markers in Nes-Cre;Wwox-flox embryos (E14.5 pulse to P1). It is rejected if laminar position of late-born neurons is normal.
5. Manipulation vs nearest rival H1: in utero electroporation of Wwox into E14.5 upper-layer-fated progenitors of the Wwox-deficient cortex, with no postnatal re-expression.
   - Seizures: little change (the drug already suppresses them).
   - Social deficit: rescued disproportionately relative to H1's postnatal-only arm.
6. Audit: CONTAMINATION: "in utero electroporation|electroporat|E14.5|birth-dating" gives 11 hits in 7 files, and the E14.5 birth-dating experiment is named in the folder, so the mechanism is already named. FRAMING: worded from CLAIM 015, the "pre-wired" wording, and the ADVERSARIAL critique. CITATION: meta_prenatal_structure_current.md (opened), CC-20260825-ADVERSARIAL-FALSIFICATION-01.md (opened l.440-600), CC-20260826-CLAIM037-01.md (opened l.120-180).

**H3. [RIVAL] Interneuron developmental defect that is dispensable for seizure threshold**
1. Statement: Impaired maturation or timing of PV interneurons weakens E/I-synchronised cortico-limbic activity needed for social behaviour. The drug raises the ictal threshold on the pyramidal side but cannot restore PV-dependent timing, so seizures fall and social behaviour stays low.
2. Held evidence: the folder holds PV+ cells −44% in one systemic KO at two weeks, and reduced spontaneous inhibition (meta_gaba_paradox_current.md). It states the marker count is not function and that the systemic null is metabolically confounded (CLAIM 036). Contradiction to the held reading: vigabatrin resolved spasms (CLAIM 031), so GABA machinery looks non-critical for seizures. I keep it because that same fact leaves the social role of GABA machinery open, and the only pre-registered test of interneuron firing (CC-20260826-EGABA-CLOSURE-01, M3) is unrun.
3. Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading says GABA dysfunction is a cause of seizures. This one says it is a cause of social dysfunction and is irrelevant to the seizure fix.
4. Rejection: Dlx5/6-Cre or Pvalb-Cre Wwox-deleted animals show normal sociability, with normal sIPSC frequency and PV-cell firing. That rejects it.
5. Manipulation vs nearest rival H1: Dlx5/6-Cre (or Pvalb-Cre) Wwox deletion in an otherwise wild-type mouse.
   - Seizures: rare or absent.
   - Social deficit: present if H3; absent if H1.
6. Audit: CONTAMINATION: "parvalbumin|Pvalb|PV+" gives 19 hits in 10 files, so the interneuron marker deficit is named. "Pvalb-Cre|Dlx5|Sst-Cre|Vip-Cre" gives 0 driver hits, so the lineage-restricted manipulation is novel. FRAMING: worded from the GABA meta, the vigabatrin observation and the unrun M3 arm, not from a result. CITATION: meta_gaba_paradox_current.md (opened), CC-20260826-EGABA-CLOSURE-01.md (grep context only, not opened), mechanism_intervention_map.md (opened).

**H4. [RIVAL] Oligodendrocyte-autonomous, demand-contingent myelin deficit in social tracts**
1. The cell-autonomous reading is preprint-only (IPOTESI). The folder's "non-cell-autonomous" claim contradicts this, and I keep H4 because the Olig2-Cre myelin phenotype was never measured.
3. Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading says myelin failure is a downstream amplifier of neuronal hyperexcitability. This one makes it an independent, seizure-insensitive driver.
4. Rejection: g-ratio, unmyelinated-axon count and optic-nerve CAP are normal in the existing Olig2-Cre line at P17, at P60 and after the cuprizone arm (CLAIM003-01 §4).
5. Manipulation vs nearest rival (neuron-driven hypomyelination, HR3): Pdgfra-CreER (OPC) Wwox deletion in otherwise wild-type mice, then a social task with and without a cuprizone challenge.

**H5. [RIVAL] The drug itself leaves or creates a social deficit that mimics "untouched"**
1. Statement: The drug's neuromodulatory action on a social-circuit node, or its injury (VABAM-type), suppresses or fixes social behaviour at the same time as it controls seizures. The deficit then looks "untouched" because disease-driven gain and drug-driven loss cancel.
2. Held evidence: CLAIM 001 holds vigabatrin–VABAM as a real safety signal, with conflicting evidence. CLAIM 031's key case was drug-treated and remained profoundly delayed. This contradicts the held reading that the deficit is independent of seizure control, because the "control" that was achieved was drug control. The folder has no ASM–social data at all; I keep the hypothesis only as a confound to be separated from the disease-intrinsic readings.
3. Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading says the drug is a neutral seizure switch. This one says it is a confounded variable.
4. Rejection: social scores are the same in drug-treated and drug-naive animals at matched seizure burden, in the same model. That rejects it.
5. Manipulation vs nearest rival H1: replace the drug with closed-loop hM4Di silencing of Emx1/Camk2a seizure-focus neurons, triggered by ECoG detection, in drug-naive Syn-Cre cKO mice, with a DREADD-in-wild-type control.
   - Seizures: suppressed.
   - Social deficit: improved if H5; unchanged if disease-intrinsic (H1–H4, H6–H10).
6. Audit: CONTAMINATION: "VABAM|iatrogenic" gives 32 hits in 14 files, but they are all about safety, none links the drug to the cognitive outcome, so the mechanism is only partly named. FRAMING: worded from the CLAIM 001 / CLAIM 031 cross-link, not from a result. CITATION: claim_registry_current.md (opened, CLAIM 001 via Grep context, CLAIM 031 via Read), working_model_current.md (opened l.60-209).

**H6. [RIVAL] Interictal discharges (events) vs network state**

2. Held evidence: interictal spikes are documented in the rat lde/lde (n=5, all mutants) and the Obeid spike endpoint is "not tested" (panel 7C at the p=0.2000 floor; CC-20260826-SEIZURE-RECONCILIATION-01 §2). The folder links interictal data to cognition nowhere, which conflicts with the claim that EEG state alone matters (CLAIM 021). I keep it as a testable split between events and state.
3. Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading says "seizure-free means EEG quiet". This one says the drug leaves events that matter.
4. Rejection: the social score is uncorrelated with interictal spike rate across ASM-treated animals and sessions. That rejects it.
5. Manipulation vs nearest rival H1: spike-triggered optogenetic silencing (inhibitory opsin) of Emx1 excitatory neurons at the spike focus, millisecond windows only, leaving the background state and PAC untouched.
   - Seizures: unchanged, already at the drug floor.
   - Social deficit: improved if H6; unchanged if H1.
6. Audit: CONTAMINATION: "interictal" gives 28 hits in 15 files (rat and Obeid endpoints; none about cognition or sociability). FRAMING: worded from the SEIZURE-RECONCILIATION three-axis split, not from a result. CITATION: CC-20260826-SEIZURE-RECONCILIATION-01.md (opened), PMID19500159.md (grep context only, not opened).
