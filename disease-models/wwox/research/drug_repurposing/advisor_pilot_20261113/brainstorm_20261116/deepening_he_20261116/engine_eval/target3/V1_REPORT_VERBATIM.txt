Framing, from what the folder holds. (1) The folder does not hold the premise "mTOR inhibition gives limited cognitive benefit" for WWOX. mTOR is **unmeasured at protein level** in any WWOX system. The one datum, organoid RNA-seq at n=2 vs 4 with raw P<0.01 and no verbatim locator, reads mTOR/EIF4EBP1 down, and the older "wrong direction" verdict was retired to INSUFFICIENT (CC-20260826-MTOR-DIRECTION-01; second pass). I treat the premise as given by the question. (2) **No WWOX intervention carries a cognitive or learning endpoint** (therapeutic_translation_second_pass.md, lines 487 and 589: "DEVELOPMENT NOT MEASURED"). Every cognitive prediction below is therefore IPOTESI. The white-matter predictions rest on DATO only where I name a held claim. (3) Tags: DATO / INFERENZA / IPOTESI / ESPANSIONE (outside the held corpus).

H1. Statement · Neuron-derived axonal signalling (non-cell-autonomous) is the main driver, so restoring WWOX in neurons alone raises OPC-to-oligodendrocyte maturation and myelination, and cognition improves through the restored circuit (INFERENZA).
Held evidence · Supports: claim_registry_current.md CLAIM 003 and 004 (Synapsin-Cre deletion gives hypomyelination with OPCs present; oligodendrocytes never transduced, yet myelin and g-ratio improve). Limits: the rescue is incomplete (~26 unmyelinated axons per field in WT vs ~52 in rescued; CC1+ and PDGFRα+ counts untested against WT). No cognition endpoint. Files: disease-models/wwox/registries/claim_registry_current.md; disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md (M9, Chain A).
Usual reading vs this one · Usual: myelination deficits are fixed by acting on oligodendrocytes, or by a growth-pathway brake such as mTOR. This one: the oligodendrocyte is a receiver, the lesion is in the axon, and mTOR inhibition acts on neither the neuron's signal nor the receiver.
Rejecting observation · Neuron-restricted re-expression (hSynI-WWOX, 60–70% of neurons) in a null background, with g-ratio and unmyelinated-axon count measured against WT in corpus callosum and optic nerve. If g-ratio does not normalise, or the residual gap is as large as the untreated null, H1 is rejected.
Discriminating manipulation vs nearest rival H2 (oligodendroglial-autonomous) · Cell-type-restricted re-expression in a fully null animal (Syn-Cre-dependent vs Olig2/CNP-Cre-dependent WWOX), at matched transduction. Prediction: neuron-only gives g-ratio normalisation and a large rise in myelinated axons, with the unmyelinated-axon gap remaining; Olig2-only gives little. White matter: callosal myelin largely restored. Cognition (IPOTESI): improves in proportion to the myelinated fraction and to network normalisation, not to g-ratio alone.

H2. Statement · A second, oligodendroglia-autonomous WWOX function (SOX10 / differentiation arm, emerging under stress or remyelination) supplies the residual gap; restoring it in the OL lineage on top of neuronal rescue closes the gap and adds a small cognitive gain (IPOTESI).
Held evidence · Supports: CLAIM 003 evidence boundary (authors attribute the residual to "an oligodendrocyte-specific WWOX function", their IPOTESI); meta_network_myelin_glia_current.md line 92; CC-20260826-CLAIM003-01.md (the Olig2-Cre; Wwox flox line exists, no myelin endpoint was ever applied, sole source for the cuprizone effect is an unrefereed preprint). Against: Olig2-Cre and GFAP-Cre deletions "no evident anomaly" at baseline (mechanism_intervention_map.md N-13). Files: those above.
Usual reading vs this one · Usual: baseline "no anomaly" = no oligodendroglial role. This one: baseline negatives are a designed false negative if the requirement is conditional on demyelinating stress (CC-20260826-CLAIM003-01 §4).
Rejecting observation · In the existing Olig2-Cre; Wwox flox/flox line, EM g-ratio, unmyelinated-axon count, CC1+/PDGFRα+ at P17 and P60, plus a 0.2% cuprizone 5-week arm. Normal at baseline and under cuprizone rejects H2.
Discriminating manipulation vs H1 · Olig2/Pdgfra-CreER Wwox deletion layered on neuron-rescued animals, with and without cuprizone. Prediction: white matter is normal at baseline and fails to remyelinate under cuprizone (maturation block: PDGFRα+ up, CC1+ down). Cognition: unchanged at baseline, impaired only after a demyelinating insult.

H3. Statement · Pathological network state in L2/3 pyramidal neurons (NMDAR- and gap-junction-dependent bursting) disrupts activity-dependent myelination; normalising the burst state, with WWOX still absent, would restore myelin and cognition (IPOTESI).
Held evidence · Supports: CLAIM 021 (bursting depends on NMDAR and gap junctions); a myelination-is-activity-dependent IPOTESI in therapeutic_hypotheses_ledger_current.md ~line 265. **Contradiction held:** CLAIM 031 and N-15 state that in WWOX children cognitive and psychomotor impairment precedes seizures and does not improve when epileptic activity is controlled (vigabatrin resolved spasms, profound delay anyway), so "less seizures, more myelin, better development" is unsupported. Memantine is flagged ambivalent (chronic NMDAR block impairs activity-dependent maturation). I keep H3 only for early, developmental-window normalisation of the network state, as distinct from symptomatic seizure control. Files: disease-models/wwox/research/therapeutic_hypotheses_ledger_current.md; disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md (R-02, N-15, §2.1 M2).
Usual reading vs this one · Usual: seizure suppression and hyperexcitability are symptoms to treat. This one: the network state is an upstream cause of the myelin lesion during the critical window, not a consequence.
Rejecting observation · Suppress L2/3 hyperactivity in the WWOX-null in P3–P21 (no WWOX restoration) and measure g-ratio and CC1+ counts. Unchanged myelin with normalised bursting rejects H3.
Discriminating manipulation vs H1 (rescue by signalling restoration) · Chemogenetic silencing of excitatory neurons (or a mechanism-level NMDAR antagonist class) during P3–P21 in the null. Prediction: myelination recovers only partially, correlating with burst frequency, while axon-intrinsic markers (MAP2, neurite) stay abnormal. Cognition: partial, if at all.

H4. Statement · Loss of functional inhibition (PV-interneuron deficit, reduced spontaneous IPSCs) makes the circuit non-myelination-competent. Restoring the inhibitory arm restores white matter in PV-connected tracts and cognition more than excitatory restoration does (IPOTESI; the biology of PV-axon myelination is ESPANSIONE).
Held evidence · Supports: CLAIM 021 (reduced spontaneous inhibition), CLAIM 005 (PV+ ~−44%). Against or unresolved: direction of GABA is not resolved in any WWOX system (no E_GABA, NKCC1/KCC2); the PV loss is from a systemically decompensated null (CLAIM 036), a confounder the design cannot separate; vigabatrin did not help (FM-018). Files: disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md (§2.3, R-07); disease-models/wwox/meta/meta_gaba_paradox_current.md.
Usual reading vs this one · Usual: the excitatory pyramidal neuron carries the myelin lesion. This one: the inhibitory arm is the primary lesion and myelin follows from circuit imbalance.
Rejecting observation · Gramicidin perforated-patch E_GABA plus sIPSC amplitude in WWOX-KO vs parental cells; normal E_GABA and sIPSC rejects the inhibition-first reading. Separately, normal PV counts in a conditional (non-metabolically-decompensated) deletion.
Discriminating manipulation vs H3 · Pvalb/Gad2-Cre-dependent WWOX restoration vs Emx1/CaMKII-Cre-dependent restoration in the null. Prediction: PV-restricted restoration repairs sIPSCs, partly repairs callosal and cortical myelin and gives the larger cognitive gain; excitatory-restricted restoration gives the opposite.

H5. Statement · WWOX loss de-represses Wnt/β-catenin, and Wnt hyperactivation in the OPC lineage arrests differentiation; lowering β-catenin tone in OPCs restores myelin and (secondarily) cognition (INFERENZA for the Wnt sign; ESPANSIONE for the OPC arrest).
Held evidence · Supports: DL-MOL-003, DL-MECH-034 (organoid nuclear β-catenin ~1.7×, WNT/LEF1/AXIN2 up, partially recovered by W-AAV; n=2 vs 4 transcriptome, with a raw-P caveat); mechanism_intervention_map.md Chain D. Against: no OPC data in any WWOX system; Wnt hyperactivation documented mainly in oncology; lithium is contraindicated on this axis, and R-03 is rated "poor developmental relevance" and an intestinal/bone toxicity liability. Files: disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md; disease-models/wwox/research/discovery_ledger_current.md.
Usual reading vs this one · Usual: myelin failure comes from a neuronal signal absence (H1). This one: part of the block is a cell-intrinsic Wnt brake on differentiation.
Rejecting observation · Nuclear β-catenin and Axin2 in sorted OPCs from null vs WT P10–P17 callosum; normal levels reject H5.
Discriminating manipulation vs H6 (GSK3β) · Olig2/Pdgfra-CreER Ctnnb1 (β-catenin) lowering in the null background. Prediction: CC1+ rises and g-ratio improves without any change in neuronal MAP2/neurite markers; cognition improves only to the extent that white matter was limiting.

H6. Statement · Loss of the WWOX docking brake on GSK3β (Axin-like motif 388–407, L404) in neurons impairs axonal maturation (tau/microtubule/neurite), so axons are not myelination-competent; this is independent of Wnt (INFERENZA).
Held evidence · CLAIM 035 (residue-resolved docking, S9-independent); lde rat has MAP2 loss with intact neuron counts (PMID31340538.md); CLAIM 016 and 035 conflict on the S9 axis, and lithium failed its specificity test (suppresses PTZ seizures in WT too; R-04). Total GSK3β is flat in the cited sources. Files: disease-models/wwox/registries/claim_registry_current.md; disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md (Chain C); disease-models/wwox/research/fulltext_dossiers/PMID31340538.md.
Usual reading vs this one · Usual: GSK3β matters for tau/neurite only, not myelin. This one: the neurite maturation defect is the proximate cause of the "no axonal signal" lesion in H1.
Rejecting observation · Neuron-restricted re-expression of WWOX L404A (docking-dead) versus WT WWOX. If L404A rescues myelin as well as WT, H6 is rejected.
Discriminating manipulation vs H5 · Neuron-restricted L404A-WWOX vs WW1-mutant WWOX re-expression in the null (WW-scaffold carries Wnt/HIF; the L404 site carries GSK3β). Prediction: L404A fails to restore MAP2 and myelin if H6 holds; WW1-mutant fails if H5 or H7 holds. Cognition follows the same pattern (IPOTESI).

H7. Statement · HIF1α-PDK1-PDH block starves axons and oligodendrocytes of oxidative support (Warburg-like state), so the energy cost of myelination cannot be met; relieving the block restores myelin and cognition (IPOTESI).
Held evidence · Supports: DL-MECH-020/034 (transcriptome, no flux); CLAIM 009. **Contradiction held:** human WWOX metabolism is normal (lactate, ammonia, acylcarnitines, MRS, muscle biopsy; DL-BIO-009, 4 independent sources), and ketogenic continuation signal is poor (1/3). The mouse hypoglycaemia is reversed by CNS-only restoration (CLAIM 004), which also links this to systemic decompensation, a confounder for H1. I keep H7 as the lowest-prior hypothesis, because the question is cell-type-specific tissue flux (axon, OL), not blood chemistry. Files: disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md (Chain E, R-05, M6).
Usual reading vs this one · Usual: metabolic state is a downstream correlate. This one: the metabolic block is upstream of myelin supply.
Rejecting observation · Seahorse OCR/ECAR in WWOX-KO neurons or OL-lineage cells vs parental; normal flux rejects H7.
Discriminating manipulation vs H8-type systemic metabolic confound · Neuron-restricted deletion of Hif1a or Pdk1 (or a PDH-activator class) in the null with glycaemia clamped normal. Prediction: if H7 is right, white matter recovers with normal glycaemia; if the lesion is systemic hypoglycaemia, only the clamp matters. Cognition (IPOTESI): improved only in the H7-true case.

H8. Statement · mTORC1 signalling is not hyperactive but hypoactive or mis-timed in OPCs/neurons of the WWOX-deficient brain, so inhibition cannot repair myelination, while restoring mTORC1/4E-BP translational tone in the OL lineage would (IPOTESI; the mTOR-OL link is ESPANSIONE).
Held evidence · Only datum: mTOR/EIF4EBP1 down in KO organoids (DL-MECH-034; n=2/4, raw P<0.01, no locator, EIF4EBP1 down inverts when read as biology). CC-20260826-MTOR-DIRECTION-01: "INSUFFICIENT, never measured"; revival trigger = a protein-level p-S6 and p-4E-BP1/4E-BP1 readout. Against: the autophagy papers point in opposite directions and are unread stubs. Files: disease-models/wwox/research/commit_candidates/CC-20260826-MTOR-DIRECTION-01.md; disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md (§2.2, N-01).
Usual reading vs this one · Usual: DEE, therefore mTORC1 hyperactivity (TSC logic). This one: the sign is unknown and may be hypo, which explains why inhibition adds little.
Rejecting observation · Protein-level p-S6 (S235/236, S240/244) and p-4E-BP1/total in sorted OPCs and neurons, with assay-positive control, null vs WT. Unchanged or increased signalling rejects H8.
Discriminating manipulation vs H2 · Pdgfra-CreER Tsc1 deletion (constitutive mTORC1 gain) vs Rptor deletion in OPCs of the null. Prediction: if H8 holds, gain raises myelin thickness and g-ratio normalises and loss worsens; if the problem is H2 (differentiation), mTORC1 gain does not alter the maturation block. Cognition: improves only if white matter was limiting.

H9. Statement · A reactive glial/inflammatory state (IL-1β/NLRP3, NF-κB, microglia) suppresses OPC maturation in specific regions; resolving it without restoring WWOX frees myelination (IPOTESI).
Held evidence · Supports: DL-MECH-012 (WWOX as brake on inflammatory signalling); ↑IBA1/GFAP area fraction. Against: CLAIM 006 / Obeid 2026 say gliosis is largely downstream (reduced by neuron-only rescue); the rat lde shows the opposite sign (cortical GFAP/Iba1 reduced), so a universal direction is not licensed; N-13 and R-06 say "the target is downstream of the target". Files: disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md (R-06, M10); disease-models/wwox/research/fulltext_dossiers/PMID31340538.md. Keep only as a region-specific (hippocampus/callosum) hypothesis.
Usual reading vs this one · Usual: gliosis is a marker. This one: gliosis is a modifier of OL maturation in regions where it is up, though not a driver.
Rejecting observation · Microglia/astrocyte depletion in the null (CSF1R-inhibitor class, or Nlrp3 deletion) with g-ratio and CC1+ counts in callosum; unchanged myelin rejects H9.
Discriminating manipulation vs H1 · Nlrp3 or microglia-specific Il1b deletion in the null, with neurons still WWOX-null. Prediction: partial white-matter gain (smaller than neuron rescue) in regions with gliosis, none in cortex of lde-type regions; cognition: little or none.

H10. Statement · A prenatal progenitor/radial-glia and migration defect creates a mis-wired callosal and cortical scaffold, so postnatal myelin and cognition are limited by structure that neuron-restricted post-mitotic rescue cannot repair; only progenitor-stage restoration restores both (INFERENZA).
Held evidence · Supports: CLAIM 014/015 (neuronal gene therapy does not repair the progenitor/radial-glia defect), meta_prenatal_structure_current.md, Iacomino 2020 migration/layering (paper 97), the window closing at P0–P5. Against: Tochigi 2019 myelin deficit is early postnatal (PND5–21), not prenatal; rescue at P0 still reaches survival and myelin. Files: disease-models/wwox/meta/meta_prenatal_structure_current.md; disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md (Chain A boundaries 3–4).
Usual reading vs this one · Usual: myelin is a postnatal oligodendrocyte process. This one: the ceiling on myelin and cognition is set before oligodendrocyte differentiation, by axon tract formation.
Rejecting observation · Compare Emx1/Nestin-Cre progenitor-stage restoration (E10–E14) with post-mitotic Syn-Cre restoration (P0) in the null: if callosal myelination and layering are identical, H10 is rejected.
Discriminating manipulation vs H1 · Timed, progenitor-stage vs post-mitotic WWOX restoration. Prediction: progenitor-stage restoration gives a larger callosal fibre number, myelinated fraction and cognitive gain; post-mitotic restoration gives the P0-rescue pattern (myelin partly restored, cortical layering and callosal complement not).

Notes: (a) The hypotheses H1/H2/H10 and H3/H4 sit on the neuron-first arm; H5/H6/H7/H8/H9 are lesions in a distinct signalling or cell compartment. N-13 (pro-myelinating agents as the wrong cell compartment) is the main held contradiction to any OL-directed arm (H2, H5, H8) and is addressed in those blocks. (b) No dose, no drug recommendation.
