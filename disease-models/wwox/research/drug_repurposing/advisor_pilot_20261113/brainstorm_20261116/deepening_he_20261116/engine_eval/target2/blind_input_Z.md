# Thirty hypotheses on why seizures can stop while a social deficit remains (statement and discriminating manipulation only)

## Z01
Statement: The drug suppresses clinical seizures but not subclinical interictal epileptiform activity or phase–amplitude-coupled bursts, and this interictal load disrupts social cognition during the critical period.
Discriminating manipulation(nearest rival H2): hM4Di (Gi-DREADD) in the interictal-spike generator (for example CA1 or CA3 pyramidal cells), leaving mPFC untouched. Predicts: spikes fall, seizure susceptibility falls further, and the social deficit improves only if spike load drives it. H2 predicts no social effect without mPFC change.

## Z02
Statement: An oligodendrocyte-lineage, stress- or activity-contingent myelination failure (unmyelinated axon excess, conduction delay) in long-range association tracts degrades inter-regional social-circuit timing. It is invisible at baseline and not seizure-driven, so seizures fall and social behaviour stays low.
Discriminating manipulationvs nearest rival (neuron-driven hypomyelination, HR3): Pdgfra-CreER (OPC) Wwox deletion in otherwise wild-type mice, then a social task with and without a cuprizone challenge.
   - Seizures: none (the Olig2-Cre line is normal on gross phenotype, with no EEG).
   - Social deficit: appears under challenge if H4; absent if neuron-driven.

## Z03
Statement: Functional output of parvalbumin (PV) interneurons in mPFC is reduced, so excitation–inhibition balance and gamma synchrony fail for social coding, while the ASM acts on pyramidal firing or release.
Discriminating manipulation(nearest rival H4, depolarising GABA): Pvalb-Cre hM3Dq (Gq-DREADD) activation in mPFC. Predicts: it rescues the social deficit with little change in seizures, because the generator is hippocampal or elsewhere. Failure of PV drive, or worsening, favours H4.

## Z04
Statement: A primary, NMDAR- and gap-junction-dependent bursting state in medial prefrontal cortex (mPFC) pyramidal neurons (a state, not a seizure) corrupts social-relevant processing, and the ASM suppresses ictal spread without normalising that state.
Discriminating manipulation(nearest rival H3, PV output deficit): mPFC-restricted deletion of the NMDAR obligatory subunit (Grin1-flox, AAV-Cre, restricted to layer 2/3 pyramidal cells) in the Wwox-deficient animal. Predicts: bursting and the social deficit are rescued, and seizure susceptibility (generated elsewhere) is unchanged. If social behaviour is not rescued, H2 is rejected.

## Z05
Statement: Impaired retinal or visual-thalamic input, with optic-nerve hypomyelination, starves face and eye-contact cues the social circuit needs. Seizure control cannot restore sensory input, so seizures fall and social behaviour stays low.
Discriminating manipulationvs nearest rival H4 (central white matter): Brn3b/Pou4f2-Cre RGC-restricted Wwox deletion in wild type.
   - Seizures: none.
   - Social deficit: restricted to visually guided tasks if H8; absent if H4.

## Z06
Statement: The ASM itself (sedation, reduced plasticity, altered synaptic transmission) adds to or masks social and cognitive function, so seizure control comes with a drug-induced behavioural cost that hides any benefit.
Discriminating manipulation(vs H6): seizure control without the drug, with hM4Di in the seizure-focus population (for example dentate granule cells). Predicts seizures suppressed and social deficit improved if H7 holds. Under H6 social improvement requires spike removal in the specific CA2/CA3 network, not just seizure control.

## Z07
Statement: Hypothalamic oxytocin (PVN) signalling to the prefrontal cortex, amygdala and VTA is reduced, which removes social salience but is irrelevant to seizure threshold.
Discriminating manipulation(vs H4, VTA dopamine): chemogenetic activation (hM3Dq) of PVN oxytocin neurons (Oxt-Cre) in adult mutants. Predicts seizures unchanged and social deficit improved. If the effect needs VTA dopamine neurons (blocked by hM4Di there), H4 is favoured instead.

## Z08
Statement: Neuron-driven, non-cell-autonomous hypomyelination slows long-range conduction (mPFC–amygdala, callosal), producing a connectivity-based social deficit that is independent of seizure generation.
Discriminating manipulation(nearest rival H1): in neuron-restricted Wwox-null mice, an oligodendrocyte-lineage-restricted pro-differentiation manipulation (Pdgfra-CreER driving Myrf or Sox10 expression) that restores myelination without neuronal WWOX. Predicts: g-ratio, conduction velocity and the social deficit improve, and seizures change little or only slightly. If social behaviour does not improve, H5 is rejected, which supports H1.

## Z09
Statement: Cerebellar (Purkinje) WWOX-dependent circuitry contributes the social and cognitive deficit through cerebello-cortical or cerebello-VTA pathways, and cannot be reached by cortical seizure control.
Discriminating manipulation(nearest rival H2): Pcp2-Cre Wwox deletion or restoration, with Crus I Purkinje-cell hM4Di inhibition as the circuit arm. Predicts: social deficit without any seizure or ECoG change (the seizure phenotype is absent). If Purkinje deletion leaves social behaviour unchanged, H6 is rejected.

## Z10
Statement: Interictal epileptiform spikes and sleep-activated discharges persist under the drug. They interrupt hippocampal–prefrontal and thalamocortical coupling in time-locked windows, independent of the background state (H1), so seizures fall and social behaviour stays low.
Discriminating manipulationvs nearest rival H1: spike-triggered optogenetic silencing (inhibitory opsin) of Emx1 excitatory neurons at the spike focus, millisecond windows only, leaving the background state and PAC untouched.
   - Seizures: unchanged, already at the drug floor.
   - Social deficit: improved if H6; unchanged if H1.

## Z11
Statement: Reduced parvalbumin-interneuron output in medial prefrontal cortex lowers the gamma/E-I balance needed for social processing, and an ASM that dampens pyramidal firing or sodium-channel-dependent bursting does not repair that interneuron hypofunction.
Discriminating manipulation(vs H1): chemogenetic activation (hM3Dq) of mPFC PV cells in adult mutants. Predicts social deficit improved acutely (reversible), seizures unchanged if the focus is hippocampal or subcortical. Under H1 it would produce no rescue.

## Z12
Statement: The drug's neuromodulatory action on a social-circuit node, or its injury (VABAM-type), suppresses or fixes social behaviour at the same time as it controls seizures. The deficit then looks "untouched" because disease-driven gain and drug-driven loss cancel.
Discriminating manipulationvs nearest rival H1: replace the drug with closed-loop hM4Di silencing of Emx1/Camk2a seizure-focus neurons, triggered by ECoG detection, in drug-naive Syn-Cre cKO mice, with a DREADD-in-wild-type control.
   - Seizures: suppressed.
   - Social deficit: improved if H5; unchanged if disease-intrinsic (H1–H4, H6–H10).

## Z13
Statement: A microglia- or cytokine-driven synaptic remodelling (the Il6/IBA1 response) persists after seizures are controlled and prunes social-circuit synapses.
Discriminating manipulation(nearest rival H7): Cx3cr1-CreER hM4Di (or Il6 deletion) in microglia of neuron-restricted Wwox-null mice. Predicts: the social deficit improves and seizures are unchanged. If the neuronal L404 mutant (H7) rescues social behaviour while microglial silencing does not, H10 is rejected.

## Z14
Statement: GABA stays depolarising or shunting (an NKCC1/KCC2 chloride gradient defect) in a subset of circuits, so inhibition is excitatory where social coding needs inhibition. The ASM suppresses seizures through a non-GABA route and leaves this circuit untouched.
Discriminating manipulation(nearest rival H3): KCC2 (Slc12a5) overexpression in mPFC pyramidal neurons using an AAV with a CaMKIIa promoter. Predicts: the social deficit is rescued while seizures are unchanged. In the same animals, Pvalb-Cre Gq activation (H3's rescue) fails or worsens the social deficit, because it increases excitatory GABA.

## Z15
Statement: Bioenergetic vulnerability (a WWOX/HIF1α → PDK1 glycolytic shift) in high-demand cells such as PV interneurons produces a social deficit that an anticonvulsant does not correct.
Discriminating manipulation(nearest rival H3): Pvalb-Cre Hif1a deletion in the Wwox-deficient animal. Predicts: bioenergetics normalise and the social deficit improves, with seizures reduced only modestly. Under H3 (output deficit) the same deletion does nothing, and only direct Gq drive helps.

## Z16
Statement: Late-born upper-layer neuron migration/layering and cytoskeletal programmes are mis-set in utero, creating a structural wiring error. Seizures are a later, drug-suppressible expression, while the social-circuit mis-wiring cannot be reached postnatally.
Discriminating manipulationvs nearest rival H1: in utero electroporation of Wwox into E14.5 upper-layer-fated progenitors of the Wwox-deficient cortex, with no postnatal re-expression.
   - Seizures: little change (the drug already suppresses them).
   - Social deficit: rescued disproportionately relative to H1's postnatal-only arm.

## Z17
Statement: De-repressed GSK3β phosphorylates Tau at S396/S404 in prefrontal pyramidal neurons, impairing dendritic and spine development. It is seizure-independent in effect, so seizures fall and social behaviour stays low.
Discriminating manipulationvs nearest rival H1: mPFC-targeted AAV-Camk2a-Cre deleting Gsk3b in Wwox-flox mice, or Tau S396/S404A knock-in restricted to mPFC.
   - Seizures: little change if H10 (a decrease would instead favour CLAIM 016).
   - Social deficit: rescued if H10; unchanged if H1.

## Z18
Statement: Hypofunction of the VTA-to-nucleus-accumbens dopamine reward pathway lowers social motivation, independent of the cortical network that generates seizures.
Discriminating manipulation(vs H3): hM3Dq in Dat-Cre VTA neurons with the oxytocin pathway left intact. Predicts social deficit improved and seizures unchanged. Placing the manipulation in the VTA alone separates it from H3 because H3 requires the PVN-to-VTA input.

## Z19
Statement: Microglial over-pruning of synapses during development reduces prefrontal excitatory connectivity and lowers social behaviour, independent of ictogenic circuitry.
Discriminating manipulation(vs H8): microglia-restricted (Cx3cr1-CreER) deletion of the gene in wild type, or rescue in the mutant, at the pruning window. Predicts seizures unchanged and social deficit following the microglial genotype. Oligodendrocyte-specific rescue would not change spine density.

## Z20
Statement: Cerebellar Purkinje-cell dysfunction (crus I/II) disrupts the cerebello-thalamo-prefrontal loop required for social behaviour, while seizures arise elsewhere.
Discriminating manipulation(vs H1/H2, cortical): Pcp2-Cre-restricted rescue (or chemogenetic activation of crus I/II Purkinje cells). Predicts seizures unchanged and social deficit improved if H5 holds. Cortical rescue then fails to repair social behaviour.

## Z21
Statement: A prenatal or perinatal wiring defect (progenitor/radial-glia migration and layering) fixes the social-circuit architecture before any seizure exists, so seizure-suppressing drugs act on a downstream excitability layer and cannot reach it.
Discriminating manipulation(nearest rival H2, ongoing network state): Wwox-flox with an embryonic progenitor driver (Emx1-Cre) versus a tamoxifen-timed postnatal excitatory-neuron driver (CaMKIIa-CreERT2), with matched restoration arms. Predicts: progenitor-only deletion gives a social deficit with little or no seizure phenotype, and postnatal deletion gives seizures with intact social behaviour. The opposite pattern supports H2.

## Z22
Statement: Complement-dependent microglial pruning, initiated by early neuronal dysfunction, becomes self-sustaining and excessively eliminates synapses in mPFC social circuits independent of ongoing seizures. Seizures fall and social behaviour stays low.
Discriminating manipulationvs nearest rival H1: Tmem119-CreER microglia-restricted C1qa deletion from P10 in Syn-Cre cKO mice.
   - Seizures: unchanged (bursting is neuron-intrinsic).
   - Social deficit: improved if H9; unchanged if H1.

## Z23
Statement: Persistent interictal discharges and abnormal sharp-wave ripples in hippocampal CA2/CA3 disrupt social-memory encoding even when overt seizures are suppressed, so ASM reduces convulsions but not subclinical epileptiform activity.
Discriminating manipulation(vs H1, structural): closed-loop optogenetic or hM4Di silencing of CA2/CA3 at spike onset. Predicts seizures reduced or unchanged, depending on the focus, and social memory improved. Under H1 silencing would change nothing.

## Z24
Statement: Oligodendrocyte or myelination deficits slow long-range prefrontal-limbic conduction, causing a social deficit unrelated to seizures.
Discriminating manipulation(vs H9, microglial): oligodendrocyte-lineage-specific (Olig2- or Pdgfra-CreER) re-expression or deletion of the gene. Predicts seizures unchanged and social deficit changing in the same direction as myelination. Microglial manipulation would leave myelin unchanged.

## Z25
Statement: Purkinje-cell degeneration or dysfunction disrupts cerebello-thalamo-prefrontal social circuitry. It is not seizure-generating and is not repaired by the drug, so seizures fall and social behaviour stays low.
Discriminating manipulationvs nearest rival H1: Pcp2(L7)-Cre-driven Wwox deletion in otherwise wild-type mice, or re-expression in the Syn-Cre cKO.
   - Seizures: none.
   - Social deficit: present with deletion if H7; rescued by re-expression only if H7, otherwise no change.

## Z26
Statement: A seizure-independent cortical network state in excitatory neurons persists after the drug. It is NMDAR/gap-junction-dependent bursting with abnormal phase-amplitude coupling (PAC) and reduced spontaneous inhibition. It corrupts the oscillatory coordination social circuits need, so seizures fall and the social deficit stays.
Discriminating manipulationvs nearest rival H2 (prenatal wiring): tamoxifen-inducible Wwox re-expression in Emx1-CreER excitatory cortical neurons, started at P21 under the drug.
   - Seizures: stay at the drug floor; the interictal ECoG state normalises.
   - Social deficit: rescued if H1; not rescued if H2.

## Z27
Statement: Impaired maturation or timing of PV interneurons weakens E/I-synchronised cortico-limbic activity needed for social behaviour. The drug raises the ictal threshold on the pyramidal side but cannot restore PV-dependent timing, so seizures fall and social behaviour stays low.
Discriminating manipulationvs nearest rival H1: Dlx5/6-Cre (or Pvalb-Cre) Wwox deletion in an otherwise wild-type mouse.
   - Seizures: rare or absent.
   - Social deficit: present if H3; absent if H1.

## Z28
Statement: The social deficit is a fixed developmental wiring defect (critical-period miswiring of prefrontal-limbic projections) that was set before any seizure or drug exposure and cannot be undone by later seizure control.
Discriminating manipulation(vs H2, ongoing interneuron dysfunction): tamoxifen-timed re-expression in the same forebrain population, early postnatal vs adult. Predicts seizures reduced in both timings (or unaffected, if they are not the target) and social deficit rescued only with the early timing. Under H2 the adult timing would also rescue.

## Z29
Statement: Thalamic (mediodorsal or reticular) dysfunction affects prefrontal gating of social cues and is separable from the cortical-hippocampal circuit that generates seizures.
Discriminating manipulation(vs H2, mPFC interneurons): hM3Dq or hM4Di on mediodorsal thalamus neurons projecting to mPFC (retrograde Cre). Predicts seizures unchanged and social deficit modulated. mPFC PV-cell activation (H2) would act locally and not depend on thalamic input.

## Z30
Statement: Loss of the direct physical brake of WWOX on GSK3β (Axin-like docking motif, residues 388–407, L404 required) produces Tau and cytoskeleton hyperphosphorylation, impaired neurite and spine maturation and social-circuit connectivity, while seizure threshold is controlled by a separate pathway.
Discriminating manipulation(nearest rival H5): in Wwox-null forebrain excitatory neurons (CaMKIIa), AAV re-expression of the L404-mutant WWOX (cannot dock GSK3β) versus wild-type WWOX. Predicts: the L404 mutant rescues network bursting and seizure susceptibility but not the social deficit, and wild-type WWOX rescues both. A mutant that does rescue social behaviour rejects H7.
