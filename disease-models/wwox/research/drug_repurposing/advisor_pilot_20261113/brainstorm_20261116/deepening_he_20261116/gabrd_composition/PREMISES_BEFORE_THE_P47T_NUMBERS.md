# Can a shift in the cell mix move bulk Gabrd with no per-cell receptor change? Arithmetic and premises, written before the P47T numbers are read into it (HE, 2026-11-16; non-canonical; not medical advice)

Written for: ADV, SCI. ADV's question: a Lamp5 fall with Sst, Npy and Cck rises in P47T cortex and PFC; what share change in which class would give a bulk Gabrd log2 of −0.8, and is that size plausible next to the Lamp5 fall? SCI runs the per-animal regression in GSE215425; this file is the class-table arithmetic only.
**Disclosure.** I have already seen the P47T rows this arithmetic will meet (Gabrd −0.80 in cortex and −0.82 in PFC; Lamp5 −0.65 and −0.86; Sst +0.84 and +0.94; Npy +1.59 and +1.12; Cck +0.70/+0.90 as relayed by ADV), in the I13 attack of the same day. What is fixed here is the arithmetic, the inputs and the reading rule; the P47T figures enter only at the last step.

## Model (composition only)
Bulk expression of a gene g in a tissue is the sum over cell classes c of (share of nuclei f_c) × (expression per nucleus e_c,g). With no per-cell change (e unchanged), a change of shares f → f′ changes bulk g by Δ log2 = log2(Σ f′_c e_c,g / Σ f_c e_c,g). RNA-sequencing normalises per library, so a share shift is read as a relative change of the weighted sum.

## Inputs (all HELD; what each can and cannot say)
* **e_c,g:** the HPA single-nuclei cluster-type table (`files/geo/HPA_single_nuclei/rna_single_nuclei_cluster_type.tsv.zip`, 48 human brain cluster types, nCPM per gene): GABRD, LAMP5, SST, NPY, CCK, PVALB, VIP, SLC17A7, GAD1. **Human**, not mouse; cluster types are broad (for example "cge derived interneurons" contains the Lamp5 neurogliaform-type cells, Vip and others); the table has cerebellar and brainstem types as well as cortical ones. The held GSE52564 class table (mouse P7 and P17 pools: neurons, astrocytes, OPC, newly formed and myelinating oligodendrocytes, microglia, endothelium) is used for the non-neuronal classes and for Gabrd's neuron against non-neuron split (neuron 1.2, microglia 3.0, others at most 1.3 FPKM at P7, a low delta age).
* **f_c (shares of nuclei in the cortex) are NOT in any held table** and are an ASSUMPTION, scanned: interneurons 10% to 25% of cortical neurons (cge-derived a minority of those), excitatory neurons the rest of the neurons, neurons 30% to 60% of nuclei, glia the rest. The held single-nucleus pool (GSE158684) is hippocampus, one pool per arm, and its class composition is SCI's reading; it is not used for the cortex.
* **Lamp5 class specificity:** from the same table: where LAMP5 is expressed (cge-derived interneurons, and other classes at a lower level), so what share of bulk Lamp5 comes from the cge-derived class.

## What the arithmetic will give (and the reading rule, fixed now)
1. **Required share.** For a class k whose share changes by a factor r (r < 1 a fall) with everything else fixed, Δ bulk Gabrd = s_k (r − 1), where s_k = f_k e_k,GABRD / Σ f e is the class's share of bulk Gabrd. A log2 of −0.8 is a factor 0.574, so s_k (1 − r) = 0.426: even if the class vanished (r = 0), it would have to carry 43% of bulk Gabrd. I compute s_k for every class under the scanned shares, and the (r, s_k) pairs that give −0.8.
2. **Plausibility next to Lamp5.** From the Lamp5 fall (−0.65 and −0.86 log2: factors 0.64 and 0.55) and the share of bulk Lamp5 that the cge-derived class carries (s_Lamp5), the implied fall of that class's share is r_cge = 1 − (1 − factor)/s_Lamp5(cge); that r is then run through the Gabrd weights to give the composition-only Gabrd change, with the rises of the Sst, Npy and Cck classes (mge-derived and cge-derived classes) added by the same method (each rise from its own marker, each marker's class specificity as the premise).
3. **Reading rule.** The composition-only Gabrd change is "comparable to the observed" if it reaches half of it (log2 −0.4) under the middle scenario and "sufficient" if it reaches −0.8 under any scanned scenario; it is "small" if it stays under −0.2 in every scenario. The words are fixed before the numbers.

## What the class tables cannot tell (stated now)
They are wild-type pools (no null cell was measured); human nuclei stand in for mouse cells; broad cluster types hide the neurogliaform subtype; Gabrd in the mouse class table is low at P7 (the age of the pool), so the mouse table cannot rank interneuron classes for delta at P13 to P17; shares are assumed; the cerebellar control (Gabrd highest in granule cells, no fall; Lamp5 up) is imperfect because the same genotype changes other things there; and nothing here says a per-cell receptor change is absent, only how large a composition shift would have to be to do the job alone.
