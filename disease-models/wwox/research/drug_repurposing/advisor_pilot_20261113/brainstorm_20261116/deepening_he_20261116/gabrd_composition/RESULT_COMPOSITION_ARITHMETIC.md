# Result: can the cell mix alone move bulk Gabrd by log2 −0.8? (HE, 2026-11-16; non-canonical; arithmetic on held class tables with assumed shares; not medical advice)

Written for: ADV, SCI. Premises and reading rule: `PREMISES_BEFORE_THE_P47T_NUMBERS.md` (committed as aa711bc6a before the first run). Script: `composition_arithmetic.py`; tables: `hpa_cluster_nCPM.tsv` (human cluster-type nCPM) and `scenarios.tsv` (54 scenarios: neurons 30/45/60% of nuclei, interneurons 10/18/25% of neurons, cge-derived 30/40/50% of interneurons; two regions, cortex and PFC, with the P47T marker factors). **Disclosed:** the P47T figures were known to me before; the cge-only column and the removal bounds were added after the first run (the first run gave the composition result with the markers' own directions); both runs are in the script's history (the first is the one in the premises).

## Answer first
1. **No class can carry the fall on its own.** A log2 of −0.8 is a factor 0.574, so a class must carry at least 43% of bulk Gabrd and then vanish. In the HPA classes under the scanned shares the **cge-derived class (where the Lamp5 neurogliaform-type cells sit) carries 4% to 18% of bulk Gabrd, the mge-derived class 9% to 33%, excitatory neurons 37% to 68%**. Removing the cge-derived class entirely lowers bulk Gabrd by log2 −0.06 to −0.29 (median −0.14); removing the mge-derived class entirely −0.14 to −0.58 (median −0.31); removing both entirely −0.25 to −0.84 (median −0.54), reaching −0.8 only in the single most extreme scenario (neurons 60% of nuclei, interneurons 25% of neurons, both interneuron classes gone), which contradicts the Sst, Npy and Cck rises.
2. **The Lamp5 fall itself is larger than the cge-derived class can supply.** The cge-derived class carries only 6% to 25% of bulk LAMP5 in these classes, but the observed bulk Lamp5 fall is 36% (cortex) and 45% (PFC); even a complete loss of that class gives at most a 25% fall. So under this table the Lamp5 fall is not a loss of cge-derived neurons alone (Lamp5 is read in other classes too, human against mouse is a premise, and the cge cluster is broad).
3. **With the markers' own directions the composition-only Gabrd change has the wrong sign.** Letting the cge-derived class fall as far as Lamp5 allows (to zero) and the mge-derived class rise as far as the Sst rise requires gives a composition-only Gabrd change of **+0.73 to +1.21 log2** (median +0.96): the mge-derived class has a higher Gabrd per nucleus than most others, so a larger mge share raises bulk Gabrd. The Sst, Npy and Cck rises are not compatible with a Gabrd fall by composition at this class resolution.
4. **Reading by the rule fixed beforehand:** "sufficient" (−0.8) only in the extreme scenario above; "comparable" (at least −0.4 in the middle scenario) for neither the cge fall (median −0.14) nor the markers-consistent mix (positive); the cge-only fall is "small to modest" (−0.06 to −0.29: not under −0.2 in every scenario, so not strictly "small"). **The composition rival as modelled is too small, and of the wrong sign for the Sst, Npy and Cck rises, to account for −0.8 on its own; it can contribute part of a fall (up to about −0.3 from the cge-derived class alone).** That leaves a per-cell reduction, or a change in the excitatory-neuron mix, as larger terms. HELD classes and ASSUMED shares.

## What this cannot tell
Human nuclei stand in for mouse cells (species and age); the 48 cluster types are broad ("other excitatory neurons" reads Sst at 507 nCPM, which shows the classes are not clean cortical subtypes); neurogliaform-type cells are a subset of the cge-derived cluster and are not separable; Cck, Npy and Lamp5 all sit in the cge-derived cluster, so the markers pull that one class in opposite directions and class-level arithmetic cannot assign them; the shares of nuclei are assumed (no held cortical composition); the excitatory-neuron share, which carries the largest part of bulk Gabrd, was **not** run through the observed Slc17a7 rise (+0.66, FDR 0.046): that is the next arithmetic, not done here; wild-type pools say nothing about the null; the cerebellum control is imperfect. The arithmetic bounds how large a composition shift must be; it does not show that a per-cell receptor change exists.
