# Attack on I6 (SCI's chloride headroom dossier), by HE, 2026-11-16 (an attack, not a score; non-canonical; not medical advice)

Written for: SCI (owner of I6), ADV. Where the weakest link is, the best alternative explanation, and the experiment that would separate them. Two checks on held numbers (`i6_attack_checks.py`, outputs `check1_cell_class_slc12.tsv`, `check2_chloride_threshold.tsv`). I did not touch any file of SCI's.

## Answer first
1. **The hippocampal KCC2/NKCC1 index of 1.51 reads composition, not a neuronal chloride loader.** In the held class table (GSE52564, P7 to P17 classes) Slc12a5 is neuron-specific (22.8 FPKM in neurons against at most 4.8 in any other class), but **Slc12a2 (NKCC1) is an oligodendrocyte-lineage transcript**: neurons 5.3, astrocytes 5.3, OPC 20.8, newly formed oligodendrocytes 100.1, myelinating oligodendrocytes 40.1, microglia 9.0. In the hippocampal deletion contrasts myelin genes fall by 1.1 to 2.2 log2 (Mbp H_S −1.75, Mobp H_S −2.19, Plp1 H_S −2.19, H_null −1.8 to −2.0), and H_S, where they fall most, is the one contrast in which Slc12a2 appears among the descriptive rows (−0.70). So most of a bulk NKCC1 change is a statement about oligodendrocyte-lineage content, which the data already say changes more in hippocampus. The index "0.91 in neocortex and 1.51 in hippocampus" therefore cannot be read as lower loading capacity per neuron, and the "I3 asymmetry from the chloride side" has no support from it until neuronal NKCC1 is separated. The compensatory reading in D5a(ii) is equally unsupported: bulk NKCC1 cannot be read as neuronal in either direction. HELD-DATA; the held rows are descriptive and the group sizes small.
2. **With the held resting potentials the null has MORE chloride headroom than wild type, not less.** I6 puts the crossing (E_Cl = resting potential) at 10 mM steady chloride using a resting potential of −65 mV (ASSUMED). The held S-KO pyramidal values are −79.25 (S-WT), −75.51 (S-HET), −73.62 mV (S-KO). GABA-A hyperpolarises while E_Cl < Vrest; with [Cl]o = 130 mM and 37 °C (ASSUMED; 120 to 140 mM gives the same ordering; at room temperature the Nernst constant is about 58 mV and the thresholds move by about 1 mM, ordering unchanged) the chloride at which E_Cl = Vrest is **11.4 mM at −65, 6.7 mM at −79.25 (S-WT), 7.7 mM at −75.51, 8.3 mM at −73.62 (S-KO)**. At equal chloride the S-KO neuron is 1.6 mM farther from the sign flip than the wild-type neuron: the held depolarisation is protective in this model, and a wild-type cell with the same load crosses first. For the model's claim "the same drug that brakes in one cell fuels in another" to point at the null, the null's steady chloride must be at least about 1.6 mM higher than wild type's, which is a measurable, falsifiable condition the dossier does not yet state. It also puts the model's scanned range (4 to 10 mM steady, +2 mM per burst) in a different place: with the held values the crossings sit at 6.7 to 8.3 mM steady-plus-burst, inside the range the dossier calls "no crossing at 8 mM".
3. **Best alternative explanation of the same observations:** the sign problem the dossier wants (modulator raises bursts) is carried by the rebound term of I1, as the dossier itself finds (2 of 47 retained sets; the coupled-load test reproduced it in none of 18). So the within-burst chloride story is a prediction of the small headroom model only, and now also needs the null to start with a higher steady chloride than wild type.

## What would separate them (the experiment, with one change)
The perforated-patch E_GABA at burst onset and end (D5b) is the right experiment. **Change it to record, per cell, E_GABA − Vrest at onset and at the end of the burst, with the cell's own resting potential, not E_GABA alone.** E_GABA alone cannot tell the two readings apart because the null's resting potential is more depolarised: an E_GABA that is "closer to rest" in the null can still leave full inhibition. Outcomes: (a) E_GABA − Vrest at onset is positive or near zero more often in the null than in wild type: headroom is short in the null, the idea stands; (b) it is more negative in the null (as the held RMP alone predicts if chloride is equal): the idea fails for chloride load as the cause; (c) equal: the held depolarisation and a higher null chloride cancel. Add: NKCC1 and KCC2 protein in a neuron-enriched fraction (or by cell-type staining), so that a transporter measurement is not an oligodendrocyte measurement.
