Scope note. The folder holds no GABA-A subunit missense data. Its only GABA-A content is: GABRB2/GABRB3 transcripts down in WWOX-KO organoids (abundance only), reduced spontaneous inhibition in L2/3 pyramidal neurons (CLAIM 021), and E_GABA, chloride, NKCC1 and KCC2 being unmeasured in every WWOX system. The "held evidence" lines below are therefore either disciplines or analogies from the WWOX proteostasis work, or such a neighbouring datum. They are labelled that way and are never direct evidence on a GABRx variant. Mechanisms that rest on general knowledge rather than on the folder are marked "(general knowledge, not held)". Two measurement cautions come from the folder's chloride work (CC-20260826-EGABA-EXPERIMENT-01). First, a GABA-evoked current must be recorded with defined [Cl-] (whole-cell, or an outside-out patch) when the question is receptor function. Second, in a gramicidin recording the current mixes receptor number with driving force. Every "GABA-evoked current" prediction below assumes the first configuration.

H1. Statement: The mutant subunit is recognised as misfolded in the ER and cleared by ER-associated degradation (ERAD) before it assembles, so lowering ERAD flux raises the ER-resident pool and, if the folded fraction can exit, the surface pool. · Held evidence: supportive by analogy only. dismissal_ledger_current.md row D-02 and N-11 in mechanism_intervention_map.md state that the ER chaperone and ERAD logic is valid only for ER/secretory proteins. WWOX is cytosolic, so that logic was withdrawn for it. GABA-A subunits are ER-inserted transmembrane proteins, so the same premise applies in the opposite direction here (disease-models/wwox/research/dismissal_ledger_current.md; disease-models/wwox/analysis/mechanism_intervention_map.md). R-09 in the same map lists proteasome inhibitors as unacceptable chronic paediatric therapy (safety constraint, not a mechanism contradiction). · Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading says the protein is lost and surface loss is the defect. This one says the clearance route is the lever, and that it must be measured rather than assumed. dismissal_ledger_current.md D-01 warns that "ubiquitin means proteasome" is a textbook default, so the route here is ERAD or ER-phagy, not assumed proteasome. · Rejecting observation: in mutant-expressing neurons, a cycloheximide chase shows no slowing of mutant-subunit decay when the ERAD E3 ligase SEL1L/HRD1 is knocked out (general knowledge, not held), and total protein is unchanged. That rejects ERAD as the clearance route. · Discriminating manipulation vs nearest rival H2 (ER folding capacity): conditional loss of the ERAD E3 complex, genetic (SEL1L/HRD1), or an ERAD-inhibition class acting on the p97/VCP step. Predicts total and ER-retained mutant subunit up. Surface expression rises only if a folded fraction exists, and is expected to stay flat or rise modestly. GABA-evoked current is unchanged or modestly up, and it is not expected to match the rise in total protein, because the added protein is largely trapped. The rival H2 instead predicts surface and current rising together with only a modest rise in total.

H2. Statement: The ER's folding and assembly capacity is the limit, so raising it lets more mutant subunit fold and reach the surface. · Held evidence: analogy only; the withdrawn molecules there were chemical ER chaperones. dismissal_ledger_current.md D-02/D-03 and N-11/N-12 in mechanism_intervention_map.md. They state that "it is a chaperone" does not give the sign of the effect, which depends on which chaperone and which route. N-12 withdrew an HSP70 co-inducer because HSC70 can deliver a mutant to the lysosome. This contradicts naive chaperone induction. I keep H2 because the held counter-argument concerns a cytosolic client (the WWOX case) and a cytosolic chaperone (HSC70). It does not settle the sign for ER-lumenal chaperones acting on an ER client. The sign for GABA-A is open and is tested by the manipulation below. · Usual reading vs this one: a chemical chaperone is expected to help any misfolded protein. This one says benefit is conditional on the client being in the ER and the chaperone acting there. Raising a cytosolic HSP70 axis could reduce the surface pool. · Rejecting observation: ATF6-arm activation (or BiP overexpression) raises BiP but leaves mutant surface biotinylated fraction unchanged or lower, with an unchanged or rising ERAD turnover rate. That rejects folding capacity as limiting. · Discriminating manipulation vs H1: induce the ATF6 arm of the unfolded-protein response (a class acting on BiP/foldases) while leaving ERAD intact. Predicts total mutant protein modestly up, surface fraction up, and GABA-evoked current up in proportion to surface, provided the folded subunits assemble. Under H1 the same manipulation changes little and ERAD block is required.

H3. Statement: The subunit folds, but assembly with its partner subunits fails, so surface loss is a stoichiometry problem that extra partner subunit corrects. · Held evidence: no held data on GABA-A assembly. The nearest held discipline is D-04 in dismissal_ledger_current.md and the "synthesis, solubility, turnover, route, function are distinct questions" rule in variant_triage_rescuability.md. · Usual reading vs this one: the usual reading says the mutant protein is unstable. This one says the mutant may be stable but fail to find its partners, which looks the same on a total-protein blot. · Rejecting observation: co-expressing a partner subunit (for example the gamma2 or beta subunit appropriate to the receptor class) does not raise mutant surface biotinylation, while a surface-trafficking-competent control mutant does respond. That rejects an assembly-limited surface pool. · Discriminating manipulation vs H1: a genetic increase in the partner subunit in the same neuron or cell, with no change to quality-control machinery. Predicts surface expression up and, if the mutant incorporates into a gating-competent pentamer, GABA-evoked current up. Total mutant protein is unchanged because it was never degraded. H1 predicts the opposite, with partner overexpression doing nothing and ERAD block raising total protein.

H4. Statement: A ligand binding the orthosteric or an allosteric site acts as a pharmacological chaperone, stabilising the mutant during assembly and raising surface expression independently of its effect on channel gating. · Held evidence: mechanism-class analogy only. proteostasis_rationale.md section 3 lists site-specific pharmacological chaperones and kinetic stabilisers and notes they need a binding pocket (the WWOX obstacle, which does not apply here because GABA-A has defined ligand sites). The same file's D-04 says stabilising does not imply function. · Usual reading vs this one: ligands are read as drugs acting on function. This one says the binding event itself is a folding aid, so a ligand can raise surface expression while blocking current. · Rejecting observation: incubating cells with an orthosteric antagonist (gabazine class), then washing out, does not raise the mutant surface fraction compared with the same cells without antagonist. That rejects ligand-driven folding. · Discriminating manipulation vs H2: a competitive antagonist exposed during biosynthesis and then washed out (a class that binds but does not gate). Predicts surface expression up. GABA-evoked current is absent during exposure and recovers to above the baseline after washout in proportion to the surface rise. H2 predicts no ligand-site dependence, and a site-dead double mutant should abolish only H4's effect.

H5. Statement: Folded mutant subunits are held back by a defect in ER export, specifically palmitoylation-dependent or COPII-dependent exit, so enhancing export raises surface expression. · Held evidence: none in the folder; no GABA-A trafficking data is held. This one rests on (general knowledge, not held) palmitoylation of the gamma2 subunit by ZDHHC3/7 and COPII export. The held discipline is that route must be measured, not assumed (dismissal_ledger_current.md D-01). · Usual reading vs this one: surface loss is read as degradation. This one says protein can be abundant and ER-resident and still lost from the surface. · Rejecting observation: a ZDHHC3/7 gain of function does not move the mutant's surface fraction, and an ER-export-competent mutant control does respond. That rejects an export bottleneck. · Discriminating manipulation vs H1: raise the activity of the palmitoyl-transferase ZDHHC3/7 (genetic) in the mutant neuron. Predicts total protein unchanged, surface up, and GABA-evoked current up in proportion. H1 predicts no effect, since H1's lesion is clearance upstream of export.

H6. Statement: The mutant reaches the surface normally, but is removed faster by regulated endocytosis and lysosomal degradation, so slowing internalisation restores the surface pool. · Held evidence: analogy. Held WWOX work separates the surface pool from synthesis and turnover (proteostasis_rationale.md section 5) and describes lysosomal HSC70-dependent delivery as the measured route for the P252A model (dismissal_ledger_current.md D-01/D-03). Direct evidence for GABA-A is not held. · Usual reading vs this one: loss of surface protein is read as defective delivery. This one says delivery is intact and removal is the defect. · Rejecting observation: a surface-biotinylation internalisation assay shows the mutant's endocytic rate equal to wild type, and dynamin/clathrin block raises the wild-type surface pool but not the mutant's. That rejects accelerated endocytosis. · Discriminating manipulation vs H1: block regulated endocytosis of the beta-subunit AP2 site (genetic, for example a phospho-site mutation that prevents AP2 binding, general knowledge, not held). Predicts surface expression up with total protein up only by the surface fraction, because ER-resident protein is not affected. GABA-evoked current rises if the surface mutant is gating-competent. H1 predicts that only ERAD manipulation, not endocytosis, works.

H7. Statement: Surface expression is largely intact but the mutant channels are gating-impaired, so the "loss of surface" premise is wrong and a positive allosteric modulator class would restore current without changing surface protein. · Held evidence: against the question's premise; held discipline from dismissal_ledger_current.md D-04 and proteostasis_rationale.md ("stable but inert", abundance and function must be measured together). N-04 in mechanism_intervention_map.md and meta_gaba_paradox_current.md caution against any broad GABAergic push in WWOX, but that concerns a clinical strategy under an unmeasured chloride gradient. Here the PAM is a bench probe at mechanism level, so I keep the hypothesis. · Usual reading vs this one: current loss follows surface loss. This one says the two come apart and the surface count alone cannot predict current. · Rejecting observation: surface biotinylation shows the mutant surface fraction is reduced to the same degree as the current is, and a PAM class (benzodiazepine-site, neurosteroid, general anaesthetic) fails to increase current per surface receptor. That rejects a gating-only lesion. · Discriminating manipulation vs H4: acute application of a PAM class to cells already expressing the mutant. Predicts surface expression unchanged, because no biosynthesis or trafficking is involved, and GABA-evoked current up by a factor independent of surface number. H4's predictions are surface up before any acute application, with washout dependence.

H8. Statement: Mutant receptors reach the cell surface but fail to be retained at inhibitory synapses because of scaffold (gephyrin/collybistin/neuroligin-2) engagement, so the loss is of synaptic rather than total surface receptor. · Held evidence: indirect. meta_gaba_paradox_current.md and the CLAIM 021 context (reduced spontaneous inhibition with halved inhibition onto L2/3 pyramidal neurons while interneurons look alike, therapeutic_translation_second_pass.md section 3.1) show a deficit in inhibitory output measured as synaptic current. No scaffold data held. · Usual reading vs this one: the surface pool is counted as one pool. This one separates synaptic from extrasynaptic surface pools. · Rejecting observation: gephyrin puncta colocalisation with the mutant subunit equals wild type, and the mIPSC amplitude deficit tracks the total surface fraction rather than being larger. That rejects a synaptic-retention defect. · Discriminating manipulation vs H6: raise gephyrin or collybistin expression (genetic) in the mutant neuron. Predicts total surface biotinylation unchanged, synaptic cluster fraction up, mIPSC amplitude up and the whole-cell GABA-puff current largely unchanged. H6 predicts total surface up and puff current up.

H9. Statement: The defect is upstream of folding, in synthesis (translation, ribosome-associated quality control, or an integrated-stress-response brake), so the surface deficit shrinks when translation of the mutant is allowed to run. · Held evidence: direct analogy. proteostasis_rationale.md section 4 records that the WWOX case could not discriminate impaired translation from premature degradation, and mechanism_intervention_map.md N-05 warns that raising synthesis of a destabilised protein can be neutral or harmful. It also records that mTOR/EIF4EBP1 was down in WWOX-KO organoids (N-01), a WWOX-specific datum that does not transfer to a GABRx variant. · Usual reading vs this one: missing protein is read as degradation. This one keeps synthesis as a separate cause, and says more synthesis can make things worse. · Rejecting observation: a nascent-protein pulse labelling shows mutant synthesis equal to wild type, so the deficit is entirely after translation. That rejects a synthesis lesion. · Discriminating manipulation vs H1: an integrated-stress-response inhibitor class (acting on eIF2alpha dephosphorylation) while ERAD is left intact. Predicts total mutant protein up. Surface rises only if folding capacity is not exceeded, otherwise the added protein is cleared and the effect can reverse. GABA-evoked current follows surface and not total protein. H1 predicts no change in synthesis with ERAD block.

H10. Statement: Part of the surface loss is non-cell-autonomous, driven by glia-derived inflammatory or neurotrophic signalling that downregulates GABA-A surface expression, so the loss is partly a response to a state and not a property of the mutant. · Held evidence: a neighbouring datum, not direct. meta_gaba_paradox_current.md records raised Il6 and glial activation in the WWOX-KO hippocampus, and mechanism_intervention_map.md (R-06 and N-13) warns that glial targets can be "the target downstream of the target": neuron-restricted rescue reduced gliosis, so treating glia treats an effect. That is a caution against this hypothesis, and I keep it only as a rival that the experiment below can reject. · Usual reading vs this one: the surface loss is read as intrinsic to the mutant protein. This one says wild-type neighbours in the same inflamed environment would also lose surface receptors. · Rejecting observation: a mutant neuron cultured alone, free of glia, shows the same surface deficit as in a mixed culture, and a wild-type neuron placed in the same conditioned medium shows no deficit. That rejects non-cell-autonomy. · Discriminating manipulation vs H1: depletion of microglia or blockade of the IL-6/JAK-STAT axis (a signalling-class manipulation) in a co-culture or slice, with the mutant genotype fixed. Predicts surface expression up in the mixed preparation only (not in the isolated neuron) and GABA-evoked current up modestly and in step with it. H1 predicts a deficit that persists in isolation and responds only to ERAD or folding manipulation.
