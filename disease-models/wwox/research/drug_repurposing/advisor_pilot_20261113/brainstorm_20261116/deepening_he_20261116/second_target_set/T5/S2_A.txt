H1. Origin: S1-2 (OUTSIDE)
Statement: Selective activation of the ATF6 arm of the unfolded-protein response raises ER folding capacity (BiP, calnexin, PDI), which increases the post-ER fraction, surface subunit and GABA-evoked current of the missense subunit.
Corpus test: NOT STATED. ATF6 = 0 hits. The nearest held text is research/discovery_ledger_current.md:390-394 ("WWOX directs adaptive-vs-terminal UPR under ER stress … in the CNS the aim is to preserve survival"). It treats the UPR as a panel to measure, and its probes are the PERK and IRE1 inhibitors GSK2656157 and KIRA6 (line 418). Adjacent support for the compartment match is research/dismissal_ledger_current.md:68 (D-02). It says the ER-chaperone route is valid only for ER/secretory proteins, which is what a GABA-A subunit is, unlike WWOX. No line contradicts it.
Usual reading assumes / this changes: The usual reading treats UPR activation as the pathogenic stress of an ER-retained mutant. This reading uses only its adaptive, folding-capacity arm as the lever and holds the PERK/ATF4 arm constant. The folder's ATF4 data (claim_registry_current.md:465) show that arm acts through other routes.
Rejection observation: ATF6 activation raises BiP and chaperone transcripts but leaves the Endo-H-resistant fraction, surface and current of the mutant unchanged.
Discriminating manipulation (vs nearest rival H6, the HDAC-class chemical chaperone): inducible ATF6 active fragment in the mutant background, PERK and IRE1 held constant, with BiP knocked down in a parallel arm. Predicted: surface and current rise together, and the rise is lost with BiP knockdown. The rival (HDAC-class, transcription-dependent) would be insensitive to BiP knockdown. Both would raise surface and current if either works. Only BiP dependence separates them.

Statement: Prolonged calnexin/UGGT1 reglucosylation cycling retains the mutant in the ER, so shortening the cycle (UGGT1 loss, or a glucosidase-inhibitor class) releases it for export and raises surface and current.
Corpus test: NOT STATED. UGGT, glucosidase and calnexin = 0. The rival would show no change in the Endo-H-resistant fraction, because it acts after export.

H8. Origin: ENGINE (S1-1 as the nearest rival)
Statement: Much of the mutant's loss goes through a K63-ubiquitin / lysosomal route, not proteasomal ERAD, so blocking ERAD/p97 raises total protein but leaves surface and current unchanged.
Corpus test: SUPPORT as a held general rule (research/dismissal_ledger_current.md:67, D-01; fulltext_dossiers/PMID27551470.md:191-192; proteostasis_rationale.md:97, "The clearance route must be measured, not assumed"). For WWOX the "proteasome" endpoint is a default never measured (fulltext_dossiers/PMID19500159.md:224). For GABA-A receptors, not stated.
Usual reading assumes / this changes: S1-1 assumes ERAD is the rate-limiting step. This one holds that an ER-to-lysosome arm, or one that bypasses the proteasome, carries the flux. A proteasome-only test could therefore return a false negative (proteostasis_rationale.md:97).
Rejection observation: SEL1L/HRD1 or p97 loss, with matched lysosome-inhibitor controls, raises the Endo-H-resistant pool and surface.
Discriminating manipulation (vs S1-1, SEL1L-HRD1 knockdown): run the SEL1L/HRD1 knockdown and a lysosomal-acidification/ER-phagy block side by side in the same cells. Predicted for this reading: only the lysosomal arm lengthens half-life and raises surface and current, and the ERAD arm raises total protein only. ERAD-first predicts the reverse.

H9. Origin: S1-6, with S1-15 as the nearest rival (INSIDE)
Statement: A binding-site ligand acting as a pharmacological chaperone raises surface during synthesis and its benefit persists after washout, whereas a pure positive allosteric modulator raises current only while present. The chaperone effect can still fail by occluding gating.
Corpus test: SUPPORT. proteostasis_rationale.md:53-57 (site-specific pharmacological chaperones are one of three families) and research/dismissal_ledger_current.md:70 (D-04, stabilizing is not restoring function; "stable and completely inert" is a demonstrated case) and mechanism_intervention_map.md:348-353 ("stability is bought with occlusion"), 612 (promote a hit only if at least two orthogonal readouts beyond abundance recover). Neurosteroid and ganaxolone = 0. Benzodiazepine "GABAergic push" is a cautioned class (mechanism_intervention_map.md:651-658, N-04).
Usual reading assumes / this changes: The usual reading treats a positive modulator as acting on gating only. This one lets a ligand act as a folding aid and makes current-per-surface-receptor a required readout, so a surface rise alone cannot be called a rescue.
Rejection observation: The ligand raises current only in proportion to existing surface receptors, with no surface rise and no persistence after washout. That would mean pure gating potentiation.
Discriminating manipulation (vs S1-15, an acutely applied modulator): pre-incubate during synthesis, then wash out fully before recording. Predicted: surface rises and a washout-persistent current component remains; the pure modulator gives neither. Record current by perforated-patch so the chloride gradient does not distort it (research/commit_candidates/CC-20260826-EGABA-EXPERIMENT-01.md:146,160).

H10. Origin: ENGINE (S1-13 and S1-14 as rivals)
Statement: Partial, physiological-level restoration of the wild-type subunit in neurons is enough, because a graded threshold below full restoration holds. If the mutant is also a dominant-negative sink, selective silencing of the mutant allele adds gain beyond what dosage alone gives.
Corpus test: SUPPORT as a held design principle, NOT STATED for GABA-A. claim_registry_current.md:593-594 (CLAIM 032: haploinsufficiency is not deleterious, the threshold is below 50%, partial mosaic correction may suffice; limits stated: known for survival and morphology, not for cognition and epilepsy). mechanism_intervention_map.md:99, 107, 164-199 (neuron-restricted loss reproduces the phenotype and neuron-restricted rescue repairs it). mechanism_intervention_map.md:660-671 (N-05) holds that a non-allele-specific boost can preferentially amplify a missense allele of unknown folding fate. research/discovery_ledger_current.md:738 (FM-013) holds that supra-physiological rescue is a risk. "Dominant-negative" = 0.
Usual reading assumes / this changes: The usual reading says a global, high-level replacement is required. This one makes the lever a graded dosage in the right cell class, with allele silencing as a separate test of interference.
Rejection observation: Neuron-restricted restoration repairs interneuron IPSCs but leaves pyramidal surface and tonic current deficient (S1-13's own rejection test), or allele-specific silencing gives no surface gain over wild-type dosage alone.
Discriminating manipulation (vs S1-13 global replacement, and vs a haploinsufficiency-only model): compare a Synapsin-promoter vector at graded expression against a Dlx-enhancer (interneuron) vector and a CaMKII (pyramidal) vector, plus allele-specific silencing of the mutant. Predicted: the Synapsin graded arm recovers surface and IPSC amplitude at sub-50% wild-type expression, the Dlx arm raises interneuron surface and IPSCs with pyramidal tonic current unchanged, and the CaMKII arm does the reverse. Allele silencing raises wild-type surface and current above the 50% level only if the mutant is a dominant-negative sink. Under pure haploinsufficiency it gives no gain.
