The decisive measurement for applicability, as a candidate for the held-source list and not a proposal to measure: **the share of exon 9 inclusion at the correct junction (exon 8 to exon 9 with the canonical acceptor sequence) in cDNA from a sample carrying c.1057-2A>G in the expressing tissue, by a junction-specific RT-PCR with a control that detects exon 8 to exon 9 independently of the variant allele (allele-specific primers or long-read sequencing), expressed as a fraction of all spliced products.** If the fraction is indistinguishable from zero, the SLC26A4-type route does not apply; if a measurable fraction exists, the comparison becomes candidate-level. ## 3. Figure 3B, read at pixel level (705 x 530 px bucket JPEG)
* **What panel B shows:** three Sanger chromatogram segments: **FL, left (Ex7|Ex8):** bases `TTGTG` | `ACGAT` with a vertical line between exons 7 and 8; **FL, centre (Ex8|Ex9):** `AGGGG` | `GTTTT`; **Δ8 (Ex7|Ex9):** `TTGTG` | `GTTTT`. Each base call is under a single clean peak at the junction in the printed segments; I saw no double peak at either junction.
* **Legend (verbatim):** "Sequencing data of the two transcripts shown in (A)."
* **What it supports:** the FL transcript is the exon 7 to 8 to 9 joined product; the Δ8 transcript joins exon 7 to exon 9. The band called FL in panel A (lane 'Normal' and treated lanes alike) runs at one position.
* **What it cannot show:** (a) the acceptor dinucleotide or the mutant G, which is intronic and not in mRNA; genotype is stated in the text ("two patients with the A-2G homozygous mutation") and no genomic trace is printed in Figure 3; (b) which sample the traces come from (untreated, ASO-treated, patient 1 or 2); (c) whether the exon 7|8 boundary sits exactly at the canonical position. I did not compare the printed bases `ACGAT` with the reference exon 8 start, so a one-nucleotide shift is not excluded by me; the authors call the transcript "FL" and show it as the same band as the healthy control; (d) a quantitative split of FL against other products in treated cells, beyond the printed percentages on the gel image.
* **Panel A, gel and bar plot:** the printed percentages are read in § 2; the bar plot shows means with error bars and `***`; n = 3 and 2 per bar by the legend ("(n = 3 or 2)").
* The conclusion for ADV-257 is unchanged: INCLUDED at rule 1 to 3, rule 4 ("no nearby cryptic or created site") rests on the authors' calling of the band and not on a treated-sample junction read by me.

## 4. Side observation from 40225931 (ADV-257 excluded it; read here by IT)
SCN2A c.1177-2A>C (authentic 3' splice site disrupted, per its text) in a minigene: "Transfection with WT SCN2A minigene resulted in a correctly spliced 451 bp product. Transfection with MUT SCN2A minigene resulted in three products: 334 bp, 451 bp, and an aberrant 611 bp products." The 334 bp product, the most prevalent, is the use of "a cryptic exonic 3′ss". It is a second AG-destroyed acceptor with a wild-type-sized residual band in a minigene, which fits the residual-use reading of § 1.4; it is not evidence of rescue and not a second included hit.
