## Claims
C1. In postnatal day 7 rat pups, a single intraperitoneal dose of retigabine (ezogabine) up to 30 mg/kg produced no apoptosis; three doses of 15 mg/kg raised apoptosis in five of eight brain regions (P between 0.009 and 0.047). Also report exactly how the paper states the dosing schedule (number of doses, interval, window) wherever it states it, and whether those statements agree. Source: Brown 2016, PMID 26865186 (PMC5214840).
C2. In P7 rat pups, total brain concentrations after a single i.p. dose were 330, 1296 and 2013 ng/ml of tissue at 5, 15 and 30 mg/kg, about 1.1, 4.3 and 6.6 uM (molecular weight 303.3), peaking 1 to 2 h after the dose, with plasma half-life 2.2 to 2.5 h. Source: PMC5214840.
C3. In infants with tuberous sclerosis treated with vigabatrin, MRI abnormalities were seen in 35.5 percent of 80 vigabatrin-treated infants against 13.5 percent of those without the drug (risk ratio 3.57), and resolved on stopping in 13 of 13 with follow-up. Source: PMID 39641935.
C4. Acute lithium toxicity is much higher in 5- and 15-day-old rats than in adult rats, and the age dependence is set by distribution and renal elimination. Source: Kersten 1981, PMID 7271442.
C5. Sources: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04639310 and NCT04912856 (say which record holds which numbers).
C6. Sources: Zou 2009, PMID 19126600, and Soriano 2010, PMID 20418696.
C7. A single injection of the competitive NMDA antagonist CGP 43487 (2.5 and 5.0 mg/kg) at P5 in rats impaired conditioned freezing at P20 while the heart-rate response was unaffected. Source: Hunt 2006, PMID 16416331.
C8. Ganaxolone given twice daily to preterm guinea-pig pups caused sedation, poor weight gain and higher neonatal mortality. Source: PMID 30237570.
C9. In a phase 3 trial of gaboxadol in children aged 4 to 12 years with Angelman syndrome, the primary endpoint (CGI-I-AS) difference was zero (P = 0.83) and gaboxadol was well tolerated. Source: PMID 37639777.
C10. In the adult epileptic rat slice, ezogabine had no effect at 0.3 uM, reduced event duration to 56 to 61 percent of control at 1 to 3 uM, had a frequency EC50 of 11.8 uM, and abolished activity at 100 uM. Source: West 2018, PMID 30328622 (PMC6215509).
C11. THIP (gaboxadol) was used at 10 and 50 uM in P5 to P8 mouse neocortical and CA1 slices; 50 uM lowered firing without abolishing network activity. Source: Liddiard 2024, PMID 38176909 (PMC10869160).
C12. Ethosuximide (200 mg/kg) and ulixacaltamide (60 mg/kg) each reduced total seizure incidence by 64 percent in Gabrb3+/D120N mice (vehicle 21.4 versus 7.7; 21.1 versus 7.5; t tests as printed). Source: Harman 2026, PMID 42274296 (PMC13395050).
