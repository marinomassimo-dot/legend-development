## 0. Answer first
1. No antiseizure agent is shown to match WWOX specifically, and no mechanism class is shown to match better than the others. What exists is a weak, single-origin ranking of classes (five-way tie at the top, three classes when the one origin is collapsed) that, where it can be compared with a baseline, equals the PTZ pharmacology profile.
2. The result that matters is a design, not a drug: a pilot slice experiment (EXPERIMENT_BRIEF_V1_1.md) that separates WWOX-specific from generic effects, and it cannot be scored until a laboratory runs it.
3. Predictive-generative capacity was measured, not improved: the starting reference is 48 percent of scored generative predictions held, with predictor equal to scorer in 51 percent of rows; no round of this programme shows leaps beating a cheap memory-free search.
4. Two instruments were built that did not exist this morning and that the next programme can reuse: a prediction ledger whose freeze order git can check, and a pinned, reproducible matrix generator (v1 and v2).

## 4. The experiment (claims E1 to E4)
E1. One pilot of four animals per genotype, both regions (neocortex and hippocampus), both genotypes at matched age, with a wild-type trigger or evoked-current control, two exposure levels per arm analysed on the ratio scale, interval and duration read separately, a tonic GABA-A current readout with the uptake condition fixed before the run; animals as ranges 6 to 34 (WO-10), 5 to 29 (one primary burst comparison), about a quarter more for two endpoints; the variance of burst suppression is not held, the pilot measures it. Source: ../experiment_brief_presidio013_sci_20261116/EXPERIMENT_BRIEF_V1_1.md.
E2. Outcome-to-top-set table made with the official generator: a specific pore blocker leaves 1 of 15 on top, a specific GABA-A PAM 2, generic suppression everywhere 4, no suppression none. A T-type blocker with interval up and duration unchanged is predicted by both H-B1 and the GABA-A rebound hypothesis (HSCI-2); the GABA-A arm's sign separates them.
E3. Held-data results that contradict a premise carried by a session (SCI, opinion): hippocampal sparing is not a recombination artefact (Wwox falls more in the hippocampus, -85 percent, than in neocortex, -68 percent); Npy up but Npy1r/2r/5r down in two laboratories; Wwox-positive nuclei fall in astrocyte-like nuclei too (28.1 to 3.3 percent) so 'neuron-specific' wording does not match; the mTORC1 signature is line-specific; the organoid is not simply immature on receptor axes.
E4. Readiness sheets (SCI, 13 representatives plus 3 GluN2B tool compounds, label-level, 31 of 31 signal quotes verbatim against DailyMed XML): flags are by the source's wording; 11 of 13 are red by the rule on CNS and antiseizure labels: this is an inventory, not a ranking and not a safety review. ADMET-AI, medchem and RDKit are absent: BLOCK-1 is 'not run' at tool level. Source: ../repurposing_readiness_sci_20261116/README.md.
