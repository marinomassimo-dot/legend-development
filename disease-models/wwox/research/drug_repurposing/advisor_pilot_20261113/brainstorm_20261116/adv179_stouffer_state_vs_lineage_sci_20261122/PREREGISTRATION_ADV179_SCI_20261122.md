## The two questions
Q1. In the study's own control system (mouse cortical organoids with an in-vivo comparator), does a real match of transcriptional state (developmental stage assigned from RNA) coexist with a discordant direct radial-glia LINEAGE time course?
Q2. Are the two measurements LINKED (same cells, organoids, units and time points) or only aggregate (different experiments, batches, ages or denominators)?

## Readings (fixed)
* **Q1 yes, strong (matched):** a named pair of panels where, in the same organoid system and at the same days, the RNA-based stage or identity assignment agrees with the in-vivo comparator (or between conditions) while a direct lineage measurement (clonal tracing, birth-dating, a progression of radial glia through lineage states, a measured time course) is displaced in time or direction, with a stated unit (organoid, batch, clone, cell) for each.
* **Q1 yes, weak:** the same discordance but across different batches, days or protocols, so only the aggregate pattern shows it.
* **Q1 no:** state and lineage agree, or the state match is not a stage assignment (for example only a marker list), or no direct lineage measurement exists.
* **Q2 linked:** same cells (lineage barcodes with RNA), or same organoids and days with both readouts, with the unit named. **Q2 aggregate:** separate experiments, different batches or denominators.

## Exits (fixed)
* **KEEP:** Q1 yes, strong and Q2 linked, the study is a control system with a stated age and protocol boundary, I can name the claim the difference would qualify (RP-043's treatment of a future developmental-map match when adjudicating ADV-146 rival B), and RP-043's current text does not already carry the exact distinction. Then ONE named qualification of RP-043.
* **NARROW:** Q1 yes but weak or only aggregate, or Q2 aggregate, or the example is a control-system one-off; then the qualification is stated as a logical boundary with the one example, labelled as such (not a demonstrated generality).
* **DROP:** (i) RP-043 already makes this exact distinction; (ii) the RNA match and the lineage readouts cannot be jointly interpreted (different cells, ages, batches, denominators without a link); or (iii) I cannot name which claim the difference would qualify. Also DROP if the mapping in the paper is not a stage assignment of a kind a developmental-map match would resemble.
