# ADV-179 result: a transcriptional-state match beside a discordant lineage time course in Stouffer et al. 2026, and what it does to RP-043 (SCI successor Z, 2026-11-22; methodological comparison in a mouse organoid control system; not WWOX evidence; no experiment, candidate, ranking or clinical implication; P-SCI-1 and its scoring inputs unchanged; no contact; no e-mail address to any service; not medical advice)

Pre-registration: `PREREGISTRATION_ADV179_SCI_20261122.md` (commit 39413339b, before any page of the paper body was read). Receipt: `RECEIPT_ADV179_SCI_20261122.json`.

## Answer first
**NARROW.** One named qualification of RP-043, stated as a logical boundary supported by one aggregate-level example in a mouse control system, not as a demonstrated generality.

**The qualification (RP-043, rival B of ADV-146):** a developmental-map match (NeuRoDev or any similar map, made by correlating cell or cluster profiles with reference pseudobulk) assigns an IDENTITY at the sampling time. It says that the sampled cells resemble a reference stage; it does not say how the cells arrived there or on what timetable. For rival B ("an immature state drives the week-16 activity gap") a match can support only "the sampled state is earlier or more immature"; adjudicating "maturation explains the gap" needs a separately measured within-line longitudinal contrast (the same per-line readout at two ages) or a lineage or birth-dating readout. The present pooled WWOX values and a future map match alone do not decide it.

Why this is NARROW and not KEEP (pre-registered rule): Q1 is yes, but at the aggregate level only; Q2 is "linked" only for a different pair (below). Why not DROP: RP-043 already says that "equal at week 7, divergent at week 16" is a cross-assay statement, not a time course (ADV-142), and ADV-162 says a stage marginal is not a within-class trajectory; neither states the map-match-is-identity qualification, and the paper is a control system where identity and lineage readouts diverge.

## Q1: can a real state match coexist with a discordant lineage time course here? Yes, at the aggregate level
**The pair (aggregate):**
* **State side, Extended Data Fig. 1e and 1f with Fig. 1i,j.** Organoid scRNA-seq (mTmG;Emx1-cre lines, Gfp+ cells, D8, D13, D20, D25; two independent cell lines and two batches each; four biological replicates per time point) was aligned to in vivo mouse cortex (E10, E13, E16, P1 from this study, plus two published datasets, E9 to E18) by (i) relative abundance of four cell types and (ii) pairwise Pearson correlation of per-cell-type pseudobulk across all shared genes, z-scored within each reference time point. The text states D8, D13, D20, D25 match E10, E13, E16, P1; Fig. 1g,j show a homogeneous RGP pool and overlapping organoid and embryo cell types.
* **Lineage side, Fig. 2g to k with Extended Data Fig. 7 and Fig. 3k,l.** MADM clones (MADM-11GT/TG;Emx1-creER lines; induced D8 to D15, analysed at D20; 4-OHT; each induction day from 3 cell lines and 3 differentiations, with some days 2 lines and 4 differentiations) against the earlier in vivo MADM framework: proliferative clones about 37 cells at D10 against about 71 at E10; all three division modes (proliferative, asymmetric neurogenic, "small neurogenic") present at every induction day while "small neurogenic" clones are not seen in vivo; less correlated sister-RGP output; lineage restriction 31/87 (35.6%) at D8 and 25/78 (32.1%) at D13 against 11/386 (2.85%) in vivo. The authors' own sentence joins them: "note that E10 and D10 are comparable transcriptionally" beside the halved clone size, and the abstract says "despite uniform single-cell transcriptional signatures of RGPs and a unitary lineage trajectory".

**What bounds that coexistence (the strongest alternatives):**
1. **The match is coarse and relative.** In ED1e the best-matching reference age is broad (RGP D8 best E9 to E10; D13 best E12; D20 broad E16 to E18; neurons D13 flat E10 to E12, D20 E16 to E17, D25 E18; read from the heatmap image), and the scale is a z-score within each reference time point, so it ranks reference ages and does not give an absolute similarity. P1 is matched by cell-type abundance (ED1f), not by that heatmap. The text itself reports hundreds of DEGs between organoid and embryonic RGPs, largely age-specific (ED14), so the "match" is partial. A day-level misalignment could contribute to the discordance; I cannot separate it. D10 was not sequenced (scRNA time points are D8, D13, D20, D25), so "E10 and D10 are comparable transcriptionally" is an interpolation.
2. **Different lines and cells.** The state data come from mTmG;Emx1-cre organoids; the lineage data from MADM-11GT/TG;Emx1-creER mESC lines. Same protocol, lineage and day labels; not the same cells, lines or organoids.
3. **Sampling and clone-size dependence.** Lineage restriction is called from CTIP2 and SATB2 in reconstructed sections; many clones are small (2 to 4 cells), where "restriction" is easier to meet by chance; recovery of whole clones in sections and mosaic induction affect it. The in vivo comparator is an earlier dataset (n = 386 clones), not a parallel run. Cell death was addressed (below 6% caspase-3; D15 against D20) and is not excluded as a minor factor, which the authors state.
4. **Clonal nesting.** Clones are the unit (GLMM for clone size; chi-square counts for restriction); clones sit within organoids, lines and differentiations. Not re-analysed.
5. **Ex-vivo context.** The authors' own interpretation is the missing niche (non-cell-autonomous cues); a mouse ESC organoid with no interneurons, vasculature or CSF. Not human, not WWOX.

## Q2: linked or aggregate?
* **The state match and the lineage discordance are AGGREGATE** (different lines and cells, same protocol and day labels). They are not a same-cell or same-organoid pair.
* **Linked pair (same cells): Fig. 4 MADM-CloneSeq.** D13-induced clones collected at D20: 287 cells from 68 clones from 45 organoids, 195 retained from 55 clones (21 asymmetric neurogenic, 34 small neurogenic); RNA identity and pseudotime by projection onto an in vivo reference. Result: no significant pseudotime difference between lineage-restricted and translaminar small neurogenic clones (Fig. 4h: two-way ANOVA with Tukey, P = 0.9; 16 translaminar, 11 DL-restricted, 7 UL-restricted clones); asymmetric against small neurogenic clones differ slightly in pseudotime (P = 0.005; the legend says the absolute difference is small). So in linked cells the RNA state of the postmitotic neurons did not carry the lineage restriction. Boundary: these are neurons at D20 from D13 induction; the RGP's RNA state at induction is not measured in the same clones, and clones, not cells, are the replicate unit.

## What this adds to RP-043, and the one thing it does not
* It supports the identity-only reading as a method statement: the match method used here (Pearson correlation to pseudobulk) is the same class as the NeuRoDev mapping described in ADV-162 (Pearson to cluster pseudobulk on about 500 genes; 15 nearest clusters). I have not tested NeuRoDev itself; the statement is about the method class.
* It does not show that a WWOX organoid's maturation is decoupled from its state, and it says nothing about WOREE, SCAR12 or rival B's truth. It qualifies how a FUTURE map match is consumed.
