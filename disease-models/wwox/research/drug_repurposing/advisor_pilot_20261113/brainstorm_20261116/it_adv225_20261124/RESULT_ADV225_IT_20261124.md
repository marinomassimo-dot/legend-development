## 3. What each section adds, with the anchor
**1 · 41489401, Table 1.** Group sizes by clinical response to memantine: Group 1 (gain-of-function, improved) 10; Group 2 (improved, then habituation) 4; Group 3 (not improved) 5; Group 4 (loss-of-function, indeterminate, no effect) 15; total 34. The in vitro columns are properties of the variant. Four variants appear on both sides of the response split with the same in vitro row: GRIN1 p.Met641Ile (individual 27, age 4, improved; individual 10, age 8, not improved; IC50 1.3 against 5.1 wild type), GRIN1 p.Met706Val (14, age 17, improved; 13, age 22, not improved; 4.1 against 4.9), GRIN2D p.Val667Ile (33 and 34, age 11, improved; 32, age 17, not improved; 4.0 against 0.57) and GRIN2B p.Gly611Val (21, age 8, improved; 3, age 18, improved then habituated; 1.9 against 1.5). What the registry statement lacks: **variant-level sensitivity does not decide the response**; and the table gives no meaning for the age column (age at treatment, at the table, or at last follow-up is not stated in the table). Not a claim: n = 4 pairs, a registry cohort. Memantine is not a WWOX datum.
**2 · 41570991, Tables 1 to 3.** Per-cell means from the four cells per condition (recomputed from the table): human glioblastoma cells, ATP 2.21e-5 to 1.17e-5 mM per cell (about -47 percent), ATP/ADP 8.73 to 6.79; mouse small-cell lung 2405, ATP 2.44e-5 to 2.18e-5, ATP/ADP 7.13 to 5.92; mouse 1578, **ATP 2.09e-5 to 4.27e-5 (about +104 percent)**, ATP/ADP 9.95 to 8.48. The text says "a significant decrease in this ratio for hGB and mSCLC 2405, with a similar tendency for mSCLC 1578": the ratio does, the total does not. Table 2 (RNA-seq, five replicates per arm, mouse 2405): the mitochondrial-encoded complex I genes fall (mt-Nd1 log2 fold change -0.41, mt-Nd3 -0.85, mt-Nd6 -0.48), while nuclear complex I subunits are flat (Ndufa1 q 0.051, Ndufa10 q 0.076). Table 3 is docking (CB-Dock2) contact residues on two structures, a prediction, not a measurement. Concentrations: 500 micromolar in the human cells and 250 micromolar in the mouse cells. What the registry lacks: the card entry for stiripentol is an LDH-inhibition statement (a held paper reports 10 to 38 percent inhibition of human LDHA by stiripentol and its enantiomers at 500 micromolar, and IC50 above 500 micromolar); this paper is a held rival mechanism at the same order of concentration.
**3 · 32023956, Tables 1 to 6.** The GLP-validated and the in-house assays differ in every parameter (cell background, 120 h against 60 h, 10 against 20 micromolar of the chaperone, n = 5 quadruplicate against n >= 3 duplicate) and in the amenability criterion (at least 3 percent absolute and 1.2-fold against at least 5 percent absolute or 1.5-fold with a floor). In vitro HEK293 over-expression, one disease (Fabry, lysosomal): transferability T4 to WWOX; it is a methodological table.
**4 · 41712149.** A review. The box states three summary sentences; the table of early-stage studies lists lorcaserin (5 plus 20 patients, a single "about 47 percent median motor seizure reduction" over a compassionate series and a retrospective series), verapamil (4 patients, 3 of 4 with 50 to 99 percent reduction, open label) and clemizole (zebrafish).
