## Why
Chain step S3 needs, for a small polar drug cleared by hepatic oxidation (CYP3A and CYP2E1 reported) and partly by renal excretion, how elimination capacity at postnatal days 12 to 17 in the mouse (and the rat as a second species) compares to the adult, and which fraction of ethosuximide clearance each route carries. Read `PREREGISTRATION_ADV122_SCI_20261120.md` in this folder (sections 2 and 5) and `framework/protocols/delegation_budget.md` sections 2, 7 and 9 first. ## Items (in this order; stop an item at the first source that answers the stated question, or when two routes are exhausted)
1. **Hepatic enzyme ontogeny in mouse and rat as printed**: Cyp3a (Cyp3a11, Cyp3a13 in mouse; Cyp3a2, 3a9, 3a18 in rat) and Cyp2e1 activity or expression at postnatal days 5 to 30 relative to adult (a single review with the figures is enough, then one primary source each). Report age, sex, strain, the measure (mRNA, protein, activity), the value relative to adult as printed.
2. **Renal ontogeny**: glomerular filtration rate and tubular function in the rat and mouse pup at P10 to P20 relative to adult, normalised how (per g kidney, per body weight, per surface area); the maturation timeline, as printed.
3. **Total-body clearance ontogeny of a probe drug** in the P12 to P17 mouse or rat (antipyrine, caffeine, theophylline, phenobarbital, a CYP3A substrate, or a renal-cleared polar drug such as inulin or antipyrine analogues): value relative to adult. One or two examples with doses, ages and matrices are enough.
4. **Ethosuximide specifics if any**: which rodent enzyme metabolises ethosuximide and whether the metabolising enzyme is induced or inhibited by anything an experimenter in the Cheng setting would use (PTZ, methylscopolamine, saline); and whether the null phenotype (growth retardation, hypoglycaemia as reported in Wwox null pups) is expected to change hepatic or renal clearance, as a statement a source makes, not your inference. If a source states it, give it; if none, say none found.
