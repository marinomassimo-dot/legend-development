## 1. What the number is, with equations and units
* Dose D = dose_mg_per_kg / 141.17 mg/mmol, in mmol/kg (72.3 mg/kg = 0.512 mmol/kg; 150 mg/kg = 1.063 mmol/kg). Apparent volume V in L/kg. Concentrations in mM = mmol/L; 1 ug/g of tissue at 1.00 g/mL = 1/141.17 mM (a 4 percent density difference is ignored).
* **Plasma (serum), total, one compartment, first-order absorption ka (per h) and elimination k = ln 2 / t_half:** C_p(t) = F D ka / (V (ka - k)) (exp(-k t) - exp(-ka t)). With ka large and k = 0, C_p = F D / V: **that is "dose over volume"**. It is the plasma concentration at distribution equilibrium with complete absorption and no elimination, for V = V_ss. It is *not* a brain concentration.
* **Brain, total, whole-brain homogenate:** C_b = Kp C_p, Kp = total brain / total plasma, dimensionless. For an unbound, passively distributed small polar drug Kp is about brain water fraction over plasma water fraction, about 0.79/0.93 = 0.85 (brain water 76.6 to 85.6 percent by age in the rat, producer D; plasma water 0.93 is an unsourced textbook value); rat steady-state ethosuximide brain/plasma 0.90 to 0.94 (abstract, PMID 590227); the rat cortex ratio is 2.04 at 5 min and 0.14 at 2 h after one dose (abstract, PMID 2047576).
* **Compared datum:** whole brains of male Swiss mice, 24 to 30 g, decapitation 45 min after 72.3 mg/kg i.p. (Tween 80 suspension), homogenised 1:2 in water, supernatant by fluorescence polarisation immunoassay, printed per g of wet tissue (PMID 22315091). Matrix: tissue homogenate, **not** plasma; time 45 min, **not** equilibrium at injection; unbound and total coincide for the plasma (no binding, label) and CSF equals plasma (label, human), so the free brain concentration is about the free plasma concentration and Kp relates total to total through water content.

## 2. What is a constraint and what is a model
| statement | status |
|---|---|
| the amount in the brain cannot exceed the amount absorbed | **physical conservation**, but trivially weak (the brain is about 1.5 percent of body mass: the implied ceiling is tens of mM) |
| a drug confined to body water and uniformly distributed cannot reach a concentration above dose over water volume | conservation **given a distribution model** (no sequestration, no active concentration, no binding); the brain's total concentration is then the water concentration times the tissue water fraction |
| "total brain concentration at 45 min is at most F D / V" with V = 0.55 to 0.75 L/kg | **a model-conditional prediction**: it needs (i) V transferred from adult human and dog to the mouse (no mouse V sourced), (ii) V_ss for the central V (a smaller central volume gives a higher early peak), (iii) Kp about 1, (iv) F, (v) negligible lag, (vi) the assay and the unit of the datum |

So the zero-elimination corner is an upper edge of the *model*, and the datum can sit above it if any of (i) to (vi) differs. The distinction that matters: a violated conservation law falsifies the anchor; a violated model ceiling falsifies *the settings*, and the anchor is one of the settings.
