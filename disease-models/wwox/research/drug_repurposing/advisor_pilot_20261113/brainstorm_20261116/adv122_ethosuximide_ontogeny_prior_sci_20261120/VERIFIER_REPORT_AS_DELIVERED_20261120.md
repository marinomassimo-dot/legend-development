## Arithmetic (my own working)
- **A1.** Molecular mass of C7H11NO2: 7×12.011 + 11×1.008 + 14.007 + 2×15.999 = 141.170 g/mol (141.168 with IUPAC 2007 weights).
- **A2.** 72.3 mg/kg ÷ 141.170 = 0.512 mmol/kg. 150 mg/kg ÷ 141.170 = 1.063 mmol/kg.
- **A3.** 134.9 µg/g = 134.9 mg/kg of tissue. At 1 g/mL that is 134.9 mg/L, and 134.9 ÷ 141.170 = 0.956 mM. Multiplied by 3 (the 1:2 w:v homogenate dilution, read as 3-fold total): 2.867 mM.
- **A4.** With no elimination, 0.512 mmol/kg in 0.65 L/kg gives 0.788 mM. The volume at which 0.512 mmol/kg equals A3 (0.956 mM) is 0.536 L/kg; for the ×3 value (2.867 mM) it is 0.179 L/kg.
