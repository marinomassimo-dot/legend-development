## 3. The anchor does not pass a mass-balance check (the finding that moves the result)
Ethosuximide is not protein bound and its CSF concentration equals plasma (label), so a total brain concentration cannot much exceed dose over volume of distribution unless brain concentrates the drug. From the held volumes (0.55 to 0.75 L/kg) the whole 72.3 mg/kg (0.512 mmol/kg) gives at most 0.68 to 0.93 mM with no elimination and a brain-to-plasma ratio of 1. **The measured 0.956 mM (branch A) is at or above that ceiling, although the mouse half-life of about 1 h would remove about 40 percent by 45 min (the bottom-up adult central value is 0.47 mM, so the measurement is 2.0 times it); branch B (2.87 mM) would need a brain-to-plasma ratio of at least 3.1 at zero elimination.** This is inference from labelled data (labels, one half-life row), not a source statement, and a brain-to-plasma ratio above 1 (rat cortex 2.04 at 5 min, abstract), a lower mouse volume, or assay bias would loosen it. Consequences stated as hypotheses: (a) branch B is disfavoured, not excluded; (b) the adult anchor is itself uncertain by a factor of about 2 in the direction of overestimate, so the pup prior is carried by two routes; (c) the sentence "linear scaling gives about 2 to 6 mM at the Cheng dose" in v3.1 3.2 is a scaling of the anchor (0.96 to 2.9 mM times 2.07), **not a measured adult effective band**; the "adult effective band about 2 to 6 mM" of the assignment text has no other source in the repository (searched) and is above the ceiling dose over volume for 150 mg/kg in the mouse (1.06 mmol/kg over 0.55 to 0.75 L/kg is 1.4 to 1.9 mM at zero elimination) unless brain concentrates the drug.

## 5. What the range means for the bridge outcome table (v3.1 3.3, 6.2, 6.5, and IT's asymmetry in its review)
* **Row "null above both potency readings" (exposure above 5 mM with no effect):** not reached at any setting of branch A or of the bottom-up route (outer bound 4.2 mM); reached only in the disfavoured branch B. Under this prior IT's stated asymmetry is the likelier case: **ethosuximide can be confirmed or left unresolved by the exposure series, and a null at an exposure of 0.6 to 5 mM cannot demote it against the 5 mM-class reading.** The prior does not prove that 5 mM cannot be passed (measurement could differ in either direction by the anchor uncertainty).
* **Pragmatic null (v3.1 6.2, measured exposure at the Cheng regimen below the potency anchor):** against the 5 mM anchor it holds at every setting of branch A and of the bottom-up route (so the 5 mM reading is the weaker foundation for any positive reading); against the 0.6 mM anchor it is reachable in principle only through the bottom-up low corner (0.22 mM); the anchor-relative low corner (0.71 mM) stays above it by 1.2 times.
* **Tolerance-limited row (6.5):** reachable in principle (no data-labelled factor excludes it); the prior cannot assess it because no source gives a toxic or tolerated exposure for the pup or any ethosuximide tolerance limit in mouse. The Cheng null mice were given 150 mg/kg and the PTZ test was reported (the age of that test is not printed in the passages I searched; other assays in that paper are at postnatal day 20): a dose already tolerated at that regimen, with an expected exposure of the order of 1 to 2 mM, not several times above the adult exposure at that dose.
* **The weak preference for ethosuximide, resting partly on an absence:** the absence is now a range with an explicit chain; the preference then depends mainly on which potency reading applies, not on whether the exposure is reachable.

## 6. The minimum the exposure step must measure to replace the prior by data
Replace the chain by one measured ratio: **plasma and brain, in the same animals, at three times (an early point about 15 to 20 min, 45 min as in the regimen, and a late point of about 2 h), in null and wild-type littermates at P12, P15 and P17, with an adult comparison at the same regimen and at the anchor's dose** (the regimen as quoted from the source; no dose is proposed). Reasons: the 45 min plasma and brain pair fixes the net factor (volume, absorption, brain-to-plasma ratio) the pup chain multiplies; three times give absorption and elimination rates (half-life, clearance), the two least sourced factors; the adult repeat at both doses decides the anchor's unit and linearity (the factor-3 branch and the factor-2 mass-balance gap, which dominate the width). Report per g of tissue with the homogenisation ratio and the back-correction stated; total concentration is enough for ethosuximide (not protein bound; CSF equals plasma) but a free fraction check in pup plasma is cheap; flush or perfuse and say which; weigh each pup (the null is growth-retarded, so the dose per animal and per kg differ).
