## Conflicts between sources
1. Age effect on level/dose: Battino 1982 (6802548) finds children under 10 y have significantly lower L/D ratios; Smith 1979 (421410) states age did not alter the plasma level to mg/kg relation. Both abstracts, mixed populations and co-medication (primidone, valproate lower L/D in Battino).
2. Adult half-life: 38.3 to 66.6 h (SmPC), 53.7 (Giaccone), 57.1 (Diezi), 48.8 (Bachmann) versus "40-60 h" (UK SmPC 10195) versus "2 to 3 days" (Hvidberg, 797496, 48 to 72 h). Broadly consistent; the children's 30 h (SmPC 10195) versus 25.7 to 41.7 h (SmPC 10767, n=5) versus 30 to 40 h (CAE review held by A).
3. Renal fraction: SmPC 10 to 20% unchanged versus about 23 to 25% from Bauer's printed total and non-renal CLs (my arithmetic, different populations, steady-state versus single dose).
4. Neonatal elimination: half-lives 32 to 41 h (Koup, Kuhnz; AB) versus 80.6 h "average life before totally eliminated" (Tejerizo; not a half-life, different measure).
5. Volume: SmPC 0.7 l/kg, Bachmann 0.63 (the same abstract also prints 0.063, an apparent typo), Giaccone 0.6 to 0.7, Diezi 0.65 to 0.70 (678 ml/kg): consistent for adults; no child or neonate value.
6. Giaccone: inducers raise CL/F about 1.7 fold (15.3 vs 9.2). Smith finds methylphenobarbitone, not the other inducers, altered the L/D; so the enzyme-inducer effect is not uniform across the abstracts.

## Where the data are thinnest (five lines)
