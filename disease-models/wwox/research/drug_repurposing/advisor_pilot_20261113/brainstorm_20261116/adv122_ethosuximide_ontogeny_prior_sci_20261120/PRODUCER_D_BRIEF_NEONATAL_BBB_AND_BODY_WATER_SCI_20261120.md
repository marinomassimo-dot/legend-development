## Items (in this order; stop an item at the first source that answers the stated question, or when two routes are exhausted)
1. **Brain-to-plasma ratios by age**: measured ratios of small polar or unbound probes or antiepileptics (phenobarbital, phenytoin, caffeine, theophylline, valproate, levetiracetam, antipyrine, sucrose or mannitol permeability, urea, sodium or lithium) in immature versus adult rodents, at postnatal days about 7 to 21: value per age, as printed.
2. **BBB maturation**: permeability of the immature rodent BBB to small molecules (the established view and the primary data: Saunders, Ek, Johansson, Ballabh, Engelhardt); transporter ontogeny relevant to a small polar drug (monocarboxylate transporters, organic anion and cation transporters, P-gp, BCRP) at P12 to P17 in mouse and rat as printed; and whether ethosuximide is a substrate of any efflux transporter (a source, or none found).
3. **Body-water and brain-water fractions by age** in the mouse and rat: total body water per body weight, extracellular fluid volume, brain water content at P10 to P20 relative to adult, as printed.
4. **Distribution volume ontogeny**: volume of distribution (L/kg) of water-distributed drugs at P12 to P17 rodents relative to adult, one or two examples.
5. **Wwox null phenotype**: any source that reports blood-brain barrier integrity, brain water, hydration, body weight or hypoglycaemia in Wwox null pups at P10 to P17 (the baseline phenotype of the model is a growth retardation with hypoglycaemia in the null at about P15 to P20); as a statement a source makes, with the age.
