CONFLICTS BETWEEN SOURCES
1. Mouse Cyp3a11 timing: Hart 2009 reports a plateau to day 15 then a second step; Cui 2012 reports peak at 20 to 45 days and a Group-3 (30 to 45 d) placement. Both agree that P12 to P15 sits before the final level; they disagree on whether the later level exceeds the earlier steady level (Hart "another steady level") or is a peak with later decline (Cui).
2. Mouse Cyp2e1: Hart 2009 and the Cui 2012 text say adult level by day 5 or day 0 on; the Cui 2012 male clustering puts Cyp2e1 in a group with highest expression 15 to 20 days. Internal tension in Cui 2012; ratios are not in text.
3. Rat CYP3A: PMC6681801 gives Cyp3a2 4-fold of birth at 7 d and 25-fold at 28 d, while Gifu 2010 gives CYP3A2 male mRNA at PD 4 "almost similar" to 12 weeks and CYP3A1 mRNA higher at PD 4 than adult; 10736428 (AB) gives CYP3A2 flat prepuberty to adult. Different normalisations (fold of birth vs ratio to adult), strains and ages; the direction of CYP3A2 ontogeny is not reconciled by text alone.
4. Rat kidney: the review says rat kidney matures "between birth and weanling"; micropuncture (AB) shows SNGFR still rising without a plateau to 60 d, and GFR lower than adult at 20 to 24 d (AB). Different measures (functional "levels" vs SNGFR/total GFR per body weight).
5. Mouse CYP3A: mRNA peaks at 20 to 45 d (Cui) vs midazolam clearance peak at 28 to 35 d (PMC8349217); the model's age function was built from the mRNA, so this agreement is not independent.
