## 0. Verdict: NARROW (one rule line, no measure nominated)

The O-LM/SST relationship is **mainly an example of a model with free cell numbers and free persistent-sodium strengths reproducing outcomes**, followed by a directionally concordant staining. It is not a worked example of selecting a discriminator, with one exception that is worth keeping apart (section 4, item 1). No out-of-fit readout separates two constrained rival models, because **no composition-only fit and no conductance-only fit was run and scored against the combined model under the same metrics**. The one reversion that is shown is a one-parameter reversion inside the fitted combined model, which HE's Rule 1 already classes as a sensitivity analysis. Of the pre-registered readings: F (fit and confirm) holds in part, P (pooled libraries) holds, K (out-of-fit separation) does not, D (already covered) holds for the reversion half only. INV-15 is not touched: the paper is an SCN8A hippocampal assembloid study with no Wwox system and no Cre-line, subtype or per-animal EEG datum.

## 2. Were composition-only and conductance-only alternatives tested against the combined model, same metrics?

**No (reported).** What exists: the combined fit (cell numbers and Na both free); one reversion of Na to iCtrl values with the mutant composition kept (Fig S3D, one run); and 34 successful combined configurations (S3F). What does not exist in the paper or its supplement: a conductance-only fit (Na free, iCtrl composition kept), a composition-only fit (numbers free, iCtrl Na kept), or the reverse reversion (mutant Na with iCtrl composition). Without those, the statement "normal cellular composition is crucial" (Results) rests on a single one-parameter reversion whose three metrics do not agree. **Inferred:** S3F shows that within a search over cell numbers and persistent Na only, every successful configuration had fewer O-LM cells; that is a necessity result inside a model class with three populations, connectivity held at the iCtrl fit and a search space not described. It does not exclude an alteration of connectivity, K currents or GABA kinetics, and it cannot be read as an exclusion of a conductance-only explanation unless the search is shown to have admitted one.

## 3. Is the confirming staining independent of the model, and what does it measure?

* **Reported:** the IHC and snRNA were done after the model run and agree in sign on three modelled classes: O-LM/SST down, excitatory up, basket/PV not significantly changed. The authors call the model's O-LM loss "validated by IHC and snRNA-seq".
* **Inferred, independence:** the sign of the O-LM change had a literature prior the authors cite (O-LM or SST loss in other models); the magnitudes do not match (model O-LM about -43 percent, pyramidal about +7 percent; staining SST fraction down by roughly 70 to 90 percent and CAMKII-alpha up by roughly 50 to 75 percent in relative terms (v)); and the model was fitted with cell numbers free to the P1 line. Agreement in sign on three classes, with a free magnitude and a prior on one of them, is concordance, not a test that could have failed.
* **What the measurements DIRECTLY measure:** IHC = a marker-defined fraction of counted nuclei (SST+GAD65+, PV+GAD65+, CAMKII-alpha+); snRNA = cluster membership by transcript markers in one pool; neither is the cell count of an O-LM class (O-LM is an anatomical class, the assembloids carry no oriens or lacunosum-moleculare layers, and the authors call the SST cells "putative O-LM"), and neither is lineage identity (no lineage tracing; the tdTomato experiment labels GE-derived cells at 2 weeks, not SST fate). The marker fraction and the model's relative cell count are different units.
* **Regional contrast, not modelled:** the cortical assembloids show the same CAMKII-alpha increase with no change in GAD65 or PV (Fig 6C, D) and a different phenotype (bursts, synchronised calcium events). That contrast is the closest thing in the paper to an observation outside the model, but the model is a CA3 circuit only and no cortical coupling metric is reported, so it separates nothing.

## 4. Fig 7 and the unit of independence

Reported: three differentiations were pooled into one tube per condition and one library was sequenced per condition (P1 only; day 120 Cx+GE and Hc+GE, day 84 Hc+GE). Differences in cluster proportions are therefore differences between two pools, with no between-differentiation variance and no test printed; the differential expression is run cell by cell (Scanpy Wilcoxon, as the Methods name it), so its p values count cells, not differentiations. **Inferred:** snRNA is a descriptive single-pool snapshot of one line; the replicated element of the genotype conclusion is the IHC (n in differentiations, P1 and P2, with the P2 control caveat), and the Reelin IHC (n = 4 to 5) is the only replicated assay on the population that the snRNA singles out. The snRNA and the IHC are two assays of one preparation, not two independent confirmations of the model.

Item 1 worth keeping apart: the ensemble in S3F is a sound device (enumerate many fits that reproduce the target and report what is invariant). Here it constrains one of three class changes, and it is bounded by what the search was allowed to vary. That is the part of the paper that points towards selecting a discriminator; it is not what the staining then "confirmed".

## 5. Comparison with the RP-045 rule and Rule 1 (done before adding a line)

* **Already covered:** (a) "a one-parameter reversion inside the fitted model" = Rule 1's last sentence in HE's ADV-153 section 1 ("two that were never re-fitted are a sensitivity analysis"); a rival needs its own re-fit with the free parameter declared. (b) Provenance at the study level (the held fit and later data from one laboratory count as one cluster) is in the RP-045 next_action text and in HE's held-out unit. (c) The READOUT TYPE / CLAIM TYPE line already names identity versus rate and bulk versus per-class.
* **Not carried:** the role of a *confirming* measurement relative to what set or fitted the model. Rule 1 asks how a rival is fitted; it does not say what a later agreeing measurement is worth if the measured quantity could have been an input, a fit target or a free variable, or whether it is a marker proxy of a modelled class. Trigger (ii) of the pilot (per-animal EEG with cell identity) is exactly such a measurement.
