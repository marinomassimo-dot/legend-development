## Readings fixed in advance (probabilities are my priors, not data)
* **C, concordance only (0.55).** Several outputs normalise under omaveloxolone alone; no manipulation of the downstream branch (ferroptosis inhibitor, iron chelator, GPX4 inhibitor or knockdown) and no NFE2L2 dependency arm separates the routes. Then the paper is a model of what one intervention does to many readouts, which cannot identify a causal path. Outcome: BOUNDED STOP, INV-08 X1 unchanged.
* **D, a separating arm exists but outside the shared culture (0.25).** A downstream or dependency arm exists in the paper but viability and peroxidation are not measured in the same culture, or n is technical replicates, or the arm is in fibroblasts only. Outcome: NARROW; at most a pointer; no new arm.
* **G, a genuine gap (0.12).** A selective downstream manipulation (or an NFE2L2/GPX4 dependency test) is run in the same FXN-depleted DRG culture as the viability readout with independent cultures as the unit, and it dissociates survival, NRF2 targets and peroxidation. Outcome: ONE source-linked addition to INV-08 X1 (downstream-rescue arm as mechanistic comparator) with a competing explanation and a rejecting observation.
* **K, matrix already covers it (0.08).** O3 plus the existing risk panel already separate what the paper adds. Outcome: BOUNDED STOP, say so.

## Checks asked for, and how each will be counted
(a) same context and culture for viability and peroxidation: yes only if both are in the same panel or the methods state the same plates; (b) separating branches: an arm counts only if it manipulates ONE branch independently of omaveloxolone; honokiol is a second compound, so the combination does not separate branches; (c) unit and n per panel: biological replicate means independent cultures or independent lines, not wells, fields or technical repeats; (d) authors versus LEGEND inference: an FXN-depleted DRG neuron or patient fibroblast model carries NO WWOX efficacy inference and none will be written.

## Decision rule
GAP only if (i) a selective downstream or dependency manipulation exists, (ii) it is in the same culture as the viability readout, (iii) independent cultures are the unit with a stated n, and (iv) the result dissociates at least two of survival, NRF2 targets and peroxidation. Anything less is STOP or NARROW. Ordering before another NRF2 chemotype needs held WWOX evidence of a ferroptosis-branch lesion in a WWOX-deficient neural cell; W07 (cancer cells, abstract, opposite direction to W01 and W02) is not that, so I expect not to order it, whatever the paper shows. Not reached will be stated (supplement, figure images, version issues).
