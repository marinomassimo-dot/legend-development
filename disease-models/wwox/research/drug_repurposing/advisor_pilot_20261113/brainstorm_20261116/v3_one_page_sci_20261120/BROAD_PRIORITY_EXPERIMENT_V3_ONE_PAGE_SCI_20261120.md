## 2. The experiment
**Line and ages.** Wwox neuronal-deletion pups with wild-type littermates; litter is the block; sex recorded. Dosing once daily **P3 to P16**, read at **P17** (the age of the held deficit). Route: clemastine oral gavage (the source dosed P3 to P10: P16 is a declared extension); thyromimetic subcutaneous (the Triac source P1 to P20: P3 to P16 declared). The comparison is "under these windows and routes", not route-independent; no late-start arm.

**Groups and n.** Knockout: vehicle 12; clemastine at 2 and at 7.5 mg/kg/day, 12 each; one CNS-penetrant thyromimetic chosen by the laboratory (Triac or Sob-AM2), 12. Wild-type: vehicle 8; clemastine high dose 8; thyromimetic 8 (the harm check). Satellite pups for exposure (4 per time point, two time points, two agents = 16) and a few pups per litter for ex vivo OPC cultures. About 100 pups for **Stage 1**; litters needed = those that yield 12 knockouts per arm. **Detectable effect:** for 80 percent power, two-sided 0.05, about 10 animals per arm detect rescue of half the deficit on the mature score (about 16 on the stage score); a quarter-deficit rescue needs about 34 (56) and is **not reachable: declared not detectable, never a negative**. The GSK-3 panel (12 knockout against 8 wild-type vehicle) detects about 1.3 standard deviations; 0.5 standard deviations needs about 64 per genotype.

**Tissue allocation (the pups serve both candidates only with this written down).** The panel needs snap-frozen tissue from animals handled identically; the lineage sections need fixed tissue; electron microscopy needs well-preserved fixed ultrastructure; the callosal compound action potential needs living slices. **One hemisphere cannot serve all four.** Therefore:
| Track | Animals | Handling | Serves | Forbids |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 (Stage 1, every arm)** | the n above | fixed interval from decapitation to freezing (seconds, set before data), no anaesthesia, or the same anaesthesia with core temperature monitored; brain split at the midline; **left or right hemisphere assigned to freezing by a list fixed in advance and balanced within genotype, arm and litter**; frozen side: cortex and hippocampus dissected on a cold plate, aliquots for the panel and nuclei; other side immersion-fixed | GSK-3 panel; nuclei lineage readout; sections (PDGFRA, committed-precursor, newly formed, mature markers; MBP); body weight | perfusion fixation (it would fix the frozen side); electron microscopy at publication quality; slice electrophysiology; any longer survival |
| **2 (Stage 2, conditional)** | the arms that moved on Track 1, vehicle and wild type | transcardial perfusion with the EM fixative | electron-microscopy g-ratio by axon calibre | biochemistry on the same animal |
| **3 (Stage 2, conditional)** | same arms | acute coronal slices | callosal compound action potential (the harm readout); the recorded slices may be fixed afterwards | frozen-tissue biochemistry from the same hemisphere |
| **4 (Stage 2, conditional)** | a longitudinal cohort of the same arms | no terminal procedure at P17 | body weight daily, survival or time to the humane endpoint (only if the line survives past P20), motor and rotarod at the earliest testable age, blood glucose at P14 and P17 | any P17 terminal readout |
Reason for staging: counts can rise without functional myelin (the conduction-harm pattern), so a Track 1 positive is **provisional until Tracks 2 and 3 are read**, and a Track 1 negative at confirmed exposure saves the cost of Tracks 2 to 4. The price: Stage 2 adds animals and the conduction harm is not visible at Stage 1. The alternative (all tracks at once) needs about four handling groups per arm and a much larger knockout supply (a heterozygote cross gives about one knockout in four, and a litter about 7 pups); it is the laboratory's choice, stated before data.

**Engagement controls (a null is read only if they pass).** (i) Terminal brain concentration of each agent in satellite pups at plasma Tmax and 24 h after the last dose, with plasma in the same pups, total with the tissue basis stated. (ii) Ex vivo OPC differentiation (MBP and CC1 fractions) in primary OPCs from knockout and control littermates, drug at the in vitro range of the source (10 to 500 nM for clemastine). (iii) Clemastine in vivo response control: the OPC-proportion shift in treated wild-type littermates (CC1-negative among SOX10-positive); a clemastine arm without it at the confirmed exposure is a failed engagement. (iv) Thyromimetic engagement: serum T3, T4 and TSH and a liver thyroid-responsive transcript (Dio1 or Thrsp); in the cortex a thyroid-response transcript **not enriched in the oligodendrocyte lineage** chosen from a cell-type atlas before data (Klf9 is a candidate from memory); Hairless is dropped. (v) The GSK-3 panel's own assay controls: lambda-phosphatase aliquot, linear-range dilution, pooled reference lysate on every blot; the observed wild-type level reported with the panel.

**Readouts.** Tier 1: stage-resolved lineage per animal (nuclei with at least 4 animals per arm as a cell-level secondary; section counts per animal as primary), MBP, g-ratio, callosal CAP. Tier 2 as Track 4. **GSK-3 panel (primary): tau pS396 and pS404 over total tau**, cortex; pattern readouts: GSK3-beta Ser9 over **total GSK3-beta** (not over actin), Tyr216 over total GSK-3, beta-catenin. Analysis fixed before data: animal as unit, litter as block, Holm over the two primary tau ratios, equivalence margin 0.80 to 1.25 for "flat"; if the interval is wider than the margin the outcome is "unresolved", never "closed".

## 4. What the experiment cannot show
Directness of the WWOX-GSK3-beta binding; any effect of Q230P on binding; a benefit of a GSK-3 inhibitor (no inhibitor arm is opened by Stage 1); which cell type carries a bulk substrate change; efficacy against the gene-addition benchmark's survival or glucose endpoints from a myelin agent alone; anything about people; a small effect (the panel and the lineage readout are large-effect detectors). The parametric-sensitivity result of the simulator (ADV-096, HE) is a caveat only and is not an input. No dose, schedule or use in any person is proposed.
