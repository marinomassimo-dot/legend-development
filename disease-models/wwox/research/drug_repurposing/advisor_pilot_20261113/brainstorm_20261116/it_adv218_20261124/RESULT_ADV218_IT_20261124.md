## 3. What the abstract-level class could not see (counter-check, with anchors)

The 2,846 modulated genes come from an uncorrected t-test at p<0.05 over about 29,000 genes (about 1,450 expected by chance at that threshold).
* "Increased apoptosis" and "increased ability for crossing the basement membrane" are both **p>0.05** in the Results (29 percent more invasive cells, not significant).

## 4. gnomAD (42991897) and the registry statements about the reference genotype class and penetrance
* **Do allele frequencies bear on any registry statement?** **No registry sentence quotes a population frequency for c.2T>C, p.Glu171Ter, c.172+1G>C or p.Arg54Ter** (`git grep` of the four alleles and of "gnomAD" under `disease-models/wwox/registries` and `analysis` on 2026-11-24: the alleles appear as ClinVar-export rows, as genotype labels of patient records, and the gnomAD word only in other alleles' papers). In the registries and analyses I searched, no sentence derives penetrance or genotype class from these frequencies, and **this paper offers no penetrance estimate**: it uses frequency only as a filter ("variants common in reference populations cannot cause fully penetrant, severe early-onset disease") and quantifies frequencies "rather than claims their discovery".
* **What the frequencies do say (hypothesis level, no individual-level information):** four WWOX loss-of-function alleles (a start-loss, a nonsense, a donor-splice, a second nonsense) occur as **heterozygous alleles in reference populations** at about 10^-5 to 10^-4 in East Asians (c.2T>C about 9.8 x 10^-5), which is carrier-frequency context for a **recessive** gene; the workbook lists WWOX as autosomal recessive with 193 ClinVar P/LP records, pLI 0, missense Z -4.44 and LOEUF 1.532 (Supplementary Table S1, constraint values as reproduced by the authors, not re-queried), i.e. a gene that tolerates heterozygous loss of function in the general population. That bears on the two cancer papers' intros, which describe WWOX as a haploinsufficient tumour suppressor: the statement concerns somatic loss of one allele in a tumour, not germline carriers, and the two readings do not conflict, but the intros should not be quoted as a statement about carriers.
* **Limits:** East Asian denominator about 45,000 alleles (c.2T>C only 40,686), detection floor about 4.5 x 10^-5, exploratory counts of two or three are artefact-sensitive, **no homozygote counts are reported** (so nothing about penetrance of the biallelic state), SNVs only.
* **Consumer:** a variant-spectrum and carrier-frequency note only; no row, no claim, no ranking change.
