## Block A01-13 (verdict: ADD, plus MUST CHANGE on the closing sentence)

- **Arithmetic.** The two figures reproduce:
  - 44.1%: Table 1 group means at week 12, TSA 749.41 against sham 1341.01.
  - 42.9%: recomputed from the individual animal values in Table 1. TSA rows 672.1, 758.8 and 866.9 give a mean of 765.9. Sham rows 1335.8 and 1346.2 give 1341.0.
  - So the paper's printed TSA mean (749.41) does not equal the mean of its own three rows (765.9). Other TSA means are also off: week 8 printed 1039.39 against rows 1026.7, and week 4 printed 1198.11 against rows 1201.5. The wild-type week-12 printed 250.27 against rows 257.1.
  - The fragment does not say what the two comparisons are, so a reader cannot interpret "42.9 and 44.1".
- **Three times wild type.** 749.41 / 250.27 = 2.99, and 765.9 / 257.1 = 2.98. Supported.
- **Sham n.** Table 1 and the Fig 3 blots list two sham animals and three treated. Methods ("three mice per group") and Limitations ("only three mice per group") say three per group. "Two sham animals against three treated" is true of the tabulated data, but the source contradicts itself.
- **Stats.** The week-12 comparison is p < 0.01 (Dunnett).
- **Text inconsistency.** The text gives the sham week-12 mean as 1341.59, the table says 1341.01, and the text gives week 8 as 1338.59. These are minor.
- **Enzyme protein.** Fig 3C, PAH/GAPDH: sham about 0.56, treated about 0.83, wild type about 0.90. Supported ("toward wild type"). Fig 3B, acetyl-HSP90/HSP90: sham about 0.55, treated about 0.88, wild type about 0.68, so treated is above wild type. Both comparisons carry ****. The uncropped Supp Fig S1 shows three blots, each with the same lane layout (2 sham, 3 treated, 3 wild type). The unit of the test behind ****P<0.0001 on 2 versus 3 animals is not stated (reader inference, UNSETTLED).
- **Higher dose.** The source says 100 mg/kg (twice the 50 mg/kg dose) caused abdominal oedema and death within eight weeks, with kidney enlargement and ascites at necropsy. The group was excluded from analysis, and its size is not given. "Exposure level" should read "dose": no exposure was measured.
- **No turnover measurement.** Supported. The whole text, supplement and PDF contain only steady-state westerns (PAH, HSP90, acetyl-HSP90), with no cycloheximide, pulse-chase or ubiquitination data. The degradation reading is the authors' inference.
- **Closing sentence: MUST CHANGE.** "Shows that a cytosolic mutant enzyme can respond to an HDAC6 lever, which butyrate does not engage."
  - "Shows" over-reaches for one small study of two sham and three treated animals, with male mice aged 18 to 28 weeks, one dose and no HDAC6-independence control.
  - "Cytosolic" is not stated in the source.
  - The paper never mentions butyrate, so "which butyrate does not engage" is not supported by this source. UNSETTLED here, and OUTSIDE this packet.
  - The mutant is Pahenu2 F263S.

Minimal replacement for the fragment (ADD):
> plasma phenylalanine fell about 43 to 44 percent from sham at week 12 (44.1 from the printed group means, 749.41 against 1341.01; 42.9 when the mean of the three tabulated treated values, 765.9, is used against the two tabulated sham values)

Further words to add: "Table 1 lists two sham animals though Methods state three per group", and "dose" for "exposure level".

Replacement for the closing sentence:
> Suggests, in one small study (Pahenu2 F263S mice, two tabulated sham against three treated animals), that a mutant liver enzyme can respond to an HDAC6 inhibitor; this paper does not test whether butyrate engages HDAC6.
