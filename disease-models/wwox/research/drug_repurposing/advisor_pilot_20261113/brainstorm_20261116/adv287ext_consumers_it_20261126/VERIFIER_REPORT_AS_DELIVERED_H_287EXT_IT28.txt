BLOCK H02-03
New fragment: "had the finding and all 12 unexposed children were normal; 14 further children were imaged before, during and after exposure and 4 of them were abnormal (29 of 95 exposed, 30.5 percent, derived);"

- Group sizes, from the abstract:
  - 107 pediatric patients with infantile spasms.
  - 95 treated and 12 not treated.
  - 81 scanned during but not before treatment, of whom 25 (30.9%) were abnormal.
  - 14 scanned before, during and after treatment, of whom four were abnormal during treatment.
  - "Patients who had not received VGB treatment (n = 12) displayed normal imaging."
- Arithmetic: 81 + 14 = 95, matching the treated total. 25 + 4 = 29. 29/95 = 30.53 percent, so 30.5 is right and "derived" is the right label.
- "Children": the cohort is pediatric infantile-spasms patients. Younger infants (12 months or younger) and cryptogenic infantile spasms were more likely to develop abnormal signal, matching the sentence's tail.
- Optional note, not required: the pooled 29 of 95 merges two imaging schedules (81 imaged during but not before treatment, 14 imaged before, during and after). Calling it "derived" is enough.

- 8 of 80 against 30 of 92: STANDS. Table 1 (VABAM row 8 vs 30; IST n = 80, ISO n = 92) and abstract (10% vs 32.6%, p < 0.001).

- Already in the context, STANDS: 38 of 172 (22.1%).
- Optional ADD on the surrounding clause "143 exposed children without an eligible examination were excluded".
  - Results: 533 screened, 390 with MRI before VGB and during follow-up. 533 − 390 = 143, which are the children with no such MRI (the limitations paragraph says the same).
  - A further 218 children who did have MRI were excluded: 18 with exposure under 30 days, 193 with exposure over 1 year at MRI review, and 7 without MRI before rapamycin initiation. 390 − 218 = 172.
  - A reader seeing only the card could think the only exclusions were the 143.
  - Minimal replacement: "143 exposed children without a qualifying MRI, and a further 218 imaged children (exposure under 30 days n = 18, over 1 year n = 193, no pre-rapamycin MRI n = 7), were excluded."
  - I did not view Figure 1 (the flow chart); the numbers come from the Results text.

The paper itself flags the rapamycin finding as retrospective, confounded and unconfirmed: the rapamycin group had longer VGB exposure (279 vs 205 days, p = 0.004), and TSC patients may have baseline MRI abnormalities that obscure VABAM. The multivariate result for rapamycin is OR 0.152, 95% CI 0.028 to 0.715, p = 0.019. The card defers the rapamycin association to DR-H02-013, which is OUTSIDE my check.
