BLOCK R-34 (Lukas 2020, PMC7037350 XML read in full)
Opened: PMID32023956_Lukas2020_PMC7037350.xml.
- "12.9 percent (23 of 178)": Results 2.3 "inconsistent for 12.9% (23/178)". STANDS.
- "validated GLP assay": the GLP assay is the clinical-phase HEK assay of the cited reference [14]. STANDS.
- Table 1 gives the assay differences:
  - Cell line: GripTite HEK293 MSR versus HEK293H.
  - Incubation: 120 h versus 60 h.
  - Chaperone concentration: 10 µM versus 20 µM.
  - Criteria: ≥3% absolute AND 1.2-fold, versus ≥5% absolute OR 1.5-fold plus a 5% minimum.
  - Not mentioned in the fragment: plate format, transfection reagent, plasmid, lysis, and replicates (n=5 quadruplicate versus n≥3 duplicate). The list is not exhaustive, but it does not mislead.
- "In-house HEK assay" and "similar": the authors write "very similar". STANDS.
- "no clinical correlation is established": the source sentence is "To date, there is no established correlation between the biochemical enzyme activity increase induced by DGJ and the clinical benefit". The same Discussion also reports patient cohorts. 14 of 14 patients matched the GLP classification, and some discordant cases showed biomarker responses (G144V and G325R failed the PBMC biochemical response but responded on biomarker; S276G/S276N/L294S were non-responders despite an amenable call).
- ADD, minimal wording: replace "no clinical correlation is established" with "no established correlation between the in-vitro activity increase and clinical benefit (the paper cites a few clinical cases, some concordant and some not)".
- Also true and unmentioned: applying the same criteria to both datasets did not reduce discordance (18.0%, 32/178, with the GLP definition applied to the in-house data). A single 7% absolute cut-off gave 9.0%.

BLOCK R-11 (Yadav 2022 ADV272, EPMC XML in full)
- 52 recruited (30 intervention and 22 comparator), 48 analysed (26 and 22). The abstract states "per-protocol analysis". The loss was 2 lost to follow-up and 2 excluded for side effects, all in the intervention arm. Open-label, with a computer-generated random number table. The comparator was a syrup with calcium and vitamin D, without magnesium. STANDS.
- Seizure control p=0.69 (text 0.693) and development p=0.09. STANDS.
- EEG p: Results and Table 2 say 0.04. The Discussion says "EEG normalization (p=0.003)". STANDS as an internal inconsistency.
- I recomputed Table 2 EEG (11/2 vs 6/7): uncorrected chi-square 4.25, p=0.0393. Fisher p=0.0968. Yates chi-square 2.72, p=0.0992. Expected counts 8.5, 4.5, 8.5 and 4.5, so two of four are below 5. 0.003 does not reproduce from Table 2. STANDS.
- ADD 1: the value 0.003 occurs elsewhere in the paper as a different comparison. The Abstract and Results use it for "clinical response better with normal initial serum magnesium, intervention group" (Table 3), and the Discussion uses it for both that and EEG. So the EEG 0.003 may be a carry-over, which is a hypothesis. Table 3's own counts (intervention: 4/2/3 versus 2/3/13) give chi-square p=0.099 (exact 0.123), not 0.003. The non-intervention rows (4/4/0 versus 3/2/8) give chi-square p=0.018 (exact 0.014), against the printed 0.42. The pooled Table 4 (5/5/21 versus 8/6/3) gives chi-square p=0.0038 (printed 0.004; exact 0.0030).
- ADD 2: the Table 2 EEG rows sum to 13 + 13 (n=26, labelled "initial abnormal EEG"), whereas the "Abnormal 22 22" row suggests 22 per arm had abnormal EEG at baseline. The EEG p therefore rests on 26 of 48 children, with the denominator unexplained.
- "No post-treatment serum magnesium is reported ... although the Discussion says supplementation raised it": the Discussion does say "magnesium supplementation increases serum magnesium levels", and only initial serum magnesium is reported (mean 2.07±0.5 mg/dl; intervention 2.10, comparator 2.04). STANDS.
- Suggested replacement wording: "EEG improvement p 0.04 in the Results and p 0.003 in the Discussion (internal inconsistency; 0.003 does not reproduce from Table 2 and is the value the paper gives for initial serum magnesium versus response in Table 3; EEG rests on 13 + 13 children)".
