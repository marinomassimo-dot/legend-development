## Deviations from the frozen sheet, stated before the run (DECISIONS_TAKEN)
* **One context for all twelve pairs, not one per pair** (sheet section 4). Reason: ADV-094's perimeter allows two delegates at most. Cost of the deviation: B can score by contrast across pairs and can notice shared structure (three pairs concern one disease). Mitigation: fixed rubric, shuffled order, one sentence naming the kind of evidence per component. The result is reported with this limit beside it.
* **The recognition probe is put to A, not to a separate fresh call.** A has seen the twelve rows together while rewriting, so recognition may be helped by contrast; a recognition rate is therefore an upper bound of single-row recognition. Reported with that limit.
* **B scores with A's rewritten rubric and the original pair rows**, not the original rubric; the original sheet stays the frozen text. If A's rewrite changes the meaning of a component, the rewrite and the original are both kept and the difference is reported.
* The dependence among the three SCN1A-class pairs and the shared drug class is reported from the KEY only after the values are saved; it is not shown to any delegate.
