# Three candidate research batches for the MEETING, ranked by decision value over cost (IT; each: question, unblocked path, sources, unit of work, decision it changes; no dose, no recommendation; mechanism level)
**1. Group-level exposure recount (IT56-Q06).** Question: do the grouped literature tables already held (Al-Baradie 2022 Table 3, 61 patients from 19 studies; Oliver 2023 Table 3, 62 published cases) state AED exposure and response per study, in particular GABA-raising agents in WWOX children? Path: held data, zero new source, zero spend. Unit: two tables, one delegate or IT alone, about 1 to 2 hours and under 60k tokens. Decision: whether I14 (ambient GABA and emergent seizure types) and the first-question 'which drug fits WWOX' can be partly answered at group level, or only by the 27 PDFs (IT56-Q03).
**2. Contrast-set pilot (generative-engine test).** Question: for three evaluated hypotheses (I13 tonic deficit, I1 rebound oscillator, I15 threshold endpoint) does a contrast set of two or three competing explanations, with each existing result assigned to the alternative it supports, give a more informative conclusion than SUPPORT/REFUTE? Path: repository only, no literature. Unit: three hypotheses x two or three explanations, one reader with a blind second reader; measures the engine (does the contrast set change which experiment is chosen first), not the biology. Cost about 2 hours, 100k tokens. Decision: whether U1 becomes a contrast-set structure and which slice arm leads the pilot; theme 2 of the meeting.
**3. Primary pharmacology under one assay (WO-12 and WO-25).** Question: the GluN2B/GluN2A ratios of ifenprodil and Ro 25-6981 under one set of glycine, Mg2+ and holding conditions (WO-12), and the Cav3.1/3.2/3.3 potencies and hERG numbers of efonidipine and flunarizine in one assay (WO-25). Path: literature, open-access first; the oldest primary papers may be closed or behind challenge pages (feasibility unknown until tried). Unit: about 6 to 8 neutral full-text dossiers with locators, one delegate (sonnet/high), estimate 20k to 45k total tokens per fully read paper (measured today: 44,598 per fully read paper including the blocked ones), so 150k to 350k. Decision: the NAM efficacy e in I7's critical-fraction model and the order of the T-type pair in INV-03/04; whether the early-window NMDAR arm of the arm table strengthens or weakens.
Not proposed first: the 27 closed/blocked PDFs (a human visit, not a batch), missense functional evidence per variant (SCI's I20 literature step is already running), WO-06 and RS-R6 (no decision this week).
