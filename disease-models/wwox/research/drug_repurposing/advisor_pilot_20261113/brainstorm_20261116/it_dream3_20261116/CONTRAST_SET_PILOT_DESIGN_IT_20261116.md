# Contrast-set pilot: design and the status of the source method (IT; non-canonical; hypotheses; mechanism level; not medical advice)
**Source method, read first (ADV's request: licence and code).** 'Contrastive Discovery: Open-Ended Scientific Discovery over Competing Explanations' (Liu, Kim, Dai, Sun; ICML 2026 virtual page https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/67941; aggregator summaries https://deeplearn.org/arxiv/868818/contrastive-discovery:-open-ended-scientific-discovery-over-competing-explanations and https://chatpaper.com/paper/375049). According to the web search of 2026-11-16 (abstract/aggregator level only; the full paper was not read): a contrast set pairs a question with mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive explanations; each experiment is scored by how its evidence redistributes belief across the set; reported across six domains: 73% of the highest-belief-shift rejected candidates resolved to a specific alternative; 67% more questions resolved than pointwise surprisal at equal budget; 78% agreement of 38 blinded experts on resolved questions. LICENCE and CODE, corrected by SCI's reading (message of 23:05Z; SCI read the arXiv 2611.14514 abstract page and HTML full text through the References; appendices only as a model-extracted summary): paper licence CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; no code, data or fixtures released that SCI could find (project pages named, not fetched); the paper's 'fixtures' are controls described in prose (reversing the sign of the evidence reversed resolutions on all 14 claims; correct alternative absent: wrong resolution on 53.4 percent, falling to 0.6 percent with an abstention alternative; unrelated evidence: appropriate-analysis 73 to 7 percent); '+67 percent' = 161.9 versus 97.2 resolved questions at K = 200 validations on six existing tabular datasets with one gpt-4o agent and LLM-elicited beliefs (the paper's own limitation); the labels in the operator scout report (A falsified/B supported, both compatible, reorders) are not the paper's wording. So there is no code to audit and no licence issue for using the pattern; our retrospective benchmark would be our own fixtures written from the paper's five control types, labelled as ours. Status: CLAIMED IN PAPER; the pilot imports nothing.
**Design (as stated at the meeting).** Question: for I13 (tonic deficit), I1 (rebound oscillator) and I15 (threshold endpoint), does replacing SUPPORT/REFUTE by a contrast set change which experiment the engine chooses first? (1) A blind delegate (sonnet/high; one active; queued behind HE's two delegates) receives only the held observations (the 0/11, 4/23, 36/42 incidence; sIPSC 57.3 v 27.5 pA with cell counts; intrinsic profile S-HT = S-KO; the K2P/Kir/Kv7 PY scan; the GAT result; the tonic-current sizes) and, for each of the three, builds a contrast set of two or three mutually exclusive explanations of the SAME held observation. (2) A second blind reader assigns each existing result to the alternative(s) it supports or leaves untouched (cells: result x alternative: supports / opposes / silent). (3) The engine's output per hypothesis: residual alternatives after the held results and the first experiment that best separates them. The engine must beat the day's actual SUPPORT/REFUTE path in (a) the number of residual alternatives after the held results, (b) the first experiment chosen, (c) agreement of the two readers on the cells (threshold fixed before the second reader starts: at least 80 percent of cells; with 3 hypotheses nothing is claimed: the output is cost and disagreement). (4) Shared check with HE's retrodiction: would the contrast set have put the phasic deficit first before SCI's rivals scan (SCI's order of results is in RIVALS_RESULT_SCI.md; the retrodiction cut is HE's). Briefing line required in every batch (ADV item 6): Sibyl's three failure-mode questions, to be pasted when HE sends them.
**Cost estimate (before launch):** about 60k to 120k total tokens for the builder and 40k to 80k for the second reader. Zero literature; zero spend.
