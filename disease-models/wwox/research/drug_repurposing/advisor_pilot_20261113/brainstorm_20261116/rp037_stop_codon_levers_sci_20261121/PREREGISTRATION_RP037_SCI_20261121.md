## Part (b): engineered suppressor tRNA as a stop-codon lever class
**Question.** Is there an in vivo brain readout (protein restoration or a functional or phenotype readout) after delivery of an engineered suppressor tRNA, in a developmental or juvenile model, with age, n, vector, route and exposure stated?
**Discriminating observation, fixed before reading:** a primary reporting such a readout.
* **ENTERS as a class** (with its exposure facts, harms and the codon classes it covers): in vivo, brain (or CNS), neonatal or juvenile animal, protein restoration or a functional or phenotype readout, with n and the vector or lipid route stated, and a control for off-target read-through of normal stop codons stated or its absence named.
* **CONDITIONAL:** in vivo but not brain (liver, lung, muscle), or brain only in adults, or neuron cultures or organoids only; carried as a named gap with the cheapest bridging observation.
* **DOES NOT ENTER:** reporter-level or cell-line evidence only, or in vivo without any protein or functional readout. Then the lever is not entered, and the work goes straight to the ErbB4/NRG1 strand without waiting (ADV's instruction).
* **Allele-matching rule (from the held table, no new calculation of efficacy):** a suppressor tRNA is specific to one stop codon and, in practice, to the amino acid it inserts; the table gives the codon class per allele (UAA, UAG, UGA counts are IT4 E1's) and whether the inserted amino acid restores the wild-type residue. A class "enters" only for the alleles whose stop codon and inserted residue the primary actually shows; the others are listed as not covered by that primary.
* **Rivals declared in advance:** (i) ataluren and aminoglycosides as the established read-through class (IT4 E1's negative CDKL5/Dravet crossover is carried, not re-argued); (ii) class-agnostic levers for class E (circuit levers, gene addition) which need no allele premise; a suppressor tRNA would have to beat them on a stated criterion, not on novelty; (iii) NMD: a suppressor tRNA acts on translation of a transcript that exists, so for NMD-sensitive alleles (23 of 32 per IT4 E1, prediction only) it is limited by template unless the primary shows otherwise.
* **Marker versus function, ages, denominators and significance** are written into every row at the first pass (ADV-123 verifier corrections): a protein band or reporter signal is a marker; a seizure, survival or behaviour readout is function.
* **Sources and perimeter:** Europe PMC and PubMed plain clients; held files before retrieval; complete reads with locators and `FULLTEXT_READ_RECEIPT` for the numbers used; one delegate at a time (producer sonnet effort medium, one blind verifier sonnet effort high, effort stated in every dispatch and read back); no operator e-mail to any service; no individual-level data.
