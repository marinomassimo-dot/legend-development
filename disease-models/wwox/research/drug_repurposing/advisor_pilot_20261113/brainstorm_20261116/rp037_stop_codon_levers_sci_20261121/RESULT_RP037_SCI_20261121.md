## Answer first
1. **(a) None of IT4 E1's three reopening conditions is met** (searches below). RP-015's nonsense half has **no new datum under those conditions**; but see 2, which is why it is not simply closed.
2. **(b) The pre-registered rule is met by one primary, so engineered suppressor tRNA ENTERS as a conditional class-E lever class, in a bounded form.** The one primary that reaches the rule (Pierce et al., Nature 2025): neonatal (P0) intracerebroventricular dosing of a prime editor that installs a suppressor tRNA in the genome of an Idua W392X mouse; in the cortex the editing frequency was 6.3 percent and IDUA enzyme activity 6.3 percent of wild type at 7 weeks, n = 3 mice, a one-sided Welch's t-test, with storage vacuolisation in cerebellar Purkinje cells and thalamic neurons resolved. Everything that qualifies it is in the table below; the strongest qualifiers are that it is one study, enzyme activity and histology are markers with no brain function readout, the dose for the Idua mice is not stated, the endogenous-allele sup-tRNA inserts leucine (not the wild-type residue), and a correction notice on the pathology report exists. The reporter-level brain paper (Nucleic Acids Research 2026) alone would have given "does not enter".
3. **Allele coverage is narrow and sits on the wrong side of the NMD line** (position level, from the held table): a stop-codon and wild-type-residue match to a sup-tRNA class shown in a primary exists for **5 of 32 alleles, all five predicted NMD-sensitive**: Arg at UGA (p.Arg54*, p.Arg264*; the arginine-UGA sup-tRNA is the best-studied class) and Tyr at UAG (p.Tyr61*, p.Tyr85*, p.Tyr107*; the Tyr sup-tRNA of Wang 2022). **None of the 8 predicted NMD-escape alleles (exon 9) nor the borderline Trp335* has a residue match in the primaries read** (their wild-type residues are Gln, Gly, Glu and Trp). Where the template is predicted to be missing, one adult liver and heart study measured that the sup-tRNA raised the nonsense Idua mRNA 4.6-fold and 10.2-fold to the wild-type level (Wang 2022), which is the only NMD-relevant measurement among the primaries, in non-brain tissues.
4. **(c) The eight exon-9 alleles keep all annotated domains and between 79 and 97 percent of the 125-to-414 region, and lose 9 to 62 C-terminal residues**; by boundaries alone that says nothing about function.
5. **What changes in the comparison:** the class-E row of the lever tables moves from "no lever, no assay" to a conditional class with the exposure facts below and a named missing WWOX measure; nothing changes for the reference genotype (it carries no premature stop; IT4 E1's caveat about an unmeasured intron-8 retention outcome stands). RP-015's nonsense half is therefore **narrowed, not closed**: conditions 1 to 3 unmet; the missing datum becomes a WWOX-specific one (a measured stop-allele transcript level and a sup-tRNA readout on a WWOX stop codon).

**Reading.** Pierce 2025 supplies the single in vivo neonatal-brain endogenous-protein result; it is for one lysosomal enzyme at 6.3 percent of wild type in cortex, n = 3. The class enters because that is what the pre-registered rule asked, not because it is established. Cross-correction (a lysosomal enzyme can be taken up by neighbouring cells) is my knowledge, not a statement of any text read: the verifier searched the Pierce and Wang texts for cross-correction, mannose and secretion and found none, so whether 6.3 percent of an enzyme transfers to a cytosolic protein such as WWOX, whose function is not enzymatic in the sense of IDUA activity, is **an open premise, labelled inference**. The only sources' threshold statements are about IDUA (a 0.5 percent residual activity in patient fibroblasts associated with attenuated disease, Wang 2022; a "1 percent or more is sufficient" sentence in Pierce attributed to Wang, which the retrieved Wang text does not contain) and about cells (20 to 70 percent in human cell models of other diseases): none concerns WWOX.

**What the sources say about rivals and harms (carried, not argued):** ataluren and aminoglycosides remain IT4 E1's established read-through class (negative CDKL5 and Dravet crossover carried); class-agnostic levers for class E (circuit levers, gene addition) need no allele premise and a suppressor tRNA would have to beat them on a stated criterion (not done here: see "Next"); NMD limits a suppressor for NMD-sensitive alleles unless the tRNA stabilises the transcript, which one primary measured in adult liver and heart. Harms as measured in the primaries: tRNA-seq of brain (n = 3, one study), whole-proteome mass spectrometry of cortex and liver for native-stop readthrough peptides (none found, reporter cohort, Pierce), a 15-week body-weight comparison in wild-type mice (Dnmt1-edited controls), ribosome profiling in lung and liver (adult, n = 2) and liver (n = 3) with readthrough at native UGA stops stated as not enhanced beyond background; no behavioural battery in any brain study. NAR 2026 statement that "rescue ... in Hurler remains subtherapeutic (<8 percent)" for UGA and arginine is not supported by the Wang and Pierce texts (their alleles are TAG with Tyr and Leu); the verifier flagged it.

## (c) The eight exon-9 predicted-escape alleles, from domain boundaries only
Held boundaries (UniProt as quoted by IT4 E1): 414 residues; WW1 16 to 49, WW2 57 to 90, region 125 to 414, active-site Tyr293. For each stop, residues kept, lost and the part of the 290-residue region kept:
p.Gln353* 352 kept, 62 lost (15.0 percent), 228 of 290 (79 percent); p.Gln354* 353, 61, 79 percent; p.Gly372* 371, 43 (10.4 percent), 85 percent; p.Gln389* 388, 26 (6.3 percent), 91 percent; p.Glu391* 390, 24, 92 percent; p.Trp398* 397, 17, 94 percent; p.Glu402* 401, 13, 96 percent; p.Gln406* 405, 9 (2.2 percent), 97 percent (borderline Trp335*: 334 kept, 80 lost, 72 percent). All keep WW1, WW2 and Tyr293. **Statement allowed at this level:** these truncations spare the annotated domains and active-site residue and remove only the C-terminal tail, so a boundary-level reason to rule them out as "non-functional by construction" does not exist; it is equally not a reason to expect function. A measured activity or stability of a WWOX C-terminal truncation was searched for by two routes (Europe PMC title and abstract: structure, enzymatic activity and C-terminal queries, 64 hits, none a truncation measurement; held full texts and notes: the only held C-terminal observation is the missense p.Gly372Arg, described by its authors as in the C-terminal substrate-specificity region and associated with the milder SCAR12 phenotype, which shows the residue is disease-relevant and is a missense, not a truncation); none found. Three of the eight are uncertain, two conflicting and three pathogenic or likely pathogenic in ClinVar (IT4 E1's verification), so loss of function cannot be assumed for them.
