## Answer first
1. **By the pre-registered rule, the suppressor-tRNA class is NOT a footnote: row 2 applies.** Gene addition has the stronger stated evidence (function readouts in an untreated-null comparison; the suppressor tRNA has a marker and histology only), but it carries a premise the suppressor tRNA does not carry, **the expression level**: measured regional overexpression of the transgene, no measured ceiling, and no harm endpoint. The two classes are therefore **not ranked**; the suppressor tRNA is carried as a **conditional alternative whose single named advantage is native expression control, which is unmeasured for WWOX**.
2. **My written prediction ("footnote is the most likely outcome") was half missed.** The prediction rested on "the benchmark has function readouts the suppressor-tRNA primary lacks"; that holds. What I did not weigh in advance was that the same benchmark's own held notes record an expression premise that the rule counts. I record the miss as informative: the footnote reading would have required counting gene addition's expression premise as zero, which the held notes do not allow.
3. **No subset exists that gene addition cannot serve, in the sources read.** Coverage cannot favour the suppressor tRNA: it is 5 of 32 held alleles by residue and codon (all predicted NMD-sensitive), none of the 8 predicted-escape alleles; gene addition is allele-agnostic by construction (32 of 32, with the caveat that its benchmark is a null background, not a stop allele). For the five matched alleles the null mouse is the closest background (a predicted NMD-sensitive nonsense allele predicts little or no product), which strengthens the benchmark's transfer to them and does not strengthen the suppressor tRNA.
4. **The open premise stays attached to every sentence about the class:** the transfer of a 6.3 percent IDUA activity in neonatal cortex (a lysosomal enzyme; one primary; n = 3) to a cytosolic protein such as WWOX is **unmeasured and unestablished**.
5. **The single cheapest observation that could move the class** (class and mechanism level; no experiment is prescribed): the fraction of wild-type WWOX protein restored, and its WW1-dependent function, at a residue-matched stop (p.Arg264* or p.Arg54* for Arg-UGA; p.Tyr85* or p.Tyr107* for Tyr-UAG) with and without the matching suppressor tRNA in cells, read against the one number the benchmark cannot supply: what fraction of wild-type protein is above the partial-rescue floor in forebrain (not measured; see row 4).

## Outcome by the pre-registered table
* **Row 1 (footnote): not met.** Its condition, "no extra premise the suppressor tRNA lacks", fails: gene addition carries the expression-level premise (measured regional supraphysiological expression, no ceiling, no harm endpoint, WWOX a tumour suppressor with a held principle of neither too little nor too much).
* **Row 2 (not ranked): met.** Gene addition has function readouts where the suppressor tRNA has markers; the suppressor tRNA lacks gene addition's expression premise by construction (native promoter and regulatory sequences), **but that advantage is a design property, not a measurement**: nothing measures native-level restoration for WWOX, and the restored fraction in the only in vivo primary was about 6 percent in one tissue.
* **Row 3 (a subset gene addition cannot serve): not met** in what was read. Not read: whether a stop allele that yields a stable truncated product interferes with an added copy; the sources give no case.
* **Row 4 (adds on top): no source argues it;** recorded as an open question without a claim.
* **Row 5 (gene addition weaker than recalled): not the case;** the benchmark is as the ADV-120 card states, with the limits already in its section 5.
