## 0. What is already known (disclosure)

Held and read before this file: DL-REPO-003; the ADV-275 result (valproate raises WWOX mRNA in four independent human neural deposits; protein not measured, proteomic repositories not queried). From general knowledge, not sourced: WWOX is a protein of low to middle abundance that shotgun proteomes of neural material may or may not quantify, and drug-perturbation proteome atlases are mostly in non-neural lines.

## 1. Question and comparator

Does any public proteomic deposit or open supplementary protein table measure WWOX protein (human Q9NZC7; mouse Q91WL8) in neural material exposed to an HDAC inhibitor, and in which direction?

Comparator: the concurrent vehicle or untreated control of the same experiment, same system, same time point. No comparison across deposits.

## 2. Eligibility, fixed now

- Neural material, human or mouse: primary neurons or glia, neural stem or progenitor cells, ESC or iPSC derived neural cells, organoids, brain or spinal-cord tissue after in vivo (including prenatal) exposure. Neuroblastoma, glioma and medulloblastoma lines are tier B: reported separately, they do not fill the gap.
- Exposure: an HDAC inhibitor alone (valproate, vorinostat, trichostatin A, sodium butyrate, entinostat, panobinostat, romidepsin, CI-994, other named HDAC inhibitors) against a concurrent control.
- Measurement: protein quantification of the whole or fractionated proteome. Phospho-only, acetyl-only and histone-only datasets are counted but cannot give a WWOX abundance value unless they also carry a total proteome.
- Retrieval: a processed protein table obtained by a plain request from the repository or the open-access article, no login, no contact address, no browser-style identity, file under 300 MB. Raw spectra are not reprocessed. A refusal or challenge page is a route result.

## 3. Reading rules, fixed now

- WWOX located by UniProt accession and by gene symbol; the file, column and row named for each value. Replicate unit and species beside every value; the unit is read from the deposit's or the paper's design text, and technical replicates (injections, fractions, repeated runs of one sample) are not units.
- **Sentinel read first:** H1-0 (human P07305, mouse P10922) and, where the deposit carries them, acetylated histone H3 or H4 sites. The sentinel passes when it rises at least 1.5-fold and, with 3 or more units per arm, differs from control at p < 0.05 (the ADV-275 lesson: a fold alone is not a sentinel). Any further sentinel chosen from a deposit's own design is named before WWOX is looked up in that deposit.
- Value: ratio of treated to control on the linear scale, with n per arm; Welch two-sided t-test on log values when n >= 3. Call: **up** (p < 0.05 and ratio >= 1.25), **down** (p < 0.05 and ratio <= 0.80), otherwise **not distinguished**. Imputed values are not used; a protein quantified in fewer than 2 units of an arm gets no ratio.
- **WWOX not detected or not quantified in a deposit is a coverage statement** (low abundance or depth), never a negative on direction.
- A dataset whose sentinel fails gives no WWOX reading for or against.

## 4. Predictions

| | Prediction | Probability or range |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | eligible tier A datasets (neural, single HDAC inhibitor arm with control, total protein quantified) | 4 (range 0 to 12) |
| P2 | at least one of them has a processed protein table retrievable by the permitted route | 0.8 |
| P3 | at least one retrievable tier A table quantifies WWOX in both arms | 0.55 |
| P4 | at least one tier A dataset with a passing sentinel quantifies WWOX with 3 or more biological units per arm | 0.25 |
| P5 | at least one such dataset calls WWOX protein up | 0.1 |

**Rejecting observation:** a tier A dataset with a passing sentinel and 3 or more biological units per arm where WWOX protein is quantified and is **down**, or is not distinguished with a 95 percent interval that excludes a 1.25-fold rise. If it occurs, the transcript control of ADV-275 does not carry to protein in that system.

**Disposition rule:** DL-REPO-003 names a protein-level positive control only if one tier A dataset with passing sentinel and n >= 3 calls WWOX up and no comparable one calls it down. Otherwise it names the missing measurement as a design: system, compound, exposure time, assay able to detect WWOX protein, unit of replication.
