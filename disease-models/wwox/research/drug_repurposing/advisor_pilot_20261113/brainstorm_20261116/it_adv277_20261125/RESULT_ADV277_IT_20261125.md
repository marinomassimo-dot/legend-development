## 1. Answer

**No protein-level positive control was found.** In the eligible deposits found on 2026-11-25, no experiment with a working exposure sentinel quantified WWOX protein.

* **The one deposit with a working sentinel does not detect WWOX.** Human dorsal forebrain organoids, 1 mM valproate from day 21, whole-organoid proteome at day 35 and day 50, three iPSC lines (BIONi010-C, HMGU1, KOLF2.1J), three pools of three organoids per arm (MassIVE MSV000097991 / PXD064286; PMID 42032298). H1-0 protein rises 1.82 to 2.59-fold in five of the six line-by-day contrasts (p 0.0002 to 0.049); the sixth (KOLF2.1J, day 35) has one quantified control and is not evaluable. With lines as units the geometric mean is 2.16 at day 50 and 2.60 at day 35 (the day-35 figure includes the line with one quantified control). **WWOX has no protein group and no precursor** in the 45 samples (5,104 protein groups, 56,276 precursors; the same search finds GAPDH 55 precursors and H1-0 9). This is a **coverage statement**, not a statement about direction.
* **Three mouse deposits are prenatal exposures sampled weeks later, where the sentinel cannot pass**; two have a retrievable table. Under the pre-registered rule they give no WWOX reading, for or against.

| Deposit | Material, exposure, units | H1-0 sentinel (treated / control) | WWOX protein (shown, **not** a reading) |
|---|---|---|---|
| PXD036413 (PMID 37524878) | mouse prefrontal cortex, valproate to the dam at E10, offspring weeks later, 4 / 4 | 1.40 [0.63 to 2.95], p 0.36: **fails** | 0.88 [0.68 to 1.16], p 0.27 |
| PXD067574 (PMID 42204145) | mouse striatum, prenatal valproate, 9 to 10 weeks, 3 / 3 (TMT) | 0.84 [0.73 to 0.97], p 0.031: **fails** | 0.99 [0.90 to 1.09], p 0.74 |
| PXD068418 (PMID 41150532) | mouse cerebellum, valproate 600 mg/kg to the dam at GD12.5, male offspring | not read | not read: the only processed archive is 4.2 GB (over the 300 MB limit); the supplement's table of differential proteins does not name Wwox |

Replicate unit for the mouse deposits is the deposited sample, read as one animal; the methods text was not checked for pooling.

**Disposition for DL-REPO-003.** The transcript positive control of ADV-275 has **no protein counterpart** in these repositories. The missing measurement is:

> human neural cells (forebrain organoids or iPSC-derived neurons) **during** valproate exposure against concurrent vehicle; WWOX protein by a targeted assay (parallel-reaction monitoring with a labelled standard peptide, or an immunoblot whose antibody is validated on a WWOX-null control); at least three independent differentiation batches or lines per arm as units; H1-0 protein as the exposure sentinel; sampled when the transcript rise is also measured.

Shotgun proteomics at the depth of the organoid deposit would not do it: it did not see WWOX at all.

## 5. A transcript observation met on the way (not counted)

The organoid paper's RNA table (Supplementary Table 2; day 50, BIONi010-C, four batches by its Supplementary Table 1) prints WWOX log2 fold change 1.04 (2.06-fold, adjusted p 1.2e-13) for valproate against control. ADV-275 listed the deposit behind it (E-MTAB-15191) as not read, for want of a processed table. It is **not** added to ADV-275's count: the transcript sentinels fixed there fail in this table (H1F0 1.08-fold, not distinguished; CDKN1A 0.71-fold, not distinguished), although H1-0 **protein** rises in the same study. This also says that a transcript sentinel can fail where the exposure plainly acted: ADV-275's sentinel rule may have discarded working experiments.

## 6. Limits

* WWOX values for PXD036413 were printed by my first scan of the spreadsheet **before** its sentinel was read; the pre-registered rule then removed the reading, so nothing depends on the order.
* The sentinel H1-0 is one protein; the deposits carry no acetyl-site data.
* DIA and TMT values as deposited; no re-search of spectra; no multiplicity correction (one pre-named protein).
* The organoid pools' independence as differentiation batches was not established from the methods text read; the unit used is the pool.
* Third-party files are not committed; digests in `data_digests.tsv`; scripts `adv277_search.py`, `adv277_read.py` (argument 1: the download folder with the subfolders `pride` and `msv97991`; argument 2: this folder), `adv277_xlsx.py`; values `adv277_values.txt`. The abbreviation search used the same script with its term list replaced and without the literature and PDC parts.
