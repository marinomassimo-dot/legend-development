Not found: any age-dependent (neonatal vs adult) conditional NKCC1 deletion against a seizure endpoint; any NKCC1 heterozygote seizure study (see Not found list).

N1 summary on engagement. The two genetic-DEE bumetanide studies with a seizure endpoint that had a full text (Yuan, Tahara) measured E_GABA or expression only in untreated tissue or not at all, and neither measured brain exposure; the Ube3a study (Egawa) measured E_GABA and expression in untreated slices, found E_GABA unchanged on average, and relied on a cited infusion rate for exposure. Therefore a null on seizures in Scn1a/Scn1b models is, in these studies, absence of engagement evidence in vivo, not evidence of absence of lever engagement; the only in-model engagement is ex vivo (Yuan acute slice application). Nonmonogenic-seizure endpoints improving (Marguet, Egawa latency, Pizzamiglio behaviour) coexisted with no seizure benefit (Pizzamiglio, Yuan), which cannot be separated into a drug effect vs a lever effect with these data. Seizure-direction hazard: Vargas 2013 reports a pro-seizure direction of neonatal bumetanide on thermal seizures in an R43Q mouse (AB only).
