# Reanalysis plan, frozen before the run (SCI, 2026-11-18)

Question. The repository card F03 and the IT13 P3a note list a lower or unchanged SLC12A2:SLC12A5 transcript ratio in Wwox-deficient material as counter-evidence to a depolarising gradient. Bulk transcripts mix cell classes: SLC12A5 (KCC2) is neuronal, SLC12A2 (NKCC1) is expressed in glia, oligodendrocytes, progenitors and neurons. Does a change in cell composition alone explain the bulk difference, and what is the neuron-restricted picture? Transcript level is not protein, localisation, transport or E_GABA, and no statement here says otherwise.

Units. GSE156243: 6 organoid libraries from one parental hESC line (4 knockout, 2 wild-type), so the genotype contrast is confounded with clone and batch; n for the genotype effect is not 4 against 2 independent biological units. GSE158684: one Cell Ranger pool per arm (P17 hippocampal nuclei, Synapsin-Cre deletion against a Cre-only control), so n is 1 against 1 libraries; nuclei are not replicates. No p-value across genotypes is computed or reported for either dataset; effect sizes are descriptive.

Computed (organoids): per library CPM of SLC12A5, SLC12A2, their ratio, and SLC12A5 and SLC12A2 normalised to a pan-neuronal panel (MAP2, RBFOX3, SYT1, SNAP25, STMN2, TUBB3, NEFL; geometric mean of CPM+1) and, for SLC12A2, to a progenitor-glia panel (SOX2, VIM, NES, GFAP, AQP4); per-library values are listed so that the clustering of knockout libraries is visible.
Computed (nuclei): class assignment of each nucleus by the maximum normalised marker score (excitatory, inhibitory, astrocyte, oligodendrocyte, microglia, OPC; markers listed in the script), composition per arm, per-class Slc12a5 and Slc12a2 CPM and detection fraction, the all-nuclei value, and a counterfactual decomposition: bulk(KO composition, control per-class values) against observed bulk(KO).
Decision rule written before the run: if the counterfactual reproduces at least 70 percent of the observed all-nuclei log2 difference for a gene, the bulk difference for that gene is called composition-explained; if it reproduces less than 30 percent, within-class; otherwise mixed. A within-class difference in neurons is a lead to measure, not a result.
Cannot show: protein, phosphorylation, membrane localisation, [Cl-]i, E_GABA, or anything about independent biological replication.
