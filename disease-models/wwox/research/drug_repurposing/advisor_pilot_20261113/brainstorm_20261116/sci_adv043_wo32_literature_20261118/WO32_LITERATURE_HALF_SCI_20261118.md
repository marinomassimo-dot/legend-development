## Answer first
**Verdict: mixed.** On *function*: a bounded null for the central oligodendrocyte lineage. In 41 title-and-abstract records and 107 further titles advanced from a full-text-wide census, no record reports a loss-of-function, gain-of-function, binding or selective-inhibitor result for HDAC4, 5, 7 or 9 in an oligodendrocyte-lineage cell with a differentiation or myelin readout. This is the second, independently queried screen that reaches the same place as IT29 P10-F (2026-11-05). On *expression*: discordant and not resolved. One 2012 abstract states HDAC9 is not expressed in oligodendrocytes of adult mouse brain (immunostaining); IT's held RNA tables detect Hdac9 in the mouse lineage (FPKM 1.7 to 2.4) and in the human oligodendrocyte group (2.90); a 2014 optic-nerve study finds HDAC4 and HDAC9 protein cytoplasmic in glia (mostly astrocytes) with HDAC7 mRNA detected and its protein not measurable. The only class IIa myelin results with a function readout are in Schwann cells of the peripheral nervous system (HDAC4 and HDAC5 required together for timely myelination; HDAC7 and HDAC9 compensate), which is not the oligodendrocyte lineage.
**"Not found in these queries" is the wording, never "absent from the literature".** The unscreened tail is named in section 4. The null is bounded, not verified: three of the key records could not be read beyond their abstracts (section 3), and the Tier 2 filter reads abstracts for titles that pass a loose rule only.

## 6. What this changes
* **The class IIa limit carried on about 30 proposals ("nothing found" for the central oligodendrocyte lineage).** It stands, now supported by a second screen with independent query strings and an abstract-level filter on the census nobody opened. Two wording changes the limit can take on, neither a change of direction: (a) the limit is about *function*; for *expression* it should say "discordant: RNA detects all four class IIa genes in the mouse lineage (IT, GSE52564), one 2012 immunostaining abstract reports HDAC9 absent from oligodendrocytes, protein-level evidence is thin"; (b) the Schwann-cell results are PNS and are a delay under double or triple loss (IT29 P10-F), not a CNS myelin lever. I did not edit any proposal or ledger.
* **IT29-P11B (TMP269 values HDAC4 130, HDAC5 80, HDAC7 40, HDAC9 20 nM).** Nothing in the screen gives a lineage readout for any of the four members, so the biochemical potencies do not map onto a lineage effect; the member with the strongest biochemical potency (HDAC9) is the one with a negative expression report in oligodendrocytes, and HDAC7 has mRNA in optic nerve glia without protein data. This is a reason not to read any potency ordering as an oligodendrocyte ordering; it is not a reason to change a catalogue decision, which stays IT's.
* **What would reopen it** (the same as IT29 P10-F item 10): an oligodendrocyte-lineage-specific deletion or catalytic-dead allele of HDAC4/5 (with HDAC7/9) with a CC1, MBP or g-ratio readout, or a class IIa compound with a knockout control in OPC differentiation culture; or a read of 23671795's methods (a human visit, since the PMC page is a reCAPTCHA).
