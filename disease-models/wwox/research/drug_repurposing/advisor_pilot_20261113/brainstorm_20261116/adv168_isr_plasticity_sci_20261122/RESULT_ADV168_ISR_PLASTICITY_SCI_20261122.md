## Answer first
**R-partial; I-1 holds for some readouts and I-2 for others. The two routes are separated GENUINELY in the paper by causal dependence readouts (eIF4E / DDX3X / mTOR-inhibitor / uORF1 dependence of ATF4 induction) and by phospho-eIF2-alpha and PERK status, in the same MEF and mouse ES cell models. They are NOT separated by ATF4 protein (up in both), by GADD34 and CHOP mRNA (up in the eIF2B-reduced state), or, at an acute comparator, by GADD34 and CHOP protein (absent at 1 h of the canonical challenge too). The brain comparison is a published-gene-list overlap, not a measurement. Decision: ONE named delta for IT on INV-02 (route-assigning pair beside ATF4; GADD34/CHOP only at a late matched time), one wording note for RP-018 / ADV-160/161 (state phospho-eIF2-alpha status before calling an eIF2B-lesion contrast "ISR"), and the glucose link kept weak with one named consumer. The ISRIB comparison itself is NOT supported or changed by this paper (it has no eIF2B-activator arm).**

So the separation does **not** follow the cell model alone (the same pattern is seen in MEFs and ES cells) and does **not** follow a wholly unmatched challenge for the eIF4E / uORF1 / DDX3X readouts, which use inhibitors and edits on both routes. It **does** follow timing for GADD34 and CHOP protein, and the transcriptome comparison rests on a cross-study dataset. The 4-day depletion also lowers phospho-eIF2-alpha below control, which the authors read as a feature of the disease state; an adapted baseline after chronic reduction is a rival reading that this paper does not exclude.

## Brain comparison (separate from the cell models; B-brain)
The only link to brain is a Venn comparison (Fig. 3k): 26 genes, including Pck2 and Atf4, shared between the MEF depletion RNA-seq and a published VWM mouse brain dataset (the authors' reference for 2 to 7 month mutant mice; not this paper's data). It is a gene-list overlap, group level, bulk tissue against fibroblasts, a point mutation against a depletion, with no brain protein, no brain phospho-eIF2-alpha and no brain c-ISR comparator measured here. The statements that the mutant mice carry a persistent ISR and respond to a small-molecule eIF2B activator are cited from earlier work, not tested here. **The paper therefore gives no evidence that VWM brain is in an s-ISR rather than a canonical ISR; it gives evidence that a shared set of ATF4-target transcripts can be raised by the eIF2B-reduced state in a cell line.** No animals, ages, sexes or per-group counts are reported for any brain comparison in this paper.

## The pair of testable interpretations (for a WWOX-deficient human neural line against its isogenic control; hypothesis level; not asserted to describe WWOX)
* **T-A, canonical route:** ATF4 high with phospho-eIF2-alpha raised and a PERK shift; ATF4 retained when eIF4E / mTOR is inhibited; GADD34 and CHOP protein rise at a late matched time; removal of phospho-eIF2-alpha (kinase-level loss or an active phosphatase fragment) lowers ATF4.
* **T-B, eIF2B-reduced route without a phospho-eIF2-alpha rise:** ATF4 high with phospho-eIF2-alpha not raised (possibly lower); ATF4 lost when eIF4E / mTOR is inhibited at a time that does not collapse the control; GADD34 and CHOP protein not raised at the late matched time; ATF4 unchanged when phospho-eIF2-alpha is removed.
* **What assigns the route:** the pair {baseline phospho-eIF2-alpha with PERK status, and ATF4 sensitivity to eIF4E / mTOR inhibition against an ATF4-high control state}; ATF4 protein alone, GADD34 or CHOP mRNA alone, and GADD34 or CHOP protein at an acute time cannot.
* **Rejecting observations:** both signatures at once in one line at one time (a mixed state: the label would then be a gradient, which the paper itself raises); or a WWOX line in which ATF4 is raised and both the phospho-eIF2-alpha and the inhibitor-sensitivity read-outs are uninformative (technical failure of the pair).

## Decision for INV-02 and the ISRIB comparison
* **INV-02: carry ONE named delta to IT (concrete, absent from the row as written; the row contains no eIF4E, mTOR-inhibitor, GADD34 or CHOP wording).** Wording for the next_test, sign-high branch and its readout list: *Assign the route by baseline phospho-eIF2-alpha (with PERK status) read together with the sensitivity of ATF4 to an eIF4E or mTOR inhibitor at a matched time; ATF4 protein and an eIF2B-activator response do not by themselves separate a phospho-eIF2-alpha-driven response from an eIF2B-reduced one. Read GADD34 and CHOP protein only at a late matched time (the paper's own acute challenge lacks them at 1 h).* Reason in one line: in the paper's own blots ATF4 rises under both routes, and GADD34 and CHOP are delayed readouts that are absent at the acute time under the canonical route too. The existing IT35 clause (phospho-eIF2-alpha-independent arm, exchange activity, phospho-epsilon, per-cell translation) already preserves the distinction at the level of "an arm exists"; what it lacks is the **interpretive rule** and the **inhibitor-sensitivity control**. Not a new mandatory test: it is a control on a readout already present, and the paper supports it only in mouse fibroblasts, mouse ES cells and one breast cancer pair, never in human neural cells.
* **INV-02 PPP1R15A / eIF2B-activator panel:** the paper has **no eIF2B-activator arm** (no ISRIB or 2BAct experiment) and the only phosphatase datum is the active GADD34 fragment that does not change ATF4 in the depleted state. So the paper neither supports nor undermines the activator arm; it supports the reading that a PPP1R15A-direction arm acts on phospho-eIF2-alpha and therefore only on the canonical route, while an eIF2B activator acts on the shared node. That asymmetry is an inference from the structure (activator not tested here) and stays labelled as such.
* **ISRIB comparison (RP-018, ADV-160/161): no change to the comparison; one wording note.** A text search of the ADV-160 and ADV-161 result files for phospho-eIF2, c-ISR, s-ISR and "canonical" finds no statement of the phospho-eIF2-alpha status of the VWM contrast. This paper shows that a VWM-type eIF2B lesion can raise ATF4-target transcripts with phospho-eIF2-alpha lower, so "ISR" in a VWM-model contrast should be written as "eIF2B-reduced state (phospho-eIF2-alpha status stated)" unless phospho-eIF2-alpha is raised. Reusable; absent; kept to this line, not a rule.
* **Closes nothing and ranks nothing:** no compound, no rank, no WWOX claim; INV-02 stays `premise_measurement`.

## Glucose link (Fig. 4l) — weak; one named consumer
Held notes reread: ADV-133 (Ludes-Meyers germline null at P18: glucose 250.6, 227.0 and 143.5 mg/dL in WT, heterozygote and knockout, n 3, 3, 4; the paper itself says the cause of the hypoglycaemia cannot be identified; a feeding-artefact rival cannot be separated; the euthanasia method bears on CO2) and `gsk3_eif2b_20261107` (Ser9 blot in Wwox-null brain at P20, after seizure onset, one single-lane blot, heterozygote not intermediate; "without a rise in phospho-eIF2-alpha" tested by no source read); the S540E source is a route result (abstract level only; bioRxiv rate limit), so nothing is resolved there. **The link is weak for three reasons:** (1) the paper's contrast is a culture medium, 25 against 2.5 mM glucose for 16 h in fibroblasts (a tenfold step, no phospho-eIF2-alpha or eIF2B activity shown), while the knockout figure is a serum value of about 8 mM against about 14 mM at P18 in 3 to 4 mice, unfed-state unknown; (2) the held GSK3 evidence is at P20 after seizures and the glucose value at P18, in different measurements; (3) LiCl is not a GSK3-specific test. **One named consumer, optional, for IT:** the INV-02 line comparison: report the culture medium glucose concentration for both lines and measure it at harvest, because a mild fall in medium glucose alone moves ATF4 without GADD34 in the paper's own fibroblasts, so a line that consumes glucose faster could look different for a reason unrelated to its genotype. Evidence strength: inference from one fibroblast experiment; no WWOX glucose-consumption datum is claimed. It neither resolves S540E nor shows a WWOX effect.

## EMPATHY and DREAM
* **Shared readout hides two mechanisms (reusable, absent in an existing note):** ATF4 protein is the same readout under both routes and can look like one ISR whose intervention direction differs (kinase-level and phosphatase levers act only on the phospho-eIF2-alpha route; an eIF2B activator acts on both). Kept as the sentence under "What assigns the route"; no new file.
* **DREAM, kept only with a consumer:** the glucose medium control above (consumer: INV-02 line comparison).
