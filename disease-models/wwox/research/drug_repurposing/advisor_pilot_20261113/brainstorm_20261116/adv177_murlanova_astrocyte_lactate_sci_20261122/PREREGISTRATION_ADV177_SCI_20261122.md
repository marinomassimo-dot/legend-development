## What SCI44 already specifies (the duplicate test)
PH1 already demands per-class readout; PH3 already carries the astrocyte-lactate-to-neuronal-MCT2 direction (adult dmPFC, Ldha); INV-07's next_test already carries marker-resolved material and a direct lactate and pyruvate measurement with redox. So "read per cell class" and "measure lactate directly" are not new. A delta must be something those texts do not carry: a measured cell-restricted perturbation of the astrocyte MITOCHONDRIAL route (not Ldha), a readout of astrocyte respiration paired with lactate in the same preparation, a neuron-side use readout, or a rescue that separates production from use.

## The three WWOX-side rivals the comparison must separate (fixed)
(a) astrocyte lactate PRODUCTION or EXPORT impaired; (b) neuronal USE (uptake via MCT2 and oxidation) impaired; (c) a generic state change (viability, cell composition, redox, seizure consequence). High circulating lactate with low local astrocyte production could coexist, and neither establishes the other.

## Readings for the paper (fixed)
* **K (KEEP):** in one preparation, with the animal or culture as the unit, a cell-restricted manipulation of astrocytes with a stated age: astrocyte respiration AND lactate (with the compartment stated: medium, tissue, intracellular) change, AND a neuron-side readout (use, not only an endpoint such as behaviour) is tested with a rescue or add-back (lactate, pyruvate or an MCT-dependence arm) that separates production from use. Then name ONE INV-07 comparison: which arm separates (a) from (b), with the strongest rival and a refuting observation.
* **N (NARROW):** perturbation, respiration and lactate exist, but the neuronal endpoint is behavioural or adult-only or has no rescue or use arm, or the lactate is a mixed-tissue value: then only a boundary or a labelled readout (compartment-labelled lactate, a statement that high lactate cannot separate production from use) changes, and it must be stated as a boundary, not a comparison.
* **D (DROP):** (i) no interpretable cell-specific contrast; (ii) the paper only repeats SCI44's cell-resolved premise and the Ldha-MCT2 direction with new labels; or (iii) the only WWOX support is a signal in pooled or mixed tissue; record as a closed connection with the reason.

## Checks I will make (fixed)
Age of the manipulation and of each readout (embryonic, neonatal, juvenile, adult); the cell-restriction tool (promoter, Cre or AAV, reporter, efficiency) and whether it reaches only astrocytes; compartments per readout (isolated mitochondria, cultured astrocytes, slices, tissue homogenate, extracellular medium); the biological unit (animals, litters, cultures, differentiations, wells, slices) against the replicate used in the tests; whether respiration and lactate come from the same preparation as the neuronal endpoint; whether the neuronal endpoint is a neuronal measurement (current, firing, respiration) or behaviour; whether any rescue separates supply from use; the GEO description before any download (and no RNA-seq reanalysis unless it is the one allowed light check); adult cognition and adult astrocyte results set no direction, age, seizure effect or compound for WWOX.

## The one allowed WWOX dataset check (fixed rule)
Only if a held or public WWOX dataset separates cell classes AND has more than one biological unit per arm: a transcript-abundance look at the perturbed gene family (Slc25a18 and the glutamate carrier paralog, the shuttle genes Ldha, Slc16a1, Slc16a3, Slc16a7) per class, for feasibility or priority only, never for flux, respiration or rescue. If the held sets carry one animal or one pool per arm, or do not separate astrocytes from neurons, I do not run it and state that.

## What would make the connection with SCI44 (DREAM)

## Rejecting observation (named in advance)
