Reading all 66 gives these **held** rows (each goes into the ledger with its file as source):

* gaboxadol: P8 to P9 mouse, 3.5 mg/kg effective, 5 highly sedating, 10 fatal, no apoptosis or neurodegeneration increase with THIP in the same slice-and-seizure series; ganaxolone: preterm guinea-pig pups with sedation, poor weight gain and higher mortality (PMID 30237570, abstract-level), label from 2 years; paediatric trials of both.
* ezogabine: P7 rat apoptosis after repeated, not single, dosing (Brown 2016; held at three depths); ICA-069673: none.
* NMDAR: memantine at neuroprotective exposures did not raise constitutive apoptosis in the developing rat, unlike MK-801 (PMID 21624454, abstract-level, "disagrees with the label's juvenile-rat apoptosis statement, not reconciled"); ketamine in children (short exposures 0.33 percent serious events, drug-provoked seizures 26/94 with epilepsy); a paediatric ketamine or d-APV neonatal-rodent row is **not held**.
* vigabatrin: retinal toxicity class (titles), a pup study of a vigabatrin analogue (lethal at the top dose); no neonatal vigabatrin row of its own.
* clemastine: neonatal hypoxia mouse pups P3 to P10, no overt toxicity at any dose, mild sedation at the two higher doses, no formal toxicity study (Odell 2024, held full text).
* thyromimetics: the peripheral thyrotoxicosis class only; lithium, tideglusib, SB216763: **none held**.

## Ledger form (one table, no new register; one row per candidate and finding)
Columns as ADV set them: **candidate | species and age window | exposure as reported (no dose) | endpoint | effect, or "not found by these routes" | source id and depth | one-origin flag | weakening observation**, plus one column **held or new** (the file for a held row). Rows carry a **class** fixed now: **SIGNAL** (a harm endpoint at an exposure inside the efficacy range, single or repeated); **SIGNAL ABOVE RANGE** (only above the efficacy range); **NO SIGNAL AT DEPTH X** (the endpoint was measured and not seen, with the depth); **NOT FOUND BY THESE ROUTES** (never "safe"; an absence needs two differently built routes). "Signal at one dose" is not "incompatible"; "not found" is not "safe".
