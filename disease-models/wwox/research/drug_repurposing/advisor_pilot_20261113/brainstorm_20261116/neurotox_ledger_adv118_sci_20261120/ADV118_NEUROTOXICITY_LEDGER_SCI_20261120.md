that is an absence, and the age window of its own risk (P3 to P7 rodents, under 3 years in the label) is the one that was not examined; the preference is therefore **conditional on the pup readout below**. **The broad arm list:** vigabatrin and lithium carry the clearest in-window signals and need a pup exposure step before any arm; the most consequential unknown is whether **ethosuximide at the Cheng regimen causes pup apoptosis on repeated dosing**, since no row exists and repeated, not single, dosing was the condition under which the nearest P7 signal (ezogabine) appeared.

## 5. What this changes and does not change
* **The ADV-117 recommendation stands** (slice priority slot, no Kv7 pilot arm), with one caution added by this ledger: **ICA-069673 triggered a seizure-like hypermotile behaviour in zebrafish larvae (K3)** where retigabine suppressed it. In a slice that could read either as the informative contrast the tool was added for or as a hyperexcitable response; it does not remove the tool, and it is a reason to read the first ICA-069673 slice result as an attribution test, not a candidate result.
* **No row in the ledger is a clinical statement**; every effect is in the species, age and preparation of its source.
* **"Not found" is not "safe".** E2, T2, SB1, K4 and TH3 are absences with the routes named; none is evidence of safety.

## 6. Limits and weakest answers (disclosed)
