## 4. What this changes (a prediction, an arm, an order, a readout; all conditional on the model and on the assumed depth map)
1. **Arm and readout (13.11 Addendum 14):** a matched **depth series** for **both** agents (about 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 of the evoked response, plus the deep point) with the burst rate against evoked depth as the readout, instead of one deep point for both and one shallow point for the pore blocker only. The discriminant is the pore blocker against d-APV **at the same depth**: below = R-D or use-dependence (not separable here), above = R-E, equal = R-A alone. A deep point alone cannot do it.
2. **P1's rejecting observation** is not specific to R-A (statement 3): a failure to suppress at deep block must be followed by the intermediate depths and, for R-E, by a PV-selective control, before R-A is weakened.
3. **Readout:** call a post-washout burst-rate fall (at most 0.7) a lasting change only if the evoked delayed response is back within 10% of baseline, and report its precision; and add an early washout read (about 5 and 15 minutes) so that an adaptation rebound of the toy's kind is not missed.
4. **What the model cannot do:** it does not separate R-D from use-dependent kinetics (NOT REPRESENTED: a use-dependent blocker blocks more during bursts than the evoked response shows, which would also give "below" at matched evoked depth); the paired sag and an evoked-response train against single pulse are the discriminants (INFERENZA from general pharmacology, ASSUMED in lane D, not a model result); R-E has no measured coupling.
