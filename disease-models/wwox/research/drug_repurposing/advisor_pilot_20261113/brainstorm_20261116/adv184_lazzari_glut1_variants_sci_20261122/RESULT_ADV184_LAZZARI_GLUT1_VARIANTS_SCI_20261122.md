# ADV-184 result: what Lazzari et al. 2026 (Mol Med; SLC2A1 missense variants in brain endothelial and HeLa cells) adds to INV-16 Step 1 for separating (a) reduced abundance, (b) altered localisation or turnover, (c) loss of transport despite surface presence (SCI successor Z, 2026-11-22; method transfer only; no HDAC5 pharmacology, direction, ranking or compound change; no GLUT1 or HDAC5 claim for WWOX from this source; no author contact; no e-mail address to any service; not medical advice)

Pre-registration: `PREREGISTRATION_ADV184_SCI_20261122.md` (commit babdc22d3, before any body text, figure or supplement was read). Receipt: `RECEIPT_ADV184_SCI_20261122.json`.

## Answer first
**NARROW to one technical qualification of Step 1's surface readout; no new arm and no stronger first test.** My pre-registered expectation (NARROW or CLOSE) was confirmed.

**The qualification (one sentence for INV-16 Step 1):** "Surface" must be a surface-specific, absolute measurement (non-permeant labelling of the endogenous transporter with an intracellular-leakage control, or an extracellular epitope in non-permeabilised cells), reported beside total protein; a colocalisation coefficient with a membrane marker in fixed, permeabilised cells does not stand for it; and glucose entry is to be expressed per surface transporter as well as per cell, so that a fall in entry with unchanged surface amount reads as (c) and not as (a) or (b).

**Why this and no more.** Step 1 already pairs transcript, total protein, surface fraction, HIF1A and EPAS1 activity and one pre-registered glucose-entry endpoint, so the three rivals are already separable in principle. What the paper adds is a concrete instance of how the surface readout can fail when it is a colocalisation coefficient: in its own Fig 1B, N34S and R126L have plasma-membrane PCC with pan-cadherin of about 0.68 and 0.63 against 0.67 for wild type, while the same variants show intracellular lysosomal accumulation (LAMP1 PCC 0.76 and 0.64 against 0.27). A membrane-marker PCC therefore did not register the intracellular retention that the paper itself documents. That bears on how Step 1's "surface fraction" is to be measured, not on what Step 1 decides.

**What the paper does not give:** a surface-specific functional control, an origin control, a glucose-entry measurement, or any neural or knockdown matrix. Glucose uptake appears only as cited literature (Table S2: reduced uptake in erythrocytes, Xenopus oocytes, CHO and Rat2 cells for N34S, R126L, R153C, R223P, R223W, P485L), measured in other systems.

## Observations, interpretations and proposed measure, kept apart
**Observations (read from the text, legends, Fig 1 image and Table S2 text; values approximate):**
* Constructs: eight missense variants (N34S, R126L, R153C, R223P, R223W, Q283R, I404V, P485L) made by site-directed mutagenesis of a C-terminal GLUT1-EGFP plasmid; transient transfection of hCMEC/D3 (human brain microvascular endothelial line) and HeLa cells for 48 h; all experiments are overexpression of tagged constructs (the authors' own limitation).
* Localisation: confocal on fixed, permeabilised cells with markers for plasma membrane (pan-cadherin), ER, Golgi, RAB5, RAB7, LAMP1, LC3; Pearson's coefficient per cell, mean of 30 cells per condition, unpaired t test at p < 0.01; "three independent biological replicates" stated for the experiments. Wild type is mainly at the membrane (cadherin PCC 0.67). Membrane PCC is lower for P485L (0.36) and Q283R (0.58) only; N34S and R126L are not different from wild type by PCC although the images show intracellular accumulation. LAMP1 PCC is higher for N34S (0.76), Q283R (0.76), R126L (0.64) against 0.27; P485L goes to Golgin-97 (0.78 against 0.09) and RAB5 (0.81 against 0.47).
* HeLa (four variants): same direction for Q283R and P485L membrane PCC; N34S and Q283R overlap LAMP1; glucose deprivation then supplementation raises wild-type LAMP1 overlap about four-fold; N34S, Q283R, P485L already have high overlap under deprivation.
* Turnover: chloroquine (100 microM, 2 h) raises wild-type and R153C GLUT1-EGFP protein but not N34S, Q283R or P485L (Western, sum of the 81 and 71 kDa bands over actin, n = 3, paired t test p < 0.05). Cycloheximide chase (0 to 24 h, n = 3): longer half-life for N34S and Q283R; R153C and P485L like wild type.
* Not in the paper: any glucose-uptake or transport measurement of its own, any surface biotinylation or surface-epitope measurement, any quantification of the plasma-membrane fraction of the tagged protein, any neural cell, any knockdown, any HDAC or HIF readout, any WWOX mention (all searched).

**Interpretations (mine):**
* The paper separates, among variants with abnormal localisation, two different (b) types: lysosomal retention with slower degradation (N34S, Q283R) and Golgi and early-endosome retention with normal half-life (P485L). That distinction needs a turnover readout beside localisation.
* R153C, R223P and R223W are normally localised here while reported (elsewhere, other systems) to have reduced transport: in a cross-source reading they look like pattern (c) (apparent normal localisation, impaired function), but the localisation and the transport come from different systems and constructs, so this is a lead, not a result of this paper.
* A colocalisation coefficient with a membrane marker is a relative, within-cell overlap measure; it did not register retention for N34S and R126L.

**Proposed measure (one, conditional, not a new arm):** in Step 1, if transcript is unchanged while total or surface GLUT1 falls, a turnover chase of endogenous GLUT1 (cycloheximide, with and without a lysosomal-flux inhibitor) would place the fall as degradation rather than synthesis. This is standard method, not something the paper discriminates; I do not propose it as a new step, only name it as the conditional branch that the paper's two (b) types illustrate. The one qualification above is the only change I put forward.

## Rivals, reading and rejecting observations
* **Strongest rival to making any change:** Step 1 already separates (a), (b) and (c), the qualification is wording that a careful reader of "surface fraction" and "functional endpoint" would apply anyway. I accept this; hence NARROW, not KEEP.
* **Rejecting observation for the qualification:** a surface-specific measurement of endogenous GLUT1 in a neural line in which surface amount and entry per transporter move in step across a range of perturbations would show the per-surface normalisation adds nothing. None exists in the paper.
* **What would reject the transfer entirely (REJECT):** the paper concerns variant GLUT1 proteins expressed from constructs; Step 1 concerns a normal protein whose regulation changes. The localisation phenotypes (lysosomal retention) are properties of mutant proteins and carry no information about HDAC5-dependent regulation. I take only the method lesson, not the phenotypes.
* **Unit and replication limits:** cells (30 per condition) are the statistical unit, from three biological replicates; the Western and chase experiments are n = 3; Fig 7 and Fig 8 images and Supplementary Figs S2 to S14 were not viewed; Table S2 was read as text.

## Decision affected, consumer and DREAM
* **Decision affected:** how an observed Step 1 result is described (which of a, b, c), not whether INV-16 proceeds (the Step 1 decision, change reversing on rescue, is unchanged). Weak.
* **Consumer:** INV-16 Step 1 readout text (via IT at its next touch of INV-16; one technical qualification). No ranking, compound, direction or HDAC5 claim changes. NONE beyond that.
* **DREAM:** total, correctly located and functional GLUT1 are three quantities, and the paper's own Fig 1 shows a relative colocalisation coefficient standing in for abundance at the membrane: the same readout-type shape as ADV-181 (a stock of MBP-positive area standing for new sheath). Association: moderate for the shape, no shared mechanism. Kept because it improves one reading rule (name the readout type before accepting a surface claim).

## Limits
Overexpression of EGFP-tagged mutant constructs in an endothelial line and HeLa; no transport assay; endothelial and HeLa matrices against the neural system of Step 1 (a transfer the paper does not support); one pilot panel of eight variants; PMC bucket route gave the full XML and the PDFs but not the eight figure image files; figures read from PDF pages (Fig 1 viewed), the others from legends; supplement partly read.
