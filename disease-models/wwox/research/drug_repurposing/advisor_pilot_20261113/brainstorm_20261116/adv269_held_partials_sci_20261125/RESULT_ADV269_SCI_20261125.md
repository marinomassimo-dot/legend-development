* No cell experiment is reported: the Methods subsections are protein-only and a search for culture, HEK, transfect and cell line finds nothing; the Acknowledgements read "Thanks to Luka Brvar, Ellen Heitzer, and Beate Rinner for performing preliminary cell culture experiments" (no data shown).
* The activity rescue is partial. Table 2 kcat/KM for NADH, P187S 70 ± 10 without and 170 ± 20 with the ligand, wild type 1250 ± 110: 14 percent of wild type. Menadione, P187S 9 ± 1 and 30 ± 4, wild type 80 ± 16: 38 percent. The abstract says "strongly improved". Table 2 arithmetic: kcat/KM recomputed from the printed kcat and KM for all eight rows agrees within the rounding of KM (for example 770/4 = 192 against a printed 170 ± 20); no inconsistent row.

By my arithmetic 400 nM is about 2 percent of the variant's Kd (0.4/(0.4+20)), enzyme 20 nM.
* All kinetic and activation assays contain exogenous FAD at ten times its Kd (0.6 µM wild type, 4.2 µM variant; Methods), yet the Discussion attributes the gain to "a higher loading of FAD cofactor". With FAD already supplied at 10 Kd, simple binding gives about 91 percent holoenzyme without ligand (my arithmetic), so the stated mechanism is not tested by these assays.
* The printed "80% increase" (activation assay: 500 µM NADH, 200 µM menadione, 380 nm) is not reconciled with Table 2 (kcat/KM 2.4-fold for NADH, kcat 300 to 770; different NADH range and wavelength).
* The ligand binds wild type more tightly than the variant (Kd 7.95 against 20.0 µM): not variant-selective.
