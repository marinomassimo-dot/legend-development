## 0. Answer first
1. **The rejecting observation did not occur.** In whole-brain mouse RNA-seq (D1) Pvalb is **0.47 of its plateau at P14** (replicates 0.36, 0.53, 0.37, 0.60) and 0.89 at P28 (plateau = mean of P28 and P63). Pvalb is at 0.02 at P0 and P3. So at P14 PV is **in its rising phase** (the first age at which it reaches half of plateau lies between P14 and P28), and the P14 slice **is** a window in which a maturation delay could show. Reading (b) of row 6 keeps its purpose at P14, P17 and P21. This is the "support" outcome of the pre-registration (Pvalb below 0.6 at P14 and at or above 0.8 at P28).
2. **The unchecked sentence is corrected, at transcript level:** Pvalb transcript in whole mouse brain is near zero until at least P3, about half of plateau at P14 and near plateau at P28; the deposit has no P7, P10, P17 or P21, so where inside P14 to P28 it crosses 0.5 is not measured. Protein is not measured.
3. **Placing the stage confound (ADV-068, ADV-070):** at P17 the three components are not equal. Chloride transporter transcripts are already near plateau at P14 (Slc12a5 0.78, Slc12a2 0.76; the Slc12a5 / Slc12a2 ratio 5.65 at P14 against 5.28 at P28 and 5.69 at P63: the ratio switch is complete between P3, 3.12, and P14); myelin transcripts are mostly between 0.6 and 0.8 at P14 and above plateau at P28 (Mbp 0.77, Plp1 0.60, Mog 0.73, Mag 0.82, Mobp 0.47; at P28 1.04 to 1.17); Pvalb is the one still rising after P14. **So the PV component of the stage confound is the live one at P17**, the chloride-transporter component is small at P17 in bulk transcript, and the myelin component is late in its rise. Under straight-line interpolation between P14 and P28 (an assumption, not a measurement) Pvalb at P17 is about 0.56 of plateau and a two-day developmental lag moves it by about 0.06 of plateau (about 11 percent of its P17 value), which is within the P14 replicate spread (0.36 to 0.60).
4. **For the assay ages:** keep P14, P17 and P21 (all inside the PV rising phase; the knockout animals are reported to die between 3 and 4 postnatal weeks, so P28 is not available in the Synapsin-Cre knockout). Because Pvalb changes by roughly 0.03 of plateau per day in this window, **litter-matched controls sampled on the same day are required**, and the age of each animal (not the litter) is the unit. An extra early point (P10) would show where the rise starts but is not needed to separate loss from delayed onset.

## 2. Pre-registered predictions against the result
1. Pvalb below 0.6 at P14 and at or above 0.8 at P28: **held** (0.47; 0.89; one P14 replicate at exactly 0.60).
2. Gad1 and Gad2 at or above 0.7 of plateau by P14: **half held** (Gad2 0.98; **Gad1 0.65 failed**).
3. Slc12a5 / Slc12a2 ratio at P14 at least twice its P3 value: **failed narrowly** (5.65 against 3.12: 1.8 times); the ratio is at plateau by P14.
4. Myelin genes below 0.5 at P14: **failed** (only Mobp, 0.47; the others 0.60 to 0.92): the myelin transcript rise precedes the PV rise, so the earlier expectation that the three overlap is wrong for P14 to P28.
5. Wwox, no prediction: a steady rise (0.66 at P3, 0.75 at P14, 0.94 at P28). **A stage difference also moves Wwox itself**, so in a conditional deletion the amount of protein to be removed differs by age (one more reason the deletion completeness of row 1 is read per animal).

## 3. Human (D2: BrainSpan RNA-seq gene RPKM, neocortical samples, 42 donors, plateau = the 11 donors aged 10 years or more)
PVALB is near zero throughout gestation and rises in the first years: donor fractions of plateau 0.09 to 0.61 at 4 to 10 months, 0.06 to 0.42 at 1 to 3 years, 0.4 to 2.2 from 4 years (donors 4 to 18 years: 1.8, 2.1, 1.5, 1.1, 0.8, 0.6, 0.4); SLC12A5 0.02 to 0.17 before 24 weeks, 0.26 at 37 weeks, 0.17 to 0.68 at 4 months, 0.4 to 1.1 by 1 year; MBP and MOBP near zero before birth, 0.16 to 0.55 at 4 months, 0.5 to 2.8 from 2 years on. **Donor-to-donor spread is large** (a 2-year donor shows GAD1 at 0.04 and MBP at 2.8 of plateau; one 25-week donor with PVALB 0.40 is an outlier), few donors per age, and a plateau from 11 donors. What this supports: the human PV rise is **protracted over years after birth**, not a two-week event, and the order (GABA synthesis and chloride transporter before PVALB, myelin around birth to years) matches the mouse order; **mouse days cannot be converted to human ages by this deposit**, and nothing here bears on any patient's age.

## 4. What the deposits cannot tell
Transcript, not protein (PV protein and the perineuronal net lag the transcript by an unknown interval); whole brain or forebrain as labelled (not neocortical layers, not the slice region); bulk tissue, so a rising Pvalb mixes cell number, cell maturation and level per cell (it cannot separate loss from delay: that is what the lineage-reporter measurement of row 6 does); one outbred strain and sexes pooled as the sheet gives them; **no P7, P10, P17 or P21** in D1, so the P17 and P21 statements are interpolations; the knockout itself is not in these data. The preparation of the earlier P17 bulk deposits (3 knockout against 4 control animals, neuron-targeted deletion) is a different tissue from D1.
