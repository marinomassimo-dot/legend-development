# ADV-147 result: WWOX transcript detection by lineage in a human fetal cortex single-cell atlas (SCI window X, 2026-11-21; hypothesis level; no dose; reference genotype class only; donors at class level; not medical advice)

Pre-registration: `PREREGISTRATION_ADV147_WWOX_FETAL_LINEAGE_SCI_20261121.md` and `ADDENDUM1_LINEAGE_MAP_AND_ROUTES_SCI_20261121.md` (lineage map, measure and route results written before any gene value was read). Scope, binding (ADV): this reports **WWOX transcript detection in a lineage and nothing stronger**; expression in a lineage does not establish, and a lower detection fraction would not reject, a cell-autonomous function. This step covers only what `analysis/wwox_subfield_celltype_expression_20260922.md` could not reach: **human, fetal, inhibitory (MGE, CGE and striatal) lineage and progenitors**; that note supports "interneurons not below excitatory" in the mouse adult from a review's description of DropViz (second hand) and records the Human Protein Atlas, Allen, Hipposeq and GTEx per-cell routes as egress-blocked then.

## Answer first
1. **WWOX transcript is detected in the human fetal inhibitory lineage**: 9.4 percent of inhibitory neurons (93 of 985 cells, Wilson 95 percent interval 7.8 to 11.4) and 21.5 percent of inhibitory progenitors (68 of 317, 17.3 to 26.3), against 10.8 percent of excitatory neurons (162 of 1,494, 9.4 to 12.5), 23.1 percent of cortical radial glia (150 of 649, 20.0 to 26.5) and 13.7 percent of cortical intermediate progenitors (37 of 270). Detection in the inhibitory neurons is **not below half** of that in excitatory neurons by any of the three measures below; the pre-registered conflict threshold (below half) is **not met**, so there is **no conflict** with the mouse adult map or with the AES 1.019 statement on this atlas.
2. **The pre-registered matched-abundance step could not be run:** only 2 of the 20 pool genes (HPRT1, GUSB) fall within plus or minus 25 percent of WWOX's dataset-wide detection (14.2 percent), fewer than the 3 the rule requires. By the rule the atlas is **UNMATCHED** and no low or zero detection is interpreted. The computed ratio against those two controls (inhibitory over excitatory 1.93) is shown for completeness and is **not used**. Here the detection is positive and not low, so the rule's withholding does not bear on the verdict above.
3. **Detection is depth-dependent and the inhibitory neurons are the shallowest group** (median genes detected per cell 1,907 against 2,955 in excitatory neurons and 3,744 in radial glia); in every group the WWOX detection rises with depth. **Post hoc, labelled, not pre-registered:** stratified on terciles of genes detected, the Mantel-Haenszel risk ratio of inhibitory neurons against excitatory neurons is **1.49** (Greenland-Robins 95 percent interval 1.07 to 2.06, from the blind recompute; the crude ratio is 0.87, so the direction depends on the depth adjustment and the strata overlap is thin). This is the substitute for the matched controls; it points the same way (not below).
4. **Within each lineage detection falls with neuronal maturity in this atlas:** late excitatory cluster nEN-late 4.4 percent and the youngest inhibitory neuron clusters nIN1 to nIN4 0 to 5.6 percent against 12 to 14 percent in the cortical inhibitory neuron clusters IN-CTX-MGE and IN-STR and 25 to 46 percent in the dividing radial glia (RG-div1, RG-div2) and MGE-div. The depth confound is not removed at cluster level; read it as a pattern to retest, not a finding.
5. **One atlas only:** 4,261 cells, the Nowakowski 2017 dataset (cortex-dev, ages about 5.85 to 37 gestational weeks pooled in the metadata, 48 labelled clusters, 3,938 assigned to a lineage). Polioudakis 2019 was not found as a UCSC dataset by name; the CELLxGENE per-stage queries failed (route result, below). **A second independent atlas has not been read.**

The human fetal inhibitory lineage was the unreachable part of that note; this fills it at the transcript-detection level for one atlas.

## Blind check
A delegate of a different model (requested `opus`, briefed at effort medium, not read back) recomputed everything from the cell metadata, the extracted expression rows and the per-cell depth file, **without opening my scripts or outputs**, from the lineage map and rules in plain words. **Every number reproduces exactly**: 3,938 assigned cells (323 excluded: 132 blank label, U1 to U4 80, 27, 28, 56); WWOX dataset-wide 558 of 3,938 (0.1417); per-group k, n, fractions and Wilson intervals as in the table; **only two controls qualify** (GUSB just inside the window at ratio 0.753, HPRT1 at 0.935; the next nearest, HMBS and SDHA, fall outside); the same tercile cuts (2,262 and 3,160) and counts; Mantel-Haenszel risk ratio **1.487** and the crude ratio **0.871**. It added a Greenland-Robins 95 percent interval for the stratified ratio, **1.07 to 2.06** (not pre-registered), and two cautions that I adopt: the crude and stratified ratios point to opposite sides of 1 (0.87 against 1.49), so the direction depends on adjusting for depth; and the overlap within strata is thin, because 77 percent of the inhibitory neurons (759 of 985) sit in the shallowest tercile and only 17 are in the deepest. The reading "not below half" holds in both; "above" rests on thin strata.

## Limits
One atlas, pooled gestational ages, droplet or plate depth heterogeneity (detection rises with genes detected in every group); detection above 0 in a TPM matrix is a crude measure and a zero is a non-detection, not an absence; WWOX is lowly expressed per cell, so per-cluster fractions of 3 to 14 percent are not distinguishable by eye from dropout; the lineage map is mine, fixed before reading values; cluster labels come from the original authors; the depth stratification and the cluster-level readings are post hoc. Donors were not tabulated (class level only). Nothing here shows a WWOX function in any cell type.
