# RP-040 result: is the low cerebellar WWOX after neonatal ICV AAV9-hSynI a platform property or specific to Obeid 2026? (SCI window V, 2026-11-21; hypothesis level; no dose, no recommendation for or against any use of any agent in any person; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

## Answer first
1. **Verdict by the registered rule: not reached. The deciding rows are not independently verified, so the result is a PROVISIONAL LEAN toward PLATFORM-LOW, carried as a lead and not as a result.** Four papers meet the qualifying-row definition (mouse, neonatal ICV or neonatal intracisternal AAV9, a synapsin promoter, a per-region read including cerebellum and cortex with a denominator and n), from four senior-author groups; **only two of them print numbers for both regions** and only one of those two was read beyond its abstract.
2. **The rows that carry numbers.** (a) Voronin 2024 (AAV9.Syn.hCDKL5, ICV at PND0, CDKL5 protein as percent of wild type, Table 2, producer reading): cerebellum over cortex 0.77 at 3e9 vg/g, 0.41 at 3e10, 0.33 and 0.25 at 3e11 (two studies), 0.24 at 5e11 (ratios of printed means; absolute cerebellar values 1.0, 0.9, 10, 18, 33 percent of wild type against cortex 1.3, 2.2, 30, 73, 136; the ratio falls with dose; the text says the cerebellum rose less than hippocampus or cortex and that ICV beat intracisternal "except in the cerebellum"). (b) McLean 2014 (rAAV9 hSYN1-GFP, neonatal ICV, harvest at 6 weeks, abstract only): cerebellum 29.6 ± 2.3 percent of neurons against motor cortex 43.5 ± 3.3, ratio 0.68 (denominator, n and age of injection not reached).
3. **Applying the rule as written (two thirds of ratio-giving rows at or below 0.5):** 4 of 6 rows, exactly at the threshold, **driven by one paper's dose series (5 of the 6 rows)**; counted by paper, one of two. **The prediction (cerebellum lower than cortex in most rows) is not contradicted by any row; the rejecting observation (a row with cerebellum at or above cortex in a qualifying stratum) was not found.** Not found is not "absent": two qualifying papers (Repudi 2021, Lukashchuk 2016) have the per-region values only as bars in a figure, which nobody read as numbers.
4. **Independence.** Repudi 2021 comes from the same research programme as the benchmark paper (Obeid 2026): it is not an independent test of "platform against this paper". No qualifying paper from an unrelated group has a numeric cerebellum-to-cortex ratio read from the main text except Voronin 2024.
5. **Verification status of the deciding rows (route result, not a negative).** The verifier did not reach Voronin 2024 by five routes (PMC page: reCAPTCHA; Europe PMC XML: HTTP 500; E-utilities: front matter only, the publisher forbids XML full text; PubMed full-text tool: empty; Cell Press: 403), and I did not either (PMC page: reCAPTCHA; Europe PMC PDF render: challenge); the producer reached the PMC page once without a challenge. **Table 2 therefore rests on one reading.** An internal inconsistency of the source as the producer transcribed it, seen independently by the verifier from the producer's own numbers: the text gives an average of 61 percent of wild type in cortex at 3e11 while the Table 2 rows give a mean of 51.5 (hippocampus 78 and cerebellum 14 agree). The verifier confirmed McLean's six numbers and "neonatal i.c.v. of rAAV9 with the hSYN1 promoter", harvest at 6 weeks; Repudi's route (ICV at P0, 2 × 10^10 per hemisphere, AAV9-hSynI) and that no per-region numbers are printed there (only "between 60 and 70 percent" pooled, n = 3).
6. **Context from other strata, not counted** (ubiquitous promoter, other age or capsid; producer tabulation): Hammond 2017 AAV9 with a ubiquitous promoter, GFP-positive cells, 3 weeks: cerebellum 11.3 against cortex 39.1 (0.29), 6 weeks 5.9 against 6.4 (0.92); Guo 2024, "less expression in the midbrain and cerebellum" than neocortex (MeP229 and JeT promoters, qualitative); Norman 2026, no FMRP signal in cerebellum or thalamus at the high dose, cortex at wild-type level. These point the same way qualitatively; they are a separate stratum and move no count.

## Which consumer changes, and which does not
* **Design brief, expression-range line (as a stated lead, not a measured range):** for a neonatal ICV AAV9 neuronal-promoter gene-addition design, state a per-region expression range and expect the cerebellum to be the lowest region; the two sources that print numbers (the benchmark's own high-dose lanes: cerebellum 0.6, 0.1 to 0.5 and about 2 times wild type at P90, P180 and P240 to P300 against cortex 2.0, 4.7 and 9.5; Voronin 2024; McLean 2014) agree in direction, and the ratio in Voronin 2024 depends on dose.
* **Ataxia or cerebellar endpoint route: no change claimed.** Whether the low cerebellar level matters for any endpoint is a function question this table does not touch; the cerebellar mechanism thread (2026-09) is not reopened.
* **RP-037 and the suppressor-tRNA row:** unchanged. The benchmark's "lowest HD value at P180 equals the knockout lane" is now read as consistent with a reach property of the platform in the cerebellum, not as evidence that about 10 percent of wild type is a survival floor.

## Limits
