## Fixed before reading: promote, readout, park
* **Candidate with a priority experiment** only if BOTH: (a) glial state is documented to rise BEFORE the first seizure (or before death) in at least one held line, so it is not shown to be only a consequence; AND (b) there is evidence under the Q2 rule that modifying glial or inflammatory state changes seizures or survival without neuronal rescue.
* **Readout like glucose (6.9), not an arm** if timing cannot be placed from held material (ADV's stop rule), or if (a) holds without (b).
* **Parked, with its reason** if gliosis first appears at or after seizure onset in every line that reports timing AND Q2 finds nothing by the rule above. Neuronal AAV9 normalising GFAP together with everything else is the pattern a downstream consequence predicts and is not evidence of independence.
* **Deliberately unfavourable reading:** a seizing germline null at 2 weeks with gliosis says nothing about order; gliosis follows seizures in most epilepsy models; microglial activation in a developing brain also carries clearance and myelination functions, so inhibiting it can be harmful as well as neutral.
