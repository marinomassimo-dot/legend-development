# ADV-104: astrocytic gliosis (GFAP) and microglial or inflammatory state as a second non-neuronal consequence axis for the broad objective (SCI, 2026-11-20; hypothesis level; no dose, no schedule, no recommendation for or against any use of any agent in any person; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

The question is `assignments/ADV-104.md` (landed on main as 6dadabeb1 while this note was being written; its four questions match the message I worked from). Texts read from held bytes: Hussain 2019 (PMID 30290271), Repudi 2021 gene therapy (34747138), Repudi 2021 Brain (33914858, operator-supplied PDF, two-column text), Obeid 2026 (42422765, JATS and its supplement PDF `mmc1.pdf`), Cheng 2020 (32000863). External literature: five searches and abstract-level reads, plus my own passage-level readings of 2026-11-20 for ganaxolone (PMID 31332963) and the preterm-rabbit and cuprizone GSK-3 papers (`../gsk3_shared_premise_sci_20261120/`).

## Answer first
1. **Timing cannot be placed before seizure onset in any held line, so by the registered stop rule the axis is carried as a READOUT beside glucose (6.9), not as an arm.** Every glial measurement held is at P14 to P20; the earliest documented seizures are at P9 (S-KO) and "within a few days after birth" (germline null); no held text measures GFAP, Iba1 or a cytokine before the first seizure in any line.
2. **Independent modifiability is not shown in the Wwox lines and is contested in the nearest well-studied model.** In the held Wwox texts the only manipulation that moves glial state is neuronal restoration, which moves everything together (the pattern a downstream consequence predicts). Glial-restricted deletion (astrocyte, GFAP-Cre; oligodendrocyte, Olig2-Cre) gives **no** phenotype (growth, weight, survival, n = 8 to 11 per arm). Externally, one report that wild-type microglia replacement arrests disease in a Rett model (Derecki 2012) was **not replicated** in three models (Wang 2015), and a third study found microglial Mecp2 status had little effect.
3. **Neither candidate agent in HE's sheets is a glia-state agent with pup brain exposure** (ganaxolone: adult-rat evidence, a microglial-clearance effect in an adult focal demyelination model with astrocytosis unaffected; clemastine: an OPC-directed muscarinic route, neonatal mouse plasma level but no brain concentration). There is no sheet for a microglia- or inflammation-directed agent, and none was created.
4. **Marginal utility: low as an arm, free as a readout.** GFAP and Iba1 are already endpoints of the gene-addition benchmark, so scoring them in the same slides of any pilot arm costs no animals. What would promote it: a pre-onset glial measurement (S-KO sampled at P5 to P8, before the P9 seizures) plus a replicated glial-state manipulation that moves seizures or survival in a neuronal-primary model. What would park it: gliosis first appearing at or after seizure onset with Q2 still empty (the current state, minus the pre-onset sampling nobody has done).

Net: one positive report, one failed replication and one nuanced third in the closest model, plus two myelination-injury models (preterm rabbit, cuprizone) whose glial modification does not transfer by their own design; nothing in a Wwox line; nothing positive in an epileptic encephalopathy model with a seizure endpoint. The Q2 rule is **not met**.

## Q3. Interventions with pup-compatible exposure facts in HE's existing sheets
* **Ganaxolone** (BATCH 3): adult-rat pharmacokinetic and intravenous titles; no brain concentration; no juvenile or pup exposure; the microglial-clearance record is no longer "unread": my passage-level reading finds no exposure, no pup and no stage readout, and a debris-clearance mechanism with no counterpart in the knockout cortex.
* **Clemastine** (BATCH 6, with the 8ca6dfa22 correction): qualitative detection in juvenile rat brain by MALDI imaging (PMID 29584714; no concentration) and a plasma Cmax of 44.0 ng/mL (total, steady state, neonatal mouse; Odell 2024). Its myelin rationale is OPC-directed, not a glial-inflammation mechanism.
* **No sheet** for a microglia-directed or inflammation-directed agent (CSF1R inhibitors, minocycline-type, IL-1 pathway). None created, per instruction.

## Q4. Marginal utility against the two broad candidates and the metabolic axis
* **Against the oligodendrocyte class and the non-lithium GSK-3 candidate:** the glial axis shares tissue and endpoints with them (GFAP and Iba1 were rescued with myelination in the same animals), so it adds a readout, not an independent target; the two broad candidates act on a lineage whose arrest the held data show (mature oligodendrocyte transcripts lower in all four P17 contrasts).
* **Against the metabolic axis (ADV-101):** both are consequences with no pre-onset measurement and no independent manipulation in a Wwox line; the metabolic axis has a measured mechanism hint (insulin, glucose uptake) and an exposure-documented class (PDK inhibition) and the glial axis has neither, so it ranks behind.
* **ADV's three tests of "more promising", applied:** a pup-enterable intervention: none (no sheet has a pup brain concentration for a glia-state agent); a consequence modifiable independently of the neuronal lesion: not shown in a Wwox line and contested in the nearest model; a test cheaper than the others: **yes, and this is the whole case for the readout**, because GFAP, Iba1 and cytokines can be scored in the same tissue as any pilot arm and, for timing, in ordinary pre-onset sections.
* **What "more promising" would mean:** (1) a per-animal glial series in the S-KO cortex or the germline null at P5, P7, P9 and P12 (GFAP, Iba1, one or two cytokines), showing a rise before the first seizure; (2) a replicated glial-state-specific manipulation moving seizures or survival in a neuronal-primary developmental model.
* **Deliberately unfavourable reading:** gliosis follows seizures in most epilepsy models and here it is measured only after them; neuronal AAV9 normalising it is what a consequence predicts; microglial phagocytosis also clears myelin debris and prunes synapses, so suppressing it could harm myelination, as the ganaxolone and Dravet records suggest; the held cortical reactive-astrocyte signal is a bulk transcript at P17 that flips with adjustment, so composition is not excluded; **its sign stays "a marker of the phenotype"** because no held timing places it before seizure onset.
