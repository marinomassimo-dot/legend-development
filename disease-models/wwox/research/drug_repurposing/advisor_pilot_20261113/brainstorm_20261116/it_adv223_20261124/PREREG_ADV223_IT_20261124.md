## Part 2: pooled-direction sweep
* **Surfaces:** `claim_registry_current`, `working_model_current`, and the Summary, Evidence boundary, Transferability, Evidence depth, Note and Reading-update fields of `paper_registry_current`, read by record with `registry_records.py get --theme TERM --source S --json` (the paper registry is never grepped).
* **Mechanism terms (the assignment's list, fixed):** redox; mitochondri*; ROS and reactive oxygen; oxidative; glycoly*; metabolic; apoptosis and apoptotic; ERK; JNK.
* **A candidate sentence** contains a mechanism term and at least one of: (i) **system mix**: a cancer or non-neural word (cancer, tumour or tumor, neoplas*, cell line, carcinoma, glioblastoma, HCC, bladder, breast, prostate) and a neural or organism word (neural, neuron, brain, organoid, fly, Drosophila, mouse, patient, child) in the same sentence; (ii) **direction mix**: a loss word (loss, knockdown, knockout, deficien*, depletion) and a gain word (gain, overexpress*, re-expression, restor*, rescue, add-back) in the same sentence; (iii) **undirected verb**: modulat*, regulat*, affect*, alter*, dysregulat*, perturb*, implicated, linked to, associated with, with **no** direction word (increase*, decrease*, higher, lower, raised, reduced, elevated, impaired, up, down) in the same sentence.
* **Score for choosing the (at most 15) sentences to label:** class (i) 3, class (ii) 3, class (iii) 1; plus 1 when the sentence sits in the claim registry or working model rather than a paper record. Ties by file order. The count of candidates at each stage and the number not labelled are reported.
* **Labels:** *pooled and unflagged* (the sentence joins directions or systems and neither it nor its own record states the difference); *pooled and flagged* (the record or sentence states gain versus loss, or the system mix: an explicit transferability tag with a stated reason, "cancer line", "not neural", an opposing source named); *not a pooling* (the sentence reports one direction in one system, or the mix is a list of independent findings each with its own direction). A transferability tag alone flags a system mix, not a direction mix.
* **Opposing sources:** for each labelled sentence the same tool is asked once for the mechanism term with `gain` or `overexpress*`, and what the registry already holds is named, not searched beyond that.
* **Predictions:** pooled and unflagged will be a minority (at most 4 of 15); most will sit in paper-record summary lines, not in claims; at least 5 will be *not a pooling* (word hits, not poolings). Falsifier: no unflagged sentence found among the labelled means the class is absent at sentence level in the registry, and the report says so.
* **Limits:** a sentence splitter and a word list find candidates, not poolings; Italian-language record text is not covered by the English terms; the unlabelled remainder is counted, not read; **nothing is edited**; proposed wording changes go as a list for the batch route.
