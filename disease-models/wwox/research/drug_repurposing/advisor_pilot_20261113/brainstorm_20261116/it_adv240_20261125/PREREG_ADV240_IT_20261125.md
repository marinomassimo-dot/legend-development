Companion files: `PREREG_ADV240_TIME_UTC.txt` and `PREREG_ADV240_SHA256.txt` (digest of this file). Step 1 (`STEP1_SERIES_LIST_IT_20261125.md`) was written first from PubMed metadata and the bucket file listing only; **no supplement table had been opened at freeze time**, and the abstracts printed in the metadata were scanned for yield sentences only.

## Rules (frozen)
1. **Inclusion:** a series listed IN in step 1 (DEE, epileptic encephalopathy or early-onset epilepsy cohort, solved cases reported, a table-like supplement present in the PMC bucket). No series is added or dropped after opening the files; a series whose supplement turns out to hold no per-case or per-gene table is **kept in the denominator list as "no table"** and counts nothing.
2. **WWOX row:** a row (or cell) of a supplement table whose gene field is WWOX, or a text cell naming WWOX as the gene of a reported case. A row naming WWOX only in a list of candidate or screened genes is not a case. Read two hits of every count before reporting it (the row and its neighbours), and compare by PMID, DOI and title.
3. **Denominators, two kinds, never mixed:** (a) *solved total as the series states it in its own text* (abstract or results), the headline denominator; (b) *rows with a reported pathogenic or likely pathogenic variant in the supplement table*, only where the table makes that countable. A WWOX count over (b) is reported only if (b) is countable from the table.
4. **Overlap:** a case that two series both report (re-reported patient) counts once; I look for it by genotype string and age/sex in the same table only if two series both name WWOX.
5. **Panel subtotal:** only where a table gives one (by panel or by test type); otherwise stated as "not given".
6. **Pooling:** a pooled share is the sum of WWOX rows over the sum of solved totals across included series with a countable table; the unit is the case, not the series; series with "no table" are excluded from the pool and listed.

## Predictions (frozen)
* **P1:** across all IN series with a countable table, the pooled WWOX share of solved cases is **under 0.5 percent** (one case in 200 or fewer).
* **P2:** at least half of the IN series open to a table with **zero WWOX rows**.
* **P3:** at least one IN series has no per-case or per-gene table in its supplement (a text or figure document only).
* **P4:** the one-in-1285 Swedish figure of ADV-228 is of the same order as the pooled share (within a factor of five either way).
* **Rejecting observations:** P1 fails if the pooled share is 0.5 percent or more; P2 fails if more than half have at least one WWOX row; P3 fails if every IN series has a per-gene table; P4 fails outside a factor of five.

## What the result will and will not say
A pooled share over series selected by title and supplement format, not by sampling; solved cases, not all cases; WWOX mostly enters severe-infantile series as a recessive or CNV diagnosis that panels of some years did not include, so a zero in an older panel series is a panel-content finding, not a rate. The result is a **base with its sources and limits**, not an incidence.
