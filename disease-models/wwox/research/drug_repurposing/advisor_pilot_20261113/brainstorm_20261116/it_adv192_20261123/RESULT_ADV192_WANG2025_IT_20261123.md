## 1. Answer first
**NARROW, as a test-design reading only.** In the one tracked OPC-restricted mouse model of this kind, the phenotype was found through region-quantified myelin histology, electron microscopy, oligodendroglial counts, slice electrophysiology and behaviour. The primary reports NO body weight, growth or survival for that mutant at all, so it cannot show that coarse readouts would have caught it; the indirect hints (below) point the other way. Therefore the Wwox glial-driver comparisons (weight, survival, gross appearance) constrain gross phenotypes only, and a missing endpoint is a missing test: they leave regional or stage-specific OPC contributions unresolved. One myelin readout exists on the Olig2-Cre line, an unquantified MBP image at P17; it is qualitative and covers one age.

The CLOSE condition (the primary shows the phenotype through coarse readouts too) is **not met**. The NARROW reason: the model is another gene (a chromatin scaffold, not WWOX), an inducible OPC driver given at P4 to P8 rather than a constitutive Olig2 knock-in, and the OPC-only design says nothing about neurons.

**Regional specificity.** The deficit is graded by region (largest in mPFC). The regional chromatin differences between mPFC and hippocampus (ATAC-qPCR, ChIP-qPCR, RT-qPCR) come from wild-type OPCs, n = 3 biological replicates, one t-test per gene, with no genotype-by-region test; the knockout RNA-seq lists unique and shared differentially expressed genes per region.

**OPC-neuron synapse: measured directly or inferred.** Directly measured on the OPC side: Homer1, gephyrin, vGAT and vGLUT1 puncta per PDGFRα area by super-resolution (n = 5 mice); GABRG3 puncta down 30.54 ± 6.55% (n = 5 mice); immunogold postsynaptic-density length (n = 22 against 19 postsynaptic elements, elements as the unit, animals not stated); OPC calcium imaging (76 cells from 3 mice against 129 cells from 3 mice, cells pooled). Functional OPC recording is thin: AMPA/NMDA ratio from n = 3 OPCs per group, described by the authors as 'a potential reduction', and the authors state they could record only three OPCs per group. Inferred: the BAF155 to synaptic-gene link rests on ChIP-seq in cultured committed OPCs (3219 genes at day 1; enrichment of synapse terms) and ChIP-qPCR and qPCR at n = 3 biological replicates. The text itself says in vitro the knockout OPCs differentiate normally (Fig 3F), which is why the authors propose a non-cell-autonomous or input-dependent mechanism; that is a proposal, not a test.

**Neuron-specific control or rescue: none.** No neuron-restricted deletion, no rescue, no pharmacological block of synaptic input. An OPC-only perturbation cannot show neurons are not needed; kept separate from the Wwox side below, where the neuronal deletion alone does reproduce the coarse phenotype.

**Replication.** Units are mice for histology, neurons for slice electrophysiology, elements for immunoEM, cells for calcium, biological replicates (animal pooling not stated) for ChIP and qPCR. No litter-level unit is stated. The reporting summary states that no sample-size calculation was used and that the knockout and littermate controls were all used in the behavioural experiments.

**Indirect coarse hints (not reported results).** The behavioural cohort reached 6 to 8 weeks and the open-field total distance did not differ. Both are consistent with a mutant that lives to adulthood without gross motor failure; neither is a survival or weight measurement, and attrition is not reported.

## 4. Decision
1. **A missing endpoint is a missing test.** The Wwox coarse null does not address a regional-myelin or OPC-synapse phenotype, because the primary reports no coarse measure for its driver and shows only that regional myelin and lineage endpoints DETECTED a phenotype under a separate OPC-directed perturbation; it does not show those finer readouts were indispensable, nor that weight and survival were normal (unreported for that driver). 'No effect on weight, growth and survival' is what the null shows. The neuronal-deletion result stays POSITIVE evidence for a neuronal contribution.
2. **Not a null that was never tested finer.** The unquantified P17 MBP image is a finer readout. A Wang-size effect in the most affected region (the bar reading puts the mPFC loss near three quarters at P14) is the kind an image comparison could reveal; the 20 to 50% regional reductions elsewhere are not settled by a sagittal image. This is a reading of a bar chart, not a printed effect size.
3. **Consequence for RP-017 (ADV owns the line):** keep an OPC-intrinsic branch with a named readout set, no new arm and no assay commission, taken from what detected the phenotype in the adjacent model (precedents, not imported defaults; ages and behavioural assays likewise): region-quantified MBP area at P14 (cortex, hippocampus, corpus callosum, striatum, cerebellum); EM myelinated-axon number and g-ratio; CC1, OLIG2 and PDGFRα counts to separate a differentiation block from lineage loss; slice sEPSC and sIPSC frequency with the amplitude and miniature controls; adult social and grooming behaviour; and an OPC-stage recombination check for the driver. Where a Wwox glial-restricted line is ever measured again, the neuronal deletion is the comparator, because it alone reproduces the coarse phenotype.
4. **Rival kept explicit:** in Wwox the neuronal deletion reproduces growth, weight and lethality by itself, so coarse readouts are dominated by neurons and could hide a smaller OPC contribution; equally, the absence of any coarse signal in the glial lines is expected whether or not an OPC contribution exists. The BAF155 model does not decide between these.
