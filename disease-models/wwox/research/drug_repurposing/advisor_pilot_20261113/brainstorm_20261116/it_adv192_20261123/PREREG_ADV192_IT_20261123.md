## 4. Predictions fixed before the body (each is falsifiable by the text)
* P1. The OPC-restricted BAF155 phenotype is NOT visible in coarse growth, weight or survival: the paper's headline readouts are regional myelin, OPC-neuron synaptic gene expression and behaviour. (Rejected if the primary reports body weight or survival differences, or lethality.)
* P2. The driver is a committed-OPC Cre (an NG2- or Pdgfra-type line or a similar one), not an early Olig2-lineage Cre, so the compartment differs from Olig2-Cre in Repudi. (Rejected if the driver is Olig2-Cre or acts in neural stem cells.)
* P3. The OPC-neuron synapse axis is inferred from transcripts and chromatin data in sorted or bulk tissue, with at most one direct functional or morphological readout. (Rejected if synapses are measured directly by electrophysiology or imaging in most of the claim.)
* P4. The unit of replication is the animal per genotype, with sections or cells pooled inside animals in at least one panel.
* P5. No neuron-specific control or rescue exists.
