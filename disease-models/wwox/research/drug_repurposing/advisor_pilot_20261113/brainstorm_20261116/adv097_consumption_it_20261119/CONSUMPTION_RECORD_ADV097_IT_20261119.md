## 0. The three distinctions every short summary of the simulation must carry
Use these three sentences (or their meaning) wherever the bisect study is summarised, so the short form does not drop them:
1. **Normalisation (HE, 2026-11-19).** The crossing strength s compares arms on different scales (factor = multiplier ** s with a different listed multiplier per arm); d-APV and the NMDAR pore blocker perturb the same parameter and tie on the scale-free multiplier axis (S-KO: -18 percent and -18 percent), so "d-APV before the pore blocker" in the published order and in registered prediction P1 is imposed by the coefficients, not found; comparisons ACROSS arms depend on the chosen normalisation and are not evidence of class superiority.
2. **Parametric sensitivity is not structural alternatives.** HE's wrapper varies parameters of one circuit structure (and a few joint scenarios); whether a class order survives a different circuit structure is not tested by it.
3. **A model step is not pharmacology.** Crossing strength is a model step, not a dose or a concentration; mapping a class to a model parameter is not a compound prediction; the exposure-effect relation per agent is SCI's choice with explicit assumptions.
