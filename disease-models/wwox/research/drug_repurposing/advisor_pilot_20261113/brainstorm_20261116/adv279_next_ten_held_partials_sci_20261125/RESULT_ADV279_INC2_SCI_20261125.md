### 30266908 (Guiberson 2018; STXBP1 Munc18-1, HEK293T and yeast): STRICT
**Class of correction:** a value carried from one panel while its sibling panel disagrees.
**Consumer:** card A01, `DR-A01-008`: "Mutant total protein about 1.8 (sorbitol) and about 2.35 (trehalose) fold of control, significance marks printed".
**Held files:** Supplementary Figure 12 (page 19 of MOESM1, PDF sha256 prefix 26434c3351f4c0f8). Panel a, G544D, sorbitol 200 mM: about 177 percent, one asterisk, three points about 230, 164, 132. Panel b, same cell line (HEK293T), same 200 mM, G544D: about 103 percent, unmarked, points about 112, 103, 95. Legend of b: "Same as in (a), except that HEK293T cells were transfected with plasmids expressing WT or mutant Munc18-1 in absence or presence of 5 mM 4-phenylbutyrate, 200 mM sorbitol, or 200 mM trehalose." Trehalose agrees between the panels (about 237 and 235). In panel a, 400 mM sorbitol and trehalose give about 0.8 and 0.9 on this mutant (reader only).
**Proposed wording:** "trehalose 200 mM raises G544D protein in both panels (about 2.35 fold); sorbitol 200 mM gives about 1.8 fold in one panel and about 1.0 in the other (n = 3 each), so no sorbitol effect is established; in HEK293T, 400 mM of either gave no rise."
**Bearing:** the "osmolyte arm" precedent rests on trehalose alone.
