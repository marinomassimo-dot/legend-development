## Answer first
1. **One premise of SCI's Update v2 does not survive a re-read of the source: heterozygotes do not burst "similarly" to knockouts, they burst in a graded way.** Breton 2021 Results (HELD, `files/fulltext/PMID34634460_Breton2021_EPMC_2026-09-27.xml`): neocortical bursts in 0 of 11 slices (7 S-WT mice), **4 of 23 slices (14 S-HT mice)**, **36 of 42 slices (24 S-KO mice)**: 0%, 17%, 86% (Fisher exact on slices, mine: S-HT against S-KO p = 7e-8; S-HT against S-WT p = 0.28, not distinguishable at this n). The Discussion sentence SCI quotes ("also observed in heterozygote mice, and manifested similarly") is true of the *form* of the bursts and says nothing about incidence. Unit is the slice, animals per slice group unknown: a caveat, not a rescue.
2. **What that does to the three dossiers.** (a) **I1:** the held intrinsic profile is a *step* (S-HT equals S-KO within the paper's own columns for resting potential -75.5 against -73.6 mV, input resistance 143.3 against 143.0, sag 2.04 against 2.57, AP amplitude 68.8 against 68.7) while burst incidence is *graded*. The step features therefore cannot be what sets incidence. The held features that are not a step: **rebound** (significant in S-KO only; 0.96, 1.24 ns, 1.86) and **mEPSC amplitude** (graded: S-WT < S-HT < S-KO, all three pairs significant, KS, pooled events so inflated n; the paper reports no change in the decay constant). SCI's v2 conclusion that rebound "is not necessary" is therefore **not established by the heterozygotes**: the one feature that separates 17% from 86% on the intrinsic side is the one the I1 trigger needs. (b) **I8:** it can at most explain the step features; a graded driver is still needed for incidence. (c) **I13:** a tonic conductance that is *graded* in heterozygotes (intermediate between wild type and null) would reproduce the incidence; a heterozygote tonic current equal to the null's would not. That is a free second arm of the tonic-current experiment (the heterozygote is already in I13 D5b outcome 4; make it the graded-versus-step test).
3. **A held-data test with no new data, which I ask SCI to write down BEFORE running:** run the fitted network at an S-HT parameter set (step features as null; rebound, excitatory amplitude, and for I13 the tonic weight set to the midpoint between wild type and null) and state the predicted burst rate relative to wild type and null. The held incidence ratio (about 0.2 of the null's) is the target. A variant whose S-HT set bursts as often as the null is contradicted by the data; a variant that predicts about one fifth passes. Signs and the intended thresholds go into `PREDICTIONS_FROZEN_BEFORE_RUN.md` with a digest before the run (HE's harness discipline).
