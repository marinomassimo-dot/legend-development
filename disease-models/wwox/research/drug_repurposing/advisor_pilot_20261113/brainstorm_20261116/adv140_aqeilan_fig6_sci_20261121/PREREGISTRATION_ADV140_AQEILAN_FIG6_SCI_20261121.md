## Question

## Rules for "comparison made" (fixed now)
* **MADE:** a statistical test is named with its result (test, p or significance mark with the compared groups defined in the legend) or a quantified side-by-side with n for each group is printed; the groups are named (genotype, line, age).
* **VISUAL:** a statement such as "appears", "seems", "resembles", "trend", or a difference read from images or traces with no quantity and no test.
* **NOT PRINTED:** the figure shows both groups but no statement or mark says they were compared (the reader must not supply the comparison).
* **UNREADABLE:** the value needs a pixel measurement that the legend's own scale does not support; recorded, not counted.
A metric is assigned to WOREE and to SCAR12 separately; a statement about "mutant organoids" without the genotype split is recorded as unsplit and is not credited to either.
Every number carries its version: the published accepted manuscript, the preprint v2 figures (a different version, marked) or the Bedrosian commentary (orients questions only; never a source of a value). GSE156243 bulk data do not answer PV and do not replace the 2026 single-cell data.
