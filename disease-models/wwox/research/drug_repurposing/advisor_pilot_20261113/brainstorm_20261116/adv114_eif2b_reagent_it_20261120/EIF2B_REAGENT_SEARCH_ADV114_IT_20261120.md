# ADV-114: reagents for separating the eIF2B epsilon route from the GSK-3 route (IT47-Q01 and IT47-Q02; IT, 2026-11-20; hypothesis level; no dose, no recommendation; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

## 0. Answer first
* **IT47-Q01 (dead eIF2B epsilon in tissue with intact Gsk3b): no source found; one retinal experiment remains the only one.** The reagent is a dominant-negative, catalytically dead eIF2B epsilon (E572A in the mouse numbering of the retinal paper). Europe PMC full text holds it in exactly two documents: the retinal paper (PMID 26974342) and a 2024 review of that paper's field (PMID 38059848), which only repeats the retinal result. A second dominant-negative eIF2B epsilon (F259L) was used in neonatal rat cardiac myocytes (PMID 15001529, abstract level only), but the abstract reports it only against isoproterenol-induced hypertrophy, not against an intact-kinase baseline. **The control stays missing; the epistasis arm cannot yet attribute the translation gain to the kinase.**
* **IT47-Q02 (phosphomimetic at the GSK-3 site): NARROWED, not answered.** One phosphomimetic exists, in one 2025 preprint (bioRxiv, Fanlo-Ucar and colleagues): eIF2B epsilon S540E (human numbering of the residue the retinal paper calls 535), in SH-SY5Y cells, behaving like eIF2B epsilon silencing in a BACE1 5' UTR luciferase reporter. It is abstract level only here (the full text is behind a rate-limit page, not a captcha), not peer reviewed, a reporter of selective translation and not a measure of global protein synthesis, and the abstract does not say it was compared with the phospho-resistant form. "Validated" is not met; "exists" is. DYRK2 at the priming serine: still shown for eIF2B epsilon only in cardiomyocytes; in neurons DYRK2 and GSK3 beta are shown acting sequentially on a different substrate (NDEL1, eLife 2019; title level only here), which supports the mechanism in neurons but is not an eIF2B epsilon result.
* **What it changes.** The ISR slice (the eIF2B/ISR arm of the broad panel) cannot be separated from the GSK-3 route by a published result today. It can be separated by a new experiment made of reagents that all exist: phospho-resistant S535A (constitutively active), the dead form E572A (or F259L), and the phosphomimetic S540E (human numbering), each with and without the GSK-3 manipulation in the same tissue, the dead form also alone. That is the design the slice must specify; section 3.

## 3. Anchor, context, competing explanation, weakening observation
* **Anchor.** The retinal paper: dead form largely negated the Gsk3b-deletion gain in OPP labelling (n 5 per group, Figure 5F).
* **Context.** Three reagents, three systems (retina in vivo, cardiac myocytes, a neuroblastoma line); none is a WWOX-deficient or oligodendrocyte-lineage system, and the kinase route is read by a phospho-site antibody in only one of them.
* **Competing explanation.** The dead form forms a pentamer without catalysis, so it should lower the eIF2B pool in any cell, with or without Gsk3b deletion; the observed "negation" is then expected whether or not the gain was kinase-specific. Only a dead-form-alone arm distinguishes the two.
* **Weakening observation.** The phosphomimetic reporter result points the other way in kind: it raises translation of a stress-gene 5' UTR, where the retinal result is a rise in global labelling; global and selective translation need not move together (inference, not a measurement here). So "eIF2B function up" and "ISR-like selective translation up" must be read with separate assays in any design built on this.
* **A design that uses only existing reagents (hypothesis).** Same tissue, same labelling readout, arms: control; Gsk3b manipulation; dead form alone; Gsk3b manipulation with the dead form; phospho-resistant form; phosphomimetic form; engagement control for any eIF2B activator (it acts on eIF2B, so it cannot separate the two routes). The dead-form-alone arm is the missing control of IT47-Q01; the phosphomimetic with the Gsk3b manipulation tests whether the kinase acts through that site.

* Absence is bounded: two routes and the strings above; full texts of the 2007 E569A paper, the cardiomyocyte paper and the preprint were not read; plain-client access to the heart paper and the preprint ended at a challenge page and a rate limit, neither bypassed.
