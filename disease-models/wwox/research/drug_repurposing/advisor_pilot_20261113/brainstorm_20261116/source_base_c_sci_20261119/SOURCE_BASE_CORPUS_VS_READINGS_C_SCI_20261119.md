## 4. PubMed 43048821 (radar signal of 2026-11-19): identity, relevance, prior reading, contribution
* **Identity (PubMed record, DOI 10.2174/0115672026523581261106063859):** Chen and colleagues, Current Neurovascular Research, published 2026-11-13. Mendelian randomisation and colocalisation of programmed-cell-death-related genes (cis expression, methylation and protein quantitative-trait loci) against **general-population cognitive function**; WWOX is one of three genes (PDK1, VAMP8, WWOX) with the highest level of evidence; higher genetically predicted WWOX expression associated with better cognition; four further transcript-supported candidates (ARF1, HSPA8, PARK7, TXNDC12); molecular docking for two. Abstract-level only.
* **Prior reading:** none (no receipt; not in the repository). **Pertinence:** low for WWOX loss of function in DEE: a common-variant population association on cognitive performance, not a model of loss of function; the direction (lower WWOX, worse cognition) is compatible with, and does not test, the disease model; PDK1 appears with the opposite direction to a PDK-inhibitor logic (higher genetically predicted PDK1, better cognition), a population statement that does not transfer. **Contribution possible:** none to a pharmacological comparison; at most a modifier-gene lead (VAMP8, HSPA8) for a later genetic question. **Decision:** not read in full; recorded as triaged, reopen if a modifier-gene question is opened.
