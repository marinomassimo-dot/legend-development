**Reading these rows.** A nominal bath value compared with a total plasma value is a comparison of two different quantities; it says where the bath value sits relative to what was found, not what any tissue receives. The highest plasma value found is the top of the few reports opened, a group mean in an anaesthetised-child nasal arm, not a biological limit. Free fraction, brain-to-plasma ratio, infant exposure, exposure in status epilepticus and a multi-day infusion beyond one case were not found in the sources read. The authors of [4] themselves call their effective concentrations "clinically relevant", citing a free anaesthetic plasma level of about 5 to 10 uM; this pack does not settle that disagreement: the pharmacokinetic reading and the primary paper disagree about the same quantity.
