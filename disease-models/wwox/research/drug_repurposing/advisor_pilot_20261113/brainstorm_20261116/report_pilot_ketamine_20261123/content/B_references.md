## Notes on identifiers (corrections found while verifying)
* The current brief view (ketamine.json) cites PMID 34747138 for "Neuronal Wwox model and incidental anaesthesia". That PMID is the EMBO Mol Med gene-therapy paper [3]; the neuronal-deletion model and the in vivo bursting recorded under ketamine and xylazine are in the Brain paper [2] (PMID 33914858). In [3] the recordings under ketamine and medetomidine were made only in adult wild-type and treated knockout mice, because untreated knockouts died within about four weeks.
* The first-line randomised trial (Ket-Mid) has a PubMed record [9]; the brief view links the publisher page only.
* Not cited as separate entries: the Ih-blocker precedent mentioned in the Breton Discussion (cited inside [1]).
