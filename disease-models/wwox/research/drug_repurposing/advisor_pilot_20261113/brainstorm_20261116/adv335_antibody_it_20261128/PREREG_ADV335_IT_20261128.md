## Denominator (frozen rule)
- **Surfaces:** `claim_registry_current.md` (through the registry tools), `working_model_current.md`, and tracked files under `disease-models/wwox/` whose file name contains `dossier`.
- **In:** a sentence, bullet or table cell asserting that WWOX **protein** is present, or has a stated level or distribution, in a named neural cell type or nervous-system region of a named or implied species, with endogenous protein as the object.
- **Out, listed separately with counts:** mRNA, in situ hybridisation, transcriptomic and reporter statements; subcellular compartment; overexpressed protein; loss or knockout phenotypes with no localisation assertion; cultured non-neural cells.
- **Unit:** one statement with its cited primary source or sources. Restatements of the same assertion from the same source are one unit, with all places listed.
- **Cap:** at most 25 units: claim registry first, then working model, then dossiers in path order; units left unexamined are counted and named.

## Classes per unit
- **C** controlled: a held cited source shows, for the antibody used, a WWOX-deficient tissue or cell control or a peptide block, on immunostaining or immunoblot of nervous tissue or neural cells (column says which of staining or blot).
- **R** control only referred to (another study, vendor statement) or shown only on non-neural material.
- **N** no specificity control stated or visible in what was read.
- **U** unresolved: no primary source cited, or source not held or not readable.
Also recorded per unit: PMID, antibody (catalogue number, host, clone), per-animal values or n, species, age as printed, held-text depth (what was read: methods, legends, figure images).

## Explanations and predictions
- **E1** each statement already rests on a controlled source. Predicts: C = all units with a held source.
- **E2** a substantial share rests on immunoreactivity without a control. Predicts: N + R at least one third of the units with a held source.
- IT38's expectation: N + R above one half.

## Frozen rejecting observation
If every unit with a held source is **C**, E2 is rejected and nothing is downgraded. E1 is rejected by a single **N** or **R** unit. Counterexample accepted against E2: a peptide-block abolition shown in the cited source (PMID 15982416 is the candidate, to be checked, not assumed).
