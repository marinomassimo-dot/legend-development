## (2) First, because it matters most: anything that could be read as a hint about a person

2. **Illustrative drug names** (valproate, carbamazepine, cannabidiol, ifenprodil, Ro 25-6981, TTA-A2, Z944, SB216763, CHIR99021, TCS2002) could read as a shopping list. ## (1) What I could not understand, or would have to ask
1. **Lithium regimen can flip the sign.** The brief leaves "exposure times" to the laboratory, but the rodent literature the repository holds has acute lithium pro-convulsive and chronic lithium threshold-raising in one mouse PTZ paper family (ADV-051 package A, rows L05 to L09), and Cheng 2020 used repeated pretreatment within an hour. A laboratory choosing a single acute pretreatment could get the opposite sign for a reason unrelated to genotype. Say that the lithium regimen (single against repeated) is a design variable to be stated before data and that Cheng's repeated pretreatment is the replication condition.
2. **"Doses are the laboratory's" leaves "an effect of the drug" undefined.** Ethosuximide, valproate and the others shift potency with age in the rat data the brief cites (age-shifted potencies); with a dose fixed at one age, a null at another age is a dose null. The brief should ask for either a dose check per age (two exposures, or a published pup exposure) or a statement that a null is read only where the panel agent shows its effect at the same age (the panel does this for the panel agent, not for ethosuximide at its own dose). No pup exposure measurement exists for any agent; say whether the laboratory is expected to measure one.
3. **Allocation against blinding.** Section 2.1 allocates "scarcest genotype first (knockouts in rotation)", which needs the genotype before the test, while the genotype is also "a coded tissue sample read ... after the test". Both cannot hold. State: genotype read before the test by a person who does not score (needed for allocation); the scorer stays blind.
4. **Stage 3 is assigned to nothing.** Section 2.6 defines minimal seizures as stages 1 to 2 and generalised tonic-clonic as stages 4 to 5. On the scale of Cheng 2020 stage 3 is unilateral forelimb clonus with myoclonic jerk, and stage 4 is bilateral forelimb clonus with rearing, which is not obviously "generalised tonic-clonic" (stage 5 is). Give stage 3 a home, and say whether stage 4 counts as generalised for incidence.
5. **"Same session, same litters" for 9 genotype-by-arm cells plus panel and age series cannot hold with about 7 pups per litter and 1.75 knockouts.** Say what "session" means (one day, one assay age) and that cells are filled across litters, with the litter as a block only for the vehicle.
6. **Which SD?** Stage 1 asks for "within-genotype SD of S", the sizing uses the SD of log S. With zeros (a treated pup with no seizure has S = 0) the log scale is undefined at the animal level, and the analysis is a ratio of means with a bootstrap. The pilot should report the SD or coefficient of variation on the scale the primary analysis actually uses (ratio of means, bootstrap over litters), or the sizing cannot be re-run.
7. **Do stages 2 and 3 share their vehicle groups?** ADV-051 proposed lithium "in the same session" as ethosuximide, with a shared control; the brief makes them separate stages and only the panel is said to share vehicle. State whether ethosuximide, the Cav3 blocker, lithium, the second GSK-3 inhibitor and valproate share one vehicle group per genotype per session (cheaper, and the interaction contrast is cleaner) or not.
8. **Panel agents: "selective Cav3" and "different class GSK-3" are defined by the laboratory's own criterion**, but availability, vehicle, route and brain exposure of tool compounds such as TTA-A2 are not trivial; say who supplies them or that the laboratory may drop the arm (the brief already marks it optional, which helps).

## (3) Contradictions with held notes
1. **Section 6 item 5 against section 9.1:** item 5 says the development arm "is not yet delivered and will be added by a dated addendum"; 9.1 is that addendum. Delete or update item 5.
2. **9.1's condition may be unreachable.** It runs the development cohort only if the provoked assay shows a selective lithium effect "with the genotype-by-treatment term" (P5). Section 3 says that term is realistic only at a pilot SD of about 0.3 or less with about 75 litters, so under the brief's own sizing the condition will usually be unmeasurable, and 9.1 would then never run. Say so, and give the fallback (the exploratory interval of 2.7), or say the development cohort is independent of the interaction result.
3. **Ifenprodil ages:** section 2.4 says "pups up to about P10 to P12 in mouse and rat"; ADV-053/ADV-054 give mouse P7 and P10 (not P14), rat P7 to P18, radiprodil maximal at PN12. Use "mouse P7 and P10; rat to P18" or drop the P12.
4. **The sizing is consistent with ADV-051/053** (one experiment, ORG-2) and with the age-window line; no other contradiction found with ADV-052, ADV-054 or ADV-058 unit 2.

## (4) The sizing section
It is **clear and honest**: the arithmetic is right, the "out of reach at SD 0.5, pilot first" sentence is the right sentence, and a laboratory can act on "pilot first". Two things would make it act-able rather than only readable. (a) **0.3 is presented as the realistic case, and the published figure points the other way.** On Cheng 2020 Fig. 7b the knockout PTZ-alone curve (N = 6) has error bars of the order of a third to a half of the mean at many time points (an eye read, SEM), i.e. an SD of S of the order of 0.7 to 1 times the mean; if so, a simple effect of 0.7 needs about 79 per arm and the interaction is far out of reach. Say that early, so the laboratory does not read 0.3 as the expected value. (b) **Give totals, not only litters:** 75 litters is about 525 pups plus panel and age-series animals, with 132 knockouts that are fragile and die by about 4 weeks; the laboratory will ask for total animals and breeding time first. The interaction also needs wild-type cells of the same size (four cells), which the sentence "wild-type and heterozygous animals are not limiting" glosses.

## (5) Would I answer, and what would I need first
Yes, to say whether I can run stages 1 and 2, and no to anything beyond a pilot until I knew the SD. First I would need: (1) **who funds it, who owns the data, and what authorship or credit is offered**: the cover says "nothing is promised", and that is exactly what a laboratory asks in its first reply (this is the operator's decision, but the cover should say that the laboratory will be told); (2) **the dose and lithium-regimen question (items 1.1 and 1.2)**, because I cannot state my design before data without it; (3) **whether the repository can supply the tool compounds** or I source them; (4) **my own ethics approval for PTZ status in fragile knockout pups**, which the brief rightly leaves to me but which decides whether stage 2 is possible at all. I would not need anything about the repository's other work.

## Second look at the changed passages only (IT, 2026-11-18, after SCI's 9.3 in d7a913ceb; the text above is not edited)
**Clear and consistent now:** examples-only names (2.4 and the header); lithium regimen as a design variable with the paper's repeated pretreatment as the replication condition (2.9); genotype read before the test by a non-scorer (2.1); stage 3 as its own category and stage 4 reported separately (2.6); a session defined (2.1); the CV on the primary-analysis scale (stage 1); one shared vehicle group per genotype per session (2.1); ifenprodil ages (2.4, agree with ADV-054); the sizing flag at the top (arithmetic reproduced: 176 animals for four equal cells at 44, 88 knockouts, about 525 pups); section 6 item 8 and the cover's decision paragraph; the sentence removed in 6.7 and 9.2.
**New or remaining, as a fix often creates one:**
1. **Dose checks multiply the animals and the sizing does not count them.** 2.9 asks two exposures per agent at each age of the series (panel agents included); the sizing flag and section 3 count one exposure per drug. Add a total for the required stages (pilot, ethosuximide, age series, panel, dose checks) or say the dose check is the first thing the pilot decides. Also say whether the knockout assay age needs the dose check (it is "each age of the series").
2. **The priors file governs conflicts, and 2.6 now equates "generalised" with stage 5 only.** The earlier text of this brief said stages 4 to 5. If P8 in the hashed priors defines generalised as stages 4 to 5, 2.6 now conflicts with the text that governs; SCI should check P8 and, if so, keep stage 4 inside "generalised" for the priors and report it separately as a secondary.
3. **One shared vehicle group for five test arms** is cheaper, but the sizing assumes equal arms and no multiplicity: the vehicle group should be larger than each arm, and "an effect = R at most 0.7 with an interval excluding 1" is not adjusted for five comparisons against one control. Say how (a larger vehicle group, a stated multiplicity rule, or accept the comparisons as separate pre-specified ones).
4. **9.1's fallback blurs its own drop rule.** The condition becomes "lithium R at most 0.7 reproduced by the second GSK-3 inhibitor and not by the comparator", which can hold when lithium also acts in wild type; the next sentence drops the cohort "if lithium is general". With an interaction that cannot be sized, "general" cannot be told from "specific". Say that "general" there means "also reproduced by the non-GSK-3 comparator", so the drop rule and the fallback use the same test.
5. **Numbers in the top flag and section 3 differ slightly:** the flag says "roughly 50 to 85 animals per arm" for a log SD of 0.6 to 0.8; section 3 gives 11, 31 and 79 for 0.3, 0.5 and 0.8, so 0.6 to 0.8 is about 44 to 79. Use one set.
6. **The cover alone understates the size.** A laboratory given only the cover reads "may need many litters"; the brief's flag says hundreds of animals for the genotype comparison and that it may be unanswerable. Add one clause to the cover: the genotype comparison may need hundreds of animals and may not be answerable; the pilot decides.
7. **Header and cover say "no schedule", while 2.9 names the paper's lithium pretreatment as the replication condition.** It is a citation of what the paper states; word it as "the regimen as stated in the paper" so no one reads it as a proposed schedule.
8. **Minor:** stage 3 (lithium, optional) is said to share the vehicle group only "within a session"; say that if stage 3 runs in a later session it needs its own vehicle group.
