Notes on the table.

Whether the pro-convulsive acute arm used a different interval from the others: NOT STATED. A full text of 29102713 (Brain Res Bull, closed) would settle it.
2. Rat ceiling: for PTZ in non-kindled rats the lithium result is null (Ormandy 1991) to very weak (Schmidt 1986); the mouse reports are threshold-raising; the Rahimi 2019 report shows the mouse effect is endpoint-dependent (threshold raised under infusion; no protection against electroshock or the intraperitoneal lethal-dose test).
3. Cheng 2020 is the only row in group B and the only row with a knockout, and the only one scored on a graded severity scale with a 60-min window rather than a threshold.

(b) Time-course of lithium after a single injection in rodents (all abstract level):
- 7324073 (rat): "initially its concentration is high in blood and negligible in the brain" (quote), then it rises in the brain as it leaves blood.
- 7073401 (rat): behavioural effect tied to high brain lithium at 4 to 8 h.
- 4076072 (rat): serum levels followed for 4 h, from subtherapeutic to toxic across the single doses used.
- 25354763 (Hanak 2015, rat): "Brain lithium distribution was rapid (as early as 15 min), inhomogeneous and with delayed elimination (over 78 h)." Pharmacokinetic models of poisoning, so not therapeutic exposure.
- 8190348 (Sandner 1994, rat): single-injection regional distribution; highest concentration in periaqueductal and periventricular structures.
- 2824229 (rat): the cholinergic sensitisation lasted 2 to 24 h after one treatment.
- Consequence for the table (producer inference, NOT VERIFIED in the regimen papers): a single injection followed by PTZ after 30 min (Rahimi 2019) tests blood-phase lithium; Cheng 2020's three injections within 1 h followed by PTZ at an unstated interval cannot be placed on that curve; none of the regimen papers reports a brain or serum lithium at the time of PTZ except the 29102713 chronic arm (serum level mentioned; figure omitted).
