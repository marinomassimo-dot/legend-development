# Onset age and EEG of Wwox seizures beyond Cheng 2020: one reading round (SCI, 2026-11-19; ADV's item 2 of the ADV-067 follow-up; non-canonical; hypothesis level; no dose, no recommendation; not medical advice)

**One-line answer.** The earliest documented ages are about **P12 for behavioural spontaneous seizures** (Cheng 2020, one laboratory, no EEG) and **about P14 for cortical ECoG spikes and generalised spike-wave discharges** in Wwox-null pups (a 2026 open full text, n = 5 per group, "a limited cohort"; recording started at about P14, so nothing earlier was recorded); slice bursting was recorded at P13 to P17 (Breton 2021); **no EEG before P14 exists in the sources read, so the true onset is not established and may be earlier.** The in vivo brief leaves the assay age to the laboratory: the documented window for any provoked-seizure assay in the null is therefore bounded below by about P12 to P14 and above by lethality at about three to four weeks.

**What this changes for the brief:** nothing in the frozen priors; a statement of fact for the laboratory (dated line 9.10). **Open, executable without a laboratory:** the Wwox rat reports (Suzuki 2009 full text and any follow-up with ages) and the P47T full text for onset ages; **needs a laboratory:** EEG before P14 in the null or the neuron-deletion line. **Limits:** one reading round as assigned; one new full text read by keyword scan; two abstracts only; figures not viewed; no independent verifier.
