## Answer first
1. **Decision for the brief: one lithium regimen as the main contrast (the Cheng 2020 repeated pretreatment, as the replication condition), not two; but with a lithium-alone arm that carries a sedation, righting-reflex and weight readout at the PTZ time, a recorded interval from the last injection to PTZ, and a second GSK-3 inhibitor of another class made MORE necessary, not less.** A single acute injection with PTZ at about 30 minutes is an optional comparator arm, not a design variable of the main contrast. Reason: no regimen in the table shows a clear sign flip against PTZ; the acute pro-convulsant result is one abstract from one laboratory family whose interval and method cannot be read; but Cheng's own panel cannot separate protection from a regimen artefact (no lithium-alone arm, no weight or sedation statement, three injections within an hour, the interval to PTZ not stated).
2. **From Cheng's own image the proportional reduction by lithium looks similar in the three genotypes** (peak stage about 1.2 to 0.3, 1.1 to 0.4 and 3.5 to 1.3, an eye reading), which fits a general effect or a regimen artefact better than a knockout-enhanced GSK-3 effect; the text and legend claim the knockout only, the image shows all three with four asterisks and the legend defines three.
3. **The seizure-test age in Cheng 2020 is bounded, not stated: the abstract says "preweaning mice" (a fact the producer missed and I found in the saved bytes), the same paper says spontaneous seizures were commonly observed after postnatal day 12, and the null mice die by about four weeks: so the test lies between about P12 and P20 (INFERENZA).** No lithium study against a provoked seizure in immature rodents was found by two routes; every pup sign below is a TRANSFER from adult mice.
4. **Case E (retrospective, see section 7):** the structured path reaches the same main decision as the simple path (one regimen) and changes one sub-decision (the second GSK-3 class: "as planned" against "more necessary") and adds two predictions that name a contradicting observation; it costs more wall time and more output, and less cache-read than the simple path run in a long-context session.

## 2. Why the regimen can be an artefact, from the sources
* **Exposure class.** Cheng's cumulative lithium within an hour is a larger exposure class than the single injections of the mouse threshold studies (dose figures omitted by rule; the comparison is of the Methods sentences).
* **Label (LAB):** "Lithium toxicity is closely related to serum lithium concentrations, and can occur at doses close to therapeutic concentrations."; "Lithium may take up to 24 hours to distribute into brain tissue, so occurrence of acute toxicity symptoms may be delayed."; neurological signs include "lack of coordination, and weakness".
* **A Racine stage of 0 or 1 cannot tell protection from reduced behavioural output** (producer inference, agreed): a sedated or ataxic pup scores low.
* **Time-phase:** lithium reaches the brain after it leaves the blood (7324073; the label); an interval not stated in Cheng cannot be placed on that curve; the one single-injection mouse study with a stated interval used 30 minutes and an infusion threshold.

## 4. Decision, on ADV's four points
1. **Table:** done (section 1). **Predictions and separating pattern:** section 3.
2. **One regimen or two:** one as the main contrast, because no clear sign flip exists across regimens in the same preparation and a second regimen would multiply arms without a source-grounded prediction; the optional acute arm is offered only for the interval question. This **changes one statement of the brief** (section 2.9 treats the regimen as a design variable): dated line 9.9 in the brief.
3. **Second GSK-3 class:** **more necessary**, because the single regimen cannot exclude a lithium-specific or regimen artefact, and a different chemotype with its own artefact check is the only discriminator in the design; the general comparator (valproate in the rat ontogeny data) stays.
4. **Stop criterion:** the only candidate flip (the acute pro-convulsant result of 29102713) is one abstract from one laboratory family with unknown interval and method and a closed full text: **not a clear flip, so no further lithium dossier**. What remains is a measurement: the lithium-alone arm, the recorded interval and, if the laboratory can, lithium levels at the PTZ time, plus the full text of 29102713 if it becomes accessible.
