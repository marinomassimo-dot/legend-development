## Answer first
1. **Part 1 agrees with SCI.** Two reads of the PTZ-alone knockout curves (panels b and d, N = 6) from the publisher PNG, calibrated on the axis ticks, give per-minute means within **0.05 stage (b) and 0.15 stage (d)** of SCI's (mean absolute difference), per-minute SEM within about 0.15, and a per-minute SD (SEM x sqrt N) of **1.15 (mine) against 1.32 (SCI) in b and 1.53 against 1.26 in d**: the two readers differ in opposite directions in the two panels, so there is no systematic bias, and SCI's vector-exact panel d is the better value where they differ. The bracket for the SD of the 60-minute summary S, by SCI's own rule (minutes independent: lower; minutes perfectly correlated: upper), is **9.5 to 62 (b) and 10.9 to 69 (d) on my read against SCI's 11.4 to 71.5 and 9.2 to 56.9** on the same minutes: within about 20% on both ends. **The sizing flag stands**: the coefficient of variation of S lies between about 0.1 and 0.6 (b) and 0.15 and 1.3 (d); the lower ends need independent minutes, which the figure itself argues against (the scatter of a curve across minutes is smaller than the per-minute SEM, SCI's observation), so a pilot CV of 0.5 to 1 is the realistic range and 0.3 the optimistic one.
2. **Part 2: the rebound still ends before 30 minutes in both new cases by SCI's frozen classes, but its end moves by 5 to 10 minutes with the seed and the time constant, and a gain time constant of 7200 s leaves a small residual.** By the frozen windows: second seed, tau_w 3600 / 1800 / 7200 s: W1 1.46 / 1.36 / 1.36 (above 1.3), **W2 1.02 / 0.99 / 1.09 (within)**, W3 0.95 / 0.96 / 0.95. Harness check: the original seed with tau_w 3600 reproduces SCI's W1 1.61 and W2 1.07 exactly. With tau_w 7200 on the original seed: W1 1.50, **W2 1.19 (within the class, above 1.1)**, W3 1.09. Date of the end of the rebound (additional 5-minute bins; first bin at or below 1.3 that stays so for three bins): **original seed 25 min (tau_w 3600) and 30 min (7200); second seed 10 (1800), 15 (3600), 20 min (7200)**: the seed moves it by 10 minutes, doubling tau_w by 5. The residual between 1.1 and 1.3 lasts to about 50 minutes (second seed) and 95 minutes (original seed) at tau_w 7200, of the order of the baseline's own bin noise (the K0 cell's 5-minute bins range 0.89 to 1.16 at the second seed).
3. **What it does to the early-read timing (Addendum 14, reads at about 5 and 15 minutes, then 30, 60, 120).** The 5-minute read sits inside the rebound in every case (rates 1.5 to 2.6 times the pre-exposure rate), and the 15-minute read is inside it for tau_w 3600 and 7200 (1.3 to 1.6) and at its end for 1800: so the early reads see the rebound as the design intends. **The 30-minute read can sit on the tail when the time constant is long** (original seed, tau_w 7200: the bin starting at minute 30 is 1.29 to 1.35, the end is called at minute 30). A 30-minute read that is not above 1.1 does not by itself show that no adaptation was present when the time constant is unknown; the design could add one read at about 45 minutes, or treat the 30-minute read as the end of the rebound zone rather than after it. The evoked-response analogue E stays within 0.99 to 1.08 in every cell, as in SCI's run (the burst rate and E part company during the rebound, as in SCI's statement 8).
Nothing here is evidence about a slice: the model is a hypothesis generator and its transfer to a slice is an INFERENZA.
