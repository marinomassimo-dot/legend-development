## 2. NQ-B: the nine genes, the pass rule, the predictions
Genes (mouse symbols; the OMA1 paper's "Chop" is Ddit3 and its "Hri" is Eif2ak1): Atf4, Ddit3, Chac1, Eif2ak1, Ppp1r15a (carried by IT6 P4, to be re-derived here as a loader check) and Fgf21, Herpud1, Dele1, Eif2s2 (not yet tabulated). Held inputs: GSE158681, GSE158682 TPM tables, the pinned Ensembl release 92 cDNA annotation (transcript-to-gene key), the contrast definitions of IT6 P4 (H_S, H_N, H_null, C_S counted; CC_SW, CC_NW, CC_SN control-versus-control as noise floor); unit = one GEO sample treated as one animal (assumed, not verified, as in ADV-068).
* Per gene and contrast: mean TPM in each arm; measurable if both arm means are at least 1.0 TPM (the universe threshold of IT6 P4); centred log2 ratio R_c (log2 of (mean KO + 0.5) over (mean control + 0.5), minus the median over the measurable universe of that contrast); animal-separated if every KO animal lies on one side of every control animal; the per-animal TPM values are written out.
* Call per gene and contrast: LOWER if R_c <= -0.3 and animal-separated; HIGHER if R_c >= +0.3 and animal-separated; otherwise NOT RESOLVED; NOT MEASURABLE if below the threshold. The 0.3 is the resolvability limit of the third system in the earlier work.
* Gene verdict: LOWER or HIGHER only if the same call appears in at least two of the three hippocampal counted contrasts (H_S, H_N, H_null), or in C_S and one hippocampal contrast; otherwise NOT RESOLVED. A gene with a call (|R_c| at least 0.3, separated) in any control-versus-control contrast is flagged NOISE-PRONE and its verdict is downgraded to NOT RESOLVED.
* Loader check: the five already-carried genes are recomputed; if any R_centred differs from the value in `key_genes.tsv` of IT6 P4 by more than 0.01, the mismatch is reported and the IT6 value is used for the carried genes.
* Predictions: B1: Fgf21 is NOT MEASURABLE in every contrast (a liver-enriched hormone). B2: none of Herpud1, Dele1, Eif2s2 receives a LOWER or HIGHER gene verdict (consistent with the earlier "ISR target set not moved"). B3: Herpud1 is measurable in every contrast. Rejecting observations: B1 rejected if Fgf21 is measurable in any contrast; B2 rejected if one of the three has a gene verdict LOWER or HIGHER; B3 rejected if Herpud1 is not measurable in some contrast.
* Disposition rule: "already carried" for a gene tabulated in IT6 P4 (cite); "answered" for a gene newly tabulated; the RP-018 reading changes only if a newly tabulated gene receives a verdict other than NOT RESOLVED or NOT MEASURABLE.

## 3. NQ-C: the codon-level statement, what is tested, the predictions
The statement tested: arginine starvation slows elongation at two of the six arginine codons (CGT, CGC, CGA, CGG, AGA, AGG) in human cells. Public processed tables used: the per-codon ribosome-density tables of GSE113751 (processed by the depositors; no raw reads, no alignment here) for HEK293T (3 h; also 6 h), HCT116 and HeLa, in arginine starvation, leucine starvation and rich medium. Lysine: the deposit has no lysine condition, so no lysine pausing is tested.
* Per codon c and sample: S = the maximum of the density row over positions -10 to +10 (the window around the codon of interest). Arg codon c is SLOWED in a cell line if S(arginine starvation, 3 h) divided by S(rich, 3 h) is at least 1.5 AND is at least 1.5 times the same ratio for leucine starvation (specificity control). The count of SLOWED Arg codons per cell line is the readout; the replication unit is the cell line (one sample each, no p value). The 6 h HEK293T sample is reported as a time check.
* Predictions: C1: exactly two of the six arginine codons are SLOWED in HEK293T at 3 h (rejected if the count is not two). C2: the same codons are SLOWED in HCT116 and HeLa (rejected if the sets differ). C3: the deposit contains no lysine condition (certain from the file list; recorded as a test of the file list).
* Disposition rule: ANSWERED for the arginine statement if the count can be taken in at least two cell lines from the processed tables (state the count); NEEDS A SOURCE WE DO NOT HOLD for any lysine codon claim (name: a lysine-limitation ribosome-profiling deposit, none located in this pass). The fly lysine measurement behind the programme's lysine premise is a different paper and is out of scope here except for one line stating what the dossier's decisive-observation sentence rests on.
* Limits stated in advance: processed tables are the depositors' normalisation; the window and the 1.5 threshold are mine and were not tuned; a codon-density maximum is not a pause time; arginine starvation of cancer-derived lines is not neural tissue and says nothing about WWOX.
