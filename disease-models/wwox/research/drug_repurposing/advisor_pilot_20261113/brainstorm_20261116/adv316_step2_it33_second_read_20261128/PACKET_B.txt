STATEMENTS
1. A sentence "not disease-specific on these data" rests on a non-significant interaction (treatment x genotype F(1,28) = 0.3129, p = 0.5804); it should read "no significant interaction detected": power is unknown and it does not show equal toxicity. The genotype main effect (F(1,28) = 6.181, p = 0.0192) is real; the 65Q vehicle bar is already about 7 percent below the 27Q vehicle bar (by eye).
2. Dose, window and unit: "100 ng/mL" with the 24 h window (stated only in Results text, not in the Fig 4 legend); one dose, one time; the readout is MTT raw OD (the neuron MTT assay is named only by the axis label; Methods describe MTT for SH-SY5Y). The fractions 0.71 and 0.64 are ratios of raw OD read by eye (0.70 and 0.64 on hand reading), not normalised values in the source.
3. The n unit is unresolved: Fig 4B states F(1,28) only (eight per cell if balanced); Fig 4A states n = 4 biological replicates; the replicate unit is not defined.
4. "raising it": WWOX level or uptake in treated neurons is not measured (exogenous protein, Pepmic, applied to the medium); "Increases in WWOX induce neuronal toxicity" is the authors' inference. The phrases "three sources now say" and "outside the vector setting" are not from this paper.
5. Silence, not a stated negative: no heat-denatured or endotoxin control; vehicle and purity not stated (the source is silent).
6. At 100 ng/mL, 24 h, SH-SY5Y falls to about 0.32 of vehicle (by eye) against about 0.64-0.70 in neurons: the same dose and time is much less toxic in neurons.
7. The SH-SY5Y dose-time series (Supplementary Figure 2: 24 h significant at 100 and 500 ng/mL; 48 h at 10, 100, 500) is as stated, and 100 ng/mL is the lowest dose with a significant 24 h loss. Figures 1, 2 and Supplementary Figure 1 do not bear on any sentence above.
