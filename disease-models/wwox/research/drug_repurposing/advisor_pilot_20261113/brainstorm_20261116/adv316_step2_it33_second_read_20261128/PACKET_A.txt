STATEMENTS
1. A record's Role line calls the variant "double-mutant AAV9-DM". The paper has no "double mutant" or "two mutations" wording and says "five point mutations"; the Role line should say "AAV9-DM (five point mutations)" or use the paper's own label.
2. "hepatic vector genome copies ~127-fold": the unit is vg per ug liver DNA at week 9 (a concentration, not total liver genomes); mouse; one dose. Fig 4A by eye: AAV9 about 1.2e8, AAV9-DM about 9.5e5 (ratio about 125), consistent with the authors' 127.
3. "~8-fold worse packaging": Fig 1B prints AAV9-DM 4.53e12 (ratio 7.90); the text prints 4.5e12 (ratio 7.96); one production run from 1.6e9 HEK293 cells, no error bar.
4. "peak 7.3e7 to 5.8e5 (a factor of 126)": printed only in Results 3.3 text, in no panel; the statistic is not stated; the ROI is "the ventral side of the animal" and the text cites "Figure 2". Fig 3 liver means (by eye) are about 1.7e7 and 1e5 at week 3 (ratio about 170): Fig 3 does not reproduce the text peaks.
5. A claim clause says "a liver-de-targeted capsid costs ~8-fold packaging and brain exposure". The ~8-fold is packaging only. Brain vg per ug DNA is about 60-fold lower (Fig 4D, by eye: AAV9 about 4.5e7, AAV9-DM about 7e5; one star, p <= 0.05). The clause should read "packaging ~8-fold lower; brain vg about 60-fold lower". Another clause in the same sentence (primate brain 1-4 vg per DG, sublinear scaling) is not from this paper.
6. Fig 5D shows no brain Fluc RNA difference from AAV9 while Fig 4D shows brain DNA lower; the abstract and Highlights say "similar biodistribution". Heart (Fig 4B) is de-targeted as much as liver (about 160-fold by eye); liver RNA (Fig 5A) falls about 28-fold, not 127. A "liver-specific" reading is not what the paper measured.
7. No toxicity endpoint of any kind is measured (Methods 2.1-2.6; one dose); WWOX appears zero times in the paper.
Study frame (check it too, as clause 8): female 8-week C57BL/6 mice, tail vein, 1e11 vg per animal, one dose; capsids AAV9, AAV9-16, AAV9-HR, AAV9-DM; CBA-Fluc cassette; imaging n = 5, tissue qPCR n = 3-5; mean +/- SEM.
