Paper: Huang 2016, WWOX in MOLT-4 T-cell leukaemia cells (phospho-forms, mitochondria, IkBalpha). The supplement (Fig. S1, Videos S1-S3) is NOT held.
STATEMENTS
1. "no mitochondrial or cytosolic purity marker row" is false as written. Fig 4C has an alpha-tubulin row under the cytosol lanes only (no marker under the mitochondria lanes); Fig 4D has a cytochrome C row under the mitochondria lanes, faint in Control, 0 and 1 h and strong at 2 and 3 h (not constant, by eye). The blot should be named.
2. "every manipulation is overexpression" and "with overexpression or dominant-negative constructs" (for the mitochondrial data) are wrong: the mitochondrial data (Fig 4C-E) are endogenous proteins in MOLT-4 with no construct. EGFP-WWOX and dn-WWOXww (K28T/D29V) appear only in binding, FRET and IP work (Fig 5B, 6F/G, 8C; Methods "DNA Constructs"). Fig 5B (rescue) is one blot pair, no n.
3. Fig 4E numbers: WWOX in mitochondria about 84 (control), 60 (IoP 3 h), 86 (TNF-alpha) percent, n = 3 in the legend (replicate unit undefined), MitoTracker overlap, not co-localisation with a partner; IkBalpha about 83, 50, 70. The printed key reads "*p>0.05; **p<0.001; ****p<0.00001": the meaning of the single asterisk is unsettled (the text says TNF-alpha was less effective). Fig 4C and 4D state no n; Fig 4D is a single blot with "% reduction" rows (WWOX 91, 25, 12 and IkBalpha 93, 93, 8 at 1, 2, 3 h) that the WWOX bands do not visibly show at this resolution (by eye, no densitometry).
4. The leg "WWOX localises to mitochondria under stress": the paper's mitochondrial manipulation is IoP, and WWOX leaves the mitochondria; the word "stress" occurs once in the page, in a reference title about p53 and IkBalpha. "Tyr-33-phosphorylated or activated WWOX is mainly localized in the mitochondria and nuclei" is a citation-backed statement (refs 21, 22, 23, 38, 39), not a measurement here. Calling IoP a low-dose maturation stimulus is not verified.
5. "nanomolar PMA": PMA 0.55 nM with the ionophore A23187 at 0.30 uM (micromolar); CD3 about 1.36-fold and CD8 about 1.17-fold at 24 h, whole lysate, n = 3. pS14, pY33 and pY287 directions are as stated but MOLT-4 only, 10-120 min, n = 3, laboratory-made antibodies; the pY33 blot picture at 120 min does not visibly show the decline.
6. Antibody validation: "homemade antibodies"; the main text asserts validation by references 21-26 and "rechecked (supplemental Fig. S1)"; no main figure shows a peptide-competition, phosphatase or phospho-null control. The content of Fig. S1 is unsettled (not held).
7. pS14 in other cell lines is unsettled (only MOLT-4 here); a review clause about another paper is outside this source.
