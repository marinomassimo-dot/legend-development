# Final sentences of the ADV-285 census (SCI, 2026-11-25; hypothesis level; not medical advice)

**S1.** Two Wwox-deficient mouse models have a functional readout of conduction in held full text. In global Wwox-/- mice at 3 weeks the transcranial motor evoked potential amplitude is 11.8 +- 5.4 against 59.2 +- 9.0 microvolt (4 against 10 animals) and the onset latency 2.44 +- 0.37 against 1.39 +- 0.13 ms (Cheng 2020, Fig 2). In Synapsin-Cre neuronal Wwox deletion the N1 (myelinated-fibre) latency of the callosal evoked response in acute slices is longer (read from the graph, about 3.2 against 2.5 ms; Wilcoxon P = 0.0104; 8 slices from 5 animals against 8 slices from 6) while the N2 (unmyelinated-fibre) latency is not different (P = 0.5737) (Repudi 2021, Fig 5D).

**S2.** Neither of the two has a drug arm for the functional readout, and neither separates a myelin cause from an axonal or excitability cause: the transcranial motor evoked potential is a muscle response along the corticospinal tract and peripheral nerve in a model that also has peripheral demyelination, and the slice comparison counts slices, not animals.

**S3.** No WWOX patient report with an evoked-potential or nerve-conduction result was found by a title and abstract search on two routes (the only hit of the patient string is a mouse paper); a result reported only in the full text of a case report would not be found.

**S4.** Adjacent hypomyelinating, dysmyelinating or leukodystrophy models with a pharmacological arm and a conduction or evoked-potential readout exist: 13 papers by abstract (5 central, 8 peripheral). One was read in open full text: 4-aminopyridine in the taiep rat, corticospinal conduction velocity 13.7 +- 0.6 against 42.9 +- 1.2 m/s in wild type, and the N2 somatosensory evoked response larger and faster after 4-aminopyridine (P < 0.001). None of the other twelve was read beyond the abstract, so no statement on their mechanism overlap with Wwox (OPC maturation, ER stress or integrated stress response, energy supply) goes beyond the abstract.

**S5.** In healthy developing wild-type mice, clemastine 50 mg/kg/day from P5 to P21 slowed the callosal N1 conduction velocity (P < 0.01, n = 10 to 12) while N2 velocity was similar (Palma 2022). In demyelinated patients the same drug shortened the visual evoked potential P100 latency delay by 1.7 ms per eye (ReBUILD, abstract level). A pro-myelinating comparator in a pup design therefore needs a wild-type drug-only arm with a conduction readout, and a conduction readout cannot be assumed to move in the direction of a structural myelin count.

**S6.** Energy supply has a conduction precedent in wild-type tissue (callosal compound action potentials are lost without glucose, lactate and pyruvate did not rescue them in the slice, and loading one oligodendrocyte with glucose prevented the loss in about 70% of experiments; abstract level) but none in a hypomyelinating model with a drug arm.

**S7.** The one measurement that would separate the explanations is the callosal N1 conduction velocity (distance-latency slope over at least three electrode distances) with the N2 response as a within-slice control, at P17 to P21, with animals as the unit, in neuronal-deletion, oligodendrocyte-lineage-deletion and Cre-negative floxed littermates, with a neuronal re-expression arm. A neuron-dependent explanation predicts slow N1 in the neuronal deletion, normal N1 in the oligodendrocyte-lineage deletion and restored N1 on re-expression; an OPC-intrinsic explanation predicts the reverse; a non-myelin explanation (axon calibre or excitability) predicts slow N1 with no g-ratio change in the same animals.

**S8.** The pup brief v2 carries a callosal conduction re-measurement with a rescue arm and animal-level statistics as an optional measurement (a candidate, not an endpoint; first-peak latency, no velocity, "never in vivo"). It does not carry the N2 control, a velocity from the distance slope, an oligodendrocyte-lineage arm, a wild-type drug-only arm, or the in vivo transcranial motor evoked potential of the global null (Cheng 2020).
