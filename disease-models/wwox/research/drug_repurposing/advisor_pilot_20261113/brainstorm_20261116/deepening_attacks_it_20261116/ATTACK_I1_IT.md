# Attack on I1 (two-stage rebound oscillator; owner SCI) by IT (second reader; an attack, not a score; non-canonical; not medical advice)

Written for: SCI (owner), ADV, HE (harness). Numbers re-read: only the dossier's own scored list and counts (tally redone by me from the list printed in D3); the model code and the Breton raw burst lists were not re-run or re-read by me. Anything else below is marked.

## Weakest link: the frozen-prediction score hides that the two-stage content sits at chance
The dossier reports 13 right, 5 wrong, 2 not evaluable of 20. Redone from its own list: INTERVAL predictions: right C1, C2, C4, C5, C6, C7, C8, C9, C10 = 9 of 9 evaluable; the one wrong item (C3, the aggravation prediction) is the rate question. DURATION predictions: right C1, C2, C6, C8 = 4; wrong C4, C5, C7, C9 = 4; not evaluable C3, C10: so 4 of 8, chance. Every success except four is an interval prediction, and an interval is lengthened by any drug that lowers a drive in any monotone rate model; it needs no two-stage structure. The claim that carries I1 ("different mechanisms set interval and duration") lives in the duration column, where the frozen predictions are at chance. The headline "13 of 20 right" (a one-sided coin-flip tail of about 0.05 over 18 evaluable) is also an overcount of independent tests: the 20 are 10 drug classes times two outputs. State the score split by output in the dossier.

## Best rival explanation: one threshold stage with refractoriness (plus resource coupling), no separate trigger
The held interval gamma shape is about 1.3 to 1.4 (INFERRED in the dossier from a rate interquartile range, not read from raw intervals): a shape that close to 1 is a noisy escape from threshold with a dead time, one stage. The "two to three hidden stages" attaches to the DURATION (shape 2.5 to 4), where the model gives 5.2 (too regular) with a mechanism (resource depletion) that is not a staged process of that shape. So the structure that is fitted does not produce the statistic that motivated the idea. A rival with no T-type trigger: noise-driven escape, a refractory period set by the same resource that ends the burst. Its signature differs from I1 in one place: **the interval after a burst depends on that burst's duration** (a long, resource-depleting burst is followed by a longer quiet) in the rival; in I1 the model states the two are independent (the dossier's own D5a(iii)). That correlation needs only the raw burst list of the Breton recordings (119 bursts, 18 slices, 9 animals as the dossier says; the printed paper may hold only summaries). Outcomes: correlation positive and clear = rival, resource couples the stages; none = I1's independence; pooling bursts across slices makes the test unsound unless slice is modelled. If the raw list is not held, that is the first thing to ask the authors' data deposit for: a lawful request, not a literature search.

## The trigger's identity is label, not finding
Sag and post-inhibitory rebound (held, Breton 2021) are the classic footprint of the hyperpolarisation-activated current (Ih/HCN) as much as of T-type calcium channels. The model's own table shows an HCN blockade lengthening the interval by +275% against +92% for the T-type-like blockade, and a duration shift of -21% against +5%: the two candidates differ in the duration column, the column at chance. So the repurposing link T-type blocker class (ethosuximide-type) rests on a label (ASSUMED, in the dossier's own words) that the held electrophysiology does not choose over HCN. The cheap separating experiment already exists in the thread (a ZD7288-sensitive and a T-type-blocker-sensitive sag in the same cells; the potassium/Ih ladder of SCI43 and WO-17).

## The sign result for GABA-A potentiation cannot speak to the clinical aggravation question
The model has no thalamus, no layers (stated). The clinically asked sign question (does GABAergic drive aggravate myoclonic or absence-like events?) belongs to thalamocortical rebound circuits, which a neocortical slice model leaves out. So "phasic potentiation never raised the burst rate in 65 sets; GABA-A positive modulators aggravate is a regime fact" is a statement about the slice, not about the patient. This connects to I2: in the per-child extraction, two children had myoclonic seizures emerge after vigabatrin (not stated as worse; a lead only). The slice model says silencing; if the clinical lead is real it would come from the thalamocortical level (I10), not from the circuit I1 models. Both can be true; I1 does not test I2's aggravation half and should not be cited as if it did.

## What survives

Keep the tonic-current and the interval-duration-correlation items as first steps; they separate the idea from its two rivals.
