## Weakest link: the held intrinsic profile cannot be the cause of the graded incidence, so it cannot carry I13 either
Heterozygotes carry the knockout intrinsic profile (rest, input resistance) yet burst in 4 of 23 slices (17%) against 36 of 42 (86%) in the knockout. Whatever sets the burst is therefore NOT the resting profile. The dossier uses the profile as observation 3 "in favour" and then as counter-evidence (K+ leak fits better). Both uses are idle: a quantity that is equal in the heterozygote and the knockout cannot explain a five-fold step between them. The quantity I13 needs is one that is GRADED by gene dose: Gabrd in the heterozygote. It is not held. This is the cheapest decisive no-literature item: the heterozygote arm of the S-line bulk data, if it exists (GSE158681/2 include which genotypes? if only WT and KO, say so), or a Gabrd read in the P47T heterozygotes if present. A tonic-current loss that is equal in het and KO is killed by the incidence; a graded one is supported.

## Best rival explanation: the Gabrd fall is a consequence of the bursts or of the cell mix, not their cause
(1) Gabrd (delta) expression is regulated by activity and by neurosteroid level; downregulation after sustained hyperexcitability is reported in epilepsy models in general (KNOWN as a general pattern, to be re-verified; I did not re-read a source). A fall in a bursting region and a fall in a non-bursting region (hippocampus 0.31, no bursts) are both reported by the dossier; so a pure consequence reading is weakened by the hippocampus, but a composition reading is not: bulk TPM at n 2 to 4 in a line with a neuron-specific deletion (S = Synapsin-Cre) mixes neurons and glia, and a change in the share of delta-expressing neurons (interneuron subtypes) moves a bulk Gabrd without any per-cell change. (2) The P47T "independent replication" is of transcript direction only (FDR 0.06 to 0.09 in cortex, 0.34 in hippocampus); P47T keeps Wwox mRNA and, as far as the held material says, no burst phenotype is recorded there: so it shows that Gabrd falls where bursting is not shown, which fits a consequence-free "lower delta" but does not show that the fall matters for the burst. (3) In P47T the hippocampal fall (-0.93) is as large as the cortical (-0.80): the regional asymmetry then rests only on the baseline abundance argument (delta 10-fold higher in cortex), which is a statement about what the cell uses, not about what changed.
Separating test, no literature: Gabrd in the S-line at an age BEFORE bursts (P7-P9 if any bulk data exist; the held GSE158681/2 are P17 only?): a cause is already low before bursts, a consequence is not. If no pre-burst data are held, that is the experiment (b'): tonic current at P8-P10 against P13-P17 in the same line.

## The named killer test is not discriminating as written
D8 kills the idea if the tonic current (gabazine-sensitive holding-current shift) equals wild type. But the dossier itself (counter-evidence iii) predicts that lower GAT-1 and GAT-3 raise ambient GABA, which can keep the whole-cell tonic current normal despite fewer delta receptors. Then "tonic current equal" is the expected result under receptor loss + transporter compensation and kills a TRUE receptor deficit (a false kill), while "tonic current lower" could arise from lower ambient GABA alone (a transporter reversal or lower release) without any receptor change. The test must read receptor and ligand separately: (a) the delta-preferring agonist-evoked current (the receptor reserve, ligand saturated: the dossier lists a delta-preferring agonist as a research label) and (b) the tonic current with the GABA uptake condition fixed first and reported. Kill criterion: equal reserve AND equal tonic current at the same ambient GABA; "equal tonic current alone" should be read as uninformative about the receptor, informative about the network (network statement survives or dies on conductance, receptor statement on reserve). Say which statement is the idea: the receptor loss (D1 link 1) or the conductance loss (D1 link 4). They are not the same test.

## The model's headline is the least independent step
The tonic weight is "the most sensitive knob" by a one-at-a-time scan of an assumed-constant network tuned to two held medians. One-at-a-time sensitivity reports the parameter whose baseline value the modeller chose to sit near a threshold; it cannot tell what the null's true tonic weight is. Surprising prediction 3 (wild type is within 40% of threshold) is an output of that tuning: the wild-type parameters are, the dossier says, not fitted. I would not carry it as a prediction until a wild-type fit exists. The model result is a statement about how steep the model is, not about WWOX.

## What survives
The measurable core is sound and cheap: tonic current plus delta-agonist-evoked current in null neocortex, null hippocampus, heterozygote neocortex, wild type. The heterozygote arm is the one that discriminates cause from profile (graded 4/23 against 36/42) and should be in the experiment as a required arm, not an option (D5b lists it only as outcome 4).

## Experiment that separates the best rival (consequence or composition) from the idea
Same slices, three ages (P8-P10, P13-P15, P16-P17): delta-agonist-evoked current per pF and tonic current, in null and wild type neocortex, plus a bursting wild-type control (a slice made to burst pharmacologically): if the delta-evoked current falls in the bursting wild-type control too, it is a consequence of bursting; if it is low in null before bursts start, it is upstream.
Not scored. Cost to run is the slice experiment already in D5b plus an age axis and one pharmacologically bursting control group.
