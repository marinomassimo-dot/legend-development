* **Every primary number reproduces.** ADV-068 cortex: PV -0.653, other interneuron -0.054, late -0.334, oligodendrocyte guard -1.023, reference +0.050, label R-B. ADV-069 P53_RESP: raw -0.116 / -0.018, N1-adjusted -0.403 / +0.122, both inside the null band at percentiles 13.2 and 32.5, N2 genotype term -0.225 (range -0.283 to -0.165), not S-ok. * **What the check changed (decision-relevant):**
  1. **ADV-068 robustness was overstated by my reading of my own frozen text.** The R-B rule reads "late at most -0.25 (the same sign and at least half the PV shift)". I implemented only the -0.25 threshold, so only Slc12a5 flipped the label when omitted. If the parenthesis is an additional condition, the full set still gives R-B but by **0.007** (0.334 against 0.327), and **three** single-gene omissions (Slc12a5, Camk2a, Scn8a) give R-D. Both readings are now sensitivities; the frozen text is not rewritten. **What does not change:** R-A (PV-specific) fails under every reading and every single-gene omission (animal separation fails and the late set is not within 0.2), so no reading of the rule supports a PV-specific premise; the decision (no PV-directed arm on this evidence; a tissue measurement instead) does not depend on R-B against R-D.
  2. **ADV-069 label ambiguity.** If the adjusted shift were placed in the unadjusted null, cluster 1 would sit outside the band and the label would be S1 (signal in one cluster only) instead of my "absent". The N1 adjustment subtracts a proliferation shift that has opposite signs in the two clusters (+0.29 and -0.14), so the adjusted cluster 1 value is largely the proliferation difference, not the set; under either reading the set is not S-ok and the decision (no DNA-damage-directed class enters the set) is unchanged. Under the alternative of computing the genotype term on the N1-adjusted value it is -0.33 with a sign change on leave-one-library-out.
  3. **ADV-070 leverage.** Confirmed and quantified: the score correlates -0.78 with the arm; slopes take both signs; c exceeds Delta for Kcnq2, Npy and the PV panel; all five seeds STAND because the covariate does little. The covariate is also computed from the same animals as ADV-068's late set (the knockout animals' scores equal ADV-068's late values), so the two are not independent.
  4. Additional composition facts from the cortex deposit: microglia guard -0.94 and astrocyte guard +0.30, so glial composition moves beyond oligodendrocytes, and the neuronal reference set (+0.05) does not correct it.
