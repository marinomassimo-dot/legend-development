## What counts as a confound (frozen)
1. **Age definition:** if the Table 1 age column is age at last follow-up or at registry entry rather than age at memantine start, the "improved is younger" pattern is not an age-at-treatment pattern.
2. **Ascertainment:** the two members of a pair come from different source types (registry against published case report), or one was assessed and judged by a different method.
3. **Outcome scale:** the pair is judged on different outcome domains or scales.
4. **Exposure:** dose or duration differ by more than a factor of two when both are stated.
5. **Co-medication:** a different antiseizure regimen at assessment.
6. **Selection:** the source is a case report (publication of favourable outcomes) against a registry.

## What would make age a candidate separator (frozen)
Age at memantine start is stated for both members of **at least three of the four sets**, the improved member is younger in at least three of them, **and** none of those sets is explained by confounds 2, 3 or 6 (confounds 4 and 5 are recorded, not exclusionary). If age at start is stated for fewer than three sets, the answer is disposition (b), not a negative.

## Dispositions

## Frozen predictions
P1. The age column of Table 1 is **age at the time of the table or the report, not age at memantine start** (probability about one half).
P2. At least one of the four sets mixes a registry individual with a published case report (confound 2).
P3. Dose and duration are stated for fewer than half of the 34 individuals in any held or open source.
P4. The disposition is (b) or (c) for at least two of the four sets; (a) for the whole question is unlikely.
