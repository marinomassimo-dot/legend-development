"an APP/PS1 line (PMID 31781019; 8 APP/PS1 and 6 wild-type male mice, 5 to 6 months, ethosuximide 200 mg/kg intraperitoneal, within-animal, three-hour recordings) changed cortico-hippocampal spikes (down), fast-spindle spikes (down) and spindle-associated spikes (up) in the same direction in both genotypes by group means; the response of simple cortical spikes had opposite signs by group means once the one wild-type outlier the authors remove from the cortical analysis is excluded (wild type up, APP/PS1 down; neither significant), muscle-twitch spikes and spike-wave discharges showed no significant effect in either genotype, significance differs by genotype (wild type: spindle and fast-spindle; APP/PS1: cortico-hippocampal and fast-spindle), and no genotype-by-drug test exists (the authors write that the wild-type response 'seemed to follow' the APP/PS1 pattern)."
