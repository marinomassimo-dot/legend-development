## 0. Findings in six lines

1. Arshad 2026 HAS an open full text (PMC12992657) and an open supplement PDF; both were read. OV350 reduced high-amplitude spasms only on p11-p13 (n = 5 vs 8), E_GABA was measured only in untreated KO slices (p14-p21), pup exposure was not measured, no sedation/weight/temperature/survival control is stated, no WT+OV350 group.
2. OV350 is a fused-aminopyrimidine-series small molecule from an AstraZeneca library, licensed to Ovid in Dec 2021; direct binding was shown for the parent (CETSA), not OV350; Ovid pages (CO) call it an IV 'tool program' with a completed healthy-volunteer Phase 1; no epilepsy/DEE indication is named for any KCC2 agent; no ClinicalTrials.gov, ChEMBL or PubChem record by that name.
3. The CLP257 dispute: Cardarelli 2017 (full text, author manuscript) says no KCC2 effect and GABA-A potentiation independent of KCC2; the Gagnon reply was reached only as a one-paragraph publisher preview; Donneger 2026 reports CLP-257 is both a KCC2 enhancer and an extrasynaptic GABA-A modulator.
4. Pup data on KCC2 agents are thin: Sullivan 2021 (CLP290, P7-P12 mice, brain levels measured, no E_GABA), Doi 2021 (CLP290 + midazolam, male P7 rats), Arshad 2026 (OV350). In S940A pups and at P10 the CLP290 benefit was absent or reversed.
5. Second route on bumetanide in pups found pup data (Cleary 2013 FT, P10 male rat brain:serum 0.007-0.009; Welzel 2023 AB brain levels) and the premises that differ (section K4).
6. KCC2 reduction is documented in several DEE/NDD models and human SLC12A5 loss-of-function; no paper found showing KCC2 UP-regulation in a DEE model; a depolarising E_GABA shift at the seizure-onset age was shown only for non-genetic infantile-spasm rats (Baek 2016) and partially for Cdkl5 KO (p14-p21).
