> ⚠️ **Competing explanation for the marker phenotype itself (narrowed 2026-11-28, ADV-328).** Blood chemistry of the systemic Wwox-null mouse is held for P18 only (glucose, total CO2, BUN, calcium; n = 3/3/4, white cells n = 2 per group; pH and creatinine not measured; [[claim_registry_current#CLAIM 036]], PMID 19936220), so "acidotic" means reduced total CO2 and "leukopenic" rests on two animals per group. Glucose has a longer record: a second paper plots tail-tip glucose of untreated or GFP-injected Wwox-null pups below wild type from the first plotted age (day 10; about 85 against 122 mg/dL, read from the plot) to day 20 to 24 (about 45; n = 5 per group, SEM, no test printed; PMID 34747138, Appendix Fig S1C and D). Hypoglycaemia and acidosis could alter interneuron marker expression and glial reactivity; neither paper tests that. A systemic null therefore carries a metabolic confounder, but the held data do not make it independent of neuronal Wwox: restoring WWOX in neurons only (AAV9-SynI-WWOX at P0; about 52 to 71 percent of NeuN-positive cells WWOX-positive on the plots; oligodendrocytes and peripheral tissues reported as lacking WWOX) brings plotted glucose back to the wild-type range and lowers GFAP-positive and Iba1-positive cell counts per field in hippocampal CA3 at P19 (n = 3 per genotype; PMID 34747138, Fig 3E to G). The conditional allele has been used since 2021 (Nestin-, Synapsin-, Olig2- and Gfap-Cre; PMID 33914858): neuronal deletion reproduces seizures from P9, growth retardation and death between about day 17 and day 28; its P17 bulk RNA-seq (Synapsin-Cre knockout against control, cortex and hippocampus pooled, 6 against 6 samples) shows higher Npy, Sst and Gfap and lower Pvalb mRNA, and no protein or section readout of an interneuron marker or of glial reactivity and no blood chemistry is reported for any conditional animal. A cell-restricted deletion therefore does not by itself separate a cell-autonomous marker change from one secondary to seizures and systemic illness, because the neuron-restricted animals are also ill. Not found in the held texts (dated 2026-11-28): an interneuron-restricted deletion, or a deletion read before seizure onset (P9) with weight and glucose measured in the same animals.
