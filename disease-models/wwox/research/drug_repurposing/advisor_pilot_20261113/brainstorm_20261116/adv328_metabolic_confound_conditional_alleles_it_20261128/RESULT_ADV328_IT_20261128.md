The Appendix has been held since 2026-10-19 (`files/supplement/PMID34747138_r2/`); SCI's ADV-074 note of 2026-11-19 recorded it as "NOT held": an absent-artefact-misplaced case.

**B (conditional allele "not used in this direction"): false as written; the answer depends on what counts as a readout.** Frozen rule: restricted deletion + a named marker read with n printed. Held texts with a restricted deletion: Repudi 2021 Brain (PMID 33914858; Nestin-, Synapsin-, Olig2-, Gfap-Cre). Marker reads in its conditional animals are **bulk mRNA only** (Supplementary Table 1, Synapsin-Cre knockout against control, P17, cortex and hippocampus pooled, 6 against 6 samples, not tissue-balanced): Npy padj 4.34E-10, log2FC +1.89; Sst 2.64E-3, +1.06; Gfap 1.13E-2, +1.68; Pvalb 3.44E-2, -0.75. Repudi 2021 EMBO Mol Med uses neuron-restricted **restoration** (not deletion), so it is adjacent to the frozen rule, not a pass.

## What the sources add that the sentence did not hold
* Neuron-restricted deletion (S-KO) is itself ill: spontaneous seizures from P9 (text), growth retardation (about 6.5 against 11.3 g at day 16, four mice per genotype, read from plot) and death at about day 17 to 28 (plot; text 3 to 4 weeks), N-KO the same with no stated seizure onset. Olig2- and Gfap-Cre knockouts show no abnormality in weight, survival or gross appearance and have no transcript data. So a cell-restricted deletion does not by itself separate a cell-autonomous marker change from one secondary to seizures and growth failure.
* Neuron-restricted restoration (AAV9 Synapsin-I-WWOX at P0) brings plotted glucose to overlap wild type and lowers GFAP-positive and Iba1-positive cell counts at P19 (n = 3 per genotype, per field; WT-against-rescued not marked); the same pups also grow normally and have no spontaneous seizures (text), so glucose rescue is not separated from seizure or growth rescue. The vector reaches about 52 to 71 percent (plotted; denominator undefined), oligodendrocytes and the peripheral tissues examined lack WWOX (one pancreas panel visibly stained, weaker than wild type, not quantified); astrocyte, microglial and OPC WWOX not examined. "Neurons only" is therefore not claimed.
* Not found in the held texts (2026-11-28): an interneuron-restricted deletion; a deletion read before seizure onset with glucose measured in the same animals (weights at P4 and P7 exist).
