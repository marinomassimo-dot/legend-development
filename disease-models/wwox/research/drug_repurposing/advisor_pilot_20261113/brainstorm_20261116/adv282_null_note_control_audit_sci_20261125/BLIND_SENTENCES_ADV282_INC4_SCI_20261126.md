## DR-A02-007

Row fields: target_id: CBS p.I278T; endpoint: liver CBS activity under long, low-exposure bortezomib, alone or with a heat-shock co-inducer; direction: NO_CHANGE; reading_depth: partial_fulltext_read

Verbatim repository sentence (the note, whole):

> With tolerated exposure the activity gain was not significant and a white-cell and lymphocyte reduction was still present; homocysteine fell by about half (mean 345 to 179 micromolar, significant linear trend) yet stayed far above normal, with no significant activity gain, and one of eight treated animals had the largest response and was also the one with the greatest weight loss and lowest white-cell counts. The authors conclude that proteasome inhibitors are probably not feasible as monotherapy at non-toxic exposure.
