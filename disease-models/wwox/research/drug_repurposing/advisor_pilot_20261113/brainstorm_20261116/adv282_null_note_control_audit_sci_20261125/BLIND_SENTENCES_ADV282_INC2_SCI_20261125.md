## DA-K01-008

Row fields: target_id: sima:GFP (the single Drosophila HIF-alpha) fluorescence, adult fly brain; endpoint: whole-brain ROI fluorescence of a sima:GFP reporter after neuronal Wwox knockdown, amyloid-beta 42 background; direction: NO_CHANGE

Verbatim repository sentence (the note, whole):

> No change (t = 0.6623, P = 0.5185, N = 7 to 9 brains; amyloid background, one genotype pair, normoxia, not measured without the transgene or under hypoxia). The reporter's identity is internally inconsistent in the paper: the Results (and legend) say 'endogenously tagged sima:GFP', the Methods name 'the genetically encoded reporter line UAS-sima:GFP' (a driver-expressed transgene, whose level reports protein stability and not transcription), and the stock table lists a GFP-tagged sima PBac line (BDSC 42672). Sima is the single fly HIF-alpha, so no HIF-1 versus HIF-2 split is possible. The authors attribute the lactate rise to Atf4, not to sima. The only neural HIF-alpha protein readout in a WWOX-loss model, invertebrate, and not evidence of absence in mammals.

Source label the repository gives: PMID 42909904 (Lucas-Clarke 2026), Results (Figure 5F and G), Methods (imaging of the sima:GFP reporter) and supplementary stock table
