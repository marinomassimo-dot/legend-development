## Answer first
1. **Composition is stated in the readable record at one place only, and it is not layer 2/3 and not P13-17.** In mouse prelimbic/infralimbic **deep-layer (V-VI)** pyramidal neurons (slices from 19- to 25-day-old mice), single-cell RT-PCR found **Kir2.3 mRNA in every CaMKII-positive cell profiled (n = 15)**, Kir2.2 in about 64%, Kir2.1 in 30% (figure legend) or 43% (text; the paper gives both), Kir2.4 in 7%; protein was shown by immunocytochemistry for Kir2.2 and Kir2.3 in **cultured** cortical neurons (soma and dendrites). The authors conclude that the Ba2+-sensitive inward-rectifier current is "attributable to channels containing Kir2.3, Kir2.2, and, to some extent, Kir2.1 subunits" (Day 2005). This is detection of messenger RNA, not abundance, not assembly, and not a share of the current.
2. **For layer 2/3 pyramidal neurons at any age I found no composition statement; for P13-17 none.** The nearest values of the share of resting conductance are the two adjacent-age rows already held (IT48-Q01: about 68% at 200 uM, P26-51; about 23% at 60 uM, P28-42), which say nothing about subunits.
3. **A "Ba2+-sensitive current" at 200 uM is not a Kir2-only current.** The same paper reproduces the 200 uM Ba2+-sensitive current of deep-layer pyramidal neurons with a Kir2 component and a linear K2P-type leak "conductance ratio of 10:1, G Kir2 / G Kleak" in a simulation, and detects KCNK3 and KCNK9 mRNA in 8 cells.
4. **Ba2+ sensitivity by subunit and by heteromer: the primary papers are closed to me.** The two primary sources an open-access paper cites for it (Liu 2001, J Physiol; Schram 2003, Cardiovasc Res) are not in PMC; the DOI page of Schram 2003 returned a Cloudflare challenge ("Just a moment...", HTTP 403), a STOP. What I can state is a **secondary** statement (Yang 2015, arterioles): homomeric Kir2.1 to Kir2.4 "differ significantly" in Ba2+ block, and heteromers (Kir2.1/2.2, Kir2.1/2.3, Kir2.3/2.4 in oocytes) show a current "similar to cardiac IK1 in both the sensitivity to Ba2+ block and kinetics". No number was read.
5. **Kir2.1-directed tools reach Kir2.1 homotetramers only, in recombinant cells.** Flecainide increases the outward current of Kir2.1 homotetramers through **Cys311**, the only residue that differs between Kir2.1 and Kir2.2/Kir2.3 in the strand concerned; Kir2.2 and Kir2.3 are not increased; **Kir2.1+Kir2.2 and Kir2.1+Kir2.3 heteromers are not increased** ("flecainide failed to increase inward and outward currents ... demonstrating that its effects were only apparent in channels composed of four Kir2.1 subunits"), and "flecainide also failed to increase IKir2.1 generated in cells cotransfected with both WT and C311A" (Caballero 2010; CHO and HEK cells, human channels, native guinea-pig ventricular tissue in other panels; no neuron).
6. **One sentence on whether a Kir2.1-specific tool or a Cys311-type activator could act on the juvenile L2/3 current at all (INFERRED, not measured):** on the readable record it could act only on Kir2.1 homotetramers, and the only neuronal composition datum (deep-layer pyramidal neurons, mRNA) detects Kir2.3 in every cell and Kir2.1 in a minority, so at most on a minor part of the Ba2+-sensitive current; whether any Kir2.1 homotetramers exist in juvenile L2/3 pyramidal neurons is not stated anywhere I could read.
7. **Premise correction.** "Cys311 status" is not a property of a neuron's current; it is a property of the subunit (Kir2.1 has it, Kir2.2 and Kir2.3 do not, per Caballero 2010), so the question reduces to whether homotetrameric Kir2.1 exists in the neurons of interest. The question splits into (a) composition (answered only for deep-layer cells, at mRNA level) and (b) tool reach (answered only for recombinant channels).

## What the record cannot state for juvenile L2/3 pyramidal neurons
* The **composition** of the Ba2+-sensitive current at any age in layer 2/3 and at P13-P17 anywhere: no mRNA, protein or assembly datum was found.
* Whether **heteromers** form in these neurons: no co-immunoprecipitation, FRET or heteromer-selective pharmacology in neurons was read; the Kir2 heteromer data read are recombinant or cardiac, or secondary.
* The **share** of the current carried by Kir2 versus K2P versus other channels at a given Ba2+ concentration (no concentration series per subunit was read).
* The **Ba2+ affinity of Kir2.3 and of heteromers relative to Kir2.1** in numbers (primary papers closed).
* Whether **homotetrameric Kir2.1** exists in these neurons, and therefore whether any Cys311-type tool has a target there.
* Any **developmental trend** of the subunits across P8 to P25 (the one composition datum is P19-25, deep layers).

## One hypothesis the record suggests (IPOTESI, a composition probe, not a treatment)
Caballero 2010 used the flecainide response itself to read native composition (guinea-pig ventricular IK1 "mainly carried by Kir2.1 homotetramers", atrial IK1 by "Kir2.x heterotetramers"). By the same logic an increase of the Ba2+-sensitive outward current by a Cys311-type agent in a neuron would mark Kir2.1-homotetrameric channels and its absence would not; the agent studied is also a Na+ channel blocker, so any such reading would need Na+ channels blocked first. Prediction before data: in juvenile L2/3 pyramidal neurons the Ba2+-sensitive outward current changes by less than the recombinant Kir2.1 homotetramer value (+22% at -120 mV, +54% at -50 mV in CHO cells, SCI49), consistent with a Kir2.3/Kir2.2-dominated composition; probability 0.65, an increase of the recombinant size 0.15, no change at all 0.20. Not run; needs a laboratory.

## Consequence for INV-18 step 2b (suggestion to the owners; nothing edited here)
A high-Ba2+ step cannot be read as Kir2-only (K2P leak is part of the 200 uM current in the one deep-layer analysis), and no subunit assignment exists for juvenile L2/3 neurons, so a **Kir2.1-specific or Cys311-type tool is not a justified positive control for the neuronal current** on this record; the family-level probe route stays as the SCI49 package B framed it (a pan-Kir2 comparator, with K2P and KATP exclusions) and the subunit question stays an open measurement (single-cell RT-PCR or immunostaining at P13-P17 in layer 2/3).
