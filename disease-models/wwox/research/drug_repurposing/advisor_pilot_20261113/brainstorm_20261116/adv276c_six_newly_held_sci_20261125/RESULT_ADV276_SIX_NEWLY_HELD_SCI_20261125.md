## 4. Class of correction and lessons
**Class: an overexpression result carried as an endogenous one** (27845895); the other five are additions of window (T3 schedule), evidence level (animal models, review framing), threshold (suggestive p) and a missing citation. Lessons: a title that says "when overexpressed" is a premise, not a detail; a claim of absence in a paper ("no apparent cell death") may rest on no assay; a legend, a Results sentence and a plot can say three things about one cell line; a printed OR outside its own CI is a paper defect to note, not to carry.
