## 1. Answer first
**Yes, one: grid A with limits.** A human **ovarian carcinoma line** (HO8910, one hospital laboratory, a different group and platform from the neural-differentiation platform of Balmer, Rempel and Waldmann) shows **WWOX protein UP with sodium valproate**, monotonic over four concentrations on a loading control, and WWOX mRNA and protein UP in xenograft tumours of valproate-treated mice. It agrees with the direction already carried in DL-REPO-003 (UP) and is **not independent of itself**: it is one cell line, one blot per panel, and no replicate unit is printed. DL-REPO-003 does not name it today (grep of the discovery ledger, 2026-11-25); the 2026-09-22 census carried it as abstract-level row B1 ("no PMC, no open access"); it was **never read**. It now is, in the journal's own open PDF.
It does **not** move the disease question: a cancer line with wild-type WWOX, concentrations of 1 to 4 mmol/L, no WWOX allele, no neural cell. Direction only.

## 2. What was read
Source: Yan H-C and Zhang J, *Asian Pacific J Cancer Prev* 2012;13(12):6429-6433 (PMID 23464470); journal PDF `files/fulltext/PMID23464470_Yan2012_APJCP_journal_pdf_2026-11-25.pdf`, sha256 `c2a1066186030672a2a4c4ed0ad6efe82a594cb370073f41a2c4fc610485d778`, 5 pages. **Route:** the DOI redirects to a KoreaScience page that did not answer (no HTTP response, twice); Crossref returned only a similarity-checking link, which I did not use; a web search named the journal's own article page and the PDF linked from it, fetched with the default client. Europe PMC and PMC hold no copy (checked). The 2026-09-22 census wrote "no open access": that was a route failure, not a held status.
Read: abstract, Methods, Results, Figure 3 to 5 images and legends. Not read: Introduction, Discussion beyond its opening, references.
* **In vitro design.** HO8910 cells, control and 1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 4.0 mmol/L sodium valproate, 48 h; Western blot 30 ug protein, anti-WWOX 1:250 (antibody source named, specificity not shown), beta-actin as the loading and blotting control on the same stripped membrane; RT-PCR end-point, 30 cycles, WWOX 598 bp, normalised to GAPDH; statistics "Student t test, one-way ANOVA, q test", P below 0.05.
* **In vitro result.** Text: WWOX "significantly and gradually increased (P below 0.05) compared with control ... in a dose-dependent manner"; **no WWOX number is printed** in the text for the in vitro arm. Figure 4 (one blot, five lanes): pixel estimate WWOX over beta-actin 0.44 (control), 0.48, 0.72, 1.20, 1.40 at 1, 2, 3, 4 mmol/L, i.e. 1.09, 1.63, 2.72, 3.17 times control (script `hdac_fig4_densit.py`; JPEG estimate, lane windows equal fifths, not a measurement). The actin band is flat to 3 mmol/L and about 11 percent lower at 4 mmol/L (raw 91,127; 94,921; 97,047; 91,522; 80,908), which inflates the top ratio slightly; the monotonic increase stands. P27, the paper's other readout, rises 0.51 to 1.99 over the same lanes.
* **In vivo design.** 20 female nude mice, ten per arm (equally divided), 0.4 percent valproate in the drinking water, tumours taken at the animal's death. **Not printed:** intake, serum level, number of tumours sampled, any per-animal quantification. Mean survival 47.12 +/- 0.73 against 34.58 +/- 0.46 days: the two arms were therefore sampled at different times.
* **In vivo result.** Tumour WWOX protein 0.46 +/- 0.065 against 0.15 +/- 0.025 (P below 0.05); mRNA 0.305 +/- 0.015 against 0.193 +/- 0.056 (P below 0.05). Figure 5A, two lanes per arm shown ("representative"), Figure 5B eight lanes with a marker (lanes 2 to 4 control, 5 to 8 experimental), bands visibly stronger in the valproate lanes. The legend of 5A says P21 where the text says P27, and the text says HO9810 once for HO8910: two typographical slips, noted, not weighted.
* **Predictions of the PREREG:** (1) monotonic WWOX increase on a loading control that does not itself trend up: met; (2) an n per group and a per-animal quantification: **not** present, as predicted; (3) no printed in vitro WWOX number: as predicted; (4) not independent of its own other readouts: yes.

## 3. Against DL-REPO-003
DL-REPO-003 says: four of five primaries read, WWOX only as probe-set rows from one hESC-to-neural platform of one group (0.6 to 2 mmol/L, up to 14 days), direction UP wherever a sign is recoverable, the fifth primary unread. This paper adds:
* a **second platform**: human ovarian carcinoma line, 1 to 4 mmol/L, 48 h, protein and end-point mRNA, different laboratory; direction UP at both levels;
* a **second concentration range** that overlaps the hESC range only at 2 mmol/L (the first dose where the pixel estimate is clearly above control is 2 mmol/L, 1.6 times);
* **no resolution of the fifth primary** (PMID 28001369, Waldmann 2017, same group as Rempel; ACS journal, not open access, no receipt, not held): that stays owed.
It also leaves unchanged: every number in this paper is a one-blot, one-line figure with no replicate unit; the HO8910 line is a cancer line with its own epigenetic state (the census notes a demethylation paper on the same line from the same city: probably not independent of that one, a different drug and a different question); a human xenograft in a mouse carries mouse stroma and the antibody and primer species specificity are not stated.

## 4. Proposed wording (applied as a dated addition in the discovery ledger entry on this branch)
*DL-REPO-003, dated addition (IT, 2026-11-25): a second platform now read at figure level: a human ovarian carcinoma line (HO8910, PMID 23464470, journal PDF) shows WWOX protein UP with sodium valproate 1 to 4 mmol/L over 48 h (pixel estimate 1.6 to 3.2 times control at 2 to 4 mmol/L, one blot, loading control beta-actin) and WWOX protein and mRNA UP in xenograft tumours (0.46 against 0.15; 0.305 against 0.193; ten mice per arm, tumours taken at death 47 versus 35 days, n per measurement not stated). Direction agrees with the hESC platform; it is a cancer line with wild-type WWOX, one blot per panel, no replicate unit; it does not address a neural cell, a WWOX allele or a patient. The fifth primary (PMID 28001369, same group as the hESC platform) stays unread.*
