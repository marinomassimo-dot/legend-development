## Answer first
1. **The baseline "WOREE against SCAR12" activity comparison does not exist as one comparison.** Activity is measured in two different assays at two ages: week-7 cell-attached recording (spikes per neuron, AM Fig. 1D) and week-16 calcium imaging (events per region, amplitude, share of active regions; AM Fig. 6A(ii), the AAV9-EGFP arms). **No held figure uses the same assay at both ages**, so "equal at week 7, 3-fold apart at week 16" is a statement across assays, not a time course. Neither age prints a WOREE-against-SCAR12 test.
2. **Week 7 (cell-attached, n = neurons, one experiment):** WT about 105 spikes per neuron; all four mutant lines about 330 to 370; brackets run from WT to each mutant (all ****) and none among the mutants. The text's "similar neuronal hyperactivity" is **VISUAL** (lines within about 10 percent; groups are lines, not genotypes; units are neurons, 14 to 30 per line). The AM cites "Fig. 1B(i) and Fig. 1C" for it; the quantification is Fig. 1D (a locator defect).
3. **Week 16 (calcium imaging, EGFP arms of Fig. 6, infected organoids):** rate 0.257 vs 0.075 Hz (ratio 3.4; **NOT PRINTED** as a test), amplitude 0.069 vs 0.061 (ratio 1.1), active regions 100 vs 100 percent. **The 3-fold gap is in event rate alone**; amplitude and share of active regions do not separate the genotypes (the share is at ceiling).
4. **Cortical markers move differently with age in SCAR12:** SATB2 fraction is reduced at week 7 (*** against WT, immunofluorescence) but not bracketed against WT at week 16 (Fig. 6B), while the preprint's week-16 SATB2 **mRNA** for SCAR12 is about 3.4 times WT (bracketed, one asterisk). WOREE is low at both ages by immunofluorescence (week 7 ****, week 16 *) and CTIP2 mRNA about 0.2 of WT (*).
5. **Radial glia:** week 7 SOX2-positive fraction is expanded in both patient-derived genotypes (WT about 13 percent, SCAR12 about 47, WOREE about 44; **** each against WT; no SCAR12-WOREE bracket). Week 16 scRNA (AM Fig. 2, one sample per genotype, no test) shows the large expansion only for WWOX-KO; WOREE about +0.07 log2 and SCAR12 about -0.27 log2.
6. **At the transcript level the two patient-derived genotypes are close:** the AM text itself reports only two genes differentially expressed between SCAR12 and WOREE neurons (preprint EV9C: two ribosomal genes beyond a 0.5 log fold-change line), and the early-neuron share is about 0.33 in WT, about 0.66 in WOREE and about 0.68 in SCAR12 (Fig. 5F; log2 fold changes about +1.0 and -1.0 for both). **Neither the transcriptome nor the early-neuron share separates WOREE from SCAR12.**
7. **Rows A to F:** the baseline data weaken row B as the explanation of the *WOREE-SCAR12 rate gap* (early-neuron shares equal) and leave A, C, E and a line effect open; row D stays untestable (EV8 annotates excitatory clusters plus one mixed cluster; no interneuron cluster). Details below.
8. **Pre-registered predictions:** P1, P3, P5, P6 held; P2 held at genotype level only; P4 not testable (no per-line quantification is printed). The rejecting observation (a printed WOREE-against-SCAR12 test for any of the five metrics) is **not met**.

## What changes between week 7 and week 16 in the WOREE-SCAR12 activity gap (question b)
* **Printed:** nothing. No test between WOREE and SCAR12 exists at either age, and the two ages use different assays (single-neuron spike counts over 4 minutes against region-level calcium events), different units (neurons against regions from organoids), different preparation states (uninfected against AAV9-EGFP-infected organoids) and different line groupings (lines plotted separately against genotypes pooled).
* **Eyeballed:** week 7, the six boxes overlap and medians sit within about 10 percent; week 16, the event-rate bars differ 3.4-fold while amplitude and active share do not.
* **Scale warning:** 350 spikes per neuron in 4 minutes is about 1.5 Hz per neuron; the week-16 event rate is 0.075 to 0.257 Hz per region. The two numbers are not on a comparable scale and must not be read as a fall in activity with age.
* **Consequence:** "equality at week 7 and divergence at week 16" is **not established** by the held material. It would need the same assay at both ages in the same lines; the AM also cites a Supplementary Fig. 2E for cell-attached data, which I could not obtain.
