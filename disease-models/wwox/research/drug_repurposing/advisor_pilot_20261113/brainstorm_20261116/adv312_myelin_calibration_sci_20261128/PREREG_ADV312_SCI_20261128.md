## Two explanations and the fixed readings
* **E-A:** structure predicts conduction in direction but not in size (a structural claim cannot carry a velocity).
* **E-B:** size also tracks across sources.
* **Effect size:** for each source the primary structural variable and the primary conduction variable are each expressed as ln(mutant or treated / control); the structural variable is the paper's own headline EM variable, with g-ratio and myelinated-axon density kept as two sub-classes; the conduction variable is velocity if given, else latency (sign inverted so that slower = negative), else amplitude (reported separately and not used for the rank test).
* **Direction agreement:** structural change toward less myelin AND slower or lower conduction (or the reverse in a gain-of-myelin preparation) counts as agreement; a structural change with the opposite or null conduction change (|ln ratio| below 0.05 for conduction and above 0.10 for structure) counts as a direction disagreement.
* **Predicted observable (frozen):** with N counted sources, E-A predicts at most 1 direction disagreement and no rank agreement (Spearman rho of |structural ln ratio| against |conduction ln ratio| below 0.5 in absolute value); E-B predicts at most 1 direction disagreement and rho of 0.7 or more. **My prediction: E-A.** Fewer than 2 direction disagreements and rho below 0.5, or no testable rho.
* **Readable table:** at least 4 CALIBRATION PAIRS for the direction count; at least 6 for a rank statement (below 6 the rank agreement is not stated and the sizes are listed side by side). Fewer than 4 = TOO FEW SOURCES: the sources and the PARTIAL ones are listed and the missing observation is registered (a central white-matter source with EM and a numbered CAP velocity or latency in the same animals, with n and stimulus).
* **Strong counterexample to look for:** a source with a large structural change (myelinated-axon density or proportion changed by 25 percent or more, or g-ratio changed by 0.05 or more) and a null conduction change (|ln ratio| below 0.05), which contradicts the direction reading; or a source with a conduction change of 20 percent or more and no structural change (|ln ratio| below 0.05), which contradicts structure as the carrier. Palma 2022 (small g-ratio change, 16 percent velocity change) is the seed of the second kind and is counted as one disagreement if it meets the rule.
