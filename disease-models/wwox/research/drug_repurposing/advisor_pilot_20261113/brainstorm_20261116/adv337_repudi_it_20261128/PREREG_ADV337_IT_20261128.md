## Classes (given by the blind reader, per sentence)
- **W** within: the paper supports the sentence and no printed limit of the paper would change what a reader does with it.
- **Q** qualified: the paper's n, unit, quantification, driver, age, or the partial loss in the S-KO row narrows it; the reader names which.
- **N** not supported or contradicted by the paper.
- **O** not a statement taken from this paper, or outside the four topics (neuron necessity or sufficiency, knockout completeness or specificity, interneurons, myelin and oligodendrocytes) and glial or survival statements taken from it.
- **U** the paper and its supplement cannot settle it.
Assessed = W + Q + N. "Beyond" = Q + N.

## Explanations, predictions, rejecting observation
- **E1** consuming sentences already carry the paper's limits. Predicts beyond at most 10 percent of assessed.
- **E2** a substantial share state completeness or necessity beyond them. Predicts beyond at least one third of assessed.
- IT's expectation: between 15 and 33 percent (E2 not met, E1 rejected).
- **Frozen rejecting observation:** beyond below one third rejects E2; beyond above 10 percent rejects E1; beyond = 0 means a receipt only and no wording anywhere.
- **Counterexample accepted against E2:** a sentence that states a loss or a phenotype for a named driver and age with the paper's own quantification counts W even though S-KO loss is incomplete, because it does not depend on completeness.
