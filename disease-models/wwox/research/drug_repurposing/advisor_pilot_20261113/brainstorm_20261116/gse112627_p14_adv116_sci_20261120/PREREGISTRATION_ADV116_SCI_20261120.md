## Condition 1 verified first, from the paper (PMID 30347190, PMC6287761; full text obtained by NCBI E-utilities, sha256 2e24828516bb665bd7f9e43011400242c8e70347bf88aff18c623bdeab432f88, text read by me; not recorded in the receipt ledger)
* **Onset:** "Seizure onset in F1.Scn1a+/- mice occurs between P16-19 (Hawkins et al., 2017; Miller et al., 2014)": cited from earlier work, not measured in this cohort; this cohort was video-monitored P21 to P25; the paper itself writes "At P14 before symptom onset". **P14 is pre-onset by the paper's own statement.**
* **Design and the strain-genotype confound:** hippocampi from 129 and [129 x B6]F1 wild-type and Scn1a+/- mice at P14 and P24; **wild-type controls are F1 and 129 genotype-matched, the F1 controls being littermates** of the F1 Scn1a+/- ("[129 x B6]F1.WT littermate controls"). **The genotype contrast at P14 is therefore made inside each strain, and genotype is NOT confounded with strain**; strain differs between the two contrasts, not within one. Litter identity of individual animals is not stated beyond that.
* **Replication unit:** "2-3 superpooled biological replicates" per group, each superpool = total RNA of 3 to 4 primary pools of 4 hippocampi (2 male, 2 female) = **12 to 16 mice per superpool**. The unit I analyse is the **superpool (named: a pool, not an animal)**; animal-level variance is invisible, sex is balanced inside every pool and therefore cannot be tested.
* **What the paper already states at P14 (seen before this pre-registration):** no hippocampal DEG between 129.WT and 129.Scn1a+/-, and only 21 DEGs "with modest differences (6 to 12 percent fold change)" in F1, enriched for "seizures" and ion transport/homeostasis terms; the gene names are in Supplementary Table 2, which I have not opened; at P24 after seizures the shared DEGs are partly astrogliosis genes (Gfap, Vim, Serpine1, Timp1 and others named in the Methods for qPCR); a GFAP protein trend after seizures. Nothing in the text reports glycolytic or oxidative-phosphorylation genes or a microglial set at P14.
* **Condition 1 passes** (pre-onset age by the paper; within-strain genotype contrast; 3 vs 3 superpools in F1 per the GEO sample names read earlier: P14_F1_KO 15, 16, 17 and P14_F1_WT 18, 19, 20; 129: P14_129_KO 21, 22, 23 and P14_129_WT 24, 25, that is 3 vs 2). If the counts file's headers disagree with these sample numbers, I stop and report.

## Fixed analysis (before any value)
* **Data:** `GSE112627_Normalized_counts_file_on_ALL_groups.tab.gz` only; **only P14 columns**; P24 columns are not opened for any computation. **Primary contrast: F1 Scn1a+/- (3) against F1 WT (3). Secondary, descriptive: 129 Scn1a+/- (3) against 129 WT (2)**, to see whether a difference is specific to the susceptible strain (n too small for a formal genotype-by-strain test; none is run).
* **Sets (same genes as ADV-115, fixed there before any search):** ASTRO_REACTIVE: Gfap, Vim, C3, Lcn2, Cd44, Serpina3n, Stat3; MICRO_ID: Cx3cr1, P2ry12, Csf1r; MICRO_ACT: Aif1, Cd68, Trem2, Tyrobp, Lyz2, Itgax; ENERGY_GLYC: Pdk1, Pdk2, Pdk3, Pdk4, Ldha, Slc16a1, Hk2, Pfkp, Pkm, Slc2a1; ENERGY_OXPHOS: Cox4i1, Ndufa4, Atp5f1a (alias Atp5a1), Uqcrc1, Sdha, Cs. Composition control: Aqp4, Aldh1l1, Slc1a2 (astrocyte identity). A gene absent from the file is dropped and counted; a set with fewer than 4 genes present is NOT SCORED.
* **Statistic:** per gene, d = mean log2(normalised value + 1) in knockout minus mean in wild type; **set score D = mean of d over the set's genes**. Direction predicted under the "early, before seizures" reading: **higher** in ASTRO_REACTIVE, MICRO_ACT, ENERGY_GLYC; no prediction for MICRO_ID or ENERGY_OXPHOS; every test is two-sided.
* **Null:** 10,000 random gene sets of the same size, each member drawn at random from the genes whose mean log2 expression across the six P14 F1 samples lies in the same expression decile as the corresponding set gene (genes of the sets themselves excluded); empirical two-sided p; **Holm across the five scored sets** reported beside the raw p.
* **Verdict classes (primary contrast):** DIFFERENT only if (i) |D| is at least 0.10 log2 units (about 7 percent, the lower end of the paper's own "modest" 6 to 12 percent DEG differences), (ii) raw empirical p at most 0.05, (iii) **leave-one-gene-out** keeps the sign and |D| at least 0.07 for every omitted gene, and (iv) **leave-one-sample-out** (each of the six samples dropped in turn) keeps the sign. BOUNDARY if exactly one of (i) to (iv) fails; NOT DIFFERENT if (i) and (ii) both fail. **BOUNDARY verdicts are reported as boundary, never as a result.** Holm is reported; it does not change a class but is stated so the reader can see the multiplicity.
* **Composition caveat (bulk and pooled):** if a reactivity set and its identity set (ASTRO_REACTIVE with the astrocyte identity trio; MICRO_ACT with MICRO_ID) both differ in the same direction, the label is "composition or state, not separable".
* **Competing explanation and weakening observation (ADV):** strain or litter effect and cell composition; any difference at P14 could reflect pools, litters, a sex-balanced average that hides sex-specific change, or a difference that disappears in the 129 background. **The observation that weakens a "difference before onset":** no set DIFFERENT, a set that differs in the 129 background in the same direction as in F1 (then not tied to the symptomatic background), or a verdict that fails leave-one-out.
* **Stop rule:** one sitting; five sets, two contrasts, one age; no new sets, no other age, no change of threshold after seeing any value; if the file cannot be read or its columns do not match the sample numbers above, stop and report.
