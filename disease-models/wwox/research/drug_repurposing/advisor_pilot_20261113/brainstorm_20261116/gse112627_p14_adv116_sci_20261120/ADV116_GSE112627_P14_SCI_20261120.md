## Answer first
1. **No pre-registered set is DIFFERENT at P14 in the primary contrast (F1 Scn1a+/- against F1 wild type, 3 against 3 superpools).** Scored sets: ASTRO_REACTIVE D = +0.143 log2 (raw p 0.34; NOT DIFFERENT, two criteria fail), MICRO_ACT D = -0.252 (raw p 0.069, Holm 0.28; **BOUNDARY**, and in the **opposite direction** to the early-activation prediction), ENERGY_GLYC D = +0.014 (NOT DIFFERENT), ENERGY_OXPHOS D = +0.028 (NOT DIFFERENT). **The weakening observation named in the pre-registration ("no set DIFFERENT") is met.**
2. **MICRO_ID and the astrocyte identity trio were NOT SCORED**: each has 3 genes, and the pre-registered rule needs at least 4. So the **composition control could not be run**, and no statement about composition versus state is made.
3. **The descriptive 129 contrast (the resistant strain, 3 against 2) shows what noise looks like in a genotype comparison with no phenotype**: ASTRO_REACTIVE D = -0.390 (raw p 0.0089, Holm 0.036) but **one gene carries it** (Lcn2 -2.80 log2; leave-one-gene-out fails: BOUNDARY), and ENERGY_GLYC D = +0.075 (raw p 0.0136, Holm 0.041) carried by Hk2 (+0.45) and below the 0.10 threshold. **Set shifts of this size occur in the strain without a phenotype; they do not read as pre-onset change in the susceptible one.**
4. **What this is and is not.** At one age, 2 to 5 days before the documented onset (P16 to P19, taken from the paper's citations of earlier work, not measured in this cohort), in bulk hippocampus, in pools of 12 to 16 mice, **no glial-reactivity, microglial-activation, glycolytic or oxidative-phosphorylation set difference is detectable at the pre-registered sensitivity** (a set-mean shift of at least 0.10 log2, about 7 percent, with 3 against 3 pools). It does **not** show absence of a change: a smaller shift, a cell-type-restricted change, a change in a few genes or a sex-specific change would pass unseen.
5. **Implication for the Wwox satellite draws (6.18, 6.19), as a transfer prior only:** the S-KO onset is P9, so **P7 is the analogue of this P14** (two days before onset against 2 to 5 days here). Under this prior a glial or energy departure at P5 or P7 would be **unexpected** (a null there is what the transfer prior predicts, so a null at P7 weakens the "glia or energy come first" reading less than a positive would strengthen it, and a positive at P5 or P7 is the informative outcome). The draws stay as designed. Strong caveats: a heterozygous interneuron-channel model, hippocampus, a different strain background and a different onset age, not the neuronal-deletion Wwox cortex; the prior is silent on the null pup (earlier onset).

## Competing explanations and the observation that would weaken, per the assignment
Strain or litter effects and composition were not separable by design here (litter identity of pools is not stated; the composition control was not scorable). **Weakening observations:** none of the sets differs (met); a set differing in the 129 strain in the same direction as in F1 (not met for the F1 sets that moved: ASTRO_REACTIVE is positive in F1 and negative in 129; MICRO_ACT is negative in both, which is what a non-specific pool or litter effect would look like). The MICRO_ACT boundary is therefore **not** read as a pre-onset microglial change.

## Limits
Pools of 12 to 16 mice hide animal-level variance and sex-specific change; the effective n is 3 against 3 (129: 3 against 2); the random-set null controls for expression-level selection, not for sample-level noise (hence the leave-one-sample-out criterion); bulk hippocampus mixes cell types; the set-level sensitivity is a 0.10 log2 shift, and the paper itself describes the P14 F1 genotype differences as "modest" (6 to 12 percent, 21 genes); the full text was read as text with no figures or supplementary tables, and the paper's Supplementary Table 2 (the 21 genes) was not opened; the onset age is cited, not measured in this cohort. A transfer prior at one age; nothing about Wwox; no dose; not medical advice.
