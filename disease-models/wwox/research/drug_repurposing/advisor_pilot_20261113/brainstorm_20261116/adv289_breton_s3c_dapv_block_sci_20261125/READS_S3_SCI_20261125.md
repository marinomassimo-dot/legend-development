## 3. What the reads say, and what limits them (labels as in the PREREG)

* PRIMARY (preregistered): d-APV amplitude at 100 uA is 0.41 of baseline (0.40 of the 100 uA baseline unit), block 0.59, error about 0.01 from the read plus the drawn error bar (about 0.07 to 0.10, type not stated). At the other strengths of 40 to 50 uA the ratio to the same-strength baseline is 0.45 to 0.46 (block 0.54 to 0.55). The checkpoint's "about 0.4 of baseline at 40 to 100 uA" holds for the 100 uA unit (0.42, 0.41, 0.40) but the ratio to the same-strength baseline is 0.46, 0.45, 0.41.
* Rejecting observation (a) (ratio at or above 0.7): NOT met; point 0.41 and upper bound well below 0.7.
* Descriptive limits, not tests: (i) the amplitude measure has a floor. The knockout reads 0.078 mV at 5 uA and the wildtype 0.089 to 0.115 mV at 10 to 100 uA, where the wildtype shows no delayed deflection (caption and text), so a response that is fully blocked would still read about 0.08 to 0.115 mV, which is 0.17 to 0.25 of the knockout baseline at 100 uA (0.458 mV). (ii) EXPLORATORY, not preregistered and decided after seeing the figure: if that floor is additive, the fraction of the response ABOVE the floor that d-APV blocked is (0.41 - f) / (1 - f) with f = 0.17 to 0.25, a residual of 0.28 to 0.21 and a block of about 0.72 to 0.79; it assumes the ratio of the means describes the mean of the per-slice ratios and that the wildtype amplitude is a fair floor, neither shown. (iii) 50 uM is the only concentration, so the plot gives one point on a concentration axis, not a curve; whether 0.6 is the ceiling of d-APV in this assay is not shown (a concentration-response is not in the paper; the plateau of a competitive antagonist at 50 uM is an ASSUMPTION, not read in a held source here).
* Washout: the evoked response is ABOVE baseline after washout, 1.29 at 100 uA, 1.36 at 40 uA, 1.22 at 50 uA by the same normalisation, p of 0.0492, 0.0372, 0.0499 against baseline in the caption's own slice-unit unpaired tests (three points; the others 0.94, 0.25, 0.20), the washout time is not stated. So "reversibly suppressed" (main text) is a return AND overshoot of the evoked response, in the same direction as the burst-rate washout bar of about 1.8 times baseline of Fig. 3D (a held reading, per-slice values not recoverable). For the brief's post-washout rule (a fall is a lasting change only if the evoked response is back within 10% of baseline) the held experiment's evoked response is outside that band on the high side.
* Unit and tests: the caption's t-tests are unpaired on slices (n = 6 slices, 3 animals) although the same slices are measured before, during and after d-APV (paired design): the pairing is discarded and the slices are the unit. No test is run here; the animal-level n is 3, one value per slice is not printed.
