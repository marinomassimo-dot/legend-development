## 5. Placement on the depth-to-rate map of brief 13.11

The map is the MODEL's (an assumed linear map from evoked-response depth to NMDAR weight; about 30 percent of the rate lost at 10 percent loss of the weight; half at 15 to 20 percent; floor at deep block; hypothesis generator, one parameter set). The held point is block of about 0.6 of the measured amplitude (0.7 to 0.8 above the floor) with spontaneous bursts reported eliminated (a held statement; the 51 bursts are presumably the baseline count, the paper does not say). At any depth beyond about 0.3 the model's burst rate is at its floor, so the held point is CONSISTENT with the model and carries no discrimination between the model and any rival that also abolishes bursts at deep block; it does not test the steep range (about 0.05 to 0.30), where the series discriminates R-A, R-D and R-E. It also cannot be placed as a rate: the paper gives the rate as eliminated, not as a number.
