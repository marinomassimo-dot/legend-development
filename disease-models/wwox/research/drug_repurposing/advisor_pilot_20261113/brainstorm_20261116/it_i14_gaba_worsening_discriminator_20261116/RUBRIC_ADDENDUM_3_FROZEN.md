# D5b rubric addendum 3 (IT; frozen by digest before the list freeze and before any search; requested by ADV for I19 and SCI's second reading of I2; non-canonical; not medical advice)

Written for: the D5b extractor and the scorer. Added per child: genotype_severity_class with three levels, defined before the first new report is opened and applied from the text alone:
* biallelic_loss_or_null_like: both alleles described as null, deletion, truncating, nonsense, frameshift or canonical splice change, or stated protein absent;
* residual_or_missense: at least one allele described as missense or in-frame change, with no statement that the protein is absent (this class is NOT shown to retain function: it is a label for 'not clearly null'; compound missense/null children fall here);
* unknown: variants not given or not classifiable from the text.
Classing is from the report's own words; no outside lookup; the verbatim variant text is the locator. Pre-declared use (ADV's I19): the variance of response and the proportion with a worsening or new-type statement by class; a class enters only with at least 8 children with a stated outcome; counts only below that; no model.
Added per exposure row (SCI's second reading of the I2 extraction): attribution_basis {stated_by_authors_for_this_agent, add_on_in_time, list_of_failures}; durability {stated_transient, stated_sustained, not_stated}; and for agents with two primary mechanisms (zonisamide, topiramate, valproate, others) BOTH mechanism_class_primary (first-listed rule) and mechanism_class_alt, so that class shares can be reported under either assignment; a not-attributable row is never recoded as single-agent because it follows success.
