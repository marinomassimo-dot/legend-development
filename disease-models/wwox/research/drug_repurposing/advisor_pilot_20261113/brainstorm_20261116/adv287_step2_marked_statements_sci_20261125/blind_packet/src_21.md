## Whole field, verbatim (/p4_card/next_measurement)
Discriminating experiment with conditional arms (case record section 7, as extended by IT10 P2.1 and IT10 P5). Step 0: in WWOX-deficient versus corrected (isogenic add-back, two independent knockout clones or lines) human neural cells measure HIF-1alpha and HIF-2alpha protein, with hydroxylation or a HIF reporter if available, nuclear and cytoplasmic fractions separately, at 5 percent oxygen (physioxia, primary) and at 21 and 1 percent; 2D or progenitor cultures are primary. Organoids at week 8 to 15 have hypoxic cores and an organoid arm therefore requires a hypoxia marker (pimonidazole-type) and size matching in both genotypes, with stressed-cell filtering of any single-cell data. Rival-explanation arms: an activity-silenced arm (tetrodotoxin-type silencing in both genotypes, because chronic activity itself raises LDHA through AMPK and HIF-1alpha) and a MYC loss-of-function arm beside arm A (MYC is a primary-author rival for HK2 and SLC2A1). If a raised HIF protein is found: arm A genetic HIF1A and EPAS1 knockdown (singly and together) is primary; arm B a PX-478-class lever beside a non-mustard HIF-1alpha inhibitor, with arm A as the specificity reference; arm C starts GENETIC (VHL or EGLN1 loss) in both knockout and corrected cells, and a prolyl-hydroxylase inhibitor runs only beside HIF1A/EPAS1-null and prolyl-hydroxylase-null controls, compound by compound (compound-specific HIF-independent actions are documented; molidustat as a non-thyromimetic option is one report, TO BE VERIFIED; not roxadustat); the direction of that arm is conditional (H-protect), not the disease direction. Deciding readout: lactate or extracellular acidification and glucose uptake plus PGK1, ALDOA, ENO1, PFKP and PKM reported individually (they belong to the guard-scope 10-gene set, so a change there confirms the seed and does not validate a lever); the six IT7 genes cannot decide. Network activity on a multielectrode array, viability and SOX2/NEUN fractions give the valence (adaptive versus harmful), relative to untreated knockout and to corrected cells, because HIF-1alpha can raise firing; a mixed outcome leaves the valence unresolved. Falsifier: neither HIF protein is higher in the WWOX-deficient neural cells (in the relevant compartment and oxygen condition), or knockdown does not move lactate or glucose uptake.
