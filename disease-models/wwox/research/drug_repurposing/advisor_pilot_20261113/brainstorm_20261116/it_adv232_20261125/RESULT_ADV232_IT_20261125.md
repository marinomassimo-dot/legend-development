## 1. Answer in six lines
1. **Step 1 finds no counterpart to any of the four panel families in a held Wwox source; the frozen rule sends this to disposition (b) and step 2 does not run.** 0 of 4 families have a counterpart, 1 is adjacent (band power in one anaesthetised in vivo recording), 2 more are computable in principle from raw recordings that exist but are not held.
2. **The panel, named from the CSNK2B paper itself (PMID 42190665) after reading its Results, ECoG Methods and the other Methods subsections in full:** (i) theta and gamma relative power (1 to 100 Hz total, theta 4 to 8, gamma 30 to 80 Hz), (ii) theta/gamma and beta/gamma ratios, (iii) band-limited magnitude-squared coherence between the two prefrontal channels, (iv) gamma-band absolute Granger strength between the same two channels (VAR order 8, bidirectional). Adult male mice (2 to 3 months), freely moving, 24-hour continuous recording after 24 hours of cable habituation, one nocturnal hour analysed, n 6 to 12 (7 per group in the untreated comparison), treatment at postnatal day 3, no post-onset arm.
3. **The closest held recording is not the same quantity, state or age:** the neuronal-deletion mouse in Repudi 2021 (Brain), ketamine-xylazine anaesthesia, postnatal day 13 to 17, one glass electrode at 300 micrometres, power elevated across delta to beta (S-KO against S-Control 0 to 4.9 and 5 to 9 Hz; 7 to 15 Hz), n 5, 7 and 7 animals; gamma is not named in the text, and one channel excludes coherence and Granger.
4. **The two held recordings that are closest in design are event counts, not spectra:** neonatal wireless single-channel ECoG at about postnatal day 14 for 7 days (Obeid 2026; spikes per hour and generalised spike-wave discharges, n 5 littermates per group; band power would be computable from the raw files, coherence and Granger would not), and adult video-EEG in the hypomorph (Hussain 2023; bilateral subdural frontal and parietal electrodes, free-moving 24-hour sessions, mice older than 6 weeks, n 3 against 3; spikes up to 3098 per hour against 0 to 68; 60 Hz low-pass filter, so gamma is cut off at 60 Hz). The second one could in principle give all four families, from raw files that the paper does not print.
5. **What the answer changes for the portfolio compounds: nothing yet.** A PV-linked network readout (gamma power, theta/gamma, coherence, gamma Granger) cannot be offered as a discriminating measurement for ethosuximide, the memantine class or GABA modulators until one of the two raw recordings above, or a new one, is analysed.
6. **Surprise (one line):** the regional PV pattern is the opposite of the template's: in the CSNK2B mouse cortical PV density is lower and hippocampal unchanged, while in the Wwox knockout hippocampal PV counts are 44 percent lower (stereology, n 7 against 7, Hussain 2019) and no held Wwox source counts cortical PV, so the template's key premise (cortical PV loss predicts the gamma disruption) is untested in Wwox, not just unrecorded.

## 3. PV side (not a panel component)
* **Hippocampus, counts:** Hussain 2019, stereology in the germline knockout, n 7 against 7, PV+ interneurons lower in the dentate gyrus (p < .005) and CA1 (p < .05), 44 percent lower overall.
* **Cortex, counts:** none held. **Cortex, transcripts:** ADV-068 per-animal P17 data, PV gene set log2 -0.65 and the late-maturing non-PV set -0.33 (ratio 0.51), the pre-registered stop rule fired at its boundary: not a PV-specific signal at that age.
* **Template:** CSNK2B cortex lower (four regions), hippocampus unchanged (p 0.92); somatostatin unchanged.

## 4. Disposition (b): the missing recording, named, and who holds the nearest
* **Missing:** a prefrontal (or frontal and parietal) two-channel field recording in a Wwox-deficient mouse and its control, in the same state and at an age window where the panel's band powers are comparable, reported as relative band power, ratios, coherence and Granger strength per animal, n of at least 6 per group; ideally with per-animal cortical PV counts from the same animals.
* **Age problem the template does not have:** the CSNK2B recordings are at 2 to 3 months. The Wwox null and neuronal-deletion mice die in the first weeks (survival curves of Repudi 2021), so an age-matched comparison exists only for the hypomorph; for null mice the panel can be read only at pup ages, where the band structure is maturing and the CSNK2B values do not transfer.
* **Nearest holders (named, no contact made):** the video-EEG raw recordings behind Hussain 2023 (adult hypomorph and wild-type, bilateral channels, 24 hours; the gamma window is cut at 60 Hz by their filter) and the continuous wireless ECoG files behind Obeid 2026 (single channel, band power and ratios only). Both papers print no data-availability route that I read for these files; the CSNK2B paper itself states that data are shared by the lead contact on request.
* **Smallest next measurement (for whoever holds the files):** relative theta and gamma power and the theta/gamma ratio from the existing Obeid single-channel pup recordings (two of four families, no new animals), and all four families from the Hussain adult recordings for the 3 against 3 mice, as a feasibility look, not a test.
