Hypothesis level; nothing is medical advice.

## Step 1 rules
* **Panel components** are those the CSNK2B paper itself presents as rescued by treatment, named after reading its ECoG Results, Methods and figure legends. If the paper presents more than four quantities, the "four-component panel" is the four families: (1) band relative power (gamma, theta), (2) band ratios, (3) interregional coherence, (4) directed connectivity (Granger).
* **A Wwox counterpart** needs all three: (a) the same quantity (or one computable from numbers the paper prints), (b) an in vivo cortical field recording (ECoG or EEG) in a Wwox-deficient rodent with a stated control, (c) per-group n and ages. A related quantity, a slice or culture recording, a spike or seizure count, or a figure trace without a number is recorded as **adjacent**, not as a counterpart.
* **Sources searched:** every held Wwox in vivo or electrophysiology paper (Repudi 2021 Brain 33914858, Repudi 2021 EMBO Mol Med 34747138, Breton 2021 34634460, and any other held paper the registry or text search returns for ECoG, EEG, electrocorticography, spectral, power, coherence), plus held tables for PV markers. Absence is claimed only after a second route (a term search across the held full texts).
* **PV side:** transcript or cell-count values for Pvalb / PV interneurons in Wwox-deficient tissue are tabulated; they are not a panel component.

## Disposition
* Step 2 runs only if **two or more** panel families have a Wwox counterpart. Otherwise disposition **b**: the missing recording is named (species, age, state, channels, quantities, n) and who would hold such data (laboratories that published the recordings; no contact is made).
* The result states what changes for the seizure compounds on the portfolio: a PV-linked network readout can be offered as a discriminating measurement, or nothing changes.

## Frozen predictions
