## 0. Answer first (the closing paragraph the assignment asks for)

**Slice priority slot, not a pilot arm. Agent: ezogabine in the 6.17 session, with ICA-069673 added as the KCNQ2/3-selective tool; no Kv7 pilot arm now.** Ezogabine is the only Kv7 agent with an in-hand brain concentration-response, a juvenile exposure measurement and a human record in the same developmental window (KCNQ2 DEE, children); it is also the only one with a **pup tolerability signal** (Brown 2016, passage level from the acquired full text: repeated exposure at a dose inside the anticonvulsant range, three doses 4 hours apart, raised neuronal apoptosis in five of eight regions at P7, while a single dose to the top tested level did not), so under the pre-registered 6.14 reading it is **not eligible as a pilot arm now** (criterion (b) fails; (a) and (c) hold in part). Azetukalner meets none of the three (no rodent exposure found, no paediatric exposure below age 12 found, one patent potency row, no within-document window). ICA-069673 passes the one rule for the slice slot (a within-document subtype contrast: KCNQ2/KCNQ3 0.69 uM against KCNQ3/KCNQ5 14.3 uM, about 21-fold, one document), as a **bath-only tool**; **XE991 does not pass the rule as I wrote it** (its held rows are a single IC50 and a different quantity; the rule asked for a within-document contrast, which a blocker control does not need): I state that and do not fill the case after the values; ADV may waive it, because the attribution of an ezogabine effect to Kv7 needs a reversal control. **What reorders the bridge preference (ethosuximide weakly over the class)**, as observations with the direction each moves it, is section 7. **One correction to a held page that this work makes necessary:** the slice panel reads "in band is 10 uM or less", but 10 uM is above the highest juvenile brain value measured (about 6.6 uM total, P7 rat, top single dose) and about three times the adult mouse electroshock brain EC50 (2.23 and 3.58 uM, two documents); a dated line is added to the panel page (section 8).
