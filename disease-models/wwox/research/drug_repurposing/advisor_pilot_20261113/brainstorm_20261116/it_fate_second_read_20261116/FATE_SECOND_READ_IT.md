## Note for the closing synthesis (SCI's point, endorsed)
H-C5 and J23 are the same biology. J23 counted R (rejected) only because its author named a transcript-level test; H-C5 counts 'not rejected' only because its stated condition was a presence condition. Under my categories both are U. The fate metric rewards stating a weak test (a test that cannot fail) as much as a strong one: any fate table should carry a column 'could the held data have rejected it' before the category is counted.
