# Note: the one measurement most of the 58 dossiers are waiting for (SCI window AB, 2026-11-24)

Hypothesis level; nothing is medical advice; no dose, no recommendation to any outside party, no contact made or implied. Source: ADV-213 coverage map (`../adv213_unmapped_phenotypes_sci_20261124/`).

**The observation.** In a keyword screen of the 58 decision texts, 44 name a reopening condition and 32 of those name exposure; read by hand, the same missing item recurs in unrelated classes: **a WWOX-specific functional response, in the same preparation, at a measured and attributable exposure**. Most dossiers have a mechanism, a human exposure bound and a comparator disease, and lack a single WWOX-versus-control drug response whose concentration was measured in the preparation that produced it.

**What measurement.** A genotype-by-compound factorial in a WWOX-deficient preparation and its matched control, with (1) the compound's free concentration measured in the bath or tissue of the same preparations (mass spectrometry, not the nominal addition), (2) a pre-written functional readout fixed before the run (the circuit readout of the class: spontaneous bursting and evoked inhibitory charge for the circuit classes; stimulus-matched recovery for the energy classes; first-sheath count and conduction for the myelin classes), (3) a vehicle and a positive-control compound of known effect in both genotypes so that a class effect can be separated from a system effect, and (4) the replication unit stated (animals or lines, not slices or wells).

**Which assay could supply it.** The held record already contains the two kinds of preparation that would host it: acute cortical or hippocampal slices from neuron-specific or hypomorphic Wwox mice (the electrophysiology of the registered slice study, juvenile age), and human WWOX-knockout cerebral organoids with network recordings (the organoid benchmark and its week-16 event-rate forecast, RP-045; that forecast is frozen and is not touched). The in vivo version is the consolidated pup brief (RP-041). A single run with three compounds from three classes (one circuit class, one energy or cofactor class, one myelin class) would convert many parked dossiers into either a reopening or a closure on the same footing.

**Who holds a system.** Only as a statement about the held record, from the registry: the slice and organoid systems above are published and held as full text and tables in LEGEND; which laboratories maintain them now is not recorded in the held record and I have not looked it up. Any approach to a laboratory is a separate step for the operator and ADV; I make none.

**What it would change.** If the response is absent at a verified exposure for a compound whose mechanism the dossier credits, that dossier closes instead of parking; if present, it reopens with an exposure that can be quoted. Either way it replaces about 30 separate "reopen when" conditions by one design. Limits: the count rests on a keyword screen and my reading of decision lines, not on the dossiers in full; the factorial is a design description, not a protocol; positive-control selection is left open.
