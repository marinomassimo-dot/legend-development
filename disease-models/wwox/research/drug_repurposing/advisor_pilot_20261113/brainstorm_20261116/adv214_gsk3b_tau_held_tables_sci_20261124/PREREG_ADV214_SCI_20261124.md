## Pass rule per table (frozen)
A table gives a **usable protein-level phospho datum (READ)** only if ALL hold: (i) WWOX-deficient material (knockout, hypomorph, knockdown or patient-derived) against matched control; (ii) the readout is phospho-protein level: site-resolved phosphopeptide intensity, a phospho-specific immunoblot quantification, or a phospho-to-total ratio, for a gene or site in the frozen list; (iii) at least three independent biological units per arm (the unit named: animals, litters, cultures, lines, not wells or cells); (iv) species, tissue or cell type and age or stage are stated in the source; (v) it is not the withdrawn Cheng 2020 densitometry. A table that fails (ii) but measures total protein of listed genes is **PROTEIN-TOTAL**; one that measures only transcripts of listed genes is **RNA-ONLY**; one with no listed gene is **ABSENT**; one that cannot be classified from the held bytes is **UNREADABLE** with the reason. PROTEIN-TOTAL is my addition to ADV's three labels and counts with RNA-ONLY for the disposition. Contradiction between two READ tables, or a READ table that fails (iii), is reported as such and counts as (b).
