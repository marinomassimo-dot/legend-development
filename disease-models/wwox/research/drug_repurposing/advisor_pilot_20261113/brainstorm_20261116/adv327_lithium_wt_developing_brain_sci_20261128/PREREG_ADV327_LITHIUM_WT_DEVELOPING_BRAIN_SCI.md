# ADV-327 pre-registration (SCI bf): does lithium change GSK3-pathway phosphorylation in wild-type developing rodent brain?

Frozen before any search or held text was opened for this question. Already known before freezing: Cheng 2020 (PMID 32000863) Fig 7d legend gives LiCl 60 mg/kg as pretreatment in a PTZ test, and the claim registry says the lithium arm is not genotype-specific (CLAIM 016); the general pharmacology that lithium raises GSK3B pSer9 in cells and adult tissue is background knowledge, not held evidence. Hypothesis level; not medical advice. Consumer: IT (GSK3 entry, RP-026), then ADV.

## Question
In wild-type (or untreated-genotype, non-disease) mouse or rat brain at developmental age, does lithium raise or lower GSK3B pSer9, beta-catenin pSer33/Ser37 (or Thr41), CRMP2 pThr514 or glycogen synthase pSer641, at a stated dose, duration and age?

## Inclusion rule (a PASS)
Primary in-vivo data; mouse or rat; exposure in utero or postnatal with brain sampled at or before P28; lithium against vehicle or saline control; immunoblot or immunoassay of a named site above; dose, route, number of doses and time from last dose to sampling stated; quantified readout with n of 3 or more animals (or pools, named as such) per arm. Total-protein-only, activity-assay-only, RNA, culture and adult data do not pass (adult and culture items may be listed as adjacent, never counted). Dose is converted to mmol Li per kg and printed beside each value; serum or brain lithium level recorded if printed.

## Two explanations
* **H1:** lithium changes at least one of these readouts in wild-type developing brain at an anticonvulsant-range exposure, so a Wwox-null difference under lithium must be read against a lithium-treated wild type.
* **H2:** it does not at anticonvulsant-range exposure; changes appear only at higher or repeated exposure.
"Anticonvulsant-range" is fixed as acute exposure at or below 2.5 mmol Li per kg (LiCl 60 mg/kg is about 1.4 mmol per kg; 106 mg/kg is 2.5).

## Predicted observables
* Passes at any dose: 2, plausible range 0 to 6. Passes at or below 2.5 mmol per kg: 0 or 1.
* Direction where a pass exists: pSer9 raised; substrate sites lowered.
* My lean: not decidable at the anticonvulsant range (70 percent); where decidable, H1.

## Strong counterexamples
* Against H2: a quantified significant change in any listed site in P7 to P28 wild-type brain at or below 2.5 mmol per kg.
* Against H1: a quantified study of that kind, with a positive control showing the antibody detects change, reporting no change at that exposure with n of 3 or more.
