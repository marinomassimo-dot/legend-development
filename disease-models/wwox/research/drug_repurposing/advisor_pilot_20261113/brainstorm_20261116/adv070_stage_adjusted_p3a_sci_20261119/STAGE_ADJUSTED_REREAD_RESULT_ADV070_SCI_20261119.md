## Answer first
1. **The covariate has little leverage in the only qualifying contrast, so the pre-registered labels are weak.** Only the cortex contrast (3 v 4) can carry a rule label. There the stage score separates the arms by only -0.33 log2 (the knockout animals -0.51, -0.30, -0.19; the controls -0.2 to +0.12), slopes on it are small and change sign from gene to gene, and the genotype term c stays close to the raw shift for most seeds. The pre-registered labelling returns **8 of the 31 channel, receptor and transporter seeds as STANDS, 23 as NO_SHIFT, none as EXPLAINED_BY_STAGE**, and Pvalb as UNSTABLE. "STANDS" here means "not removable by this covariate at this n", not "independent of stage". The positive reference did not work as a test: the PV panel shifts by only -0.35 in cortex (below the 0.5 floor, NO_SHIFT) and Pvalb itself is UNSTABLE under leave-one-animal-out.
2. **Where the covariate has more leverage, shifts shrink.** In the hippocampal neuron-targeted contrast (stage separation -0.59, 3 v 2, descriptive) several seeds fall to about zero after adjustment (Kcnj9 -1.71 to -0.07 with a narrow leave-one-out range, Slc12a5 -0.57 to -0.03, Grm3 -0.85 to 0.00, Pvalb -1.75 to -0.42) and some change sign (Grin2c to +0.82, Kcnq2 to +0.63). Cortex and hippocampus therefore disagree about whether those seeds are stage-linked, and at 2 to 5 animals with a score that is itself lower in the knockout arm (collinear), **the held data cannot settle it**. That is the result: stage adjustment is **not decisive** here.
3. **Two seeds are robust to the covariate in every contrast read:** the activity-regulated panel is **lower** in the knockout (cortex -1.34, hippocampus neuron-targeted -0.41 with an adjusted term of opposite sign and a very wide leave-one-out range, stem-cell-targeted -0.73, null -0.88; the adjusted term keeps its sign in the three contrasts with a shift of at least 0.5), so the held deposits show **no activity-regulated hyper-signature** at sampling; and Npy is **higher** (+0.95 in cortex, adjusted term retained in 3 of 3 hippocampal contrasts), but Npy is background-confounded in P3a's control-line contrasts and fits seizure-induced expression in other cells, not a cell count.
4. **Effect on arm cells and the Kv7 opener probe: no tier change by me; annotations for IT to map (section 4).** The Kv7 probe's premise is unchanged: the Kcnq2 transcript is lower in cortex (-0.71, term -0.81), is **background-confounded** in P3a, and is retained in none of the three hippocampal contrasts after adjustment; the XE991-sensitive current and resting potential in the same slices remain the discriminating measurement.
5. **Reasoned stop.** Further in silico adjustment on these deposits cannot separate stage from genotype at n of 2 to 4; the discriminating observation is the tissue measurement at two ages already added to the brief (Addendum 19).

## 1. Method as run (pre-registered), and two flags
* Covariate: the ADV-068 late-maturing set, unchanged (Grin2a, Slc12a5, Nrgn, Camk2a, Scn8a, minus the reference set Snap25, Rbfox3, Syt1, Stmn2, Tubb3, Map2); seeds that belong to either set were removed from the score for their own analysis (leave-self-out: Slc12a5, Grin2a, Scn8a). Per animal and seed: log2(TPM + 0.5); per contrast an ordinary least squares fit y = a + b x + c KO over all animals; raw shift Delta; c with its leave-one-animal-out range (not computed for the 2 v 2 contrast: fewer than four animals would remain).
* Sanity: the raw per-animal shift agrees in sign with P3a's published log2 ratio in **106 of 106** comparisons with |P3a| of at least 0.2.
* **Flag 1 (to ADV, in the pre-registration): the assignment text says "three or fewer animals per arm" as descriptive; read literally every contrast is descriptive and nothing can stand.** I applied the ADV-068 rule (fewer than three per arm) as primary and report the literal reading as a second column in `out/seed_summary.tsv`, where every seed is descriptive. ADV may overrule.
* **Flag 2 (implementation detail, stricter than registered):** the descriptive count "contrasts in which Delta and c share a sign" was implemented as "|Delta| at least 0.5 and c keeping the sign with |c| at least 0.3"; it is a description, not a label.
* Background flag (from P3a, not recomputed): a seed is flagged if any control-against-control contrast has |log2 ratio| above 0.25. 33 of the 38 genes are flagged; only Pvalb, Kcnj9, Gabrb3, Gabrg2 and Wwox are clean. The flag is conservative by design (2 v 3 control contrasts are noisy), and **the control-line animals themselves score +0.33 on the stage score against wild type**, as large as the cortex knockout effect, so the within-line contrasts are the right ones.

## 5. Alternatives that remain
Composition (oligodendrocyte loss; astrocyte rise) not removed by a neuronal late-set score; the Cre-line control background (control lines sit +0.33 above wild type on the score); the cortex deletion is the weakest (about 68 percent fall of Wwox); tissue difference (cortex against hippocampus disagree); late-stage illness at P17 (the score cannot separate delay from late-stage state); collinearity of the score with genotype at n of 5 to 7; the late set mixes "late" with "excitatory" (Slc12a5, Camk2a); my gene sets are a judgement.

## 6. Reasoned stop, limits and cost
The held P17 deposits cannot separate stage from genotype for these seeds at n of 2 to 4: seeds that stand in cortex only, or shrink in hippocampus, are **descriptive**; only IEG (lower) and Npy (higher) are stable to the covariate in every contrast read, and Npy carries a background flag. The next discriminating observation is the optional tissue measurement at two ages already in the brief (Addendum 19); nothing further is run in silico. No independent verifier (as assigned: the pivotal rows are labelled descriptive, not promoted). Cost: main-session turns only, no delegate; tokens not read back.
