# Brainstorm meeting record: funding-grade in-silico packages (ADV host, 2026-11-19)

Called by the operator's question; brief in `BRIEF_FOR_THE_TEAM_ADV_20261119.md`. Participants who replied: SCI, IT, HE (the Mirror is not live). Hypothesis level; not medical advice; reference genotype class only; costs only as ranges the operator supplies. No personal or individual-level information is used or recorded.

## What the three replies agree on (against ADV's first draft)
1. **Hindcast is a diagnostic, not a licence** (all three; the Anthropic protein-design report says the validated relationship "was not steep enough to have flagged the failures in advance"). A set of about a dozen analogous drug-outcome pairs is small, positive-skewed and built from the literature our score reads; at 6 against 6 only an almost perfect ranking separates from chance. Use it to see whether components separate known successes from failures, report the interval, never gate funding on it.
2. **Expected value of information with invented priors is theatre** (SCI, IT): with wide ranges it ranks nothing. The funding view is **ordinal**: decision-readiness class, weakest link, shared-assay flag, operator-supplied cost range; information value only as a sensitivity. Weight sensitivity claims no probability and need not wait for any hindcast (HE).
3. **The prediction ledger exists** (HE, SCI): `prediction_ledger.tsv` with a guard, SCI's prospective ledger with a binned sidecar and a log score against naive baselines, two second ledgers; what is missing is a `summary` with n beside each rate; no calibration below about 30 predictions.
4. **A shared assay is the cheapest "one study, several candidates"** (IT; ADV position 2): the matched-block slice preparation serves the NMDAR pore blocker against d-APV, ethosuximide with the wild-type age series and a Kv7 opener; the E_GABA measurement also serves any chloride-class candidate.
5. **Compute is not the limit; inputs are** (HE): 4 CPUs, about 10 GB RAM available, no GPU; a hindcast and Monte Carlo over about 10 candidates and 6 components take seconds to minutes.
6. **The decisive single change, said three ways:** one page per fundable pair or candidate stating which result would change which decision, with the operator's cost range (HE); a pre-registered outcome-to-decision table with an interpretable-null control so a null does not end as UNRESOLVED (SCI); one dossier per fundable candidate whose item 3 names a decision, reviewed by a person who can say "we would not do anything different" (IT).

## The finding of the meeting (SCI)
The "two in vitro studies" frame mixes cost classes. A study of about 15,000 to 20,000 euro resolves a **mid-chain** link (target engagement, direction), never the last two links of the chain (phenotype relevance; transfer to this variant and age). The pup brief's stages are an in vivo cost class (about 300 animals at 11 per cell, a planning figure, not a forecast). For the conservative cluster the pediatric safety is already known and the unknown is benefit in this genotype, which no in vitro study answers: a **clinical-data project** (the eight published clinical series on onset age and drug response, and the aggregate clinical questions) is the default first step of cluster 2, and its first step costs no money (the texts wait for the human supply).

## What transfers from Anthropic's work, read in full text by a delegate (sonnet, effort high; quotes checked by script by SCI)
Sources read: the protein-binder design report (18 Aug 2026), the companion scorpion-toxin report (20 Oct 2026, partly), the enzyme-system discovery post (23 Sep 2026), the scientific model-building post (6 Nov 2026). Not read: references, the dataset, appendix tables of the companion report, the enzyme preprint.
- **Transfers:** freeze the scoring rubric and thresholds in a dated file before ranking and freeze the delivered order; test the rubric on known positives and negatives first; keep positive and negative controls and report the failed ones; report failures with successes; blind the person reading the study outcome to the rank; fund by rank AND diversity; keep several mechanistic models compatible with the literature and fund the study that separates them or confirms what all share; reproduce one published result from public data as a method check.
- **Does not transfer:** scale (318,484 designs, 1,320 tested, about 10,000 GPU hours); ground truth (binding is fast and binary with 391 confirmed binders; efficacy in this disease is slow, multi-factor, with almost no documented positives); throughput (two studies cannot sample the top, middle and bottom of a ranking); a calibrated confidence metric for a literature-based drug ranking; the endpoint (all stop at binding, unknown function or no biological validation; none ranks drugs); baselines (no matched expert campaign; no source shows the method beats a human-ranked list).
- A validated criterion is a **filter, not a guarantee**; the authors name binding, not function, as the chief limitation of their evidence.

Positives: stiripentol, fenfluramine and cannabidiol in SCN1A Dravet; everolimus in TSC; ganaxolone in CDKL5; the ketogenic diet in GLUT1 deficiency; pyridoxine in ALDH7A1; sodium-channel blockers in KCNQ2 and in SCN2A and SCN8A gain of function. Run blind, components fixed first, names disguised, date-cut; contamination reduced but not removed (a language model has seen these outcomes).

## Dated corrections (ADV, 18:51Z on 2026-11-19, host clock), after IT's ADV-092 delivery
1. **Ethosuximide is not part of the slice preparation.** The meeting record and the ADV-091 text listed ethosuximide with the wild-type age series as served by the matched-block slice preparation. The ordering page puts it in the PUP series (row 3, an in vivo cost class), and the slice package has no such arm. The shared slice assay serves the NMDAR pore blocker against d-APV, the Kv7 opener (cell against slice) and, as an add-on, the E_GABA measurement; ethosuximide sits with the pup series (glucose, age series, rescue window). The dossier on the shared assay follows this correction.
2. **"The pediatric safety of the conservative cluster is already known" overstated.** Held labels say ethosuximide is not established below 3 years and lithium is labelled from 7 years; pediatric use experience exists for several agents but does not cover this age range or this genotype. Lithium is therefore placed in the ambitious portfolio in the funding view. The unknown remains benefit in this genotype, and the clinical-data project stays the default first step of cluster 2; the safety statement needs its own cells per agent and age.
3. The funding view is a standalone page, not a tab of the repurposing view (default taken by IT: content not yet decided public); embedding is a bounded follow-up if the operator wants it. The class values in it are IT's reading of cited rows, not a re-audit.
