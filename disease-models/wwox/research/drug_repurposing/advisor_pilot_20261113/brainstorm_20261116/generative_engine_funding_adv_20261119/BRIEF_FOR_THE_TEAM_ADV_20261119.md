# Brainstorm brief: from "unresolved" to a funding-grade in-silico package (ADV, 2026-11-19)

Source: the operator's brainstorming question of 2026-11-19 (chat), and a first reply by another model that the operator pasted. This brief is non-canonical, hypothesis level, not medical advice, reference genotype class only. No personal or individual-level information is used or recorded.

## The question (operator, paraphrased)
In a rare disease with no cure the literature will never confirm anything definitively: everything stays UNRESOLVED, even gene therapy until a success is described and generalised. In vitro tests cost about 15,000 to 20,000 euro per study; the Foundation cannot ask a new test for every candidate and then another after processing the result. So: (1) how do we make the in-silico analyses as solid, robust and deep as possible (generative engine, computation, predictive analysis, probabilistic simulation), enough to justify one costly in vitro study; how did Anthropic make discoveries in biology and how far did they go in generative predictive analysis; (2) how do we compare, with ranking and score, all explorations that need in vitro testing so that only TWO are funded: one for the ambitious, disease-modifying portfolio and one for a second, conservative cluster (supplements, anti-seizure drugs and molecules safe in children: not a cure, but benefits without excessive risk). Make this a serious plan that makes the generative engine more concrete and pragmatic, less "unresolved, in vitro everywhere".

## The first reply's main points (to be challenged, not adopted)
- "Unresolved" should qualify a precise question (target engagement; direction; seizures or development; transfer to this variant and age), not the whole candidate.
- The repository already separates evidence from investment priority and has the two portfolios; the gap is linking its parts to the choice of investments. Cited facts (checked by ADV: the next-priority function excludes cost by design; the simulation harness states that simulated strength is not shown to correspond to drug exposure or action; the LINCS route was rejected with controls).
- Transferable from Anthropic's published work (NOT yet read by us in full): validate the selection criterion on known outcomes before using it to choose new experiments (protein-design report); keep multiple models compatible with the data and look for conclusions that survive across them (scientific model building); large candidate sets narrowed to a few deep proposals (enzyme discovery). Compute scale is far beyond ours.
- An analysis chosen per uncertainty, not every method for every candidate; probabilistic simulation is only as good as the source of its probabilities; five agents sharing a premise are not five confirmations.
- A visible score by components (biological rationale, plausible functional benefit, pharmacological plausibility, robustness, risk profile, value of the experiment), unknown stays unknown, sensitivity to weights, and a separate funding priority (cost, interpretability, cheaper routes, cost of the next step); choose the PAIR, not the top two names.
- First increment: 6 to 10 existing opportunities across the two portfolios; rebuild decision dossiers; choose the still-decisive analyses; two shortlists with decomposed scores and sensitivity; up to two proposals each against its best discarded alternative.

## ADV's positions (to be challenged)
1. Replace "confirmation" with DECISION-READINESS: a candidate project is fundable when its chain (target engaged, direction, phenotype relevance, transfer to variant and age) is each stated with its evidence class and its weakest link, the experiment's outcome changes a decision, and no cheaper route gives the same information.
2. Fund an ASSAY AND PANEL, not a candidate: one validated readout (the matched-block slice experiment, the E_GABA measurement, the lineage-reporter PV count) run once against several candidates and controls lowers the cost per candidate; the packages already have this shape (ordering page).
3. HINDCAST before FORECAST: test our scoring on public ground truth (known effective and ineffective drugs in analogous developmental and epileptic encephalopathies or channelopathies); only a score that ranks known cases correctly may rank funding.
4. A PREDICTION LEDGER: every pre-registered prediction (today SCI's expression time course: 1 held, 2 failed in part, 1 failed) is recorded with its outcome; our hit rate calibrates how much weight a new prediction gets.
5. EXPECTED VALUE OF INFORMATION as the core of the funding priority, with cost as an operator-supplied range (never invented by agents), and complementarity in choosing the pair.
6. The conservative portfolio may need a CLINICAL-DATA project (aggregate questions to a registry or a clinical service) rather than a laboratory one: it is cheaper per answer and bears on the benefit and risk cells of the second cluster; the choice of two need not be two laboratory studies.
7. Probabilistic simulation only after hindcast, with probabilities derived from evidence classes as ranges, compared with simple baselines, with a sensitivity report; never a single "probability of benefit".

## What each role is asked (reply in a few lines each, or 'no change, because')
- **SCI (science):** (a) which in-silico analyses can truly raise decision-readiness for the top opportunities and which would be theatre; list 6 to 10 opportunities across the two portfolios; (b) design a minimal hindcast set (analogous diseases, known outcomes, public sources) and say what it could and could not validate; (c) read the PRIMARY Anthropic sources (the protein-design technical report and the enzyme-discovery and model-building posts) and extract ONLY the selection-validation methods that transfer to us, with what does not transfer.
- **IT (integration):** (a) can the ranking and the dashboard carry a funding-priority view (components, unknown as unknown, sensitivity, complementary pair) without a new layer; (b) the decision-dossier template and the consumers; (c) where cost ranges would come from and how they are shown; (d) what already exists that I may be forgetting.
- **HE (capability):** (a) what simulation, probabilistic and hindcast capability exists (the bisect harness result, calibration loaders) and what is missing; (b) the compute budget of the VPS and a cheap prediction ledger; (c) the smallest tool change, if any, and what you would not build.
- **All:** the strongest objection to ADV's seven positions; the single change that would most improve what the Foundation receives.
