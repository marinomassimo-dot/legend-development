## 1. The question and the answer in short
The question: in a rare disease with no cure everything ends UNRESOLVED and each in vitro study costs 15,000 to 20,000 euro, so the Foundation can fund at most two; how do we make the in-silico analysis strong enough to justify them, and how do we rank the candidates that need laboratory work?

The answer: **do not replace "unresolved" with a score; replace it with a decision-grade package.** A candidate is fundable when (i) its chain (target engaged, direction, phenotype relevance, transfer to variant and age) is written with the evidence class and the weakest link of each step; (ii) the study's outcome changes a named decision, with an outcome-to-decision table for positive, negative and ambiguous results and an interpretable-null control (so a null does not end as UNRESOLVED again); (iii) no cheaper route gives the same information; (iv) the cost range comes from the laboratory's quote. The ranking stays **ordinal** (components separate, unknown printed as unknown, no sums, cost never a sort key); a probability of benefit is not offered because nothing here can support one.

## 2. What the team changed in the first draft
Four of ADV's seven positions were demoted or reshaped by the three replies. The hindcast is a diagnostic, not a gate (small, positive-skewed, built from the literature our score reads). Expected value of information with invented priors is theatre (with wide ranges it ranks nothing): it appears only as a sensitivity. The prediction ledger already existed (it now has a summary with the n beside each rate). A shared assay is the cheapest "one study, several candidates". Compute is not the limit; the inputs are.

**The finding of the meeting (SCI):** a 15,000 to 20,000 euro study resolves a **mid-chain** link (engagement, direction), never phenotype relevance or transfer to this variant and age; the pup series is a different cost class (about 300 animals at 11 per cell for simple-effect stages; a genotype-by-treatment interaction needs about 88 to 1,300 animals depending on the variance). For the conservative cluster the unknown is benefit in this genotype, which no in vitro study answers: the **clinical-data project** is its default first step, and the first step costs no money.

## 3. What was learned from Anthropic's published discovery work (read in full text by a delegate; quotes checked by script)
Transferable: freeze the scoring rubric and the delivered order before ranking; test the rubric on known positives and negatives; keep controls and report failures; blind the person reading the outcome to the rank; fund by rank and by diversity; keep several models compatible with the data; reproduce one published result as a method check. Not transferable: scale (hundreds of thousands of designs, about ten thousand GPU hours), ground truth (binding is fast and binary with hundreds of confirmed binders; efficacy here is slow with almost no documented positives), a calibrated confidence metric, the endpoint (their evidence stops at binding or unknown function) and baselines. A validated criterion is a **filter, not a guarantee**, in the authors' own words. Not read: references, datasets, some appendices.

## 4. What was built in one afternoon (all on main, no laboratory cost)
- A decision-dossier template with the outcome-to-decision table and the interpretable-null control, and the "would we do anything different" check (IT).
- A read-only **ordinal funding view**, tested: components separate, unknown as unknown, three lexicographic orderings, shared-assay flag, a pair table, cost only from an operator-supplied file (header only today), first line "funding order is not research order" (IT).
- An opportunity map of ten candidates across the two portfolios, corrected after review (IT), and a pilot dossier on the **shared slice preparation** with three readout blocks and a named null per block (SCI).
- A prediction-ledger summary and a hindcast scoring function with exact permutation p and intervals, never pass or fail (HE).
- A **hindcast of 12 drug-outcome pairs**, frozen before scoring and scored blind. Result: **no separation shown by any component or the sum** (AUC 0.44 to 0.63, intervals include 0.5) in a **contaminated** run (the scorer recognised 10 of 12 pairs). It is closed as a diagnostic: the components say how much evidence existed, not who succeeded; they stay ordinal labels; no re-freeze, because a language-model scorer cannot be made blind to well-known outcomes and 6 against 6 shows only AUC of about 0.81 or more. **Consequence: the proposal rests on the chain of reasons, not on a score.**
- A scripted quote-and-number scan of the 26 consumed claims (978 items): zero genuine defects where anchors are held; the real defect class is wording overshoot (found in four of four claims audited by blind readers), prevented now by a write-time rule.

## 5. The proposal (draft, revision 3; the operator decides)
**Pick 1 (spans both portfolios): one shared slice preparation** of the neuron-targeted knockout with three separable blocks: E_GABA by perforated patch with an early-age point (a suggestion for the laboratory), the NMDAR pore blocker against d-APV, and the Kv7 opener with mediation; the PV count as an add-on. E_GABA resolves its weakest links; Kv7 partly; NMDAR no. It changes: whether a chloride arm becomes eligible and in which direction; whether a Kv7 route is neuron-mediated, Kv7-specific and larger in the null; which tied NMDAR-class agent leads. **Pick 2: the clinical-data project** for the conservative cluster: the published clinical series plus aggregate questions to a clinical service (counts only), with a pre-registered rule fixed BEFORE reading (two passes; floors on patients with a stated response per class and on independent series; classes named in advance; an asymmetric negative; a positive names a comparator candidate, never a mechanism); **not yet decision-grade** until that rule is fixed. The one conservative option with a named decision, ethosuximide with the age series, is in the in vivo cost class and is not a pick. Costs are not supplied; the pair is a labelled reading, not a ranking.
