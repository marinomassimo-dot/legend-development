# Draft proposal of a PAIR for the Foundation (ADV host, 2026-11-19)

Status: **draft for the operator's judgement**; hypothesis level; not medical advice; reference genotype class only. The host compares and proposes; **it does not fund**. Costs are **not supplied** (no agent invents one): the operator provides ranges from the laboratories' own quotes (`cost_ranges.tsv`, header only today). The classes below are the readings of the dossier and of IT's map (`it_adv092_20261119/opportunities.tsv`), checked by blind readers where stated; nothing here is a probability of benefit, and the hindcast (12 pairs, contaminated) showed **no separation by any component**, so the case rests on the chain of reasons and the outcome-to-decision tables, not on a score. No personal or individual-level information is used or recorded.

## The proposal in one paragraph
Fund **one shared-assay slice study** for the ambitious portfolio and **one clinical-data project** for the conservative cluster. They share no premise (the first rests on mouse slice physiology, the second on human clinical records), both act on the middle of the chain or on no-cost first steps, and both answer a question that changes a named decision. Neither resolves the last two links (phenotype relevance; transfer to this variant and age): no study at this price does, and the pair does not pretend to.

## Pick 1 (ambitious): the matched-block slice preparation with three readout blocks
- **What:** one slice preparation of the neuron-targeted knockout and age-matched littermate controls, run once, with (a) the use-dependent NMDAR pore blocker against d-APV (matched fractional block, post-washout reads), (b) E_GABA by gramicidin-perforated patch, ages including an early point near the onset of seizures (about P9 to P12) and P14 to P17, with controls at each age, (c) the Kv7 opener with a mediation arm (cell against slice). Optional add-on on the same animals: the lineage-reporter PV fraction at P14, P17 and P21 (tissue staining, no drug). Deletion completeness at the protein level per animal and age is recorded first: an incomplete deletion at the earliest age makes a null not a negative.
- **Why it is the more promising of the ambitious options** (for which endpoint, model, phase and evidence level): it is the only ambitious readout that (i) no Wwox system has yet measured for E_GABA and that can close or open a whole class (chloride levers, and the rank of GABA-A modulators under the burst-time reading), (ii) shares one preparation across three candidates, (iii) sits in the cost class of a single laboratory study, and (iv) resolves the weakest link for the E_GABA and Kv7 blocks (the NMDAR block leaves L4 then L3 open after its best outcome and is stated so). Acute network-state endpoint, mouse slice, neuron-targeted deletion, evidence level hypothesis to inference; no claim about seizures in children or about development.
- **Decisions it changes:** whether a chloride-class arm enters the in vivo design and in which direction; which of five tied NMDAR-class agents leads and whether the aggravation harm appears; whether a Kv7 opener's effect is cell-intrinsic or circuit-level.
- **What each outcome does (short form):** E_GABA depolarised in knockout cells beyond controls: open a chloride-lever branch and re-rank GABA-A modulators; normal: close the branch with its reasons; ambiguous (deletion incomplete at that age or variability above the dossier's sizing): repeat only the early age. The interpretable-null controls are named in the dossier; the Kv7 control is partial and says so.
- **Fragile premises:** the knockout slice phenotype is a model, not a patient; one laboratory and one preparation; the age windows rest on one whole-brain transcript dataset (marker onset), labelled as such; the Synapsin-Cre deletion may be incomplete early.
- **Needs from the operator:** a laboratory with this preparation (**reserved**), its cost range, and the lead time. Status of the package: the dossier passed blind checks (19 statements: 8 supported, 9 supported with limit, 2 overshoot repaired; a second check of the repairs was owed), one cheaper route is untried (the authors' per-slice data and supplement are not held: access, not money; the supply sheet lists it).

## Pick 2 (conservative): the clinical-data project
- **What:** the antiseizure responses and onset ages already written in the published clinical series (eight series blocked by access today) read completely, plus a short set of **aggregate** questions to a clinical service or registry (counts in age bands only, no individual record; draft exists). First step costs no money.
- **Why it is the more promising of the conservative options:** for this cluster the pediatric exposure is partly known but not for this genotype or age range (labels: ethosuximide not established below 3 years; lithium from 7); what is unknown is **benefit in this genotype**, which no in vitro study answers. It bears on the benefit and harm cells of the whole cluster, it is the cheapest route, and it would tell the pup series whether any conservative agent justifies its in vivo stage.
- **Decision it must name (the map lists none yet, so it goes to the 'would we do anything different' reader):** proposed: whether any antiseizure agent is worth carrying into the pup series as a comparator, and which age window the series must cover.
- **Fragile premises:** published series are retrospective, uncontrolled and confounded by co-therapy and natural history; the access to the texts and to any channel is the operator's.
- **Needs from the operator:** the human supply (decision page item 14, one sheet, about a fifteen-minute task), and the choice of any clinical channel (**reserved**).
