## The proposal in one paragraph
**Pick 1 is one shared slice preparation that spans both portfolios** (its E_GABA and NMDAR blocks are ambitious candidates; its Kv7 block is a conservative candidate in the map); **Pick 2 is then the clinical-data project** for the conservative cluster. They share no premise (mouse slice physiology against human clinical records). Neither resolves the last two links of the chain (phenotype relevance; transfer to this variant and age): no study at this price does, and the pair does not pretend to.

## Pick 1: the matched-block slice preparation with three separable readout blocks
- **What:** one preparation of the neuron-targeted knockout and age-matched littermate controls, with (a) the use-dependent NMDAR pore blocker against d-APV (matched fractional block, post-washout reads); (b) E_GABA by gramicidin-perforated patch, with controls at each age and an early point that the dossier and the ordering page offer as **a suggestion for the laboratory (P9 to P10)**, behavioural seizures starting about P12 and, in the neuron-targeted line, from P9 by the paper's text; (c) the Kv7 opener with a mediation arm (cell against slice). Optional add-on on the same animals: the lineage-reporter PV fraction at P14, P17 and P21. Protein-level deletion completeness per animal and age is recorded first: an incomplete deletion at the earliest age makes a null not a negative. **Each block is optional and separable** (package S2).
- **What each block resolves, said exactly:** E_GABA: **yes** (its weakest links L1 and L2 are the two it resolves). Kv7: **partly** (L2, L3 and L4 tie as lowest; the block resolves L2 only; L3 and L4 stay unknown). NMDAR: **no** (L4, then L3, stay open after its best outcome). Phenotype relevance and transfer to variant and age stay open in all three.
- **Why it is the more promising of the ambitious options** (acute network-state endpoint, mouse slice, neuron-targeted deletion; evidence level hypothesis to inference): no Wwox system has measured E_GABA and its result can open or close the eligibility of a chloride arm; one preparation serves three candidates; and a laboratory study of this shape is the cost class the operator described, **if the laboratory's quote agrees**.
- **Cost, honestly:** three readout blocks, an early-age arm, protein-level deletion completeness and an optional tissue count **may fit one study; the quote decides; the blocks are separable** (drop or defer one). The 15,000 to 20,000 euro per-study figure buys, by SCI's finding, a mid-chain link.
- **Lead items I cannot know:** whether any laboratory still runs this preparation; the line's availability (the line appears to come from the Jerusalem group; not confirmed); ethics approval; the yield of slices at the early age.
- **Decisions it changes (the dossier's own wording):** whether a **chloride arm becomes eligible and in which direction** (an NKCC1 arm is conditional and second); whether a **Kv7 route is neuron-mediated, Kv7-specific and larger in the null than in wild type** (a conservative-cluster arm); which of the tied NMDAR-class agents leads and whether an aggravation harm appears.
- **What each E_GABA outcome does:** depolarised beyond controls: a chloride arm becomes eligible; normal: the branch closes with its reasons; ambiguous (deletion incomplete at that age or variability above the dossier's sizing): repeat only the early age. The interpretable-null controls are named per block in the dossier; the Kv7 control is partial and says so.
- **Fragile premises:** a knockout slice phenotype is a model, not a person; one laboratory and one preparation; the age windows rest on one whole-brain transcript dataset (marker onset), labelled as such; the Synapsin-Cre deletion may be incomplete early.
- **Status of the package:** the dossier passed two blind checks (the first: 19 statements, 8 supported, 9 supported with limit, 2 overshoot; the second, of the repairs: 9 statements, 2 supported, 5 with limit, 1 overshoot, 1 unverifiable); SCI repaired again and the **third-round repairs are not audited**. One cheaper route is untried (the authors' per-slice data and supplement are not held: access, not money; the supply sheet lists it).
- **Needs from the operator:** a laboratory with this preparation (**reserved**), its quote, and the lead time.

## Pick 2: the clinical-data project (honest framing)
- **What:** the antiseizure responses and onset ages already written in the published clinical series (eight series blocked by access today), read completely, plus a short set of **aggregate** questions to a clinical service or registry (counts in age bands only, no individual record; a draft exists). **The first step costs no money, so it can start without passing the decision test; the test applies to any clinical-channel spend.**
- **Why this and not a laboratory study for the conservative cluster:** the pediatric exposure to some agents is partly known but not for this genotype or age range (the held labels: ethosuximide not established below 3 years); what is unknown is **benefit in this genotype**, which no in vitro study answers. Safety is its own cell **per agent and age** (unknown where unknown); lithium sits in the ambitious cluster in the map (OPP-04).
- **The decision test, stated honestly:** the decision I propose is whether any antiseizure agent is worth carrying into the pup series as a comparator and which age window the series must cover. Retrospective, uncontrolled series confounded by co-therapy will most likely return nothing that moves it, and then nobody would do anything different: so the project is **not yet decision-grade** until the rule below is fixed BEFORE any response is read. IT's four corrections make it defensible as a **pre-registered proposal** (N stays the reader's or the operator's to confirm, because it depends on what pass 1 finds):
  1. **Two passes.** Pass 1 is blind to direction: extract per series only the number of patients of the genotype class, the age band, and whether each patient's drug and response are stated (no outcome tallies). Then fix N and the classes. Pass 2 reads the responses. N is set on the **denominators**, not on the series count: a floor on patients with a **stated response per class** (a floor such as 10, a proposal, fixed after pass 1) plus a floor of **3 independent series**.
  2. **Independence.** Series that re-report the same patients or the same registry count once; the rule says "independent cohorts".
  3. **Classes named before reading**: those the pup series could actually carry (its present comparators and any class the cluster already lists), not a class found after reading. With five or more classes a hit for SOME class in 3 of 8 series is likely by chance.
  4. **The negative row is asymmetric:** it means a **stated** non-response or worsening; a series that does not mention a drug belongs to the sparse row, never to the negative.

| Series content (rule as fixed after pass 1) | What it would do |
|---|---|
| **Positive:** a consistent direction of response (or an onset-age pattern) for a pre-named class, meeting both floors (stated-response patients per class; independent series) | Name that class as a **comparator candidate** for the pup series and set the age window. It names a comparator, never a mechanism: a consistent response in this genotype class does not separate "genotype-relevant" from "general antiseizure effect" (the loop's own lithium lesson: lithium lowered the PTZ stage in all three genotypes). |
| **Negative:** a stated non-response or worsening meeting both floors | Drop that class from the conservative cluster; say so. |
| **Ambiguous = sparse** (floors not met, or the drug not mentioned) | The project returns "not decision-grade": no clinical-channel spend is justified; the pup series keeps its present comparators. |
- **Forgotten and now named:** the one conservative option whose decision is already named is **ethosuximide with the wild-type age series (OPP-03, the pup series)**; it fails only the weakest-link test (phenotype relevance stays open) and sits in the in vivo cost class, which is why it is not one of the two picks.
- **Needs from the operator:** the human supply (decision page item 14, one sheet), and the choice of any clinical channel (**reserved**).

Not a 15,000 to 20,000 euro study. |
