## Definitions

* **Quoted age:** the age or stage the sentence names. A stage word with no number (developing, early development) is "unspecified stage" and counted separately.
* **Sampled age** for a readout: an age at which the paper reports a measurement of that readout (Tochigi 2019, lde rat, male, cortex: PND 5, 10, 15, 21; Hussain 2019, KO mouse, hippocampus: two weeks only, plus newborn neurospheres in culture, which is not histology).
* **Sentence matches** if its quoted age equals a sampled age of the readout it states. **Sentence generalises** if it states the readout at a stage or age with no sample, or in a region or species the paper did not sample.
* **Functional measurement at that age:** yes only when an electrophysiology, seizure, conduction or behaviour measurement exists in the held sources for the same genotype and the same age window (not the same paper required); marker, area, count or band is never functional.
