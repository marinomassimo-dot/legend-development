## 2. The quantities the proposed WO-21 condition needs (fixed before reading)

* **Proximal engagement of the inositol route:** receptor-evoked PIP2 resynthesis kinetics (a recovery time after stimulation, with its baseline), and receptor-evoked store calcium release (peak or area), each with its own baseline.
* **Genetic dependence:** the lithium effect on those two quantities present in wild-type cells and absent in IMPA1-deleted cells of the same background.
* **Rescue:** a restoring manipulation of the inositol route (for example myo-inositol add-back) that reverses the lithium effect on the proximal quantities, measured in the SAME preparation as the functional endpoint.
* **GSK3 contrast:** a mechanistically independent GSK3 probe with its own engagement readout (beta-catenin accumulation or a phospho-substrate), acting on the same proximal and functional endpoints; a probe is counted only if its engagement is shown.
* **Functional endpoint:** excitability, calcium signalling or firing in neurons; store release is kept apart from general firing or burst suppression and from GSK3 output.
* Lithium concentration and time are recorded as laboratory conditions only.
