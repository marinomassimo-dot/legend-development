## The two interpretations against the paper (pre-registered A and B)
* **A (deficit predicts rescue):** supported in direction for the early phase of ONE variant (early current deficit, opener moves several parameters toward control) and weakened by the late phase (no current deficit, the opener still acts, with exacerbation of biphasic parameters). It cannot be tested across variants (a current exists for one) and no treated control was shown.
* **B (acute quieting, divergent later):** supported in two parts: acute quieting persists at the age when the current difference is gone (late phase, same line), and acute retigabine at an older age is stated to act alike across genotypes (supplement not seen). The "later divergence even after current recovery" part is observed (network differs while the current does not) but is a cross-culture comparison with no causal test, and the preliminary one-week data point the other way for two parameters.
* The two are NOT separated by this paper: a treated control line with the current measured in the same wells or cells is what would separate them, and that is not in the main text. This is the pairing demand.

## Where the existing text already stands, and the delta
* `bridge_slice_panel_sci_20261120`: one S-KO slice session, burst frequency and duration as the ordering readout, d-APV and carbenoxolone as controls, end-of-recording resting potential, XE991 reversal; **wild type beside appears only for the E_GABA recording**; no baseline Kv7 current; no opener-treated control arm.
* `kv7_class_adv117_sci_20261120` section 7: "A Kv7 function measurement in the S-KO showing normal channels" is neutral to toward the class as a brake, away from a DEE-style rationale. That row already assigns a direction to the measurement but treats it as a separate, unrequested observation.
* NP3A3 / WO-17: a missing resting potential and M-current measurement in neuron-deleted against control slices; the brief has no Kv7 or M-current readout; the pup brief carries no Kv7 content (closed for the pup brief).
**What the paper adds to those texts (the delta):** (1) a normal Kv7 current at one age does not show that a network phenotype was never channel-driven or that an opener is only a brake: in this model the current difference is transient (early only) while the network difference grows later; so a baseline measurement is interpretable only at the age and animals of the burst ladder and is not a stand-in for other ages; (2) opener potency shifts with maturation although the current density does not (IC50 473 to 849 nM in the variant line), so a concentration ladder in one age window is not a measure of the defect; (3) an opener that quiets the preparation is not shown to correct anything unless the control preparation is treated alike (here: not in the main figures); (4) "baseline" must say whether it is drug-free or opener-defined: the paper's IKM is measured with the opener on board.

## ONE optional readout for the named consumer (NP3A3 / WO-17; slice panel; hypothesis level; not a gate, not an arm, no dose)
In the session that carries the ezogabine ladder: in cells from the same animals and slices (same age window, wild type or control beside, sex recorded), record one baseline Kv7 readout (a drug-free XE991-sensitive current, or, if an opener-defined current is used as in the paper, state it as a ceiling), and treat control cells or control slices with the same ladder on the same readout the burst panel uses where the control preparation shows activity. Written reading rule: a suppression of null-slice bursts is read as an effect of the opener, and as a correction of the null state only if the baseline current differs from control at that age AND the control preparation does not respond alike; otherwise it is read as a brake. Falsifier: equal baseline current and alike suppression in control (reads as brake; matches the ADV-117 row, now paired); a lower baseline in the null with a response that scales with it would favour A.
Evidence strength: weak (one variant, cross-culture, no main-text treated control, supplement unreached, one group). IT may leave the text as it is; a clause is offered only because the paired baseline, the treated control and the age-specificity are not in the current slice text.

## Limits and what would change this
Adjacent human model of a channel gene; excitatory neurons only with mouse astrocytes; three DEE variants without isogenic controls except G290D; patch at room temperature; the supplementary figures and tables (including Supplementary Fig 13A, Tables 5 and 6, the variant-by-variant network comparison against the isogenic line) were not reached; no number recomputed (no source data); figure images viewed: Fig 1 and Fig 4 only; ages are the paper's culture days, not an animal age, and none is assigned to WWOX. What would turn this into a measured dissociation (P-pair): current and network in the SAME wells or cells, an opener-treated isogenic control line at the same ages, and a read after washout.

## Rejecting observation (restated)
A treated control in the main figures, or a current measured in the network wells, would have made P-pair; its absence, with the cross-culture and single-variant limits, keeps P-logic. A paper showing no current difference at any age would have closed it.
