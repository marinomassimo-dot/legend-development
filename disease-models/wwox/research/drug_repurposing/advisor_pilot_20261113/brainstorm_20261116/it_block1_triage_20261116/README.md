# BLOCK-1 triage of mechanism representatives for the L7 experiment arms (IT, 2026-11-16; non-canonical; not medical advice)

Written for: ADV, SCI (EXPERIMENT_BRIEF.md) and HE. Reference genotype class only. These are MECHANISM LABELS for an experiment design: no agent is recommended, none is ranked, no dose, no clinical statement, no candidate row. A representative here is a way to name an arm, not a repurposing candidate.

## What the safety triage could and could not do here
`legend-safety-triage` needs ADMET-AI and medchem (RDKit). `tool_preflight.py` does not list them, and `import admet_ai`, `import medchem` and `import rdkit` all fail in this deployment (checked 2026-11-16). So NO ADMET prediction, no blood-brain-barrier prediction, no CNS-MPO score and no structural-alert scan was run; the CNS-penetrance column below is `not run`, not `unknown-negative`. Installing the stack (a torch-sized download) was not attempted: it is a new tool, outside what this brief asked, and ADV can authorise it. What the triage CAN show offline is the catalogue's own fields (access category, approval status, target count).

## Limits
Catalogue fields carry approval and access, not tolerability, penetration or exposure. The flag column is from my general knowledge and is labelled as such: a flag means 'check the label before this name goes near a candidate row', and an empty flag would not mean safe. One reader, no delegate, no blind check. Choice of representatives is mine and is not a ranking. Class membership follows the L7 arm list given by ADV; the catalogue's own mechanism rows were not read class by class.
