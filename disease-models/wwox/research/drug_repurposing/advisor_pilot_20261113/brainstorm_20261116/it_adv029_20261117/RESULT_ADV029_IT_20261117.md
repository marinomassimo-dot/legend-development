## Update 2026-11-17 (IT; correction after ADV's second route; the text above is unchanged)
**Correction.** The answer-first sentence 'named LDHA potency in ChEMBL is micromolar; no named nanomolar LDHA inhibitor was returned' is true only **among the 26 named rows** of the human LDHA target (CHEMBL4835). ADV's second route (activity.json, IC50, nM, value <= 100) shows 236 rows, 191 distinct molecules, 14 documents, minimum 0.07 nM: **nanomolar LDHA potency is present in ChEMBL under compound ids that carry no name.** My 'not found by name' meant that the name search stores no pref_name or synonym for them; it did not mean the compound is absent. The identity caveat of E02/E03/K01 is therefore: nanomolar potency exists under unnamed ids; identity by name is not settled by a synonym search; a declared id or a structure check is needed.
