## 5. DREAM line (legend-dream pass, hypothesis level)
The GSK3 neighbour (PAPER 495) and the ADV-214 result ("no held phospho-protein table") meet at one gap: neither a Tau-phospho plus beta-catenin pair under two inhibitor classes nor any phospho table exists in held material, so the minimum assay named in ADV-214 stands unchanged, and PAPER 495 supplies only the precedent for the phospho-GSK3 plus beta-catenin pair in a neonatal white-matter model. Connection nobody had made: PAPER 157 links WWOX to SOX10 stability through a WW1 and PPxY interface (lysate co-precipitation level), while PAPER 495 shows that raising beta-catenin and TCF4 by kinase inhibition, but not by Wnt3A ligand, moves oligodendrocyte maturation and myelin in a haemorrhage model. If both held, a GSK3-inhibitor arm in an oligodendroglia-specific Wwox-null remyelination assay would separate "SOX10 stability" from "beta-catenin and TCF4 maturation" in INV-06's role question; this is a hypothesis for the portfolio, not a claim, and it needs an assay that does not yet exist in held data.
