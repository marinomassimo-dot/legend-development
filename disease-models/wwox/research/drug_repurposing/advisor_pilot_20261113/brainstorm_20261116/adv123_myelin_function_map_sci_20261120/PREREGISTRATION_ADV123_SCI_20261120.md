## 1. Inclusion (fixed)
A row is admitted only if the same study (or a directly linked pair) measured **both** a functional readout (survival, weight, behaviour, seizure, electrophysiology, motor or cognitive score in humans) **and** a myelin readout (EM g-ratio or fibre counts, MBP or PLP staining or blot, myelin-sensitive MRI, myelinated-fibre density) **after a rescue or an intervention** in a disorder or model whose **primary lesion is neuronal or in thyroid-hormone transport or action** (Mct8/Slc16a2, Oatp1c1, Dio2, thyroid hormone receptor access). Arms: (a) rescue studies (genetic restoration, ASO, gene therapy) in such models (Rett, Angelman, Pitt-Hopkins, Wwox, other); (b) myelin-directed interventions (clemastine, thyromimetics, other pro-differentiation agents) in such models with a functional outcome; (c) the human MCT8 deficiency record of thyromimetic treatment (function and myelination imaging, as reported). **Excluded from the counted map, listed once as a boundary note:** primary oligodendrocyte or myelin disorders (Plp1, Mbp, leukodystrophies), demyelination models (EAE, cuprizone, lysolecithin), and injury models (neonatal hypoxia, preterm white-matter injury) where the lesion is not neuronal.

## 2. Row schema (one table, one row per intervention and model)
`row_id | arm | disorder, model, lesion and cell type | intervention (agent or restoration, cell type, age at start, duration) | functional readout and change as printed | myelin readout (method) and change as printed | TIMING code | ages of each readout | source id | depth (FT, AB, LAB) | one-origin flag | competing explanation | weakening observation | locator and verbatim quote (at most 300 characters)`.
**TIMING codes:** `F_FIRST` (function improved at a time when the myelin readout had not yet changed or was measured later, two or more times in the same study); `M_FIRST` (myelin improved before function, or myelin improved without the neuronal lesion corrected and function followed); `F_ONLY` (function improved, myelin unchanged or not normalised); `M_ONLY` (myelin improved, function unchanged); `SAME_TIME` (both measured at one time only, no order); `M_NOT_MEASURED` (listed in the boundary note, not counted). A row without both readouts is not a row.

## 3. Closing readouts, fixed in advance
Three readings; the closing paragraph states which the evidence supports and which fail, with counts of rows by arm, depth and origin:
* **R1 downstream:** myelin improvement follows neuronal rescue with a lag, and myelin-directed interventions do not improve function where the neuronal lesion persists. Supported if the counted map holds at least two independent-origin rescue rows with `F_FIRST` or `SAME_TIME` and normalised myelin, and no full-text `M_FIRST`/`M_ONLY`-with-function row in arm (b) with the neuronal lesion uncorrected.
* **R2 independently modifiable and a contributor:** a myelin-directed intervention improves a functional readout while the neuronal lesion persists. Supported if arm (b) holds at least two independent-origin full-text rows (different laboratories) with `M_FIRST` or myelin gain plus function gain without lesion correction, or a human MCT8 row with myelin imaging change plus function change under a thyromimetic that does not correct the neuronal cause.
* **R3 not resolved:** neither threshold met, or rows conflict at equal depth; the closing paragraph then names the single observation that would split R1 from R2.
Weights: an abstract-only row can support but never meets a threshold alone; a row sharing one laboratory or one animal cohort with another counts once (one-origin flag); a row where the myelin measure is a bulk marker without ultrastructure is marked weak.
**What each reading does to the broad comparison** (stated in the closing paragraph from the result): R1 lowers the weight of an oligodendrocyte-differentiation arm as a contributor (it is a readout), R2 raises it, R3 leaves it; the same for the thyromimetic arm, which is read separately because the thyromimetic target (thyroid hormone access) is upstream of both neurons and oligodendrocytes (a thyromimetic effect does not separate R1 from R2 unless the neuronal lesion is shown to persist). **The reversing observation** is named for the supported reading.
