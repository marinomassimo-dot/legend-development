## Conflicts between sources
- Clemastine in wild-type juvenile mice (Palma: slower conduction, fewer myelinated axons) versus clemastine in Tcf4+/tr at P28-P42 (Bohlen: N1/N2 ratio up, g-ratio down). Different model, age (P5-P20 vs P28-P42), dose and readouts; not reconciled by either paper.
- Bohlen text "normalised" versus its own EM: total myelinated-axon density not restored.
- Behaviour: clemastine normalises hyperlocomotion in Tcf4+/tr (Bohlen) but not in Cntnap2-/- (B6 preprint).
- Behaviour rescued with unchanged OL molecular phenotype (B9, AB) versus behaviour rescue attributed to myelination (B5, B6, B7); the authors of B5 state clemastine's polypharmacology prevents attribution, and use sobetirome as the second agent.
- Triac normalises FluoroMyelin and function (B1) while grey matter is not rescued (B4): the persisting lesion is partial.
