## Why
Read `PREREGISTRATION_ADV123_SCI_20261120.md` in this folder (sections 0 to 3) and `framework/protocols/delegation_budget.md` sections 2, 7 and 9 first. The question: in disorders and models with a neuronal (or thyroid-transport) primary lesion, is oligodendrocyte differentiation a readout downstream of the neuronal lesion, or independently modifiable and a contributor to recovery? The map needs rows where BOTH a functional and a myelin readout were measured after a rescue or an intervention, with the timing. Already held in the repository (do not repeat; read their rows only as leads): the neuronal AAV9 Wwox study (Repudi 2021: survival, weight, clasping, glucose and spike-wave closed at high dose; an EM myelin count not normalised, n of 3 at P17; its EM panels are measured in `brainstorm_20261116/benchmark_card_adv120_sci_20261120/`), the Wwox gene-therapy papers PMIDs 34747138 and 42422765 (held), and the OL-class report of ADV-095. No Rett or Angelman rescue with a myelin readout is held.

## Items (in this order; stop an item at the first source set that answers it or when two routes are exhausted; aim at up to 12 admitted rows for this arm)
1. **Rett and MECP2:** Mecp2 reactivation or restoration (genetic reactivation, AAV9-MECP2, ASO or gene replacement) with any myelin or white-matter readout (EM, MBP, MRI, OPC markers) and a functional readout (survival, behaviour, breathing, motor). Also Mecp2 deletion restricted to neurons or to oligodendrocyte lineage with myelin and function (these show which cell type carries the myelin phenotype; list them as lesion-localisation rows, not rescue rows, unless a rescue was done).
2. **Angelman and UBE3A:** Ube3a reinstatement (genetic, ASO) with a myelin readout (hypomyelination reports in Ube3a-deficient models) and a functional readout.
3. **Other neuronal-lesion rescues with both readouts:** Pitt-Hopkins (TCF4) restoration, Fragile X (Fmr1), CDKL5, Tsc1/Tsc2 and mTOR (neuronal loss with a rapamycin or genetic rescue and a myelin readout), Huntington gene-lowering with a myelin readout, and any other neuronal-lesion gene-restoration or ASO study that printed a myelin readout. Prefer studies that measured at two or more ages.
4. **Wwox:** read the myelin and function passages of the held Wwox gene-therapy full texts (PMIDs 34747138, 42422765; the receipt ledger and `files/fulltext` hold them) and of Repudi 2021 if reachable: for each rescue with a myelin readout give the row (function change, myelin change, ages, dose arms as printed, n). Say whether the printed EM count is per animal or per field and the n.
5. **Timing question:** for every admitted rescue row, state whether function and myelin were measured at the same age or at several ages and which moved first, as printed.
