## Three requested lines

1. **Triples I could not reach:** none was not reached. V12 (supplementary S11/S12) and V13 (supplementary table s2/s3 and the postmortem results) were read at main-text depth only. Routes: Europe PMC `fullTextXML` returned HTTP 500 once each for PMC7065955 (V03), PMC3225598 (V06), PMC7611932 (V13) and PMC8110025 (V14); NCBI efetch `db=pmc` then worked for the first three. For V14 efetch returned the abstract only, so I used the PMC article page for the full text. V11 came from bioRxiv full-text HTML (HTTP 200, no challenge page).

2. **Disagreements between a proposition and its source (age, comparison group, statistic):**
   - V07: the "near WT" text rests on a comparison with p = 0.4833, but the KO+shCtrl vs KO+shRptor comparison has the same p = 0.4833, so no significant gain over the KO control group is shown.
   - V06: the text gives n = 12 rapamycin-treated CKO mice but the Fig 7A legend gives n = 9 (vehicle n = 6). The MBP comparison is P40 to 44 treated mice against controls, with no vehicle CKO at that age.
   - V05: "no MBP difference between genotypes" holds for white matter only, and qualitatively ("apparent"); gray-matter MBP differed ("pronouncedly reduced").
   - V03/V01: Phan 2020 reports no g-ratio difference in untreated Tcf4+/tr EM, while the V01 source reports an elevated g-ratio in vehicle-treated Tcf4+/tr mice. V03's cell-autonomy claim rests on OL-lineage counts, not myelin.
   - V14: "near-normal" is abstract wording only, and the abstract's "developmental quotients" are actually months-equivalent values.
   - V04: cooperative-drinking improvement is partial (percentage curve not significant; latency and number significant); the Y-maze/NOR "unchanged" result is against a baseline with no deficit.

3. **Passages bearing on whether the lesion is neuronal, glial or global:**
   - V08 (Wwox-null, neuronal AAV9): "neuronal restoration of WWOX positively regulates myelination likely by promoting OPC differentiation in a non-cell autonomous manner."
   - V09 (Wwox): "oligodendrocyte-specific WWOX expression driven by the MBP promoter resulted in weak expression and no phenotypic improvement, demonstrating that restoration in this lineage alone is insufficient". The Discussion adds this "may reflect the limited oligodendrocyte tropism of AAV9... rather than a lack of relevance for oligodendrocyte WWOX expression". Earlier work is cited: deletion in nestin-lineage progenitors or in postmitotic neurons "closely phenocopy[ies] global Wwox-null mice".
   - V03 (Tcf4): "reductions in myelination due to Tcf4 mutations are cell autonomous" (also in the V01 source's Discussion), but the Phan Discussion adds "we suggest ASD mutations affecting neuronal function may lead to non-cell autonomous defects in myelination".
   - V11 (Cntnap2): "the myelination deficits in cntnap2 -/- mice arise non-cell-autonomously", since cntnap2 is "predominantly expressed in axons with minimal expression in oligodendrocytes" and mutant oligodendrocytes myelinate nanofibers normally in vitro.
   - V12 (Angelman): "an intrinsic requirement for oligodendroglial UBE3A in OPC proliferation and myelination".
   - V07 (Tsc1): "Emx1-Cre... expressed in excitatory neurons but also in some glial cells including oligodendrocytes", and the authors "hypothesize" a non-cell-autonomous myelin increase from neuronal Raptor reduction.
   - V06 (Tsc1): Emx1-Cre targets "dorsal neural progenitor cells", which give rise to neurons, astrocytes and "a subset of cortical oligodendrocytes"; rapamycin treatment "largely reversed the glia pathology but not abnormal neuronal lamination".
   - V13/V14 (MCT8): the sources describe a global delay in myelination ("global delay in myelination" in V13), with no cell-type attribution.
