## Why
Read `PREREGISTRATION_ADV123_SCI_20261120.md` in this folder (sections 0 to 3) and `framework/protocols/delegation_budget.md` sections 2, 7 and 9 first. The question: in disorders and models with a neuronal (or thyroid-transport) primary lesion, is oligodendrocyte differentiation a readout downstream of the neuronal lesion, or independently modifiable and a contributor to recovery? The map needs rows where BOTH a functional and a myelin readout were measured after a rescue or an intervention, with the timing. Already held in the repository (do not repeat; read their rows only as leads): the tiratricol label (EU approval 12 Feb 2025 for peripheral thyrotoxicosis in MCT8 deficiency, from birth; the openFDA label of 2026-10-01 for adults and pediatric patients, effectiveness from a single-arm trial and a double-blind trial; text read in part), the phase 2 Triac trial abstract (PMID 31377265: the primary endpoint is serum T3).

## Items (in this order; stop an item at the first source set that answers it or when two routes are exhausted; aim at up to 12 admitted rows for this arm)
1. **The human trials and cohorts:** the Triac trial I and its extension, the randomised double-blind trial of Triac in MCT8 deficiency (Triac Trial II or the pivotal trial in the label), the pre-symptomatic or early-start Triac reports, and any report of DITPA, sobetirome or other thyromimetics in MCT8 deficiency: for each, the functional outcomes as reported (motor, developmental or neurocognitive scores, weight, heart rate, T3) and the **myelination** readouts (MRI myelination score or myelin-sensitive imaging), with the ages at start and at each assessment and the number of patients, as reported (no dose or advice).
2. **Myelination imaging specifically:** any paper that reports MRI myelination before and after thyromimetic treatment in MCT8 deficiency (including case reports and the delayed-myelination natural-history papers), and what changed and when. Say clearly when the paper reports a function change without an imaging change, or an imaging change without a function change.
3. **Natural history:** the MRI myelination course of untreated MCT8 deficiency by age (delayed myelination that partly resolves), because a myelination change under treatment must be read against it.
4. **Label and regulatory text:** the effectiveness statements as printed (which endpoint, which trial), without dose.
5. **One-origin check:** the same cohort reported in several papers counts once.
