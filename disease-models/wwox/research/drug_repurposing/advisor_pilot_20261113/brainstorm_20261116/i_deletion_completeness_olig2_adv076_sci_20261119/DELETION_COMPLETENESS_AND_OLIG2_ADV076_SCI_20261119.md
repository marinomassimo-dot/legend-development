**Held supplementary table (neuronal-knockout bulk RNA-seq, cortex and hippocampus together, 6 against 6):** the Wwox row gives log2 fold change about -1.86; its first six values (172.7, 187.0, 331.4 and 18.8, 18.8, 28.0 normalised counts) match the cortex and hippocampus figures quoted by the audit; I did not map the column headers myself, so the assignment of those six values to tissue rests on the audit and on the magnitude (a ten-fold difference) agreeing with my deposit values.

**What it shows.**
1. **Residual bulk Wwox is not deletion completeness.** The control cortex expresses about five times the hippocampal Wwox TPM (18.8 against 3.4), and bulk tissue carries non-neuronal Wwox that a neuron-targeted deletion does not remove; the apparent 20 to 50 percent residual in cortex mixes incomplete neuronal deletion, glial expression and a three-fold spread among the controls themselves.
2. **In the ADV-068 primary contrast one knockout animal is indistinguishable from the controls by Wwox:** KO3's 9.0 TPM lies inside the control range (8.0 to 26.5); its PV and late-set values (-0.29, -0.19) also lie inside the control range. The signature **orders with residual Wwox in rank across the three animals for PV** (residual 20, 27, 48 percent give -0.95, -0.72, -0.29); the late set does not order strictly (-0.30, -0.51, -0.19); n is 3.
3. Deletion is **more complete in the hippocampus** (about 2 to 22 percent residual) than in the cortex, yet the hippocampal neuron-targeted contrast has the larger PV shift; across lines the PV shift does not simply track residual Wwox (the progenitor-targeted line has the least residual and the smallest shift).
**What it does to ADV-068 to ADV-070 (frozen criteria not rewritten; this is sensitivity):**
* ADV-068: the primary contrast includes a knockout animal whose Wwox is within the control range. Leaving it out (a sensitivity, not the primary) leaves two knockouts (PV -0.83, late -0.40, late-to-PV ratio 0.49), which is below the three-animal threshold, so no rule label applies and the strict reading of the half-PV condition would not be met. The dose-ordering across animals (point 2) makes a Wwox-dependent signature more plausible than a batch effect, at n = 3. Together with the earlier rule-reading correction this strengthens the statement that the cortical R-B verdict is fragile; the **decision (no PV-specific premise) is unchanged**.
* ADV-070: the stage score of the knockout animals is the same quantity as ADV-068's late value, and across the three cortex animals it moves with residual Wwox; **stage and deletion depth cannot be separated at n = 3**, one more reason the covariate's labels are weak.
* Slice pilot and pup brief: do not assume a Synapsin-Cre deletion is uniform across regions and animals; **record deletion completeness per animal and per region at the protein level** (immunostaining or a per-slice check), and report each effect against it; a transcript residual is not a substitute.
**Limits:** one age (P17); bulk transcripts; TPM; control spread; the supplement column mapping is unverified by me; no protein-level deletion data in any held deposit.

## (b) Olig2 counts fall while Mbp area is unchanged (Hussain 2023, P47T knock-in)
Read by the delegate in full text with the main figures viewed; **the supplement (Figures S6a to d, tables) is not held.**
1. **The "dissociation" is a cross-region contrast, not a within-region one.** Olig2-positive cells were counted in the **corpus callosum** (80 and 250 days; wild type against P47T/P47T; legend n = 3 mice per group, each dot a region, about 9 or 10 dots per group; unpaired t test on regions, pseudoreplication; graph-read about 205 against 120 at 80 days and about 235 against 115 at 250 days, 40 to 50 percent lower; unit of the count not stated). Mbp was stained in the **parietal cortex above the callosum**; the main text gives only "no significant differences ... (Supplementary Figs 6a-d)": no antibody, metric, age, sex, n or test in the Methods or main text (the anti-Mbp antibody is not in the Methods list).
2. Olig2 marks all oligodendrocyte-lineage cells; the paper cannot say which stage is lost. No stage markers were stained. The transcript deconvolution is inconsistent: the text says reduced mature-oligodendrocyte and progenitor scores in the hippocampus, the figure shows the mature-oligodendrocyte score not significant there (P = 0.43) and significant in the prefrontal cortex (P = 0.02); no cortical result is shown where Mbp was stained.
3. Not measured: Luxol fast blue, electron microscopy (so no g-ratio), any other myelin protein, conduction of any kind, heterozygotes, sex-stratified data. The Fig 4 title says "hippocampi" while the quantitation is callosal; the region size notation (500 square micrometres) is evidently a unit slip.
**Consequence for the myelin cards and the composition caveat:** the audit's item 8 is **not a usable dissociation** (cross-region, the Mbp side unverifiable without the supplement, the Olig2 side n = 3 with regions as units); the P47T report supports "fewer Olig2-positive cells in the callosum at two adult ages" as a composition caveat for any P47T bulk readout (consistent in direction with the oligodendrocyte-lineage loss of the null and conditional lines), not "oligodendrocytes lost without hypomyelination"; the myelin cards' structural endpoints (electron microscopy, MBP, CC1, with neuronal-rescue evidence in the null and conditional lines) are unchanged.
**Access:** the Hussain 2023 supplement (Mbp panels and tables) is the one item that would settle the Mbp side: add to the supply list.
