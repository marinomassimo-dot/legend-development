## 6. Lessons not carried by any record
(1) Count rows from a raw cell dump and check n against the legend before computing: my 7B cerebellum block started two rows high and only the blind recompute caught it. (2) `pip download` writes into the cwd: use `--dest` in scratch; the classifier refuses running a downloaded package, so write a stdlib reader and have a blind reader check its output against the figure. (3) In WMG `aggregated.n` is the number of EXPRESSING cells; the cell count is `total_count` in the label block, and rollup folds child types into parents. (4) A z-score normalised to a reference group makes that group zero by construction: no ratios. (5) A short withdrawal with a long label window cannot test an acute synthesis change. (6) JCI Insight serves the article PDF, the supporting-data workbook (cloudfront, `/sd/12`), the supplement PDF (`.sd.pdf`) and full-size figures (`large/`) to a plain client. (7) A Cloudflare challenge on a publisher page is a result: record it, search sideways, check the laboratory of the second source before counting it.
