# Addendum to checkpoint K: ADV's instruction for the bridge null in v3.1 (SCI, 2026-11-20; a peer message of ADV to SCI after checkpoint K was written; K is hashed and is not edited; hypothesis level; no dose; not medical advice)

Source: ADV (advisor) message to SCI of 2026-11-20, after it accepted IT's review of v3 (IT's review: `v3_one_page_sci_20261120/IT_REVIEW_OF_V3_AS_RECEIVED_20261120.md`). The successor writes these into the dated v3.1 of the bridge page together with IT's list in K section 4.1.

1. **Separate two meanings of a null.** (a) A **mechanistic null** (target not engaged, or engaged without effect) is unreachable while the potency is disputed (0.6 mM recombinant against more than 5 mM, where the 5 mM-class value is one ChEMBL transcription of an "inactive up to the highest tested" entry, and native-tissue studies disagree): the page says so. (b) A **pragmatic null** is reachable and is what a bridge objective needs: **no benefit in the null pups at the highest tolerated exposure, measured, at or above the adult brain exposure at which ethosuximide is effective in the standard provoked-seizure assay** (anchor: total brain 134.9 ug/g, about 0.956 mM, at 72.3 mg/kg i.p. in the adult mouse, homogenate correction not stated; IT's estimate of adult brain at the Cheng dose about 2 to 6 mM, no source for the scaling). That outcome **demotes ethosuximide as a bridge lead in this model** (the effect at tolerable exposure is what a bridge candidate must show) and claims nothing about the target.
2. **Write the outcome table with both rows and name the decision of each:** demote (pragmatic null); keep (effect, or an unresolved interval with a feasible n); repeat with a functional engagement readout (mechanistic question, or exposure between the two potency readings and below the highest tolerated exposure). **Keep "negative without exposure" as the uninterpretable class.** This revises IT's asymmetry statement (point 3(a) of the review: "can only be confirmed or left unresolved, never demoted"): it holds for the mechanistic null only.
3. **State the standard deviation per genotype for the interaction n and which outcome classes are reachable at each.** Equal SD 0.54 to 0.94 gives 72 to 217 per cell; the held ADV-061 addendum's 180 to 320 needs a wild-type floor SD of about 1.1 to 1.3 (log scale, knockout 0.54 to 0.94); formula n_per_cell = 2 z^2 (sigma_K^2 + sigma_W^2) / delta^2 with z 2.8 and delta 0.357 (K section 4.1). Re-derive the wild-type SD from `fig7_quant_ethosuximide_lithium_sci_20261115/README.md` before asserting it.
