## 5. Lessons that no record carries
(1) `pmc_bucket_fetch.py` returns figures and the article PDF for a genuinely open-access paper (Obeid 2026: 8 figures, PDF, two supplements) and only a restricted stub for a closed one (Cell Death Differ 2014); the PDF's embedded figure may be a single 300 ppi raster, so "vector first" can end at raster with the resolution stated. (2) PIL and matplotlib are absent: measure markers with `pngtopnm` or `convert ... ppm`, numpy, connected components by colour and the axis ticks as calibration. (3) A panel's n and p can contradict its legend and the Results sentence (Fig 7C: 2 against 3 points, p = 0.2000 against "n = 5" and "significant"): read panel, legend and sentence separately. (4) Commit the pre-registration BEFORE reading any value; mine was written before the image was opened but committed with the result, and the note says so. (5) The nohup wrapper PID is not the push process: `pgrep -f` the real command and wait on that PID. (6) A repository note ("no wild-type arm") and a legend can disagree and both be unverified: say which source says what and leave it unresolved. (7) An abstract of an earlier source can contradict the later paper's own claim (rat "normal serum" against raised urea and creatinine): second-hand statements cannot carry a dissociation. (8) My pre-registered rule left a hole (neither promote nor park): state the hole instead of filling it after the data. (9) Wild-type glucose differs about two-fold between laboratories (250 and 130 mg/dL): severity of hypoglycaemia cannot be compared across papers. (10) The peer's "restart" figure and the census figure can differ: report the census figure.
