## Trigger being tested

ADV read the whole abstract; it says interneuron dysfunction contributes, treatment partially restored firing properties in vitro and reduced seizure burden in vivo, and names no population beyond "interneuron", no molecule, model, age, n, EEG measure or comparator. ## Question
Does ANY public material beyond that abstract state (a) the cell population, (b) the intervention, (c) the model and age, (d) the per-animal EEG measure with n, (e) the comparators?

## Level definitions (fixed now; the wording of INV-15 is written at the highest level that is met, and no higher)
* **L1, effect on seizures:** a statement that an intervention reduced seizure burden, with the model named. Met at abstract level by the SfN abstract only if a model is identified; a bare "in vivo" is L1 without a model (recorded as L1-unanchored).
* **L2, selective recovery of a cellular function:** a named cell population (for example parvalbumin-expressing, somatostatin-expressing, an excitatory class) whose measured property (firing, intrinsic excitability, synaptic input) is restored by the intervention, with the comparison against untreated mutant and against wild type stated. "Interneuron" alone is not a population; "firing properties" in an unnamed cell is L2-unanchored.
* **L3, evidence that L2 explains the EEG effect:** the same animals or a matched cohort show the cellular recovery and the EEG change, plus a manipulation that separates them (cell-type-specific rescue, silencing or occlusion), or a per-animal association with n. A parallel statement that both happened is not L3.
Each statement of a document is assigned to a level with its verbatim locator; "no level reached" is a valid assignment.
