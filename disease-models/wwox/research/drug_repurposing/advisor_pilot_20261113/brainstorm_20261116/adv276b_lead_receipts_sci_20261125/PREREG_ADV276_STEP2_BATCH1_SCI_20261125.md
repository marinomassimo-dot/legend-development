## What counts as read

For each paper: the passages, tables, legends and Methods statements that carry the consumer's sentence, plus searches recorded with date and query. A class is `read` only when all of it was read; otherwise the receipt is `queried_not_full_read`. A supplement is read only where the consumer's number or reagent sits in it and the file is obtainable by a permitted route.

## Failure rule

A paper fails the check when a table, legend or Methods statement contradicts the consumer's sentence, or when the sentence omits species, replicate unit, dose or exposure window in a way that changes what it can support. The exposure window (who was exposed, when, for how long) is written beside every in vivo number.
