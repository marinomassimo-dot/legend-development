## 3. Lessons

1. A line that says "recording at room temperature" needs the sentence of the Methods that gives the recording, not the recovery.
2. A printed n can sit above a different unit: axons under three mice, wells under three experiments, the same organoids before and after drug.
3. **A producer's search negative can be false:** the phrase "myelinated area" was reported absent after a search of the body; it is in the abstract. Every negative needs the whole XML including title and abstract.
4. A brief that quotes a consumer clause can send a producer after a clause that belongs to another source (the serum-glucose clause is our own Wwox data): label such clauses in the brief.
5. A text claim ("every induction day") can be contradicted by the paper's own source-data sheet: open the sheet behind a general statement.
6. A source-data P value may only reproduce under a different test than the legend names (paired against Student's); recompute before repeating it.
