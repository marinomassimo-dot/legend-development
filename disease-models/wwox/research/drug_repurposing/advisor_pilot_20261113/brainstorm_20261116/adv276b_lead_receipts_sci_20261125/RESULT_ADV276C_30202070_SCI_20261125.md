## 1. What the chloroquine statement rests on

**Supplementary Figure 1b** is the only place chloroquine appears as an experiment (query `chloroquine|CQ` over main text and Supplementary Information, 2026-11-25).

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Cells | human primary fibroblasts, NPC1 I1061T/I1061T only |
| Chloroquine | **10 µM**, 24 h, one concentration |
| Bafilomycin A1 | 100 nM, 24 h, same blot |
| Replicates | three independent experiments, mean ± s.e.m.; ANOVA with Tukey |
| NPC1, relative intensity (SCI pixel measurement of the bar chart) | vehicle about 1.06; chloroquine about 1.43; brefeldin A (6 h) about 0.50; bafilomycin about 3.38 |
| Printed significance | chloroquine not significant; bafilomycin `**` |

So chloroquine gave about a 1.35-fold rise that did not reach significance, beside about 3.2-fold for bafilomycin.

* **No NH₄Cl experiment** (query `NH4Cl|ammonium chloride`, main text and Supplementary Information, zero hits, 2026-11-25).
* **Chloroquine was not tested in the control line or in any compound heterozygote.**
* The paper has **four** compound-heterozygous lines, not three (Methods, Cells: GM17912, GM03123, GM17926, GM17924; plus control GM08399 and homozygote GM18453). Bafilomycin raised NPC1 in all four; MG132 did so significantly in two (Figure 1d and Supplementary Figure 1d).
* The authors write that the difference "likely reflects" greater pH neutralisation by bafilomycin and that other possibilities cannot be excluded. No lysosomal pH measurement accompanies the comparison in the passages and panels read.

## 2. What else the figures show

* Main Figure 1a: untreated I1061T is a few percent of untreated control; bafilomycin brings it to about 1.25 times untreated control (the basis of "recovered to WT levels"), while bafilomycin-treated control is about 2.5 times. Values read by eye from the printed bars.
* The bafilomycin effect differs widely between the two panels (about 3-fold in Supplementary Figure 1b, more than tenfold in Figure 1a, where the vehicle bar is near the floor).
* Main Figure 1c: in I1061T, accumulation under bafilomycin is not significant at 8 h or 16 h and is significant at 20 h and 24 h. An inhibitor arm of 16 h or less could read as negative on this protein.
* The cycloheximide chases (60 µg/ml; with or without serum, or after siRNA) do **not** start from a bafilomycin-stabilised pool. "Chase from the stabilised condition" is this repository's inference, not what the paper did.
