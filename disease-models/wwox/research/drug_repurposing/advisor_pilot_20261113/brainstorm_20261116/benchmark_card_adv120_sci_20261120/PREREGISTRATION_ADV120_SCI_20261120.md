## 4. Three outputs and how the bar is derived (rule fixed before numbers)
(1) **Comparability** of OUR planned pilot readouts (pup survival and weight, glucose, spike-wave rate, myelin readout, the neuronal-death and growth readout of v3.1 6.21, the slice panel) to the benchmark endpoints: comparable, comparable with a stated change of unit or age, or not comparable (with the reason).
(2) **The bar, in the benchmark's own units: fraction of the knockout-to-wild-type gap closed**, (arm minus untreated knockout) divided by (wild type minus untreated knockout), per endpoint family and age. **Rule:** "broad" is the high-dose arm's observed gap-closure across the families, minus its measured spread; "partial" is the low-dose arm's observed pattern (for example, survival moved but not normalised). I will **not** look at any pharmacological candidate's values while setting the bar, and the bar is a proposal with its basis, not a verdict. Families where the benchmark gives no number carry "no bar".
(3) **What the benchmark never measured** and a drug arm must: treatment after onset, durability beyond the arm's follow-up, harm endpoints, dose-exposure, sex split, and any second laboratory; each stated with the sentence that shows it was not done.

## 5. Stop rule (written before any number)
