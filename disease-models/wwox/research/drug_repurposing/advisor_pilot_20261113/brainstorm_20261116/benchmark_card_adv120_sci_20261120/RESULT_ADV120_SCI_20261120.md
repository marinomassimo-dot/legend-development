# ADV-120 result: the benchmark card for "broad functional recovery" (SCI successor window, sitting 2, 2026-11-20; hypothesis level; no dose or use recommendation; reference genotype class only; not medical advice)

**Status.** Measured under the frozen stop rule of `PREREGISTRATION_ADV120_SCI_20261120.md`: 15 panels, each read with two y calibrations, no literature search, no delegate, no spend, no candidate value looked at while the bar was set. Every number below is in `OUTPUT_card_numbers_sitting2.txt`, produced by `compute_card_numbers.py` from the raw `measurements/*.json`; every quoted sentence is checked verbatim by `quote_check.py` (`OUTPUT_quote_check.txt`: 32 of 32 found). The benchmark is **two papers of one laboratory**: Obeid 2026 (PMID 42422765; neonatal ICV AAV9, two doses, low LD and high HD) and Repudi 2021 (PMID 34747138; EM). "Unmyelinated axons 52 versus 26" is from the 2021 paper, **not** from Obeid 2026.

## 1. The answer first
1. **High dose closes most of the knockout-to-wild-type gap on every family where an untreated knockout exists in the same panel; low dose closes about 0.2 to 0.5 and none of adult survival.** Gap closed (HD / LD): survival at P60 1.03 / 0.44, at P90 0.86 / 0.00; weight P10 0.79 / 0.19, P15 0.56 / 0.40; clasping at P18 0.94 / 0.51; glucose at P10 0.52 / 0.30 (the interval is too wide to use, see 3); spike-wave 0.83 / no low-dose arm.
2. **The reference ceiling is not 100 percent.** Control-injected wild type plateaus at 71.8 percent alive from 125 days (measured), and HD at 76.7 to 76.8 percent to 300 days; HD over WT is 1.07 at 300 days.
3. **Survival to P20 or P30 cannot separate partial from broad.** LD closes 0.90 at P20 and 0.80 at P30 (HD 1.00 at both); the two arms separate only from about P60 (0.44 against 1.03) and are decisive at P90 (0.00 against 0.86).
4. **The bar** (section 4) is set in the benchmark's own units and ages; **what the benchmark never measured** is in section 5. The working-model wording "a threshold, not a graded continuum" is **not supported as worded** (section 6; a proposal, not an edit).
