## 1 The answer first
- **The host's browser works; the failures were the snap's private `/tmp`.** `repurposing_view.py browser` line 2 names `/usr/bin/chromium-browser` (the snap, Chromium 157.0.8103.50: starts, headless DevTools OK in 1.2 to 1.6 s; the 3593 revision needs GLIBC 2.43 and does not run, 3583 does).
