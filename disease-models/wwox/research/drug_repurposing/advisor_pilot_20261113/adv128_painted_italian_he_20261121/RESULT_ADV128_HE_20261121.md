# ADV-128 result (HE, 2026-11-21): Italian text painted on the non-Italian view pages

**Answer first.** Two classes were found, split as the assignment asked. (1) UI labels typed in Italian in the generator, outside the catalogue: 10 template headings and the footer label, the whole deepened-cases table and its notes, and the matching-pilot table with its labels. Fixed with 34 UI keys (4 translations each, existing translations reused where the same passage was already in the content catalogue), through the same mapping method as ADV-127. (2) Authored Italian content (pilot-case fields, work-order summaries, evidence sentences, `predictive_test.json` lines): counted, not translated; the smallest path is below. Painted before/after per page is in the table.

## Method
- Painted text read through `repurposing_view.Cdp` (snap Chromium, `TMPDIR` under the home, pages copied to a non-hidden directory: the first scan of the files under `.claude/` painted 5 nodes and was discarded). Every visible text node (a tree walk, `getClientRects` non-empty), outside `[lang=it]` and `.orig` passages. 16 pages (shareable and full, en/fr/es/de, 1280 and 390 px).
- Detector written first, three parts (scripts in this folder): A: an Italian interface string of `ui_source.json` equal to or inside a node (wording differs from its translation); B: two or more Italian function words; N: a character-trigram discriminator, Italian vs (English plus the page language), trained on the repository's own strings (held-out accuracy about 99% in both directions on strings of 12 or more characters, mean log-likelihood-ratio threshold 0.1), applied to prose-like nodes only (three or more words, no URL, no snake-case identifier). Two earlier detectors (a lexicon one and an identical-to-the-Italian-page one) were dropped for false positives: hundreds of English words and identifiers.
- Positive control: the pre-ADV-127 English page trips A on 70 nodes (the kind strings).
- Counted once per distinct node; 1280 and 390 px give the same counts (differences of 1 node at most).

## Split of the remainder
- **Group 1, UI labels (fixed).** Shareable EN: 23 to 11 flagged; all the Italian labels are gone; what is left there is 4 authored items (below) and 7 detector false positives (`CIViC (via DGIdb)` and three like it, `[Cell Mol Life Sci, 2023]`, `data too sparse`, one English paragraph that contains a UI string). Full pages: the label items are gone too (the repository source no longer holds an Italian heading or table header outside the catalogue; three literals remain by design: `catalog.jsonl.gz`, `none in record`, and the value `un solo sistema`, which is a context-verdict word printed raw in two table cells: a lead).
- **Group 2, authored Italian content (not translated, counted).** About 155 distinct nodes in the full edition and 4 in the shareable one. Origins: `matching_pilot_cases.json` (75: the pilot's context, benefit, evidence and refutation fields, full edition only), ranking evidence sentences built from requirement rows (about 12 patterns of the form `: <requirement> — <reason>`), the work-order `summary_it` shown after the translated kind (`test a hypothesis: Capire quale di tre letture...`), and one-offs from `predictive_test.json` (`rete con segno costruita solo da risorse esterne...`, `; stesso percentile, estrazione del verificatore`), `unknown_is_not_negative.json`, `nrf2_probe_vs_development.json`, `ack1_conditional_on_protein.json`, `member_evidence.tsv`, `units.tsv`, `comparators.tsv`, `compound_view.tsv`. The shareable four: the work-order summary above, the electrophysiology feasibility note (`Elettrofisiologia su fette del knockout neurone-specifico...`), and the two `predictive_test.json` lines.
- **Smallest path for the owners.** These strings are scientific wording: translating them is not a harness change. The existing mechanism already covers it: authored Italian passages go through the content catalogue (`i18n --what collect` finds a passage that is rendered through `lx`/`lxi`, the translator adds it to `content_<lang>.json`). The pilot fields and the work-order `summary_it` are rendered with plain `E(...)`, so they bypass it: wrapping them in `lxi(...)` makes them show the `orig` marker (honest, immediately) and puts them in the inventory for translation; that is a code change of a few lines per renderer and a translation job of roughly 150 passages for the content owners. Nothing was done to them here.

## What the scan cannot see
Tooltips (`title`, `aria-label`, `placeholder` attributes: the footer label was fixed from the template read, not seen painted), text inside closed `<details>` (zero client rects: the pilot and the deepened tables are closed by default and were counted from the opened-by-script state only where the page opens them; the static test reads them from the HTML), text drawn in SVG, generated content (CSS `::before`), text that appears only after interaction (filters, language switch, modals), and print layouts. Detector N misses short labels (under 12 characters or fewer than three words: `Limiti dichiarati` would be missed alone; the static check below covers the catalogue ones) and can flag English proper-noun strings. The static guard is `italian_ui_strings_left` in `test_repurposing_view_i18n.py`: any Italian UI-catalogue value that is a whole text node of a translated page fails, for the five shareable pages and the full English and German builds.
