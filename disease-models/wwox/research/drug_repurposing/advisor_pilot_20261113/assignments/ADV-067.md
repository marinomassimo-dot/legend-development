question: A real bifurcation for the pharmacological comparison and for the proposed experiment: the sign of lithium's effect against provoked seizures in young rodents depends on regimen in the literature IT read (acute lithium raising the PTZ clonic threshold in one mouse family and pro-convulsant in a later paper of the same family; chronic threshold-raising; 'very weak' or no effect in non-kindled rats; a many-fold potentiation of pilocarpine-type seizures), while Cheng 2020 used repeated pretreatment within about an hour and reports lithium significant in all three genotypes. Decide with the sources what each regimen reading predicts and whether it changes the experiment: (1) reread, from full text where open (Cheng 2020 Methods; the Bahremand and Payandemehr family papers; the rat reports behind 'very weak'), what regimen, species, age, stimulus and endpoint each used, in a table that separates ACUTE single pretreatment, REPEATED pretreatment within hours, and CHRONIC dosing; (2) for each regimen, the predicted direction in wild-type, heterozygous and knockout pups on the in vivo brief's readings (general effect, GSK-3-specific effect, regimen artefact such as sedation or lithium toxicity near the therapeutic range from the label), and the outcome pattern that would separate them; (3) the decision: does the in vivo brief need two lithium regimens as a design variable (with which one replicating Cheng) or is one enough, and does the second GSK-3 inhibitor of another class become more or less necessary; (4) a stop criterion: stop after the table and the decision (no further lithium dossier unless the table shows a regimen with a clear sign flip); say what remains a measurement. Use the existing brief section 4 and the frozen priors as the frame; do not edit the priors file; a dated line in the in vivo brief only if the decision changes a statement.
