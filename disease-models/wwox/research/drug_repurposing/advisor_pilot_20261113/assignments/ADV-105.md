key: energy-supply-and-glial-state-after-onset-external-search
recipient: Science
question: ADV-101 and ADV-104 asked whether the metabolic and glial axes PRECEDE seizure onset and whether a manipulation moves seizures WITHOUT neuronal rescue, searched the held texts and seven title-only PubMed queries, and carried both as readouts. Two different questions were left unanswered by that: (A) does correcting brain energy supply or glial state AFTER onset contribute to MAINTENANCE of the phenotype or to FUNCTIONAL RECOVERY (a post-onset question; the neuronal AAV9 early window has no after-onset arm, so it cannot say); (B) which agents have juvenile or paediatric exposure data ('pup-enterable'): 'no sheet found' is not 'incompatible'. Do a TARGETED EXTERNAL reading (PubMed by plain client, then full text where open; abstract-only items are marked), not a re-run of any closed screen: (1) energy-supply interventions in neonatal and paediatric epilepsy where the lesion is not a neuronal-protein loss: GLUT1 deficiency and PDH deficiency (ketogenic diet, ketone esters, triheptanoin, PDK inhibition by DCA in congenital lactic acidosis), neonatal hypoglycaemia-seizure models: what was changed, at which age, with what effect on seizures or survival, whether the effect is shown AFTER onset, and the juvenile or paediatric exposure facts (blood ketones, brain or CSF concentration, label) for each agent; (2) glial-state manipulations after onset in developmental epilepsy or hypomyelination models (microglia depletion or modulation, astrocyte-targeted agents) with age and endpoint; (3) a coherence check with the held null-pup facts from ADV-101: hypoglycaemia from the second week with LOWER insulin and higher glucose uptake, glycolysis and lactate: say whether an energy-supply or PDH-axis intervention is coherent with that pattern or contradicted by it. Keep the distinction explicit in every row: exploratory evidence (anchored to its source, context, a competing explanation such as 'the energy state is a consequence of seizures' and the observation that would weaken the idea) versus promotion criteria (which stay unchanged and are not requirements for investigating). Output: one note with a table per question A and B, the pre-registered stop rule, and a one-paragraph answer to: should an energy-supply readout or arm be explored in the null pups, at what age (post-onset arm?), and what would weaken it.
why_now: The operator integration of 2026-11-20 asked that promotion criteria not become requirements for investigation and that 'not found in held sheets' not be read as 'no useful search'; ADV-101 Q3 and ADV-104 Q3 limited themselves to HE sheets and so could not see paediatric exposure data that exist for several of these agents in human disease.
