question: ADV-117 found a pup tolerability signal for ezogabine (neuronal apoptosis after repeated efficacy-range dosing at P7, Brown 2016); the same age window carries known developmental neurotoxicity signals for several classes that sit among our candidates (NMDAR blockers, GABAergic agents, vigabatrin, anticonvulsants in general), and the broad pilot doses from P3 to P16 and the bridge reads at P13 to P17. No place in the repository lines up, for EVERY candidate on the two objectives, what is reported about neonatal and juvenile neurotoxicity (apoptosis, myelination, growth, behaviour) at the ages and exposures that matter. Build ONE ledger (one table, no new register), one row per candidate (ethosuximide, the T-type compounds ulixacaltamide and TTA-A2, NMDAR pore blockers and d-APV, gaboxadol and ganaxolone, ezogabine and ICA-069673, vigabatrin, lithium, the GSK-3 inhibitors tideglusib and SB216763, clemastine, the thyromimetics), columns: species and age window tested, exposure statement as reported (no dose), the neurotoxicity or developmental endpoint, the effect size or 'not found by these routes', source id and depth (abstract or full text), one-origin flag, and the weakening observation. Use HE sheet_query first, then a targeted external reading with a stop rule fixed before numbers; 'not found' is not 'safe' and 'signal at one dose' is not 'incompatible'; each row keeps a competing explanation (dose, species, exposure schedule, confound). End with one paragraph: which candidates carry a signal inside the pilot windows, which carry none found, and whether the bridge preference or the broad arm list changes; plus the one measurement that would settle the most consequential unknown (for example a pup safety readout riding on the exposure step).
why_now: ADV-117 changed the reading of the Kv7 class through a tolerability signal that earlier class-level comparisons did not weigh; the weak preference for ethosuximide has no neonatal harm row at all; this is the cross-cutting comparison that can reorder candidates on both objectives and uses material that is mostly reachable by plain-client search.
