question: Two small units that turn the gaps found by ADV-053 and ADV-056 into questions someone can answer, both at mechanism and design level, no spend, aggregates only. (1) CLINICAL-CHANNEL QUESTION SET. Your vigabatrin dossier ended with items that cannot come from a literature search (start age, co-therapy timing and instrumented outcome of any chronic agent in children of the class; whether the pallidal and thalamic change in the two children was symptomatic or resolved; visual assessment in children with optic involvement exposed to any drug). Draft the SHORTEST neutral set of aggregate questions (three to six) that would change the comparison most, ordered by how much one answer would change it (a value-of-information ordering with the reason for each, linked to the endpoint cell it would change in the ADV-053 table), in a form a clinician or a registry could answer WITHOUT any individual-level record: counts only, bands, cells under 3 merged, no dates, no centre, no name, no free text about a person, and a first line that says nothing is medical advice and that no agent is recommended. State what would NOT be asked (anything identifying). It is a drafting job for the operator's use if a channel exists; it asks for nothing to be sent. Check the repository first for an existing registry-style or aggregate-collection proposal and reuse it. (2) DEVELOPMENT-ENDPOINT ARM FOR A NON-GENE AGENT. ADV-053 found that the development endpoint has only gene addition (U07, prevention in an early window; survival to about day 17, weight series, composite motor score, GFAP-positive cells, myelination) and that no non-gene agent has any development endpoint. Specify at design level (no dose, no schedule): which of U07's own endpoints could be measured in an early-window arm of a non-gene agent in the Wwox-null pup, in the SAME animals as the first in vivo experiment of ADV-051 or in a separate cohort, what a direct comparison with the AAV9 result would and would not allow (prevention against rescue, the high-dose row of the card, one laboratory, a ceiling or overexpression endpoint that a drug does not have), what controls and per-animal summaries it needs, and which agents of the ADV-051 arm list make sense for it and which do not (lithium and ethosuximide were given only before a provoking injection). State whether it should be a separate arm of the brief 13.11 first in vivo design (SCI owns the brief; give SCI the text, do not edit the brief yourself).
