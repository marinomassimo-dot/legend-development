# PREREG ADV-296 (HE, 2026-11-26): read-only check for "negative beside an unread citation"

Written before any scanner code and before any run. Source of the design: ADV message to HE on 2026-11-26 (assignment
`assignments/ADV-296.md` not yet on main when this was written; the prediction and reject rules below are copied from that message).

## Question
Can a mechanical read-only scan flag the sentences in the adv098 sheets, `*.card.json` and `*.packet.json` where a negative
("not found", "none found", "no ... by these routes", "not measured") stands in the same file within a few lines of a citation
the file itself says was read by title only or not read (PMID with "title read", "abstract not read", "titles read", "not read")
and the two share the subject?

## Known cases (the scan must flag both on the text as first written, repository state 75981649b = parent of the ADV-293 PREREG)
1. Batch 3: "Not measured or not found: ... free brain exposure" and the summary line beside PMID 17362924 ("title read; adult
   rat, abstract not read"); the abstract reports plasma and CNS concentrations by microdialysis.
2. Batch 10: "Not found by these routes: a measured brain or CSF concentration of tazemetostat given alone" beside PMID 32674144
   ("title read only; what it showed about exposure is not read"); the abstract reports the brainstem tumour at 3.74 percent of serum.

## Prediction (fixed now, from ADV's assignment)
The scan flags both known cases and at most 60 sentences over the whole scope; the first twenty flagged sentences, read against
the PubMed abstract of the cited record, show precision of at least 3 of 20 (a flagged sentence counts as a hit when the abstract
holds a statement that bears on the negative).

## Reject rules (fixed now)
Reject the scan if it misses either known case, or flags more than 200 sentences, or precision is under 3 of 20.

## Method (fixed now)
- Scope: `adv098_agent_sheets_he_20261119/BATCH*.md` at 75981649b (before any ADV-293 correction), plus
  `drug_repurposing/cards/*.card.json` and `*.packet.json` at the working tree.
- Unread-citation mention: a PMID within one sentence of a marker among "title read", "titles read", "title only", "abstract not
  read", "not read", "title truncated" (same sentence or the same parenthesis).
- Negative sentence: contains "not found", "none found", "no ... found", "not measured", "no ... figure", "not confirmed"
  or "by these routes".
- Link: negative sentence within 8 lines (sheets) or 3 sentences (JSON strings) of the unread mention and sharing at least two
  content words with the mention's own sentence (stop words and the generic words found, routes, read, title, abstract removed).
- Precision read: the first twenty flagged sentences in file order, each against the abstract of the cited PMID (one delegate,
  sonnet, effort high, abstracts fetched by me or from PubMed records; HE verifies each hit).
- No spend, no file outside this folder written, one delegate at a time.
