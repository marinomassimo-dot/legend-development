## Failure modes seen (for a v3, not applied)
1. Cross-compound windows: a PMID in the gaboxadol paragraph links to ganaxolone negatives eight lines up.
2. "The abstract gives no figure" about one record is linked to an unread citation about another.
3. Boilerplate (recipe sentences, "not found by these routes" as a convention) matches.
4. The check needs the link card -> sheet unit (the card's sentence provenance) to reach cards at all.
5. Word overlap alone misses the semantic gap (exposure / concentration / plasma); the topic classes are hand-made.

## What it is good for

sheets. It is not a gate and not a count of errors; each flagged sentence still needs the abstract read.
