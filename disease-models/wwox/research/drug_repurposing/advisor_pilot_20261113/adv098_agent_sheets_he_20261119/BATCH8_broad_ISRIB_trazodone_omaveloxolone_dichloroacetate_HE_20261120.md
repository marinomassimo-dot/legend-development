## COMPARABLE?
- **No pair is comparable:** four different mechanisms (an integrated-stress-response inhibitor, a serotonin receptor and transporter ligand, an NRF2 activator, a pyruvate dehydrogenase kinase inhibitor) and, for three of them, the ChEMBL rows do not measure the mechanism that makes them a candidate (below).
- **Dichloroacetate across PDK isozymes: comparable within one document.** One 2017 document (CHEMBL4007440, ELISA with full-length His6-PDHA1 as substrate, 30 minutes) gives IC50 PDK1 1.03 mM, PDK2 0.50 mM, PDK3 5.56 mM, PDK4 0.73 mM: an 11-fold range across the four isozymes, four assays of the same design. ChEMBL marks all four values "Outside typical range". **Corrected 2026-11-25 (HE):** the first version left out the PDK4 row (activity 18040568), which the held answer already contained, and said "one assay"; SCI's ADV-283 reading found PDK4 missing from the line. PDK3 is the least inhibited of the four. This is no ground for treating PDK3 as the relevant isoform in this genotype class (`../brainstorm_20261116/adv283_pdk_isoform_second_dataset_sci_20261125/RESULT_ADV283_SCI_20261125.md`). **The order and the size of these values are that document's only (corrected 2026-11-25, HE; see the dated line at the end):** CHEMBL4177695 (2017, GST-tagged PDK from Sf9 cells) reads PDK1 5.1 mM and PDK2 9.0 mM, PDK1 and PDK2 in the reverse order; CHEMBL6121009 (2025, radiometric) reads PDK1 3.66 mM, PDK2 and PDK3 above 5 mM and PDK4 0.108 mM; two 2023 rows give PDK1 only as bounds (above 0.1 mM; EC50 above 0.05 mM). "PDK3 is the least inhibited" is therefore a statement about CHEMBL4007440. Another 1999 row gives IC50 above 2 mM for PDK in vitro and an EC50 of 100 microM in normal human dermal fibroblasts: a different readout; no order between the two documents is read.
- **Trazodone across sources:** 5-HT2A Ki 5.6 nM and IC50 20 nM (DrugMatrix, [3H]ketanserin) against IC50 243 nM in an HTRF cAMP assay (2020): a 12-fold spread by assay type (14-fold when the rat receptor row, IC50 17 nM, 1999, is counted (corrected 2026-11-25, HE; see the dated line at the end)). 5-HT1A Ki 118 nM (human frontal cortex, 8-OH-DPAT displacement, 2022) against EC50 785 nM (FLIPR agonist assay, 2020): binding and function are different quantities.

## What changes for SCI's unit C and the proteostasis screen
1. For the three mechanism-led candidates (ISRIB, omaveloxolone, trazodone-as-ISR) the ChEMBL rows do **not** measure the mechanism that makes them candidates: ISRIB has one cell-reporter row, omaveloxolone none on KEAP1 or NRF2, trazodone none on the ISR. 3. Pediatric label limits are tight: omaveloxolone from 16 years, trazodone not established with a boxed warning, dichloroacetate and ISRIB no label.
