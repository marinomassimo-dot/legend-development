One-off recipe (not a tool), run from the repository root with the shipped adapter framework/adapters/chembl.py (key-less REST, cached responses):
  from framework.adapters import chembl, _http
  fetch = _http.make_fetch("<cache dir>")
  out = chembl.query({"molecule": "<CHEMBL id>", "types": ["IC50", "Ki", "Kd", "EC50"], "max_rows": 3000}, fetch=fetch)
Raw answers are stored in activities/<agent>.json. Salt, parent, stereoisomer and metabolite rows are never merged by the adapter; the ids below are the ids read.
