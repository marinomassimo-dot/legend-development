# ADV-098 batch 9: GSK-3 inhibition with non-lithium chemistry (HE, 2026-11-20)

Written for: SCI (the GSK-3 comparison, unit C), ADV, IT. Same rules, routes and recipe as batches 1 to 8: public sources only, research concentrations apart from any patient dosing, no dose, hypothesis level, not medical advice, "not found by these routes" is not absence. Raw ChEMBL answers outside git with digests in `SHA256SUMS_raw_activities.txt`. **Identity by structure for each agent:** the PubChem name lookup returns a structure and an InChIKey; the ChEMBL molecule named in its synonym list carries the **same InChIKey** in all five cases: tideglusib CHEMBL3545157, SB-216763 CHEMBL102714, CHIR-99021 CHEMBL412142 (ChEMBL calls it LADUVIGLUSIB, max phase 1), LY2090314 CHEMBL362558, AR-A014418 CHEMBL259850. Lithium for reference: see batch 1 (no ChEMBL activity row; no GSK3B value by these routes).

## COMPARABLE?
- **GSK-3 alpha against beta: comparable only within one document and one assay.** CHIR-99021: 10 against 7 nM (2004), 7.8 against 4.9 nM (2017, same biotin-CREB peptide assay), Ki 10 against 3.98 nM (the "Navigating the Kinome" panel): alpha about 1.4- to 2.5-fold weaker than beta. LY2090314: 1.5 against 0.9 nM (2013) and 1.5 against 0.9 nM (2019): 1.7-fold. AR-A014418: Ki 25.1 nM for both (the same kinome panel: no difference); a 2011 document gives 70 microM (alpha) against 330 microM (beta) in a different, much weaker FRET assay, which is a different assay and not a contrast. SB-216763: alpha 34 nM and an alpha/beta combined 75 nM in one 2004 document; no separate beta value in that document. **Tideglusib: beta only** (no alpha row by IC50). So the five agents are, at most, about 1.4- to 2.5-fold beta-preferring or not distinguished; none is shown to separate alpha from beta by an order of magnitude.
- **Across agents: not set in one order.** Beta IC50 values lie from 0.7 to 1.1 nM (LY2090314), 3 to 30 nM (CHIR-99021), 7 to 92 nM (SB-216763), 38 to 104 nM Ki and IC50 (AR-A014418) and 5 nM to 2 microM (tideglusib, a spread of 400-fold across documents), in different assays (peptide substrate, ATP concentration, pre-incubation, cell lysate). Only within a document are two agents set side by side, and no document here measures the five together. **No ordering of agents is read from these values.**
- **Tideglusib's mode matters for comparability.** The 2015 row reads "non-competitive inhibition of human GSK-3beta in the presence of ATP" (IC50 60 nM), and a 2021 document gives 139 nM with a 3-hour pre-incubation against 1.78 microM without it: a time-dependent, non-ATP-competitive inhibition, so its IC50 depends on pre-incubation and is not set against the ATP-competitive agents' IC50 values at one ATP concentration. Several documents give 60 nM for tideglusib (CHEMBL3734740, CHEMBL5096147, CHEMBL6153053): the same number from three documents is likely one origin quoted forward.
- **One discrepancy to flag, not used:** a 2015 document (CHEMBL5653620) gives LY2090314 on GSK-3 as 1,500 nM (alpha) and 900 nM (beta), exactly 1,000 times the values in other documents (1.5 and 0.9 nM); the ratio between alpha and beta is the same, so this is probably a unit or curation difference, not a measurement; not used.

## Kinase selectivity as measured (the rows, not a conclusion)
- **CHIR-99021:** in the PubChem "Navigating the Kinome" set (document CHEMBL1201862) 124 Ki rows on 9 targets named by gene symbol; the only targets within 10-fold of the GSK3B Ki (3.98 nM) are GSK3A (10 nM). The kinases outside GSK-3 in that set carry Ki values of 6 to 10 microM in the rows I read (for example MRCK alpha 10 microM, CAMK2G 6.3 microM, CSNK1G2 7.9 microM). The panel is not the whole kinome.
- **AR-A014418:** 79 rows on 6 targets in the same set; only GSK3A and GSK3B (Ki 25.1 nM each) are within 10-fold.
- **LY2090314:** 80 rows on 24 targets in the same set, **with no GSK3B Ki in that document**, so no within-document selectivity ratio can be formed there. Its other rows are the GSK-3 documents above.
- **SB-216763 and CHIR-99021, document CHEMBL1201861:** this is the **Sanger cell-line viability screen** (IC50 against more than 550 cell lines), **not a kinase panel**; it says nothing about kinase selectivity and is excluded.
- **Tideglusib, Kinobeads (Klaeger 2017 Science, "The target landscape of clinical kinase drugs", 290 rows, CHEMBL3991601):** Kd above 30 microM for GSK3A and GSK3B: ATP-competitive kinobeads cannot capture a non-ATP-competitive or irreversible ligand, so this panel is **uninformative for tideglusib's own target**, and its off-target rows are not read as selectivity.
- `selectivity` stays `NOT_ASSESSED` for all five by design; "reported against" is not "selective for".

## Rodent exposure (PubMed, two routes each; titles read, nothing beyond)
- **None of the five has a brain or plasma concentration found by these routes.** Tideglusib: specific queries ("brain concentration mice rats", "pharmacokinetics brain distribution") 0 records; the broad queries return in vivo efficacy papers by title, for example "Tideglusib Ameliorates Ischemia/Reperfusion Damage by Inhibiting GSK-3beta and Apoptosis in Rat Model of Ischemic Stroke" (PMID 35152130, 2022), "Tideglusib improves novel object recognition memory in the preclinical DBA/2J mdx mouse model of Duchenne muscular dystrophy" (PMID 42100730, 2026), "NP031112, a thiadiazolidinone compound, prevents inflammation and neurodegeneration under excitotoxic conditions" (PMID 17522320, 2007). A CNS effect is reported; an exposure was not found.
- **SB-216763:** "Efficacy of small-molecule glycogen synthase kinase-3 inhibitors in the postnatal rat model of tau hyperphosphorylation" (PMID 17906685, 2007): a **juvenile (postnatal) rat** in vivo study by title; exposure not read. **CHIR-99021:** in vivo mouse titles (intrahippocampal kainate epileptogenesis, PMID 38522659, 2024; a Parkinson's neuroinflammation model, PMID 42486828, 2026): efficacy, exposure not read. **LY2090314:** methamphetamine-induced stereotypy in mice (PMID 41017565, 2025): a CNS pharmacodynamic effect by title. **AR-A014418:** morphine-induced locomotor activation (PMID 36907972, 2023). All by title; none read.
- **Not found by these routes:** a free or total brain concentration, a juvenile concentration, for any of the five.

## Label and trial status of tideglusib (checked, not assumed)
- **Label:** openFDA returns NO_MATCH_FOUND for tideglusib: no US label found by this route (not a statement about development status elsewhere).
- **Trials (ClinicalTrials.gov API, status fields only, results not read; 10 studies returned):**
  - Congenital myotonic dystrophy: NCT03692312, phase 2/3, ages 6 to 16 years, **completed**; NCT05004129, phase 2/3, ages 6 to 45 years, **recruiting**; an expanded-access record NCT07119775, **available**.
  - Myotonic dystrophy type 1, adolescent and adult: NCT02858908, phase 2, ages 12 to 45 years, completed.
  - Autism spectrum disorders: NCT02586935, phase 2, ages 12 to 17 years, completed.
  - Alzheimer's disease: NCT00948259 (phase 1/2, 60 to 85 years, completed) and NCT01350362 (phase 2, 50 to 85 years, completed).
  - Progressive supranuclear palsy: NCT01049399, completed.
  - Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis: NCT05105958, phase 2, **not yet recruiting**; arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy: NCT06174220, phase 2, recruiting.
  So **paediatric programmes exist** (from age 6 years, for congenital myotonic dystrophy and a trial in adolescents with autism); no result or efficacy statement is read from the status fields. The registry says nothing about any WWOX-related disorder.
- **The others:** SB-216763, AR-A014418 and LY2090314 are research compounds; CHIR-99021 is a research reagent with a ChEMBL max phase of 1 under another name (laduviglusib); no label looked for.

## What is not found by these routes
Beta-selective separation from alpha by an order of magnitude for any agent; a kinome-wide selectivity panel that includes GSK-3 for tideglusib (the only panel read cannot see its mechanism); any measured exposure in brain or plasma in rodents for the five; a juvenile exposure; a harm profile (kept for SCI's comparison cell: the trial registry lists studies and statuses, not adverse events).

## What changes for SCI's GSK-3 comparison
1. The five agents are all ATP-competitive or non-ATP-competitive GSK-3 inhibitors with low-nanomolar to sub-micromolar potency in vitro and **no measured exposure**; the lithium reference has no measured GSK-3 row here at all.
2. Tideglusib has the only documented paediatric clinical programme (congenital myotonic dystrophy, from 6 years); this is an indication-specific status, not a signal for another disorder, and its potency is time- and assay-dependent.
3. Alpha against beta: at most 2.5-fold in the same-document pairs; so "GSK-3 beta selective" is not a property these data support for any of them.
