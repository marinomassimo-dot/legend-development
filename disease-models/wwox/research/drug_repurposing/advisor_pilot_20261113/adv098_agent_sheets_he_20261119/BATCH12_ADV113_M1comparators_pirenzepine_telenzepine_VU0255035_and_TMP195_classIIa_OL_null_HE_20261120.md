## Answer first
- **Part A (IT50-Q09): no disclosed-structure M1-selective antagonist has a measured EBP datum by the routes searched, and the one primary datum that exists is indirect and does not name its compounds in the text layer.** ChEMBL holds 334 activity rows on the four EBP targets (264 molecules) and none is for pirenzepine, telenzepine, VU0255035, clemastine or benztropine; Europe PMC abstract searches pairing EBP, emopamil binding protein or sterol isomerase with pirenzepine, telenzepine or "M1 antagonist" returned 0 records. The only primary datum is Hubler 2018 (PMID 30046109, held full text): of a panel of six muscarinic antagonists that all inhibited M1, M3 and M5 at the 2 microM screening dose, **only clemastine and benztropine inhibited EBP in OPCs**; four of the six (the ones that did not inhibit EBP) are named only in an Extended Data figure that the text layer does not carry; clemastine and benztropine are named in the text (corrected 2026-11-26, HE, ADV-293; see the dated line at the end). So the pair is separated from the muscarinic route **inside Hubler's own panel by four non-EBP muscarinic antagonists whose identity I could not read**; pirenzepine and telenzepine are two of five muscarinic reagents named in the Methods (INFERENCE that they are among the four). The design therefore cannot rest on "the M1-selective comparator has no EBP activity" as an assumption: it needs a measured sterol arm for the comparator chosen.
- **Part B (WO-32): a bounded null, with two routes and the second one now run.** No record found by Europe PMC (SCI, 41 title-and-abstract records, 2026-11-18; HE census, 2026-11-20) or by a PubMed-native title/abstract search (HE, 2026-11-20) reports a loss-of-function, gain-of-function or class IIa-inhibitor result in an oligodendrocyte-lineage cell with a differentiation or myelin readout. The class IIa myelin results with a function readout are Schwann-cell (peripheral). It is a bounded null of abstract-level searches, **not a verified absence** (section "What it cannot say").

### What it cannot say
- **It cannot say class IIa inhibition is harmless or inert in oligodendrocytes.** No oligodendrocyte-lineage experiment was found; absence of a result is not a result of no effect.
- **Abstract level only.** Class IIa genes can be studied in a Hdac4 conditional knockout whose abstract names neither oligodendrocytes nor myelin, or in a table; full-text matches were screened only by title (SCI's 692 Tier 2 titles, 107 advanced and read at abstract level); IT's census counts of 278 (HDAC9) and 215 (HDAC7) full-text-wide records were never opened and are not reconciled with SCI's counts (different query strings).
- **TMP195 exposure in the central nervous system is not known from these routes**: no brain or plasma concentration was found (batch 10), and the one in vivo brain-disease paper (42332767) has no exposure figure in its abstract.
- **Two further routes not run** (Google Scholar, OpenAlex full text): the rule that an absence claim needs two routes is met only for the abstract-level searches (Europe PMC plus PubMed), not for full text.
- **Species and age:** the stroke work is adult rat, the lineage RNA is mouse P17 sorted classes and human adult cortex groups, the Schwann work is mouse and rat.
