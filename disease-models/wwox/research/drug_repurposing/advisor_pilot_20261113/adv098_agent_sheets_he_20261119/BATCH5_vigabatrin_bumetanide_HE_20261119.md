## COMPARABLE?
- **Vigabatrin against bumetanide: not comparable.** One inactivates an enzyme (GABA aminotransferase, irreversible), the other inhibits a transporter (NKCC1): different targets, different quantities, no common axis.
- **Vigabatrin across its own rows: not one potency.** It is an irreversible inactivator, so an IC50 depends on the pre-incubation time and a Ki is an inactivation constant. Rows: Ki 0.85 mM (human, 2002), 3.2 mM (human, two documents, 2012 and 2019), 3.2 mM and 0.29 mM (pig brain, 2003 and 2020), 10 mM (rat, two documents, 2012); IC50 41 microM (rat brain homogenate, 2013) and 39.7 microM (mouse brain homogenate, 30 minutes of pre-incubation, 2019). A Ki of millimolar and an IC50 of tens of microM describe different experiments; the species and the pre-incubation decide. No order across species is read.
- **Bumetanide NKCC1: one value only.** IC50 1.54 microM for NKCC1 in human HT-29 cells (rubidium uptake, 20 minutes, atomic absorption, document CHEMBL5230131, 2020). No row for NKCC2 (SLC12A1), none on a neuronal or rodent preparation, none in a second laboratory: not found by these routes. A selectivity for NKCC1 over NKCC2 therefore cannot be read from these sources.

## Vigabatrin (ChEMBL CHEMBL89598, NAME_EXACT; CHEMBL96 listed by the identity answer, not merged and not pulled)
- **Targets and potency:** see the rows above (GABA aminotransferase, ABAT: human, pig, rat and mouse). 15 rows; 9 on ABAT in vitro (3 human, 3 rat, 2 pig, 1 mouse). The six other rows are four transporter panel rows (BSEP, MRP2, MRP3, MRP4: IC50 above 133 microM) and two of a SARS-CoV-2 cytotoxicity screen (above 20 microM): no activity at the concentrations tested (corrected 2026-11-25, HE; see the dated line at the end).
- **Selectivity / off-targets:** `NOT_ASSESSED`; nothing in these rows contradicts a selective enzyme target; the clinically known retinal effects are not in these rows (see the label line).
- **Rodent exposure (PubMed):** preclinical tissue distribution and metabolic correlations (PMID 30631446, 2019, title read: "Preclinical tissue distribution and metabolic correlations of vigabatrin, an antiepileptic drug associated with pot[ential retinal toxicity]"); preferential accumulation of the active S-(+) isomer in murine retina (PMID 33385945, 2021, title read); rat pharmacokinetics with melatonin (PMID 39884559, 2025, title read). Brain concentration papers: the search "vigabatrin brain concentration rat" returned 85 records, the first six being pharmacology and method papers (not read). **Not found by these routes (not read):** a juvenile exposure; a free brain concentration. A pharmacodynamic readout (brain GABA) is the usual measure and is not an exposure.
- **Label (openFDA SPL, 3 labels, newest 2024-11-18):** adjunctive treatment of refractory complex partial seizures from age 2 years (and, in other labels of the same agent, infantile spasms: not read here); the boxed warning states **permanent bilateral concentric visual field constriction** (and possible central retinal damage). No dose given here.
- **Not measured or not found:** juvenile exposure; a measured effect on ABAT at a stated brain concentration; any in vivo potency in the rows.

## Bumetanide (ChEMBL CHEMBL1072, NAME_EXACT)
- **Targets and potency (28 rows):** NKCC1 (SLC12A2) IC50 1.54 microM (above). Off-target-type rows: carbonic anhydrase II Ki 6.98 microM and carbonic anhydrase III Ki 3.4 mM (human recombinant, 2024); organic anion transporter (rat Slc22a6, PAH uptake in oocytes) Ki 5.5 microM; aquaporin-4 IC50 100 microM (rat, oocytes); BSEP IC50 526 microM in one assay and above 133 and 135 microM in two others (vesicle assays, 2010 to 2013); 16 antiparasitic rows (corrected 2026-11-25, HE; see the dated line at the end) with no value (a 2010 screen); SARS-CoV-2 screen above 20 microM.
- **Selectivity:** `NOT_ASSESSED`. NKCC2: not found by these routes. The CA II value (6.98 microM) lies within a factor of 5 of the NKCC1 value from a different assay: not an identified contrast.
- **Rodent exposure (PubMed, titles read):** a literature on brain penetration exists: "Multiple blood-brain barrier transport mechanisms limit bumetanide accumulation, and therapeutic potential, in the brain" (PMID 28192112, 2017); "Consequences of inhibition of bumetanide metabolism in rodents on brain penetration and effects of bumetanide in chronic models of epilepsy" (PMID 24251546, 2014); probenecid raising bumetanide brain concentrations (PMID 25449033 and 25490899, 2015); "In vivo effects of bumetanide at brain concentrations incompatible with NKCC1 inhibition on newborn DGC structure and function in mice" (PMID 25463517, 2015); a pharmacokinetic-pharmacodynamic model for brain-permeant NKCC1 inhibitors (PMID 33279441, 2021). **By titles only**, brain concentrations are limited and described as insufficient for NKCC1 inhibition in some settings; the numbers were **not read**. Neonatal rats appear in the neonatal-brain searches (PMID 33711029, 2021 and others, titles only). A juvenile free brain concentration: not confirmed by these routes.
- **Label (openFDA SPL, 3 labels, newest 2025-01-08):** indicated for edema associated with heart, hepatic and renal disease in adults; "safety and effectiveness in pediatric patients below the age of 18 have not been established"; the label notes a potent displacement of bilirubin in vitro with sera from critically ill neonates. A boxed warning on profound diuresis exists. No dose given here.
- **Not measured or not found:** NKCC2 and NKCC1 values in one assay; neuronal NKCC1 potency; a juvenile free brain concentration (the numbers behind the titles above).

## What changes for SCI's exposure-effect choice (batch 5)
1. For vigabatrin the in vitro numbers cannot be turned into a potency at the brain without the pre-incubation and species stated; the reliable axis is the pharmacodynamic one (GABA-T inactivation), and a label with a boxed vision-loss warning is a harm cell by itself.
2. For bumetanide there is exactly one NKCC1 value and a body of literature saying brain exposure is the limit; the numbers sit behind titles that I did not read, so the exposure side of any mapping must come from SCI's delegate reading them.
