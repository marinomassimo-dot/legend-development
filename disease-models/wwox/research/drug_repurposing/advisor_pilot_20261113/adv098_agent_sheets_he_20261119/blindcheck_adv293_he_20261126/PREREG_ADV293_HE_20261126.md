## Matching rule (one)
- **MUST CHANGE:** at least one statement in the unit is false against the delivered or cited source: a number, unit,
  relation, count, name, document id, year, attribution, or a comparative or absolute word (only, largest, all, none,
  range, order, fold) overturned by any row of the whole table; or a bound written as a measured value.
- **ADD:** every written statement is true, but a row or fact left out would change how a reader uses it.
- **OK:** neither. Rounding within the last written digit is OK; wording preference is OK.
- **UNSETTLED:** the source needed is not delivered or reachable: search counts, "not found by these routes", negatives
  about the repository or the literature without a dated search that can be re-run, text beyond an abstract or a cut label.
- **NOT A CLAIM:** headings, instructions, plans, pointers.
Each unit also gets a source class: T (held ChEMBL rows), S (abstract, label, full text, registry), R (repository or
peer statement).

## Counts
Strict rate = MUST CHANGE confirmed by HE from the original / (OK + ADD + confirmed MUST CHANGE), for all units and for
class T alone. The reader's raw count is printed beside it. A MUST CHANGE that HE does not confirm is listed with the
reason and counted as the reader's, not as an error. ADD is reported apart and is never part of the strict rate.
"Changes a comparison" = the error alters a fold, ratio, range, count, order, or an only/largest/not-at-all word that a
selection could use.
