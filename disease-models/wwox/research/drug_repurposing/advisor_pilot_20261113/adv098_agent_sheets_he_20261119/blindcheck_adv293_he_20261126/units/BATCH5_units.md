## COMPARABLE?
U5.1 (L6) **Vigabatrin against bumetanide: not comparable.**
U5.2 (L6) One inactivates an enzyme (GABA aminotransferase, irreversible), the other inhibits a transporter (NKCC1): different targets, different quantities, no common axis.
U5.3 (L7) **Vigabatrin across its own rows: not one potency.**
U5.4 (L7) It is an irreversible inactivator, so an IC50 depends on the pre-incubation time and a Ki is an inactivation constant.
U5.5 (L7) Rows: Ki 0.85 mM (human, 2002), 3.2 mM (human, two documents, 2012 and 2019), 3.2 mM and 0.29 mM (pig brain, 2003 and 2020), 10 mM (rat, two documents, 2012); IC50 41 microM (rat brain homogenate, 2013) and 39.7 microM (mouse brain homogenate, 30 minutes of pre-incubation, 2019).
U5.6 (L7) A Ki of millimolar and an IC50 of tens of microM describe different experiments; the species and the pre-incubation decide.
U5.7 (L7) No order across species is read.
U5.8 (L8) **Bumetanide NKCC1: one value only.**
U5.9 (L8) IC50 1.54 microM for NKCC1 in human HT-29 cells (rubidium uptake, 20 minutes, atomic absorption, document CHEMBL5230131, 2020).
U5.10 (L8) No row for NKCC2 (SLC12A1), none on a neuronal or rodent preparation, none in a second laboratory: not found by these routes.
U5.11 (L8) A selectivity for NKCC1 over NKCC2 therefore cannot be read from these sources.

## Vigabatrin (ChEMBL CHEMBL89598, NAME_EXACT; CHEMBL96 listed by the identity answer, not merged and not pulled)

U5.13 (L11) 15 rows; 9 on ABAT in vitro (3 human, 3 rat, 2 pig, 1 mouse).
U5.14 (L11) The six other rows are four transporter panel rows (BSEP, MRP2, MRP3, MRP4: IC50 above 133 microM) and two of a SARS-CoV-2 cytotoxicity screen (above 20 microM): no activity at the concentrations tested.

U5.17 (L13) Brain concentration papers: the search "vigabatrin brain concentration rat" returned 85 records, the first six being pharmacology and method papers (not read).
U5.18 (L13) **Not found by these routes (not read):** a juvenile exposure; a free brain concentration.
U5.19 (L13) A pharmacodynamic readout (brain GABA) is the usual measure and is not an exposure.
U5.20 (L14) **Label (openFDA SPL, 3 labels, newest 2024-11-18):** adjunctive treatment of refractory complex partial seizures from age 2 years (and, in other labels of the same agent, infantile spasms: not read here); the boxed warning states **permanent bilateral concentric visual field constriction** (and possible central retinal damage).

## Bumetanide (ChEMBL CHEMBL1072, NAME_EXACT)
U5.22 (L18) **Targets and potency (28 rows):** NKCC1 (SLC12A2) IC50 1.54 microM (above).
U5.23 (L18) Off-target-type rows: carbonic anhydrase II Ki 6.98 microM and carbonic anhydrase III Ki 3.4 mM (human recombinant, 2024); organic anion transporter (rat Slc22a6, PAH uptake in oocytes) Ki 5.5 microM; aquaporin-4 IC50 100 microM (rat, oocytes); BSEP IC50 526 microM in one assay and above 133 and 135 microM in two others (vesicle assays, 2010 to 2013); 16 antiparasitic rows with no value (a 2010 screen); SARS-CoV-2 screen above 20 microM.
U5.24 (L19) **Selectivity:** `NOT_ASSESSED`.
U5.25 (L19) NKCC2: not found by these routes.
U5.26 (L19) The CA II value (6.98 microM) lies within a factor of 5 of the NKCC1 value from a different assay: not an identified contrast.
U5.27 (L20) **Rodent exposure (PubMed, titles read):** a literature on brain penetration exists: "Multiple blood-brain barrier transport mechanisms limit bumetanide accumulation, and therapeutic potential, in the brain" (PMID 28192112, 2017); "Consequences of inhibition of bumetanide metabolism in rodents on brain penetration and effects of bumetanide in chronic models of epilepsy" (PMID 24251546, 2014); probenecid raising bumetanide brain concentrations (PMID 25449033 and 25490899, 2015); "In vivo effects of bumetanide at brain concentrations incompatible with NKCC1 inhibition on newborn DGC structure and function in mice" (PMID 25463517, 2015); a pharmacokinetic-pharmacodynamic model for brain-permeant NKCC1 inhibitors (PMID 33279441, 2021).
U5.28 (L20) **By titles only**, brain concentrations are limited and described as insufficient for NKCC1 inhibition in some settings; the numbers were **not read**.
U5.29 (L20) Neonatal rats appear in the neonatal-brain searches (PMID 33711029, 2021 and others, titles only).
U5.30 (L20) A juvenile free brain concentration: not confirmed by these routes.
U5.31 (L21) **Label (openFDA SPL, 3 labels, newest 2025-01-08):** indicated for edema associated with heart, hepatic and renal disease in adults; "safety and effectiveness in pediatric patients below the age of 18 have not been established"; the label notes a potent displacement of bilirubin in vitro with sera from critically ill neonates.
U5.32 (L21) A boxed warning on profound diuresis exists.
U5.33 (L22) **Not measured or not found:** NKCC2 and NKCC1 values in one assay; neuronal NKCC1 potency; a juvenile free brain concentration (the numbers behind the titles above).
