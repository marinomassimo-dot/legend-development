4. **A conditional contrast (IPOTESI, unresolved premise stated, nothing measured here):** *If* WWOX status gates JNK-inhibitor-induced cell death in the way the HCC pair suggests, then in a WWOX-null neural background a JNK inhibitor should reduce a stress readout (tau phosphorylation, c-Jun phosphorylation) **without** the apoptosis-sensitising effect seen where WWOX is present; the two readouts would dissociate. Unresolved premise: whether the HCC pair is a WWOX effect at all (two different lines; one chemotype), and whether JNK is the target (off-target kinase activity of the compound class). Competing explanations: (i) line identity, not WWOX status; (ii) off-target kinase inhibition; (iii) WWOX-JNK1 direct interaction (the paper's mechanism line); (iv) a stress-pathway state difference rather than WWOX itself. Transfer limit: hepatocellular carcinoma lines, not neurons; JNK is pro- or anti-apoptotic by cell type; no in vivo datum; nothing about a compound being usable.
5. **The rejecting experiment (design only):** an isogenic WWOX-null versus rescued pair in one background (a neural line is the relevant one), JNK inhibitors of two or three different chemotypes plus a genetic JNK1/2 control, readouts apoptosis AND phospho-c-Jun AND tau phosphorylation. It rejects the HCC-derived pattern if the null shows no inhibitor-sensitivity difference once the second chemotype and the genetic control are in; it rejects the dissociation if the stress readout fell only together with apoptosis. P-a and P-b of `prediction_frozen.md` are the stated bets.
6. **A supported abstention is still possible:** if the body shows no WWOX-status contrast beyond the abstract, or the gain and loss arms are not comparable, the case closes as "the paper does not carry the contrast" and the lens moves to the next item (P325 as companion, P313).
