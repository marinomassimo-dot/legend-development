AGREEMENT: no combination is split; bumetanide+phenobarbital, vigabatrin-versus-hormones, class rows (NMDA blockade, "statins", RXR antagonists) all stay in the panel file. No class transfer in the code; the stage join is by base name only. Package letters keep vigabatrin D and E, and retigabine D and E, apart (different endpoints), so no merge across incompatible endpoints between packages. "undetermined" is never turned into a negative (it is simply ignored in the flag logic).

DISAGREEMENTS / OVERSTATEMENTS
1. The panel regex is applied to the raw intervention string including parentheses, so a comma, "agonist/inhibitor/activator", "versus", or a leading digit sends single compounds to the panel. 12 single-compound rows are wrongly "unattributed": R44 (9-cis retinoic acid, leading digit), R52 (neostigmine, word "inhibitor"), B20 (MDL29,951), B30 (GC-1, "agonist"), D15 (clobazam, comma), D20 and D21 (OV350, comma/"activator"), D24 (NBI-921352, "inhibitor"), E26 E27 E28 (clonazepam, comma in the parenthesis), E37 (pynegabine). Consequences for flags: clonazepam (E26 favourable + E28 context_dependent) and OV350 (D20 favourable in pups, D21 context_dependent in adults) would be flagged and are MISSED. The README/GAIN claim that panel rows are "combinations or several compounds" is therefore false for these 12 rows; the stated reason text in panel_rows_unattributed ("combination or several compounds in one row") is wrong for them.
2. CQ-IT30-01 (benztropine) and CQ-IT30-03 (clemastine): the "not reproduced across contexts" wording rests only on a context_dependent row each (R05: faster remyelination at week 2, no difference later because spontaneous repair is complete; R19: lower EAE score and more preserved myelin, but authors say small molecules cannot prove remyelination). Neither is a null or an adverse result. The question text ("contexts that gave a benefit and the ones that did not") states more than the rows support; benztropine R05 actually had a benefit (acceleration, not extent). The column `directions` shows this honestly, but the question sentence does not.
3. CQ-IT30-04 (rapamycin): the question merges three endpoints (E29 spasm frequency, E30/E31 learning, E32 other seizure types) in one sentence and asks "which of these contexts is the reference genotype's", but one of the contexts (E31) is healthy control pups, which is a safety/off-model row and not a candidate context. The context strings are also cryptic ("adverse: rat | no model") because system is truncated at the first comma. The flip is real in the rows; its framing is loose.
4. CQ-IT30-05 (vigabatrin): the flip D03 vs D25 is real, but the most relevant vigabatrin rows are in the panel and absent from the question: D06 (null within a pharmacoresistance panel), E39-E41 (infantile spasms trials) and E42 (adverse development in two WWOX-null children, CLAIM031). The question therefore omits the only WWOX-genotype vigabatrin observation.
5. CQ-IT30-02 (bumetanide): built from E01/E03/E05 only; all combination rows (favourable E02/E04, human E07-E10) are excluded by design. Defensible (compound_only logic) but the question reads as if bumetanide had three contexts in total. Attribution shown as "mixed" (E01 is target_controlled), which is correct.
6. Design limit not stated: grouping by (compound, package) hides cross-package contrasts (retigabine favourable on iPSC discharge in D22 versus adverse/null on adult behaviour after neonatal exposure in E24/E25; vigabatrin D vs E17). Not an error (endpoints differ) but "flags a sign that flips with context" is narrower than it sounds.

## 2. Bumetanide card F03 (cards/F03_bumetanide_chloride_gradient.packet.json)

Added in commit cd69d3cd: evidence_against[5], [6], [7] and not_known[3].
- [5] Claims: recurrent seizures raise intracellular chloride and bumetanide limits it (20810895, partial) = E03, depth partial_fulltext, CONFIRMED. Bumetanide alone little/no efficacy in hypoxia (23536761, partial) = E05, CONFIRMED. Two later asphyxia studies no potentiation (33258158, 33955541, abstracts) = E11, E13 abstract_only, CONFIRMED depth. Row list "E03 E05 E07 E09 E11 E13" matches the cited statements (E08 and E10 are used in [7] but not listed in the header of [5]).
- Wording problem: "even at higher exposure" is attached to both asphyxia studies. Only E13 (33955541) is the higher-exposure row; E11 (33258158) is "clinically relevant exposure".
- Selectivity: the same sources hold favourable rows that are not on the card: E04 (23536761, bumetanide plus phenobarbital cut seizure incidence about 75%), E01 (16227993, target-controlled slice effect), E02, and E14/E15 (33955541: bumepamine, which does not inhibit NKCC1, did potentiate phenobarbital). The card says "state- and model-dependent, not a general property of the target"; that is an interpretive summary, supportable by E03/E05/E07/E11 but it is told from the against side only and drops that the NKCC1-inhibitor-free derivative worked. For an evidence_against field this is acceptable if labelled; the label does say IT30 non-WWOX.
- [6] E07 (25765333, abstract only: stopped early, limited seizure reduction, serious adverse reactions) CONFIRMED (the card drops "serious"). E09 (33201535, partial): row says "post hoc, adjusted for baseline imbalance, not powered". The card says "only after post-hoc adjustment", which is slightly stronger than the row (the row does not say the effect appeared only after adjustment). The source's own quoted conclusion (additional reduction attributable to bumetanide, no increase in serious adverse effects) is not shown. "Two results disagree; neither addresses Wwox-deficient lesion" CONFIRMED. Human result is labelled "not a genetic lesion", so it is NOT presented as WWOX evidence.
- [7] Hearing: E08 3 of 11 survivors (abstract), E10 2/26 vs 0/13 (partial). Card hedges ("confounded", "unknown whether attributable, not a safety conclusion either way"). No safety conclusion is drawn: CONFIRMED. Minor: the aminoglycoside/gentamicin attribution by the authors of 33201535 is compressed into "same confounders".
- not_known[3] "none of those preparations carries a Wwox deficiency or a measured depolarising GABA response": the first half is true. The second half is contradicted by E03 (20810895), which measured intracellular chloride with Clomeleon and reports GABA-A effect inversion after recurrent seizures. Should read: no Wwox deficiency; the chloride rise there is seizure-induced and was not measured in a genetic model.
- Source ids attach to the right statements; depth labels are the rows' own (abstract_only for 25765333/33258158/33955541, partial for 20810895/23536761/33201535).
