## (d) DepMap

Method as described in Q18 (figshare 24Q4, CRISPRGeneEffect.csv + expression, 1103 models with both tables; lowest 15 percent expression versus median-and-above; Welch t; Pearson r; no covariates, no multiple-test adjustment; lineage sensitivity run) is described transparently and its self-stated limits are appropriate. Not recomputed: the expression-conditional contrast (needs the 507 MB expression file; not asked). Remarks: (1) expression is not loss; WWOX sits in FRA16D and many lines carry copy-number deletion, so a copy-number-defined contrast would be closer to a loss genotype; (2) with about 17,900 tests, t about -4.6 to -5.6 is at or near the Bonferroni boundary, so "no robust dependency" is a fair summary and "exploratory" is right.
Recomputed here (public figshare file https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/27993248 -> CRISPRGeneEffect.csv, 428,678,699 bytes, column "WWOX (51741)", all rows with a value): n = 1178 models, mean +0.055, median +0.062, SD 0.132, min -0.552 (ACH-002014), max +0.663; below -0.5: 1; below -0.3: 15; below -1: 0; 5th/25th/75th/95th percentile -0.160/-0.023/+0.134/+0.269. The package's 1103 is the subset with expression too; its mean +0.057 and "1 below -0.5" agree. WWOX non-essential: CONFIRMED.

## Comparison with the package

Disagreements (substantive)
1. H1/M02/README 3: "floor effect explains the pattern equally well" is stronger than the figure and conflicts with the census reading; "equally well" should be "cannot be excluded" (lithium's WT panel is significant from a similarly low baseline but is a different cohort and n; the ethosuximide WT/HET n are larger and still n.s.).
2. M04 transfer_breakers omit that the WT arm exists and glucose fell in it, and that the placebo KO showed no rise; the census text "not a tested WT null effect" should not be carried.
3. M02 "genotype-by-drug pattern" and H1 title "genotype-dependent seizure modifier": no interaction test exists in the source (one-way ANOVA per the Methods). The wording must say "difference in within-genotype significance, interaction untested".
4. I04: direction (library versus query gene) is not in the Alliance record (roles unspecified); the row presents it as fact.
5. I01 perturbation text misdescribes the Sod arm (mutant allele, not RNAi).

Overstatements / implications to soften
* README point 1 opens "Yes". The nine rows are single-laboratory, provoked/ex vivo/non-neural/acquired-in-culture; "Yes" should be "Yes, as a measured change in a stated endpoint; none rescues a developmental, myelin or survival endpoint". The README does say this later (points 3, 10), so this is tone, not content.
* M05 labelled MEASURED MODIFIER: it shows NMDAR dependence of an ex vivo burst, n = 5 slices from 3 animals, no WT drug arm; acceptable only with that wording.
* M07/H3: restoring "senescence induction" in fibroblasts is not an evident therapeutic outcome in neurons; the row's breaker list says "not a neural phenotype" but not "acquired during passage, desired direction unclear".
* No rescue implication was found; README 8 and 10 correctly exclude dependency (O01, O02) and Wwox-as-modifier rows (I01-I03). No dependency is presented as a modifier of a neurological phenotype. Good.

Hypotheses (controllable / counter-evidence / transfer breakers)
* H1: controllable (approved drug; genetic T-type arm), counter-evidence present, breakers present. Fix the floor-effect sentence (above) and add "interaction never formally tested; n = 6 per KO arm".
* H2: controllable only for the genetic Hif1a arm; digoxin is correctly "probe, not proposal". Counter-evidence lacks (i) WT glucose fell under digoxin, (ii) target transcript panels at n = 2-3 (census), (iii) the nominal 100 mg/kg dose. Breakers present.
* H3: controllable (NAC), counter-evidence and breakers present. Add "phenotype is passage-acquired; senescence induction is not a neural endpoint".
* H4: lacks its own counter-evidence and transfer-breaker lines (defers to card E02). Add from the source: rescue is "partial", Ldh knockdown is reported detrimental in other fly Abeta42 models, Wwox reduced not absent, amyloid context; no mammalian controllable Ldh perturbation is named.
* "Not advanced" list: fine.

Absence statements: N01/N02 and README 2, 5, 10 correctly say "bounded search". Independently reproduced for Alliance only; MGI/IMPC not reproduced. Absence of a hit in a bounded search is not absence.
