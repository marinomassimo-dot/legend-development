## 0. Findings that matter most (read these first)

1. **Three of the orchestrator's own edits carry a statement that is false against the source (R1 R90, R91, R92).** The edit text says the homozygous condition "was tested only in organotypic culture". It was not. Results, in vivo section (Fig. 10): conditional homozygous PV-Cre;Tsc1 flox/flox mice were treated in vivo with rapamycin P14-21. PV perisomatic puncta density reached wild-type level in BOTH heterozygous and homozygous adults; social behaviour (approach and novelty) was rescued in heterozygous but NOT in homozygous mice; Purkinje-cell abnormalities in homozygotes were only partially rescued. The audit's governing passage pointed to the culture sentence and did not see Fig. 10; the orchestrator copied the error. Direction of the damage: R90 (innervation) now understates a real homozygous in vivo rescue and asserts a false design fact; R91 (behaviour) is right to confine "full" to haploinsufficiency, but gives the wrong reason and loses a decisive in vivo negative (homozygous behaviour not rescued). Not consumer-bound (no R90-R92 in any O-text), but the rows are wrong as they now stand.
2. **O8 says the kinase late arm had "two alleles"; the source says one.** PMC11866500: the electrographic/seizure rescue experiment used Cdkl5 FLEX/+ ; CAGG-CreER females only (Fig. 5; n = 6 early, n = 5 late). STOP females were observed weekly for seizures but were not in the rescue EEG experiment. R1 R23 (unedited) says "two alleles and two Cre drivers", and the R24 edit left that conflict in place. CONSUMER-BOUND (O8 anchors R23, R24).
3. **O10 and the work order say sheath structure has no post-onset precedent and that none of the late sources measured sheath morphometry. The full text of PMID 37149081 (Aspa-nur7, PMC10330635) contradicts this for a metabolic/oligodendrocyte-enzyme lesion.** Single rAAV-ASPA given at 6 weeks to symptomatic mice (pre-injection H&E at 6 weeks shows vacuoles and tissue loss); electron microscopy of the anterior commissure at 1 and 4 weeks gives G-ratio, myelin layers per axon and axons per section, all described as "normalized" in the juvenile (symptomatic) group; a later "late-stage, post-symptomatic adult" arm gave only partial correction. R2 R74 is graded abstract-only with age not_stated and "no age, comparator or method was read"; that is now out of date, and the sentence "None of these measured sheath morphometry" in O10 is wrong for this source. The neuron-driven qualifier in O10/would_change/U07 still stands (Aspa is an oligodendrocyte enzyme), so the "first of its kind for a neuron-driven lesion" wording survives, but the unqualified work-order sentence does not. CONSUMER-BOUND. Note: the PubMed abstract says "oligodendrocyte and neuronal lineage markers", the PMC full-text abstract says "myelination and neuronal lineage markers" (different versions); the R2 R74 edit follows the PubMed abstract exactly and is itself fine.
4. **O10 pools two models in a way the Cx47 source does not support**: "oligodendrocyte number, apoptosis, glial markers ... moved far more" than the sheath in both pre-onset models. In the Cx47/Cx32 source (R2 R55) total oligodendrocyte number did NOT differ between treated and mock groups (22.53 vs 23.2 CC1+ cells per area; Olig2+ non-significant trend). Number was restored only in the jimpy model. CONSUMER-BOUND.
5. **R2 R55 edit introduces a small error**: it calls astrogliosis "(qualitative)" and says values are in the running text. The running-text sentence on astrogliosis is qualitative, but GFAP and Iba1 total immunofluorescence were quantified (n = 5 per group, Fig. 5F-G, "confirms these findings"). Apoptosis (50.29 +/- 11.13 percent to 5.25 +/- 1.43 percent, P = 0.0073) and macrophages (7.43 +/- 0.87 to 1.28 +/- 0.61 per section, P < 0.001) ARE in the prose, so the main point of the edit stands.
6. **R19 edit is right but leaves the row and O12 inconsistent.** After the edit R19 no longer demonstrates a harm, yet O12 still anchors R19 and says "two interventions changed behaviour in treated wild-type animals". The remaining support for "two" must come from R18 (Kcnt1 EPM, descriptive, source reads it as benefit) and the Fmr1 compound row (R1 R77) or R41; R19 should be dropped from the O12 anchor list or the sentence reworded. R19's `transfer_conditions` ("the intervention has a measurable cost in the unaffected brain") is stale and now overshoots its own endpoint_detail.
