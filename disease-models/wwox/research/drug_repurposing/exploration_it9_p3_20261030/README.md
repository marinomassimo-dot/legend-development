## 1. Decision (first)

* **What emerged.** Four questions have seed-specific proposals (both nulls, |Z| > 3): Q2, Q4 protein, Q5, Q6; Q1, Q3, Q7 have none (abstention below). The 778 seed-specific families collapse to **606 parent compounds**; **20 were taken to deepening** (15 non-reference + 5 reference points; 2 of the 15 are artefact controls). The set spans thirteen target classes (melatonin receptor, HIF-2alpha, O-GlcNAcase, PPARG, estrogen receptor, an ER-stress relay, EP300, caspase-1, RORC, DYRK1A, cereblon/CUL4A, Aurora B, DOT1L) and has 2 of 20 antiseizure/NMDAR compounds (felbamate, memantine; both as references, 10 percent, cap 25 percent; no T-type).
* **The honest strength of it.** Of the nine compounds that LEGEND links by catalogue id (cards, units, queue) and that appear in any P2 table, none is seed-specific (all are direct-search or top-list hits); one more, belnacasan, is named only in the text of the abstention card J01 and is seed-specific in Q5. The engine therefore does not reproduce the carded answers and I do not read that as validation or refutation. DCA is recovered only through its own target (P2 T3). Every selected proposal rests on **one network edge (13 of 15 non-reference proposals) or a 3-hop chain (ramelteon, implitapide)** from a target to one seed gene; the seed datum is a transcript or mouse protein change.
* **What was discarded, and which datum changed the decision.**
  1. *Identity.* The P2 funnel line "resolved 16,475 -> usable 13,379" is not nested: only **4,038** families are both ChEMBL-resolved and usable; 9,341 usable families are DGIdb-only. This datum (found in aggregate counts, Amendment 2) changed rule F4 from "no ChEMBL id = out" to identity routes; **221 of 606 seed-specific parents remain unresolved and are excluded for identity only (UNKNOWN, not inactive)**; 113 more were name-matched and stay flagged UNVERIFIED.
  2. *Feasibility (kept separate from promise).* Among the 606 seed-specific parents (a parent can fail more than one test): F4 identity unresolved 221, F5 no human-exposure statement 267, F2 topical-only 45, F3 protein/antibody 6, F6 withdrawn 4, **F1 cytotoxic anticancer 0 by the written test**. `excluded.tsv` lists each excluded parent with its promise columns intact.
  3. *Hubs.* 316 of 606 parent compounds (52 percent) reach their seed through a hub target (degree >= 60, 95th percentile); five clusters (NR3C1 86 parents, AR 38, PGR 35, ESR2 29, VDR 25 = 213) are single-target nuclear-receptor ligands whose target is a hub; the largest non-hub clusters are JAK2 (37), AURKB (36) and MTNR1A (33). Under the amended hub-artefact definition (hub target AND median promiscuity >= 5) **no cluster qualifies**; under the original reading (hub target alone, hub_target_share >= 0.5) 24 of 61 clusters do. So the hub-ness is reported as a flag and used as a tie-break (non-hub first), not as an exclusion; the large steroid-receptor clusters contribute no selected family because the selection key prefers a new mechanism group, non-hub, higher |Z|. That is a judgment of the rule, not a finding that they are wrong.
  4. *A sign problem found by reading.* Pomalidomide/lenalidomide enter Q6 as MIMICS through "CRL4(CRBN) inhibitor" -> CUL4A -> MYC (-1). The real mechanism (molecular glue degrading IKZF1/3) lowers MYC in myeloma, so the compatible-action sign is probably wrong for the class; kept (pomalidomide as selected, lenalidomide as control) with the problem flagged for P4b.
  5. *An alias artefact.* AR-12 (a PDPK1 inhibitor) appears in the Q2 direct-search arm because "PDK1" is an alias of PDPK1; it is not a PDH-kinase hit. Kept as a reference for that reason.
* **How much of the universe was really examined** (units: families = catalogue records joined by parent/InChIKey block): catalogued 40,268; identity-resolved 16,475; usable by the method 13,379 (of which resolved 4,038); with gene symbol 13,307; **examined 12,560 (31.2 percent of the catalogue)**; non-zero 2,190 to 5,187 per set; seed-specific 124 (Q2), 52 (Q4 protein), 160 (Q5), 491 (Q6). 26,889 records have no usable signed action and 28,776 no structure key (the signature arm): UNKNOWN. After identity, exposure and modality tests, **eligible for selection** (feasible, not promiscuous, not seed-own-target): Q2 20 parents (2 opposes, 18 mimics), Q4 22 (20 / 2), Q5 39 (33 / 6), Q6 160 (114 / 42). Q2 opposes is thin: 2 eligible of 53 seed-specific.
* **Abstentions (precise).** Q1: its only seed unit is the WWOX protein itself in a non-neural tier-C system, so no robust Z; the direct arm lists 4 families with any catalogue row on a seed gene (unsigned). Q3: the ISR_SIGN_UNRESOLVED guard forces all 17 units to uncertain direction (0 defined seeds): 0 robust proposals (2 families have |z| > 3 on raw Q under the in-degree null only). Q7: one phospho seed (GSK3B pSer9) with no kinase-activity direction: 0 robust; raw-Q hits are the GSK3B hub. None of the three yields a selected family from seed evidence; Q3 contributes one reference (sirolimus, direct-search arm). The LINCS signature arm is "not informative" in every set (P2).
