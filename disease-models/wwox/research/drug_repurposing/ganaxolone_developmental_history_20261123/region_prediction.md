# Stage-matched regional patterning as a competing explanation

Frozen after the WWOX and elapsed-time panel results, before extracting any expression in the regional dataset. GSE93811 contains two unselected, separately patterned human cultures of the same iPSC line (2242-1), both at day 105, matching week 15 of the WWOX organoids. Each region has one deposited profile and thousands of cells; cells do not supply independent organoid replicates.

Does an increased contribution of subpallial relative to pallial tissue reproduce the WWOX bulk direction? Sum deposited molecules by gene across cells and normalize each regional aggregate to a million molecules. Hold the three receptor genes and eight effector genes that qualified in WWOX fixed. Mix the two regional RNA profiles before log transformation, increasing the subpallial RNA fraction from 0 to 1 in steps of 0.1. Compare changes against a pure pallial baseline. Estimate a mixture fraction independently using the four GABA-lineage genes GAD1, GAD2, SST and CALB2. Choose the grid fraction minimizing mean squared discrepancy between their WWOX changes and regional mixture changes. Then ask if that fraction gives both receptor-down and effector-up, examining both individual arms and GABRB3 separately; also compare the remaining grid fractions to assess sign dependence. A positive directional result demonstrates plausibility of tissue mixture as a confound in this external system. It cannot estimate a real subpallial cell fraction or exclude a WWOX-intrinsic change. If the effector direction but not the receptor decrease transfers, distinguish the two arms and report the residual gene(s). Compare pooled molecules with an equal-cell sensitivity (cells with at least 500 total deposited molecules), to gauge differential RNA recovery. No minimum fit error was defined for quantitative calibration; report the four component residuals, regardless of agreement.
