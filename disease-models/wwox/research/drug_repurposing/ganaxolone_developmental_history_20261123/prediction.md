# Does WWOX loss reproduce the late dissociation between GABA receptor and signaling transcripts after a developmental neurosteroid exposure?

An external ventral human organoid study used allopregnanolone (ALLO), 125 nM, DIV28–67, followed by observation at DIV78–138. The paper's Figure 4 and its accompanying results name five receptor-subunit transcripts increased at DIV138, alongside eight downstream GABA-signaling elements that decreased collectively. Source design and named results were inspected before freezing. GSE156243 is the primary target (WWOX-null human organoids at week 15). Earlier WWOX publications reported altered GABA-lineage representation. No newly computed receptor or effector panel effects inspected before freezing. Concurrent versus discontinued exposures have not been compared in the source; a late association with prior ALLO exposure is the supported description.

Primary panels as named in the source: receptor GABRA1 GABRA5 GABRB1 GABRB3 GABRG2. Effector SLC32A1 SLC6A1 SLC6A11 KCNJ3 GPHN GABARAP GABBR2 GABBR1. Predicted match to the late ALLO condition requires positive WWOX KO-minus-control receptor mean, negative effector mean, at least three fifths of each panel moving in the corresponding direction and persistence of both mean signs after omitting each library. A complete reciprocal pattern requires these criteria with reversed signs. A shared shift of both arms fails either pattern even if the receptor-minus-effector contrast differs from zero. Require a mean of ≥1 CPM across controls per included gene and coverage of ≥4/5 and ≥6/8. Recalculate with all mapped genes. Show individual genes, library effects, the 15 possible choices of two controls, and GAD1/GAD2/CALB2/SST plus TUBB3/MAP2/RBFOX3/SNAP25 as descriptive context for cell representation. A bulk score cannot establish receptor physiology, cell identity, steroid levels or pharmacological direction in a genetic disorder.

Independent control for ordinary organoid age: obtain each gene's control baseline abundance in GSE156243, and its mean log-expression and within-iPSC-line slope versus log2(days) in unperturbed cortical spheroids GSE150123. Restrict primary reference to days 40–160, report the number of informative lines and repeat with the full age range. Use standardized distances on the three features and generate 2,000 disjoint control panels by random sampling from the 50 nearest non-target genes per target, drawing without replacement within each iteration. Report the empirical distribution of the difference between arms in the WWOX data; an unusual difference beyond the two-sided central 95% of decoys supports specificity only if the two individual arms meet the frozen direction rules. Match on reference features without using WWOX KO values. A tissue-development match across studies motivates testing an interaction of genotype with steroid exposure and time since removal; ALLO is a related endogenous steroid and no equivalence to ganaxolone is assumed.
