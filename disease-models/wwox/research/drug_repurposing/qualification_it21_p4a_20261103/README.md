## 2. Rules, and what order they were written in

Fixed before the table below was assembled but **after the retrievals of section 3 had been seen** (disclosed: I picked the six from the hand-over table, retrieved, read, and only then wrote these four lines, so they describe how I decided, they do not pre-register it):
* REJECTED_INFORMATIVE: a decisive link of the chain is contradicted, or absent from the WWOX-relevant system, by a retrieved datum (target not expressed, the compound's documented action goes the other way, attribution to the listed target fails), and the reason is stated so it can be reused.
* INCONCLUSIVE: a decisive datum is missing or the sign is contested with no cheap resolution inside LEGEND's resources; the missing datum is named.
* KEPT_CONDITIONAL: no decisive link is contradicted in the relevant system, target engagement is documented in cells at a stated potency, a feasible discriminating test exists; role still unknown; status unchanged.
* QUALIFIED_HYPOTHESIS: a sourced role of the observed change and a measured link in a WWOX system. None.

## 4. Per proposal: foundation -> decisive assumption -> counter-evidence -> next test -> result that would change the decision

The full text of every field is in `qualification_it21_p4a.tsv` (22 columns, the same fields in the order asked), and the sign, level, system and transfer of every edge in `edge_provenance_it21_p4a.tsv` (22 rows: measurement / inference / hypothesis, disease-transferred or not, local data check, grade). The core of each:

**0006 nanatinostat (REJECTED_INFORMATIVE).** Foundation: class I HDAC inhibitor (HDAC1/2/3 3-7 nM) with a 200 nM HDAC5 row; HDAC5 -> SLC2A1 from one reference (knockdown in human primary muscle cells lowers GLUT1). Assumption: the compound lowers SLC2A1 through HDAC5. Counter-evidence: attribution (67-fold), single non-neural reference, local association opposite (HDAC5 0.81x with SLC2A1 2.67x in KO organoid; hNSC WWOX-deleted 49.0 -> 10.5 with SLC2A1 276 -> 455), and an unmodelled adjacent relation (HDAC3 acts with WWOX in breast cancer cells, title only). Next test: HDAC5 knockdown v class IIa-selective v nanatinostat v a second class I inhibitor, SLC2A1/HK2 and glucose uptake in WWOX-deficient neural progenitors. Changes the decision: HDAC5-specific lowering at HDAC5-engaging concentrations; or class I inhibition changing SLC2A1 either way (then a different, class I / HDAC3 proposal).

**0008 IOX1 (REJECTED_INFORMATIVE).** Foundation: biochemical KDM4C IC50 0.2-0.6 uM. Assumption: lowers HIF activity via KDM4C without touching the HIF prolyl hydroxylases. Counter-evidence (verbatim locators L1-L4): "IOX1 was found to stabilise HIF1α, relative to the DMSO control, at a concentration of 0.2 mM"; "decreases hydroxylation of HIF1α Pro564 and Asn803 ... and thus of PHD and FIH activity"; KDM4A cell EC50 86 uM; ChEMBL PHD2 IC50 14.3-15 uM; KDM4C is the least expressed KDM4 member (11.7-17 cpm v KDM4A/B 92-246; absent in hNPC); the edge's single reference is a review. The direct action raises HIF, the opposite of the intent, by the route that made roxadustat a DROP in IT9. Next test (only if the role is first shown protective): a concentration window with H3K9me3 up, HIF1alpha unchanged, HK2/SLC2A1 down. Changes the decision: such a window, reproduced by KDM4 knockdown.

**0057 CGI-1746 (REJECTED_INFORMATIVE).** Foundation: BTK IC50 0.61-81 nM; BTK -> STAT3 (B-cell review) -> NPY (hypothalamic leptin work). Assumption: BTK is present and acting in the NPY-producing cell. Counter-evidence: BTK 0.07-0.50 cpm KO and WT organoid, ABSENT hNPC (HPA brain cluster: macrophages and microglia); NPY 0.05-0.12 cpm in KO v 3.2-7.7 WT, so the seed is a near-zero count; documented off-targets for this exact compound (sigma-1 receptor, 26S proteasome in BTK-negative cells). Next test: Btk and Npy by cell class in the mouse snRNA data (GSE158684, free, not run here), then step 0 of the hand-over; a compound step only in a BTK-positive system with BTK knockout and a different BTK inhibitor as controls. Changes the decision: BTK detected in the NPY-producing class with NPY following BTK knockdown; BTK only in microglia moves the idea to Q5 as a different proposal.

**0195 GSK343 (KEPT_CONDITIONAL, narrowed).** Foundation: PRC2 IC50 4 nM (Ki 1.2 nM), EZH1 240 nM, >100 uM on 13 other enzymes, cell H3K27 methylation 174 nM; GSK-343 lowered cytokines in an MPTP mouse (abstract). Assumption: EZH2 inhibition lowers IL6/TNF in Wwox-deficient glia. Counter-evidence: PMID 29132376, itself a reference of the edge, reports EZH2 inhibition RAISES IL6, IL1B (glioblastoma cells) and TNF/iNOS (microglia); P-gp/BCRP substrate in vitro; fetal NSC viability IC50 9 uM at 48 h; the human neural systems carry no IL6/TNF reads (0 / ABSENT). Next test: Wwox-null v wild-type mouse microglia/astrocyte cultures or slices, GSK343 0.17-1 uM v a second chemotype v Ezh2 knockdown, 4-7 days, transcript and protein. Changes the decision: falling cytokines reproduced by the controls keeps it conditional; rising cytokines rejects H_HARM for this axis.

**0374 tizaterkib (INCONCLUSIVE).** Foundation: ERK2 IC50 0.3-0.66 nM, cell pERK 1.7-5.7 nM; Erk2 phosphorylates Bcl3 (Mol Cell 2017); Bcl-3 represses LPS-induced TNF in macrophages (titles). Assumption: ERK inhibition removes Bcl3 repression so the cytokines are spared or raised. Counter-evidence: the direct Tpl2/ERK requirement for LPS-induced TNF (Cell 2000, title only) predicts TNF down; CDK5 300 nM is a neuron-relevant off-target; the 101-104% inhibition of MEK/BRAF/RAF1/COT in ChEMBL comes from coupled cascade assays read on ERK2 and is not an off-target finding (direct assays at 1 uM: -3.5 to 5.8%; direct COT inhibition NOT measured); no CNS datum. Next test: Wwox-null v WT microglia/astrocyte cultures, 10-100 nM with ravoxertinib and ERK2 knockdown as controls (one experiment serves both ERK compounds). Changes the decision: lowering contradicts the arm as posed (re-pose as a lowering probe in a new record); raising with reduced Bcl3 phosphorylation supports the chain. The arm label H_PROTECT is not changed here.

**0512 SRT-2104 (INCONCLUSIVE).** Foundation: phase 2, human PK (title), BBB penetration in HD mice (abstract); curated ChEMBL mechanism ACTIVATOR of SIRT1. Assumption: engages SIRT1 in neural progenitors and SIRT1 lowers MYC transcript. Counter-evidence: ChEMBL has **no SIRT1 activity row** for the compound, and the three references behind the curated mechanism are clinical papers (smokers' cardiovascular effects, psoriasis, arterial stiffness); the class-level activation is disputed (Pacholec 2010 v Hubbard 2013, abstracts); the edge's reference list contains both signs (Kim 2012, Hong 2012, Yuan 2009: SIRT1 lowers c-Myc; Menssen 2012, Xie 2019: SIRT1 stabilises c-MYC), almost all at protein level in cancer lines; SIRT1 is ABSENT in hNPC. Next test: SRT-2104 v SIRT1 knockdown v EX-527 in WWOX-KO organoid/hNPC with engagement read as native-substrate deacetylation, MYC transcript AND protein, SOX2+/NEUN+ fractions. Changes the decision: engagement plus SIRT1-dependent MYC fall with rising neuronal fraction keeps it conditional; MYC rising or no engagement rejects it.

## 5. Surprising, and what it says about the generator

1. **A direction wording bug in the P2 hand-over for down seeds.** `intended_effect_on_seed_readout` is keyed on the arm only (`run_generation_it21.py` line 530): 0057 (seed NPY down, H_HARM) is told "lowers the seed-gene readout" though its hypothesised benefit is to restore NPY. Not edited here (P2 is the generator's package); the effect on this record is nil because the benefit and the chain were read from the other fields.
2. **Three of six edges are contradicted or split by the references the network itself cites** (EZH2 -> IL6/TNF by PMID 29132376; SIRT1 -> MYC by PMIDs 22190494 and 31290724; KDM4C -> HIF1A resting on a review). This is the IT9 P4c-e failure type "sign contradiction in the cited paper" again, and no mode or identity filter sees it. A cheap check that would have caught two of the three: compare the sign of the network record with the sign stated in the abstracts of its own references.
3. **The documented target in the engine can be the weakest target of the compound** (nanatinostat: HDAC5 at 200 nM v HDAC1 at 3 nM). The generator ranks the compound by its chain through the listed target, not by whether that target is the compound's main action.
4. **A curated "ACTIVATOR" row can rest on no activity measurement** (SRT-2104: three clinical references, no SIRT1 value).
5. **A coupled-cascade readout looks like an off-target hit** (tizaterkib on MEK/COT/BRAF/RAF1, 101-104%); misread, it would have suggested direct TPL2 inhibition, which is exactly the kinase that matters for TNF.
6. **The human neural systems that carry the Q2/Q4/Q6 seeds express neither the cytokine seeds (IL6, TNF about 0) nor, for Q4, the target (BTK)**: a proposal can be unreadable where it was generated. Q5 seeds are mouse, so Q5 compounds can only be tested in mouse glial systems.
7. The two ERK1/2 compounds handed over (0296, 0374) and the HDAC cluster members are one mechanism cluster each: they must not be counted as independent support.
