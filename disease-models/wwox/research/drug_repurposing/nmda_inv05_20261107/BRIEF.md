**Question.** What type and degree of NMDA modulation could reduce pathological activity in WWOX systems while preserving physiological transmission, viability and development? Two legitimate possibilities, both open: (1) correcting an alteration of the NMDA receptor; (2) compensating a circuit imbalance that originates elsewhere, even with NMDA receptors not pathologically increased. A receptor increase is not a precondition for exploring a symptomatic benefit; benefit is not deduced from burst suppression alone.
