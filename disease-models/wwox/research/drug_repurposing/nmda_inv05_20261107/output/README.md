## Answer

1. DATO: d-APV (50 micromolar) removed bursts in the Wwox neuron-knockout slices at postnatal day 13 to 17 (prior complete read, not reopened). INFERENZA: this fixes dependence on NMDAR activity only; it does not give a receptor alteration, the responsible subunit, or a degree of block that spares physiology. Both possibilities of the brief stay open.
2. INFERENZA: no source read here supplies a "type and degree" of modulation for WWOX. What exists is a set of constraints on how a result could be read, and one comparison worth running first (item 9). No WWOX model with an NMDAR modulator was identified (Europe PMC strings Q15, Q16: titles only, not a null).
3. DATO (PMID 38529699, abstract): radiprodil, a GluN2B-selective NAM, reduced onset and severity of audiogenic seizures dose-dependently in mice homozygous for a GluN2A gain-of-function change, more in females. INFERENZA: a GluN2B-directed lever worked where the lesion sits in GluN2A. Efficacy therefore separates "circuit lever" from "subunit that carries the lesion"; the same applies to any radiprodil effect in WWOX slices. Whether the mutant receptors are GluN2A/GluN2B assemblies is not in the abstract. Acute pre-stimulus dosing, reflex seizures: not chronic control, not development.
4. DATO (PMID 39970743, abstract): infant with ATP1A2-related DEE, status epilepticus partly responsive to ketamine, seizure-free once memantine was added; the genetic result came in the second week of memantine. INFERENZA: concomitant ketamine, timing and spontaneous termination cannot be separated; "apparent efficacy" is the authors' word. The transferable premise is only that a circuit can need NMDAR activity while the lesion is elsewhere (possibility 2); the pump-glutamate link is ATP1A2-specific. One case does not support chronic efficacy or superiority of memantine.
5. DATO (PMID 20720132, full text): in rat autaptic hippocampal neurons (day 13 in vitro, room temperature) 1 micromolar memantine blocked 27.1% of the synaptic NMDAR EPSC and 50.4% of a bath-NMDA "extrasynaptic" current, ratio 1.94 +/- 0.10; at -25 mV with 1 mM Mg2+, 10 micromolar blocked 39.8% against 64.3%. The authors tie it to activity pattern, premise of channel activation lasting minutes or longer, and report little NR2-subunit preference. INFERENZA: preference is two-fold, not exclusion; the "extrasynaptic" current is sustained high agonist after MK-801 occlusion, not tonic ambient glutamate; bursts in the WWOX preparation last on the order of a second (carried, unit unconfirmed) with a median interval of 26 s, so the minutes-scale premise does not carry over by default.
6. Counter-evidence (PMID 24101301, abstract): memantine and ketamine were indistinguishable on synaptic pharmacodynamics, extrasynaptic inhibition and spontaneous network activity; ketamine protected more only in oxygen-glucose deprivation. It compares drugs, not synaptic against extrasynaptic for one drug, so it does not refute item 5; it does remove the basis for treating memantine and ketamine as a contrast. No direct replication or refutation of the 2:1 ratio was found (PubMed and Europe PMC; hits not read in full: not a null).

## Corrections to the external report (judged, its ranking not adopted)

* Age shows composition: no. Cortical GluN2B-to-GluN2A timing in this mouse is unverified (carried); not usable.
* Radiprodil effect locates the defect in GluN2B: no (item 3). It would support participation of GluN2B-containing receptors in sustaining bursts, tested only with a target-specific control (X2).
* Pharmacological failure excludes the target: only with verified exposure, achieved block and conditions (Mg2+, voltage, agonist); none of these is established for the WWOX slice.
* "Extrasynaptic/tonic means inaccessible to memantine" and its mirror "extrasynaptic means preferred": both wrong as stated (item 5). Memantine blocks synaptic receptors substantially.
* Probe classes: ifenprodil, Ro 25-6981, radiprodil are GluN2B NAMs; memantine, ketamine, MK-801 are pore blockers with no detected subunit selectivity, and are not interchangeable (MK-801 blocked synaptic and extrasynaptic equally, ratio 1.02, same paper). ChEMBL "negative allosteric modulator" on memantine is vocabulary. Radiprodil GluN2A potency is not retrievable.
* Human experience, approval elsewhere and safety in WWOX development are different levels; no source here addresses development in WWOX. Outcomes after treatment of status epilepticus (item 4) are not attributable to the drug. A single antagonist failing in another disease does not refute the strategy; a WWOX failure would also need verified exposure.

## Conditional hypothesis (IPOTESI)

IF the NAM-sensitive fraction of the isolated NMDAR current in knockout pyramidal cells is substantial AND a GluN2B NAM abolishes bursts at a smaller measured current reduction than d-APV needs, with evoked AMPA-mediated responses and wild-type activity spared, THEN a GluN2B-directed reduction is a candidate for lowering burst-generating gain in this slice preparation (benefit = fewer cortical bursts in slices; seizure control, maturation and function untested). IF the curves coincide at matched current, total NMDAR current is the limiting gain and the window depends on how non-burst transmission responds, unknown. A raised receptor current is not required for either.

## Priority open question and the decision it changes

Does burst suppression in knockout slices need GluN2B-containing current, or does any matched reduction of total NMDAR current do the same, at levels that spare non-burst transmission, and does it act through pyramidal gain or through interneurons? Answer changes: whether radiprodil is carried as a comparator or the strategy is framed as a degree-limited reduction of gain with any antagonist; whether memantine's activity-pattern premise is used at all.
