# Priority questions as usable briefs (IT44)

Non-canonical working note. Not medical advice; no dose, schedule or route. Reference genotype class only. Reuses the investigations table, the open-questions map and the packages named; creates no registry. Each brief states the decision it can change, the premise, the in-silico work still useful, the one indispensable measurement, what favours each alternative, and when to stop. A science session can start from a brief plus the pointers; nothing here is commissioned, and no contact or message is implied.

## B1. INV-18 (K+ branch D): is a resting K+ conductance lost, or is the cell smaller with a shunt?
* **Decision it can change:** whether any K+ arm (opener or blocker) is proposed at all, or only the Ih tool arm and the capacitance measurement; whether the Kv7 abstention stands.
* **Unresolved premise:** capacitance by genotype and the cell-selection rule (a smaller cell with a fixed shunt gives the same implied reversal); the Ba2+-sensitive current per pF; which channel family carries it.
* **In-silico work still useful:** (a) primary-source reading of which K2P members are Ba2+-sensitive and whether any Kir2 activator exists (IT44 S11 reader, `generation_it44_20261110/verify_s11/`); (b) cell-type expression of the candidate families in juvenile cortex; (c) SCI43's open reading task on juvenile layer II/III shares (`next_brief_sci43_20261110.md`).
* **Indispensable measurement:** capacitance and access resistance by genotype (ladder step 0), then Ba2+-sensitive current per pF with synaptic blockers (steps 1-2). No compound before step 2.
* **Favours the alternatives:** equal capacitance with higher Rin and a smaller Ba2+ current per pF favours a lost conductance (KH1); lower capacitance with flat tau favours a smaller cell (KH5); a contrast that shrinks under synaptic blockade favours a network consequence (KH4); equal Ba2+ current per pF with excess sag favours Ih (KH3).
* **Stop criterion:** stop reading when each channel family has a juvenile layer II/III figure or a bounded not-found with the search strings; stop proposing a K+ arm if capacitance explains the Rin difference.

## B2. INV-07 with INV-19: is the pyruvate dehydrogenase step restrained at all, and is it HIF-linked?
* **Decision it can change:** whether a kinase-directed lever is ever ordered; whether the HIF1A-to-BNIP3 and the PDH questions share one sample series.
* **Unresolved premise:** no neural measurement of PDH amount, phosphorylation at the three sites or activity; the signs conflict between systems; the reference compound's one human trial was stopped early for nerve toxicity (abstract level).
* **In-silico work still useful:** kinase isoform expression by cell type (IT43-Q02, SCI44 A); the primary pharmacology of the typed inhibitors and the symbol check (SCI44 A); the alternative actions of each compound.
* **Indispensable measurement:** one isogenic WWOX-deficient against add-back human neural pair at matched oxygen, reading BNIP3, the four kinase transcripts, PDH phosphorylation and activity before and after dephosphorylation from the same lysates.
* **Favours the alternatives:** BNIP3, kinases and phosphorylation all up: HIF-linked restraint, lever conditional on it; BNIP3 up with PDH unrestrained: INV-19 stands, lever down; neither up: culture or oxygen condition; lactate dependence of the lineage reopens the question with opposite sign.
* **Stop criterion:** stop if PDH is unrestrained in matched neural cells (the lever is closed for that cell type, not refuted elsewhere); stop reading expression data once isoform by cell type is answered for neurons and the oligodendrocyte lineage.

## B3. INV-05: how much NMDA-receptor current must go before the burst stops, and does a GluN2B-directed arm beat total gain?
* **Decision it can change:** whether a GluN2B-selective negative modulator is a better question than total block, and whether a second tool of the same site is needed.
* **Unresolved premise:** the abolition threshold x (percent of the isolated NMDA-receptor current removed by the full competitive antagonist); the removable fraction of an ifenprodil-site modulator in the slice; bath magnesium, zinc, pH and temperature as measured conditions.
* **In-silico work still useful:** the primary source of the second ifenprodil-site tool (IT43-Q01, SCI44 B) so that the engine can re-derive its role; the maximum and site of onfasprodil.
* **Indispensable measurement:** the isolated NMDA-receptor current with measured bath conditions (matrix M01), then x, then the modulator's removable fraction.
* **Favours the alternatives:** burst fall at a smaller removed fraction than x favours GluN2B weight; a plateau below x leaves it unresolved (a ceiling set by composition and zinc), not refuted; coincident curves favour total gain or a subunit-agnostic threshold.
* **Stop criterion:** stop proposing the GluN2B arm if the removable fraction is far below x with no composition explanation; stop tool-reading once the second tool has a primary source or a bounded not-reached.
