## D. The explanations

**E1 (HIF-driven induction).** Reasoning follows the sources and the labels are right. Omissions that bear on it: (a) 41324728 is read by its own authors as HIF-1alpha inhibition being protective (neuron counts, mitochondrial area, seizure latency) with BNIP3 up, and 42323821 (abstract) reports HIF1A-BNIP3 mitophagy as protective in TBI neurons; E1's harm column cites only the 41972676 direction. (b) The 41324728 BNIP3 rise starts before HIF-1alpha falls and the KC7F2 arm is a pre-treatment (section A, D07). (c) Public datum available in GSE156243, not used: BNIP3L, the other HIF-target receptor, is not up (-0.19 log2; 0.88x), and NDRG1 (+0.15), CA9 (+0.06), PGK1 (-0.09), PDK1 (-0.34) and ENO2 (-0.02) are flat or down while HK2, ADM, SLC2A1, P4HA1 and BHLHE40 are up: the "HIF pattern" is partial in the organoid, and EPAS1 (HIF-2alpha) is up 3.8x (padj 0.0019) while HIF1A is not. This is a public co-signature that partly separates E1 now and sharpens M2 (HIF-2alpha first). (d) Driver the sources support and the producer omitted: **EGR1**, named in the read source 42323821 as a repressor of the HIF1A promoter in neurons, is LOWER in the KO organoid (CPM 11.5 v 23.0, log2 -0.97; deposit DESeq2 log2FC -1.04, padj 0.038, down file). Sign-wise this fits an EGR1-to-HIF1A route, but HIF1A mRNA is flat (-0.25), EGR1 is an immediate-early gene sensitive to culture handling, n is 4 v 2, and the support is abstract level: IPOTESI, a cheap public check for M2/M3, not a finding.

**E2 (HIF-independent induction or stabilisation).** Two substantive corrections. (1) EGR1 is placed here ("repressors such as EGR1 acting at HIF1A") but EGR1-to-HIF1A-to-BNIP3 is a HIF-dependent route (it acts upstream of HIF1A); it belongs in E1 as an upstream regulator. (2) "A Mst1/2 inhibitor lowers stress-induced BNIP3 and mitophagy in the source (HEK cells)" attributes to XMU-MP-1 a BNIP3 effect the source does not show: XMU-MP-1 (5 uM, 16 h pretreatment then CCCP 10 uM or DFP 1 mM for 24 h, HEK293) reduced the loss of Mfn1, SDHB and Cox4 (a mitophagy proxy), whereas the fall in stress-induced BNIP3 was shown with Mst1/2 double shRNA or siRNA knockdown. The compound description "ATP-competitive Mst1/2 inhibitor" matches the source (it cites a prior paper for it); the action at BNIP3 is genetic-knockdown evidence only. Missed drivers supported by sources I could reach (abstract level, not read in full by me, so only candidates): p53 as a direct BNIP3 regulator in mouse brain and neurons (38043200: HDAC6 knockout, p53 K320 acetylation inversely related to BNIP3 in brain, neurons more affected), FoxO3a to BNIP3 protein in human brain (34475505, ApoE4 carriers, FoxO3a repression with lower BNIP3), NF-kB with BNIP3 in hippocampal neural cells under CoCl2 (40541802, correlative), NF-kB/HIF-1alpha to BNIP3 in glioblastoma (42874824, a cancer model). The statement "no source read here names them" is true of the sources read, but it must not be taken as absence (section E). KC7F2: the source describes it as inhibiting HIF-1alpha protein synthesis at the translational level "by inhibiting 4E-binding protein and the phosphorylation of S6K"; the producer's "HIF-1 translation inhibitor, selectivity unknown" is acceptable but should add that the described mechanism runs through the mTOR-axis nodes 4E-BP and S6K, which is itself a candidate route to a BNIP3 change independent of HIF-1alpha.

**E3 (culture artefact).** Reasoning sound; the held-table numbers (culture-time slope -0.35 [-1.10, +0.39]; marker-predicted shift +0.01) are reproduced from the table. The HT22 normoxic drift (about 1 to 17 over 48 h) is a bar reading from Fig 4A and is right. Weak point already stated by the producer: no hypoxia marker exists in the organoid deposit.

**E4 (no protein change).** Sound as IPOTESI. Its scale support now rests on D08 as corrected: protein about 2.2 to 2.35 matched, mRNA matched fold about 25 to 80 for 6 to 24 h but about 4 at 48 h.

**E5 (abstention).** Justified: no WWOX-deficient neural protein, flux or HIF-protein datum exists (section E), the direction of BNIP3 function is stress dependent (D07, D12, D13, 42792606 descriptive only), and the abstention is paired with a discriminating question (BNIP3 protein in isogenic KO v add-back at matched oxygen; fall on HIF1A and EPAS1 knockdown; effect of BNIP3 knockdown on survival, ROS, membrane potential) and with outcomes that move the priority. The sentence "No public dataset answers it" is true for the whole question but a public partial separation exists (BNIP3L, NDRG1, CA9, PGK1 and EPAS1 pattern, EGR1; M7 covers only the neuron-centring part).

**Pharmacological actions written as if a source gave an effect at BNIP3.** Only the XMU-MP-1 sentence in E2 (above). PX-478, EZN-2968 and nitroglycerin are correctly stated as having no action at BNIP3 in any source read. No dose, schedule or route appears.

**Measurements the producer missed** (public, no wet work): EGR1 and BNIP3L from GSE156243 as above; HIF3A is flat (-0.03); DDIT4 +0.75 and MTOR +0.57 (stress, mTOR axis) in the same deposit as context for the 4E-BP/S6K route; hypoxia-marker or HIF-target coherence by a panel of non-BNIP3 HIF-1-specific genes (PGK1, NDRG1, CA9 are flat). Wet-work gaps: a KC7F2-style control with an mTOR-axis comparator (rapamycin-class or 4E-BP read-out) in M2/M3 so that BNIP3 changes after a translation inhibitor are not read as HIF effects.
