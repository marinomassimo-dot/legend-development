## What it says (DATO, abstract level, the whole abstract read)
* Frames WWOX-related epileptic encephalopathy (WOREE, DEE28) as caused by loss-of-function variants, with cellular and circuit mechanisms "poorly understood".
* States that dysfunction of inhibitory cortical circuits may play a central role in DEEs and that parvalbumin-expressing interneurons regulate network excitability and synchronisation.
* States the approach: genetic mouse models, in vivo electrophysiology and ex vivo slice recordings; cell type-specific manipulations to examine which neuronal populations contribute to the epileptic phenotype; intrinsic excitability and synaptic properties; and, in parallel, "the potential of targeted pharmacological modulation of neuronal activity".
* Says the work "aims to provide new insights".
