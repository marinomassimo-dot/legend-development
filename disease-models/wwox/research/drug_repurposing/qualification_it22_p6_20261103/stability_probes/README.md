# IT22 P6: qualification of the proteostasis levers for a destabilised WWOX missense protein (2026-11-03)

Non-canonical research material, producer output (not self-ratified). Public edition, WWOX-DEE reference genotype class (Q230P class; G372R and P47T are other alleles, never pooled). Not medical advice; no dose, route or schedule is proposed. Zero spend, no registration, no e-mail address sent anywhere. Only this directory was written; no existing file was edited, no ledger record was made (the five receipt JSONs in `receipts/` are for the integrator).

## 1. Decision first

* **The lever is qualified as CONDITIONAL on a substrate that is not shown to exist; none is promoted.** The weakest link is not any compound: it is that no WWOX model carries a variant protein shown above the assay floor (IT12 P1a: Q230P fibroblast no band; G372R faint 0.1 to 0.3 of an unrelated wild type on one membrane, similar to the KO lane on a second; P47T present at control-like level, so a stability lever does not apply there). The substrate is UNCONFIRMED, not excluded (`absent artefact is not an absent protein`).
* **Best rationale-to-discrimination ratio: the lysosomal-flux arm (chloroquine, with HSPA8 and LAMP2 genetics as the concordance controls), and inside HSC70 modulation, forigerimod only as one arm of it.** Reason: it is the only arm with a positive datum on a WWOX missense (P252A, over-expressed, non-neural), and the experiment that tests it also tests every other lever (same chase, same readout). Forigerimod alone is a weaker lever than the chloroquine arm: its own paper reads its action as the same lysosomal-flux slowing that chloroquine produces, it is a peptide with no CNS datum, and its HSC70 affinity is micromolar (Kd 7.32 uM, SPR).
* **The HDAC6 arm is the weaker of the two named levers on mechanism**: the one cytosolic-enzyme precedent (a PAH mouse, tubastatin A) assigns the effect to HSP90 acetylation, but a loss of HSP90 chaperone function is the classical route to client degradation, so the sign of the proposed mechanism is not self-evident and no turnover was measured. It stays deferred behind the fate of the lesion, as card A01 says.
* Nothing in this package says a stability lever would restore WWOX **function**: there is no WWOX activity readout, and in a human SDR core substitutions that stabilised the protein lost activity (Lou 2018, carried from the 2026-09-22 precedents file, not re-read here).

## 3. Lever L1: HSC70 (HSPA8) modulation, forigerimod (IT21 G01, KEPT_CONDITIONAL)

* **Identity and form.** Forigerimod = the P140 peptide, a 21-mer spliceosomal U1-70K fragment with a phosphoserine at position 140 (ChEMBL parent CHEMBL3989409 per G01). Synthetic peptide; parenteral in the sources (intravenous or intraperitoneal in mice). A 2009 sentence states a phase IIa trial and a phase IIb in progress (indication SLE; current status not verified).
* **Action and measured pharmacology (types kept apart).**
  * Binding: SPR equilibrium **Kd 7.32 uM** (P140) and 6.72 uM (non-phosphorylated 131-151), recombinant HSC70, one-site Langmuir fit (19390596). Scrambled peptide did not bind; **no binding to Hsp70** (same paper).
  * Function in vitro: luciferase refolding by reticulocyte lysate inhibited **50.2 percent at 40 uM and 100 percent at 80 uM**, reversed by excess HSC70 (21173017). This is a functional inhibition measure, not a Kd; the two are not converted or ranked against each other. The peptide **aggregates at 50 uM or more** in culture medium and PBS (19390596), inside the functional range: potency in cells may be aggregate-confounded.
  * Cells: in lupus-mouse (MRL/lpr) splenic B cells, LC3-II and p62 rose and flux fell "at the autolysosome stage"; HSC70 level fell 32 percent in treated B cells; Hsp70 not affected (21173017). The authors compare the stage to chloroquine's and lay out chaperone-mediated autophagy only as a model (Figure 5C).
* **Mechanism on a missense SDR-domain protein: what the primary sources say.** Nothing. Neither paper involves WWOX, an SDR protein, a missense client or a neural cell. The only WWOX-side basis is 41124647: P252A (over-expressed, thyroid-cancer lines) has faster chase turnover, lower protein with unreduced mRNA, is restored by chloroquine and NH4Cl and not by 3-MA; **co-IP with HSC70 is positive for P252A and negative for wild type and P282A**; the chaperone-mediated autophagy route rests on a KFERQ-like motif (LRSVQ, residues 187-191) that the authors "speculated" about, plus a **docking** result. No HSPA8 or LAMP2A knockdown sentence was found. So HSC70 is a co-IP partner of an over-expressed allele, not a shown degradation factor.
* **CNS access.** None measured. The one distribution study shows lung and spleen; the peptide is a cell-level probe, not a CNS candidate. Brain exposure: unknown.
* **WWOX-relevant evidence.** P252A only, not Q230P and not G372R (transfer firewall: no read-across between missense alleles). Q230P: one blot, no band, transcript normal (single fibroblast line, no matched control lane).
* **Inference chain.** (1) A destabilised SDR missense exists as protein in the target neural cell; (2) it is removed by an HSC70-dependent lysosomal step; (3) a documented HSC70 binder lowers that step in neural cells at an attainable level; (4) the spared protein is functional. Links 1 and 4 have no datum; link 2 has one co-IP and one chloroquine rescue in a different allele; link 3 has lupus B-cell data only.
* **Decisive assumption.** The substrate exists and its loss is **degradation-limited**, not synthesis-limited (impaired translation, the authors of the Q230P paper leave it open) and not stable-but-inert.
* **Counter-evidence and opposite effects.**
  1. HSC70 also folds (the peptide's own measured effect is **less** refolding): blocking it can lower, not raise, the folded fraction of a marginal client (H_PROTECT, the mirror of G01).
  2. Chaperone-mediated autophagy was not tested in the P252A paper; 3-MA null fits it but so do other lysosomal routes.
  3. The effect of the peptide in the lupus papers is selective to activated, HSC70-high lymphocytes (normal cells spared, per the authors); whether neural cells respond is unknown.
  4. *Background, unsourced:* HSC70 is essential in synaptic-vesicle recycling; a global HSC70 blocker is a neuronal-function risk and a seizure-relevant unknown.
  5. Peptide delivery to the brain is an unsolved step even if the biology holds.
* **Minimum discriminating experiment** (section 6) with forigerimod as one arm. **Closing result:** if HSPA8 and LAMP2A knockdown do not raise the variant protein in a cycloheximide chase while chloroquine does, the HSC70 route is closed and the lysosomal-pH route stays; if neither raises it, the degradation-limited premise is closed for that allele and cell.
* **Missing data.** Any HSC70 or LAMP2A perturbation on a WWOX variant; any forigerimod datum in a non-immune or neural cell; brain exposure; the peptide's activity above or below its aggregation limit in culture; which HSC70 function (folding, uncoating, CMA) the peptide blocks in a neuron.
* **Feasibility.** Cell-level probe: feasible (peptide synthesis or research supply, not verified; micromolar, aggregation ceiling 50 uM). Animal or human CNS use: not feasible as it stands.

## 4. Lever L2: pan-HDAC and HDAC6 arm (card A01 R3; survived the vorinostat reverse analysis)

* **Identity and form.** Two different objects, not interchangeable. (a) Vorinostat, a pan-HDAC inhibitor, oral, approved for another indication. (b) A selective HDAC6 inhibitor (tubastatin A in the precedent; tool compound). Vorinostat enters this lever only as the pan-HDAC representative of V1, with the A01 caution that class I action did not reproduce the soluble mutant gain and worsened one EEG count (card, carried).
* **Action and measured pharmacology (types kept apart).** Vorinostat local Guide to Pharmacology rows (V1): HDAC1 1.3 nM, HDAC6 1.6 nM, HDAC5 3,600 nM (affinity type per that file, not re-checked here; no value converted). **CNS exposure, a different type:** total CSF concentration in two pediatric brain-tumour patients, mean 75.4 nM, range 6.4 to 197.2 nM, unbound fraction calculated about 99 percent, vs panobinostat not detected (32493453). Vorinostat's CSF level is therefore measured, but in tumour patients with leptomeningeal disease on concomitant chemotherapy (n=2): it does not describe a WWOX patient and says nothing about HDAC6 engagement in neurons. Tubastatin A brain exposure: a PubMed query found no record; unknown.
* **Mechanism on a missense enzyme: what the primary source says.** The one cytosolic-enzyme precedent (PAH enu2 mouse, tubastatin A, intraperitoneal, 12 weeks, 50 mg/kg twice weekly) reports more liver mutant PAH protein with more acetylated HSP90 and about 50 percent lower plasma phenylalanine (still several-fold wild type, per card A01); the authors' mechanism is that acetylated HSP90 has **impaired chaperone function**, "thereby inhibiting degradation of misfolded protein". No chase or half-life was measured (search of the local text). The proposed sign is not self-evident (*background, unsourced:* loss of HSP90 function usually promotes client degradation); the precedent is an effect, not a mechanism. A higher dose caused kidney enlargement, ascites and deaths.
* **CNS access.** Vorinostat: measured CSF presence (above). HDAC6-selective inhibitors: unknown here.
* **WWOX-relevant evidence.** None for any HDAC in a WWOX system. The HDAC3-WWOX relation is a title only; the only curated direction of HDAC compounds on WWOX expression is a decrease (V1 R4). No evidence that WWOX is an HSP90 or HDAC6 client.
* **Inference chain.** substrate exists, then clearance of the variant runs through an HDAC6/HSP90-regulated step, then HDAC6 inhibition at a tolerable level lowers it in neural cells, then function returns. Each link beyond the first is open; the second is supported only by one mouse effect in a different protein.
* **Decisive assumption.** Same as L1 (degradation-limited substrate) **plus** that the HDAC6/HSP90 step is on the path of this client.
* **Counter-evidence and opposite effects.** Butyrate (class I) did not raise the soluble Munc18-1 mutant and raised wild type (card A01 DR-A01-007, carried); class I action worsened one EEG count (DR-A01-005, carried). A pan-HDAC arm brings class I transcription effects and so can raise the wild-type and transgene signal without any stabilisation (the MVK screen artefact in the precedents file). *Background, unsourced:* haematological and gastrointestinal dose-limiting toxicity; HDAC inhibitors are reported both as pro- and anti-seizure and as neurodevelopmental hazards depending on compound and window; none of this was checked here.
* **Minimum discriminating experiment:** the same chase as section 6 with a selective HDAC6 inhibitor and an HDAC6 knockdown as the concordance pair; vorinostat only as a pan comparator. **Closing result:** HDAC6 knockdown and inhibitor must agree; if the pan compound raises the variant only through mRNA (qPCR beside the chase), it is a transcription effect and closes as such.
* **Missing data.** Any HDAC datum on a WWOX variant; HDAC6-selective brain exposure; chase data in the PAH precedent; whether WWOX is an HSP90 client.
* **Feasibility.** Cell-level: easy (commercial tool compounds, not verified). In vivo CNS: unknown. Deferred by A01 until the fate of the lesion is known; that deferral stands.

## 6. Minimum discriminating experiment (one design for all levers)

1. **Gate 0, substrate (decides everything).** G372R first (patient iPSC-derived neural cells and the isogenic corrected and KO lines), then Q230P in an endogenous-locus system. WWOX protein with an antibody validated against the KO line on the **same blot**, a KO lane as the floor, soluble and pellet fractions separately, an endogenous-locus tag (for example a small luminescent tag knocked into the allele) to lift the detection limit, and WWOX mRNA beside protein. **Result that closes it:** protein not above the KO floor in any condition, after fractionation and tag, means no substrate in that allele and cell; every lever below is then moot for it (P47T is already excluded as a deficit model).
2. **Gate 1, turnover.** Cycloheximide chase, mRNA in parallel, with: chloroquine and NH4Cl, bafilomycin A1 (lysosomal), MG-132 (proteasome control), forigerimod, a selective HDAC6 inhibitor, vorinostat as pan comparator; genetic comparators HSPA8 and LAMP2A knockdown, HDAC6 knockdown. **Concordance rule:** a compound counts only if its genetic comparator agrees; a rise that tracks mRNA is a transcription effect. Include wild type in every arm (a rise equal to wild type's is not a rescue).
3. **Gate 2, function.** No WWOX activity readout exists. Until one is defined, report "protein raised, function unmeasured" and stop; do not claim rescue. A candidate surrogate read-out is G01's docking-function test (pTau with a docking-dead control), itself unvalidated.

Decision rules written before running: rise with lysosomal arms and LAMP2A or HSPA8 knockdown, flat with proteasome and HDAC arms: L1 route supported, L2 closed for this allele. Rise with HDAC6 knockdown and inhibitor concordantly: L2 supported. Rise with nothing: degradation-limited premise closed. Rise without function gain: stable-but-inert, deep recovery closed on this route.

Brain exposure of forigerimod and of tubastatin A; any WWOX or neural datum for L1 or L2; whether chaperone-mediated autophagy, as against another lysosomal route, clears P252A; PAH precedent supplement; the 2009 clinical status of forigerimod today; the claims marked background.
