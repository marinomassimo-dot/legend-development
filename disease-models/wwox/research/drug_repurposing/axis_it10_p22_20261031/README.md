## 6. Next experiment (queue-ready; falsifiers stated)

**Step 0 (gate).** Isogenic WWOX-deficient vs corrected human neural cultures containing microglia or CNS-macrophage-like cells (second system: Wwox-null mouse primary neuron-glia co-culture). Measure nuclear NRF2 and KEAP1 protein, NQO1 activity and NQO1, HMOX1, GCLM, SRXN1 transcripts, ROS reporter at baseline and after an oxidative challenge, IL-6 and IL-1beta protein after LPS (with and without ATP; caspase-1 p20 if an inflammasome readout is used). **Step 1.** Only if step 0 shows raised ROS, an already-low NRF2 tone, or a cytokine protein above the assay floor: NFE2L2-dependence-controlled activation (KEAP1 knockdown; omaveloxolone; monomethyl fumarate) and NFE2L2 knockdown, in both genotypes; read viability, lipid peroxidation, MEA network activity, SOX2/NEUN fractions, cytokine protein. Prediction fixed beforehand: arm C predicts lower IL-6 and IL-1beta and **no change in TNF**; arm R predicts lower ROS and better viability in knockout than in corrected cells, NFE2L2-dependent. Not decided by GFAP/IBA1 counts (HELD_OUT_ENDPOINT) or the six IT7 genes (IT7_READOUT6).
**Falsifiers:** no KO-vs-corrected difference in ROS or NRF2 tone and no cytokine protein (both arms fail); activation changes nothing while NFE2L2 knockdown also changes nothing (inert axis); an effect that survives NFE2L2 knockdown (off-target).
Research priority MEDIUM (cheap, informative whichever way, and it answers the open Q5 role question for every cytokine lever). Development requirements HIGH (paediatric, perinatal-exposure, interaction limits above).
