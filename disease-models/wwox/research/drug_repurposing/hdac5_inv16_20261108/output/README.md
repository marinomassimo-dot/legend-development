## Answer first: the three questions stay apart, and none is answered for a WWOX-relevant system

1. **Q1, does modifying HDAC5 change SLC2A1?** DATO: one origin, knockdown in human primary muscle cells, lower GLUT1 with higher GLUT4 and uptake (PMID 22991226, abstract only, H07). The drug arm (Scriptaid, pan-HDAC) names GLUT4, not GLUT1 (H08). DATO, counter: HDAC4 in mouse heart holds GLUT1 down, the opposite direction (H11, H12; abstracts). INFERENZA: the sign is paralogue- and tissue-dependent; the edge is a muscle knockdown association. NON RISOLTO: any neural or human neural readout. Not found after Q01, Q04, Q05 (PubMed, Europe PMC titles), 2026-11-08.
2. **Q2, which function?** DATO: for another output in rat neurons, loss of catalytic function that kept HDAC3 binding abolished a transcript and a behaviour (PMID 39923817, existing partial read; H09); D870N is used only in the activity assay; authors keep "another factor" open (H10). DATO: HDAC4 reporter repression does not follow enzyme activity (H04, H06). Nothing exists for SLC2A1. IPOTESI: a non-catalytic route through HIF-1alpha is plausible for a class IIa protein, shown for HDAC7 only (H13, H14, abstract); testable by reading HIF1A and EPAS1 beside SLC2A1.
3. **Q3, does the change help in WWOX deficiency?** NON RISOLTO. DATO (module): SLC2A1 is higher in the organoid and shWWOX human neural cells, lower in P17 mouse brain, with composition, age and model confounds (H17). DATO: GLUT1 serves the barrier and passes glucose to astrocytes and neurons; GLUT1 loss is a disease lesion (H15, H16). INFERENZA: systemic hypoglycaemia, brain glucose, GLUT1 amount, membrane localisation and glucose fate are different quantities; neither direction is favoured; OCR/ECAR, lactate or uptake alone are not benefit.

## What the report confirms, corrects, leaves open
- Confirms (DATO): the HDAC5-to-SLC2A1 edge has a single muscle origin; the full text of 22991226 stays unrecovered (no new attempt made, per the brief); no direct SLC2A1 regulation, catalytic, localisation or partner arm is shown by it.
- Corrects (DATO, primary source read): the biochemical inversion. In class IIa the **histidine** sits where other HDACs have the catalytic **tyrosine**; replacing His by Tyr **raises** activity about 1,000-fold in HDAC4, and the cited extension to HDAC5 (H1006Y) is in a supplement not opened (PMID 17956988, partial full text, H01 to H03). So "a His-to-Tyr change kills activity" must not be written; the Tyr-to-His direction lowers activity in class I enzymes. INFERENZA: a Tyr variant is a direction control (activity up), not a catalytic-dead arm.
-- Not carried: HDAC4 or HDAC7 results are labelled with their origin and never stand for HDAC5; GLUT4 results are not GLUT1 results.
