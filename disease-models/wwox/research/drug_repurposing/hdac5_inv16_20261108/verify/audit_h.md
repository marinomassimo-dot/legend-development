(abstract records, one full-text call that returned an empty body), Europe PMC REST search and core records,
PMC open page for PMID 17956988 and PMID 39923817 (read through a summarising page fetch, which returns the
quoted sentences in fragments; the quotes below are those returned by the tool and were not checked against the
PDF), and the IT28, NB3 and NB3b packages named as allowed sources.

client for the XML. (2) One curl to the PMC article page was refused by the permission classifier as bot-check
circumvention (it carried a browser user-agent string, which was my mistake); no further curl was made to that
host. The PMC open page was then read through the page-fetch tool, which met no challenge, no reCAPTCHA, no
Cloudflare page. (3) The PubMed full-text call for PMC2077257 returned an empty full_text field. (4) No contact
address was sent to any service.

## 2. Three-question items

Q1 (does modifying HDAC5 change SLC2A1). "Established" matches the sources: one knockdown observation in human primary muscle cells, lower GLUT1, higher GLUT4 and uptake, abstract-level. "Not established" is complete on direct vs indirect, level and neural sign. Three corrections:
 (a) "HDAC4 data in heart point to HDAC4 lowering GLUT1" needs the H11 correction (exercise/AMPK route, not an HDAC4-inhibition GLUT1 result) and the NB3b caveat that HDAC4 sign is itself context-dependent (HDAC4 goes with higher GLUT1 in hepatocellular carcinoma cells, PMID 37843550, per nb3b README line 10; a 2025 paper reports HDAC4 suppressing a MEF2A-GLUT1/3 axis, found only as a Europe PMC title hit, PMID 40250106, not read). The Q1 text drops that caveat and so reads "paralogue-dependent" but not "HDAC4 itself mixed".
 (b) A mechanistic route for a muscle-like sign exists and is unused: HDAC5 knockdown lowering HIF-1alpha activity (PMID 26061431; neurons, PMID 34021541 abstract: HDAC5 knockdown lowered HIF1A and PFKFB3) would predict lower SLC2A1 with HDAC5 knockdown. This is abstract-level, a pointer, not a finding.
 (c) The three of four PubMed-tool hits for HDAC5 AND GLUT1 that I read (33164260, 32236769, 15280364) contain no HDAC5 in the abstract; they are HDAC4/HDAC7 papers. Only 22991226 is an HDAC5 GLUT1 observation. This matches the package's single-observation claim.
Sign, function and desirability are kept separate. Verdict: SUPPORTED with the corrections above.

Q2 (which function). "Nothing for SLC2A1" is supported by the searches below. H09/H10/H04/H06 statements match. Two corrections: the 1,000-fold figure is catalytic-domain only (H02); and "A non-catalytic route via HIF-1alpha is shown for HDAC7 only" is true only of the package's sources. HDAC4/5 association with HIF-1alpha (PMID 19071119) and a catalytic cytosolic HDAC5 route to HIF-1alpha (PMID 26061431) are reported; the HDAC5 evidence for HIF-1alpha is therefore both non-catalytic-looking and catalytic, which makes the HIF route a candidate confounder of any "catalytic" reading in Step 2. Verdict: OVERSHOOT on the "HDAC7 only" sentence; the rest SUPPORTED.

Q3 (improves a function). "Nothing established" matches H15-H17 and the module. Direction discordance matches nb3b. Verdict: SUPPORTED. No favoured sign for desirability is carried.

## 5. H1-H4

H1. Confirmed in PMC2077257: His in place of the catalytic Tyr (verbatim); HDAC4 H976Y >1,000-fold on the bacterial catalytic domain and >20-fold on full-length HDAC4-FLAG, on acetylated histones; HDAC5 H1006Y and HDAC7 H843Y from mammalian cells show "strongly increased activity" (SI Fig. 11, no number in main text; HDAC5 not tested in the reporter); HDAC4 H976Y did not increase MEF2 reporter repression in HeLa. The H1 text should specify the construct for the 1,000-fold.
H2. Confirmed from the abstract: HDAC5 knockdown in muscle goes with lower GLUT1; HDAC4 in mouse heart goes with higher GLUT1 when inhibited/phosphorylated (exercise, PMID 32236769) and with repression of GLUT1 (PMID 33164260). Searches (mine): PubMed tool, query `(HDAC5 OR "histone deacetylase 5") AND (SLC2A1 OR GLUT1 OR "glucose transporter 1")` was translated correctly with the groups kept (4 hits: 22991226, 33164260, 32236769, 15280364; the last three have no HDAC5 in the abstract). The second PubMed query, written unparenthesised `HDAC5 AND SLC2A1 OR GLUT1 OR glucose transporter 1 AND (neuron OR brain OR neural)`, was mis-nested as `((HDAC5 AND SLC2A1) OR GLUT1 OR (glucose transporter AND 1)) AND (neuron OR brain OR neural)`: 3,842 hits, a query defect, not a search, and the "glucose transporter 1" phrase was split. Europe PMC (TITLE_ABS, grouped): HDAC5 with SLC2A1/GLUT1/GLUT-1/glucose transporter 8 hits, only 22991226 an HDAC5-GLUT1 observation (the others are GLUT4 papers: 32771698, 31068415, 25011668, 18216015, 25549240, 16620308, 21047791); HDAC5 with glucose-transporter/GLUT4/uptake 23 hits (all GLUT4 or other); HDAC5 with neuron/neural and glucose/GLUT1/SLC2A1 4 hits, none reading SLC2A1; class IIa/HDAC4/HDAC5 with SLC2A1/GLUT1 and neural terms 0 hits; WWOX with HDAC5/HDAC4 0 hits. No neural SLC2A1 readout after HDAC5 manipulation found by these routes (title/abstract only). GLUT4 results in the HDAC5 literature (muscle, adipocytes, H9c2) must not be moved to GLUT1.
H3. Confirmed: abstract of PMID 15280364 says HDAC7 binds HIF-1alpha and raises its activity through an HDAC7-HIF-1alpha-p300 complex and the interaction was HDAC7-only within class II in that assay. "Nothing here shows it for HDAC5" holds for the package's sources but is not true of the literature (omitted points below).
H4. Confirmed: PMID 39923817 abstract and open text. Tested in neurons (NAc, rat): CSCS, 3SA-CSCS, 3SA, WT; only 3SA lowered Scn4b mRNA, 3SA-CSCS did not. D870N: HEK293T deacetylase assay only. Limit: "we cannot rule out the possibility that the CSCS mutation disrupts HDAC5 binding to some other factor"; CSCS kept HDAC3 and MEF2C binding and the NAc distribution. Do not call CSCS a clean catalytic-dead arm; the authors do not.

## 6. Omitted decisive points

1. HDAC5 and HIF-1alpha: PMID 19071119 (HDAC4 and HDAC5 associate with HIF-1alpha through the inhibitory domain and enhance transactivation, more p300, less FIH-1), PMID 26061431 (HDAC5 knockdown impairs HIF-1alpha accumulation and transactivation; cytosolic enzymatically active HDAC5, Hsp70 deacetylation), PMID 34021541 (HDAC5 knockdown reduced HIF1A and PFKFB3 in hippocampal neurons). They change H14, Q2 and the "HDAC7 only" claim, and offer a route from HDAC5 to SLC2A1 through HIF-1. Abstract-level only.
2. HDAC4's own SLC2A1/GLUT1 sign is mixed (PMID 37843550 higher GLUT1 in HCC cells, per nb3b; PMID 40250106 title only). Q1's sign contrast should carry it.
3. The 1,000-fold gain applies to the bacterial HDAC4 catalytic domain, not to full-length HDAC4 (>20-fold) and not to HDAC5 (no number in main text).
4. The HDAC5 muscle abstract reports a GLUT4 promoter-activity result; a GLUT1 promoter result is absent (H07 wording).
5. The same PMID 17956988 passage states that activity of ectopic wild-type class IIa HDACs from mammalian cells reflects copurified class I HDACs; the H1006Y activity is "resistant to apicidin", which is the paper's argument that it is intrinsic. Any mammalian-cell H3 acetylation read in XH1/XH4 inherits this.
