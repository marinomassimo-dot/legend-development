## Question (one, named)
In wild-type rodent (mouse or rat) neocortical layer 2/3 pyramidal neurons at about P13 to P17 (acute slices), by how much does an HCN/Ih blocker (ZD7288, cilobradine, zatebradine, ivabradine, or genetic HCN loss) change resting input resistance (or input conductance, or holding current near rest)? Report it as a share of input conductance: 1 - Rin_before / Rin_after, labelled DERIVED when you compute it from group means.
