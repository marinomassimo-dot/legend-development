# IT29: phenotype-first generation, compared with the current path (P2 to P8)

Non-canonical research material, not medical advice. Public edition, WWOX-DEE reference genotype class. Zero spend; no contact. Baseline `main` = `8c7c46cf`; current stage consumed by the baseline method: IT28 `results_s9`; this package adds `generation_it29_p5_20261105/results_s10`.

## Answer first
The functional path added something **circumscribed and conditional** on the principal case, and a different kind of thing on the contrasting case.
* **Case 1 (oligodendrocyte differentiation and myelination).** The current path could not sign a single association (546, all abstentions). With the signed perturbation table and the need's intervention targets extended from 1 to 25, the same search layer signs **97 rows over 80 families** (29 consistent, 68 opposed; marker-level and non-WWOX evidence, retrospective). The signed table (`signed_rows_case1_it29.tsv`, 33 rows) is a **hand-curated layer** written by the Orchestrator from the 106 evidence rows of packages A to C (the converter run on the raw evidence TSVs signs far fewer targets, because those files use free-text action fields); the independent verifier (P14) checked all 31 original rows against their evidence rows and found 8 where the curation downgraded the direction to context-dependent on purpose. **Neither ingredient works alone**: extending the targets without the signs gives no signed row (2,557 associations: 2,077 unresolved, 206 not an intervention target, 274 excluded source artefacts), and the signs without extending the targets are never reached (the need lists CHRM1 as its only intervention target; the other five are readout markers).
* **Its one finding that adds a carried limit.** HDAC3 loss in the oligodendrocyte lineage is adverse for maturation (genetic, partial full text; HDAC1+2 single losses are null and the double loss removes the lineage, so no single-target sign is given to HDAC1 or HDAC2), so a non-selective HDAC inhibitor has a counter-premise on the myelin endpoint that the HDAC5/HDAC3 proposals did not carry. Whether pharmacological inhibition reproduces genetic loss is unsettled (a catalytic-dead complex did not reproduce the fate switch; the inhibitor rows are Olig2/number-level and viability-confounded). It is now a carried limit on 30 proposals (`results_s10`: 20 HDAC5 and 10 HDAC3 main target; only `carried_limits` moved; 220 eligible; the 15 hand-over rows are the same, one of them (IT21P2-0005) with added limit text). This limit ties two threads the programme had treated separately (the glycolysis-driven HDAC5 thread and the myelination need). The generator caps `carried_limits` at 1,600 characters (not raised); in one proposal the sentence is cut after its scope clause.
* **No compound is qualified as a pro-myelinating lever for WWOX.** Every favourable result is an oligodendrocyte-intrinsic differentiation accelerator in wild-type cells or after injury; none was tested in a neuron-driven hypomyelination model or any Wwox-deficient system, and in this lesion the neuronal signal, not the oligodendrocyte, is the proposed driver (IT14 P4; INV-06 stays a role question).
* **Case 2 (network hyperexcitability).** Compound-level evidence from other epilepsy models could not sign targets (25 of 26 rows are compound-only). It was used differently: the **sign of several agent classes flips with the lesion class** (sodium-channel blockers, vigabatrin, Kv7 openers), so a single perturbation that discriminates 'inhibition-loss-like' from 'excitatory-gain-like' is more informative than any candidate (`case2_lesion_conditional_it29.tsv`). Ethosuximide is not corroborated by the DEE-model screens.
* **Not a new hypothesis in the sense of a new molecule.** Every compound surfaced was already in the catalogue (19 of 20 in ChEMBL names, the 20th by DGIdb); the novelty is a signed relation compound - action - context - benefit that the record could not express, and a counter-premise.

* **Retrospective.** The reference list and the signs come from the same literature search, so arm C against the list measures reach and bookkeeping, not independent predictive skill. The circular case is flagged; the five others use a sign taken from a different study than the one naming the compound (liothyronine and sobetirome via THRA loss; sobetirome's own evidence targets THRB at abstract level and the THRA link is secondary; bexarotene via RXR agonism in purified precursors; trichostatin A and fimepinostat via HDAC3 genetics). No prospective test is possible with current data.
* **One independent partial test came from the scouting step (P12).** A public acute rat precursor screen of 727 compounds (CC BY, figshare, derived rows only, abstract-level reading) recovers a direction for 11 of the 20 reference compounds (a name lookup recovers none), agrees in sign for five independent ones (benztropine, ipratropium, oxybutynin, ifenprodil, mevastatin) and **contradicts** clobetasol and ketoconazole (adverse), with miconazole and tamoxifen biphasic and liothyronine probably an assay artefact. On that evidence the glucocorticoid-receptor and CYP51 signs were set to **unresolved** (a discordance on the same question stays visible), which removed 106 NR3C1 rows from the consistent count. The screen's methods are unread, so the contradictions are not yet explained (different species, system and assay are candidates).
* **Contribution of the new data v the method** (P6-E): new data alone (B) gives presence without direction; the existing method alone (A) abstains; the signed rows without target extension are unreachable; the extension without signs signs nothing. The value is the join.
* **Out of catalogue or unqualifiable.** The nine not reached (clemastine, quetiapine, miconazole, ketoconazole, amorolfine, tamoxifen, ifenprodil, mevastatin, neostigmine) are in the catalogue but their recorded mechanisms point to targets that carry no target-controlled endpoint evidence (compound-only effects, fungal CYP51, H1, ER, ACHE). They are listed, not qualified.
* **The muscarinic class is deliberately left unresolved.** Mouse M1 genetics, human M3 knockdown and the EBP chemistry disagree (only 2 of 6 antimuscarinics enhance differentiation, and those inhibit EBP), so CHRM1 and CHRM3 stay `unresolved` with the discordance in the qualifier. Signing them would have raised the count of 'consistent' rows with a false confidence.
* **Volume is not quality.** The first version of this table counted 137 consistent rows, 106 of them glucocorticoid-receptor agonists; the independent screen above and the verifier's reading removed that sign, and the 97 signed rows are rows over families, not proposals.

## Case 2 comparison (`case2_lesion_conditional_it29.tsv`)
Arm A (current) abstains on the seizure need (91 associations: action not stated, coupling not measured). Arm B finds every named agent in the catalogue (retigabine under ezogabine). Arm C is not a signed-coupling run: it is a lesion-conditional table that (1) finds no DEE-model corroboration for ethosuximide (the T-type thread rests on one pup experiment; absence of corroboration is not refutation), (2) qualifies clobazam as lesion-specific with exposure- and age-dependent harm in naive neonates, (3) records that in other models sodium-channel blockers worsen inhibition-loss lesions and help an excitatory-gain lesion (zebrafish and mouse evidence, not WWOX), and (4) lists a class the module had not considered (serotonergic agents, fish evidence only). These are research observations, not a ranking of drugs. **What generalises:** the discipline (endpoint, attribution, transfer breakers, opposite results kept visible). **What does not:** the executable join, because case 2 evidence is compound-level, not target-controlled. The shortcut removed: no rule keyed on a compound name exists in the converter; the reference list lives only in the comparison script.

## Discriminating perturbations (P8)
1. **Case 2.** A sodium-channel blocker challenge in Wwox-deficient slices distinguishes 'inhibition-loss-like' (worsens) from 'excitatory-gain-like' (helps) and is informative without any therapeutic intent. It needs the measured sIPSC deficit (IT14 P3) as its comparator; every drug arm should carry a treated wild-type or heterozygote control and a function read-out (interneuron number, maturation), because early network silencing can cost PV interneurons (Wong 2018, abstract-to-partial level).
2. **Case 1.** An arm in Wwox-null oligodendrocyte-precursor monoculture or the Wwox-null DRG co-culture, with a sterol-pathway comparator beside the muscarinic one, would show whether any of the intrinsic accelerators acts when the lesion is neuronal. A neuronal-activity manipulation crossed with a pro-differentiation agent is the untested interaction (no source crosses them).
3. **Cross-case.** The endpoint 'more myelin' is not automatically benefit in an epileptic circuit (hyperexcitability itself drives myelination; blocking it lowered seizure burden in one model), so a myelin gain needs a function read-out.
