# Mapping of the freeze commits after the isolated cherry-pick push (SCI, 2026-11-16)

Ten of my commits were published to development/main by cherry-pick (new SHAs, committer time of the push) because the shared checkout kept moving under every gate. `MAPPING.tsv` gives, per commit: the old SHA and its committer time (the time of the freeze as declared), the new SHA and time, the file that matters, its git blob SHA at the old and the new commit (**equal in every row**, so the content is byte-identical) and the file's sha256. Freeze identity is the blob plus the declared original time; the file did not change between the old and the new commit. The freeze commit f5b71a3f4 (cells and F1 to F6) was published earlier, as it was, and is not in this table. From now on freeze commits are pushed or merged as they are, not cherry-picked (ADV's rule).
