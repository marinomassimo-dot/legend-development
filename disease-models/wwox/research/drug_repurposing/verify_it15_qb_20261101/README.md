## 3. Item 7, step by step

1. "Tau pS396/S404 in the knockout is GSK3-driven": the only support is RNAi in SH-SY5Y (PMID 22193544); step supported as a hypothesis, no activity measurement anywhere. Fine as written.
2. Wnt-direction prediction by class, with the sources I could find:
   - ATP-site inhibitors (LY-2090314, CT99021, SB-216763): beta-catenin stabilisation reported in lymphoma cells (PMID 37316860 abstract), and now sourced in SH-SY5Y by CHEMBL6153053. Direction "up": SUPPORTED. The package labelled it "background knowledge, not retrieved"; it can be upgraded to retrieved.
   - Lithium: direction "up" is background only; lithium effect on beta-catenin was not retrieved here. Still unsourced.
   - Tideglusib: the same PMID 37316860 abstract reports "no indication of GSK3 inhibition at doses of tideglusib that affected cell viability". The chain's prediction that tideglusib raises beta-catenin like the other GSK3 classes is therefore not only unsourced but has a counter-datum; mark "direction untested, one adverse report".
   - IWR-1 as "Axin-pathway Wnt-lowering control": direction (down) is right (ChEMBL STF IC50 180 nM, activity 3480457), but it is not an orthogonal control: the compound works by tankyrase inhibition raising Axin, and ChEMBL records show it needs GSK3 to act (increase in beta-catenin Ser33/Ser37/Thr41 phosphorylation, activities 3480409-11). Under a GSK3 inhibitor its effect on beta-catenin should be blunted, so "beta-catenin falls under the Axin control" cannot be read alongside GSK3 inhibitors as a separate lever; the prediction that it leaves Tau untouched has no source and tankyrase has many substrates.
3. The "opposite direction" conflict (pSer9 down predicts more GSK3 activity, hence lower beta-catenin, yet Wnt is up): weaker than stated. McManus et al. (PMID 15791206 abstract) show in Ser21/Ser9-to-Ala knock-in cells that Wnt3a still inactivates GSK3 normally (beta-catenin stabilisation and transcription are unaffected), i.e. Ser9 phosphorylation is not the Wnt switch. A pSer9 fall therefore says little about the destruction-complex GSK3 pool, and the two WWOX observations are not shown to conflict. The organoid Wnt-up readout is also 4 vs 2 libraries and maturity-confounded (module's own limit). The chain should present the antagonism as possible, not as established.
4. Overclaim check: the README's section 1 says an "organoid Wnt direction (up) opposite to what higher GSK3 activity predicts" as a reason for abstention; given item 3 it is a reason for caution, not a contradiction.
