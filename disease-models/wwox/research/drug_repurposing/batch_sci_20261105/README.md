1. **The open questions were reconstructed** (`map/README.md`, 115 rows from 160 source items, each with status and where it is published). 92 are open: 28 need a measurement the repository cannot make, 11 are blocked by data access, 20 are executable at zero spend and not claimed. 14 look open but are concluded; 11 contradictions between records and three engine defects are listed (`integration/FROM_THE_MAP.md`). The trial-registry census re-run is unchanged since 2026-10-20.
2. **Symptomatic response versus development (INV-10, `CH-SB-CONCORDANCE`).** DATO: of 36 to 38 published WWOX children given vigabatrin, a hormonal regimen, the ketogenic diet or cannabidiol, 6 have both a symptomatic and a developmental outcome recorded; in 4 a partial, transient or clinically-defined improvement came with development not progressing, in 2 neither moved; no child achieved a sustained, instrumented response and none shows a developmental gain. INFERENZA: that is what the baseline in other genetic and structural encephalopathies predicts (a responder with poor development is the majority outcome in infantile spasms and near-universal in genetic strata), so the discordance is not by itself a WWOX-specific feature; coupling is carried by unknown aetiology and a normal first neurological examination. The comparison holds only as a binary (the baseline uses scores with a cut; WWOX statements are narratives). The ketogenic-diet denominator is still not stated (at least 3 exposed); two homozygous Q230P children had a diet exposure with no attributable response, which meets CLAIM 042's revival trigger for that configuration only.
3. **Vigabatrin and white-matter safety (`CH-SB-VGB-WHITEMATTER`).** DATO: the infant MRI abnormality is common and reproducible among imaged children (three independent centres, 22 to 44.7 percent; about 26 percent per exposed child where reported; also seen in never-exposed children), mostly silent, imaging mostly resolving, but reversibility is endpoint-specific in the rat (myelin content partial; regional T2 stayed elevated). The age band is supported in infants (a controlled, blinded cross-age study and further cohorts), not reproduced in a cohort whose exposure began under 12 months, and the animal half is abstract level. A cohort-level human test of a delayed-myelination baseline did not demonstrate an excess (abstract, n = 76). Siblings: no reachable white-matter measurement, open by blockage. The GABA-axis explanation is IPOTESI and two-sided. The two WWOX children are probably inside a 64-child cohort that supplies the denominator (18 of 64) and a measured hormonal association; not independent corroboration.
4. **Restoration after onset (INV-01, `CH-SB-POSTONSET-RESTORATION`).** DATO: across 39 external sources (173 rows) circuit and physiology endpoints and some cellular structure recovered after onset, tissue-level structure and developmentally built behaviours generally did not, seizure endpoints are split by restoration mode and endpoint rather than cleanly by gene class, restoring abruptly killed animals in two models, and the perinatal window of the closest analogue was never shown to be biological (the failing later arm did not measure the restoration achieved). White-matter abnormality was reversed after a documented onset in glial or metabolic leukodystrophy models; **sheath morphometry after onset in a neuron-driven lesion was not found**. IPOTESI for the added arm of INV-01: measure the restoration achieved and its distribution, carry a within-animal baseline and a treated wild-type arm, make quantified sheath structure the discriminating endpoint and pair spike-wave rate with a rhythm measure; `INV-01` keeps rank 1.
