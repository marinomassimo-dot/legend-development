## What the precedents say, in six lines

1. After a manifested phenotype, circuit and physiology endpoints and some cellular structural endpoints recovered in several models; tissue-level structure and developmentally built behaviours generally did not; failures are reported inside otherwise positive papers.
2. Seizure endpoints are split, not negative: channel-gene models recovered after onset (one with a within-animal pre-treatment baseline in adults); a kinase and a ligase model did not under conditional re-expression; antisense reactivation of the ligase locus rescued provoked susceptibility and the EEG rhythm but not poly-spike frequency; an adult synaptic-regulator restoration abolished interictal spiking. Gene class correlates with the split and is not established as its cause; WWOX is on the non-channel side. Spike-wave discharge rate is an interictal network measure.
3. White matter after onset: reversed in glial or metabolic leukodystrophy models (vacuolation and water, motor deficit; one abstract-level systemic arm at P20); sheath morphometry after onset in a neuron-driven lesion was not found; where the sheath was measured (pre-onset arms) it moved least.
4. The perinatal-only window in a short-lived null is not shown to be biological: the failing later arm never measured the restoration achieved; other short-lived nulls were treated later with benefit.
5. Restoring abruptly killed animals in two models; a treated wild-type arm is needed because interventions changed behaviour in normal animals.
6. Human timing precedents (SMA, MLD) associate stage with outcome and never separate it from eligibility.
