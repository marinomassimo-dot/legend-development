## Answer first

1. **Substrate.** The signed layer examined here is **31 curated rows (9 compound rows, 22 genetic) and a 20-compound comparison (15 favourable, 5 adverse)**. The 216-row layer quoted in IT29's README is **not committed**; no number is reproduced on it here (the "106 of 137 consistent rows are glucocorticoid-receptor agonists from one study" remains IT29's statement).
2. **Documented EBP activity.** Four favourable compounds have documented EBP inhibition in a primary source (benztropine, clemastine, tamoxifen: Hubler 2018; quetiapine: one main-text sentence in Sax 2022, cell sterol profiling, figures/tables/supplement unreached). A fifth, **ifenprodil**, has database binding rows only (nanomolar emopamil-site Ki in guinea-pig liver membranes, six rows, plus one human EBP row), and **Hubler attributes it to TM7SF2** ("affinity for TM7SF2 (ifenprodil)"): the two sources differ in attribution and give no functional EBP datum for it in OPCs.
3. **The four-way split of the 15 favourable compounds (5 / 3 / 3 / 4).** (i) **EBP documented** (four, plus ifenprodil binding-only); (ii) **indirect sterol-profile negatives** (three, single-source OPC profiles, not enzyme assays): clobetasol (Sax: "the only compound that did not inhibit cholesterol synthesis"), liothyronine and bexarotene (Hubler: "minimal effects on sterol levels in OPCs"); (iii) **another sterol enzyme with EBP itself unmeasured** (three): amorolfine (a TM7SF2/sterol 14-reductase inhibitor in Hubler and Sax; the text reports 14-dehydrozymostenol and zymostenol accumulation for amorolfine and TASIN-1 together and does not say which compound gives which), miconazole and ketoconazole (CYP51); (iv) **EBP unmeasured** (four): atropine, ipratropium, oxybutynin, sobetirome. Atropine, ipratropium and oxybutynin are named neither in Hubler's open full text nor in the main text of Sax that was read, and have no row in the 334 ChEMBL EBP-orthologue rows or in a keyword scan of the assay descriptions of 28,353 activity rows of 23 parent ids: **unmeasured, not inactive**. Hubler's four inert antimuscarinics are unnamed in the text and probably differ from these compounds (INFERENZA), so a conflict with the screen hits is not shown. No azole EBP datum and no mammalian amorolfine EBP datum was found; a fungal delta8-delta7 isomerase inhibition by phenylmorpholines exists at abstract level and **one** ChEMBL row gives fenpropimorph a Ki of 30 nM at human EBP ("close analogue" is the verifier's inference): an amorolfine EBP effect is plausible and unmeasured (IPOTESI).
4. **Evidence rows (not an independent share).** Of 22 unique favourable evidence rows, **11 (12 by row text, since R08's text names benztropine) involve a documented compound**; they come from **7 papers, 5 with such a compound**; R08, R14 and R36 are shared; 7 of the 11 have no target control. Neither "5 of 15" nor "11 of 22" is a stand-alone summary.
5. **The 9 compound rows of the 31-row layer.** S1-06 and S1-07 (EBP) are EBP by design; S1-05 (CYP51A1) and S1-08 (TM7SF2) are other sterol enzymes by design, with EBP unmeasured for their compounds (not cleared); S1-09 (NR3C1: clobetasol) has an indirect sterol-profile negative; S1-10 and S1-11 (RXRA, RXRG) are **retinoids** (9-cis retinoic acid, HX630, PA024; R42) whose sterol profile is unmeasured in the sources read; S1-02 and S1-04 (CHRM1, CHRM3 compounds) are already `context_dependent` with the contested attribution in the curation note. The genetic muscarinic rows S1-01 (CHRM1 loss; culture, with an uninjured adult in vivo null) and S1-03 (CHRM3 loss; abstract level) are favourable, with the ceiling caveat in S1-01. **Nothing is said here about the 22 genetic rows or the 216-row layer, and the EBP-share of the layer is not shown to be small or large.**
6. **The decision (INFERENZA).** Marker-level rows for clemastine, benztropine, tamoxifen and quetiapine (and ifenprodil by binding) **carry a documented EBP route and must carry an EBP-confound limit**; EBP is unmeasured for atropine, ipratropium, oxybutynin and sobetirome; for clobetasol, liothyronine and bexarotene an indirect sterol negative lowers but does not remove the question (a sterol negative does not show the receptor is the mediator). A **sterol-profile (zymostenol/zymosterol) arm per compound before any target attribution** is the discriminating control: this is a design position, not a result, and the "amine CNS" set is not evidence-defined.
7. **Consumer sentences (locators in `consumer_delta.md`).** The card F02 `identity` (card, packet and a copy in `join/cards.json`), the INV-06 `evidence_available` sentence ("signs several intrinsic precursor-differentiation routes (muscarinic-receptor, ...)"; also in `ranking.json` and the generated view pages), `needs/needs.json` line 4480 and the IT14 glia README rows C1 and C2 attribute the clemastine/benztropine/quetiapine effect to a stated class without an EBP qualification.
