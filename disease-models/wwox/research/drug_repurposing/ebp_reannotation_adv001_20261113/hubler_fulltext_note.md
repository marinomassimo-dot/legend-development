# Hubler 2018 (PMID 30046109, PMC6423962): what the open full text says about the compounds of this re-annotation (2026-11-13)

Non-canonical. Read by the Orchestrator from the Europe PMC `fullTextXML` of the open-access article (HTTP 200; the route the Harness session reported in ADV-002; one request; stored as `files/fulltext/PMC6423962_PMID30046109_europepmc_fulltext_2026-11-13.xml`, gitignored). Text, Methods and Extended Data legends only; **no figure image and no per-compound heatmap value was read**. This note was written while the blind verifier V15 was working and is **not** part of what the verifier read; the annotation tables are updated from it after the verifier's verdicts.

* **Ifenprodil and tamoxifen in vivo.** "brain-penetrant molecules with affinity for **TM7SF2 (ifenprodil)** and **EBP (tamoxifen)**" were evaluated in the lysolecithin lesion; "following 8 days of treatment with ifenprodil or tamoxifen, remyelination was widespread". So **Hubler attributes ifenprodil to TM7SF2, not EBP**; the ChEMBL rows (emopamil-site binding at the sterol delta8-delta7 isomerase, Ki 2.7–19.8 nM) document EBP binding for ifenprodil. The two statements conflict in attribution and neither is a functional EBP-inhibition datum for ifenprodil in OPCs: ifenprodil's category is **EBP binding documented in the database, attribution contested**.
* **Benztropine, clemastine, tamoxifen, U50488:** "induced accumulation of zymostenol and zymosterol and decreased basal sterol levels, indicative of inhibition of EBP in OPCs"; "we confirmed that benztropine, clemastine, tamoxifen, U50488, and several HTS hits all inhibited EBP directly in a biochemical assay (Fig. 3c)". Liothyronine and bexarotene "showed minimal effects on sterol levels in OPCs".
* **Amorolfine:** "Only molecules targeting CYP51 (ketoconazole), TM7SF2 (amorolfine) and EBP (TASIN-1) enhanced formation of MBP+ oligodendrocytes"; "concentrations of amorolfine and TASIN-1 that enhanced oligodendrocyte formation also led to accumulation of 14-dehydrozymostenol and **zymostenol**". Zymostenol accumulation is also the EBP-substrate readout: **amorolfine's category stays sterol-pathway (TM7SF2) with an EBP-like readout; direct EBP activity is not shown**.
* **The six muscarinic antagonists:** "a panel of six muscarinic receptor antagonists that all showed near-completed inhibition of the M1, M3, and M5 muscarinic receptor subtypes at the HTS dose of 2 µM (Extended Data Fig. 6m,p). Among these molecules, only clemastine and benztropine inhibited EBP in OPCs, and only clemastine and benztropine enhanced oligodendrocyte formation." The Methods list scopolamine, homatropine, pirenzepine, telenzepine and p-fluorohexahydro-sila-difenidol (Sigma H127) among the purchased molecules; the four inert antimuscarinics are **probably** four of these five, but the text names none of them (the heatmap labels are in a figure not read): IT51-Q03 is not resolved by the text.
* **Not named anywhere in the full text:** atropine, ipratropium, oxybutynin, quetiapine (zero hits). Their EBP status is **unmeasured in this primary**, as in ChEMBL.
* The sentence "Across various antimuscarinics, SERMs, and EZH2 inhibitors, the ability to inhibit EBP, rather than each molecule's canonical activity, predicts enhanced oligodendrocyte formation" is the authors' conclusion (EpiSC/OPC monoculture, n = 2 wells); it concerns the compounds they tested.
