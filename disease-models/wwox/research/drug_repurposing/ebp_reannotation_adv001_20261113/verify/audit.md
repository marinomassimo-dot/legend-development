Yes, and not as an EBP inhibitor.** The in vivo sentence reads: "brain-penetrant molecules with affinity for TM7SF2 (ifenprodil) and EBP (tamoxifen)"; brain sterol profiling "of mice treated with miconazole, ifenprodil, and tamoxifen" is read as "inhibition of CYP51, TM7SF2, and EBP". Extended Data legends show lanosterol, zymostenol and 14-dehydrozymostenol panels for ifenprodil in glioma cells and spheroids; their values are not in the text layer (figures not viewed): whether ifenprodil raised zymostenol is therefore unverifiable here.
