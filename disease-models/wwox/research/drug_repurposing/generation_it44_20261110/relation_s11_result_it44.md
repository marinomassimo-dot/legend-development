# IT44 P3: what the path produced for S11 (adding a resting K+ conductance), and what the independent reader changed

Non-canonical working note. Not medical advice. Frame written before the search: `relation_s11_frame_it44.md`. Mechanism path unchanged (`s11/`), activity path `activity_search.py`, independent reader `verify_s11/VERDICTS.md` (sonnet/high, blind to verdicts, 71 tool calls).

## What the paths returned
* **Mechanism path (104 rows, 51 molecules):** openers on the K2P family exist in the catalogue: volatile anaesthetics (not for this use), a fenamate-type activator, an approved glutamate-release modulator, two research activators of the TREK subfamily, arachidonic acid (a physiological lipid: role physiological_intervention), a Kir4.x/Kir2.3-class activator (tenidap on KCNJ4) and PIP2 for Kir2.1. Blockers are correctly EXCLUDED for this relation (Ba2+, Cs+, spermine, anandamide, fluoxetine-class, local anaesthetics) and kept as discriminator tools. **For Kir2 (KCNJ2/KCNJ12) the catalogue holds no pharmacological activator.**
* **Activity path (1,039 molecules; 664 outside screening deposits; 30 with a catalogue status):** mostly IC50 rows (blockers); two EC50 pointers (the glutamate-release modulator and a fenamate), both on TREK-1 and both from a secondary review document, not a primary paper; no direction is taken from them. KCNK4 (TRAAK), KCNK1, KCNK13, KCNJ4 and KCNJ12 were unread (no target in the cached index).

## What the independent reader established (and corrected)
* **Premise that changes the question.** TREK-1 is only weakly Ba2+-sensitive (IC50 0.56 mM in oocytes; no effect at 0.8 mM in CHO cells), TREK-2 36 percent blocked at 2 mM, TRAAK only partially at high concentration; TWIK-1 is the one K2P with an IC50 in the window and the sources disagree (100 versus 960 micromolar); TASK-1, TASK-3 and THIK-1 not reached. A resting conductance lost at 200 micromolar Ba2+ or less therefore points to Kir (or TWIK-1) rather than to TREK or TRAAK, and **the K2P openers in the catalogue act on exactly the Ba2+-weak subfamily**. The only Kir2 opener reached (zacopride) is Kir2.1-only (not Kir2.2 or Kir2.3), cardiac and CHO data, no neuronal data. No Kir2.2/2.3 opener was reached (abstract searches only: not reached, not absent).
* **Opener caveats.** The approved K2P-active compound needs about 100 micromolar for K2P activation, blocks sodium channels at 1-5 micromolar and inhibits glutamate release; its TREK-1 activation is transient (cAMP/PKA) while TRAAK activation is sustained. The research activator BL-1249: EC50 5.5, 8.0 and 48 micromolar on TREK-1, TREK-2 and TRAAK, no effect up to about 80 micromolar on TWIK-1, TASK-1/2/3, THIK-1 or TRESK; Kv, Kir and hERG selectivity not reached. No K2P or Kir activator with an input-resistance effect in cortical pyramidal neurons was reached.
* **Correction of my own brief:** I gave the reader a wrong ChEMBL target id (it was Kir2.1, not TREK-1); the reader found the rows on the right target by itself. The pointer table was built from the target symbols and is not affected.
