# IT21 P2: where WWOX-guided hypothesis generation breaks today, and a refreshed generator run (2026-11-03)

Non-canonical research material. Actor `orchestrator-delegate` (package P0+P2), task IT21, branch `task/it21-p0p2`, baseline `main` = `5771e94f`. **Not medical advice.**
Every proposal below is a conditional hypothesis (compound, action, biological context, hypothesised benefit, discriminating experiment). A compatible action of a context-free
regulatory network is not a predicted benefit; an RNA change is never read as target activity; a conditional hypothesis (H_HARM / H_PROTECT) is a research question with the
measurement that resolves its role, and **this pipeline cannot call it qualified** (section 5). Public edition: the subject is the WWOX-DEE reference genotype class, never a person.
Nothing here edits the four scientific current files, a registry, a card, a need, the ranking or the receipt ledger. Zero spend, no external contact, no registration.

## 3. Results

**Control.** The unguarded rebuild on today's catalogue and the same engine reproduce the IT10 v4 seed-specific list exactly (1,202 and 1,202 rows, identical keys; `results/control_reproduction.json`). The differences below are therefore due to the guards and to nothing else.

**Guards.** 1,202 seed-specific rows become 980: **235 withdrawn** (19.6 percent), 13 gained. Of the 235, 231 are a direction withdrawn at the row's own main target (`results/withdrawn_vs_it10.tsv`, column `cause`); by what withdrew it: 139 republished copies of a ChEMBL claim whose level the copy does not carry, 80 group/family/interaction records written above the gene, 12 records whose family disagrees on the action or whose target is a glue substrate receptor (P8-fix, below), 4 other. 120 of the 235 are the same compound still seed-specific in a twin family (the catalogue holds one compound twice), so **115 compound-at-set-and-arm rows lose their novelty outright** and 120 only lose a duplicate. The 13 gained rows are re-ranking effects of a different action set, not findings (`results/gained_vs_it10.tsv`). 3 direct-search rows (anti-IL-1B and anti-TNF biologics) lose their direction; the association stays. By set, withdrawn: Q6 expression 109, Q5 70, Q6 protein 36, Q2 15, Q4 5.

**The guards' own anchors** (`results/anchors_it21.tsv`): bepridil keeps CACNA1H only (26 targets before); terodiline, suloctidil and phloroglucinol (26 to 31 targets each) lose every group-expanded calcium-channel direction; ethosuximide keeps CACNA1G/H/I (a record at the module's own level), lithium carbonate, memantine and dichloroacetate keep theirs in their ChEMBL family and lose it in the DGIdb-only twin family (one origin counts once); vigabatrin (ABAT) keeps it in both.

**Proposals.** 980 seed-specific rows are carried as conditional hypotheses: 552 `CONDITIONAL_H_HARM`, 428 `CONDITIONAL_H_PROTECT` (no question has a sourced role, so no row is anything else). They fall in 150 mechanism clusters (question, arm, main target). `results/proposals_it21.tsv` holds each one with the seed row(s) that generated it, origins, chain, the z under the four nulls, identity, modality, earlier decision, the hand-over blocker.
Handover blockers (the first one met, in the order of `eligible()`; the cluster rule last): identity not resolved 659, arm with no stated benefit hypothesis 71 (Q4/Q6/Q7 `mimics`: kept, not handed over), mechanism cluster already examined 32, reach-matched null not confirming 48, compound decided earlier 8, modality not small molecule 21, **eligible 141** (32 clusters), handed over **13** (cap of 3 per question and arm).

## 5. A conditional hypothesis never becomes qualified here

`STATUS_VOCAB` is closed (`CONDITIONAL_H_HARM`, `CONDITIONAL_H_PROTECT`, `HYPOTHESIS_ROLE_SOURCED_NOT_QUALIFIED`, `HELD_UNCERTAIN_ACTIVE_MOIETY`, `DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, and, from P8-fix, `CLOSED_AS_WRITTEN`: a qualification record rejected the proposal in the form it was written; this module never produces it, nothing moves a row out of it or into a qualified state, and a reopening is a new record); `check_status` refuses any other value and any value naming qualification, and is called on every row before it is written. Even a role that a source states raises an arm to `HYPOTHESIS_ROLE_SOURCED_NOT_QUALIFIED`. Tests: `StatusNeverQualified` runs every combination of role (6), arm (5) and leaning (3) through the production disposition function; a fully supported toy row (all criteria met, extreme z) stays `CONDITIONAL_H_HARM`; the committed tables carry no qualified status.

## 6. Limits identified

* L1 Seeds are IT9. D1 is not repaired: adding the NRF2 organoid, the snRNA and the IT14 glia observations needs a source reading per row (level, species, tier, direction, one origin) that was outside this package and outside zero-spend retrieval of new files. The Q5 and Q6 lists keep their S084 to S086 dependence (mouse transcript, tier B; TNF only significant in the P47T cerebellum, see `CORRECTION_NOTE_IT10_20261031.md`).
* L2 Compatible action, not effect. Edges are CollecTRI/SIGNOR regulatory edges of a generic network; the seed is a transcript or protein observation; the compound's action is a catalogue row. Nothing links a compound to a change in a WWOX-deficient system. Q6 depends on one human seed (MYC) and is covariate-sensitive (IT11: 44 of 180 protein rows leave under a reach-matched null).
* L3 The module-relative group guard is conservative outside the module. A pan-class record loses its direction on every member when fewer than half its components are among the module's 46 intervention symbols; roxadustat is one case (a ChEMBL protein-family record on EGLN1, EGLN2, EGLN3 and P4HTM, 0 of 4 in the module; its DGIdb-only twin keeps EGLN2 through a different row, whose source was not examined). That is correct for a member-level score and wrong as a statement about a class-level hypothesis; unknown is not negative, and a class-level arm is a separate question this package did not build.
* L4 Identity. 659 of 980 rows are DGIdb-only families; a twin of a ChEMBL family is not merged on name. Salt twins are counted once in the hand-over only.
* L5 HGNC is the 2026-10-30 snapshot; not refreshed. The reach null exists for 5 of 10 sets (the 5 of IT11).
* L6 The hand-over rule is mine and written in code (`HANDOFF_RULE`); a different cap, chain-length order or cluster definition gives another 13. Eligible clusters: 32; 19 stay unhanded.
* L7 No qualification was run: neural expression of the main target, edge sign in context, potency at relevant exposure, labels, safety and trials are all open for every row.
* L8 `novelty_perimeter` is a name search in LEGEND cards, units and its own earlier exclusion and candidate lists; it is never a literature claim.
* L9 (P8-fix, verifier C1) Sign guard for glue-type and conflicting actions. A family whose ChEMBL records disagree on the action at one gene (up against down, or a `MODULATOR` statement beside a signed one) carries no sign there, and neither does a glue substrate receptor (`CRBN`, `DCAF15`) for any compound: lenalidomide, pomalidomide, thalidomide and SJ995973 recruit neosubstrates to CRBN, so an 'inhibitor' sign on CRBN predicted the wrong direction on IKZF1. The association stays (unknown is not negative). The rule lives in `refresh_actions_it21.py` (`conflict_or_glue`, reason class `action_conflict_or_glue_modulator`); the search layer has no equivalent guard, so none was reused. Only ChEMBL curated mechanisms count for the conflict test (a DGIdb 'modulator' beside a ChEMBL inhibitor is source noise: counting it withdrew MEK inhibitors). Effect: 6 signed pairs lost (CRBN of four glue compounds, ADRB2 of labetalol, S1PR1 of etrasimod), 12 seed-specific rows withdrawn, 992 -> 980 proposals; the engine, `withdrawn`, `anchors`, `direct` and `propose` steps were re-run on the changed table, proposal ids were kept for surviving rows, the 13 handed-over rows are the same, and the 12 proposals with main target CRBN left the table.
* L10 (P8-fix, verifier C3) The by-name conservatism of the republished-claim and group guards removes a true direction from antibodies and single-antigen drugs whose DGIdb record is a copy of a ChEMBL claim without its level (examples checked by the verifier: nemolizumab IL31RA, brontictuzumab NOTCH1, voclosporin PPP3CA, amcinonide and fluorometholone NR3C1, ethynodiol PGR; 115 of the withdrawn compound rows now have no surviving twin). No by-name allow list was added: the existing guard has no per-compound waiver other than the documented-level rows of `coverage/source_artefacts.tsv`, and a list in this module would be a second place where the rule lives. It is a known conservative limit: antibody and steroid classes are systematically under-signalled here, their association stays, and the way to restore a direction is a documented-level row (`target_level_documented:`) with its evidence, which the refresh already honours.
