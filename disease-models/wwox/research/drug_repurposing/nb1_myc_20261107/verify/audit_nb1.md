## 4. Levers

**L1 (reduce MYC, graded).**
- Missing premise is internally inconsistent with the evidence the module itself cites. It says MYC protein in radial glia and in MYC+ SOX2+ cells above the corrected line is "unmeasured in WWOX systems". C01/E02 record that the MYC+ fraction of SOX2+ cells (protein, immunostaining) is raised in KO, SCAR12 and WOREE organoids, and the paper adds immunoblot and immunostaining in KO 2D NSCs (Fig 3L; Supp 5B-C). What is unmeasured is the per-cell MYC amount or activity in radial glia, MYC in a rescued/corrected arm, and any MYCN/MYCL comparison. Reword accordingly.
- Expected result and "wrong if": consistent, and the "same lowering in corrected organoids" control is the right generic-acceleration test; per the prior manifest the published A51 experiment has no such arm (WT NT and WT DMSO only), so the lever's control is not available in the source. "Progenitor fraction falls" and "no loss of SOX2+ pool" are compatible only if absolute counts are measured, as the lever states.
- Tools: EN4 data match the source (50 uM per legend, continuous exposure, one line, reduces c-MYC protein). Three corrections: (a) "two other tools failed": 10074-G5 is a MYC/MAX dimerisation inhibitor and was not expected to lower abundance, so it is "did not meet the abundance criterion", not "failed"; (b) EN4 is identified (H2) and C171 is not conserved in MYCN or MYCL; (c) 50 uM is the highest concentration used in the EN4 discovery paper (its solubility limit; IC50 6.7 uM biochemical, 2.8 uM reporter), so "tolerable concentrations" should carry the number.
- "Compound justified: not justified as a name; genetic lowering is the justified first tool": consistent with the evidence. Nothing is stated as a favourable prediction; both expected results are conditional.

**L2 (restore cell-cycle timing without changing MYC).**
- "Wrong if ... as with an antimitotic in the source" uses E27 as precedent; the nocodazole experiment cannot discriminate (microtubule poison during neurite outgrowth). Remove it as a precedent.
- Missing premise says the cell-cycle shift may not be robust because density curves are near-superimposed; the same paper has an orthogonal protein-level readout (pH3+ SOX2+ fraction raised in KO and WOREE, Supp 6A-B), and Fig 4C shows S fraction up 1.7-fold. State both.
- "No compound justified": consistent with E50 (G1 shortening only).

### H2 (EN4 identity)

- 41842973 says (Results): "EN4, which targets cysteine 171 of c-MYC within an intrinsically disordered region, reducing c-MYC and MAX thermal stability and inhibiting MYC transcriptional activity (47)"; reference 47 is Boike L et al., Cell Chem Biol 2021;28:4-13, doi 10.1016/j.chembiol.2020.09.001. Used at 50 uM (legend Fig 6F, H), time-dependent c-MYC fall with NGN2 rise; "continuous EN4 ... nearly to the same degree as correcting the allele"; "limited to the NPC stage had no effect". Single patient line.
- An open route does identify it: the PubMed record for PMID 32966806 (PMC7854864) is the discovery paper. The abstract: a covalent ligand EN4 that "targets cysteine 171 of MYC within a predicted intrinsically disordered region", reduces MYC/MAX thermal stability, inhibits MYC transcriptional activity, "impairs tumorigenesis". From the PMC author manuscript (read by keyword search of the returned text, not end to end): cysteine-reactive acrylamide warhead; IC50 6.7 uM (MYC/MAX DNA binding, biochemical), 2.8 uM (MYC luciferase reporter); 50 uM was the maximum concentration used (solubility limit; not soluble at 100 uM); "this C171 on c-MYC is not conserved in N-MYC or L-MYC and may suggest that EN4 may be selective for c-MYC" (stated as a possibility; not tested); proteome-wide cysteine profiling in 231MFP breast cancer cells saw EN4 selective across more than 1400 peptides with 8 other off-targets, including JUN (C99); MYC itself was not observed in that experiment. So identity and a selectivity hypothesis are available; potency is moderate and the 41842973 dose is at the ceiling.

## 6. Omitted decisive points

1. S0 contains an activity-level readout the items do not use: MYC ChIP-seq in KO and WT hESC-derived NSCs (Fig 4J; Supp 7C-E). In WT, MYC binding is enriched at promoters of early-postmitotic-neuron genes (BACH2, CACNA1E, LRRC7, CNTN3, SOX11) and that enrichment is "lost in WWOX-KO NSCs" (KO mean density about 1.1 vs WT about 1.85 on the Fig 4J scale), while DKC1 and CSDE binding is stronger in KO. That is binding lost at neuronal genes with MYC protein high, not a clean gain of activity. Also a MYC regulon (positive targets) enrichment in high-potency cells (Fig 4I): a signature read, correlated with CytoTRACE2 potency, not activity.
2. MYC protein exists beyond the SOX2+ fraction (immunoblot in 2D NSCs; unguided KO organoids), which contradicts L1's "unmeasured".
3. The DE in Fig 3F is per cell (Wilcoxon), so the capped significance is cell-level pseudoreplication; E06 says "pseudobulk".
4. Alternative upstream or parallel drivers measured in the same paper: Wnt (TCF4 raised, Supp 6F; Wnt enrichment in high-potency cells, Fig 4I) and p53 (high in KO NSCs, Supp 6E). A51 hits Wnt, CKIalpha and CDK7/9 as well, so the A51 rescue cannot be attributed to MYC. The module's items say "not MYC-selective" but do not name Wnt as a competing explanation.
5. Sign contrast not drawn in the chain: in chick (E11) and mouse NSC (E30) tissue models MYC gain gives more neurons and fewer progenitors; in WWOX KO organoids MYC is up while neurons are down and RGs up. Transferring the gain-of-function model predicts the wrong sign, which argues the MYC rise is compensatory, ineffective or counterbalanced (or that MYC loss is the lever with the sign hazard of T01).
