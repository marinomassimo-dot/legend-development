# NB-1: MYC in WWOX-deficient progenitors, from observation to role to action

Label nb1-p, 2026-11-07. Class tags in the tables: wwox_system, external_mechanistic_or_methodological, computational, transfer_inference. Not medical advice; no dose or regimen is stated.

## Answer first

- DATO: the starting paper (PMID 42397075, cited by its complete read, not re-read) measures MYC as a transcript in radial glia (single cell) and as a fraction of SOX2+ cells (organoids, week 16, single-digit n); it has no selective MYC perturbation. The only perturbation is a multi-kinase inhibitor (A51), and the neuron-stage AAV9-WWOX add-back restores layer markers without changing radial-glia genes. In the SCAR12-class line MYC is up with a wild-type-like radial-glia fraction; POU5F1 is also among the genes with capped significance (MYC effect about twice POU5F1's; per-cell test) (rows E01 to E08, E80 to E83).
- DATO: in the routes searched, the only model in which lowering MYC changes a progenitor maturation phenotype is a human progenitor and spheroid system with a different upstream lesion (L-2-hydroxyglutarate excess). There shRNA to MYC doubled neurite length and restored calcium responses; a tool lowering c-MYC worked only with continuous exposure; a microtubule poison did not restore neurite growth, which cannot test proliferation correction; MYCN was raised only modestly against a more than 12-fold rise for MYC. One donor line carries those experiments (E22, E24, E25, E27, E29; EN4 identity E82).
- DATO: elsewhere MYC is not simply a progenitor-maintenance factor. In chick tissue MYC or MYCN gain drives neurogenesis and exhausts the radial-glial pool, while loss lowers neuron output; in mouse early loss shrinks the pool and late loss is reported with earlier astrocyte differentiation of glial-biased cells (null comparisons unpublished in the source); MYCN loss is compensated by c-myc in chick (E10 to E16, E30 to E34, E40).
- DATO: a WWOX to MYC relation exists outside brain only (Wwox and Trp53 double deletion raises Myc and WWOX restoration lowers it, contrast against Trp53 deletion alone; breast cancer cell lines, abstract level) (E60 to E63).
- INFERENZA: nothing separates the working categories yet. The MYC rise is compatible with a marker of retained progenitor state, a difference of composition, a consequence of an upstream change, a compensatory response and a driver; the evidence for "driver" is transferred, not WWOX evidence. The category for MYC in WWOX-deficient progenitors is therefore unresolved or context-dependent (C01 to C06, T02).
- INFERENZA: raised MYC cannot be called harmful, and lowering it cannot be called therapeutic, on this evidence; in several models lowering shrinks or redirects the pool. A lever aimed at MYC has an explicit unmeasured risk (T01). A proliferation signature is not MYC activity; MYC, MYCN and MYCL were separated only in rows E14, E22 and E10. In the same paper MYC protein is high while MYC binding at early-neuron gene promoters is lost in knockout neural stem cells (E80), Wnt and p53 are also raised and A51 acts on Wnt, CKI alpha and CDK7/9 (E81), and the A51 experiment lacks wild-type plus tool and knockout plus vehicle arms (E83). The cited tissue models give more neurons when MYC is raised, the knockout has MYC up with fewer neurons: the transferred gain-of-function reading predicts the wrong sign (C11).
- IPOTESI (L1): graded, genetic lowering of MYC (not MYCN or MYCL unless measured) in WWOX-deficient radial glia of isogenic organoids, in two windows (radial-glia phase; neuron stage only), reading absolute counts of SOX2+, NEUN+, CTIP2+ and SATB2+ cells, death, glia and MYCN. Wrong if markers do not move, the pool shrinks or glia rise, or the same lowering in the corrected line gives the same shift.
- IPOTESI (L2): restore cell-cycle timing without touching MYC, factorial against L1. Wrong if cycling falls and markers do not move, or MYC falls by itself. No compound is justified for either; the one MYC-directed tool read (EN4, in the other-disease model) is a covalent ligand of MYC cysteine 171 (identity from the auditor, PMID 32966806, E82), used at the maximum of its discovery paper, with off-targets, not conserved in MYCN or MYCL and not used in WWOX models.
- NON RISOLTO: whether MYC amount or activity per cell is raised (protein is measured as a fraction and by immunoblot; binding at neuronal genes is lost), whether MYC is needed for the composition phenotype, whether the window is prenatal only, and whether correcting proliferation improves maturation or function. No MYC-selective perturbation of a WWOX system was found by the routes searched (Q16 to Q21; the auditor ran two routes, nine strings, titles and abstracts only, and agrees); the one non-selective perturbation is A51 inside 42397075. That is a statement about those routes, not "rescue impossible".

## What the starting paper's contrasts measure, and their limits

System: human cerebral organoids, one laboratory. Genotype: engineered knockout and two lines derived from donors (WOREE-class, SCAR12-class) against wild type. Cell type: radial glia (single-cell RNA, transcript), SOX2+ cells (immunostaining, protein fraction), later NEUN, CTIP2 and SATB2 fractions. Stage: week 16 for the MYC fraction, weeks 8 to 15 for A51, week 10 add-back read at week 16. Composition: fractions, not absolute counts per organoid. Replication: organoids are the unit, single-digit n in three of four arms; the three experiments carrying the thesis have no independent differentiations. A51 is not MYC-selective; the add-back is an intervention response; an AAV9-EGFP control is shown, a wild-type arm with the vector is not. MYCN and MYCL were not examined and MYC was not measured in mouse.

## Chain (full table: chain.tsv)

Measured: MYC up in radial glia and in the SOX2+ fraction (C01); MYC-high without the composition phenotype (C02). Inferred: cell-cycle and maturity state may explain the rise (C03); a WWOX to MYC upstream relation is plausible from non-neural data (C04); MYC lowering can shrink or redirect pools (C06). Hypothesised: MYC as maintainer of the maturation block (C05) and a cycle-first alternative (C07). Unresolved: the neuron-stage compartment, metabolic dependence (adult data only) and the separation of progenitor, maturation and function outcomes (C08 to C10).
