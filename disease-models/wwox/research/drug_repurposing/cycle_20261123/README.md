# WWOX drug-repurposing decision register, 23 November 2026

**11:00 UTC checkpoint.** The authorized window ends at 17:05 UTC. The six-library human GRIA2 splice-junction count is in progress. This register records the laboratory's research decisions. None of the four compounds was directly tested in a WWOX-deficient preparation by the new analyses.

In WWOX cells, measure absolute surviving cortical neuron yield alongside pathway and firing changes. For the steroid mechanism, match neuronal subtype and initial GABA current, then distinguish a lasting effect after withdrawal from the acute response. ## Independent review

The other laboratory chat confirmed receipt and completion of its perampanel review at 07:31 UTC. Its independent recalculation established that the relative myelin-stage contrast had the same direction in Wwox deficiency and in the acquired kainate injury model. The correction is on main. At 09:56 UTC the same reviewer returned a separate ACK and verdict on ethosuximide. It reproduced the third-place rank of the three-marker panel, showed that removing NKD1 lowers it to 22/218, and found no vehicle in the treated sample's block. It retained the priority of a targeted replication. The direct C2C12 β-catenin results were retrieved after that review.
