## Additional discriminating tests after 11:00 UTC

- **Ganaxolone and interneuron myelin.** A separate OPC-specific mouse receptor deletion increased mean internode length by 23.42 µm and myelinated branch-point coverage by 35.83 percentage points. The signs held under every possible assignment of the observed cells to the stated mice. The fast-spiking RNA panel decreased in both Wwox-deletion regions, during acquired epileptic injury and in younger normal forebrain compared with later forebrain. These observations prioritize measuring geometry on identified axons at matched firing and myelin abundance. They give no evidence that ganaxolone corrects geometry in WWOX. [Geometry and competing explanations](../ganaxolone_opc_geometry_20261123/README.md)
- **Ganaxolone and endogenous steroid substitution.** A mouse Wwox expression experiment connected the gene to metabolism of two C19 steroids. In the source figure the androsterone endpoint is substrate disappearance, and mock cells already consumed about 86% by 96 h. A second enzyme capable of the same mouse conversion was expressed in every tested mutant brain sample. Both proposed precursor and product can modulate GABA-A receptors. The sign of any endogenous deficit requires stereoisomer-resolved product formation and testing of measured free steroid mixtures. [Metabolic test](../ganaxolone_steroid_flux_20261123/README.md)
- **Ethosuximide and GSK3.** At 10 µM, the drug missed the two prespecified GSK3-inhibitor similarity criteria jointly in both validated 24-hour cancer-cell contexts. In PC3 the full-panel correlation passed on one chemical reference, but this high rank disappeared on switching inhibitors. These results narrow the low-concentration, general mechanism. The higher-exposure neural replication remains the discriminating test. [Independent direct drug dataset](../ethosuximide_gsk3_lincs_20261123/README.md)
- **Memantine and human radial glia.** Healthy human tissue links α7 nicotinic receptor signaling to progenitor–neuron balance. In WWOX organoids the combined CHRNA7 and CHRFAM7A contrast is +0.015 log2 units and changes sign on library omission. At the adult CSF reference of 0.36 µM, a unit-slope curve for the recombinant human α7 receptor predicts 6.7% block; a slope of 0.5 gives 21.2%. Native rat potency cannot settle the human question. Direct α7 current and surviving neuronal yield in the same human lineage would change the decision. [Independent human developmental test](../memantine_nicotinic_fate_20261123/README.md)

- **Memantine and calcium-permeable AMPA currents.** Relative GluA1 supply fell in both neuronal-deletion mouse regions and rose in human organoids. The fraction of calcium-permeable current is unknown. A submicromolar block depends strongly on receptor composition and dose–response slope. At 0.36 µM, the most sensitive ordinary TARP-associated configuration gives 3.5% modeled block at unit slope and 15.9% at slope 0.5. Current in matched WWOX-deficient human neurons is the discriminating measurement. [Channel, exposure and RNA analysis](../memantine_permeable_ampa_20261123/README.md)

- **Ganaxolone and survival of damaged progenitors.** In WWOX-deficient organoids the ventricular cleaved-caspase-3/SOX2 median is about 9%, versus 25% in controls. A putative additional anti-apoptotic drug mechanism therefore requires measuring repair and healthy neuronal yield. The receptor PAQR6 exceeds 1 CPM in 6/6 whole organoid libraries and 5 FPKM in 6/6 fetal ventricular-zone samples, but reaches 1 FPKM in 0/4 profiles of sorted cycling apical radial glia. PAQR7 and PAQR8 were detected above that floor in all four. An independent single-cell atlas detected PAQR6 in 2/62 ventricular radial glia from eight eligible sample groups, versus 13/62 for PAQR7 and 14/62 for PAQR8. In a ventricular-zone dissection subset of the same atlas, all five PAQR6-positive cells were radial glia, and one outer radial glial cell contributed 78% of the observed summed signal across 415 cells. This keeps a rare-state effect open. At population-model pediatric peak concentrations in CDKL5 deficiency, 1% unbound gives 7.4–8.8 nM parent in plasma versus 20 nM nominal in the cell survival assay; free culture and brain exposures remain unknown. The M2 metabolite chromosome result occurred at high concentration and was followed by negative in-vivo assays. An anatomical signal cannot assign the pharmacological response to the damaged lineage. [PAQR and progenitor fate](../ganaxolone_paqr_survival_20261123/README.md)

- **Direct ethosuximide evidence in Wwox-null mice.** The previously published PTZ experiment shows a lower mean behavioral stage late after pretreatment. In a new analysis of the existing group curves, the compatible reduction in clonic-or-worse mouse-minutes over minutes 31–60 was 11.1–51.7 percentage points under the primary graphical assumptions, and −2.2 to +55.6 points with wider reading uncertainty. The acute behavioral effect warrants separate treatment from WNT engagement or developmental recovery. [Severity bounds and controls](../ethosuximide_severity_bounds_20261123/README.md)

## White-matter energy supply and memantine

Two hours of 1 µM memantine suppressed oligodendroglial NMDA current by 56–70% depending on the recording solution. In the tested low-concentration nerve protection arm another blocker was also present. Separately, after glial Grin1 deletion, the late conduction deficit measured from published group curves was 16.5 percentage points on glucose. It closed on lactate (mutant 1.4 points above control). The descriptive change in gap was +17.9 points. Fuel and exposure duration can therefore distinguish two opposing white-matter hypotheses. Neither tissue experiment combined memantine with WWOX loss. [Drug, fuel and axonal recovery](../memantine_oligodendroglial_fuel_20261123/README.md)

## Could circulating steroid select ganaxolone responders?

In the randomized PCDH19 trial, the published total and low-Allo-S subgroup quartiles constrain the number of ≥50% seizure responders. The sample drug–placebo advantage was 4.8 points at low Allo-S versus 25 points above the cutoff under all seven compatible quartile conventions. Only four participants per arm were in the higher stratum. In a separate CDKL5 trial, the low stratum also lacked a larger numerical treatment effect. Low plasma allopregnanolone sulfate therefore lacks a basis as a WWOX eligibility criterion. [Subgroup reconstruction and limits](../ganaxolone_steroid_biomarker_20261123/README.md)

## Human exposure context for ethosuximide and perampanel

In Oliver et al.'s 13-person WWOX-DEE series, the medication table lists perampanel for two participants and ethosuximide for one of those two. Both were classified as having drug-resistant epilepsy. Drug-specific changes or isolated treatment effects were unavailable. In a published adult missense case exposed to both agents among several regimens, persistent seizures and no sustained overall improvement were reported. Protein function was unmeasured, and exposure to multiple agents prevents attribution to any one. Together these observations constrain transfer of the acute mouse ethosuximide result and the perampanel myelin hypotheses to people. [Cohort, Table 2](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/epi.17542) · [Adult case](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11537890/) · [Existing laboratory assessment](../../fulltext_dossiers/PMID39507621.md)

## Stage and firing control for perampanel

In early healthy cortical slices, two interventions reducing activity increased the PLP-positive fraction of oligodendroglia by 16.4 and 23.6 percentage points. Adding AMPA lowered that fraction by 16.2 and 15.8 points while raising the premyelinating BCAS1 fraction. The receptor antagonist NBQX increased early PLP by 24.3 points against its own control. This closes an interpretive gap in the perampanel dossier: an early myelin-marker increase can arise through activity suppression alone. The actionable endpoint is lasting sheath number and conduction at matched neuronal firing. [Stage-resolved comparison](../perampanel_developmental_activity_20261123/README.md)

## Ethosuximide, calcium and cortical lineage choice

Reducing CaV3.1 expression in embryonic mouse cortical progenitors increased Pax6-positive cells by 20.9 points and lowered Tbr1-positive cells by 19.6 points. In healthy human fetal tissue CACNA1G reached 1 FPKM in zero of four sorted apical radial glia preparations, and 1 TPM in only 2/285 ventricular/dividing radial glia in an independent atlas. The untreated WWOX organoid bulk contrast was +0.153 log2 units. This supports a stage-specific comparison of native current and surviving neuronal yield against the competing hippocampal WNT hypothesis. [Three-source reanalysis](../ethosuximide_progenitor_calcium_20261123/README.md)

## Timing-only model of memantine block

A reimplementation of the published 22-state human GluN1/2A receptor model integrated current for five glutamate pulses, clustered over the Wwox field-burst timescale or spread evenly. At 0.36 µM drug, periodic block was 7.27% versus 7.08% under the primary conditions. The 0.19-point difference missed the five-point threshold. The modeled receptor omitted extracellular Mg, and its high-calcium IC50 exceeded the measured value by 2.2-fold. WWOX cellular calcium, free drug and cell-type-specific firing remain discriminating measurements. [Model, external calibration and sensitivity](../memantine_burst_kinetics_20261123/README.md)

At 13:21 UTC independent reconstruction confirmed the timing difference and both modeled IC50 values. Replaying 10–20 s concentration steps kept the high-calcium model IC50 near 1.50 µM. Graphical readback of the published fitted curves matched the simulation within 0.4 points, while the individual-cell experimental potency discrepancy remained. [Checks and limits](../memantine_burst_kinetics_20261123/README.md)

## Ethosuximide and the direction of callosal myelin change

In WAG/Rij absence epilepsy, ethosuximide raised callosal g-ratio by 0.0270 more than in control rats, consistent with limiting excess sheath thickness. Untreated strain differences in the percentage of axons myelinated were only 1–2 points. In P17 Wwox-null mice the structural deficit includes many fewer myelinated axons per field (about 46 versus 131 WT), and neuronal Wwox restoration increased the count to about 105. The rat result favors measuring tract-specific thickness, coverage and conduction separately before claiming a WWOX myelin effect. [Animal-level source reanalysis and discriminating prediction](../ethosuximide_callosal_myelin_20261123/README.md)

## Ganaxolone, tonic conductance and native chloride

A 10 nM ganaxolone response in layer II/III neurons of a different genetic epilepsy model corresponds to 6% of the Wwox-null mouse WT–KO input-conductance gap when transferred as whole-cell current; scaling by effective capacitance gives 34%. Wwox heterozygotes and nulls have nearly identical mean input resistance (143 MΩ), yet their slices burst at very different rates. Native GABA reversal, spike threshold, per-cell tonic conductance and matched firing therefore carry more information than input resistance as a selection marker. [Quantitative transfer and sign-changing prediction](../ganaxolone_tonic_conductance_20261123/README.md)
