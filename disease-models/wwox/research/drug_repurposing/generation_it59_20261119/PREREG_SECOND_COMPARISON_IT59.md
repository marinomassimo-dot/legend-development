**Why the answer is not copied from the page:** the page itself lists the six in an order. To keep that order out of the answer, the ordering page and the coverage table are NOT in either arm's material (see 2).

## 3. Outcome (the winner rule)
A blind verifier (fresh delegate; sonnet; effort read back; does not know which arm is which; reports presented in an order fixed by the parity of the last hexadecimal digit of this file's SHA-256: even = M first) judges, per pre-declared category, which order better serves the decision, after opening the originals for the claims that decide the order and saying which: (i) a more informative comparison of what the measurements could change; (ii) a more defensible conditional hypothesis; (iii) a choice that separates the alternatives better; (iv) a retrieved question the other arm missed; (v) NO DIFFERENCE; (vi) HARM or needless complexity.
**HARM, defined now:** an order justified by a sentence that the repository has since CORRECTED or withdrawn and that the arm states as a fact; a measurement credited with changing a decision it cannot change (for example a conduction re-measurement credited with settling a heterozygote question); a sign or function transferred across species, age or preparation without saying so; an absence claim without its search perimeter.
**The known-wrong or superseded sentences of the repository, listed BEFORE the runs (an arm that states one as fact has inherited it):** (a) 'heterozygote transcranial motor-evoked-potential latency equals the null' (audit G as delivered; corrected: partial prolongation, 2.13 against 2.44 and 1.39 ms); (b) 'the P47T supplement is not held' (it is); (c) 'vigabatrin retina and optic cell YES' (PARTIAL by the blind verifier); (d) 'structure precedes seizure, brain-wide apoptosis' (only the E16.5 cortical thinning precedes the seizure age); (e) 'glucose normalises while animals die' for the cytomegalovirus vector (glucose was measured at P14 only); (f) 'Breton' as the author of the S-HT panels (Repudi 2021, PMID 33914858).
**NO DIFFERENCE, fixed now:** the same first choice and no item that the verifier judges able to change the order or what is needed to take it; recorded as traceability only; then no repetition with more readers and no further arm.
