# Reading debt against the receipt ledger: the count, and the rows that are mislabelled (IT, 2026-11-19)

Non-canonical working note. Reading only: nothing in the registries was edited (registry corrections go through the batch route; HE chooses the form of any tool, ADV's condition). Reproduce: `python3 disease-models/wwox/research/drug_repurposing/generation_it59_20261119/reading_debt_reconcile_it59.py` from the repository root (a reading script over `coverage_report`, `fulltext_receipts` and `batch_queue.py --json`; it writes `reading_debt_classified_it59.tsv` and `queue_start_here_with_depth_it59.tsv`). Tree: tip after `c3eb33041ac9`, ledger of 2199 events.

## Answer first
1. **Three tools give three numbers for "catalogued, never processed", and none of them is the number of papers nobody has read.** `coverage_report.py`: **310** (all CORPUS placeholders). `batch_queue.py` ("start here — catalogued, never processed"): **242**. What the ledger and the registry together support: **195**.
2. **Of the 310 reading-debt entries, 115 (37 percent) are papers that already have a receipt** (87 complete, 28 partial), owned by another registry entry: the placeholder duplicates a processed record. `receipt_owners` assigns a receipt to ONE record on purpose, so as not to count it twice, but the placeholder is then still counted as debt. Of these 115, 67 say so in their status (59 'promoted', 6 'resolved', 2 'deep-dived', each with a pointer to the record), and **45-46 do not** (27 'not_processed', 18 'screened — corpus placeholder': a stale status on a stub whose paper was processed under another record); one has a receipt by event id but no index key for the placeholder's PMID (identity mismatch).
3. **195 entries have no receipt and no pointer**: 116 'screened — corpus placeholder', 72 'not_processed', 1 'background_only', and **6 'deep-dived — registry placeholder' with no receipt at all** (a declaration of a deep dive that the ledger does not attest: the 'declaration is not attestation' class, to be checked one by one, not assumed read or unread).
4. **The batch queue's 'start here — catalogued, never processed' list (242) contains 53 papers whose own depth column is beyond catalogued**: 23 'full text', 21 'partial full text', 9 'abstract only' (list: `queue_start_here_with_depth_it59.tsv`); 116 are 'catalogued only' and 73 'screened'. A batch started from that list would begin on papers already read, in part or in full.
5. **What is NOT affected:** the growth-anchor ratchet (`growth_anchors.py check`: registry_only = 4, unread_premises = 0, PASS) does not count placeholders; the dashboard counters shown on the public page are receipt and evidence counters, not this debt. So the overstatement lives in the coverage page and in the queue that steers the corpus choice, not in the ratchet or the public counters.

## What this does not say
It does not say 195 papers are unread: a paper can be read without a receipt (a legacy declaration), a receipt can exist under a PMID the registry entry does not carry (6 entries have neither PMID nor DOI), and a 'screened' placeholder has had its abstract screened. It says only: the number of papers that nothing in the ledger or the registry shows as processed is 195, not 310 or 242, and 115 + 53 listed rows are demonstrably not 'never processed'. It also counts records, not unique papers (duplicate PMIDs among the 195 were not collapsed).

## Proposed correction path (for HE to choose the form; nothing built)
(a) `coverage_report.py`: a reading-debt row is an entry whose effective depth is catalogued AND whose PMID/DOI has no receipt owned by another entry AND which has no promoted/resolved pointer; the 115 become a separate line 'placeholder duplicating a processed record', not debt. (b) `batch_queue.py`: the 'start here' action must follow the effective depth (a record with depth beyond screened is not 'never processed'); the 53 rows are the test case. (c) Registry: the 45-46 stale placeholder statuses and the 6 unattested deep-dive declarations go through the batch route as a disposition list (this note's TSV), not by hand. HE decides whether (a) and (b) are one recipe row or two tool changes; I do not build either without that agreement.
