## 6. The one design (one page; sent to ADV before any query is run)
**Question.** Is there an independent report (not the Brain 2021 laboratory) that a Wwox-deficient neuron or OPC alone shifts OPC differentiation or myelination, or that it does not, and does the engine's retrieval for the reopened row find it?
**Decision it changes.** RP-017 (OPC-intrinsic against neuron-dependent) and the held oligodendrocyte-differentiation class candidate; the NP4G2 row (stays OPEN unless a report is found or two routes find none).
**Arms (equivalent inputs, same date, same databases: PubMed and Europe PMC including preprints, plain clients, generic client name, no contact parameter, no spend).**
* B, the engine path as it is: the row's question text through the scout's bridge terms after HE's fix, top 20 records per source (run by the scout code in read-only mode if it allows it; otherwise its query built by hand from the same rule, stated).
* S, the simple baseline: one field-tagged PubMed query, `Wwox AND (oligodendrocyte OR myelin*)`, top 50.
* M, the minimal change: a concept-level set written before the run, facets: gene (Wwox, WWOX, Wox1, WOX1, 'WW domain-containing oxidoreductase', FRA16D), cell (oligodendrocyte precursor, OPC, NG2, Olig2, oligodendrocyte differentiation, myelination, MBP, hypomyelination), system (DRG co-culture, coculture, monoculture, in vitro myelination, organoid, conditional knockout with an Olig2, Nestin, Cnp or Cre line); at most 12 queries per source, top 50 each, deduplicated.
**Truth and known positives.** K = the papers of the repository registry that name Wwox together with a myelin or oligodendrocyte term, computed by script from the registry fields before the run (its size is fixed in the run record). Contamination, stated: K is known to IT and to the repository; M is written before K is computed, but M's author has read much of K, so a recall gain on K is not independent evidence; the unique-find outcome below is the one that carries information.
**Outcomes, fixed now.** (1) Recall of K within each arm's first 50 records. (2) Every record outside K and not already held is classified by a blind verifier (a fresh delegate, sonnet, effort high, title and abstract and PMID only, arm labels removed, statements only) as R (reports a Wwox-deficient oligodendrocyte-lineage differentiation or myelination readout), A (adjacent) or N (no). (3) New R items by arm after deduplication, and which arm found each uniquely. Abstract-level only; any R item is a separate full-text task.
**Benefit would be shown if** M finds at least one new R item that B and S both missed, or M's recall of K exceeds B's by at least 20 percentage points. **No benefit if** M's recall is at most B's plus 5 points and M has no unique R item. **Harm if** M returns more than three times as many N items per R item as B (a precision loss): then M is restricted, not adopted.
**Source against method.** All arms query the same databases on the same day, so a difference is a method difference; items that come only from preprint servers are listed apart, since that is a source contribution.
**Limits.** One case; K retrospective; a retrieval comparison, not a validation of generation; 'not found by these routes' never means absent (two routes, PubMed and Europe PMC, spellings stated); no claim leaves this record except as a dated line.
**Cost.** One verifier delegate (about 40 thousand tokens), queries by me; no spend; about two hours.
**Where the result goes.** A dated line on the NP4G2 row and RP-008/RP-017. If M helps, the minimal change goes to HE as a per-row facet list for the reopen queries, reusing `scout_daily.py` and `repurposing_bridge.py`, switchable and removable by one flag; if it does not, nothing is added and the earlier lexical fix stands.
