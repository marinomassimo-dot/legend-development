## 2. B. Names and species

1. **GYKI 53655 vs 52466 vs LY numbers.** Verified in the sources: Yelshanskaya defines GYKI as GYKI 53655 and names 52466 only as the prototype; Digby and Dominguez-Sala state GYKI-53655/GYKI53655 (Digby gives the hydrochloride, Tocris; Dominguez-Sala Sigma-Aldrich, no salt); Gangwar names both in the Introduction and uses neither. Therefore the `names_used.tsv` rows for these five papers are **correct** (checked against Methods 2.4 of Digby, Methods of Dominguez-Sala, key resources table of Gangwar, Introduction and Acknowledgments of Yelshanskaya).
2. **The equivalence the producer called unverified** is in the Bleakman abstract: "LY293606 (GYKI53405) and LY300168 (GYKI53655)", and "the active isomer of LY300168, LY303070". So DATO (abstract level): GYKI 53655 = LY300168 (racemic), GYKI 53405 = LY293606, the (-)-isomer of 53655 is LY303070. The abstract does not mention GYKI 53784; I take that name from class knowledge and do not rely on it. A new caution from the same abstract: the activity of the racemic compounds resides in the (-)-isomer, so "GYKI-53655" (racemate, supplier-purchased in Digby and Dominguez-Sala) is not the same entity as LY303070.
3. **C2 name_mismatch.** Correct that the earlier package's kinetic constants (k_on 1.6e5 per M per s, k_off 3.2 per s) are for GYKI **52466** on kainate-evoked currents; the source is Donevan and Rogawski 1993 (abstract says so), not "a citing paper". The mismatch with 53655 (what the slice and structure data use) stands.
4. **Recombinant or culture written as tissue**: D03 and D14 are labelled; D10, D11, D12, D15 labelled recombinant or culture or binding. No place where a recombinant value is written as neocortical tissue. Two labelling points: the Digby EPSC block (D05) is in mEC and barrel cortex L5 pyramidal cells of P14-P19 mice at 30-32 C under -90 mV Cs-based clamp, not the Wwox layer II/III preparation (the row says transfer, correct); Dominguez-Sala's latency readout is not a block measure (the row says so).
5. **Abstract-level used as verification**: D11, D12, D13, D04 (Hale) are labelled abstract; none is used as a verification in `comparison.tsv`, except C2's "kainate window reported as narrower (cited in Gangwar Introduction, not measured there)", which is a second-hand statement while a first-hand abstract with numbers (Bleakman: under 30 percent inhibition of GluR5, GluR6 and GluR6+KA2 at 100 uM for GYKI52466, LY293606 and LY300168; AMPA IC50 about 5-6 uM for LY300168 at recombinant human GluR1 and GluR4; about 1.5 uM in rat Purkinje cells) exists.

## 3. C. The comparison

**Cell by cell against A1.**

* **C1 (NBQX).** Logic matches A1 decisions 1, 2 and 4. Checks: (i) DATO "NBQX and CNQX also block kainate receptors" is Digby Discussion 4.6, correct. (ii) The UPDATE sentence "at a working concentration near ten times the GluA2 Ki the GluK1-3 occupancy by binding Ki would still be low" is an overstatement by my arithmetic: 0.77 uM against Ki 2.6, 3.4 and 5.4 uM is 23, 18 and 12.5 percent occupancy while GluA2 is about 91 percent, with the 4 C, sf9, two-radioligand caveats. Say "12-23 percent by Ki arithmetic" and keep the arm name "AMPA/kainate unless the slow component is measured". (iii) "Surmountability ... abstract-level" is correct: no surmountability measurement for NBQX was found; my string (S1) found none either. (iv) The statement that matched with d-APV "the pair shares one weakness" is correct and consistent with A1 point 4.
* **C2 (GYKI 53655).** Mostly correct. Corrections: the NMDA and GABA-A neutrality, noncompetitive mode, voltage independence and absence of use dependence are available at abstract level (Donevan, for 52466; 53655 not shown); the kainate window is available at abstract level for 53655 itself (Bleakman); the cell "block not surmounted by raised glutamate (voltage independent, activation not required: D02)" cites evidence that does not address agonist level. Surmountability evidence for noncompetitive tools: Donevan abstract (noncompetitive) and Chen 2014 (extent of block "similar at all kainate concentrations", but AMPA-evoked IC50 shifts 0.4 to 0.9 uM across 10 to 100 uM AMPA, p = 0.0075 and 0.0003, which the authors read as "an interaction between perampanel and AMPA binding"). So write "largely insensitive to agonist level in culture (about two-fold IC50 shift for perampanel over a ten-fold AMPA range)" instead of "not surmounted". Sequence of the argument "unlike tools, so a coincident curve is less likely an artefact" stands.
* **C3 (perampanel).** The reasoning "agreement across tools at one site is not independent confirmation" is right. Missing: (i) in cultured neurons perampanel inhibits **kainate-evoked currents at the same potency as AMPA-evoked ones** (IC50 0.51-0.58 uM; Chen Table), so no AMPA-over-kainate window can be read from neurons (kainate currents in these cells are largely AMPAR-mediated, INFERENZA); (ii) the human neocortical slice precedent (below); (iii) the displacement data (Hanada abstract). "Occlusion would show one site" is a sound design but the Hanada displacement values and the structures already say one site for CP465022, GYKI 52466 and PMP at the binding level; the open point is functional occlusion in tissue.
* **Same-site statement.** It rests on what the structures show **for GluA2 homomer, rat, at 3.8-4.4 A**, density at four equivalent sites for each of three inhibitors, mutagenesis supporting direct contributions (S615A, F623A two orders of magnitude, EA07), docking, and per-inhibitor contacts (D519, S788). It does not show mutual exclusion in neurons, nor that the three bind the same state. Residues are 100 percent conserved in human GluA1-4 (Results). GYKI-52466 collar site in a second structure (Hale, abstract only; PMC full text exists and was not read by either of us). The statement is adequate for "one pocket"; the comparison should add "one pocket, different contacts, different kinetics, different kainate behaviour" so that a disagreement between the two is read as such.
* **Surmountability.** Handled correctly for NBQX versus d-APV; handled loosely for the noncompetitive tools (above). Not handled: the tool's block fraction at high repetition (M-A4 covers this).
* **"A3 not triggered".** Provisional judgement (before the phase note): none of the five demonstrations is defeated by a limit I could find. (1) A near-complete, steady block: GYKI 53655 reaches about 94 percent at the top concentration of Digby's mEC curve (normalised peak about 0.06, n = 3; concentration read from the axis position, about 30 uM) so a verified near-complete block is reachable, and time to steady state is a measurement, not a defeater. (2) NMDAR neutrality: abstract-level for GYKI 52466 (Donevan), for perampanel (culture, Chen; Hanada abstract: minimal effect only at 30 uM). (3) Intrinsic properties: unmeasured for the Wwox slice but see Digby below. (4) Surmountability: noncompetitive options exist. (5) Kainate window: GYKI 53655 abstract-level wide on recombinant GluR5/GluR6; perampanel not wide for GluK5-containing receptors. So A3 stays untriggered; the producer's Q15 entry is a fair record, but the reason is stronger than the one given in `comparison.tsv` because the abstracts were not used.
* **Measurement only the Wwox slice can supply written as if a source gave it?** No instance found. `new_measurements.tsv` M-A1 to M-A7 are labelled design and none is filled from a source. One row needs softening: M-A3 ("no literature stands in") should note Digby's partial datum below.
