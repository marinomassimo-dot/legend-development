### What the repaired path still lets through (the cross-cutting result of the primary-paper reading)
The v3 filters (record mode against seed level, HGNC identity, active moiety) removed the artefacts they were built for. Reading the primary paper behind each decisive edge shows five failure types that no mode or identity filter can see; each decided at least one candidate:
1. **Family- or complex-level credit**: SIGNOR records on complexes or families inherit to the named member. gamma-secretase -> NOTCH3 (review without NOTCH3), NOTCH1_NOTCH3_RBPJ -> MYC (NOTCH1 paper), kinases -> FOXO6 (review about "FOXO" factors). E-03, E-05.
2. **Review-sourced records**: the SIGNOR edges ARNT_HIF1A -> BNIP3 and -> HK2 cite one 2016 review (and SLC2A1 that review plus one paper); the edge is true in the primary literature (Bruick 2000) but the engine record has no context. E-01.
3. **Mode mislabel in the resource**: the CollecTRI/ExTRI2/GOA "transcriptional" PIN1 -> MYC record rests on papers about MYC protein stability and activity; none of the ten abstracts reports MYC mRNA. The F1 mode filter reads the resource's label and cannot see this. E-06.
4. **Sign contradiction in the cited paper**: FOXO3 -> SLC2A1 is "+" in CollecTRI while the cited abstract says FOXO3a represses HIF-1 activity at the Glut-1 promoter. E-02.
5. **Level mismatch between the record and the seed**: EGLN2 -> FOXO3 is FOXO3 protein stability (breast cancer cells, MEFs), METTL3 -> USP13 is mRNA stability in GIST cells, USP13 -> MYC is protein stability in glioma stem cells. Mode-compatible, but not seed-level, not neural. E-02, E-04.
Also: local data can contradict an edge sign (FOXO6 down with MYC up in the KO organoid, E-05). Suggestion for the engine (not implemented): flag records whose only reference is a review (Europe PMC publication type) and records on complex or family sources, as the identity filter already does for protein complexes in the compound side.
