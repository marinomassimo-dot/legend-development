## E-02 roxadustat: DROP kept, reason replaced

* The reason "the proposal pushes the seeds in the disease direction by construction (mimics)" (`qualification_E.tsv`, E-02, `decisive_datum`; `records/E-02_roxadustat.md`, Decision and section 5) is **WITHDRAWN**. It assumed that the HIF-dependent shift is harmful. The direction is not established: genetic loss of neuronal HIF-alpha worsens neonatal hypoxic-ischaemic injury in mice, seizure models give both signs, and three rivals need no HIF role (`hif_it10_p21_20261031/direction_evidence_it10_p21.tsv`, `hypotheses_measurements_it10_p21.tsv`). A prolyl-hydroxylase inhibitor is a **conditional arm** (H-protect), not a "mimic" and not a "direction control".
* **The DROP (as a lever) stands, on label and pharmacology grounds:** EU label seizures "common" (1.1 to 2.0 against 0.2 to 1.6) and secondary hypothyroidism; thyroid-receptor action (EC50 THRB 12.9 nM, THRA 32.7 nM in ChEMBL, from Morita 2025 and not from the label); HIF-independent actions of prolyl-hydroxylase inhibitors that differ between members (PMID 42150425); brain necrosis in rats at 3 to 6 times the human AUC; no paediatric data; and the engine chain EGLN2 -> FOXO3 -> SLC2A1 is not what the compound does (HIF stabilisation via EGLN1 is the direct route; no EGLN2 row; the FOXO3 -> SLC2A1 reference states repression).
* The ranking reads the corrected reason through `ranking_it8_p9_20261029/qualification_corrections.tsv` (an overlay; the stored table is untouched). Unit U48 keeps its class (EXCLUDED, DROP).
* Molidustat as a prolyl-hydroxylase inhibitor without thyromimetic action rests on one 2025 report and stays TO BE VERIFIED.

## E-01 PX-478: wording

The fibroblast observation behind the E-01 text ("cytoplasmic HIF-1alpha lower, nuclear fraction unchanged") is incomplete: the same polyclonal puromycin-selected CRISPR pool (WWOX protein about 8.5-fold lower, mRNA about 3-fold lower) shows HRE-luciferase higher in 3 of 4 conditions, HIF1A mRNA 1.5 to 1.9-fold higher (significant in 2 of 4), total HIF-1alpha by immunocytochemistry higher in 2 (normoxia) and lactate higher in all 4; the nuclear blot is reported as "no difference observed" (the authors' wording, not a demonstrated equivalence), while their Discussion claims increased nuclear translocation, which their own blot does not show. Card K01 and the need NEED-PDH-PYRUVATE-AXIS carry the restated text (IT10 P5).
