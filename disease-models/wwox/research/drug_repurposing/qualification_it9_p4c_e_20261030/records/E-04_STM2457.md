# E-04 STM2457 (Q6 protein, opposes; METTL3 -> USP13 -> MYC protein), mechanism probe

Scientist-b4, 2026-10-30. Not medical advice. Q6 MYC seed: WEAK-DIRECTION-ONLY. This family was selected under amendment E1 as a `mechanism_probe` (research tool, no human exposure information); STC-15, the clinical METTL3 inhibitor, has no resolved ChEMBL identity (a name search returned unrelated molecules) and was excluded by F4.

## Decision (first)

**DROP.** Decisive data: genetic loss of METTL3/METTL14 impairs generation of human neural progenitors from hESCs and their neuronal differentiation (2025), and m6A depletion prolongs the radial glia cell cycle and extends cortical neurogenesis in mouse (Yoon 2017) - the opposite of the progenitor-to-neuron progression that a MYC-lowering reading would want; the two chain edges come from GIST cells and glioma stem cells. Biological promise LOW; depth MEDIUM; feasibility LOW (tool compound, intraperitoneal dosing in mice, no CNS or human datum); priority none. "Insufficient and adverse support", not "no activity": STM2457 is a potent, selective METTL3 inhibitor in leukaemia models.

## 2. Target, action, measures
ChEMBL: 1 activity row (METTL3, IC50 4,790 nM, assay type B, one document); no curated mechanism. Primary paper (Yankova 2021, PMC HTML, passages): biochemical IC50 16.9 nM on METTL3/METTL14, SPR Kd 1.4 nM, SAM-competitive, equipotent on human and mouse METTL3 in cellular thermal shift, selective in an RNA methyltransferase and kinase panel (Methods). The 4,790 nM and 16.9 nM values are different assays and are not pooled. STM2457 lowers m6A on MYC and HOXA10 mRNA in MOLM-13 (direct route, not through USP13); the polysome and western results are named for BRD4 and SP1; the MYC protein blot is in a figure I did not open.

## 3. Exposure and nervous-system data
None for humans. Mice: intraperitoneal injection once daily, 30-50 mg/kg, for up to two weeks; no change in HSC numbers, blood counts or body weight in wild-type mice; no brain datum. A 2026 neuroblastoma paper (abstract and passages) uses SK-N-SH cells and zebrafish.

## 4. Safety and counter-evidence
Genetic and developmental: **Yoon 2017 (PMC HTML; Summary and passages)**: Mettl14 knockout in embryonic mouse brain prolongs radial glia cell cycle and extends cortical neurogenesis into postnatal stages; Mettl3 knockdown also prolongs cell cycle and maintains radial glia; m6A signalling regulates human cortical neurogenesis in forebrain organoids. **METTL3/METTL14 in human neural fate (2025, PMC full text, abstract and passages)**: inducible deletion impairs NPC generation from hESCs, deleted NPCs show poor long-term proliferation and fail to differentiate into neurons; METTL3 sustains chromatin accessibility at neurogenesis loci. An inhibitor is not a deletion (partial, transient), which is a transfer limit, not a rebuttal. Seizure-provoking reports: none found.
