## Decision (first)

**DROP.** Decisive data: (1) the CSNK1A1 -> FOXO6 and DYRK1A -> FOXO6 SIGNOR records both cite one 2009 review of FOXO factors in cancer that names FOXO6 twice (family list, generic text) and reports no experiment on FOXO6: family-level credit; (2) in the WWOX-KO organoid FOXO6 mRNA is LOWER (0.57x KO/WT, n=4 v 2) while MYC is higher (1.52x), the opposite co-movement to the FOXO6 -> MYC edge (+), which rests on gastric and colorectal cancer cell papers; (3) D4476 inhibits CK1 delta/epsilon, CK1 alpha and ALK5 within about 6-fold of each other and has no DYRK1A row. Biological promise LOW; depth MEDIUM; feasibility LOW-MEDIUM in vitro (supply UNVERIFIED), no human exposure; priority none. "Insufficient support", not "no activity".

## 4. Safety and counter-evidence
CK1 inhibition acts on Wnt/beta-catenin, circadian and other pathways, and ALK5 inhibition on TGF-beta signalling; both are developmental pathways (inference from the targets, not measured here). Beta-catenin stabilisation is an alternative route to MYC up under CK1 inhibition, which is another reason the FOXO6 step cannot be credited with the "mimics" direction (inference, not tested). Seizure-provoking reports: none found.

* **FOXO6 expression in the relevant cells**: present in the human organoid (70-219 cpm); absent from the hNPC CAGE table (absent, not zero); HPA adult: bulk tissue "not detected", single-cell class "cell type enhanced" with low nCPM in brain cell types (neurons 2.3-5.8, astrocytes 2.0, OPC 3.0, oligodendrocytes 3.2), protein not detected. Mouse (Salih 2012, PMC HTML, Abstract and Results on expression): FoxO6 mRNA is predominantly CNS, protein enriched in adult hippocampal CA1/CA3, and both protein and mRNA decrease between birth and adulthood in hippocampus and cortex; FoxO6 knockout mice are viable with impaired memory consolidation. So FOXO6 is a neuronal factor with developmental expression, which makes the edge biologically possible but not shown for MYC in radial glia.
