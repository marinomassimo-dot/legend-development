**Consequences for the module (applied in the IT13 integration):** the authors' 'similar to WT' holds for event rate only; for active ROIs (70 versus 45) and amplitude (0.044 versus 0.023) the recovery is partial. The firing recovery is not shown to be WWOX-specific (no WT+WWOX or dead-mutant arm), so it may include a transgene effect (C5 in the candidacy table). The producer's table values are 'by eye' and agree with the re-measure within reading error.
