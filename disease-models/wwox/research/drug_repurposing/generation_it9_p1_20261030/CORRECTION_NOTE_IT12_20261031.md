# Correction note (2026-10-31), added by IT12 P6; the original files are not rewritten

Source: IT12 P1-main (`product_it12_p1a_20261031/`, `text_amendments_it12_p1a.md`), with the corrections of an independent verifier who re-read the sources.

1. `seeds.tsv` S004 ("SCAR12 lines (two individuals) ... very low WWOX by immunoblot") and the `context_matrix` rows that carry it: read as "the SCAR12 organoid class is two siblings carrying ONE allele (c.1114G>C p.Gly372Arg, homozygous), a C-terminal missense; it is neither the reference class allele (Q230P) nor 'SCAR12 missense lines' in general. WWOX is 0.1 to 0.3 of an unrelated wild type on one membrane (preprint v2 Fig. EV1N, loading control HSP90); on a second blot (EV6F, loading control GAPDH) the SCAR12 band is similar to the knockout lanes, so it is not shown above the assay floor. WOREE lines show no visible band (EV1M). Wild-type band saturation is clear in EV1M and only mild in EV1N and EV6F, so ratios against the wild-type lane are not linear in EV1M. The published Brain supplement was not read." The generalisation to other missense alleles is not supported.
2. `questions.tsv` Q1 unit text: the reference class allele Q230P has no band in one non-neural line (sc-20528 antibody; a negative control exists, namely CRISPR knockout clones, and three positive cancer or HEK lines, but there is no healthy fibroblast control and no heterozygote lane; the epitope is not stated), so "made and cleared" versus "not made" is open; the only neural lines with a missense allele are G372R (SCAR12) and G137V (WCH, iPSC only).
3. Tochigi 2019 (lde/lde rat): record "trace frameshift product", not "null". The 'null' call of the earlier antibody depending on the antibody is the authors' hypothesis (the newer antibody HPA050992, aa 32 to 110, is said to be more sensitive), not a demonstrated fact. Mobility: the Results say 'lower', the Discussion and the legend 'slightly heavier' (46.2 kDa; the frameshift product is predicted +0.8 kDa).
4. Hussain 2023 reports protein only in cerebellum (n = 2 per group, not quantified); regions are in the RNA data.
5. Conclusion wording: no model has a protein level shown above the assay floor; the substrate for a stability lever is UNCONFIRMED, not excluded. Priority of N12 (ACK1) is not raised; its scope is narrower; G372R is the one model to retest first, after gate G0. New trigger R9 (`recovery_it10_p23_20261031/reopen_searches.tsv`).
