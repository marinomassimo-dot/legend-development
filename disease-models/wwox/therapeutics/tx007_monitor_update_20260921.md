## 2 · The substantive finding — a sponsor-level precedent LEGEND did not hold

**The WWOX program has a designation: `MZ-9138`** (AAV9 gene therapy for WOREE, Mahzi Therapeutics).
LEGEND held the sponsor's name but not the asset's.

More importantly, **the same sponsor already has a first-in-class ICV AAV9 gene-replacement trial
running in a different rare neurodevelopmental disorder**, and it is the nearest available template
for what a WWOX trial would look like:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Asset | **`MZ-1866`** — AAV9-**`TCF4`** gene replacement |
| Disease | **Pitt Hopkins syndrome** (a *different gene, different disease* — see the caveat below) |
| Registration | **`NCT07135050`** — Phase 1/2, the **"UNITE"** study |
| **Route** | 🔴 **Intracerebroventricular (ICV) injection** — **the same route as the WWOX n=1** |
| **Age eligibility** | 🔴 **2–25 years** |
| Design | Single dose; enrolment ~2 years with repeated developmental and safety assessments |
| Status | Started **19 December 2025**; **first patient dosed** (sponsor release); recruiting |
| Regulatory | **FDA Rare Pediatric Disease Designation, August 2026** |

### 2.1 🔴 Why this matters, and it is about the WINDOW

`TX-007` carries a window caveat drawn from the group's own preclinical work: neuronal gene therapy
*"rescues excitability/function but does **NOT** repair the progenitor defect (radial glia/
neurogenesis), largely prenatal/early-postnatal. The older the patient, the smaller the reversible
fraction."* And `discovery_ledger_current.md` records the matching translational signal from
`PMID 42422765`: the paper is written *"in chiave IND-enabling"* with a declared target of
*"clinical intervention **in infants**"*, and the ledger flags that as *"un segnale sui **criteri di
eleggibilità per età**, potenzialmente sfavorevole a pazienti più grandi"* — then asks for exactly
this follow-up: *"follow-up mirato su Mahzi / WWOX Foundation per stato pipeline, IND, criteri di
età."*

**This is that follow-up, and the answer points the other way.** The sponsor's own first ICV AAV9
neurodevelopmental trial enrols **2–25 years** — not infants, not neonates. The WWOX n=1 was an
eight-month-old, but the *registered, protocolised* program at the same company, same vector, same
route, is built for children and young adults.

**What that does and does not license:**
- ✅ It is a **concrete data point on the age-eligibility question the ledger asked**, and it
  weakens — it does not refute — the expectation that a WWOX program would be neonate-only.
- ✅ It shows an **ICV AAV9 protocol in a pediatric neurodevelopmental population is operationally
  and regulatorily feasible** at this sponsor, with endpoints, dosing and safety monitoring defined.
- ❌ It says **nothing whatever about efficacy at age 2–25 in any disease**, and nothing about
  WWOX biology. **A sponsor's enrolment window is a commercial, regulatory and recruitment
  decision, not a biological finding.** Designing a trial for ages 2–25 is not evidence that
  benefit is obtainable at those ages; it is evidence that the sponsor is willing to test it.
- ❌ 🔴 **`TCF4` is not `WWOX`.** Pitt Hopkins is a haploinsufficiency of a transcription factor;
  WOREE is a biallelic loss of an oxidoreductase. **The transfer here is PLATFORM, ROUTE and
  REGULATORY PATH — never mechanism, never dose, never expected benefit.**

### 2.2 The regulatory precedent
The **Rare Pediatric Disease Designation** on `MZ-1866` establishes that this sponsor is using the
RPDD route (which carries a priority-review-voucher incentive). A WWOX RPDD would be the natural
analogue and is a specific, checkable thing for **M3** to watch for by name.

### 2.3 What arrives before a WWOX trial does
The UNITE study started December 2025 with ~2-year follow-up. 🔴 **Its safety readout — ICV AAV9 in
children, at this sponsor — will exist before a WWOX trial completes, and possibly before one
opens.** That makes `NCT07135050` a **leading indicator for `TX-007`'s SAFETY score**, which
`therapeutic_strategies_current.md` currently holds at `1–2` with *"AAV9 CNS: n=1, immunogenicity/
dose/long-term unknown"*. It does not change that score today — no readout exists — but it names
where the first real evidence will come from.
