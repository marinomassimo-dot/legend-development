# ADV host practice — rules validated in work (non-normative)

Companion of [`advisor.md`](advisor.md). The role contract states the perimeter; this file keeps the **practice** the host has learned by doing, so that a new chat, a successor session or another machine starts with it instead of rediscovering it. It was migrated on 2026-11-19, at the operator's request, from the host's private working notes, which stay as a personal copy and are no longer the only one.

**Status and limits.** Non-normative: it adds no authority, gate or approval and never overrides the canonical rules (`LEGEND_CORE.md`, `epistemic_discipline.md`, `cross_session_transport.md`). Every rule names why it exists, its dated evidence and when to retire or revise it. A rule that has not changed a decision for a long time, or is now enforced by a tool, is retired here (move it to a dated history line, do not delete the lesson). Keep it short: the cap is about 25 rules; a new rule that does not fit replaces the weakest one. The method is [EMPATHY](../framework/protocols/cross_session_transport.md#empathy--learning-operator-intent); this file is its host-side seat for lessons learned from work, not from operator messages.

No personal or individual-level information is used or recorded.

## 1 · Direction and continuity

| # | Rule | Why (what went wrong) | Evidence | Retire or revise when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ordinary continuity of the laboratory is the host's. The operator is a boost, not a dependency: choose and assign inside the roadmap, take ordinary decisions without asking, keep going while the operator is away. | Single-source or single-measure blocks were generalised into "all remaining work depends on the operator". | Operator instruction 2026-11-18; roadmap entry; transport §12 | Operator changes the mandate |
| 2 | A blocked path is not a blocked portfolio. At every closure run the candidate-to-candidate loop (result, alternative, comparison, deepening, consumers, next choice) and say what "more promising" means: for which endpoint, model, phase, mechanism and evidence level. Tolerability or an elegant rationale is not efficacy. | A local wait (blocked source, laboratory-only step, empty written queue) was read as exhaustion of the work. | Operator integration 2026-11-16; the 2026-11-19 night plateau | A tool prints the comparison by itself |
| 3 | At EVERY tick, whatever the verdict, read `tick()["activity"]` (keys `sessions`, `idle_share`, and the `recipients` view once landed; the top-level `sessions` is a different thing). If every assignment recipient has been idle for more than about an hour and no blocker that names an awaited event covers it, choose the next investment from the **whole roadmap**, not only the strand just closed, and assign it in that turn. A plateau of the strand is never a reason to wait. | 2026-11-19: a DEFERRED tick (cadence gate) and then an EVALUATE concluding "nothing to assign" left two roles idle 2.0 to 2.8 hours with the rule present; the same text read cold assigns, read warm in a window of about 500k tokens waits. | HE's measurement 2026-11-19; the Phase 1 cold/warm pair (one pair, inferred) | The tick prints the "idle without a blocker" line and it has worked for two weeks |

Verify take-in-charge in the recipient's record, not in the message. | Directives without follow-through left sessions idle; a "wait" was in fact a refused push. | Operator 2026-11-15 (permanent) | — |
| 5 | A reserved decision blocks only itself. Reserved (operator): naming a laboratory or a clinical channel, the gate-cache use, a human visit for closed PDFs, a registry query, the engine budget, the sponsor question. ## 2 · Evidence discipline (the host's own reading)

| # | Rule | Why | Evidence | Retire or revise when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | An access failure is not a statement about what the repository holds. Before calling a paper closed, abstract-level or single-source, check the receipt ledger and `files/` (SHA-256 search) and state the depth of the check. | A conduction thread was closed on a peer's word because the open-access route failed; the full text was held and measured it. | 2026-11-19 | A tool answers "held or not" from the ledger |
| 9 | An audit row, a dossier line or a peer's summary is not a source fact. Read the sentence in the held text (one grep with context) before writing a number, a comparison or a temporal order into any brief; mark anything not re-read as "per <who>, not re-checked by me". | Two briefs bound for a laboratory carried audit-row wordings the source contradicts ("equal to the null", "structure precedes seizure"). | 2026-11-19 (caught by the Orchestrator) | — |
| 10 | A PubMed check verifies the quotation, not the inference; the full text decides. An abstract is not a read. | A claim taken from an abstract (P11 to P14 direction) was withdrawn after the full text showed deaths, a temperature confound and no raised incidence. | 2026-11-18 | — |
| 11 | A verifier's negative finding can be false: check it against the source, repair by sourcing, let the same verifier adjudicate; never delete a true clause to clear a verdict. | A verifier searched hyphenation variants only and missed a verbatim sentence. | 2026-09-13 | — |
| 12 | Before a blind audit, confirm the source artefact is on disk; an all-UNVERIFIABLE result means the artefact is missing, not that the reading is unsupported. | One auditor returned UNVERIFIABLE for all triples because the text was absent. | 2026-09 | The audit tool checks presence itself |
| 13 | A declaration is not an attestation: a registry "full text reviewed" is not a reading; a receipt whose manifest declares no artefact is not a pass. Check `fulltext_receipts.py status` / `reading_state.md` and `evidence_presence.py`. | Coverage and triage treated declarations as readings. | 2026-09-13; 3 of 594 manifests vacuous, fixed 2026-11-19 | — |
| 14 | An absence ("not found") needs two routes and a stated depth. Keep the distinction between data, inference, hypothesis and model result. | Searches anchored on the question's own gene names miss the contradicting families. | 2026-11 science batch | — |
| 15 | A comparison that tests whether the loop's materials add anything needs the same complete originals in both arms, one fixed minimum search step, no pointer to the ordered answer in the lean pack and enforced word caps; otherwise it shows only that more originals beat fewer. Run one only when a decision that the answer would change is scheduled. | The second materials test was confounded by its own design. | 2026-11-19 (IT's design rules template) | A clean repeat settles it |
| 16 | A depth screen cannot see a wording overshoot. For a claim with many consumers, audit the interpretive layer (title words, clinical meaning, quantifiers, attribution) against the anchor's own sentences; the numbers usually hold. The write-time rule is the cheap prevention. | Four of four audited claims overshot in the interpretive layer with a clean data layer. | 2026-11-19 audits of claims 025, 021, 002, 005 | The write-time lint WARN is in use |

| # | Rule | Why | Evidence | Retire or revise when |

One delegate at a time; none recursive. | An inherited maximum effort made one dispatch about 13 times dearer; later every successor window ran at the default until it was read back. | 2026-11-06 budget; 2026-11-19 read-back | The default changes |
| 18 | A long window costs mainly the context re-read at every turn, not the output. ## 5 · Self-audit (the host's correction log)

The repository's principle is diagnosis, correction log and micro-upgrade: not an archive of errors and not bureaucracy, but self-improvement. For the host it works like this.

- **When.** After an error that changed a record, a decision or another actor's work, or a near miss that repeats; not for every slip. It is never required and never a ritual.
- **What it says.** What happened; the **rational cause** (which belief, shortcut or tool made it reasonable at the time); the correction; the smallest change that prevents the same error (a rule here, a tool check, a recipe row) or "none, because"; and later whether it recurred. New errors are acceptable; a repeated error means the micro-upgrade was wrong or missing, and a promise is not an upgrade.
- **Where.** A dated note under `disease-models/wwox/research/session_evaluations/` (the existing path of the self-evaluation protocol); the first one is `self_eval_adv_host_20261119.md`. A rule that proves its value moves into the tables above.
- **At a closure** the host adds one line to its record: the criterion that changed a choice and its follow-through, or "no effect". That line is the observable EMPATHY needs; it is never forced.

## 6 · How a new session uses this file

Read it after `roles/advisor.md` when acting as the ADV host (the router row "Acting as the ADV host" points here through the role), and keep the numbered rule you apply visible in the record when it changes a choice. If a rule is wrong, correct it with a dated line and the evidence; do not add a copy elsewhere.
