# Sponsorship statement

The **WWOX Foundation** formally sponsors LEGEND-WWOX and supports its development as a public, disease-level research framework for WWOX-related disorders.

The collaboration covers:

- strategic and technical co-leadership;
- connection with the international WWOX research and patient-organization ecosystem;
- support for the Anthropic AI for Science rare-disease Track 1 application;
- development of public, interoperable research outputs from public evidence.

This sponsorship does not change the repository's scientific or privacy boundaries. Public outputs remain provenance-tracked, disease-level artifacts; non-public individual-level material is excluded.

## Ownership and attribution

Sponsorship and ownership are deliberately kept distinct, because conflating them would
misstate who can grant rights over this material.

- **Copyright holder:** the LEGEND project author, an individual volunteer with the WWOX
  Foundation, under [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) (MIT). The volunteer relationship transfers neither
  authorship nor copyright to the Foundation.
- **Why no personal name appears here.** The rights holder is identified in the project's
  private ownership record, not in the public edition. This is the same boundary the rest of
  the repository observes: naming an individual in a repository about a rare paediatric
  disorder can re-identify a person **by inference**, which is exactly the linkage the
  three-layer design exists to prevent. The publication gate enforces it — it treats direct
  personal identifiers as blocking, with no allowlist, and that decision was taken
  deliberately rather than worked around.
- **The WWOX Foundation is the sponsor**, not a copyright holder and not a co-author. It
  endorses and supports the work described above; it does not warrant the scientific content,
  and nothing here is issued as Foundation medical guidance.
- **Contact:** through the [WWOX Foundation](https://www.wwox.org/). No personal email
  addresses appear anywhere in this repository — the publication gate blocks them by design.
