## 1. Executive assessment

**What exists already.** An operator indication can reach a later decision today through six real paths, none of them designed for "learn from this prompt": (1) the *operator section of the drug-repurposing roadmap*, which `cross_session_transport.md` §12 (HE53) names as "the durable seat" of chat instructions (`transport:562-567`); (2) dated, `PERMANENT`-tagged subsections of §12 that carry the rationale and are the "operating pointer" of a roadmap entry (`transport:685`, `:719`); (3) the **recall text** of the ADV host, which quotes the standing instruction at every hourly tick (`strategic_advisor.py:377`); (4) **mandate records** whose steps carry a `source` and are re-read by the watch and by the revival request (`mandate_watch.py:738-749`); (5) the shared habit table **README §13** ("When / Do / Evidence and limit", 22 rows, `framework/scripts/README.md:294`), which already moved lessons out of one chat's private memory on 2026-11-08; (6) the **private auto-memory** of Claude Code (69 notes, 45 typed `feedback`, an index of 68 lines / 14.9 KB injected into every session of this project; none tracked in git; Claude-Code-and-this-machine only). Two further surfaces are *described but not materialised*: `LEARNING_INDEX` and `ACTIVE_LESSONS` (Annex E; `ANNEX_INDEX.md:75-76` says "pending"; `CLAUDE.md:81` says "not yet materialized"), and `learning/` (185 session-learning files, last written 2026-09-28, read by no decision path).

**Where learning is lost (demonstrated, not assumed).** Three different places, and "not enough memory" is the wrong diagnosis for two of them:
- *Provenance and intent are not kept* (class A): a recent operator correction (translation fixes must be checked on the page that is produced) survives as a technical rule, tests and code comments, but **no file records that the operator said it or why**; a fresh session found the rule and a technical reason and could not attribute either to the operator (probe C).
- *The rule exists, is findable and is cheap to retrieve, and is still not applied in a long warm window* (classes D/E/F): the "idle roles are not a reason to wait" rule is stated in five places and was retrieved correctly, with its rationale, by cold sessions (probes A and B: 5 to 17 turns, 0.09 to 0.54 USD reported); the live ADV session, with the same text in its recall, decided on 2026-11-19 07:18 to wait with both roles idle 2.8 h (its own later account: it misread "idle"). The same lesson is documented as having recurred on 2026-09-14, 2026-11-16 and 2026-11-19.
- *Accumulation without supersession*: the roadmap's operator section is a long list of dated, partly overlapping entries; a cold reader listed five stale or conflicting statements in it (§5).

**Minimal intervention recommended (for Phase 2 to confirm).** Do not add a memory store. (i) Make the existing operator seat *readable and checkable*: one entry per standing indication with scope, rationale, provenance and a `supersedes`/status mark, stale entries marked, a router row that points to it by situation. (ii) Put the *decision-time* reminder where the decision is already taken and the text is short: the revival request and the recall text already exist and can carry at most three situation-keyed items. (iii) Keep the selectivity (what teaches) with the actor, as two questions, not as an automatic capture. (iv) Measure with the probe harness that already exists (DREAM-1) before and after. This is §6 option O1+O3 below.

**Main uncertainties.** One warm-versus-cold pair (07:18) suggests that context length, not retrieval, drives the application failure; that is an inference from one case. The probes ran a Claude model on read-only tools; Codex's retrieval behaviour was not measured. No restart-after-compaction test was possible without disturbing production windows; the one real replacement observed (the SCI fresh-window trial) inherited the private memory index.

## 4. Real-case traces

Eight cases were requested; five are traced with evidence, three are reported as not traced rather than reconstructed.

1. **Continuity after a closed increment; waiting for the MEETING (traced, strongest case).** *Indication and provenance:* 2026-09-14 (idle sessions with under 10 % budget used, `transport:490`), 2026-11-15/16/17/18 operator entries in the roadmap (`roadmap_current.md:88,97-98`, "a future MEETING date is not a reason to leave useful capacity idle"), the 2026-11-18 PERMANENT instruction. *Rationale present:* yes, in several places (disease severity and urgency, `roadmap:88`; the 09-14 incident). *Stored:* roadmap, §12 (`:577,616-639,685`), SKILL (`:128`), `roles/advisor.md`, the recall text. *Retrieval:* a cold, memory-free session asked "what do you do at this closure" found all of it and its rationale in 17 turns (probe A); a cold session asked to *decide* chose to assign, citing §12/HE57, in 5 turns (probe B). *Observed behaviour:* on 2026-11-19 the warm ADV session (a window of about 500k tokens) concluded at 06:18 DEFERRED and at 07:18 EVALUATE "nothing to assign, roles in motivated wait, next MEETING 11:25"; IT and SCI were idle 05:38 to 08:28 (measured from the transcripts; they restarted at the 05:25 MEETING and at an operator prompt). ## 8. IMPLEMENTATION HANDOFF FOR CODEX

**Order.** (1) Run the baseline: probes A, B, C (or the DREAM-1 `collect.py` on new cases) and the idle-gap census on the last day; keep the numbers. (2) O1: restructure the seat and mark the stale items, no new file. (3) Router row and (4) the Phase 2 criterion as two questions in one place. (5) Only then O3. (6) Re-run the same probes and the next two real closures.

**Places to insert logic.** *Interception*: the receiving actor, when it transcribes an instruction (the HE53 convention); the minimal place for a check is the sentence that already tells actors to transcribe (`transport:564-566`), not a hook. *Where to keep it*: the roadmap operator section (`roadmap_current.md:9-110`), because the radar, the advisor and the ADV skill already read it and it is repository-visible to every runtime. *Findability*: one router row in `CLAUDE.md` (table near lines 63-64) naming the seat and *when* to read it (before choosing next work; when two instructions conflict). *Retrieval at the decision*: `request_text` (`mandate_watch.py:749`) and `recall_prompt` (`strategic_advisor.py:385`), reading the seat by situation key, capped at three items with their rationale; the tick's activity block (`session_activity`, `strategic_advisor.py:1090`) is the measurable trigger that already works. *Provenance and rationale*: fields in each seat entry (who, where recorded, date, scope, why, status, supersedes); the provenance rule exists (`transport:565`). *Consolidate/update/remove*: a dated line that supersedes, never an edit of history (the repository convention); stale items marked, not deleted.

**Tests worth writing.** (a) A fixture seat with two entries, one superseded, and a test that the revival text carries the active one and not the superseded one; (b) a cold-session probe pair frozen as cases (the three here are the seed), run before/after, with the canary; (c) a test that a temporary mandate step does not appear in the seat; (d) a test that a scientific label is never changed by a seat entry (assert the epistemic fields are outside its scope).

**A complete example path.** Operator tells a session: choose the next investment yourself when everything is idle, because the disease is severe and time matters. The receiving actor asks (1) what must I do now: assign; (2) does it teach: yes, a criterion with a rationale, scope `advisor`, status standing. It writes one seat entry (provenance, rationale, scope, supersedes the older idle wording). The next revival or recall quotes that entry by its situation key ("all recipients idle"), the tick's activity block measures the idle gap, and the next closure's gap is read from the transcripts. A superseding message adds a dated line and flips the old status; it never rewrites the old line.

**Still to decide in Phase 2.** The criterion for "what teaches" (this report deliberately gives none); the number of items the decision-time reminder may carry; whether the seat stays one file or splits per actor; whether a Codex session needs a different start path than the router; how an inferred preference is marked so it is never read as an instruction.

**Leave unchanged.** `LEGEND_CORE` §21c–§21e, the authority matrix, the scientific current files, the receipts ledger, the watch's identity rules, `roles/*` contracts beyond a pointer, Annex E (leave it described, do not materialise it now), the private memory (do not copy it into the repository), the delegation budget.
