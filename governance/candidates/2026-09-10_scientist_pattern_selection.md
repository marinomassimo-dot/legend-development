comparable experimental-context field on both sides of a relation); the nearest rows are relation-
extraction models whose validation status is `DESCRIBED`, and an extracted context would itself be
an unverified value — the class NE-4 belongs to. The appropriate fix is the small internal field
proposed in the roadmap, authored by the reader while the document is open.
