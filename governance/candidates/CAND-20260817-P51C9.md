### 2.1 · The P5.1 amendment, and the asymmetry it had to survive

`reviews/` becomes a control-plane root. `learning/` needs **no edit** to become content — it is
content by omission from the list — which is exactly why it receives an explicit sentence: a
determination that is binding, was made, and leaves no durable trace is branch (B) of the C-9
model in its purest form. Recording only the half that required an edit would have left the
`learning/` decision written nowhere.

*(The asymmetry is the orchestrator's catch.)*

### 2.2 · The Orchestrator worktree is a structural correction, not an unblocking

Annex D.1 gives `WORK_COMMIT` to *"ogni attore, proprio branch"* and `CANONICAL_BATCH_COMMIT` to
*"solo Orchestrator, root"*. The root's branch is `main`, so an Orchestrator resident there had
**no branch on which a `WORK_COMMIT` was possible** — alone among six actors. Its output could not
become durable, could not reach a candidate, and left the root permanently unclean.

It is argued as an improvement to the posture §14 calls *"critico nella root"*: the root now
carries a writer only **inside a batch window** rather than continuously. That framing, and the
reason it matters — *a change argued as a workaround gets reverted as one* — are the
orchestrator's.
