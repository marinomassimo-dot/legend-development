# Ranking rubric — legend-hypothesis-forge

Five criteria, 0–3 each. The total is a **triage**, not a verdict: always justify in words why one hypothesis beats another. Safety is a **gate**, not just a weight: safety = 0 → the hypothesis is `flagged` and cannot advance, whatever the total.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Actionability** (is there a concrete route for the case, now or soon?) | purely theoretical | conceivable but distant route | real route with known obstacles | ready route / existing reagent-drug |
| **Evidence strength** (what supports it in the WM?) | ESPANSIONE only | isolated IPOTESI | INFERENZA from convergence | direct DATO (≥1 robust source) |
| **Safety (BLOCK-1)** — *gate* | serious risk / violates a guardrail | serious risk to mitigate | manageable, known risk | favourable profile, reversible |
| **Time-to-benefit** | years / post-GT | months-years | weeks-months | acts now on a closing window |
| **Value-of-the-partial** (does a partial rescue count?) | all-or-nothing | marginal benefit | useful partial benefit | every % counts and adds up |

## Weights (declared mission bias)
- **Time-sensitivity and value-of-the-partial weigh more** than static variables: a lever that buys time *now*, even partially, beats a "perfect" but post-GT lever.
- **Translatability** is a multiplier, not an addend: a hypothesis not applicable to this genotype/window is parked even if elegant.

## Use
1. Assign the 5 scores with a one-line rationale each.
2. Apply the safety gate.
3. Order by total, then break ties with time-to-benefit.
4. In the ledger write the score **and** the rationale — never the number alone.
