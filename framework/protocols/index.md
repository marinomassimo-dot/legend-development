### 2.5 Clinical vs research tiering

Operational distinction:

- **Direct clinical tier** = clinical decisions for a patient, today
  - Change → potentially a MAJOR working-model bump
  - Urgent-commit category 3 may apply
- **Research tier** = research, gene therapy, repurposing, future
  - Change → typically a MINOR bump
  - Influences future decisions, not immediate ones
