# Learned gates registry

This registry preserves reusable error-prevention procedures learned during
LEGEND development. It contains method only: no private case history, family
relationship, unpublished clinical fact or patient-level recommendation.

A gate constrains what an analysis may claim. It is not evidence and does not
replace primary-source reading, domain review or experimental validation.

Status vocabulary:

- `ACTIVE_METHOD`: applied as a documented reasoning constraint;
- `ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE`: enforced by public code and regression tests;
- `PROPOSED`: specified but not yet validated across enough examples;
- `PENDING_EXECUTABLE`: active as method, not yet encoded in a public checker;
- `SUPERSEDED`: absorbed by a later, stronger gate. The row stays because a gate records *how
  a failure was understood at the time*, and deleting it would erase the lineage that makes
  the successor legible. It must name its successor and must not be applied as guidance.

## Transfer, provenance and control design

| Gate | Trigger | Required representation / decision rule | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `MECHANISM_TRANSFER_FIREWALL` | A causal edge is transferred across variants, models or experimental systems | Attach `variant/model`, `experimental system` and `epistemic level` to every edge. A transferred edge remains a bridge hypothesis until measured in the target system. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `CONTROL_SPECIFICITY_RULE` | A positive control is used to validate a different endpoint | State the exact intermediate validated by the control. Never generalise control validity to an unmeasured biological endpoint. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `POCKET_NOT_PRODUCT` | A computational pocket is promoted toward a therapeutic strategy | Require, in order: correct mechanism, recoverable fold, functional orthogonality, direct binding and functional rescue. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `NUMERIC_PROVENANCE_FIREWALL` | A structural or quantitative number supports a mechanism | Record file/row, numbering scheme, selected atoms or statistic, units, tool/version and reproducible command. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `NUMERIC_GLYPH_FIDELITY_GATE` | A direction, sign or ranking is read from a number extracted out of a PDF text layer | The text layer is not the document. Publisher fonts routinely encode `−` (U+2212) as an absent glyph and an en-dash as a stray letter, so an extractor silently returns `3.55` where the page prints `−3.55` — inverting every log2 value in a table and manufacturing a contradiction that does not exist. Before any directional claim from an extracted table: (a) verify the extraction against the **rendered page image**, not another text pass, and (b) treat a table that disagrees with its own group heading as an extraction fault until disproved, never as an author error. Cheap tell: search the document for `U+2212` and for a knockout notation such as `Capn1/` — a genotype that lost its superscript minus signs proves the whole file dropped them. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `CONTROL_TOPOLOGY_CHECK` | A control is called structurally independent or “off-site” | Check 3D contacts, burial, sensitivity to the same perturbation and independent functional data. Sequence distance is insufficient. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `CAUSAL_AXIS_GATE` | A perturbation is interpreted as proving a whole pathway | Conclude only on the directly measured axis and include `INCONCLUSIVE` in the outcome matrix. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `POCKET_PROVENANCE_GATE` | A pocket/druggability output supports GO/NO-GO | Preserve structure crop/input, preparation, tool/version, parameters, raw output, distance metric, lining residues and ensemble definition. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `TAG_PROXIMITY_CHECK` | A tagged construct is used as if the tag were neutral | Check tag distance from motifs and functional surfaces and require an untagged or alternative-tag control where relevant. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `IMPORTED_PREMISE_ATTRIBUTION_GATE` | A source is read that other surfaces already cite **as the origin of a premise** | Reading a paper answers "what does it say". It does not answer "does it say the thing we credit it with", and those are different questions. Before closing such a read, run the **inbound** audit: list every surface naming this source as a premise's origin, then mark each premise with one of **five** verdicts — `CONFIRMED_FIRST_HAND`; `ABSENT` (the source measures nothing bearing on it); `PASSED_THROUGH` (present only as this source's own citation of a third paper); `CONTRADICTED` (the source asserts the opposite); `SOURCE_ABSTRACT_OVERSTATES` (the source's own abstract states it unqualified while its body qualifies or negates it). `PASSED_THROUGH` obliges re-attribution to the terminal source and queueing it, because the chain is where species, allele class and assay silently change — **and the terminus must itself be audited, not assumed friendly.** Three measured instances, one session, 2026-08-06, each a different verdict. **(1) `ABSENT`:** five canonical surfaces credited PMID 19936220, a mouse paper, with *epileptogenesis*; it has no EEG, seizure, behaviour or brain histology, and its only brain measurement is organ weight. The co-cited premise in the same sentence (early death) *was* first-hand, which is exactly why the sentence read as verified — **a true clause next to a false one is the camouflage.** **(2) `CONTRADICTED`:** the terminus, PMID 19500159, does not merely fail to support the mouse claim — it states in three places, and in a Table 2 whose Epilepsy row is empty for both mouse models, that Wwox-null **mice show no epilepsy**. Chasing a chain to its end is not enough; the end can point the other way. **(3) `SOURCE_ABSTRACT_OVERSTATES`:** PMID 19500159 attributed the rat dwarfism to low pituitary GH, citing PMID 17803050. That paper's **abstract** says "decreased concentrations of plasma growth hormone" unqualified; its **body** reports the difference as not significant and concludes the dwarfism "cannot be explained solely by low levels of plasma GH". The citing author read the abstract. **Operational consequence:** when a cited premise appears as a short quotable phrase, diff the source's abstract against its body before accepting it — that shape is the signature of an abstract-sourced citation, and a validator that separates the two surfaces will localise it in seconds. **Why no existing check catches any of the three:** the unread-premise ratchet asks whether a citation was *read*, and all three citations were fully readable and would have "cleared" on being opened. Only the inbound audit asks whether the source says what it is credited with. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `SEALED_CONSUMER_CIRCULARITY_GATE` | *(superseded 2026-08-06 by `FREEZE_SCOPE_GATE`, which absorbs it as a corollary and is executable)* | Kept for lineage, not for use. It said: a verifier sealed by the baseline it checks cannot be repaired, only abandoned, so escalate the choice as a scientific-lifecycle decision and let the regression stay red until it is answered. Both halves were right and both are now stale as instructions. `FREEZE_SCOPE_GATE` states the verifier-exclusion corollary directly and adds the part this row missed: the real defect was not only that the verifier was sealed, but that **living canonical files were pinned by whole-file hash at all**, so the seal fired on an edit outside the derivation's scope. The DisMech Phase-2 window was closed on 2026-08-06 with a three-tier re-seal, and the red regression it describes is green. **Use `FREEZE_SCOPE_GATE` and its executable check.** | SUPERSEDED |

## Repair and synthesis integrity

| Gate | Trigger | Required representation / decision rule | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `REPAIR_PROPAGATION_MATRIX` | A baseline claim is reversed or materially narrowed | Enumerate source claim, corrected formulation, every canonical/non-canonical mirror, contaminated candidate and stale search string. Close only after a post-repair audit classifies every residual occurrence. | PENDING_EXECUTABLE |
| `DIRECTIONAL_CONTEXT_GATE` | Studies report apparently opposite directions | Record dose/abundance, duration, cell type, stress state, genotype and endpoint time. Merge only comparable edges; otherwise preserve a non-monotonic/context-dependent model. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `FUNCTIONAL_ORTHOGONALITY_PANEL` | A rescue or stabiliser is evaluated | Require independent readouts for abundance/localisation, relevant partner interaction, directional functional output, and viability/stress within a physiological window. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `ASSAY_SEMANTICS_GATE` | Titles/captions label an assay more strongly than methods allow | Record compared groups, perturbation/dose, physical measurand, inferred biological endpoint and specificity/directness. Protocol and groups override editorial labels. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `DEGRADATION_DIRECTION_GATE` | A degradation study contains several proteins, markers or inhibitors | Record substrate, recruiter/regulator, recognition determinant, compartment/pathway, perturbation and molecular species whose abundance changes. Unknown roles stay unknown. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `REPORTER_IDENTITY_GATE` | A reporter name implies a pathway | Record catalogue ID, molecular design, signal-generating event, primary/alternative pathways and specificity controls. Catalogue and primary method literature override shorthand names. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `MECHANISM_DIRECTNESS_GATE` | Marker plus phenotype is turned into an unmeasured intermediate | Separate perturbation, directly measured physical variable, observed endpoint, inferred intermediate/external premise and the assay that could falsify it. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `TARGET_ATTRIBUTION_GATE` | A compound-associated rescue is called target-mediated | Record identity/dose, binding or engagement, inactive chemical control, genetic epistasis, resistant rescue, off-target/cytotoxicity and direct-vs-surrogate endpoint. Require orthogonal chemical and genetic support. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `EVIDENCE_REUSE_GATE` | A review, reused cohort or same-lineage publication appears to replicate a finding | Record source type, new-vs-reused data, originating paper/cohort/figure, laboratory independence, same-experiment mechanism and relevant interests. Reuse improves traceability, not replication count. | ACTIVE_METHOD |

## Outcome, comparator and rescue validity

| Gate | Trigger | Required representation / decision rule | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `OUTCOME_CLAIM_GATE` | A study uses prognostic, predictive or biomarker language | Record biomarker, prespecified outcome, comparison, effect size/uncertainty, covariates, validation and incremental utility. Without a linked analysis, classify as descriptive/exploratory. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `STAGE_MATCHED_COMPARATOR_GATE` | Omics or phenotype groups differ in genotype and biological stage/context | Record genotype, stage, tissue/cell type, age/exposure, clonal selection and missing comparator. Genotype attribution requires matched, longitudinal or isogenic separation. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `PROTEIN_STATE_IDENTITY_GATE` | Abundance is used as proof of functional rescue | Represent abundance, solubility, PTM, localisation, partner occupancy, functional output and dose/stress. Stable, sequestered or mislocalised protein is not a rescue. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `STIMULUS_CONDITIONAL_RESCUE_GATE` | A rescue is measured only at baseline or only under challenge | Record baseline, defined stressor, dose/time, target state, desired output, off-axis outputs, assay-sensitivity control and rescue dose. A GO requires physiological dose and no pathological hyper-response. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `ALLELIC_ARCHITECTURE_RESOLUTION_GATE` | Exon-level CNV/SV calls drive allele-specific interpretation or design | Resolve affected exons, breakpoints, phase/cis-trans, discontinuous events, transcript/junction, protein isoform and assay closing each level. Exon overlap alone is insufficient. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `DUAL_DIAGNOSIS_ATTRIBUTION_GATE` | A report contains more than one plausible molecular diagnosis | Separate variants/classification, phase, functional evidence, gene-specific phenotypes, diagnostic biomarkers, target-specific treatment and temporal outcome. Keep attribution confounded until independently disambiguated. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `DENOMINATOR_FIRST_COHORT_GATE` | A cohort percentage is interpreted as prevalence, yield or prognosis | Record source population, selection, total denominator, tested, negatives, platforms, exclusions, access/cost and centre/founder clustering before interpreting percentages. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `CUTPOINT_ENDPOINT_DRIFT_GATE` | A continuous marker is dichotomised by a data-derived "optimal cutpoint", and results are then compared across endpoints, cohorts or signatures | The cutpoint is part of the result. Record, per cell: the cutpoint method, whether it was re-optimised **per endpoint**, the resulting group sizes, the total comparisons performed and whether multiplicity was corrected. Decision rules: (a) a marker significant for one endpoint and not another is **not** an endpoint difference unless the cutpoint is held fixed — re-optimisation makes "high" a different set of patients in each column; (b) no effect size is quotable without its group `n` beside it, and a group below a stated floor is exploratory only; (c) an intersection signature that discards discordant subjects reports what fraction of the cohort it discarded; (d) an asterisk on a hazard ratio whose confidence interval spans 1 marks a different test — say which. Measured instance (PMID 37519886): WWOX-high in GSE31684 is 15 patients for OS, 28 for DSS and 29 for PFI; the flagship `HR 0.190` compares 25 patients with 7 and discards 58 of 93; the same comparison in a cohort four times larger is null. | ACTIVE_METHOD |

## Reproducibility, replication and prediction

| Gate | Trigger | Required representation / decision rule | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PRE_EXTERNAL_CLAIM_VERIFICATION_GATE` | A factual or numeric claim will leave the repository | Reproduce it with a command or reconcile it with the current source file; cite the verification next to the number. Plausible explanation is not verification. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `SYNTHETIC_REPLICATE_GATE` | Bootstrap, pseudo-replication or augmentation increases apparent sample count | Record original biological n, technical n, synthetic n, randomisation unit, synthetic-data steps and uncertainty returned to the biological level. Synthetic samples never become independent experiments. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `CROSS_LINE_REPLICATION_GATE` | Different cell lines are pooled as replicates | Separate independent lines, within-line biological replicates, technical replicates, randomisation unit and treatment-by-line model. Cross-line convergence is contextual generalisation, not exchangeable replication. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `ASSOCIATION_TO_PREDICTION_GATE` | An association is called a predictor | Separate association, prognostic model, validated prediction and clinical utility. Language cannot exceed the level supported by timing, validation, discrimination, calibration and incremental value. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `TREATMENT_PREDICTION_INTERACTION_GATE` | Outcome among treated subjects is interpreted as differential response | Distinguish prognostic effect, treatment effect and treatment-by-marker interaction. Require an appropriate control, prespecified interaction and independent replication before “predictive biomarker”. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `PHOSPHOSTATE_TRANSITION_GATE` | Cross-sectional PTM states are drawn as a temporal switch | Require same-system time course, stoichiometry/localisation, modifying enzymes, validated mutants, necessity/sufficiency and rescue. Otherwise use `reciprocal state association`. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `RANK_OVERRIDE_SELECTION_GATE` | A ranking, score or feature-selection algorithm is run, and the entity carried forward is **not** the one it ranked first | A ranking used to justify a choice and then overridden is not evidence for that choice. Record: the ranked list with its scores, which entity was actually taken, the stated override criterion, and whether that criterion is the hypothesis under test. If it is, the selection is circular and every downstream result is conditioned on it — say so where the result is reported, not only in the methods. Also record what happened to the discarded top entity: silence about it is the tell. Measured instance (PMID 37519886): mSVM-RFE ranked `HIPK1-AS1` first (AvgRank 1.0, `p < 0.001`) and `LINC01137` second (2.6); `LINC01137` was carried forward because it moved in the direction the ceRNA model required, and the first-ranked feature is not mentioned again. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `SYNTHESIS_MEDIATION_GATE` | A review or integrative paper combines separately true observations into a new mechanistic arrow | Partition the chain by provenance: which links are measured in this paper, which are imported from named primaries, which are general background, and which exist only because the author joined them. A conjunction of true premises is not evidence for the mediating arrow. Until one system perturbs the proposed mediator and measures the downstream endpoint—with necessity/rescue where the claim requires causality—record the joined path as `INFERENZA`, route load-bearing support to the primaries, and forbid it from creating or upgrading a causal claim. Measured instance (PMID 33255508): public OPC expression + an external lipid study + an external trafficking interactome + general myelin biology were joined into `WWOX -> lipid/trafficking -> myelination`; no neural mediation experiment was performed. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `DEVELOPMENTAL_TIMEPOINT_ATTRIBUTION_GATE` | A congenital or germline model is used to assign an observed phenotype to a prenatal developmental window | The time at which the lesion exists is not the time at which the endpoint was measured. Record the earliest sampled age for each carried phenotype and distinguish: `measured prenatal`, `measured postnatal`, and `prenatal origin inferred`. A postnatal endpoint may support a developmental continuum or motivate a prenatal mechanism, but cannot itself establish prenatal misassembly, migration failure or irreversibility. Measured instance (PMID 31340538): a germline Wwox frameshift was repeatedly called a prenatal cortex anchor although every new experiment was performed at PND5–21; the primary result is early postnatal neurite/glial/myelin maturation. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `LOCATOR_OVERSHOOT_GATE` | A completed reading narrows, reverses, corroborates or removes a `consolidated baseline` claim, declares a MAJOR change class, or produces a rejection that closes a research direction | **Audit the locators blind before the reading touches canonical state.** A reading can be careful, honest and complete, and still say more than its source — and no structural check sees it: the receipt is valid, the manifest passes, every locator resolves, and the proposition still overshoots the sentence beneath it. Give a separate agent only the `(proposition, quote, anchor)` triples plus the source artefact — **never** the dossier, the reader's identity, the ledger entry or the conclusions — and require two answers per triple: does this quote support this proposition, and does the source say MORE or LESS than the proposition claims. Verdicts: `SUPPORTED` · `OVERSHOOT` · `UNDERSHOOT` · `NOT_IN_SOURCE` · `UNVERIFIABLE_SURFACE`. Any `NOT_IN_SOURCE` blocks canonical promotion; overshoot and undershoot must be rewritten or argued in the commit candidate; a figure reading is an attestation and must be declared as one — "no significance marker is drawn" is not "the difference was tested and found absent". 🔴 **Blindness is the active ingredient, not the tooling.** Measured 2026-08-06: two reviewers with full context passed the same work back and forth twice, each finding real defects in the other's; a reviewer who did not know the authorship then found eight more in an hour. Knowing who wrote something makes a reviewer reconstruct intent instead of testing behaviour. Measured instance (PMID 30290271): a complete, well-executed reading recorded *"NPY: whole hippocampus not significant"* while the paper reports no statistic for that comparison at all — its text says only that the count "is shown in Fig. 2D". A panel reading, legitimate and different from a reported test. Deliberately NOT applied to every reading: a gate that fires on everything gets switched off, and complete reads are already the throughput bottleneck. Invocable as `legend-locator-audit`. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `PATTERN_ALREADY_SOLVED_GATE` | About to build a guard, freeze, ratchet, invariant or verification mechanism | **Search this repository for the same concept before writing it, and if it exists, adopt it or write down why you are diverging.** The recurring failure is not ignorance — it is a solution present in the repo, applied correctly at one site, and not carried to the second site that needed it. Three measured instances in a single day, 2026-08-06: (1) the receipt ledger was pinned by `append_only_prefix` because someone thought about growth *for the file that grows*, while the canonical registries in the same baseline were pinned whole-file and broke on the next batch; (2) the corpus marker regex was hand-copied into four guards that could drift apart silently, until one shared definition replaced them; (3) `tracked_paths()` sat in `test_release_surface.py`, with a docstring already stating that a directory holding no tracked files is fine, while two checks *in that same file* asked the local disk instead and made it permanently red — after which it absorbed a genuine defect. 🔴 **Only part of this is mechanizable, and pretending otherwise is the same error one level up.** What a test can catch: a definition duplicated across guards (see `test_every_guard_uses_the_one_shared_definition`). What no test catches: the same *concept* implemented twice with different rigour, or an existing helper left unused three functions away. That half is caught by an adversarial reader who does not know who wrote what — which is why the blind review before infrastructure merges is a rite and not a suggestion. The cheapest concrete habit: when a mechanism feels novel, grep for its vocabulary (`prefix`, `anchor`, `tracked`, `scope`, `ratchet`) before writing it. Twice out of three, the word was already there. 🔴 **Variant found 2026-08-09, and it is the harder one: the defence exists *here*, works, and has no test of its own because another defence covers it.** It passes every suite, it reads as healthy redundancy, and it dies silently the first time the outer defence is narrowed — which always arrives disguised as cleanup. Measured twice in two days, both in the same validator: reverting `_normalise_text` to `str.split()` escaped the entire suite, and so did putting `\f` back into the whitespace class, because the `SUSPECT` surface check refuses the artifact before the normaliser can ever be observed. Neither was found by review or by the suite; both were found only by mutating that specific line. **The rule: every layer carries at least one assertion that names it.** Overlapping defences are good; a defence that exists only as a side effect of another is not a defence, it is a coincidence with a good track record. When adding a guard behind an existing one, write the unit assertion for the inner layer *before* the integration test, or the integration test will hide it. 🔴 **Third variant, 2026-08-10, and it is not about the code at all: the verification ran where the defect could not occur.** `is_nested_checkout` was added to the publication gate and mutation-tested from inside a per-session worktree — a checkout that, by construction, contains no nested checkout. Every one of the 54 regression targets was green, and none of them could have been anything else. Two walkers had been left unpruned (`test_link_targets.markdown_files`, `test_release_surface.unexpectedly_ignored_files`), and both failed immediately in the shared checkout: 351 files of another repository reported as public files this one had silently hidden. **The rule: a green suite is evidence only if the environment it ran in is capable of exhibiting the defect.** Before trusting a fix, either reproduce the trigger where you are testing, or state plainly that you did not. And the durable form is the one the two repaired suites now carry: **the test mounts the condition itself** — a temp root with a real nested checkout — plus a companion assertion that the fixture would have failed without the fix, so the next reader cannot verify in a place where failure is impossible. The three variants together: a defence not carried to the second site; a defence with no test of its own; a test with no environment that can break it. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `FREEZE_SCOPE_GATE` | A provenance freeze pins the SHA-256 of a file that is designed to keep changing — an append-only ledger, a canonical registry, any living state | **Freeze what you consumed, not the container it arrived in.** Three tiers, chosen per input, never one rule for all: (1) *immutable artefacts* — authored data, contracts, outputs, derivation scripts — whole-file SHA-256, where any change is a real violation; (2) *append-only ledgers* — pin **prefix length + prefix digest** at freeze, so growth is legal and only rewriting the frozen prefix is a violation; (3) *living canonical files* — pin the **extracted scope actually consumed** (the specific claim/paper blocks) plus the revision, so an edit outside the scope is not a violation and an edit inside it is the one you want to hear about. A whole-file hash over living state does not report "my dependency changed", it reports "the file moved", and those are different facts: it fires constantly on irrelevant edits and cannot fire *harder* on the one edit that matters. Two corollaries. **A verifier must never be a sealed input of the seal it implements** — otherwise the seal can only be abandoned, never repaired, because every correction to the checker falsifies it. And **a freeze must declare its own lifecycle**: frozen-at revision plus whether the window is open or closed, so a later reader is not forced to re-litigate whether a failure is real. Measured instance (DisMech Phase-2, 2026-08-06): the baseline pinned `claim_registry_current.md` and `paper_registry_current.md` by whole file; `BATCH_20260806_001` broke it by editing CLAIM 005, a claim outside the export scope of CLAIM 016/024/035, which had not moved at all. Both the verifier script and its test suite were themselves sealed inputs, so the defect could not be corrected in place. 🔴 **This entry was wrong when first written and `scripts/test_freeze_scope.py` refuted it on first run.** The prose claimed the receipt ledger was also whole-file pinned and that the append-only pattern "was not adopted"; in fact the same baseline pins it correctly with `verification_policy: append_only_prefix`, three lines above the two entries that are wrong. The tiering was already understood by whoever wrote the freeze — it was applied to the growing file and not to the living ones. Enforce this gate with the executable check, never with this row: a registry entry is the mechanism that already failed, and it failed again here within ten minutes of being written. 🔴 **Measured edge, `BATCH_20260806_002`, hours after the three-tier re-seal:** the scoped seal fired on `CLAIM 035` and `PAPER 056` when four new sibling blocks were **appended after them**. Diffed against HEAD, `CLAIM 016`, `CLAIM 024`, `PAPER 019` and `PAPER 055` were **byte-identical**, and the only change to the other two was the trailing `\n\n---\n` separator that appending a next block necessarily adds — those two had been the **last blocks in their files**, so their extraction previously ran to EOF with no separator. Nothing the derivation consumed changed. **The refinement this implies:** a block-scoped extractor must either strip trailing block separators, or the freeze must avoid pinning a block that is last in a growing file, because *"a new record was added after mine"* and *"my record changed"* are different facts and the tier-3 rule exists precisely to keep them apart. ✅ **Resolved 2026-08-07** (`rev.17`), on the operator's authorisation and along the growth principle: the extractor now strips trailing separators; the scope digests were **recomputed from the frozen blob at `8ca27121`, never from the live file**, so that the live file then agreeing is *positive proof* nothing consumed changed rather than a re-seal by assertion — the fix is also the verification; and digests are now recorded **per block**, so a report can say *"3 of 47 exported blocks moved, here they are"* instead of *"the scope changed"*. **Fourth tier, added by the same work:** a freeze over living state **reports drift, it does not assert violation**. Historical drift informs (`scope_drift`); the gate bites only at export time (`assert_exportable`), where shipping a contribution derived from superseded claims is the actual risk. Acknowledgements bind to the exact live digest and **expire the moment the block moves again**, because otherwise *resolved* decays into *ignored once, ignored forever*. Mutation-tested 7/7. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `MARKER_TO_FUNCTION_GATE` | Marker-positive cell counts or enzyme abundance are compressed into cell loss, transmitter quantity, circuit function or a medication rule | Keep four layers separate unless each is measured: (1) marker-positive abundance, (2) lineage/survival, (3) transmitter concentration or synthesis flux, and (4) electrophysiological/network function. Regional marker counts cannot become whole-system cell loss; enzyme protein cannot become neurotransmitter abundance; neither can become a drug recommendation. Record the exact region, significance pattern and assay, then name the missing discriminator. Measured instance (PMID 30290271): PV/NPY stereology and GAD65/67 Western were repeatedly summarized as interneuron loss, reduced GABA synthesis and E/I imbalance although no lineage, GABA or electrophysiological endpoint was measured; NPY was significant only in DG. | ACTIVE_METHOD |

## Externalisation, automation and workflow authority

| Gate | Trigger | Required representation / decision rule | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `EXTERNAL_DOSSIER_CANONICAL_DRIFT_GATE` | A dossier is labelled ready for external review | Build `strong statement → primary source → current state → superseded claim → sustainable epistemic level`. Any unexplained divergence from a canonical repair blocks release. | PENDING_EXECUTABLE |
| `CLAIM_MIRROR_PARITY_GATE` | A claim is added, removed or repaired in the canonical registry | Compare the complete set of `CLAIM NNN` registry IDs with the Working Model BLOCK 2 table. A missing, orphaned or duplicate mirror ID blocks BATCH_COMMIT even when every individual wikilink resolves. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `PIPELINE_HANDOFF_FORMAT_GATE` | One stage's output file is fed to the next stage as input | A completeness gate that blocks on zero records proves the chain *noticed* the break, not that the chain works. Assert the stronger property: every format the producing stage can emit must be an accepted input of the consuming stage, with a regression test on the **most natural operator input**, not only the decorated one. Observed instance: the intake triage echoes bare PMIDs in its `Input` column while the sweep matched only a literal `PMID` prefix, so a plain PMID list — the commonest batch there is — yielded zero records at every run. Loosening a parser is not enough; scope the widened match so it cannot swallow years, counts or other in-range digits. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `OBLIGATION_WITHOUT_ARTIFACT_GATE` | A workflow requires a step that leaves no output of its own — assess the group, measure field density, expand multi-hop, cross-query the existing corpus | **An obligation that produces no artifact cannot be enforced, because absence is silent.** Post-hoc review is structurally blind here: it inspects what was written, and a skipped step writes nothing. Two inversions close it. (a) Give every such obligation a **required slot** in a work manifest, where it is either evidenced — integer counts, resolved identifiers, measured hits — or **explicitly waived with an argument long enough to disagree with**; a short waiver like "n/a" is how a checklist dies, so reject it. (b) **Gate the strongest claim on the manifest, at the moment it is made, not afterwards**: `complete_fulltext_read` is refused unless the manifest validates, which makes the over-claim unavailable rather than merely auditable. Stated residual: a manifest can be filled with hollow but well-formed content — this makes that *reviewable* instead of invisible, and makes the honest path cheaper than the dishonest one. Enforced by `deepdive_manifest.py` + `fulltext_receipts.py record` + `session_self_eval.py`. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `READING_MUST_LAND_GATE` | A full text is recorded as completely read | Reading that reaches no ledger, queue or registry is indistinguishable from reading that never happened — and worse, the receipt asserts coverage the system cannot show. Require (a) the PMID/DOI and a real record ID to coexist in the same structured landing section — an incidental process-note mention does not count — and (b) **every output the receipt declares to resolve**: named files must exist, named record IDs must be present in the file that names them, and a named output file must contain the receipt's PMID, DOI or receipt ID. Existence alone is insufficient because concurrent studies can reuse the same candidate filename. A generated view derived from the ledger the paper failed to reach does not count as landing. Enforced by `session_self_eval.py`. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `PDF_PAGE_COUNT_RECONCILIATION_GATE` | Page count is used to support `complete_fulltext_read` | PDF metadata, `file`, a browser and the parser can disagree. Coverage is anchored to the document actually enumerated by the sequential-reading parser: record its page count, count supplements separately, and explain every mismatch in the dossier/receipt before claiming completeness. A larger metadata number is not safer; it can create fictitious unread pages or false reassurance. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `SELF_ASSESSMENT_IS_NOT_EVIDENCE_GATE` | A session or agent reports on how well its own process ran | An agent grading its own work returns green through its own blind spots: on 2026-07-26 a session reported "the process ran well" while four substantive gaps were open, each invisible to it precisely because it was the thing not done. Split the review: everything checkable becomes an executable check that can fail the session, and the residue stays as **written** judgement questions whose unanswered state is itself visible. Never let the same pass both perform the work and certify it, and never write the summary before the diagnosis — a takeaway drafted first describes a session that went well. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `SELF_EVALUATION_MUST_REACH_WORKFLOW_GATE` | A diagnostic protocol exists but the orchestrator can close without invoking it | A strong checklist outside the normative route is orphan documentation. Wire it into the bootstrap, operating core and orchestrator **before** growth/takeaways; require an executable verdict plus a durable written diagnosis. Route executable failures into `legend_lint.py`, the component that actually emits `BLOCK_BATCH_COMMIT`, and regression-test that an orphan read changes the LINT verdict. A weak judgement answer owes a proportional micro-upgrade. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `COVERAGE_BEFORE_EXPANSION` | New blocking logic is proposed | Add positive, negative and end-to-end regression coverage for existing guards before expanding their authority. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `MUTATION_BASELINE_GATE` | A mutation battery counts a defect as caught because its target exits non-zero | Run the exact target unmutated first. Only a green baseline can make a later red result informative; a baseline-red target is `UNTESTABLE`, is excluded from the caught denominator and makes the overall run incomplete/FAIL. Also require every distinct target to be green in the intended clean environment before trusting the aggregate score. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `VERIFICATION_SCOPE_DISCLOSURE_GATE` | A validator has optional integrity checks but prints a successful verdict | The success line must name what was and was not verified. A structural pass may not read like artifact, hash or exact-quote verification; the strict profile names each enabled control. Test both profiles so later wording cannot silently restore the overclaim. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `RADAR_DECISION_SEPARATION_GATE` | Automated scouting proposes repository changes | Separate read-only radar/reporting from human-reviewed curation and implementation. Radar cannot modify canonical state, install dependencies or execute external code. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `READING_DEBT_PARTITION_GATE` | A batch checkpoint reports one residual count | Partition into `READ_NEXT`, `ACCESS_DEBT`, `AUDIT_OR_INTEGRITY_ONLY` and `SUPPLEMENT_DEBT`, with one unambiguous resume cursor. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `FULLTEXT_RECEIPT_PERSISTENCE_GATE` | Any route retrieves, queries, samples or completely reads a full text | Resolve PMID/DOI, query the authoritative disease ledger, emit the honest depth and section coverage, then append under lock before claiming completion. Missing/invalid history, an unpersisted event or a non-exhaustive route cannot clear reading debt. Historical uncertainty stays `legacy_reconstruction`/`unknown_legacy`. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `EVIDENCE_SURFACE_BINDING_GATE` | A route asks to persist a new `complete_fulltext_read` | Provenance labels are assertions, not proof. Use one authoritative persistence choke point and fail closed there: require a lawful local full-text snapshot with matching SHA-256; a current-schema work manifest with zero declared gaps; and, for every carried proposition, a non-abstract surface plus a fingerprinted artifact. Match text locators against XML/HTML/TXT/DOCX while separating an XML abstract from its body; bind visual locators to the inspected image hash. Permit corpus/abstract material only at honest non-clearing depths. Mutation-test each refusal individually so an unrelated failing test cannot mask its removal. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `RECEIPT_CORRECTION_WITHOUT_REREAD_GATE` | An immutable receipt has correct reading evidence but a wrong output path/record caused by a collision | Never rewrite history and never manufacture a second read. Append a `receipt_correction` linked to the latest event; keep identity, analysis time, evidence depth, source fingerprint and coverage byte-equivalent, changing only correction metadata and outputs. Consumers select the latest equal-depth event; regressions reject substantive evidence drift. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `APPEND_ONLY_IS_A_MECHANISM_GATE` | Any file is described as append-only, immutable, authoritative or a durable ledger | Calling a file append-only does nothing; the word must be produced by a mechanism. Require (a) a per-record hash chain, so a rewritten or deleted historical record invalidates every record after it, (b) an **external tail anchor** in a different file, because a chain cannot see truncation — lopping off the last records leaves a perfectly self-consistent prefix — and (c) a writer that re-verifies the whole prior history *inside its lock*, so an append cannot extend, or launder, a corrupted past. State the residual limit explicitly: a coordinated edit of ledger *and* anchor is reviewable, not undetectable. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `CONTRACT_MUST_REACH_THE_GATE` | A new integrity contract is added to the system | A contract enforced only by reporting tools is documentation. Trace it to the component that can actually emit the blocking verdict — here `legend_lint.py`, the sole source of `BLOCK_BATCH_COMMIT`/`BLOCK_SYSTEM` — and verify with an adversarial test that the damage the contract describes really changes the verdict. Views that "fail closed" (coverage reports, queues) constrain nobody: nothing consults them before a commit. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `HISTORICAL_DEBT_RATCHET_GATE` | Legacy records assert something the current evidence standard cannot certify | Do not delete them (falsifies history) and do not retroactively upgrade them (manufactures evidence). Freeze both the count and exact record identities: a count-only ratchet can be bypassed by removing one grandfathered assertion and adding a new unsupported one elsewhere. Block every non-grandfathered identity; report a falling set as an invitation to lower the baseline and allowlist. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `DISMECH_DELTA_GATE` | LEGEND claims novelty relative to an external schema/tool/KB | Audit the real external resource first and state only what it demonstrably does not represent. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `EVIDENCE_CELL_CROSS_REVIEW_GATE` | A large reading batch is delegated | Separate primary reader, adversarial reviewer and graph/synthesis curator; require coverage, anchors, epistemic tags, novelty delta and unresolved-conflict escalation. | PROPOSED |
| `SURFACE_CONTAINMENT_PRECHECK_GATE` | A structured surface is chosen, **before** the paper is opened | **A surface can be complete and still not contain what you need to read.** Deposits differ in *where* they put content, not only in whether they have it: measured 2026-08-11 on the shared corpus, **7 of 34 parseable XML surfaces place every `<fig>` outside `<body>`**, and 2 more place some there. An agent reading through a body-scoped extractor sees no caption on those, gets no error, and receives text that reads as complete — and the manifest still validates, because a validator checks what you QUOTED, not what you were able to SEE. This is a **reading** gap, not a verification one: `deepdive_manifest._xml_surfaces` walks the article and separates only the abstract, so a caption locator verifies wherever the caption sits. Two consequences. (a) Count `<fig>` inside `<body>` against `<fig>` in the article before declaring an `article_text`; if they differ, choose the route that contains them or declare that captions are read separately. Routes differ per article and per publisher, so there is no preferable route — on `PMID 23435430` Europe PMC contains all eight captions and `efetch` none, while on `PMID 27308504` `efetch` was the correct choice for a different need. **Ask the question of each article, about what the reading needs to quote.** (b) The count belongs in the manifest beside `figure_coverage`, which today measures how many figures carry a locator and **not whether their captions were legible**. Cost of complying: one command, about a second, plus about a minute to read the captions. Cost of skipping, measured: `PMID 24308844` was read with a body-scoped extractor and its manifest carried no caption locator at all — an absence that looked like a budget choice for two days and was an invisible surface. The caption pass then produced a character-verified quote for a finding the reading had only been able to attest from pixels, and a second locator on an engineered mutant annotated as a feature of the wild-type protein. Two limits, both stated: this is about containment, not corruption — a contained caption can still be wrong; and it is meaningless on HTML, where a page has one `<body>` and everything is inside it by construction, so the answer there is *not applicable*, never *clean*. Measured by `framework/scripts/caption_census.py`, which requires an absolute root and echoes it back — the first census of this class was wrong because a shell had inherited a worktree cwd — and counts by tag name, never by regex over markup: a competing census used `re.findall(r"<fig\b", ...)`, which matches `<fig-count>`, a metadata counter that lives in `<article-meta>` and is therefore always outside the body. **A plausible count is not a verified count, and the difference is never visible in the result — only in the predicate.** | ACTIVE_METHOD → PROPOSED_EXECUTABLE (the manifest slot is contract territory; see `FT-058`) |

## Release, front-door and claim-maturity gates

These gates were learned by applying the framework **to itself** while preparing
this public edition. They govern what a repository, README or proposal is
allowed to claim about its own maturity — the same false-negative/overclaim
discipline turned inward.

| Gate | Trigger | Required representation / decision rule | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CAPABILITY_CLAIM_MATURITY_GATE` | A public document advertises a capability | Classify every capability as `SPECIFIED` (workflow documented), `IMPLEMENTED` (code present), `BUNDLED` (required assets/dependencies shipped) or `EXTERNAL/OPTIONAL` (separate install or authorization). A present `SKILL.md` proves specification, never distributed executability. Predictive backends are described as hypothesis generators, never as validation. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `FRONT_DOOR_MATURITY_GATE` | An entry document mixes existing assets with intentions | Within the first two screens, in order: `AVAILABLE NOW`, `IN DEVELOPMENT`, the problem, the scientific question, the planned outputs. Present tense only for what exists; future tense or `in development` for unfrozen benchmarks, unrun evaluations and unsubmitted work. An observed error is a *candidate* benchmark case until evidence and adjudication freeze it. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `README_EVIDENCE_FUNNEL_GATE` | A showcase document catalogues before it demonstrates | Order the argument as a funnel: problem → cross-domain insight → observable transformation → epistemic discipline → failure loop → the question → repository map → limits. Detail only the decisive differences; defer the census to a capability file. Every status claim uses `available`, `work in progress` or `planned/proposed`. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `TRACK_FIT_GATE` | A proposal is written against a multi-track call | Before freezing, tag every work package and budget line `PRIMARY_SCIENTIFIC_OUTCOME`, `ENABLING_METHOD` or `OUT_OF_TRACK`. Title, first question, main deliverable, metrics and the budget majority must converge on the declared track's outcome; tooling, provenance and evaluation stay enabling methods. A reviewer must be able to answer "which mechanism will be better understood?" in one sentence. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `ANCHOR_MIGRATION_GATE` | Links, headings or record identifiers are renamed, translated or migrated | A record can survive a rewrite while its *navigability* dies. Before declaring a migration complete: (a) every fragment must resolve against the **complete** target heading, (b) records defined outside headings must use block IDs rather than an impossible heading fragment, and (c) **the migration must be re-run through the privacy gate** — inlining a heading copies its text into every referring line, so a heading carrying an identifier propagates it repository-wide. A green file-existence check is not a green link check. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |
| `LOCATOR_ASSERTION_SCOPE_GATE` | A locator, quote or citation is screened for corruption, drift or unsupported content | **Screen everything the locator asserts, not only the string it quotes.** A locator is `proposition` + `snippet` + `anchor`, and each of the three makes a claim about the source. On 2026-08-09 a corrupted-surface sweep classified 24 of 29 locators as clean by testing the `snippet` alone. `entries[28]` quoted `Total rats 228 83 233.3 77.8 0.47` — no corrupted character, because the quote stops at the χ² value — while its **anchor** asserted *"chi-square 0.47, P > 0.50"*, and that row reads `0.47 \x1f0.50` in the text layer with the column header printed as `D2` for `χ²`. The one statement the surface could not support lived in the field nobody screened. It surfaced only because it was the single locator for which no unique search needle could be derived (its words are all digits) — **a defect found by the incidental failure of an unrelated mechanism is a defect nobody was looking for**, the same shape as the swallowed-neighbour and abstract-corpus findings. Corollary for image anchors: containment must be verified against *the span the locator names*, and a bare quote fragment does not identify a location — `search_for` returned hits in two columns and the check reported the wrong rectangle. Each image locator therefore carries a needle unique on its page. | ACTIVE_METHOD |
| `PUBLIC_RELEASE_GATE` | Any material leaves a private workspace for a public one | Verify semantic privacy and re-identifying *combinations* (not keywords), provenance labelling, secrets, link/dependency integrity, README↔repository consistency and clean-clone executability. Keyword scrubbing is explicitly insufficient: the aggregate of individually harmless facts is the attack surface. | ACTIVE_EXECUTABLE |

> **Why these are in a scientific registry.** The failure they prevent is the
> same one the scientific gates prevent — a claim outrunning its evidence — with
> the repository itself as the object. `CAPABILITY_CLAIM_MATURITY_GATE` exists
> because "the skill file is present" was very nearly accepted as proof that a
> capability shipped.

## Executable seed

The public runtime currently enforces a smaller executable subset:

- missing-current and commit-boundary separation;
- duplicate paper/claim/discovery/commit-candidate identifiers;
- exact claim-ID parity between the canonical registry and Working Model mirror;
- allowed claim/paper states;
- paper/corpus claim-link integrity;
- premise tagging and revival triggers for negative conclusions;
- batch completeness and publication-integrity routing;
- study deduplication and retraction checking;
- full-text receipt schema/semantics, atomic anchored append, linear lineage, identity-based
  historical ratchet, exhaustive route classification and fail-closed coverage/queue joins;
- public-release privacy, provenance, secrets, links and clean-clone checks.
