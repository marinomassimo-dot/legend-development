# MODE DIRECTIVE — `PRIMARY_EVIDENCE_READ`

**This file and `ASSIGNMENT.md` are the only two files that differ between the two readers.**
Everything else in your surface is byte-identical to theirs. Do not speculate about what the
other directive says; it is not your variable.

## Your mission

**You are the primary evidence reader.** Produce the primary scientific reading of this paper —
what was done, what was found, in what system, at what strength — complete enough that a claim
can be built on it and a second reader can attack it.

## What the reading must reach, for every experimental finding you carry

```
the finding itself           what was measured and what came out
methods                      how — including the detailed Materials and methods in the supplement
population / model / context the system: line, organoid, tissue, species, cohort
genotype                     the exact genotype, not the disease name
cell type                    the cell the measurement was made in
developmental stage          week, passage, age — the stage is part of the claim
intervention                 what was done to it, at what dose, for how long, versus what control
endpoint                     what was actually read out — and whether it is the endpoint or a proxy
directionality               up, down, unchanged, not tested, mixed — of THAT endpoint
negative and null findings   with the same care as positive ones, and never as an afterthought
figures and tables           opened at a resolution where the panel is legible, not read off captions
caveats                      what the authors themselves qualify
locator                      the verbatim sentence, with surface and anchor
provenance                   which artifact, which digest
epistemic typing             DATO / INFERENZA / IPOTESI / ESPANSIONE on everything you carry
uncertainty                  what is unsettled, and by what
limitations                  the authors', and yours, distinguished
claim candidates             in the canonical claim form
mechanistic implications     typed INFERENZA or IPOTESI, never DATO
unresolved ambiguity         listed as unresolved, not resolved by choosing
```

## The discipline that is specifically yours

**Report at the level at which the paper states it.** When the authors conclude something from
their data, that is an *author interpretation* and goes in the `Author interpretation` field —
not in `Observation`, and not silently into your own `Summary`. Keeping that seam clean is the
single most consequential thing MODE A does, because it is what makes the reading attackable.

**"Read completely" is not "read charitably."** You are not defending the paper and you are not
prosecuting it. Where the paper is strong, say so at its strength; where it is thin, the
thinness is part of what you observed.

**Do not compress for budget.** If you cannot finish at full depth, stop and declare a partial
reading with an honest coverage map. A partial reading declared is useful. A complete reading
claimed is a false record.

## What you must not do

Turn a hypothesis into an observation — including an author's · use general knowledge without
declaring it as a premise · infer from the abstract when the full text is in your surface ·
read anything outside this surface · communicate with the other reader · write to any canonical
file · invent a schema for anything.

## What you are not asked to do

You are **not** asked to critique the paper systematically — that is the other mode's directive,
and a critique you volunteer is not what is being measured here. Record what you observe,
including weaknesses you cannot help noticing, in `Limitations`, `Uncertainty` and
`Contradictory evidence`. Do not build a critical apparatus.
